About KeyChinGu

The real Korea we want to share with foreign friends visiting Seoul for the first time

What is KeyChinGu?

KeyChinGu (Key + Chingu, "the key to becoming friends") is a solo media project publishing 617+ curated guides that help foreign travelers enjoy Seoul more deeply and accurately.

Centered on 8 areas — Hongdae, Gangnam, Jongno, Itaewon, Seongsu, Yeonhui-dong and more — we produce cafe, restaurant, bar, shopping, experience, nature, and culture content as a full 4-language set (Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese).

🔍 102 Deep Dives 🎉 + 📚 103 Meta Guides

To fully cover the foreigner lifecycle across 8 axes in 2026, we run two clusters:

  • 🔍 102 Deep Dives 🎉 — the standard "12-pick, 4-axis" pattern (6 FAQs + 3 full-day courses + 5 seasonal axes). Arrival & essentials 11 + food & drink 11 + culture & tradition 13 + K-content 10 + wellness 7 + shopping 3 + nightlife 4 + outskirts 7.
  • 📚 103 Meta Guides 🎉 (a 100+ milestone) — 18 segments (Japan, China, Southeast Asia, Europe, US, Middle East, etc. — 15.75M/year) + 48 category metas (including 8 Korean-food deep-dive sub-categories: bunsik, liquor, tteok, gukbap, hanjeongsik, BBQ, noodles, dumplings) + 5 by-travel-party types + 24 cities beyond Seoul + 5 practical metas (K-ETA, transit, SIM) + 3 seasonal events (BIFF in Oct, chimaek in Jul, Jongmyo Jerye in May — 2 UNESCO items).

Anchored by 2 UNESCO items (700-year-old Andong soju, tteok-making 2018) + a 4-season K-dessert full coverage (spring, summer, fall, winter).

Why did we build it?

Foreign travelers regularly face three pain points in Korea:

  • Limits of Google & TripAdvisor — Korea's locally popular spots live on Naver and Instagram, but foreign-language info is scarce.
  • Each area has a different vibe — Hongdae (MZ), Gangnam (luxury), Jongno (tradition), Itaewon (global). One-size-fits-all recommendations satisfy no one.
  • Missing practical details — hours, payment methods, foreigner-friendliness are what matter, yet guidebooks leave them out.

KeyChinGu solves all three with a full 4-language set + area-by-area curation + explicit foreigner-friendliness.

Who is this content for?

  • FIT (independent travelers) — travelers who enjoy Seoul at their own pace instead of package tours.
  • The MZ generation (20s–30s) — a trend-sensitive group finding hotspots via Instagram and Twitter.
  • Repeat visitors — 2nd or 3rd time in Seoul, wanting deep-dive content beyond Myeongdong and Gyeongbokgung.
  • Long-term residents — students, digital nomads, exchange students who need everyday life + weekend culture content.
