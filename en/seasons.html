🍂 Korea by Season

Korea's seasonal windows are short — two weeks of cherry blossoms, three weeks of silver grass, two months of autumn delicacies. This hub shows what's in season now and what's coming next, so you can read ahead and arrive ready.

🧳 How to prepare — every seasonal guide includes a pre-trip checklist and a timing calendar. For season-limited spots and foods, which week you arrive decides what you get.
