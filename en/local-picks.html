🍚 Locals' Picks

현지인 맛집 — Where Korean Viewers Sent Us

Korean viewers wrote their own neighborhood eateries into the comments of a nationwide food video. We collected them by region. Most are old-school places and small-town canteens that never make a tourist list, and all KeyChinGu did was check on Naver that the name, address and category are real.

📋 Source and limits — the starting point is a table of 217 places compiled from the best comments on one video; 192 of them checked out on Naver local search. There are no ratings, rankings or stars here. Opening hours, closing days and prices were not collected, so check Naver Place on the day you go.

🚄 Every guide says how to reach each town from its station or bus terminal and adds one traveler tip per city (minimum order, cash, running out of ingredients). Where we could not confirm the last stretch, it says "check" instead of a guess.

🏙 The Seoul, Gyeonggi and Incheon guides stay outside the tourist circuit, in the neighborhoods people actually live in. That region is already dense with area guides and Food Deep Dives, so wherever the same restaurant also appears in another guide, the verification notes say so.
