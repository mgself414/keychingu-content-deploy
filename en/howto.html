🧭 First Time in Korea

The No-Panic Manual — 처음 한국 · 당황 제로 가이드
初到韩国 · 零慌乱指南

You don't need to speak Korean. Kiosks, buses, delivery apps — we walk you through every machine and screen you'll face in Korea, showing exactly what appears and which button to press. A simulation series to read in bed before your flight.

🧭 Each guide covers one situation from start to finish, procedure by procedure — buttons as [ Takeout (포장) ], screen text as 「Select order type」, physical actions in bold. Not a list of recommendations — an operating manual. Other KeyChinGu guides tell you where to go; this series shows you how to do things.
