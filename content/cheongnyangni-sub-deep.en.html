Cheongnyangni Market Guide — Cheongnyangni Fruit and Vegetable Market with 170-plus Shops, Cheongnyangni Fish Market, Gyeongdong Market, Seoul Yangnyeongsi Market, Hongneung Arboretum

Subtitle: Cheongnyangni Fruit and Vegetable Market (청량리청과물시장), 1958, varies by shop, cards accepted · Cheongnyangni Fish Market (청량리수산시장), about 200m from Cheongnyangni Station Exit 6 · Gyeongdong Market (경동시장), 08:00–18:00, closed on alternate Sundays, varies by shop · Seoul Yangnyeongsi Market (서울 약령시장) · Hongneung Arboretum (홍릉시험림), closed Mondays, reservations required on weekdays — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Several markets are clustered near Cheongnyangni Station. There is Cheongnyangni Fruit and Vegetable Market, which sells fruit, Cheongnyangni Fish Market, which sells fish, Gyeongdong Market, known for ginseng and medicinal herbs, and Seoul Yangnyeongsi Market, a market for herbal medicine ingredients. In Cheongnyangni-dong (청량리동), also in Dongdaemun-gu, there is a forest, Hongneung Arboretum.

This guide covers only five places, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Walking Yangnyeongsi and Gyeongdong Market is covered separately in #598 Seoul Yangnyeongsi and Gyeongdong Market Guide, and herbal medicine experiences in #681 Herbal Tea and Yangnyeongsi Guide.

🍎 Cheongnyangni Fruit and Vegetable Market — The Fruit Market That Moves at Night

Cheongnyangni Fruit and Vegetable Market (4 Wangsan-ro 33-gil, Dongdaemun-gu, Seoul (서울 동대문구 왕산로33길 4), Jegi-dong, ☎ 02-962-1650) is a wholesale market specializing in fruit and vegetables, in business since 1958. It has 170-plus shops. Farm products arriving from Gyeonggi-do and Gangwon-do gather here and are then sold on across the country.

  • Opening hours: Registered as 00:00–24:00, and they differ by shop. As a wholesale market, it is busy from midnight until dawn.
  • Closed days: They differ by shop.
  • What is sold: Mainly fruit, and many shops also sell vegetables, seafood, and food.
  • Cards: Accepted. Parking is available, and there are restrooms.

🐟 Cheongnyangni Fish Market — The Biggest Market in the Cheongnyangni Area

Cheongnyangni Fish Market (52-11 Gosanja-ro 34-gil, Dongdaemun-gu (동대문구 고산자로34길 52-11), ☎ 02-967-4210) is a seafood market that is mainly wholesale but also does retail. According to the tourism organization's description, it is the largest in scale and range of items among the markets in the Cheongnyangni area.

  • Location: About 200m from Cheongnyangni Station Exit 6 toward Sinseol-dong.
  • Opening hours and closed days: They differ by shop.
  • What is sold: Yellow croaker, squid, shrimp, and more.
  • Facilities: There are restrooms, and no parking.

🌱 Gyeongdong Market — Ginseng and Medicinal Herbs

Gyeongdong Market (3 Gosanja-ro 36-gil, Dongdaemun-gu (동대문구 고산자로36길 3), Jegi-dong, ☎ 0507-1311-8721) is a farm products market that opened in June 1960. After Gyeongdong Building was completed in 1985, the country's largest ginseng market and a shopping arcade specializing in herbal medicine moved in.

  • Opening hours: 08:00–18:00, varying by shop.
  • Closed days: Alternate Sundays, varying by shop.
  • What is sold: Farm products, fruit, medicinal herbs, ginseng, and seafood.
  • Facilities: There are restrooms, and no parking.

🌿 Seoul Yangnyeongsi Market — The Herbal Medicine Market

Seoul Yangnyeongsi Market (10 Yangnyeong Jungang-ro, Dongdaemun-gu (동대문구 약령중앙로 10), ☎ 02-969-4793) is a market that sells herbal medicine ingredients wholesale and retail. Yangnyeongsi began as a gathering place for medicinal herb dealers, created in the reign of Hyojong of Joseon to collect rare medicinal ingredients. Today's Seoul Yangnyeongsi came about in the mid-1960s when herbal medicine merchants gathered around Cheongnyangni Station and the Majang-dong intercity bus terminal, and it grew in the 1970s when medicinal herb dealers from Jongno 4-ga and 5-ga moved in. According to the tourism organization's description, it is the largest in scale and history among the nation's yangnyeongsi.

  • Opening hours and closed days: They differ by shop.
  • What is sold: Herbal medicine ingredients and medicinal herbs.
  • Parking: Not available.

🌳 Hongneung Arboretum — A Forest in the Same Dongdaemun-gu

Korea Forest Research Institute Hongneung Arboretum (57 Hoegi-ro, Dongdaemun-gu (동대문구 회기로 57), Cheongnyangni-dong, ☎ Research Planning Division 02-961-2584) is Korea's first arboretum, created in 1922 when a forestry experiment station was established. It was named "Hongneung" because it stands on the site of Empress Myeongseong's tomb. Its area is about 41.8ha, and it has a conifer garden, a broadleaf tree garden, a herbaceous plant garden, and a shrub garden.

  • Opening hours: Weekdays 10:00–17:00, weekends 09:00–17:00. Last entry is 16:30.
  • Closed days: Every Monday, May 1, January 1, and the Seollal and Chuseok holidays.
  • Weekdays: Entry is only through the forest interpretation program. Only online reservations are accepted, and if fewer than 4 people reserve, it may be canceled.
  • Weekends: You enter without a reservation.
  • Parking: Not available.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Seeing the fruit and vegetable market only in the daytime — As a wholesale market, it is busy from midnight until dawn.
  2. Going to Hongneung Arboretum on a weekday without a reservation — On weekdays, you enter only through a forest interpretation program reserved online. On weekends, you enter without a reservation.
  3. Bringing a car — There is no parking at Cheongnyangni Fish Market, Gyeongdong Market, Seoul Yangnyeongsi Market, or Hongneung Arboretum.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. When is a good time to go?

A. The hours differ by shop at the four markets, and the fruit and vegetable market is busy from midnight until dawn. Hongneung Arboretum is closed on Mondays and requires a reservation on weekdays, so to go without a reservation, combine it with a weekend.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. According to the tourism organization, cards are accepted at Cheongnyangni Fruit and Vegetable Market. Card information is not registered for the other markets.
