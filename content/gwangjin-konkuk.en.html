🎯 How to Use This Guide

The old "12 picks" had no basis for any of the prices or hours of its 12 restaurants and cafes, and even the places with official data were wrong: Children's Grand Park Station was put on Line 5 (it is Line 7), the park was called "560,000 pyeong" (it is 536,094.5 ㎡, about 160,000 pyeong), the zoo was listed as 10–18 (it is 10–17), the zoo, amusement rides and botanical garden were called "all free" (only entry is free), Ttukseom was described as "5 minutes from Ttukseom Resort Station" (it is Jayang Station Exit 2, 438 m), and Achasan was tied to the Hanyang Doseong (한양도성) (its fortresses are Goguryeo (고구려) forts). The "Park Chung-hee order," the "founded 1946, 6,000+ international students" claim and the yacht/SUP prices also have no source. This time we list only values read on 2026-10-08 from VisitSeoul, the Seoul Facilities Corporation, the Han River Project Headquarters and each official site. For food we point only to Konkuk Food Street (건대 맛의거리) and Yangkkochi Street (양꼬치거리) (secondary sources); choose individual shops by review counts in a map app.

🌳 Seoul Forest and Ttukseom — A Half Day by the Han River

  • Seoul Forest (서울숲): Free 24 hours. The ecological forest is 05:30–21:30; the insect garden and butterfly garden are closed Mondays, and the butterfly garden runs only May–Oct (VisitSeoul). Seoul Forest Station (서울숲역) Exits 3/4 (242 m) are closest, and Ttukseom Station (뚝섬역) Exit 8 is 537 m.
  • Ttukseom Hangang Park (뚝섬한강공원): Free 24 hours. Instead of the old "5 minutes on foot from Ttukseom Resort Station," the official access is Jayang Station (자양역) Exit 2, 438 m (VisitSeoul), and Jabeolle is at Exit 3 (Han River Project Headquarters). Jabeolle (자벌레) (Hangang Peulpeul (한강 플플)): Tue–Thu 10–19, Fri–Sat 10–22 (extended from Aug 1), Sun/holidays 10–20, closed Mondays, Jan 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok, free (2nd-floor kids cafe 5,000). Prices for yachts, SUP, barbecue and Ddareungi bikes are not on the official page, so they are not listed.

🎡 Children's Grand Park — Free, and the Zoo Closes at 17:00

  • Park 05–22, zoo 10–17, open year-round, entry free (some facilities paid — "all free" was wrong). Opened 1973-05-05, 536,094.5 ㎡ (Seoul Facilities Corporation). The station is Line 7 Children's Grand Park Station (어린이대공원역) (the exit number is not on the official page), or Line 5 Achasan Station.

🛍 Konkuk — Common Ground, Lotte and Food Street

  • Common Ground (커먼그라운드) (200 Achasan-ro): 11–22; as of 2026-10-08 the official site shows it open, with no closure or construction notice. Closed days and exits are not posted.
  • Lotte Department Store Konkuk Star City (롯데백화점 건대스타시티점): 10:30–20, restaurant floor until 21:00, closed Oct 12 (Mon) (official). Line 2 Konkuk University Station Exit 5, Line 7 Exit 4.
  • Konkuk Food Street (건대 맛의거리) and Yangkkochi Street (양꼬치거리): Line 2 Konkuk University Station Exit 1; hours vary by shop — both are secondary sources. Shop names and prices are not listed.

⛰ Achasan — A 1 km Walk to the Trailhead

  • Free 24 hours (VisitSeoul). It is 1 km from Achasan Station Exit 2 and 1.1 km from Gwangnaru Station Exit 1 — farther than the old "10 minutes on foot from Line 5." The fortresses along the ridge are Goguryeo sites (not Hanyang Doseong).

🗺 Two Routes

  • A, a park day: Seoul Forest (morning) from Seoul Forest Station → Ttukseom Hangang Park and Jabeolle from Jayang Station (skip on Mondays) → dinner at Konkuk Food Street near Konkuk University Station (choose shops in a map app).
  • B, family: Children's Grand Park (before the zoo closes at 17:00) → Achasan trailhead, 1 km from Achasan Station → Common Ground in Konkuk (open until 22:00).

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Putting Children's Grand Park on Line 5 — it is Line 7 Children's Grand Park Station (Line 5 is Achasan Station).

2. Zoo until 18:00 — it is 17:00.

3. Visiting Jabeolle or the Seoul Forest insect garden on a Monday — closed.

4. Getting lost around Ttukseom Resort Station — the official access is Jayang Station Exits 2 and 3.

5. Old 12-restaurant prices — none of them had a source.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Is everything free? — Seoul Forest, Ttukseom, Jabeolle, Children's Grand Park entry and Achasan are free. Some Children's Grand Park facilities are paid.

2. Where do I get off for Ttukseom? — Line 7 Jayang Station Exit 2 (438 m); Jabeolle is Exit 3.

3. What about the Children's Grand Park zoo? — 10–17, open year-round.

4. Is Common Ground still open? — Yes, it is open (official, 11–22).

5. Where do I eat? — Konkuk Food Street and Yangkkochi Street (secondary). Choose shops in a map app.

6. How many hours is Achasan? — It is 1 km to the trailhead. Hiking times are not posted officially.
