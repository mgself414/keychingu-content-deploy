🎯 Why an exclusive guide to Gwangjin, Konkuk University, and Ttukseom?

⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Konk University = I passed because it's a university neighborhood" — No. 1 foreign student value unknown

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. Konkuk University is recognized as a “university town” and is passed over, but in reality, 6,000 foreign students at Konkuk University + (the number one private university for foreigners from China, Southeast Asia, and Russia) + international student belt at Sejong University and Hanyang University = the area with the most diverse demographics of foreigners in Korea. Konk University Western/Italian 12 Cluster is foreigner-friendly + standard English menu. The #1 deep dive for foreigners studying abroad in Korea.

2. "Ttukseom Han River = Just the Han River" — Bicycle, yacht, barbecue unknown

Ttukseom Hangang Park is not just the Han River, but Hangang Bicycle Road No. 1 + Han River Yacht/SUP Rental + Han River Barbecue (designated area) + Han River Night View all in one place. Different from Yeouido Hangang Park (fountain, cherry blossoms) — Activities, MZ, and family No. 1. 5-minute walk from Ttukseom Garden Station on Line 7.

3. "Children's Grand Park = Just for kids" — Korea's No. 1 free zoo, Miji**

Children's Grand Park is Korea's first urban zoo, amusement park, and botanical garden, opened in 1973 under the direction of President Park Chung-hee — 560,000 pyeong. Free for foreign families + Free zoo + Free botanical garden. Korea's first urban comprehensive park. Families with foreign children ranked first. Children’s Grand Park Station, Line 5.

Scenery of Gwangjin, Konkuk University, and Ttukseom

Gwangjin, Konkuk University, and Ttukseom are located in the center of Gwangjin-gu area combining foreign students + youth + the Han River — with the opening of Konkuk University in 1946 + the opening of Children's Grand Park in 1973 + the creation of Ttukseom Hangang Park, it has established itself as the No. 1 hot zone in eastern Korea.

Konkuk University/Konkuk University Station Sub-area is near Konkuk University Station on Lines 2 and 7 — Konkuk University campus + 6,000 foreign students + + 50+ foreign-friendly Western, Italian, and Japanese restaurants. The #1 deep dive for foreigners studying abroad in Korea.

Ttukseom·Guui·Jayang sub-area is the Ttukseom Garden area, Guui Station, and Jayang Station on Line 7 — Ttukseom Hangang Park + East Seoul General Terminal (local departure hub) + Han River bicycles and yachts.

Children's Grand Park sub-area is directly connected to Children's Grand Park Station on Line 5 — Free admission to Korea's No. 1 urban zoo, amusement park, and botanical garden.

Achasan sub-area is a 10-minute walk from Gwangnaru Station and Achasan Station on Line 5 — Hanyangdoseong, Goguryeo fortress ruins, and Han River view are the top hiking destinations.

Transportation: Line 2, Line 7 Konkuk University Station (Kon National University) / Line 7 Ttukseom Park Area (Ttukseom Hangang Park) / Line 5 Children's Grand Park Station / Line 7 Guui Station·Jayang Station. Kakao Taxi recommended. Bicycle Ttareungi OK.

⚙ FAQ (8)

Q1. Foreigners admitted to Konkuk University campus?

Free entry OK — 5-minute walk from exit 2 of the main gate (Konkuk University Station). Ilgam Lake (campus central pond) + KU Zoo + KU Museum/Art Gallery Free. 6,000 foreign students at Konkuk University+ = Frequent meeting of foreigners on campus.

Q2. How do I rent a bicycle or yacht at Ttukseom Hangang Park?

Bike Ttareungi (Seoul City public bicycle, 1,000 won per hour) + Hangang Yacht/SUP rental (within Ttukseom Hangang Park, 09:00~18:00, 30,000~50,000 won/hour) + Hangang Bicycle Path (Ttukseom → Jamsil → Gwangnaru). Foreign credit card registration OK.

Q3. Children’s Grand Park entrance fee?

Free — Free for both foreigners and Koreans. Line 5, Children’s Grand Park Station, Exit 1. Free admission to zoos, amusement parks, and botanical gardens. No. 1 priority for traveling with foreign children.

Q4. What are Konkuk University’s foreign-friendly stores?

Macho Chef, Sato Gyu Katsu, New Wave Seoul, Yobi, and Minji Super have English menu + card payment + foreigner-friendly standard. Konkuk University's foreign student demographics are diverse, so some menus are available in Japanese and Chinese.

Q5. Is Ttukseom Han River barbecue available?

Available — Available in designated barbecue area in Ttukseom Hangang Park. Advance reservation required (Seoul City Hangang Park website English UI OK). Zone 1, approximately 30,000 won/4 people. 1st season from May to October.

Q6. Is it possible for foreigners to climb Mt. Acha?

OK — 287m light trek (1-2 hours). OK for foreign families and beginners in mountaineering. Top Han River view + #1 photo of Goguryeo fortress ruins. Line 5 Gwangnaru Station/Achasan Station.

Q7. Where to go from Dong Seoul Terminal?

Gangwon-do (Chuncheon, Gangneung, Sokcho) / Chungbuk (Cheongju, Chungju, Danyang) / Gyeongbuk (parts of Andong, Gyeongju) / Jeonbuk (Jeonju, Gunsan, Iksan). The number one starting point for foreigners traveling around Korea. English ticket OK + KOBUS English UI.

Q8. Gwangjin, Konkuk University + Han River + Gangnam route?

OK — Konkuk University Station (Lines 2 and 7) → Gangnam (Transfer to Line 2, 30 minutes) / Jamsil (Line 2, 4 stops) / Apgujeong (Lines 2 and 3) / Ttukseom Hangang → Hangang Bicycle (10km to Jamsil). 1 full day.
