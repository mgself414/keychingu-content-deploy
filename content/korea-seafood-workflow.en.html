Korean Seafood Ordering, Cooking & Pairing Workflow — From Tongyeong Oysters to Mapo Ganjang-gejang

Subtitle: A 5-step workflow for foreign travelers + 4 cooking methods + pairing, timing, and English phrases

ISSUE 03 · #874 · 2026-06-09

🎯 Why You Need a Korean Seafood Workflow Guide

Knowing the comprehensive guide to Korea's 6 seafood types isn't the same as knowing how to order, cook, and pair them. Korean seafood culture breaks down into (a) authentic markets (Tongyeong (통영), Jagalchi (자갈치), Sorae Port (소래포구), Mokpo (목포) — live tanks on the ground floor, a restaurant upstairs) (b) raw fish restaurants and specialty shops (Seoul's Gangnam (강남), Hongdae (홍대)) (c) ganjang-gejang (간장게장, soy sauce marinated crab) specialty restaurants (Mapo (마포), Gangnam) (d) MZ-generation-favorite soy sauce shrimp and blue crab stew spots. There are 5 core cooking methods: raw (회, hoe), steamed (찜, jjim), grilled (구이, gui), soup/stew (국·탕, guk-tang), and soy sauce marinated (간장 양념, jang). Foreign travelers commonly don't know that (1) live octopus (산낙지, san-nakji) should be chewed slowly (2) oysters (굴, gul) can be eaten raw or cooked (3) ganjang-gejang is eaten by cracking the shell with your hands (4) blue crab stew (꽃게탕, kkotge-tang) is a true fall specialty. This guide covers the 5-step workflow, cooking methods, and sample English phrases.

★ The 5-Step Workflow at a Glance

Step Action Time Key Points for Travelers
1️⃣ Choose a channel Market / raw fish restaurant / ganjang-gejang specialty shop / MZ trend spot Instant Choose based on value, authenticity, enthusiast appeal, or trendiness
2️⃣ Order the menu By type·kg·"gwan" (관, a traditional weight unit) / per person / per piece 1〜5 min kg pricing = markets
3️⃣ Receive & prep 5 cooking methods: raw, steamed, grilled, soup, soy sauce marinated 10〜30 min Live fish, raw oysters, or cooked
4️⃣ Eat & pair Vinegar chili sauce (chojang, 초장), soy sauce, soju, beer 30〜90 min Chew live octopus slowly
5️⃣ Pay Card accepted / markets = some cash only / no tipping 1〜5 min Splitting the bill (1/N) is fine

Total time needed: Market = 1〜2 hrs / Raw fish restaurant = 1.5〜2.5 hrs / Ganjang-gejang = 1〜2 hrs / MZ trend spot = 1〜1.5 hrs

🦐 Workflow by Channel

Type A — Markets (Tongyeong, Jagalchi, Sorae Port, Mokpo)

  • Highlight: Live seafood butchered on the spot, with tanks on the 1st floor and a restaurant on the 2nd
  • Hours: Varies by market (06:00〜22:00, AM6:00〜PM10:00)
  • Price: Priced by kg, plus a table/prep fee (sangchimbi, 차림비) of ₩5,000〜15,000 (~$4〜11 / ~28〜85 RMB / ~550〜1,650 JPY)
  • Menu: Live fish, raw oysters, live octopus, blue crab, king prawn (daeha, 대하)
  • Foreign-friendliness: ★★★★ (English available)

Type B — Raw Fish Restaurants & Specialty Shops (Gangnam, Hongdae, Itaewon (이태원))

  • Highlight: A full authentic spread, mid-to-high tier
  • Hours: 17:00〜24:00 (PM5:00〜PM12:00)
  • Price: ₩50,000〜150,000 per person
  • Menu: Raw fish + spicy fish stew (maeuntang, 매운탕) + soy sauce shrimp + live octopus
  • Foreign-friendliness: ★★★★ (English available)

Type C — Ganjang-gejang Specialty Restaurants (Mapo, Gangnam)

  • Highlight: The #1 spot for ganjang-gejang — MZ-generation Instagram favorite, authentic
  • Hours: 11:30〜22:00 (AM11:30〜PM10:00)
  • Price: ₩30,000〜80,000 per person (one whole marinated crab)
  • Menu: Ganjang-gejang, blue crab stew, crab rice bowl (gejang-bibimbap, 게장비빔밥)
  • Foreign-friendliness: ★★★★★ (a global enthusiast favorite)
  • Recommended: Mapo's crab-marinade alley (gejang golmok, 게장 골목), authentic spots in Gangnam

Type D — MZ-Trend Soy Sauce Shrimp & Blue Crab Stew (Hongdae, Gangnam, Seongsu (성수))

  • Highlight: MZ-generation trend — Instagram-famous soy sauce shrimp, soy sauce seasoning
  • Hours: 11:30〜24:00
  • Price: ₩25,000〜50,000 per person
  • Menu: Soy sauce shrimp (saewoo-jang, 새우장), mini soy sauce crab, MZ-style twists
  • Foreign-friendliness: ★★★★★ (the #1 pick among MZ-generation diners)

🌶 Sample English Phrases for Travelers

  • "Live oysters" = "Live oysters, please (raw oysters/saeng-gul, 생굴)"
  • "Chew the live octopus slowly" = "Live small octopus — chew slowly! (watch for choking risk)"
  • "One serving of ganjang-gejang" = "1 person serving of soy sauce crab"
  • "Spicy blue crab stew" = "Spicy blue crab stew, please"
  • "Soy sauce shrimp" = "Shrimp in soy sauce (an MZ-generation trend)"
  • "Do you take cards?" = "Card accepted?"

⚠ 7 Common Mistakes Foreign Travelers Make

1. "Oysters are a year-round dish": Oysters are best in winter, December〜March #1; raw oysters in spring/summer carry a food-poisoning risk.

2. "Live octopus is eaten like regular sashimi": Live octopus should be eaten alive, cut into small pieces, and chewed slowly (there's a real choking risk).

3. "Blue crab is available year-round": Blue crab is best in fall, September〜November #1 (the true season for ganjang-gejang).

4. "Ganjang-gejang is eaten with chopsticks": Ganjang-gejang is eaten by cracking the shell open with your hands — mixing the leftover marinade into rice (gejang-bibimbap) is also perfectly normal.

5. "Soy sauce shrimp is garlic-seasoned": Soy sauce shrimp is soy-sauce-based (garlic-seasoned shrimp is a different dish).

6. "Tongyeong oysters are nothing special": Tongyeong oysters are the #1 authentic pick from the South Sea (raw, in season December〜March).

7. "Blue crab stew is always spicy": Blue crab stew comes in both spicy and mild versions — either is fine.

⚙ 8 Frequently Asked Questions

1. What's the best first seafood experience?: A ganjang-gejang specialty restaurant (the #1 enthusiast favorite) → then Tongyeong oysters in winter.

2. What's Instagram/vlog-worthy?: Mapo ganjang-gejang, Tongyeong oysters, West Sea blue crab, Mokpo live octopus.

3. What's trending with the MZ generation?: Soy sauce shrimp, MZ-style blue crab stew, mini ganjang-gejang.

4. Where do ganjang-gejang enthusiasts go?: Mapo's crab-marinade alley, authentic spots in Gangnam.

5. Is it safe during pregnancy?: Raw oysters and live octopus are off-limits; cooked seafood is fine.

6. What about allergies?: Shellfish and mollusk allergies should be disclosed in advance.

7. Payment and tipping?: Cards are accepted / markets are partly cash-only / no tipping.

8. Can I get a VAT refund?: Food and drink are generally not eligible for VAT refunds.

⚠ Medical, Safety & Dietary Disclaimer

Seafood carries a food-poisoning risk (especially raw oysters in spring/summer), and live octopus carries a choking risk. Pregnant travelers should avoid raw oysters and live octopus; cooked seafood is fine. Children should be at least 5 years old and stick to cooked seafood. Disclose allergies (shellfish, mollusks, gluten in soy sauce) in advance. If you suspect food poisoning, call ☎ 1339 (medical hotline, English available). This guide is general information, not medical advice.

Dietary restriction fallback:

  • Halal/Kosher: Shellfish and mollusks are partially halal-acceptable (opinions vary by authority) / not kosher.
  • Vegetarian/Vegan: None of the 6 seafood types are vegetarian / vegetarian options include bibimbap and temple food.
  • Gluten-free: Raw and grilled dishes are fine / soy-sauce-marinated dishes contain gluten (tamari can be substituted).
