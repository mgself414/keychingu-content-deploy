🏛 5 Pillars Deep Dive

① Namgang (남강) Lanterns (Main · Enthusiast #1)

  • Signature: Namgang (남강) + Lantern Matrix #1 · 2.8M+ annual enthusiasts · 19:00-22:00 (7 PM - 10 PM) Night View Matrix
  • Foreigner-Friendly Rating: ★★★★★ (Insta · MZ + Family Enthusiast Matrix #1)
  • Hours: 09:00-22:00 (9 AM - 10 PM) · Free · Partially Paid (Boat ₩10,000-₩20,000 (~$7.5-$15 / ~55-110 RMB / ~1,100-2,200 JPY))
  • Recommendation: Night View + Lantern Boat + Insta Matrix #1

② Chokseokru (촉석루) + Jinjuseong (진주성) ([#1055 cross](1055_jinju_chokseokru.md))

  • Signature: Yeongnam (영남, southeastern region of Korea) #1 Pavilion · Imjin War (임진왜란) 1592 Battle of Jinju (진주대첩, a major battle during the Imjin War) Matrix · Uigi Nongae (의기 논개, a patriotic gisaeng who sacrificed herself during the Imjin War) Enthusiast
  • Foreigner-Friendly Rating: ★★★★ (English Signage · History Enthusiast)
  • Hours: 09:00-18:00 (9 AM - 6 PM) · Chokseokru (촉석루) ₩2,000 (~$1.5 / ~11 RMB / ~220 JPY) · Jinjuseong (진주성) + Museum ₩5,000 (~$3.75 / ~27 RMB / ~550 JPY)

③ Jinju Naengmyeon (진주냉면) + Heotjesabap (헛제사밥)

  • Signature: Korea's #1 Jinju Naengmyeon (진주냉면, cold Korean noodles) Signature · Heotjesabap (헛제사밥, a bibimbap-like dish traditionally made with ingredients used for ancestral rites) Jinju (진주) Signature Matrix
  • Foreigner-Friendly Rating: ★★★★ (Not spicy · Foreigner-friendly introduction)
  • Price: Jinju Naengmyeon (진주냉면) = ₩12,000-₩18,000 (~$9-$13.5 / ~66-99 RMB / ~1,300-2,000 JPY) · Heotjesabap (헛제사밥) = ₩15,000-₩25,000 (~$11-$18.5 / ~82-137 RMB / ~1,650-2,750 JPY)
