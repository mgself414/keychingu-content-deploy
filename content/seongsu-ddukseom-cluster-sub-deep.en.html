🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to visit around Ttukseom Hangang Park in Gwangjin-gu and Ungbongsan in Seongdong-gu, Seoul. They are Ttukseom Hangang Park, where the activities change with the seasons, Ungbongsan and its artificial climbing wall for sunsets and night views, Salgoji Bridge, a stone bridge from the early Joseon period, and a lamb skewer street near Konkuk University Station and a food alley near Wangsimni Station. Seoul Forest (서울숲) and Seongsu-dong (성수동) are in the #701 Seoul Forest and Seongsu Guide.

The facilities that are open depend on the season. The windsurfing area is registered as May~October, and the sledding hill as late December~mid-February.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Ttukseom Hangang Park — Park, Byeokcheon Madang, Windsurfing Area, Swimming Pool and Sledding Hill

Ttukseom Hangang Park (뚝섬한강공원) [Gangbyeonbuk-ro 2273 (강변북로 2273), Gwangjin-gu (광진구), ☎ 02-3780-0521~4] was known as a riverside amusement park (강변유원지) even before it became a Hangang park. It has a waterside plaza, a rose garden, a nature study area, a children's playground, an X Games area, an artificial climbing wall and a pleasure boat dock. It is described as a place for water sports such as windsurfing, water skiing and motorboating in summer, and as a sledding hill and skating rink in winter. There is a 243m-long exhibition space shaped like an inchworm, and it connects to Ttukseom Resort Station (뚝섬유원지역) on the subway.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.
  • Parking: Available (674 spaces). 1,000 won for the first 30 minutes, then 200 won every 10 minutes.

Ttukseom Hangang Park Byeokcheon Madang (뚝섬한강공원 벽천마당) [Jayang-dong (자양동), Gwangjin-gu (광진구), ☎ Ttukseom Information Center (뚝섬안내센터) 02-3780-0521~4] has a fountain that rises from the ground and rock climbing facilities. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is one of the 3 great Hangang fountain parks in Seoul, along with the Water Fountain at Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도한강공원) and the Mirror Fountain at Nanji Hangang Park (난지한강공원). At night you can see an illuminated fountain.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.
  • Parking: 1,000 won for the first 30 minutes, then 200 won every 10 minutes, with a daily maximum of 10,000 won.

Ttukseom Windsurfing Area (뚝섬 윈드서핑장) [Gangbyeonbuk-ro 2326 (강변북로 2326), Gwangjin-gu (광진구), ☎ Ttukseom Information Center (뚝섬안내센터) 02-3780-0521~4] is a water leisure facility next to the tennis courts in Ttukseom Hangang Park. You can do windsurfing, paddleboarding, kayaking, paddle yoga and banana boating, and several private operators give lessons and rent equipment.

  • Operating period: May~October. Opening hours: Vary by operator.

Ttukseom Outdoor Swimming Pool (뚝섬수영장) [Jayang-dong 112 (자양동 112), Gwangjin-gu (광진구)] is an outdoor pool inside Ttukseom Hangang Park that can hold about 3,500 people. It has an adult pool, a children's pool, an infant pool and a lazy river, plus a 4m-high Aqua Ring and a sunbathing area. There are no registered values for the operating period and fees.

  • Discounts: 50% off for people aged 65 and over, people with disabilities, persons of national merit and others.

Hangang Park Ttukseom Sledding Hill (한강공원 뚝섬 눈썰매장) [Gangbyeonbuk-ro 139 (강변북로 139), Gwangjin-gu (광진구), ☎ 02-452-5955] is divided into a large slope and a small slope. It has a snow play area, rides and an ice smelt fishing experience area. Tickets can be bought on site or reserved online. The small slope is mainly for children, and guardians must also go up and ride down on a tube. There are men's and women's changing rooms and paid lockers.

  • Opening hours: Late December~mid-February 10:00~17:00. It is registered that the period may change.

📍 Ungbongsan — Ungbongsan and Rock Climbing Park

Ungbongsan (응봉산) [Ungbong-dong (응봉동), Seongdong-gu (성동구), ☎ 02-2286-6061] is a mountain about 95m high. In the Joseon period it was where the king released hawks to hunt, so it was also called Maebongsan (매봉산). To the south there used to be Dongbinggo (동빙고), an ice storehouse. Today it is a neighborhood park, and people come to see Seoul's sunsets and night views. Inside there are a children's park, a rock climbing park and a badminton court.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Ungbongsan Rock Climbing Park (응봉산 암벽등반공원) [Dokseodang-ro 60-gil 13-1 (독서당로60길 13-1), Seongdong-gu (성동구), ☎ Ungbongsan Management Office (응봉산 관리사무소) 02-2286-6061] is on the way up Ungbongsan. An artificial wall was built where stone had been quarried and abandoned, and it has a bouldering area and a tall vertical wall. There are changing rooms and a plaza. You can use it after reserving through the Seoul public reservation system. It is free to use, but climbing equipment is neither rented nor sold, so you must bring the equipment you need.

  • Opening hours: Weekdays 10:00~21:00, weekends and holidays 10:00~18:00. Closed days: Every Monday.

📍 Jungnangcheon — Seoul Salgoji Bridge and Salgoji Sports Park

Seoul Salgoji Bridge (서울 살곶이 다리) [Seongsu-dong (성수동), Seongdong-gu (성동구), ☎ Culture and Sports Division of Seongdong-gu Office (성동구청 문화체육과) 02-2286-5193] is a stone bridge whose construction began in 1420 and stopped, was resumed in 1475, and was completed in 1483. It was also called "Jebangyo (제반교, 濟盤橋)" because crossing it felt like walking on level ground. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the largest bridge made in the early Joseon period, and it used 64 stone pillars. The pillars were made diamond-shaped to reduce water resistance. Part of it was washed away by heavy rain in the 1920s and was repaired in the 1970s, but concrete was attached on the right side, so it lost some of its original appearance.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Not available.

Salgoji Sports Park (살곶이체육공원) [Jungnangcheon Seojajeongeo-gil 209 (중랑천서자전거길 209), Seongdong-gu (성동구), ☎ 02-2286-6090] is near where Cheonggyecheon (청계천) meets Jungnangcheon (중랑천). It has an in-line skating rink, a soccer field, a basketball court and a badminton court, and the bicycle path and the pedestrian path are separated. It is described as running a free water play area in summer.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: 50 won every 5 minutes.

📍 Food Streets — Jayang-dong Lamb Skewer Street and Wangsimni Food Alley

Jayang-dong Lamb Skewer Street (자양동 양꼬치거리) [Jayang-dong (자양동), Gwangjin-gu (광진구), ☎ Seongsu Tourist Information Center (성수관광안내소) 02-466-2286] is also called the "Chinese Food Culture Street (중국음식문화거리)". Leave Konkuk University Station (건대입구역) on Subway Lines 2 and 7 by Exit 5, pass Rodeo Street (로데오거리), and go down about 50m toward the Han River. Lamb skewer restaurants and fusion Chinese restaurants run by ethnic Koreans from China are gathered here, and there are more Chinese signs than Korean ones.

  • Opening hours·closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: 250 won every 5 minutes.

Wangsimni Food Alley (왕십리맛골목) [Mazo-ro 17 (마조로 17), Seongdong-gu (성동구)] is a food street near Wangsimni Station (왕십리역). Korean, Chinese and Japanese restaurants are gathered here. There are no registered values for opening hours and closed days.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The windsurfing area opens May~October and the sledding hill opens late December~mid-February — it is registered that the sledding hill period may change.
  2. At Ungbongsan Rock Climbing Park you must reserve and bring your own equipment — it is free to use and closed on Mondays.
  3. Parking is not available at Salgoji Bridge — Salgoji Sports Park charges 50 won every 5 minutes.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. What can I do at Ttukseom Hangang Park in winter?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, Ttukseom Sledding Hill (뚝섬 눈썰매장) opens late December~mid-February 10:00~17:00, with a snow play area and an ice smelt fishing experience. The description of Ttukseom Hangang Park also mentions a skating rink in winter.

Q. Where can I see night views?

A. Ungbongsan (응봉산) is described for its sunsets and night views and is always open. At Byeokcheon Madang (벽천마당) in Ttukseom Hangang Park, you can see an illuminated fountain at night.
