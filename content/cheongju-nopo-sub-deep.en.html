Cheongju Market Guide — Yukgeori Comprehensive Market, Seomun Market Samgyeopsal Street, Bukbu Market, Suamgol

Subtitle: Yukgeori Comprehensive Market (육거리종합시장), permanent market since 1950 · Seomun Market Samgyeopsal Street (서문시장 삼겹살거리), created 2012 · Bukbu Market (북부시장), 10:00–20:00, 127 stalls · Suamgol Mural Village and Cafe Street (수암골), always open — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Three markets are at the center of a market visit in Cheongju. Yukgeori Comprehensive Market carries on the Cheongju market-day tradition, Seomun Market has Cheongju-style samgyeopsal (pork belly) alley, and Bukbu Market is the oldest traditional market in Cheongju. Suamgol, with murals and view cafes, is also covered here.

This guide covers only the three markets and Suamgol, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). The food at Yukgeori Comprehensive Market is covered separately in #625 Cheongju Yukgeori Market.

🛒 Yukgeori Comprehensive Market — The Permanent Market Carrying On the Cheongju Market Day

Cheongju Yukgeori Comprehensive Market in Seokgyo-dong, Sangdang-gu, Cheongju (청주시 상당구 석교동), ☎ 043-222-6696, started in 1950 or later as a permanent market carrying on the Cheongju market day. The name comes from the road in front of the market, which was a six-way intersection (yukgeori, 육거리).

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by stall.
  • What they sell: Farm products, seafood, and sundries. Rice cake, jeon (pan-fried patty), and clothing shops are gathered across several sections.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available.

🥓 Seomun Market Samgyeopsal Street — Cheongju-Style Pork Belly

Seomun Market Samgyeopsal Street at 18 Musimdong-ro 392beon-gil, Sangdang-gu, Cheongju (청주시 상당구 무심동로392번길 18), Seomun-dong (서문동), ☎ 043-221-6633, is a pork belly alley that Seomun Market merchants opened inside the market in 2012. Seomun Market is west of the Seomun-dong five-way intersection, and around 1960 it was counted as the largest market in Cheongju. To the right of the intersection is a pig's trotters alley.

  • How it is grilled: In Cheongju, pork was traditionally soaked in simmered soy sauce (jirangmul, 지랑물) before grilling, or sprinkled with coarse salt and grilled. It comes with scallion salad or aged kimchi.
  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Parking is available.

🧱 Bukbu Market — Cheongju's Oldest Traditional Market

Cheongju Bukbu Market at 12-4 Hyanggun-ro 31beon-gil, Cheongwon-gu, Cheongju (청주시 청원구 향군로31번길 12-4), ☎ 043-224-8085, has the longest history among Cheongju's traditional markets. It consists of a residential-commercial building and alley markets, with 127 stalls and 25 street vendors.

  • Opening hours: 10:00–20:00, varying by stall.
  • What they sell: Clothing, shoes, groceries, rice cake, tofu, and daily necessities. Food such as pig's trotters and black goat is also available.
  • What to see: Young entrepreneur shops have moved in, and there are murals here and there in the alleys.
  • Parking: Market parking lot: 500 won for 30 minutes, 800 won for 60 minutes, 1,400 won for 120 minutes, 2,600 won for 240 minutes.
  • How to get there: By bus, get off at Uam Elementary School (우암초등학교) or in front of the KEPCO Chungbuk Branch (한국전력 충북지사).

🎨 Suamgol — Mural Village and View Cafe Street

Suamgol Mural Village at 13-6 Suam-ro 56beon-gil, Sangdang-gu, Cheongju (청주시 상당구 수암로56번길 13-6), Su-dong (수동), ☎ 043-253-1330, is a hillside village that formed when refugees settled here after the Korean War. Murals were painted in 2007 as a public art project, and visitors increased after it became known as a TV drama filming location.

  • Opening hours: Always open, year-round. The tourism organization data asks visitors to refrain from visiting after 21:00 because residents live here.
  • View: It sits on the slope of Uamsan (우암산), so you can look down over downtown Cheongju.
  • Suamgol Cafe Street at 26 Suam-ro 20beon-gil, Sangdang-gu, Cheongju (청주시 상당구 수암로20번길 26), ☎ Cheongju Traveler Center 070-4120-0411: A street of terrace-style cafes along the road at the top of the village, where you can see the night view after dark.
  • For Suamgol and Cheongnamdae (청남대), see #1042 Cheongju Suamgol + Cheongnamdae.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Treating Seomun Market and Yukgeori Comprehensive Market as the same place — They are separate markets. The samgyeopsal street is in Seomun Market.
  2. Going to Suamgol late at night — It is a residential area, and there is a notice asking visitors to refrain from visiting after 21:00.
  3. Assuming Bukbu Market opens early in the morning — According to the tourism organization, it is open 10:00–20:00, varying by stall.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where do I go to eat samgyeopsal in Cheongju?

A. Seomun Market Samgyeopsal Street. It is the Cheongju style, soaked in soy sauce before grilling or sprinkled with coarse salt and grilled.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. Whether cards are accepted is not registered in the tourism organization's data for the markets in this guide.
