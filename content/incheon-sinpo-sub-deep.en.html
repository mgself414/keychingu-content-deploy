Incheon Sinpo International Market and the Open Port District — From the Birthplace of Dakgangjeong to Chinatown and the Open Port Museum on Foot (Official Info as of 2026)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Sinpo International Market (신포국제시장) is a century-old permanent market in Incheon's old downtown (the official page calls the district Jemulpo-gu (제물포구), covering the former Jung-gu area), and Incheon Chinatown (인천 차이나타운), the open-port museums and Jayu Park (자유공원) are all within walking distance. The addresses, hours and fees below are only the values read on the official pages on 2026-10-07. Anything the official pages do not give, such as walking times from the stations, is left out. Three claims from the older guide did not match official sources and were dropped: "a premium vegetable market for foreign residents in the late 19th century," "an international market near the international ferry terminal," and "Jungang Market (food)."

🍗 Sinpo International Market — Where Dakgangjeong Began

  • Incheon's first modern permanent market, with about 100 years of history. It started as Sinpo Market and was renamed Sinpo International Market in 2010 (official). It has about 162 stalls.
  • It is the market where dakgangjeong (닭강정) was first developed (official). Gonggal-ppang (puffed hollow bread), jjolmyeon (spicy chewy noodles) and five-color dumplings are also presented together as "original snacks," but the official wording makes no standalone origin claim for jjolmyeon.
  • Open 10:00–21:00, closed the first Monday of each month and on the day of Seollal and Chuseok (varies by shop). Leaflets are available in English, Chinese and Japanese (official). Parking is paid.
  • Photo spots (official): Snowflake Village Youth Mall (눈꽃마을 청년몰) and Lighthouse Park (등대공원).
  • Prices are not on the official page, so none are given here.

🧵 "Jungang Market" Is a Wedding-Goods Street

  • The Jungang Market on the official page is the Jungang Market Traditional Wedding-Goods Street (중앙시장 전통혼수거리) (11 Jungang-ro, about 98 shops, open year-round). It specializes in hanbok, bedding and wedding goods and is not a food market. It is on the Dongincheon Station (동인천역) side. The older guide listing it as a food market was an error.

🏮 Chinatown, the Open Port District and Jayu Park — On Foot

  • Incheon Chinatown (인천 차이나타운): formed after the port opened in 1883 and the Chinese concession was established in 1884 (official). Known as the jajangmyeon street. Hours vary by shop.
  • Incheon Open Port Museum (인천개항박물관): 09:00–18:00, closed Mondays, Jan 1, Seollal and Chuseok; adult 500 won (official). It covers Incheon in the open-port era.
  • Daebul Hotel Exhibition Hall (대불호텔 전시관): an exhibition hall on the site of Korea's first Western-style hotel. 09:00–18:00, closed Mondays, adult 1,000 won (official).
  • Jayu Park (자유공원): designed by Sabatin in 1888 (official), open 24 hours, free. Harbor views.
  • Songwol-dong Fairy Tale Village (송월동 동화마을): a mural village, open 24 hours, free.

🚆 Wolmido — Wolmi Sea Train

  • A monorail that circles Wolmido (월미도). Closed Mondays, adult 11,000 won on weekdays and 14,000 won on weekends, 6.1 km in about 42 minutes (official). There is no foreign-language guidance (official), so bring a translation app.
  • The official pages do not give a transport mode or travel time from Sinpo to Wolmido, so none is given here (use a map app).

🚇 Getting There

  • Sinpo International Market (신포국제시장) sits in the old downtown between Dongincheon Station (동인천역) (Line 1) and Sinpo Station (신포역) (Suin-Bundang Line). The official pages do not say which exit is how many minutes away, so none is given. Search "Sinpo International Market" in a map app.
  • The market, Chinatown, Jayu Park and the open-port museums are all within walking distance of one another.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Visiting the market on the first Monday — it is closed. Both museums close every Monday.

2. Looking for food at "Jungang Market" — it is a wedding-goods street.

3. Taking the Wolmi Sea Train on a Monday — it is closed.

4. Hunting for the original jjolmyeon shop — the official pages name only dakgangjeong as the original.

5. Expecting foreign-language guidance — the market has leaflets in English, Chinese and Japanese, but the Wolmi Sea Train has no foreign-language guidance.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. What is the half-day order? — Sinpo International Market (dakgangjeong for lunch) → Chinatown → Jayu Park → Open Port Museum and Daebul Hotel Exhibition Hall (500 and 1,000 won).

2. What do the two museums cost together? — 1,500 won for an adult.

3. When is the market closed? — The first Monday of each month, and the day of Seollal and Chuseok.

4. Is English available? — The market has leaflets in English, Chinese and Japanese (official). English service from staff is not posted.

5. How do I get to Wolmido? — Not posted officially. Use a map app.

6. Why "Jemulpo-gu" and not "Jung-gu"? — The official page uses Jemulpo-gu (제물포구) as the district name in 2026.
