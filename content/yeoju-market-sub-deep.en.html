Yeoju Guide — King Sejong's Tomb (Yeongneung) and History Hall, Silleuksa Temple, Yeoju Ceramics World, Empress Myeongseong's Birthplace

Subtitle: Yeoju Yeongneung (英陵) and Yeongneung (寧陵), closed Mondays · King Sejong History Hall (세종대왕 역사문화관), free · Silleuksa Temple (신륵사), 08:00–18:00 · Yeoju Ceramics World (여주도자세상), 10:00–18:00, closed Mondays · Empress Myeongseong's Birthplace and Memorial Hall (명성황후 생가·기념관), closed Mondays — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

In Yeoju, King Sejong's tomb is the center. You can group together Yeongneung, the tombs of Sejong and Hyojong, the King Sejong History Hall at the same address, Silleuksa Temple, a temple on the Namhangang River, Yeoju Ceramics World, a pottery shopping mall within the same tourist site, and Empress Myeongseong's Birthplace.

Instead of a market, this guide covers the five places above, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Icheon ceramics continue in #673 Icheon Guide.

👑 Yeoju Yeongneung (英陵) and Yeongneung (寧陵) — The Tombs of Sejong and Hyojong

Yeoju Yeongneung and Yeongneung (269-10 Yeongneung-ro, Sejongdaewang-myeon, Yeoju (여주시 세종대왕면 영릉로 269-10), ☎ 031-885-3123) are two tombs with the same name. Yeongneung (英陵) is the tomb of Sejong, the 4th king of Joseon, and Queen Soheon, and Yeongneung (寧陵) is the tomb of Hyojong, the 17th king of Joseon, and Queen Insun. It is registered as a World Heritage Site in the tourism organization's information.

  • 英陵 (Yeongneung, King Sejong): Among the Joseon royal tombs, it was the first joint-burial tomb to enshrine a king and queen in one mound. It was moved to its present site in 1469.
  • 寧陵 (Yeongneung, King Hyojong): It is the first tomb where the king's and queen's mounds are placed one above the other on the same hill. The upper one is Hyojong, and the lower one is Queen Insun.
  • Opening hours: February–May 09:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00), June–August 09:00–18:30 (last entry 17:30), September–October 09:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00), November–January 09:00–17:30 (last entry 16:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (if Monday is a public holiday, it is open that day and closed on the next weekday instead). Parking is available.

📜 King Sejong History Hall

King Sejong History Hall (same address as Yeongneung, ☎ King Sejong Heritage Management Office 031-885-3123) is an exhibition hall introducing King Sejong, King Hyojong, and the Joseon royal tombs. It has 3 permanent exhibition rooms, a special exhibition room, a video room, and a cafe.

  • Opening hours: March–May 09:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00), June–August 09:00–18:30 (last entry 17:30), September–October 09:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00), November–February 09:00–17:30 (last entry 16:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Admission: Free.
  • Note: In February, the closing times of Yeongneung (18:00) and the History Hall (17:30) are registered differently.

🛕 Silleuksa Temple — A Temple with a View of the Namhangang River

Silleuksa Temple (73 Silleuksa-gil, Cheonsong-dong, Yeoju (여주시 신륵사길 73, 천송동), ☎ 031-885-2505) is a temple at the foot of Bongmisan Mountain, on the bank of the Namhangang River. It is a rare temple built by a river rather than in the mountains. It is known as a place where the Zen Master Naong stayed in the late Goryeo period, and in 1472 it became the royal prayer temple of Yeongneung and was called Boeunsa. Because it has a multi-story brick pagoda, it was also called the "Brick Temple" (벽절).

  • Opening hours: 08:00–18:00, open year-round. Parking is available.
  • Silleuksa Tourist Site (☎ 031-884-9300): Always open, open year-round. It is good for walking along the river, and has a yellow-sail boat dock, a sculpture park, and the Yeoju Museum. The Namhangang Suspension Bridge is nearby.

🏺 Yeoju Ceramics World — Ceramics Exhibitions and Shopping

Yeoju Ceramics World (7 Silleuksa-gil, Cheonsong-dong, Yeoju (여주시 신륵사길 7, 천송동), ☎ 031-884-8644) is a ceramics exhibition and sales space of the Korea Ceramic Foundation inside the Silleuksa Tourist Site. It is also where the Gyeonggi International Ceramic Biennale is held.

  • Opening hours: 10:00–18:00, last entry 17:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, and the days of Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Layout: Gyeonggi Living Ceramics Museum, a ceramics shopping mall, and a craft creation support center. The mall has a souvenir and small-goods art shop and a living-ceramics lifestyle shop.

🏠 Empress Myeongseong's Birthplace and Memorial Hall

Empress Myeongseong's Birthplace (71 Myeongseong-ro, Neunghyeon-dong, Yeoju (여주시 명성로 71, 능현동), ☎ Empress Myeongseong Birthplace Management Office 031-881-9730) is the house where Empress Myeongseong, consort of Joseon's King Gojong, was born and lived until age eight. Of the buildings first built in 1687, only the main house remains, and the other buildings were rebuilt in 1996.

  • Opening hours: March–October 09:00–18:00 (last entry 17:30), November–February 09:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, the days of Seollal and Chuseok, and closure days set by the foundation.
  • Empress Myeongseong Memorial Hall: A public museum built by Yeoju City in 2017, across from the birthplace. Its hours and closed days are the same as the birthplace's, and it is free. Parking is 500 won for light cars, 1,000 won for compact cars, and 2,000 won for mid-size and large vehicles.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to Yeoju on a Monday — Yeongneung, the History Hall, Yeoju Ceramics World, and Empress Myeongseong's Birthplace and Memorial Hall are all closed every Monday. Only Silleuksa Temple and the Silleuksa Tourist Site are open year-round.
  2. Thinking Yeongneung is one place — There are two tombs: Sejong's Yeongneung (英陵) and Hyojong's Yeongneung (寧陵).
  3. Thinking Ceramics World is open late — It closes at 18:00, and entry is until 17:00.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. How much is the admission to King Sejong's tomb?

A. No admission fee for Yeongneung is registered in the tourism organization's information. The King Sejong History Hall at the same address is free.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. For the places in this guide, whether cards are accepted is not registered in the tourism organization's information.
