Yeoju Hangul Market and King Sejong’s Tomb sub-deep 12 selections for foreigners

Category: A Outskirts/Nature

Parents deep: #380 Market full set·#510 Gyeonggi day-trip·#663 Andong sub

Yeoju Hangul Mayor, King Sejong the Great’s 12th election

1. Yeoju Hangul Market (five-day market on May 5 and 10)

  • Discrimination: Legacy of King Sejong’s creation of Hangul + 5-10 day market·Yeoju traditional market·Hangul street → Hangeul market renamed 2017
  • Foreigners visit: visitkorea·open tourism·Gyeonggi-do K-Tourism Market candidate
  • kr cross: #380·#618·#663·#510

2. Tomb of King Sejong the Great (Yeongneung, UNESCO)

  • Discrimination: Joint tomb of King Sejong and Queen Soheon 1469·UNESCO 40th Joseon royal tomb registration 2009·Creation of Hangul 1443
  • 2026 600th anniversary of King Sejong’s accession to the throne + 583rd anniversary of Hangul
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN Orthodox 1st place·#506 Holiday Hangeul Day cross

3. Tomb of King Hyojong (Yeongneung)

  • Discrimination: Joint Royal Tombs of Hyojong and Queen Inseon·10-minute walk near Sejong’s Royal Tombs·UNESCO Royal Tombs of the Joseon Dynasty
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

4. Birthplace of Empress Myeongseong (Birthplace of Empress #1, Korea)

  • Discrimination: Empress Myeongseong (1851-1895)·Assassination in 1895 during Japanese colonial Eulmi Incident·Yeoju Birthplace + Memorial Hall
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

5. Yeoju Ceramics (Korea’s top three ceramic production centers)

  • Discrimination: Icheon, Yeoju, Gwangju, Korea’s three major ceramics·Yeoju Pottery Festival May·Ceramic Village 80+
  • kr cross: #600 Ceramics·#673 Icheon sub

6. Silleuksa Temple (Namhan River Temple)

  • Discrimination: Goryeo Dynasty + Joseon Temple·Gangwolheon Pavilion on the cliff of the Namhan River·11 Treasures
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Orthodox enthusiast·#509 Temple cross

7. Namhan River + Gangcheon Island

  • Discrimination: Yeoju #1 nature spot·Namhan River bike path 234km cluster·April cherry blossom mania
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Nature·Bicycle Mania·#550 Bicycle cross

8. Yeoju rice (the king’s signature rice)

  • Discrimination: Yeoju Jinsangmi (Royal Rice)·The King’s Table Yeoju Rice Korea #1 Quality·Yeoju Rice Authentic
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP Authentic enthusiast·Souvenir OK (preservation)

9. Yeoju Ceramic World Biennale

  • Discrimination: First held in 2001, April-May every other year, World Ceramic Biennale, jointly held in Icheon + Gwangju
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Mania·#668 K-Art cross

10. Yeoju Museum + Hangeul Street

  • Discrimination: Sejong + Hangeul Museum (adjacent to Hangeul Street)·Hunminjeongeum + K-Hangeul culture
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·#506 Hangul Day cross

11. Yeoju five-day market on the 5th and 10th

  • Discrimination: Last 5th and 10th day of every month·Simultaneous holding of Yeoju Hangeul Market + 5-day market·Gyeonggi-do authentic 5-day market
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP Authentic Mania·#663 Andong Oil Market cross

12. Yeoju day-trip + 1 night recommended

  • Access: Yeoju Station (Seongnam-Yeoju Gyeonggang Line) 50 minutes, or KTX Yangpyeong Station → 30 minutes by car
  • City Tour: Market – Sejong’s Royal Tomb – Silleuksa Temple, full day, 6 hours / KRW 25,000
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN day-trip 1st place (1 hour from Seoul)

FAQ 6

Q1. Is Yeoju day-trip possible? A. 1 hour in the metropolitan area (Gyeonggang Line) · Full day OK / Sejong Royal Tomb + Market + Silleuksa Temple first.

Q2. King Sejong’s Tomb admission fee? A. 3,000 won·King Hyojong’s Tomb Yeongneung set admission·Operating 09:00-18:00·Closed on Mondays.

Q3. When is the 5-day market in the Korean Market? A. Last 5th and 10th of every month (5-day intervals)·Permanent + 5-day market in parallel.

Q4. When is the Yeoju Ceramic Festival? A. May every year (April-May)·World Ceramic Biennale every other year (odd years).

Q5. How to get to Empress Myeongseong’s birthplace? A. 15 minutes by car from downtown Yeoju. Full set of memorial hall + birthplace + shrine.

Q6. Silleuksa Temple entrance fee? A. Free (open 24 hours)·Gangwolheon Pavilion Sunset Mania.
