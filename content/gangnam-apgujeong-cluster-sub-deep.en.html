Apgujeong & Jamwon Half-Day Guide — Apgujeong Rodeo Street (압구정 로데오거리), Hyundai Department Store Apgujeong Main Store (현대백화점 압구정본점), Galleria Luxury Hall (갤러리아 명품관) and Jamwon Hangang Park (잠원한강공원), 4 Stops (Confirmed October 2026)

Subtitle: Hyundai Department Store Apgujeong Main Store (현대백화점 압구정본점) connects directly to Apgujeong Station (압구정역) Exit 6, and its 3rd-floor desk gives you your tax refund on the spot (Hyundai Department Store official) · Galleria Luxury Hall (갤러리아 명품관) has two buildings, WEST and EAST, open Mon–Thu 10:30–20:00 and Fri–Sun 10:30–20:30 (Galleria official) · Jamwon Hangang Park (잠원한강공원) is about a 15–20 minute walk from Apgujeong Station Exit 6 (Hangang Park official) · the Jamwon swimming pool (잠원 수영장) is closed for remodeling and is scheduled to reopen in 2028 (Hangang Park official) — as of 2026-10-22

ISSUE 03 · #713 · 2026-06-02

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Come out of Apgujeong Station (압구정역) Exit 6 and the department store's front entrance is right there; walk north for a little over 15 minutes and the river breeze starts to blow under Hannam Bridge (한남대교). This guide covers just one neighborhood, Apgujeong-dong (압구정동). It is a half-day route: by day you take in Rodeo Street (로데오거리) and two department stores, and around sunset you walk down to Jamwon Hangang Park (잠원한강공원). It is for first-time Gangnam (강남) visitors who want a feel for what Apgujeong is like, and for anyone who wants to browse the luxury halls and settle the tax refund in one go.

Gangnam Station and COEX (코엑스) (#301), Cheongdam (청담) luxury street and galleries (#296), and Banpo (반포) Hangang Park and Sebitseom (세빛섬) (#712) are covered in the pieces listed under 🔗 Related Content below. Every hour, address and fee here is a value opened directly on the official page; values we could not open are marked as such.

🛍 Apgujeong Rodeo Street (압구정 로데오거리) — A Neighborhood for Walking

  • Apgujeong Rodeo Street (압구정 로데오거리) — around Apgujeong-dong (압구정동), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Always open, open year-round, no stroller rental (VisitKorea). VisitKorea says the street became a fashion hub in the early 1990s and today offers not just shopping but plenty of restaurants and things to see
  • K-Star Road (Gangnamdol / 강남돌) — along a roughly 1 km stretch from Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역) Exit 2 to Cheongdam Intersection (청담사거리), teddy-bear statues called "Gangnamdol" (강남돌), dressed in the images of K-pop groups, line the street. In front of Galleria Department Store (갤러리아백화점) stands a 3 m-tall Psy Gangnamdol, with 17 more elsewhere (VisitSeoul, article revised June 2024). We could not confirm whether all of them are still in place
  • Opening hours of individual shops on Rodeo Street (로데오거리) differ from store to store, so this guide does not list them. For the luxury flagship street on the Cheongdam (청담) side see Walking Cheongdam — Louis Vuitton Maison and House of Dior Architecture, SongEun, Espace Louis Vuitton and Atelier Hermès Galleries, Dosan Park, and for Garosu-gil (가로수길) in Sinsa (신사) see #295

🏬 Hyundai Department Store Apgujeong Main Store (현대백화점 압구정본점) — The Department Store Linked to the Station

  • Address: 165 Apgujeong-ro (압구정로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Main phone 1588-3650 (Hyundai Department Store official)
  • Hours: Mon–Thu 10:30–20:00, Fri–Sun 10:30–20:30, restaurant floor 11:00–22:00 (Hyundai Department Store official branch page). Closed days are posted each month on the branch page calendar. The tenant-shop information on VisitKorea lists closures as "holidays, once a month"
  • Getting there: the passage inside Apgujeong Station (압구정역) Line 3 Exit 6 leads to the store's 2nd basement-level entrance, and if you go out Exit 6 you reach the main entrance. Airport Bus 6006 stops at the Apgujeong Station stop (Hyundai Department Store official)
  • Services for foreign visitors (Hyundai Department Store official service facilities page)
  • Tax Refund — 3rd floor, 02-3449-5207. The department store refunds the tax-free amount on goods foreign customers bought, on the spot
  • Baggage storage — 1st basement level, 02-3449-5550

💎 Galleria Luxury Hall (갤러리아 명품관) — Two Buildings on Either Side of the Road

  • Address: WEST, 343 Apgujeong-ro (압구정로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul; EAST, 407 Apgujeong-ro (압구정로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Main phone 1544-6600 (Galleria official)
  • Hours: Mon–Thu 10:30–20:00, Fri–Sun 10:30–20:30. The Gourmet 494 (고메이494) food hall keeps the same hours, and last order is 30 minutes before closing. The October 2026 business information shows "no closed days". Next month's information changes in the last week of each month (Galleria official). VisitKorea lists "closed once a month"
  • Building: WEST and EAST are separated by the road. The fish-scale-patterned facade of WEST changes color at night (VisitKorea)
  • Services for foreign visitors (Galleria official customer facilities page)
  • Foreign visitor service center — WEST 5th floor, 02-3449-4151 / 02-3449-4411
  • Concierge desk — WEST 1st floor, 02-3449-4020
  • Refund method: both on-the-spot and after-purchase refunds are available at WEST (VisitKorea tourism information)
  • For the brand lineups of the two department stores and Gangnam luxury shopping as a whole see #771, and for a comparison of Seoul department stores see #449

🌊 Jamwon Hangang Park (잠원한강공원) — The Han River, a Walk Down from Apgujeong

  • Basic information: 221-124 Jamwon-ro (잠원로), Seocho-gu (서초구), Seoul. 5.4 km long from the south end of Yeongdong Bridge (영동대교) to upstream of Jamsu Bridge (잠수교, Banpo Bridge), area 474,213㎡. Information center 02-3780-0531~3, administrative hours 09:00–18:00 (Hangang Park official). The park is always open and open year-round (VisitKorea)
  • Getting there (Hangang Park official "Directions" page)
  • Line 3 Apgujeong Station (압구정역) Exit 6 → toward Hyundai High School (현대고등학교), about 15–20 minutes on foot (Apgujeong access gate / 압구정나들목)
  • Line 3 Sinsa Station (신사역) Exit 5 → toward Sinsa Middle School (신사중학교), about 15–20 minutes on foot (Sinsa access gate / 신사나들목)
  • Line 3 Jamwon Station (잠원역) Exit 4 → toward Jamwon Hansin Apartments (잠원한신아파트), about 15 minutes on foot (Jamwon access gate / 잠원나들목)
  • Get off the Hangang Bus (한강버스) at Apgujeong Pier (압구정 선착장) and you connect to the park
  • Things to see and do: a 5.4 km riverside bike path. The bike rental shop is upstream of the south end of Hannam Bridge (한남대교), in front of Jamwon Kiosk No. 2 (잠원제2호매점). The attractions the official page highlights are the Jamwon Grass Garden (잠원 그라스정원) pink muhly, the Jamwon Honeybee Forest (잠원 꿀벌숲) and the Silkworm Learning Center (누에체험학습장). There is also water leisure such as water skiing and wakeboarding, plus floating cafe and restaurant facilities (Hangang Park official)
  • Hangang Circulating Sightseeing Car (Haechi Car / 해치카): a free loop shuttle running Dongjak Station (동작역) Exit 2 → Banpo Hangang Park (반포한강공원, Sebitseom / 세빛섬) → Jamwon Hangang Park (잠원한강공원, Seoul Wave / 서울웨이브). In 2026 it runs April 4 to November 30, every 20 minutes. Mon–Fri 14:00–20:00, weekends and public holidays 11:00–20:00, with no service 16:00–17:00 (Hangang Park official). For the Banpo (반포) side of the route see #712
  • Swimming pool: the Jamwon Hangang Park pool (잠원 한강공원 수영장) is closed for remodeling and is scheduled to reopen in 2028. Seoul advises using the Ttukseom (뚝섬) and Yeouido (여의도) Hangang Park pools instead. The Hangang Park pools have finished all of their 2026 operation, and the 2027 schedule has not been announced yet (Hangang Park official)
  • Sledding hill: in the 2025–2026 season it operated on the Jamwon pool site from December 31, 2025 to February 18, 2026, and admission was ₩6,000 (~$4.50) regardless of age (Hangang Park official). Because the pool is under construction, whether it reopens in Jamwon (잠원) for winter 2026–2027 has not been announced yet
  • Facilities: 3 convenience stores, 9 restrooms. 6 parking lots with 722 spaces, 06:00–24:00 (Hangang Park official)
  • For a comparison of all the Seoul Hangang parks see #706

🧰 Apgujeong Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Refunds, Getting Around

  • Solo: you can walk into all four places alone with no reservation. For spots in a department store restaurant floor that suit solo diners see #348 Seoul Solo Diner Map
  • Payment and refunds: bring your passport. Hyundai Department Store (현대백화점) refunds on the spot at the 3rd-floor Tax Refund desk (Hyundai Department Store official), and Galleria WEST (갤러리아 WEST관) offers both on-the-spot and after-purchase refunds (VisitKorea). For the conditions and airport procedure see the #1231 Tax Refund Guide and the #1304 Airport Kiosk Procedure Edition. For cards and mobile payment see #527
  • Getting around: Line 3 Apgujeong Station (압구정역) alone reaches both Hyundai Department Store (현대백화점) and Jamwon Hangang Park (잠원한강공원). For transit cards see #1298. The official page advises taking public transit because the area around the Hangang Park gets congested on weekends and public holidays
  • Han River manners: you must take your trash back with you, and breaking this rule carries a ₩100,000 (~$75) fine; parking outside designated spots carries a ₩50,000 (~$38) fine. Smoking is banned inside the park and cooking is not allowed (Hangang Park official)

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "I'll swim at the Jamwon pool in summer" — it is closed for remodeling and scheduled to reopen in 2028. For summer swimming go to the Ttukseom (뚝섬) or Yeouido (여의도) Hangang Park pools (Hangang Park official)

2. "Department stores close at the same time every day" — both department stores close at 20:30 Fri–Sun and 20:00 Mon–Thu (each official page)

3. "Tax refunds only work at the airport" — the 3rd floor of Hyundai Department Store Apgujeong Main Store (현대백화점 압구정본점) refunds on the spot (Hyundai Department Store official)

4. "The Han River is right in front of Apgujeong Station" — it is about a 15–20 minute walk from Exit 6, and you reach the park through the Apgujeong access gate (압구정나들목) (Hangang Park official)
