3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. The perception that “just stopping by Starbucks is enough”

Seoul Cafe Culture local roastery and dessert signature overwhelms global chains. Noted (donuts), Matcha (matcha), and Ruri Coffee (Geisha coffee beans) have an average English review of 5.0/5.0 from foreigners, and many say, “The cafes in Seoul are more interesting than Tokyo.” If it's your first visit, you won't regret starting with a signature dessert cafe instead of a global chain.

2. Prejudice that Hanok Cafe = “Tourist Destination”

Bukchon's Hanok Cafe (Low Roof·Ichae) has many 5-star English reviews from foreigners — "Almost hidden cafe, great place to take a break" and "Could enjoy our drinks in small sitting rooms or out in the sun in the courtyard" are repeated. Hanok = not a cliché, but a real place for foreigners to relax.

3. Myeongdong and Namdaemun are perceived as “only for shopping”

There is a cafe in Myeongdong and Namdaemun that is surprisingly popular with foreigners. Matcha Myeongdong Main Branch (Matcha) and Moongchichi Cafe (inside Namdaemun Market) received 4.5+ reviews in both English and Japanese. It can be used as a cafe for a break while shopping at the market.

3. FAQ — 6 frequently asked questions by foreigners

Q1. Payment — Is WeChat Pay/Alipay available?

  • All 8 stores Card, Kakao Pay, Samsung Pay OK. WeChat and Alipay are partially supported by Noted, Matcha Myeongdong, and Mahogany (confirmation required at checkout).
  • Noted’s self-checkout kiosk supports multiple languages ​​(English, Japanese, Chinese).

Q2. Do you have an English menu?

  • Noted / Matcha Myeongdong / Mahogany / Low Roof: English menu ✓
  • Ruri / Browner / Munchichi / Lee Chae: English menu part — Staff English OK

Q3. Is it possible for one person to visit?

  • OK for one person in all 8 stores. Korean cafe culture is very natural for one person.

Q4. Are vegan, halal, or religious meals possible?

  • Most desserts are based on milk, eggs, and butter → For vegans, only beverages (Americano, tea) are recommended
  • None of the 8 stores have Halal certification → Only beverages are safe (coffee, tea)

Q5. Are children allowed?

  • Mahogany / low roof / mungchichi: OK for children (seats available)
  • Ruri / Lee Chae / Brauner: Not recommended for children under 5 years old due to narrow space.

Q6. Is there WiFi?

  • All 8 stores ✓. Ask the staff for the password.
