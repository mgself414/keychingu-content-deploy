⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Massage = Anywhere": Korean massage offers a full range: traditional Hanbang (한방, Korean herbal medicine), Thai, Swedish, foot, and aroma.

2. "Massage = Expensive": Foot massage starts from ₩30,000 (~$22 / ~160 RMB / ~3,200 JPY), aroma from ₩80,000, and hotel spas from ₩200,000+ for enthusiasts.

3. "Hotel Spa = Inaccessible": Signiel, Shilla, and Banyan Tree are top foreigner-friendly spots.
