8 Seoul Yoga & Pilates Studios — Places You Can Try Just Once on a Trip: English Classes, How to Book, Single-Class Prices

Subtitle: Six yoga studios you can join with a single-class pass or a one-day pass, no membership needed, plus two pilates and barre studios with a booking window for foreign travelers. Each one is confirmed as open on Kakao Map, and the prices are only values we opened ourselves: owner-registered values, official pages, and booking platforms. Classes are marked 'English' only where an official notice or a review says so.

ISSUE 06 · #767 · 2026-11-24 · Seoul Yoga·Pilates Single Classes

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 8

This list is for travelers who want to try one morning yoga class and one pilates class during a few days in Seoul. Korean yoga studios mostly sell memberships. For example, the Kakao Map (카카오맵) menu of Yoga Project Hannam (요가프로젝트 한남점) lists class-count tickets for 10, 30, 50, and 100 classes and period tickets of 1 to 12 months (registered 2026-07-16). Some studios also sell a separate 'single-class pass' (1회권) or 'one-day' (원데이) option in between. We picked only those.

Pilates is different. Korean pilates runs as private lessons or small-group lessons on equipment such as the reformer. Even the regular classes at Cheonho Barosun Pilates (천호바로선필라테스) in this article are small groups of up to 4 (2026-11-22 blog). The single-class price of The Whale Studio (더웨일스튜디오) is listed on its Kakao menu as a 'first-visit trial.' So for pilates we included only two places that have a traveler booking window (an Airbnb service) or a registered group trial.

There are three selection rules.

  1. The Kakao Map place info says "open for business."
  2. A blog review, photo, review, or official-page notice was posted in 2026.
  3. The single-class price was confirmed from a value the owner registered on Kakao Map (registered within the last 2 years), from an official page, or from a booking platform we opened ourselves.

⚠ Notation principle: Business hours are Kakao Map values (checked 2026-11-24). Where the owner registered hours by day of the week, we call them 'owner-registered values'; where hours are only displayed on the map without a day-by-day registration, we call them 'Kakao Map displayed hours.' A yoga studio's business hours are just the window when the door is open, and the class timetable is separate. Check actual class times on each studio's booking page. Next to each price we put the source and the registration date. 'English' is carried over only as far as an official notice, a platform description, or a review says it.

① Yoga — 6 Places You Can Join with a Single-Class Pass

  • The Flow Room (더플로우룸) — A basement yoga studio in Itaewon (이태원). The official site says "All our yoga classes are led in English," which makes it the one place on this list where English classes are confirmed officially. The official FAQ price for a single (drop-in) class is 25,000 won, and no membership is needed. You book and pay for classes in advance on Bookamat. If there is room they accept walk-ins, but the official FAQ does not recommend it. Mats, blocks, and straps are included in the class fee, and a mat towel is recommended. For beginners it recommends Alignment, Vinyasa 1, Yin, Hatha, and Hatha Flow (official FAQ). The address is Noksapyeong-daero 32-gil 47 (녹사평대로32길 47), B1, Yongsan-gu (용산구); the nearest station is Itaewon Station (이태원역) (Line 6) Exit 4, an 8-minute walk. Kakao Map has no hours registered, and the regular timetable can only be seen on Bookamat. The official site carries a notice on regular classes for the October 8–10 holiday, a notice on Sunday 12:00 (Vinyasa 2) and 13:30 (Vinyasa 1) classes, and a notice on the 11th temple yoga trip of 2026.
  • Yoga Culture Ashram (요가문화원) — A hatha yoga studio on Gyedong-gil (계동길), north of Anguk Station (안국역). The registered price is 35,000 won for a single practice pass (Kakao Map, registered 2026-04-06). Owner-registered hours are Mon·Wed·Fri 07:00–21:00, Tue·Thu 09:00–21:00, Sat 10:30–12:00, and it is closed Sundays and public holidays. The address is Gyedong-gil 5, Room 302 (계동길 5 302호), Jongno-gu (종로구); the nearest station is Anguk Station (Line 3) Exit 3, a 2-minute walk. An English review was posted on Kakao Map on 2026-11-14. It says the writer went while staying in Seoul in October and found the class intense and challenging. All 3 Kakao reviews were posted during the studio's review-event period (11/9–11/20, with 1 extra practice pass for writing a review). Another review said teaching style differs from teacher to teacher (2026-11-13). Book or ask through the KakaoTalk channel 'Yoga Culture Ashram (요가문화원)' or at 02-741-0079. You can combine it on foot with Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌한옥마을).
  • Boiled Yoga (보일드요가) — A small-group yoga studio in Susong-dong (수송동), south of Anguk Station (안국역). It teaches hatha, yin, and vinyasa classes (official site). The registered prices are 40,000 won for a one-day yoga practice (Kakao Map, registered 2025-12-16) and 77,000 won for a 7-day trial pass (Kakao Map, registered 2026-07-08). A 2026-08-24 blog review also gave the one-day price as 40,000 won. If you stay in Seoul for a week, the 7-day trial pass is worth a look. The same review explains how to book. On Naver Booking (네이버 예약) you have to go through 'Regular Practice (정규수련) > Select Time (시간 선택) > Class Pass (횟수권)' to see the one-day option. The official contact page says to send inquiries about classes and booking by Instagram DM (@boiledyoga). Owner-registered hours are Mon·Wed 06:40–21:30 (break time 16:30–18:15), Tue·Thu·Fri 06:40–20:00 (break time 13:30–18:15), Sat·Sun 08:00–09:30, and it is closed on public holidays. The address is Yulgok-ro 4-gil 6, 3F (율곡로4길 6 3층), Jongno-gu (종로구); the nearest station is Anguk Station (Line 3) Exit 6, a 5-minute walk. The title of the 2026-08-24 review includes 'yin yoga and tea talk' (인요가와 차담), meaning there was a tea time after class.
  • Yoga Project (요가프로젝트) (Hannam·Sinsa·Seongsu) — A yoga studio with three shared branches. According to the official site, the single-class pass is 45,000 won and can be used at any of the Sinsa, Seongsu, and Hannam branches within 10 days. The Sinsa branch lists the same price on Kakao Map too (regular practice single-class pass 45,000 won, registered 2026-11-03). Classes are taught in Korean with brief English guidance (official). You pay for your first class through each branch's Naver Booking, and from the next class you book through the 'ON Studio (온스튜디오)' app (official). Shared mats and props are available, and you bring your own towel and comfortable clothes (official). The Hannam branch (Daesagwan-ro 31-gil 7-6, 5F (대사관로31길 7-6 5층), Yongsan-gu (용산구); the elevator is inside Core Coffee on the first floor) has this Kakao Map notice: "Regular Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri, Sun 06:45–21:20, Thu 06:45–21:30, Sat 06:45–21:00." It is a 13-minute walk from Hannam Station (한남역) (Gyeongui-Jungang Line) Exit 1. The Seongsu branch (Wangsimni-ro 4-gil 26-7, 3F (왕십리로4길 26-7 3층), Seongdong-gu (성동구); 2 minutes from Ttukseom Station (뚝섬역) (Line 2) Exit 7) and the Sinsa branch (Nonhyeon-ro 151-gil 55, 5F (논현로151길 55 5층), Gangnam-gu (강남구); 8 minutes from Sinsa Station (신사역) (Line 3 and Shinbundang Line) Exit 8) run on the class schedule. Check times on the official timetable or in the ON Studio app. A 2026-10-26 blog review said the Hannam branch allows pets and that valet parking is 5,000 won for 2 hours.
  • Softbowl Studio (소프트보울스튜디오) — A hatha yoga studio on the 3rd floor of an alley building in Yeonnam-dong (연남동). The registered price is 35,000 won for a one-day class (Kakao Map, registered 2026-10-02). A 2026-10-03 blog review (a post containing a booking-app referral code) said a mat was laid out every time the writer went, the mats are Manduka, and props such as blocks and blankets are kept inside. The same review also mentions the full-window view, pets being allowed, and a rather narrow space. Kakao Map has no hours, so check the class timetable on Instagram @softbowl.studio. The address is Seongmisan-ro 23an-gil 23, 3F (성미산로23안길 23 3층), Mapo-gu (마포구). Kakao Map directions say it is an 11-minute walk from Gajwa Station (가좌역) (Gyeongui-Jungang Line) Exit 1. A 2026-11-12 review (a booking-app partner reward post) said the writer did a Tuesday evening hatha class under a Christmas tree.
  • Hatha Yoga An Songpanaru Station branch (하타요가안 송파나루역점) — A hatha yoga studio in Songpa (송파). It takes foreign travelers through the Airbnb service 'Hatha Yoga Practice in Seoul.' The price shown on the page we opened on 2026-11-24 is 26,400 won per person (regular price 33,000 won). The language is English, the class is 70 minutes from 11:30 to 12:40, up to 10 people at a time, ages 18 and over (Airbnb). Students sometimes bring their dogs (Airbnb description). The page says nothing about mats. The Kakao Map displayed hours are Mon–Fri 06:30–22:00 and Sat 09:00–21:00, and it is closed Sundays. The address is Baekjegobun-ro 411, 5F (백제고분로 411 5층), Songpa-gu (송파구); the nearest station is Songpanaru Station (송파나루역) (Line 9) Exit 1, a 5-minute walk. It pairs well with a day around Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) and Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰).

② Pilates·Barre — 2 Places with a Traveler Window

Both places have a window (a group trial or Airbnb) where a traveler can book once and go.

  • The Whale Studio (더웨일스튜디오) — A pilates, barre, and yoga studio in front of Jamsil Saenae Station (잠실새내역). The registered prices are 38,000 won for a group lesson trial (first visit) and 55,000 won for a private lesson trial (first visit) (Kakao Map, registered 2026-11-18). Both trial menu descriptions carry 'English Available' (Kakao menu). The group trial is a small group: 3:1 for pilates and 5:1 for barre·yoga (same menu description). The Airbnb service 'K-Pop Barre in Seoul, Taught in Korean & English' is run in Korean and English. The 2026-11-24 displayed prices are 34,000 won per person for the K-Barre group class (regular price 40,000 won) and 102,000 won for a 1:1 private class (regular price 120,000 won), ages 18 and over (Airbnb). Booking and inquiries are taken through the KakaoTalk channel and Naver Booking (studio blog). Owner-registered hours are Mon–Fri 09:00–22:30, Sat·Sun 09:00–17:00, and public holidays 10:00–18:00. The address is Baekjegobun-ro 7-gil 49, Suite 201, 2F of the commercial building (백제고분로7길 49 상가 2층 201호), Songpa-gu (송파구); the nearest station is Jamsil Saenae Station (Line 2) Exit 3, a 4-minute walk. Parking is free (studio blog).
  • Cheonho Barosun Pilates (천호바로선필라테스) — An equipment pilates studio in Cheonho-dong (천호동), Gangdong-gu (강동구). It takes travelers through the Airbnb service 'Recharge in Seoul: Pilates for Travelers.' The lead instructor teaches in Korean and an assistant instructor helps in English. The 2026-11-24 displayed prices are as follows. The 1:2 session (1 guest, 2 instructors) is 290,000 won per person. The duet (2 guests) is 180,000 won per person, with a minimum booking of 300,000 won. A family of 4 is 120,000 won per person, with a minimum of 480,000 won. All are 1 hour and ages 18 and over. Cancel up to a day before for a full refund (Airbnb). It is the most expensive place in this article, a semi-private format with two instructors. The Kakao Map displayed hours are Mon–Sat 09:00–22:00, and it is closed Sundays. The address is Jinhwangdo-ro 23-gil 7, Room 105 (진황도로23길 7 105호) (Cheonho 3-dong public complex building), Gangdong-gu (강동구); the nearest station is Cheonho Station (천호역) (Lines 5 and 8) Exit 5, an 11-minute walk. A 2026-11-22 blog review said even the regular classes are small groups of up to 4.

🗺 Route — A Day with One Yoga Class, Two Ways

A. Morning yoga in Anguk + Bukchon (except Sundays and public holidays)

  1. Morning An early class at Boiled Yoga (보일드요가) or a morning class at Yoga Culture Ashram (요가문화원) (check the hours by day of the week)
  2. Late morning Walk up from Anguk Station (안국역) to Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌한옥마을)
  3. After lunch Come back down toward Samcheong-dong (삼청동) and Insa-dong (인사동)

Both places are within a 5-minute walk of Anguk Station, and both close on public holidays.

B. Evening yoga in Itaewon·Hannam (weekdays)

  1. Afternoon Stroll around the Itaewon (이태원) and Hannam (한남) neighborhoods
  2. Evening The Flow Room (더플로우룸) (check the Bookamat timetable) or Yoga Project Hannam (요가프로젝트 한남) (regular classes until 21:20 by the Kakao Map notice, until 21:30 on Thursday)
  3. After class Dinner near Itaewon Station (이태원역) or Hangangjin Station (한강진역)

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Showing up empty-handed without a mat or towel — The places that officially say they provide mats are The Flow Room (더플로우룸) and Yoga Project (요가프로젝트). Yoga Project says to bring a towel, and The Flow Room recommends a mat towel. For the rest, ask when you book.
  2. Thinking you can walk into pilates the way you can into yoga — Korean pilates is built around private or small-group equipment lessons. Some places, like The Whale Studio (더웨일스튜디오), list the single-class price as a 'first-visit trial.' While traveling, pick The Whale Studio's group trial or a place with a traveler window such as Airbnb.
  3. Going without checking the day of the week — Yoga Culture Ashram (요가문화원) closes Sundays and public holidays, Boiled Yoga (보일드요가) closes on public holidays, and Hatha Yoga An (하타요가안) and Cheonho Barosun Pilates (천호바로선필라테스) close Sundays. Boiled Yoga is open only 08:00–09:30 in the morning on Sat·Sun.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. Which places are sure to have English classes? — The Flow Room (더플로우룸), where the official site says "All our yoga classes are led in English." From Airbnb descriptions, there are Hatha Yoga An (하타요가안) (English), the K-Barre at The Whale Studio (더웨일스튜디오) (Korean and English), and Cheonho Barosun Pilates (천호바로선필라테스) (English assistant instructor). Yoga Project (요가프로젝트) is Korean-language classes with brief English guidance.
  2. What is the cheapest way to try one class? — By regular price, The Flow Room drop-in at 25,000 won is the lowest, and Hatha Yoga An on Airbnb (regular price 33,000 won, 26,400 won displayed on 2026-11-24) is next. If you stay a week, there is also the Boiled Yoga 7-day trial pass (77,000 won).
  3. Which places can I go to on weekends? — By Kakao Map hours, The Whale Studio (더웨일스튜디오) is open Sat·Sun 09:00–17:00, Hatha Yoga An (하타요가안) Sat 09:00–21:00, and Yoga Culture Ashram (요가문화원) Sat 10:30–12:00, while Boiled Yoga (보일드요가) has only the morning 08:00–09:30 on Sat·Sun. Yoga Project Hannam (요가프로젝트 한남) has regular Sat·Sun hours in its Kakao Map notice.
