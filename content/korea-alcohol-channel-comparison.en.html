Foreigner's Guide: Korean Pubs vs. Makgeolli Houses vs. Izakayas vs. Bars — Where to Drink Alcohol in Korea?

> Subtitle: Korean Alcohol 4-Channel Comparison Matrix + Top Pick for 5 Segments + Guide to Deciding by Time, Budget, and Company

ISSUE 03 · #830 · 2026-06-08

🎯 Why a 4-Channel Alcohol Comparison Guide?

Even if you know the [#828 Korean Alcohol 6-Type Matrix](828_korean_alcohol_6_type_matrix.md) and [#829 Workflow](829_korean_alcohol_ordering_workflow.md), channel selection is the next decision. Korean alcohol venue channels are: (1) Korean Pubs/Pojangmacha (한국 주점·포차) (Soju/소주, Maekju/맥주, BBQ Anju/안주), (2) Makgeolli Houses/Traditional Pubs (막걸리집·전통주점) (Traditional Makgeolli/막걸리, Jeon/전, Nokdujeon/녹두전), (3) Japanese Izakaya (일식 이자카야) (Sake/사케, Highball/하이볼, Kkochi/꼬치), and (4) MZ Bars/Highball Bars (MZ 바·하이볼 바) (Whiskey, MZ Trends). Prices range from ₩15,000 (~$11 / ~80 RMB / ~1,600 JPY) to ₩100,000+, with differences in atmosphere for hoeshik (회식, Korean company dinner/gathering), traditional, late-night, and trendy vibes. This guide directly compares the four channels + ranks them for each segment (solo, couple, family, MZ, budget-conscious). This applies the hoe (회, sliced raw fish), BBQ (Korean barbecue), gukbap (국밥, Korean soup with rice), bunsik (분식, Korean snack foods), myeonryu (면류, noodle dishes), chicken, dessert, kimchi (김치, fermented cabbage), boyangsik (보양식, nourishing health foods) cluster pattern to alcohol.

🎯 Top Pick by Segment

👤 Solo Traveler (Solo)

  • 1st Pick: MZ Bars/Highball Bars (MZ 바·하이볼 바) — Solo walk-ins OK, English/MZ, ₩25,000~50,000

  • 3rd Pick: Japanese Izakaya (일식 이자카야) (authentic)
  • Avoid: Korean Pubs (한국 주점) (less suitable for hoeshik (회식) atmosphere)

  • 2nd Pick: Japanese Izakaya (일식 이자카야) (quiet venue for lunch)
  • 3rd Pick: Makgeolli House (막걸리집) (traditional Hanok (한옥) atmosphere)

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "4 channels = similar": Atmosphere, menu, and prices are completely different. Hoeshik (회식) = Pub / Traditional = Makgeolli House (막걸리집) / Japanese = Izakaya (이자카야) / MZ = Bar.

2. "Korean Pubs (한국 주점) = No Foreigners": Many foreigner-friendly Korean Pubs (한국 주점) in Hongdae (홍대)/Itaewon (이태원). Some English menus.

3. "Makgeolli Houses (막걸리집) = Only Traditional": Hanok (한옥) Makgeolli Houses (막걸리집) in Insadong (인사동)/Ikseondong (익선동) = Traditional + MZ Fusion (milk Makgeolli/막걸리, peach Makgeolli/막걸리).

4. "Izakaya (이자카야) = Only Japanese": Korean Izakaya (이자카야) = Many Korean chefs/ingredients + Japanese style (Japanese cuisine in Korea).

5. "MZ Bars (MZ 바) = Only Expensive": Highball Bars (하이볼 바) from ₩25,000 per person = English menus, trendy, Instagrammable, full set for value.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. First alcohol venue = which channel?: MZ Bars/Highball Bars (MZ 바·하이볼 바) are No. 1 (English, trendy). For authentic Korean = Makgeolli House (막걸리집).

2. Instagram/Vlog: MZ Bars (MZ 바) (Highball/하이볼), Makgeolli Houses (막걸리집) (Hanok (한옥) vlog), Izakaya (이자카야) (authentic Japanese).

3. Hoeshik (회식)/Business Entertainment: Korean Pubs/Pojangmacha (한국 주점·포차) (ilcha (일차, the first round of a Korean drinking/dining outing)) + Japanese Izakaya (일식 이자카야) (formal entertainment) + Hotel Izakaya (이자카야) (luxury).

4. Date/Anniversary: Makgeolli House (막걸리집) Hanok (한옥) (traditional) + MZ Bars (MZ 바) (trendy) + Izakaya (이자카야) (formal).

5. Family-Friendly: Korean Pub (한국 주점) family Hof (호프) venues + Japanese Izakaya (일식 이자카야) lunch.

6. Allergies/Pregnancy: Refer to [#828/#829 disclaimer](828_korean_alcohol_6_type_matrix.md). Pregnant individuals = No alcohol.

7. Payment/Tips: Korea = No tips / Hotels = Some service charge / Card OK / Kakao Pay (카카오페이)/Toss (토스) transfer.

⚠ Medical, Safety, Legal Disclaimer

Korean alcohol = alcoholic. Drunk driving 0.03% BAC (Blood Alcohol Content) results in license suspension + criminal penalties. Pregnant individuals/those on medication = No alcohol. Under 19 years old = legally prohibited from drinking alcohol. Declare allergies (gluten, soy, fruit, nuts) in advance. In case of suspected food poisoning or excessive drinking, call ☎ 1339 (medical, English). This guide provides general information, not medical advice.

Nofo (노포)/Venue Information Disclaimer: Venue operating hours/menu changes — check NAVER (네이버)/Kakao Map (카카오맵) or official website before visiting.

Dietary Restrictions Fallback:

  • Halal/Kosher: Alcohol = non-Halal/non-Kosher. → Non-alcoholic options (Sikhye/식혜, Sujeonggwa/수정과, non-alcoholic beer).
  • Sikhye (식혜, a sweet Korean rice beverage)
  • Sujeonggwa (수정과, a traditional Korean cinnamon punch)
  • Non-alcoholic: Sikhye (식혜), Sujeonggwa (수정과), Chrysanthemum tea + non-alcoholic beer, non-alcoholic Cheongju (청주) + MZ Bar (MZ 바) non-alcoholic cocktails.
