2-week course for foreigners’ first visit — Korea Nationwide Full Set Mania

> Subtitle: 14-day full set — Seoul + Busan + Jeju + Gangneung + Jeonju + Gyeongju No. 1 foreign enthusiasts

ISSUE 03 · #267 · 2026-05-20

🎯 Why 2-week course for foreigners

Foreign mania deep dive 1st place = 13 nights and 14 days (long-distance foreigner + retirement·sabbatical·workation). Full set across Korea (Seoul + Busan + 4 outlying cities) + No. 1 foreign enthusiast.

★ 2 week full set matrix

Week 1 — Seoul deep dive (Day 1~7)

  • Day 1 Arrival + Myeongdong·N Seoul Tower
  • Day 2 Palace, Hanok, Bukchon
  • Day 3 Hongdae, Yeonnam, Yeonhui
  • Day 4 Gangnam·K-pop·HYBE
  • Day 5 Jamsil·Lotte World·Seokchon
  • Day 6 Han River and Noryangjin Fisheries
  • Day 7 Duty Free, Itaewon, Rest

Week 2 — Busan + outlying cities (Day 8~14)

  • Day 8 KTX Seoul → Busan + Haeundae · Gwangalli
  • Day 9 Busan Jagalchi, Gamcheon, Seomyeon
  • Day 10 KTX Busan→Gyeongju (#256) + Bulguksa Temple·Seokguram Grotto
  • Day 11 Gyeongju + Andong Hahoe Village (outskirts)
  • Day 12 KTX Gyeongju → Jeonju (#255) + Hanok·Bibimbap
  • Day 13 LCC or KTX Gangneung (#254) + Anmok·Jeongdongjin
  • Day 14 KTX Gangneung → Seoul + Departure from Incheon Airport

Or Alternative — Add Jeju (Day 11~12)

  • Day 11~12 Gimpo → Jeju LCC (#253) + Seongsan, Hallasan, Udo

⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make in 2 weeks

1. "2 weeks = All cities OK": 2 weeks = 7 days in Seoul + Busan + 4 outlying cities Full set. 5+ The city is too tight.

2. "2 weeks = too long": 2 weeks = No. 1 foreign enthusiasts + Deep dive nationwide in Korea + No. 1 user satisfaction.

3. "2 weeks = expensive": 2 weeks = Hotel, transportation, food ~$3000~5000 standard + No. 1 cost-effectiveness for foreign enthusiasts.

Variant 2 weeks

  • A standard (this guide): 7 days in Seoul + 3 days and 2 nights in Busan + 4 outlying cities
  • B Mania Full Set: 5 days in Seoul + 3 days in Busan + 4 days in Jeju + 2 days outside
  • C K-pop·K-drama: 10 days in Seoul K-pop·K-drama deep dive + 2 days in Busan + Gangneung (#254 sunrise)
  • D Family·Children (#232): 8 days for Seoul family + 3 days for Busan family + 3 days for Jeju family
  • E Food·Culture Mania: Seoul + Jeonju (#255 Hanjeongsik·Bibimbap) + Gyeongju + Busan
