First-Time Visitor's 2-Week Itinerary — Korea Full Set Enthusiast

Subtitle: 14-Day Full Set — Seoul + Busan + Jeju + Gangneung + Jeonju + Gyeongju: The #1 Spot for Foreign Enthusiasts

ISSUE 03 · #267 · 2026-05-20

🎯 Why a 2-Week Itinerary for Foreign Visitors?

The top choice for foreign enthusiasts looking for a deep dive = 13 nights, 14 days (perfect for long-haul travelers + those on retirement, sabbatical, or workation). It's a full set of Korea (Seoul + Busan + 4 provincial cities) + the #1 spot for foreign enthusiasts.

★ 2-Week Full Set Matrix

Week 1 — Seoul deep dive (Day 1~7)

  • Day 1 Arrival + Myeongdong (명동) · N Seoul Tower (N서울타워)
  • Day 2 Palaces · hanok (한옥, traditional Korean house) · Bukchon (북촌)
  • Day 3 Hongdae (홍대) · Yeonnam (연남) · Yeonhui (연희)
  • Day 4 Gangnam (강남) · K-pop · HYBE
  • Day 5 Jamsil (잠실) · Lotte World (롯데월드) · Seokchon (석촌)
  • Day 6 Han River (한강) · Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진수산시장)
  • Day 7 Duty-Free · Itaewon (이태원) · Rest

Week 2 — Busan + Provincial Cities (Day 8~14)

  • Day 8 KTX Seoul (서울) → Busan (부산) + Haeundae (해운대) · Gwangalli (광안리)
  • Day 9 Busan (부산) Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장) · Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을) · Seomyeon (서면)
  • Day 10 KTX Busan (부산) → Gyeongju (경주) (#256) + Bulguksa Temple (불국사) · Seokguram Grotto (석굴암)
  • Day 11 Gyeongju (경주) + Andong Hahoe Folk Village (안동 하회마을) (Provincial)
  • Day 12 KTX Gyeongju (경주) → Jeonju (전주) (#255) + hanok (한옥) · Bibimbap (비빔밥, mixed rice with vegetables)
  • Day 13 LCC or KTX Gangneung (강릉) (#254) + Anmok Beach (안목해변) · Jeongdongjin (정동진)
  • Day 14 KTX Gangneung (강릉) → Seoul (서울) + Incheon Airport (인천공항) Departure

Or Alternative — Add Jeju (Day 11~12)

  • Day 11~12 Gimpo (김포) → Jeju (제주) LCC (#253) + Seongsan Ilchulbong Peak (성산일출봉) · Hallasan Mountain (한라산) · Udo Island (우도)

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make During a 2-Week Trip

1. "2 weeks = all cities OK": 2 weeks = 7 days in Seoul (서울) + Busan (부산) + 4 provincial cities for a full set. 5+ cities is too packed.

2. "2 weeks = too long": 2 weeks = the #1 spot for foreign enthusiasts + a deep dive into all of Korea + top user satisfaction.

3. "2 weeks = expensive": 2 weeks = ~$3000~5000 (approx. ₩4,000,000-6,700,000) standard for hotels, transportation, and food + great value for money at the #1 spot for foreign enthusiasts.

2-Week Variants

  • A Standard (This Guide): 7 days in Seoul (서울) + 2 nights/3 days in Busan (부산) + 4 provincial cities
  • B Enthusiast Full Set: 5 days in Seoul (서울) + 3 days in Busan (부산) + 4 days in Jeju (제주) + 2 provincial days
  • C K-pop·K-drama: 10-day K-pop·K-drama deep dive in Seoul (서울) + 2 days in Busan (부산) + Gangneung (강릉) (#254 for sunrise)
  • D Family·Children (#232): 8 family days in Seoul (서울) + 3 family days in Busan (부산) + 3 family days in Jeju (제주)
  • E Food·Culture Enthusiast: Seoul (서울) + Jeonju (전주) (#255 for hanjeongsik (한정식, full-course Korean meal) · bibimbap (비빔밥)) + Gyeongju (경주) + Busan (부산)
