🎯 Why a Gangneung Guide?

Gangneung (강릉) = Korea's #1 Coffee City · Anmok Beach (안목해변) = Korea's #1 Coffee Street (40+ Cafes) · Gyeongpodae (경포대) · Jeongdongjin (정동진) Sunrise · Chodang Tofu (초당두부) · Gangneung Hanjeongsik (강릉 한정식, Korean traditional set meal). Foreigner Pain Points = (a) Misconception: "Gangneung (강릉) = just a regular beach" (b) Korea's #1 Coffee City, with signature cafes like Terarosa (테라로사) and Bohemian (보헤미안) (c) Gyeongpodae (경포대) = #3 of Gwandong Palgyeong (관동팔경, Eight Scenic Views of Gwandong) (d) Jeongdongjin (정동진) = Korea's #1 Sunrise Spot (after Ganjeolgot (간절곶)) (e) Chodang Tofu (초당두부) = Gangneung's (강릉) unique signature (f) KTX (Korea Train eXpress) 1 hour 56 minutes.

This guide covers 5 Pillars, 12 Signatures + Anmok Beach (안목해변) + Gyeongpodae (경포대) + Jeongdongjin (정동진).
