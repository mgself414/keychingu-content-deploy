⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Seeing Itaewon as "clubs and bars only" — and skipping the dining scene

The most frequent mistake among foreign visitors. Many perceive Itaewon purely as a nightlife zone — clubs, bars, and street food — and completely miss the modern dining and global restaurants in Hannam (한남) and Sinyongsan (신용산). In reality, Hannam (한남) is Korea's #1 global fine dining destination, densely packed with hotel dining venues, Michelin-listed restaurants, and modern Western cuisine. Pair nightlife with a proper dinner crawl and you unlock twice the Itaewon experience.

2. Treating Itaewon, Hannam, and Sinyongsan as three separate neighborhoods

Foreign visitors tend to fixate on Itaewon Station (이태원역), but 50% of top dining venues are concentrated in Hannam (한남) and Sinyongsan (신용산). All are within a 10–15 minute walk from Itaewon Station (이태원역), Hangangjin Station (한강진역), or Sinyongsan Station (신용산역). Combining them into a single route makes a 4–5 restaurant dining crawl entirely manageable. The ideal foreign visitor itinerary: Itaewon (이태원) → Hannam via Hangangjin Station (한강진역) → Sinyongsan (신용산) → Yongsan 4-dong (용산 4동) in one day.

3. Not accounting for reservations at hotel dining venues and view restaurants

Mondrian Blind Spot (몬드리안서울이태원 블라인드 스팟), Hankuk N Seoul Tower (한쿡 N서울타워점), Cliff Seoul (클리프 서울), and other hotel or view venues require reservations 2–4 weeks in advance. Walk-ins will almost certainly be turned away. Book via OpenTable, hotel concierge, or Naver Reservations (네이버 예약) well ahead of your visit. Venues with English reservations available: Mondrian Blind Spot, Hankuk N Seoul Tower, Cliff Seoul, Casa Corona (카사 코로나), Domanda Sinyongsan (도만다 신용산점), and Ugly Society (어글리소사이어티) — 6 venues total.

The Dining Landscape of Itaewon, Hannam & Sinyongsan

The Itaewon (이태원) · Hannam (한남) · Sinyongsan (신용산) area has Korea's highest concentration of foreign residents — a legacy shaped by the nearby US military base (Yongsan / 용산), global corporate headquarters, and a cluster of foreign embassies. Since the late 2010s, it has cemented its place as Korea's #1 zone for global dining, modern Western cuisine, and hotel fine dining.

Itaewon proper (이태원 본권) is the heartland of global dining — Mexican (Casa Corona / 카사 코로나), international pub (Korea Hope / 코리아 호프), and modern Western cuisine (Soul / 소울, Ugly Society / 어글리소사이어티, The Place Dining / 더플레이스다이닝) are all clustered within a 10-minute walk of Itaewon Station (이태원역). Five or more venues here carry a 4.7+ rating.

Hannam-dong (한남동) is the luxury and Michelin territory — the stretch around Hangangjin Station (한강진역) and Hannamdae-ro (한남대로) is home to upscale grills (N Grill / 엔그릴, Cliff Seoul / 클리프 서울, S.caf) and the Leeum Museum café (Camel Coffee Leeum / 카멜커피 리움미술관점). It's the #1 choice for foreign visitors seeking luxury dining.

Sinyongsan (신용산) & Yongsan 4-dong (용산 4동) represent the emerging global dining scene — modern dining and global venues like Domanda Sinyongsan (도만다 신용산점) are rising around Sinyongsan Station (신용산역) and Yongsan Station (용산역), integrating naturally with Yongsan's business and tourism traffic.

Namsan (남산) is the view dining territory — Hankuk N Seoul Tower (한쿡 N서울타워점) stands as the signature view dining destination, best combined with a Namsan stroll.

① Global & International (3 Venues)

1. Domanda Sinyongsan (도만다 신용산점) ★4.72

  • Address: 55 Hangang-daero 23-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Sinyongsan's global dining destination — international menu with a modern atmosphere. A 3-minute walk from Sinyongsan Station (신용산역), this is one of the hottest new spots in the area.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — English menu, direct access from Sinyongsan Station (신용산역), foreign cards accepted, 3 foreign-language reviews
  • Recommended Items: Signature global tasting menu, seasonal dishes, wine pairing
  • Price Range: ₩40,000–70,000 per person (~$29–50 / ~215–380 RMB / ~3,870–6,770 JPY)
  • Tip: Reserve 2 weeks ahead. Best for business dinners and special occasions in Sinyongsan (신용산). Convenient for foreign visitors traveling through Yongsan Station (용산역) by KTX.

2. Korea Hope (코리아 호프) ★4.68

  • Address: 142 Itaewon-ro, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Itaewon's international pub. Global beers and casual pub fare — a genuine gathering point for the foreign community.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English menu, 5-minute walk from Itaewon Station (이태원역), 5 foreign-language reviews
  • Recommended Items: Global draft beer selection, pub snacks, seasonal anju (안주 — Korean drinking snacks)
  • Price Range: ₩20,000–35,000 per person (~$14–25 / ~110–190 RMB / ~1,930–3,380 JPY)
  • Tip: Walk-ins welcome. Great for casual outings with international friends. Works on both weekdays and weekends.

3. Casa Corona (카사 코로나) ★4.62

  • Address: 23 Itaewon-ro 27ga-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Authentic Mexican dining in Itaewon. True-to-form Mexican dishes that feel instantly familiar to visitors from the US and Latin America.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English menu, on the main Itaewon foreign visitor circuit, 5 foreign-language reviews
  • Recommended Items: Signature Mexican dishes, tacos, margaritas
  • Price Range: ₩25,000–45,000 per person
  • Tip: Walk-ins welcome. An essential stop on any Itaewon global dining crawl. Open for lunch and dinner on weekdays.
