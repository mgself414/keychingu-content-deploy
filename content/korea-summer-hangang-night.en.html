Han River Night Views Guide — Banpo Moonlight Rainbow Fountain, Cruises, Hangang Bus, Some Sevit and Nodeul Island, Official Info as of October 2026

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Han River night views are not just a summer thing. In October 2026 the Banpo Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (반포 달빛무지개분수) still runs 3 shows every night, cruises still depart at 22:00, the Hangang Bus (한강버스) runs on 2 lines (east and west), and Nodeul Island (노들섬) hosts a light festival through 10/11. The times and fares below are only the values read on 2026-10-08 from Seoul city notices and the operators' official pages. The Hangang Project Headquarters site body would not open, so for the fountain and pools we substituted the official Seoul city notice, and the Hangang Bus fare is given as two figures (₩2,500 vs ₩3,000) because the official sources disagree. Some values from the older guide no longer match and were dropped: "world #1" for the fountain (now the 2008 Guinness 'longest bridge fountain' at 1,140 m), "cruise ₩15,000–₩25,000", and "fountain at 12, 21 and 21:30". The summer pools (6/19–8/30) and the Seoul International Fireworks Festival (9/5) are over.

⛲ Banpo Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (반포 달빛무지개분수) — 3 Shows a Night

  • The fountain sprays from both sides of Banpo Bridge and holds the 2008 Guinness record for the 'world's longest bridge fountain' (1,140 m) (Seoul city). We use this wording instead of the older guide's "world #1".
  • In 2026 it was cut to 3 shows a day to save energy (19:30, 20:00 and 20:30, 20 minutes each) (Seoul city notice 2026-06-01). Whether it runs in October, the season end date, and the cancellation criteria such as wind are not officially posted — check the Hangang Project Headquarters notice before you go.
  • The park is free and open 24 hours. Take Line 9 Sinbanpo Station (신반포역) Exit 1, or Express Bus Terminal Station (고속터미널역) Exits 8-1 and 8-2.

🚢 Cruises and the Hangang Bus (한강버스) — On the Water

  • E-Land Cruise (이랜드크루즈) (Yeouido pier, 290 Yeoui-dong-ro): Sunset 18:00 · Moonlight Music 19:00 · Starlight 22:00 · Fireworks Music 19:30 (designated days). Fares: Tour ₩19,900 / Sunset and Starlight ₩25,900 / Moonlight ₩32,900 / Fireworks ₩45,900 (official).
  • Hangang Bus (한강버스): Resumed 2026-03-01. East line Jamsil–Yeouido, west line Magok–Yeouido, 7 piers (+ Seoul Forest (서울숲) through October). The official sources split on the adult fare, ₩2,500 versus ₩3,000, so confirm before boarding. The last night run is also conflicting across sources (east line arrival 20:27 vs 22:30) — for a night ride, use the timetable of the day.

🏝 Some Sevit, Nodeul Island and Seouldal

  • Some Sevit (세빛섬) (Banpo): Restaurants and a rooftop bar (official). Hours and entry fee are not officially posted.
  • Nodeul Island (노들섬): Seoul Light Hangang Bitseom Festival (서울라이트 한강 빛섬축제), 10/2–10/11, 18:30–22:30, with a free shuttle and a special cruise from Yeouido (Seoul city). A 1.5 km waterside walkway opened 9/18.
  • Seouldal (서울달): Aug–Oct Sun–Thu 12:00–22:00, Fri and Sat 12:00–23:00 (Seoul city). The 2026 fare and any Monday closure are not posted.

🍗 Yeouido — Chimaek and Ramyeon Culture

  • VisitSeoul names chimaek (치맥) (fried chicken and beer) and convenience-store instant ramyeon as the "must eat" at Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도한강공원) (official). Take Yeouinaru Station (여의나루역) Exits 2 and 3. Station and exit details for the Ttukseom (뚝섬), Mangwon (망원) and Jamwon (잠원) parks are not officially posted.

🏊 Summer Things — Over for This Year

  • Han River pools and water-play areas 2026: 6/19–8/30, night opening until 22:00 from 7/3, pools ₩3,000/₩4,000/₩5,000 and water-play areas ₩1,000/₩2,000/₩3,000, Jamwon (잠원) not operating (Seoul city). Next year's notice comes in June.
  • The Seoul International Fireworks Festival (서울세계불꽃축제) 2026 ended on 9/5 at Yeouido.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Waiting for the 21:00 fountain show — in 2026 it is 3 shows at 19:30, 20:00 and 20:30.

2. Budgeting ₩20,000 for the cruise — Moonlight is ₩32,900 and Fireworks is ₩45,900.

3. Assuming one Hangang Bus fare — ₩2,500 vs ₩3,000 conflict. Confirm before boarding.

4. Looking for the pools or fireworks in October — they are over. Go to the Nodeul Island light festival (through 10/11) instead.

5. Returning by Hangang Bus at night — last-run times conflict across sources. Treat the subway as your default.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. A one-evening course? — Yeouido chimaek (Yeouinaru Exit 2) → cruise at 19:00 or 22:00 → or the Banpo fountain 19:30–20:50.

2. Is the fountain free? — Free. The park is open 24 hours.

3. Where do I board the cruise? — Yeouido pier (290 Yeoui-dong-ro).

4. How far does the Hangang Bus go? — Jamsil–Yeouido and Magok–Yeouido. Seoul Forest through October.

5. What festival is on in October? — Nodeul Island light festival, 10/2–11, 18:30–22:30, free.

6. Until what time does Seouldal run? — 23:00 on Fri and Sat, 22:00 otherwise (Aug–Oct).
