Chuncheon Full Guide — Lakeside City of Gangwon-do, Dakgalbi, Nami Island

> Subtitle: Dakgalbi, Nami Island, Soyanggang River, Uiamho Lake, Kim Yu-jeong Literary Village, Gangchon + For Foreign Outskirt Enthusiasts

ISSUE 03 · #327 · 2026-05-20

🎯 Why a Chuncheon Full Guide?

Chuncheon (춘천) = Lakeside City of Gangwon-do (강원도) + Korea's #1 Dakgalbi (닭갈비) (spicy stir-fried chicken) (1 hr 10 min by ITX Cheongchun (ITX 청춘) / 1 hr by KTX). It's the top spot for foreign K-drama enthusiasts (Winter Sonata (겨울연가) at Nami Island (남이섬)) + a prime destination for foreign outskirt lovers.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Chuncheon (춘천) = A city in Gangwon-do (강원도)": Chuncheon (춘천) = The provincial capital of Gangwon-do (강원도) + a lakeside city + a destination for foreign outskirt enthusiasts.

2. "Nami Island (남이섬) = Only for Winter Sonata (겨울연가)": Nami Island (남이섬) = The #1 spot for foreign K-drama enthusiasts + integrated with Gapyeong Jaraseom (가평 자라섬) and Cheongpyeong (청평).

3. "Chuncheon Dakgalbi (춘천 닭갈비) = Anywhere": Chuncheon Myeongdong Dakgalbi Alley (춘천 명동 닭갈비 골목) = The birthplace of Korea's #1 Dakgalbi (닭갈비) + a spot for foreign enthusiasts.
