⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Hallasan (한라산) = just a regular mountain": Korea's #1·1947m·UNESCO.

2. "Baeknokdam (백록담) = just a regular lake": Summit crater lake, a #1 must-see·Winter enthusiast #1 experience.

3. "Witseoreum (윗세오름) = the summit": Witseoreum (윗세오름) = 1700m + experience·Summit 1947m = enthusiast experience.

4. "Winter hiking = only for enthusiasts": Winter Baeknokdam (백록담) = enthusiast #1 experience·Crampons (아이젠) essential.

5. "All 4 courses are the same": Yeongsil (영실)·Witseoreum (윗세오름)·Seongpanak (성판악)·Gwaneumsa (관음사) = completely different experiences.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Top priority for foreign beginners: Yeongsil (영실)·Witseoreum (윗세오름) = 4-5h #1 experience.

2. Summit (Baeknokdam (백록담)): Seongpanak (성판악)·Gwaneumsa (관음사) = 8-10h·enthusiast experience.

3. Winter (December-February): Crampons (아이젠) essential·Winter clothing·enthusiast #1 experience.

4. Advance reservation: Summit courses = online advance reservation essential·1-2 weeks in advance.

5. Seasons: Spring (azaleas)·Autumn (autumn foliage)·Winter (Baeknokdam (백록담)) #1 experience.

6. Recommended persona: Families·Seniors = Yeongsil (영실) + Witseoreum (윗세오름) / MZ·Enthusiasts = Summit + Photos / Enthusiasts·Hiking = Summit + 1-night stay.

⚠ Medical·Safety Disclaimer

Hallasan (한라산) hiking = Pregnant individuals·Heart patients·Hypertension patients should consult a doctor·Crampons (아이젠) + winter clothing essential in winter. Children = Yeongsil (영실)·Witseoreum (윗세오름) OK·Summit NOT recommended. Emergency ☎ 1339 (Medical)·1330 (Tourism)·119 (Emergency Services).
