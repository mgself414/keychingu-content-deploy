7 Hallasan Trails — Distance and Time by Course, Seasonal Entry Cutoffs, Summit Reservation, and the Bus There

Subtitle: For travelers walking Hallasan for the first time, this guide groups 7 trails in one place: Seongpanak, Gwaneumsa, Eorimok, Yeongsil, Donnaeko, Eoseungsaengak and Seokguram. The two trails to the summit, Baengnokdam, are Seongpanak and Gwaneumsa, and the summit section of both needs a reservation. In October–March (months 10–3), you must pass the Seongpanak and Gwaneumsa entrances by 11:30, the Eorimok and Yeongsil checkpoints by 12:00, and Donnaeko by 10:00. You reserve by choosing an entry time slot (05:00–08:00 or 08:01–11:30). Entry starts at 05:00. Reservations for the next month open at 09:00 on the first working day of each month. To Seongpanak, bus 281 runs from Jeju Bus Terminal and express bus 181 from Jeju International Airport. Bus 240 goes to Eorimok and Yeongsil. For a short walk, Eoseungsaengak is 1.3 km, about 30 minutes one way. The values were read on 2026-11-25.

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 7

This guide is made for foreign travelers who want to walk Hallasan (한라산) for a day on Jeju. It is written so that you can match the course, times, reservation and bus before you leave, even if you do not know Korean.

Hallasan is managed not by Korea National Park Service (국립공원공단) but by Jeju Special Self-Governing Province (제주특별자치도). So it has its own reservation website and its own entry-hours table. There are 7 trails.

  • Trails to the summit (Baengnokdam (백록담)): Seongpanak (성판악) and Gwaneumsa (관음사). The summit section needs a reservation.
  • Trails to Witse Oreum (윗세오름) and Nambyeok Junction (남벽분기점): Eorimok (어리목) and Yeongsil (영실). Walked without a reservation.
  • Trail to Nambyeok Junction: Donnaeko (돈내코). Walked without a reservation.
  • Short trails of about 1 hour or less: Eoseungsaengak (어승생악) and Seokguram (석굴암). Walked without a reservation.

You cannot enter Hallasan at any time you like. Each trail has a time by which you must pass the entrance, and a time at which you must start coming down. In winter those times move earlier. So for each course this guide first writes down "by what time you must pass the entrance".

⚠ Notation principle: Distances, times, closure times, fares and directions are values read on 2026-11-25. Sources are the Hallasan trail reservation website of Jeju Special Self-Governing Province (visithalla.jeju.go.kr) and the Hallasan National Park (한라산국립공원) website of Jeju Special Self-Governing Province (jeju.go.kr/hallasan). Hallasan counts October–March (months 10–3) as the winter season and April–September (months 4–9) as the summer season. November falls in the winter season, so the winter values come first and the summer values are included in the tables. Closure times, reservation rules, fares and bus service can change.

📋 3 Things to Decide First

1. Will You Go to the Summit?

There are two trails to the summit, Baengnokdam: the Seongpanak course and the Gwaneumsa course. According to the official course guide of the Hallasan trail reservation website (read 2026-11-25), Seongpanak is 9 hours round trip and Gwaneumsa is 10 hours round trip. It is a hike that uses the whole day.

The other five courses do not reach the summit. Eorimok and Yeongsil go past Witse Oreum to Nambyeok Junction. Donnaeko also goes to Nambyeok Junction. The website describes Yeongsil as "a trail that cannot reach Baengnokdam (the summit)".

If the summit is not your goal, walk to Witse Oreum from Yeongsil or Eorimok. If you have only 1 hour, walk Eoseungsaengak. This is this guide's own advice.

2. By What Time Can You Pass the Entrance?

Entry is from 05:00 in both winter and summer. If you pass the times below, you cannot go up past that point. There are also times at which you must start coming down.

3. Have You Reserved?

Two sections need a reservation: the Jindallaebat to Baengnokdam section of the Seongpanak course and the Samgakbong to Baengnokdam section of the Gwaneumsa course. When you reserve, you choose an entry time slot. There are two slots, 05:00–08:00 and 08:01–11:30. You pass the entrance within the slot you reserved. If you cannot go, cancel before that slot ends. How to reserve is written in the "How to Reserve the Summit" section below.

The English notice on the Hallasan trail reservation website (2025-05-12) says that Eorimok, Yeongsil, Donnaeko, Eoseungsaengak and Seokguram are open without a reservation.

① Seongpanak Trail

  • What is different: one of the two trails to the summit. According to the Hallasan National Park website of Jeju Special Self-Governing Province (read 2026-11-25), the Seongpanak office (성판악 관리사무실) is at an altitude of 750 m.
  • Distance and time: 9.6 km according to the official course guide of the Hallasan trail reservation website (read 2026-11-25). To the summit it is 4 hours 30 minutes one way and 9 hours round trip. To Jindallaebat it is 3 hours one way. The Hallasan National Park website writes 5 hours to the summit on the same trail.
  • Difficulty: C (easy) from the visitor center (탐방안내소) to Saraak Spring (사라악샘), B (moderate) from Saraak Spring to Jindallaebat, and A (hard) from Jindallaebat to Baengnokdam.
  • Facilities on the trail: Sokbat Shelter (속밭대피소) is unstaffed and Jindallaebat Shelter is staffed. There are toilets at the Seongpanak office, Sokbat Shelter and Jindallaebat Shelter. There is no shop along the whole route. Sara Oreum Observatory (사라오름전망대) is 600 m off the trail, at the 5.8 km point.
  • Winter (October–March, months 10–3) closure times: you must pass the trail entrance by 11:30 and Jindallaebat by 11:30. At the summit (East Ridge) you start coming down at 13:30, and at Jindallaebat at 15:30.
  • Summer (April–September, months 4–9) closure times: the trail entrance and Jindallaebat by 12:30. At the summit (East Ridge) you start coming down at 14:30, and at Jindallaebat at 16:30.
  • Reservation time slots: for the summit section you choose an entry time slot (05:00–08:00 or 08:01–11:30) when you reserve. You pass the entrance within the slot you reserved. If you cannot go, cancel before that slot ends.
  • How to get there: from Jeju Bus Terminal (제주버스터미널), take bus 281. Bus 181 is an express bus from Jeju International Airport (제주국제공항) and takes about 35 minutes counted from the airport stop (Hallasan trail reservation website notice 2026-06-05). Bus 281 takes about 45 minutes from Jeju Bus Terminal and about 50 minutes from Seogwipo Bus Terminal (서귀포버스터미널). The first bus is 06:00 and the last bus is 22:00 (Hallasan trail reservation website notice 2026-06-05). The address is 1865 516-ro, Jocheon-eup, Jeju-si (제주시 조천읍 516로 1865).
  • Parking: the Seongpanak parking lot holds at most 156 cars. You can park at the free transfer parking lot (199 cars) near Jeju International University (제주국제대학교) and take bus 281. Roadside parking is subject to enforcement (Hallasan trail reservation website notice 2026-06-05).
  • When to leave: the round trip is 9 hours and the winter entrance cutoff is 11:30. The first 281 bus is at 06:00, so an entry at 05:00 cannot be met by bus. If you go by bus, reserve the 08:01–11:30 slot. This is this guide's own advice.
  • Inquiries: Seongpanak Branch (성판악지소) 064-725-9950.

② Gwaneumsa Trail

  • What is different: the other of the two trails to the summit. Compared with Seongpanak, the distance is shorter and the time is longer.
  • Distance and time: 8.7 km according to the official course guide of the Hallasan trail reservation website (read 2026-11-25). From Gwaneumsa District Campground (관음사지구야영장) to Samgakbong Shelter (삼각봉대피소) it is 6 km, and from Samgakbong Shelter to the summit (East Ridge (동능)) it is 2.7 km. To Samgakbong Shelter it is 3 hours 20 minutes one way, to the summit 5 hours one way, and 10 hours round trip.
  • Winter (October–March, months 10–3) closure times: you must pass the trail entrance by 11:30 and Samgakbong by 11:30. At the summit (East Ridge) you start coming down at 13:30, and at Samgakbong Shelter at 15:30.
  • Summer (April–September, months 4–9) closure times: the trail entrance and Samgakbong by 12:30. At the summit (East Ridge) you start coming down at 14:30, and at Samgakbong Shelter at 16:30.
  • Reservation time slots: for the summit section you choose an entry time slot (05:00–08:00 or 08:01–11:30) when you reserve. You pass the entrance within the slot you reserved. If you cannot go, cancel before that slot ends.
  • How to get there: according to the Hallasan National Park website (read 2026-11-25), take bus 281 from Jeju City Intercity Bus Terminal (제주시 시외버스터미널) and get off at "Jeju Daehakgyo (제주대학교)". Transfer to bus 475, ride about 15 minutes, and get off at "Gwaneumsa Deungsanro Ipgu (관음사 등산로 입구)". The address is 588 Sanrokbuk-ro, Jeju-si (제주시 산록북로 588).
  • Camping and cooking: the only place on Hallasan where you can camp and cook is the Gwaneumsa District Campground.
  • When to leave: the round trip is 10 hours and the winter entrance cutoff is 11:30. Because you change buses once, leave your lodging earlier than for Seongpanak. This is this guide's own advice.
  • Inquiries: Gwaneumsa Branch (관음사지소) 064-756-9950.

③ Eorimok Trail

  • What is different: a trail to Witse Oreum and Nambyeok Junction that you walk without a reservation. It does not reach the summit. According to the Hallasan National Park website of Jeju Special Self-Governing Province (read 2026-11-25), the entrance is at an altitude of 970 m and Witse Oreum Shelter (윗세오름대피소) is at 1,700 m.
  • Distance and time: 6.8 km in total according to the Hallasan National Park website. From Eorimok (어리목) to Witse Oreum it is 4.7 km, and from Witse Oreum to Nambyeok Junction it is 2.1 km. To Witse Oreum it is 2 hours and to Nambyeok Junction 3 hours one way.
  • Difficulty: C (easy) from the visitor center (탐방안내소) to Eorimok Valley (어리목계곡), A (hard) from Eorimok Valley to Sajebi Dongsan (사제비동산), B (moderate) from Sajebi Dongsan to Mansedongsan (만세동산), C from Mansedongsan to Witse Oreum Shelter, and C from Witse Oreum Shelter to Nambyeok Junction.
  • Altitudes on the trail: Sajebi Dongsan is 1,423 m, Mansedongsan is 1,606 m, and Nambyeok Junction is 1,600 m.
  • Facilities on the trail: Witse Oreum Shelter is staffed. There is no shop along the whole route. A vending machine at the entrance sells water and drinks. There are toilets at Eorimok Plaza (어리목광장) and Witse Oreum Shelter. Sajebi Spring (사제비샘) can dry up in a dry spell.
  • Winter (October–March, months 10–3) closure times: you must pass the trail checkpoint by 12:00. From Witse Oreum toward Nambyeok you must pass by 13:00. At Nambyeok Junction you start coming down at 14:00, and at Witse Oreum at 15:00.
  • Summer (April–September, months 4–9) closure times: trail checkpoint 15:00, and from Witse Oreum toward Nambyeok 14:00. At Nambyeok Junction you start coming down at 15:00, and at Witse Oreum at 17:00.
  • How to get there: according to the Hallasan National Park website, take bus 240. It takes about 35 minutes from Jeju City Intercity Bus Terminal and about 50 minutes from Jungmun Rotary (중문로터리). Get off at "Eorimok Ipgu (어리목 입구)" and walk. For the walking time, the "How to Get Here" screen of the same website writes about 10 minutes and the Eorimok course screen writes about 15 minutes. The address is 2070-61 1100-ro, Jeju-si (제주시 1100로 2070-61) (Haean-dong San 220-13 (해안동 산 220-13)).
  • Inquiries: Hallasan National Park Eorimok Main Office (한라산국립공원 어리목본소) 064-713-9950~1.

④ Yeongsil Trail

  • What is different: of the trails to Witse Oreum, the one with the shorter walking time. The Hallasan trail reservation website writes Yeongsil (영실) as "a trail that cannot reach Baengnokdam (the summit)".
  • Distance and time: 5.8 km in total according to the official course guide of the Hallasan trail reservation website (read 2026-11-25). From Yeongsil to Witse Oreum (윗세오름) it is 3.7 km, and from Witse Oreum to Nambyeok Junction (남벽분기점) it is 2.1 km. To Witse Oreum it is 1 hour 30 minutes and to Nambyeok Junction 2 hours 30 minutes.
  • One more walk at the entrance: the 2.4 km from Yeongsil Management Office (영실관리사무소) (altitude 1,000 m) to Yeongsil Rest Area (영실휴게소) (altitude 1,280 m) is a section shared with the road. The website writes 3 hours 15 minutes one way if you start from the management office and 2 hours 30 minutes one way if you start from the rest area.
  • Winter (October–March, months 10–3) closure times: you must pass the trail checkpoint by 12:00. From Witse Oreum toward Nambyeok you must pass by 13:00. At Nambyeok Junction you start coming down at 14:00, and at Witse Oreum at 15:00.
  • Summer (April–September, months 4–9) closure times: trail checkpoint 15:00, and from Witse Oreum toward Nambyeok 14:00. At Nambyeok Junction you start coming down at 15:00, and at Witse Oreum at 17:00.
  • How to get there: according to the Hallasan National Park website of Jeju Special Self-Governing Province (read 2026-11-25), take bus 240. It takes about 50 minutes from Jeju City Intercity Bus Terminal (제주시 시외버스터미널) and about 30 minutes from Jungmun Rotary (중문로터리). The address is San 1-1, Hawon-dong, Seogwipo-si (서귀포시 하원동 산 1-1).
  • Inquiries: Yeongsil Branch (영실지소) 064-747-9950.

⑤ Donnaeko Trail

  • What is different: the trail that goes up to Nambyeok Junction from the Seogwipo (서귀포) side. Its entry cutoff is the earliest of the 7 courses. In winter it is 10:00.
  • Distance and time: 7 km. From Donnaeko Visitor Center (돈내코 탐방안내소) to Pyeonggwe Shelter (평궤대피소) it is 5.3 km, and from Pyeonggwe Shelter to Nambyeok Junction (남벽분기점) it is 1.7 km. For the time, the Hallasan trail reservation website writes about 3 hours 30 minutes one way and the Hallasan National Park website of Jeju Special Self-Governing Province writes 3 hours 50 minutes to Nambyeok Junction.
  • Difficulty: according to the Hallasan National Park website (read 2026-11-25), B (moderate) from the visitor center to Pyeonggwe Shelter, and B from Pyeonggwe Shelter to Nambyeok Junction.
  • Facilities on the trail: Pyeonggwe Shelter is unstaffed. There is no shop or spring along the whole route. Bring drinking water. There is a toilet at the visitor center.
  • Winter (October–March, months 10–3) closure times: you must pass the entrance by 10:00.
  • Summer (April–September, months 4–9) closure times: you must pass the entrance by 11:00.
  • How to get there: the Hallasan National Park website writes that you take bus 281 and get off at "Seogwipo Saneop Gwahak Godeunghakgyo Ap (서귀포산업과학고등학교 앞)", transfer to bus 611 or 612 and get off at "Chunghonmyoji Gwangjang (충혼묘지광장)", and then walk about 20 minutes. The Hallasan trail reservation website writes that you get off at "Seogwipo Gwahak Godeunghakgyo Ipgu (서귀포과학고등학교입구)" and walk about 1 hour. The address is San 1-2, Sanghyo-dong, Seogwipo-si (서귀포시 상효동 산 1-2).
  • Inquiries: Donnaeko District Information Center (돈내코지구안내소) 064-710-6920.

⑥ Eoseungsaengak Trail

  • What is different: the shortest of the 7 courses. You do not go to the summit of Hallasan (한라산) but climb the summit of Eoseungsaengak (어승생악).
  • Distance and time: 1.3 km to the Eoseungsaengak summit, about 30 minutes one way, according to the official course guide of the Hallasan trail reservation website (read 2026-11-25).
  • Entrance: the entrance is next to Eorimok Visitor Center (어리목 탐방안내소).
  • Closure time: in both winter and summer you must pass the entrance by 17:00.
  • How to get there: the Hallasan National Park (한라산국립공원) website of Jeju Special Self-Governing Province (read 2026-11-25) guides you to bus 240.
  • When to walk: it suits a day when only half a day is left because of your flight. You can also walk it after you have walked the Eorimok (어리목) course and come down. This is this guide's own advice.

⑦ Seokguram Trail

  • What is different: a short trail walked without a reservation. It is the path to the Seokguram hermitage (석굴암 암자) on the Hallasan side, in Nohyeong-dong (노형동), Jeju-si. It is a different place from Seokguram Grotto in Gyeongju (경주).
  • Distance and time: 1.5 km one way to the Seokguram hermitage, 50 minutes, according to the official course guide of the Hallasan trail reservation website (read 2026-11-25).
  • Entrance: the trail starts from a parking lot that you reach by walking about 10 minutes along a cedar forest path from the Cheonwangsa (천왕사) entrance on the 1100 Road (1100도로). The website calls this parking lot the Jeju National Hoguk Cemetery (국립제주호국원) parking lot and also the Chunghon Cemetery (충혼묘지) parking lot.
  • Closure time: in both winter and summer you must pass the entrance by 17:00.
  • How to get there: the Hallasan National Park website guides you to bus 240.

🎫 How to Reserve the Summit

The values below are the 2026-11-25 notation of the official guide of the Hallasan trail reservation website of Jeju Special Self-Governing Province (visithalla.jeju.go.kr).

  • Sections you reserve: the Jindallaebat to Baengnokdam section of the Seongpanak course and the Samgakbong to Baengnokdam section of the Gwaneumsa course.
  • When it opens: from 09:00 on the first working day of each month, you apply for use in the next month. You can reserve dates up to the last day of the next month. On the day reservations open, many users connect and you wait in a queue. Dates can sell out while you wait (website notice 2026-06-05).
  • Entry time slots: when you reserve, you choose an entry time slot. There are two slots, 05:00–08:00 and 08:01–11:30, and you must pass the entrance within the slot you reserved. The quota per slot is 800 and 200 people for Seongpanak and 400 and 100 people for Gwaneumsa (Hallasan trail reservation website notice 2025-10-24, in force from 2025-12-01). Cancel before the slot you reserved ends. If you do not cancel and do not go in, you get a penalty on reserving.
  • Number of people: up to 4 people at a time. One person reserves once a week. It is counted by the day you visit.
  • Foreign-language screens: the website has English, Japanese and Chinese screens.
  • Identity verification for foreigners: the website notice (2020-01-23) writes that foreigners verify with SNS verification or email verification on the foreign-language screen.
  • Finish the reservation: a reservation that is not completed is cancelled automatically after 1 day.
  • On the day: each person shows 1 QR code and an ID. A passport can also be used as an ID.
  • If you do not go: if you do not cancel and do not go, you cannot reserve for 3 months for 1 time and for 1 year for 2 times. If you cannot go, cancel before the slot you reserved ends.
  • Waiting list: there is none.
  • Inquiries: Hallasan trail reservation website 064-713-9953. Phone inquiries are weekdays 09:00–17:30.

Once your travel dates are set, first write down the day that month's reservations open. This is this guide's own advice.

🚫 Prohibited Acts and Winter Preparation

This is what the Hallasan National Park website of Jeju Special Self-Governing Province (read 2026-11-25) writes.

  • Drinking and smoking: drinking and smoking are prohibited in Hallasan National Park.
  • Camping and cooking: only at the Gwaneumsa District Campground (관음사지구 야영장).
  • Taking things: everything that damages nature is prohibited, including taking plants, insects, soil and stones.
  • Paths: walk only on the designated trails.
  • Hiking alone: the website recommends avoiding hiking alone on a summit trip and going together with others.
  • Boxed lunches and fire sources: according to the reservation guide of the Hallasan trail reservation website, bringing in disposable boxed lunches and fire sources is prohibited. You can bring gimbap (김밥) and hamburgers.
  • Days you cannot hike: according to the same guide, you cannot hike on Seollal (설날) and Chuseok (추석).
  • Shops: there is no shop on the trails. Prepare water and food before you go up the mountain.
  • Winter gear: the website writes that in winter you should prepare winter clothing, crampons, gloves, warm water and a lantern. Wear hiking boots. Prepare a rain coat in advance too.
  • Weather closures: when a heavy rain, typhoon or heavy snow advisory is issued, the trails are partly closed. When a warning is issued, they are fully closed and people on the mountain are sent down. A strong wind warning is also a reason for full closure. They are also closed when fog limits visibility to within 20 m, or when more than 30 cm of snow has fallen and the path cannot be seen.
  • The morning you go: open the "Real-time Trail Information" screen (jeju.go.kr/hallasan/info/info/realtime.htm) of the Hallasan National Park website. In an accident or an emergency, call 119.

⚠ 6 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to the summit without a reservation. The Jindallaebat to Baengnokdam section of Seongpanak and the Samgakbong to Baengnokdam section of Gwaneumsa need a reservation. Reservations for the next month open at 09:00 on the first working day of each month. You pass the entrance within the entry time slot (05:00–08:00, 08:01–11:30) you chose when you reserved.
  2. Starting late in winter. In October–March (months 10–3), you must pass the Seongpanak and Gwaneumsa entrances by 11:30. Eorimok and Yeongsil are 12:00 and Donnaeko is 10:00.
  3. Trying to reach the summit by Yeongsil or Eorimok. These two courses go only as far as Nambyeok Junction, past Witse Oreum. The only trails to the summit are Seongpanak and Gwaneumsa.
  4. Reserving and then not going without cancelling. You cannot reserve for 3 months for 1 time and for 1 year for 2 times. If you cannot go, cancel before the slot you reserved ends.
  5. Driving to Seongpanak and arriving late. The Seongpanak parking lot holds at most 156 cars. Roadside parking is subject to enforcement. You can park at the free transfer parking lot (199 cars) near Jeju International University and take bus 281.
  6. Trying to buy water and food on the mountain. There is no shop on the trails. Donnaeko also has no spring along the whole route.

⚙ 7 FAQs

  1. I have never hiked. Where is easy? — Eoseungsaengak is 1.3 km, about 30 minutes one way. The Seokguram course is 1.5 km, 50 minutes one way. Both are walked without a reservation, and in winter you only need to pass the entrance by 17:00. For a little more walking, Yeongsil to Witse Oreum is 1 hour 30 minutes.
  2. Can I go without a rental car? — Yes. To Seongpanak, bus 281 runs from Jeju Bus Terminal and Seogwipo Bus Terminal. From Jeju International Airport, express bus 181 runs. The first 281 bus is at 06:00. Bus 240 goes to Eorimok and Yeongsil. For Gwaneumsa, take bus 281 and transfer to bus 475 at "Jeju Daehakgyo (제주대학교)". Look at the timetable on the Jeju Bus Information System (bus.jeju.go.kr).
  3. Which course goes only as far as Witse Oreum? — Yeongsil and Eorimok. Yeongsil is 3.7 km and 1 hour 30 minutes to Witse Oreum. Eorimok is 4.7 km and 2 hours. Bus 240 goes to both courses. In winter you pass the checkpoint by 12:00 and start coming down from Witse Oreum at 15:00.
  4. Can I go when it snows or rains? — When a heavy rain, typhoon or heavy snow advisory is issued, the trails are partly closed. When a warning is issued, they are fully closed. A strong wind warning is also a reason for full closure. They are also closed when fog limits visibility to within 20 m, or when more than 30 cm of snow has fallen and the path cannot be seen. On the morning you go, open the real-time trail information screen of the Hallasan National Park website.
  5. Can I get a certificate that I reached the summit? — Yes. From 2026-09-01 you can apply directly on the reservation website and receive it as a PDF. At the summit, enter your reservation number and upload a photo, then press 'Summit Certificate (등정인증서)' in your reservation lookup. The existing method of receiving it at the unmanned terminal at the entrance can also still be used. Either way, you must apply on the day you hike and receive it on the same day (website notice 2026-08-27).
  6. How much is parking? — For a small car it is 1,000 won for the first 1 hour, 500 won every 20 minutes after that, and 13,000 won at most per day. For a medium or large vehicle it is 2,000 won, 800 won and 20,000 won at most.
  7. How do I reserve the summit? — You reserve on the Hallasan trail reservation website. From 09:00 on the first working day of each month, use for the next month opens. When you reserve, you choose an entry time slot (05:00–08:00, 08:01–11:30). You pass the entrance within the slot you reserved. If you cannot go, cancel before that slot ends.
