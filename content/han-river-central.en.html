Han River Central Park Guide — Yeouido·Ichon

Subtitle: Han River's 11 Parks Central 2 + Foreign Enthusiast (#06 Expansion)

ISSUE 03 · #306 · 2026-05-20

🎯 Why Han River Central Parks?

The Han River Central area, encompassing Yeouido (여의도) and Ichon (이촌), is the number one spot for foreign enthusiasts. It's Korea's prime location for spring cherry blossoms (#230), finance, and media deep dives.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Yeouido (여의도) = Han River Only": Yeouido (여의도) is a full package: Yunjeung-ro (윤중로) in spring + Han River + 63 Building + The Hyundai + KBS.

2. "Yunjeung-ro (윤중로) = Koreans Only": Yunjeung-ro (윤중로) is Korea's #1 cherry blossom spot + foreign vloggers' top spot.

3. "Ichon (이촌) = Hard to Get To": Ichon (이촌) is the #1 foreign resident area (#252) + a foreign enthusiast spot.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Foreigners' #1 Priority in Yeouido (여의도): Yunjeung-ro (윤중로), 63 Building, The Hyundai, KBS.

2. Foreigners in Ichon (이촌): Foreign resident's top spot, Han River.

3. Foreigners' Spring Cherry Blossoms: Yunjeung-ro (윤중로) peaks in the 2nd week of April (#303).

4. Foreigners' Night View: Yeouido (여의도) and 63 Building are foreign enthusiast favorites.
