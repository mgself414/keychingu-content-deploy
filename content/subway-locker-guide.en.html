Korean Subway Storage Lockers (T-Locker), the No-Panic Procedure Sheet — Follow the Touchscreen Exactly

① What This Piece Covers — What T-Locker Is

T-Locker (Ttota-lakeo, 또타라커) is a subway station storage locker system run by Seoul Metro, installed across lines 1–9 at 269 stations, 332 locations, 5,511 units. With just a credit card or T-money, you can use one right on the spot with no app — the app is only an option if you want to reserve and pay remotely.

If you're still deciding where to leave your luggage in the first place (coin locker vs. luggage delivery vs. moving it between hotels), start with #1232. This piece covers only how someone who's already decided on T-Locker operates the screen once they're standing at the machine.

② Size & Pricing Table — Picking the Right Locker for Your Bag

Size Dimensions (cm) Weekday 4-hr rate Overage (per hour) Notes
Small 50×30×60 ₩2,200 ₩500 Backpack, small carry-on bag
Medium 50×45×60 ₩3,300 ₩800 Small to medium suitcase
Large 50×90×60 ₩4,400 ₩1,000 Large suitcase OK
  • Prohibited items: valuables · food · plants/animals · hazardous materials — don't put these in.
  • Large lockers are relatively fewer in number, so they can sell out during busy hours (see ⑦).

③ Storage Procedure — 6 Touchscreen Steps

From the first screen at the machine to the door opening, this order holds regardless of brand or station.

Step What's on Screen What to Do
1 Language selection screen Tap one of 4 languages — EN / 中 / 日 / KR — choosing English keeps every following screen in English
2 「Storage / Pickup」 selection Tap [ Storage ]
3 List of empty lockers Select your preferred size among the lockers shown in blue on screen
4 Payment screen Pre-pay by credit card or T-money (transit card) — no cash accepted
5 「bimilbeonho seoljeong (비밀번호 설정) — Set password」 Enter a 6-digit password yourself, or choose QR issuance
6 Door opens Put your bag in and close the door to lock it
  • If you got a QR code, screenshot it and save it to your photo album — it's a one-time code issued on the spot, not emailed to you.
  • If you reserved remotely through the app, the OTP issued must be entered within 3 minutes to stay valid — on-the-spot issuance in front of the machine is simpler than app reservation if you want room to breathe while entering it.

④ Retrieval Procedure — 3 Steps to Get Your Bag Back

Step What's on Screen What to Do
1 「Storage / Pickup」 selection Tap [ Pickup ]
2 「bimilbeonho ipnyeok (비밀번호 입력) — Enter password」 Enter your 6-digit password yourself, or scan your QR
3 Settlement screen If you're over time, an extra-payment screen appears — pay and the door opens; if not, the door opens right away
  • Overage fees run ₩500–1,000 per hour depending on size — only charged for time past the 4-hour mark.

⑤ Handling a Lost Password — the No-Panic Order

1. Try entering it again first — mixing up the digits is the most likely cause. Use the screen's re-entry button and try 2–3 more times.

2. Check your QR screenshot — if you got a QR instead of a password, don't rack your brain trying to recall digits — go straight to your photo album and find the QR capture first.

3. Still won't open? Go to the station office — ask a staff member at the nearest station office for help, using your payment record (a card approval text, or the reservation log in your app account) as evidence. Your payment record itself is your proof of identity.

⑦ 3 Panic Scenarios

1. You forgot your password or never screenshotted the QR — follow the ⑤ order. With a payment record, the station office can resolve most of these.

2. Large lockers are sold out — at major transfer stations during peak hours, large lockers fill up first. Searching for T-Locker at a nearby station (a transfer station 5–10 minutes' walk away) and heading there is faster than waiting on the spot.

3. The overage fee piles up more than you expected / T-money balance runs short — the 4-hour rate looks cheap, but come back after a full day of sightseeing and ₩500–1,000 per hour has stacked up over several hours. Just switch to a card right there on the payment screen — no need to panic if your balance falls short.
