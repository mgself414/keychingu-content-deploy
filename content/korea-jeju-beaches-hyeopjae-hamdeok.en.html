Jeju Beach Guide — Hyeopjae, Hamdeok, Jungmun Saekdal and Iho Tewoo: 2026 Official Season Dates, Fees and Off-Season Walks

Subtitle: 2026 beach season Jun 24–Sep 6, swimming 10:00–19:00 (Hyeopjae until 21:00 Jul 15–Aug 15, no night swimming at Hamdeok) / parasol ₩20,000, platform ₩30,000 / Jungmun Saekdal free, 152 parking spaces, surfing / Seoubong trail 2.5 km, free / Cheonjeyeon Falls ₩2,500 / Biyangdo ferry from Hallim Port about 4 times a day — as of 2026-10-08, per the official pages of the Jeju Tourism Organization (visitjeju) and Seogwipo City

ISSUE 04 · #1059 · 2026-06-08

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Jeju's beaches are completely different during the official swimming season (Jun 24–Sep 6, 2026) and the rest of the year. During the season there are swimming hours (10:00–19:00), lifeguards and paid parasols; from October on there is no swimming, but the sand, coastal walking paths and sunsets are all still there. The figures below are only what we read on 2026-10-08 on the official pages of the Jeju Tourism Organization, visitjeju (제주관광공사) (visitjeju.net) and Seogwipo City (서귀포시). Bus numbers, shower fees and the Jungmun/Iho Tewoo season dates are not on the official pages, so we mark them "not posted." Figures from the older guide such as "Hyeopjae free 24 hours" (the sand is open, but swimming is time-limited and rentals are paid), "June–August season," and "5-star hotels ₩200,000–600,000" or "surfing ₩30,000–80,000" differ from the official sources, so we removed them.

🏖 Hyeopjae and Hamdeok — The Two Headliners, West and East

  • Hyeopjae (Hallim (한림)): Shallow emerald water with Biyangdo Island (비양도) straight ahead. 2026 season Jun 24–Sep 6, swimming 10:00–19:00, and until 21:00 from Jul 15 to Aug 15 (official). Parasol ₩20,000, platform ₩30,000. The ferry to Biyangdo leaves not from Hyeopjae but from Hallim Port (한림항).
  • Hamdeok (Jocheon (조천)): Same season and hours, no night swimming (official). Next to it, the Seoubong (서우봉) trail is 2.5 km and free — some sections have no lighting, so go before sunset.
  • Entry is free at both; parking and shower fees are not posted officially.

🏄 Jungmun Saekdal — Surfing and Waterfalls

  • Inside the Jungmun Tourist Complex (중문관광단지) in Seogwipo City (서귀포시). Free, 152 parking spaces, about 560 m long, four-color sand, with showers, changing rooms, a kiosk and a campground (official). International surf contest in June–July. The 2026 season dates are not on the official page; press reports say they match the other beaches.
  • Pair it with Cheonjeyeon Falls (천제연폭포) (09:00–17:50, adult ₩2,500) and Jusangjeolli Cliff (주상절리대) (09:00–17:50, parking free), which are within walking distance.
  • From Jeju Airport (제주공항), limousine bus 600 runs via the Jungmun Tourist Complex to the Seogwipo KAL Hotel every 18–20 minutes (official).

✈ Iho Tewoo — Next to the Airport

  • At 20 Dori-ro in Jeju City, a beach known for its horse-shaped lighthouses. The 2026 season dates and hours are not officially posted. A good stop when your flight time leaves a gap.

🍂 From October — Without Swimming

  • Once the season ends there are no lifeguards or parasols and no swimming. What remains: the Hyeopjae sunset, Seoubong at Hamdeok, and the Jungmun waterfall and Jusangjeolli Cliff. The wind is strong, so bring a hat and a windbreaker.

🚌 Getting There

  • Airport → Jungmun: limousine bus 600 (official). City bus numbers to Hyeopjae and Hamdeok are not on the official pages, so we don't list them — use a map app.
  • East (Hamdeok), west (Hyeopjae) and south (Jungmun) are far from one another. Combining two or more in one day means a lot of travel time.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Swimming in mid-September — the 2026 season ends Sep 6.

2. Night swimming at Hamdeok — no night swimming. Only Hyeopjae stays open until 21:00, for one month in summer.

3. Taking the Biyangdo ferry from Hyeopjae — it leaves from Hallim Port.

4. Climbing Seoubong at dusk — some sections are unlit. Come down before sunset.

5. Four beaches in one day — the east, west and south trips are long.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. When is this year's season? — Jun 24–Sep 6, swimming 10:00–19:00 (Hyeopjae until 21:00 Jul 15–Aug 15).

2. Entry fee? — Free. Parasol ₩20,000, platform ₩30,000.

3. Surfing? — Jungmun Saekdal (free, 152 parking spaces). Lesson fees are not officially posted.

4. Waterfall fee? — Cheonjeyeon adult ₩2,500, 09:00–17:50.

5. Biyangdo? — From Hallim Port about 4 times a day, 15 minutes. Canceled in bad weather.

6. Which beach is closest to the airport? — Iho Tewoo. For Jungmun, take limousine bus 600.
