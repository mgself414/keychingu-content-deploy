⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "Jinju (진주) = unknown": Imjin War (임진왜란) Matrix #1 · Chokseokru (촉석루) Yeongnam (영남) #1 · Jinju Naengmyeon (진주냉면) Korea #1 · Jinju Lantern Festival (유등축제) Enthusiast #1.

2. "Chokseokru (촉석루) = ordinary pavilion": Yeongnam (영남) #1 · Imjin War (임진왜란) 1592 Matrix.

3. "Jinju Fortress (진주성) = ordinary fortress": 1592 + 1593 Great Battle of Jinju (진주대첩) Matrix #1.

4. "Jinju Lantern Festival (유등축제) = ordinary festival": Every October · Korea Enthusiast Matrix #1.

5. "Jinju Naengmyeon (진주냉면) = same everywhere": Korea's #1 Signature · Different matrix from Pyeongyang (평양) and Hamheung (함흥) styles.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Top Priority for Foreigner Beginners: Chokseokru (촉석루) + Jinju Fortress (진주성) + Jinju Naengmyeon (진주냉면) = 1 day.

2. Jinju Lantern Festival (유등축제) (October): Every 1st-2nd week of October · Foreigner Enthusiast Matrix #1 · Advance booking recommended.

3. Jinju Naengmyeon (진주냉면) vs. Pyeongyang (평양)·Hamheung (함흥): Jinju (진주) = seafood·ribs + spicy·different matrix.

4. Season: Spring·Autumn best · October Jinju Lantern Festival (유등축제) Enthusiast #1.

5. Allergies: Jinju Naengmyeon (진주냉면) = seafood·ribs·soybeans, please inform in advance.

6. Recommended Persona: Family·Seniors = Chokseokru (촉석루) + Jinju Naengmyeon (진주냉면) / MZ·Instagram = Cafe + Jinju Lantern Festival (유등축제) / Enthusiast·History = Jinju Fortress (진주성) + overnight stay.
