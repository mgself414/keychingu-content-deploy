Article 3 body EN — The broth is quiet, almost plain. The dough is chewy; potatoes and zucchini add warmth. One bowl to understand the depth inside simplicity.

Article 2 body EN — The foreign cans met kimchi and gochujang in one pot. Early on, it was called "Johnson Tang," after U.S. President Lyndon B. Johnson.

Article 3 body EN — Uijeongbu Budae-jjigae Street (의정부 부대찌개 거리) and Songtan Choi's House (송탄 최네집) still carry the original lineage. Foreign ingredients found harmony in Korea's spicy broth.

Article 2 hed 실향민의 서울 Refugees in Seoul
Article 2 body KR 1950년 전쟁을 피해 남하한 실향민들이 서울에 가게를 열었다. 을지면옥·필동면옥·우래옥이 그 계보를 잇는다. —
Article 2 body EN — In the 1950s, refugees from the North opened shops in Seoul. Eulji Myeonok (을지면옥), Pildong Myeonok (필동면옥), and Woo Lae Oak (우래옥) still carry the lineage.

Article 3 body EN — It returned to the spotlight at the 2018 inter-Korean summit dinner. Seventy years of division, held in one cold bowl.
