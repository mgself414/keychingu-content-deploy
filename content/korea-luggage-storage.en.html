🎯 Why a "Luggage Storage·Delivery" Guide

Korea luggage storage·delivery = services to store/send heavy bags when sightseeing after checkout or moving to the next city. The biggest foreigner misconception = "you have to drag your bags around." In reality Korea has well-developed coin lockers (stations·airports)·storage services·hotel-to-hotel·airport delivery. Pain points = (a) coin-locker location·payment (b) not knowing delivery services exist (c) airport↔hotel·hotel↔hotel delivery (d) size·fee·timing. "luggage storage Seoul / luggage delivery Korea airport" is searched constantly on Reddit·YouTube.

This guide covers 12 luggage-service signatures + coin lockers·delivery + airport·hotel-to-hotel + size·fee + booking matrix.

⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "Must drag bags around": Go hands-free with coin lockers·storage·delivery.

2. "Lockers are always available": In peak season·popular stations big lockers fill up·check vacancy by app.

3. "Delivery arrives same day": Hotel-to-hotel·city-move delivery is next-day by default·apply the day before.

4. "Deliver right at the airport": Allow time·fee per bag·booking recommended.

5. "Cash only": Coin lockers take T-money/card·check overseas cards for apps.

⚙ FAQ (6)

1. Where are coin lockers?: Subway·train stations·airports·near spots·T-money pay.

2. Large suitcases?: Seoul Station/airport big lockers·staffed storage apps.

3. Airport→hotel delivery?: Incheon Airport desk·T-luggage etc.·specify hotel·same/next day.

4. Hotel-to-hotel move?: Apply day before·next-day arrival·via front desk.

5. Fees?: Lockers ₩2,000~15,000·delivery ₩15,000~40,000/bag.

6. Recommended personas: Same-day = lockers/front / move = delivery / large = big lockers.

⚠ Medical·Safety·Loss Disclaimer

Keep tags/QR for lockers·storage·carry valuables. Check tracking number·time for delivery. Beware overtime fees·unclaimed handling. Check damage·loss compensation terms. No hazardous items. Emergency ☎ 1330·112.
