Busan International Film Festival (BIFF) Visitor Guide — Centered on Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당) in Haeundae's Centum City, Booking a ₩10,000 Regular Screening, and the 2026 31st Edition Results (Autumn Season, Official Values as of October 2026)

Summary: The 31st BIFF in 2026 ran October 6–15 (BIFF official) · 8 theaters with 31 screens, mostly clustered in Centum City, Haeundae-gu (해운대구) (BIFF official) · Regular screenings ₩10,000 (~$7.50), opening/closing ceremonies ₩30,000 (~$22.50) (BIFF official ticketing guide) · English booking requires signing up with email verification (BIFF official English ticketing guide) · The 32nd edition in 2027 has no announcement yet — as of 2026-10-21

ISSUE 03 · #482 · 2026-05-27

🎯 How to Use This Guide

When the sun goes down, people file into the open-air theater under the roof of Busan Cinema Center (Yeonghwaui Jeondang / 영화의전당), and the screen light flickers in the night breeze off the Suyeong River. The festival calls these open-air screenings "Open Cinema," and in 2026 it programmed 7 films (BIFF official overview). This guide is for travelers heading to the Busan International Film Festival (BIFF) for the first time. It covers where screenings are held, how to buy tickets, and how to use Busan's film spaces when the festival is not on, using official page values only.

The 2026 festival is over. So 2026 values are written as "this is how it ran," and next year's schedule is left blank until an announcement is made. Booking methods and prices can change every year, so check the biff.kr notices again for 2027.

📅 How the 2026 31st Edition Went

  • Dates: October 6 (Tue)–15 (Thu), 2026. From 2026 the opening day moved one day earlier, from Wednesday to Tuesday (BIFF official overview)
  • Scale: 8 theaters, 31 screens. 246 films from 59 countries in the official selection, and 69 Community BIFF films (including open-air screenings) (BIFF official results)
  • Attendance: 277,027 in total, of whom 200,043 were film audiences (BIFF official results)
  • Opening film: Nat-gwa Bam-eun Seoro-ege (낮과 밤은 서로에게), directed by Kim Jong-kwan (BIFF official overview)
  • Competition section: It was newly created in 2025, so 2026 was its second year. The Busan Award grand prize went to Ppang-gwa Chaek (빵과 책) by director Majid Majidi (BIFF official award list)
  • Festival standing: In 2026 it was designated an "A-list festival" by the International Federation of Film Producers Associations (FIAPF), and the Busan Award winner became eligible for submission to the U.S. Academy Awards' International Feature Film category (BIFF official overview). The festival describes itself as "Korea's first international film festival" and "Asia's largest film festival" (BIFF official introduction). The first opening ceremony was held on September 13, 1996 (BIFF official introduction)
  • 2027 32nd edition: Neither dates nor the booking schedule have been announced (BIFF official, checked 2026-10-21). Don't guess the dates; wait for the official notice

🎬 Screening Venues — Clustered in Centum City

The festival venues are packed into the Centum City area of Haeundae-gu, so you can move between them on foot. All addresses are values given in the festival's venue operation guide (BIFF official).

  • Busan Cinema Center (Yeonghwaui Jeondang / 영화의전당) — 120 Suyeonggangbyeon-daero, Haeundae-gu. A 5-minute walk from Exits 12 and 6 of Centum City Station on Line 2 (Busan Cinema Center official directions). The outdoor theater "Roof Theater" (루프씨어터) hosts Open Cinema and outdoor stage greetings. In 2026, Open Cinema entry was 18:30 (planned) on October 7–14, with all seats unreserved (BIFF official venue operation guide)
  • CGV Centum City (CGV센텀시티) — 35 Centumnam-daero, Haeundae-gu, 7th floor of Shinsegae Department Store. Before the department store opens, enter through Gate 3 in front of Centum City Station Exit 12 (BIFF official)
  • Lotte Cinema Centum City (롯데시네마 센텀시티) — 59 Centumnam-daero, Haeundae-gu, 8th floor of Lotte Department Store. Before opening hours, use the far-left door of the 1st-floor main entrance (toward subway Exit 8) (BIFF official)
  • KOFIC Standard Screening Room (영화진흥위원회 표준시사실) — 130 Suyeonggangbyeon-daero, Haeundae-gu, 2nd floor of the Korean Film Council building (BIFF official)
  • Sohyang Theater Woori Bank Hall (소향씨어터 우리은행홀) — 55 Centumjungang-ro, Haeundae-gu (BIFF official)
  • Busan Viewers Media Center Open Hall (부산시청자미디어센터 공개홀) — 42 Centumjungang-ro, Haeundae-gu, 2nd floor (BIFF official)
  • Community BIFF (커뮤니티비프) — Screenings at Megabox Busan Theater (메가박스 부산극장) and Catholic Center Space 101.1 (가톨릭센터 공간 101.1) (BIFF official results). We could not confirm the addresses of these two venues on the festival pages

🎟 How to Buy Tickets — Per the 2026 Ticketing Guide

  • Booking opens: In 2026, the opening/closing ceremonies, Open Cinema, Midnight Passion, Actors' House, and Community BIFF opened on September 17 at 14:00; regular screenings, Master Class, and Cine Class opened on September 21 at 14:00 (BIFF official ticketing guide)
  • Prices: Opening/closing ceremonies ₩30,000 (~$22.50), regular screenings and Cine Class ₩10,000 (~$7.50), Actors' House and Master Class ₩15,000 (~$11), Midnight Passion ₩20,000 (~$15) (BIFF official ticketing guide)
  • Sign-up for foreigners: The English ticketing guide says you receive email verification at first sign-up, then log in with the registered email and a 6-digit numeric password. Even if you signed up last year, you must register again (BIFF official English ticketing guide). The Korean guide uses mobile phone number verification
  • Payment: Credit or debit cards, per the English guide (BIFF official English ticketing guide). The on-site BIFF box office accepts cards and cash (BIFF official ticketing guide)
  • Ticket limit: Up to 2 tickets per screening code. A waiting number is assigned when traffic is heavy. The guide recommends the Chrome browser (BIFF official ticketing guide)
  • On-site purchase: All regular seats are sold online first. If they don't sell out, or if canceled tickets appear, you can buy them at the BIFF box office during the festival (BIFF official ticketing guide)
  • Cancellation: Up to 60 minutes before the screening. No fee up to 6 days before the screening date, ₩1,000 (~$0.75) at 5–3 days before, ₩3,000 (~$2.25) from 2 days before (for opening/closing ceremonies, ₩3,000 (~$2.25) and ₩9,000 (~$6.75)) (BIFF official ticketing guide)
  • Entry: You can enter from 15 minutes before the screening, and entry is restricted once 15 minutes have passed since the start. A screenshot of a mobile ticket or a KakaoTalk notification message will not get you in. Children under 4 cannot enter even with a guardian (BIFF official venue operation guide)

🎤 What You Can See Without a Ticket

  • Outdoor stage greetings: Directors and actors introduce the film for about 20 minutes. In 2026 they were held at the Roof Theater (루프씨어터) of Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당) on October 7–11 (BIFF official outdoor stage greetings)
  • Open Talks and outdoor stage greetings need no fee or application; you sit in the seats provided, as many as there are. The festival notes that waiting lines audience members make on their own have nothing to do with festival operations (BIFF official outdoor stage greetings)

🏛 When the Festival Is Not On — Busan Cinema Center

  • Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당) screens films year-round, even outside the festival. In the 2026 timetable, under the "Cinematheque" section, "2026 Cine Replay," "2026 Popular Film Program," and "2026 Art Film Program" are listed for 2026-01-01 to 12-31 (Busan Cinema Center official). Main phone number 051-780-6000 (Busan Cinema Center official)
  • We could not confirm admission fees or closing days on the Busan Cinema Center main page

🧰 BIFF Practicalities — Solo, Booking, Payment, Getting Around

  • Solo: You can buy seats starting from 1 ticket. Inside the theaters, seating is assigned (BIFF official venue operation guide)
  • Booking timing: In 2026, booking opened about 3 weeks before the festival began (BIFF official ticketing guide). The 2027 opening date has not been announced. Get news through the festival newsletter (BIFF official)
  • Payment: For card payment in general, see Foreigner Korea Payment System Deep Dive
  • Seoul to Busan: We could not confirm KTX travel times on the Korail official page. Check times and fares per train on the Korail booking screen — Taking the KTX for the First Time: The No-Panic Manual — Yes, There's No Ticket Gate
  • Within Busan: Centum City Station is on Line 2 (Busan Cinema Center official). For how to read the Busan subway, see Busan Transit Grammar — A City You Can Read with Just Two Lines
  • Food and drink: No alcohol or outside food inside theaters, and some theaters allow bottled water only. At the outdoor Roof Theater (루프씨어터), drinks with lids and simple food such as sandwiches and gimbap (김밥, rice rolls) are fine, but alcohol (including non-alcoholic beer) and soup dishes are not (BIFF official venue operation guide)
  • Inquiries: BIFF call center 1666-9177, weekdays 10:00–17:00. Email cs@biff.kr (BIFF official)

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "I'll just buy a ticket on site" — All regular seats are sold online first. Only leftover and canceled tickets appear at the on-site box office (BIFF official ticketing guide)

2. "I'll get in with a phone screenshot" — A screenshot of a mobile ticket or a notification message will not get you in (BIFF official venue operation guide)

3. "Any hotel will do" — The 6 official screening venues in 2026 were all at Centum City addresses in Haeundae-gu. If you want to see several films a day, a place with easy access to Centum City Station is more convenient (BIFF official venue operation guide)

4. "I signed up last year, so I can book as is" — Booker registration is done anew every year (BIFF official ticketing guide)
