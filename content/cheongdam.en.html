Walking Cheongdam (청담동) — Louis Vuitton Maison and House of Dior Architecture, SongEun (송은), Espace Louis Vuitton and Atelier Hermès Galleries, Dosan Park (도산공원) (Verified October 2026)

Subtitle: Louis Vuitton Maison Seoul (루이비통 메종 서울), designed by Frank Gehry (프랭크 게리), opened October 2019 (Louis Vuitton official) · SongEun (송은) free Mon–Sat 11:00–18:30 (SongEun official) · Espace Louis Vuitton Seoul (에스파스 루이 비통 서울) on the 4th floor, free with advance reservation (Visit Gangnam) · Dosan Park (도산공원) open at all times (VisitKorea) — as of 2026-10-22

ISSUE 03 · #296 · 2026-05-20

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Pass the sleek glass facade on Apgujeong-ro (압구정로), turn one alley, and you find pine shadows reflected inside a concrete building; walk a little further and the earthen path of Dosan Park (도산공원) is under your feet. This guide is for people who want to treat Cheongdam (청담동) as a neighborhood to walk and look at, not just to shop. There are three strands. Luxury flagship architecture (buildings you view from the outside), galleries (free and paid exhibition spaces inside or beside the luxury buildings), and Dosan Park (도산공원) (the park and its memorial hall). Addresses, hours and admission fees are only what we opened directly on official institution and brand pages, the Visit Gangnam tourism site and VisitKorea TourAPI; where a value was not there, we wrote "could not be confirmed".

What to buy inside the stores and how tax-free shopping and refunds work are not covered in this guide. That is in Foreign Visitors' Guide to Luxury Shopping in Gangnam (강남): 12 Clusters, Deep Dive.

🏛 Flagship Architecture — Cheongdam (청담) Seen From the Outside

The brand-owned buildings stand at Nos. 454–464 on Apgujeong-ro (압구정로) and on Dosan-daero 45-gil (도산대로45길). We do not cover prices or stock.

  • Louis Vuitton Maison Seoul (루이비통 메종 서울) — 454 Apgujeong-ro (압구정로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Designed by Frank Gehry (프랭크 게리) and opened in October 2019. Open daily 11:00–21:00; closed January 1, Seollal (설날, Lunar New Year) and Chuseok (추석, Korean harvest festival) day (Louis Vuitton official store page). The top floor holds the art exhibition space Espace Louis Vuitton Seoul (에스파스 루이 비통 서울) (see the gallery section below)
  • House of Dior (하우스 오브 디올) — 464 Apgujeong-ro (압구정로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. A 6-story standalone boutique with a curved exterior like a white flower bud, and Café Dior (카페 디올) on the 5th floor. Mon–Sat 11:00–20:00, Sun 11:00–19:00, phone 02-513-0300 (Visit Gangnam). VisitKorea TourAPI lists daily 11:00–20:00, so sources differ; call to check on a Sunday. Line 7 Cheongdam Station (청담역) is close (Visit Gangnam)
  • Gucci Cheongdam Flagship (구찌 청담 플래그십) — 462 Apgujeong-ro (압구정로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Daily 11:00–20:00, closed on traditional holidays (VisitKorea TourAPI).
  • Prada Cheongdam (프라다 청담점) — 463 Apgujeong-ro (압구정로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul, 1st–2nd floors. Daily 11:00–20:00, closed on traditional holidays (VisitKorea TourAPI).
  • Maison Hermès Dosan Park (메종 에르메스 도산 파크) — 7 Dosan-daero 45-gil (도산대로45길), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Designed by the French architecture firm RDAI and opened in 2006 (Hermès Foundation official). It stands on the same Dosan-daero 45-gil (도산대로45길) as Dosan Park (도산공원). The store's opening hours could not be confirmed because the official Hermès page would not open

🖼 Galleries — Exhibition Halls Inside Luxury Buildings

Most art spaces around Cheongdam (청담) and Sinsa (신사) are free, but each closes on a different weekday. The first thing to check when planning is the day of the week.

  • Espace Louis Vuitton Seoul (에스파스 루이 비통 서울) — 4th floor of Louis Vuitton Maison Seoul (루이비통 메종 서울) (454 Apgujeong-ro (압구정로)). 12:00–19:00, closed January 1, Seollal (설날) and Chuseok (추석). Admission is free but needs advance reservation. It stages touring exhibitions built from the Fondation Louis Vuitton collection (Visit Gangnam). We could not confirm the title of the exhibition now showing — check the reservation page
  • SongEun (송은) — 441 Dosan-daero (도산대로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. The first building Herzog & de Meuron (헤르조그 & 드 뫼롱) built in Korea (SongEun official). Mon–Sat 11:00–18:30 (last entry 18:10), closed Sundays and public holidays, and also closed while exhibitions are being installed. All exhibitions are free. Self-guided visits and docent tours (Mon–Sat 15:00 and 17:00) are booked through Naver. No parking. 15 minutes on foot from Exit 8 of Line 7 Cheongdam Station (청담역), 13 minutes from Exit 4 of Suin-Bundang Line Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역). The next exhibition is 《LIGHT, FORM AND VISION: Roman Heritage in Dialogue with Korean Contemporary Art》 (October 29 – November 14, 2026) (SongEun official). Phone 02-3448-0100
  • Atelier Hermès (아뜰리에 에르메스) — Basement level 1 of Maison Hermès Dosan Park (메종 에르메스 도산 파크) (7 Dosan-daero 45-gil (도산대로45길)). Free admission, run by the Hermès Foundation since 2008 (Hermès Foundation official). Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat 11:00–19:00, Sun 12:00–19:00, closed Wednesdays (Visit Gangnam). Do not use the front show window; take the elevator at the parking-lot entrance down, pass the café, and you reach the exhibition hall (Visit Gangnam). The current exhibition is not listed on the foundation page, so we could not confirm it. Phone 02-3015-3248
  • Horim Museum Sinsa Annex (호림박물관 신사분관) — Horim Art Center (호림아트센터), 317 Dosan-daero (도산대로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Unlike the three above, this is a classical art museum. Tue–Sat 10:30–18:00 (last entry 1 hour before closing), closed Sundays, Mondays, January 1, and the Seollal (설날) and Chuseok (추석) holidays. Admission: adults ₩10,000 (~$7.50), youth, people with disabilities and seniors ₩7,000 (~$5.25). The Sinsa Annex currently shows 《Sigan (시時간間)》 (September 1 – December 12, 2026). From Exit 5 of Suin-Bundang Line Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역), go straight 400 m toward Hakdong Intersection (학동사거리), then head toward Sinsa Station (신사역) (Horim Museum official).

🌳 Dosan Park (도산공원) — A Forest in the Middle of the Luxury Street

  • Dosan Park (도산공원) — 20 Dosan-daero 45-gil (도산대로45길), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Open at all times, all year, opened November 10, 1973. Inquiries: Gangnam-gu Office (강남구청) Parks and Greenery Division (공원녹지과) 02-3423-6244 (VisitKorea TourAPI). The park honors the independence activist Dosan Ahn Changho (도산 안창호) and has his statue, a memorial hall, and his grave, where he lies with his wife Lee Hyeryeon (이혜련). A memorial ceremony is held every year on March 10 (VisitKorea). Reach it from Line 3 Apgujeong Station (압구정역) or Suin-Bundang Line Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역) (Visit Gangnam)
  • Dosan Ahn Changho Memorial Hall (도산안창호기념관) — Inside Dosan Park (도산공원) (20 Dosan-daero 45-gil (도산대로45길)). 10:00–18:00 (last entry 17:30), closed January 1 and the Seollal (설날) and Chuseok (추석) holidays, admission free, phone 02-541-1800 (VisitKorea TourAPI). Built in 1998, it displays the life and belongings of Ahn Changho (안창호) (VisitKorea). The hall's own website would not open normally this time

You can combine Atelier Hermès (아뜰리에 에르메스) → Dosan Park and Memorial Hall (도산공원) → Horim Museum Sinsa Annex (호림박물관 신사분관) in one go, since they sit on the same road. Atelier Hermès (아뜰리에 에르메스) is closed on Wednesdays, and the Horim Museum on Sundays and Mondays.

🧰 Cheongdam (청담) Walking Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Getting Around

  • Solo: The galleries, park and memorial hall are all easy to visit alone. For eating alone, see Seoul Solo Diner Map — Full Meta Guide to 12 Signature Solo Restaurants, Bars and Cafés for Foreigners
  • Reservations: Espace Louis Vuitton Seoul (에스파스 루이 비통 서울) needs advance reservation (Visit Gangnam). SongEun (송은) uses Naver reservation (SongEun official). The pages we opened for Atelier Hermès (아뜰리에 에르메스), Dosan Ahn Changho Memorial Hall (도산안창호기념관) and Horim Museum Sinsa Annex (호림박물관 신사분관) carried no wording that a reservation is required
  • Matching the weekday: On Sundays SongEun (송은) and the Horim Museum Sinsa Annex (호림박물관 신사분관) are closed; on Mondays the Horim Museum Sinsa Annex; on Wednesdays Atelier Hermès (아뜰리에 에르메스). On a Tue, Thu, Fri or Sat that is not a public holiday, you can see all four galleries
  • Payment and refunds: Most galleries are free, so there is little to pay. For in-store payment and tax refunds, see Foreign Visitors' Payment Systems in Korea, Deep Dive and the luxury shopping guide above
  • Getting around: For the flagship street to the east, use Line 7 Cheongdam Station (청담역); for the Dosan Park (도산공원) side, Line 3 Apgujeong Station (압구정역) and Suin-Bundang Line Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역) (Visit Gangnam). SongEun (송은) has no parking, so go by public transport (SongEun official). For transit cards, see Korean Transit Cards: 3 Options, Zero-Panic Manual — T-money, WOWPASS or Climate Companion Card (기후동행카드)?
  • Gallery etiquette: At SongEun (송은), food and drink may not be brought into the galleries, photography rules differ by exhibition, and flash is never allowed. Wheelchairs and strollers for children aged 24 months and under may enter (SongEun official)

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Luxury-building galleries are VIP-only" — Espace Louis Vuitton Seoul (에스파스 루이 비통 서울), Atelier Hermès (아뜰리에 에르메스) and SongEun (송은) are all free to enter. Espace Louis Vuitton Seoul (에스파스 루이 비통 서울), however, needs advance reservation (Visit Gangnam, Hermès Foundation, SongEun official)

2. "Cramming the galleries into Sunday" — SongEun (송은) is closed on Sundays and public holidays, and the Horim Museum Sinsa Annex (호림박물관 신사분관) on Sundays and Mondays (SongEun official, Horim Museum official)

3. "Atelier Hermès (아뜰리에 에르메스) is entered through the store's front door" — Take the elevator at the parking-lot entrance down to basement level 1. It is closed on Wednesdays (Visit Gangnam)

4. "Dosan Park (도산공원) closes at night" — The park is open at all times. What closes is the memorial hall inside the park (10:00–18:00) (VisitKorea TourAPI)
