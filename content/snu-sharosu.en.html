🎯 Why is Seoul National University and Sharosu-gil an exclusive guide?

Seoul National University and Sharosu-gil are Gwanak-gu's Korea #1 National University and MZ youth hot zone — Seoul National University (Korea's first national university opened in 1946) + Sharosu-gil (from the main gate of Seoul National University Station towards Nakseongdae, MZ Instagram hotspot borrowed from Garosu-gil) + Sillim and Nakseongdae youth bar streets compressed into one area. "Seoul's Cambridge·Oxford", a combination of Korean academics, youth, and MZ desserts.

Compared to Hongdae (MZ, indie), Gangnam (luxury), and Itaewon (global), Seoul National University and Sharosu-gil are national university, youth, and Instagram-related. Even though it is an area adjacent to Gangnam in the flow of foreign tourists, they pass because it is perceived as “so far away because it is south” — in reality, it is 30 minutes to Gangnam from Seoul National University Station Line 2 and 25 minutes to Hongik University. Korea #1 National University deep dive + No. 1 foreign Instagram hotspot.

In particular, Sharosu-gil (Seoul National University Station Exit 2 → about 1.5km in the direction of Nakseongdae) is an MZ Instagram hotspot whose name is borrowed from Garosu-gil in Gangnam. It is crowded with 100+ cafes, restaurants, meat restaurants, and dessert stores. Foreign Korean youth ranked 1st in deep dive. Following #138 Hongdae, #142 Gangnam, #170 Daehakro, #171 Sinchon, and #173 Seocho, ISSUE 03 ⑮ edition — Key Friend's first entry into Gwanak-gu (using 33 clusters).

⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Seoul National University = just a university" — Unrecognized value of Korea's #1 national universities

Foreigners perceive Seoul National University as "just a university," but in reality it is Korea's No. 1 national university since its opening in 1946 — No. 1 in politics, economics, and academia in Korea + Nobel Peace Prize winner President Kim Dae-jung's alma mater + World's top 30 universities. Gwanak Campus 700,000 pyeong + natural slope + museum and art gallery is the #1 deep dive in Korean studies for foreigners.

2. "Sharosu-gil = Gangnam Garosu-gil fake" — Unique MZ hotspot unknown

Sharosu-gil (Seoul National University Station → Nakseongdae 1.5km) borrows its name from Garosu-gil, but its own MZ hotspot — 100+ cafes, restaurants, meat restaurants, and desserts. Garosu-gil in Gangnam is luxury + luxury goods / Sharosu-gil is youth + students + MZ Instagram = different results. Foreign student·MZ Instagram hot zone.

3. "Seoul National University campus = You can't enter" — Foreigners are not allowed to enter

Seoul National University campus Free entry for foreigners — Main gate (Seoul National University Station) Exit 2 or campus shuttle bus (Main gate → Library → College of Agriculture and Life Sciences, etc.). Beodeulgol·Jahayeon·Museum·Art Gallery Free admission. Festival every May (Seoul National University Festival – Campus Open).

Scenery of Seoul National University and Sharosu-gil

Seoul National University and Sharosu-gil are located in the northeastern part of Gwanak-gu. Korea #1 National University + MZ Youth Hot Zone — With the opening of Seoul National University in 1946 + the rise of Sharosu-gil in the late 2010s, it established itself as a Korean academic + MZ youth combination area.

Seoul National University Gwanak Campus sub-area is a 700,000 pyeong comprehensive campus — a full set of nature (at the foot of Mt. Gwanak), museum, art gallery, library, and student cafeteria. 5 minutes by campus shuttle bus from Seoul National University Station (Line 2).

Sharosu-gil sub-area is Seoul National University Station Exit 2 → about 1.5km toward Nakseongdae — 100+ cafes, restaurants, and meat restaurants. A full 30-minute walk. Ranked 1st on MZ Instagram.

Sillim·Nakseongdae sub-area is a street of youth bars and meat restaurants — Korean college students and MZ nighttime hot zone. Lively after midnight.

Seoul Namseoul Museum of Art is adjacent to Nambu Ring Road — free admission + deep dive into foreign art.

Transportation: Line 2 Seoul National University Station (Sharosu-gil Exit 1) / Sillim Station (Sillim Bar) / Nakseongdae Station (Nakseongdae) / Sadang Station (Foreigners living). Kakao Taxi recommended. Bicycle Ttareungi OK.

⚙ FAQ (8)

Q1. Foreigners admitted to Seoul National University campus?

Free entry OK — 5-minute walk from Exit 2 of the main gate (Seoul National University Station) or on the campus shuttle bus (main gate → library → College of Agriculture and Life Sciences). Beodeulgol·Jahayeon (Pond)·Museum·Art Gallery Free. Festival every May (Seoul National University Festival) = Campus opening + 1st season for foreigners.

Q2. How to get to Sharosu-gil?

Exit 2 of Seoul National University Station on Line 2 → Enter Gwanak-ro 14-gil → Walk 1.5km toward Nakseongdae. 30-minute walk full course. Quiet on weekdays from 14:00 to 17:00, crowds explode on weekends.

Q3. Seoul National University → Gangnam/Hongdae route?

Seoul National University Station (Line 2) → Gangnam Station (30 minutes, 5 stops) / Hongik University Station (25 minutes, 7 stops). adjacent area. Seoul National University Campus + Sharosu-gil + Gangnam or Hongdae 1 full day available.

Q4. What are the foreigner-friendly stores in Gwanak-gu?

Meat Grillers, Coffee Stir-fry Woman, Honne, Buksaetong English menu + card payment + foreigner-friendly standard. Sillim Bar is mainly in Korean, but has photo menus and staff friendly.

Q5. Gwanak Mountain hiking course?

Seoul National University main gate (Gwanaksan Mountain hiking trail entrance) → Yeonjuam → Summit → Shrine / Gwacheon. About 4 to 5 hours. 1st place in spring and fall. Foreign family + hiking club OK. #148 Hiking guide connection.

Q6. When is the Seoul National University festival?

2nd to 3rd week of May every year (3 days) — Campus open + stage performance + student booth. Free entry for foreigners. 1st place in Korea University Festival Deep Dive.

Q7. Is Sharosu-gil safe at night?

24-hour safety — Seoul National University student/MZ hot zone + CCTV + plainclothes police patrol. Nighttime OK for foreigners. Kakao Taxi recommended after midnight.

Q8. Is it possible to connect Sillim + Sadang + Seocho?

OK — Sillim (Line 2) → Sadang (Transfer to Lines 2 and 4) → Seocho (Line 2) and Gangnam (Line 2) = 10 to 20 minutes by subway. 1 full day OK.
