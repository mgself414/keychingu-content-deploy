🎯 Why This Everland + Caribbean Bay (캐리비안베이) Guide?

Everland (에버랜드) is Korea's #1 theme park, established in 1976 and home to the T-Express (T-익스프레스) (Korea's #1 roller coaster with a 77° drop), the Tulip Festival (튤립 축제) (spring), and Halloween (핼러윈) (autumn) matrix. Caribbean Bay (캐리비안베이) is Korea's #1 water park (established 1996) and the #1 summer enthusiast matrix. Foreign visitors often have these pain points: (a) the misconception that "Everland (에버랜드) is just a regular theme park," (b) not realizing it's Korea's #1 theme park, operating for 50+ years since 1976, and home to the T-Express (T-익스프레스), Korea's #1 roller coaster, (c) underestimating its zoo (Korea's #1 safari), (d) overlooking its seasonal matrix (spring tulips, summer water park, autumn Halloween (핼러윈), winter illuminations), and (e) not knowing about its full English support and appeal to MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) and family enthusiasts.

This guide covers 5 pillars, 12 signatures + T-Express + Water Park + Seasons.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Everland (에버랜드) = Just a regular theme park": It's Korea's #1 theme park, operating for 50+ years since 1976, and home to the T-Express (T-익스프레스), Korea's #1 roller coaster.

2. "T-Express (T-익스프레스) = Just a regular roller coaster": It's Korea's #1 with a 77° drop and 6.6km track.

3. "Zoo = Small zoo": It's Korea's #1 safari, Lost Valley (로스트 밸리).

4. "Seasons = Only spring": Spring tulips + summer water park + autumn Halloween (핼러윈) + winter illuminations = Year-round matrix.

5. "Caribbean Bay (캐리비안베이) = Just a regular water park": It's Korea's #1, operating since 1996, and the #1 summer enthusiast matrix.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Top priority for first-time foreign visitors: Everland (에버랜드) one-day pass = full day · Check the seasonal matrix.

2. T-Express (T-익스프레스) line: Weekday mornings = 15-30 minutes · Weekends + peak season = 1-2 hours · Q-Pass (Q-패스) recommended (₩15,000-30,000).

3. Caribbean Bay (캐리비안베이) season: Full summer season (June-August) · Year-round OK (indoor pools).

4. Everland (에버랜드) + Caribbean Bay (캐리비안베이): Integrated pass · 1-night package recommended.

5. Allergies: Restaurants provide allergen information in advance · Full English menu available.

6. Recommended persona: Families · Children = Lost Valley (로스트 밸리) + Seasons / MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) · Insta = T-Express (T-익스프레스) + Halloween (핼러윈) · Illuminations / Enthusiasts = 1-Night Stay + Full Package.
