📍 Bucheon — Korea Manhwa Museum

The Korea Manhwa Museum (1 Gilju-ro, Wonmi-gu, Bucheon-si, Sangdong (부천시 원미구 길주로 1, 상동), ☎ 032-310-3090~1) opened on October 12, 2001. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the first and largest museum dedicated to manhwa in Korea, preserving and exhibiting rare materials and the original drawings of veteran artists. You can see how manhwa has changed from the 1900s to the present, and alongside the permanent exhibition there are special exhibitions and hands-on and educational programs.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:00).
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok holidays.
  • Parking: Available (free).

Bucheon International Comics Festival — 29th Edition, 2026

The Bucheon International Comics Festival (부천국제만화축제) was held around the Korea Manhwa Museum and the Korea Manhwa Contents Agency (한국만화영상진흥원) (☎ 032-310-3071). The 29th edition in 2026 was held September 18~20 under the theme "만화·웹툰 만만합니다" ("Comics and Webtoons Are Easy").

  • Hours: 10:00~20:00, Sunday 10:00~18:00.
  • Admission: Paid, 5,000 won (3,000 won with advance registration).
  • Programs: According to the information registered with the Korea Tourism Organization, they included manhwa planning and special exhibitions, the Gyeonggi International Cosplay Festival (경기국제코스프레페스티벌), artist talks and signing sessions, animation screenings, outdoor market booths, an amateur cosplay contest, food trucks, an outdoor manhwa reading room and more. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the International Cosplay Championship (국제코스프레챔피언십), with cosplayers from 14 countries around the world, was also on the program.

📍 Icheon — Cheonggang Cartoon History Museum

The Cheonggang Cartoon History Museum (389-94 Cheonggangchang-ro, Majang-myeon, Icheon-si (이천시 마장면 청강가창로 389-94), ☎ 031-639-5790) opened on December 10, 2002. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is based on the rare manhwa materials that Cheonggang College of Cultural Industries (청강문화산업대학교) has collected since it opened in 1996, and it shows the history of manhwa by theme with some 2,000 kinds of rare Korean manhwa materials, including publications and handwritten manuscripts. There are also exhibitions related to animation and games.

  • Opening hours: Weekdays 09:00~17:00 (last entry 16:30).
  • Closed days: Weekends, public holidays.
  • Activities: Manhwa and animation creation workshops and more. Parking: Available.

📍 Chuncheon — Animation Museum and Toy Robot Museum

The Animation Museum of the Chuncheon Animation Museum and Toy Robot Museum (854 Baksa-ro, Seo-myeon, Chuncheon-si (춘천시 서면 박사로 854), ☎ 033-245-6470) opened on October 1, 2003. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it introduces the origins and types of animation, production techniques and processes, and the history of Korean and world animation, and you can try making sound effects and dubbing. In the Toy Robot Museum, visited together with it, you can try drones, spider robots, RC cars and more.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:00).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, Seollal and Chuseok Day.
  • Admission: Individuals 7,000 won, Chuncheon citizens 5,000 won, groups 5,600 won. Free for children under 24 months and others.

📍 Gimpo — Gimpo Manhwa Library

The Gimpo Manhwa Library (63 Sugi-ro, Gochon-eup, Gimpo-si (김포시 고촌읍 수기로 63), inquiries to the Gimpo City Library Division (김포시 도서관과) ☎ 031-5186-4836) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, the first manhwa-focused library in Gimpo, made in December 2024 by remodeling the first floor of the Civil Service Call Center into a small library. It has about 6,200 manhwa volumes and about 2,000 general books, ranging from classic manhwa to webtoons that are the source of films and dramas, educational manhwa and graphic novels.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~21:00 (preparation time 12:00~13:00).
  • Closed days: Every Monday, statutory public holidays.
  • Parking: Free.

📍 Uljin — Lee Hyun-se Manhwa Street

The Lee Hyun-se Manhwa Street (3 Maehwa 4-gil, Maehwa-myeon, Uljin-gun (울진군 매화면 매화4길 3), inquiries to the Uljin County Office Culture and Tourism Division (울진군청 문화관광과) ☎ 054-789-6903) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, a street where the works of Lee Hyun-se (이현세), a leading manhwa artist of the 1980s and 1990s, have been turned into murals. About 50 panels are painted along the wall from the entrance of the Maehwa-myeon office to the welfare center.

  • Opening hours: Open at all times, year-round. Parking: Available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The Cheonggang Cartoon History Museum is closed on weekends — It is open only on weekdays 09:00~17:00, and weekends and public holidays are registered as closed.
  2. Many places are closed on Mondays — The Korea Manhwa Museum, the Chuncheon Animation Museum and the Gimpo Manhwa Library are registered as closed every Monday (Chuncheon closes the next day if Monday is a public holiday).
  3. The Gimpo Manhwa Library has a 12:00~13:00 preparation time — It is listed as preparation time in the opening hours registered with the Korea Tourism Organization.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. When was the 2026 Bucheon International Comics Festival held?

A. The 29th edition in 2026 was held September 18~20 around the Korea Manhwa Museum and the Korea Manhwa Contents Agency.

Q. Is there an admission fee?

A. The Chuncheon Animation Museum and Toy Robot Museum is registered at 7,000 won for individuals, and the Bucheon International Comics Festival at 5,000 won (3,000 won with advance registration). For the other 4 places, the Korea Tourism Organization data has no admission entry.
