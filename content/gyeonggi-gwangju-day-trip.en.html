A Day Trip from Seoul to Gwangju, Gyeonggi (경기 광주) — Booking Hwadam Botanic Garden (화담숲) for Fall Color and Walking the Namhansanseong (남한산성) Wall: How to Reach "the Other Gwangju" That Shares the Name

Subtitle: Gwangju-si (광주시) in Gyeonggi-do, about 25km southeast of Seoul, is a different city from Gwangju Metropolitan City in Jeonnam · Hwadam Botanic Garden's 2026 autumn peak season runs 10/23~11/15 with 100% advance online booking and no on-site ticketing, adults ₩11,000, monorail extra · Namhansanseong is free 24 hours; only the Haenggung (행궁) palace charges ₩2,000 and closes Mondays · Buses 52 and 9 from Line 8 Sanseong Station (산성역) take 26~43 min · Gonjiam's someori-gukbap (beef-head soup) street — as of 2026-10-19

🎯 Same "Gwangju", Different City — This Guide Is Gwangju in Gyeonggi

Korea has two cities written Gwangju in the Roman alphabet. One is Gwangju Metropolitan City (광주광역시) in Jeollanam-do, the big southern city you reach by KTX (#319, #1026). The other is Gwangju-si, Gyeonggi-do (경기도 광주시), which sits about 25km southeast of Seoul (Gwangju City press release, 2026-04). This guide is about the latter. If you type only "Gwangju" into a map app, Jeonnam comes up first, so search for Gwangju-si, Gyeonggi-do or for the destination itself (Hwadam Botanic Garden, Namhansanseong).

Two places in this city draw foreign visitors in autumn: Hwadam Botanic Garden (화담숲) in Docheok-myeon (도척면), an arboretum famous for fall color, and Namhansanseong (남한산성) in Namhansanseong-myeon (남한산성면), the mountain fortress where a Joseon king held out for 47 days in 1636 and now a UNESCO World Heritage site. On Reddit's r/koreatravel, questions about how to book Hwadam came up three times in the past year and questions about getting to Namhansanseong three times [Secondary — checked 2026-10-19].

The catch is different for each. Hwadam is about booking (no on-site tickets in autumn); Namhansanseong is about the bus (26~43 min by bus from the end of the subway line). The two are in the same city but about 25km apart, so seeing both in one day takes a plan.

Travel times with no official timetable are marked [Est.]. Before you set out, check again on a map app (Naver Map or KakaoMap).

① Hwadam Botanic Garden (화담숲) — In Autumn 2026, Booking Is Everything

  • Location and hours: 278-1 Docheogwit-ro (도척윗로), Docheok-myeon, Gwangju-si, Gyeonggi-do, +82-31-8026-6666. 09:00~18:00 (last admission 17:00), closed every Monday (check the website for Monday operation during peak season), subject to change by season and weather (KTO English official · TourAPI). 16 themed gardens with about 4,000 plant species (KTO English)
  • Fees: adults ₩11,000, teens and seniors ₩9,000, children ₩7,000, free under age 2. The monorail is not included in admission (KTO English official). Monorail section 1 ₩5,000, loop ₩9,000 [Secondary — 2026-09 press and blogs]
  • 2026 autumn peak season 10/23 (Fri) ~ 11/15 (Sun): ticket sales opened 9/16 at 13:00, and during this period it is 100% advance online booking with no on-site ticketing (only leftover monorail tickets are sold on site). Peak color is announced as late October to early November [Secondary — Wikitree 2026-09-16]. The daily cap is 10,000 visitors (Gwangju City press release 2026-04)
  • Where to book: the reservation menu on hwadamsup.com (the official site loads only through scripts, so we could not read its text this time). We could not officially confirm whether online booking works with a foreign phone number — a 2023 Gyeonggi-do English blog said "without a Korean number, buy on the day with your passport", but with no on-site ticketing in autumn 2026 it is safest to assume that route is closed
  • A package for foreigners (fallback): a shuttle-plus-ticket package from Seoul. The Trazy product runs Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri from 4/1 to 11/20, departing Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역) 08:45, Namsan Yejang Bus Terminal (남산 예장 터미널) 09:15 and Dongdaemun (동대문) 09:30, foreign passport required (not sold to Koreans), admission included, monorail not included (sales page checked 2026-10-19; the price is not shown on the page and the booking box showed CLOSED — we could not tell whether dates were sold out or the widget failed to load, so check availability yourself). Klook also lists a Hwadam plus Korean Folk Village (한국민속촌) day tour [Secondary — product page not opened]
  • Walk vs monorail: a 2026-04 KTO article recommends "getting off at monorail platform 2 or 3 and walking down (40~60 min)". In peak season, monorail slots fill up faster than admission tickets [Secondary]

② From Gonjiam Station (곤지암역) to Hwadam

  • Train: Gyeonggang Line (경강선) Gonjiam Station. From Gangnam Station take the Sinbundang Line to Pangyo Station and transfer to the Gyeonggang Line: about 40 min from Gangnam and twenty-odd minutes from Pangyo [Secondary — press]. The Gyeonggang Line runs about every 20 min (cut to 18 min at rush hour), so move by the train times [Secondary]
  • Shuttle bus: a shuttle runs between Gonjiam Station and Hwadam every 30 min on weekdays and every 20 min on weekends (Gwangju City press release 2026-04-02 — a spring-season release, so the autumn peak interval is unconfirmed). There are also notices saying it leaves from Exit 2 and is free, but we could not open the original to confirm [Secondary]
  • City bus: from Gonjiam Station take bus Gwangju 9 to the "Hwadamsup" stop, then walk about 21 min, or 11 min by taxi (KTO travel article 2026-04)
  • If you are short on time, take a taxi from the station. The address is in ① above

③ Namhansanseong (남한산성) — The Fortress Is Free, Only the Palace Charges

  • UNESCO World Heritage (inscribed 2014 [Secondary, cross-checked]). According to the Korea Heritage Service's English description, it is a large-scale mountain fortress that Joseon built in the 17th century in preparation for war with the Manchu Qing, where King Injo withstood a 100,000-strong Qing army under Emperor Taizong for 47 days in 1636 (Korea Heritage Service, English)
  • Know the two-tier structure first (Gyeonggi-do Namhansanseong World Heritage Center, updated 2026-09-10):
Item Fee Hours Closed
Namhansanseong (walls and trails) Free (admission fee abolished 2007-01-01) Always open, year round Never
Namhansanseong Haenggung (행궁) Adults ₩2,000, teens ₩1,000 · free for ages 6 and under, 65 and over, Gyeonggi residents and hanbok wearers Apr~Oct 10:00~18:00 / Nov~Mar 10:00~17:00, tickets sold until 30 min before closing Every Monday (except public holidays)
  • Getting there (official): from Line 8 Sanseong Station (산성역), take bus 52 or 9 (9-1 on holidays) to Namhansanseong (last stop), 26~43 min (Gyeonggi center). From Jamsil Station by subway to Sanseong Station, it is about 44 min in total including the bus. Notices say the boarding stop is the Korea Polytech (한국폴리텍) stop by Exit 2 (VisitKorea search summary, original not opened [Secondary]), so confirm the stop on the signs or a map app
  • From Gyeonggi Gwangju Station (경기광주역): bus 15-1 to Namhansanseong (last stop), about 58 min (Gyeonggi center) — this is the connecting leg for Plan C

④ Both in One Day — Doable, but Tight

1. Book Hwadam for the first morning slot (09:00 entry — leave Seoul around 07:15). Ride the monorail for section 1 only and walk down, and two and a half hours is enough, out by 11:30

2. Shuttle or bus to Gonjiam Station by about 12:15 → Gyeonggang Line to Gyeonggi Gwangju Station ([Est. about 10 min]) → bus 15-1, about 58 min (official) → Namhansanseong last stop at 14:00~14:30

3. Haenggung (closes 18:00 in October, 17:00 from November) → Course 2, 3km, 1 hr → down by 17:00 → bus 52 or 9 → Sanseong Station → Line 8. Check last-bus times on the stop signs or a map app

  • Alternative route: Gonjiam Station → Gyeonggang Line to Imae Station (이매역) → Suin-Bundang Line to Moran Station (모란역) → Line 8 to Sanseong Station [Est. 50~60 min] → bus. Two transfers, similar time
  • Pick one lunch — someori-gukbap in front of Gonjiam Station (before moving) or the traditional food village at Namhansanseong (after arriving). Both and the schedule will not hold

⚠ 5 Traps That Actually Catch People

1. Ending up in Gwangju, Jeonnam — in map and booking apps, "Gwangju" brings up Jeonnam first. Gwangju-si in Gyeonggi is not the KTX; it is the Gyeonggang Line and Line 8.

2. Going to Hwadam without a booking — in autumn 2026 (10/23~11/15) there is no on-site ticketing. If online booking with a foreign number does not work, look into a passport-required package (Trazy, Klook), noting that Trazy's booking box showed CLOSED as of 10/19.

3. Mondays — Hwadam closed, the Haenggung closed. Among the gukbap places, Choemija and Bongcheon Makguksu also close Mondays.

4. The Haenggung closes at 17:00 in November — if you arrive in the afternoon on Plan C, go into the palace first and walk the wall after.

5. The Gyeonggang Line's 20 min headway — miss a train at Gonjiam Station and it is 20 min to the next one. Line that up with the shuttle (every 20~30 min) and an hour disappears.

⚙ FAQ 3

1. Can I do both in one day without a car? — Yes (④). Enter Hwadam at 09:00, take bus 15-1 from Gyeonggi Gwangju Station, and in November do the Haenggung first. For a relaxed pace, split them into two days as Plan A and Plan B.

2. I could not get a Hwadam booking. — In peak season there is no on-site ticketing. Look at the Trazy package (Tue~Fri, foreign passport required — the booking box showed CLOSED as of 10/19, so check availability), or move your date past 11/15 outside peak season (check the website for whether it is open).

3. If I pick just one? — If foliage is the goal, Hwadam; if you want to walk without spending, Namhansanseong (fortress free, Haenggung ₩2,000). Both are outdoors, so skip rainy days.
