Seoul Silver Grass & Pink Muhly — Autumn's Version of Cherry Blossoms: The Complete Grass Season Guide

Subtitle: If spring has cherry blossoms, autumn has grass — a late-September-to-early-November season guide to silver grass at Haneul Park (하늘공원) and pink muhly at Olympic Park (올림픽공원). 5 spots + a peak-week calendar + a sunset-linked route (#1308) + a pre-trip checklist.

ISSUE 04 · #1313 · 2026-08-20

🎯 What Grass Season Is — the Other Color of Autumn Foreigners Miss

Korean autumn usually means red foliage (#229) — but Seoul's autumn has two more colors: wind-swept silver grass and cotton-candy-like pink muhly grass. Short like cherry blossom season, and just as photo-driven — this is autumn's cherry blossom.

The two seasons overlap in early-to-mid October — those two weeks are the peak of Seoul's grass season.

① 5 Spots

1. Haneul Park (하늘공원) — the silver grass sanctuary

  • The whole hill inside World Cup Park (월드컵공원) is silver grass. Every mid-October the Seoul Silver Grass Festival (서울억새축제) runs (free, based on past years — check the schedule before you go), with a night opening and lit-up grass during festival week.
  • Silver grass + sunset is Seoul's #1 autumn photo combo — the same location as Spot 5 in the sunset guide (#1308), same formula: arrive 45 minutes before golden hour.
  • 20+ min walk from World Cup Stadium Station (월드컵경기장역), or the 291 steps, or the Maengkkongi electric car (맹꽁이 전기차, paid).

2. Noeul Park (노을공원·난지 일대) — Haneul Park's quiet neighbor

  • The alternative when Haneul Park is packed during festival week. Same Sangam (상암) cluster, so it pairs easily with Haneul Park for a half-day route.

3. Olympic Park Wildflower Hill (올림픽공원 들꽃마루) — the face of pink muhly

  • Seoul's best-known pink muhly field. In autumn, pink muhly and yellow cosmos fill the hill (the planting mix can vary year to year — check the park's notice before visiting).
  • Walking distance from Mongchontoseong Station (몽촌토성역) and Hanseong Baekje Station (한성백제역). Easy to pair with a Jamsil (잠실) day (Lotte World (롯데월드), Seokchon Lake (석촌호수)).

4. Seoul Forest (서울숲) — the most central and accessible

  • A ginkgo path, reeds, and an autumn garden all in one park — flows naturally into a Seongsu (성수) day (#740 Seoul Forest + Seongsu Autumn). More of an "autumn all-rounder" than a grass-only stop.

5. Yangju Nari Park (양주 나리공원) — the day-trip option

  • The go-to for a grass day trip near Seoul — a globe amaranth and pink muhly autumn flower festival typically runs late Sept to mid Oct (free, check dates on-site). A same-day trip by subway plus bus.
  • Bigger in scale than the in-city spots — worth the trip if photos are the goal.

🧳 Pre-Trip Checklist

  • [ ] Check your travel week against the calendar — see which window your dates fall in. If pink muhly is the goal, mid-October onward is too late
  • [ ] Skip or accept the festival-weekend crowds — weekend afternoons during the silver grass festival peak with people. For photos, weekday afternoon + sunset finish (the #1308 formula) is the answer
  • [ ] Work backward from sunset time — Haneul Park's formula is arriving 45 minutes before sunset (October sunset ≈ 17:30–18:10). Add 20 more minutes if you're waiting for the Maengkkongi car
  • [ ] Dress for wind — it's windy up on the hill. A light jacket + walking shoes (for the 291 steps)
  • [ ] No drones — most of Seoul's downtown parks are no-fly zones. Bring a selfie stick or mini tripod instead
