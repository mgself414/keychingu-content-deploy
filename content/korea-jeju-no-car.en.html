Foreigner's Car-Free Jeju Guide — 12 Picks Across a 4-Day Bus & Taxi Route

> Subtitle: Complete public-transport playbook for foreigners without an International Driving Permit — Express·Trunk·Sightseeing Loop Bus 810/820 + KakaoT + East/West day routes + where-to-base strategy

ISSUE 04 · #1212 · 2026-07-02

🎯 Why a "Car-Free Jeju" Guide

The biggest pain point for foreigners visiting Jeju Island (제주도) is the rental car. Korea usually requires an International Driving Permit (IDP) to rent, so travelers without an IDP — non-drivers, solo travelers — get stuck at "Is Jeju even doable without a car?" That's exactly why questions like "4 nights in Jeju without a car — where to stay and what's realistic by bus?" keep coming up on Reddit's r/koreatravel and r/jeju (a real, measured demand signal).

Bottom line first: Since Jeju's 2017 public-transport overhaul, the bus network is dense. Combine the Express (red)·Trunk (blue)·Branch (green) buses + the Sightseeing Loop Bus 810/820 with KakaoT (카카오T) taxi, and you can cover the core 12 picks in 3 nights / 4 days with no rental car. The keys are choosing your base well and splitting your route East vs West.

This guide covers 12 car-free signatures + a 3-night/4-day bus route + where-to-base strategy + real-time app setup.

🚌 The 4 Car-Free Transport Tools — Deep Dive

① Express Bus (급행버스, red, 100-series)

  • Signature: Fewest stops between the airport and major hubs·fastest Jeju↔Seogwipo
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆ (routes shown in English on KakaoMap)
  • Tip: 101/102 = coastal loop express·fastest airport→Seogwipo/Seongsan direction

② Trunk Bus (간선버스, blue, 200–300 series)

  • Signature: Main workhorse linking sights·201 (east coast)·202 (west coast)·281 (across Hallasan)
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆
  • Fare: base ₩1,150–1,200 (~$0.85 / ~6 RMB / ~120 JPY) by transit card·distance surcharge on long legs

③ Sightseeing Loop Bus 810/820 (관광지 순환버스)

  • Signature: 810 = East (Daecheon–Seongsan, Geomunoreum, etc.)·820 = West (Jungmun–Osulloc, Hwasun, etc.) — loops sights only
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★★ (car-free optimized·flat ₩1,150)
  • Tip: Hop multiple sights in one day·the backbone of the East/West day routes

④ KakaoT Taxi (카카오T)

  • Signature: Fills trunk-line gaps·late night·when you have luggage — last-mile
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆ (English app·register a card)
  • Tip: Jeju taxis can be slow to dispatch outside the city center → leave buffer time·save destinations in Korean

⚠ 5 Common Car-Free Mistakes

1. "Jeju is impossible without a car": The overhauled network + Loop Bus 810/820 + KakaoT covers the core 12 picks. The assumption is wrong.

2. "Any accommodation works": For car-free travel, your base is 80% of it. Lock into one: Jeju City (near airport·bus hub) or Seogwipo (Jungmun·Olle). Remote pensions = bus-desert hell.

3. "East and West in one day": Jeju is big (3+ hrs round trip). Split East and West across different days to be realistic.

4. "Ignore the last bus": Branch and loop buses stop early (6–8 PM). Keep KakaoT as backup for after-sunset moves.

5. "U-do·Seongsan same-day round trip is tight": Give Seongsan + U-do a full day. Check ferry timetables in advance.

⚙ FAQ (6)

1. Do I really not need a car?: Core sights are fine by bus. But deep-interior oreum (volcanic cones)·night moves — pair with KakaoT.

2. How does Loop Bus 810/820 work?: East = 810, West = 820. Flat ₩1,150·loops major sights. Check real-time on KakaoMap (카카오맵).

3. Seongsan sunrise: Open 04:00~20:00·entry ₩5,000 (~$3.7)·sunrise ~05:00–07:00 by season.

4. Getting to U-do: Ferry from Seongsan Port ~15 min·round trip ~₩10,500·island loop bus/e-bikes on U-do.

5. Hallasan car-free: Buses reach the Seongpanak (성판악, Trunk 281) and Eorimok (어리목, 240) trailheads. Summit Baengnokdam (백록담) needs advance reservation.

6. Recommended personas: Solo·non-driver = Jeju City base + East/West days / Couple = Seogwipo + U-do / Budget = bus + free nature (Hyeopjae·Osulloc tea fields).

⚠ Medical·Safety·Dietary Disclaimer

Hallasan hiking = pregnant travelers·heart patients consult a doctor·winter crampons·Seongpanak summit advance reservation. Hairtail sashimi·abalone = shellfish/fish allergy caution. Prepare for motion sickness on long bus rides. Emergency ☎ 1339·1330·119.

👥 5 Segment Routes

Solo Traveler

  • Recommended: single Jeju City base + East/West day routes (use loop buses)
  • Budget: ₩250,000~400,000/person (3N4D)
  • Signature: cafes·beaches·markets·solo-friendly black-pork nofo (노포, 30+ year old restaurants)

Couple

  • Recommended: Seogwipo base + U-do cycling + part of the Olle Trail (bus + walk)
  • Budget: ₩500,000~800,000/2 people (3N)
  • Signature: sunsets (Hyeopjae·Jungmun)·cafes·haenyeo (해녀, women free-divers) eateries

Family with Kids

  • Recommended: Jeju City base + loop-bus hopping + KakaoT backup
  • Budget: ₩600,000~1,000,000/4 people (3N)
  • Signature: U-do·beaches·folk village·strollers: pair with taxis

Millennial/Gen-Z

  • Recommended: Woljeongri·Hamdeok·Hyeopjae cafe strips (walkable from bus)
  • Budget: ₩250,000~450,000/person
  • Signature: ocean-view cafes·insta spots·U-do e-bikes

Budget Traveler

  • Recommended: bus + free nature (Hyeopjae·Osulloc tea fields·Hallasan entry free)
  • Budget: ₩200,000~350,000/person
  • Signature: market street food·free nature·guesthouses·transit-card transfer discounts
