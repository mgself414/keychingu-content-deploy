3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. The "Agency HQ = Free Entry" Misconception

The HYBE, SM, YG, and JYP headquarters allow free access to exterior photo spots, but the interiors are either paid exhibition halls (HYBE INSIGHT) or closed to the public entirely. HYBE INSIGHT requires an advance Naver reservation — walk-up tickets are typically sold out on weekends.

2. The "CF Filming Locations Are Always Open" Misconception

Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌 한옥마을) — a regular backdrop for Samsung and Hyundai Motor CFs — most closely resembles its on-screen look before 10 a.m. On weekend afternoons, tourist crowds make it hard to recreate the "quiet CF alley" atmosphere.

3. The "Billboards & OOH Ads Show Anytime" Misconception

The K-POP SQUARE in Gangnam (강남) (SM's exterior wall) and the OOH ads near Hongdae (홍대) are concentrated around idol comeback seasons. Fandom-funded ads (birthday and support ads) rotate frequently by season, so checking X (Twitter) hashtags like #GangnamAd and #CoexAd beforehand is the realistic way to confirm posting schedules.

12 Picks, 4 Axes — Places & Experience Guide

Axis 1: K-Advertising Museums & Agency Experience Spaces (3 picks)

1. HYBE INSIGHT

  • Location: 42 Hangang-daero, Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seoul (5-min walk from Yongsan Station)
  • Admission: KRW 29,000 for adults / KRW 2,000 discount for online reservations
  • Hours: Tue-Sun 10:00-19:00 (closed Mondays, last entry 18:00)
  • Highlights: Exhibits on album artwork, CF behind-the-scenes footage, and music video production for HYBE artists including BTS, SEVENTEEN, and NewJeans. Original advertising campaign objects — costumes, props, original poster art — on physical display.
  • Foreign visitor fit: Full English, Japanese, and Chinese guidance throughout. Audio guide app provided. Google Maps review language distribution suggests an estimated 60%+ foreign visitor share.
  • Reservations: Naver Reservations or hybe.com. Weekend and holiday slots often sell out 2 weeks ahead → weekday morning slots recommended.

2. SMTown COEX Artium

  • Location: 513 Yeongdong-daero, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, COEX Artium (5-min walk from Samseong Station)
  • Admission: Free entry to floors 1-3 / goods store and photo booths are paid
  • Hours: 11:00-22:00 (open year-round)
  • Highlights: SM's official pop-ups, exhibitions, and goods store. The K-POP SQUARE LED screen on the exterior wall is a landmark of Gangnam's OOH advertising scene. CF behind-the-scenes goods for EXO, aespa, NCT, and SHINee always on display.
  • Foreign visitor fit: Easy to reach along the COEX Mall route from Samseong Station. High share of foreign visitors.
  • Tip: K-POP SQUARE ads alternate between official SM campaigns and fandom-funded ads. Search X (Twitter) for "K-POP SQUARE ad" to check the posting schedule for your favorite idol's ad in advance.

3. YG Headquarters Exterior + YG Street, Hongdae

  • Location: YG headquarters — 10-min walk from Hapjeong Station / YG Street — 5-min walk from Hongik University Station
  • Admission: Free (exterior viewing)
  • Highlights: YG Street is packed with BLACKPINK and BIGBANG fandom goods stores and cafes. Not an official company space, but the block where you can experience the fandom economy most directly.
  • Tip: The YG headquarters exterior photo spot is freely accessible. Interior entry is not permitted. BLACKPINK×Chanel collaboration ad objects are only displayed during official pop-up event periods.

Axis 2: CF Filming Location Pilgrimage (3 picks)

4. Bukchon Hanok Village — Samsung, Hyundai Motor & K-Beauty CF Backdrop

  • Location: Gyedong-gil and Bukchon-ro area, Jongno-gu, Seoul — 5-min walk from Anguk Station (안국역)
  • Admission: Free
  • Best visit time: 9:00-10:00 a.m. (before tourist crowds arrive)
  • CF connections: Samsung Galaxy S series, Hyundai IONIQ, Sulwhasoo, and Innisfree have all filmed CFs against Bukchon's alleys. The staircase view known as the "8 Views of Bukchon" appears repeatedly in domestic and international ads.
  • Foreign visitor fit: English, Chinese, and Japanese signage available. Hanbok rental lets you recreate CF-style photos (KRW 15,000-30,000 per person). This is a residential neighborhood — visit quietly before 10 a.m.

5. DDP (Dongdaemun Design Plaza) — K-Advertising & Media Art Hub

  • Location: 281 Eulji-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul (1-min walk from Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station)
  • Admission: Free for the exterior and plaza / KRW 5,000-15,000 for special exhibitions
  • Hours: Exterior open 24 hours / exhibition halls 10:00-20:00
  • CF connections: Brand collaboration pop-ups such as NewJeans×Coca-Cola and aespa×Gentle Monster are held here repeatedly. The media art facade (exterior LED) is most vivid after 8 p.m.
  • Foreign visitor fit: Full English signage. Zaha Hadid's design makes the exterior an internationally recognized architectural landmark. Check ddp.or.kr for the special exhibition schedule.

6. Gwanghwamun Square & Sejong-daero OOH Advertising Street

  • Location: 172 Sejong-daero, Jongno-gu, Seoul (1-min walk from Gwanghwamun Station)
  • Admission: Free
  • CF connections: A concentrated zone for large banners and bus shelter ads tied to product launches and national event campaigns. Global Samsung, LG, and Hyundai Motor campaigns are posted here in bilingual English-Korean format.
  • Foreign visitor fit: The top foreign-tourist spot in Seoul. Photo compositions combining the King Sejong statue, Gyeongbokgung Palace, and large OOH ads in a single frame appear repeatedly on social media.
  • Tip: OOH density peaks around national holidays (March 1st Independence Movement Day, Liberation Day) when major campaign ads are typically swapped out.

Axis 3: Global K-Advertising Icon Sites (3 picks)

7. Gangnam K-POP SQUARE (SM COEX Exterior LED)

  • Location: Samseong-dong, Gangnam-gu, Seoul (right outside Samseong Station Exit 6)
  • Admission: Free / LED runs from sunrise to 22:00
  • Highlights: A large LED screen on the exterior wall of COEX Artium. Official ads for aespa, EXO, and NCT alternate with fandom-funded ads (birthday ads).
  • Tip: Follow X hashtags like #GangnamAd and #CoexAd for the fandom ad schedule. Many tweets are in English, so foreign visitors can track them too.

8. Hongdae K-pop Street & Fandom Advertising

  • Location: Hongik-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul (2-min walk from Hongik University Station)
  • Admission: Free
  • Highlights: A dense zone of fandom advertising — subway screen doors, bus shelters, and digital signage. BTS, SEVENTEEN, and NewJeans fandom ads are regularly posted on the screen doors at Hongik University Station.
  • Foreign visitor fit: The top commercial district for foreign visitors. Packed with fandom goods stores and used record shops.
  • Tip: Screen door ads rotate daily. Visiting on an idol's comeback day gives the best odds of seeing the official comeback ad. Comeback schedules are typically announced on official social media 2 weeks in advance.

9. Itaewon (이태원) & Hannam Luxury K-AD Street (BTS×LV / BLACKPINK×Chanel)

  • Location: Itaewon-ro and Hannam-daero area, Yongsan-gu, Seoul (5-10 min walk from Itaewon or Hannam Station)
  • Admission: Free for the street / pop-up events separate (free to around KRW 10,000)
  • Highlights: Global luxury brand collaboration pop-ups such as BTS×LV and BLACKPINK×Chanel are mostly held near the Hannam and Itaewon flagship stores. Large exterior ads go up each time a brand changes its ambassador.
  • Foreign visitor fit: Hannam-dong ranks among the top Seoul neighborhoods for foreign residents and visitors. Communicating in English is not difficult. Home to concentrated LV, Dior, and Gucci flagship stores.
  • Tip: Many pop-ups require advance reservation. Following the brand's official Korean social media is recommended. Even without an active pop-up, the flagship exteriors are worth visiting as photo spots.

Axis 4: Foreign Enthusiast Specialty Experiences (3 picks)

10. K-pop Fan Advertising Tour (Self-Guided Course)

  • Method: No separate ticket needed. Gather ad posting schedules from fandom communities (X, fan cafes) and visit the key spots directly.
  • Key spots: Gangnam K-POP SQUARE / Hongik University Station screen doors / COEX Mall digital signage / Sinchon U-Plex exterior / near Jamsil Lotte Tower
  • Cost: A 1-day subway pass at KRW 5,000 covers every spot.
  • Foreign visitor fit: A growing number of K-pop fans from Japan, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East travel to Korea specifically for "fan-devotion ad tours." Reviews shared actively on X under hashtags like #AdTour and #Seoul, easy for foreign visitors to search and follow.
  • Tip: Fandom ads are best lit in the morning (7-9 a.m.) and during evening rush hour (18:00-20:00).

11. K-Advertising Pop-Up Cafe & Goods Tour

  • Method: Official pop-up cafes run irregularly by HYBE, SM, JYP, and YG. Pop-up spaces are leased around Hongdae, Gangnam, and Seongsu during comeback and album release seasons.
  • Cost: Drinks KRW 6,000-12,000 / limited goods KRW 3,000-30,000
  • Foreign visitor fit: Pop-up interiors display CF and music video behind-the-scenes photos and props for an "ad set" feel. Official photocards and limited goods available for purchase.
  • Info sources: Official X accounts of HYBE, SM, JYP, YG / Weverse app notices. Pop-ups are typically announced a week ahead. Expect a 2-3 hour wait on opening day and weekends → visiting right after weekday opening (11:00-12:00) is recommended.

12. Korea Advertising Museum + TVCF Online Archive

  • Korea Advertising Museum: Located in Gwacheon (과천). Covers Korean advertising history from the 1900s to today. Free admission (as of June 2026, reconfirm before visiting). About 40 minutes by subway from central Seoul → pairs well with the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art in Gwacheon for a half-day course.
  • TVCF.co.kr: A database of Korean advertisements. Free access to original BTS and BLACKPINK ad footage. Accessible to foreign visitors without signing up. Enter an idol's name in the search bar to auto-find related CFs.
  • Foreign visitor fit: Ad footage itself carries a low language barrier. English signage inside the museum is limited, but since the experience centers on watching video, it works without English.

FAQ — 5 Questions Foreign Visitors Often Ask

Q1. What's the alternative if HYBE INSIGHT reservations are full?

Cancellations tend to appear right after weekday opening. Turning on notifications in the Naver Reservations app is recommended. Alternative: combine SMTown COEX Artium (free) with a DDP special exhibition for a similarly dense 1-day course.

Q2. Where can I see the BTS×LV collaboration ad in person?

During BTS comeback seasons, it appears on the exterior of the LV store in Hannam, on Gangnam's K-POP SQUARE, and on the large billboard on Sinmun-ro. It's not posted at all times, so checking BTS's official X account (@BTS_twt) or LV Korea's official Instagram for the posting schedule is essential.

Q3. Can I tour all the agency headquarters in one go?

HYBE (Yongsan) → SMTown COEX (Gangnam) → YG near Hongdae (Mapo) → JYP Cheongdam (Gangnam) works as a half-day to full-day route by subway and on foot. HYBE INSIGHT is the only headquarters with a formal paid interior tour. The rest are exterior photo spots only.

Q4. Where can I check the fandom ad tour schedule?

Searching X (Twitter) for "#[idol name] ad" or "#Seoul ad" is fastest. Many tweets are in English, so foreign visitors can track them too. Ads are typically announced 1-2 days before posting.

Q5. Which spots are suitable for visiting with children?

HYBE INSIGHT, DDP, Gwanghwamun Square, Bukchon Hanok Village, and SMTown COEX Artium are all fine for children. The Hongdae and Itaewon nighttime ad tours are not recommended due to crowding and late hours.

Season Matrix

Season K-Advertising Events Recommendation
Spring (Mar-May) First-half comeback season. Advertising and pop-ups concentrated. Best time for Bukchon CF filming locations ★★★★★
Summer (Jun-Aug) Summer CF and brand collaboration campaigns. Relatively quiet before MAMA ★★★
Fall (Sep-Nov) Second-half comeback season + MAMA (November). Advertising and pop-ups at their peak ★★★★★
Winter (Dec-Feb) Year-end campaigns + New Year brand ads. DDP night lighting + winter OOH ★★★★

The November MAMA season concentrates idol advertising across Seoul all at once. For K-pop fans, November is the year's peak month by "ad density."
