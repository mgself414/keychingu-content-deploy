✈️ Use + Payment (foreigner practical)

  • Payment: mostly card·transit card·easy-pay (KakaoPay·Samsung Pay)·some cash·photo booths take card
  • Kiosk: increasingly English·touch order
  • Entry: late-night unmanned convenience stores use app/QR·some card verification
  • Trust culture: no staff, but honest self-checkout — a distinctly Korean experience
  • Emergency: ☎ 1339·1330·119

⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "Unmanned = free/abandoned": It's a self-checkout trust culture — always use the kiosk·self-checkout.

2. "Cash only": Mostly card·easy-pay·unmanned stores are actually card-centric.

3. "Self photo booth = sticker photos": High-quality self photo booths (인생네컷)·props·frames·SNS.

4. "Kiosks are Korean-only": English is increasingly supported — look for the language button.

5. "Vending = drinks only": Many ramyeon·flower·cosmetics·goods vending machines.

⚙ FAQ (6)

1. Unmanned cafe payment?: Kiosk order·card/easy-pay·₩1,500~2,500 americano.

2. Using a self photo booth?: Enter booth·pose·props·₩4,000~6,000·instant print + QR.

3. Late-night unmanned convenience?: App/QR entry·self scan checkout·barcodes.

4. Where are quirky vending machines?: Highway rest stops (ramyeon)·subway stations (flowers)·tourist spots (goods).

5. English OK?: Kiosks increasingly support English·touch language select.

6. Recommended personas: SNS = self photo booth·goods vending / practical = unmanned cafe·laundry / quirky = ramyeon·flower vending.

⚠ Medical·Safety·Dietary Disclaimer

Instant-cook vending (ramyeon) = hot water caution. Check hygiene·expiry yourself at unmanned stores. Late-night unmanned store safety. Check allergy labels (meal kits·snacks). Emergency ☎ 1339·1330·119.
