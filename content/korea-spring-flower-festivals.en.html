Korea Spring Flower Festival Guide: Gwangyang Plum Blossom Village, Gurye and Icheon Cornelian Cherry Flower Festivals, Jinhae Gunhangje, Sinan Tulip Park, Cheongnamdae Yeongchunje, Danyang and Yeongju Sobaeksan Royal Azalea Festivals

Subtitle: Gwangyang Plum Blossom Village (March, always open) · Gurye Cornelian Cherry Village (always open) · 2026 Gurye Cornelian Cherry Flower Festival (March 14-22) · 2026 Icheon Baeksa Cornelian Cherry Flower Festival (April 3-5) · 2026 Jinhae Gunhangje (March 27-April 5) · Sinan Tulip Park (closed Mondays) · 2026 Cheongnamdae Yeongchunje (April 24-May 6, paid) · 2026 Sobaeksan Royal Azalea Festival (Danyang May 22-24, Yeongju May 23-24) — all 2026 festivals have ended and 2027 dates are not yet registered — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers Korea's spring flower festivals and flower spots in the order the flowers bloom. They are plum blossoms and cornelian cherry in March, cherry blossoms in March-April, spring tulips and the Cheongnamdae spring flowers in April-May, and royal azaleas on Sobaeksan in May.

All the 2026 festivals have ended. As of November 16, 2026, when this guide was written, the 2027 dates were not yet registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data. The festival information below is the 2026 registered values and is for reference only. Gwangyang Plum Blossom Village and Gurye Cornelian Cherry Village are always open, and Sinan Tulip Park is registered as a place you can visit during its open hours, except Mondays, regardless of festivals.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 March Plum Blossoms — Gwangyang Plum Blossom Village

Gwangyang Plum Blossom Village (광양 매화마을) [Mokgil 34-2 (목길 34-2), Daap-myeon (다압면), Gwangyang-si, ☎ 061-797-3333] is a village on the slopes of Baegunsan (백운산) by the lower Seomjingang (섬진강), where plum blossoms bloom every March. The Gwangyang Plum Blossom Festival is an event that opens the Cheongmaesil Nongwon (청매실농원), run by Hong Ssang-ri (홍쌍리), a Korean Food Master (대한민국식품명인), and its surroundings to welcome spring visitors. The farm provides the venue and the village residents prepare the festival food. Under the plum blossoms you can taste plum makgeolli (매실막걸리) and chive pancakes (부추전).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available (free).

📍 March-April Cornelian Cherry — Gurye Cornelian Cherry Village, Gurye Cornelian Cherry Flower Festival, Icheon Baeksa Cornelian Cherry Flower Festival

Gurye Cornelian Cherry Village (구례 산수유마을) [Wian-ri (위안리), Sandong-myeon (산동면), Gurye-gun, ☎ 061-783-1039] is a mountain ecovillage where cornelian cherry blooms yellow every March. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the cornelian cherry here accounts for more than 70% of the national total. A cornelian cherry festival is held in spring and a cornelian cherry fruit festival in autumn, and it is close to Section 7 of the Jirisan Dulle-gil (지리산 둘레길).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

2026 Gurye Cornelian Cherry Flower Festival (구례산수유꽃축제) [registered address Sangwan 1-gil 45 (상관1길 45), Sandong-myeon, Gurye-gun, event venue the Gurye Cornelian Cherry Flower Festival grounds or the Cornelian Cherry Culture Center (산수유문화관), ☎ 061-780-2390] is held every March around Sandong-myeon on the slopes of Jirisan (지리산). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is set against the largest cornelian cherry colony in the country. In 2026 it ran March 14-22, 09:00-18:00, and it was free.

  • Registered 2026 programs: A harvest-wish rite, an opening performance, a cornelian cherry fruit-pitting contest, a cornelian cherry rice cake pounding experience, making children's characters and key rings, a cornelian cherry flower road walk and busking.

2026 Icheon Baeksa Cornelian Cherry Flower Festival (이천백사 산수유꽃축제) [Wonjeok-ro 775beon-gil 12 (원적로775번길 12), Baeksa-myeon (백사면), Icheon-si, ☎ Icheon-si (이천시) 031-631-2104] has Icheon Cornelian Cherry Village (이천산수유마을) as its registered event venue. The area at the foot of Wonjeoksan (원적산) around Dorip-ri (도립리), Songmal-ri (송말리) and Gyeongsa-ri (경사리) is the cornelian cherry growing area, and trees over 100 years old form a colony, including Dorip-ri, whose whole village is covered in cornelian cherry trees. In 2026 it ran April 3-5, 10:00-17:00. Admission was free and some experiences were paid.

  • Registered 2026 programs: A local singing contest, a celebratory performance, making cornelian-cherry-shaped cookies and wooden photo crafts, traditional games (swing, seesaw jumping and tuho pitch-pot), and food such as cornelian cherry hangwa, makgeolli and green onion pancakes (파전).

📍 March-April Cherry Blossoms — Jinhae Gunhangje

2026 Jinhae Gunhangje (진해군항제) [Changwon-si Jinhae-gu area (창원시 진해구 일원), ☎ 055-546-4310] began on April 13, 1952, when a statue of Admiral Yi Sun-sin (이순신) was erected at Bukwon Rotary (북원로터리) and a memorial rite was held, and it has run under the name Jinhae Gunhangje since 1963. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, more than 2 million domestic and international visitors come during the festival, and Jinhae has 360,000 flowering cherry trees. In 2026 it ran March 27-April 5. It was free, and the Cherry Blossom Music Festival (체리블라썸뮤직페스티벌) was paid.

  • Registered 2026 programs: An opening ceremony, a military band and honor guard festival, an Admiral Yi memorial rite and victory procession (이충무공 추모대제·승전행차), a Black Eagles air show, the Cherry Blossom Music Festival, military base opening events, the Yeojwacheon Starlight Festival (여좌천 별빛축제) and a Jinhaeru (진해루) multimedia fireworks show.
  • Cherry blossom spots (registered descriptions): Naesumyeon Ecological Park (내수면 생태공원), Yeojwacheon (여좌천), Gyeonghwa Station (경화역), Jinhae Tower (진해탑) and Jinhaeru. You can go up to Jinhae Tower on the Jehwangsan (제황산) monorail.
  • Military base opening: The Republic of Korea Naval Academy (해군사관학교) and the Jinhae Naval Base Command (해군진해기지사령부), which are not normally open to visitors, are described as opening during the Gunhangje.

📍 Spring Tulips and April-May Spring Flowers — Sinan Tulip Park, Cheongnamdae Yeongchunje

Sinan Tulip Park (신안튤립공원) [Daegi-ri (대기리), Imja-myeon (임자면), Sinan-gun, ☎ 061-240-4041] is a park next to Daegwang Beach (대광해수욕장) where you can see tulips and spring flowers together with the sea. About 3 million tulips of about 20 varieties are planted, and they are described as blooming in the warm spring. There is a windmill observatory, a glass greenhouse (a succulent exhibition hall and a subtropical plant exhibition hall) and a waterside garden.

  • Opening hours: March-October 09:00-18:00, November-February 09:00-17:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday. Parking: Available. Stroller rental: Not available.

2026 Cheongnamdae Spring Flower Festival Yeongchunje (청남대 봄꽃축제 영춘제) [Cheongnamdae-gil 646 (청남대길 646), Munui-myeon (문의면), Sangdang-gu (상당구), Cheongju-si, ☎ 043-250-5080] is a Chungbuk spring festival held every year since Cheongnamdae (청남대) opened in 2003. In 2026 it ran April 24-May 6, 09:00-18:00, with no closures during the festival.

  • Admission (2026 registered values): Adults 6,000 won, teenagers and soldiers 4,000 won, children and seniors 3,000 won. A 1,000 won discount for groups (20 or more) and Chungcheong region residents. Monorail: adults (ages 14-64) 5,000 won, others 3,000 won. Children under school age with one accompanying person, pregnant women, and people with disabilities up to grade 3 with one accompanying person are registered as free.
  • Registered 2026 programs: A permanent performance daily 13:00-15:00, a wildflower works exhibition, a national park photo exhibition, eco-friendly and herbal medicine experiences, a winery experience and a special exhibition.

📍 May Royal Azaleas — Danyang and Yeongju Sobaeksan Royal Azalea Festivals

2026 Danyang Sobaeksan Royal Azalea Festival (단양 소백산 철쭉제) [Sangsang Street (상상의거리), the Danyang Waterside Stage (단양수변무대) and the Sobaeksan (소백산) area, ☎ 043-423-0701] is held every May to coincide with the royal azaleas at the summit of Sobaeksan. In 2026 it ran May 22-24, 10:00-22:00. It was free and some experiences cost 1,000-20,000 won.

  • Registered 2026 programs: An opening concert, fireworks, a Sobaeksan mountain god rite, a royal azalea and wildflower theme hall, a Danyang dialect contest, busking and a folk market.

2026 Yeongju Sobaeksan Royal Azalea Festival (영주 소백산 철쭉제) [Sobaeksan area, Yeongju-si and Punggi Insam Culture Pop-up Park (풍기인삼문화팝업공원), ☎ Yeongju-si (영주시) 054-630-8703] is a mountain-type festival set on Sobaeksan, with trekking and ecology commentary timed to the royal azalea bloom. In 2026 it ran May 23-24, 10:00-21:00, and it was free.

  • Registered 2026 programs: Sobaeksan trekking and a mountaineering contest, a walk on the Jungnyeong Old Road (죽령옛길), a forest concert and a local agricultural specialty products market.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. All the 2026 spring flower festivals have ended — The 2027 dates are not yet registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.
  2. Cheongnamdae Yeongchunje has an admission fee — It was 6,000 won for adults in 2026.
  3. Sinan Tulip Park is closed on Mondays — It is open March-October 09:00-18:00 and November-February 09:00-17:00.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which flower spots can you visit outside festival periods?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, Gwangyang Plum Blossom Village and Gurye Cornelian Cherry Village are always open and open year-round (both bloom in March). Sinan Tulip Park is open except on Mondays.

Q. When were the 2026 spring flower festivals held?

A. The Gurye Cornelian Cherry Flower Festival ran March 14-22, the Jinhae Gunhangje March 27-April 5, the Icheon Baeksa Cornelian Cherry Flower Festival April 3-5, the Cheongnamdae Yeongchunje April 24-May 6, and the Sobaeksan Royal Azalea Festival May 22-24 in Danyang and May 23-24 in Yeongju.
