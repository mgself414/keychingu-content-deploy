🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to see cherry blossoms in Seoul. They are four cherry blossom festivals held by districts and the Anyangcheon cherry blossom road, which is open all the time even without a festival.

All the 2026 festivals ended in April. As of November 16, 2026, when this guide was written, the 2027 dates were not yet registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data. The festival information below is the 2026 registered values and are for reference only.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Yeouido — Yeongdeungpo Yeouido Spring Flower Festival

Yeongdeungpo Yeouido Spring Flower Festival (영등포 여의도 봄꽃축제) [Yeouiseo-ro Yunjung-ro Cherry Blossom Road (여의서로 윤중로 벚꽃길), ☎ 02-2670-3128] is a festival held every April since 2005 along the Yeouiseo-ro cherry blossom road and the Hangang Park (한강공원) area. About 1,886 cherry trees bloom, along with more than 80,000 spring flowers of 13 kinds, including azaleas (진달래), forsythia (개나리) and royal azaleas (철쭉). In 2026 it ran April 3-7, 11:30-21:30, and it was free.

  • Registered 2026 programs: An opening parade (a walk along the flower road), Bomkkot Stage (봄꽃스테이지) performances, DJ sets and film screenings, street art performances, an Art Cube (아트큐브) exhibition, a gourmet garden and spring flower road cafes, a pop-up garden decorated by citizen gardeners, and a festival tour for visually impaired visitors.

📍 Songpa — Seokchon Lake Cherry Blossom Festival

Songpa-gu Lake Cherry Blossom Festival (송파구 호수벚꽃축제) [Seokchon Lake (석촌호수), Songpanaru-gil 206 (송파나루길 206), Songpa-gu, ☎ Songpa-gu Office (송파구청) 02-2147-2800] is a festival held at Seokchon Lake every spring. When about 1,000 flowering cherry trees (왕벚나무) bloom along the lake, they form a cherry blossom tunnel. You can see the blossoms by day and the blossoms lit by landscape lighting in the evening. In 2026 it ran April 3-11, 10:00-21:00.

  • Registered 2026 programs: A cherry blossom welcome opening, a full bloom concert, busking performances, a flea market, food trucks, photo zones and landscape lighting.
  • Fee: Performances were free; the flea market, food trucks and experience booths were paid.

📍 Yangjaecheon — Yangjaecheon Cherry Blossom Lantern Festival

The 8th Yangjaecheon Cherry Blossom Lantern Festival (제8회 양재천 벚꽃 등 축제) [Yangjaecheon Cherry Blossom Road, Yeongdong 1-gyo to Yeongdong 2-gyo section (영동1교~영동2교), ☎ Seocho Cultural Center (서초문화원) 02-2155-8607] was a spring culture and arts event hosted by the Seocho Cultural Center, registered as showing a night landscape that combined the natural scenery with lighting to match the cherry blossom season. In 2026 it ran March 30-April 19, 13:00-20:00, and it was free.

  • Registered 2026 programs: An opening ceremony on April 3 at 18:30 (Yangjaecheon waterside stage), stage performances April 3-5 (a pungnyu concert, a children's musical, an opera gala concert, a cherry blossom picnic concert and an outdoor cinema), a Cherry Blossom & AI Smart Experience Zone and a food zone.

📍 Dongdaemun — Jangan Cherry Blossom Road Trot Festival X Spring Flower Festival

2026 Trot Festival X Spring Flower Festival (트로트축제X봄꽃축제) [Jangan 1 Waterside Park (장안1 수변공원), ☎ Dongdaemun-gu Office (동대문구) 02-2127-4711] is a festival that combines the Dongdaemun-gu spring flower festival with trot performances. In 2026 it ran April 4-5, 11:00-21:00, and it was free. Before and after the festival, photo zones and night landscape lighting were registered as being set up on Jangan Cherry Blossom Road (장안벚꽃길) between Gunja-gyo (군자교) and Gyeomjae-gyo (겸재교).

  • Registered 2026 programs: A celebratory performance, a district art troupe performance, a local artists' contest, activities for children and teenagers, promotion and experience booths, and food booths run by residents.

📍 Even Without a Festival — Anyangcheon Embankment Cherry Blossom Road

Anyangcheon Embankment Cherry Blossom Road (안양천제방벚꽃길) [Yangpyeong-dong 1-ga (양평동1가), Yeongdeungpo-gu, ☎ Yeongdeungpo-gu Office (영등포구청) Garden City Division (정원도시과) 02-2670-3772] is a cherry blossom road that runs along the Anyangcheon (안양천) embankment from Yeongdeungpo-gu to Gwangmyeong-si (광명시). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the longest cherry blossom road in Seoul. Around the stream there are flower beds, an ecological park, sports facilities, rest areas, four-season theme gardens (a rose garden, a grass garden and others) and photo zones, and many people ride bicycles.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. All the 2026 cherry blossom festivals have ended — The 2027 dates are not yet registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.
  2. The 2026 festivals ran between March 30 and April 19 — Yeouido and Seokchon Lake started on April 3.
  3. The flea market, food trucks and experience booths at the Seokchon Lake festival were paid — The performances were free.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. When are the Seoul cherry blossom festivals held?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered values, in 2026 the Yeouido Spring Flower Festival ran April 3-7, the Songpa-gu Lake Cherry Blossom Festival April 3-11, the Yangjaecheon Cherry Blossom Lantern Festival March 30-April 19, and the Trot Festival X Spring Flower Festival April 4-5. The 2027 dates are not yet registered.

Q. Can you walk a cherry blossom road outside festival periods?

A. The Anyangcheon Embankment Cherry Blossom Road is always open and open year-round. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the longest cherry blossom road in Seoul.
