1. Yangjae Stream (15.6km, origin of Gwanaksan Mountain, #43 and new axis)

  • Distinction: 15.6km·Gwanaksan Mountain → Tancheon confluence·Passing through Gangnam·Seocho·Ecological maintenance in the 2000s·Distinction from #43: Stream itself + bicycle + cherry blossoms
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·#43 cross

2. Yeongdong Bridge 5 Cherry Blossom Tunnel (Gangnam #1 Cherry Blossoms)

  • Distinction: 3km cherry blossom tunnel in Gangnam·2018+ Official Festival·No. 1 among night lighting enthusiasts·Low congestion (Local No. 1)
  • Time: Every year from late March to early April, about 2 weeks
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ Mania No. 1·SNS No. 1

3. Yangjaecheon Biking/Running (Gwanaksan → Tancheon)

  • Differentiation: Yangjaecheon full set bike course·Gwanaksan → Tancheon confluence·Approximately 15km full set·Ttareungi OK
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP MZ Mania·#550 Bicycle cross

4. Yangjae Citizen’s Forest (Ecological Park)

  • Discrimination: Adjacent to Yangjae Stream/Citizen's Forest Station (Shinbundang Line)·Bicycle + Walk + Date #1·Quiet atmosphere
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·Family·Lovers

5. Yangjaecheon Cherry Blossom Festival (early April)

  • Discrimination: Around April 3-5 every year·Gangnam-gu + Seocho-gu official·Night lighting + food truck + performance·Free
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ Mania No. 1

6. Tancheon (35.6km·Gangnam ~ Yongin)

  • Distinction: 35.6km Gangnam + Bundang + Yongin·Confluence of the Han River (Jamsil)·Seoul + Gyeonggi cluster
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Mania·day-trip

7. Tancheon Spring Cherry Blossoms + Fall Foliage

  • Distinction: Spring cherry blossoms (March-April)·Autumn foliage (October-November)·Season cluster 1st place·Free + bicycle
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ Mania·SNS

8. Tancheon 35.6km bicycle ride (joining the Han River)

  • Differentiation: Full set of Tancheon bicycles (Gangnam ~ Bundang ~ Yongin)·Han River joining (Jamsil)·3-4 hour course
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP MZ Mania·#550 cross

9. Tancheon Water Park (Summer July-August)

  • Discrimination: Free summer water park·Bundang + Gangnam Family Mania #1·July-August only
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP·CN family enthusiasts

10. Yangjaecheon + Tancheon Gangnam 1-day cluster

  • Discrimination: Yangjae Citizen's Forest → Yangjaecheon → Tancheon → Jamsil 1-day full set·No. 1 for foreign enthusiasts in Gangnam·Shinbundang Line + Line 3 + Line 2
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ No. 1 EN·JP·CN mania

11. Yangjaecheon Night Lighting (Yeongdong 5th Bridge, April)

  • Discrimination: April Yeongdong 5th Bridge + Yangjaecheon Night Lights·SNS No. 1·Free + Safe·Cafe + Store Accommodation
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ Mania #1·Night #1

  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ Mania·#43 cross

FAQ 6

Q1. Difference from #43? A. #43 = meal + walk (remains the same)·#697 = river itself + bicycle + cherry blossoms + night lights (new axis).

Q2. Yeongdong 5 Bridge cherry blossom season? A. Approximately 2 weeks from late March to early April every year·3km tunnel·Night lighting + festival around April 3-5·Free.

Q3. Bicycle access to Yangjae Stream? A. Ddareungi Yangjae Citizen's Forest Station (Shinbundang Line)·Dogok Station (Line 3)·15km full set·Free line.

Q4. Tancheon + Han River cluster? A. Tancheon 35.6km → Jamsil Han River confluence·Bicycle 3-4 hours·Family + MZ Mania.

Q5. Yangjaecheon night lighting season? A. Spring cherry blossom season (March-April) Yeongdong 5 Bridge night lighting·No. 1 on SNS·Safe + Free.

Q6. Foreigner’s first visit to #697? A. April Yeongdong 5 Bridge Cherry Blossoms + Night Lights (1st priority)·Yangjae Citizen’s Forest + Bicycle (Family + Date).
