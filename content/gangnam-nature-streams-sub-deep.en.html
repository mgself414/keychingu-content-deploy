🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to walk and rest along Yangjaecheon (양재천) and Tancheon (탄천), the streams that flow through Seoul's Gangnam area. They are Yangjaecheon Neighborhood Park (양재천 근린공원) and Maeheon Citizens' Forest (매헌시민의 숲) in Seocho-gu (서초구), one spring festival, Seoul Trail Course 8 in Songpa-gu (송파구), and a Tancheon wetland ecological park in Seongnam (성남). Apart from the festival, all 4 places are always open and have no closed days.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Yangjaecheon — Yangjaecheon Neighborhood Park and Maeheon Citizens' Forest

Yangjaecheon Neighborhood Park (양재천 근린공원) [Yangjae-dong (양재동), Seocho-gu, ☎ Seocho-gu Office (서초구청) Garden and Leisure Division (정원여가과) 02-2155-7148] is a nature park in the city near Yangjae Citizens' Forest Station (양재시민의숲역). A long walking path runs along Yangjaecheon, and the pedestrian path and the bike path are separate, so it is good for walking or cycling.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available.

Maeheon Citizens' Forest (매헌시민의 숲) [Maeheon-ro 99 (매헌로 99), Seocho-gu, ☎ 02-575-3895] is a park created in November 1986, ahead of the 1986 Asian Games and the 1988 Olympics, to improve the area around the Yangjae Tollgate (양재 톨게이트), then the gateway to Seoul. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is the first park in Korea to bring in the forest concept. 250,000 trees of 70 species are planted, including pine, zelkova, Korean maple, horse chestnut and Korean pine.

  • North area: Yun Bong-gil Memorial Hall (매헌윤봉길의사기념관), a floor fountain, a children's playground.
  • South area: the Memorial Tower for the Baekma Unit (유격 백마부대 충혼탑), the Memorial Tower for Korean Air Flight 858 (대한항공 858편 위령탑), the Memorial Tower for the Victims of the Sampoong Department Store Collapse (삼풍백화점 붕괴 희생자 위령탑), the Memorial Monument for the Victims of the Umyeonsan Landslide (우면산 산사태 희생자 추모비).
  • Zone 3: an area Seocho-gu has laid out as a culture and arts park, with a play yard, an outdoor performance hall, an exhibition hall, a sculpture park and a meeting plaza.
  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

📍 Yangjaecheon Spring Festival — Yangjaecheon Cherry Blossom Lantern Festival

The 8th Yangjaecheon Cherry Blossom Lantern Festival (제8회 양재천 벚꽃 등 축제) [Yangjaecheon cherry blossom path, section from Yeongdong 1 Bridge (영동1교) to Yeongdong 2 Bridge (영동2교), ☎ Seocho Cultural Center (서초문화원) 02-2155-8607] was a spring arts and culture event hosted by the Seocho Cultural Center. Timed to the cherry blossom season, it presented a night landscape that combined natural scenery and lighting. In 2026 it ran from March 30 to April 19, from 13:00 to 20:00, and it was free.

  • Opening ceremony: April 3 (Fri) 18:30, at the Yangjaecheon Waterside Stage (양재천 수변무대) (261-23 Yangjae-dong, Seocho-gu).
  • Stage programs: April 3 the Yangjaecheon Pungnyu Concert (양재천 풍류 콘서트), April 4 a children's musical and an opera gala concert, April 5 a cherry blossom picnic concert and outdoor cinema.
  • Side programs: a Cherry Blossom & AI Smart Experience Zone, a food zone, and Seocho Cultural Footsteps (서초문화발걸음) on April 4~5.

📍 Tancheon — Seoul Trail Course 8 Jangji-Tancheon (Songpa) and Tancheon Freshwater Fish Wetland Ecological Park (Seongnam)

Seoul Trail Course 8 Jangji-Tancheon (서울둘레길 8코스 장지·탄천코스) [Bangi-dong (방이동), Songpa-gu, ☎ 02-779-7902~4] is a course themed 'an urban stream path where the natural ecology has been restored' on the Seoul Trail (서울둘레길), which circles Seoul in 21 courses totaling 156.5km. You can walk while seeing the living things and natural environment that visit the stream. It is close to Olympic Park Station (올림픽공원역) and Suseo Station (수서역).

  • Length and time: 8.6km in total, about 2 hours 35 minutes.
  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.

Tancheon Freshwater Fish Wetland Ecological Park (탄천민물고기습지생태원) [Taepyeong-dong (태평동), Sujeong-gu (수정구), Seongnam-si, ☎ Seongnam City Hall (성남시청) Environmental Policy Division (환경정책과) 031-729-3153] is an ecological park created in 2009 to restore freshwater fish and other creatures endangered by urbanization and pollution of Tancheon. Five habitats have been restored, for freshwater fish, dragonflies (frogs) and aquatic insects among others. It has stepping-stone shallows, a freshwater fish experience area, observation decks and an ecological trail, and depending on the season you can see rapeseed, cosmos, barley and Korean wheat.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Not available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The 2026 Yangjaecheon Cherry Blossom Lantern Festival is over — it ran from March 30 to April 19.
  2. Tancheon Freshwater Fish Wetland Ecological Park has no parking — it is in Sujeong-gu, Seongnam.
  3. Seoul Trail Course 8 takes about 2 hours 35 minutes — it is 8.6km in total and close to Olympic Park Station and Suseo Station.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Can I cycle along Yangjaecheon?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, the walking path of Yangjaecheon Neighborhood Park is divided into a pedestrian path and a bike path, so it is good for cycling. It is always open.

Q. Is there a course for walking along Tancheon?

A. Yes, Seoul Trail Course 8 Jangji-Tancheon (Songpa-gu). It is 8.6km in total, about 2 hours 35 minutes, and always open.
