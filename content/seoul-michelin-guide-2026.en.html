Seoul Michelin Guide 2026 for Foreign Travelers

From 3 Stars to Bib Gourmand — 15 Curated Spots for Foreigners + Reservation & Dress Code Matrix

The Michelin Guide Seoul 2026 features a total of 80+ establishments, including 2 x 3-star, 9 x 2-star, 24 x 1-star, and 50+ Bib Gourmand restaurants. However, only a portion of these are suitable for foreign travelers (considering English menus, international credit cards, dietary options, and reservation availability). This guide curates 15 establishments that are a perfect fit for foreign travelers, special occasions, and foodies, categorized by star rating, region, and budget.

1. 3 Stars (2 establishments) — The Pinnacle of Korean Gastronomy

La Yeon (라연)

  • Address: 249, Dongho-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul (Shilla Hotel 23F)
  • Signature: Authentic Hansik (한식, Korean cuisine) fine dining (Chef Kim Sung-il)
  • Price Range: Lunch ₩220,000 (~$160 / ~1,150 RMB / ~24,000 JPY) - ₩280,000 / Dinner ₩320,000 - ₩450,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (English menu, sommelier, formal dress code, within Shilla Hotel (신라호텔))
  • Reservations: 2-4 weeks in advance (hotel concierge or Catchtable (캐치테이블))
  • Recommended For: US/European special occasions (anniversary, proposal)

Mosu (모수)

  • Address: 12, Hannam-daero 27-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Modern Hansik (한식, Korean cuisine) (Chef Ahn Sung-jae — the first Korean chef to earn 3 Michelin stars abroad)
  • Price Range: Dinner ₩300,000 - ₩390,000 (course only)
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (English fluent staff, dietary options, wine pairing, smart casual dress code)
  • Reservations: 1-2 months in advance (Mosu (모수) official website)
  • Recommended For: European travelers, gourmands, travel highlight

2. 2 Stars (4 out of 9 establishments are foreigner-friendly)

Jungsik (정식당)

  • Address: 4, Dosan-daero 11-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: New Korean fine dining (originating from Jungsik (정식당) NYC)
  • Price Range: Dinner ₩280,000 - ₩360,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (NYC origin, English fluent staff, wine cellar, popular with foreign regulars)
  • Reservations: 2-4 weeks in advance (Catchtable (캐치테이블))

Gotgan (곳간) by Shilla Stay

  • Address: 249, Dongho-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul (Shilla Hotel 23F)
  • Signature: Modern Hansik (한식, Korean cuisine) (Chef Im Jung-sik)
  • Price Range: Dinner ₩250,000 - ₩320,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (Hotel setting, English available, formal dress code)

Kang Min-chul Restaurant (강민철 레스토랑)

  • Address: 23, Dosan-daero 81-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Contemporary Hansik (한식, Korean cuisine) (Chef Kang Min-chul)
  • Price Range: Dinner ₩220,000 - ₩290,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★ (English menu, reservations via Catchtable (캐치테이블))

Lasse (라쎄)

  • Address: 16, Dosan-daero 51-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Scandinavian-Korean fusion (Chef Lee Jun)
  • Price Range: Dinner ₩240,000 - ₩310,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (Perfect for European travelers, English fluent staff, wine pairing)

3. 1 Star (5 out of 24 establishments are foreigner-friendly)

Kwonsooksoo (권숙수)

  • Address: 9, Dosan-daero 51-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Hansik (한식, Korean cuisine) featuring fermented ingredients and Jang (장, Korean sauces)
  • Price Range: Dinner ₩180,000 - ₩240,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★ (Deep dive into Hansik (한식), picture menu option)

Jin Jin (진진)

  • Address: 10, Wausan-ro 29na-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Chinese fine dining (Chef Wang Yuk-seong)
  • Price Range: Dinner ₩150,000 - ₩210,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (Hongdae (홍대) area, Chinese cuisine, popular with foreign regulars)

Onjium (온지음)

  • Address: 49, Hyojaro, Jongno-gu, Seoul (Hyojadong (효자동))
  • Signature: Reconstructed Gungjung Hansik (궁중 한식, Korean royal court cuisine)
  • Price Range: Dinner ₩280,000 - ₩360,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (Perfect for European travelers, royal court cuisine, English guidance)

Joo Ok (주옥)

  • Address: 23, Gangnam-daero 162-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Contemporary Hansik (한식, Korean cuisine) (Chef Shin Chang-ho)
  • Price Range: Dinner ₩210,000 - ₩280,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (Gangnam (강남) area, English fluent staff)

Kojima (코지마)

  • Address: 14, Apgujeong-ro 60-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Japanese Omakase (오마카세, chef's choice meal)
  • Price Range: Dinner ₩300,000 - ₩450,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (Japanese cuisine, English available, special occasion)

4. Bib Gourmand (4 out of 50+ establishments are foreigner-friendly) — Reasonable Prices

Uraeok (우래옥)

  • Address: 62-29, Changgyeonggung-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Pyeongyang Naengmyeon (평양냉면, cold buckwheat noodles) since 1946 (Korea's oldest Pyeongyang Naengmyeon (평양냉면) restaurant)
  • Price Range: ₩15,000 (~$11 / ~80 RMB / ~1,600 JPY) - ₩30,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (Number one Pyeongyang Naengmyeon (평양냉면) for foreigners, picture menu)

Bakgane Bindaetteok (박가네 빈대떡) at Gwangjang Market (광장시장)

  • Address: 88, Changgyeonggung-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Bindaetteok (빈대떡, mung bean pancake), Jeon (전, Korean savory pancakes), and market food
  • Price Range: ₩8,000 - ₩20,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (Korean market experience, CNN recommended for foreigners)

Jinju Jip (진주집)

  • Address: 24-12, Dadong-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Kongguksu (콩국수, cold soybean noodle soup) (summer signature)
  • Price Range: ₩12,000 - ₩18,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★ (Local favorite, summer-limited)

Kangmin Seoul (강민서울)

  • Address: Cheongdam-dong, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Casual Hansik Jeongsik (한정식, Korean set meal)
  • Price Range: ₩40,000 - ₩80,000
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★ (Introduction to Hansik Jeongsik (한정식))

5. Foreigner Fit Tips

Reservation Methods

  • Catchtable (캐치테이블) (Korean Catchtable (캐치테이블) app, English available): Jungsik (정식당), Kang Min-chul (강민철), Lasse (라쎄), Joo Ok (주옥)
  • Official Website: Mosu (모수), La Yeon (라연), Onjium (온지음) (Mosu (모수) requires 1-2 months in advance)
  • Hotel Concierge: La Yeon (라연), Gotgan (곳간) (within Shilla Hotel (신라호텔))
  • International Reservation Platforms: Some support OpenTable and Resy

English Availability

  • Top Tier: La Yeon (라연), Mosu (모수), Jungsik (정식당), Lasse (라쎄), Kojima (코지마), Jin Jin (진진) (English fluent staff, sommelier)
  • Mid-to-High Tier: Kang Min-chul (강민철), Gotgan (곳간), Joo Ok (주옥), Onjium (온지음) (English menu, basic English communication)
  • Local Tier: Kwonsooksoo (권숙수), Uraeok (우래옥), Gwangjang Market (광장시장), Jinju Jip (진주집) (Picture menu, translation apps recommended)

Dress Code

  • Formal (jacket required): La Yeon (라연), Gotgan (곳간), Jungsik (정식당) (dinner)
  • Smart casual: Mosu (모수), Lasse (라쎄), Kang Min-chul (강민철), Joo Ok (주옥), Kojima (코지마), Onjium (온지음)
  • Casual: Jin Jin (진진), Kwonsooksoo (권숙수), Uraeok (우래옥), Gwangjang Market (광장시장), Jinju Jip (진주집)

Dietary Options

  • Vegetarian: Mosu (모수), Lasse (라쎄), Onjium (온지음) (upon prior request)
  • Halal: La Yeon (라연) (upon prior request via hotel concierge), some Muslim-friendly establishments in Hannam (한남)
  • Gluten-free: Mosu (모수), Lasse (라쎄), Jungsik (정식당) (upon prior request)
  • Allergies: All fine dining restaurants can accommodate prior requests

Budget (per person, per meal)

  • 3-star: ₩220,000 - ₩450,000 (lunch is cheaper)
  • 2-star: ₩220,000 - ₩360,000
  • 1-star: ₩150,000 - ₩360,000
  • Bib Gourmand: ₩8,000 - ₩80,000

7. Recommendations by Persona

US Persona (special occasion·anniversary)

  • Top Pick: La Yeon (라연) (3-star, Shilla Hotel (신라호텔), formal dress, perfect English)
  • Second Pick: Jungsik (정식당) (NYC origin + Seoul)
  • Lunch Option: La Yeon (라연) lunch course (₩220,000~) — 30% cheaper than dinner

European Persona (foodie·deep culinary experience)

  • Top Pick: Mosu (모수) (3-star, Chef Ahn Sung-jae, pinnacle of modern Hansik (한식))
  • Second Pick: Lasse (라쎄) (Scandinavian-Korean fusion)
  • Third Pick: Onjium (온지음) (Gungjung Hansik (궁중 한식), perfect for European travelers ★)

Families/Groups of 4-6

  • Top Pick: Kwonsooksoo (권숙수) (1-star, fermentation-focused, suitable for course meals)
  • Second Pick: Jin Jin (진진) (Chinese fine dining, great table ambiance)
  • Third Pick: Uraeok (우래옥) (Bib Gourmand, Pyeongyang Naengmyeon (평양냉면), family dining)

Japanese Cuisine Lover

  • Top Pick: Kojima (코지마) (Omakase (오마카세), Apgujeong (압구정))

Reasonable Price·Cultural Experience

  • Top Pick: Bakgane Bindaetteok (박가네 빈대떡) at Gwangjang Market (광장시장) (Bib Gourmand, CNN recommended)
  • Second Pick: Uraeok (우래옥) (Pyeongyang Naengmyeon (평양냉면) since 1946)

8. Seasonal Recommendations

  • Spring (April-May): La Yeon (라연)·Mosu (모수)·Jungsik (정식당) (terrace, view)
  • Summer (June-August): Jinju Jip (진주집) (Kongguksu (콩국수))·Uraeok (우래옥) (Pyeongyang Naengmyeon (평양냉면))·La Yeon (라연) (cool hotel environment)
  • Autumn (October-November): Kwonsooksoo (권숙수)·Onjium (온지음) (traditional, fermentation season)
  • Winter (December-February): Mosu (모수)·Lasse (라쎄)·Kang Min-chul (강민철) (indoor, suitable for course meals)

9. Michelin Reservation Checklist

  • [ ] Reserve 1-4 weeks in advance via Catchtable (캐치테이블) / official website (3-star restaurants 2 months in advance)
  • [ ] Check dress code (formal vs smart casual)
  • [ ] Request dietary options in advance (vegetarian, halal, allergy)
  • [ ] Payment card (many international cards OK, some only accept Korean cards)
  • [ ] Recommended to arrive 15 minutes early (for formal establishments)
  • [ ] Check if photography is allowed (some establishments have restrictions)
