Seoul Michelin Guide 2026 for Foreign Travelers

From 3-Star Mingles (밍글스) to Bib Gourmand meals under ₩10,000 — 16 picks from the 2026 guide. Ratings, addresses, closing days, booking channels and price bands were checked against the official Michelin Guide page and each restaurant's official website (2026-10-07). Fully revised on 2026-10-07.

The Michelin Guide Seoul & Busan 2026 was announced on March 5, 2026. In Seoul, 178 restaurants made the list: 1 x 3 Stars (Mingles (밍글스)), 10 x 2 Stars, 31 x 1 Star and 51 x Bib Gourmand. Mingles (밍글스) kept its 3 Stars for a second straight year, Mosu (모수) won 2 Stars right after its return, and Sosuheon (소수헌) rose from 1 Star to 2 Stars.

From those 178, this guide picks 16 that a first-time visitor to Seoul can actually book and reach. There are three criteria: is there an open booking channel through the official website or a hotel, is there English guidance or a hotel setting where you can communicate, and is it on a typical travel route such as Gwanghwamun (광화문), Bukchon (북촌), Gangnam (강남) or Jamsil (잠실).

⚠ 5 Things First-Time Bookers Miss

1. Most fine dining closes on Mondays. Mingles (밍글스), Mosu (모수), Kwonsooksoo (권숙수), Kang Minchul (강민철) and L'Amant Secret (라망 시크레) are closed Mondays, and Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) is closed Monday and Tuesday. For a Monday-night celebration, go to La Yeon (라연) inside the hotel (lunch served daily) or to a Bib Gourmand.

2. Some places open bookings on a fixed day. Bicena (비채나) opens next month's reservations at 10:00 AM on the 1st of each month. Other restaurants often have similar rules, so read the booking notice on the official website first.

3. Hotel restaurants have a dress code and an age limit. Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) and L'Amant Secret (라망 시크레) require business casual, restrict men's tracksuits, slippers, sandals and shorts, and do not admit children under 8.

4. Some places do not offer allergy substitutions at all. Bicena (비채나) states that it "cannot provide substitutions for allergies or disliked ingredients." Every course contains meat, seafood, nuts, raw food and gluten. If you are vegetarian or have allergies, ask each restaurant before booking, and pick somewhere else if they cannot accommodate you.

5. Some Bib Gourmand places take cash only. The Michelin page lists Gaeseong Mandu Koong (개성만두 궁) as "cash only." Onjium (온지음) also carries a "cash only" note on its Michelin page, so check before you go.

① 3 Stars (1 restaurant)

Mingles (밍글스)

  • Address: 2F, 19, Dosan-daero 67-gil, Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul
  • Cuisine: Korean. Michelin highlights its own signature elements such as the "Mingling Pot" and the "Sauce Trio." Chef Kang Mingoo
  • Rating: 3 Stars for a second straight year in 2026. The only 3-Star restaurant in Seoul
  • Hours: Lunch Tue–Sat 12:00–15:00, closed Mon and Sun (per the Michelin page. Check the official website for dinner)
  • Price band: ₩₩₩₩
  • Booking: Directly with the restaurant. Official website restaurant-mingles.com · phone +82-2-515-7306
  • Notes: Valet parking, wine list, cards accepted. Whether English guidance is available is not confirmed

② 2 Stars (5 of 10)

La Yeon (라연)

  • Address: 23F, Shilla Hotel (신라호텔), 249, Dongho-ro, Jung-gu (중구), Seoul
  • Cuisine: Korean. A modern take on Korean tradition that puts clean flavors ahead of excessive technique
  • Hours: Lunch daily 12:00–14:30 (per the Michelin page)
  • Price band: ₩₩₩₩
  • Booking: Shilla Hotel (신라호텔) official website, Naver Reservation (네이버 예약), phone +82-2-2230-3367. Since it is inside the hotel, English service can be arranged through the hotel concierge
  • Notes: Wheelchair accessible, wine and sake list, valet parking. The most popular choice for anniversaries

Mosu (모수)

  • Address: 4, Hoenamu-ro 41-gil, Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seoul
  • Cuisine: Modern. Chef Ahn Sung-jae. Signatures are the abalone taco, sesame tofu and burdock tart
  • Rating: New 2 Stars in 2026 after its return
  • Hours: Tue 18:00–22:00, Wed–Sat 12:00–15:00, closed Mon and Sun (per the Michelin page. Reconfirm before booking)
  • Price band: ₩₩₩₩
  • Booking: Directly with the restaurant. Official website mosuseoul.com · phone +82-2-6272-5678
  • Notes: Valet parking, wine list. Whether English guidance is available is not confirmed

Jungsik (정식당)

  • Address: 11, Seolleung-ro 158-gil, Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul
  • Cuisine: Contemporary. Chef Yim Jungsik. Regarded as a pioneer who brought Korean cooking to the world stage
  • Price band: ₩₩₩₩
  • Booking: From the official website jungsik.kr, which links to CatchTable (캐치테이블), or by email reservation@jungsik.kr · phone +82-2-517-4654. With Korean and English sites, it is the easiest 2-Star to book from overseas
  • Notes: Suitable for solo diners and groups, wheelchair accessible, valet parking, wine list

Kwonsooksoo (권숙수)

  • Address: 4F, 37, Apgujeong-ro 80-gil, Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul
  • Cuisine: Korean. Chef Kwon Woojung. A course where modern technique meets Korean tradition
  • Hours: Lunch Tue–Sat 12:00–15:00, closed Mon and Sun (per the Michelin page)
  • Price band: ₩₩₩₩
  • Booking: Direct booking with the restaurant only (it does not use third-party booking platforms). Official website kwonsooksoo.com · phone +82-2-542-6268
  • Notes: Valet parking, wine list

Soigné (스와니예)

  • Address: Suite 201, 2F, Sinsa Square, 652, Gangnam-daero, Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul
  • Cuisine: Contemporary. Chef Lee Jun. A tasting course that weaves seasons and ingredients into a narrative of "episodes"
  • Hours: Lunch Mon–Sat 12:00–15:00, closed Sun (per the Michelin page)
  • Price band: ₩₩₩₩
  • Booking: Directly with the restaurant. Official website soigneseoul.com · phone +82-2-3477-9386
  • Notes: Wheelchair accessible, valet parking, wine list

③ 1 Star (5 of 31)

Kang Minchul Restaurant (강민철 레스토랑)

  • Address: 5F, 18, Dosan-daero 63-gil, Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul (a new space after relocating in 2025)
  • Cuisine: Contemporary French. French technique with Korean and Asian inspiration. Chef Kang Minchul
  • Hours: Tue–Fri 12:00–14:30 · 18:00–21:30, Sat 12:00–14:30 · 18:30–22:00, closed Mon and Sun
  • Price band: ₩₩₩₩
  • Booking: Directly with the restaurant. Official website kangminchul.com · phone +82-2-545-2511
  • Notes: Michelin marks it as "comfortable for dining alone." A good fit for solo-traveling foodies

Onjium (온지음)

  • Address: 4F, 49, Hyoja-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul (west of Gyeongbokgung (경복궁))
  • Cuisine: Joseon royal court cooking reinterpreted with traditional and modern techniques. Chefs Cho Eun-hee and Park Seong-bae
  • Hours: Lunch Tue–Fri 12:00–15:00 (per the Michelin page. Reconfirm before booking)
  • Price band: ₩₩₩₩
  • Booking: Phone +82-2-6952-0024. The official website onjium.org switches between Korean and English, but it focuses on introducing the institute, so confirm booking information by phone
  • Notes: The Michelin page carries a "cash only" note. Easy to combine with a route through Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) and Seochon (서촌)

Bicena (비채나)

  • Address: 81F, Lotte World Tower (롯데월드타워), 300, Olympic-ro, Songpa-gu (송파구), Seoul
  • Cuisine: Korean. A refined course whose distinction comes from basics such as jang (fermented sauces) and kimchi. It looks out over the whole of Seoul
  • Hours: Lunch daily 11:30–14:30 (per the Michelin page)
  • Price: Lunch "Sancheon" course ₩170,000 (~$120) · dinner "Guhak" course ₩280,000 · Family Special lunch ₩240,000 / dinner ₩310,000 (official website, 2026-10-07)
  • Booking: From the official website bicena.com, which links to CatchTable (캐치테이블), or by phone +82-2-3213-1261. Next month's reservations open at 10:00 AM on the 1st of each month
  • Dietary: No substitutions for allergies or disliked ingredients. Every course contains meat, seafood, nuts, raw food and gluten
  • Notes: Korean and English site. Wheelchair accessible. At ₩₩₩, it is relatively affordable among the starred restaurants and has a view, making it a good first fine-dining pick

Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든)

  • Address: 36F, Josun Palace (조선 팰리스), 231, Teheran-ro, Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul
  • Cuisine: Modern Korean. In a space like a garden in the city, seasonal ingredients are presented with illustrated cards and explanations
  • Hours: Wed–Sun lunch 12:00–14:30 · dinner 18:00–22:00, closed Mon and Tue (official website)
  • Price band: ₩₩₩₩ (course prices not published on the official page)
  • Booking: CatchTable (캐치테이블) only since December 1, 2025. The phone line +82-2-727-7610 is said to be delayed by the volume of inquiries
  • Dress code and age: Business casual required (men's tracksuits, slippers, sandals and shorts restricted), children under 8 not admitted
  • Notes: Hotel website in Korean and English. Wheelchair accessible, counter seats, view

L'Amant Secret (라망 시크레)

  • Address: 26F, L'Escape Hotel (레스케이프 호텔), 67, Toegye-ro, Jung-gu (중구), Seoul (Namdaemun (남대문) · Hoehyeon (회현))
  • Cuisine: Contemporary. A modern interpretation of Western cooking using Korean seasonal ingredients. Chef Son Jongwon
  • Hours: Tue–Sat lunch 12:00–15:00 · dinner 18:00–22:00, closed Sun and Mon (official website)
  • Price band: ₩₩₩₩ (course prices not published on the official page)
  • Booking: Via the Josun Hotel (조선호텔) dining reservations page, or by phone +82-2-317-4003
  • Dress code and age: Business casual required (men's tracksuits, slippers, sandals and shorts restricted), children under 8 not admitted
  • Notes: Hotel website in Korean and English. An easy walk from lodgings in Myeongdong (명동) and Namdaemun (남대문)

④ Bib Gourmand (5 of 51) — A Taste of Michelin for Under ₩10,000

Bib Gourmand is a rating that means "good food at a reasonable price." The 2026 Seoul list of 51 includes many names familiar to travelers, such as Pildong Myeonok (필동면옥), Samcheongdong Sujebi (삼청동 수제비), Geumdwaeji Sikdang (금돼지식당), Okdongsik (옥동식) and Samdae Samgyejangin (3대 삼계장인). Here we picked four places along the Gwanghwamun (광화문) · Bukchon (북촌) · Insadong (인사동) route and one in the Hongdae (홍대) area.

Woo Lae Oak (우래옥)

  • Address: 62-29, Changgyeonggung-ro, Jung-gu (중구), Seoul (Euljiro 4-ga (을지로4가))
  • Cuisine: Pyongyang naengmyeon. A Korean beef brisket broth seasoned with only salt and soy sauce
  • Hours: Tue–Sun 11:30–21:00, closed Mon (per the Michelin page)
  • Price band: ₩
  • Booking: No online reservations. Phone +82-2-2265-0151. The line is long at mealtimes
  • Notes: Family-friendly, valet parking, cards accepted. The broth is beef, so it is hard for vegetarians

Jin Jin (진진)

  • Address: 60, World Cup buk-ro 1-gil, Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul (Hongdae (홍대) · Seogyo (서교))
  • Cuisine: Chinese. Chef Wang Yuk-sung. A Chinese restaurant that cut its menu to 10 dishes and lowered its prices
  • Hours: Mon–Sat 11:30–15:00, Sun 12:00–15:00 (per the Michelin page. Confirm dinner by phone)
  • Price band: ₩₩
  • Booking: Phone +82-70-5035-8878
  • Notes: Cards accepted. Shared Chinese dishes suit a group of 4 to 6

Gaeseong Mandu Koong (개성만두 궁)

  • Address: 11-3, Insadong 10-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul (Insadong (인사동))
  • Cuisine: Dumplings. Wrappers from dough fermented for 3 hours, filled with pork and vegetables, in a clear broth simmered 8 hours from beef and 10 kinds of vegetables
  • Hours: Mon–Fri 11:30–15:00, Sat 11:30–21:00, Sun 11:30–20:00
  • Price band: ₩
  • Booking: Phone +82-2-733-9240 · official website koong.co.kr
  • Notes: Cash only (as stated on the Michelin page). In the middle of Insadong (인사동), it is a good stop while shopping. The filling contains pork, so it is not suitable for halal or vegetarian diets

Hwangsaengga Kalguksu (황생가 칼국수)

  • Address: 78, Bukchon-ro 5-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul (Bukchon (북촌))
  • Cuisine: Kalguksu and dumplings. It started as Bukchon Kalguksu in 2001 and took its current name in 2014. Noodles in beef-bone broth, handmade dumplings and boiled pork in the old style
  • Hours: Daily 11:00–21:30
  • Price band: ₩
  • Booking: Phone +82-2-739-6334
  • Notes: Cards accepted. The most popular lunch spot after a walk through Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌 한옥마을)

Mijin (미진)

  • Address: Unit 116-2, Le Meilleur Jongno Town (르메이에르 종로타운), 19, Jong-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul (Gwanghwamun (광화문) · Jonggak (종각))
  • Cuisine: Korean-style cold buckwheat noodles. A place that has held its ground around Gwanghwamun (광화문) since 1952. A soy-based broth richer than Japanese soba, with noodles made in the building's basement factory
  • Hours: Daily 10:30–21:00
  • Price band: ₩
  • Booking: Phone +82-2-730-6198
  • Notes: Cards accepted. Walkable from Gwanghwamun Square (광화문 광장) and Gyeongbokgung (경복궁)

⑤ Michelin Selected (1 restaurant) — The Longest Line in Anguk Right Now

Muguok (무구옥) — New Selected in 2026

  • Address: 7, Yulgok-ro 1-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul (a hanok in the alley between Anguk Station (안국역) and Gyeongbokgung (경복궁))
  • Cuisine: North Korean-style chicken dishes. The signature is the North Korean-style samgyebaekban for ₩18,000, a set meal of rice with chicken broth made from domestic chicken simmered in an Anseong cauldron (price from Diningcode, 2026-10-07)
  • Hours: The Michelin page lists only lunch 11:30–14:00. Per Diningcode, dinner is 17:30–21:00 (break 15:00–17:30)
  • Price band: ₩
  • Booking: Not possible. Walk-in waitlist registration only. The Michelin page says "waitlist registration starts 1 hour before opening"
  • Waiting tip: The most efficient approach is to register on the waitlist in front of the shop before it opens, then stroll around Bukchon (북촌) and Anguk (안국) and come back. The Korea Tourism Organization's Japanese-language account (@kto.kantabi, 2026-09-30) announced that from October, "waitlist registration starts at 10:00 and doors open at 11:00." Check the shop's Instagram @muguok_seoul for the day's hours
  • Notes: There is an English notice. It specializes in chicken, so there are no vegetarian options

⑥ Booking in Practice — How to Book from Overseas

  • Start with places whose official sites are in Korean and English: Jungsik (정식당) (CatchTable or email), Bicena (비채나) (CatchTable or phone), Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) (CatchTable only) and L'Amant Secret (라망 시크레) (Josun Hotel dining reservations) show their booking route right on the English page.
  • Places with an email or phone channel are safer for overseas booking: CatchTable (캐치테이블) is a Korean app. We could not confirm this time whether you can sign up with an overseas phone number. Jungsik (정식당) has email (reservation@jungsik.kr), La Yeon (라연) goes through the hotel concierge, and for the rest, phone is the reliable route.
  • Direct-booking-only places: Kwonsooksoo (권숙수) does not use third-party platforms. Book only through its official website or by phone.
  • Booking opening day: Bicena (비채나) opens the next month at 10:00 on the 1st of each month. Popular courses fill up that day, so set an alarm.
  • For Bib Gourmand, time matters more than booking: Woo Lae Oak (우래옥), Hwangsaengga (황생가), Mijin (미진) and Muguok (무구옥) have lines. The wait is shorter just after opening (11:00–11:30) or around 15:00.

⑧ Recommendations by Situation

  • Anniversary or proposal: La Yeon (라연) (hotel 23F, book via the concierge) → Bicena (비채나) (81F view, prices published) → L'Amant Secret (라망 시크레) (26F, business casual)
  • Food is the whole point: Mingles (밍글스) (3 Stars) → Mosu (모수) and Jungsik (정식당) (2 Stars) → Soigné (스와니예) ("episodes" course)
  • Your first Korean course meal: Bicena (비채나) (₩₩₩, English site, prices published) or Onjium (온지음) (royal court cuisine, next to Gyeongbokgung (경복궁))
  • Solo-traveling foodie: Kang Minchul Restaurant (강민철 레스토랑) (Michelin's "comfortable alone" note), Muguok (무구옥) (a set meal for one)
  • Family or groups of 4 to 6: Jin Jin (진진) (shared Chinese dishes), Woo Lae Oak (우래옥) (family-friendly note), Bicena (비채나) Family Special
  • Under ₩20,000 budget: Woo Lae Oak (우래옥) · Hwangsaengga Kalguksu (황생가 칼국수) · Gaeseong Mandu Koong (개성만두 궁) (cash) · Mijin (미진) · Muguok (무구옥)
