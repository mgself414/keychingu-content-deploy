Foreigner Seoul Michelin Guide 2026

> From 3 star to Bib Gourmand — Curation of 15 places suitable for foreigners + Reservation/Dress code matrix

The Michelin Guide Seoul 2026 includes 2 3-star restaurants, 9 2-star restaurants, 24 1-star restaurants, and 50+ Bib Gourmand places, for a total of 80+. However, there are only a few stores that are suitable for foreigner personas (English menu, overseas cards, dietary options, reservation possibilities). This guide curates 15 places that fit foreign segment, special occasion, and foodie personas on three axes: star rating, region, and budget.

1. 3 star (2 locations) — The pinnacle of Korean gastronomy

La Yeon

  • Address: 249 Dongho-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul (23rd floor, Shilla Hotel)
  • Signature: Authentic Korean fine dining (Chef Kim Seong-il)
  • Price range: Lunch ₩220,000~280,000 / Dinner ₩320,000~450,000
  • Foreigners fit: ★★★ (English menu, sommelier, dress code formal, Shilla Hotel internal)
  • Reservation: 2-4 weeks in advance (hotel concierge or catchtable)
  • Recommended: USA/Europe special occasion (anniversary·proposal)

Parameter (Mosu)

  • Address: 12 Hannam-daero 27-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Modern Korean food (Chef Seongjae Ahn — Korea’s first overseas 3 star)
  • Price range: Dinner ₩300,000~390,000 (course only)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ (English fluent·dietary option·wine pairing·dress code smart casual)
  • Reservation: 1-2 months in advance (parameter official website)
  • Recommended: European persona, gourmet, travel highlight

2. 2 star (4 out of 9 places suitable for foreigners)

Jeongsikdang (Jungsik)

  • Address: 4, Dosan-daero 11-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: New Korean fine dining (NYC Jeongsikdang’s original home)
  • Price range: Dinner ₩280,000~360,000
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ (NYC hometown, English fluent, wine cellar, foreigner regular)
  • Reservation: 2-4 weeks in advance (catchtable)

Storage by Shilla Stay

  • Address: 249 Dongho-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul (23rd floor, Shilla Hotel)
  • Signature: Modern Korean food (Chef Lim Jeong-sik)
  • Price range: Dinner ₩250,000~320,000
  • Foreigners fit: ★★★ (hotel, English, dress code formal)

Kang Min-cheol Restaurant

  • Address: 23 Dosan-daero 81-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Contemporary Korean food (Chef Kang Min-cheol)
  • Price range: Dinner ₩220,000~290,000
  • Foreigners fit: ★★ (English menu/reservation catchtable)

Lasse

  • Address: 16 Dosan-daero 51-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Scandinavian-Korean fusion (Chef Lee Jun)
  • Price range: Dinner ₩240,000~310,000
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ (European persona fit·English fluent·wine pairing)

Onjium

4. Bib Gourmand (4 out of 50+ places that fit foreigners) — Reasonable price

Woo Rae-ok

  • Address: 62-29 Changgyeonggung-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Pyeongyang Naengmyeon 1946~ (Korea’s oldest Pyeongyang Naengmyeon)
  • Price range: ₩15,000~30,000
  • Foreigners fit: ★★★ (No. 1 choice for foreigners: Pyongyang Naengmyeon·picture menu)

Gwangjang Market Parkgane Bindaetteok

  • Address: 88 Changgyeonggung-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Bindaetteok, exhibition, market food
  • Price range: ₩8,000~20,000
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ (Korean market experience·CNN foreigner recommendation)

Jinju House

  • Address: 24-12 Dadong-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Kongguksu (summer signature)
  • Price range: ₩12,000~18,000
  • Foreigners fit: ★★ (locals, summer only)

Kangmin Seoul

  • Address: Cheongdam-dong, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Hanjeongsik Casual
  • Price range: ₩40,000~80,000
  • Foreigner fit: ★★ (Introduction to Korean cuisine)

5. Foreign fit tips

How to make a reservation

  • catchtable (Korean catchtable app·English OK): Jeong Jeongdang, Kang Min-cheol, Lasse, Juok
  • Official site: Mosu·Rayeon·Onzium (Mosu official site 1-2 months ago)
  • Hotel Concierge: Layeon·Gotgan (Silla Hotel internal)
  • International Reservation Platform: Partial support for OpenTable·Resy

English language availability

  • Top: Layeon, Mosu, Jeongsik Restaurant, Lasse, Kojima, Jinjin (English fluent, sommelier)
  • Slander: Kang Min-cheol, Gotgan, Ju-ok, Onjium (English menu, basic English service)
  • Local: Kwon Sook-su, Woo Rae-ok, Gwangjang Market, Jinju House (picture menu, translation app)

Dress code

  • Formal (jacket required): La Yeon, Gotgan, Formal Restaurant (Dinner)
  • Smart casual: Mosu, Lasse, Kang Min-cheol, Ju-ok, Kojima, Onzium
  • Casual: Jinjin, Kwon Sooksu, Woo Rae-ok, Gwangjang Market, Jinju House

Dietary options

  • Vegetarian: Mosu, Lasse, Onzium (on prior request)
  • Halal: La Yeon (request hotel concierge in advance), some Hannam muslim friendly stores
  • Gluten free: Mosu·Lasse·Restaurant (advance request)
  • Allergies: All fine dining can be requested in advance

Budget (1 meal per person)

  • 3 star: ₩220,000~450,000 (cheaper for lunch)
  • 2 star: ₩220,000~360,000
  • 1 star: ₩150,000~360,000
  • Bib Gourmand: ₩8,000~80,000

7. Recommendations by persona

American persona (special occasion·anniversary)

  • 1st place: Layeon (3 star, Shilla Hotel, dress formal, perfect English)
  • 2nd place: Jeongsikdang (NYC home + Seoul)
  • Lunch Option: La Yeon Lunch Course (₩220,000~) — 30% cheaper than dinner

European persona (foodie/gourmet deep)

  • 1st place: Mosu (3 star, chef Ahn Seongjae, pinnacle of modern Korean food)
  • 2nd place: Lasse (Scandinavian-Korean fusion)
  • 3rd place: Onzium (court Korean food, European persona fit ★)

Family/group of 4-6 people

  • 1st place: Kwon Sook-su (1 star, suitable for fermentation, course)
  • 2nd place: Jinjin (Chinese fine dining·table atmosphere)
  • 3rd place: Woorae-ok (Bib, Pyongyang cold noodles, family meal)

Japanese lover

  • 1st place: Kojima (Omakase, Apgujeong)

Reasonable price and cultural experience

  • 1st place: Gwangjang Market Parkgane Bindaetteok (Bib·CNN recommendation)
  • 2nd place: Woo Rae-ok (1946 ~ Pyongyang Naengmyeon)

8. Recommendations by season

  • Spring (April-May): Layeon, Mosu, formal dining room (terrace, view)
  • Summer (June-August): Jinju House (bean noodle soup), Wooraeok (Pyongyang cold noodles), Layeon (Hotel cool)
  • Fall (October-November): Kwon Sook-su and Onjium (traditional/fermentation season)
  • Winter (December-February): Mosu·Lasse·Kang Mincheol (indoor·course fit)

9. Checklist before making a Michelin reservation

  • [ ] catchtable 1-4 weeks in advance / official site reservation (2 months in advance for 3 star)
  • [ ] Check dress code (formal vs smart casual)
  • [ ] Request dietary options in advance (vegetarian·halal·allergy)
  • [ ] Payment card (many foreign cards OK, some Korean cards only)
  • [ ] dietary request in advance
  • [ ] Recommended 15 minutes before arrival (formal store)
  • [ ] Check whether photo taking is OK (limited to some stores)
