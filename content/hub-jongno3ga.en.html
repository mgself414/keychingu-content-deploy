Why Jongno 3-ga (종로3가) as a Hub — the Traditional Crossroads of Ikseon-dong (익선동), Insa-dong (인사동), and Gwangjang Market (광장시장)

Jongno 3-ga Station is a triple transfer station where Line 1, Line 3, and Line 5 meet. Step out of the station and you're right at the Ikseon-dong hanok street; one stop away lie Insa-dong, Gwangjang Market, Bukchon (북촌), and Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) — the heart of northern downtown Seoul, where tradition and hanok are packed most densely. From here it branches out to Anguk (안국) · Gyeongbokgung (Line 3) · Jonggak (종각) · Gwangjang Market (Line 1) · Gwanghwamun (광화문) · Euljiro (을지로) (Line 5). If DDP (③) is the southeastern district of shopping and late nights, Jongno 3-ga is the northern axis of palaces, markets, and hanok.

This guide is a new axis orthogonal to the line-by-line series (#1241–#1249). "Radial from one station, Jongno 3-ga, in three directions" — it weaves together what appears on each line, centered on this hub. It is the fifth installment of the Subway Course Series (Phase 2 · Transfer Hubs).

Jongno 3-ga Hub — Curation by 3 Directions: When You Transfer Here

🟠 Direction 1. Line 3 — Anguk · Gyeongbokgung (Palaces · Hanok · Galleries)

  • 🚇 Direction: Anguk (Bukchon · Samcheong (삼청) · Insa-dong) · Gyeongbokgung (Seochon (서촌)) · Euljiro 3-ga (을지로3가)
  • 🍜 Eat: Anguk · Samcheong (Hoadeu Gallery Cafe (호아드 갤러리카페) · Mangata (만가타) · Yeongyeongdang (연경당)) · Seochon (Tosokchon Samgyetang (토속촌 삼계탕) · Jalppajin Memil Seochon (잘빠진메밀 서촌) · Ppyeotanjip Seochon (뼈탄집 서촌))
  • 👀 Play: Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌 한옥마을) · Samcheong-dong (삼청동) (#1242) · Gyeongbokgung · Seochon (#1244) · Insa-dong traditional street · galleries · crafts
  • Travel time: Anguk 2 min · Gyeongbokgung 5 min
  • cross-ref: #1242 Line 3 (Anguk · Gyeongbokgung) · #1244 Line 5 (Gwanghwamun)

🔵 Direction 2. Line 1 — Jonggak · Gwangjang Market (Downtown · Traditional Market)

  • 🚇 Direction: Jonggak (Bosingak (보신각) · Cheonggyecheon (청계천) · Kyobo Book Centre (교보문고)) · Jongno 5-ga (종로5가) (Gwangjang Market) · Dongdaemun (동대문)
  • 🍜 Eat: Gwangjang Market (bindaetteok mung-bean pancakes · mayak gimbap mini rolls · yukhoe beef tartare) · Uraeok (우래옥) with Pyeongyang naengmyeon (cold buckwheat noodles) · Abebe Bakery (아베베 베이커리) · Jangsu Samgyetang (장수삼계탕)
  • 👀 Play: Gwangjang Market (traditional market food · #1236 night-market line) · Bosingak · Cheonggyecheon (downtown stroll) · Jongno (종로) bookstores · jewelry street
  • Travel time: Jonggak 2 min · Jongno 5-ga 2 min
  • cross-ref: #1252 DDP hub (Dongdaemun direction) · #1236 summer night market

🟣 Direction 3. Line 5 — Gwanghwamun · Euljiro (Downtown · Hip-jiro (힙지로))

  • 🚇 Direction: Gwanghwamun (Gyeongbokgung · Gwanghwamun Square (광화문광장)) · Euljiro 4-ga (을지로4가) (Hip-jiro) · Dongdaemun History & Culture Park (동대문역사문화공원) (#1252)
  • 🍜 Eat: Gwanghwamun downtown Korean food · Euljiro nogari (dried pollack snacks) · hip cafes (#1236) · adjacent to Ikseon-dong
  • 👀 Play: Gwanghwamun Square · Gyeongbokgung (#1244) · Euljiro Hip-jiro (#1236 alleys) · DDP direction (#1252)
  • Travel time: Gwanghwamun 3 min · Euljiro 4-ga 3 min
  • cross-ref: #1244 Line 5 (Gwanghwamun) · #1236 Euljiro · #1252 DDP hub

🎯 Jongno 3-ga Station Itself — Before You Transfer

  • 🏮 Ikseon-dong hanok street: 1920s hanok-alley cafes and dining (Seoul Coffee Ikseon Main Branch (서울커피 익선본점) · Uju Bakery Ikseon (우즈베이커리 익선) · Sullagil Bibi (순라길 비비) · Muwi Nagwon (무위낙원) · Seoul 88 Beer (서울88맥주)) — right in front of the station
  • 🖌 Insa-dong: traditional crafts · galleries · tea houses · Ssamziegil (쌈지길) — one block away
  • 🌳 Jongmyo (종묘) · Tapgol Park (탑골공원): UNESCO World Heritage Jongmyo · Tapgol Park, the birthplace of the March 1st Independence Movement
  • 💡 Hub tip: 3-line transfers take a 3–5 min walk. Ikseon-dong is right at Exit 1. Jewelry and lighting wholesale arcades.

5 Transfer & Usage Tips — Get 200% Out of the Jongno 3-ga Hub

Tip 1. Jongno 3-ga is a "Tradition & Hanok Hub"

Ikseon-dong hanok is right in front of the station. With Lines 1·3·5, Insa-dong, Bukchon, Gyeongbokgung, and Gwangjang Market are all one or two stops away.

Tip 2. The 3 Directions Have Different Characters

Line 3 = palaces · Bukchon · hanok, Line 1 = traditional market · downtown, Line 5 = Gwanghwamun · Euljiro. Match it to your purpose.

Tip 3. Walk Ikseon-dong

From Exit 1, the Ikseon-dong hanok street is right there. Walkable all the way to Insa-dong, Tapgol Park, and Jongmyo.

Tip 4. Gwangjang Market: Daytime & Evening

The old shops for bindaetteok, mayak gimbap, and yukhoe run from daytime into the evening. Night-market atmosphere (#1236 line).

Tip 5. One Transit Card

One T-money card covers all of Lines 1·3·5. From Jongno 3-ga to anywhere downtown.
