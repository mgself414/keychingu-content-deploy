Spring Cherry Blossom Season Chart — Full Set of Weekly Spots in March & April

🎯 Why a Spring Cherry Blossom Season Chart?

The top inbound priority for foreign visitors in spring is the peak cherry blossom season in April. This is a follow-up to #230, providing weekly spots for the season chart. It aims for weekly SEO traffic (a prime spot for foreign enthusiasts).

★ Weekly Matrix (5-Week Full Set)

March 4th Week (3/25~3/31) — Spring Begins

  • Southern Region (Jeju (제주) #253) Cherry blossoms begin + Busan (부산) camellias
  • Seoul National Cemetery (국립서울현충원) (#181 Dongjak (동작)) First cherry blossoms begin

April 1st Week (4/1~4/7) — Seoul Progresses

  • Yeouido Cherry Blossom Road (윤중로) (Yeouido (여의도) #169) Cherry blossoms progress + foreign enthusiasts
  • Banpo Hangang Park (반포 한강) (#173) Cherry blossoms begin
  • Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) (#82) Cherry blossoms begin

April 2nd Week (4/8~4/14) — Peak Season

  • Yeouido Cherry Blossom Road (윤중로) Peak ★ Korea's #1 cherry blossom spot (#230)
  • Seoul Forest (서울숲) · Olympic Park (올림픽공원) · Campuses (#204) Peak
  • Changdeokgung Palace (창덕궁) · Changgyeonggung Palace (창경궁) (#207) Peak + hanbok (traditional Korean attire)

April 3rd Week (4/15~4/21) — Outskirts

  • Jinhae Gunhangje Festival (진해 군항제) ★ Korea's #1 cherry blossom festival
  • Gyeongju Bomun Lake (경주 보문호) (#256) Peak
  • Seoul's Last Cherry Blossoms

April 4th Week (4/22~4/30) — Outskirts Late Season

  • Bukhansan Mountain (북한산) · Gangneung (강릉) (#254) Outskirts Cherry Blossoms
  • Jeonju Hanok Village (전주 한옥마을) (#255) Foreign enthusiasts

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreign Visitors

1. "April = Cherry blossoms everywhere": April 1st week: Yeouido Cherry Blossom Road (윤중로) begins → April 2nd week: Seoul peak → April 3rd week: Jinhae (진해) and outskirts peak.

2. "Jinhae (진해) = Too far to go": KTX (Korea Train eXpress) to Jinhae (진해) + prime spot for foreign enthusiasts (Korea's #1 cherry blossom festival).

3. "Cherry blossoms = Only during the day": Night cherry blossoms at Yeouido Cherry Blossom Road (윤중로) · Banpo (반포) · Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) = #1 spot for foreign Instagrammers.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Foreign visitors' cherry blossom peak: April 2nd week (Seoul) · April 3rd week (Jinhae (진해)).

2. Top spots for foreign visitors: Yeouido Cherry Blossom Road (윤중로) · Banpo (반포) · Changdeokgung Palace (창덕궁) · Jinhae (진해) · Gyeongju (경주).

3. Foreign visitors' advance booking: Top priority hotels and KTX (Korea Train eXpress) for April enthusiasts.

4. Foreign visitors' cherry blossoms + hanbok (traditional Korean attire) (#207): Free entry to 4 palaces + foreign enthusiasts.
