10 Seoul Cafes — Only Where Foreign Reviews Showed Up First

Subtitle: Roasting cafes in Hongdae and Mangwon and a Han River pier cafe, bakery, ice cream, and bingsu cafes in Seongsu, a souffle pancake cafe and a hand-drip cafe in Haebangchon, a terrace cafe in Hannam-dong, and an espresso bar in Samgakji. Only cafes where foreign-language reviews appeared first on Naver Map made the cut — 10 of them, grouped into 3 zones. Seoul zones are linked by subway, so the routes are three half-day courses. The in-cafe rules like holding a seat, mugs, and the return counter are in #1378

ISSUE 06 · #1440 · 2026-11-23 · Seoul Cafes

🎯 Why "Three-Language Reviews" Is the Bar

There is one selection rule — cafes on Naver Map (네이버 지도) with English, Japanese, and Chinese reviews together. Instead of Korean popularity rankings, we picked places foreign visitors have already found and left traces in three languages. The judgment is that such a place is less unfamiliar for the next foreign visitor. All 10 venues have reviews in all three languages. Review counts are a 2026-11 snapshot, listed for every venue. None of the 10 venues in this piece appeared in other KeyChinGu pieces.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours, closed days, and menu prices are the values the owner registered on Kakao Map (카카오맵) (checked 2026-11-23). Prices are the registered price for one drink and one dessert. Where no price is registered, or the menu was registered more than 2 years ago, the cell says "check Kakao Map on the day." Dietary cues are only what can be read from menu names, the owner's descriptions, and Kakao Map summaries. Check last order on Kakao Map. Ask staff before ordering about ingredients and allergies. Nearby stations are listed by name only. Check the route from the station to the venue and the time it takes with Kakao Map directions on the day.

① Hongdae·Mapo — 3 Venues

One in Seogyo-dong (서교동) and two in Mangwon (망원). Analog Garden opens at 08:00 on weekends, the earliest of the 10 by registered hours. Newcase is open until 22:00. KOMAME is closed on Monday.

  • Analog Garden (아날로그가든) — a roastery and brunch cafe in Seogyo-dong. Kakao Map tags include roasting specialty (로스팅전문) and Australian brunch (호주브런치). Five menu items are registered — double shot espresso (더블샷 에스프레소), long black (롱블랙), flat white (플랫화이트), cafe latte (카페라떼), and cappuccino (카푸치노) — but all of the price registrations are more than 2 years old, so check current prices on Kakao Map on the day. No food menu is registered, so look in the shop. Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00, Sat·Sun 08:00–18:00, and no closed day is registered. 145 Donggyo-ro, Mapo-gu (마포구 동교로 145). Nearby stations are Hapjeong Station (합정역) and Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역) (Line 2) and Sangsu Station (상수역) (Line 6). No parking lot. 15 foreign reviews (5·5·5). Dietary cues: the lattes, cappuccino, and flat white contain milk, and the coffee drinks have caffeine. Kakao Map has a "vegan food" (비건푸드) tag, but no such menu item is registered, so ask which menu item it is when you order.
  • Newcase (뉴케이스) — a Han River view cafe on the 3rd floor of the Hangang Bus Mangwon Pier (망원선착장) building. The name on Kakao Map is "Newcase Mangwon Main Branch (뉴케이스 망원본점)", and blog posts mention board games and a listening library. Registered prices are Americano 5,000 won, cafe latte 5,500 won, Newcase cafe latte (뉴케이스카페라떼) 6,000 won, vanilla latte 6,500 won, matcha latte 7,500 won, avocado banana latte (아보카도바나나라떼) 8,500 won, yuzu black (유즈블랙) 8,500 won, cheesecake 6,500 won, Newcase cheesecake (뉴케이스치즈케이크) 8,500 won, and Pis & Cheese (피스&치즈) 12,000 won. Open daily 10:00–22:00. 420 Mapo-naru-gil, Mapo-gu (마포구 마포나루길 420). Nearby stations are Mangwon Station (망원역) (Line 6) and Hapjeong Station (Line 6). It's away from the station, so do the directions first. Pets-allowed tag. No parking lot, and the public parking lot at nearby Mangwon Hangang Park (망원한강공원) is paid. 12 foreign reviews (4·4·4). Dietary cues: the lattes contain milk. The avocado banana latte has avocado and banana in it, as the name says. The two cheesecakes are desserts made with cheese (dairy). The Kakao Map summary describes Pis & Cheese as a cake with pistachio and cheese, so avoid it if you have a nut allergy. Matcha and coffee have caffeine.
  • KOMAME — a sandwich and coffee shop in Mangwon-dong. The name on Kakao Map is "Komame (코마메)". Jambon-beurre (잠봉뵈르), hummus sandwich (후무스샌드위치), toast (토스트), shrimp cream croquette (새우크림고로케), Americano, cafe latte, and decaf are registered, but not one price is registered, so check Kakao Map on the day. Closed Mondays; Tue–Thu·Sun 09:30–18:00, Fri·Sat 09:30–19:00. 20 Worldcup-ro 23-gil, Mapo-gu (마포구 월드컵로23길 20). Nearby stations are Mangwon Station and Mapo-gu Office Station (마포구청역) (Line 6). No parking. Of its 8 foreign reviews, 5 are in English. Dietary cues: the jambon-beurre is a sandwich of pork ham (jambon) and jalapeno mayonnaise in a baguette (Kakao Map intro). Mayonnaise contains eggs, and the jalapeno can make it spicy. Beurre is French for butter. Hummus is usually made from chickpeas mixed with sesame paste (tahini). The shrimp cream croquette contains shrimp and cream, and the coating is usually made with flour and eggs. Sandwiches and toast are bread, so they contain wheat flour. Decaf coffee is offered separately.

② Seongsu — 3 Venues

Clustered around Seongsu Station (성수역), Ttukseom Station (뚝섬역), and Seoulsup Station (서울숲역). One bakery, one ice cream shop, and one bingsu shop. Gidalbing is open until 23:00, the latest closing of the three in this zone by registered hours.

  • Cat&F (캣앤에프) — a bakery cafe on Yeonmujang-gil (연무장길). The name on Kakao Map is "Bakery Cafe CAT&F (베이커리카페 CAT&F)". One menu item is registered, the Bungnyangi bread (붕냥이빵), but its price registration is more than 2 years old, so check the current price on Kakao Map on the day. Look at the other breads and drinks in the shop. Only public holiday hours of 09:00–22:00 are registered, and the hours by day of the week are empty. If you're going on a weekday or weekend, check that day's hours on Kakao Map. 29-9 Yeonmujang-gil, Seongdong-gu (성동구 연무장길 29-9). Nearby stations are Seongsu Station and Ttukseom Station (Line 2). No parking lot. 15 foreign reviews (5·5·5). Dietary cues: the owner's description says the Bungnyangi bread is baked with sweet corn, whole red bean paste, and walnuts. If you have a walnut (nut) allergy, avoid it. Ask about the ingredients of other breads and drinks when you order.
  • Beurre Beurre Seongsu (뵈르뵈르 성수) — an ice cream and latte shop on Seongsu-iro (성수이로). Its Kakao Map category is ice cream. Americano, latte, strawberry latte (딸기라떼), matcha latte (말차라떼), chocolate latte (초코라떼), and shake (쉐이크) are registered, but no prices are registered, so check Kakao Map on the day. Open daily 11:00–22:00. 22 Seongsu-iro 7ga-gil, Seongdong-gu (성동구 성수이로7가길 22). Nearby stations are Seongsu Station and Ttukseom Station (Line 2). Branches with the same name are in several places in Seoul (Itaewon (이태원), Daehangno (대학로), Konkuk Univ. (건대), and others), so find it by the address. 13 foreign reviews (5·4·4). Dietary cues: the lattes and shakes contain milk. The strawberry latte has strawberry and the chocolate latte has chocolate in the name. Matcha and coffee have caffeine.
  • Gidalbing (기댈빙) — a bingsu dessert cafe in an alley near Seoul Forest (서울숲). It uses the 1st and 2nd floors. The only registered menu item is Kkamang-sogeumi (까망소금이) at 2,900 won, with no description of what it is. Don't read it as a bingsu price — check bingsu and drink prices on Kakao Map on the day. Open daily 11:00–23:00. 24-8 Seoulsup 2-gil, Seongdong-gu (성동구 서울숲2길 24-8) (per Kakao Map). Nearby stations are Seoulsup Station (Suin-Bundang Line) and Ttukseom Station (Line 2). 11 foreign reviews (5·3·3). Dietary cues: with only one menu item registered, there are no cues to read from menu names. Ask when you order what goes into the bingsu.

③ Yongsan (Haebangchon·Hannam·Samgakji) — 4 Venues

Two in Haebangchon (해방촌), one in Hannam-dong (한남동), and one in Yongridan-gil (용리단길) near Samgakji Station (삼각지역). Kakao Map tags it as Yongridan-gil. MOTHER Offline is open 09:00–24:00, the latest of the 10 by registered hours. The two Haebangchon venues don't open before 12:00.

  • Toteoseu (토터스) — a mochi souffle pancake dessert cafe on Sinheung-ro (신흥로) in Haebangchon. It uses the 1st and 2nd floors. Registered prices are strawberry mochi souffle (딸기 모찌 수플레) 18,000 won, purple sweet potato mochi souffle (자색 고구마 모찌 수플레) 16,000 won, Dubai chocolate mochi souffle (두바이 초코 모찌 수플레) 21,000 won, matcha tiramisu mochi souffle pancake (말차 티라미수 모찌 수플레 팬케이크) 15,000 won, Puffle (푸플레) 11,000 won, custard pudding (커스터드 푸딩) 6,000 won, Americano 5,000 won, latte 5,500 won, and flat white 5,500 won. The filter coffee price is not registered. Open daily 12:00–21:20. Check last order on Kakao Map. 81 Sinheung-ro, Yongsan-gu (용산구 신흥로 81). Nearby stations are Noksapyeong Station (녹사평역) (Line 6) and Sookmyung Women's Univ. Station (숙대입구역) (Line 4). It's away from the station, so look at the route in a map app first. The Kakao Map summary mentions that weekend parking is hard and shows a no-kids zone mark. 15 foreign reviews (5·5·5). Dietary cues: souffle pancakes contain eggs and dairy and may be a wheat flour batter. The pudding is a dessert with eggs and dairy. Mochi is made from glutinous rice, but it may contain wheat flour, so ask if gluten is a concern. Dubai chocolate usually uses pistachio (nuts), kadaif (wheat), and tahini (sesame). Ask when you order what the Puffle is. The latte and flat white contain milk. Matcha and coffee have caffeine.
  • Cafe Tajagi (카페 타자기) — a hand-drip cafe on the 2nd floor of a building on Sinheung-ro in Haebangchon. Kakao Map tags include vintage (빈티지) and rooftop (루프탑). Registered prices are Choco Tajagi (초코타자기) 7,000 won, Choco Jeongbok (초코정복) 7,000 won, Americano 5,500 won, cafe latte 6,000 won, and banana bread 7,000 won. Hours differ by day. Mon·Tue·Fri–Sun 12:00–22:00, Wednesday 13:00–21:00, Thursday 12:00–21:00. 97-5 Sinheung-ro, Yongsan-gu (용산구 신흥로 97-5). Nearby stations are Noksapyeong Station (Line 6) and Sookmyung Women's Univ. Station (Line 4). It's away from the station, so look at the route in a map app first. No parking. 12 foreign reviews (4·4·4). Dietary cues: the Kakao Map summary of the banana bread mentions butter and cinnamon, and as a bread it contains wheat flour. Choco Tajagi is an iced mocha-base coffee, so it has caffeine, and Choco Jeongbok is a cocoa drink. The cafe latte contains milk, and coffee has caffeine.
  • MOTHER Offline (마더오프라인) — a terrace cafe in Hannam-dong. The name on Kakao Map is "Mother Offline (마더오프라인)". Registered prices are Americano 7,000 won, cafe latte 7,500 won, flat white 7,500 won, matcha latte 9,000 won, lemon pound cake 7,000 won, brownie & vanilla ice cream (브라우니&바닐라아이스크림) 9,000 won, Basque cheesecake (바스크치즈케이크) 10,000 won, jambon cheese sandwich (잠봉치즈샌드위치) 10,000 won, and yogurt granola bowl (요거트그래놀라 볼) 5,000 won. At 7,000 won, the Americano is the most expensive of the five venues with a registered Americano price. Open daily 09:00–24:00. 6 Itaewon-ro 55na-gil, Yongsan-gu (용산구 이태원로55나길 6). Nearby stations are Hangangjin Station (한강진역) and Itaewon Station (이태원역) (Line 6). Valet and paid parking are available, and there is a pets-allowed tag. 11 foreign reviews (5·3·3). Dietary cues: the lattes and flat white contain milk. The ice cream in the brownie & vanilla ice cream is dairy, and brownies commonly contain eggs and wheat flour. Basque cheesecake usually contains cream cheese and eggs. The jambon cheese sandwich contains pork ham, cheese, and wheat bread. The yogurt in the yogurt granola bowl is dairy, and ask whether the granola contains nuts. Lemon pound cake is a cake that commonly contains wheat flour and eggs. Matcha and coffee have caffeine. Kakao Map also lists alcoholic menu items such as wine, cocktails, and beer, so in the evening it becomes a place that serves alcohol.
  • Fjord (피요르드) — an espresso bar and dessert cafe on Yongridan-gil near Samgakji Station. Registered prices are espresso 4,800 won, Americano 5,500 won, latte 5,800 won, cappuccino 5,800 won, dark chocolate (다크초코) 7,000 won, affogato 7,500 won, and Gold Rush espresso (골드러시에스프레소) 9,500 won. The Fredo espresso (프레도 에스프레소) and marshmallow latte (마시멜로 라떼) price registrations are more than 2 years old, so check Kakao Map on the day. Hours are Mon·Tue 10:00–19:00, Wed–Sun 10:00–21:30. 2-7 Hangang-daero 53-gil, Yongsan-gu (용산구 한강대로53길 2-7). Nearby stations are Samgakji Station (Lines 4 and 6) and Sinyongsan Station (신용산역) (Line 4). No parking lot, and a pets-allowed tag. 9 foreign reviews (5·2·2). Dietary cues: the latte and cappuccino contain milk. Affogato is a menu item where espresso is poured over ice cream (dairy). The marshmallow latte has marshmallow in it, as the name says. The OJ Americano (오제이 아메리카노) is a drink of espresso mixed with orange juice and sparkling water (owner's description). All coffee menu items have caffeine.

🗺 Route — Three Half-Day Courses

Seoul zones are linked by subway, so you can link two zones in one day. Still, a half-day course of two or three cafes within one zone is the comfortable one. Check the moves between venues and the time they take with Kakao Map directions on the day.

A. Hongdae·Mangwon half day (Tue–Sun)

  1. Morning Analog Garden in Seogyo-dong (from 09:00 on weekdays, 08:00 on weekends)
  2. Lunch KOMAME sandwich in Mangwon (Tue–Thu·Sun until 18:00, Fri·Sat until 19:00). It's closed on Monday
  3. Afternoon to evening Newcase at the Han River pier (until 22:00)

B. Seongsu half day

  1. Daytime Bread at Cat&F. Hours by day of the week aren't registered, so check that day's hours on Kakao Map before you leave
  2. Afternoon Ice cream or a latte at Beurre Beurre Seongsu (11:00–22:00)
  3. Evening Gidalbing bingsu near Seoul Forest (until 23:00)

C. Samgakji·Haebangchon·Hannam half day

  1. Morning Fjord espresso near Samgakji Station (from 10:00)
  2. After lunch Toteoseu souffle pancake in Haebangchon (from 12:00) or Cafe Tajagi (from 13:00 on Wednesday only)
  3. Evening MOTHER Offline in Hannam-dong (until 24:00)

KOMAME is closed on Monday. Fjord closes at 19:00 on Mon·Tue. The two Haebangchon venues don't open before 12:00. The rules in Korean cafes for holding a seat, mugs, and the return counter are in #1378.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Planning a Mangwon course on a Monday — KOMAME is closed on Monday. If you go to Mangwon on a Monday, you can only pair Analog Garden and Newcase. On the same Mon·Tue, Fjord also closes at 19:00, so don't put it in an evening course. Cafe Tajagi opens at 13:00 on Wednesday only.
  2. Going to Cat&F on a guess about its hours — Kakao Map has only the public holiday hours of 09:00–22:00 registered, and the weekday and weekend hours are empty. Check that day's hours on Kakao Map before you go.
  3. Going to a similar-named venue or a different branch — Analog Garden has a similar-named "Analog Chimpanzee Garden (아날로그침팬지가든)" (42 Poeun-ro (포은로 42)) and a Gimpo branch (김포점) as separate venues. Beurre Beurre has branches with the same name in several places in Seoul (Itaewon, Daehangno, Konkuk Univ., and others). The Kakao Map names also differ from this piece's spellings. KOMAME shows up as "Komame (코마메)", MOTHER Offline as "Mother Offline (마더오프라인)", Cat&F as "Bakery Cafe CAT&F (베이커리카페 CAT&F)", Newcase as "Newcase Mangwon Main Branch (뉴케이스 망원본점)", and Cafe Tajagi as "Tajagi (타자기)". In your map app, search by this piece's street addresses, not the venue names.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. If I could pick just one? — Three venues have 15 foreign reviews (5·5·5), one in each zone. In Hongdae·Mapo it's Analog Garden (opens at 08:00 on weekends), in Seongsu Cat&F (check hours on Kakao Map on the day), and in Yongsan (Haebangchon·Hannam·Samgakji) Toteoseu (12:00–21:20). Pick the zone closest to where you're staying.
  2. Where can I go late in the evening? — By registered hours, MOTHER Offline is open until 24:00 and Gidalbing until 23:00. Next are Newcase and Beurre Beurre Seongsu (22:00 daily) and Cafe Tajagi (22:00 on Mon·Tue·Fri–Sun).
  3. If I want to know more about using cafes? — The in-cafe rules in Korea, like holding a seat, mugs, and the return counter, are in #1378. Jeju cafes are in #1438, the same format.
