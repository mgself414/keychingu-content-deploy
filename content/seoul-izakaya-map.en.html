Seoul Izakaya Guide — 6 Skewer, Small-Plate, and Meal Izakaya (Waraku, Tenkai, Marimo, Urang, Grill Ajisai, Midong Sikdang) (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: Waraku (와라쿠) 18:00~01:00, closed Sundays (VisitSeoul) · Tenkai (텐카이) has 40-some kinds of skewers, Hyatt Hotel LL floor in Hannam-dong (한남동) (VisitSeoul) · Marimo (마리모) 18:00~04:00 (VisitSeoul) · Midong Sikdang (미동식당) requires one drink per person (VisitSeoul) · Alcohol is allowed from January 1 of the year you turn 19 (Easy Law, Korea Ministry of Government Legislation) — as of 2026-10-21

ISSUE 03 · #487 · 2026-05-27

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Fat drips from skewers over charcoal and smoke rises, and sake sloshes in a small cup — Waraku (와라쿠) is introduced as a place that serves authentic kushiyaki (grilled skewers) (VisitSeoul). This guide is a map for travelers who want to try a Japanese-style pub (izakaya) in Seoul. There are three branches: grilled skewers, places centered on small plates, and places that also serve meals. Businesses were picked only if they are introduced on Seoul's official tourism site VisitSeoul as an izakaya, Japanese skewer house, or pub, and had their information revised in 2026. Because the trade changes often, shops we could not confirm were left out.

The old guide gave rankings and prices such as "Hannam (한남) = Korea's biggest izakaya street" and "Itaewon (이태원) ₩30,000~50,000 per person, Gangnam (강남) ₩100,000~300,000." There is no official source, so all of it was deleted. Of the six places confirmed this time, the only one in Hannam-dong (한남동) is Tenkai (텐카이), inside the Hyatt Hotel (VisitSeoul).

🍢 Grilled Skewers — 2 Places

  • Waraku (와라쿠) — Apgujeong-ro 4-gil 13-7 (압구정로4길 13-7), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Mon~Sat 18:00~01:00, closed Sundays. Authentic Japanese-style kushiyaki, signature items Negima (네기마), Shouniku (쇼우니꾸), Dontoro (돈도로), and Gyutan (규단). 721 m from Sinsa Station (신사역) Exit 6. Phone 0507-1416-2295 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)
  • Tenkai (텐카이) — Sowol-ro 322 (소월로 322) (Hannam-dong (한남동), Hyatt Hotel) LL floor, Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seoul. Sun~Thu 18:00~24:00, Fri and Sat 18:00~01:00, closed on holidays. It grills 40-some kinds of skewers of meat, poultry, seafood, and vegetables, and every table is tatami. Sake, soju, and fruit wine. The website is in Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, and Russian. Phone 02-799-8274 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)

🍶 Small-Plate Focus — 2 Places

  • Marimo (마리모) — Dosan-daero 49-gil 10-1 (도산대로49길 10-1) (Sinsa-dong (신사동)), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Tue~Sun 18:00~04:00, closed Mondays and holidays. An izakaya specializing in tofu dishes. Goma dubu (고마두부) made with black sesame, and mochi dubu (모찌두부) with cheese (VisitSeoul English), plus gyoza-tang (교자탕, dumpling soup), oden-tang (오뎅탕, fish cake soup), al-tang (알탕, fish roe soup), and hoedeopbap (회덮밥, raw fish rice bowl). 480 m from Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역) Exit 5. Phone 02-545-5885 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)
  • Urang Cheongdam branch (우랑 청담점) — Apgujeong-ro 79-gil 79 (압구정로79길 79) (Cheongdam-dong (청담동)), JM Building 1F, Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Daily 11:00~24:00. Order yakiniku and a small brazier comes out, where you grill meat, vegetables, and rice balls yourself. Urang-nabe (우랑나베, hot pot), snow crab shabu-shabu, and sashimi. Phone 0507-1318-4010 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)

🍱 Places That Also Serve Meals — 2 Places

  • Grill Ajisai (그릴아지사이) — Teheran-ro 13-gil 8-10 (테헤란로13길 8-10) (Dongyoung Building (동영빌딩)), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Mon~Fri 11:30~14:00 and 17:30~23:00, closed Sat and Sun. A Japanese head chef serves dishes in the style of mainland Japan. At lunch there are lines for tonkatsu, sukiyaki, and omurice, and at dinner sake goes alongside. Ganbare Otosan SET (간바레오또상SET), shiromi (시로미, white fish), ton-shabu salad (돈샤브 샐러드), and kaisendon (가이센동, seafood rice bowl). Price range in the ₩10,000s. 239 m from Yeoksam Station (역삼역) Exit 4. Phone 02-565-7442 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)
  • Midong Sikdang (미동식당) — Chungjeong-ro 6-gil 59 (충정로6길 59), Seosomun Apartment (서소문아파트) 1F, Unit 6, Seodaemun-gu (서대문구), Seoul. Weekdays 17:15~23:00, closed Sat and Sun. A small pub on the ground floor of a residential-commercial apartment building over 50 years old. Order for your party size and the kitchen serves an omakase small-plate set from ingredients it chooses. One drink per person is required, from draft beer to bottled sake. If customers are waiting, the limit is 2 hours 30 minutes, up to 4 people. 293 m from Seodaemun Station (서대문역) Exit 7. Phone 02-313-0958 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Vegetarian: None of the six official pages lists a vegetarian menu. Marimo (마리모) has tofu dishes and Tenkai (텐카이) has vegetable skewers, but we could not confirm the ingredients in soups and sauces — ask before ordering (VisitSeoul)
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid in Itaewon (이태원) (Usadan-ro 10-gil 15 (우사단로10길 15), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00~21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF). Recognition can change, so check before you visit. Every izakaya in this guide serves alcohol
  • Allergies: Seafood dishes are plentiful, such as Urang's (우랑) snow crab shabu-shabu and sashimi and Marimo's (마리모) al-tang (fish roe soup) (VisitSeoul). Tell them about shellfish or fish allergies in advance
  • Price range: The only officially posted value is Grill Ajisai's (그릴아지사이) "₩10,000s" (VisitSeoul Korean edition). For the rest, prices are not posted — order from the menu board
  • For a show-it card, see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Izakaya Practicalities — Age, Solo, Reservations, Payment, Ordering, Getting Around

  • Age: Under Korean law a "minor" is anyone under 19, and a person who has reached January 1 of the year they turn 19 is excluded (Easy Law, Korea Ministry of Government Legislation). Bring your passport
  • Solo: The official pages do not say whether they seat solo diners. Midong Sikdang (미동식당) takes up to 4 people (VisitSeoul). For places closed on weekends (Grill Ajisai (그릴아지사이), Midong Sikdang (미동식당)), go on a weekday
  • Reservations: None of the six official pages lists a reservation route. Ask by phone
  • Payment: For cards and mobile payments, see #527 Korea's Payment System
  • Ordering: At Midong Sikdang (미동식당), just say how many people and the small plates come out. If you cannot make yourself understood, see #1339 Ordering Etiquette. For comparing kinds of drinking places, see #830 Drinking Pub vs Izakaya vs Bar
  • Getting around: Marimo (마리모) (04:00) and Waraku (와라쿠) (01:00) close later than the last subway. For the way back, see #1395 If You Missed the Last Train

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "All izakaya stay open until dawn" — Grill Ajisai (그릴아지사이) closes at 23:00 and rests on weekends. Midong Sikdang (미동식당) is also open only until 23:00 on weekdays (VisitSeoul)

2. "You can order just the small plates" — Midong Sikdang (미동식당) requires one drink per person (VisitSeoul)

3. "Tenkai is a shop in an Itaewon alley" — Its address is the Hyatt Hotel LL floor in Hannam-dong (한남동) (Sowol-ro 322 (소월로 322)) (VisitSeoul)

4. "You can drink only after your 19th birthday" — The standard is January 1 of the year you turn 19 (Easy Law, Korea Ministry of Government Legislation)
