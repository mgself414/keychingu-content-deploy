Hongdae Cafés & Desserts: 89-Spot Guide — Curated for International Visitors

> Subtitle: Hongdae (홍대)·Yeonnam (연남)·Hapjeong (합정)·Mangwon (망원) Café & Dessert Category Curation — Deep Dive from the #158 Comprehensive Guide

ISSUE 03 · #160 · 2026-05-14

🎯 Top 5 Tips for International Visitors

1. Yeonnam (연남)·Hapjeong (합정)·Mangwon (망원) = One Hongdae Route: All 4 sub-areas are within a 10–15 min walk from Hongik University Station (홍익대입구역). String them together and hit 5+ spots in one go.

2. Weekday 14:00–17:00 (2–5 PM) is Golden Hour: Weekends and late nights mean 1-hour+ queues. English-friendly spots are quietest on weekday afternoons — best time for smooth service too.

3. Counter Seats & Reservations: Japanese-style compact cafés are mostly counter seating. Aim for around 19:00 (7 PM) on weekdays. English-capable venues take reservations via Naver or KakaoTalk channel.

4. Foreign Cards Widely Accepted: Hongdae is a top international tourist corridor, so foreign cards, Samsung Pay, and KakaoPay (간편결제 — Korea's tap-and-pay apps similar to Apple Pay or Alipay) work at most spots. For older spots or tiny shops, carry around ₩30,000 (~$22 / ~160 RMB / ~3,300 JPY) in cash just in case.

5. Insta-Worthy Routing: Hanok (한옥 — traditional Korean wooden house) alleys, gallery spaces, vintage interiors, and themed cafés are everywhere. For the best shots with fewer crowds, go at 11:00 (11 AM) or after 19:00 (7 PM).

🥖 Bakeries — A Pilgrimage for Bread Lovers (19 Spots)

Hongdae (홍대) is bread country. Bagels, milk bread, cream buns, campagne, Japanese-style bakeries — it's all here in one neighbourhood.

Spots with a Clear Signature

  • Suspicious Bagel (수상한베이글) — Bagel specialist with strong visual game
  • Aoi Tori (아오이토리) — Japanese anpan (red bean bun) and melon bread done right
  • Puha Cream Bun (푸하하크림빵) — Sells out daily, no exceptions
  • Kim Jinhwan Confectionery (김진환 제과점) — Old-school bakery with subtle, chewy, non-gimmicky bread
  • Modyeon (모뎐) — Modern-concept bakery
  • 395 Bread (395빵집) — Classic neighbourhood bakery energy

Milk Bread, Campagne & Crusty Loaves

  • Bread Tree (빵나무) — Overwhelming variety

  • Chimney Mansion (침니맨션) — Chimney-stack interior as the signature
  • Kurinoki Bakery (쿠리노키제빵) — Japanese-style bakery

Dessert Breads & Snack Buns

  • TYTD (티와이티디) — Modern bakery

> #### 🎯 Deep Pick — Kim Jinhwan Confectionery (김진환 제과점)

> A traditional neighbourhood bakery that has been talked about among bread enthusiasts around Sinchon (신촌) and Hongdae (홍대) for years. The variety is extraordinary and tasting samples are available, so simply browsing the shelves is a pleasure in itself. What sets it apart is the texture: chewy, clean, and never overpowering — bread you could eat every day without getting tired of it. A sign outside the shop lists the day's fresh-bake times, so plan your visit accordingly. It's right next to Sanullim 1992 (산울림1992), making the two easy to combine on one route.

>

> #### 🎯 Deep Pick — Aoi Tori (아오이토리)

> True to its name, "Blue Bird" (青い鳥), this bakery does Japanese-style breads — anpan (red bean buns), melon bread — with real precision. It consistently ranks in the top 5 Hongdae bakeries on big-data dining platforms like Diningcode. The shop is small and queues are common, so a weekday morning visit is your best bet.

>

> #### 🎯 Deep Pick — Puha Cream Bun (푸하하크림빵)

> The name says it all: a cream bun specialist. A milk-cream base rotates through seasonal flavours — Earl Grey, corn, pistachio, and more. Selling out within 30 minutes to an hour of opening is completely normal here, so check the opening time before you go and get there early.

> #### 🎯 Deep Pick — 943 Kings Cross (943 킹스크로스)

> The "943" in the name comes directly from Harry Potter's Platform 9¾. This British-style café recreates the Hogwarts Express concept from London's King's Cross station, complete with vintage trunks, dormitory-style interiors, and British desserts (scones and tea). A must for any Harry Potter fan. For a concept café, the drink quality is surprisingly solid — this is a place worth lingering in, not just snapping and leaving.

> #### 🎯 Deep Pick — Café Comma (카페꼼마)

> A books-and-coffee space operated by Munhakdongne (문학동네), one of Korea's leading literary publishers. The name "Comma (꼼마)" means exactly that — a pause, a breath. The intention is a space where readers can take a moment between chapters. Curated books are placed throughout the café and you're free to pick any up and read. Generous seating and high ceilings make it ideal for laptop work too. Hongdae has plenty of book-and-coffee cafés, but for depth of concept and operational polish, Café Comma (카페꼼마) sits at the top.
