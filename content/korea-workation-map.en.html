Korea Workation Guide — Digital Nomad (F-1-D) Visa Requirements and 9 Workation Spaces (3 in Busan, Suncheon, Goseong, Buan, Yeosu, Jeju Sehwa, Ulsan) (As of November 2026)

Subtitle: Digital Nomad (Workation) visa in full operation from 2026-06-30, up to 3 years (Ministry of Justice official press release) · Income requirement based on 2025 GNI per capita: ₩52,410,000 to ₩104,830,000, tiered by age and region of stay (Ministry of Justice) · Employment and for-profit activity in Korea restricted (Ministry of Justice) · Busan workation official site busaness.com (Busan City official page) · Lodging-type workation inside Suncheon Bay National Garden (VisitKorea) · 6 spaces on the workation-theme public vote (Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism · Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery") — as of 2026-11-24

🎯 How to Use This Guide

You open your laptop and the garden unrolls all the way around the screen — that is the 360-degree garden-view workspace of "Garden Workation" (Jeongwon Workation / 정원워케이션), a lodging inside Suncheon Bay National Garden (VisitKorea). This guide is for remote workers who want to bring an overseas job to Korea for a few weeks to a few months. It covers three things: the visa (Digital Nomad F-1-D requirements and length of stay), the entry paperwork for short visits (K-ETA), and places to work (3 public workation centers in Busan, 2 lodging-type workations on the southern coast, 4 dedicated spaces from the public vote — Buan, Yeosu, Jeju Sehwa, Ulsan). Values below were opened directly on the official pages of the Ministry of Justice, Busan City, VisitKorea, local governments and operators on 2026-10-21 and 2026-11-24, plus Kakao Map registered values (2026-11-24).

🛂 Digital Nomad (Workation) Visa F-1-D

The Ministry of Justice, which ran this visa as a pilot from 2024, announced on 2026-07-07 that it will be in full operation from June 30, 2026 (Ministry of Justice official press release).

  • Who: Foreigners employed by an overseas company who can work remotely and have worked in the same field for at least 1 year, plus their family (spouse and minor children). Applicants must be 18 or older (accompanying children are exempt)
  • Where to apply: Korean embassies and consulates abroad
  • Income requirement: A multiple of the previous year's gross national income (GNI) per capita published by the Bank of Korea, as of the application date. The amounts based on 2025 GNI are below (Ministry of Justice press release attachment)
Age Seoul metropolitan area (excluding population-decline / watch areas) Non-metropolitan and population-decline (watch) areas
Age 18–34 1.5x GNI — ₩78,620,000 1x GNI — ₩52,410,000
Age 35 and over 2x GNI — ₩104,830,000 1.5x GNI — ₩78,620,000
  • If you come with family: 2x GNI in the metropolitan area regardless of age, 1.5x in non-metropolitan and population-decline (watch) areas (Ministry of Justice)
  • Insurance: Private medical insurance of at least ₩100,000,000 covering hospital treatment and repatriation for the whole stay (Ministry of Justice)
  • Documents: Visa application form, passport, 1 photo, fee, income proof such as an employment certificate, payslips and bank transaction records, criminal record certificate, proof of medical insurance, and proof of family relationship if accompanied. To use the non-metropolitan requirement you need a lease of at least 1 month or a booking confirmation from a workation-specialized facility or lodging (Ministry of Justice)
  • Length of stay: 1 year from the entry date at foreigner registration, extendable by 1 year at a time for a maximum of 3 years (Ministry of Justice)
  • What you cannot do: Employment and for-profit activity in Korea are restricted (Ministry of Justice)
  • Changing status: If you enter on a short-term tourist visa (B-1, B-2, C-3) and meet the requirements, you can switch to the Digital Nomad visa (Ministry of Justice)
  • During the pilot period (January 2024 to May 2026), 743 visas were issued. As of May 2026, 340 of the 398 foreigners registered under this visa (about 85%) stay in the metropolitan area (Ministry of Justice official press release)
  • Visa fee (basic table posted by the Korean Embassy in the U.S.): The basic fees posted by the embassy are US$40 for a single-entry visa of 90 days or less, US$60 for a single-entry visa of 91 days or more, US$70 for a double-entry visa, and US$90 for a multiple-entry visa; amounts are adjusted or waived depending on passport nationality (Embassy of the Republic of Korea in the U.S. official notice, posted 2025-07-21)

✈ Coming for a Short Stay — K-ETA

  • K-ETA temporary exemption: January 1 to December 31, 2026 (K-ETA official notice, posted 2025-12-23). Guidance on exempt nationalities is on k-eta.go.kr.
  • If you have not obtained a K-ETA, you fill in an arrival card. The steps are in Pre-Departure Checklist, the Zero-Panic Manual — What to Finish Before You Reach the Airport
  • If you arrive for a short stay and then want to stay longer, see the "Changing status" line above

🌊 Busan — 3 Public Workation Spaces

Busan City runs "Busan-style workation", bundling lodging support, workspaces (hub centers, satellite centers, coworking spaces, book cafes and more) and tourism programs. The application and information desk is the official site busaness.com (Busan City official English page "Workation Destination").

  • Yeongdo Global Satellite Center — Lavals Hotel, Yeongdo-gu (Yeongdo / 영도), Busan. In operation since March 2025, a lounge-style workspace open 24 hours (Busan City official press release, 2025-08-07). According to the same release, in 2025 the city invited about 150 overseas digital nomads for 2-week stays in the "Busan Global Workation", held 4 times from August 18 to October 23.
  • Hue&Work Seo-gu Workation Center — Songdo Beach area (Songdo / 송도), Seo-gu, Busan. A public space specialized for workation, in operation since February 2026, used by participants of Busan-style workation programs. Busan City introduces it as the second public workspace after the "Mulmeong Lounge" (Mulmeong Lounge / 물멍 라운지) opened in 2025 (Busan City official press release, 2026-02-02). The address is Unit 301, Building B, 3rd floor, Ijin Bay City Shopping Area, 192 Songdohaebyeon-ro, Seo-gu, Busan; open weekdays 09:00–18:00, phone 051-621-0203, paid parking (Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-24))
  • Busan Workation Hub Center (부산워케이션거점센터) — 24th floor, Asti Hotel (Asti Hotel / 아스티호텔), 7-8 Jungang-daero 214beon-gil, Dong-gu, Busan (address is the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-24)). Weekdays 08:30–20:30; closed on weekends and public holidays. It has individual workspaces and corporate meeting spaces, and the ZOOM room can be used up to 3 hours per person. 5 minutes on foot from Busan Station and the subway station; no parking support. Eligible users are Busan-style workation participants (approved applicants). Phone 051-468-8995 (Busan workation official site www.busaness.com, 2026-11-24). Busan-style workation participants do a QR check-in when they visit the hub or satellite centers (Busan workation official site application notice, viewed 2026-11-24). Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" workation-theme public vote: 89 votes, 19th place
  • Who applies: The Busan City press release says companies and institutions apply through busaness.com. The site's introduction describes the target as "domestic and overseas companies capable of remote work, such as ICT, finance, culture and games", and it has member sign-up/login and a "Workation application" menu. Application inquiries 051-792-3311; the notice has an English guide attached and the site has an ENG menu at the top (Busan workation official site www.busaness.com, 2026-11-24)

🌿 Southern Coast — 2 Lodging-Type Workations

  • Garden Workation (Jeongwon Workation / 정원워케이션) — 152-55 Gukgajeongwon 1-ho-gil (Gukgajeongwon 1-ho-gil / 국가정원1호길), Suncheon-si (Suncheon / 순천), Jeonnam-Gwangju Special City (as written by VisitKorea). A glamping-style lodging inside Suncheon Bay National Garden with a workspace offering 360-degree garden views; it also runs guided garden tours, experience programs and short travel courses (VisitKorea, updated 2026-07-01). Booking is made on the Suncheon City "Suncheon Garden Workation" site. Payment must be made to the designated account within 24 hours of applying to complete the booking; otherwise it is cancelled. Check-in 16:00, check-out 11:00 the next day, inquiries 061-746-2963. The cabin house is an experience facility inside the National Garden, so eating and cooking are not allowed, and guests use a day pass (entry pass) to move between the National Garden and the garden workation (Suncheon City official site, cabin house application guide, 2026-11-24). Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" workation-theme public vote: 107 votes, 9th place
  • Badagongnyong Workation (Badagongnyong Workation / 바다공룡 워케이션) — 467-16 Daega-ro (Daega-ro / 대가로), Daega-myeon, Goseong-gun (Goseong / 고성), Gyeongnam. A village-type workation run under the name "Yolocation" by the resident-owned company Badagongnyong Co., Ltd. (Badagongnyong / 바다공룡), where you travel through a small village and join local community activities (VisitKorea). It accepts inquiries for custom corporate workations. Phone 0507-1381-4428, team@dinosea.com (official site yolocation.dinosea.com)

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

  1. "The nomad visa ends after 1 year" — After full operation it is extended 1 year at a time, up to 3 years (Ministry of Justice, in force from 2026-06-30)
  2. "The income threshold is one fixed amount in dollars" — It is 1 to 2 times the previous year's GNI per capita, depending on age and where you stay. Ages 18–34 staying outside the metropolitan area need 1x (₩52,410,000) (Ministry of Justice)
  3. "With this visa I can also take work from a Korean company" — Employment and for-profit activity in Korea are restricted (Ministry of Justice)
  4. "You must get it at your home-country embassy" — If you enter on a tourist visa (B-1, B-2, C-3) and meet the requirements, you can change status (Ministry of Justice)
