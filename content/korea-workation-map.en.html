Korea Workation Guide — Digital Nomad (F-1-D) Visa Requirements and 4 Workation Spaces (Busan Yeongdo, Busan Songdo, Suncheon Garden Workation, Goseong Badagongnyong) (Official Values, October 2026)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

You open your laptop and the garden unrolls all the way around the screen — that is the 360-degree garden-view workspace of "Garden Workation" (Jeongwon Workation / 정원워케이션), a lodging inside Suncheon Bay National Garden (VisitKorea). 🛂 Digital Nomad (Workation) Visa F-1-D

The Ministry of Justice, which ran this visa as a pilot from 2024, announced on 2026-07-07 that it will be in full operation from June 30, 2026 (Ministry of Justice official press release).

  • Who: Foreigners employed by an overseas company who can work remotely and have worked in the same field for at least 1 year, plus their family (spouse and minor children). Applicants must be 18 or older (accompanying children are exempt)
  • Where to apply: Korean embassies and consulates abroad
  • Income requirement: A multiple of the previous year's gross national income (GNI) per capita published by the Bank of Korea, as of the application date. The amounts based on 2025 GNI are below (Ministry of Justice press release attachment)
Age Seoul metropolitan area (excluding population-decline / watch areas) Non-metropolitan and population-decline (watch) areas
Age 18–34 1.5x GNI — ₩78,620,000 1x GNI — ₩52,410,000
Age 35 and over 2x GNI — ₩104,830,000 1.5x GNI — ₩78,620,000
  • If you come with family: 2x GNI in the metropolitan area regardless of age, 1.5x in non-metropolitan and population-decline (watch) areas (Ministry of Justice)
  • Insurance: Private medical insurance of at least ₩100,000,000 covering hospital treatment and repatriation for the whole stay (Ministry of Justice)
  • Documents: Visa application form, passport, 1 photo, fee, income proof such as an employment certificate, payslips and bank transaction records, criminal record certificate, proof of medical insurance, and proof of family relationship if accompanied. To use the non-metropolitan requirement you need a lease of at least 1 month or a booking confirmation from a workation-specialized facility or lodging (Ministry of Justice)
  • Length of stay: 1 year from the entry date at foreigner registration, extendable by 1 year at a time for a maximum of 3 years (Ministry of Justice)
  • What you cannot do: Employment and for-profit activity in Korea are restricted (Ministry of Justice)
  • Changing status: If you enter on a short-term tourist visa (B-1, B-2, C-3) and meet the requirements, you can switch to the Digital Nomad visa (Ministry of Justice)
  • During the pilot period (January 2024 to May 2026), 743 visas were issued. As of May 2026, 340 of the 398 foreigners registered under this visa (about 85%) stay in the metropolitan area (Ministry of Justice official press release). We could not confirm the processing time or fee for each embassy — ask the embassy you will apply to

✈ Coming for a Short Stay — K-ETA

  • K-ETA temporary exemption: January 1 to December 31, 2026 (K-ETA official notice, posted 2025-12-23). The notice has no list of eligible countries — scan your passport on the k-eta.go.kr application screen and it shows whether you are exempt
  • If you have not obtained a K-ETA, you fill in an arrival card. The steps are in Pre-Departure Checklist, the Zero-Panic Manual — What to Finish Before You Reach the Airport
  • If you arrive for a short stay and then want to stay longer, see the "Changing status" line above

🌊 Busan — 2 Public Workation Spaces

Busan City runs "Busan-style workation", bundling lodging support, workspaces (hub centers, satellite centers, coworking spaces, book cafes and more) and tourism programs. The application and information desk is the official site busaness.com (Busan City official English page "Workation Destination").

  • Yeongdo Global Satellite Center — Lavals Hotel, Yeongdo-gu (Yeongdo / 영도), Busan. In operation since March 2025, a lounge-style workspace open 24 hours (Busan City official press release, 2025-08-07). According to the same release, in 2025 the city invited about 150 overseas digital nomads for 2-week stays in the "Busan Global Workation", held 4 times from August 18 to October 23. We could not confirm the 2026 event schedule
  • Hue&Work Seo-gu Workation Center — Songdo Beach area (Songdo / 송도), Seo-gu, Busan. A public space specialized for workation, in operation since February 2026, used by participants of Busan-style workation programs. Busan City introduces it as the second public workspace after the "Mulmeong Lounge" (Mulmeong Lounge / 물멍 라운지) opened in 2025 (Busan City official press release, 2026-02-02). The detailed address, number of seats and opening hours are not in the press release, so we could not confirm them
  • Who applies: The Busan City press release says companies and institutions apply through busaness.com. We could not confirm the application procedure for individual freelancers (the site is rendered only by script, so we could not read its body)
  • The 24th-floor "hub center (50 seats)" of Asti Hotel (Asti Hotel / 아스티호텔) near Busan Station is confirmed only through 2023 press coverage [secondary]. We could not confirm whether it operates now

🌿 Southern Coast — 2 Lodging-Type Workations

  • Garden Workation (Jeongwon Workation / 정원워케이션) — 152-55 Gukgajeongwon 1-ho-gil (Gukgajeongwon 1-ho-gil / 국가정원1호길), Suncheon-si (Suncheon / 순천), Jeonnam-Gwangju Special City (as written by VisitKorea). A glamping-style lodging inside Suncheon Bay National Garden with a workspace offering 360-degree garden views; it also runs guided garden tours, experience programs and short travel courses (VisitKorea, updated 2026-07-01). Prices and booking route are not posted on VisitKorea — its homepage field links to the Suncheon City site
  • Badagongnyong Workation (Badagongnyong Workation / 바다공룡 워케이션) — 467-16 Daega-ro (Daega-ro / 대가로), Daega-myeon, Goseong-gun (Goseong / 고성), Gyeongnam. A village-type workation run under the name "Yolocation" by the resident-owned company Badagongnyong Co., Ltd. (Badagongnyong / 바다공룡), where you travel through a small village and join local community activities (VisitKorea). It accepts inquiries for custom corporate workations. Phone 0507-1381-4428, team@dinosea.com (official site yolocation.dinosea.com). Prices are not posted on the official site

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "The nomad visa ends after 1 year" — After full operation it is extended 1 year at a time, up to 3 years (Ministry of Justice, in force from 2026-06-30)

2. "The income threshold is one fixed amount in dollars" — It is 1 to 2 times the previous year's GNI per capita, depending on age and where you stay. Ages 18–34 staying outside the metropolitan area need 1x (₩52,410,000) (Ministry of Justice)

3. "With this visa I can also take work from a Korean company" — Employment and for-profit activity in Korea are restricted (Ministry of Justice)

4. "You must get it at your home-country embassy" — If you enter on a tourist visa (B-1, B-2, C-3) and meet the requirements, you can change status (Ministry of Justice)
