⚠ 3 Workation Mistakes

1. "Stay visa = tourist 90 days only" — not knowing the Digital Nomad Visa: People assume 90 days max. In reality, the Digital Nomad Visa (launched 2024, 1 year, monthly income $8,500+, for foreign freelancers) plus Working Holiday visas (Australia, Canada, etc.) allow a 1-year stay.

2. "Coworking = only in the US" — not knowing Korea's coworking cluster: People think US only. In reality, WeWork, FastFive, D.CAMP, SparkPlus, and Local Stitch (Korea's #1 cluster) in Gangnam, Seongsu, and Hongdae are foreigner-friendly with English support.

3. "Hotels only" — not using long-stay lodging: People book only hotels. In reality, long-stay hostels and guesthouses at ₩30,000~50,000/night (~$23~38) + Airbnb 1-month+ discounts + serviced residences (Gangnam, Itaewon) form the foreign-nomad cluster.
