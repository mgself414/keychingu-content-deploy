Why Jagalchi Market Now — Korea #1 Fisheries + Japanese Mania City

Jagalchi Market is Korea #1 Fish Market (Nampo-dong, Busan) + 7th floor modernized in 2006 + No. 1 Japanese tourist enthusiast (visit only for seafood). Fresh and cheap + lively atmosphere of street stalls + authentic Gyeongsang dialect = full set for foreign enthusiasts.

Seoul Market Series (#581 Namdaemun, #582 Plaza, #583 Dongdaemun, #585 Pocha, #587 Dawn) cluster expanded to Busan — Jagalchi = Orthodox fishery enthusiast + No. 1 Japanese tourist + Cost-effective raw fish enthusiast. 12-wire 4-axis matrix — Jagalchi full set

Axis 1: 1st floor raw fish and seafood store (3 lines) — Cost-effectiveness #1

#1. Jagalchi 1st floor raw fish store — Market #1 Signature

  • 📍 Location: Jagalchi, Jung-gu, Busan (Exit 7 of Jagalchi Station, Line 1)
  • 🕐 Opening: 02:00-22:00
  • 🚫 Closed: 1st and 3rd Tuesdays (regular)
  • Signature: White meat raw fish (flatfish, rockfish, sea bream, flounder) + red pepper paste, garlic, vegetables
  • Foreigner Infrastructure: Japanese Mania #1 (Japanese partially OK)
  • Foreigner tip: Purchase in store → Cook at the restaurant on the 2nd floor. Full set 30,000-60,000 won per person.

#2. Jagalchi shellfish and crustacean store

  • 📍 Location: Jagalchi 1st floor (near #1)
  • 🕐 Opening: 02:00-22:00
  • Signature: Abalone, clam, crab, shrimp + season limited
  • Foreigner tip: Shellfish enthusiasts + seasonal price changes.

#3. Jagalchi street stalls and instant tastings

  • 📍 Location: Street stalls around the Jagalchi building
  • 🕐 Open : 12:00-22:00 (variable)
  • Signature: Instant raw fish, grilled food, udon, soup + Authentic Busan
  • Foreigner tip: Japanese tourist vlog #1. Cost effective + authentic atmosphere.

Axis 2: 2nd floor restaurant (3 lines) — raw fish, grilled fish, spicy soup

#4. Jagalchi 2nd floor raw fish restaurant — photo menu

  • 📍 Location: Jagalchi Building 2nd floor (entire restaurant cluster)
  • 🕐 Open : 11:00-22:00
  • Signature: Photo menu + set menu (full set of raw fish, spicy stew, and noodles)
  • Foreigner Infrastructure: Photo menu = OK even if English is not good
  • Foreigner tip: No. 1 on first visit for foreigners. 25,000-50,000 won per person.

#5. Jagalchi spicy stew and seafood stew restaurant

  • 🕐 Open : 11:00-22:00
  • Signature: Spicy fish soup, seafood soup, crab soup
  • Foreigner tip: Mania of raw fish + spicy stew full set.

#6. Jagalchi instant grill restaurant

  • 🕐 Open : 11:00-22:00
  • Signature: Instant grilling (balone, shrimp, crab) + cost-effectiveness
  • Foreigner tip: Grilled food mania + Authentic Busan food.
