🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장) in Jung-gu (중구), Busan (부산), along with the places registered with it. It covers Jagalchi Market, where you buy and eat seafood, Yang-gopchang Alley (양곱창 골목), where beef tripe and intestines are grilled over briquette fires, a cruise boat around the southern harbor, a dried seafood wholesale market and an underground shopping center, and a jokbal (pig's feet, 족발) alley. Some places are closed on Tuesdays. Jagalchi Market is closed on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Tuesdays, and the underground shopping center on the 2nd and 4th Tuesdays. Gukje Market (국제시장), Bupyeong Kkangtong Market (부평깡통시장), Bosu-dong Book Alley (보수동책방골목) and BIFF Square (BIFF 광장) are in the #610 Busan Old Downtown Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Traditional Market — Busan Jagalchi Market

Busan Jagalchi Market (부산 자갈치시장) [Jagalchi-haean-ro 52 (자갈치해안로 52), Jung-gu (중구), Busan (부산), ☎ 051-713-8000] is described as one of Busan's representative traditional markets, along with Gukje Market (국제시장) and Bupyeong Kkangtong Market (부평깡통시장). It is divided into an outdoor traditional market and an indoor modern market. You can eat seafood such as hoe (raw fish, 회), grilled fish (생선구이) and maeuntang (spicy fish stew, 매운탕), and it sells marine products, dried seafood and shellfish.

  • Opening hours: 05:00~22:00. Closed days: 1st, 3rd and 5th Tuesdays of each month. Parking: Available.

📍 Where to Grill and Where to Ride — Jagalchi Yang-gopchang Alley and Jagalchi Cruise

Busan Jagalchi Yang-gopchang Alley (부산 자갈치 양곱창 골목) [Gudeok-ro 73 (구덕로 73), Jung-gu (중구), Busan (부산), ☎ Busan Jung-gu Tourist Information Center (부산 중구 관광안내소) 051-254-5504] is in the alley right in front of you when you come out of Exit 10 of Jagalchi Station (자갈치역) on the Busan Subway. Some shops hold several restaurants, so you choose a restaurant inside, and some shops have only one. The tradition of grilling yang-gopchang (beef tripe and intestines, 양곱창) over briquette fires continues, with salt-grilled (소금구이), seasoned-grilled (양념구이) and jeongol (hot pot, 전골) dishes. Business hours differ by restaurant, so it is described as better to check before you go.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by store. Fee: Free entry. Parking: Varies by store.

Jagalchi Cruise (자갈치 크루즈) [Jagalchi-haean-ro 60 (자갈치해안로 60), Jung-gu (중구), Busan (부산), ☎ 051-241-0909] is a cruise boat that circles the Busan southern harbor (남항). It is a boat with a capacity of 306 passengers: the 1st floor has the cabin and a performance hall, the 2nd floor has a snack bar, a lounge and a cabin, and the 3rd floor is a deck. It sails to Jagalchi Market, Songdo Amnam Park (송도 암남공원) and Taejongdae Park (태종대공원). It is described that if you depart at 14:00 you can see the Yeongdo Bridge (영도대교) being raised, and at 18:00 you can see the sunset.

  • Opening hours: 11:00~18:00. The closing time is registered as 20:00.
  • Departure times: Winter (November to March) 11:00, 14:00, 16:00 and 18:00. Summer (April to October) 11:00, 14:00, 16:00, 18:00 and 20:00.
  • Closed days: Closed Tuesdays in summer (per venue registration). No weekday closure is listed for winter (November to March). This varies with weather and season.
  • Fee: Adult 29,000 won, middle and high school students 20,000 won, children 12,000 won. Busan residents and seniors aged 65 and over 24,000 won, persons with disabilities (grades 1~3) 21,000 won.
  • What to bring: You must bring photo ID when boarding. Parking: Available.

📍 Dried Seafood and Underground Shopping — Nampo-dong Dried Seafood Wholesale Market and Nampo-dong Underground Shopping Center

Nampo-dong Dried Seafood Wholesale Market (남포동건어물도매시장) [Nampo-dong 1-ga 67 (남포동1가 67), Jung-gu (중구), Busan (부산), ☎ 051-254-3311] is a market specializing in dried seafood at the east end of Jagalchi Market. More than 200 shops sell dried seafood such as dried anchovies, dried squid, shrimp and dried fish strips (포), along with laver (김) and seaweed (미역). They sell not only to middle wholesalers but also to ordinary consumers.

  • Opening hours: 08:00~19:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Nampo-dong Underground Shopping Center (남포동 지하도상가) [Gudeok-ro Underground 44 (구덕로 지하 44), Jung-gu (중구), Busan (부산), ☎ 051-713-8200] is an underground shopping arcade. It is 517m long and 25~34m wide, and has heating and cooling. It sells women's clothing, men's clothing, casual wear, handicrafts and accessories.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~22:00. Closed days: 2nd and 4th Tuesdays of each month.
  • Parking: Available. Credit cards: Accepted.

📍 Jokbal Alley and Tourist Special Zone — Bupyeong Jokbal Alley and Yongdusan Jagalchi Tourist Special Zone

Bupyeong Jokbal Alley (부평족발골목) [Gwangbok-ro 12beon-gil 3 (광복로12번길 3), Jung-gu (중구), Busan (부산), ☎ Busan Jung-gu Office Tourist Information Center (부산 중구청 관광안내소) 051-254-5504] is an alley where jokbal restaurants continue from the Gukje Market intersection to the Bupyeong intersection. It started from one small shop in the 1980s and grew into an alley.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free entry. Parking: Not available.

Yongdusan Jagalchi Tourist Special Zone (용두산 자갈치 관광특구) [Jagalchi-haean-ro 52 (자갈치해안로 52), Jung-gu (중구), Busan (부산), ☎ Busan Jung-gu Office Tourist Information Center (부산 중구청 관광안내소) 051-254-5504] is a tourist area of Busan's old downtown that takes in the Yongdusan Park (용두산공원) observatory, Gwangbok-dong (광복동), Jagalchi Market, Gukje Market, the Bupyeong-dong Kkangtong Night Market (부평동 깡통 야시장), Yeongdo Bridge and Bosu-dong Book Alley (보수동 책방 골목). Street foods such as eomuk (fish cake, 어묵), ssiat-hotteok (seed-filled pancake, 씨앗호떡), bibim-dangmyeon (mixed glass noodles, 비빔당면), patjuk (red bean porridge, 팥죽) and patbingsu (red bean shaved ice, 팥빙수) are described.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Jagalchi Market is closed on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Tuesdays — the Nampo-dong Underground Shopping Center is closed on the 2nd and 4th Tuesdays.
  2. You need photo ID to board the Jagalchi Cruise — winter (November to March) departures are registered as 11:00, 14:00, 16:00 and 18:00.
  3. Business hours differ by restaurant in Yang-gopchang Alley — where a shop holds several restaurants, you choose one inside.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. What time does Jagalchi Market open?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, it opens 05:00~22:00 and is closed on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Tuesdays of each month.

Q. How much is the cruise fare?

A. The Jagalchi Cruise boarding fares are registered as adult 29,000 won, middle and high school students 20,000 won, children 12,000 won, Busan residents and seniors aged 65 and over 24,000 won, and persons with disabilities (grades 1~3) 21,000 won.
