Why an Art Museum Line by Subway — Stringing Scattered Art Spots into One Line

Seoul's art museums and museums are world-class — Leeum (리움미술관) (traditional + contemporary art), MMCA Seoul/National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art Seoul (국립현대미술관 서울관), the National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) (Pensive Bodhisattva), the Seoul Museum of Art/SeMA (서울시립미술관), the Amorepacific Museum of Art/APMA (아모레퍼시픽미술관), and DDP (동대문디자인플라자) (Zaha Hadid architecture + exhibitions). The problem is that they are scattered all over the city center. But every one of them is within walking distance of a subway station, so you can string them together in one day on a single T-money card.

This guide is the third axis, orthogonal to the line-by-line series (#1241–#1249) and the hub series (#1250–#1254). "Cross multiple lines and tie art into one thread" — it weaves together which venue to visit in which order, which station to get off at, and how to walk, all organized around the theme. This is the fourth installment of the Subway Course Series (Phase 2 · Themed Journeys).

6 Art Museums & Museums × Subway Access — Which Station to Get Off At

🖼 Leeum Museum of Art — The Pinnacle of Traditional + Contemporary

  • 🚇 Access: Line 6 Hangangjin Station (한강진역), Exit 1, 5-min walk (adjacent to Itaewon (이태원))
  • ✨ Highlights: Permanent collection of traditional art (celadon, white porcelain) + contemporary art · architecture by Mario Botta, Jean Nouvel, and Rem Koolhaas · check the reservation system
  • ⏱ Time needed: 1.5~2 hours
  • cross-ref: #1245 Line 6 (Itaewon · Hangangjin)

🏛 MMCA Seoul — Contemporary Art Next to Gyeongbokgung

  • 🚇 Access: Line 3 Anguk Station (안국역), Exit 1, 8-min walk · 10-min walk from Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역)
  • ✨ Highlights: Korea's flagship contemporary art venue · linked to the Samcheong (삼청) & Bukchon (북촌) gallery cluster (#908) · right next to Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁)
  • ⏱ Time needed: 1.5 hours
  • cross-ref: #1255 5 Grand Palaces (Gyeongbokgung) · #908 Bukchon galleries

🗿 National Museum of Korea — Pensive Bodhisattva · Free

  • 🚇 Access: Line 4 & Gyeongui-Jungang Line (경의중앙선), Ichon Station (이촌역), Exit 2, direct connecting passage
  • ✨ Highlights: The Pensive Bodhisattva (반가사유상) (Room of Quiet Contemplation) · permanent exhibition free · next to the National Hangeul Museum (한글박물관) · Yongsan Family Park (용산가족공원)
  • ⏱ Time needed: 2 hours+ (it's vast)
  • cross-ref: #1243 Line 4 (Ichon) · #1248 Gyeongui-Jungang

🎨 Seoul Museum of Art (SeMA) — Next to Deoksugung · Free Permanent Collection

  • 🚇 Access: Lines 1·2 City Hall Station (시청역), Exit 10, 5-min walk (Deoksugung (덕수궁) Stonewall Walkway)
  • ✨ Highlights: Permanent collection free · pairs with a stroll along the Deoksugung Stonewall Walkway · Jeongdong (정동) early-modern street
  • ⏱ Time needed: 1 hour
  • cross-ref: #1255 5 Grand Palaces (Deoksugung)

💄 Amorepacific Museum of Art (APMA) — Architecture + Contemporary Art

  • 🚇 Access: Line 4 Sinyongsan Station (신용산역), right at Exit 1 · 5-min walk from Line 1 Yongsan Station (용산역)
  • ✨ Highlights: David Chipperfield architecture (the company headquarters building) · contemporary art special exhibitions · links to Yongnidan-gil (용리단길)
  • ⏱ Time needed: 1~1.5 hours
  • cross-ref: #1243 Line 4 (Sinyongsan) · #1251 Gongdeok Hub (toward Yongsan)

🏗 DDP Dongdaemun Design Plaza — Zaha Hadid · Design Exhibitions

  • 🚇 Access: Lines 2·4·5 Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station (동대문역사문화공원역), connected via Exit 1
  • ✨ Highlights: Zaha Hadid's curved architecture is an exhibit in itself · design & media art exhibitions · LED roses at night (#1252)
  • ⏱ Time needed: 1~1.5 hours
  • cross-ref: #1252 DDP Hub

🎯 Universal Art Tour Tips — Before You Go

  • 🎫 Free venues first: Permanent exhibitions at the National Museum of Korea and SeMA are free. Leeum and special exhibitions are paid — check the reservation system.
  • 🚫 Beware Monday closures: Many art museums and museums close on Mondays (varies by venue) — confirm before attempting a full traverse.
  • 📸 Photography rules: Permanent exhibitions often allow photos, but special exhibitions frequently prohibit them — check the signage.
  • 💡 Routing tip: Group by zone — the north (MMCA · SeMA = palace pairing), Yongsan (National Museum of Korea · APMA = Line 4 axis), Itaewon (Leeum), and DDP.

3 Subway Traverse Courses on the Art Museum Line

Course 1. Palaces + Art (North, Half Day)

  • Anguk MMCA Seoul (+ Samcheong galleries, #908) → Line 3 → City Hall SeMA (+ Deoksugung Stonewall Walkway, #1255)
  • fit: A combo of palaces, galleries, and early-modern streets. High share of free admission.

Course 2. Yongsan Art Axis (Line 4)

  • Ichon: National Museum of Korea (Pensive Bodhisattva · free) → Line 4 Sinyongsan: APMA (+ Yongnidan-gil for dinner)
  • fit: Two stops on Line 4 — an art day with minimal transit.

Course 3. Contemporary Art & Architecture (East)

  • Hangangjin: Leeum (reserve) → Line 6 → DDP (Hadid architecture · LED roses at night, #1252)
  • fit: For contemporary art and architecture enthusiasts. Perfect nighttime finish.

5 Transfer & Usage Tips — Get 200% Out of the Art Museum Line

Tip 1. Every Venue Sits on a Different Line

Leeum = Line 6, MMCA = Line 3, National Museum of Korea = Line 4 & Gyeongui-Jungang, SeMA = Lines 1·2, APMA = Line 4, DDP = Lines 2·4·5. Group by zone.

Tip 2. Mix Free and Paid

Free permanent collections at the National Museum of Korea and SeMA + paid Leeum and special exhibitions — build a combo that fits your budget.

Tip 3. Save Mondays for the Palaces

Many art museums close on Mondays — switch your Monday course to the 5 Grand Palaces (#1255) or the markets (#1257) (palaces have their own closed days too — check).

Tip 4. Check Reservation Systems

Popular venues like Leeum use timed-entry reservations — booking online before your visit is the safe move.

Tip 5. One Transit Card for Everything

A single T-money card covers every line to all 6 venues. Travel between zones takes 15~25 min.

FAQ 6 — Art Museum Line Questions Foreign Visitors Ask Most

Q1. How many venues per day is reasonable?

  • 2~3 venues. With a large venue (National Museum of Korea) included, 2. Group by zone (north / Yongsan / east).

Q2. How is this different from the line-by-line and hub editions?

  • Line-by-line = "one line, vertical," hub = "one station, radial," this edition = "crossing multiple lines to string an art theme" = the third axis.

Q3. Which ones can I see for free?

  • Permanent collections at the National Museum of Korea and the Seoul Museum of Art are free. DDP's exterior and some exhibitions are free too.

Q4. What is the Pensive Bodhisattva?

  • The National Museum of Korea's signature piece — on permanent display in its dedicated Room of Quiet Contemplation (free).

Q5. Do I need reservations?

  • For Leeum, checking the reservation system is essential, and special exhibitions often use timed entry. Free permanent-collection venues generally allow walk-ins.

Q6. Can I visit galleries too?

  • The Samcheong & Bukchon gallery cluster (#908) around MMCA is the best free gallery tour.
