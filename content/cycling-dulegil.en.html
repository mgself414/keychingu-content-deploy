⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Korean Bicycle = Han River Only": Korean Bicycle = Hangang 287km + Four Major Rivers 1853km (Nakdong River, Geumgang, Yeongsan River) + Seoul Dulle-gil 156km + Jeju 240km + East Sea Line, West Sea Line Full set.

2. "Ttareungi = Foreigners can't rent": Ttareungi = English app OK for foreigners + Payment by credit card·KakaoPay·Naver Pay + 1,000 won/1 hour No. 1 cost-effectiveness.

3. "Four Major Rivers = Too Difficult": Four Major Rivers Bicycle Path = Certification Center + Countryside Pass + Foreign Mania 1 Night 2 Days~1 Week Full Set + Foreign Mania No. 1.

4. "Seoul Dulle-gil = Hiking": Seoul Dulle-gil 156km = 8 various courses, city center, nature, and flat areas + Foreign enthusiasts walking and biking OK.

5. "Outskirts bike = can't ride": Jeju Fantastic Bicycle Road 240km·Nakdong River 385km = No. 1 outskirts for foreign enthusiasts + English guide/rental OK.
