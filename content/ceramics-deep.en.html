Korean Ceramics Travel Guide — Icheon Yespark and Yeoju Ceramic World, Gangjin and Buan Celadon Museums, Mungyeong Ceramics Museum and Tea Bowl Festival, Gimhae Buncheong, Yanggu Baekja

Subtitle: Icheon Ceramic Art Village (이천도자예술마을, open at all times) · Yeoju Ceramic World (여주도자세상, closed Mondays) · Gangjin Goryeo Celadon Museum (강진 고려청자박물관, adults 2,000 won) · Buan Celadon Museum (부안청자박물관, adults 3,000 won) · Mungyeong Ceramics Museum (문경도자기박물관, free, one-day workshop 15,000 won) · Gimhae Buncheong Ceramics Museum (김해분청도자박물관, free) · Yanggu Baekja Museum (양구백자박물관, 3,000 won, wheel workshop 20,000 won) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers 7 regional museums and villages plus 2 festivals where you can see, buy and make Korean ceramics by hand. Icheon Sagimakgol Pottery Village (이천 사기막골 도예촌) and the Icheon Ceramics Festival are covered separately in #724 Icheon Sagimakgol Pottery Village Guide, and the Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum and Bunwon Baekja Archive in Gwangju, Gyeonggi are covered in #718 Gwangju Royal Ceramics Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Most museums are closed on Mondays, so check the table first.

📍 Gyeonggi — Icheon Ceramic Art Village and Yeoju Ceramic World

Icheon Ceramic Art Village (Icheon Yespark) (109 Doja-yesul-ro 5beon-gil, Sindun-myeon, Icheon-si (이천시 신둔면 도자예술로5번길 109), ☎ 0507-1461-1996) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, a village where each studio has its own character, so you can look around and buy what you like. Some studios offer ceramic-making programs.

  • Opening hours: Open at all times. Closed days: Open year-round (varies by shop).
  • Workshop: Ceramic-making workshop (ages 5 and up).
  • Parking: Available (about 40 spaces).

Yeoju Ceramic World (Yeoju Dojasesang) (7 Silleuksa-gil, Cheonsong-dong, Yeoju-si (여주시 신륵사길 7, 천송동), ☎ 031-884-8644) is inside Silleuksa National Tourist Site (신륵사 국민관광지) and is a ceramics exhibition and sales cultural complex run by the Korea Ceramic Foundation (한국도자재단). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it consists of the Gyeonggi Living Ceramics Museum (경기생활도자미술관), a ceramics shopping mall and a Craft Creation Support Center (공예창작지원센터). The mall has an art shop selling small gift items, a living shop selling mid- and low-priced everyday ceramics, a gallery shop selling handmade high-end everyday ceramics, and brand shops. The Gyeonggi International Ceramic Biennale (경기세계도자비엔날레) is held here, and workshop programs run at Bandal Space (반달스페이스).

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:00).
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok Day.
  • Parking: Available.

📍 Celadon — Gangjin and Buan

The Gangjin Goryeo Celadon Museum (33 Cheongjachon-gil, Daegu-myeon, Gangjin-gun (강진군 대구면 청자촌길 33), ☎ 061-430-3755) lets you, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, listen to explanations of the exhibited relics with an audio guide and a smart pad, and it has a celadon-making workshop hall where you shape clay by hand.

  • Opening hours: Goryeo Celadon Museum 09:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30). Digital Museum 10:00~17:00, Celadon-Making Workshop Hall 09:00~18:00, Celadon Sales Hall 09:00~17:30 (all three have preparation time 12:00~13:00).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (if Monday is a public holiday it is open, and the museum is closed on the first weekday after). The Celadon-Making Workshop Hall and the Celadon Sales Hall are open year-round.
  • Admission: Adults (ages 19~64) 2,000 won, teenagers (ages 13~18) and soldiers ranked staff sergeant (하사) or below 1,500 won, children (ages 7~12) 1,000 won.
  • Parking: Free.

The Buan Celadon Museum (1493 Cheongja-ro, Boan-myeon, Buan-gun (부안군 보안면 청자로 1493), ☎ 063-580-3964) opened in April 2011 on the site of Yucheon Elementary School (유천초등학교). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it has an exhibition hall with authentic relics, a ceramic-making workshop hall, and an outdoor historic park where you can see the kiln sites that fired Goryeo celadon.

  • Opening hours: March~October 10:00~18:00 (ticket sales close 17:30), November~February 10:00~17:00 (ticket sales close 16:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, Seollal and Chuseok Day.
  • Admission: Adults 3,000 won, teenagers and soldiers and police 2,000 won, children 1,000 won.
  • Parking: Free.

📍 Mungyeong — Ceramics Museum and Tea Bowl Festival

The Mungyeong Ceramics Museum (2416 Mungyeong-daero, Mungyeong-eup, Mungyeong-si (문경시 문경읍 문경대로 2416), ☎ 054-550-6416) was built, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, to tell the history of Mungyeong ceramics, known as a kiln site for early Joseon buncheong ware and white porcelain, and it exhibits earthenware, celadon, white porcelain, and modern and contemporary ceramics. Behind the building are the Mungyeong traditional kiln, the mangdaengi kiln (망댕이가마), and an outdoor workshop.

  • Opening hours: March~October 09:00~18:00, November~February 09:00~17:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Admission: Free.
  • One-day workshop: 15,000 won for individuals (materials and firing fee included, delivery fee extra). 50% off on the last Wednesday of every month (Culture Day).

Mungyeong Tea Bowl Festival — 28th Edition, 2026

The Mungyeong Tea Bowl Festival (문경찻사발축제) was held in 2026 as its 28th edition on May 1~10 in and around Mungyeongsaejae Provincial Park (문경새재도립공원) (☎ 054-571-7677). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, Mungyeong ceramics are fired in a traditional wood-fired kiln (mangdaengi kiln) at over 1,300°C.

  • Hours: 09:00~18:00.
  • Admission: Free (workshop fees extra).
  • Programs: According to the information registered with the Korea Tourism Organization, they included an international artists exchange exhibition, a masterpiece ceramics exhibition, a tea bowl competition, tea bowl making, a tea ceremony experience, a traditional foot-wheel contest and more.

📍 Gimhae — Buncheong Ceramics Museum and Buncheong Ceramics Festival

The Gimhae Buncheong Ceramics Museum (17 Buncheong-ro, Jillye-myeon, Gimhae-si (김해시 진례면 분청로 17), ☎ 055-345-6037) opened, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, to carry on the tradition of Gimhae ceramics from the Three Kingdoms to the Joseon period and to establish Gimhae as the home of buncheong ceramics. It holds 3 to 4 special exhibitions a year and runs a permanent ceramic workshop program.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, Seollal and Chuseok Day.
  • Admission: Free.

Gimhae Buncheong Ceramics Festival — 31st Edition, 2026

The Gimhae Buncheong Ceramics Festival (김해분청도자기축제) was held in 2026 as its 31st edition on November 6~10 at Lotte Garden Park Gimhae (김해 롯데가든파크) (551 Jangyu-ro, Sinmun-dong (장유로 551, 신문동)) (☎ 055-330-3244).

  • Hours: 10:00~20:00, Monday and Tuesday 10:00~18:00.
  • Fee: It was listed as "paid" in the information registered with the Korea Tourism Organization, together with a 20% discount on all ceramic items.
  • Programs: According to the information registered with the Korea Tourism Organization, they included a Buncheong History Theme Hall, a Ceramic Master Hall (wheel demonstrations), ceramic sales by local potters, buncheong wheel workshops, coiling workshops and mug decorating, a public ceramics auction and more.

📍 Yanggu — Yanggu Baekja Museum

The Yanggu Baekja Museum (5182 Pyeonghwa-ro, Bangsan-myeon, Yanggu-gun (양구군 방산면 평화로 5182), ☎ 033-480-7238) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, a place that documents the 600-year history of white porcelain production in the Yanggu area and researches and exhibits Yanggu white clay. A surface survey of the Yanggu area confirmed 40 kiln sites. The workshop room has electric, gas and wood-fired kilns, so you can experience everything from preparing the clay to shaping and kiln firing.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the first weekday after if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, the mornings of Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Admission: 3,000 won (free for ages 7 and under, ages 65 and over, and others).
  • Workshop fee: General workshop for individuals 15,000 won, wheel workshop for individuals 20,000 won (materials and firing fee included, delivery fee paid on receipt).

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Most museums are closed on Mondays — Yeoju, Gangjin, Buan, Mungyeong, Gimhae and Yanggu are all registered as closed every Monday. Of these, Gangjin's Celadon-Making Workshop Hall and Celadon Sales Hall are open year-round.
  2. Some places send your workshop piece by delivery — the one-day workshop at the Mungyeong Ceramics Museum is registered with the delivery fee extra, and the Yanggu Baekja Museum workshop with the delivery fee paid on receipt.
  3. Buan and Mungyeong close early in winter — from November to February the Buan Celadon Museum is open until 17:00 and the Mungyeong Ceramics Museum until 17:00.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where can I make ceramics myself?

A. The places with workshops in the Korea Tourism Organization registered information are Icheon Ceramic Art Village (some studios), Yeoju Ceramic World (Bandal Space workshop program), Gangjin Goryeo Celadon Museum (Celadon-Making Workshop Hall), Buan Celadon Museum (workshop hall), Mungyeong Ceramics Museum (one-day workshop 15,000 won), Gimhae Buncheong Ceramics Museum (permanent workshop) and Yanggu Baekja Museum (general workshop 15,000 won, wheel workshop 20,000 won).

Q. Which places have no admission fee?

A. The Mungyeong Ceramics Museum and the Gimhae Buncheong Ceramics Museum are registered as free. Icheon Ceramic Art Village and Yeoju Ceramic World have no admission item in the Korea Tourism Organization registered information.
