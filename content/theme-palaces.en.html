Why the Five Grand Palaces by Subway — Threading a Scattered Joseon Dynasty into One Line

Seoul's Five Grand Palaces are the heart of the 500-year Joseon dynasty — Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) (the main palace) · Changdeokgung (창덕궁) (a UNESCO World Heritage Site) · Changgyeonggung (창경궁) (sharing a single wall with Changdeokgung) · Deoksugung (덕수궁) (the modern era · Seokjojeon (석조전)) · Gyeonghuigung (경희궁) (the West Palace). The catch is that they are scattered across the northern part of the city center. But every one of them is within a 5-minute walk of a subway station, so you can tour them all in a single day on one T-money card.

This guide is a third axis, orthogonal to the line-by-line editions (#1241~#1249) and the hub editions (#1250~#1254). "Cross several lines to thread the Five Grand Palaces into one" — it weaves together which palace in which order, at which station you get off, and how you walk, all around a theme. It is the first installment of the Subway Course Series (Phase 2 · Theme Journey).

The Five Grand Palaces × Subway Access — Which Station Do You Get Off At

🏯 Gyeongbokgung — Main Palace · Changing of the Guard

  • 🚇 Access: Line 3 Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역), right at Exit 5 · Line 5 Gwanghwamun Station (광화문역), 10-min walk
  • ✨ Highlights: Geunjeongjeon (근정전) · Gyeonghoeru (경회루) · Hyangwonjeong (향원정) · Changing of the Guard (at Gwanghwamun (광화문), twice daily) · adjacent to the National Palace Museum (국립고궁박물관) and the National Folk Museum (국립민속박물관)
  • ⏱ Duration: 1.5~2 hours (large)
  • cross-ref: #37 Gyeongbokgung guide · #1242 Line 3

🌳 Changdeokgung + Changgyeonggung — Huwon (후원) Garden · One Shared Wall

  • 🚇 Access: Line 3 Anguk Station (안국역), 5-min walk from Exit 3 · the two palaces are connected by a shared wall
  • ✨ Highlights: Changdeokgung's Huwon (a.k.a. Biwon (비원), UNESCO · timed entry) · Injeongjeon (인정전) · Changgyeonggung (the Daeonsil (대온실) greenhouse · Chundangji (춘당지) pond) · night openings (Moonlight Tour (달빛기행) season)
  • ⏱ Duration: Changdeokgung 1.5 hours (2 hours including Huwon) · Changgyeonggung 1 hour
  • cross-ref: #18 Bukchon (북촌) · #1254 Jongno 3-ga (종로3가) hub

🏛 Deoksugung — Modern Era · Seokjojeon · Doldam-gil (돌담길, Stone-Wall Road)

  • 🚇 Access: Lines 1·2 City Hall Station (시청역), right at Exit 2 · Line 5 Gwanghwamun Station, 8-min walk
  • ✨ Highlights: Seokjojeon (Western-style modern architecture) · Junghwajeon (중화전) · a stroll along the Deoksugung Doldam-gil (Stone-Wall Road) · Changing of the Guard · the Jeongdong (정동) modern-history street
  • ⏱ Duration: 1~1.5 hours
  • cross-ref: #40 Namsan (남산) · Myeong-dong (명동) night view (adjacent downtown)

🍂 Gyeonghuigung — Free · The Quiet West Palace

  • 🚇 Access: Line 5 Seodaemun Station (서대문역) / Gwanghwamun Station, 7-min walk
  • ✨ Highlights: Free admission (the only one of the Five Grand Palaces) · Sungjeongjeon (숭정전) · adjacent to the Seoul Museum of History (서울역사박물관) · a healing palace with few crowds
  • ⏱ Duration: 40 min~1 hour
  • cross-ref: #284 free-admission spots

🎯 Combined-Use Tips for the Five Grand Palaces — Before You Set Out

  • 🎫 Integrated admission ticket: A combined ticket for the four major palaces (Gyeongbokgung · Changdeokgung + Huwon · Changgyeonggung · Deoksugung) + Jongmyo (종묘) Shrine (about 10,000 KRW · valid 3 months) — better value than buying individually. Confirm the price and composition officially.
  • 👘 Hanbok free admission: Wear hanbok and enter the four major palaces free. Plenty of hanbok rental shops near Anguk and Gyeongbokgung (#379 · #33).
  • 🚫 Mind the closing days: Each palace has a different closing day (Gyeongbokgung differs from Changdeokgung/Changgyeonggung/Deoksugung). Before a full-day traverse, checking each palace's closing day is essential.
  • 💡 Route tip: Line 3 (Gyeongbokgung · Anguk) covers 3 of the Five Grand Palaces (Gyeongbokgung · Changdeokgung · Changgyeonggung) — build around the Line 3 axis and only move for City Hall (Deoksugung) and Seodaemun (Gyeonghuigung).

Three Subway Traverse Courses for the Five Grand Palaces

Course 1. Half-Day Essentials (2 Palaces · Line 3)

  • Gyeongbokgung Station (Gyeongbokgung · Changing of the Guard) → Line 3 Anguk Station (Changdeokgung · Huwon) → lunch in Ikseon-dong (익선동) (#1254)
  • fit: Short on time · essentials only. Main palace + World Heritage.

Course 2. Full-Day Traverse (4 Palaces · Hanbok)

  • Gyeongbokgung (morning · Changing of the Guard) → Anguk: Changdeokgung + Changgyeonggung (Huwon) → City Hall: Deoksugung (Seokjojeon · Doldam-gil) → the Jeongdong modern-history street
  • fit: A full day in hanbok. Get your money's worth from the integrated ticket. Traverse the Joseon dynasty.

Course 3. Quiet Palaces · Modern Era (Gyeonghuigung · Deoksugung)

  • Seodaemun: Gyeonghuigung (free · calm) → Seoul Museum of History → City Hall: Deoksugung (Seokjojeon · Doldam-gil) → Jeongdong
  • fit: Healing with few crowds · modern architecture. Avoid the crowded palaces.

Five Transfer & Usage Tips — Getting 200% Out of the Five-Palace Subway Traverse

Tip 1. Anchor on Line 3

Gyeongbokgung and Anguk (Changdeokgung · Changgyeonggung) are on Line 3. Three of the Five Grand Palaces sit on a single Line 3 stretch. Just attach the rest — Deoksugung (City Hall) and Gyeonghuigung (Seodaemun).

Tip 2. Wear Hanbok, Enter Free

Wear hanbok and the four major palaces are free. Rent one near Anguk or Gyeongbokgung and wear it all day (#379 · #33).

Tip 3. Bundle It with the Integrated Ticket

The four-palace + Jongmyo combined ticket (about 10,000 KRW) beats buying individually. Essential for a full-day traverse. Confirm the price officially.

Tip 4. Time the Changing of the Guard

The changing-of-the-guard ceremony at Gyeongbokgung and Deoksugung runs about twice a day. Arrive on schedule for a free spectacle.

Tip 5. Check Closing Days First

Each palace has a different closing day, so on a full-day traverse one of them might be shut. Confirming officially with each palace before you set out is essential.

FAQ 6 — Common Foreigner Questions on the Five-Palace Subway Traverse

Q1. Can you see all Five Grand Palaces in one day?

  • The four core palaces are doable in a day (Gyeongbokgung · Changdeokgung · Changgyeonggung · Deoksugung). Adding Changdeokgung's Huwon makes it tight — a half-day of 2 palaces or a full day of 4 palaces is recommended.

Q2. How does this differ from the line-by-line and hub editions?

  • Line-by-line = "one line, read vertically," hub = "radiating from one station," and this edition is "crossing several lines to thread the Five Grand Palaces by theme" = the third axis.

Q3. How do you connect them by subway?

  • Line 3 covers three: Gyeongbokgung and Anguk (Changdeokgung · Changgyeonggung). Just add Deoksugung (Lines 1·2, City Hall) and Gyeonghuigung (Line 5, Seodaemun). One T-money card.

Q4. Is it really free if you wear hanbok?

  • Yes. Wear hanbok and the four major palaces are free admission. Plenty of rental shops near Anguk and Gyeongbokgung (#379).

Q5. Is the integrated ticket worth it?

  • The four-palace + Jongmyo combined ticket (about 10,000 KRW) is cheaper than buying individually. Essential for a full-day traverse. Confirm the price officially.

Q6. Which palace is less crowded?

  • Gyeonghuigung (free · calm) and Changgyeonggung are relatively quiet. Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung are the most crowded.
