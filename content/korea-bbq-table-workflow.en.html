Korean BBQ Table Workflow — From Seating to Payment in 7 Steps

Subtitle: For Foreign Tourists: Staff-Grilled vs. Self-Grilled BBQ + Ssam, Banchan, Additional Orders + Payment Tips

ISSUE 03 · #805 · 2026-06-07

🎯 Why a Korean BBQ Table Workflow Guide?

Foreigners' Korean BBQ pain point #2 = "I've ordered, what's next?". Even if you know #804 Cuts & Grades, understanding the table progression workflow requires separate learning. Korean BBQ comes in three types: (a) staff-grilled restaurants, (b) self-grilled restaurants, and (c) semi-automatic (staff only cuts). Foreigners often (1) miss the grilling timing and burn the meat, (2) don't know how to order more and can't call the staff, (3) are unfamiliar with the ssam, banchan, and after-meal systems, or (4) get confused by service charges and taxes at the payment stage. This guide provides a 7-step workflow + differences by restaurant type + foreigner-friendly tips.

★ 7-Step Workflow at a Glance

Step Action Time Key Points for Tourists
1️⃣ Getting Seated Entry + Headcount check + Table assignment 1~5 min For 4+ people, choose between traditional floor seating (jwajik) or chairs. Requesting a well-ventilated spot is OK.
2️⃣ Menu Selection Menu + Deciding on cuts, servings (inbun), and sets 5~10 min 1.5-2 servings (inbun, Korean serving unit) per person is standard. For first-timers, 2 types of cuts + 1 marinated option recommended.
3️⃣ Grilling Staff-grilled / Self-grilled / Semi-automatic Varies by cut Staff-grilled = Grill master / Self-grilled = More freedom / Semi-automatic = Staff only cuts
4️⃣ Ssam + Banchan Ssam vegetables, garlic, chili, 5-10 types of banchan Unlimited All banchan (free Korean side dishes) = Free refills. "반찬 좀 더 주세요" (Banchan jom deo juseyo) = "Banchan more, please".
5️⃣ Additional Order Add more meat + Alcohol/Drinks + After-meal 5~10 min Ring the staff bell OR call out "여기요!" (yeogiyo, excuse me). Additional menu items = Charged separately.
6️⃣ After-meal (Meal) Naengmyeon, Doenjang-jjigae, Bokkeumbap, Gonggibap 10~15 min Korean style = The end of a hoesik (Korean company dinner/gathering) = Meal course. ₩5,000-10,000 (~$3.7-7.4 / ~26-52 RMB / ~500-1,000 JPY) per person.
7️⃣ Payment Card OR Cash + Receipt 3~5 min Mostly counter payment / Some table payment / Bongsa-ryo (service charge) usually not applied outside hotels.

Total Time (for 2 people): 1~1.5h (budget-friendly restaurants) ~ 2~3h (upscale/hoesik restaurants)

🔥 Grilling System Comparison by Restaurant Type

Type A — Staff-Grilled (Most Tourist-Friendly)

  • Features: Staff grills and cuts from start to finish. #1 recommendation for foreigners.
  • Recommended Restaurants (Seoul): Gangnam (강남), Cheongdam (청담), Lotte World (롯데월드), Shilla Hotel (신라호텔) Japanese/Korean restaurants
  • Pros: No grilling failure / English guidance available / Automatic grilling time for each cut
  • Cons: Many upscale restaurants / May have a separate grill master fee of ₩3,000-5,000 per person or ₩5,000-10,000 per table.
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★★

Type B — Self-Grilled (Budget-Friendly)

  • Features: You grill yourself. You also cut the meat yourself.
  • Recommended Restaurants (Seoul): Hongdae (홍대), Sinchon (신촌), Gangnam (강남) budget-friendly BBQ / alley nofo (30+ year traditional restaurant)
  • Pros: Lower price (₩25,000-50,000 per person) / More freedom / Local atmosphere
  • Cons: Risk of grilling failure (especially chicken/pork) / Requires cutting skills
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★ (Many places don't have English menus)

Type C — Semi-Automatic (Staff only cuts)

  • Features: You grill, and staff cuts the meat with scissors. A hybrid type.
  • Recommended Restaurants: Uijeongbu (의정부), Gildong (길동) jeongyuk-sikdang (meat restaurants, often with a butcher shop) / Many general BBQ specialty restaurants
  • Pros: Safe cutting + Grilling freedom / Excellent value for money
  • Cons: Grilling is your responsibility
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★

For first-time visitors = Type A or C first (Type B is recommended after you get familiar with Korean BBQ).

🍃 Ssam + Banchan System (Banchan System)

Free Basic Offerings (Free Refill)

  • Ssamchaeso (쌈채소, vegetables for wraps): Lettuce (sangchu), Perilla (kkaennip), Chicory, Bok choy
  • Garlic, Chili, Ssamjang (쌈장, spicy dipping sauce for wraps)
  • 3 types of Kimchi: Cabbage Kimchi (baechu-kimchi), Radish Kimchi (kkakdugi), Young Radish Kimchi (yeolmu-kimchi)
  • 5-7 types of banchan: Spinach namul (seasoned vegetable dish), Bean sprout namul, Japchae (glass noodles stir-fried with vegetables), Dubu-jorim (braised tofu), etc.

How to Eat? — Making Ssam

1. Spread one ssamchaeso leaf on your palm.

2. Place 1-2 pieces of well-grilled meat.

3. Add 1-2 pieces of garlic + 1/2 teaspoon of ssamjang.

4. Add a little kimchi/chili + rice (optional).

5. Eat in one bite (Korean standard = whole bite).

Common mistake for foreigners: Not being able to fit the whole ssam in their mouth and taking half-bites. Korean recommendation = one whole bite (taking half-bites might look awkward, but it's not a violation). It's recommended to make smaller ssam to fit in one bite.

Additional Ordering System

  • "반찬 좀 더 주세요" (Banchan jom deo juseyo) — Free unlimited refills
  • "고기 추가요" (Gogi chugayo) — Charged separately
  • "공기밥 하나요" (Gonggibap hanayo) — ₩1,000-2,000
  • "이거 새 거 주세요" (Igeo sae geo juseyo) — OK if you burn the meat at a self-grilled restaurant (many places offer this service).
  • "따로 계산할 수 있어요?" (Ttaro gyesanhal su isseoyo?) — Separate payment (policy varies by restaurant).
  • "환기 좀 더 강하게 해주세요" (Hwangi jom deo ganghage haejuseyo) — You can request stronger ventilation from the staff.

🍷 Drink & Alcohol Pairing

Korean BBQ Alcohol Matrix

Alcohol Type Price (Bottle/Glass) Pairing
Soju (소주, Korean distilled spirit) ₩4,000-6,000 OK with all cuts. Hoesik (company dinner) staple. Alcohol 16-17%.
Beer ₩4,000-8,000 Refreshing, light. Excellent with spicy marinated dishes.
Somaek (소맥, soju + beer mix) Restaurant price #1 choice for Korean hoesik. Popular with foreigners. Ratio 1:3 or 1:2.5 (1:2 is too much soju).
Makgeolli (막걸리, Korean rice wine) ₩5,000-8,000 Traditional, sweet. Excellent on rainy days + with spicy dakgalbi.
Highball ₩8,000-12,000 Japanese-style/upscale BBQ. Smooth + citrusy.
Wine ₩30,000-100,000 Hanwoo 1++ (premium Korean beef) / Luxury BBQ. Medium-bodied red.

Pairing Recommendations

  • Samgyeopsal (삼겹살, pork belly) + Soju = Korean BBQ Classic
  • Chadolbagi (차돌박이, beef brisket) + Beer = Light pairing
  • Hanwoo Kkotdeungsim (한우 꽃등심, premium Korean beef ribeye) + Wine = Luxury pairing
  • Dakgalbi (닭갈비, spicy stir-fried chicken) + Makgeolli = Spicy flavor soothing

Foreigner's Note: 1 bottle of soju contains alcohol equivalent to 1-1.5 glasses of wine. More than 2 bottles per person can be risky.

⚠ 7 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Menu says 1 serving (inbun) = enough for 1 person": Usually, 1 serving (inbun) = 100-150g. 1.5-2 servings per person is the standard amount.

2. "Banchan = 1-time serving": Unlimited free refills. Call out "반찬 좀 더 주세요" (Banchan jom deo juseyo).

3. "Ssam = Cut and eat": Korean recommendation = one whole bite. Taking half-bites might look awkward (but it's not a violation). It's recommended to make smaller ssam.

4. "Staff = Automatic call": In Korean restaurants, you need to call the staff yourself. Ring the bell OR say "여기요!" (yeogiyo, excuse me).

5. "Meat = Main and that's it": Korean hoesik (company dinner) = Meat → After-meal (naengmyeon, stew, bokkeumbap) course. After-meal items are ordered separately.

6. "Card payment = At the table": Varies by restaurant. Mostly counter payment (Korean style). Some places allow table card payment.

7. "Grilling failure = Just eat it": If you burn the meat too much at a self-grilled restaurant, you can ask the staff "이거 새 거 주세요" (Igeo sae geo juseyo, can I get a new one?) (many places offer this service).

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. First BBQ = Which restaurant type?: Type A (Staff-Grilled) is the #1 recommendation. Gangnam (강남), Cheongdam (청담), Shilla Hotel (신라호텔) Japanese BBQ are recommended.

2. Is solo BBQ possible?: Possible at some restaurants (honbap (eating alone) / 1-person BBQ specialty restaurants). Generally, a minimum of 2 people is recommended.

3. Safe grilling times: Pork well-done (3-5 min per side) / Beef medium (2-3 min) / Chicken well-done (5-7 min on hot plate).

4. Lunch vs. Dinner: Jeomsim teukseon (점심 특선, lunch course) = 30-50% discount. First round of hoesik (company dinner) is dinner.

5. English-friendly restaurant recommendations: Gangnam (강남), Itaewon (이태원), Hongdae (홍대), Cheongdam (청담) = many English menus. Rural areas/golmok (골목, alley) = Korean is standard.

6. Refusing somaek (soju + beer mix) pressure: Say "I don't drink soju" or hold your cup upside down with your palm = refusal signal. Most Koreans respect this.

7. Allergies: Must inform in advance. Hansik (Korean food) seasoning = garlic, soy sauce, honey, gochujang (Korean chili paste) + gluten (wheat in soy sauce).

8. Separate payment: Individual payment with friends = "더치페이 가능?" (Deochipei ganeung?, Can we split the bill?) Some places allow it / some don't. Korean pattern = one person pays, then others transfer money later (Kakao Pay (카카오페이) / Toss (토스) money transfer is common ganpyeon gyeolje (간편결제, simple payment) method).

⚠ Medical, Safety, & Dietary Disclaimer

Direct BBQ cooking = burn risk (grill 250-350°C). For children, request family seating in advance + keep them away from the grill. Pork/chicken must be well-done (food poisoning, Salmonella, E.coli). Pregnant women/immunocompromised individuals = cook thoroughly. Inform about allergies in advance. If food poisoning is suspected, call ☎ 1339 (medical, English). This guide provides general information, not medical advice.

Dietary restriction fallback: General BBQ restaurants = no Halal, Kosher, Vegetarian menus. For Halal BBQ, search in advance for some restaurants in Itaewon (이태원) and Gangnam (강남) (e.g., Eid, Makan, Halal BBQ Korea). For vegetarian/vegan options, refer to #799 Vegetarian guide OR consider other restaurants separately.

💰 Essential Tourist Tips

  • Total Time (for 2 people):
  • Budget-friendly BBQ = 1~1.5h
  • General = 1.5~2h
  • Hoesik (company dinner) / Upscale = 2~3h
  • Average budget per person (As of June 2026, prices subject to change):
  • Budget-friendly = ₩30,000-50,000 + 1 bottle of soju
  • General = ₩50,000-80,000 + 2 bottles of alcohol
  • Upscale = ₩100,000-200,000 + wine
  • Jeomsim teukseon (점심 특선, Lunch Special): Weekdays 11:30-14:00, 30-50% discount.
  • Payment Pattern:
  • Counter payment = 80% (Korean style)
  • Table payment = 20% (More common in English-friendly restaurants)
  • Bongsa-ryo (서비스료, service charge): Some upscale Japanese/Korean BBQ in hotels may charge 10% / General restaurants usually do not.
  • VAT Refund: Food and beverages are generally not eligible for VAT refund.
