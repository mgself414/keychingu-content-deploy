Jeju Green Tea Guide — Osulloc Tea Museum, Seogwang Tea Fields, Seongeup Green Tea Village and Green Tea Cave

Subtitle: Osulloc Tea Museum (오설록 티뮤지엄), free admission, summer season 09:00–19:00 · winter season 09:00–18:00, open year-round · Seogwang Tea Fields (서광차밭), same address · Seongeup Green Tea Village (성읍녹차마을), 09:00–17:00 · Seongeup Green Tea Cave (성읍녹차동굴), 09:00–17:30 — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

In Andeok-myeon (안덕면), Seogwipo (서귀포시), Amorepacific's Osulloc Tea Museum and the Seogwang Tea Fields share one address (15 Sinhwayeoksa-ro (신화역사로 15)), and on Jungsangan-dong-ro (중산간동로), Seogwipo, there is Seongeup Green Tea Village, which has a lava cave inside its green tea fields.

This guide covers only these four places, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI).

🏛 Osulloc Tea Museum — Andeok-myeon

Osulloc Tea Museum (15 Sinhwayeoksa-ro, Andeok-myeon, Seogwipo-si (서귀포시 안덕면 신화역사로 15), ☎ 0507-1418-5312) is a tea museum that Amorepacific opened in September 2001 (month 9), and it is the first tea museum in Korea according to the Korea Tourism Organization. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, 1.8 million people (180만 명) visit each year. There are experience programs from tea seating to tea classes.

  • Fees: Free admission, experience programs are separate. Free parking.
  • Opening hours: Summer season 09:00–19:00, winter season 09:00–18:00. They may change in bad weather.
  • Closed days: Open year-round.
  • The shop registered at the same address is registered as 09:00–18:00 and accepts cards. Restrooms available.

🍃 Seogwang Tea Fields

Seogwang Tea Fields (15 Sinhwayeoksa-ro, Andeok-myeon (안덕면 신화역사로 15), ☎ 064-794-5312) are tea fields laid out in Seogwang-ri (서광리), Seogwipo, and the Osulloc Tea Museum is adjacent to the tea fields. Osulloc has cleared them since 1983, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization they are the largest tea-producing site in Korea. Together with Dosun Tea Garden (도순다원) and Hannam Tea Garden (한남다원) in Seogwipo, they are run by the Amorepacific Group. They are near Sanbangsan (산방산), and the Chusa Kim Jeonghui exile site (추사 김정희 유배지) and the Gotjawal (곶자왈) forest are close.

  • Opening hours: Summer season 09:00–19:00, winter season 09:00–18:00. They may close early depending on the weather.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Parking available. Strollers are registered as "not allowed."

🍵 Seongeup Green Tea Village and Green Tea Cave

Seongeup Green Tea Village (4778 Jungsangan-dong-ro, Seogwipo-si (서귀포시 중산간동로 4778), ☎ 064-787-2254) is a tea garden that grows green tea and even makes products from it. There is a cafe that sells green tea rice cakes and ice cream, and there are a foot bath experience area and a kart experience area.

  • Opening hours: 09:00–17:00, open year-round. Parking available.

Seongeup Green Tea Cave (4772 Jungsangan-dong-ro (중산간동로 4772), ☎ Oneureun Nokcha Hanjan (오늘은 녹차한잔) 064-787-6888) is a natural lava cave inside the Seongeup Green Tea Village tea garden, known for photos taken from inside looking toward the entrance. If you walk 15–20 minutes from the parking lot following the green tea fields, a small forest appears, and inside the forest there is a small cave and a larger one.

  • Opening hours: 09:00–17:30, open year-round. Parking available.
  • Getting there: Set your navigation destination to "Oneureun Nokcha Hanjan" or "Seongeup Green Tea Village." The 1st floor of the "Oneureun Nokcha Hanjan" cafe is a souvenir shop and foot bath experience area, and the 2nd floor is a bakery cafe.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Thinking Osulloc and Seongeup Green Tea Village are one place — Osulloc and the Seogwang Tea Fields are at 15 Sinhwayeoksa-ro, Andeok-myeon, and Seongeup Green Tea Village is at 4778 Jungsangan-dong-ro, so the addresses differ.

2. Looking for the cave right in front of the parking lot — You have to walk 15–20 minutes from the parking lot following the green tea fields.

3. Thinking it opens as usual on bad-weather days — The Osulloc Tea Museum and Seogwang Tea Fields may change hours or close early depending on the weather.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Is there an admission fee?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, admission and parking at the Osulloc Tea Museum are free and experience programs are paid separately. For the other places, fee information is not listed.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, cards are accepted at the Osulloc Tea Museum shop. For the other places, card information is not listed.
