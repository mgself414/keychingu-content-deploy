12 Busan Cafes in Gwangalli, Namcheon, and Suyeong — Where Foreign Reviews Showed Up in Three Languages First

Subtitle: Jeonpo (#246, #1321), Yeongdo (#1321), and the Dessert Road (#1324) left an axis untouched — the alleys behind Gwangan Bridge, the Namcheon coast road, the Suyeong backstreets, the Millak waterfront, the Hwangnyeongsan view. Only cafes with English, Japanese, and Chinese reviews together on Naver Map made the cut — 12 of them, grouped into 4 zones for a half-day route. In-cafe rules (holding seats, mugs, the return counter) are covered in #1378.

ISSUE 06 · #1381 · 2026-09-17 · Busan Cafe Series Part 4

🎯 Why "Three-Language Reviews" Is the Bar

There are already three Busan cafe pieces — Jeonpo Cafe Street (전포카페거리, #246), How to Drink Coffee in the City of a Champion (#1321), and From Red Bean to Gelato (#1324). But running the foreign-review data again showed 72 of 81 Busan cafes weren't in any of them, and nearly half of those were crowded into Suyeong District (수영구) — Gwangalli (광안리), Namcheon (남천), Suyeong (수영), and Millak (민락). This piece is that gap.

There's one selection rule — cafes on Naver Map with English, Japanese, and Chinese reviews together. Not Korean popularity rankings — the judgment is that a place foreign visitors have already found and left traces in three languages is the safest pick for the next one. Review counts are a 2026-09 snapshot, and this piece lists that count for every cafe.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours, closed days, and menu items are listed only when confirmed from a public source; unconfirmed fields are left as "check Instagram." Walking time from the station is given only at the zone level — for the walk to an individual cafe, checking Naver Map's walking directions on the spot is more accurate. Cafe-use rules (holding a seat, mugs, the return counter) are in #1378.

① Gwangalli Back Alleys — 4 Cafes

One block in from the Gwangalli beach road is where the alleys start. Walking distance from Gwangan Station (광안역) and Geumnyeonsan Station (금련산역), Line 2.

  • Noah Brewers — a filter-coffee-focused roastery. The Noah blend, an Ethiopia single origin, and a long black are the signatures. The only cafe with an even 5·5·5 split across the three languages — meaning plenty of foreign visitors come for the coffee itself. Check Instagram for hours.
  • Sea Otter's Pie — around 10 kinds of pie (meat pie, escargot pie, apple pie). The pie outranks the coffee here, so it works well as a lunch substitute. 11:30–20:00, closed Tuesdays.
  • Cafe Picnic — Earl Grey milk-jam toast, and two resident Shiba Inus (Jack and Marco). 11:00–20:00. Go in daytime if the dogs are the point.
  • Ilgijang — a bakery for financiers and madeleines. Its foreign reviews are just 2, both English, but it's here for this zone's "dessert to go" role. Check Instagram for hours.

② Namcheon Coast Road — 3 Cafes

Namcheon faces Gwangan Bridge head-on along its coast road. Walking distance from Namcheon Station (남천역), Line 2 — or walk the beach over from Gwangalli.

  • Cafe E.ple — a dog-friendly cafe plus dessert. 10:00–24:00, open year-round (closures announced on Instagram). The most foreign reviews in this zone (5·4·4).
  • BCBL coffee roasters — a roastery with a Gwangan Bridge view from the 2nd floor. Americano, black orange. Tue–Sun 10:00–21:00 (last order 20:30), Mon 11:00–21:00. 5 English reviews — a heavier English-speaking share.
  • Singi Coffee Namcheon — seasonal-topping pancakes (fig in fall, strawberry in winter), drip bags and cookies. It appears to share an Instagram with its Yeongdo (영도) branch, so check that account for the Namcheon location's own hours.

③ Suyeong-Millak Alleys — 4 Cafes

The back alleys of Suyeong Station (수영역), Lines 2 and 3, and the Millak waterfront. Quieter than the Gwangalli side, with a morning cafe.

  • Daily Lucky — bread-focused, a Greek yogurt plus rye bread plate, drip coffee. Opens at 07:00 — the earliest cafe in this piece, so starting the morning here makes the route work. Check Instagram for closing time.
  • Deep Sleep Coffee — a 1-minute walk from Suyeong Station Exit 10. Pound cake and a strong latte, plus a monthly rotating show from local artists. 10:00–19:00. The only one of the 12 with confirmed station access, so make it the first stop on a rainy day.
  • 1-in Samuso — red-bean-paste sweets with a design that changes monthly. Mon–Sat 12:00–18:00, closed Sundays. A 3·3·3 split across the three languages.
  • Ilikeit — coffee plus a furniture-and-home-goods collab space called "Itteum (잇틈)," minimal and vintage. On the Millak waterfront side. Check Instagram for hours.

④ Hwangnyeongsan View — 1 Cafe

  • Bluebang Hwangnyeongsan — a Busan night-view cafe. Hwangnyeongsan honey tea ₩8,000, cafe latte ₩7,000, vanilla bean latte ₩7,500, cold brew ₩6,000. 11:00–02:00 the next day (last order 01:30). No subway access — it's near the Hwangnyeongsan Lookout Rest Area parking lot, so a taxi is the answer. Keep it separate from the half-day route as its own evening leg.

🗺 Half-Day Route — Gwangalli → Namcheon → Suyeong-Millak

1. Morning Suyeong Station → Daily Lucky (07:00–) bread plate → Deep Sleep Coffee (10:00–, 1 min from Suyeong Station Exit 10)

2. Midday One stop on Line 2 to Namcheon Station → Cafe E.ple → BCBL's 2nd-floor Gwangan Bridge view (→ Singi Coffee's pancakes optional)

3. Afternoon Walk the beach from Namcheon to the Gwangalli back alleys (about 15–20 min) → Noah Brewers filter coffee → Sea Otter's Pie → either Cafe Picnic or Ilgijang

4. Evening, separate Taxi to Bluebang Hwangnyeongsan for the night view (until 02:00 the next day)

5. Fit the Millak zone (1-in Samuso, Ilikeit) in between Suyeong Station and Gwangalli, or plan it for daytime around 1-in Samuso's noon opening.

The seat-holding, mug, and return-counter rules are the same at every cafe — first time here, read #1378 first. Only Cafe E.ple and Cafe Picnic allow dogs.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. 1-in Samuso on Sunday, Sea Otter's Pie on Tuesday — both closed. Check the day first.

2. Adding Bluebang Hwangnyeongsan to the walking route — there's no subway access. Go separately by taxi.

3. Skipping "check Instagram" — for the cafes without listed hours in this piece, that day's answer is whatever the Instagram profile's business notice says.

⚙ 3 FAQs

1. Just one pick? — For coffee, Noah Brewers (a 5·5·5 split across the three languages); for the view, BCBL's 2nd floor (Gwangan Bridge); for the night view, Bluebang.

2. Why isn't Jeonpo here? — Jeonpo Cafe Street is already covered in #246 and #1321. This piece only covers outside Jeonpo, the Suyeong District where foreign reviews cluster.

3. Payment and ordering? — All 12 are standard card-payment cafes. For order/pickup/return procedures see #1288 and #1378; if your card won't register, see #1301.
