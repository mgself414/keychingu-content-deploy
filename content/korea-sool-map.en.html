🎯 How to Use This Guide

Open a brewery door and the steam of cooking rice and the smell of nuruk (누룩, the grain fermentation starter) reach your nose first. This guide groups Korean sool (술, alcoholic drinks) not as places to go drink but as places where it is made and chosen. There are four branches: tasting and choosing in Seoul, makgeolli (막걸리, milky rice wine) breweries and alleys, distilled spirits and master-brewed liquors, and craft beer breweries. Places, hours and age limits come only from VisitKorea, thesool.com (더술닷컴) and Easy-to-Find Living Law (찾기쉬운 생활법령). No experience fee was posted anywhere.

This edition is for anyone who wants to see how a bottle is brewed before buying it, and anyone who wants to add one brewery to a travel route. The etiquette of ordering and pouring in a bar is handed over to #829 Korean Alcohol Ordering and Pairing Workflow.

The old guide was wrong in two places. The Traditional Liquor Gallery is not in "Insadong"; its official address is 18 Bukchon-ro, Jongno-gu (thesool.com), and Andong soju is not "UNESCO traditional liquor"; VisitKorea lists it as Gyeongsangbuk-do Intangible Cultural Heritage No. 12. "Kooksoondang (국순당) home-brewing experience (Seoul)", "Top 5 makgeolli breweries" and soju prices were cut for lack of an official basis.

🍶 Tasting and Choosing in Seoul

  • Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리) — 18 Bukchon-ro (북촌로), Jongno-gu, Seoul. A traditional liquor space set up by the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs and the Korea Agro-Fisheries and Food Trade Corporation. It displays and sells liquors by food master craftsmen (myeongin, 명인) and prize winners of Korean liquor competitions, picks a monthly "sool of the month" (이달의 시음주) and runs a tasting program. Programs for foreigners are in English, Japanese and Chinese (thesool.com). 10:00~19:00, 02-555-2283, reservations via a Naver booking link (VisitKorea). Closing days and fees not posted
  • Chunpung Brewery (춘풍양조장) — 101-3 Dasan-ro (다산로), Jung-gu, Seoul (Sindang-dong, 신당동). A brewery that makes makgeolli and runs tours and tastings for overseas tourists. Open 11:00~19:00, tours at 11:00 / 15:00 / 17:00, closed every Sunday and Monday, age 19 or older by international age (만 19세), 1899-8876, no parking (VisitKorea). Fee not posted
  • For a Seoul makgeolli introduction, see #136 Seoul Makgeolli and Traditional Liquor 101

🌾 Makgeolli Breweries and Alleys

  • Geumjeongsanseong Makgeolli (금정산성 막걸리) — 453 Sanseong-ro (산성로), Geumjeong-gu, Busan, at the foot of Geumjeongsan at 400m above sea level. Brewed by Korea's first master craftsman (myeongin) in the makgeolli field, and VisitKorea writes "the only makgeolli in the country designated as a local folk liquor". 08:30~18:00, open year-round, 051-517-0202. For group reservations of 5 or more: a tour plus experiences of pressing nuruk, brewing makgeolli and filtering it (VisitKorea). Fee not posted
  • Geumpung Brewery (금풍양조장) — 8 Samnangseong-gil (삼랑성길), Ganghwa-gun, Incheon. In 2021 it became the first on Ganghwa Island to receive a regional specialty liquor license (takju [탁주, unfiltered rice liquor] and yakju [약주, clear rice wine]). Monday to Thursday 10:30~17:30, Friday to Sunday 10:30~18:00, open year-round, contact before visiting is required, 0507-1374-1931, cards accepted (VisitKorea). Fee not posted
  • Jeonju Traditional Liquor Museum (전주 전통술박물관) — 74 Hanji-gil (한지길), Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si, Jeonbuk, inside Hanok Village. A public Type 2 museum, free admission. Tuesday to Sunday 10:00~18:00 (until 20:00 from June to October), closed every Monday, on January 1, and on the day of Seollal (설, Lunar New Year) and Chuseok (추석, Korean Thanksgiving), 063-287-6305. It sells regional traditional liquors on consignment (VisitKorea)
  • Samcheon-dong Makgeolli Alley, Jeonju (삼천동 막걸리골목) — Samcheon-dong 1-ga (삼천동1가), Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si, along about 200m of Geomasan-ro (거마산로). Order one kettle of makgeolli and 20-plus side dishes follow, such as grilled fish, jeon (전, pan-fried patties), stew and samhap (삼합, a platter of three foods). Jeonju Future Heritage (VisitKorea). Dozens of shops, hours vary by shop. For a Jeonju route, see #384 Jeonju Travel Map

🔥 Distilled Spirits and Master-Brewed Liquors

  • Myeongin Andong Soju (명인안동소주) — 6 Saneopdanji 6-gil (산업단지6길), Pungsan-eup, Andong-si, Gyeongbuk. Master craftsman Park Jae-seo (박재서) (Traditional Food Master No. 6). Andong soju is a 45% ABV pure-grain distilled liquor (VisitKorea). Tasting, an exhibition hall, and experiences of making nuruk and brewing traditional liquor; 09:00~18:00, advance reservation required, closed every Saturday and Sunday, 054-856-6903 (VisitKorea). Fee not posted
  • Andong Soju Traditional Food Museum (안동소주전통음식박물관) — 71-1 Gangnam-ro (강남로), Andong-si, Gyeongbuk. Founded 1995, free admission, 09:00~17:00, open year-round, 054-858-4541. It has a demonstration hall, a tasting hall and a shop (VisitKorea). VisitKorea lists Andong soju as "Gyeongsangbuk-do Intangible Cultural Heritage No. 12, Traditional Food Master No. 20"
  • Hansan Sogokju Brewery (한산소곡주 양조장) — 1118 Chungjeol-ro (충절로), Hansan-myeon, Seocheon-gun, Chungnam. The brewery of master craftsman Woo Hee-yeol (우희열); tasting is always available, and brewing and demonstrations are announced in advance. Hours are "please inquire by phone", 041-951-0290, use the Hansan Ramie Hall (한산모시관) parking lot (VisitKorea)
  • Jeju Saemju Brewery (제주샘주 양조장) — 283 Aewon-ro (애원로), Aewol-eup, Jeju-si, Jeju. Selected as a "visiting brewery" (찾아가는 양조장), with tastings of gosori-sul (고소리술) and omegi-sul (오메기술, Jeju millet liquor), and experiences with omegi-tteok (오메기떡, millet rice cake), shindari (쉰다리, fermented rice drink) and cocktails. 09:30~18:00 (break 12:00~13:00), closed during Seollal and Chuseok holidays, all ages, 064-799-4225 (VisitKorea)
  • For a bundle of brewery visits, see #689 Brewery Tours

🍺 Craft Beer Breweries

  • Magpie Brewery (맥파이 브루어리) — 23 Donghoecheon 1-gil (동회천1길), Jeju-si, Jeju. The brewery of Magpie Brewing Company, which started in Gyeongnidan-gil (경리단길). 12:00~20:00 (last order 19:00), closed every Monday and Tuesday, tours and tastings for age 8 and older by international age (만 8세), 0507-1383-0227. Tours are weekends only, 4 times a day, and no reservation is needed, but groups reserve by email or phone (VisitKorea)
  • Jeju Beer (제주맥주) — 62-11 Geumneung Nongong-gil (금능농공길), Hallim-eup, Jeju-si, Jeju. Glass tasting, making your own glass and a brewery tour. 12:30~19:30 (brewery pub last order 19:00), closed every Monday and Tuesday, and the day before and the day of Seollal and Chuseok, 064-798-9872 (VisitKorea)
  • Korea Craft Brewery (코리아 크래프트 브류어리) — 97 Wonnamsandan-ro (원남산단로), Wonnam-myeon, Eumseong-gun, Chungbuk. Beer brand "ARK". Weekdays 08:00~17:30, closed Saturday and Sunday, 043-927-2600. All tours are by group reservation of 15 or more only (VisitKorea). Individual travelers should check the program on the website that includes a bus from Seoul (VisitKorea)

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Price range: There are no officially posted fees for the experiences and tours in this guide. The only places confirmed free are admission to the Jeonju Traditional Liquor Museum and the Andong Soju Traditional Food Museum (VisitKorea)
  • Vegetarian: The problem is the side dishes more than the drink. At the Samcheon-dong Makgeolli Alley, side dishes such as grilled fish and samhap are laid out by default (VisitKorea) — ask separately for vegetable-only side dishes
  • Halal and alcohol-free: Every place deals in alcohol. Whether the shindari experience at Jeju Saemju contains alcohol is not posted — ask when you reserve. A KMF-certified Korean restaurant in Seoul is Eid (이드) — 15 Usadan-ro 10-gil (우사단로10길), Yongsan-gu, daily 12:00~21:00 (VisitSeoul)
  • Allergies: Ingredients (rice, wheat nuruk, malt, fruit) differ by drink, so check the bottle label
  • For a card to show staff, see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Practical Tips for Korean Sool — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Ordering, Getting There

  • Age: Under Korean law a "minor" (청소년) is under 19, excluding anyone who has reached January 1 of the year they turn 19. Selling alcohol to a minor is punishable by up to 2 years in prison or a fine of up to ₩20,000,000 (~$15,000), and the seller must verify age and identity (Easy-to-Find Living Law). Bring your passport
  • Solo: The Geumjeongsanseong experience (5 or more) and Korea Craft Brewery (15 or more) have group conditions (VisitKorea). For places that are good for drinking alone, see #348 Seoul Solo Diner Map
  • Reservations: Myeongin Andong Soju requires advance reservation, and Geumpung Brewery requires contact before visiting (VisitKorea). The Traditional Liquor Gallery uses Naver booking
  • Payment: Geumpung Brewery accepts cards (VisitKorea). The others state nothing — #527 Korean Payment Systems
  • Ordering: for how to order and pour in a bar, see #829 Korean Alcohol Ordering and Pairing Workflow; if you cannot communicate, see #1339 Ordering Etiquette
  • Getting there: most breweries are on the edge of a city — do not drive after drinking. For Andong, Seocheon and Eumseong, see #1297 Express and Intercity Bus Manual; for transit cards, #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "The Traditional Liquor Gallery is in Insadong" — the official address is 18 Bukchon-ro, Jongno-gu (thesool.com, VisitKorea)

2. "Andong soju is a UNESCO heritage" — VisitKorea lists it as Gyeongsangbuk-do Intangible Cultural Heritage No. 12. We found no basis for a UNESCO listing

3. "At Korean age 20 you may drink / you must wait until your 19th birthday by international age" — both are wrong; the standard is January 1 of the year you turn 19 (Easy-to-Find Living Law)

4. "I can drop by a brewery any time" — Chunpung Brewery is closed Sunday and Monday, Magpie and Jeju Beer on Monday and Tuesday, and Myeongin Andong Soju on weekends (VisitKorea)
