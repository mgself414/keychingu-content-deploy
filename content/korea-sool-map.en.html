⚠ 3 Sool Mistakes

1. "Korean alcohol = soju only" — not knowing makgeolli and traditional liquor: People drink only soju. In reality, there's a full experience of makgeolli (Jangsu, Kooksoondang, Jipyeong, Pocheon, Seoul Jangsu — the 5 great breweries) and traditional liquor (Andong soju with 700 years of UNESCO-linked history, Munbaeju and Igangju Joseon royal court liquors).

2. "Drinking = Korean-language only" — not knowing the English-friendly cluster: People assume Korean only. In reality, the Itaewon & Haebangchon craft beer cluster (70%+ foreign clientele, English menus), the Traditional Liquor Gallery in Insadong (English tastings), and the Kooksoondang home-brewing class are all foreigner friendly.

3. "Makgeolli = one kind" — not knowing brewery diversity: People think there's one type. In reality: Jangsu (Seoul, smooth), Kooksoondang (nationwide standard), Jipyeong (Yangpyeong, full-bodied), Pocheon Dowon (Pocheon, crisp), Seoul Jangsu (Seoul, deep flavor) — a full tour of the 5 great breweries, plus new non-alcoholic makgeolli.

🍯 5 Tips for Foreign Visitors

1. Makgeolli = the standard Korean first drink — 5~6% ABV, fruity notes, the #1 gateway for foreigners

2. Soju = ₩1,500~2,000 (~$1.10~1.50) per bottle — Korea's #1 value drink · pairs with samgyeopsal (삼겹살) pork belly (#337)

3. Craft beer = Itaewon, Haebangchon, Yeonnam — foreigner-friendly cluster with English menus

4. Traditional liquor = the Insadong gallery — English tastings, purchases possible (#458 Wellness fit)

5. Reserve hotel bars in advance — Signiel, The Shilla, Four Seasons (#344·#469, same pattern as hotel bingsu)
