Foreigner Mangwon-dong deep dive

> Mangwon-dong is a great place to experience the present of Korea, combining the liveliness of the traditional market, the leisure of the Han River Park, and the unique alley cafes and restaurants. It is an ideal destination for foreign tourists who want to explore at their own pace amidst the hustle and bustle of Seoul.

At a glance: Mangwon-dong key information

Item Content

Mangwon-dong (Mangwon-dong) is a vibrant and evolving neighborhood in western Seoul, known for its unique blend of traditional charm and modern sensibilities. It offers a more authentic and less touristy experience compared to more central districts. Visitors can explore a lively traditional market, discover independent cafes and boutiques, and enjoy the relaxed atmosphere of the Han River Park. This area is particularly appealing to those seeking local culture, delicious food, and a chill vibe.

1. Not enough cash when visiting the market: Although there are many places in Mangwon Market that accept card payments, there are still small shops and street food stalls that only accept cash. In particular, cash is much more convenient and faster for small payments or instant snacks. Currency exchange booths and ATMs can be difficult to find, so it's a good idea to have a small amount of cash on hand before visiting the market.

2. Misunderstanding of Mangwon Hangang Park accessibility: Mangwon Hangang Park is one of the main attractions of Mangwon-dong, but it takes about 20-25 minutes to walk from Mangwon Station to the park. It is farther away than you think, so walking after using public transportation may take more time than expected. You can access it more conveniently by using Ttareungi (Seoul City's public bicycle) or considering taking a bus that stops one or two stops away.

3. Negligent time planning when exploring the alleys: Mangwon-dong has many hidden gem-like shops in the small and large alleys. It's good to look at a map and go to a specific destination, but if you just walk around, it's fun to discover unexpected attractions. However, this process may take more time than expected, causing you to miss other schedules or miss meal times. It is effective to take ample time to explore at your leisure, or to plan your route by deciding in advance the stores you want to visit.

15 stores by region and sub-cluster

Mangwon-dong can be broadly divided into three areas: Mangwon Market and residential alleys, Mangnidan-gil (Poeun-ro area), and Near Mangwon Hangang Park.

1. Mangwon Market & Residential Alleyways

  • Mangwon Market
  • Location: 5-minute walk from Mangwon Station (14 Poeun-ro 8-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul)
  • Signature: Gangjeong (dakjeong), croquette, noodle soup, cheap and fresh ingredients
  • Price: ₩5,000~20,000 (based on snacks)
  • Foreigners fit: ★★★ (Good for experiencing the liveliness of Korean traditional markets)
  • English communication level: Some stores have basic English communication or use translation apps.
  • Recommended timing: It is good to visit after lunch time (1:00 PM to 4:00 PM) to enjoy snacks and look at dinner ingredients.
  • Tip: Be sure to try the fried chicken and croquette. There are good public restrooms in the market. Some places offer discounts when paying with ‘Zero Pay’.
  • Mangwon Tteokbokki
  • Location: 7-minute walk from Mangwon Station (76 Mangwon-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul)
  • Signature: Instant tteokbokki (style boiled and eaten at the table)
  • Price: ₩10,000~20,000 (for 2 people)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★☆ (You can enjoy Korean soul food in a local way)
  • English communication level: The menu is written in English, and ordering is simple.
  • Recommended timing: Lunch or dinner time. It's especially good on rainy or cold days.
  • Tip: It is recommended to add fried food, ramen noodles, etc. with tteokbokki and stir-fry fried rice in the remaining broth.
  • Almang Store - Zero Waste Shop
  • Location: 10-minute walk from Mangwon Station (14 World Cup-ro 23-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul)
  • Signature: Refill station (detergent, shampoo, etc.), eco-friendly household items, products from artists based in the Mangwon-dong area
  • Price: ₩5,000~30,000 (depending on product)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★☆ (Recommended for travelers interested in sustainable consumption)
  • English communication level: Able to communicate in basic English
  • Recommended timing: It's a good place to stop by while exploring the alleys of Mangwon-dong.
  • Tip: Bring your own container to refill detergent, etc. This is a space where you can get a glimpse of Korea’s eco-friendly movement.
  • Cafe M.O.M
  • Location: 8 minutes walk from Mangwon Station (109 Poeun-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul)
  • Signature: Hand drip coffee, cozy atmosphere, homemade desserts
  • Price: ₩5,000~8,000 (based on drinks)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★☆ (Good for relaxing in a quiet and comfortable atmosphere)
  • English communication level: Basic English communication ability, English menu provided
  • Recommended timing: It's great to enjoy a cup of coffee after lunch or spend time reading in the afternoon.
  • Tip: This is a great place to relax and enjoy the scenery of the alleyways of Mangwon-dong.
  • Eojeodachaekbang - Independent Bookstore
  • Location: 12-minute walk from Mangwon Station (45 World Cup-ro 23-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul)
  • Signature: Independent publications, small exhibitions, book-related goods
  • Price: ₩10,000~30,0
