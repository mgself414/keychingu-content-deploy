Gangneung Market Guide — Downtown Jungang Market, Jumunjin Fish Market and Comprehensive Market, Anmok Coffee Street

Subtitle: Gangneung Jungang Market (강릉 중앙시장), opened 1980, hours vary by stall · Jumunjin Fish Market (주문진수산시장), 07:00–22:00, open year-round · Jumunjin Comprehensive Market (주문진종합시장), 07:00–20:00, 108 stalls · Anmok Coffee Street (안목 커피거리), always open — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Markets in Gangneung fall into two areas. One is Gangneung Jungang Market, in the Seongnam-dong (성남동) area of downtown Gangneung. The other is Jumunjin Fish Market and Jumunjin Comprehensive Market in Jumunjin-eup (주문진읍), beside Jumunjin Port (주문진항). By the sea there is also Anmok Coffee Street, lined with cafes.

This guide covers only the markets and the coffee street, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). For Gyeongpo (경포), Jeongdongjin (정동진), lodging, and the rest of a Gangneung trip, follow the Related Content below.

🧺 Gangneung Jungang Market — Downtown's Main Traditional Market

Gangneung Jungang Market at 21 Geumseong-ro, Gangneung (금성로 21), ☎ Jungang Market Merchants Association 033-648-4447, is a traditional market in the Seongnam-dong area of Gangneung. It was registered and opened on January 18, 1980, the third market to open in downtown Gangneung after Dongbu Market and Seobu Market.

  • What they sell: Fish, dried seafood, clothing, hanbok, and sundries. At roadside and alley stalls, local specialties change with the season.
  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by stall.
  • During Danoje: According to the tourism organization, each year during the Gangneung Danoje Festival (강릉 단오제), the festival atmosphere around the Namdaecheon Danoje grounds (남대천 단오장) spills into the market, which gets livelier.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available.
  • Wolhwa Street (월화거리) (2111 Gyeonggang-ro, Gangneung (경강로 2111), Imdang-dong): Always open. The street is themed on the "Namdaecheon Wolhwajeong legend," the love story of Muwollang and Lady Yeonhwa from the Silla period.

🐟 Jumunjin Fish Market — The Fish Market by the Harbor

Jumunjin Fish Market at 38 Sijang-gil, Jumunjin-eup, Gangneung (주문진읍 시장길 38), ☎ 033-661-7302, is a traditional market that has formed since 1936 around Jumunjin Port, famous for squid fishing. It is connected to the Comprehensive Market, the dried seafood market, and the raw fish center (회센터).

  • Opening hours: 07:00–22:00, open year-round.
  • What they sell: Seafood and dried seafood such as squid, mackerel, pollock, Pacific saury, and crab. Assorted raw fish and frozen fish are also sold.
  • How to eat: You can taste fresh raw fish on the spot.
  • Facilities: Parking is available.
  • Jumunjin Port (1758-22 Haean-ro, Jumunjin-eup (해안로 1758-22)): A major fishing port on the east coast with about 350 boats coming and going, where squid, sand lance, pollock, herring, and anchovy are caught in large numbers.

🛒 Jumunjin Comprehensive Market — The Arcade Market

Jumunjin Comprehensive Market at 11-3 Gusijang-gil, Jumunjin-eup, Gangneung (주문진읍 구시장길 11-3), ☎ 033-661-2180, opened in 2006 and was modernized in November 2009 when an arcade was installed. It has 108 stalls.

  • Opening hours: 07:00–20:00, varying by stall.
  • What they sell: Food, clothing, sundries, seafood, and local agricultural specialties.
  • Festivals: According to the tourism organization, a pufferfish dish festival and a squid festival are held every year.
  • Facilities: Restrooms are available, and parking holds about 76 cars.

☕ Anmok Coffee Street — Seaside Cafe Street

Gangneung Coffee Street (Anmok Coffee Street) at 20-1 Changhae-ro 14beon-gil, Gangneung (창해로14번길 20-1), ☎ Gangneung Anmok Traveler Center 033-640-4928, became known as a coffee destination in the early 1980s, and from the 1990s coffee masters gathered here and cafes serving their own roasted beans multiplied. A coffee festival is held every year.

  • Opening hours: The street is always open. Each cafe has its own hours.
  • Anmok Sunrise Park (안목해맞이공원) (Gyeonso-dong (견소동)): A park connected to Anmok Beach and a sunrise spot. The "ANMOK" lettering sculpture is the photo spot.
  • For how to choose an Anmok cafe, see #1037 Gangneung Anmok Beach Coffee.

🚉 Getting There and Nearby

  • From Seoul, take the KTX Gangneung Line to Gangneung Station. Trains and the 2-day, 1-night route are covered in #1106 Gangneung KTX 2 Days 1 Night.
  • Jungang Market is downtown Gangneung, while Jumunjin Fish Market and Comprehensive Market are in Jumunjin-eup.
  • Tourist information: Gangneung Station Tourist Information Center ☎ 033-642-8692 · Tourism interpretation service ☎ 1330.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Treating Jungang Market and the Jumunjin markets as the same place — Jungang Market is downtown Gangneung (Geumseong-ro), while Jumunjin Fish Market and Comprehensive Market are in Jumunjin-eup.

2. Assuming Jungang Market has one set of hours — According to the tourism organization, opening hours and closed days vary by stall.

3. Looking for Jumunjin Fish Market's closed day — According to the tourism organization, it is open 07:00–22:00, year-round.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where do I go to eat raw fish?

A. Jumunjin Fish Market. You can taste raw fish on the spot, and it is open 07:00–22:00.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. Whether cards are accepted is not registered in the tourism organization's data for the markets in this guide.
