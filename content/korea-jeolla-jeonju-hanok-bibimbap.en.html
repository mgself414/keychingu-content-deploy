🎯 Why This Jeonju Guide?

Jeonju (전주) is Korea's #1 Hanok (한옥) Village (800+ traditional Korean houses), the origin of Bibimbap (비빔밥), and Korea's #1 matrix for Korean food enthusiasts. Common pain points for foreigners include: (a) the misconception that "all hanok villages are the same," (b) Jeonju (전주) is Korea's #1 with 800+ hanoks (한옥) and a different matrix from Jongmyo (종묘), Gangwon-do (강원도), and Andong (안동), (c) Bibimbap (비빔밥) is rooted in Jeonju (전주) as its origin matrix, (d) Hanjeongsik (한정식 - Korean full-course meal), Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥 - bean sprout soup with rice), and Jeonju Makgeolli (막걸리 - Korean rice wine) are its signatures, (e) it's an MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) + Instagram + Hanbok (한복 - traditional Korean attire) + cafe matrix, and (f) it's just a 1 hour 50 minute KTX ride away.

This guide covers 5 key pillars, 12 signatures, plus the Hanok Village (한옥마을), Bibimbap (비빔밥), and Hanbok (한복).

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "All hanok villages are the same": Jeonju (전주) is Korea's #1 with 800+ hanoks (한옥), offering a different matrix from other hanok (한옥) villages.

2. "Bibimbap (비빔밥) is the same everywhere": Jeonju (전주) is Korea's #1 origin with bean sprouts + Hwangpomuk (황포묵) as signatures.

3. "Hanbok (한복) is only for palaces": Jeonju Hanok Village (전주 한옥마을) is an MZ + Instagram Enthusiast Matrix #1.

4. "Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥) is just a regular gukbap (국밥 - Korean soup with rice)": It's a Jeonju (전주) signature and part of the Korean Enthusiast Matrix.

5. "Makgeolli (막걸리) + Jeon (전) is a common pairing": It's Korea's #1 Enthusiast Matrix and a Michelin-level pairing ([#688 cross](688_makgeolli_deep_dive.md)).
