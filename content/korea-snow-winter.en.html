Where to See Snow in Korea — Regional Gaps in "Snow Days" from KMA Climate Normals (January: Ulleungdo 17.7 days · Seoul 7.1 days · Busan 1.4 days), 2027 Snow Festival Announcement Status (Daegwallyeong 1/30~2/9 planned · Taebaeksan and Chilgapsan not yet announced), and Snowy Scenery Spots Reached by Gondola or Cable Car, with Fares

Subtitle: KMA (Korea Meteorological Administration) 1991~2020 normal "snow days" (days on which snow fell, not snow depth) — January Ulleungdo 17.7 days · Gwangju 9.9 days · Daejeon 9.8 days · Seoul 7.1 days · Jeju 7.2 days · Bukgangneung 4 days · Busan 1.4 days · Daegwallyeong Snow Flower Festival 2027 1/30~2/9 9:30~17:30 (planned) · Taebaeksan Snow Festival and Chilgapsan Ice Fountain Festival 2027 not yet announced · Muju Deogyusan Gondola round trip adult 25,000 won · Balwangsan Cable Car round trip adult 27,000 won · Daegwallyeong Sheep Ranch adult 9,000 won · Check rail times and fares directly in the Korail+ app

🎯 How to Use This Guide

The question foreigners visiting Korea in winter ask most often is "Can I see snow?" The answer depends on where you are. In the same January, Busan has snow on only about one day a month, Ulleungdo has more than ten, and Seoul sits in between. This guide first shows that gap using the official KMA normals, then lists the 2027 announcement status of three snow festivals, where your chances of meeting snow are higher, and the fares and operating hours (official figures) of places where you can ride a gondola or cable car up to snowy scenery when snow has fallen.

Two things up front. ① The only snow indicator published in the KMA normals is "snow days" (the number of days on which any one of snow, snow showers, powder snow, blowing snow, showery sleet, sleet, or graupel was observed), and it is not snow depth. Also, only 21 observation stations are covered by this indicator, so there are no values for Daegwallyeong, Taebaek, Sobaeksan, or Deogyusan — for those places we give festival and operating information instead of normals. ② We could not confirm the travel time or fare of the KTX or Mugunghwa train from Seoul on the official Korail site as of 2026-11-10. Check them yourself using the Korail+ app steps in #1287 Riding the KTX for the First Time.

Places already covered separately are linked — all of Pyeongchang (#412 · #1039), climbing Hallasan (한라산) (#1057), Hwacheon Sancheoneo (화천 산천어) and Pyeongchang trout ice fishing (#1069), snowy scenery in central Seoul (#1319), snow sledding parks (#1416), and Ulleungdo (울릉도) (#656).

❄ Where Do the Most Snowy Days Fall — KMA Normals (1991~2020) "Snow Days"

Taken from the KMA Open MET Data Portal's 1991~2020 climate normals fileset, sheet "Days by Phenomenon" (현상별일수), the snow days entry (unit: days, monthly normals). Snow days are the number of days on which any one of snow, snow showers, powder snow, blowing snow, showery sleet, sleet, or graupel was observed, and not the amount of snow on the ground (snow depth). May~September are 0 at every station, and April and October are under 1 day at most stations (at most Ulleungdo (울릉도) April, 1 day), so they are left out of the table.

Three ways to read it.

  • Ulleungdo (울릉도) is the highest of the 21 listed stations (17.7 days in January), and on the mainland the inland and west-coast stations (Gwangju (광주), Daejeon (대전), Mokpo (목포), Cheongju (청주), Jeonju (전주)) have more than Seoul (서울) in December and January. Bukgangneung (북강릉) on the mainland east coast has few in December and January and then peaks in February (5.9 days) and March (4.3 days) — for Bukgangneung (북강릉), February is the better month.
  • The south coast and southern east coast (Yeosu (여수), Changwon (창원), Busan (부산), Ulsan (울산), Pohang (포항)) have 1.4~3.1 days even in the peak month. Do not build snow into a winter Busan itinerary (#1419).
  • There are no normals for Daegwallyeong (대관령), Taebaek (태백), Sobaeksan (소백산), or Deogyusan (덕유산) in this table (they are not listed stations). Check the snow there through the festival and operating information below.

🎪 Three Snow Festivals — 2027 Announcement Status (as of 2026-11-10)

Daegwallyeong Snow Flower Festival (대관령 눈꽃축제) (Pyeongchang) — 2027 35th edition, 1/30~2/9 "planned"

  • 2027 schedule: The vendor participation notice posted by the festival committee on 2026-09-08 states "Festival period: Jan 30 (Sat) ~ Feb 9 (Tue) 2027, 9:30~17:30 (planned)" (notice on the official Daegwallyeong Snow Flower Festival website). There is not yet an official schedule or fare announcement for visitors.
  • Based on the 2026 edition (official website, TourAPI): 2026-02-13~02-22, 10:00~17:00, Songcheon area, Daegwallyeong-myeon (대관령면 송천 일원). Regular fare adult (middle school and older) 10,000 won, child (elementary school and under) 8,000 won, 5-ride snow sledding ticket 5,000 won sold separately, free under 24 months. Advance-purchase discounts were 30% (7,000 won) for 1/1~1/18, 20% (8,000 won) for 1/19~1/31, and 10% (9,000 won) for 2/1~2/12. There was a "festival closing notice" on 2026-02-22 and a notice on 2026-02-23 titled "Due to the early closing of the festival on February 22…" — treat this as precedent that the dates can change, and check the notices before departure for 2027 as well.
  • Getting there (official 2026-edition transport guide): KTX Jinbu Station (진부역) → Jinbu Terminal → Hoenggye Terminal (횡계터미널) → 10-minute walk → venue. Hoenggye Bus Terminal 033-335-5289. Inquiries 033-335-3995.

Taebaeksan Snow Festival (태백산 눈축제) (Taebaek) — 2027 not yet announced

  • No 2027 announcement (as of 2026-11-10, the festival website's notice board has only after-the-fact posts for the 2026 edition, the Taebaek City press releases have 0 posts on the snow festival, and the Taebaek City tourism website has only the 2026 schedule. The website of the organizer, Taebaek City Cultural Foundation, would not open, so we could not view it).
  • Based on the 2026 edition (official website): 33rd "2026 RE⧗AL Taebaeksan Snow Festival (태백산 눈축제)", 9 days from Saturday, January 31 to Sunday, February 8, 2026, around Taebaeksan National Park (태백산국립공원), at Danggol Plaza (당골광장) (180 Sodo-dong, Taebaek, Gangwon (강원 태백시 소도동 180)). The large snow sculpture exhibition had night opening 10:00~22:00, and the snow sledding and ice sledding theme park was 10:00~17:00. The Danggol route ran 36 trips on weekdays and weekends (first bus from the terminal 07:25, last bus 21:35), and the festival-period shuttle ran every 10 minutes on weekdays and every 7 minutes on weekends. 🚠 Above the Snowy Mountain Without Hiking — Gondolas and Cable Cars

  • Muju Deogyusan Resort Sightseeing Gondola (무주 덕유산 리조트 관광곤돌라) (185 Manseon-ro, Seolcheon-myeon, Muju-gun, Jeonbuk (전북 무주군 설천면 만선로 185)): 2.659km of line up to Seolcheonbong (설천봉) (1,520m above sea level), then about 20 minutes on foot from there to Hyangjeokbong (향적봉) (resort official). Fares: round trip general adult 25,000 won, child 20,000 won; one-way (for Hyangjeokbong shelter users) adult 20,000 won, child 16,000 won. The winter operating schedule on the official page is "December 13 ~ winter closing day", Monday to Friday, upbound 09:00~16:00 and downbound until 16:30, but no year is stated and there are no Saturday or Sunday hours — confirm the 2026-27 opening date from the resort notice. For weekends and public holidays from October to February, advance reservations open at 17:00 two weeks before the ride date, counting the ride date (official). ☎ 063-322-9000.
  • Balwangsan Sightseeing Cable Car (발왕산 관광케이블카) (Mona Yongpyeong, 2F Dragon Plaza, 715 Olympic-ro, Daegwallyeong-myeon, Pyeongchang-gun (평창군 대관령면 올림픽로 715 드래곤프라자 2층)): 3.7km one way, about 18 minutes, 7.4km round trip. Fares adult round trip 27,000 won, one-way 23,000 won; child (36 months to elementary school) round trip 23,000 won, one-way 19,000 won. 09:00~17:00 (downbound until 18:00), closed on the second and fourth Monday of every month, with same-day emergency closures in strong wind or lightning. The Balwangsan Gi Skywalk (발왕산 기 스카이워크) (same address) is 09:00~17:00 and closed every Monday, so its closing days differ. To check operation 033-330-7380, ticket office 033-330-7423.
  • Daegwallyeong Sheep Ranch (대관령양떼목장) (483-32 Daegwallyeongmaru-gil, Daegwallyeong-myeon, Pyeongchang-gun (평창군 대관령면 대관령마루길 483-32)): individual adult 9,000 won, child 7,000 won, preferential 6,000 won, Naver Reservation and Klook advance-purchase discounts (9 tickets per transaction). ⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

  1. "Snow days = snow on the ground" — KMA snow days are the number of days on which snow, sleet, graupel, and so on were observed. Not the depth of snow on the ground (❄).
  2. "It doesn't snow in the south" — Gwangju (광주) January 9.9 days and Daejeon (대전) 9.8 days are more than Seoul (서울) (7.1 days). The places with little snow are the south coast and southern east coast (Busan (부산) 1.4 days · Changwon (창원) 2.0 days · Ulsan (울산) 2.1 days · Pohang (포항) 2.2 days · Yeosu (여수) 3.1 days, by peak month) (❄).
  3. "The Daegwallyeong Snow Flower Festival is confirmed for 1/30~2/9" — it is the "planned" value from the vendor notice. In 2026 there was also an early closing notice (🎪).
  4. "Last year's fare and hours are this year's fare" — the Daegwallyeong and Chilgapsan 2027 fares are not yet announced, and the Deogyusan Gondola winter schedule has no year. Check the official page again before departure (🚠).

⚙ FAQ 4

Q1. I'm coming to Seoul in early December — can I see snow?

Seoul's snow-day normal is 6.4 days in December (about one day in five) and 7.1 days in January (about one day in four). Days when snow settles are fewer than that. There is no value for early December alone. To raise your chances, put a snow festival in late January to early February or a snowy mountain via gondola in your schedule.

Q2. What snowy scenery can I reach from Seoul as a day trip?

This guide could not confirm rail travel times (check in the Korail+ app). Pyeongchang's Jinbu Station (진부역) (Daegwallyeong, Woljeongsa Temple) and Taebaek Station (태백역) are the train starting points that appear in the festival official guides. For a winter day trip near Seoul without hiking, see #1420.

Q3. Are the snow festivals free?

Daegwallyeong 2026 was adult 10,000 won and child 8,000 won (5-ride snow sledding ticket 5,000 won sold separately), and Chilgapsan 2026 was admission 12,000 won and admission + sled 32,000 won. Taebaeksan's fare is not written on its website. The 2027 fares for all three are before announcement.

Q4. Is February too late?

On the east coast, Bukgangneung (북강릉) peaks in February at 5.9 snow days, and Ulleungdo (울릉도) goes from 17.7 in January to a continued 13.2 in February. The Daegwallyeong 2027 festival also runs until February 9 (planned). However, since Daegwallyeong 2026 had a closing notice, check the notices after mid-February.
