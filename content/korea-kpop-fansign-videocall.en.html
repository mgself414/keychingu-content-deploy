🎯 Why a "Fansign·Video Call" Guide

A fansign (paentsa / 팬싸)·video call (yeongtong / 영통) = an event where you buy albums to enter, and a lottery lets you meet your bias 1:1. The biggest foreign-fan misconception = "it's like a fan meeting — just buy a ticket." In reality it's an album-purchase-quantity-based entry·lottery, split into offline fansigns / online video calls / mini fan meetings. Pain points = (a) not knowing the entry concept·platforms (b) which album site to enter through (c) win odds·quantity strategy (d) how video calls/fansigns run·language. "kpop fansign how to / video call event" is searched constantly on Reddit·YouTube.

This guide covers the entry concept + album platforms + odds·strategy + video call vs fansign + etiquette matrix.

✈️ How to Enter (foreigner practical)

  • Where to enter: buy albums at the group's designated album platform (Music Korea·Withmuu·Soundwave etc.) → submit the entry form
  • Odds·strategy: more albums = higher win odds·random draw·set a budget cap (avoid overspending)
  • Video call flow: if you win, designated app·assigned time·1~2 min·short greeting·questions·tidy background
  • Payment: albums by card·overseas payment·entry itself is included in the album price·no separate fee
  • Emergency: ☎ 1330 (tourism)·112·119

⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "Just buy a ticket": Fansigns·video calls are album-purchase lottery·not ticket sales.

2. "Enter and you win": Random draw·more albums helps but nothing is guaranteed.

3. "Can't do it from abroad": Video calls work for overseas fans·no visit·run via a designated app.

4. "Any album shop": Only the group's designated platform counts·check overseas shipping.

5. "Overspend to win": Beware overspending·set a budget cap·healthy fan activity.

⚙ FAQ (6)

1. Can foreigners enter?: Yes·especially video calls are best for overseas fans·no visit.

2. How to enter?: Buy albums at the group's designated platform → submit entry form → lottery.

3. Win odds?: Proportional to album quantity·random draw·no guarantee·manage budget.

4. How does a video call work?: If you win, designated app·assigned time·1~2 min video·greeting·questions.

5. Language?: Simple Korean/English·prep translation phrases·your bias tries too.

6. Recommended personas: Overseas = video call / visiting = fansign·mini fan meeting / collecting = albums·photocards.

⚠ Medical·Safety·Spending Disclaimer

Beware overspending — buying bulk albums for better odds should stay within a budget cap. Healthy fan activity encouraged. Beware scams·resale outside designated platforms. Manage personal info (video app·address). Minors need guardian consent. Emergency ☎ 1330·112·119.
