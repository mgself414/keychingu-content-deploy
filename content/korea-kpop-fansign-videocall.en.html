Fansign and Video Call Entry Guide for Foreigners — Counting Entries, KakaoTalk Calls and English-Name Rules from 5 Weverse Shop and Ktown4u Notices (Checked October 2026)

Subtitle: An album counts as one entry per copy, not per order (TXT official notice) · Video calls run on KakaoTalk for 1 minute to 1 minute 30 seconds (TREASURE and YEONJUN official notices) · Foreigners enter only under the same English name as on their ID, and the physical passport or Alien Registration Card (외국인등록증) is checked (TXT and TREASURE official notices) · Once winners are announced, entry items cannot be refunded (each official notice) — as of 2026-10-21

ISSUE 04 · #1223 · 2026-07-03

🎯 How to Use This Guide

It is nine at night. A KakaoTalk video call lights up your phone, and a 1 minute 30 second clock starts running (TREASURE [NEW WAV] official notice, 2026-06-25 21:00). A fansign (팬사인회) or video call fansign (the fan slang is "fansign" and "video call", 팬싸 and 영통 in Korean) is not a ticket you buy. You buy designated albums at a designated shop, each copy you buy becomes one entry, and only people picked in a draw get ID-checked and take part.

This guide covers what to check in the notice before you press the entry button, for fans living abroad or staying in Korea. The basis is 5 notices that the agencies posted on Weverse. Every round quoted here has already ended, and dates, headcounts and album versions change from round to round. For your next entry, read that round's notice from the top.

🛒 Where to Enter — Only the Shops the Notice Names

You can enter only with the designated version of the album, bought at the shop the notice names. The same album bought at a different store does not count for that round.

  • Weverse Shop (위버스샵) — The operator is Weverse Company Inc., and it has an English page (Weverse Shop official). The TXT and YEONJUN rounds accepted only albums bought on Weverse Global (TXT official notice)
  • Ktown4u (케이타운포유) — The menu has Event and Notice boards (Ktown4u official). The TREASURE [PLEASURE] round named the Ktown4u online mall KR·EN·CN as its shop, and announced winners in a notice on the Ktown4u homepage (TREASURE official notice)
  • Version restrictions — The [PLEASURE] video call covered only BLUE VER., and the [NEW WAV] video call only RAW VER. (TREASURE official notice). The YEONJUN round counted only the [VIDEO CALL FANSIGN] version and Compact Ver. (TXT official notice). For the TXT 4th full album, the designated albums for the video call and the in-person fansign were different (TXT official notice)

Entry rules for other album retailers could not be confirmed on their official sites, so they are not listed.

🔢 How Entries Are Counted — One Album, One Entry

  • Copies, not orders — The number of entries is the number of albums bought, not the number of orders. A set product counts as the number of albums inside it (TXT official notice)
  • Multiple orders add up — Buy 3 copies at once and 5 on another day, and the same ID gets 8 entries (TREASURE official notice)
  • Only payments completed inside the entry period — The [PLEASURE] in-person round ran 2025-02-24 11:00 to 2025-02-27 23:59 (KST), by completed payment (TREASURE official notice). [NEW WAV] ran 2026-06-06 11:00 to 2026-06-08 23:59, only three days (TREASURE official notice)
  • Automatic entry vs. manual entry — The Ktown4u rounds and [NEW WAV] entered you automatically with the purchase (TREASURE official notice). For the TXT 4th full album, you had to go to the Weverse entry page with the account that made the purchase and type your name, date of birth, phone number and KakaoTalk ID (video call only) yourself, and could not edit them after submitting (TXT official notice)
  • Cancel and you lose entries — Cancelling every order used for entry cancels the entry too (TXT official notice), and the YEONJUN round stated that cancelling only some leaves entries equal to the albums that remain (TXT official notice)

The notices do not say how many copies it takes to win. It is a draw, so no number of copies guarantees a win.

📱 Video Calls — Half the Battle Is Your KakaoTalk Account

  • App — The TXT 4th full album, [NEW WAV] and YEONJUN rounds used KakaoTalk video calls (TXT and TREASURE official notices). The [PLEASURE] round used WeChat or KakaoTalk (TREASURE official notice)
  • One ID, the one you entered — [NEW WAV] stated that calls go only to the KakaoTalk ID entered at application and that it cannot be changed later (TREASURE official notice)
  • Friend-add settings — The YEONJUN round said to keep KakaoTalk "Allow ID search" and "Allow friend add" turned on, and to be addable until 2 hours before the start (TXT official notice)
  • Length — About 1 minute per member and 5 minutes in total (TXT 4th full album), 1 minute 30 seconds ([PLEASURE], [NEW WAV]), and 1 minute (YEONJUN) (each official notice)
  • If you miss the call — After two failed connection attempts the call moves to the next winner, and one more attempt is made at the end. If you still do not answer, you cannot take part (TXT and TREASURE official notices). The notice told fans to check their network in advance (TREASURE official notice)
  • During the call — Staff verify your identity before the call, and the call is recorded and monitored. Inappropriate words or behavior get the call cut off immediately (TXT official notice)
  • Shipping the signed album — Open one of the albums you bought, have it signed, and send it to INNOMAS within 2 weeks after the event. Costs from a wrong address, unpaid customs duty or a refused delivery are the buyer's (TXT official notice). In the YEONJUN round you could not choose which version gets signed, and recipients in the US pay customs duty themselves (TXT official notice)

✍ In-Person Fansigns — The Venue Goes Only to Winners

  • Venue not public — The TXT 4th full album in-person fansign (2025-07-28) and the [PLEASURE] in-person fansign (2025-03-15) told the venue only to winners (TXT and TREASURE official notices). Before entering, first work out whether you can be in Korea on that date
  • Announcement time — [PLEASURE] announced at 2025-02-28 16:00, and the TXT 4th full album after 2025-07-21 20:00 (each official notice). From announcement to event, TXT gave a week and [PLEASURE] a little over two weeks
  • Admission for you alone — Only the winner may enter, and proxy pickup or transfer is not allowed. Violators are removed and may be excluded from later events (TREASURE official notice)

🪪 Rules on Foreign Names and ID

  • English name as is — Foreigners enter with the English name printed on their passport or ID, and Korean-script names are not accepted (TXT official notice). [NEW WAV] stated that the English name you enter must match your ID exactly (TREASURE official notice)
  • Accepted ID — For foreigners, a passport or an Alien Registration Card (외국인등록증) (TXT and TREASURE official notices)
  • Physical only — Photos of ID, printouts, screenshots of mobile ID and expired ID are not accepted. Name, date of birth and photo must match the entry details (TREASURE [NEW WAV] official notice)
  • Age — TXT 4th full album required age 14 or older (TXT official notice). The YEONJUN round required age 13 or older on the entry date, but if you fall below the regional threshold (14 in Korea, 13 in the US, 16 elsewhere), you cannot enter even with a guardian's consent (TXT official notice). [PLEASURE] required guardian consent before purchase for anyone born in 2007 or later (TREASURE official notice)

🧰 Practical Entry Tips — Solo, Prep, Payment, Shipping

  • Solo: Participation is one person at a time, and only the winner takes part (each official notice). Entering by proxy differs by round. [NEW WAV] and [PLEASURE] allowed proxy entry but only the winner may take part (TREASURE official notice), while the YEONJUN round banned proxy entry and third-party entry-service sites and listed them as grounds for cancelling a win (TXT official notice)
  • Prep (booking): Some rounds had an entry period of only three days (TREASURE official notice). Line up your Weverse account, shop account, English name and KakaoTalk ID in advance
  • Payment: I could not confirm which foreign-card payment methods Weverse Shop and Ktown4u offer on the pages I opened. For Korean payment in general, see Foreigner's Korea Payment System Deep Dive
  • Shipping: The Ktown4u site shows FEDEX and SF EXPRESS tracking labels (Ktown4u official). The signed album arrives after the event, separately from the one you bought (TXT and TREASURE official notices)
  • Getting there (if you win in-person): For transit cards, see Korea's 3 Transit Cards, a Zero-Panic Manual — T-money, WOWPASS or Climate Card, Which to Buy

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Buy a lot and you will win" — The notices say only that you get one entry per copy bought. It is a draw, and once winners are announced, entry items cannot be refunded (TXT and TREASURE official notices). Spend only what you can afford to lose

2. "Any version will do" — Each round counts only its designated versions. The [NEW WAV] video call covered only RAW VER. (TREASURE official notice)

3. "I can fix my KakaoTalk ID later" — Entry details cannot be changed after submission (TXT and TREASURE official notices)

4. "I will show my ID as a photo on my phone" — Only a physical passport or Alien Registration Card (외국인등록증) is accepted (TREASURE official notice)
