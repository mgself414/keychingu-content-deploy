⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Temple food = same as vegan": Temple food = Vegan + Oshinchae (garlic, green onion, chives, Dalrae, Heunggeo) not used + practice philosophy Difference. For regular vegans, garlic and green onions are OK and temple food is NO.

2. "Balwoo Gongyang = Michelin, so it's only expensive": Balwoo Gongyang = Seon, Won, Yeom, Hee, and Beopsik 5 courses 30K~80K + Directly managed by Korean Buddhist Culture Project + English menu/private room for foreigners + 1st priority for foreign guests.

3. "OK without reservation": Balwoo Gongyang·Aseungji·Maji = Reservation required 2-3 days in advance. Mountain Village, Daily Life, Vegan Greetings, Soirum = Walk-in OK (reservation recommended on weekends).

4. "Hyanghyang World = Restaurant": Hyanghyang World = Cooking class experience center, not a restaurant + Direct lecture by a monk + English class for foreigners (Sat) + 10K~20K experience + No. 1 foreign culture enthusiast.

5. "Jingwansa = Like a regular restaurant": Jingwansa = Temple stay or pre-booked mountain temple food experience program Required + Advance application for foreign enthusiasts.
