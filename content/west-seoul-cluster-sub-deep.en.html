🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to visit around Gongdeok and Mapo Station and around Seongsan and Sangam in Mapo-gu, Seoul. They are an alley of pig's trotter (jokbal) and grilled meat restaurants, the old tram terminus where an independence demonstration took place in 1919, Munhwa Bichuk Giji, where oil tanks were turned into a cultural space, the World Cup Stadium and the market near it, and the Korean Film Archive with its film museum. World Cup Park (월드컵공원) and Haneul Park (하늘공원) are in the #741 Western Seoul Park Guide, and Yeouido (여의도) is in the #705 Yeouido Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Gongdeok and Mapo Station — Jokbal Alley, Food Culture Street and Demonstration Site

Gongdeok-dong Jokbal Alley (공덕동 족발골목) [Mallijae-ro 19 (만리재로 19), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ Mapo Tourist Information Center (마포관광정보센터) 02-334-7878] is an alley that started from a small restaurant on one side of a market. A place that boiled sundaeguk (순댓국, blood sausage soup) as meals for market workers and made jokbal (족발) as a snack to go with drinks did well, and jokbal restaurants multiplied around it until it became an alley. It is described as having places that have sold sundaeguk and jokbal for generations.

  • Opening hours·closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Not available.

Mapo Food Culture Street (마포음식문화거리) [Yonggang-dong (용강동), Mapo-gu (마포구)] is an eating alley area also called "Mapo Yonggang Matkkaebi-gil (마포용강 맛깨비길)". Restaurants line both sides of the main road starting from Exit 1 of Mapo Station (마포역). There are many grilled meat restaurants serving jumulleok (주물럭), pork skin (껍데기) and pork ribs (돼지갈비), and there are 37 restaurant alleys in all. Because it was the neighborhood where Tojeong Lee Ji-ham (토정 이지함) lived, the street has a statue of him and fish sculptures.

  • Opening hours·closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Not available.

Mapo Tram Terminus March 1st Movement Demonstration Site (마포전차종점 3·1운동 만세 시위지) [Mapo-daero 20 (마포대로 20), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ Culture and Arts Division of Mapo-gu Office (마포구청 문화예술과) 02-3153-8350] is the stretch from today's Mapo Station (마포역) on Subway Line 5 leading into Mapo Bridge (마포대교). From the Japanese colonial period until the late 1960s, a tram terminus stood here. Around 8:00 PM on March 1, 1919, about 1,000 people gathered here and shouted for independence. There is a memorial monument.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Not available.

📍 Seongsan-dong — Munhwa Bichuk Giji, Seoul World Cup Stadium and Mapo Agricultural and Marine Products Market

Munhwa Bichuk Giji (문화비축기지) [Jeungsan-ro 87 (증산로 87), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ 02-376-8420] is a cultural park made by renovating the former Mapo Oil Stockpiling Base. After the 1973 oil shock, 5 tanks were built in 1976~78 and stored 69,070,000 L of oil, and the base was closed for safety reasons ahead of the 2002 World Cup. In 2013 its present use was decided through a citizen idea contest. The 5 tanks became cultural spaces, and T6, built from the steel plates of a dismantled tank, is a community space. It is described as hosting markets, exhibitions, performances and workshops.

  • Opening hours: The outdoor park is always open. Tanks T1~T6 and Cafe Tank6 are open Tue~Sun 10:00~18:00. Entry to the tanks may be restricted depending on the space use schedule.
  • Closed days: The tanks and cafe are closed every Monday. If a Monday is a public holiday, they are open.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: Available (42 spaces), 150 won every 5 minutes for passenger cars.

Seoul World Cup Stadium (서울월드컵경기장) [Worldcup-ro 240 (월드컵로 240), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ 02-2128-2002] is a stadium built for the 2002 FIFA World Cup. The description gives a maximum of 66,000 people, and the seating entry is registered as 66,704 seats in the main stadium and 1,012 seats in the auxiliary stadium. National team home matches and K League matches are held there.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: At the stadium parking lot (west side), small cars 150 won every 5 minutes, medium cars 300 won, large vehicles 450 won.

Mapo Agricultural and Marine Products Market (마포농수산물시장) [Worldcup-ro 235 (월드컵로 235), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ Merchants' Association (상인회) 02-309-0419] is a two-story market building near Seoul World Cup Stadium (서울월드컵경기장). The description says it opened in 1998, while the opening date entry is registered as 2026-03-12, so the two values do not match. The first floor has seafood, fruit and vegetable shops and a supermarket, and the second floor has a raw fish center and a restaurant area.

  • Opening hours·closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Available.

📍 Sangam-dong — Korean Film Archive

Korean Film Archive (한국영상자료원) [Worldcup-buk-ro 400 (월드컵북로 400), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ 02-3153-2001] is a public institution that started in 1974 as the Korean Film Depository (한국필름보관소), and it is in the Sangam DMC (상암 DMC) complex. It collects and preserves film materials. At Cinematheque KOFA (시네마테크KOFA) it screens domestic and foreign classic, art and independent films, and at the Korean Film Museum (한국영화박물관) you can see the history of Korean film. At the Film Library (영상도서관) you can view film materials.

  • Opening hours: Korean Film Archive 10:00~19:00, Korean Film Museum 10:30~19:00, Film Library 10:00~18:50.
  • Closed days: Every Sunday and Monday, January 1, the Seollal (설) and Chuseok (추석) holidays, founding anniversary day, and Labor Day.
  • Fee: Free.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The Korean Film Archive is closed on Sundays and Mondays — the tanks and cafe at Munhwa Bichuk Giji are closed on Mondays.
  2. The alleys around Gongdeok and Mapo Station have no parking — the Jokbal Alley, the Food Culture Street and the demonstration site are all registered as having no parking.
  3. The tanks at Munhwa Bichuk Giji close at 18:00 — the outdoor park is always open.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which places are free to see?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, Munhwa Bichuk Giji (문화비축기지), the Korean Film Archive (한국영상자료원), the Mapo Tram Terminus March 1st Movement Demonstration Site (마포전차종점 3·1운동 만세 시위지) and the Mapo Food Culture Street (마포음식문화거리) are free.

Q. Can I shop for groceries near the World Cup Stadium?

A. Mapo Agricultural and Marine Products Market (마포농수산물시장) is near the stadium. It sells seafood, fruit and vegetables on the first floor, and the second floor has a raw fish center and a restaurant area.
