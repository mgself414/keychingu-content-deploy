⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Hanok = Bukchon (북촌) only": Buam (부암), Pyeongchang (평창), Jeongneung (정릉), Nakseongdae (낙성대) = Outer Hidden Hanok Top Spot for Enthusiasts.

2. "Seowon (서원 - Confucian academy) = Koreans only": Dosan (도산) and Sosu Seowon (소수서원) (UNESCO 2019) = Top Spot for Foreign Enthusiasts.

3. "Deoksugung (덕수궁) = No Hanok": Jeonggwanheon (정관헌) and Jungmyeongjeon (중명전) = Korea's Top Western-style Hanok + Foreigner Enthusiast Spot.
