Korea Trail Running Course Guide — the Seoul Mountain Trails of Hanyangdoseong Fortress Wall Trail, Bukhansan Dulle-gil and Achasan, the Coastal Paths of Igidae, Jeju Olle Course 7 and Namhae Baraegil, and the Mountain Courses of Gajisan, Mudeungsan and Hyangjeokbong: 20 Places

Subtitle: 20 places for which registered Korea Tourism Organization information matching the voted course was found, among those chosen in the "Trail Running" theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" public vote · The places with a registered course length and travel time include Bukhansan Dulle-gil (3.1~5.2㎞), Seoul Dulle-gil Course 5 Achasan Course (4.6㎞), Namhae Baraegil Course 11 (13.6㎞), Daegwallyeong Old Road (13.4㎞) and Yeongdeok Blue Road Course 3 (17.6㎞) · Gyeongju Namsan, Deogyusan Hyangjeokbong and Mudeungsan have registered hours of use — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide covers 20 of the 100 places chosen in the "Trail Running" (트레일 러닝) theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" (명소발굴 100×100) (candidates recommended by travel experts, voted on by the public), for which registered information matching the voted course was found in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data. The ranks and vote counts in the table are the results of that vote. Places where it is not certain that the Korea Tourism Organization registration matches the voted course, such as Seoul Namsan Dulle-gil (서울 남산 둘레길), Namhansanseong Dulle-gil (남한산성 둘레길), Chiaksan Dulle-gil (치악산 둘레길) and Cheongpungho Jadeurak-gil (청풍호 자드락길), and places for which no registered information was found, such as Jeju Olle Course 10 (제주올레 10코스) and the Hallasan hiking trails (한라산 탐방로), are not included. Sallyeoni Forest Path (사려니숲길), Seogwipo Healing Forest (서귀포 치유의 숲) and Jeolmul Natural Recreation Forest (절물자연휴양림), which are mainly forest paths, are covered in the #1427 Korea Sup-meong, Mul-meong and Barefoot Walking Guide, and Saebyeol Oreum (새별오름) and Yongnuni Oreum (용눈이오름) in the #1423 Jeju Oreum Guide, so they are left out here.

Trail running is exercise done on unpaved paths such as mountain trails. In the text, course lengths and travel times are walking-based values registered with the Korea Tourism Organization. The Korea Tourism Organization information has no explanation of whether a course suits running, and the ranks in the table are only vote results.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Fields with no registered value are marked "No registered value". Where a name or address on the vote list differs from the Korea Tourism Organization registration, the main text says so.

📍 Mountain Trails Reached from Central Seoul — Hanyangdoseong Fortress Wall Trail, Bukhansan Dulle-gil, Achasan and Gwanaksan

Hanyangdoseong Fortress Wall Trail (address registered with the Korea Tourism Organization: San 1-29, Nusang-dong, Jongno-gu / 종로구 누상동 산1-29) is a path that follows the 18.6㎞ loop of the Seoul fortress wall linking Bugaksan (북악산), Inwangsan (인왕산), Naksan (낙산) and Namsan (남산). It is divided into four courses: the Baegak Section (Changuimun to Hyehwamun), the Naksan Section (Hyehwamun to Heunginjimun), the Namsan (Mongmyeoksan) Section (Jangchung Gymnasium to Baekbeom Square) and the Inwang Section (Donuimun site to Changuimun). The vote list gives the address as 229-41 Sindang-dong, Jung-gu (중구 신당동 229-41).

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value.

Bukhansan Dulle-gil is a low-lying, level walking path made by linking existing side trails so that you can walk gently around the foot of Bukhansan (북한산). It has 21 sections over a total of 71.5㎞. Three sections were entered separately in the vote.

  • Section 10 Naesimyoyeok-gil (내시묘역길) (Jingwan-dong, Eunpyeong-gu, from in front of the Bangpae Education Unit to Hyoja-dong Public Cemetery / 은평구 진관동, 방패교육대 앞~효자동공설묘지): 3.5㎞, about 1 hour 45 minutes. There is a Dulle Bridge (둘레교) 8m high.
  • Section 1 Sonamusup-gil (소나무숲길) (Ui-dong, Gangbuk-gu, from the Uiryeong-gil entrance to the upper Solbat Neighborhood Park / 강북구 우이동, 우이령길 입구~솔밭근린공원 상단): 3.1㎞, about 1 hour 30 minutes. Wide and gentle, and some stretches follow Ui Valley (우이계곡).
  • Section 8 Gureumjeongwon-gil (구름정원길) (Bulgwang-dong, Eunpyeong-gu, from the upper Bukhansan Ecological Park to in front of Jingwan Ecological Bridge / 은평구 불광동, 북한산생태공원 상단~진관생태다리 앞): 5.2㎞, about 2 hours 30 minutes. There is a 60m-long deck path (skywalk) crossing the valley above the Gugi Tunnel (구기터널).
  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value for all three sections.

Achasan (213 Gingorang-ro, Gwangjin-gu / 광진구 긴고랑로 213, ☎ Achasan Management Office 02-450-1655) is 295.7m above sea level, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization description it takes about 40 minutes to reach the summit on a hiking trail. From the summit a 360-degree view of Seoul opens out, including Jamsil (잠실), Namsan, Bukhansan and Dobongsan (도봉산). Seoul Dulle-gil Course 5 Achasan Course (from Kkaldakgogae Rest Area to Gwangnaru Station / 깔딱고개 쉼터~광나루역) is 4.6㎞, about 2 hours 10 minutes. The vote list gives it under the name "Achasan Jeongsang-gil Course" (아차산 정상길코스), but it cannot be told from the registered information whether this is the same path as Seoul Dulle-gil Course 5.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Gwanaksan (Daehak-dong area, Gwanak-gu / 관악구 대학동 일대, ☎ Gwanak-gu Leisure City Division 02-879-6552) is 632.2m high. The trail starting at the Seoul National University (서울대학교) entrance is the main one, and there is also a route climbing from behind Gwacheon Middle School (과천중학교). All of Gwanaksan's trails are described as converging on Yeonjudae (연주대), which sits on a cliff. The vote list gives the address as Jungang-dong, Gwacheon-si, Gyeonggi (경기 과천시 중앙동).

  • Opening hours, fee: No registered value. Closed days: Open year-round.

📍 Coastal Paths Along the Sea — Igidae, Jeju Olle Course 7 and Namhae Baraegil

Igidae Coastal Trail (San 25, Yongho-dong, Nam-gu, Busan / 부산 남구 용호동 산25) lies within the Busan National Geopark (부산국가지질공원) and is also a section of Course 2 of Galmaetgil (갈맷길), a Busan coastal path. It runs past Dongsaengmal (동생말), Eoullimadang (어울마당) and Nongbawi (농바위) to Oryukdo Sunrise Park (오륙도 해맞이공원) over rock cliffs, a footbridge and decks. You can walk looking out at Haeundae (해운대), Gwangandaegyo Bridge (광안대교) and Marine City (마린시티), and Oryukdo Skywalk (오륙도스카이워크) is at Sunrise Park at the far end.

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value.

Jeju Olle Course 7 (start: Jeju Olle Visitor Center, 22 Jungjeong-ro, Seogwipo / 서귀포시 중정로 22 제주올레 여행자센터, ☎ 064-762-2190) is a coastal olle running from the visitor center past Beophwan Port (법환포구) to Wolpyeong (월평). It passes Oedolgae (외돌개) and "Subong-ro" (수봉로), a path made with a shovel and pickaxe by olle keeper Mr. Kim Subong (김수봉). Jeju Olle as a whole is 27 courses, 437km.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: No registered value.

Namhae Baraegil Course 11 Darangi Jigeot-gil (Nam-myeon, Namhae-gun, from Gacheon Darangi Village to the Namhae Baraegil Small Art Museum / 남해군 남면, 가천다랭이마을~남해바래길 작은미술관) is a coastal path from Gacheon Darangi Village by way of Sachon Beach (사촌해변) to Pyeongsan Port (평산항). It is 13.6㎞ and takes 5 hours 30 minutes. "Barae" (바래) is described as the work of Namhae people gathering only as much seafood as they needed from tidal flats and rocks in time with the tides. The vote list gives the address as 1193 Impo-ri, Nam-myeon (남면 임포리 1193).

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value.

📍 Mountain Courses — Gajisan, Ulsanbawi, Hyangjeokbong, Mudeungsan, Taebaeksan, Gyeryongsan and Gyeongju Namsan

Gajisan (registered with the Korea Tourism Organization as Gajisan Provincial Park, Sannae-myeon, Miryang / 가지산도립공원, 밀양시 산내면, ☎ Miryang City Hall Forest and Greenery Division 055-359-5297) is a mountain on the border of Miryang, Ulju-gun in Ulsan and Cheongdo (청도). According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is the highest of the mountain group called the "Yeongnam Alps" (영남 알프스), and you can traverse the ridge as far as Unmunsan (운문산). In autumn silver grass fields spread from Seoknamgogae Pass (석남고개) to the summit. The vote list gives the address as Sangbuk-myeon, Ulju-gun, Ulsan (울산 울주군 상북면).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. The registration says to call for details.

Seoraksan Ulsanbawi (1140-115 Seoraksan-ro, Sokcho / 속초시 설악산로 1140-115, ☎ Seoraksan National Park 033-801-0900) is a rocky mountain of six peaks standing like a folding screen nearly 900m above sea level, with a circumference of 4㎞. You start from the Sogongwon (소공원) entrance and climb by way of Sinheungsa Temple (신흥사) and Heundeulbawi (흔들바위).

  • Opening hours: Always open (registered as call for details). Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Deogyusan Hyangjeokbong (Seolcheon-myeon, Muju-gun / 무주군 설천면, ☎ Deogyusan National Park Office 063-322-3174) is 1,614m above sea level, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization description it is the fourth highest peak in South Korea. The Seolcheonbong to Hyangjeokbong section reached by gondola is popular as a short course. Course 1 is 0.6㎞ and Course 2 is 8.5㎞, and a traverse of about 14.8㎞ to Namdeogyusan (남덕유산) is also possible. The vote list gives the address as San 1, Deoksan-ri, Anseong-myeon, Muju-gun (무주군 안성면 덕산리 산1).

  • Opening hours (registered value): Months 4~10 04:00~15:00, months 11~3 05:00~14:00. Entry may be restricted depending on the weather.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Mudeungsan National Park (5 Dongsan-gil 7beon-gil, Dong-gu, Gwangju / 광주 동구 동산길7번길 5, ☎ Mudeungsan National Park Office 062-227-1187) was designated a national park in 2013. The highest peak, Cheonwangbong, is 1,187m above sea level, and the columnar jointed formations of Seoseokdae (서석대), Ipseokdae (입석대) and Gwangseokdae (광석대) are described as its most distinctive feature. The vote list gives the address as 460 Mudeungsancheonwangbong-gil, Buk-gu (북구 무등산천왕봉길 460).

  • Opening hours (registered value): Months 3~11 04:00~17:00, months 12~2 04:00~16:00. The closing time for months 3~11 reads the registered notation "04:00~1700" as 17:00.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Taebaeksan National Park (59 Beonyeong-ro, Taebaek / 태백시 번영로 59, ☎ 033-550-0013) is centered on Yeongbong (1,560m), where the Cheonjedan (천제단) altar stands, followed by Janggunbong (1,567m), Munsubong (1,517m) and Buswaebong (1,546m); the highest peak is Hambaeksan (1,572m). The summit area is known as a colony of Korean yew (주목). The vote list gives the address as Hyeol-dong, Taebaek (태백시 혈동).

  • Opening hours: The registration says it varies by section and to see the website. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Gyeryongsan National Park (327-6 Donghaksa 1-ro, Banpo-myeon, Gongju / 공주시 반포면 동학사1로 327-6, ☎ Gyeryongsan National Park Office 042-820-3100) was designated Korea's second national park in 1968. Centered on the summit Cheonhwangbong (847m), it has 16 peaks and about 10 valleys.

  • Opening hours, closed days: Restricted depending on weather conditions, and the registration says to see the website.

Gyeongju Namsan (Bae-dong, Gyeongju / 경주시 배동, ☎ Gyeongju Namsan Research Institute 054-777-7142) is a mountain made up of more than 40 valleys flowing down from Geumobong (468m) and Gowibong (494m). More than 100 temple sites, more than 80 stone Buddhas and more than 60 stone pagodas are scattered across it, and it is described as an open-air museum.

  • Opening hours (registered value): Months 4~10 04:00~16:00, months 11~3 05:00~15:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Registered as not possible.

📍 Forest Paths, Old Roads and More — Solmaru-gil, Daegwallyeong Old Road, Odaesan Seonjae-gil, Sanmagi Old Road, Mecheowat Forest Path and Yeongdeok Blue Road

Solmaru-gil (233 Dotjil-ro, Nam-gu, Ulsan / 울산 남구 돋질로 233) is a hiking trail linking ridges thick with pine trees. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is known as a "premier 60-ri walking path in the heart of the city", and it has park lights along its whole length, so it is used at night as well. The park lights are turned off late at night. The vote list gives it under the name "Solmaru-gil Namsan Course" (솔마루길 남산코스) with the address San 107-2, Sinjeong-dong, Nam-gu (남구 신정동 산107-2).

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value.

Daegwallyeong Old Road (Gangneung Baugil Section 2, start: 5754 Gyeonggang-ro, Daegwallyeong-myeon, Pyeongchang-gun / 강릉 바우길 2구간, 출발: 평창군 대관령면 경강로 5754) is an intermediate course of 13.4㎞, 6~7 hours, from in front of the New and Renewable Energy Exhibition Hall at the Daegwallyeong summit to Bogwang-ri, Seonsang-myeon, Gangneung (강릉시 선상면 보광리), as written in the registration. It was the road that linked Yeongdong and Yeongseo, and was designated a Scenic Site in 2010. The vote list gives the address as Eoheul-ri, Seongsan-myeon, Gangneung (강릉시 성산면 어흘리).

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value.

Odaesan Seonjae-gil (Jinbu-myeon, Pyeongchang-gun, from Woljeongsa Temple to Sangwonsa Temple / 평창군 진부면, 월정사~상원사) is a forest path of about 9㎞ from Woljeongsa to Sangwonsa. It is described as mostly flat and easy to walk. It is an old road that monks and believers used before the road was built in the late 1960s. The vote list gives the address as 713-77 Odaesan-ro (오대산로 713-77).

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value.

Sanmagi Old Road (1 Myeongtaejae-ro Oesa 4-gil, Chilseong-myeon, Goesan-gun / 괴산군 칠성면 명태재로외사4길 1) is a walking path restored from the old road that ran from Saorang Village (사오랑 마을) to Sanmagi Village (산막이 마을), a mountain village. Most of the route is decking, and it runs along the lake of Goesan Dam (괴산댐). There are two courses.

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value.

Mecheowat Forest Path (755 Seoseong-ro, Namwon-eup, Seogwipo / 서귀포시 남원읍 서성로 755) is a forest path for walking through ranch, grassland and primeval forest together. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, "mecheo" (머체) in Jeju dialect means a place where stones are piled up and shrubs grow thick, and "wat" (왓) means field. It starts along a stone wall and passes a cypress grove (편백나무 군락지) and the Seojungcheon (서중천) lookout. You can also walk with an interpretive guide.

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value.

Yeongdeok Blue Road Course 3 Windy Hill (바람의 언덕) (Gangu-myeon, Yeongdeok-gun, from Gangu Snow Crab Street to Changpomal Lighthouse / 영덕군 강구면, 강구대게거리~창포말등대, ☎ Yeongdeok County Office Culture and Tourism Division 054-730-6349) is a course of 17.6㎞ in total, about 4 hours 55 minutes one way, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization description it is a themed travel course linking Yeongdeok's forest tourist attractions. From Gangu Snow Crab Street it passes the sunrise hiking-trail entrance (해맞이 등산로 입구), Geumjin Cloud Bridge (금진 구름다리) and the Gobulbong entrance (고불봉 입구) to Changpomal Lighthouse. The vote list gives it as "Yeongdeok Blue Road" (영덕 블루로드) with no course number.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.

⚠ 3 Things to Know in Advance

  1. Mountains with hours of use — Deogyusan Hyangjeokbong (months 11~3 05:00~14:00), Gyeongju Namsan (months 11~3 05:00~15:00) and Mudeungsan (months 12~2 04:00~16:00) have registered hours. Entry to Hyangjeokbong may be restricted depending on the weather, and Gyeryongsan is also restricted depending on weather conditions.
  2. Leave plenty of time for long courses — On the registered walking basis, Yeongdeok Blue Road Course 3 takes about 4 hours 55 minutes one way, Daegwallyeong Old Road 6~7 hours and Namhae Baraegil Course 11 5 hours 30 minutes.
  3. Ranks in the table are vote results — The Korea Tourism Organization information has no official explanation of whether a course suits running.

⚙ FAQ 3

Q. Which courses are in Seoul?

A. Hanyangdoseong Fortress Wall Trail, Bukhansan Dulle-gil Sections 1, 8 and 10, Achasan and Gwanaksan. Of the registered lengths, Bukhansan Dulle-gil Section 1 (3.1㎞) is the shortest.

Q. Which places are registered as courses along the sea?

A. Busan Igidae Coastal Trail, Jeju Olle Course 7 and Namhae Baraegil Course 11. Yeongdeok Blue Road Course 3 is written in the Korea Tourism Organization description as a course linking forest tourist attractions.

Q. Who was 1st in the vote?

A. Gajisan (145 votes). According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is the highest mountain of the "Yeongnam Alps" group. 2nd was Busan Igidae Coastal Trail (125 votes).
