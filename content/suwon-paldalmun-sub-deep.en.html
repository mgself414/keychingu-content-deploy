Suwon Market Guide — Paldalmun Market, Suwon Tongdak Street, Jidong Market Sundae, Motgol Market

Subtitle: Paldalmun Market (팔달문시장), the "market made by a king" · Suwon Tongdak Street (수원통닭거리), about 11 cauldron-fried chicken shops · Jidong Market (지동시장), 20-plus sundae specialty restaurants · Motgol Comprehensive Market (못골종합시장), 09:00–19:00 · Yeongdong Market (영동시장), hanbok — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Suwon has traditional markets and food alleys of different character. Paldalmun Market began in the reign of King Jeongjo (정조), Suwon Tongdak Street fries whole chickens in cauldrons, Jidong Market is famous for its sundae alley, Motgol Comprehensive Market focuses on side dishes and cooking ingredients, and Yeongdong Market is known for hanbok.

This guide covers only the markets and the tongdak street, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Suwon Hwaseong and Haenggung Palace (행궁) are covered in the Related Content below, and hanbok shopping at Yeongdong Market is covered separately in #624 Suwon Yeongdong Market.

👑 Paldalmun Market — The "Market Made by a King"

Paldalmun Market at 776-2 Jeongjo-ro, Paldal-gu, Suwon (수원시 팔달구 정조로 776-2), ☎ 031-251-5153, is called the "market made by a king" together with Yeongdong Market. The story goes that King Jeongjo of Joseon summoned the descendants of Gosan Yun Seondo (고산 윤선도), who had run a trading business in Haenam, Jeolla-do, to Suwon, and, as merchants from all over the country began to turn their attention to Suwon, the market took shape. It is a market that has shared its history with Suwon Hwaseong.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by stall.
  • Zones: Divided into the tongdak street starting at Maehyanggyo Bridge (매향교), the furniture street, and the fashion street inside the shopping arcade. The fashion street has many youthful, trend-minded budget clothing shops.
  • What they sell: Clothing, food, and sundries. There is also plenty of street food.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available. The market also runs a merchants' broadcasting station.

🍗 Suwon Tongdak Street — Chicken Fried in Cauldrons

Suwon Tongdak Street at 16 Jeongjo-ro 800beon-gil, Paldal-gu, Suwon (수원시 팔달구 정조로800번길 16), information ☎ Suwon Comprehensive Tourist Information Center 031-5191-4672, is a small alley 100m long on Paldal-ro. About 11 shops fry chicken here, from the original shop that opened in 1970 to newer ones.

  • How it is fried: Pouring oil into a large cauldron and frying is the tradition of this alley.
  • Menu: Two choices, fried and seasoned.
  • Well-known shops (per the tourism organization): Maehyang Tongdak (매향통닭), which has kept its place in the alley since its stall days in the 1970s, plus Yongseong Tongdak (용성통닭) and Jinmi Tongdak (진미통닭).
  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Parking is available.

🥢 Jidong Market — Sundae Alley and an Art Space

Jidong Market at 19 Paldalmun-ro, Paldal-gu, Suwon (수원시 팔달구 팔달문로 19), ☎ 031-256-0202, is an old permanent market that grew up against the backdrop of the Suwon fortress wall.

  • Jidong sundae: With 20-plus sundae specialty restaurants gathered here, it is counted as one of Korea's top three sundae alleys, together with Sundae Town in Sillim-dong (신림동), Seoul, and Sundae Town at Anyang Jungang Market (안양 중앙시장).
  • What they sell: Farm products, seafood, and livestock products.
  • Jidong Art Forum (지동 아트 포라): An art creation space on the market's 2nd floor, where, per the tourism organization, 6 artists working in illustration, ceramics, and painting have studios.
  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by stall.
  • Facilities: Restrooms are available. Parking is free for 1 hour, then 150 won per 10 minutes.

🧺 Motgol Comprehensive Market — Side Dishes and Ingredients

Motgol Comprehensive Market at 10-12 Suwoncheon-ro 258beon-gil, Paldal-gu, Suwon (수원시 팔달구 수원천로258번길 10-12), ☎ 031-246-5638, is a comprehensive market selling mainly cooking ingredients such as side dishes, rice cakes, fish, vegetables, meat, and dried seafood. Its area is about 2,700㎡.

  • Opening hours: 09:00–19:00, varying by stall.
  • Closed days: Open year-round, varying by stall.
  • Facilities: Restrooms are available.

👘 Yeongdong Market — Hanbok and Fabric

Suwon Yeongdong Market at 6 Suwoncheon-ro 255beon-gil, Paldal-gu, Suwon (수원시 팔달구 수원천로255번길 6), ☎ 031-251-0171, began as a cattle market in the reign of King Jeongjo and has a history of more than 200 years. Of its 300-plus shops, 40-plus are hanbok shops.

  • Opening hours: 09:00–20:00. Closed days vary by stall.
  • What they sell: Hanbok, fabric, textiles, clothing, and sundries.
  • Cards: Accepted (varies by shop).
  • For details, see #624 Suwon Yeongdong Market.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Assuming the tongdak street menu is varied — According to the tourism organization, the menu is two choices, fried and seasoned.
  2. Assuming every market opens at the same time — Motgol Comprehensive Market is 09:00–19:00 and Yeongdong Market is 09:00–20:00, while Paldalmun Market and Jidong Market vary by stall.
  3. Assuming parking at Jidong Market is free — It is free for up to 1 hour, then 150 won is added every 10 minutes.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where do I go to eat sundae?

A. Jidong Market. 20-plus sundae specialty restaurants are gathered there.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. According to the tourism organization, Yeongdong Market accepts cards, varying by shop. No card information is registered for Paldalmun Market, Jidong Market, or Motgol Comprehensive Market.
