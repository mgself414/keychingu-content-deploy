Foreigner Gangnam cafe/dessert deep dive

> Apgujeong·Dosan·Cheongdam·Sinsa luxury cluster — 15 design cafe·specialty·Michelin desserts

Gangnam is the pinnacle of Korean luxury and design. Apgujeong Rodeo, Dosan Park, Cheongdam-dong, and Sinsa-dong Garosu-gil are dense with specialty cafes, luxury desserts, design cafes, and Michelin desserts. This guide includes 15 locations suitable for foreign segments·special occasion·MZ persona + daily movement matrix.

1. Gangnam cafe distribution

  • Apgujeong Rodeo: Trendy, MZ, and desserts (cf. Garosu-gil, Sinsa)
  • Dosan Park·Dosan-daero: Design cafe·warehouse·Michelin cluster
  • Cheongdam-dong: Luxury/special occasion fit
  • Shinsa Garosu-gil: Trendy, Indie, Intermediate
  • Samsung·Yeoksam: Business·specialty
  • Jamsil Signiel: 555m·infinity view (★ special)
