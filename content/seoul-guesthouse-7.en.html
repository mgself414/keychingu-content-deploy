(b) A booking matrix for foreign enthusiasts using the guesthouse categories on Hostelworld and Booking.com. ⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Guesthouses are too dirty or noisy": Modern guesthouses are the #1 matrix for cleanliness, WiFi, shared kitchens, lounges, and washing machines. Hostelworld reviews 8.0+ indicate an enthusiast matrix + foreigner fit. Choose between dormitory (multiple people) or private (individual room) matrix.

2. "Guesthouses are difficult to book": There are 3 official channels: (a) Hostelworld = #1 matrix for foreign backpackers (b) Booking.com / Agoda = English interface + card payment (c) Airbnb = guesthouse-like rooms. For foreign enthusiasts, Hostelworld is recommended.

3. "Can I communicate in English at guesthouses?": All 7 guesthouses have English-speaking staff and English check-in, making them the #1 matrix for foreign backpackers. Hongdae (홍대), Itaewon (이태원), and Myeongdong (명동) have many foreign staff. Jongno (종로), Gangnam (강남), Sinchon (신촌), and Dongdaemun (동대문) are all English-friendly.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Best itinerary recommendations for foreign guesthouses: (a) Nightlife = Hongdae (홍대) (1), Itaewon (이태원) (2), Sinchon (신촌) (6) (b) Shopping & City Center = Myeongdong (명동) (3), Dongdaemun (동대문) (7) (c) Hanok (한옥) & City Center = Jongno (종로) (4) (d) Business = Gangnam (강남) (5). For foreign enthusiasts, prioritize your preferred itinerary.

2. Dormitory vs. Private Room: (a) Dormitory (₩30,000-70,000/night, ~$22-52 / ~160-380 RMB / ~3,200-7,500 JPY) = 4-12 people shared + social matrix for foreign backpackers (b) Private Room (₩60,000-130,000/night, ~$44-96 / ~320-700 RMB / ~6,500-14,000 JPY) = individual room + shared or private bathroom/shower matrix. For foreign enthusiasts, a dormitory is recommended for the first visit, followed by a private room for subsequent visits.

3. Guesthouse Etiquette: (a) Check-in = 14:00-16:00 (2-4 PM) · Check-out = 10:00-11:00 (10-11 AM) (b) Dormitory = quiet bedding + headphones (c) Shared kitchen & lounge = clean after use (d) Shower & toilet = keep short + clean (e) WiFi & lockers are standard (f) English-speaking staff = OK at all 7 locations.

4. Foreigner Guesthouse Seasons: Spring blossoms (April in Jongno (종로) & Sinchon (신촌)) / Summer (June-August for Hongdae (홍대) nightlife) / Autumn foliage (October in Jongno (종로) & Insa (인사)) / Winter (December-February offers 30-50% off, best prices). For foreign enthusiasts, spring and autumn are peak seasons, while winter offers enthusiast prices.
