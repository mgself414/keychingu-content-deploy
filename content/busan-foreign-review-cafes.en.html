13 Busan Cafes — Only Where Foreign Reviews Showed Up First

Subtitle: A rooftop cafe and a hanok cafe on the Gijang coastal road, a rooftop cafe, a hanok cafe, and a 5th-floor coffee bar in the old downtown of Jungang-dong and Nampo-dong, a hanok cafe on Mangyang-ro in Choryang, a gallery cafe in Huinnyeoul Village on Yeongdo, a dessert cafe and a coffee shop in Seomyeon and Jeonpo, a drip-coffee shop by Oncheoncheon Stream in Dongnae, an ocean-view cafe in Millak-dong, a hand-drip cafe in Namcheon, and a weekend-only traditional tea house in Daeyeon-dong. Only cafes where foreign-language reviews appeared first on Naver Map made the cut — 13 of them, grouped into 4 zones. Busan's zones are far from one another, so the routes are four courses, one zone per day. Gwangalli·Namcheon is in #1381, Seomyeon·Jeonpo is in #246, and roasteries are in #1321.

🎯 Why "Foreign-Language Reviews" Is the Bar

There is one selection rule — cafes on Naver Map (네이버 지도) with English, Japanese, and Chinese reviews left first. Instead of Korean popularity rankings, we picked places foreign visitors have already found and left traces at. The judgment is that such a place is less unfamiliar for the next foreign visitor. Of the 13, 12 have reviews in all three languages. Nasub (나수브) has 8 reviews in English, Japanese, and Chinese (5·3·0), with no Chinese reviews. Copenhagen (코펜하겐) and Cafe Dennis (카페데니스) have 2 reviews each in Japanese and Chinese, so the evidence is on the thin side. Besides the Huinnyeoul branch, Cafe Maren (카페마렌) has a same-named branch on Yeongdo, and the reviews are not split by branch, so it is unclear whether they belong to the Huinnyeoul branch. Review counts are a 2026-11 snapshot, listed for every venue. None of the 13 venues in this piece appeared in the body of other KeyChinGu pieces.

① Gijang — 2 Venues

Both are in Gijang-eup, east of Haeundae. Cafe Coralani (코랄라니) is about 1.2 km from Songjeong Station on the Donghae Line, and Ha.nok (하녹) has no nearby station shown by Kakao Map. Both are too far to walk from a station, so before you set out, look up a bus or taxi route with Kakao Map directions. Check the move between the two on the day with the directions as well.

🗺 Route — One Zone Per Day, Four Courses

Busan's zones are far from one another, so doing one zone per day involves less travel. Gijang, Yeongdo, and Choryang are far from a station or Kakao Map does not show a station, so check the moves between venues and the time they take with Kakao Map directions on the day.

A. Gijang one day

  1. Morning to lunch Ha.nok (하녹) in Gijang-eup (from 11:00)
  2. Afternoon to evening Cafe Coralani (코랄라니) on the Gijang coastal road (until 22:00)

On weekdays Ha.nok closes at 20:00, so go to Ha.nok first.

B. Old downtown·Choryang·Yeongdo one day

  1. Morning Yeongyeongjae (연경재) in Jungang-dong (from 08:30)
  2. Late morning to afternoon Choryang 1941 (초량1941) in Choryang-dong (10:30–18:00)
  3. Afternoon Cafe Maren Huinnyeoul (카페마렌 흰여울점) on Yeongdo (10:00–22:00)
  4. Evening Ryuis Coffee Bar (류이즈 커피바) in Nampo-dong (until 22:00, last order 21:30) or Good Old Days Cafe (굿올데이즈카페) in Jungang-dong (until 21:00, last order 20:30)

Choryang 1941 closes at 18:00, so do not put it late in the afternoon.

C. Seomyeon·Jeonpo·Dongnae one day (except Wednesday)

  1. Morning Cafe Dennis (카페데니스) in Jeonpo (08:00–18:00)
  2. Afternoon Lazy Moment Coffee Stand (레이지모먼트 커피스탠드) in Suan-dong, Dongnae (11:00–19:00, closed Wed)
  3. Evening Knockout (넉아웃) in Seomyeon (until 24:00, last order 23:00 on Sat·Sun)

D. Suyeong·Nam-gu weekend one day (Sat·Sun)

  1. Morning Copenhagen (코펜하겐) in Namcheon (09:00–18:30)
  2. Afternoon Om South Korea (옴싸우스코리아) in Daeyeon-dong (Sat·Sun 13:00–21:00)
  3. Night Nasub (나수브) in Millak-dong (Sat·Sun until 02:00)

On a weekday, drop Om South Korea and link Copenhagen → Nasub. On Tuesday Copenhagen is closed too, leaving only Nasub in this zone.

Which days are bad: Mon–Fri, Om South Korea is closed. Copenhagen is closed on Tuesday and Lazy Moment on Wednesday. So the Tuesday Suyeong·Nam-gu course leaves only Nasub, and the Wednesday Seomyeon·Jeonpo·Dongnae course leaves only Knockout and Cafe Dennis. The places that close early are Cafe Dennis and Choryang 1941 (18:00), Copenhagen (18:30), Lazy Moment (19:00), and Ha.nok (20:00 on weekdays).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to Om South Korea on a weekday — Om South Korea (옴싸우스코리아) is open only Sat·Sun 13:00–21:00. If you go all the way to Daeyeon-dong Mon–Fri, the door is closed. On Tuesday Copenhagen (코펜하겐) is closed too, and on Wednesday Lazy Moment (레이지모먼트) is. Check the day of the week first, then pick the zone.
  2. Putting an early-closing place late in the afternoon — Cafe Dennis (카페데니스) and Choryang 1941 (초량1941) close at 18:00, Copenhagen at 18:30, and Lazy Moment at 19:00. Put these four in the morning or early afternoon, and for the evening use Knockout (넉아웃) (until 24:00), Nasub (나수브) (until 01:00·02:00), Cafe Coralani (코랄라니), and Cafe Maren (카페마렌) (until 22:00).
  3. Going to a different branch with the same name — Cafe Maren (카페마렌) has other branches besides the Huinnyeoul branch. Lazy Moment Coffee Stand (레이지모먼트 커피스탠드) also has other branches besides the Suan-dong branch. Knockout (넉아웃) is spelled "녹아웃" on its menu board. In your map app, search by this piece's street addresses, not the venue names.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. If I could pick just one? — Four venues have 15 foreign reviews (5·5·5). In the old downtown it is Good Old Days Cafe (굿올데이즈카페) (Exit 1 of Jungang Station, 117 m, daily 10:30–21:00), in Gijang Cafe Coralani (코랄라니) (daily 10:00–22:00), on Yeongdo Cafe Maren Huinnyeoul (카페마렌 흰여울점) (daily 10:00–22:00), and on a weekend Om South Korea (옴싸우스코리아) (Sat·Sun only). Pick the zone closest to where you are staying.
  2. What if I avoid caffeine or milk? — The drinks the owner's description says are caffeine-free are the barley coffee at Nasub (나수브) and the pregnancy tea and children's tea at Om South Korea (옴싸우스코리아). Knockout (넉아웃) has a decaf Americano. Instead of milk, there are the OATLY LATTE (oat) and ALMOND LATTE (almond) at Cafe Dennis (카페데니스), the soy milk walnut latte at Ha.nok (하녹), and the coconut milk milk tea at Om South Korea. If you have a nut or sesame allergy, avoid the black sesame, walnut, almond, pistachio, and peanut menu items in each venue's dietary cues.
  3. Where can I go late at night? What about alcohol? — By registered hours, Nasub (나수브) is open until 01:00 Mon–Thu and 02:00 Fri–Sun, and Knockout (넉아웃) until 24:00. Next are Ryuis Coffee Bar (류이즈 커피바), Cafe Coralani (코랄라니), and Cafe Maren Huinnyeoul (카페마렌 흰여울점) (daily 22:00). The only venue with an alcohol menu item registered is Yeongyeongjae (연경재) (pear makgeolli). Cafe Dennis (카페데니스) has menu items named Beomeosa Ale and DRAUGHT registered with no description, so ask when you order whether they are alcohol. Nasub has a cocktail bar tag, and Ryuis Coffee Bar has whiskey in the Kakao Map summary, but neither has an alcohol menu registered. The legal drinking age in Korea is Korean age 19.
