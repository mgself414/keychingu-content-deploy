5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Thinking Lotte World and Lotte Tower Are the Same Place

Lotte World (롯데월드) = theme park (Adventure + Magic Island) / Lotte Tower (롯데타워) = 123-floor skyscraper (observatory + duty-free) / Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) = shopping mall. All three are near Jamsil Station (잠실역), but each has a separate admission fee. Full set: ₩80,000–120,000.

2. Confusing Lotte World Adventure and Magic Island

  • Adventure (어드벤처) = indoor + some outdoor (less affected by weather)
  • Magic Island (매직아일랜드) = outdoor on Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) — part of Lotte World
  • All-day pass ₩66,000+ = both included / Adventure only ₩59,000

3. Mistiming the Lotte Tower Observatory Visit

Seoul Sky Observatory (서울스카이, floors 117–123) = ₩31,000. Best time: ±30 minutes around sunset — you get both daytime views and the night skyline. Recommend weekday afternoons between 4–6 PM.

4. Not Knowing Seokchon Lake Is Free

Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) walks are free. Spring cherry blossoms, autumn foliage, and winter illuminations are all free. Even without entering Lotte World, the outdoor lake scenery is a top highlight.

5. Not Planning Connections to Other Neighborhoods

Jamsil (잠실) is at the eastern edge of Gangnam — Gangnam (강남) and Myeongdong (명동) are 30 min–1 hour away by subway. Plan a full day in Jamsil and don't try to combine with distant neighborhoods. Garak Market (가락시장) and Olympic Park (올림픽공원) are the best nearby add-ons.

★ Jamsil Full Set — Lotte World + Lotte Tower + Seokchon Lake

1️⃣ Lotte World Adventure (롯데월드 어드벤처) + Magic Island (매직아일랜드)

Location: Jamsil Station (잠실역) Exit 4 (underground direct access)

Admission:

  • All-day pass (adult): ₩66,000 (Adventure + Magic Island)
  • Adventure only: ₩59,000
  • Evening ticket (from 16:00): ₩47,000
  • Foreigner package: ₩60,000 (English guide included)

Highlights:

  • Indoor Adventure (실내 어드벤처) = minimal weather impact (Korea's only fully indoor theme park)
  • Magic Island (매직아일랜드) = outdoor rides on Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) with Lotte World characters
  • Popular rides: Gyrodrop (자이로드롭), Atlantis (아트란티스), French Revolution (후렌치레볼루션)
  • Aquarium requires separate ticket (combo with Seoul Sky available)
  • English guide and English app supported

Tips:

  • Weekday entry = 30–60 min wait
  • Weekend entry = 1–2 hour wait
  • Foreigner fast track = extra ₩30,000

2️⃣ Lotte Tower — Seoul Sky Observatory (롯데타워 서울스카이)

Location: Jamsil Station (잠실역) Exit 1, directly connected (inside Lotte World Mall)

Admission:

  • Seoul Sky (서울스카이), floors 117–123: ₩31,000 (day, sunset, and night views all great)
  • Foreigner discount pass: ₩28,000

Highlights:

  • World's 5th tallest · Korea's #1 building (555 m · 123 floors)
  • Floor 117 glass-bottom Sky Deck — #1 Instagram spot for foreign visitors
  • ±30 min around sunset = best timing for both day and night views
  • Café and gift shop on floor 116

Tips:

  • Buy a ₩10,000 observatory café drink before entry = seated viewing + beverage
  • Photography OK (no tripods)

3️⃣ Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) Walk

Location: Jamsil Station (잠실역) Exit 7, 5-minute walk

Admission: Free

Highlights:

  • 2.5 km lakeside path (30–40 min walk)
  • Spring cherry blossoms (early April) · autumn foliage · winter illuminations — seasonal highlight #1
  • Rubber Duck (러버덕) (seasonal installation) = top Instagram spot for foreign visitors
  • 5-minute walk from Lotte World and Lotte Tower
  • Night views + Lotte Tower reflection in the lake = best photo op

One Line: "Jamsil's free bonus — four-season scenery + Lotte Tower (롯데타워) reflection"

④ Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) + Duty Free

Location: Jamsil Station (잠실역) Exit 1, directly connected

Admission: Free

Highlights:

  • Korea's largest shopping mall (basement–6th floor)
  • Korean and global brands + duty-free shop (Jamsil branch)
  • Food court with Korean, Japanese, and Western options
  • Coin lockers + wheelchair rental = family-friendly for foreign visitors

⑤ Garak Market (가락시장) — Korea's Largest Wholesale Agricultural & Seafood Market

Location: Line 8 Garak Market Station (가락시장역) — 1 stop from Jamsil Station (잠실역)

Highlights:

  • 4:00–10:00 AM = wholesale trading (not recommended for tourist visits)
  • 9:00 AM–6:00 PM = retail open to public (foreigner OK)
  • Seafood restaurants (회·해산물 식당) popular with foreign visitors
  • 10 minutes from Jamsil by subway or on foot

⑥ Olympic Park (올림픽공원)

Location: Lines 5, 8, 9 — Olympic Park Station (올림픽공원역), 1–2 stops from Jamsil Station (잠실역)

Admission: Free

Highlights:

  • 1988 Seoul Olympics main stadium site
  • 5 km walking paths + lake and sculpture garden
  • 88 Seoul Olympics Memorial Hall (88서울올림픽기념관) with English guides
  • Spring wildflowers and autumn foliage — top seasonal picks

⑦ Songnidan-gil (송리단길) — Jamsil's Rising Café & Restaurant Street

Location: Jamsil Station (잠실역) Exits 2–3, 10-minute walk

Highlights:

  • Jamsil's newest café and restaurant alley
  • Trendy cafés, brunch spots, and dessert shops
  • MZ generation's #1 Instagram destination (Jamsil = family neighborhood + Songnidan-gil = MZ vibes)

⑧ Jamsil Seokchon Lake Night Market (잠실 석촌호수 야시장) — Seasonal

Location: Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) lakeside

Season: Spring, summer, and autumn (April–October, weekends)

Highlights:

  • Street food, handcrafts, and live music
  • Free entry / food ₩5,000–15,000
  • Family-friendly for foreign visitors

8 Notes for Foreign Visitors

✅ Lotte World · Lotte Tower · Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) = separate admission tickets

✅ All-day pass vs. evening ticket — price differs by time slot

✅ Lotte Tower (롯데타워) ±30 min around sunset = best timing

✅ Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) is free — check seasonal events (cherry blossoms · Rubber Duck · illuminations)

✅ 100% card payment + foreign cards accepted

✅ Wheelchair rental = Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) 1F information desk

✅ Stroller rental = Lotte World (롯데월드) entrance

✅ After midnight: use Kakao T (카카오T) — subway service ends

FAQ

Q1. Does English work here?

Lotte World · Lotte Tower · Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) = English, Chinese, and Japanese guides available. Garak Market (가락시장) and Songnidan-gil (송리단길) are mostly Korean, but photo menus are fine.

Q2. Is it good for families with children?

Lotte World (롯데월드) · Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) · Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) = family #1 pick. Wheelchairs, strollers, play areas, and diaper changing rooms all available.

Q3. Only have one day?

Lotte World (10–17:00) + Seokchon Lake (17–18:00) + Lotte Tower (19–21:00) = the perfect full-day answer.

Q4. Worried about the cost?

Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) free + Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) free + Lotte World (롯데월드) evening ticket ₩47,000 = best value combo.

Q5. What if it rains or snows?

Lotte World Adventure (실내 어드벤처, indoor) + Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) + Lotte Tower (롯데타워, indoor) = no impact from rain or snow.

Q6. Can I connect to other neighborhoods?

Jamsil (잠실) is at the eastern edge. Garak Market (가락시장) · Olympic Park (올림픽공원) (10 min by subway or on foot) are fine add-ons. Gangnam (강남) and Myeongdong (명동) are 30 min–1 hour by subway.
