🎯 Why is Seoul’s shaved ice a complete deep dive?

No. 1 summer/MZ/luxury mania for foreigners = Seoul shaved ice deep dive (follow-up to #233 Dessert·#361 Bakery·#385 Four Seasons Summer·#502 Table). 2026 Seoul shaved ice = Global trend (Dubai, Ube, Yangtze Canal Road) + Korean authentic (mugwort, red beans, injeolmi) + Hotel luxury (KRW 130,000-150,000) Simultaneous explosion period.

To foreigners, shaved ice is known only as "Patbingsu = red bean shaved ice," but in 2026, Seoul's shaved ice scene is quickly absorbing global dessert trends such as Dubai chocolate (Pistachio + Kataifi), Ube in the Philippines, and Yangtze Kanru in China, while at the same time a very interesting time in which works that reinterpret traditional ingredients such as mugwort, red beans, and injeolmi are exploding.

⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Bingsu = only red bean shaved ice": 2026 Seoul shaved ice = Dubai Chocolate (KORIYA Seongsu), Ube (MOVEMOVE), Yangzi Kanlu (Orange Songpa) Global Trend + Mugwort (Deer Daon), Red Bean (Red Bean Janggun), Injeolmi (Sulbing) Authentic + Hotel Luxury 130,000 won Full set.

2. "1 shaved ice per person": Korean shaved ice = Average size for 2-3 servings·1 shaved ice per person is too much + Foreign enthusiasts recommended to accompany friends or lovers.

3. "Hotel shaved ice OK without reservation": Shilla the Library·Signiel 79th floor·Four Seasons = Online reservation·Open run (10AM) required + Mango season peaks in July and August.

4. "Shaved ice = Photo slowly": Trend shaved ice = Photo within 1 minute before melting + Dubai Chocolate (KORIYA) Maintains Kadaiif texture within 5 minutes.

5. "Confusion between Injeolmi and Red Beans": Injeolmi = Tteok / Red Beans (Pat) = Red Beans + Basic Korean Dessert Alphabet · Required for foreign enthusiasts.

① 6 trend shaved ice (Trend-Driven 2026)

1. Ohlinzi Orange ★ Yangzikan Road (Songpa)

  • Address: 5th floor, Haven Building, 10 Baekjegobun-ro 41-gil, Songpa-gu
  • Transportation: Walking distance from Songpa Station and Jamsil Station
  • Signature: Yangzi Kanlu (杨枝甘露, Hong Kong, Guangdong) — Mango, grapefruit, carrot tropical
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Signature, Instagram, and blog proof shots that are rare in Korea
  • Pro Tip: Expect only the slightly bitter and sweet taste of grapefruit

2. KORIYA ★ Dubai Chocolate (Seongsu)

  • Address: B1F, 10-20 Wangsimni-ro 4-gil, Seongdong-gu
  • Transportation: 8-minute walk from Seongsu Station Exit 3
  • Signature: Pistachio + Kataifi + Rice Cake + Milk Shaved Ice
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Global SNS trend + Instant familiarity with foreigners
  • Pro Tip: Kataifi loses its appeal when it gets soggy. Take a photo and eat it within 5 minutes of serving

3. MOVEMOVE ★ Uwe Snow (Seongsu)

  • Address: 56 Seongsuil-ro, Seongdong-gu
  • Transportation: 7 minutes walk from Ttukseom Station, 10 minutes walk from Seongsu Station
  • Signature: Philippine purple Ube + studio concept
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — 2026 Ube Trend Original, Starbucks, and CU also launch ube menu
  • Pro Tip: Ube has a deeper sweetness than Korean red beans + coconut flavor. Even if you can’t drink matcha, ube is a challenge

4. Among Aemong ★ Tomato Turtle (Yeonnam)

  • Address: 2nd floor, 13 Yeonnam-ro 3-gil, Mapo-gu
  • Transportation: 7-minute walk from Hongik University Entrance Exit 3. Directly connected to Incheon Airport by Airport Railroad
  • Signature: Daejeo Tomato Sorbet, Turtle Shape, 『Cloud Bread』 Alpha Generation Sensitivity
  • Time: 13:00~21:00 · Closed on Tuesdays
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — 7K Reasonable·Recommended last course before departure
  • Pro Tip: Tomato = vegetable with salty and sweet taste that breaks common sense

5. Moonduck Moonduk Cafe ★ Pineapple (Euljiro)

  • Address: 2nd floor, 42-3, Supyo-ro, Jung-gu
  • Transportation: 3-minute walk from Exit 11 of Euljiro 3-ga Station
  • Signature: Whole pineapple·Pavlova·Old vs. Modern Old meets New
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Typical of Seoul loved by foreigners
  • Pro Tip: Before and after shaved ice Euljiro Nogari Alley and Golbaengi Alley The best atmosphere for a walk or evening

6. Dear Daon ★ Mugwort Pudding (Yeonnam)

  • Address: #101, 33-9 Donggyo-ro 38-gil, Mapo-gu
  • Transportation: 10-minute walk from Hongik University Entrance Exit 3
  • Signature: Mugwort cream + mugwort pudding + milk shaved ice triple layer·Mugwort pudding shaved ice 8,900 won
  • Payment: Seoul Pay OK · Catch Table Reservation
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — mugwort = a luxurious dessert that breaks the image of a medicinal herb
  • Pro Tip: There are actually 2 types of mugwort pudding and custard on the menu. If it’s your first time, definitely mugwort pudding (₩8,900). Pudding gift box OK.

② 6 Hidden Gems

7. Patjanggun Red Bean General ★ Low-sugar red beans (Eunpyeong·Gusan)

  • Address: 1st floor, 150 Yeonseo-ro, Eunpyeong-gu (Daejo-dong)
  • Transportation: 5-minute walk from Gusan Station (Line 6)
  • Signature: No sweetness, just red bean texture·Minimal red bean shaved ice·7~9K cost-effectiveness #1
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Enjoy Deep Local·Japan·Southeast Asia without hesitation
  • Pro Tip: Recommended for gourmets for the true taste of red beans. Expect strong sweetness

8. Dongbinggo Dongbinggo ★ 30 years of authenticity (Ichon)

  • Address: Hyundai Apartment Mall, 319 Ichon-ro, Yongsan-gu
  • Transportation: 5-minute walk from Ichon Station (Line 4, Gyeongui-Jungang Line)
  • Signature: 30 years of authentic red bean + milk ice + glutinous rice cake, Korean red bean shaved ice standard
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Close to Hannam and Itaewon, incorporated into the residential area for foreigners
  • Pro Tip: Busiest between 3 and 5 o'clock, full on weekend afternoons, Connected with a walk at Ichon Hangang Park

9. Bubing Bubing ★ Matcha (Buam-dong)

  • Address: Main branch in Buam-dong, Jongno
  • Transportation: Gyeongbokgung Station (Line 3) → Bus No. 1020/7022
  • Signature: Secluded at the foot of a mountain·"Is there a place like this in Seoul?" Foreigners' reactions #1
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — In addition to the main branch, waiting at Bukchon, Yeonnam branches, and Catch Table
  • Pro Tip: Buam-dong Seokpajeong·Seoul Museum of Art·Changguimun Half-day course·Bukchon branch recommended in case of lack of accessibility

10. Okryumong ★ Multi-store tradition

  • Address: Children’s Grand Park Branch, Eastpole (Guui) Branch, etc.
  • Transportation: Children’s Grand Park Station, Guui Station
  • Signature: Traditional red bean shaved ice + Oreum snowflake shaved ice (mountain shape) + Injeolmi and bean powder classic
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Oriental medicine concept, truly local outside of foreigners’ flow lines
  • Pro Tip: You can move around without an umbrella on a rainy day Eastpole directly managed store Convenient for foreigners

11. Mealtop ★ 40 years of department stores

  • Address: 5th floor, Hyundai Department Store Apgujeong Main Branch, Shinsegae Department Store Main Branch, etc.
  • Transportation: Apgujeong Station, Hoehyeon Station
  • Signature: Milk shaved ice + red bean porridge side, glutinous rice cake, 40-year-old authentic, season limited (Shine Muscat, green tea)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Department store shopping route·#406 duty free integration
  • Pro Tip: Department store business hours 10:30~20:00·Right after lunch from 1~3pm

12. Hotel Bingsu Scene ★ Luxury 130,000~150,000 won

🍯 Foreigner Shaved Ice 5 Tips

1. A bowl for multiple people — Average size for 2-3 people, 1 shaved ice per person

2. Injeolmi vs Red Bean — Injeolmi = rice cake, red bean = red bean, two confusing toppings for foreigners

3. Picture takes less than 1 minute before melting — The trendier the shaved ice, the faster it goes.

4. Almost no cash accepted — Credit card, Samsung Pay, Seoul Pay only, overseas card activated in advance (#421 integrated)

5. Hotel shaved ice reservation required — Almost impossible without Shilla, Signiel, Four Seasons online reservation/open run
