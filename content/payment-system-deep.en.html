How to Pay in Korea — Foreign Cards, ATMs, Currency Exchange, Prepaid Transit Cards, Mobile Payments, Tax Refunds, Chosen by Situation

Subtitle: We lined up the order in which a short-stay traveler picks a way to pay in Korea. How far foreign cards work and where they do not, finding ATMs that accept foreign cards and how fees get added, airports, banks, and exchange booths, prepaid cards such as T-money, WOWPASS, NAMANE, and CHECK iN SEOUL, Alipay+, WeChat Pay, KakaoPay, and Naver Pay, buses and unmanned checkouts that do not take cash, and the limit for instant refunds at the store. Every item ends with "In this case, use this."

ISSUE 03 · #527 · 2026-11-25

🎯 Who This Is For

This piece is for travelers who stay in Korea from a few days to a few weeks. We assumed no Korean bank account and no Korean phone number. This piece does not dig deep into each payment method one by one. It is a map for deciding what to take out in which situation. Step-by-step details for a single card are in separate pieces, so we linked to them at the end of each section.

⚠ Notation rule: Next to every fee, limit, and price we attached the source and the check date. "Official" is used only for values taken from the original page of the operating organization or company website, opened directly. Anything that appears in only one news report is marked "needs confirmation." Payment terms change often, so when real money is at stake, check the app and website again that day. Which shops accept what differs from shop to shop. It is faster to ask before you pay.

① Foreign Cards — Mostly Accepted, but Some Shops Do Not

The Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) English website says most Korean businesses accept credit cards and that Visa, MasterCard, and American Express can be used at large hotels, department stores, and general shops. The same article also says some shops do not accept cards, so check before you buy (VisitKorea official, date shown on the page 2023-03-27, opened 2026-11-25).

  • UnionPay: The UnionPay International website says most merchants in Korea accept signature-type UnionPay cards, and over 3,000,000 (300만) accept PIN-type cards (UnionPay official). This is the company's own announcement, and we could not confirm it from another source. Some shops do not accept it. Setting up overseas use for a card issued in mainland China is written in #1333.
  • JCB: We could not find an official source stating JCB's merchant coverage in Korea (2026-11-25). Do not arrive with only one JCB card. Bring one more card of another brand.
  • Pay in won (KRW): If the card terminal or receipt shows a screen asking whether to charge in your home currency, choose won. Paying in a currency other than the local one adds a second currency conversion and raises the charge (KB Kookmin Card (KB국민카드) overseas-use guide, official). This guide explains using a Korean card abroad, and that the same principle applies to foreign cards is this piece's assumption.

In this case, use this

  • The card was declined → Try a card of another brand. If that still fails, use a prepaid card (④) or cash.
  • You see no card terminal, as at markets and street stalls → First ask whether they take cash or QR payment. Places where you need cash are collected in #1301.

② Withdrawing From an ATM With a Foreign Card — Pick a Machine That Accepts Foreign Cards

  • Not every machine works: Some ATMs in Korea reportedly do not accept foreign-issued cards. Of several ATMs from the same bank, only some may work, and some banks reportedly have no machine at all that accepts foreign cards. Sometimes a sticker or sign tells you. If there is no sign, see whether the menu asks for the country that issued your card. If only the domestic card menu appears, the machine reportedly will not work (Institute for Basic Science (기초과학연구원) living guide for foreigners, written around 2019 by our estimate, opened 2026-11-25).
  • Support by bank: We could not find an official list from the banks themselves saying which bank's ATMs accept foreign cards (needs confirmation). The UnionPay website says there are more than seventy thousand (7만) ATMs in Korea where you can withdraw won with a UnionPay card (UnionPay official, the company's own number).
  • Fees can come in two layers: The Citibank Korea (씨티은행) and KB Kookmin Card (KB국민카드) guides explain using a Korean card at ATMs abroad. There, the local ATM usage fee is set by the ATM operator and differs by machine (Citibank Korea overseas ATM guide, official), and the card issuer separately charges an international brand fee and a per-transaction fee. For example, the KB Kookmin Card (KB국민카드) debit card has a brand fee of 1.0~1.1% for Visa and 1.0% for Mastercard, plus 3 dollars per transaction (example based on a Korean KB card, KB Kookmin Card official). That a Korean ATM fee and your own issuer's fee are layered when you use a foreign card at a Korean ATM is this piece's assumption. Check the real amounts in #1286 and your own card issuer's guide.
  • Withdrawing with a credit card can become a cash advance: At overseas ATMs, a credit card shows both a cash withdrawal and a cash advance (short-term loan) menu, so choose exactly the menu you want (Citibank Korea official). KB Kookmin Card (KB국민카드) says its credit cards cannot be used for overseas cash withdrawal, only for cash advances (KB Kookmin Card official). Both are guides for using a Korean card abroad. Check with your issuer how your own credit card is handled.

In this case, use this

  • The machine screen shows only a Korean domestic card menu → Move to another machine.
  • The screen shows a fee and asks you to confirm → Look at the amount and go ahead or cancel. If a screen asks you to choose a currency, press won (KRW).
  • For the screen-by-screen procedure and what to do when the card does not come out, see #1286.

③ Currency Exchange — Airport, Banks, Licensed Exchange Booths

The Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) tells you to exchange money at a bank or licensed exchange booth, and says the airport exchange booth is the easiest for most travelers (VisitKorea FAQ, official, opened 2026-11-25).

Incheon Airport (인천공항) bank branches (KB Kookmin (KB국민), Hana (하나), Woori (우리)) Weekdays 09:00~16:00, closed on weekends and public holidays (same page) Available in both Terminal 1 and Terminal 2

  • Some places do not exchange old banknotes (VisitKorea official).
  • There is also a way to load foreign cash straight onto a prepaid card. WOWPASS says it accepts 16 currencies for cash top-ups (WOWPASS official). CHECK iN SEOUL kiosks also accept 16 foreign currencies, but the currencies may differ from kiosk to kiosk (Lotte Card (롯데카드) official, opened 2026-11-22).

In this case, use this

  • You arrive late at night → The standard hours of airport exchange booths are 06:00~22:00, and only some open 24 hours. After 22:00, get by with a card or an ATM that accepts foreign cards and exchange the next day. Airport bank branches close at 16:00 on weekdays.
  • You enter with a lot of cash, or you want to change leftover won back when you leave → The reporting threshold and how to change money back are in #435.

⑤ Mobile Payments — The QR at the Counter Decides Whether Your App Works

  • Alipay+ partner wallets: The Zero Pay standard QR that the Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) promoted is linked to 17 overseas mobile payments including Alipay and WeChat Pay, so you can pay with your home app without installing a Korean app (korea.net, 2024-11-29. A 2024-11 source with no merchant count). The Korea Times named Alipay (mainland China), AlipayHK, Touch 'n Go (Malaysia), PayPay (Japan), MPay (Macau), and GCash (the Philippines) as wallets used to pay through Alipay+ in Korea (reported 2025-12-09, needs confirmation). Whether your wallet works is found in the overseas payment guide of the wallet app.
  • WeChat Pay: It is on the list linked to the Zero Pay standard QR above (korea.net, 2024-11-29). Whether it works at a store without that QR differs from store to store.
  • KakaoPay: KakaoPay said it would start Japan's PayPay payments at domestic KakaoPay offline merchants in September 2025 (Herald Business (헤럴드경제) report 2025-09-24, no merchant count given, needs confirmation). We could not find an official guide on the conditions for a foreigner to sign up directly in the KakaoPay app and use a foreign card (needs confirmation).
  • Naver Pay and Naver Map: Naver introduced passport verification on June 4, 2026. Foreigners with an overseas passport can use reservations, orders, and payments in Naver Map without a Korean phone number (Naver official press release, 2026-06-09). The press release does not say whether foreign card payment is possible. The feature to register an overseas-issued credit card in Naver Pay was only reported as "planned" in May 2025, and we could not confirm it from an official source (Asia Economy (아시아경제) 2025-05-16, needs confirmation).

In this case, use this

  • There is a QR sign at the counter → Check whether your wallet's logo is there and ask "QR gyeolje dwaeyo? (QR 결제 돼요?)" ("Can I pay by QR?").
  • There is no sign → Pay with a card or prepaid card.
  • The order for travelers from the Chinese-speaking world who use Alipay, WeChat Pay, and UnionPay together is in #1333.

⑥ Unmanned Kiosks and Places That Do Not Take Cash

  • Cashless buses: The Seoul city government (서울시) posted a list of "cashless bus" routes on its transport website as of January 2026 (Seoul city official). Namsan Circulation Buses 01A and 01B also do not take cash (Seoul Media Hub (서울시 미디어허브) 2024-11-28). Seoul said it lets passengers without a transit card board first and then pay by bank transfer or mobile T-money, and that it made notices in English, Chinese, and Japanese for foreign passengers (2024-02-15). The Korea Times reported that as of April 2026, 180 of Seoul's 394 bus routes do not take cash (2026-11-11, needs confirmation).
  • Cashless stores: Some stores and bank branches do not take cash. The Bank of Korea (한국은행) wrote in a report that policies to increase cash acceptance are needed (Korea Times report 2026-11-11). Which stores refuse cash differs from store to store, so this piece does not list them.
  • Kiosks: We could not find an official source that generalizes whether ordering kiosks and self-checkouts at restaurants and cafes accept foreign cards (needs confirmation). If the card does not read, go to the staffed counter.

In this case, use this

  • You are taking a bus → Load your transit card before you board (④).
  • The kiosk screen is only in Korean → How to follow the screens in order is in #1270, and self-checkouts and late-night unmanned convenience stores are in #1306.

⑦ Tax Refunds — Instant Refund at the Store, City Refund, Airport Refund

Method Conditions (source)
Common A foreigner staying in Korea less than 6 months, purchase of 15,000 won or more, new goods (unopened and unused), leaving the country within 3 months of the purchase date (VisitKorea official, updated 2025-10-21)
Instant refund at the store 15,000 won or more and under KRW 1,000,000 (100만 원) per payment, total of KRW 5,000,000 (500만 원) or less for the whole trip (VisitKorea official · Special Provisions on Value-Added Tax and Individual Consumption Tax for Foreign Tourists (외국인관광객 등에 대한 부가가치세 및 개별소비세 특례규정), Article 6 ②, Korea Law Information Center (국가법령정보센터))
City refund counter Processes only receipts of the refund company that runs that counter. KRW 6,000,000 (600만 원) or less per receipt. You get the refund on an overseas credit card (including Alipay and WeChat Pay) or in cash right away (VisitKorea official)
Airport and port refund Accepts receipts of all refund companies. No limit on purchases during the stay. Cash is received in the duty-free zone after security screening. If you take the refund on an overseas credit card, Alipay, or WeChat Pay, it takes 1~2 months to arrive. If there is no counter or it is closed, write where to receive it on the receipt and drop it in the designated box. Procedures differ by airport and port, so check with on-site staff (VisitKorea official)

In this case, use this

  • You buy under KRW 1,000,000 (100만 원) at one store → Show your passport when you pay and get the instant refund.
  • You could not get it at a city counter → The airport and port counters accept receipts of all companies. To choose which of the three methods fits your shopping, see #1231. For the airport kiosk screen order, see #1304. For duty-free shopping, see #775.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Choosing your home currency on the payment screen — If the card terminal or ATM asks for a currency, choose won (KRW). If you choose another currency, a second conversion happens (the principle in the KB Kookmin Card (KB국민카드) official guide; applying it to foreign cards is this piece's assumption).
  2. Keep trying at an ATM that does not accept foreign cards — Even among ATMs of the same bank, some machines are set apart for foreign cards. If you see only the domestic card menu, move to another machine (Institute for Basic Science (기초과학연구원) living guide, written around 2019 by our estimate).
  3. Riding a bus without a transit card — Seoul has bus routes that do not take cash (Seoul city official). Before you leave the airport, buy a card with a transit function and load it.
