9 Seoul Late-Night Eats — Tteokbokki, Nogari, Dakhanmari, Gamjatang, and Samgyeopsal Open Until Half Past 10 at Night

Subtitle: 5 spots around Euljiro, Dongdaemun, Sindang, and Yaksu, which are two nogari hofs in Euljiro, a dakhanmari place in Dongdaemun, a 24-hour tteokbokki place in Sindang, and Geumdwaeji Sikdang in Yaksu, plus 4 more, a haejangguk place in Yongsan, a gamjatang place in Seongbuk, a samgyeopsal place in Gyodae, and a chicken place in Buam-dong. Only places whose weekday closing time registered by the owner on Kakao Map is 22:30 or later made the cut. Closing times run from 22:30 to 05:00, and one place is open 24 hours. Some places close early on Sundays, and those are noted separately. Bunsik in general is in #471, and Korean BBQ in general is in #479.

#780 · 2026-11-24 revised · Seoul Late-Night Eats

🎯 Who This Is For

It is aimed at travelers whose evening plans end late and who look for a meal after 10 PM, and at people who want a beer with some side dishes (anju, 안주) near their lodging. There is one selection rule. We kept only places whose weekday closing time registered by the owner on Kakao Map (카카오맵) is 22:30 or later. We also considered well-known gomtang (곰탕), naengmyeon (냉면), and samgyetang (삼계탕) restaurants, but dropped them because they close between 16:00~22:00.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours, closed days, and menu prices are the values the owner registered on Kakao Map (카카오맵) (checked 2026-11-24). Prices are listed only for menus registered within the last 2 years, and older menus are marked "check Kakao Map on the day." Dietary cues are only what can be read from menu names and the owner's descriptions. Ask staff about ingredients and allergies before ordering. Nearby stations are the station, exit, and distance from Kakao Map directions.

① Euljiro·Dongdaemun·Sindang·Yaksu — 5 Venues

Euljiro-samga Station (을지로3가역) and Sindang Station (신당역) are connected by Line 2. The dakhanmari place in Dongdaemun (동대문) is a walk from Dongdaemun Station (동대문역) (Lines 1·4), and Geumdwaeji Sikdang is a walk from Yaksu Station (약수역) (Lines 3·6), so each leg needs a transfer. Starting at a nogari hof, then moving on to dakhanmari and tteokbokki (떡볶이) is the smoothest order.

Wonjo Manseon Hof Nogari Chain Main Branch (원조만선호프 노가리체인본점)

A hof (beer hall, 호프) in Euljiro's nogari alley (노가리골목). It opens daily 12:00~05:00, so apart from the 24-hour place it closes latest of the venues in this piece. Registered prices (2025-10-30) are nogari (노가리, dried young pollock) 2,000 won, seafood ramyeon (해물라면) 13,000 won, soup tteokbokki (국물떡볶이) 13,000 won, fried chicken gizzards (닭똥집튀김) 15,000 won, and semi-dried squid (반건조오징어) 16,000 won. No prices are registered for the chicken menu. The address is 19 Euljiro 13-gil, Unit 2 on Floor 1, Jung-gu (중구 을지로13길 19 1층 2호), 150 m from Exit 3 of Euljiro-samga Station (을지로3가역) (Lines 2·3). Other places in the alley carry the names "Wonjo Manseon Hof (원조만선호프 2)", "Manseon Hof (만선호프)", and "Manseon (만선3)", so search by address.

  • Dietary cues: Nogari is dried young pollock (fish). Squid and seafood ramyeon are seafood, and dakttongjip (닭똥집) is chicken gizzard. The handmade pork cutlet (수제돈까스) is pork. As a hof, it is centered on alcohol. No registered menu reads as vegetarian.

Eulji OB Bear (을지OB베어)

A hof right outside Exit 9 of Euljiro-samga Station, and the Kakao Map tags include long-established shop (노포). It is open Mon–Sat 15:00~23:30 and Sun 15:00~22:00. Registered prices are original nogari & dried pollock (오리지널 노가리&황태) 6,000 won (2026-07-22), dried filefish (쥐포) 10,000 won, silkworm pupae (번데기) 11,000 won, boneless fried chicken (순살 후라이드 치킨) 16,000 won, boneless seasoned chicken (순살 양념치킨) 17,000 won (2026-11-04), and cheese buldak (치즈불닭) 23,000 won. The address is Dongju Building, Floor 1, 49-2 Chungmu-ro, Jung-gu (중구 충무로 49-2 동주빌딩 1층), 56 m from Exit 9 of Euljiro-samga Station.

  • Dietary cues: Nogari, dried pollock, and dried filefish are dried-fish snacks. Beondegi (번데기) is silkworm pupae. The Johnsonville sausage (존슨빌 소시지) is pork. The chicken is chicken meat, and the batter usually contains wheat flour. It is centered on alcohol.

Jinokhwa Halmae Wonjo Dakhanmari Main Branch (진옥화할매원조닭한마리 본점)

A restaurant in Dongdaemun's dakhanmari alley (닭한마리 골목). It is open daily 10:30~01:00, with no days off. The one registered menu is dakhanmari (닭한마리) at 33,000 won (2025-11-28). The address is 18 Jong-ro 40ga-gil, Floors 1~3, Jongno-gu (종로구 종로40가길 18 1~3층), 332 m from Exit 10 of Dongdaemun Station (동대문역) (Lines 1·4). Several dakhanmari places with similar names are nearby, so search by address.

  • Dietary cues: It is a dish where a whole chicken is boiled in a pot, so chicken is the centerpiece. Many places let you add kalguksu noodles (칼국수 사리), and the noodles are wheat. Prices for add-ins and seasonings are not on Kakao Map, so ask when you order.

I Love Sindang-dong (아이러브신당동)

An instant tteokbokki (즉석 떡볶이) place in Sindang-dong's tteokbokki alley (신당동 떡볶이 골목). It is registered as open 24 hours. Registered prices are Sindang-dong tteokbokki (신당동 떡볶이) 23,000 won for 3 people and 28,000 won for 4 people, cheese, seafood, tears (눈물), and I Love tteokbokki (아이러브 떡볶이) at 27,000 won (medium) and 32,000 won (large), and jjajang tteokbokki (짜장 떡볶이) (medium, mild) at 23,000 won (registered 2025-08). The 2-person portion and the (small) size were registered more than 2 years ago, so check Kakao Map on the day. The address is Seonghwan Building, Floor 1, 50 Toegye-ro 76-gil, Jung-gu (중구 퇴계로76길 50 성환빌딩 1층), 259 m from Exit 8 of Sindang Station (신당역) (Lines 2·6).

  • Dietary cues: It is gochujang-seasoned tteokbokki, so it is spicy. A menu name with "tears" (눈물) reads as the spicier side. Seafood tteokbokki contains seafood and cheese tteokbokki contains dairy. Instant tteokbokki often includes fish cake (어묵, a processed fish product), so ask when you order if you are vegetarian. Check the ingredients of the jjajang tteokbokki.

Geumdwaeji Sikdang (금돼지식당)

A grilled-pork place near Yaksu Station. The Kakao Map tags include "Seoul Gourmet 100" (서울미식100선). It is open daily 11:30~23:00, but according to the owner's description, only Floor 1 stays open until 23:00 and Floors 2·3 close at 22:00. Registered prices are snowflake pork neck (눈꽃목살) 150g 23,000 won, pork neck (등목살) 150g 24,000 won, pork jowl (항정살) 120g 25,000 won, whole-pork kimchi stew (통돼지김치찌개) (small) 8,000 won, and Suntory highball (산토리하이볼) 10,000 won (registered 2026). Fresh pork belly (본삼겹) was registered more than 2 years ago, so check Kakao Map on the day. The address is 149 Dasan-ro, Jung-gu (중구 다산로 149), 237 m from Exit 2 of Yaksu Station (Lines 3·6).

  • Dietary cues: It is mostly pork. If you avoid pork, there is Korean beef skirt (한우 안창살) 120g at 65,000 won and Korean beef yukhoe (한우육회) 120g at 36,000 won. Yukhoe is raw beef. Several alcohol menus, including highballs, are registered.

② Other Neighborhoods — 4 Venues

The neighborhoods are far apart, so it is better to pick the one closest to your lodging.

Yongmun Haejangguk (용문해장국)

A haejangguk (hangover soup, 해장국) place in Yongmun-dong, Yongsan. The Kakao Map tags include long-established shop (노포) and breakfast (아침식사). It is open Mon–Sat 06:00~23:00 and Sun 06:00~15:00. Registered prices are gakbap (깍밥) 9,000 won, tray noodles (쟁반육면) 15,000 won, zucchini pancake (호박면전) 15,000 won, jeongol (전골) 36,000 won, suyuk (수육, boiled pork) 42,000 won, and spicy braised pork bones (매운뼈찜) 42,000 won (registered 2026). The price of a bowl of haejangguk is not registered. The address is 110 Hyochangwon-ro, Yongsan-gu (용산구 효창원로 110), 173 m from Exit 3 of Hyochang Park Station (효창공원앞역) (Line 6·Gyeongui-Jungang Line).

  • Dietary cues: Bone haejangguk (뼈해장국) and braised pork bones (뼈찜) are usually made with pork spine bones. The minari Korean beef yukhoe (미나리한우육회) (20,000 won) is raw beef. Check the broth ingredients when you order.

Taejo Gamjaguk (태조감자국)

A gamjatang (pork bone and potato stew, 감자탕) place near Sungshin Women's Univ. Station (성신여대입구역). The Kakao Map tags include Centennial Shop (백년가게) and long-established shop (노포). It is open daily 10:10~23:00. Registered prices (2025-11) are traditional Johda (전통 좋~다) 25,000 won, Choegoda (최고다) 30,000 won, and Mujinjang (무진장) 35,000 won, with siraegi, aged kimchi, and soybean-curd gamjatang starting at 27,000 won for Johda, and braised or grilled pork bones starting at 29,000 won for Johda. "Johda, Choegoda, Mujinjang" are three tiers of rising price. The address is Sehak Building, Floor 1, 43 Bomun-ro 34-gil, Seongbuk-gu (성북구 보문로34길 43 세학빌딩 1층), 315 m from Exit 2 of Sungshin Women's Univ. Station (Line 4·Ui-Sinseol Line).

  • Dietary cues: Every menu lists "domestic spine bones" (국산등뼈). Gamjatang is a pork spine dish, so it does not suit you if you avoid pork. The soybean-curd gamjatang (콩비지 감자탕) contains soybeans.

Gyodae Icheungjip Gyodae Main Branch (교대이층집 교대본점)

A samgyeopsal (pork belly, 삼겹살) place outside Gyodae Station (교대역). It is open daily 12:00~00:30. The only menu with a price registered within 2 years is flower samgyeopsal (꽃삼겹) 150g at 18,000 won (domestic, 2026-06-26). The fresh samgyeopsal, pork jowl, stew, and Korean beef menus were registered more than 2 years ago, so check Kakao Map on the day. The address is Cheonghwa Building, Floor 2, 24 Seochodae-ro 50-gil, Seocho-gu (서초구 서초대로50길 24 청화빌딩 2층), 124 m from Exit 14 of Gyodae Station (Lines 2·3). The Kakao Map tags include corkage free (콜키지프리).

  • Dietary cues: The main menu is pork. Korean beef brisket (한우 차돌박이) and striploin (채끝등심) are also on the menu, but prices are to be checked on the day. There are meal menus such as doenjang stew (된장찌개) and kimchi stew (김치찌개).

Gyeyeolsa (계열사)

A chicken place in Buam-dong. It is open Tue–Sat 12:00~22:30, Sun and public holidays 12:00~22:00, and closed Mondays. Registered prices are fried chicken (후라이드치킨) 24,000 won (2026-04-04) and spicy boneless chicken feet (매콤 뼈없는 닭발) 18,000 won (2025-04-25). The rest, including dakdoritang (닭도리탕) and golbaengi somyeon (골뱅이소면), were registered more than 2 years ago, so check Kakao Map on the day. The address is 7 Baekseok-dong-gil, Floor 1, Jongno-gu (종로구 백석동길 7 1층). Kakao Map directions have no nearby subway station information, so go by bus or taxi. A different place with the same name is at number 61 on the same road, so search for number 7.

  • Dietary cues: The chicken is chicken meat, and the batter usually contains wheat flour. Fish cake soup (어묵탕), mackerel pike kimchi stew (꽁치김치찌개), dried cuttlefish (한치), dried pollock (먹태), whelk (골뱅이), and gwamegi (과메기) are seafood, and beondegi soup (번데기탕) is silkworm pupae. None of the registered menus reads as vegetarian.

🗺 Routes — Two Options

A. Euljiro → Dongdaemun → Sindang (evening to late night)

  1. Around 18:00 Nogari and beer at Eulji OB Bear (을지OB베어) (open from 15:00)
  2. Around 20:00 Move to Jinokhwa Halmae Wonjo Dakhanmari (진옥화할매원조닭한마리) (until 01:00). It is near Dongdaemun Station
  3. After 23:00 Tteokbokki at I Love Sindang-dong (아이러브신당동) near Sindang Station (24 hours). If you want more beer later, go back to Wonjo Manseon Hof (원조만선호프) in Euljiro (until 05:00)

B. Yaksu → Sindang (meat + tteokbokki)

  1. Before 21:00 Enter Geumdwaeji Sikdang (금돼지식당). Only Floor 1 stays open until 23:00
  2. After the meal Move on to I Love Sindang-dong at Sindang Station

Check the travel time between places on the day with Kakao Map directions.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to Yongmun Haejangguk (용문해장국) on a Sunday night — It closes at 15:00 on Sundays. Eulji OB Bear (을지OB베어) also closes at 22:00 on Sundays.
  2. Planning Gyeyeolsa (계열사) on a Monday — It is closed on Mondays. Enter the address so directions do not land on the different place with the same name (Baekseok-dong-gil 61).
  3. Arriving at Geumdwaeji Sikdang (금돼지식당) after 22 o'clock and being shown to Floor 2 — Floors 2·3 close at 22:00. If you go late, assume only Floor 1 seats are left, and go in early.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. Where can I go after 1 AM? — By registered hours, there are two places, I Love Sindang-dong (아이러브신당동) (24 hours) and Wonjo Manseon Hof (원조만선호프) (until 05:00). Jinokhwa Halmae Wonjo Dakhanmari (진옥화할매원조닭한마리) closes at 01:00.
  2. What if I don't eat pork? — Choose dakhanmari (Jinokhwa), chicken (Gyeyeolsa, Eulji OB Bear), nogari snacks (the two places in Euljiro), or tteokbokki (I Love Sindang-dong). The gamjatang, haejangguk, and samgyeopsal places are centered on pork.
  3. Can I go alone? — Tteokbokki has registered prices from 3 people, and 2 people is to be checked on the day. Gamjatang, dakhanmari, and jeongol are served by the pot. If you are alone, ask before you go in whether a nogari hof or a single serving of meat can be ordered.
