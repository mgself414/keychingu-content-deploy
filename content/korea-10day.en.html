10-day course for foreigners’ first visit — Seoul + Busan + full set of outlying cities

> Subtitle: #228 7-day expansion + full set of 2 days and 1 night ~ 3 days and 2 nights in outlying city

ISSUE 03 · #266 · 2026-05-20

🎯 Why 10-day course for foreigners

Foreign Janggi Mania #1 = 9 Nights 10 Days (Foreign Mania Deep Dive). 7 days in Seoul + Busan + outlying cities (Jeju, Gangneung, Jeonju, Gyeongju) full set.

★ 10-day full set matrix

Day 1~5 Seoul (Foreigner full set #228)

  • Day 1 Myeongdong·N Seoul Tower / Day 2 Palace·Hanok / Day 3 Hongdae·Yeonnam / Day 4 Gangnam·K-pop·HYBE / Day 5 Jamsil·Lotte World

Day 6~7 Busan 2 days 1 night (#243~#247)

  • Day 6 KTX Seoul → Busan + Haeundae, Gwangalli, Marine City
  • Day 7 Jagalchi·Gamcheon·Seomyeon → KTX Busan → Seoul

Day 8~9 Outer Cities (Select Variant 1)

  • A Jeju (#253): LCC Gimpo↔Jeju + Seongsan, Hallasan, Udo
  • B Gangneung (#254): KTX + Anmok, Jeongdongjin, Pyeongchang skiing
  • C Jeonju (#255): KTX + Hanok·Bibimbap + Outskirts of Gunsan
  • D Gyeongju (#256): KTX Singyeongju + Bulguksa Temple, Seokguram Grotto, Yangdong Village

Day 10 — Han River, duty free, departure

  • Banpo Han River + Incheon Airport duty free + departure

⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make in 10 days

1. "10 days = too long": 10 days = No. 1 foreign enthusiast deep dive + Seoul·Busan·outskirts full set + 1st place in user satisfaction.

2. "10 days = All spots OK": 10 days = 7 days in Seoul + Busan + 1 outlying city (1 among Jeju, Gangneung, Jeonju, and Gyeongju).

3. "10 days = expensive": 10 days = Hotel, transportation, food cost ~$1500~3000 standard + No. 1 cost-effectiveness for foreign enthusiasts.

Variant 10 days

  • A standard (this guide): 7 days in Seoul + 2 days and 1 night in Busan + 2 days and 1 night outside of Busan
  • B K-pop Mania: 6 days in Seoul + 1 night in Busan + KARTS·KBS·MBC Mania
  • C Outskirts Mania: Seoul 5 days + Busan 2 days + Jeju 3 days
  • D Family·Children (#232): 7-day family in Seoul + Busan + outskirts
  • E Food Mania: Seoul + Jeonju (#255) + Busan + Suburb Mania
