10-Day Itinerary for First-Time Foreign Visitors — Seoul + Busan + Outlying Cities Full Package

Subtitle: #228 7-Day Extension + 1N2D to 2N3D Outlying City Full Package

🎯 Why a 10-Day Itinerary for Foreign Visitors?

The top choice for long-term foreign enthusiasts is the 9-night, 10-day trip — a deep dive for foreign enthusiasts. It's a full package: 7 days in Seoul (서울), plus Busan (부산) and one outlying city — Jeju (제주), Gangneung (강릉), Jeonju (전주), or Gyeongju (경주).

★ 10-Day Full Package Matrix

Day 1~5 Seoul (Foreigner Full Package #228)

  • Day 1 Myeongdong (명동)·N Seoul Tower / Day 2 Palaces·Hanok (한옥) / Day 3 Hongdae (홍대)·Yeonnam (연남) / Day 4 Gangnam (강남)·K-pop·HYBE / Day 5 Jamsil (잠실)·Lotte World

Day 6~7 Busan, 1 Night 2 Days (#243~#247)

  • Day 6 KTX Seoul→Busan + Haeundae (해운대)·Gwangalli (광안리)·Marine City (마린시티)
  • Day 7 Jagalchi (자갈치)·Gamcheon (감천)·Seomyeon (서면) → KTX Busan→Seoul

Day 8~9 Outlying City (Choose One Variant)

  • A Jeju (#253): LCC Gimpo (김포)↔Jeju + Seongsan (성산)·Hallasan (한라산)·Udo (우도)

  • Banpo Hangang Park (반포 한강공원) + Incheon Airport (인천공항) duty-free + departure

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make on a 10-Day Trip

1. "10 days is too long": 10 days is the ultimate deep-dive itinerary for foreign enthusiasts — the full Seoul + Busan + outlying city package that delivers the highest satisfaction.

2. "10 days means every spot is fair game": 10 days really means 7 days in Seoul + Busan + one outlying city (Jeju, Gangneung, Jeonju, or Gyeongju — pick one).

3. "10 days is expensive": 10 days runs on a standard hotel/transport/food budget of roughly $1,500-3,000 — genuinely good value for the depth of experience enthusiasts get.

🛂 10-Day Visa & Practical Info (Full Package for Foreign Arrivals)

Visa (10 days·tourism purpose):

  • K-ETA: visa-free entry to Korea (US·EU·Japan·Australia and 100+ countries·online application·₩10,000·48h processing)
  • C-3 general tourism: 90-day visa-free entry (countries with a visa-waiver agreement with Korea)
  • Visa required: China·Russia·Indonesia·some Southeast Asian countries — apply in advance

Arrival procedure:

1. Arrival → immigration ("Foreigner" line)

2. Check K-ETA or visa

3. Baggage·customs (cash over $10,000 must be declared)

4. AREX or limousine bus → Seoul

Must-have apps for foreign enthusiasts:

  • Naver Map (Korea's #1 map app·English support)·Subway Korea (subway)·KakaoMap (restaurants)
  • Papago (Samsung's Korean translator)·Klook·KKday (Korea tour bookings — #1 for foreign enthusiasts)
  • Visit Korea (official Korea Tourism Organization app)

T-money & transit:

  • T-money card: ₩4,000 (buy at any convenience store) — works automatically on subway and bus
  • Or use a credit card (foreign cards accepted) — standard for foreign enthusiasts
