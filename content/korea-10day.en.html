10-Day Itinerary for First-Time Foreign Visitors — Seoul + Busan + Outlying Cities Full Package

> Subtitle: #228 7-Day Extension + 1-2 to 2-3 Nights in Outlying Cities Full Package

ISSUE 03 · #266 · 2026-05-20

🎯 Why a 10-Day Itinerary for Foreign Visitors?

The top choice for long-term foreign enthusiasts is a 9-night, 10-day trip (a deep dive for foreign enthusiasts). This full package includes 7 days in Seoul (서울), Busan (부산), and an outlying city (Jeju (제주), Gangneung (강릉), Jeonju (전주), or Gyeongju (경주)).

★ 10-Day Full Package Matrix

Day 1~5 Seoul (서울) (Foreigner Full Package #228)

  • Day 1 Myeongdong (명동)·N Seoul Tower / Day 2 Palaces·Hanok (한옥) / Day 3 Hongdae (홍대)·Yeonnam (연남) / Day 4 Gangnam (강남)·K-pop·HYBE / Day 5 Jamsil (잠실)·Lotte World

Day 6~7 Busan (부산) 1 Night, 2 Days (#243~#247)

  • Day 6 KTX from Seoul (서울) to Busan (부산) + Haeundae (해운대)·Gwangalli (광안리)·Marine City (마린시티)
  • Day 7 Jagalchi (자갈치)·Gamcheon (감천)·Seomyeon (서면) → KTX from Busan (부산) to Seoul (서울)

Day 8~9 Outlying City (Variant Option 1)

  • A Jeju (제주) (#253): LCC Gimpo (김포)↔Jeju (제주) + Seongsan (성산)·Hallasan (한라산)·Udo (우도)
  • B Gangneung (강릉) (#254): KTX + Anmok (안목)·Jeongdongjin (정동진)·Pyeongchang (평창) Ski
  • C Jeonju (전주) (#255): KTX + Hanok (한옥)·Bibimbap (비빔밥) + Outlying Gunsan (군산)
  • D Gyeongju (경주) (#256): KTX Singyeongju (신경주) + Bulguksa (불국사)·Seokguram (석굴암)·Yangdong Village (양동마을)

Day 10 — Han River (한강)·Duty-Free·Departure

  • Banpo Hangang Park (반포 한강공원) + Incheon Airport (인천공항) Duty-Free + Departure

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make on a 10-Day Trip

1. "10 days = too long": 10 days = the ultimate deep dive for foreign enthusiasts + Seoul (서울)·Busan (부산)·Outlying Cities full package + top user satisfaction.

2. "10 days = all spots OK": 10 days = 7 days in Seoul (서울) + Busan (부산) + 1 outlying city (one of Jeju (제주), Gangneung (강릉), Jeonju (전주), or Gyeongju (경주)).

3. "10 days = expensive": 10 days = standard hotel·transport·food costs of ~$1500~3000 + excellent value for money for foreign enthusiasts.

10-Day Variants

  • A Standard (This Guide): 7 days in Seoul (서울) + 1 night, 2 days in Busan (부산) + 1 night, 2 days in an outlying city
  • B K-pop Enthusiast: 6 days in Seoul (서울) + 1 night in Busan (부산) + KARTS·KBS·MBC enthusiast focus
  • C Outlying City Enthusiast: 5 days in Seoul (서울) + 2 days in Busan (부산) + 3 days in Jeju (제주)
  • D Family·Children (#232): 7 days in Seoul (서울) for family + Busan (부산) + an outlying city
  • E Food Enthusiast: Seoul (서울) + Jeonju (전주) (#255) + Busan (부산) + outlying city enthusiast focus
