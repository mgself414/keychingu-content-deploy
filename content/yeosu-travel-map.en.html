Yeosu Guide — Night-Sea Pocha Street, Odongdo, Aqua Planet, Hyangilam: 2026 Official Info (Island Expo Sep 5–Nov 4 Under Way)

Subtitle: Odongdo free, camellia train ₩1,000 / Aqua Planet Yeosu 09:30–19:00, adult ₩37,900 / Pocha Street 18:00–01:00, closed first Wednesdays / Hamel Lighthouse free / Marine cable car fares unconfirmed because its official site would not open / Jinnamgwan Hall reopened May 2025 — per the Yeosu City tourism and operator official pages as of 2026-10-08

ISSUE 03 · #396 · 2026-05-26

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Yeosu is a night destination — the lit-up Dolsan Bridge (돌산대교), the pocha street beneath Geobukseon Bridge (거북선대교), a cable car over the water. By day, there are the camellia groves of Odongdo Island (오동도) and Aqua Planet Yeosu (아쿠아플라넷 여수), and a bit farther out, Hyangilam Hermitage (향일암). The hours, fees and addresses below are only the values read on 2026-10-08 from the Yeosu City tourism pages and the Aqua Planet official site. The official site of the Yeosu Marine Cable Car would not open because of a certificate error, so we list no fare or hours for it (we do not print figures from secondary sources). The 2026 Yeosu Island World Expo (2026 여수세계섬박람회) (Sep 5–Nov 4) is under way right now, so lodging and transport are more crowded than usual. Figures from the old guide such as "Korea's #1 night sea," "5%+ of foreign visitors," and "₩80,000–500,000 per person," plus the 2012 Expo facility listings, are left out because they have no source or their current operation is unconfirmed.

🌃 Night — Pocha Street, Hamel Lighthouse, Dolsan Bridge

  • Nangman Pocha Street (낭만포차 거리) (102 Hamel-ro): under Geobukseon Bridge (거북선대교), 18 stalls, 18:00–01:00, card payment accepted (Yeosu City official). Closed the first Wednesday of each month — but not in Apr–Jun, Aug–Oct, or during holiday periods. Closed in bad weather. A secondary source's "winter 17:00–24:00" differs from the official hours, so we do not print it.
  • Hamel Lighthouse (하멜등대) (96 Hamel-ro): free, year-round. A short walk from the pocha street.
  • Dolsan Bridge (돌산대교): night lighting in about 50 colors (official). The lighting-on time is not posted.

🌳 Day — Odongdo and Aqua Planet

  • Odongdo Island (오동도): free, outdoors 24 hours. From the parking lot it is about a 15-minute walk along the breakwater, or take the Odongdo camellia train (오동도 동백열차) one way for ₩1,000 (Mar–Oct 09:30–18:00, Nov–Feb until 17:00, rest 12:00–13:00). About 10 minutes by car from downtown (official). Walking time from Yeosu Expo Station (여수엑스포역) is not officially posted.
  • Aqua Planet Yeosu (아쿠아플라넷 여수) (61-11 Odongdo-ro): daily 09:30–19:00, ticket sales end 18:00, open year-round. Adult ₩37,900 · teen/senior ₩34,900 · child ₩32,900 (official).

🚡 Cable Car, Jinnamgwan, Hyangilam — What We Could Not Confirm

  • Yeosu Marine Cable Car (여수해상케이블카): Dolsan Park (돌산공원) ↔ Jasan Park (자산공원), 1.5 km, 50 cabins (35 standard, 15 crystal) — that much is Yeosu City official. The operator's site would not open, so hours, fares and closures are unconfirmed — ask at the ticket office or call the operator.
  • Jinnamgwan Hall (진남관) (National Treasure No. 304, 11 Dongmun-ro): the reopening on 2025-05-30 after repairs comes from news reports. Hours and fees are not officially posted.
  • Hyangilam Hermitage (향일암): a sunrise temple at the southern end of Dolsan. City buses 111, 111-1, 116 (official). Hours and fees not posted.

🏝 2026 Yeosu Island World Expo — Sep 5–Nov 4

  • Official schedule 2026-09-05 to 11-04 (Yeosu City). During this period, book lodging first, and expect a line at the pocha street on weekends.

🚄 Getting There

  • KTX from Yongsan to Yeosu Expo Station — not Seoul Station. We could not open the official Korail page, so we list no travel time or fare (a secondary source says about 3 hours).
  • Yeosu Airport, Geomundo and Baekdo are skipped because we could not open their official pages this time.
  • Tourist information hotline 1330 (24 hours; Korean, English, Japanese).

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Going to the pocha street on a first Wednesday night — it is closed (except Apr–Jun, Aug–Oct and holiday periods).

2. Trusting a blog's cable car fare — not officially confirmed. Check on site.

3. Looking for the KTX at Seoul Station — it departs from Yongsan Station (용산역).

4. Booking lodging the same day during the Expo — Sep 5–Nov 4, reserve first.

5. Driving onto Odongdo — cars only go as far as the parking lot. Walk 15 minutes or take the camellia train.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. What if I have one day? — Afternoon at Odongdo (free) → Aqua Planet → evening at Nangman Pocha Street → night views from Hamel Lighthouse and Dolsan Bridge.

2. What time is the pocha street open? — 18:00–01:00.

3. How much is Aqua Planet? — Adult ₩37,900, ticket sales end at 18:00.

4. What about the cable car? — Dolsan ↔ Jasan, 1.5 km. Fares and hours are not officially confirmed.

5. How do I get to Hyangilam? — City buses 111, 111-1, 116.

6. Where can I get help in a foreign language? — 1330 (24 hours, Korean, English, Japanese).
