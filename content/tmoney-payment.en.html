🎯 Why a Full Guide to Korea T-money?

The number one pain point for foreigners inbound to Korea is payment and transportation cards. This guide integrates the full set: Korea T-money, Cashbee (캐시비), mobile pay, credit cards, and foreigner-friendly options (Visa·Master·JCB·UnionPay·Amex (아멕스)). It's the top priority to resolve within the first 30 minutes of a foreigner's arrival.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "T-money = Rechargeable only": T-money has 3 types — (1) Single-use (from a yeok (역 - station) ticket machine) (2) Rechargeable (from a pyeonuijeom (편의점 - convenience store)) (3) Mobile T-money (NFC). The rechargeable type is the top pick for foreigners (₩4,000 (~$3 / ~20 RMB / ~400 JPY) + top-up).

2. "Currency Exchange (환전) = Anywhere": Korea's top spots for currency exchange are Myeongdong (명동)·Incheon International Airport (인천공항)·Hongdae (홍대)·Jonggak (종각)·Itaewon (이태원) + banks (KEB Hana (외환)·Woori (우리)·Hana (하나)·KB Kookmin (KB)). Myeongdong (명동) offers the best exchange rates (number one for saseol hwanjeonso (사설 환전소 - private money exchange offices)). Incheon International Airport (인천공항) has the worst rates.

3. "Credit Card (신용카드) = OK Anywhere": General Korean credit card acceptance is OK (Visa·Master·JCB·UnionPay·Amex (아멕스)) + some small stores only accept cash. It's recommended to carry T-money + a credit card + around ₩50,000 (~$37 / ~250 RMB / ~5,000 JPY) in cash.

4. "Foreigner Refund (외국인 환급) = Automatic": Duty-free refund (myeonse hwangeup (면세 환급) - Tax Refund) = post-purchase refund (receipt submission + refund card). Purchases over ₩30,000 (~$22 / ~150 RMB / ~3,000 JPY) + foreign passport required + Incheon International Airport (인천공항)·Gimpo International Airport (김포공항)·Hongdae (홍대) are top refund spots.

5. "WOWPASS = Same as Korea T-money": WOWPASS = foreigner exclusive + foreign currency exchange + integrated payment (2022). It includes T-money functionality + is the number one foreigner-friendly option. It's the top card for foreigners inbound.

① Transportation Cards (4)

1. T-money Card (T-money 카드) ★ Korea's Number 1

  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Korea's number one gyotongkadeu (교통카드 - transportation card)
  • Issuance: Pyeonuijeom (편의점 - convenience stores) (CU·GS25·7-Eleven·E-mart24·Ministop) for ₩4,000 (~$3 / ~20 RMB / ~400 JPY) + top-up
  • Transfer (환승): Free transfer to other noseon (노선 - lines) or beoseu (버스 - buses) within 30 minutes (maximum 4 transfers)
  • Balance Refund: ₩500 deposit non-refundable

2. Cashbee (캐시비)

  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — T-money alternative + Lotte·BC Card integrated
  • Issuance: Pyeonuijeom (편의점 - convenience stores) + use like a regular card

3. RailPlus (레일플러스)

  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — KTX·SRT + general jihacheol (지하철 - subway)·beoseu (버스 - bus) integrated
  • Issuance: KORAIL yeok (역 - stations) + top pick for KTX enthusiasts among foreigners

4. WOWPASS ★ Foreigner Exclusive

  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Foreigner exclusive card + foreign currency exchange (환전) + T-money + payment
  • Issuance: Myeongdong (명동)·Incheon International Airport (인천공항)·Hongdae (홍대) WOWPASS kiosks + USD·JPY·CNY·EUR exchange (환전) + instant issuance

② Foreigner Cards (1)

5. Korea Tour Card

  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Foreigner exclusive card + duty-free·tourist attraction discounts
  • Issuance: Incheon International Airport (인천공항) + Myeongdong (명동) + website

③ Mobile Pay (3)

6. Mobile T-money (모바일 T-money) (Android NFC)

  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Android NFC + Naver Pay·Toss·KakaoPay payment (2024 expansion)
  • iPhone not supported

7. Samsung Pay (삼성페이) + Apple Pay

  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Foreigner card registration (Visa·Master) + in-store payment
  • Supported by 90%+ of Korean stores

8. Direct Credit Card Payment (신용카드 직접 결제)

  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — 2024 expansion + direct payment at some jihacheol (지하철 - subway)·beoseu (버스 - bus) yeok (역 - stations)
  • Visa·Master·JCB·UnionPay·Amex (아멕스) OK

④ Taxi·Vehicles (2)

9. KakaoT Taxi (카카오T 택시)

  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Korea's number one taxi app + English OK + card payment

10. Uber Korea + Tada + UT

  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Uber Global·UT Japan integrated·Tada Korean-style

⑤ Foreigner Refunds (2 + Additional 4)

11. Card Cashback·Refund (카드 캐시백·환급)

  • Over ₩30,000 (~$22 / ~150 RMB / ~3,000 JPY) duty-free refund (myeonse hwangeup (면세 환급) - Tax Refund) + foreign passport + Incheon International Airport (인천공항)·Gimpo International Airport (김포공항) refund

12. Foreign Currency Exchange (외국인 환전) + Foreign Currency ATM

  • Myeongdong (명동)·Incheon International Airport (인천공항)·Hongdae (홍대)·Jonggak (종각) exchange offices·banks + Citibank·StanChart·Hana (하나)·Shinhan (신한) foreign currency ATMs

+ Additional 4

  • T-money Transfer (환승) Rules: Free transfer to other noseon (노선 - lines) or beoseu (버스 - buses) within 30 minutes (maximum 4 transfers)
  • Overseas Terminal Payment (해외 단말기 결제): Generally OK at Korean stores + terminals recognize foreign cards (few exceptions)
  • Foreigner SIM (외국인 SIM): Incheon International Airport (인천공항) + Myeongdong (명동) + Hongdae (홍대) foreigner SIM (KT·SK·LG U+) + eSIM expansion
  • Foreigner-Friendly ATM (외국인 친화 ATM): Incheon International Airport (인천공항)·Myeongdong (명동)·Hongdae (홍대) 24-hour foreign currency ATMs (Citibank·StanChart·Hana (하나)·Shinhan (신한))

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. Top Pick for Foreigner T-money Issuance (외국인 T-money 발급): Incheon International Airport (인천공항) + pyeonuijeom (편의점 - convenience stores) + WOWPASS (foreigner exclusive + exchange integrated).

2. Mobile T-money Usage (모바일 T-money 사용): Android NFC + Naver Pay·KakaoPay·Toss payment. iPhone not supported.

3. Top Pick for Currency Exchange (환전): Myeongdong (명동) saseol hwanjeonso (사설 환전소 - private money exchange offices) (best rates) → banks (KEB Hana (외환)·Woori (우리)·Hana (하나)·KB Kookmin (KB)) → Incheon International Airport (인천공항) (worst rates).

4. Credit Card Payment (신용카드 결제): OK at 90%+ of Korean stores (Visa·Master·JCB·UnionPay·Amex (아멕스)). Some small stores only accept cash + recommended to carry ₩50,000 (~$37 / ~250 RMB / ~5,000 JPY) in cash.

5. Foreigner Refund (외국인 환급 - Tax Refund): Over ₩30,000 (~$22 / ~150 RMB / ~3,000 JPY) + foreign passport + receipt submission + Incheon International Airport (인천공항)·Gimpo International Airport (김포공항) refund booths (allow 90 minutes before departure).

6. KakaoT Foreigner Usage (카카오T 외국인 사용): English OK + card registration (Visa·Master) + English receipts available.

7. Foreigner SIM·eSIM (외국인 SIM·eSIM): Incheon International Airport (인천공항) + Myeongdong (명동) + Hongdae (홍대) + KT·SK·LG U+ + eSIM (Airalo·Holafly Global OK).

8. 24-hour Foreign Currency ATM (외화 ATM): Incheon International Airport (인천공항)·Myeongdong (명동)·Hongdae (홍대)·City Hall (시청) Citibank·StanChart·Hana (하나)·Shinhan (신한).

🗺 4 Full-Day Courses for Foreigners

A. Immediate Arrival at Incheon International Airport (인천공항) (First 30 Minutes for Foreigners)

  • Immigration → Currency exchange (환전) (at Incheon International Airport (인천공항) or after arriving in Myeongdong (명동)) → T-money or WOWPASS issuance → Airport Railroad (공항철도) → Hotel

B. Foreigner Payment Full Set for One Day

  • 09:00 (9 AM) Myeongdong (명동) exchange office (환전소) → 10:00 (10 AM) T-money top-up → 13:00 (1 PM) Free jihacheol (지하철 - subway)·beoseu (버스 - bus) transfer (환승) → 17:00 (5 PM) In-store credit card (신용카드) payment → 20:00 (8 PM) KakaoT Taxi (카카오T 택시)

C. Foreigner Duty-Free Refund (외국인 면세 환급) (Before Departure)

  • 08:00 (8 AM) Hotel check-out → 10:00 (10 AM) Myeongdong (명동)·Hongdae (홍대) receipt organization → 11:00 (11 AM) Incheon International Airport (인천공항) refund booth (allow 90 minutes before departure) → Duty-free refund (면세 환급)

D. Foreigner Outskirts Day Trip (Wide Area + Diverse Payments)

  • 09:00 (9 AM) T-money → Gwangyeok (광역 - wide-area) Line 9 → Gangnam (강남) credit card (신용카드) → KakaoT Taxi (카카오T 택시) → Outskirts mobile pay

🌸 5 Seasons·Times

  • Year-round (연중): T-money 24 hours + pyeonuijeom (편의점 - convenience store) top-up OK
  • Incheon International Airport (인천공항) Arrival 19:00-22:00 (7 PM - 10 PM): Exchange booths are quiet (fewer arriving foreigners)
  • Myeongdong (명동) Exchange Office (환전소): 09:00-21:00 (9 AM - 9 PM) (direct foreign currency payment (외환)) + real-time exchange rate fluctuations
  • Mobile T-money Usage (모바일 T-money 사용량): 2024 expansion + Naver Pay·KakaoPay·Toss foreigner payment
  • Foreigner Refund (외국인 환급): During holidays (myeongjeol (명절) - Seollal (설날 - Lunar New Year)·Chuseok (추석 - Korean Thanksgiving)), Incheon International Airport (인천공항) refund booths are extremely crowded → allow 2 hours before departure
