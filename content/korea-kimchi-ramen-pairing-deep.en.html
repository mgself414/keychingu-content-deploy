🍜 5 types of ramen deep dive

1. Shin Ramyun (Nongshim) — Global #1 Spicy

  • Release: 1986 (Nongshim)
  • Export: 110 countries, Ramen Global #1
  • Signature: Spicy, beef base, deep soup
  • Kimchi Pair: Cabbage Kimchi (ripe) = ★★★★★ #1
  • Foreigner Pain Point: Spicy level (standard for Koreans, strong for some foreigners)
  • Mania Course: Shin Ramyun + aged kimchi + egg + green onion = Korea #1 Mania Matrix

2. Jin Ramen (Ottogi) — Korean Market #2

  • Release: 1988 (Ottogi)
  • Market: Korean Market #2 (Next to Shin Ramyun)
  • Type: Spicy + Mild 2 types
  • Signature: Balanced base, less oil, clean soup
  • Kimchi Fair:
  • Spicy + cabbage kimchi = ★★★★★
  • Mild taste + Dongchimi = ★★★★ (Foreigner introduction #1)
  • Pain point for foreigners: Mild taste = No. 1 for foreigners (softer than Shin Ramyun)

3. Samyang Ramen (Samyang Ramen) — Korea’s first (1963)

  • Launched: 1963 (Korea’s first ramen)
  • Signature: Nostalgia, Old Taste, Ramen Classic
  • Kimchi Fair: Mold Eunji = ★★★★★ (Authentic Nostalgia)
  • Pain point for foreigners: Less spiciness than Shin Ramyun, soup is clearer
  • Global awareness: Fire Chicken Noodles (Samyang, 2012) = Global Spicy Challenge #1 (separate line)

4. Neoguri (Nongshim) — Spicy seafood

  • Release: 1982 (Nongshim)
  • Signature: Seaweed (seaweed, kelp) base, spicy
  • Kimchi Fair: Cabbage Kimchi·Bachelor = ★★★★
  • Pain point for foreigners: Spicy + seafood = Japanese people’s familiarity ↑, Westerners’ likes and dislikes
  • Mania Course: Raccoon + Aged Geunji = Spicy·Sour Matrix Mania

5. Chapagetti (Nongshim) — Jjajang Ramen

  • Release: 1984 (Nongshim)
  • Signature: Jjajang Base·Soup X·Oil
  • Kimchi Pair: Kakdugi #1 = ★★★★★ (Jjajang+Kakdugi = Korean well-established standard)
  • Foreigner Pain Point: Soup
  • Mania Course: Chapagetti + Kkakdugi + Pickled Radish = Korean Jjajangmyeon Restaurant Standard Matrix
  • Global Exposure: Movie "Parasite" (2019) · Chapaguri = Chapagetti + Raccoon (Oscars effect)

🔥 How to make Korean-style ramen (foreigner standard)

Basic 5-minute ramen (1 serving)

1. Boil 550ml water (about 2 and a half cups measuring cup or paper cup)

2. Noodles + soup + vegetable stuff in boiling water (3 minutes)

3. Crack 1 egg (optional) (boil for 1 more minute)

4. Green onion/kimchi topping = Separate bowl or on top of ramen

5. Total Time: 4-5 minutes

Korean Mania Variation

  • Aged paper ramen: When starting to boil ramen, 1 handful of aged paper + a little bit of aged paper broth → Spicy and sour matrix
  • Cheese Ramen: After boiling the ramen, top with 1 slice of cheese → Spicy/savory matrix
  • Egg Ramen: Eggs broken (scrambled) or whole (soft-boiled) → Standard
  • Kongnamul Ramen: 1 handful of bean sprouts in boiling water → Siwon and hangover matrix
  • Tteok Ramen: Ramen + rice cake (garaetteok) = standard snack food
  • Dumpling Ramen: Ramen + 4-5 frozen dumplings = Mania Course

Common mistakes made by foreigners (boiling method)

1. Too much water = 550ml standard. Foreigners often use 700~800ml → the soup is bland

2. Kimchi = only on ramen: Korean style = Kimchi in separate bowl (X on ramen). take turns eating one bite at a time

3. Ramen + Kimchi = One Bowl vs Separate: Korean separate bowl standard. However, old paper ramen = boiled in old paper (exception)

4. Soup = Use All: Standard. Increase or decrease spiciness = use 1/2 soup

5. Eggs = Added at the beginning of boiling: Korean style = Added at the last minute (soft-boiled). Hard-boiled eggs added at the beginning

⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Shin Ramyun = Too Spicy": Shin Ramyun = Korean standard (foreigner spiciness level 7~8/10). Jin Ramyun Mild or Samyang Ramyun = Introduction #1.

2. "Ramen + Kimchi = One Bowl": Korean = Separate bowl of Kimchi (X above Ramen). The only exception is aged paper ramen (boiled in aged paper).

3. "편의점 라면 = 별로": 한국 편의점 라면 = 신라면·진라면·삼양 표준. 마트 라면과 동일 (편의점 마진만 +30%).

4. "짜파게티 + 김치 = 안 맞음": 짜파게티 + 깍두기 = 한국 노포 짜장면집 표준 페어. 영화 "기생충"의 짜파구리 = 짜파게티+너구리.

5. "라면 = 일본 라멘과 동일": 한국 라면 = 즉석 라면 (인스턴트)·국물 매콤·5분. 일본 라멘 = 노포·돼지뼈 베이스·1시간+. Completely different food.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. No. 1 choice for foreigners: Jin Ramyun Mild + Dongchimi (not spicy) or Shin Ramyun + Cabbage Kimchi (Korean Mania #1).

2. 편의점 콤보 가성비: GS25 You Us 배추김치 200g (1,500원) + 신라면 (1,200원) = 2,700원 코스.

3. 묵은지 라면: 신라면 또는 삼양라면 + 묵은지 1주먹 (끓는 물에 함께) = 한국 매니아 #1.

4. 짜파구리 (영화 기생충): 짜파게티 + 너구리 = 2017년 KBS 예능 첫 등장·2019년 기생충 글로벌 노출.

5. 치즈 라면: 신라면 + 슬라이스 치즈 1장 (마지막 위에) = 매콤·고소 매트릭스·외국인 친화.

6. 알레르기: 라면 스프 = 새우·마늘·콩·계란 알레르겐. Kimchi = Shrimp sauce, anchovy fish sauce, crustaceans, garlic. Check the English label on the packaging.

⚠ Medical/Safety/Dietary disclaimer

Ramen = processed food (salt ↑·MSG·oil). Kimchi = Fermentation (salinity ↑, spiciness). 염분 합산 1봉지 라면 + 김치 = 일 권장 염분 초과 가능. 임신부·고혈압·신장 환자 = 라면+김치 빈도 주의. 라면 스프 = 일부 알레르겐 (새우·마늘·콩·계란) 함유. If food poisoning is suspected ☎ 1339.

Dietary restrictions fallback:

  • Halal·Kosher: Ramen soup = non-halal (large pork and beef base)·Kimchi = non-halal (salted shrimp) → **Ramen
  • Vegetarian·Vegan: Ramen = Non-vegetarian (soup) → Vegetarian Ramen (Ottogi Vegetarian Ramen·Samyang Vegetarian sold separately) ·Kimchi = Temple food sold separately
  • 글루텐 free: 일반 라면 = 글루텐 함유 (밀가루) → 글루텐 free 라면 별도 (소수)
