🎯 Why a Guide to Taehwa River + Sipnidaesup

Ulsan Taehwa River (태화강) = Korea's #2 National Garden (국가정원) (designated in 2019)·830,000m²·12 themed gardens. Sipnidaesup (십리대숲) = Korea's #1 urban bamboo forest (도심 대숲) at 4.3km. Foreigner pain points = (a) Misconception that "Taehwa River (태화강) = just a regular river" (b) National Garden (국가정원) designated in 2019 (Korea's #1 = Suncheonman (순천만) / #2 = Taehwa River (태화강)) (c) Sipnidaesup (십리대숲) = 4.3km·year-round walking·Instagram matrix #1 (d) MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) + cafe + Instagram fit (e) Full set of 12 themed gardens.

This guide covers 5 axes, 12 signatures + Sipnidaesup (십리대숲) + 12 themed gardens + English communication for foreigners.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Taehwa River (태화강) = just a regular river": Since 2019, Korea's #2 National Garden (국가정원)·830,000m²·12 themes.

2. "Sipnidaesup (십리대숲) = a small bamboo forest": 4.3km·Korea's #1 urban bamboo forest (도심 대숲)·Year-round walking·Instagram matrix #1.

3. "National Garden (국가정원) = just a simple park": 12 themed gardens·Korean·Foreign·Future·Experiential·Family·Children Enthusiast #1.

4. "Season = only Spring (봄)": Spring (봄) (Cherry Blossoms) + Autumn (가을) (Reeds·Fall Foliage) = Both Seasons Enthusiast Matrix #1.

5. "Bicycle (자전거) = not for foreigners": 4.3km bicycle (자전거) + walking = MZ Enthusiast Matrix·Rental ₩5,000~10,000/hour (~$3.7-7.4 / ~26-52 RMB / ~550-1,100 JPY).
