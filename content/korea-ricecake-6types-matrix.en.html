Korean Tteok 6-Type Matrix — The Full Holiday & Feast Classics Set, from Garaetteok to Songpyeon

Subtitle: 6 types for foreign tourists — garaetteok, songpyeon, injeolmi, baekseolgi, gyeongdan, chaltteok-bokki + holidays, first birthdays, feasts, and MZ trends

ISSUE 03 · #855 · 2026-06-09

🎯 Why a Korean Tteok Matrix

The pain point foreign visitors have with Korean tteok (떡, rice cake) = "I only know tteokbokki (떡볶이)". Korean tteok = the #1 classic food of Korean holidays, first birthdays, weddings, and feasts + a prime gateway into Korean culture. They're all "Korean rice cake," yet garaetteok (가래떡, the Seollal (설날, Lunar New Year) tteokguk (떡국, rice cake soup) signature), songpyeon (송편, the Chuseok (추석, Korean harvest festival) signature), injeolmi (인절미, the feast classic coated in roasted soybean powder), baekseolgi (백설기, the white tteok for first birthdays and celebrations), gyeongdan (경단, skewer-style snack balls), and chaltteok-bokki (찰떡볶이, the MZ-trend bunsik (분식, snack food)) each carry different holidays, ingredients, and culture. Foreign visitors typically (a) only know tteokbokki and haven't tried other tteok, (b) don't know songpyeon is the Chuseok signature, (c) don't know tteokguk is the Seollal classic, and (d) don't know injeolmi is a soybean-powder feast food. This guide covers the 6-type matrix + holiday and feast classics + a starter priority list for foreign visitors.

🥢 6-Type Deep Dive

🥢 Garaetteok — Seollal #1

  • Signature: The Seollal tteokguk classic, symbolizing "turning one year older"
  • Ingredients: Non-glutinous rice (not glutinous), shaped as a long cylinder
  • Tteokguk (rice cake soup):
  • Ingredients: Sliced garaetteok + beef, mandu (만두, dumplings), egg, gim (김, dried seaweed), green onion
  • Meaning: The Seollal classic, the new year's start
  • Foreign-visitor friendliness: ★★★★★ (a global-style holiday dish)
  • Where to find: Korean restaurants, traditional tteok shops, and supermarkets in Seollal season (Jan–Feb)
  • Price: Garaetteok 500g = ₩5,000–10,000 (~$4–8) / tteokguk per person = ₩8,000–15,000 (~$6–11)

🌙 Songpyeon — Chuseok #1

  • Signature: The Chuseok classic, half-moon shape, symbolizing abundance
  • Ingredients: Rice flour + fillings of sesame, beans, chestnut, or jujube
  • Varieties:
  • Kkae songpyeon (깨송편): Black sesame (the classic)
  • Kong songpyeon (콩송편): Bean filling
  • Bam songpyeon (밤송편): Chestnut filling
  • MZ songpyeon: Chocolate, cheese, strawberry (MZ trend)
  • Foreign-visitor friendliness: ★★★★★ (the #1 Chuseok vlog subject)
  • Where to find: Traditional tteok shops and supermarkets in Chuseok season (Sep–Oct)
  • Price: Songpyeon, 10 pieces = ₩5,000–10,000 (~$4–8)

🟤 Injeolmi — Feast Classic

  • Signature: Feasts, weddings, good fortune — the soybean-powder classic
  • Ingredients: Glutinous rice + coating powder (soybean flour, sesame)
  • Foreign-visitor friendliness: ★★★★ (soybean-powder Instagram favorite)
  • Where to find: Traditional tteok shops, markets, MZ cafes (variations)
  • Price: Injeolmi 500g = ₩5,000–10,000 (~$4–8)
  • MZ trends: Injeolmi toast, injeolmi bingsu (빙수, shaved ice), injeolmi latte

⚪ Baekseolgi — First Birthdays & Celebrations

  • Signature: First birthdays, celebrations, good fortune — white for purity
  • Ingredients: Rice flour + sugar (simple)
  • Foreign-visitor friendliness: ★★★★ (the first-birthday classic)
  • Where to find: Traditional tteok shops, first-birthday party orders
  • Price: Baekseolgi per gwan (관, traditional unit ≈3.75 kg) = ₩30,000–80,000 (~$23–60) (for feasts)

🍡 Gyeongdan — Snack & Instagram

  • Signature: Mini tteok, soybean-powder coating, an Instagram favorite
  • Ingredients: Glutinous rice + soybean powder, sesame, honey
  • Varieties:
  • Soybean-powder gyeongdan (the classic)
  • Sesame gyeongdan
  • MZ gyeongdan (chocolate, cheese)
  • Foreign-visitor friendliness: ★★★★ (Instagram favorite, snack)
  • Price: Gyeongdan, 10 pieces = ₩5,000–10,000 (~$4–8)

🌶 Chaltteok-bokki — MZ Trend

  • Signature: Glutinous rice cake + trendy sauces (rose, cream, cheese)
  • Bunsik cluster link: #810 bunsik tteokbokki cluster + variations
  • Foreign-visitor friendliness: ★★★★★ (the #1 MZ vlog subject)
  • Price: ₩5,000–10,000 (~$4–8)
  • Where to find: MZ cafes in Hongdae, Gangnam, and Seongsu

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. "Korean tteok = only tteokbokki": There's a full set of 6 types + holiday, feast, and first-birthday classics.

2. "Garaetteok = just regular tteok": Garaetteok is the classic Seollal tteokguk rice cake + a long cylinder shape.

3. "Songpyeon = year-round": Songpyeon is Chuseok-only (Sep–Oct). Scarce at other times.

4. "Injeolmi = just regular tteok": Injeolmi is the soybean-powder + feast and wedding classic.

5. "Baekseolgi = just regular white tteok": Baekseolgi is the classic first-birthday and celebration tteok (from ₩30,000 (~$23) per gwan, for feasts).

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. #1 starter for foreign visitors: Garaetteok (Seollal tteokguk) → songpyeon (Chuseok) → injeolmi (feasts), in that order.

2. Instagram & vlogs: Chuseok songpyeon making, injeolmi toast, MZ chaltteok-bokki.

3. MZ trends: Chaltteok-bokki, injeolmi toast, injeolmi bingsu, MZ songpyeon.

4. Holiday dates: Seollal (lunar 1/1 · 2026 = solar Feb 17), Chuseok (lunar 8/15 · 2026 = solar Sep 25).

5. First-birthday tteok: Baekseolgi, susu-gyeongdan, mujigae-tteok (₩30,000–80,000 (~$23–60) per gwan).

6. Allergies: Declare beans, sesame, tree nuts (chestnut, pine nut), and honey in advance.

7. Payment & tipping: Tteok shops = card OK / markets = cash recommended / no tipping.

8. VAT refund: Not for general food and drinks / duty-free tteok = refund possible.

⚠ Medical, Safety & Dietary Disclaimer

Tteok is ordinary food. Choking risk (especially garaetteok and injeolmi = firm and sticky; children and seniors should chew thoroughly). Pregnant travelers = moderate amounts of soybean powder and nuts. Children = choking caution #1 (cut garaetteok and injeolmi small). Declare allergies (beans, sesame, tree nuts, honey) in advance. If food poisoning or choking is suspected, call ☎ 1339 (medical, English available). This guide is general information, not medical advice.

Dietary-restriction fallback:

  • Halal & kosher: All 6 types contain no animal ingredients = OK (check for honey).
  • Vegetarian & vegan: All 6 types are vegetarian OK (not vegan if honey is included).
  • Gluten-free: All 6 types are 100% rice = gluten-free OK.
