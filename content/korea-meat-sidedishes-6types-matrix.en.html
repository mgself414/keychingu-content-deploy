🥬 How to Eat Meat — Ssam, Banchan, Etiquette (VisitKorea)

  • Ssam (쌈): Put meat on a lettuce or perilla leaf, add Ssamjang (쌈장) (gochujang + doenjang) and garlic, and wrap it. The official guidance is to open wide and eat it in one bite. Ssambap (쌈밥) is rice with meat or fish wrapped in a leaf.
  • Banchan (반찬): Stir-fries, kimchi and braised dishes sit next to the rice, and what you pair them with is up to you. Banchan and water refills are free, and there is no tip (VisitKorea).
  • Samgyeopsal (삼겹살): Rich, tender pork belly. Grill it on the plate, dip it in ssamjang or salt and sesame oil, and wrap it in lettuce or eat it with garlic.
  • The number of side dishes (3, 5 or 7 cheop) and the etiquette of elders eating first appear only on pages that did not open, so we do not list them.

🍱 Where They Show Up as Banchan

  • Galbijjim, japchae, bulgogi and jangjorim are common in Hanjeongsik (한정식) and Baekban (백반) spreads, while jeyuk-bokkeum is a common single dish at baekban and taxi-driver restaurants. We list no specific hanjeongsik restaurant because we could not open an official listing page this time (see the #337 Seoul food map picks).

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Jeyuk and bulgogi marinades contain soy sauce, garlic and sugar, and ssamjang contains soybean paste (VisitKorea definitions). Seoul has 1 halal-certified Korean restaurant (Eid, VisitSeoul). For vegetarian options, check before ordering. For food poisoning or emergencies, call 119.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Treating jeyuk and bulgogi as different meats — pork bulgogi = jeyuk-bokkeum (VisitKorea). Bulgogi is usually beef.

2. Worrying about the cost of banchan refills — they are free (VisitKorea).

3. Leaving a tip — not needed.

4. Sharing a ssam — eat it in one bite (official guidance).

5. Calling 1339 in an emergency — 1339 is the infectious disease hotline. For emergencies, call 119.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. What is the difference between jeyuk-bokkeum and pork bulgogi? — They are the same dish (VisitKorea).

2. Banchan refills? — Free.

3. Where should I go for meat? — Geumdwaeji Sikdang (금돼지식당) and Ggupdang (꿉당) (Bib Gourmand), Budnamujip (버드나무집) (beef short rib), BBQ YUL Gangnam (30,000–50,000 won per person).

4. Where can I get galbijjim? — Hanjeongsik and baekban spreads. We did not confirm a listed hanjeongsik restaurant this time.

5. What does it cost? — Only BBQ YUL has a posted price. Michelin shows only ₩ symbols.

6. Halal? — 1 certified Korean restaurant (Eid). No BBQ restaurant shows a halal label.
