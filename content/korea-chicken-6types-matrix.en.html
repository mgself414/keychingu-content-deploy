Korean Chicken 6 Types Matrix — K-Chicken Global #2 Food

Subtitle: For Foreign Tourists: Yangnyeom, Huraideu, Ganjang, Maeun, Pa-dak, Cheogajip 6 Types + Brand Signature + Foreigner's Introduction

ISSUE 03 · #816 · 2026-06-07

🎯 Why a Korean Chicken Matrix?

Foreign tourists' pain point with Korean chicken = "How is it different from KFC?". Korean chicken = K-pop and K-drama's next global recognition #2 K-food. Korean chicken brands like BBQ, BHC, Kyochon (교촌), Goobne (굽네), NeNe (네네) are expanding globally. Yangnyeom-chicken (양념치킨), Ppuringkle (뿌링클), Banban (반반), and Chimaek (치맥, chicken + beer) are Korean inventions. Foreigners (a) don't know the difference from KFC, (b) find the variety of sauces overwhelming, (c) are unaware of the Banban (반반) option, and (d) don't know about the delivery system or the atmosphere of Korean chicken restaurants. This guide presents a 6-type matrix + brand-specific signatures + recommended entry points for foreigners. Chimaek (치맥) culture is a must-experience Korean invention.

🍗 Chicken 6 Types Deep Dive

🍗 Yangnyeom-chicken (양념치킨) — Korea's #1 Signature

  • Signature: Originated in Daegu (대구) in the early 1980s (popularized by Pelicana (페리카나)·Mexican Chicken (멕시칸치킨) brands).
  • Sauce Types:
  • Classic Yangnyeom (일반 양념): Gochujang (고추장, Korean chili paste) + soy sauce + garlic + honey (sweet + spicy).
  • Spicy Yangnyeom (매운 양념): Additional Cheongyang chili pepper.
  • Soy Sauce Yangnyeom (간장 양념): Soy sauce + garlic base.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (Korean signature·global recognition).
  • Recommended Brands: BHC (비에이치씨) Ppuringkle (뿌링클)·BBQ (비비큐) Golden Olive (황금올리브)·Kyochon (교촌)·Puradak (푸라닭).
  • Price: ₩22,000~26,000 (1 whole chicken, usually 2~3 servings).

🐔 Huraideu (후라이드) — Basic #1

  • Signature: Crispy coating·basic chicken·#1 beer pairing.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (familiar to KFC·recognized crispiness).
  • Difference vs. KFC:
  • Korean Huraideu (한국 후라이드) = Double fried = extra crispy.
  • KFC = single fried + mashed potatoes pairing.
  • Recommended Brands: BBQ (비비큐) Golden Olive (황금올리브)·Kyochon (교촌) Huraideu (후라이드)·Norangtongdak (노랑통닭).
  • Price: ₩20,000~24,000.

🍯 Ganjang-chicken (간장치킨) — Kyochon (교촌) Signature

  • Signature: Kyochon Chicken (교촌치킨, 1991, Korea's #1 Ganjang).
  • Sauce: Soy sauce + garlic + honey + red pepper powder (sweet·savory).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (soy sauce friendly·premium pairing).
  • Recommended Pairing: Ganjang-chicken (간장치킨) + Mu (무, pickled radish) + Baek-kimchi (백김치, white kimchi).
  • Price: ₩22,000~26,000.

🌶 Maeun-chicken (매운치킨) — Hangover Cure·Enthusiast

  • Signature: Cheongyang chili pepper + spicy sauce.
  • Types:
  • Buldak (불닭): Extremely spicy (global challenge).
  • Spicy Yangnyeom (매운 양념): Yangnyeom (양념) with added Cheongyang chili pepper.
  • Gochu-chicken (고추치킨): Rich in chili peppers.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (recommended for spicy food beginners).
  • Recommended Brands: BHC (비에이치씨) Hot Huraideu (핫후라이드)·BBQ (비비큐) Hot Golden Olive (핫황금올리브)·NeNe (네네) Maeun (매운).
  • Price: ₩22,000~28,000.

🧅 Pa-dak (파닭) — Scallion Enthusiast

  • Signature: Huraideu (후라이드) + generous scallion (대파) topping.
  • Types:
  • Pa-dak Huraideu (파닭 후라이드): Basic.
  • Pa-dak Yangnyeom (파닭 양념): Yangnyeom (양념) + scallions.
  • Ganjang Pa-dak (간장 파닭): Ganjang (간장) + scallions.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (value for money + vegetable pairing).
  • Recommended Brands: NeNe (네네) Pa-dak (파닭)·Norangtongdak (노랑통닭) Pa-dak (파닭).
  • Price: ₩24,000~28,000.

🧂 Ppuringkle/Nun-kkot (뿌링클·눈꽃) — MZ (Millennials and Gen Z) Vlog

  • Signature: BHC (비에이치씨) Ppuringkle (뿌링클, signature launched in 2014) — popularized the powdered cheese topping category.
  • Sauce: Powdered cheese (Cheddar·Parmesan base).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (MZ (Millennials and Gen Z) vlog·Instagram hotspot).
  • Types:
  • Ppuringkle (뿌링클) (BHC (비에이치씨)): Signature cheese powder.
  • Nun-kkot Chicken (눈꽃치킨): Powdered topping category.
  • Cheogajip Yangnyeom (처갓집 양념): Cheogajip (처갓집, 1979) = separate Yangnyeom (양념)·Ganjang (간장) brand (distinct from powdered cheese).
  • Price: ₩24,000~28,000.

🍻 Chimaek (치맥) — Korean Invention Goes Global

Chimaek (치맥) = Chicken + Maekju (맥주, Beer)

  • Korean Invention: Chimaek (치맥) culture of Korean chicken + draft beer (생맥주) established in the 1990s.
  • Global Spread: Exploded in popularity in China and Southeast Asia due to the K-drama "My Love from the Star (별에서 온 그대)" (2014).
  • Standard Pairing:
  • Huraideu (후라이드) + Draft beer (생맥주) = Classic.
  • Yangnyeom (양념) + Somaek (소맥, Soju + Beer) = Korean hoesik (회식, company dinner/gathering).
  • Ganjang (간장) + Makgeolli (막걸리, traditional Korean rice wine) = Traditional.
  • Maeun (매운) + Soju (소주, Korean distilled liquor) = Hangover cure.

Difference Between Chimaek-jip (치맥집) vs. Chicken-jip (치킨집)

  • Chicken-jip (치킨집): Specializes in chicken (meal-focused).
  • Chimaek-jip (치맥집)·Beer Hof (맥주 호프): Chicken + alcohol (snack/anju-focused).
  • Pocha (포차): Chicken + snacks (anju) + alcohol (nighttime street food tent/pub).

  • Draft beer (생맥주): ₩4,000~6,000.
  • Soju (소주)·Beer (맥주): ₩4,000~6,000.
  • Tteokbokki (떡볶이)·Twigim (튀김) (Bunsik (분식, Korean street food) combination): ₩5,000~10,000.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Korean Chicken = KFC": Korean chicken = double fried + Yangnyeom (양념) sauce (Korean invention). KFC = single fried. Different food.

2. "Yangnyeom (양념) = just spicy": Yangnyeom (양념) = sweet + spicy (honey·gochujang (고추장) base). Not just spicy.

3. "1 whole chicken = 1 serving": 1 whole chicken = 2~3 servings. For one person, order a half chicken (단품 메뉴, a la carte menu) or 5 chicken drumsticks, etc.

4. "Chimaek (치맥) = just chicken + beer": Chimaek (치맥) = Korean invention, a cultural highlight of K-pop·K-drama. Draft beer (생맥주) + Huraideu (후라이드) is the classic.

5. "Banban (반반) = not a menu item": Banban (반반) = half Yangnyeom (양념) + half Huraideu (후라이드), optimal for foreigners. Available at all restaurants.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. Foreigner Entry Priority #1: Yangnyeom (양념) + Huraideu (후라이드) Banban (반반) + Draft beer (생맥주) full set (Chimaek (치맥)).

2. Instagram·Vlog Priority #1: Ppuringkle (뿌링클) (cheese powder) + Yangnyeom-chicken (양념치킨).

3. MZ (Millennials and Gen Z) Trend: Puradak (푸라닭) spicy flavor·Ppuringkle (뿌링클)·Ganjang (간장) + Honey Combo.

4. Global Brands: Kyochon (교촌) (Ganjang (간장) #1) + BBQ (비비큐) (50+ countries) + BHC (비에이치씨) (Korea #1).

5. Allergies·Pregnancy: Gluten (튀김옷, batter)·soy (간장, soy sauce)·egg (양념, sauce)·nuts (꿀, honey) should be declared in advance.

6. Vegetarian·Halal·Kosher: Chicken = non-vegetarian. Halal Korean chicken = almost none. Some global BBQ (비비큐) branches offer halal options.

7. Payment·Tips: Card OK / Delivery = Baemin (배민)·Yogiyo (요기요) foreigner OK / No tips.

8. VAT Refund: Food and beverages generally not eligible for refund.

⚠ Medical·Safety·Dietary Disclaimer

Chicken = fried (high fat). Pregnant women·immunocompromised individuals = spicy chicken·excessive consumption caution. Children = mild Yangnyeom (양념)·Huraideu (후라이드) recommended. Declare allergies (gluten·soy·egg·nuts) in advance. Delivery chicken = recommended to consume within 30 minutes~1 hour (food poisoning risk). If food poisoning is suspected, ☎ 1339 (medical, English). This guide provides general information, not medical advice.

Restaurant·Brand Information Disclaimer: Chicken brand restaurant·menu·payment policies change frequently — recommended to check NAVER/Kakao Map or official websites before visiting.

Dietary Restriction Fallback:

  • Halal·Kosher: Almost no certified Korean chicken restaurants. → Search for some halal restaurants in Itaewon (이태원) OR BBQ (비비큐) global branches with halal options in advance.
  • Vegetarian·Vegan: Chicken = non-vegetarian. → Refer to [#799 Vegetarian·Vegan](#799).
  • Gluten-free: Batter = flour. Goobne (굽네) (oven-baked chicken) = some gluten-free options.
