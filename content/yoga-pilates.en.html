⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Yoga = Korean only": Foreigner-friendly yoga means YogaTribe (이태원) and YogaJang (한남) offer full English support for foreigner enthusiasts.

2. "Meditation = Temples only": Meditation includes Templestay (템플스테이) (#207), yoga studios, and hotel meditation for a full experience. A Templestay is a unique Korean cultural program offering a glimpse into monastic life at a traditional Buddhist temple.

3. "Wellness = Expensive": Foreigner-friendly yoga costs ₩25,000~50,000 (~$18-37 / ~130-260 RMB / ~2,600-5,200 JPY) per session, making it a top spot for enthusiasts.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Top 1 Yoga for Foreigners: YogaTribe (이태원)·YogaJang (한남) are English-friendly.

2. Pilates for Foreigners: G·M·Dajung Pilates in Gangnam (강남)·Itaewon (이태원) are popular with foreigner enthusiasts.

3. Meditation for Foreigners: Temple Templestays (템플스테이) at Jingwansa (진관사)·Bongeunsa (봉은사).

4. 2-Day 1-Night Wellness for Foreigners: Full Templestay (템플스테이) + Yoga package.
