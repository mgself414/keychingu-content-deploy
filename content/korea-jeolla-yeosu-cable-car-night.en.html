🏛 5 Pillars Deep Dive

① Yeosu Maritime Cable Car (여수 해상케이블카) (1.5km·Korea #1)

  • Signature: 1.5km · Korea's #1 Maritime Cable Car (해상케이블카) · Transparent Floor Matrix
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★★ (English·MZ + Enthusiast)
  • Hours: 09:00-22:00 (9:00 AM-10:00 PM) (seasonal) · Round trip ₩17,000 (~$12.5 / ~90 RMB / ~1,800 JPY) to ₩25,000 (~$18.5 / ~130 RMB / ~2,600 JPY) (Standard/Transparent Floor)

② Yeosu Night Sea (여수 밤바다) (Night View Matrix #1)

  • Signature: Busker Busker's "Yeosu Night Sea" (여수 밤바다) (2012) = Korea Matrix #1 · MZ + Insta Enthusiast
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★★ (English·MZ + Insta)

③ Hyangiram (향일암) (Yeosu's Premier Sunrise Spot·South Sea)

  • Signature: South Sea Sunrise Matrix #1 · 1300+ year old sachal (사찰) (Korean Buddhist temple) · New Year 1/1 Enthusiast
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★ (English Signature/Spiritual Enthusiast)

④ Yeosu Sashimi/Seafood (여수 회·해산물) (South Sea Signature)

  • Signature: Fresh South Sea Sashimi (회) · Gatgimchi (갓김치) (mustard leaf kimchi) and Dolgejang (돌게장) (marinated stone crab) Yeosu Signature
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★ (Partial English/Enthusiast)
  • Price: Sashimi (회) = ₩50,000 (~$37 / ~260 RMB / ~5,200 JPY) to ₩120,000 (~$89 / ~620 RMB / ~12,500 JPY) for 2-3 people

⑤ 1-Night Stay (Pension/Hotel)

  • Signature: Yeosu 1-Night Stay Enthusiast · Ocean View + Full Package
  • Price: 1-night stay = ₩80,000 (~$59 / ~410 RMB / ~8,300 JPY) to ₩300,000 (~$220 / ~1,550 RMB / ~31,000 JPY)
