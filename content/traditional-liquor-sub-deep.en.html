12 traditional Korean liquors

1. Makgeolli (Korea #1 Traditional Liquor/National Intangible Heritage 2021)

  • Discrimination: 2021 National Intangible Heritage Listing (Overall Makgeolli Making Culture)·Production of 1000+ species nationwide·6-8% ABV·Rice + Yeast Fermentation
  • Signature: Seoul Jangsu, Guksoondang, Jipyeong, Hansan Sogokju, Busan Sanseong
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ 1st place for enthusiasts·1st place for beginners

2. Gyeongju Gyodong Beopju (National Intangible Cultural Heritage No. 86-3, 300 years)

  • Discrimination: 300 years of Gyeongju Choi family’s Gayangju+·National Intangible Cultural Heritage No. 86-3·Sweet rice + yeast + pine leaves·About 17% ABV
  • Location: Gyo-dong, Gyeongju·Gyeongju #1 Jeongjeongju
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Authentic Mania·#664 Gyeongju cross

3. Andong Soju (45-proof distilled liquor)

  • Discrimination: Introduced from the Yuan Dynasty during the Goryeo Dynasty·45% ABV (Korea #1 Authentic Distilled Liquor)·Andong Soju Museum·Intangible Cultural Heritage
  • Signature: Andong Soju Museum + Tasting·No. 1 souvenir
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·#663 Andong cross

4. Hansan Sogokju (Baekje, 1500, “little liquor”)

  • Discrimination: 1500 years ago during the Baekje period+ Authentic liquor·Hansan Village, Seocheon, South Chungcheong Province·Hard to get up after just one sip, “a lame person”·18% ABV
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Authentic enthusiast·Intangible cultural asset

5. Moon Bae-ju (National Intangible Cultural Heritage No. 86-1, Pyeongyang Orthodox)

  • Discrimination: National Intangible Cultural Heritage No. 86-1·Pyongyang Authentic + South Korean Succession·40% ABV Distilled Alcohol·Pear Blossom Fragrance
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

6. Myeoncheon Dugyeonju (Chungnam, Azalea Flower)

  • Discrimination: National Intangible Cultural Heritage No. 86-2·Myeoncheon, Chungcheongnam-do·Azalea Flower Fermented Wine·Goryeo Welfare and Legend
  • Time: Spring, April, azalea peak
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP MZ·SNS 1st place

7. Bokbunjaju (Jeonbuk Gochang #1·Antioxidant)

  • Discrimination: Gochang, Jeollabuk-do #1 Producing area·Bokbunja (raspberry)·No. 1 in antioxidants·14-16% ABV·Sweet
  • Time: June-July bokbunja harvest
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ Mania·No. 1 Fruit Liquor

8. Plum wine, wild grape wine, and Schisandra chinensis (three types of fruit wine)

  • Discrimination: Top 3 traditional Korean fruit wines·Plum (Gwangyang, Hadong), wild wild grapes (wild wild grapes + wine), Schisandra chinensis (Mungyeong #1)
  • Signature: Gwangyang Plum Korea #1·Mungyeong Omijaju Wine
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiast·No. 1 gift

9. Soju (Chamisul, Jinro, Cheongha, Jinro, Cheom-like)

  • Discrimination: Korea #1 Liquor (Market 50%+)·16-25% ABV·Chamisul·Jinro·Chorumlike·1908 Jinro Authentic
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ Mania No. 1·Cheap 1,500-3,000 won

10. Korean alcohol + snack pairing (Michelin recommended)

  • Distinction: Michelin Guide recommended makgeolli + kimchi pancake + green onion pancake·Soju + pork belly·traditional liquor + Korean food full set
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania #1·#357 Korean food cross

FAQ 6

Q1. Makgeolli price range? A. Convenience store + supermarket 1,500-3,000 won·Premium 5,000-15,000 won·1000+ types produced nationwide.

Q2. Recommended alcohol content for foreigners? A. Beginner = Makgeolli (6-8%)·Bokbunja (14%)·Intermediate = Soju (17%)·Plumju (14%)·Advanced = Andong Soju·Munbaeju (40-45%).

Q3. Foreign intangible cultural property alcohol? A. Makgeolli (2021), Gyeongju Gyo-dong Beopju (86-3), Myeoncheon Dugyeonju (86-2), Munbaeju (86-1), Andong Soju, Hansan Sogokju.

Q4. Michelin-recommended pairing? A. Makgeolli + kimchi pancakes and green onion pancakes (1st place)·Soju + pork belly·bokbunja + seafood.

Q5. Duty-free carry-in? A. 1L duty-free OK (most countries)·Makgeolli + soju + bokbunja full set recommended·Incheon Airport duty-free shop.

Q6. Korean drinking manners? A. Receive with both hands, turn your head and drink (in front of an adult), pour after emptying the glass·No. 1 among foreign enthusiasts.
