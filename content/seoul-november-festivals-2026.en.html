Seoul November Festival & Event Calendar 2026 — Design Festival, Autumn Festa, Gwanghwamun Media Art, Last Foliage: What's Confirmed and What's Not Yet Announced

Subtitle: Seoul Design Festival Nov 26–29 at COEX · Seoul Autumn Festa through Nov 29 · Sejong Center media facade "Dyed in Light" daily 19:00–23:00 through Nov 30 · Gwanghwamun Bitmorak Festival through Dec 31 · 8 museum exhibitions closing in November · Bukhansan peak foliage Nov 4 (VisitKorea forecast) — as of 2026-10-11. The 2026 dates for the Seoul Lantern Festival, the Korea Kimjang Festival and the Christmas market are not yet announced

🎯 How to Use This Calendar

Compared with October (#1375, #1404), November in Seoul has fewer big festivals; the focus is exhibitions, nighttime media art and the tail end of the foliage. And much of the November schedule is not out yet — the city of Seoul usually publishes its "November event plan" at the end of October. So this guide separates what was date-confirmed on official pages on 2026-10-11 from what is not yet announced. For unannounced events, last year's dates are given as reference only.

⚠ Correction, also applied to #1404: The earlier #1404 said the Seoul Lantern Festival (서울빛초롱축제) "opens Nov 1", but its source article was about the 17th edition in 2025 (Dec 12–Jan 18, 2026). The 2026–27 edition is not announced as of Oct 11 (#1404 is being corrected too).

🍂 November Seasonal Notes

  • Last of the foliage: VisitKorea's 2026 foliage forecast puts first color on Bukhansan (북한산) at Oct 23 and peak at Nov 4. A foliage-map news report (secondary) also says the Seoul metropolitan area peaks from late October — so the first week of November is the last chance for city foliage. Foliage walks are in #743 and #273.
  • Suneung (수능) on Nov 19 (Thu): the 2027 college entrance exam date (per news reports; the original Korea Institute for Curriculum and Evaluation notice was not read). Just know it is a day with a lot of test-taker travel.
  • Pepero Day (빼빼로데이) on Nov 11: a commercial occasion, not an official holiday. Convenience stores and supermarkets put out more stick-cookie displays (secondary).
  • First snow: Seoul's normal first snow falls in late November (Nov 21 per news reports; varies a lot by year).

🗓 Week-by-Week Route

  • Nov 1–8: last week of foliage at Bukhansan (북한산) and in the city → evening media facade at the Sejong Center (세종문화회관) (19:00–23:00) → Bitmorak at Gwanghwamun Square (광화문광장).
  • Nov 9–22: catch the exhibitions closing in November (Deoksugung (덕수궁) Nov 8, Thaddaeus Ropac Nov 14, National Museum of Korea Nov 22) · watch for the Craft Museum's Monday closure.
  • Nov 23–30: Seoul Design Festival (서울디자인페스티벌) (COEX, Nov 26–29) + the Leeum and National Museum exhibitions closing Nov 29. If the Kimjang Festival and Lantern Festival announcements come out, they may fit into this week.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Planning the Lantern Festival for November — in 2025 it opened on Dec 12. 2026–27 is not announced.

2. Expecting to make kimchi at the Kimjang Festival — in 2025 there was no experience for the general public (Seoul city notice).

3. Treating the November schedule as final right now — the city of Seoul's November event plan is usually released at the end of October. Check again before you go.

⚙ FAQ 3

1. What should I not miss in November? — The Sejong Center "Dyed in Light" (daily 19:00–23:00, through Nov 30), the Seoul Design Festival (Nov 26–29), and the tail end of the foliage (through Nov 4).

2. What's free? — The media facade, the Gwanghwamun Bitmorak Festival, and the Seoul Craft Museum's evening opening.

3. When does the Christmas mood begin? — The Gwanghwamun Market and the Lantern Festival were in mid-December in 2025. The 2026 schedule is not announced.
