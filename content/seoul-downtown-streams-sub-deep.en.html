FAQ 6

Q1. How to reserve the Cheonggyecheon EN guide? A. Seoul City official EN free guide·Reservation 1 week in advance (visitseoul.net)·Cheonggye Plaza ~ Samil Bridge 1 hour free·No. 1 enthusiast for some popular times.

Q2. When is the Lantern Festival? A. Every year from early December to early January, about 2 weeks·Free·Pokemon and other collaborations·5 minutes from Gwanghwamun Station·17th (2024-2025).

Q3. Access to Cheonggye Plaza? A. 5 minutes walk from Exit 5 of City Hall Station, 5 minutes from Exit 5 of Jonggak Station, 7 minutes from Exit 5 of Gwanghwamun Station, Connected to 11 subway stations.

Q4. Is Cheonggyecheon safe at night? A. Open free all year round, LED bridge lighting after sunset·Police + many guides·No. 1 safety for foreign enthusiasts.

Q5. Seongbukcheon hidden gem? A. 3.68km·Hansung University Station (Line 4)·Hanok cafe cluster + urban regeneration·No. 1 fall foliage (October-November).

Q6. Cheonggyecheon + Gwangjang cluster? A. Cheonggyecheon + Gwangjang Market 5 minutes walk·Bindaetteok + Makgeolli pairing (#688 cross)·Lunch + night view 1-day full set.
