Hoe-jip (회집) vs Il-sik-jip (일식집) vs Sijang (시장) for Foreigners — Where to Eat Raw Fish?

Subtitle: Korean Raw Fish 3-Channel Comparison Matrix + Top Recommendation by Segment + Decision Guide based on Time, Budget, and Companions

ISSUE 03 · #791 · 2026-06-07

🎯 Why a 3-Channel Comparison Guide?

The pain point for foreigners regarding Korean raw fish is: "Where should I eat?" Even if you know about #789 seasonal fish species and are familiar with #790 market workflow, channel selection is the first decision. Korean raw fish channels fall into three categories: (1) Hoe-jip (회집) (Korean-style raw fish restaurant), (2) Il-sik-jip (일식집) (Japanese-style restaurant), and (3) Sijang (시장) (market, 1st floor stalls + 2nd floor dining). They vary in price from ₩30,000 (~$22 / ~160 RMB / ~3,200 JPY) to ₩200,000, atmosphere from budget-friendly to luxurious, and raw fish style from hwal-eo (활어) (live fish) to suk-seong (숙성) (aged fish). This guide directly compares these three channels, provides top recommendations by segment (solo, couple, family, business), and offers a decision matrix based on time, budget, and companions.

🎯 Top Recommendation by Segment

👤 Solo Traveler

  • 1st Pick: Hoe-jip (회집) solo menu (Hongdae (홍대)·Gangnam (강남) solo raw fish restaurants) — Solo raw fish + Maeun-tang (매운탕) set ₩35,000~₩55,000, alcohol optional
  • 2nd Pick: Sijang (시장) (Noryangjin (노량진) solo stalls) — Cost-effectiveness ★★★★★, ₩25,000~₩35,000
  • 3rd Pick: Il-sik-jip (일식집) lunch course (shortened Omakase (오마카세) version, ₩50,000~₩80,000 lunch cost-effectiveness)
  • Avoid: Il-sik-jip (일식집) traditional Omakase (오마카세) (low cost-effectiveness for solo diners, difficult to adapt to 2-hour long meal)

💑 Couple

  • 1st Pick: Il-sik-jip (일식집) Omakase (오마카세) (1st anniversary·special occasion·romantic) — ₩100,000~₩150,000 per person, photos ★★★★★
  • 2nd Pick: Hoe-jip (회집) (cost-effective date) — ₩55,000 per person + alcohol
  • 3rd Pick: Sijang (시장) (local date, #590 Jagalchi (자갈치) 2nd floor night view)
  • Avoid: Weekday daytime Sijang (시장) (lacks atmosphere)

👨‍👩‍👧 Family

  • 1st Pick: Hoe-jip (회집) (family-style) — 4-person full set ₩200,000~₩280,000, many children's menus (twigim (튀김) (fried food)·donkatsu (돈가스) (pork cutlet) available separately)
  • 2nd Pick: Sijang (시장) (Jagalchi (자갈치) Main Building 2nd floor family rooms·some kids' zones) — Cost-effectiveness·experience
  • 3rd Pick: Il-sik-jip (일식집) (lunch course, too quiet for children)
  • Avoid: Omakase (오마카세) (difficult for children to adapt)

💼 Business

  • 1st Pick: Il-sik-jip (일식집) traditional Omakase (오마카세) (Gangnam (강남)·Cheongdam (청담)·Shilla Hotel (신라호텔) Japanese restaurants) — ₩150,000~₩300,000 per person, formality ★★★★★
  • 2nd Pick: High-end Hoe-jip (회집) (authentic Korean, refined atmosphere) — ₩80,000~₩120,000 per person
  • 3rd Pick: Sijang (시장) (only if a relaxed atmosphere is desired)
  • Avoid: Sijang (시장) stalls (lacks formality)

🎒 Backpacker·Budget

  • 1st Pick: Sijang (시장) (Noryangjin (노량진) weekday daytime) — ₩25,000~₩35,000 per person, unlimited alcohol ₩30,000 extra
  • 2nd Pick: Hoe-jip (회집) lunch special (lunch only, ₩25,000~₩40,000)
  • 3rd Pick: Sijang (시장) (Soraepogu (소래포구) offers good value on both weekdays and weekends)
  • Avoid: Omakase (오마카세) (exceeds budget)

🎯 3-Channel Deep Dive

🐟 Hoe-jip (회집) (Korean-style Raw Fish Restaurant)

Core Value: Hwal-eo (활어) (live fish) + Han-sang (한 상) (full table setting) full set + Maeun-tang (매운탕) (spicy fish stew) course. Authentic Korean raw fish experience.

  • Recommended Restaurants (Seoul): High-end raw fish restaurants in Gangnam (강남)·Cheongdam (청담)·Yeouido (여의도) / Value-for-money in Hongdae (홍대)·Sinchon (신촌) / Family-style in Jamsil (잠실)·Dongdaemun (동대문)
  • Recommended Restaurants (Provinces): Jagalchi (자갈치)·Haeundae (해운대) raw fish street in Busan (부산) / Gangneung (강릉)·Sokcho (속초) / Dajji-jip (다찌집) in Tongyeong (통영)
  • Menu Pattern: Raw fish (1~2 types) + Hoe-muchim (회무침) (spicy raw fish salad) + Maeun-tang (매운탕) + Ssam-chaeso (쌈채소) (wrap vegetables) + Banchan (반찬) (side dishes) 5~10 types + Rice
  • Price Anchor: Lunch ₩30,000~₩50,000 / Dinner ₩50,000~₩80,000 / High-end ₩100,000+
  • Reservation: Recommended 1~2 days in advance for weekends·special occasions. For restaurants where English reservations are difficult → use hotel concierge.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: Gangnam (강남)·Cheongdam (청담)·Hongdae (홍대) = ★★★★ / Provinces = ★★~★★★ (fewer English menus)
  • Recommended Fish Species for Foreigners: Gwang-eo (광어) (flounder)·U-reok (우럭) (rockfish)·Do-mi (도미) (sea bream) (mild white fish)

🍣 Il-sik-jip (일식집) (Japanese-style in Korea, Korean-run Japanese cuisine)

Core Value: Suk-seong-hoe (숙성회) (aged raw fish) + Course + Jang-in Jeong-sin (장인 정신) (artisan spirit). Japanese sashimi style with Korean chefs and mostly domestic seafood.

  • Types:
  • Omakase (お任せ): 1:1 with chef, 8~15 courses, ₩100,000~₩300,000 per person
  • Jeong-tong Il-sik (정통 일식) (Traditional Japanese): Sashimi·Nigiri (니기리)·Udon (우동)·Twigim (튀김) (fried food) a la carte menu
  • Chains (e.g., Sushi Choice (스시초이스)·Mijin (미진)·Sushi Meijin (스시메이진)): Value-for-money ₩30,000~₩50,000
  • Recommended Restaurants (Seoul): Omakase (오마카세) in Cheongdam (청담)·Apgujeong (압구정) area / Japanese restaurant golmok (골목) (alley) in Gangnam (강남) / Japanese restaurants in Shilla (신라)·Lotte Hotel (롯데호텔) (hotels often have 10% VAT + 10% bong-sa-ryo (봉사료) (service charge) separately)
  • Menu Pattern (Omakase (오마카세)): Tsukemono (츠케모노) (pickled vegetables) → Sashimi 5~7 → Nigiri (니기리) 8~12 → Jorim (조림) (braised)·Gui (구이) (grilled) → Miso-shiru (미소시루) (miso soup) → Dessert
  • Price Anchor: Chains ₩30,000~₩50,000 / Traditional ₩80,000~₩150,000 / Omakase (오마카세) ₩150,000~₩300,000
  • Reservation: Traditional·Omakase (오마카세) = Essential 1~2 weeks in advance (Catchtable (캐치테이블) or Opentable (오픈테이블) apps). Top-tier Omakase (오마카세) in Cheongdam (청담) often have a 1~3 month wait.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ — Many English menus, Japanese tourists adapt well
  • Special Features: Wasabi (와사비)·Gan-jang (간장) = free, additional sake (사케) (Japanese rice wine)·Cheongju (청주) (Korean clear rice wine) are good value (₩30,000~₩80,000 per bottle)

🛒 Sijang (시장) (1st Floor Stalls + 2nd Floor Table Setting)

Core Value: Ga-seong-bi (가성비) (cost-effectiveness) ★★★★★ + Hwal-eo (활어) (live fish) freshness + Local Atmosphere. For full workflow, refer to #790.

  • 5 Recommended Markets: Jagalchi (자갈치) (Busan (부산)) / Noryangjin (노량진) (Seoul (서울)) / Tongyeong Jungang Hwal-eo (통영중앙활어) (Gyeongnam (경남)) / Soraepogu (소래포구) (Incheon (인천)) / Guryongpo (구룡포) (Gyeongbuk (경북))
  • Menu Pattern: Raw fish (selected at stall) + Sang-cha-rim (상차림) (table setting) (2nd floor restaurant) + Maeun-tang (매운탕) (fish stew made from leftover bones)
  • Price Anchor: Market style ₩25,000~₩50,000 per person + Sang-cha-rim-bi (상차림비) (table setting fee) + Maeun-tang-bi (매운탕비) (spicy fish stew fee) = Total ₩40,000~₩70,000
  • Reservation: Weekday walk-in / Weekend·Season (December Bang-eo (방어) (yellowtail)·September Jeon-eo (전어) (gizzard shad)) advance phone call recommended
  • Foreigner Friendliness: Jagalchi (자갈치) ★★★★★ / Noryangjin (노량진) ★★★★ / Tongyeong (통영) ★★★★ / Soraepogu (소래포구) ★★★ / Guryongpo (구룡포) ★★
  • Workflow: Refer to #790 4-step guide

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Hoe-jip (회집) = Il-sik-jip (일식집)": Korean-style Hoe-jip (회집) and Japanese-style Il-sik-jip (일식집) are different categories. Menus, prices, and atmospheres all differ.

2. "Omakase (오마카세) = only expensive sashimi": Omakase (오마카세) = Chef's 8~15 course meal (sashimi is only part of it). The price includes the entire course.

3. "Sijang (시장) = always cheap": While market raw fish prices themselves are low, the Sang-cha-rim-bi (상차림비) (table setting fee) + Maeun-tang-bi (매운탕비) (spicy fish stew fee) paid separately can make the total similar to a Hoe-jip (회집) (though with more freedom).

4. "Weekday daytime is always inefficient": Jeom-sim Teuk-seon (점심 특선) (lunch course) = 30~50% discount at both Hoe-jip (회집) and Il-sik-jip (일식집). Best value.

5. "Omakase (오마카세) is a must for anniversaries": For an authentic Korean raw fish experience, a Go-geup Hoe-jip (고급 회집) (high-end Korean raw fish restaurant) (Han-sang Cha-rim (한 상 차림) (full table setting)) offers a fresh experience for foreigners, perhaps more so than Japanese cuisine.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. First raw fish challenge = where?: Hoe-jip (회집) is the 1st pick (authentic Korean experience). Il-sik-jip (일식집) is recommended for foreigners with previous Japanese dining experience.

2. Foreigner's 1st pick menu: Hoe-jip (회집) = Gwang-eo (광어)·U-reok (우럭) white fish / Il-sik-jip (일식집) = Sashimi + Nigiri (니기리) / Sijang (시장) = Seasonal fish species (#789)

3. Lunch vs. Dinner: Jeom-sim Teuk-seon (점심 특선) (lunch special) is 30~50% cheaper. Recommended for Hoe-jip (회집)·Il-sik-jip (일식집). Sijang (시장) has less atmosphere at lunch.

4. Allergy pre-notification: Hoe-jip (회집) = Maeun-tang (매운탕) shrimp cross-contamination / Il-sik-jip (일식집) = Tsukemono (츠케모노) (pickled vegetables) allergy possible / Sijang (시장) = common pot issue

5. Atmosphere·Dress code: Omakase (오마카세) = smart casual / High-end Hoe-jip (회집) = casual / Sijang (시장)·Chains = free

6. Card payment: Il-sik-jip (일식집)·Hoe-jip (회집) = Card OK / Sijang (시장) stalls = Cash recommended / Sang-cha-rim (상차림) restaurants = Card OK

7. Raw fish beginner fish species recommendation: Gwang-eo (광어) (flounder)·U-reok (우럭) (rockfish)·Do-mi (도미) (sea bream) (mild white fish). Deung-pu-reun-saeng-seon (등푸른생선) (blue-backed fish like Go-deung-eo (고등어) (mackerel)·Jeon-gaeng-i (전갱이) (horse mackerel)) not recommended.

8. VAT refund: Restaurants·Sijang (시장) are generally not eligible for tax refund (general food and beverage). Duty-free shopping refunds are separate.

⚠ Medical·Safety Disclaimer

Hwal-eo Hoe (활어 회) (live raw fish) = raw consumption. Pregnant women, immunocompromised individuals, and those with shellfish allergies are advised to consult a doctor beforehand. Maeun-tang (매운탕) (spicy fish stew) shared pot shrimp cross-contamination is possible — allergy sufferers should request separate cooking or avoid it. In case of suspected food poisoning, call ☎ 1339 (Medical Call Center, English available). Raw fish is not recommended for infants under 3 years old (academic guidelines). This guide provides general information and is not medical advice.

Dietary Restriction Fallback: Raw fish = inherently non-Halal (live animals·no certified slaughter). For companions with Halal, Kosher, or vegetarian diets → Il-sik-jip (일식집) Yasai (野菜) (vegetable) course·Udon (우동)·Twigim (튀김) (fried food) menu separately OR separate dining at a general restaurant near the Sijang (시장). Hoe-jip (회집)·Il-sik-jip (일식집) rarely have separate vegetarian markings on menus — check by phone or with a translation app beforehand.
