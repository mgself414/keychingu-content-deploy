⚠ 3 Common Emergency Mistakes

1. "Emergency = only 119" — not knowing 1330 foreigner assistance: People only know 119. In fact, 1330 (24h foreigner assistance in 11 languages) covers medical, transport, and tourism inquiries alike.

2. "Hospitals = Korean only" — not knowing foreigner-friendly hospitals: People assume Korean-only service. In fact, the international patient centers at Samsung Seoul, Asan Medical Center, and Severance + the foreigner-friendly clusters in Myeongdong, Gangnam, and Itaewon offer English and Chinese support.

3. "Lost items = just report to the hotel" — not knowing the Tourist Police / Incheon Airport: People only think of the hotel. In fact, the Tourist Police (foreigner-friendly, English service) and the Incheon Airport Lost & Found Center are the specialists.
