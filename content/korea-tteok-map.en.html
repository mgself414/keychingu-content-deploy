Korea Tteok (Rice Cake) Guide — Tteok Museum, Nagwon-dong Tteok Street, Gangneung Chodang Chaltteok: 11 Rice Cake Shops and Workshops, Plus Holiday Tteok (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: "Tteok making" (떡 만들기) has been a National Intangible Heritage item since 2021-11-01 (Korea Heritage Service) · Tteok Museum (떡박물관) Mon-Sat 10:00-18:00, adults ₩3,000 (~$2.25) (VisitKorea) · 2027 Seollal (Lunar New Year) Feb 6-8, Chuseok Sep 14-16 (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute) · tteokguk (rice cake soup) for Seollal, songpyeon for Chuseok (Korea Heritage Service) — as of 2026-10-21

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Every time the tteok mallet (떡메) hits the steamed glutinous rice there is a dull thud, and soybean powder from your hands falls onto your trousers. At Yangyang Songcheon Tteok Village (송천떡마을), they still steam the rice over a wood fire, pound it with a mallet and shape it by hand (VisitKorea). This guide splits tteok into three tracks: the Tteok Museum and tteok-making classes, holiday tteok (tteokguk for Seollal, songpyeon for Chuseok, Dongji, Daeboreum), and tteok shops (Nagwon-dong Tteok Street (낙원동 떡전거리) in Seoul and Chodang (초당) in Gangneung). Addresses, hours and prices are only what was read on VisitKorea, the Korea Heritage Service and the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute. It is for people who want to eat tteok itself, not just tteokbokki, and for anyone in Korea during a holiday period.

The old version of this guide said "tteok making = UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage (2018)". That was wrong. Tteok making was designated National Intangible Heritage on November 1, 2021 (Korea Heritage Service) and is not a UNESCO-listed item. The old guide's rice cake called "Hayangsu" (하이양수), its "Danyang tteok" (단양 떡) and its ranking of old shops could not be traced to an official source, so they were removed.

🏛 The Tteok Museum and Tteok-Making Classes

  • What is tteok: Grain flour that is steamed in a pot (siru), steamed and pounded, boiled in water, pan-fried in oil, or shaped and then steamed. It appeared at life-cycle rites such as a baby's first birthday, weddings, 60th birthdays and ancestral rites, and at every seasonal holiday (Korea Heritage Service)
  • National Intangible Heritage "Tteok making": Designated 2021-11-01, classified as traditional living customs and food life (Korea Heritage Service). Only the item was designated, with no individual or group holder
  • Tteok Museum (떡박물관): 71 Donhwamun-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul. Mon-Sat 10:00-18:00 (last entry 17:30), closed Sundays, Seollal and Chuseok. Adults ₩3,000 (~$2.25), ages 36 months to under 19 ₩2,000 (~$1.50), free for under 36 months and 65 and over. Tel. 02-741-5447. Exhibits are organized by solar term, life-cycle rite and method, and it runs hands-on classes (VisitKorea)
  • Songalsongal Tteok Studio (송알송알 떡공방): B103, Hyundai Hillstate commercial building, 39-15 Sugi-ro, Gochon-eup, Gimpo-si, Gyeonggi. One-day classes for foreigners (baram-tteok (wind rice cake), fruit seolgi cake, flower songpyeon). Mon/Wed/Fri 10:30-17:30, Tue/Thu 09:00-17:30, Sat 10:00-16:00, closed Sundays and public holidays. Tel. 0507-1315-3034 (VisitKorea). It is described as being close to Gimpo Airport
  • Yangyang Songcheon Tteok Village (송천떡마을): 107 Tteokmaeul-gil, Seo-myeon, Yangyang-gun, Gangwon. Making chapssal-tteok (glutinous rice cake) and injeolmi, plus mallet pounding. 08:00-18:00 (closes when the tteok sells out), open year-round. Tel. 033-673-8977 (VisitKorea)
  • Hamyang Mulle-bangah Tteok Village (물레방아떡마을): 53 Sinan-gil, Anui-myeon, Hamyang-gun, Gyeongnam. Making traditional injeolmi and tteok cakes. 09:00-17:00, open year-round. Tel. 055-963-6649 (VisitKorea)
  • No class prices appear on official pages except for the Tteok Museum — ask when you book

🏮 Holiday Tteok — Seollal, Daeboreum, Chuseok, Dongji

  • Seollal = tteokguk: People believed that eating tteokguk on New Year's Day made you a year older (Korea Heritage Service). The 2027 Seollal holiday is Feb 6-8, with a substitute holiday on Feb 9 (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute)
  • Jeongwol Daeboreum (first full moon) = yaksik: Yaksik (sweet rice with jujube and nuts) was eaten on Daeboreum (Korea Heritage Service). The 2027 Jeongwol Daeboreum falls on Feb 21 (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute)
  • Chuseok = songpyeon: A holiday tteok shaped from the new harvest and offered at ancestral tables and graves. It varies by region, such as potato songpyeon, radish songpyeon and ramie-leaf songpyeon (Korea Heritage Service). The 2027 Chuseok holiday is Sep 14-16 (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute)
  • Dongji (winter solstice) = red bean porridge, sometimes red bean tteok: When Dongji falls in the early (Aedongji) or middle (Jungdongji) part of the 11th lunar month, there is a custom of making red bean siru-tteok instead of red bean porridge (National Folk Museum of Korea). Dongji 2026 is Dec 22 (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute)
  • Saebom Tteokguk Guksu (새봄떡국국수): 24 Saemunan-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul. 13 kinds of tteokguk (maeseng-i oyster tteokguk, live abalone tteokguk, etc.). Weekdays 09:00-20:30 (last order 20:00), weekends 09:00-19:30, open year-round. Tel. 02-739-0661 (VisitKorea). Price not posted
  • Where to buy songpyeon: Sanyatteul Solhyang Gohyang Tteokjip (산야뜰솔향고향떡집) in Gangneung (songpyeon, injeolmi, gijeongddeok, a steamed fermented rice cake), Cheongchun Cafe & Tteokbang (청춘카페&떡방, chestnut songpyeon) in Pyeongchang, and Songalsongal Tteok Studio (flower songpyeon class) — details in the sections above and below (VisitKorea)

🏪 Tteok Shops — Nagwon-dong Tteok Street and Gangneung Chodang

  • Nagwon Tteokjip (낙원떡집): A tteok shop on Nagwon-dong Tteok Street, Jongno, Seoul. The first generation, who had learned royal court tteok, began selling tteok around Wonseo-dong in 1910, and the shop is now in its fourth generation (Encyclopedia of Korean Culture, Academy of Korean Studies). The address, hours and prices could not be confirmed on an official page
  • Gangneung Chodang Chaltteok (강릉 초당찰떡): 20 Chodangsundubu-gil 77beon-gil, Gangneung. Chaltteok made by pounding rice cooked at dawn (black sesame, injeolmi), and grilled chaltteok. 09:00-18:00, open year-round, made and sold the same day, so it can sell out early. 0507-1361-1205 (VisitKorea)
  • Gangwonok Chapssal-tteok (강원옥 찹쌀떡): 89-8 Chodangsundubu-gil, Gangneung. Glutinous rice cakes made with Chodang soft tofu. 09:30-18:30, open year-round. 0507-1449-0599 (VisitKorea)
  • Gangneung Saimdang Injeolmi (강릉사임당인절미): 349-2 Changhae-ro, Gangneung. Injeolmi with soybean powder, coffee or black sesame. 09:00-21:00, open year-round (VisitKorea)
  • Sanyatteul Solhyang Gohyang Tteokjip (산야뜰솔향고향떡집): 21-6 Siyeong-gil, Gangneung. Chapssal-tteok, injeolmi, songpyeon, gijeongddeok. 07:00-19:00, open year-round, phone reservations 033-647-0451 (VisitKorea)
  • Cheongchun Cafe & Tteokbang (청춘카페&떡방): 15 Jinbusijang-dwitgil, Jinbu-myeon, Pyeongchang-gun, Gangwon. A tteok cafe run by seniors from the Pyeongchang Senior Club, with different tteok every day. Mon-Fri 10:00-17:00 (open on weekends when the 5-day market is held), 0507-1413-7714 (VisitKorea)
  • None of the five places has a price field on its VisitKorea page

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Price range: The only officially posted won price is the Tteok Museum admission (adults ₩3,000, ~$2.25). Tteok shops and class venues do not post prices
  • Vegetarian: Tteok is grain flour at its base (Korea Heritage Service), but tteokguk broth differs by shop — Saebom Tteokguk Guksu's menu includes oyster and abalone tteokguk (VisitKorea). Ask about the broth before ordering
  • Allergies: Injeolmi coating is soybean (soybean-powder injeolmi, VisitKorea), and Gangwonok's chapssal-tteok contains soft tofu, fresh cream and cream cheese (VisitKorea). Avoid them if you have a soy or milk allergy
  • Halal: VisitSeoul lists Eid (이드) in Itaewon (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil, Yongsan-gu; daily 12:00-21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF). Certification can change, so check before you go. None of the tteok shops in this guide carry a halal label
  • Show-it cards: #1300 Dietary Request Manual

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Tteok making is UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage" — it is National Intangible Heritage (designated 2021-11-01) (Korea Heritage Service)

2. "Visiting the Tteok Museum on a Sunday" — it is closed on Sundays, Seollal and Chuseok (VisitKorea)

3. "Dongji always means red bean porridge" — in Aedongji and Jungdongji years there is also a custom of making red bean siru-tteok (National Folk Museum of Korea)

4. "Touring tteok shops on the holiday itself" — the Tteok Museum is closed on Seollal and Chuseok, and shop closing days vary by store. During the 2027 Seollal holiday (2/6-2/8) and Chuseok holiday (9/14-9/16), call first
