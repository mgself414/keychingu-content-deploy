⚠ 3 Tteok Mistakes

1. "Tteok = only tteokbokki" — not knowing the 5 traditional tteok: Tteokbokki only. In reality, the full experience covers the 5 types injeolmi, songpyeon, yakbap, chaltteok, jeungpyeon + seasonal garaetteok, yakgwa, and haiyangsu.

2. "Tteok = Korean-style, not for foreigners" — not knowing the Japanese mochi fit: Assuming it doesn't suit foreign palates. In reality, it draws foreigner-friendly enthusiasts: #190 Japan mochi-culture fit, #192 Southeast Asia tteok enthusiasts, #194 USA K-drama tteok exposure (Crash Landing on You, D.P., etc.).

3. "Tteok = no seasons" — not knowing holiday tteok: Assuming it's season-free. In reality, there are 4 seasons — Seollal garaetteok, Jeongwol Daeboreum yakbap, Chuseok songpyeon, Dongji red bean tteok + UNESCO holiday fit.

🍯 5 Tips for Foreign Visitors

1. Injeolmi = the #1 gateway for foreigners — roasted soybean powder, strawberry, green tea, #469 bingsu injeolmi sub

2. Songpyeon = Chuseok only — 15th day of the 8th lunar month (Sept–Oct season only)

3. Tteok-making = a UNESCO experience — 2018 Intangible Cultural Heritage · English-language experiences available for foreigners

4. Long-established tteok shops = Nakwon & Danyang — Korea's #1 long-established tteok shops · Instagram signatures

5. Mochi vs. tteok = fermented vs. non-fermented — the differentiator for #190 Japan enthusiasts (jeungpyeon is the closest mochi fit)
