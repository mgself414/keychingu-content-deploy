🎯 How to Use This Guide

Spring shifts by one to two weeks every year. So instead of writing "cherry blossoms in early April," we rewrote this guide to record when the blossoms actually opened and the festivals actually ran in 2026. For 2027, plan around those dates, then confirm once the bloom forecast comes out in late February to early March. The 2026 Seoul bloom date (Mar 29) comes from a Korea Meteorological Administration (기상청) press release (official). The festival dates are press and blog sources, because the local government sites would not open. No 2027 dates exist anywhere yet. We removed the old guide's "No.1 Jinhae / No.2 Goyang" ranking, its "Anmyeondo biennial flower fair" claim, and its budget figures, because they were unsourced or wrong.

📅 How to Plan for 2027

1. Late February to early March: Check the private weather companies' bloom forecast (in 2026 it came out on Feb 26) and the Korea Meteorological Administration (기상청) outlook. As of Oct 8, no official 2027 forecast has been published.

2. Book flights and lodging for the forecast ± 1 week. In Seoul and Yeouido, full bloom arrives about a week after the first bloom.

3. Confirm festival dates on each local government's official site. They tend to fall around the 2026 dates in the table above. Jinhae, Yeouido and Seokchon Lake shift their dates to match the bloom.

4. The south (Gwangyang, Gurye, Jinhae) comes first, then Seoul and Gyeongju. The Goyang flower fair and roses come in May.

🌷 Beyond Cherry Blossoms

  • Plum and cornelian cherry (mid-March, Gwangyang (광양) and Gurye (구례)): two weeks before the cherry blossoms. Add them to a south-coast itinerary.
  • Tulips (Taean, April–May): since 2026 the fair is at Magheompo (마검포) in Nam-myeon. The old guide's "Anmyeondo (안면도) biennial flower fair" is outdated information.
  • Flower fair (Goyang, late April–early May) and roses (Jungnang, mid-May).
  • Palace nights (Changdeokgung Moonlight Tour (창덕궁 달빛기행), Apr 16–May 31, 30,000 won, lottery). We could not confirm the 2026 dates for the Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) spring night opening.

😷 Yellow Dust and Fine Dust

  • Spring brings frequent yellow dust and fine dust. Check the AirKorea (에어코리아) (airkorea.or.kr) forecast. We give no figures this time because the official page could not be read.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Booking for "early April cherry blossoms" — in 2026 the bloom was Mar 29. Plan from the forecast.

2. Visiting Jinhae in mid-April — in 2026 it ended Apr 5.

3. Looking for tulips at Kkotji in Taean — since 2026 they are at Magheompo (마검포).

4. Buying Moonlight Tour tickets on site — booking is by lottery.

5. Trusting the old guide's "No.1 / No.2" — it has no source.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. When will Seoul's cherry blossoms bloom in 2027? — Not yet known. We confirm after the late-February to early-March forecast. In 2026 it was Mar 29.

2. What blooms earliest? — Gwangyang Maehwa Festival (광양 매화축제) plum and Gurye Sansuyu Festival (구례 산수유축제) cornelian cherry (mid-March).

3. Cherry blossom festival admission? — Yeouido is free (blog). Taean tulips are 14,000 won; Goyang is unconfirmed.

4. Cherry blossoms in Seoul as a day trip? — Yeouido Yunjung-ro (여의도 윤중로), Seokchon Lake (석촌호수), Yangjaecheon (양재천).

5. Cherry blossoms at a palace at night? — Changdeokgung Moonlight Tour (창덕궁 달빛기행) (Apr 16–May 31, 30,000 won, lottery).

6. Where do I confirm dates? — The Korea Meteorological Administration (기상청) bloom announcement plus each local government's official site.
