🎯 Why “Korea Spring Map” meta

Korean spring season (March to May) sees a surge in foreign enthusiasts — Japan, China, Southeast Asia, and Europe are ranked No. 1 among cherry blossom and flower exhibition enthusiasts. #349 Seoul 4 Seasons only covers Seoul, but this guide covers Korea Nationwide Spring Matrix (Seoul, Jinhae, Yeouido, Taean + outskirts). Key points: (1) Cherry blossoms (early and mid-April, Jinhae Gunhangje, Yeouido Yunjung-ro, Gyeongju, Seoul Forest, Namsan) (2) Plum blossoms (March, Gwangyang and Yangsan) (3) Tulips and flower fairs (April and May, Taean Anmyeondo and Everland) (4) Spring greens and strawberry season (5) Hanbok and palace spring scenery.

This guide is Spring 4th Axis 12 Signature + 1 week full package. Season/Festival Category Meta Part 1.

⚠ 3 Spring Mistakes

1. "Cherry blossoms = Seoul Bay" — Jinhae, Gyeongju, Gwangyang Miji: Only Seoul is recognized. In reality, Jinhae Gunhang Festival (Korea #1) + Gyeongju (Silla+Cherry Blossoms) + Gwangyang Plum Blossoms Full experience of spring flowers across Korea.

2. "Cherry blossom season is short" — No use of plum, tulip, and flower fairs: Only one week of cherry blossoms is recognized. In reality, Plum (March) → Cherry Blossoms (April) → Tulip/Flower Expo (April-May) = 3 months full experience.

3. "Spring = only flowers" — Recognition of spring vegetables and gourmets: Recognition of only flowers. In reality, it is Spring greens (March to May), strawberry season, water parsley, spring limited edition gourmet signature.
