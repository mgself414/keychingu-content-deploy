Chungcheong Day Trips for Foreign Visitors: Four Routes (Gongju, Buyeo, Cheongju, Seosan), Pick One Per Day (Official Info as of 2026)

Subtitle: One day from Seoul. Pick a single route: Gongju UNESCO Baekje, Buyeo Sabi Baekje, Cheongju Cheongnamdae, or Seosan Haemi Eupseong Fortress and Ganwolam. Hours, fees and closing days are only the values read on each official site on 2026-10-07

ISSUE 04 · #1041 · 2026-06-08

🎯 How to Use This Guide

The four Chungcheong cities are far apart. Gongju (공주) and Buyeo (부여) are close by bus, but Cheongju (청주) (in North Chungcheong) and Seosan (서산) (on the South Chungcheong west coast) are each a long way from Gongju. The old guide's "Cheongju → Gongju → Buyeo → Seosan" in one day is not possible. Pick one route per day, and with two days, pair Gongju and Buyeo. The hours, fees and closing days below were read on each official site on 2026-10-07, and anything I could not read is marked "unconfirmed".

Monday warning: Gongju National Museum (국립공주박물관), Buyeo National Museum (국립부여박물관), Baekje Cultural Land (백제문화단지) and Cheongnamdae (청남대) are closed on Mondays (official). On a Monday, build your day around outdoor sites such as Gongsanseong Fortress (공산성), Busosanseong Fortress (부소산성), Jeongnimsa Temple Site (정림사지), Haemi Eupseong Fortress (해미읍성) and Ganwolam (간월암).

① Gongju: UNESCO Ungjin Baekje (details in #1043)

  • Gongsanseong Fortress (공산성): Mar-Oct 09:00-18:00 · Nov-Feb 09:00-17:00. Adults ₩3,000 · teens ₩2,000 · children ₩1,000. Free for ages 65+ and preschoolers (official).
  • Tomb of King Muryeong and Royal Tombs (무령왕릉과 왕릉원): same hours, same fees. Combined ticket (Tomb of King Muryeong + Gongsanseong) ₩6,000 for adults (official).
  • Gongju National Museum (국립공주박물관): 09:00-18:00 · free · closed Mondays (Tuesday if Monday is a substitute holiday) · Jan 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok.
  • Magoksa Temple (마곡사) (UNESCO mountain monastery, 2018): free · open year-round · bus 770 from Gongju Intercity Bus Terminal (공주종합터미널), about 40 minutes (official).
  • Getting there: KTX or SRT between Yongsan and Gongju Station takes about 1.5 hours (Gongju city KTX page). From the station into town, take a bus or taxi.
  • Baekje Cultural Festival 2026: Oct 3-11, held in Gongju and Buyeo at the same time (official).

② Buyeo: UNESCO Sabi Baekje

Buyeo is Sabi (사비), the last capital of Baekje (the old guide's "Sabi capital" entry under Gongju was an error). Of the 8 sites in the UNESCO Baekje Historic Areas, 5 are in Buyeo (Busosanseong Fortress, Gwanbuk-ri, Jeongnimsa Temple Site, the Royal Tombs at Neungsan-ri, and Naseong), 2 are in Gongju and 2 are in Iksan (UNESCO official list).

  • Jeongnimsa Temple Site and Jeongnimsa Temple Site Museum (정림사지·정림사지박물관): Mar-Oct 09:00-18:00 · Nov-Feb 09:00-17:00 · open year-round. Museum free (Buyeo county, official). This is where the Baekje five-story stone pagoda stands.
  • Busosanseong Fortress (부소산성), Nakhwaam Rock (낙화암) and Goransa Temple (고란사): same hours · open year-round. Adults ₩2,000 · teens ₩1,100 · children ₩1,000, free for ages 65+ (Buyeo county, official). Tourist information center 041-830-2880.
  • Buyeo Royal Tombs (부여왕릉원) (the Neungsan-ri tombs): same hours · open year-round. Adults ₩1,000 · teens ₩600 · children ₩400 (official).
  • Buyeo National Museum (국립부여박물관): free · closed Mondays · Jan 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok (official). The official table is unclear on closing time, so confirm on site.
  • Baekje Cultural Land (백제문화단지) (a restored theme park): Mar-Oct 09:00-18:00 · Nov-Feb 09:00-17:00 · closed Mondays (open if Monday is a public holiday). Adults ₩6,000 · teens ₩4,500 · children ₩3,000, free for ages 65+ (official). 041-408-7290.
  • Gungnamji Pond (궁남지): no official posting of fees or hours that I could confirm (an outdoor pond garden).
  • Getting there: Buyeo has no railway. Take an intercity bus from Seoul Nambu Bus Terminal, or an intercity bus from Gongju (check travel time on the booking site; I could not read it officially).

③ Cheongju: Cheongnamdae (the Presidential Retreat)

  • Cheongnamdae (청남대): visiting hours 09:00-18:00 (Dec-Jan 17:00) · ticket sales 09:00-16:30 (Dec-Jan 15:30) · closed Mondays (the next weekday if Monday is a public holiday), Jan 1, and on Lunar New Year and Chuseok days. Adults ₩6,000 · teens ₩4,000 · children ₩3,000 · free for ages 65+ and under 7 · free parking (official). The monorail runs 09:30-18:00 (not running 12:00-13:00).
  • Getting there (official): you drive in, park, and buy tickets at the ticket office. The old guide's "shuttle required" is not in the current guidance. There is no direct public transit on the official page, and the Cheongju City Tour Bus (booked through Cheongju's integrated reservation system) is the alternative. Address: 646 Cheongnamdae-gil, Munui-myeon, Sangdang-gu, Cheongju.
  • Temporary October closures (official social media, Oct 2): Oct 6 (Tue) and Oct 12 (Mon).
  • Suamgol Mural Village (수암골 벽화마을): an outdoor neighborhood, so no hours or fees (no official posting that I could confirm). Take a bus or taxi from central Cheongju.
  • Osong Station (오송역): from Seoul take the KTX or SRT to Osong Station, then a bus or taxi into central Cheongju. The old guide's "KTX Cheongju Station" is wrong. KTX does not stop at Cheongju Station.

④ Seosan: Haemi Eupseong Fortress and Ganwolam

  • Haemi Eupseong Fortress (해미읍성) (No. 1 of the Seosan 9 Scenic Spots): the wall is 1,800 m around and 5 m high, built from the 18th year of King Taejong to the 3rd year of King Sejong of Joseon and completed in 1491 · a site Pope Francis visited · on the Korea Tourism 100 list (official). Address: 143 Nammun 2-ro, Haemi-myeon, Seosan · 041-661-8005. Hours and fees are not posted on the official page.
  • Ganwolam (간월암) (No. 3): a sea temple that is an island at high tide and joined to land at low tide. The official page gives the Ganwoldo (간월도) tide table, so check it before you go. Founded by Monk Muhak, royal preceptor to King Taejo of Joseon · a sunset spot. Address: 119-29 Ganwoldo 1-gil, Buseok-myeon, Seosan.
  • Rock-carved Buddha Triad in Yonghyeon-ri (용현리 마애여래삼존상) (No. 2): National Treasure No. 84, the "Baekje Smile" (official).
  • Getting there: from Seoul, take an express or intercity bus to Seosan Terminal (check travel time on the booking site). The three sites are far apart, so from Seosan a car is the realistic choice.
  • Food information such as Seosan crab gejang and eoguljeot (spicy fermented oysters) is left out of this guide, because I could not read a source for it on the official tourism pages.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Two or more routes in one day: Cheongju and Seosan do not combine with Gongju and Buyeo. One route per day.

2. Museums or Cheongnamdae on a Monday: Gongju National Museum, Buyeo National Museum, Baekje Cultural Land and Cheongnamdae are closed.

3. "KTX Cheongju Station": it does not exist. Go to Osong Station.

4. Thinking Sabi is Gongju: Sabi is Buyeo. Gongju is Ungjin (웅진).

5. Going to Ganwolam without the tide table: at high tide you cannot walk in. Check the official tide table.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Which route if it's my first time? Gongju. The KTX goes straight there and it has the best English guidance (#1043).

2. What about two days? Gongju one day and Buyeo one day. Travel between Gongju and Buyeo by bus.

3. Cheongnamdae without a car? The official guidance only covers driving in, and the Cheongju City Tour Bus is the alternative.

4. Seosan without a car? Buses reach the terminal, and after that a taxi or rental car is the realistic choice.

5. What is the total cost? Gongju combined ticket ₩6,000 + free museum / four Buyeo sites about ₩9,000 together / Cheongnamdae ₩6,000. Meals are extra.

6. What about the Baekje Cultural Festival? In 2026 it runs Oct 3-11 in Gongju and Buyeo at the same time (official). During the festival, Gongju lodging fills up first.
