1. "Bukchon·Ikseon = A place to go only during the day": Bukchon·Ikseon·Insa·Seochon Hanok = Night lantern·Tea ceremony·Hanok cafe Matrix #1. Hanok at night = foreign mania photogenic + meditation wellness matrix. 18:00~22:00 = Hanok best at night.

2. "Tea ceremony = too formal": Korean tea ceremony (茶道) = Foreign enthusiast wellness matrix #1 + English explanation OK many. Insa·Bukchon Tea House = Tea Ceremony Etiquette Guide (English) + Hanbok Experience cross. Foreign enthusiast entry-level fit.

3. "Hanok cafe = take off your shoes": Sitting (tea ceremony, veranda) = take off your shoes. Bar table/chair type = Shoes OK. Shoe cabinet/shoes OK sign at store entrance. Foreign enthusiasts = Available for both sitting and chair types.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Best time for Hanok at night: Best between 18:00 and 22:00 after sunset (Lamplight/Quiet Matrix #1). Tea Ceremony = 10:00~17:00 best (tea ceremony experience store operating hours). Night Hanok Wine Bar/Cocktail Bar = 19:00~24:00 best.

2. How ​​to make a reservation: Tea ceremony experience store = Naver reservation or Instagram DM recommended (English OK). Hanok cafe·wine bar·cocktail bar = catchtable or walk-in. Foreign Mania = English Interface OK.
