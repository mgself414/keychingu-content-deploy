1. "Gukbap = one dish" — missing the 5 axes: Seen as a single dish. In reality it's the seolleongtang · galbitang · sundae-guk · ppwem-gukbap · gomtang 5 axes, plus regional clusters — Busan dwaeji gukbap (#382 standalone) and Naju gomtang (#384).

2. "Gukbap = hangover cure only" — missing that it's the #1 everyday meal: Hangover cure only. In reality, it's the full experience of Korea's #1 everyday meal · breakfast to start the day (old-school spots open at 06:00) · office-worker lunch · 24h Jongno 3-ga (#338) hangover cure.
