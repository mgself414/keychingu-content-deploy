Korean Gukbap Guide — Seolleongtang, Gomtang and Pork Gukbap at 11 Places (Imun Seolnongtang (이문설농탕), Hadongwan (하동관), Mapo-ok (마포옥), Okdongsik (옥동식), Hapcheon Gukbapjip (합천국밥집)) in Seoul and Busan (Official Figures, October 2026)

Subtitle: Imun Seolnongtang (이문설농탕) opens at 08:00, open year-round (VisitKorea) · Hadongwan (하동관) opened in 1939, Mon–Sat 07:00–16:00 (Michelin page) · Mapo-ok (마포옥) since 1949, opens daily at 07:00 (Michelin page) · Two Seoul pork gukbap Bib Gourmand spots (Okdongsik (옥동식) and Anam (안암)) · Busan's Jeongjitgan (정짓간) is open 24 hours (VisitKorea) — as of 2026-10-21

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Steam rises from an earthenware bowl (ttukbaegi (뚝배기)), and hot broth has already soaked in between the grains of rice — this is toryeom (토렴), where the rice is warmed by ladling hot broth over it and pouring it off again and again (Michelin pages for Mapo-ok (마포옥), Hapjeong-ok (합정옥) and Okdongsik (옥동식)). This guide covers only gukbap (국밥), meaning rice served in soup. There are four branches: seolleongtang (설렁탕, ox-bone soup) and doganitang (도가니탕, beef knee cartilage soup), gomtang (곰탕, clear beef soup), Seoul pork gukbap, and Busan pork gukbap. Stews and hangover soups boiled in a pot are split off into the #486 Jjigae and Haejangguk Guide. We picked only places listed on the official Michelin Guide site and in VisitKorea tourism data, for travelers who want to start the day with a warm bowl early in the morning.

The old guide called Hadongwan (하동관) a "Gangnam galbitang (갈비탕) place", but on its Michelin page Hadongwan is a gomtang (곰탕) restaurant in Myeongdong (12 Myeongdong 9-gil (명동9길), Jung District). Claims such as a foreigner popularity ranking, "5 great old restaurants over 50 years old" and "99% card acceptance" had no source, so we removed them.

🥣 Seolleongtang and Doganitang — 3 Places in Seoul

  • Imun Seolnongtang (이문설농탕) — 38-13 Ujeongguk-ro (우정국로), Jongno District, Seoul. Mon–Sat 08:00–21:00, Sun 08:00–20:00, break 15:00–16:30, open year-round, may close early when ingredients run out. Signature dish is seolnongtang (설농탕, ox-bone soup); doganitang (도가니탕), meoritang (머리탕, head-meat soup) and suyuk (수육, boiled sliced meat) are also served. Phone 02-733-6526 (VisitKorea). The beef leg bones are simmered about 17 hours and the fat is skimmed off. Michelin describes it as Seoul City license No. 1 restaurant (Michelin page; no Michelin selection or Bib Gourmand mark). The founding year differs between sources: the VisitKorea tourism data says 1907, while a VisitKorea article (written in 2017) says 1904
  • Mapo-ok (마포옥) — 312 Tojeong-ro (토정로), Mapo District. Mon–Fri 07:00–21:00, Sat–Sun 07:00–17:00. Opened in 1949. A clear broth made from brisket, chadol (차돌, beef brisket flank) and leg bones, topped with a generous pile of brisket. Chadoltang (차돌탕) is also recommended. Phone +82 2-716-6661, cards accepted (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Daeseongjip (대성집) — 5 Sajik-ro (사직로), Jongno District. Mon–Fri 10:30–14:30 and 17:00–20:00, Sat 10:30–14:00 and 17:00–19:00, closed Sun. Served in a milky broth with doga (도가니, knee cartilage), seuji (스지, beef tendon) and sat'ae (사태, shank). Phone +82 2-735-4259 (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Price: The Michelin pages show only the ₩ symbol. A 2017 VisitKorea article lists Imun Seolnongtang's seolleongtang (설렁탕) at ₩9,000 (~$6.75), but we could not confirm today's price

🍲 Gomtang — 3 Places in Seoul

  • Hadongwan (하동관) — 12 Myeongdong 9-gil (명동9길), Jung District. Mon–Sat 07:00–16:00, closed Sun. Opened in 1939. Gomtang with brisket, naepo (내포, tripe and offal) and yang (양, tripe) served with Seoul-style kkakdugi (깍두기, cubed radish kimchi). Payment is up front, and at breakfast and lunch you queue and share a table. It closes when the day's prepared amount runs out. Phone +82 2-776-5656 (Michelin page; no Michelin selection or Bib Gourmand mark)
  • Hapjeong-ok (합정옥) — 21 Yanghwa-ro 1-gil (양화로1길), 2nd floor, Mapo District. Mon–Sat 11:00–22:00, closed Sun. Only about 100 servings of soup are prepared per day, and it closes when they sell out. Gomtang, suyuk (수육, boiled sliced meat) and sokdaetguk (속댓국, cabbage-heart soybean paste soup). Phone +82 2-322-4822 (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Neungdong Minari (능동미나리) — 28 Hangang-daero 40-gil (한강대로40길), Yongsan District. Mon–Fri 10:00–23:00, Sat–Sun 09:30–23:00. A clear Korean-beef gomtang (곰탕) topped with plenty of minari (미나리, water dropwort). Reservations possible via Naver (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)

🐖 Seoul Pork Gukbap — 2 Places

  • Okdongsik (옥동식) — 44-10 Yanghwa-ro 7-gil (양화로7길), Mapo District. Mon–Fri 11:00–15:00 and 17:00–22:00, Sat–Sun 11:00–20:30. A clear broth made from the fore and hind leg meat of Jirisan black pigs (지리산 흑돼지), rice warmed by toryeom (토렴) and thinly sliced pork. About 10 seats, limited to 100 bowls a day. Counter seating (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Anam (안암) — 10 Bukchon-ro 5-gil (북촌로5길), Jongno District. Mon–Wed 11:30–15:00 and 17:00–21:00, Thu–Sun 11:30–21:00. A clear broth with cheongyang chili (청양고추) and kale oil, and spare ribs and neck meat from Spanish Duroc pigs. Portions are "regular" or "extra meat", and coriander can be added on request. Counter seating, cards accepted (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)

🌊 Busan Pork Gukbap — 3 Places

  • Hapcheon Gukbapjip (합천국밥집) — 235 Yongho-ro (용호로), Nam District, Busan. 09:30–20:00, break 14:00–14:30, open year-round. Signature dish is ttaro-gukbap (따로국밥, rice served separately from the soup); suyuk baekban (수육백반, boiled pork set meal) and seokkeo ttaro-gukbap (섞어따로국밥, mixed-meat version) are also available (VisitKorea). Solo diners are not accepted 11:00–13:00. Suyuk (수육) is limited in quantity and sells out if you come late (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Anmok (안목) — 3 Gwangnam-ro 22beon-gil (광남로22번길), Suyeong District, Busan. Daily 11:00–21:00. Michelin advises tasting the broth before adding dadaegi (다대기, seasoned chili paste) or saeujeot (새우젓, salted shrimp). It is described as comfortable for dining alone. Counter seating (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Jeongjitgan (정짓간) Sinpyeong Main Branch — 6 Bibong-ro (비봉로), 1st floor, Saha District, Busan. Open 24 hours, "closes at 12 on Tuesdays" (as written — call to check whether that means noon or midnight), open year-round. Besides pork gukbap, it serves sundae-gukbap (순대국밥, blood-sausage gukbap) and naejang-gukbap (내장국밥, offal gukbap) (VisitKorea). Opened in 2011, Naver reservations possible (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand). Many cells in the Michelin page's day-by-day hours are blank, so we listed the VisitKorea values
  • For Busan food in general, see #1320 Three Things You Only Eat in Busan

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Price range: All 11 places in this guide carry the ₩ price symbol (the lowest tier) on their Michelin pages. None has an officially posted price in won — the 2017 VisitKorea article values (Imun Seolnongtang (이문설농탕) ₩9,000 (~$6.75), Hadongwan (하동관) ₩12,000 (~$9)) are not today's prices
  • Vegetarian: Gukbap broth is made from beef bones or beef (seolleongtang, gomtang) or pork (pork gukbap) (each Michelin page). It is safest to assume that no place can switch to a vegetarian broth. VisitKorea notes that most kimchi seasoning also contains salted seafood, fish sauce or meat stock
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil (우사단로10길), Yongsan District, daily 12:00–21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF). None of the gukbap places in this guide carries a halal label, and pork gukbap is a pork dish
  • Allergies: Gomtang and seolleongtang may come with offal such as yang (양, tripe) and naepo (내포) mixed in (Michelin pages for Hadongwan (하동관) and Daeseongjip (대성집)) — ask in advance to leave it out
  • For a show-it-to-the-staff card, see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Gukbap Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Ordering, Getting There

  • Solo: Gukbap is one bowl per person, so it is easy to go alone. However, Hapcheon Gukbapjip (합천국밥집) does not accept solo diners 11:00–13:00 (Michelin page). For a solo-dining map, see #348 Seoul Solo Diner Map
  • Reservations: Most places take no reservations and you queue. Naver reservations are open at Neungdong Minari (능동미나리) and Jeongjitgan (정짓간) (Michelin pages)
  • Payment: Hadongwan (하동관) is pay-first (Michelin page). For cards and mobile payment, see #527 Korea's Payment Systems
  • Ordering: For places that close early once the day's batch runs out, such as Hadongwan (하동관) and Hapjeong-ok (합정옥), go before lunch. If there is a language barrier, see #1339 Ordering Etiquette
  • Getting there: For Seoul subway and buses, see #1298 Choosing from the 3 Transit Cards; for Busan, #1325 Busan Public Transit Grammar

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Hadongwan is a Gangnam galbitang place" — On its Michelin page, Hadongwan (하동관) is a gomtang (곰탕) restaurant in Myeongdong. It is closed on Sundays and closes at 16:00

2. "Old-school gukbap places stay open late into the night" — Hadongwan (하동관) closes at 16:00, Daeseongjip (대성집) at 19:00 on Saturdays, and Mapo-ok (마포옥) at 17:00 on weekends (Michelin pages). For late-night gukbap, there is little besides the 24-hour Jeongjitgan (정짓간) in Busan (VisitKorea)

3. "Pork gukbap exists only in Busan" — Seoul also has two Michelin Bib Gourmand pork gukbap places (Okdongsik (옥동식) and Anam (안암))

4. "Gukbap costs the same everywhere" — There are no officially posted prices. Photograph the menu board before you order
