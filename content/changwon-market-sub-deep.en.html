A Day in the Markets of Changwon Masan: Fish Market, Food Alley, Agujjim, Art Village

Subtitle: Masan Fish Market (마산어시장; 200+ raw-fish restaurants and fish tanks, 10:00~22:00 varies by shop) · Burim Market Food Alley (부림시장 먹자골목; 1970s workers' eatery alley) · Odong-dong Agujjim Street (오동동 아구찜거리; started in the early 1960s, about 20 shops) · Changdong Art Village (창동예술촌; 10:00~18:00, closed Mondays) · Sangnam Market (상남시장) in downtown Changwon — verified with Korea Tourism Organization data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Changwon City includes the former Changwon, Masan, and Jinhae (진해). Jinhae is the one famous for spring cherry blossoms, but for markets and food you head to old Masan, right on the sea. A fish market overflowing with seafood, an alley where factory workers once ate noodles on the way home, the agujjim, braised spicy monkfish (아귀찜), street, and an alley that revived the old downtown as an art village are all in Masanhappo-gu.

This guide is arranged in the order three Masan markets · Changdong Art Village · downtown Changwon market, and operating details are mostly copied from values registered with the Korea Tourism Organization's tourism data (TourAPI). For Jinhae cherry blossoms, follow the Jinhae guide under Related Content below.

🐟 Masan Fish Market — Where South Coast Seafood Gathers

Masan Fish Market at 7 Bogyori-ro, Masanhappo-gu (복요리로 7), Dongseong-dong, Changwon, ☎ 055-224-0009, has 200+ raw-fish restaurants and fish tanks according to the Korea Tourism Organization. You can see fish caught off the south coast in one place, from premium species to mackerel and pollack, and dried fish and salted seafood are sold too.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~22:00, varies by shop. Closing days are also set by each shop.
  • Cards: Varies by store. Bring a little cash.
  • History: It is said to have begun when a village grew up around Masanchang (마산창), a Joseon-era storehouse for grain collected as tax, and the "Masanjang" (마산장) market opened.
  • Parking: Available.

🍜 Burim Market Food Alley — The Factory Workers' Evening Alley

Burim Market Food Alley at 352 3·15-daero, Masanhappo-gu (3·15대로 352), ☎ 055-240-3729, lies on the way from Burim Market toward Changdong. In the 1970s, workers from the Masan Free Export Zone factories came here after work for a cheap meal and a drink.

  • What to eat: Noodles, spicy cold noodles, cold noodle soup, jjolmyeon, japchae.
  • Opening hours and closing days: Vary by shop.
  • Parking: 500 won for the first 30 minutes, then 200 won per 10 minutes, up to 6,000 won a day.
  • The Korea Tourism Organization says the nearby Burim Market Creative Craft Village makes a good add-on stop.

🥘 Odong-dong Agujjim Street — Where the Tourism Organization Says Agujjim Began

Odong-dong Agujjim Street at 18 Eosijang 6-gil, Masanhappo-gu (어시장6길 18), Namseong-dong, is the street the Korea Tourism Organization presents as the place where agujjim first started in Masan. According to that introduction, in the early 1960s the "Hokburi Halmeoni" (혹부리 할머니), who ran a sea eel restaurant in Odong-dong, steamed monkfish brought by fishermen with soybean paste, red pepper paste, bean sprouts, water dropwort, and green onion. Word spread among fishermen, and the street grew into one with about 20 shops.

  • The street is packed with specialists in dried-monkfish agujjim made with dried monkfish.
  • Opening hours and closing days vary by shop, and cards are accepted. Inquiries: Changwon City Hall Tourism Division ☎ 055-225-3690.
  • One street restaurant registered with the Korea Tourism Organization, as an example: Masan Jeontong Agujjim (마산전통아구찜) at 29 Dongseobuk 17-gil, Masanhappo-gu (동서북17길 29), ☎ 055-221-8989 — 10:00~22:30, open year-round, signature dish agujjim, with mideodeok-jjim (미더덕찜; steamed sea squirt), boiled monkfish slices, and monkfish soup also on the menu (Korea Tourism Organization registered information).
  • A comparison with steamed dishes from other regions is in #896 Jjim Comparison.

🎨 Changdong Art Village — Alleys of the Old Downtown

Changdong Art Village at 24 Odongseo 6-gil, Masanhappo-gu (오동서6길 24), ☎ 055-222-2155, is an urban regeneration alley that opened on May 25, 2012. In the old downtown that was the center of Masan culture and art in the 1950s and 60s, three themed alleys were created: Munsin Art Alley (문신예술골목), Masan Art Trace Alley (마산예술흔적골목), and École de Changdong Alley (에꼴드창동골목). More than 60 resident facilities operate here.

  • Operation: 10:00~18:00 (12:00~13:00 prep time), closed every Monday.
  • Experiences: Workshop experiences include drawing crafts, woodworking, glass crafts, and oil painting. To sign up for an experience, call ☎ 055-224-7997.
  • The Burim Market Food Alley continues toward Changdong, so it pairs well with a market visit.

🏙 Downtown Changwon — Sangnam Market

Sangnam Market at 28 Madimi-ro, Seongsan-gu (마디미로 28), Changwon, ☎ 055-262-1494, is a market in a modern building in Changwon's central commercial district. It opened on October 22, 2000, and a five-day market is held along with the permanent market.

  • By floor: 1F produce, seafood, and meat; 2F sundries, clothing, and bedding; 3F restaurant floor.
  • It also sells things hard to find in supermarkets, such as earthenware jars and medicinal herbs.
  • Opening hours vary by shop, and cards work at some shops.

🚄 Getting There

  • KTX: The KTX stops at three stations in Changwon: Changwon Jungang Station (창원중앙역), Changwon Station (창원역), and Masan Station (마산역), per news reports. For old Masan, where the markets are, get off at Masan Station. The booking steps are covered in #1287 Riding the KTX for the First Time.
  • Masan Fish Market, Burim Market, Odong-dong, and Changdong are all in Masanhappo-gu. From Masan Station, get around by city bus or taxi.
  • For an itinerary combined with Busan, also see #244 Busan Jagalchi and Gukje Market Guide.
  • Tourist interpretation helpline ☎ 1330 · Emergency ☎ 119.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Getting off at Changwon Jungang Station (창원중앙역) or Changwon Station (창원역) to look for the fish market — the markets and food alleys are in old Masan (Masanhappo-gu). Get off at Masan Station.
  2. Visiting Changdong Art Village on a Monday — Changdong Art Village is closed every Monday (Korea Tourism Organization registration data). Markets open shop by shop.
  3. Looking only for fresh-monkfish agujjim on agujjim street — Odong-dong is a street of dried-monkfish agujjim specialists that use dried monkfish.

⚙ FAQ 3

Q. Can I see the Jinhae cherry blossoms on the same day?

A. Jinhae is also part of Changwon City. For the cherry blossom season schedule in Jinhae, see #1062 Jinhae Gunhangje Cherry Blossoms, and fit the Masan markets into the morning or evening.

Q. How do I eat raw fish at the fish market?

A. The fish market has 200+ raw-fish restaurants and fish tanks. Methods and prices differ by shop, so ask before you choose.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. According to Korea Tourism Organization information, it varies by store at Masan Fish Market, cards are accepted at Odong-dong Agujjim Street, and it varies by shop at Sangnam Market.
