Locals' Picks ⑨ Incheon & Northwest Gyeonggi — 30 Eateries Korean Viewers Named, on the Side You Reach Straight From the Airport

Subtitle: An axis the Incheon meta guide (#394) and the Incheon day-trip pieces (#1011-1015) never covered — the 30 Incheon and Northwest Gyeonggi spots Korean viewers named in YouTube comments are scattered across Yeongjongdo and Cheongna, the Gimpo Goldline corridor, Paju's Gyoha, and Ilsan, Uijeongbu, Dongducheon, and Pocheon. KeyChinGu checked only existence, address, and business type on Naver — no evaluation. The nine spots reachable by Airport Railroad open this piece.

ISSUE 06 · #1390 · 2026-09-18 · Locals' Picks ⑨

🎯 Why "Comment Recommendations" Is the Bar

This corner has one source. A Korean YouTube channel posted a "nationwide matjip" video, viewers left comments naming their neighborhood eateries, and the most systematically organized comments by region got turned into a table of 217 spots. Since it's a list Koreans recommended to Koreans, foreign reviews are nearly nonexistent. This corner's criteria and limits are covered in Part ① (#1382).

KeyChinGu checked only whether the name, address, and business type actually exist on Naver local search (2026-09-17). Of 35 spots in this region, 30 were confirmed; the 5 that couldn't be confirmed (Paju, 2 in Gimpo, Ilsan, Incheon) were dropped. There are no visit reviews, rankings, or star ratings, and no hours or prices either.

What makes this piece useful for travelers is Incheon International Airport. AREX (공항철도) local trains stop at Unseo (운서), Yeongjong (영종), and Cheongna International City (청라국제도시), and four of the list's nine Incheon spots sit in those three station catchments. That means you can eat a meal without heading into the city on your arrival or departure day. The rest stretches along the Gimpo Goldline (김포 골드라인) corridor, Paju's Gyoha, Ilsan, and all the way to Pocheon, which has no railway at all.

One more thing. Incheon's administrative districts changed on July 1, 2026 — going from 2 counties and 8 districts to 2 counties and 9 districts, with Jemulpo-gu (제물포구), Yeongjong-gu (영종구), Seohae-gu (서해구), and Geomdan-gu (검단구) newly created. So when you see "Yeongjong-gu" or "Seohae-gu" in an address, it isn't a typo — those are the official district names (Cheongna belongs to Seohae-gu).

① Incheon — 9 Spots, Airport Railroad and Incheon Subway

Incheon is a city with an airport. The list's nine spots split into 4 on the Airport Railroad axis (Yeongjong, Unseo, Cheongna) and 5 on the Incheon Subway/Suin-Bundang Line axis (Songdo, Dongchun, Nonhyeon, Namdong Industrial Complex, Bupyeong).

  • Gyodong Jjamppong (교동짬뽕) · 72 Neopdi-ro, Yeongjong-gu, Incheon, 1F · Restaurant · Chinese restaurant · AREX Unseo Station (운서역) area
  • Ttowa Sundaeguk (또와순대국) · 20 Yeongjong-daero 162beon-gil, Yeongjong-gu, Incheon · Korean · sundae/sundaeguk · AREX Yeongjong Station (영종역) area

Two spots on Yeongjongdo. A card to use before heading into Seoul from the airport, or when your hotel is near the airport the night before departure. Yeongjong-gu was newly created in July 2026.

  • Rei (레이) · 5-21 Cheongnarubi-ro 42beon-gil, Seohae-gu, Incheon · Chinese · Chinese restaurant · AREX Cheongna International City Station
  • Araenyeok Mul Dakgalbi (아랜역물닭갈비) · 99 Cheongna Emerald-ro, Seohae-gu, Incheon, B1 · Korean · dakgalbi · Same Cheongna

Cheongna International City reaches Seoul Station in one ride on the Airport Railroad. The Line 7 extension targets the 2030s and is still unopened, so the Airport Railroad is currently the only subway line here. Mul-dakgalbi (물닭갈비) is a Taebaek-style dakgalbi simmered in broth — there's one spot doing it in the Gangwon piece (#1384) too.

  • Gukbap Sanghoe (국밥상회) · 12 Daeam-ro 22beon-gil, Yeonsu-gu, Incheon, 1F · Restaurant · Korean · Songdo, Incheon Line 1 area
  • Sinuiju Chapssal Sundae (신의주찹쌀순대) · 10 Aenggogae-ro 246beon-gil, Yeonsu-gu, Incheon · Korean · sundae/sundaeguk · Dongchun-dong, located at Incheon Line 1 Dongchun Station (동춘역)

The same name exists in Nowon too (#179, #617) — treat it as a different branch, a different place and confirm by address.

  • Nonhyeon Makchang Gui (논현막창구이) · 9 Cheongneung-daero 595beon-gil, Namdong-gu, Incheon · Korean · grilled pork · Suin-Bundang Line Incheon Nonhyeon Station (인천논현역) and Sorae Pogu Station (소래포구역) area

A different neighborhood that just happens to share a name with Nonhyeon-dong in Seoul's Gangnam. Sorae Pogu is in the same area.

  • Tteokbokki (떡볶이) · 226 Namdongseo-ro, Namdong-gu, Incheon · Korean street food · tteokbokki · Namdong Industrial Complex, Namdong Industrial Park Station (남동인더스파크역, Suin-Bundang Line) area — the exact walking distance from the station is unconfirmed

The name is simply "Tteokbokki." Since it's inside an industrial complex, weekend hours especially need checking.

  • Bareun Jungsik (바른중식) · 45 Galwoldong-ro, Bupyeong-gu, Incheon · Chinese · Chinese restaurant · Bupyeong Station (부평역) — Incheon's first transfer station, where Line 1 meets Incheon Line 1

Traveler tip — sundaeguk, gukbap, tteokbokki, and jjamppong (짬뽕) all work for solo orders; grilled makchang usually starts at a 2-person minimum. AREX local trains are different from the express — you need the local train that stops at Unseo, Yeongjong, and Cheongna.

② Gimpo & Paju — 13 Spots, the Goldline Corridor and Express-Bus Country

Gimpo's axis is the Gimpo Goldline (Yangchon to Gimpo Airport, 10 stations), while Paju's Gyoha leans on express buses more than rail. Paju has both a GTX-A Unjeong Jungang Station (운정중앙역) and a Gyeongui-Jungang Line Unjeong Station (운정역) — separate stations 3.5km apart.

  • Mujigae Bunsik (무지개분식) · 15 Junggu-ro, Gimpo-si · Korean street food · tteokbokki · Downtown Gimpo — check for the nearest Goldline station
  • Odaltong Bunsik (오달통분식) · 56 Bukbyeonjung-ro 65beon-gil, Gimpo-si · Korean street food · general bunsik · Bukbyeon, near Geolpobukbyeon Station (걸포북변역)
  • Chanumul Hanu Jeongyuk Sikdang (찬우물한우정육식당) · 91 Eunhaengyeongsajeong-ro, Gochon-eup, Gimpo-si, 1F · Korean · meat dishes · Gochon Station (고촌역)
  • Hanseong Chicken (한성치킨) · 85 Seongmo-ro 73beon-gil, Yangchon-eup, Gimpo-si · Restaurant · fried chicken · Yangchon Station (양촌역) (Goldline terminus)
  • Hwanggeumareul Nanneun Dak (황금알을낳는닭) · 28-76 Yanggok2-ro 60beon-gil, Yangchon-eup, Gimpo-si · Korean · chicken dishes · Same Yangchon-eup
  • 178 Doc (178도씨) · 10 Daemyeonghang1-ro 92beon-gil, Daegot-myeon, Gimpo-si · Korean · raw fish · Daegot-myeon isn't on the Goldline — reachable only by bus (check the route number). Toward Daemyeonghang

Five of Gimpo's six spots are reachable by Goldline; only the one near Daemyeonghang is bus-only.

  • Mulmannan Hanu (물만난한우) · 24 Gyoha-ro 681beon-gil, Paju-si, Bldg 1, 1F · Restaurant · Korean · Gyoha district
  • Simhaksan Dotori Guksu (심학산도토리국수) · 12 Gyoha-ro 681beon-gil, Paju-si · Restaurant · Korean · Same street

A hanu spot and a dotori guksu (도토리국수) place sit right next to each other on the same street. Dotori guksu is noodles made by mixing in acorn flour.

  • Saengseon Gunneun Maeul (생선굽는마을) · 8-18 Dolgoji-gil, Paju-si · Restaurant · Korean · Paju's Dolgoji-gil
  • Cheongsan Eojuk (청산어죽) · 99 Dolgoji-gil, Paju-si · Restaurant · Korean · Same street

Eojuk (어죽) is a porridge made by boiling down freshwater fish and simmering it together with rice.

  • Pyeongyang Son Mandu (평양손만두) · 507 Waseoksunhwan-ro, Paju-si, World Tower 9-cha, 2F · Korean · ibuk eumsik (이북음식) · Gyoha's Waseok

A different place from the old shop of the same name in Seoul's Buam-dong (#134). Paju borders North Korea, so ibuk eumsik signs are common here.

  • Imone Samgyeopsal (이모네삼겹살) · 33-7 Beonyeong-ro, Paju-si, 1F · Korean · grilled pork · Paju's Beonyeong-ro
  • Ttinghoa (띵호아) · 25 Soricheon-ro, Paju-si · Chinese · Chinese restaurant · Paju's Soricheon-ro

Paju's seven spots cluster in the Gyoha and Unjeong new-town area, where the Gyeongui-Jungang Line (경의중앙선) only brushes the eastern edge, so express-bus dependence runs high. Coming from Seoul, check the bus route first.

Traveler tip — samgyeopsal, hanu, and chicken are easier with 2+ people; bunsik, noodles, and eojuk work fine solo. Gimpo Goldline trains are short and get very crowded during commute hours — lunch and afternoon slots are more comfortable.

③ Goyang & North Gyeonggi — 8 Spots, From Ilsan to Pocheon

Goyang (Ilsan and Deogyang), Uijeongbu, Dongducheon, and Pocheon. The farther north of Seoul you go, the less rail there is, and Pocheon has none at all.

  • Ilsan Kalguksu Bonjeom (일산칼국수본점) · 467 Gyeongui-ro, Ilsandong-gu, Goyang-si · Korean · kalguksu/mandu · Gyeongui-Jungang Line Ilsan Station (일산역) area (it's the Gyeongui-Jungang Line, not Line 3)
  • Bowon (보원) · 297-21 Janghang-ro, Ilsandong-gu, Goyang-si, Bldg A, Unit 2 · Restaurant · Korean · Ilsan's Janghang — Line 3 Jeongbalsan Station (정발산역) area
  • Ilsan Gyoja (일산교자) · 289 Wondang-ro, Deogyang-gu, Goyang-si · Korean · kalguksu/mandu · Line 3 Wondang Station (원당역)

The name has "Ilsan" in it, but it's actually in Deogyang-gu. Gyoja is a Sino-Korean word for mandu (dumplings).

  • Myeongdong Jjigae Maeul (명동찌개마을) · 21 Biu-ro 20beon-gil, Uijeongbu-si · Restaurant · Korean · Uijeongbu — Line 1 Uijeongbu Station (의정부역) and Uijeongbu LRT (의정부경전철, U-Line)
  • Urinara (우리나라) · 11 Dongil-ro 128beon-gil, Uijeongbu-si · Restaurant · Korean · Same Uijeongbu (full name is "Urinara Gukbap Bonjeom")
  • Jinmiok (진미옥) · 185-1 Saengyeon-ro, Dongducheon-si · Korean · gomtang/seolleongtang · Line 1 Dongducheon Jungang Station (동두천중앙역) and Dongducheon Station (동두천역) area
  • Chogyetang (초계탕) · 35 Eosu-ro, Dongducheon-si · Korean · ibuk eumsik · Same Dongducheon

Chogyetang is a chilled North Korean-style summer dish — cold chicken broth with vinegar and mustard stirred in. The store's name is literally the menu.

  • Hongdukkae Guksijip (홍두깨국시집) · 24 Jeongmundong-gil, Pocheon-si, 1F · Korean · kalguksu/mandu · Pocheon has no railway — from Seoul, take an express bus like the 3006 toward Jamsil, or an intercity bus bound for Pocheon Public Bus Terminal

Guksi is the Gyeongsang and Gangwon dialect word for guksu (noodles), and hongdukkae is the wooden rolling pin used to roll out dough. The name means "hand-rolled kalguksu shop."

Traveler tip — Uijeongbu and Dongducheon connect to Seoul on a single Line 1 ride. It takes time, but no transfers means it's manageable even with luggage. Pocheon is bus-only, so check the last return bus first.

🚄 Fitting This Into a Metro-Area Route

  • Arrival/departure day at the airport — take the AREX local train to Unseo and Yeongjong (2 spots each) or Cheongna (2 spots). A full meal without heading into the city.
  • Working it into the Incheon day-trip course (#1011-#1015) — Songdo, Dongchun, Nonhyeon, and Bupyeong sit on the Incheon Subway/Suin-Bundang Line axis.
  • From Gimpo Airport into Gimpo — one Goldline ride reaches Gochon, Geolpobukbyeon, and Yangchon. Only Daegot-myeon needs a bus.
  • Paju (#1249 and other northern pieces) — Gyoha and Unjeong run mainly on express buses. Just remember that GTX-A Unjeong Jungang Station and Gyeongui-Jungang Line Unjeong Station are different stations. GTX-A's middle segment between Seoul Station and Suseo has not yet opened.
  • Ilsan, Uijeongbu, and Dongducheon — reachable by Line 3, the Gyeongui-Jungang Line, and Line 1 respectively. Pocheon is express-bus only.
  • Heading to southeast Gyeonggi — Suwon, Seongnam, Yongin, and Ansan are covered in #1391.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Taking the AREX express train — the express runs nonstop between Seoul Station and Incheon Airport. The one that stops at Unseo, Yeongjong, and Cheongna is the local train.

2. Assuming Paju's Unjeong Station and Unjeong Jungang Station are the same station — the Gyeongui-Jungang Line's Unjeong Station and GTX-A's Unjeong Jungang Station are different stations 3.5km apart.

3. Looking for Ilsan Kalguksu on Line 3 — Ilsan Station is on the Gyeongui-Jungang Line. Line 3 is over by Jeongbalsan and Madu.

⚙ 3 FAQ

1. Who picked this list? — A table compiled from local eateries Korean YouTube viewers named in comments. KeyChinGu confirmed only existence, address, and business type, and didn't rank anything.

2. What are "Yeongjong-gu" and "Seohae-gu" in Incheon addresses? — Districts created by Incheon's administrative reorganization on July 1, 2026. Jemulpo-gu, Yeongjong-gu, Seohae-gu, and Geomdan-gu were newly established, and Cheongna belongs to Seohae-gu. Nothing changes for travelers — it's just new notation.

3. Want just one meal near the airport? — One of the four Unseo/Yeongjong spots is the simplest choice. Hours weren't collected though, so check on Naver Map the same day.
