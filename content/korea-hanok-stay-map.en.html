Korea Hanok Stay Guide — 13 Hanok Stays in Seoul, Jeonju, Andong, and Gyeongju (Rakgojae, Midam, Jeonju Hanok Stay Experience Center, Gangneung Ojuk Hanok Village) (Official Values, October 2026)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Lie back on a warm ondol (heated) floor and the rafters run overhead — at Siwon Hanok Experience Center (시원 한옥체험관), every room has a loft made from an old attic (VisitKorea). This guide is a map for travelers who want to spend a night in a hanok (a traditional Korean house). There are four branches: Seoul Bukchon and Seochon, Jeonju Hanok Village, Andong Hahoe and Gyeongju, and regional hanok villages and experience centers with many rooms. Lodgings were picked only from places listed in VisitKorea tourism information (TourAPI lodging data), and check-in times, room counts, and refund rules are the values written there.

The old guide described "Hanok Stay" as if it were still a live certification. In 2017 the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism announced that it would merge the Good Stay, Hanok Stay, and Korea Stay certifications into the Korea Tourism Quality Certification, and hanok experience businesses under the Tourism Promotion Act fall within its scope (Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism press release). Numbers such as "700 hanok in Jeonju" and "₩30,000~80,000 a night" were deleted because they have no source.

🏯 Seoul Bukchon and Seochon — 4 Places

  • Rakgojae Seoul Bukchon Hanok Hotel (락고재 서울 북촌 한옥호텔) — Gyedong-gil 49-23 (계동길 49-23), Jongno-gu (종로구). A 130-year-old hanok renovated into a hotel. 5 Korean-style rooms, up to 13 guests. Check-in 15:00, check-out 11:00, no cooking. Parking by advance reservation, ₩15,000 (~$11) a night. Book on the website; 100% refund up to 10 days before use, no refund on the day. 02-742-3410 (VisitKorea)
  • Midam Hanok Guest House (미담 한옥 게스트 하우스) — Bukchon-ro 6-gil 25-6 (북촌로6길 25-6) (Gyedong (계동)), Jongno-gu (종로구). Between Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung palaces, a hanok over 90 years old in a "ㄷ" shape. 3 Korean-style rooms, up to 10 guests. Check-in 15:00, check-out 11:00, no cooking, laundry room. Reserve by phone, 010-3143-7096. Refund 60% for 5~3 days before use, 10% for 1 day before or the day of (VisitKorea)
  • Adamhanok (아담한옥) — Pilun-daero 6-gil 21 (필운대로6길 21) (Tongin-dong (통인동)), Jongno-gu (종로구). About a 10-minute walk from Gyeongbokgung Station. 1 whole house (up to 4 guests), kitchen and living room on the 1st floor, bedroom on the 2nd, an open-air bath in the yard. Check-in 15:00, check-out 11:00, cooking allowed. Phone 0504-0904-2257 (VisitKorea)
  • Ihwa Hanok (이화한옥) — Pilun-daero 26-25 (필운대로 26-25) (Nuha-dong (누하동)), Jongno-gu (종로구). 10 minutes on foot from Gyeongbokgung Station Exit 2, 5 minutes on foot from Tongin Market. 1 whole house, up to 8 guests. Check-in 18:00, check-out 14:00, no parking. Booked through an OTA (online travel agency), and refunds follow that company's rules (VisitKorea)

🏘 Jeonju Hanok Village — 2 Places

  • Jeonju Hanok Stay Experience Center (전주 한옥숙박체험관) — Eunhaeng-ro 56-1 (은행로 56-1), Wansan-gu (완산구), Jeonju. Inside the Eunhaeng-ro alley of Hanok Village. All rooms are ondol, with lofts. 7 Korean-style rooms. Check-in 15:00, check-out 10:30. Breakfast of toast, americano, fruit, and tea. Refund 100% 7 days before the stay, 50% 6~3 days before, 0% 2~1 days before. 010-8669-6700 (VisitKorea)
  • Siwon Hanok Experience Center (시원 한옥체험관) — Omokdae-gil 45-41 (오목대길 45-41) (Gyodong (교동)), Wansan-gu (완산구), Jeonju. 3 rooms (Modubang (모두방), Sarangbang (사랑방), Gippeumbang (기쁨방)), lofts made from the old attic. Each room has a bathroom, WiFi, and air conditioning, and the yard has tuho (arrow-throwing) and jegichagi (shuttlecock kicking). Check-in 14:00, check-out 10:30, no cooking. 0507-1402-7840 (VisitKorea)
  • For alleys outside Hanok Village, see #255 Jeonju Hanok Village Full Guide

🌾 Andong Hahoe and Gyeongju — 3 Places

  • Rakgojae Hahoe Hanok Hotel (락고재 하회한옥호텔) — It is in two places in Hahoe Village. The tile-roofed main building (Jeonseo-ro 186-20 (전서로 186-20), Pungcheon-myeon (풍천면), Andong (안동시)) is near the village market and parking lot, 18 rooms, check-in 16:00~20:00, a mugwort steam sauna, 0507-1354-3418. The thatched annex (Hahoe Gangbyeon-gil 51 (하회강변길 51), Pungcheon-myeon (풍천면)) has 4 thatched rooms (Anbang (안방), Geonneonbang (건넌방), Sarangchae (사랑채), Byeolchae (별채)) on the Nakdong River bank facing Buyongdae, check-in 16:00, no cooking, 010-8860-3410. Check-out is 11:00 at both (VisitKorea)
  • Gyeongju Gyodong Hanokjip (경주 교동한옥집) — Cheonwonan-gil 21-6 (천원안길 21-6) (Gyodong (교동)), Gyeongju (경주시). 5 minutes on foot from Woljeonggyo Bridge and Gyochon Hanok Village, with a yard where children can play. 1 Korean-style room, up to 4 guests, check-in 15:00, check-out 11:00. Refund 100% 7 days before, 50% 6~4 days before, no refund from 3 days before. 010-9594-5490 (VisitKorea)
  • Bulguksa Hanok Dongodang (불국사한옥동오당) — Jintti-gil 5-58 (진티길 5-58) (Jinhyeon-dong (진현동)), Gyeongju (경주시). 5 minutes on foot from Bulguksa Temple. 3 Korean-style rooms, shared kitchen, barbecue area and campfire. Check-in 15:00, check-out 11:00. 0507-1324-6350 (VisitKorea)

🏡 Regional Hanok Villages and Experience Centers — 4 Places

  • Gangneung Ojuk Hanok Village (강릉오죽한옥마을) — Jukheon-gil 114 (죽헌길 114), Gangneung (강릉시). 51 rooms (standard to VIP type), shared cooking area, traditional games yard, traditional culture experience center. Check-in 15:00, check-out 11:00. Parking for about 50 cars. 033-655-1117 (VisitKorea)
  • Gongju Hanok Village (공주한옥마을) — Gwangwang Danji-gil 12 (관광단지길 12), Gongju (공주시). New hanok with a gudeuljang (ondol stone slab) experience, group buildings and individual buildings. Pottery and hanji (Korean paper) experiences in the workshop. Check-in 15:00, check-out 11:00. 041-881-2828 (VisitKorea)
  • Gimhae Hanok Experience Center (김해한옥체험관) — Wangneung-gil 40 (왕릉길 40), Gimhae (김해시). 7 buildings with 13 rooms (Sarangchae (사랑채), Anchae (안채), Byeolchae (별채), Bakkatchae (바깥채)), each room with a modern bathroom. English and Japanese guidance and traditional experience programs. Check-in 17:00, check-out 11:00, no cooking. 055-322-4735~6 (VisitKorea)
  • Choi Champandaek Hanok Lodging (최참판댁 한옥숙박시설) — Pyeongsa-ri-gil 86 (평사리길 86), Akyang-myeon (악양면), Hadong (하동군). Near Choi Champandaek, the setting of the novel Toji (『토지』), 6 buildings with 9 rooms. Check-in 15:00, check-out 11:00, cooking allowed, barbecue area. 055-883-2225 (VisitKorea)

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Breakfast: The only place with breakfast listed in the tourism information is Jeonju Hanok Stay Experience Center (전주 한옥숙박체험관) (toast, americano, fruit, tea) (VisitKorea). Ask the others when you book
  • Cooking: Adamhanok (아담한옥) and Choi Champandaek Hanok Lodging (최참판댁 한옥숙박시설) allow cooking; Rakgojae Seoul (락고재 서울), Midam (미담), Gimhae Hanok Experience Center (김해한옥체험관), and Siwon (시원) do not (VisitKorea). For a vegetarian or halal diet, a whole house with cooking is convenient
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid in Itaewon (이태원) (Usadan-ro 10-gil 15 (우사단로10길 15), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00~21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF). Recognition can change, so check before you visit. None of the hanok lodgings in this guide are marked as offering halal breakfast
  • Health: Rakgojae Seoul (락고재 서울) has a wood-fired sauna made of cheongitoro (천기토로) clay, and Adamhanok (아담한옥) has an open-air bath in the yard (VisitKorea). If you are pregnant or have a heart condition, ask the lodging before using them
  • For a show-it card, see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Hanok Stay Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Check-in, Getting Around

  • Solo: Whole-house hanok (Adamhanok (아담한옥), Ihwa Hanok (이화한옥)) are rented as one house regardless of headcount. If you are alone, a room-type lodging (Midam (미담), Jeonju Hanok Stay Experience Center (전주 한옥숙박체험관)) fits better
  • Reservations: Each lodging uses its own website, phone, or OTA, and refund rules differ (VisitKorea). Refuse requests for bank transfers outside the app, as in the #1414 Short-Stay Booking Manual
  • Rates: The tourism information has no room rates. They vary by season, day of the week, and OTA, so capture the price on the booking screen. The only officially published money is Rakgojae Seoul's parking at ₩15,000 (~$11) a night (VisitKorea)
  • Check-in: Mostly 15:00; Ihwa Hanok 18:00, Gimhae 17:00, Hahoe tile-roofed main building 16:00~20:00. Check-out is 10:30~14:00 (VisitKorea). For shoes and the boiler, see #1380 First-Night Lodging Manual; if you are 18 or 19, see #1399
  • Getting around: Jeonju, Andong, Gyeongju, and Gangneung are reached by express or intercity bus — #1297 Bus Terminal Manual

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "The 'Hanok Stay' mark is the current official certification" — It was merged into Korea Tourism Quality Certification in 2017 (Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism)

2. "All hanok have 15:00 check-in and 11:00 check-out" — Ihwa Hanok (이화한옥) is 18:00 in and 14:00 out, and both Jeonju places check out at 10:30 (VisitKorea)

3. "With a hanok you can cook your own meals" — Rakgojae Seoul (락고재 서울), Midam (미담), and Gimhae (김해) do not allow cooking (VisitKorea)

4. "If I cancel I get everything back" — Gyeongju Gyodong Hanokjip (경주 교동한옥집) gives no refund from 3 days before, and Rakgojae Seoul (락고재 서울) none on the day (VisitKorea)
