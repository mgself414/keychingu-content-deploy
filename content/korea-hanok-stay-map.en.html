⚠ 3 Common Hanok Stay Mistakes

1. "Hanok = Bukchon in Seoul only" — not knowing Jeonju, Andong, and Gyeongju: Bukchon only. In reality it is a nationwide hanok cluster: Bukchon (Seoul) + Jeonju Hanok Village 700+ (Korea's #1) + Andong Hahoe UNESCO + Gyeongju Yangdong UNESCO (KTX 1.5~2h).

2. "Hanok = same as a hotel" — not knowing the full 1-night experience: Assuming it's just a hotel. In reality the full 1-night hanok experience (ondol heated floors, hanbok, tea ceremony, traditional breakfast, English-friendly booking) is the #1 draw for foreign enthusiasts.

3. "Hanok = expensive" — not knowing the value clusters: Assuming it's pricey. In reality prices span a wide range: Jeonju & Andong hanok guesthouses ₩30,000~80,000 (~$23~60)/night + Bukchon ₩80,000~150,000 (~$60~113) + luxury ₩200,000+ (~$150+) (Gangjin, Andong, Gyeongju).
