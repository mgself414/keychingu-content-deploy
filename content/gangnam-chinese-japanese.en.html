⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. Recognition of “Gangnam = Korean food, BBQ, and pork belly only” + Chinese and Japanese food pass

The most common mistake foreigners make while moving around Gangnam. Gangnam is only perceived as an area for Korean BBQ, pork belly, and chicken. In reality, Gangnam-gu is the mecca of Korean luxury Japanese Omakase + Chinese modern dining — there are many Japanese and mainland Chinese chef stores in Apgujeong and Cheongdam. If you only eat Korean food for 7-8 days, it is recommended to add 1 Chinese and 1 Japanese restaurant each.

2. Apgujeong, Cheongdam, and COEX are viewed as the same neighborhood

The tone of each sub-area in Gangnam-gu is very different. Apgujeong (trendy modern dining·K-pop fan) ≠ Cheongdam (luxury omakase·gallery) ≠ COEX (shopping mall + business dining) ≠ Seolleung·Dosan (hidden gems). It is recommended to focus on 1-2 sub-areas rather than grouping them into one route.

3. Japanese Omakase Reservation/Dress Code Not Considered

Gangnam Cheongdam Japanese Omakase (Cheongdam Yoon, Cheongdam Makoto, etc.) reservation required (recommended 1-2 weeks in advance) + smart casual dress code. Foreigners may be refused entry if they walk in. KakaoTalk channel or Naver reservation Check in advance. Price range ₩100,000~300,000 (Omakase for 1 person) — Luxury dinner budget.

① Chinese modern dining (2 locations)

1. Mutan Apgujeong Main Branch (Mutan Apgujeong)

  • Signature: Apgujeong Chinese modern dining main branch. 10 foreign reviews (Phase 2 cluster 1st place, signal 44.3) — Korean Chinese modern signature.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Strongest foreigner reviews, English menu and service
  • Recommended menu: Signature Chinese course, Modern Chinese dim sum, Wine pairing
  • Price range: ₩40,000~80,000 (dinner for 1 person)
  • Tip: Walking distance from Apgujeong Station. No. 1 recommendation for foreigners’ first Chinese meal in Gangnam — Reservations are recommended for both weekdays and weekends.

2. Mutan COEX

  • Signature: Mutan COEX Starfield Branch. 10 foreign reviews (signal 43.2) — Same lineup as the main store.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — COEX shopping mall + foreigner business/exhibition route
  • Recommended menu: Same as main branch, course·dim sum
  • Tip: 5-minute walk from Samsung Station, inside COEX Starfield. Ideal as a dinner course after visiting COEX Aquarium and SM Town.

② Chinese hidden gems (2 places)

3. Gongli

  • Signature: Seolleung Chinese authentic hidden. 5 foreign reviews (signal 24.0).
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Seolleung neighborhood, rich in foreigner reviews
  • Recommended menu: Signature Chinese menu, authentic Chinese course
  • Price range: ₩25,000~50,000 (1 person)
  • Tip: Walking distance from Seolleung Station. Authentic and reasonably priced Chinese food hidden gem, different from the luxury of Apgujeong and Cheongdam.

4. Luwi

  • Signature: Dosan Chinese casual dining. 7 foreign reviews (signal 23.3).

③ Japanese Omakase/Luxury (2 locations)

5. Cheongdamyun Japanese Omakase

  • Signature: Cheongdam Japanese Omakase Signature. The home of Korean Japanese Omakase.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Luxury concept, English service
  • Recommended menu: Signature Omakase Course (sushi·sashimi·seafood)
  • Price range: ₩150,000~300,000 (dinner for 1 person)
  • Tip: Walking distance from Apgujeong Station. Recommended 1-2 weeks in advance. Smart casual dress code. No. 1 anniversary/date dinner.

6. Cheongdam Makoto

  • Signature: Authentic Cheongdam Japanese cuisine. Apgujeong-ro 80-gil Japanese Chef Store.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Authentic Japanese food + English service
  • Recommended menu: Signature Japanese course, authentic sushi, seafood
  • Price range: ₩100,000~200,000 (dinner for 1 person)
  • Tip: Apgujeong and Cheongdam within walking distance. Reservation required. Same intersection as Cheongdamyun — two Japanese omakase stores can be compared.

④ Japanese casual/seafood (2 locations)

7. Jinsusa

  • Signature: Gangnam Japanese cuisine hidden. 7 foreign reviews (No. 1 in Phase 2 Japanese cuisine, signal 27.0) — Introduction to Japanese cuisine in Gangnam.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Plenty of foreigner reviews, close to Gangnam Station
  • Recommended menu: Signature sushi·sashimi, Japanese set meal
  • Price range: ₩30,000~70,000 (dinner for 1 person)
  • Tip: Walking distance from Gangnam Station. First choice for foreigners’ first Japanese restaurant — Reasonable price, easier to enter than Cheongdam Omakase.

8. Natural Haedam Japanese Crab Village (Haedam Daege)

  • Signature: Gangnam Japanese food, snow crab, and seafood store. 6 foreign reviews (signal 18.8).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Snow crab/seafood concept, English menu
  • Recommended menu: Wild snow crab, signature seafood, Japanese course
  • Price range: ₩60,000~120,000 (dinner for 1 person)
  • Tip: Walking distance from Gangnam Station. Signature of snow crab season (November-March) — Other seafood courses available at other times. Suitable for family and group dining.
