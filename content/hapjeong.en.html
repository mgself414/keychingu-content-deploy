⚠ The 5 biggest mistakes foreigners make in Hapjeong

1. Recognition of “Hapjeong = part adjacent to Hongdae”

Common mistakes in foreign travel routes. Hapjeong is recognized as part of Hongdae’s main district. In reality, Hapjeong Station walking distance is the most popular with foreigners in the Hongdae area — L7 Hotel, Mecenatpolis + Yanghwa-ro, Dokmak-ro, authentic style.

2. L7 Hongdae Hotel + Urban Plant + The Place Bundle Unknown

Foreign hotel guests only see the restaurant on the first floor of L7. In fact, L7 22nd floor floating rooftop bar (#222 no. 6) + L7 The Place Western Dining + Urban Plant Brunch near L7 are the full L7 series set.

3. Mecenatpolis Shopping Mall + Dining Combination Miji

Hapjeong Station Mecenatpolis is an integrated shopping and dining store for foreigners. JAJU, Olive Young, Dining, and Cafe integration. Good for tying it to one line.

4. Hapjeong Western/Italian Authentic Area Pass

Yeonnam and Yeonhui are the top destinations for foreigners to eat food. In reality, Hapjeong Yanghwaro Italian Authentic (Spring Bistro, Ragoo Hapjeong, New York Apartment #153) is more authentic. Recommended for foreigners who prefer classic over modern.

5. Walking distance to Yanghwa-ro and Dokmak-ro, Migoryeo

From Hapjeong Station, it is a 5-minute walk to Yanghwa-ro and a 10-minute walk to Dokmak-ro. Good for tying it to one line. No need to go to the subway.

① Mecenatpolis·L7 Hotel (2 locations)

1. Urban Plant Hapjeong

  • Signature: The strongest brunch store in Hapjeong. 25 foreign reviews (signal 111.5) — Hongdae area cluster strongest.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Best foreigner reviews + English store name
  • Recommended menu: Brunch plate, signature drink
  • Tip: 5 minutes walk from Hapjeong Station. Close to L7 Hotel. First recommendation for foreigners in Hapjeong. Peak hours: 10-14 on weekends.

2. The Place Hongdae L7

  • Signature: L7 Hotel Western Dining. 21 foreign reviews (signal 64) — Hotel form.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Hotel English service
  • Recommended menu: Hotel Western course, signature plate
  • Tip: Inside L7 Hongdae Hotel. Both hotel guests and outside visitors are allowed. Dinner reservations recommended.

② Hapjeong Italian Authentic (2 locations)

3. Omil Hongdae

  • Signature: Modern style in foreign painting. 20 foreign reviews (signal 100) — Hapjeong style hidden is the best.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Abundant foreigner reviews
  • Recommended menu: Signature Western course, seasonal menu
  • Tip: 7 minutes walk from Hapjeong Station, 178 Yanghwa-ro. Dinner reservation recommended.

4. Bistro Goat Hongdae Branch

  • Signature: Hapjeong form hidden. 18 foreign reviews (signal 65).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Plenty of foreigner reviews
  • Recommended menu: Signature bistro menu, wine
  • Tip: Walking distance from Hapjeong Station. Weekday lunch walk-in available.

③ Hapjeong nightlife (2 places)
