⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Gyeongbokgung Palace = Free admission only": Gyeongbokgung Palace = 3K admission fee (free when wearing Hanbok) + Changing of the Guards ceremony twice a day + Foreign Mania #1 + English docent (three times a day).

2. "Changdeokgung Palace Sponsorship = Enter": Changdeokgung Palace Sponsorship (Secret Garden) = Reservation required + English guide (6 times a day) + UNESCO No. 1 + No. 1 foreign enthusiast.

3. "Hanbok = Expensive": Hanbok rental = 15~30K/3 hours + free palace entrance package + Hanboknam, Hanbok rental, unique Hanbok cluster (Bukchon, Insa-dong, Samcheong-dong).

4. "Changgyeonggung Palace at night = All year round": Changgyeonggung Palace at night = Spring, fall, and summer only (April, May, August, September, October) + No. 1 foreign Instagram + Foreign night enthusiast.

5. "Jongmyo = just a shrine": Jongmyo = UNESCO + Jongmyo Jeryeak (UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity) + 600 years of ancestral rites of the Joseon Dynasty, foreign authentic enthusiasts.
