Seoul Bunsik Map: 8 Places for Tteokbokki, Gimbap, Mandu, Sundae and Fried Snacks in Markets, Alleys and Chains, With Hours and Prices Checked (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: Gwangjang Market food alley 09:00–23:00 year-round (Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사)) · mayak gimbap KRW 4,000 and tteokbokki KRW 3,000 (Monyeo Gimbap (모녀김밥), DiningCode (다이닝코드)) · Tongin Market lunchbox cafe Wed–Mon 11:00–15:00, 10 yeopjeon coins for KRW 5,000 (Seoul Tourism Organization (서울관광재단) · Korea Tourism Organization) · Mangwon Market 10:00–21:00 (Seoul Tourism Organization) · Sindang-dong Mabokrim 2-person set KRW 17,000, closed on the 2nd and 4th Mondays (DiningCode · Korea Tourism Organization) · Shinjeon, Yupdduk, Jaws and Isaac use official menu names, prices are on each shop's menu board

🎯 How to use this guide

Monyeo Gimbap (모녀김밥) in Gwangjang Market (광장시장) opens at 06:30. The mini seaweed rolls are brushed with sesame oil and rolled by hand, KRW 4,000 a plate, and from the pot next to them come tteokbokki (떡볶이) at KRW 3,000 and fish cake broth at KRW 1,000 (DiningCode, 2026-10-29). Bunsik (분식, Korean snack-shop food) is what Koreans eat standing up in front of school, after overtime, or while wandering a market, so the menus are short and the prices are small. This guide sorts the places in Seoul where you can eat bunsik into 3 markets, 1 alley and 4 chains, and lists only what has a checked time and price for each shop. For deeper stories such as tteokbokki chain comparisons, types of gimbap and old mandu (dumpling) shops, it links to a dedicated guide.

① In the markets: three standing-room bunsik spots

  • Gwangjang Market food alley (Jongno 5-ga): 88 Changgyeonggung-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul. The food alley is open 09:00–23:00, year-round, while the regular shops run 09:00–18:00 and close on Sundays; phone +82-2-2267-0291 (Korea Tourism Organization). You sit one seat at a time on long benches, so eating alone feels natural. At Monyeo Gimbap in the gimbap alley, a plate of mayak gimbap is KRW 4,000, tteokbokki KRW 3,000, odeng KRW 1,000, and yubuchobap (유부초밥, tofu pouch rice) KRW 4,000; its same-day hours are 06:30–19:00, and 11 "eating alone" visit-purpose tags are recorded (DiningCode, secondary source, 2026-10-29). For the bindaetteok and yukhoe (육회, beef tartare) old shops, see #582 Gwangjang Market old shops, 12 picks.
  • Tongin Market lunchbox cafe (Seochon): 18 Jahamun-ro 15-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul. The market is open 07:00–21:00 (varies by shop), and the lunchbox cafe is open Wed–Mon 11:00–15:00, Sat and Sun 11:00–16:00, closed every Monday and the third Sunday of each month. It is 515m from Gyeongbokgung Station Exit 2 on Line 3, phone +82-507-1378-0940 (Seoul Tourism Organization, updated 2026-09-10). At the second-floor lunchbox cafe you buy a bundle of 10 yeopjeon coins for KRW 5,000, carry a mini tray around, and use the coins to fill it with gireum tteokbokki (기름떡볶이, oil-fried tteokbokki), jeon (전, pan-fried pancakes) and side dishes at the participating shops (Korea Tourism Organization article, 2023). Only shops with a sign take the coins (Seoul Tourism Organization). For how many coins each item costs, see #589 Tongin Market coin lunchbox.
  • Mangwon Market (Mangwon-dong): 27 Poeun-ro 6-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul. Open daily 10:00–21:00 (varies by stall), 351m from Mangwon Station Exit 2 on Line 6, phone +82-2-335-3591 (Seoul Tourism Organization, updated 2026-10-16). Dakgangjeong (Qu's Dakgangjeong (큐스닭강정)), croquettes (Hwang Inho Croquette (황인호고로케)) and tteokgalbi (떡갈비, grilled short rib patties) are the market's signatures, and the tteokbokki and fried-snack stalls are deeper inside the alley. For prices shop by shop, see #596 Mangwon Market, 12 picks.

② In the alley: Sindang-dong jeukseok tteokbokki

  • Sindang-dong Tteokbokki Town: 10-18 Dasan-ro 33-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul. It is introduced as the place where gochujang tteokbokki began around 1953; inquiries +82-2-2236-9135 (Korea Tourism Organization). The standard is jeukseok tteokbokki, where you boil rice cakes, fish cake, ramyeon noodles and mandu in a pot on the table, so many shops start at a 2-person set.
  • Mabokrim Tteokbokki (마복림 떡볶이): 5 Dasan-ro 35-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul. 2-person set KRW 17,000, 3-person set KRW 20,000, add-ons are egg KRW 1,000, vegetables KRW 2,000, mandu KRW 2,000 and jjolmyeon (쫄면, chewy wheat noodles) KRW 2,000; same-day hours 09:00–22:50, free parking, and visit-purpose tags are lunch 96, date 48 and eating alone 3 (DiningCode, secondary source, 2026-10-29). Closed on the 2nd and 4th Monday of each month, phone 02-2232-8930 (Korea Tourism Organization article). A 2-person set is a lot for one, so the Gwangjang Market side is easier if you're alone. For the whole neighborhood, see #1311 Sindang-dong.

③ In the chains: the same menu board everywhere

  • Shinjeon Tteokbokki (신전떡볶이): The official menu has 13 items: tteokbokki, cheese tteokbokki, odeng, mandu, japchae roll, gimmari, cheese stick, Shinjeon gimbap, spam mayo cup rice, tuna mayo cup rice, Shinjeon rice, spicy odeng and toast. The official site lists neither prices nor spice levels (Shinjeon Tteokbokki official). Check prices on the shop's menu board or in the app.
  • Yupdduk Tteokbokki (엽기떡볶이): The official menu is the Yupgi menu, rose menu, mala tteokbokki, mala rose tteokbokki, 2-person Yupgi tteokbokki and Yupgi dakbokkeumtang (spicy braised chicken), with separate categories for chicken feet, sets, sides, toppings and meal kits. The site has no prices or spice-level system, and there is a notice for the new "milder taste" menu (Dongdaemun Yupdduk Tteokbokki official). If you can't handle spice, pick the mildest option when ordering.
  • Jaws Tteokbokki (죠스떡볶이): The official site has no menu or prices (Jaws Food official). Check the menu board in the shop.
  • Isaac Toast (이삭토스트): The official menu includes ham and cheese toast, grilled bulgalbi, French brioche bacon potato, real chili shrimp and soy crunch chicken, and prices are not posted (Isaac Toast official). Lines form on the morning commute, so takeout is the default.
  • For the taste differences and ordering steps of the three tteokbokki chains, see #66 Tteokbokki chains full guide; for types of gimbap, #59 Seoul gimbap; for old mandu shops, #481 Mandu guide.

🍽 Meal budget and diet

  • A market meal: At Monyeo Gimbap, mayak gimbap KRW 4,000 + tteokbokki KRW 3,000 + odeng KRW 1,000 comes to KRW 8,000 (DiningCode, secondary source). At Tongin Market, 10 yeopjeon coins for KRW 5,000 is one meal's worth (Korea Tourism Organization article). Jeukseok tteokbokki starts at a KRW 17,000 2-person set, so it works better for two or more (DiningCode, secondary source).
  • Halal and vegetarian: We could not find a bunsik shop certified by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF) (한국이슬람교중앙회). Gimbap commonly contains ham, tteokbokki broth often contains fish cake (fish), and sundae is pork intestine. For a list of certified restaurants, see #453 Vegetarian and halal guide; for how to say "less spicy, no meat" with a show-and-point card, see #1300 Dietary request manual.
  • Spice: Yupdduk Tteokbokki is spicy by default and has a separate mild menu (official). Sindang-dong jeukseok tteokbokki gets spicier and saltier toward the end as the broth reduces while it boils.

🧰 Practical: ordering, paying, eating alone

  • Ordering: In the markets, say how many plates and sit down, and you're done; at chains, choose at the counter or a kiosk. For the order from the stall to payment, see #811 Bunsik ordering workflow.
  • Paying: Most places take cards, but at market stalls a little cash is faster. There is no tipping → #1275 Paying etiquette.
  • Eating alone: Monyeo Gimbap in Gwangjang Market has 11 "eating alone" tags (DiningCode) and Mabokrim has 3, so market-style spots are easier alone. For all the restaurants that are good for sitting alone, see #348 Seoul Solo Diner Map.
  • Hours: Gwangjang Market stays open until 23:00, but gimbap shops like Monyeo Gimbap close at 19:00 (DiningCode, same-day basis). The Tongin Market lunchbox cafe ends at 15:00 (16:00 on weekends) (Seoul Tourism Organization).

⚠ 4 common mistakes

1. Going to Tongin Market in the evening: The lunchbox cafe is open 11:00–15:00 (until 16:00 on Sat and Sun) and is closed on Mondays and the third Sunday (Seoul Tourism Organization). Go at lunchtime.

2. Thinking gimbap is open late because Gwangjang Market is: The food alley is open until 23:00, but Monyeo Gimbap closes at 19:00 (DiningCode, same-day basis). Gimbap in the daytime, bindaetteok and yukhoe at night.

3. Jeukseok tteokbokki in Sindang-dong alone: The portion is large, starting from a KRW 17,000 2-person set (DiningCode). If you're alone, go for the plate-style bunsik at Gwangjang Market or a 1-person menu at a chain.

4. Deciding on chain prices in advance: Shinjeon, Yupdduk, Jaws and Isaac all have no prices on their official sites, and prices differ by shop. Choose after reading the menu board.
