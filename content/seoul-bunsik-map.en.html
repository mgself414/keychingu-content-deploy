⚠ 3 Bunsik Mistakes

1. "Bunsik = only tteokbokki" — missing the 5 axes: Recognizing tteokbokki alone. In reality, bunsik has 5 axes = tteokbokki · gimbap · mandu · sundae · twigim + the full daily-food experience of Korean students and office workers (Gwangjang · Tongin · Mangwon · Myeongdong clusters).

2. "Street food = unhygienic" — missing the Gwangjang · Tongin credentials: Hygiene worries. In reality, Gwangjang Market Jin Okhwa (Michelin Bib Gourmand) · Tongin Market (certified ₩5,000 (~$3.75) coin lunchbox) + Korean bunsik hygiene is above the OECD average.

3. "Bunsik = expensive" — missing that it's the #1 value: Assuming it's pricey. In reality, gimbap ₩5,000–7,000 (~$3.75–5.25) · tteokbokki ₩3,000–5,000 (~$2.25–3.75) · Tongin lunchbox ₩5,000 (~$3.75) — Korea's #1 value-for-money (#343 Budget full package · one full meal experience for ₩10,000 (~$7.50) per person).

1. Gwangjang Jin Okhwa = Korea's #1 mayak gimbap — Michelin Bib Gourmand · opens 8 AM · queuing recommended

3. Tongin Market lunchbox = ₩5,000 (~$3.75) in coins — Korea's #1 coin lunchbox · a foreign-visitor cult-favorite Instagram signature
