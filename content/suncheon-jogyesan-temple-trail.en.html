Suncheon's Jogyesan Temple Trail, in Autumn — From Seonamsa to Songgwangsa, Walking Between Two Great Temples over the Mountain

Subtitle: Seen Suncheon's gardens and market (#1049, #1368)? What's left is the mountain. Jogyesan's two temples — UNESCO's Seonamsa and Sangha temple Songgwangsa — sit back to back, linked by Cheonnyeon Bulsim-gil. Bus in, walk 4-5 hours over the pass, bus back. Fall color: late Oct-early Nov.

ISSUE · #1371 · 2026-09-10

🎯 The Frame — Walking Between Two Temples

Suncheon has two major temples. In the east, in Seungju-eup (승주읍), stands Seonamsa (선암사), one of the seven temples on UNESCO's 2018 World Heritage list "Sansa, Buddhist Mountain Monasteries in Korea" (산사, 한국의 산지승원). In the west, in Songgwang-myeon (송광면), stands Songgwangsa (송광사), the Sangha Jewel among the three temples Korean Buddhism calls the Three Jewel Temples (삼보사찰) — the temple of monks. Both sit on the same mountain, Jogyesan (조계산), on opposite slopes. Suncheon city has developed the mountain trail between the two temples as "Namdo Sambaengni-gil Course 9, Cheonnyeon Bulsim-gil (남도삼백리길 9코스 천년불심길)" — starting at Seonamsa, passing the Wild Tea Experience Center (야생차체험관), the Ecology Experience Site (생태체험장), and Keun-Gulmokjae Pass (큰굴목재), before reaching Songgwangsa via the barley-rice house.

This piece is the order for walking that trail in autumn, in one direction. Both temples are visited, but there's no shop list. Lunch is one stop — the barley-rice house on the pass — and that's the whole course.

  • Total time: about 9 hours from Suncheon Station (50 min one-way by bus + 40 min at Seonamsa + 4-5 hours walking + lunch + 40 min at Songgwangsa + 1 hour 25 min one-way by bus) — all walking-segment minutes are estimates
  • Best time: weekday, the first morning bus from Suncheon Station — the barley-rice house closes at 4:30 pm, and in November the sun sets around 5 pm. Fall color runs late October to early November for southern mountains (varies by year)
  • Incline: yes — uphill from Seonamsa to Keun-Gulmokjae Pass, downhill toward Songgwangsa. Sneakers or better, 1L of water, cash

Why Cheonnyeon Bulsim-gil — One Mountain Monastery, One Sangha Temple

Seven Sansa temples of Korea — Tongdosa, Buseoksa, Bongjeongsa, Beopjusa, Magoksa, Seonamsa, and Daeheungsa — joined UNESCO's list in June 2018. Seonamsa is one of them. Songgwangsa is not on that list — instead it carries a different weight, as the Sangha Jewel (승보) of the Three Jewels of Buddha, Dharma, and Sangha, the temple that has produced many great monks. A UNESCO mountain monastery and a Sangha temple sitting on opposite sides of one mountain, linked by a course you can walk in a day, exists only here in Korea.

The distance is commonly given as "about 12km," but sources vary from 8.7km to 12km, and Suncheon city's official page doesn't state a distance at all. What's certain is the time — a comfortable 5 hours, lunch included. This is a different route from the hiking course that summits the peak, Janggunbong (장군봉), so don't confuse it with a hiking app's "Jogyesan traverse" record.

📖 Narrative 1 — The Pass the Monks Crossed: Keun-Gulmokjae Pass is the pass monks from both temples once crossed to visit each other. The barley-rice house atop the pass has stood at that crossing point for a long time, and today it's the only midway shop and lunch stop for walkers. One rice house on the road from temple to temple — the shape of this course is itself the narrative.

🚶 The Order — From Seonamsa to Songgwangsa

The starting point is Suncheon Station (순천역) — not a KTX stop, not Seoul's Line 1, but a regional rail station. From the stop in front of the station, city bus Line 1 (12 times a day, roughly every 80 minutes) takes about 50 minutes to Seonamsa. Line 16 (3 times a day) also runs there. Fare is 1,400 won by transit card; transfer free within 60 minutes of getting off. Check the day's first departure time with Suncheon city's bus information.

① Suncheon Station → Seonamsa (🚌 about 50 min · Line 1)

  • The bus climbs the mountain road through Seungju-eup and you get off at the last stop. There's a ticket booth, but admission is free (free since the cultural heritage viewing fee was abolished). The approach is long — about 1km of forest trail before you reach the temple.

② Seonamsa (about 40 min here)

  • The first sight is Seungseongyo Bridge (승선교), a rainbow-shaped stone bridge over the stream (a National Treasure). True to a UNESCO Sansa temple, little has been altered — faded dancheong paintwork, a narrow courtyard.
  • Look for two things: the Seonammae plum tree (선암매) in the passage beside Palsangjeon Hall (팔상전) — a Natural Monument roughly 600 years old (it blooms in spring, but the tree itself stands there in autumn too) — and the old haeuso outhouse (해우소), still called "Korea's most beautiful restroom" and still in use.
  • Look for the Cheonnyeon Bulsim-gil trail marker at the upper end of the temple grounds, toward the Wild Tea Experience Center.

③ Seonamsa → Wild Tea Experience Center → Ecology Experience Site → Keun-Gulmokjae Pass (🚶 about 2 hr · estimate)

  • All of the course's climb is here. Past tea fields and cypress forest, up to the pass. Fill up on water at the temple — there's no shop before the pass.
  • 📖 Narrative 2 — Two Temples, Two Characters: Seonamsa is small, old, and quiet. Songgwangsa is large, with many new buildings and heavier car traffic — foreign Google reviews put "a beautiful loop path" and "feels more like a place people live than a Joseon-era temple" side by side. Cross the pass and the temple's face changes. That contrast is what you're walking to see.

④ Keun-Gulmokjae Barley-Rice House — Lunch (about 40 min here)

  • Below the pass sits the Jogyesan barley-rice house (조계산 보리밥집). Barley rice comes with seasoned vegetables, soybean paste soup, and pajeon (barley rice around 9,000 won). 10 am–4:30 pm, closed Mondays and Tuesdays, cash or bank transfer only — subject to change, so confirm before you leave. This is the only named-business-level stop on the course, though we keep the name generic.
  • Get this far and you're past the halfway point of the course. On the days it's closed (Mon/Tue), pack a lunch.

⑤ Barley-Rice House → Songgwangsa (🚶 about 1 hr 30 min · estimate)

  • Downhill. Follow the valley into the Songgwangsa temple grounds. Reaching the ticket booth means you've arrived — temple admission itself is reported to have switched to free, while the on-site Seongbo Museum (성보박물관) may charge separately (3,000 won), so check the posted sign on-site.

⑥ Songgwangsa → Suncheon Station (about 40 min here + 🚌 about 1 hr 25 min · Line 111)

  • A loop through the scale of this Sangha temple. If you still have time and legs, take the left path past Cheongnyanggak (청량각) from the ticket booth to Bulliram Hermitage (불일암) — the hermitage where Ven. Beopjeong (법정 스님) once lived — a steep 30-minute walk on Musoyu-gil (무소유길), the "Non-Possession Trail."
  • Wrapping up: from the Songgwangsa stop, Line 111 (17 times a day, roughly every 60 minutes) takes about 1 hour 25 minutes back to Suncheon Station. Line 63 (4 times a day) also runs. The last bus time isn't on the official schedule — confirm it the day of — this is the single most important number on this course. Buses run more often from the Songgwangsa side than from Seonamsa's side (Line 1, 12 times a day), which is why this course walks in this direction.

🙏 Manners for This Course — Temple and Mountain

  • No photos, shoes, or hats inside the main halls. Step aside when you meet a monk on the path.
  • Walk mountain trails in single file. Say something before passing the person ahead of you. Music stays in earphones.
  • The barley-rice house turns tables fast — clear your seat once you've eaten. Bring cash.
  • Carry your trash back down. Bins are rare on both the mountain and the temple grounds.
  • No smoking anywhere on the mountain. Autumn is wildfire season.

💡 Where People Get Tripped Up

  • Trust the time, not the distance: "12km" varies by source (8.7 to 12km). Plan on 5 hours, lunch included.
  • This overlaps autumn wildfire-caution season: nationally Oct 20–Dec 15 (2025 figures, announced yearly). Whether Jogyesan Provincial Park has its own entry restrictions should be checked with Suncheon city or the park before you leave — peak fall color and fire restrictions fall in the same window.
  • The last bus: Line 111's last departure isn't on the official page. Aim to reach Songgwangsa before 4 pm.
  • Barley-rice house closed Mon/Tue, cash only: cards don't work. On closed days, there's no midway shop at all.
  • Songgwangsa is not a UNESCO mountain monastery: only Seonamsa is listed. Plenty of write-ups lump the two temples together as "both UNESCO sites."
  • Not to be confused with Wanju Songgwangsa (완주 송광사): search "Songgwangsa" on templestay booking sites and Wanju Songgwangsa in North Jeolla comes up too. Pick "Suncheon Songgwangsa."

FAQ 6

Q1. What about the reverse direction (Songgwangsa → Seonamsa)?

  • Possible. But buses on the Seonamsa side (Line 1) run only 12 times a day, so the last leg gets tight. If you'd rather save the climb for later, reverse works too — but this direction is safer for catching the last bus.

Q2. I'm worried about my fitness level.

  • This is a pass route, not a summit climb. If you can walk flat ground for 4 to 5 hours, you're fine. The climbing is concentrated in segment ③.

Q3. Can I do this the same day as the National Garden?

  • Not realistically. This course alone runs 9 hours from Suncheon Station. Save the garden and wetland for another day (#1049); the market and old town wall are #1368.

Q4. What if I want to stay overnight at a temple?

  • Both Seonamsa and Suncheon's Songgwangsa run Templestay (템플스테이) — book at templestay.com (#332). Staying at Seonamsa and setting out in the morning makes this course easiest.

Q5. When does fall color peak?

  • Late October to early November for southern mountains. It varies year to year, so check the Korea Forest Service (산림청)'s fall foliage forecast. The same window falls in wildfire-caution season, so check restriction notices too.

Q6. What about going by taxi?

  • Suncheon city runs a tour taxi package (3 hours for 80,000 won, 5 hours for 120,000 won, city reservation system). One-way meter fare wasn't confirmed. The city bus is the honest choice.
