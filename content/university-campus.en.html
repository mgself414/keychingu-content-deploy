🎯 Why a Seoul University Campus Walking Guide?

One of the top contents for foreign vlogs and Instagram in Korea is Korean university campuses — the main buildings, libraries, ponds, and cherry blossom paths of Korea's prestigious universities are consistently ranked as top photo spots for foreigners, and frequent backdrops for K-dramas ("Our Beloved Summer," "Cheese in the Trap," "SKY Castle," "Itaewon Class"). Korean university campuses combine traditional European/American architecture + Korean natural gardens + the vibrant atmosphere of the MZ generation (Millennials and Gen Z).

All campuses offer free admission for foreigners + free of charge + standard English guidance. The two prime seasons are spring cherry blossoms (first to second week of April) and autumn foliage (October-November), plus the campus festival season in May. ⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Universities = Only for Students" — Unaware of Free Entry for Foreigners

Korean university campuses offer free admission for foreigners + free of charge + standard English guidance. Free campus maps are distributed at the main gate + campus shuttle buses (Seoul National University (서울대)·Konkuk University (건대) etc.) are available. Foreigners can enter student cafeterias, libraries, museums, and art galleries. The annual campus festival in May is a campus open season + a top priority season for foreigners.

2. "Spring Cherry Blossoms = Only Yeouido (윤중로)·Han River (한강)"

The top spots for spring cherry blossoms are Yonsei University's (연세대) Baekyang-ro (백양로), Korea University's (고려대) Central Plaza (중앙광장), Kyung Hee University's (경희대) Peace Hall (평화의 전당) Plaza, and Ewha Womans University's (이화여대) Hangaram Plaza (한가람 광장). These are hidden gems unknown to many foreigners, besides Yeouido (여의도) and Han River Parks (한강공원). They are less crowded on weekdays from 9-10 AM or after 6 PM.

3. "University Campuses = All Similar"

Korean university campuses come in 4 types: European-style architecture (Kyung Hee University (경희대)·Yonsei University (연세대)·Ewha Womans University (이화여대)) vs Traditional Korean architecture (Sungkyunkwan University (성균관대)·Dongguk University (동국대)) vs Nature·Mountains (Seoul National University (서울대)·Kookmin University (국민대)·some parts of Korea University (고려대)) vs Modern·Han River (한강) (Hanyang University (한양대)·Chung-Ang University (중앙대)·Konkuk University (건대)). All 4 types are top spots for foreigner Instagram + top for spring cherry blossoms and autumn foliage.

⚙ FAQ 8 Questions

Q1. Is admission to university campuses free for foreigners?

Mostly free + 24-hour exterior access + free campus maps distributed at the main gate. Campus shuttle buses are available (Seoul National University (서울대)·Konkuk University (건대) etc.). English guidance is standard.

Q2. Which campuses are top for spring cherry blossoms?

Kyung Hee University's (경희대) Peace Hall (평화의전당) Plaza, Yonsei University's (연세대) Baekyang-ro (백양로), Korea University's (고려대) Central Plaza (중앙광장), Ewha Womans University's (이화여대) Hangaram Plaza (한가람 광장). Peak season is the first to second week of April. Less crowded on weekdays from 9-10 AM or after 6 PM.

Q3. Which campuses are top for autumn foliage?

Korea University's (고려대) Central Plaza (중앙광장), Yonsei University's (연세대) Baekyang-ro (백양로), Seoul National University's (서울대) Jahayeon (자하연), Kookmin University's (국민대) Bukhansan (북악산). Peak season is October-November.

Q4. When are campus festivals held?

Annually in the second to third week of May (for 3 days) — Campus open house + stage performances + student booths. Free admission for foreigners. #1 for a deep dive into Korean university youth culture.

Q5. What is the demographic of foreign students?

Konkuk University (건국대) 6,000+ (Korea's #1, China·Southeast Asia·Russia) + Ewha Womans University (이화여대) (Female foreign students #1) + Yonsei University (연세대) (Language exchange #1) + Hankuk University of Foreign Studies (외대) (Foreign language #1).

Q6. Can foreigners eat at student cafeterias?

Yes — Free admission for foreigners. Prices range from ₩6,000-9,000 (~$4-7 / ~30-45 RMB / ~600-900 JPY) per meal (same as Korean students). English menus may be limited, but photo menus are usually available.

Q7. Which campuses are K-drama filming locations?

Yonsei University (연세대) ("Our Beloved Summer"·"Cheese in the Trap")·Korea University (고려대) ("SKY Castle")·Kyung Hee University (경희대) ("Cheese in the Trap")·parts of Seoul National University (서울대) ("Mr. Sunshine"). Frequent backdrops for Korean youth·romance K-dramas.

Q8. Can I walk around campus wearing Hanbok (한복)?

Sungkyunkwan University (성균관대)·Dongguk University (동국대)·Ewha Womans University's (이화여대) Felice Chapel (펠리스 채플) are recommended — Hanok (한옥) architecture + Hanbok (한복) combination is #1 for Instagram. Hanbok (한복) rental in Bukchon (북촌)·Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) is 30 minutes away.
