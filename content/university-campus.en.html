12 Seoul University Campus Walks — Campuses Chosen by Public Vote, and Where You Can and Cannot Go In

Subtitle: The 12 Seoul campuses that received votes in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the campus tour theme, grouped into 4 zones to fit a walking route. Each school opens a different range to outsiders. Ewha Womans University does not run tours for the general public and bars outsiders after 22:00. At Korea National University of Arts (K-Arts), individuals may walk around freely. Buildings designated as Historic Sites or Treasures, free museums, and exhibition halls that are closed right now are all listed with dates.

ISSUE 06 · #204 · 2026-11-24 · Seoul University Campus

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 12

This list is for people who want to walk a Korean university campus while traveling. Seoul campuses have buildings designated as national heritage and university museums that open for free. Some campuses still have Joseon-era buildings, like Munmyo (문묘) next to Sungkyunkwan University (성균관대학교), and some, like K-Arts (한예종), sit right beside a Joseon royal tomb.

There is one selection rule. We included all 12 Seoul campuses that placed within the top 38 of the Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) "100×100 Spot Discovery" (명소발굴 100×100) public vote on the campus tour theme. They are ranks 1–6 (Kyung Hee, Ewha, Seoul National, Yonsei, Korea, Sungkyunkwan) and ranks 12, 19, 20, 30, 37, and 38 (Konkuk, Hanyang, K-Arts Seokgwan-dong, University of Seoul, Dongguk, Chung-Ang). Every campus ranked in between is outside Seoul. The top vote-getters outside Seoul are KAIST (rank 7), Korea Maritime and Ocean University (rank 8), and Pusan National University (rank 9).

A school is a place for classes and research, not a tourist attraction. So instead of "which one is prettiest," we wrote "how far can you go in" first. Where a school has published its rules for outsiders, we copied its wording.

⚠ Notation principle: Museum and art museum hours, closed days, admission fees, tour information, and directions are the values read on each school's and institution's website on 2026-11-24. The heritage designation types and dates are values from the Korea Heritage Service (국가유산청) National Heritage Portal (국가유산포털), and the registration year of the University of Seoul (서울시립대학교) museum building is the value in the school's campus guide. The opening hours of Munmyo and Myeongnyundang (명륜당) in Seonggyungwan (성균관) are values from the Seoul tourism portal (VisitSeoul, revised 2026-09-16). The hours and fees of Uireung (의릉) are values from the Royal Palaces and Tombs Center (궁능유적본부). Walking times are copied only where the school wrote them itself. Votes and ranks are values from the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the campus tour theme. Hours and closures can change.

🚪 Entry Etiquette and Rules — Read This First

Each school opens a different range to outsiders. We copy the rules the schools state themselves first. Kyung Hee (경희대학교), Korea (고려대학교), Sungkyunkwan (성균관대학교), Konkuk (건국대학교), Hanyang (한양대학교), University of Seoul (서울시립대학교), Dongguk (동국대학교), and Chung-Ang (중앙대학교) do not post separate rules for outsiders walking their campuses on their sites, so follow the basic etiquette at the end of this section.

Ewha Womans University (이화여자대학교) — This is the wording in the school's campus tour guide.

  • "For the time being, we are not running tours for general visitors." A VR tour is offered instead. Groups from middle schools, high schools, and private educational institutions can get a campus leader tour by applying in advance.
  • "Entry to the inside of all classroom and research spaces on campus is restricted."
  • "Photography and filming that infringe on students' portrait rights or are for commercial purposes are prohibited."
  • "Outsiders cannot enter the campus after 22:00."
  • "Group visits without prior approval may be denied entry."

Korea National University of Arts, Seokgwan-dong Campus (한국예술종합학교 석관동캠퍼스) — The English visitor guide separates individuals from groups.

  • Individuals: "For individuals, the campus tour can NOT be arranged, and you may walk around the campus freely according to your interests." This means no tour is offered to individuals, but you may walk around the campus freely.
  • Groups: a group of more than 10 people applies as a group visit. Tours must be requested 3–4 weeks ahead.

Yonsei University (연세대학교) — The 2026 academic year tour notice (posted 2026-01-12) says: "Group tours run by outside companies unrelated to Yonsei University, or run on their own by groups or organizations that come on their own initiative, are absolutely not permitted, and violators may be expelled." This means a group that arrives on a travel agency tour and gives its own commentary is not allowed. Individuals can join the walk-in tour the school runs (see the Yonsei entry below).

Seoul National University (서울대학교) — This is the wording in the Office of Student Affairs tour guide. "For visits to and use of on-campus facilities not included in the official campus tour of the Office of Student Affairs (for example, using on-campus restaurants), visitors (organizations) should contact the relevant facility directly." To use a student cafeteria or an individual building, you have to ask that facility separately.

General etiquette to follow at the other schools

  • Do not go inside lecture halls, research buildings, or library reading rooms. Walk on the paths, plazas, and outside the buildings.
  • Walk especially quietly during exam periods. Do not talk loudly on the phone or play music in front of buildings.
  • Do not take photos that show a student's face large. Do not photograph through the windows of classrooms where a class is in session.
  • When you go in a group, keep it small so you do not block the path. If the school has a group tour procedure, follow it.
  • Cars enter only through the designated entrance. Dongguk (동국대학교) writes that "all vehicles can enter only through the Dongguk University main gate."

① Sinchon Zone (신촌권) — 2 Campuses

Ewha Womans Univ. Station (이대역) and Sinchon Station (신촌역) are both on Line 2. Yonsei's guide also describes Sinchon Station Exit 3 as the way "toward Yonsei University and Ewha Womans University." The two schools pair well in a half day.

Ewha Womans University (이화여자대학교)

  • Public vote: 186 votes · rank 2 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" (명소발굴 100×100) public vote on the campus tour theme.
  • Highlight — Pfeiffer Hall (파이퍼 홀): The official name is "Seoul Ewha Womans University Pfeiffer Hall" (서울 이화여자대학교 파이퍼 홀). It is a Registered National Heritage (국가등록문화유산), registered on 2002-05-31. The National Heritage Portal gives the period as 1935. It is 1 main building with a total floor area of 4,281.3㎡, owned and managed by the school corporation Ewha Haktang (이화학당). The address is 52 Ewhayeodae-gil, Seodaemun-gu (서대문구 이화여대길 52). Entry to classroom spaces is restricted, so view it from outside.
  • Highlight — Ewha Womans University Museum (이화여대박물관): Open Mon–Fri 09:30–17:00. On the last Wednesday of each month, "Culture Day" (문화가 있는 날), it stays open until 19:00. Closed Sat, Sun, and public holidays, and also during the exhibition changeover in January–February each year (may vary). Admission is free.
  • Current status: The museum website carries a notice of closure from 2026-11-26 (Thu) to 2026-12-01 (Tue).
  • Getting there: Come out of Line 2 Ewha Womans Univ. Station (이대역) Exit 2 or 3 and go straight for 300m.
  • Entry: No tours for the general public, entry to the inside of classroom and research spaces restricted, no outsiders after 22:00. Follow the wording in "Entry Etiquette and Rules" above.

Yonsei University Sinchon Campus (연세대학교 신촌캠퍼스)

  • Public vote: 172 votes · rank 4 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" (명소발굴 100×100) public vote on the campus tour theme.
  • Highlight — Underwood Hall (언더우드관): The official name is "Seoul Yonsei University Underwood Hall" (서울 연세대학교 언더우드관). Its designation type is Historic Site (사적), designated on 1981-09-25. The period is given as 1924, the area is 777㎡, and it is owned and managed by the school corporation Yonsei University (학교법인 연세대학교).
  • Highlight — Yonsei University Museum (연세대학교박물관): Mon–Sat 09:30–17:00, last entry 16:30. Closed Sundays and public holidays. Admission is free, though rented exhibitions may charge a separate fee. It is a university museum that also opens on Saturdays, so it fits a weekend plan.
  • School tours: Based on the 2026 academic year tour notice (posted 2026-01-12).
  • Walk-in tour: for individuals or groups of fewer than 10, Mon–Fri at 13:00 and 16:00, no reservation, departing from Baekyang Nuri (백양누리). It takes 40 minutes in Korean.
  • Foreign-language tour: for foreign individuals and groups who find it hard to follow a Korean tour. It takes 50 minutes in English and you apply on the English website.
  • Group tour: middle school students and older, 10–100 people, Mon–Fri at 10:00 and 14:00, 75 minutes. Apply by 8 days before the tour date.
  • Getting there: Line 2 Sinchon Station (신촌역) Exit 2 (toward Yonsei University and Severance Hospital) or Exit 3 (toward Yonsei University and Ewha Womans University). The address is 50 Yonsei-ro, Seodaemun-gu (서대문구 연세로 50).

② Northeast Zone (동북권) — 5 Campuses

Start at Hyehwa (혜화, Sungkyunkwan University) and head east through Anam (안암, Korea University), Hoegi (회기, Kyung Hee University and University of Seoul), and Seokgwan-dong (석관동, K-Arts and Uireung). From Hoegi Station (회기역) alone you can reach both Kyung Hee (Exit 1) and the University of Seoul (Exit 2). Doing it all in one day is tight, so splitting it over two days is better.

Sungkyunkwan University Humanities and Social Sciences Campus (성균관대학교 인문사회과학캠퍼스)

  • Public vote: 130 votes · rank 6 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" (명소발굴 100×100) public vote on the campus tour theme.
  • Highlight — Seoul Munmyo and Seonggyungwan (서울 문묘와 성균관): A Joseon-era site attached to the campus. "Seoul Munmyo and Seonggyungwan" is a Historic Site (사적). It was designated on 1964-11-10 and covers 40,089㎡. The Korea Heritage Service (국가유산청) owns it and Jongno-gu (종로구) manages it.
  • Highlight — Daeseongjeon, Dongmu, Seomu, Sammun, Myeongnyundang (대성전·동무·서무·삼문·명륜당): These five buildings are designated a Treasure (보물) under the name "Seoul Munmyo and Seonggyungwan" (1963-01-21). The National Heritage Portal gives the period as the 35th year of King Seonjo (1602). The location is 25-1 Seonggyungwan-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 성균관로 25-1, Myeongnyun 3-ga, 명륜3가).
  • Opening hours (Seoul tourism portal): Summer season (Mar–Oct) 09:00–18:00, winter season (Nov–Feb) 09:00–17:00. Entry to Myeongnyundang (명륜당) is free.
  • Getting there: Come out of Line 4 Hyehwa Station (혜화역) Exit 4 and walk. The Seoul tourism portal gives 749m from Hyehwa Station Exit 4 to Myeongnyundang at Munmyo. You can also take village bus Jongno 02 (종로02) from Line 3 Anguk Station (안국역) Exit 2 or Line 1 Jonggak Station (종각역) Exits 2·3. The school address is 25-2 Seonggyungwan-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 성균관로 25-2).

Korea University Seoul Campus (고려대학교 서울캠퍼스)

  • Public vote: 169 votes · rank 5 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" (명소발굴 100×100) public vote on the campus tour theme.
  • Highlight — Main Building and Central Library (본관·중앙도서관): Both buildings are Historic Sites, designated on 1981-09-25.
  • Seoul Korea University Main Building (서울 고려대학교 본관): period given as 1934, area 1,510㎡. Owned by the school corporation Korea Jungang Hakwon (고려중앙학원).
  • Seoul Korea University Central Library (서울 고려대학교 중앙도서관): period given as 1937, area 1,827㎡.
  • Highlight — Korea University Museum (고려대학교박물관): Open 10:00–17:00, last entry 1 hour before closing. Closed every Sunday, Monday, statutory public holidays, and days the museum sets. Permanent exhibitions are free, and special and thematic exhibitions charge a separate fee.
  • School tours: Regular tours led by student ambassadors run on announced dates. Only individuals and small groups of 20 or fewer apply. Groups of 20 or more apply separately as group tours (based on the notice posted 2023-03-14; later announcements go up on the school's tour board).
  • Getting there: Line 6 Korea Univ. Station (고려대역) Exit 1 (humanities and social sciences area), Line 6 Anam Station (안암역) Exit 4 (natural sciences area and Korea University Medical Center), Anam Station Exit 2 (rear gate of the College of Political Science and Economics). The address is 145 Anam-ro, Seongbuk-gu (성북구 안암로 145).

Kyung Hee University Seoul Campus (경희대학교 서울캠퍼스)

  • Public vote: 188 votes · rank 1 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" (명소발굴 100×100) public vote on the campus tour theme.
  • Highlight — Grand Peace Palace (평화의전당): Construction began in 1976 and it opened in 1999, the school's 50th anniversary. The school introduces the building as "medieval Gothic style." It has 4,400 seats.
  • Highlight — Kyung Hee Central Museum (경희대 중앙박물관): On the 4th floor of the Central Library (중앙도서관). Regularly open Mon–Fri 10:00–17:00 and closed Saturdays and public holidays. Admission is free.
  • School tours: The admissions office (입학처) campus tour accepts applications only from high school groups. Individual tour applications are not accepted. For travelers, that means walking the campus on your own.
  • Getting there: Go out of Line 1 and Jungang Line Hoegi Station (회기역) Exit 1. In front of Exit 1, take village bus 01 and get off at Kyung Hee Medical Center (경희의료원). The address is 26 Kyungheedae-ro, Dongdaemun-gu (동대문구 경희대로 26).

University of Seoul (서울시립대학교)

  • Public vote: 63 votes · rank 30 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" (명소발굴 100×100) public vote on the campus tour theme.
  • Highlight — Museum building (박물관 건물): The school's campus map says the museum building was completed in 1937. It also describes it as the oldest building on campus together with Gyeongnonggwan (경농관). The school's explanation is that it was "designated a Seoul Metropolitan City Registered Cultural Heritage in 2020 for recognition of the historical value of modern architecture." Exhibition Rooms 1–3 are on the ground floor of the building.
  • Getting there: Come out of Cheongnyangni Station (Univ. of Seoul Station entrance, 청량리역) Exit 3 and go straight for about 300m to arrive on foot. Exits 4 and 5 are for bus transfers. If you walk from Hoegi Station (회기역) Exit 2, you enter by the side gate and rear gate. The address is 163 Seoulsiripdae-ro, Dongdaemun-gu (동대문구 서울시립대로 163).
  • Route tip: See the University of Seoul from Hoegi Station Exit 2, then go back to Hoegi Station Exit 1 for Kyung Hee, and one station links two campuses.

Korea National University of Arts, Seokgwan-dong Campus (한국예술종합학교 석관동캠퍼스)

  • Public vote: 75 votes · rank 20 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" (명소발굴 100×100) public vote on the campus tour theme.
  • Entry: Individuals may walk freely without a tour. Groups of more than 10 apply 3–4 weeks ahead (see "Entry Etiquette and Rules" above).
  • Highlight — campus exhibition spaces (캠퍼스 전시 공간): The school's campus map marks the Gallery on the 1st floor of the Headquarters Building (본부동), the Kang Tae-Hee Gallery on the 2nd floor of the School of Fine Arts (미술원), Gallery B104 on basement level 1, and the Art Theater (Lee O Young Art Theater, 예술극장). The school does not give exhibition hours separately, so only the locations are copied.
  • Highlight — Seoul Uireung Royal Tomb (서울 의릉, right next door): Next to the campus is Uireung, a Joseon royal tomb.
  • Its designation type is Historic Site (사적), designated on 1970-05-26. The period is given as the 4th year of King Gyeongjong of Joseon (1724) and the area is 434,386㎡. The location is 146-20 Hwarang-ro 32-gil, Seongbuk-gu (성북구 화랑로32길 146-20), managed by the Joseon Royal Tombs Central District Office (조선왕릉중부지구관리소). By the notice of 2011-07-28 the name changed from "Uireung" to "Seoul Uireung."
  • Opening hours: Feb–May and Sep–Oct 09:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00), Jun–Aug 09:00–18:30 (last entry 17:30), Nov–Jan 09:00–17:30 (last entry 16:30).
  • Closed day: every Monday. If Monday is a public holiday or substitute holiday, it opens and closes on the next non-holiday day.
  • Admission: adult individual 1,000 won, the same for Koreans and foreigners. Groups of 10 or more pay 800 won. For foreigners, adult means ages 19–64.
  • You go up to the tomb mound with a guide. It runs on Saturdays at 10:00 and 14:00 from Mar to Dec, and on Sundays at 13:00 and 14:00 from Mar to Jun and Sep to Nov.
  • Getting there: 10 minutes on foot from Dolgoji Station (돌곶이역) Exit 8 and 10 minutes on foot from Sangwolgok Station (상월곡역) Exit 3. Both routes enter through the rear gate entrance. From Sinimun Station (신이문역) Exit 1 it is 20 minutes on foot. The Dolgoji Station route written in the school's guide is as follows. "Go straight 65m from Exit 8 -> turn left past Seongyo Building (선교빌딩, formerly Dongwoo Building, 구 동우빌딩), go straight 70m -> turn right past Lucky Discount Mart (럭키할인마트), go straight 100m -> go 80m straight down the alley to the left of Seokgwan Police Box (석관파출소)." The Sangwolgok Station route is "Go straight 70m from Exit 3 -> turn right past Maleunsaem Gwangcheon Church (맑은샘 광천교회), go straight 270m."

③ East·Han River Zone (동부·한강권) — 2 Campuses

Konkuk Univ. Station (건대입구역) and Hanyang Univ. Station (한양대역) are both on Line 2, so one line connects them. Both schools' museums close on Sundays. The Konkuk museum also closes on Saturdays, so pair them on a weekday.

Konkuk University Seoul Campus (건국대학교 서울캠퍼스)

  • Public vote: 100 votes · rank 12 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" (명소발굴 100×100) public vote on the campus tour theme.
  • Highlight — Konkuk University Museum (건국대학교 박물관): The museum says it opened in 1963. Open Mon–Fri 10:00–16:00 and closed for lunch 12:00–13:00. Closed Sat, Sun, and public holidays, and admission is free.
  • On view now: The permanent exhibition 《Munhwa Jeonjae (文畵制鐫)》 (《문화제전》) runs from 2026-09 to 2028-09 in the 2nd-floor special exhibition hall of the museum. About 150 items from the collection are shown. There is also a separate Sangheo Memorial Exhibition Hall (상허기념전시실) on the founder Dr. Yu Seok-chang (유석창).
  • School tours: The school's tour guide accepts only middle and high school student groups and nonprofit educational institution groups (30–120 people). It states "individual student applications are not accepted." Travelers walk on their own.
  • Getting there: Line 2 Konkuk Univ. Station (건대입구역) Exit 2, Line 7 Konkuk Univ. Station Exit 4, Line 7 Children's Grand Park Station (어린이대공원역) Exit 3. The address is 120 Neungdong-ro, Gwangjin-gu (광진구 능동로 120).

  • Highlight — Hanyang University Museum (한양대학교 박물관): Open 10:00–17:00 and closed every Sunday and public holiday. Admission is free.

Station (한양대역), Line 2 Yongdap Station (용답역), Suin-Bundang Line Wangsimni Station (왕십리역), and Gyeongui-Jungang Line Wangsimni Station. Line 2 Hanyang Univ. Station is the station with the same name as the school. Trunk buses 121, 302, and N62 and branch buses 2012, 2014, 2016, and 2222 stop at the bus stop in front of the main gate. The address is 222 Wangsimni-ro, Seongdong-gu (성동구 왕십리로 222).

④ Namsan·South Zone (남산·남부권) — 3 Campuses

Dongguk is in Pil-dong, Jung-gu (중구 필동), Chung-Ang is in Heukseok-dong, Dongjak-gu (동작구 흑석동), and Seoul National is in Gwanak-gu (관악구). They are far apart, so attach them one at a time to nearby plans rather than combining them.

Dongguk University Seoul Campus (동국대학교 서울캠퍼스)

  • Public vote: 57 votes · rank 37 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" (명소발굴 100×100) public vote on the campus tour theme.
  • Highlights: A campus in Pil-dong, Jung-gu (중구 필동). The on-campus museum has posted a closure notice, so instead of a facility we included it as a place to walk the campus paths on the Namsan slope. Attach it to a Chungmuro (충무로) and Pil-dong (필동) plan.
  • Getting there: Line 3 Dongguk Univ. Station (동대입구역) Exit 6, Line 3·4 Chungmuro Station (충무로역) Exit 1. The address is 30 Pildong-ro 1-gil, Jung-gu (중구 필동로1길 30).

🗺 Route — Half-Day Courses by Zone

The zones are far from each other. Plan 1 zone a day, 2 at most. If you want to include a museum, fix the day of the week first. Most university museums open on weekdays only.

A. Sinchon Zone (Mon–Fri recommended; to see both museums, avoid 2026-11-26 to 12-01)

  1. Morning Ewha Womans Univ. Station (이대역) Exits 2·3 → outside of Pfeiffer Hall (파이퍼 홀) at Ewha, Ewha Womans University Museum (이화여대박물관, Mon–Fri 09:30–17:00)
  • If you pick the 16:00 Yonsei tour, see the Yonsei University Museum (연세대학교박물관, last entry 16:30) before the tour
  1. Afternoon Move toward Sinchon Station (신촌역) → Yonsei 13:00 or 16:00 walk-in tour (Mon–Fri, departing from Baekyang Nuri, 백양누리), Underwood Hall (언더우드관), Yonsei University Museum (Mon–Sat until 17:00)
  2. Before evening If you will stay on the Ewha campus, leave before 22:00

B. Northeast Zone West — Sungkyunkwan and Korea (Tue–Sat recommended)

  1. Morning Hyehwa Station (혜화역) Exit 4 → Munmyo and Myeongnyundang (문묘·명륜당, until 17:00 from Nov to Feb)
  2. Afternoon Line 6 Korea Univ. Station (고려대역) Exit 1 → Korea University Main Building and Central Library (본관·중앙도서관), Korea University Museum (고려대학교박물관, closed Sun·Mon, 10:00–17:00)

C. Northeast Zone East — Hoegi and Seokgwan-dong (Tue–Fri recommended)

  1. Morning Hoegi Station (회기역) Exit 1 → Kyung Hee Grand Peace Palace (평화의전당), Central Museum (중앙박물관, Mon–Fri)
  2. Around lunch Hoegi Station Exit 2 → exterior of the University of Seoul museum building (서울시립대 박물관)
  3. Afternoon Dolgoji Station (돌곶이역) Exit 8 or Sangwolgok Station (상월곡역) Exit 3 → K-Arts (한예종) campus → Seoul Uireung (서울 의릉, closed Mondays, last entry 16:30 from Nov to Jan)

Uireung is closed on Mondays and the Kyung Hee museum is closed on Saturdays. That is why C fits Tue–Fri.

D. East·Han River Zone — Konkuk and Hanyang (Mon–Fri recommended)

  1. Morning Konkuk Univ. Station (건대입구역) Exit 2 → Konkuk University Museum (건국대학교 박물관, 10:00–16:00, closed 12:00–13:00)
  2. Afternoon Line 2 to Hanyang Univ. Station (한양대역) → Hanyang University Museum (한양대학교 박물관, until 17:00)

E. Namsan·South Zone — Dongguk, Chung-Ang, Seoul National

These three are far from each other, so attach them to other plans rather than combining them. Attach Dongguk (동국대학교) to a Chungmuro (충무로) and Pil-dong (필동) plan. Chung-Ang (중앙대학교) has a shuttle on weekdays during the semester. For Seoul National (서울대학교), avoid the MoA exhibition preparation period (2026-11-23 to 12-02) and avoid Mondays. You cannot see the Kyujanggak (규장각) exhibition hall within 2026.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Assuming every campus is equally open — Ewha Womans University (이화여자대학교) has no tours for the general public, blocks entry to classroom and research spaces, and does not let outsiders in after 22:00. Yonsei (연세대학교) prohibits tours run on their own by outside companies or ad hoc groups. The one school that says individuals may walk freely is K-Arts (한예종). Check each school's rules separately.
  2. Going to a museum on a weekend or Monday — The Kyung Hee, Ewha Womans, and Konkuk museums open only Mon–Fri, and the Hanyang museum closes on Sundays. The Korea University museum closes on Sun and Mon. The Museum of Art, SNU (서울대학교미술관, MoA) and Uireung (의릉) close on Mondays. The places that open on weekends are the Yonsei University Museum (Sat), the Museum of Art, SNU (Tue–Sun, but an exhibition preparation period runs through 2026-12-02), and Uireung (closed Mondays only).
  3. Setting out late on a winter afternoon — From November, closing times move earlier. Seonggyungwan Munmyo (성균관 문묘) closes at 17:00 from Nov to Feb, and Uireung's last entry is 16:30 from Nov to Jan. The Konkuk University Museum (건국대학교 박물관) closes at 16:00. Plan to arrive before 3 p.m.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. Is there a campus tour in English? — Yonsei has a 50-minute English tour for foreigners, and you apply on the English website. Yonsei's Korean walk-in tour departs from Baekyang Nuri (백양누리) with no reservation, Mon–Fri at 13:00 and 16:00. Seoul National (서울대학교) takes online applications for the Office of Student Affairs regular tours (including Sat and Sun), and the guide does not state the language of the tour. Ewha Womans (이화여자대학교) does not run tours for the general public and points to a VR tour. K-Arts (한예종) does not offer individual tours, but you may walk around freely instead.
  2. Are there places that cost money? — The places that state free admission are the Kyung Hee Central Museum (경희대 중앙박물관), the Ewha Womans University Museum (이화여대박물관), the Yonsei University Museum (연세대학교박물관), the permanent exhibitions of the Korea University Museum (고려대학교박물관), the Konkuk University Museum (건국대학교 박물관), the Hanyang University Museum (한양대학교 박물관), and the Museum of Art, SNU (서울대학교미술관). Myeongnyundang (명륜당) is also free. Of the places that state a fee, those that charge are Uireung (의릉, adult 1,000 won, groups of 10 or more 800 won) and the special and thematic exhibitions at the Korea University museum.
  3. What is closed in late November to early December 2026? — The Ewha Womans University Museum (이화여대박물관) is closed 2026-11-26 to 2026-12-01. The Museum of Art, SNU (서울대미술관) has no exhibition 2026-11-23 to 2026-12-02. The Seoul National University Kyujanggak (규장각) exhibition hall is closed for construction until 2026-12-31.
