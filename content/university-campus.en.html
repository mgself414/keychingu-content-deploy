⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "University = Students only" — Foreigners are not allowed to enter freely

Korean university campuses have free admission for foreigners + free + English guidance as standard. Free campus map distribution at the main gate + Shuttle buses on campus (Seoul National University, Konkuk University, etc.) OK. Foreigners are allowed to enter student cafeterias, libraries, museums, and art galleries. Every May campus festival = campus opening + first season for foreigners.

2. "Spring cherry blossoms = Yunjung-ro and Han River Bay"

The top places for spring cherry blossoms are Yonsei University Baekyang-ro, Korea University Central Square, Kyung Hee University Peace Hall Square, and Ewha Womans University Hangaram Square. Hidden gems not many foreigners know about, including Yunjung-ro (Yeouido) and Hangang Park. Quiet between 9 and 10 o'clock on weekdays or after 18 o'clock.

3. "College Campus = Similar"

Korean University Campus 4 Conclusion: European-style architecture (Kyunghee University, Yonsei University, Ewha Womans University) vs Korean traditional architecture (Sungkyunkwan University, Dongguk University) vs Nature/Mountains (Seoul National University, Kookmin University, parts of Korea University) vs Modern/Hangang (Hanyang University, Chung-Ang University, Konkuk University). 4 results: 1st place on foreign Instagram + 1st place for spring cherry blossoms and fall leaves.

① No. 1 spring cherry blossoms (3 campuses)

1. Kyung Hee University Seoul ★ No. 1 most beautiful campus in Korea

  • Address: 26 Kyunghee-daero, Dongdaemun-gu, Seoul (10-minute walk from Hoegi Station on Line 1 or 15-minute walk from Cheongnyangni Station)
  • Category: University/Campus
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — No. 1 most beautiful campus in Korea on Instagram for foreigners
  • Signature: Established in 1949 + Hall of Peace (1999, Korea’s first auditorium) + Main building neo-European style architecture (Piranezi style) + Cherry blossoms at the main gate square + Nobel Peace Prize Dean + Korea’s first hotel and tourism management department
  • Spring/Fall: No. 1 cherry blossoms at the main building square in the first to second weeks of April + No. 1 fall foliage in October-November
  • Why: Korea’s No. 1 Beautiful Campus — No. 1 on Instagram for foreigners. The Hall of Peace + the main building’s new European-style architecture is a common stage for K-dramas and entertainment shows.
  • K-drama: “Cheese in the Trap” + some background from “That Year We Were”

2. Yonsei University Sinchon Campus ★ Baekyang-ro Cherry Blossoms

  • Address: 50 Yonsei-ro, Seodaemun-gu, Seoul (10-minute walk from Sinchon Station, Line 2)
  • Category: University/Campus
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Korea’s No. 1 prestigious private university + No. 1 foreign exchange student
  • Signature: Korea's first modern private university established in 1885 + Baekyang-ro Cherry Blossom 1 + Underwood Hall (1924) + Cheongsong University + Gwanghyewon + Classic British and American private university style architecture
  • Spring/Fall: No. 1 cherry blossoms in Baekyang-ro during the first and second weeks of April + Autumn leaves in Baekyang-ro during October and November
  • Why: Korea’s No. 1 private university + No. 1 short-term language training/exchange student for foreigners + Baekyang-ro Cherry Blossoms = Korea’s prestigious deep dive
  • K-drama: A staple in Korean K-drama (university youth/romance)

3. Korea University Anam Campus ★ Spring cherry blossoms and fall foliage

  • Address: 145 Anam-ro, Seongbuk-gu, Seoul (5-minute walk from Anam Station on Line 6 or Korea University Station on Line 1)
  • Category: University/Campus
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Prestigious Korean private university + Oxford-style architecture in England
  • Signature: Established in 1905, Korea's first private university + Central Square, Main Building, Central Library (1937, Korea's first library built) + Jeonggyeong Hall, SK Future Hall, Alumni Hall + Authentic architecture in the style of Oxford, England
  • Spring/Fall: Cherry blossoms at Central Square in April + 1st place for autumn leaves in October-November
  • Why: Korea’s No. 1 private university + deep dive into authentic British architecture. Oxford/Cambridge style campus.

② Korean prestigious literature and history (3 campuses)

4. Seoul National University (Gwanak Campus) — See #174

  • Signature: 1946 Korea’s first national university + 700,000 pyeong campus + Jahayeon (pond) + Beodeulgol + Museum/Art Gallery + Festival every May
  • Spring·Fall: Natural combination of spring flowers and fall foliage (at the foot of Mt. Gwanak)
  • Why: Korea’s No. 1 national university deep dive (#174 Seoul National University guide sisters)

5. Sungkyunkwan University ★ Korea’s first university during the Joseon Dynasty

  • Address: 25-2 Sungkyunkwan-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul (5-minute walk from Hyehwa Station, Line 4)
  • Category: University/Traditional Architecture
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Korea’s No. 1 traditional architecture + Established in 1398 (Joseon’s first comprehensive university)
  • Signature: 1398 Joseon Korea’s first educational institution + Myeongnyundang·Daeseongjeon·Jonggyeonggak (Korea’s No. 1 Joseon architecture) + Spring Flower Hanok combination
  • Spring·Fall: Spring flowers + Hanok architecture = No. 1 on Instagram for foreigners (“Palace·Hanok campus”)
  • Why: Korea’s No. 1 traditional architecture + deep dive into Korea’s highest educational institution during the Joseon Dynasty. No. 1 for foreigners to walk wearing Hanbok.
  • Admission Fee: Free / Hours 09:00~18:00

6. Ewha Womans University — See #171

  • Signature: 1886 Korea’s first, world’s largest women’s university + Felice Chapel, main building, ECC basement, Hangaram Square
  • Spring/Fall: Spring cherry blossoms + 1st place on Instagram among foreign female students
  • Why: Korea’s No. 1 Women’s University deep dive (#171 Sisters of Sinchon and Ewha Womans University)

⚙ 8 FAQs

Q1. Free admission to university campuses for foreigners?

Free for most + 24-hour exterior + Free campus maps distributed at the main gate. On-campus shuttle bus OK (Seoul National University, Konkuk University, etc.). English guidance standard.

Q2. No. 1 campus for spring cherry blossoms?

Kyunghee University Peace Hall Square, Yonsei University Baekyang-ro, Korea University Central Square, Ewha Womans University Hangaram Square. Peak in the first to second weeks of April. Quiet between 9 and 10 o'clock on weekdays or after 18 o'clock.

Q3. The #1 campus for fall foliage?

Korea University Central Square, Yonsei University Baekyang-ro, Seoul National University Jahayeon, Kookmin University Bukaksan. Peak season October-November.

Q4. When is the campus festival?

2nd to 3rd week of May every year (3 days) — Campus open + stage performance + student booth. Free entry for foreigners. Ranked 1st among Korean university youth deep dives.

Q5. Foreign student demographics?

Konkuk University 6,000+ (1st in Korea, China, Southeast Asia, Russia) + Ewha Womans University (1st in foreign female students) + Yonsei University (1st in language training) + University of Foreign Studies (1st in foreign languages).

Q6. Is the student cafeteria open to foreigners?

Available — Free entry for foreigners. Price 6,000~9,000 won/meal (Korean student price). English menu is lacking, but photo menu is OK.

Q7. K-drama campus stage?

Yonsei University (“That Year We”, “Cheese in the Trap”), Korea University (“Sky Castle”), Kyung Hee University (“Cheese in the Trap”), Seoul National University (part of “Mr. Sunshine”). A regular in Korean youth and romance K-dramas.

Q8. Walking in campus hanbok?

Sungkyunkwan University, Dongguk University, and Ewha Womans University’s Palace Chapel recommended — Hanok architecture + Hanbok combination #1 on Instagram. Bukchon/Gyeongbokgung Hanbok rental 30 minutes away.
