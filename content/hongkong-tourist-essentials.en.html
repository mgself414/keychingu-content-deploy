⚠ 3 common mistakes Hong Kong tourists make

1. "Korean Michelin = the same as Hong Kong Michelin" — Unaware of the differences between Korean and Korean fine dining: Hong Kong's own Michelin 60+ enthusiasts assume that Korean Michelin is the same. In reality, Korean fine dining (Mingles, Hansik Space, Onjieum, Layeon) = something new that doesn’t exist in Hong Kong. Korean food + fermentation + season + Korean ceramics and culture = Hong Kong mania’s thirst for full experience.

2. "5-star hotels = the same everywhere" — Not aware of the differences between Korean 5-star luxury: Hong Kong Peninsula, Mandarin, and Four Seasons enthusiasts assume that Korean 5-star hotels are the same. In reality, Lotte Signiel (555m Korea #1), Shilla Hotel (Korea 5-star #1 reputation), Four Seasons Gwanghwamun (palace view), and Westin Chosun (Korea's first 5-star 1914) each have a distinctive concept. Korea's 5 stars = 4 pillars: Hanok, palace, city, and luxury.

3. "Hong Kong Cantonese = limited to Korea" — Insufficient recognition of lack of Cantonese-friendly infrastructure: Hong Kong Cantonese enthusiasts expect Cantonese service in Korea → There are very few stores that actually provide Cantonese service. 100% English-friendliness is OK, but Cantonese is limited to some hotels and duty-free shops. Full experience recommended with English menu and service.

① Michelin·Gourmet (4)

1. Mingles Cheongdam, Michelin 3 stars 2024

  • Signature: Korea Michelin 3 stars 2024. The pinnacle of Korean fine dining. Ranked 1st by Hong Kong Michelin Mania.
  • Recommended Menu: Seasonal Tasting (10~12 courses, KRW 350,000~)
  • Tip: Cheongdam-dong. English site for advance reservation 1-3 months in advance. 350,000~500,000 won per person.

2. Korean Food Space Gangnam (Hansik Gonggan, Michelin 1 star)

  • Signature: Michelin 1 star. Korean fine dining fermentation season.
  • Recommended menu: Korean food course (KRW 250,000~)
  • Tip: Gangnam. Advance reservation in English.

3. Onjium Tongui-dong (Onjium, Michelin 1 star)

  • Signature: Michelin 1 star. Traditional Korean food + near Hanbok Line.
  • Recommended menu: Traditional Korean food course (KRW 200,000~)
  • Tip: Tongui-dong (Seochon). Advance reservation.

4. Shilla Hotel La Yeon

  • Signature: Shilla Hotel Korean fine dining. Korean 5-star #1 hotel restaurant.
  • Recommended menu: Korean food course (KRW 250,000~)
  • Tip: Shilla Hotel in Jangchung-dong.

② Luxury·Hotel (3)

5. Four Seasons Hotel Gwanghwamun

  • Signature: 5-star luxury + Palace view (Gyeongbokgung Palace, Changdeokgung Palace). #1 Hong Kong enthusiast.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Some English and Cantonese support, 5-star rooms
  • Recommended: Deluxe room (500,000~) + Charles H bar + Keyring Lounge
  • Tip: 5 minutes walk from Gwanghwamun Station. Palace package first.

6. Lotte Signiel Jamsil

  • Signature: 555m Korea #1 Luxury Hotel (Lotte World Tower, 76th to 101st floors).
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — English, Japanese, and Chinese support, 555m view
  • Recommended: Signiel Room (800,000~) + Sky Bar + SkyDeck 117th and 118th floors
  • Tip: Directly connected to Jamsil Station. Corner room Han River/Olympic view.

7. Shilla Hotel & Duty Free (Jangchung)

  • Signature: Korea 5-star #1 reputation (1979) + duty-free signature.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — 100% English, tax-free English payment
  • Tip: 10 minutes walk from Dongguk University Station. Duty-free full shopping.

③ Shopping·Luxury (3)

8. Olive Young Myeongdong Town

  • Signature: 95% foreigners — Hong Kong K-beauty enthusiasts.
  • Tip: Myeongdong Station Exit 9. Tax free.

9. Lotte Duty Free Myeongdong

  • Signature: Hong Kong luxury brand direct purchase enthusiast.
  • Recommended Category: Luxury brand (Chanel·LV·Dior, etc.) + K-beauty + Whiskey·Perfume
  • Tip: Myeongdong Station. Incheon Airport pickup available.

10. Hyundai Department Store Apgujeong/Gangnam (Hyundai Department Store)

  • Signature: Korean luxury department store signature. Apgujeong·Gangnam cluster.
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ — Some English and Cantonese, text-free
  • Tip: Apgujeong Rodeo Station (Suin Bundang) or Apgujeong Station (Line 3).

④ Gourmet·Tradition·Mania (2)

11. Gwangjang Market Gourmet + Michelin Bib Gourmand (Gwangjang Market Michelin)

  • Signature: Some stores in Gwangjang Market Michelin Bib Gourmand + Drug Gimbap, Twist, Jinokhwa Chicken.
  • Tip: Jongno 5-ga Station. Quiet on weekdays from 14:00 to 17:00.

12. Samcheong·Insadong Hanok Cafes

  • Signature: Hanok cafe cluster in the 1920s. Cafe Onion, Blue Bottle Samcheong, traditional refreshments.
  • Tip: 10 minutes walk from Anguk Station. Hanbok + Hanok Cafe Bundle.
