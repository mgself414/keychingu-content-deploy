⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Made by Hong Kong Tourists

1. "Korean Michelin = Hong Kong Michelin" — Misunderstanding the unique aspects of Korean fine dining: Hong Kong's (홍콩) own 60+ Michelin restaurants lead enthusiasts to assume Korean Michelin is the same. In reality, Korean fine dining (Hansik Fine Dining, 한식 파인 다이닝) at places like Mingles (밍글스), Hansik Gonggan (한식공간), Onjium (온지음), and La Yeon (라연) offers a novelty not found in Hong Kong (홍콩). Korean cuisine (한식) + fermentation + seasonal ingredients + Korean ceramics and culture provide a complete experience that Hong Kong (홍콩) enthusiasts crave.

2. "5-star hotels = all the same" — Misunderstanding the unique aspects of Korean 5-star luxury: Hong Kong (홍콩) Peninsula, Mandarin, and Four Seasons (포시즌스) enthusiasts assume Korean 5-star hotels are identical. In reality, Lotte Signiel (롯데시그니엘) (555m, Korea's #1), Shilla Hotel (신라호텔) (Korea's #1 5-star reputation), Four Seasons Gwanghwamun (포시즌스 광화문) (palace view), and The Westin Chosun (웨스틴조선) (Korea's first 5-star hotel, 1914) each offer distinct concepts. Korean 5-star hotels are characterized by 4 pillars: hanok (한옥, traditional Korean house), palace, city, and luxury.

3. "Hong Kong Cantonese = limited in Korea" — Underestimating the lack of Cantonese-friendly infrastructure: Hong Kong (홍콩) Cantonese speakers expect Cantonese service in Korea, but in reality, very few establishments offer it. 100% English friendliness is fine, but Cantonese is limited to some hotels and duty-free shops. We recommend a full experience with English menus and service.
