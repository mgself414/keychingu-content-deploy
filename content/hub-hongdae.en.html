Why the Hongdae Ipgu Hub — the Nightlife Gateway Where Foreigner Lodging Clusters Most

Hongdae Ipgu (홍대입구) Station is a triple transfer station where Line 2, the Gyeongui-Jungang Line (경의중앙선), and the Airport Railroad (공항철도) meet. It connects directly to Incheon Airport (인천공항) via the Airport Railroad, and it holds the densest cluster of guesthouses and hostels in Seoul, making it a favorite basecamp for foreign travelers. From here you branch out to Sinchon (신촌) and Idae (이대) (Line 2), Yeonnam (연남), Hapjeong (합정), and Sangsu (상수) (Line 2), Seogangdae (서강대) and Yongsan (용산) (Gyeongui-Jungang), and Gimpo Airport (김포공항) (Airport Railroad). By day, a stroll through Yeontral Park (연트럴파크) along the Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길); by night, the Hongdae (홍대) club district — a hub of youth that runs 24 hours.

This guide is a new axis orthogonal to the line-by-line series (#1241–#1249). "Radiating in three directions from the single station of Hongdae Ipgu" — it weaves together what each line delivers, centered on the hub. It is the fourth installment of the Subway Course Series (Phase 2 · Transfer Hubs).

Transfer & Use Tips 5 — Get 200% Out of the Hongdae Ipgu Hub

Tip 1. Hongdae Ipgu Is a 'Foreigner Lodging Gateway'

Direct to Incheon Airport via the Airport Railroad (#1249) + the greatest concentration of guesthouses. After arrival, the ideal basecamp for traveling Seoul.

Tip 2. The Three Directions Have Different Characters

Line 2 = university district · riverside, Gyeongui-Jungang = local · lodging · walks, Airport Railroad = arrival · domestic flights. Pick a direction by purpose.

Tip 3. Walk the Gyeongui Line Forest Park

From Hongdae Ipgu, stroll the abandoned-rail park (#1235) to Yeonnam and Gongdeok. Yeontral Park — walking instead of the subway.

Tip 4. The Night Is the Real Thing

The Hongdae club district, live clubs, and busking come alive at night. Check late-night transit and night buses.

Tip 5. One Transit Card for Everything

A single T-money card covers Line 2, Gyeongui-Jungang, and the Airport Railroad — all of them. The Airport Railroad commuter train also takes T-money.
