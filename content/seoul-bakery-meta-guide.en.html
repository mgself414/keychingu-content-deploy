Seoul Bakery Meta Guide for Foreigners

5 areas × 5 types — French, sourdough, Korean bread, dessert meta full set

Seoul bakeries are an area that surprises foreigners. Starting from Lee Seongdang in Gunsan in 1945 (Korea's oldest, see #1108), to the SPC Paris Baguette global brand, Korean bakeries have 80 years of history and are at a world-class level. Although it became known to foreigners later than specialty coffee, in the past five years, Korean sourdough, hard bread, and Korean signature bread (salt bread, bean butter, and injeolmi toast) have appeared on foreigners' SNS. This guide is a 5-region × 5-type matrix and a full set for foreign segment/foodie personas.

1. Korean bakery scene (foreigner fit fact)

80 years of history

  • Lee Seongdang Gunsan (1945~) — Korea’s oldest bakery·Refer to #1108
  • Taegeukdang (1946~) — Seoul Jangchung·Nopo
  • Napoleon Confectionery (1968~) — Apgujeong, Seoul

Global brand

  • SPC Paris Baguette — 6,000 stores worldwide
  • Tous Les Jours — CJ·Global
  • Dunkin Donuts — American brand popular in Korea

specialty bakery river

  • Many sourdough, hard bread, and French bakeries
  • No. 1 Instagram/MZ trend

Korean signature bread (foreigners find it fascinating)

  • Salt bread, bean butter, injeolmi toast, cheese bread, red bean bread
  • Cake (whipped cream cake = Korean unique lightness)

6. Foreign fit tips

English language availability

  • Top: Maison de la Company, Signiel, SPC Paris Baguette (partial English menu)
  • Slander: Le Baker, Bakersfield, Fawcett, Napoleon (picture menu)
  • Local: Taegeukdang·Iseongdang (mainly in Korean, photos + fingers)

Price range

  • 1 bread: ₩3,000~8,000
  • Specialty sourdough: ₩5,000~12,000
  • Long-established red bean bread: ₩2,500~5,000
  • Hotel Luxury: ₩8,000~20,000

Korean bread etiquette (foreigner fit)

  • Free choice of store tray + tongs
  • Pay in one go at checkout
  • In-store tasting OK (most)
  • Photos OK (mostly)
  • Gluten label (specialty stores only)

By persona

  • European foodie: Pattern A (Hanok + French)
  • specialty enthusiast: Pattern B (sourdough)
  • history buff: Pattern C (Nopo·Iseongdang)
  • family fit: SPC Paris Baguette stores (many menus displayed in English)

By season

  • Spring: Strawberry season (Korean bakery unique strawberry dessert)
  • Summer: Shaved ice + bread dessert cafe
  • Autumn: Chestnut and sweet potato seasonal bread
  • Winter: Hoppang/warm bread + coffee
