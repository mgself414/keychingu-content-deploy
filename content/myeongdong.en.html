5 Mistakes Tourists Often Make in Myeongdong (명동)

1. Not Using the Street Money Changers in Myeongdong (명동)

Myeongdong (명동) is lined with street exchange booths that beat airport rates. You'll get 2–3% better rates than Incheon Airport or banks — that's ₩2,000–3,000 saved on every ₩100,000 exchanged. If it's your first visit, exchange cash in Myeongdong (명동) before you start shopping.

2. Forgetting to Collect Tax Refund Receipts (₩30,000+)

Korea's foreigner tax refund gives you 9–10% back on any single receipt of ₩30,000 or more with a foreign passport. Keep your cosmetics and duty-free receipts in an envelope — you can get an instant refund at in-store booths (inside Innisfree (이니스프리), Aritaum (아리따움) and others) or process everything at the airport refund counter before departure.

3. Confusing Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) with Ordinary Dumpling Shops

Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) — a 60-year institution — is the top address for authentic Korean-style handmade dumplings and kalguksu (칼국수, knife-cut noodle soup). Prices look similar to other dumpling places nearby, but the quality gap is enormous. If you're here for dumplings, go to Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) main branch.

4. Eating Only Street Food

Street food in Myeongdong (명동) (₩3,000–5,000) is great value, but skipping the real restaurants — Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자), Hadongkwan (하동관), Jinjuhoegwan (진주회관) — means missing half of what Myeongdong (명동) has to offer. For lunch, go to a classic old-school spot.

5. Skipping Photos at Myeongdong Cathedral (명동대성당) and Myeongdong-gil (명동길)

Myeongdong Cathedral (명동대성당) (Korea's first Gothic-style cathedral, built 1898) + Myeongdong-gil (명동길) (the main shopping street) = Instagram gold. If you only shop and leave, you've experienced half of Myeongdong (명동).

★ K-Beauty Big 3 — Olive Young (올리브영) vs Innisfree (이니스프리) vs ETUDE (에뛰드)

These are the three cosmetics stores tourists encounter most often in Myeongdong (명동). Even spending the same ₩20,000, the experience differs hugely — multi-brand shop vs. single-brand boutique, sheet mask-focused vs. makeup-focused.

1️⃣ Olive Young (올리브영) Myeongdong Central Branch — Korea's No. 1 Health & Beauty Multi-Brand Shop

Location: Heart of Myeongdong-gil (명동길) — 5 branches in Myeongdong (명동) including Myeongdong Central (명동중앙점) and Myeongdong Town (명동타운점)

Key Products:

  • Sheet masks (Mediheal (메디힐) · Innisfree (이니스프리) · Selfusion C (셀퓨전C)): ₩1,000–3,000 per sheet
  • Korea-exclusive brands (Rom&nd (롬앤) · Dear Dahlia (디어달리아) · Laneige (라네즈)): ₩15,000–30,000
  • Global brands (CeraVe · The Ordinary): ₩10,000–25,000
  • Cotton pads, cotton buds & daily essentials: ₩2,000–5,000

Why Go:

  • Multi-brand shop = all K-Beauty in one place — try 10 different products for ₩50,000
  • Instant tax refund available on Korean brands (₩30,000+)
  • English, Chinese & Japanese-speaking staff always on hand
  • Membership sign-up (foreign phone numbers accepted) → 5–10% discount

One-liner: "K-Beauty gateway #1 — try everything under one roof"

Money Exchange & Tax Refund — Full Guide

Money Exchange (환전)

  • Myeongdong (명동) street exchange booths = 2–3% better rates than Incheon Airport or banks
  • Found throughout Myeongdong-gil (명동길) — look for signs reading "환전 / Exchange"
  • ₩2,000–3,000 difference per ₩100,000 exchanged
  • Operating hours: 10:00–22:00
  • Recommended: exchange cash here rather than using a credit card for currency

Tax Refund (세금환급)

  • Single receipt ₩30,000 or more + foreign passport = 9–10% refund
  • Instant refund booths (inside stores like Olive Young (올리브영), Innisfree (이니스프리) etc.) — deducted from your total at the register
  • Airport refund counter — located inside Incheon Airport departure gates
  • Required: receipts + passport + the same payment card

FAQ

Q1. Does English work here?

Everywhere. Myeongdong (명동) stores are the most experienced with foreign visitors in all of Korea.

Q2. Where's the best place to exchange money?

Myeongdong (명동) street exchange booths — best rates, no contest. Banks are not recommended.

Q3. Any vegan options?

Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) and Hadongkwan (하동관) are both meat-based. For vegan options, see Topic #69.

Q4. Traveling with kids?

Hadongkwan (하동관) gomtang (곰탕) + department store food courts are kid-friendly. Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) also has suitable options for children.

Q5. Only have one hour?

Olive Young (올리브영) 30 min + Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) 30 min = the one-hour express course.

Q6. Open on Sundays?

Most places are open. Department stores may be closed on the third Sunday of each month.
