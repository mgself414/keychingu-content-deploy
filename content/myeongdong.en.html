5 Mistakes Foreigners Make in Myeongdong (명동)

1. Not exchanging money at Myeongdong street exchange booths

Myeongdong (명동) has street exchange booths with better rates than the airport — 2–3% better than Incheon Airport (인천공항) or banks. That's ₩2,000–3,000 more per ₩100,000 exchanged. Exchange here first, then start exploring.

2. Forgetting to collect tax refund receipts (₩30,000+)

Korea's foreigner tax refund: single receipt ₩30,000+ + foreign passport = 9–10% back. Keep cosmetic and department store receipts in one envelope → redeem at in-store instant refund booths (Olive Young (올리브영) / Innisfree (이니스프리)) or at the Incheon Airport (인천공항) refund counter.

3. Mixing up Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) with ordinary dumpling shops

Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) (60-year institution) is Korea's definitive handmade dumpling & knife noodle restaurant. The price looks similar to regular shops, but the quality gap is huge. For dumplings: Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) main branch, no exceptions.

4. Only eating street food and missing the sit-down classics

Street food (₩3,000–5,000) is great value, but skipping Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) · Hadongwan (하동관) · Jinjuhoeegwan (진주회관) means you've missed half of what Myeongdong (명동) offers. Lunch at a legacy restaurant is the right call.

5. Not photographing Myeongdong Cathedral (명동성당) and Myeongdong-gil (명동길)

Myeongdong Cathedral (명동성당) (1898, Korea's first Gothic-style cathedral) + Myeongdong-gil (명동길) (main shopping street) = Instagram top picks. Leave without photos and you've only experienced half of Myeongdong (명동).

★ K-Beauty Big 3 — Olive Young vs Innisfree vs ETUDE

The three cosmetic stores foreigners encounter most in Myeongdong (명동). Even with the same ₩20,000 budget, the difference between multi-brand shop vs. single brand, sheet masks vs. makeup-focused matters.

1️⃣ Olive Young (올리브영) Myeongdong Central — Korea's No.1 H&B Multi-brand Shop

Location: Center of Myeongdong-gil (명동길) — 5 branches in Myeongdong (명동)

Key Items:

  • Sheet masks (Mediheal (메디힐) · Innisfree (이니스프리) · Cell Fusion C): ₩1,000–3,000/sheet
  • Korea-exclusive brands (Rom&nd (롬앤) · Dear Dahlia (디어달리아) · Laneige (라네즈)): ₩15,000–30,000
  • Global brands (CeraVe · The Ordinary): ₩10,000–25,000
  • Cotton pads, Q-tips, daily essentials: ₩2,000–5,000

Key Facts:

  • Multi-brand = try all K-Beauty in one place — 10 different products for ₩50,000
  • Instant tax refund available (receipts ₩30,000+)
  • English / Chinese / Japanese-speaking staff on site
  • Membership signup (foreign phone numbers OK) → 5–10% discount

One line: "K-Beauty first stop — everything in one place"

🍢 Myeongdong Street Food — Evening Stall Complete Guide

Money Exchange & Tax Refund Full Guide

Money Exchange

  • Myeongdong (명동) street exchange booths = best rates in Korea — 2–3% better than Incheon Airport (인천공항) or banks
  • Multiple booths along Myeongdong-gil (명동길) (signs: "환전 / Exchange")
  • ₩2,000–3,000 more per ₩100,000 exchanged
  • Hours: 10:00–22:00
  • Recommend cash exchange over credit card for street food

Tax Refund

  • Single receipt ₩30,000+ + foreign passport = 9–10% refund
  • Instant refund booths (inside Olive Young (올리브영), Innisfree (이니스프리) etc.) — deducted at checkout
  • Airport refund counter — inside Incheon Airport (인천공항) departure gate
  • Receipts + passport + same card required

FAQ

Q1. Is English spoken?

100%. Myeongdong (명동) stores are Korea's most experienced at serving foreign visitors.

Q2. Where to exchange money?

Myeongdong (명동) street exchange booths = best rates. Banks not recommended.

Q3. Vegan options?

Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) and Hadongwan (하동관) are both meat-based. For vegan, see #69.

Q4. Traveling with children?

Hadongwan (하동관) gomtang (곰탕) + department store food courts OK. Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) also has kids-friendly menu.

Q5. Only 1 hour?

Olive Young (올리브영) 30 min + Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) 30 min = 1-hour express course.

Q6. Open on Sundays?

Most shops open. Department stores may close on the third Sunday of the month.

Q7. Best street food in Myeongdong (명동)? (What to eat at Myeongdong street food?)

Chicken skewer (닭꼬치) + Hotteok (호떡) + Cream Shrimp (크림새우) — foreigners' top 3. Stalls fully open after 17:00 — bring ₩20,000–30,000 cash.

Q8. What is Myeongdong (명동) famous for?

K-Beauty (Olive Young (올리브영) · Innisfree (이니스프리) · ETUDE (에뛰드)) + street food evening stalls + Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) 60-year institution + money exchange & duty-free — one-stop shopping, food, and experience for visitors.

Q9. How long to spend in Myeongdong (명동)?

Minimum 2 hours (Olive Young (올리브영) + street food). Half day (4–5 hours) recommended — K-Beauty Big 3 + sit-down lunch + evening street food stalls + Cathedral (명동성당).
