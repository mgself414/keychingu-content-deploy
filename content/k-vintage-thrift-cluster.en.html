9 Seoul Vintage and Secondhand Shops — From Thousand-Won Clothing Piles to a Hannam-dong Showroom

Subtitle: The Dongmyo flea market and the Seoul Pungmul Market in Sinseol-dong, two charity thrift shops that sell donated goods, three vintage shops in Hongdae, Sangsu, and Mangwon, and two in Itaewon and Hannam. Only 9 places that Kakao Map shows as open and that got visit reviews in the second half of 2026 made the cut, split into 3 zones. Each zone is built as a half-day course.

ISSUE 06 · #772 · 2026-11-24 · Seoul Vintage Shopping

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 9

This list is for travelers who like digging through old clothes and old things. Secondhand shopping in Seoul comes in three kinds. At the markets near Dongdaemun (동대문) you dig through clothing piles and street stalls. At charity thrift shops such as Beautiful Store (아름다운가게) and Goodwill Store (굿윌스토어) you buy donated goods cheaply. At vintage shops in Hongdae (홍대), Mangwon (망원), and Hannam (한남) you buy clothes the owner picked and cleaned. This piece covers all three.

There are two selection rules. The Kakao Map (카카오맵) place info has to say "open for business," and a blog review or a Kakao Map review or photo has to have been posted in the second half of 2026. Shops whose recent activity stopped in 2024–2025 and shops with controversial reviews were left out.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours and closed days are the values registered on Kakao Map (checked 2026-11-24). Clothing prices are not registered on Kakao Map, so we copied the actual purchase prices from blog reviews and Kakao Map reviews, with dates. They are what one person paid, so the same item can cost something else on another day. Nearby stations and exits are from Kakao Map directions. We could not confirm whether cards are accepted, so bring cash for the street stalls and small shops.

① Dongdaemun·Jongno — 3 Venues

The two markets are close to Dongmyo-ap Station and Sinseol-dong Station, so they pair well in one day. Beautiful Store Anguk is at the entrance to Bukchon (북촌), so it slots easily into an Insadong·Bukchon day.

  • Dongmyo Flea Market (동묘벼룩시장) — a street of stalls and vintage shops in the alley in front of Exit 3 of Dongmyo-ap Station (동묘앞역). Some spots have you digging through clothing piled on the ground, and some shops hang clothes on racks. Prices come from a review and are values from a visit in late 2026-10. A 2026-11-17 blog review wrote that pile T-shirts were 1,000–5,000 won and hanger clothes were around 10,000 won. The author added that prices vary a lot from shop to shop. Kakao Map registers it as daily 10:00–18:00. The same review wrote that there are no official hours and that they differ by shop and stall. The address is the area around 102-8 Sungin-dong (숭인동 102-8), Jongno-gu (종로구). The nearest station is Dongmyo-ap Station (Lines 1·6) Exit 3, a 1-minute walk. This review wrote that on a Saturday afternoon in late October the crowd was bigger than imagined, and that with few parking spaces it is a hassle to go by car.
  • Seoul Pungmul Market (서울풍물시장) — an indoor flea market in Sinseol-dong (신설동). The building is split into color-named halls (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, purple) and Cheongchun 1-beon-ga (청춘1번가) (official website, 2026-11-24). It sells antiques, household goods, bowls, and vintage clothes. The market's official blog introduced the vintage clothes corner separately in a 2026-11-16 post. A 2026-10-11 blog review says most bowls are 1,000 won and a glass cup is about 3,000 won. The cup price is an estimate. Kakao Map hours are Wed–Mon 10:00–19:00, and it is closed on Tuesday. The official blog (2026-11-02) also announces it is closed every Tuesday. The address is Cheonho-daero 4-gil 21 (천호대로4길 21), Dongdaemun-gu (동대문구). The nearest station is Sinseol-dong Station (Lines 1·2 and the Ui-Sinseol Line) Exit 9, a 4-minute walk. It is indoors, so it is a good pick for a rainy day.
  • Beautiful Store Anguk (아름다운가게 안국점) — a charity thrift shop that sells donated clothes, bags, books, and bowls. A 2026-06-02 review lists a plain white tee at 6,000 won and bags at 15,000–20,000 won. A 2026-09-25 review lists a knit at 6,000 won, books at 1,000–3,000 won, and 5 notebooks for 1,000 won. Not everything is cheap. A 2026-09-30 Kakao Map review complained that a used sweater was priced at 100,000 won. Hours are Mon–Fri 10:30–20:00 and Sat·Sun·public holidays 10:30–18:00. It closes for the three-day Lunar New Year (Seollal) and Chuseok holidays and on Labor Day. The address is Bukchon-ro 21 (북촌로 21), 1st floor, Jongno-gu (종로구). The nearest station is Anguk Station (Line 3) Exit 2, a 5-minute walk.

② Hongdae·Sangsu·Mangwon — 4 Venues

One large store at Hongik Univ. Station, one 2-floor shop in front of Sangsu Station, and two near Mangwon Station. Root Hongdae stays open until 24:00, so it suits an evening. Terrace Wear is closed on Monday and Goodwill Store on Sunday.

  • Root Hongdae (루트 홍대) — a large vintage clothing store in the basement in front of Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역). A 2026-10-20 blog review described the store as 250 pyeong. The same review mentions a 3+1 promotion, a field jacket at 19,000 won, and jackets in the 10,000–150,000 won range. Promotions change from time to time. It is open daily 10:00–24:00. The address is Yanghwa-ro 140 (양화로 140), Basement 1, Mapo-gu (마포구). The nearest station is Hongik Univ. Station (Line 2, the Airport Railroad, and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line) Exit 9, a 3-minute walk. A 2026-11-15 blog post was titled "Root Hongdae, where many foreigners go." Menswear and womenswear hang mixed together, so check the label and size yourself.
  • Vintage Shop Page One (빈티지샵 페이지원) — a vintage shop that uses floors 1 and 2 right in front of Sangsu Station (상수역). Besides clothes it sells accessories, hats, small goods, and bowls. A 2026-11-04 blog review mentions a corner selling blouses at 3,000 won each. Hours differ by day of the week. They are Tue–Thu 12:30–20:50, Fri–Sun 13:00–20:50, and Monday 13:00–19:55. The blog also notes that hours change often. The address is Dongmak-ro 75 (독막로 75), floors 1–2, Mapo-gu (마포구). The nearest station is Sangsu Station (Line 6) Exit 1, a 2-minute walk.
  • Terrace Wear (테라스웨어) — a vintage shop at the start of the alley that leads to Mangwon Market (망원시장). A 2026-10-28 blog review named the French work jacket as its signature item. Another review (2026-06-04) says there were many Levi's jeans. Prices aren't always low. A 2026-08-21 Kakao Map review wrote that there were clothes over 100,000 won. Hours are Tue–Sun 12:30–20:00, and it is closed on Monday. The address is Worldcup-ro 19-gil 25 (월드컵로19길 25), 1st floor, Mapo-gu (마포구). The nearest station is Mangwon Station (Line 6) Exit 2, a 4-minute walk.
  • Goodwill Store Mapo (굿윌스토어 마포점) — a charity thrift shop that sells donated clothes, books, and household goods. A 2026-07-12 blog review lists a book at 2,000 won. A 2026-05 Kakao Map review also said it found plenty of books. Many reviews say it is stronger on books and sundries than on clothes. Hours are Mon–Sat 10:00–19:00, and it is closed on Sunday. The address is Worldcup-ro 94 (월드컵로 94), Myeong Building (명빌딩) 1st floor, Mapo-gu (마포구). The nearest station is Mangwon Station (Line 6) Exit 1, a 3-minute walk.

③ Itaewon·Hannam — 2 Venues

Both are small stores that hang only clothes the owner picked. Plastic Banana opens at 13:00 and is closed on Monday.

  • Plastic Banana (플라스틱바나나) — a vintage shop in the alley between Noksapyeong Station (녹사평역) and Itaewon. A 2026-11-02 Kakao Map review (in English) named leather, denim, and wool men's jackets, thick wool and flannel shirts, and US-size jeans as strengths. No price is confirmed from Kakao Map or recent reviews (2026-11-24). Hours are Tue–Sun 13:00–21:00, and it is closed on Monday. The address is Noksapyeong-daero 40-gil 41 (녹사평대로40길 41), 1st floor, Yongsan-gu (용산구). The nearest station is Noksapyeong Station (Line 6) Exit 3, a 6-minute walk.
  • Vintage Shop Of Heart (빈티지샵 오브하트) — a showroom on floors 2 and 3 on Itaewon-ro in Hannam-dong (한남동). A 2026-11-03 blog review mentions tweed jackets, luxury ties, vintage watches, and wool outerwear. A 2025-03 review wrote that all the clothes are dry-cleaned before hanging, so there is no smell. A 2025-08-27 review wrote that tweed is on the pricey side, but flawed-fabric pieces can be had for 20,000–30,000 won. It is open daily 10:00–20:00. The address is Itaewon-ro 212 (이태원로 212), floors 2–3, Yongsan-gu (용산구). The nearest station is Itaewon Station (Line 6) Exit 3, a 6-minute walk.

🗺 Route — Two Half-Day Courses

The zones are in different neighborhoods, so plan only one or two zones in a day. Check the walk between shops with Kakao Map directions that day.

A. Dongmyo·Pungmul Market·Anguk (except Tuesday)

  1. Morning Dongmyo Flea Market (Kakao Map 10:00 on)
  2. Before lunch Seoul Pungmul Market in Sinseol-dong. It is one stop from Dongmyo-ap Station on Line 1
  3. Afternoon Beautiful Store Anguk at Anguk Station (until 20:00 Mon–Fri, until 18:00 Sat·Sun). Pair it with sightseeing in Bukchon·Insadong

The Pungmul Market is closed on Tuesday. On a Tuesday, skip stop 2 and go from Dongmyo straight to Anguk.

B. Sangsu·Hongdae·Mangwon (Tue–Sat recommended)

  1. Daytime Page One at Sangsu Station (from 12:30 Tue–Thu, from 13:00 Fri–Sun)
  2. Afternoon Terrace Wear near Mangwon Station (closed Mon) and Goodwill Store Mapo (closed Sun)
  3. Evening Root Hongdae at Hongik Univ. Station (until 24:00)

Terrace Wear is closed on Monday and Goodwill Store on Sunday. To see both, go Tue–Sat. Set aside a separate half day for the two Itaewon·Hannam shops, linked late in the afternoon. Plastic Banana opens at 13:00.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to the Pungmul Market on Tuesday, or Mangwon·Itaewon on Monday — Seoul Pungmul Market is closed on Tuesday, Terrace Wear and Plastic Banana on Monday, and Goodwill Store Mapo on Sunday. Match the day of the week first.
  2. Looking for a price tag and leaving empty-handed — one review says prices at Dongmyo differ by shop and stall. Pick up the item you chose and ask the price.
  3. Buying without trying it on — vintage is one piece per item, and overseas size labels are mixed in. Before you pay, ask whether fitting is possible and whether exchanges or refunds are accepted.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. If I only go to one place? — To dig through a lot cheaply, Dongmyo Flea Market. For a rainy day, or if you also want bowls and antiques, Seoul Pungmul Market (closed Tue). To try on chosen clothes in comfort, Root Hongdae (open daily until 24:00).
  2. Which places can I visit in the evening? — By registered hours, Root Hongdae is open until 24:00, Plastic Banana until 21:00, Page One until 20:50 (19:55 Mon), and Beautiful Store Anguk until 20:00 on weekdays.
  3. What can be a souvenir other than clothes? — Bowls and cups at Seoul Pungmul Market (1,000–3,000 won in reviews), books at Beautiful Store and Goodwill Store, and small goods and bowls at Page One. For new crafts, see the traditional souvenirs piece #774.
