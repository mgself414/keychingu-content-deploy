It cross-references 10+ KeyChinGu (키친구) family-friendly content pieces (Hangang (한강) River, jjimjilbang (찜질방), museums, hanbok (한복), Gwangjang Market (광장시장), amusement parks, etc.) to create a comprehensive funnel.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes for Foreign Families

1. "Kids stay in the hotel kids' room" — No outings

This is the most common mistake for foreign families visiting Korea for the first time. Stress from traveling with children leads to staying only at the hotel. In reality, Korea has excellent family-friendly infrastructure — museums, science centers, Hangang (한강) Parks, jjimjilbang (찜질방), and amusement parks all offer safe, family-friendly itineraries.

2. Perception that "Gwangjang Market (광장시장) is for adults"

Foreigners often perceive Gwangjang Market (광장시장) as a place for adult drinking. In reality, Bindaetteok (빈대떡), Mayak Gimbap (마약김밥), and traditional desserts are kid-friendly, and the market atmosphere itself is a signature experience for children. Gwangjang Market (광장시장) is highly recommended as a top choice for families with children aged 5-10.

3. Unawareness of kids' hanbok (한복) sizes

Foreign families often only rent hanbok (한복) for adults. In reality, hanbok (한복) rental shops near Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) and Changdeokgung (창덕궁) offer kids' sizes — perfect for family photos. Request kids' sizes when making a reservation at a hanbok (한복) shop.

4. Unawareness of jjimjilbang (찜질방) etiquette for families

Foreign families visiting a jjimjilbang (찜질방) for the first time might not know which places are suitable for children. In reality, Dragon Hill Spa (드래곤힐 스파), Sijonespa (시조네스파), and Spa Lei (스파레이) are family-friendly (offering kids' admission fees, kids' rooms, and kids' menus). Learn about [#34 Jjimjilbang (찜질방) Etiquette](34_jjimjilbang_etiquette.md) beforehand.

5. Perception that K-pop fan itineraries are not suitable for families

Foreign K-pop fan families might think it's difficult to include children in K-pop itineraries. In reality, SMTOWN Coex (SM타운 코엑스) is family-friendly + Trend Pot (트렌드팟) K-pop collaboration stores are suitable for children. The 14:00 course (SMTOWN) in [#207 K-pop Fan Itinerary](207_kpop_fan_one_day_itinerary.md) is family-appropriate.

Family Travel Etiquette · Precautions

Essential Etiquette:

1. Museums · Art Galleries: Maintain quiet + photo etiquette.

2. Palace Hanbok (한복) Experience: Free admission when wearing hanbok (한복), time etiquette.

3. Jjimjilbang (찜질방): Kids' etiquette + check kids' size clothes/items.

4. Amusement Parks: Check height requirements and age matching beforehand.

Precautions:

  • Advance reservation recommended for hanbok (한복) experience (ensures kids' sizes).
  • Many museums are closed on Mondays — recommended Sunday-Thursday.
  • Kids aged 6 and above recommended for jjimjilbang (찜질방).
  • Amusement parks have weekend wait times of 60min+ — recommended weekdays.
