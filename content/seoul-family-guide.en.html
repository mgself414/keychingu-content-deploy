⚠ Five major mistakes when traveling with foreign family members

1. "Children stay in the hotel kids room" — do not go out

The most common mistakes made by foreign families on their first visit to Korea. Stayed only in hotels due to stress with children. In reality, Korea's family-friendly infrastructure is very well-equipped — museums, science museums, Hangang Park, saunas, and amusement parks are all safe for family fans.

2. Recognition that “Gwangjang Market is for adults”

Foreigners perceive Gwangjang Market as an adult drinking place. In fact, Bindaetteok, drug gimbap, and traditional desserts are child-friendly + The market atmosphere itself is a signature child experience. Gwangjang Market #1 recommended for children aged 5-10.

3. Hanbok Experience: Child size awareness

The Hanbok experience for foreign families is for adults only. In fact, Gyeongbokgung Palace and Changdeokgung Hanbok stores have children’s sizes — Hot cuts of family photos. Request child size when making a reservation at a hanbok store.

4. Ignorant of manners with family in jjimjilbang

First visit to a jjimjilbang for a foreign family - Not aware of the division between stores that allow children and those that do not. In fact, Dragon Hill Spa, Sijo Nespa, and Spa Ray are family-friendly (child admission fee, kids room, kids menu). #34 Pre-study on sauna etiquette.

5. Recognition that K-pop fans cannot bring foreign family members

When a foreign family is a K-pop fan, it is perceived as difficult to travel to K-pop with children. Actually, SM Town COEX (family friendly) + Trendpot K-pop collaboration store Children are allowed. #207 K-pop fan 14:00 course (SM Town) suitable for families.

  • National Hangeul Museum (Yongsan) — Hangeul experience, foreign family signature
  • National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Seoul (#152 Dessert — Terrarosa Café combined) — Art Museum + Café Family Course
  • Sangsang Madang Design Square (#202) — Design Gallery + Family Friendly

In the end, Seoul with foreign families

Korean culture, tradition, and MZ trends are all family-friendly — Museums, palace hanbok, Gwangjang Market, Han River, sauna, amusement park, K-pop, shopping, all 8 categories are family-friendly. For a foreign family's first time in Korea, 5-day course (Hanbok, palace + Han River + amusement park or museum + Myeong-dong, sauna) is the baseline.

Matching by child age:

  • 3-5 years old: Hanbok, amusement park, zoo (Seoul Grand Park)
  • 5-8 years old: Han River, museum, Gwangjang Market, Korean food for children
  • 8+: K-pop, science museum, bicycle, shopping
  • 10-15 years: All activities + family dinner
