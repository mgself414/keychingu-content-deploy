Locals' Picks ⑦ Jeonnam, Gwangju & Jeju — 10 Eateries Korean Viewers Named, From the Station Alley to Moseulpo

Subtitle: An axis the Gwangju one-day guide (#1026), Mokpo Yudalsan (#1050), and the Jeju locals' picks piece (#1348) don't cover — picking only the 10 Jeonnam, Gwangju, and Jeju spots out of a nationwide roundup of eateries Korean viewers recommended in YouTube comments, verified for existence, address, and business type on Naver local search, with no evaluation attached. Gwangju/Mokpo/Naju 5 → Boseong/Haenam 2 → Jeju 3. Naju and Haenam are both first appearances on KeyChinGu, and this region's administrative name changed in July 2026.

ISSUE 06 · #1388 · 2026-09-18 · Locals' Picks ⑦

🎯 Why "Comment Recommendations" Is the Bar

This corner has one source. A Korean YouTube channel posted a "nationwide eateries" video, viewers wrote in their own neighborhood spots in the comments, and the most systematically organized comments were compiled by region into a table of 217 places. It's a list Koreans recommended to Koreans, so foreign reviews are scarce and many are small-town restaurants that never make tourist lists. This corner's criteria and limits are laid out in Part ① (#1382).

KeyChinGu checked only whether the name, address, and business type actually exist on Naver local search (2026-09-17). Of 11 Jeonnam, Gwangju, and Jeju spots, 10 were confirmed; the 1 that wasn't (Gochang) was dropped. There are no visit reviews, rankings, or star ratings, and no hours or prices — check Naver Place on the day you go.

There's one reason the addresses in this piece may look unfamiliar. On July 1, 2026, Gwangju Metropolitan City and Jeollanam-do merged into a single Jeonnam-Gwangju Consolidated Special City (전남광주통합특별시) — short form Gwangju Special City (광주특별시). Base-level names like Mokpo-si, Naju-si, Boseong-gun, and Haenam-gun stayed the same, so it's normal for a map app to show something like "Jeonnam-Gwangju Consolidated Special City, Mokpo-si…" There's nothing travelers actually need to change — only the notation is new.

① Gwangju, Mokpo & Naju — 5 Spots in Cities Along the Honam Line

The Honam Line (호남선) splits off at Iksan and runs down through Gwangju Songjeong, Naju, and Mokpo (the Iksan story is in #1385). These five spots sit along that line, and one of them is among the rare cases you can walk to straight from the station.

  • Yeongmyeong Gukbap (영명국밥) · 5 Songjeong-ro 8-gil, Gwangsan-gu (광산구 송정로8번길 5) · Korean · haejangguk · about an 8-min walk from Gwangju Songjeong Station

The alley in front of Gwangju Songjeong Station is known for a cluster of tteokgalbi (떡갈비) restaurants packed into about 200m. This gukbap place sits in that same alley, making it the one spot you can walk to straight off the KTX with your bags. The haejangguk is available as a single order.

  • Wonjo Garakji (원조가락지) · 8-2 Yeongsan-ro 258-gil, Mokpo-si (목포시 영산로258번길 8-2) · Restaurant · Korean · Mokpo old downtown — Mokpo Station area, walk time to check
  • Hanseong Heuk Dwaeji (한성흙돼지) · 8 Hadangnambu-ro 61-gil, Mokpo-si (목포시 하당남부로61번길 8) · Korean · hanjeongsik · Hadang new town — some distance from Mokpo Station. Since September 1, 2026, a new express bus has connected Mokpo Station directly with Hadang, Namak, and Oryong

Mokpo is split between the old downtown (around the station and Yudalsan) and the Hadang/Namak new town. The two sit in separate living zones, so covering both in one day means extra travel.

  • Noanjip (노안집) · 1-3 Geumseonggwan-gil, Naju-si (나주시 금성관길 1-3) · Korean · gomtang & seolleongtang · Naju Eupseong Geumseonggwan (나주읍성 금성관) front street — bus/taxi from Naju Station
  • Nampyeong Halmaejip (남평할매집) · 2 Geumseonggwan-gil, Naju-si (나주시 금성관길 2) · Restaurant · Korean · across the street

Naju is a city known for gomtang, and the two restaurants face each other on the same Geumseonggwan-gil. That said, the Geumseonggwan building itself is under demolition-and-repair construction as of September 2026, with reopening targeted for 2027, so don't go expecting to tour it. The restaurant street itself is unaffected.

Tip for foreign visitors — Gomtang and haejangguk can be ordered solo, while hanjeongsik is typically for 2 or more. For gomtang, the local style is to season it yourself with salt and scallions, and pour in a little kkakdugi (깍두기) brine.

② Boseong & Haenam — 2 Spots in the South Where Rail Recently Changed

These two counties saw rail conditions change in 2025. With the opening of the Mokpo–Boseong Line (목포보성선), Haenam got a station for the first time, and Boseong ended up split between its existing station and a new one. On the other hand, electrification of the Gyeongjeon Line (경전선) between Gwangju Songjeong and Suncheon is still under construction as of 2026 (opening targeted for 2030), so it isn't a route travelers can use yet.

  • Miryeok Yangtang (미력양탕) · 2 Yejae-ro, Miryeok-myeon, Boseong-gun (보성군 미력면 예재로 2) · Restaurant · Korean · mind the station choice — Boseong has both the existing Boseong Station (보성역) and Sinboseong Station (신보성역), which opened separately with the Mokpo–Boseong Line in August 2025. Which one is closer to Miryeok-myeon is not confirmed, so check your route against the destination before booking

Boseong is the county of the green tea fields (#426, #676). Miryeok-myeon is a township seat outside that tourist route.

  • Kkotdam (꽃담) · 1F, 160-12 Haenam-ro, Haenam-eup, Haenam-gun (해남군 해남읍 해남로 160-12 1층) · Korean · hanjeongsik · Haenam Station opened new on September 27, 2025, but it's on the outskirts, about 8km from downtown, so a bus transfer is required. Intercity buses run 8 times a day from Mokpo, 24 times a day from Gwangju

Haenam is a county known for Ttangkkeut (땅끝), or "Land's End," also called Tomal (토말). Hanjeongsik in this southern region tends to come with an especially wide spread, so planning for 2 or more is the right call.

Tip for foreign visitors — Namdo-style hanjeongsik comes with so many side dishes that solo orders are usually difficult. If you're alone, the gomtang/haejangguk options (Group ①) are more realistic.

③ Jeju — 3 Spots in Three Directions Reachable by Airport Bus

Most of Jeju is reachable by bus from the airport, even without a rental car. The three spots on this list each lie in a different direction — downtown, southeast, and southwest.

  • Imje (임제) · 2F, 126 Tapdong-ro, Jeju-si (제주시 탑동로 126 2층) · Korean · grilled pork · about 20 min from Jeju Airport (제주공항) to Tapdong by airport bus or Route 316

Tapdong is a Jeju-si neighborhood with a seaside promenade. Jeju-style grilled pork is known for using heuk dwaeji (흑돼지), or black pork, and orders usually start at 2 servings.

  • Tamna Ganjang Gejang (탐라간장게장) · 10-4 Pyoseondangpo-ro, Pyoseon-myeon, Seogwipo-si (서귀포시 표선면 표선당포로 10-4) · Restaurant · Korean · From Jeju Airport, Route 121 runs to Pyoseon (via Seongeup). Travel time to check

Ganjang gejang (간장게장) is raw crab marinated and fermented in soy sauce, eaten with rice. Pyoseon is a southeastern township with a beach and a folk village.

  • Miyeongine (미영이네) · 42 Hamohanggu-ro, Daejeong-eup, Seogwipo-si (서귀포시 대정읍 하모항구로 42) · Korean · raw fish (hoe) · From Jeju Airport, Express Bus 151 runs toward Daejeong/Moseulpo (listed terminus: Unjin Port)

Moseulpo is a port town in southwest Jeju. Naver's classification is raw fish (hoe).

Tip for foreign visitors — Jeju buses split into express (blue-toned route numbers) and trunk lines, and a transit card carries transfers between them. Hoe, ganjang gejang, and grilled meat are all menus that work best for 2 or more, so if you're solo, it's safer to call ahead and check whether a single order is possible.

🚄 How to Fold This Into a Regional Route

  • A meal on a Gwangju Songjeong Station transfer — An 8-min walk off the KTX. The closest spot to a station in this entire corner.
  • An overnight in Mokpo — Split your plan between the old downtown (station/Yudalsan) and the Hadang new town. The express bus launched in September 2026 connects the two directly.
  • Naju — Bus or taxi from Naju Station to Geumseonggwan-gil. The Geumseonggwan building is under construction, so stick to the restaurant street.
  • Boseong & Haenam — The green tea fields (#426) and Ttangkkeut are the tourist axis; these two spots sit outside it. Bus is the default in Haenam.
  • Jeju — Three directions from the airport. Covering downtown (Tapdong), the southeast (Pyoseon), and the southwest (Moseulpo) all in one day is a stretch.
  • Coming down from Jeonbuk — From Iksan, the Honam Line runs to Gwangju Songjeong and Mokpo. The Jeonbuk piece is #1385.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Putting a Geumseonggwan visit on the itinerary — It's under demolition-and-repair construction as of September 2026, with reopening targeted for 2027. The restaurant street is unaffected.

2. Booking under a vague "Boseong Station" — The existing Boseong Station and Sinboseong Station are separate. Check the route against your destination first.

3. Getting off at Haenam Station and walking to downtown — It's about 8km from downtown. A bus or taxi transfer is required.

⚙ 3 FAQs

1. Who picked this list? — It's a table compiled from local eateries Korean YouTube viewers wrote in the comments. KeyChinGu verified only existence, address, and business type, and did not rank anything.
