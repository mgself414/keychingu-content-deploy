Locals' Picks ⑥ Chungbuk — 9 Eateries Korean Viewers Named, Six of Them in Chungju

Subtitle: An axis untouched by the Cheongju full guide (#328), Suamgol (#1042), and the Cheongju market old-timers piece (#650) — picking only the 9 Chungbuk spots out of a nationwide roundup of eateries Korean viewers recommended in YouTube comments, verified for existence, address, and business type on Naver local search, with no evaluation attached. Chungju 6 → Cheongju 2 → Jincheon 1. Chungju and Jincheon are both first appearances on KeyChinGu, and as of late 2024 Chungju became a city reachable from the greater Seoul area by train in about an hour.

ISSUE 06 · #1387 · 2026-09-18 · Locals' Picks ⑥

🎯 Why "Comment Recommendations" Is the Bar

This corner has one source. A Korean YouTube channel posted a "nationwide eateries" video, viewers wrote in their own neighborhood spots in the comments, and the most systematically organized comments were compiled by region into a table of 217 places. It's a list Koreans recommended to Koreans, so foreign reviews are scarce and many are small-town restaurants that never make tourist lists. This corner's criteria and limits are laid out in Part ① (#1382).

KeyChinGu checked only whether the name, address, and business type actually exist on Naver local search (2026-09-17). Of 14 Chungbuk spots, 9 were confirmed; the 5 that weren't (3 in Chungju, 2 in Cheongju) were dropped. This region had the highest drop rate of any so far — meaning the comment list is that much more likely to contain outdated names. There are no visit reviews, rankings, or star ratings, and no hours or prices.

The axis of the Chungbuk piece is Chungju. Six of the nine spots are in Chungju, and three of those six aren't downtown at all — they're in township centers like Jungangtap-myeon, Suanbo-myeon, and Salmi-myeon. And on November 30, 2024, Chungju became a city reachable from Pangyo in 65 minutes, 8 times a day, when the Jungbu Naeryuk Line (중부내륙선) KTX-Eum was extended to Mungyeong. It's the same line as Mungyeong in the Daegu/Gyeongbuk piece (#1383).

① Chungju — 6 Spots, the City the Train Reached and the Townships Beyond It

The Jungbu Naeryuk Line reached Bubal in 2021, and when it was extended to Mungyeong on November 30, 2024, Chungju became a city reachable from Pangyo in 65 minutes, 8 times a day. That said, Chungju Station sits on the city's outskirts, so getting to a downtown restaurant means one more leg by bus or taxi.

  • Samjeong Myeonok (삼정면옥) · 21 Gwana3-gil, Chungju (충주시 관아3길 21) · Korean · Naengmyeon · Gwanagol, the old government-office quarter — whether it's walkable from Chungju Station is not confirmed
  • Onggolchan Ongsimi Kalguksu (옹골찬옹심이칼국수) · 305 Tangeumdae-ro, 1F, Chungju (충주시 탄금대로 305 1층) · Korean · Kalguksu & Mandu · Downtown, on Tangeumdae-ro — check

Ongsimi (옹심이) is a dumpling ball made from grated potato. Adding potato ongsimi to kalguksu is common across the Gangwon–Chungbuk inland belt (the Gangwon piece is #1384).

  • Ijeongdong Mukbap (이정동묵밥) · 13-2 Soejeoul1-gil, Chungju (충주시 쇠저울1길 13-2) · Restaurant · Korean · Downtown — check

Mukbap (묵밥) is julienned acorn jelly served with rice in cold broth. It's known as a summer dish, but many places serve it year-round.

  • Memil Madang (메밀마당) · 103 Jungangtap-gil, Jungangtap-myeon, Chungju (충주시 중앙탑면 중앙탑길 103) · Korean · Makguksu · Jungangtap-myeon — local bus from Chungju Station, about 20 runs a day; travel time to check

This township has Jungangtap (Chungju's seven-story stone pagoda at Tappyeong-ri) and Tangeumho Lake. Makguksu (막국수) is buckwheat noodles tossed in cold broth or sauce, ordered as either mul (broth) or bibim (sauced).

  • Hyangnamu Sikdang (향나무식당) · 11 Jangteo1-gil, Suanbo-myeon, Chungju (충주시 수안보면 장터1길 11) · Korean · Hanjeongsik · Suanbo-myeon — about 30 minutes by bus from Chungju Bus Terminal (충주공용버스터미널), fare 2,200 won

Suanbo is a township known for its hot springs. Hanjeongsik (한정식) is a spread of many side dishes served at once, so it usually runs 2 or more people.

  • Deullim Hoetjip (들림횟집) · 374 Palbonghyangsan-gil, Salmi-myeon, Chungju (충주시 살미면 팔봉향산길 374) · Korean · Raw Fish · ⚠ access caution — buses toward Salmi-myeon run only 5 times a day, and the Danwol–Hyangsan stretch has been fully closed to vehicles since July 2026 due to rockfall risk (expected to last until end of 2027), so a detour is needed. Check whether the closure is still in effect before you set out

This spot serves freshwater raw fish. Because of the closure above, it's the hardest of the nine spots in this piece to reach.

Tip for foreign visitors — Makguksu, kalguksu, and mukbap can be ordered for one; hanjeongsik and raw fish default to 2 or more. Township restaurants often open for lunch and close early once they run out of ingredients, so check the hours first.

② Cheongju — 2 Spots, Between Osong Station and Seongan-gil

Cheongju is confusing because it has two stations. Osong Station (오송역) is the high-speed rail station (in Heungdeok-gu) where KTX and SRT stop, and Cheongju Station (청주역) is a separate station served only by conventional Chungbuk Line trains. The old downtown Seongan-gil and Seowon-gu, where this piece's two spots are, are not walkable from either station — take a bus or taxi.

  • Wonjo Jungwontap Makguksu (원조중원탑막국수) · 17 Sangdang-ro 58beon-gil, Sangdang-gu, Cheongju (청주시 상당구 상당로58번길 17) · Korean · Makguksu · Around Seongan-gil, the old downtown — bus/taxi from the station

"Jungwontap" in the name is the old name for Chungju's Jungangtap. So there's a makguksu house on the list for both Chungju and Cheongju.

  • Don Pocha (돈포차) · 6 Cheonsu-ro 18beon-gil, Seowon-gu, Cheongju (청주시 서원구 천수로18번길 6) · Restaurant · Korean · Seowon-gu — check

Pocha (포차) comes from pojangmacha (포장마차, street tent bar) — it means a place that serves drinks and anju (안주, drinking food) in the evening.

Tip for foreign visitors — Cheongju sightseeing centers on Suamgol, Yukgeori Market, and Jikji (#1042, #625, #411); these two spots sit outside that axis, in the everyday city. Check the bus route first when heading from Osong Station into downtown Cheongju.

③ Jincheon — 1 Spot, Gwanghyewon

  • Haru Donkkaseu (하루돈까스) · 18 Guam-gil, Gwanghyewon-myeon, Jincheon-gun (진천군 광혜원면 구암길 18) · Japanese · Donkkaseu · Around Gwanghyewon Terminal — Anseong Bus 17 toward Anseong, or transfer to Cheongju Bus 711 at Jincheon Terminal toward Cheongju

Gwanghyewon-myeon sits at Chungbuk's northwestern tip, bordering Gyeonggi's Anseong. That's why the bus toward Anseong is simpler than the one toward Cheongju. Donkkaseu is a dish that can be ordered for one, so it suits solo travelers passing through.

Tip for foreign visitors — Township-level terminals sometimes have no ticket window or take cash only. Bring small bills ahead of time (#1301).

🚄 Fitting This Into a Regional Route

  • Greater Seoul → Chungju as a day trip — Jungbu Naeryuk Line KTX-Eum from Pangyo, 65 minutes, 8 runs a day. Check the timetable before you go.
  • Around Chungju — the three downtown spots (Gwanagol, Tangeumdae-ro, Soejeoul1-gil) are taxi rides; Jungangtap-myeon has about 20 bus runs a day; Suanbo is about 30 minutes from the terminal.
  • Chungju → Mungyeong — the same Jungbu Naeryuk Line. It connects with the two Mungyeong spots in the Daegu/Gyeongbuk piece (#1383) on a single line.
  • Osong Station transfer — the gateway for reaching Cheongju/Sejong by KTX. It's the same station used in the Daejeon/Sejong/Chungnam piece (#1386).
  • Jincheon Gwanghyewon — part of Gyeonggi Anseong's everyday reach. From Seoul, routing via Anseong is simpler.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Heading straight for Salmi-myeon's Hyangsan — the Danwol–Hyangsan stretch has been closed since July 2026 due to rockfall risk (expected until end of 2027). Check the detour route first.

2. Mistaking Osong Station for downtown Cheongju — Osong Station is the high-speed rail stop, Cheongju Station is the Chungbuk Line stop, and Seongan-gil is yet another place. All three are connected only by bus or taxi.

3. Walking from Chungju Station to downtown — the station was relocated to the city's outskirts. Bus or taxi is the default.

⚙ 3 FAQs

1. Who picked this list? — It's a table of local eateries Korean YouTube viewers wrote into the comments. KeyChinGu only verified existence, address, and business type, and did not rank anything.

2. Why does Chungbuk have so many drops? — 5 of 14 spots couldn't be confirmed on Naver by name and area. They were left out of the body given the chance of closures, renamings, or spelling differences.

3. Chungju or Cheongju for makguksu? — This piece doesn't rank anything. Both spots came from the roundup, and makguksu itself has four more spots in the Gangwon piece (#1384).
