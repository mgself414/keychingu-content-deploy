Jeonju Nambu Market, Youth Mall and Night Market Guide — 3 Minutes on Foot from Hanok Village, Official Info for 2026

Subtitle: Nambu Market dates to 1473, sits beside Pungnammun Gate, and is a 3-minute walk from Hanok Village / Youth Mall on the 2nd floor 11:00–23:00, closed Mondays / Night market Fri–Sat evenings (hours vary by season) / Early-morning Dokkaebi Market 04:00–10:00 / Gyeonggijeon Shrine ₩3,000 — based on official Jeonju City and Jeonbuk tourism pages as of 2026-10-07

Operating info verified: On 2026-10-07 we checked Jeonju City Culture & Tourism (tour.jeonju.go.kr), Jeonbuk Tourism (tour.jb.go.kr) and a Korea Tourism Organization article.

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Nambu Market (남부시장) in Jeonju is a 3-minute walk from Jeonju Hanok Village (전주 한옥마을) across Pungnammun Gate (풍남문) (per Jeonju City, official). The daytime market, the 2nd-floor Youth Mall (청년몰), the Friday–Saturday Nambu Market Night Market (남부시장 야시장) and the early-morning Dokkaebi Market (도깨비시장) all share one building and its alleys, so if you match the time of day, it slots straight into a Hanok Village itinerary. The values below are only what we read on official Jeonju City and Jeonbuk tourism pages on 2026-10-07, and where sources conflict, as with the night market hours, we say so. The older guide had no addresses or hours at all.

🏮 Nambu Market — A Market Since 1473, Beside Pungnammun Gate

  • Jeonju City (official): a market that began in 1473, right next to Pungnammun Gate (Treasure No. 308) and a 3-minute walk from Hanok Village. Address: Pungnammun 1-gil 19-3, 063-284-1344.
  • Signature dishes of the market (official): kongnamul-gukbap (콩나물국밥) (bean-sprout rice soup, served the toryeom way, with the broth ladled in and out) and pisundae-gukbap (피순대국밥) (blood sausage rice soup). The official market tour page lists just two shops: Yetnal Pisundae (옛날피순대) (open 24 hours) and Pungnammun Bibimbap (풍남문비빔밥) (04:00–18:00). We do not list any other shops or prices.
  • Market-wide opening hours are not posted officially (a secondary source says 09:30–22:00), and they differ from shop to shop.

🛍 Youth Mall — 2nd Floor, 11:00–23:00, Closed Mondays

  • On the 2nd floor of Nambu Market, address Pungnammun 2-gil 53. 11:00–23:00, closed Mondays, about 20 shops (Jeonju City, official). The official page says "hours differ by shop," so check ahead if you have a specific shop in mind.
  • The older guide's verdicts such as "a success model for MZ and hipsters" do not appear in official materials, so we dropped them.

🌙 Night Market — Fridays and Saturdays Only

  • It opens only on Fridays and Saturdays, with food stalls along a 250 m alley (Jeonbuk Tourism, official). Food runs ₩3,000–5,000 (official).
  • The hours are where sources conflict: Jeonbuk Tourism says Mar–Oct 18:30–23:30 and Nov–Feb 18:00–24:00. A Korea Tourism Organization article says 18:00–24:00 (summer) and 17:00–22:00 (winter). We could not read a confirmed 2026 figure, so treat it as "Fri and Sat evenings, seasonal" and check on the day.
  • A winter (Jan–Feb) pause was reported (2024). If you visit in winter, confirm it is operating first.
  • The number of stalls differs by source (33, 40 and 45), so we do not list one.

🌅 Dokkaebi Market — 4 AM

  • Nambu Market's dawn market, 04:00–10:00 (Jeonju City, official). A Jeonju early-morning walk paired with kongnamul-gukbap.

🏛 Gyeonggijeon Shrine — Pair It with Hanok Village

  • 44 Taejo-ro. Mar–May and Sep–Oct 09:00–19:00, Jun–Aug 09:00–20:00, Nov–Feb 09:00–18:00, last entry 1 hour before closing. For non-Jeonju residents: adults ₩3,000, teens/soldiers ₩2,000, children ₩1,000, free on the last Wednesday of each month and 50% off on the last Saturday (Jeonju City, official).
  • We could not open the official page for Jeondong Cathedral (전동성당), so we list no hours or fees.

🚉 Getting There

  • From Jeonju Station (전주역) to Hanok Village and Nambu Market: bus and taxi travel times are not on the official pages, so we do not list them. Search for "Jeonju Nambu Market" in a map app.
  • From Hanok Village, cross Pungnammun Gate and you are at the market (official "3-minute walk").

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Youth Mall on a Monday — it is closed.

2. Night market Sunday through Thursday — it opens only on Fridays and Saturdays.

3. Taking the winter night market for granted — there is a precedent for a pause. Confirm it is operating.

4. Walking past Gyeonggijeon without knowing the fees — free on the last Wednesday, half price on the last Saturday.

5. Assuming every shop in the market is open — hours differ by shop (official notice).

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Can I combine it with Hanok Village in one day? — Yes, it is a 3-minute walk. Hanok Village and Gyeonggijeon by day → Youth Mall or the Fri–Sat night market in the evening.

2. What time is the night market? — Friday and Saturday evenings. Seasonal hours conflict between sources, so check on the day.

3. Where can I get breakfast? — Dokkaebi Market (04:00–10:00), or kongnamul-gukbap (toryeom-style) or pisundae-gukbap.

4. What are the Youth Mall hours? — 11:00–23:00, closed Mondays.

5. How much is Gyeonggijeon? — Adults ₩3,000. Free on the last Wednesday.

6. How long from Jeonju Station? — Not posted officially. Use a map app.
