Jeonju Market Guide — Nambu Market Youth Mall and Fri–Sat Night Market, Pungnammun, Sinjungang Market, Moraenae Market, Samcheon-dong Makgeolli Alley

Subtitle: Jeonju Nambu Market (전주 남부시장), 09:30~23:00 (varies by shop) · Night market (야시장), every Friday and Saturday · Pungnammun (풍남문), open year-round · Jeonju Sinjungang Market (전주 신중앙시장) and Moraenae Market (전주 모래내시장), varies by shop · Samcheon-dong Makgeolli Alley (삼천동 막걸리골목) · Blood sausage (pi-sundae, 피순대) at Nambu Market · Kongnamul gukbap (콩나물국밥) on Dongmun-gil (동문길) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

According to the Korea Tourism Organization, Jeonju's four major markets are Nambu Market, Dongbu Market, Jungang Market and Moraenae Market. Among them, Jeonju Nambu Market (19-3 Pungnammun 1-gil (풍남문1길 19-3)) is known for its second-floor youth mall and its Friday and Saturday night market.

This guide covers only Jeonju's markets and market food, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Hanok Village (한옥마을), Gyeonggijeon (경기전) and Jeondong Cathedral (전동성당) are covered in the #384 Jeonju Guide.

🏮 Jeonju Nambu Market — Youth Mall and Night Market

Jeonju Nambu Market (19-3 Pungnammun 1-gil, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si (전주시 완산구 풍남문1길 19-3), ☎ 063-284-1344) is said to have originated from the "Nammun-bak Market" (남문밖시장) of the early Joseon period, and it opened as a regular public market in 1905. About 800 shops sell silk fabrics, furniture, dried seafood, vegetables, fruit, medicinal herbs and more.

  • Youth mall: It opened on the second floor of Building 6, in a market that had many empty stalls. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the atmosphere is similar to the alleys of Samcheong-dong and Hongdae in Seoul.
  • Night market: It is held every Friday and Saturday. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, snacks such as a blood sausage (pi-sundae, 피순대) set meal, mung bean pancakes (nokdujeon, 녹두전) and bibimbap rolls are popular.
  • Opening hours: 09:30~23:00, varying by shop. Closed days also vary by shop.
  • Cards: Accepted (varies by shop). Parking: Available. Restrooms available.

Jo Jeom-rye Nammun Pisundae (조점례남문피순대) — A Sundae Shop in Nambu Market

Jo Jeom-rye Nammun Pisundae (39 Pungnammun 2-gil, Wansan-gu (완산구 풍남문2길 39), ☎ 063-232-5006) is a sundae specialty shop inside Nambu Market. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the blood sausage (pi-sundae, 피순대) is made with seonji (congealed blood, 선지), tofu, meat, eggs and vegetables, with chopped gopchang (곱창) and more. The sundae gukbap (순대국밥) comes with sundae and gopchang in a clear broth.

  • Signature dishes: Sundae gukbap. Also amppong (암뽕), blood sausage (pi-sundae), japchae sundae (잡채순대) and more.
  • Opening hours: 06:00~22:00, open year-round.
  • Cards: Accepted. Takeout: Available. For parking, use the Nambu Market parking lot. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the wait can be long depending on the time of day.

🏯 Pungnammun — South Gate of the Old Jeonju Fortress

Pungnammun (1 Pungnammun 3-gil, Jeondong, Wansan-gu (완산구 풍남문3길 1, 전동), ☎ 063-287-6008) is the south gate of the old Jeonju Fortress. It was destroyed in the Jeongyu War (정유재란), and in the 10th year of King Yeongjo (1734) it was rebuilt and called "Myeonggyeonru" (명견루); in the 44th year of King Yeongjo (1768), Governor Hong Nak-in (홍낙인) rebuilt it again and gave it the name "Pungnammun." Under King Sunjong (순종), the fortress walls and gates were torn down for city planning and suffered heavy damage, and its current appearance was recovered through three years of repair work starting in 1978.

  • Opening hours: Open year-round. Parking: Not available.

🍡 Jeonju Sinjungang Market — Rice-Cake Alley

Jeonju Sinjungang Market (33 Taepyeong 5-gil, Taepyeong-dong, Wansan-gu (완산구 태평5길 33, 태평동), ☎ 063-274-7535) has a rice-cake alley (tteok golmok, 떡 골목) at its entrance, according to the Korea Tourism Organization. It sells a variety of rice cakes, which are described as popular as snacks or gifts.

  • What is sold: Fashion clothing, hanbok, underwear, curtains, bedding and more.
  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop.
  • Parking: Paid, but free if you buy something inside the market. Restrooms available.

🥬 Jeonju Moraenae Market — An Everyday Market

Jeonju Moraenae Market (8-8 Moraenae 4-gil, Inhu-dong 2-ga, Deokjin-gu (덕진구 모래내4길 8-8, 인후동2가), ☎ 063-278-5802) is an everyday market that has existed since the 1970s. Produce from nearby areas such as Wanju steadily comes in.

  • What is sold: Vegetables, fruit, meat, dried seafood, clothing and more.
  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop.
  • Parking: Available. Restrooms available.

🍶 Samcheon-dong Makgeolli Alley

Samcheon-dong Makgeolli Alley (Samcheon-dong 1-ga, Wansan-gu (완산구 삼천동1가), for inquiries call the Jeonju City Hall (전주시청 도시정비과) at ☎ 063-281-5256) is a place where dozens of long-established makgeolli taverns are gathered over about 200 m, centered on Geomasan-ro (거마산로). It formed around the 1990s and was designated a Jeonju Future Heritage (전주미래유산).

  • How to eat: According to the Korea Tourism Organization, even if you order only one kettle of makgeolli, more than 20 side dishes, including grilled fish, jeon (전, pan-fried dishes), jjigae (찌개, stews), fritters and samhap (삼합), come with it, plus seasonal side dishes. According to the same description, queues sometimes form at each shop in the evening.
  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Available.

🍲 Kongnamul Gukbap — Waengi Kongnamul Gukbap Restaurant

Jeonju Waengi Kongnamul Gukbap Restaurant (88 Dongmun-gil, Wansan-gu (완산구 동문길 88), ☎ 063-287-6980) is a kongnamul gukbap (콩나물국밥, bean sprout rice soup) restaurant that opened in August 1988. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is boiled with bean sprouts, aged kimchi and a little seafood in a half-and-half mix of anchovy broth and water.

  • Signature dishes: Jeonju Waengi Gukbap (전주왱이국밥). A children's gukbap is also available.
  • Opening hours: 07:00~21:00, open year-round.
  • Cards: All cards accepted. Takeout: Not available. Parking: Available.

📅 Jeonju Gamaek Festival — August 2026 Edition

The Jeonju Gamaek Festival (전주 가맥축제) expanded Jeonju's "gamaek" (가맥) culture, in which people ate dried seafood and drank beer together at neighborhood shops, into a festival. The 2026 edition was held from August 6 to 8, 18:00~23:00, around the Jeonbuk National University Complex Sports Stadium (전북대학교 복합경기장), and admission was 1,000 won.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Showing up on any day for the night market — Nambu Market's night market is held every Friday and Saturday.
  2. Trying to take kongnamul gukbap to go — Waengi Kongnamul Gukbap Restaurant is registered as not offering takeout.
  3. Trying to park at Pungnammun — Pungnammun is registered as having no parking. Nambu Market is registered as having parking.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Until what time is Nambu Market open?

A. It is registered in the Korea Tourism Organization data as 09:30~23:00, and it varies by shop.

Q. Is parking at Sinjungang Market free?

A. It is paid, but it is registered as free if you buy something inside the market.
