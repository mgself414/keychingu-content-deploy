9 Running Courses Outside Seoul — Bamboo Groves, Lakes and Beaches Chosen by Public Vote, with Distances and How to Get There

Subtitle: The 9 courses outside Seoul that received votes in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme, grouped into 4 zones: Ulsan·Daegu, Gyeongju, Busan and Jeju. Ulsan's Simnidaesup Bamboo Grove is about 4 km and free 24 hours a day, while Jeju's Saryeoni Forest Path is about 10 km with exit by 17:00. For Gyeongju's Bomun Lake, the agencies give different lap lengths, and in Jeju's Aewol the walking path and the road have similar names. Distances, opening hours, fees and bus numbers are listed only as the agencies state them.

ISSUE 06 · #1445 · 2026-11-24 · Running Courses Outside Seoul

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 9

This list is for people who want to run for a day while traveling in Korea. Use it when you leave Seoul for Ulsan, Daegu, Gyeongju, Busan or Jeju and want to add a morning or evening run. It mixes bamboo forest paths, lake loops, beaches, forest paths and coastal walks.

There is one selection rule. From the top 14 in the Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) "100×100 Spot Discovery" (명소발굴 100×100) public vote on the running course theme, we picked the lake, coastal and forest courses outside Seoul. Ranks 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 11, 12 and 14 are the 9 courses in this guide. Ranks 2, 6 and 9 are the Han River parks in Seoul (Yeouido, Banpo, Ttukseom), so they are left out here. Rank 13, Yeongnam Alps Haneul Eoksae-gil (영남알프스 하늘억새길), is a mountain ridge path, so it is left to the trail running guide (#1428). Rank 8, Yeonhwaji Pond (연화지) in Gimcheon (137 votes), is not included as a regular course because the Korea Tourism Organization description only says it is laid out for walking along the reservoir and has night lighting.

These 9 are not tracks made for running but paths shared with walkers and tourists. That is why we wrote how many km each one is, when it opens and closes, and how to get there.

⚠ Notation principle: Distances, opening hours, fees and directions are values read on 2026-11-24 from the Korea Tourism Organization, city and district offices and regional tourism portals (Ulsan City, Gyeongju City, Busan City, Suyeong-gu, Visit Busan, Daegu City, Visit Jeju), and the Jeju Bus Information System. Bus numbers are the routes serving each stop on the reading date. Items for which an agency gives no figure are not written. No running times are included, and only the travel times the agencies wrote are copied as they are. The walking time from Suseongmot Station (수성못역) is a Kakao Map route value (2026-11-24). Votes and ranks are values from the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme. Opening hours and bus routes can change.

🚦 Before You Run — Common Safety and Manners

None of the 9 is a path made only for running. The points below are not laws or facility rules but general manners for paths that many people share.

  • On coastal roads next to a car road, do not step down onto the road. Visitor reviews on Visit Jeju (비짓제주) also say that Dodu Rainbow Coastal Road is not wide, so watch for vehicles. In narrow sections, walk instead of running and keep an eye on vehicles.
  • Slow down on crowded paths. Gwangalli, Dongbaekseom Island, the area around Cheomseongdae Observatory and Handam Coastal Walk are in the middle of tourist areas. Walk in narrow sections.
  • Follow the rules set by each facility. Bicycles and ATVs cannot use the Bomun Lake path. At Saryeoni Forest Path you cannot carry food in, only water. At Handam Coastal Walk, on-site controls and notices come first.
  • If the weather is bad, call it off. Saryeoni Forest Path may close part or all of the route in bad weather such as heavy rain or heavy snow. On days with strong waves, view Yongduam Rock (용두암) only from the designated paths and safety facilities.
  • In an accident or emergency, call 119.

① Ulsan·Daegu — 2 Places

Ulsan and Daegu are different cities. Rather than combining them in one go, add one to each city's itinerary. Neither has opening hour limits.

Ulsan Taehwagang Simnidaesup Bamboo Grove Path (울산 태화강 십리대숲길)

  • Public vote: 229 votes · rank 1 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • Distance: The Ulsan City guide for Taehwagang National Garden (태화강 국가정원) gives Simnidaesup Bamboo Grove (십리대숲) as about 4 km. It says about 500,000 bamboo grow on a total area of 109,866㎡, and that there is an eco-friendly walking path inside the grove.
  • Hours · Fee: No admission fee. You can enter 24 hours a day with no entry limit. The information center is open 9:00–18:00.
  • Night: Eunhasu-gil (Milky Way Path) (은하수길), the night-lit section, is lit from sunset to 23:00. If you run after dark, plan within these hours.
  • How to get there: Take a bus and get off at the stop "Taehwagang National Garden / Donggang Hospital" (태화강 국가정원 / 동강병원). The address is 5 Saetgang-nam-gil, Jung-gu, Ulsan (울산 중구 샛강남길 5), in Taehwa-dong (태화동).
  • From Taehwagang Station (태화강역): Buses 523, 713, 763, 1115, 1733, 9001
  • From KTX Ulsan Station (KTX울산역): Buses 523, 1733, 5001, 5003, 5004
  • From the intercity and express bus terminal (시외·고속버스터미널): Buses 417, 523, 713, 723, 763, 1115, 1733, 9001
  • Parking: There are 14 car parks. The paid ones are Annex 1 (215 spaces), 2 (447 spaces) and 3 (123 spaces), and the free ones are Taehwagang Observatory (태화강전망대) (28 spaces), Donggulpia (동굴피아) (55 spaces) and Samho Yeonan public lot (삼호연안 공영) (175 spaces).
  • Inquiries: Taehwagang National Garden information center 052-229-3147.

Daegu Suseongmot Lake (대구 수성못)

  • Public vote: 134 votes · rank 11 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • Distance: Both the Daegu City tourism page and the Korea Tourism Organization travel article give the loop around the lake as about 2 km. The Korea Tourism Organization article says one lap on foot takes 40-some minutes. 5 laps come to about 10 km.
  • Sections and surface: The Korea Tourism Organization article (written October 2020) describes the lake's edge in 4 sections. The east is a 180 m waterside deck road (수변데크로드), the south has the swan boat dock (오리배 선착장) and a path under Yoshino cherry trees (왕벚나무 산책로, a path you can walk barefoot), the west is a decomposed granite path (마사토 산책로) covered with coarse sand, and the north is toward the floating stage (수상무대). There is no set starting point.
  • Hours · Fee: The Daegu City tourism page gives the hours as "no restrictions" and the fee as "free". The Korea Tourism Organization article also gives the lakeside path as 24 hours, open all year, free.
  • Night: The Daegu City tourism page says lights reflected on the water sparkle at night. The Korea Tourism Organization article says lights come on at the weeping willows (왕버들) and the waterside deck road.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map route, it is 16 minutes on foot (about 950 m) from Exit 1 of Suseongmot Station (수성못역) on Daegu Metro Line 3. The address is 35-5 Yonghak-ro, Suseong-gu, Daegu (대구광역시 수성구 용학로 35-5).
  • Facilities: The Daegu City tourism page lists "free public parking available". There are restrooms and a diaper changing table. Inquiries 053-761-0645.

② Gyeongju — 2 Places

Both places are reached on one bus route, Bus 10 (Bus 11 in the opposite direction). The Gyeongju City tourism page says this bus leaves the Gyeongju express/intercity bus terminal and goes to the stop "Gyeongju Station" (경주역), Cheomseongdae Observatory (첨성대), Bunhwangsa Temple (분황사), Bomun Tourist Complex (보문관광단지) and Bulguksa Temple (불국사), in that order. The first bus is at 06:00, the last bus at 21:40, and buses run every 20 minutes. Because the last bus at 21:40 leaves before Cheomseongdae Observatory and Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (동궁과 월지) close at 22:00, you can combine them in one day by circling Bomun Lake (보문호) in the morning and heading toward Cheomseongdae Observatory in the evening.

Gyeongju Bomun Lake (경주 보문호)

  • Public vote: 152 votes · rank 3 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • Distance: The agencies give different figures. The Korea Tourism Organization gives about 8 km and Gyeongju City gives about 6 km. Both say it takes about 2 hours to complete. To run an exact distance, measure it yourself with a watch or an app.
  • Rules: Bicycles and ATVs cannot use the path. The Korea Tourism Organization and Gyeongju City wrote the same thing.
  • How to get there: Take Bus 10 from the Gyeongju express/intercity bus terminal. The stops on the Bomun side are "Bomun Tourist Complex · Gyeongsangbuk-do Culture and Tourism Corporation" (보문단지·경상북도문화관광공사), "Hilton · The K Hotel" (힐튼·더케이호텔), "KT Training Center" (KT수련원) and "Gyeongju World" (경주월드), in that order. No stop named "Bomun Lake" appears in the Gyeongju City bus guide, so look for "Bomun Tourist Complex" (보문단지). Bus 10-1 does not go on to Bulguksa Temple but turns back at Gyeongju World.
  • Places to rest: The Gyeongju City guide says there are small rest areas at Mulneoul Park (물너울공원) and Sarang Park (사랑공원), and a Park Mok-wol poem monument (박목월 시비) along the path.

Gyeongju Cheomseongdae Observatory area (경주 첨성대 일대)

  • Public vote: 139 votes · rank 7 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • Route: The section named in the vote is the path linking Hwangseong Park (황성공원), Bukcheon Stream (북천), Bunhwangsa Temple (분황사), Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (동궁과 월지) and Cheomseongdae Observatory (첨성대). No agency gives the distance of the path linking these 5 places, so no distance is written. Because it is a path that runs between heritage sites, the runner sets the course distance.
  • Hwangseong Park: The Korea Tourism Organization says that on the pine forest path, clusters of lilyturf (맥문동) bloom in summer.
  • Cheomseongdae Observatory: According to the Gyeongju City tourism page, it is open 09:00–22:00. In winter it is open until 21:00. It is open all year and free. The night scenic lighting is on from after sunset until 22:00.
  • Nearby sight — Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (동궁과 월지): Open 09:00–22:00, with ticket sales ending at 21:30. Admission is 3,000 won for adults, 2,000 won for teenagers and 1,000 won for children. The night scenic lighting is on from after sunset until 22:00.
  • How to get there: Take Bus 10 (Bus 11 in the opposite direction) from the Gyeongju express/intercity bus terminal. The stop list has "Wolseong-dong Community Center" (월성동사무소), "Cheomseongdae" (첨성대) and "Daereungwon" (대릉원, Daereungwon Tomb Complex), in that order. The same route also passes Bunhwangsa Temple, Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond, and the Gyeongju National Museum (국립경주박물관).

③ Busan — 2 Places

Gwangalli and Dongbaekseom Island are both reached on Busan Metro Line 2. For Gwangalli it is Geumnyeonsan Station (금련산역) or Gwangan Station (광안역), and for Dongbaekseom Island it is Dongbaek Station (동백역). Gwangalli is a beach with a length of 1.4 km, and Dongbaekseom Island is a short coastal loop path.

Busan Gwangalli Beach (부산 광안리해수욕장)

  • Public vote: 148 votes · rank 4 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • Distance: Busan City gives the beach as "total area 82,000㎡, beach length 1.4 km, beach width 25–110 m". Going from end to end and back is 2.8 km, and 2 round trips come to 5.6 km.
  • Hours · Fee: Visit Busan (비짓부산) gives it as always open, open all year, free. It says you can walk and cycle on the beach theme street and that performances are held in summer.
  • How to get there: Exit guidance differs by agency.
  • Suyeong-gu (수영구) guide: Busan Metro Line 2 Geumnyeonsan Station (금련산역) Exit 3, 5 minutes on foot, Gwangan Station (광안역) Exit 3, 10 minutes on foot.
  • Visit Busan guide: Line 2 Gwangan Station (광안역) Exit 5, about 13 minutes on foot.
  • Bus: Suyeong-gu says to take Buses 41, 49, 38, 42, 51, 62, 83, 83-1 and 108-1 and get off at "Gwangalli Beach" (광안리해수욕장), 3 minutes away. Visit Busan lists Buses 42 and 62.
  • Address: Around 219 Gwangan Haebyeon-ro, Suyeong-gu, Busan (부산 수영구 광안해변로 219 일원), Gwangan-dong (광안동).
  • Parking: Visit Busan directs you to "nearby public parking lots".

Busan Dongbaekseom Island (부산 동백섬)

  • Public vote: 136 votes · rank 10 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • Distance: Visit Busan gives the coastal path around Dongbaekseom Island as "about 950 m". 5 runs come to 4.75 km. The section named in the vote is the path from Haeundae Beach (해운대 해변) to the Dongbaekseom Island loop, but no agency gives a distance that includes the beach section.
  • Slope: The Korea Tourism Organization accessibility guide says there is a section of about 10 degrees slope at the entrance to the path, and that the ramps to the rest areas and viewing decks are 5–9 degrees. It is short, but not a path that is all flat.
  • Hours · Fee: Always open, open all year, with no admission fee.
  • How to get there: Exit Busan Metro Line 2 Dongbaek Station (동백역) by Exit 1. Visit Busan gives 17 minutes on foot from Exit 1. By bus, take Bus 1003 and get off at "Dongbaekseom" (동백섬).
  • Facilities: There are restrooms. The Busan City Nurimaru (누리마루) guide says the Dongbaek Park annex parking lot is paid and that parking around Dongbaekseom Island is limited and fills up on weekends and public holidays, so it recommends public transport.
  • Nearby sight — Nurimaru APEC House (누리마루 APEC하우스): Open 09:00–18:00, with entry ending 1 hour before closing. Closed on the first Monday of each month. Entry is free. Telephone 051-743-1974~5.

④ Jeju — 3 Places

Saryeoni Forest Path is a forest path in the island's interior, and Handam and Dodu are seaside paths. All 3 have bus stops. Bus numbers are the routes serving each stop, looked up by stop name in the Jeju Bus Information System.

Jeju Saryeoni Forest Path (제주 사려니숲길)

  • Public vote: 143 votes · rank 5 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • Distance: Visit Jeju gives about 10 km. It runs from the Bijarim-ro entrance (비자림로 입구) in Bonggae-dong, Jeju-si, past the direction of Mulchat Oreum (물찻오름), to the Bulgeun Oreum (붉은오름) (Namjo-ro) entrance in Gasi-ri, Pyoseon-myeon, Seogwipo-si. There are no separately named sections. Going from end to end and back is about 20 km.
  • Surface: Near the Bulgeun Oreum entrance the path is red volcanic cinder (붉은 화산송이길). The Jeolmul dwarf bamboo section (절물 조릿대길) has an uneven floor with differences in height.
  • Hours: 09:00–17:00, and you must be out by 17:00. Open all year. In bad weather such as heavy rain or heavy snow, part or all of the route may be closed. If you plan a round trip, include the time to come back out and start early.
  • Mulchat Oreum: Entry is normally blocked. You have to turn back near the entrance. It is sometimes opened temporarily for events with a reservation.
  • Rules: You cannot carry food in. Water is allowed. Plastic bottles go in the collection bins near the information center.
  • How to get there: Visit Jeju says to get off at the Namjo-ro "Saryeoni Forest Path" stop and says both entrances can be reached by public transport. The stops and routes in the Jeju Bus Information System are as follows.
  • Stop "Namjo-ro Saryeoni Forest Path" (남조로 사려니숲길): Buses 131, 132, 231, 232
  • Stop "Saryeoni Forest Path" (사려니숲길): Buses 212, 232 (the stop toward Gyorae Entrance (교래입구) also shows Bus 222-1)
  • Parking: There is no parking lot around the Bijarim-ro entrance. Visit Jeju recommends the parking lot near the Namjo-ro Bulgeun Oreum entrance. If you are with elderly people or a stroller, it advises entering from the Namjo-ro entrance.

Jeju Handam Coastal Walk — vote name "Aewol Coastal Path" (Handam Coastal Walk, Aewol)

  • Public vote: 134 votes · rank 12 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme. The vote name is "Aewol Coastal Path" (애월 해안로), and the section is from Gwakji Beach (곽지해수욕장) to Aewol Port (애월항).
  • Tell the names apart first: There are 2 paths with similar names.
  • Handam Coastal Walk (한담해안산책로): A walking path of about 1.2 km linking Aewol Port (애월항) to Gwakji Gwamul Beach (곽지과물해변). It is also called the Gwakgeum Olle path (곽금올레길). The two ends of the section written in the vote (Gwakji Beach to Aewol Port) are the same as the two ends of this walk (Gwakji Gwamul Beach to Aewol Port).
  • Aewol Coastal Road (애월해안도로): A coastal driving road of about 9 km from Hagwi (하귀) to Aewol, that is, a car road. Visit Jeju says that bicycle lanes and walking sections have been built along the road and that it is part of the Jeju Round Bicycle Path.
  • Distance: Handam Coastal Walk is about 1.2 km. Going there and back is 2.4 km, and 2 round trips come to 4.8 km.
  • Surface: Visit Jeju says it is "built flat overall". There is a notice that the ramp at the main entrance is steep, so wheelchair users should be careful.
  • Hours · Fee: The Visit Jeju usage guide says always open, open all year. You can go with a dog, and the leash or harness is held at 2 m or less. On-site controls and notices come first.
  • Night: Visit Jeju says lights come on around the path when the sun is going down.
  • How to get there: These are the stops and routes in the Jeju Bus Information System.
  • Stop "Handam-dong" (한담동): Buses 202, 202-1, 202-2, 202-4
  • Stop "Gwakji Beach" (곽지해수욕장): Buses 202, 202-1, 202-2, 795
  • Stop "Aewol Transfer Stop (Aewol-ri)" (애월환승정류장(애월리)): Buses 102, 102-1, 202, 202-1, 202-2, 202-4
  • Address: 1359 Gwakji-ri, Aewol-eup, Jeju-si (제주시 애월읍 곽지리 1359).

Jeju Dodu Rainbow Coastal Road (제주 도두 무지개해안도로)

  • Public vote: 131 votes · rank 14 in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme. The section named in the vote is from the Dodu-dong Rainbow Coastal Road (도두동 무지개 해안도로) past Eoyeong Park (어영공원) to Yongduam Rock (용두암).
  • Distance: Visit Jeju only says the rainbow-colored guard wall section is about 15–20 minutes away on foot. No agency gives a figure in km, so no distance is written.
  • Nature of the path: It is a coastal road beside a car road. A Visit Jeju visitor review says the road is not wide, so watch for vehicles. Do not step down onto the road, and in narrow sections walk and keep an eye on vehicles.
  • How to get there: The stop name in the Jeju Bus Information System is "Dodubong Dongjjok" (도두봉 동쪽). Visit Jeju says that if you get off at this stop it is right in front of you.
  • "Dodubong Dongjjok [South]" (도두봉 동쪽[남]): Buses 445, 447, 454, 1113
  • "Dodubong Dongjjok [North]" (도두봉 동쪽[북]): Buses 444, 447, 453, 1111, 1112
  • Parking: You can come by car and by bus. Large buses use the parking space across from Oraemul Square (오래물광장).
  • Dogs: You can go with a dog. Hold the leash or harness within 2 m and bring waste bags.
  • Nearby sight — Yongduam Rock (용두암): The Visit Jeju usage guide says always open, open all year, with free viewing of the view. The address is 15 Yongduam-gil, Jeju-si (제주시 용두암길 15). On days with strong waves, view it from the designated paths and safety facilities. Tourist site inquiries 064-728-3917.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Thinking Aewol Coastal Road and Handam Coastal Walk are the same path — If you pin "Aewol Coastal Road" (애월해안도로) on the map, you get a driving road of about 9 km from Hagwi to Aewol. The walking path linking Gwakji Gwamul Beach and Aewol Port is Handam Coastal Walk (한담해안산책로), at about 1.2 km. Look for the bus stops as "Handam-dong" (한담동) or "Gwakji Beach" (곽지해수욕장) too.
  2. Entering Saryeoni Forest Path late in the afternoon — You must be out by 17:00. Going all the way and back is about 20 km. You cannot carry food in, so bring only water. On days of heavy rain or snow it may be closed.
  3. Planning around a single distance figure — For Bomun Lake, the Korea Tourism Organization gives about 8 km and Gyeongju City gives about 6 km. For the Cheomseongdae Observatory area and Dodu Rainbow Coastal Road, no agency gives a figure in km. To fill a set distance, use laps or round trips at places that have a figure, such as Suseongmot Lake (about 2 km), Dongbaekseom Island (about 950 m), Handam (about 1.2 km) and Gwangalli (1.4 km).

⚙ 3 FAQs

  1. Where can I run after dark? — Simnidaesup Bamboo Grove (십리대숲) can be entered 24 hours a day, and the Eunhasu-gil (은하수길) lights are on from sunset to 23:00. Suseongmot Lake (수성못) has no hours restriction. Gwangalli, Dongbaekseom Island and Handam Coastal Walk are always open. At Handam, lights come on around the path at dusk. Cheomseongdae Observatory (첨성대) is open until 22:00 (21:00 in winter) and its scenic lighting is on after sunset. At Saryeoni Forest Path (사려니숲길) you must be out by 17:00, so it is not for the evening. For Bomun Lake (보문호) and Dodu Rainbow Coastal Road (도두 무지개해안도로) the agencies do not give separate hours, and Dongbaekseom Island (동백섬) is always open but has no lighting information. Run at these 3 in daylight.
  2. Is there anywhere that costs money? — No agency says it charges a fee for the running path itself. Simnidaesup Bamboo Grove, Suseongmot Lake, Gwangalli and Cheomseongdae Observatory are listed as free, and Dongbaekseom Island has no admission fee. Among the nearby sights, the one that costs money is Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (동궁과 월지) (adults 3,000 won, teenagers 2,000 won, children 1,000 won). Entry to Nurimaru APEC House (누리마루 APEC하우스) and viewing at Yongduam Rock (용두암) are free.
  3. Can I get there without a rental car? — All 9 can be reached by subway station or bus. The 2 in Busan are on Busan Metro Line 2, and the 2 in Gyeongju are on one route, Bus 10 from the terminal. Ulsan's Simnidaesup Bamboo Grove is reached by bus from Taehwagang Station (태화강역), KTX Ulsan Station (KTX울산역) and the terminal. For the 3 in Jeju, look for the stops "Namjo-ro Saryeoni Forest Path" (남조로 사려니숲길) and "Saryeoni Forest Path" (사려니숲길), "Handam-dong" (한담동) and "Gwakji Beach" (곽지해수욕장), and "Dodubong Dongjjok" (도두봉 동쪽). Daegu's Suseongmot Lake is a 16 minute walk from Exit 1 of Suseongmot Station (수성못역) on Daegu Metro Line 3 (Kakao Map).
