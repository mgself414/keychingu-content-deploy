⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Seomun Market = Just a Market": Korea #3 Traditional Market·Korea #3 Night Market (Daily 19:00-23:30). Next to Gwangjang Market (Seoul) and Jagalchi (Busan).

2. "Flat dumpling = same as Seoul dumpling": Flat dumpling = Seomun Market exclusive signature·thin dumpling skin·Seoul·Busan

3. "Night market = Same as other night markets": Seomun Market Night Market = Daily 19:00-23:30·Soju + Snacks Matrix·Foreign Mania Matrix #1.

4. "Kalguksu = Spicy": Seomun Market Kalguksu = Not spicy, light matrix. 1st place for foreigners to enter.

5. "Galbijjim = expensive Korean food": Seomun Market Galbijjim = KRW 25,000~45,000 (for 2-3 people) · Cost-effectiveness matrix · Long-established signature.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. No. 1 choice for foreigners: Kalguksu + flat dumplings + braised ribs = full set · non-spicy option.

2. Night Market Hours: Daily 19:00~23:30 (Open all year round, closed on some holidays and in case of rain).

3. Payment: Card OK·Cash at some long-established stores·Refund at some stores (50,000 won+ tax free).

4. Language: Some English menus (foreigner-friendly store)·Finger + Photo Order OK.

5. Season: Best in spring (April-May)·Fall (September-October)·Summer heat wave·Winter sub-zero cold Some night market stores are closed.

6. Recommended persona: Family·Senior = Daytime market (kalguksu, braised ribs, flat dumplings) / MZ·Spicy Mania = Night market (tteokbokki, sundae, soju).

⚠ Medical/Safety/Dietary disclaimer

Night market = Spicy, oily, differences in street vendor hygiene. Pregnant women, high blood pressure, kidney patients = Be careful of frequency. Allergy (crustaceans, salted shrimp, nuts, eggs, milk, flour) prior notice. Food poisoning/stomachache ☎ 1339.

Dietary restrictions fallback:

  • Halal·Kosher: Braised short ribs (beef OK·Halal certification X) / Tteokbokki·Sundae = Non-halal. → Kalguksu (vegetarian OK) Some stores
  • Vegetarian·Vegan: Tteokbokki·Flat dumpling (egg
  • Gluten free: Kalguksu, flat dumplings, braised ribs, vermicelli = contains gluten (flour)

✈️ Foreigner Pain Point #1 — Night Market Safety + Payment

  • Night market safety: Korean night market = Nationwide safety and low crime rate. However, beware of pickpockets, mobile phones, and wallets.
  • Payment: Card OK 80%, Cash 10,000 won, 50,000 won notes recommended in advance (Cash only at some long-established stores)
  • Table + Stall: Night market + table OK·Korean standard for shared seating (foreigner friendly)
  • Foreigner-friendly store: English menu + photos + finger ordering OK (same pattern as Seoul Plaza and Busan Jagalchi)
