You Get Your Duty-Free Items at the Airport — The Zero-Panic Manual for the Pickup Counter

Subtitle: Anything you buy at a downtown or online duty-free shop can't leave the store with you — it's waiting for you at the airport pickup counter on departure day. This covers what your exchange voucher means, how to find the counter, the kiosk-and-queue-number process, timing math, and the sealed-bag rule for liquids — the last checkpoint of duty-free shopping.

ISSUE 04 · #1332 · 2026-08-24 · korea-first-timer series

🎯 How to Read This Manual — Notation Rules

  • Buttons and machine actions are written in brackets: [ yeogwon seukaen (여권 스캔) ] — Passport Scan
  • Signs and notices are written in corner brackets, exactly as posted (often bilingual already): 「면세품 인도장 (Duty Free Pickup)」
  • Physical actions you take are bolded

The one-line takeaway: Korea's downtown and online duty-free shops run on a "buy now, collect at the airport" system. If the register hands you a voucher instead of your item, that's completely normal — it's the design, not a mistake.

① Why You Don't Get the Item Right Away — The System in 30 Seconds

Duty-free goods can legally only be sold to someone who's actually leaving the country. So anything bought at a downtown duty-free shop (Shilla, Shinsegae, Lotte, and others) or an online duty-free store gets routed through a customs-controlled area to a pickup counter inside the departure zone, and you only collect it after you've cleared departure immigration.

  • What you get at checkout: an exchange voucher (doubles as a receipt, or a barcode if it's mobile) — this is your proof of ownership. Don't lose it.
  • The one exception: anything bought at a duty-free shop inside the airport's departure zone itself — you get that on the spot.

② Before You Buy — Timing Is Everything

Check Standard
Online duty-free orders Recommended to complete your order at least 3 days before departure — last-minute orders may not be ready at the pickup counter
In-store purchases downtown Safe up until the day before departure. Same-day purchases may or may not be deliverable, depending on the store and your flight — staff will check your flight time and tell you
Buffer for pickup It's safest to finish pickup-counter collection 1–2 hours before boarding — peak-season queues are the wild card
  • At the time of purchase, you must present your passport and your flight number and departure date/time. Your voucher gets locked to that specific flight.

③ On Departure Day — Finding the Pickup Counter and Collecting Your Items

1. Go through check-in, security, and departure immigration as normal — the pickup counter sits inside the duty-free zone

2. Follow the 「면세품 인도장」 / 「Duty Free Pickup」 signs. At Incheon Airport T1, there are two counters on the 4th floor, near Gate 11 and Gate 46 — head for whichever is closer to your boarding gate

3. At the kiosk by the counter entrance, [ yeogwon seukaen (여권 스캔) ] — Passport Scan — and a queue ticket prints out

4. When your number appears on the display board, go to the counter — hand over your passport and exchange voucher and you'll get your items

5. Check the quantity and items against your order before opening the bag, right there at the counter — while you still have flight time to spare is your golden window for fixing any mistake

④ If You Have Liquids — The Sealed-Bag (STEB) Rule

  • Liquids handed over at the pickup counter — alcohol, cosmetics, and the like — come sealed inside a clear STEB (Security Tamper-Evident Bag) along with the receipt.
  • Don't open it before you reach your final destination — this matters especially if you're transiting through a third country, where security may confiscate a bag that's already been opened.
  • On a direct flight home, it's fine as carry-on — this sealed bag is precisely the exception to the 100ml liquid rule.

⑤ Three Panic Scenarios

Situation What to Do
You lost your exchange voucher In most cases, your purchase can be looked up with just your passport — at the counter, show your passport and name the duty-free retailer you bought from
Your number hasn't been called and boarding is approaching Go to the counter, show your boarding pass, and flag that your flight is imminent — it's standard practice to prioritize passengers who are close to boarding
You boarded without collecting your items Contact the duty-free retailer's customer service and they'll process it as a cancellation and refund — the money goes back to your payment card; nothing simply vanishes
