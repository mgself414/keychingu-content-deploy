Axis 1: Cheonggyesan 청계산 (3 picks) — #1 Foreign-Intro Foliage Hike

Cheonggyesan 청계산 = mountain on the Seongnam 성남 · Gwacheon 과천 · Seoul Seocho 서초 · Gangnam 강남 boundary (elev. 618 m at Maebong 매봉). Peak foliage = early to mid-November (KNPS 국립공원공단 normals). Foreign-intro hike = Shinbundang Line 신분당선 Cheonggyesan-ipgu Stn 청계산입구역 + express bus 1005 access OK · trails well-marked · some English signage.

⚠ Top 3 Mistakes Foreign Tourists Make

1. "Peak foliage = mid-October" — actually late Oct to mid-Nov for Cheonggyesan 청계산 / Gwanaksan 관악산

Many foreign guidebooks say "Korea autumn = late October." Reality: Cheonggyesan 청계산 / Gwanaksan 관악산 = Gwanaksan last week Oct – week 1 Nov peak / Cheonggyesan week 1 – week 2 Nov peak (KNPS 국립공원공단 / KMA 기상청 normals). Foreigners arriving in mid-October frequently miss the foliage and leave. Rule = the golden 3-week window for foliage hikes is Oct 25 – Nov 15.

2. "Can't go without hiking boots" — intro courses are fine with sneakers

30%+ of foreigners give up thinking "hiking = hiking boots required." Actually Cheonggyesan Okneobong 옥녀봉 (2) · Gwanaksan Nakseongdae 낙성대 Hoamsan 호암산 (8) = sneakers OK intro trails. However: fitness 2+ = trekking shoes recommended (Yetgol Maebong 매봉 1 · Gwacheon 과천 6) / fitness 3 = hiking boots required (Isubong 이수봉 3 · Seoul Nat'l Univ 4 · Sillim 신림 5 · Sadang 사당 7). Korean Daiso 다이소 · Olive Young 올리브영 · outdoor shops = ₩50,000–150,000 (foreigners can buy hiking boots same day).

3. "No food at the summit" — Maebong 매봉 · Yeonjudae 연주대 have kiosks and springs

Many foreigners assume "summit = nothing → go empty-handed." Actually Cheonggyesan Maebong 매봉 = kiosk + toilet / Gwanaksan Yeonjudae 연주대 = Yeonjuam 연주암 spring + small kiosk / Cheonggyesan Yetgol 옛골 = post-hike chicken-and-pajeon-and-makgeolli village (foreign signature). Rule = summit food + post-hike village = #1 foreign hiking-vlog signature.

2. Week 1 Nov (Nov 1 – 7) — Gwanaksan 관악산 + Cheonggyesan Maebong 매봉 simultaneous peak: foreign autumn vlog viral 2 weeks begin · golden 7 days.

💡 Foreign Autumn Hiking Tips 5

1. Arrive about a week before peak foliage: foreign first Korean autumn = arrive last week Oct + hike week 1 Nov recommended.

2. Buy hiking / trekking shoes in Korea: Daiso 다이소 (sneakers) · Olive Young 올리브영 (trekking) · outdoor specialty shops (hiking boots) · some English sizing help.

FAQ 6

Q1. Foreigner: Cheonggyesan 청계산 vs Gwanaksan 관악산 recommendation?

A. First hike = Cheonggyesan (fitness 1–2) · serious hike = Gwanaksan (fitness 3 + temple view). Peak foliage = Cheonggyesan week 1–2 Nov / Gwanaksan last week Oct – week 1 Nov.

Q2. Foreigner: sneakers OK?

A. Intro courses (Okneobong 옥녀봉 · Nakseongdae 낙성대 · Hoamsan 호암산) = sneakers OK. Fitness 2+ (Yetgol Maebong 매봉 · Gwacheon 과천) = trekking shoes recommended. Fitness 3 (Isubong 이수봉 · Seoul Nat'l Univ · Sillim 신림 · Sadang 사당) = hiking boots required.

Q3. Foreigner: English info at trailheads?

A. Partial English. Cheonggyesan-ipgu Stn 청계산입구역 / Gwanaksan 관악산 trailheads = English signage + partial QR Korean / English trail maps. Mobile hiking apps with GPS recommended.

Q4. Food at the summit?

A. Cheonggyesan Maebong 매봉 = kiosk + toilet / Gwanaksan Yeonjudae 연주대 = Yeonjuam 연주암 spring + small kiosk / generally seasonal summit kiosks (autumn = open) · weekdays may close partly.

Q5. Autumn rain?

A. Autumn rain = slippery + lower perceived temp. Rain jacket + change of clothes required. On rainy days = #903 monsoon indoor or #905 heatwave cool-route indoor alternatives.

Q6. Foreign hiker accident / emergency?

A. Korea National Park Service hiking safety = 119 hiking-incident English OK. In emergency = call 119 + share location (hiking-app GPS). See #405 foreign-tourist emergency medical full guide.
