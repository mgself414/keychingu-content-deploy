🎯 Why a Deopbap 4-Channel Comparison Guide

Even if you know the #891 Deopbap 6-Type Matrix + #892 Workflow — channel selection is the next decision. Korean deopbap (덮밥, rice bowl) channels = (1) chain dosirak (도시락, lunch box) shops (Hansot (한솥) · Bon Dosirak (본도시락), value #1) · (2) MZ Korean restaurants (myeongran (명란, pollock roe) · salmon, Instagram #1) · (3) home-style baekban (백반) diners (home-style · #854 cross) · (4) hanjeongsik (한정식, Korean full-course) (beef, luxury) — 4 in total.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. "The 4 channels = similar": Completely different. Value = chain / MZ = MZ / family = home-style / luxury = hanjeongsik.

2. "Chain = fast food": Chain = authentic Korean food at great value.

3. "MZ = instant": MZ = expect a waiting line.

4. "Home-style = only expensive": Home-style = ₩8–15k (~$6–11.25), great value.

5. "Hanjeongsik = bibimbap (비빔밥)": Hanjeongsik = a beef deopbap course is possible.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. First channel: Chain (#1 value) → MZ (#1 trend).

2. Instagram: MZ (#1).

3. MZ: MZ (#1).

4. Family: Home-style (#1).

5. Budget: Chain (#1).

6. Allergies · pregnancy: See #891.

7. Payment: Card OK · kiosks in English.

8. VAT: No refund.

⚠ Medical · Safety · Dietary Disclaimer

All 4 deopbap channels serve ordinary food. Declare allergies in advance. ☎ 1339.

Dietary restriction fallback: Halal · kosher = fish OK / vegetarian = these are non-vegetarian dishes / gluten-free = ask for Tamari.
