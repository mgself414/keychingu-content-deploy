23_Itaewon World Food Tour

One-Line Intro

Itaewon (이태원) is Seoul's most international neighborhood — eat your way around the world in one street.

From Middle Eastern and Indian to Mexican and American BBQ, it's all here on a single strip.

Who This Is For

  • Travelers looking for diverse options beyond Korean cuisine
  • Vegetarians and Muslim travelers who need halal-friendly dining

Recommended Course (3 Hours)

1. 12:00 — Itaewon Station (이태원역) Exit 3 (Free): Enter Halal Street (이슬람 거리), grab Turkish baklava or a kebab as a snack (₩3,000–6,000)

2. 12:30 — Halal Street Lunch (₩12,000–16,000): Lamb kebab or curry at a halal restaurant

3. 14:00 — Stroll up Usadanno Hill (우사단로) (Free): Gyeongridangil (경리단길) vibes — indie cafés and select shops

4. 14:30 — Indian Restaurant Dessert Stop (₩4,000–6,000): Mango lassi or masala chai

5. 15:30 — Antique Street Shopping (이태원 앤틱 거리) (Prices vary): Vintage clothing and antique goods

Tips for International Visitors

  • Language: Itaewon (이태원) has the highest English proficiency rate in Seoul — you can get by almost entirely in English.
  • Halal check: Just ask, "Is this halal?" — most restaurants on Halal Street (이슬람 거리) will answer yes.
  • Safety note: The club street behind Hamilton Hotel (해밀턴 호텔) gets extremely crowded at night. Keep a close eye on your belongings.
  • Payment: Cards accepted almost everywhere. Smaller halal eateries may prefer cash — bring some just in case.
  • Getting here: From Itaewon Station (이태원역, Line 6) Exit 3, all major spots are a 5–15 minute walk.

Simple English Summary

Itaewon (이태원) is Seoul's most international district, with restaurants from 30+ countries within easy walking distance. The Halal Street (이슬람 거리) near Exit 3 is a must for Muslim travelers — Turkish, Pakistani, and Middle Eastern food is all here. Vegetarians have many options at the Indian restaurant cluster on Usadanno (우사단로) hill. Nearly all staff in the area speak English, making it the easiest Seoul neighborhood to navigate without Korean.

Estimated Total Cost

  • Budget: ₩20,000 (Halal Street lunch + dessert + subway)
  • Standard: ₩45,000 (lunch + café + light shopping)
  • Splurge: ₩80,000 (international restaurant dinner + cocktail bar)
