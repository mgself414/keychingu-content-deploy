⚠ 3 Common Autumn Dessert Mistakes

1. "Autumn = only foliage" — Unaware of dessert fit: Only foliage. In reality, it's a full experience of Persimmon (Gam, 감) (Dangam (단감), Bansi (반시), Hongsi (홍시), Gotgam (곶감)), Chestnut (Bam, 밤) (Gongju (공주), Cheongyang (청양)), Pumpkin (Hobak, 호박) (Danhobak (단호박), Halloween), Sweet Potato (Goguma, 고구마) (Gungoguma (군고구마, roasted sweet potato)) + dessert fusion.

2. "Persimmon = just a fruit" — Unaware of dessert diversity: Just a fruit. In reality, there are Dangam (단감, crisp persimmon) (September-November), Bansi (반시, flat semi-dried persimmon) (October), Hongsi (홍시, soft-ripe persimmon) (November after frost), Gotgam (곶감, dried persimmon) (December~Sangju (상주)) — 4 seasonal types + Gotgam (곶감) cake, Hongsi (홍시) Bingsu (빙수, shaved ice dessert).

3. "Autumn desserts = only Korean style" — Unaware of Halloween/pumpkin fit: Only Korean style. In reality, there are October Halloween pumpkin desserts (#451 Theme Park Lotte World (롯데월드) Halloween #1 for foreign enthusiasts, Starbucks global pumpkin latte).

🍯 5 Tips for Foreigners

1. Persimmon (Gam, 감) Season = September-December — Dangam (단감, crisp persimmon) (September-November), Hongsi (홍시, soft-ripe persimmon) (November after frost), Gotgam (곶감, dried persimmon) (December~Sangju (상주))

2. Chestnut (Bam, 밤) = Gongju (공주), Cheongyang (청양) — Korea's #1 chestnut cluster, limited to September-October season

3. Pumpkin (Hobak, 호박) = Halloween October — #451 Theme Park Lotte World (롯데월드) Halloween, Starbucks pumpkin latte

4. Gungoguma (군고구마, roasted sweet potato) = Street Food #1 — Autumn start~winter street food, MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) enthusiasts

5. #423 Autumn Linkage — Combine Seorak (설악) foliage, Bongpyeong (봉평) buckwheat flowers, Jeongseon (정선) silver grass with autumn dessert itineraries
