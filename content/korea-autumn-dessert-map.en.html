Korea Autumn Dessert Guide — 15 Chestnut, Persimmon, Sweet Pumpkin and Sweet Potato Dessert Spots (Bakery Bammaeul, Suyeonsanbang, Cafe Haeyoung, Haenam Sweet Potato Bread Financier) in Seoul and Producing Regions (Official Values, October 2026)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Open the paper bag and the smell of roasted sweet potato rises up; the bread in your hand is heavy like a real sweet potato — Haenam Sweet Potato Bread (해남고구마빵) is shaped exactly like one (VisitKorea). This guide covers shops that sell desserts made with chestnut, persimmon (hongsi, soft ripe persimmon, and gotgam, dried persimmon), sweet pumpkin and sweet potato. In each group, places you can eat at in Seoul come first, and the producing regions (Gongju, Yeongdong, Cheongdo, Sangju, Haenam) come after. Every shop is listed in the Korea Tourism Organization's tourism data (VisitKorea, TourAPI) with a menu item using that ingredient.

The menu items here are regular menu items listed in the VisitKorea menu field, and none is marked "autumn only." The old guide's chain-store and hotel autumn-limited desserts were dropped because no 2026 official announcement could be confirmed, and the ranking language was deleted because it had no source. Street roasted sweet potatoes in winter are in #476 Winter Snack Guide, and rice cakes (tteok) are in #473 Tteok Guide.

🌰 Chestnut — 2 in Seoul, 2 in Gongju

  • Lazy Youth (레이지 유스) — 1F, 22 Muhak-ro 6-gil (무학로6길 22), Seongdong-gu, Seoul (Doseon-dong, 도선동). 12:00-24:00 (last order 23:10), open every day. The signature chestnut Basque cheesecake is made without starch or wheat flour. Takeout available. Tel 02-6470-7770 (VisitKorea)
  • Cafe Miu (카페미우) — 1F, 7 Saechang-ro 14-gil (새창로14길 7), Yongsan-gu, Seoul (near Hyochang Park Station, 효창공원역). Weekdays 09:00-19:00, weekends 11:00-21:00, closed Mon. A hand-drip specialty cafe that serves candied chestnuts. Tel 02-813-0315 (VisitKorea)
  • Bakery Bammaeul (베이커리 밤마을) — 5-9 Baekmigoeul-gil (백미고을길 5-9), Gongju-si, Chungnam (Geumseong-dong, 금성동). 09:00-21:00, open every day. The signature item is the Bammaeul pie made with Gongju's local chestnuts, plus chestnut eclairs, chestnut cream cheese tarts and chestnut madeleines. Boxed packaging for sale, cards accepted, free parking. Close to Gongsanseong Fortress (공산성). Tel 041-853-3489 (VisitKorea)
  • Local Coffee (로컬커피) — 55-7 Yongdang-gil (용당길 55-7), Gongju-si, Chungnam (Gyo-dong, 교동). The listing gives Mon-Sat 12:00-21:00 and Sun 12:00-18:00 together with "closed every Monday" — call to check on Mondays. A cafe made from an old house built in 1943, with the chestnut latte as its signature item. Tel 0507-1331-4707 (VisitKorea)

🍊 Persimmon (Hongsi and Gotgam) — 1 in Seoul, 3 in Producing Regions

  • Soul (소울) — 50 Jahamun-ro (자하문로 50), Jongno-gu, Seoul (Changseong-dong, 창성동). 11:30-21:00 (last order 20:30), closed Tue-Wed. A rice dessert shop that uses rice flour. It has gotgam-mari (a roll of dried persimmon, 곶감말이), and also serves red bean paste cream seolgi (steamed rice cake), Gaeseong juak (fried glutinous rice pastry soaked in syrup) and soft rice cake. Tel 0507-1318-5194 (VisitKorea)
  • Cafe Haeyoung (카페 해영) — 34-4 Oeman 1-gil (외만1길 34-4), Yanggang-myeon, Yeongdong-gun, Chungbuk. 11:00-18:00 (last order 17:30), closed Tue-Wed (open if it falls on a public holiday). A hanok (traditional Korean house) cafe converted from a country house; it cooks persimmon puree and persimmon jam from its own Yeongdong dried persimmons. The signature items are the Yeongdong dried persimmon latte, cheese dried persimmon jar and dried persimmon jam butter croffle, and the persimmon jam and cheese dried persimmon jar can be bought separately. Tel 043-744-4888 (VisitKorea)
  • Deoksan Bangjik (덕산방직) — 6-12 Deoksan 1-gil (덕산1길 6-12), Maejeon-myeon, Cheongdo-gun, Gyeongbuk. Weekdays 10:30-19:00, weekends 10:30-20:00 (last order 30 minutes before closing), open every day. A cafe converted from a 1990s textile factory, serving a soft persimmon latte (hongsi latte) and soft persimmon smoothie made with Cheongdo's local hongsi. Tel 0507-1347-0618 (VisitKorea)
  • Sangju Dried Persimmon Distribution Center, Sangju Dried Persimmon Direct Sales Center (상주곶감유통센터 상주곶감직판장) — 1154 Yeongnamjeil-ro (영남제일로 1154), Sangju-si, Gyeongbuk. 08:00-19:00, open every day. An agricultural cooperative formed in 2005 with investment from more than 600 Sangju dried persimmon farms. It sells dried persimmons, dried persimmon slices, dried persimmon chapssal-tteok (glutinous rice cake), dried persimmon yeot (traditional hard candy), dried persimmon yakgwa and persimmon vinegar. Tel 054-536-0907 (VisitKorea)

🎃 Sweet Pumpkin — 3 in Seoul

  • Suyeonsanbang (수연산방) — 8 Seongbuk-ro 26-gil (성북로26길 8), Seongbuk-gu, Seoul (Seongbuk-dong, 성북동). Wed-Fri 11:30-17:50 (last order 17:00), Sat 11:30-20:00 (last order 19:10), Sun 11:30-19:50 (last order 19:00), closed Mon-Tue. A traditional teahouse run by the granddaughter of Yi Tae-jun in the Seoul Folk Cultural Heritage house "Sanghyeo Yi Tae-jun's House." The signature item is sweet pumpkin bingsu (shaved ice), and it also has danhobak beombeok (sweet pumpkin mash). Tel 02-764-1736 (VisitKorea). For a walking route through the Seongbuk-dong alleys see #1345 Alley Walk 6: Seongbuk-dong
  • Magpie & Tiger Seongsu Tea Room (맥파이앤타이거 성수티룸) — 97 Seongsui-ro (성수이로 97), Seongdong-gu, Seoul. 11:00-21:30, open every day. A tea room serving tea and desserts, with sweet pumpkin pound cake on the menu. Tel 0507-1306-1629 (VisitKorea)
  • Milpani (밀파니) — 273 Wirye-seoro (위례서로 273), Songpa-gu, Seoul (Geoyeo-dong, 거여동). 09:00-21:30, open every day. A bakery cafe with sweet pumpkin mochi bread on the menu; its sweet-potato-shaped sweet potato bread is introduced as the signature item. Tel 0507-1480-0824 (VisitKorea)

🍠 Sweet Potato — 2 in Seoul, 2 in Haenam

  • Need Sweet (니드스윗) — 9 Achasan-ro 1-gil (아차산로1길 9), Seongdong-gu, Seoul (near Exit 1 of Ttukseom Station, 뚝섬역). 12:00-22:00, open every day. A cake cafe that makes every menu item in house, with a Real Sweet Potato Cheesecake. Reservations and takeout available. Tel 0507-1467-7864 (VisitKorea)
  • Plaid Cafe Seoul Forest (플래드카페 서울숲) — 12-7 Seoulsup 2-gil (서울숲2길 12-7), Seongdong-gu, Seoul. Daily 11:00-21:00. Sweet potato tiramisu is on the menu. Takeout available. Tel 0507-1356-8511 (VisitKorea)
  • Haenam Sweet Potato Bread Financier (해남고구마빵 피낭시에) — 8 Eupnae-gil (읍내길 8), Haenam-gun, Jeonnam. 09:30-20:00, closes early if the Haenam sweet potato bread sells out. Closed on the days of Seollal (Lunar New Year) and Chuseok. The sweet-potato-shaped Haenam sweet potato bread is made with Haenam sweet potatoes and Haenam rice instead of wheat flour. It also has sweet potato tarts and honey sweet potato financiers. It is made fresh each day, so refrigerate or freeze it after buying. No parking. Tel 0507-1393-6262 (VisitKorea)
  • Mongsol Bakery Sweet Potato Bread (몽소베이커리 고구마빵) — Unit 102, Jeongha Medical Center, 5 Nambusunhwan-ro (남부순환로 5), Haenam-eup, Haenam-gun, Jeonnam. 07:30-22:00, closed Sun. A natural sourdough bakery serving chal-goguma bread (chewy sweet potato bread) filled with Haenam sweet potato paste and a sweet potato brioche. Parking lot in front of the shop, delivery orders available. Tel 061-533-0085 (VisitKorea)

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Price range: None of the 15 shops has a price in its VisitKorea listing. Prices not published — check by phone or at the shop's menu board
  • Gluten: Places introduced as using something other than wheat flour are Lazy Youth's chestnut Basque cheesecake (no starch or wheat flour), Soul (rice flour) and the sweet potato bread at Haenam Sweet Potato Bread Financier (sweet potato and rice) (VisitKorea). Other items at the same shop may contain wheat
  • Vegetarian: Cheesecake, tiramisu and croffles contain dairy, and pies and tarts usually contain butter and eggs — if you are vegan, ask before ordering
  • Alcohol: The gotgam makgeolli (dried persimmon rice wine, 곶감막걸리) sold at Sangju Dried Persimmon Direct Sales Center is alcohol (VisitKorea)
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Itaewon's Eid (이드) (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil, Yongsan-gu, daily 12:00-21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF). Certification can change, so check before you visit. None of the shops in this guide carries a halal label
  • For show-and-tell cards see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Autumn Dessert Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Ordering, Getting Around

  • Solo: Slices of cake and single breads are one-person portions, so going alone is easy. For a map of places to go alone, see #348 Seoul Solo Diner Map
  • Reservations: The place listed as accepting reservations is Need Sweet (VisitKorea). For the others, just walk in. The sweet potato bread at the Financier closes the shop once it sells out, so go in the morning
  • Payment: The place listed as accepting cards is Bakery Bammaeul (VisitKorea). For cards and mobile payment, see #527 Korea Payment Systems
  • Ordering: Many cafes have you order at the counter and then hand you a vibrating pager — #1288 Cafe Pager Manual. When you cannot make yourself understood, see #1339 Ordering Etiquette
  • Getting around: In Seoul, see #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards. For Gongju, Yeongdong, Cheongdo, Sangju and Haenam, see #1297 Express and Intercity Bus Manual and #1287 Riding the KTX for the First Time

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Dried persimmon festivals are held in autumn" — The past editions in VisitKorea's festival information are the Nonsan Yangchon Dried Persimmon Festival 2025-12-12 to 14, the Haman Dried Persimmon Festival 2026-01-23 to 25 and the Yeongdong Dried Persimmon Festival 2026-01-30 to 02-01. Dates for the 2026-27 editions have not been posted yet — check the seasonal announcements

2. "You have to go all the way to Gongju for chestnut desserts" — Seoul also has places with chestnut items listed (Lazy Youth and Cafe Miu, VisitKorea)

3. "Country cafes are open every day" — Cafe Haeyoung is closed Tue-Wed and Mongsol Bakery on Sunday. Seoul's Soul (Tue-Wed) and Suyeonsanbang (Mon-Tue) also close on weekdays (VisitKorea)

4. "Last year's autumn-only menu returns this year" — This edition could not confirm any official announcement of 2026 autumn-limited menus at chains or cafes. Check the shop's notice before you go
