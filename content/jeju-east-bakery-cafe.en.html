One Line of Bakeries on Jeju's East Coast — From Boribbang and Manong Baguette to Six Spots in Gimnyeong

Subtitle: There are already plenty of "Jeju ocean-view cafe" lists. This piece looks the other way — it strings together the bakeries and dessert shops Jeju locals actually queue for along one line of the east coast road. The original house of Jeju Boribbang (보리빵, barley bread), the Manong Baguette (마농바게트) — manong (마농) is the Jeju word for garlic — and a cluster that surprised us when we pinned it on the map: six spots within 1 km in Gimnyeong-ri (김녕리). Plus the trap of closing days that split across Tue, Wed, and Thu

🎯 Why "Bread" Instead of "Ocean View"

Search for cafes on Jeju's east side and the results all lean one way: windows onto the sea, white buildings, emerald Woljeongri (월정리). They are good cafes, but whichever list you open, the same places come up.

This piece changes the axis. Our baseline was the reviews piled up on a gourmet rating platform, and what that data left standing on the east side of Jeju wasn't ocean-view cafes but bakeries and dessert specialists. The original house of Jeju Boribbang (보리빵, barley bread), a baguette made with manong (마농), the Jeju word for garlic, a chocolate specialist that won a Blue Ribbon three years in a row — that kind of place. They rarely show up on tourist cafe lists.

Once we pinned everything on the map, one more thing appeared. In Gimnyeong-ri (김녕리), Gujwa-eup (구좌읍), there are six cafes and dessert shops within a 1 km radius. Gimnyeong (김녕) isn't as well known a name as Hamdeok (함덕) or Woljeongri (월정리), but for density you can hop around on foot, it is the tightest on the east side.

So the structure here is simple. Sinchon (신촌) → Hamdeok (함덕) → Gimnyeong (김녕) → Woljeongri (월정리) · Songdang (송당) → Sehwa (세화) → Seongsan (성산). One line along Jeju's east coast road from west to east, and wherever you start, it connects to the next stop.

⚠ Valid as of: confirmed end of September 2026. East Jeju cafes commonly close on a specific weekday and some cut hours in winter — call ahead for anything marked "Unconfirmed."

🌾 Sinchon (신촌) — The Original Jeju Boribbang, and They Sell Only Three Things

Sinchon Deokindang (신촌덕인당) (Sinbuk-ro 36, 신북로 36, Jocheon-eup, 조천읍) is the name that comes up first when people talk about Jeju Boribbang (보리빵, barley bread). It sells only three items: old-style red bean barley bread (옛날팥보리빵), old-style barley bread (옛날보리빵), and mugwort steamed bun (쑥찐빵).

Why boribbang counts as Jeju food comes down to the land. Jeju is a volcanic island where water drains away fast, so rice paddies were hard to farm, and barley was the staple grain instead. The grain Jeju people ate before rice became common survives, as is, in bread. Unlike wheat bread it doesn't puff up — it is dense and mild. If you expect a flashy dessert you'll be disappointed; if you're going to taste an old grain, it's the right place.

09:00–17:00, closed 2nd and 4th Sundays. Arrive late in the afternoon and the popular items may be gone.

🥖 Hamdeok (함덕) — Manong Is Jeju for Garlic

The signature item at Odrang Bakery Hamdeok branch (오드랑베이커리 함덕점) (Johamhaean-ro 552-3, 조함해안로 552-3, Jocheon-eup, 조천읍) is the Manong Baguette (마농바게트). Manong (마농) is the Jeju word for garlic — on a standard-Korean menu it would have been written as "garlic baguette" (마늘바게트). The egg tart is another item that gets mentioned alongside it.

The reason this shop is the most useful one in this piece is time. 07:00–22:00, open year-round — the only place on this list open from early morning to night. Use it as your anchor when you start the east side early in the morning or finish late. It sits on the Hamdeok Beach coast road, so it is within the same radius as the beach.

One block into the alley behind the beach are Danish (다니쉬) (Hamdeok 16-gil 56, 함덕16길 56) and Bora Jibung (보라지붕) (Hamdeok 19-gil 6, 함덕19길 6). The two are within walking distance of each other.

☕ Gimnyeong (김녕) — Six Spots Within 1 km

This is the discovery of this piece. In Gimnyeong-ri (김녕리), Gujwa-eup (구좌읍), there are six cafes and dessert shops within a 1 km radius. Park once and tour them on foot.

  • Guau Chocolat (구아우쇼콜라) (Gimnyeonghang 2-gil 14, 김녕항2길 14) — a chocolate specialist. Won a Blue Ribbon three years in a row. 10:30–17:00, closed Tue and Wed — with two closing days, check the weekday first if this shop is your target.
  • Badaneun Gimnyeong (바다는김녕) (Gimnyeong-ro 21-gil 25, 김녕로21길 25) — a brunch cafe. You can see the sea from the window. 09:00–17:00, closed Thu. It opens the same hours in November to February too.
  • Kkamang Dog (까망도그) (Gimnyeong-ro 21-gil 23, 김녕로21길 23) — the same street as Badaneun Gimnyeong, the very next street number.
  • The Moment Gimnyeong (더모먼트김녕) (Gimnyeong-ro 22-gil 3, 김녕로22길 3) — 1F and 2F.
  • Gimnyeong-e Saneun Gim Yeonghun (김녕에사는김영훈) (Gimnyeong-ro 6-gil 2, 김녕로6길 2) — the name is the sign, literally ("Kim Yeonghun who lives in Gimnyeong").
  • Cheongchundang Chapssal Kkwabaegi Gimnyeong branch (청춘당찹쌀꽈배기 김녕점) (Gimnyeong-ro 70-1, 김녕로 70-1) — glutinous rice twisted doughnuts.

Two shops sit side by side on Gimnyeong-ro 21-gil (김녕로21길), and 22-gil (김녕로22길) is right next to it, so walking just 21-gil and 22-gil gets you three shops. If one is closed, you move next door — this is a stretch where the different closing days actually work in your favor.

🍮 Woljeongri (월정리) · Songdang (송당) — One Block Behind the Beach

The row facing Woljeongri Beach already has well-known ocean-view cafes. What this piece adds is what's behind them.

  • Oneuldo Hwachang (오늘도화창) (Woljeong 5-gil 56, 월정5길 56, Gujwa-eup, 구좌읍) — a white house with a green roof. It sits in an alley set back from Woljeongri Beach.
  • Ujeongwon Bakery (우정원베이커리) (Songdangdong 2-gil 7-39, 송당동2길 7-39, Gujwa-eup, 구좌읍) — Songdang (송당) isn't on the coast; it's in the mid-mountain area (중산간). Easy to slot in on a day you're heading to Bijarim Forest (비자림) or Yongnunni Oreum (용눈이오름).

🌅 Sehwa (세화) · Seongsan (성산) — A View of Udo, a View of Ilchulbong

  • Cafe Hallasan (카페한라산) (Myeonsu 1-gil 48, 면수1길 48, Gujwa-eup, 구좌읍) — on the Sehwa side.
  • Orrrn (오른) (Haemaji-haean-ro 2714, 해맞이해안로 2714, Seongsan-eup, 성산읍) — sit along the coast road with a view of Udo (우도). 10:30–20:00 (last order 19:30), until 19:00 in winter, closed 2nd and 4th Wednesdays. One of the few places on this list open into the evening.
  • Fritz Jeju Seongsan branch (프릳츠 제주성산점) (Ilchul-ro 222, 일출로 222, Seongsan-eup, 성산읍) — the Jeju branch of a coffee brand that started in Seoul. It has a rooftop with a view of Seongsan Ilchulbong (성산일출봉). It opens at 08:00, so it fits as breakfast after coming down from the sunrise peak. Closing time differs by source — 19:00 or 20:00.
  • Seongsan Gwajajeom (성산과자점) (Ilchul-ro 286, 일출로 286, Seongsan-eup, 성산읍) — the same Ilchul-ro (일출로) as Fritz, a few street numbers apart. You can walk between the two.

💰 Budget and Vegetarian — Two Things to Know First

Prices split into three tiers. The figures below are not menu prices but the average spend per person recorded by Revit Guide (레빗가이드) users — they shift with how much you buy, so use them only to tell the tiers apart.

  • Grab bread and go (around ₩2,000) — Sinchon Deokindang (신촌덕인당) about ₩1,960, Cheongchundang Chapssal Kkwabaegi (청춘당찹쌀꽈배기) about ₩2,400. Barley bread and twisted doughnuts are cheap per piece, so picking up several barely adds up.
  • A drink and a slice (₩6,000–13,000) — Seongsan Gwajajeom (성산과자점) about ₩6,000, Gimnyeong-e Saneun Kim Younghoon (김녕에사는김영훈) about ₩7,200, Danish (다니쉬) about ₩7,900, Orrrn (오른) about ₩8,250, Fritz (프릳츠) about ₩8,400, Odrang Bakery (오드랑베이커리) about ₩9,400, Ujeongwon Bakery (우정원베이커리) about ₩9,800, The Moment Gimnyeong (더모먼트김녕) about ₩10,500, Guau Chocolat (구아우쇼콜라) about ₩11,700, Oneuldo Hwachang (오늘도화창) about ₩11,800, Cafe Hallasan (카페한라산) and Borajibung (보라지붕) around ₩13,000.
  • Sit-down brunch (₩20,000s) — Bada-neun Gimnyeong (바다는김녕) about ₩21,600, the only full meal on this list.

Vegetarian is easier here than in the soup piece (#1406). There, pork broth was the heart of every dish, so there was no way around it. These are breads and desserts, so no meat stock is involved. In particular, the three items at Sinchon Deokindang (신촌덕인당) — red-bean barley bread, plain barley bread and mugwort steamed bun — are built on red bean, mugwort and barley.

That said, you must ask each shop about butter, milk and eggs — most bakeries use dairy, and none of the shops in the table is vegan-only or halal-certified. If you keep strict vegan or halal, treat asking about ingredients as a given. The same applies to desserts that may contain gelatin (mousses and jellies).

🚗 Route — One Line Along the Coast Road

Jeju's east side is connected by a single coast road, so the order sets itself. What follows assumes a rental car; without one, also see #1212 (on foot/bus) and #1402 (taxi tour).

From Jeju City heading east

1. Sinchon (신촌) — Sinchon Deokindang (opens 09:00). The closest first stop from Jeju City.

2. Hamdeok (함덕) (about 10 minutes by car from Sinchon) — Odrang Bakery. Same radius as Hamdeok Beach. Danish and Bora Jibung in the alley are on foot.

3. Gimnyeong (김녕) (about 10 minutes from Hamdeok) — park here and go on foot through 21-gil and 22-gil. Pick two shops that match the closing days.

4. Woljeongri (월정리) (about 10 minutes from Gimnyeong) — Oneuldo Hwachang. The beach is the front row; this shop is in the back alley.

5. Sehwa (세화) (about 10 minutes from Woljeongri) — Cafe Hallasan.

6. Seongsan (성산) (about 15 minutes from Sehwa) — Orrrn or Fritz. Pair it with your Ilchulbong plans.

A variation that adds the mid-mountain area: from Gimnyeong or Woljeongri, turn south and pass through Songdang (송당) (Ujeongwon Bakery) to link with Bijarim Forest and Yongnunni Oreum.

Reverse order — morning Ilchulbong, then back west also works. Fritz (08:00) → Orrrn (10:30) → Sehwa → Gimnyeong → Hamdeok → Sinchon. But Sinchon Deokindang closes at 17:00, so leaving it as the last stop makes for a tight schedule.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Going to Gimnyeong without checking closing days — Guau Chocolat is closed Tue and Wed, and Badaneun Gimnyeong is closed Thu. On a day you're targeting Gimnyeong, pick the weekday first. With six shops clustered you can substitute if one is closed, but if you're going to see one particular shop, it's a wasted trip.

2. Mistaking Deokindang's and Orrrn's biweekly closings for monthly ones — Sinchon Deokindang is closed on the 2nd and 4th Sundays, and Orrrn on the 2nd and 4th Wednesdays. It isn't "closed Sundays" or "closed Wednesdays" — it's the 2nd and 4th of that month.

3. Setting out late in the afternoon — Of the 6 shops with confirmed hours, half close at 17:00 (Deokindang, Guau Chocolat, Badaneun Gimnyeong). The ones open into the evening are Odrang Bakery (22:00), Orrrn (20:00), and Fritz (19:00–20:00). An east-side cafe itinerary should start in the morning by default.

⚙ 3 FAQs

Q. Why are there almost no ocean-view cafes?

We left them out on purpose. The "Jeju ocean-view cafe" lists are already numerous, and whichever you look at, the same places appear. This piece uses a bakery and dessert specialist axis so it doesn't overlap with those lists. If you want a seat with a sea view, Badaneun Gimnyeong, Orrrn, and Fritz Jeju Seongsan branch in this piece meet that condition too.

Q. How many shops can I visit in one day?

Bakeries aren't places you sit at for long, so four or five is manageable. Grouping two or three on foot in Gimnyeong makes the count climb fast. But if you sit down and rest at every cafe, three is realistic.

Q. What do I search for on Naver Map?

Entering the shop name in Korean as written is more accurate. You can also paste the street addresses from the table. Searching by item, such as "Jeju boribbang" (제주 보리빵) or "manong baguette" (마농바게트), also brings up other branches in the same family — Deokindang and Odrang Bakery both have several branches in Jeju, so check main branch versus branch.
