⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Hangang Swimming Pool (한강수영장) = Swimming in the River" — Not knowing it's an outdoor pool

Foreigners search "swim in Hangang River" → try to enter the Hangang River (한강) in their clothes, thinking it's swimming in the river (dangerous, legally prohibited). In reality, Hangang Swimming Pool (한강수영장) is an outdoor pool next to Hangang Park (한강공원) (a purified swimming pool) — operating for free in July-August. Entering the main Hangang River (한강) is prohibited for safety reasons. Searching "Hangang outdoor swimming pool" in English is accurate. Operated at 5 locations: Banpo (반포), Ttukseom (뚝섬), Yeouido (여의도), Jamsil (잠실), and Gwangnaru (광나루). For detailed operating periods, check the official Seoul (서울) city website.

2. "All Waterparks are the Same" — Not knowing the differences between Korea's 4 major waterparks

Foreigners "only know Caribbean Bay (캐리비안베이)" and complain about the price. In reality, Korea has 4 types of waterparks:

  • Caribbean Bay (캐리비안베이) (₩60K+) = Korea's largest, #1 in foreigner recognition, 1-hour wait in July-August
  • Lotte Waterpark (롯데 워터파크) (₩50K) = Gimhae (김해) (Busan (부산) area), can be combined with a Busan (부산) trip
  • Woongjin Playdoci (웅진플레이도시) (₩40-55K) = Closest to Seoul (서울) city, good value for money (가성비)
  • Ocean World (오션월드) (₩55-70K) = Hongcheon (홍천), Gangwon-do (강원도), natural scenery + can be combined with ski season
  • Selection Rule: Value for money (가성비) = Woongjin (웅진) / Full foreigner experience = Caribbean Bay (캐리비안베이) / Busan (부산) combo = Lotte (롯데) / Nature = Ocean World (오션월드)

3. "East Sea and West Sea = Same Sea" — Choosing incorrectly without knowing the differences

The East Sea (동해) (Gangneung (강릉), Sokcho (속초), Busan (부산)) = clear water, surfing, sand beaches / The West Sea (서해) (Eulwangni (을왕리), Yeongjongdo (영종도), Incheon (인천)) = mudflats (갯벌), sunsets, shallow water. When foreigners search "Seoul nearest beach," Eulwangni (을왕리) (West Sea (서해)) is the #1 result — 1.5 hours next to Incheon Airport (인천공항).
