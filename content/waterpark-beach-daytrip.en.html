⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Han River Swimming Pool = swimming in the river" — Not knowing it is an outdoor pool

A foreigner searches for “swim in Hangang River” → tries to swim in the Hangang River with his clothes on, thinking he is swimming in the river (dangerous and legally prohibited). In fact, Han River Swimming Pool is an outdoor pool (purified water pool) next to Hangang Park — Open for free from July to August. Water in the main stream of the Han River is prohibited for safety reasons. "Hangang outdoor swimming pool" English search is accurate. Operated in 5 locations: Banpo, Ttukseom, Yeouido, Jamsil, and Gwangnaru; check the Seoul Metropolitan Government's official website for detailed operation period.

2. "Water parks are the same" — Don't know the difference between the four major water parks in Korea

Foreigners who only know about Caribbean Bay go there and complain that it is expensive. 4 real Korean water parks:

  • Caribbean Bay (60K+) = Korea’s largest, most recognized by foreigners, 1 hour wait between July and August
  • Lotte Water Park (50K) = Gimhae (Busan area), can be combined with Busan trip
  • Woongjin Play City (40~55K) = Close to Seoul city, cost-effective
  • Ocean World (55~70K) = Hongcheon, Gangwon-do, combined with natural scenery + ski season
  • Selection rule: Cost-effectiveness = Woongjin / Foreigner full set = Caribbean Bay / Busan combination = Lotte / Nature = Ocean World

3. "East Sea and West Sea = same sea" — Wrong choice without knowing the difference

East Sea (Gangneung, Sokcho, Busan) = Clear water, surfing, sand beach / West Sea (Eurwangri, Yeongjong Island, Incheon) = Tidal flat, sunset, shallow water. When foreigners search for “Seoul nearest beach,” Eulwangri (West Sea) ranks first — 1.5 hours next to Incheon Airport. However, foreigners expecting a sand beach were disappointed (mainly mud flats). "Korean beach with clean water" = Donghae (Gangneung·Busan), KTX 2-3 hours required. Consider your route in advance when visiting Korea for the first time.

§1. Water park near Seoul (40,000~75,000 won)

1. Caribbean Bay ★ Korea’s largest and most recognized by foreigners.

  • Location: Everland, Yongin, Gyeonggi (Subway Bundang Line + shuttle bus or direct bus to Gangnam Station)
  • Price: 1-day all-day ticket KRW 60,000~75,000 (varies by season, Everland combo + KRW 30,000)
  • Time: 1 hour from Gangnam, Seoul (shuttle bus or car)
  • Signature: Korea’s No. 1 water park globally. Overwhelming exposure to foreign vlogs and Instagram. Multiple wave pools, tube slides, and aqua loops
  • Congestion: 1 hour wait on weekends between July and August. Recommended on weekdays or at the beginning/end of the season (end of June, end of August)
  • Recommended by foreigners: First Korean water park = Caribbean Bay. Everland combo for 2 days and 1 night full set

2. Lotte Water Park (Busan area, day trip possible)

  • Location: Gimhae, Gyeongnam (30 minutes to Busan)
  • Price: 50,000~65,000 won
  • Time: KTX 2.5 hours from Seoul → Gimhae shuttle bus 30 minutes
  • Signature: Busan day trip combination possible. Water Park + Busan Haeundae 1 Night 2 Days Full Set
  • Recommended for foreigners: #1 choice for travelers to Busan. Cooler compared to Caribbean Bay

3. Woongjin Play City (closest to Seoul city)

  • Location: Bucheon, Gyeonggi-do (Bucheon Sports Complex Station, Subway Line 7)
  • Price: KRW 40,000~55,000 (No. 1 cost-effectiveness)
  • Time: 1 hour from Gangnam, Seoul (subway + bus)
  • Signature: Closest water park in Seoul + cost-effectiveness — Recommended short-term day trip for foreigners
  • Recommended for foreigners: Foreigners with a short schedule and limited budget. Overwhelming accessibility to the city center

4. Ocean World (Vivaldi Park, Hongcheon, Gangwon)

  • Location: Hongcheon, Gangwon (2 hours by shuttle bus from Gangnam, Seoul)
  • Price: 55,000~70,000 won
  • Time: Shuttle bus 2 hours
  • Signature: Natural scenery + ski season combined with Vivaldi Park (winter skiing + summer water park). Foreigners enjoying the nature of Gangwon-do
  • Recommended for foreigners: 1 night at Vivaldi Park + Ski season is winter. Summer = Ocean World + Gangwon-do Forest day trip

§2. Free Han River swimming pool (Seoul city center, July-August)

5. Ttukseom Hangang Swimming Pool ★ Foreign vlog signature

  • Location: Ttukseom Hangang Park (3-minute walk from Ttukseom Garden Station on Subway Line 7)
  • Price: Free
  • Time: 30 minutes from downtown Seoul
  • Signature: No. 1 foreign vlog free Han River swimming pool, operated from July to August + Ttukseom Night Market (#356) combined
  • Recommended for foreigners: Han River swimming pool first try = Ttukseom. Night market + food truck + Han River night view full set

6. Banpo Hangang Swimming Pool (fountain show combined)

  • Location: Banpo Hangang Park (10-minute walk from Express Bus Terminal Station on Subway Lines 3, 7, and 9)
  • Price: Free
  • Signature: Banpo Bridge Moonlight Rainbow Fountain Show (#356) Combination = Swimming + Night Market + Fountain Show Triple
  • Recommended for foreigners: Hangang Night Full Set (#356 Hangang Night Market cross-ref)

7. Yeouido Hangang Swimming Pool (63 Building view)

  • Location: Yeouido Hangang Park (5-minute walk from Yeouinaru Station on Subway Line 5)
  • Signature: 63 Building night view + swimming pool, Yeouido Night Market combined (#356)
  • Recommended by foreigners: Instagram “Seoul skyline + outdoor pool” signature

8. Jamsil Hangang Swimming Pool (Lotte World Tower view)

  • Location: Jamsil Hangang Park (10-minute walk from Jamsil Station on subway lines 2 and 8)
  • Signature: Lotte World Tower 555m view + swimming pool = Jamsil Bridge fountain show combined
  • Recommended for foreigners: Jamsil = Lotte World Adventure (#280 rainy day) + Han River swimming pool + Signiel (#357 shaved ice) full set

§3. Beach day trip from Seoul

9. Eulwangri Beach (next to Incheon Airport, West Sea)

  • Location: Yeongjongdo, Incheon (Subway Airport Railroad Incheon Airport Station + Bus or Taxi)
  • Price: Free (Beach) / Restaurants and cafes separate
  • Time: 1.5 hours from Seoul Station (Airport Railroad + 30 minutes by bus)
  • Signature: Closest beach in Seoul + Highly recognized by foreigners (adjacent to Incheon Airport)
  • Recommended for foreigners: A short beach experience on your first visit to Korea. However, tidal flats and shallow water depth (expect sand beach)
  • Combined: Incheon Airport free time + Eurwangri = trans foreigner day trip

10. Gangneung Gyeongpo·Jeongdongjin (KTX 2 hours, Donghae)

  • Location: Gangneung, Gangwon (KTX Gangneung Station)
  • Price: KTX one-way approximately 30,000 won + meals and transportation separate
  • Time: 2 hours by KTX from Seoul Station → 10 minutes within Gangneung city
  • Signature: Clear East Sea water + sand beach + coffee street (Gangneung Anmok Beach). Foreigner East Sea first-try
  • Recommended for foreigners: Jeongdongjin sunrise at dawn → Gyeongpo Beach swimming → Anmok Beach Coffee Street → KTX return = 1-day full set

11. Busan Haeundae day trip (KTX 3 hours)

  • Location: Haeundae, Busan (KTX Busan Station + Subway)
  • Price: KTX one-way approx. 60,000 won
  • Time: 2.5~3 hours by KTX from Seoul Station
  • Signature: Korea’s No.1 Beach + Busan City Full Set (Gwangalli, Jagalchi, Gukje Market)
  • Recommendation for foreigners: A full day trip to Busan for 2 days and 1 night is recommended (a day trip is possible, but it is tight). KTX + 1 night hotel total 200,000~300,000 won

12. Jeju day trip (1 hour flight, 2 days and 1 night recommended)

  • Location: Jeju Island (Gimpo Airport·Incheon Airport → Jeju Airport)
  • Price: Airplane one-way KRW 50,000~150,000 (season/airline)
  • Time: 1 hour by plane (when departing from Seoul Gimpo Airport)
  • Signature: Korea’s No. 1 resort + Hallasan + Beach + Black Pig. Foreigner must-visit
  • Recommendation for foreigners: **Day trip Airline ticket + hotel + rental car total: approximately 300,000~500,000 won

Foreigner friendly tip

1. Language: Caribbean Bay, Lotte Water Park, Haeundae, Gangneung = English OK. Han River Swimming Pool, Woongjin, Eulwangri = Focus on Korean, use Papago

2. Reservation:

  • Water Park = Peak season (weekends from July to August) Online reservation required, immediate entry available on weekdays
  • KTX = SRT·Korail official app (English available), Reservation recommended 2-3 days in advance
  • Airplane = LCCs such as Jin Air, Jeju Air, and T'way are 60-80% cheaper (foreign payment OK)

3. Payment: Water park, KTX, airplane = 100% card / Han River swimming pool = free / Beach restaurant = some cash

4. Swimsuit: Swimwear is required at the Han River swimming pool (shorts and regular clothes are not allowed). Water park tickets can be purchased in-store (foreigners tend to buy expensive items → advance purchase is recommended)

5. Sun protection: Strong UV rays in Korean summer, SPF 50+ required. Convenience store/Olive Young 3,000~15,000 won

6. Tube/Tube Bag: Han River swimming pool has its own tube, water park includes admission fee or separate rental 5,000-10,000 won.

7. Hotels: Busan·Gangneung·Jeju = Many foreigner-friendly hotels (Booking.com·Agoda). Reservation required 1 week in advance during July-August season

8. Halal/Vegan:

  • Restaurants in the water park = Focus on general Korean food, chicken, and ramen, with limited halal and vegan options
  • Beach cities (Busan, Gangneung, Jeju) = 1 to 3 halal and vegan restaurants (Booking search)
  • Refer to #202 Vegan/Halal Guide

Season information

  • Mid-May to June: Han River swimming pool opens (starts in June), water park season opens
  • Mid-July ~ Mid-August: Peak season, 1-hour wait at water park on weekends, beach full.
  • Late August ~ Early September: Last of the season, Hangang Swimming Pool closes in early September.
  • After September: Some water parks until September (Caribbean, etc.), Han River Swimming Pool
  • October to next May: All closed, indoor water parks (Lotte, some) open all year round
