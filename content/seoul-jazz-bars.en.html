① Long-established/1st generation 3 (Korea’s top three jazz)

1. Janus ★ 1st generation Korean jazz vocalist

  • Where: Gwanghwamun area, Jongno-gu (reopening in September 2025)
  • History: 1978 Late Park Seong-yeon, relocation number 6 (Sinchon → Daehakro → Ewha Womans University back gate → Cheongdam → Seocho → Apgujeong → Gwanghwamun)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — ‘Starting point’ of Korean jazz history·Living museum
  • Linked course: Gyeongbokgung Palace night opening → Janus → Walk around Gwanghwamun Square
  • Pro Tip: Park Seong-yeon tribute stage on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, 30 minutes before arrival, Instagram DM, No. 1 foreign mania

2. Even for a thousand years ★ One of Korea’s top three jazz establishments

  • Nakwon Branch: 2nd floor, 134 Supyo-ro, Jongno-gu (Nakwon Musical Instrument Mall)
  • Jonggak Branch: 1st basement and 2nd floor, 6 Samil-daero 15-gil, Jongno-gu
  • Time: 18:00~24:00, daily
  • History: 1987 Daehakro → Jongno (2 stores, Nakwon and Jonggak)
  • Foreigners★: ◎◎ — Korean 1st generation~new artists, daily lineup, friendly for beginners to jazz
  • Linked course: Ikseon-dong Hanok Street → Nakwon Musical Instrument Mall (1979) → Even for a thousand years
  • Pro Tip: After looking around the 1st to 3rd floors of the 1979 Nakwon Musical Instrument Mall, go up for a tour of Korean music history and foreign enthusiasts.

3. All That Jazz ★ Korea’s oldest (oldest) 1976

  • Where: Itaewon-dong, Yongsan-gu (renewed in 2023)·#144 integrated
