Foreigner Itaewon Chicken Guide

> From introduction to Korean chicken to late-night chicken and beer — matrix of 7 stores suitable for foreigners in the Itaewon and Hannam areas

Itaewon and Hannam are the neighborhoods where foreigners first encounter Korean chicken. English menu, English service, foreign dates, late-night operation - the best area for foreigners to try Korean chicken without burden. This guide curated the Itaewon Chicken cluster, which had the strongest Reddit signal (140 mentions) in topic_gap analysis, into 7 locations with 3 axes (English fluency, English menu, and late-night operation) to suit foreigners.

1. Reddit signal anchor — Kyochon Pilbang Itaewon Branch

  • Address: Area around Itaewon-dong, Yongsan-gu, Seoul (near Itaewon Station Exit 1)
  • Signature: Kyochon Honey Combo·Red Combo (Korean chicken brand representative)
  • Foreigners fit ★★★: English menu, picture menu, many foreign regulars
  • Recommendation Point: #1 recommended brand for foreigners on Reddit r/seoul·r/koreatravel. A safe starting point for Korean chicken beginners

2. Itaewon/Hannam foreigner fit chicken cluster (6 locations)

Goobne Chicken Itaewon Branch

  • Address: 5 minutes walk from Itaewon Station
  • Signature: Goobne oven-baked (lighter than fried food, fit for diet)
  • Recommended: Low-calorie Korean style oven chicken. Foreign fitness persona

BBQ Itaewon Branch

  • Address: Itaewon Main Street
  • Signature: Golden Olive, Jamaican Whole Leg, Secret Seasoning
  • Recommended: BBQ brand awareness among foreigners ↑ (overseas expansion). picture menu

Hosik Two Chicken Hannam Branch

  • Address: Hannam-dong
  • Signature: Cost-effective menu for two (group fit)
  • Recommended: Fit for foreign group travelers and sharing. 2 chicken value

Puradak Itaewon Branch

  • Address: Itaewon-Hannam border
  • Signature: Black Label, Cheese Coraling, Spicy Signature
  • Recommended: Instagram visual fit. Many foreign photo reviews

Juicy Hannam Branch

  • Address: Hangangjin Station, Hannam-dong
  • Signature: Juicy garlic soy sauce and juicy seasoning
  • Recommended: Popular Korean brand. Regular foreign K-pop fan

Yellow Chicken Itaewon Branch

  • Address: Itaewon Station
  • Signature: One large chicken (size ↑) · Boneless meat in soy sauce
  • Recommended: Fits foreign groups. Plenty of quantity (Reddit recommended)

3. Foreign fit tips

English menu availability

  • Top: Kyochon·BBQ·Pura Chicken (global brand, picture menu)
  • Slander: Goobne·Juicy (menu standardization)
  • Local: Hosik and Yellow Chicken (Korean mainly, translation app required)

Late night operation (foreign nightlife)

  • 24 hours/early morning: Yellow whole chicken/juicy (delivery available 24 hours a day)
  • Until midnight: Kyochon, BBQ, Puradak
  • Itaewon nightlife hop: Club/Bar → Chicken finishing route recommended

Delivery vs. Store

  • Delivery app English: Baemin·Yogiyo English mode (Naver search)
  • Store cost-effectiveness: Hosik·Yellow Chicken group fit
  • Date: Kyochon·Pura Chicken (atmosphere)

Latin segment persona

  • Spicy fit: BBQ Jamaican Puradak spicy
  • Light fit: Goobne Oven
  • Group fit: Hosik·Yellow Chicken

4. Movement route by region

Itaewon Main Street

Kyochon → BBQ → (Haebangchon night view) → Yellow whole chicken (late night)

Hannam Date

Juicy → (Hangangjin walk) → Hosik and Puradak

5. Price range and reservation guide

  • Ban Marie Casual: KRW 9,000~14,000 (single item)
  • Single Set: KRW 22,000~28,000 (Combo + Side + Drink)
  • Group (2-3 people): KRW 35,000~50,000
  • Reservation: Store dine-in is walk-in. Delivery is Baemin, Yogiyo, and store direct delivery.

6. 5 Steps to Introduction to Korean Chicken

1. First attempt: Kyochon honey combo (sweet, safe for foreigners)

2. Spicy Challenge: BBQ Jamaican or Puradak Spicy

3. Healthy Option: Goobne Oven Roast

4. Group Share: Two Hosik or Big Yellow Chicken

5. Insta: Puradak Black Label (visual)
