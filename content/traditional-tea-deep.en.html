Korea Tea Region Guide: Boseong Daehan Dawon, Korea Tea Museum, Dahyang Grand Festival; Hadong Hwagae Dongcheon Wild Tea Fields, Wild Tea Museum, Wild Tea Culture Festival; Suncheon Traditional Wild Tea Experience Center

Subtitle: Boseong Daehan Dawon (open year-round) · Korea Tea Museum (tea ceremony education, tea making) · Hadong Wild Tea Museum (free admission, tea ceremony experience 5,000 won) · Hwagae Dongcheon Wild Tea Fields (always open) · Tea Cafe Hadong · Suncheon Traditional Wild Tea Experience Center · 2026 Boseong Dahyang Grand Festival and Hadong Wild Tea Culture Festival (5/1~5/5, ended) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places in three of Korea's tea regions, Boseong in Jeonnam, Hwagae in Hadong in Gyeongnam, and Suncheon in Jeonnam, where you can see tea fields, drink tea, and experience the tea ceremony. Each tea field, museum, tea house, and experience center has its opening hours and closed days registered, and some also have fees registered.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Boseong — Daehan Dawon, Korea Tea Museum, and Boseong Dahyang Grand Festival

Daehan Dawon (대한다원) (763-65 Nokcha-ro (녹차로), Boseong-eup (보성읍), Boseong-gun (보성군), ☎ 061-852-4540) is a tea field of more than 300,000 pyeong on a mountain slope overlooking the Bongsan-ri (봉산리) area of Boseong-eup. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it produces high-quality organic green tea, and if you walk through the tea fields and climb a peak 350 m above sea level, you can see a view that stretches out to the sea. Giant cedar trees stand at the entrance. The Korean tea released by the farm is named 'Bongno (봉로)', and some visitors pick tea leaves themselves. It is a filming location for the dramas "Summer Scent (여름향기)," "The Legend of the Blue Sea (푸른 바다의 전설)," and "Yeokjeok (역적)."

  • Opening hours: Mar~Oct 09:00~18:00, Nov~Feb 09:00~17:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available. Large vehicles use the large-vehicle parking lot. Credit cards: Accepted.

Korea Tea Museum (한국차박물관) (775 Nokcha-ro (녹차로), Boseong-eup, Boseong-gun, ☎ 061-852-0918) is a tea museum with 1 basement floor and 5 floors above ground. It is divided into the Tea Culture Hall (차문화관) on the 1st floor, the Tea History Hall (차역사관) on the 2nd floor, and the Tea Life Hall (차생활관) on the 3rd floor, and it has a storage room and exhibition rooms.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~17:00 (last entry 16:30). Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, the day of Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Experiences: Tea ceremony education, tea making, tea food making.
  • Parking: Available.

2026 Boseong Dahyang Grand Festival (2026 보성다향대축제) (49th, ☎ 061-850-5211~4) was held May 1~5, 2026 around the Korea Tea Culture Park (한국차문화공원) and the Boseong tea fields. Its theme was Boseong matcha, the hours were 10:00~19:00, and it was free.

  • Programs: Held were the 90-minute Boseong tea and dessert course 'Afternoon at the Tea Field (Grand Tea Party)', a tea making experience of pan-roasting tea leaves yourself, tea leaf picking, a green tea foot bath experience, a matcha whisking experience, and more.

📍 Hadong Hwagae — Hwagae Dongcheon Wild Tea Fields, Hadong Wild Tea Museum, Tea Cafe Hadong, and Hadong Wild Tea Culture Festival

Hwagae Dongcheon Wild Tea Fields (화개동천 야생차밭) (694 Jeonggeum-ri (정금리), Hwagae-myeon (화개면), Hadong-gun (하동군), ☎ Hadong County Office (하동군청) Tourism Promotion Division (관광진흥과) 055-880-2375) are in Hwagae-myeon, which, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, is the first place in Korea where tea was planted, with 1,200 years of wild tea production history. The tea here was a tribute offering to the king. In the valley of Hwagae-dong (화개동) on Jirisan (지리산), tea plants grow in cloud shapes among the rocks, and this place is one of the 10 Views of Hadong (하동 10경) selected by Hadong County. From a pavilion on a hill near Jeonggeum Tea Farm (정금다원), you can look down on the tea fields.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available.

Hadong Wild Tea Museum (하동야생차박물관) (571-25 Ssanggye-ro (쌍계로), Hwagae-myeon, Hadong-gun, ☎ museum 055-884-2955, experience center 055-884-2896) is a museum built to preserve and carry on the tea culture of Hwagae, known as the place where Korean tea was first planted and where it originated. It offers participatory experience exhibits and tea education.

  • Opening hours: Mar~Oct 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30), Nov~Feb 10:00~17:00 (last entry 16:30). Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, the day of Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Fee: Museum admission free. Tea ceremony experience 5,000 won, tea leaf picking experience 3,000 won, pan-roasting experience 9,000 won, Doncha (돈차) experience 9,000 won.

Hadong Wild Tea Healing Center Tea Cafe Hadong (하동야생차치유관 티카페하동) (571-27 Ssanggye-ro, Hwagae-myeon, Hadong-gun, ☎ 070-4171-8873) is a tea house run directly by Hadong County. It serves drinks and tea food made with tea grown in Hadong. The signature menu item is Jaeksaltang (잭살탕), and there are also Hadong traditional tea (하동전통차), Ujeon (우전) green tea, Byeolcheonji Waffle (별천지와플), and more.

  • Opening hours: Mar~Oct 09:30~18:30, Nov~Feb 09:00~18:00 (last order 17:50). Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, the day of Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Parking: Available. Takeout: Available. Credit cards: Accepted.

2026 Hadong Wild Tea Culture Festival (2026 하동야생차문화축제) (29th, ☎ 055-880-6582~4) was held May 1~5, 2026 around the Hadong Wild Tea Healing Center and the Hadong Wild Tea Museum. It is a government-designated honorary cultural tourism festival, and the hours were 10:00~18:00.

  • Fee: Free admission, some experiences paid. Tea ceremony experience 5,000 won, green tea foot bath therapy (foot bath and fermented tea) 10,000 won, 3-type outdoor experience ticket 5,000 won.
  • Programs: Held were the Sibaeji Heondarye (시배지헌다례), a tea ceremony experience, a tea making experience, a pan-roasted tea experience with foreigners, a tea class, green tea foot bath therapy, a walk on the Byeolcheonji path inside the gourd bottle (호리병속 별천지길), and more.

📍 Suncheon — Suncheon Traditional Wild Tea Experience Center

Suncheon Traditional Wild Tea Experience Center (순천전통야생차체험관) (450-1 Seonamsa-gil (선암사길), Seungju-eup (승주읍), Suncheon-si (순천시), ☎ 061-749-4500) is an experience center on the road to Seonamsa Temple (선암사) on Jogyesan (조계산). You can try a tea ceremony experience and a dasik (다식) experience, making and drinking tea grown in Suncheon yourself, and it also offers hanok (한옥) lodging. Group reservations are available.

  • Opening hours: Varies by experience. Closed days: Every Monday, Seollal and Chuseok holidays.
  • Experiences: Tea ceremony experience, dasik experience, and more.
  • Parking: Available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Many places close on Mondays — Korea Tea Museum, Hadong Wild Tea Museum, Tea Cafe Hadong, and Suncheon Traditional Wild Tea Experience Center are registered as closed every Monday. Daehan Dawon and the Hwagae Dongcheon Wild Tea Fields are open year-round.
  2. They close early in winter — Daehan Dawon is open until 17:00 from November to February, and Hadong Wild Tea Museum until 17:00 (last entry 16:30) from November to February.
  3. The 2026 tea festivals are over — The Boseong Dahyang Grand Festival and the Hadong Wild Tea Culture Festival were both held May 1~5, 2026.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where can I experience the tea ceremony?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, Korea Tea Museum (tea ceremony education), Hadong Wild Tea Museum (tea ceremony experience 5,000 won), and Suncheon Traditional Wild Tea Experience Center (tea ceremony experience).

Q. Which places can I enter for free?

A. Hadong Wild Tea Museum is registered as free admission, and the Hwagae Dongcheon Wild Tea Fields are always open. Experiences at Hadong Wild Tea Museum are 3,000~9,000 won.
