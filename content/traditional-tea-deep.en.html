FAQ 6

Q1. Traditional tea price range? A. 6,000-12,000 won per cup (average of Insa-dong and Bukchon) · 30,000-100,000 won for 100g directly from the producer (depending on grade) · Jeju Osulloc set 18,500 won.

Q2. Where can I make a reservation for the tea ceremony experience? A. Tea Therapy·Heesum·Rakkojae 3-Lecture EN Hands-on Workshop·1-2 hours 50,000-80,000 won·Advance reservation required·Jeju Osulloc Tea Ceremony Class 30,000-35,000 won.

Q3. Tea garden admission fee + recommendation? A. Boseong Daehan Tea Garden 4,000 won·Osulloc free·Hadong Wild Tea Culture Center 4,000 won. First visit = Osulloc (free + EN full set) · Authentic enthusiast = Hadong · 1st place = Boseong.

Q4. Difference between green tea and fermented tea? A. Green tea = 0% oxidized (grassy smell, fresh)·Yellow tea = semi-oxidized, unique to Korea (honey-sweet taste)·Fermented tea = microbial fermentation (30% enhanced nutrition, high GABA)·Black tea = 80-100% oxidized (small quantity produced in Korea).

Q5. EN/JP menu store full set? A. Confirmed: Osulloc (EN/CN/JP)·Traditional Tea Garden (EN)·Tea Drinking Garden (EN staff)·Tea Therapy·Heesum·Rakkojae (EN). Advance confirmation recommended: Old Teahouse, Suyeonsanbang, Chamdajeong, Forty Winks.

Q6. Purchasing souvenir tea? A. Insadong + Boseong + Jeju 1st place·Woojeon (Best product only in April)·Osulloc Duty Free Shop (Incheon Airport)·Chamdajeong herbal tea packaging OK·Bringing in overseas OK (1kg duty free).
