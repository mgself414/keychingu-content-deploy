Locals' Picks ⑩ Southeast Gyeonggi — 24 Eateries Korean Viewers Named, One Train Line Each From Suwon to Yangpyeong

Subtitle: An axis Suwon Hwaseong (#321) and the Line 9 / Bundang piece never covered — the 24 southeast Gyeonggi spots Korean viewers wrote into YouTube comments are scattered one or two per city across 13 cities: Suwon Haenggung-dong, Seongnam Bundang, Yongin Suji and Cheoin, Anyang Indeogwon, Osan and Pyeongtaek, Ansan, Siheung, Hanam, Gwacheon, Uiwang, Namyangju, and Yangpyeong. KeyChinGu checked only existence, address, and business type on Naver — no ratings. Most are one train line from Seoul.

ISSUE 06 · #1391 · 2026-09-18 · Locals' Picks ⑩

🎯 Why "Comment Recommendations" Is the Bar

This corner has one source. A Korean YouTube channel posted a "nationwide matjip" video, viewers left comments naming their neighborhood eateries, and the most systematically organized comments by region got turned into a table of 217 spots. Since it's a list Koreans recommended to Koreans, foreign reviews are nearly nonexistent. This corner's criteria and limits are covered in Part ① (#1382).

KeyChinGu checked only whether the name, address, and business type actually exist on Naver local search (2026-09-17). Of 26 southeast Gyeonggi spots, 24 were confirmed; the 2 that couldn't be confirmed (one near Migeum Station, one in Ansan) were dropped. There are no visit reviews, rankings, or star ratings, and no hours or prices either.

This part's feature is one or two spots per city. With 24 spots scattered across 13 cities, it's less about digging into one city and more about "what's near the station one train line out from Seoul." Line 4's Indeogwon and Beomgye (Anyang, Uiwang, Gwacheon), the Suin-Bundang Line's Pangyo and Moran (Seongnam), the Shinbundang Line's Suji (Yongin), Line 1's Suwon, Osan, and Pyeongtaek, Line 5's Misa (Hanam), and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line's Dosim and Asin (Namyangju, Yangpyeong) — all reachable from Seoul with no transfer or just one.

Hours, closed days, and prices are entirely uncollected — check Naver Place the day you go.

① South Axis — 10 Spots, Down Along Line 1

Suwon, Osan, and Pyeongtaek are connected by one Line 1; Ansan is on Line 4 and the Suin-Bundang Line; Siheung is on the Seohae Line. All reachable from Seoul with no transfer or just one.

  • John & Jin Pizza Pub (존앤진피자펍) · 27 Jeongjo-ro 905-beongil, Paldal-gu, Suwon (수원시 팔달구 정조로905번길 27) · Restaurant · Pizza · Haenggung-dong — in the catchment of Suwon Station (Line 1 / Suin-Bundang Line / KTX) and Suwonsicheong Station (Suin-Bundang Line), bus or walk from either

Haenggung-dong is the neighborhood inside Suwon Hwaseong. It's the only spot here that sits right inside the Hwaseong itinerary (#321, #624).

  • Daeheung Sikdang (대흥식당) · 9-12 Osan-ro 278beon-gil, Osan, Jungbu Sanghoe (오산시 오산로278번길 9-12 중부상회) · Korean · sundae/sundaeguk (순대·순댓국) · Osan Station area
  • Saemal Haejangguk (새말해장국) · 95 Hyeonchung-ro, Osan (오산시 현충로 95) · Korean · gamjatang (감자탕) · downtown Osan
  • Unam Myeongga (운암명가) · 65 Daewon-ro, Osan, Unam Plaza (오산시 대원로 65 운암프라자) · Korean · jjigae/jeongol · downtown Osan

Three spots in Osan. Osan Station and Osandae Station are different stations with similar names — check against your destination.

  • Kimnejip Budae Jjigae (김네집부대찌개) · 15 Jungangsijang-ro 25beon-gil, Pyeongtaek (평택시 중앙시장로25번길 15) · Korean · jjigae/jeongol · Pyeongtaek Jungang Market — Pyeongtaek Station area
  • Manse Haejangguk (만세해장국) · 1754-8 Manse-ro, Pyeongtaek (평택시 만세로 1754-8) · Korean · haejangguk (해장국) · Pyeongtaek's Manse-ro
  • Huimang Sikdang (희망식당) · 35 Anjeongsunhwan-ro 172beon-gil, Paengseong-eup, Pyeongtaek (평택시 팽성읍 안정순환로172번길 35) · Restaurant · Korean · no railway reaches Paengseong-eup — bus transfer from Pyeongtaek Station or Pyeongtaek Bus Terminal

Budae jjigae (부대찌개) is a stew boiled with ham and sausage alongside kimchi, a dish that started around US military bases. Pyeongtaek is one of the cities where that background is most visible.

  • Sigol Sundae (시골순대) · 351 Yongsin-ro, Sangnok-gu, Ansan, Daea Sangga (안산시 상록구 용신로 351 대아상가) · Korean · sundae/sundaeguk · Sangnoksu Station (Line 4) and Handaeap Station (Line 4 / Suin-Bundang Line) area

This is a different shop from the same-named place in Hongdae (#158, #159).

  • Idong Eomma Kimchi Jjigae (이동엄마김치찌개) · 43 Samtaegi 1-gil, Sangnok-gu, Ansan (안산시 상록구 삼태기1길 43) · Korean · Korean set meal (한정식) · same Sangnok-gu
  • Wonjo Daktang (원조닭탕) · 23 Sincheon-ro 44beon-angil, Siheung (시흥시 신천로44번안길 23) · Korean · chicken dishes · Seohae Line's Sincheon Station (not Line 1)

Foreign visitor tip — sundaeguk (순댓국), haejangguk (해장국), gamjatang (감자탕), and gimbap (김밥) can be ordered for one; budae jjigae and kimchi jjigae are often two-serving minimum. Line 1 has both express and local trains, so check for the express when heading to Suwon, Osan, or Pyeongtaek.

② Bundang·Suji Axis — 5 Spots, Pangyo and the Shinbundang Line

Seongnam and Yongin form the new-town axis southeast of Seoul. The Suin-Bundang Line and Shinbundang Line carry most of it; only Yongin's Cheoin-gu is different.

  • Aeham Budae Jjigae (애햄부대찌개) · 27-8 Seongnamdae-ro 997beon-gil, 1F, Jungwon-gu, Seongnam (성남시 중원구 성남대로997번길 27-8 1층) · Korean · jjigae/jeongol · Moran Station (Line 8 / Suin-Bundang Line transfer) area

Moran is Line 8's terminus and a Bundang Line transfer station. The Line 8 extension to Pangyo is only at the plan-approval stage and hasn't broken ground yet — for now, Line 8 ends at Moran.

  • Yunmirwon (윤밀원) · 154 Baekhyeon-ro, Bundang-gu, Seongnam (성남시 분당구 백현로 154) · Korean · jokbal/bossam (족발·보쌈) · near Pangyo Station (Suin-Bundang Line)

Pangyo is a business district packed with IT companies. Weekday lunches get busy.

  • Gamchirae (감치래) · 104 Gogi-ro, Suji-gu, Yongin (용인시 수지구 고기로 104) · Korean · noodles · Suji's Gogi-ro — Dongcheon Station (동천역) on the Shinbundang Line
  • Miga Huoguo (미가훠궈) · 15 Pungdeokcheon-ro 140beon-gil, 1F, Suji-gu, Yongin (용인시 수지구 풍덕천로140번길 15 1층) · Chinese · Chinese restaurant · Sujigucheong Station (Shinbundang Line) area

Huoguo (훠궈) is Chinese-style shabu-shabu. Suji is a residential new town about 20-some minutes from Gangnam on the Shinbundang Line.

  • Jeongdam Sikdang (정담식당) · 108 Hanteo-ro, Cheoin-gu, Yongin (용인시 처인구 한터로 108) · Korean · makguksu (막국수) · the Shinbundang Line doesn't reach Cheoin-gu — the Yongin EverLine and local buses cover it

Even within Yongin, Suji-gu (Shinbundang Line) and Cheoin-gu (EverLine) have completely different transit.

Foreign visitor tip — jokbal (족발) and huoguo are typically two-or-more servings; noodles and makguksu (막국수) can be ordered for one. The Shinbundang Line charges a separate surcharge fare, so keep some extra balance on your transit card.

③ Indeogwon Axis and the East — 9 Spots, One Stop Around Line 4 and East of the Han

Anyang, Uiwang, and Gwacheon cluster around Line 4's Indeogwon Station; Hanam, Namyangju, and Yangpyeong follow the Han River east of Seoul.

  • Grazing (그레이징) · 201 Gwanyang-ro, 1F, Dongan-gu, Anyang (안양시 동안구 관양로 201 1층) · Brunch cafe · Indeogwon Station (Line 4) area
  • Ttakjoa Wang Gopchang (딱좋아왕곱창) · 65 Gwanpyeong-ro 305beon-gil, Dongan-gu, Anyang (안양시 동안구 관평로305번길 65) · Korean · gopchang/makchang (곱창·막창) · Pyeongchon area
  • Mosil Gimbap (모실김밥) · 423 Gwanak-daero, 1F, Dongan-gu, Anyang (안양시 동안구 관악대로 423 1층) · Snack · gimbap · Anyang's Dongan-gu — Beomgye Station (Line 4) area

All three Anyang spots are in Dongan-gu (Pyeongchon new town), connected to Seoul by one line, Line 4.

  • Dolkkae Maeul Maetdol Sundubu (돌깨마을맷돌순두부) · 11 Poil-ro, 1F, Uiwang (의왕시 포일로 11 1층) · Korean · tofu dishes · Poil district — walking distance to Indeogwon Station

Sundubu (순두부) is unpressed, soft tofu simmered into a stew. The name means it's ground with a millstone.

  • Nongjang Sikgudeul (농장식구들) · 22-13 Chanumul-ro, Gwacheon (과천시 찬우물로 22-13) · Korean · meat dishes · Gwacheon Station (Line 4) area

Gwacheon is home to Seoul Grand Park and Seoul Race Park. Just a few stops from Sadang on Line 4.

  • Mangwoldong Dakgalbi (망월동닭갈비) · 21 Misagangbyeonbuk-ro 24beon-gil, Hanam (하남시 미사강변북로24번길 21) · Korean · dakgalbi (닭갈비) · Misa Station (Line 5) area
  • Jokbaldang (족발당) · 290-3 Misagangbyeonhangang-ro, Hanam (하남시 미사강변한강로 290-3) · Korean · jokbal/bossam · same Misa

Misa Riverside City is a new town linked straight to Seoul by the Line 5 Hanam extension. Just a few stops from Gangdong.

  • Cheonggiwa (청기와) · 593-16 Sure-ro, Wabu-eup, Namyangju (남양주시 와부읍 수레로 593-16) · Korean · meat dishes · near the Gyeongui-Jungang Line's Dosim Station (Wabu-eup, Dogok-ri)

This is a different shop from the same-named places in Seoul's Mok-dong and Yangcheon (#130, #185).

  • Daddy's Barbecue (대디스바베큐) · 15-24 Okcheonmunhwamaeul 3-gil, Okcheon-myeon, Yangpyeong (양평군 옥천문화마을3길 15-24) · Korean · meat dishes · Okcheon-myeon — the Gyeongui-Jungang Line's Asin Station is the nearby one (not Yangpyeong Station)

Yangpyeong is a rural area one line out from Seoul on the Gyeongui-Jungang Line. For Okcheon-myeon, Asin Station is closer than Yangpyeong Station.

Foreign visitor tip — gopchang (곱창), dakgalbi, jokbal, and grilled meat are typically two-serving minimum; gimbap and sundubu can be ordered for one. The Gyeongui-Jungang Line runs less frequently, so check the timetable first.

🚄 Fitting This Into a Metro-Area Route

  • A Suwon Hwaseong day (#321, #624) — John & Jin Pizza Pub in Haenggung-dong sits right inside that route.
  • Line 1 southbound — Suwon → Osan (3 spots) → Pyeongtaek (3 spots). Taking the express saves time; only Paengseong needs a bus.
  • Pangyo/Bundang business district — Moran and Pangyo, two spots. Steer clear of weekday lunch.
  • Shinbundang Line's Suji — 20-some minutes from Gangnam. Sujigucheong and Dongcheon, two spots.
  • Line 4's Indeogwon radius — Anyang's 3 + Uiwang's 1 + Gwacheon's 1 cluster on one axis. The most efficient grouping in this part.
  • East of the Han — Line 5's Misa (2 spots) → the Gyeongui-Jungang Line's Dosim (Namyangju) → Asin (Yangpyeong). Easy to string together in one day.
  • Heading northwest — Incheon, Gimpo, Paju, and Ilsan are in #1390; Seoul is in #1389.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Looking for Yangpyeong's Okcheon-myeon at Yangpyeong Station — the Gyeongui-Jungang Line's Asin Station is the nearby one.

2. Assuming Siheung's Sincheon Station is on Line 1 — it's the Seohae Line's Sincheon Station. Line 1 has no station of the same name.

3. Mixing up Osan Station and Osandae Station — different stations with similar names. Check against the destination address.

⚙ 3 FAQs

1. Who picked this list? — A table of regional favorites Korean YouTube viewers wrote in comments. KeyChinGu checked only existence, address, and business type — no ranking.

2. Can this be a day trip from Seoul? — Most are one train line or one transfer away. Only Paengseong (Pyeongtaek) and Yongin's Cheoin-gu add a bus or light rail leg.

3. Is it okay to go alone? — Sundaeguk, haejangguk, gamjatang, noodles, makguksu, gimbap, and sundubu can be ordered for one. Budae jjigae, jokbal, gopchang, and dakgalbi are typically two-serving minimum.
