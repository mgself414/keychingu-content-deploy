🍶 How to Choose a Bar — 4 Types, Beyond the Picks

  • Traditional liquor and brewery pubs: Places that brew or distill their own makgeolli and spirits, or have a long traditional-liquor list. Slow Brew Pub Gangnam is listed on VisitSeoul (see the table above). The brand domain does not open.
  • Wine bars: Back alleys of Garosu-gil and Apgujeong Rodeo. English menus and corkage differ from place to place — there is no official statement.
  • Pocha and cooking pubs: The nightlife strip north of Gangnam Station. Many open past midnight, but hours are set venue by venue. (Pocha (포차) are Korean-style tent-bar pubs.)
  • Speakeasies and bars: Find signless bars by map-app review count. Whether reservations are possible is a matter for the door.
  • In every case: whether a place is open that day, last order, and cash versus card can be confirmed only through the venue's official channel or on site. This guide goes only as far as the district.

🚇 Getting Around at Night — Last Trains, Taxis, Buses

  • Subway runs until roughly midnight (VisitSeoul). Last-train times for Gangnam, Sinsa and Apgujeong Rodeo stations are not on any official page, so check the station signboard or an app.
  • Taxi: 23:00–02:00 is the most expensive window (140 won/131 m). Deluxe taxis run 240 won/151 m from 22:00 to 04:00.
  • Owl Bus: The only Seoul Metropolitan Government material saying it runs midnight to 05:00 dates from 2016–17, so routes and fares are not listed.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Searching by the old eight names — zero official confirmation, and one may not exist.

2. Assuming the last train is 00:30 — varies by station, not posted officially.

3. Showing up without ID — no sales to anyone under 19, and ID may be required (by law).

4. Using the daytime rate for a 23:00–02:00 taxi — that window is 140 won/131 m.

5. Treating Songridan-gil as Gangnam — it is in Songpa.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Where should I go? — North of Gangnam Station, Garosu-gil (Sinsa Station Exit 8), or Apgujeong Rodeo (Rodeo Station Exit 2).

2. Which bars do you recommend? — Zest and Alice (Asia's 50 Best 2026), Le Chamber (secondary) and Slow Brew Pub Gangnam (VisitSeoul). For anything else, use a map app.

3. Will I be carded? — No sales under 19, and ID may be required (Youth Protection Act).

4. When is the last train? — About midnight (VisitSeoul). Check the station signboard.

5. How much more do taxis cost at night? — 140 won/131 m from 23:00 to 02:00 (standard).

6. What about English? — Call 1330 (the number is official). Venue English menus are a matter for the door.
