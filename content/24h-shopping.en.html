41 · Seoul's 24-Hour Shopping Map — Why the Lights Never Go Out

> Master List ID: 054 (F-Shopping) · Priority: P1 · Targets: CN● · JP○ · EN○

> Sources: souvenir_research / Dongdaemun late-night shopping notes / KTO statistics

> (souvenir_research/50_seoul_cluster_map.json)

In One Line

Seoul is effectively a 24-hour shopping city thanks to three pillars: Dongdaemun (동대문), 24-hour convenience stores, and Incheon Airport (인천공항). Even past the standard 22:00 closing time, you can complete an entire shopping run — including tax refund — by 5 AM. This guide maps that route on a single page.

Who This Is For

  • FIT travelers on long layovers of 6 to 12 hours in Seoul
  • Chinese and Japanese tourists whose only free time falls after 22:00 once a group itinerary wraps
  • Wholesale fashion buyers chasing Dongdaemun's pre-dawn market
  • Last-minute shoppers with red-eye flights leaving Seoul that same night

1. The Three Pillars of 24-Hour Shopping in Seoul

Pillar Main Area Core Hours Main Items Section
Wholesale & retail fashion Dongdaemun (동대문, East Gate) 22:00 ~ 05:00 K-fashion, fabric, accessories §2
24-hour convenience stores Myeongdong (명동), Hongdae (홍대), Gangnam (강남), citywide 24 hours K-food, snacks, daily goods §3
Airport duty-free Incheon (인천) T1·T2, both arrival and departure halls 05:30 ~ 23:00 + select 24h zones Duty-free K-Beauty, liquor, cigarettes §4

Standard retail hours in Korea are 10:00 to 22:00. Anything outside those three pillars closes at 22:00, no exceptions. So when someone says "let's go shopping after 10 PM" in Seoul, it always means one of these three.

2. Dongdaemun (동대문) — Seoul's Late-Night Fashion Heart

For the deep dive: see the Dongdaemun late-night shopping guide (31_동대문_심야_쇼핑.md). This guide treats Dongdaemun as a connector inside the bigger 24-hour map.

2-2. The Pre-Dawn Dongdaemun Route (23:00 → 04:00)

1. 23:00 — Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station (동대문역사문화공원역) on Line 2, Exit 14. Walk to Doota (3 min).

2. 23:30 — Start at Doota's 8F and work down (5F women's, 4F men's, 3F accessories).

3. 01:00 — Move to APM Luxe (5 min on foot). Buyer-focused floors are most active now.

4. 02:30 — Migliore B1 late-night food court. Gukbap (국밥, rice soup) and ramen run ₩5,000~8,000.

5. 03:30 — Round the wholesale floors at Kwanghee. For individual buyers, ask for single-piece purchase (낱개 가능?) and expect a 10–20% premium.

6. 04:30 — Walk to DDP (8 min). Take night photos, then catch the first train at Dongdaemun Station (동대문역) on Line 4 at 05:30.

2-3. Watch Out For

  • Wholesale arcades default to bundles of 5–10 pieces (5~10장 묶음). Buying singles means a 10–20% markup.
  • Some stalls take cash only. Doota and APM Luxe accept cards including foreign-issued ones.
  • Tax refund happens at the Doota 1F booth or the booth next to Dongdaemun History & Culture Park (passport required).

3. Convenience Stores — Seoul's Citywide 24-Hour Mini Mart

Seoul's three big convenience-store chains — CU, GS25, and Emart24 — run roughly 99% of their locations 24 hours a day. They are the most realistic late-night shopping channel for any visitor.

3-3. Convenience Store Tips

  • 7-Eleven only runs 24h at select branches — plan your route around CU and GS25 instead.
  • Cards, Samsung Pay, and Apple Pay all work 100% — convenience stores have the widest foreign-card acceptance in Korea.
  • No tax refund at regular convenience stores. For duty-free, see §4 (airport).
  • Refrigerated items (such as banana milk character goods, which can be transported unbagged) — be careful on long transfers.

4. Incheon Airport — Korea's Only Genuinely 24-Hour Duty-Free

4-1. Departure vs. Arrival Hall: Two Separate Worlds

Korea's airport duty-free is structurally split between departure and arrival halls.

Zone Location Hours When You Use It
Departure duty-free T1 3F, T2 3F 05:30~22:30 (adjusted by flight schedule) 1–3 hours before boarding
Arrival duty-free T1 1F, T2 1F 24 hours at select stores Right after arrival (start of trip)
Airport convenience stores T1·T2 throughout 24 hours Anytime

4-2. Stores Open 24 Hours

Store Terminal Notes

  • Lotte, Shilla, and Shinsegae duty-free run 05:30 to 22:30. Late-night layover passengers only have arrival duty-free + convenience stores as options.
  • Cigarettes and liquor at airport duty-free require passport scan at departure. Even at arrival duty-free, the bring-in cap is 1L liquor / 200 cigarettes.

4-3. Three-Hour Pre-Boarding Routine (Incheon T1)

1. T-180 min — Complete check-in and security (about 40 min total).

2. T-140 min — Enter the 3F duty-free zone. Hit the K-Beauty floors at Lotte, Shilla, and Shinsegae in sequence.

3. T-80 min — CU on 4F for honey butter almond and ramyeon sets.

4. T-30 min — Move to gate. Quick last-minute check at the gate-side gift shop.

5. Time-of-Night Master Map

🌙 22:00 ~ 24:00 — When Regular Stores Are Closing

  • Open: Major Dongdaemun malls (Doota, Migliore), all convenience stores, select Olive Young Myeongdong (closes 23:00).
  • Best move: Head to Doota in Dongdaemun. Layover passengers should aim for the airport duty-free last-call window at 22:30.
  • Caution: Most road shops in Myeongdong and Hongdae are already shut by 22:00. Reconfirm your destination after 10 PM.

🌌 00:00 ~ 03:00 — Seoul's Late-Night Prime Time

  • Open: All Dongdaemun stores, all convenience stores, select arrival-hall duty-free at Incheon.
  • Best move: APM Luxe + Migliore B1 food court → wholesale rounds at Kwanghee.
  • Transport: Subway last train runs around midnight. If you miss it, taxi from Dongdaemun to most Seoul hotels runs ₩10,000~15,000.

🌅 03:00 ~ 06:00 — Last Round Before First Train

  • Open: Most Dongdaemun arcades (Kwanghee until 06:00, Doota until 05:00).
  • Best move: Final wholesale buy at Kwanghee → DDP night photos → Dongdaemun Station first train at 05:30.
  • Taxi note: 20% surcharge between 00:00 and 04:00. Use the KakaoTaxi app.

☀️ 06:00 ~ 10:00 — Before Regular Retail Reopens

  • Open: Convenience stores, some big-box marts (Emart from 7:00), hotel shops.
  • Best move: Have breakfast and wait for Myeongdong / Hongdae road shops to open at 10.

6. Payment, Currency Exchange, and Tax Refund

6-1. Payment

  • Cards: VISA, Mastercard, JCB, UnionPay (银联 / 은련) — accepted at all major Dongdaemun malls, convenience stores, and the airport.
  • Mobile pay: Samsung Pay (Korean-issued cards), Apple Pay (rolling out at select stores in 2026), WeChat Pay and Alipay (select Myeongdong and Dongdaemun stores).
  • Cash: Mandatory at Dongdaemun wholesale arcades and some traditional markets.

6-2. Currency Exchange

  • 24-hour exchange: Woori Bank Myeongdong booth (우리은행 명동 환전소, 08:00~22:00) and Incheon Airport exchange counters (T1 3F, 24 hours).
  • Late-night emergency: Convenience-store ATMs accept foreign cards (₩3,000~5,000 fee).
  • Tip: Keep ₩50,000~100,000 cash on hand. For larger purchases, card + tax refund is the better play.

6-3. Tax Refund (VAT Refund for Foreigners)

  • Immediate refund: For purchases over ₩30,000, refunded at the store (monthly cap ₩5,000,000).
  • Airport refund: T1 3F kiosk or refund booth — passport, receipts, and unused goods required.
  • Refund cap: ₩1,000,000 per person per day, ₩5,000,000 per month.

7. Pre-Departure Checklist (Late-Night Shopping)

  • [ ] Passport (required for tax refund and duty-free)
  • [ ] Two international cards (in case one fails or is lost)
  • [ ] ₩50,000~100,000 cash for wholesale arcades
  • [ ] KakaoTaxi app installed (supports EN / CN / JP)
  • [ ] Hotel name card photo saved on phone (show to drivers)
