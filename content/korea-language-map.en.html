⚠ 3 Common Language Mistakes

1. "Korean = Difficult" — Unaware of 1-day Hangeul (한글) learning: Assuming it's difficult. In reality, Hangeul (한글) (10 vowels + 14 consonants) can be learned in 1 day + it's the most scientific writing system.

2. "Translation App = Only Google" — Unaware of Papago: Only recognizing Google. In reality, Papago (네이버) (Naver's #1 Korean translation app + camera, voice, and menu photo translation) is more accurate.

3. "TOPIK = Only for Korean Enthusiasts" — Unaware of visa utilization for foreigners: Only recognizing it for enthusiasts. In reality, TOPIK (한국어능력시험) Level 3+ = eligibility for Korean university admission + Working Holiday visa + some other visa qualifications.
