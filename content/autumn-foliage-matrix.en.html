★ Weekly Matrix (8 week full set)

October — Outlying mountains first (North → South)

Week 1 of October (10/1~10/7) — Outer town start

  • Seoraksan Mountain (Gangwon-do) ★ Korea’s No. 1 fall foliage week 1 begins
  • Odaesan (Gangwon-do) — Foreign mountain enthusiasts
  • Bukhansan Ui Gate (#186 Gangbuk #182 Eunpyeong) — Starting on the outskirts of Seoul

2nd week of October (10/8~10/14) — Off-campus progress

  • Seorak Mountain peak + 1st place on the outskirts of Gangwon-do
  • Bukhansan Mountain summit area (outskirts of Seoul)
  • Gwanak Mountain (#174 Gwanak) — On the outskirts of Seoul

3rd week of October (10/9~10/21) — Mountains outside Seoul

  • Bukhansan Peak + No. 1 foreign enthusiasts
  • Dobongsan·Suraksan (#177 Nowon·Dobong)
  • Achasan (#178 Gwangjin) — Eastern Seoul

October 4th week (10/22~10/28) — Entering downtown Seoul

  • Olympic Park (#82 Jamsil) — Fall foliage in downtown Seoul
  • Changdeokgung Palace Sponsor (Secret Garden) (#207 Temple/Palace) — UNESCO No. 1 Autumn Leaves Sponsored by UNESCO
  • Jongmyo (#207) — UNESCO Jongmyo Autumn Leaves

November — Peak downtown Seoul + southern cities

1st week of November (10/29~11/4) — Peak downtown Seoul

  • Peak of patronage at Changdeokgung Palace (No. 1 destination for foreign enthusiasts)
  • Olympic Park Peak (#82)
  • Seoul Forest (#01) — No. 1 in downtown Seoul

2nd week of November (11/5~11/11) — Seoul peak + Busan start

  • Namsan + N Seoul Tower Trail (#13·#224) — No. 1 in downtown Seoul
  • Naksan Park·Inwangsan (#172) — Superb view of the city center
  • Eungbongsan Mountain (#51·#189) — The best night view of downtown Seoul

3rd week of November (11/12 - 11/18) — Southern Peak

  • Naejangsan Mountain (Jeonbuk) ★ Korea’s No. 1 autumn foliage peak
  • Jirisan (South) — Foreign enthusiasts
  • Busan (#243~#247) — Southern start

Week 4 of November (11/19~11/25) — Southern + Outer Region Reviews

  • Busan BIFF·Haeundae (#243·#247) — Southern peak
  • Yeosu·Tongyeong·South Coast (#257) — Southern hidden gem
  • Outer ferry (Jeju·Ulleung) — Outer town mania

⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Autumn leaves = No. 1 from October": Autumn leaves start = 1st week in October, Gangwon-do (Seorak Mountain) → 4th week in October, downtown Seoul → Peak 1-2 weeks in November.

2. "Changdeokgung Palace Sponsorship = Everywhere": Changdeokgung Palace Sponsorship (Secret Garden) = UNESCO 1st place for autumn leaves + Advance reservation required.

3. "Bukhansan = at once": Bukhansan = Ui Gate in the 1st week of October → Autumn leaves in the summit area in the 3rd week of October Different each week.

4. "Busan Autumn Leaves = None": Busan Autumn Leaves = Best time to visit 3-4 weeks in November + No. 1 spot for foreign enthusiasts.

5. "Autumn leaves on the outskirts = I can't see them": Mt. Seorak, Mt. Naejang, Mt. Jiri = No. 1 place for fall foliage for foreign enthusiasts + KTX, express bus.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. No. 1 time for foreigners to see autumn leaves: 1st to 2nd week of November, peak time in downtown Seoul + Advance reservation required.

2. No. 1 spot for foreigners to see autumn leaves: Changdeokgung Palace Garden, Olympic Park, Seoul Forest, Namsan Mountain, Naejangsan Mountain.

3. Advance reservation for autumn leaves for foreigners: Sponsored by Changdeokgung Palace, the first place sold out to enthusiasts 1 month in advance.

4. Foreign Leaves Outskirts: Seoraksan Mountain, Odaesan Mountain, Naejangsan Mountain, Jiri Mountain, and Busan BIFF.

5. Foreigner Autumn Leaves Vlog No. 1: Changdeokgung Palace, Olympics, Namsan, N Seoul Tower, Banpo Han River.

6. Foreign Autumn Leaves + BIFF (#247): Busan Autumn Leaves + BIFF No. 1 foreign mania during the 3rd to 4th week of November.
