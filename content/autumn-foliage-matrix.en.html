🎯 Why an Autumn Foliage Timing Matrix?

Autumn foliage in October-November is the #1 inbound priority for foreign tourists visiting Korea in fall. This is a follow-up to #229 Autumn Foliage Full Guide, providing an accurate weekly spot matrix. It aims for weekly SEO traffic (the #1 search for foreign autumn enthusiasts).

★ Weekly Matrix (8-Week Full Set)

October — Outlying Mountains First (North → South Progression)

October 1st Week (Oct 1-7) — Outskirts Begin

  • Seoraksan (설악산) (Gangwon-do) ★ Korea's #1 foliage spot begins in the 1st week
  • Odaesan (오대산) (Gangwon-do) — For foreign mountain enthusiasts
  • Bukhansan Uyi Gate (북한산 우이 게이트) (#186 Gangbuk #182 Eunpyeong) — Outlying Seoul begins

October 2nd Week (Oct 8-14) — Outskirts Continue

  • Seoraksan (설악산) Peak + #1 outlying area in Gangwon-do
  • Bukhansan Peak Area (북한산 정상권) (Outlying Seoul)
  • Gwanaksan (관악산) (#174 Gwanak) — Outlying Seoul continues

October 3rd Week (Oct 9-21) — Outlying Seoul Mountains

  • Bukhansan (북한산) Peak + #1 spot for foreign enthusiasts
  • Dobongsan (도봉산)·Suraksan (수락산) (#177 Nowon·Dobong)
  • Achasan (아차산) (#178 Gwangjin) — Eastern Seoul

October 4th Week (Oct 22-28) — Entering Downtown Seoul

  • Olympic Park (올림픽공원) (#82 Jamsil) — #1 downtown Seoul foliage spot
  • Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원) (Secret Garden) (#207 Temples·Palaces) — #1 UNESCO Secret Garden foliage spot
  • Jongmyo (종묘) (#207) — UNESCO Jongmyo foliage

November — Downtown Seoul Peak + Southern Cities

November 1st Week (Oct 29-Nov 4) — Downtown Seoul Peak

  • Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원) Peak ( #1 spot for foreign enthusiasts)
  • Olympic Park (올림픽공원) Peak (#82)
  • Seoul Forest (서울숲) (#01) — #1 downtown Seoul spot

November 2nd Week (Nov 5-11) — Seoul Peak + Busan Begins

  • Namsan (남산) + N Seoul Tower Trail (N서울타워 둘레길) (#13·#224) — #1 downtown Seoul spot
  • Naksan Park (낙산공원)·Inwangsan (인왕산) (#172) — Downtown scenic view
  • Eungbongsan (응봉산) (#51·#189) — Downtown Seoul night view peak

November 3rd Week (Nov 12-18) — Southern Peak

  • Naejangsan (내장산) (Jeonbuk) ★ Korea's #1 foliage peak
  • Jirisan (지리산) (Southern) — Foreign enthusiasts
  • Busan (부산) (#243~#247) — Southern region begins

November 4th Week (Nov 19-25) — Southern + Late Outskirts

  • Busan BIFF·Haeundae (해운대) (#243·#247) — Southern peak
  • Yeosu (여수)·Tongyeong (통영)·Southern Coast (남해안) (#257) — Southern hidden gem
  • Outlying Islands Ferry (Jeju (제주)·Ulleung (울릉)) — Outlying island enthusiasts

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Foliage = #1 from October": Foliage starts in the 1st week of October in Gangwon-do (강원도) (Seoraksan (설악산)) → Downtown Seoul in the 4th week of October → Peak in the 1st-2nd weeks of November.

2. "Changdeokgung Huwon = Anywhere": Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원) (Secret Garden) = #1 UNESCO foliage spot + advance reservation required.

3. "Bukhansan = All at once": Bukhansan (북한산) = Uyi Gate (우이 게이트) in the 1st week of October → Peak area foliage in the 3rd week of October, varies weekly.

4. "Busan foliage = None": Busan (부산) foliage = Peak in the 3rd-4th weeks of November + #1 spot for foreign enthusiasts.

5. "Outlying foliage = Can't go": Seoraksan (설악산)·Naejangsan (내장산)·Jirisan (지리산) = #1 foliage spots for foreign enthusiasts + KTX (Korea Train eXpress)·Express Bus.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Top foliage timing for foreigners: 1st-2nd weeks of November, Downtown Seoul peak + advance reservation required.

2. Top foliage spots for foreigners: Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원)·Olympic Park (올림픽공원)·Seoul Forest (서울숲)·Namsan (남산)·Naejangsan (내장산).

3. Foreigner foliage advance reservation: Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원) is the #1 spot that sells out for enthusiasts 1 month in advance.

4. Outlying foliage for foreigners: Seoraksan (설악산)·Odaesan (오대산)·Naejangsan (내장산)·Jirisan (지리산)·Busan BIFF.

5. Top foliage vlog spots for foreigners: Changdeokgung (창덕궁)·Olympic Park (올림픽공원)·Namsan (남산)·N Seoul Tower (N서울타워)·Banpo Hangang (반포 한강).

6. Foreigner foliage + BIFF (#247): Busan (부산) foliage in the 3rd-4th weeks of November + BIFF is the #1 spot for foreign enthusiasts.
