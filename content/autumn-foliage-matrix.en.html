Autumn Foliage Timing Matrix — Full Set of Weekly Spots in October & November

Subtitle: #229 Autumn Foliage Full Guide Time Breakdown — Weekly Spots for Oct 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th Weeks + Nov 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th Weeks

ISSUE 03 · #273 · 2026-05-20

🎯 How to use this guide

Korean foliage turns first on the high mountains in the north, then moves down to the south and into the cities. So the best place to go depends on when you visit. The matrix below breaks the season into single weeks, from the 1st week of October to the 4th week of November, and lists good spots for each. Peak shifts 1-2 weeks earlier or later depending on the weather each year, so check foliage news the week before you leave. For the big picture, see the #229 Autumn Foliage Full Guide.

★ Weekly Matrix (8-Week Full Set)

October — Outlying Mountains First (North → South Progression)

October 1st Week (Oct 1-7) — Outskirts Begin

  • Seoraksan (설악산) (Gangwon-do) ★ One of the first mountains in Korea to turn
  • Odaesan (오대산) (Gangwon-do) — Together with Seoraksan, where Gangwon-do's mountain foliage begins
  • Bukhansan Uyi Entrance (북한산 우이 쪽 입구) (#186 Gangbuk #182 Eunpyeong) — Northern edge of Seoul, turning from the higher ground first

October 2nd Week (Oct 8-14) — Outskirts Continue

  • Seoraksan (설악산) Peak — Trails get crowded on weekends, so weekdays are better
  • Bukhansan Peak Area (북한산 정상권) (Outlying Seoul)
  • Gwanaksan (관악산) (#174 Gwanak) — Outlying Seoul continues

October 3rd Week (Oct 15-21) — Outlying Seoul Mountains

  • Bukhansan Peak Area (북한산 정상권) at Peak — A national park you can reach by subway
  • Dobongsan (도봉산)·Suraksan (수락산) (#177 Nowon·Dobong)
  • Achasan (아차산) (#178 Gwangjin) — Eastern Seoul

October 4th Week (Oct 22-28) — Entering Downtown Seoul

  • Olympic Park (올림픽공원) (#82 Jamsil) — A wide, flat park that's easy to walk, with foliage throughout
  • Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원) (Secret Garden) (#207 Temples·Palaces) — The garden of a UNESCO World Heritage palace. Timed-entry reservations
  • Jongmyo (종묘) (#207) — A UNESCO World Heritage shrine, with quiet foliage
  • Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) Inner Grounds + Samcheong-ro (삼청로) (#37) — When Seochon (서촌) gets crowded, retreat inside the palace (see 🏯 line below)
  • Seosulla-gil (서순라길), the Changdeokgung (창덕궁)·Jongmyo (종묘) stone wall path — red foliage along the wall (see 🏯 line below)
  • Hwadamsup (화담숲)·Nami Island (남이섬)·Garden of Morning Calm (아침고요수목원)·Hoam Art Museum Heewon (호암미술관 희원) — Garden day trips outside Seoul (Hwadamsup is 100% advance reservation, see 🌳 line below)

November — Downtown Seoul Peak + Southern Cities

November 1st Week (Oct 29-Nov 4) — Downtown Seoul Peak

  • Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원) Peak — Reserve as soon as your date is set
  • Olympic Park (올림픽공원) Peak (#82)
  • Seoul Forest (서울숲) (#01) — A forest in the city, easy to pair with a Seongsu-dong (성수동) walk
  • Jeongdong Observatory (정동전망대) + National Museum of Korean Contemporary History (대한민국역사박물관) Rooftop — Deoksugung (덕수궁)·Gyeongbokgung foliage from above (free, see 🏯 line below)
  • Ginkgo Line: Sungkyunkwan (성균관)·Deoksugung Stone Wall Path·Samcheongdong-gil (삼청동길) — For yellow ginkgo leaves, see #1314
  • Free admission to the 4 Grand Palaces and Jongmyo, Oct 29-Nov 15 — No entrance fee at Changgyeonggung (창경궁), Gyeongbokgung and more (Changdeokgung Huwon special tour excluded)
  • Suseongdong Valley (수성동계곡)·Deosup Chosochaekbang Bookstore (더숲 초소책방)·Buam-dong (부암동) — From Seochon up into the Inwangsan foothills (see 🍁 line below)

November 2nd Week (Nov 5-11) — Late Downtown Seoul Peak

  • Namsan (남산) + N Seoul Tower Trail (N서울타워 둘레길) (#13·#224) — A foliage path you can walk up to the tower
  • Naksan Park (낙산공원)·Inwangsan (인왕산) (#172) — City views along the fortress wall path
  • Eungbongsan (응봉산) (#51·#189) — The Han River and downtown night view
  • Hanyangdoseong (한양도성) Namsan Section + Baekbeom Plaza (백범광장) — Fortress wall and N Seoul Tower in one frame (see 🏯 line below)
  • Yongsan Park (용산공원) Officers' Quarters Complex 5 (장교숙소5단지) — Former U.S. military housing turned public park, a quiet foliage walk (closed Mondays, see 🏯 line below)
  • Gilsangsa (길상사)·Choi Sun-u's Old House (최순우 옛집)·Changgyeonggung (창경궁)·Unhyeongung (운현궁) — Seongbuk-dong temple and hanok, plus palaces (Changgyeonggung daytime visits close at 17:30 on Nov 10-15, see 🍁 line below)

November 3rd Week (Nov 12-18) — Southern Peak

  • Naejangsan (내장산) (Jeonbuk) ★ The signature foliage spot of the south
  • Jirisan (지리산) (Southern) — A big mountain, so an overnight stay is recommended
  • Busan (부산) (#243~#247) — Southern city foliage begins

November 4th Week (Nov 19-25) — Southern Wrap-up

  • Busan (부산) (#243·#247) — City park foliage wraps up
  • Yeosu (여수)·Tongyeong (통영)·Southern Coast (남해안) (#257) — Late autumn paired with seaside cities

🏯 Downtown Palace, Fortress Wall & Viewpoint Line — Where Seoul's Local Photographers Keep Returning (2026 update)

In September 2026, multiple local photographers on Seoul Instagram kept recommending the same spots over and over in #서울단풍 #서울가을 posts. These are downtown red-foliage and viewpoint spots reachable on foot from a subway station — not mountains. The yellow ginkgo routes — Sungkyunkwan Myeongnyundang (명륜당)·Deoksugung Stone Wall Path·Jeongdong-gil (정동길)·Samcheongdong-gil·Hyoja-ro (효자로) — are covered separately in the #1314 Ginkgo Street guide. Peak is generally late October to mid-November, shifting 1-2 weeks each year depending on the weather.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. "Anywhere is in foliage in October": In early October only the high mountains of Gangwon-do are turning. Downtown Seoul is generally late October to mid-November.

2. "I can just walk into Changdeokgung Huwon": The Huwon is a timed tour for limited groups, so reserving ahead is safer. Even with the Oct 29-Nov 15 free palace opening, the Huwon special tour is excluded.

3. "A garden or palace tour on Monday": Hwadamsup, Hoam Art Museum, Changgyeonggung, Unhyeongung, Choi Sun-u's Old House and Yongsan Park Officers' Quarters Complex 5 close on Mondays. Palace closure days differ by palace (#37·#207).

4. "If I miss Seoul, it's over": The south comes later. Naejangsan and Busan are best in mid-to-late November.

5. "I can't reach the gardens near Seoul without a car": Hwadamsup, Nami Island and Garden of Morning Calm are 1-1.5 hours away by train or bus (see 🌳 line above). But Hwadamsup won't let you in without a reservation.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. When is downtown Seoul foliage best? Generally late October to mid-November. It shifts 1-2 weeks each year.

2. Where can I go by subway? Changdeokgung, Changgyeonggung, Gyeongbokgung, Jongmyo, Seosulla-gil, Namsan, Olympic Park, Seoul Forest, Gilsangsa (see 🏯·🍁 lines above).

3. Which places need reservations? Changdeokgung Huwon (timed reservation), Hwadamsup (100% advance reservation), and Changgyeonggung's Nov 10-15 night visits "Donggwol Dongrak" (동궐동락).

4. Where can I see foliage for free? The palaces and Jongmyo Oct 29-Nov 15, plus Jeongdong Observatory, the National Museum of Korean Contemporary History rooftop, Yongsan Park, Unhyeongung and Choi Sun-u's Old House.

5. Where are the day trips outside Seoul? Hwadamsup, Nami Island, Garden of Morning Calm and Hoam Art Museum Heewon (see 🌳 line above).

6. Where can I see yellow ginkgo leaves? They're covered separately in the #1314 Ginkgo Street guide.
