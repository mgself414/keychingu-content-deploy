9 Seoul Cafes with Autumn Foliage Views — Inwangsan Forest Paths, Palace Roofs, Windows Below Namsan

Subtitle: Three cafes on the slopes of Inwangsan and Buam-dong, four hanok teahouses and palace-view cafes in Bukchon, Jongmyo, Deoksugung, and Seongbuk-dong, and two more by Namsan and Seoul Forest. Out of the places where trees show through a window or courtyard, only 9 that Kakao Map (카카오맵) shows as open and that got visit reviews in autumn 2026 made the cut, split into 3 zones. Where a place gives a reason to visit outside foliage season, we added one line on it.

ISSUE 06 · #742 · 2026-11-24 · Seoul Foliage Cafes

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 9

This list is for travelers who want to see autumn foliage but don't want a long mountain walk. Seoul's foliage cafes come in three kinds. On the slopes of Inwangsan and Bukaksan you see forest and city together. In the palace and hanok neighborhoods you see the trees of the old palaces over tiled roofs. By Namsan and Seoul Forest you look down on the park woods.

There are three selection rules. The Kakao Map place info has to say "open for business." A blog review or Kakao Map review has to have been posted in October–November 2026. And the reviews have to mention a forest, palace, or park view.

The foliage timing differs every year. By the Korea Meteorological Administration's 1991–2020 normals, Bukhansan foliage starts around October 15 and peaks around October 28. How late the season runs depends on the weather. A 2026-11-19 blog review said a few days of rain plus the cold snap around the college entrance exam had left the foliage season at Deoksugung and Jeongdong-gil nearly over.

Diet notes are what can be read from menu names and Kakao Map descriptions, plus how such dishes are generally made. Ask the staff about ingredients and allergies before you order. For places far from a station or up a hill, we copied the route written in blog reviews, with dates.

① Inwangsan·Buam-dong — 3 Places

All three are too far to walk from a subway station, and there are hills. Take a bus or village bus up. Sanmotungi and Huijak sit side by side on Baekseok-dong-gil (백석동길), so you can do both in one go.

  • Deosup Chosochaekbang (더숲초소책방) — A book cafe made from an old guard post halfway up Inwangsan. A 2026-11-20 blog review said you can see downtown Seoul and the foliage through the floor-to-ceiling glass. Registered prices are cafe mocha 6,500 won, oat latte 6,500 won, chrysanthemum tea 5,500 won, and soy milk 5,000 won. It opens daily 08:00–22:00, open all year. The address is Inwangsan-ro 172 (인왕산로 172), Jongno-gu. The same review gives three ways to get there. First, take village bus 09 from Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역), get off at Suseong-dong Valley (수성동계곡), and walk up 690m. Second, walk about 25 minutes on a mountain path from Suseong-dong Valley. Third, take the village bus from Gyeongbokgung Station to the Yun Dong-ju Literary Museum (윤동주문학관) and walk 887m. The parking lot is very small. Beyond foliage season: It is open until 22:00, so it works for a visit to see the city night view (a 2026-11-21 blog title says "night view spot"). Diet note: Oat latte and soy milk are on the menu, so you can drink without dairy. Lattes and mocha contain milk, and coffee has caffeine.
  • Sanmotungi (산모퉁이) — A cafe on a hill in Buam-dong. A 2026-11-22 blog review introduced it as a filming location for the drama "Coffee Prince No. 1 Shop." A 2026-11-17 blog title also says "Seoul foliage cafe." Registered prices are Americano 8,000 won, sweet potato latte 9,000 won, Almond Breeze 9,000 won, and handmade citron tea 10,000 won. It opens daily 11:00–18:50. The address is Baekseok-dong-gil 153 (백석동길 153), Jongno-gu. The 2026-11-22 review said the slope is steep and advised taking a village bus (such as Jongno 05) and walking from there. Beyond foliage season: A 2026-05 Kakao Map review said there are plenty of seats, outdoor seating, and a good view. Diet note: Almond Breeze is, as the name says, an almond (nut) drink. The sweet potato latte and cafe latte contain milk. Only drinks are registered on the menu.
  • Huijak (희작) — A cafe on the basement 1st floor of the building right next to Sanmotungi. A 2026-11-16 blog review described the Inwangsan full-width window view and a weekend wait. Registered prices are Americano 6,800 won, latte 7,800 won, Basque cheesecake 9,800 won, and brownie 9,500 won. It is open Wed–Mon 11:00–18:30 and closed Tuesday. The address is Baekseok-dong-gil 155 (백석동길 155), basement 1st floor, Jongno-gu. The same review gives the route. Take bus 7022 from Exit 3 of Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역), get off at "Jahamun Pass·Yun Dong-ju Literary Museum" (자하문고개.윤동주문학관), and walk about 16 minutes. Beyond foliage season: A 2026-11-17 review said the Inwangsan scenery looked good even on a cloudy day. Diet note: The homemade lemonade is topped with a sorbet made with Irish whiskey (the owner's description on Kakao Map). Skip it if you avoid alcohol. Basque cheesecake contains cheese (dairy) and usually eggs.

② Palace·Hanok Neighborhoods — 4 Places

One in Bukchon, one on Seosunra-gil west of Jongmyo, one in the City Hall annex beside Deoksugung, and one in Seongbuk-dong. Chatteul is closed Tuesday and Suyeon Sanbang is closed Monday and Tuesday. Darak opens at 13:30 on weekdays.

  • Chamasineun Tteul Chatteul (차마시는뜰 차뜰) — A hanok traditional teahouse on a Bukchon hill. A 2026-11-22 blog review said they dropped in partly to see the foliage. English names are registered alongside the menu items. Registered prices are ssanghwatang 10,000 won, jujube tea 10,000 won, mugwort tea 12,000 won, sweet pumpkin rice cake 11,000 won, and hangwa (traditional sweets) 5,000 won (registered 2025-12-31). It is open Wed–Mon 10:00–19:00 and closed Tuesday. The address is Bukchon-ro 11na-gil 26 (북촌로11나길 26), Jongno-gu. The nearest station is Anguk Station (안국역) (Line 3), Exit 2, a 15-minute walk. A 2026-07 Kakao Map review said the way there is quite hard to find. Beyond foliage season: A 2026-11-18 blog review mentions omija bingsu and a traditional string performance. Diet note: Apart from green tea, the jujube, mugwort, chrysanthemum, and omija drinks are teas made from flowers, fruit, and herbs, so there is no coffee. The sweet pumpkin rice cake takes about 20 minutes to come out (the owner's description). Ssanghwatang is a tea simmered from herbal medicine ingredients.
  • Cafe View (카페뷰) — A cafe on the 4th floor of a building on Seosunra-gil near Jongno 3-ga. Its Kakao Map tags include rooftop. A 2026-11-19 blog review introduced it as a cafe with a wide-open view of Jongmyo. Registered prices are Americano 6,000 won, cafe latte 7,000 won, View Royal milk tea 9,000 won, and fraisier 8,000 won. It opens daily 12:00–23:00. The address is Seosunra-gil 27-5 (서순라길 27-5), 4th floor, Jongno-gu. The nearest station is Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역) (Lines 1, 3, 5), Exit 8, a 4-minute walk. Beyond foliage season: It is open until 23:00, so it works for an evening visit. A 2026-11-15 blog review said they went for the Jongmyo view on a rainy day. Diet note: Milk tea and lattes contain milk. A fraisier is usually made with strawberries, cream, and sponge cake (flour and eggs).
  • Cafe Darak (카페 다락), Jeongdong Observatory (정동전망대) — A cafe inside Jeongdong Observatory on the 13th floor of the Seoul City Hall Seosomun annex. Floor-to-ceiling windows look down on the Deoksugung roofs and the Jeongdong area. A 2026-11-19 blog review posted Deoksugung autumn photos taken the week before and said a few days of rain and cold had nearly ended the foliage. Registered prices are Americano (HOT) 3,000 won, cafe latte (HOT) 3,500 won, butterscotch Einspanner 5,000 won, and chamomile 2,500 won (registered 2025-05). Cafe hours are the Kakao Map registered values: weekdays 13:30–17:30, Sat·Sun 09:00–17:00. Jeongdong Observatory also opens on Friday and Saturday evenings (until 21:00 in winter, per the official Seoul city page). That is the observatory's operating time, not the cafe's business hours. Jeongdong Observatory does not open on public holidays (official Seoul city page). The address is Deoksugung-gil 15 (덕수궁길 15), 13th floor, Jung-gu. The nearest station is City Hall Station (시청역) (Lines 1, 2), Exit 12, a 5-minute walk. Beyond foliage season: The observatory charges no admission (2026-11-22 blog), so it is a good place to rest for an hour before or after visiting Deoksugung. Diet note: The Einspanner is topped with cream (dairy). Herbal teas have no caffeine.
  • Suyeon Sanbang (수연산방) — A hanok traditional teahouse in Seongbuk-dong. It is the house where the novelist Yi Tae-jun lived (the Sangheo Yi Tae-jun House). A 2026-10-23 blog review (visited in October) said they enjoyed the autumn mood looking at the lush garden. Registered prices are jujube ssanghwa tea 15,500 won and sweet pumpkin bingsu 16,000 won (registered 2025-10-25). The price range is on the high side. Hours are Wed–Fri 11:30–17:50 and Sat·Sun 11:30–19:50, and it is closed Monday and Tuesday. Last order is 17:00 Wed–Fri and 19:00 Sat·Sun. The address is Seongbuk-ro 26-gil 8 (성북로26길 8), Seongbuk-gu. The same review gives the route. From Hansung Univ. Station (한성대입구역) (Line 4), transfer to bus 1111, 1112, or 2112, get off at the Seongbuk Museum of Art (성북구립미술관), and walk less than 5 minutes. Beyond foliage season: A 2026-06 Kakao Map review praised the sweet pumpkin bingsu and the charm of a space with history. Diet note: The jujube ssanghwa tea contains jujubes and pine nuts (nuts) (Kakao Map AI summary). The sweet pumpkin bingsu is milk shaved ice topped with red bean and rice cake (Kakao Map AI summary).

③ Namsan·Seoul Forest — 2 Places

One on the Huam-dong hill west of Namsan, and one beside Seoul Forest in Seongsu-dong. Comfortable Namsan is open until 22:00, and Forest Seoul Forest is open until 22:00 only on Friday and Saturday.

  • Comfortable Namsan (콤포타블 남산) — A cafe on the 4th floor of the Daewon Jeongsa annex in Huam-dong. A 2026-11-21 blog review said the windows on both sides look down on the Namsan scenery and the neighborhood. Registered prices are Americano (M) 4,500 won, latte (M) 5,000 won, nutty cream latte 6,500 won, and Uji matcha latte 6,500 won. It opens daily 10:00–22:00. The address is Duteobawi-ro 60-gil 49 (두텁바위로60길 49), 4th floor, Yongsan-gu. The same review gives the route. It is about a 25-minute walk from Sookmyung Women's Univ. Station (숙대입구역); or take village bus 02 for about 10 minutes, get off, and walk 7 minutes. Parking is free for the first 1 hour (Kakao Map AI summary). Beyond foliage season: A 2026-11-12 Kakao Map review said it is a good place to watch the sunset. Diet note: The espresso martini (12,000 won) is a cocktail with alcohol. Chai latte, milk tea, and matcha latte contain milk.
  • Forest Seoul Forest (포레스트 서울숲) — A dessert cafe on the cafe street beside Seoul Forest. It uses the space from the basement 1st floor up. A 2026-11-06 blog review said it has a rooftop that looks down on Seoul Forest park. Menu items are registered but no prices are, so look at the in-store menu. Hours are Mon–Thu·Sun 10:00–21:00, Fri 10:00–22:00, and Sat 11:00–22:00. The address is Seoulsup 2-gil 20-12 (서울숲2길 20-12), Seongdong-gu. The nearest station is Ttukseom Station (뚝섬역) (Line 2), Exit 8, a 10-minute walk. Parking is not available. Beyond foliage season: It rotates seasonal fruit desserts. The Kakao Map menu has winter seasonal items such as strawberry bingsu. Diet note: The bingsu is milk ice (Kakao Map AI summary). The croffle is a butter and flour dough. Tiramisu usually contains mascarpone cheese and eggs.

🗺 Route — Two Half-Day Courses

A. From Buam-dong down Inwangsan (except Tuesday)

  1. Daytime Take bus 7022 from Exit 3 of Gyeongbokgung Station to "Jahamun Pass·Yun Dong-ju Literary Museum." Walk to Huijak (희작) (from 11:00) or Sanmotungi (산모퉁이) (from 11:00)
  2. Afternoon Come back toward the Yun Dong-ju Literary Museum and go on to Deosup Chosochaekbang (더숲초소책방). A 2026-11-20 blog review gave 887m from the Yun Dong-ju Literary Museum stop
  3. Before sunset Look at the city view from the guard-post bookshop, then walk down to the Suseong-dong Valley stop (690m) and take village bus 09 back to Gyeongbokgung Station

On Tuesday Huijak is closed, so go to Sanmotungi (산모퉁이).

B. From Bukchon to Jongmyo (except Tuesday)

  1. Morning Traditional tea at Chamasineun Tteul Chatteul (차마시는뜰 차뜰) (from 10:00) on the hill
  2. Afternoon The Deoksugung view from Cafe Darak (카페 다락) near City Hall Station (weekdays 13:30–17:30) (Sat·Sun 09:00–17:00). Pair it with a Deoksugung visit
  3. Evening The Jongmyo-side view from Cafe View (카페뷰) (until 23:00) near Jongno 3-ga Station

On public holidays Jeongdong Observatory is closed, so skip stop 2. Plan Suyeon Sanbang (수연산방) in Seongbuk-dong as a separate half day, Wed–Sun.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going without checking the day — Chatteul (차뜰) and Huijak (희작) are closed Tuesday, and Suyeon Sanbang (수연산방) is closed Monday and Tuesday. Jeongdong Observatory does not open on public holidays (official Seoul city page).
  2. Trying to walk from the subway station — Deosup Chosochaekbang (더숲초소책방), Sanmotungi (산모퉁이), Huijak (희작), and Comfortable Namsan (콤포타블 남산) are up on hills. Take a bus or village bus and walk from there. Wear non-slip sneakers.
  3. Fixing on a single foliage date — Foliage shifts a few days earlier or later every year. The Korea Meteorological Administration normals are a 30-year average. Rain or a cold snap can end it in a few days.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. When is the foliage at its best? — By the Korea Meteorological Administration normals, Bukhansan foliage starts around October 15 and peaks around October 28 (1991–2020 average). It moves earlier or later every year.
  2. Which places can I visit in the evening? — By registered hours, Cafe View (카페뷰) is open until 23:00, and Deosup Chosochaekbang (더숲초소책방) and Comfortable Namsan (콤포타블 남산) until 22:00. Forest Seoul Forest (포레스트 서울숲) is open until 22:00 on Friday and Saturday.
  3. What about rainy days? — Cafe Darak (카페 다락) (Deoksugung), Cafe View (카페뷰) (Jongmyo), and Huijak (희작) (Inwangsan), where you watch from inside the glass, are the safe picks.
