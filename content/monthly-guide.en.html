🎯 Why a Seoul Monthly Guide?

Foreign tourists' pain point for inbound travel to Korea by season = What's the top spot each month? This guide integrates the top spot for each month from January to December + seasonal enthusiast culture. SEO monthly traffic (January, May, October are top months).

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Korea = Four Seasons = All Good": Enthusiast experiences differ each month in Korea = April cherry blossoms, May family activities, July monsoon, October autumn foliage, December illumination. Each month has a different top spot.

2. "April = Cherry Blossoms #1": April = Peak season (first to second week of April) + Top inbound season for foreigners + Advance booking essential.

3. "July~August = Too Hot to Visit": July~August = Top #1 for Han River (한강) Chimaek (치맥 - chicken and beer), Jjimjilbang (찜질방 - Korean bathhouse/spa), and indoor enthusiasts + Top #1 for foreigner enthusiasts.

4. "October~November = Only Autumn Foliage": October~November = Autumn foliage + BIFF ([#247]) + Cheonggyecheon Lantern Festival (청계천 빛초롱축제) + Top inbound season for foreigners.

5. "December~February = Too Cold to Visit": December~February = Top #1 for illumination, Jjimjilbang (찜질방 - Korean bathhouse/spa), hot springs, and ski enthusiasts + Top #1 for foreigner vlogs.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Top Months for Foreigners: April (cherry blossoms), October~November (autumn foliage, BIFF), December (illumination) are the top 3 seasons.

2. Months with Most Foreign Inbound Tourists: April, May, October, November (advance booking essential).

3. Value-for-Money Months for Foreigners: January, February, June, July, August, September (hotels and flights are cheaper).

4. Korean Holidays for Foreigner Enthusiasts: Seollal (설날 - Lunar New Year) (February), Chuseok (추석 - Korean Thanksgiving) (September), Sambok (삼복 - three hottest days of summer) (July~August) are traditional favorites for foreigners.

5. Seasonal Outskirt Enthusiasts for Foreigners: Jinhae (진해) cherry blossoms in April, Seoraksan (설악산) in October, Gangwon (강원) skiing in December.

6. Outskirt Ferry Enthusiasts for Foreigners: June~August (Jeju (제주), Ulleungdo (울릉도), Hongdo (홍도)) are top inbound destinations for foreigners.
