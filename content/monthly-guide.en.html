⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Korea = four seasons = all are good": Korea Different mania every month = Spring Cherry blossoms in April, Family in May, Rainy season in July, Fall foliage in October, Illumination in December The first spot of each month is different.

2. "April = No. 1 cherry blossoms": April = Peak season (1st to 2nd week of April) + No. 1 foreign inbound + Advance reservation required.

3. "July-August = I can't come because it's too hot": July-August = No. 1 Han River chicken and beer, sauna, and indoor enthusiasts + No. 1 foreign enthusiasts.

4. "October~November = Autumn leaves only": October~November = Autumn leaves + BIFF (#247) + Cheonggye Lantern Festival + No. 1 season for foreign inbound.

5. "December to February = I can't come because it's cold": December to February = No. 1 fan of illumination, sauna, hot springs, and skiing + No. 1 foreign vlog.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. No. 1 month for foreigners: April (cherry blossoms), October-November (autumn leaves, BIFF), December (illuminations) 3 seasons No. 1.

2. Months with the most inbound foreigners: April, May, October, November (advance reservation required).

3. Cost-effective monthly for foreigners: January, February, June, July, August, September (cheaper hotels and airline tickets).

4. Foreigner enthusiasts for Korean holidays: Traditional foreigner enthusiasts for Lunar New Year (February), Chuseok (September), and Sambok (July-August).

5. Foreigner enthusiasts by season: Cherry blossoms in Jinhae in April, Mt. Seorak in October, and skiing in Gangwon in December.

6. Foreigner ferry enthusiasts: No. 1 inbound for foreigners from June to August (Jeju, Ulleung, Hongdo).
