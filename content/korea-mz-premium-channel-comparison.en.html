⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "The 4 channels are all similar": Prices, reservations, and dress codes are completely different. MZ = cafe / Bib = New Wave / Michelin = fine dining / modern hanjeongsik = MZ hanjeongsik.

2. "Michelin = walk-in": Booking 1–3 months in advance is essential.

3. "Michelin = only expensive": Lunch = ₩50–80k (~$38–60), genuinely good value.

4. "No dress code": Michelin = smart casual or above.

5. "MZ cafe = budget option": MZ cafes = ₩12–30k (~$9–23), MZ trend · Instagram #1.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. First channel: MZ Korean cafe (#1) → Michelin lunch (value).

2. Instagram: MZ Korean cafes · Michelin.

3. MZ: MZ Korean cafe (#1).

4. Luxury: Michelin La Yeon (3 stars #1).

5. Lunch value: Michelin lunch.

6. Allergies · pregnancy: See [#900](#900).

7. Payment: Cards OK / Michelin = 10% service charge.

8. VAT: No refund.

⚠ Medical · Safety · Dietary Disclaimer

All 4 MZ premium Korean dining channels serve regular food. Declare allergies in advance. ☎ 1339.

Dietary restriction fallback: Halal · kosher = Michelin · New Wave = declare when booking / vegetarian · vegan = MZ cafes · New Wave OK / gluten-free = Tamari.
