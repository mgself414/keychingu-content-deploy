13 Jeju Cafes — Only Where Foreign Reviews Showed Up First

Subtitle: After the east Jeju bakery line (#1407), the local favorites and the Oil Market piece (#1348), and 14 Jeju Restaurants (#1437), here is the next axis — a roastery near the airport, a roasting room in the old downtown with English menu names, a garden cafe in Gyorae, coastal cafes in Woljeong and Gimnyeong, Aewol sourdough, and a Seogwipo bakery. Only cafes where foreign-language reviews appeared first on Naver Map made the cut — 13 of them, grouped into 4 zones so you can pick by where you're staying. The in-cafe rules like holding a seat, mugs, and the return counter are in #1378

ISSUE 06 · #1438 · 2026-11-23 · Jeju Cafes

🎯 Why "Three-Language Reviews" Is the Bar

There is already a Jeju bakery and cafe piece that picked venues — the east Jeju bakery line (#1407). None of the 13 venues in this piece appeared in #1407. Still, Cafe Guhalgu Jeju (카페구할구 제주) in Woljeong-ri (월정리) is on the same alley (Woljeong 5-gil) as the Woljeong-ri venue in #1407 (Oneuldohwachang (오늘도화창)), and Cafe Moalboal (카페모알보알) in Gimnyeong is in the same neighborhood as the six Gimnyeong venues in #1407, so you can fit them into that day's route.

There's one selection rule — cafes on Naver Map (네이버 지도) with English, Japanese, and Chinese reviews together. Not Korean popularity rankings — the judgment is that a place foreign visitors have already found and left traces in three languages is the less unfamiliar pick for the next one. Review counts are a 2026-11 snapshot, and this piece lists that count for every venue. There are two exceptions — Mamarong (마마롱) and Janmulgyeol (잔물결) have no Chinese reviews (both 5·1·0), but they're a cake-and-dessert cafe in the Aewol hills and a hand-drip roastery in Geumneung, two places with a different character in the west zone, so they're in.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours, closed days, and menu prices are the values the owner registered on Kakao Map (카카오맵) (checked 2026-11-23). Prices are the registered price for one drink and one dessert. Where no price is registered, or the menu was registered more than 2 years ago, the cell says "check Kakao Map on the day." Dietary cues are only what can be read from menu names and the owner's menu descriptions — ask staff before ordering about ingredients and allergies. Jeju is big, so moving between zones means a car or a bus. This piece doesn't give travel times — check them on the day with Kakao Map directions.

① Jeju City (Airport·Old Downtown) — 2 Venues

Near Jeju Airport and the old downtown. Both open at 09:00 — together with Sisterfield, the earliest of the 13 venues. You can fit your first cup on arrival day or your last cup on departure day here.

  • Yudong Coffee Sogeum Gongjang Branch (유동커피 소금공장점) — a roastery cafe in Yongdam-samdong (용담삼동), near Jeju Airport. Americano 5,500 won, cafe latte (카페라떼) 5,500 won, cold brew (콜드브루) 5,000 won, ssuk-tteok-latte (쑥떡라떼) (mugwort rice cake latte) 6,000 won, Tsunami Haeundae Coffee (쓰나미 해운대커피) 6,500 won. For the Americano you choose from 4 kinds of beans (owner's description). No prices are registered for bread and desserts, so look in the shop. With 15 foreign reviews in total, it has the most in this piece, and it's the only venue with 5 each in English, Japanese, and Chinese. Daily 09:00–18:00 (last order 17:30), closed on Seollal and Chuseok day. 30 Yongmaseo 1-gil, Jeju-si (제주시 용마서1길 30). Kakao Map has free-parking and wheelchair tags. Dietary cues: the lattes contain milk. The ssuk-tteok-latte has rice cake in it, as the name says, so ask about the ingredients. The coffee drinks have caffeine.
  • Kieki Roasting Room (키에키 로스팅 룸) — a roasting room in Idoil-dong (이도일동), the old downtown. Most of its 12 Kakao Map menu items are registered in English — Flat white 5,500 won, Latte 5,500 won, Cappuccino 6,000 won (Only hot), Matcha latte 6,500 won, espresso 4,500 won, Biscotti 2,800 won, Almond bun 5,500 won, Sand cookie 4,500 won, and Latte & biscotti 8,000 won, a set of a latte and biscotti (ask about nuts in this set's biscotti too). Daily 09:00–18:00. 88-1 Jungang-ro, Jeju-si (제주시 중앙로 88-1) (the name on Kakao Map is "Kieki Roasting Room (키에키 로스팅 룸)"). Kakao Map lists no parking lot, so if you drive, find a nearby parking lot first. Dietary cues: the Almond bun contains almonds (nuts). Biscotti is an Italian cookie often baked with nuts such as almonds, so ask whether it contains any. The cardamom latte (kiroro spice oat) has oat in the menu name, but ask when ordering whether the milk is swapped for an oat drink. The matcha and coffee in the Matcha latte have caffeine.

② East (Jocheon·Gujwa) — 3 Venues

Split into Gyorae (the Jocheon hills), Woljeong, and Gimnyeong. Gyorae is in the Jocheon hills, and Woljeong and Gimnyeong are on the northeast coast. Woljeong and Gimnyeong are in the same neighborhoods as the #1407 bakeries, so you can combine them on the same day.

  • Cafe Malo (카페말로) — an outdoor garden cafe in Gyorae-ri (교래리), Jocheon-eup. It's registered on Kakao Map under the name "Malo (말로)". There are 3 menu items — malonara-latte (말로나라떼), Americano, and cafe latte — but the price registration is from 2023-04, so check current prices on Kakao Map on the day. Daily 11:00–18:00 (same on public holidays). 1785-12 Namjo-ro, Jocheon-eup (조천읍 남조로 1785-12). Kakao Map has pets-allowed, wheelchair, and free-parking tags. Of its 7 foreign reviews, 5 are in English. Dietary cues: the lattes contain milk, and the coffee drinks have caffeine.
  • Cafe Guhalgu Jeju (카페구할구 제주) — an old Jeju house cafe in an alley of Woljeong-ri. The only menu item registered on Kakao Map is the Americano at 6,500 won, so check the prices of other drinks and desserts in the shop. Daily 10:00–19:30 — the latest closing of the three in this zone. 48-2 Woljeong 5-gil, Gujwa-eup (구좌읍 월정5길 48-2). Free-parking and takeout tags. 10 foreign reviews (4·3·3). Dietary cues: with only the Americano registered, there are no cues to read from menu names — when you choose a dessert, ask about the ingredients.
  • Cafe Moalboal (카페모알보알) — an ocean-view cafe on the coastal road of Gimnyeong-ri (김녕리). Cafe latte 8,000 won, Jeju matcha latte (제주 말차라떼) 8,500 won, goguma-latte (고구마라떼) (sweet potato latte) 8,500 won — 15 drinks are registered, but the prices for the Americano, cold brew, and brown sugar latte (흑당라떼) and others were registered in 2024-04, so check Kakao Map on the day, and there are no dessert prices. Daily 10:30–18:30. 141 Gujwahaean-ro, Gujwa-eup (구좌읍 구좌해안로 141). Kakao Map has a no-kids zone tag — if you're with children, pick another place. Pets-allowed and free-parking tags too. 10 foreign reviews (5·3·2). Dietary cues: the lattes contain milk, and the matcha latte has matcha, the brown sugar latte has brown sugar, and the goguma-latte has sweet potato in the name. The matcha and coffee have caffeine.

③ West (Aewol·Hallim) — 4 Venues

Aewol (Gwangnyeong·Jangjeon) and the Hallim coast (Gwideok·Geumneung). Three of the four have short operating days or hours, so on a day you tour the west, match the day of the week and the hours first.

  • Mamarong (마마롱) — a cake and eclair dessert cafe along Pyeonghwa-ro in Gwangnyeong-ri (광령리), Aewol-eup. It has eclairs, Mamarong cake (마마롱 케이크), pound cake, and Americano, but the Kakao Map prices are registered as "variable price," so check Kakao Map on the day. The owner's intro says every cake is made the same day with animal-based fresh cream. Closed Mondays, Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00. 2783 Pyeonghwa-ro, Aewol-eup (애월읍 평화로 2783). Pets-allowed, reservation, and takeout tags. Of its 6 foreign reviews, 5 are in English, and none are in Chinese. Dietary cues: the eclairs, cakes, and pound cake are desserts made with flour, eggs, and fresh cream (dairy). The Kakao Map intro mentions a chestnut aroma. The coffee has caffeine.
  • Mampi (맘피) — a sourdough bakery in Jangjeon-ri (장전리), Aewol-eup. Sourdough is bread leavened with a natural starter grown from flour and water instead of commercial yeast — the owner's description says the house brot (하우스브로트) and the choco baguette are made with a natural starter. Choco baguette (초코바게트) 7,000 won, milk loaf (우유식빵) 7,000 won, house brot (하우스브로트) 8,500 won, White (화이트) (milk coffee) 6,000 won. Open only Mon–Thu 11:00–17:00 — closed Fri, Sat, and Sun. 117 Jangjeon-ro, Aewol-eup (애월읍 장전로 117). Kakao Map lists takeout only, so check in the shop whether you can eat in. Dietary cues: baguettes and loaves are wheat breads, so they contain gluten. The milk loaf and the White contain milk, and the choco baguette contains chocolate. The coffee has caffeine.
  • Jejusicha (제주시차) — a drink-focused dessert cafe in Gwideok-ri (귀덕리), Hallim-eup. Honey latte (꿀라떼) 6,500 won, flat white (플랫화이트) 5,500 won, vanilla latte smoothie (바닐라라떼스무디) 7,000 won — 3 items are registered, and there are no dessert prices. Daily 12:00–17:00, and no closed day is registered. 20-14 Gwideok 5-gil, Hallim-eup (한림읍 귀덕5길 20-14). Kakao Map has a no parking lot tag, and a pets-allowed tag too. 12 foreign reviews (4·4·4), the most in the west zone. Dietary cues: the honey latte has honey and milk, and the vanilla latte smoothie is also a latte, so it contains milk. The coffee drinks have caffeine.
  • Janmulgyeol (잔물결) — a hand-drip roastery in Geumneung-ri (금능리), Hallim-eup. The only menu item registered on Kakao Map is the Janmulgyeol blend hand drip (잔물결 블렌드 핸드드립), and the price registration is from 2024-02, so check current prices on Kakao Map on the day. Daily 11:00–18:00. 58-1 Geumneung-gil, Hallim-eup (한림읍 금능길 58-1). Reservation, free-parking, and takeout tags. Janmulgyeol City Roasters (잔물결시티로스터스) (Nohyeong) and Janmulgyeol Coffee House (잔물결커피하우스) (Ido), with similar names, are separate venues on the Jeju City side. Of its 6 foreign reviews, 5 are in English, and none are in Chinese. Dietary cues: hand-drip coffee has caffeine. No other menu items are registered, so look in the shop.

④ Seogwipo·South — 4 Venues

Seoho-dong (서호동) and Gangjeong-dong (강정동) (west) in Seogwipo, Hahyo-dong (하효동), and Deoksu-ri (덕수리) in Andeok. Chaejeomseok is in Seoho-dong, Sisterfield in Gangjeong-dong, Sunaokohi in Hahyo-dong, and Oh My Salon in Andeok, so the four are scattered — look at the locations in a map app first.

  • Chaejeomseok Bakery (채점석 베이커리) — a bakery in Seoho-dong, Seogwipo. It has madeleines (마들렌), tteuigim-soboro (튀김소보루) (fried streusel bun), squid-ink cream cheese (오징어먹물크림치즈), walnut basque (호두바스크), and olive ciabatta (올리브치아바타), but the prices were registered in 2019–2023, so check current prices on Kakao Map on the day. No drinks are registered. Registered as daily 10:00–22:00, the latest of the 13 venues, but the owner's note says "closes early once the prepared bread is sold" (준비된 빵이 팔리면 조기 마감). 29 Seohonam-ro 32beon-gil, Seogwipo (서귀포 서호남로32번길 29) (the name on Kakao Map is "Chaejeomseok Bakery Main Branch (채점석베이커리 본점)"). Reservation and takeout tags. 11 foreign reviews (5·3·3), the most in this zone. Dietary cues: the breads are wheat, so they contain gluten. The squid-ink cream cheese contains squid ink (seafood) and cream cheese (dairy). In the walnut basque, "basque" usually means a Basque cheesecake, so it may contain cream cheese, eggs, and dairy, and it also contains walnuts (nuts). Madeleines commonly contain eggs and butter — if you have an allergy, ask staff.
  • Sisterfield (시스터필드) — a bakery in Gangjeong-dong, Seogwipo (the name on Kakao Map is "Sisterfield Bakery (시스터필드베이커리)"). Olive campagne (올리브 깜빠뉴) 4,500 won (registered 2025-12). The milk tea pound cake (밀크티 파운드), lemon ginger pound cake (레몬진저 파운드), butter scone (버터 스콘), and green olive pastry (그린 올리브 페스토리) are prices registered in 2019–2021, so check Kakao Map on the day. Both pound cake menu names also carry the English "(1 piece)". No drinks are registered. Daily 09:00–18:00. 8 World Cup-ro, Seogwipo (서귀포 월드컵로 8) — other businesses (consulting, tax) share the same name, so find it by the address. Reservation and takeout tags. 9 foreign reviews (5·2·2). Dietary cues: the butter scone has butter (dairy), and the milk tea pound cake has milk tea in it, as the name says. The campagne and pastry are wheat breads, so they contain gluten, and the olive items contain olives.
  • Sunaokohi (스나오코히) — a roasting and hand-drip cafe in Hahyo-dong. It has Americano types A and B, cafe latte, milk tea, matcha latte, and the desserts "Nokcha-ui Mat (녹차의 맛)" and "figue" — the prices were registered in 2023–2024, so check Kakao Map on the day. The bean flavor notes the owner wrote are cinnamon, strawberry, and honey for Americano A and chocolate and biscuit for B, and ask when ordering whether anything like syrup is added separately. Daily 11:00–18:00. 2 Hasinsang-ro 9beon-gil, Seogwipo (서귀포 하신상로9번길 2). 6 foreign reviews (2·2·2). Dietary cues: the lattes and milk tea contain milk. The dessert "figue" is French for fig, and "Nokcha-ui Mat (녹차의 맛)" has green tea in the name. The coffee, matcha, and green tea have caffeine.
  • Oh My Salon (오마이살롱) — a coffee and dessert cafe in Deoksu-ri, Andeok-myeon. The only menu item registered on Kakao Map is bam-jorim (밤조림) (candied chestnuts), made by simmering domestic chestnuts in the shop, and the price registration is from 2023-05, so check the current price and the drink prices on Kakao Map on the day. Mon–Sat 12:00–17:00 (last order 16:30), closed Sunday. 32 Deoksuhoegwan-ro 74beon-gil, Andeok-myeon (안덕면 덕수회관로74번길 32). Pets-allowed, wheelchair, and reservation tags. Of its 6 foreign reviews, 4 are in English. Dietary cues: the bam-jorim contains chestnuts. No drink menu is registered, so look in the shop.

🗺 Route — Pick by Where You're Staying

Jeju's zones are a car or bus ride apart, so a route that hops through several zones in one day doesn't work. Pick one zone based on where you're staying and that day's plans.

A. Staying in Jeju City (airport·old downtown)

  1. Arrival day·departure day Yudong Coffee Sogeum Gongjang Branch near the airport (09:00–18:00, last order 17:30)
  2. Old downtown walking day Kieki Roasting Room (09:00–18:00) — it has no parking lot, so put it on a day you go on foot

B. East-side days (Gyorae·Woljeong·Gimnyeong)

  1. Hills day Cafe Malo in Gyorae (11:00–18:00)
  2. Woljeong beach day Cafe Guhalgu (10:00–19:30) — combine it with the #1407 bakery on the same alley
  3. Gimnyeong coast day Cafe Moalboal (10:30–18:30) — it's a no-kids zone, so if you're with children, switch to the Woljeong side

C. West-side days (Aewol·Hallim)

  1. Mon–Thu Mampi bread in Aewol (11:00–17:00) → Jejusicha in Hallim (12:00–17:00) or Janmulgyeol in Geumneung (11:00–18:00)
  2. Fri–Sun Mampi is closed, so Mamarong cake in Aewol (10:00–18:00) → Janmulgyeol in Geumneung
  3. Monday Mamarong is closed — plan around Mampi, Jejusicha, and Janmulgyeol

D. Staying in Seogwipo·Jungmun

  1. Morning Sisterfield in Gangjeong-dong (from 09:00) — pound cakes are sold per 1 piece
  2. Late morning to early afternoon Chaejeomseok Bakery in Seoho-dong — it closes early once the bread runs out, so go early
  3. Day you head to Hahyo-dong, east of Seogwipo Sunaokohi (11:00–18:00)
  4. Day you head to Andeok (Mon–Sat) Oh My Salon (12:00–17:00)

Mamarong is closed on Monday, Mampi on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, and Oh My Salon on Sunday. Yudong Coffee is closed on Seollal and Chuseok day. If you're going by bus, check the last bus with Kakao Map directions before you leave. For Jeju restaurants, see #1437, the same format.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to places with short operating days or hours late in the afternoon — Mampi is open only Mon–Thu 11:00–17:00 and closed Fri, Sat, and Sun. Jejusicha is open daily 12:00–17:00, and Oh My Salon Mon–Sat 12:00–17:00 (last order 16:30). Mamarong is closed on Monday. If you're touring the west and drop in after 17:00, three of them are already closed.
  2. Trusting the registered "until 22:00" and going in the evening to buy bread — Chaejeomseok Bakery is registered as 10:00–22:00, but the owner's note says "closes early once the prepared bread is sold" (준비된 빵이 팔리면 조기 마감). If bread is the goal, go early.
  3. Going to a different venue with a similar name — Janmulgyeol has Janmulgyeol City Roasters (Nohyeong) and Janmulgyeol Coffee House (Ido) as separate venues in Jeju City. Cafe Malo is registered on Kakao Map as "Malo (말로)", Sisterfield as "Sisterfield Bakery (시스터필드베이커리)", and Chaejeomseok as "Chaejeomseok Bakery Main Branch (채점석베이커리 본점)". In your map app, search by this piece's street addresses, not the venue names.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. If I could pick just one? — The venue with the most foreign reviews in total, and the only one with 5 each in English, Japanese, and Chinese, is Yudong Coffee Sogeum Gongjang Branch (15, 5·5·5). It's near Jeju Airport, so you can fit it into arrival day or departure day. If bread is the goal, Chaejeomseok Bakery with 11 reviews (5·3·3) or Sisterfield (check prices on Kakao Map on the day).
  2. Which places have English menu names on Kakao Map? — The venue that registered its Kakao Map menu names in English is Kieki Roasting Room (most of its 12). At Sisterfield, "(1 piece)" is attached to the two pound cake menu names. For the rest, nothing is known about whether an English menu exists, so have a translation app ready.
  3. If I want to know more about using Jeju bakeries and cafes? — The east Jeju bakeries and the six Gimnyeong venues are in #1407. The in-cafe rules in Korea, like holding a seat, mugs, and the return counter, are in #1378. Jeju restaurants are in #1437, the same format, and the five-day market is in #1348.
