A Day in Busan's Gijang — Haedong Yonggungsa (해동용궁사), the Osiria Coastal Trail, Daebyeon Port (대변항) Anchovies and Gijang Market (기장시장), All on One Bus from Haeundae

Subtitle: Haedong Yonggungsa (해동용궁사) is free, open daily 04:30~19:20 · One bus, the 181 from Haeundae Station Exit 7, covers Yonggungsa → Daebyeon Port → Gijang Market · Fresh anchovy sashimi at Daebyeon Port is a spring and autumn thing · National Science Museum Busan (국립부산과학관) is closed Mondays · 5 spots for hagfish (gomjangeo), seafood kalguksu and seaweed soup (confirmed through Naver reviews) — as of 2026-10-14

🎯 How to Do Gijang

Gijang County (기장군) is the stretch of coast just east of Haeundae in Busan. Many people see only Haedong Yonggungsa (해동용궁사), the temple on the sea, and head back, but the same bus line also passes the Osiria Coastal Trail (오시리아 해안산책로), Daebyeon Port (대변항, anchovies) and Gijang Market (기장시장). Take the 181 bus from Haeundae Station Exit 7 and that one bus gets you to almost all of them. This guide builds a one-day route in that order.

Travel times with no official timetable are marked [Est.]. Before you set out, check again on a map app (Naver Map or KakaoMap).

① Haedong Yonggungsa (해동용궁사) — The Earlier, the Better

  • Free entry, open daily 04:30~19:20 (last entry 18:50), address: 86 Yonggung-gil, Gijang-eup (Visit Busan)
  • Getting there: from Haeundae Station (Line 2) Exit 7, take bus 181, 100 or Haeundae 9; or from Osiria Station (Donghae Line) Exit 1, take bus 139 (Visit Busan)
  • The 108 steps behind the Iljumun gate and the path along the shore have a lot of stairs. Allow extra time with strollers or anyone with trouble walking
  • There is a paid parking lot, but we could not confirm the fee this time. Pets may enter in a carrier or with a muzzle

② Osiria Coastal Trail (오시리아 해안산책로) — The Seaside Path Right Beside Yonggungsa

Open all hours and free, with no parking lot. It runs along the shore past the Yongwangdan (용왕단) altar on the coastal rocks [Secondary]. We could not confirm an official length or walking time. If you want to walk farther, this stretch belongs to Haeparang Trail Course 2 (Gongsu Village → Yonggungsa → Daebyeon Port, about 8km) (Gijang County).

⚠ Bus 185 at the stop right in front of Osiria Station goes the other way (toward Jangsan Station). To reach the science museum, board across the street from the station.

④ Daebyeon Port (대변항) — The Anchovy Harbor

The port's fresh anchovy sashimi and anchovy stew are its signature dishes (Gijang County). Anchovies are caught twice a year, in spring and autumn, and the Gijang Anchovy Festival (기장멸치축제) is held around April each year (the 30th, 4/24~26 in 2026, per press reports [Secondary]). From Haeundae Station, take the 181 and get off at "Daebyeon Port Entrance (대변항입구)" (Visit Busan). Out of season, there may be no anchovy sashimi.

⑤ Gijang Market (기장시장) — Seaweed and Snow Crab

In Daera-ri, Gijang-eup, open daily 07:00~22:00 (no closing days), about 6 min on foot from Gijang Station (Donghae Line) Exit 1. It is known for Gijang seaweed in spring, cutlassfish in autumn and the snow crab alley (Visit Busan).

⑥ Farther Out — Jukseong, Ilgwang, Chilam and Ahopsan Forest

  • Jukseong Church (죽성성당): not a real church but a building put up as the set for the 2009 SBS drama "Dream" (remodeled in 2016, Gijang County). Its seaside exterior is a popular photo spot. Sources disagree on whether the interior is open. There is no public transit information, so a taxi is easiest
  • Ilgwang Beach (일광해수욕장) and Chilam (칠암): on Haeparang Trail Course 3 (Daebyeon Port → Jukseong → Ilgwang → Imrang, about 20.5km). Chilam is famous for conger eel (Gijang County)
  • Ahopsan Forest (아홉산숲) (Cheolma-myeon): open daily 09:00~18:00 (entry until 17:00), adults ₩8,000 (~$6), no reservation needed for individuals. No pets or strollers (Visit Busan)

⚠ 5 Traps That Actually Catch People

1. Science museum bus 185 direction — Not the stop in front of Osiria Station but the one across the street. Closed Mondays.

2. Luge closing time — 18:00 or 20:00 depending on the source. Check the official site on the day.

3. Walking to Yonggungsa — Walking from Osiria Station is far. Take bus 139.

4. Jukseong Church is a film set — It is not a church that holds Mass. Go by taxi.

5. Anchovy season — Fresh anchovy sashimi is a spring and autumn thing. In October, the anchovy festival (April) is already over.

⚙ FAQ 3

1. Is one day from Haeundae enough? — Yonggungsa, the coastal trail, Daebyeon Port and Gijang Market fit in one day on the 181 line. If you add the luge and the science museum, split it up like Plan B.

2. Which places charge admission? — Haedong Yonggungsa, the coastal trail and the exterior of Jukseong Church are free. The science museum is ₩3,000 (~$2.25) and Ahopsan Forest is ₩8,000 (~$6). Check Lotte World and the luge prices on their official sites.

3. What are the Donghae Line fares? — We could not confirm the official fare table this time. Just tap your transit card (#1298).
