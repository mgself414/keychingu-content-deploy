⚠ 3 Common Mistakes International Visitors Make

1. "Korean alcohol = soju (소주) only"

The most common misconception among foreign visitors. Korean drinking culture goes far beyond soju (소주; distilled Korean spirit, ~20% ABV). Jongno (종로) traditional taverns — Ho Seonsaengjeon (호선생전), Uri Sool-jip Daramjwi (우리술집 다람쥐), and Gyodong Jeon Seonsaeng (교동전선생) — offer a full traditional spirits menu: makgeolli (막걸리; fermented rice brew, 6~8% ABV), yakju (약주; clear fermented rice wine), and cheongju (청주; premium refined rice wine) — all three side by side.

2. Thinking Ikseon-dong wine bars are just regular wine bars

The wine bars in Ikseon-dong (익선동) — Gireogi Dungji (기러기둥지), La Ikseon (라익선), and Woods Whale (우즈 웨일) — aren't ordinary wine spots. The real appeal is hanok (한옥; traditional Korean wooden architecture) ambiance + Korean fusion anju (안주; drinking snacks/dishes) + wine pairing, with selections curated by Korean designers. The ideal visitor itinerary: stroll the Ikseon-dong (익선동) hanok (한옥) alley, then settle in for a glass of wine.

3. Confusing pojangmacha (포차), izakaya, and sool-jip (주점)

  • Pojangmacha / Pocha (포차) — Nakwon Pocha (낙원포차), NY Pocha (뉴욕포차): Korean-style street/indoor drinking tents (think: open-air casual bar). Casual vibe, wide anju (안주) selection, great for groups.
  • Izakaya — Baesusa (배수사): Japanese-style small bar, counter seating, best for 1–3 people.
  • Sool-jip / Jujeon (주점) — Ho Seonsaengjeon (호선생전), Uri Sool-jip (우리술집): Traditional Korean tavern with hansik (한식; Korean cuisine) anju (안주) and traditional spirits, group-friendly.
  • Dining bar / Yori-jujeon (요리주점) — Bakhakdasik (박학다식), Bogul House (보글하우스): Food-forward drinking spot; casual, meal-compatible.

The Jongno (종로) · Ikseon-dong (익선동) · Jonggak (종각) Bar Scene

Jongno (종로) is the heartland of Korean traditional drinking culture and Ikseon-dong (익선동) hanok (한옥) wine bars — distinct from Gangnam (강남) (luxury), Hongdae (홍대) (indie/casual), and Itaewon (이태원) (global pub scene).

Central Jongno (종로) & Jonggak (종각) are dense with traditional Korean taverns, pojangmacha (포차; Korean-style drinking tents), and beer bars — Ho Seonsaengjeon (호선생전), Uri Sool-jip Daramjwi (우리술집 다람쥐), Nakwon Pocha (낙원포차), Seoul 88 Beer (서울88맥주), all within 5 minutes on foot from Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역) or Jonggak Station (종각역).

Ikseon-dong (익선동) is the epicenter of hanok (한옥) wine bars — Gireogi Dungji (기러기둥지) and La Ikseon (라익선) sit right inside the hanok (한옥) alley. A top Instagram hotspot for international visitors, combining traditional architecture with wine and Korean fusion bites.

Jongno-gu Office (종로구청) & Insadong (인사동) lean more traditional — Gyodong Jeon Seonsaeng (교동전선생) and Woods Whale (우즈 웨일) blend classic tavern and modern wine bar sensibilities. Best paired with an Insadong (인사동) daytime stroll.
