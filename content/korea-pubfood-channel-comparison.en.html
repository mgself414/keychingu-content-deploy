Korean Anju Guide for Foreigners: Jangsudong (장수동) vs. Gwangjang (광장) vs. Pojangmacha (포장마차) vs. Hanjeongsik (한정식) — Where Should You Get a Drink?

Subtitle: A side-by-side comparison of Korea's 4 anju (안주, drinking-food) channels + the top pick for 5 visitor segments + a decision guide by time, budget, and company

ISSUE 03 · #863 · 2026-06-09

🎯 Why a 4-Channel Anju Comparison Guide

[#861 The 6-Type Anju Matrix](#861) + [#862 The Anju Workflow](#862) cover the basics — but picking a channel is the next decision. Korea's anju channels break down into (1) Jangsudong (장수동) Jokbal (족발, pig's trotters) Alley (Seongbuk (성북), Korea's #1, open 17:00–03:00 for late-night), (2) Gwangjang (광장) Market Bossam (보쌈, boiled pork wraps) Alley (the #1 vlog spot, daytime, MZ Instagram), (3) Jongno (종로) Pojangmacha (포장마차, street food tent) (soju (소주) diehards, golbaengi-muchim (골뱅이무침, spicy whelk salad)), and (4) Hanjeongsik (한정식, Korean set-course meal) Course (authentic suyuk (수육, boiled pork slices) and hongeo-samhap (홍어삼합, fermented skate combo)) — four very different worlds. Prices swing from 12,000 won to 150,000 won, and the vibe shifts from late-night regulars to vlog crowds to soju drinkers to formal dining. This guide compares all four channels head-to-head, plus the top pick for each segment (solo, couple, family, MZ, and budget travelers).

🎯 Top Pick by Segment

👤 Solo Travelers

  • Top pick: Gwangjang (광장) Bossam (보쌈) (daytime, vlog-famous, single portions OK)
  • 2nd: Pojangmacha (포장마차) (for soju diehards)
  • 3rd: Jangsudong (장수동) Jokbal (족발) (single portions available)
  • Skip: Hanjeongsik (한정식) (poor value for solo diners)

💑 Couples

  • Top pick: Jangsudong (장수동) Jokbal (족발) (a late-night date)
  • 2nd: Gwangjang (광장) Bossam (보쌈) (a daytime date, vlog-famous)
  • 3rd: Hanjeongsik (한정식) (a formal date)
  • Skip: Pojangmacha (포장마차) (less romantic, old-school nofo (노포) vibe)

👨‍👩‍👧 Families

  • Top pick: Gwangjang (광장) Bossam (보쌈) (daytime, family-friendly, vlog-famous)
  • 2nd: Hanjeongsik (한정식) (a proper family meal)
  • 3rd: Jangsudong (장수동) Jokbal (족발) (family dinner)
  • Skip: Pojangmacha (포장마차) (a drinking spot, not for kids)

🎨 MZ Trendsetters

  • Top pick: Gwangjang (광장) Bossam (보쌈) (#1 for vlogs and Instagram)
  • 2nd: Jangsudong (장수동) Jokbal (족발) (late-night vlog content)
  • 3rd: MZ-favorite jokbal (족발) chains (viral hits)
  • Skip: Hanjeongsik (한정식) (not really an MZ scene)

🎒 Backpackers & Budget Travelers

  • Top pick: Pojangmacha (포장마차) (12,000–25,000 won, great value)
  • 2nd: Gwangjang (광장) Bossam (보쌈) (split one order between 2–3 people)
  • 3rd: Jangsudong (장수동) Jokbal (족발) (split one order)
  • Skip: Hanjeongsik (한정식) (too pricey)

🦶 4-Channel Deep Dive

🦶 Jangsudong (장수동) Jokbal (족발) Alley

Core value: Korea's #1 + a late-night favorite + open 17:00–03:00.

  • Location: The Jangsudong (장수동) area of Seongbuk-gu (성북구), Seoul
  • Signature: authentic jokbal (족발), mini-jok (mini-size portion), and ban-ban (half spicy, half soy-garlic)
  • Price anchor: 25,000–50,000 won per order (2–3 people)
  • Hours: 17:00–03:00 (Korea's #1 late-night pick)
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (English menus, chain locations)
  • Recommended spots: the original Jangsudong (장수동) shops and jokbal (족발) chain outlets

🥬 Gwangjang (광장) Market Bossam (보쌈) Alley

Core value: Korea's #1 vlog spot + MZ Instagram fame + daytime family dining.

  • Location: Gwangjang (광장) Market, Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul
  • Signature: bossam (보쌈), bossam-kimchi, wrap greens — the #1 MZ Instagram spot
  • Price anchor: 20,000–40,000 won per order (2–3 people)
  • Hours: 09:00–22:00 (day and evening)
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (#1 vlog spot, English menus)
  • MZ vlog: the bossam (보쌈) alley strip, alongside the bindae-tteok (빈대떡, mung bean pancake) and mayak-gimbap (마약김밥, "addictive" mini gimbap) cluster

🍶 Jongno (종로) & Gangnam (강남) Pojangmacha (포장마차)

Core value: Korea's #1 soju spot + golbaengi-muchim (골뱅이무침) + authentic nofo (노포) atmosphere.

  • Location: the Jongno (종로) pojangmacha (포장마차) alley, Gangnam (강남) pojangmacha, and Dongdaemun (동대문)
  • Signature: golbaengi-muchim (골뱅이무침), gyeran-mari (계란말이), udon, ramyeon
  • Price anchor: 12,000–25,000 won per anju (안주)
  • Hours: 18:00–02:00
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★ (Korean-only, authentic — bring a translation app)
  • For soju diehards: soju (소주) + golbaengi-muchim (골뱅이무침) = Korea's #1 classic pairing

🍱 Hanjeongsik (한정식) Course

Core value: A 6-course meal + authentic suyuk (수육) and hongeo-samhap (홍어삼합) + royal-court roots.

  • Recommended spots:
  • The Shilla Seoul (신라호텔) La Yeon (라연) (Michelin 3-star) — authentic suyuk (수육)
  • Samcheonggak (삼청각) — authentic
  • Insadong (인사동) Hanjeongsik — authentic and affordable
  • Jeolla (전라) Hanjeongsik — authentic hongeo-samhap (홍어삼합)
  • Price anchor: 40,000–150,000 won for a hanjeongsik course
  • Hours: 11:30–22:00
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "All 4 channels are basically the same": Price, vibe, and hours are completely different. Late-night = Jangsudong (장수동) / vlog-famous = Gwangjang (광장) / soju = pojangmacha (포장마차) / authentic = hanjeongsik (한정식).

2. "Jangsudong is a daytime spot": Jangsudong (장수동) runs 17:00–03:00, a late-night favorite — not daytime.

3. "Pojangmacha has English menus": Pojangmacha (포장마차) is Korean-only and authentic — bring a translation app.

4. "Gwangjang bossam is just a meal": Gwangjang (광장) works as both a vlog-famous snack stop and a full meal — one order is meant to be shared by 2–3 people.

5. "Hanjeongsik is always expensive": Hanjeongsik (한정식) ranges 40,000–150,000 won — affordable lunch options exist.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. Which channel for your first anju experience?: Start with Gwangjang (광장) Bossam (보쌈) (daytime, the #1 pick for first-timers) → then Jangsudong (장수동) Jokbal (족발) (late-night).

2. Best for Instagram and vlogs?: Gwangjang (광장) Bossam (보쌈) (#1), Jangsudong (장수동) Jokbal (족발), and Jongno (종로) Pojangmacha (포장마차).

3. Best for soju diehards?: Pojangmacha (포장마차) (#1, most authentic) plus Jangsudong (장수동) Jokbal (족발).

4. Best for dates and anniversaries?: Jangsudong (장수동) Jokbal (족발) or Hanjeongsik (한정식).

5. Best with family?: Gwangjang (광장) Bossam (보쌈) or Hanjeongsik (한정식).

6. Allergies and pregnancy?: See the [#861 disclaimer](#861).

7. Payment and tipping?: Card is fine everywhere / markets and pojangmacha (포장마차) sometimes require cash / no tipping.

8. VAT refund?: Food and drink generally aren't eligible.

⚠ Medical, Safety & Dietary Disclaimer

All 4 anju channels serve standard Korean food. Pregnant travelers and children can eat with limits (no alcohol, no hongeo (홍어)). Disclose allergies (pork, seafood, chili) ahead of time. Alcohol is off-limits for pregnant travelers and minors. If you suspect food poisoning, call ☎ 1339 (medical, English available). This guide is general information, not medical advice.

Dietary restriction fallbacks:

  • Halal & kosher: All 4 channels involve pork or seafood → alternatives are bibimbap, japchae, and temple food ([#854](#854)).
  • Vegetarian & vegan: None of the 4 channels are vegetarian → go for temple food or bibimbap instead.
  • Gluten-free: Suyuk (수육), bossam (보쌈), and jokbal (족발) are OK / the somyeon (소면, thin noodles) in golbaengi-muchim (골뱅이무침) is not / some jeyuk (제육) marinades are not.

  • Average cost across the 4 channels (as of June 2026):

  • Gwangjang (광장) Bossam (보쌈) = 20,000–40,000 won per order (2–3 people)
  • Jangsudong (장수동) Jokbal (족발) = 25,000–50,000 won per order (2–3 people)
  • Hanjeongsik (한정식) course = 40,000–150,000 won per person
  • Payment and tipping: Card is fine everywhere / markets and pojangmacha (포장마차) sometimes require cash / no tipping
  • Emergency: ☎ 1339 (medical, English available) / ☎ 1330 (tourist interpretation)
