Korean Tteok Ordering & Holiday Workflow — From Seollal Tteokguk to Chuseok Songpyeon Making

> Subtitle: 5-step workflow for foreign tourists + holiday food etiquette, hands-on experiences, and payment tips

ISSUE 03 · #856 · 2026-06-09

🎯 Why a Korean Tteok Workflow Guide

Even if you know the [#855 tteok 6-type matrix](#855) — ordering, holidays, and hands-on experiences are a separate story. Korean tteok (떡, rice cake) comes through (a) traditional tteok shops (long-standing stores in Gwangjang Market and Insadong), (b) the holiday seasons (Seollal (설날, Lunar New Year) and Chuseok (추석, Korean harvest festival) = the #1 rice cakes for family gatherings), (c) MZ cafes (injeolmi (인절미) toast, chaltteok-bokki (찰떡볶이)), and (d) first-birthday and wedding orders (baekseolgi (백설기) and injeolmi sold by the gwan (관, traditional unit ≈3.75 kg)). Foreign visitors typically (1) don't know the holiday dates or their meaning, (2) don't know about songpyeon (송편)-making experiences, (3) don't know how to eat tteokguk (떡국, rice cake soup), and (4) don't know vacuum-packed tteok makes a take-home souvenir. This guide covers the 5 steps + holiday etiquette + English phrases.

★ The 5-Step Workflow at a Glance

Step Action Time Key Point for Foreign Visitors
1️⃣ Choose a channel Tteok shop / supermarket / MZ cafe / hands-on venue Instant Match it to the season and purpose
2️⃣ Pick a menu item/product Tteok + holiday season + vacuum packing 2–5 min Check for season-limited menus
3️⃣ Receive & taste In-store tasting / tteok-shop pickup / hands-on class Varies Songpyeon making = 1–2h experience
4️⃣ Eat & pair Pair with tea, soup, or side dishes 15–60 min Classic pairings for tteokguk, songpyeon, injeolmi
5️⃣ Buy & pay In store, duty-free, or online 1–5 min Vacuum-packed = OK as a take-home souvenir

> Total time: Tteok shop = 10–30 min / eating in store = 30–60 min / songpyeon class = 1–2h

🗓 Holiday-Season Workflows

Seollal (Lunar New Year, lunar 1/1) Workflow

1. Prepare tteokguk: Sliced garaetteok (가래떡) + beef broth, mandu (만두, dumplings), egg, and gim (김, dried seaweed)

2. New Year greetings: Perform sebae (세배, the New Year bow) to elders and say "Saehae bok mani badeuseyo (새해 복 많이 받으세요)"

3. Eat tteokguk: One bowl = symbolically turning one year older

4. Sebaetdon (세뱃돈): New Year cash gifts from elders to children

5. Seollal 2026: February 17 on the solar calendar (lunar 1/1)

Chuseok (Korean Harvest Festival, lunar 8/15) Workflow

1. Songpyeon-making experience (with a family or at a hands-on venue)

2. Charye (차례, ancestral rite) & seongmyo (성묘, visiting family graves): Family gathering

3. Eat songpyeon: Fillings of sesame, beans, or chestnut

4. Chuseok 2026: September 25 on the solar calendar (lunar 8/15)

Recommended Experiences for Foreign Visitors

  • Songpyeon-making class in Bukchon Hanok Village (autumn, Sep–Oct) — #1 for foreign visitors
  • Tteok-making class in Jeonju Hanok Village
  • Traditional tteok-shop tasting tour in Insadong

🌶 Sample English Phrases for Foreign Visitors

  • "떡국 하나" = "One tteokguk, please"
  • "송편 10개" = "Ten songpyeon, please"
  • "인절미 작게 잘라주세요" = "Cut injeolmi small, please" (choking caution for children)
  • "송편 만들기 체험" = "Songpyeon making experience"
  • "진공 포장 사고 싶어요" = "I want vacuum-packed rice cake, please"
  • "새해 복 많이 받으세요" (sebae) = "Happy New Year (Lunar)"
  • "카드 돼요?" = "Card accepted?"

⚠ 7 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. "Tteok = only tteokbokki (떡볶이)": There's a full set of 6 types + holiday and feast classics.

2. "Seollal & Chuseok = solar calendar": Korean holidays follow the lunar calendar (Seollal lunar 1/1, Chuseok lunar 8/15) → the solar dates change every year.

3. "Songpyeon = year-round": Songpyeon is Chuseok-only (Sep–Oct).

4. "Garaetteok = just regular tteok": Garaetteok is the classic Seollal tteokguk rice cake, shaped as a long cylinder.

5. "Injeolmi = just a regular snack": Injeolmi is the classic feast and wedding tteok, coated in roasted soybean powder.

6. "Baekseolgi = just regular tteok": Baekseolgi is the classic first-birthday and celebration tteok (from ₩30,000 (~$23) per gwan).

7. "Tteok = no choking risk": Garaetteok and injeolmi carry a higher choking risk (cut small for children and seniors).

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. Which tteok first: Tteokguk (Seollal classic) → songpyeon (Chuseok) → injeolmi, in that order.

2. Instagram & vlogs: Chuseok songpyeon making, MZ injeolmi toast, traditional tteok shops in Insadong.

3. MZ trends: Chaltteok-bokki, injeolmi toast, injeolmi bingsu (빙수, shaved ice), MZ songpyeon.

4. Recommended experiences: Songpyeon-making classes in Bukchon and Jeonju (autumn, Sep–Oct).

5. Gifts & duty-free: Vacuum-packed tteok works as a take-home souvenir (₩15,000–30,000 (~$11–23)).

6. Allergies: Declare beans, sesame, tree nuts, and honey in advance.

7. Payment & tipping: Tteok shops and duty-free = card OK / markets = cash / no tipping.

8. VAT refund: Not for food and drinks / duty-free tteok = refund possible.

⚠ Medical, Safety & Dietary Disclaimer

Tteok is ordinary food. Choking risk (garaetteok and injeolmi — cut small for children and seniors). Pregnant travelers = moderate amounts of soybean powder and nuts. Children = choking caution #1. Declare allergies (beans, sesame, tree nuts, honey) in advance. If choking or food poisoning is suspected, call ☎ 1339 (medical, English available). This guide is general information, not medical advice.

Dietary-restriction fallback:

  • Halal & kosher: All 6 types contain no animal ingredients = OK (check for honey).
  • Vegetarian & vegan: All 6 types are vegetarian OK (not vegan if honey is included).
  • Gluten-free: All 6 types are 100% rice = gluten-free OK.
