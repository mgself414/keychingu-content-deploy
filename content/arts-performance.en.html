⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Performance = Korean only": Korea's No. 1 performance hall = Some performances with English subtitles (LG Arts Center·Seoul Arts Center). Indie musical, jazz, dance = non-verbal OK.

2. "Daehakro Performance = Korean": Daehakro 50+ small theater = Korean indie-oriented English works by season + Dance/musical non-verbal works OK.

3. "Korean Film Festival = Busan Bay": BIFF (Busan) + BFFC (Bucheon) + JIFF (Jeonju) + Seoul Environmental Film Festival, Seoul International Pride Film Festival, etc. 5+ international film festivals throughout the year.

① General performance hall (3)

1. Seoul Arts Center ★ Korea’s No. 1 performance hall

  • Address: 2406 Nambusunhwan-ro, Seocho-gu (Nambu Terminal Station, Line 3) (#173)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Korea’s No. 1 comprehensive performance + Hangaram Art Museum, Calligraphy Museum, Opera House, and Concert Hall
  • Signature: Opened in 1988 + Concert Hall (2,500 seats), Opera House (2,300 seats), Jayu Small Theater, Hangaram Art Museum
  • Reservation: Reservation office English UI (#206 sister)

2. LG Art Center (Magog)

  • Address: 136 Magokjungang-ro, Gangseo-gu (#175)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎ — 2022 Gangnam → Magok Relocation to No. 1 concert hall in Korea
  • Signature: Concert, musical, play, ballet + some English subtitles
  • Details: #175 Magok/Gimpo Airport Sister

3. Sejong Center for the Performing Arts (Gwanghwamun)

  • Address: 175 Sejong-daero, Jongno-gu
  • Foreigners ★: ◎ — Korea’s No. 1 municipal performance hall
  • Signature: Opened in 1978, Concert Hall, Grand Theater + Adjacent to Gwanghwamun Square

② Daehakro Indie Musical (3)

4. ARKO Arts Theater ★ Korea’s No. 1 indie musical

  • Address: 100 Daehak-ro, Jongno-gu (Line 4 Hyehwa Station) (#170)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Korea’s No. 1 indie/experiment
  • Signature: Opened in 1981, Korea’s first public indie performance venue in 1981 + Some English subtitles

5. Dongsoong Art Center

  • Address: Daehak-ro area, Jongno-gu (#170)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎ — 50+ 1 Small Theater Street
  • Signature: Daehakro indie musical/theater cluster

6. Hakchun Blue

  • Address: Daehak-ro, Jongno-gu (#170)
  • Foreigner ★: ○ — Korea’s No. 1 indie (1991~)
  • Signature: The birthplace of Korean indie rock + No. 1 in Korean indie music, including Kim Gwang-seok, Jeong Tae-chun, and Nochatsa

③ Movie theater·IMAX (3)

7. CGV Yongsan I’Park Mall ★ IMAX No. 1

  • Address: 55 Hangang-daero 23-gil, Yongsan-gu (#187 Yongsan)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎ — Korea’s No. 1 IMAX·Soundplex
  • Signature: Korea’s largest screen + English subtitles for foreigners

8. Megabox COEX (Gangnam)

  • Address: COEX Mall, 513 Yeongdong-daero, Gangnam-gu
  • Foreigners ★: ◎ — COEX Integrated/Dolby Cinema

9. Lotte Cinema World Tower (Jamsil) ★ Superplex

  • Address: Lotte World Mall, 300 Olympic-ro, Songpa-gu (#82 Jamsil)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎ — Korea’s No. 1 Superplex + Lotte Tower integration

④ Film Festival (3 + additional 4)

10. BIFF, Busan International Film Festival

  • When: First week of October every year (10 days)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Asia’s No. 1 film festival
  • Signature: Founded in 1996, Asia’s largest film festival, Haeundae Film Center

11. Bucheon International Fantastic Film Festival (BFFC)

  • When: Every July
  • Foreigners ★: ◎ — Korea’s No. 1 fantasy and horror film festival
  • Signature: Founded in 1997, No. 1 among foreign enthusiasts

12. Jeonju International Film Festival (JIFF)

  • Period: April to May every year
  • Foreigners ★: ◎ — Korea’s No. 1 independent and experimental film festival
  • Signature: Founded in 2000, foreign enthusiast deep dive

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Reservation: Booking site English UI (Interpark, Yes24, Ticket Link). Foreign payment OK.

2. English subtitles: Part of the LG Arts Center/Seoul Arts Center/Sejong General Performance.

3. Daehakro Indie Stage: Mainly Korean, but non-verbal dance and jazz are OK.

4. Available to foreigners at movie theaters: English subtitles are OK in almost all movie theaters.

5. International Film Festival: BIFF (Busan, October), BFFC (Bucheon, July), JIFF (Jeonju, April-May).

6. Korea’s No. 1 Performance/Film Mania Day 1: Seoul Arts Center Hangaram Art Museum + Performance + LG Arts Center/CGV IMAX Combination.
