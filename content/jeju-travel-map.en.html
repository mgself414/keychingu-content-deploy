⚠ 3 Jeju mistakes

1. "Jeju = only a tourist destination" — No experience of Olle Trail or Haenyeo: Only recognized tourist attraction hopping. In fact, Olle Trail 26 Course 437km walking tour + Haenyeo experience/museum is Jeju’s signature deep dive.

2. "Jeju food = only black pork" — raw fish, seafood, tangerines : Only black pork is recognized. In reality, it is a full experience of Dongmun Market raw fish, seafood + Hallabong, tangerines, green tea.

3. "Jeju = 2 nights only" — Udo and Biyangdo outskirts not used: Only the main island is recognized. In reality, 3 nights and 4 days + Udo and Biyangdo outskirts added = full experience.
