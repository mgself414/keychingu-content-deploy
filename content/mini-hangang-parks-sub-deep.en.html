Mangwon and Hapjeong Guide: Mangwon Market, Mangwon World Cup Market, Mangwonjeong Site, Mangwon Retention Basin Sports Park, Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine, Yanghwajin Foreign Missionary Cemetery and Yanghwajin Hall Exhibition Center

Subtitle: Mangwon Market (hours vary by shop) · Mangwon World Cup Market (10:00~22:00, open year-round) · Mangwonjeong Site (always open, free) · Mangwon Retention Basin Sports Park (always open) · Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine (09:30~17:00, closed Mondays) · Yanghwajin Foreign Missionary Cemetery (Mon~Sat 10:00~17:00, closed Sundays) · Yanghwajin Hall Exhibition Center (10:00~15:30) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to visit in Mangwon-dong and Hapjeong-dong in Mapo-gu, Seoul. They are two traditional markets with plenty of food, the pavilion Mangwonjeong that gave the neighborhood its name, a Catholic martyrs' shrine, and the Yanghwajin cemetery where foreign missionaries are buried. Mangwonjeong Site, Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine, Yanghwajin Foreign Missionary Cemetery and Yanghwajin Hall Exhibition Center are registered as free to enter. Mangwon Hangang Park (망원한강공원) and Yanghwa Hangang Park (양화한강공원) are in the #706 Hangang Parks Guide: 9 Hangang Parks, Nanji, Mangwon and Ichon on the North Bank, Gangseo, Yanghwa, Banpo, Jamwon, Jamsil and Gwangnaru on the South Bank.

The closed days differ. Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine is closed on Mondays, and Yanghwajin Foreign Missionary Cemetery and Yanghwajin Hall Exhibition Center are closed on Sundays.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Mangwon-dong Markets — Mangwon Market and Mangwon World Cup Market

Mangwon Market (망원시장) [Poeun-ro 6-gil 27 (포은로6길 27), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ 02-335-3591] is a traditional market that sells street food, farm and livestock products, fruit and household goods. It is near Mangwon Station (망원역) on Subway Line 6 and is known together with Mangridan-gil (망리단길).

  • Opening hours·closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Available.

Mangwon World Cup Market (망원월드컵시장) [Mangwon-dong (망원동), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ 02-322-6656] is a traditional market that started in 1975 and was modernized into an arcade-style market in 2006. About 50 shops sell agricultural and marine products, meat, snack food and household goods, and many of the shops focus on wholesale. It is about a 10-minute walk from Mangwon Station (망원역).

  • Opening hours: 10:00~22:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

📍 Pavilion and Sports Park — Mangwonjeong Site and Mangwon Retention Basin Sports Park

Mangwonjeong Site (망원정 터) [Donggyo-ro 8an-gil 23 (동교로8안길 23), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ Mapo-gu Office (마포구청) 02-3153-8372] is a pavilion in Hapjeong-dong (합정동) and a Seoul Metropolitan Monument. In 1424, the 6th year of King Sejong (세종), Grand Prince Hyoryeong (효령대군) built a villa here, and when Sejong visited the next year, welcome rain fell, so he is said to have named it "Huiujeong (희우정)". In 1484, the 15th year of King Seongjong (성종), Grand Prince Wolsan (월산대군) renovated it extensively and called it Mangwonjeong (망원정). It disappeared because of the great flood of 1925 and development of the Han River, and it was restored in October 1989. The neighborhood name Mangwon-dong (망원동) comes from this pavilion.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Mangwon Retention Basin Sports Park (망원유수지체육공원) [Worldcup-ro 25-gil 190 (월드컵로25길 190), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ Mapo-gu Office Sports Promotion Division (마포구청 체육진흥과) 02-3153-9873] is a sports park that opened in 2004 after a retention basin used for flood control was redeveloped. It has a soccer field, a foot volleyball court, a basketball court, an in-line skating practice area, a multipurpose track and a gateball court, and it has night lighting.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Use is restricted when it rains or a weather advisory is issued.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

📍 Shrine and Cemetery — Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine, Yanghwajin Foreign Missionary Cemetery and Yanghwajin Hall Exhibition Center

Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine (절두산 순교성지) [Tojeong-ro 6 (토정로 6), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ 02-3142-4434] is where many Catholics were executed during the Byeongin Persecution (병인박해) of 1866. It was originally called Jamdubong (잠두봉) because it resembles a silkworm's head, but after that event it got the name Jeoldusan (切頭山), meaning "head cut off". The Catholic Church in Korea opened a martyrs' memorial hall in October 1967 to mark the 100th anniversary of the martyrdom. Within the shrine are an exhibition hall of historical records and relics of the Catholic Church in Korea, a reliquary room holding the remains of 28 saints, a pilgrimage chapel and an outdoor exhibition hall.

  • Opening hours: 09:30~17:00. Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Fee: Free. It is registered that pilgrims can make voluntary offerings. Parking: Available.

Yanghwajin Foreign Missionary Cemetery (양화진외국인선교사묘원) [Yanghwajin-gil 46 (양화진길 46), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ 02-332-9174] holds the graves of 417 missionaries from 15 countries, including South Africa, New Zealand, Denmark, Germany and the United States. They influenced Korean society by founding hospitals and schools, and they include Bethell (배설), Hulbert (헐버트), Underwood (언더우드) and Appenzeller (아펜젤러). It is managed by the Centennial Church of Korean Christianity (한국기독교 100주년 기념교회).

  • Opening hours: Mon~Sat 10:00~17:00. Closed days: Every Sunday. Fee: Free.
  • Guided tours: Mon~Fri 10:00, 11:30, 14:00. Sat 10:00, 11:30, 13:30, 15:00. There are no tours on January 1, during the Seollal (설) holiday, during the Chuseok (추석) holiday, or on Christmas. It is registered that visitors should arrive 15 minutes before the tour starts.
  • Tour reservations: Reserve on the website from 6 months ahead until 9:00 PM the day before the visit. Same-day reservations are not accepted. If you need a foreign-language tour, write it in the other notes when you apply.
  • Foreign-language tours: English, Japanese and Chinese. The video linked by the QR code on the foreign-language tour leaflet can also be used.
  • Parking: Available. It is registered that large buses should use Mangwon Parking Lot No. 1 (망원 제1주차장).

Yanghwajin Hall Exhibition Center (양화진홀 전시관) [same address, ☎ 02-332-9174] is an exhibition hall inside the cemetery. It uses materials arranged by theme to show who the missionaries were, why they came to Joseon (조선), and what they did.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~15:30. Closed days: Every Sunday. Fee: Free.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine is closed on Mondays, and the Yanghwajin cemetery and exhibition hall are closed on Sundays — by the registered values, all three are open on Tuesday through Saturday.
  2. Guided tours of the Yanghwajin cemetery must be reserved by 9:00 PM the day before — same-day reservations are not accepted.
  3. Yanghwajin Hall Exhibition Center closes at 15:30 — the cemetery is open until 17:00.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Can I get a foreign-language tour at the Yanghwajin cemetery?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, tours are available in English, Japanese and Chinese. When you reserve on the website, write in the other notes that you need a foreign-language tour.

Q. Which market is open until late?

A. Mangwon World Cup Market (망원월드컵시장) is registered as open 10:00~22:00, year-round. Mangwon Market (망원시장) varies by shop.
