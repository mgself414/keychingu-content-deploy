🎯 Why a K-pop Fan's Full Guide?

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by International Visitors

1. "K-pop stores are only in Myeongdong (명동)": A true deep dive requires visiting the full set of 4 major headquarters: HYBE (Yongsan (용산)), SM Town (Gangnam (강남)), YG (Hapjeong (합정)), and JYP (Gangdong (강동)), in addition to Myeongdong (명동).

2. "Headquarters are inaccessible": HYBE INSIGHT (Yongsan (용산)) and SM Town COEX Artium (Gangnam (강남)) allow free entry for international fans (외국인 매니아 자유 입장) + merchandise stores (굿즈 매장) + cafes (카페). The exterior of the headquarters buildings are also popular photo spots (photo spot).

3. "Concerts are expensive": Besides concerts (콘서트), K-pop dance classes (e.g., 1MILLION Dance Studio (원밀리언 댄스 스튜디오) in Hongdae (홍대))·K-pop cafes (YG·SM collaborations)·KARTS (한국예술종합학교), Korea's top K-pop training institution (K-pop 양성기관), are also accessible for visits.
