⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. Perceiving "Gangnam = Hansik (Korean food)·BBQ·Samgyeopsal (pork belly) only" + Skipping Chinese/Japanese Cuisine

This is the most common mistake foreigners make when planning their Gangnam (강남) itinerary. They often only associate Gangnam (강남) with Hansik (Korean food) BBQ, samgyeopsal (pork belly), and chicken. However, Gangnam-gu (강남구) is actually a mecca for luxury Japanese omakase (chef's choice dining) and modern Chinese dining in Korea — with many Japanese and Chinese native chef restaurants in Apgujeong (압구정) and Cheongdam (청담). Eating only Hansik (Korean food) for 7-8 days can become monotonous for foreigners — we recommend adding one Chinese and one Japanese restaurant.

2. Viewing Apgujeong (압구정), Cheongdam (청담), and COEX (코엑스) as the Same Neighborhood

Each sub-area in Gangnam-gu (강남구) has a very distinct vibe. Apgujeong (압구정) (trendy modern dining·K-pop fan) ≠ Cheongdam (청담) (luxury omakase·galleries) ≠ COEX (코엑스) (shopping mall + business dining) ≠ Seolleung (선릉)·Dosan (도산) (hidden gems). It's recommended to focus on 1-2 sub-areas rather than trying to combine them into a single itinerary.

3. Not Considering Reservations or Dress Codes for Japanese Omakase (chef's choice dining)

Japanese omakase (chef's choice dining) restaurants in Cheongdam (청담), Gangnam (강남) (e.g., Cheongdamyun (청담윤) and Cheongdam Makoto (청담 마코토)), require reservations (1-2 weeks in advance recommended) + smart casual dress code. Foreigners attempting to walk in may be refused entry. Please check KakaoTalk Channel or Naver Reservation in advance. Prices range from ₩100,000~300,000 (~$75-225 / ~540-1,620 RMB / ~11,500-34,500 JPY) for a single omakase (chef's choice dining) — a luxury dinner budget.

The Landscape of Chinese & Japanese Dining in Gangnam (강남), Apgujeong (압구정), Cheongdam (청담), and COEX (코엑스)

Gangnam-gu (강남구) is the mecca of luxury and modern dining in Korea. While #142 Gangnam Cafes, #143 Gangnam Hansik (Korean food), #166 Gangnam Bars, and #203 Gangnam Cafes Phase 2 cover cafes, Hansik (Korean food), and bars, this guide focuses on the Chinese and Japanese dining sectors.

Apgujeong (압구정) (2/8) — A modern dining hub. Home to the flagship Mutan (무탄) (a signature modern Chinese brand). Can be combined with K-pop fan itineraries and Apgujeong K-style street.

Cheongdam (청담) (2/8) — The mecca for luxury omakase (chef's choice dining) and authentic Japanese cuisine. Home to Cheongdamyun (청담윤) and Cheongdam Makoto (청담 마코토), among other Japanese omakase (chef's choice dining) establishments. A top choice for foreigners seeking a luxury dinner.

COEX (코엑스) (1/8) — A Mutan (무탄) branch + shopping mall dining. Naturally fits into foreign visitors' COEX (코엑스) itineraries (exhibitions·aquarium).

Seolleung (선릉)·Dosan (도산) (2/8) — Chinese hidden gems (Gongli (공리)·Luwi (루위)). Authentic Chinese cuisine with lower foreign recognition.

Gangnam (강남)·Yeoksam (역삼) (2/8) — Casual Japanese & seafood (Jinsusa (진수사)·Haedam Daege (해담일식)). Entry-level Japanese restaurants within walking distance of Gangnam Station (강남역).

① Modern Chinese Dining (2 Places)

1. Mutan Apgujeong (무탄 압구정 본점)

  • Signature: Apgujeong's (압구정) flagship modern Chinese dining. 10 foreign reviews (Phase 2 cluster #1, signal 44.3) — Korea's modern Chinese signature.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Strong foreign reviews, English menu & service
  • Recommended Menu: Signature Chinese course, modern Chinese dim sum (Chinese dumplings), wine pairing
  • Price Range: ₩40,000~80,000 (~$30-60 / ~220-430 RMB / ~4,600-9,200 JPY) (per person dinner)
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Apgujeong Station (압구정역). Highly recommended as the top choice for foreigners' first Gangnam (강남) Chinese experience — reservations recommended for both weekdays and weekends.

2. Mutan COEX (무탄 코엑스점)

  • Signature: Mutan's (무탄) COEX Starfield (코엑스 스타필드) branch. 10 foreign reviews (signal 43.2) — Same lineup as the main branch.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — COEX (코엑스) shopping mall + foreign business·exhibition itinerary
  • Recommended Menu: Same as the main branch, courses·dim sum (Chinese dumplings)
  • Tip: 5-minute walk from Samseong Station (삼성역), inside COEX Starfield (코엑스 스타필드). Optimal for a dinner course after visiting COEX Aquarium (코엑스 아쿠아리움) or SMTOWN (SM타운).

② Chinese Hidden Gems (2 Places)

3. Gongli (공리)

  • Signature: Seolleung's (선릉) authentic Chinese hidden gem. 5 foreign reviews (signal 24.0).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Seolleung (선릉) neighborhood, abundant foreign reviews
  • Recommended Menu: Signature Chinese dishes, authentic Chinese course
  • Price Range: ₩25,000~50,000 (~$19-37 / ~135-270 RMB / ~2,900-5,750 JPY) (per person)
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Seolleung Station (선릉역). An authentic and reasonably priced Chinese hidden gem, different from the luxury of Apgujeong (압구정)·Cheongdam (청담).

4. Luwi (루위)

  • Signature: Dosan's (도산) casual Chinese dining. 7 foreign reviews (signal 23.3).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Trendy Dosan (도산) alley, abundant foreign reviews
  • Recommended Menu: Signature casual Chinese dishes, seasonal menu
  • Price Range: ₩30,000~60,000 (~$22-45 / ~160-320 RMB / ~3,450-6,900 JPY) (per person)
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Dosan Park (도산공원) and Apgujeong Station (압구정역). Naturally fits into a trendy Dosan (도산) alley itinerary.

③ Japanese Omakase & Luxury (2 Places)

5. Cheongdamyun Omakase (청담윤 일식 오마카세)

  • Signature: Cheongdam's (청담) signature Japanese omakase (chef's choice dining). A hub for Japanese omakase (chef's choice dining) in Korea.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Luxury concept, English service
  • Recommended Menu: Signature omakase (chef's choice dining) course (sushi·sashimi·seafood)
  • Price Range: ₩150,000~300,000 (~$110-225 / ~810-1,620 RMB / ~17,250-34,500 JPY) (per person dinner)
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Apgujeong Station (압구정역). Reservations recommended 1-2 weeks in advance. Smart casual dress code. Top choice for anniversaries or date nights.

6. Cheongdam Makoto (청담 마코토)

  • Signature: Cheongdam's (청담) authentic Japanese cuisine. A Japanese chef's restaurant on Apgujeong-ro 80-gil (압구정로80길).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Authentic Japanese + English service
  • Recommended Menu: Signature Japanese course, authentic sushi, seafood
  • Price Range: ₩100,000~200,000 (~$75-150 / ~540-1,080 RMB / ~11,500-23,000 JPY) (per person dinner)
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Apgujeong (압구정)·Cheongdam (청담). On the same street as Cheongdamyun (청담윤) — allows for comparison between two Japanese omakase (chef's choice dining) restaurants. Reservations essential.

④ Casual Japanese & Seafood (2 Places)

7. Jinsusa (진수사)

  • Signature: Gangnam's (강남) Japanese hidden gem. 7 foreign reviews (Phase 2 Japanese #1, signal 27.0) — Entry-level Japanese in Gangnam (강남).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Abundant foreign reviews, close to Gangnam Station (강남역)
  • Recommended Menu: Signature sushi·sashimi, Japanese jeongsik (set meal)
  • Price Range: ₩30,000~70,000 (~$22-52 / ~160-380 RMB / ~3,450-8,000 JPY) (per person dinner)
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Gangnam Station (강남역). Top choice for foreigners' first Japanese restaurant experience — reasonably priced, easier to access than Cheongdam (청담) omakase (chef's choice dining).

8. Haedam Daege (자연산 해담일식 대게마을)

  • Signature: Gangnam (강남) Japanese·daege (snow crab)·seafood restaurant. 6 foreign reviews (signal 18.8).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Daege (snow crab)·seafood concept, English menu
  • Recommended Menu: Wild daege (snow crab), signature seafood, Japanese course
  • Price Range: ₩60,000~120,000 (~$45-90 / ~320-650 RMB / ~6,900-13,800 JPY) (per person dinner)
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Gangnam Station (강남역). Signature during daege (snow crab) season (November-March) — other seafood courses available outside of this period. Suitable for family or group dining.
