🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places in Seogwipo, Jeju where you can see canola flowers. They are three canola fields around Seongsan Ilchulbong, the Noksan-ro Canola Flower Road and festival in Gasi-ri, the Sanbangsan Canola Field, and Eongdeongmul Valley in Jungmun.

By the values registered with the tourism organization, some canola fields can be seen in winter. Ilchulbong Canola Field is open Dec-Mar and Joltti Canola Field from mid-November to mid-March. The rest are presented mainly for spring.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Seongsan — Ilchulbong Canola Field, Joltti Canola Field and Canola Flower Cultivation Complex

Ilchulbong Canola Field (일출봉유채밭) [Seongsan-ri (성산리), Seongsan-eup, Seogwipo, ☎ Jeju Tourist Information Center (제주관광정보센터) 064-740-6000] is a canola field between Seongsan Ilchulbong (성산일출봉) and Gwangchigi Beach (광치기해변). The canola flowers usually bloom from December to March, and beyond the field you can see the sea and Hallasan (한라산). The blooming season differs from year to year. It is always open, but the tourism organization's introduction recommends going before sunset to see the canola flowers.

  • Season: Dec-Mar. Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available.

Joltti Canola Field (졸띠유채밭) [Dongnyuam-ro 8 (동류암로 8), Seongsan-eup, Seogwipo, ☎ information 064-757-2076] is a canola field near Seongsan Ilchulbong, from which you can see Seongsan Ilchulbong, Udo (우도) and a reed field together. You can also try horseback riding around the canola field. According to the tourism organization's introduction, the blooming season used to be March-May, but thanks to improved varieties and adjusted sowing times, you can now see it from November to May, with the peak in March. You can take photos with Seongsan Ilchulbong framed inside a heart-shaped structure.

  • Opening hours: Mid-Nov to mid-Mar 09:00-18:00.
  • Closed period: Late Mar to early Nov each year. Parking: Available.
  • The registered values do not match. The blooming introduction says November to May (peak in March), but the opening hours are registered as running to mid-March and the closed period as starting in late March.

Canola Flower Cultivation Complex (유채꽃재배단지) [Goseong-ri (고성리), Seongsan-eup, Seogwipo, ☎ Jeju Olle Visitor Center (제주올레여행자센터) 064-762-2167] is a canola field on a long, narrow strip of land that was originally sea but was joined to Seongsan Ilchulbong as sand and soil piled up. The sea spreads out on both sides of the field. Nearby are Seongsanpo Port (성산포항), where the ferries to Udo depart, Seopjikoji (섭지코지) and Ojo Port (오조포구).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

📍 Gasi-ri — Noksan-ro Canola Flower Road and Seogwipo Canola Flower Festival

Noksan-ro Canola Flower Road (녹산로 유채꽃도로) [Gasi-ro 565beon-gil 20 (가시로565번길 20), Pyoseon-myeon, Seogwipo, ☎ Seogwipo Comprehensive Tourist Information Center (서귀포종합관광안내소) 064-732-1330] is a driving road where canola flowers and cherry blossoms bloom together along about 10km from the entrance to Gasi-ri (가시리) village. It was chosen as one of the 100 most beautiful roads in Korea. It passes Noksanjang (녹산장) and Gapmajang (갑마장), which were horse ranches in the Joseon era, and you can see Hallasan and the Gasi-ri wind power complex. Nearby are Jorangmal Experience Park (조랑말체험공원) and Gapseoni Oreum (갑선이오름).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Not available.

2026 Seogwipo Canola Flower Festival (서귀포유채꽃축제) [around Jorangmal Experience Park (조랑말체험공원), Gasi-ri, Pyoseon-myeon, ☎ Seogwipo-si (서귀포시) 064-760-3942] was held on April 4-5, 2026, from 10:00 to 20:00. Admission was free. It was a festival for walking through the 100,000㎡ canola field along Noksan-ro, The registered main programs were night opening and a running program, and the side programs included one-day classes, village-specific programs and busking performances.

📍 West and Jungmun — Sanbangsan Canola Field and Eongdeongmul Valley

Sanbangsan Canola Field (산방산유채꽃밭) [Sagye-ri (사계리), Andeok-myeon, Seogwipo, ☎ Jeju Olle Visitor Center 0507-1435-2179] is a canola field you can reach from the public parking lot in front of Sanbangsan Land (산방산랜드). There are several fields with Sanbangsan (산방산) as the backdrop, and you choose one to enter. They are private land tended by an individual, so a photo fee is charged. The flowers usually bloom fully in March. Nearby are Sanbangsan, Yongmeori Coast (용머리해안) and Sagye Coast (사계해안).

  • Opening hours: 10:00-18:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: Photo fee (amount not registered). Parking: Available.

Eongdeongmul Valley (엉덩물계곡) [Saekdal-dong, Seogwipo, ☎ Jeju Tourist Information Center 064-740-6000] is a valley east of the Lotte Hotel in Jungmun Tourist Complex (중문관광단지). Its name comes from the story that the rocks are so big and rugged that even animals looking for water could not get close, and only backed their rumps in to relieve themselves before turning back. In spring canola flowers bloom along the slopes of the valley. It is part of Olle Course 8 (올레 8코스) and is also called Jungmun Moonlight Walking Park (중문달빛걷기공원). You park at the Jungmun Beach (중문해수욕장) parking lot and walk.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Joltti Canola Field is closed from late March to early November — It is open from mid-November to mid-March, 09:00-18:00.
  2. Sanbangsan Canola Field is private land, so a photo fee is charged — It is open 10:00-18:00.
  3. Parking is not available at Noksan-ro Canola Flower Road — It is not registered as parking available.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Can you see Jeju canola flowers in winter too?

A. By the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, Ilchulbong Canola Field is listed for Dec-Mar, and Joltti Canola Field is listed as operating mid-Nov to mid-Mar, 09:00-18:00.

Q. Where can you see canola flowers and cherry blossoms together?

A. The Noksan-ro Canola Flower Road in Gasi-ri. Canola flowers and cherry blossoms bloom together along about 10km, and it is always open. Parking is not available.
