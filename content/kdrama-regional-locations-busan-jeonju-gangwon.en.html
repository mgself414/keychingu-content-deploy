🎯 Why a K-Drama Regional & Outskirts Deep Dive?

Regional and outskirts K-Drama filming locations = Foreign Fan Matrix #1: Deeper Korea beyond Seoul (서울) + KTX (Korea Train eXpress) & 1N2D full package. Pain points for foreign tourists: (a) The misconception that "K-Dramas are only in Seoul (서울)" — Busan (부산), Jeonju (전주), Gangwon (강원), Incheon (인천), Gyeonggi (경기), Jeju (제주) = 6 major regional clusters Matrix #1. (b) Pairs perfectly with [#1101 Seoul's 12 K-Drama Spots](1101_seoul_kdrama_12_spots.md) — Seoul (서울) 1N2D + Regional 1N2D = a 2N3D or 3N4D full package, ideal for K-Drama fans. (c) Iconic works like "Goblin" (도깨비) in Jumunjin (주문진), Gangneung (강릉); "Parasite" (기생충) in Busan (부산); "Itaewon Class" (이태원 클라쓰) in Gwangalli (광안리), Busan (부산); "Dr. Romantic" (낭만닥터 김사부) in Gangwon (강원) = Masterpieces + 2024-2026 New Release Matrix. (d) KTX takes 1 to 2.5 hours = Foreign fan 1N2D Matrix cross + [#1078 Regional Foodie 1N2D](1078_regional_foodie_1n2d.md) pair. (e) K-Drama regional trips + Hanok Stay ([#1076 Hanok Stay 7 Spots](1076_hanok_stay_7_spots.md)), Regional Foodie 1N2D ([#1078 Regional Foodie 1N2D](1078_regional_foodie_1n2d.md)), and [#1101 Seoul K-Drama](1101_seoul_kdrama_12_spots.md) cross.

This guide provides a foreign perspective on 9 regional and outskirts K-Drama spots across 6 cities + KTX, itineraries, 1N2D matrix, and a full fan package.

"Busan (부산) = only for raw fish and Haeundae": Busan (부산) K-Drama = Gwangandaegyo (광안대교) Bridge night view (Itaewon Class (이태원 클라쓰)), Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을) (Reborn Rich (재벌집 막내아들)), Haeundae (해운대) (Friend (친구), Love in the Big City (대도시의 사랑법)) cluster. Summer (6~8월, June-August: Busan (부산) Haeundae (해운대), Gangneung (강릉) Anmok Beach (안목해변), Jeju (제주) Hallasan (한라산)) = Beach K-Drama. Winter (12~2월, December-February: Gangneung (강릉) sunrise, Busan (부산) Hoe (회, raw fish), Jeju (제주) Hallasan (한라산) snow) = Matrix fan.
