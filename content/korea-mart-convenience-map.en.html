Near Dongmyo Station (동묘앞역) on Lines 1 and 6. Customer center 02-2290-1234 (E-mart official store page)

📅 How to Read the Mandatory Closing Days

  • Hypermarkets close twice a month, and the weekday differs by the district (gu) where the store is. Of the 5 above, Seoul Station and Cheonggyecheon in Jung-gu (중구) close on the second and fourth Wednesday, and the Mapo, Yongsan and Songpa stores close on Sunday (Lotte Mart and E-mart official store pages). The Lotte Mart official list also shows Seocho, Cheongnyangni and Haengdangyeok as Wednesday closings
  • Do not assume all of Seoul closes on the same day; check the closing day on the store's own page before you go
  • Late at night, Seoul Station is open until 24:00, and the other 4 until 23:00 (official store pages)

🥗 How to Find Halal and Vegetarian Food

  • Vegetarian: VisitKorea says the easiest way to spot vegan products while shopping is to look for the "vegan certification logo" on the package (VisitKorea vegetarian travel tips). If there is no logo, look on the back for pork, beef, chicken, gelatin, anchovy, shrimp and fish sauce in the ingredient list
  • Kimchi and side dishes (banchan, 반찬): Kimchi seasoning mostly contains salted fermented seafood (jeotgal), fish sauce or meat broth (VisitKorea vegetarian travel tips). Do not assume the kimchi or the side dishes in a lunch box at a mart are vegetarian
  • Halal: We could not confirm any halal-certified corner listing in the official information for the 11 spots in this guide. VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (이태원) (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil (우사단로10길), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00–21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF, 한국이슬람교중앙회). Certification can change, so check before you visit. 7-Eleven Itaewon 2 (46 Usadan-ro (우사단로)) is on the same Usadan-ro
  • For show-and-tell cards, see the #1300 Dietary Request Manual, and for vegetarian restaurants, the #291 Seoul Vegan Full Guide

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Hypermarkets only close on Sunday" — E-mart Cheonggyecheon and Lotte Mart Seoul Station close on Wednesday (official store pages)

2. "Marts all open until midnight" — Only Seoul Station is open to 24:00; the other 4 close at 23:00 (official store pages)

3. "Convenience stores are all open 24 hours" — GS25 Namsan Seoul Tower is open 09:00–22:30 (VisitKorea tourism information)

4. "You cannot get a tax refund at a mart" — Lotte Mart Seoul Station and World Tower, and E-mart Mapo, are listed with immediate + post-purchase refunds (VisitKorea tourism information)
