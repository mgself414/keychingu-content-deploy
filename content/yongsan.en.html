🎯 Why a Standalone Yongsan (용산) Guide?

Yongsan (용산) is an area in Yongsan-gu (용산구) that combines Korean railways, a U.S. military base, and the Han River. It includes Yongsan Station (용산역) (Korea's first railway station opened in 1900, departure point for KTX Honam Line), Sinyongsan (신용산) (IPark Mall, Dragon City, Amorepacific HQ), Samgakji (삼각지) (a historic military stronghold), and Ichon (이촌) and Hyochang (효창) (family-friendly neighborhoods east of the Han River). This is a separate deep dive into the main Yongsan-gu (용산구) area, distinct from Itaewon (이태원) (#144), Hannam (한남) (#79), and Haebangchon (해방촌) (#79).

Compared to Hongdae (홍대) (known for MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) culture), Gangnam (강남) (luxury), and Itaewon (이태원) (global vibe), Yongsan (용산) combines railways, history, and MZ culture. For international tourists, it's a deep dive into the KTX Honam Line departure point, Yongsan Park (용산공원), Sinyongsan's (신용산) MZ culture, and Amorepacific HQ. This marks KeyChinGu's first entry into the main Yongsan-gu (용산구) area (Yongsan cluster 141 cases).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Yongsan (용산) = Itaewon (이태원)" — Underestimating the main area's value: Yongsan-gu (용산구) offers a separate deep dive into Yongsan Station (용산역), Sinyongsan (신용산), and Samgakji (삼각지), combining Korean railways, MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) culture, and a U.S. military base, distinct from Itaewon (이태원) (#144).

2. "Yongsan Park (용산공원) = Unknown": It's a former U.S. military base transitioning into Korea's premier public park. It's set to become the top urban park in Korea.

3. "Amorepacific HQ (아모레퍼시픽 본사) = Inaccessible": It features David Chipperfield's architecture + free access to the exterior and exhibition hall.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Yongsan Station (용산역) KTX for foreigners? OK, KOBUS and KORAIL offer English UIs.

2. Amorepacific HQ (아모레퍼시픽 본사) admission? Exterior and 1st-floor gallery are free.

3. Yongsan Park (용산공원) status? Set to become Korea's top urban park (currently transitioning).

4. National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) free? Yes, free admission with English guidance for foreigners.

5. Sinyongsan IPark Mall (신용산 아이파크몰)? Instant tax refunds available for foreigners.

6. Yongsan (용산) + Itaewon (이태원) itinerary? 10-minute walk or one stop on Subway Line 1.
