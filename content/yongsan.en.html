🎯 Why Yongsan exclusive guide?

Yongsan is the Korean railway, US military base, and Han River combined area in Yongsan-gu — Yongsan Station (1900 Korea's 1st railway station, KTX Honam Line departure point) + Shinyongsan (I'Park Mall, Dragon City, Amore Pacific headquarters) + Samgakji (historical military base) + Ichon and Hyochang (family neighborhood east of the Han River) combined. Separate from Itaewon, Hannam (#144), and Haebangchon (#79) Yongsan-gu main area deep dive.

Compared to Hongdae (MZ), Gangnam (luxury), and Itaewon (global), Yongsan has railway, station, and MZ texture. Foreign tourists' route: KTX Honam Line departure + Yongsan Park + Shinyongsan MZ + Amore Pacific headquarters deep dive.
