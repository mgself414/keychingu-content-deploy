7 Seoul Rooftop Bars — Namsan Views in Hoehyeon and Haebangchon, 15th-Floor Rooftops in Hannam and Hongdae, Seongsu, and a 17th-Floor Rooftop in Gangnam

Subtitle: Two bars in Hoehyeon and Haebangchon with Namsan views and one in Hannam, two in Hongdae and Sangsu, one in Seongsu, and one on the 17th floor of a hotel in Gangnam's Eonju. Only rooftop bars that Kakao Map (카카오맵) shows as open (checked 2026-11-24) and that got visit photos or blog reviews within the last two months made the cut. All 7 close at 11 p.m. or later. The registered price of one cocktail, for the three places that have one, runs from 13,000 won (Sangsu Rooftop Urban Beach) to 18,000 won (Hotel Cappuccino). In Korea you can buy alcohol from the year you turn 19 by birth year, so bring your passport. For summer open-air rooftops see #1236, and for cocktail bars in general see #1166.

#778 · 2026-11-24 revised · Seoul Rooftop Bars

🎯 Who This Is For

This list is aimed at travelers who want a drink while watching Seoul's night view around sunset. There are three selection rules. We kept only places where the Kakao Map status is open, where visit photos or blog reviews have been posted since 2026-09, and where the owner's intro, the seating info, or the hotel's official page lists a rooftop (outdoor roof) seating area. Places with no rooftop seating, such as observation decks (Seoul Sky) or basement bars, were left out even if they are well known.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours and menu prices are the values the owners registered on Kakao Map (checked 2026-11-24). Only Side Note Club uses the hours from the Ryse Hotel official page. We listed a price only if its menu item was registered within the last 2 years. Where there is no drink price or it is older than 2 years, we wrote "no registered drink price." For those places, check the price on the in-store menu. Diet notes list only what can be read from menu names and the owner's description. Ask the staff about ingredients and allergies before you order. Nearby stations are the station, exit, and distance from Kakao Map directions.

🔞 Age Rule — Common to All 7

The Youth Protection Act defines a minor as "under 19 by international age." But once January 1 of the year you turn 19 arrives, you are no longer counted as a minor. That means it is the year of birth, not the birthday, that counts (Geumcheon-gu Office youth protection guide, 금천구청). In 2026, people born in 2007 can order alcohol. Side Note Club states on the hotel's official page that "only guests aged 19 and over are admitted." The other 6 places do not register any age rule. Even so, they sell alcohol, so you may be asked for ID at the door or when ordering. For foreigners, the passport is the ID.

① Hoehyeon·Haebangchon·Hannam — 3 Places

Nelbosco and Sunset Mood mention Namsan in the owner's intro. Seventy Eight Two lists a rooftop in the Kakao Map seating info. Nelbosco opens in the daytime, while Sunset Mood and Seventy Eight Two open around dusk. They are too far apart to link on foot, so it is better to move by taxi.

Nelbosco Rooftop Lounge & Bar (넬보스코 루프탑라운지&바)

A lounge bar on the roof (R5 floor) of a building near Exit 1 of Hoehyeon Station. The owner's intro calls it "a bar with the feel of a relaxing garden where you can see Namsan." It is open daily 11:30–23:00, so it sells coffee and brunch by day and drinks in the evening. Registered prices are low-sugar Long Beach Island 15,000 won, Irish coffee 12,000 won (2026-11-19), stracciatella margherita pizza 22,500 won, Mediterranean seafood stew 39,000 won, and Americano 4,700 won (registered 2026). The address is Toegye-ro 8-gil 16 (퇴계로8길 16), R5 floor, Jung-gu, 106m from Exit 1 of Hoehyeon Station (회현역) (Line 4).

  • Diet note: The salsiccia in the salsiccia olive pizza and salsiccia cream pasta is Italian sausage. The capricciosa pizza comes with ham. The seafood stew, shrimp pasta, and fried calamari (squid) are seafood. The margherita and camembert pizzas and the caprese are built around cheese. The owner wrote that the Irish coffee can be had with or without alcohol.

Sunset Mood (선셋무드)

A wine bar on the 4th floor in Haebangchon. The owner's intro calls it a "Namsan-view rooftop wine bar" and says reservations are taken on Catch Table. It is open daily 17:30–02:00. Wine and drink prices are not registered. Instead, the owner registered a request to order "1 bottle or 3 or more glasses per table" (2026-07-25). Registered snack prices are oven-roasted potatoes 16,000 won, blueberry cream cheese & crackers 17,000 won, honey baked brie 18,000 won, bisque ssamjang cream pasta 18,000 won, kimchi pancake & meat patty jeon 19,000 won, and pork-rib marinade grill 23,000 won (2026-09~10). The address is Hoenamu-ro 41 (회나무로 41), 4th floor, Yongsan-gu, 778m from Exit 2 of Noksapyeong Station (녹사평역) (Line 6).

  • Diet note: The pork rib grill is pork, and the meat patty jeon contains meat. Bisque is usually a sauce made from crustaceans such as shrimp or crab. With several cheese and cream snacks, it is hard to choose if you avoid dairy. If you don't drink, the ordering condition makes it a poor fit.

Seventy Eight Two (세븐티에잇투)

A cocktail bar on the 3rd–4th floors on Daesagwan-ro in Hannam-dong. The Kakao Map seating info lists a rooftop and private rooms. It is open Sun–Thu 18:00–02:00 and Fri·Sat 18:00–03:00, the latest closing in this piece. Cocktail prices are not registered. Registered snack prices are nachos 13,000 won, baked brie 15,000 won, gambas al ajillo 23,000 won, rose tteokbokki 25,000 won, assorted sausage set 25,000 won, Hawaiian chop steak 28,000 won, and cheese & fruit 33,000 won (2026-10-12). The address is Daesagwan-ro 77 (대사관로 77), 3rd–4th floors, Yongsan-gu, 555m from Exit 1 of Hannam Station (한남역) (Gyeongui-Jungang Line). There is no parking lot of its own.

  • Diet note: The sausage and the melon prosciutto (dried ham) are usually pork. Gambas is a shrimp dish. The snacks with no meat or seafood in the name are the nachos, baked brie, and cheese & fruit. Rose tteokbokki is tteokbokki made with cream.

② Hongdae·Sangsu — 2 Places

Hongik Univ. Station and Sangsu Station are connected on foot. Rooftop Urban Beach opens first at 17:00, and Side Note Club stays open until 02:00.

Ryse Hotel Side Note Club (라이즈호텔 사이드노트클럽)

A cocktail bar on the 15th floor of the Ryse Hotel in Hongdae. The hotel presents it as a "rooftop social lounge." The owner's intro calls it "a cocktail bar that takes in the whole night street of Hongdae at a glance." Hours, per the hotel's official page, are daily 17:00–02:00 (checked 2026-11-24). Kakao Map lists something different, 12:00–01:00 with last order at 00:30. If you go in the evening, the two overlap for hours. The official page admits only guests aged 19 and over and says all prices include service charge and VAT. The cocktail prices registered on Kakao Map date from 2023–2024, so we did not list them. The address is Yanghwa-ro 130 (양화로 130), 15th floor, Mapo-gu, 288m from Exit 9 of Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역) (Line 2·Gyeongui-Jungang Line·Airport Railroad). Reserve through the booking link on the hotel page or by email.

  • Diet note: The menu registered on Kakao Map (2023–2024) had cocktails made with ingredients such as peanut bourbon, makgeolli ice cream, and omija cordial. It may differ from the current menu, so if you have a nut allergy, say so when you order.

Rooftop Urban Beach (루프탑어반비치)

A cocktail bar on the 6th floor of a building near Sangsu Station. Its Kakao Map tags include rooftop and exotic. It is open Sun–Thu 17:00–24:00 and Fri·Sat 17:00–00:20. Registered prices are Peach Crush 13,000 won (2025-04-15), Kahlua Milk 13,000 won (2026-06-26), and a 3-drink set 20,000 won (2025-04-15). Snacks are Mexican cheese nachos 17,000 won, maple french fries 18,000 won, French toast 19,000 won, canapes 19,000 won, meatballs 21,000 won, and orange chicken 26,000 won (2025-04). The address is Dokmak-ro 9-gil 38 DE Building (독막로9길 38 DE빌딩), 6th floor, Mapo-gu, 497m from Exit 1 of Sangsu Station (상수역) (Line 6).

  • Diet note: The Kahlua Milk contains milk. The orange chicken is chicken. The meatballs don't say what meat they use, so ask. French toast usually uses eggs and milk. The snacks with no meat in the name are the french fries and the nachos (ask about the toppings before ordering).

③ Seongsu·Gangnam — 2 Places

The two are separated by the Han River. Pick whichever is closer to your lodging.

Archive Seoul (아카이브 서울)

A wine bar on the 5th floor of a building near Seoul Forest. Its Kakao Map tags include rooftop and Seoul Forest. It is open Mon–Fri 18:00–01:00 and Sat·Sun 17:00–01:00. All registered menu items date from 2022–2024, so we did not list prices. Check them on the in-store menu. The address is Wangsimni-ro 4-gil 26-7 (왕십리로4길 26-7), 5th floor, Seongdong-gu, 70m from Exit 7 of Ttukseom Station (뚝섬역) (Line 2), the closest to a station in this piece. For reservations, a Catch Table link is attached on Kakao Map.

  • Diet note: The registered menu included shrimp aglio e olio (shrimp), chimichurri short rib grill, buffalo wings (chicken), a charcuterie board (cured meats), and caprese (cheese). Ask at the venue whether they are still on sale.

Hotel Cappuccino Rooftop Bar (호텔카푸치노 루프탑바)

A cocktail bar on the 17th floor of Hotel Cappuccino in front of Eonju Station. It is open daily 17:30–23:00, last order 22:00. The owner's notice on Kakao Map says "the indoor space of the rooftop bar is open all year round," so there is a separate indoor seating area. Registered prices are 18,000 won for every cocktail, such as gin and tonic, negroni, old fashioned, and Aperol spritz. The Shirley Temple (non-alcoholic) is also 18,000 won. Unlimited draft beer is 19,000 won, only on weekdays 17:30–20:00 (registered 2026-04~06). Snacks are marinated olives 17,000 won, nacho chips and truffle french fries 20,000 won, and a charcuterie plate 39,000 won. The address is Bongeunsa-ro 155 (봉은사로 155), 17th floor, Gangnam-gu, 154m from Exit 1 of Eonju Station (언주역) (Line 9). Only hotel valet parking is available.

  • Diet note: The octopus, squid, tiger shrimp, and vongole items are seafood. Charcuterie is cured meat. The items with no meat or seafood in the name are pomodoro linguine (26,000 won), burrata cheese salad (28,000 won), and lime granita with lemon gelato (15,000 won). If you don't drink, pick the Shirley Temple.

🗺 Route — Two Courses

A. Hoehyeon·Haebangchon·Hannam (afternoon to late night)

  1. Before sunset First drink at Nelbosco (넬보스코) (11:30–23:00) with a view of Namsan. It is 106m from Exit 1 of Hoehyeon Station
  2. After 17:30 Take a taxi to Sunset Mood (선셋무드) in Haebangchon. For two or more people, one bottle of wine fits
  3. Around midnight Seventy Eight Two (세븐티에잇투) in Hannam (until 03:00 on Fri·Sat)

B. Hongdae·Sangsu (evening to 2 a.m.)

  1. 17:00 Start with a 13,000 won cocktail at Rooftop Urban Beach (루프탑어반비치)
  2. After 21:00 Move to the 15th floor of Side Note Club (사이드노트클럽) near Hongik Univ. Station (until 02:00)

Sunset time changes with the season, so pick the first venue to match that day's sunset time. Check the travel time between venues with Kakao Map directions.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Ordering just one drink at Sunset Mood (선셋무드) — The owner registered a request for 1 bottle or 3 or more glasses per table. If you want a light single drink on your own, other places are better.
  2. Going to Side Note Club (사이드노트클럽) in the daytime — Kakao Map says it opens at 12:00, but the hotel's official hours start at 17:00. Go in the evening.
  3. Expecting Cappuccino's unlimited draft beer on a weekend — The 19,000 won unlimited draft beer is only on weekdays 17:30–20:00.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. Which places are worth visiting even if I don't drink? — Nelbosco (넬보스코) has coffee, tea, and juice, and you can get the Irish coffee without alcohol. Hotel Cappuccino (호텔카푸치노) has a non-alcoholic cocktail, the Shirley Temple (18,000 won). Sunset Mood (선셋무드) has an alcohol-order condition and is not a fit.
  2. Which places stay open until dawn? — By registered hours, Seventy Eight Two (세븐티에잇투) (Fri·Sat 03:00), Sunset Mood (선셋무드) (02:00), and Side Note Club (사이드노트클럽) (hotel official 02:00). Nelbosco (넬보스코) and Hotel Cappuccino (호텔카푸치노) close at 23:00.
  3. Can I go in winter too? — All 7 have weekly hours registered as seen on 2026-11-24. The one place where the owner wrote separately about winter operation is Hotel Cappuccino, which says its indoor space is open all year. For the other 6, there is no registered notice about winter outdoor seating.
