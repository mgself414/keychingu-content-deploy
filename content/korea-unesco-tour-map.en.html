⚠ 3 Common UNESCO Tour Mistakes

1. "Korea has few UNESCO listings" — not knowing about the 14 sites: The assumption is "few." In reality it is 14 World Cultural + 2 Natural + 22 Intangible + 18 Memory of the World = 56 entries (global TOP 20) — plus the world's #1 density relative to land area.

2. "UNESCO = Seoul only" — not knowing the nationwide cluster: Seoul only. In reality it is a nationwide cluster: Changdeokgung (창덕궁) & Jongmyo (종묘) (Seoul) + 5 sites in Gyeongju + Andong + Ganghwa + 7 Sansa monasteries (nationwide) + Jeju natural heritage + 4 Getbol tidal flats.

3. "Packages = Korean-language only" — not knowing KTO's English offerings for enthusiasts: Korean only. In reality KTO UNESCO Tour packages come in English, Chinese, and Japanese + English-speaking guides and heritage interpreters, fully covering foreign enthusiasts.
