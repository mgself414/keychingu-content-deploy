How to Bundle Korea's 17 World Heritage Sites One Day at a Time: Seoul, Around Seoul, Gyeongju, Andong, Baekje, Jeju, with Closing Days and Reservations (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: Korea has 17 World Heritage properties (15 cultural, 2 natural), the latest inscriptions being the Petroglyphs along the Bangucheon Stream (반구천) in 2025 and the expansion of the Getbol tidal flats in 2026 (UNESCO) · Jongmyo (종묘) closes on Tuesdays and Changdeokgung (창덕궁) on Mondays (Korea Heritage Service Royal Palaces and Tombs Center) · Changdeokgung's Rear Garden (후원) opens for online first-come booking from 10:00 six days before (palace admission fee page) · Seongsan Ilchulbong (성산일출봉) closes on the first Monday of each month (VisitKorea). As of 2026-10-21

🎯 How to Use This Guide

In front of Jongmyo's main hall, Jeongjeon (정전), a single long roof stretches all the way to the far end of the wide paved courtyard: there are 19 chambers holding the spirit tablets (Korea Heritage Service Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). This guide does not explain Korea's World Heritage sites one by one in depth. It bundles only, with official values, where to stay, which days a site is closed, and what to reserve in advance. The story of each property is handed off to the regional guide links. It is for travelers who stay several days in Seoul and want to fit in one World Heritage site per day.

The old guide gave the number of World Heritage sites as "14". The UNESCO official list shows 17 for Korea (15 cultural, 2 natural). The Gaya Tumuli (가야고분군) were added in 2023 and the Bangucheon petroglyphs in 2025, and the tidal flats (갯벌) were enlarged in 2026 (UNESCO). Totals and rankings such as "14 World Cultural + 2 Natural + 22 Intangible + 18 Documentary = 56" and "the world's highest density per area" were removed because they have no source.

🏙 Central Seoul: Jongmyo, Changdeokgung, Joseon Royal Tombs

  • Jongmyo (종묘): Jongno 157 (종로 157), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul. Closed Tuesdays (open if a public holiday, then closed on the next first weekday). 09:00 to 17:30 in Nov to Jan, 09:00 to 18:00 in Feb to May and Sep to Oct, 09:00 to 18:30 in Jun to Aug, last admission 1 hour before closing. Foreigners aged 19 to 64: ₩1,000 (~$0.75) (Korea Heritage Service Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). On Mon, Thu and Fri it is a timed visit entering at set times with a guide, and English tours are at 10:00, 12:00, 14:00 and 16:00. On Sat, Sun, public holidays and Culture Day it is a free visit (Korea Heritage Service Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). A 5-minute walk from Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역) exits 11 and 8
  • Changdeokgung (창덕궁): Yulgok-ro 99 (율곡로 99), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul. Closed Mondays. Hours follow the same seasons as Jongmyo. Hall admission for foreigners aged 19 to 64: ₩3,000 (~$2.25). The Rear Garden (후원) costs ₩5,000 (~$3.75) separately and you must also buy the hall admission ticket; booking is online first-come from 10:00 six days before the visit date until 1 day before, or on-site first-come from 09:00 on the day. In Apr to May and Oct to Nov both sell out quickly (Korea Heritage Service Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). A 5-minute walk from Anguk Station (안국역) exit 3
  • Joseon Royal Tombs (조선왕릉): 40 tombs scattered across 18 sites (UNESCO). The nearest in Seoul is Seolleung and Jeongneung (선릉과 정릉) (Seolleung-ro 100-gil 1 (선릉로100길 1), Gangnam-gu (강남구)): 06:00 to 21:00, last admission 20:00, closed Mondays (VisitKorea)
  • How to bundle: on Sat, Sun, public holidays and Culture Day, Jongmyo's Bukshinmun (북신문) gate on the Yulgok-ro (율곡로) side opens (Korea Heritage Service Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). The combined palace ticket at ₩6,000 (~$4.50) covers the four grand palaces and Jongmyo, is valid for 6 months, and excludes the Rear Garden (Korea Heritage Service Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). For an order to tour just the palaces in one day, see #1255 Seoul's five grand palaces; for the Jongmyo rites, Jongmyo Daeje Viewing Guide: Twice a Year, Chunhyang in May and Chuhyang in November, Free Entry to the 2026 Chuhyang Daeje on November 7, Plus Regular English Tour Times

🚆 Around Seoul: Suwon Hwaseong, Namhansanseong, Ganghwa Dolmens

  • Suwon Hwaseong (수원 화성): Jangan-gu (장안구), Suwon-si (수원시), Gyeonggi-do (경기도). Open year-round with no closing day; inquiries Suwon Cultural Foundation 031-290-3600 (VisitKorea). For the detailed route, see #1032 Suwon Hwaseong
  • Namhansanseong (남한산성): Sueojangdae (수어장대) is in Namhansanseong-myeon (남한산성면), Gwangju-si (광주시), Gyeonggi-do. Open year-round with no closing day; Namhansanseong World Heritage Center 031-8008-5155 (VisitKorea)
  • Ganghwa Dolmen Site (강화 고인돌 유적): Bugeun-ri (부근리), Hajeom-myeon (하점면), Ganghwa-gun (강화군), Incheon, open at all times (VisitKorea). The other two sites of the same World Heritage property are in Gochang (고창), Jeonbuk, and Hwasun (화순), Jeonnam (UNESCO)
  • The three lie in different directions, so one site per day is a comfortable plan. For intercity buses, see #1297 express and intercity bus manual

🏯 Gyeongju and Ulsan: Silla and Prehistory

  • Bulguksa (불국사): Bulguk-ro 385 (불국로 385), Gyeongju-si (경주시). 09:00 to 18:00 (last admission 17:00), open year-round (VisitKorea)
  • Seokguram Grotto (석굴암): Seokgul-ro 238 (석굴로 238), Gyeongju-si. Open year-round, opens at 09:00 and closes between 17:00 and 18:00 depending on the season (VisitKorea listing)
  • Gyeongju Historic Areas (경주역사유적지구): hours differ by facility. Inquiries Terminal Tourist Information Center 054-772-9289 (VisitKorea)
  • Yangdong Village (양동마을): Yangdongmaeul-gil 93 (양동마을길 93), Gangdong-myeon (강동면), Gyeongju-si. 09:00 to 19:00 in Apr to Sep, 09:00 to 18:00 in Oct to Mar, last admission 1 hour before closing. You cannot enter the houses without the owner's permission (VisitKorea)
  • Petroglyphs along the Bangucheon Stream (반구천의 암각화): two sites in Ulju-gun (울주군), Ulsan, the Daegok-ri (대곡리) and Cheonjeon-ri (천전리) petroglyphs (UNESCO). Both are open at all times; inquiries Ulju-gun Office Tourism Division 052-204-0336 (VisitKorea)
  • One day is too short for Gyeongju. For lodging and transport, see #1372 Korea Golden Route 1, and for all of Gyeongju, #256 Gyeongju full guide

📜 Andong and Inland Yeongnam: Villages, Seowon, Mountain Monasteries, Janggyeong Panjeon, Gaya

  • Hahoe Village (하회마을): Jeonseo-ro 186-8 (전서로 186-8), Pungcheon-myeon (풍천면), Andong-si (안동시). 09:00 to 18:00 in Apr to Sep (last admission 17:30), 09:00 to 17:00 in Oct to Mar (last admission 16:30), open year-round (VisitKorea). It is one World Heritage property together with Yangdong Village, but the two villages are 90 km apart (UNESCO)
  • Dosan Seowon (도산서원): Dosanseowon-gil 154 (도산서원길 154), Dosan-myeon (도산면), Andong-si. 09:00 to 18:00 in Feb to Oct, 09:00 to 17:00 in Nov to Jan, open year-round (VisitKorea). The World Heritage property "Seowon, Korean Neo-Confucian Academies" consists of 9 academies (UNESCO)
  • Sansa, Buddhist Mountain Monasteries in Korea (산사): 7 temples: Tongdosa (통도사), Buseoksa (부석사), Bongjeongsa (봉정사), Beopjusa (법주사), Magoksa (마곡사), Seonamsa (선암사) and Daeheungsa (대흥사) (UNESCO). Andong has Bongjeongsa. For Seonamsa, see #1371 Suncheon Jogyesan thousand-year Buddhist path
  • Haeinsa (해인사): Haeinsa-gil 122 (해인사길 122), Gaya-myeon (가야면), Hapcheon-gun (합천군). 09:00 to 17:30, open year-round, may change so a phone check is recommended: 055-934-3000 (VisitKorea)
  • Gaya Tumuli (가야고분군): among them, Jisan-dong Tumuli in Goryeong (고령 지산동 고분군) (Daegaya-eup (대가야읍), Goryeong-gun (고령군)) is open at all times (VisitKorea)

🏞 Baekje, the South Coast and Jeju

  • Baekje Historic Areas (백제역사유적지구): 8 sites in Gongju (공주), Buyeo (부여) and Iksan (익산) (UNESCO). Gongju's Gongsanseong (공산성) is open 09:00 to 18:00 in Mar to Oct and 09:00 to 17:00 in Nov to Feb, closed on January 1 and on the days of Seollal and Chuseok (VisitKorea). Buyeo's Jeongnimsa Temple Site Five-Story Stone Pagoda (정림사지 오층석탑) is open at all times, while the Jeongnimsa Temple Site Museum (정림사지박물관) is closed Mondays (VisitKorea). To continue, see #1043 Gongju UNESCO Baekje
  • Getbol, Korean Tidal Flats (한국의 갯벌): inscribed in 2021, expanded in 2026. It is now 6 areas: Yeoja Bay (여자만) in Boseong (보성), Suncheon (순천), Yeosu (여수) and Goheung (고흥); Tando Bay (탄도만) in Sinan (신안) and Muan (무안); Hamhae Bay (함해만) in Muan; Gochang (고창); Seocheon (서천); and Seosan (서산) (UNESCO). Suncheon Bay Wetland (순천만습지), the visitor base on the Suncheon side, is closed on the last Monday of each month (VisitKorea). Check the boundaries of the World Heritage areas on the UNESCO map
  • Seongsan Ilchulbong (성산일출봉): Ilchul-ro 284-12 (일출로 284-12), Seongsan-eup (성산읍), Seogwipo-si (서귀포시). 04:30 to 20:00 in May to Aug, 06:00 to 18:00 in Nov to Feb, ticket sales close 1 hour before. Closed on the first Monday of each month (VisitKorea)
  • Manjanggul Cave (만장굴): Manjanggul-gil 182 (만장굴길 182), Gujwa-eup (구좌읍), Jeju-si (제주시). 09:00 to 18:00 (last admission 17:00), closed on the first Wednesday of each month (VisitKorea)
  • You can get around Jeju without a car: #1212 Jeju without a car

🥗 Dietary Needs

  • Vegetarian: in Korean food outside temples, even the kimchi seasoning often contains salted fermented seafood, fish sauce or meat stock (VisitKorea). If you are looking for temple meals near the mountain monasteries, see #1137 Korean temple food culture
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (Usadan-ro 10-gil 15 (우사단로10길 15), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00 to 21:00) as one of the few restaurants the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF, 한국이슬람교중앙회) recognizes as halal. Certification can change, so check before you visit. We could not confirm halal restaurants near the regional heritage sites on official pages
  • For a show-the-server card, see #1300 dietary request manual

🧰 World Heritage Route Practicalities: Alone, Reservations, Payment, Getting Around

  • Overlapping closing days: on Mondays Changdeokgung and the Royal Tombs are closed, and on Tuesdays Jongmyo is closed (Korea Heritage Service Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). On Jeju, Seongsan Ilchulbong closes the first Monday and Manjanggul the first Wednesday (VisitKorea). Match these first when you build a day-by-day schedule
  • Reservations: only the Changdeokgung Rear Garden (online 6 days ahead) effectively requires booking. For Jongmyo's timed visit, parties of fewer than 10 buy on site without a reservation (Korea Heritage Service Royal Palaces and Tombs Center)
  • Alone and payment: if you time it to Jongmyo's guided tour hours, even a solo traveler can hear the English commentary. For payment, see #527 Korean payment systems
  • Getting around: for KTX, see #1287 riding KTX for the first time; in the city, #1298 choosing among three transit cards

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Korea has 14 World Heritage sites": the UNESCO list has 17 (October 2026). The old number predates the Gaya Tumuli (2023) and the Bangucheon petroglyphs (2025)

2. "All the palaces close on Monday": Changdeokgung closes on Mondays and Jongmyo on Tuesdays (Korea Heritage Service Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). Put Jongmyo on Monday and Changdeokgung on Tuesday

3. "The combined ticket covers the Rear Garden too": the combined palace ticket excludes the Changdeokgung Rear Garden. Book the Rear Garden separately (Korea Heritage Service Royal Palaces and Tombs Center)

4. "Hahoe and Yangdong are in the same neighborhood": they are the same World Heritage property but 90 km apart (UNESCO). Reach Hahoe from Andong and Yangdong from Gyeongju
