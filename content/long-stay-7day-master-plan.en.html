7-day+ master plan for foreign long-term travelers

> For 7-day+ stay personas from the U.S. and Europe — 4-day stay in Seoul + 3-day movement matrix near KTX

The average length of stay for foreigners in Korea is 5.8 days (Korea Tourism Organization 2025), and for those from the U.S. and Europe, the average stay is 7.2 days, and the average stay is 7-10 days. However, most guidebooks focus on “3-5 days in Seoul” → 7 days + persona is an information gap. This guide is a 7-day + master plan tailored to American and European personas — 4 days of deep dive in Seoul + 3 days of cluster near KTX (Gangneung, Jeonju, Gunsan).

1. Definition of persona

Long-term traveler from the United States

  • Average flight ticket $1,200~1,800 (round trip) → Low efficiency for short stay → 7-14 days stay
  • Fluent in English, cultural curiosity ↑
  • Interests: K-pop·K-drama·K-food·temple stay·DMZ·traditional Hanok

Long-term traveler from Europe

  • Average vacation of 2-4 weeks → 7-10 days in Korea alone or combo with Japan and China
  • Emphasis on food culture, history, natural scenery, design, and museums
  • Interests: Korean food, deep dive, art museum, palace, nature (Gangneung, Jeju)

2. 7-day standard itinerary (4 in Seoul + 3 near KTX)

Day Region Core activities
1 Seoul city center Incheon Airport → Hotel·Myeongdong·Dongdaemun night market
2 Seoul Traditions Gyeongbokgung Palace, Bukchon Hanok Village, Insa-dong, Samcheong-dong
3 Seoul Trendy Hongdae, Hapjeong, Han River Picnic
4 Seoul gourmet Gwangjang Market, Gangnam, Itaewon Multinational food
5 KTX Gangneung (2 days and 1 night) Departure from Seoul Station 07:00 → Gangneung Jeongdongjin·Gyeongpo Beach·Coffee Street
6 Gangneung → KTX Seoul → KTX Jeonju or Gunsan again KTX efficient movement line
7 Jeonju or Gunsan 1 day → KTX Seoul → Incheon Airport Hanok Village, Restaurants, Modern Architecture

3. 7 day route variation (can be extended to 10 or 14 days)

Route A — Mountains and Sea (8-10 days)

4 days in Seoul + 2 days and 1 night in Gangneung + 2 days and 1 night in Busan KTX + 1 night on Jeju Air

Route B — History and Tradition (7-10 days)

Seoul 4 days + Jeonju 2 days 1 night + Gyeongju KTX 1 night 2 days + Andong (optional)

Route C — Modern/Design (7-9 days)

5 days in Seoul + 2 days and 1 night in Gwangju + 1 night of modern architecture in Gunsan and Jeonju

Route D — Full set (12-14 days)

Seoul 5 days + Gangneung 2 days + Jeonju 2 days + Busan 2 days + Jeju 2 days + Seoul 1 day

6. Foreign fit tips

7 Days+ Persona Special Information

  • Accommodation mix: Seoul hotel (1-4 nights) + Gangneung guesthouse (1 night) + Jeonju Hanok stay (1 night)
  • Transportation card: T-money pre-charge (10 days sufficient: ₩50,000)
  • Data SIM: 10-30 GB plan (₩30,000~50,000) — 7 days+ fit
  • Cash vs. Card: City/tourist attraction cards OK, market/long-established store cash recommended (₩100,000 emergency)
  • Language App: Papago + Google Translate + Naver Map (T Map English is weak)
  • KTX Reservation: Korail site or app (English OK) — Advance reservation required on weekends and public holidays

American Persona Recommendation

  • DMZ tour (1 day, ~$80, English guide)
  • K-pop pilgrimage (Apgujeong, Gangnam, COEX SMTOWN)
  • Myeongdong and Itaewon global food
  • Gangneung Coffee Street (Specialty coffee scene)

European Persona Recommendations

  • 4 grand palaces (Gyeongbokgung Palace, Changdeokgung Palace, Deoksugung Palace, Changgyeonggung Palace)
  • Art Museum deep (National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Leeum, National Museum of Korea)
  • Jeonju Hanok Village stay (1 night)
  • Andong Hahoe Village (optional, if scheduled for 3 days and 2 nights)
