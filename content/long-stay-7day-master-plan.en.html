Korea 7+ Day Long Stay Master Plan for Foreign Travelers

For US/Europe-based 7+ day stay personas — Seoul 4 days + KTX (Korea Train eXpress) nearby cities 3 days itinerary matrix

The average stay for foreign visitors in Korea is 5.8 days (Korea Tourism Organization 2025), but for those from the US and Europe, it's an average of 7.2 days, most frequently 7-10 days. However, most guidebooks focus on "Seoul 3-5 days," leaving a knowledge gap for 7+ day travelers. This guide provides a 7+ day master plan tailored for US/Europe personas — a 4-day deep dive into Seoul (서울) + a 3-day cluster of nearby cities accessible by KTX (Korea Train eXpress), including Gangneung (강릉), Jeonju (전주), and Gunsan (군산).

1. Persona Definition

US-based Long-Stay Travelers

  • Average round-trip airfare $1,200~$1,800 → Short stays are less efficient → Prefer 7-14 day stays
  • English fluent, high cultural curiosity
  • Interests: K-pop, K-drama, K-food, temple stay, DMZ, traditional hanok (한옥, Korean house)

Europe-based Long-Stay Travelers

  • Average vacation 2-4 weeks → Korea alone for 7-10 days or combined with Japan/China
  • Value food culture, history, natural scenery, design, museums
  • Interests: Hansik (한식, Korean food) deep dive, art museums, palaces, nature (Gangneung (강릉), Jeju (제주))

Seoul (서울) 4 days + Gangneung (강릉) 1 night/2 days + Busan (부산) KTX 1 night/2 days + Jeju (제주) flight 1 night

Seoul (서울) 4 days + Jeonju (전주) 1 night/2 days + Gyeongju (경주) KTX 1 night/2 days + Andong (안동) (optional)

Seoul (서울) 5 days + Gwangju (광주) 1 night/2 days + Gunsan (군산) & Jeonju (전주) modern architecture 1 night

Seoul (서울) 5 days + Gangneung (강릉) 2 days + Jeonju (전주) 2 days + Busan (부산) 2 days + Jeju (제주) 2 days + Seoul (서울) 1 day

6. Foreign Traveler Fit Tips

Special Guidance for 7+ Day Personas

  • Accommodation Mix: Seoul (서울) hotel (1-4 nights) + Gangneung (강릉) guesthouse (1 night) + Jeonju (전주) hanok (한옥) stay (1 night)
  • Transportation Card: Pre-load T-money (T-money is a prepaid transit card) (₩50,000 (~$37 / ~180 RMB / ~5,200 JPY) is sufficient for 10 days)
  • Data SIM: 10-30 GB plan (₩30,000~50,000 (~$22-37 / ~110-180 RMB / ~3,100-5,200 JPY)) — suitable for 7+ days
  • Cash vs. Card: Card is fine in city centers and tourist spots, but cash is recommended for markets and nofo (노포, 30+ year traditional restaurants) (₩100,000 (~$74 / ~370 RMB / ~10,400 JPY) for emergencies)
  • Language Apps: Papago + Google Translate + Naver Map (T Map is weak in English)
  • KTX Booking: Korail website or app (English OK) — advance booking is essential for weekends and public holidays

Recommended for US Personas

  • DMZ Tour (1 day, ~$80, English guide)
  • K-pop pilgrimage (Apgujeong (압구정), Gangnam (강남), COEX (코엑스) SMTOWN)
  • Myeongdong (명동) and Itaewon (이태원) global cuisine
  • Gangneung (강릉) Coffee Street (Specialty coffee scene)

Recommended for European Personas

  • Four Grand Palaces (Gyeongbokgung (경복궁), Changdeokgung (창덕궁), Deoksugung (덕수궁), Changgyeonggung (창경궁))
  • Art Museum Deep Dive (National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art (국립현대미술관), Leeum (리움), National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관))
  • Jeonju (전주) hanok (한옥) village stay (1 night)
  • Andong Hahoe Folk Village (안동 하회마을) (optional, for a 2 nights/3 days itinerary)
