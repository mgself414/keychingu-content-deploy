5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. Thinking "Korean Chicken = Plain Fried (Huraideu (후라이드))"

Korean fried chicken comes in yangnyeom (양념, sweet-spicy sauce), ganjang (간장, soy), spicy, cheese, garlic, and many more styles. Ordering plain fried chicken only means missing half the experience. For your first order, go banban (반반, half fried + half yangnyeom)—it's the right answer.

2. Ordering One Whole Chicken Per Person

One whole Korean chicken = 800g–1kg. Way too much for one person. The Korean-style sweet spot: 2 people share 1 chicken + 2–3 beers = ₩30,000.

3. Skipping the Chimaek (치맥, Chicken + Beer) Pairing

About 80% of Korean chicken is enjoyed as chimaek (치맥)—chicken with beer. Cola or water is fine, but the authentic pairing is Cass (카스), Terra (테라), or Hite (하이트) Korean beer.

4. Ordering Spicy Without Knowing the Heat Level

Korean yangnyeom chicken can be quite spicy—BHC's Maepso (맵소금), Kyochon's (교촌) Red Combo (레드콤보), BBQ's Hot Black (핫블랙) can catch you off guard. Start with yangnyeom (sweet-spicy, mild) before moving to spicy yangnyeom (매운양념, medium-hot).

5. Not Using Delivery Apps (Coupang Eats (쿠팡이츠)·Baemin (배민)·Yogiyo (요기요))

Korean fried chicken is the #1 delivery food in Korea. These apps offer English UI—order from your hotel without ever leaving. Foreign cards + a Korean phone number registration work fine.

★ The Big 3 Korean Chicken Chains — BHC vs Kyochon (교촌) vs BBQ

You'll run into these three everywhere in Seoul. Even spending the same ₩20,000, the sauce, spice level, and signature flavors are completely different.

1️⃣ BHC (Better and Happier Chicken) — #1 by Store Count + Widest Signature Lineup

Brand: Korea's #1 by store count — 1,800+ locations nationwide

Signature Menu:

  • Ppureungkl (뿌링클) (consommé seasoning): ₩20,000 — BHC's iconic item, foreigners' #1 pick
  • Maepso (맵소금) (spicy salt): ₩21,000
  • Huraideu (후라이드, plain fried): ₩18,000
  • Yangnyeom chicken (양념치킨): ₩20,000

Key Points:

  • #1 for signature variety — 7–10 options beyond plain fried
  • Ppureungkl (뿌링클) = the go-to first Korean chicken experience for foreigners
  • Higher delivery ratio than dine-in — top pick on delivery apps
  • English menus widely available

One-liner: "Korea's widest chicken signature lineup — start with Ppureungkl (뿌링클)"

2️⃣ Kyochon (교촌치킨) — #1 for Global Expansion

Brand: Present in Korea, USA, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East — highest foreign-visitor recognition

Signature Menu:

  • Honey Combo (허니콤보) (honey garlic): ₩23,000–26,000 — Kyochon's (교촌) signature
  • Red Combo (레드콤보) (spicy): ₩24,000
  • Original (오리지날, soy sauce): ₩20,000
  • Combo (콤보, half-and-half): ₩22,000–25,000

Key Points:

  • Ganjang (간장, soy) marinade = authentic Korean-style (savory-sweet, not just spicy)
  • Honey Combo (허니콤보) = fewest foreigners dislike it (sweet + garlic)
  • Consistent quality across Korean and overseas locations — familiar to international visitors who've tried it abroad
  • English menus + global standard

One-liner: "Korea's #1 soy-marinated chicken — Honey Combo (허니콤보) sweet-and-savory perfection"

3️⃣ BBQ (Best of the Best Quality) — Hwanggeumollive (황금올리브) Classic Fried

Brand: Korea → global expansion — 1,500+ locations

Signature Menu:

  • Hwanggeumollive (황금올리브) (original fried): ₩19,000 — BBQ's iconic item
  • Jamaica Tongdari (자메이카 통다리) (Jamaican-style drum): ₩22,000
  • Hot Black (핫블랙) (spicy): ₩23,000
  • Garlic Yangnyeom (갈릭 양념): ₩22,000

Key Points:

  • #1 for classic fried chicken — fried in olive oil
  • Hwanggeumollive (황금올리브) = the right answer for foreigners' first fried chicken experience
  • Hot Black (핫블랙) is among the spiciest Korean chicken options — only for those who can handle serious heat
  • English menus widely available

One-liner: "Olive-oil fried = the authentic base of Korean fried chicken"

8 Must-Know Tips for Foreigners

✅ 2 people per chicken (1 whole is too much) — 800g–1kg

✅ Banban (반반, half fried + half yangnyeom (양념)) is the right first order

✅ Chimaek (치맥) = pair with Cass (카스) · Terra (테라) · Hite (하이트)

✅ Spice ladder: yangnyeom (양념, mild-sweet) → spicy yangnyeom (매운양념, medium-hot) → Hot Black (핫블랙, BBQ maximum)

✅ Delivery apps — Coupang Eats (쿠팡이츠) · Baemin (배민) · Yogiyo (요기요) all have English UI

✅ Hotel delivery available — pick up at lobby (1F)

✅ 100% card accepted

✅ Allergens (eggs, soy, nuts, dairy in some seasonings)

FAQ

Q1. Is English available?

Big 3 + Goobne (굽네) = English menus available. Older neighborhood spots are mostly Korean, but photo menus are fine.

Q2. Can I dine solo?

Goobne (굽네), Kyochon (교촌), BHC offer single-portion menus at select locations. Or look for half-chicken (반마리) options. Delivery typically starts from one whole chicken.

Q3. Any vegan options?

Fried chicken = almost no vegan options. For vegan, see #69.

Q4. Bringing kids?

Kyochon (교촌) Original (오리지날) or plain fried = not spicy, kid-friendly. Skip spicy menus for children.

Q5. Worried about the price?

Norang Tongdak (노랑통닭) ₩14,000 = best budget option / market tongdak (통닭) ₩15,000 = classic Korean market experience.

Q6. Late-night cravings?

24-hour delivery locations (select spots in Gangnam (강남), Hongdae (홍대), Itaewon (이태원)) — filter by "24 hours" on delivery apps.
