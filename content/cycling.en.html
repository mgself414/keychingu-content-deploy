🎯 Why a Seoul Cycling & Riding Guide?

Seoul's Han River (한강) cycling path stretches 130km — one of the finest urban cycling infrastructures in the world, connecting Haengju Bridge (행주대교) in the west to Gangdong (강동) in the east along both banks of the Han River, with over 90% dedicated cycling lanes. Ttareungi (따릉이), Seoul's public bike-share system, has 3.3 million+ registered users, and foreigners can sign up via the English-language app. Keywords like "Han River bike" and "Seoul cycling" appear frequently on Reddit r/koreatravel. ⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Ttareungi registration requires a Korean phone number

The Ttareungi app (available in English) requires Korean phone SMS verification. A Korean SIM card / carrier registration + a payment card is essential. Short-stay visitors are advised to ask their hotel or a Korean friend for help, or use private bike rental (Han River delivery service 1577-0190 + bike rental ₩3,000/hour (~$2.20 / ~16 RMB / ~330 JPY)).

2. Confusing cycling paths with pedestrian paths

Han River cycling paths are marked in green; pedestrian paths are grey. Some sections (Banpo, Yeouido) are shared — slow down. Bridges such as Hangang Bridge (한강대교) and Dongho Bridge (동호대교) have separate cycling lanes on the side.

3. Direct sunlight and heatstroke between 11:00–16:00 in summer

The Han River cycling path has limited shade. In summer, ride early morning 06:00–09:00 or evening 19:00–22:00. Avoid direct sunlight between 11:00 and 16:00. Helmet, water, and sunscreen are essential.

Seoul Cycling Infrastructure

Seoul's cycling culture and infrastructure at a glance:

  • Han River cycling path 130km = Haengju Bridge (행주대교) in Gangseo (강서) ↔ Gangdong (강동) (both banks)
  • 90%+ dedicated cycling lanes — top-tier urban cycling safety
  • Ttareungi (따릉이) = Seoul's public bike-share, 3.3M+ users, ₩1,000/hour
  • Private bike rental = kiosks at Han River parks, Banpo (반포) & Yeouido (여의도) areas ₩3,000–₩5,000/hour
  • Cycling-dedicated crossings = Jamsu Bridge (잠수교, Banpo), Dongho (동호), Jamsil (잠실), Gwangnaru (광나루), etc.
  • Bike + subway = bikes allowed on some subway lines (weekends/holidays) or in the rear car of Line 9 (9호선) on weekdays

① Beginner (2 Routes)

1. Banpo (반포) Han River Park Route

The #1 beginner cycling spot for foreign visitors — Banpo Han River 8km loop. Start from the south end of Banpo Bridge (반포대교), ride to Jamsu Bridge (잠수교), along the south bank of the Han River, and return — 8km total. Available via Ttareungi (따릉이) or private rental at ₩3,000/hour. During the Moonlight Rainbow Fountain season (April–October), the evening ride from 19:30–21:30 is the best.

  • Starting point: 5-min walk from Exit 1, Sinbanpo Station (신반포역, Line 9)
  • Distance / Time: 8km / approx. 45–60 min
  • Level: Beginner (flat, 100% cycling path)
  • Signature: Moonlight Rainbow Fountain, Jamsu Bridge (잠수교), Han River views
  • Rental: Ttareungi (따릉이) or private rental ₩3,000/hour
  • Why: The classic first Han River cycling experience for foreign visitors. Great scenery, solid infrastructure, easy rental.

2. Yeouido (여의도) Han River Park Route

A 6km loop around Yeouido (여의도) — Yunjungno (윤중로) + 63 Building views. 3-min walk from Exit 4, Yeouinaru Station (여의나루역, Line 5). Peak season is spring (March–April) when Yunjungno (윤중로) cherry blossoms are in full bloom. Runner's Station offers free locker access.

  • Starting point: Exit 4, Yeouinaru Station (여의나루역, Line 5)
  • Distance / Time: 6km / approx. 35–50 min
  • Level: Beginner (flat)
  • Signature: Yunjungno (윤중로) cherry blossoms, 63 Building, Hangang Railway Bridge (한강철교)
  • Rental: Ttareungi (따릉이) + private rental near Runner's Station
  • Why: Top pick during spring cherry blossom season. Combines well with Runner's Station facilities.

② Night Ride & Ttareungi Favorites (2 Routes)

3. Han River Night Ride 13km (Ttareungi #1)

Voted #1 Ttareungi route by Seoul citizens — Ttukseom (뚝섬) → Dongho Bridge (동호대교) → Banpo Bridge (반포대교) → Nodeul Island (노들섬), 13km. Takes in all the major Han River night-view spots in one ride. Best-maintained dedicated cycling path; comfortable for both beginners and experienced riders.

  • Starting point: Ttukseom Hangang Park Station (뚝섬유원지역, Line 7)
  • Distance / Time: 13km / approx. 70–90 min
  • Level: Intermediate (flat, medium distance)
  • Signature: Ttukseom (뚝섬) night view → Dongho (동호) → Banpo fountain → Nodeul Island (노들섬) 360° view
  • Recommended time: Sunset 17:00–20:00 (extend to 21:00–22:00 during fountain season)
  • Why: The #1 night-view cycling route for foreign visitors. Top-rated by Ttareungi citizen vote.

4. Nodeul (노들) ~ Yeouido (여의도) ~ Saetgang (샛강) ~ Yanghwa (양화) 9km (Ttareungi #2)

90% dedicated cycling lanes — the safest 9km route. Nodeul Station (노들역) → Yeouido (여의도) waterfront plaza → Saetgang (샛강) ecological park → Yanghwa (양화) Han River Park. Best for families and first-time riders (near-zero traffic hazard).

  • Starting point: Nodeul Station (노들역, Line 9)
  • Distance / Time: 9km / approx. 50–70 min
  • Level: Beginner (90% dedicated cycling path, safest route)
  • Signature: Saetgang (샛강) ecological park, Yeouido (여의도) waterfront, Yanghwa (양화) Han River
  • Recommended time: Spring or autumn mornings 09:00–11:00
  • Why: Best for families and beginners among foreign visitors. Ranked #1 for safety.

③ Loop & Full Route (2 Routes)

5. Jamsu Bridge (잠수교) ~ Haengju Bridge (행주대교) North-South Loop 50km

A full loop along both banks of the Han River — start at Jamsu Bridge (잠수교), turn at Haengju Bridge (행주대교), return to Jamsu Bridge (잠수교). Experience both the south and north banks of the Han River. Best in spring and autumn. Gateway course for cycling enthusiasts.

  • Starting point: Jamsu Bridge (잠수교), Banpo (반포) — Exit 1, Sinbanpo Station (신반포역, Line 9)
  • Distance / Time: 50km / approx. 3.5–5 hours
  • Level: Intermediate-Advanced (flat but long distance)
  • Signature: Both banks of the Han River, Banpo fountain, Yeouido (여의도), Jamsil (잠실), Haengju Bridge (행주대교)
  • What to bring: Helmet, 2L water, snacks, basic toolkit (tube repair, etc.)
  • Why: The go-to full-day loop for Han River enthusiasts. Comfortably done in one day.

6. Han River Full Route 130km

Complete traverse of the Han River cycling path — Haengju Bridge (행주대교) in Gangseo (강서) ↔ Paldang (팔당) in Gangdong (강동). The entire Han River. 14–16 hours (one day or two days / one night). Official National Cycling Route certification pass available upon completion.

  • Starting point: Haengju Bridge (행주대교) in Gangseo (강서), or Jamsil (잠실) (east end of Seoul)
  • Distance / Time: 130km / approx. 14–16 hours (1 day) or 2 days / 1 night
  • Level: Advanced (long-distance)
  • Signature: Full Han River traverse, National Cycling Route certification
  • What to bring: Helmet, 4L water, snacks, toolkit, spare tube, lights (night riding possible)
  • Why: The ultimate Han River challenge for cycling enthusiasts. The #1 bucket-list ride for foreign cycling fans.

④ Outskirts & Downtown (2 Routes)

7. Tancheon (탄천) & Yangjae Stream (양재천) — Gangnam (강남) Outskirts

A quiet Gangnam (강남) outskirts ride, 15–25km. Start from Yangjae Citizens' Forest Station (양재시민의숲역), follow Tancheon (탄천) all the way to the Han River. More peaceful and natural than the Han River cycling path. Popular with foreign residents in Gangnam (강남) for commuting + leisure riding.

  • Starting point: Yangjae Citizens' Forest Station (양재시민의숲역, Shinbundang Line)
  • Distance / Time: 15–25km / approx. 1.5–2 hours
  • Level: Intermediate (flat, quiet)
  • Signature: Tancheon (탄천), Yangjae Stream (양재천), natural scenery of Gangnam (강남) outskirts
  • Recommended time: Autumn foliage or spring cherry blossoms
  • Why: A great alternative to the Han River. Combines Gangnam (강남) with a cycling experience.

8. Cheonggyecheon (청계천) Downtown Cycling

Gwanghwamun (광화문) → Cheonggyecheon (청계천) LED lights → Dongdaemun (동대문) → DDP (동대문디자인플라자), 8km. Cycling right through the heart of downtown Seoul. Partly dedicated cycling lane, partly shared with pedestrians. Night LED lighting is the highlight.

  • Starting point: Gwanghwamun Station (광화문역, Line 5) or Jongak Station (종각역, Line 1)
  • Distance / Time: 8km / approx. 50–60 min
  • Level: Beginner (flat, downtown)
  • Signature: Cheonggyecheon (청계천) LED lights, Gwanghwamun (광화문), DDP (동대문디자인플라자) night view
  • Recommended time: Evening 19:00–22:00 (LED lighting)
  • Why: Downtown cycling experience. Combines Jongno (종로) / DDP sightseeing with riding.

⚙ FAQ — 8 Questions

Q1. Can foreigners sign up for Ttareungi (따릉이)?

A Korean phone number + Korean payment card is required. Foreign passport or ARC alone is not sufficient for direct sign-up. It is recommended to ask your hotel or a Korean friend for help, or use private rental (kiosks at Banpo (반포) & Yeouido (여의도) Han River parks, ₩3,000/hour). Some foreign e-SIMs may work.

Q2. Where can I rent a private bike?

  • Banpo (반포) Han River Park — on-site kiosk rental ₩3,000–₩5,000/hour
  • Yeouido (여의도) Han River Park — near Runner's Station + private rental
  • Ttukseom (뚝섬) Han River Park — kiosk + bike storage facility
  • Jamsil (잠실) Han River Park — wide selection of private rentals
  • Nodeul Island (노들섬) — rental at the Nodeul Island (노들섬) entrance

Q3. Where do I get a helmet and gear?

Most private rental kiosks include a helmet (not legally required but strongly recommended). For serious riders, Decathlon (데카트론) (Gangnam (강남) / Jamsil (잠실)) sells helmets for ₩30,000–₩80,000.

Q4. What's the best time to ride?

  • Spring & Autumn: 08:00–11:00 (quieter) or sunset 16:00–19:00
  • Summer (July–August): Early morning 06:00–09:00 (avoid heat) or evening 19:00–22:00
  • Winter (December–February): Midday 11:00–14:00 (warmest)

Q5. What to do on a rainy day?

Cycling on the Han River in rain = dangerous (slippery surfaces, accident risk). Rainy day alternatives:

  • Museum visits, DDP (동대문디자인플라자), or walking around Insadong (인사동)
  • Hotel gym (stationary bike)

Q6. Cycling groups for foreigners?

Seoul Cycling Club (Facebook + Strava) — foreign-friendly weekly rides. Han River Foreign Cyclists (Instagram group). English is fine.

Q7. Can I take my bike on the subway?

Bikes are allowed on Seoul Metro — during certain hours (weekends / public holidays) or in the rear car of Line 9 (9호선, 구호선) on weekdays. Useful for getting back after a long Han River ride.

Q8. Post-ride wind-down (food & jjimjilbang)?

  • Han River chimaek (치맥) (fried chicken + beer) — Han River delivery service 1577-0190 (post-ride classic)
  • Cafe or gukbap (국밥) (Korean hangover soup / hearty rice soup) — depends on where you finish (#142 Gangnam (강남) cafe guide / #133 gukbap (국밥) guide)
  • Jjimjilbang (찜질방) (Korean sauna & relaxation facility — like a public bathhouse combined with a rest lounge) — Yangjae (양재) / Jamsil (잠실) (#7 jjimjilbang (찜질방) guide)

15:00 → Banpo (반포) Han River Park 8km private rental (1 hour) → transition to night ride at 17:00 → Han River Night Ride 13km (Ttukseom (뚝섬)-Banpo (반포)-Nodeul (노들), 1.5 hours) → Instagram photos at Nodeul Island (노들섬) → Han River ramen + chimaek (치맥) to wrap up

05:00 → Start at Haengju Bridge (행주대교) in Gangseo (강서) → Han River Full Route 130km → Arrive at Gangdong (강동) / Paldang (팔당) at 19:00 → Get certification, celebrate, jjimjilbang (찜질방)

🌸 5 Seasonal & Time Hooks

1. Spring (April–May) Yunjungno (윤중로) Cherry Blossom Ride: Yeouido (여의도) 6km + cherry blossoms at peak bloom. Instagram #1 pick for foreign visitors.

2. Summer (July–August) Dawn & Night Rides: Beat the heat. Early morning 06:00–09:00 or evening 19:00–22:00.

3. Autumn (October–November) Foliage Ride: Stunning fall colors along both banks of the Han River and Tancheon (탄천).

4. Fountain Season (April–October) Banpo (반포) Night Ride: Moonlight Rainbow Fountain 19:30–21:30 + Banpo (반포) 8km ride.

5. Winter (December–February) Quiet Han River: Sub-zero temperatures are a risk — switch to glamping or indoor activities. Riding recommended only when temperatures are above 4°C.
