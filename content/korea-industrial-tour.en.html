Industrial Tourism in Korea — 4 Corporate Tours and Showrooms Individual Travelers Can Book (Samsung Innovation Museum (삼성이노베이션뮤지엄), Hyundai Motorstudio Goyang (현대 모터스튜디오 고양), Hyundai Motor Ulsan Plant (현대자동차 울산공장), Hite Jinro Gangwon Plant (하이트진로 강원공장)) — Official Values, October 2026

🎯 How to Use This Guide

The tour bus crawls through the plant and stops at the export pier — the Hyundai Motor Ulsan Plant (현대자동차 울산공장) tour runs from the culture hall through Plant 3 to this pier (Hyundai Motor official reservation page). This guide is for travelers who want to see for themselves how Korean companies make things. There is one criterion: places that are in operation on the company's official reservation page and that individuals or small groups can apply for. Four places meet it, divided into exhibition types (Samsung and Hyundai Motorstudio) and factory types (Hyundai Ulsan and Hite Jinro Gangwon). Places that are group-only, or whose official page we could not open, are listed separately under ⚠ and at the bottom.

🏛 Exhibition Type — 2 Places You Can Visit Alone

  • Samsung Innovation Museum (삼성이노베이션뮤지엄) (S/I/M) — 129 Samsung-ro, Yeongtong-gu, Suwon, Gyeonggi-do, 031-200-3113 (Suwon City tourism official page)
  • Hours: weekdays 13:00–14:00 docent tour (reservation required); Saturdays 10:00–17:00 self-guided (last entry 16:00)
  • Closed: public holidays, substitute holidays, Labor Day and December 31
  • Fee: free
  • Age: 13 and over. Elementary school students and younger must be accompanied by a guardian (1 guardian per 2 children)
  • Languages: weekday docents are available in Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese and Spanish, for groups of up to 30 applicants (Suwon City tourism official page). The official site has an English "Tour Reservation" menu (Samsung Innovation Museum official site)
  • Hyundai Motorstudio Goyang (현대 모터스튜디오 고양) — 217-6 Kintex-ro, Ilsanseo-gu, Goyang, Gyeonggi-do; Exit 1 of GTX-A Kintex Station (킨텍스역). Customer center 1899-6611 (Hyundai Motorstudio official site)
  • Hours: exhibition 09:00–20:00. Experience exhibits 10:00–18:20 (10:00–19:00 on weekends, public holidays and peak season), closed Mondays. The permanent exhibition and 4D Ride require advance reservation; ticketing closes 15 minutes before
  • Closed: New Year's Day, and Seollal and Chuseok day plus the following day
  • Fees (individuals): permanent exhibition adults ₩10,000 (~$7.50), teens ₩7,000 (~$5.25), children ₩5,000 (~$3.75); 4D Ride adults ₩7,000 (~$5.25); combined ticket adults ₩16,000 (~$12)
  • Test drives: 10:00–17:00, advance reservation required, closed Mondays
  • The site has Korean, English and Chinese screens (Hyundai Motorstudio official site)

🏭 Factory Type — 2 Places That Are Harder to Book

  • Hyundai Motor Ulsan Plant (현대자동차 울산공장) — 700 Yeompo-ro, Buk-gu, Ulsan (main gate of the Ulsan plant) (Hyundai Motor official reservation page)
  • Course: culture hall → Plant 3 → export pier, about 1 hour 30 minutes. You tour on a bus provided by the plant
  • Fee: free. Lunch, transport and souvenirs are not provided
  • Individual tours: up to 4 people per applicant. Children aged 11 and under: up to 2 per guardian. Some sessions carry the label "families with minors and students only; adult group applications not accepted"
  • How to book: bookings are taken in 3-month blocks, and the schedule opens at 9 a.m. on the first working day of each month. Online applications go through i-PIN or identity verification on a phone in your own name — if you have no Korean phone, ask the tour inquiry desk first
  • Foreign groups: group tours are accepted by the bus only, and groups that include foreigners apply under the "companies, government offices and foreigners" type
  • Inquiries: 052-215-1021~1024 (09:00–17:00, excluding lunch 11:10–12:10), ulsantour@hyundai.com
  • Photography inside the plant and bringing in photographic equipment are prohibited
  • Hite Jinro Gangwon Plant (하이트진로 강원공장) — 49 Dodun-gil, Bukbang-myeon, Hongcheon-gun, Gangwon-do; tour inquiries 033-430-8250~3 (Hite Jinro official site)
  • Eligibility: adults aged 19 and over, advance reservation only. Of Hite Jinro's plants, the Gangwon Plant (강원공장) is the one currently running tours (Hite Jinro official FAQ)
  • Days: Monday to Friday only (no Saturdays, Sundays or public holidays)
  • Applications: at least 3 weeks before your desired date, first come first served. Confirmation is sent 2 weeks before. Phone applications are not accepted
  • Group size: up to 45 people per application. With 20 to 40 people per session, a small party may tour together with another group in the same time slot (Hite Jinro official FAQ)
  • Fee: free

🧰 Industrial Tourism Practicalities — Going Alone, Booking, ID, Getting There

  • Going alone: you can visit Samsung Innovation Museum (삼성이노베이션뮤지엄) (Saturday self-guided) and Hyundai Motorstudio Goyang (현대 모터스튜디오 고양) by yourself. Hyundai Motor Ulsan (현대자동차 울산공장) has an individual-tour slot, and at Hite Jinro a small party joins another group
  • When to book: Hyundai Ulsan opens at 09:00 on the first working day of each month, and Hite Jinro closes applications 3 weeks ahead. Once your travel dates are set, book these two first
  • ID checks: Hyundai's online application uses Korean-style identity verification. Emailing first (ulsantour@hyundai.com) is faster
  • Payment: of the 4 places, only the experience exhibits at Hyundai Motorstudio Goyang (현대 모터스튜디오 고양) charge a fee. For cards and mobile payment, see Foreigner Korea Payment System Deep Dive
  • Getting there: Suwon and Goyang are reached by metropolitan subway — Choosing Your Korea Transit Card: The No-Panic Manual — T-money, WOWPASS, or Climate Card. For Ulsan and Hongcheon, see Korean Express & Intercity Buses: The No-Panic Manual — Knowing They Use Different Apps Is Half the Battle
  • A day in Ulsan: Ulsan Industrial Tourism

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "I can apply for a Kia plant as an individual too" — Kia AutoLand (기아 오토랜드) tours accept only formal groups of 15 to 30 from educational institutions and government offices that can submit an official letter; individuals and travel-agency groups are not accepted. Foreign groups must be accompanied by a Korean guide (Kia official page)

2. "Any Hite Jinro plant offers tours" — the plant currently running tours is Gangwon (강원공장). The Icheon Plant (이천공장) is stated not to run tours (Hite Jinro official site)

3. "I can take photos inside the plant and post them" — the Hyundai Motor Ulsan Plant (현대자동차 울산공장) prohibits photography and bringing in photographic equipment (Hyundai Motor official reservation page)

4. "The Samsung museum is open all weekend" — only Saturday is self-guided; on weekdays it is a 13:00 docent tour by reservation. It is closed on public holidays (Suwon City tourism official page)
