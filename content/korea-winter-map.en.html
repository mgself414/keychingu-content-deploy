🎯 Why “Korea Winter Map” meta

Korean winter (December-February) Foreign enthusiasts — No. 1 snow enthusiasts in Southeast Asia, Hong Kong, Taiwan, and the Middle East. Key points: (1) Skiing (Pyeongchang, Jeongseon, Yongpyeong, Alpensia, Phoenix, Gonjiam) (2) Illumination (DDP, Lotte Tower, Gwanghwamun, Everland, Cheonggyecheon) (3) Snow Festival (Daegwallyeong Snow Flower, Taebaek Snow Festival, Hwacheon Sancheoneo) (4) Hot Springs (Icheon Termeden, Cheongsong Mineral Water, Suanbo, Dogo) (5) Winter Gourmet (Dongji red bean porridge, dumpling soup, bungeoppang, hotteok, samgyetang).

⚠ 3 Winter Mistakes

1. "Winter = Skiman" — Illuminations/Snow Festival Recognition: Skiman recognition. In reality, it is skiing + illumination (Seoul, theme park) + snow festival (Gangwon) + hot spring (nationwide) full experience.

2. "Not knowing about the Hwacheon Sancheoneo Festival" — Unaware of Korea's #1 Winter Festival: Recognizing absence. In fact, Hwacheon Sancheoneo Fish Festival (January 500,000+ foreigners, ice fishing, bare-handed catching) is a mania signature.

3. "Hot springs = only Japan" — Unperception of Korean hot spring full experience: Japanese perception. In fact, Icheon Termeden, Suanbo, Dogo, Cheongsong, Onyang Korean hot spring cluster full experience.
