⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Korean islands = only Jeju (제주)": Korea's outer regions offer 12+ islands including Ulleung (울릉), Dokdo (독도), Hongdo (홍도), Wando (완도), Cheongsando (청산도), Bogil (보길), Oedo (외도), Maemul (매물), and more, providing a full set for foreign enthusiasts.

2. "'Ulleungdo (울릉도) = inaccessible'": Ulleungdo (울릉도) is accessible via ferries from Pohang (포항), Gangneung (강릉), or Hupo (후포) in 3.5-10 hours, a top spot for foreign enthusiasts, with an additional 1.5-hour trip to Dokdo (독도).

3. "'Hongdo (홍도) = where is it?'": Hongdo (홍도) is Korea's #1 cliff island (known for its red rocks), reachable by ferry from Mokpo (목포) in 2 hours 30 minutes, and a top foreign Instagram hotspot.

4. "'Ferry season = year-round OK'": Korean ferries have seasonal operations (frequent cancellations due to winter storms and summer typhoons), making advance booking a top priority for foreign enthusiasts.

5. "'Oedo (외도) = Tongyeong (통영)'": Oedo (외도) departs by ferry from Geoje (거제) (#257) (not Tongyeong (통영)), it is Korea's #1 garden island and a top foreign Instagram spot.
