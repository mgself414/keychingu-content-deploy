Places in Korean Songs Guide — Deoksugung and Gwanghwamun from 'Gwanghwamun Yeonga' (Gwanghwamun Love Song), Yeosu from 'Yeosu Bambada' (Yeosu Night Sea), Dongbaekseom from 'Dorawayo Busanhange' (Come Back to Busan Port) and Yudalsan from 'Mokpoui Nunmul' (Tears of Mokpo): 20 Places

Subtitle: 20 places chosen by region, among those picked in the "Places in the Lyrics" theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" public vote and registered with operating information at the Korea Tourism Organization · The song-to-place link follows the song titles given in the vote's recommendation reasons (lyrics are not reproduced) · The places with a registered entrance fee are Deoksugung (individual 1,000 won) and the Gwanghwamun entry (adult 3,000 won) · Deoksugung closes on Mondays, and the Gwanghwamun entry's registered value is closed on Tuesdays · Some Chilgapsan courses are closed (2026.10.20~12.15) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide covers 20 of the 100 places chosen in the "Places in the Lyrics" (가사 속 그곳) theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" (명소발굴 100×100) (candidates recommended by travel experts, voted on by the public), for which operating information is registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data. The ranks and vote counts in the table are the results of that vote. These are not all the places linked to Korea Tourism Organization information; they were chosen with rank and regional distribution in mind. Yeosu Yi Sun-sin Square (여수 이순신광장), Seokchon Lake (석촌호수), Haeundae (해운대) and Oryukdo (오륙도) were also on the vote but are not included.

Which song a place is linked to is copied from the song titles and singer names given in the vote's recommendation reasons. Lyrics are not reproduced because of copyright. Where the Korea Tourism Organization description also mentions a song (Kim Kwang-seok Dasi Geurigi-gil (김광석다시그리기길), Hwagae Market (화개장터), Yudalsan (유달산), Chilgapsan (칠갑산) and others), that fact is noted separately.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Fields with no registered value are marked "No registered value". Where an address on the vote list differs from the Korea Tourism Organization registration, the main text says so.

📍 Seoul — Deoksugung, Gwanghwamun, Marronnier Park, Cheonggyecheon, Seochon Village and Seonyudo Park

Deoksugung (99 Sejong-daero, Jung-gu / 중구 세종대로 99, ☎ 02-771-9951) is the imperial palace of the Korean Empire (대한제국), proclaimed in 1897. Its old name is Gyeongungung (경운궁), and it has traditional buildings alongside Western-style ones. The vote's recommendation reason cites the Deoksugung Stone Wall Road (덕수궁 돌담길) and Jeongdong Church (정동교회) that appear in Lee Moon-sae (이문세)'s 'Gwanghwamun Yeonga' (Gwanghwamun Love Song, 광화문 연가).

  • Opening hours: 09:00~21:00 (last entry 20:00). The exhibition halls Seokjojeon (석조전), Dondeokjeon (돈덕전) and Jungmyeongjeon (중명전) have separate hours.
  • Closed days: Every Monday (when it overlaps a public holiday or substitute holiday it opens, and it closes on the first non-holiday day after). Fee: Individual 1,000 won. Admission to the Deoksugung Art Museum (덕수궁 미술관) is bought separately.

Gwanghwamun (161 Sajik-ro, Jongno-gu / 종로구 사직로 161, ☎ 02-3700-3901) is the main gate of Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁). According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is the most formal palace gate, with three arched gateways (홍예문) set in its base, and its restoration work was completed on 2010-8-15. The vote's recommendation reason lists it as a symbol of Seoul that appears in 'Gwanghwamun Yeonga'. The vote list gives the address as 12 Hyoja-ro, National Palace Museum of Korea (효자로 12 국립고궁박물관).

  • The hours, closed days and fee below are values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization's Gwanghwamun entry. The registered values do not say separately when the gate itself opens or whether a fee is charged, and the fee field also carries "call for details".
  • Opening hours: Months 1~2 and 11~12 09:00~17:00, months 3~5 and 9~10 09:00~18:00, months 6~8 09:00~18:30. Last entry is 1 hour before each closing time.
  • Closed days: Every Tuesday (when it overlaps a public holiday or substitute holiday it opens, and it closes on the first non-holiday day after). Fee: Adult (ages 25~64) 3,000 won, foreigner (ages 19~64) 3,000 won. Parking: Available (about 240 spaces).

Marronnier Park (104 Daehak-ro, Jongno-gu / 종로구 대학로 104, ☎ 02-2148-4158) is a park on the site of the former Seoul National University College of Liberal Arts and Sciences and College of Law (옛 서울대학교 문리대·법대). It is named after the marronnier trees planted in 1929, and performances and cultural events are held on its outdoor stage. The vote's recommendation reason lists it as a Daehangno (대학로) landmark linked to the song 'Cocktail Sarang' (Cocktail Love, 칵테일 사랑).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.

Cheonggyecheon (Changsin-dong, Jongno-gu / 종로구 창신동, ☎ Seoul Facilities Corporation 02-2290-6114) is a 10.84㎞ stream flowing between Jongno-gu and Jung-gu. Between 2003 and 2005 a 5.84㎞ section was restored and 22 bridges were built. The vote's recommendation reason lists it as a place that appears in RM's solo song 'Seoul'.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Entry may be restricted for safety when it rains. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Seochon Village (45 Pirundae-ro, Jongno-gu / 종로구 필운대로 45, ☎ Bukchon Village Information Center 02-2148-4161) is the name for the village west of Gyeongbokgung. It has old shops, hanok (한옥) and alley murals, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization description it is popular as a filming location for films, dramas and music videos. The vote's recommendation reason lists it as alleys that carry the mood of IU (아이유)'s album 'Kkotgalpi' (Flower Bookmark, 꽃갈피).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Seonyudo Park (343 Seonyu-ro, Yeongdeungpo-gu / 영등포구 선유로 343, ☎ 02-2631-9367~8) is an island used as a water purification plant from 1978 to 2000 and turned into an ecological park in 2002. It has the "Garden of Green Pillars" (녹색기둥의 정원), where only the concrete pillars remain, and the pedestrian bridge Seonyugyo (선유교). The vote's recommendation reason lists it as a place that appears in RM's solo song 'Seoul'.

  • Opening hours: 06:00~24:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Registered as not possible.

📍 Yeosu — Yeosu Marine Cable Car, Dolsan Bridge and Nangman Pocha Street

Several Yeosu attractions that cited Busker Busker (버스커버스커)'s 'Yeosu Bambada' (Yeosu Night Sea, 여수 밤바다) as the recommendation reason were on the vote in the "Places in the Lyrics" theme.

Yeosu Marine Cable Car (3600-1 Dolsan-ro, Dolsan-eup, Yeosu / 여수시 돌산읍 돌산로 3600-1) is a cable car linking Dolsan (돌산) and Jasan (자산) over the sea. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is the fourth sea-crossing marine cable car in Asia, and it operates 15 Crystal Cabins with tempered-glass floors and 35 regular cabins.

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value.

Dolsan Bridge (3617-7 Dolsan-ro, Dolsan-eup, Yeosu / 여수시 돌산읍 돌산로 3617-7, ☎ Yeosu City Hall 1899-2012) is a bridge linking Dolsan with the mainland. Since October 2000 it has been lit with scenic lighting that produces 50-odd colors. The vote's recommendation reason lists it as a night-view spot that goes well with 'Yeosu Bambada' (no singer is given).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Yeosu Nangman Pocha Street (102 Hamel-ro, Yeosu / 여수시 하멜로 102) is a street of pojangmacha (포장마차) food carts in the plaza under Geobukseon Bridge (거북선대교). 18 pocha buildings operate, and it is described as a place to enjoy Yeosu night views and busking performances together.

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value.

📍 Busan — Gwangalli Beach, Haeundae Dongbaekseom and Dalmaji Hill

Gwangalli Beach (219 Gwangan-haebyeon-ro, Suyeong-gu / 수영구 광안해변로 219, ☎ 051-622-4251) is a beach known for its night view of Gwangandaegyo Bridge (광안대교). In summer you can surf and paddleboard, and performances, festivals and drone shows are held. The vote's recommendation reason lists it as a place that goes with the summer mood of IVE's 'After LIKE'.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Haeundae Dongbaekseom (around 67 Dongbaek-ro, Haeundae-gu / 해운대구 동백로 67 일대, ☎ 051-749-7621) is a place that became connected to the mainland through sedimentation but is still called an island. The walking path around the island has a statue and poem stone of Choe Chi-won (최치원), a mermaid statue (인어상) and Nurimaru APEC House (누리마루 APEC하우스). The vote's recommendation reason lists it as the Dongbaekseom that appears in Cho Yong-pil (조용필)'s 'Dorawayo Busanhange' (Come Back to Busan Port, 돌아와요 부산항에). The vote list gives the address as 710-1 U-dong (우동 710-1).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Dalmaji Hill (184 Dalmaji-gil, Haeundae-gu / 해운대구 달맞이길 184, ☎ 051-749-4084) is Dalmaji Pass (달맞이 고개), halfway up Waosan (와우산) on the way from the east end of Haeundae Beach to Cheongsapo (청사포). According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the moonrise here was counted among the Eight Views of Korea (대한 팔경), and Haewoljeong Pavilion (해월정), built in 1997, is introduced as the symbol of Dalmaji Park (달맞이 공원). The vote's recommendation reason lists it as a place that appears in Choi Baek-ho (최백호)'s 'Busane Gamyeon' (If You Go to Busan, 부산에 가면).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

📍 Islands — Hyeopjae Beach and Ulleungdo

Hyeopjae Beach (329-10 Hallim-ro, Hallim-eup, Jeju-si / 제주시 한림읍 한림로 329-10, ☎ 064-728-3981) is a beach known for its white sand mixed with shell powder and for Biyangdo (비양도) island offshore. The water is shallow and the slope is gentle. The vote's recommendation reason recommends it as a good place to see the Jeju sea scenery in 'Jejudoui Pureun Bam' (Blue Night of Jeju Island, 제주도의 푸른 밤). The Korea Tourism Organization description mentions a song Seventeen (세븐틴) sends to their fans, but the song title is not given in the registered values. The vote list gives the address as 2497-1 Hyeopjae-ri, Hallim-eup (한림읍 협재리 2497-1).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Ulleungdo (5-3 Dodong 1-gil, Seo-myeon, Ulleung-gun / 울릉군 서면 도동1길 5-3, ☎ 054-790-6454) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the ninth largest island in Korea, formed by volcanic activity. It has 44 attached islets and 9 old-road courses (Ulleung Haedam-gil, 울릉 해담길) made by restoring paths that residents once walked. The vote's recommendation reason lists it as the place made famous by 'Ulleungdo Twist' (울릉도 트위스트).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

📍 Land of Old Songs — Kim Kwang-seok Dasi Geurigi-gil, Hwagae Market, Sangju Silver Sand Beach, Yudalsan, Wolchulsan and Chilgapsan

Kim Kwang-seok Dasi Geurigi-gil (2238 Dalgubeol-daero, Jung-gu, Daegu / 대구 중구 달구벌대로 2238, ☎ Daegu Tourist Information Center 053-627-1337) is a mural street decorated on the theme of the life and music of Kim Kwang-seok (김광석) in the alley beside Bangcheon Market (방천시장) in Daebong-dong (대봉동), where he lived. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, work began in 2010, and there are Kim Kwang-seok sculptures and murals along 350m of wall. A "Kim Kwang-seok Singing Contest" (김광석 노래 부르기 경연대회) is held every autumn. The vote list gives the address as 6-11 Daebong-dong (대봉동 6-11).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Hadong Hwagae Market (15 Ssanggye-ro, Hwagae-myeon, Hadong-gun / 하동군 화개면 쌍계로 15, ☎ 055-883-5722) is a traditional market where the Seomjin River (섬진강) and Hwagaecheon Stream (화개천) meet. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it was a marketplace where people of Yeongnam (영남) and Honam (호남) exchanged goods, the setting of Kim Dong-ri (김동리)'s novel 'Yeongma' (역마), and is widely known through the song 'Hwagaejangteo' (Hwagae Market, 화개장터). It is a permanent market that reopened in 2001. The vote's recommendation reason cites Cho Young-nam (조영남)'s 'Hwagaejangteo'. The vote list gives the address as Tap-ri, Hwagae-myeon (화개면 탑리).

  • Opening hours, closed days: Registered as varying by shop.

Namhae Sangju Silver Sand Beach (17 Sangju-ro, Sangju-myeon, Namhae-gun / 남해군 상주면 상주로 17, ☎ Namhae County Office Marine Leisure Team 055-860-3073) is a 2㎞ half-moon-shaped sand beach with Geumsan (금산) standing behind it like a folding screen. The slope is gentle, and a summer festival and a winter sunrise festival are held. The vote's recommendation reason lists it as the setting of Duldaseot (둘다섯)'s 'Bambae' (Night Boat, 밤배). The vote list gives the address as 17-4 Sangju-ro (상주로 17-4).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Free. Shower room adult 2,000 won.

Mokpo Yudalsan (San 27-3, Jukgyo-dong, Mokpo / 목포시 죽교동 산27-3, ☎ 061-270-8411) is a mountain introduced as the last peak of the Noryeong Mountains (노령산맥). It has five pavilions, and the Dadohae (다도해) island sea can be seen from the summit. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, there is a monument to 'Mokpoui Nunmul' (Tears of Mokpo, 목포의 눈물), sung by Lee Nan-young (이난영), at the foot of the mountain.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Yeongam Wolchulsan National Park (280-43 Cheonhwangsa-ro, Yeongam-eup, Yeongam-gun / 영암군 영암읍 천황사로 280-43, ☎ 061-473-5210) was designated the 20th national park in 1988. The summit is Cheonhwangbong (천황봉, 809m). The vote's recommendation reason states that Wolchulsan often appears in Ha Chun-hwa (하춘화)'s 'Yeongam Arirang' (영암아리랑).

  • Opening hours: Months 4~10 04:00~15:00, months 11~3 05:00~14:00. Entry may be restricted depending on weather conditions.
  • Closed days: The registration says there are trail closure periods and to see the website. Fee: Free.

Cheongyang Chilgapsan Provincial Park (241 Janggok-gil, Daechi-myeon, Cheongyang-gun / 청양군 대치면 장곡길 241, ☎ 041-940-2721) is a mountain 561m above sea level, designated a provincial park in 1973. It has 9 hiking trails, and the course descending from Hanti Pass (한티고개) to Janggoksa Temple (장곡사) is the most visited, taking about 3 hours. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, there is a Chilgapsan Song Monument (칠갑산노래비) around Chilgapsanjang (칠갑산장). The vote's recommendation reason cites Ju Byeong-seon (주병선)'s 'Chilgapsan' (칠갑산).

  • Opening hours: Entry may be restricted depending on weather conditions.
  • Closed days: The registration says some courses are closed 2026.10.20~12.15. Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

⚠ 3 Things to Know in Advance

  1. Closed days differ — Deoksugung closes on Mondays, and the Korea Tourism Organization's Gwanghwamun entry has a Tuesday closure registered (registered as call 02-3700-3901 for details).
  2. Chilgapsan partial course closure period — A closure of some courses on 2026.10.20~12.15 is registered (check again after the period). Wolchulsan also has trail closure periods.
  3. Song links follow the vote's recommendation reasons — Song titles and singers are copied from the recommendation reasons. The places where the Korea Tourism Organization description also mentions a song include Kim Kwang-seok Dasi Geurigi-gil, Hwagae Market, Yudalsan and Chilgapsan.

⚙ FAQ 3

Q. Which places are linked to 'Gwanghwamun Yeonga'?

A. Deoksugung (1st in the vote) and Gwanghwamun (2nd). The vote's recommendation reason cites the Deoksugung Stone Wall Road and Jeongdong Church together.

Q. Where can I see the scenery of 'Yeosu Bambada'?

A. The Yeosu places in this guide are the Yeosu Marine Cable Car, Dolsan Bridge and Yeosu Nangman Pocha Street. Dolsan Bridge has been lit with scenic lighting since 2000.

Q. Which places have an entrance fee?

A. The places with a registered entrance fee are Deoksugung (individual 1,000 won) and the Gwanghwamun entry (adult 3,000 won). The Yeosu Marine Cable Car, Yeosu Nangman Pocha Street, Marronnier Park, Hadong Hwagae Market and Namhae Sangju Silver Sand Beach have no registered entrance fee value (for Sangju Silver Sand Beach only the shower room fee is registered).
