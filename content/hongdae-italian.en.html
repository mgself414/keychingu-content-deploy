⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. See “Hongdae = Indie/Club Only” — Italian Pass

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. They only recognized Hongdae as an indie music, club, and street food area, and missed at least 5 Italian restaurants in Hongdae, Yeonnam, Hapjeong, and Mangwon. In fact, Hongdae area is the No. 1 area for new Italian restaurants in Korea — Pasta signatures, Italian courses, modern wine pairings, and casual pizzas are concentrated in one area. Hongdae culture + Italian dinner = The most foreign date/meeting route in Hongdae.

2. Yeonnam, Hapjeong, and Mangwon are considered separate neighborhoods

Foreigners only look at Hongdae and find Italians in Gangnam and Itaewon, but Hongdae Station + Yeonnam + Hapjeong + Mangwon = No. 1 route for Italians. More than 8 Italian restaurants within a 15-minute walk from Hongik University Station. A 30-minute walk through Yeonnam’s alleys + 1 Italian restaurant is the best date course. Instead of going to the Italian restaurant in Gangnam or Itaewon, find a solution within the Hongdae area.

3. Italian reservation/time zone not considered

Popular Italian (Gwangya, Aurum, Fogg, Langt, Yeonnam) has a standard 1-2 hour wait or reservation required on weekends. Best Hansan during weekdays 18-19 PM — Lunch at 13:00 or after 21:00 is also OK on weekends. Closed on Mondays and Tuesdays. Stores available. Advance reservations through Instagram, Catch Table, or Naver are recommended. Course stores have limited seating, so weekdays are given priority.

Hongdae, Yeonnam, Hapjeong, Mangwon Italian scenery

Since the early 2020s, Hongdae has been the #1 area for new Korean Italian restaurants. Trendy, casual, modern differentiated from Gangnam (high-end, fine dining), Itaewon (trendy, multinational), and Hannam (Michelin, course).
