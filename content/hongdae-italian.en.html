⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Hongdae (홍대) = Indie/Clubs Only" — Skipping Italian

This is the most common mistake for foreign tourists. Many foreigners perceive Hongdae (홍대) only as an area for indie music, clubs, and street food, missing out on 5+ Italian restaurants in the Hongdae (홍대), Yeonnam (연남), Hapjeong (합정), and Mangwon (망원) areas. In reality, the Hongdae (홍대) area is Korea's top emerging region for Italian cuisine — signature pasta, course Italian, modern wine pairings, and casual pizza spots are concentrated within one itinerary. Hongdae (홍대) culture + Italian dinner = the most foreigner-friendly Hongdae (홍대) date/gathering itinerary.

2. Viewing Yeonnam (연남), Hapjeong (합정), and Mangwon (망원) as Separate Neighborhoods

Foreigners often only look at Hongdae (홍대) and search for Italian food in Gangnam (강남) or Itaewon (이태원), but Hongdae Station (홍대역) + Yeonnam (연남) + Hapjeong (합정) + Mangwon (망원) = the top Italian itinerary. 8+ Italian restaurants are within a 15-minute walk from Hongik University Station (홍대입구역). A 30-minute stroll through Yeonnam (연남) alleys (golmok, a narrow street or alleyway) + one Italian restaurant makes for the best date course. Instead of going to Gangnam (강남) or Itaewon (이태원) for Italian, solve it within the Hongdae (홍대) area.

3. Not Considering Italian Restaurant Reservations and Timings

Popular Italian restaurants (Gwangya (광야), Aurum (아우룸), Fog (포그), Lante Yeonnam (랑트 연남)) typically require a 1-2 hour wait or reservation on weekends. Weekdays 18:00-19:00 (6-7 PM) are best for a quieter experience — on weekends, 13:00 (1 PM) for lunch or after 21:00 (9 PM) is also fine. Some establishments are closed on Mondays and Tuesdays — advance booking via Instagram or Catchtable/Naver is recommended. Course restaurants have limited seating, so weekdays are preferred.

Since the early 2020s, the Hongdae (홍대) area has established itself as Korea's top emerging region for Italian cuisine.
