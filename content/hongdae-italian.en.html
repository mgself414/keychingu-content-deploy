⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Thinking "Hongdae = indie music & clubs only" — skipping Italian entirely

The most common mistake in foreign visitor itineraries. Visitors see Hongdae (홍대) as purely an indie music, club, and street food zone and completely miss the 5+ Italian restaurants spread across Hongdae, Yeonnam (연남), Hapjeong (합정), and Mangwon (망원). In reality, Hongdae is Korea's #1 zone for up-and-coming Italian restaurants — pasta signatures, Italian course dining, modern wine pairings, and casual pizza all within a single walkable area. Hongdae culture + Italian dinner = the most authentic foreign-visitor date or group dinner itinerary.

2. Treating Yeonnam, Hapjeong, and Mangwon as completely separate neighborhoods

Many foreign visitors only look in Hongdae and then head to Gangnam or Itaewon for Italian. But Hongdae Station (홍대입구역) + Yeonnam (연남) + Hapjeong (합정) + Mangwon (망원) form the #1 Italian dining corridor. 8+ Italian restaurants within a 15-minute walk of Hongdae Station. A 30-minute stroll through Yeonnam's alleyways (골목 — the charming back-street lane culture unique to Seoul neighborhoods) + one Italian dinner = the best date course. Skip the Gangnam or Itaewon detour and keep it in the Hongdae cluster.

3. Not planning around reservations and timing

Popular spots like Gwangya (광야), Aurum (아우룸), Fog (포그), and Lante Yeonnam (랑트 연남) typically require 1–2 hours of waiting on weekends, or reservations are essential. Weekday 18:00–19:00 (6–7 PM) is the sweet spot — weekends, aim for a 13:00 (1 PM) lunch or after 21:00 (9 PM). Some restaurants close on Monday and Tuesday — check Instagram or book in advance via Catchtable or Naver. Course-dining spots have limited seating; always prioritize weekdays.

The Italian Scene in Hongdae · Yeonnam · Hapjeong · Mangwon

Since the early 2020s, the Hongdae cluster has established itself as Korea's #1 zone for emerging Italian restaurants. It carves out a distinctly trendy · casual · modern identity, separate from Gangnam (high-end, fine dining), Itaewon (trendy, multinational), and Hannam (Michelin, course dining).

Hongdae Core (홍대 본권) is the #1 Italian neighborhood — 6+ pasta, course, pizza, and casual Italian spots within a 10–15-minute walk of Hongdae Station (홍대입구역). Gwangya (광야), Riike (리이크), Aurum (아우룸), 23 Platter (23플래터), and The Place Hongdae L7 (더플레이스 홍대L7점) are the foreign-visitor top picks. A true trendy hotspot.

Yeonnam (연남) is the modern Italian + course dining district — multiple spots along Yeonnam-dong's alleyways (골목) occupy hanok (한옥 — traditional Korean wooden house) or villa buildings renovated into Italian restaurants. Neuru Yeonnam (느루 연남점), Lante Yeonnam (랑트 연남), Ann's Kitchen Yeonnam (안스키친 연남점), and Daily Round Yeonnam (데일리라운드 연남) define the modern atmosphere. A top date-night destination for foreign visitors.

Hapjeong (합정) specializes in signature pasta + wine pairing — in the alleyways between Hapjeong Station (합정역) and Mangwon Station (망원역), you'll find signature pasta restaurants alongside wine-pairing course spots. Dot Hapjeong (도트 합정) and Fog (포그) lead the ambiance game. Best for a wine + course combination evening.

Mangwon (망원) offers casual neighborhood Italian — relaxed spots near Mangwon Market (망원시장) and the Han River (한강). Neighborhood vibes with reasonably priced menus.
