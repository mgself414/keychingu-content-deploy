⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Gangnam = only downtown": Gangnam = 5 sub-areas (Gangnam Station (강남역), Dosan (도산), Cheongdam (청담), Apgujeong (압구정), Sinnonhyeon (신논현)) with 100 selections.

2. "Gangnam = expensive": Gangnam prices come in 3 tiers (#310): value for money / standard / luxury.

3. "Gangnam = weekdays only": Weekends are top priority for sell-outs among foreign enthusiasts.
