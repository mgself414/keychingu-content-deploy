Your First Day in Gangnam (강남): A Zone-by-Zone Map of 11 Spots in 5 Zones — Gangnam Square (강남스퀘어 광장), Starfield Library (별마당 도서관), Bongeunsa Temple (봉은사), Garosu-gil (가로수길), Dosan Park (도산공원) (Checked October 2026)

Subtitle: Gangnam Square (강남스퀘어 광장) sits between Exits 11 and 12 of Gangnam Station (강남역) (VisitGangnam) · Starfield Library (별마당 도서관) is open daily 10:30~22:00 and free (VisitSeoul) · Bongeunsa Temple (봉은사) is 135m from Exit 1 of Bongeunsa Station (봉은사역) (VisitSeoul) · Garosu-gil (가로수길) is by Exit 8 of Sinsa Station (신사역) (VisitSeoul) · Dosan Park (도산공원) is open at all times (VisitKorea) — as of 2026-10-22

ISSUE 03 · #301 · 2026-05-20

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Come up at Exit 11 of Gangnam Station (강남역) and behind the heart sculpture a huge media wall plays K-pop videos nonstop (VisitGangnam). Gangnam (강남) is not one neighborhood. This guide is a map for travelers on their first day in Gangnam who need to decide which station to get off at and where to start. We split it into five zones: Gangnam Station (강남역) · Sinnonhyeon (신논현), Samseong (삼성) (COEX · Bongeunsa Temple), Sinsa Garosu-gil (신사 가로수길), Apgujeong (압구정) · Cheongdam (청담), and Dosan Park (도산공원). For each zone we picked 1~3 places listed by the official Gangnam-gu tourism site (VisitGangnam), the Seoul Tourism Organization (VisitSeoul), and VisitKorea tourism information, and the subway stations and exits are copied only as those pages state them. For deeper dives into cafes, shopping, and restaurants, follow the links attached to each zone.

🌃 Gangnam Station (강남역) · Sinnonhyeon (신논현) — 2 Spots

  • Gangnam Square (강남스퀘어 광장) — 396 Gangnam-daero (강남대로 396), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul, between Exits 11 and 12 of Gangnam Station (강남역). Subway Line 2 and the Shinbundang Line (신분당선). It has a huge media wall playing K-POP videos, a permanent performance stage, and a heart sculpture reading 'I L♡VE GANGNAM style'. Along a stretch of about 760m from Gangnam Station (강남역) to Sinnonhyeon Station (신논현역), 18 three-sided LED columns (the media poles 'G-LIGHT') line the street, which makes it a good evening walk (VisitGangnam)
  • Gangnam Station Underground Shopping Center (강남역지하쇼핑센터) — B396 Gangnam-daero (강남대로 지하396), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. 10:00~23:00, phone 02-553-1898. It connects directly to Gangnam Station (강남역) on Line 2 and has more than 200 shops. It is described as selling clothes, cosmetics, electronics, and small goods at relatively low prices (VisitGangnam)
  • For cafes in this zone, see #142 Gangnam Cafe Guide; for planning a daily budget, see #310 Gangnam Budget Guide

🏛 Samseong (삼성) — COEX, Starfield Library, Bongeunsa Temple: 3 Spots

  • Starfield COEX Mall (스타필드 코엑스몰) — 513 Yeongdong-daero (영동대로 513), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. It connects directly to Exits 5 and 6 of Samseong Station (삼성역) on Line 2 and Exit 7 of Bongeunsa Station (봉은사역) on Line 9. Open year-round, with hours varying by store (VisitSeoul). VisitGangnam lists the COEX Mall (코엑스몰) hours as 10:30~22:00. COEX (코엑스) is an exhibition and convention facility that opened in March 1979 (VisitKorea). There are 3 lockers inside the mall (opposite Zara (자라) F109 · next to Topten (탑텐) F115 · next to Gowa Flower (고와플라워) N101, as of 2025, VisitSeoul)
  • Starfield Library (별마당 도서관) — B1 of Starfield COEX Mall (스타필드 코엑스몰). Daily 10:30~22:00, open year-round, free. 353m from Exit 5 of Samseong Station (삼성역), 328m from Exit 7 of Bongeunsa Station (봉은사역) (VisitSeoul). Its 13m-tall shelves hold about 70,000 books, plus about 600 magazine titles including foreign magazines. Phone 02-6002-3031 (VisitGangnam)
  • Bongeunsa Temple (봉은사) — 531 Bongeunsa-ro (봉은사로 531), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. 135m from Exit 1 of Bongeunsa Station (봉은사역) on Line 9. A temple founded in 794 during the Silla (신라) era, it runs Templestay (an overnight temple stay) and experiences such as dawn chanting, Seon (Zen) meditation, and tea ceremony. Every May on Buddha's Birthday, a lantern festival is held nearby in Samseong-dong (삼성동) (VisitSeoul). Opening hours differ by source: VisitGangnam says 05:00~22:00, VisitKorea says 09:00~18:00, and VisitSeoul says "it varies with the Dharma service (religious service) schedule". If you are going late, confirm by phone at 02-3218-4800
  • For comparing department stores and shopping malls, see #449 Seoul Department Store and Shopping Mall Guide

🌳 Sinsa Garosu-gil (신사 가로수길) — 2 Spots

  • Garosu-gil (가로수길) in Sinsa-dong (신사동) — 454m from Exit 8 of Sinsa Station (신사역) on Line 3 and the Shinbundang Line (신분당선); Exit 4 of Apgujeong Station (압구정역) on Line 3 is also close (VisitSeoul). The street runs from the intersection in front of Hyundai High School (현대고등학교) to the Dosan-daero (도산대로) three-way junction east of Sinsa Station (신사역). Today fashion and beauty stores and pop-up stores cluster here. Opening hours differ shop by shop, and parking is not available (VisitKorea)
  • Horim Museum Sinsa Branch (호림박물관 신사분관) — 317 Dosan-daero (도산대로 317), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Tue~Sat 10:30~18:00, closed Mon and Sun. Close to Sinsa Station (신사역). It opened in June 2009, and the pottery-shaped museum building holds 3~4 special exhibitions a year on floors 2~4. Phone 02-541-3525 (VisitGangnam). The admission fee is not on the page, so we could not confirm it
  • To spend a full day on Garosu-gil (가로수길), see #295 Garosu-gil Guide and #30 Garosu-gil Shopping Course

💎 Apgujeong (압구정) · Cheongdam (청담) — 2 Spots

  • K-Star Road (K Star ROAD) — A walking route through Cheongdam-dong (청담동), Sinsa-dong (신사동), and Nonhyeon-dong (논현동) that follows the favorite shops of Hallyu (Korean Wave) stars and the talent agencies. It is divided into Zone A from the Dosan Park (도산공원) intersection to the Sinsa Station (신사역) intersection, Zone B from the Dosan Park (도산공원) intersection to the Hakdong (학동) intersection, Zone C from the Hakdong (학동) intersection to the Cheongdam (청담) intersection, and Zone D from the Cheongdam (청담) intersection to the south end of Yeongdong Bridge (영동대교) (VisitGangnam). A 3-minute walk from Exit 2 of Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역) on the Suin-Bundang Line (수인분당선) (VisitSeoul, page revised in 2022)
  • Gangnam Medical Tourism Center (강남메디컬투어센터) (tourist information center) — 161 Apgujeong-ro (압구정로 161), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Weekdays 10:00~18:00, closed weekends and public holidays. 3-minute walk from Exit 6 of Apgujeong Station (압구정역) on Line 3. It handles multilingual tourist information, maps and guidebooks, luggage storage, and tax refunds. Phone 1661-2230 (VisitGangnam)
  • For Apgujeong Rodeo (압구정 로데오), department stores, and the Han River at Jamwon (잠원), see the #713 Apgujeong · Jamwon guide (in Related Content at the bottom); for walking Cheongdam (청담), see Walking Cheongdam — Louis Vuitton Maison and House of Dior Architecture, SongEun, Espace Louis Vuitton and Atelier Hermès Galleries, Dosan Park; for luxury shopping, see #771 Gangnam Luxury Shopping Guide

🌲 Dosan Park (도산공원) — 2 Spots

  • Dosan Park (도산공원) — 20 Dosan-daero 45-gil (도산대로45길 20), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Open at all times, year-round. Opened on November 10, 1973. Inquiries: Gangnam-gu Office Parks and Green Spaces Division (강남구청 공원녹지과), 02-3423-6244 (VisitKorea). 882m from Exits 2 and 3 of Apgujeong Station (압구정역) on Line 3 (VisitSeoul). The park honors the independence activist Dosan Ahn Chang-ho (도산 안창호) and has a statue, a memorial hall, and the grave where he lies beside his wife, Lady Lee Hye-ryeon (이혜련) (VisitKorea)
  • Dosan Ahn Chang-ho Memorial Hall (도산안창호기념관) — Inside Dosan Park (도산공원). Free, phone 02-541-1800 (VisitKorea). Hours differ by source: VisitKorea says 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30, closed January 1 and during the Seollal (설, Lunar New Year) and Chuseok (추석, Korean Thanksgiving) holidays), VisitSeoul says 10:00~18:00 (closed Sundays, Seollal (설), Chuseok (추석), and Christmas), and VisitGangnam says 10:00~17:00. If you plan to go on a Sunday, call first to check
  • For fine dining around the park, see #22 Gangnam Fine Dining Guide

🧰 Gangnam First-Day Essentials — Solo, Luggage, Payment, Getting Around

  • Getting around (route facts only): Gangnam Station (강남역) and Samseong Station (삼성역) are both on Line 2. Gangnam Station (강남역) and Sinsa Station (신사역) are linked by the Shinbundang Line (신분당선). Sinsa Station (신사역) and Apgujeong Station (압구정역) are on Line 3. Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역) is on the Suin-Bundang Line (수인분당선), and Bongeunsa Station (봉은사역) is on Line 9 (VisitGangnam · VisitSeoul, each page). There are no official values for travel time between sections, so we did not list any. For transit cards, see #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards
  • Luggage: On weekdays, use the luggage storage at Gangnam Medical Tourism Center (강남메디컬투어센터) (VisitGangnam); on weekends, use the 3 lockers at COEX Mall (코엑스몰) (VisitSeoul)
  • Payment and refunds: Gangnam Medical Tourism Center (강남메디컬투어센터) also provides tax refund guidance (VisitGangnam). For cards and mobile payment, see #527 Korea's Payment Systems
  • Solo: Starfield Library (별마당 도서관), Bongeunsa Temple (봉은사), and Dosan Park (도산공원) are free spaces that suit a quiet solo walk (each official page lists them as free and open at all times). For eating alone, see #348 Seoul Solo Diner Map
  • When language fails: tourist information line 1330 (VisitSeoul)

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Gangnam = Gangnam Station" — The Gangnam-gu tourism introduction also separately lists Apgujeong Rodeo (압구정 로데오) and Cheongdam (청담) select shops, restaurants around Gangnam Station (강남역), Garosu-gil (가로수길) K-beauty, and COEX (코엑스) (VisitKorea). On day one, pick just two or three zones

2. "The tourist information center is open on weekends" — Gangnam Medical Tourism Center (강남메디컬투어센터) is closed on weekends and public holidays (VisitGangnam)

3. "The park closes at night" — Dosan Park (도산공원) is open at all times (VisitKorea · VisitSeoul)

4. "Bongeunsa Temple (봉은사) opens at the same hours every day" — The three official sources give different hours. It varies with the Dharma service (religious service) schedule (VisitSeoul)
