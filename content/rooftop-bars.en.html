🎯 Why Seoul Rooftops & Terraces?

The number one spot for inbound foreign tourists to experience Seoul's night view is rooftop and terrace cafes and bars. They offer the best urban panorama of Korea, stunning nightscapes, and are a top choice for Instagram enthusiasts. You'll find a complete set of foreign tourist favorites in Hannam (한남), Itaewon (이태원), Gangnam (강남), and Hongdae (홍대).

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Rooftops = Expensive": Korean rooftops typically cost ₩15,000 (~$11 / ~80 RMB / ~1,600 JPY) to ₩50,000 (~$37 / ~270 RMB / ~5,300 JPY) per person (luxury hotels ₩100,000+ (~$74 / ~540 RMB / ~10,600 JPY)). They are a top spot for foreigners offering great value for money.

2. "Hotel Rooftops = Cannot Enter": 5-star hotel rooftops and lounges are top priority for foreigner-friendliness, English-speaking staff are available, and they are a number one spot for foreign enthusiasts.

3. "Rooftops = Only in Summer": Korean rooftops are a year-round favorite, offering spring and summer night views, autumn foliage night views, and winter illuminations.

4. "Hannam (한남) Rooftops = Anywhere": Hannam (한남) is the number one residential area for foreigners (#144) and a top spot for rooftop establishments, making it a favorite among foreign visitors.

5. "Jamsil (잠실) Lotte Tower = Too Expensive": Signiel (시그니엘) (#9) or Seoul Sky (#82) are the number one spots for foreign enthusiasts, offering Korea's #1 night view from 555m.
