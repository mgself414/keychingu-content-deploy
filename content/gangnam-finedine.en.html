Gangnam (강남) Fine Dining Guide — 8 Michelin-Starred Picks (2026)

Subtitle: 7 of Seoul's 10 two-Star restaurants are in Gangnam-gu (강남구). From 3-Star Mingles (밍글스) to a 1-Star French restaurant, we checked the rating, address, closing days and booking channel of 8 Star restaurants in Gangnam that you can reserve and reach on foot, using the official Michelin page and each restaurant's official site (2026-10-07)

ISSUE 02 · #22 · 2026-05-15 (fully revised 2026-10-07 — checked against the official Michelin 2026 list)

🎯 Why Gangnam (강남)?

In the MICHELIN Guide Seoul & Busan 2026 (announced March 5, 2026), Seoul has 1 three-Star restaurant, 10 two-Star restaurants and 31 one-Star restaurants. The only 3-Star, Mingles (밍글스), is in Cheongdam (청담), and 7 of the 10 two-Star restaurants have a Gangnam-gu (강남구) address (Jungsik (정식당), Kwonsooksoo (권숙수), Soigné (스와니예), Evett (에빗), Alla Prima (알라 프리마), Mitou (미토우) and Restaurant Allen (레스토랑 알렌)). If you only do fine dining once in Seoul, Gangnam (강남) has the widest choice.

From these, this guide picks the 8 that a first-time visitor can realistically book and find. There are three criteria. Is there a booking channel open through the official site, email or phone? Is there English information, or is the restaurant inside a hotel where you can get help? And can you reach it by subway, around Dosan Park (도산공원), Apgujeong (압구정) and Teheran-ro (테헤란로)?

All of them are in Michelin's top price band (₩₩₩₩). None publishes its course price on its official site, so we have not listed prices. Check when you book.

⚠ 4 Things First-Time Bookers Miss

1. Almost everything closes on Monday. 7 of the 8 are closed on Mondays; only Soigné (스와니예) opens on Monday. Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) is also closed on Tuesday.

2. Booking channels differ from place to place. Evett (에빗), Restaurant Allen (레스토랑 알렌) and Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) take reservations only through Catch Table. Kwonsooksoo (권숙수) uses no third-party platform and takes direct bookings with the restaurant only. Jungsik (정식당) also accepts email (reservation@jungsik.kr).

3. Hotel restaurants have dress code and age rules. Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) requires business casual, restricts men's tracksuits, slippers, sandals and shorts, and does not admit children under 8.

4. Ask about allergies and vegetarian needs when you book. Courses are built around meat and seafood. Some places do not offer substitutes at all (Jamsil Bichena (잠실 비채나) is one), so if it is not possible, choose another restaurant.

① 3 Stars (1 restaurant)

Mingles (밍글스)

  • Address: 19, Dosan-daero 67-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, 2F (Cheongdam (청담))
  • Cuisine: Korean. Michelin highlights the house-developed elements such as the "Mingling Pot" and the "Sauce Trio." Chef Kang Min-goo
  • Rating: 3 Stars for the second year in a row in 2026. Seoul's only 3 Stars
  • Hours: Lunch Tue–Sat 12:00–15:00, closed Mon and Sun (per the Michelin page. Check dinner on the official site)
  • Booking: Directly with the restaurant. Official site restaurant-mingles.com · phone +82-2-515-7306
  • Notes: Valet parking, wine list, cards accepted. English support not confirmed

② 2 Stars (5 of the 7 in Gangnam (강남))

Jungsik (정식당)

  • Address: 11, Seolleung-ro 158-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul (Cheongdam (청담) · Apgujeong Rodeo (압구정로데오))
  • Cuisine: Contemporary. Chef Yim Jung-sik. Regarded as a pioneer who took Korean cooking to the world stage
  • Booking: The official site jungsik.kr links to Catch Table, or email reservation@jungsik.kr · phone +82-2-517-4654. Korean and English sites make this the easiest to book from overseas
  • Notes: Suitable for solo diners and groups, wheelchair accessible, valet parking, wine list

Kwonsooksoo (권숙수)

  • Address: 37, Apgujeong-ro 80-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, 4F (Cheongdam (청담))
  • Cuisine: Korean. Chef Kwon Woo-joong. A course where modern techniques meet Korean tradition
  • Hours: Lunch Tue–Sat 12:00–15:00, closed Mon and Sun (per the Michelin page)
  • Booking: Direct booking with the restaurant only (it does not use third-party booking platforms). Official site kwonsooksoo.com · phone +82-2-542-6268
  • Notes: Valet parking, wine list

Soigné (스와니예)

  • Address: 652, Gangnam-daero, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, Sinsa Square (신사스퀘어) 2F, Room 201 (Sinsa (신사))
  • Cuisine: Contemporary. Chef Lee Jun. A tasting course that weaves season and ingredients into a story told as "episodes"
  • Hours: Lunch Mon–Sat 12:00–15:00, closed Sun (per the Michelin page)
  • Booking: Directly with the restaurant. Official site soigneseoul.com · phone +82-2-3477-9386
  • Notes: Wheelchair accessible, valet parking, wine list. The only one open on Monday

Evett (에빗)

  • Address: 10-5, Dosan-daero 45-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul (Dosan Park (도산공원))
  • Cuisine: Innovative · Korean. Chef Joseph Liddelow. Foraged ingredients and house-fermented pastes; the signature is the miso donut
  • Hours: Tue–Thu 17:20–22:30, Fri–Sat 12:00–14:30, closed Mon and Sun (per the Michelin page. Reconfirm before booking)
  • Booking: The official booking page takes Catch Table only. For 5 or more, email reservation@restaurantevett.com
  • Notes: Korean and English site. Wheelchair accessible, valet parking, wine list

Restaurant Allen (레스토랑 알렌)

  • Address: 231, Teheran-ro (테헤란로), Gangnam-gu, Seoul, Centerfield (센터필드) East 2F, E205 (Yeoksam (역삼))
  • Cuisine: Contemporary. Chef Seo Hyun-min. Seasonal cooking with a light French influence. Reviewers single out the sourdough raised with makgeolli yeast
  • Hours: Tue 18:00–22:00, Wed–Sat 12:00–15:00, closed Mon and Sun (per the Michelin page)
  • Booking: Catch Table online (from 30 days ahead), phone +82-2-6985-7214 (Tue–Sat 10:00–22:00). For 5 or more or a private room, inquire directly. A deposit is required. Email info@restaurantallen.com
  • Notes: English website. Wheelchair accessible, wine list. Same Teheran-ro (테헤란로) 231 block as Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든)

Other 2 Stars in Gangnam (강남) — Alla Prima (알라 프리마) (13, Hakdong-ro 17-gil, innovative, counter seating, phone +82-2-511-2555) and Mitou (미토우) (24, Dosan-daero 70-gil, Japanese, Tue–Sat 18:00–22:00, phone +82-10-7286-9914) are also 2 Stars in 2026. We could not confirm English information or online booking on either official site, so we recommend them only if you can book by phone.

③ 1 Star (2 restaurants)

Kang Minchul Restaurant (강민철 레스토랑)

  • Address: 18, Dosan-daero 63-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, 5F (new space after its 2025 move, Cheongdam (청담))
  • Cuisine: Contemporary French. French technique with Korean and Asian inspiration. Chef Kang Min-chul
  • Hours: Tue–Fri 12:00–14:30 · 18:00–21:30, Sat 12:00–14:30 · 18:30–22:00, closed Mon and Sun
  • Booking: Directly with the restaurant. Official site kangminchul.com · phone +82-2-545-2511
  • Notes: Michelin marks it as a "comfortable place to dine alone." A good fit for solo-travelling food lovers

Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든)

  • Address: 231, Teheran-ro (테헤란로), Gangnam-gu, Seoul, Josun Palace (조선 팰리스) 36F (Yeoksam (역삼))
  • Cuisine: Modern Korean. Seasonal ingredients presented with illustrated cards and explanations in a space like a garden in the city
  • Hours: Wed–Sun lunch 12:00–14:30 · dinner 18:00–22:00, closed Mon and Tue (official site)
  • Booking: Catch Table only from December 1, 2025. The site says the phone line, +82-2-727-7610, is slow because of the volume of inquiries
  • Dress code · age: Business casual required (men's tracksuits, slippers, sandals and shorts restricted), no children under 8
  • Notes: Hotel site in Korean and English. Wheelchair accessible, counter seating, view from the 36th floor

④ Booking in Practice — How to Reserve from Abroad

  • Start with the ones you can see on an English site: Jungsik (정식당) (Catch Table or email), Evett (에빗) (Catch Table), Restaurant Allen (레스토랑 알렌) (Catch Table · phone · email), Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) (Catch Table only).
  • Catch Table is a Korean app. We could not confirm whether you can sign up with an overseas phone number. Jungsik (정식당), Evett (에빗) (5 or more) and Allen (알렌) have email channels, and Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) has a hotel concierge, so these are the safe options for booking from overseas.
  • Direct booking only: Kwonsooksoo (권숙수). Book only through the official site or by phone.
  • Booking-opening rule: Restaurant Allen (레스토랑 알렌) opens 30 days ahead. Other places often have similar rules, so read the booking notes on the official site first.
  • Lunch is the entry point. Mingles (밍글스), Kwonsooksoo (권숙수), Soigné (스와니예) and Allen (알렌) (Wed–Sat) serve lunch. Check dinner hours on the official site.

🗺 3 Routes

A. Dosan Park (도산공원) Line (on foot)

  • Stroll Dosan Park (도산공원) → lunch at Mingles (밍글스) (Tue–Sat) or dinner at Evett (에빗) (Tue–Thu) → café in Apgujeong Rodeo (압구정로데오) (#142) → dinner at Kang Minchul (강민철) (Tue–Sat)
  • Mingles (밍글스), Evett (에빗) and Kang Minchul (강민철) sit in the side streets on either side of Dosan-daero. To visit two in a day, split them into lunch and dinner

B. Cheongdam (청담) · Sinsa (신사) Two-Star Line

  • Lunch at Kwonsooksoo (권숙수) (Apgujeong-ro 80-gil) → Cheongdam (청담) luxury street → dinner at Soigné (스와니예) (652 Gangnam-daero) or Jungsik (정식당) (Seolleung-ro 158-gil)
  • Kwonsooksoo (권숙수) and Jungsik (정식당) are about a 10-minute walk apart. Soigné (스와니예) is near Sinsa Station (신사역), so take a taxi or one subway stop

C. One Block on Teheran-ro (테헤란로)

  • Restaurant Allen (레스토랑 알렌) (Centerfield (센터필드) East 2F) and Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) (Josun Palace (조선 팰리스) 36F) are both at 231 Teheran-ro (테헤란로). Lunch at Allen (알렌) (Wed–Sat) → dinner at Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) (Wed–Sun) — both book through Catch Table

⚙ FAQ

1. Which places definitely have English? Jungsik (정식당), Evett (에빗) and Restaurant Allen (레스토랑 알렌) have English pages on their official sites, and Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) is inside a hotel, so you can use the concierge. We could not confirm Mingles (밍글스), Kwonsooksoo (권숙수), Soigné (스와니예) or Kang Minchul (강민철) this time.

2. Can I go without a reservation? Assume not. All 8 serve courses, Kwonsooksoo (권숙수) takes direct bookings only, and Evett (에빗), Allen (알렌) and Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) take Catch Table only.

3. Where can I go on Monday? Only Soigné (스와니예) (lunch Mon–Sat).

4. Can I dine alone? Michelin marks Kang Minchul Restaurant (강민철 레스토랑) as a "comfortable place to dine alone." Jungsik (정식당) is marked "suitable for solo diners and groups." Alla Prima (알라 프리마), Mitou (미토우) and Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) have counter seating.

5. What if I'm with children? Eatanic Garden (이타닉 가든) does not admit children under 8. For the rest, ask when you book.

6. What about price? All 8 are in Michelin's top price band (₩₩₩₩), and none publishes course prices on its official site. Restaurant Allen (레스토랑 알렌) requires a deposit.
