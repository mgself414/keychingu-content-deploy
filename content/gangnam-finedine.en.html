22 – Gangnam (강남) Fine Dining: Top 5 Picks

One-Line Intro

Gangnam's top 5 fine dining picks for a special night out in Seoul (서울).

From Korean omakase to modern hansik (한식), here are 5 Michelin-starred restaurants for an unforgettable evening in Gangnam (강남).

Who This Is For

  • Travelers planning a special anniversary dinner or business dinner
  • Food lovers curious about the modern reinterpretation of Korean cuisine

Recommended Itinerary (4–5 hours, dinner)

1. 18:30 Arrive & Check In (reservation required): Book 2 weeks to 1 month in advance

2. 19:00 Amuse-bouche + First Course: Add champagne or traditional Korean liquor (jeongtongju, 전통주) pairing for ₩30,000–50,000

3. 20:30 Main Courses: 5–10 courses centered on Korean beef (hanwoo, 한우), seafood, and seasonal vegetables

4. 21:30 Dessert + Tea: Traditional Korean sweets — sikhye (식혜), yakgwa (약과), and herbal tea

5. 22:00 Nearby Cheongdam-dong (청담동) Bar (optional): Wind down at a cocktail or whisky bar

Tips for Foreign Visitors

  • Useful Korean Phrases
  • "Yeyak haessseumnida, [ireum]imnida (예약했습니다, [이름]입니다)" — I have a reservation under [name]
  • "Chaesikida (채식입니다)" — I am vegetarian (notify the restaurant in advance)
  • "Allereugi ga issseumnida (알레르기가 있습니다)" — I have an allergy (recommended to communicate in writing at the time of booking)
  • Dress Code & Etiquette: Smart casual attire recommended. Keep photography discreet; flash photography is not allowed.
  • Payment: Credit card only. All major international cards accepted.
  • How to Book: Via the restaurant's official website/email, or through Catch Table or Naver (네이버) reservation apps.

Simple English Summary

Gangnam (강남) is home to several Michelin-starred Korean restaurants offering multi-course menus that blend traditional cooking with modern presentation. Book at least 2–4 weeks in advance as these spots fill up quickly. Most restaurants provide English menus and staff are experienced with international guests. Dress code is smart casual; flash photography is generally discouraged. Vegetarian and allergy accommodations are available if requested in advance.
