Gyeongbokgung Palace Guide — A Complete Guide for International Visitors

Subtitle: Changing of the Guard, free admission in hanbok, the 5 highlights, free English guided tours — a complete "Gyeongbokgung Palace" guide for international visitors

ISSUE 02 · #37 · 2026-05-15 (SERP restructure 2026-06-27)

🎯 Why Gyeongbokgung?

Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) is the largest palace of the Joseon Dynasty (built in 1395) — the #1 Korean history experience for international visitors. It sits at the northern end of central Seoul, behind Gwanghwamun Square (광화문 광장).

What is Gyeongbokgung known for — ① the Changing of the Guard (수문장 교대식) (twice daily, free) ② free admission in hanbok (한복 무료입장) (wearing hanbok waives the entrance fee) ③ the 5 highlights such as Geunjeongjeon (근정전) and Gyeonghoeru (경회루) ④ free English guided tours ⑤ a short walk to Bukchon (북촌), Samcheong-dong (삼청동) and the Blue House (청와대).

🚇 Getting There & Routes

  • Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) Station (Line 3) Exit 5 → National Palace Museum → Heungnyemun Gate (closest entrance to the main gate)
  • Gwanghwamun (광화문) Station (Line 5) Exit 2 → Gwanghwamun Square → Gwanghwamun Gate (good for the Changing of the Guard)
  • Recommended route: Gwanghwamun (guard ceremony) → Geunjeongjeon → Gyeonghoeru → Hyangwonjeong → National Folk Museum → Bukchon · Samcheong-dong (5 min walk)

💂 Changing of the Guard (수문장 교대식) — Don't Miss It

Gyeongbokgung's top free spectacle. Twice a day (10:00 · 14:00) in front of Gwanghwamun Gate, the royal guard shift of the Joseon era is re-enacted — traditional music band, colourful uniforms, and a march. About 15–20 minutes, free.

  • Times: 10:00 · 14:00 (~20 min each) — closed Tuesdays, may be cancelled in rain
  • Best spot: arrive 10–15 minutes early to grab a spot directly in front of Gwanghwamun. Side angles also make great photos
  • Tip: the 14:00 session is less crowded than the morning. The route flows straight into the palace entrance afterward.

👘 Free Admission in Hanbok (한복 무료입장)

Wear hanbok (traditional Korean dress) and your Gyeongbokgung admission is free. Photos in hanbok inside the palace are the #1 experience for international visitors.

  • Free-entry rule: a proper hanbok with both an upper and lower garment (official guideline)
  • Rental: many hanbok rental shops near Gyeongbokgung (5 min walk), ₩15,000–35,000 per outfit, in 2–4 hour blocks. See the #33 hanbok guide
  • Tip: hanbok + spring cherry blossoms or autumn foliage = the best photos. Book the rental shop early in the morning.

🏯 The 5 Highlights

# Highlight Signature
1 Gwanghwamun (광화문) (main gate) Changing of the Guard · the face of Gyeongbokgung
2 Geunjeongjeon (근정전) (throne hall) Joseon coronation hall · golden throne
3 Gyeonghoeru (경회루) pavilion over a pond · the #1 photo spot
4 Hyangwonjeong (향원정) small pavilion · Insta hot shot
5 National Folk Museum & National Palace Museum (국립민속·국립고궁박물관) free admission · deep dive into Joseon artifacts

A 5-minute walk away: Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌 한옥마을), Samcheong-dong (삼청동), and the Blue House (청와대) (now open to the public).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes International Visitors Make

1. Ignoring the Tuesday closure — Gyeongbokgung is closed every Tuesday. Always check before you go.

2. Not using free hanbok admission — wearing hanbok waives the fee; the photos + free entry are worth more than the rental.

3. Missing the guard ceremony times — 10:00 · 14:00 sharp (closed Tuesdays). Arrive 15 minutes early.

⚙ FAQ

Q1. Admission & reservations?

Admission ₩3,000, free in hanbok. Free English guided tours (11:00 · 13:30 · 15:30, 3x daily). No reservation needed — walk-in OK.

Q2. Free English guided tour?

Free English guide: 11:00 / 13:30 / 15:30 (closed Tuesdays). About 1 hour. Meet at the Gwanghwamun info booth.

Q3. Combining with hanbok?

Hanbok rental shops near Gyeongbokgung (5 min walk). See the #33 hanbok guide.

Q4. Seasonal picks?

Spring (April cherry blossoms): Gyeongbokgung blossoms + hanbok, top pick. Autumn (November foliage): Gyeonghoeru foliage + hanbok. Winter (Dec–Feb): quiet + hanbok + warm layers.

Q5. A 1-day combined course?

10:00 Gyeongbokgung → 14:00 Changing of the Guard → 15:00 Bukchon Hanok Village → 17:00 Samcheong-dong café → 19:00 Korean dinner.

Q6. Is Gyeongbokgung worth visiting?

The largest and most representative of Korea's five grand palaces. The Changing of the Guard + hanbok experience is a Seoul must-do. Half a day is plenty.

Q7. How long do you need?

The highlights (guard ceremony + 5 highlights) take 2–3 hours. Add the museums, Bukchon and Samcheong-dong for a half to full day.

Q8. Night opening?

There are special night-opening periods in spring and autumn (reservation required, sells out fast). Check the Korea Heritage Service announcements for dates.
