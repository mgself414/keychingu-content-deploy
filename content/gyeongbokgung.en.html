37_Gyeongbokgung 3-Hour Complete Route

One-Line Intro

Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) in 3 hours: the essential Joseon palace route, nothing missed.

See every key highlight of Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) without wasting energy — a fully packed visit in 3 hours.

Who This Is For

  • Travelers on a tight schedule, or those with only one day in Seoul
  • First-time visitors to Gyeongbokgung (경복궁)

Key Spots & Activities

Recommended Route (3 Hours)

1. 09:30 Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역) Exit 5 → Photo in front of Gwanghwamun (광화문) (10 min)

2. 09:40 Enter: Pay ₩3,000 by T-money or card (free if wearing hanbok (한복))

3. 09:45 Heungnyemun (흥례문) → Yeongjegyo (영제교) (5 min): View Geunjeongjeon (근정전) from the stone bridge

4. 09:50 Geunjeongjeon (근정전) (20 min): Look closely at the throne, phoenix motifs, and wolde (월대) platform; take front and side photos

5. 10:10 Sajeongjeon (사정전) (10 min): The king's working space; browse the interior displays

6. 10:20 Gyeonghoeru (경회루) (20 min): Stroll around the pond, take photos from the bridge, enjoy seasonal flowers

7. 10:40 Hyangwonjeong (향원정) (15 min): Cross Chwihyanggyo (취향교) bridge (restoration complete); photo with the pavilion as backdrop

8. 11:00 National Folk Museum of Korea (국립민속박물관) (30 min): Exhibitions on Korean everyday history; free admission

9. 11:30 Jibokjae (집옥재) (10 min): Chinese-style brick building; relatively quiet photo spot

10. 11:40 Exit + Head to Samcheongdong (삼청동): Via the north gate Sinmumun (신무문) or east gate Geonchunmun (건춘문)

Tips for Foreign Visitors

  • Useful Korean Phrases
  • "입장권 주세요" — One ticket, please
  • "영어 오디오 가이드 있어요?" — Is there an English audio guide?
  • "화장실 어디예요?" — Where is the restroom?
  • Cultural Notes: Respect signs marking no-entry zones inside buildings. Do not touch displayed cultural artifacts.
  • Payment: Admission tickets available by cash or card. Free entry when wearing hanbok (한복).
  • Free English Tour: Daily at 11:00 AM (approx. 1 hour). Meet just inside Gwanghwamun (광화문).

Simple English Summary

Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) is the main palace of the Joseon (조선) Dynasty (built 1395), located in central Seoul. Admission is ₩3,000 — free if wearing hanbok (한복). The must-see highlights are Geunjeongjeon (근정전) (throne hall, best photo), Gyeonghoeru (경회루) (pavilion over a pond), and the National Folk Museum of Korea (국립민속박물관) (free, inside the palace grounds). A free English-language guided tour departs daily at 11am from just inside the main gate. The palace is closed on Tuesdays. Plan about 3 hours for a thorough visit.

Estimated Total Cost

  • Budget: ₩3,000 (admission only, self-guided on foot)
  • Standard: ₩15,000 (admission + hanbok (한복) rental + audio guide)
  • Luxury: ₩50,000 (premium hanbok (한복) rental + Gyeonghoeru (경회루) special tour + brunch in Samcheongdong (삼청동))
