⚠ 3 Wellness Mistakes

1. "K-spa = Tourist sauna only" — Luxury K-spa·Hotel SPA not recognized: Sauna = Only cost-effective route is recognized. In reality, it is a combination of Dragon Hill Spa VIP + Shilla Hotel SPA + Signiel SPA luxury cluster + oriental medicine.

2. "Temple stay = only rural areas" — Urban temples not recognized: Only rural temples are recognized. In reality, Jingwansa Temple (Seoul 1018), Bongeunsa Temple (Gangnam 794), and Jogyesa Temple (Jongno) Full temple stay experience in English for foreigners in the city.

3. "Oriental medicine = Korean only" — English oriental medicine clinic is not recognized: Oriental medicine only recognizes Korean language services. In reality, Itaewon, Gangnam, and Myeongdong Oriental Medicine Clinics provide English service + acupuncture, moxibustion, and herbal medicine foreign-friendly cluster.
