⚠ 3 Common Theme Park Mistakes

1. "Theme park = just one" — missing the Lotte World vs Everland distinction: Visiting only one. In reality, Lotte World (urban Jamsil · indoor + outdoor · 95% English signage for foreigners) vs Everland (suburban Yongin · T Express for thrill fans · pandas) — each offers different value. A 2-night full package covering both is recommended.

2. "Summer = Han River only" — not using Caribbean Bay and Ocean World: Han River only. In reality, Caribbean Bay (next to Everland, Jul–Aug) + Ocean World (Hongcheon, Gangwon) are Korea's #1 water parks. Big with foreign fans.

3. "Theme parks = season doesn't matter" — missing Halloween and Christmas: Assuming seasons don't matter. In reality, Lotte World Halloween (October) and Everland Christmas (December) are #1 with foreign fans + peak festival season for foreign visitors.
