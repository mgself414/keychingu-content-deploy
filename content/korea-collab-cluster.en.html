How to Find Korean Collab Products — Permanent Stores (KWANGYA@SEOUL, K-Pop Square Hongdae, Ktown4u COEX, Olive Young Myeongdong) and How to Read Pop-Ups and Official Channels (Official Values as of October 2026)

Summary: KWANGYA@SEOUL daily 10:30–20:00, free admission (VisitSeoul) · K-Pop Square Hongdae daily 11:00–22:00 (VisitSeoul) · Ktown4u COEX 4th floor is a comeback pop-up space (VisitSeoul) · Olive Young Myeongdong daily 10:00–22:30 (VisitSeoul) · Limited-time products change within weeks, so this only covers "where to look" — as of 2026-10-21

ISSUE 03 · #906 · 2026-05-27

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Step out of Exit 4 of Seongsu Station (성수역), and there is a long line in front of a pink building wrapped in idol faces — that is the Seongsu pop-up scene as VisitSeoul describes it (VisitSeoul Seongsu trend article). Collab products where idols, dramas, or characters team up with brands come and go within weeks. So instead of a list of products on sale right now, this guide covers the stores that keep collabs on permanent display, the spaces where pop-ups rotate, and the official channels that announce new releases first.

If you are curious about which brand teamed up with which idol, see How Korean Brand Collabs Come Together — Convenience Store × Idols and Music, Beauty × K-pop and Characters: 4 Types, Plus Past Cases from Official Press Releases. This article is about finding and buying those products in Seoul.

🎤 K-pop Goods and Collabs — 3 Permanent Stores

  • KWANGYA@SEOUL (광야@서울) — B1, 83-21 Wangsimni-ro, Seongdong-gu, Seoul. 73m from Exit 5 of Seoulforest Station (서울숲역) on the Suin-Bundang Line. Daily 10:30–20:00, closed on Seollal (Lunar New Year) and Chuseok (Korean Thanksgiving) (Instagram notice). Free admission. Phone +82-2-6233-6729 (VisitSeoul). VisitSeoul introduces it as "a cultural experience space where K-POP fans meet artists," with a media zone, an AR guestbook, and four-cut photo frames. The website language is Korean (VisitSeoul)
  • K-Pop Square Hongdae (케이팝 스퀘어 홍대) — 141 Yanghwa-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul. 145m from Exit 1 of Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역) on Line 2, the Airport Railroad, and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line. Daily 11:00–22:00. Phone +82-2-322-9631 (VisitSeoul). It reopened in the former LINE FRIENDS Hongdae flagship space and offers artist pop-ups, exhibitions, and limited goods (VisitSeoul)
  • Ktown4u COEX (케이타운포유 코엑스) — 2nd–4th floors of COEX, 513 Yeongdong-daero, Gangnam-gu, Seoul. 162m from Exit 6 of Samseong Station (삼성역) on Line 2. Free admission (VisitSeoul)
  • 2nd floor SQUARE: a permanent store selling the latest K-pop albums, goods, and magazines plus beauty and fashion items, 11:00–20:00
  • 3rd floor Academy: one-day K-pop dance and vocal classes, 12:00–22:00. Only students and consultation customers may enter
  • 4th floor SPACE: a space for themed pop-ups such as artist comeback events, 11:00–20:00
  • Foreign-language guidance is available, and the website comes in Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Russian, and Malay (VisitSeoul)

💄 Beauty Collabs — Two Olive Young Locations

  • Olive Young Myeongdong Flagship (올리브영 명동 플래그십) — 53 Myeongdong-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul. 356m from Exit 8 of Myeongdong Station (명동역) on Line 4. Daily 10:00–22:30. Phone 1577-4887 (VisitSeoul). VisitSeoul introduces it as the largest beauty store in Korea and says the store is divided into 14 themed zones. The perfume bar, beauty care, and body care zones are popular (VisitSeoul)
  • Olive Young N Seongsu (올리브영N 성수) — A store of about 1,400 pyeong (~4,600 m²) that opened in late November 2024 to mark Olive Young's 25th anniversary. In the 1st-floor "N. Playground," brand pop-ups change regularly, and the café stand offers a new collab menu every month (VisitSeoul Seongsu trend article, written 2025-09-30, updated 2026-06-15). The address and hours could not be confirmed because VisitSeoul has no separate page for it
  • For shopping at Olive Young stores themselves, see 47 · Olive Young Real Buying Guide

📣 Where Pop-Ups and New Releases Show Up First

  • The space's official channels: KWANGYA@SEOUL announces closing days on Instagram (VisitSeoul). For places where pop-ups rotate, such as K-Pop Square Hongdae and Ktown4u COEX's 4th floor, check each store's official channel for the next schedule
  • Agency official online shop: Weverse Shop describes itself as "the official goods store for fans worldwide," and sells official fan club memberships and Weverse Shop-exclusive products. Some products are sold by third-party sellers, and WEVERSE COMPANY only acts as an intermediary (Weverse Shop official). We could not confirm supported languages or shipping regions on the main page
  • Convenience stores: There is guidance that CU's app "Pocket CU" has a product stock lookup menu ([secondary] blog). We could not confirm an official BGF Retail notice. For the differences among the four convenience store chains, see Korea's Big 4 Convenience Stores Compared: CU vs GS25 vs 7-Eleven vs emart24
  • Seongsu pop-up street: VisitSeoul writes that "new pop-ups open every day" in Seongsu-dong (성수동), and introduces Yeonmujang-gil (연무장길), where pop-up stores and brand collab galleries continue one after another (VisitSeoul Seongsu trend article). For a Seongsu pop-up route, see Seongsu Pop-Up Deep Dive

🧰 Collab Shopping Practicalities — Solo, Booking, Payment, Getting Around

  • Solo: All four stores are shops you can browse by yourself. KWANGYA@SEOUL and Ktown4u COEX are free to enter (VisitSeoul)
  • Reservations: The permanent stores need no reservation. The 3rd-floor classes at Ktown4u COEX admit only students and consultation customers (VisitSeoul). Popular pop-ups often require advance reservations or on-site waiting, so check that pop-up's notice
  • Payment: For cards and mobile payment in general, see Foreigner Korea Payment System Deep Dive; for unmanned checkouts, see Self-Checkout and Night Unmanned Stores in Korea: The No-Panic Manual — From Door Tap to Payment
  • Getting around: Seoulforest Station (서울숲역; KWANGYA@SEOUL) · Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역; K-Pop Square) · Samseong Station (삼성역; Ktown4u) · Myeongdong Station (명동역; Olive Young) make a one-day subway route. For a transit card, see Choosing Your Korea Transit Card: The No-Panic Manual — T-money, WOWPASS, or Climate Card; for the order in which to connect K-pop landmarks, see Seoul K-pop Pilgrimage by Subway: Six Fan Spots on a Single T-money
  • When words fail: Manners When Words Fail — Ordering Etiquette in the Age of Translation Apps and Kiosks

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Agency spaces have expensive admission" — KWANGYA@SEOUL and Ktown4u COEX are free to enter (VisitSeoul)

2. "The LINE FRIENDS Hongdae store" — That space became K-Pop Square Hongdae (VisitSeoul)

3. "Olive Young Myeongdong doesn't stay open late" — The Myeongdong flagship is open until 22:30 every day (VisitSeoul)

4. "Going to find a collab product I saw on a blog" — Collab products are limited in time, so by the time a post appears they have often ended. Check what is on now at the permanent stores and official channels
