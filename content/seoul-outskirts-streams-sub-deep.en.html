Jungnangcheon and Anyangcheon Guide: Jungnang Rose Park, Anyangcheon Embankment Cherry Blossom Path, Four Seasons Theme Garden, Family Garden and Water Play Area, Seoul Trail Courses 13 and 14

Subtitle: Jungnang Rose Park (always open, rose festival in May) · Anyangcheon Embankment Cherry Blossom Path (always open) · Anyangcheon Four Seasons Theme Garden (always open) · Anyangcheon Family Garden (always open) · Anyangcheon Water Play Area (10:00~18:00, closed Mondays) · Seoul Trail Course 13 (8km) and Course 14 (10.2km) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to visit along Jungnangcheon (중랑천) in northeast Seoul and Anyangcheon (안양천) in the southwest. They are one rose park in Jungnang-gu (중랑구), four places on the Anyangcheon stretch through Yeongdeungpo (영등포), Yangcheon (양천) and Guro (구로), namely a cherry blossom path, gardens and a water play area, and two Seoul Trail courses that walk along Anyangcheon. Apart from the water play area, all are always open.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Jungnangcheon — Jungnang Rose Park

Jungnang Rose Park (중랑장미공원) [Muk-dong (묵동), Jungnang-gu, ☎ Jungnang-gu Office (중랑구청) Parks and Green Space Division (공원녹지과) 02-2094-2372] is a park along the Jungnangcheon path where many rose varieties are planted section by section. A rose festival is held in May, when the roses bloom. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, more than 2 million people visit the festival every year, and the 5.4km rose tunnel along the Jungnangcheon embankment is the largest in Korea. There are varieties such as Angela, Pink Perfume and Gold Pasade, along with an observation deck overlooking Jungnangcheon, photo zones and night lighting.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Not available.

📍 Anyangcheon — Embankment Cherry Blossom Path, Four Seasons Theme Garden, Family Garden and Water Play Area

Anyangcheon Embankment Cherry Blossom Path (안양천제방벚꽃길) [Yangpyeong-dong 1-ga (양평동1가), Yeongdeungpo-gu, ☎ Yeongdeungpo-gu Office (영등포구청) Garden City Division (정원도시과) 02-2670-3772] is a cherry blossom path that runs along the Anyangcheon embankment from Yeongdeungpo-gu to Gwangmyeong-si (광명시). According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is the longest cherry blossom path in Seoul. Around the embankment and the stream there are flower beds, an ecological park, sports facilities, resting places, the Four Seasons Theme Garden (rose garden, grass garden and more) and photo zones.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Anyangcheon Four Seasons Theme Garden (안양천 사계절 테마원) [Sinjeong-dong (신정동), Yangcheon-gu, ☎ Yangcheon-gu Office (양천구청) Parks and Green Space Division (공원녹지과) 02-2620-3592] is a garden inside Anyangcheon Ecological Park (안양천생태공원), which stretches along Anyangcheon. You can see flowers each season in the rose garden, grass garden and herb garden, and there is no admission fee. In summer a children's water play area opens next to the rose garden. It is 750m from Exit 2 of Dorimcheon Station (도림천역) on Subway Line 2.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: Available. Stroller rental: None.

Anyangcheon Family Garden (안양천가족정원) [Anyangcheon-ro 812 (안양천로 812), Yangcheon-gu, ☎ Yangcheon-gu Office Parks and Green Space Division 02-2620-3592] is a waterside park under Omokgyo Bridge (오목교) that turned an abandoned riverside lot into a park through the Anyangcheon lower reach improvement project. A 264m brook runs through it, and there are a seasonal flower garden, a lawn yard, a grass garden and a rose garden.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Anyangcheon Water Play Area (안양천 물놀이장) [285-34 Sindorim-dong (신도림동), Guro-gu, ☎ Guro-gu Office (구로구청) Sports Promotion Division (체육진흥과) 02-860-2547] is a water play area that opens in summer. It is designed with a safe water depth, and lifeguards are always on duty. Around it there are shade tents, benches, a bike path and a walking path.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00. It operates for 50 minutes of each hour and rests for 10 minutes. 12:00~13:00 is a break and cleaning time.
  • Closed days: Every Monday, rainy days.

📍 Walking Along Anyangcheon — Seoul Trail Courses 13 and 14

The Seoul Trail (서울둘레길) is a walking path that circles Seoul in 21 courses totaling 156.5km. Of these, Courses 13 and 14 follow Anyangcheon. Both courses can also be walked in the evening (information ☎ 02-779-7902~4).

Course 13 Anyangcheon Upper Course (13코스 안양천 상류코스) [Gyeongsu-daero 1431 (경수대로 1431), Manan-gu (만안구), Anyang-si] is themed 'a cherry blossom path along the railway'. Cherry blossoms bloom in spring. The length is somewhat long, but there are subway stations along the way.

  • Length and time: 8km in total, about 2 hours 5 minutes. Opening hours: Always open.

Course 14 Anyangcheon Lower Course (14코스 안양천 하류코스) [Guro-dong (구로동), Guro-gu] is themed 'Anyangcheon memory lane', and unlike the other courses that are mostly forest paths, it follows the stream and the Hangang (한강). There are exercise facilities, walking paths, amenities and resting places along the way.

  • Length and time: 10.2km in total, about 2 hours 25 minutes. Opening hours: Always open.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Jungnang Rose Park has no parking — the rose festival is held in May, when the roses bloom.
  2. The Anyangcheon Water Play Area is closed on Mondays and rainy days — 12:00~13:00 is a break and cleaning time.
  3. Seoul Trail Course 13 is registered at an address in Anyang-si — its address is Gyeongsu-daero 1431, Manan-gu, Anyang-si.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where is the longest cherry blossom path in Seoul?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is the Anyangcheon Embankment Cherry Blossom Path. It runs from Yeongdeungpo-gu to Gwangmyeong-si and is always open.

Q. When should I go to see roses?

A. Jungnang Rose Park holds its rose festival in May, when the roses bloom. The park is always open.
