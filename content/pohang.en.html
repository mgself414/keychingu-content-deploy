⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Pohang = Industry Only": Pohang (포항) = POSCO + Jukdo Market (죽도시장) + Homigot (호미곶) Sunrise + Naeyeonsan Mountain (내연산) + Foreign Enthusiast.

2. "Homigot (호미곶) = Where is it?": Homigot (호미곶) = Korea's No. 1 sunrise spot (easternmost point) + Hands of Mutual Prosperity (상생의 손) for Instagram + Foreign Enthusiast.

3. "Pohang·Gyeongju = Same City": Pohang (포항)·Gyeongju (경주) = KTX 30 minutes + Foreigner 1 Night 2 Days Full Package (Pohang + #256 Gyeongju).

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Foreigner's Pohang Itinerary: KTX Seoul→Pohang 2 hours 30 minutes.

2. Foreigner's Pohang Priority: Homigot (호미곶), Jukdo Market (죽도시장), Yeongildae (영일대), POSCO.

3. Foreigner's Pohang Food: Jukdo Market (죽도시장) Hoe (회) (sliced raw fish), Gwamegi (과메기) (semi-dried herring or mackerel pike), Yeongdeok Daege (영덕 대게) (snow crabs).

4. Foreigner's Pohang 1 Night 2 Days: Day 1 Homigot (호미곶), Jukdo Market (죽도시장), Yeongildae (영일대) / Day 2 Integrated with Gyeongju (#256).
