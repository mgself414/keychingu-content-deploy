30 — Garosu-gil (가로수길) Shopping Full-Day Course

One-Line Intro

Garosu-gil (가로수길): Seoul's most polished shopping street — tree-lined, brand-heavy, and Instagrammable.

Seoul's most sophisticated shopping strip, where flagship stores, cafés, and galleries line a ginkgo-tree boulevard.

Who Should Go

  • Travelers who enjoy luxury and contemporary brand shopping
  • Travelers who want to mix café stops and meals with a stylish street atmosphere

Recommended Itinerary (6-Hour Full Day)

1. 10:30 — Sinsa Station (신사역), Line 3, Exit 8 (5 min walk): Enter the northern entrance of Garosu-gil (가로수길)

2. 11:00 — Café Brunch (₩12,000–18,000): Eggs Benedict and coffee at a Garosu-gil (가로수길) café

3. 12:00 — Flagship Store Tour (1–2 hrs): Shop in order — Kirsh (키르시) → Wooyoungmi (우영미) → Acne Studios (아크네 스튜디오)

4. 14:00 — Explore Serosu-gil (세로수길) (1 hr): Indie brands and vintage shops in the side alleys

5. 15:00 — Café Break (₩6,000–10,000): Specialty coffee + dessert

6. 16:00 — Le Creuset (르크루제) Flagship: Browse or buy Korea-exclusive cookware colors

7. 17:00 — Dinner Prep: Reserve a nearby Garosu-gil (가로수길) restaurant, or head over to Cheongdam-dong (청담동)

Tips for Foreign Visitors

  • Useful Korean Phrases
  • "Segeum hwanggeup ganeungheyo?" (세금 환급 가능해요?) — "Is a tax refund available?" Look for the Tax Refund sign at the store entrance.
  • "Dareun saekgal/saijeu isseoyo?" (다른 색깔/사이즈 있어요?) — "Do you have other colors/sizes?"
  • "Pojanghae juseyo" (포장해 주세요) — "Gift wrap, please."
  • Cultural Note: Garosu-gil (가로수길) has a polished, upscale vibe. It's considered good manners to greet staff before browsing.
  • Payment: Cards accepted everywhere — foreign cards are all fine. Bring your passport when shopping for a tax refund.
  • Tax Refund (TAX REFUND): Spend ₩30,000 or more at a single store and you can reclaim 10% VAT. Receipt + passport required.

Simple English Summary

Garosu-gil (가로수길) is Seoul's premier lifestyle shopping street, lined with international flagship stores and Korean designer boutiques beneath ginkgo trees. Top stops include Kirsh (키르시) — Korean streetwear with an iconic cherry logo — Wooyoungmi (우영미) for upscale Korean menswear, and Acne Studios (아크네 스튜디오) for Swedish luxury. The side alleys of Serosu-gil (세로수길) hide indie brands and vintage shops at lower price points. Tax refund (VAT 10%) is available at most stores for purchases over ₩30,000 — bring your passport.

Estimated Total Budget

  • Budget: ₩30,000 (café brunch + browsing only, no shopping)
  • Standard: ₩150,000 (2 café stops + 1 Kirsh (키르시) item)
  • Luxury: ₩500,000+ (luxury brand shopping + dinner in Cheongdam-dong (청담동))
