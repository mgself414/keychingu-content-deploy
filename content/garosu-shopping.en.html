Garosu-gil Shopping One-Day Course — A Full Guide for Foreign Visitors

Subtitle: A one-day shopping course combining Sinsa's Garosu-gil main street + Serosu-gil side alleys + Dosan Park — designer boutiques, flagship stores, and cafés

ISSUE 02 · #30 · 2026-05-15 (v2 full update)

🎯 Why a Garosu-gil-Only Guide

Garosu-gil (가로수길) is Korea's No. 1 trendy shopping street near Sinsa-dong (신사동) and Apgujeong (압구정) — designer boutiques, luxury flagship stores, K-beauty shops, and cafés cluster across the Garosu-gil and Serosu-gil area. It's the top pick for foreign visitors chasing a trendy shopping route (a different vibe from Seongsu).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Confusing Garosu-gil with Serosu-gil — Garosu-gil (the main street) and Serosu-gil (the side alleys) have different vibes. Walk both.

2. Mixing up Sinsa Station (신사역) and Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역) — Garosu-gil is at Exit 1 of Sinsa Station. Apgujeong Rodeo Station is near Dosan Park (도산공원).

3. Ignoring the weekday 14:00–17:00 golden hour — weekends mean long lines and crowded Instagram hotspots. Go on a quiet weekday afternoon instead.

⚙ FAQ

Q1. Is English service / card payment available?

A. Flagships and luxury stores are 100% English-friendly. Even small shops usually have English menus and take cards. Foreign cards, Samsung Pay, and Kakao Pay all work.

Q2. What does the one-day course look like?

A. 11:00 Sinsa Station Exit 1 → 11:30 Garosu-gil K-beauty flagship → 13:00 café lunch → 14:00 designer boutiques → 16:00 Serosu-gil alleys → 17:00 Dosan Park café → 18:00 Apgujeong dinner (#143).

Q3. Recommendations by budget?

A. Budget ₩6K–30K: cafés, K-beauty minis. Standard ₩30K–80K: full-line K-beauty, some designer pieces. Luxury ₩100K+: flagship and luxury brands.

Q4. Best spot for K-beauty shopping?

A. The Garosu-gil K-beauty flagships — the Amorepacific store and the Olive Young (올리브영) Garosu-gil branch (#47). Get the full lineup in one stop at the flagship.

Q5. Can I combine it with Dosan Park and Apgujeong?

A. Garosu-gil → 8-min walk to Dosan Park → 10-min walk to Apgujeong Rodeo. Highly recommended as a one-day route.
