Gyeongju KTX 1 night 2 days full guide for foreigners

Silla Millennium Ancient City and 5 UNESCO World Heritage Sites — KTX Singyeongju 2 hours → Bulguksa Temple, Seokguram Grotto, Royal Tombs, Hwangnidan-gil, 1 night and 2 days

Gyeongju is a thousand years old during the Silla Dynasty (57 BC ~ 935 AD). 5 UNESCO World Heritage Sites (Bulguksa Temple, Seokguram Grotto, Gyeongju Historic Sites, Yangdong Village, and Sancheong Records). A 2-day, 1-night destination that best suits history, tradition, and UNESCO personas in Korea. This guide is a standard route suitable for foreigners + 10 stores + bicycle route.

1. How to get to Gyeongju

Options Take price Recommended
KTX Singyeongju (Seoul Station) 2h 5m ₩49,500 ★★★ Foreigner default
SRT Singyeongju (Suseo) 2h 5m ₩44,000 Suseo departure fit
Intercity Bus (Gangnam) 4h ₩28,000 low budget

Reservation: Korail English (https://www.letskorail.com/) — 1 week before the weekend

Note: Singyeongju Station ≠ Gyeongju Station. Singyeongju Station is for KTX only, 20 minutes from downtown.

2. Standard 2 days and 1 night itinerary

Day 1 (Arrival + Bulguksa Temple·Seokguram Grotto)

  • 07:00 Seoul Station KTX
  • 09:05 Arrival at Singyeongju Station
  • 09:30 Hotel luggage storage (Bomun Tourist Complex or downtown)
  • 10:30 Bulguksa Temple (UNESCO, peak of Silla architecture, English audio)
  • 13:00 Lunch near Bulguksa Temple (Korean meal)
  • 14:30 Seokguram Grotto (UNESCO, Silla Sculpture Peak, English audio)
  • 17:00 Walk around Bomun Lake (Bomun Tourist Complex)
  • 19:00 Hwangridan-gil dinner (trendy Korean food/cafe cluster)
  • 21:00 Night view of Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond ★ (Foreigner SNS signature)

Day 2 (Daereungwon + Hwangnam Bread + Return)

  • 09:00 Hotel breakfast
  • 10:00 Daereungwon + Cheonmachong (Royal Tombs cluster, bicycle walk)
  • 12:00 Lunch at Hwangridan-gil (Hwangnam bread, traditional cafe)
  • 13:30 Cheomseongdae + Gyerim (bicycle route)
  • 15:00 Gyeongju National Museum (English audio)
  • 17:00 Singyeongju Station
  • 17:30 KTX bound for Seoul
  • 19:35 Arrival at Seoul Station

4. Foreign fit tips

English language availability

  • Top: Bulguksa Temple, Seokguram Grotto, Gyeongju National Museum (English audio, foreign visitors)
  • Middle class: Daereungwon, Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (English sign), Hwangnam Bread
  • Local: Hwangridan-gil store (picture menu/translation app)

Budget (2 days and 1 night per person)

  • KTX round trip: ₩99,000
  • Accommodation per night (hotel): ₩80,000~250,000 (Bomun vs. downtown)
  • 4 meals: ₩60,000~120,000
  • Entrance fee (Bulguksa Temple, Seokguram Grotto, Museum, etc.): ₩30,000~50,000
  • Bicycle rental: ₩5,000~10,000
  • City transportation (taxi, bus): ₩30,000
  • Total average ₩300,000~570,000

By persona

  • European history buff: Bulguksa Temple + Seokguram Grotto + Gyeongju National Museum (UNESCO trio, 1 day)
  • architecture lover: Bulguksa Temple + Seokguram Grotto + Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond night view
  • foodie: Hwangridan-gil + Hwangnam bread + Korean gourmet hop
  • Photo fit: Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (night view) + Cheomseongdae (sunrise) + Hwangnam-dong Hanok
  • K-drama fan: Donggung Palace, Wolji Pond, and Hwangridan-gil (multiple dramas)
  • Family fit: Bicycle route (Daereungwon → Cheomseongdae → Hwangridan-gil)

By season

  • Spring (April-May): Bomun Lake Cherry Blossoms + Daereungwon Bicycle (peak season)
  • Summer (June-August): Bomun Lake + Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond night view
  • Fall (October-November): Autumn leaves at Daereungwon and Bulguksa Temple ★
  • Winter (December-February): Museum, indoor, hot spring (optional)

Accommodation Recommendations

  • Luxury: Hilton Gyeongju (Bomun Tourist Complex), Kolon Hotel
  • Intermediate: Lahan Select Gyeongju·Hwangridan-gil Boutique
  • Low budget/Hanok stay: Hwangridan-gil Hanok guesthouse (₩60,000~120,000/night)

Bicycle rental (★ unique experience for foreigners)

  • 5-10 bicycle rental places on Hwangridan-gil
  • ₩5,000~10,000 per day
  • Daereungwon → Cheomseongdae → Gyerim → Hwangridan-gil 30 minutes
