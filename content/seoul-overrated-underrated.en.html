Overrated vs Underrated Seoul — What Foreign Reviews Say About Rebalancing Your Time

Subtitle: Not "don't go" — "re-split your time." 6 recurring overrated spots from Western traveler reviews and videos (with conditions that redeem each one) + 8 underrated spots + 5 conditional spots that come alive once you shift the time slot. Every entry comes with an alternative that meets the same need.

ISSUE 04 · #1310 · 2026-08-20

🎯 The Criteria — Why This Guide Isn't Just "Personal Taste"

This isn't one person's opinion — it's a pattern that repeats across foreign reviews and travel videos. Three criteria:

1. Experience density vs. crowding — Do you get back what you waited for?

2. Sentiment in foreign-language reviews — Do English reviews keep repeating "crowded / touristy / not worth it," or "hidden / authentic / wish I knew earlier"?

3. A real alternative exists — Is there a place within walking or subway distance that meets the same need better?

⚠️ An "overrated" call does not mean it's a bad place. It means it's a spot you can deprioritize on a first-time 3–4 day itinerary. If you have plenty of time, go to all of them.

② Underrated — 8 Spots Where Foreign Reviews Ask "Why Didn't I Know About This?"

1. Ikseon-dong (익선동) — Go here instead of Myeongdong. Modern sensibility woven into hanok (한옥, traditional Korean house) alleys — small in size but dense in experience. A 3-minute walk from Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역).

2. Inwangsan (인왕산) Fortress Wall Trail — A 40-minute in-city hike rewards you with a view looking down over the Gyeongbokgung (경복궁)–Gwanghwamun (광화문) axis. Sunset hour is the peak (#1308). For hiking basics, see #148.

3. Seoul Jungang Market (서울중앙시장) — The alternative to Gwangjang Market. A real market with more merchants and residents than tourists, plus nopo (노포, restaurants that have been open 30+ years) food. 1 minute from Sindang Station (신당역) — for details, see the Sindang-dong guide.

4. Cheonggyecheon (청계천), Euljiro Section — Skip the touristy Gwanghwamun stretch for the everyday-life section between Sewoon-gyo (세운교) and Supyogyo (수표교). Locals walking here after work is the real scene (course spot #5 in #1309).

5. Haneul Park (하늘공원) — Seoul's number-one October combination of silver grass and sunset, yet foreign visitor density is still low (#1308).

6. Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길) — A park built on a decommissioned rail line between Hongdae (홍대) and Yeonnam (연남). Called "Seoul's High Line" — cafe-lined alleys and a walking path in one stretch.

7. Leeum Museum (리움미술관) — Reviews repeatedly call it the answer to "if you visit only one museum in Seoul," thanks to the building plus its permanent collection. In Hannam-dong (한남동).

8. Ihwa-dong Mural Village (이화동) — Pair it with the Naksan Park (낙산공원) fortress wall trail (#1308, spot 10) for a complete half-day hillside course.

③ Conditional — 5 Spots That Come Alive With the Right Time Slot or Reservation

Spot Failure Condition Success Condition
Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) (#37) Crowded from 10:00 AM through the afternoon Early morning, right after opening — includes the royal guard-changing ceremony
Changdeokgung (창덕궁) Secret Garden (후원) Trying to walk in same-day Advance reservation is mandatory — sells out early, guided-tour only
Bukchon (북촌) Hanok Village (#38) Midday group crowds Early morning or evening + stay quiet — it's a residential area
DDP Expecting the daytime indoor exhibits The architecture at night + the pre-dawn wholesale fashion malls (from 8 PM)
Jamsil (잠실) Baseball Stadium Trying to book online with a foreign card Buy tickets on-site same day, or use a booking platform — the cheering culture itself is the content

④ How to Use This Guide — Sample 3–4 Day Itinerary

  • Day 1 (Classic): Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) early morning → Bukchon (북촌) and Ikseon-dong (익선동) → evening on the #1309 Myeongdong→Euljiro course
  • Day 2 (Local): Lunch at Seoul Jungang Market (서울중앙시장) → Cheonggyecheon (청계천) → sunset at Eungbongsan (응봉산) (#1308) → evening in Seongsu (성수)
  • Day 3 (Views and Art): Leeum (리움) → Hannam-dong (한남동) and Itaewon (이태원) → night views on the Namsan (남산) walking trail (#40)
  • Treat the 6 overrated spots with a "30 minutes, only if you're passing by" rule.
