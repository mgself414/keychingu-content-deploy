Gangneung KTX 2-Day Full Guide for Foreign Travelers

> Seoul Station KTX 1.8 hours → East Sea + Coffee + K-drama Filming Locations — A 2-day itinerary perfect for foreign travelers

Gangneung (강릉) is a top day trip destination for long-term foreign travelers. From Seoul Station (서울역), a KTX train takes just 1 hour and 54 minutes to reach Jeongdongjin (정동진) on the East Sea, Anmok Coffee Street (안목 커피거리), and famous K-drama filming locations. This guide provides a standard 2-day itinerary along with 10 recommended spots for foreign visitors.

1. How to Get to Gangneung (강릉)

Option Duration Price Recommendation
KTX Gangneung Line (Seoul Station) 1h 54m ₩27,600 (~$20 / ~100 RMB / ~2,800 JPY) ★★★ Foreigner default
ITX-Saemaeul (ITX-새마을) (Cheongnyangni) 5h 30m ₩17,400 Budget-friendly
Intercity Bus (시외버스) (Dongseoul) 3h 00m ₩17,500 Good for late-night departure

Reservation: Korail English website (https://www.letskorail.com/) — Essential to book at least one week in advance for weekends and public holidays.

2. Standard 2-Day Itinerary

Day 1 (Arrival + Jeongdongjin (정동진) · Gyeongpo (경포))

  • 07:00 AM: KTX Gangneung Line (강릉선) from Seoul Station (서울역)
  • 08:54 AM: Arrive at Gangneung Station (강릉역)
  • 09:30 AM: Jeongdongjin (정동진) (Sandglass Park (모래시계공원) · Jeongdongjin Sunrise Spot (정동진 해돋이 명소))
  • 12:00 PM: Lunch in Gangneung (강릉) (Chodang Tofu Village (초당두부 마을) — Chodang Sundubu (초당순두부, soft tofu stew))
  • 02:00 PM: Gyeongpo Beach (경포해변) + Gyeongpo Lake (경포호) walk
  • 04:00 PM: Anmok Coffee Street (안목 커피거리) (Coffee Street, #1 for foreign tourists)
  • 07:00 PM: Dinner (Gangneung Hoe (회, sliced raw fish) / Agutang (아구탕, monkfish stew))
  • 09:00 PM: Hotel check-in

Day 2 (Coffee · Experience · Souvenirs · Return)

  • 08:00 AM: Hotel breakfast
  • 09:30 AM: Gangneung Coffee Museum (강릉 커피박물관) (Home of coffee brands like Terarosa (테라로사) and Bohemian (보헤미안))
  • 11:00 AM: Ojukheon (오죽헌) (Historic site of Shin Saimdang (신사임당) and Yi I (이이) from the Joseon Dynasty (조선시대), English guidance available)
  • 01:00 PM: Lunch (Hoe jeongsik (회 정식, sliced raw fish set meal) or Makguksu (막국수, buckwheat noodles))
  • 02:30 PM: Gangneung Jungang Market (강릉 중앙시장) (Fish market, street food, souvenirs)
  • 04:00 PM: Gangneung Station (강릉역)
  • 04:30 PM: KTX to Seoul (서울)
  • 06:24 PM: Arrive at Seoul Station (서울역)

3. 10 Recommended Spots & Experiences for Foreigners

Cafes (For Coffee Lovers)

1. Terarosa (테라로사) Main Branch — The birthplace of Gangneung's specialty coffee brand. English menu available.

2. Bohemian Park I-choo (보헤미안 박이추) Main Branch — Another iconic Gangneung coffee brand. Popular among foreign regulars.

3. Coffee Cupper (커피커퍼) — A representative cafe on Anmok Coffee Street (안목 커피거리).

Food

4. Chodang Sundubu Village (초당순두부 마을) — Gangneung's signature dish. Picture menus available.

5. Gangneung Hoejip (강릉 회집) (Sliced Raw Fish Restaurant) (Jeongdongjin (정동진) or Jumunjin (주문진)) — Fresh seafood from the East Sea.

6. Jang Kal-guksu (장칼국수, spicy noodle soup) / Makguksu (막국수, buckwheat noodles) — Gangneung's nofo (노포, 30+ year traditional restaurant) (Sortino recommended).

Experiences & Sightseeing

7. Jeongdongjin (정동진) — Famous sunrise spot + Sandglass Park (모래시계공원).

8. Gyeongpo Beach (경포해변) — The East Sea's signature beach.

9. Ojukheon (오죽헌) — Historic site of Shin Saimdang (신사임당) and Yi I (이이) from the Joseon Dynasty (조선시대) (English audio guide).

10. Coffee Museum (커피박물관) — Learn about the history of coffee brands + hands-on experiences.

4. Foreigner-Friendly Tips

English Availability

  • High: Coffee brands (Terarosa (테라로사) · Bohemian (보헤미안) — English menus) · Ojukheon (오죽헌) (English audio guide)
  • Mid-High: Jeongdongjin (정동진) · Gyeongpo (경포) (English signboards)
  • Local: Chodang Tofu Village (초당두부 마을) · Hoejip (회집) · Market (시장) (picture menus, translation apps)

Budget (Per Person for 2 Days)

  • KTX Round Trip: ₩55,200
  • Accommodation 1 night: ₩80,000~250,000 (Hotel vs. Guesthouse)
  • Meals 4 times: ₩60,000~120,000
  • Entrance fees · Experiences: ₩20,000~40,000
  • City transportation (Taxi · Bus): ₩30,000
  • Total Average: ₩260,000~500,000 (~$190-360 / ~1,200-2,400 RMB / ~26,000-50,000 JPY)

By Season

  • Spring (April-May): Jeongdongjin (정동진) cherry blossoms + Gyeongpo (경포) walks
  • Summer (June-August): Gyeongpo (경포) · Jeongdongjin (정동진) · Jumunjin (주문진) beaches (swimming)
  • Autumn (October-November): Ojukheon (오죽헌) autumn foliage + Coffee Street (커피거리)
  • Winter (December-February): Pyeongchang (평창) (adjacent to Gangneung) skiing + Jeongdongjin (정동진) sunrise

By Persona

  • Coffee Enthusiast: Day 1 Anmok (안목) → Day 2 Coffee Museum (커피박물관) + Terarosa (테라로사) · Bohemian (보헤미안)
  • Beach & Relaxation: Day 1 Gyeongpo (경포) · Jeongdongjin (정동진) → Day 2 Jumunjin (주문진) (seafood)
  • K-drama Fan: Jeongdongjin (정동진) (drama Sandglass (모래시계)) + Gangmun Beach (강문해변) (Goblin filming location)
  • European Persona: Ojukheon (오죽헌) + Coffee brands + Gangneung hanok (한옥, traditional Korean house) stay
  • Family-Friendly: Gyeongpo Beach (경포해변) + Coffee Museum (커피박물관) + Jeongdongjin (정동진)

Accommodation Recommendations

  • Luxury: St. John's Hotel (세인트존스 호텔) (Jeongdongjin (정동진)), InterContinental Gangneung (인터컨티넨탈 강릉)
  • Mid-range: Lakia Sandpine (라카이 샌드파인) (Gyeongpo Beach (경포해변)), Golden Tulip Gangneung (골든튤립 강릉)
  • Guesthouse: Gangneung (강릉) city center (₩30,000~60,000)
  • Hanok Stay: Gangneung Seon-gyojang (강릉 선교장) (Joseon (조선) noble house, unique for foreign visitors)

6. KTX Combos with Other Cities

For long-term travelers staying 7+ days, consider combining Gangneung (강릉) with other cities:

  • Gangneung 1 night + return to Seoul (서울) → KTX Jeonju (전주) 1 night (Total 3 days, 2 nights)
  • Gangneung 2 nights (relaxation) + Busan (부산) KTX 2 nights (Total 5 days, 4 nights)
  • Gangneung 1 night + KTX Jeongdongjin (정동진) → Seoul (서울) → Incheon International Airport (인천공항) departure (Total 3 days)
