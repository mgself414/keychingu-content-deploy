Gangneung KTX 1 night 2 days full guide for foreigners

Seoul Station KTX 1.8 hours → East Sea + coffee + K-drama filming location — 2 days and 1 night route suitable for foreigners

Gangneung is the #1 day trip destination for foreign long-term travelers. 1 hour 54 minutes by KTX from Seoul Station → Jeongdongjin Donghae + Anmok Coffee Street + K-drama filming location. This guide covers the standard route for 2 days and 1 night + 10 stores suitable for foreigners.

1. How to get to Gangneung

Options Take price Recommended
KTX Gangneung Line (Seoul Station) 1 hour 54 minutes ₩27,600 ★★★ Foreigner default
ITX-Saemaeul (Cheongnyangni) 5 hours 30 minutes ₩17,400 low budget
Intercity Bus (Dong Seoul) 3 hours ₩17,500 midnight departure fit

Reservation: Korail English site (https://www.letskorail.com/) — Required 1 week in advance on weekends and public holidays

2. Standard 2 days and 1 night itinerary

Day 1 (Arrival + Jeongdongjin·Gyeongpo)

  • 07:00 Seoul Station KTX Gangneung Line
  • 08:54 Arrival at Gangneung Station
  • 09:30 Jeongdongjin (Hourglass Park·Jeongdongjin sunrise spot)
  • 12:00 Gangneung Lunch (Chodang Tofu Village — Chodang Soft Tofu)
  • 14:00 Gyeongpo Beach + Gyeongpo Lake walk
  • 16:00 Anmok Coffee Street (Coffee Street, 1st place for foreigners)
  • 19:00 Dinner (Gangneung raw fish and monkfish stew)
  • 21:00 Hotel check-in

Day 2 (Coffee, Experience, Souvenirs, Return)

  • 08:00 Hotel breakfast
  • 09:30 Gangneung Coffee Museum (home of coffee brands such as Terrarosa and Bohemian)
  • 11:00 Ojukheon (Joseon Dynasty Shin Saimdang and Ii historical sites, English guide)
  • 13:00 Lunch (sashimi set meal or Makguksu)
  • 14:30 Gangneung Jungang Market (fish market, food, souvenirs)
  • 16:00 Gangneung Station
  • 16:30 KTX bound for Seoul
  • 18:24 Arrival at Seoul Station

3. 10 stores and experiences suitable for foreigners

Café (Coffee Persona)

2. Bohemian Park Ichu Main Store — Gangneung coffee brand. foreign regulars

3. Coffee Cupper — Anmok Coffee Street representative cafe

Food

4. Chodang Soft Tofu Village — Gangneung signature. picture menu available

Experience/Tourism

7. Jeongdongjin — Sunrise spot + Hourglass Park

10. Coffee Museum — Coffee brand history + experience

4. Foreign fit tips

English language availability

  • Top: Coffee brand (Terrosa · Bohemian — English menu) · Ojukheon (English audio)
  • Slander: Jeongdongjin·Gyeongpo (English sign)
  • Local: Chodang Tofu Village, Gathering, Market (picture menu, translation app)

Budget (2 days and 1 night per person)

  • KTX round trip: ₩55,200
  • Accommodation per night: ₩80,000~250,000 (hotel vs guesthouse)
  • 4 meals: ₩60,000~120,000
  • Entrance fee/experience: ₩20,000~40,000
  • City transportation (taxi, bus): ₩30,000
  • Total average ₩260,000~500,000

By season

  • Spring (April-May): Jeongdongjin Cherry Blossoms + Gyeongpo Walk
  • Summer (June-August): Gyeongpo, Jeongdongjin, Jumunjin Beach (sea bathing)
  • Fall (October-November): Ojukheon Autumn Leaves + Coffee Street
  • Winter (December-February): Pyeongchang (adjacent to Gangneung) skiing + Jeongdongjin sunrise

By persona

  • Coffee Mania: Day 1 Discernment → Day 2 Coffee Museum + Terrarosa Bohemian
  • Beach·Recreation: Day 1 Gyeongpo·Jeongdongjin → Day 2 Jumunjin (seafood)
  • K-drama fan: Jeong Dong-jin (drama Hourglass) + Gangmun Beach (Goblin filming location)
  • European Persona: Ojukheon + coffee brand + Gangneung Hanok stay
  • Family fit: Gyeongpo Beach + Coffee Museum + Jeongdongjin

Accommodation Recommendations

  • Luxury: St. John's Hotel (Jeongdongjin), InterContinental Gangneung
  • Intermediate: Lakai Sandpine (Gyeongpo Beach), Golden Tulip Gangneung
  • Guesthouse: Downtown Gangneung (₩30,000~60,000)
  • Hanok stay: Gangneung Seongyojang (Joseon noble house, unique for foreigners)
