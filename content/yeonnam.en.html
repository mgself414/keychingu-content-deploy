⚠ Five major mistakes foreigners make in Yeonnam

1. Recognition of “Yeonnam = part of Hongdae” → No separate movement lines

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. Yeonnam is recognized as a part of Hongdae main district. In reality, Yeonnam-dong is the #1 trendy sub-area in Hongdae MZ — The atmosphere is completely different from downtown Hongdae. A 4-5 hour deep dive is recommended as a separate course.

2. Hongik University Station Exit 3 → Yeonnam Dongjin Market Alley unknown

From Hongik University Station, foreigners can only go to the Hongik University Main Pass at Exit 9 and the Yeonnam Pass at Exit 3. In reality, 5 minutes walk from Exit 3 = Start of Yeonnam Dongjin Market alley — Western food, cafes, and brunch crowded together at 50m intervals.

3. Gyeongui Line Forest Trail Walking Course Miji

Just look at the stores in Yeonnam-dong. In reality, Yeonnam-dong ↔ Yeonhui-dong Gyeongui Line Forest Trail (1.5km) is Yeonnam Jeongsu — the store + walking combination course provides the greatest satisfaction to foreigners.

4. Not aware of Yeonnam store reservation manners

Reservation recommended for Yeonnam Modern Western Style Brunch (Salre, Yeonnam Taste, Langt). Weekend walk-in 60 minute+ wait. Check the KakaoTalk channel in advance.

5. Confusion between Yeonnam and Yeonhee stores

Mixed addresses in Yeonnam-dong, Yeonhui-dong, and Yeonhui-ro. Yeonnam-dong = Mapo-gu (Hongdae area) · Yeonhui-dong = Seodaemun-gu (adjacent to Hongdae area). This guide covers Yeonnam-dong + Yeonhui-ro Mapo-gu.

2. Yeonnam Chwihyang
