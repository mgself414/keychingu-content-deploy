🎯 Why “Seoul Solo Diner” is meta

Motivation for a full culinary experience for a foreigner visiting Korea (#342 Solo meta follow-up). Korean food is a tradition for 2-4 people, but Korea's single-person restaurant, bar, cafe, and store infrastructure is #2 (after Tokyo) among global cities. Myeongdong, Itaewon, Hongdae, Gangnam 1-person Korean food, international, BBQ, bar cluster + GS25·CU 1-person lunch box + 1-person coin karaoke + 1-person sauna + 1-person cafe.

⚠ 3 Solo Diner Mistakes

1. "Korean food = 2 or more people" — One-person Korean food store Miji: Korean food for a household of 2 to 4 people. In reality, it is Hansot, Gimbap Heaven, Saemaeul Restaurant, and Hanam Pork House set for 1 + hotel Korean meal course for 1 + market full experience for 5,000 won per person.

2. "BBQ=2 or more people" — 1 person BBQ store unknown: BBQ for 2 people household. In reality, I am a fan of Saemaeul Restaurant, Hanam Pork House, and Broadcast Meat Party (#179) 1-person set.

3. "Bar · Pocha = 2 or more people" — 1-person Pocha enthusiast Miji: Assumption of 2 Pocha. In reality, it is Jongno 3-ga food stall + Euljiro Nogari Alley 1-person counter + 1-person full experience of makgeolli, soju, and beer.

Recommended route

[A] Solo Diner 1 week full package

  • D1: Myeongdong 1-person BBQ + street food
  • D2: Gwangjang Market drug gimbap for one person + Myeongdong Korean food
  • D3: Itaewon International for 1 person + 24h at night
  • D4: Jongno 3-ga 1-person Pocha + Euljiro Nogari Alley
  • D5: Hansot·Kimbab Heaven + 24h convenience store + karaoke
  • D6: BBQ for 1 person at Saemaeul Restaurant + 24h Jjimjilbang
  • D7: Departure

  • Breakfast: Gwangjang Drug Gimbap
  • Lunch: Limited meal for one person or BBQ
  • Afternoon: One-person cafe/izakaya
  • Dinner: Jongno 3-ga Pocha for one person

[C] 24h single mania 1 night stay

  • Dinner: Myeongdong BBQ → Jongno 3-ga
  • Night: 24h sauna + 24h karaoke
  • Dawn: 24-hour convenience store lunch box
