🎯 How to Use This Guide

The bass hits your chest, and every time the water cannon fires, thousands of hands go up at once — Korean summer festivals mostly end with you soaked. This guide is for travelers who want to go to an EDM festival in Korea, and it picks out only the events that are actually running now, with their official schedules and entry rules.

Korea's big EDM festivals change dates and venues every year, and some years they skip. So we first sorted each event by "was a 2025–2026 edition confirmed on official channels?" Only confirmed events are recommended; for events with no recent official schedule, we only record what happened. The next year's date for the same festival is left blank until it is announced.

⚡ EDC Korea — Autumn, an Overnight Festival at an Incheon Resort

  • 2026 edition: October 3–4, Discovery Park at Inspire Entertainment Resort (인스파이어 엔터테인먼트 리조트), 127 Gonghangmunhwa-ro, Jung-gu, Incheon. Performances 17:00–03:00, gates open 17:00, ticket booth 16:00–02:00 (EDC Korea Hours & Info)
  • The official site currently displays "THANK YOU KOREA! UNTIL NEXT TIME..." The 2027 schedule has not been announced (EDC Korea official)
  • Age: 19 and older. Tickets are booked only after adult verification, and ID is checked at entry before you exchange for a wristband. Accepted ID for foreigners: passport, Alien Registration Card, home-country ID plus a passport copy, or U.S. military ID (EDC Korea Hours & Info)
  • Tickets: Up to 4 per person (per 1 ID). Domestic buyers get refunds as required by law (a fee applies); overseas buyers cannot get refunds (EDC Korea FAQ). No re-entry (EDC Korea Hours & Info)
  • Rain: It goes ahead in the rain. It may be canceled only under heavy-rain, strong-wind, or typhoon warnings, such as 110mm or more in 6 hours (EDC Korea Hours & Info)
  • Getting there: About 8 miles from Incheon International Airport (EDC Korea Travel). In 2026, paid official shuttles (booked via the Queen's Smile app or mobile web) ran from Seoul, Gyeonggi, Busan, Daegu, Gwangju, Cheonan, and Daejeon, along with free Inspire shuttles. The first shuttle back to the Seoul metropolitan area left at midnight (EDC Korea Shuttles)
  • Accommodation package: In 2026, Inspire room packages started from Deluxe King at ₩680,000 (~$510) and were available only to EDC ticket holders (EDC Korea INSPIRE)

💦 S2O Korea — Midsummer, Water Guns at Seoul Land

  • 2026 edition: Saturday July 11 and Sunday July 12. Official 1-day tickets: 7/11 ₩149,000 (~$112), 7/12 ₩139,000 (~$104); 2-day ticket ₩239,000 (~$179) (S2O official notice)
  • Venue and hours: S2O Stage at the East Gate parking lot (동문주차장) inside Seoul Land (서울랜드), 13:00–22:00; Welcome Stage at the Venice stage, 12:30–22:00 (S2O 2026 timetable notice)
  • Where to buy: NOL ticket · Naver Reservation (네이버예약) · Megatix · Damai (大麦) · MAISEAT · LINK2ASIA (uutix, klook, traveloka, ctrip) (S2O official notice). With overseas sellers, it is fairly easy to buy with a foreign card
  • Age: Only those born on or before December 31, 2007, as of 2026. Both the ticket buyer and companions need ID at wristband exchange. For foreigners: passport, Alien Registration Card, or home-country ID (S2O guide)
  • What you can bring: Personal water guns are allowed (empty at the Seoul Land front gate security check). Food and drinks other than sealed water of 500ml or less, bags larger than 30×30cm, camping chairs, and picnic mats are not allowed (S2O guide)
  • Getting there: Expecting the parking lot to fill up, the guide recommends Line 4 Seoul Grand Park Station (대공원역) (S2O guide)
  • The 2027 schedule has not been announced (S2O official, checked 2026-10-21)

🌊 Waterbomb — A Water Festival Mixing K-pop, EDM, and Hip-Hop

  • Character: The official site divides its program into K-POP, EDM, and HIP-HOP. It is not an EDM-only festival (Waterbomb official)
  • Seoul 2027: July 24–25, 2027 (Sat–Sun), early-bird sales on. We could not confirm the venue on the official page (Waterbomb official)
  • Regional tour: The official tour list includes Busan and Sokcho in 2026 (Waterbomb World Tour)
  • Age: In 2027, 19 and older, only those born on or before December 31, 2008 (Waterbomb FAQ)
  • Tickets: Non-transferable; no on-site sales if sold out online. Buyers on the global site do not get a separate mobile ticket; take your booking details to the global ticket booth (Waterbomb FAQ)
  • On-site: Food and drink booths take cards only, no cash. Paid lockers and changing rooms are available (Waterbomb FAQ)

📼 Two Events With No Recent Official Schedule

  • Ultra Korea — The last date on the official site is September 20, 2025, at Oil Tank Culture Park (Munhwa Bichukgiji / 문화비축기지) in Seoul. 2025 1-day tickets were ₩130,000–160,000 (~$98–$120), 19 and older (Ultra Korea Tickets). On the Ultra Worldwide list, Korea appears without a date (Ultra Worldwide). We could not confirm a 2026 announcement. So this guide does not recommend Ultra Korea as an event to build your schedule around
  • World DJ Festival (월드디제이페스티벌) — The official site says the free street EDM show "Make Korea Dance" (대한민국을 춤추게 하라) was held on Saturday October 18, 2025, 16:00–21:00 at Hongdae Red Road R2 (홍대 레드로드 R2), 70m from Hongik Univ. Station Exit 8 (홍대입구역 8번 출구). In 2024 it was held October 26 at Hongdae Red Road R5 (Woldipe official). We could not confirm a 2026 schedule

🧰 EDM Festival Practicalities — Solo, Booking, Payment, Getting Around

  • Solo: You can buy a single ticket for all three events. If you bought several tickets, the buyer and companions must collect wristbands together (EDC Korea Ticket Info · Waterbomb FAQ · S2O guide)
  • ID: Be sure to carry your physical passport. Minors cannot enter or get a refund even with a ticket (EDC Korea Hours & Info · Waterbomb FAQ)
  • Booking timing: The organizers announce that ticket prices rise in stages and that tickets can sell out (EDC Korea FAQ). For schedule announcements, watch each event's official site and official Instagram
  • Payment: For card payment in general, see Foreigner Korea Payment System Deep Dive; for a transit card on the ground, see Choosing Your Korea Transit Card: The No-Panic Manual — T-money, WOWPASS, or Climate Card
  • Getting home at night: For events that end near dawn like EDC, check the official shuttle timetable first (EDC Korea Shuttles). For nighttime routes, see Seoul Night-Out Planner — Solo, Date & Friends Itineraries from Sunset to Sunrise
  • Scalping: All three events prohibit transfers between individuals and say they take no responsibility for problems from purchases outside official ticket sellers (EDC Korea FAQ · Waterbomb FAQ)

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Ultra Korea is every June in Jamsil (잠실)" — The last date on the official site was September 20, 2025, at Oil Tank Culture Park (Munhwa Bichukgiji / 문화비축기지), and no 2026 schedule has been posted (Ultra Korea official)

2. "If I can't go, I'll get a refund on a ticket bought overseas" — EDC Korea gives no refunds to overseas buyers (EDC Korea FAQ)

3. "A photo of my ID on my phone is fine" — EDC Korea does not accept digital ID, social media, or email for adult verification (EDC Korea Hours & Info)

4. "Step out once and come back in" — EDC Korea has no re-entry (EDC Korea Hours & Info)
