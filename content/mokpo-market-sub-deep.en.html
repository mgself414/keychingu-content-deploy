Mokpo General Fish Market, Skate, Three-legged Octopus sub-deep 12 selections for foreigners

Mokpo General Fish Market and 12 skates

1. Mokpo General Fishery Market (Southwest Sea #1)

  • Distinction: About 130 stores, 3 alleys (fish, dried fish, salted fish), About 100 skate stores (#1 cluster), Created in the 1980s
  • Foreigners visit: visitkorea·Mokpo Culture and Tourism·Korean Dinner KBS Broadcast Topic
  • kr cross: #380·#618·#658·#666

2. Skate (domestic distribution 80%)

  • Discrimination: Mokpo Market Skate Domestic #1 80%·Marinated Skate (Hongtak + Makgeolli)·Jeonnam Authentic·About 100 stores
  • Signature: Namdo Hometown Fishery (Specializing in Skate)·Hongtak Samhap (Skate + Aged Kimchi + Boiled Pork)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP Mania·#1 Challenge Food·CN ★★★

3. Three-legged Octopus (Mokpo 9mi)

  • Discrimination: Mokpo + Shinan tidal flat limited fishing·Baby octopus with thin legs·"Three-legged" octopus
  • Signature: Wild octopus (whole, raw), three-legged octopus tangtangi, three-legged octopus yeonpo-tang
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·Challenge food + SNS

4. Mokpo 9 Meals (9 authentic foods)

  • Discrimination: Officially selected by Mokpo Culture and Tourism 9 delicacies (skate, octopus with three legs, black cutlassfish, rockfish, seasoned crab, croaker, juniper, steamed monkfish, octopus scallop)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·Full Set

5. Yudal Mountain + Nojeokbong Peak

  • Discrimination: Mokpo #1 Mt. Yudal 228m·Imjin Invasion of Korea Admiral Yi Sun-sin Nojeokbong Episode·Summit View of Dadohae
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Nature Mania

6. Mokpo Cable Car (Marine Cable Car)

  • Distinction: Opened in 2019·Korea #1 Length 3.23km·Southwest Sea marine cable car·Yudalsan + Gohado connection
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ·SNS #1

7. Gohado (Remains of Yi Sun-sin)

  • Discrimination: Yi Sun-sin 1597 Battle of Myeongnyang·Gohado Observatory·#1 Korean Protector·Cable car end stop
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

8. Mokpo Coastal Passenger Terminal (Island day-trip)

  • Discrimination: Dadohae 1004 Island·Hongdo·Heuksando·Bigeumdo·Dochodo·Southwest Sea island cluster #1
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Nature enthusiast·1 night recommended

9. Peace Square (Night Fountain Show)

  • Discrimination: Mokpo #1 night view spot·Music fountain show after sunset·Beach walk·Artificial beach
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ JP·CN night view enthusiast

10. Mokpo Modern History Museum (former Japanese Consulate)

  • Discrimination: 1900 Japanese Consulate → 1907 Japanese Colonial History Museum·Modern architecture·Registered cultural property
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

11. 1897 opening of port + modern cultural heritage

  • Discrimination: Mokpo port opened in 1897 (after Incheon 1883)·Modern streets + Jeoksan houses·Jeong Jong-yeong and Lee Hoon-dong houses
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

12. KTX Mokpo Station + 1 night recommended

  • Access: Yongsan Station → Mokpo KTX 2 hours and 40 minutes (Honam Line KTX opened in 2015) · City bus 1·3·25
  • City Tour: Market - Yudal Mountain - Cable Car - Peace Square Full day 8 hours / 60,000 won
  • Foreigners fit: ★★★ EN·JP 1-night stay recommended (1 day trip tight)

FAQ 6

Q1. Is Mokpo day-trip possible? A. KTX 2 hours 40 minutes - recommended for 1 night. For a day trip, only market + Yudal Mountain + cable car.

Q2. Recommended stores for skate? A. About 100 stores in the market·Namdo Hometown Fisheries (specializing in skate)·Cooked skate challenge food - Ginger and vinegar included.

Q3. Price of three-legged octopus? A. 8,000-15,000 won per fish (live octopus)·Tangtanggi 20,000 won~/2-3 people.

Q4. Cable car price? A. Round trip 23,000 won·Korea #1 Length 3.23km·No. 1 for sunrise and sunset enthusiasts.

Q5. How to get to Hongdo + Heuksando? A. Coastal Passenger Terminal → Hongdo 2 hours and 30 minutes, Heuksando 2 hours · 1-2 flights daily.

Q6. When is the Peace Square Fountain Show? A. 1 hour after sunset every day from April to October·Only weekends from November to March·Free admission.
