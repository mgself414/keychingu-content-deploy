Mokpo Market Guide — Hongeo at the Comprehensive Fish Market, Octopus at Cheongho Market, Dongbu Market, Dried Seafood Street, Modern History Museum

Subtitle: Mokpo Comprehensive Fish Market (목포 종합수산시장), 07:00–20:00, closed on the first and third Sundays · Cheongho Market (청호시장), closed on the first and third Sundays, cards accepted · Dongbu Market (동부시장), 08:00–22:00 · Dried Seafood Street (건해산물 거리) · Mokpo Modern History Museum (목포근대역사관), 09:00–17:30, closed Mondays — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

For markets in Mokpo, four places are the main ones. The Mokpo Comprehensive Fish Market, facing Mokpo Port, specializes in hongeo (홍어, skate). Cheongho Market has many shops that mainly sell octopus. The Dried Seafood Street near Mokpo Port centers on dried fish and salted seafood. Dongbu Market in Yongdang-dong (용당동) is a large traditional market selling fruit, vegetables, and fish.

This guide covers only the four markets, one hongeo restaurant, and the Mokpo Modern History Museum, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Yudalsan Mountain and the marine cable car are covered separately in #1050 Mokpo Yudalsan + Hongeo.

🐟 Mokpo Comprehensive Fish Market — Hongeo and Dried Fish

Mokpo Comprehensive Fish Market (6 Haean-ro 267beon-gil, Gwangdong 1-ga, Mokpo (목포시 해안로267번길 6, 광동1가), ☎ 061-245-5096) is a wholesale market for seafood and dried fish facing Mokpo Port. The former Dongmyeong-dong Fish Market (동명동어시장) was reborn under its present name in June 2004, when it set up a corporation during a facilities modernization project.

  • Opening hours: 07:00–20:00.
  • Closed days: The first and third Sundays of each month.
  • What is sold: Mokpo's specialty hongeo, dried fish, fresh fish, and salted seafood.
  • Layout: It is divided into three lines. There is Line 1, which gathers the main items, and specialty streets that group shops by item.
  • Cards: They differ by shop.
  • Facilities: Use the public parking lot. Strollers cannot be used.

🐙 Mokpo Cheongho Market — The Market with Many Octopus Shops

Mokpo Cheongho Market (28 Seokhyeon-ro, Mokpo (목포시 석현로 28), ☎ 061-284-1379) is a market known for octopus. According to the tourism organization's description, it has the most octopus shops in the country. Hongeo is handled at about 6 shops.

  • Closed days: The first and third Sundays of each month. Opening hours differ by shop.
  • Facilities: A commercial-building-type market opened in 1999, with 156 shops on the 1st floor and 10 furniture shops on the 2nd floor. Air conditioning runs in summer.
  • Origin labeling: It was selected as the best market for seafood origin labeling.
  • Cards: Accepted.
  • Facilities: Restrooms (separate for men and women) and parking are available.

🛒 Mokpo Dongbu Market — A Large Traditional Market in the City

Mokpo Dongbu Market (15 Yeongsan-ro 257beon-gil, Yongdang-dong, Mokpo (목포시 영산로257번길 15, 용당동), ☎ Dongbu Market Merchants' Association 061-276-5787) grew from street vendors gathering from 1956, and according to the tourism organization's description it is the largest market in Mokpo. An arcade was installed during construction from 2006 to 2008.

  • Opening hours: 08:00–22:00, differing by shop. Closed days also differ by shop.
  • What is sold: Seafood, local agricultural products, food, meat, clothing, and sundries.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available.

🦑 Mokpo Dried Seafood Street — Dried Fish to Taste and Buy

Mokpo Dried Seafood Street (Bukman-dong area, Mokpo (목포시 북만동 일대), ☎ Mokpo City Hall 061-272-2171) is a street of dried fish and seafood shops near Mokpo Port.

  • What is sold: Dried seafood such as croaker, hongeo, anchovies, and kelp, along with salted seafood.
  • Tasting: Many shops let you taste before you buy.
  • Opening hours and closed days: They differ by shop.
  • Parking: There is no dedicated parking lot, so use a nearby public parking lot.

🍽 If You Eat Hongeo — A Restaurant Registered with the Tourism Organization

Deokin Hongeojip (덕인홍어집) (1-1 Yeongsan-ro 73beon-gil, Mokpo (목포시 영산로73번길 1-1), ☎ 061-242-3767) is a restaurant that has served Heuksan hongeo and samhap for over 25 years. Samhap is a dish in which moderately fermented hongeo is topped with boiled pork and ripe kimchi and eaten in one bite.

  • Opening hours: 12:00–21:00. Closed every Monday and during holiday periods.
  • Menu: Heuksan hongeo, hongeo samhap, steamed hongeo, and hongeo liver soup.
  • Cards: Accepted. Reservations by phone are possible, and some menu items can be taken out.

🏛 Mokpo Modern History Museum, Halls 1 and 2

It consists of two buildings, and one admission ticket covers both.

  • Hall 1 (6 Yeongsan-ro 29beon-gil, Mokpo (목포시 영산로29번길 6), ☎ 061-242-0340): Built in 1898 as the Japanese Consulate in Mokpo, it is the oldest building in Mokpo. It opened as a museum in 2014, and its two-story exhibition hall presents 7 themes.
  • Hall 2 (18 Beonhwa-ro, Mokpo (목포시 번화로 18), ☎ 061-270-8728): Built in 1920 as the Mokpo branch of the Oriental Development Company, it is a Jeollanam-do Monument. It displays photographic materials from the Japanese colonial period.
  • Opening hours: 09:00–17:30, last entry 17:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday). Hall 2 is also closed on January 1.
  • Admission: Adults 2,000 won, teenagers and soldiers 1,000 won, elementary school students 500 won (Halls 1 and 2 included). Kindergarten children and those aged 65 and over, among others, enter free. Cards accepted at Hall 2.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Thinking everything is open on Sundays — The Comprehensive Fish Market and Cheongho Market are closed on the first and third Sundays of each month.

2. Pairing a hongeo restaurant and the museum on a Monday — Deokin Hongeojip and the Mokpo Modern History Museum are both closed every Monday.

3. Buying museum tickets twice — The admission fee includes both Hall 1 and Hall 2.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where do I go to see octopus?

A. Cheongho Market. It has many shops that mainly sell octopus, and about 6 hongeo shops as well.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. According to the tourism organization, cards are accepted at Cheongho Market and Deokin Hongeojip, while the Comprehensive Fish Market differs by shop. Card information is not registered for Dongbu Market and the Dried Seafood Street.
