Seoul Christmas Markets 2026 — Only Where a Market Actually Sets Up: The Hyundai Seoul H Village (11/10~12/31, free, reservation or on-site waiting), Lotte Town Market (11/19~2027/1/3), Ttukseom Romantic Han River Market (12/24~27), Myeongdong Cathedral and Everland not yet announced — How to Book, How to Get There, Links to the Lights Guide

Subtitle: The Hyundai Seoul 5th-floor H Village 2026.11.10~12.31 daily 10:30~19:30, closed 11/26 and 12/18, free admission, advance reservation on The Hyundai Hi (4 tickets per account) + on-site QR waiting + foreign-visitor-only desk (official) · Lotte Town Christmas Market 11/19~2027/1/3 (46 days), free General Reservation Ticket + paid Fast Pass (press reports) · Ttukseom Romantic Han River Christmas Market 12/24~27 (Seoul city's Fun Seoul) · Myeongdong Cathedral and Everland not announced for 2026, Lotte World not confirmed · Lights and trees in #745, November calendar in #1411

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Search "Seoul Christmas market" and you get department store facade lights and tree photos all mixed together, so it is hard to tell which places are real markets with rows of booths and which are lights you only look at. This guide covers only the places where a market is actually open in the 2026 season — places with booths, villages, and food zones, where you need a reservation or a wait. Lights, media facades, and tree lightings are in #745 Seoul Illumination, and the full November event schedule is in #1411.

The values listed here are marked official when we saw them directly on the organizer's or operator's official pages on 2026-11-10 (Hyundai Department Store's The Hyundai Hi (더현대Hi) and newsroom, Seoul city's Fun Seoul (펀서울), the Nodeul Island (노들섬) website), and press reports when only news articles exist. The regular business hours of the department stores and Nodeul Island are Korea Tourism Organization TourAPI values. Where no 2026 announcement exists yet, we wrote "not announced for 2026" and listed last season's values separately; where the official page would not open, so we could not even see whether an announcement exists, we wrote "could not be confirmed." Do not assume this year is the same as last year, and check the official page again before you go. Admission ticket prices circulating on blogs could not be confirmed, so they are not included.

🏙 The Hyundai Seoul (더현대 서울) H Village — Dates, Hours, Closures, and Reservations All Official

  • Dates and hours: November 10 (Tue) to December 31 (Thu), 2026, daily 10:30~19:30 (holidays included). Closed November 26 (Thu) and December 18 (Fri). Admission is free (The Hyundai Hi official notice). This differs from the department store's business hours (Mon~Thu 10:30~20:00, Fri~Sun 10:30~20:30, TourAPI), so check the H Village hours separately.
  • Where: The Hyundai Seoul 5th floor Sounds Forest (사운즈 포레스트). This year's theme is 'Christmas in New York City,' with 13 red-brick buildings up to 10m tall and yellow taxis (Hyundai Department Store newsroom, 10/30).
  • Reservations: Advance reservations are made on The Hyundai Hi, the premium curation mall (membership sign-up required). The 1st round opened on Thursday, November 5 at 11 a.m., with a maximum of 4 tickets per account. There is no official announcement yet of the schedule for the 2nd round and after, so check The Hyundai Hi notices again in early December.
  • If you came without a reservation: You can join the on-site waiting list with the QR codes placed on each floor from the 2nd to the 4th floor of the department store (newsroom).
  • Foreign visitors: Hyundai Department Store opens advance H Village reservations for foreign visitors through OTAs such as Klook, and runs a dedicated waiting registration desk for foreign visitors on site (newsroom). We could not confirm the membership requirements for The Hyundai Hi, so foreign visitors should use the OTA route or the on-site desk.
  • Getting there (official 'Directions'): Line 9 Yeouido Station Exit 3 → underground moving walkway about 500m → B2 entrance / Yeouido Station Exit 4 → straight about 400m → main entrance / Line 5 Yeouinaru Station Exit 1 → rear entrance. Address: 108 Yeouidae-ro, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul (서울 영등포구 여의대로 108). ☎ 1588-3650.
  • The official hours are 19:30 and the reservation platform is The Hyundai Hi. If you have heard other numbers or platform names, check against the official notice.

🎪 Lotte Town (롯데타운) Christmas Market — Opens 11/19, Two Kinds of Tickets

  • Dates and venue (Financial News (파이낸셜뉴스) report, 11/8): November 19, 2026 to January 3, 2027, 46 days. The World Park (월드파크) outdoor lawn plaza at Lotte World Tower (롯데월드타워) (300 Olympic-ro, Songpa-gu, Seoul (서울 송파구 올림픽로 300)). With a 19th-century English theme, it will have 5 zones, 40-plus booths, a 17m clock tower, and a 2-story carousel across about 2,645㎡.
  • 2 kinds of tickets (Etoday (이투데이) and Cheonji Ilbo (천지일보) reports, 11/8): ① Free 'General Reservation Ticket' — reserve, then wait on site to enter ② Paid 'Fast Pass Ticket' — immediate entry with no waiting + two KRW 5,000 discount coupons. The Fast Pass price was not in the articles, so we could not confirm it.
  • Reservation schedule (press reports): 1st round November 9 at 2 p.m., on Lotte Department Store Mall and Naver Reservation (네이버 예약). 2nd round November 20, 3rd round December 7.
  • Daily 18h 'Christmas Time Show' (a media show on the Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) exterior wall, press reports). For lighting details, see #745.
  • There is no 2026 announcement of operating hours. In 2025 it ran November 20 to January 4, 2026, daily 11:30~22:00 (Newsis (뉴시스) 2025-11-20). The 2026 entry method is the 2 types above: free General Reservation Ticket + paid Fast Pass (press reports). Do not take last year's prices and hours at face value.
  • We could not confirm the exit number at Jamsil Station (Lines 2 and 8). Follow the signs toward Lotte World Tower.

🎄 Romantic Han River Christmas Market (Ttukseom) — Registered with Seoul City, Four Days 12/24~27

  • It is registered on Seoul city's festival portal Fun Seoul as '2026 Romantic Han River Christmas Market · 2026-12-24~12-27 · Ttukseom Hangang Park' (festacode=415). Hours, fees, and organizer are still blank.
  • As of 2025 (Seoul Media Hub (서울시 미디어허브) and Newsis): 12/19~25, 14:00~21:00, at Ttukseom Hangang Park, with a market zone on the 1st floor of Jabeolle and a food zone under Cheongdam Bridge (청담대교). Experience program materials fee KRW 8,000~KRW 10,000, Naver Reservation (until the day before the operating day) + on-site sign-up for remaining spots. It was hosted by Seoul city (Future Han River Headquarters (미래한강본부)).
  • Getting there (2025 venue): Jabeolle connects directly to Line 7 Jayang Station (Media Hub). We will check whether the 2026 venue is the same after the announcement.

⛪ Myeongdong Cathedral (명동대성당) Christmas Market — Not Announced for 2026, Announced on 12/18 Last Year

  • Not announced for 2026. The 2025 announcement (the Archdiocese of Seoul's (서울대교구) 'Myeongdong, Lighting Up Winter') came out on December 18. Dates and hours are fixed after the announcement is seen.
  • As of 2025: December 24, 11~21h; December 25, 10~20:30; plaza in front of the Catholic Center (가톨릭회관). 17 booths by young artists (handmade religious items and everyday crafts), with priests selling mulled wine and sausages. The Myeongdong Cathedral Rose Garden and light festival ran from November 29 to January 4 of the following year. No admission fee is listed.
  • Address: 74 Myeongdong-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul (서울 중구 명동길 74). We could not confirm the subway exit — for the Myeongdong area guide, see #40 Namsan & Myeongdong.

🏛 Gwanghwamun (광화문) Market and Theme Parks — Not Yet Announced / Could Not Be Confirmed

  • Gwanghwamun Market: As one of the programs of Seoul Winter Festa 2026 (12/11~2027/1/31), only 'planned for December' is posted on Fun Seoul. We will check date and venue details after the announcement — for all of Winter Festa, see #745 and #1411.
  • Everland Christmas Fantasy: No 2026 announcement (as of Samsung C&T (삼성물산) newsroom 11/10). In 2025 it ran November 28 to December 31, with a parade, a fireworks show every night, and sledding from mid-December. The 2025 announcement came out on November 24, so check again in late November. Getting there: Bundang Line Giheung Station → Everline terminus → free shuttle (Everline 05:30~23:30, official). We could not confirm the fares — #451 Theme Parks.
  • Lotte World Adventure: The 2026 schedule, fares, and transport all could not be confirmed (we could not read the body of the official page). In 2025, the winter season 'Twinkle Miracle' ran 11/22~2026/3/2, with a Christmas parade twice a day (14h and 20h) (Kukinews (쿠키뉴스) 2025-11-24).
  • Nodeul Island has no 2026 Christmas market notice on the official website as of November 10 (only notices on the November food trucks, winter operating hours, and parking lot closures). The facility is open winter Tue~Sun 10:00~20:00, closed Mondays, January 1, Seollal (Lunar New Year), and Chuseok (TourAPI). For Seoul Forest, we could not confirm a 2026 notice.

🧰 Practical Tips — Reservations, Free or Paid, Closures, Foreign-Visitor Counters

  • The reservation platform differs by place: The Hyundai = The Hyundai Hi (OTAs such as Klook for foreign visitors) · Lotte = Lotte Department Store Mall and Naver Reservation · Ttukseom = Naver Reservation in 2025. We could not confirm the sign-up requirements of each platform. The only place with an officially announced route for foreign visitors is The Hyundai (OTA booking and an on-site foreign-visitor desk).
  • Separate free from paid: The Hyundai H Village is free to enter (only reservation or waiting). Lotte has a free General Reservation Ticket and a paid Fast Pass separately. Food and goods inside the market are of course paid.
  • Check closure days: The Hyundai H Village is closed 11/26 and 12/18. For other places, check the 2026 announcement.
  • December 24~25 schedule: The Ttukseom market is registered for only the four days 12/24~27, and the Myeongdong Cathedral Christmas Market was two days, 12/24~25, in 2025 (not announced for 2026). For The Hyundai and Lotte, check the reservation rounds on the official pages.
  • Cold: Ttukseom, Myeongdong, and Gwanghwamun are outdoors. The Hyundai H Village is indoors on the 5th floor. For cold-weather gear, see #1318 Seoul Cold Snap Survival Guide.
  • For lights, media facades, and tree lightings (Shinsegae (신세계) Main Store 11/6~2027/1/31 17~24h, Lotte Main Store, DDP, Cheonggyecheon Seoul Lantern Festival (서울빛초롱축제)), see #745.

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

  1. "You can't get into The Hyundai without a reservation" — There is on-site waiting with the QR codes on floors 2 to 4, and there is also a foreign-visitor-only desk (🏙). But it does not open at all on the closure days 11/26 and 12/18.
  2. "Just go to the Lotte market like last year" — In 2026 there are 2 types, a free General Reservation Ticket and a paid Fast Pass, and there are reservation rounds (11/9, 11/20, 12/7). The operating hours are not yet announced (🎪).
  3. "The Ttukseom market runs all December" — It is registered for only four days, 12/24~27 (🎄).
  4. "Last year's blog price is this year's price" — We could not confirm the 2026 official prices for the Fast Pass, Everland, and Lotte World. Trust only the numbers on the reservation page.

⚙ 4 FAQs

Q1. I don't have a Korean phone number. Can I reserve The Hyundai H Village?

Hyundai Department Store said it opens advance reservations for foreign visitors through OTAs such as Klook and runs a dedicated waiting registration desk for foreign visitors on site (newsroom 10/30). We could not confirm the membership requirements for The Hyundai Hi. If you could not reserve, go by on-site QR waiting on floors 2 to 4.

Q2. How much is the Lotte Town market Fast Pass?

The 2026 price was not in the articles, so we could not confirm it. Since there is a way in with the free 'General Reservation Ticket,' check the price yourself on the Lotte Department Store Mall or Naver Reservation screen.

Q3. Which markets can I visit on the evening of December 24?

The Ttukseom Romantic Han River Christmas Market (12/24~27) is registered with Seoul city (2026 hours not announced, 2025 was 14~21h). The Myeongdong Cathedral Christmas Market was 12/24 11~21h in 2025, and 2026 is not announced. For whether reservations are available for 12/24 at The Hyundai and Lotte, check each reservation page.

Q4. When is the Gwanghwamun Christmas market?

Only 'planned for December' is posted, as a Seoul Winter Festa (12/11~2027/1/31) program. Check the date in the Fun Seoul announcement.
