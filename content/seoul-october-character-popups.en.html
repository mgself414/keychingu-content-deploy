Seoul October Character Pop-Up & Exhibition Calendar 2026 — Crayon Shin-chan, Pokémon, Atashin'chi, Hunter × Hunter: 10 Spots by Closing Date

Subtitle: The 9 character pop-ups/experience exhibitions and 2 standing merchandise spots actually running in Seoul in October 2026, ordered by closing date — the Pokémon 30th-anniversary pop-up at HiKR Ground (하이커그라운드, through 10/11), Atashin'chi (아따맘마, through 10/18), the Hunter × Hunter collab café (through 10/18), and the Crayon Shin-chan experience exhibition (opens 10/17, through 2027/1/24). Nearly everything clusters around one building — Yongsan I'Park Mall (용산 아이파크몰) — and one subway line, Line 2 (Hongdae → Konkuk University → Seongsu).

🎯 Why an "October Character Pop-Up Calendar"

Seoul's standing character-goods stores already have a guide (#1350 Seoul Character Goods Shop Tour). Pop-ups are a different animal — they run for 2–4 weeks and then vanish. October 2026 is especially stacked with IPs that carry heavy Japanese and Greater China fan bases all landing at once: the Pokémon 30th-anniversary mega-festa, Atashin'chi (a Tooniverse-generation favorite), the Hunter × Hunter collab café, and the domestic debut of a Crayon Shin-chan interactive exhibition that made headlines abroad first. For a foreign traveler, the question isn't "where's the character mecca," it's "what pop-up is actually open the week I'm in Seoul."

This piece answers only that question — dates, locations, hours, and booking method, pinned down in a table. It pulls together the wave of pop-up notices that Seoul's local Instagram accounts all posted in late September, then cross-checks them against official channels and press coverage.

⚠ Note on timing: This is an October-2026-only calendar. Schedules are as checked on 2026-09-29, and pop-ups often close early or extend. Anywhere marked "per Instagram notice" couldn't be confirmed through the operator's official channel or press coverage and rests on Instagram announcements alone — check that account's latest post before visiting. "Unconfirmed" means there's no official information and it needs on-site confirmation.

🅰 Closing Before October 11 — Pokémon, TWIMI, Harry Potter, Sanrio Stationery, Tiny Ville

  • Pokémon "Pikachu's Autumn Outing" (through 10/11): A 30th-anniversary mega-festa jointly run by Pokémon Korea, the Korea Tourism Organization, and AREX. The venue, HiKR Ground, is a Korea Tourism Organization-run complex built for foreign visitors (right by Cheonggyecheon), making this the best-fit stop on this list for a foreign travel route. The lower floors hold media-art and hands-on exhibits (Tue–Sun, closed Mondays); the 5th floor is the pop-up store (daily 10:00–20:00), selling hanbok-Pikachu merchandise and capsule toys. Admission is free. During the run, Pokémon GO has a catchable orange-hanbok Pikachu; the AREX airport railway express train is wrapped in a Pokémon theme; and hanbok-Pikachu photo zones go up at Incheon Airport Terminals 1 and 2 (인천공항 제1·2여객터미널) and the Seoul Station City Airport Terminal (서울역 도심공항), along with a stamp rally running from major AREX hubs to HiKR Ground (per Gametoc report). The exhibition and pop-up main event is held only at HiKR Ground, so on the day you arrive, go airport photo zone → HiKR Ground.
  • TWIMI pop-up (through 10/4): A forest-treasure-hunt-themed pop-up for TWS's official character TWIMI, at P&R Seongsu in Seongsu. Booking runs through Weverse — a global app overseas K-pop fans already use, which makes it more accessible for foreigners than a Naver Reservations flow (whether a Korean phone number is required is unconfirmed). Only relevant if you're in Seoul during the first week of October.
  • Harry Potter Mahou Dokoro pop-up (through 10/11): 3F Dopamine Station, Living Park, Yongsan I'Park Mall. The Chiikawa Shop is on the same floor, so it's two stops in one visit (see 🅳 below).
  • Sanrio Characters stationery pop-up (through 10/11, per Instagram notice): 2F, AK Plaza Hongdae — a stationery-focused pop-up centered on diaries and stickers. The notice says physical ID is checked at entry — foreign visitors should bring a passport (whether a passport is accepted is unconfirmed; a September pop-up on the 4th floor of the same building also required ID).
  • Tiny Ville (through 10/11, per Instagram notice): A character-goods pop-up (SpongeBob clickers and the like, plus hidden gacha/prize machines) inside Sweet Park, the dessert hall on B1 of Shinsegae Gangnam. It connects directly to Express Bus Terminal station, so it slots easily into a Gangnam shopping route, but it hasn't been confirmed through any official operator channel — check the Instagram notice before going.

🅱 Into the Third and Fourth Week of October — Atashin'chi, Hunter × Hunter, Usahana

  • Atashin'chi (through 10/18): 2F Design Square, KT&G Sangsangmadang Hongdae. The set recreates the anime family's living room wholesale, and the goods lineup centers on everyday items — diaries, glasses stands, keyrings. It's the original Japanese IP (あたしンち), so it's a welcome name for Japanese travelers too. Kuji (lottery draws) run only on weekends (Sat/Sun), 16:00–18:00, ₩2,300 per draw, capped at 200 people a day (per Instagram notice) — go on a weekend afternoon if the kuji is the point. It stays open late, until 21:00.
  • Hunter × Hunter pop-up & collab café (through 10/18): 3F V-SQUARE, Lotte Cinema Konkuk University. A café with character-themed tables serving a collab menu named after Nen abilities, plus a goods zone. It prioritized Naver Reservations holders early in the run and switches to walk-in later, so walk-in admission is likely from the second week of October on (the exact switch date is unconfirmed). The separate Anyplus collab that ran in Hapjeong and Busan this past spring is a different event — don't mix up the old information with this one.
  • Usahana's first solo pop-up (through 10/27, per Instagram notice): The domestic debut solo pop-up for the Sanrio character Usahana, on the 6th floor of Living Park at Yongsan I'Park Mall (the floor where character pop-ups rotate through). Three local accounts posted the same schedule and hours, but it hasn't been confirmed through Sanrio's official channel. Runs on a Naver Reservations system — if booking is hard, try on-site standby on a weekday morning.

🅲 Opening October 17 — The Crayon Shin-chan Exhibition Runs Into Winter

  • Crayon Shin-chan: Let's Play! A Space-Time Adventure (10/17–2027/1/24): The domestic debut of a Crayon Shin-chan interactive exhibition that made headlines abroad first, in the Yeonmujang 5-gil area of Seongsu. It's built around mini-games and hands-on activities rather than pieces to look at. Booking defaults to the producer's own platform, bemill, with an early-bird round 1 (9/28–10/2, 20% off) and round 2 (10/3–10/6, 10% off) — regular price is posted at ₩23,000 (per IG). The point that matters for foreign visitors: it's also listed on KKday, which gives a way to book with an international card (pricing and language support are unconfirmed). It runs into winter, so only travelers visiting before October 17 miss it. The street address varies between posts (9-13 / 9-29), so confirm the address on the booking page before you go.

🅳 Standing Spots — One Building at Yongsan I'Park Mall, Plus Gukje Electronics Center

  • Yongsan I'Park Mall's Living Park: The single most efficient building on this October calendar. 3F Dopamine Station has the official, standing Chiikawa Shop Yongsan (opened February 2026) plus the Harry Potter Mahou Dokoro pop-up (through 10/11); 6F has the Usahana pop-up (through 10/27) and a standing Marvel Store. It connects directly to Yongsan Station, so it fits into an hour or two around a KTX or AREX transfer. Dopamine Station stays open until 22:00.
  • Gukje Electronics Center (Seocho, Nambu Bus Terminal): A figure-and-gacha shopping complex where, since the Chiikawa movie's release, local fans have made "conquest maps" of the Chiikawa plush, goods, and gacha shops scattered across floors 2 through 9. It's not one official store but a sum of many small shops, so it's the place to find stock the official shop doesn't carry. Open 10:00–20:00, closed the 1st and 3rd Sunday of every month (per Instagram notice) — check the date first if you're planning a Sunday visit.

🗓 A Two-Day Route — One Yongsan Building Plus One Subway Line

Day 1 · Yongsan → Cheonggyecheon: Morning at Yongsan I'Park Mall (3F Chiikawa Shop and Harry Potter → 6F Usahana and Marvel) → Line 1 to Jonggak → afternoon at HiKR Ground's Pokémon pop-up (through 10/11) → a walk along Cheonggyecheon. After 10/11, swap HiKR Ground for Gukje Electronics Center at Nambu Bus Terminal (Line 3) instead.

Day 2 · Line 2: Hongdae → Konkuk University → Seongsu: Morning at Hongdae Sangsangmadang's Atashin'chi (opens 11:00) plus AK Plaza's Sanrio stationery pop-up (through 10/11) → one ride on Line 2 to Konkuk University for lunch at the Hunter × Hunter collab café → two more stops to Seongsu for TWIMI (through 10/4) or the Crayon Shin-chan exhibition (from 10/17). All three sit on Line 2, so there's no transfer.

Weekend kuji switch: Atashin'chi's kuji only sells Sat/Sun 16:00–18:00 — on a weekend, flip the Day 2 order, hitting Seongsu and Konkuk University first and arriving in Hongdae by 16:00.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Trusting a September post and showing up anyway — the Lotte World Level2 kidult pop-up in Jamsil (through 9/30) and the Inuyasha pop-up in Hongdae (through 9/22) have already closed. A pop-up's closing date outranks whatever post you saved. Read this table by closing date too.

2. Assuming Naver Reservations will just work — Usahana and the Sanrio stationery pop-up run mainly on Naver Reservations, which can shut out anyone without a Korean phone number. Front-loading your plan with venues booked through Weverse (TWIMI) or KKday (Crayon Shin-chan), or ones with free walk-in access (Chiikawa Shop, Gukje Electronics Center, HiKR Ground's 5F pop-up), lowers that risk.

3. Showing up without ID — the AK Plaza Hongdae pop-up has posted notice that it checks physical ID. Bring your actual passport, not a photo of it on your phone.

⚙ 3 FAQs

1. If you can only pick one? — Before 10/11, go with HiKR Ground's Pokémon pop-up: it's built for foreign visitors, admission is free, and you can even pick up the Pokémon GO-exclusive hanbok Pikachu. After 10/11, go with Yongsan I'Park Mall (Chiikawa Shop + Usahana + Marvel, one building).

2. Where can you book without a Korean phone number? — TWIMI runs through Weverse, and the Crayon Shin-chan exhibition is listed on KKday. The Hunter × Hunter café goes walk-in once its early reservation window ends. For the rest of the Naver Reservations pop-ups, weekday-morning on-site standby is the realistic fallback.

3. What time draws the smallest crowd? — Pop-up lines run longest around weekend opening hours. I'Park Mall's Dopamine Station (open until 22:00) and Atashin'chi (until 21:00) are calmer on weekday evenings.
