🎯 Why are Gangdong and Cheonho exclusive guides?

Gangdong and Cheonho are located in Gangdong-gu's Korea's No. 1 prehistoric and Baekje historic site, east of the Han River, middle-class family area — Amsa Prehistoric Site (Korea's No. 1 Neolithic Era site, excavated in 1925) + Pungnaptoseong (Baekje City Wall Remains, Historic Site No. 11) + Cheonho Hangang Park and Gwangnaru (east of the Han River) + Gangdong I-Park the River Mall and Cheonho Rodeo Street (Gangdong youth hot zone) are compressed into one area. Korean prehistoric and Baekje history deep dive + east side of the Han River + foreign family friendly combination.

⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Gangdong = It's near Jamsil, so you only need to look at Jamsil" — Gangdong readers don't know the value

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. Gangdong is recognized only as “next to Jamsil” and is passed over, but in reality, Gangdong is Korea’s No. 1 prehistoric and Baekje history site — Amsa Prehistoric Site (Korea’s No. 1 Neolithic site, excavated in 1925) + Pungnap Toseong (Baekje Hanseong City Wall) + Mongchontoseong = 6,000 years of Korean history deep dive. Different from Jamsil (Hyundai Theme Park). Dunchon Olympic Park is the sister area of ​​Jamsil.

2. "Amsa Prehistoric Site = Just a Ruin" — Korea's No. 1 Neolithic Site, Unknown

Amsa Prehistoric Site Excavated in 1925, is an integrated site of Korean Neolithic, Bronze, and Iron Age — Korea's No. 1 prehistoric site. Combine-patterned pottery (6,000 years ago, Korea's first pottery) + restoration of a dugout = No. 1 deep dive in Korean ancient history for foreigners. 10-minute walk from Exit 4 of Amsa Station, entrance fee 1,000 won (English guidance for foreigners + museum combined).

3. "Cheonho = just a neighborhood" — Miji, a youth hot zone east of the Han River

Cheonho is not just a neighborhood, but Gangdong Youth/MZ Hot Zone — Cheonho Rodeo Street + Gangdong I-Park the River Mall (Hangang View Shopping Mall) + Cafe, Bar, Izakaya Hot Zone. Adjacent to Cheonho Station transfer station for Lines 5 and 8 + Gangil and Amsa new towns. Jamsil (Line 2) 5 minutes away.

① Nature, Culture, History (3 places)

1. Amsa Prehistoric Site ★ Korea’s first Neolithic site

  • Address: 875 Olympic-ro, Gangdong-gu, Seoul (10-minute walk from Exit 4 of Amsa Station, Line 8)
  • Category: Prehistoric Sites/Museums
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — No. 1 deep dive in Korean ancient history
  • Signature: Korea's first prehistoric site excavated in 1925 + 6,000-year-old comb-patterned pottery (Korea's first pottery) + Integrated Museum of Neolithic, Bronze, and Ironware + Restoration of the dugout
  • Why: No. 1 deep dive in Korean ancient history — English guide for foreigners + museum integration. The birthplace of Korea's Neolithic Age + experience 6,000 years of history.
  • Admission fee: 1,000 won (same for foreigners) / Hours 09:30~18:00 (Closed on Mondays)

2. Pungnaptoseong Earthen Fortress ★ Baekje Hanseong Fortress

  • Address: Pungnap-dong area, Songpa-gu and Gangdong-gu, Seoul
  • Category: Baekje Toseong Fortress/Historical Sites
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Baekje Hanseong City Wall Remains
  • Signature: Historic Site No. 11 Baekje Hanseong (BC 18~AD 475) Capital City + Earthen Fortress Circumference 3.5km + Adjacent to Hanseong Baekje Museum
  • Why: Among the four major ancient states of Korea (Goguryeo, Baekje, Silla, Gaya), Baekje Hanseong is the 1st deep dive — the 1st place in Korean ancient history for foreigners.
  • Admission Fee: Free (separate from Hanseong Baekje Museum)

⚙ FAQ (8)

Q1. Entrance to Amsa prehistoric site for foreigners?

1,000 won (same for foreigners) / 09:30~18:00 (closed on Mondays). English information for foreigners + museum integration (Neolithic, Bronze, Iron). No. 1 in restoration of 6,000-year-old comb-patterned pottery and pit huts. 10-minute walk from Exit 4 of Amsa Station, Line 8.

Q2. How do you see Pungnaptoseong?

Free + 24 hours (A walk around Toseong Fortress is possible) — Approximately 3.5km around Pungnap-dong. Hanseong Baekje Museum (near Songpa-gu/Mongchontoseong Station, free) integrated deep dive #1. English guide for foreigners + deep dive into Baekje, one of Korea's four most ancient countries.

Q3. Gangdong Hangang Bicycle Course?

Gwangnaru → Cheonho → Jamsil → Ttukseom → Banpo → Yeouido = Approximately 30km Han River Bicycle Path (Korea’s No. 1 bicycle course). Gwangnaru is the starting point for bicycles on the east side of the Han River + Ttareungi rental is OK. #149 Bicycle guide east starting point.

Q4. What are the foreigner-friendly stores in Gangdong and Cheonho?

Daram Gangdong Main Branch, Hanam Pork House, Sushi Doku, Raccoon Bagel, and Little Chatswood have English menu + card payment + foreigner-friendly standard. 119 Chicken feet is the number one vlog for foreigners because it is a Korean challenge food.

Q5. Is it possible to connect Gangdong + Jamsil?

OK — Gangdong (Lines 5 and 8) → Jamsil (Line 8, 2nd stop) = 5 minutes by subway. Amsa Prehistoric Site + Pungnaptoseong (Gangdong) + Mongchontoseong + Dunchon Olympic Park + Jamsil (Songpa) = 1 day full set of Korean ancient history and the east side of the Han River.

Q6. 119 Spicy chicken feet available to foreigners?

Select 3 levels of spiciness OK (mild, normal, spicy). For foreigners, it is recommended to start with a mild flavor. A foreign challenge food that often appears in Korean vlogs and mukbangs. Card payment + English menu partially OK.

Q7. Gangdong I-Park the River Mall tax exemption?

Instant duty-free refund for foreigners at some stores (KRW 30,000 or more + passport). Han River View Shopping Mall (2014) + full set of cafe, restaurant, and movie theater. 5 minutes walk from Cheonho Station.

Q8. Gangdong night safety?

24-hour safety — Gangdong is a Korean middle-class, family neighborhood + CCTV and plainclothes police standards. Nighttime OK for foreigners. Kakao Taxi recommended after midnight.
