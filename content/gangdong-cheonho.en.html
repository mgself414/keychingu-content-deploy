🎯 Why a Dedicated Guide for Gangdong (강동) · Cheonho (천호)?

Gangdong (강동) · Cheonho (천호) is Gangdong-gu's (강동구) primary hub for Korean prehistoric and Baekje (백제) history, the eastern Hangang River (한강) area, and a middle-class family-friendly destination.
