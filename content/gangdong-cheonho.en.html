🎯 Why a Dedicated Guide for Gangdong (강동) · Cheonho (천호)?

Gangdong (강동) · Cheonho (천호) is Gangdong-gu's (강동구) primary hub for Korean prehistoric and Baekje (백제) history, the eastern Hangang River (한강) area, and a middle-class family-friendly destination. It combines the Amsa Prehistoric Site (암사 선사유적지) (Korea's top Neolithic Age (신석기 시대) archaeological site, excavated in 1925) + Pungnaptoseong Earthen Fortress (풍납토성) (a Baekje (백제) capital city ruin, Historic Site No. 11) + Cheonho Hangang Park (천호 한강공원) · Gwangnaru (광나루) (eastern Hangang River (한강)) + Gangdong I-Park The River Mall (강동아이파크더리버몰) · Cheonho Rodeo Street (천호 로데오거리) (Gangdong's (강동) youth hotspot) all in one compact area. It offers a deep dive into Korean prehistoric and Baekje (백제) history, the eastern Hangang River (한강), and a foreign family-friendly experience.
