Gangnam Hidden Restaurants: 6 Picks for Foreigners

> 6 hidden gems in Gangnam (강남), Yeoksam (역삼), Nonhyeon (논현), and Dogok (도곡) perfect for foreigners — based on Reddit signals + deep dive into uncovered spots.

Gangnam (강남) is a hub for foreign business and luxury personas. The restaurant cluster shows very strong Reddit signals (162), but 6 out of 10 establishments are currently uncovered by content. This guide curates these 6 uncovered spots plus other hidden gems.

1. 6 Uncovered Spots (Topic Gap Anchor)

Bryan's Coffee Dogok (브라이언스커피 도곡)

  • Address: Dogok-dong (도곡동), Gangnam-gu (강남구)
  • Signature: Specialty coffee + casual Western cuisine
  • Recommended for: Foreign regulars in Dogok (도곡) and Maebong (매봉). Perfect for weekend brunch.

Crestown Nonhyeon Customs Intersection Branch (크레스타운 논현세관사거리점)

  • Address: Nonhyeon (논현) & Customs Intersection
  • Signature: Multinational casual dining
  • Recommended for: Foreign business lunches. High possibility of English menus.

Triode Gangnam (트리오드)

  • Address: Main Gangnam (강남) area
  • Signature: Modern concept dining
  • Recommended for: Instagrammable visuals ★★★. Foreigner dates.

Salon de la Pose (살롱드라뽀)

  • Address: Sinsa (신사), Gangnam-gu (강남구)
  • Signature: French bistro
  • Recommended for: Foreigner dates. Wine pairing.

Urban Plates Gangnam (어반플레이트 강남)

  • Address: Yeoksam (역삼), Gangnam-gu (강남구)
  • Signature: Healthy bowls & casual Western cuisine
  • Recommended for: Foreign fitness enthusiasts & workationers. English menus available.

Salt Gangnam (솔트)

  • Address: Main Gangnam (강남) area
  • Signature: Casual fine dining
  • Recommended for: Foreign business gatherings.

2. Foreigner-Friendly Tips

  • Top Tier: Urban Plates (어반플레이트), Salt (솔트), Crestown (크레스타운) (100% English)
  • Mid-High Tier: Bryan's Coffee (브라이언스커피), Triode (트리오드), Salon de la Pose (살롱드라뽀)
  • All Gangnam (강남) Fit: Standard English service.

Area-Specific Itineraries

  • Dogok (도곡) & Maebong (매봉): Bryan's Coffee (브라이언스커피) → (Stroll in Dogok (도곡))
  • Nonhyeon (논현) & Sinsa (신사): Salon de la Pose (살롱드라뽀) → Crestown (크레스타운) (Dinner)
  • Yeoksam (역삼) & Gangnam (강남): Urban Plates (어반플레이트) → Salt (솔트) → Triode (트리오드) (Instagrammable spots)

Price Range

  • Brunch: ₩18,000~28,000 (~$13-20 / ~95-150 RMB / ~1,900-3,000 JPY)
  • Dining: ₩35,000~60,000 (~$25-45 / ~185-320 RMB / ~3,700-6,400 JPY)
  • Fine Dining: ₩55,000~120,000 (~$40-90 / ~290-640 RMB / ~5,800-12,800 JPY)
  • Reservations: Catchtable (캐치테이블) & OpenTable are foreigner-friendly.

Business Meeting Fit

  • Formal Meetings: Salt (솔트) & Crestown (크레스타운) (private rooms available)
  • Casual: Bryan's Coffee (브라이언스커피) & Urban Plates (어반플레이트)
  • Dates: Salon de la Pose (살롱드라뽀) & Triode (트리오드)
