Why Line 4 — the Line Connecting Shopping, Markets, and the Arrival Gateway

If Line 2 (#1241) is youth and Line 3 (#1242) is tradition, then Line 4 (the sky-blue line) is the line of shopping, markets, and the arrival gateway. Myeongdong, Namdaemun Market (남대문시장), Dongdaemun — the big three of foreigner shopping — are all on Line 4. On top of that, it passes through Seoul Station, the arrival gateway (Airport Railroad · KTX), and connects on to the culture of the National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) (Ichon) and Daehangno (Hyehwa). Line 4 is often the very first line you ride after arriving in Korea.

This guide organizes the 8 key tourism stations of Line 4, station by station. It pairs Myeongdong and Dongdaemun shopping landmarks (linked to existing deep dives) with restaurants drawn from accumulated data via per-station nearest-neighbor mapping. This is the third installment in the subway line-by-line series.

5 Line Tips — Get 200% Out of Line 4

Tip 1. Line 4 is the 'shopping · gateway line'

Myeongdong · Namdaemun · Dongdaemun = the big three of foreigner shopping. Cosmetics, fashion, markets, and late-night shopping all on one line. Seoul Station is the first arrival gateway.

Tip 2. Seoul Station = arrival one-stop

Incheon Airport → Airport Railroad → Seoul Station. Sort out luggage delivery (#1232) · currency exchange · SIM (#414) at Seoul Station, then start your trip.

Tip 3. Don't miss the free museums and exhibitions

The War Memorial of Korea (Samgakji), National Museum of Korea, and National Hangeul Museum (Ichon) are all free. Fits rainy days and culture buffs (#1240).

Tip 4. Dongdaemun is best at night

The Dongdaemun shopping malls (Doota · APM) stay open until dawn. DDP's night lighting and late-night shopping are Dongdaemun's charm.

Tip 5. With a single transit card

One T-money card covers Line 4, transfers, and buses. Buy and top up at Seoul Station convenience stores or Airport Railroad stations.

FAQ 6 — Common Foreigner Questions About Line 4

Q1. How is Line 4 different from the other lines?

  • Line 4: shopping · markets · arrival gateway (Myeongdong · Namdaemun · Dongdaemun · Seoul Station). Line 2 (#1241): youth · food. Line 3 (#1242): palaces · hanok · tradition.

Q2. Which stations are for shopping?

  • Myeongdong (cosmetics · fashion) · Hoehyeon (Namdaemun Market) · Dongdaemun History & Culture Park (DDP · late-night malls). Daytime Myeongdong through nighttime Dongdaemun.

Q3. What do you do at Seoul Station?

  • Arrival gateway (Airport Railroad · KTX). Seoullo 7017 · Culture Station Seoul 284 · Lotte Mart. Luggage delivery · currency exchange · SIM one-stop (#1232 · #414).

Q4. Where can I go for free?

  • War Memorial of Korea (Samgakji) · National Museum of Korea · National Hangeul Museum (Ichon) · Naksan Park (Hyehwa) are all free.

Q5. Is the restaurant information accurate?

  • It is based on a Naver Map crawl, so it is for reference only. Re-confirming hours and location with a map app before visiting is a must (closures and changes are possible).

Q6. What are the day courses?

  • Shopping (Myeongdong → Hoehyeon → Dongdaemun) / arrival · culture (Seoul Station → Samgakji → Ichon) / tradition · Daehangno (Dongdaemun → Hyehwa). See the three courses above.
