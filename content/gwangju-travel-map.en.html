⚠ Gwangju 3 mistakes

1. "Gwangju = 5·180,000" — Recognition of Arts/Biennale : Only democratization is recognized. In reality, it is Gwangju Biennale + City Museum of Art + Modern Culture in Yangnim-dong No. 1 Korean Arts Center.

2. "Mudeungsan = hiking only" — Lake and bicycle not recognized: Only hiking is recognized. In reality, it is Gwangjuho Lake, Jeungsimsa Temple, and Yaksasa Temple, a natural full experience in the city.

3. "Gourmet food = only tteokgalbi" — Meat pancake and skate not recognized: Only tteokgalbi is recognized. In reality, Yuk Jeon, Skate Samhap, Songjeong Tteokgalbi Gwangju Gourmet Pool.
