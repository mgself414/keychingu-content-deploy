⚠ 3 Banchan Mistakes

1. "Banchan = ordinary Korean food only" — missing head-family, noble-household, and royal court cuisine: The ordinary-only assumption. In reality, there are 4 lineages — jongga head-family (Andong · Jeonju) · banga noble-household (Joseon aristocracy) · royal court (Joseon, #484) · temple food (#453 · #463) — making the full experience across all 11 Korean food subcategories.

2. "Temple food = plain vegetarian" — missing Netflix Chef's Table: The plain-vegetarian assumption. In reality, it is a global celebrity-chef cluster — Jingwansa National Intangible Cultural Heritage · Seonjae's Ven. Jeong Kwan on Netflix Chef's Table · Haeinsa fitting the UNESCO Tripitaka Koreana.

3. "Banchan = restaurant side dishes only" — missing the banchan-shop cluster: The side-dish-only assumption. In reality, Gwangjang banchan shops · Tongin ₩5,000 (~$3.75) dosirak · Myeongdong English-menu banchan dosirak form a value-for-money pool beloved by foreign visitors.
