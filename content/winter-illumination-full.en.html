🎯 Why a Winter Illumination & Festival Full Guide?

Korean winter (December to February) = peak season for inbound tourists + a frequent backdrop in K-dramas. This guide is a follow-up to #90 Winter Illumination TOP 8, consolidating winter spots by area and theme. It's a full package for New Year, Dongji (동지), Christmas, and Seollal (설날).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Tourists

1. "Winter = Indoors Only Because It's Cold": Korean winter temperatures typically range from -10°C to 0°C, but it offers a full package of illuminations, festivals, skiing, hot springs, and jjimjilbang (찜질방 - Korean bathhouse and spa). Dress appropriately (heat-tech, long coat, gloves, hat) for Instagram-worthy moments.

2. "Illuminations = Everywhere": Korea's top illumination spots are the Seoul Lantern Festival (서울빛초롱축제) (Cheonggyecheon Stream (청계천) November-December) + Lotte World (롯데월드) (December-February) + DDP Night LED + Seoul Forest (서울숲) & Gwanghwamun Square (광화문 광장) Christmas Tree. These are the four illumination highlights.

3. "Bosingak (보신각) Bell-Ringing = Can Skip It": The Bosingak (보신각) bell-ringing on December 31st from 23:30 (11:30 PM) to 24:00 (12:00 AM) is Korea's premier New Year event + free entry for tourists + televised broadcast. Gwanghwamun Square (광화문 광장) and downtown Seoul will be packed with tens of thousands of people.

① Illumination Hub (4)

1. Seoul Lantern Festival (서울빛초롱축제) ★ Cheonggyecheon Stream (청계천)

  • Address: Cheonggye Plaza (청계광장) → Dongdaemun (동대문) 5.8km (#172)
  • Tourist ★: ◎◎ — Korea's #1 winter illumination festival
  • Period: Annually from the first week of November to mid-December (approx. 1 month)
  • Signature: Traditional Korean lanterns · LED lights + 5.8km night stroll along Cheonggyecheon Stream (청계천)
  • Admission: Free

2. Lotte World (롯데월드) Illumination

  • Address: Lotte World (롯데월드), Songpa-gu (송파구) (#82 Jamsil (잠실))
  • Tourist ★: ◎◎ — Top choice for families and tourists with children
  • Period: December to New Year (approx. 2 months)
  • Signature: Korea's #1 urban theme park illumination

3. DDP Night LED (Limited Winter Season)

  • Address: DDP, Jung-gu (중구) (#172·#224)
  • Tourist ★: ◎ — 45,000 LED exterior wall · Limited winter season patterns
  • Admission: Free

4. Seoul Forest (서울숲) Illumination + Gwanghwamun Square (광화문 광장) Christmas Tree

  • Address: Seoul Forest (서울숲) (#01·#205) + Gwanghwamun Square (광화문 광장)
  • Tourist ★: ◎ — Top urban winter Instagram spot
  • Period: First week of December to end of December

② Indoor Experiences (3)

5. Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원) Greenhouse ★ Korea's Largest Botanic Park

  • Address: 161 Magokdong-ro (마곡동로), Gangseo-gu (강서구) (#175·#205)
  • Tourist ★: ◎◎ — 500,000 pyeong (approx. 400 acres) · 5 plant museums (Tropical & Mediterranean Halls · Greenhouse) + warm indoor space in winter
  • Admission Fee: ₩5,000 (~$4 / ~20 RMB / ~500 JPY)
  • Period: Top choice for December-February

6. N Seoul Tower (N서울타워)

  • Address: Namsan Park (남산공원), Yongsan (용산) (#13·#224)
  • Tourist ★: ◎◎ — 265m · Top winter night view + cable car
  • Period: Year-round + Top winter Instagram spot

7. Lotte Tower (롯데타워) Seoul Sky

  • Address: Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰), Songpa-gu (송파구) (#82·#224)
  • Tourist ★: ◎◎ — 555m · 123rd floor · Top winter night view
  • Period: Year-round + Winter sunset ±30 minutes

③ Korean Tradition·Festivals (3)

8. New Year Bosingak (보신각) Bell-Ringing ★ December 31st Midnight

  • Address: Bosingak (보신각), Jongno-gu (종로구) (Jonggak Station (종각역) Line 1)
  • Tourist ★: ◎◎ — Korea's #1 New Year event
  • Period: Annually on December 31st from 23:30 (11:30 PM) to 24:00 (12:00 AM) (Televised broadcast)
  • Signature: 33 bell rings + massive crowds at Gwanghwamun Square (광화문 광장) + countdown

9. Seollal (설날) (Lunar New Year's Day)

  • Tourist ★: ◎ — Traditional Korean New Year
  • Signature: Tteokguk (떡국 - Korean rice cake soup) (#200) + Sebae (세배 - traditional Korean bow) + Hanbok (한복 - traditional Korean attire) + Yunnori (윷놀이 - traditional Korean board game) + Traditional Korean dish = one year older
  • Events: Palaces + Hanok Villages (한옥마을 - traditional Korean house villages) + Tourist-friendly traditional Korean holiday experiences

10. Dongji (동지) (December 22nd)

  • Tourist ★: ◎ — Traditional Korean Winter Solstice
  • Signature: Patjuk (팥죽 - red bean porridge) + Traditional Korean Lunar Winter Solstice (longest night of the year) rituals
  • Restaurants: Traditional Korean full-course meals · #234 + Temple food at Jingwansa Temple (진관사) (#182)

④ Outskirts Activities (2 + 4 Additional)

11. Outskirts Ski Resorts (Gangwon-do (강원도))

  • Tourist ★: ◎◎ — Top choice for ski and snowboard enthusiasts
  • Period: December-February

  • 17:00 (5:00 PM) Gwanghwamun Square (광화문 광장) Christmas Tree → 19:00 (7:00 PM) Seoul Lantern Festival (서울빛초롱축제) Cheonggyecheon Stream (청계천) → 22:00 (10:00 PM) DDP Night LED

B. Family·Children Winter Full Day

  • 10:00 (10:00 AM) Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원) Greenhouse (#175) → 13:00 (1:00 PM) Children's Grand Park (어린이대공원) (#178) → 16:00 (4:00 PM) Lotte World (롯데월드) Illumination (#82)

C. Night View·Landmark Winter

  • 17:00 (5:00 PM) N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) sunset → 19:00 (7:00 PM) Eungbongsan Mountain (응봉산) night view (#51·#189) → 21:00 (9:00 PM) Lotte Tower (롯데타워) Seoul Sky → 22:30 (10:30 PM) DDP

D. Outskirts Ski 1-Night, 2-Day Trip
