⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Winter = cold, so only indoors": -10~0°C is the standard for Korean winters, but full set of illumination, festival, skiing, hot spring, and sauna. Appropriate attire (heattech, long coat, gloves, hat) + #1 on Instagram.

2. "Illumination = Everywhere": No. 1 illumination in Korea = Seoul Lantern Festival (Cheonggyecheon, November~December) + Lotte World (December~February) + DDP Night LED + Seoul Forest·Gwanghwamun Christmas Tree. Illumination 4 result.

3. "Bosingak bell ringing = You can't go": December 31, 23:30~24:00 Bosingak bell ringing = Korea's No. 1 New Year's event + Free entry for foreigners + TV broadcast. Crowd explosion at Gwanghwamun Square and downtown Seoul (tens of thousands of people).

① Illumination No. 1 (4)

1. Seoul Lantern Festival ★ Cheonggyecheon

  • Address: Cheonggye Plaza → Dongdaemun 5.8km (#172)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Korea’s No. 1 Winter Illumination Festival
  • Period: Every year from the first week of November to mid-December (about 1 month)
  • Signature: Authentic Korean traditional lantern·LED + 5.8km night walk along Cheonggyecheon
  • Admission: Free

2. Lotte World Illumination

  • Address: Lotte World, Songpa-gu (#82 Jamsil)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Family and foreign children 1st priority
  • Period: December - New Year (about 2 months)
  • Signature: Korea’s No. 1 urban theme park illumination

3. DDP night LED (winter season only)

  • Address: Jung-gu DDP (#172·#224)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — 45,000 LED exterior wall/winter season limited pattern
  • Admission: Free

4. Seoul Forest Illumination + Gwanghwamun Square Christmas Tree

  • Address: Seoul Forest (#01·#205) + Gwanghwamun Square
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — No. 1 on winter Instagram in the city
  • When: First to last week of December

② Indoor experience (3)

5. Seoul Botanical Garden Greenhouse ★ Korea’s largest botanical garden

  • Address: 161 Magokdong-ro, Gangseo-gu (#175·#205)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — 500,000 pyeong, 5 plant museums (tropical, Mediterranean museum, greenhouse) + Warm interior in winter
  • Admission fee: 5,000 won
  • Period: December - February 1st place

6. N Seoul Tower

  • Address: Yongsan Namsan Park (#13·#224)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — 265m, 1st place for winter night view + cable car
  • Time: All year round + Winter #1 on Instagram

7. Lotte Tower Seoul Sky

  • Address: Lotte World Mall, Songpa-gu (#82·#224)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — 555m, 123rd floor, 1st place for winter night view
  • Time of year: All year round + ±30 minutes before sunset in winter

③ Korean Traditions/Festivals (3)

8. New Year’s Bosingak bell ringing ★ Midnight, December 31

  • Address: Bosingak, Jongno-gu (Line 1 Jonggak Station)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Korea’s No. 1 New Year’s event
  • When: December 31st every year, 23:30~24:00 (TV broadcast)
  • Signature: Ringing the bell 33 + Gwanghwamun Square crowd explosion + Countdown

9. Lunar New Year (January 1st in the lunar calendar)

  • Foreigners ★: ◎ — Korean Orthodox New Year
  • Signature: Tteokguk (#200) + Saebae + Hanbok + Yutnori + A bowl of authentic Korean food = One year older
  • Event: Palace + Hanok Village + Foreigner-Friendly Korean Authentic Holiday Experience

10. Comrade (December 22)

  • Signature: Red bean porridge + Korean traditional lunar solstice (longest night) ritual
