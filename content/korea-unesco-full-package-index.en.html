🗺 5 Segment Itinerary Recommendations for Foreigners

Segment 1 — Introduction (3 Nights)

  • Seoul (서울) + Gyeongju (경주) Full Set: Jongmyo Shrine (종묘)·Changdeokgung Palace (창덕궁)·Bulguksa Temple (불국사)·Seokguram Grotto (석굴암) = 4 UNESCO Signa
  • Budget: ₩500,000~800,000 per person (~$360-580 / ~2,600-4,200 RMB / ~53,000-85,000 JPY)

Segment 2 — Mania (1 Week)

  • Seoul (서울) + Gyeongju (경주) + Andong (안동) + Gongju (공주)·Buyeo (부여) = 7 Signa
  • Budget: ₩1,200,000~2,000,000 per person (~$870-1,450 / ~6,300-10,500 RMB / ~128,000-213,000 JPY)

Segment 3 — Global Mania (2 Weeks)

  • Nationwide UNESCO Full Set + Intangible Cultural Heritage Seasons: 15+ Signa
  • Budget: ₩2,500,000~4,500,000 per person (~$1,800-3,250 / ~13,000-23,500 RMB / ~266,000-480,000 JPY)

Segment 4 — Seasonal Mania (Mania·By Season)

  • Spring: Yeon Deung Hoe (연등회) (#1061·April Mania Matrix #1)
  • Summer: Gangneung Danoje Festival (강릉 단오제) (#1065·June)
  • Autumn: BIFF + Baekje Cultural Festival (백제문화제) (October) + Intangible Cultural Heritage Matrix
  • Winter: Kimjang (김장) (#751) + Gangwon (강원) Ice Fishing (빙어)·Trout (송어) Matrix

Segment 5 — Nature Mania

  • Jeju Volcanic Island and Lava Tubes (제주 화산섬·용암동굴) (#1057·#1058)·Getbol, Korean Tidal Flats (한국의 갯벌)·Seowon, Korean Neo-Confucian Academies (한국의 서원) + Sansa, Buddhist Mountain Monasteries (산사)
