🎯 Why a Full Guide to Korean Vintage & Second-hand Shops?

Korean vintage and second-hand items are a top priority for MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) tourists and Instagrammers, offering a deep dive into 1960s-80s Korean fashion and accessories. This guide integrates previously scattered information from #49 Dongmyo Guje (동묘 구제), #50 Gwangjang Vintage (광장 vintage), #156 Yeonhui (연희), #01 Seongsu (성수), and #138 Hongdae Vintage Area (홍대 vintage area) into one comprehensive resource.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Vintage = Dongmyo (동묘) only": Korean vintage has 4 pillars — Market Vintage (Dongmyo (동묘) · Gwangjang 2F (광장 2층) · Pungmul (풍물)) + MZ Hot Zones (Yeonhui (연희) · Hongdae (홍대) · Seongsu (성수) · Itaewon (이태원) · Ikseon (익선)) + Second-hand Books & Apps (Aladdin (알라딘) · Bungaejangteo (번개장터)) + Wholesale & Outlets (Gangnam Terminal (강남터미널) · Gasan (가산)).

2. "Dongmyo (동묘) = too big": Dongmyo (동묘) is directly connected to Dongmyoap Station (동묘앞역) on Subway Line 9, 1, and 6 and features a 1km vintage street. It's a 30-minute walk. For Itaewon LCT (이태원 LCT) and Yeonhui TTA (연희 티티에이), we recommend combining it with a deep dive into a single store.

3. "Second-hand = untrustworthy": The Korean second-hand market is reliable with Bungaejangteo (번개장터) · Junggonara (중고나라) apps (Korea's #1 second-hand trading platforms) + Aladdin (알라딘) (books) · Outlets (chain guarantee). Foreigner-friendly for price negotiation.
