⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Vintage = Dongmyo Bay": 4th axis of Korean vintage — Market Vintage (Dongmyo, 2nd floor of the square, Pungmul) + MZ Hot Zone (Yeonhui, Hongdae, Seongsu, Itaewon, Ikseon) + Used Book·App (Aladdin·Beongae Market) + Wholesale·Outlet (Gangnam Terminal, Gasan).

2. "Dongmyo = too big": Dongmyo is directly connected to Dongmyo Station on Lines 9, 1, and 6 + 1km vintage street. 30-minute walking course. Itaewon LCT·Yeonhee TTA is recommended as a deep dive combination in one store.

3. "Used goods = Can't trust": Korean second-hand market = Bungae Market·Junggonara app (Korea's No. 1 second-hand transaction platform) + Aladdin (book)·Outlet (chain guarantee). Foreigner price negotiation friendly.
