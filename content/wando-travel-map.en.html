Wando Travel Map — Full Meta Guide to Cheongsando (청산도) & Dadohae (다도해) in Wando (완도) for Foreign Tourists: 12 Signatures

ISSUE 03 · #427 · 2026-05-26

🎯 Why "Wando Travel Map" Meta Guide?

Visiting Wando (완도) as a foreign tourist — KTX transfer via Gwangju (광주) or Suncheon (순천) takes about 5 hours. Key highlights: (1) Cheongsando (청산도) (Korea's #1 Slow City since 2007, part of the International Slow City Network) (2) Bogildo (보길도) (Buyongdong (부용동) by Gosan Yun Seon-do (고산 윤선도)) (3) Wando Arboretum (완도수목원) & Marine Healing (해양치유) (4) Dadohaehaesang National Park (다도해 해상국립공원) + Sunrise (5) Wando (완도) Abalone (전복), Sea Mustard (미역), & Kelp (다시마) (Korea's #1).

This guide presents Wando's (완도) 4 Pillars and 12 Signatures.
