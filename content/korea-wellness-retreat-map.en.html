Korea Wellness Retreat Guide — 7 Templestay, Hanbang and Meditation Spots (Bongeunsa, Geumsunsa, Myogaksa, Seoul Hanbang Promotion Center, Yeoyongguk, Museum SAN, Healience Sun Village) (Official Values, October 2026)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

You are in the middle of a forest of Gangnam towers, yet one gate away a cup of tea and a cushion are waiting — Bongeunsa (Bongeunsa / 봉은사) keeps its Templestay hall open every Tuesday and invites you to simply come, sit, and go, even if you know nothing about Buddhism (Templestay official site). This guide is for travelers who want a few days to rest body and mind. There are three tracks: Templestay (3 temples in Seoul bookable in foreign languages), Hanbang (traditional Korean medicine: a herbal-medicine museum with foot baths, and a Hanbang spa — 2 places), and meditation and forest stays (2 places in Gangwon). For Templestay we use the official booking site templestay.com; for the rest we picked only places listed in the "88 Excellent Wellness Tourism Destinations" of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Korea Tourism Organization. Where a price is not on an official page, we say so.

🪷 Templestay — 3 Places in Seoul

The Templestay official site (templestay.com) labels all three temples "Temple for foreigner" and has an English booking screen. For general inquiries, contact the Templestay Information Center (56 Ujeongguk-ro (Ujeongguk-ro / 우정국로), Jongno-gu, Seoul, 4th floor, 02-2031-2000).

  • Bongeunsa (Bongeunsa / 봉은사) — 531 Bongeunsa-ro (Bongeunsa-ro / 봉은사로), Gangnam-gu, Seoul, 02-3218-4846. A temple founded in 794 in the Silla era. English programs (Templestay official site):
  • BONG-PASS — Every Tuesday 10:00–16:00, come and go freely in the Templestay hall for tea, meditation and rest (except special event days, posted for 2026-03-01 to 12-31)
  • Regular Templestay [Sat–Sun] — 2 days, 1 night (posted through 2026-12-27)
  • Temple tour & Tea [Thu] · Buddhist Culture [Thu] — day programs (through 2026-12-24)
  • Twilight Resonance — Sunday evening bell-ringing and evening service experience (through 2026-12-27)
  • Five Elements Yin Yoga Night — a yin yoga program, posting period 2026-07-24 to 10-23. We could not confirm the next session
  • Geumsunsa (Geumsunsa / 금선사) — 196-2 Gugi-dong (Gugi-dong / 구기동), Jongno-gu, Seoul, 02-395-9955, quick inquiries on WhatsApp +82 10-2685-9913. A temple about 600 years old at the foot of Bukhansan National Park. Relaxational Templestay (2 days, 1 night) and a group-only day program (11:00–14:00, group inquiries on Fri and Sat). It lists a personal tumbler, toiletries, towel, spare clothes and sneakers as things to bring (Templestay official site)
  • Myogaksa (Myogaksa / 묘각사) — 31 Jongno 63-ga-gil (Jongno 63-ga-gil / 종로63가길), Jongno-gu, Seoul, 02-763-3109. It runs a day program "Where is your mind?" (10:00–14:00) and a 2-day-1-night program, and says booking dates usually open one month ahead (Templestay official site)
  • Price: None of the three temples shows a price in its program list — pick a date and check on the booking screen
  • If you want a mountain temple outside Seoul, Daeheungsa (Daeheungsa / 대흥사) in Haenam and Beomeosa (Beomeosa / 범어사) in Busan are also in the 88 (88 Excellent Wellness Tourism Destinations, VisitKorea). We did not open the Templestay schedules of those two temples this time

🌿 Hanbang — 2 Places in Seoul

  • Seoul Hanbang Promotion Center (Seoul Hanbang Jinheung Center / 서울한방진흥센터) — 26 Yangnyeongjungang-ro (Yangnyeongjungang-ro / 약령중앙로), Dongdaemun-gu, Seoul (VisitKorea). About 5 minutes on foot from Exit 2 of Jegi-dong Station (Jegi-dong Station / 제기동역), Subway Line 1. March–October 10:00–18:00, November–February 10:00–17:00, closed every Monday (Seoul City "Seoul in My Hands", 2026-10-12). It has the Seoul Yangnyeongsi Museum of Oriental Medicine (Seoul Yangnyeongsi Hanuiyak Bakmulgwan / 서울약령시한의약박물관) on the 2nd floor, an outdoor-courtyard herbal foot bath, and the Bojewon (Bojewon / 보제원) clinic. The foot bath sells out on some days, so a Seoul City article says it is better to go early on weekends. It is a Hanbang cultural complex topped with a hanok roof, built on the site of Bojewon, a Joseon-era relief institution (VisitKorea). We could not confirm an official price for the experiences
  • Yeoyongguk Hanbang Spa (Yeoyongguk Hanbang Seupa / 여용국 한방스파) — 60 Seokchonhosu-ro 12-gil (Seokchonhosu-ro 12-gil / 석촌호수로12길), 2nd floor, Songpa-gu, Seoul (VisitKorea). It first analyzes your constitution and skin condition, then tailors Hanbang ingredients, aroma and massage. The official site has English and Japanese screens (Yeoyongguk official site). We could not confirm opening hours or prices on the official page — ask on the booking screen or by phone
  • Clinic treatment (acupuncture, herbal medicine) is medicine, not wellness. If treatment is your goal, see Foreigner Korea Hanbang Korean Medicine Top 12 Deep Dive

🌲 Meditation and Forest Stays — 2 Places in Gangwon

  • Museum SAN Meditation Hall (Museum SAN Myeongsangwan / 뮤지엄산 명상관) — 260 Oak Valley 2-gil (Oak Valley 2-gil / 오크밸리2길), Jijeong-myeon, Wonju-si, Gangwon (VisitKorea). Inside the museum designed by Tadao Ando and opened in May 2013, the meditation hall next to the Stone Garden offers meditation focused on aroma scent and the sound of singing bowls (VisitKorea). We could not confirm session times or fees because the official site (museumsan.org) is rendered only by script
  • Healience Sun Village (Hilieonseu Seonmaeul / 힐리언스 선마을) — 122 Jongjasan-gil (Jongjasan-gil / 종자산길), Hongcheon-gun, Gangwon (VisitKorea). A well-aging healing resort co-created by psychiatrist Dr. Si-hyung Lee, with 92 rooms, a spa, a jjimjilbang (Korean sauna), a therapy center and a red-clay path halfway up Mt. Jongjasan. There are also rooms that allow pets (VisitKorea). We could not confirm rates or the program schedule

🧰 Wellness Practicalities — Solo, Booking, Payment, Transport

  • Solo: Templestay is booked individually by default. Programs that are group-only, like Geumsunsa's day program, carry "Group" in the title (Templestay official site)
  • Booking: On the English screen of templestay.com you pick the date first, then the program. Myogaksa opens booking dates usually one month ahead. The foot bath at Seoul Hanbang Promotion Center sells out on the day on some days (Seoul City)
  • Clothes and things to bring: Per Geumsunsa's guide, a tumbler, toiletries, towel, spare clothes and sneakers (Templestay official site). If it is your first time, read the Templestay Beginner's Guide
  • Meals: At Templestay you share the temple meal (gongyang / 공양). Balwoo gongyang (balu gongyang / 발우공양) is a traditional meal etiquette in which you leave no food behind (Templestay official site). Write allergies and dietary requests in the booking beforehand, and for a card to show, see Less Spicy, Vegan, Halal: The No-Panic Manual — Dietary Requests You Just Show
  • Payment: Foreigner Korea Payment System Deep Dive
  • Getting around: The 3 Seoul temples and the 2 Hanbang places are reached by subway — Choosing Your Korea Transit Card: The No-Panic Manual — T-money, WOWPASS, or Climate Card. For Wonju and Hongcheon, see Korean Express & Intercity Buses: The No-Panic Manual — Knowing They Use Different Apps Is Half the Battle

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Templestay is only for Buddhists" — Bongeunsa says you do not need to know Buddhism or how to meditate (Templestay official site)

2. "Templestay always means sleeping over" — There are day programs, such as Bongeunsa's Thursday day program and Tuesday BONG-PASS, and Myogaksa's 10:00–14:00 (Templestay official site)

3. "On a Monday I can see everything at the herbal medicine market" — Seoul Hanbang Promotion Center is closed on Mondays (Seoul City)

4. "Hanbang spa = Hanbang clinic treatment" — Yeoyongguk is a constitution-tailored beauty and care spa. Treatment is received separately at a Hanbang clinic
