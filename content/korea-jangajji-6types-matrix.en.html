Korean Jangajji (장아찌) 6-Type Overview — From Cucumber, Radish & Chili to Garlic, Perilla Leaf & Butterbur: The Complete Fermented Side-Dish Guide for Foreign Visitors

Subtitle: A foreign visitor's guide to Korea's 6 jangajji types — cucumber, radish, chili pepper, garlic, perilla leaf, butterbur — plus hanjeongsik (한정식, Korean course-meal) and home-cooked fermented banchan (반찬, free Korean side dishes)

ISSUE 03 · #867 · 2026-06-09

🎯 Why a Korean Jangajji Overview

The foreign visitor's pain point with Korean jangajji = "I thought kimchi (김치) was the only fermented food in Korea." Jangajji is actually the #1 hanjeongsik banchan + a home-cooking staple + a fermentation-fan favorite. Even under the same label "Korean pickle," Oi-jangajji (오이장아찌, summer signature) · Mu-jangajji·Danmuji (무장아찌·단무지, Korea's answer to Japan's takuan) · Gochu-jangajji (고추장아찌, spicy, a nofo (노포, restaurant in business 30+ years) favorite) · Maneul-jangajji (마늘장아찌, aged a full year) · Kkaennip-jangajji (깻잎장아찌, hanjeongsik classic) · Meowi-jangajji (머위장아찌, spring-only nofo staple) each have their own ingredients, season, and fan base. Foreign visitors typically don't realize that (a) kimchi and jangajji are different foods, (b) hanjeongsik banchan come with unlimited free refills, (c) some jangajji age for a full year, or (d) Korean danmuji and Japan's takuan aren't the same pickle. This guide covers a 6-type overview + hanjeongsik banchan + a starter priority list for foreign visitors.

🥒 6-Type Deep Dive

🥒 Oi-jangajji (오이장아찌) — #1 Starter Pick

  • Signature: summer #1 · home cooking · banchan (반찬, free Korean side dishes) + hanjeongsik
  • Ingredients: cucumber + soy sauce, sugar, vinegar & garlic
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (#1 starter pick; cucumber is globally familiar)
  • Aging time: 1–7 days (quick pickle)
  • Recommended venues:
  • Hanjeongsik restaurants (#837) — the #1 banchan
  • Gajeongsik baekban (가정식 백반, home-style set meal) spots (#854) — great value
  • MZ (MZ, Korea's Gen MZ trendsetters) cafe cluster
  • Price: included with hanjeongsik banchan / 1 pack at a supermarket = ₩3,000–6,000 (~$2–4)

🥕 Mu-jangajji·Danmuji (무장아찌·단무지) — Globally Familiar #1

  • Signature: year-round · full hanjeongsik banchan spread · danmuji (yellow color)
  • Ingredients: radish + soy sauce, sugar & turmeric (danmuji's yellow color)
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (danmuji is globally familiar, often via Japanese cuisine)
  • Aging time: 7–30 days
  • Recommended venues: hanjeongsik restaurants · gimbap (김밥) spots (#840) · samgyeopsal (삼겹살) restaurants · MZ cafes
  • Price: included with hanjeongsik banchan / supermarket = ₩2,000–5,000 (~$1–4)

🌶 Gochu-jangajji (고추장아찌) — Spicy Nofo Favorite

  • Signature: spicy · nofo favorite · soju (소주, Korean distilled liquor) pairing
  • Ingredients: green & red chili + soy sauce, vinegar & sugar
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★ (spicy, pairs well with soju)
  • Aging time: 7–30 days
  • Recommended venues: nofo restaurants · markets · hanjeongsik restaurants · soju bars
  • Price: market = ₩5,000–10,000/pack (~$4–7)

🧄 Maneul-jangajji (마늘장아찌) — Aged 1 Year

  • Signature: aged a full year · firm and spicy · the #1 fan favorite
  • Ingredients: garlic + soy sauce, vinegar & sugar, aged 1 year
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★ (year-long-aging favorite, popular on Instagram)
  • Aging time: 1 year (traditional) / 1 month (quick version)
  • Recommended venues: hanjeongsik restaurants · nofo restaurants · traditional Jeolla-region spots
  • Price: market = ₩8,000–20,000/pack (~$6–15)

🌿 Kkaennip-jangajji (깻잎장아찌) — Hanjeongsik Classic

  • Signature: a true hanjeongsik banchan classic · the distinct aroma of perilla leaf
  • Ingredients: perilla leaf + soy sauce, chili & garlic seasoning
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★ (hanjeongsik classic)
  • Aging time: 1–7 days
  • Recommended venues: hanjeongsik restaurants (#837) · traditional nofo spots · home cooking
  • Price: included with hanjeongsik banchan

🍃 Meowi-jangajji (머위장아찌) — Spring Nofo Pick

  • Signature: a true spring (Mar–Apr) nofo classic · favorite among nofo regulars
  • Ingredients: butterbur + soy sauce & vinegar
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★ (nofo regulars' favorite)
  • Aging time: 7–30 days
  • Recommended venues: traditional nofo hanjeongsik restaurants · countryside traditional spots
  • Price: nofo restaurants = ₩10,000–20,000/pack (~$7–15)

🍽 Hanjeongsik, Home Cooking & Market Signatures

Hanjeongsik Full Banchan Spread (#837)

  • Price: ₩8,000–15,000 per person (~$6–11) (jangajji included)

  • Namdaemun Market side-dish shops
  • Jeonju Hanok Village (전주 한옥마을, hanok (한옥, traditional Korean house) neighborhood) nofo restaurants — traditional recipes
  • Price: ₩5,000–20,000/pack (~$4–15)

  • Price: ₩3,000–10,000/pack (~$2–7)

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. "Kimchi and jangajji are the same thing": kimchi = fermented and heavily seasoned, often spicy (napa cabbage, radish) / jangajji = pickled in soy sauce or vinegar (cucumber, radish, chili) — different foods.

2. "Danmuji is a Japanese food": Korean danmuji = radish pickle + turmeric (yellow color); Japan's takuan is fermented in rice bran — a different food.

3. "Maneul-jangajji only takes a week": traditional maneul-jangajji is aged for a full year (firm, spicy, a fan favorite).

4. "Jangajji is never spicy": gochu-jangajji and kkaennip-jangajji are spicy (they contain chili).

5. "Hanjeongsik banchan cost extra": hanjeongsik banchan come with unlimited free refills (jangajji included).

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. Top starter picks for foreign visitors: oi-jangajji and mu-jangajji (globally familiar) → kkaennip-jangajji (hanjeongsik classic) → maneul-jangajji (for year-long-aging fans).

2. Instagram & vlog: a full hanjeongsik banchan spread, year-aged maneul-jangajji, and Gwangjang Market's jangajji alley.

3. MZ trend: affordable hanjeongsik banchan spreads and jangajji fusion dishes at MZ cafes.

4. Soju favorites: gochu-jangajji + maneul-jangajji (great drinking snacks).

5. Halal & Kosher: all 6 types are OK (soy sauce contains some gluten).

6. Allergies: declare soy sauce's gluten content in advance.

7. Payment & tipping: markets = cash or card / supermarkets = card OK / no tipping.

8. VAT refund: food & beverages are generally not eligible for refund.

⚠ Medical, Safety & Dietary Disclaimer

Jangajji is an everyday food, fermented and pickled in soy sauce. Pregnant women = OK. Children = OK. Declare allergies (soy sauce's gluten, chili's spiciness) in advance. If food poisoning is suspected, call ☎ 1339 (medical, English available). This guide is general information, not medical advice.

Dietary restriction fallback:

  • Halal & Kosher: all 6 types = OK (plant-based).
  • Vegetarian & vegan: all 6 types = OK (fully vegetarian and vegan).
  • Gluten-free: soy sauce contains gluten → tamari works as a substitute.
