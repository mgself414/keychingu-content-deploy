🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers 10 calligraphy halls, museums and old houses where you can see Korean calligraphy and brush culture. Places connected to Chusa Kim Jeong-hui (추사 김정희, 1786~1856), a late Joseon scholar and calligrapher, are in Gwacheon, Yesan and Jeju (they are also listed together in the FAQ below). The Insadong street in Seoul is covered in #297 Insadong Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Most places close on Mondays, but Gangam Seoyegwan in Jeonju is closed on Sundays, and the Korean Calligraphy Birim Museum in Yesan is open year-round.

📍 Seoul and Gwacheon — Seoul Calligraphy Museum and Chusa Museum

The Seoul Calligraphy Museum (2406 Nambusunhwan-ro, Seocho-gu, Seoul (서울 서초구 남부순환로 2406), Seocho-dong (서초동), ☎ 0507-1382-1656) is inside the Seoul Arts Center (예술의전당) and opened in 1988 together with the concert hall. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it holds more than 1,300 pieces, including modern and contemporary calligraphy and painting history materials such as Geunyeok Seohwasa (근역서화사) and East Asian contemporary calligraphy works, and it was upgraded to a specialized calligraphy museum in March 2002.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~19:00. Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Admission: Varies by exhibition.

The Chusa Museum (78 Chusa-ro, Gwacheon-si (과천시 추사로 78), Juam-dong (주암동), ☎ 02-2150-3650) opened on June 3, 2013. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is a place that researches, exhibits and offers experiences about the life, scholarship and art of Chusa Kim Jeong-hui. It has 3 permanent exhibition rooms (Chusa's Life on the 2nd floor, Chusa's Scholarship and Art on the 1st floor, and the Fujitsuka Donation Room (후지츠카) on the 1st basement floor) and a permanent experience room, and the restored Gwajicho-dang (과지초당) stands outdoors.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00 (last entry 17:00).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, Seollal and Chuseok holidays, and closing days set by the Gwacheon mayor.
  • Admission (individuals): Adults 2,000 won, middle and high school students and soldiers 1,000 won, elementary school students 500 won. Groups (20 or more): adults 1,000 won, middle and high school students and soldiers 500 won, elementary school students 300 won. Free for ages 6 and under and ages 65 and over.

📍 Yesan — Chusa Old House, Chusa Memorial Hall and Korean Calligraphy Birim Museum

The Chusa Kim Jeong-hui Old House (261 Chusagotaek-ro, Sinam-myeon, Yesan-gun (예산군 신암면 추사고택로 261), inquiries to the Chusa Memorial Hall ☎ 041-339-8248) is the former home of Chusa Kim Jeong-hui. According to Korea Tourism Organization data (citing the Korea Heritage Service), it is said to have been built by Kim Jeong-hui's great-grandfather Kim Han-sin (김한신), and it consists of 2 buildings, the anchae (main quarters) and the sarangchae (men's quarters).

  • Opening hours: Summer season (March~October) 09:00~18:00, winter season (November~February) 09:00~17:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, Seollal and Chuseok days.
  • Parking: Available.

The Chusa Memorial Hall (295 Chusagotaek-ro, Sinam-myeon, Yesan-gun (예산군 신암면 추사고택로 295), ☎ 041-339-8248) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, a new exhibition space opened beside the old house for the 240th anniversary of Chusa's birth. It covers the changes in Chusa's calligraphy style during his Jeju exile and Sehando (세한도), and in the experience room you can try brush calligraphy and stone rubbing. From the observation deck on the 2nd floor you can look down on the Yonggung-ri White Pine (용궁리 백송), a natural monument.

  • Opening hours: Summer season (March~October) 09:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30), winter season (November~February) 09:00~17:00 (last entry 16:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the first weekday after if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, Seollal and Chuseok days.
  • Admission: Registered as "none" in the Korea Tourism Organization information.

The Korean Calligraphy Birim Museum (7 Nokmunbaksalmi-gil, Sinyang-myeon, Yesan-gun (예산군 신양면 녹문박살미길 7), ☎ 0507-1414-8610) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, a museum that displays more than 700 works of calligraphy and the Four Gentlemen (sagunja) paintings by modern and contemporary calligraphers and painters, carved in stone and set upright. You can see seal script, clerical script, regular script, semi-cursive script, cursive script and Hangul calligraphy works.

  • Opening hours: 09:30~17:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Admission: Free.

📍 Jeju — Jeju Chusagwan

Jeju Chusagwan (44 Chusa-ro, Daejeong-eup, Seogwipo-si (서귀포시 대정읍 추사로 44), ☎ 064-710-6865) was built in December 2010 to honor Chusa Kim Jeong-hui. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it has a Chusa commemorative hall (추사기념홀) and 3 exhibition rooms, and displays Chusa's signboard calligraphy, letter calligraphy and more.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the first non-holiday day after if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, Seollal and Chuseok days.
  • Admission: Free.

📍 Jeonju, Inje and Cheonan — Calligrapher Memorial Halls

Gangam Seoyegwan (74 Jeonjucheon-dong-ro, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si (전주시 완산구 전주천동로 74), ☎ 063-285-7442) is, according to Korea Tourism Organization data (citing the official Gangam Seoyegwan website), a specialized calligraphy museum opened with the wish to give back to society the collection that the calligrapher Gangam Song Seong-yong (강암 송성용) gathered over his lifetime, and it holds more than 1,300 pieces including calligraphy and paintings, books and the Four Friends of the Study (munbangsau).

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Every Sunday, Seollal and Chuseok days.
  • Admission: Free. Parking: Free.

Yeocho Seoyegwan (154 Manhae-ro, Buk-myeon, Inje-gun (인제군 북면 만해로 154), ☎ 033-461-4081) is a specialized calligraphy museum built to honor the calligrapher Yeocho Kim Eung-hyeon (여초 김응현). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it holds more than 6,000 items including Kim Eung-hyeon's works, personal belongings and materials on calligraphy methods, and the building, designed by the architect Lee Seong-gwan (이성관), received the Excellence Award at the 2012 Korea Architecture Culture Awards.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok days.
  • Admission: Free. Parking: Free.

Chwimukheon Seoyegwan (3 Jonghaphyuyangji 1-gil, Mokcheon-eup, Dongnam-gu, Cheonan-si (천안시 동남구 목천읍 종합휴양지1길 3), inquiries to the Cultural Policy Team ☎ 041-521-5167) displays, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, the writings, archive and videos of the calligrapher Chwimukheon In Yeong-seon (취묵헌 인영선).

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, Seollal and Chuseok days. Temporary closures during exhibition preparation are announced separately.
  • Admission: Free. Parking: Free.

📍 Chuncheon — Butiyagi Museum

The Butiyagi Museum (906 Baksa-ro, Seo-myeon, Chuncheon-si (춘천시 서면 박사로 906), ☎ 033-255-0580) is a museum specializing in brushes, founded in 2017. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, on the 1st floor you can learn the history and making process of traditional brushes, and on the 2nd floor you can try making a calligraphy artwork and making your own brush, and you can also watch a master make brushes in the brush workshop. The director, Park Gyeong-su (박경수), is a holder of the Gangwon Special Self-Governing Province Intangible Heritage of Chuncheon brush making (춘천필장).

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday, Seollal and Chuseok days.
  • Admission: Free. The visit is registered as taking about 1 hour.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Only Gangam Seoyegwan is closed on Sundays — most of the other calligraphy halls and museums are closed on Mondays. The Korean Calligraphy Birim Museum is open year-round.
  2. The two Yesan sites close early in winter — the Chusa Old House and Chusa Memorial Hall are open until 17:00 from November to February (the Korean Calligraphy Birim Museum is open until 17:00 all year).
  3. Admission to the Seoul Calligraphy Museum differs by exhibition — the Korea Tourism Organization registered information says "varies by exhibition."

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where can I find hands-on programs?

A. Among the 10 places in this guide, the ones with experiences registered in the Korea Tourism Organization information are the Chusa Memorial Hall (brush calligraphy and stone rubbing), the Butiyagi Museum (making a calligraphy artwork and making a brush) and the Chusa Museum (a permanent experience room, with the experience content not registered). The place where brush calligraphy is explicitly listed is the Chusa Memorial Hall.

Q. Which places are connected to Chusa Kim Jeong-hui?

A. The Chusa Museum in Gwacheon, the Chusa Kim Jeong-hui Old House and Chusa Memorial Hall in Yesan, and Jeju Chusagwan in Seogwipo.
