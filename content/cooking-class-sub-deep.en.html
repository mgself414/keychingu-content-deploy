Seoul Korean Food Culture Experience Guide: Museum Kimchikan, Tteok Museum, Hansik Culture Space Ieum, Traditional Liquor Gallery, Bukchon Traditional Fermentation Workshop Bitdam, Chunpung Brewery, Omi Cooking Lab

Subtitle: Museum Kimchikan (10:00~18:00, ages 19 and over 5,000 won, closed Mondays) · Tteok Museum (Mon~Sat 10:00~18:00, adults 3,000 won, closed Sundays) · Hansik Culture Space Ieum (cooking class 15,000 won, closed Mondays) · Traditional Liquor Gallery (free, English tastings at 3 and 4) · Bitdam (one-day class 36,000 won, materials extra, closed Mon and Tue) · Chunpung Brewery (tour 110,000 won, ages 19 and over) · Omi Cooking Lab (class 110,000 won, reservation required) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places in Seoul where you can see Korean food culture such as kimchi, tteok (떡), jang (장) and traditional liquor, and try making it yourself. They are Museum Kimchikan (뮤지엄 김치간) in Insa-dong (인사동), the Tteok Museum (떡박물관) on Donhwamun-ro (돈화문로), Hansik Culture Space Ieum (한식문화공간 이음) and the Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리) at Bukchon-ro 18 (북촌로 18), Bitdam (빚담) in Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌한옥마을), Chunpung Brewery (춘풍양조장) in Sindang-dong (신당동), and Omi Cooking Lab (오미요리연구소) in Jegi-dong (제기동). Kimchikan experiences and Omi Cooking Lab classes require a reservation, and Traditional Liquor Gallery tastings are by internet reservation, and closed days differ from place to place. It helps to look at the table below first. Temple food experiences are in the #800 Seoul Temple Food and Templestay Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 See and Learn — Museum Kimchikan and Tteok Museum

Museum Kimchikan (뮤지엄 김치간) [Insadong-gil 35-4 (인사동길 35-4), Insadong Maru (인사동마루) main building floors 4~6, Jongno-gu, ☎ 02-2088-8531] is a food museum that introduces kimchi and kimjang (김장) culture. It first opened under the name Kimchi Museum in Pil-dong (필동), Jung-gu, and in 1987 the food company Pulmuone (풀무원) took it over and began running it. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the first kimchi museum in Korea and the only one in Seoul. The 'gan' (간) in the name comes from words meaning a space, such as sura-gan (수라간) and got-gan (곳간).

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30). Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok holidays, December 25.
  • Fee: Ages 19 and over 5,000 won, ages 8~18 3,000 won, 36 months~age 7 2,000 won. Groups of 20 or more pay 4,000 won, 2,000 won and 1,000 won respectively. Children under 36 months, those aged 65 and over, and people with disabilities and their escorts are free, and you must bring ID that can confirm age.
  • Experiences: There are paid programs It's Kimchi, Kimchi Basic and Kimchi Master, and a free program, Kimchi School (김치학교). Advance reservation required, age 6 and over. Parking: Not available.

Tteok Museum (떡박물관) [Donhwamun-ro 71 (돈화문로 71), Jongno-gu, ☎ 02-741-5447] is a museum that opened in January 2002 with a collection of more than 3,000 old kitchen items. It displays tteok by season, life-cycle ritual and method of making, and also displays the cooking tools used to make tteok. It runs experience programs where you make and taste tteok yourself.

  • Opening hours: Mon~Sat 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30). Closed days: Every Sunday, Seollal, Chuseok.
  • Fee: Adults 3,000 won, 36 months~under 19 2,000 won. Free for children under 36 months and those aged 65 and over.

📍 Bukchon-ro 18 — Hansik Culture Space Ieum and Traditional Liquor Gallery

Hansik Culture Space Ieum (한식문화공간 이음) [Bukchon-ro 18 (북촌로 18), Jongno-gu, ☎ 0507-1465-8488] is a complex cultural space run by the Hansik Promotion Institute (한식진흥원). Inside the red brick building there are an exhibition hall, a library, a cafe and an experience space. The exhibition hall shows the history of traditional jang and traditional liquor, and the Hansik library in the basement is free for anyone to read in. At the Hansik Learning Space (한식배움터) you practice everything from seasonal dishes such as tteokguk (떡국) to traditional desserts such as gotgam-danji (곶감단지) and susu-bukkumi (수수부꾸미), and you can take the finished dish home. The cooking class schedule changes every month, and reservations are made on the official website.

  • Opening hours: Tue~Sun 10:00~19:00. Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Fee: Cooking class 15,000 won. Reservation: Website.

Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리) [Bukchon-ro 18 (북촌로 18), Jongno-gu, ☎ 0507-1406-6220] is a traditional liquor promotion space created together by the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs (농림축산식품부) and the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism (문화체육관광부). It is run by experts such as traditional liquor sommeliers, and each month it changes the theme and holds an event that introduces and tastes traditional liquor. The Korea Tourism Organization's introduction says it is in 'Insa-dong', but the registered address is Bukchon-ro 18, the same as Hansik Culture Space Ieum.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~19:00. Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, the day of a traditional holiday (명절). Fee: Free.
  • Tasting: Korean at 12, 1, 2, 5, 6. English at 3, 4. Tastings are by internet reservation, and programs specialized for foreigners require advance reservation. Groups must inquire at least one month in advance.

📍 Learn and Experience — Bitdam, Chunpung Brewery and Omi Cooking Lab

Bukchon Traditional Fermentation Workshop Bitdam (북촌전통발효공방 빚담) [Bukchon-ro 12-gil 5-12 (북촌로12길 5-12), Jongno-gu, ☎ 02-766-7858] is a hanok workshop in Bukchon Hanok Village where you can experience fermentation using nuruk. There are programs such as making nuruk, making almeju (알메주), making low-salt nuruk salt and a seasonal traditional liquor experience, and a regular course where you learn nuruk theory and practice.

  • Opening hours: 11:00~17:00. Closed days: Every Monday and Tuesday.
  • Fee: One-day class 36,000 won (materials extra). It is registered that the cost of the regular course is to be inquired about separately. Parking: Not available.

Chunpung Brewery (춘풍양조장) [Dasan-ro 101-3 (다산로 101-3), Jung-gu, ☎ 1899-8876] is a brewery that makes makgeolli. It runs brewery tours and tastings, and it is registered that it introduces makgeolli to overseas tourists.

  • Opening hours: Business 11:00~19:00. Tours 11:00, 15:00, 17:00.
  • Closed days: Every Sunday and Monday. It is registered that tours on closed days are to be inquired about separately.
  • Fee: Tour A 110,000 won. Ages 19 and over. Parking: Not available.

Omi Cooking Lab (오미요리연구소) [Yangnyeongsi-ro 97-1 (약령시로 97-1), 1st floor, Dongdaemun-gu (동대문구), ☎ 010-5060-5250] is a cooking school where you learn Korean food with seasonal ingredients bought at Gyeongdong Market (경동시장), Cheongnyangni Market (청량리시장) and Yangnyeongsi Market (약령시장). You can learn Korean food that carries fermentation culture, and menus are arranged to suit vegetarian, allergy and religious needs. Classes are held in English and Chinese, and the capacity of a regular class is 8~10 people. It won the Seoul Tourism Awards (서울관광대상) in 2021.

  • Class hours: 10:00~13:00. Reservation required (online). Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: 110,000 won. Parking: Available. Credit cards: Accepted.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Some experiences need a reservation — Kimchikan experience programs and Omi Cooking Lab classes require a reservation, Traditional Liquor Gallery tastings are by internet reservation, and Ieum cooking classes can be reserved on the website.
  2. Closed days differ from place to place — Kimchikan, Ieum and the Traditional Liquor Gallery are closed on Mondays, the Tteok Museum on Sundays, Bitdam on Mondays and Tuesdays, and Chunpung Brewery on Sundays and Mondays. Omi Cooking Lab is open year-round. In addition, Kimchikan is closed on January 1, Seollal and Chuseok holidays and December 25, the Traditional Liquor Gallery on January 1 and the day of a traditional holiday (명절), and the Tteok Museum on Seollal and Chuseok.
  3. Only those aged 19 and over can join the Chunpung Brewery tour — tour times are 11:00, 15:00, 17:00.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Are there programs held in English?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, the Traditional Liquor Gallery has English tastings at 3 and 4, and Omi Cooking Lab classes are held in English and Chinese.

Q. How much do the making experiences cost at each place?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, the Hansik Culture Space Ieum cooking class is 15,000 won, the Bitdam one-day class is 36,000 won (materials extra), and Omi Cooking Lab is 110,000 won. Fees are not registered for the Kimchikan paid programs and the Tteok Museum experiences, and Kimchikan's Kimchi School is registered as a free program.
