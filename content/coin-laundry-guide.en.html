Korea's Coin Laundromats, the Zero-Panic Manual — Follow the Washing Machine Screen Exactly

Subtitle: Complete guide to 24-hour unmanned coin laundromats like WashEnjoy — why you should never confuse them with Cleantopia · the washing machine's 4-step screen flow · how to add dryer time · a feel for pricing · 12 survival words · a packing-light strategy for long-term travelers

🎯 How to Read This Manual — Screen Notation Rules

This uses the same notation as #1270, the kiosk guide. So the machine screen never feels unfamiliar the first time you stand in front of it, it writes down exactly "what you see and which button you press."

  • Buttons on the screen appear in brackets: [ Start (시작) ]
  • Screen titles and prompts appear in quotes: "koseureul seontaekhaseyo (코스를 선택하세요) — please select a course"
  • Actions you do by hand are bold: "put your laundry in the drum and close the door all the way"

① Why Korea Runs on Unmanned 24-Hour Coin Laundromats

Korean accommodations (guesthouses and Airbnbs especially) often have no washing machine, or have one but no dryer. Send laundry out through a hotel and it's common to be charged separately per shirt and to wait more than a day. Neighborhood coin laundromats, by contrast, run 24 hours — put your load in yourself, wait around, and both washing and drying are done in about an hour. The felt cost is far lower than hotel laundry service. For travelers, the question stops being "where do I send my laundry" and becomes "which machine do I use, and which button do I press" — this manual is that button.

② WashEnjoy vs. Cleantopia — Two Names You Must Never Confuse

This is the exact spot where people walk in based on the sign alone and panic — "why isn't there a single machine here?" The names sound similar, but they're completely different services.

Category WashEnjoy and other coin laundromats Cleantopia
Model Unmanned self-service — you load it and unload it yourself Drop-off chain — you hand your clothes to staff
Hours Mostly 24 hours, unstaffed Has store hours, staffed
Getting it back Right there on the spot (about an hour) Return to pick up, same day to several days later
Paying Insert coins/card directly into the machine Settle up with staff at the counter
Use it when You need laundry done today, urgently Dropping off dry cleaning, alterations, etc.
  • Most travelers are headed for WashEnjoy or another coin laundromat. If the sign says "coin" or you can see washing machines lined up through the storefront window, you've found the right place.
  • If it's a Cleantopia sign but the inside is just a counter and clothes racks with no washing machines in sight, you've walked into the wrong place — go find the unmanned store in a nearby alley instead.

③ The Master Washing Machine Flow — Follow the Screen Exactly

The sequence is the same no matter the brand.

Step Screen/Action What to Do Trap
1 Find an empty machine Pick one whose door doesn't show [ In Use (사용중) ] If the light is off but the door won't open, move to another machine
2 Load your laundry Put it in the drum and close the door all the way You don't need to add detergent or fabric softener — most machines dispense them automatically, so there's no need to buy any
3 "koseureul seontaekhaseyo (코스를 선택하세요) — please select a course" Pressing [ Standard (표준) ] is your safe default (use the heavy-duty or bedding course only for large loads) If you're not sure which course, always pick [ Standard (표준) ]
4 "gyeolje sudaneul seontaekhaseyo (결제 수단을 선택하세요) — please select a payment method" [ Coin (동전) ] or [ Card (카드) ] → no coins? break bills into 500-won coins at the coin-exchange machine on the wall Card-only and coin-only machines are mixed in, so check the screen first
5 [ Start (시작) ] Starts automatically once payment goes through, with remaining time counting down on screen On some machines, skipping the Start button means payment goes through but the machine never runs

One wash cycle usually finishes in the 30-40 minute range. When it's done, the door opens automatically, or the screen shows "wallyo (완료) — done."

④ Using the Dryer — Adding Time in 10-Minute Increments

The washer and dryer are separate machines. Move your finished laundry into an empty dryer nearby.

1. Open the dryer door and transfer your wet laundry in

2. Insert coins or a card → time is added in 10-minute blocks, roughly 500-1,000 won each

3. Add as many more coins as you want and the time extends accordingly — rather than putting it all in at once, checking the state with 5-10 minutes left and adding more then leads to fewer misfires

4. If it isn't fully dry yet, don't open the door — just add more coins and keep it running

⑤ A Feel for Pricing — What Should It Cost

Item Price Range
One wash About 3,000-6,000 won
10 minutes of drying About 500-1,000 won

The exact amount is whatever number the machine screen shows. The ranges above are just a gut check for "this looks normal" — if the screen price is way outside that range, use a different store.

Three matter most — pyojun (표준) — selecting a course, dongjeon gyohwan (동전교환) — your way out of a failed payment, and sijak (시작) — making it go.

⑦ While You Wait — Route Tips

Washing plus drying takes about an hour total. Most coin laundromats sit in commercial strips with a convenience store or cafe just a 1-2 minute walk away. Set a timer, grab a quick bite at the nearby convenience store, or sit and wait at a cafe, and the time passes without feeling wasted. Some laundromats have chairs to sit in, but the space is often cramped, so if your bags aren't too heavy, stepping out for a bit is the better move.

⑧ Strategy for Long-Term Travelers — Why to Pack Light

The simple fact that coin laundromats exist in every neighborhood changes how you should pack. Even for a trip of two weeks or more, packing just 3-4 days' worth of clothes and doing laundry mid-trip is overwhelmingly better for your carry-on's weight and bulk. A day's laundry costs just a few thousand won, so stopping by a laundromat once or twice during your trip reduces your travel burden far more than packing extra clothes would. Save nearby coin laundromat locations to your map app before checking into your accommodation, and you can slot it naturally into your itinerary.

⑨ 3 Panic Scenarios

1. You don't have coins — don't panic, just find the "dongjeon gyohwan (동전교환) — coin exchange" machine on the screen or the wall first. Most accept bills up to 10,000 won. If you can't find one, getting coins ahead of time at a nearby convenience store also works.

2. You're stuck because you don't know which course to pick — just press [ Standard (표준) ]. Unless it's an unusually large bedding item or a delicate fabric, the standard course is enough.

3. The cycle finished, but the previous person's clothes are still in there — this happens when someone washed their load but never moved it to the dryer. Don't force someone else's washer open — use a different empty machine or wait a bit. If the light shows "done" but it's been sitting untouched a long time, moving to a different machine is faster.
