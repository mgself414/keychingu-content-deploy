Herbal Tea & Yangnyeongsi Guide — Seoul Oriental Medicine Center, Gyeongdong Market Ginseng, Daegu Yangnyeongsi Museum of Oriental Medicine and Oriental Medicine Experience Town

Subtitle: Seoul Oriental Medicine Center (서울한방진흥센터) and Seoul Yangnyeongsi Museum of Oriental Medicine (서울약령시한의약박물관), March–October 10:00–18:00, adults 1,000 won · Gyeongdong Market (경동시장), 08:00–18:00, closed on alternate Sundays, varies by shop · Daegu Yangnyeongsi Museum of Oriental Medicine (대구 약령시 한의약박물관), 09:00–18:00, closed Mondays · Daegu Yangnyeongsi Oriental Medicine Experience Town (대구약령시 한방의료체험타운), 10:00–17:30 — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Herbal tea is tea made by simmering or steeping medicinal herbs. The places where you can see the herbs that go into it with your own eyes and try oriental medicine are the yangnyeongsi (약령시, herbal medicine markets) of two cities. They are the Seoul Oriental Medicine Center and Gyeongdong Market in Jegi-dong (제기동), Seoul, and the Daegu Yangnyeongsi Museum of Oriental Medicine and the Oriental Medicine Experience Town in Jung-gu, Daegu.

This guide copies only the operating details of the four places from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). The Seoul Yangnyeongsi route is covered separately in #598 Yangnyeongsi (Cheongnyangni and Gyeongdong), Daegu's Yakjeon Alley in #1002 Daegu Yangnyeongsi Herbal Medicine Street, and the kinds of medicinal tea in #682 Medicinal Tea Deep Dive.

🌿 Seoul Oriental Medicine Center and the Seoul Yangnyeongsi Museum of Oriental Medicine

Seoul Oriental Medicine Center (26 Yangnyeongjungang-ro, Dongdaemun-gu, Seoul, Jegi-dong (서울 동대문구 약령중앙로 26, 제기동), ☎ 02-969-9241) is an oriental medicine complex cultural facility inside Seoul Yangnyeongsi. On the 2nd floor is the Seoul Yangnyeongsi Museum of Oriental Medicine. The Seoul Yangnyeongsi area was the site of Bojewon, a relief institution of the Joseon period. According to the tourism organization's description, Seoul Yangnyeongsi is where 70% of the herbal medicines traded in Korea are distributed, and about a thousand oriental medicine businesses such as clinics and herbal pharmacies are gathered there.

  • Opening hours: March–October 10:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00), November–February 10:00–17:00 (last entry 16:00).
  • Closed days: January 1, Seollal, and Chuseok.
  • Admission: Adults 1,000 won, students (ages 7–18) and soldiers 500 won. Free for those under age 6 and aged 65 and over.
  • Parking: 500 won per 10 minutes.
  • Seoul Yangnyeong Market (10 Yangnyeongjungang-ro, ☎ 02-969-4793): A wholesale and retail market for herbal medicines. Opening hours differ by shop, and parking is not available.

🛒 Gyeongdong Market — Ginseng and Herb Shops

Gyeongdong Market (3 Gosanja-ro 36-gil, Dongdaemun-gu, Seoul, Jegi-dong (서울 동대문구 고산자로36길 3, 제기동), ☎ 0507-1311-8721) is a market specializing in farm products that opened in June 1960. After the Gyeongdong Building was completed in 1985, a ginseng market and herbal medicine shops appeared.

  • Opening hours: 08:00–18:00, differing by shop.
  • Closed days: Alternate Sundays, differing by shop.
  • What is sold: Farm products, fruit, medicinal herbs, ginseng, and seafood.
  • Facilities: Restrooms are available, and parking is not.

🏛 Daegu Yangnyeongsi Museum of Oriental Medicine

Daegu Yangnyeongsi Museum of Oriental Medicine (49 Dalgubeol-daero 415-gil, Jung-gu, Daegu, Namseong-ro (대구 중구 달구벌대로415길 49, 남성로), ☎ 053-253-4729) shows the history of Daegu Yangnyeongsi, more than 350 years old, and Yakjeon Alley. It exhibits about 300 items, including rare herbal medicines, oriental medicine books such as Donguibogam, and medicine-cutting blades.

  • Opening hours: 09:00–18:00, last entry 17:30.
  • Closed days: Every Monday (open if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, and the days of Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Experiences: Herbal foot bath, herbal soap making, and herbal lip balm making.
  • Parking: Available.

🧘 Daegu Yangnyeongsi Oriental Medicine Experience Town

Daegu Yangnyeongsi Oriental Medicine Experience Town (45 Jungang-daero 77-gil, Jung-gu, Daegu (대구 중구 중앙대로77길 45), ☎ 053-422-1075) is a place that introduces oriental medicine through experience programs.

  • Opening hours: 10:00–17:30.
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, and the days of Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Parking: Available.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Visiting Daegu Yangnyeongsi on a Monday — The Museum of Oriental Medicine and the Oriental Medicine Experience Town are closed every Monday (the museum opens if it is a public holiday).

2. Thinking the Seoul Oriental Medicine Center is closed on Mondays — Its regular closed days are only January 1, Seollal, and Chuseok.

3. Bringing a car to Gyeongdong Market — Parking is not available at Gyeongdong Market and Seoul Yangnyeong Market. Parking at the Oriental Medicine Center is 500 won per 10 minutes.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where do I go to try oriental medicine myself?

A. At the Daegu Yangnyeongsi Museum of Oriental Medicine you can do a herbal foot bath and herbal soap making. In Seoul, the Seoul Oriental Medicine Center runs exhibits and experiences.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. For the places in this guide, whether cards are accepted is not registered in the tourism organization's information.
