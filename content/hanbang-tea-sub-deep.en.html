Q2. Admission to the Oriental Medicine Promotion Center + Chamdajeong? A. Oriental Medicine Museum 1,000 won, Chamdajeong Cafe free·Wheelchairs OK, group seating for 45 people.

Q3. Ssanghwacha vs Sipjeondaebotang? A. Ssanghwacha = 6 herbs, recovery from fatigue, daily life·Sipjeondaebotang = 10 medicinal herbs, energy, season change #1.

Q4. Can foreigners prescribe herbal medicine? A. Oriental Medicine Promotion Center Medical Department (oriental medicine doctor)·Foreigners available (confirm interpretation in advance)·Treatment fee separate.
