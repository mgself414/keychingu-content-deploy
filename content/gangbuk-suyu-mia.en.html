Gangbuk Suyu · Mia · Ui Guide — Bukhansan Dulle-gil Trails, the April 19th Cemetery and 9 Neighborhood Restaurants, All Reached by the Ui-Sinseol Line (Official Info as of 2026)

Subtitle: Bukhansan Dulle-gil Section 1 is 3.1 km from Bukhansan Ui Station, and Uiryeong-gil requires a reservation / April 19th National Cemetery main gate 06:00–18:00, memorial hall closed Mondays / Dream Forest always open / Hwagyesa Temple offers a Temple Stay — based on the official pages of the Korea National Park Service, the Ministry of Patriots and Veterans Affairs and Gangbuk-gu as of 2026-10-07. 🎯 How to Use This Guide

In Gangbuk-gu's Suyu, Mia and Ui neighborhoods, the single Ui-Sinseol Line (우이신설선) (Bukhansan Ui ↔ Sinseol-dong, 13 stations) links the Bukhansan Dulle-gil trailheads, the April 19th National Cemetery, Solbat Park and Hwagyesa Temple, while many well-reviewed neighborhood restaurants cluster around Suyu Station (수유역) (Line 4). For the attractions, we list only the hours, addresses and section distances read on the official pages of the Korea National Park Service, the Ministry of Patriots and Veterans Affairs and Gangbuk-gu on 2026-10-07. The 9 restaurants are the top picks by review count (rv) from the KeyChinGu place pool, so check hours and closing days in a map app on the day. We corrected three claims from the older guide that differ from the official sources: "Dulle-gil Section 1 including the April 19th Cemetery, 4 hours," "Ui Station," and "40–50 minutes from Gangnam."

🚈 Ui-Sinseol Line — The Backbone of This Area

  • Bukhansan Ui → Solbat Park → April 19th National Cemetery → Gaori → Hwagye → Samyang → Samyang Sageori → Solsaem → Bukhansan Bogukmun → Jeongneung → Sungshin Women's University → Bomun → Sinseol-dong, 13 stations (official, from the operator).
  • On weekdays the first train from Bukhansan Ui is 05:30 and the last is 00:37, with trains every 5 minutes during the day (official). Transfer at Sungshin Women's University (Line 4), Bomun (Line 6) and Sinseol-dong (Lines 1 and 2).
  • The "Ui Station" in the older guide does not exist — the name is Bukhansan Ui Station (북한산우이역).

🥾 Bukhansan Dulle-gil and Uiryeong-gil

  • Section 1, Pine Forest Trail: starts near Bukhansan Ui Station, 3.1 km and about 1.5 hours (official). It is a Dulle-gil trail, so you can walk it without hiking boots.
  • Section 2, Pilgrimage Trail: 2.3 km and about 1 hour 10 minutes, passing the April 19th National Cemetery (official). Sections 1 and 2 together take half a day.
  • Uiryeong-gil (Section 21): 6.8 km and about 3.5 hours, reservation required — reservation.knps.or.kr; foreigners, ages 65+ and people with disabilities reserve by phone (official). Check the official page for the daily visitor limit and the days that require a reservation.
  • We could not read the official pages for the Baegundae summit course or the Ui Visitor Center hours this time, so they are not listed.

🕊 April 19th National Cemetery — On Dulle-gil Section 2

  • A national cemetery for the victims of the April 19 Revolution of 1960 (Ministry of Patriots and Veterans Affairs). Main gate Mar–Oct 06:00–18:00 and Nov–Feb 07:00–17:00; memorial hall and paying respects 09:30–17:30 (16:30 in winter); memorial hall closed Mondays (official). The fee and English guidance are not posted officially.
  • 600 m and 5 minutes from April 19th National Cemetery Station on the Ui-Sinseol Line (official).

🌲 Dream Forest, Solbat Park and Hwagyesa Temple

  • Dream Forest: always open, Wolgye-ro 173, reached by bus and subway to the East Gate and West Gate (official). The fee, closing days and night lighting are not posted officially.
  • Solbat Park: Samyang-ro 561, about 1,000 pine trees across 34,955 m² (Gangbuk-gu). Pair it with Dulle-gil Section 1.
  • Hwagyesa Temple: Hwagyesa-gil 117, Temple Stay offered (official — applications and fees are on the temple's page). Reached from Hwagye Station.

🛒 Suyu Traditional Market and Ui Market

  • Gangbuk-gu's official market list includes Suyu Traditional Market (수유전통시장) and Ui Market (우이시장) (address and phone are official). Opening hours and closing days are not posted officially — a weekday daytime visit is the safe choice.

🚶 2 Half-Day Courses

  • A Dulle-gil + Cemetery: Bukhansan Ui Station → Dulle-gil Section 1 (1.5 h) → Section 2 (1 h 10 min) and the April 19th National Cemetery (memorial hall closed Mondays) → April 19th National Cemetery Station → restaurants near Suyu Station.
  • B Temple + Park: Hwagye Station → Hwagyesa Temple → Solbat Park → Bukhansan Ui Station → dinner in Suyu.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Searching for "Ui Station" — it is Bukhansan Ui Station.

2. Going to Uiryeong-gil without a reservation — a reservation is required. Foreigners reserve by phone.

3. Looking for the April 19th Cemetery on Dulle-gil Section 1 — the cemetery is on Section 2.

4. The memorial hall on Monday — closed (the cemetery grounds are open during the main gate hours).

5. Trusting restaurant hours — they are not in this guide. Check a map app on the day.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Do I have to hike? — Dulle-gil Sections 1 and 2 are walks, not climbs (3.1 km + 2.3 km).

2. Until what time is the cemetery open? — Main gate until 18:00 (17:00 in Nov–Feb), memorial hall until 17:30 (16:30 in winter).

3. Where do I reserve Uiryeong-gil? — reservation.knps.or.kr; foreigners by phone.

4. Is there a park entrance fee? — Solbat Park is free. The Dream Forest fee is not posted officially (the park itself is always open).

5. Does the Ui-Sinseol Line run at night? — The weekday last train is 00:37 (from Bukhansan Ui).

6. How are the restaurants chosen? — By review count in the KeyChinGu place pool. It is not a taste rating.
