⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners make

1. "Gangbuk = Bukhansan Bay" — Citizen's daily life cluster unknown: Gangbuk was covered in connection with #177 hiking in #186, but Suyu·Mia citizen's daily life store (Sushinokando 11,822 RV Gangbuk #1·Ok Ryuheon·High Ground Bakery) was not covered.

2. "Gangbuk = Nothing to see except Bedtown" — History/nature cluster Unknown: A number of history/nature clusters, including the 4/19 Cemetery (commemorating the 4/19 Revolution), North Seoul Dream Forest (Beon-dong), and entering section 1 of the Bukhansan Trail (Ui Station).

3. "Suyu is far" — Not aware of accessibility to Line 4 and Uisinseol Line: Suyu Station (Line 4) — 30 minutes from downtown. Ui Station (Ui New Line) — 40 to 50 minutes from Gangnam. #177 Nowon·Dobong hiking + Gangbuk feeding restaurant Bundle 1-day course available.
