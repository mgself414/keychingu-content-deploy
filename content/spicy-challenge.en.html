⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Fire Chicken Bokkeum Ramen Challenge = At a Korean Restaurant": Fire Chicken is a convenience store/mart ramen. X. Buldak at a Korean restaurant. For foreign vlogs, buy it at the supermarket + cook it yourself.

2. "Spiciness = all the same": Korean spicy flavor 3 axes — Gochujang (salty + sweet)·Red pepper powder (purely spicy)·Chinese spicy (mala, spices). Spicy braised ribs are made with red pepper paste, Maeuntang is made with red pepper powder, and Malatang is Chinese.

3. "Spicy Challenge = Drink + Something Cool": Korean-Style Spicy Detox = Rice·Milk·Cheese (Cold Water A K-drama regular.

① Buldak Ramen ★★★★★ Foreigner #1

  • Signature: Samyang Hot Chicken Fried Noodles (Convenience store GS25, CU, 7-Eleven)
  • Price: 1,500~2,500 won/bag
  • Foreign demand: BBC·MrBeast·foreign vlogger regular + Korean fire noodle challenge
  • Why: Undisputed No. 1 Korean spicy food vlog for foreigners — 5 levels of spiciness (original, cheese, black bean paste, habanero, nuclear buldak).

② Spicy Tteokbokki ★★★★

  • Signature: Sindang-dong Tteokbokki Street + Ma Bokrim + Sassy Tteokbokki (#66 Tteokbokki Chain)
  • Price: 5,000~12,000 won
  • Why: Korea’s No. 1 spicy street food and snack.

③ Spicy Galbijjim ★★★★

  • Signature: Bongchujjimdak·Spicy Galbijjim authentic store
  • Price: 22,000~38,000 won/person
  • Why: Korean authentic spicy braised ribs (variation of ribs other than Jjimdak).

④ Stir-fried Octopus ★★★★

  • Signature: Authentic Korean restaurant + Busan style
  • Price: 18,000~30,000 won/person
  • Why: Authentic Korean spicy seafood.

⑤ Agujjim, Monkfish ★★★★

  • Signature: Authentic Busan style + Incheon style
  • Price: 30,000~80,000 won (2~3 people)
  • Why: Busan-style authentic spicy seafood — bean sprouts + mideodeok.
