Seoul on a Rainy Day — 10 Indoor Spots to Spend the Whole Day (Official Info as of 2026-10)

Subtitle: 3 free museums (National Museum of Korea · MMCA Seoul · Seoul Museum of Art) · DDP · 63 Building (63 SkyPicnic · Centre Pompidou Hanwha) · 2 aquariums + Starfield Library · Lotte World Adventure indoor theme park · Seoul Botanic Park greenhouse · jjimjilbang — hours, fees and closures follow each official page. The National Hangeul Museum is closed through 2028, and Dragon Hill Spa in Yongsan has ceased operating.

ISSUE 03 · #280 · 2026-05-20

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Seoul's rain concentrates in the July–August monsoon and the September typhoons, but spring and autumn get a rainy day or two as well. We sorted 10 indoor spots for those days by whether you can reach them from the station without getting wet, what the fee is, and which day they close. Operating details are the values checked on each official page on 2026-10-07; anything we could not confirm is marked as such. Two places from the older guide are left out: the National Hangeul Museum (국립한글박물관) is closed through the second half of 2028 for fire recovery and expansion, and Dragon Hill Spa (드래곤힐스파) in Yongsan has ceased operating since COVID (per KTO's VisitKorea listing 'Closed').

🏛 3 Free Museums — Open Until 9 PM on Wed & Sat

  • National Museum of Korea: Permanent exhibits are free. It is open until 21:00 on Wednesdays and Saturdays, which makes a good rainy evening. From 2026-07-06 to 2027-01-28 some permanent galleries are closed for renovation, so check the website for the gallery you want to see.
  • MMCA Seoul: East of Gyeongbokgung. Also open until 21:00 on Wed & Sat. Fees vary by exhibition, so check the official site.
  • SeMA Seosomun: At the end of the Deoksugung stone-wall road. Tue–Fri 10:00–20:00, closed Mondays and Jan 1. Weekend hours change by season.
  • All three are weak on Mondays — the National Museum of Korea does open on Mondays (per its official schedule), but both art museums close on Mondays.

🧱 DDP — Dongdaemun on a Rainy Day

  • A curved building designed by Zaha Hadid. Exhibitions and the Design Market (B2) run 10:00–20:00, closed only on Jan 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok. Walking through the building itself is free.
  • After dark the exterior lights come on, and the fashion malls across the street stay open late — a rainy-night shopping route.

🏙 63 Building — Observatory, Museum and Restaurants Under One Roof

  • Renovated in June 2026. The 60th-floor 63 SkyPicnic (daily 10:00–22:00, ₩27,000), the annex Centre Pompidou Hanwha (closed Mon, from ₩28,000) and 8 restaurants. You can spend a whole rainy day here. Details in #705.

🐠 2 Aquariums + Starfield Library

  • Lotte World Aquarium: Inside Lotte World Mall. Adult/teen ₩37,000, child ₩33,000, free under 36 months (official). A package with Seoul Sky is ₩62,000.
  • COEX Aquarium: 10:00–20:00, last entry 19:00 (official). The official page shows no list price and advertises reduced online fares (up to 43% off), so book before you go.
  • Starfield Library: In the middle of COEX Mall, 10:30–22:00, free (official). The most crowded indoor photo spot on a rainy day.

🎢 Lotte World Adventure — Indoor Theme Park

  • An indoor adventure under a dome plus the outdoor Magic Island. On a rainy day, covering just the indoor side fills the day. The After 4 ticket from 4 PM (adult ₩55,000) suits a rainy afternoon. The all-day pass includes the Folk Museum (last entry 18:00).

🌿 Seoul Botanic Park Greenhouse — Magok

  • Themed garden (including the greenhouse) adult ₩5,000. In October 2026 it is closed on the 6th, 12th, 19th and 26th (official notice). Winter (Nov–Feb) 09:30–17:00; check the official schedule for other seasons.

♨ Jjimjilbang

  • Dragon Hill Spa in Yongsan has stayed closed since COVID (reopening undecided, KTO 'Closed'). For 24-hour venues now operating and how to use them, see #007.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Visiting an art museum on Monday — MMCA and SeMA close on Mondays. The National Museum of Korea, DDP and the aquariums are open.

2. Going to the National Hangeul Museum or Dragon Hill Spa based on old info — both are closed right now.

3. Going to Seoul Botanic Park on a closed day — in October it is closed on the 6th, 12th, 19th and 26th.

4. Paying the walk-up price at COEX Aquarium — the official page advertises reduced online fares.

5. Buying a Lotte World 1-Day pass in the morning — if you only want a rainy afternoon, get After 4.

⚙ FAQ 5

1. A day without spending money? — National Museum of Korea (permanent exhibits free) → DDP → Starfield Library. All three connect to a station.

2. Where is open until 9 PM? — National Museum of Korea and MMCA Seoul on Wed & Sat, 63 SkyPicnic (daily until 22:00), Starfield Library (until 22:00).

3. With kids? — Lotte World Aquarium (free under 36 months), Lotte World Adventure, Seoul Botanic Park greenhouse.

4. When is the monsoon? — Usually July, with typhoons in August–September. Check the weather with the Korea Meteorological Administration (#259 monthly guide).

5. Where are the jjimjilbang? — #007.
