⚠ 3 common mistakes Japanese tourists make

1. "Isaak Toast is not on Naver Map" — Korean search failed

The most common pitfalls for first-time visitors to Japan. Isaac Toast (Japanese Myeongdong breakfast signature) has weak Korean search results on Naver Map. Many Koreans don't know the name itself, but it was exposed as "イサックトースト (Isakutoast)" on Japanese broadcasts and TikTok. Search Japanese "イサックトースト" on Google Maps → Myeongdong branch exposed. Recommended to bookmark in Google Maps + Japanese before arrival.

2. "Minari = Anywhere" — Wandering around Seongsu and Neungdong and other neighborhoods

Japanese water parsley fad (2-3 years ago) + Japan's regulation of raw fish 15 years ago = When visiting Korea, water parsley + raw fish combination must-eat. However, it is easy for foreigners to get lost if they do not know the Korean language for “water parsley gomtang.” Signature stores of water parsley gomtang and water parsley meat bibimbap are concentrated in the Seongsu-Neungdong area (walking distance from Ttukseom Station on Line 7 and Children's Grand Park Station on Line 5) — A K-Insta hotspot frequented by Japanese celebrities. Search for “water parsley gomtang Neungdong” or “ミナリコムタン” on Google Maps.

3. "Lotte Mart is just a mart" — Wandering through the regular shelves without knowing about customized services for foreigners

On the last day of the trip, I entered a regular supermarket with my suitcase → I wandered through the regular shelves looking for Korean snacks and souvenirs. In reality, Lotte Mart Seoul Station branch is fully equipped with 40% of foreign sales + exclusive foreigner corner + overseas delivery + currency exchange + 3-hour free storage + rearrangement of souvenir-focused stands. Suitcase storage + full shopping available 3 hours before going to the airport. If traveling with a suitcase, use the first floor locker first.

Scenery of the Japanese National Rules Course

The number of Japanese tourists visiting Korea will increase rapidly to approximately 2.8 million in 2024 and 1 million+ in the first quarter of 2025. Combination of pre-corona recovery + increase in Korea-Japan LCC flights to ~20 routes + low yen + cumulative exposure to K-culture. Re-visit rate 44.8%, average visit 7 times (overwhelming #1 among foreign segment) — In other words, Japanese tourists follow the “several short and meaningful repetitions” pattern rather than “full experience once”. For this reason, daily consumption experiences (cafes, street food, markets, pharmacies, supermarkets) rather than nature and history** are the key routes.

"Korea Bullet Travel (韓国弾丸ツアー)" is a type of day trip that has become signature content on Japanese SNS and YouTube. Departure from Tokyo (Narita, Haneda, Kansai) at 2 a.m. → Arrival at Incheon Airport at 5 a.m. → Anguk-dong Cafe at 7 a.m. → Myeong-dong → Gwangjang Market → Seongsu → Lotte Mart → 10 p.m. Incheon Airport → Tokyo at dawn. A total challenge of 12 to 14 hours. Although it is short, it is a full 4-axis experience including K-cafe + Myeongdong K-beauty + Gwangjang Market + Seongsu.

Anguk·Samcheong Cafe + Myeongdong Core + Gwangjang Market + Seongsu + Lotte Mart Seoul Station 5 axes are the true structure of the Japanese national rules course. Gangnam, Hongdae, and Itaewon are ranked low in the main travel routes of Japanese tourists — What Japanese people crave when visiting Korea is the everyday scenes seen in K-dramas and mukbang, and those scenes are most evident in Anguk, Myeong-dong, plazas, and Seongsu.

① Anguk·Samcheong Cafe (2)

1. London Bagel Museum Anguk

  • Signature: A must-have cafe for Japanese tourists #1. It's Japanese from 7 a.m. on weekdays. Last year’s sales of KRW 93.8 billion (Top among all Korean cafes). 3 minutes walk from Anguk Station (Line 3).
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Highest frequency on Japanese SNS and TikTok, English and Japanese menus
  • Recommended menu: Signature bagel (plain, itta olive, parmesan), 6 types of cream cheese pairing
  • Tip: Open at 7pm on weekdays / short line before 9pm. Wait 1 to 2 hours in the afternoon. The Jamsil branch and Yeouido branch have a low proportion of Japanese people — Anguk main branch ranks first in Japanese national rules.

2. Cafe Onion Anguk

  • Signature: Hanok cafe from the 1920s. Japanese signature with 'Pandor' Japanese review. Staff fluent in Japanese.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Hanok + Japanese-speaking service, #1 on Instagram
  • Recommended menu: Pandor (sugar powder bread), signature latte, seasonal dessert
  • Tip: 7-minute walk from Anguk Station (Line 3), 8 Yulgok-ro 3-gil — 5-minute walk from London Bagel Anguk. 2 cafe bundle courses available. Photo from Hanok garden — Japanese Instagram signature.

② Myeongdong Breakfast·Shopping (5)

3. Isaac Toast Myeongdong

  • Signature: Koreans don't know much, but Japanese know it. Highest frequency of exposure on Japanese broadcasts and TikTok. Naver Map search results are weak, Myeongdong branch is exposed when searching for Japanese "イサックトースト" on Google. Approximately 70% of stores speak Japanese.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Japanese cluster, familiar with Japanese language service
  • Recommended menu: Bacon Egg Toast, Ham Cheese Toast (sweet eggs similar to Japanese egg dishes)
  • Tip: Walking distance from Myeongdong Station (Line 4). 8-10 a.m. line — Japanese people's first meal pattern after arriving early in the morning. Cost effective 4,500~6,500 won per person.

4. Myeongdong Abalone Porridge

  • Signature: Japanese tourist breakfast signature — Not salty but savory to suit the Japanese taste. From the Japanese menu entrance.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Japanese menu, English service
  • Recommended menu: Basic abalone porridge (17,000 won~), vegetable porridge, pumpkin porridge — An introduction to Korean porridge for Japanese people
  • Tip: Myeongdong Core — Walking distance from Myeongdong Station (Line 4). It is so well known among Japanese people that there is a line even at odd hours (10-12, 14-16). It was exposed as the signature “Korean porridge” in Japanese media.

5. Olive Young Myeongdong Town

  • Signature: 95% of foreign sales — No. 1 store in the country. 2024 Foreign sales: 1 trillion won. Japan's No. 1 K-beauty shopping site.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Information in English, Chinese, and Japanese, foreign language support
  • Recommended category: K-beauty full set (skincare, makeup, body), season limited edition, mask pack box
  • Tip: 5-minute walk from Myeongdong Station (Line 4) Exit 9. Peak at 17:00-21:00 in the evening — Quiet for breakfast and lunch. Utilize tax-free display stands.

6. Daiso Myeongdong Station Branch (Daiso Myeongdong)

  • Signature: 12th floor super large store — many foreigners. Especially beauty corner hot. Cost-effective K-Life shopping from 1,000 to 5,000 won.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Foreign language information, clear price indication
  • Recommended Category: K-beauty cost-effectiveness, Korean character goods, kitchenware, souvenirs
  • Tip: Directly connected to Myeongdong Station (Line 4) Exit 6. 12th floor full tour takes 1 hour+ — No. 1 carrier shopping for Japanese people. When paying tax-free 50,000 won+.

7. Myeongdong Pharmacy District — 6 months +19 (Myeongdong Pharmacy District)

  • Signature: Myeongdong pharmacies surged by +19 in the past 6 months. Last year, foreign pharmacy sales were KRW 141.4 billion — +142% compared to the previous year. Japanese pharmacy shopping signature.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Japanese guidance, foreign language assistance from pharmacists
  • Recommended category: Functional ointment/cream (atopy/acne), skin care (Centella/cica), herbal nutritional supplements, diet supplements
  • Tip: Pharmacy cluster around Exit 6 of Myeong-dong. Pharmacy ≠ General cosmetics store — The reliability of pharmacist prescriptions and professional recommendations is high-value to Japanese people. Olive Young (mask pack) + Pharmacy (functional skin care) Bundle shopping pattern.

③ Gwangjang Market·Seongsu Gourmet (3)

8. Gwangjang Market Jin Ok-hwa Halmae Dakhanmari Gwangjang

  • Signature: No. 1 Japanese K-Nabe Mania. Similar to Japanese nabe, but combining Korean-style sauce and kalguksu. Foreign reviews are very rich.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — English and Japanese menu, many foreigners
  • Recommended menu: One whole chicken (2-4 servings), kalguksu (noodle soup), additional garlic, rice cake (rice cake)
  • Tip: Gwangjang Market Core — 5-minute walk from Jongno 5-ga Station (Line 1). Peak from 18:00 to 21:00 → Weekday lunch from 11:00 to 14:00 or from 14:00 to 17:00. Adding Korean-style garlic and sauce is a Japanese signature way to enjoy it.

9. Gwangjang Market Street Food Trio

  • Signature: 3 types of Japanese Gwangjang Market must-eat items. Twist is loved by Japanese people for its rice cake texture. Drug kimbap (mustard + mini kimbap) + Bindaetteok (mung bean pancake) classic.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Rich exposure to Japanese media, photo menu
  • Recommended menu: Twist (quick turnover), drug gimbap + banquet noodles, mung bean bindaetteok + makgeolli
  • Tip: 3 minutes walk from Jongno 5-ga Station (Line 1). Twist line is long but turns around quickly — Wait 15-20 minutes. Bindaetteok + Makgeolli are available on weekdays from 14:00 to 17:00.

10. Neungdong Minari Gomtang & Yukhoe Bibimbap

  • Signature: Japanese water parsley fad + regulation of yukhoe 15 years ago = Must-eat water parsley + yukhoe combination when visiting Korea. Japanese celebrities also visit often.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Plenty of Japanese reviews, English menu
  • Recommended menu: Water parsley meat bibimbap (sesame oil + water parsley + sweet and sour sauce), water parsley gomtang (fragrant + stop before the scent gets too much)
  • Tip: Seongsu·Neungdong area — 10-minute walk from Children's Grand Park Station (Line 7) or 12-minute walk from Ttukseom Station (Line 2). Japanese Instagram Signature — Recommended to enter popular stores before 13:00.

④ Lotte Mart·Souvenirs (2)

11. Gwangjang Kimchi & Sesame Oil Shopping at Gwangjang Market

  • Signature: Japanese Gwangjang Market Unexpected shopping item #1 — Kimchi + Sesame oil. Kimchi is essential despite the difficulty of carrier packaging, many sesame oil products are labeled in English and Japanese (reflecting demand from foreigners).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English/Japanese label, vacuum packaging option
  • Recommended Category: Vacuum-packed kimchi (pork, radish, radish kimchi), sesame oil (bottle, small), sesame salt, red pepper paste
  • Tip: Gwangjang Market kimchi corner + sesame oil corner cluster. Last shopping recommended the day before going to the airport — Kimchi is fresh + smell. Olive Young + Café Onion are also located in the plaza, so you can shop at the market and relax at the café.

12. Lotte Mart Seoul Station

  • Signature: Foreign sales 40%+ — No. 1 last shopping before going to the airport. Overseas shipping + currency exchange + international courier + 3-hour free storage + rearrangement of shelves to focus on souvenirs.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Information in English, Chinese, and Japanese, foreign language support, luggage travel welcome
  • Recommended category: Korean snacks (Pepero, Mongshell matcha, Choco pie, matcha, glutinous rice cake pie), banana-flavored milk, Biyotte, sugar-free and sugar-free snacks, beauty and nutritional supplements, Korean culture product center ('Eat Pork Belly' t-shirt)
  • Tip: 3 minutes walk from Seoul Station (Lines 1, 4, Airport Railroad). 3-hour course before going to the airport — Suitcase storage on the first floor + full shopping + currency exchange + overseas shipping option. Store order for foreigners only = Souvenirs → Snacks → Beverages → Beauty → Nutritional supplements.
