Japan to Korea Travel Guide: 90 Days Visa-Free, Checking K-ETA, Automated Immigration, PayPay, Haneda–Gimpo (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: Japanese ordinary passports enter visa-free for 90 days (Ministry of Foreign Affairs list) · the temporary K-ETA exemption runs through 2026-12-31 (K-ETA notice); whether you qualify shows up when you scan your passport on the application screen · from 2025-12-01 Japanese passports can also use automated immigration clearance (Ministry of Justice) · PayPay works at Korean Alipay+ and ZeroPay merchants (PayPay official) · JAL flies Haneda–Gimpo. As of 2026-10-21

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Just over two hours from Haneda, suitcase wheels rumble in a line through the Gimpo Airport (김포공항) international arrivals hall. Roll your bag down to the subway station and you are already in Seoul. This guide lists what travelers coming from Japan should check before departure, using only values from official pages: entry documents (visa-free days, K-ETA, e-Arrival Card), the airport immigration line (automated clearance), payment (PayPay and cards), and flights. It is meant for frequent short-trip visitors and first-timers alike.

Figures from the old guide, such as "44.8% repeat rate, 7 visits on average", "2.8 million visitors in 2024", "95% of sales from foreigners", "last year's sales of 93.8 billion won" and "70% of in-store conversations in Japanese", were removed because no official source could be found. The 12-hour day-trip route and the store rankings were also dropped for lack of evidence.

🛂 Entry Documents: 90 Days Visa-Free, K-ETA, e-Arrival Card

  • Visa-free stay: Japanese ordinary passport holders may stay 90 days without a visa (reciprocity) (Ministry of Foreign Affairs official list "Countries whose nationals may enter Korea without a visa", as of February 2024, revised 2026-01-08)
  • Temporary K-ETA exemption: it runs from January 1 to December 31, 2026 (K-ETA official notice, posted 2025-12-23). The notice does not list the eligible countries, so we could not confirm from the notice whether Japan is included. Scan your passport on the k-eta.go.kr application screen and it will tell you whether you are "exempt". Whether it will be extended into 2027 has not been decided
  • e-Arrival Card: if you did not actually obtain a K-ETA, submit the e-Arrival Card. It is free at e-arrivalcard.go.kr from 3 days before arrival, and a paper form is also accepted (Ministry of Justice official site, #1289 e-Arrival manual)
  • Medicine: for the rules on bringing over-the-counter and prescription drugs, see #1352 medication import guide

🚪 The Airport Immigration Line: Automated Clearance

  • Japan was added in the second expansion of automated immigration clearance on 2025-12-01 (18 countries and regions), and the list reached 42 on 2026-03-16 (Ministry of Justice official). We could not find an official age rule for the arrival gates
  • On your first entry, register your passport scan, fingerprints and face at the registration line in the immigration hall, and from that day you can use the automated gates. We could not confirm the registration counter's opening hours from official documents; for the step-by-step, see #1412 automated clearance manual
  • On departure, short-term visitors aged 17 or older who gave fingerprints and a face scan on entry can use the automated gates (Ministry of Justice immigration official guidance)

💳 Payment: PayPay and Cards

  • PayPay overseas payment mode: in Korea it works at Alipay+ merchants and ZeroPay (only when you scan the QR code posted at the shop). Convenience stores CU, GS25, 7-ELEVEN and emart24, Starbucks, and taxis in Seoul are on the official list of places that accept it (PayPay official Korea page)
  • Verify your identity in Japan: overseas payment mode only works for accounts that completed identity verification inside Japan, and new registration or identity verification cannot be done abroad (PayPay official help)
  • Subway and buses need a separate transit card: #1298 choosing among three transit cards. For cards and mobile payments in general, see #527 Korean payment systems

✈ Flights

  • Haneda–Gimpo: JAL's operating schedule (2026-03-29 to 10-24) lists round-trip Haneda–Seoul (Gimpo) flights (JAL official). We could not open the winter schedule from October 25 onward, so search by date
  • Routes from other airports such as Narita, Kansai and Fukuoka were not checked against official pages this time
  • From Gimpo into the city, Line 9 is the easy option: #1247 Line 9 complete guide

👥 Traveling Companions: Children and Seniors

  • Children usually go through the regular line instead of the automated gates: the Ministry of Justice's own explainer shows adults at the gates and kids in the family lane. We could not find an official age rule, so if you travel with a child, it is easier to queue together in the regular line
  • Each family member should check their own K-ETA exemption by scanning their passport. An e-Arrival Card is submitted per person

🥗 Dietary Needs

  • Most kimchi seasoning contains salted fermented seafood, fish sauce or meat stock, and sundubu-jjigae (soft tofu stew, 순두부찌개) and dubu-jorim (braised tofu, 두부조림) often contain salted shrimp too (VisitKorea official). If you are vegetarian or have allergies, prepare a card to show before ordering: #1300 dietary request manual

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "I'm visa-free, so I don't have to submit anything": if you did not obtain a K-ETA, you must still submit the e-Arrival Card (Ministry of Justice)

2. "The K-ETA exemption will continue": the announced temporary exemption lasts only through 2026-12-31 (K-ETA notice). What comes after is undecided

3. "I'll set up PayPay once I get to Korea": identity verification can only be done inside Japan (PayPay official)

4. "The whole family can use the automated line": children usually go through the regular line, so queue there together
