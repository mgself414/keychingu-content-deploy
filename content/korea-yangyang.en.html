Foreigner's Yangyang Travel Guide — Surfing Mecca·Naksansa·Hajodae on the East Coast

> Subtitle: Complete guide to Yangyang (Gangwon) for foreigners — a "surfing mecca + seaside temple" town unlike Sokcho·Gangneung · Surfyy Beach·Jukdo Beach·Naksansa·Hajodae + surf lessons·East Sea sashimi matrix

ISSUE 04 · #1226 · 2026-07-03

🎯 Why a "Yangyang" Guide

Yangyang (양양, Gangwon) = Korea's surfing mecca + an East Coast town of a seaside temple (Naksansa)·sunrise cliffs. The biggest foreigner misconception = "the Gangwon East Sea = only Sokcho·Gangneung." In reality Yangyang stands apart from Sokcho·Gangneung on a surfing (Surfyy Beach·Jukdo)·seaside-temple·surf-town culture axis. Pain points = (a) Sokcho bias hides Yangyang's existence (b) access·how to take surf lessons (c) surf spots·town culture like Surfyy Beach (d) local East Sea sashimi·pine mushroom. "surfing Korea Yangyang / Surfyy beach" is a long-tail search on Reddit·YouTube.

This guide covers Yangyang's 12 signatures + surfing mecca + Naksansa·Hajodae + surf lessons + surf-town matrix.

✈️ Access·Getting Around (foreigner practical)

  • Access: Seoul → Yangyang (Dong-Seoul bus 2h30m)·~2 hours via Seoul-Yangyang Expressway·Yangyang Int'l Airport (some international routes)
  • Getting around: rental car recommended (surf spots are spread out)·intercity bus+taxi·some surf-shop shuttles
  • Base: Jukdo·Ingu surf guesthouses/pensions·Naksan resort
  • Payment: card·cash mix·surf shops take cards
  • Emergency: ☎ 1330 (tourism)·112·119

⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "Gangwon East Sea = only Sokcho·Gangneung": Yangyang stands apart on a surfing mecca·surf town axis.

2. "Surf anytime": Book ahead in summer peak·confirm lessons·board rental in advance.

3. "Naksansa is an ordinary temple": It's a seaside temple above the East Sea·Uisangdae sunrise spot.

4. "Fine without a car": Surf spots are spread out·rental car recommended·combine bus+taxi.

5. "Nothing to eat": Local delicacies — East Sea sashimi·autumn salmon·pine mushroom.

⚙ FAQ (6)

1. How to get there?: Dong-Seoul bus 2h30m·Seoul-Yangyang Expressway·Yangyang Airport (some international routes).

2. Beginners can surf?: Yes·beginner lessons at Jukdo·Ingu·Surfyy·board/wetsuit rental·English-friendly shops.

3. What to see?: Surfyy Beach·Jukdo Beach·Naksansa·Hajodae·Uisangdae sunrise.

4. Need a car?: Recommended·surf spots spread out·bus+taxi also possible.

5. Food?: East Sea sashimi·autumn salmon·pine mushroom·seafood.

6. Recommended personas: Surfing = Surfyy·Jukdo / sunrise = Naksansa·Hajodae / vibe = surf town.

⚠ Medical·Safety·Dietary Disclaimer

Surf with safety rules — beginners must take lessons·life vest·beware rip currents·stay in lifeguard zones. Summer UV·jellyfish. East Sea water is cold (wetsuit). Cliff safety at Naksansa etc. Seafood allergens. Sokcho hospitals for emergencies. Emergency ☎ 1330·112·119.
