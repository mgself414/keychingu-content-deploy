⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "Jeju food = regular Hansik (Korean food)": Korea's #1 Signature Matrix · a completely different matrix.

2. "Heukdwaeji (black pork) = same everywhere": Jeju = Korea's #1 Heukdwaeji (black pork) Signature Matrix · not the same as other cities.

3. "Haenyeo (female divers) = a thing of the past": UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage 2016 · Demonstration Matrix #1.

4. "Galchihoe (hairtail sashimi) = not for foreigners": Jeju's #1 Signature Matrix · popular among foreign enthusiasts.

5. "Malgogi (horse meat) = not for foreigners": Enthusiast Matrix · Optional Matrix.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Top Priority for Foreign Beginners: Heukdwaeji (black pork) + Galchihoe (hairtail sashimi) + Haenyeo (female divers) Food = 1 Day.

⚠ Medical · Safety · Dietary Disclaimer

Heukdwaeji (black pork) / Galchihoe (hairtail sashimi) / Malgogi (horse meat) = Declare allergies (crustacean, fish, soy, nuts) in advance. Hoe (raw fish) = Not recommended for pregnant women or those with weakened immune systems. Emergency ☎ 1339 / 1330.

Dietary Restrictions:

  • Halal / Kosher: Heukdwaeji (black pork) = non-Halal · Vegetarian options available at some stores
  • Vegetarian / Vegan: Haenyeo (female divers) food = non-vegetarian · Sachal eumsik (temple food) (#800 cross) = Vegetarian Matrix
  • Gluten-free: Heukdwaeji (black pork) / Hoe (raw fish) = OK (if you omit ganjang (soy sauce))
