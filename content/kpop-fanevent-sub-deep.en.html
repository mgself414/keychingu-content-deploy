5 Types of Korean K-pop Fan Events — Music Show Audience, Fansign Entry, Birthday Cafes, Official Pop-Ups, Concert Booking: Participation Rules as Seen in Agency Notices (Confirmed October 2026)

Subtitle: For a music show audience, fan club membership holders apply first-come, first-served through a 30-minute window (TXT official notice) · Foreign fans verify identity with a passport or Alien Registration Card (CORTIS official notice) · Fansign entries are automatic, one per album bought at the designated seller (TXT·TREASURE official notices) · KSPO DOME has 15,000 seats (KSPO&CO Olympic Park) — as of 2026-10-21

ISSUE 03 · #607 · 2026-05-31

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Six-thirty in the morning, outside the KBS Annex Public Hall (신관 공개홀) in Yeouido (여의도), passport in hand, waiting for your number to be called. That is how a music show pre-recording begins (CORTIS official notice 2026-04-24). Some events, like concerts, you just buy a ticket for, but most K-pop fan events require you to apply under the rules written in the agency notice, win, and pass an identity check before you get in.

This guide lays out five types of events that foreign fans visiting Korea can take part in. Dates and headcounts change every round, so we show what the notice asks for from the agency notice originals. Every notice cited here is from a round that has already ended.

📺 Music Show Audience — Applications Come Through Fan Club Notices

We could not find a foreigner-specific audience application window on the broadcasters' websites. Actual applications are opened by the performing artist's agency through a notice to the fan club. Every notice contains the items below.

  • Eligibility: The notices for TXT's KBS2 Music Bank (뮤직뱅크, 2026-07-17) and SBS Inkigayo (인기가요) pre-recording (2026-07-19) said only MOA Membership holders could apply. The age limit was 15 or older in Korea and the US and 16 or older in other countries and regions, and anyone under the limit could not enter even with a guardian (TXT official notice)
  • Application window: It opened for 30 minutes the day before the broadcast. For Music Bank it was July 16, 13:30~14:00, and for Inkigayo July 18, 10:30~11:00 (KST) (TXT official notice)
  • Headcount: Music Bank's live broadcast picked 5 people plus 3 reserves, and Inkigayo's pre-recording picked 120 people plus 5 reserves, first-come, first-served (TXT official notice). It varies a lot from round to round
  • Album · verification: For CORTIS's Music Bank pre-recording (2026-04-24), on top of COER Membership, they required buying at least 3 albums or 1 set on Weverse Global, plus a printout of the Melon download history. Phone screen captures were not accepted (CORTIS official notice)
  • Venue: Headcount check was held outside the KBS Annex Public Hall (신관 공개홀) in Yeouido (여의도) (13 Yeoui-gongwon-ro (여의공원로), Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구), Seoul) (CORTIS official notice), and outside the SBS Public Hall (공개홀) in Deungchon-dong (등촌동) (TXT official notice)
  • ID for foreigners: Passport or Alien Registration Card. If you wrote your name in English when applying, only your passport is used to check. Student IDs, photos, and printouts are not accepted (CORTIS official notice)
  • On-site rules: No filming, recording, or audio recording inside the studio; losing the wristband you receive means restricted entry; forming your own waiting line is banned. If you win and then don't show up, you can't take part in public broadcast events for that album's promotion period (TXT official notice)

For day-of-week schedules by broadcaster, continue with Foreign Fan's Guide to Attending K-pop Music Shows — How to Apply, Based on 5 Open Halls (Inkigayo, M Countdown, Music Bank, Show! Music Core, Show Champion) and 9 Agency Audience Notices.

✍ Fansign · Video Call — One Album, One Entry

A fansign doesn't sell tickets. Buy albums at the designated seller and you get as many entries as you bought, with winners drawn by lottery.

  • Weverse Shop (위버스샵) method (TXT 4th regular album, June–July 2025) — During the entry period, you are automatically entered for the total number of albums ordered on Weverse Global's Weverse Shop (위버스샵). Set products count by the number of records inside. You fill in the required information on the Weverse entry page with the same ID you bought with. Winners were announced July 21 at 20:00 (KST) (TXT official notice)
  • Ktown4u (케이타운포유) method (TREASURE, March 2025) — The entry sellers were the Ktown4u online mall in KR, EN, and CN. Even if you split the purchase across several orders, the combined quantity counts as entries. Winners were announced in a notice on the Ktown4u homepage, and the event on March 15 was for 50 people (TREASURE official notice)
  • Rules for foreigners — The English name you entered must match the English name on your ID, and you cannot enter with a Korean name (TXT official notice). For foreigners, only a physical passport or Alien Registration Card is accepted, not photos (TREASURE official notice)
  • Cancellation · transfer — If you cancel all the products you bought for entry, the entry is automatically cancelled too, and after winners are announced, payment cancellation and refunds are not allowed for entry-only products. A winning ticket cannot be picked up by proxy, transferred, or sold (TXT official notice)

For how video calls work, see Fansign and Video Call Entry Guide for Foreigners — Counting Entries, KakaoTalk Calls and English-Name Rules from 5 Weverse Shop and Ktown4u Notices.

🎂 Birthday Cafe — The Unofficial Event Fans Run

A birthday cafe is not run by the agency; fans rent an ordinary cafe for a few days to hold it. There is no official notice, so we don't recommend venues. We give only how to find them and the manners.

  • How to find them: Search X or Instagram for the artist's name together with "Saengka" (생카) or "Birthday Cafe" (생일카페). In the organizing fan account's notice, check the period, opening hours, drink order conditions, and number of perks
  • Manners: It is a cafe in business. Order a drink and line up as the organizers direct. Don't argue with cafe staff because the perks ran out. Don't block other customers or the neighboring shops
  • Watch out: The organizer is an individual, so the schedule can change suddenly

For the full flow, see Idol Birthday Cafes for Foreign Fans: How to Find a Saengka, Etiquette, and How to Read Subway Birthday Ads.

🛍 Official Pop-Ups · Experience Spaces — Places You Enter Without a Reservation

  • KWANGYA@SEOUL (광야@서울) — B1, 83-21 Wangsimni-ro (왕십리로), Seongdong-gu (성동구), Seoul, 73m from Exit 5 of Seoulsup Station (서울숲역) on the Suin-Bundang Line. Daily 10:30~20:00, closed on Seollal (설날) and Chuseok (추석) (Instagram notice). Phone +82-2-6233-6729. The website is in Korean (VisitSeoul)
  • Ktown4u COEX (케이타운포유 코엑스) — Floors 2–4 of COEX, 513 Yeongdong-daero (영동대로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul, 162m from Exit 6 of Samseong Station (삼성역) on Line 2. SQUARE on floor 2 is a permanent store (11:00~20:00), and SPACE on floor 4 is a pop-up space where artists meet fans. The website is in Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Russian, and Malay (VisitSeoul)
  • Convenience store pop-up — CU held a pop-up at its Seongsu Dessert Park branch (성수디저트파크점) from June 20 to July 5, 2026, timed to the release of RIIZE's mini album (BGF Retail press release 2026-06-15). The event has already ended

For where to see permanent stores and pop-up notices, see "How to Find Korean Collab Products" under Related Content below.

🎫 Concert Booking — Use the Sites Made for Foreigners

  • NOL World (world.nol.com) — The English page has K-POP and Concert ticket menus, accommodation booking, and a show-plus-stay bundle called "Play&Stay" (NOL World official). We could not confirm the payment methods on the pages we opened
  • Melon Ticket Global (tkglobal.melon.com) — A site for foreigners with English, Chinese, and Japanese menus. The operator is Kakao Entertainment (Melon Ticket Global official). We could not open the purchase guide page
  • Venue KSPO DOME — The gymnastics arena inside Olympic Park (올림픽공원), with 15,000 seats. As of October 21, the Olympic Park official site carried a notice that parts of the grounds are restricted due to construction on the swimming pool, KSPO DOME, and other facilities (KSPO&CO Olympic Park official)

For the booking steps, see K-pop Concerts·Tickets·Festivals Foreigner Full Guide.

🧰 Fan Event Practicalities — Solo, Booking, Payment, Getting Around

  • Solo: Audience and fansign applications are per person and only that person enters. Applying by proxy or transferring is banned (TXT·CORTIS official notices)
  • Booking: Before you come, set up the fan club membership and Weverse account. The audience application window has opened for only 30 minutes the day before the broadcast (TXT official notice)
  • Payment: Foreigner Korea Payment System Deep Dive
  • Getting around: To make the early-morning headcount check, it's better to stay near the public hall. For transit cards, see Choosing Your Korea Transit Card: The No-Panic Manual — T-money, WOWPASS, or Climate Card

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "I'll buy audience tickets on the broadcaster's site" — From the notices, the audience was not ticket sales but a first-come, first-served application for fan club membership holders, and the agency opened applications through a notice (TXT official notice)

2. "A Korean name is fine for the entry" — Foreigners enter only with the same English name as on their ID (TXT official notice)

3. "I'll show my ID with a photo on my phone" — Only a physical passport or Alien Registration Card is accepted (CORTIS·TREASURE official notices)

4. "I won, so I can cancel the album" — After winners are announced, payment cancellation and refunds are not allowed for entry-only products (TXT official notice)
