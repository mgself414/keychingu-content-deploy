Foreign Korean Market Index

> Seoul + local cities Korean market full matrix — Keyfriend existing publish content index

The Korean market is the number one unique experience for foreign personas. Gwangjang Market is the number one foreign vlog recommended by CNN and the New York Times, and local markets have characteristics of each city (central Gangneung, southern Jeonju, Sinheung, Gunsan, etc.). This index organizes all market-related content on the KeyFriend site by region and city.

4. Recommended one-day market hop

Pattern A: 1 day as mayor of Seoul (foreigners ranked 1st)

  • 09:00 Tongin Market (Seochon, lunch box cafe, foreigner unique)
  • 12:00 Gwangjang Market (Lunch, Bindaetteok, Drug Gimbap)
  • 15:00 Mangwon Market (afternoon, MZ, dessert)
  • 19:00 Gwangjang Market (dinner, night market, soju)

Pattern B: Local market day trip (KTX)

  • KTX Busan → Jagalchi Market + BIFF Square (same day or 1 night)
  • KTX Gangneung → Jungang Market (same day, seafood)
  • KTX Jeonju → Nambu Market + Hanok Village (1 night)

Pattern C: Alien vintage hunting

  • Gwangjang Market 2F Vintage Arcade
  • Dongmyo Old Market (Jongno 3-ga Night Market)
  • Telephoto vintage cluster

5. Foreign fit tips

Payment/Language

  • Cash recommended: Plaza/traditional market (₩100,000 emergency fee)
  • Card OK: Only some stores (gradually expanding)
  • picture menu + translation app: fit most stores
  • English signboard: Gwangjang Market and parts of Jagalchi

Time zone

  • Gwangjang Market: 09-23 o'clock (peak: 12-20 o'clock)
  • Jagalchi: 05-22 o'clock (peak: 09-18 o'clock)
  • Tongin Market: 09-19 o'clock (peak: 12-17 o'clock)
  • Telephoto: 10-22 o'clock (peak: 17-21 o'clock)

Market recommendations by persona

  • foodie: Gwangjang Market + Jagalchi (seafood)
  • vintage·Vintage: Dongmyo + Square 2F
  • MZ·Trendy: Mangwon·Hwangridan-gil
  • history buff: Namdaemun, Gukje Market, Haemang-dong, Gunsan
  • Hanok Village lover: Tongin, Nambu
