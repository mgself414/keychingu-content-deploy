Foreigner's First Visit 3-Day Itinerary (Short-Term) — Short but Full Set

> Subtitle: #228 7-Day Short-Term Variant — Gangnam, Hongdae, Palaces, Han River 3-Day Compressed Full Set

🎯 Why a 3-Day Itinerary for Foreigners?

The most common itinerary for foreign inbound tourists to Korea is a 2-night, 3-day short trip. It's the #1 choice for business travelers and those on short holidays. This is a #228 7-Day Variant — a compressed full set of the essential 3 days.

★ 3-Day Full Set Matrix

Day 1 — Arrival + Myeongdong · N Seoul Tower

  • 09:00 AM Arrive at Incheon International Airport (인천국제공항) → Airport Railroad (공항철도) → Hotel (Myeongdong (명동) · Gangnam (강남) · Hongdae (홍대))
  • 12:00 PM Lunch in Myeongdong (명동) ([#13·#172 Myeongdong Full Guide](13_172_myeongdong_full_guide.md))
  • 02:00 PM Myeongdong (명동) Duty-Free + Shopping (Lotte (롯데) · Shinsegae (신세계) · Duty-Free Shop)
  • 05:00 PM N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) + Cable Car (Foreigner's #1 Priority)
  • 08:00 PM Korean Food (한식) in Myeongdong (명동) ([#234 Hanjeongsik or K-BBQ](234_hanjeongsik_or_kbbq.md))

Day 2 — Palaces · Hanok · Hongdae

  • 09:00 AM Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) + Hanbok (한복) Experience ([#207 Temples · Palaces](207_temples_palaces.md))
  • 12:00 PM Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌 한옥마을) ([#226 Hanok Houses](226_hanok_houses.md))
  • 02:00 PM Ikseondong (익선동) Hanok (한옥) Cafe ([#225 Cafes](225_cafes.md))
  • 05:00 PM Hongdae (홍대) · Yeonnam (연남) ([#138 Hongdae Full Guide](138_hongdae_full_guide.md))
  • 08:00 PM Hongdae (홍대) Night Market (야시장) · Izakaya (이자카야)

Day 3 — Gangnam · Han River · Departure

  • 09:00 AM Gangnam (강남) K-pop ([#221 K-pop](221_kpop.md)) + Garosu-gil (가로수길)
  • 12:00 PM Gangnam (강남) Korean Food (한식) ([#143 Gangnam Korean Food](143_gangnam_korean_food.md))

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make on a 3-Day Trip

1. "3 days = all spots OK": 3 days means 3 core axes (Myeongdong (명동) · Palaces · Gangnam (강남)) + only 1 additional area. This differentiates it from the 5-day or 7-day ([#228 Foreigner's 1-Week Itinerary](228_foreigners_1_week_itinerary.md)) full sets.

2. "3 days = difficult for K-pop enthusiasts": K-pop enthusiasts should dedicate Day 3 to Gangnam (강남) · HYBE Insight + at least one extra time slot.

3. "3 days = forgetting foreigner refunds": Day 3 Incheon International Airport (인천국제공항) Tax Refund requires at least 90 minutes before departure.
