Foreigner’s first visit 3-day course (short-term) — short but full set

🎯 Why 3-day course for foreigners

The most common itinerary for foreigners inbound to Korea = 3 days and 2 nights short term. No. 1 business + short vacation foreign enthusiasts. #228 7-Day Variant — The core 3-day compressed full set.

★ 3-day full set matrix

Day 1 — Arrival + Myeongdong·N Seoul Tower

  • 09:00 Arrival at Incheon Airport → Airport Railroad (#237) → Hotel (Myeongdong, Gangnam, Hongdae)
  • 12:00 Myeongdong lunch (#13·#172 Myeongdong full strategy)
  • 14:00 Myeongdong duty free + shopping (Lotte, Shinsegae, duty free)
  • 17:00 N Seoul Tower + Cable Car (Foreigners 1st priority)
  • 20:00 Myeongdong Korean food (#234 Korean food or K-BBQ)

Day 2 — Palace, Hanok, Hongdae

  • 09:00 Gyeongbokgung Palace + Hanbok Experience (#207 Temple·Palace)
  • 12:00 Bukchon Hanok Village (#226 Hanok)
  • 14:00 Ikseon-dong Hanok Cafe (#225 Cafe)
  • 17:00 Hongdae·Yeonnam (#138 Hongdae full strategy)
  • 20:00 Hongdae Night Market·Izakaya

Day 3 — Gangnam, Han River, Departure

  • 09:00 Gangnam K-pop (#221) + Garosu-gil
  • 12:00 Gangnam Korean Food (#143 Gangnam Korean Food)
  • 14:00 Banpo Han River + Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (#243 Han River)
  • 17:00 Duty free moment + Incheon Airport (#238 T-money refund)
  • 20:00 Departure

⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make in 3 days

1. "3 days = All spots OK": 3 days = 3 core axes (Myeongdong, palace, Gangnam) + 1 area added only. Different from the full set of 5th and 7th (#228).

2. "3 days = K-pop mania difficult": K-pop mania = Day 3 Gangnam·HYBE Insight + at least 1 hour.

3. "Day 3 = Forgot refund for foreigners": Day 3 Incheon Airport refund (Tax Refund) = Must allow 90 minutes before departure.

Variant 3 days

  • A short-term full strategy (standard for this guide): Myeongdong, palace, Hongdae, Gangnam + Han River
  • B K-pop Mania: HYBE·SM·YG·Gangnam → Myeongdong → Jamsil (#82)
  • C Food Mania: Gwangjang Market → Gangnam Korean Food → Hongdae Night Market
  • D Nature/Walk: Bukhansan → Han River → Seoul Forest
