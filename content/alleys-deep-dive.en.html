⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Alleys = Instagram only": Gollmok (골목, alleys) are a blend of hanok (traditional Korean houses), MZ (Millennial & Gen Z) trends, and traditional charm, making them the #1 spot for foreign vlogs.

2. "Ikseondong = Anywhere": Ikseondong (익선동) is Korea's #1 hanok (traditional Korean house) alley, a top priority for foreign enthusiasts.

3. "Euljiro = Industrial Complex": Euljiro Nogari Alley (을지로 노가리 골목) is the #1 spot for both traditional Korean and MZ enthusiasts.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Top 1st choice alleys for foreigners: Ikseondong (익선동), Seochon (서촌), Yeonnam (연남), Haebangchon (해방촌), Itaewon (이태원).

2. Hanok alleys for foreigners: Ikseon (익선), Seochon (서촌), Bukchon (북촌) are popular with foreign enthusiasts.

3. MZ alleys for foreigners: Gyeongnidan-gil (경리단길), Haebangchon (해방촌), Yeonnam (연남), Mangnidan-gil (망리단길), Seongsu (성수).

4. Traditional alleys for foreigners: Euljiro Nogari Alley (을지로 노가리 골목), Jongno Pimatgol (종로 피맛골).
