🏮 Ssamziegil — Insadong’s core shopping mall

3-minute walk from Exit 6 of Anguk Station · Daily 10:00~21:00

Ssamziegil is a spiral open gallery shopping mall that is characterized by a structure that goes up and around a courtyard. The most popular Insadong landmark among foreigners.

Layer-by-floor configuration

floor Configuration Representative store
B1 Basement Gallery·Cafe Exhibition/Traditional Food
1F Crafts·Stationery·Accessories Hanji props, ceramics, traditional stationery
2F Clothing·Fashion·Handmade Hanbok modernization/Korean designer brand
3F Cafe·Food·Gallery Traditional Tea Cafe, Injeolmi Ice Cream
4F Rooftop Terrace Seoul city center view/photo spot

Photo spots & recommended routes

  • Courtyard Spiral Staircase: Angle looking up from below — No. 1 on Instagram for foreigners
  • Rooftop Terrace: Skyline toward Bukaksan Mountain
  • B1 Gallery Entrance: Traditional lantern decoration background

Practical tips for Ssamziegil

  • Cash required; many small stores (most cards are OK, cash at street stalls)
  • Handmade crafts → Many one-time limited products (stock runs out quickly)
  • Check special event: [ssamziegil.co.kr](https://ssamziegil.co.kr/)

🍵 Traditional Tea House — Insa-dong Tea Culture Experience

There are sit-down teahouses all over the alleys of Insa-dong. The experience of drinking green tea, Schisandra chinensis, jujube, and ginger tea in a teapot is especially popular among foreigners.

2 recommended teahouses

tea house location Features price range
Suhee Tea House Insa-dong 12-gil alley Hanok sitting table, traditional tea ceremony, foreign regulars Tea ₩8,000~15,000
Guicheon (Guicheon) Insa-dong main street Retro space and injeolmi set related to poet Cheon Sang-byeong Tea + dessert ₩12,000~18,000

⚠ 3 things foreigners should be aware of

1. Price cannot be negotiated: Most stores in Insa-dong have fixed prices — attempts to bargain are rude.

2. Congestion between 12:00 and 14:00 on weekends: Lots of crowds and lines. 10am or weekdays recommended.

3. Restrictions on bringing in food: Bringing in outside food is not allowed in some cafes in Ssamziegil.

❓ PAA (자주 묻는 질문)

Q1. 인사동에서 꼭 가야 할 곳은?

쌈지길(나선형 공예 쇼핑몰)과 전통찻집 1곳. 이 두 곳만 해도 2시간은 충분히 씁니다.

Q2. 쌈지길 입장료가 있나요?

무료. 24개 이상 공방·카페·갤러리 입점, 구매만 하면 됩니다.

Q3. 전통찻집 예약 필요한가요?

예약 불필요. 주말 낮에는 대기 10~20분 예상. 평일이나 오전 방문 추천.

Q4. 인사동 몇 시간이면 충분한가요?

쇼핑+찻집+쌈지길 = 최소 3시간. 한정식 식사 포함 시 4~5시간.

Q5. 한지·공예품 국제 배송 가능한가요?

일부 상점에서 국제 배송 서비스 제공. 사전 문의 필수. 작은 아이템은 직접 캐리어 반입 권장.

Q6. 인사동 최적 방문 시간대는?

주말 오전 10~12시 (노점 활성, 상대적으로 한산). 낮 12~14시는 인파 최고조.

Q7. 북촌과 함께 묶어도 되나요?

가능. 안국역 기준 도보 10분 거리. 인사동 오전 → 북촌 오후 = 외국인 정통 하루 코스 정석.

Q8. 인사동 쌈지길에서 체험할 수 있는 공예 프로그램이 있나요?

도자기 물레 체험·한지 염색·매듭 만들기 등 예약 or 현장 접수 운영 (₩15,000~30,000). 쌈지길 1~2F 공방에 문의.
