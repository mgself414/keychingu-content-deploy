🏮 Ssamziegil (쌈지길) — Insadong (인사동)'s Iconic Shopping Mall

3-min walk from Anguk Station (안국역) Exit 6 · Daily 10:00–21:00

Ssamziegil (쌈지길) is a spiral open-gallery shopping mall wrapping around a central courtyard. It's the most popular landmark in Insadong (인사동) for international visitors.

Floor-by-Floor Guide

Floor Contents Highlights
B1 Underground gallery · café Exhibitions · traditional street food
1F Crafts · stationery · accessories Hanji (한지) goods · celadon (도자기) · traditional stationery
2F Clothing · fashion · handmade Modern hanbok (한복) · Korean designer brands
3F Cafés · food · gallery Traditional tea café · injeolmi (인절미) ice cream
4F Rooftop terrace Seoul cityscape · photo spot

Photo Spots & Recommended Route

  • Central spiral staircase: Looking up from below — #1 Instagram shot among international visitors
  • Rooftop terrace: Skyline toward Bugaksan (북악산)
  • B1 gallery entrance: Traditional lantern (등) decoration backdrop

Ssamziegil (쌈지길) Tips

  • Small shops often prefer cash (most accept cards; outdoor stalls = cash)
  • Handmade crafts → limited stock, sell out fast
  • Check special events: [ssamziegil.co.kr](https://ssamziegil.co.kr/)

🍵 Traditional Tea Houses (전통찻집) — Tea Culture Experience

Traditional tea houses (찻집) line the alleys of Insadong (인사동). Sitting floor-level (좌식) and sipping tea from ceramic vessels (다기) is a signature experience for international visitors.

💎 Hanji (한지) & Traditional Craft Shopping

⚠ 3 Things to Know

1. No bargaining: Insadong (인사동) shops run fixed prices — haggling is considered rude.

2. Midday weekends 12–14:00 are crowded: Visit before 12:00 or on weekdays for a calmer experience.

3. No outside food: Some cafés inside Ssamziegil (쌈지길) do not allow outside food/drinks.

❓ FAQ

Q1. What's the must-do in Insadong (인사동)?

Ssamziegil (쌈지길) (the spiral craft mall) and one traditional tea house (찻집). These two alone fill 2 hours comfortably.

Q2. Is there an entrance fee for Ssamziegil (쌈지길)?

Free entry. 24+ craft studios, cafés, and galleries inside — you only pay for purchases.

Q3. Do I need to reserve a tea house (찻집)?

No reservation needed. Expect a 10–20-min wait on weekend afternoons. Weekday mornings are best.

Q4. How long should I spend in Insadong (인사동)?

Shopping + tea + Ssamziegil (쌈지길) = minimum 3 hours. Add a Korean set meal (한정식) and allow 4–5 hours.

Q5. Can I get hanji (한지) or craft items shipped internationally?

Some shops offer international shipping — ask in advance. For small items, carrying in your luggage is easier.

Q6. What's the best time to visit Insadong (인사동)?

Weekend mornings 10:00–12:00 (street stalls active, relatively manageable crowds). Noon to 14:00 is peak.

Q7. Can I combine Insadong (인사동) with Bukchon (북촌)?

Yes. Bukchon (북촌) is a 10-min walk from Anguk Station (안국역). Insadong (인사동) in the morning + Bukchon (북촌) in the afternoon = the classic international heritage day route.

Q8. Are there hands-on craft workshops at Ssamziegil (쌈지길)?

Yes — pottery wheel (물레), hanji (한지) dyeing, knotwork (매듭) classes available via walk-in or reservation (₩15,000–30,000). Check the craft studios on floors 1–2.
