Itaewon Hannam Chinese Fine Dining Guide for Foreigners

> 7 Chinese Restaurants in Itaewon (이태원) and Hannam (한남) Perfect for Foreigners — From Hotel Fine Dining to Local Casual Spots

Itaewon (이태원) and Hannam (한남) are areas where foreigners can experience a variety of ethnic cuisines. The Chinese food cluster here shows strong interest from foreigners (140 mentions on Reddit), yet our site content currently covers 0%. This guide expands on the topic_gap anchor, China Room at Mondrian Hotel, by curating 7 Chinese restaurants in Itaewon (이태원) and Hannam (한남).

1. Reddit·Signal Anchor — China Room at Mondrian Hotel

  • Address: Mondrian Seoul Itaewon Hotel, Hannam-dong, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Hotel Chinese fine dining (Cantonese + Sichuan fusion)
  • Foreigner Fit ★★★: Hotel dining, English menu, sommelier, private room
  • Recommendation: Best fit for foreign fine dining. Perfect for a luxury hotel persona.
  • Price: Course meals ₩80,000 (~$60 / ~430 RMB / ~8,500 JPY) to ₩150,000

2. 7 Chinese Restaurants in Itaewon (이태원) & Hannam (한남)

China House at Shilla Hotel Hannam (신라호텔 한남)

  • Address: Shilla Hotel Hannam (신라호텔 한남)
  • Signature: Authentic Cantonese fine dining
  • Recommendation: The pinnacle of Korean hotel Chinese cuisine. Ideal for foreign business or anniversaries.

Palsun at Grand Hyatt Namsan (그랜드 하얏트 남산)

  • Address: Grand Hyatt Seoul (Namsan (남산) area)
  • Signature: Cantonese course meals + dim sum
  • Recommendation: Michelin-level. A default choice for foreign hotel dining.

Dowon at The Plaza Hotel (더 플라자 호텔)

  • Address: The Plaza Hotel Seoul (더 플라자 호텔 서울)
  • Signature: Cantonese + Sichuan fusion
  • Recommendation: Foreign business meetings. English menu, sommelier.

Jinjin Hannam (진진 한남)

  • Address: Hannam-dong (한남동)
  • Signature: Authentic spicy Sichuan cuisine
  • Recommendation: Perfect for foreigners who love spicy food. Instagram-worthy visuals.

Yunga Myeongga Itaewon (윤가명가 이태원)

  • Address: Itaewon (이태원)
  • Signature: Casual Chinese food + authentic jjajangmyeon (짜장면, Korean-style black bean noodles) and jjambbong (짬뽕, Korean-style spicy seafood noodle soup)
  • Recommendation: An introduction to Korean-style Chinese food for foreigners.

Marahao Hannam (마라하오 한남)

  • Address: Hannam-dong (한남동)
  • Signature: Malatang (마라탕, spicy Chinese hot pot) and Mala Xiangguo (마라샹궈, spicy Chinese stir-fry) (authentic Sichuan (사천) cuisine)
  • Recommendation: For foreign mala fans. English menu.

Manrihyang Itaewon (만리향 이태원)

  • Address: Itaewon Station (이태원역)
  • Signature: Local Chinese restaurant + nofo (노포, 30+ year traditional restaurant) authenticity
  • Recommendation: A hidden discovery for foreigners. Great value for money.

3. Foreigner Fit Tips

English Menu & Service

  • Top Tier (100% English): China Room, China House, Palsun (팔선), Dowon (도원) (Hotel Dining)
  • Mid-Upper Tier: Jinjin (진진), Marahao (마라하오) (Accustomed to serving foreigners)
  • Local Tier: Yunga Myeongga (윤가명가), Manrihyang (만리향) (Korean-focused, picture menu)

Price Range

  • Hotel Fine Dining: ₩80,000 to ₩200,000 per person (course meals)
  • Dining: ₩35,000 to ₩60,000 per person
  • Casual: ₩12,000 to ₩20,000 per person (jjajangmyeon (짜장면) / jjambbong (짬뽕))
  • Malatang (마라탕): ₩15,000 to ₩25,000 per person

By Persona

  • Fine Dining (Anniversary): China House, Palsun (팔선), Dowon (도원)
  • Business Meeting: Dowon (도원), China Room
  • Spicy Food Fan: Jinjin (진진), Marahao (마라하오)
  • Casual / Value for Money: Yunga Myeongga (윤가명가), Manrihyang (만리향)
  • Date: Jinjin (진진), Marahao (마라하오)

Reservations

  • Hotels: Hotel concierge, Catchtable, OpenTable
  • Dining: Naver Reservation (네이버 예약), Catchtable
  • Nofo (노포): Walk-in
