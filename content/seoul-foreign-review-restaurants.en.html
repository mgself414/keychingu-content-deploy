9 Seoul Restaurants — Only Where Foreign Reviews Showed Up First

Subtitle: A grilled-fish old-timer in Jongno 3-ga and a pizza place in Anguk, Middle Eastern falafel in Hongdae and wood-fired pizza in Sinsu-dong, a Chinese restaurant in Seongsan-dong, then frozen pork belly, chicken feet, an old-school set-meal restaurant, and buckwheat noodles in Yeoksam, Gangnam, and Nonhyeon. Only restaurants where foreign-language reviews appeared first on Naver Map made the cut — 9 of them, grouped into 3 zones. Seoul zones are linked by subway, so the routes are three one-day courses. Cafes in the same format are in #1440

🎯 Why "Three-Language Reviews" Is the Bar

There is one selection rule — restaurants on Naver Map (네이버 지도) with English, Japanese, and Chinese reviews together. Instead of Korean popularity rankings, we picked places foreign visitors have already found and left traces in three languages. The judgment is that such a place is less unfamiliar for the next foreign visitor. All 9 venues have reviews in all three languages. However, Deoije (더이제) and Naeng325 (냉325) have only 1 review per language, 3 in total, so the evidence is thin. Review counts are a 2026-11 snapshot, listed for every venue. None of the 9 venues in this piece appeared in other KeyChinGu pieces.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours, closed days, and menu prices are the values the owner registered on Kakao Map (카카오맵) (Kakao Map owner-registered values, 2026-11-23). Prices were checked by each menu's registration date. Where a menu was registered more than 2 years ago, we wrote "check the price on Kakao Map on the day." Set compositions are copied from what the owner wrote in the menu description; for sets with no owner description, we say it is a Kakao Map summary. Summaries written by Kakao Map's AI are labeled "Kakao Map summary"; they are not written by the venue, so treat them as reference only. Dietary cues are only what can be read from menu names and the owner's descriptions. Ask staff before ordering about ingredients and allergies. For stations, we give the exit and distance from Kakao Map directions, and for Naeng325 and Pizzeria Ducky (피제리아 더키) we also add the walking time Kakao Map shows.

① Jongno·Anguk — 2 Venues

One near Anguk Station and one near Jongno 3-ga Station. Hanil Sikdang opens at 07:00, so you can eat breakfast there. Plop closes for a break 15:30–17:00.

  • Plop Anguk (플롭 안국) — a pizza place about 120 m from Exit 2 of Anguk Station. The name on Kakao Map is "Plop Anguk Branch (플롭 안국점)," and the Kakao Map summary says it has a hanok interior and lets you choose several flavors with half-and-half and quarter pizzas. Registered prices are Too Much Pepperoni pizza (투머치 페퍼로니 피자) (S) 19,000 won, Bulgogi Onion Taco pizza (불고기어니언타코 피자) (S) 21,000 won, Mad Cheese Ball Chicken pizza (매드치즈볼닭 피자) (S) 21,000 won, Bacon Mushroom Potato pizza (베이컨머쉬드포테이토 피자) (S) 22,000 won, Shrimp Garlic Gambas pizza (새우갈릭 감바스 피자) (S) 22,000 won, and Green Onion Cream open pasta (대파 크림 오픈파스타) 13,500 won. Pizza prices are for size S. Open daily 11:30–21:30, break 15:30–17:00, last order 21:00, and no closed day is registered. 1st floor, 5 Bukchon-ro 2-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 북촌로2길 5 1층); the nearest station is Anguk Station (Line 3), Exit 2. No parking lot. 10 foreign reviews (5·3·2), and the per-language rating is 5.0 in all three. The Kakao Map summary says it is crowded with office workers at lunch and the interior is small, so for a relaxed meal go in the morning or late afternoon.

② Hongdae·Mapo — 3 Venues

One right in front of Hongik Univ. Station, one in Sinsu-dong near Daeheung Station, and one in Seongsan-dong about 900 m from Mangwon Station. The three are far apart, so pick one per meal. Falachili and Deoije are closed on Monday.

Falafel plate 13,000 won (falafel, pita bread, hummus, vegetables, and fries, with house-made tahini sauce and harissa sauce), falafel sandwich 11,000 won (falafel — fried chickpeas — and vegetables in pita bread), chili cheese burrito 11,000 won (house-made chili con carne, vegetables, cheese, and sour cream), and chili plate 14,000 won (chili, tortilla, sausage, vegetables, and fries). On weekdays it opens only in the evening. 1st floor, 10-8 Yanghwa-ro 23-gil, Mapo-gu (마포구 양화로23길 10-8 1층); the nearest station is Hongik Univ. Station, Exit 3. 11 foreign reviews (5·3·3), and the per-language rating is 5.0 in all three. Dietary cues: falafel is fried chickpeas, tahini is a sesame sauce, and harissa is a hot chili sauce. Chili con carne is chili with meat, and the chili plate contains sausage. The chili cheese burrito contains cheese and sour cream (dairy). Pita bread and tortillas are wheat-flour bread. The Kakao Map summary says it has a variety of vegan menu items, but since it is an AI summary, if you are vegan, ask which menu items are vegan when you order. The Kakao Map summary mentions terrace seating and that pets are allowed. 11 foreign reviews (5·3·3). Dietary cues: many menu items are seafood (shrimp, scallops, oysters, and so on), but because it is Chinese food, meat may be used in the broth and sauces. Mensho-sa is shrimp meat fried between slices of bread, so it contains wheat flour. The mala scallops and eohyang-style dishes may be spicy.

③ Gangnam·Yeoksam·Nonhyeon — 4 Venues

Clustered around Gangnam, Yeoksam, and Nonhyeon stations. Lunch is Namho Sikdang or Mereumil Jinmijip; dinner is Naeng325 or Jippul Dakbal. Jippul Dakbal opens only at 17:00, Namho Sikdang is closed on weekends, and Mereumil Jinmijip is closed on Sunday.

The Kakao Map summary says the Naengsam-only set comes with frozen pork belly, pork jowl (뽈항정살), either yukhoe or seasoned cockles, and steamed egg, so ask about the composition when you order. 🗺 Route — Three One-Day Courses

Seoul zones are linked by subway, so lunch and dinner can be in different zones. Still, a course that links two meals within one zone involves less travel. Check the moves between venues and the time they take with Kakao Map directions on the day.

A. Jongno·Anguk one day

  1. Morning Grilled fish at Hanil Sikdang in Jongno 3-ga (from 07:00)
  2. Lunch or early dinner Plop pizza in Anguk (11:30–15:30, 17:00–21:30). 15:30–17:00 is the break time

B. Hongdae·Mapo one day (Tue–Sun)

  1. Lunch Pizzeria Ducky in Sinsu-dong (from 11:00) or Deoije in Seongsan-dong (11:30–15:00, when going with several people)
  2. Dinner Falachili at Hongik Univ. Station (Tue–Fri from 17:00, Sat·Sun from 12:00)

C. Gangnam·Yeoksam·Nonhyeon one day

  1. Lunch On a weekday, the set meal at Namho Sikdang in Yeoksam (Mon–Fri, before 15:00); on Saturday, Mereumil Jinmijip in Nonhyeon (Mon–Sat, before 15:00)
  2. Dinner Jippul Dakbal near Gangnam Station (17:00–24:00) or the BBQ at Naeng325 in Yeoksam (until 24:00)

On Sunday, Namho Sikdang and Mereumil Jinmijip are closed, Naeng325 opens at 16:00, and Jippul Dakbal at 17:00. On Sunday, none of these 4 venues is open for lunch, so eat lunch on course A or B and come to Gangnam for dinner.

On Monday, Falachili and Deoije are closed. On Sat·Sun Namho Sikdang is closed, and on Sunday Mereumil Jinmijip is closed. Jippul Dakbal does not open before 17:00.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going without checking the day of the week — Namho Sikdang is closed on Sat·Sun, and Mereumil Jinmijip is closed on Sunday. If you plan a Gangnam lunch on a weekend, only Mereumil Jinmijip is left on Saturday, and on Sunday Naeng325 (from 16:00) and Jippul Dakbal (from 17:00) are also dinner-only, so none of the 4 Gangnam venues is open for lunch. On Monday, Falachili and Deoije are closed, so on the Hongdae·Mapo course only Pizzeria Ducky is left. Falachili opens only at 17:00 on weekdays.
  2. Arriving during the break time — Plop closes 15:30–17:00, Deoije and Namho Sikdang 15:00–17:00, and Mereumil Jinmijip 15:00–16:00. For a late lunch, go in before 15:00.
  3. Going to a similarly named venue or a different branch — Hanil Sikdang and Namho Sikdang each have a separate same-named venue in Jeju. Mereumil Jinmijip has a separate Yeoksam Branch (on Teheran-ro 25-gil), and Jippul Dakbal has a separate Nonhyeon Branch. The Kakao Map names also differ from this piece's spellings. Plop shows up as "Plop Anguk Branch (플롭 안국점)" and Pizzeria Ducky as "Pizzeria Ducky (피제리아더키)." In your map app, search by this piece's street addresses, not the venue names.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. If I could pick just one? — The only venue where all three languages reach 5 reviews each is Hanil Sikdang (15 reviews, 5·5·5). It is open 07:00–22:00 and has no closed day registered, so it is easy to fit into a schedule. For dinner, it is Jippul Dakbal Gangnam Branch (13 reviews), with 5 Japanese and 5 Chinese reviews.
  2. What if I don't eat meat or want to avoid raw food? — If you eat fish, Hanil Sikdang's menu is entirely grilled fish. Falachili's falafel is fried chickpeas. The Kakao Map summary says Falachili has a variety of vegan menu items, but since it is an AI summary, ask about the ingredients of each menu item, and the broth ingredients of the cold buckwheat noodles and perilla-oil buckwheat noodles at Mereumil Jinmijip, when you order. If you avoid raw food, watch out for the yukhoe at Naeng325 and the basic side dishes (seasoned raw crab and soy-marinated shrimp, per the Kakao Map summary).
  3. Where is good for eating alone? — The venues with an eating-alone tag on Kakao Map are Hanil Sikdang, Namho Sikdang Yeoksam Main Branch, and Mereumil Jinmijip Nonhyeon Direct Store. However, Namho Sikdang's stews are registered as orders for 2 or more people, so if you are alone, pick a 1-person menu item such as the country-table set meal or grilled fish. Jippul Dakbal has 1-person chicken feet (12,900 won) registered. The sets at Deoije and Naeng325 are meant for sharing, so they suit two or more people better than one.
