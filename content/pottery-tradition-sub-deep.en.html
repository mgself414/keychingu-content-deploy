Gwangju Royal Ceramics Guide — Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum, Gonjiam Ceramic Park, Bunwon Baekja Museum, Gwangju Royal Ceramics Festival

Subtitle: Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum (경기도자박물관) (Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00, closed Mondays, adults 3,000 won) · Gonjiam Ceramic Park (곤지암도자공원) (open at all times) · Bunwon Baekja Museum (분원백자자료관) (10:00–18:00, closed Mondays, free) · Gwangju Royal Ceramics Festival (광주왕실도자페스티벌) (Apr 24–May 5, 2026) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Gwangju (광주) in Gyeonggi-do (경기도) is the home of the gwanyo (관요, government-run royal kilns) that produced ceramics for the royal court for 500 years of the Joseon dynasty. The Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum sits inside the Gonjiam Ceramic Park in Gonjiam-eup (곤지암읍), and in Namjong-myeon (남종면) the Bunwon Baekja Museum stands on the Bunwon-ri (분원리) site, the last gwanyo kiln site of Joseon.

This guide covers only Gwangju's ceramics-related places and festival, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Icheon's pottery village is covered in the #673 Icheon Guide, Yeoju Ceramic World in the #672 Yeoju Guide, and the Gangjin Goryeo Celadon Museum in the #657 Gangjin Guide.

🏛 Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum — Korean Ceramics from the Beginning to the Present

Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum (727 Gyeongchung-daero, Gonjiam-eup, Gwangju-si, Gyeonggi-do (경기도 광주시 곤지암읍 경충대로 727), ☎ 031-799-1500) was built to collect, research, and exhibit materials ranging from the early celadon and white porcelain of Gyeonggi-do, including the Gwangju gwanyo, to modern and contemporary ceramics. It displays important relics and works made by master craftsmen from the dawn of Korean ceramics to the present day.

  • Opening hours: Tuesday–Sunday 10:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, the days of Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Admission (individuals): Adults (age 19 and over) 3,000 won; students (ages 7–19) and military/police 2,000 won. Free for children under 7, adults 65 and over, people with disabilities, and others. Those eligible for a discount or free entry must bring ID.
  • Parking: Free.

🌳 Gonjiam Ceramic Park — A 200,000-Pyeong Park Around the Museum

Gonjiam Ceramic Park (727 Gyeongchung-daero, the same address as the Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum) covers 200,000 pyeong (20만 평) and consists of the indoor Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum and an outdoor park. Most of the park grounds are a Paleolithic site, so there is a Paleolithic site exhibition hall.

  • Sights: A small performance hall and a large performance hall, a children's adventure playground, and a Spanish sculpture park.
  • Traditional kiln site: A place where visitors and wood-fired kiln specialists experience Korean traditional kilns.
  • Craft Hill (공예의 언덕): Eight workshops behind the playground hold craft classes and sometimes run an art flea market.
  • Opening hours: Open at all times, open year-round. Parking available, strollers allowed.

🏺 Bunwon Baekja Museum — The Last Gwanyo Kiln Site of Joseon

Bunwon Baekja Museum (1642-1 Sansu-ro, Namjong-myeon, Gwangju-si (광주시 남종면 산수로 1642-1), ☎ 031-766-8465) was built on the site of Bunwon-ri, the last gwanyo of Joseon. Bunwon-ri was submerged in 1973 when the Paldang Dam was built, so its old appearance does not remain; the museum opened in 2003 in the renovated, closed Bunwon Elementary School (분원초등학교).

  • Exhibits: The 2nd floor is a seminar room, so the relics are on the 1st floor only. On display are Joseon white porcelain from Gwangju kiln sites, the changes in government-run ceramics, and a miniature reconstruction of a kiln site. At the entrance stands the Bunwon memorial stele (분원 선정비) honoring the merits of the kiln-site officials.
  • Experiences: Designing your own white porcelain mug, making a Joseon white porcelain model. Advance reservation required.
  • Opening hours: 10:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, the days of Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Admission: Free. Parking is free.

📅 Gwangju Royal Ceramics Festival — April 24–May 5, 2026

The 2026 edition of the Gwangju Royal Ceramics Festival was held at Gonjiam Ceramic Park from April 24 to May 5, 10:00–17:30, free of charge, under the theme "Royal Dignity Blooming Above the Waterway: Play of Time" (물길 위로 피어난 왕실의 품격 : 시간의 유희).

  • Programs (2026): Gwangju royal ceramics exhibition and sale, a royal ceramics master craftsmen exhibition, busking and fringe performances, a royal ceramics madanggeuk (outdoor theater), A Potter's Day (royal ceramics experience), a river market, and more.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going on a Monday — The Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum and the Bunwon Baekja Museum are both closed every Monday. The park is open at all times.
  2. Going to the Bunwon Baekja Museum experiences without a reservation — Mug design and white porcelain model making require an advance reservation.
  3. Leaving your ID behind when you qualify for a discount — The Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum asks those eligible for a discount or free entry to bring ID.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Do I have to go to the museum and the park separately?

A. No. Gonjiam Ceramic Park and the Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum are registered at the same address (727 Gyeongchung-daero), and the museum is the park's indoor space.

Q. What is the address of the Bunwon Baekja Museum?

A. It is registered at 1642-1 Sansu-ro, Namjong-myeon, Gwangju-si. It is in a different administrative district (Namjong-myeon) from Gonjiam Ceramic Park (Gonjiam-eup).
