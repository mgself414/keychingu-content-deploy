🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places where you can watch and learn Korean traditional performances, mask dance (talchum / 탈춤) and ganggangsullae (강강술래). It covers 1 performance venue in Seoul, 3 mask dance training centers in Andong (안동), Yangju (양주) and Incheon (인천) and 1 mask dance festival in Andong, and 1 ganggangsullae training center (Haenam (해남)). The three mask dance training centers are registered as free of charge.

📍 Seoul — National Jeongdong Theater

National Jeongdong Theater (국립정동극장) (43 Jeongdong-gil (정동길), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-751-1500) opened in 1995 under the name 'Jeongdong Theater (정동극장)', in the spirit of Wongaksa Theater (원각사), the first modern-style theater in Korea. It has staged traditional regular performances year-round since 2000, and later changed its name to 'National Jeongdong Theater'. Along with performances in many genres, it also serves as a second-stage production theater that finds and nurtures early-stage creative works.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Varies with the performance. Closed days: Every Monday. Varies with the performance.
  • Fee: Varies with the performance.

📍 Mask Dance — Hahoe Mask Dance Training Center, Andong International Mask Dance Festival, Yangju Byeolsandae Nori and Eunyul Talchum

The Hahoe Mask Dance Training Center (Talnori Jeonsugwan) (탈놀이전수관) (3-15 Hahoejongga-gil (하회종가길), Pungcheon-myeon (풍천면), Andong-si, ☎ Hahoe Byeolsingut Talnori Preservation Society (하회별신굿탈놀이보존회) 054-854-3664) is where Hahoe Byeolsingut Talnori (하회별신굿탈놀이), carried on in Hahoe Village (하회마을) for more than 800 years, is passed down. Hahoe Byeolsingut Talnori is a National Intangible Heritage mask drama handed down in Hahoe Village since the mid-12th century, in which characters wearing Hahoe masks (하회탈), along with pungmul (풍물), kkwaenggwari (꽹과리) and dance, satirize the society of the time. Transmitters, including the holders of the intangible heritage, perform it.

  • Regular performance: March to December Tuesday to Sunday 14:00~15:00, January to February Saturday and Sunday 14:00~15:00. May vary with the performance. Closed days: March to December every Monday, January to February every Monday to Friday.
  • Fee: Regular performance admission is free. Experience programs: 1-night 2-day experience camp 50,000 won, mask making 10,000 won, learning mask dance 10,000 won.

The 2026 Andong International Mask Dance Festival (2026 안동국제탈춤페스티벌) (239 Yuksa-ro (육사로), Andong-si, ☎ 054-840-3400) is a festival where you see Korea's mask dance, inscribed on the UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity list, together with mask dances from many countries around the world. In 2026 it was held September 24 to October 4 around Jungang Line 1942 Andong Station (중앙선 1942 안동역), Talchum Park (탈춤공원) and the old downtown, and the hours were 10:00~22:00. It was hosted by the Korea Spirit and Culture Foundation (한국정신문화재단).

  • Fee: Free. Some experience programs and performances at the mask dance theater were paid.
  • Programs: Domestic and international mask dance performances, madanggeuk (마당극) and creative plays, the World Mask Play Contest (세계탈놀이경연대회), Daedong Nanjang (대동난장), street parades, and mask making and mask dance learning experiences and more were held.

The Yangju Byeolsandae Nori Training and Education Center (양주별산대놀이전수교육관) (47 Buheung-ro 1399beon-gil (부흥로1399번길), Yangju-si, ☎ 031-840-9986) is where Yangju Byeolsandae Nori (양주별산대놀이) is passed down. Yangju Byeolsandae Nori is a branch of the Seoul and Gyeonggi region's sandae dogam mask drama (산대도감극), a mask dance that has been settled in Yangju for about 300 years. It consists of 8 acts (gwajang / 과장) and 8 scenes, and satirizes the contradictions of yangban (양반) society and the lives of common people through dance, silent drama, humor and blessings. Characters such as a monk who broke his vows, a yangban, a shaman and a servant appear. It was designated a National Intangible Heritage in 1964 and was inscribed on the UNESCO Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2022.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~20:30. Closed days: Legal holidays.
  • Fee: Free. Viewing time: 1 hour. Parking: Free.

The Eunyul Talchum Training Center (은율탈춤전수관) (78 Subongan-gil (수봉안길), Michuhol-gu (미추홀구), Incheon, ☎ 0507-1340-7823) is a training center built to preserve and promote Eunyul Talchum (은율탈춤). Eunyul Talchum is a mask dance that originated in the Eunyul (은율) region of Hwanghae-do (황해도), and was designated a National Intangible Heritage in February 1978. It took root in South Korea when Hwanghae-do mask dance performers came south during the Korean War (6·25 전쟁), and Incheon was later chosen as its home of transmission. In 1983 a training ground was set up inside Subong Park (수봉공원) in Sungui-dong (숭의동), Michuhol-gu. At the outdoor performance hall it holds free performances that invite Eunyul Talchum and mask dance transmitters from other regions.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Fee: Free.

📍 Ganggangsullae — Haenam Ganggangsullae Training Center

The Ganggangsullae Training Center (강강술래 전수관) (12 Gwangwangrejeo-ro (관광레저로), Munnae-myeon (문내면), Haenam-gun, ☎ 061-532-8810) is an experience and promotion hall inside the Usuyeong Tourist Site (우수영관광지) in Haenam. Ganggangsullae, a National Intangible Heritage, is a communal folk game in which women gathered on bright moonlit nights around Chuseok (추석) to sing and dance, and it has been handed down mainly in the seaside villages of South Jeolla (전라남도). At the training center you can learn the origin and transmission history of ganggangsullae, and try an experience of learning ganggangsullae and women's farming songs (부녀농요). The experience must be booked in advance with the Haenam Usuyeong Ganggangsullae Preservation Society (사단법인 해남우수영강강술래진흥보존회). There is a ganggangsullae monument around the training center.

  • Opening hours, closed days and fee: No registered value.
  • Parking: Free.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The Hahoe mask dance regular performance is held only on weekends in January and February — March to December it runs Tuesday to Sunday, and January to February Saturday and Sunday, 14:00~15:00.
  2. The ganggangsullae experience must be booked in advance — book with the Haenam Usuyeong Ganggangsullae Preservation Society.
  3. The 2026 Andong International Mask Dance Festival has ended — it was held September 24 to October 4.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where can I watch a mask dance performance for free?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, the Hahoe Mask Dance Training Center regular performance (March to December Tuesday to Sunday 14:00, January to February Saturday and Sunday 14:00) is free. Eunyul Talchum Training Center holds free performances at its outdoor performance hall.

Q. Where can I learn mask dance myself?

A. At the Hahoe Mask Dance Training Center you can try learning mask dance (10,000 won), mask making (10,000 won) and the 1-night 2-day experience camp (50,000 won).
