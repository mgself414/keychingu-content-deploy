Gapyeong and Yangpyeong Nature Trip Guide: Jara Island, Gapyeong Rail Bike, Cheongpyeong Lakeside, Yumyeongsan National Recreation Forest, Jungmisan Observatory in Yangpyeong

Subtitle: Jara Island (always open) · Gapyeong Rail Bike (6 departures on weekdays, 7 on weekends) · Cheongpyeong Lakeside (always open) · Yumyeongsan National Recreation Forest (daily 09:00~18:00) · Jungmisan Observatory (20:30~01:30, reservation only) · 2026 Jara Island Flower Festa (9/12~10/5, ended) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to enjoy nature in Gapyeong and Yangpyeong, Gyeonggi-do. They are Jara Island on the Bukhangang (북한강), the rail bike that crosses the river, the shore of Cheongpyeong Lake, Yumyeongsan Recreation Forest, and the Jungmisan Observatory for watching stars. Nami Island (남이섬), The Garden of Morning Calm (아침고요수목원), Dumulmeori (두물머리) and more are in the #754 Gapyeong and Yangpyeong Day Trip Guide.

The Jungmisan Observatory is reservation only and has registered operating hours of 20:30~01:30.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Bukhangang — Jara Island and the Jara Island Flower Festa

Jara Island (자라섬) [Jaraseom-ro 60 (자라섬로 60), Gapyeong-eup (가평읍), Gapyeong-gun, ☎ 031-8078-8028] is an island formed in the Bukhangang (북한강) when Cheongpyeong Dam (청평댐) was built in 1943, about 800m in a straight line from Nami Island (남이섬). It got its name as the island that a 'hill shaped like a softshell turtle' looks upon. It is made up of four islands: Dongdo, Seodo, Jungdo and Namdo. Seodo has an auto camping ground, Jungdo has an ecological culture park with a lawn plaza more than 100m across, and at the western end of the camping ground is the Nature and Ecology Theme Park Ihwawon (이화원). It is where the Jarasum International Jazz Festival (자라섬국제재즈페스티벌) is held.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

2026 Colorful Garden Jara Island Flower Festa (2026 Colorful Garden 자라섬 꽃 페스타) [Jara Island (자라섬), ☎ Gapyeong County Office Tourism Division (가평군청 관광과) 031-580-2601] was held from September 12 to October 5, 2026, 08:00~18:00. Of the 7,000 won admission, 5,000 won was refunded in local currency, and Gapyeong residents entered free.

  • Registered 2026 programs: A flower garden healing course, busking and band performances, sales of local agricultural specialties, experiences such as balloon art, Bukhangang boat service, and a round-trip electric vehicle from the Jara Island entrance to the event site.

📍 Rail and Lake — Gapyeong Rail Bike and Cheongpyeong Lakeside

Gapyeong Rail Bike (가평 레일바이크) [Jangteo-gil 14 (장터길 14), Gapyeong-eup (가평읍), Gapyeong-gun, ☎ 031-582-7788] is a round-trip course that starts at Gapyeong Station (가평역), passes the Bukhangang railway bridge and the zelkova tunnel, and returns from Gyeonggang Station (경강역). The Bukhangang railway bridge is 30m high. You may not ride beyond the seating capacity. Avoid sudden stops while running, getting off without permission and horseplay with the person next to you, and drinking and smoking are prohibited. You must get off only after it has come to a complete stop.

  • Opening hours: Weekdays 09:00~18:00, weekends and public holidays 09:00~18:30.
  • Departure times: 6 departures on weekdays (10:00, 11:30, 13:30, 15:00, 16:30, 18:00), 7 on weekends and public holidays (09:00, 10:30, 12:00, 14:00, 15:30, 17:00, 18:30).
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Free.

Cheongpyeong Lakeside (청평호반) [Yumyeong-ro 1906-15 (유명로 1906-15), Seorak-myeon (설악면), Gapyeong-gun, ☎ Gapyeong-gun Tourist Information Center (가평군 관광안내소) 031-582-8830] is the shore of Cheongpyeong Lake (청평호), formed when Cheongpyeong Dam was built on the Bukhangang, and one of the 8 Scenic Views of Gapyeong. It is about 50km from Seoul. On the deck path 'Cheongpyeong Lake Trail' (청평 호수길), built by setting steel pillars into the rock cliff of Ureopsan (울업산), you can walk while looking at the rocky mountain and the lake, and it is part of the Sinseonbong loop trail (신선봉 순환 둘레길).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Strollers: Not allowed.

📍 Forest and Stars — Yumyeongsan National Recreation Forest and Jungmisan Observatory

Yumyeongsan National Recreation Forest (국립유명산자연휴양림) [Yumyeongsan-gil 79-53 (유명산길 79-53), Seorak-myeon (설악면), Gapyeong-gun, ☎ 031-589-5487] is a recreation forest that opened in 1989. It has a 2.8km walking trail, a forest training center, log cabins and a campground. In a native plant garden of about 79,338㎡ it has planted 42 species of woody plants and 322 species of herbaceous plants, and it has Hyangno Botanical Garden (향로식물원), a rock garden, a wetland plant garden and a lawn plaza. It offers free forest interpretation, forest healing programs and woodcraft experiences.

  • Opening hours: Daily opening 09:00~18:00. For lodging, check-in is 15:00 and check-out is 11:00.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available. Capacity: 3,000 people.

Jungmisan Observatory (중미산 천문대) [Jungmisan-ro 1268 (중미산로 1268), Okcheon-myeon (옥천면), Yangpyeong-gun, ☎ 0507-1319-0306] is a learning center for astronomy, nature and science experiences. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the first institution in Korea to offer astronomy, space and science experience learning for infants and children, and one of the places near the Seoul metropolitan area where you can see the most stars.

  • Opening hours: 20:30~01:30. It is reservation only, and the hours change with the number of people.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.
  • Programs: Same-day constellation tour, group programs (1 night 2 days, night, day, full day schedules) and more.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The Jungmisan Observatory is reservation only — The registered operating hours are 20:30~01:30, and they change with the number of people.
  2. The Gapyeong Rail Bike departs only at set times — 6 departures on weekdays, 7 on weekends and public holidays.
  3. The 2026 Jara Island Flower Festa has ended — It was held from September 12 to October 5.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. What time does the Gapyeong Rail Bike depart?

A. According to the information registered with the Korea Tourism Organization, it departs on weekdays at 10:00, 11:30, 13:30, 15:00, 16:30 and 18:00, and on weekends and public holidays at 09:00, 10:30, 12:00, 14:00, 15:30, 17:00 and 18:30. It is open year-round.

Q. Where can you see the stars?

A. The Jungmisan Observatory (중미산 천문대) in Yangpyeong. It operates 20:30~01:30 by reservation, and it has a same-day constellation tour program.
