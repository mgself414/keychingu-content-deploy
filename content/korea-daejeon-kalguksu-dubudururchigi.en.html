🍳 Tofu Duruchigi deep (Daejeon exclusive signature)

Signature

  • Name: Tofu + Duruchigi (seasoned stir-fry)
  • Ingredients: Tofu + pork (or kimchi) + seasoning (red pepper powder, soy sauce, garlic)
  • Category: Daejeon Nopo Exclusive Signature·Seoul·Busan·Daegu
  • Origin: 1970 ~ Start of Daejeon Dunsan and Yuseong Nopo

Foreign Pain Points

  • "Tofu = regular tofu dish" Misconception
  • Tofu + Spicy + Pork = Daejeon Mania Matrix
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★ (Spicy Mania Matrix)
  • Price: 12,000~22,000 won/2-3 people

Mania Fair

  • Tofu Duruchigi + Makgeolli = Korea #1 Mania Fair ([#687 cross](#687))
