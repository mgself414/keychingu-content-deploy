📜 Is Yajang Legal? — The Outdoor-Dining Conditions

  • Food Sanitation Act (식품위생법) enforcement rule, Article 42(1)13: a rest-area eatery, general restaurant, or bakery that serves food at an outside space adjoining the premises needs documents proving a legitimate right to use that space. In other words, yajang is conditioned on "space attached to the shop plus a right to use it," which is different from unlicensed selling on a public road. MFDS opened a legislative notice (2020-137, dated 2020-04-06). The full text was available only through a law-mirror site, so the effective date (presumed amended 2020-12-31, in force 2021-01) could not be confirmed officially. law.go.kr returned a traffic error. No separate Seoul rule was confirmed.
  • So the old guide's claim that every spot is a legal outdoor-seating spot cannot be asserted. This guide covers streets only.

🍺 By Street — Where to Sit

  • Euljiro Nogari Alley (을지로 노가리골목): Euljiro 3-ga Station (을지로3가역) Exit 3, 86 m. The only official page that mentions outdoor tables is VisitKorea. Hours and days off vary by shop, and the two official pages disagree on the address, so find it by the station exit.
  • Jongno 3-ga Pocha Street (종로3가 포장마차거리) and Ikseon-dong (익선동): pocha stalls at Exit 6, Ikseon-dong at Exit 4 (123 m). Time Out ranked it the world's coolest neighborhood of 2026 (#585).
  • Gwangjang Market (광장시장) Food Alley: daily 09:00–23:00, open Sundays too (the main market is closed). Bindaetteok (빈대떡) and Mayak Gimbap (마약김밥).
  • Hongdae Red Road (홍대 레드로드): Exits 9 and 8, with the busking zone at R2. The shops are inside the side alleys.
  • Hangang Park (한강공원): open 24 hours. The Banpo Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (반포 달빛무지개분수) runs 3 shows (per the June 1 notice; October operation unconfirmed). Chimaek (치맥) at Yeouido and Banpo depends on park rules and the delivery vendor.

🚇 Getting Around at Night

  • Subway about 05:30 to midnight, varying by station (VisitSeoul) — check the sign inside the station for the last train.
  • Owl Bus (올빼미버스): per the April 2022 Seoul press release, 14 routes, 23:00–06:00, 2,150 won by card at the time (N51, N75, N72, and others). Current routes and fares are unverified — check the Seoul transit information. Late-night bus adult fare 2,500 won (Seoul city; revisions after 2022 unverified).
  • The 1330 chat service is available Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 (Seoul city). The phone number is official.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Searching for the old 10 names — zero official confirmation.

2. Trusting "open until 2 a.m." — it varies by shop. There are no official hours.

3. Sitting anywhere along the roadside — yajang requires space attached to the shop plus a right to use it.

4. Trusting the 2022 Owl Bus routes — currently unverified.

5. Gwangjang main market on Sunday — closed. The food alley is open.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Where is yajang most certain? — Manseon Hof (만선호프) in Nogari Alley (sidewalk tables, 12:00–24:00) and Mullae Pocha 1422 (물래포차1422) in Mullae (rooftop, 15:00–03:00) — both listed.

2. Until what time? — No official hours (varies by shop). The subway runs to midnight.

3. What about prices? — Not officially posted.

4. Is it legal? — Outdoor dining is allowed when the space adjoins the shop and a right to use it is proven (MFDS). It differs from street to street.

5. How do I get home at night? — Subway before midnight; after that, a taxi or the Owl Bus (recheck the routes).

6. What about English? — 1330. English menus at individual shops are on-site matters.
