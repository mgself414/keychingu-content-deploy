🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to visit in Seocho-gu (서초구), Seoul. They are Seorae Village (서래마을), where many French people live, and Montmartre Park (몽마르뜨공원) above it, Seoripul Park (서리풀공원) linked by the Nuedari Bridge (누에다리), the National Library of Korea (국립중앙도서관) and the Seoul Arts Center (예술의전당), Umyeonsan (우면산), which you can climb from the city, Seocho Cultural Art Park (서초문화예술공원) in Yangjae-dong (양재동), and the wedding goods market at the Express Bus Terminal (고속터미널). The parks and mountains are registered as open year-round and always open. Banpo Hangang Park (반포한강공원) and the Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (달빛무지개분수) are in the #702 Banpo Bridge Moonlight Rainbow Fountain Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Seorae Village and Hill Parks — Seorae Village, Montmartre Park and Seoripul Park

Seorae Village (서래마을) [Seorae-ro 32 (서래로 32), Seocho-gu (서초구), ☎ 02-120] is a neighborhood with French-style restaurants, cafes, wine bars and shops. Its name means "the stream in front of the village winds around and around". In 1985 the French School in Seoul (서울 프랑스 학교) moved here, French people began to live together in the area, and people from other countries moved in as well. The street has the Seoul French School and a road sign reading "Attention Ecole" in French.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Stores may differ from store to store. Fee: Free.

Montmartre Park (몽마르뜨공원) [Banpo-dong (반포동), Seocho-gu (서초구), ☎ Seocho-gu Office Green City Division (서초구청 푸른도시과) 02-2155-5483] is a park that opened in 2003 above Seorae Village. It got this name because it is close to Seorae Village, where many French people live. It has poems by French poets and sculptures, busts of Vincent van Gogh (빈센트 반 고흐), Paul Gauguin (폴 고갱) and Pablo Picasso (파블로 피카소), the "Lee Mi-ja Garden" (이미자 뜰), a memorial space for the singer Lee Mi-ja (이미자), and a playground. If you cross the Nuedari Bridge at the end of the park, it leads to Seoripul Park.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Not available.

Seoripul Park (서리풀공원) [Seocho-dong (서초동), Seocho-gu (서초구), ☎ Seocho-gu Office Green City Division (서초구청 푸른도시과) 02-2155-6861] was off-limits because a military unit had been there since the 1970s, and it was created in 2019 after the unit moved away in 2015. A walking path of about 4km, running across the middle of Seocho-gu from Banpo-dong (반포동) to Bangbae-dong (방배동), continues to Montmartre Park. The Nuedari Bridge and the Seoripul Bridge (서리풀다리) connect paths cut off by roads, and you can see the Han River (한강) to the north and Umyeonsan to the south.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Not available.

📍 Books and Performances — National Library of Korea and Seoul Arts Center

The National Library of Korea (국립중앙도서관) [Banpo-daero 201 (반포대로 201), Seocho-gu (서초구), ☎ 02-590-0500] is a library that collects and preserves the nation's documents. It moved from Namsan (남산) to its present site on May 28, 1988, and consists of a main building and annexes.

  • Opening hours: Thursday to Tuesday 09:00~18:00, Wednesday 09:00~21:00.
  • Closed days: Second and fourth Monday of each month, public holidays other than Sundays. Fee: Free.

The Seoul Arts Center (예술의전당) [Nambusunhwan-ro 2406 (남부순환로 2406), Seocho-gu (서초구), ☎ 1668-1352] is a complex arts and culture space at the foot of Umyeonsan. It has an Opera House (오페라하우스) for opera, plays, dance and musicals, a Concert Hall (음악당) with a hall dedicated to classical music, the Hangaram Art Museum (한가람미술관), the Hangaram Design Museum (한가람디자인미술관) and the Seoul Calligraphy Museum (서울서예박물관). There are 7 performance halls and 3 art museums or museums in total. Events such as outdoor screenings and the World Music Fountain are described as being held at the Stairs Plaza (계단광장) and the Music Plaza (음악광장).

  • Opening hours, closed days and fee: Vary by performance and exhibition.
  • Parking: Free if you leave within 30 minutes, regular rate 1,000 won every 10 minutes. 6,000 won for 5 hours for performance audiences, 4,000 won for 3 hours for exhibition audiences.

📍 Umyeonsan — Somangtap, Nature Ecological Park and Seoul Trail Course 10

Umyeonsan Somangtap (우면산 소망탑) [Seocho-dong (서초동), Seocho-gu (서초구), ☎ Seocho-gu Office Green City Division (서초구 푸른도시과) 02-2155-6883] is a stone tower at the summit of Umyeonsan. Visitors placed stones one by one with their wishes, and they became a large tower. Umyeonsan lies on the border between Seocho-gu and Gwacheon-si (과천시), Gyeonggi (경기도), and its name comes from its resemblance to a sleeping cow. The slope is gentle, and most adults are described as reaching the Somangtap in a little over 1 hour. There is an observation deck near the tower, and you can see the night view.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Umyeonsan Nature Ecological Park (우면산자연생태공원) [Umyeon-dong (우면동), Seocho-gu (서초구), ☎ Seocho-gu Office Green City Division (서초구청 푸른도시과) 02-2155-6883] is a park that opened on July 23, 2004. Umyeonsan is 293m high, and on its southern foot there is a dolmen (지석묘), a Bronze Age site. Around the walking paths and hiking trails there are a mineral spring, Daeseongsa Temple (대성사), the tomb of Jeong Do-jeon (정도전) and the Seoul Arts Center.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Seoul Trail Course 10 Umyeonsan Course (서울둘레길 10코스 우면산코스) [Yangjae-dong (양재동), Seocho-gu (서초구), ☎ 02-779-7902~4] is a course themed "Seoul seen from the mountains" among the Seoul Trail (서울둘레길), which circles Seoul in 21 courses totaling 156.5km. It runs from Maeheon Citizens' Forest (매헌시민의숲) to the Sadang Station (사당역) fork, and the slope is gentle. It is close to Yangjae Citizens' Forest Station (양재시민의숲역) and Sadang Station on the subway.

  • Length and time: 7.6km in total, about 3 hours 20 minutes. Opening hours: Always open. Fee: Free.

📍 Yangjae and Banpo — Seocho Cultural Art Park and Gangnam Express Bus Terminal Wedding Goods Market

Seocho Cultural Art Park (서초문화예술공원) [Baumoe-ro 12-gil 40 (바우뫼로12길 40), Seocho-gu (서초구), ☎ Management Office (관리사무소) 02-2155-8601] is a park created in May 1994 in Zone 3 of Maeheon Citizens' Forest (매헌시민의숲). It has a sculpture park, a special exhibition hall, an outdoor stage and a meeting plaza, and two metasequoia paths.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

The Gangnam Express Bus Terminal Wedding Goods Market (강남고속터미널 혼수상가) [Sinbanpo-ro 194 (신반포로 194), 4th floor, Seocho-gu (서초구), ☎ 02-533-2489] is a wedding goods and home interior shopping arcade that mainly sells bedding, curtains and silk fabrics. Wedding goods shops have been moving in since 1982, and there are more than 250 bedding shops, more than 100 curtain specialty shops and more than 40 sewing workshops. It also sells hanbok (한복), embroidery goods and fabric.

  • Opening hours: 09:30~19:30. Closed days: Every Sunday.
  • Parking: 1,500 won every 15 minutes, 6,000 won for 1 hour.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The National Library of Korea is closed on the second and fourth Monday of each month — on Wednesdays it is open until 21:00.
  2. Parking is not available at Montmartre Park and Seoripul Park — Umyeonsan Somangtap, Umyeonsan Nature Ecological Park and Seocho Cultural Art Park have parking.
  3. The Express Bus Terminal Wedding Goods Market is closed on Sundays — it is open 09:30~19:30.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. How long does it take to reach Umyeonsan Somangtap, and to walk the Seoul Trail Umyeonsan Course?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, most adults reach the Somangtap in a little over 1 hour. Seoul Trail Course 10 Umyeonsan Course is 7.6km in total, about 3 hours 20 minutes.

Q. Which park is close to Seorae Village?

A. Montmartre Park is above Seorae Village, and if you cross the Nuedari Bridge at the end of the park, it leads to Seoripul Park.
