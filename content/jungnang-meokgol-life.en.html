1. "Jungnang (중랑) = Meokgol (먹골) only" — Lack of awareness of Myeonmok (면목)·Sangbong (상봉)·Mangwoo (망우) deep dive: While #184 covered Meokgol (먹골), Myeonmok (면목) (Cheonsanga Jang-eo-ga (천상애장어가)), Mangwoo (망우) (Urim Market (우림시장)·Mangwoo Sikdang (망우식당)), and Sangbong (상봉) (Izakaya·Cafe) are separate clusters.

2. "Unaware of Jungnang (중랑) Izakayas" — Okinara (오키나라) (10,046 reviews) is strong: Jungnang's (중랑) #1 establishment is a Japanese-style izakaya (Japanese-style pub) — strong potential for foreign enthusiasts (especially the Japanese segment). High proportion of Japanese reviews.

3. "Unaware of Jungnang (중랑) hiking" — Lack of awareness of Yongmasan (용마산)·Achasan (아차산) trail access: Access to Yongmasan (용마산)·Achasan (아차산) hiking trails is possible from Jungnang (중랑). #177 Nowon (노원)·Dobong (도봉) + Jungnang (중랑) Yongma (용마)·Acha (아차) combined = Full experience of the 5 mountains in Northeast Gangbuk (강북)·Gangdong (강동).
