32 Hongdae (홍대) Vintage Shop Tour

One-Line Intro

Hongdae (홍대) vintage: Seoul's indie fashion scene where second-hand becomes statement pieces.

Vintage shops in Hongdae (홍대) are far more than thrift stores — they're a curated mecca for second-hand fashion.

Who Should Go

  • Travelers interested in vintage fashion and second-hand clothing
  • Travelers who want to dive deep into K-indie fashion culture

Recommended Route (3 Hours)

1. 13:00 — Exit 9, Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역 9번 출구): First sweep of the street vintage stalls (items from ₩5,000–15,000)

2. 13:30 — Noriter (놀이터) Vintage: Hongdae's main vintage shop; browse the season's highlights

3. 14:15 — Viva Vintage (비바 빈티지): Denim & leather specialists; strong value-for-money picks

4. 15:00 — Hongdae Flea Market (홍대 프리마켓) (weekends only): Individual-seller flea market

5. 15:30 — Yeonnam-dong (연남동) Vintage Alley: Relaxed browsing paired with a café stop

6. 16:30 — April Vintage (에이프릴 빈티지): Japanese vintage pieces; premium curation

Tips for Foreign Visitors

  • Useful Korean Phrases
  • "입어봐도 돼요?" — "Can I try this on?"
  • "M사이즈 있어요?" — "Do you have size M?"
  • "이거 빈티지 맞아요?" — "Is this authentic vintage?"
  • Cultural Notes: Most vintage shops operate at fixed prices — bargaining is generally not accepted. Trying on clothes is usually fine.
  • Payment: Smaller vintage shops tend to be cash-only. Larger stores typically accept cards.
  • Best Bargain Spot: The weekend Hongdae Flea Market (홍대 프리마켓) is the one place where individual sellers participate directly and prices are negotiable.

Simple English Summary

Hongdae's (홍대) vintage scene is one of Seoul's most vibrant, with dozens of curated second-hand stores catering to fashion-forward shoppers. Prices are generally lower than equivalent vintage shops in Tokyo or New York. Key shops include Noriter (놀이터) (70s–90s pieces), Viva Vintage (비바 빈티지) (denim specialists), and April Vintage (에이프릴 빈티지) (Japanese vintage curation). On weekends, the Hongdae Flea Market (홍대 프리마켓) at the Playground features individual sellers with bargaining possible. Most shops open from noon; bring some cash for smaller stalls.

Estimated Total Budget

  • Budget: ₩15,000 (1–2 items from street stalls)
  • Standard: ₩60,000 (1–2 curated vintage pieces)
  • Splurge: ₩150,000+ (multiple Japanese vintage finds)
