🎯 Why a Standalone Hongdae Vintage Guide

The Hongdae area (Hongdae (홍대) · Yeonnam (연남) · Hapjeong (합정) · Mangwon (망원)) is Korea's #1 vintage and secondhand zone — a blend of Japanese, American, and European vintage clothing with Korea's own thrift markets. It's the top pick for trendy, indie-minded foreign visitors.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Expecting new-clothes pricing — Vintage runs ₩30K–150K standard. Designer vintage can hit ₩200K+. Some price negotiation is possible.

2. Skipping the size check — Vintage clothing sizing varies (Japanese and American vintage labels differ from Korean). Always try items on.

3. Assuming every shop takes cards — Larger shops accept cards, but smaller shops and markets are cash-only. Carry ₩30K–50K in cash.

⚙ FAQ

Q1. Is English service and card payment available?

A. Some Yeonnam and Hongdae vintage shops have English menus. Pricing and size labels are foreigner-friendly. Payment splits roughly 70% card / 30% cash.

Q2. What's a good one-day course?

A. 11:00 Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역) Exit 3 → 11:30 Yeonnam Vintage Alley → 13:00 lunch → 14:00 Hapjeong vintage → 16:00 Mangwon vintage → 17:00 wrap up at a café.

Q3. Recommendations by price range?

A. Budget ₩15K–50K: Mangwon and Gwangjang thrift. Standard ₩50K–150K: Yeonnam and Hongdae vintage. Splurge ₩200K+: Hapjeong curated shops and designer vintage.

Q4. What are the popular shops?

A. Yeonnam Dongjin Market's vintage row, Hapjeong's curated shops, and the basement thrift row at Gwangjang Market (#50). Shops change often — check Instagram beforehand.

Q5. Can this be combined with Gwangjang Market thrift?

A. Yes — pairing #50 Gwangjang Market's thrift row with a Hongdae vintage deep dive makes the best one-day course.
