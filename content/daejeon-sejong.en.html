🎯 Why the Daejeon Sejong Full Guide?

Daejeon = Korea's science and KTX (Korea Train eXpress) central hub (KTX from Seoul to Daejeon takes 1 hour). Sejong = Korea's administrative capital (adjacent to KTX). For foreign enthusiasts + Hanwha Eagles ([#240 KBO Baseball](240_kbo_baseball.md)) integrated.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Daejeon = Just a Transfer Point": Daejeon = Korea's #1 science hub + KAIST + Expo + foreign enthusiasts.

2. "Sungsimdang (성심당) = Everywhere": Daejeon's Sungsimdang (성심당) = Korea's #1 bakery + foreign vloggers' #1 spot (twigim-soboro (fried streusel bread) · buchu-ppang (chive bread)).

3. "Sejong = Inaccessible": Sejong = Korea's administrative capital + Government Complex + foreign enthusiasts.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Foreigner's Daejeon Itinerary: KTX (Korea Train eXpress) from Seoul to Daejeon takes 1 hour + Sejong (세종) is adjacent.

2. Foreigner's Top Priority in Daejeon: Expo, Science Museum, KAIST, Sungsimdang (성심당), Hanwha Eagles Park.

3. Foreigner's Daejeon Food: Sungsimdang (성심당), kalguksu (Korean knife-cut noodles), dwaeji-jjigae (spicy pork stew).

4. Foreigner's 1 Night, 2 Days in Daejeon·Sejong: Day 1 Daejeon city center · Expo · Sungsimdang (성심당) / Day 2 Sejong Government Complex · Lake Park.
