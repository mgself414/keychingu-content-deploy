⚠ 3 Common Payment·SIM Mistakes

1. "Cash only is needed" — not knowing cards & QR: Assuming cash. In reality, 99% card acceptance + Apple Pay (2023–) + Samsung Pay + WeChat Pay & LINE Pay are foreigner-friendly.

2. "SIM only at Incheon Airport" — not using eSIM: Only knowing the airport. In reality, Airalo & Holafly eSIMs (download the app + scan a QR code, activate before arriving in Korea) are the enthusiast pick.

3. "WiFi only at hotels" — not knowing public Seoul WiFi: Hotels only. In reality, public Seoul WiFi (all major subway stations, parks & tourist spots) + free WiFi at cafes & convenience stores.
