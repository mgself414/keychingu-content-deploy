Korea Payment and Connectivity Guide: Your First Day in Order, eSIM, Transit Card, Card Payments, Currency Exchange and Tax Refund

Subtitle: KT foreign visitor eSIM 5 days KRW 27,500, 7 days KRW 35,000 (11GB a day, then 5Mbps) · Climate Card (기후동행카드) short-term pass 1 day KRW 5,000 to 7 days KRW 20,000 (airport buses excluded) · Public Wi-Fi SEOUL_Secure (ID and password seoul) · 14 exchange counters at Incheon Terminal 1 · Tax refund from KRW 15,000 per receipt, instant refund under KRW 1,000,000 per purchase — checked with the carriers, T-money, Seoul Metropolitan Government (서울시), Incheon International Airport Corporation (인천국제공항공사) and Korea Tourism Organization (VisitKorea)

ISSUE 03 · #431 · 2026-05-26

🎯 How to use this guide

When the arrivals hall doors slide open, the carrier booths, the exchange boards with flickering rates and the glow of the convenience store all hit you at once among the sound of suitcase wheels. Your phone still says "No Service" and there is not one won note in your wallet. This guide sorts what to do, and in what order, from that moment until you reach the city, into five branches: connectivity, transit card, card payments, currency exchange and tax refund.

The figures are only the values read on each organization's and company's website on 2026-11-01. Airport facility figures are for Incheon Terminal 1. For each branch, the step-by-step procedure continues in a dedicated manual below.

📶 Connectivity: the first thing in the arrivals hall

  • KT foreign visitor SIM and eSIM (data plans): 1 day KRW 6,500 · 3 days KRW 18,000 · 5 days KRW 27,500 · 7 days KRW 35,000 · 10 days KRW 38,500 · 30 days KRW 71,500. High speed up to 11GB a day, then up to 5Mbps for the rest of the day (reset at 00:00 every day). You must install it within 90 days of purchase, and an eSIM's period runs in 24-hour units from the moment you install the QR code — if you install it before departure, days are used up before you even reach Korea. The QR code installs only once, and if you delete it, you cannot get it again. After purchase, no refunds or device changes are possible, so first check that your phone supports eSIM and is carrier-unlocked. The number comes as 010 and is activated after entry is confirmed. To make calls, you must top up at least KRW 5,500 (KT user guide, 2026-10).
  • LG U+ Korea eSIM: 1 day KRW 6,500 · 3 days KRW 18,000 · 5 days KRW 27,500. The roaming center at Incheon Terminal 1, arrivals floor 1, Gate F is open 24 hours, and Gate A is open 06:00~22:00 (LG U+).
  • Incheon Terminal 1 carrier booths: the three carriers' roaming centers total 11 (KT 4, LG U+ 3, SKT 4), and each company has 1 that is open 24 hours (Incheon International Airport Corporation (인천국제공항공사)).
  • Seoul public Wi-Fi: the names are SEOUL (press the "general access" button each time you connect) and SEOUL_Secure (ID and password are both seoul, connects automatically once set up). It covers major streets, traditional markets, parks, streams, bus stops, tourist attractions and city buses. It is a network anyone can use, so do not enter card numbers or make financial transactions (Seoul Metropolitan Government (서울시)).

📸 With a KT eSIM, if you get off the plane, join the Incheon Airport Wi-Fi and install the QR code, you lose not a single day. For other companies' eSIMs, first check on the sales page when the period starts. For step-by-step screens see #1303 eSIM Activation Manual, and for the Wi-Fi connection screens see #1302 Public Wi-Fi Manual.

🚇 Transit card: you still cannot pass the gates with a foreign card

Seoul subway gates do not yet let you tap in with a foreign card. Seoul Metropolitan Government (서울시) plans to replace the terminals on Lines 1 to 8 by 2027 (Seoul Metropolitan Government (서울시), 2025-10). So you need one transit card on day one.

  • T-money: card price about KRW 3,000 (more for some designs), top-up separate. The T-money Travel Card, which comes with tourist discounts, is about KRW 4,000. When you get your remaining balance back, a KRW 500 fee is deducted, and the card price is not refunded. Convenience store refund limits are under KRW 20,000 at GS25 and KRW 30,000 or less at CU and emart24 (T-money).
  • Climate Card (기후동행카드) short-term pass: 1 day KRW 5,000 · 2 days KRW 8,000 · 3 days KRW 10,000 · 5 days KRW 15,000 · 7 days KRW 20,000, with the physical card at KRW 3,000 separate. The period is counted in calendar days starting from the day you top up — if you top up late at night you will hardly get to use the first day, so top up the next morning. The Shinbundang Line, GTX-A, buses from other cities and provinces, and intercity and airport buses are not covered, and the Airport Railroad works only when you get off at Incheon Airport Station — you cannot use it on the way from Incheon Airport into Seoul (Seoul Metropolitan Government (서울시) and T-money). It is sold at kiosks in Line 1 to 8 stations and at convenience stores near stations.
  • WOWPASS: the card issued at kiosks or via the app is KRW 5,000, and you need your passport (Korea Tourism Organization (VisitKorea)). The T-money combo card TRAVEL CARD+ (WOWPASS) sold at convenience stores is KRW 6,000 (Tmoney (티머니)). It has T-money functions built in, so you do not need to buy a separate T-money. You top it up by exchanging foreign currency into won and use it as a transit card and payment card, and you can withdraw cash up to KRW 100,000 at a time for a KRW 1,000 fee (WOWPASS).
  • Some top-up machines accept cash only. Keep a little won on hand for your first top-up.

For a table to choose among the three by itinerary, see #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards, and for transfer rules see #238 T-money Payment and Transfers.

💳 Card payments: one foreign card, and a little cash in your wallet

  • Foreign cards: hotels, department stores and regular shops widely accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express, but some shops do not take cards, so ask first (Korea Tourism Organization (VisitKorea)). Where cash is easier is covered in #1301 Where You Need Cash.
  • Apple Pay: on Apple's official support list, the only Korean card issuer is Hyundai Card (현대카드). There is no official notice that Apple Pay works at Korean shops with foreign-issued cards — bring a physical card (Apple).
  • Alipay, WeChat Pay and other foreign mobile payments: 17 foreign mobile payment apps can pay at ZeroPay (제로페이) QR merchants (Korea.net, 2024-11). For which Chinese-region apps work where and which do not, see #1333 Chinese Travelers' Payment Manual for Korea.
  • Japan's LINE Pay: the Japanese service ended on April 30, 2025 (LY Corporation). Travelers coming from Japan should prepare another way to pay.

💱 Exchange and ATMs: at the airport, only what you need for day one

  • Incheon Terminal 1: 14 exchange counters (6 on floor 1, 8 on floor 3), of which 6 are open 24 hours. 21 ATMs, of which 10 are open 24 hours (Incheon International Airport Corporation (인천국제공항공사)).
  • Banks: weekdays 09:00~16:00 (Korea Tourism Organization (VisitKorea)). Bank branches at the airport are also closed on weekends and public holidays.
  • Passport: many places ask to see your passport when you exchange money, so bring it.
  • At the airport, exchange only enough for a transit card top-up and a first meal, and pay the rest by card or WOWPASS to travel lighter. For how to use exchange counters in the city, see #435 Currency Exchange Guide, and for the ATM screen steps, see #1286 ATM Cash Withdrawal.

🧾 Tax refund: from KRW 15,000 on a single receipt

  • Minimum amount: at a shop with a "Tax Refund" sign, KRW 15,000 or more on a single receipt (Korea Tourism Organization (VisitKorea), revised 2025-10). Look for the sign before you buy.
  • Instant refund at the store: a single payment of KRW 15,000 or more and under KRW 1,000,000, and a trip total of KRW 5,000,000 or less. Show your passport when you pay and you are charged the price reduced by the refund amount.
  • Downtown refund: KRW 6,000,000 or less per receipt, and you declare it at the airport counter when you leave. Refund counters at airports and ports have no purchase amount limit.
  • Conditions: a foreigner staying in Korea for less than 6 months must leave Korea within 3 months of the purchase date, and the item must be unopened. At the airport, go to the refund counter or kiosk before you check your bags. At the airport you either receive the refund within 1 to 2 months to a foreign credit card, Alipay or WeChat Pay, or in cash at the counter in the departure hall (Korea Tourism Organization (VisitKorea)).
  • For the order of the airport kiosk screens see #1304 Tax Refund Kiosk, and for the whole system see #1231 Tax Refund Guide.

🧰 First-day order: follow it as it is

  1. Arrivals hall: join the airport Wi-Fi, install the eSIM QR code and turn on data. If you use a SIM card, go to a carrier booth.
  2. Exchange: at an exchange counter, change only enough won for a transit card top-up and a first meal. If you will use WOWPASS, get one issued with your passport at the kiosk, and then skip step 3.
  3. Convenience store: buy a T-money card and top it up. The Climate Card does not work on the way from the airport to Seoul, so ride with T-money.
  4. Next morning: if it is a day you will ride a lot of subway and city buses in Seoul, buy a Climate Card short-term pass at a station kiosk. The physical card costs KRW 3,000 separately, so if you stay only 2 to 3 days, first work out whether T-money is enough.
  5. When shopping: at a shop with a "Tax Refund" sign, if it is KRW 15,000 or more, take out your passport. On your departure day, go to the refund counter before you check your bags.

For choosing between train and bus from the airport to the city, see #1285 From Incheon Airport to the City, and for taking a taxi when card registration fails, see #1273 Kakao T Taxi.

⚠ 4 common mistakes

  1. "Climate Card from the airport to Seoul": airport buses are not covered, and the Airport Railroad works only when you get off at Incheon Airport Station. From Incheon Airport to Seoul, use T-money.
  2. "I'll install the eSIM at home before leaving": the KT eSIM's period runs from the moment you install it, and the QR code installs only once. Install it on airport Wi-Fi after you arrive.
  3. "It's unlimited, so I can video call freely": the KT foreign visitor plan slows to up to 5Mbps after 11GB a day. It speeds up again at 00:00 the next day.
  4. "Tax refund starts from KRW 50,000": it starts from KRW 15,000 per receipt (it was lowered from KRW 30,000 in 2024, Korea.net).

⚙ FAQ 4

Q1. How much cash should I exchange?

At the airport, enough for a transit card top-up and a first meal is fine. Hotels, department stores and regular shops widely accept foreign cards, (Korea Tourism Organization (VisitKorea)). Where cash is needed is covered in #1301.

Q2. Which is better, a SIM card or an eSIM?

If your phone supports eSIM and is unlocked, an eSIM lets you skip the booth line. A KT eSIM's period runs from the moment you install it, so install it on airport Wi-Fi after you arrive. If your phone does not support it, buy a SIM card at a carrier booth at Incheon Terminal 1 (11 booths, 1 open 24 hours per company).

Q3. Can I get around on public Wi-Fi alone?

SEOUL_Secure is available on streets, in markets, in parks, at bus stops and on city buses, but it connects only where it is installed, and Seoul Metropolitan Government (서울시) advises against financial transactions. If you want to keep using map and translation apps, keep a data plan.

Q4. What about the balance left on my transit card?

With T-money you get the balance back minus the KRW 500 fee, and the card price is not refunded. For the WOWPASS balance, follow the kiosk instructions to get it back. For the refund counters in detail see #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards.
