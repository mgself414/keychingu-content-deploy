6 Korean National Parks — Distances and Times for a First Course, Winter Entry Cutoffs, and Where You Need a Reservation

Subtitle: For travelers going to a Korean national park for the first time, this guide groups 6 parks: Bukhansan, Seoraksan, Hallasan, Jirisan Nogodan, Gyeongju and Taeanhaean. Korea has 24 national parks, and every trail has set times when you can enter. Bukhansan closes its trail entrance from 16:00 in December–February (months 12–2). On the Hallasan Seongpanak course you must pass the entrance by 11:30 in October–March (months 10–3), and the summit section needs a reservation. Jirisan Nogodan is by reservation on weekends and public holidays. The Gurye County Office bus timetable lists the Nogodan route to Seongsamjae as "runs from late April to mid-November (months 4–11)". The Seoraksan Gwongeumseong Cable Car costs 16,000 won round trip and posts its operating hours for the day every day. In Gyeongju, the 1.4 km stretch from the Seokguram side to the Tohamsan summit is closed for construction from 2026-11-01 to 2027-03-31. The autumn forest fire watch period runs from 2026-11-15 to 12-15. The values were read on 2026-11-25.

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 6

This list is made for foreign travelers who want to walk a mountain or a sea path for a day in Korea. It is written so that you can match times and reservations before you leave, even if you do not know Korean.

According to the official introduction of Korea National Park Service (국립공원공단) (read 2026-11-25), Korea has 24 national parks. 19 are mountain-type, 4 are marine and coastal-type, and 1 is historic-site-type. This guide picked 6 of them that differ from each other in character.

  • A mountain you reach from Seoul by subway and bus: Bukhansan (북한산).
  • A mountain with a cable car and a valley path: Seoraksan (설악산).
  • A mountain whose summit section needs a reservation: Hallasan (한라산).
  • A ridge that opens by reservation on weekends: Jirisan Nogodan (지리산 노고단).
  • A historic-site-type park where you walk between temples and a grotto: Gyeongju (경주).
  • A coastal-type park where you walk along the shore: Taeanhaean (태안해안).

You cannot enter a Korean national park at any time you like. Each trail has a time when the entrance is closed, and in winter that time moves earlier. So for each park this guide first writes down "by what time you must pass the entrance" and "whether a reservation is needed".

⚠ Notation principle: Hours, distances, fares and directions are values read on 2026-11-25. Sources are the website of Korea National Park Service and its reservation website, the Korea Law Information Center (국가법령정보센터) (Natural Parks Act and its Enforcement Decree), the Jeju Special Self-Governing Province Hallasan trail reservation website, the Seorak Cable Car (설악케이블카) website, Gurye County Office (구례군청), Gyeongju City Hall (경주시청), the Bulguksa Temple (불국사) website and the Republic of Korea Policy Briefing (정책브리핑). Fares are adult list prices. The winter month range differs by park. Bukhansan is written as December–February (months 12–2), Seoraksan, Jirisan and Gyeongju as November–March (months 11–3), and Hallasan as October–March (months 10–3). The entry-hours table and the transport guide of Korea National Park Service carry no update date. Closed sections, operating hours, fares and bus service can change.

📋 8 Rules to Know Before You Go

1. There Are 24 National Parks and Different Bodies Manage Them

The official introduction of Korea National Park Service (read 2026-11-25) says "24 national parks are currently designated and managed". The Park Service manages 23 of them. Hallasan National Park (한라산국립공원) is managed by Jeju Special Self-Governing Province. The Odongdo District (오동도지구) of Hallyeohaesang National Park (한려해상국립공원) is managed by Yeosu-si (여수시).

So Hallasan has its own reservation website and its own entry-hours table. In the entry-hours guide on the Park Service website, the Hallasan box links to the Jeju Province website.

The No. 1 national park is Jirisan, designated in 1967.

2. Some Places Cost Money

These are the fares seen directly for the 6 parks in this guide.

Place Fare (adult list price, 2026-11-25) Source
Seoraksan Gwongeumseong Cable Car 16,000 won round trip (middle school students and older) Seorak Cable Car website
Bulguksa admission Free (since 2023-05-04) Bulguksa website
Bulguksa parking Passenger car 2,000 won Bulguksa website
Gyeongju city bus Adult 1,500 won, 50 won off with a transit card Gyeongju City Hall

The boarding guide on the Seorak Cable Car website says "There is no admission fee" at the cultural heritage zone entrance of Sogongwon (소공원).

3. The Autumn Forest Fire Watch Period Is 2026-11-15 to 12-15

On 2026-11-01 Korea National Park Service posted the "2026 Autumn National Park Trail Access Closure Notice" (Notice No. 2026-68). Two press releases from Park Service offices (Palgongsan and Songnisan) give the closure period as November 15 to December 15. During this period each park closes some trails.

The closed sections differ by park. Songnisan (속리산) closes 13 sections and opens 12 sections. On the Dobongsan (도봉산) side of Bukhansan National Park, the 1 km from Eunseogam to Darakwon (은석암~다락원) is closed. Seoraksan closes its high-ground trails, and the Sogongwon–Biseondae path can still be walked in this period. In Gyeongju, the Bulguksa–Seokguram parking lot (석굴암주차장) section is partly open.

4. The Designated Entry-Hours System — The Time You Pass the Entrance Is Fixed

The official guide of Korea National Park Service (read 2026-11-25) explains this system as "a system that designates and runs entry and closure times for each trail, taking into account the hiking destination, the distance and the hiking time". It has been in force since May 16, 2015 in all national parks except Taeanhaean National Park.

After the closure time you cannot go up from the trail entrance. The time differs by park and by trail. In winter it moves earlier. The values for the 6 parks in this guide are in the at-a-glance table above and in the park sections below.

5. The Reservation System — Trails, Shelters and Campgrounds

Trail reservation system. The list of the trail reservation system on the Park Service reservation website (read 2026-11-25) has the sections below.

  • Dadohaehaesang Jinridang (다도해해상 진리당)
  • Bukhansan Uiryeong-gil (북한산 우이령)
  • Seoraksan Gombaegol and Heullimgol (설악산 곰배골, 흘림골)
  • Woraksan Oksunbong and Gudambong (월악산 옥순봉·구담봉)
  • Jirisan Nogodan (지리산 노고단)
  • Chiaksan Hyangnobong (치악산 향로봉)
  • Hallyeohaesang Hallyeodongbu (Haegeumgang–Ujebong–Seokgae Beach) (한려해상 한려동부(해금강~우제봉~석개해변))

Bukhansan Uiryeong-gil (우이령길) is included in the trail reservation system. Jirisan Nogodan is by reservation on weekends and public holidays and you go without a reservation on weekdays. The Park Service's Seoraksan entry-hours table lists Heullimgol as "trail reservation system (one-way, run separately during October–November (months 10–11))".

Shelters. The parks with shelters are 4: Jirisan, Seoraksan, Deogyusan (덕유산) and Sobaeksan (소백산). Reservations are first come, first served and registration opens at 14:00 on the first weekday of each month. As one example of the fares, the Deogyusan Sakgatjae Shelter (삿갓재 대피소) costs 20,000 won on weekdays and 30,000 won on weekends and in peak season. The Sakgatjae Shelter cannot be used from 2026-11-15 to 12-15.

Campgrounds. It is a lottery system. Registration opens on the 1st of each even-numbered month.

Refunds. If you cancel up to 3 days before, you get a full refund. 10% is deducted at 2 days before, 20% at 1 day before, and 30% before 12:00 on the day.

Hallasan. You reserve not with the Park Service but on the Hallasan trail reservation website of Jeju Special Self-Governing Province. It is written in the Hallasan section below.

6. Prohibited Acts and Administrative Fines

Article 27 of the Natural Parks Act (Act No. 21848, in force 2026-07-07) prohibits camping, cooking, smoking, parking and dumping waste outside designated places. The amounts are the values of Enforcement Decree, Attached Table 3 of the Natural Parks Act. If you repeat the same violation within the last year, the amount goes up.

Act Administrative fine, first violation Upper limit set by law
Smoking outside a designated place 600,000 won 2,000,000 won
Camping outside a designated place 200,000 won 500,000 won
Entering an area where entry is prohibited 200,000 won 500,000 won
Cooking or dumping waste outside a designated place 100,000 won 200,000 won
Drinking in a place where drinking is prohibited 100,000 won 200,000 won

The amounts in the table are the standard amounts for imposition. Depending on circumstances they can be reduced or increased within a range of half.

  • Drinking: it is not prohibited in the whole park. Article 25 of the Enforcement Decree sets shelters and their ancillary facilities, and the trails and mountain summit points that the park designates, as places where drinking is prohibited.
  • Pets: Article 26 of the Enforcement Decree sets bringing in animals such as dogs and cats as an act that the park management agency can restrict or prohibit. Assistance dogs for people with disabilities are excluded. Whether it is actually blocked follows the notice of each park. If you do a restricted or prohibited act, the administrative fine for a first violation is 600,000 won.
  • Other things a park can block: carrying flammable materials in areas the park designates, and bathing or washing clothes in a valley.

7. Check Closures on the Morning You Go

On the website of Korea National Park Service, the screen "Safe Visit (안전한 탐방) > Trail Closure Information (통제정보)" shows the situation of each park (office) in a table. If you click the name of an office, the closed sections open. On 2026-11-25, the three offices of Jirisan, Odaesan (오대산), Chiaksan (치악산), Songnisan, Sobaeksan, Juwangsan (주왕산), Taebaeksan (태백산), Palgongsan (팔공산) and others were "partially closed".

When snow falls, more is closed. According to the Park Service press release posted on the Policy Briefing (2026-11-15), the winter measures period is from 2026-11-15 to 2027-03-15. When a heavy snow advisory is issued, only some low-ground trails are opened. When the advisory is lifted, they are reopened after a safety check. The Park Service announces closure information on its website, on social media and on disaster-alert message electronic boards.

8. English Screens Are Only for Shelters and Campgrounds

  • Park Service website: the "English" button at the top does not go to an English website. A window opens that tells you how to use the browser's translation function.
  • Park Service reservation website: if you press the "EN" button at the top, you go to the English screen (reservation.knps.or.kr/foreigner/main.do). The menu of the English screen has two items: Shelters (대피소) and Campsites (야영장). The trail reservation system menu is not on the English screen.
  • Korean reservation screen: reserving a trail requires login. The non-member login on the Korean login screen is a method of real-name verification with a mobile phone or an i-PIN (아이핀).
  • So: among the courses chosen in this guide, the one where a Park Service trail reservation applies is Jirisan Nogodan. Nogodan is reached without a reservation on weekdays. Reservations on weekends and public holidays are taken on the internet, on site and by the automatic phone line (1670-9202).
  • Hallasan: the Hallasan trail reservation website has English, Japanese and Chinese screens.
  • Reservation inquiries: Korea National Park Service visit and facility reservation guide 1670-9201. Weekdays 09:00–18:00, with 12:00–13:00 closed.

① Bukhansan (Bukhansan National Park)

  • What is different: according to the official introduction of Korea National Park Service (read 2026-11-25), it was designated in 1983 as the 15th national park. The area is 76.922㎢. The Park Service introduces it as "a nature park in the middle of a city, which is rare in the world". Of the 6 parks, it is the one where you go from a Seoul subway station to the entrance by city bus.
  • First course: the Bukhansanseong Course (북한산성 코스). In the official Park Service course guide it is Bukhansanseong Visitor Support Center (북한산성탐방지원센터) – Daeseomun Gate (대서문) – Borisa Temple (보리사) – Baegundae Peak (백운대), 3.4 km, 2 hours 40 minutes, difficulty "medium". The section is one direction, from the center to Baegundae Peak. The 2 hours 40 minutes is taken as the time going up. The time to come down is added separately.
  • An easier path: the Bukhansan Dulle-gil (북한산 둘레길). The Park Service introduces the Dulle-gil as "a low-ground level walking trail built so that you can walk gently along the foot of Bukhansan". Section 9 Masil-gil (9구간 마실길) is 1.5 km, about 45 minutes, difficulty "easy". Section 10 Naesimyoyeok-gil (10구간 내시묘역길) is 3.5 km, about 1 hour 45 minutes, difficulty "easy".
  • Time limit: according to the official designated entry-hours system table of the Park Service, the Bukhansanseong Ipgu to Wimun Gate (북한산성입구~위문) section has entry at 04:00 and closure at 16:00 in December–February (months 12–2). In March–November (months 3–11), it is entry at 04:00 and closure at 17:00. The explanatory text of the same table gives the winter season as November–March (months 11–3), which differs from the table header. Plan to pass the entrance before 16:00 in November and March too. This is this guide's own advice.
  • Reservation: the Bukhansanseong Course and Dulle-gil Sections 9 and 10 are not in the list of the reservation system. Bukhansan Uiryeong-gil is included in the trail reservation system. The starting point of Uiryeong-gil is in Gangbuk-gu (강북구), Seoul.
  • How to get there: according to the official transport guide of the Park Service, take bus 704 from Line 3 Gupabal Station and get off at "Bukhansanseong Ipgu". The address of the starting point, the Bukhansanseong Visitor Support Center, is 45 Daeseomun-gil (대서문길), Eunpyeong-gu, Seoul.
  • Winter caution: in the closure information list seen on 2026-11-25, there was no notice closing the Bukhansanseong Course. The partial closure of the Bukhansan Office (북한산사무소) was about 100 m from Gucheon Falls Observatory to the lower part of Daedongmun Gate (구천폭포 전망대~대동문) on the Gangbuk-gu side. On the Dobongsan side, 1 km from Eunseogam to Darakwon is closed in the forest fire watch period.
  • Inquiries: Bukhansan National Park Office (북한산국립공원사무소) 02-909-0497.

② Seoraksan (Seoraksan National Park)

  • What is different: two walking paths and the cable car start together from Sogongwon (소공원), the entrance on the Sokcho (속초) side. It is one of the 4 parks that have shelters. The address of the Sogongwon entrance is 1091 Seoraksan-ro (설악산로), Sokcho-si, Gangwon-do.
  • First course — Sogongwon to Biseondae: according to the official course guide of Korea National Park Service (read 2026-11-25), about 3.0 km, about 1 hour 20 minutes one way. It is a gentle valley path with some slightly sloped stone steps. The Park Service writes that children and the elderly can also walk it and that it can be visited in the forest fire watch period too.
  • A little more — Sogongwon to Ulsanbawi Rock: according to the official course guide of the Park Service, 3.8 km one way, 2 hours, difficulty "medium". Seoraksan-dong Visitor Support Center (설악동탐방지원센터) to Heundeulbawi Rock (흔들바위) (Gyejoam (계조암)) is 2.8 km, 1 hour, and is not a strain for children and the elderly. Heundeulbawi Rock to Ulsanbawi Rock (울산바위) is 1.0 km, 1 hour, with steep stairs.
  • Time limit: according to the official designated entry-hours table of the Park Service (winter season, months 11–3), the paths between Sogongwon and Ulsanbawi Rock, Biseondae (비선대), Gwongeumseong (권금성) and Towangseong Falls Observatory (토왕성폭포전망대) are restricted "from after sunset until 2 hours before sunrise the next day". The path from Biseondae to Geumganggul Cave (금강굴) is 04:00–14:00 and you return by the way you came. In the winter season, people who go further in from Biseondae after 12:00 write their names in an entry register.
  • Reservation: the two courses above from Sogongwon are not in the list of the reservation system. The places in Seoraksan that are on the list are Gombaegol (곰배골) and Heullimgol (흘림골).
  • Gwongeumseong Cable Car fare: according to the official website of the Seorak Cable Car (read 2026-11-25), only round trips are sold. Adult (middle school students and older) 16,000 won, child (36 months to elementary school students) 12,000 won. Under 36 months is free.
  • Gwongeumseong Cable Car hours: there is no fixed timetable. The website posts the operating hours for the day every day. Operation stops when the wind is strong. Tickets do not take reservations and are sold only on site on the day. The interval between rides is 5–15 minutes. If it stops because of strong wind or bad weather, unused tickets are fully refunded.
  • How to get there: according to the guide on the official Seorak Cable Car website, take bus 7 or 7-1 from Sokcho Intercity Bus Terminal. The bus passes the Express Bus Terminal (고속버스터미널), Seoraksan Entrance (설악산 입구), Seoraksan National Park Visitor Center (설악산 국립공원 탐방안내소) and Kensington Hotel (켄싱턴 호텔), and stops at "Seoraksan Jucha-jang (Jongjeom) (설악산 주차장(종점))". The cable car boarding point is a 5 minute walk from the Sogongwon entrance.
  • Winter caution: in the Park Service closure information seen on 2026-11-25, Seoraksan was closing its high-ground trails for autumn natural resource protection and forest fire prevention. It announced that Geumganggul Cave and Daeseung Falls (대승폭포) can be visited until 14:00. The Park Service's Seoraksan entry-hours table says that entry is closed when a weather warning is issued. The cable car stops in strong wind, so open the website first on the morning of the day you want to ride.
  • Inquiries: Seorak Cable Car 033-636-4300.

③ Hallasan (Hallasan National Park)

Hallasan is managed not by Korea National Park Service but by Jeju Special Self-Governing Province. The values below are the 2026-11-25 notation of the official guide of the Hallasan trail reservation website of Jeju Special Self-Governing Province (visithalla.jeju.go.kr).

  • What is different: there are two courses to the summit, Baengnokdam (백록담): the Seongpanak course (성판악 코스) and the Gwaneumsa course (관음사 코스). The summit section of both courses needs a reservation. The other courses are walked without a reservation but do not reach the summit.
  • Course to the summit — Seongpanak: 9.6 km. To the summit it is 4 hours 30 minutes one way and 9 hours round trip. To Jindallaebat (진달래밭) it is 3 hours one way. Difficulty is C (easy) from the visitor center to Saraak Spring (사라악샘), B (moderate) from Saraak Spring to Jindallaebat, and A (hard) from Jindallaebat to Baengnokdam.
  • Course to the summit — Gwaneumsa: 8.7 km. To Samgakbong Shelter (삼각봉대피소) it is 3 hours 20 minutes one way, to the summit 5 hours one way, and 10 hours round trip.
  • Courses walked without a reservation: Eoseungsaengak (어승생악) is 1.3 km, about 30 minutes one way, and its entrance is next to the Eorimok Visitor Center (어리목 탐방안내소). The Seokguram course (석굴암 코스) is 1.5 km, 50 minutes. The Yeongsil course (영실 코스) is 5.8 km, 1 hour 30 minutes to Witse Oreum (윗세오름) and 2 hours 30 minutes to Nambyeok Junction (남벽분기점). The website describes Yeongsil as "a trail that cannot reach Baengnokdam (the summit)".

  • Courses that need a reservation: the Jindallaebat to Baengnokdam section of the Seongpanak course and the Samgakbong to Baengnokdam section of the Gwaneumsa course. The English notice on the website (2025-05-12) says that Eorimok, Yeongsil, Donnaeko, Seokguram and Eoseungsaengak are open without a reservation.
  • When reservations open: from 09:00 on the first working day of each month, you apply for use in the next month. Up to 4 people at a time, and one person reserves once a week.
  • Foreign-language screens: the Hallasan trail reservation website has English, Japanese and Chinese screens. When you finish the reservation, an entry and descent QR code comes to the phone number you entered.
  • On the day: each person shows 1 QR code and an ID. A passport can also be used as an ID.
  • If you reserve and do not go: if you do not cancel and do not go, you cannot reserve for 3 months for 1 time and for 1 year for 2 times. There is no waiting list.
  • How to get there: the English notice on the website says that the Seongpanak parking lot is small and fills up early, and recommends bus 281 or 181.
  • Winter caution: the Jeju Special Self-Governing Province Hallasan National Park website says that there is no shop on the trails, so prepare crampons, enough water and a rain coat in advance. From Seongpanak to the summit is 9 hours round trip and the entrance cutoff is 11:30. The gate opens at 05:00, so pass the entrance early in the morning. This is this guide's own advice.

④ Jirisan Nogodan (Jirisan National Park, Nogodan)

  • What is different: Jirisan (지리산) is the No. 1 national park, designated in 1967. Nogodan (노고단) is a peak that you climb on foot from Seongsamjae (성삼재). The Park Service writes that the path from Seongsamjae to Nogodan Pass (노고단고개) is wide and flat and can be walked by men and women of all ages.
  • First course: according to the official course guide of Korea National Park Service (read 2026-11-25), Seongsamjae Rest Area (성삼재휴게소) to Munenggi (무넹기) is 2 km, 25 minutes, and Munenggi to Nogodan Pass is 2.7 km, 35 minutes. Together it is 4.7 km and about 1 hour going up. The time to come down is added separately. From Nogodan Pass to the summit, according to the guide on the Park Service reservation website, it is 500 m, 30 minutes one way, and the detour is 700 m.
  • Time limit: the Nogodan guide on the Park Service reservation website writes the operating hours as 5:00–17:00 and the last entry as 16:00. The Park Service designated entry-hours table (winter season, months 11–3) writes 04:00–16:00 from Seongsamjae toward Nogodan and 05:00–16:00 for the Nogodan summit. Plan to arrive before 16:00.
  • Reservation: weekends and public holidays are by reservation and weekdays are without a reservation. The reservation quota is 1,870 people. Reservations are taken on the internet, on site and by the automatic phone line (1670-9202).
  • How to get there — check the bus operating period: the Gurye County Office (구례군청) bus timetable (read 2026-11-25) writes the Nogodan route (Seongsamjae) from Gurye (구례) via Hwaeomsa (화엄사) as "runs from late April to mid-November (months 4–11)". In December–February (months 12–2), do not make a plan to go to Seongsamjae by bus.
  • How to get there — check the road too: the Nogodan guide on the Park Service reservation website says to be sure to check the closure of the trail and of County Road 12 (군도 12호선) of Gurye County (toward Seongsamjae).
  • Winter caution: the Park Service guide writes that you must put on crampons when the path freezes. Depending on the weather, the whole Seongsamjae to Nogodan section can be closed. In the closure information seen on 2026-11-25, the three offices of Jirisan were "partially closed".
  • To go higher: according to the Park Service designated entry-hours table (winter season), the path from Nogodan Pass into the main ridge traverse is 04:00–11:00. Jungsan-ri (중산리) on the Cheonwangbong side is 04:00–13:00 and Baekmu-dong (백무동) is 04:00–12:00.

⑤ Gyeongju (Gyeongju National Park)

  • What is different: Korea National Park Service introduces Gyeongju National Park (경주국립공원) as "the only historic-site-type national park". Tohamsan (토함산) and Namsan (남산) are inside the park. The mountain path between Bulguksa Temple (불국사) and Seokguram Grotto (석굴암) is the trail.
  • First course: according to the official visit news of the Park Service (read 2026-11-25), the Tohamsan Bulguksa–Seokguram visit course is 2.8 km in total, about 1 hour. In the forest fire watch period, the Bulguksa to Seokguram parking lot (석굴암주차장) section is partly open.
  • Construction closure — you cannot go to the Tohamsan summit: according to the notice on the Gyeongju National Park screen of the Park Service (read 2026-11-25), the 1.4 km stretch from the Seokguram side to the Tohamsan summit is closed for construction from 2026-11-01 to 2027-03-31. It is planned to be opened temporarily for the sunrise on 2027-01-01.
  • Time limit: according to the official designated entry-hours table of the Park Service, the Samneung Visitor Support Center (삼릉 탐방지원센터) in the Namsan District (남산지구) is 05:00–15:00 in winter (months 11–3) and 04:00–16:00 in summer (months 4–10).
  • Bulguksa hours and fare: according to the official Bulguksa website (read 2026-11-25), admission is free from 2023-05-04. Entry starts at 09:00 and the last entry is 17:00 in November–January (months 11–1), 17:30 in February and October, and 18:00 in March–September (months 3–9). It is open all year. Parking is 2,000 won for a passenger car.
  • Seokguram address: 238 Seokgul-ro (석굴로), Gyeongju-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do (Jinhyeon-dong (진현동)).
  • How to get there: according to the official guide of Gyeongju City Hall (read 2026-11-25), city bus 10 passes the Terminal (터미널), Cheomseongdae (첨성대) and Bomun (보문) and turns around at Bulguksa. Measured at the terminal, the first bus is 06:00, the last bus is 21:40, and it comes about every 20 minutes. Bus 11 runs the same route in the opposite direction. From the Bulguksa stop to Bulguksa Temple it is a 3–5 minute walk. The fare is adult 1,500 won, and with a transit card 50 won is taken off.
  • Winter caution: Bulguksa can be entered until 17:00 in November–January (months 11–1). On the Namsan Samneung side, the entrance is closed at 15:00. Set out in the morning. This is this guide's own advice.
  • Inquiries: Gyeongju National Park Visitor Center (경주국립공원 탐방안내소) 054-778-4100.

⑥ Taeanhaean (Taeanhaean National Park)

  • What is different: the official introduction of Korea National Park Service (read 2026-11-25) describes Taeanhaean National Park (태안해안국립공원) as "the only coastal-type national park in Korea with diverse coastal ecosystems". The coastline is about 198 km and there are 27 beaches. You do not climb a mountain but walk along the shore.
  • First course: the Taean Haebyeon-gil (태안해변길) course 5, Noeul-gil (노을길). According to the official beach path guide of the Park Service, it is 12 km, 3 hours 40 minutes from Baeksajang Port (백사장항) toward Kkotji (꽃지). It passes Sambong Beach (삼봉해변), Gijipo Coastal Dunes (기지포 해안사구) and the golden rain tree grove (모감주나무 군락지) at Bangpo (방포).
  • How to get there: according to the Park Service guide (read 2026-11-25), take the Taean-bound city bus from Anmyeon Bus Terminal and get off at "Baeksajang (백사장)". It takes 15 minutes. For a short walk, there is the 1.9 km, about 30 minute section from Baeksajang Port to Baeksajang Observatory (백사장전망대).
  • Time limit: the Park Service's designated entry-hours system is run in all parks except Taeanhaean National Park. There is no separate time when the entrance is closed.
  • Reservation: Noeul-gil is not in the list of the trail reservation system.
  • Winter caution: the Park Service's Taeanhaean screen seen on 2026-11-25 had a notice of a partial closure of the beach path. A notice of action guidelines for winter natural disasters was also there. 12 km takes 3 hours 40 minutes, so leave Baeksajang Port in the morning to finish before the sun sets. This is this guide's own advice.
  • Inquiries: Anmyeondo Branch Office (안면도분소) 041-673-1066.

⚠ 6 Common Mistakes

  1. Trying to go to Seongsamjae by bus in winter. The Gurye Nogodan route bus runs from late April to mid-November (months 4–11). Also look at the closure of County Road 12.
  2. Going up Hallasan to the summit without a reservation, or late. The summit section of Seongpanak and Gwaneumsa needs a reservation. In October–March (months 10–3), you must pass the entrance of both courses by 11:30. Reservations for the next month open at 09:00 on the first working day of each month.
  3. Thinking the Gwongeumseong Cable Car has a fixed timetable. The operating hours are posted on the website every day. It stops when strong wind blows. Tickets are sold only on site on the day.
  4. Trying to walk on from Seokguram to the Tohamsan summit. The 1.4 km stretch from the Seokguram side to the Tohamsan summit is closed for construction from 2026-11-01 to 2027-03-31.
  5. Looking for a trail reservation on the English screen of the Park Service reservation website. The English screen has only the Shelters and Campsites menus. Nogodan is reached without a reservation on weekdays, and on weekends and public holidays reservations are also taken on site and by the automatic phone line.
  6. Climbing Bukhansan late in the afternoon. The entrance is closed from 16:00 in December–February (months 12–2). For the Bukhansanseong Course, plan 2 hours 40 minutes to go up.

⚙ 5 FAQs

  1. I have never hiked. Where is easy? — The Park Service lists three easy places. Seoraksan Sogongwon–Biseondae (3.0 km, about 1 hour 20 minutes one way) is written as walkable by children and the elderly. Jirisan Seongsamjae–Nogodan Pass (4.7 km, about 1 hour going up) is written as wide and flat. Bukhansan Dulle-gil Section 9 (1.5 km, about 45 minutes) and Section 10 (3.5 km, about 1 hour 45 minutes) are difficulty "easy". On Hallasan, Eoseungsaengak is 1.3 km, about 30 minutes one way.
  2. Where do I need a reservation? — Two of the 6. On Hallasan you reserve the summit section of the Seongpanak and Gwaneumsa courses on the Hallasan trail reservation website. Jirisan Nogodan is by reservation on weekends and public holidays. The Bukhansanseong Course, the two courses from Seoraksan Sogongwon, Gyeongju Bulguksa–Seokguram and Taean Noeul-gil are not in the list of the reservation system seen on 2026-11-25. The Seoraksan Gwongeumseong Cable Car does not take reservations and sells tickets on site on the day.
  3. What changes until December 15? — It is the autumn forest fire watch period (2026-11-15 to 12-15), so each park closes some trails. Seoraksan closes its high-ground trails and opens Sogongwon–Biseondae. In Gyeongju, the Bulguksa–Seokguram parking lot section is partly open. For the closed sections, click the office name on the trail closure information screen of the Park Service website.
  4. What happens if I drink or smoke on a mountain? — If you smoke outside a designated place, the administrative fine for a first violation is 600,000 won. Drinking is prohibited at shelters and on trails and summits the park designates, and the administrative fine is 100,000 won. Cooking and dumping waste outside a designated place are also 100,000 won. The amounts are the values of Enforcement Decree, Attached Table 3 of the Natural Parks Act.
  5. Can I go when it snows? — According to the Park Service press release (2026-11-15), when a heavy snow advisory is issued, only some low-ground trails are opened. After the advisory is lifted, they are reopened after a safety check. Seoraksan closes entry when a weather warning is issued. Jirisan Nogodan can close the whole Seongsamjae–Nogodan section depending on the weather. In an accident or an emergency, call 119.
