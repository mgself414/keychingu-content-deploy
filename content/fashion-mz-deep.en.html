8 Seoul K-Streetwear Stores — Flagships and Select Shops in Hongdae, Seongsu, Hannam, and Sinsa

Subtitle: MUSINSA STANDARD, thisisneverthat, and SAN SAN GEAR in Hongdae·Hapjeong, MUSINSA EMPTY, Matin Kim, and IMMIS in Seongsu, Mardi Mercredi in Hannam-dong, and ADERERROR in Sinsa-dong. Only 8 Korean-brand stores and select shops that Kakao Map shows as open and that got visit reviews in the second half of 2026 made the cut, split into 4 zones. Each store lists a representative item's price, its size labels, and its hours, with dates.

ISSUE 06 · #531 · 2026-11-24 · Seoul Streetwear Shopping

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 8

This list is for travelers who want to try on Korean-brand clothes in the store and buy them there. Seoul's streetwear stores cluster in four neighborhoods: Hongdae·Hapjeong (홍대·합정), Seongsu (성수), Hannam (한남), and Sinsa·Apgujeong (신사·압구정). This piece narrows it to four neighborhoods. It includes flagship stores that brands run themselves and select shops that gather several brands. Secondhand and vintage shops are covered separately in the vintage piece #772.

There are two selection rules. The Kakao Map (카카오맵) place info has to say "open for business," and a blog review or photo has to have been posted in the second half of 2026. Stores whose activity stopped before the first half of 2026 were left out.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours are the values the owner registered on Kakao Map (checked 2026-11-24). For prices, we list first the value opened on each brand's official online store on 2026-11-24. For brands whose official store page would not open, we copied the in-store price written in a blog review, with its date. A tax refund is noted only for Matin Kim Seongsu, where a visit review describes a refund booth next to the cash register (2026-10-24 review). Nearby stations and exits are from Kakao Map directions.

The general tax refund rules (eligible amount, immediate refund, airport procedure) are in the duty-free shopping piece #775. Show your passport when you pay.

① Hongdae·Hapjeong — 3 Venues

One large store in front of Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역), and two on Jandari-ro (잔다리로) behind Exit 3 of Hapjeong Station (합정역). MUSINSA STANDARD stays open until 23:00, so it works as the last stop of the evening. The two Jandari-ro stores are a few minutes apart on foot.

  • MUSINSA STANDARD Hongdae (무신사 스탠다드 홍대점) — a store for the in-house brand of the online select shop Musinsa. It has plenty of basics such as shirts, knits, slacks, and blazers. Kakao Map says it reopened after a renovation on 2025-11-27. On the official store, the Lightweight Relaxed Basic Blazer had a list price of 81,900 won. On 2026-11-24 it was on sale at 32,690 won, 60% off. The size of the discount differs from period to period. The official store's size options are M, L, XL, and 2XL. A 2026-11-22 blog review said the author paid at a self-checkout. It is open daily 11:00–23:00. The address is Yanghwa-ro 144 (양화로 144), B1–B2, Mapo-gu (마포구), and the nearest station is Hongik Univ. Station (Line 2 · AREX · Gyeongui-Jungang Line) Exit 9, a 2-minute walk. There is also a Seongsu store at Yeonmujang-gil 83 (연무장길 83), open daily 11:00–22:00 (Kakao Map, 2026-11-24).
  • thisisneverthat (디스이즈네버댓) — the Hongdae store of a street casual brand. A 2026-02-15 blog review wrote that the Hongdae store was renovated and reopened. A 2026-10-28 blog review (visited about a month earlier) wrote that the author saw the T-Logo SEOUL hoodie in the store at 105,000 won. The author asked for L and XL, and L fit. The author also wrote that the fitting rooms and big mirrors were well set up. It is open daily 11:00–21:00. The address is Jandari-ro 32 (잔다리로 32), 1st floor, Unit 101, Mapo-gu (마포구), and the nearest station is Hapjeong Station (Lines 2·6) Exit 3, a 7-minute walk.
  • SAN SAN GEAR Flagship Hapjeong (산산기어 플래그십 합정) — the flagship of a brand that sells outdoor-feel clothes and bags. Reviews describe it as "a store that looks like a converted house." A 2026-11-22 blog review (visited 11-15) lists the REQUIEM cap at 58,000 won and the VOID cargo pants at 209,000 won. A laptop pouch sold only at the flagship was 88,000 won. Bottoms come in sizes 01–04. In the same review, a staff member said to read 01 as about S, 02 as M, 03 as L, and 04 as XL. It is open daily 12:00–21:00. The address is Jandari-ro 36 (잔다리로 36), and the nearest station is Hapjeong Station (Lines 2·6) Exit 3, a 7-minute walk. There is also a flagship at Itaewon-ro 55-gil 37-10 (이태원로55길 37-10) in Hannam-dong (Mon–Thu 11:00–20:00, Fri–Sun 11:00–21:00, Kakao Map 2026-11-24).

② Seongsu — 3 Venues

All three are within a 10-minute walk of Exits 3 and 4 of Seongsu Station (성수역). You can see them in one loop along Yeonmujang-gil (연무장길). IMMIS opens last, at 12:00.

  • MUSINSA EMPTY Seongsu (무신사 엠프티 성수) — a select shop on floors 1–4, run by Musinsa. It mixes Korean and overseas designer brands, sneakers, and pop-ups. A 2026-03 Kakao Map review wrote that it has many brands hard to find in Korea and often holds pop-ups. A 2026-11 blog review mentions a Brooks Brothers × Eastlogue (이스트로그) pop-up. The official store's new-arrivals list (2026-11-24) shows a 9090 logo hoodie at 154,000 won, an Ohos (오호스) denim jacket at 188,000 won, and an UNDERCOVER jacket at 3,000,000 won (sale price 2,400,000 won). It is open daily 11:00–22:00. The address is Seongsu-i-ro 97 (성수이로 97), floors 1–4, Seongdong-gu (성동구), and the nearest station is Seongsu Station (Line 2) Exit 3, a 2-minute walk. Kakao Map lists paid parking and wheelchair access.
  • Matin Kim Seongsu Flagship Store (마뗑킴 성수 플래그십 스토어) — the flagship store of a brand that sells clothes, bags, and wallets. On the official store, the WASHED RIB denim pants were 188,000 won (2026-11-24). Sizes are S–XL. A 2026-11-20 blog review shows wallet price tags of 88,000 won, 138,000 won, and 148,000 won, and an outdoor cafe. A 2026-10-24 blog review said there is a tax refund counter next to the cash register and the signs are written in English first. It is open daily 11:00–21:00. The address is Yeonmujang 3-gil 9 (연무장3길 9), 1st floor, Seongdong-gu (성동구), and the nearest station is Seongsu Station (Line 2) Exit 4, a 7-minute walk.
  • IMMIS Seongsu Flagship Store (이미스 성수 플래그십 스토어) — a small store of a brand that mainly sells ball caps and bags. A review says the caps are hung by color, which makes choosing easy (2026-10-09). On the official store, the heart patch ball cap is 49,000 won and comes in one size. On 2026-11-24 it was on sale at 39,200 won. A 2026-07-14 blog review shows most caps at 49,000 won and a leopard-print eco bag at 32,000 won. Hours are Mon–Thu 12:00–19:30 and Fri–Sun 12:00–20:00. A 2024-07 Kakao Map review says the author arrived 10 minutes before closing and was not let in. Avoid arriving just before closing. The address is Yeonmujang-gil 29-17 (연무장길 29-17), 1st floor, Seongdong-gu (성동구), and the nearest station is Seongsu Station (Line 2) Exit 4, a 6-minute walk.

③ Hannam — 1 Venue

A store in an alley near Hangangjin Station (한강진역). It closes at 19:00, so go in the afternoon.

  • Mardi Mercredi 1 (마르디 메크르디 1) — the Hannam-dong store of a brand that sells sweatshirts and knits with floral graphics. On the official store, the LA FLEUR raglan sweatshirt is 128,000 won and comes in two sizes, S and M (2026-11-24). A 2026-10-15 blog review introduced adult clothes and dog clothes, and the dog clothes were in the 45,000–49,000 won range. Kakao Map hours are Tue–Sun 12:00–19:00, and Monday 11/30 is registered as a closed day. A 2026-11-11 blog review wrote that it closes on the last Monday of every month. The same review noted that flagship No. 4 has a different address, Daesagwan-ro 5-gil 28 (대사관로5길 28). The address is Itaewon-ro 54-gil 58 (이태원로54길 58), 1st floor, Yongsan-gu (용산구), and the nearest station is Hangangjin Station (Line 6) Exit 3, an 8-minute walk.

④ Sinsa — 1 Venue

A store you walk to from Sinsa Station (신사역), on the Garosu-gil (가로수길) side. It is open until 21:00.

  • ADERERROR Sinsa Space (아더에러 신사 스페이스) — a designer casual brand's store that uses an entire building. A 2026-10-07 blog review wrote that the author bought a short-sleeve tee in early September for 159,000 won. A 2026-09-13 review shows a shoulder bag from the 26FW collection at 630,000 won. Even within the same brand, tees and bags differ a lot in price. It is open daily 11:00–21:00. The address is Dosan-daero 11-gil 31 (도산대로11길 31), Gangnam-gu (강남구), and the nearest station is Sinsa Station (Line 3 · Shinbundang Line) Exit 8, a 7-minute walk. There is also an ADERERROR Seongsu Space at Seongsu-i-ro 82 (성수이로 82) (daily 11:00–21:00, Kakao Map 2026-11-24).

🗺 Route — Two Half-Day Courses

The zones are in different neighborhoods, so plan only one or two zones in a day.

A. Seongsu Yeonmujang-gil (afternoon)

  1. After 12:00 IMMIS near Exit 4 of Seongsu Station (from 12:00)
  2. Afternoon Matin Kim Seongsu Flagship (until 21:00). Take a break at the outdoor cafe
  3. Evening MUSINSA EMPTY Seongsu in front of Exit 3 of Seongsu Station (until 22:00)

B. Hapjeong·Hongdae (afternoon to night)

  1. Afternoon SAN SAN GEAR (from 12:00) and thisisneverthat (until 21:00) behind Exit 3 of Hapjeong Station
  2. Night MUSINSA STANDARD Hongdae at Exit 9 of Hongik Univ. Station (until 23:00)

Hannam and Sinsa are on opposite sides of the Han River. Seeing Mardi Mercredi (until 19:00) first and then moving on to ADERERROR Sinsa Space (until 21:00) fits the hours.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to Hannam in the evening — Mardi Mercredi 1 closes at 19:00, and Monday 11/30 is registered as a closed day. A review says it closes on the last Monday of every month. Put Hannam in the afternoon.
  2. Reading the size number as a waist measurement — a staff member said the 01–04 on SAN SAN GEAR bottoms are close to S–XL (review 2026-11-22). The Mardi Mercredi sweatshirt comes in two sizes, S and M, and the IMMIS ball cap is one size. Even the same L runs differently from brand to brand, so be sure to try it on.
  3. Paying without your passport — a tax refund requires your passport at the register. A 2026-10-24 visit review for Matin Kim Seongsu describes a tax refund booth next to the register. The rules are in #775.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. If I only go to one place? — To see many brands at once, MUSINSA EMPTY Seongsu. For basics, MUSINSA STANDARD Hongdae (open daily until 23:00). One blazer was on sale at 32,690 won (official store 2026-11-24).
  2. Which places can I visit in the evening? — By registered hours, MUSINSA STANDARD Hongdae is open until 23:00, MUSINSA EMPTY Seongsu until 22:00, and thisisneverthat, SAN SAN GEAR Hapjeong, Matin Kim Seongsu, and ADERERROR Sinsa until 21:00.
  3. A gift of around 50,000 won? — The IMMIS ball cap is 49,000 won list price on the official store (2026-11-24). The SAN SAN GEAR cap was 58,000 won (review 2026-11-22, which said many items were sold out).
