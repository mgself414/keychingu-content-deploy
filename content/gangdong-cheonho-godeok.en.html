🎯 Why a Gangdong (강동) Lifestyle Deep Dive?

KeyChinGu #183 Gangdong (강동)·Cheonho (천호) guide (first entry into Gangdong-gu (강동구)) focused on Cheonho Market (천호시장) and Gwangnaru Hangang Park (광나루 한강공원). However, the deep dive did not cover 5 neighborhoods: Godeok (고덕), Gil-dong (길동), Dunchon (둔촌), Amsa (암사), and Seongnae (성내), from the 87-item Gangdong (강동) cluster (65 local non-chain establishments). This guide presents 12 establishments across 4 categories, selected based on top review counts and a full foreign visitor experience angle. This cluster includes establishments with strong potential for foreign visitor recognition, such as Sushidoku N Godeok (스시도쿠 엔 고덕점) (an overwhelming 21,322 reviews), Sweet Salt (스윗솔트) (14,983 reviews), and Brand New Hyimond (브랜뉴하이몬드) (9,606 reviews).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreign Visitors

1. "Gangdong (강동) = Cheonho Market (천호시장) only" — Lack of awareness about deep dives into Godeok (고덕) and Gil-dong (길동): First-time visitors to Gangdong (강동) often only recognize Cheonho Market (천호시장), covered in #183. In reality, the cluster of 5 neighborhoods is rich with Godeok (고덕) (Sushidoku N (스시도쿠 엔) with 21,322 reviews, Gangdong (강동) #1) + Gil-dong (길동) (Sangmu Chobap (상무초밥) with 3,965 reviews) + Dunchon (둔촌) (Dunchon Jugong (둔촌주공) reconstruction ENTER), and more.

2. "Gangdong (강동) cafes = chains only" — Unaware of the signature cafe cluster: There are many signature cafes in the Cheonho (천호)·Gangdong (강동)·Godeok (고덕) cluster, such as Sweet Salt (스윗솔트) (14,983 reviews), Brand New Hyimond (브랜뉴하이몬드) (9,606 reviews), and Memorable Moment (메모러블모먼트) (3,359 reviews).

3. "Gangdong (강동) = eastern edge of Seoul" — Unaware of excellent transfer accessibility: Gangdong (강동) offers excellent downtown accessibility with Subway Line 5 (5호선)·Line 8 (8호선) + Gwangnaru Hangang (광나루 한강) + transfer at Cheonho Station (천호역) on Line 5 (5호선) + Line 9 (9호선) terminus. Jamsil (잠실) (#82) is just a 30-minute walk or 5 minutes by Line 8 (8호선) — making a Gangdong (강동) + Jamsil (잠실) day trip possible.
