⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners make

1. "Gangdong = Cheonho Market only" — Godeok·Gildong deep dive unknown: Only Cheonho Market covered by #183 is recognized during the first visit to Gangdong. In fact, it is rich in 5-dong clusters, including Godeok (Sushi Doku yen 21,322 rv Gangdong #1) + Gildong (Sangmu Sushi 3,965 rv) + Dunchon (Dunchon Jugong Reconstruction ENTER).

2. "Gangdong cafe = only chain" — Signature cafe cluster unknown: There are many signature cafes in the Cheonho, Gangdong, and Godeok clusters, including Sweet Salt (14,983 rv), Brand New Highmond (9,606 rv), and Memorable Moment (3,359 rv).

3. "Gangdong = the eastern end of Seoul" — Not aware of transfer accessibility: Gangdong has excellent downtown accessibility with Lines 5 and 8 + Gwangnaru Hangang + Transfer to Cheonho Station for Line 5 + Terminal for Line 9. Jamsil (#82) 30 minutes on foot or Line 8 5 minutes — Gangdong + Jamsil 1-day bundle available.
