🎯 Why a Full Guide on Foreigner Visas and K-ETA?

The number one pain point for foreigners entering Korea = Visa and K-ETA. Since the introduction of K-ETA in 2021, it has become the top priority for visa-free foreigners, integrating a full set of tourist, work, student, and marriage visas. This is the primary resource for the first 30 days of preparation for inbound foreigners.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "K-ETA = Visa-Free = Same?": K-ETA = Mandatory pre-authorization for visa-free foreigners (since 2021). Visa-free foreigners from 112 countries (e.g., US, Japan, EU) must apply for K-ETA at least 24 hours before departure.

2. "Visa = Permanent = One-time OK?": Foreigner visas = full set of stay period, number of entries, and renewal. Renew 30 days before expiration; Alien Registration Card (ARC) is mandatory for stays over 90 days.

3. "Visa-Free = 90 days every time OK?": Visa-free 90 days = cumulative 90 days within 6 months (subject to change) + Korea Immigration Service (Churipguk Gwalliso) is foreigner-friendly.

4. "Medical Tourism = General Visa OK?": K-Medical ([#242 K-Medical](242_k_medical_guide.md)) = C-3-3 Medical Tourism Visa + foreigner-friendly + within 90 days + top pick for foreigners.

5. "E-2 English = All Foreigners?": E-2 English Instructor = TEFL/TESOL certification + degree + foreigner-friendly Korean English academies are a top choice for foreigners.
