⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "K-ETA = visa-free = same": K-ETA = Required prior permission for visa-free foreigners (2021~). Visa-free foreigners from 112 countries including the US, Japan, and EU = K-ETA application required 24 hours before departure.

2. "Visa = Permanent = OK once received": Foreigner visa = Full set of stay/number of stays/renewal. Renew 30 days before expiration. Alien Registration Card (ARC) is required for stays of more than 90 days.

3. "Visa-free = 90 days, OK every time": Visa-free 90 days = 90 days accumulated in 6 months (variable) + Korea's No. 1 foreigner-friendly immigration office.

4. "Medical tourism = General visa OK": K-Medical (#242) = C-3-3 medical tourist visa + Foreigner friendly + Within 90 days + No. 1 foreign enthusiast.

5. "E-2 English = all foreigners": E-2 English instructor = TEFL/TESOL certification + degree + No. 1 foreigner friendly Korean English academy = No. 1 foreigner enthusiast.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. K-ETA application: Official site + 24 hours before departure + KRW 10,000 + Foreign enthusiast required.

2. Alien Registration Card (ARC): Required for stays of more than 90 days + Alien Mania No. 1 (Immigration Office).

3. Foreign Visa Renewal: 30 days before expiration + Hi Korea Online + Immigration Office.

4. Foreign worker (E-9·E-7): Korea’s No. 1 foreign worker system + No. 1 foreign mania.

5. Foreigner Marriage Visa (F-6): Korean spouse + foreigner friendly foreigner enthusiast.

6. Foreigner 1345 (Comprehensive information for foreigners): No. 1 foreigner-friendly comprehensive + interpretation in English, Chinese, Japanese, Russian, and Arabic.
