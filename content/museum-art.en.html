Full Guide to Korean Museums and Art Galleries — 12 Picks: National Museum of Korea, MMCA, Leeum, Kansong

Subtitle: 11 places in Seoul plus 1 day-trip. Which are free, which need a reservation, and which offer English interpretation, based on official websites (checked 2026-10-06)

ISSUE 03 · #377 · 2026-05-24 (fully revised 2026-10-07)

🎯 How to Read This Guide

Seoul's big museums are more often free than you might expect. The permanent exhibitions at the National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관), the National Folk Museum of Korea (국립민속박물관) and the National Palace Museum of Korea (국립고궁박물관) are free, and so are the Seoul Museum of History (서울역사박물관), the Seoul Museum of Craft Art (서울공예박물관) and the Seoul Museum of Art (서울시립미술관). What costs money is mostly special and temporary exhibitions.

Closing days differ from place to place, though. Public museums mostly close on Mondays, but the National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) and the National Folk Museum of Korea (국립민속박물관) are open on Mondays. Many private galleries require a reservation. Check the closed-days and reservation columns in the table below first.

⚠ 4 Things First-Time Visitors Often Miss

1. If you bunch your museum visits on a Monday, most doors will be closed. The places open on Monday are mainly the National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관), the National Folk Museum of Korea (국립민속박물관) and MMCA Seoul (국립현대미술관 서울관). The National Palace Museum of Korea (국립고궁박물관) closes only on the last Monday of each month.

2. The National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) also closes its exhibition halls on four Mondays a year. On the first Monday of March, June, September and December, the permanent and special exhibition halls are closed.

3. The Kansong Art Museum (간송미술관) does not open just any time. It opens only during exhibitions, and you must book in advance through Interpark or Naver to get in.

4. Wednesday and Saturday nights are museum time. The National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) and MMCA Seoul (국립현대미술관 서울관) stay open until 21:00 on Wednesdays and Saturdays. MMCA is free during those hours (18:00–21:00).

① National Museums and Galleries (4)

1. National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관)

  • Address: 137 Seobinggo-ro, Yongsan-gu (용산구 서빙고로 137) · about 150 m from Exit 2 of Ichon Station (이촌역), Line 4 and Gyeongui-Jungang Line
  • Hours: Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun 09:30–17:30; Wed and Sat 09:30–21:00 (last admission 30 minutes before closing)
  • Closed: January 1, Seollal, Chuseok. Permanent and special exhibition halls are closed on the first Monday of March, June, September and December
  • Fee: Permanent exhibition free. Special exhibitions charged separately
  • English: Regular English docent tours run daily at 10:30 and 13:00 in October 2026 (meet in front of the Korean Empire Room on the 1st floor of the Permanent Exhibition Hall). An English guidebook PDF is also available.
  • Highlights: Signature artifacts on the website include Kim Hong-do's "Seodang" (School), a blue-and-white porcelain jar with an orchid design, and the Diamond Sutra. The special exhibition "Hansik" (Korean Food) runs until October 25.

2. MMCA Seoul (국립현대미술관 서울관)

  • Address: 30 Samcheong-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 삼청로 30) · Exit 1 of Anguk Station (안국역), Line 3, or Exit 4 of Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역)
  • Hours: Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun 10:00–18:00; Wed and Sat 10:00–21:00
  • Closed: January 1, Seollal, Chuseok. Temporary closure on December 1
  • Fee: Each exhibition has its own ticket. Combined ticket 10,000 won (when there are 3 or more special exhibitions). Free on Wed and Sat 18:00–21:00 and on the last Wednesday of each month; visitors aged 24 and under or 65 and over are also free.
  • Highlights: Do Ho Suh exhibition (until February 9, 2027), Korea Artist Prize 2026 (until December 6). It connects on foot with Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) and Bukchon (북촌).

3. National Folk Museum of Korea (국립민속박물관)

  • Address: 37 Samcheong-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 삼청로 37), inside Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) · about a 15-minute walk from Exit 1 of Anguk Station (안국역), Line 3
  • Hours: March–October 09:00–18:00 (Wednesdays until 20:00); November–February 09:00–17:00. Last admission 1 hour before closing
  • Closed: January 1, Seollal, Chuseok
  • Fee: Free (Gyeongbokgung Palace admission is separate)
  • English: Free docent tours in Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese (limited schedule)
  • Note: Permanent Exhibition Hall 1 is closed for renovation from September 30 to December 21, 2026 (reopens December 22). Halls 2 and 3 (Korean Daily Life and the Korean Life Cycle) can be visited.

4. National Palace Museum of Korea (국립고궁박물관)

  • Address: 12 Hyoja-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 효자로 12) · 2-minute walk from Exit 5 of Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역), Line 3
  • Hours: Mon–Fri and Sun 09:30–17:30; Sat 09:30–21:00; until 21:00 on the last Wednesday of each month
  • Closed: January 1, Seollal, Chuseok, last Monday of each month
  • Fee: Free (until further notice)
  • English: Exhibition commentary in English, Japanese and Chinese is offered on weekdays between 13:00 and 15:00.
  • Highlights: Relics from 500 years of the Joseon royal court. Right next to Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁), so it pairs well with a palace visit.

② Private Art Museums (3)

5. Leeum Museum of Art (리움미술관)

  • Address: 60-16 Itaewon-ro 55-gil, Yongsan-gu (용산구 이태원로55길 60-16) · about 5 minutes uphill from Exit 1 of Hangangjin Station (한강진역), Line 6
  • Hours: Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00 (ticket sales close at 17:30)
  • Closed: Mondays, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok days
  • Fee: The traditional art permanent exhibition and the Gabriel Orozco garden are free. Special exhibitions are paid (combined ticket 25,000 won for adults, 18,000 won for a single exhibition). 50% off on the last Wednesday of each month
  • Reservation: Online booking opens 14 days before the visit date (4 tickets per person). On-site tickets are also available, but you may have to wait.
  • English: Digital guide (Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese)
  • Highlights: The digital interactive displays in the traditional art permanent exhibition. The website address has changed to leeumhoam.org.

6. Kansong Art Museum (간송미술관)

  • Address: 102-11 Seongbuk-ro, Seongbuk-gu (성북구 성북로 102-11) · 20-minute walk from Exit 6 of Hansung Univ. Station (한성대입구역), Line 4
  • Opening: Open only during exhibitions. Regular exhibitions are held in spring (late April to early May) and fall (late September to early October)
  • Reservation: Advance reservation required (Interpark, Naver)
  • Current exhibition: "Sangseo (箱書): Reading the Collection," October 7 to December 6, 2026 (the 120th anniversary of Gansong's birth). Works include Kim Si's "Seonrok Wanwol," Im Hui-ji's "Pungjuk," and Jang Seung-eop's "Chujeong Gwiseon."
  • Check the exhibition detail page for fees and daily hours.

7. Amorepacific Museum of Art (APMA) (아모레퍼시픽미술관)

  • Address: 100 Hangang-daero, Yongsan-gu (용산구 한강대로 100) · Exit 1 of Sinyongsan Station (신용산역), Line 4 (an underground passage connects to the headquarters building)
  • Hours: Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00
  • Closed: Mondays, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok holidays
  • Reservation: "Sol LeWitt: Open Structure" requires advance reservation (the next month's bookings open on the 15th of each month). Overseas and out-of-town visitors can also get on-site tickets.
  • English: Free exhibition commentary through the mobile app APMA GUIDE
  • Highlights: Sol LeWitt's first large-scale solo exhibition in Korea (45 works, 13 wall drawings). The headquarters building itself is also worth seeing.

③ Seoul Municipal (3) — All Free

8. Seoul Museum of Art, Seosomun Main Branch (SeMA) (서울시립미술관 서소문본관)

  • Address: 61 Deoksugung-gil, Jung-gu (중구 덕수궁길 61) · 4-minute walk from Exit 10 of City Hall Station (시청역), Line 2
  • Hours: Tue–Thu 10:00–20:00; Fri 10:00–21:00; Sat, Sun and public holidays 10:00–19:00 (18:00 from November to February). Last admission 1 hour before closing
  • Closed: Mondays (open if it is a public holiday), January 1
  • Fee: Free. Special exhibitions are paid
  • Highlights: "Yoo Youngkuk: The Mountain Is Within Me" (until October 25), followed by "Lynn Hershman Leeson: Listen" (October 21 to February 21, 2027). The building preserves the facade of the former Supreme Court. It stands at the end of the Deoksugung Stone-Wall Road (덕수궁 돌담길).

9. Seoul Museum of History (서울역사박물관)

  • Address: 55 Saemunan-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 새문안로 55), next to Gyeonghuigung Palace (경희궁)
  • Hours: Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat, Sun 09:00–21:00; Thu 09:00–18:00 (extended evening hours until November 30, 2026)
  • Closed: Mondays (open if it is a public holiday), January 1
  • Fee: Free
  • English: Permanent exhibition panels and QR guides are in Korean, English, Chinese and Japanese. The 1st-floor information desk lends free audio guides (Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese) in exchange for an ID card (for the 3rd-floor permanent exhibition hall only).
  • Highlights: The 3rd-floor permanent exhibition covers Joseon-era Hanyang through modern Seoul. Special exhibition "Grand Tour of Seoul City Planning" runs until November 8.

10. Seoul Museum of Craft Art (서울공예박물관)

  • Address: 4 Yulgok-ro 3-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 율곡로3길 4) · 1-minute walk from Exit 1 of Anguk Station (안국역), Line 3
  • Hours: 10:00–18:00. Until November 30, 2026, open until 21:00 Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat, Sun (Exhibition Hall 1, floors 1–2)
  • Closed: Mondays (open if it is a public holiday), January 1
  • Fee: Free
  • Highlights: Permanent exhibition "Artisans Benefiting the World," special exhibition "Equinox: Autumn_Spring" (until March 7, 2027). It sits between Bukchon (북촌) and Insadong (인사동), so it is easy to drop in while walking.

④ Design and Day Trip (2)

11. Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) (동대문디자인플라자)

  • Address: 281 Eulji-ro, Jung-gu (중구 을지로 281) · Exit 1 of Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station (동대문역사문화공원역), Lines 2, 4 and 5
  • Hours: 10:00–20:00 (may vary by facility and exhibition)
  • Closed: January 1, Seollal and Chuseok days
  • Highlights: "Wayne Thiebaud" exhibition (September 19 to February 21, 2027), Seoul Design Week (October 14–25). Rooftop tours and architecture tours are available. For the night view, see #172.

12. Museum SAN (뮤지엄 산, Wonju, Gangwon)

  • Address: 260 Oak Valley 2-gil, Jijeong-myeon, Wonju-si, Gangwon (강원 원주시 지정면 오크밸리2길 260)
  • Hours: 10:00–18:00 (ticket sales close at 17:00)
  • Closed: Monday
  • Fee: All admission is paid. Basic pass (outdoor garden + Paper Museum + art museum) 23,000 won for adults, Signature ticket 59,000 won
  • Reservation: The James Turrell Pavilion, Meditation Hall and Antony Gormley's GROUND require timed reservations (online or on site). The Turrell Pavilion and Meditation Hall cannot be visited on their own.
  • English: English tour every Friday at 12:00 (interpretation receivers available for rent)
  • Note: A day trip from Seoul takes the whole day. Check the website for public transportation information.

⚙ FAQ

1. How can I fill a day without spending money? National Palace Museum of Korea (국립고궁박물관) → National Folk Museum of Korea (국립민속박물관, inside Gyeongbokgung Palace) → Seoul Museum of Craft Art (서울공예박물관) → Seoul Museum of History (서울역사박물관). All the museums are free, and if you also tour Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁), you pay only the palace admission separately.

2. Where is English interpretation most reliable? The National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관, daily English docent) and the Seoul Museum of History (서울역사박물관, audio guide). Leeum (리움미술관) uses a digital guide.

3. Where can I go on a Monday? The National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관), the National Folk Museum of Korea (국립민속박물관) and MMCA Seoul (국립현대미술관 서울관). The National Palace Museum of Korea (국립고궁박물관) is open except on the last Monday.

4. Where can I go at night? On Wednesdays and Saturdays, the National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) and MMCA (국립현대미술관) until 21:00; on Saturdays, the National Palace Museum of Korea (국립고궁박물관) until 21:00. Until November 30, the Seoul Museum of History (서울역사박물관) and the Seoul Museum of Craft Art (서울공예박물관) are also open until 21:00 Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat and Sun.

5. Where should I not go without a reservation? The Kansong Art Museum (간송미술관, advance reservation), the Sol LeWitt exhibition at the Amorepacific Museum of Art (아모레퍼시픽미술관, reservations, with on-site tickets also offered to overseas visitors), and the Turrell Pavilion and Meditation Hall at Museum SAN (뮤지엄 산).

6. What if I'm with children? The National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관), the National Folk Museum of Korea (국립민속박물관) and the Seoul Museum of Craft Art (서울공예박물관) have children's museums. All three require online advance reservations.

🗺 3 One-Day Courses

A. Gyeongbokgung Free Route (on foot)

  • 09:30 National Palace Museum of Korea (국립고궁박물관) → 11:00 Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) + National Folk Museum of Korea (국립민속박물관) → lunch in Samcheong-dong (삼청동) → 15:00 MMCA Seoul (국립현대미술관 서울관) → 16:30 Seoul Museum of Craft Art (서울공예박물관)

B. Yongsan and Hannam

  • 09:30 National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) → lunch near Sinyongsan (신용산) → 14:00 Amorepacific Museum of Art (아모레퍼시픽미술관) (confirm your reservation) → 16:00 Leeum Museum of Art (리움미술관) (Hangangjin Station, 한강진역)

C. Wednesday Night Downtown

  • 14:00 Seoul Museum of Art (서울시립미술관) → Deoksugung Stone-Wall Road (덕수궁 돌담길) → 16:00 Seoul Museum of History (서울역사박물관) → dinner → 19:00 National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) (open until 21:00 on Wednesdays)

🌙 Fall 2026 Late-Night Openings at Seoul City Cultural Venues (Sep 15 – Nov 30)

Seoul municipal cultural venues that normally open late only on Friday nights will stay open until 21:00 on Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat and Sun from September 15 to November 30, 2026 (the Seoul city program "문화로 야금야금", roughly "nibbling on culture"). You can now tour the palaces and markets by day, have dinner, then add a museum to the evening.

  • Participating venues: Seoul Museum of History (서울역사박물관) · Seoul Museum of Craft Art (서울공예박물관) · Hanseong Baekje Museum (한성백제박물관) · Unhyeongung (운현궁) · Namsangol Hanok Village (남산골한옥마을) · Sejong Chungmugong Story (세종·충무공이야기)
  • Seoul Museum of History (서울역사박물관): The 1st-floor special exhibition hall, 3rd-floor permanent exhibition hall and the Seoul city model video hall stay open.
  • Seoul Museum of Craft Art (서울공예박물관): Only floors 1–2 of Exhibition Hall 1 are open until 21:00, and admission is free. Closed on Mondays; Thursdays until 18:00.
  • Each venue opens a different area, so check each venue's website before you go. From December, only Friday-night openings remain.
