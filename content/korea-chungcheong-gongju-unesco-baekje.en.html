Gongju: Walking Baekje's Ungjin Royal Fortress, Royal Tombs and UNESCO Sites in a Day (Official Info as of Oct 2026)

Subtitle: Gongsanseong Fortress, the Tomb of King Muryeong and Royal Tombs, Gongju National Museum (Gongju National Museum, free), Magoksa Temple, Seokjang-ri Museum and Gongju Hanok Village. Fees, hours, closures and bus numbers follow the official Gongju city tourism and museum sources · Baekje Cultural Festival 2026 (Oct 3-11, held in Gongju and Buyeo at the same time) · KTX Gongju Station to Yongsan about 1.5 hours

ISSUE 04 · #1043 · 2026-06-08

🎯 Why Go to Gongju

Gongju is Ungjin (웅진), the capital Baekje used for 64 years (475-538) after losing the Han River region and moving south. Its royal fortress is Gongsanseong Fortress, on a hill beside the Geumgang River (금강), and the king who ruled from it lies in the Tomb of King Muryeong. Together with sites in Buyeo (부여) and Iksan (익산), they became the UNESCO World Heritage "Baekje Historic Areas" in 2015, while Magoksa Temple, deep in the mountains, was listed separately in 2018 as part of "Sansa, Buddhist Mountain Monasteries in Korea". The KTX from Seoul reaches Gongju Station (공주역) in about 1 hour 30 minutes, so a day trip works, and in autumn the chestnut harvest overlaps with the Baekje Cultural Festival. The fees and hours below follow the official pages of the Gongju city tourism site, Gongju National Museum and the Baekje Cultural Festival as of 2026-10-07.

① Gongsanseong Fortress: Ungjin Baekje's Royal Fortress

  • The main draw is the wall path that loops along the Geumgang River (around 2.6 km; follow the on-site signs for the exact route). Enter from Geumseoru Gate (금서루, the west gate), and the riverside stretch near Gongbungnu Pavilion (공북루) is the most open section.
  • During the Baekje Cultural Festival (Oct 3-11) the fortress and its surroundings become festival grounds, so the route differs from usual. Weekday mornings are quiet.
  • Buy the combined ticket (₩6,000) and you can see the Tomb of King Muryeong and Royal Tombs on the same day.

② Tomb of King Muryeong and Royal Tombs + Gongju National Museum: See Them as a Set

  • The royal tombs sit on a hill with seven tombs. King Muryeong's tomb was found in 1971 during drainage work and is the only Baekje royal tomb discovered intact, never looted; an inscribed stone tablet inside tells us who was buried there (official guide). You see the tomb's structure at the on-site exhibition hall.
  • The excavated objects (coffin ornaments, gold crown ornaments and more) are in the Ungjin Baekje Gallery of Gongju National Museum, across the road. It is free and closed on Mondays, so go Tuesday to Sunday, royal tombs first, then the museum.

③ Magoksa Temple: UNESCO Mountain Monastery (2018)

  • It is open year-round with no admission fee. Its distinctive layout splits the temple into a south and a north compound on either side of a stream, and it also runs a temple stay (book through the temple's website).
  • Bus 770 takes about 40 minutes from Gongju Bus Terminal and runs 14 times a day, so check the timetable first. A taxi saves time.

④ Seokjang-ri Museum: The Paleolithic Age

  • The riverside Seokjang-ri site on the Geumgang is where Korean Paleolithic excavation first began. The museum opens at the same time as Gongsanseong and closes on Jan 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok. KTX Gongju Station users get in free (official).

⑤ Gongju Hanok Village: If You Want to Stay Overnight

  • This is a hanok lodging complex with traditional-culture programs attached. Check-in is 15:00. Gongju city has announced that overnight bookings will stop from September 2027, so look at other lodging for trips after that.

⑥ Food: Gongju Chestnuts

  • Gongju is chestnut country. Places listed in Korea Tourism Organization guides: Bammaeul (밤마을) (chestnut pie and chestnut eclairs), Lucia's Garden (루치아의뜰), Mimaji (미마지) and Cheongsin Farm (청신목장). In winter there is the Winter Gongju Roasted Chestnut Festival (겨울공주 군밤축제), held Feb 4-8 in 2026. Check on site for the harvest season.

🎪 Baekje Cultural Festival 2026: Oct 3 (Sat) to Oct 11 (Sun), Gongju and Buyeo at the Same Time

  • This is the 72nd edition. It is held in Gongju (Geumgang Sinkwan Park, Gongsanseong and the royal city center) and in Buyeo (Jeongnimsaji Temple Site and Gwanbuk-ri Ruins area) over the same period (official). If you read this after October 7, it is already under way or over, so check the official site for that day's program.
  • During the festival, the area around Gongsanseong sees crowds and traffic controls. If you only want to see the heritage sites, the days before or after the festival are easier.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. "Gongsanseong is the Sabi capital": Sabi is Buyeo. Gongju is Ungjin (475-538).

2. Visiting the museum on a Monday: Gongju National Museum is closed Mondays. The royal tombs and Gongsanseong close only on Jan 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok (check on site for Gongsanseong closures).

3. Not knowing about the combined ticket and buying separately: the Gongsanseong + royal tombs combined ticket is ₩6,000.

4. Going to Magoksa without checking bus times: bus 770 runs 14 times a day.

5. Visiting only the heritage sites during the festival: Oct 3-11 the Gongsanseong area is festival grounds. For a quiet visit, go before or after.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Can I do it as a day trip from Seoul? Yes, KTX to Gongju Station takes about 1.5 hours. Gongsanseong + royal tombs + museum is a half day.

2. What are the admission fees? Gongsanseong and the royal tombs are ₩3,000 each, or ₩6,000 for the combined ticket. The museum and Magoksa are free.

3. Can I go inside King Muryeong's tomb? You see the structure at the on-site exhibition hall, and the excavated objects at Gongju National Museum.

4. When is the Baekje Cultural Festival? Oct 3-11, 2026, in Gongju and Buyeo at the same time.

5. Where can I sleep in a hanok? Gongju Hanok Village (check-in 15:00, ☎ 041-881-2828). Bookings are announced to stop from September 2027.

6. How do I combine it with Buyeo? Stay one night. See the Buyeo guide (#1044).
