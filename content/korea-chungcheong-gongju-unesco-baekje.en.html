Gongju (공주), Chungcheong Province for Foreigners — UNESCO Baekje Historic Areas 2015 · Gongsanseong Fortress · Royal Tomb of King Muryeong

> Subtitle: Gongju (공주) UNESCO Baekje Historic Areas 2015 + Gongsanseong Fortress (공산성) + Royal Tomb of King Muryeong (무령왕릉) & Museum + Hanok Village (한옥마을) for Foreign Tourists

ISSUE 04 · #1043 · 2026-06-08

🎯 Why a Gongju (공주) Guide?

Gongju (공주) is home to the UNESCO Baekje Historic Areas 2015, the Sabi Capital Matrix of the Baekje (백제) Kingdom (BC 18~AD 660), Gongsanseong Fortress (공산성), the Royal Tomb of King Muryeong (무령왕릉), the Songsan-ri Tombs (송산리 고분군), and related museums. Common pain points for foreign visitors include: (a) the misconception that "Gongju (공주) is not well-known," (b) the fact that UNESCO 2015 comprises 8 clusters (4 in Gongju (공주) + 4 in Buyeo (부여) = Baekje (백제) Matrix), (c) the Royal Tomb of King Muryeong (무령왕릉) was discovered in 1971 in perfect condition and is a top destination for history enthusiasts, (d) Gongsanseong Fortress (공산성) is a Baekje (백제) Sabi Capital cluster, (e) there's a full English information set for foreign enthusiasts, and (f) Gongju Hanok Village (공주 한옥마을) + Hanbok (한복) experiences.

This guide covers 5 Pillars, 12 Signatures + UNESCO + Baekje (백제) + Royal Tomb of King Muryeong (무령왕릉).

⚠ Medical · Safety Disclaimer

Gongju (공주) is safe and foreigner-friendly. Gongsanseong Fortress (공산성) has some steep sections on the fortress wall; seniors should be cautious. Emergency ☎ 1339 · 1330.

✈️ Foreigner Pain Point #1 — UNESCO Baekje (백제) 8 Clusters

  • UNESCO 2015 Baekje Historic Areas (백제역사유적지구): 4 in Gongju (공주) + 4 in Buyeo (부여) = 8 Clusters
  • Gongju (공주) 4: Gongsanseong Fortress (공산성) · Songsan-ri Tombs (송산리 고분군) (Royal Tomb of King Muryeong (무령왕릉)) · Jeongjisan Mountain Site (정지산 유적) · Songsan-ri Tombs Museum (송산리 고분군 박물관)
  • Buyeo (부여) 4: Jeongnimsa Temple Site (정림사지) · Gwanbuk-ri Site (관북리 유적) · Busosanseong Fortress (부소산성) · Neungsan-ri Tombs (능산리 고분군)
  • Iksan (익산) + Additional: Iksan Mireuksa Temple Site (익산 미륵사지) · Wanggung-ri Site (왕궁리 유적) (Expansion)
