⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Cherry Blossoms = Yoon Jung-roman": Yoon Jung-ro and others Banpo Hangang Park, Seoul Forest, Olympic Park, Campus (Kyunghee, Yonsei, Korea, Ewha Womans University), Palace (Changdeokgung Palace Huwon, Changgyeonggung Palace), Namsan, Nakseongdae Full set.

2. "Cherry blossom season = April": Spring flower season precision:

  • Third to fourth week of March: Jeju and Busan (South first)
  • First to second week of April: Peak in Seoul and metropolitan area (Yunjung-ro, Banpo, campus, palace)
  • Third week of April: Gangwon-do and outlying mountains (Bukhansan Mountain and Gwanaksan Mountain)
  • First week of May: Outskirts No. 1 Jinhae, Gyeongju follow-up, other outskirts

3. "Cherry blossoms = crowded even on weekdays": Quiet between 9 and 10 o'clock or after 18 o'clock on weekdays. Crowds explode on weekends (especially Yunjung-ro and Banpo).

2. Spring Flower Festival Period: Yeouido Spring Flower Festival (first week of April), Seoul Grand Park, Jinhae Gunhang Festival.

3. Foreigner Hanbok + Spring Flowers: Free admission to Changdeokgung Palace and Changgyeonggung Palace Hanbok + Spring Flowers combined.
