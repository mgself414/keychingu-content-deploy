🎯 Why a Spring Cherry Blossom Full Guide?

Korea's spring cherry blossom season (first to second week of April) is the #1 inbound season for foreigners. This is an integrated full guide to cherry blossom spots, distributed by area and theme. It ranks #1 in foreign Instagram and vlogs every year.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Cherry Blossoms = Yunjeung-ro (윤중로) Only": Besides Yunjeung-ro (윤중로), there's a full set of spots including Banpo Hangang Park (반포 한강공원)·Seoul Forest (서울숲)·Olympic Park (올림픽공원)·Campuses (Kyung Hee (경희)·Yonsei (연세)·Korea (고려)·Ewha Womans University (이화여대))·Palaces (Changdeokgung Palace (창덕궁) Huwon (후원)·Changgyeonggung Palace (창경궁))·Namsan (남산)·Nakseongdae (낙성대).

2. "Cherry Blossom Season = April": Precise Timing for spring flowers:

  • Third to Fourth Week of March: Jeju (제주)·Busan (부산) (southern regions first)
  • First to Second Week of April: Seoul (서울)·Gyeonggi (수도권) peak (Yunjeung-ro (윤중로)·Banpo (반포)·campuses)
  • Third Week of April: Gangwon-do (강원도)·outlying mountains (Bukhansan (북한산)·Gwanaksan (관악산))
  • First Week of May: Outlying #1 Jinhae (진해)·Gyeongju (경주) follow-up·other outlying areas

3. "Cherry Blossoms = Crowded Even on Weekdays": Quieter on weekdays from 9-10 AM or after 6 PM. Weekends are extremely crowded (especially Yunjeung-ro (윤중로)·Banpo (반포)).

① Hangang (한강) River & Citizen Parks (4)

1. Yunjeung-ro (윤중로) (Yeouido (여의도)) ★ Korea's #1 Cherry Blossom Spot

  • Address: Yeouiseo-ro (여의서로), Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구) (#169)
  • Foreigner Rating ★: ◎◎ — Korea's #1 Cherry Blossom Spot, 1.7km long
  • Timing: First to Second Week of April (peak for 3-5 days)
  • Event: Yeouido Spring Flower Festival (여의도 봄꽃축제) (First Week of April, 1 week)
  • Details: #169 Yeouido Sisters

2. Banpo Hangang Park (반포 한강공원)

  • Foreigner Rating ★: ◎ — Combination of Jamsu Bridge (잠수교) + Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (달빛무지개분수) + spring flowers (#173)
  • Timing: First to Second Week of April

3. Seoul Forest (서울숲)

  • Foreigner Rating ★: ◎◎ — 350,000 pyeong (approx. 287 acres) of spring flowers + Deer Garden + Hangang (한강) River (#01·#205)
  • Timing: First to Second Week of April

4. Olympic Park (올림픽공원)

  • Foreigner Rating ★: ◎ — 5km walking path + 88 Seoul Olympic Memorial Hall (88서울올림픽기념관) (#82·#205)
  • Timing: First to Second Week of April

  • Foreigner Rating ★: ◎ — Korea's first women's university established in 1886 + spring flowers (#171·#204)

③ Palaces & Traditional Sites (2)

9. Changdeokgung Palace (창덕궁) + Huwon (후원) ★ UNESCO Spring Flowers

  • Foreigner Rating ★: ◎◎ — UNESCO World Heritage Site 1997 + Huwon (후원) (Secret Garden (비원)) spring flowers (#207)
  • Admission Fee: Palace ₩3,000 (~$2.2 / ~15 RMB / ~330 JPY) + Huwon (후원) ₩5,000 (~$3.7 / ~25 RMB / ~550 JPY) (advance reservation)
  • Free palace admission when wearing hanbok (한복) (Korean traditional attire)

10. Changgyeonggung Palace (창경궁)

  • Foreigner Rating ★: ◎ — Former Japanese colonial era zoo → restored + spring flowers (#207)

🗺 4 Full-Day Cherry Blossom Courses

A. Seoul's #1 Cherry Blossom Full Day (First to Second Week of April)

  • 09:00 Yunjeung-ro (윤중로) (Yeouido (여의도) #169) → 12:00 Banpo Hangang Park (반포 한강공원) (#173) → 14:30 Seoul Forest (서울숲) (#01) → 17:00 Olympic Park (올림픽공원) (#82)

B. Campus Cherry Blossom Full Day

  • 09:00 Kyung Hee University (경희대) Grand Peace Palace (평화의전당) → 12:00 Korea University (고려대) Central Plaza (중앙광장) → 14:30 Yonsei University (연세대) Baekyang-ro (백양로) → 17:00 Ewha Womans University (이화여대) Hangaram Plaza (한가람 광장)

C. Palace & Traditional Cherry Blossom Full Day

  • 09:00 Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) + hanbok (한복) → 12:00 Changdeokgung Palace (창덕궁) + Huwon (후원) (advance reservation) → 14:30 Changgyeonggung Palace (창경궁) → 17:00 Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌 한옥마을) (traditional Korean house village)

D. Outlying 2-Day 1-Night Trip (First Week of April)

  • Day 1: Jinhae Gunhangje Festival (진해 군항제) (Gyeongnam (경남), Korea's #1 outlying cherry blossom festival)
  • Day 2: Gyeongju Bomun Lake (경주 보문호) (UNESCO combination)
