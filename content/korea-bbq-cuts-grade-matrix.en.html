Korean BBQ Cuts & Grades Matrix — What Meat Should I Order?

Subtitle: 12 Pork, Beef, & Chicken Cuts for Foreign Tourists + Hanwoo Grades + Foreigner Recommendation Priority

ISSUE 03 · #804 · 2026-06-07

🎯 Why a Korean BBQ Cuts & Grades Matrix?

Foreigner Korean BBQ pain point #1 = "Why is there so much on the menu?". Korean BBQ has a culture of differentiated cuts unlike Japanese, American, or Brazilian BBQ — even for pork, Samgyeopsal (삼겹살, Pork Belly), Moksal (목살, Pork Neck), Hangjeongsal (항정살, Pork Jowl), and Galbi (갈비, Pork Rib) are all different menu items. Hanwoo (한우, Korean native beef) comes in 1++, 1+, 1, 2, 3 grades, plus imported options (US/Australian Wagyu). Foreigners either (a) don't know the cut names and only order popular items, (b) don't understand the grade differences and only order expensive cuts, or (c) miss out on cost-effective cuts like Chadolbaegi (차돌박이) and Galmaegisal (갈매기살). This guide provides a matrix of 6 pork + 6 beef + 3 chicken cuts + Hanwoo (한우) grades + foreigner recommendation priority.

Imported Wagyu (US·Australia)

  • US Wagyu — Similar price to Hanwoo (한우) 1+, higher marbling — found in some Japanese restaurants and BBQs in Gangnam (강남)
  • Australian Wagyu — Cost-effective ★★★ (70~80% of Hanwoo (한우) 1)
  • US/Australian Sirloin (Choice·Prime) — 1.5 times the price of Hanwoo (한우) 2~3 grades

Pork Grades — Han-don (한돈)

  • Han-don (한돈) = Korean pork, 1+·1·2 grades (managed by Han-don (한돈) Self-Help Fund, unlike Hanwoo (한우), there is no 1++)
  • Imported Pork (Spain·Canada·Chile, etc.) = Cost-effective ★★★ (60~70% of Han-don (한돈))
  • Iberico (이베리코, Spain) = black pig (Bellota grade is 100% acorn-fed, regular Iberico (이베리코) is grain-fed), ₩25,000~40,000 per serving (special menu)

Kkotdeungsim (꽃등심, Hanwoo (한우)) 2~3 minutes (both sides) medium-rare ~ medium Preserve marbling, slight pink is OK

Safety Principle: Pork·chicken = well-done is essential (food poisoning·salmonella risk). Beef (Hanwoo (한우)·Wagyu) can be medium.

⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "Samgyeopsal (삼겹살) = all pork": Samgyeopsal (삼겹살, belly) is just one cut. Moksal (목살)·Hangjeongsal (항정살)·Galmaegisal (갈매기살) are different cuts, different prices, and different textures.

2. "Hanwoo (한우) = always expensive": Hanwoo (한우) Grade 2 Chadolbaegi (차돌박이) = ₩28,000/100g is cost-effective. 1++ Deungsim (등심) ₩80,000 vs Hanwoo (한우) 1+ Kkotdeungsim (꽃등심) ₩50,000 = 1+ is optimal for beginners.

3. "Imported = always inferior to Hanwoo (한우)": US·Australian Wagyu is similar in quality to Hanwoo (한우) 1+. Prices are 70~80%. Cost-effective ★.

4. "English menu = all the same": "Beef Ribeye" can refer to Hanwoo (한우)·US·Australian beef. Origin and grade confirmation is essential.

5. "Grilling = always well-done": Chadolbaegi (차돌박이) (30 seconds·thin) / Kkotdeungsim (꽃등심) (2~3 minutes·medium) / Hangjeongsal (항정살) (5 minutes slowly) — it varies by cut.

⚙ FAQ (8 Questions)

1. Top Priority for Foreigner Beginners: Samgyeopsal (삼겹살) (#1) + Chadolbaegi (차돌박이) (#2) + Hanwoo (한우) 1+ Kkotdeungsim (꽃등심) (#3). Recommended as a 3-item bundle.

2. Top 3 Cost-effective Cuts: Chadolbaegi (차돌박이, beef) + Galmaegisal (갈매기살, pork) + Dakgalbi (닭갈비, chicken)

3. Top 3 Luxury Cuts: Hanwoo (한우) 1++ Ansim (안심) + Hanwoo (한우) 1++ Kkotdeungsim (꽃등심) + Iberico (이베리코, Spanish black pig)

4. Spicy Options: Yangnyeom Galbi (양념갈비)·Maeun Galbi (매운갈비)·Dakgalbi (닭갈비)·Gochujang (고추장) marinade·Cheongyang chili pepper accompaniment

5. Vegetarian·Halal·Kosher: BBQ is inherently non-Halal·non-Kosher·non-vegetarian. Vegetarian fallback = Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥, soybean sprout soup)·Bibimbap (비빔밥)·various namul (나물, seasoned vegetables) (other menu items).

6. Allergies: Beef·pork·chicken + marinade (garlic·soy sauce·gochujang (고추장)·honey) should be declared in advance. Gluten = many marinades contain soy sauce (wheat).

7. Payment·Tips: Card OK·no tipping culture. Some hotel BBQs have a 10% service charge separately.

8. Lunch vs Dinner: Jeomsim Teukseon (점심 특선, lunch course) is 30~50% cheaper on weekdays 11:30~14:00, dinner offers atmosphere·first round of hoesik (회식, company dinner/gathering).

⚠ Medical·Safety·Dietary Disclaimer

BBQ = self-cooking. Proper cooking of meat (pork well-done·beef medium or more) is essential. Pregnant women·immunocompromised individuals = cook more thoroughly (well-done). Declare allergies (beef·pork·chicken·marinade garlic·soy sauce + gluten) in advance. In case of suspected food poisoning ☎ 1339 (medical, English). This guide provides general information, not medical advice.

Dietary Restrictions Fallback:

  • Halal·Kosher: Regular Korean BBQ restaurants do not have Halal·Kosher certification. → Recommend separating from general Korean restaurants. Exceptions include some Halal-certified BBQ restaurants in Itaewon (이태원) and Gangnam (강남) (e.g., Eid·Makan·Halal Korean BBQ – search and book in advance).
  • Vegetarian·Vegan: BBQ is meat-centric. → Recommend Bibimbap (비빔밥)·Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥)·Sachal Eumsik (사찰음식, temple food) separately (other restaurants).
  • Islam·Judaism·Hinduism: For religious dietary needs = recommend checking with the restaurant in advance.

💰 Essential Tourist Tips

  • Average Budget per Person (As of June 2026, prices subject to change):
  • Cost-effective = ₩30,000~50,000 (Samgyeopsal (삼겹살, pork)·Chadolbaegi (차돌박이, beef))
  • General = ₩50,000~80,000 (Hanwoo (한우) 1+ + Yangnyeom Galbi (양념갈비))
  • Luxury = ₩100,000~200,000 (Hanwoo (한우) 1++ + Wagyu + Wine)
  • Beverage·Alcohol Culture:
  • Soju (소주, Korean distilled spirit) ₩4,000~6,000 / Beer ₩4,000~8,000 / Wine ₩30,000~100,000 / Cider·Cola ₩2,000~3,000
  • Somaek (소맥) (soju (소주) + beer bomb shot) = Korea's #1 hoesik (회식, company dinner/gathering) culture, high foreigner curiosity ★. Ratio 1:3 or 1:2. Forced bomb shots are OK to refuse (recommend trying one glass).
  • Children's Safety: Not recommended for children under 6 years old to have spicy marinades or raw meat. Medium·well-done meat + ssam (쌈, leafy wrap) + rice recommended. Beware of burns from hot plates·charcoal grills (for restaurants with children = request family seating in advance).
  • Jeomsim Teukseon (점심 특선): Weekdays 11:30~14:00 = 30~50% discount (outside of hoesik (회식) hours).
  • Payment: Card OK / No tips / Some hotels have a 10% service charge.
  • VAT Refund: Food and beverages are generally not eligible for refund.
