🎯 How to Use This Guide

A roasted sweet potato handed over in a paper bag warms your palm, and a bite of freshly griddled hotteok lets the brown sugar syrup run out. This guide splits Korea's December-to-February snacks into four tracks: street snacks such as bungeoppang (fish-shaped pastry) and roasted sweet potatoes, hotteok (sweet filled pancake), Dongji red bean porridge (patjuk), and strawberry desserts. Street stalls move around constantly, so instead of pinning down shops, this guide records "where these are commonly sold" from official VisitKorea and Korea.net articles. For hotteok and red bean porridge shops that stay put, it only copies the address and hours from their VisitKorea listing pages. It is for people visiting Korea in winter who are deciding what to buy on the street.

The old guide's "bungeoppang ₩1,000-2,000 (~$0.75-$1.50)", "hotel strawberry ₩130,000-150,000 (~$98-$113)" and the reputation that "Busan Nampo-dong ssiat hotteok (seed hotteok) is the best" were removed because no official source could be found. It also grouped strawberry festivals as a winter event, but in 2026 the Nonsan, Wanju Samnye and Jinju strawberry festivals were all held in March-April (VisitKorea).

🐟 Street Snacks — Bungeoppang, Roasted Sweet Potatoes, Roasted Chestnuts, Egg Bread

Street stalls change location, so only the types of sellers are carried over from official articles.

  • Bungeoppang: A small fish-shaped pastry (VisitKorea, 2025-11). It used to be sold mainly at pojangmacha, but the number of selling carts has shrunk, and it is now also sold through delivery and supermarkets (microwave versions) (Korea.net Monthly KOREA, 2020-03). The same article says "ten years ago you got 5-10 for ₩1,000 (~$0.75), now 2-3" — that is a 2020 figure, so check the current price on site
  • Roasted chestnuts and roasted sweet potatoes: Roasted in their skins over a brazier. They used to be common on the street, but are now also sold in convenience stores and subway stations (VisitKorea, 2025-11)
  • Jjin-ppang (steamed bun) and wang-mandu (big dumpling): Easy to buy at convenience stores in winter (VisitKorea, 2025-11)
  • Gyeran-ppang (egg bread): A bread with a whole egg set on top (VisitKorea, 2025-11). No description of where it is sold
  • Where they cluster: Myeongdong Station Food Street (Line 4 Myeongdong Station Exit 6, go straight and keep left, VisitKorea)
  • Bungeoppang shop (fixed store): Bungeorang Odeng (붕어랑 오뎅) — 73 Baego-ro, Pyeongchang-eup, Pyeongchang-gun, Gangwon. Signature items bungeoppang and fish cake. 10:00-19:00, closed Sundays, 033-333-0118 (VisitKorea). Price not posted

🥞 Hotteok

Hotteok is a chewy pancake filled with brown sugar, cinnamon and nuts (VisitKorea, 2025-11).

  • Gunsan Jungdong Hotteok (군산중동호떡): 52 Seorae-ro, Gunsan-si, Jeonbuk. Third generation, hotteok that is not fried in oil (filling: white glutinous barley, black soybean, black rice, black sesame). Mon-Sat 10:00-19:00, Sun 13:00-18:00, open year-round. 063-445-0849. They recommend eating takeout within an hour (VisitKorea)
  • Jjoljjol Hotteok (쫄쫄호떡): 40-1 Sangdang-ro 55beon-gil, Sangdang-gu, Cheongju-si, Chungbuk. 10:50-19:30 (last order 19:00), open year-round (Wednesdays takeout of hotteok and tteokbokki only). 043-221-2208. Card payment is listed as "none" (VisitKorea)
  • Shingane Hotteok Gimbap Tteokbokki (신가네 호떡 김밥 떡볶이): 61 Chungnyeol-daero 249beon-gil, Dongnae-gu, Busan. Glutinous rice hotteok griddled to order. 06:00-22:00, closed on the 2nd and 4th Tuesday of each month, cards accepted. 051-552-2644 (VisitKorea)
  • Jeju Dongmun Market Hotteok Alley (제주 동문시장 호떡골목): 16 Dongmun-ro, Jeju-si, to the left of Gate 1 of Dongmun Traditional Market. A row of shops selling bingtteok (buckwheat crepe roll), mugwort hotteok and japchae hotteok. Hours vary by shop (VisitKorea)

🥣 Dongji Red Bean Porridge

  • Dongji: The 22nd of the 24 solar terms, the day with the longest night. People believed that eating Dongji red bean porridge made you a year older, and sprinkled the porridge on gates and walls to ward off bad luck (National Folk Museum of Korea). Dongji 2026 is December 22 at 05:50 (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute)
  • In Aedongji and Jungdongji years there is also a custom of making red bean siru-tteok (steamed red bean rice cake) instead of porridge (National Folk Museum of Korea)
  • Sindangdong Cheon Patjuk (신당동천팥죽): 33 Dasan-ro 44-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul. Red bean porridge and red bean kalguksu (noodles). 10:30-20:00 (last order 19:30), closed Sundays, cards accepted. 02-2237-6385 (VisitKorea)
  • Yetnal Patjukjip (옛날팥죽집): 276 Baekje-daero, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si, Jeonbuk. Saealpatjuk, red bean kalguksu, pumpkin porridge. 10:00-20:00, open year-round, cards accepted. 063-226-2022 (VisitKorea)
  • Munhori Patjuk (문호리팥죽): 641 Bukhangang-ro, Seojong-myeon, Yangpyeong-gun, Gyeonggi. Red beans simmered without starch and served unseasoned. 11:00-19:20 (break 14:50-16:00), closed Mondays. 031-774-5969 (VisitKorea)
  • Yetnal Patjuk (옛날팥죽, Jeju): 130 Seongeupminsok-ro, Seogwipo-si, Jeju, next to Seongeup Folk Village. Saealpatjuk starts at 2 servings. 10:00-17:00, closed Mondays and the Seollal and Chuseok holidays. 064-787-3479 (VisitKorea)
  • Halmae Patbingsu Danpatjuk (할매팥빙수단팥죽): 24 Yongho-ro 90beon-gil, Nam-gu, Busan. Certified as a Century Shop (baengnyeon gage), sweet red bean porridge and patbingsu (shaved ice with red beans). 09:00-22:00, open year-round (VisitKorea)
  • None of the five posts prices — confirm by phone

🍓 Strawberry Desserts — Season Notice Not Yet Out

  • Hotel strawberry desserts and buffets: No official 2026-27 season announcement was found (2026-10-21). There are only press reports that last season's notices came out around December [secondary] — check dates and prices after each hotel's official website posts its notice
  • Strawberry festivals are in spring: 2026 Wanju Samnye Strawberry Festival Mar 6-8, Nonsan Strawberry Festival Mar 26-29, Jinju Strawberry Festival Apr 10-12 (VisitKorea). 2027 schedules not yet posted

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Price range: This guide has no officially posted won prices. The Korea.net bungeoppang price comes from a 2020 article
  • Vegetarian: Whether bungeoppang and hotteok batter contains milk or eggs is not on any official page. Whether the red bean porridge uses meat stock is also not posted — ask before ordering
  • Allergies: Hotteok filling contains nuts (VisitKorea). Gunsan Jungdong Hotteok's filling has black soybean and black sesame, and gyeran-ppang contains a whole egg (VisitKorea). Tell staff in advance about nut, soy, sesame or egg allergies
  • Halal: VisitSeoul lists Eid (이드) in Itaewon (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil, Yongsan-gu; daily 12:00-21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF). Certification can change, so check before you go. None of the shops in this guide carry a halal label
  • Show-it cards: #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Winter Snack Practicalities — Solo, Booking, Payment, Ordering, Getting Around

  • Solo: Street stalls and hotteok shops are takeout by default, so they are easy to use alone. However, at Jeju Yetnal Patjuk the saealpatjuk starts at 2 servings (VisitKorea). For places to sit alone, see #348 Seoul Solo Diner Map
  • Booking: The shops in this guide post no booking information. Book hotel strawberry offerings on the hotel's website after the notice is out
  • Payment: Jjoljjol Hotteok lists cards as "none"; Sindangdong Cheon Patjuk, Yetnal Patjukjip and Shingane accept cards (VisitKorea). Street stall payment methods could not be confirmed officially — #527 Korean Payment Systems
  • Ordering: When language gets in the way, see #1339 Ordering Etiquette
  • Getting around: For Gunsan, Cheongju and Jeonju, see #1297 Express and Intercity Bus Manual; for Seoul, see #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards. For a day route in Gunsan, see #1108 Gunsan KTX 2 Days 1 Night

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Go to a strawberry festival in winter" — all three 2026 festivals were held in March-April (VisitKorea)

2. "Roasted sweet potatoes only at street stalls" — they are now also sold in convenience stores and subway stations (VisitKorea)

3. "A bag of bungeoppang for ₩1,000 (~$0.75)" — already in 2020 it was 2-3 for ₩1,000 (~$0.75) (Korea.net). Check the current price on site

4. "Yangpyeong or Seongeup red bean porridge on a Monday" — Munhori Patjuk and Jeju Yetnal Patjuk are closed on Mondays (VisitKorea)
