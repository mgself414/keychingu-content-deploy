⚠ 3 Winter Dessert Mistakes

1. "Winter = Too cold for desserts" — Misunderstanding of full street food scene: Not too cold. In reality, Bungeoppang (붕어빵) (red bean, custard cream, pizza - Nov-Mar), Hotteok (호떡) (seed, honey, nut), Gungoguma (군고구마) (roasted sweet potato), and Gyeranppang (계란빵) (egg bread) are Korea's #1 winter street food cluster (₩1,000-2,000; #1 for foreign enthusiasts).

2. "Strawberries = Only in spring" — Misunderstanding of the Dec-May season: Not only spring. In reality, Ttalgi (딸기) (strawberries) are available from December to May (#474 spring sub-deep) + hotel strawberry season starts in December (#469 hotel bingsu (빙수) follow-up).

3. "Illuminations = Just for photos" — Misunderstanding of dessert integration: Not just photos. In reality, DDP, Lotte Tower, Cheonggyecheon (청계천), and Everland (에버랜드) Christmas (linked to #424, #451) + illumination cafe cluster (dessert fusion, Instagram-worthy).
