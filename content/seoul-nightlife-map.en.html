⚠ 3 Nightlife Mistakes

1. "Clubs = same as the US/Europe" — not knowing Korean club etiquette: Assuming they're identical. In reality, Korean clubs (cover charge ₩30,000–50,000 (~$22–37) · casual dress code NG · guest lists) require knowing the etiquette in advance. Octagon is the #1 foreigner-friendly club.

2. "Bars = hotels only" — not knowing the Cheongdam & Itaewon speakeasies: Hotels only. In reality, Alice Cheongdam · Le Chamber (World's 50 Best · Cheongdam speakeasy cluster) serve Korea's #1 cocktails + reservations required.

3. "Late night = dangerous" — not knowing the 24h safety scene: Assuming danger. In reality, #1 in OECD safety (#176 walkable Seoul): 24h convenience stores · noraebang (노래방) karaoke · pocha (포차) stalls · safe even solo — the #1 full experience for foreign enthusiasts.
