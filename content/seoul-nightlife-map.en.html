🎯 How to use this guide

Climb the stairs to the 2nd floor, 333m from Exit 3 of Itaewon Station, and you reach a jazz club that opened in 1978. From Monday to Thursday there is no cover charge, so one KRW 5,000 drink gets you live music, and on Fridays and Saturdays it stays open until 1:00 am (Seoul Tourism Organization). Seoul's nights branch out like this: a night of listening to jazz, a night over a drink at a cocktail bar, a night of city lights from a view lounge, a night of grilling skewers in a tent bar, and then clubs. This guide covers those five lanes using only places whose hours and conditions were confirmed on official pages, and for clubs it gives the entry rules instead of venue names. It also settles the taxi fare home in numbers.

🎷 Jazz: a light that has been on since 1978

  • All That Jazz (올댓재즈) (Itaewon): 216 Itaewon-ro, Yongsan-gu, Seoul, 2nd floor. Sun-Thu 18:00~24:00, Fri-Sat 18:00~01:00, open year-round. It is introduced as Korea's first jazz club, opened in Itaewon in 1978 by a Chinese-American, Mr. Ma Myeong-deok. Mon-Thu there is no cover charge, only a minimum drink of KRW 5,000, and the Fri-Sat charge is not listed on the page. 333m from Exit 3 of Itaewon Station (Line 6), phone +82-2-795-5701 (Seoul Tourism Organization, updated 2026-06-01).
  • Hours and performance fees for Boogie Woogie, Evans, Jazz Story, Dido Jazz Lounge and Yeonnam 5701 are listed venue by venue in the #470 Seoul Jazz Bar Guide.

🍸 Cocktail bars: a drink you book ahead

  • Charles H. (찰스 H.) (Four Seasons Hotel Seoul): 97 Saemunan-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul, LL floor. 17:00~01:30. It ranked No. 7 in Asia's 50 Best Bars 2022 (No. 1 in Korea) and No. 42 in World's 50 Best Bars 2021. 136m from Exit 7 of Gwanghwamun Station, phone +82-2-6388-5500 (Seoul Tourism Organization, updated 2026-09-03). Hotel bars often seat guests with reservations first, so call to ask about a seat before you go.
  • Glam Lounge (글램 라운지) (Itaewon): 26 Itaewon-ro 27ga-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul, 2nd floor. Sun-Thu 19:00~03:00, Fri-Sat 19:00~05:00, year-round. No minors. 111m from Exit 1 of Itaewon Station (Line 6), phone +82-2-796-6853 (Seoul Tourism Organization, updated 2026-06-02).
  • Bars by neighborhood: #140 Hongdae Bars, #163 Jongno Bars, #166 Gangnam Bars; hotel bars: #496 Seoul Hotel Dining and Bars.

🌃 View lounges: grab a seat before the sun goes down

  • Signiel Seoul The Lounge (시그니엘 더라운지) (Jamsil): 79th floor, Lotte World Tower, 300 Olympic-ro, Songpa-gu, Seoul. Daily 11:00~22:00, phone 02-3213-1212. The Lounge Classic course is KRW 200,000 per person at the regular price (Lotte Hotels e-shop). We could not confirm the current hours of Bar 81 on the 81st floor from an official page, so call to ask before you go.
  • Free night views are closer at the Han River bridges and parks → #2 Free Night View Spots; for a list of rooftop bars, see #260 Seoul Rooftops.

🏮 Pocha: skewers and draft beer under a tent

  • Jongno 3-ga pojangmacha (포장마차, tent bars) street: tent bars line up at every exit of Jongno 3-ga Station. Hours differ from stall to stall, and menu prices vary day by day. For how to walk it and the route onward to Ikseon-dong and Jongmyo, see #585 Jongno 3-ga Pocha and Outdoor Drinking.
  • Euljiro Nogari Alley: a draft beer alley that has been going since 1980. Three outdoor-drinking streets in Seoul and the seaside pocha streets are in #485 Korea Pojangmacha Guide.
  • Some pocha are still quicker with cash, so carry a little → #1275 Paying Etiquette.

🎧 Clubs: rules over names

  • Age: In Korea a minor is anyone under 19, and a person who reaches January 1 of the year they turn 19 is treated as an adult. Venues where minors are barred from entry or employment must check age at the door, and alcohol cannot be sold to minors (Easy-to-Find Korean Laws, Youth Protection Act). For foreigners the passport is the ID, and many places do not accept a photo or copy.
  • Why we don't list venue names: Clubs open and close on short cycles. As of October 29, 2026, when this guide was written, we could not find a club whose current operation is confirmed on an official tourism page. Whether you go to Hongdae, Itaewon or Gangnam, check that day's official social media notice for opening, cover charge and dress code.
  • For the flow of a club night (entry, lockers, ordering, taxis), see the #454 Club Nightlife Guide.

🧰 Getting home: the numbers after the last train

  • Taxi: The Seoul standard taxi daytime base fare is KRW 4,800 for 1.6km (then KRW 100 per 131m or 30 seconds). The late-night surcharge of 20-40% runs 22:00-04:00: base fare KRW 5,800 from 22:00-23:00 and 02:00-04:00, and KRW 6,700 from 23:00-02:00. Leave Seoul and a 20% out-of-city surcharge applies, up to 60% when it overlaps with late night. Deluxe and large taxis are KRW 7,000 for 3km (KRW 8,400 late night). The phone-booking fee is KRW 1,000 by day and KRW 2,000 at night (Seoul Metropolitan Government transport, fares in effect from 2023-02-01).
  • If the subway has stopped: the four ways home (night bus, taxi, jjimjilbang) are in #1395 If You Missed the Last Train. For taxi apps, see #1273 Kakao T Taxi.
  • Phone: Tourist interpretation 1330 (from abroad +82-2-1330) · police 112 · fire and ambulance 119. If you are worried about walking alone at night, see the Seoul safety services in the #460 Solo Female Guide.

⚠ 4 common mistakes

1. Going to a club with your passport left at the hotel: Age is checked at the door. Many places do not accept photos or copies (Easy-to-Find Korean Laws, on-site practice).

2. Expecting the Mon-Thu price at a jazz club on a Friday night: All That Jazz's "no cover" applies Mon-Thu, and the Fri-Sat charge is not on the official page (Seoul Tourism Organization). Ask before you go in.

3. Pricing a taxi at the daytime fare after 11 pm: From 23:00-02:00 the base fare is KRW 6,700 with a 40% surcharge (Seoul Metropolitan Government). Move before 10 pm and there is no surcharge.

4. Arriving at a view lounge after 10 pm: Signiel Seoul The Lounge closes at 22:00 (Lotte Hotels & Resorts). Take your seat before the sun goes down.
