⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Hotel breakfast = hotel guests only": Some hotel breakfasts may be used outside of the hotel (Signiel STAY, Shilla Park View, JW Griffin 60-80K). Advance reservation required.

2. "Afternoon tea = British style only": Korean hotel afternoon tea = Authentic Korean traditional sweets and traditional tea + British fusion (Signiel BAR 81, Shilla the Library, Four Seasons Maru) + No. 1 on Instagram for foreign enthusiasts.

3. "Executive Lounge = Expensive": Club Room (Club Floor) +50~100K per night → Breakfast, tea time, happy hour (unlimited alcohol), check-in separate Full set = No. 1 for cost-effectiveness enthusiasts.

4. "Hotel breakfast = Western food only": Korean 5-star breakfast = Western food + Korean + Japanese + Chinese + Indian + vegan corner full set + foreigner-friendly multinational.

5. "Cost-effectiveness = not very good": L7 Hongdae (15K) · Novotel Dongdaemun (20K) Breakfast = No. 1 cost-effectiveness for foreign enthusiasts + Various Korean and Western dishes.
