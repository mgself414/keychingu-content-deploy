⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Hotel breakfast = for guests only": Some hotel breakfasts are open to external guests (Signiel (시그니엘) STAY, Shilla (신라) The Parkview, JW Griffin (그리핀) from ₩60,000~₩80,000 (~$45-60 / ~320-430 RMB / ~6,400-8,500 JPY)). Advance reservation is essential.

2. "Afternoon tea = British style only": Korean hotel afternoon tea is a fusion of traditional Korean hangwa (한과) (confectionery) and jeontongcha (전통차) (tea) with British style (Signiel (시그니엘) BAR 81, Shilla (신라) The Library, Four Seasons Maru (마루)) + a top spot for foreigner Instagrammers.

3. "Executive Lounge = expensive": A Club Room (Club Floor) stay for 1 night, adding ₩50,000~₩100,000 (~$38-75 / ~270-540 RMB / ~5,300-10,600 JPY), includes a full set of breakfast, tea time, happy hour (unlimited alcoholic beverages), and separate check-in = a top spot for gasungbi (가성비) (value for money) enthusiasts.

4. "Hotel breakfast = Western food only": Korean 5-star hotel breakfasts offer a full set of Western, Hansik (한식) (Korean), Ilsik (일식) (Japanese), Jungsik (중식) (Chinese), Indian, and vegan corners + foreigner-friendly international options.

5. "Value for money = not good": L7 Hongdae (홍대) (₩15,000 (~$11 / ~80 RMB / ~1,600 JPY)) and Novotel Dongdaemun (동대문) (₩20,000 (~$15 / ~110 RMB / ~2,100 JPY)) breakfasts are foreigner enthusiasts' #1 gasungbi (가성비) (value for money) choice + a variety of Korean and Western dishes.
