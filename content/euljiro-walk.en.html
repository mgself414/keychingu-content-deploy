3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. Perception that “Euljiro = simple shopping/office street”

Euljiro has many reviews in English by foreigners Open stores 4.8★ + Bakeries 5.0★ + Dumpling houses 5.0★ — in other words, it is the center of a cluster of gourmet and cafes that Korean locals really go to. This is a course you won't regret going by focusing on long-established and hidden stores instead of global chains.

2. Recognition that “Daelim Shopping Center = just a shopping mall”

Pau (dumplings) is located on the bridge of Daelim Shopping Mall. Foreigner's English review: "Shop is on the bridge level and not on street level! We only realized this after walking the entire stretch of building twice" — It's not on the first floor, but on the upper floor of the bridge, so it's common to find it only after turning around. Save time if you know in advance.

3. Recognition that “evening is the main event in Euljiro”

In fact, open for breakfast and lunch a cluster of long-established restaurants — Sarangbang Kalguksu (open from 11:00), Asoto Bakery (open from 09:00). You must start in the morning to do one full course. Dinner alone is half the fun.

1. Asoto Bakery

> "The atmosphere embodies the retro 80s. Siphon coffee and pastries are the pride of this place. They serve cafe au lait instead of caffe latte."

> "A very cute cafe with a cozy atmosphere. I ordered the melon bread, strawberry parfait, and coffee, and everything was delicious and so cute."

  • Japanese reviews from foreigners: ★ 5.0/5.0 (5) — Very high awareness among Japanese (melon bread is popular as a diet dessert)
  • Signature: Melon bread / Dango dessert / Siphon coffee / Cafe au lait

11:30 ~ 12:30 · 1st lunch (snack)

2. Pao — On the bridge of Daelim Shopping Center

  • Category: Dumplings and donuts (freshly fried single item)
  • Location: Daelim Shopping Center, 157 Eulji-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul (3-minute walk from Euljiro 3-ga Station, On the 2nd floor bridge!)
  • Foreign English reviews (127 Google Maps / 8 foreign languages, ★ 5.0/5.0):

> "Best bread I've tried in Seoul! I loved how they fry the bread after ordering. We tried the original, cheese and red bean flavored donuts."

> "Shop is on the bridge level and not on street level! We only realized this after walking the entire stretch of building twice before heading up."

> "It's situated on the top floor of Daelim plaza. The items are fried-to-order so expect a little wait time but it's totally worth it."

  • Signature: Freshly fried donuts (original / cheese / red beans) — frying time ~5 minutes after ordering
  • Foreigner Advantage: English menu / OK for 1 person / Card/Easy payment
  • Key tips for foreigners: Located on the bridge on the 2nd floor (1st floor X). Take the stairs to the upper floor from the entrance of Daelim Shopping Center

  • Budget: 3 types of donuts = ₩9,000 per person

3. FAQ — 6 frequently asked questions by foreigners

Q1. Is it really possible to walk around?

  • Full course approximately 4~5km, 13 hours (09:00~22:00+). Medium 30-60 minutes walk + 30-90 minutes per store.
  • If you are not confident on foot, you can go 1 subway stop from Chungmuro ​​Station or Euljiro 3-ga Station.

Q2. Payment — Is WeChat Pay/Alipay available?

  • Pau, Asoto Bakery, Old's Stago, Ruli Coffee, Sarangbang Kalguksu: Card, Kakao Pay, and Samsung Pay are all OK.
  • Kasu·Inhyeongolbang: Card OK / WeChat·Alipay requires store confirmation.
  • Kasu, Hanju Diary, and Inhyeon Golbang are not covered by foreigners, so English information on the payment system is weak — Staff are encouraged to present their cards in Korean + with hand gestures.

Q3. Do you have an English menu?

  • Pau / Asoto Bakery / Oldestago / Ruri Coffee: English menu ✓
  • Sarangbang Kalguksu: English menu (friendly staff can speak English)
  • One Week Diary / Kasu / Inhyeon Golbang: Mainly in Korean (photos/hand gestures or Japanese if Japanese is available)

Q4. Is it possible for one person to visit?

  • Pau, Asoto, Sarangbang, Ruri, Oldestago: ✓ (natural alone)
  • Weekly Diary, Kasu, Inhyeonggobang: Limited seating + reservation recommended → Recommended on weekday evenings

Q5. Are vegan, halal, or religious meals possible?

  • Vegan: Only drinks from Ruri Coffee and Asoto Bakery (Americano, tea) are allowed. Animal ingredients are used in pau donuts.
  • Halal: No certified stores. Drinks only recommended.

Q6. Are children allowed?

  • Sarangbang Kalguksu, Asoto, Oldestago: OK with children
  • Kasu, Inhyeon Golbang, One Week Diary: narrow space + alcohol-centric → not recommended for children
