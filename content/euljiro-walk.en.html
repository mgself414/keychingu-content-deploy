3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. The perception that "Euljiro (을지로) = just a shopping/office street"

Euljiro (을지로) has numerous foreign English reviews: nofo (노포) 4.8★ + bakery 5.0★ + dumpling shop 5.0★ — meaning it's a central cluster of gourmet food and cafes that Korean locals truly frequent. To avoid regret, it's best to explore nofo (노포) and hidden spots rather than global chains.

2. The perception that "Daelim Sangga (대림상가) = just a shopping mall"

Pao (빠우, dumplings) is located on the bridge level of Daelim Sangga (대림상가). Foreign English reviews state: "Shop is on the bridge level and not on street level! We only realised this after walking the entire stretch of building twice" — It's common for visitors to only find it after walking around the building twice because it's on an upper bridge level, not the ground floor. Knowing this in advance will save you time.

3. The perception that "Euljiro (을지로) is mainly for evenings"

In reality, the cluster of popular restaurants includes nofo (노포) that open for breakfast and lunch — Sarangbang Kalguksu (사랑방칼국수) (opens 11:00 AM~), Asoto Bakery (아소토베이커리) (09:00 AM~). To complete the full course, you need to start in the morning. Visiting only in the evening means missing out on half the fun.
