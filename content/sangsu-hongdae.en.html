⚠ Five major mistakes in downtown Sangsu and Hongdae

1. Recognition of “Hongdae = street·club”

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. The main area of ​​Hongdae is recognized only as a street/club area. In reality, Sangsu and Wausan-ro are the center of diversity in the Hongdae area — a concentration of Japanese, Italian, lifestyle and design stores.

2. Hongdae main area vs. Yeonnam/Hapjeong route cannot be separated

Sangsu and Wausan-ro are within walking distance of Hongik University Station and Sangsu Station. Yeonnam (Exit 3) ≠ Sangsu·Seogyo (Exit 9) ≠ Hapjeong (Hapjeong Station) Sub-area separated. Instead of trying to group it into one route, focus on 1-2 sub-areas.

3. Object Seogyo branch + Fabi = Korean lifestyle store fair unknown

For foreign lifestyle fans, Wausan-ro Object Seogyo Branch + Fabi + Merrymore + Letterpierre = Korean lifestyle brand deep dive possible. A different area from Myeongdong K-beauty.

4. Hidden nightlife pass like Ale Crew Pizza Pub

Hongdae nightlife for foreigners = only clubs and bars recognized. In fact, brunch like Ale Crew Pizza Pub + Hidden Beer Pub has a high level of satisfaction among foreigners. Instead of a noisy club.

5. Sangsu business hours and manners unknown

Sangsu Lifestyle Store is open from 12-20 p.m. Wausan-ro nightlife 19-02. Time zone matching.
