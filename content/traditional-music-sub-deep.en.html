12 selections of Korean traditional music

1. Pansori (UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity 2003)

  • Discrimination: 2003 UNESCO registration (Korea #1)·Occurred in the 17th century in the late Joseon Dynasty, 1 singer + 1 drummer·5 Madang (Chunhyangga, Simcheongga, Heungboga, Sugungga, Jeokbyeokga)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP Mania #1·Korean Authentic Music #1

2. Samulnori (full set of 4 instruments)

  • Distinction: Kkwaenggwari + gong + janggu + drum 4 instruments·1978 Deoksu Kim formed Samulnori·Full set of traditional Korean percussion
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ Mania·No. 1 Activity Mania

3. Nongak (UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity 2014)

  • Discrimination: 2014 UNESCO registration·Korean traditional rural music + dance·Intangible Cultural Heritage No. 11 (limited to 5 regions)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiast·Festival enthusiast

4. Korean traditional music (jeongak, folk music, creative music)

  • Discrimination: Jeongak (court music, traditional music), folk music (common people, pansori), creative work (modern), 3 branches of traditional Korean music
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

5. National Gugak Center (Seoul, Korea #1 Gugak Institute)

  • Discrimination: Opened in 1951·Korea #1 Gugak Institute·Seoul Main Center·Folk Gugak Center·Namdo Gugak Center·Busan Gugak Center 4 branches·Performances every week
  • Operation: Seoul main office (next to Seocho/Seoul Arts Center)·EN/JP full set
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ No. 1 among EN·JP·CN enthusiasts·Performance throughout the year

6. Jongmyo Jeryeak (UNESCO 2001 first Korean)

  • Discrimination: 2001 UNESCO 1st listing (Korea #1)·Jongmyo Jerye music of the Joseon royal family·Jongmyo Jerye performance on the first Sunday of May every year
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP Authentic Mania·Climax once a year·#379 cross

7. Gayageum (Korean traditional 12-string string instrument)

  • Discrimination: Initiated in the 6th century during the Gaya period·Korean traditional 12-string strings·Sanjo, jeongak, creation
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·No. 1 on SNS

8. Geomungo + Haegeum + Daegeum (3 traditional instruments)

  • Discrimination: Geomungo (6-string string, jeongak), Haegeum (2-string bow), Daegeum (bamboo hoengjeok), Orthodox 4th class along with gayageum
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Mania·Classic Full Set

9. Ganggangsullae (UNESCO 2009)

  • Discrimination: 2009 UNESCO registration·Jeonnam authentic female song + dance·Hold hands and rotate in a circle on a full moon night·1592 Spirit of resistance against the Japanese invasions of Korea
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiast·Family enthusiast

10. Authentic concert hall + music store

  • National Gugak Center (Seoul Main Center): Korea #1·Weekly performances
  • Korea House (Namsan): Royal cuisine + full performance set (#685 cross)
  • Gwangju National Asia Culture Center: Asian authentic music cluster
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiasts

11. New folk songs and original Korean traditional music (modern + traditional fusion)

  • Discrimination: Inalchi, Jambi Nai, Song Sohee, Mnet’s Pungryu Captain, etc. Popular·Hyundai + authentic fusion·MZ No. 1 mania
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ Mania #1·YouTube #1

12. Traditional music experience (instrument + performance class)

  • Discrimination: 1-hour musical instrument experience (Gayageum, Janggu, Gkwaenggwari)·1-2 hours 50K-100K·National Gugak Center + Private·Some EN guides
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Family·MZ Mania·No. 1 on SNS

FAQ 6

Q1. National Gugak Center performance price? A. 5K-50K per performance·Free for some·EN/JP subtitles OK·Cluster next to Seoul Arts Center.

Q2. UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity? A. Full set of Jongmyo Jeryeak 2001 (1st), Pansori 2003, Ganggangsullae 2009, Arirang 2012, Kimchi 2013, Nongak 2014, Tug of War 2015, Jeju Haenyeo 2016.

Q3. Samulnori vs. Nongak? A. Samulnori = 1978 Kim Deok-su·4 instrument indoor performance·Nongak = authentic rural/outdoor + dance full set.

Q4. Recommended performance for foreigners? A. First visit = Samulnori (activity)·Mania = Pansori (UNESCO)·Orthodoxy = Jongmyo Jeryeak (May).

Q5. Korea House traditional performance? A. Namsan Hanok Village·Royal cuisine + authentic performance full set·100K-300K full set·EN Guide No. 1.

Q6. Where can I experience musical instruments? A. National Gugak Center·Private + EN Guide·1-2 hours 50K-100K·Full set of Gayageum, Janggu, and Gkwaenggwari.
