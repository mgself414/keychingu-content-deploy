🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places where you can listen to or experience gugak, Korean traditional music. There are 3 gugak venues in Seoul, 3 national gugak centers in Jindo, Namwon and Busan, and 2 gugak museums in Namwon and Yeongdong. Venue fees differ by performance, so few fees are registered. Both museums have opening hours registered, but only Nangye Gugak Museum has a fee registered.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Seoul — Seoul Namsan Gugakdang, Seoul Donhwamun Gugakdang, and Gugak Performance Jinyeon

Seoul Namsan Gugakdang (서울남산국악당) (28 Toegye-ro 34-gil (퇴계로34길), Pil-dong 2-ga (필동2가), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-6358-5500) is a venue specializing in gugak, built in 2007. The performance hall is in the basement under a one-story hanok (한옥) building above ground. The sunken garden 'Chimsangwon (침상원)', connected to the basement 1 performance hall lobby, is a terraced garden that evokes the feel of Gyotaejeon (교태전) in Gyeongbokgung (경복궁), and natural light comes in even though the hall is underground.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~21:00. On performance days it closes when the performance ends. Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Fee: Varies by performance. Seating: 300 seats.

Seoul Donhwamun Gugakdang (서울돈화문국악당) (102 Yulgok-ro (율곡로), Waryong-dong (와룡동), Jongno-gu (종로구), ☎ 0507-1413-7001) is a venue specializing in gugak, named after Donhwamun (돈화문) of Changdeokgung (창덕궁). The Seoul city government bought the gas station site across from Changdeokgung and built it. It mixes traditional hanok and modern architectural styles, and has an indoor hall for hearing gugak with natural acoustics and a Gugak Madang (국악마당) for outdoor performances.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Operating hours differ depending on whether there is a performance. Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Fee: Varies by performance. Seating: 140 people.

Gugak Performance Jinyeon (국악공연 진연) (10 Insadong 5-gil (인사동5길), Jongno-gu, ☎ 02-953-0515) is a 60-minute traditional arts performance held in a hanok-concept venue. You can listen to the music and talk with the artists, and Korean music, K-pop, classical music and world folk songs are played on traditional instruments. It combines playing, singing and an instrument experience. It is registered for the schedule of January 1 to December 31, 2026.

  • Performance times: Daily 11:00, 14:00, 16:00. No performances on public holidays. Age: All ages.
  • Fee: General 65,000 won, 50% discount for people with disabilities, free for children under 24 months.
  • Program: Part 1 Gilje (길제), offering jeonghwasu (정화수) and praying for the audience's well-being; Part 2 Jinyeon (진연), royal court traditional performance, jeongak (정악) and sanjo (산조); Part 3 Hwahap (화합), playing the music of many countries on traditional instruments.

📍 Regional National Gugak Centers — Jindo, Namwon, Busan

National Gugak Center Namdo (국립남도국악원) (3818 Jindo-daero (진도대로), Jindo-gun (진도군), ☎ 061-540-4031) is a gugak center in Jindo, which is rich in traditional arts such as pansori (판소리), sinawi (시나위), sanjo, Jindo Arirang (진도아리랑), Ganggangsullae (강강술래), Namdo Deulnoreo (남도들노래), Jindo Ssitgimgut (진도씻김굿) and Jindo Bukchum (진도북춤). A regular performance is held every Saturday at 15:00. It has Jinakdang (진악당), a gugak-only theater of 520-plus seats, Dalbitmadang (달빛마당), an outdoor venue in the madangnori (마당놀이) format, and Byeolbitmadang (별빛마당), a small outdoor venue.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Every Sunday and Monday.
  • Fee: Varies by experience program. Experiences: Visiting classroom concerts (찾아가는 교실 음악회), Namdo Gugak Experience Bobaeseom Gugak Nadeuli (보배섬 국악나들이), and more.

National Gugak Center Namwon (국립민속국악원) (54 Yangrim-gil (양림길), Namwon-si (남원시), ☎ 063-620-2329) is a national music institution that opened in 1992 in Namwon, the home of pansori, and it passes on and spreads folk music centered on pansori and changgeuk (창극). It has Yewondang (예원당), Yeeumheon (예음헌), the Gugak Exhibition Hall (국악전시관) and the Folk Music Archive (민속악 자료실), and holds regular performances such as the Saturday regular performance and Pansori Madang (판소리 마당), as well as changgeuk performances newly created from the five pansori madang (마당).

  • Opening hours: Gugak Exhibition Hall 09:00~18:00. The performance halls vary with the performance. Closed days: Gugak Exhibition Hall open year-round.
  • Fee: Varies by performance. Seating: 680 seats.

National Gugak Center Busan (국립부산국악원) (2 Gugak-ro (국악로), Busanjin-gu (부산진구), ☎ 051-811-0114) is a national cultural arts institution that opened on October 28, 2008 to carry on the traditional performing arts of the Busan and Yeongnam (영남) region. It does performances, education and research together.

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: Varies by performance.

📍 Gugak Museums — Gugak Sanctuary (Namwon) and Nangye Gugak Museum (Yeongdong)

Gugak Sanctuary (국악의 성지) (69 Bijeon-gil (비전길), Unbong-eup (운봉읍), Namwon-si, ☎ 063-620-5740~2) is a gugak museum and exhibition hall built in Unbong-eup at the foot of Jirisan (지리산). It has the birthplace of Song Heung-rok (송흥록), who perfected Dongpyeonje (동편제) and was called Gawang (가왕), as well as a Gugak Exhibition and Experience Hall, a Dokgongsil (독공실), an outdoor performance hall, a cemetery for gugak musicians, and a shrine. It exhibits instrumental music and jeongak materials and relics donated by master singers.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, the day of Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Experiences: Hansam dance (한삼무용) experience, learning pansori by following along, making miniature gugak instruments, gugak performances, and more.
  • Parking: Available (free).

Nangye Gugak Museum (난계국악박물관) (9 Gugak-ro (국악로), Simcheon-myeon (심천면), Yeongdong-gun (영동군), ☎ 043-740-3886) is a museum specializing in gugak that opened in 2000. It introduces the life and achievements of Nangye Park Yeon (난계 박연), an early Joseon (조선) musician born in Yeongdong, in its video room and the Nangye Room (난계실). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, Park Yeon is counted as one of Korea's three great musical sages, along with Wang San-ak (왕산악) of Goguryeo (고구려) and Ureuk (우륵) of Silla (신라). The Gugak Room has more than 80 gugak instruments such as the daegeum (대금), gayageum (가야금), haegeum (해금) and pyeonjong (편종), as well as folk instruments from around the world.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok holidays, the day after public holidays. It is open during the Nangye Gugak Festival (난계국악축제).
  • Fee: Adult 2,000 won, teenagers, children, soldiers and Yeongdong-gun residents 1,500 won. For groups of 30 or more, adult 1,500 won, teenagers, children, soldiers and Yeongdong-gun residents 1,000 won.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Venue fees differ by performance — Seoul Namsan Gugakdang, Seoul Donhwamun Gugakdang, National Gugak Center Namwon and National Gugak Center Busan have no fixed fee registered.
  2. Many places close on Mondays — Seoul Namsan Gugakdang, Seoul Donhwamun Gugakdang, National Gugak Center Namdo, Gugak Sanctuary and Nangye Gugak Museum are closed on Mondays. National Gugak Center Namdo is also closed on Sundays.
  3. Jinyeon does not perform on public holidays — On all other days it performs 3 times a day.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which gugak performances can I see at a set time?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, they are Gugak Performance Jinyeon (daily 11:00, 14:00, 16:00, except public holidays) and the National Gugak Center Namdo regular performance (every Saturday 15:00). National Gugak Center Namwon also holds a Saturday regular performance.

Q. Where can I experience gugak myself?

A. Gugak Sanctuary (Hansam dance, learning pansori by following along, making miniature gugak instruments), Gugak Performance Jinyeon (a performance that includes an instrument experience), and the experience programs of National Gugak Center Namdo (visiting classroom concerts, Namdo Gugak Experience Bobaeseom Gugak Nadeuli).
