🎯 Why are there 3 levels of price for Korean food?

Price range for foreign Korean food enthusiasts Pain Point = 6,000 won~ Cost-effective / 30,000 won Standard / 200,000 won Luxury 3-stage matrix (#234 follow-up).

★ 12-line matrix at a glance (4 stores × 3 levels)

Cost-effectiveness (KRW 6,000~15,000 per person) — No. 1 for foreign enthusiasts

1. A full meal/set meal (one meal) ★ Authentic Korean value for money

2. Temple Food Jingwansa Temple (#291) — Foreign Mania

3. Foreigner-friendly Korean food (Insa-dong) — Full set in English

4. Bakery + Korean Food Combo — MZ Mania

Luxury (KRW 50,000~200,000+ per person) — No. 1 luxury destination for foreigners

9. Shilla Hotel Layeon ★ Michelin — No. 1 luxury destination for foreigners

11. Kwon Sook-su (Cheongdam) ★ Michelin — Foreign Luxury

12. Lotte Hotel Mugunghwa·Signiel Layeon — Luxury for foreigners

⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Hanjeongsik = Expensive": Hanjeongsik = 6,000 won~ Cost-effectiveness level 3 matrix + Foreign enthusiasts.

2. "Cost-effectiveness = not very good": Korean full meal/set meal = 6,000~15,000 won + No. 1 cost-effectiveness for foreigners.

3. "Luxury = Not allowed": Michelin limited meal = No. 1 foreign luxury enthusiast + Full set in English.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Cost-effectiveness of Korean traditional meals for foreigners: Baekban, set meal, temple food 6K~15K won.

2. Standard Korean food for foreigners: Garden, old palace, palace, modern 15K~50K won.

3. Luxury Korean food for foreigners: Shilla Layeon, Mingles, Kwon Sooksu Michelin.

4. Foreigner’s one-night stay (#277 Newlyweds): Luxury + hotel.
