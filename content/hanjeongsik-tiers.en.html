🎯 Why a 3-Tier Hanjeongsik Price Matrix?

The price pain point for foreign Hanjeongsik (한정식, Korean set meal) enthusiasts = a 3-tier matrix of ₩6,000~ for Budget / ₩30,000 for Standard / ₩200,000 for Luxury (following up on #234).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Hanjeongsik (한정식) = Only Expensive": Hanjeongsik (한정식) = ₩6,000~ Budget 3-Tier Matrix + Foreign Enthusiasts.

2. "Budget = Not Good": Korean Baekban (백반, a simple Korean meal with rice and side dishes) / Jeongsik (정식, a Korean set meal) = ₩6,000~15,000 + Foreigners' Top Budget Pick.

3. "Luxury = Inaccessible": Michelin Hanjeongsik (한정식) = Foreigners' Top Luxury Enthusiast Pick + Full English Menu Available.
