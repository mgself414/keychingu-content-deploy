Han River Core Parks Guide — Banpo, Jamwon, Ttukseom

🎯 Why the Han River Core Parks?

The core Han River (한강) experience means Banpo (반포), Jamwon (잠원), and Ttukseom (뚝섬), top destinations for foreign enthusiasts. A deep dive into Korea's best spots for night views, chimaek (치맥, chicken and beer), cycling, and BBQ.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Han River (한강) = Banpo (반포) only": The core Han River (한강) experience = Banpo (반포) + Jamwon (잠원) + Ttukseom (뚝섬), the #1 hub for foreign enthusiasts.

2. "Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (달빛무지개분수) = Simple": It's the Guinness World's #1 bridge fountain + the top spot for foreigner vlogs.

3. "Ttukseom (뚝섬) BBQ = Not possible": Ttukseom Hangang (뚝섬 한강) BBQ & camping is Korea's #1 spot for foreign enthusiasts.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Top 1 Han River (한강) Core for Foreigners: Banpo (반포), Jamwon (잠원), Ttukseom (뚝섬).

2. Top 1 Night View Spot for Foreigners: Banpo Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (반포 달빛무지개분수).

3. BBQ & Camping for Foreigners: Ttukseom Hangang (뚝섬 한강) (Top Foreigner Vlog Spot).

4. Cycling for Foreigners: Banpo (반포), Jamwon (잠원), Ttukseom (뚝섬) (#149).

💡 Foreigner Pain Point Guide

  • Language: Prioritize stores with full English, Japanese, and Chinese support.
  • Payment: 80% card, some nofo (노포, 30+ year traditional restaurant) establishments may require cash.
  • VAT Refund: Look for "Tax Refund" signs in stores, refunds for purchases over ₩50,000, airport refund counters.
  • Emergency: ☎ 1339 (Medical, English), ☎ 1330 (Tourist Interpretation), ☎ 112 (Police), ☎ 119 (Fire, Ambulance).
