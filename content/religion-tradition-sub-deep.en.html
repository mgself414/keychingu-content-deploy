🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers old cathedrals and churches in Seoul and Catholic martyrs' shrines. They are Myeongdong Cathedral (명동성당), Yakhyeon Cathedral (약현성당), Seoul Anglican Cathedral (성공회 서울주교좌성당) and Jeongdong Church (정동교회) in Jung-gu (중구), Wonhyo-ro Sacred Heart Church (원효로 예수성심성당) and the Saenamteo (새남터) and Danggogae (당고개) shrines in Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seosomun Historical Park (서소문역사공원) in Jung-gu, and Gahoe-dong Cathedral (가회동성당) and Seungdong Church (승동교회) in Jongno-gu (종로구). All places with a registered admission fee are free. Opening days differ from place to place, so it helps to look at the table below first. Seoul Anglican Cathedral is closed on weekends, and Wonhyo-ro Sacred Heart Church is open on weekends only. Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine (절두산 순교성지) in Mapo-gu (마포구) is in the #708 Mangwon and Hapjeong Guide, and temples in Seoul are in the #800 Seoul Temple Food and Templestay Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Jung-gu — Myeongdong Cathedral, Yakhyeon Cathedral, Seoul Anglican Cathedral and Jeongdong Church

Seoul Myeongdong Cathedral (서울 명동성당) [Myeongdong-gil 74 (명동길 74), Jung-gu, ☎ 02-774-1784] is the cathedral of the Archdiocese of Seoul. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is where the Korean Catholic community first formed, and it is the first large Gothic-style Catholic cathedral built on the Korean peninsula and the first parish church in Korea. It was designated a historic site on November 22, 1977. The remains of martyrs such as Bishop Imbert (엥베르), Father Maubant (모방) and Father Chastan (샤스탕) are enshrined in the crypt church. There are Mass and confession times on weekdays and weekends, and it is registered that those who are not believers can take part as well.

  • Opening hours: Office Tue~Sat 09:00~19:00, Sun 07:00~21:00. Regular confession Mon 13:00~17:00, Tue~Fri 11:00~20:00, Sat and Sun 10:00~20:00.
  • Closed days: Office every Monday. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Seoul Yakhyeon Cathedral (서울 약현성당) [Cheongpa-ro 447-1 (청파로 447-1), Jung-gu, ☎ 02-362-1891] is a brick church built in 1892. After religious freedom was allowed in 1886 and the number of believers grew, it was set up as a mission station under Myeongdong Cathedral. Father Doucet (두세) prepared the site, and Father Coste (코스트) took charge of the design and construction supervision. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the first Western-style church building built in Korea. It has some Gothic elements, but overall it is in the Romanesque style, with round arches and other features. 'Yakhyeon' (약현) is a shortened form of 'Yakjeonhyeon' (약전현), meaning a hill with a medicinal herb field.

  • Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Seoul Anglican Cathedral (대한성공회 서울주교좌성당) [Sejong-daero 21-gil 15 (세종대로21길 15), Jung-gu, ☎ 02-730-6611] is the cathedral of the Seoul Diocese of the Anglican Church of Korea. Bishop Trollope (트롤로프), the 3rd Anglican bishop of Joseon, built it to a design by the Englishman Arthur Dixon (아더 딕슨). The original plans were found in a library in England in 1993, and it was completed in 1996 in its original form. The building adds traditional Korean construction techniques to the Romanesque style. Inside are stone pillars symbolizing the 12 apostles and a mosaic of Jesus Christ, and in the crypt church there is a bronze plate where Bishop Trollope's remains are laid. It is 200m from Exit 3 of City Hall Station.

  • Opening hours: 11:00~16:00. Closed days: Weekends.
  • Guidance: A Korean-language guide volunteer is always on hand inside the cathedral. Foreign-language guidance is registered as by advance inquiry. Parking: Not available.

Seoul Jeongdong Church (서울 정동교회) [Jeongdong-gil 46 (정동길 46), Jung-gu, ☎ 02-753-0001~3] is the church where the American Methodist missionary Appenzeller (아펜젤러) held the first service in 1887 at Bethel Chapel (베델 예배당). The present building was begun in 1895 and dedicated on December 26, 1897. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the first church built after Protestantism arrived, and the only 19th-century church building that remains today. It is in a Gothic style simplified in the American manner, and in 1926 the two wings were widened to give today's square shape.

  • Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

📍 Yongsan-gu — Wonhyo-ro Sacred Heart Church, Saenamteo Memorial Church and Danggogae Martyrs' Shrine

The Former Yongsan Seminary and Wonhyo-ro Sacred Heart Church (옛 용산신학교와 원효로 예수성심성당) [Wonhyo-ro 19-gil 49 (원효로19길 49), Yongsan-gu, ☎ 02-701-5501] are two buildings designed and supervised by Father Coste. The Yongsan Seminary was built in 1892 and the Sacred Heart Church in 1902. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the Yongsan Seminary is the first seminary building in Korea. The church was built on a hill, so it has 3 floors on the south side and 2 on the north, and when you enter you see the stained glass in the center. Both buildings are registered as Catholic holy sites, and it is where the remains of Father Kim Dae-geon (김대건) were enshrined for more than 50 years.

  • Opening hours: Weekends 09:00~17:00. Closed days: Weekdays. It is registered that it opens on weekends only because of stronger student safety measures.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Saenamteo Memorial Church (새남터기념성당) [Ichon-ro 80-8 (이촌로 80-8), Yongsan-gu, ☎ 02-716-1791] is a church whose construction began in 1984 to mark the 200th anniversary of the founding of the Korean Catholic Church and was completed three years later. Saenamteo (새남터) was used from the early Joseon period as a military drill ground and an execution ground, and many Catholic believers were executed here during the Sinyu Persecution (신유박해) of 1801 and the Gihae (기해), Byeongo (병오) and Byeongin (병인) persecutions. Inside the church there is the Saenamteo Memorial Hall, and the remains of martyrs are enshrined.

  • Fee: Free. Parking: Not available.

Danggogae Martyrs' Shrine (당고개순교성지) [Cheongpa-ro 139-26 (청파로 139-26), Yongsan-gu, ☎ 02-711-0933] is where 10 men and women believers were martyred near the end of the Gihae Persecution of 1839. They were originally to be executed at the crossroads outside Seosomun (서소문), but merchants outside Seosomun asked for the execution ground to be moved because of the Seollal market season, so they were executed here. It was laid out on the theme 'The pain of the wild rose, the fragrance of the plum blossom', and it has the 14 stations of the Way of the Cross. It is a 15-minute walk from Exit 5 of Hyochang Park Station on Line 6.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~17:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Free.

📍 Jung-gu — Seosomun Historical Park and Seosomun Shrine History Museum

Seosomun Historical Park (서소문역사공원) [Chilpae-ro 5 (칠패로 5), Jung-gu, ☎ Seosomun Shrine History Museum 02-3147-2401] is a park made on the site of the 'execution ground at the crossroads outside Seosomun'. Many Catholics were executed here from the Sinyu Persecution to the Byeongin Persecution, and 44 of them became saints. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the largest Catholic shrine in Korea. The park was laid out with the martyrs' memorial tower built in 1984 left as it was.

Seosomun Shrine History Museum (서소문성지역사박물관) opened inside the park in June 2019. The permanent exhibition hall is on the 2nd basement floor, and the 'Sky Plaza' (하늘광장) on the 3rd basement floor has an open ceiling, so you can see the sky from underground. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it won the top excellence prize (최우수상) at the 2019 Seoul Architecture Awards. A Seosomun history tour with commentary is held every Thursday.

  • Opening hours: 09:30~17:30. Museum last entry 17:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday. The museum is also closed on January 1 and on Seollal and Chuseok days.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: 216 spaces, 2,000 won for the first 30 minutes, then 600 won every 10 minutes.

📍 Jongno-gu — Gahoe-dong Cathedral and Seungdong Church

Gahoe-dong Cathedral (가회동성당) [Bukchon-ro 57 (북촌로 57), Jongno-gu, ☎ 02-763-1570] is in the Bukchon (북촌) area where, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, Father Ju Mun-mo (주문모) celebrated the first Mass on Joseon soil at the house of Choe In-gil (최인길) on Easter Sunday, April 5, 1795. It became a full parish in 1949, and the present new sanctuary, built after the old one was torn down, was completed on November 21, 2013. The building uses both Eastern and Western architectural styles.

  • Mass: Sundays 07:00, 10:00, 18:00, Saturdays 18:00, first Saturday of each month 07:00. Weekdays Mon 07:00, Tue 19:00, Wed 10:00, Thu 19:00, Fri 10:00.
  • Closed days: Registered as every Monday and public holidays. The Mass timetable in the same registration also has a Monday 07:00 Mass.
  • Parking: Available.

Seungdong Church (승동교회) [Insadong-gil 7-1 (인사동길 7-1), Jongno-gu, ☎ 02-732-2341] is a church founded in 1893 by the American missionary Rev. Samuel Moore (새뮤얼 무어). The present red brick sanctuary was begun in 1910 and finished in 1912. On February 20, 1919, student representatives including Kim Won-byeok (김원벽), a Yeonhui College (연희전문학교) student who was president of this church's Christian Endeavor Society, held the first student leaders' meeting here to prepare the independence movement. In 1922 the Joseon Young Women's Christian Association (조선여자기독교청년회) was founded at this church.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Worship times are not registered. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Seoul Anglican Cathedral is closed on weekends, and Wonhyo-ro Sacred Heart Church is open on weekends only — the Anglican cathedral is open 11:00~16:00, and the Sacred Heart Church is open on weekends 09:00~17:00.
  2. Seosomun Historical Park and Seosomun Shrine History Museum are closed on Mondays — the museum is also closed on January 1 and on Seollal and Chuseok days, and last entry is 17:00.
  3. Gahoe-dong Cathedral's closed days and Mass timetable are registered differently from each other — the closed days are every Monday and public holidays, but the Mass timetable has a Monday 07:00 Mass.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Can I take part in Mass if I am not a believer?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, those who are not believers can take part in Mass and confession at Myeongdong Cathedral.

Q. Which places have foreign-language guidance?

A. It is registered that Seoul Anglican Cathedral takes foreign-language guidance by advance inquiry.
