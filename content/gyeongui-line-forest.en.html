🎯 Why do you need a walking meta for Gyeongui Line Forest Trail?

Gyeongui Line Forest Trail is a trendy walking trail in Seoul that regenerates the old Gyeongui Line railway into a park — 6.3km from Hyochang-dong, Mapo-gu to Yeonhui-dong, Seodaemun-gu. Yeonnam-dong·Yeonhui-dong segment 1.5km** ranks first among foreigners (0-minute walk from Exit 3 of Hongik University Station).

This meta guide combines Gyeongui Line Forest Trail + store stops — #220 Yeonnam·#224 Yeonhui-dong guide and maps cafe, brunch, and western food stops along the trail. Foreigner search terms ("Gyeongui Line Forest Park" · "Seoul forest walk" · "Yeonnam-dong walk"): Foreigner walk·nature fan demand is strong.

⚠ Five major mistakes foreigners make along Gyeongui Line Forest Trail

1. Recognition of “Gyeongui Line Forest Trail = General Park”

Foreigners perceive Gyeongui Line Forest Trail as a regular park like Seoul Forest or Yeouido Park. In reality, Old Gyeongui Line Railway Regeneration Park — a signature store + walk combination. It cannot be separated from the store flow in Yeonnam-dong and Yeonhui-dong.

2. Yeonnam-dong → Yeonhui-dong walking direction unknown

I don't know where the first Gyeongui Line Forest Trail for foreigners will start and end. Recommended: Start from Exit 3 of Hongik University Station → Finish at Yeonhui-dong (progressive hidden gems).

3. Couldn't find time to combine walking trail + store stop

Foreigners only walk or shop. In reality, 30 minutes of walking + 30 minutes of store stops × 4 spots = 4 hours full course is recommended.

4. Not sure about season/time zone suitability

Spring (cherry blossoms), summer (evening, sunset), fall (autumn leaves), winter (daytime sunlight) — optimal times vary by season. In summer, 18-21 PM, in winter, 12-15 PM.

5. Not sure the difference between Gyeongui Line Forest Trail and Han River Park

While Hangang Park is a wide area and traffic area, Gyeongui Line Forest Trail is a hidden walkway in the Yeonnam-Yeonhui neighborhood. The key to foreigner satisfaction is combining hidden stores.

① Yeonnam-dong ① — Starting from Hongik University Station (0.3 km)

Starting point: Exit 3 of Hongik University Station → Enter Gyeongui Line Forest Road → Dongjin Market Alley

Store stop:

  • Turtle Hip Yeonnam (#220, signal 46.2) — Yeonnam Cafe Signature
  • Carmelo Yeonnam (#220) — Yeonnam brunch style

Walk Tips:

  • Go left from exit 3
  • Gyeongui Line Forest Trail entrance + sign photo hot cut
  • Separated traffic lanes for bicycles and pedestrians

Time required: 15 minutes (5 minutes walking + 10 minutes store stop)

② Yeonnam-dong ② — Dongjin Market ~ Yeonnam Market (0.4 km)

Transit: Dongjin Market Alley + Yeonnamjang (Foreign Instagram Hotspot)

Store stop:

  • Dot Blanket Yeonnam Brunch Cafe (#220, signal 49) — Yeonnam Brunch Signature
  • Yeonnamjang — Yeonnam Instagram hotspot + old brewery regeneration cafe and dining

Walk Tips:

  • Detour available in 5 minutes on foot from Dongjin Market alley
  • Yeonnamjang is a seasonal cafe and event
  • Alley Instagram hot cuts

Time required: 30 minutes (10 minutes walking + 20 minutes store stop)

③ Yeonnam-Yeonhui border (0.3 km)

Main: Gyeongui Line Forest Trail main trail + bench + bicycle path

Gyeongui Line Forest Trail recommendations by season

Spring (March-May): No. 1 cherry blossom season — Next hotspot for cherry blossoms on Yunjung-ro in early to mid-April. Lots of cherry blossoms in Yeonnam-dong segment.

Summer (May-September): Sunset season 18-21 PM — Take an evening walk to avoid the midday heat. Store stop dinner combination.

Fall (September-November): No. 1 fall foliage season — Fall foliage + walking in mid-to-late October. Combined cafe seasonal menu.

Winter (December-February): Sunshine season 12-15 PM — A good time to avoid the cold and get some sunlight.

Gyeongui Line Forest Trail recommended course for foreigners (3 patterns)

[A] Full 1.5km course (4 hours, most common)

Start at Exit 3 of Hongik University Station → Yeonnam-dong ① ② → Boundary → Yeonhui-dong ① ② Finish

  • 4-5 store stops + walk + dinner

Hongik University Station → Yeonnam-dong ① Cafe → Border walk → Yeonnam finish

  • 2-3 cafes + a walk

  • Focus on hidden stores in Yeonhui-dong + take a walk
