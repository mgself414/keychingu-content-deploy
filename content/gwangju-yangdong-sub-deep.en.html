Gwangju Market Guide — Yangdong Market, Daein Market's Saturday Night Market, Namgwangju Market, Malbau Market Days, 1913 Songjeong Station Market

Subtitle: Yangdong Market (양동시장), 07:00–21:00, Gwangju's largest · Daein Market (대인시장), 08:00–20:00, Saturday Art Night Market · Namgwangju Market (남광주시장), seafood and raw fish restaurants · Malbau Market (말바우시장), 07:00–19:00, market days on the 2nd, 4th, 7th and 9th · 1913 Songjeong Station Market (1913송정역시장), 11:00–22:00, cards accepted — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Gwangju has five traditional markets, each with its own character. Yangdong Market is the largest, Daein Market is where artists and merchants share the space, Namgwangju Market is where seafood arrives before dawn, Malbau Market has its own market days, and 1913 Songjeong Station Market is in the alley across from KTX Gwangju-Songjeong Station (광주송정역).

This guide covers only these five markets, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Closed days differ from market to market, so check the date first. For the rest of a Gwangju trip, such as May 18 Democracy Plaza (5·18 민주광장), the National Asia Culture Center (국립아시아문화전당), and Mudeungsan (무등산), follow the Related Content below.

🐟 Yangdong Market — Gwangju's Largest Market

Yangdong Market at 238 Cheonbyeonjwa-ro, Seo-gu, Gwangju (광주 서구 천변좌로 238), ☎ 062-366-0884, is the largest traditional market in Gwangju. It is said to have started as a market held on the 2nd and 7th of each month on the sandy riverbank under Gwangju Bridge in the 1910s.

  • Opening hours: 07:00–21:00, varying by stall. It closes on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Sundays, though this also varies by stall.
  • What they sell: Agricultural and fishery products, ancestral rite goods, wedding goods, skate (홍어), and whole fried chicken. People still say that if you want to buy skate, you have to go to Yangdong Market.
  • What to try: The tourism organization names three Yangdong Market foods: Yangdong Tongdak (양동통닭), named together with Suil Tongdak (수일통닭), one-thousand-won noodles, and fried lettuce.
  • During May 18: During the May 18 Democratic Uprising in 1980, merchants of Yangdong Market and Daein Market gave rice balls, drinks, and medicine to the citizen army.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available.
  • Nearby: Yanglim Historic Cultural Village (양림역사문화마을), the National Asia Culture Center, and May 18 Memorial Park (5·18 기념공원).

🎨 Daein Market — A Market with Art, and the Saturday Night Market

Daein Market at 13-9 Jebong-ro 184beon-gil, Dong-gu, Gwangju (광주 동구 제봉로184번길 13-9), ☎ 062-233-3001, opened in May 1959 and covers 11,760㎡ in 6 zones. It was centered on wholesale fish and vegetables, and sits within the city of Gwangju.

  • Opening hours: 08:00–20:00, varying by stall. Closed days also vary by stall.
  • What they sell: Agricultural and fishery products, dried seafood, raw fish restaurants, and manufactured goods.
  • How it became an art market: In 2008, the Gwangju Biennale's "Real Estate Office Project" (복덕방 프로젝트) put artworks in empty stalls, and in 2013 it was selected as a culture and tourism market. Today it is a space where artists and merchants share the same place.
  • Saturday Art Night Market (토요예술야시장): Held every Saturday evening. According to the tourism organization, it has been confirmed to continue through 2028.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available.

🌊 Namgwangju Market — Dawn Seafood and Raw Fish Restaurants

Namgwangju Market at 18-6 Jebong-ro 17beon-gil, Dong-gu, Gwangju (광주 동구 제봉로17번길 18-6), ☎ 0507-1492-1101, grew up naturally on the site of the old Namgwangju Station.

  • Opening hours: Vary by stall. Closed on the 1st and 3rd Sundays of each month.
  • Seafood: Around 2:00 to 5:00 in the morning, seafood arrives from fish auctions at ports and harbors and goes out to other markets and restaurants in the city. It is also known as a place where many raw fish restaurants are gathered.
  • What they sell: Food, seafood, vegetables, fruit, dried seafood, dishes, and clothing.
  • Facilities: Restrooms are available, and parking holds about 140 small cars.

🐎 Malbau Market — Market Days on the 2nd, 4th, 7th and 9th

Malbau Market at 62 Dongmun-daero 85beon-gil, Buk-gu, Gwangju (광주 북구 동문대로85번길 62), ☎ 062-262-4082, is the leading traditional market of Gwangju's Buk-gu. Its name comes from a horse-hoofprint-shaped rock (malbau, 말바우). A story says that General Kim Deok-ryeong (김덕령), a righteous army leader of the mid-Joseon period, shot an arrow and then rode his horse faster than the arrow flew, and the horse's hoofprint was left on the rock. The rock no longer exists.

  • Opening hours: 07:00–19:00.
  • Market days: The 2nd, 4th, 7th and 9th.
  • What they sell: Farm products, seafood, livestock products, and clothing.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available. It was registered as a recognized market in 2005.

🚉 1913 Songjeong Station Market — The Food Alley in Front of Gwangju-Songjeong Station

1913 Songjeong Station Market at 13 Songjeong-ro 8beon-gil, Gwangsan-gu, Gwangju (광주 광산구 송정로8번길 13), ☎ 062-942-1914, is a daily market that has been alongside Songjeong Station since 1913. It is in the alley across from KTX Gwangju-Songjeong Station. The market has been renovated, and the opening date registered with the Korea Tourism Organization is April 18, 2016.

  • Opening hours: Mon–Thu 11:00–22:00, Fri–Sun 11:00–23:00. Closed on the 2nd and 4th Mondays of each month.
  • What to try: Hotteok (호떡), skewers, egg rice, fish cake, croquettes, and red ginseng yogurt. The noodle factory sells one-thousand-won banquet noodles.
  • Facilities: Cards accepted, restrooms available, and a public parking lot for about 48 cars.
  • For Korean set meals (hanjeongsik) around Songjeong Station, see #1030 Gwangju Hanjeongsik + Songjeong Station Market.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Visiting Yangdong Market or Namgwangju Market on a Sunday — Yangdong Market is closed on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Sundays, and Namgwangju Market on the 1st and 3rd Sundays.
  2. Visiting Songjeong Station Market on a Monday — 1913 Songjeong Station Market is closed on the 2nd and 4th Mondays of each month.
  3. Assuming the Daein Market night market runs every day — The Saturday Art Night Market is held every Saturday evening.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which market can I visit right after arriving in Gwangju by KTX?

A. 1913 Songjeong Station Market. It is in the alley across from Gwangju-Songjeong Station, and it is open 11:00–22:00 Mon–Thu and 11:00–23:00 Fri–Sun.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. According to the tourism organization, 1913 Songjeong Station Market accepts cards. No card information is registered for Yangdong Market, Daein Market, Namgwangju Market, or Malbau Market.
