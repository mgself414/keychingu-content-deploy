🎯 为什么要提供广津、建国大学、纛岛的独家导游？

广津、建国大学、纛岛位于广津区号。 1 foreign student area, MZ Youth, and Han River combined area — Konkuk University (opened in 1946, a prestigious Korean private university + 6,000 foreign students+) + Ttukseom Hangang Park + Children's Grand Park (opened in 1973, Korea's first urban zoo) + Dong Seoul Terminal (local departure hub) compressed into one area. 韩国东部排名第一的热点地区结合了外国学生、青少年和家庭的需求。

Compared to Hongdae (MZ, indie), Gangnam (luxury), and Itaewon (global), Gwangjin, Konkuk University, and Ttukseom are university, youth, and Han River. Although it is overlooked by Gangnam and Hongdae in the flow of foreign tourists, Konkuk University, Sejong University, Hanyang University foreign student belt + Ttukseom Hangang Park bicycle/Instagram + Children's大公园免费动物园是外国学生和家庭排名第一的深度潜水胜地。距离 2 号线和 7 号线建国大学站 + 汉江自行车道上的江南和弘大不到 30 分钟路程。

In particular, Konk University Western/Italian Cluster (12 cases including Macho Chef, Rony Loti, New Wave Seoul) is foreigner-friendly + MZ Instagram hot zone. 纛岛汉江公园是汉江深度潜水、自行车、游艇和烧烤的第一场所。继#138弘大、#169汝矣岛、#170大学路、#171新村、#172DDP、#173瑞草、#174首尔大学、#175麻谷、ISSUE 03 ⑰ — 重要朋友首次进入广津区（使用74个集群）。

⚠ 外国人常犯的3个错误

1. "Konkuk University = I passed because it's a university neighborhood" — No. 1 foreign student value unknown

国外旅游路线中最容易犯的错误。 Konkuk University is recognized as a “university town” and is passed over, but in reality, 6,000 foreign students at Konkuk University + (the number one private university for foreigners from China, Southeast Asia, and Russia) + international student belt at Sejong University and Hanyang University = the area with the most diverse demographics of foreigners in Korea. Konk University Western/Italian 12 Cluster 适合外国人+标准英文菜单。 The #1 deep dive for foreigners studying abroad in Korea.

2. "Ttukseom Han River = Just the Han River" — Bicycle, yacht, barbecue unknown

Ttukseom Hangang Park is not just the Han River, but Hangang Bicycle Road No. 1 + Han River Yacht/SUP Rental + Han River Barbecue (designated area) + Han River Night View all in one place. Different from Yeouido Hangang Park (fountain, cherry blossoms) — Activities, MZ, and family No. 1. 5-minute walk from Ttukseom Garden Station on Line 7.

3. "Children's Grand Park = Just for kids" — Korea's No. 1 free zoo, Miji**

Children's Grand Park is Korea's first urban zoo, amusement park, and botanical garden, opened in 1973 under the direction of President Park Chung-hee — 560,000 pyeong. Free for foreign families + Free zoo + Free botanical garden.韩国第一座城市综合公园。有外国孩子的家庭排名第一。 5号线儿童大公园站。

Scenery of Gwangjin, Konkuk University, and Ttukseom

Gwangjin, Konkuk University, and Ttukseom are located in the center of Gwangjin-gu area combining foreign students + youth + the Han River — with the opening of Konkuk University in 1946 + the opening of Children's Grand Park in 1973 + the creation of Ttukseom Hangang Park, it has established itself as the No. 1 hot zone in eastern Korea.

Konkuk University/Konkuk University Station Sub-area is near Konkuk University Station on Lines 2 and 7 — Konkuk University campus + 6,000 foreign students + + 50+ foreign-friendly Western, Italian, and Japanese restaurants. The #1 deep dive for foreigners studying abroad in Korea.

Ttukseom·Guui·Jayang sub-area is the Ttukseom Garden area, Guui Station, and Jayang Station on Line 7 — Ttukseom Hangang Park + East Seoul General Terminal (local departure hub) + Han River bicycles and yachts.

Children's Grand Park sub-area is directly connected to Children's Grand Park Station on Line 5 — Free admission to Korea's No. 1 urban zoo, amusement park, and botanical garden.

Achasan sub-area is a 10-minute walk from Gwangnaru Station and Achasan Station on Line 5 — Hanyangdoseong, Goguryeo fortress ruins, and Han River view are the top hiking destinations.

Transportation: Line 2, Line 7 Konkuk University Station (Kon National University) / Line 7 Ttukseom Park Area (Ttukseom Hangang Park) / Line 5 Children's Grand Park Station / Line 7 Guui Station·Jayang Station.推荐Kakao出租车。自行车 Ttareungi 好。

⚙ 常见问题解答 (8)

Q1。 Foreigners admitted to Konkuk University campus?

免费入场 — 从正门（建国大学站）2号出口步行5分钟。 Ilgam Lake (campus central pond) + KU Zoo + KU Museum/Art Gallery Free. 建国大学6,000名外国学生+ = 校园里经常见到外国人。

第二季度。如何在纛岛汉江公园租用自行车或游艇？

Bike Ttareungi (Seoul City public bicycle, 1,000 won per hour) + Hangang Yacht/SUP rental (within Ttukseom Hangang Park, 09:00~18:00, 30,000~50,000 won/hour) + Hangang Bicycle Path (Ttukseom → Jamsil → Gwangnaru).国外信用卡注册OK。

第三季度。儿童大公园门票？

免费 — 对外国人和韩国人免费。 Line 5, Children’s Grand Park Station, Exit 1. Free admission to zoos, amusement parks, and botanical gardens. 不。 1 priority for traveling with foreign children.

第四季度。 What are Konkuk University’s foreign-friendly stores?

Macho Chef, Sato Gyu Katsu, New Wave Seoul, Yobi, and Minji Super have English menu + card payment + foreigner-friendly standard.建国大学的外国学生人口结构多种多样，因此一些菜单有日语和中文版本。

Q5。 Is Ttukseom Han River barbecue available?

可以 — 可以在纛岛汉江公园的指定烧烤区使用。需要提前预约（首尔市汉江公园网站英文UI可以）。 Zone 1, approximately 30,000 won/4 people. 1st season from May to October.

Q6。 Is it possible for foreigners to climb Mt. Acha?

OK — 287m light trek (1-2 hours).适合外国家庭和登山初学者。 Top Han River view + #1 photo of Goguryeo fortress ruins. Line 5 Gwangnaru Station/Achasan Station.

Q7。从 东首尔客运站 去哪里？

江原道（春川、江陵、束草）/忠北（清州、忠州、丹阳）/庆北（安东、庆州部分地区）/全北（全州、群山、益山）。外国人游览韩国的第一起点。 English ticket OK + KOBUS English UI.

Q8。 Gwangjin, Konkuk University + Han River + Gangnam route?

OK — 建国大学站（2号线、7号线）→江南（换乘2号线，30分钟）/蚕室（2号线，4站）/狎鸥亭（2号线、3号线）/纛岛汉江→汉江自行车（距蚕室10公里）。 1 full day.
