Sokcho + Seoraksan Sogongwon — Bus from Seoul, 3 Paths to Choose from Sogongwon, the Gwongeumseong Cable Car, and 5 Places in Sokcho City

Subtitle: This guide is for travelers who take an express bus from Seoul to Sokcho and visit Seoraksan Sogongwon. The values come from searching the 2026-11-26 departures on the Express Bus booking website. As written on the booking site, "Seoul Gyeongbu" to Sokcho takes 2 hours 20 minutes, and a premium (udeung) ticket costs 24,300 won. In Sokcho, take city bus 7 or 7-1 and get off at "Seoraksan Jucha-jang (Jongjeom)". The city bus fare is 1,700 won for an adult. The paths you can choose from Sogongwon are three: Biseondae (3.0 km, about 1 hour 20 minutes one way), Ulsanbawi Rock (3.8 km, 2 hours one way) and Towangseong Falls Observatory (2.8 km, 1 hour 30 minutes one way). The Gwongeumseong Cable Car costs 16,000 won round trip, and tickets are sold only on site on the day. The cable car posts its operating hours for the day every day and stops when the wind is strong. The autumn forest fire watch period for Seoraksan runs from 2026-11-15 to 12-15. The 5 places to see in Sokcho city are given as Kakao Map registered values. The values were read on 2026-11-25.

ISSUE 06 · #1038 · 2026-11-25 · Sokcho·Seoraksan

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why This Order

This guide is made for foreign travelers who want to take a bus from Seoul to Sokcho and walk in Seoraksan (설악산) for a day. It is written so that you can decide on the bus, the path and the cable car before you leave, even if you do not know Korean.

This guide is written in the order you travel.

  • From Seoul to Sokcho: express bus.
  • From Sokcho to Sogongwon: city bus 7 or 7-1.
  • At Sogongwon (소공원): choose one of 3 walking paths. The Gwongeumseong Cable Car (권금성 케이블카) also starts from Sogongwon.
  • In Sokcho city: 5 places — a market, a lighthouse, a ferry dock, a Ferris wheel and a museum.

Sogongwon is the entrance to Seoraksan on the Sokcho (속초) side. The address of the Sogongwon entrance is 1091 Seoraksan-ro (설악산로), Sokcho-si, Gangwon-do (강원도). The 3 walking paths and the cable car all start here. So this guide covers only Sogongwon, which you reach from Sokcho with one bus.

This guide does not cover restaurants, cafes or places to stay. Sokcho food is in #1408 under Related below.

② From Sokcho to Sogongwon — City Bus 7 or 7-1

  • The bus to take: bus 7 or 7-1. According to the official city bus guide of Sokcho City Hall (read 2026-11-25), both buses start from Jangsa-dong (장사동) and end at Seoraksan. Bus 7 goes via Yeongnang-dong (영랑동). Bus 7-1 goes via the Medical Center (의료원).
  • Where to board and get off: according to the guide on the official Seorak Cable Car website (read 2026-11-25), take bus 7 or 7-1 from Sokcho Intercity Bus Terminal. The bus passes the Express Bus Terminal, Seoraksan Entrance (설악산 입구), Seoraksan National Park Visitor Center (설악산 국립공원 탐방안내소) and Kensington Hotel (켄싱턴 호텔), and stops at "Seoraksan Jucha-jang (Jongjeom) (설악산 주차장(종점))".
  • Stop name: the stop where you get off is not named "Sogongwon". It is "Seoraksan Jucha-jang (Jongjeom)". It is the last stop, so ride to the end.
  • Fare: according to the official guide of Sokcho City Hall, it is 1,700 won for an adult, 1,360 won for middle and high school students and 850 won for elementary school students.
  • Transfer: if you transfer to another city bus within 1 hour 30 minutes, one transfer is free. You must tap your transit card on the reader when you get off. Taking the same route again, and routes 1, 1-1, 9 and 9-1, are excluded from the free transfer.
  • Cable car boarding point: it is a 5 minute walk from the Sogongwon entrance.

③ 3 Paths to Choose from Sogongwon

All three paths start from the Sogongwon entrance (1091 Seoraksan-ro, Sokcho-si, Gangwon-do). The distances and times are the values of the official course guide of Korea National Park Service (read 2026-11-25). All times are one way. Add the time to come back separately.

Path Distance Time one way Difficulty Shape of the path
① Sogongwon–Biseondae (비선대) About 3.0 km About 1 hour 20 minutes The Park Service writes that children and the elderly can also walk it A gentle valley path with slightly sloped stone steps
② Sogongwon–Ulsanbawi Rock (울산바위) 3.8 km 2 hours Medium No strain up to Heundeulbawi Rock (흔들바위), and the 1.0 km after it is steep stairs
③ Sogongwon–Towangseong Falls Observatory (토왕성폭포전망대) 2.8 km 1 hour 30 minutes Easy The first about 1.2 km is also used by strollers and wheelchairs, and the last 0.4 km is steeply sloped deck stairs

Path ① Sogongwon–Biseondae

  • Distance and time: about 3.0 km, about 1 hour 20 minutes one way. These are the values of the Park Service course guide.
  • Shape of the path: it is a gentle valley path. There are slightly sloped stone steps.
  • For whom: the Park Service writes that children and the elderly can also walk it.
  • Forest fire watch period: the Park Service writes that this path can be visited in the forest fire watch period too.
  • To go further — Geumganggul Cave (금강굴): the path from Biseondae to Geumganggul Cave can be entered only 04:00–14:00 in the winter season (November–March, months 11–3). You return by the way you came.

Path ② Sogongwon–Ulsanbawi Rock

  • Distance and time: 3.8 km, 2 hours one way. Difficulty is "medium".
  • First section: Seoraksan-dong Visitor Support Center (설악동탐방지원센터) to Heundeulbawi Rock (Gyejoam (계조암)) is 2.8 km, 1 hour. The Park Service writes that this section is not a strain for children and the elderly.
  • Second section: Heundeulbawi Rock to Ulsanbawi Rock is 1.0 km, 1 hour. It is steep stairs.
  • For a shorter walk: turn back at Heundeulbawi Rock. This is this guide's own advice.

Path ③ Sogongwon–Towangseong Falls Observatory

  • Park Service course name: it is "Biryong Falls (Towang Falls Observatory)" (비룡폭포(토왕폭전망대)). From Sogongwon you go past Yukdam Falls (육담폭포) and Biryong Falls (비룡폭포) to Towangseong Falls Observatory.
  • Distance and time: 2.8 km, 1 hour 30 minutes one way. Difficulty is "easy".
  • First section: Sogongwon to Biryong Falls is 2.4 km, 1 hour. The Park Service writes that about 1.2 km from Sogongwon can also be used by strollers and wheelchairs.
  • Second section: Biryong Falls to the observatory is 0.4 km, 30 minutes. It is deck stairs and steeply sloped.
  • For a shorter walk: turn back at Biryong Falls. This is this guide's own advice.

Rules That Apply to All Three Paths

  • Entry restriction: according to the official designated entry-hours system table of Korea National Park Service (winter season, months 11–3, read 2026-11-25), the paths between Sogongwon and Ulsanbawi Rock, Biseondae, Gwongeumseong (권금성) and Towangseong Falls Observatory are restricted "from after sunset until 2 hours before sunrise the next day". Plan to be back at Sogongwon before the sun sets.
  • Entry register: in the winter season, people who enter Seoraksan after 12:00 write their names in an entry register.
  • Forest fire watch period: the Park Service's Seoraksan closure map (read 2026-11-25) gives the closure period as 2026-11-15 to 12-15. In this period Seoraksan closes its high-ground trails.
  • Paths open in the forest fire watch period: Sogongwon–Biseondae is walked in this period too. The path to Ulsanbawi Rock and the path to Towangseong Falls Observatory are marked as open sections on the Park Service closure map (read 2026-11-25).
  • On the morning you go: open Seoraksan on the screen "Safe Visit (안전한 탐방) > Trail Closure Information (통제정보)" on the Park Service website. If the closure changes, a notice is posted on this screen.
  • Inquiries: Seoraksan National Park Office (설악산국립공원사무소) 033-801-0900.

④ Gwongeumseong Cable Car

The values are from the official Seorak Cable Car website (read 2026-11-25).

  • Fare: only round trips are sold. There is no one-way ticket. Adult (middle school students and older) is 16,000 won. Under 36 months is free. Child 12,000 won covers those above that age up to elementary school students.
  • Discounts: there is no group discount. The senior fare (age 65 and older, 14,000 won) is written on the website as applying only to "Korean nationals themselves".
  • How to buy a ticket: reservations are not taken. Tickets are sold only on site on the day.
  • Operating hours: there is no fixed timetable. The first page of the website posts the operating hours for the day every day.
  • Interval between rides: 5–15 minutes. It is every 5 minutes when there are many guests, and every 10–15 minutes when there are not.
  • Strong wind: operation stops when the wind is strong. If it stops because of strong wind or bad weather, unused tickets are fully refunded.
  • Refunds: up to 10 minutes before boarding, the refund is full. After that, a refund or a change of time returns the fare minus 20%.
  • Boarding point: it is a 5 minute walk from the Sogongwon entrance. The address, as a Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25), is 1085 Seoraksan-ro, Sokcho-si, Gangwon Special Self-Governing Province.
  • On the morning you go: open the website first, before you take the bus in Sokcho. This is this guide's own advice.
  • Inquiries: Seorak Cable Car 033-636-4300.

⑥ What Changes in Winter

  • Range of the winter season: the entry-hours table for Seoraksan of Korea National Park Service gives the winter season as November to March of the next year.
  • Path to Geumganggul Cave: the path from Biseondae to Geumganggul Cave is 04:00–14:00 in the winter season. In the summer season it is 03:00–14:00. The closing time is the same, 14:00.
  • The sun sets early: on the three paths from Sogongwon, entry is restricted from after sunset until 2 hours before sunrise the next day. Ulsanbawi Rock is 2 hours one way. Set out from Sogongwon in the morning. This is this guide's own advice.
  • Entry register: in the winter season, people who enter Seoraksan after 12:00 write their names in an entry register.
  • Until December 15: it is the forest fire watch period, so high-ground trails are closed. Sogongwon–Biseondae is walked.
  • Weather warnings: the Park Service closure information seen on 2026-11-25 said that Geumganggul Cave and Daeseung Falls (대승폭포) can be visited until 14:00. It also said that they are closed when a weather warning is issued.
  • Cable car: it stops in strong wind.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

  1. Looking for a "Sogongwon" stop on the bus. The stop where buses 7 and 7-1 stop is named "Seoraksan Jucha-jang (Jongjeom)". Ride to the last stop.
  2. Thinking Sokcho's two terminals are the same place. The Express Bus Terminal is at 3988 Donghae-daero (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25) and the Intercity Bus Terminal is at 16 Jangan-ro. They are two different places.
  3. Thinking the Gwongeumseong Cable Car has a fixed timetable. The operating hours are posted on the website every day. It stops when strong wind blows. Tickets are sold only on site on the day.
  4. Trying to buy a one-way cable car ticket. Only round trips are sold. Adult 16,000 won, child 12,000 won.
  5. Setting out late in the afternoon for Geumganggul Cave or Ulsanbawi Rock. The path to Geumganggul Cave closes at 14:00. Ulsanbawi Rock is 2 hours one way, and entry is restricted after sunset.

⚙ 5 FAQs

  1. Which of the three paths is easy? — The Park Service lists two as easy. Sogongwon–Biseondae (3.0 km, about 1 hour 20 minutes one way) is written as walkable by children and the elderly. Sogongwon–Towangseong Falls Observatory (2.8 km, 1 hour 30 minutes one way) is difficulty "easy". But the last 0.4 km of the Towangseong Falls Observatory path is steeply sloped deck stairs. Sogongwon–Ulsanbawi Rock (3.8 km, 2 hours one way) is difficulty "medium".
  2. Is there anything I need to reserve? — The three paths in this guide are not in the list of the trail reservation system seen on 2026-11-25. The Gwongeumseong Cable Car does not take reservations and sells tickets on site on the day. For the express bus timetable, search on the Express Bus booking website with the date.
  3. Is there a path I can use with a stroller or a wheelchair? — The Park Service writes that on the Towangseong Falls Observatory path, about 1.2 km from Sogongwon can also be used by strollers and wheelchairs. The 0.4 km from Biryong Falls to the observatory is steeply sloped deck stairs.
  4. What changes until December 15? — It is the autumn forest fire watch period (2026-11-15 to 12-15), so Seoraksan closes its high-ground trails. Sogongwon–Biseondae is walked in this period too. The paths to Ulsanbawi Rock and Towangseong Falls Observatory are marked as open sections on the Park Service closure map (read 2026-11-25).
  5. What happens to my ticket if the cable car stops? — If operation stops because of strong wind or bad weather, unused tickets are fully refunded. Up to 10 minutes before boarding, the refund is also full. On a day the cable car is stopped, walk the Sogongwon–Biseondae path. This is this guide's own advice.
