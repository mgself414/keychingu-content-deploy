3. "Pet emergency = No English": English OK = Gangnam 24-hour pet hospital + Foreign medical tourism (#401) + #405 Emergency + No. 1 foreign mania.

4. "Pet day-trip = No": Pet day-trip = Gapyeong·Yangpyeong·Paju·Jeju·Gangwon + #430·#439 rental car + cafe/restaurant with pets + Foreign family mania.

5. "Pet plane = expensive": Airplane = Cage in-cabin (5kg or less) vs cargo (5kg+) + Policies by airline (English OK for Korean Air·Asiana foreigners) + Full set of quarantine documents.
