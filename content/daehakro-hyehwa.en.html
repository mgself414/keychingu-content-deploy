🎯 Why is it an exclusive guide to Daehakro and Hyehwa?

Daehakro and Hyehwa are on the east side of Jongno-gu and near Seongbuk-gu Korea's No. 1 youth and culture hub — they are hidden by Myeong-dong and Insa-dong in the flow of foreign tourists, but Hakrim, Korea's first cafe in 1956, Daehakro Performance Hall Street, Ewha Mural Village, Marronnier Park, areas where modern Korean cultural history is compressed.

Compared to Hongdae (commercial, MZ), Itaewon (global), and Gangnam (luxury), Daehakro and Hyehwa are youth, indie, and traditional. First choice when foreign tourists want to experience "Korean indie culture + authentic Korean intellectuals of the 1950s and 1960s" during a 4-5 day trip to Seoul. Sungkyunkwan (the highest educational institution during the Joseon Dynasty) and Sungkyunkwan University are also nearby — Experience Korean academic and cultural traditions simultaneously.

In particular, Hakrim (founded in 1956) is Korea's first authentic cafe and a hangout for Korean intellectuals such as Park Kyung-ni, Lee Ho-cheol, and Kim Ji-ha. The #1 store for foreigners to deep dive into Korean culture. Following #138 Hongdae, #142 Gangnam, #144 Itaewon, #156 Yeonhui-dong, and #169 Yeouido, join ISSUE 03 ⑪ section — Key Friend's first entry into Jongno-gu East and Seongbuk-gu.

⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Hongdae and Gangnam are enough" — Daehakro and Hyehwa Pass

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. Daehakro and Hyehwa are within a 15-minute walk from Insa-dong, Jongno or 1 stop from Hyehwa Station on Line 4, but they are recognized as separate neighborhoods and passed. In reality, Korean youth and culture (indie, orthodox, intellectuals) are differentiated from Hongdae and Gangnam. A combined “tourism + cultural deep dive” course is possible by combining it into one route. Hakrim + Horse Chestnut Park + Ewha Mural Village set is the No. 1 Korean cultural experience for foreigners.

2. "Hakrim is just a cafe" — 1956 Korea's first cafe value unknown

Founded in 1956, Haklim is Korea's first authentic cafe — a hangout for Korean intellectuals such as Park Kyung-ni, Lee Ho-cheol, and Kim Ji-ha. A symbolic space of change in Korean society during the Park Chung-hee and Chun Doo-hwan eras. Foreigners pass by simply seeing it as a “cafe” — the actual value is No. 1 Hyundai Jisungsa in Korea. The price is the same as a regular cafe (around 8,000 won), and the signature Vienna coffee is recommended.

3. "Ihwa Mural Village is a well-known Instagram hotspot" — Dongseon Migoryeo

Ewha Mural Village has gradually become known to foreigners, but it is not tied to Daehakro and Hyehwa in the same route — it is actually a 10-minute walk from Hyehwa Station and on the same route as Naksan Park and Sungkyunkwan. Daehakro Lunch + Hakrim Cafe + Ewha Mural Village + Naksan Park Full-day course is the best. Also, since this is a local resident area, quietness and photography manners are required.

Scenery of Daehakro and Hyehwa

Daehakro and Hyehwa are located on the east side of Jongno-gu and near Seongbuk-gu. Korea's No. 1 youth, indie, and culture district — Seoul National University's College of Liberal Arts (now Sungkyunkwan University) in 1946, and after the relocation of the Dongsung-dong headquarters in 1953, it became the birthplace of Korean university culture. Currently, the area is densely packed with Korean indie musicals, small theaters, youth cafes, and culinary bars.

Daehakro Main Street is located at Exit 1~2 of Hyehwa Station on Line 4 → Marronnier Park → Dongsungro Line. The entire walk is possible in 30 minutes. The main street of Daehakro.

Dongsung-dong·Myeongnyun-dong The sub-area is an alley of cafes, restaurants, and small theaters. 50+ indie performance venues crowded together. Korea's No. 1 musical and theater youth destination.

Naksan Park·Ewha Mural Village is a 10-15 minute walk from Hyehwa Station — a hot spot for foreigners on Instagram. Hanyangdoseong Naksan Course (Seoul Hanyang Castle Trekking).

Sungkyunkwan·Sungkyunkwan University is a 5-minute walk from Hyehwa Station on Line 4 — the highest educational institution of the Joseon Dynasty. Experience Korean academic traditions.

Transportation: Directly connected to Hyehwa Station on Line 4 (Exits 1 and 2). Jongno 3-ga, Jongno 5-ga, Insa-dong 15 minutes on foot. Kakao Taxi recommended. Bicycle Ttareungi OK.

⚙ FAQ (8)

Q1. Is Hakrim really Korea’s first cafe?

Established in 1956 — The first café to combine authentic “coffee + music + culture” outside of Korean coffee shops and salons. A gathering place for Korean intellectuals such as Park Kyung-ni, Lee Ho-cheol, and Kim Ji-ha. The number one place in Korean intellectual history during the Park Chung-hee and Chun Doo-hwan eras. Foreigners rank first when it comes to deep diving into Korean cafe culture.

Q2. Can foreigners see the Daehakro performance?

Continuous performance of Korean musicals and plays in 50+ small theaters. Although it is mainly in Korean, indie musical, jazz, and dance performances can be experienced non-verbally. Arko Arts Theater and Dongsung Art Center are the main performance venues. Interpark ticket English UI OK.

Q3. What are the Ehwa Mural Village times?

10-12:00 AM + 14-16:00 PM is best — avoid foreign Instagram crowds + natural light. This is a local resident area, so quietness and photography manners are required. Some alleys have signs prohibiting photography.

Q4. What are the foreign-friendly stores in Daehakro and Hyehwa?

Kobe Beef Katsu, Oreno Katsu, and New York Night Market English menu + card payment standard. Haklim focuses on authentic Korean, but menu photos and barista friendly are OK.

Q5. Sungkyunkwan entrance fee and time?

Free admission / 09:00~18:00. English guide for foreigners OK (advance reservation required). Myeongryundang and Daeseongjeon are ranked 1st in traditional Korean architecture.

Q6. How to get to Naksan Park’s night view?

Hyehwa Station Exit 1 → 10-minute walk to Naksan Park → Hanyangdoseong Naksan Course → Night view of Seoul. Sunset between 17 and 7 p.m. + Best night view. free. Hanyangdoseong Trekking 1.5km.

Q7. Is it possible to bundle Daehakro + Insa-dong + Jongno?

OK — Daehakro (Hyehwa Station) → Jongno 5-ga (15 minutes walk or 1 stop on Line 4) → Insadong (2 subway stops or 20 minutes walk). One full day course available.

Q8. Is Daehakro bar safe at night?

Korea’s No. 1 youth area — 24-hour safety. Korean plainclothes police patrol + CCTV. Foreigner Night Hop Foreigner Friendly (New York Night Market).
