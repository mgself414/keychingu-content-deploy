⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Thinking Hongdae (홍대) = clubs only — ignoring the bar scene

Many visitors associate Hongdae (홍대) mainly with clubs like Rolling Hall (롤링홀) and Club FF (클럽 FF). In reality, 18+ bars in the Hongdae (홍대) area have ★5.0 ratings from English-language reviews. When you want to sit, drink, and talk, bars are the answer.

2. Ordering only beer — missing Korean traditional liquor

Makgeolli (막걸리, milky rice wine), cheongju (청주, clear rice wine), and soju (소주, distilled spirit) are the heart of Korean drinking culture. Slow Village Brewery (느린마을 양조장) and Chaek Bar (책바) are ideal entry points. Makgeolli from ₩6,000–12,000 / cheongju (청주) from ₩15,000.

3. Avoiding solo drinking because it feels awkward

Solo drinking — known as honsul (혼술) — is completely normal among Korean MZ culture. Solo-drink specialist bars like Naejan (내잔), Honsulbar Godo (혼술바 고도), and Blending Bar (블렌딩바) offer single-seat layouts and single-portion menus. Perfect for solo travelers.

The Hongdae (홍대) Drinking Scene

Hongdae (홍대), Yeonnam (연남), Hapjeong (합정), and Mangwon (망원) have a rich bar culture beyond clubs — a completely separate track from live music at Rolling Hall (롤링홀) or DJ sets at Club FF (클럽 FF). Since the late 2020s, third-wave bars (craft beer, traditional liquor, signature cocktails) have concentrated in Hapjeong (합정) and Yeonnam (연남).

Four visitor intents:

1. Rooftop & View — One drink with a city skyline (Floating at L7, The View (더뷰))

2. Korean Traditional Liquor — Makgeolli (막걸리), cheongju (청주), soju (소주) (Slow Village Brewery (느린마을 양조장), Chaek Bar (책바))

3. Familiar Pub & Beer — Craft beer, pizza (Ale Crew (에일크루), Blending Bar (블렌딩바))

4. Solo-Drink Friendly — Single seats, single-portion menus (Naejan (내잔), Honsulbar Godo (혼술바 고도))

① Rooftop & View (2 Venues)

Floating at L7 Hongdae (L7 홍대 루프탑바 플로팅)

The rooftop bar of L7 Hongdae Hotel (롯데 호텔 그룹, Lotte Hotel Group) — drinks with a full city night view. 4-star hotel facilities with full English service and menus, making it one of the most foreigner-friendly options in the area. Drinks from ₩15,000–25,000.

  • Address: Yangwha-ro (양화로) 141, Mapo-gu (마포구), rooftop of L7 Hongdae Hotel — Hongdae (홍대)
  • Hours: Daily 18:00–01:00 (seasonal variation)
  • Signature: Hotel rooftop + city night view
  • Why visit: Best first bar stop in Hongdae (홍대) for foreign visitors. English service + 4-star hotel standards.

The View (더뷰)

A bar in the Hongdae (홍대) area with panoramic Han River (한강) and Seoul skyline views. Floor-to-ceiling windows with prime window seating. Menu includes wine, cocktails, and light food. Multiple ★5.0 English reviews.

  • Address: Mapo-gu (마포구) area — Hongdae (홍대)
  • Hours: Daily 17:00–01:00 (venue-dependent)
  • Signature: Han River (한강) & city view + floor-to-ceiling windows
  • Why visit: Best non-rooftop view bar. Great for dates and small groups.

② Traditional Liquor & Signature (2 Venues)

Slow Village Brewery Hongdae (느린마을 양조장 홍대점)

A brewery-direct bar specializing in Korean makgeolli (막걸리, milky rice wine). Fresh makgeolli (막걸리), cheongju (청주, clear rice wine), and seasonal fruit makgeolli (막걸리) supplied daily from the brewery. The safest and most approachable spot for foreign visitors to start their Korean traditional liquor journey. English menu available.

  • Address: Eoulmadang-ro (어울마당로) area, Mapo-gu (마포구) — Hongdae (홍대)
  • Hours: Daily 17:00–01:00 (seasonal variation)
  • Menu: Makgeolli (막걸리) ₩6,000–12,000 per bottle / Cheongju (청주) from ₩15,000
  • Signature: Brewery-direct + seasonal limited makgeolli (막걸리)
  • Why visit: Top pick for traditional Korean liquor introduction. Affordable for families and couples.

Chaek Bar (책바)

A books + drinks signature concept bar — walls lined with books, curated selection of whisky, wine, and Korean traditional spirits. Part English pub, part Korean bookshop. A favorite for foreign visitors who love Instagram-worthy interiors and a quiet atmosphere.

  • Address: Wausan-ro (와우산로) area, Mapo-gu (마포구) — Hongdae (홍대)
  • Hours: Daily 19:00–02:00 (closed Mondays)
  • Menu: Whisky ₩10,000–20,000 per glass / Wine from ₩9,000 / Korean traditional spirits
  • Signature: Books + whisky & wine concept + curated selection
  • Why visit: Quiet atmosphere + introduction to both Korean and international spirits. Best spot for photos.

③ Foreigner Pub & Beer (2 Venues)

Ale Crew Pizza Pub Hongdae (에일크루 피자펍 홍대점)

A craft beer + NY-style pizza pub in Hongdae (홍대). Multiple English-speaking staff, English menu, ★5.0 rating with 5+ English reviews. Best for groups and the most foreigner-friendly venue in this guide.

  • Address: Wausan-ro (와우산로) area, Mapo-gu (마포구) — Hongdae (홍대)
  • Hours: Daily 17:00–02:00
  • Menu: Craft beer ₩7,000–10,000 per glass / Pizza ₩18,000–25,000
  • Signature: Craft beer + NY-style pizza
  • Why visit: Best for foreign groups. Highest English accessibility in this guide.

Blending Bar Yeonnam (블렌딩바 서울혼술바 연남점)

A solo-friendly cocktail bar in Yeonnam-dong (연남동). ★5.0 from foreign reviewers. Ample single seats and single-portion menu options, plus a curated signature cocktail list. Ideal for solo foreign travelers.

  • Address: Yeonnam-dong (연남동) area, Mapo-gu (마포구) — Yeonnam (연남)
  • Hours: Daily 18:00–02:00 (closed Mondays)
  • Menu: Cocktails ₩12,000–18,000
  • Signature: Solo-friendly + signature cocktail curation
  • Why visit: Great on a Yeonnam (연남) route + solo drink introduction + foreigner-friendly.

④ Solo Drink & Trendy (2 Venues)

Naejan Solo-Drink Bar Mangwon (내잔 서울혼술바 망원점)

A dedicated honsul (혼술) bar in Mangwon (망원) — the entire concept is built around single seats and single-portion menus. Best choice for solo foreign travelers. English-speaking staff, quiet atmosphere.

  • Address: Mangwon-dong (망원동) area, Mapo-gu (마포구) — Mangwon (망원)
  • Hours: Daily 18:00–01:00 (seasonal variation)
  • Menu: Whisky & soju (소주) cocktails ₩8,000–15,000
  • Signature: Single-seat layout, dedicated honsul (혼술) concept
  • Why visit: Perfect wrap-up stop after Mangwon Market (망원시장). Solo traveler's go-to.

Honsulbar Godo Yeonnam (혼술바 고도 연남점)

An MZ-focused honsul (혼술) micro-bar in Yeonnam-dong (연남동). Rated ★4.6. Small space with curated music and a low-key menu. Great for solo travelers and MZ-generation visitors.

  • Address: Yeonnam-dong (연남동) area, Mapo-gu (마포구) — Yeonnam (연남)
  • Hours: Daily 19:00–02:00
  • Menu: Cocktails ₩10,000–15,000 / Whisky from ₩9,000 per glass
  • Signature: MZ honsul (혼술) vibe + curated music
  • Why visit: A different solo-drink tone from Blending Bar (블렌딩바). Intimate small-venue atmosphere.

⚙ FAQ — 8 Questions

Q1. Best first-night bar itinerary in Hongdae (홍대) for foreign visitors?

Start at 19:00 → Floating at L7 (L7 루프탑바 플로팅) (1 hr, rooftop cocktail) → walk to Slow Village Brewery (느린마을 양조장) (1 hr, makgeolli (막걸리) intro) → Ale Crew Pizza Pub (에일크루 피자펍) (1.5 hrs, dinner + beer).

Q2. Best place to start with Korean traditional liquor?

Slow Village Brewery (느린마을 양조장) is #1 (brewery-direct, authentic makgeolli (막걸리)) → Chaek Bar (책바) for curated variety. Start with makgeolli (막걸리), then explore cheongju (청주) and soju (소주).

Q3. Which venues have English menus and service?

Floating at L7 (L7 루프탑바 플로팅) / Ale Crew (에일크루) / Blending Bar (블렌딩바) / Chaek Bar (책바) — high English accessibility. Slow Village Brewery (느린마을 양조장) / Naejan (내잔) / Honsulbar Godo (혼술바 고도) — English menus available but staff English is limited.

Q4. Best solo honsul (혼술) spots for foreign visitors?

Naejan Mangwon (내잔 망원) (dedicated single seats) → Blending Bar Yeonnam (블렌딩바 연남) (solo + cocktails) → Honsulbar Godo (혼술바 고도) (MZ atmosphere). All are safe for solo travelers.

Q5. What are typical price ranges?

Hotel rooftop: ₩15,000–25,000 / Cocktails: ₩10,000–18,000 / Makgeolli (막걸리): ₩6,000–12,000 per bottle / Craft beer: ₩7,000–10,000 per glass. Average per-person spend: ₩30,000–50,000.

Q6. Best indoor options for rainy days?

Chaek Bar (책바) (cozy books + drinks) → Slow Village Brewery (느린마을 양조장) (brewery atmosphere) → Ale Crew Pizza Pub (에일크루 피자펍) (group-friendly). Avoid rooftops on rainy days.

Q7. Age restrictions and ID requirements?

Legal drinking age in Korea is 19 years or older. Foreign visitors must carry their passport at all times. Entry will be refused without valid ID.

Q8. Wheelchair and accessibility?

Floating at L7 (L7 루프탑바 플로팅) and Ale Crew (에일크루) — hotel/standard venue level access. Slow Village Brewery (느린마을 양조장), Chaek Bar (책바), Naejan (내잔), and Honsulbar Godo (혼술바 고도) involve narrow alleys or stairs and may be difficult to access.

🌸 5 Seasonal & Timing Tips

1. Summer (June–August) Rooftop Season: Floating at L7 (L7 루프탑바 플로팅) and The View (더뷰) are at their peak. Night views + cocktails on a warm evening.

2. Autumn (September–November) Makgeolli (막걸리) Season: Slow Village Brewery (느린마을 양조장) releases autumn limited editions; pair with wine at Chaek Bar (책바).

3. Winter (December–February) Warm Indoor Pubs: Ale Crew Pizza Pub (에일크루 피자펍) and Naejan (내잔) for warm cocktails inside.

4. Friday & Saturday Peak Hours (22:00–02:00): Floating at L7 (L7 루프탑바), Blending Bar (블렌딩바), and Honsulbar Godo (혼술바 고도) get crowded. Aim for weekdays or 19:00–21:00 for a quieter experience.

5. MZ Trending Spots (monthly rotation): Chaek Bar (책바) and Honsulbar Godo (혼술바 고도) regularly refresh their Instagram-friendly setups each season.
