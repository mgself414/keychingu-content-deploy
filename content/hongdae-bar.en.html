⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Thinking "Hongdae = clubs only" — and ignoring bar options

Many international travelers associate Hongdae (홍대) with clubs like Rolling Hall or Club FF. In reality, 18+ bars and drinking spots in the Hongdae area have earned ★5.0 ratings from foreign visitors. When you want a quieter drink and real conversation, bars are the answer — and they're where you'll find the deepest dive into Korean drinking culture.

2. Ordering only beer because it feels safe — and missing Korean traditional spirits

Makgeolli (막걸리, Korean milky rice wine), cheongju (청주, clear rice wine), and soju (소주, distilled Korean spirit) are the soul of Korean drinking. Slow Village Brewery (direct-from-brewery makgeolli), Chaek Bar (curated traditional spirits), and Arugam (wine fusion with Korean food) are all beginner-friendly. Makgeolli ₩6,000–12,000 (~$4–9 / ~35–85 RMB / ~580–1,160 JPY); cheongju from ₩15,000 (~$11 / ~80 RMB / ~1,450 JPY); Korean wine pairing from ₩25,000 (~$18 / ~135 RMB / ~2,420 JPY).

3. Avoiding solo drinking because it feels awkward

Korean MZ (밀레니얼·Z세대, roughly millennials and Gen Z) have fully normalized혼술 (honsul, solo drinking — the Korean culture of enjoying a drink alone without feeling self-conscious). Solo-specialist bars — Naejan (내잔), Honsulbar Godo (혼술바 고도), and Blending Bar (블렌딩바) — all offer single seats and single-serving menus. Perfect first stop for solo travelers. Counter seating + English menu + one signature drink = the Korean solo bar experience.

The Hongdae Bar Landscape

Hongdae (홍대), Yeonnam (연남), Hapjeong (합정), and Mangwon (망원) have a rich bar and drinking culture beyond clubs — a completely separate track from live music (Rolling Hall) and DJ clubs (Club FF). From the late 2020s onward, third-wave drinking spots (craft beer, traditional spirits, signature cocktails) have clustered in Hapjeong and Yeonnam.

Hongdae Core hosts rooftop bars, traditional spirit spots, and signature venues — Floating at L7, The View (더뷰), Slow Village Brewery (느린마을 양조장), Chaek Bar (책바), Macaroni Funky Club (마카로니펑키클럽), and more.

Hapjeong (합정) is the home of signature wine and craft beer — Arugam (아루감), Ale Crew Pizza Pub (에일크루 피자펍), and others, all within a 5–10 minute walk of Hapjeong Station (합정역).

Yeonnam (연남) is the solo-drinking and cocktail zone — Blending Bar (블렌딩바), Honsulbar Godo (혼술바 고도), Naejan (내잔), and other solo-friendly venues.

Mangwon (망원) is casual bars and solo spots — Naejan Mangwon (내잔 망원점), Blending Bar Mangwon (블렌딩바 망원점), all walkable from Mangwon Station (망원역).
