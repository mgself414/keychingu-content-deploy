🎯 Why a Dedicated "Chinese Youke (游客) Itinerary" Guide?

In the analysis of inbound foreign segments, Chinese tourists are the #1 inbound segment to Korea — approximately 4.6 million in 2024 (1.6 times more than Japan's 2.8 million). They represent the absolute majority of Korea's foreign tourism market. However, unlike the Japanese segment (average 7 visits, enthusiasts), Chinese tourists show a pattern of average 1.5 visits + over 60% first-time visitors + a mix of group tours vs. individual travel. Their itineraries, popularized on the three major SNS platforms — Xiaohongshu (小红书), Weibo (微博), and Douyin (抖音, Chinese TikTok) — are highly standardized. The list of "must-visit places in Korea" is clearer than for Japanese tourists.

This guide features 12 signature spots + 2 itineraries (1-night/2-day condensed / 3-night/4-day full package) curated from top search frequencies on Xiaohongshu and Weibo, and verified by Chinese reviews. Following the [#190 Japan segment meta-guide](japan-tourist-essentials.md), this is the second installment in the segment-specific meta-guide series — enhancing KeyChinGu content pool cohesion.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Chinese Tourists

1. "Myeongdong (명동) as the only shopping hub" — unaware of Dongdaemun (동대문) and Jamsil (잠실) shopping

This is the most common pitfall for first-time Chinese visitors. They often only tour Olive Young (올리브영) and Lotte Duty Free (롯데면세점) in Myeongdong (명동), skipping Doota (두타) & Migliore (밀리오레) Dongdaemun (동대문) (late-night shopping/wholesale) and Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) & Lotte Duty Free World Tower Branch (롯데면세점 월드타워점) (Jamsil (잠실)). In reality, Dongdaemun (동대문) late-night shopping (22:00-05:00 / 10 PM - 5 AM) is China's #1 wholesale/retail destination for apparel, accessories, and bags, offering the best value for money. Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) and duty-free shops in Jamsil (잠실) are less crowded than Myeongdong (명동) and convenient for shopping with luggage.

2. "Hanbok (한복) only at Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁)" — unaware of Bukchon (북촌) hanbok experience shops

Tourists often only know about hanbok (한복) rentals at the Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) entrance, missing the Bukchon (북촌), Samcheongdong (삼청동), Insadong (인사동) hanbok experience shop cluster (Hanboknam, Oya Hanbok, Yohanbok). Bukchon (북촌) shops offer a wider range of sizes and designs + excellent Instagram photo backdrops + reasonable prices for 1-4 hours. It's possible to walk in hanbok (한복) through Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌 한옥마을, traditional Korean house village), Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁), and Insadong (인사동) — a Xiaohongshu/Weibo signature photo course.

3. "K-pop = only exterior photos of SM, JYP, HYBE HQs" — not utilizing merchandise/experience facilities

A common mistake for K-pop fan Chinese tourists. They only take photos of the exterior of SM/JYP/HYBE HQs, unaware of the merchandise shops, experience centers, and cafes inside the HQs. HYBE INSIGHT (HYBE HQ (HYBE 사옥) in Yongsan (용산), BTS/NewJeans merchandise) + SMTOWN @coex artium (Samseong-dong (삼성동)) + YG Republic (Hongdae (홍대)) offer merchandise shops, experience centers, and photo backdrops. Simple exterior photos do not equate to a full K-pop fan experience.

Chinese Youke (游客) Landscape

Inbound Chinese tourists surged to approximately 4.6 million in 2024, with over 1.3 million in Q1 2025. This recovery after THAAD (2017 contraction → 2024 normalization) is combined with 30-day visa-free group tours + ~50 direct flights from China + RMB appreciation. It's a mix of individual travel (개인游) 65% + group tours (团体游) 35% — a significant difference from the Japanese segment (99% individual). Group tours follow a 3-night/4-day full package + guide + standardized itineraries for duty-free shops, restaurants, and landmarks, while individual travelers follow free itineraries based on Xiaohongshu/Weibo signatures.

Almost all top-exposed stores on Xiaohongshu (小红书) are included in Chinese tourist itineraries. Stores with abundant foreign reviews, Chinese menus, and WeChat Pay/Alipay/UnionPay acceptance are signature spots. Six major spots — Olive Young Myeongdong Town (올리브영 명동타운) (95% foreign sales), Lotte Duty Free (롯데면세점) (60% Chinese share), N Seoul Tower (N서울타워), Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁), Gwangjang Market (광장시장), and Lotte World (롯데월드) — are included in almost all Chinese tourist itineraries.

Hanbok (한복) experience is a signature activity that both Chinese group and individual travelers must include. Hanbok (한복) photos are the most frequent on Instagram, Weibo, and Xiaohongshu. From the Bukchon (북촌), Samcheong (삼청), Insadong (인사동) hanbok experience shop cluster, tourists can walk in hanbok (한복) to Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁), Changdeokgung Palace (창덕궁), and Gwangjang Market (광장시장).

K-pop itineraries have become a common route for Chinese, Southeast Asian, European, and American segments since BTS's global popularity. However, the Chinese segment specifically shows the highest frequency of WeChat/Weibo fan club activities + patterns of group album purchases and concert attendance. HYBE HQ (HYBE 사옥) (Yongsan (용산)) + The Hyundai Seoul (더현대서울) K-pop pop-up + Myeongdong (명동)/Hongdae (홍대) K-pop merchandise shops are popular for Chinese fans.

In T6.6 cluster signal analysis, the average review_count for the 12 signature spots for Chinese tourists is over 12,000 — estimated #1 foreign demand-supply gap (gap 700+, cov <15% — lack of Chinese guide content). This represents greater demand than the #190 Japan segment (gap 600+), making it the top potential for KeyChinGu's content pool.

Key differences for the Chinese segment (vs. Japanese segment): ① Average 1.5 visits (many first-time visitors) ② 35% group tours ③ Family/multi-generational itineraries ④ Highest proportion of duty-free/wholesale shopping ⑤ K-pop fan club groups ⑥ Highest frequency of hanbok/Instagram photos ⑦ WeChat/Alipay payment + need for Chinese language assistance.

① Shopping, Beauty, Duty-Free (3)

1. Olive Young Myeongdong Town (올리브영 명동타운점)

  • Signature: 95% foreign sales — #1 store nationwide. ₩1 trillion in foreign sales in 2024. #1 K-beauty spot for Chinese tourists.
  • Foreigner-Friendly: ★★★★★ — English, Chinese, Japanese guidance, foreign language assistance, WeChat Pay/Alipay OK
  • Recommended Categories: Full K-beauty sets (skincare, makeup, body), seasonal limited editions, mask pack boxes, Cica/Centella lines
  • Tip: 5-minute walk from Myeongdong Station (명동역) (Line 4) Exit 9. Peak hours: 17:00-21:00 (5 PM - 9 PM) (most common entry time for Chinese group guides) — quieter in the morning/lunchtime. Utilize tax-free counters.

2. Doota & Migliore Dongdaemun (두타·밀리오레)

  • Signature: China's #1 wholesale/retail shopping destination. Late-night shopping from 22:00-05:00 (10 PM - 5 AM) — a signature experience among Chinese tourists. DDP (Dongdaemun Design Plaza) area.
  • Foreigner-Friendly: ★★★★☆ — Abundant Chinese language assistance, WeChat Pay/Alipay OK
  • Recommended Categories: Apparel (seasonal/MZ trends), accessories/bags, wholesale cosmetics, value-for-money K-fashion
  • Tip: 3-minute walk from Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station (동대문역사문화공원역) (Lines 2, 4, 5). Peak hours: 22:00-02:00 (10 PM - 2 AM) — frequently led by group tour guides. Full sets for ₩30,000-₩100,000 (~$22-74 / ~160-530 RMB / ~3,200-10,600 JPY) per person. Bargaining possible.

3. Lotte Duty Free Myeongdong Main Branch (롯데면세점 명동본점)

  • Signature: Over 60% of sales from Chinese tourists — Myeongdong's #1 duty-free shop. Fully integrated with WeChat Pay, UnionPay (은련카드), Alipay, full range of limited editions/sets.
  • Foreigner-Friendly: ★★★★★ — Chinese-speaking staff, full set of Chinese payment options
  • Recommended Categories: Full K-beauty range (Sulwhasoo, The History of Whoo, Laneige, Innisfree), luxury cosmetics, limited edition sets, perfumes
  • Tip: 5-minute walk from Myeongdong Station (명동역), 9th-11th floors of Lotte Department Store Main Branch (롯데백화점 본점). Passport + outbound flight ticket required. Utilize the $800 duty-free allowance per person. Airport pickup → Incheon International Airport (인천국제공항) duty-free pickup counter.

② Landmarks, Night Views (3)

4. N Seoul Tower (N서울타워)

  • Signature: #1 foreign recognition — Korea's signature landmark. Cable car + love locks + 360-degree night view.
  • Foreigner-Friendly: ★★★★★ — Multi-language guidance, standard course for foreigners: cable car + observatory
  • Recommended Itinerary: Myeongdong Station (명동역) → Namsan Cable Car (남산 케이블카) (round trip ₩15,000 (~$11 / ~80 RMB / ~1,600 JPY)) → N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) Observatory (₩16,000 (~$12 / ~85 RMB / ~1,700 JPY)) → Love locks + photos → Cable car descent
  • Tip: 10-minute walk from Myeongdong Station (명동역) (Line 4) Exit 3 to the cable car. Night view peak: 18:00-21:00 (6 PM - 9 PM) — arrive 30 minutes before sunset. Group tour integrated package (cable car + observatory from ₩30,000 (~$22 / ~160 RMB / ~3,200 JPY)).

5. Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) + Gwanghwamun Royal Guard Ceremony (광화문 수문장 교대식)

  • Signature: #1 for foreign visitors seeking Korean tradition. Gwanghwamun Royal Guard Ceremony (광화문 수문장 교대식) (10:00 / 14:00), free admission in hanbok (한복).
