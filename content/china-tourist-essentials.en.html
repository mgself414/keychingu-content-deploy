⚠ 3 common mistakes Chinese tourists make

1. "Myeongdong Bay Shopping Base" — Dongdaemun/Jamsil Shopping Mall

The most common pitfalls for first-time Chinese tourists. Take a full tour of Myeongdong Olive Young and Lotte Duty Free Shop and pass Dongdaemun Doota and Migliore (early morning shopping wholesale) + Lotte World Mall and Lotte Duty Free World Tower Branch (Jamsil). In fact, Dongdaemun early morning shopping (22:00 - 05:00) is the No. 1 wholesale and retail destination for Chinese tourists — Best value for money clothing, miscellaneous goods, and accessories. Lotte Mall/Duty Free Shop in Jamsil is quieter than Myeong-dong and convenient for luggage shopping.

2. "Hanbok = only at Gyeongbokgung Palace" — Bukchon Hanbok Experience Store Miji

It is recognized that only hanbok rentals are available at the entrance to Gyeongbokgung Palace, and the Bukchon, Samcheong-dong, and Insa-dong hanbok experience store cluster (Hanboknam, Oya hanbok, and Yohanbok) is an undiscovered pattern. Bukchon store rich in size and design + excellent Instagram photo backdrop + reasonable price for 1-4 hours. You can fully walk to Bukchon Hanok Village + Gyeongbokgung Palace + Insa-dong while wearing Hanbok — Xiaohongshu/Weibo Signature Certified Course.

3. "K-pop = SM·JYP·HYBE office building exterior photos only" — Not utilizing goods and experiences

K-pop fan Common mistakes made by Chinese tourists. I only took pictures of the exterior of the SM/JYP/HYBE building and did not know about the goods shop, experience center, and cafe inside the building. HYBE Insight (Yongsan HYBE building, BTS·NewJeans goods) + SMTOWN @coex artium (Samseong-dong) + YG Republic (Hongdae) etc. Goods shop/experience center + photo backdrop. Simple exterior of the office building ≠ Full K-pop fan experience.

① Shopping·Beauty·Tax Free (3)

1. Olive Young Myeongdong Town

  • Signature: 95% of foreign sales — No. 1 store in the country. 2024 Foreign sales: 1 trillion won. No. 1 K-beauty in China.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Information in English, Chinese, and Japanese, foreign language support, WeChat and Alipay OK
  • Recommended Category: K-beauty full set (skincare, makeup, body), season limited edition, mask pack box, Cica·Centella line
  • Tip: 5-minute walk from Myeongdong Station (Line 4) Exit 9. Peak 17-21 PM (Chinese group guide peak entry time) — Quiet for breakfast and lunch. Use tax-free stands.

2. Dongdaemun Doota & Migliore Dongdaemun

  • Signature: No. 1 wholesale and retail shopping in China. Dawn shopping from 22:00 to 05:00 — A signature among Chinese tourists. DDP area.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Rich Chinese response, WeChat and Alipay OK
  • Recommended Category: Clothing (season/MZ trend), accessories/bags, wholesale cosmetics, cost-effective K-fashion
  • Tip: 3-minute walk from Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (Lines 2, 4, and 5). 22:00~02:00 Peak — Group tour guide frequently leads. Full set 30,000~100,000 won per person. Price can be negotiated.

3. Lotte Duty Free Myeongdong Main Branch

  • Signature: 60%+ of sales from Chinese tourists — No. 1 duty-free shopping mall in Myeongdong. WeChat, UnionPay Card, Alipay full integration, limited edition/set pool.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Chinese staff, full set of Chinese payments
  • Recommended Category: K-beauty full (Sulwhasoo, Whoo, Laneige, Innisfree, Laneige), luxury cosmetics, limited set, perfume
  • Tip: 5-minute walk from Myeongdong Station, 9th to 11th floors of Lotte Department Store main branch. Passport + departure ticket required. Take advantage of the $800 tax exemption limit per person. Airport pickup → Incheon Airport duty free shop pickup counter.

② Landmark/Night view (3)

4. N Seoul Tower (Namsan Tower)

  • Signature: Foreign recognition #1 — Korean signature landmark. Cable car + Love Locks + 360 degree night view.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Multilingual guidance, cable car/observatory standard course for foreigners
  • Recommended route: Myeongdong Station → Namsan Cable Car (round-trip 15,000 won) → N Seoul Tower Observatory (16,000 won) → Love Lock + Photo → Cable Car Down
  • Tip: Myeongdong Station (Line 4) Exit 3 → 10-minute walk by cable car. Night view peak between 18:00 and 21:00 — Enter 30 minutes before sunset. Group tour integrated package (cable car + observatory 30,000 won~).

5. Gyeongbokgung + Gwanghwamun Guard Changing Ceremony

  • Signature: Foreign Korean Tradition #1. Gwanghwamun Guard Changing Ceremony (10:00, 14:00), Hanbok admission free.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Multilingual audio guide, Hanbok entrance exemption, 100% English signage
  • Recommended route: Gyeongbokgung Station (Line 3) Exit 5 → Gyeongbokgung Palace → Gwanghwamun Guard Changing Ceremony → National Folk Museum → Blue House Walk
  • Tip: Opens at 09:00, closed on Tuesdays. Admission fee waived for Hanbok rental (KRW 15,000~30,000) + Instagram photo synergy. Gwanghwamun Changing Ceremony 10:00, 14:00 sharp — arrived exactly on time.

6. Lotte World Tower Seoul Sky SkyDeck (Lotte World Tower Seoul Sky)

  • Signature: Korea #1 555m, world's 5th highest skyscraper. Observation decks on the 117th, 118th, and 123rd floors, glass floor.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Multilingual guidance, full package for Chinese, Japanese, and Southeast Asian groups
  • Recommended route: Jamsil Station (Lines 2 and 8) → Lotte World Tower → Seoul Sky Observatory (31,000 won on weekdays, 33,000 won on weekends) → Glass Floor
  • Tip: Night view peak between 18:00 and 21:00 — Enter 1 hour before sunset. A view of N Seoul Tower (Namsan) and the Jamsil area in the southeast. Lotte World Mall + Duty Free Shop bundle course available.

③ Hanbok/Tradition/Market (3)

7. Bukchon Hanbok Experience — Hanboknam, Oya, Yohanbok (Bukchon Hanbok Experience)

  • Signature: The number one Hanbok experience for Chinese tourists. Xiao Hongshu·Weibo Signature Certification Course. Plenty of sizes and designs.
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ — Support in Chinese, Japanese, and English, size compatible with foreigners
  • Recommended Stores: Hanboknam (Bukchon Main Branch), Oya Hanbok (Samcheong-dong), Yohanbok (Insa-dong) — KRW 15,000 - 30,000 for 1 to 4 hours
  • Tip: 5-10 minutes walk from Anguk Station (Line 3). Additional hair and makeup options (KRW 10,000~20,000). You can do a full walk in Bukchon → Gyeongbokgung → Insa-dong while wearing Hanbok - Synergy with no admission fee.

8. Gwangjang Market Street Food

  • Signature: Market with highest frequency of WeChat and Douyin videos in China. Full set of Bindaetteok, drug gimbap, kkwabaegi, banquet noodles, and tteokbokki.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Chinese, Japanese, English menu, photo menu, many foreigners
  • Recommended menu: Mung bean bindaetteok + makgeolli, drug gimbap, kkwabaegi (rice cake texture), banquet noodles, tteokbokki, Jinokhwa chicken
  • Tip: 3 minutes walk from Jongno 5-ga Station (Line 1). Peak from 15:00 to 21:00 — Quiet from 14:00 to 17:00 on weekdays. Many WeChat and Alipay payment stores (stores accepting foreigners).

9. Insadong Traditional Gallery & Souvenirs

  • Signature: No. 1 group tour guide in China — A collection of souvenirs including traditional tea, calligraphy, Korean paper, traditional liquor, and ceramics.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Multilingual information, abundant group support
  • Recommended stores: Ssamziegil (traditional miscellaneous goods), Insa Art Center, Tongin Store (traditional tea and ceramics), Hanji Workshop
  • Tip: 5 minutes walk from Anguk Station (Line 3). Saturday and Sunday traffic-free streets 11:00~22:00 — Recommended walking times. Area with the most group guides + museums and galleries.

④ K-pop·Theme Park·Insta (3)

10. HYBE Headquarters (Hybe Insight HQ Yongsan)

  • Signature: BTS, New Jeans, Seventeen, Enhyphen Goods Shop + HYBE Insight Museum. Yongsan Hangang-daero.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — K-pop fan route #1, multilingual information
  • Recommended route: 5-minute walk from Yongsan Station (Line 1, KTX) → Photo of the exterior of HYBE headquarters → HYBE Insight Museum (reservation, foreign enthusiasts) → Goods shop
  • Tip: Advance reservation for HYBE Insight Museum is required (English reservation page for foreigners). You can enter the goods shop without reservation. 2~3 hours of office building exterior + goods shop + full museum experience.

11. Lotte World Adventure Jamsil

  • Signature: Korea #1 Urban Theme Park — No. 1 for families, couples, and groups. Mixed indoor/outdoor + night parade.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Multilingual information, foreigner pass (1 Day Pass 64,000 won), WeChat and Alipay OK
  • Recommended route: Directly connected to Jamsil Station (Lines 2 and 8) → Lotte World → Night Parade → Magic Island (Seokchon Lake)
  • Tip: Foreigners 1 Day Pass 64,000 won — Same price as Korean nationals. Short entry lines on weekdays. Lotte World Tower SkyDeck + Lotte Mall + Duty Free Shop Bundle Jamsil Full Day Course available.

12. Yeouido Hangang Park Chimaek

  • Signature: China Weibo/Douyin Certification Course #1. Chicken and beer (chicken + beer) + night view of the Han River + 63 Building view.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Hangang Park guide in English, delivery app available in foreign languages
  • Recommended route: Yeouinaru Station (Line 5) Exit 1 → Hangang Park Lawn → BBQ, Kyochon, BHC delivery + convenience store beer
  • Tip: Spring, summer, fall peak 18:00~22:00 — Sunset, night view + full chicken and beer set. Foreigners can use Delivery app (Yogiyo, Baedal Minjok English version) + card payment. 25,000~40,000 won per person.
