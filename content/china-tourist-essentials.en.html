Mainland China to Korea Travel Guide: Visa for Independent Travel, 15-Day Visa-Free for Groups (Through 2026-12-31), 30 Days on Jeju, Payments (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: Mainland Chinese ordinary passports are not on the visa-free country list, so independent travel requires a visa (Ministry of Foreign Affairs list) · groups of 3 or more recruited by designated travel agencies enter visa-free for 15 days, extended to 2026-07-01 through 12-31 (Ministry of Foreign Affairs press release, Consulate General in Qingdao notice) · Jeju allows 30 days visa-free for both individual and group travelers (Ministry of Foreign Affairs press release) · not on the 42-country automated immigration list (Ministry of Justice). 🎯 How to Use This Guide

When the arrivals doors open, a guide holding a travel agency flag calls out the names of a tour group, and beside them a solo traveler walks past with a phone map already open. They stepped off the same plane, but their entry conditions differ. This guide lists what travelers coming from mainland China should check before departure, using only values from official pages: entry documents (visa for independent travel, group visa-free entry, Jeju), the airport immigration line, payment, and flights. Read it according to whether you are an independent traveler or part of a tour group.

🛂 Entry Documents: Independent Visa, Group Visa-Free, Jeju

  • Independent travelers: mainland China does not appear in the Asia section of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs list "Countries whose nationals may enter Korea without a visa" (Ministry of Foreign Affairs official list, as of February 2024, revised 2026-01-08). Whether you travel alone or with family, you need to obtain a visa; follow the guidance of the Korean diplomatic mission in China for how to apply
  • Temporary visa-free entry for tour groups: groups of 3 or more recruited by a domestic specialized travel agency designated by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, or by a visa-application-agent travel agency (overseas specialized travel agency) designated by a Korean mission in China, may travel nationwide visa-free for up to 15 days. People identified as high-risk are excluded and must obtain a visa separately (joint press release of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Ministry of Justice, 2025-09-07)
  • Period: first 2025-09-29 to 2026-06-30 → extended to 2026-07-01 through 2026-12-31 (Consulate General in Qingdao notice, 2026-06-23). Nothing has been announced for 2027 onward
  • It does not apply to independent travelers: if you do not go through an agency, follow the "independent" row above
  • Jeju: Jeju Special Self-Governing Province continues to allow Chinese nationals 30 days visa-free for both individual and group tourism (Ministry of Foreign Affairs press release, 2025-09-07). The conditions for traveling outside Jeju are not in this material; check at HiKorea
  • K-ETA: the temporary K-ETA exemption (2026-01-01 to 12-31, K-ETA official notice) concerns passports of visa-free countries. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs also tells visa-free entrants to check K-ETA. Independent travelers who arrive with a visa are not subject to K-ETA
  • e-Arrival Card: holders of a Korean visa are subject to electronic arrival declaration. Group (electronic) visa holders and visa-free group tourists via Yangyang and Muan airports are listed as exempt (e-Arrival Card official site). Whether travelers in this new group visa-free scheme must submit it could not be confirmed on official pages; ask your specialized travel agency. For the procedure, see #1289 e-Arrival manual

🚪 The Airport Immigration Line

  • Mainland China is not on the list of 42 countries and regions to which automated immigration clearance expanded on 2026-03-16 (Ministry of Justice, #1412 automated clearance manual). Expect the regular inspection line on entry
  • On departure, short-term visitors aged 17 or older who gave fingerprints and a face scan on entry can use the automated gates (Ministry of Justice immigration official guidance)

Where Alipay, WeChat Pay and UnionPay cards work cannot be summed up in one line, because their merchant networks differ. 👥 Traveling Companions

  • Group visa-free entry requires 3 or more people (Ministry of Foreign Affairs press release). A two-person family trip does not qualify
  • If one member of the party is classified as high-risk, only that person must obtain a visa separately (Ministry of Foreign Affairs press release); the travel agency submits the name list in advance

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Groups are visa-free, so I am too": only groups of 3 or more recruited by a designated travel agency qualify. Independent travel requires a visa (Ministry of Foreign Affairs press release)

2. "30 days visa-free": the nationwide group visa-free period is 15 days. The 30 days is Jeju's visa-free entry (Ministry of Foreign Affairs press release)

3. "Group visa-free ended in June": it was extended through 2026-12-31 (Consulate General in Qingdao notice). What comes after has not been announced

4. "The automated line will be faster": mainland Chinese passports are not on the 42-country/region automated immigration list (Ministry of Justice)
