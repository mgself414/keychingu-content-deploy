⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Honeymoon = Luxury Only": Honeymoons in Seoul combine luxury with budget-friendly dating spots (e.g., Banpo (반포) night view for free + Insaengnecut (인생네컷) (self-photo booth) for ₩4,000 (~$3 / ~20 RMB / ~400 JPY)).

2. "K-Wedding = For Koreans Only": K-Wedding (Korean wedding style) studios in Cheongdam (청담) and Gangnam (강남) are the top destination for foreign honeymoon enthusiasts, offering self-wedding packages.

3. "Honeymoon Hotels = Anywhere": Signiel (시그니엘), Banyan Tree (반얀트리), Shilla (신라), and Conrad (콘래드) are the top destinations for foreign honeymooners.
