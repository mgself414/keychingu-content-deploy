5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. "Korean bread = super sweet" — A Misconception

Korean bread is polarized: danpatbbang (단팥빵, red bean bun) and soboro-ppang (소보로빵, streusel bun) = sweet, while croissants and baguettes = authentic European style. If you're sensitive to sweetness, go for the European-style line.

2. Cakes Are Actually More Expensive in Korea

Korean cakes run ₩6,000–9,000 per slice, with whole cakes at ₩30,000–50,000 — pricier than Japan or Europe. For value, check out convenience store or Daiso (다이소) desserts.

3. Thinking You Always Need a Reservation for Cake

Walk-in is fine for regular cakes. Signature and birthday cakes require a reservation 1–3 days in advance. Both Paris Baguette (파리바게뜨) and Tous Les Jours (뚜레쥬르) apps support English booking.

4. Not Knowing About 24-Hour Bakeries

Select locations in Gangnam (강남), Hongdae (홍대), and Itaewon (이태원) operate 24 hours. Great for late-night bread and coffee, or as a hotel breakfast alternative.

5. Missing Korea-Exclusive Menu Items

Items like patbingsu-ppang (팥빙수빵, shaved-ice-flavored bread), injeolmi-ppang (인절미빵, rice cake bread), and heukimja cake (흑임자케이크, black sesame cake) are Korea-only and top souvenir picks for foreign visitors — you won't find them at overseas locations.

FAQ

Q1. Is English available?

Paris Baguette (파리바게뜨) and Tous Les Jours (뚜레쥬르) = English menus + English app. OUR Bakery (아우어) and trendy standalones are mostly Korean, but photo menus make ordering easy.

Q2. Do I need to reserve cakes in advance?

Paris Baguette (파리바게뜨) and Tous Les Jours (뚜레쥬르) apps (English supported) allow reservations 1–3 days ahead. Foreign phone numbers and foreign cards are accepted.

Q3. Any vegan bread options?

Sogeum-ppang (소금빵, salt bread) and baguettes may be vegan in some cases. Many Korean breads contain milk and eggs — check with the staff directly.

Q4. Hotel breakfast alternative?

Paris Baguette (파리바게뜨) at ₩7,000–10,000 = 2 pieces of bread + 1 coffee. Available at dawn at 24-hour locations.

Q5. Traveling with children?

Paris Baguette (파리바게뜨) and Tous Les Jours (뚜레쥬르) are family-friendly. Red bean buns and streusel buns are kid favorites.

Q6. Can I bring a cake box back to my home country?

Paris Baguette (파리바게뜨) cake boxes are a top souvenir pick for foreign visitors. However, if checking luggage on a flight, cakes may lose their shape — bring a cooler bag + padded packaging + a sturdy bag.
