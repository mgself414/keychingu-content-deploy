How to Read Korean Restaurant Rankings — Blue Ribbon Survey, Seoul Gourmet 100 and the MICHELIN Guide: Who Chooses, How, and What the Marks Mean

Subtitle: A comparison of three marks that often appear on Korean restaurant listings · Blue Ribbon is a private guide chosen by reader ratings (Seoul's Restaurants 2027, 1,602 Seoul restaurants, 42 with 3 ribbons) · Seoul Gourmet 100 is 100 restaurants chosen by the Seoul city government from the recommendations of 60 food experts (2026, 25 new) · MICHELIN Guide Seoul 2026 has 42 Seoul starred restaurants — confirmed from each operator's official announcement (2026-11-18); only the map-app star-rating item is based on news reports

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Korean restaurant signs, map apps and social media often carry marks such as "Blue Ribbon", "Seoul Gourmet 100" and "MICHELIN". The three differ in who chooses, how they choose, and what the mark means. This guide sets out what each one is and how many restaurants appear in its latest edition.

The figures are copied from official materials published by each operator (the Blue Ribbon official site, the Seoul city press release and official site, and the MICHELIN Guide official site). Anything we could not check in an official source is marked separately with "according to news reports".

The MICHELIN Guide Seoul is covered in detail in #1109 Seoul Michelin Guide 2026 for Foreign Travelers, so only the numbers needed for comparison are given here.

📍 Blue Ribbon Survey — Ribbons Chosen by Readers

The Blue Ribbon Survey (블루리본 서베이) is a restaurant guide published since 2005 by the private publisher BR Media (비알미디어). According to the official press release, the 2027 edition marks its 22nd year of publication.

  • How it chooses: According to the official introduction, it collects reader ratings on the official site all year round and publishes them once a year as a book. According to the official press release, from the 2019 edition on, restaurants are chosen by reader ratings alone, without a final evaluation by an expert panel, and the result is shown as a number of ribbons instead of a score. More than 90,000 (9만) readers took part in the 2027 edition ratings.
  • What the ribbons mean: According to the official introduction, every restaurant in the book is worth a visit, and the more ribbons it has, the higher the chance of being satisfied whenever you go. The 3-ribbon restaurants are presented as the best in each Seoul category.
  • Latest edition Seoul's Restaurants 2027 (서울의 맛집 2027): Published on October 13, 2026. It lists 1,602 Seoul restaurants (42 more than the previous year), with 42 at 3 ribbons, 314 at 2 ribbons and 685 at 1 ribbon.
  • Newly awarded 3 ribbons: 2 restaurants, Gigas (기가스), a Mediterranean restaurant in Namchang-dong (남창동), and Layeon (라연), modern Korean cuisine at The Shilla Seoul (서울신라호텔). The other 40 of the 3-ribbon restaurants were kept from the previous year. The 3-ribbon restaurants in the official announcement include Mingles (밍글스), Mosu Seoul (모수서울), Kwonsooksoo (권숙수), Onjium Restaurant (온지음레스토랑) and Jungsik (정식당).
  • What "19 new restaurants" means: The 19 in the 2027 edition press release is not the number of restaurants newly awarded ribbons. It is the number of "notable new restaurants" that opened within the last year.
  • Where to look: The official site (bluer.co.kr) and app. According to an official site news item (2026-10-15), the reservation app CatchTable (캐치테이블) was named a "2026 official partner". An English version was not confirmed.

📍 Seoul Gourmet 100 — 100 Restaurants Chosen by the City from Expert Recommendations

Seoul Gourmet 100 (서울미식 100선) is a list of restaurants chosen by the Seoul city government. According to the Seoul city press release, the 2026 edition was announced on October 6, 2026.

  • How it chooses: 60 food experts, including food journalists, culinary travelers, academic researchers, chefs and tourism specialists, each recommend 3 restaurants per category across 7 categories, 21 in all. The Seoul city press release says "60 domestic food experts", while the official site says "60 domestic and international food experts". The criteria also differ: the press release gives global appeal, expertise and buzz, while the official site gives originality, expertise and buzz.
  • Categories of the 2026 list of 100: Korean 27, Western 23, Asian 15, Grill 10, Vegetarian 8, Cafe & Dessert 9, Bar & Pub 8. Of these, 25 are new. This year, for the first time, a separate "Seoul Cafe & Dessert 50" list was also announced.
  • Examples of new entries: The Seoul city press release names the 25 new restaurants. They include Korean restaurants Seoryeong (서령), Yurok (유록) and Haenamcheonilgwan (해남천일관), Asian restaurants Palseon (팔선) and Hobin (호빈), Western restaurants L'Amant Secret (라망시크레) and Muoki (무오키), the grill restaurant Montan (몽탄), and the bar and pub Baekgom Makgeolli (백곰막걸리). The full list of 100 is on the official site.
  • Where to look: The official site, Taste of Seoul (tasteofseoul.visitseoul.net). The site has an English menu link and connects to Naver, Kakao, CatchTable and Google Maps. English body text was not confirmed.
  • Seoul Gourmet Week (서울미식주간): According to the Seoul city press release, it was announced that Seoul Gourmet Week 2026 would be held on October 23~29.

📍 MICHELIN Guide Seoul 2026 — Stars, Bib Gourmand and Selected

According to the official MICHELIN Guide site, MICHELIN Guide Seoul & Busan 2026 was announced in Busan on March 5, 2026.

  • 42 Seoul starred restaurants: 3-star 1, 2-star 10, 1-star 31. According to the official MICHELIN Guide site, Seoul's 3-star restaurant is Mingles (밍글스), which kept its 3 stars again this time.
  • Total selection: Seoul 178, Busan 55, 233 in total. Bib Gourmand 71 and Selected 116 were announced as combined Seoul and Busan figures.
  • What stars, Bib Gourmand and Selected mean, and information on Seoul restaurants, are covered in #1109 Seoul Michelin Guide 2026 for Foreign Travelers.

📍 How Map-App Star Ratings Differ

Unlike Seoul Gourmet 100 and MICHELIN, star ratings on map apps such as Naver Map (네이버지도) and Kakao Map (카카오맵) are scores left by visitors. Blue Ribbon also uses reader ratings, but it differs in that it publishes them once a year in a book as ribbons.

  • According to news reports, Naver stopped showing star ratings in 2021 and reintroduced star ratings in 2026.
  • We could not find an official help page from either app explaining how the star rating is calculated.

📍 Reading the Three Together

  • The choosers differ — According to the official descriptions, Blue Ribbon is chosen by readers, Seoul Gourmet 100 by food experts gathered by the Seoul city government, and MICHELIN by MICHELIN inspectors. Ribbon counts and star counts are marks given on different criteria.
  • Restaurants on more than one list — Mingles (밍글스) is on the 3-ribbon list of the Blue Ribbon 2027 edition and is listed with 3 stars on the official MICHELIN Guide site. Palseon (팔선), Hobin (호빈), L'Amant Secret (라망시크레) and Muoki (무오키) are on both the Seoul Gourmet 100 2026 new-entry list and the Blue Ribbon 2027 edition 3-ribbon list.
  • They change with each edition — Blue Ribbon publishes a book once a year (official introduction), and 25 restaurants newly entered the Seoul Gourmet 100 2026 edition. Check which year's edition a mark on a restaurant refers to, and look up the latest list on the official site.

⚠ 3 Things to Know in Advance

  1. "19 new restaurants" is not a count of new ribbons — The 19 in the Blue Ribbon 2027 edition are "notable new restaurants" that opened within the last year, and the restaurants newly awarded 3 ribbons are the 2, Gigas (기가스) and Layeon (라연).
  2. Check whether a place is open separately — The restaurant names in this guide are those listed in the official announcements, and we did not check whether they are open or how to reserve before a visit.
  3. English support differs by system — MICHELIN has an English official site, and Seoul Gourmet 100 has an English menu link on its official site. For Blue Ribbon, only the Korean-language site was confirmed.

⚙ FAQ 3

Q. Are Blue Ribbon's 3 ribbons and MICHELIN stars the same standard?

A. No. According to the official descriptions, Blue Ribbon is chosen by reader ratings and MICHELIN by inspectors. According to the Blue Ribbon official introduction, the number of ribbons shows "the chance of being satisfied whenever you go".

Q. Who chooses Seoul Gourmet 100?

A. According to the Seoul city press release, it is chosen from the recommendations of 60 food experts in 7 categories (the press release says "domestic" experts, while the official site says "domestic and international"). The 2026 edition has 100 restaurants, of which 25 are new.

Q. Where can foreigners see the lists?

A. For MICHELIN, use the English official site (guide.michelin.com). For Seoul Gourmet 100, use the official site Taste of Seoul (tasteofseoul.visitseoul.net). Blue Ribbon is on the official site bluer.co.kr, which was confirmed only in Korean.
