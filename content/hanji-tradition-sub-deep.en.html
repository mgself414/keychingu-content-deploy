Hanji Guide: Jeonju Hanji Museum, Jeonju Traditional Hanji Center, Jeonju Hanji Culture Festival, Wanju Daeseung Hanji Village, Wonju Hanji Theme Park, Andong Hanji, Goesan Hanji Experience Museum

Subtitle: Jeonju Hanji Museum (free admission) · Jeonju Traditional Hanji Center (hanji papermaking experience) · 2026 Jeonju Hanji Culture Festival (October 8~10, ended) · Wanju Daeseung Hanji Village (9 hanji factories) · Wonju Hanji Theme Park · Andong Hanji (making hanji 5,000 won) · Goesan Hanji Experience Museum — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places where you can see hanji (한지) (Korean paper) and, at some of them, try making it yourself, grouped by region. It covers 4 places in Jeonju and Wanju, 1 in Wonju, 1 in Andong and 1 in Goesan. One museum has free admission, and the place with a registered experience fee is Andong Hanji.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Jeonju and Wanju — Jeonju Hanji Museum, Jeonju Traditional Hanji Center, Jeonju Hanji Culture Festival, Daeseung Hanji Village

The Jeonju Hanji Museum (전주한지박물관) (59 Palbok-ro (팔복로), Deokjin-gu (덕진구), Jeonju-si, ☎ 063-210-8103) is a museum run by Jeonju Paper (전주페이퍼), which makes newsprint and publishing paper. It changed its name from a paper museum to the Jeonju Hanji Museum in 2007. It holds hanji crafts, hanji-making tools, old documents and old books, and holds a special exhibition on a different theme every year.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~17:00 (last entry 16:30). Closed days: Every Monday and Sunday, January 1, Seollal (설) and Chuseok (추석) holidays.
  • Fee: Free.

The Jeonju Traditional Hanji Center (전주전통한지원) (100-10 Hanji-gil (한지길), Wansan-gu (완산구), Jeonju-si, ☎ 010-8959-7757) is a place that makes hanji with traditional hanji-making techniques. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, more than 80% of the hanji made here is exported to Japan. You can learn the hanji-making process, such as scooping the paper, adding patterns and drying.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Experiences: Hanji doll crafts, cloisonne crafts (칠보공예), a hanji papermaking experience and more. Parking: Not available.

The 2026 Jeonju Hanji Culture Festival (2026 전주한지문화축제) (20 Hyeonmu 1-gil (현무1길), Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si, at the Korea Traditional Culture Center (한국전통문화전당), ☎ 063-281-1553) is a hanji festival that began in 1997. Every year it holds the National Hanji Craft Competition, and it also holds the National Children's Hanji Art Contest. In 2026 it was held October 8~10 at the Korea Traditional Culture Center, from 10:00 to 20:00, and admission was free. It was hosted by Jeonju City.

  • Programs: The Jeonju Hanji Sports Day, the Hanji Culture Zone (experiences and sales), the Hanji Experiment Zone, the Hanji Play Zone, a hanji lantern exhibition, an exhibition of award-winning works from the 32nd National Hanji Craft Competition, an exhibition from the 6th National Children's Hanji Art Contest and more were held.

Wanju Daeseung Hanji Village (완주 대승한지마을) (18 Bogeom-gil (보검길), Soyang-myeon (소양면), Wanju-gun, ☎ 063-242-1001) is a village with 9 hanji factories and about 10 holders of hanji-production skills. At the Hanji Life History Exhibition Hall and the experience center, you can make paper yourself or make hanji crafts.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00, in winter (November to February) 09:00~17:30. Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Experiences: Papermaking experience, making a picture frame with hanji papermaking, making hanji rubber shoes, making kites and more. Age: All ages. Parking: Available.

📍 Wonju — Wonju Hanji Theme Park

Wonju Hanji Theme Park (원주한지테마파크) (151 Hanji-gongwon-gil (한지공원길), Wonju-si, ☎ 033-734-4739) is a hanji culture complex that opened on September 9, 2010. Since 2011 the Hanji Culture Foundation (한지문화재단) has run it under contract and holds the Hanji Cultural Festival. In the Hanji History Room on the 1st floor you can see the history of hanji and how it is made, and if you reserve you can join a hanji experience program.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok holidays.
  • Experiences: Hanji experience, hanji papermaking and more. Parking: Available.

📍 Andong and Goesan — Andong Hanji and Goesan Hanji Experience Museum

Andong Hanji (안동한지) (13 Nabau-gil (나바우길), Pungsan-eup (풍산읍), Andong-si, ☎ 054-858-7007) is a place that makes traditional hanji near the entrance of Hahoe Village (하회마을). It makes pure hanji (純紙) in the traditional way from the water of Pungsan and domestic paper mulberry (dak, 닥나무). It produces white-dak hwaseonji (화선지) (calligraphy paper) for calligraphy, ordinary hwaseonji, changhoji (창호지) (door paper) for pasting on doors and more.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~17:00. Closed days: Open year-round. On weekends you can only take a factory tour.
  • Experiences: Making hanji 5,000 won. Age: All ages. Capacity: 100 people. Parking: Available (about 50 small cars).

The Goesan Hanji Experience Museum (괴산 한지체험박물관) (233 Wonpung-ro (원풍로), Yeonpung-myeon (연풍면), Goesan-gun, ☎ 043-832-3223) is a place where Sinpung Hanji (신풍한지) makes hanji from domestic paper mulberry and runs an experiential learning center. A hanji museum led by Ahn Chi-yong (안치용), a holder of intangible heritage and a hanji master, stands together with it, so you can see hanji-making tools and relics. In its main steps, hanji goes through this process: in November and December, one-year-old paper mulberry branches are cut and boiled, the bark is peeled and dried, then boiled again and bleached, and mulberry pulp (닥죽) is made to scoop the paper. You can take part in experiences if you reserve before you go.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Experiences: Traditional hanji papermaking, making hanji coasters, wildflower paper papermaking, tearing hanji to decorate. Parking: Available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Many places close on Monday — Apart from Andong Hanji, all the museums and experience centers close every Monday. Jeonju Hanji Museum is also closed on Sunday.
  2. Andong Hanji offers only factory tours on weekends — It is open year-round, but on Saturday and Sunday you can only take a tour.
  3. The 2026 Jeonju Hanji Culture Festival has ended — It was held October 8~10.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where can I try making hanji myself?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, they are Jeonju Traditional Hanji Center (hanji papermaking), Wanju Daeseung Hanji Village (papermaking experience), Wonju Hanji Theme Park (hanji papermaking, reservation), Andong Hanji (making hanji 5,000 won) and Goesan Hanji Experience Museum (traditional hanji papermaking, reservation).

Q. Where can I visit for free?

A. Jeonju Hanji Museum has free admission (09:00~17:00, closed Monday and Sunday).
