🎯 Why a Dedicated Seongdong·Wangsimni Guide?

Seongdong·Wangsimni refers to the Wangsimni (왕십리)·Geumho (금호)·Oksu (옥수)·Majang (마장)·Haengdang (행당) Han River-connected area within Seongdong-gu (성동구) — a separate deep dive into the main Seongdong-gu area distinct from Seongsu (#01·#145). It combines Wangsimni Station (왕십리역) (Korea's #1 five-line transfer station for Lines 2, 5, KTX, Gyeongui-Jungang Line, Suin-Bundang Line) + Han River views in Geumho (금호) and Oksu (옥수) + traditional butcher shops and meat restaurants in Majang (마장) + MZ (Millennials and Gen Z, a popular term in Korea referring to young generations) hotspots in Haengdang (행당). This is KeyChinGu's first entry into the main Seongdong-gu area (seongdong cluster 168 cases).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Seongdong (성동) = Seongsu (성수) only" — Undervaluing the main area: Beyond Seongsu (#01·#145), Wangsimni (왕십리)·Geumho (금호)·Oksu (옥수)·Majang (마장)·Haengdang (행당) offer a deep dive into the Han River, transfer hubs, and butcher culture.

2. "Wangsimni Station (왕십리역) = just a station": It's Korea's #1 five-line transfer station (Lines 2, 5, KTX, Gyeongui-Jungang Line, Suin-Bundang Line). Also the departure point for KTX Gangneung Line.

3. "Majang Livestock Market (마장축산물시장) = unknown": Korea's #1 butcher wholesale market (opened in 1961). Direct transactions for Hanwoo (한우, premium Korean beef) and pork. Foreigner price negotiation OK.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Wangsimni Station (왕십리역) transfer? Korea's #1 five-line transfer. Departure point for KTX Gangneung Line.

2. Majang Livestock Market (마장축산물시장) accessible to foreigners? Yes. Price negotiation + immediate sashimi or BBQ OK.

3. Eungbongsan (응봉산) night view? Top 1 Han River night view spot (#51 Eungbongsan Palgakjeong (응봉산 팔각정)).

4. Seongdong (성동) + Seongsu (성수) itinerary? 10-minute walk or 1 stop on Line 2 or Suin-Bundang Line.

5. Hanyang University (한양대) campus? Free entry OK.

6. Oksu (옥수)·Geumho (금호) Han River (한강) cycling? Han River bicycle paths + Ttareungi (따릉이, Seoul's public bike rental service).
