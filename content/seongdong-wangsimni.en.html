⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Seongdong = Seongsuman" — Value of this area is unknown: Seongsu (#01·#145) and others Wangsimni, Geumho, Oksu, Majang, Haengdang = Han River, transfer, butchery deep dive.

2. "Wangsimni Station = just a station": Korea's No. 1 5-way transfer (2·5, KTX, Gyeongui-Jungang Line, Suin Bundang Line). KTX Gangneung Line departure point.

3. "Majang Livestock Products Market = Unknown": Korea's No. 1 meat wholesale market (opened in 1961). Direct wholesale of Korean beef and pork. Foreigner price negotiation OK.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Transfer to Wangsimni Station? Korea’s No. 1 quintuple transfer. KTX Gangneung Line departure point.

2. Is Majang Livestock Products Market open to foreigners? possible. Price negotiation + instant raw fish and grill OK.

3. Night view of Eungbongsan Mountain? No. 1 night view of the Han River (#51 Eungbongsan Palgakjeong).

4. Seongdong + Seongsu route? 10 minutes on foot or 1 stop on Line 2 or Suin Bundang Line.

5. Hanyang University campus? Free entry OK.

6. Oksu·Geumho Han River bike? Han River Bicycle Path + Ttareungi.
