This piece lists only places with 2026 dates and hours, in date order. Check your travel dates against the table and pick what is open that day.

① Department Store Façades and Media Façades — See Them in the Evening

The Shinsegae Main Store (신세계 본점) plays its video from 17:00 until midnight. The Hyundai Department Store and Galleria decorations are viewed during store hours. The Hyundai Seoul H Village (더현대 서울 H빌리지) (11/10~12/31) is in #746.

  • Shinsegae Main Store "Shinsegae Square" (신세계스퀘어) — A Christmas video plays on the media façade on the outer wall of the main store. The run lasts until January 31, 2027, and it plays daily 17:00~24:00. It opened on November 6 (Shinsegae Group Newsroom (신세계그룹 뉴스룸) 2026-11-05). The address is 63 Sogong-ro (소공로 63), Jung-gu (중구), and the nearest station is Hoehyeon Station (회현역) (Line 4) (Kakao Map (카카오맵)). You watch the video from outside the building, so you can see it regardless of the department store's closed days (11/30 and others, as registered on Kakao Map).
  • Hyundai Department Store Trade Center branch "Christmas in New York City" (현대백화점 무역센터점 크리스마스 인 뉴욕 시티) — Starting with the Trade Center branch on October 27, Hyundai Department Store opened a New York street-themed decoration at all its stores (Hyundai Department Store press release · News1 (뉴스1) 10/30). The main entrance plaza has a 13m tree and a photo zone, and the 10th floor has a 6m cat sculpture and a yellow taxi. The end date has not been announced. The address is 517 Teheran-ro (테헤란로 517), Gangnam-gu (강남구), and the nearest station is Samseong Station (삼성역) (Line 2).
  • Galleria Luxury Hall (갤러리아 명품관) — The façade decoration done together with Loro Piana and the tree on the Luxury Hall's East Plaza are up October 30 to December 31 (Etoday (이투데이) · News1 report of 2026-11-02). The name registered on Kakao Map is "Galleria Department Store WEST branch (갤러리아백화점 WEST점)". The address is 343 Apgujeong-ro (압구정로 343), Gangnam-gu (강남구), and the nearest station is Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역) (Suin-Bundang Line).

② Seoul City and District Events — Mostly Free, Outdoors

  • Seokchon Lake Luminarie (석촌호수 루미나리에) — The lights are on October 30, 2026 to February 28, 2027, daily 17:30~22:30 (Seoul Metropolitan Government Media Hub (서울시 미디어허브) 2026-11-11). Times may change with sunset and on-site conditions. The venue is around the east and west lakes of Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) (around 166 Songpanaru-gil (송파나루길 166), Songpa-gu (송파구)), and it is free. It is about a 5-minute walk from Exits 2 and 3 of Jamsil Station (잠실역) (Lines 2 and 8) (same article). Combine it with the Lotte World Mall Time Show (18~21, on the hour) and you see both in one Jamsil evening.
  • Sejong Center Curtain Call Garden winter market (세종문화회관 커튼콜가든 겨울마켓) — November 17, 2026 to January 3, 2027, daily 11:00~21:00, open with no days off (Seoul Media Hub 2026-11-17). The venue is the Garden of Art (예술의 정원) in the back courtyard of the Sejong Center (세종문화회관). You go in with no reservation or entrance fee. It sells winter snacks such as bungeoppang (붕어빵) and mulled wine, plus handicrafts.
  • Seoul Lantern Festival (서울빛초롱축제) — December 11 (Fri), 2026 to January 31 (Sun), 2027, daily 18:00~23:00 (Seoul Tourism Organization (서울관광재단) business introduction page). The venue runs from Cheonggye Plaza (청계광장) to Samil Bridge (삼일교) on Cheonggyecheon (청계천). The page shows no fee. The festival has not opened yet, so detailed programs have not been announced.
  • Gwanghwamun Market (광화문 마켓) — Runs December 11, 2026 to January 10, 2027 around Gwanghwamun Plaza and Sejongno Park. Hours are Sunday to Thursday 17:30~22:00, Friday and Saturday 17:30~22:30, and admission is free (Visit Seoul, run by the Seoul Tourism Organization, posted 2026-11-05, checked 11/25). It is one program of the Seoul city winter festival "Seoul Winter Festival" (서울윈터페스티벌) (December 11 to January 31, 2027, Gwanghwamun Plaza and 6 other sites, Fun Seoul).
  • Seoul Light DDP 2026 Winter (서울라이트 DDP) — It is registered on Seoul's festival portal Fun Seoul (펀서울) as December 17 to 31, 2026, at DDP (Dongdaemun Design Plaza (동대문디자인플라자)). Hours, fee, and the 2026 program have not yet been posted on Fun Seoul (the page description is from 2025). It is near Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station (동대문역사문화공원역).
  • Ttukseom Romantic Hangang Christmas Market (뚝섬 로맨틱한강 크리스마스 마켓) — 12/24~27, 14:00~21:00, under Cheongdam Bridge (청담대교) and on the 1st floor of the Hangang Plplu (한강플플) (Fun Seoul). The fee has not been announced. For last season's information, see #746.

③ Theme Park Winter Seasons — You Need a Ticket

  • Everland "Christmas Wish" (에버랜드 크리스마스 위시) — November 21 to December 31 (Samsung C&T Newsroom (삼성물산 뉴스룸) 2026-11-16). A "Giant Wish Tree" about 10m tall stands near the main gate. The "Bling Bling X-mas Parade" and the night multimedia fireworks show "Magic in the Sky" run from November 27 to the end of the year (newsroom original wording: "from the 27th"). The parade marches about 30 minutes from the Rose Garden entrance to Carnival Plaza. The newsroom gives no times for the two shows. The snow sledding slopes open in turn in December. To get there, take Bundang Line to Giheung Station (기흥역) → Everline terminus → free shuttle (Everland's official transport guide confirmed in #746). Separately from Christmas Wish, the winter festival "Holiday Wish" (홀리데이 위시), held with Chiikawa (Korean name: Meonjakgwi / 먼작귀), runs from December 19 to March 24, 2027 (Samsung C&T Newsroom 2026-11-25). The announcement does not explain how the two events relate. It is the first collaboration between a Korean theme park and Chiikawa. Goods, photo zones, and activity programs are planned, and the detailed program and times have not been released yet.
  • Caribbean Bay "Winter Spa Cabi" (캐리비안 베이 윈터 스파 캐비) — A water park next to Everland. From December 3 to March 1, 2027 it opens hot-water pools, open-air baths, and a Finnish sauna (Samsung C&T Newsroom 11/16, 11/25). This year it is decorated together with Chiikawa (Korean name: Meonjakgwi / 먼작귀) (newsroom 11/25).
  • Lotte World Adventure winter season "Cookie Run X Lotte World: Sweet Winter Adventure" (롯데월드 어드벤처 겨울 시즌) — November 21 to March 1, 2027. The "Happy Christmas Parade" goes around the 1st-floor parade course daily at 14:00 and 20:00. The Garden Stage performance is daily at 18:30 (based on the Insight report). All values are from the Insight (인사이트) report of 2026-11-09. The address is 240 Olympic-ro (올림픽로 240), Songpa-gu (송파구), and the nearest station is Jamsil Station (잠실역) (Lines 2 and 8).

  • Iceberg Lounge (아이스버그 라운지) — A cafe and cocktail bar in the printing-shop alley of Mullae-dong (문래동). The Instagram feature (@cafe.ing, 11/20) shows a red carpet, a chandelier, and a tree. Dietary note: the cocktail menu contains alcohol. "The Iceberg" (디 아이스버그) (8,000 won) is described by the owner as a non-alcoholic whisky cold brew. "The Cloud" (더 클라우드) (7,000 won) contains macadamia (nut) cream. Cheesecake and tiramisu contain dairy.
  • Another Side (어나더사이드) — A large cafe near Balsan Station (발산역). The Instagram feature (@cafe.ing, 11/14, marked as an ad) shows a tree and a Christmas photo zone. Dietary note: the jambon-beurre contains pork ham and butter. The carrot rapee egg sandwich (당근라페 에그 샌드위치) contains egg, and the tuna sandwich (참치 샌드위치) contains fish. Lattes and croissants contain milk and butter.

🗺 Routes — Two Evening Options

A. Myeongdong to Gwanghwamun to Cheonggyecheon (after 12/11)

  1. Daytime Bungeoppang (붕어빵) and mulled wine at the Sejong Center Curtain Call Garden (세종문화회관 커튼콜가든) winter market (11:00~21:00)
  2. Dusk Shinsegae Main Store (신세계 본점) media façade (from 17:00)
  3. Evening Walk toward Samil Bridge (삼일교) from Cheonggye Plaza (청계광장) along the Seoul Lantern Festival (서울빛초롱축제) (18:00~23:00)

Before December 11, use Route B.

B. A Jamsil Evening

  1. Afternoon Lotte World Adventure (롯데월드 어드벤처) parade (14:00), only on days you have a ticket
  2. 18:00~21:00 Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) Time Show (on the hour). If you have a Lotte Town Christmas Market (롯데타운 크리스마스 마켓) reservation, see it together (#746)
  3. Night Seokchon Lake Luminarie (석촌호수 루미나리에) (~22:30)

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Expecting the Everland parade in the first week of late November — Christmas Wish (크리스마스 위시) started on 11/21, but from 11/21~26 there is only the tree. The parade and fireworks start on the 27th.
  2. Looking for the lanterns in early December — The Cheonggyecheon Seoul Lantern Festival (서울빛초롱축제) starts on December 11. On evenings before that, see the Shinsegae Main Store (신세계 본점) façade or the Seokchon Lake Luminarie (석촌호수 루미나리에).
  3. Planning the Christmas holiday like a normal evening — 12/25 (Fri) to 27 (Sun) is a three-day holiday, and the Ttukseom (뚝섬) market also runs around then (12/24~27). Outdoor events get crowded, so mix in indoor cafes and department stores. For cold-weather preparation, see #1318.

⚙ 3 FAQs

  1. What if I only have one day? — If it is after December 11, we recommend Route A. The Myeongdong (명동) Shinsegae façade (17:00~) and the Cheonggyecheon (청계천) lanterns (18:00~23:00) are fairly close.
  2. What is open on the night of December 24? — Going by the table, the Shinsegae façade (~24:00), the lanterns (~23:00), Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) (~22:30), the Gwanghwamun Market (a Thursday, so ~22:00, going by the hours for each day of the week), the Ttukseom market (~21:00), and Curtain Call Garden (~21:00) are within their run periods that day. Among cafes and bars, Iceberg Lounge (아이스버그 라운지) is open until 24:00 by its Kakao Map registration.
  3. What about the Gwanghwamun Market and the Myeongdong Cathedral Christmas events? — The Gwanghwamun Market (광화문 마켓) runs 12/11~2027/1/10 around Gwanghwamun Plaza and Sejongno Park, Sunday to Thursday 17:30~22:00 and Friday and Saturday 17:30~22:30, with free admission (Visit Seoul, checked 11/25). The official website of Myeongdong Cathedral (명동대성당) could not be opened and no 2026 Christmas event press coverage was found (as of 2026-11-25). For last season's information, see #746; for lighting spots, see #745.
