⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Korean temples = Japanese/Chinese temples": Korean temples combine Buddhism, nature, and Korean-style dancheong (단청 - traditional decorative coloring) and munchangsal (문창살 - window lattices). This differs from Japanese temples (simple nature integration) and Chinese temples (grand colors). Deep dive into Korean dancheong (단청), Korean autumn foliage, and Korean temple food at Jinkwansa (진관사), Gilsangsa (길상사), and Bongeunsa (봉은사).

2. "Joseon's (조선) 4 Grand Palaces = all the same": The four palaces each have distinct characteristics:

  • Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) = Main Palace·Royal Guard Changing Ceremony (No. 1 photo spot for foreigners)
  • Changdeokgung (창덕궁) = Nature-friendly·Huwon (후원 - Secret Garden) (UNESCO)
  • Changgyeonggung (창경궁) = History of Japanese colonial era zoo → restoration
  • Deoksugung (덕수궁) = Modern Korea (대한제국, Daehan Jeguk - Korean Empire, Russo-Japanese War) + Seokjojeon (석조전 - Western-style stone building)

3. "Sungkyunkwan (성균관) = just a school": Sungkyunkwan (성균관) was Joseon's (조선) No. 1 educational institution from 1398 (all 28 Joseon (조선) kings were educated here) + Myeongnyundang (명륜당), Daeseongjeon (대성전), and Jongyeonggak (존경각) represent Korea's No. 1 traditional architecture. It's a top priority for foreigners to deep dive into hanbok (한복 - traditional Korean attire) and Korean academia.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Free admission to palaces when wearing hanbok (한복)?: All Gyeongbokgung (경복궁), Changdeokgung (창덕궁), Changgyeonggung (창경궁), and Deoksugung (덕수궁) offer free admission if wearing hanbok (한복).

2. UNESCO World Cultural Heritage sites: Changdeokgung (창덕궁) (1997)·Jongmyo (종묘) (1995)·Seooreung (서오릉) (1995)·Suwon Hwaseong Fortress (수원화성) (1997, Gyeonggi-do (경기도)).

3. Royal Guard Changing Ceremony: Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) at 10:00 AM·2:00 PM + Deoksugung (덕수궁) at 11:00 AM·2:00 PM·3:30 PM. No. 1 photo spot for foreigners.

4. Templestay (템플스테이) available for foreigners: Jinkwansa (진관사), Bongeunsa (봉은사), Gilsangsa (길상사) all offer English programs. Advance reservation is essential.

5. Buddha's Birthday (부처님 오신 날) Lotus Lantern Festival (연등회): May (Lunar April 8th) + full set of events at Jogyesa (조계사), Jinkwansa (진관사), Bongeunsa (봉은사), etc.

6. Palace·Temple·Jongmyo (종묘) 1-day course: Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) → Changdeokgung (창덕궁) → Jongmyo (종묘) → Jogyesa (조계사) (30-minute walk) full set.
