⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Korean temples = same as Japanese and Chinese temples": Korean temples = combination of Buddhism + nature + Korean-style Dancheong and Munchangsal. A different texture from Japanese temples (simple combination of nature) and Chinese temples (huge colors). Jingwansa Temple, Gilsangsa Temple, Bongeunsa Temple Deep dive into Korean Dancheong, Korean autumn leaves, and Korean temple food.

2. "The four major palaces of the Joseon Dynasty = all the same": The four palaces have different personalities:

  • Gyeongbokgung Palace = Changing ceremony of the main palace and gatekeepers (No. 1 photo spot for foreigners)
  • Changdeokgung Palace = Nature-friendliness and support (UNESCO)
  • Changgyeonggung Palace = Japanese zoo → restoration history
  • Deoksugung Palace = Modern Korea (Korean Empire, Russo-Japanese War) + Seokjojeon (Western style)

3. "Sungkyunkwan = just a school": Sungkyunkwan is 1398 Joseon's No. 1 educational institution (All 28 kings of Joseon were educated here) + Myeongryundang, Daeseongjeon, and Jonggyeonggak are Korea's No. 1 traditional architecture. 1st place for foreigners to deep dive in hanbok and Korean studies.

① Korea’s five major temples (3)

1. Jinkwansa Temple ★ 1018·Temple Stay No. 1

  • Address: 73 Jingwan-gil, Eunpyeong-gu (#182)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Korea’s top 5 temples + No. 1 temple stay for foreigners
  • Signature: Established in 1018, adjacent to Eunpyeong Hanok Village, No. 1 temple food in Korea + Temple stay in English for foreigners (advance reservation)
  • Details: #182 Eunpyeong and Bukhansan sisters

2. Bongeunsa Temple

  • Address: 531 Bongeunsa-ro, Gangnam-gu (Bongeunsa Station, Line 9)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎ — No. 1 temple in downtown Gangnam
  • Signature: 794 establishment, adjacent to COEX, Gwaneumjeon Hall, Korean urban temple scenery
  • Admission fee: Free + English guidance
  • Details: #142 Gangnam sisters

3. Gilsangsa Temple

  • Address: 68 Seonjam-ro 5-gil, Seongbuk-gu
  • Foreigners ★: ○ — 1997 Korea’s No. 1 traditional temple
  • Signature: Lucky Sanghwa (Jaya City) → Beopjeong Monk → 1997 Temple. Authentic Korean atmosphere
  • Admission Fee: Free

② Urban temple (3)

4. Jogyesa Temple ★ No. 1 place for Buddhism in Korea

  • Address: 55 Ujeongguk-ro, Jongno-gu
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Korean Jogye Order Headquarters/Temple No. 1 in the city center
  • Signature: Established in 1910, Korea’s first Buddhist center, Lotus Deunghoe (May, Buddha’s birthday)
  • Admission Fee: Free

5. Cheongryongsa Temple

  • Address: Naksan, 65 Dongmangsan-gil, Jongno-gu (adjacent to #170)
  • Foreigners ★: ○ — No. 1 Korean Bhikkhuni Temple
  • Signature: Established in 1396, combined with a Korean nun (nun) temple and a walk in Naksan Mountain

6. Doseonsa ★ Bukhansan Mountain

  • Address: Ui-dong, Gangbuk-gu (#177·#186)
  • Foreigners ★: ○ — Bukhansan Mountain Ui hiking combined
  • Signature: Establishment of Silla 862, Bukhansan Mountain climbing, No. 1 foreign mountain climbing enthusiast
  • Details: #186 Sisters Gangbuk and Suyu

③ Four major palaces of Joseon (4)

7. Gyeongbokgung (Gyeongbokgung) ★ Joseon’s main palace

  • Address: 161 Sajik-ro, Jongno-gu
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Joseon’s No. 1 palace, No. 1 photo spot for foreigners
  • Signature: 1395 Establishment·Geunjeongjeon·Gyeonghoeru·Guardian Changing Ceremony (10:00·14:00)
  • Admission fee: 3,000 won + Free if you wear Hanbok
  • Time: 09:00~18:00 (Closed on Tuesdays)

8. Changdeokgung ★ UNESCO 1997

  • Address: 99 Yulgok-ro, Jongno-gu
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — UNESCO World Heritage
  • Signature: 1405 Establishment·Nature-Friendly + Sponsorship (Biwon) Korea’s No. 1 Garden·Nakseonjae
  • Admission fee: KRW 3,000 + KRW 5,000 for sponsorship (advance reservation)
  • Time: 09:00~18:00 (Closed on Mondays)

9. Changgyeonggung Palace

  • Address: 185 Changgyeonggung-ro, Jongno-gu
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Japanese zoo → Korean restoration
  • Signature: Established in 1483, Japanese occupation 1909 Zoo → 1986 restored, nature-friendly garden
  • Admission fee: 1,000 won

10. Deoksugung ★ Modern Korea, Korean Empire

  • Address: 99 Sejong-daero, Jung-gu, adjacent to City Hall
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Modern Korea (1897 Korean Empire declared) deep dive
  • Signature: Seokjojeon (Western style, built in 1900)·Junghwajeon·Walk along Jeongdong-gil
  • Admission fee: 1,000 won
  • Time: 09:00~21:00 (Closed on Mondays)

12. Sungkyunkwan ★ Joseon’s No. 1 educational institution

  • Signature: Myeongnyundang·Daeseongjeon·Jonggyeonggak Korea’s No. 1 traditional architecture + No. 1 place for foreigners to take a walk in Hanbok
  • Admission Fee: Free
  • Details: #170·#204 sisters

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Free admission to palaces with Hanbok?: Gyeongbokgung Palace, Changdeokgung Palace, Changgyeonggung Palace, and Deoksugung Palace Free admission to all palaces with Hanbok.

2. UNESCO World Heritage: Changdeokgung Palace (1997), Jongmyo Shrine (1995), Seooreung (1995), Suwon Hwaseong Fortress (1997, Gyeonggi-do).

3. Guard Changing Ceremony: Gyeongbokgung Palace 10:00·14:00 + Deoksugung Palace 11:00·14:00·15:30. #1 photo spot for foreigners.

4. Temple stay available to foreigners: Jingwansa Temple, Bongeunsa Temple, and Gilsangsa Temple are all available in English. Advance reservation required.

5. Buddha’s Birthday Lantern Festival: Full set for May, April 8th of the lunar calendar + Jogyesa Temple, Jingwansa Temple, Bongeunsa Temple, etc.

6. Palace, Temple, Jongmyo 1-day course: Gyeongbokgung → Changdeokgung → Jongmyo → Jogyesa Temple 30-minute walk full set.
