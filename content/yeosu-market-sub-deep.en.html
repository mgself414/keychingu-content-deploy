Yeosu Market Guide — Dawn Gyodong Market, Yeosu Fish Market, the 4·9 Five-Day Seo Market, and the Seafood Specialty Market Sashimi-Restaurant Arcade

Subtitle: Yeosu Gyodong Market (여수 교동시장, 04:00–15:00) · Yeosu Fish Market (여수수산시장, 05:00–22:00, cards accepted) · Seo Market (서시장, 06:00–20:00, market days on dates ending in 4 or 9) · Yeosu Seafood Specialty Market (여수수산물특화시장, 09:00–20:00, raw-fish restaurant complex) · Yi Sun-sin Square (이순신광장) and Jinnamgwan (진남관) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Yeosu trips are usually remembered for the night sea, Nangman Pocha (낭만포차), and the sea cable car, but the markets are worth a daytime visit too. Yeosu city has Gyodong Market with long rows of dawn stalls, Yeosu Fish Market selling live fish and dried seafood, Seo Market with its own market days, and the Seafood Specialty Market, a sashimi-restaurant arcade that cooks live fish from tanks on the spot.

This guide lists the four markets in order of opening time, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Closed days differ from market to market, so check the date first.

🌅 Yeosu Gyodong Market — The Stall Market You Visit at Dawn

Yeosu Gyodong Market at 17 Gyodong Sijang 1-gil, Yeosu (교동시장1길 17), ☎ 061-666-3778, began as a small spot where fish caught at sea were laid out on stalls, and is now a permanent market nearly 1 km long. With more stalls than shops, it feels like a rural five-day market.

  • Opening hours: 04:00–15:00. Because it is a dawn market, many vendors finish for the day by 1–2 p.m. It is closed on the 2nd and 4th Sunday of each month.
  • What they sell: Fish and dried seafood are the core, with vegetables, fruit, and medicinal herbs too. Hairtail (갈치), yellow croaker (참조기), shrimp, clams, Yangtae (양태, bartail flathead), and Seodae (서대, tongue sole) are piled up at stall after stall.
  • How to buy: Many stalls pile fish in heaps rather than selling them one by one, and call out a price for the heap.
  • What to try: The tourism organization introduces Geumpungsaengi (금풍생이) as a fish famous in Yeosu for its taste.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available.

🐟 Yeosu Fish Market — Live Fish, Dried Seafood, and Food

Yeosu Fish Market at 24 Yeogaekseonteomineol-gil, Yeosu (여객선터미널길 24), ☎ 061-662-7268, is introduced by the tourism organization as a traditional seafood market with a 50-year history and the largest of Yeosu's 3 major fish markets. It completed modernization work in 2011.

  • Opening hours: 05:00–22:00. It is closed on the 2nd and 4th Tuesday of each month.
  • Zones: It is divided into live fish, dried seafood, and food. The live fish market uses seawater pumped up from 400 m underground.
  • Dried seafood: Seafood is prepared at a processing area on the market building's rooftop and dried in the sea breeze before sale.
  • Food market: It sells Yeosu specialties gat kimchi (갓김치, mustard greens kimchi) and jeotgal (젓갈, salted seafood).
  • Fish worth a look: Species rarely seen in markets around the Seoul area turn up, such as Mulmegi (물메기, snailfish), Bollak (볼락, rockfish), and Spanish mackerel (삼치) for sashimi.
  • Facilities: Cards accepted; restrooms and parking available.

🧺 Seo Market — 4·9 Market Days and the Food Alley

Seo Market at 16-6 Jwasuyeong-ro, Yeosu (좌수영로 16-6), ☎ Seo Market Merchants' Association 061-642-6720, is a traditional market that mainly sells seafood and clothing, near the Yeosu Coastal Ferry Terminal (여수연안여객선터미널) and the fisheries cooperative auction hall (수협 공판장). It has 207 shops.

  • Opening hours: 06:00–20:00.
  • Market days: The 4th, 9th, 14th, 19th, 24th, and 29th of each month. On non-market days, an afternoon market mostly runs.
  • Pungmul Street (풍물거리): Many visitors from outside the city come here looking for fish and raw fish.
  • Food alley: It sells gopchang jeongol (곱창전골, intestine hot pot), pork head meat, and jokbal (족발, braised pig's feet).

🍣 Yeosu Seafood Specialty Market — Tank-Side Sashimi Arcade

Yeosu Seafood Specialty Market at 60-31 Namsan-ro, Yeosu (남산로 60-31), ☎ 061-643-9944, is a raw-fish restaurant complex where you pick live fish, sea cucumber, octopus, sea squirt, and blue crab from the tanks and have them prepared on the spot. It has about 100 merchants, and seawater drawn up near Dolsan Bridge is purified and sterilized for use in the tanks.

  • Opening hours: 09:00–20:00. It is closed on the 1st and 3rd Tuesday of each month.
  • What they sell: Live fish, fresh (non-live) fish, dried fish, and shellfish.
  • Facilities: Parking is available.

🏯 Next to the Markets — Yi Sun-sin Square and Jinnamgwan

  • Yi Sun-sin Square (이순신광장, 74 Jungang-ro, Yeosu / 중앙로 74): Open at all times. It has a turtle ship (거북선) recreated close to the original form, and you can go inside. A Turtle Ship Festival is held every May.
  • Yeosu Jinnamgwan (여수 진남관, 11 Dongmun-ro, Yeosu / 동문로 11, ☎ 061-659-5710): A government guesthouse (객사) built in 1599 on the site Yi Sun-sin used as the main base of the Jeolla Left Naval Headquarters, and a National Treasure. It is the largest surviving single-story provincial government building. Open 09:00–18:00, year-round, with a relics exhibition hall. After a 10-year restoration, it reopened in May 2025 (per press reports).

🚉 Getting There and Nearby

  • The KTX departs from Yongsan Station and stops at Yeosu Expo Station (여수엑스포역). From there, take a city bus or taxi to the markets.
  • For the night sea, Nangman Pocha, Odongdo (오동도), and the cable car, see #396 Yeosu Guide, which covers operating hours and fares.
  • Tourism interpretation service ☎ 1330.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Visiting Gyodong Market late in the afternoon — It is a dawn market, so many vendors pack up by 1–2 p.m., and it closes at 15:00.
  2. Not knowing which markets close on Tuesdays — Yeosu Fish Market closes on the 2nd and 4th Tuesday, and the Seafood Specialty Market closes on the 1st and 3rd Tuesday.
  3. Knowing Seo Market's market days as only two dates a month — Market days fall on the 4th, 9th, 14th, 19th, 24th, and 29th of each month, on every date ending in 4 or 9. On non-market days, an afternoon market mostly runs.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. How many markets can I see in one day?

A. Visit Gyodong Market early in the morning, then move on to Yeosu Fish Market. If the date ends in 4 or 9, you can catch the Seo Market's market day on the same day.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. According to the tourism organization, cards work at Yeosu Fish Market. No card information is registered for Gyodong Market, Seo Market, or the Seafood Specialty Market.
