Why the Hangang Line by Subway — Five Scattered Parks on One String

Seoul's Hangang Parks (한강공원) are riverside retreats stretched along the river that cuts across the city — Yeouido (여의도) (cherry blossoms, fireworks festival), Banpo (반포) (Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (달빛무지개분수), Sebitseom (세빛섬)), Ttukseom (뚝섬) (picnics, waterside stage), Mangwon (망원) (local, hip), Jamsil (잠실) (Lotte World Tower (롯데월드타워) view). The problem: they are scattered east to west along the river. But every one of them sits within walking distance of a subway station, so you can string them together in one day on a single T-money card.

This guide is the third axis, orthogonal to the line-by-line series (#1241–#1249) and the hub series (#1250–#1254). "Hop across multiple lines and tie the Hangang (한강) into one" — it weaves together which park in which order, which station to get off at, and how to walk in, all organized around the theme. This is the second installment of the Subway Course Series (Phase 2, Themed Journeys).

5 Hangang Parks × Subway Access — Which Station to Get Off At

🌸 Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도한강공원) — Cherry Blossoms, Fireworks, Downtown Skyline

  • 🚇 Access: Line 5 Yeouinaru Station (여의나루역) Exits 2 & 3, right at the park · Line 5 & 9 Yeouido Station (여의도역), 10 min on foot
  • ✨ Highlights: Spring Cherry Blossom Festival (Yunjungno (윤중로)) · Autumn Seoul International Fireworks Festival · Bamdokkaebi Night Market (밤도깨비 야시장)-style street food (#904) · The Hyundai Seoul (더현대 서울) nearby
  • ⏱ Time needed: 1.5–2 hours
  • cross-ref: #586 Hangang (Yeouido) · #1244 Line 5 · #1251 Gongdeok (공덕) Hub (toward Yeouido)

⛲ Banpo Hangang Park (반포한강공원) — Moonlight Rainbow Fountain, Sebitseom

  • 🚇 Access: Line 3, 7 & 9 Express Bus Terminal Station (고속터미널역) Exit 8-1, 10 min on foot · Line 9 Sinbanpo Station (신반포역)
  • ✨ Highlights: Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (Banpo Bridge (반포대교), seasonal nighttime shows) · Sebitseom (night views) · Jamsugyo Bridge (잠수교) stroll
  • ⏱ Time needed: 1–1.5 hours (time it to the fountain schedule)
  • cross-ref: #1247 Line 9 (Express Bus Terminal) · #1238 Romantic Seoul Views

🧺 Ttukseom Hangang Park (뚝섬한강공원) — Picnics, Waterside Vibes

  • 🚇 Access: Line 7 Ttukseom Hangang Park Station (뚝섬유원지역) Exits 2 & 3, right at the park (the station is inside the park)
  • ✨ Highlights: Picnic lawns · Hangang ramyeon (한강라면) (convenience-store instant-ramyeon cookers) · Jabeolle (자벌레) observation & culture space · Seoul Forest (서울숲) & Seongsu (성수) nearby
  • ⏱ Time needed: 1–2 hours
  • cross-ref: #1246 Line 7 · #1250 Wangsimni (왕십리) Hub (toward Seongsu)

🌿 Mangwon Hangang Park (망원한강공원) — Local, Hip, Sunsets

  • 🚇 Access: Line 6 Mangwon Station (망원역) Exit 1, 10 min on foot (via Mangwon Market (망원시장))
  • ✨ Highlights: Grab takeout at Mangwon Market → riverside picnic · Sunset spot · Hongdae (홍대) & Yeonnam (연남) nearby
  • ⏱ Time needed: 1–1.5 hours
  • cross-ref: #1245 Line 6 (Mangwon) · #1253 Hongik Univ. (홍대입구) Hub

🗼 Jamsil Hangang Park (잠실한강공원) — Tower Views, River Cruise

  • 🚇 Access: Line 2 Jamsillaru Station (잠실나루역), 5 min on foot · Line 2 & 8 Jamsil Station (잠실역), 15 min on foot
  • ✨ Highlights: Lotte World Tower view · Hangang cruise dock · Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) nearby
  • ⏱ Time needed: 1–1.5 hours
  • cross-ref: #1241 Line 2 (Jamsil)

🎯 Common Hangang Tips — Before You Go

  • 🍜 Hangang ramyeon: The convenience-store instant-ramyeon cooker is a Hangang signature — kiosks at Ttukseom, Yeouido, and Banpo. Sorting your trash for recycling is a must.
  • 🧺 Picnic rentals: Plenty of mat and tent rental shops near the parks (seasonal). Use the food-delivery pickup zones.
  • 🚲 Ttareungi (따릉이) (bike share): Public bikes let you ride the riverside paths between parks — combine subway + bike.
  • 💡 Routing tip: Daytime parks (Yeouido, Ttukseom, Mangwon) → sunset (Mangwon) → night fountain & views (Banpo, Jamsil) is the time-of-day optimum.

3 Hangang Line Subway Traverse Courses

Course 1. West Hangang Day (Yeouido & Mangwon)

  • Yeouinaru (Yeouido cherry blossoms / Hangang ramyeon) → Line 6 Mangwon (market takeout → riverside sunset) → Hongdae (night, #1253)
  • fit: Best in spring and autumn. City views + local vibes + nightlife.

Course 2. Night-View Traverse (Ttukseom & Banpo)

  • Ttukseom Hangang Park Station (daytime picnic & Hangang ramyeon) → Line 7 → Line 9 Express Bus Terminal (Banpo Moonlight Rainbow Fountain & Sebitseom night views)
  • fit: Picnic + night views. Checking the fountain schedule is a must.

Course 3. East Hangang & Tower (Jamsil Axis)

  • Jamsillaru (Jamsil Hangang Park & river cruise) → Jamsil (Seokchon Lake & Lotte World Tower observatory)
  • fit: Tower views, cruise, shopping. Best for families.

5 Transfer & Usage Tips — Get 200% Out of the Hangang Line

Tip 1. Every Park Is on a Different Line

Yeouido = Line 5, Banpo = Line 3, 7 & 9, Ttukseom = Line 7, Mangwon = Line 6, Jamsil = Line 2. Sketch your course by line first.

Tip 2. Fountains and Sunsets Live or Die by Timing

The Banpo Moonlight Rainbow Fountain runs seasonally on a timetable; for the Mangwon sunset, arrive 30 minutes before sundown. Check the schedules first.

Tip 3. Market Takeout → Riverside Picnic

Grabbing takeout at Mangwon Market (#1245) or Tongin Market (통인시장) and eating by the river is the local way. Take your trash back with you.

Tip 4. Summer Nights, Winter Days

Summer means night river breezes; winter means comfortable daytime walks. Flip your time slots by season.

Tip 5. One Transit Card for Everything

A single T-money covers every line to all 5 parks. Ttareungi (bike share) pairs in via app payment too.

FAQ 6 — Hangang Line Questions Foreigners Ask Most

Q1. Can I cover all the Hangang Parks in one day?

  • All 5 is a stretch — a combo of 2–3 is realistic (west: Yeouido & Mangwon / east: Ttukseom & Jamsil / night views: Banpo).

Q2. How is this different from the line-by-line and hub series?

  • Line-by-line = "one line, vertical", hub = "one station, radial"; this installment = "hopping across multiple lines to string the Hangang theme" = the third axis.

Q3. What is Hangang ramyeon?

  • Ramyeon you cook yourself in a convenience-store instant-ramyeon cooker — the Hangang picnic signature. Kiosks at Ttukseom and Yeouido.

Q4. When does the Moonlight Rainbow Fountain run?

  • Seasonal, nighttime timetable operation (suspended in rain). Checking the official schedule before visiting is a must.

Q5. Which park is the best?

  • First visit = Yeouido (access, sights), local vibes = Mangwon, picnic = Ttukseom, night views = Banpo.

Q6. Can I connect them by bike?

  • Yes. Ttareungi (public bike share) links the riverside bike paths — subway + bike is the efficient combo.
