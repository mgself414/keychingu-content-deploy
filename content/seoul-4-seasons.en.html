⚠ 4 Seasons 3 mistakes

1. "Spring = cherry blossoms and palaces only" — spring hanbok + spring flower hiking cluster ignorance: Spring movement = only cherry blossoms and palaces recognized. In reality, it is Yeouido + Namsan + Bukhansan Spring Flower Hiking + Spring Hanbok Full Experience Matrix.

2. "Summer = Han River Bay" — Busan, Yeosu, Gangneung KTX unknown: Summer movement = recognition of the Han River Bay. In fact, you can experience the full summer experience on KTX 1~3h to Haeundae in Busan, the night sea in Yeosu, and Gangneung.

3. "Winter = illumination only" — Pyeongchang/Gangwon skiing ignorance: Winter = only illumination recognized. In fact, Taebaek·Pyeongchang·Gangwon skiing (KTX 2~3h, Pyeongchang 2018 Winter Olympics facility) Enthusiast full experience.
