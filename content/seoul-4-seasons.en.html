🎯 Why "Seoul 4 Seasons" Meta?

Foreign visitors to Korea have distinct seasonal motivations — Spring (March-May): Cherry Blossom Enthusiasts / Summer (June-August): Hangang River (한강) & Beaches / Autumn (September-November): Autumn Foliage / Winter (December-February): Illuminations & Snow. All 8 segments crave a full seasonal signature experience.

This guide presents a 4 Seasons × 4 Signatures = 16 Matrix + seasonal courses. KeyChinGu Category Meta Series Part 8 complete.

⚠ 3 Common "4 Seasons" Mistakes

1. "Spring = Cherry Blossoms & Palaces Only" — Missing the Spring Hanbok (한복) + Spring Flower Hiking Cluster: Many perceive spring itineraries as limited to cherry blossoms and palaces. In reality, it's a full experience matrix including Yeouido (여의도) + Namsan (남산) + Bukhansan (북한산) Spring Flower Hiking + Spring Hanbok (한복).

2. "Summer = Hangang River Only" — Missing Busan (부산), Yeosu (여수), & Gangneung (강릉) via KTX: Summer itineraries are often seen as only involving the Hangang River (한강). Actually, a full summer experience is possible, extending to Busan Haeundae (부산 해운대), Yeosu Night Sea (여수밤바다), and Gangneung (강릉) within 1-3 hours via KTX.

3. "Winter = Illuminations Only" — Missing Pyeongchang (평창) & Gangwon (강원) Skiing: Winter is often associated solely with illuminations. In fact, Taebaek (태백), Pyeongchang (평창), & Gangwon (강원) Skiing (2-3 hours via KTX, utilizing Pyeongchang 2018 Winter Olympics facilities) offers a full experience for enthusiasts.

[Autumn September-November] Foliage & Hiking Enthusiasts

  • 1 Week: Namsan (남산) + Inwangsan (인왕산) + Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) + Bukhansan (북한산) & Dobongsan (도봉산) + Seoul Forest (서울숲)
