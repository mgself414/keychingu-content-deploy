3. Foreign fit tips

English menu availability

  • Top: Moulin de Lee · Eat & Go (foreign regular · picture menu)
  • Middle class: Shaved ice specialty (tourist attraction/picture menu)
  • Local: Seongsim rice cake mill, walnut snack, Yakgwa, Gangjeong long-established store (picture·translation app)

Foreign persona fit

  • K-pop fan: Instagram visual rice cake and dessert (Moulandrie and shaved ice)
  • traditional culture lover: Long-established orthodox (Seongsim Tteok Mill, Gangjeong, Yakgwa)
  • food explorer: hop on all stores
  • family·group: Eat & Go take-out + market experience

Price range

  • 1 pc of rice cake: 1,000~3,000 won
  • Yakgwa·Gangjeong small box: KRW 5,000~12,000
  • Shaved Ice: KRW 9,000~18,000 (per serving)
  • 1 bag of walnut snacks: 5,000~8,000 won

Experience fit

  • Old-established atmosphere: Seongsim Rice Cake Mill (1st generation long-established store)
  • Insta: Specializing in moulin de li and shaved ice
  • Gifts/Souvenirs: Yakgwa/Gangjeong (foreigner ke-gift)
