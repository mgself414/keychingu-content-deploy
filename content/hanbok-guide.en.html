33_Hanbok Experience – Complete Guide to Gyeongbokgung (경복궁)

One-Line Intro

Wear hanbok at Gyeongbokgung (경복궁): free palace entry + traditional costume experience in one go.

Dress in traditional Korean hanbok (한복) and get free admission to the palace — the most complete way to experience Korean heritage.

Who This Is For

  • Travelers who want to experience Korean traditional culture firsthand and take commemorative photos
  • Travelers looking for that perfect Instagram-worthy aesthetic shot

Recommended Itinerary (4 Hours)

1. 09:30 Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역), Line 3, Exit 5: Head to the Hanbok Rental Village (한복 대여촌) to start your rental

2. 10:00 Hanbok rental (15,000–25,000 KRW / 4 hrs): Pick your style; hairpins and norigae (노리개, traditional ornaments) included

3. 10:30 Enter Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) (free in hanbok): Enter through the main gate, Gwanghwamun (광화문)

4. 10:45 Photos in front of Geunjeongjeon Hall (근정전) (20 min): The most popular photo spot in the palace

5. 11:15 Stroll around Gyeonghoeru Pavilion (경회루) pond (30 min): Stunning hanbok photos with the pond as your backdrop

6. 12:00 National Folk Museum of Korea (국립민속박물관) (45 min): Browse exhibits on traditional Korean culture

7. 13:00 Head toward Bukchon (북촌) (optional): Visit Samcheong-dong (삼청동) cafés and Bukchon (북촌) still in your hanbok

8. 14:00 Return hanbok: Head back to your rental shop

Tips for International Visitors

  • Useful Korean Phrases
  • "Hanbok billigo sipeoyo (한복 빌리고 싶어요)" → "I'd like to rent a hanbok"
  • "S/M/L saijeuu juseyo (S/M/L 사이즈 주세요)" → "Please give me size S/M/L"
  • "Pohamddweo isseoyo? (포함돼 있어요?)" → "Is this included in the price?"
  • Cultural Etiquette: No food or drinks allowed inside Gyeongbokgung (경복궁). Avoid running or making loud noises. Hanbok skirts can drag on unpaved paths — skip the high heels.
  • Payment: Hanbok rentals accept both card and cash. Palace admission can be paid with T-money or card.
  • Best Time for Photos: 09:30–11:00 right after opening — fewer crowds and beautiful morning light.

English Guide (Simple English Summary)

Renting a hanbok (한복, traditional Korean dress) and visiting Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) is the most popular cultural experience in Seoul (서울). Hanbok rental shops cluster near the East Gate, with prices starting at 15,000 KRW for a 4-hour rental including accessories. Wearing hanbok grants free palace entry (saving 3,000 KRW). The best photo spots are in front of Geunjeongjeon Hall (근정전) and the Gyeonghoeru Pavilion (경회루) pond. Visit on weekday mornings to avoid crowds — the palace is closed on Tuesdays.

Estimated Total Cost

  • Budget: 15,000 KRW (basic hanbok rental + free palace entry)
  • Standard: 30,000 KRW (premium hanbok rental + café + printed photos)
  • Luxury: 80,000 KRW (hanbok rental + Gyeonghoeru (경회루) interior tour + brunch in Samcheong-dong (삼청동))
