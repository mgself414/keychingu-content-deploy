⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Korean Baseball = Same as MLB": KBO = Korea's #1 authentic cheering culture (cheering songs · cheering squad · chimaek (치맥, chicken and beer) · outfield seats). The #1 difference from MLB = a top culture shock for foreign vlog enthusiasts.

2. "Jamsil (잠실) = One stadium for LG/Doosan (두산) = One team daily": Jamsil Baseball Stadium (잠실 야구장) = shared by LG and Doosan (두산) (Korea's only stadium shared by two teams). It's okay for LG → Doosan (두산) → LG on alternating days at the same stadium.

3. "Foreigner tickets = Difficult to book": KBO foreigner tickets = Klook · Interpark · Trip.com English full set. Booking 1 week in advance is OK + Korean Series (한국시리즈) sells out 1 month in advance. Outfield seats are #1 for cheering enthusiasts + chimaek (치맥) OK.

4. "Chimaek (치맥) = Outfield only": KBO chimaek (치맥) + foreign enthusiasts = OK in both outfield and infield seats + some stadiums allow outside food + KBO BBQ + a variety of chicken, beer, and jjajangmyeon (짜장면, black bean noodles).

5. "Playoffs = Foreigners can't watch": KBO Playoffs · Korean Series (한국시리즈) (October-November) = #1 inbound for foreigners + Korea's top sports event + Klook · Interpark foreigner booking OK 1 week in advance.
