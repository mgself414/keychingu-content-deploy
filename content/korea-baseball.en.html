⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Korean baseball = same as MLB": KBO = No. 1 in authentic Korean cheering culture (cheering song, cheering squad, chicken and beer, outfield). No. 1 difference in MLB = No. 1 foreign vlog mania culture shock.

2. "Jamsil = LG/Doosan one stadium = 1 team every day": Jamsil Baseball Stadium = LG and Doosan shared (Korea's only stadium shared by 2 teams). Same stadium LG → Doosan → LG every other day OK.

3. "Foreigner ticket = Reservation difficult": KBO foreigner ticket = Klook·Interpark·Trip.com English full set. Reservation OK 1 week in advance + Korean series sold out 1 month in advance. No. 1 outfield cheering enthusiast + Chicken and beer OK.

4. "Chicken and Beer = Outfield Only": KBO Chicken and Beer + Foreign Mania = Both outfield and infield OK + Some food brought in from outside the stadium + KBO BBQ + Various chicken, beer, and black bean noodles.

5. "Playoffs = Foreigners cannot watch": KBO Playoffs·Korean Series (October~November) = No. 1 inbound for foreigners + No. 1 sports event in Korea + Klook·Interpark Foreigners can make reservations 1 week in advance.
