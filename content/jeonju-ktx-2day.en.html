Jeonju KTX 2-Day Full Guide for Foreign Travelers

> The Mecca of Korean Cuisine and Hanok (한옥) traditional houses — 1.8 hours from Seoul (서울) by KTX → Hanok Village (한옥마을) + Bibimbap (비빔밥), Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥), and Makgeolli (막걸리) Matrix

Jeonju (전주) is a UNESCO City of Gastronomy (Creative Cities Network) and home to over 800 hanok (한옥, traditional Korean houses). It's a top 1-night, 2-day destination for foreign travelers interested in Korean food and tradition. This guide provides a foreigner-friendly standard 2-day itinerary and 10 recommended spots.

1. How to Get to Jeonju (전주)

Option Duration Price Recommendation
KTX to Iksan (익산) + Intercity Bus (from Seoul Station (서울역)) 1h 50m + 30m ₩35,000 (~$26 / ~180 RMB / ~3,700 JPY) ★★★ Fastest
KTX to Jeonju (전주) (Honam Line) 2h 0m ₩34,800 Direct
Intercity Bus (from Gangnam (강남)) 3h ₩17,800 Budget-friendly / Late-night

Booking: Korail English (https://www.letskorail.com/)

2. Standard 2-Day Itinerary

Day 1 (Arrival + Hanok Village (한옥마을) + Night View)

  • 07:00 AM: Seoul Station (서울역) KTX Honam Line
  • 09:00 AM: Transfer at Iksan Station (익산역) or arrive at Jeonju Station (전주역)
  • 10:00 AM: Arrive at Hanok Village (한옥마을)
  • 10:30 AM: Gyeonggijeon (경기전) + Royal Portrait Museum (어진박물관) (Portrait of King Taejo Yi Seong-gye (이성계) of Joseon (조선))
  • 12:30 PM: Jeonju Bibimbap (전주비빔밥) Lunch (Gajok Hoegwan (가족회관), Gogung (고궁), and other main branches)
  • 02:00 PM: Stroll through Hanok Village (한옥마을) + Hanbok (한복, traditional Korean attire) Experience + Traditional Craft Workshop
  • 04:00 PM: Jaman Mural Village (자만벽화마을) (Photo-friendly)
  • 06:00 PM: Makgeolli Alley (막걸리 골목) (Samcheondong (삼천동)) Dinner + Makgeolli Han Sang (막걸리 한 상, a set meal with various dishes served with makgeolli)
  • 09:00 PM: Hanok Stay (한옥 stay, stay in a traditional Korean house) Check-in

Day 2 (Breakfast + Gaeksa (객사) / Art / Departure)

  • 08:00 AM: Hanok Stay (한옥 stay) Breakfast (traditional Korean meal)
  • 09:30 AM: Pungnammun Gate (풍남문) + Gaekridan-gil (객리단길) (trendy cafes and desserts)
  • 11:30 AM: Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥, a hearty bean sprout soup with rice) (Hyeondaeok (현대옥), Sambaejip (삼백집), Waeng-ijip (왱이집) — Jeonju's 3 best)
  • 01:00 PM: Jeondong Catholic Cathedral (전동성당) (Korean Catholic pilgrimage site, built in 1908)
  • 02:30 PM: Nambu Market (남부시장) (fish market, souvenirs, Cheongnyeon Mall (청년몰, Youth Mall, a section of traditional markets featuring young entrepreneurs and trendy shops))
  • 04:00 PM: Jeonju Station (전주역)
  • 04:30 PM: KTX to Seoul (서울)
  • 06:30 PM: Arrive at Seoul Station (서울역)

3. 10 Foreigner-Friendly Spots & Experiences

Food (Jeonju's Signature Gastronomy)

1. Gajok Hoegwan (가족회관) — Top choice for Jeonju Bibimbap (전주비빔밥) (chef-recommended, picture menu)

2. Gogung (고궁) — Bibimbap (비빔밥) fine dining (popular with foreign visitors)

3. Sambaejip (삼백집) — Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥) nofo (노포, a traditional restaurant with 30+ years of history) (since 1947)

4. Waeng-ijip (왱이집) — Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥) (local favorite)

5. Samcheondong Makgeolli Alley (삼천동 막걸리 골목) — Makgeolli Han Sang (막걸리 한 상) (₩30,000~50,000 (~$22-37 / ~160-260 RMB / ~3,200-5,300 JPY) per set)

Hanok (한옥) & Experiences

6. Gyeonggijeon (경기전) — Royal Portrait of King Taejo (태조) of Joseon (조선) (English audio guide)

7. Hanok Village (한옥마을) Hanbok (한복) Experience — Hanbok (한복) for 1 hour ₩10,000~20,000 (~$7-15 / ~50-100 RMB / ~1,100-2,100 JPY)

8. Jaman Mural Village (자만벽화마을) — Photo-friendly, Instagram signature spot

9. Jeondong Catholic Cathedral (전동성당) — Korean Catholic pilgrimage site, Romanesque architecture

10. Nambu Market (남부시장) Cheongnyeon Mall (청년몰) — Trendy cafes, workshops, souvenirs

4. Foreigner-Friendly Tips

English Accessibility

  • High: Gyeonggijeon (경기전) (English audio guide), Gajok Hoegwan (가족회관), Gogung (고궁) (foreigner-friendly, picture menu)
  • Medium-High: Hanok Village (한옥마을) (English signboards), Jeondong Catholic Cathedral (전동성당)
  • Local: Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥) nofo (노포), Makgeolli Alley (막걸리 골목) (picture menu, translation app recommended)

Budget (1 person, 2 days/1 night)

  • KTX Round trip: ₩70,000
  • Accommodation 1 night (Hanok Stay (한옥 stay)): ₩90,000~250,000
  • Meals (4): ₩60,000~120,000 (Makgeolli Han Sang (막걸리 한 상) ₩30,000+)
  • Admission Fees & Experiences (Hanbok (한복) + Gyeonggijeon (경기전)): ₩30,000~50,000
  • Local Transportation (taxi, walking): ₩20,000
  • Total Average ₩270,000~510,000 (~$200-380 / ~1,400-2,700 RMB / ~28,000-54,000 JPY)

By Persona

  • US Persona: Bibimbap (비빔밥) + Hanbok (한복) Experience + Hanok Village (한옥마을) Stroll (Day 1) → Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥) + Jeondong Catholic Cathedral (전동성당) (Day 2)
  • European Persona: Gyeonggijeon (경기전) + Hanok Stay (한옥 stay) 1 night + Makgeolli Alley (막걸리 골목) (Day 1) → Gaekridan-gil (객리단길) Cafe + Nambu Market (남부시장) (Day 2)
  • Foodie: Gajok Hoegwan (가족회관) → Makgeolli Alley (막걸리 골목) → Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥) nofo (노포) → Gaekridan-gil (객리단길) Desserts (4-hour gourmet hop)
  • Photo-Friendly: Jaman Mural Village (자만벽화마을) + Hanok Village (한옥마을) + Hanbok (한복) + Jeondong Catholic Cathedral (전동성당)
  • K-drama Fan: Hanok Village (한옥마을) (numerous drama filming locations)

Hanok Stay (한옥 stay) Recommendations

  • Luxury: Lagung (라궁) (Hanok (한옥) Hotel, foreigner-friendly)
  • Mid-Range: Insahanida (인사하니다), Guest Hanok (게스트 한옥)
  • Budget: Hanok Village (한옥마을) Guesthouse (₩50,000~80,000 (~$37-60 / ~260-420 RMB / ~5,300-8,500 JPY) / night)

By Season

  • Spring (April-May): Hanok Village (한옥마을) + Jaman Mural Village (자만벽화마을) (cherry blossoms)
  • Summer (June-August): Cool Hanok Stay (한옥 stay) + Gaekridan-gil (객리단길) Night View
  • Autumn (October-November): Hanok Village (한옥마을) Autumn Foliage ★ Best
  • Winter (December-February): Hanok Stay (한옥 stay) (ondol (온돌, traditional Korean underfloor heating system)) + Makgeolli (막걸리) + Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥)
