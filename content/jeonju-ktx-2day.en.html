Jeonju KTX 1 night 2 days full guide for foreigners

Mecca of Korean cuisine and Hanok — Seoul KTX 1.8 hours → Hanok Village + Bibimbap, Bean Sprout Gukbap, Makgeolli Matrix

Jeonju is a UNESCO food city (Creative Cities Network) + has over 800 Hanok villages. The #1 destination for foreign gourmet and traditional personas for 2 days and 1 night. This guide is suitable for foreigners and includes a standard route for 2 days and 1 night + 10 stores.

1. How to get to Jeonju

Options Take price Recommended
KTX Iksan + Intercity Bus (Seoul Station) 1h 50m + 30m ₩35,000 ★★★ Fastest
KTX Jeonju (Honam Line) 2h 0m ₩34,800 Direct
Intercity Bus (Gangnam) 3h ₩17,800 Low budget/late night

Reservation: Korail English (https://www.letskorail.com/)

2. Standard 2 days and 1 night itinerary

Day 1 (Arrival + Hanok Village + Night View)

  • 07:00 Seoul Station KTX Honam Line
  • 09:00 Transfer to Iksan Station or Jeonju Station
  • 10:00 Arrive at Hanok Village
  • 10:30 Gyeonggijeon + Portrait Museum (Portrait of King Taejo of the Joseon Dynasty Lee Seong-gye)
  • 12:30 Jeonju bibimbap lunch (family center, old palace, etc. main branch)
  • 14:00 Walk around Hanok Village + Hanbok Experience + Traditional Workshop
  • 16:00 Jaman Mural Village (photo fit)
  • 18:00 Makgeolli Alley (Samcheon-dong) Dinner + One Table of Makgeolli
  • 21:00 Hanok stay check-in

Day 2 (Breakfast + guest house, art, return)

  • 08:00 Hanok stay breakfast (traditional Korean food)
  • 09:30 Pungnammun + Gaekridan-gil (Trendy cafe and dessert)
  • 11:30 Kongnamul Gukbap (Hyundai Ok, Sambaekjip, Waengijip — Jeonju 3rd place)
  • 13:00 Jeondong Cathedral (Korean Catholic holy site, built in 1908)
  • 14:30 Nambu Market (Fish Market, Souvenirs, Youth Mall)
  • 16:00 Jeonju Station
  • 16:30 KTX bound for Seoul
  • 18:30 Arrival at Seoul Station

3. 10 stores and experiences suitable for foreigners

Food (Jeonju gourmet signature)

1. Family Center — Jeonju Bibimbap No. 1 (chef recommendation/picture menu)

2. Foreign Palace — Bibimbap fine dining (foreign regulars)

3. Sambaekjip — Long-established bean sprout soup (1947~)

4. Waengijip — Bean sprout soup (recommended by locals)

5. Samcheon-dong Makgeolli Alley — One Prize of Makgeolli (₩30,000~50,000 per Prize)

Hanok/Experience

6. Gyeonggijeon — King Taejo of the Joseon Dynasty (English audio)

7. Hanok Village Hanbok Experience — Hanbok 1 hour ₩10,000~20,000

8. Jaman Mural Village — Photo fit·Insta signature

9. Jeondong Cathedral — Korean Catholic shrine, Romanesque architecture

10. Nambu Market Youth Mall — Trendy cafes, workshops, souvenirs

4. Foreign fit tips

English language availability

  • Top: Gyeonggijeon (English audio), Family Hall, Old Palace (picture menu frequented by foreigners)
  • Slander: Hanok Village (English sign)·Jeondong Cathedral
  • Local: Long-established bean sprout soup/makgeolli alley (picture menu/translation app)

Budget (2 days and 1 night per person)

  • KTX round trip: ₩70,000
  • Accommodation for 1 night (Hanok stay): ₩90,000~250,000
  • 4 meals: ₩60,000~120,000 (a bowl of makgeolli ₩30,000+)
  • Entrance fee/experience (Hanbok + game): ₩30,000~50,000
  • City transportation (taxi, walking): ₩20,000
  • Total average ₩270,000~510,000

By persona

  • American Persona: Bibimbap + Hanbok experience + Hanok village walk (Day 1) → Bean sprout soup + Jeondong Cathedral (Day 2)
  • European Persona: Gyeonggijeon + Hanok stay for 1 night + Makgeolli Alley (Day 1) → Gaekridan-gil Cafe + Nambu Market (Day 2)
  • foodie: Family Center → Makgeolli Alley → Long-established bean sprout soup → Gaekridan-gil dessert (4-hour gourmet hop)
  • Photo fit: Jaman Mural Village + Hanok Village + Hanbok + Jeondong Cathedral
  • K-drama fan: Hanok Village (filming location for many dramas)

Hanok stay recommended

  • Luxury: Ragung (Hanok hotel, fit for foreigners)
  • Intermediate: Greetings/Guest Hanok
  • Low Budget: Hanok Village Guesthouse (₩50,000~80,000/night)

By season

  • Spring (April-May): Hanok Village + Jaman Mural Village (cherry blossoms)
  • Summer (June-August): Hanok stay cool + Gaekridan-gil night view
  • Fall (October-November): Fall foliage in Hanok Village ★ Best
  • Winter (December-February): Hanok stay (Ondol) + Makgeolli + Bean sprout soup
