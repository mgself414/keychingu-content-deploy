⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Hongdae = food, cafes, clubs only" — Select Shop·Lifestyle Pass

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. Hongdae is perceived only as a food, cafe, and nightlife area. In reality, Hongdae and Seogyo are the birthplace of Korean lifestyle and select shops — Object (a new lifestyle brand in the 2010s), vintage and vintage alleys, and K-beauty megastores are concentrated within walking distance. Adding one select shop to Korean food + cafes doubles the completeness of the Hongdae movement line.

2. K-beauty=Pattern that only goes to Myeongdong and Olive Young main stores

The No. 1 K-beauty destination for foreigners is Myeongdong and Olive Young’s main branch. In reality, Hongdae Olive Young Town (megastore concept) + Trend Pot (K-pop collaboration limited store) has a different tone from Myeong-dong — Trend Pot is a limited edition K-pop collaboration store with BTS, New Jeans, etc., so it is more valuable than Myeong-dong for K-pop fans. Instead of just going to Myeongdong, we recommend adding the Hongdae course.

3. I don’t know where the vintage/second hand shop is + Business hours are 14:00~22:00

Vintage stores in Hongdae area include hidden gems that are not widely recognized by foreigners, such as Vintage Santa and Omni People Series. It is difficult to find as a walk-in as it is distributed in the alleys of Donggyo and Yeonnam. Also, business hours are mostly 14:00~22:00 (not open in the morning) — It is recommended that the morning course be divided into a bakery/cafe course, and after 14:00 p.m., a vintage/editorial shop course.

Scenery of Hongdae, Seogyo, and Yeonnam select shops

Hongdae has emerged as the home of Korean lifestyle and select shops since the late 2010s. Its strengths are local, trendy, and reasonably priced, which differentiates it from luxury boutiques in Gangnam and Cheongdam and industrial select shops in Seongsu.

Object (established in 2014) is a leading Korean lifestyle select shop brand with branches in Hongdae, Seogyo, Samcheong, Seongsu, and Gangnam. Elta Object (sub-brand, kitchen appliances) and Object Seogyo Branch (home and daily necessities) in this guide are the strongest signals in the cluster with 20 foreign reviews each.

Hongdae Vintage Alley (Vintage Santa, Omni People Series, ID Vintage, etc.) has little awareness of foreign fashion, but is the home of Korean vintage fashion. Distributed at 50m intervals in Yeonnam and Seogyo alleys.

Olive Young Hongdae Town is a megastore concept that is different from regular Olive Young branches — the store area, SKU, and experience corner are all 2-3 times that of regular branches. Trend Pot is a K-pop collaboration store where you can directly purchase limited edition goods such as BTS, New Jeans, and Seventeen.

Sangsang Madang Design Square (operated by KT&G) combines a Korean design gallery + lifestyle store + cafe — the #1 introduction to Korean design for foreigners.

① Object signature (2 places)

1. Elta Object

  • Signature: Hongdae lifestyle and kitchenware select shop. Object sub-brand — 20 foreign reviews (Phase 2 cluster strongest signal).
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Abundant foreigner reviews, Hongdae main route
  • Recommended categories: Korean lifestyle/kitchenware, signature products, Korean design goods
  • Tip: Walking distance from Hongik University Station. 1st place in foreign recognition among Object series stores. The store is not large, so it's perfect for browsing within an hour.

2. Object Seogyo

  • Signature: Seogyo home and daily necessities select shop. Object Bone Lineup — 20 foreign reviews.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Abundant reviews from foreigners, Seogyo Trendy Alley
  • Recommended Category: Home & Living Goods, Korean Lifestyle Goods, Signature Miscellaneous Goods
  • Tip: 8 minutes walk from Hongik University Station, inside Seogyo alley. 10-minute walk from Elta Object — Two store bundle course available.

② Vintage hidden gems (2 locations)

3. Vintage Santa

  • Signature: Hongdae old clothing hidden gem. 10 foreign reviews — Entering Korean vintage fashion.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Abundant foreigner reviews, vintage concept
  • Recommended Category: American/European vintage clothing, 80s-90s limited edition, denim/outerwear
  • Tip: Walking distance from Hongik University Station. Opening 14:00-22:00 Recommended. Vintage stores have large fluctuations in size and inventory — pay for items you like right away.

4. Omni People Heavy

  • Signature: Hongdae vintage/sale series store. Focused on American heavy vintage.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★☆☆ — Vintage concept, Korean-focused service
  • Recommended Category: Heavy vintage (military/workwear), American 80s and 90s goods
  • Tip: Walking distance from Hongik University Station. Same street as Omni People Trendy (sister store) — 2 store bundles available.

③ K-beauty·Olive Young (2 locations)

5. Olive Young Hongdae Town

  • Signature: Hongdae K-beauty Megastore. Area/SKU 2-3 times larger than regular branches. 10 foreign reviews.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Megastore + English service + No. 1 K-beauty for foreigners
  • Recommended Category: Korean K-beauty full set (skincare, makeup, body), signature limited edition
  • Tip: 3 minutes walk from Exit 9 of Hongik University Station. First recommendation for foreigners’ first K-beauty store — The store is larger and quieter than Myeong-dong.

6. Olive Young Trend Pot by Olive Young Hongdae

  • Signature: K-pop collaboration limited store — Limited edition goods such as BTS, New Jeans, and Seventeen can be purchased directly.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — No. 1 in K-pop fan movement, English response
  • Recommended Category: K-pop collaboration cosmetics, limited edition goods, season limited packages
  • Tip: Walking distance from Hongik University Station. If you are a K-pop fan, do not go to Myeongdong, but give priority to Trend Pot — Many limited edition collaboration items can only be purchased at Trend Pot.

④ Design·Lifestyle (2 locations)

7. Sangsang Madang Design Square

  • Signature: Korean design gallery + lifestyle store + cafe operated by KT&G. 10 foreign reviews.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Design concept + English menu
  • Recommended Category: Korean design goods, visual design publications, gallery exhibition viewing
  • Tip: 7 minutes walk from Hongik University Station. A live hall and cinema next to Design Square are also connected — a design + performance + movie combo course is possible.

8. Life & Collect

  • Signature: Hongdae visual design and lifestyle curation store. 7 foreign reviews.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★☆☆ — Visual design concept, focus on Korean language response
  • Recommended Category: Visual design goods, lifestyle curation, Korean designer collaboration
  • Tip: Walking distance from Hongik University Station. A hidden gem for foreigners looking for Korean design goods.
