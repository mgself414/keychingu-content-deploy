⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by International Tourists

1. "Hongdae (홍대) = Food, Cafes, Clubs Only" — Skipping Select Shops & Lifestyle Stores

This is the most common mistake in foreign tourist itineraries. Many perceive Hongdae (홍대) only as an area for food, cafes, and nightlife. In reality, Hongdae (홍대) and Seogyo (서교) are actually the birthplace of Korean lifestyle and select shops (편집샵) — with Object (오브젝트) (a nascent lifestyle brand from the 2010s), vintage and second-hand clothing alleys (골목), and K-beauty megastores all concentrated within walking distance. Adding just one select shop (편집샵) to your Korean food + cafe itinerary will double the completeness of your Hongdae (홍대) experience.

2. K-beauty = Only Going to Myeongdong (명동) & Main Olive Young Stores

The #1 K-beauty itinerary for international visitors is often Myeongdong (명동) and the main Olive Young (올리브영) store. However, Olive Young Hongdae Town (올리브영 홍대 타운) (a megastore concept) + Trend Pot (트렌드팟) (a K-pop collaboration limited store) in Hongdae (홍대) offer a different vibe from Myeongdong (명동). Trend Pot (트렌드팟), in particular, is a K-pop collaboration limited edition store featuring BTS, NewJeans, etc., making it more valuable for K-pop fans than Myeongdong (명동). We recommend adding Hongdae (홍대) to your course instead of only visiting Myeongdong (명동).

3. Not Knowing Where Vintage & Second-hand Stores Are + Not Considering 14:00-22:00 Operating Hours

Vintage stores in the Hongdae (홍대) area, such as Vintage Santa (빈티지산타) and Omni People (옴니피플) series, are hidden gems with lower recognition among international visitors. They are scattered within the Donggyo (동교) and Yeonnam (연남) alleys (골목), making them hard to discover by simply walking by. Also, most operating hours are 14:00-22:00 (2 PM - 10 PM) (they don't open in the morning) — we recommend separating your morning course for bakeries and cafes, and your afternoon course (after 2 PM) for vintage and select shops (편집샵).

The Select Shop (편집샵) Landscape of Hongdae (홍대), Seogyo (서교), and Yeonnam (연남)

The Hongdae (홍대) area has emerged as the hub (본거지) of Korean lifestyle and select shops (편집샵) since the late 2010s. Its strength lies in being local, trendy, and reasonably priced, differentiating it from the luxury boutiques of Gangnam (강남) and Cheongdam (청담), or the industrial select shops (편집샵) of Seongsu (성수).

Object (오브젝트) (established 2014) is a representative brand of Korean lifestyle select shops (편집샵) — with branches in Hongdae (홍대), Seogyo (서교), Samcheong (삼청), Seongsu (성수), and Gangnam (강남). Elta Object (엘타오브젝트) (a sub-brand, kitchenware) and Object Seogyo (오브젝트 서교점) (home & living goods) in this guide show the strongest cluster signal with 20 foreign reviews each.

The Hongdae (홍대) Vintage Alley (골목) (including Vintage Santa (빈티지산타), Omni People (옴니피플) series, and ID Vintage (아이디빈티지)) is the home of Korean second-hand fashion, though it has low recognition among international fashion enthusiasts. They are distributed every 50m in the Yeonnam (연남) and Seogyo (서교) alleys (골목).

Olive Young Hongdae Town (올리브영 홍대 타운) is a megastore concept, different from regular Olive Young (올리브영) branches — its store area, SKU, and experience corners are 2-3 times larger than typical branches. Trend Pot (트렌드팟) is a K-pop collaboration limited store where you can directly purchase limited edition goods from BTS, NewJeans, SEVENTEEN, and more.

Sangsang Madang Design Square (상상마당 디자인스퀘어) (operated by KT&G) combines a Korean design gallery + lifestyle store + cafe — making it the #1 entry point for international visitors to Korean design.

① Object Signature (2 Places)

1. Elta Object (엘타오브젝트)

  • Signature: A lifestyle and kitchenware select shop (편집샵) in Hongdae (홍대). A sub-brand of Object (오브젝트) — 20 foreign reviews (strongest signal in Phase 2 cluster).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Abundant foreign reviews, on Hongdae's (홍대) main thoroughfare.
  • Recommended Categories: Korean lifestyle·kitchenware, signature products, Korean design goods.
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Hongik University Station (홍대입구역). Ranks #1 in foreign recognition among Object (오브젝트) series stores. The store isn't large, so it's suitable for browsing within an hour.

2. Object Seogyo (오브젝트 서교점)

  • Signature: A home and living goods select shop (편집샵) in Seogyo (서교). Object's (오브젝트) main lineup — 20 foreign reviews.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Abundant foreign reviews, in a trendy Seogyo (서교) alley (골목).
  • Recommended Categories: Home·living goods, Korean lifestyle goods, signature sundries.
  • Tip: An 8-minute walk from Hongik University Station (홍대입구역), located in a Seogyo (서교) alley (골목). A 10-minute walk from Elta Object (엘타오브젝트) — you can combine both stores into one course.

② Vintage Hidden Gems (2 Places)

3. Vintage Santa (빈티지산타)

  • Signature: A hidden gem for second-hand clothing in Hongdae (홍대). 10 foreign reviews — your entry into Korean vintage fashion.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Abundant foreign reviews, vintage concept.
  • Recommended Categories: American·European vintage clothing, 80s-90s limited editions, denim·outerwear.
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Hongik University Station (홍대입구역). Operating hours are recommended from 14:00-22:00 (2 PM - 10 PM). Vintage stores have significant variations in size and stock — if you find an item you like, purchase it immediately.

4. Omni People Heavy (옴니피플헤비)

  • Signature: A vintage and second-hand clothing store in the Hongdae (홍대) series. Focuses on American heavy vintage.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★☆☆ — Vintage concept, primarily Korean-language service.
  • Recommended Categories: Heavy vintage (military·workwear), American 80s-90s goods.
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Hongik University Station (홍대입구역). Located in the same alley (골목) as Omni People Trendy (옴니피플트렌디) (a sister store) — you can combine both stores.

③ K-beauty·Olive Young (올리브영) (2 Places)

5. Olive Young Hongdae Town (올리브영 홍대 타운)

  • Signature: A K-beauty megastore in Hongdae (홍대). 2-3 times larger in area and SKU compared to regular branches. 10 foreign reviews.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Megastore + English service + #1 choice for foreign K-beauty shoppers.
  • Recommended Categories: Full range of Korean K-beauty (skincare·makeup·body), signature limited editions.
  • Tip: A 3-minute walk from Hongik University Station (홍대입구역) Exit 9. Highly recommended as the #1 choice for first-time K-beauty shoppers — it's larger and less crowded than Myeongdong (명동).

6. Trend Pot by Olive Young Hongdae (올리브영 트렌드팟 바이 올리브영홍대)

  • Signature: A K-pop collaboration limited store — you can directly purchase limited edition goods from BTS, NewJeans, SEVENTEEN, and more.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — #1 itinerary for K-pop fans, English service.
  • Recommended Categories: K-pop collaboration cosmetics, limited edition goods, season-limited packages.
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Hongik University Station (홍대입구역). If you're a K-pop fan, prioritize Trend Pot (트렌드팟) over Myeongdong (명동) — many collaboration limited editions are only available here.

④ Design·Lifestyle (2 Places)

7. Sangsang Madang Design Square (상상마당 디자인스퀘어)

  • Signature: A Korean design gallery + lifestyle store + cafe operated by KT&G. 10 foreign reviews.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Design concept + English menu.
  • Recommended Categories: Korean design goods, visual design publications, gallery exhibitions.
  • Tip: A 7-minute walk from Hongik University Station (홍대입구역). The live hall and cinema next to Design Square (디자인스퀘어) are also connected — allowing for a design + performance + movie combo course.

8. Life & Collect (라이프앤콜렉트)

  • Signature: A visual design and lifestyle curation store in Hongdae (홍대). 7 foreign reviews.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★☆☆ — Visual design concept, primarily Korean-language service.
  • Recommended Categories: Visual design goods, lifestyle curation, Korean designer collaborations.
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Hongik University Station (홍대입구역). A hidden gem for international visitors looking for Korean design goods.
