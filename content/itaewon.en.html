⚠ 3 Common Mistakes First-Time International Visitors Make

1. Only seeing "Itaewon = the foreigner street" and missing Hannam & Gyeongnidan

A 10–15 minute walk from Itaewon (이태원) Station brings you to Hannam (한남) — Michelin, galleries, luxury — and Gyeongnidan (경리단) — trendy MZ cafés and bars. See only Itaewon and you've seen just 30% of the area.

2. Assuming the foreigner street is all expensive

Itaewon has plenty of casual global eateries (Mexican, Indian, Middle Eastern) from ₩10,000. Even Michelin spots (Mosu, Blind Spot) offer reasonable lunch courses from ₩60,000.

3. Not knowing the difference between the Islamic street and Hannam

Itaewon's Islamic street (kebab, halal, Middle Eastern) vs. Hannam (luxury, galleries, Michelin) vs. Gyeongnidan (MZ, trendy) — three sub-tones coexist in one area.

A Look at Itaewon & Hannam

Itaewon (이태원) is Korea's largest area for expat residents and tourists (next to embassies and the former US base). Hannam (한남) — luxury, galleries, Michelin — plus Gyeongnidan-gil (경리단길) — MZ cafés and bars — and Haebangchon (해방촌, HBC) rooftops are all within walking distance. English-friendliness is 100% — English menus and foreign staff at nearly every venue.

Six things international visitors come for:

1. World cuisine & Michelin (Mosu, Casa Corona, Blind Spot)

2. Seoul Central Mosque & the halal street (서울중앙성원, Usadan-gil)

3. Museums & galleries (Leeum, Hannam)

4. Unique shopping (antique furniture, custom leather, multicultural fashion)

5. Namsan walk (N Seoul Tower)

6. Expat pubs & trendy bars (Rose & Crown, Southside Parlor)

🚇 Getting There & Routes (Itaewon Station course)

The standard entry point is Itaewon (이태원) Station (Line 6). The whole area links up on foot.

  • Exits 1 & 3 → Itaewon-ro main street: shopping street, world cuisine, pubs
  • Exit 3 → Usadan-ro: Seoul Central Mosque (서울중앙성원) & halal street (10 min walk)
  • Toward Hangangjin Station (Line 6): Hannam — Leeum Museum (리움미술관), Mosu, galleries
  • Toward Noksapyeong Station (Line 6): Gyeongnidan-gil (경리단길) & Haebangchon (해방촌, HBC) rooftops
  • Itaewon / Haebangchon → Namsan Park on foot → N Seoul Tower

Crowd tip: weekend evenings and festivals can get very crowded on the main street — weekdays and daytime are calmer, and take the narrow, sloped alleys slowly. It's best to avoid heavily packed crowd zones.

🕌 Culture & Landmarks (Mosque · Leeum · Namsan)

Seoul Central Mosque (서울중앙성원)

Korea's largest mosque (built 1976), on the Usadan-ro hill in Itaewon. The exterior and interior (visitable outside prayer times in modest dress) plus the surrounding Islamic street (Usadan-gil, 우사단길) lined with halal and Middle Eastern eateries. A must for Muslim travellers, and an exotic alley experience for everyone else.

  • Location: Usadan-ro, Yongsan-gu (10 min walk from Itaewon Station Exit 3)
  • Hours: exterior & street anytime / interior outside prayer times, modest dress
  • Signature: Korea's Islamic center + halal street
  • Why: Itaewon's multicultural symbol. Muslim-friendly with authentic halal food.

Leeum Museum of Art (리움미술관)

The Samsung Foundation art museum in Hannam — Korean traditional art + modern art + special exhibitions. The architecture itself (designed by Mario Botta, Jean Nouvel and Rem Koolhaas) is a landmark. For art lovers among international visitors.

  • Location: Itaewon-ro 55-gil, Yongsan-gu (Hannam, Hangangjin Station Exit 1)
  • Hours: Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00 (closed Mon, reservation recommended)
  • Signature: traditional + modern art + architecture
  • Why: the heart of Hannam's luxury & art axis. Pairs with a Michelin (Mosu) course.

Namsan Park · N Seoul Tower (남산 · N서울타워)

From Itaewon and Haebangchon, walking trails lead up into Namsan Park. Climb the slope and the city noise fades as you trek toward N Seoul Tower. Pairs well with Comfortable Coffee (Namsan view).

  • Location: Itaewon / Haebangchon → Namsan Park (on foot or by cable car)
  • Signature: a walk in the city + N Tower night view
  • Why: adds nature and a viewpoint to your Itaewon food course.

🛍 Shopping — Multicultural Street · Antique Furniture · Custom Leather

Itaewon Shopping Street (이태원 쇼핑거리)

The Itaewon-ro main street — multicultural fashion, vintage, souvenirs and bags. Larger sizes for foreigners and global items, plus unusual souvenirs. Some shops allow haggling.

  • Location: Itaewon-ro main street (around Itaewon Station)
  • Signature: multicultural fashion & souvenirs
  • Why: an exotic alternative to Myeongdong shopping. Large sizes and global brands.

Antique Furniture Street & Custom Leather (앤티크 가구거리 & 수제화·맞춤 가죽)

Itaewon is traditionally famous for its antique furniture street (around Bogwang-ro) and custom leather goods & shoes (a tailoring tradition dating to the US base era). For vintage, interior and leather enthusiasts.

  • Location: around Bogwang-ro & Itaewon furniture street
  • Signature: antique furniture + custom leather & shoes
  • Why: Itaewon's historic shopping niche. A course for interior and vintage lovers.

② Expat Pub (2 venues)

Rose & Crown (로즈앤크라운)

A British-style pub in Itaewon — Fish & Chips, London beers, darts. ★5.0 + 5 English reviews. A favourite of expat residents. Best for group gatherings and watching sports.

  • Address: Itaewon-ro, Yongsan-gu
  • Hours: Daily 17:00–02:00
  • Menu: Fish & Chips from ₩18,000 / British draft beer from ₩9,000
  • Signature: authentic British pub
  • Why: #1 for expat group gatherings. A proper British atmosphere.

Casa Corona (카사 코로나)

A Mexican & Latin bar in Itaewon. ★5.0 + 5 English reviews. Tacos, burritos, margaritas. The top spot for authentic Mexican in Korea.

  • Address: Itaewon-ro, Yongsan-gu
  • Hours: Daily 17:00–02:00 (closed Mon)
  • Menu: Tacos & burritos ₩12,000–25,000 / Margaritas from ₩12,000
  • Signature: authentic Mexican & Latin
  • Why: rare authentic Mexican in Korea. Popular with foreigners and Korean MZ alike.

③ Michelin & World Food (2 venues)

Mosu Seoul (모수 서울)

A Michelin modern Western restaurant in Hannam (Chef Sungjae Ahn). Korean ingredients + global technique. Listed in the Michelin Guide. For Michelin enthusiasts among international visitors.

  • Address: Hannam-dong, Yongsan-gu
  • Hours: Tue–Sun 12:00–22:00 (closed Mon, reservation required)
  • Menu: Lunch course from ₩100,000 / Dinner from ₩220,000
  • Signature: Michelin modern Western
  • Why: Hannam's Michelin pick. For business and luxury dinners.

Blind Spot, Mondrian Seoul Itaewon (몬드리안 서울 이태원 블라인드 스팟)

A 5-star hotel Western restaurant (part of Mondrian Seoul Itaewon). ★5.0 + 5 English reviews. Hotel service + modern Western. Top English-friendliness.

  • Address: Itaewon-ro, Yongsan-gu, Mondrian Hotel
  • Hours: Daily 06:00–22:00 (hotel restaurant)
  • Menu: Western course & à la carte ₩50,000–120,000
  • Signature: 5-star hotel modern Western
  • Why: safe, upscale Western dining. For business travellers and tourists.

⚙ FAQ (11)

Q1. A first Itaewon course?

Itaewon Station 11:00 → Ekobox Coffee specialty → Casa Corona lunch tacos → walk the Seoul Central Mosque & halal street → walk to Hannam → Leeum Museum in the afternoon → Rose & Crown dinner → Southside Parlor at night.

Q2. English-friendliness?

Every venue has full English menus and service. Itaewon is Korea's most English-friendly area, with a high share of foreign staff.

Q3. Michelin reservations?

Mosu Seoul: 1–2 weeks ahead via Tabling or The Catch. Partial English interface.

Q4. Price range?

Café ₩6,500–12,000 / Pub ₩15,000–30,000 / Michelin ₩60,000–250,000 / Trendy bar ₩12,000–22,000. (The mosque and shopping street are free to walk.)

Q5. Hannam vs. Itaewon vs. Gyeongnidan?

Hannam = Michelin, galleries, luxury (Mosu, Leeum). Itaewon = foreigner street, world food, Islamic street (Casa Corona, Rose & Crown). Gyeongnidan = MZ trendy (Rain Report).

Q6. Halal, kosher, vegetarian?

Itaewon's Islamic street (around Seoul Central Mosque, Usadan-gil) has many halal venues. Mosu Seoul offers a vegetarian course on request. Casa Corona has some vegetarian options.

Q7. A night course?

Itaewon 16:00 → Hannam galleries & Leeum → Mosu dinner → Rose & Crown or Southside Parlor to finish the night (until ~02:00).

Q8. Wheelchair access?

Comfortable Coffee / Blind Spot / Rain Report / Leeum — flat. Itaewon's alleys, the Usadan-ro hill and the antique street are narrow with many steps and slopes.

Q9. Is Itaewon worth visiting?

Strongly recommended if you want world cuisine, Islamic culture, a museum (Leeum), unusual shopping and nightlife in one neighbourhood. For tradition and palaces, Jongno is better; for large-scale K-beauty shopping, Myeongdong.

Q10. What is Itaewon known for?

Korea's largest multicultural area — world cuisine (halal, Mexican, Michelin), Seoul Central Mosque & the halal street, Leeum Museum, antique furniture & custom leather, and expat nightlife.

Q11. Itaewon vs. Hongdae / Gangnam?

Itaewon = multicultural, world cuisine, foreigner-friendly. Hongdae = indie music, MZ. Gangnam = luxury, cosmetics. For global food and exotic culture, Itaewon is #1.

🌸 5 Seasonal & Timing Hooks

1. Spring (Mar–May) Namsan cherry blossoms: Comfortable Coffee + a Namsan walk.

2. Summer (Jun–Aug) night courses: Southside Parlor & Rose & Crown peak at night.

3. Autumn (Sep–Nov) Hannam galleries + Michelin: Mosu + a Leeum/Hannam gallery stroll.

4. Winter (Dec–Feb) pub season: Rose & Crown's British pub with warm mulled wine + beer.

5. Halal / Ramadan: Itaewon's Islamic street venues offer special iftar during Ramadan (May–Jun).
