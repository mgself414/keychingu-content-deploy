⚠ 3 Common Mistakes First-Time International Visitors Make

1. Treating Itaewon (이태원) as just the "foreigner street" and missing Hannam (한남) & Gyeongnidan (경리단)

Just 10–15 minutes on foot from Itaewon Station (이태원역) you'll find Hannam (한남) — Michelin dining, galleries, luxury — and Gyeongnidan (경리단) — MZ-friendly trendy cafés and bars. Sticking only to Itaewon (이태원) means you're seeing only 30% of what this area has to offer.

2. Assuming the "foreigner district" means everything is expensive

Itaewon (이태원) has plenty of casual global eateries (Mexican, Indian, Middle Eastern) starting from ₩10,000. Even Michelin-level spots like Mosu Seoul (모수 서울) and Blind Spot (블라인드 스팟) offer lunch courses from around ₩60,000 — quite reasonable.

3. Not distinguishing between the Itaewon Halal Street, Hannam (한남), and Gyeongnidan (경리단)

The Itaewon (이태원) Halal Street (kebabs, halal food, Middle Eastern) sits alongside Hannam (한남) — luxury, galleries, Michelin — and Gyeongnidan (경리단) — MZ, trendy. Three very different sub-tones coexist within a single walkable area.

The Itaewon & Hannam Landscape

Itaewon (이태원) is Seoul's largest expat and international tourist hub, located near major embassies and the former US military base. Hannam (한남) — luxury, galleries, Michelin — and Gyeongnidan-gil (경리단길) — MZ cafés and bars — are all within walking distance. English-friendliness is effectively 100%: English menus and non-Korean staff are standard at nearly every venue here.

① Cafés & Desserts (2 Venues)

Comfortable Coffee Namsan (콤포타블 커피 남산점)

A view café near Namsan Tower (남산타워). ★5.0 + 5 English reviews. Namsan scenery paired with specialty coffee. Best visited for a late-morning or afternoon session.

  • Address: Namsangongwon-gil (남산공원길), Yongsan-gu (용산구)
  • Hours: Daily 10:00–22:00
  • Menu: Specialty coffee ₩6,500~ / Desserts ₩7,000–12,000
  • Signature: Namsan Tower view + specialty coffee
  • Why go: Combines Namsan sightseeing with a quality café stop. A great first move for international visitors.

Ekobox Coffee Studio Itaewon (이코복스 커피 스튜디오 이태원점)

Specialty hand-drip coffee in a studio-style setting in the heart of Itaewon (이태원). ★4.8 + 5 English reviews. Designer-collab interiors throughout.

  • Address: Itaewon-dong (이태원동), Yongsan-gu (용산구)
  • Hours: Daily 10:00–22:00
  • Menu: Specialty coffee ₩7,000–12,000
  • Signature: Itaewon studio café
  • Why go: Perfect for international coffee enthusiasts exploring Itaewon (이태원). Highly English-friendly.

② Expat Pubs (2 Venues)

Rose & Crown (로즈앤크라운)

A classic British pub in Itaewon (이태원) — Fish & Chips, London beers, and darts. ★5.0 + 5 English reviews. A longstanding favourite among expat residents. Ideal for group gatherings and sports viewing.

  • Address: Itaewon-ro (이태원로), Yongsan-gu (용산구)
  • Hours: Daily 17:00–02:00
  • Menu: Fish & Chips ₩18,000~ / British draft beer ₩9,000~
  • Signature: Authentic British pub experience
  • Why go: The #1 spot for expat group nights out. As close to a genuine London pub as you'll get in Seoul.

Casa Corona (카사 코로나)

A Mexican & Latin bar in Itaewon (이태원). ★5.0 + 5 English reviews. Tacos, burritos, and margaritas — the go-to introduction to authentic Mexican food in Korea.

  • Address: Itaewon-ro (이태원로), Yongsan-gu (용산구)
  • Hours: Daily 17:00–02:00 (closed Mon)
  • Menu: Tacos & burritos ₩12,000–25,000 / Margaritas ₩12,000~
  • Signature: Authentic Mexican & Latin
  • Why go: Genuine Mexican food, rare in Korea. Popular with both expats and Korean MZ diners.

③ Michelin & World Food (2 Venues)

Mosu Seoul (모수 서울)

Michelin-starred modern cuisine in Hannam (한남) by Chef Ahn Sung-jae (안성재). Korean ingredients meet global technique. Listed in the Michelin Guide. A magnet for international fine-dining enthusiasts.

  • Address: Hannam-dong (한남동), Yongsan-gu (용산구)
  • Hours: Tue–Sun 12:00–22:00 (closed Mon; reservation required)
  • Menu: Lunch course ₩100,000~ / Dinner ₩220,000~
  • Signature: Michelin modern cuisine
  • Why go: Hannam's (한남) Michelin destination. Ideal for business dinners and luxury occasions.

Blind Spot, Mondrian Seoul Itaewon (몬드리안 서울 이태원 블라인드 스팟)

The fine-dining restaurant of the 5-star Mondrian Seoul Itaewon (몬드리안 서울 이태원) hotel. ★5.0 + 5 English reviews. Hotel-grade service paired with contemporary Western cuisine. Maximum English-friendliness.

  • Address: Itaewon-ro (이태원로), Yongsan-gu (용산구) — inside Mondrian Hotel (몬드리안 호텔)
  • Hours: Daily 06:00–22:00 (hotel restaurant)
  • Menu: Western set & à la carte ₩50,000–120,000
  • Signature: 5-star hotel contemporary Western dining
  • Why go: Safe, upscale Western food in a hotel setting. Perfect for business travellers and tourists wanting a premium, hassle-free dining experience.

④ Trendy Bars (2 Venues)

Southside Parlor (사우스사이드 팔러)

An American-style cocktail bar in Itaewon (이태원). ★5.0 + 5 English reviews. Classic American cocktails in a stylish atmosphere. Popular with expat residents and Korean MZ crowd alike.

  • Address: Itaewon-ro (이태원로), Yongsan-gu (용산구)
  • Hours: Daily 19:00–02:00
  • Menu: Cocktails ₩15,000–22,000
  • Signature: Authentic American-style cocktail bar
  • Why go: The go-to Itaewon (이태원) night-out spot. A genuine mix of international and Korean MZ energy.

Rain Report Gyeongnidan (레인리포트 경리단)

A trendy café-bar hybrid on Gyeongnidan-gil (경리단길). ★5.0 + 5 English reviews. Signature drinks and Insta-worthy interiors throughout.

  • Address: Gyeongnidan-gil (경리단길), Yongsan-gu (용산구)
  • Hours: Daily 11:00–01:00
  • Menu: Café drinks ₩6,500~ / Cocktails ₩12,000~
  • Signature: Gyeongnidan (경리단) café-bar hybrid
  • Why go: The defining stop on the Gyeongnidan (경리단) circuit — tonal sweet spot between café and bar.

⚙ FAQ — 8 Questions

Q1. What's the best first-visit itinerary for Itaewon (이태원)?

Itaewon Station (이태원역) 11:00 → Ekobox Coffee Studio (이코복스 커피) specialty coffee → Casa Corona (카사 코로나) lunch tacos → walk to Hannam (한남) → Mosu Seoul (모수 서울) or Blind Spot (블라인드 스팟) afternoon → Rose & Crown (로즈앤크라운) evening → Southside Parlor (사우스사이드 팔러) late night

Q2. How English-friendly is the area?

All 8 venues offer English menus and English-capable service. Itaewon (이태원) is Seoul's most English-friendly neighbourhood, with a notably high proportion of non-Korean staff.

Q3. How do I book Michelin restaurants?

Mosu Seoul (모수 서울): Reserve 1–2 weeks in advance via Tabling or The Catch. Both platforms have partial English interfaces.

Q4. What's the price range?

Cafés ₩6,500–12,000 / Pubs ₩15,000–30,000 / Michelin dining ₩60,000–250,000 / Trendy bars ₩12,000–22,000.

Q5. How do Hannam (한남), Itaewon (이태원), and Gyeongnidan (경리단) differ?

Hannam (한남) = Michelin, galleries, luxury (Mosu Seoul). Itaewon (이태원) = international street, world food (Casa Corona, Rose & Crown). Gyeongnidan (경리단) = MZ trendy scene (Rain Report).

Q6. Any halal, kosher, or vegetarian options?

Itaewon (이태원) Halal Street (near the Seoul Central Masjid) has numerous halal venues. Mosu Seoul (모수 서울) can accommodate vegetarian courses upon advance request. Casa Corona (카사 코로나) offers some vegetarian options.

Q7. Recommended evening itinerary?

Itaewon (이태원) 16:00 → Hannam (한남) gallery walk → Mosu Seoul (모수 서울) dinner → Rose & Crown (로즈앤크라운) or Southside Parlor (사우스사이드 팔러) for a late-night finish (~02:00).

Q8. Wheelchair accessibility?

Comfortable Coffee Namsan (콤포타블 커피 남산점), Blind Spot (블라인드 스팟), and Rain Report Gyeongnidan (레인리포트 경리단) have flat, accessible entrances. Many venues in the narrower Itaewon (이태원) backstreets have steps and limited width — check ahead.

🌸 5 Seasonal & Timing Highlights

1. Spring (March–May) Namsan cherry blossoms: Pair Comfortable Coffee Namsan (콤포타블 커피 남산점) with a stroll through Namsan Park (남산공원).

2. Summer (June–August) night-out season: Southside Parlor (사우스사이드 팔러) and Rose & Crown (로즈앤크라운) hit their peak energy on warm summer nights.

3. Autumn (September–November) Hannam gallery + Michelin: Combine Mosu Seoul (모수 서울) with a gallery walk through Hannam (한남) — perfect autumn afternoon.

4. Winter (December–February) pub season: Rose & Crown (로즈앤크라운) with warm Glühwein and draught beer on a cold night is hard to beat.

5. Halal & Ramadan: Itaewon (이태원) Halal Street venues run special Iftar menus during Ramadan (typically May–June, depending on the year).
