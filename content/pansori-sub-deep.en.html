🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers the museums, training halls and birthplaces that honor pansori master singers and theorists, region by region. It covers three Sin Jae-hyo sites in Gochang (고창), two Dongpyeonje (동편제) places in Gurye (구례) and Namwon (남원), one Seopyeonje (서편제) place in Boseong (보성), and one in Gongju (공주). Every place with a registered fee is free.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data. Gugak performance venues and the National Gugak Center are in the #686 and #695 guides.

📍 Gochang — Sin Jae-hyo Pansori Museum, Sin Jae-hyo House and Sin Jae-hyo Pansori Park

The Gochang Sin Jae-hyo Pansori Museum (고창 신재효판소리박물관) (100 Dongni-ro (동리로), Gochang-eup (고창읍), Gochang-gun, ☎ 063-560-8061) is a museum honoring Dongni Sin Jae-hyo (신재효), a pansori theorist, adapter and patron, along with master singers such as Jin Chae-seon (진채선) and Kim So-hui (김소희). It was built on the site of the Sin Jae-hyo house and is 500m from Gochang Intercity Bus Terminal (고창시외버스터미널). The round exhibition hall 'Hall of Fame (명예의 전당)' has photo panels of master singers. The museum has Sori Madang (소리마당), which reconstructs the artistic world of pansori, Aniri Madang (아니리마당), which spotlights Sin Jae-hyo and the singers of Gochang, and Hon Madang (혼마당), where you experience pansori through digital video.

  • Opening hours: Summer 09:00~18:00, winter 09:00~17:00. Last entry 30 minutes before closing. Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal (설) and Chuseok (추석) days.
  • Fee: Free.

The Gochang Sin Jae-hyo House (고창신재효고택) (same address, ☎ Gochangeupseong (고창읍성) tourist information center 063-560-8055) is the house of Sin Jae-hyo (1812~1884), who compiled pansori into a system. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is believed to have been built in the 1st year of King Cheoljong (철종) (1850) and is known to have been rebuilt by his son in the 3rd year of Gwangmu (광무) (1899). It was originally a modest thatched sarangchae (사랑채), and until it was designated a National Folk Cultural Heritage it was used as an annex of the Gochang police station; afterward the state managed it and restored its old appearance. It is a '一'-shaped thatched house 6 kan (칸) wide and 2 kan deep, made up of 1 kitchen kan, 2 room kan, 1 daecheong (대청) kan and 1 combined room kan.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available.

Sin Jae-hyo Pansori Park (신재효판소리공원) (148 Jung-georidangsan-ro (중거리당산로), Gochang-eup, Gochang-gun, ☎ 063-560-8035) is a pansori space built beside the fortress wall path of Gochangeupseong. It remembers Sin Jae-hyo, who came from the ajeon (아전) clerk class of Gochang and compiled pansori into a system, and it consists of 3 hanok (한옥) buildings with a total floor area of 614㎡. It has a seminar room, a pansori experience room, a pansori deukeum (득음) practice room and an outdoor stage for pansori education and experience.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~17:00 (break 12:00~13:00). Closed days: Every Saturday and Sunday.
  • Experiences: At the Pansori Academy (판소리아카데미) you can take theory lectures from professors specializing in pansori and pansori transmission lessons from master singers. Groups of 10 or more can apply for a performance in advance, with pansori commentary, listening and learning one passage. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, performances with visitors and trainees also take place on the outdoor ground on weekends. The registered closed days are Saturday and Sunday.
  • Parking: Available.

📍 Dongpyeonje — Gurye Dongpyeonje Pansori Training Hall and Namwon Dongpyeonje Birthplace

The Dongpyeonje Pansori Training Hall (동편제 판소리전수관) (53 Dongpyeonje-gil (동편제길), Gurye-eup (구례읍), Gurye-gun, ☎ 061-782-6868) is a training hall built in 2000 around the Song Man-gap (송만갑) birthplace in Baengnyeon-ri (백련리), Gurye-eup, to honor Song Man-gap, a Dongpyeonje master singer from Gurye, and to train pansori talent. Pansori is divided into Dongpyeonje (Eastern school) and Seopyeonje (Western school) around the Seomjin River (섬진강). According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, Gurye sori keeps the original form of Dongpyeonje pansori, with Song Man-gap, the gukchang (국창) of the late Joseon (구한말) period, as its peak. It runs pansori transmission education and a gugak class, and holds the national pansori contest in memory of master singer Song Man-gap. Nearby are the Dongpyeonje Pansori Memorial Hall (동편제 판소리기념관), which opened on November 3, 2001, the Song Man-gap birthplace, and memorial monuments to master singers Song Man-gap, Park Bong-rae (박봉래) and Park Bong-sul (박봉술).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: Free. Pets: Not allowed.

Dongpyeonje Birthplace (동편제탯자리) (7 Bijeon-gil (비전길), Unbong-eup (운봉읍), Namwon-si, ☎ 063-634-7535) is Bijeon Village (비전마을) in Unbong-eup, where Song Heung-nok (송흥록), the gawang (가왕) who founded Dongpyeonje pansori, was born. It is also the sori hometown of the female master singer Park Cho-wol (박초월). Song Heung-nok applied jinyangjo (진양조), the slowest tempo in folk music, to pansori and widened its range of expression. The gwigokseong (귀곡성) (ghost-wail sound) of the prison scene in 'Chunhyangga (춘향가)' is recognized as a singing technique he created. Dongpyeonje was carried on by his younger brother Song Gwang-nok (송광록) and his grandson Song Man-gap. Since a gugak sanctuary began to be built in Bijeon Village in 2000, the Song Heung-nok birthplace and the Park Cho-wol old house have been restored.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Open year-round.

📍 Seopyeonje — Boseong Pansori Sanctuary

Pansori Sanctuary (판소리성지) (17-8 Yeongcheon-gil (영천길), Hoecheon-myeon (회천면), Boseong-gun, ☎ 061-852-5206) honors Gangsan (강산) Park Yu-jeon (박유전), the Joseon-period master singer who founded Seopyeonje, and the master singers who carried on the Seopyeonje lineage, such as Jeong Jae-geun (정재근), Jeong Eung-min (정응민) and Jo Sang-hyeon (조상현). In the Dogang Village (도강마을) area of Yeongcheon-ri (영천리), Hoecheon-myeon, there are the Park Yu-jeon monument, the Jeong Eung-min birthplace and Deukeumjeong (득음정). Park Yu-jeon was born in Sunchang (순창), North Jeolla, and moved to Gangsan Village (강산마을) in Boseong in 1852, where he founded Seopyeonje; he later also created Gangsanje (강산제), which harmonized the strengths of Dongpyeonje and Seopyeonje. It runs pansori experience classes and education programs.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. A Saturday regular performance (토요상설공연) is held every Saturday at 14:00 from September to December. Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Groups: Groups of 30 or more visitors can watch a master singer's pansori and folk song performance for free.

📍 Gongju — Park Dong-jin Pansori Training Hall

The Park Dong-jin Pansori Training Hall (박동진판소리전수관) (22-14 Mureungjungmal-gil (무릉중말길), Gongju-si, ☎ 041-858-0045) is a training hall built in Mureung-dong (무릉동), Gongju, the birthplace of Park Dong-jin (박동진), holder of the Jeokbyeokga (적벽가) performance skill, a National Intangible Cultural Heritage. It teaches the 5 pansori batang (바탕), Biblical pansori, danga (단가) and Namdo folk songs, and alongside training master singers it runs pansori experiences and cultural education for citizens.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Every Sunday and Monday, public holidays, Seollal and Chuseok holidays.
  • Fee: Free.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Closed days differ — Sin Jae-hyo Pansori Park is closed Saturday and Sunday, Park Dong-jin Pansori Training Hall is closed Sunday and Monday, and Sin Jae-hyo Pansori Museum and Pansori Sanctuary are closed Monday. Sin Jae-hyo House, Dongpyeonje Pansori Training Hall and Dongpyeonje Birthplace are open year-round.
  2. The three Gochang places are gathered on the Gochangeupseong Culture Experience Street (고창읍성 문화체험거리) — Sin Jae-hyo Pansori Museum and Sin Jae-hyo House share an address, and Sin Jae-hyo Pansori Park is beside the fortress wall path of Gochangeupseong.
  3. The Boseong Saturday regular performance runs only from September to December — It is every Saturday at 14:00.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where can I see a pansori performance?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, they are the Saturday regular performance at Boseong Pansori Sanctuary (every Saturday at 14:00, September to December). Sin Jae-hyo Pansori Park lets groups of 10 or more apply for a performance in advance, and Pansori Sanctuary shows a performance free to groups of 30 or more.

Q. Where can I try learning pansori myself?

A. Sin Jae-hyo Pansori Park (Pansori Academy, learning one passage of pansori), Park Dong-jin Pansori Training Hall (pansori experience for citizens), Pansori Sanctuary (pansori experience class) and Dongpyeonje Pansori Training Hall (gugak class).
