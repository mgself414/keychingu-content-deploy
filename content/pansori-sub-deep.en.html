12 Korean Pansori

1. Pansori (UNESCO 2003·Korean traditional music #1)

  • Discrimination: 2003 UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity (Korea #1)·Occurred in the late Joseon Dynasty in the 17th century·1 singer + 1 drummer·8 hours+ performance
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ No. 1 EN·JP·CN mania

2. Chunhyangga (Pansori #1·5 Madang)

  • Discrimination: Pansori #1 Madang·Lee Mongryong + Chunhyang·Joseon Dynasty Authentic Love Story·Approximately 8 hours of performance
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·Subtitles + Guide

3. Simcheongga (Pansori #2 Madang·Hyosim)

  • Discrimination: Simcheong + Filial Piety·Joseon Dynasty traditional filial piety story·About 6-7 hours of performance·Impressed Mania
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiasts

4. Heungboga (Pansori #3 Madang·Satire)

  • Discrimination: Heungbu + Nolbu·Jebi's Repayment·Joseon Dynasty Authentic Satire·Approximately 6 Hour Performance·Family Mania
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiasts

5. Sugungga (Pansori #4 Madang·Animals)

  • Discrimination: Rabbit + Zara·Underwater Adventure·Joseon Dynasty Authentic Satire + Animals·Performance for about 5-6 hours
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiasts

6. Jeokbyeokga (Pansori #5 Madang·Romance of the Three Kingdoms)

  • Discrimination: Romance of the Three Kingdoms + Battle of Red Cliffs + Korean Pansori Painting·Cao Cao, Liu Bei, Guan Yu·Approximately 7-8 hours of performance·History Mania
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiasts

7. National Gugak Center Pansori Performance (Seoul)

  • Discrimination: National Gugak Center (Seoul Main Center)·Pansori performance every week·EN/JP subtitle full set·Price 5K-50K
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania #1·#686 cross

8. Pansori performance at Korea House (accompanied by Namsan and royal cuisine)

  • Discrimination: Korea House authentic performance and royal cuisine full set·EN/JP/CN full set·100K-300K full set
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania #1·#685 cross

9. Master pansori singer (Living Cultural Heritage)

  • Discrimination: Living Cultural Heritage No. 5 (Pansori)·Orthodox singers Sang-Hyeon Jo, Sook-Seon Ahn, Il-Ju Lee, and So-Hee Kim·Many enthusiasts
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

10. Inalchi + Jambinai (Sinminyo + Pansori fusion)

  • Discrimination: Inalchi "The Tiger is Coming Down" Global Hit·Jambinai Metal + Korean Traditional Music Fusion·Mnet Pungryu Captain·MZ + YouTube Mania
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ Mania #1·YouTube #1

11. Pansori Experience + Class (Seoul)

  • Discrimination: National Gugak Center·Private Pansori Experience·1-2 hours 50K-100K·Partial EN guide·Basic vocalization
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiasts

12. Gwangju + Jeonbuk Pansori cluster (birthplace of orthodoxy)

  • Discrimination: Gwangju + Jeonbuk Jeongeup, Gochang, Imsil = Birthplace of Korean Pansori, Gwangju Biennale, Dongri Shin Jae-hyo Museum
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiast·#643 Gwangju cross

FAQ 6

Q1. Pansori performance price? A. National Gugak Center 5K-50K·Korea House full set 100K-300K·EN/JP subtitles full set.

Q2. A foreigner's first pansori? A. Chunhyangga (love story) or Heungboga (satire) 1st place·Check subtitles + guide.

Q3. Inalchi vs. authentic Pansori? A. Orthodox = 1 singer + 1 drummer·6-8 hours·Inalchi = Shinminyo·5-10 minutes·MZ + YouTube.

Q4. Master singers who are living cultural assets? A. Orthodox master singers Sang-Hyeon Jo, Sook-Seon Ahn, Il-Ju Lee, So-Hee Kim, and Song-Hee Park.

Q5. Pansori experience? A. National Gugak Center·Private 1-2 hours 50K-100K·Check EN guide in advance·Basic vocalization + melody.

Q6. Birthplace of Pansori? A. Jeonbuk Jeongeup, Gochang, Imsil, Gwangju·Born in the late 17th century of the Joseon Dynasty·Dong-ri Shin Jae-hyo Museum (Gochang).
