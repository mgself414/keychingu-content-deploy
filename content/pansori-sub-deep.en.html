Pansori Guide — What Foreign Visitors Can Actually See in Fall and Winter 2026, and the Five Madang (Official Schedules)

Subtitle: National Gugak Center Saturday Fine Gugak every Sat 15:00 · National Theater of Korea full-length pansori 10/24 and 11/14, changgeuk Oedipus 11/26–12/5 · Seoul Donhwamun Traditional Theater 10/25 Chunhyangga · Seoul Namsan Gugakdang — per each official site as of 2026-10-07. Pansori was proclaimed by UNESCO in 2003 and inscribed on the Representative List in 2008

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Pansori (판소리) is narrative music in which one singer performs for hours, accompanied by a single gosu, the drummer (고수). Singing one full story from start to finish is called full-length pansori, wanchang (완창); depending on the singer and the story it runs 3 to 8 hours (the performances we checked run 170 and 300 minutes). The staged version, changgeuk (창극), usually runs around 2 hours. Realistically, a foreign visitor who wants to "see pansori" has three options: (1) Saturday Fine Gugak (토요명품) at the National Gugak Center (국립국악원), held every Saturday, a general gugak (traditional music) program rather than pansori alone; (2) full-length pansori and changgeuk at the National Theater of Korea (국립극장); or (3) one-off shows at Donhwamun and Namsan gugak halls. The schedules, prices and addresses below are only values we read on each official site on 2026-10-07, and English subtitles are not mentioned on any official page, so they are unconfirmed. Three claims from the older guide were removed because they had no source or were wrong: "National Gugak Center pansori every week, EN/JP subtitles, 5,000–50,000 won", "peak autumn pansori festivals nationwide", and "Korea House 100,000–300,000 won".

🏛 4 Venues

National Gugak Center — Saturday Fine Gugak

  • 2364 Nambusunhwan-ro, Seocho-gu. Walk from Nambu Bus Terminal Station (남부터미널역) on Line 3 (the exit number is unconfirmed on the official site).
  • Saturday Fine Gugak: every Saturday from 2026-01-03 to 12-26, 15:00–16:20, Umyeondang. Seat A 30,000 · Seat B 20,000 won. Some sessions include a pansori excerpt, but it is not a pansori-only performance; check each session's program on the official page.

National Theater of Korea — Full-Length Pansori and Changgeuk

  • 59 Jangchungdan-ro, Jung-gu. From Dongguk Univ. Station (동대입구역) on Line 3, Exit 6, take the shuttle (from 1 hour to 20 minutes before showtime, every 10–15 minutes) or walk 10–15 minutes (official).
  • Full-length pansori: one master singer performs a whole story. 10/24 Lee Gwangbok, Heungboga (170 min); 11/14 Seo Jinhui, Simcheongga (300 min); 20,000 won each, Daloreum Theater.
  • Changgeuk: staged productions by the National Changgeuk Company. "Oedipus" runs 11/26–12/5 (50,000/35,000/20,000 won). The year-end pansori "Sori Jeongdam" on 12/26 is 30,000 won.

Seoul Donhwamun Traditional Theater

  • 102 Yulgok-ro, Jongno-gu (in front of Donhwamun Gate, Changdeokgung). 10/25 Na Yunyeong, Manjeong-style Chunhyangga. Prices and directions are not posted on the official page; check the booking page.

Seoul Namsan Gugakdang

  • 28 Toegye-ro 34-gil, Jung-gu (inside Namsangol Hanok Village). Jangdan Yuhui festival on 10/10–11. We could not read any other pansori-only schedule on the official page as of 10/7.

Jeonju — National Intangible Heritage Center and the Sori Festival

  • We could not read a performance program for the National Intangible Heritage Center (국립무형유산원) (Jeonju) on its official page. The Jeonju International Sori Festival 2026 ran Aug 12–16 and is over (its lineup included the Five Madang of Pansori and the Young Pansori Five Madang); watch for the 2027 announcement in the first half of the year.

🎼 The Five Madang — What You Will Be Hearing

Madang The story in one line For first-timers
Chunhyangga The love between Chunhyang, a gisaeng's daughter, and the nobleman Yi Mongnyong; Byeon Hakdo's forced demands, and the secret royal inspector The most popular. Often staged as changgeuk too
Simcheongga Simcheong throws herself into the sea for her blind father Long, sorrowful passages — 11/14 full-length, 300 min
Heungboga Poor Heungbo, greedy Nolbo, and the swallow's gratitude Lots of laughter, so good for a first time — 10/24 full-length, 170 min
Sugungga (수궁가) A turtle goes to fetch a rabbit's liver to cure the Dragon King's illness Animal satire, good with children
Jeokbyeokga (적벽가) The Battle of Red Cliffs from Romance of the Three Kingdoms, in pansori Masculine, with long narrative text; for enthusiasts
  • Length varies by madang and by singer. We do not give fixed figures such as the old guide's "Chunhyangga about 8 hours".

🗣 Language and Subtitles

  • No venue states on its official page whether English subtitles are offered (unconfirmed). Changgeuk often uses a subtitle screen, but check each production on its booking page.
  • Even if you cannot follow the lyrics (sasseol), you can keep up through the singer's expressions and the drummer's interjections (chuimsae). Start with a madang full of laughter, such as Heungboga.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Treating Saturday Fine Gugak as a pansori show — it is a general gugak program. For pansori alone, go to the National Theater's full-length series.

2. Assuming full-length pansori is a 2-hour show — it is 170 to 300 minutes. Ask the venue about intermission and re-entry rules.

3. Aiming for the autumn Jeonju Sori Festival — in 2026 it ended in August.

4. Walking up from Dongguk Univ. Station — there is a shuttle from Exit 6.

5. Taking English subtitles for granted — not stated officially. Check before you book.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. First time? — The 10/24 full-length Heungboga at the National Theater (170 min, 20,000 won), or the changgeuk Oedipus (from 11/26).

2. Just a short taste? — Saturday Fine Gugak at the National Gugak Center, 80 minutes (sessions that include a pansori excerpt).

3. How much? — Full-length 20,000 won, Saturday Fine Gugak 20,000–30,000 won, changgeuk 20,000–50,000 won (official).

4. What if I wear hanbok? — 50% off Saturday Fine Gugak on 10/24 (official).

5. In Jeonju? — The Sori Festival is in August. The National Intangible Heritage Center schedule is unconfirmed.

6. When did UNESCO recognize it? — Proclaimed in 2003, inscribed on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2008.
