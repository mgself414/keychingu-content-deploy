⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Dessert = Korean modern cafe only": 4 axes of Korean desserts — Street (Bungeoppang, Hotteok) + Traditional (Yakgwa, Injeolmi) + Modern (Noted, LBM, MAILET) + Luxury (Silla Hotel). Experience all 4 results.

2. "Bunggeobbang = All year round": Bungeobbang is only available during the winter season from November to March. I can't see it from April to October. The #1 street for foreigners to visit in Korea in winter.

3. "Bingsu = summer only": Bingsu is all seasons in Korea (it's hot even in winter). Sulbing Main Branch (#171) No. 1 for foreigners.
