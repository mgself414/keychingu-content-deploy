Full Guide to Korean Desserts — 12 Traditional, Street, Modern, and Seasonal Picks

🎯 Why a Full Guide to Korean Desserts?

Korean desserts are a top priority for foreign vlogs and Instagram, combining seasonal, street, traditional, and modern elements. ⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Desserts = Only Modern Korean Cafes": Korean desserts have four pillars — Street (Bungeoppang (붕어빵) · Hotteok (호떡)) + Traditional (Yakgwa (약과) · Injeolmi (인절미)) + Modern (Knotted (노티드) · LBM · MAILLET) + Luxury (Shilla Hotel (신라호텔)). Experience all four categories.

2. "Bungeoppang = Year-round": Bungeoppang (붕어빵) is seasonal, available only from November to March (winter). You won't find it from April to October. It's a top street food for foreigners visiting Korea in winter.

3. "Bingsu = Only Summer": Bingsu (빙수) is enjoyed year-round in Korea (even popular in winter). Sulbing (설빙) main branch (#171) is a top choice for foreigners.

① Street Food · Traditional (3)

1. Bungeoppang (붕어빵) ★ Top Street Food

  • Period: November-March (winter season)
  • Price: ₩1,000-2,000 for 3 (~$0.75-1.50 / ~5-10 RMB / ~100-200 JPY)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Top winter street food for K-vlogs. Bungeoppang (붕어빵) are fish-shaped pastries, typically filled with red bean paste.
  • Signature: Red bean, custard cream, cheese, sweet potato

2. Hotteok (호떡)

  • Period: November-March (winter) + some year-round
  • Price: ₩1,500-3,000
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Myeongdong (명동) · Insadong (인사동) street food. Hotteok (호떡) are sweet, pan-fried pancakes often filled with brown sugar syrup, nuts, or seeds.

3. Gyeran-ppang (계란빵)

  • Price: ₩2,000-3,000
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Myeongdong (명동) · Hongdae (홍대) street food, combines egg + bread. Gyeran-ppang (계란빵) are small, savory-sweet loaves of bread with a whole egg baked inside.

② Traditional · Hangwa (2)

4. Yakgwa (약과) ★ Top Foreigner Gift

  • Signature: Traditional Korean confectionery (hangwa (한과)), made with honey and sesame oil, popular as a gift for foreigners. Yakgwa (약과) are deep-fried, honey-glazed cookies with a distinct floral pattern.
  • Season: Holidays + year-round
  • Details: [#48 Korea Must-Buy TOP20](48_korea_must_buy_top20.md)

5. Injeolmi (인절미) · Songpyeon (송편) · Chaltteok (찰떡)

  • Stores: Tteokhamji Jamsil (떡함지 잠실) ([#82 Gangnam Jamsil Guide](82_gangnam_jamsil_guide.md)) · Jongno Tteokjip Mia (종로떡집 미아) ([#186 Mia Guide](186_mia_guide.md)) · Minseolgi (민설기) ([#186 Mia Guide](186_mia_guide.md)). Tteok (떡) refers to traditional Korean rice cakes. Injeolmi (인절미) is coated in roasted soybean powder, Songpyeon (송편) are crescent-shaped rice cakes often eaten during Chuseok, and Chaltteok (찰떡) are chewy glutinous rice cakes.
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Deep dive into traditional Korean tteok (떡).

③ Modern · MZ (4)

6. Bingsu (빙수) ★ Sulbing Main Branch

  • Stores: Sulbing Ewha (설빙 이대점) ([#171 Sinchon Ewha Guide](171_sinchon_ewha_guide.md)) · all Korean cafes · hotels. Bingsu (빙수) is a popular Korean shaved ice dessert with various toppings.
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Original branch for Korea's signature bingsu.
  • Signature: Injeolmi bingsu (인절미 빙수), red bean bingsu (팥빙수), mango bingsu (망고 빙수), green tea bingsu (녹차 빙수)
  • Price: ₩9,000-15,000

7. Modern Yakgwa (Lifestyle Yakgwa)

  • Stores: Insadong (인사동) · Ikseondong (익선동) modern yakgwa cafes. This refers to traditional yakgwa (약과) reimagined for a modern, often cafe-style, experience.
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Traditional yakgwa combined with modern cafe culture.

8. Knotted Donuts (노티드 도넛) ★ Korea's #1 Donut Brand

  • Stores: Anguk (안국) · Gangnam (강남) · Jamsil (잠실) · Gasan (가산) ([#82 Gangnam Jamsil Guide](82_gangnam_jamsil_guide.md) · [#180 Gasan Guide](180_gasan_guide.md) · [#225 Korean Cafes Full Guide](225_korean_cafes_full.md))
  • Signature: Hazelnut, vanilla cream, character donuts

9. London Bagel Museum (LBM) ★ Korea's #1 Bagel Brand

  • Stores: Anguk (안국) · Yeouido (여의도) · Jamsil (잠실) · Gasan (가산) ([#169 Bagel Guide](169_seoul_bagel_guide.md) · [#82 Gangnam Jamsil Guide](82_gangnam_jamsil_guide.md) · [#180 Gasan Guide](180_gasan_guide.md))
  • Signature: Garlic bagel, chai latte, 12 New York-style varieties

10. MAILLET Pastries

  • Stores: Seorae Village (서래마을) ([#173 Seoraemaeul Guide](173_seoraemaeul_guide.md) · [#225 Korean Cafes Full Guide](225_korean_cafes_full.md))
  • Signature: Macarons, croissants, pain au chocolat, eclairs, supplied to Shilla Hotel (신라호텔)

④ Luxury · Experience (2 + Additional 4)

11. Shilla Hotel (신라호텔) Pastries · Desserts

  • Stores: Shilla Hotel Jangchung-dong (신라호텔 장충동) ([#13 Namsan Guide](13_namsan_guide.md) sister content)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Korea's #1 hotel desserts + top luxury choice for foreigners.
  • Signature: Hotel lounge + French + Korean traditional

12. Geumokdang (금옥당) · Traditional Hangwa (연희)

  • Stores: Yeonhui-dong (연희동) ([#156 Yeonhui Guide](156_yeonhui_guide.md))
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Combines traditional Korean hangwa (한과) with a tea ceremony (dado (다도)). Hangwa (한과) are traditional Korean confections. Dado (다도) is the traditional Korean tea ceremony.
  • Signature: Traditional Korean hangwa (한과) · yakgwa (약과) · injeolmi (인절미)

+ Additional 4

  • Yaksik (약식) — Sticky rice + honey · nuts (Korean holiday food)
  • Kkul-tteok (꿀떡) — Honey + tteok (떡) combination, often for holidays
  • Maejakgwa (매작과) — Traditional Korean hangwa (한과) shaped like plum blossoms
  • Gangjeong (강정) — Traditional Korean puffed rice or grain crackers · nuts

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Top 1 Desserts for Foreigners: Knotted Donuts (노티드 도넛) · LBM Bagels · MAILLET Pastries · Sulbing Bingsu (설빙 빙수) · Bungeoppang (붕어빵) (winter).

2. TOP Gift Desserts: Yakgwa (약과) · Injeolmi (인절미) · MAILLET Macarons · Knotted (노티드) box · Geumokdang (금옥당) Hangwa (한과).
