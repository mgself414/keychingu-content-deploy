Northeast Seoul Forests and Parks Guide: Seoul Children's Grand Park, Achasan, Achasan Fortress and Goguryeojeong, Seoul Trail Course 5, Yongma Waterfall Park and Yongmasan Skywalk

Subtitle: Seoul Children's Grand Park (park 05:00~22:00) · Achasan (295.7m, always open) · Achasan Fortress (always open) · Achasan Goguryeojeong (always open) · Seoul Trail Course 5 Achasan Course (4.6km, about 2 hours 10 minutes) · Yongma Waterfall Park (always open) · Yongmasan Skywalk (about 160m, always open) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers parks and mountains in the northeast of Seoul, in Gwangjin-gu and Jungnang-gu. They are Seoul Children's Grand Park in Gwangjin-gu, Achasan with its Three Kingdoms-era sites and the trails around it, and the artificial waterfall park and viewing deck on Yongmasan in Jungnang-gu. All seven places are registered as open year-round. For Seoul Forest and Seongsu, see the #701 Seoul Forest and Seongsu Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Gwangjin — Seoul Children's Grand Park

Seoul Children's Grand Park (서울어린이대공원) [Neungdong-ro 216 (능동로 216), Gwangjin-gu, ☎ 02-450-9311] is a 536,094.50㎡ park that opened on Children's Day, May 5, 1973. It has a zoo, amusement rides and educational facilities. When it reopened on May 5, 2009, it newly opened a marine animal house where you can see polar bears and seals, a Little Animal Village (꼬마동물마을), a Parrot Village (앵무마을) and a musical fountain. In the middle of the park are the 8,000-seat open-air Neungdong Forest Stage (능동 숲속의 무대) and an indoor children's playground in Palgakdang (팔각당).

  • Opening hours: Park 05:00~22:00. Zoo 10:00~17:00. Amusement park 10:00~18:30 on weekdays, 10:00~19:30 on weekends.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

📍 Achasan — Achasan, Achasan Fortress, Goguryeojeong and Seoul Trail Course 5

Achasan (아차산) [Gingorang-ro 213 (긴고랑로 213), Gwangjin-gu, ☎ Achasan Management Office (아차산관리사무소) 02-450-1655] is a 295.7m mountain that spans Seoul and Guri-si (구리시). About 40 minutes up the hiking trail, the summit gives a view of all Seoul, including Jamsil (잠실), Namsan (남산), Bukhansan (북한산) and Dobongsan (도봉산). The terrain is not steep. In the Three Kingdoms period, Baekje used it as a military base against Goguryeo, and it is known as a place where Baekje, Silla and Goguryeo fought over the Han River.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Achasan Fortress (아차산성) [Gwangjang-dong (광장동), Gwangjin-gu, ☎ Gwangjin-gu Office (광진구청) Culture and Arts Division (문화예술과) 02-450-7593] is a fortress Baekje built to stop Goguryeo invasions. It is said that Goguryeo's King Jangsu (장수왕) dragged Baekje's King Gaero (개로왕) here and killed him, and that Goguryeo's General Ondal (온달) was killed here by an arrow from Silla troops. Excavations have turned up Goguryeo walls, building sites and a pond site, and since 2015 a Silla drainage facility and Goguryeo pottery.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Not available.
  • The registered information for Achasan Fortress gives the height of Achasan as 285m above sea level, so its registered value differs from the 295.7m in the Achasan entry.

Achasan Goguryeojeong (아차산 고구려정) [Guui-dong (구의동), Gwangjin-gu, ☎ Gwangjin-gu Office (광진구청) Parks and Green Space Division (공원녹지과) 02-450-7776] is a pavilion rebuilt in July 2009 after historians were consulted. The concrete octagonal pavilion built in 1984 had tilted and was torn down in January 2008. The name was changed from Palgakjeong (팔각정) to Goguryeojeong. It overlooks the Han River and Seoul.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Seoul Trail Course 5 Achasan Course (서울둘레길 5코스 아차산코스) [Myeonmok-dong (면목동), Jungnang-gu, ☎ 02-779-7902~4] is a course themed 'a path linking people and history' on the Seoul Trail (서울둘레길), which circles Seoul in 21 courses totaling 156.5km. The Achasan Ecological Park (아차산 생태공원) is introduced as a sight, and the course is close to Sagajeong Station (사가정역) and Gwangnaru Station (광나루역).

  • Length and time: 4.6km in total, about 2 hours 10 minutes. Opening hours: Always open.

📍 Yongmasan — Yongma Waterfall Park and Yongmasan Skywalk

Yongma Waterfall Park (용마폭포공원) [Yongmasan-ro 250-12 (용마산로 250-12), Jungnang-gu, ☎ Management Office (관리사무소) 02-2094-2965] is a park that opened in May 1993 using the rock face of Yongmasan, a former aggregate quarry. It has three artificial waterfalls. The central Yongma Falls is two-tiered and 51.4m high, the Cheongnyong Falls (청룡폭포) on the left is 21m, and the Baekma Falls (백마폭포) on the right is 21.4m. It has a soccer field, a climbing arena, a basketball court, a jokgu (족구) court and a children's playground.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Yongmasan Skywalk (용마산 스카이워크) [Myeonmok-dong San 1-3 (면목동 산1-3), Jungnang-gu, ☎ 02-2094-2382] is a wooden deck walkway about 160m long and up to 10m high, built near the summit of Yongmasan in November 2025 under the Seoul Trail 2.0 project. You can see from Namsan Seoul Tower (남산 서울타워) to Bonghwasan (봉화산), Dobongsan and Bukhansan, and also sunsets and night views. It belongs to Seoul Trail Course 4 (Mangu·Yongmasan, 망우·용마산) and connects to the barrier-free walking path Yongmasan Donghaenggil (용마산 동행길), which starts at Sagajeong Park (사가정공원). The slope was made gentle, so you can reach the observation deck with a wheelchair or stroller. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, about 230,000 people visited in February 2026 alone.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Not available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The zoo at Seoul Children's Grand Park opens 10:00~17:00 — The park is open 05:00~22:00, and the amusement park runs until 18:30 on weekdays and 19:30 on weekends.
  2. You cannot park at Achasan Fortress or Yongmasan Skywalk — Parking is available at Achasan, Goguryeojeong and Yongma Waterfall Park.
  3. Seoul Trail Course 5 takes about 2 hours 10 minutes — It is 4.6km in total and close to Sagajeong Station and Gwangnaru Station.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Is there an observation deck I can reach by wheelchair?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, Yongmasan Skywalk has a gently sloped deck, so you can reach the observation deck with a wheelchair or stroller. It is always open.

Q. How long does it take to reach the Achasan summit?

A. About 40 minutes on the hiking trail. It is introduced as 295.7m above sea level (registered as 285m in the Achasan Fortress entry) with terrain that is not steep.
