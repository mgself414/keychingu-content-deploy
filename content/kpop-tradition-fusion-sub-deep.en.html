🧵 Seoul — Craft Museum, Bukchon and Namsangol

  • Seoul Museum of Craft Art (서울공예박물관): Free admission, 10–18, closed Mondays, evening hours extended through 11/30 (official). Book experiences on the official booking page — in October, 'Najeon Box: Holding Craft' (through 10/27) and 'Night Studio: Autumn Glow Glass Art'. Check the booking page for fees and English guidance. The najeon lacquer box (나전함) program is the one to watch before it ends.
  • Bukchon Traditional Crafts Experience Center (북촌전통공예체험관) (24-5 Bukchon-ro 12-gil): 16 workshops and 46 programs, closed only on Seollal and Chuseok, booked first come, first served through the Seoul public reservation service (서울시 공공서비스예약) (press, 2026-05). Hours (10–18 May–Oct, 10–17 Nov–Feb) come from a secondary source. You can walk from Anguk Station (안국역) (exit not confirmed). Many programs use hanji (한지), so it suits paper-craft fans.
  • Namsangol Hanok Village (남산골한옥마을): 'Artist's Time' experiences and performances run through 10/25 (VisitSeoul, official). The details that experiences run Fri–Sun, 50 minutes, 15 people come from a 2025 article, so 2026 may differ. The official site (hanokmaeul.or.kr) does not open. The nearest station is Chungmuro Station (충무로역).

🏺 Icheon — Ceramics

  • Icheon Cerapia and World Ceramic Center (이천 세라피아·세계도자센터) (kocef.org): 2026 Gyeonggi Ceramic Biennale (경기도자비엔날레), 9/18–11/1 (official). Hours, closures, admission and wheel-throwing fees are not on the official page, so call 031-631-6501. The 2026 (40th) Icheon Ceramics Festival (이천도자기축제) ran 4/24–5/5 and is over (press).
  • Lines from the older guide such as the Sagimakgol (사기막골) workshop and "overseas shipping" have no source, so we removed them. Transport from Seoul (Gyeonggang Line) is also unconfirmed.

📜 Jeonju — Hanji

  • Jeonju Hanji Museum (전주한지박물관): 09–17, closed Sundays, Mondays and public holidays, free (experience included) (official, #384). It sits apart from the Hanok Village, so take a taxi or bus.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Using the older guide's fees — none are posted officially. Check the booking page.

2. Going to the Craft Museum on a Monday — it is closed. Bukchon is open (closed only Seollal and Chuseok).

3. Showing up at a Bukchon workshop without booking — booking is first come, first served through the public reservation service.

4. Expecting the Icheon spring festival — it is over. In autumn, go for the biennale (through 11/1).

5. Looking for 'K-pop hanji merch' — no source. Look at finished pieces in the Craft Museum shop and Bukchon workshops instead.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. A half day in Seoul? — Anguk Station Exit 1 → Seoul Museum of Craft Art experience (booking) → Bukchon Traditional Crafts Experience Center (booking) → Samcheong galleries (#285).

2. What is free? — Craft Museum admission and the Jeonju Hanji Museum (experience included). Program fees are separate and not posted.

3. English? — No official page states English guidance at any of these places (unconfirmed). Call 1330.

4. A day of ceramics? — The Icheon biennale (9/18–11/1). Call for hours and fees.

5. With children? — Whether any of Bukchon's 46 programs accept children is on the booking page.

6. What ends in October? — The Craft Museum's 'Najeon Box' on 10/27 and Namsangol's 'Artist's Time' on 10/25.
