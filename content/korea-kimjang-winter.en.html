🥬 Kimchi Matrix (UNESCO context)

Listed by UNESCO (December 5, 2013)

  • Official name: "Kimjang, making and sharing kimchi in the Republic of Korea"
  • Reason for listing: Community, generational transmission, seasonal food culture (Cooking itself
  • Note: Kimchi itself is not listed. Kimjang (making and sharing) tradition is subject to registration.

Kimjang Core Matrix

steps Description time

3. Making kimchi Pickled cabbage + seasoning mix 1h
4. Fermentation 1 week (room temperature) + 6 months (cold temperature) 6 months

⚠ Medical/Safety/Dietary disclaimer

Kimjang = Fermented food·Allergies (shrimp salt, anchovy fish sauce, crustaceans, garlic, red pepper powder) Prior notice required. Option to adjust spiciness = White kimchi, Dongchimi ([#822 cross](#822)). Pregnant women, high blood pressure, kidney patients = Be careful of salt. Emergency ☎ 1339 (English) / ☎ 1330 (Tourist Interpreter).

Dietary restrictions fallback:

  • Halal·Kosher: Regular kimchi = Non-halal (including salted shrimp and anchovy fish sauce) → Temple food kimchi (#800 cross) = Vegetarian·Halal·Kosher OK
  • Vegetarian/Vegan: Regular kimchi = Non-vegetarian → Temple food kimchi or vegan kimchi (soy sauce-based, some stores)
  • Gluten free: Kimjang itself = OK (soy sauce

✈️ Pain points for foreigners #1 — Guide to taking kimchi

  • Packaging: Vacuum packaging (free in some stores, 5,000-10,000 won in some)
  • Expiration date: Vacuum = 1-3 months (refrigerated) · 1-2 weeks after opening
  • Checked baggage OK: Kimchi = Liquid type (>100ml) = Checked baggage only·Carry-on X
  • Import restrictions by country:
  • Australia/New Zealand: Strict import of fermented foods, prior notification recommended
  • Some U.S. states: Vegetable fermentation and reporting recommended
  • EU·Japan·China: Mostly OK, small amount
  • Recommended stores: Insadong Authentic, Jeonju Hanok Village, Duty Free Shop Vacuum Packaging (Incheon Airport)
