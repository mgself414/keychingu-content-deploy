Why is Gwangjang Market a long-established store now? — Netflix Street Food Foreigner #1 vlog market

Gwangjang Market opened in 1905 and is the first permanent market in Seoul with a 120-year history. Beyond a simple traditional market, Netflix Street Food: Asia Season 1 "Seoul" episode main stage explodes in global recognition of foreigners — It is a Korean #1 food market that is exposed as #1 when searching YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram vlog "Korean Street Food." Exits 8 and 10 of Jongno 5-ga Station You can immediately sample a full set of Bindaetteok, Yukhoe, Yakyak Gimbap, and Hotteok within a 1-minute walk.

In particular, real long-established stores such as 3 generations and 60 years of Parkgane's Bindaetteok·1956 Buchon Yukhoe (60 years, Michelin)·Netflix appearance Mother and Daughter Little Drug Kimbap·100% mung bean original Sunhee's Bindaetteok·Jinju Yukhoe's 4 branches — Visited by many foreign vlog hunters, BTS, and Blackpink K-pop enthusiasts. If Namdaemun Market (#581) is an “authentic Korean food establishment” cluster, Gwangjang Market is a “street food No. 1” cluster — the two markets have different characteristics.

This matrix classifies the 12 proven Gwangjang Market long-established stores into 4 axes (Bindaetteok, mung bean pancake / raw meat, raw meat / drug gimbap, snacks / hotteok, noodles, snacks). Gwangjang Market internal pool cover + nearby Jongno 5-ga long-established store cross.

FAQ 6 — Frequently asked questions about Gwangjang Market by foreigners

Q1. Where should I start when I first visit Gwangjang Market?

  • Yukhoe Mania (Vlog No. 1): #4 Buchon Yukhoe (Michelin 1956) → #5 Jinju Yukhoe (4th branch distributed)

  • Old-established restaurant (Dakhanmari): #12 Jinokhwa·Gwangjang Chicken Hanmari (open all year round)

Q2. Gwangjang Market vs. Namdaemun Market (#581) — Which one fits?

  • Gwangjang Market (#582): Netflix Street Food + No. 1 foreign vlog + Full set of Bindaetteok, Yukhoe, Yakgi Gimbap, and Hotteok — MZ, Instagram, SNS mania fit
  • Namdaemun Market (#581): Best market in 1414 #1 + Boiled cutlassfish, Pyongyang naengmyeon, and tail gomtang Authentic Korean food — JP·EN Authentic long-established restaurant mania fit
  • Recommended: First visit = Gwangjang Market (foreigner vlog verification), authentic enthusiast = Namdaemun
  • cover both: Jongno 5-ga Station (plaza) → Hoehyeon Station (Namdaemun) Subway Line 1 3rd stop (30-minute walk)

Q3. A store that offers English menus and communication for foreigners?

  • EN Menu OK 1st Place: #4 Buchon Yukhoe (Michelin + Foreigner vlog) / #7 Mother and Daughter Little Drug Gimbap (Netflix)
  • Point to photo menu: #1 Park Gane · #2 Sunhee's (1-2 types of Bindaetteok) / #10 Gwangjang Hotteok (Simple Hotteok)
  • Use of translation apps: Many small, long-established stores — Papago/Google Translate camera translation
  • Simple Korean: “bindaetteok hana” / “yukhoe bibimbap” / “mayak gimbap”

Q4. Average price range of long-established stores in Gwangjang Market?

  • 1 piece of Bindaetteok: KRW 6,000~9,000 (OK for 1-2 people to share)
  • Yukhoe: 25,000~40,000 won (share for 2-3 people)
  • Yukhoe Bibimbap: 12,000~18,000 won
  • Drug Kimbap: 6 pieces 3,000 won / set 11,000 won (Drug Kimbap + Tteokbokki + Bindaetteok)
  • Hotteok: Cheese 2,500 won, sweet red beans 2,000 won
  • One whole chicken: 30,000~50,000 won (2-3 people)
  • Handmade Kalguksu: 8,000~12,000 won

Q5. Payment/cash/card tips?

  • Focus on cash payments: Snack alleys, hotteok, and many small, long-established stores
  • Card OK Store: #4 Buchon Yukhoe / #5 Jinju Yukhoe / #1 Park Gane / #2 Sunhee’s (large scale)
  • Prepare Korean Won: KRW 30,000~50,000 cash recommended per person (cover 3-4 stores per day)
  • Jongno 5-ga Station ATM: Many near exits 8 and 10 (KB, Shinhan, Woori)

Q6. Do you plan to visit on Sundays and public holidays?

  • Open on Sundays (confirmed): #4 Buchon Yukhoe·#5 Jinju Yukhoe (open all year round)·#7 Mother and Daughter Little Drug Kimbap (daily)·#12 Jinokhwa Chicken Hanmari (open all year round)
  • Note: Closed on Sundays: Some small, long-established stores
  • Closed on the third Wednesday: #1 Park Gane — Open on the first, second, and fourth Wednesdays
  • Closed on Tuesdays: #2 Soonhee’s — When visiting on Tuesdays, #1 Park Gane cover
