🎯 Why the "Jindo Travel Map" Meta Guide?

Foreign visitors to Jindo (진도) — KTX Gwangju (광주) transfer 4.5h. Key attractions: (1) Jindo Moses Miracle Sea Road (진도 신비의 바닷길) (Korea's #1 Moses Miracle + 500,000+ foreign visitors at March festival) (2) Jindo Dogs (진돗개) (Natural Monument #1, Jindo Dog Theme Park) (3) Jindo Arirang (진도아리랑) (UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity) (4) Unrimsanbang (운림산방) (Korea's #1 Southern School of Painting base) (5) Battle of Myeongnyang (명량대첩) (Yi Sun-sin 1597).

This guide covers Jindo's 4 Pillars and 12 Signatures.
