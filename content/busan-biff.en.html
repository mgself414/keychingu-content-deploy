Busan International Film Festival and Centum City·Old Downtown — 7 Spots: Busan Cinema Center, Spa Land, Art Museum, Movie Museum, Hours and Fares

Subtitle: For first-time visitors to Busan, this guide groups the basic information on the Busan International Film Festival (BIFF), 6 film and culture facilities in the Centum City area where the festival is held, and 1 movie museum in the old downtown. The 31st festival was held on 2026-10-06 to 15, and a general screening cost 10,000 won. Busan Cinema Center shows films even when there is no festival, and an adult regular ticket is 8,000 won (for mainstream film programs). Spa Land is open 09:00–22:00 at 26,000 won for adults, and Busan Museum of Art is closed on Mondays and is free for a limited time as of 2026-11-25. Hours and fares are values seen on 2026-11-25.

ISSUE 06 · #247 · 2026-11-25 · Busan Centum City·Film

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 7

This list is made for foreign travelers visiting Busan who picked it because they heard the name of the film festival, and for those who want a place to spend half a day indoors rather than at the beach.

The Busan International Film Festival is an event, and the 31st edition was held for 10 days, 2026-10-06 to 15, and has ended. So this issue puts the festival information first, and then lists 7 places that are open even when it is not festival time.

It is divided into 2 zones.

  • Centum City zone (6 spots): Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당), Shinsegae Centum City (신세계백화점 센텀시티점), Spa Land (스파랜드), BEXCO (벡스코), Busan Museum of Art (부산시립미술관), Space Lee Ufan (이우환공간).
  • Old downtown zone (1 spot): Busan Museum of Movies (부산영화체험박물관).

The 6 spots in Centum City (센텀시티) split between 2 stations on Subway Line 2. Busan Cinema Center, Shinsegae and Spa Land and BEXCO are reached on foot from Centum City Station (센텀시티역), and Busan Museum of Art and Space Lee Ufan from BEXCO Station (벡스코역). Busan Museum of Movies is not in Centum City but in Jung-gu (중구), and is reached on foot from Line 1 Jungang Station (중앙역).

Other nearby places are in other issues. BIFF Square (BIFF광장) in Nampo-dong (남포동) is in #244, Dalmaji-gil, Cheongsapo and the Beach Train are in #243, and Gijang and Haedong Yonggungsa are in #270.

✈ From Gimhae Airport and Busan Station to Centum City

  • Busan Airport Limousine 1 (부산 공항 리무진 1) passes through Centum City. According to the Busan City English notice (read 2026-11-25), the airport limousine was reorganized on 2026-01-05. Route 1, toward Haeundae·Gijang, stops at BEXCO and Shinsegae Centum City. It costs 9,500 won for adults, 7,600 won for teenagers and 4,800 won for children. The Busan City notice lists Route 1 as 2 lines. The first and last buses from the airport are Jangsan line 07:10 · 16:50 and Gijang line 10:10 · 21:40. BEXCO and Shinsegae Centum City are in the stop list of the notice. Cash is not accepted, and you pay with a transit card or credit card.
  • Route 2, toward Seomyeon·Bujeon, does not pass through Centum City.
  • You cannot get from Busan Station to Centum City in 1 subway ride. Busan Station is a Subway Line 1 station and Centum City Station is a Line 2 station. There is no through train connecting the 2 stations.
  • From Busan Station, 1 bus goes all the way. According to the official Busan Cinema Center guide, take bus 5-1, 40, 139 or 1001 from the stop in front of Busan Station and get off at 'In front of Shinsegae Department Store'.

🎬 Busan International Film Festival (BIFF) — What to Know First

The Busan International Film Festival (BIFF) is a film festival held in Busan every year. The information below is from the official festival website.

When It Started and When It Was Held

  • 1st edition: held from Friday, September 13 to Saturday, September 21, 1996. According to the official festival record, it screened 169 films from 31 countries, and the screening venues were the Suyeongman outdoor screening site (수영만 야외상영관) and the Nampo-dong theater district.
  • 31st edition: held for 10 days, from Tuesday, October 6 to Thursday, October 15, 2026.
  • Character of the festival: the festival introduces itself on its official website as an 'A-list festival' officially designated by the International Federation of Film Producers Associations (FIAPF).

Where It Was Held (31st Edition)

The official festival overview gave the screening venues as "8 theaters, 31 screens".

  • Busan Cinema Center
  • CGV Centum City (CGV센텀시티) (35 Centum Nam-daero, 7th floor of Shinsegae Department Store)
  • Lotte Cinema Centum City (롯데시네마 센텀시티) (59 Centum Nam-daero, 8th floor of Lotte Department Store)
  • Korean Film Council Standard Screening Room (영화진흥위원회 표준시사실)
  • Sohyang Theater Woori Bank Hall (소향씨어터 우리은행홀)
  • Busan Viewer Media Center Open Hall (부산시청자미디어센터 공개홀)
  • Community BIFF (커뮤니티비프): Megabox Busan Theater (메가박스 부산극장), Catholic Center Space 101.1 (가톨릭센터 공간 101.1)

Busan Cinema Center and CGV Centum City are in Haeundae-gu (해운대구). The Culture Hall on the 9th floor of Shinsegae Department Store was also used as a festival venue.

How Tickets Were Bought (31st Edition)

The following is based on the 31st edition (2026-10-06 to 15).

  • The rule for the 31st edition was online sales. The official festival guide said "all regular seats are sold online". The booking site was the ticket booking page of the festival website (biff.kr).
  • Only leftover and canceled tickets appeared at the on-site ticket booths. Only when tickets were not sold out online or canceled tickets appeared could they be bought at the BIFF ticket booths during the festival.
  • There was mobile phone number verification. For online booking, new bookers registered and went through mobile phone number verification once, the first time.

  • BIFF ticket booths: there were booths at the Busan Cinema Center outdoor ticket booth (October 6–15, 08:30–20:30), on the 6th floor of Cinemountain (시네마운틴), on the 7th floor of CGV Centum City, on the 8th floor of Lotte Cinema Centum City and at other venues.

What to Follow in the Theater (31st Edition)

The following is based on the 31st edition (2026-10-06 to 15).

  • Entry was restricted 15 minutes after the screening started.
  • You could not enter with a screenshot of a mobile ticket.
  • Children under 4 years old could not enter.
  • All seats at the Busan Cinema Center outdoor theater (Roof Theater) were unreserved.

🚦 Before You Walk — Shared Notes

  • The free period can end. The Busan Museum of Art website describes the free admission as "temporary".
  • There is another station with the same name. On Kakao Map, Subway Line 2 BEXCO Station and Donghae Line BEXCO Station are registered as different stations. The exit numbers in this issue are for Line 2 BEXCO Station.
  • Call 119 for accidents or emergencies and 112 to report a crime.

① Centum City Zone — 6 Spots

This is the zone that uses Subway Line 2 Centum City Station and BEXCO Station. The first 4 spots are reached on foot from Centum City Station, and the last 2 from BEXCO Station.

Busan Cinema Center

The main stage of the Busan International Film Festival, and a theater that shows films even when there is no festival. The values below are from the official Busan Cinema Center website, except for lines that say "Kakao Map".

  • Big Roof: the large roof covering the top of the building. It is 60.8 m wide, 162.5 m long, weighs 4,000 tons and covers 10,000 m², and it carries 23,142 LED sets. Busan Cinema Center introduces this roof as "registered in the Guinness Book as the world's longest roof among cantilever-structured buildings supported by a single column".

  • Special programs differ by program. The Cinematheque special program of 2026-11-24 to 12-12 is 7,000 won regular and has no screenings every Monday. There are also programs with a flat 4,000 won and a flat 5,000 won.
  • Booking: online booking on the website and on-site ticket booths are both used. The booth is the cinema ticket booth on the 6th floor of Busan Cinema Center.
  • Refunds: a full refund is given until 20 minutes before the screening starts. On-site cancellations are accepted 09:00–18:00.
  • Facility hours (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25): daily 09:00–21:00. Screening times follow the program timetable.
  • Architecture tour: there is an architecture tour where a docent walks with you. It is 5,000 won per person and takes about 40 min. It takes 5 to 30 people at a time, and you must book 10 days before the start. It is for ages 13 and older (middle school students and older). Inquiries are at 051-780-6143. Booking requires 5 people, so it is not a program a solo traveler can join on the same day.
  • Address: 120 Suyeonggang-byeon-daero (U-dong 1467), Haeundae-gu, Busan. Phone 051-780-6000.
  • Directions (official Busan Cinema Center guide): it is a 5 min walk from Subway Line 2 Centum City Station Exit 12 or 6.
  • Bus (official Busan Cinema Center guide): get off at 'In front of Shinsegae Department Store' and walk for 5 min. Buses 5-1, 36, 39, 40, 63, 115, 139, 141, 155, 181, 307 (seated), 1001 (express) and 1002 (express) stop there.
  • Timing: the Busan Cinema Center guide says a 5 min walk, and Kakao Map directions (Exit 6, 847 m) say 14 min. If you bought a ticket with a set time, move on the basis of 14 min.

Shinsegae Centum City

  • What it is: the Guinness World Records page lists Shinsegae Centumcity Department Store as the 'largest department store'. The area is 293,905 m² and the certification date is June 26, 2009.
  • Hours (official Shinsegae Department Store guide): 10:30–20:00. On extended-hours days it is open until 20:30, and the extension is mainly on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays. The restaurant floors are 11:00–21:00.
  • Closing days: closing days differ by month. The closing days of the month are shown on the store calendar on the department store website.
  • Film and culture facilities: there is the Shinsegae Culture Hall on the 9th floor, and Shinsegae Gallery. The Culture Hall was used as a Busan International Film Festival venue. On the 7th floor there is CGV Centum City, a festival screening venue.
  • Address: 35 Centum Nam-daero, Haeundae-gu, Busan.
  • Directions: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 3 min walk (171 m) from Line 2 Centum City Station Exit 12.

Spa Land

A spa facility on the 1st floor of Shinsegae Centum City. The values below are from the official Shinsegae Department Store page, except for lines that say "Kakao Map".

  • Hours (official page, 2026-11-25): 09:00–22:00. Entry closes at 21:00.
  • Directions: it is on the 1st floor of the department store. The department store is a 3 min walk from Centum City Station Exit 12 per Kakao Map directions.
  • Fare: adults 26,000 won, students 21,000 won.
  • Student fare: it is split by year of birth. The reference year changes every year.
  • Time of use: once you enter, you can use it for up to 4 hours. If you go over, 5,000 won is added per hour. If you spend 10,000 won or more inside Spa Land, not counting the admission fee, you can use it for up to 6 hours.
  • Age: elementary school students and older can enter. Elementary school students use it with a guardian of the same sex.
  • Luggage: suitcases cannot be brought into the inside locker room. Leave them in the suitcase lockers in front of the ticket booth. The basic charge is 3,000 won for 5 hours and 1,000 won per additional hour.
  • Timing: entry closes at 21:00 and it closes at 22:00. To use the full 4 hours, go in before 18:00. The department store closes at 20:00 (20:30 on extended-hours days), so see the department store first and put Spa Land after it.

BEXCO

  • What it is: a place where events are held. What there is to see depends on the event held that day.
  • Hours (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25): 10:00–18:00. It differs by event.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25): 1500 U-dong, Haeundae-gu, Busan.
  • Directions: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 6 min walk (374 m) from Line 2 Centum City Station Exit 1.
  • Station name: Line 2 and the Donghae Line each have a separate BEXCO Station. The place Kakao Map directions use for the walk to the BEXCO building is Centum City Station Exit 1.

Busan Museum of Art

The values below are from the official Busan Museum of Art website, except for lines that say "Kakao Map". The website shows "Main building operating normally" as of 2026-11-25.

  • Viewing hours: Tuesday to Sunday 10:00–18:00. Entry closes at 17:00.
  • Closing days: every Monday and January 1. If Monday is a public holiday, it closes the next day.
  • Fare: free for a limited time as of 2026-11-25. The regular prices on the fare table are 5,000 won for adults and 3,000 won for teenagers (ages 7–19) for the main building, and 7,000 won for adults for the combined ticket that includes Space Lee Ufan.
  • Address: 58 APEC-ro, Haeundae-gu, Busan.
  • Directions: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 5 min walk (298 m) from Subway Line 2 BEXCO Station Exit 5.
  • Do not mix up the stations: the nearest station is BEXCO Station, not Centum City Station.

Space Lee Ufan

An exhibition space that the Busan Museum of Art website guides together with the main building. The values below are from the official Busan Museum of Art website, except for lines that say "Kakao Map". The website shows "Space Lee Ufan operating normally" as of 2026-11-25.

  • Viewing hours: 10:00–18:00. Entry closes at 17:30.
  • Fare: free for a limited time for adults as well, as of 2026-11-25.
  • Inquiries: 051-740-2601.
  • Directions: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 6 min walk (391 m) from Line 2 BEXCO Station Exit 5.
  • How to combine: it uses the same Exit 5 as Busan Museum of Art. From the exit it is 298 m to the main museum building and 391 m to Space Lee Ufan. The main building's entry closes 30 minutes earlier, at 17:00, so see the main building first and then go to Space Lee Ufan.

🗺 Routes — Half Day and Full Day

The order is set to match each spot's opening and closing times. The 6 spots in Centum City are grouped into 4 on the Centum City Station side and 2 on the BEXCO Station side.

Half Day A — Art Museums on the BEXCO Station Side (avoid Mondays)

  1. Line 2 BEXCO Station Exit 5 → Busan Museum of Art (5 min walk per Kakao Map directions). It opens at 10:00.
  2. Space Lee Ufan (6 min walk from Exit 5). Entry is until 17:30.
  3. Move to Centum City Station on Line 2 and go from Exit 1 → BEXCO (6 min walk). Include it on a day with an event.

Half Day B — Indoors on the Centum City Station Side (any day of the week, except department store closing days)

  1. Line 2 Centum City Station Exit 12 → Shinsegae Centum City (3 min walk per Kakao Map directions). It opens at 10:30.
  2. Spa Land on the 1st floor of the department store. 4 hours per entry.
  3. Busan Cinema Center (5–14 min walk from Centum City Station Exit 12 or 6 (5 min per Busan Cinema Center guide, 14 min per Kakao Map)). Walk under the Big Roof, and watch a film if you bought a ticket.

Full Day — Art Museum in the Morning, Spa Land in the Afternoon, Film in the Evening (avoid Mondays)

Busan Museum of Movies stops ticketing at 17:00 and is in Jung-gu, so put it on a day you tour the old downtown.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. The Busan International Film Festival is a 10-day event, and Busan Cinema Center is a building. The 31st festival ended on 2026-10-06 to 15. Busan Cinema Center shows films regardless of the festival, and the regular fare of the 2026 mainstream film program is 8,000 won. For the 31st festival, all regular seats were sold online, and only leftover and canceled tickets appeared at the on-site ticket booths.
  2. There are 2 stations even within Centum City. Busan Cinema Center, Shinsegae Department Store, Spa Land and BEXCO are reached on foot from Line 2 Centum City Station. Busan Museum of Art and Space Lee Ufan are reached from Line 2 BEXCO Station Exit 5 (per Kakao Map directions). The BEXCO building is reached on foot from Centum City Station Exit 1. The Donghae Line also has a separate BEXCO Station.
  3. Not every place with 'BIFF' or 'film' in its name is in Centum City. Busan Museum of Movies is in Jung-gu and is a 5 min walk from Line 1 Jungang Station Exit 1 (Kakao Map). BIFF Square in Nampo-dong is also in the old downtown. Busan Station is on Line 1 and Centum City Station is on Line 2, so you must change lines to go between the 2 zones.

⚙ 3 FAQs

  1. Which spots charge a fare and which are free? — The places that charge a fare are Spa Land (adults 26,000 won, students 21,000 won, 4 hours), Busan Museum of Movies (adults 10,000 won, combined 14,000 won), film admission at Busan Cinema Center (regular 8,000 won, for mainstream film programs) and the Busan Cinema Center architecture tour (5,000 won per person). Busan Museum of Art and Space Lee Ufan are free for a limited time as of 2026-11-25, and the regular price of the main building is 5,000 won for adults. Shinsegae Department Store has no admission fee.
  2. How do I get from Gimhae Airport to Centum City? — Airport Limousine 1 (toward Haeundae·Gijang) stops at BEXCO and Shinsegae Centum City. Per the Busan City notice, it costs 9,500 won for adults. The first and last buses from the airport are Jangsan line 07:10 · 16:50 and Gijang line 10:10 · 21:40. The Busan Cinema Center guide says it takes 50 min from the airport to 'Shinsegae Department Store'.
  3. Can I go to Centum City on a Monday? — Busan Museum of Art and Busan Museum of Movies are closed every Monday. The Busan Cinema Center Cinematheque special program of 2026-11-24 to 12-12 also has no screenings on Mondays. Spa Land is open 09:00–22:00 (as of 2026-11-25) and Shinsegae Department Store is open 10:30–20:00, and the department store's closing days differ by month and are shown on the website calendar.
