⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "BIFF is only in October": Besides BIFF (October, 1 week), the Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당) is open year-round + BIFF Square (BIFF 거리) (Nampo-dong) year-round + deep dive for foreign film enthusiasts.

2. "Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당) is a regular cinema": Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당) = Korea's No. 1 Cinema + BIFF Headquarters + No. 1 spot for foreign enthusiasts + 4,000 m² external LED (No. 1 spot for night views in the outdoor plaza during BIFF).

3. "Haedong Yonggungsa Temple (해동용궁사) is hard to get to": Haedong Yonggungsa Temple (해동용궁사) = Busan's (부산) No. 1 Temple + Temple on the Sea + Foreigners' No. 1 Instagram spot + adjacent to Gijang (기장) + Foreign enthusiasts' No. 1 priority.

4. "Gijang (기장) outskirts are inaccessible": Gijang (기장) = Busan's (부산) No. 1 outskirts deep dive + Jukseong Dream Cathedral (죽성성당)·Haedong Yonggungsa Temple (해동용궁사)·outskirts beaches·foreign enthusiasts.

5. "Centum City Shinsegae (센텀시티 신세계) is just a department store": Centum City Shinsegae (센텀시티 신세계) = Guinness World's No. 1 Department Store + Foreign enthusiasts' No. 1 spot + adjacent to BIFF Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당).
