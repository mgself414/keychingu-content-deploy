🎯 Why a Jjim 4-Channel Comparison Guide

Even once you know the [#894 Jjim 6-Type Matrix](#894) + [#895 Workflow](#895) — channel choice is the next decision. Korean jjim channels = (1) Andong jjimdak (안동찜닭) (nationwide #1 traditional) · (2) Hanjeongsik (한정식) galbi-jjim (갈비찜) (royal cuisine #1) · (3) Masan agu-jjim (마산 아구찜) (spicy #1) · (4) Jagalchi (자갈치) haemul-jjim (해물찜) (seafood cross) — 4 options.

🥘 4-Channel Deep Dive

🐔 Andong jjimdak (안동찜닭)

  • Recommended: Andong traditional · nationwide Andong jjimdak cluster · MZ
  • Price: ₩25,000~50,000 (~$19–38) per order

🥩 Hanjeongsik galbi-jjim (갈비찜)

  • Recommended: La Yeon at The Shilla Seoul · Samcheonggak (삼청각) · Insadong (인사동)
  • Price: hanjeongsik course ₩40–150k (~$30–113) / à la carte ₩30–60k (~$23–45)

🐟 Masan agu-jjim (아구찜)

  • Recommended: Masan traditional · Seoul old-school joints · MZ
  • Price: ₩25,000~50,000 (~$19–38) per order

🦀 Jagalchi haemul-jjim (해물찜)

  • Recommended: Jagalchi · Tongyeong (통영) · seafood specialty restaurants
  • Price: ₩30,000~80,000 (~$23–60) per order

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. "The 4 channels = about the same": Completely different. Traditional = Andong / Royal cuisine = hanjeongsik / Spicy = Masan / Seafood = Jagalchi.

2. "Andong jjimdak = generic": Andong = nationwide #1 traditional.

3. "Hanjeongsik = only expensive": Hanjeongsik = à la carte from ₩30,000 (~$23) possible.

4. "Masan agu-jjim = ordinary spicy": Masan = Korea's spicy #1.

5. "Haemul-jjim = sashimi": Haemul-jjim = a spicy steamed dish · different from the #864 hoe (회) cluster.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. First channel: Andong jjimdak (#1) → hanjeongsik.

2. Instagram: Masan agu-jjim.

3. MZ: Masan agu-jjim.

4. Spicy: Masan (#1).

5. Hanjeongsik: galbi-jjim.

6. Allergies & pregnancy: See [#894](#894).

7. Payment: Card OK · split the bill.

8. VAT: No refund.

⚠ Medical, Safety & Dietary Disclaimer

All 4 jjim channels are ordinary food. Declare allergies in advance. ☎ 1339.

Dietary restriction fallback: Halal & Kosher = chicken/beef OK · pork no · seafood OK / Vegetarian = not vegetarian / Gluten-free = Tamari.
