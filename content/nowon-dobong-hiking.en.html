⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Seoul Hiking = Bukhansan Bay" — Nowon-Dobong 3 famous mountain passes

The most common mistake in foreign hiking routes. Only Bukhansan Mountain is recognized, but Suraksan Mountain, Dobongsan Mountain, and Bulamsan Mountain are not known. In reality, Suraksan Mountain, Dobongsan Mountain, and Bulamsan Mountain are concentrated in Nowon and Dobong-gu + The hiking trail is shorter than Bukhansan Mountain and there are fewer foreign enthusiasts. Dobongsan Mountain is one of the five famous mountains in Korea (Seorak, Jiri, Gyeryong, Sobaek, and Dobong), but its recognition among foreigners is less than 1/10. If you want to deep dive into the city-wide walkability covered in #176 walkable_seoul into an actual hiking course, Nowon and Dobong are the top places.

2. "Climbing = Full hiking gear required" — Tried with regular sneakers and slipped

The most common injury among foreigners climbing a mountain for the first time. Some sections of Suraksan, Dobongsan, and Bulamsan have rocks and pebbles — Hiking shoes or trail running shoes are recommended. There are reports every year of people slipping while wearing regular city sneakers and ending up in the emergency room. If you don't have hiking shoes, you can rent or purchase them for a day at Nowon Station Hiking Equipment Store (Military Gear, Onsite, Geni) or global brand stores. In addition to hiking shoes, 1L of water + snacks + hat + sunscreen are required.

3. "After hiking, makgeolli and cold noodles are available only at the mountain entrance" — Do not enter Nowon city

The pattern is to eat only makgeolli and mung bean pancake at the restaurant at the entrance of the mountain after descending the mountain and then return to the hotel. In fact, many makgeolli, cold noodles, and BBQ signature stores within walking distance of Nowon Station, Junggye Station, and Changdong Station — There are more stores in Nowon downtown, such as Surak-io Naengmyeon (9,227 reviews), Junggye Gogi Party (7,523 reviews), and Taereung Pungcheon Eel (4,240 reviews). Entrance of the mountain after descending → Move downtown to Nowon Station/Junggye Station The pattern is the real Nowon deep dive.

Nowon and Dobong hiking scenery

Nowon-gu and Dobong-gu in northeastern Seoul are Korea's strongest urban hiking base, containing three of Seoul's five famous mountains (Suraksan Mountain, Dobongsan Mountain, and Bulamsan Mountain). Although it is a bed town with an area of ​​35.4㎢ and a population of 500,000, it is possible to access the hiking trail within an hour from a hotel in the city by transferring to 5 subway lines: Line 4, Line 7, Uisinseol Line, Line 1, and Gyeongchun Line + Directly connected to the hiking trail at the foot of the mountain.**

Suraksan (638m) is a famous granite mountain on the border of Nowon-gu and Uijeongbu-si. 10-minute walk from Nowon Station and Suraksan Station (Line 7). Foreigner climbing #1 — 3 beats: flat hiking trail + 360-degree panorama from the summit + quietness.

Dobongsan (740m) is one of the five famous mountains in Korea. Dobong-gu/Uijeongbu-si border, 5-minute walk from Dobongsan Station (Line 1, Line 7). Uiam, Jaunbong, Sinseondae Peak County + Eastern end of Bukhansan National Park. Although it is less recognized by foreigners than Bukhansan Mountain, it is rated equally by mountaineering enthusiasts.

Bulam Mountain (508m) is a granite peak on the border of Nowon-gu and Namyangju-si. Enter Sanggye Station (Line 4) + Junggye Station (Line 7). Sunrise and sunset attractions + Night view of Nowon Bedtown.

Seoul Trail Section 1 (Suyu Station ~ Ui Station, 8.5km, 4 hours) is a course connecting Nowon and Dobong along the eastern circumference of Bukhansan National Park. English sign + QR code + restroom available. No. 1 foreign trekking enthusiasts.

① Three famous mountains (3)

1. Suraksan (638m)

  • Signature: A famous granite mountain on the border between Nowon and Uijeongbu. Foreigners entering the mountain #1 — The hiking trail is flat + quiet + summit panorama.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — 70% of hiking trail signs in English, directly connected to Line 7
  • Recommended course: 10-minute walk from Suraksan Station (Line 7) → Suraksan Falls → Dosolbong → Summit (2~2.5 hours one way, 4~5 hours round trip)
  • Highlight: 360-degree view of Nowon, Uijeongbu, and the Han River from the summit + Trekking on the Dosolbong ridge
  • Tip: Spring/Fall Recommended (April~June/September~November). Hiking shoes are required. Last water fountain 30 minutes before summit — 2L of water recommended. After descending the mountain, go to the restaurant area of ​​Nowon Station.

2. Dobongsan (740m)

  • Signature: One of Korea’s five famous mountains. Eastern end of Bukhansan National Park + Uiam, Jaunbong, and Sinseondae peaks. Foreign recognition is lower than Bukhansan, but enthusiast ratings are equal.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — 80% of hiking trail signs in English, transfer to Lines 1 and 7
  • Recommended Course: 5-minute walk from Dobongsan Station (Line 1, Line 7) → Dobong Exploration Support Center → Uiam (detour) or Jaunbong (go straight) → Summit (3 hours one way, 5-6 hours round trip)
  • Highlights: View of Uiam, Jaunbong, and Sinseondae peaks + Dobong Valley + Bukhansan Mountain ridge
  • Tip: Advanced course — Jaunbong and Sinseondae have some rocky sections. Beginners should bypass Uiam. Receive a free English map at the Dobong Exploration Support Center. After descending, go to the restaurant area at Dobongsan Station or Nowon Station.

3. Buramsan (508m)

  • Signature: Granite peak on the border of Nowon-gu and Namyangju-si. Sunrise and sunset attractions + Night view of Nowon Bedtown. Short hiking time + no foreigners.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★☆☆ — 60% English signs, transfer to Lines 4 and 7
  • Recommended course: 15-minute walk from Sanggye Station (Line 4) or Junggye Station (Line 7) → Hakdoam → Summit (1.5 to 2 hours one way, 3 to 4 hours round trip)
  • Highlight: Summit sunrise and sunset + Nowon night view + Hakdoam rock-carved Buddha (treasure)
  • Tip: Short hiking time (half-day course) — Recommended for foreigners who are short on time. Enter Yeoksan at sunset time (normal at 17:00-18:00 in spring and fall). After descending the mountain, move to the Sanggye and Junggye restaurants.

② Seoul Dulle-gil (1)

4. Seoul Trail Section 1 (Suyu~Ui)

  • Signature: The first of 21 sections of Seoul Dulle-gil. Suyu Station → Ui Station, 8.5km, 4 hours, Moderate. A course for foreign enthusiasts that connects Nowon, Gangbuk, and Dobong along the eastern perimeter of Bukhansan National Park.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — 100% English signage, QR code, shelter restroom, and drinking fountain
  • Recommended Course: Suyu Station (Line 4) → April 19 Cemetery → Yeongbong Ridge → Jeongneung → Ui Station (Ui New Seol Line) (total 8.5km, 4 hours)
  • Highlights: April 19 Cemetery (commemorating the April 19 Revolution in Korea) + Yeongbong Ridge + Jeongneung (Royal Tomb of Queen Shindeok of the Joseon Dynasty)
  • Tip: Receive a free English guidebook + stamp book at Seoul Tourist Plaza (City Hall, Myeongdong) before the start. Section 1 + Section 8 (Seoul Forest ~ Gwangnaru) is recommended for foreigners to try for the first time. After arriving at Ui Station, transfer to Line 4 on the Ui New Line → Move to downtown Nowon and Changdong.

Recommended route

[A] Suraksan one-day hike + Nowon Station restaurant course (one day)

Breakfast: Nowon Station → Gubeopwon Croquette (snack on the way to the hike) → Line 7 → Suraksan Station

Morning ~ Lunch: Suraksan Mountain Climbing (4-5 hours round trip) → Summit → Descent

Afternoon: Nowon Station → Daham Dakgalbi or Surak Io Naengmyeon → Cafe Forest (break)

  • Recommended time: 08:00 ~ 18:00
  • No. 1 foreigner’s first hiking course in Nowon

[B] Dobongsan + Dullegil Course (1 day)

Morning: Dobongsan Station → Dobong Exploration Support Center (receive English map)

Morning~Lunch: Hiking Dobongsan Mountain (4~5 hours by Uiam detour or 5~6 hours by Jaunbong Peak)

Afternoon: Ui Station (Ui New Seol Line) → Part of Section 1 of Seoul Dulle-gil (2-3 hours) → Line 4 → Nowon Station → Junggye Meat Party

  • Recommended time: 07:00 ~ 20:00
  • Trekking Mania Full Day Course

[C] Mt. Bulam sunrise + bakery/cafe course (half day)

Sunrise: 04-05:00 Sanggye Station → Bulam Mountain hiking (1.5-2 hours) → Summit sunrise (spring/fall 05-06:00)

Morning ~ Lunch: Hassan → Nowon Station → Gubeonwon Croquette + Cafe Forest

  • Recommended time: 04:00 ~ 13:00
  • Sunrise Mania short course

[D] 3 Mountain Full Challenge (2 days)

Day 1: Suraksan Mountain (morning) + Nowon Station Restaurant (afternoon) + Bulamsan Mountain Sunset (evening)

Day 2: Dobongsan Mountain (morning) + Seoul Dulle-gil Section 1 (afternoon)

  • Recommended time: 2 nights Nowon/Changdong hotel base
