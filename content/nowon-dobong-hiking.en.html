⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Seoul hiking = Bukhansan (북한산) only" — Skipping Nowon (노원)·Dobong (도봉)'s 3 famous mountains

This is the most common mistake in foreign hikers' itineraries. They only know Bukhansan (북한산) and are unaware of Suraksan (수락산)·Dobongsan (도봉산)·Buramsan (불암산). In reality, Suraksan (수락산)·Dobongsan (도봉산)·Buramsan (불암산) are concentrated in Nowon-gu (노원구)·Dobong-gu (도봉구) + have shorter trails than Bukhansan (북한산) and are less crowded with foreign enthusiasts. Dobongsan (도봉산) is one of Korea's five great mountains (Seorak, Jiri, Gyeryong, Sobaek, Dobong), but its foreign recognition is less than 1/10th of Bukhansan (북한산). To deep dive into the city-wide walkability covered in #176 walkable_seoul with actual hiking courses, Nowon (노원)·Dobong (도봉) is the #1 priority.

2. "Hiking = full hiking gear required" — Attempting with regular sneakers and slipping

This is the most common injury for first-time foreign hikers. Parts of Suraksan (수락산)·Dobongsan (도봉산)·Buramsan (불암산) have rocks and gravel — hiking boots or trail running shoes are absolutely recommended. There are annual reports of people slipping and going to the emergency room after attempting with regular city sneakers. If you don't have hiking boots, you can rent or buy them at hiking gear stores at Nowon Station (노원역) (Military Gear, Onsite, Gen-I) or global brand stores. Besides hiking boots, 1L of water + snacks + hat + sunscreen are essential.

3. "Post-hike makgeolli (막걸리)·naengmyeon (냉면) only at the mountain entrance" — Not entering Nowon (노원) city center

This pattern involves eating only makgeolli (막걸리, Korean rice wine) and nokdujeon (녹두전, mung bean pancake) at a restaurant near the mountain entrance after descending, then returning to the hotel. In reality, many signature makgeolli (막걸리)·naengmyeon (냉면, cold noodles)·BBQ spots are within walking distance of Nowon Station (노원역)·Junggye Station (중계역)·Changdong Station (창동역) — stores in the Nowon (노원) city center like Surakio Naengmyeon (수락이오냉면) (9,227 reviews), Junggye Gogi Party (중계고기파티) (7,523 reviews), Taereung Pungcheon Eel (태릉풍천장어) (4,240 reviews) offer more variety. The pattern of descending from the mountain entrance → moving to Nowon Station (노원역)·Junggye Station (중계역) city center is the real Nowon (노원) deep dive.

Nowon·Dobong Hiking Scenery

Nowon-gu (노원구)·Dobong-gu (도봉구), in northeastern Seoul, is Korea's strongest urban hiking hub, embracing three of Seoul's five great mountains (Suraksan (수락산)·Dobongsan (도봉산)·Buramsan (불암산)). Although it's a bedroom community with an area of 35.4㎢ and a population of 500,000, five subway lines (Line 4, Line 7, Ui-Sinseol Line (우이신설선), Line 1, Gyeongchun Line) connect directly to mountain trails, allowing access to hiking routes within an hour from downtown hotels.

Suraksan (수락산) (638m) is a famous granite mountain on the border of Nowon-gu (노원구)·Uijeongbu-si (의정부시). It's a 10-minute walk from Nowon Station (노원역)·Suraksan Station (수락산역) (Line 7). It's the #1 entry point for foreign hikers — offering easy trails, 360-degree panoramic views from the summit, and a peaceful atmosphere.

Dobongsan (도봉산) (740m) is one of Korea's five great mountains. Located on the border of Dobong-gu (도봉구)·Uijeongbu-si (의정부시), it's a 5-minute walk from Dobongsan Station (도봉산역) (Line 1·7). It features a cluster of peaks including Uiam (우이암)·Jaunbong (자운봉)·Sinseondae (신선대) and is the eastern edge of Bukhansan National Park (북한산국립공원). While its foreign recognition is lower than Bukhansan (북한산), it's equally highly rated by hiking enthusiasts.

Buramsan (불암산) (508m) is a granite peak on the border of Nowon-gu (노원구)·Namyangju-si (남양주시). Access is from Sanggye Station (상계역) (Line 4) or Junggye Station (중계역) (Line 7). It's a famous spot for sunrise and sunset + offers night views of the Nowon (노원) bedroom community.

Seoul Trail Section 1 (서울둘레길 1구간) (Suyu Station (수유역)~Ui Station (우이역), 8.5km, 4 hours) is a course that connects Nowon (노원)·Gangbuk (강북)·Dobong (도봉) along the eastern edge of Bukhansan National Park (북한산국립공원). It features English signage + QR codes + well-equipped rest areas and restrooms. It's a top priority for foreign trekking enthusiasts.

① 3 Famous Mountains (3)

1. Suraksan (수락산) (638m)

  • Signature: A famous granite mountain on the border of Nowon (노원)·Uijeongbu (의정부). #1 entry for foreign hikers — easy trails + peaceful atmosphere + panoramic summit views.
  • Foreigner-Friendly Score: ★★★★☆ — 70% English trail signage, direct connection via Line 7
  • Recommended Course: Suraksan Station (수락산역) (Line 7) 10-minute walk → Suraksan Waterfall (수락산 폭포) → Dosolbong (도솔봉) → Summit (one-way 2~2.5 hours, round-trip 4~5 hours)
  • Highlight: 360-degree views of Nowon (노원)·Uijeongbu (의정부)·Hangang (한강) from the summit + Dosolbong (도솔봉) ridge trekking
  • Tip: Recommended in spring·autumn (April~June / September~November). Hiking boots are essential. Last water station 30 minutes before the summit — 2L of water recommended. After descending, move to the Nowon Station (노원역) restaurant area.

2. Dobongsan (도봉산) (740m)

  • Signature: One of Korea's 5 great mountains. Eastern edge of Bukhansan National Park (북한산국립공원) + Uiam (우이암)·Jaunbong (자운봉)·Sinseondae (신선대) peak cluster. Lower foreign recognition than Bukhansan (북한산), but equally rated by enthusiasts.
  • Foreigner-Friendly Score: ★★★★☆ — 80% English trail signage, transfer via Line 1·7
  • Recommended Course: Dobongsan Station (도봉산역) (Line 1·7) 5-minute walk → Dobong Information Center (도봉탐방지원센터) → Uiam (우이암) (detour) or Jaunbong (자운봉) (straight) → Summit (one-way 3 hours, round-trip 5~6 hours)
  • Highlight: Uiam (우이암)·Jaunbong (자운봉)·Sinseondae (신선대) peaks + Dobong Valley (도봉계곡) + views of Bukhansan (북한산) ridge
  • Tip: Advanced course — Jaunbong (자운봉)·Sinseondae (신선대) have some rocky sections. Beginners should detour via Uiam (우이암). Get a free English map at Dobong Information Center (도봉탐방지원센터). After descending, move to the Dobongsan Station (도봉산역) restaurant area or Nowon Station (노원역).

3. Buramsan (불암산) (508m)

  • Signature: A granite peak on the border of Nowon-gu (노원구)·Namyangju-si (남양주시). Famous spot for sunrise·sunset + night views of the Nowon (노원) bedroom community. Short hiking time + peaceful for foreigners.
  • Foreigner-Friendly Score: ★★★☆☆ — 60% English signage, transfer via Line 4·7
  • Recommended Course: Sanggye Station (상계역) (Line 4) or Junggye Station (중계역) (Line 7) 15-minute walk → Hakdoam Temple (학도암) → Summit (one-way 1.5~2 hours, round-trip 3~4 hours)
  • Highlight: Summit sunrise·sunset + Nowon (노원) night views + Hakdoam Temple (학도암) Maaebul (마애불, Buddhist rock carving) (Treasure)
  • Tip: Short hiking time (half-day course) — recommended for foreigners short on time. Enter by calculating sunset time backwards (spring·autumn 5 PM - 6 PM at summit). After descending, move to the Sanggye (상계)·Junggye (중계) restaurant area.

② Seoul Trail (1)

4. Seoul Trail Section 1 (서울둘레길 1구간) (Suyu (수유)~Ui (우이))

  • Signature: The first of Seoul Trail's 21 sections. Suyu Station (수유역) → Ui Station (우이역), 8.5km, 4 hours, Moderate. A foreign enthusiast course connecting Nowon (노원)·Gangbuk (강북)·Dobong (도봉) along the eastern edge of Bukhansan National Park (북한산국립공원).
  • Foreigner-Friendly Score: ★★★★★ — 100% English signage, QR codes, well-equipped rest areas, restrooms, water stations
  • Recommended Course: Suyu Station (수유역) (Line 4) → April 19th National Cemetery (4·19묘지) → Yeongbong Ridge (영봉 능선) → Jeongneung Royal Tomb (정릉) → Ui Station (우이역) (Ui-Sinseol Line (우이신설선)) (total 8.5km, 4 hours)
  • Highlight: April 19th National Cemetery (4·19묘지) (commemorating Korea's April Revolution (4·19혁명, a student-led uprising in 1960)) + Yeongbong Ridge (영봉 능선) + Jeongneung Royal Tomb (정릉) (Joseon Royal Tombs (조선 왕릉, UNESCO World Heritage Site) of Queen Sindeok (신덕왕후))
  • Tip: Before starting, get a free English guidebook + stamp book at Seoul Tourism Plaza (서울관광플라자) (City Hall (시청)·Myeongdong (명동)). Sections 1 + 8 (Seoul Forest~Gwangnaru) are recommended for first-time foreign challengers. After arriving at Ui Station (우이역), transfer to Line 4 via Ui-Sinseol Line (우이신설선) → move to Nowon (노원)·Changdong (창동) city center.
