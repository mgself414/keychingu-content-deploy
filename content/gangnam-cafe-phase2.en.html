The Cafe Scene in Cheongdam (청담)·Dogok (도곡)·Gaepo (개포)·Tower Palace (타워팰리스)

Gangnam-gu (강남구) represents the luxury + specialty + celebrity chef segments of the Korean cafe market. While #142 covered the main lineup of Garosu-gil (가로수길)·Sinsa (신사)·Gangnam-daero (강남대로), this guide focuses on the Cheongdam (청담)·Tower Palace (타워팰리스)·Dogok (도곡)·Gaepo (개포) sub-areas.

Cheongdam (청담) (1/8) is a mecca for luxury cafes — places like Goscen Inc. Cheongdam (고센인코퍼레이티드 청담점) are top choices for foreign luxury cafe fans. It has the highest foreigner recognition among Gangnam-gu (강남구) cafe sub-areas.

Dogok (도곡)·Tower Palace (타워팰리스) (3/8) — Cafes within luxury residential complexes, such as Artisée (아티제) and A Twosome Place Tower Palace (투썸 타워팰리스점). They are naturally exposed to foreigners visiting the complex or on business itineraries.

Gaepo (개포)·Seolleung (선릉) (2/8) — Neighborhood dessert cafes like The Cheese Shop (더치즈샵) and Minimalle (미니말레) in Gaepo (개포) and Seolleung (선릉). These are hidden gems on the outskirts, adjacent to Dogok (도곡) and Seocho (서초).

Gangnam Main Area (강남 본권) (2/8) — Main cafes on Gangnam-daero (강남대로) like Pere-Epice (페르에피스), Coffee Brewer (커피브루어), and CAFE 00:00. These are natural inclusions for foreigners on their first visit to Gangnam (강남).

① Cheongdam (청담)·Tower Palace (타워팰리스) Luxury (2 Places)

1. Goscen Inc. Cheongdam (고센인코퍼레이티드 청담점)

  • Signature: The strongest foreigner-reviewed cafe in Cheongdam (청담). 5 foreigner reviews (Phase 2 cluster signal #1, signal 14.8) — An introductory luxury cafe in Cheongdam (청담).
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Cheongdam (청담) luxury concept, abundant foreigner reviews
  • Recommended Categories: Signature beverages, luxury desserts, Cheongdam (청담) ambiance
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Cheongdam Station (청담역). Phase 2 #1 recommended store — If it's a foreigner's first luxury cafe experience in Gangnam (강남), this is the top choice. Weekdays and afternoons are recommended.

2. Artisée Tower Palace (아티제 타워팰리스점)

  • Signature: Tower Palace (타워팰리스) luxury bakery cafe. 5 foreigner reviews (signal 11.0).
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Luxury bakery + English service
  • Recommended Categories: Signature bakery items, desserts, cafe beverages
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Dogok Station (도곡역). Located within the Tower Palace (타워팰리스) complex — a natural stop for foreigners visiting the complex or on business itineraries.

② Celebrity Chef·Bakery (2 Places)

3. Pere-Epice by Kim Young-mo (페르에피스_By김영모)

  • Signature: Chef Kim Young-mo's (김영모) signature desserts. 5 foreigner reviews (signal 11.0) — A celebrity chef bakery.
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Celebrity chef concept, popular with foreign dessert fans
  • Recommended Categories: Kim Young-mo's (김영모) signature desserts, limited edition bakery items, seasonal menus
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Gangnam Station (강남역). Top choice for foreign fans of celebrity chef desserts. Limited edition desserts may sell out — recommended to visit in the morning or early afternoon.

4. The Cheese Shop Seolleung (더치즈샵 선릉점)

  • Signature: Seolleung (선릉) cheese specialty bakery and cafe. A Gangnam (강남) cheese specialty store.
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★☆☆ — Cheese specialty, some English menus
  • Recommended Categories: Cheese bakery items, signature cheese dishes, cafe beverages
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Seolleung Station (선릉역). A hidden gem for foreign cheese fans. Recommended to check opening hours, typically 12:00 PM (12:00) to 9:00 PM (21:00).

③ Specialty·24-Hour (2 Places)

5. Coffee Brewer (커피브루어)

  • Signature: Hidden specialty coffee in Gangnam (강남). 4 foreigner reviews (signal 10.3) — An introductory hidden gem for specialty coffee in Gangnam (강남).
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Specialty concept, English menus
  • Recommended Categories: Signature hand-drip coffee, specialty lattes, seasonal beverages
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Gangnam Station (강남역). A hidden gem for foreign specialty coffee fans.

6. CAFE 00:00

  • Signature: Signature 24-hour cafe. 3 foreigner reviews (signal 9.0) — A 24-hour cafe in Gangnam (강남).
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — 24-hour operation, English store name
  • Recommended Categories: Signature beverages, 24-hour desserts, late-night cafe ambiance
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Gangnam Station (강남역). Open 24 hours — a top choice for K-pop fans and business travelers on late-night itineraries. Recommended for foreigners adjusting to time differences.

④ Dogok (도곡)·Gaepo (개포) Hidden (2 Places)

7. A Twosome Place Dogok Tower Palace (투썸플레이스 도곡타워팰리스점)

  • Signature: Dogok (도곡) Tower Palace (타워팰리스) branch of a chain cafe. 3 foreigner reviews (signal 4.5).
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Chain cafe + reliable English menus
  • Recommended Categories: Signature beverages, bakery items, desserts
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Dogok Station (도곡역). Located within the Tower Palace (타워팰리스) complex — a natural stop for foreigners visiting the complex. Offers chain reliability + a luxury location.

8. Minimalle Bakery & Coffee Bar Gaepo (미니말레 베이커리&커피바 개포점)

  • Signature: Gaepo (개포) minimalist bakery cafe. A neighborhood hidden gem.
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★☆☆ — Gaepo (개포) neighborhood, minimalist concept
  • Recommended Categories: Minimalist bakery items, signature beverages, desserts
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Gaepo Station (개포역). A hidden gem on the outskirts of Dogok (도곡)·Gaepo (개포) — recommended for foreigners seeking a different atmosphere from other stores.
