⚠ 3 Reasons to Choose the Phase 2 Guide

1. Hidden gems verified by foreigners, following the #142 Gangnam Cafe deep dive

The 12 selections in #142 covered the main lineup of Gangnam (강남) cafes (Garosu-gil (가로수길) · Sinsa (신사) · Gangnam-daero (강남대로)). These 8 selections, however, are filtered only for venues with 3-5+ foreigner reviews — places in Cheongdam (청담) · Tower Palace (타워팰리스) · Dogok (도곡) · Gaepo (개포) where foreigners actually visited and left English, Japanese, or Chinese reviews. This represents real demand exposed on Reddit and Google Maps.

2. Combination of Cheongdam & Tower Palace luxury cafes + celebrity chef bakeries

Beyond the most common venues for foreigners in Gangnam (강남) (Starbucks · chain dessert shops), this guide delves into the luxury segment of Cheongdam (청담) · Tower Palace (타워팰리스) and celebrity bakeries like Chef Kim Young-mo (김영모). These are true differentiating points for Gangnam (강남) cafes.

3. 24-hour cafes & specialty hidden gems

There is strong demand for 24-hour cafes among K-pop fans and business travelers. 24-hour cafes like CAFE 00:00 and specialty hidden gems like Coffee Brewer (커피브루어) are foreigner-specific venues, offering a different experience from typical Korean routes.

Cafe Scene in Cheongdam, Dogok, Gaepo, Tower Palace

Gangnam-gu (강남구) represents the luxury + specialty + celebrity chef segments of Korea's cafe market. While #142 covered the main lineup of Garosu-gil (가로수길) · Sinsa (신사) · Gangnam-daero (강남대로), this guide focuses on the Cheongdam (청담) · Tower Palace (타워팰리스) · Dogok (도곡) · Gaepo (개포) sub-areas.

Cheongdam (청담) (1/8) is a mecca for luxury cafes — Goscen Inc. Cheongdam (고센인코퍼레이티드 청담점) and others are top choices for foreign luxury cafe fans. It has the highest foreigner recognition among Gangnam-gu (강남구) cafe sub-areas.

Dogok (도곡) · Tower Palace (타워팰리스) (3/8) — Cafes like Artisée (아티제) and A Twosome Place (투썸플레이스) in the luxury residential complex. Naturally exposed to foreigners visiting the complex or on business routes.

Gaepo (개포) · Seolleung (선릉) (2/8) — Neighborhood dessert cafes like The Cheese Shop (더치즈샵) and Minimalle (미니말레) in Gaepo (개포) · Seolleung (선릉). Hidden gems on the outskirts, adjacent to Dogok (도곡) · Seocho (서초).

Central Gangnam (강남) (2/8) — Main Gangnam-daero (강남대로) cafes like Pere-Epice (페르에피스), Coffee Brewer (커피브루어), and CAFE 00:00. Naturally part of a foreigner's first visit to Gangnam (강남).

In T6.6 cluster analysis, the Cafe·Gangnam cluster is Top 4 (gap 312, cov 43%) — with Phase 1 #142 + this Phase 2 guide, the goal is to reach 70% coverage.

① Cheongdam (청담) · Tower Palace (타워팰리스) Luxury (2 Places)

1. Goscen Inc. Cheongdam (고센인코퍼레이티드 청담점)

  • Signature: Cheongdam (청담)'s strongest foreigner review cafe. 5 foreigner reviews (Phase 2 cluster signal #1, signal 14.8) — An introductory venue for luxury cafes in Cheongdam (청담).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Cheongdam (청담) luxury concept, abundant foreigner reviews
  • Recommended Categories: Signature beverages, luxury desserts, Cheongdam (청담) ambiance
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Cheongdam Station (청담역). Phase 2's top recommended venue — if it's your first luxury cafe experience in Gangnam (강남), this is the #1 choice. Weekdays and afternoons are recommended.

2. Artisée Tower Palace (아티제 타워팰리스점)

  • Signature: Tower Palace (타워팰리스) luxury bakery cafe. 5 foreigner reviews (signal 11.0).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Luxury bakery + English service
  • Recommended Categories: Signature bakery items, desserts, cafe beverages
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Dogok Station (도곡역). Inside the Tower Palace (타워팰리스) complex — naturally on the route for foreigners visiting the complex or on business.

② Celebrity Chef · Bakery (2 Places)

3. Pere-Epice by Kim Young-mo (페르에피스_By김영모)

  • Signature: Chef Kim Young-mo (김영모)'s signature desserts. 5 foreigner reviews (signal 11.0) — A celebrity chef bakery.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Celebrity chef concept, popular with foreign dessert fans
  • Recommended Categories: Chef Kim Young-mo (김영모)'s signature desserts, limited edition bakery items, seasonal menus
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Gangnam Station (강남역). #1 choice for foreign fans of celebrity chef desserts. Limited desserts may sell out — recommended to visit early in the morning or afternoon.

4. The Cheese Shop Seolleung (더치즈샵 선릉점)

  • Signature: Seolleung (선릉) cheese specialty bakery and cafe. A Gangnam (강남) cheese specialty venue.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★☆☆ — Cheese specialty, some English menus
  • Recommended Categories: Cheese bakery items, signature cheese dishes, cafe beverages
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Seolleung Station (선릉역). A hidden gem for foreign cheese fans. Recommended to check operating hours (12:00 PM - 9:00 PM).

③ Specialty · 24-Hour (2 Places)

5. Coffee Brewer (커피브루어)

  • Signature: Gangnam (강남) specialty coffee hidden gem. 4 foreigner reviews (signal 10.3) — An introductory hidden gem for specialty coffee in Gangnam (강남).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Specialty coffee concept, English menu
  • Recommended Categories: Signature hand-drip coffee, specialty latte, seasonal beverages
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Gangnam Station (강남역). A hidden gem for foreign specialty coffee fans.

6. CAFE 00:00

  • Signature: Signature 24-hour cafe. 3 foreigner reviews (signal 9.0) — A Gangnam (강남) 24-hour cafe.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — 24-hour operation, English venue name
  • Recommended Categories: Signature beverages, 24-hour desserts, night cafe ambiance
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Gangnam Station (강남역). 24-hour operation — #1 choice for K-pop fans and business travelers on night routes. Recommended for foreigners experiencing jet lag.

④ Dogok (도곡) · Gaepo (개포) Hidden (2 Places)

7. A Twosome Place Dogok Tower Palace (투썸플레이스 도곡타워팰리스점)

  • Signature: Dogok (도곡) Tower Palace (타워팰리스) branch of a chain cafe. 3 foreigner reviews (signal 4.5).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Chain cafe + reliable English menu
  • Recommended Categories: Signature beverages, bakery items, desserts
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Dogok Station (도곡역). Inside the Tower Palace (타워팰리스) complex — a natural course for foreigners visiting the complex. Chain reliability + luxury location.

8. Minimalle Bakery & Coffee Bar Gaepo (미니말레 베이커리&커피바 개포점)

  • Signature: Gaepo (개포) minimalist bakery cafe. A neighborhood hidden gem.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★☆☆ — Gaepo (개포) neighborhood, minimalist concept
  • Recommended Categories: Minimalist bakery items, signature beverages, desserts
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Gaepo Station (개포역). A hidden gem on the Dogok (도곡) · Gaepo (개포) outskirts — recommended for foreigners seeking a different ambiance from other venues.
