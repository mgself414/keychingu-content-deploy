DDP·Dongdaemun at Night — 13 Spots: What Is Open Until What Time

Subtitle: For travelers who reach Dongdaemun in the evening, this guide groups 13 spots in order of closing time. Dongdaemun History Museum and Seoul City Wall Museum close at 18:00. The DDP building and the Design Store are open 10:00–20:00. The DDP night lighting at Oullim Square, 365 Light, runs for about 5 minutes at the top and the half hour of every hour according to a Seoul Metropolitan Government Media Hub article (2026-09-28), and the last show starts at 22:00. From November to February it starts at 18:00. Seoul Light DDP 2026 Winter is free and runs December 17–31, 18:00–22:00, until 23:00 on Fridays, Saturdays and December 24. Doota Mall is open 10:30–24:00. Migliore Dongdaemun is registered as open until 02:00 by the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25) and is closed on Mondays. DDP Fashion Mall opens around 8 in the evening and closes at 05:00 the next day, and is closed on Fridays and Saturdays. Cheonggyecheon Stream is open 24 hours and blocks entry when it rains. The values were read on 2026-11-25.

ISSUE 06 · #172 · 2026-11-25 · DDP·Dongdaemun

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 13

This list is made for foreign travelers who arrive in Dongdaemun (동대문) in the evening. Closing times differ greatly from place to place in Dongdaemun. Museums and daytime markets close around 18:00. The DDP (Dongdaemun Design Plaza) (동대문디자인플라자) building closes at 20:00. Shopping malls close between 21:00 and 02:00. Fashion arcades that open only at night open around 8 in the evening and close at 05:00 the next day.

So this issue divides the spots by closing time, not by type.

  • ① Closing at 18–19 (4 spots): Dongdaemun History Museum·Dongdaemun Stadium Memorial (동대문역사관·동대문운동장기념관), Seoul City Wall Museum (한양도성박물관), Dongdaemun Jonghap Market (동대문종합시장), Pyeonghwa Market (평화시장).
  • ② Open until 20–22 (3 spots): DDP building·Design Store (디자인스토어), DDP night lighting (365 Light (365라이트)·Seoul Light DDP Winter (서울라이트 DDP 겨울)), Hyundai City Outlets Dongdaemun (현대시티아울렛 동대문점).
  • ③ Until midnight, or past midnight (4 spots): Doota Mall (두타몰), Migliore Dongdaemun (밀리오레 동대문), DDP Fashion Mall (DDP패션몰), apM Place (apM플레이스).
  • ④ Not tied to hours (2 spots): Heunginjimun Gate (흥인지문), the Ogansugyo Bridge (오간수교) section of Cheonggyecheon Stream (청계천).

The DDP building and the night lighting close at different times, so they are counted separately. Dongdaemun History Museum and Dongdaemun Stadium Memorial share the same hours and fare, so they are counted as one spot.

Look at the time now and read the group that fits first. Restaurants and cafes are not included in this issue.

📅 First, Check the Day of the Week — Closing Days

Half of a night plan drops out depending on the day of the week. Check the day before the time.

🚇 Two Stations Share the Work

  • DDP is connected to the station. The official DDP guide (read 2026-11-25) says "directly connected to Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station".
  • Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station (동대문역사문화공원역): per Kakao Map directions, Line 2, Line 4 and Line 5 stop here. DDP is a 3 min walk (185 m) from Exit 1. Dongdaemun History Museum and DDP Fashion Mall are at Exit 2, apM Place is at Exit 10, and Migliore Dongdaemun is at Exit 14.
  • Dongdaemun Station (동대문역): per Kakao Map directions, Line 1 and Line 4 stop here. Doota Mall, Hyundai City Outlets Dongdaemun, Pyeonghwa Market and Ogansugyo Bridge are at Exit 8. Heunginjimun Gate and Dongdaemun Jonghap Market are at Exit 9, and Seoul City Wall Museum is at Exit 10.
  • Line 4 stops at both stations.

⚠ 3 Easy Mistakes

  1. The DDP building closes at 20:00, but the night lighting goes on after that. The operating hours in the official DDP guide are 10:00–20:00. 365 Light is lighting seen outdoors at Oullim Square (어울림광장), so its hours run separately. According to the Seoul Metropolitan Government Media Hub article (2026-09-28), it starts every day at 19:00 and the last show starts at 22:00 (about 5 min per show). From November to February it starts at 18:00. If you want to see inside the building, go in before 20:00.
  2. Doota Mall closes at 24:00. The business hours in the official Doota Mall guide (read 2026-11-25) are 10:30–00:00, Mon–Sun. The places that open past midnight are Migliore Dongdaemun and the two fashion arcades that open only at night (until 05:00). Migliore Dongdaemun is registered as open until 02:00 (Kakao Map registered value).
  3. The fashion arcades that open only at night are closed on Fridays and Saturdays. DDP Fashion Mall is "closed on Fridays, Saturdays and holiday periods" per the official Seoul Facilities Corporation guide. apM Place is also registered as closed on Fridays and Saturdays by the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25). Migliore Dongdaemun, by contrast, is closed on Mondays (Kakao Map registered value).

① Closing at 18–19 — 4 Spots

Two museums and two daytime markets. If you arrive in the evening, these four are already closed or about to close. Move them to the next day.

Dongdaemun History Museum · Dongdaemun Stadium Memorial (Dongdaemun History Museum · Dongdaemun Stadium Memorial)

  • What it is: two exhibition halls in Dongdaemun History & Culture Park (동대문역사문화공원). Seoul Museum of History publishes the visiting guide.
  • Visiting hours: per the official Seoul Museum of History guide (read 2026-11-25), both are open Tuesday–Sunday 10:00–18:00. They are closed 12:00–13:00. Entry ends at 17:30.
  • Closing days: Mondays, January 1, the days of Lunar New Year and Chuseok.
  • Fare: free.
  • How to get to Dongdaemun History Museum: the Kakao Map registered address is 281 Eulji-ro (을지로), Jung-gu (중구), Seoul. Per Kakao Map directions, it is a 7 min walk (360 m) from Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station Exit 2.
  • Timing: the lunch hour (12:00–13:00) is empty. If you go in the afternoon, enter after 13:00 and before 17:30.

Seoul City Wall Museum

  • Visiting hours: per the official Seoul City Wall Museum guide (read 2026-11-25), Tuesday–Sunday 09:00–18:00. Entry ends at 17:30.
  • Closing days: Mondays, January 1.
  • Fare: free.
  • Address: Seoul Design Support Center (서울디자인지원센터), floors 1–3, 283 Yulgok-ro (율곡로), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul. Phone 02-724-0243.
  • How to get there: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 7 min walk (293 m) from Dongdaemun Station Exit 10.
  • Timing: it opens at 09:00, so it is the first of this issue's museums to open. Put it before Dongdaemun History Museum (10:00).

Dongdaemun Jonghap Market

  • What it is: a market that sells fabrics, garment trims, accessory trims, and wedding goods and home interior items. The official history of Dongdaemun Jonghap Market says it opened in December 1970.
  • Business hours: the official business hours guide of Dongdaemun Jonghap Market (read 2026-11-25) differs by item.

  • Whole-market closure: the 2nd and 4th Sundays.
  • Address: 266 Jong-ro (종로), Jongno-gu, Seoul.
  • How to get there: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 3 min walk (164 m) from Dongdaemun Station Exit 9.
  • Timing: a daytime market that closes at 18:00–19:00. Do not put it in a night plan.

Pyeonghwa Market

  • Business hours: per the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25), Monday–Friday 09:00–18:00, Saturday 09:00–17:30.
  • Closing days: per the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25), Sunday is a closing day.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25): 274 Cheonggyecheon-ro (청계천로), Jung-gu, Seoul.
  • How to get there: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 5 min walk (205 m) from Dongdaemun Station Exit 8.
  • Timing: by the Kakao Map registered value it is a daytime market. On Saturdays it closes 30 minutes earlier.

② Open Until 20–22 — 3 Spots

These can be seen even if you arrive at 6 in the evening. The closing order is the DDP building (20:00), Hyundai City Outlets Dongdaemun (21:00 or 21:30), then the DDP night lighting. For 365 Light, the last show starts at 22:00 (about 5 min per show).

DDP Building · Design Store (Dongdaemun Design Plaza)

  • What it is: the official DDP introduction says it was designed by "the world-renowned architect Zaha Hadid" (자하 하디드). It opened on March 21, 2014.
  • Operating hours: per the official DDP guide (read 2026-11-25), 10:00–20:00. The note "some spaces run flexibly depending on the program" is attached.
  • Closing days: January 1, the days of Lunar New Year and Chuseok.
  • Address: 281 Eulji-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul.
  • How to get there: the official DDP guide says "directly connected to Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station". Per Kakao Map directions, it is a 3 min walk (185 m) from Exit 1.
  • Design Store: per the official DDP guide, "every day 10:00-20:00 / open all year (closed on Chuseok and Lunar New Year)". The main shop is at Oullim Square. Inquiries 02-2153-0632.

DDP Architecture Tour — There Are English Sessions

These are values from the official DDP notice (read 2026-11-25).

  • Period: 2026-03-21 to 12-31. It runs Tuesday–Saturday. It is closed on Sundays, Mondays, January 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok.
  • From December 1: 10:30 Korean, 11:00 English, 14:30 English, 16:00 Korean. Some sessions may be limited depending on internal circumstances (official notice).
  • Duration: 60 minutes.
  • Fare: 10,000 won for adults. The English tour is also 10,000 won. On-site payment accepts cards only.
  • Reservation: reserve through Naver (네이버) and KakaoTalk (카카오톡). If seats remain, you can register on site.
  • Meeting point: the Lounge tour desk (라운지 투어데스크). It is at the Art Hall (아트홀) 1st floor, near the A3 and A4 entrances.
  • Timing: all sessions are in the daytime. To take the English tour, come in the daytime first, in time for 11:00 or 14:30.

DDP Night Lighting — 365 Light · Seoul Light DDP Winter (DDP Night Lights)

365 Light

These are values from the Seoul Metropolitan Government Media Hub article (2026-09-28).

  • Time: it starts every day at 19:00. In November–February it starts at 18:00.
  • Shows: they start at the top and the half hour of every hour. One show is about 5 minutes. The last show starts at 22:00.
  • Where: near the Relics Exhibition Area (유구전시장) at DDP Oullim Square.
  • Operation: it has run all year round since September 2026. The article says the operating hours and show times can change.

Seoul Light DDP 2026 Winter

These are values from the official DDP notice (read 2026-11-25).

  • Period: December 17, 2026 (Thursday) to December 31 (Thursday).
  • Time: 18:00–22:00. 22:00 is the time operation ends. On Fridays, Saturdays and December 24 (Thursday) it is extended to 23:00.
  • Where: the front of DDP and the surrounding area.
  • Fare: free to view.
  • Changes: it can change depending on the weather and conditions on site.
  • Timing: you can watch for two more hours after the building closes at 20:00. If you are in Dongdaemun on December 17–31, see the inside of DDP first before 20:00 and then go outside.

Hyundai City Outlets Dongdaemun

  • Business hours: per the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25), Monday–Thursday 10:30–21:00, Friday–Sunday 10:30–21:30.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25): 20 Jangchungdan-ro (장충단로) 13-gil, Jung-gu, Seoul.
  • How to get there: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 7 min walk (387 m) from Dongdaemun Station Exit 8.
  • Timing: it closes first among this issue's shopping malls. Put it before Doota Mall (24:00).

③ Until Midnight, or Past Midnight — 4 Spots

These are open even if you arrive after 10 at night. Doota Mall is open until 24:00 every day. After that, it depends on the day of the week.

Doota Mall

  • Business hours: per the official Doota Mall guide (read 2026-11-25), 10:30–00:00, Mon–Sun. Some restaurants and cafes on each floor close earlier than this (official Doota Mall guide). The Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25) is the same, 10:30–24:00 every day.
  • Address: 275 Jangchungdan-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul.
  • How to get there: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 6 min walk (303 m) from Dongdaemun Station Exit 8.
  • Timing: it closes at 24:00. See DDP, which closes at 20:00, and Hyundai City Outlets Dongdaemun, which closes at 21:00–21:30, first, and put Doota Mall after them.

Migliore Dongdaemun

  • Business hours: per the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25), Tuesday–Sunday 10:30–02:00.
  • Closing days: per the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25), closed on Mondays.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25): 263 Jangchungdan-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul.
  • How to get there: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 4 min walk (223 m) from Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station Exit 14.
  • Timing: it stays open after Doota Mall closes at 24:00. It is registered as open until 02:00 (Kakao Map registered value). Leave it out of a Monday night plan.

DDP Fashion Mall

  • What it is: a fashion arcade run by Seoul Facilities Corporation. It opens at night.
  • Business hours: the notation in the official Seoul Facilities Corporation guide (read 2026-11-25) is "20:10 – 05:00 the next day (shops set up by 20:00)".
  • Closing days: per the same guide, "closed on Fridays, Saturdays and holiday periods".
  • Address: 22 Majang-ro (마장로), Jung-gu, Seoul. Phone 02-3405-4040.
  • How to get there: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 7 min walk (405 m) from Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station Exit 2.
  • Timing: the start time in the official guide is 20:10. Go on a Sunday–Thursday night. It does not open on Friday night and Saturday night.

apM Place

  • Business hours: per the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25), Sunday–Thursday 20:00–05:00.
  • Closing days: per the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25), Friday and Saturday are registered as closing days.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25): 276 Eulji-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul.
  • How to get there: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 2 min walk (93 m) from Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station Exit 10.
  • Timing: it opens on the same days as DDP Fashion Mall. Pair the two on a Sunday–Thursday night.

④ Not Tied to Hours — 2 Spots

These can be visited even after all the shopping malls have closed.

Heunginjimun Gate

  • What it is: the official notation of the Korea Heritage Portal is "Seoul Heunginjimun" and its category is Treasure. It was designated on January 21, 1963. The era is given as the 6th year of King Gojong of Joseon (1869).
  • Address: 288 Jong-ro (Jongno 6-ga), Jongno-gu, Seoul. Jongno-gu manages it.
  • Hours: Kakao Map has no operating hours, and it is a place you see from outside the gate.
  • How to get there: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 2 min walk (97 m) from Dongdaemun Station Exit 9.
  • Timing: it is the same Exit 9 as Dongdaemun Jonghap Market. In the daytime pair it with the market, and at night pair it with Ogansugyo Bridge on Cheonggyecheon Stream.

Cheonggyecheon Stream · Ogansugyo Bridge Section (Cheonggyecheon Stream · Ogansugyo Bridge)

  • Opening: per the official Seoul night-view spots guide (read 2026-11-25), Cheonggyecheon Stream is open 24 hours, all year round. It is free. The guide is based on Cheonggye Plaza (청계광장).
  • On rainy days: per the same guide, entry is blocked when rain is forecast or falling.
  • Inquiries: Cheonggyecheon Control Center (청계천종합상황실) 02-2290-7111~3.
  • Length: per the official Seoul Facilities Corporation guide, the total length of Cheonggyecheon Stream is 8.12 km, and the restored section is 5.84 km.
  • Ogansugyo Bridge: per the Seoul Facilities Corporation guide, it is a bridge that cars use. One of the four high-jet fountains of Cheonggyecheon Stream is at Ogansugyo Bridge. It is 269 m to Beodeuldari Bridge (버들다리) on the upstream side and 245 m to Malgeunnaedari Bridge (맑은내다리) on the downstream side.
  • How to get there: per Kakao Map directions, Ogansugyo Bridge is a 3 min walk (132 m) from Dongdaemun Station Exit 8.
  • Timing: it is the same Exit 8 as Doota Mall and Pyeonghwa Market. On a day when rain is forecast, leave it out of the plan.

💳 Immediate Tax Refund Limit

This is the cap set by Article 6(2) of the Special Regulations on Value-Added Tax and Individual Consumption Tax for Foreign Tourists (「외국인관광객 등에 대한 부가가치세 및 개별소비세 특례규정」) (in effect 2026-01-02, read 2026-11-25). It is under 1,000,000 won per transaction and 5,000,000 won or less in total after entry, and both amounts include tax. The immediate tax refund (즉시환급) is given when you show your passport at a shop with a Tax Free sign.

⚙ 3 FAQs

  1. What is open if I arrive at 10 at night? — Doota Mall is open until 24:00 (official Doota Mall guide). Migliore Dongdaemun is registered as open until 02:00 Tuesday–Sunday (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25). On Sunday–Thursday, DDP Fashion Mall and apM Place are open until 05:00 the next day. Cheonggyecheon Stream is open 24 hours. The DDP building (20:00) and Hyundai City Outlets Dongdaemun (21:00 or 21:30) have already closed.
  2. Does DDP cost money? Is there English guidance? — Seoul Light DDP 2026 Winter is free to view (official DDP notice). Dongdaemun History Museum, Dongdaemun Stadium Memorial and Seoul City Wall Museum are also free. What costs money is the DDP Architecture Tour, 10,000 won for adults. The English sessions are at 11:00 and 14:30 from December 1. It runs only Tuesday–Saturday.
  3. How much tax refund can I get right at the register? — The cap set by Article 6(2) of the Special Regulations on Value-Added Tax and Individual Consumption Tax for Foreign Tourists is under 1,000,000 won per transaction and 5,000,000 won or less in total after entry, and both amounts include tax. The immediate tax refund is given when you show your passport at a shop with a Tax Free sign.
