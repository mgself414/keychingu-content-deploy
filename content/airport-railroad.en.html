Why the Airport Railroad — The First Train You Ride in Korea

For most foreign travelers landing at Incheon Airport, the first public transportation they take is the Airport Railroad (AREX). From Incheon Airport T1·T2 to Seoul Station (서울역), the Express train runs nonstop in about 43–51 minutes, while the All-stop train passes through Gimpo Airport (김포공항), Hongik Univ. (홍대입구), and other stations. This first choice determines your entire first-day route.

This guide is specialized for the first-day arrival route, not line sightseeing. ① Express vs All-stop — which one to take ② which station to get off at depending on where you're staying ③ how to handle T-money, SIM, and luggage at the station you get off at — it covers everything from the arrival gate to hotel check-in in a single read.

Express vs All-stop — The First Choice

Best for Seoul Station area · lots of luggage · comfort Getting off at Hongdae (홍대) · Gongdeok (공덕) · Gimpo Airport · value

How to decide: If your accommodation is in Hongdae or Mapo (마포), the All-stop train, getting off at Hongik Univ. or Gongdeok, is the right answer (the Express only goes to Seoul Station). For the Seoul Station, Myeongdong (명동), or City Hall (시청) area, the Express is more comfortable. ※Fares and times subject to change — check before boarding.

Station-by-Station Getting-Off Guide — If You Get Off Here

✈️ #1. Incheon Airport Terminals T1·T2 (인천공항 T1·T2) — Starting-Point Practicalities

  • Order after arrival: Immigration → baggage claim → board AREX at the B1 Transportation Center (T1) / underground Transportation Center (T2)
  • Handle before leaving: SIM/eSIM (#414 arrival-floor booths) · buy & top up T-money (convenience stores) · currency exchange/cards (#435 ATM) · request luggage delivery (#1232 counter — send your bags ahead to your accommodation and tour hands-free)
  • 🍜 Eat: airport food court · convenience stores (your first Korean cup ramyeon)
  • Foreigner tip: Express train tickets at the Transportation Center ticket machines/counter. For the All-stop train, just tap T-money.

✈️ #2. Gimpo Airport Station (김포공항역) — Domestic Flights · Line 9 Express Transfer

  • Off the train: Lotte Mall Gimpo Airport (롯데몰 김포공항) (shopping · supermarket) · domestic terminal (transfers to Jeju (제주) · Busan (부산))
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 9 Express (#1247 — fast to Yeouido (여의도) · Gangnam (강남) · Jamsil (잠실)) · Line 5 (#1244) · Gimpo Goldline (김포골드라인)
  • Get off here if: staying in Gangnam or Jamsil (the Line 9 Express transfer is the shortcut) · connecting to a domestic flight

✈️ #3. Magongnaru Station (마곡나루역) — Botanic Park · Quiet New Town

  • Off the train: Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원) (#1240 grand greenhouse) · LG Arts Center (LG아트센터) · Magok (마곡) new town
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 9
  • Get off here if: keeping the first day light (a botanic-park stroll) · Magok business trips

✈️ #4. Digital Media City (DMC) Station (디지털미디어시티역) — Media City · Sangam (상암)

  • Off the train: Sangam MBC · media complex · Haneul Park (하늘공원) (#1238 silver grass) · World Cup Stadium (월드컵경기장)
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 6 · Gyeongui-Jungang Line (경의중앙선) (#1248)
  • Get off here if: K-broadcasting tours · heading to the World Cup Stadium area

✈️ #5. Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역) — The No.1 Accommodation Hub for Foreign Visitors

  • Off the train: the Hongdae & Yeonnam (연남) guesthouse cluster (the top accommodation area for foreign visitors) · Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길) · the Hongdae streets
  • 🍜 Eat: Yeonnam cafes · Hongdae's ramen street (#1241)
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 2 (#1241 — anywhere in the city) · Gyeongui-Jungang Line
  • Get off here if: staying in Hongdae, Yeonnam, or Hapjeong (합정) — take the All-stop train and get off here — that's the right answer (the Express doesn't stop here)

✈️ #6. Gongdeok Station (공덕역) — The Mapo Hub

  • Off the train: Gongdeok food alley (#1248) · Gyeongui Line Forest Park · the Mapo officetel & hotel area
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 5 · Line 6 · Gyeongui-Jungang Line (a quadruple hub)
  • Get off here if: staying in Mapo · Yeouido (Line 5) · Itaewon (이태원) (Line 6)

✈️ #7. Seoul Station (서울역) — Terminus · Gateway to Downtown

  • Off the train: Express train terminus. KTX (regional travel) · Lines 1·4 (#1243) · the Myeongdong · City Hall downtown area
  • Handle here: luggage delivery/storage (#1232 Seoul Station counter) · a walk on Seoullo 7017 (서울로7017) · Lotte Mart (롯데마트) (groceries)
  • 🍜 Eat: El Cafe (엘카페) · Seoul Station food court
  • Get off here if: staying in Myeongdong · City Hall · Jongno (종로) · heading straight to the regions by KTX · riding the Express train

3 First-Day Arrival Route Scenarios

Scenario 1. Staying in Hongdae (the most common for foreign visitors)

  • Incheon Airport All-stop train → get off at Hongik Univ. (approx. 50–55 min) → Exit 3, check in at your accommodation → Gyeongui Line Forest Park stroll + dinner in Yeonnam
  • Point: Do not take the Express (it carries you all the way to Seoul Station). The All-stop train is the right answer.

Scenario 2. Staying in Myeongdong / Downtown

  • Incheon Airport Express train → Seoul Station (43–51 min · reserved seat) → luggage delivery or Line 4 to Myeongdong (#1243) → check-in → dinner in Myeongdong
  • Point: With lots of luggage, the Express + Seoul Station luggage-delivery combo is the comfortable way.

Scenario 3. Staying in Gangnam / Jamsil

  • Incheon Airport All-stop train → get off at Gimpo Airport → transfer to the Line 9 Express (#1247) → Sinnonhyeon (신논현)/Bongeunsa (봉은사)/Seokchon (석촌) → check-in
  • Point: The Gimpo Airport + Line 9 Express route is a shortcut compared to going via Seoul Station.

5 Arrival Practicalities — Finish Them in the First Hour

Tip 1. Get your SIM/eSIM at the airport

Carrier booths on the arrival floor (#414). eSIM can also be purchased online before departure. Without data, you can't use map apps.

Tip 2. One T-money card covers everything

Buy and top it up at convenience stores. AREX All-stop trains, the subway, and buses all run on it. Only the Express train needs a separate ticket.

Tip 3. Send your luggage ahead

Send your bags to your accommodation from the airport luggage-delivery counter (#1232) and tour hands-free from day one. If you arrive at Seoul Station, use the Seoul Station counter.

Tip 4. Express and All-stop platforms are different

At the Incheon Airport Transportation Center, the Express (orange) and All-stop (blue) platforms are separate. Check the signage.

Tip 5. Your accommodation decides the train

Hongdae · Mapo = All-stop; Seoul Station · Myeongdong = Express; Gangnam = All-stop + Line 9 Express at Gimpo Airport. See the 3 scenarios above.

FAQ 6 — Airport Railroad Questions Foreign Visitors Ask Most

Q1. Express or All-stop — which should I take?

  • Your accommodation decides. Hongdae · Mapo = All-stop (get off at Hongik Univ. or Gongdeok); Seoul Station · Myeongdong = Express; Gangnam = All-stop + Line 9 Express at Gimpo Airport. See the table & scenarios above.

Q2. How much is the fare?

  • Express approx. ₩11,000 (~$8) · all reserved seats / All-stop in the ₩4,000 range (~$3) · T-money. ※Subject to change — check before boarding.

Q3. Where do I get a T-money card?

  • Buy and top it up at airport convenience stores. Works on All-stop trains, the subway, and buses (#237). Only the Express train needs a separate ticket.

Q4. What if I have a lot of luggage?

  • Send it ahead to your accommodation with airport luggage delivery (#1232), or take the Express train (luggage space · reserved seats) + Seoul Station luggage delivery.

Q5. I'm staying in Hongdae — can I take the Express?

  • Not recommended — the Express runs nonstop to Seoul Station, so it doesn't stop at Hongik Univ. Take the All-stop train.

Q6. What if I arrive late at night?

  • Check the last AREX train (around midnight). If you miss it, late-night buses (N6001 etc.) or a taxi. Check the times in advance on a map app.
