5 Common Mistakes Visitors Make

1. Arriving Too Early

Illuminations switch on after sunset (17:30–18:00). Show up at 16:00 and you'll just be standing in the dark. Arrive at 17:00–18:00 and enjoy the golden hour right after lights-on.

2. Underestimating the Cold

Seoul winters = -5–5°C with strong wind. Heavy puffer coat + gloves + hat + hand warmers are non-negotiable. Hand warmers at convenience stores & Daiso (다이소): ₩1,000–2,000.

3. Only Knowing About the New Year's Eve Countdown

If Bosingak (보신각) on December 31 is the only event on your radar, you'll be stuck in a crowd of 500,000+. DDP (동대문), Cheonggyecheon (청계천), Sinchon (신촌), and Lotte Tower (롯데타워) have illuminations running 1–2 months — plenty of time to visit at your own pace.

4. Assuming Every Spot Is Free

Lotte Tower (롯데타워) observation deck ₩31,000 / N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) ₩21,000 = paid. Everything else — Gwanghwamun (광화문), Cheonggyecheon (청계천), DDP (동대문) outdoors — is free.

5. Treating Korean Christmas Like a Western or Japanese Holiday

Korean Christmas = Dec 24–25 public holiday, with a strong date/friend-outing vibe. Family holiday season is Lunar New Year (설날, February). Foreign visitors: hotel and restaurant reservations spike dramatically over the year-end period — book early.

① DDP — Dongdaemun Design Plaza (동대문 디자인 플라자) — Top Pick for Visitors

Location: Dongdaemun (동대문) — Line 2 · 4 · 5, Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station (동대문역사문화공원역)

Lights On: Late November – February (longest season of all eight spots)

Admission: Free (indoor exhibitions separate)

Highlights:

  • Zaha Hadid's spaceship-shaped architecture + illuminations = #1 Instagram spot
  • LED Rose Garden — one of Seoul's hottest SNS locations
  • Connects seamlessly with Dongdaemun (동대문) shopping
  • English signage throughout

One-liner: "The #1 winter night spot for visitors — spaceship architecture meets LED roses"

② Cheonggyecheon (청계천) + Seoul Lantern Festival (서울빛초롱축제) — December Only

Location: Cheonggye Plaza (청계광장) → Gwangyo (광교) — City Hall (시청) · Jonggak (종각) stations

Lights On: December 1–31 (Seoul Lantern Festival period only)

Admission: Free

Highlights:

  • Traditional Korean lanterns + modern LEDs lining the stream
  • 2 km walking route — about 1 hour on foot
  • Connects to Gwanghwamun (광화문) and City Hall (시청)
  • Traditional Korean aesthetics meet contemporary design — a foreign visitor favorite

One-liner: "2 km of Korean lanterns and LEDs along Cheonggyecheon (청계천) — December only"

③ Gwanghwamun Square (광화문 광장) + New City Hall Media Art

Location: Gwanghwamun (광화문) — Line 5, Gwanghwamun Station (광화문역)

Lights On: Early December – January

Admission: Free

Highlights:

  • Christmas tree in Gwanghwamun Square (광화문 광장) + media art projected on the New City Hall façade
  • Pairs perfectly with Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) Night Tour (separate ticket ₩3,000)
  • 5-minute walk from Cheonggyecheon (청계천)

④ Lotte Tower (롯데타워) + Jamsil (잠실) — Seoul's Tallest View

Location: Jamsil (잠실) — Line 2 · 8, Jamsil Station (잠실역)

Lights On: Late November – February

Admission: Observation deck ₩31,000 (Seoul Sky, 117F) / Outdoor & Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) free

Highlights:

  • 123-floor observation deck = 360° Seoul night panorama
  • Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) tree and illuminations
  • Connects with Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) + Lotte World (롯데월드)

One-liner: "Korea's tallest building + the full Jamsil (잠실) illumination package"

⑤ Sinchon (신촌) · Hongdae (홍대) Streets — MZ Night Vibes

Location: Sinchon Station (신촌역) · Hongdae Entrance Station (홍대입구역)

Lights On: Early December – January

Admission: Free

Highlights:

  • Street-wide trees, LEDs, cafés, and bars
  • The go-to area for MZ-generation visitors — pairs with Hongdae (홍대) dining and clubs
  • Peak Instagram season at Christmas

⑥ N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) — Trees, Hearts & Love Locks

Location: Myeongdong (명동) · Namsan (남산) — Line 4, Myeongdong Station (명동역) + cable car

Lights On: Late November – February

Admission: Cable car ₩14,000 + observation deck ₩21,000

Highlights:

  • Christmas tree + LED heart installation + Love Lock fence
  • The definitive K-drama filming location
  • Connects with Myeongdong (명동) shopping

One-liner: "The authentic K-drama winter date spot"

⑦ Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도 한강공원) — Sunrise & City Lights

Location: Yeouido (여의도) — Line 5 · 9, Yeouido Station (여의도역)

Lights On: Early December – January

Admission: Free

Highlights:

  • Hangang (한강) riverside scenery + 63 Building (63빌딩) lights
  • Best spot in the city for January 1 sunrise
  • Cold factor is real — hot chocolate and a warm café are essential

⑧ Myeongdong Cathedral (명동대성당) + Myeongdong Street (명동길) — Christmas Mass

Location: Myeongdong (명동) — Line 4, Myeongdong Station (명동역)

Lights On: Early December – January

Admission: Free (Cathedral Mass requires prior registration)

Highlights:

  • Korea's first Gothic cathedral, built in 1898
  • Midnight Christmas Mass — open to foreign visitors (some services partially in English)
  • Myeongdong Street (명동길) shopping + Christmas trees

8 Important Tips for Visitors

✅ Arrive at 17:00–18:00 = golden hour right as the lights come on

✅ Heavy puffer coat + gloves + hat + hand warmers (-5–5°C)

✅ New Year's Eve (Dec 31) crowd: 500,000+ — stay near your hotel

✅ Hotel & restaurant reservations Dec 24–31 fill up fast — book at least one week ahead

✅ Card + mobile pay works everywhere (convenience stores for hand warmers & hot drinks)

✅ Photography is welcome (avoid capturing strangers' faces)

✅ After midnight: use Kakao T (카카오T) — subway stops running

✅ Snow & ice — watch your step (sneakers OK, boots recommended)

FAQ

Q1. Does English work?

DDP (동대문) · Lotte Tower (롯데타워) · N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) · Myeongdong Cathedral (명동대성당) all have English-speaking staff. Cheonggyecheon (청계천) and Gwanghwamun (광화문) have English signage and QR codes.

Q2. What's the best countdown option?

Bosingak Bell Ringing (보신각 타종) at Jonggak (종각) / City Hall (시청) is the traditional Korean experience — but expect 500,000+ people. For a safer, more comfortable countdown, try the Lotte Tower (롯데타워) observation deck or a hotel rooftop bar.

Q3. Good for families?

DDP (동대문) · Lotte Tower (롯데타워) · N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) are all family-friendly. Myeongdong Cathedral (명동대성당) is also fine with children.

Q4. Only have one day?

DDP (동대문) (afternoon) → Cheonggyecheon (청계천) (evening) → Myeongdong (명동) (night) — doable by foot and subway in a single day.

Q5. Trying to keep costs low?

Six spots are completely free (DDP (동대문) · Cheonggyecheon (청계천) · Gwanghwamun (광화문) · Sinchon (신촌) · Hongdae (홍대) · Yeouido (여의도) · Myeongdong (명동)) — a ₩5,000 hot chocolate is your main expense.

Q6. What if it rains or snows?

Lotte Tower (롯데타워) observation deck (indoor) + Myeongdong Cathedral (명동대성당) (indoor) + a café with night views = the perfect rainy/snowy backup plan.
