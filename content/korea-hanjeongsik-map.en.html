⚠ 3 Common Hanjeongsik Mistakes

1. "Hanjeongsik = an ordinary Korean course meal" — not knowing the Michelin tier: Assumed ordinary. In reality, La Yeon Michelin 3★ · Mingles 3★ · Hansikgonggan · Onjium + Handal fine dining + temple cuisine (Seon Jae, Netflix Chef's Table) — Korea's #1 Michelin cluster.

2. "Hanjeongsik = Seoul only" — not knowing the Jeonju & Andong heritage restaurants: Assumed Seoul-only. In reality, Jeonju hanjeongsik (#384 UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy · Pungnyeonok · Dami · Gung) · Andong heotjesabap (#397 · UNESCO Hahoe #456) — Korea's #1 heritage and tradition heartlands.

3. "Hanjeongsik = no reservation needed" — not knowing reservations are essential: Assumed walk-in. In reality, La Yeon · Mingles · Hansikgonggan · Onjium = reservations required one month in advance + Jeonju & Andong heritage restaurants (weekend reservations recommended in peak season) + English-language reservations available for foreign visitors.

🍯 5 Tips for Foreign Visitors

1. La Yeon · Mingles = book one month ahead — English reservations available · official websites · CatchTable

2. Jeonju = UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy — KTX 1.5h · Hanok Village hanjeongsik · ₩50,000–150,000 per person (~$38–113)

3. Andong heotjesabap = traditional ancestral-rite food — UNESCO Hahoe + Andong soju pairing
