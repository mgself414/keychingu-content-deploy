5 Mistakes Foreigners Often Make

1. Believing "all jazz bars are the same"

Seoul's jazz scene spans a 1976 old-school club (All That Jazz) → a 2021 trendy lounge (Dido) → a tiny venue inside a traditional market (Sound Dog). The Big 3 lineage vs Cheongdam luxury lounge vs Yeonnam hidden bar — same KRW 30,000 buys completely different experiences. See §Big 3 Comparison below.

2. Not knowing when to clap

Jazz clapping happens right after each musician's improvised solo — not at the start or end of a song. If unsure, just follow the Korean diner next to you.

3. Missing the last subway after midnight

Seoul's subway stops around midnight. Since most jazz sets run 21:00~24:00, install KakaoT (English-supported) and register your card in advance.

4. Walking in without an Instagram DM reservation

Korean jazz bars take reservations primarily via Instagram DM. Old-schools take phone / text; trendy spots use CatchTable (English). Walk-in is nearly impossible on weekends or popular line-ups.

5. Carrying only a card to old-school venues

Chunnyundoando, Sound Dog, etc. may lack card terminals. Always back up with KRW 50,000 cash.

★ Korea's Big 3 Jazz Clubs Showdown — Janus vs Chunnyundoando vs All That Jazz

The three old-school giants of Seoul's jazz scene that foreigners compare most. All started 1970s~80s + carry the history of Korean jazz itself — same "live music + drink at KRW 30,000" but the texture of time and atmosphere are completely different.

1️⃣ Janus (야누스) — Mother of Korean Jazz Vocals, Built by Park Seong-yeon

Location: Gwanghwamun area, Jongno-gu, Seoul (re-opened September 2025)

Founded: 1978 in Sinchon — 2025 marks the 7th venue

Vibe: Living museum of Korean jazz vocals, classic vocal-led live

Price (May 2026):

  • Cover + 1~2 drinks: KRW 30,000~50,000
  • No dinner option (drinks-focused)

Core highlights:

  • Founded 1978 by the late Park Seong-yeon, Korea's 1st-generation jazz vocalist — the starting point of Korean jazz vocal history
  • 6 relocations across 47 years (Sinchon → Daehakno → Ewha → Cheongdam → Seocho → Apgujeong → Gwanghwamun) — the "wandering jazz club" is itself the story
  • Heart of Gwanghwamun — natural pairing with Gyeongbokgung night opening + Cheonggyecheon stroll

Seating / Reservations: Instagram DM or phone — recommended in advance. No English info sheet → DM the Instagram 30 min before arrival to confirm seating

Golden time: Tue / Wed Park Seong-yeon tribute nights — most moving for foreigners

One-line verdict: "The stage Park Seong-yeon built — listening to history itself"

Best for: Fans of Korean jazz vocal history / Gwanghwamun night route / Music-curator perspective

2️⃣ Chunnyundoando (천년동안도) — Neighborhood Salon Above Nakwon Music Arcade

Locations:

  • Nakwon branch: 134 Supyo-ro 2F, Jongno-gu (inside Nakwon Music Arcade)
  • Jonggak branch: 6 Samil-daero 15-gil, B1-2F, Jongno-gu

Founded: 1987 in Daehakno → 1990s split into Nakwon + Jonggak

Vibe: Daily different lineup from veterans to newcomers, low-pressure neighborhood salon

Price:

  • Cover + 1 drink: KRW 25,000~40,000
  • Side dishes: KRW 10,000~25,000

Core highlights:

  • Inside Nakwon Music Arcade 2F — Korean musicians historically bought instruments and went straight upstairs for jam sessions
  • Name meaning: "May the island that plays jazz last a thousand years" — story baked into the name
  • Lineup changes daily from 1st-gen to newcomers — lowest barrier for jazz beginners
  • Hours: daily 18:00~24:00

Seating / Reservations: Instagram DM or weekday walk-in. Some seats lack card terminals — cash backup needed

Golden time: Arrive 1 hr early → browse 1~3F instrument stores (mini Korean music industry tour)

One-line verdict: "Neighborhood salon above Nakwon — the real stage of Korean musicians"

Best for: Daily availability / Jazz beginners / Korean music industry roots / Jongno night route

3️⃣ All That Jazz (올댓재즈) — Korea's Oldest Jazz Club, #1 Foreigner Pick

Location: Itaewon, Yongsan-gu, Seoul (renovated 2023)

Founded: 1976 — Korea's oldest continuously operating jazz club

Vibe: Modern renovated space, #1 foreigner-friendly

Price:

  • Cover + 1~2 drinks: KRW 30,000~50,000
  • Food: KRW 10,000~30,000

Core highlights:

  • Daily live for 49 years — open 364 of 365 days (closed only on Memorial Day)
  • Heart of Itaewon → high foreigner ratio, English menu and signage complete
  • 2023 renovation = wider seat spacing, upgraded sound system
  • Easiest to slot into a tonight's plan — "Where to go tonight?" answer

Seating / Reservations: allthatjazz.kr / Kakao channel / Instagram @allthatjazz_itaewon. Weekend = pre-book required

Golden time: Weekday 21:00 — check Instagram in advance for big band vs vocal trio lineup

One-line verdict: "Korea's oldest jazz club — #1 first-jazz pick for foreigners"

Best for: First jazz experience / Foreign friend / family / English-menu must / Itaewon route

8 Cautions for Foreigners (Checklist)

✅ Clapping timing

  • Clap after each solo (right when each musician's improvisation ends)
  • Clapping at song start / end feels off. Just follow the Korean next to you

✅ Photo / video etiquette

  • Photos during live = discouraged (especially at Club Evans)
  • Intermissions / before & after shows OK
  • Musicians / other guests' faces — ask permission

✅ Dress code

  • Cheongdam Once In A Blue Moon = smart casual
  • Others = clean casual works
  • Boogie Woogie = jeans + hoodie OK

✅ Reservations (Instagram DM is fastest)

  • Korean jazz bars respond faster on Instagram DM than phone / SMS — and English flows more naturally
  • Trendy (Dido) = CatchTable English / Asome Ground = LINE English
  • Weekend = book 1~2 weeks ahead

✅ Payment

  • Cards mostly OK
  • Old-school venues (Chunnyundoando, Sound Dog) may lack card terminals → KRW 50,000 cash backup

✅ Drinking manners

  • Two hands when pouring or receiving (Korean drinking etiquette)
  • Public drunkenness / loud noise is unwelcome here too
  • Solo foreigners → counter seats with 1~2 cocktails

✅ Late-night return

  • Subway stops around midnight
  • Install KakaoT (English) + register card in advance
  • Uber works only partially in Korea (Black / VIP only)

✅ Safety / crime

  • Korea ranks among OECD's safest
  • Still, after midnight in narrow alleys / near drunks → mind your phone & wallet
  • Solo women → English-friendly venues like Asome Ground / All That Jazz recommended

FAQ

Q1. Will English work?

All That Jazz, Asome Ground, Dido, SEOB's explicitly support English. Janus, Chunnyundoando, Sound Dog are Korean-first, but Instagram DM in advance gets you through.

Q2. Is going alone OK?

Asome Ground (1-seat layout), SEOB's (counter), All That Jazz (wide seating) are most solo-friendly. Once In A Blue Moon is a dinner lounge — not great solo.

Q3. Can I bring kids?

Most venues are 19+ (alcohol service). Family-friendly only at All That Jazz some hours. Always confirm in advance for minors.

Q4. Budget tight?

Club Evans (cover KRW 15,000~20,000 + 1 drink), Asome Ground (KRW 20,000 drink incl.) — best value pair. Korean jazz entry at KRW 20,000.

Q5. Walk-in possible?

Chunnyundoando, Boogie Woogie, Club Evans = weekday walk-in possible. Janus, Once In A Blue Moon, Dido = nearly impossible on weekends. Book 1~2 weeks ahead for weekends.

Q6. After 23:00?

Boogie Woogie (~02:00) and All That Jazz (~24:00) are options. Others close at midnight. Take KakaoT back to your hotel after midnight.

Seasonal Notes

Spring (Mar~May)

  • Gwanghwamun (Janus) + Gyeongbokgung night opening + jazz = top season
  • Foreign tourist surge — book 1~2 weeks ahead for weekends

Summer (Jun~Aug)

  • In heat, minimize outdoor route → underground / indoor venues (Dido, All That Jazz)
  • Boogie Woogie's midnight jam = the coolest move

Autumn (Sep~Nov)

  • #1 jazz season — pairs with Jarasum / Seoul International Jazz Festival
  • Cheongdam Once In A Blue Moon dinner + autumn-leaf walk

Winter (Dec~Feb)

  • Asome Ground Christmas season adds Sat + Dec 25 — popular with foreigners
  • Cold past midnight → KakaoT essential, minimize walking
