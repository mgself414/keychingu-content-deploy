Seoul Year-End Performance Guide 2026 — Two Nutcrackers, the New Year's Eve Concert and Gugak Send-Offs: December Shows That Sell in October and November, and How Foreigners Book

Subtitle: Confirmed for 2026 — Universal Ballet's Nutcracker Dec 17 to 30 at the Sejong Center Grand Theater (VIP 140,000 won to B 30,000 won, on sale since Oct 15) · the Seoul Philharmonic's Beethoven Ninth Dec 17 to 18 at the Seoul Arts Center · three year-end gugak shows at the National Theater of Korea (Dec 11, 26, 31) · the musical A Christmas Carol Dec 2 to 27 · Lotte Concert Hall Christmas concerts Dec 21 and 25 — The Korean National Ballet's Nutcracker and the Seoul Arts Center New Year's Eve Concert (sold out in two days after its Oct 30 opening in 2025) were not yet announced for 2026 as of Oct 19 · the Seoul Arts Center website does not accept overseas cards

ISSUE 03 · #1417 · 2026-10-19

🎯 The Question This Guide Answers

"I'm in Seoul in December — can I see The Nutcracker?", "I want to count down to the New Year in a concert hall," "Can I book with a foreign card?" — foreigners planning a winter trip ask these three things every year. December performances in Seoul sell in October and November. In 2025 the Seoul Arts Center New Year's Eve Concert opened on October 30 and sold out every seat within two days, and Universal Ballet's 2026 Nutcracker has been on sale since October 15. English-language "best year-end shows" articles come out in mid December, when the good seats are gone. As of October 19 this guide puts 8 performances for 2026 confirmed directly on sales and official pages (7 on NOL, 1 on the Ministry of Culture performance listing) and the 2025 pattern of 2 not yet announced into one table, and spells out, venue by venue, the payment and pickup rules that guides for foreigners leave out.

⚠ Reference date 2026-10-19. "Confirmed" means we read the dates and prices on the sales or official page ourselves; "not announced" means we give the 2025 pattern and the expected announcement timing. Ballet, orchestral and gugak (국악) instrumental concerts have a low language barrier, but the musical and the pansori (판소리) are performed in Korean, so check separately whether subtitles are provided.

① Ballet — The Same Nutcracker, Two Companies, Two Theaters

Every December two companies in Seoul stage their own Nutcracker in different theaters. Dates, prices and age limits differ, so do not mix them up.

  • Universal Ballet (Sejong Center Grand Theater) — 2026-12-17 to 30. The 19:30 shows are on 12/17, 18, 22, 23, 24, 29 and 30; the two-show days (14:00 and 18:00) are 12/25 (Fri), 26 (Sat) and 27 (Sun) (NOL sales page; showtimes may change). Running time 120 minutes (20-minute intermission). Age 4 and up (born on or before 2022-12-31). VIP ₩140,000 (~$105) · R ₩120,000 (~$90) · S ₩90,000 (~$67.50) · A ₩60,000 (~$45) · B ₩30,000 (~$22.50) — some seat grades cost different amounts depending on the date, so check "View all prices" on NOL, and since proof such as a health insurance card or a household register is required to verify a child's age, ask in advance whether a passport is accepted for a foreign child. General sales started 2026-10-15 at 14:00. The Vainonen (Mariinsky) version, closer to a classical staging (Korea Times [Secondary]). Booking closes 2 hours before each show, and cancellation before midnight on the purchase day is free (NOL)
  • Korean National Ballet (Seoul Arts Center Opera Theater) — the 2026 run was not announced as of Oct 19. In 2025 it ran Dec 13 to 25, Tue to Fri 19:30, Sat and Sun 14:00 and 18:30, no Monday shows, 120 minutes, 48 months and up, R ₩120,000 (~$90) · S ₩100,000 (~$75) · A ₩60,000 (~$45) · B ₩30,000 (~$22.50) · C ₩10,000 (~$7.50) (Seoul Arts Center English page). This is the Bolshoi's Yuri Grigorovich version, in which the Nutcracker is danced by a child from the company's academy rather than played by a wooden doll (Korea Times [Secondary]). The 2025 announcement came in late November — check the Seoul Arts Center site in November
  • How to choose: Universal Ballet's December 17 opening is confirmed. If you leave before that, only the Korean National Ballet fits, but its 2026 schedule is not announced yet (in 2025 it opened Dec 13), so plan after the late-November announcement. On age, Universal Ballet admits children born on or before 2022-12-31 (age 4), and the Korean National Ballet was 48 months and up in 2025 — recheck the 2026 rule in the announcement

② Classical — The Choral Symphony and the New Year's Eve Concert

  • Seoul Philharmonic, "Jaap van Zweden's Ode to Joy" — 2026-12-17 (Thu) and 18 (Fri) 19:30, Seoul Arts Center Concert Hall (the NOL page we read is for the Dec 17 show; the Dec 18 show has a separate page, so we could not cross-check its prices and times — recheck before booking). Schumann's Konzertstück for four horns plus Beethoven's Symphony No. 9 "Choral". 100 minutes (15-minute intermission), elementary school age and up. From R ₩150,000 (~$112.50) down to C ₩10,000 (~$7.50). Booking until 1 hour before the concert (NOL)
  • Seoul Arts Center New Year's Eve Concert — held in the Concert Hall on the night of December 31. In 2025 it started at 21:30, and after the concert a countdown and fireworks set to classical music followed in the outdoor plaza, with the Korean National Symphony Orchestra (국립심포니오케스트라), priced R ₩120,000 (~$90) · S ₩100,000 (~$75) · A ₩70,000 (~$52.50) · B ₩50,000 (~$37.50) · choir seats (hapchangseok, 합창석) ₩50,000 (~$37.50), and sold out every seat within two days of the October 30 opening; extra choir seats were released on December 4 (Seoul Arts Center press release). The 2026 edition was not announced as of Oct 19 — it is usually announced in late October, so open the Seoul Arts Center site (sac.or.kr) as soon as you read this

③ Gugak — Three Year-End Shows at the National Theater of Korea

The National Theater of Korea sits on the slopes of Namsan (남산) at 59 Jangchungdan-ro (장충단로), Jung-gu (중구). Walk up from Line 3 Dongguk University Station (동대입구역) Exit 6, or take the pre-show shuttle (check the theater's notice for whether it runs). All three shows are for elementary school age and up.

  • National Orchestra of Korea 2026 Winter Concert — Dec 11 (Fri) 19:30, Haeoreum Theater, 90 minutes. R ₩70,000 (~$52.50) · S ₩50,000 (~$37.50) · A ₩30,000 (~$22.50)
  • National Changgeuk Company 2026 Year-End Pansori "Sori Jeongdam" — Dec 26 (Sat) 15:00, Daloreum Theater, about 90 minutes. All seats ₩30,000 (~$22.50). Pansori is built on sung Korean narrative, so check whether subtitles are offered
  • National Changgeuk Company 2026 Year-End Concert "Eojildeojil" — Dec 31 (Thu) 19:30, Haeoreum Theater, 100 minutes. R ₩60,000 (~$45) · S ₩40,000 (~$30) · A ₩20,000 (~$15). As a December 31 night show it is an alternative to the New Year's Eve Concert
  • Booking closes at 17:00 the day before for Mon to Sat shows and at 11:00 the day before for Sunday shows (NOL). Cancellation fees run from ₩4,000 (~$3) per ticket up to 30% depending on how close to the date you cancel

⚠ Five Traps People Actually Fall Into

1. Starting to book after arriving in December — the 2025 New Year's Eve Concert sold out in two days. Buy in October or November.

2. Overseas card declined on the Seoul Arts Center website — the official notice says it does not work. Use the box office on the day or NOL World.

3. Name mismatch — your NOL World account name must match your passport. Dropping a middle name can get you stopped at the door.

4. Children's age limits — The Nutcracker is age 4 or 48 months; the National Theater, Seoul Philharmonic and Yuhki Kuramoto shows are elementary school age and up. Preschoolers are not admitted.

5. Mixing up the two Nutcrackers — Universal Ballet is at the Sejong Center (Gwanghwamun), the Korean National Ballet at the Seoul Arts Center (Nambu Bus Terminal). Different dates, different theaters.

⚙ FAQ 3

1. Do I need to understand Korean? — Ballet, orchestral and OST concerts do not depend on language. The musical A Christmas Carol and the year-end pansori are in Korean, so ask the venue about subtitles.

2. Is there a dress code? — No formal wear is required. Winter coats can go in the theater cloakroom (operated venue by venue).

3. What about the last train after the show? — The New Year's Eve Concert and the year-end concert end late at night. For Seoul's last subway trains and late-night transport, see #1395.
