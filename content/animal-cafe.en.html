7 Seoul Animal Cafes — Cats, Dogs, and Sheep, with Entry Fees and Touch Rules

Subtitle: Four cat cafes, two dog cafes, and one sheep cafe in Myeongdong, Gangnam, Hongdae, and Hapjeong. Each one is confirmed as open on Kakao Map, and prices are written with their source and date (snack prices follow reviews). Raccoon and meerkat cafes were left out after checking the legal rules.

ISSUE 03 · #442 · 2026-11-25 · Seoul Animal Cafes

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 7

This list is for travelers who want to spend an hour with cats or dogs, and for families looking for an indoor place to rest on a rainy day. Pricing in Korean animal cafes differs from place to place. Some include a drink in the entry fee, some charge by the hour, and some only ask you to order a drink. How you may give treats and the age rules for children also differ by shop.

There are three selection criteria.

  1. The Kakao Map (카카오맵) place page says "open," and blog reviews or photos were posted in October–November 2026.
  2. The entry fee or drink price was confirmed from an owner-registered value on Kakao Map (registered within the last 2 years) or from an official page.
  3. The animals are cats, dogs, or sheep. The reason raccoon and meerkat cafes are left out is in the "Law" section below.

⚠ Notation principle: Business hours are Kakao Map values (checked 2026-11-25). Hours the owner registered are called 'owner-registered values,' and hours only displayed on the map are called 'Kakao Map displayed hours.' Prices carry their source and registration date, and rules taken from reviews carry the review date.

① 4 Cat Cafes

  • Roofcatme Cat Cafe Myeongdong Branch (루프캣미 고양이카페 명동점) — A cat cafe on the 2nd floor of a building near Myeongdong Station (명동역). The entry ticket is 20,000 won for adults and 15,000 won for children age 12 and under (Kakao Map, registered 2026-11-10). Three reviews from October–November 2026 say a drink is included in the ticket (you scan a coupon and pull it from a machine yourself). The official site has no drink notice, and a 2026-01 Kakao review says it became a paid item from February. You pay at a kiosk and receive a locker-key wristband (official site). The official rules say not to pull a cat's tail or any part of its body. According to the same two reviews, churu (츄르, a cat treat) is 3,000 won, 1 per team. You give it only to cats wearing a scarf, using the pink spoon, and you do not touch cats wearing a 'DANGER' badge. There is no age limit, but children age 12 and under must enter with a guardian (official). At the exit there are lint rollers and deodorizer (2026-11-11 review). The Kakao Map displayed hours are 12:00~22:00 daily, with last entry at 21:30. Regular closures are twice a month (in November, the 2nd and the 16th), and the December schedule is not yet on the official site. The address is Myeongdong 8ga-gil 27 (명동8가길 27), 2F, Jung-gu (중구), a 3-minute walk from Myeongdong Station (Line 4) Exit 9.
  • Cat Cafe Mocha Gangnam Branch (고양이카페 모카 강남점) (MOCHA) — On the 5th floor of a building near Gangnam Station (강남역). It has floor-to-ceiling windows (2026-11-12 review) and comics (March 2025 Kakao review). The official page lists the weekday price as 10,000 won for the first 30 minutes, then 2,000 won per 10 minutes, up to 28,000 won. On weekends, public holidays, and certain days it is 15,000 won for the first 30 minutes, up to 38,000 won. The drink-bar fee is included in the entry fee (official). You must be 4 or older to enter, and children age 12 and under need a guardian with them (official). According to the 2026-11-12 review, you check in yourself on a tablet at the entrance and pay when you leave. The tablet has a language button, and cat treats are 5,000 won. An October 2025 Kakao review says you cannot touch the cats while they are eating. Hours on the official page are 12:00~22:00, last entry 21:30, open all year. The address is Teheran-ro 1-gil 29 (테헤란로1길 29), Yongil Building (용일빌딩) 5F, Gangnam-gu (강남구), a 4-minute walk from Gangnam Station (Line 2 and Sinbundang Line) Exit 11.
  • Jipsaui Haru Gangnam Branch (집사의하루 강남점) — Introduced as a shelter-cat cafe where rescued cats live (2026-11-18 review). Entry is 10,000 won (Kakao Map, registered 2025-09-08), and a drink is an Americano or tea at 2,000 won (Kakao Map, registered 2025-06-24). Both a November 2025 Kakao review and the 2026-11-18 review say a drink is optional, bought only if you want one. According to the same review, there is no time limit on weekdays and 90 minutes on weekends and holidays. Churu is squeezed onto a spoon for you. The Kakao Map displayed hours are 12:00~22:00 daily, and a review says it opens at 11:00 on weekends and holidays (2026-11-18). The address is Teheran-ro 1-gil 40 (테헤란로1길 40), 5F, Gangnam-gu (강남구), in the same alley as the Mocha Gangnam Branch. It is a 5-minute walk from Gangnam Station Exit 11.
  • Cat Multi Cafe MAO (고양이멀티카페 MAO) (마오) — On the 3rd and 4th floors of a castle-styled building in a Hongdae (홍대) alley. There are 22 cats (2026-11-21 review, based on the sign at the entrance), and you can use a Nintendo Switch, a PlayStation, board games, and OTT streaming (same review). Pricing is hourly: 30 minutes 8,000 won, 1 hour 10,000 won, 2 hours 13,000 won, 3 hours 16,000 won, and an all-day pass (you may go out and come back) 22,000 won (Kakao Map, registered 2026-10-22). According to the 2026-11-06 review, you pick drinks and snacks separately and pay for everything at once on your way out, using the necklace you receive when you enter. To keep the cats from slipping out, press the bell and wait until a staff member opens the door. When you leave, you also fasten the latch. You take your shoes off to enter. The Kakao Map displayed hours are 13:30~22:00 daily. The address is Wausan-ro 21-gil 36-10 (와우산로21길 36-10), 3F and 4F, Mapo-gu (마포구), an 8-minute walk from Hongik University Station (홍대입구역) (Line 2, Airport Railroad, and Gyeongui-Jungang Line) Exit 9.

② 2 Dog Cafes

  • THE Gwiyeowo (THE귀여워) (더귀여워) — A dog cafe on the 2nd floor of a building between Sangsu Station (상수역) and Hongdae. There is a separate cat room inside (two 2026-11-19 reviews). By Kakao Map menu, an entry ticket alone is 11,000 won, and a ticket bundled with a drink is 13,000~14,000 won (registered 2025-12-31). The visit lasts 90 minutes. The in-store notice copied into a 2026-11-19 review says 'one drink per person required (entry fee included when you order a drink)' and bans outside food and snacks. The guardian-accompaniment age is written as under 13 in the review quoting this notice and as 13 and under in another review from the same day. You cannot bring your own dog. Dog treats are 1,000 won, 1 per team, bought only with cash or bank transfer (same review). There is a time when staff gather the dogs on the stairs and take a group photo. By owner-registered values it is open 12:30~21:00 daily and closed on the days of Seollal (설날) and Chuseok (추석). The address is Eoulmadang-ro 44-1 (어울마당로 44-1), La Comma Building (라꼼마빌딩) 2F, Mapo-gu (마포구), a 5-minute walk from Sangsu Station (Line 6) Exit 1.
  • Shibayashiki (시바야시키) — A Shiba Inu cafe in Hapjeong (합정). It has seven Shiba Inu (Kakao Map menu description), and a review mentions a Japanese-style interior (2026-11-05). The fee is 16,000 won per person for 90 minutes, with 1 drink included (Kakao Map, registered 2026-10-15). According to the 2026-11-05 review, it runs in fixed sessions. They start at 13:00, 15:00, 17:00, 19:00, and 21:00, each lasting 90 minutes, and booking on Naver Booking (네이버 예약) is recommended. The review says capacity looks like around 10 people. A 2026-11-18 Kakao review lists three house rules. Do not touch the dogs' paws. Do not hug them against their will. Do not approach too close first. The Kakao Map displayed hours are 13:00~22:30 daily. The address is Eoulmadang-ro 5-gil 47 (어울마당로5길 47), 1F, Mapo-gu (마포구), a 6-minute walk from Hapjeong Station (Lines 2 and 6) Exit 3.

③ 1 Sheep Cafe

  • Thanks Nature Cafe (땡스네이쳐카페) — A cafe in the basement of a shopping arcade near Hongik University Station (홍대입구역), where two sheep live. There is no entry fee, and you only order a drink or dessert (September 2026 Kakao review). The registered prices are Americano 4,400 won and cafe latte 5,400 won (Kakao Map, registered 2026-04-20). According to the 2026-11-17 review, one order per person is the rule, and for waffles and bingsu (shaved ice) one order for two people is also allowed. The sheep there now are two males born in April 2026, brought from the Yangtte Ranch (양떼목장) in Daegwallyeong (대관령) (same review). Before touching the sheep, you order first at the kiosk and wash your hands at the sink on the right. The cautions in the same review are these. Do not disturb the sheep at feeding times (10:00, 13:00, 16:00, 19:00). Do not put your hand in their mouths. Do not hug them against their will. They may bite shoelaces, hair, and accessories, so be careful. The Kakao Map displayed hours are Mon–Sat 11:00~20:00 and Sun 12:00~20:00. Last order is 19:30 (review). The address is Hongik-ro 10 (홍익로 10), Prugio Arcade (푸르지오상가) B1, Unit 121, Mapo-gu (마포구), a 5-minute walk from Hongik University Station Exit 9.

⚖ Why Raccoon and Meerkat Cafes Are Left Out — The Law

Article 8-3, Paragraph 1 of the Wildlife Protection and Management Act (야생생물 보호 및 관리에 관한 법률) says: "No one may display live wild animals in a facility that has not been permitted as a zoo or aquarium under Article 8 of the Act on the Management of Zoos and Aquariums (동물원 및 수족관의 관리에 관한 법률)." (There are provisos for some low-risk species other than mammals, for public-interest purposes such as academic research and education, and for holders of a wildlife business permit.) This provision was newly created by Act No. 19088, promulgated 2022-12-13. Under Article 1 of the Addenda, it took effect on 2023-12-14, one year after promulgation. A violation is punishable by up to 2 years in prison or a fine of up to 20 million won (Article 69, Paragraph 1, Item 17 of the same Act).

Businesses already operating get a grace period. Article 3 of the Addenda allows display, for 4 years after the effective date, "limited to the held animals reported," for those who reported their display status to the provincial governor or mayor before the effective date. In its 2023-12-05 announcement the Ministry of Environment (환경부) put this grace period at "until December 13, 2027" (Korea Policy Briefing (정책브리핑)). For operators who filed for the grace period, the latter part of Article 3, Paragraph 1 of the Addenda requires that they not commit the prohibited acts in Article 15, Paragraph 1, Item 4 of the Act on the Management of Zoos and Aquariums (acts set by Presidential Decree, such as riding, touching, and feeding, that cause held animals unnecessary pain, fear, or stress for the purpose of public entertainment or shows). A violation carries an administrative fine of up to 5,000,000 won.

The meerkat and raccoon cafes in Hongdae (홍대) and Hyehwa (혜화) (those shown as open on Kakao Map, based on the 'raccoon cafe' (라쿤카페) tag) are left off this list. We could not confirm from public records whether they filed under Article 3 of the Addenda, and display becomes banned for them after the grace period ends on 2027-12-13.

The cats and dogs in this guide are 'companion animals' (반려동물) as set by Article 3 of the Enforcement Rule of the Animal Protection Act (동물보호법 시행규칙) (dogs, cats, rabbits, ferrets, guinea pigs, and hamsters). Article 43 of the same Enforcement Rule defines a business that displays 5 or more of the operator's own animals for the purpose of showing them or letting people touch them as an 'animal exhibition business,' and this business must be registered with the district office (Article 73 of the Animal Protection Act (동물보호법)). Sheep fall under 'livestock' (sheep) in Article 2 of the Livestock Industry Act (축산법).

🗺 Route — Two Half-Day Plans with One Animal Cafe

A. One hour between Myeongdong shopping

  1. Afternoon Browse the Myeongdong street
  2. At dusk Roofcatme Myeongdong Branch (last entry 21:30)
  3. Evening Head toward Euljiro (을지로) or Namsan (남산)

B. Hongdae·Hapjeong afternoon (after 1 p.m.)

  1. 13:00 Shibayashiki first session (entry on the hour) or a drink with the sheep at Thanks Nature Cafe
  2. After 15:00 THE Gwiyeowo toward Sangsu or MAO in a Hongdae alley
  3. Evening Dinner around Hongik University Station

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Walking in without checking the pricing method — Mocha charges per 30 minutes, MAO sells time passes, Roofcatme and Shibayashiki charge a flat entry ticket, and Thanks Nature Cafe asks you to order a drink.
  2. Picking up or chasing the animals first — Roofcatme says not to touch cats with a 'DANGER' badge, and Shibayashiki says not to hug dogs against their will. Thanks Nature Cafe says not to hug the sheep against their will. Sit down, wait for the animals to come to you, and give treats only in the way the shop sets.
  3. Not checking the children's age rule — The only confirmed ones are Mocha (age 4 and over), Roofcatme (age 12 and under with a guardian), and THE Gwiyeowo (under 13 / 13 and under with a guardian).

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. Can I go if I have allergies? — Cat cafes have a lot of fur indoors. Roofcatme keeps lint rollers and deodorizer at the exit (review), and a MAO review recommends comfortable clothes that can get fur on them. If you have allergies, talk to your doctor first.
  2. What is the cheapest way to see animals? — Thanks Nature Cafe has no entry fee, only the drink price (Americano 4,400 won). Among cat cafes, MAO's 30 minutes for 8,000 won is the lowest.
  3. Which places need a reservation? — Shibayashiki runs in sessions, so Naver Booking is recommended (review). Mocha's official page says no reservation is needed. For the rest, you pay right away at the on-site kiosk or counter.
