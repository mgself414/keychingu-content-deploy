⚠️ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

Mistake 1 — Naver Webtoon ≠ LINE Webtoon

Naver Webtoon (webtoon.naver.com) defaults to a Korean UI, with English translations for only some titles. To read in English right away, use LINE Webtoon (webtoon.com) or the Naver Webtoon Global app. When a title exists on both platforms, the number of English-translated episodes can differ.

Mistake 2 — Kakao Page payment

Kakao Page's coin purchases are built around Kakao Pay or Korean cards. Foreign visitors get a smoother experience going through Google Play / App Store in-app purchases. Confirm in advance whether you can pay without a local bank account.

Mistake 3 — How manhwabang work

Korean manhwabang are self-serve — you walk in and pull whatever books you want off the shelf yourself. Don't expect staff to field "recommend me a comic," and many locations offer no English service. It helps to look up the Korean title beforehand using Google Translate plus a Korean-language title search.

12 Picks Across 4 Axes

Axis 1: Webtoon Platforms (3 Picks)

1. Naver Webtoon

  • Location: Online platform — webtoon.naver.com · app "Naver Webtoon (네이버 웹툰)"
  • Signature titles: Tower of God (신의 탑) · Solo Leveling · Itaewon Class · Yumi's Cells
  • Cost: Free serialization (some completed titles go paid) · paid early-access previews ₩100-300/episode · cookie purchase packages available
  • Foreigner fit: Defaults to a Korean UI. Some titles have English versions. Switch to English via the Naver Webtoon Global app
  • Access: Search "Naver Webtoon" on the App Store/Google Play — free signup
  • English available: Some titles in EN, the rest Korean
  • Timing: Fixed serialization days (each creator has a set day, Mon-Sun)
  • Practical tip: The original Tower of God is available to read free and in full on Naver Webtoon (confirm as of 2025)

2. Kakao Page (카카오페이지)

  • Location: Online platform (page.kakao.com · app "Kakao Page")
  • Signature titles: Solo Leveling · Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint · Business Proposal · The Remarried Empress
  • Cost: Free via "wait-for-free" (24-48h wait) · coin purchase ₩100-500/episode · monthly pass available
  • Foreigner fit: Korean UI. No English service as of 2026-06. For EN versions, check global partner platforms like Tapas or MangaPlus
  • Access: App Store/Google Play · coins can be purchased via Google Play in-app payment
  • English available: None (the platform itself is Korean-only)
  • Timing: The wait-for-free cycle varies by title (check in-app)
  • Practical tip: The original Solo Leveling webtoon completed its run on Kakao Page. For the EN translation, check Tapas or search for the global publisher's channel

3. LINE Webtoon (Global)

  • Location: Online platform (webtoon.com · app "WEBTOON")
  • Signature titles: Tower of God · Lore Olympus · True Beauty · My Dear Cold-Blooded King (EN, global)
  • Cost: Free serialization · Fast Pass (early access) coin system · coins $0.99-$4.99
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ Full English service, English-language payment, global cards accepted
  • Access: webtoon.com, or "WEBTOON" on the App Store/Google Play
  • English available: 100% EN (English is the default UI)
  • Timing: English translations of Korean originals can lag the Korean serialization by one to several episodes
  • Practical tip: The #1 recommended platform for foreign visitors. Many titles can be read free with no account required

Axis 2: Manhwabang & Culture (3 Picks)

4. Manhwabang — Hongdae & Gangnam, Seoul

  • Location (Hongdae): The alleys in front of Hongdae (홍대), Mapo-gu (between Hapjeong and Hongik Univ. stations). Leading venues change over time — search "Hongdae manhwabang" on Naver Map before visiting to confirm it's still open
  • Location (Gangnam): Around Gangnam Station and Sinchon. Same advice — check Naver Map on the spot
  • Cost: ₩3,000-5,000/hour (varies by venue) · some offer a package that includes a drink
  • Foreigner fit: Moderate. There's a language barrier, but getting in works fine with gestures and a translation app. Some venues stock original Japanese manga, which helps Japanese-reading visitors
  • Access: Within a 5-10 minute walk of Line 2 Hongik Univ. Station Exit 9 / around Line 2 Gangnam Station
  • English available: Mostly none. A Google Translate app is essential
  • Timing: Crowded weekend afternoons and evenings. Weekday mornings through lunch are quieter, at the same price
  • Practical tip: Jot down the Korean title of what you want to read before visiting. Korean readers commonly search using short forms — Solo Leveling as "Hollep" (혼렙) — so bring the Korean title along to make searching easier

5. Bucheon Comics Museum (부천만화박물관)

  • Location: 1 Giljju-ro, Wonmi-gu, Bucheon, Gyeonggi (near the Boksagol Cultural Center)
  • Transport: About a 10-minute walk from Line 7 Bucheon City Hall Station, or transfer to a bus from Line 1 Bucheon Station
  • Cost: Adults ₩5,000 · teens ₩3,000 · children ₩2,000 (confirm as of 2026-06)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ Exhibits on Korean comics history plus hands-on facilities. Some English signage. Korea's largest museum dedicated to comics
  • English available: Some English signage · confirm on-site whether an English audio guide is available
  • Hours: Tue-Sun 10:00-18:00 (closed Mondays and the day after a public holiday — check the official site before visiting)
  • Timing: Time your visit to the Bucheon International Comics Festival (BICOF, typically July-August) for extra outdoor exhibits and events. Check bicof.com for the festival schedule
  • Practical tip: The museum alone fills half a day to a full day. Pairing it with other Bucheon sightseeing makes for an efficient trip

6. Comics Culture Cluster — Goods, Bookshops, Cafés

  • Hot Toys/figure district: Merchandise shops near COEX in Gangnam-gu, or around Hongdae. Official merchandise for specific webtoons is sold at places like Kakao Friends Shop and LINE Friends flagship stores
  • LINE Friends flagship (Seoul): The LINE Friends store in Hongdae (check the official site for current operating status and location before visiting)
  • Webtoon-original filming-location cafés: Cross-ref #507 (web-drama filming locations) — around Itaewon and Seongsu
  • Independent comics bookstores: Independent-publishing comics bookstores exist around Hongdae and Hapjeong in Seoul. Search "comics specialty bookstore" on Naver Map
  • Foreigner fit: Merchandise shops are relatively easy. Prioritize venues that accept English-language payment
  • Timing: Season-limited merchandise tends to launch during a title's event periods

FAQ 6

Q1. Where should I start if I'm new to K-webtoons?

LINE Webtoon (webtoon.com) is the #1 choice. English UI, global payment, many free titles. Start with Tower of God, True Beauty, or Lore Olympus. If you also want to see the Korean originals, use the Naver Webtoon app alongside it.

Q2. How much does it cost?

Many titles on LINE Webtoon EN can be read free. Paid early access (Fast Pass) runs $0.99-$4.99 in coin packages. Kakao Page runs ₩100-500/episode in coins, or free via wait-for-free (24-48h wait). Manhwabang run ₩3,000-5,000/hour.

Q3. Which titles can I read in English?

Most major Korean originals have an English translation available through the LINE Webtoon EN hub. For Solo Leveling in English, check Tapas or a global publisher's channel (licensing varies by platform).

Q4. Can a foreign visitor go to a manhwabang alone?

Yes. It's a self-serve setup — walk in and pull whatever books you want. A Google Translate app helps bridge the language barrier. It's easier if you've jotted down the Korean title of what you want to read beforehand.

Q5. How far is the Bucheon Comics Museum from Seoul?

About 40-50 minutes by Line 7 from Gangnam Station. Get off at Bucheon City Hall Station, then a 10-minute walk. Doable as a day trip.

Q6. How do K-anime and K-webtoons connect?

Popular webtoons like Solo Leveling and Tower of God have been adapted into anime. Watch the anime on Crunchyroll (global, EN subtitles) and read the original on LINE Webtoon or Naver Webtoon. See the #636 K-anime deep dive for more.
