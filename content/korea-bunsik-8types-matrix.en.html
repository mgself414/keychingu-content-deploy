Korean Bunsik 8-Type Matrix — A Full Set of Street Food from Tteokbokki to Mandu

> Subtitle: 8 Types of Tteokbokki, Gimbap, Sundae, Twigim, Mandu, Rabokki, Eomuk, and Hot Dogs for Foreign Tourists + Regional Signatures

ISSUE 03 · #810 · 2026-06-07

🎯 Why a Korean Bunsik Matrix?

The pain point for foreign tourists regarding Korean bunsik (분식, Korean snack food) is: "There are too many types of street food!" Bunsik means cost-effectiveness (가성비, gasungbi) + lightness + street food + a prime spot for foreign Instagrammers. Even tteokbokki (떡볶이) varies in Sindang-dong (신당동) vs. Myeong-dong (명동) vs. Busan (부산), and gimbap (김밥) comes in many forms like mayak gimbap (마약김밥), kkoma gimbap (꼬마김밥), and tuna gimbap (참치김밥). Foreigners often (a) find it hard to choose from too many menu options, (b) don't know how to adjust the spiciness, (c) suspect prices are too low (₩1,500~5,000, which is ~$1.1-$3.7 / ~8-27 RMB / ~160-530 JPY), and (d) don't know how to eat them. This guide provides an 8-type matrix + regional signatures + a priority list for foreign beginners.

🍢 Bunsik 8-Type Deep Dive

🌶 Tteokbokki (떡볶이) — Foreigner #1

  • Signature: Mabokrim (마복림) in Sindang-dong (신당동) (1953, originator of jeukseok tteokbokki (즉석떡볶이, cook-at-your-table tteokbokki)) / Myeong-dong Tteokbokki Alley (명동 떡볶이 골목)
  • Types:
  • Gungmul Tteokbokki (국물떡볶이, Spicy soupy): Most common
  • Jeukseok Tteokbokki (즉석떡볶이, Cooked at table): Sindang-dong (신당동) style
  • Gungjung Tteokbokki (궁중떡볶이, Royal court): Soy sauce seasoned · royal traditional
  • Rose Tteokbokki (로제떡볶이): MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) trend (cream + spicy)
  • Spiciness Levels: Not spicy → Slightly spicy → Spicy → Very spicy. "An maepge haejuseyo" (안 맵게 해주세요) = "Less spicy, please"
  • Price: ₩3,500~6,000 (~$2.6-$4.4 / ~19-32 RMB / ~370-630 JPY) / Jeukseok (즉석) = ₩30,000 for 4 people (~$22 / ~160 RMB / ~3,150 JPY)

🍙 Gimbap (김밥) — Foreigner Beginner #1

  • Signature: Gwangjang Market (광장시장) Mayak Gimbap (마약김밥, "addictive" nickname) / Cheonguk Gimbap (천국김밥) (chain #1)
  • Types:
  • Tuna Gimbap (참치김밥) — Foreigner #1 priority (lowest chance of failure #1)
  • Mayak Gimbap (마약김밥, mini size) — Gwangjang Market (광장시장) #1 spot
  • Kkoma Gimbap (꼬마김밥, Mini) — 1-bite size, good for children
  • Dongaseu Gimbap (돈가스김밥, Pork cutlet) · Cheese Gimbap (치즈김밥) — MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) trend
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (many English menus, photo menus, recognized as Korean sushi)
  • Price: ₩3,000~5,000 (~$2.2-$3.7 / ~16-27 RMB / ~315-530 JPY)

🩸 Sundae (순대)

  • Signature: Gwangjang Market Sundae Alley (광장시장 순대골목) / Sillim-dong Sundae Town (신림동 순대타운)
  • Foreigner Entry Barrier: Sundae (순대) = blood sausage. Not recommended for first Korean meal, for curious enthusiasts only.
  • Recommended Toppings: Tteokbokki (떡볶이) sauce · dadaegi (다대기, spicy seasoning paste) · deulkkaegaru (들깨가루, perilla seed powder)
  • Price: ₩4,000~7,000 (~$3.0-$5.2 / ~21-38 RMB / ~420-730 JPY)

🍤 Twigim (튀김) — Bunsik Enthusiast

  • Types: Ojingeo Twigim (오징어튀김, fried squid) · Saeu Twigim (새우튀김, fried shrimp) · Goguma Twigim (고구마튀김, fried sweet potato) · Gimmari Twigim (김말이튀김, fried seaweed roll) · Mandu Twigim (만두튀김, fried dumpling) · Yachae Twigim (야채튀김, fried vegetables)
  • Recommended Pairing: Dipping in tteokbokki (떡볶이) sauce (most common)
  • Price: ₩1,000~3,000 (~$0.7-$2.2 / ~5-16 RMB / ~100-315 JPY) per piece
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (similar to Japanese tempura)

🥟 Mandu (만두)

  • Signature: Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자) (1966 — kalguksu + mandu nofo (노포, 30+ year traditional restaurant), high foreigner recognition) / Gwangjang Market Mandu (광장시장 만두)
  • Types:
  • Gogi Mandu (고기만두, Meat): Pork · beef
  • Kimchi Mandu (김치만두): Spicy
  • Dangmyeon Mandu (당면만두, Glass noodle): Japchae (잡채) dumpling
  • Mul Mandu (물만두, Boiled) · Jjin Mandu (찐만두, Steamed) · Gun Mandu (군만두, Pan-fried)
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (recognized as Chinese dim sum · similar to Japanese gyoza)
  • Price: ₩4,000~7,000 (~$3.0-$5.2 / ~21-38 RMB / ~420-730 JPY)

🍜 Rabokki (라볶이)

  • Signature: Ramen + tteokbokki (떡볶이) + eomuk (어묵) + mandu (만두) combined. MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) enthusiast #1 priority.
  • Types: Shin Ramyun Rabokki (신라면 라볶이) · Samyang Rabokki (삼양 라볶이) · Cream Rose Rabokki (크림 로제 라볶이)
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (recognized as ramen)
  • Price: ₩5,000~8,000 (~$3.7-$5.9 / ~27-43 RMB / ~530-840 JPY)

🍢 Eomuk (어묵) — Winter Enthusiast

  • Signature: Busan Samjin Eomuk (부산 삼진어묵) (1953, Korea #1) / Street Eomuk (길거리 어묵) (skewered)
  • Types:
  • Kkochi Eomuk (꼬치 어묵, ₩1,000/pc): Street food enthusiast
  • Busan Eomuk (부산 어묵, various shapes · tastes): Busan (부산) eomuk #1 spot
  • Eomukguk (어묵국, udon broth + eomuk): #1 priority on cold days
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (similar to Japanese oden)
  • Price: ₩1,000~2,000 (~$0.7-$1.5 / ~5-11 RMB / ~100-210 JPY) per piece

🌭 Korean Hot Dog (핫도그) — Foreigner Vlog #1

  • Signature: Myeong-dong (명동) · Hongdae (홍대) · Gangnam (강남) hot dog store cluster
  • Types:
  • Mozzarella Cheese Hot Dog (모짜렐라 치즈 핫도그, Cheese melting): Foreigner vlog #1 spot
  • Gamja Hot Dog (감자 핫도그, Potato): MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) trend
  • Seoltang Hot Dog (설탕 핫도그, Sugar coated): Classic
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (foreigner Instagram · vlog #1 spot)
  • Price: ₩3,000~5,000 (~$2.2-$3.7 / ~16-27 RMB / ~315-530 JPY)

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Bunsik (분식) = all spicy": Only spicy bunsik = tteokbokki (떡볶이) · rabokki (라볶이) · kimchi mandu (김치만두). Gimbap (김밥) · mandu (만두) · hot dog (핫도그) · twigim (튀김) = not spicy.

2. "Prices are too cheap = suspicious": Bunsik prices of ₩3,000~7,000 are normal. Korean bunsik offers excellent cost-effectiveness (가성비, gasungbi) ★★★★★.

3. "Sundae (순대) = regular sausage": Sundae (순대) = blood sausage. Not recommended for first Korean meal.

4. "Mayak Gimbap (마약김밥) = contains drugs": "Mayak" (마약) = nickname (meaning "addictive"). In 2021, the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety and related ministries jointly recommended "refraining from using the word 'mayak'" → alternative labels (kkoma gimbap (꼬마김밥) · Mini Gimbap). However, many signs in Gwangjang Market (광장시장) still say "Mayak Gimbap", and it's commonly used as a search term by foreigners = "Mayak Gimbap".

5. "Sindang-dong Tteokbokki (신당동 떡볶이) = one restaurant": Sindang-dong (신당동) = tteokbokki alley (골목, alley) cluster (many stores besides Mabokrim (마복림)). It is the birthplace of jeukseok tteokbokki (즉석떡볶이).

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. Top 1st priority for foreign beginners: Tteokbokki (떡볶이) (not spicy) + Tuna Gimbap (참치김밥) + Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자) Mandu (만두) 3 types.

2. Top 1st priority for Instagram/vlog: Mozzarella Cheese Hot Dog (모짜렐라 치즈 핫도그) + Gwangjang Market (광장시장) Mayak Gimbap (마약김밥).

3. MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) Trends: Rose Tteokbokki (로제떡볶이) · Cream Rabokki (크림 라볶이) · Cheese Hot Dog (치즈 핫도그).

4. Bunsik Enthusiast Hotspots: Sindang-dong Tteokbokki (신당동 떡볶이) · Gwangjang Market Gimbap (광장시장 김밥) · Busan Eomuk (부산 어묵) · Myeongdong Kyoja Mandu (명동교자 만두) · Insa-dong Hotteok (인사동 호떡).

5. Allergies: Gluten (wheat · soy sauce) · seafood (eomuk · twigim) · soy (doenjang seasoning) should be declared in advance.

6. Vegetarian · Halal · Kosher: Gimbap (김밥) (excluding tuna · cheese) = vegetarian OK / Tteokbokki (떡볶이) generally includes eomuk (어묵) · dashi broth (다시 국물, anchovy · skipjack tuna) = not vegetarian, must request "eomuk (어묵) · dashi (다시) excluded" / Hot dog (핫도그) · mandu (만두) · sundae (순대) · eomuk (어묵) = not vegetarian.

7. Payment · Tips: Cards OK at many places / Market bunsik = cash recommended / No tips.

8. VAT Refund: Food and beverages generally not eligible for refund.

⚠ Medical · Safety · Dietary Disclaimer

Many bunsik (분식) places are street stalls or market stores. For hygiene, directly check the turnover rate and cleanliness of the stall. For children, spicy tteokbokki (떡볶이) · rabokki (라볶이) are not recommended; non-spicy gimbap (김밥) · mandu (만두) · hot dogs (핫도그) are advised. Pregnant women should be cautious with saeujeot (새우젓, salted fermented shrimp) · dadaegi (다대기, spicy seasoning paste) · sundae (순대) (blood content). Declare allergies (seafood · gluten · soy · nuts) in advance. In case of suspected food poisoning, call ☎ 1339 (Medical, English). This guide provides general information and is not medical advice.

Dietary Restriction Fallback:

  • Halal (할랄) · Kosher (코셔): Tteokbokki (떡볶이) · gimbap (김밥) · mandu (만두) · hot dogs (핫도그) = not halal (seasoning · meat). → Some halal bunsik stores in Itaewon (이태원).
  • Vegetarian (채식) · Vegan (비건): Gimbap (김밥) (excluding tuna) · cheese gimbap (치즈 김밥) = possible for vegetarians. Tteokbokki (떡볶이) = possible without eomuk (어묵). → [#799 Foreigner Korean Vegetarian Vegan Matrix 12 Deep Dive](799_foreign_korean_vegetarian_vegan_matrix_12_deep_dive.md).
  • Gluten-free: Tteokbokki (떡볶이) seasoning · rabokki (라볶이) · mandu (만두) = high in gluten. Twigim (튀김) = flour · seafood must be confirmed in advance.
