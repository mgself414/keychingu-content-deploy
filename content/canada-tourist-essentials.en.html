Canada to Korea Travel Guide: K-ETA Exemption, 180 Days Visa-Free, Automated Immigration, Vancouver/Toronto Flights, Embassy (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: Temporary K-ETA exemption for Canadian passports through 2026-12-31 (Embassy of Korea in Canada notice) · visa-free for tourism and business for up to 180 days (Government of Canada, travel.gc.ca) · eligible for automated immigration clearance from 2026-03-16 (Ministry of Justice) · Korean Air sells Vancouver and Toronto–Incheon tickets (Korean Air official) · Embassy of Canada in Korea +82 2 3783 6000. 🎯 How to Use This Guide

In the Incheon Airport arrivals hall, someone holding a Canadian passport stands at the automated gates instead of the regular inspection line: since March 16, 2026, Canadian citizens are also eligible for automated immigration clearance (Ministry of Justice). This guide lists, using only values from official pages, four things people coming to Korea from Canada for a short trip should check before departure: entry documents (K-ETA, e-Arrival), immigration inspection (automated gates), flights, and money and contacts on the ground. Anything we could not confirm on official pages is stated as such. Long stays such as working holidays, study and employment are not covered in this edition.

🛂 Entry Documents: K-ETA and the e-Arrival Card

  • Temporary K-ETA exemption: it runs from January 1 to December 31, 2026 (K-ETA official notice). The notice has no list of eligible countries, but the Embassy of Korea in Canada announced that the "temporary exemption policy is extended for Canadian passport holders until December 31, 2026" (embassy notice, revised 2026-03-13). The Consulate General in Toronto's notice also says "Canada exempt" (2025-12-18). Even so, before departure, scan your passport on the k-eta.go.kr application screen and it will tell you whether you are "exempt"
  • Can I still apply if exempt? Yes. If you want benefits such as skipping the arrival form, you can apply for K-ETA, and a fee applies in that case (Embassy of Korea in Canada notice). Whether it will be extended into 2027 has not been announced yet
  • Visa-free length of stay: the Government of Canada says that for tourism or business you can stay up to 180 days without a visa (travel.gc.ca, updated 2026-10-15). The Consulate General in Toronto's post also says "up to 6 months" (revised 2024-09-11). After entering as a tourist, you cannot change your status of stay (travel.gc.ca)
  • Income-earning activity is separate: if your purpose involves paid activity, you must obtain a visa (Consulate General in Toronto). Study and work visas must be obtained outside Korea (travel.gc.ca)
  • e-Arrival Card: if you did not actually obtain a K-ETA, submit the e-Arrival Card. It is free online from 3 days before arrival, and you may also fill in a paper form on arrival. The Government of Canada warns "do not use sites that charge for the arrival card" (travel.gc.ca). For the steps, see #1289 e-Arrival Card manual

🛃 Immigration Inspection: Automated Gates

  • The Ministry of Justice started automated immigration clearance for foreigners with 4 countries and regions on 2025-11-03 and widened it twice, on 2025-12-01 and 2026-03-16. Canada was added on 2026-03-16, bringing the total to 42 countries and regions (Ministry of Justice posting, 2026-06-01)
  • Age conditions, eligible airports and automated departure clearance are compiled in #1412 automated clearance and Smart Pass manual
  • Although also in North America, the United States is not on this list of 42 countries (Ministry of Justice). Your line may differ from companions with US passports

✈ Flights

  • Korean Air: its official site has Vancouver (YVR)–Incheon and Toronto (YYZ)–Incheon fare pages, showing fares by date for departures from October 2026 into 2027 (Korean Air official). Whether the flights are nonstop and the weekly frequencies are not stated on these pages. A 2019 Korean Air Newsroom article introduced Incheon–Toronto as "7 times a week, daily nonstop", but that is too old a value to use as the current frequency
  • Air Canada: the official Seoul route page did not open. Flights from other cities such as Montreal and Calgary could not be confirmed either; search by date on a booking site
  • From Incheon Airport into central Seoul: #1249 Airport Railroad (AREX) guide

🥗 Dietary Needs

  • Vegetarian: many people think kimchi is vegetarian, but most kimchi seasoning contains salted fermented seafood, fish sauce or meat stock (VisitKorea). Tofu stew and braised tofu often contain salted shrimp too (VisitKorea)
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (Usadan-ro 10-gil 15 (우사단로10길 15), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00 to 21:00) as one of the few restaurants the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF, 한국이슬람교중앙회) recognizes as halal. Certification can change, so check before you visit. For more places, see #453 vegan and halal guide
  • Allergies and dietary requests: for a Korean-language show-the-server card, see #1300 dietary request manual

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "I'm K-ETA exempt, so I don't have to submit anything": if you did not obtain a K-ETA, you must still submit the e-Arrival Card. Online it is free (travel.gc.ca)

2. "The exemption will continue next year too": the exemption period announced now runs through 2026-12-31 (K-ETA notice). If you depart in 2027, check again

3. "Canada's eTA and Korea's K-ETA are the same thing": Canada's eTA is a permit needed to enter Canada (Ministry of Foreign Affairs overseas safety travel site). It is separate from entering Korea

4. "I'm visa-free, so I can work there": income-earning activity requires a visa (Consulate General in Toronto), and changing status of stay after entering as a tourist is not allowed (travel.gc.ca)
