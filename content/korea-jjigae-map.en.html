2. "Budae-jjigae = Korean-style only" — missing the #194 US-military-base fit: Korean-style only, they assume. In reality it is a Uijeongbu & Songtan US-military-base (#194 USA fit) fusion of ramyeon, sausage, and Spam = a Korean-American blend.
