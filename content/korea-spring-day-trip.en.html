Gapyeong and Yangpyeong Day Trip Guide: Nami Island, The Garden of Morning Calm, Petite France, Dumulmeori, Semiwon, Yangpyeong Sansuyu Village, Yongmunsa Ginkgo Tree

Subtitle: Nami Island (08:00~21:00) · The Garden of Morning Calm (08:30~19:00) · Petite France (09:00~18:00) · Dumulmeori (always open) · Semiwon (09:00~18:00, closed Mondays Nov~Mar) · Yangpyeong Sansuyu Village (in bloom Mar~Apr, always open) · Yongmunsa and its Ginkgo Tree (always open) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places you can visit in a day in Gapyeong and Yangpyeong, Gyeonggi-do, east of Seoul. They are three places in Gapyeong (the Nami Island ferry terminal, The Garden of Morning Calm and Petite France) and four in Yangpyeong (Dumulmeori, Semiwon, Sansuyu Village and Yongmunsa). Only Semiwon closes on Mondays from November to March, and the others are registered as open year-round.

In spring, Yangpyeong Sansuyu Village turns yellow in March~April.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Gapyeong — Nami Island, The Garden of Morning Calm and Petite France

Nami Island (남이섬) [Namiseom-gil 1 (남이섬길 1), Chuncheon, ☎ 031-580-8114] is an island with the concept 'Fairy Tale Land, Island of Songs' that holds cultural events, concerts and exhibitions. It belongs to Chuncheon in Gangwon-do, but to enter you go to the Nami Island ferry terminal in Gapyeong, Gyeonggi-do. It became famous as a filming location of the drama 'Winter Sonata' (겨울연가). It has a Song Museum, a picture book playground, a UNICEF lounge, bicycles, a sharing train and the Hotel Jeonggwanru (정관루).

  • Opening hours: 08:00~21:00. Ferry operating hours are run separately.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available (about 600 cars).
  • Experiences: Glass and ceramic crafts, wood-fired pizza making and more.

The Garden of Morning Calm (아침고요수목원) [Sumokwon-ro 432 (수목원로 432), Sangmyeon, Gapyeong-gun, ☎ 1544-6703] is a horticultural arboretum set against the backdrop of Chukryeongsan (축령산), and it opened in May 1996. It has 20 gardens with different themes, and the Sunken Garden (하경정원), laid out in the shape of the Korean Peninsula. It holds more than 5,000 kinds of plants, including more than 300 species from Baekdusan (백두산). In winter, it is introduced as holding the Five-Color Star Light Garden Festival (오색별빛정원전) with lighting. It is a filming location for several dramas and variety shows.

  • Opening hours: 08:30~19:00 (last entry 18:00). Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available. Experiences: Moss and flower terrariums, drawing pots and more.

Petite France (쁘띠프랑스) [Hoban-ro 1063 (호반로 1063), Cheongpyeong-myeon (청평면), Gapyeong-gun, ☎ 031-584-8200] is a French cultural village about 10km along the lakeside road from Cheongpyeong Dam (청평댐) toward Nami Island, with the concept 'Flowers, Stars and the Little Prince'. It has the Saint-Exupery Memorial Hall, the Music Box House where you can hear a 200-year-old music box, a French traditional house exhibition hall and an antiques exhibition hall. It is a filming location for several dramas.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. The closing time changes with the season and weather. Night opening hours are announced separately.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available. Experiences: Little Prince experience, plaster experience, sledding experience and more.

📍 Yangpyeong — Dumulmeori and Semiwon

Yangpyeong Dumulmeori (양평 두물머리) [Dumulmeori-gil 145 (두물머리길 145), Yangseo-myeon (양서면), Yangpyeong-gun, ☎ Dumulmeori Tourist Information Center (두물머리 관광안내소) 031-775-8700] is where the Bukhangang (북한강) and Namhangang (남한강) rivers merge, the start of the Hangang (한강). It has early morning mist and sunrise, yellow-sail boats and a zelkova tree more than 400 years old. It was chosen among the 100 Korean Tourism Sights, and it also survives in works by Gyeomjae Jeong Seon (겸재 정선) and others.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Semiwon (세미원) [Yangsu-ro 93 (양수로 93), Yangseo-myeon, Yangpyeong-gun, ☎ 031-775-1835] is a garden of water and flowers surrounded by Paldang Lake (팔당호) on three sides, with about 270 kinds of plants including aquatic plants. In June 2019 it was designated as Gyeonggi-do's first local garden. It is known as the spot where Jeong Yak-yong (정약용) built a boat bridge across the Namhangang. It has the Lotus Museum, the Water Lily Greenhouse, Seokchangwon (석창원) and Sangchunwon (상춘원).

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Tickets are sold until 30 minutes before closing. During the lotus festival, the hours are run separately.
  • Closed days: Open year-round from April to October, and every Monday from November to March (closed the next day instead if Monday is a public holiday).
  • Parking: Available. Experiences: Aquatic plant classroom, lotus fan making, natural handkerchief dyeing and more.

📍 Yangpyeong — Sansuyu Village and Yongmunsa

Yangpyeong Sansuyu Village (양평 산수유마을) [Sansuyu 1-gil 1 (산수유1길 1), Gaegun-myeon (개군면), Yangpyeong-gun, ☎ 031-772-2257] is home to about 7,000 Cornelian cherry trees more than 100 years old. Every March~April the village turns yellow, and in autumn the trees bear red fruit. Around early April each year it holds the Sansuyu Korean Beef Festival (산수유한우축제), themed on Cornelian cherry blossoms, with rural experiences. It is introduced as offering foods and tea made from Cornelian cherry fruit and blossoms, and native Korean beef, a specialty of Gaegun-myeon.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Yongmunsa (용문사) [Yongmunsan-ro 782 (용문산로 782), Yongmun-myeon (용문면), Yangpyeong-gun, ☎ 031-773-3797] is a temple of the Jogye Order of Korean Buddhism (대한불교조계종) at the foot of Yongmunsan (용문산), said to have been founded by Master Daegyeong (대경대사) in the 2nd year of King Sindeok of Silla (신라 신덕왕), 913. It has the Daeungjeon (대웅전) main hall, Sanryeonggak (산령각), Chilseonggak (칠성각) and a temple stay training hall, and the temple stay can be chosen from a rest type and an experience type. Going 2km up the valley from the temple brings you to Yonggak Rock (용각바위), and 1km further is Madang Rock (마당바위).

  • Yongmunsa Ginkgo Tree (용문사 은행나무) [☎ Yongmunsan Tourist Complex Information Office (용문산 관광단지 안내사무소) 031-773-0088]: A natural monument estimated to be about 1,100 years old and 42m tall. According to Korea Heritage Service material carried in the Korea Tourism Organization data, it holds the highest record for age and height among Korea's ginkgo trees. A legend says Crown Prince Maui (마의태자) planted it, and another says it grew from the walking staff of Master Uisang (의상대사).
  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available (about 700 cars). Light cars 1,500 won, small cars 3,000 won, mid-size and large cars 5,000 won.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Nami Island belongs to Chuncheon but you enter from the Gapyeong ferry terminal — It opens 08:00~21:00, and ferry operation is run separately.
  2. Semiwon closes on Mondays from November to March — From April to October it is open year-round.
  3. The Garden of Morning Calm stops entry at 18:00 — It is open 08:30~19:00.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where is a good place to see flowers in spring?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, Yangpyeong Sansuyu Village turns yellow with Cornelian cherry blossoms in March~April, and it is introduced as holding the Sansuyu Korean Beef Festival around early April. It is always open.

Q. Where do you enter Nami Island?

A. Nami Island belongs to Chuncheon, Gangwon-do, but you must go to the Nami Island ferry terminal in Gapyeong, Gyeonggi-do to enter. Ferry operating hours are run separately.
