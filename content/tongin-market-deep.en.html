Why Tongin Market Now — Yeopjeon Lunchbox Cafe Korea #1 Experience

Tongin Market is a Korea #1 Yeopjeon lunch box experience market located in Seochon, Jongno, Seoul (18 Jahamun-ro 15-gil). If the general market (#581 Namdaemun, #582 Plaza, #583 Dongdaemun) is a menu unit cluster — 1 coin = 500 won + store hopping + constructing your own lunch box = No. 1 experience for foreign enthusiasts. Clear differentiation from Gwangjang Market (#582·Netflix vlog)·Namdaemun (#581·authentic)·Dongdaemun (#583·fashion) = "Yeopjeon experience·Lunch box cafe·Seochon culture·MZ·family" cluster.

In particular, Oil Tteokbokki (Korea #1 Signature)·Chicken Skewers (generous toppings) + Yeopjeon Lunchbox Cafe (Weekdays 11-16, Weekends 11-17:00, Closed on Tuesdays and 3rd Sunday of the Week) = Introduction to Korean market culture on your first visit as a foreigner #1. This matrix is ​​3 lunch box cafes + 3 Yeopjeon stores + 3 Seochon cross + 3 main markets = 12 lines and 4 axes.

12-wire 4-axis matrix — Tongin full set

Axis 1: Lunchbox Cafe and Yeopjeon Experience (3rd Line) — Korea #1 Market Experience

#1. Tongin Market Lunchbox Cafe — Yeopjeon Experience Main Branch

  • 📍 Location: 18 Jahamun-ro 15-gil, Jongno-gu (Customer Satisfaction Center inside Tongin Market, 10-minute walk from Gyeongbokgung Station)
  • 🕐 Business: Weekdays 11:00-16:00 / Weekends 11:00-17:00
  • 🚫 Closed: Every Tuesday + 3rd Sunday (regular)
  • Yeopjeon: 1 piece = 500 won / purchase by 15:00 on weekdays and 16:00 on weekends
  • Experience: Rent a coin storage box and lunch box → Market store hopping → Create your own lunch box
  • Foreigner tip: Foreign Mania #1 Experience. Fits family, MZ, solo. A full lunch box set for 10,000 won.

#2. Tongin Market Oil Tteokbokki — Korea #1 Signature

  • 📍 Location: Tongin Market Snack Alley
  • 🕐 Business : 11:00-18:00 (by store)
  • Signature: Sweet and salty oil tteokbokki (soy sauce base + oil, Tongin Market limited to Korea #1)
  • Foreigner tip: 4-6 coins (2,000-3,000 won). Photo Mania·Insta Signature. Broadcast on SBS and KBS.

#3. Tongin Market chicken skewers and store hopping — plenty of toppings

  • 📍 Location: Tongin Market Snacks and Skewers Alley
  • 🕐 Open : 11:00-18:00
  • Signature: chicken skewers (generous toppings) + various tteokbokki, fried foods, kimbap, appetizers, and side dishes
  • Foreigner tip: If you have coins, 2-3 per store = cost-effective full set.

FAQ 6 — Tongin Market frequently asked questions by foreigners

Q1. How to visit Tongin Market for the first time?

1. Arrival at Lunchbox Cafe (#1) → Yeopjeon + lunch box rental

2. Market store hopping (#4·#5·#6) — Buy food with 1 Yeopjeon = 500 won

3. Return to the lunch box cafe → eat the lunch box

  • Foreigner's first visit: 10 coins (KRW 5,000) = Full lunch box OK

Q2. Tongin Market vs. other markets?

  • Tongin (#589): Yeopjeon lunch box experience + Seochon culture → MZ·Family·Experience Mania
  • Square (#582): Netflix vlog + Bindaetteok·Yukhoe → MZ·SNS
  • Namdaemun (#581): 1414 Authentic Korean food → JP·EN Authentic
  • Dongdaemun (#583): 24h fashion → CN·JP fashion
  • Recommended: First experience = Tongin (family) / vlog = Square / Authentic = Namdaemun / Fashion = Dongdaemun

Q3. English OK?

  • EN OK 1st place: #1 Lunchbox Cafe (visitseoul official English guide)
  • Variation in number of English speakers by store — translation app recommended

Q4. Price range?

  • Yeopjeon Lunch Box (Experience): KRW 5,000-10,000 per person (10-20 Yeopjeon)
  • Oil Tteokbokki: 2,500-4,000 won
  • chicken skewers: 2,000-3,000 won
  • Appetizers and side dishes: 1,500-3,000 won
  • Cash: Recommended KRW 10,000 per person (to be used after converting into Korean currency)

Q5. Closing/time plan?

  • Tuesday and third Sunday = Lunchbox Cafe closed (avoid visiting)
  • Recommended days: Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday + Sunday (excluding the third week)
  • Time: 12-14:00 lunch peak (waiting possible) · 11:00 or 14:30 recommended
