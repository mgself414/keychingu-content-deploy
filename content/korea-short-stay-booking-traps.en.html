Booking a Short-Term Stay in Korea, a Zero-Panic Manual — Refuse Off-App Bank Transfers, Pick a Registered Stay, and Keep Evidence When the Price Differs

Subtitle: Airbnb has not accepted bookings for Korean listings without a business registration certificate (yeongeopsingo-jeung, 영업신고증) since 2026-01-01 (officetels are removed) · A request to pay by bank transfer or paper invoice is likely a scam (Official: Airbnb and Booking.com) · From 2026-08-04, Foreign Tourist Urban Homestay (외국인관광 도시민박업) and Hanok Experience (한옥체험업) operators face business suspension for charging more than the posted price · Report damage to the Korean National Police Agency ECRM (Electronic Crime Reporting Management system, 4 languages) — as of 2026-10-13

🎯 The Question This Guide Answers

"My host wants me to send the deposit to a bank account instead of through the app," "My booking was suddenly canceled," "It's a different officetel (오피스텔) from the photos" — this question came up 4 times on Reddit in one month. Korean short-term stays have a rule that other countries do not have. Even a place that rents out a home must complete a business registration (yeongeopsingo, 영업신고), and since 2025-10-16 Airbnb has been filtering out Korean listings without a registration certificate. Knowing this rule helps you avoid most of the traps.

① Business Registration for Korean Stays (Yeongeopsingo, 영업신고) — Why It Matters

To rent out someone's home or room to travelers in Korea, the host must register as a lodging business. The legal types listed in the Airbnb Korea help center are Foreign Tourist Urban Homestay (외국인관광 도시민박업), Rural Homestay (농어촌민박업), Hanok Experience (한옥체험업), general lodging (ilban sukbakeop, 일반숙박업), residential lodging (saenghwal sukbakeop, 생활숙박업), hostel (hoseutelep, 호스텔업) and tourist hotel (gwanggwang hotelep, 관광호텔업).

  • Foreign Tourist Urban Homestay (외국인관광 도시민박업): A business in which a city resident provides lodging and meals in their own home so foreign tourists can experience Korean family culture (Seoul Metropolitan Government). Detached houses, apartments and similar are allowed, but officetels (오피스텔) and neighborhood commercial facilities (geunrin saenghwal siseol, 근린생활시설) cannot be designated. In principle it is a business type for foreign tourists (Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism)
  • Airbnb policy: Submitting registration information and the certificate is mandatory, from 2024-10-02 for new listings and from 2025-10-16 for existing ones. Listings that did not submit cannot take bookings with check-in on or after 2026-01-01, and officetels, which cannot be registered, were removed (Airbnb Newsroom). This is a policy Airbnb set itself, not a legal obligation

By industry estimates, of about 72,400 Airbnb listings in Korea at the end of 2024, only about 7,200 were legal (News1, 2025-09 [Secondary]). That is why listings disappeared in large numbers in 2025-2026, and many travelers had a booking canceled or reversed after reserving.

What travelers can do

  • Avoid a place if officetel (오피스텔) appears in the description or photos
  • If something seems off, ask the host for a copy of the registration certificate (yeongeopsingo-jeung, 영업신고증). A legitimate listing has already submitted these documents to Airbnb
  • In Seoul, see the Foreign Tourist Urban Homestay guide (Seoul Culture portal). We could not confirm an official way to look up a registration number from a listing page this time

② Requests to Pay Outside the App — Always Refuse

"If you have been asked to pay by bank transfer, cashier's check or money order, or you were given a PDF or paper invoice, it is very likely a scam." — Airbnb Korea help center

Booking.com also says it "does not ask for transfers to an account different from the payment policy in your booking confirmation," and tells guests not to send payments that are not in the policy.

  • Common tactics: "Send the ₩300,000 (~$225) deposit to my account first," "I'll give you a discount if we do it outside the app," "The booking got tangled, so send the money again" (Reddit cases, 2026-09 to 10)
  • Money sent outside the app is hard to get back or protect through the platform
  • If the host asks to move to an off-app contact (KakaoTalk, WhatsApp), keep the conversation inside the app too

③ When the Price Differs — What Changed on 2026-08-04

Under an amendment to the Enforcement Decree of the Tourism Promotion Act (approved by the Cabinet on 2026-07-28, effective 2026-08-04), Foreign Tourist Urban Homestay (외국인관광 도시민박업) operators must also display prices at the premises and on online booking screens, and charging more than the posted price is now subject to penalties. For Hanok Experience (한옥체험업), the penalty for a violation was raised from a corrective order to business suspension (Ministry of Economy and Finance press release). The government explains this is not a price cap but a "ban on charging above the posted price."

  • Take a screenshot of the booking confirmation screen and receipt
  • If asked for an extra charge on site, compare it with the posted price, record it, and notify the platform customer center first
  • The penalty steps (such as a 5-day suspension for a first offense) are confirmed only by news reports [Secondary]

⚠ 5 Traps That Actually Catch People

1. "Just send the deposit to my account first" — A transfer outside the app is outside platform protection.

2. A cheap officetel (오피스텔) listing — It cannot be registered, so it is a removal target on Airbnb.

3. A sudden cancellation after booking — Listings can vanish because of the certificate policy. Recheck your booking status 1-2 weeks before departure.

4. An extra charge on site — Charging above the posted price is subject to penalties (Urban Homestay and Hanok Experience). Keep evidence.

5. Mistaking a business license for a registration certificate — A business license (saeopja deungnokjeung, 사업자등록증) and a lodging business registration certificate (yeongeopsingo-jeung, 영업신고증) are different documents [Secondary].

⚙ FAQ 3

1. Can I stay with a Korean friend? — In principle, Foreign Tourist Urban Homestay (외국인관광 도시민박업) is a business type for foreign tourists (Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism). If you plan to stay together, check with the host beforehand. Hanok Experience (한옥체험업) and Rural Homestay (농어촌민박업) can also be used by Koreans [Secondary].

2. Is it the same for a "villa" bought on Booking or Agoda? — The ban on paying outside the platform is the same in Booking.com's official guidance. We could not confirm Agoda's policy this time.

3. What should I do at the stay on my first day? — For shoes, the boiler and trash, see #1380.
