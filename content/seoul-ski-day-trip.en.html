Seoul Day-Trip Ski Guide: Gonjiam, Vivaldi Park, Elysian Gangchon, Jisan, Oak Valley, 2026-27 Season Fare Announcement Status and How to Get There

Subtitle: Gonjiam (timed lift, weekday 2 hours from 69,000 won, 2026-27 official) · Oak Valley (lift single 66,000 won, combo 80,000 won, 2026-27 official) · Vivaldi Park (foreigner-only Seoul shuttle 2026-12-05~2027-03-01) · Elysian Gangchon (Gyeongchun Line Baegyangni Station) · Jisan (Icheon) — based on the official pages of the resorts and the shuttle operator as of November 20, 2026

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide is for ski day trips that leave Seoul in the morning and return in the evening, and it gathers five ski resorts. They are Gonjiam Resort (곤지암리조트) in Gwangju, Gyeonggi, Vivaldi Park (비발디파크) in Hongcheon, Elysian Gangchon (엘리시안 강촌) in Chuncheon, Jisan Resort (지산리조트) in Icheon and Oak Valley (오크밸리) in Wonju.

As of November 20, 2026, the resorts that have posted 2026-27 season lift fares on their official pages with a season label are two, Gonjiam and Oak Valley. Vivaldi Park has posted a fare table, but the table has no season label. Elysian Gangchon has not yet announced 2026-27 fares, so the 2025-26 values are listed for reference, and Jisan could not list fares because its fare page is being renewed. None of the five has yet announced a confirmed opening date. Gonjiam says only "sometime in December" for its opening.

Gangwon ski resorts such as Pyeongchang are in the #412 Pyeongchang Travel Guide and the #424 Korea Winter Travel Guide.

🎿 Gonjiam Resort — The Resort That Announced 2026-27 Fares

Gonjiam Resort (곤지암리조트) (Gwangju, Gyeonggi) is introduced in the resort guide as "40 minutes from Seoul". It announced the 2026-27 season opening as "sometime in December" and the closing as "early March", and said the opening date will be announced on the official website after snowmaking and safety checks are finished. Snowmaking began on November 18.

  • Lift system: Timed 2, 4 and 6 hour tickets.
  • Lift regular price (adult): Weekday 2 hours 69,000 won, 4 hours 81,000 won, 6 hours 87,000 won. Weekend 2 hours 83,000 won, 4 hours 96,000 won, 6 hours 105,000 won.
  • Lift plus equipment rental regular price (adult): Weekday 105,000 won, 117,000 won, 123,000 won; weekend 122,000 won, 135,000 won, 144,000 won (in the order of 2, 4 and 6 hours).
  • Early bird: On sale on NOL until December 6. Lift weekday 44,000 won, 52,000 won, 56,000 won; weekend 54,000 won, 62,000 won, 68,000 won (in the order of 2, 4 and 6 hours).
  • Weekday late-night ticket NIGHT OWL: 30,000 won. Valid Mon~Thu 22:00 to 01:00 the next day, from Monday, December 28, 2026 to Thursday, January 14, 2027. On sale on NOL until December 3, and it cannot be used Fri~Sun, on public holidays or on the day before a public holiday.
  • Early morning: Opens at 07:00 on peak-season weekends and 08:00 on weekdays.
  • Foreigner discount: There is no announced foreigner-only discount.

🎿 Oak Valley — The Resort That Posted a 2026-27 Fare Table With a Season Label

Oak Valley (오크밸리) (Wonju, Gangwon) changed the header of its fare table to "26/27 season" when it posted it. There is no opening day announcement yet, and it is written that night operation and the discounts for members, hotel guests and local residents apply from Friday, December 11, 2026.

  • Lift (adult): Single ticket 66,000 won (one of morning 09~13, afternoon 13~17, or night 18:30~22:00), combo ticket 80,000 won (day 09~17, or afternoon plus night 13~22).
  • Equipment rental: Single 28,000 won, combo 35,000 won.
  • Card discount: The fare table lists partner discounts, including 40% off on weekdays and 30% off on weekends when paying with NH Nonghyup (NH농협), Shinhan (신한) or Toss Face Pay (토스 페이스페이).
  • How to get there: In the 2025-26 season, a free shuttle (weekends only) ran between KTX Wonju Station (원주역), Wonju Terminal (원주터미널), Manjong Station (만종역) and the resort, and there was no shuttle going directly from Seoul. There is no 2026-27 shuttle announcement yet.
  • Foreigner discount: None (only member, hotel guest, local resident, preferential and partner card discounts).

🎿 Vivaldi Park — The Resort With a Foreigner-Only Seoul Shuttle

Vivaldi Park (비발디파크) (Hongcheon, Gangwon) changed the operating period in its usage guide to "Open (TBD) ~ 2027.3.1". It also posted lift and rental fare tables, but the tables have no season label.

  • Lift (adult, price posted on November 20, 2026): Weekday 5 hours 75,000 won, 6 hours 79,000 won, 7 hours 83,000 won. Weekend and public holiday 5 hours 91,000 won, 6 hours 95,000 won, 7 hours 99,000 won.
  • Equipment rental (adult, same basis): Weekday 6 hours 34,000 won, 7 hours 36,000 won, 8 hours 38,000 won. Weekend 43,000 won, 45,000 won, 47,000 won.
  • Helmet: Required. Without one, boarding the lift and gondola is restricted. The rental fee (same basis) is 6 hours 5,000 won, 7 hours 6,000 won, 8 hours 7,000 won.
  • Operating hours: 09:00~17:30, night until 23:00, late night until 03:00 the next day (late night is closed on Mondays, but open on February 8, 2027).
  • Foreigner-only Seoul shuttle (vivabus): The operator stated the 2026-27 season operating period as December 5, 2026 to March 1, 2027, and reservations for this period opened on November 16. If the opening date is earlier than that, it announced that it will open reservations for the period from the opening date to December 4 after announcing the opening date.
  • Seoul to resort: Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역) 06:30 → Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (동대문역사문화공원역) 07:00 → Konkuk Univ. Station (건대입구역) 07:20. A separate route leaves Sports Complex Station (종합운동장역) at 07:20 (opening~3/1). On 12/5~2/21 there are also trips from Hongik Univ. Station 09:00 → Konkuk Univ. Station 10:00 and Sports Complex Station 10:30. The timetable has no season label.
  • Resort to Seoul: Departs 17:00 (to Konkuk Univ., Dongdaemun and Hongik Univ., and to Sports Complex). There is also a 13:00 departure to Sports Complex (opening~3/1) and to Hongik Univ. (12/25~2/6).
  • Fare (no season label on the vivabus page): Adult one way 10,000 won, round trip 20,000 won; child under 13 one way 8,000 won, round trip 16,000 won. This is a foreigner-only fare. It does not run if there are no passengers.
  • Cancellation: Cancel before 16:00 (Korea time) the day before for a 100% refund; no refund after that, including no-shows.

🎿 Elysian Gangchon — The Resort You Reach by Train (2026-27 Fares Not Announced)

Elysian Gangchon (엘리시안 강촌) (Chuncheon, Gangwon) has the Gyeongchun Line Baegyangni Station (백양리역) inside the resort, and introduces itself as a little over 1 hour by ITX-Cheongchun. As of November 20, there is no announcement of 2026-27 fares or an opening date. Below are the 2025-26 values that remain on the official page.

  • 2025-26 operating period: December 5, 2025 to March 8, 2026.
  • 2025-26 lift (adult, on-site weekday/weekend): 2 hours 62,000 won/75,000 won, 4 hours 72,000 won/85,000 won, 6 hours 82,000 won/95,000 won.
  • 2025-26 rental (adult): Equipment 4 hours 33,000 won, 7 hours 38,000 won; helmet 5,000 won.
  • 2025-26 discount: ITX-Cheongchun riders get 40% off lift and 50% off rental.
  • Foreigners: There is an English booking page (GLOBAL BOOKING). There is no foreigner-only discount.

🎿 Jisan Resort — Icheon, Reached by Bus (2026-27 Fares Not Announced)

Jisan Resort (지산리조트) (Icheon, Gyeonggi) began selling 2026-27 early bird lift tickets on Lotte ON (롯데온) on November 5, but the announcement gives no amount, and the lift and rental fare pages say "homepage being renewed" as of November 20. The early bird lift ticket can be used from after the opening event ends until Sunday, February 28, 2027.

  • Operating hours: Day 09:00~17:00, night 18:30~24:00, late night 24:00~02:00.
  • Public transport: Take an intercity bus to Icheon Terminal (이천터미널) from Dong Seoul Terminal (동서울터미널, about 1 hour 20 minutes) or Gangnam Terminal (강남터미널, about 1 hour), then take bus 12 from Icheon Terminal to Jisan Mart Entrance (지산마트입구) (about 40 minutes) and walk 15 minutes.
  • Foreigners: There is no official foreigner guidance or discount.

⚠ 3 Things to Know in Advance

  1. None of the five has yet announced a confirmed opening date — Gonjiam says "sometime in December", Vivaldi Park says "Open (TBD)", and Oak Valley wrote that night operation starts on December 11. The announced operating period for the 2025-26 season at Elysian Gangchon was December 5, 2025 to March 8, 2026.
  2. The only resort whose 2026-27 Seoul shuttle operating period can be confirmed on an official page is the Vivaldi Park foreigner-only shuttle — the Seoul shuttle pages of Gonjiam, Jisan and Elysian either still show last season's guidance or require a login. For Elysian there is the Gyeongchun Line, for Jisan an intercity bus to Icheon, and for Oak Valley there was a free shuttle (weekends only) between KTX Wonju Station and the resort in the 2025-26 season.
  3. Some resorts make helmets mandatory — at Vivaldi Park, boarding the lift and gondola is restricted without a helmet. Elysian Gangchon made wearing one mandatory from January 1, 2026.

⚙ FAQ 3

Q. Which ski resorts have a foreigner discount?

A. Gonjiam, Oak Valley, Elysian Gangchon and Jisan have no official resort foreigner-only discount. Vivaldi Park has not posted a foreigner discount. The Vivaldi Park foreigner shuttle is a foreigner-only fare (one way 10,000 won, no season label on the vivabus page).

Q. What is the lift fare for a short session on 2026-27 official fares?

A. This is based on Gonjiam and Oak Valley, which have 2026-27 official fares. The Gonjiam weekday 2 hour ticket is 69,000 won and the early bird is 44,000 won (on sale on NOL until December 6). The weekday late-night ticket NIGHT OWL is 30,000 won, valid 12/28~1/14 Mon~Thu 22:00 to 01:00 the next day, and 300 are sold per use date until December 3. The Oak Valley single ticket is 66,000 won and is a 4 hour ticket (night: 3.5 hours on weekdays, 5 hours on weekends). Vivaldi Park, Elysian Gangchon and Jisan have no fares with a 2026-27 season label, so they were left out of the comparison.

Q. Can I go without my own equipment?

A. Gonjiam, Oak Valley, Vivaldi Park and Elysian Gangchon have equipment rental. Gonjiam announced a bundled lift plus equipment fare, Oak Valley announced 2026-27 rental-only fares, and Vivaldi Park announced rental-only fares with no season label. Elysian Gangchon is based on the 2025-26 fare table.
