🎫 Fares — Your Child's Age Is the Fare

Seoul subway (Seoul Metro fare guide)

  • Toddlers: free under 6 (full age). Up to 3 children per guardian; from the 4th child on, each pays the child fare
  • Children: 6 and over but under 13 (elementary school age). The base fare is ₩550, the same for a transit card and a single-journey card
  • Teenagers: 13 to 18. The transit-card base fare is ₩900. A single-journey card is charged the adult fare (₩1,650)
  • Adults: transit-card base fare ₩1,550 (up to 10km)
  • To get the discount: child and teen fares apply only after you buy a transit card and register it as a child or teen card (Seoul city transport guide)

Korail trains (including KTX) (Korail FAQ)

  • Under 6: can ride free. If you reserve a seat of their own, there is a 75% discount on the ticket price
  • Under 13: 50% discount on the ticket price
  • Korail also lists multi-child and "Mom-Comfortable KTX" discount products, but we could not confirm whether foreign travelers can use them

Gyeongbokgung (경복궁): ₩3,000 for foreign adults (full age 19~64) (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center, 궁능유적본부). We could not confirm the child fare in the fee table we opened this time

🧸 Places for Kids — 5 Spots

  • National Museum of Korea Children's Museum (국립중앙박물관 어린이박물관) — 137 Seobinggo-ro, Yongsan-gu, Seoul (National Museum of Korea). 09:30~17:20, 5 sessions a day (80 minutes each), free admission. Online reservation only: slots open at 00:00, 14 days before the visit date, up to 5 tickets at a time. No on-site tickets. The exhibition rooms are designed mainly for ages 5~9, and the "Degulgugul Playground" (데굴데굴놀이터) is a space for infants and toddlers up to 36 months. Closed on January 1, Seollal (Lunar New Year) and Chuseok; the remaining closed day in 2026 is Monday, December 7 (National Museum of Korea Children's Museum website). Inquiries 02-2077-9647
  • National Folk Museum of Korea Children's Museum (국립민속박물관 어린이박물관) — 37 Samcheong-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul, inside the National Folk Museum of Korea (National Folk Museum of Korea). 09:30~16:50, January to December, free admission, reserve on the website in advance. Closed on January 1, on Seollal day and on Chuseok day. There is no parking lot at the museum, so use the (paid) Gyeongbokgung parking lot. About a 15-minute walk from Exit 1 of Anguk Station (안국역) on Subway Line 3 (National Folk Museum of Korea website)
  • Seoul Children's Museum Sangsangnara (서울상상나라) — 216 Neungdong-ro, Gwangjin-gu, Seoul, next to the main-gate parking lot of Seoul Children's Grand Park (Sangsangnara). 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:00), closed every Monday, also closed on January 1 and during the Seollal and Chuseok holidays. Children 36 months and over and adults are all ₩4,000; free under 36 months (verified with a resident registration copy, health insurance card or baby health handbook). 5 minutes on foot from Exit 1 of Children's Grand Park Station (어린이대공원역) on Subway Line 7 (Sangsangnara website)
  • Seoul Children's Grand Park (서울어린이대공원) — 216 Neungdong-ro, Gwangjin-gu, Seoul (Seoul Facilities Corporation, 서울시설공단). The park is open all year, 05:00~22:00, free admission. The zoo is open 10:00~17:00. The botanical garden is closed from May 27, 2026 until the second half of 2027 (planned) (Seoul Facilities Corporation, Children's Grand Park website). It pairs easily with Sangsangnara in one trip
  • National Gwacheon Science Museum (국립과천과학관) — Gwacheon, Gyeonggi. Permanent exhibition: adults (20~64) ₩4,000, teenagers and children (7~19) ₩2,000, free under 7. The permanent exhibition halls need no advance reservation, and on the last Wednesday of every month individual fares are 50% off (National Gwacheon Science Museum fee guide). We could not confirm opening hours and closed days because the official page is drawn only by script — inquiries 02-3677-1500

For more rainy-day options, see Rainy-Day Seoul with Kids: 12 Best Indoor Family Spots, and for theme parks, Korea Theme Park Foreigner Full Guide.

🍼 Strollers and Nursing Rooms — Locations the Facilities Announced

  • National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관): the nursing room is on the 1st floor of the permanent exhibition building, across from the gift shop; the stroller rental desk is on the 1st floor between the permanent exhibition entrance and the lockers; diaper-changing tables are in every restroom. The Children's Museum entrance also has a calming room, "Maeum Boduemso" (마음보듬소), for children overwhelmed by sensory stimulation (National Museum of Korea accessibility guide)
  • Seoul Children's Museum Sangsangnara (서울상상나라): you can leave your own stroller behind the 1st-floor ticket counter, but strollers are not rented out. Nursing rooms are at the center of the 2nd floor (inside the baby playground) and the center of the 3rd floor. In the 3rd-floor Family Lounge (10:00~17:00) you can eat snacks and lunch boxes you brought, but no hot water is provided and instant noodles and delivery food are not allowed (Sangsangnara facilities guide)
  • Gyeongbokgung (경복궁): the facilities list in the Royal Palaces and Tombs Center visitor guide separately marks a stroller rental desk and a nursing room, and it also describes "paths wheelchairs and strollers can take." We could not confirm the rental conditions

🧰 Practical Tips with Kids — Booking, Payment, Meals, Getting Around, When Sick

  • Booking: the National Museum of Korea Children's Museum opens at 00:00, 14 days ahead, and the reservation is cancelled automatically if you have not entered within 30 minutes after your session starts. If you skip without cancelling, your visits are restricted for 60 days (National Museum of Korea reservation guide). The National Folk Museum of Korea Children's Museum also requires a website reservation first

Sangsangnara has an infirmary on the 2nd floor for simple first aid (Sangsangnara)

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Let's do all the children's museums on a Monday" — Sangsangnara is closed every Monday (Sangsangnara). The National Museum of Korea Children's Museum is also closed on Monday, December 7, 2026 (National Museum of Korea)

2. "We'll just buy tickets at the National Museum of Korea Children's Museum when we get there" — it is online reservation only, with no on-site tickets (National Museum of Korea)

3. "The child fare is the same everywhere up to age twelve" — on the subway a child is under 13, while the National Gwacheon Science Museum puts ages 7~19 into one fare. The cutoff differs by facility (Seoul Metro · National Gwacheon Science Museum)

4. "We'll rent a stroller at Sangsangnara" — it offers storage only, no rental (Sangsangnara)
