  • Signature: Korea #1 Beach 1.5km·10 million+ visits per year·Gwangan Bridge Night View Matrix #1

3. "Gamcheon = Simple Village": Santorini's Busan·#1 Instagram·Pastel Matrix·1950~.

4. "Haedong Yonggungsa Temple = Just a Temple": Korea #1 Marine Temple 1376·New Year's Sunrise Mania.
