Korean Jjim 6-Type Matrix — The Full Set: Dakjjim, Galbijjim, Agujjim, Haemuljjim, Megijjim & Jjimdak

Subtitle: Spicy jjim for foreign tourists + hanjeongsik tradition + MZ trends

ISSUE 03 · #894 · 2026-06-09

🎯 Why a Korean Jjim Matrix

The biggest pain point foreign visitors have with Korean jjim (찜) = "I only know jjimdak — I don't know the other jjim dishes." Korean jjim = hanjeongsik tradition + spicy-food fandom + MZ trends. Even under the same label "Korean steamed/braised dish," you have dakjjim (닭찜) (Andong jjimdak — the traditional #1), galbijjim (갈비찜) (hanjeongsik · royal-court tradition), agujjim (아구찜) (spice #1 · MZ Instagram favorite), haemuljjim (해물찜) (cross-over with the raw-fish cluster), megijjim (메기찜) (Jeolla tradition), and donggeurangttaeng-jjim (동그랑땡찜) (holiday tradition). Foreign visitors typically don't know that (a) Andong jjimdak (안동찜닭) = the nationwide #1 fan favorite, (b) galbijjim = hanjeongsik tradition, (c) agujjim = the #1 spicy dish, and (d) megijjim = Jeolla tradition. This guide covers the 6-type matrix + tradition + the spicy-fan angle.

🥘 Deep Dive into the 6 Types

🐔 Andong jjimdak (안동찜닭) — Tradition #1

  • Signature: Andong tradition #1 · soy-spicy · glass noodles · nationwide #1
  • Ingredients: chicken + soy-spicy marinade + glass noodles, potato, onion, carrot
  • Recommended spots: Andong traditional restaurants · nationwide Andong jjimdak cluster · MZ Korean restaurants
  • Price: ₩25,000~50,000 (~$19–38) per gwan (관, a large sharing platter for 2–3 people)
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ (#1 traditional)

🥩 Galbijjim (갈비찜) — Hanjeongsik #1

  • Signature: Hanjeongsik tradition · royal court · soy marinade
  • Ingredients: beef short ribs + soy sauce, Korean pear, carrot, shiitake mushroom, pine nuts
  • Recommended spots: hanjeongsik (한정식) restaurants ([#837](#837)) · La Yeon at The Shilla Hotel · Samcheonggak
  • Price: hanjeongsik course ₩40,000~150,000 (~$30–113) (galbijjim included) / a la carte ₩30,000~60,000 (~$23–45)
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ (hanjeongsik #1)

🐟 Agujjim (아구찜) — Spice #1

  • Signature: Spice #1 · MZ Instagram · bean sprouts
  • Ingredients: agu (monkfish) + bean sprouts · spicy marinade
  • Recommended spots: Masan traditional restaurants · Seoul old-school eateries · MZ Korean restaurants
  • Price: ₩25,000~50,000 (~$19–38) per gwan (2–3 people)
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★ (spice fans)

🦀 Haemuljjim (해물찜) — Seafood

  • Signature: seafood cross-over · spicy
  • Ingredients: squid, shrimp, clams, bean sprouts + spicy marinade
  • Recommended spots: Jagalchi · Tongyeong · seafood specialty restaurants
  • Price: ₩30,000~80,000 (~$23–60) per gwan (2–3 people)
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★

🐠 Megijjim (메기찜) — Jeolla Tradition

  • Signature: Jeolla tradition · spicy · old-school eateries
  • Ingredients: megi (catfish) + dried radish greens · spicy marinade
  • Recommended spots: Jeolla old-school eateries · Gangwon traditional restaurants
  • Price: ₩20,000~40,000 (~$15–30) per gwan
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★

🥩 LA galbijjim (LA갈비찜) (LA-style) — Global

  • Signature: global · MZ trend · American style
  • Ingredients: LA-cut short ribs (thin-sliced) + soy sauce and Korean-pear marinade
  • Recommended spots: MZ Korean restaurants · global-friendly restaurants
  • Price: ₩25,000~50,000 (~$19–38) per gwan
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★ (global)

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. "Jjim = one single dish": jjim = a 6-type matrix.

2. "Andong jjimdak = generic": Andong jjimdak = the nationwide traditional #1 · soy-spicy.

3. "Galbijjim = just expensive": galbijjim = hanjeongsik tradition · royal-court cluster.

4. "Agujjim = mild": agujjim = spice #1 (one of the spiciest dishes in Korea).

5. "Megijjim = generic": megijjim = Jeolla tradition · old-school eateries.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. Top pick for first-time foreign visitors: Andong jjimdak + galbijjim.

2. Instagram · vlog: agujjim (spice #1) · Andong jjimdak.

3. MZ trend: agujjim · LA galbijjim.

4. Spice fans: agujjim (#1).

5. Hanjeongsik tradition: galbijjim.

6. Allergies: declare seafood and soy-sauce gluten allergies in advance.

7. Payment · tipping: cards OK / no tipping.

8. VAT refund: food and beverages generally not refundable.

⚠ Medical · Safety · Dietary Disclaimer

Jjim = ordinary food · some dishes are spicy. Pregnant women · children = OK (adjust spice level). Declare allergies (seafood · soy-sauce gluten) in advance. If you suspect food poisoning, call ☎ 1339.

Dietary restriction fallback: Halal · kosher = chicken, beef, seafood OK · no pork content / vegetarian · vegan = not vegetarian-friendly / gluten-free = Tamari.
