🎯 Why is Guro·Gasan Digital an exclusive guide?

⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Guro·Gasan = industrial complex, so there is nothing to see" — The value of Korea's No. 1 industrialization site is not recognized

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. Guro and Gasan are recognized only as “IT industrial complexes” and passed, but in reality, Korea’s No. 1 industrialization deep dive — Guro Digital Complex (Korea’s first export industrial complex in 1965) + Gasan Digital Complex (1990s clothing and IT industrial complex) = Deep dive into the history of Korea’s industrialization in the 60s and 90s. The first place to experience the Korean economic development model. No. 1 among foreigners interested in business and history.

2. "Outlet = In Gangnam·Myeongdong" — Gasan Mario Outlet Miji

Foreigners only recognize the outlet as "Gangnam Shinsegae Lotte", but Gasan Mario Outlet (opened in 1995, Korea's No. 1 fashion outlet) + Lotte Factory Outlet Gasan + W Mall Gasan are Korea's largest fashion outlet hot zones. 30-70% of Gangnam Department Store price (wholesale/outlet price) + Instant duty-free refund for foreigners Standard. No. 1 shopping deep dive for foreigners (Gangnam, Myeongdong, etc.).

3. "Foreigners living = Itaewon/Seorae Bay" — Guro/Gasan foreigner demographic unknown

Although foreign residential areas are recognized only as "Itaewon and Seorae", Guro and Gasan are the No. 1 residential areas for foreigners in Korea, Southeast Asia, Russia and Central Asia (Garibong-dong, Guro-dong, etc.). No. 1 demographic for Korean foreign workers and immigrants + Abundance of multicultural food, groceries, and religious facilities. Foreign anthropology deep dive #1.

Guro·Gasan Digital Landscape

Guro-Gasan Digital is located on the border of Guro-gu and Geumcheon-gu A combination area of Korean industrialization, foreigners' residence, and outlets — Establishment of Guro Industrial Complex (Korea's first export industrial complex) in 1965 + Gasan Digital Complex in 1990s + Mario Outlet in 1995 + Garibong-dong in 2000s With the rise of foreign residents, it established itself as Korea's No. 1 industrial and foreigner residence.

Guro Digital Complex sub-area is near Guro Digital Complex Station on Line 2 — the birthplace of Korea's industrialization + IT and software companies moving in + a hot zone for lunch and dining for Korea's first generation office workers.

Gasan Digital Complex sub-area is near Gasan Digital Complex Station on Lines 1 and 7 — IT, clothing, and fashion industrial complex + Mario Outlet, Lotte Factory Outlet, W Mall Outlet belt + foreign workers and immigrants.

Sindorim sub-area is directly connected to Sindorim Station on Lines 1 and 2 — No. 1 transfer station in Korea (Seoul → Incheon, Gyeonggi, Bucheon transfer) + D Cube City, Techno Mart shopping mall.

Doksan·Geumcheon sub-area is Doksan Station/Geumcheon-gu Office Station on Line 1 — an authentic Korean neighborhood + expansion of foreign residences near Gasan.

Transportation: Line 1, Line 2 Sindorim Station (Transfer No. 1) / Line 2 Guro Digital Complex Station / Line 1, Line 7 Gasan Digital Complex Station / Line 1 Doksan Station·Geumcheon-gu Office Station. Kakao Taxi recommended.

⚙ FAQ (8)

Q1. How to get to Mario Outlet/tax free?

5 minutes walk from Gasan Digital Complex Station on Lines 1 and 7. Time 10:30~21:00. Instant tax-free refund for foreigners Standard (over 30,000 won + passport). Gangnam Department Store 30-70% price. Seasonal sales every month.

Q2. Foreigners admitted to Guro Digital Complex?

Free entry to industrial complex streets — Free walking for foreigners. The birthplace of Korean industrialization in the 1960s + a building occupied by IT and software companies. Weekday lunchtime office workers are lively.

Q3. Is Garibong-dong foreign residential area safe?

24-hour safety — Korean plainclothes police + CCTV + foreigner-friendly neighborhood. No. 1 residence for Korean Chinese, Chinese, Southeast Asian, and Russian foreigners. No. 1 foreign anthropology/multicultural deep dive. Abundant foreign food (Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai, Uzbek, etc.).

Q4. What are the foreigner-friendly stores in Guro and Gasan?

Noted Gasan Public, Katsu Ology, Baek So-jeong, Mario Outlet English menu + card payment + foreigner-friendly standard. Noted has a variety of foreign demographics, so some multilingual menus are OK.

Q5. Sindorim D Cube/Techno Mart shopping time?

D Cube City 10:30~22:00 / Techno Mart 10:00~20:30. Directly connected to Sindorim Station on Lines 1 and 2. Foreign IT/electronics wholesale/retail + transfer combination.

Q6. Gasan·Guro + Gangnam·Hongdae route?

Gasan Digital (Line 1, Line 7) → Gangnam (50 minutes by subway) / Hongdae (40 minutes by subway). Sindorim Station (Lines 1 and 2) = Korea's No. 1 transfer station, available for transfer to Gangnam, Hongdae, and Incheon Airports. 1 full day.

Q7. Gasan Outlet + Gangnam Duty Free Difference?

Gasan Outlet: Korean clothing outlet price 30~70% / Mario·Lotte Factory·W Mall = Wholesale·Retail / Duty free refund for foreigners OK. Gangnam Duty Free: Shinsegae, Lotte, and Hyundai Department Store regular price + duty free. Gasan is cost-effective and wholesale, while Gangnam is luxury and regular price.

Q8. Guro·Gasan night safety?

24-hour safety — Korean IT industrial complex + foreign residents + transfer station for lines 1 and 2 = CCTV and plainclothes police standards. Nighttime OK for foreigners. Kakao Taxi recommended after midnight.
