Guro·Gasan Digital Guide — 12 Picks for Foreign Tourists + 4 Industrial Complex & Shopping Spots

Subtitle: Korean Food & BBQ + Bakery & Cafe + Global Dining + Industrial Complex & Outlets — 12 Picks in Guro (구로) & Geumcheon (금천) Across 4 Themes + A Deep Dive into Korea's IT Industrial Hub & Primary Foreigner Residential Area

ISSUE 03 · #180 · 2026-05-18

🎯 Why a Dedicated Guro·Gasan Digital Guide?

Guro·Gasan Digital (구로·가산디지털) is the No. 1 hub for Korea's IT industrial complex, foreign residents, and outlets in Guro-gu (구로구) and Geumcheon-gu (금천구). This area compacts the Guro Digital Complex (구로디지털단지) (Korea's first export industrial complex in 1965, the birthplace of Korea's industrialization) + Gasan Digital Complex (가산디지털단지) (1990s apparel & IT industrial complex, primary residence for foreign workers) + Mario Outlet (마리오 아울렛) & Lotte Factory Outlet (롯데팩토리아울렛) (Gasan (가산) fashion outlets) + Sindorim D-Cube City (신도림 디큐브시티) & Technomart (테크노마트) (Korea's top transfer station shopping). It offers a deep dive into Korea's industrialization and is the No. 1 area for diverse foreign demographics.

Compared to Hongdae (홍대) (MZ (Millennials and Gen Z) & indie culture), Gangnam (강남) (luxury), and Itaewon (이태원) (global), Guro·Gasan (구로·가산) is characterized by industry, foreign residency, and outlets. While often overlooked by foreign tourists in favor of Gangnam (강남) or Hongdae (홍대), it's a top priority for those interested in foreign residency, shopping, and deep dives: it's the No. 1 residential area for Korean-Chinese, Southeast Asian, and Russian foreign workers, home to Mario Outlet (마리오 아울렛), Korea's largest fashion outlet, and offers a deep dive into Korean IT shopping at Sindorim Technomart (신도림 테크노마트).

Especially, Mario Outlet (마리오 아울렛) (Gasan Digital (가산디지털), Korea's No. 1 fashion outlet, opened in 1995) is a hot zone for wholesale and retail Korean apparel brands — a top choice for foreign shopping, tax refunds, and wholesale prices.
