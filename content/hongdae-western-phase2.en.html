⚠ 3 Reasons to Choose This Phase 2 Guide

1. The real foreign favorites beyond #153's ★4.6+ ratings

The #153 list of 12 used a ratings matrix — averaging Korean and foreign scores. These 8 are filtered by foreign reviews of 8+ only. Spots where foreigners actually visited and left English, Japanese, or Chinese reviews — real demand that shows up on Reddit and Google Maps English searches.

2. 5 Yeonnam hidden gems + diversity across Hapjeong and Hongdae

The true heart of foreign Western dining demand is Yeonnam (연남). Five of the Phase 2 eight — Bluechow Pasta, Richard House, Yangsik Guok, Sareureu, and Neuru — are tucked inside the alleys (골목, narrow neighborhood streets) of Donggyo-ro (동교로) and Yeonhui-ro (연희로). A different world from Korean Instagram hotspots.

3. Genuine hidden gems with no public ★ rating

Most of these spots have no public star rating — either not yet indexed in Google Maps or Naver's rating system, or validated purely through foreign reviews. Places Korean guidebooks skip, but that foreigners naturally discover.

The Western Cuisine Scene in Hongdae · Yeonnam · Hapjeong — Phase 2

The #153 guide's ★4.6+ list of 12 was rating-verified — averaging Korean and foreign scores. These 8 are foreign-signal verified — only spots with 8–13 Google foreign reviews. Two completely different lenses.

Yeonnam-dong (연남동) (5/8) is the overwhelming center. Authentic Italian, casual Western, and neighborhood hidden gems are packed within 50-meter stretches along Donggyo-ro (동교로), Yeonhui-ro (연희로), and Seonmisan-ro (성미산로). They appear frequently in foreign reviews — repeatedly mentioned in Reddit r/koreatravel Western dining recommendation threads.

Hapjeong (합정) (1/8) — home to authentic Italian like Dot Hapjeong (도트 합정). Within a 5-minute walk from Hapjeong Station (합정역) inside the alleys.

Hongdae proper (홍대 본권) (2/8) — Omil Hongdae (오밀 홍대) and The Place Hongdae L7 (더플레이스 홍대L7점). The Place tops Phase 2 with 13 foreign reviews — a natural stop for foreign guests staying at L7 Hotel.

① Italian · Pasta (2 Spots)

1. Bluechow Pasta Yeonnam (블루쇼파스타 연남)

  • Address: 46 Seonmisan-ro 23-gil (성미산로23길), Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul
  • Signature: Pasta specialist on Seonmisan-ro (성미산로) in Yeonnam. 10 foreign reviews — a hidden gem tucked inside Yeonnam's Italian alley.
  • Foreign-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — abundant foreign reviews, trendy Yeonnam alley (골목, narrow neighborhood street)
  • Recommended: Signature pasta, ragù / Bolognese, Italian snack plates
  • Tip: 5-minute walk from Dongjin Market (동진시장), Yeonnam-dong. Weekday lunch or dinner recommended. Popular among foreign Redditors.

2. Richard House Yeonnam (리차드하우스 연남)

  • Address: 36 Donggyo-ro 46-gil (동교로46길), Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul
  • Signature: Full-scale Italian on Donggyo-ro (동교로) in Yeonnam. 10 foreign reviews — the essence of Yeonnam Italian dining.
  • Foreign-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — abundant foreign reviews, Yeonnam hotspot
  • Recommended: Signature Italian course, pasta, wine pairing
  • Tip: 12-minute walk from Hongik University Station (홍대입구역) Exit 3. Dinner reservation recommended. Can be combined with nearby Yeonnam Western dining spots into one route.

② Hapjeong · Authentic Italian (2 Spots)

3. Dot Hapjeong (도트 합정)

  • Address: 69 Seongjil-gil (성지1길), Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul
  • Signature: Authentic Italian on Seongjil-gil (성지1길) in Hapjeong. 9 foreign reviews — a hidden Italian gem in Hapjeong.
  • Foreign-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — neighborhood Hapjeong vibe, abundant foreign reviews
  • Recommended: Signature Italian dishes, pasta, wine
  • Tip: 7-minute walk from Hapjeong Station (합정역). Weekday dinner recommended. Deep in the Hapjeong alleys (골목) — confirm location on Google Maps before heading out.

4. Yangsik Guok (양식구옥)

  • Address: 27 Donggyo-ro 38-gil (동교로38길), Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul
  • Signature: Rising Western dining spot on Donggyo-ro (동교로) in Yeonnam. 10 foreign reviews — a fresh face in Yeonnam's Western scene.
  • Foreign-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — abundant foreign reviews, Yeonnam hotspot
  • Recommended: Signature Western dishes, wine, desserts
  • Tip: 10-minute walk from Hongik University Station (홍대입구역) Exit 3. Located on the same Donggyo-ro (동교로) strip as Maison Brick (메종브리크) and Bistro Ora (비스트로 오라) from #153 — easy to combine into one Western dining route.

③ Modern Western (2 Spots)

5. Omil Hongdae (오밀 홍대)

  • Address: 178 Yanghwa-ro (양화로), Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul
  • Signature: Modern Western dining on Yanghwa-ro (양화로) in Hongdae. 10 foreign reviews — a rising star in Hongdae Western cuisine.
  • Foreign-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Hongdae downtown location, abundant foreign reviews
  • Recommended: Signature modern Western course, wine, seasonal menu
  • Tip: 5-minute walk from Hongik University Station (홍대입구역) Exit 9. Walk-ins possible on weekdays; reservations recommended on weekends.

6. The Place Hongdae L7 (더플레이스 홍대L7점)

  • Address: 141 Yanghwa-ro (양화로), Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul
  • Signature: Hotel Western dining inside L7 Hotel. 13 foreign reviews (Phase 2 #1) — the go-to spot for foreign hotel guests.
  • Foreign-Friendliness: ★★★★★ — hotel location, English service, safest first choice for foreigners
  • Recommended: Hotel Western course, signature Western plates, wine
  • Tip: Inside L7 Hotel — open to non-guests as well. 7-minute walk from Hongik University Station (홍대입구역) Exit 9. If it's your first Western dining experience in Korea, this is the safe bet.
