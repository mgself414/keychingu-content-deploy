⚠ 5 Major Mistakes Foreigners Make at Summer Hangang (한강)

1. "Summer Hangang (한강) = Heat" Misconception — Skipping Nights & Early Mornings

The most common mistake for foreigners on their first summer Hangang (한강) trip. They skip Hangang (한강) due to midday (12:00-16:00) heat. In reality, nighttime (18:00-22:00) is Hangang's (한강) peak season (#06 Chimaek (치맥) · #137 Yajang (야장) · #147 Night Running) + early morning (05:00-07:00) for sunrise (#147). Avoid the heat and utilize these signature times.

2. Only Visiting Banpo (반포) & Yeouido (여의도), Skipping Hidden Parks

Banpo (반포) and Yeouido (여의도) are the top 1st and 2nd most recognized by foreigners. However, Ttukseom (뚝섬) (Cycling + Seoul Forest (서울숲) + Seongsu (성수)), Nanji (난지) (Camping + World Cup Park (월드컵공원)), and Ichon (이촌) (Sunset + Tranquility) often lead to higher foreigner satisfaction. Banpo (반포) and Yeouido (여의도) can be crowded on weekends — weekdays and hidden parks are recommended.

3. Only Knowing Chimaek (치맥), Skipping Other Hangang (한강) Activities

Chimaek (치맥) is the number one Hangang (한강) activity for foreigners. In reality, cycling (#149 Ttareungi (따릉이)), running (#146·#147), camping (#151), water sports (Water Park (워터파크) · SUP), and #137 Yajang (야장) offer equal value. A combo course of 4-5 Hangang (한강) activities is recommended.

4. Not Differentiating Between May vs. July-August Seasons

May (Spring·Early Summer): Lingering cherry blossoms + walking courses. June-July (Jangma (장마)): Nighttime + indoor combo courses. July-August (Peak Season): Nighttime + camping + water sports. September (Start of Autumn): Last chance for Hangang (한강) + beginning of autumn foliage. Each month has a different vibe.

5. Not Knowing Where to Buy Food & Drinks

Foreigners often try to bring picnic food and drinks from their hotel. In reality, pyeonuijeom (편의점, convenience store, like 7-Eleven or FamilyMart) (within a 5-minute walk of Hangang (한강) parks), chicken delivery (#06 Chimaek (치맥)), and market food (#137 Yajang (야장)) are the baseline.

Foreigner's Summer Hangang (한강) Etiquette·Precautions

Essential Etiquette:

1. Food·Drinks: Pyeonuijeom (편의점) + chicken delivery baseline. Outside food restricted in some establishments.

2. Trash: Korean waste separation (bunri sugeo (분리수거)) (Hangang Park (한강공원) trash can etiquette).

3. Smoking: Hangang Park (한강공원) smoking areas are limited.

4. Pets: Restricted in some parks (Nanji (난지)·Ttukseom (뚝섬) allowed).

Precautions:

  • Jangma (장마) (June-July): Hangang Park (한강공원) closures possible — check weather forecast in advance.
  • Typhoons (August-September): Hangang (한강) water sports·camping cancellations possible.
  • Summer mosquitoes (mogi (모기))·insects: Mosquito spray essential for camping·nighttime activities.
  • UV Rays: Sunscreen essential during midday (12:00-16:00).
