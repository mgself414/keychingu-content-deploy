The Han River is one of the top spots for foreigners traveling to Korea — very frequently searched on Reddit, Google, and YouTube. ⚠ Five major summer mistakes foreigners make at the Han River

1. Recognition of “Summer Han River = Heat” — Night/Dawn Pass

The most common mistakes along the Han River during the first summer as a foreigner. Passing the Han River in the middle of the day (12-16 p.m.) due to the heat. In reality, 18-22 p.m. = Han River peak season (#06 chicken and beer·#137 night market·#147 night running) + Sunrise 5-7 a.m. (#147). Avoid the heat and take advantage of signature times.

2. Hidden park pass only for Banpo and Yeouido

Banpo and Yeouido are ranked 1st and 2nd in recognition by foreigners. In reality, Ttukseom (Bike + Seoul Forest + Seongsu) · Nanji (Camping + World Cup Park) · Ichon (Sunset + Hangout) have higher foreigner satisfaction. Banpo and Yeouido are crowded on weekends — weekdays and hidden parks are recommended.

3. Just know chicken and beer and pass other activities along the Han River

The number one choice for foreigners along the Han River is chicken and beer. In reality, Bicycle (#149 Ttareungi) · Running (#146·#147) · Camping (#151) · Water Sports (Water Park·SUP) · #137 Camping are of the same value. Han River 4-5 activity bundle course recommended.

4. May vs July-August season not separated

May (spring/early summer): Lingering cherry blossoms + walking course. June-July (rainy season): Night/indoor combined course. July-August (peak season): overnight + camping + water sports. September (beginning of fall): End of Han River + beginning of fall foliage. Different tons every month.

5. I don’t know where to buy food and drinks

Foreigners try to bring food and drinks for the Han River picnic from the hotel. In reality, convenience store (within 5 minutes walk from Han River Park) · chicken delivery (#06 chicken and beer) · market food (#137 night market) are the baseline.

Summer Han River manners and precautions for foreigners

Essential Manners:

1. Food/Beverage: Convenience store + chicken delivery baseline. Bringing in outside food is restricted in some stores

2. Trash: Separated recycling in Korea (Hangang Park trash can manners)

3. Smoking: Hangang Park smoking area limited

4. Pets: Restricted in some parks (Nanji and Ttukseom only)

Note:

  • Rainy season (June-July): Hangang Park may be closed — check the weather forecast in advance
  • Typhoon (August-September): Han River water sports and camping can be canceled
  • Summer mosquitoes and insects: Mosquito spray is essential for camping and night activities
  • UV rays: UV protection during midday (12-16:00)
