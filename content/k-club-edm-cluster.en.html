7 Seoul Clubs — 4 Techno and House in Itaewon, 2 Hip-hop and Live in Hongdae, 1 EDM in Cheongdam

Subtitle: 4 techno and house clubs lined up along Itaewon-ro between Noksapyeong and Itaewon, 2 clubs near Sangsu Station (one hip-hop club and one live club), and 1 large EDM club on Dosan-daero in Cheongdam. For each venue we noted the cover charge, the days and hours it opens, the genre, and what to check at the door. We also put together the age limit and ID rules for entering clubs, and safety tips on drinks, getting home, and lost items.

#777 · 2026-11-24 revised · Seoul Clubs

🎯 Who This Is For

This piece is for travelers who want to dance all night to DJ music in Seoul. It is easiest to choose by genre. Techno and house are in Itaewon, hip-hop, K-pop, and band shows are in Hongdae, and big-stage EDM is in Cheongdam. We kept only places that are confirmed as open (status Y) on Kakao Map (카카오맵) and have visit reviews or event notices posted in 2026.

⚠ Notation principle: Opening hours are the values registered by the owner on Kakao Map (checked 2026-11-24). For places with no hours on Kakao Map, we used the values from dated event notices or visit reviews and put the source next to them. A club's cover charge changes with the day and the DJ playing that night. The amounts below are examples from dated reviews and event notices, so check each club's Instagram notice for that day before you go. Walking distances are measured on OpenStreetMap walking routes, and the station exits and walking times are Kakao Map directions values.

① Noksapyeong to Itaewon — 4 Techno and House Clubs

All four are around Itaewon-ro between Noksapyeong Station and Itaewon Station. It is 311m (4 minutes on foot) from Cakeshop to Volnost, 471m (6 minutes) from Volnost to FAUST, and 392m (5 minutes) from FAUST to Soap. At techno and house clubs, who is playing that night matters most, so each club posts its DJ lineup in advance on Instagram and Resident Advisor (RA).

Cakeshop (케이크샵)

A small basement club near Noksapyeong Station that sometimes hosts touring international DJs. The Kakao Map registered hours are Fri·Sat 22:00~05:00. According to a visit review posted 2026-11-03, the writer bought a ticket for the 2026-07-04 international DJ show on RA for 22,500 won, and a drink inside cost about 10,000 won. Another review by the same author (2026-11-02) bought a January 30 show at the early-bird price of about 20,000 won and wrote that there was no re-entry on days with a well-known guest. The genre moves between techno, house, and bass music depending on the event (blog 2026-09-12). One review says that on days with a big guest the main DJ only came on around 2 a.m. (Kakao review 2026-02-03). The same review says it gets very hot inside, so leave your jacket at the coat check. The address is Itaewon-ro (이태원로) 134, Basement 1, a 6-minute walk from Exit 3 of Noksapyeong Station (Line 6).

  • At the door: On days with a big guest, RA tickets can sell out first, so confirm your ticket before you go.

Volnost (볼노스트)

A club devoted to dark lighting and heavy techno. The name means 'freedom' in Czech. The Kakao Map registered hours are Thu 20:00~02:00, Fri·Sat 23:00~06:00, and it is closed Sun~Wed. The blog The Edit (2026-10-14) lists a cover charge of 14,000 won and the genre as dark and experimental techno, and says filming is restricted. According to the same post, the entrance is at the end of a narrow passage between buildings. A 2026-01-25 Kakao Map review says that if you take your things out of the locker once, you have to pay again. The address is Itaewon-ro (이태원로) 142, 6 minutes from Exit 3 of Noksapyeong Station.

  • At the door: This is a place you find by following the sound, not the signboard. If you put your things in a locker, it is better not to take them out until the night ends.

FAUST (파우스트)

A 3-floor club where the main floor plays techno and the 'Tanz Bar (탄츠바)' next to it plays house (visit review 2026-03-24). Kakao Map lists only Fri·Sat from 23:00 with no closing time. The 2026-10-24 (Sat) show by an international techno duo was announced as 23:00 to 05:00 the next day (blog 2026-10-20). As of the 2026-03-24 review, the cover charge is 15,000 won and coat check is 2,000 won. According to FAUST's official house rules, you may be asked for ID if you look like a minor, and if you cannot show any ID you cannot enter. Men dressed in a suit, a uniform, or a dress shirt may be refused entry. Groups that spoil the atmosphere, such as all-male groups and bachelor parties, and guests who do not know that night's program may be restricted. Commercial filming indoors is banned, and they recommend keeping your phone in your pocket. The address is Bogwang-ro 60-gil (보광로60길) 7, Floor 3, 2 minutes from Exit 3 of Itaewon Station (Line 6).

  • At the door: Relaxed outfits are welcome here. Know at least that night's DJ name before you go.

Soap Seoul (소프 서울)

A house and electronic music club that moved to its current location in 2026 (blog 2026-11-20). The address on the official website is Itaewon-ro (이태원로) 217, Basement 2. Kakao Map has no hours, so we could not confirm its regular opening days. Check Soap's Instagram (@soapseoul) for the regular opening days. As an event example, the 2026-11-20 (Fri) event was announced as 22:00~05:00, 20,000 won sold at the door (1 drink included), open only to those born in 2007 or earlier. A 2026-09-25 visit review also gave a cover of 20,000 won (for that day's event), and said the inside is smoke-free (e-cigarettes only) and that groovy R&B and house played early in the night. A 2026-09-12 Kakao Map review also said smoking is not allowed indoors and that the music changes with each DJ. It is 6 minutes from Exit 2 of Itaewon Station.

  • At the door: The birth-year condition is announced for each event. Bring your physical ID.

② Sangsu Station — 2 Hip-hop and Live Clubs

The two are 149m apart (2 minutes on foot). Both are closer to Sangsu Station (Line 6) than to Hongik Univ. Station.

Club NB2 (클럽 NB2)

A Hongdae hip-hop club running since 1999 (review 2026-01-20, 'since 1999'). The Kakao Map registered hours are Mon~Fri 22:00~06:00, Sat·Sun 21:30~07:00, so it opens every day. According to a 2025-07-08 visit review, you pay 10,000 won at the door and get a wristband and one free drink coupon, hip-hop and K-pop play, and there are many foreign tourists. A 2026-01-20 review (a visit in the early morning hours) also gives a cover of 10,000 won. The cover can change depending on the time and day. Ordering a bottle at a table, a bottle of tequila was 99,000 won (review 2025-10-06). The address is Wausan-ro (와우산로) 72, Basement 1~2, 6 minutes from Exit 2 of Sangsu Station.

  • At the door: This is the easiest place to get into in this piece. It also opens on weekdays, so it is easy to fit into a schedule.

Club FF (클럽 FF)

A basement club that does band shows and dance parties together. The official blog describes it as a 'live & dance club' and says the venue holds 160 standing. The Kakao Map registered hours are Wed~Sun 19:00~06:00. On show days the band stage opens first in the evening (review 2026-08-28). According to a 2026-08-28 visit review, on a day with 4 bands the cover was 40,000 won (bank transfer at the door), and ID was checked at the entrance. The Kakao Map menu prices (2025-06-11) are 8,000 won for a cocktail, 3,000 won for a soft drink, and 60,000 won for a bottle of liquor. The address is Wausan-ro 17-gil (와우산로17길) 12, Basement 1, 6 minutes from Exit 1 of Sangsu Station.

  • At the door: The review says they paid by bank transfer at the door. Check Club FF's Instagram notice for the payment method and that day's price. If you do not have a Korean bank account, ask about the payment method before you visit.

③ Cheongdam — 1 Large EDM Club

LION SUPER CLUB (라이언슈퍼클럽)

A large EDM club that opened in early 2026. Both Kakao Map and the official website say Fri·Sat 23:00~06:00. According to a 2026-02-21 visit review (posted 2026-02-25), the cover charge varied by event, such as 80,000 won, 30,000 won, 10,000 won, and free, and you can buy tickets both online (the dumbs app) and at the door. The same review described festival-style lighting and screens, tech house and EDM sets, and an interior that does not smell of cigarettes. The official website has a 'lost and found' menu. The address is Dosan-daero (도산대로) 535, a 12-minute walk from Exit 13 of Cheongdam Station (Line 7), so a bus or taxi is more convenient.

  • At the door: The cover charge differs greatly by event, so check that day's price at @lionseoul.

🗺 Two Nights Out

A. Itaewon Techno (4~6 minutes on foot between clubs)

  1. 22:00~23:00 Exit 3 of Noksapyeong Station → Cakeshop (Fri·Sat) or Volnost (Thu~Sat)
  2. Around 01:00 On Fri·Sat, move to FAUST (471m from Volnost). Go back and forth between the main floor and the Tanz Bar
  3. Some days have no re-entry, so it is better to hear that night's main DJ at one place before you move

B. Sangsu Station (149m between clubs)

  1. From 19:00 Enter Club FF (open Wed~Sun). Check the Instagram notice for whether it is a band show day
  2. Around 23:00 Move to NB2 and carry on with hip-hop and K-pop
  3. After the subway stops, go home by the night owl bus or a taxi in 'Safety and Getting Home' below

🛡 Safety and Getting Home

  • Drinks: Do not drink any alcohol or drink whose contents you cannot be sure of. Carry your glass with you when you go to dance or to the restroom, or get a fresh one. If you suddenly feel dazed or have gaps in your memory, suspect it, do not throw away the glass you were drinking from, and call 112 to report it. You can have blood and urine tests through the police (Incheon Metropolitan City guidelines on preventing drug-related sexual crimes, 2024-12-19). In Korea the police number is 112 and ambulances are 119 (UK government foreign travel advice).
  • Interpretation: The Korea Tourism Organization's tourist interpretation hotline 1330 supports English, and from 07:00~24:00 it also offers three-way call interpretation (UK government foreign travel advice).
  • Getting home: Seoul's night owl buses (late-night buses) run on 14 routes from 23:00~06:00, and the fare is 2,500 won by card (Seoul traffic website, revised 2026-03-20). Check the stop and route in advance where you will board. For a taxi, walk out to the main road to catch one.
  • Lost items: For items lost on buses and taxis, use the Seoul 'Public Transit Integrated Lost Property Center'. The operator keeps items for 7 days and then hands them to the police station, after which you search the National Police Agency's lost property portal (lost112.go.kr). You can also ask the 120 Dasan Call Center (Seoul traffic website, revised 2025-04-16). For a bag left at a club's coat check, some places, like FAUST's house rules, do not take responsibility for lost valuables, so keep your passport and wallet on your body.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Planning an Itaewon club for Sunday through Wednesday — The days registered on Kakao Map are Fri·Sat for Cakeshop and FAUST and Thu~Sat for Volnost. We could not confirm Soap's regular opening days. For a weeknight, NB2, which opens every day, and Club FF, which opens from Wednesday, are the realistic choices.
  2. Treating the cover charge as a fixed amount — Even the same club ranges from free to 80,000 won depending on the event (LION SUPER CLUB review 2026-02-25). For international DJ shows, tickets are sometimes bought in advance on RA (Cakeshop review 2026-11-03).
  3. Carrying only a photo of your ID — Some places do not accept a photo or copy on a phone at the door. Foreigners should bring the original passport. Clubs check age at the entrance, and FAUST's house rules also say you may be asked for ID.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. From what age can you enter a club? — The Youth Protection Act treats anyone under 19 as a youth, but excludes them once January 1 of the year they turn 19 has passed. It is counted by birth year regardless of birthday, so in 2026 people born in 2007 or earlier can buy alcohol. The same Act bars selling alcohol to youths and requires the seller to check age and identity. Soap Seoul's 2026-11-20 event was also announced as 'open to those born in 2007 and earlier'. Each club may set its own stricter standard, and even if a club's own rule says 18, the birth-year standard of Korean law takes priority.
  2. Is there a dress code? — FAUST is the one place where we confirmed an official rule. It welcomes free and bold outfits, and says men dressed in a suit, a uniform, or a dress shirt may be refused entry. For the other clubs we could not confirm a fixed rule. At techno clubs you stand for a long time, so comfortable shoes are better.
  3. Do you need to reserve? — For Cakeshop, on days with an international DJ there are RA tickets (review 2026-11-03), and LION SUPER CLUB sells tickets in the dumbs app and at the door (review 2026-02-25). For NB2 and Volnost we could not confirm any ticketing information. Check each club's Instagram before you go.
