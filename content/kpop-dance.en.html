35_K-pop Dance Class

One-Line Intro

Learn K-pop moves in Seoul — from BTS to BLACKPINK choreography, taught by real instructors.

Who This Is For

  • K-pop fans and travelers who want to learn actual idol choreography
  • Travelers looking for a unique, hands-on experience during their Korea trip

Recommended Itinerary (3 Hours)

1. 10:00 Confirm Your Reservation: Advance online booking is required at almost all studios (official website or Klook)

2. 11:00 Arrive at the Studio: Comfortable workout clothes and sneakers are a must

3. 11:15 Warm-up + Basic Moves (30 min): Foundational K-pop dance steps

4. 11:45 Learn the Choreography (60 min): Full routine for 1–2 popular idol songs

5. 12:45 Film Your Performance (15 min): Record what you learned — perfect Instagram content

6. 13:00 Free Practice Time (optional): Stay an extra 30–60 min to keep practicing

Tips for Foreign Visitors

  • Language: Most classes designed for international visitors are conducted entirely in English — no Korean needed.
  • What to Bring
  • Comfortable workout clothes (joggers, leggings, or shorts)
  • Sneakers (dance shoes are a plus)
  • Water bottle and towel
  • Smartphone for filming
  • How to Book: Strongly recommended to book in advance via Klook, KKday, or the studio's official website before arriving in Korea. Walk-in spots are rarely available.
  • Cultural Note: Live streaming during class is generally prohibited. Photos and videos are allowed only during designated times.

Simple English Summary

K-pop dance classes in Seoul are one of the most popular tourist activities, offering a 1–2 hour session where you learn actual idol choreography. Studios like 1 MILLION Dance Studio (원밀리언) — founded by famous YouTube dancers — and Idol School (아이돌 스쿨) offer foreigner-friendly classes conducted entirely in English. Classes typically cost ₩30,000–50,000 and culminate in a filmed performance of the learned choreography, perfect for social media content. Book in advance via Klook or KKday; walk-in spots are rare.

Estimated Total Cost

  • Budget: ₩25,000 (one basic class)
  • Standard: ₩50,000 (premium class + transportation)
  • Splurge: ₩120,000 (2 classes + SM Artium (SM아티움) experience + printed photos)
