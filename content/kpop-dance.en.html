⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Showing up without a reservation — it doesn't work

1Million and Just Jerk require booking 3–7 days ahead. Walk-ins are almost never available. English-language booking is possible via Klook or Trip.com. Korean office workers and foreign fans compete for the same slots — popular instructors' classes fill up 1+ week in advance.

2. Ignoring the skill level — falling behind

Most classes are labeled "Open Level" — fine for beginners and intermediate dancers — but some are marked Pro/Advanced. Check your own level beforehand; beginners should pick Open Level.

3. Not bringing workout clothes and sneakers

Casual clothes and slides are uncomfortable. Workout clothes and sneakers are recommended. Some studios rent sneakers for ₩5K.

⚙ FAQ

Q1. Is English service and booking available?

A. 1Million, Just Jerk, and Stage9 (스테이지나인) all offer English service. Klook and Trip.com allow advance booking in English. Hotel concierges can also book for you.

Q2. Can I keep up without knowing Korean?

A. Classes are mostly taught by demonstration — no Korean needed. Instructors count "5·6·7·8" and show each move.

Q3. Recommendations by budget?

A. Budget ₩20K–35K: Stage9 and other Hongdae (홍대) studios, single class. Standard ₩30K–50K: 1Million, Just Jerk, Klook packages. Luxury ₩70K–200K: agency workshops, 1-on-1 classes.

Q4. What are the most popular songs in class?

A. BTS (Dynamite, Butter), BLACKPINK (Pink Venom, Shut Down), NewJeans (Hype Boy, Super Shy), IVE, LE SSERAFIM, and more — rotates by season.

Q5. What's the difference between 1Million and Just Jerk?

A. 1Million: the most globally recognized, with a broad range of K-pop, hip-hop, and choreography styles. Just Jerk: authentic K-pop choreography + dedicated NewJeans choreography coaching.
