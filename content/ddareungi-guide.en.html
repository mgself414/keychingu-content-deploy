Ddareungi (따릉이) Public Bike, No-Panic Procedure — Follow the Screens Exactly

Subtitle: Complete guide to Seoul's public bike system Ddareungi (따릉이) — app setup → rental → return step-by-step · 3 causes of QR scan failure · the real trap of "returned it but charges keep running" · connecting routes to Hangang River riding

🎯 How to Read This Procedure — Screen Notation Rules

This uses the same notation as #1270 (the kiosk piece). Instead of "do this," it writes down exactly "what appears on the screen, which button to press, and in what order."

  • Buttons on screen appear in brackets: [ Rent ]
  • Screen titles and prompts appear in corner brackets: 「Bannap wanryo (반납 완료)」
  • Hand actions appear bold: "push the lock lever all the way down"

① What Is Ddareungi — Rent Anywhere in Seoul, Return at Any Station

Ddareungi (따릉이) is Seoul's public bike system. Unmanned rental stations (docking racks) sit at subway stations, parks, and major streets, and the pickup station and the return station don't have to match — wherever you're riding in the city, park it at any nearby station and you're done. There's none of the "you have to bring it back to where you rented it" burden of a rental car, so for short trips it's often even faster than a taxi or bus.

That convenience, though, makes it easy to rush through the return steps — and that's the real trap covered in ④.

② App Setup — From Install to Buying a Pass

1. Search the app store for Ddareungi (따릉이, Seoul Bike) and install it

2. On the first-launch language screen, select [ Foreigner (외국인) ] → it automatically switches to English mode. This one choice turns every screen afterward into English.

3. Buy a pass — just buy as much as you need

Pass Price
1-hour pass ₩1,000
2-hour pass ₩2,000
All-day pass (1-day) ₩5,000

4. Payment: you can register a foreign card (Visa, Mastercard, JCB) — enter your card details right inside the app

This much is worth finishing at your accommodation ahead of time. If you install the app, switch the language, and buy a pass before you're standing in front of a bike, everything from ③ on takes only seconds.

③ Rental Master Flow — This Order, Right at the Bike

Step What to Do Pitfall
1 Tap [ Rent ] at the bottom of the app If you haven't bought a pass, you'll get stuck here — finish ② first
2 Point your phone camera at the QR code on the terminal above the bike's rear wheel and scan it Location (GPS) and Bluetooth must be ON before you scan — if they're off, the scan won't register at all
3 Scan succeeds → a "click" sound and the lock releases No sound means it's still locked. Don't force the bike out — rescan instead

Once you hear the "click," that bike is yours now. Just check the seat height and go.

④ Return Master Flow — The Real Trap Is Here

Mistakes happen far more often on return than on rental. Pushing the lever down and walking away without checking is the single most common mistake.

Step What to Do Pitfall
1 Push the bike into any station's docking rack No need to return to a specific station (see ①)
2 Push the lock lever all the way down by hand Pushing it only halfway can register as "not locked"
3 Confirm 「Bannap wanryo (반납 완료) — Return Complete」 via app push notification or KakaoTalk alert ★★ This is the biggest trap — even if the lever is down, if you don't confirm return-complete through the app or KakaoTalk alert, the system still shows it as rented and charges keep running
4 If no notification arrives, open the app's rental history (currently-in-use bike) directly to recheck the status If it still shows as rented, try pushing the lever again; if that doesn't change it, go to Plan B in ⑨

Pushing the lever down isn't the end. Confirming the notification is the end. That one line is the most important sentence in this whole procedure.

⑤ When the QR Scan Won't Work — Only 3 Possible Causes

Cause How to Check Fix
Location (GPS) OFF Check location services status in phone settings Turn it on and rescan
Bluetooth OFF Check the control center at the top of your phone Turn it on and rescan
Terminal-side problem (dead battery, etc.) Both of the above are on, but it still keeps failing Give up on that bike and move to the next bike over and try again

Just check these three in order and most cases resolve within 30 seconds.

⑦ Basic Bike Lane Rules + Connecting to Hangang River Riding

On city streets, use the bike lane beside the road (green pavement); where there isn't one, the rule is to ride along the edge of the road, not on the sidewalk. Pedestrians have priority — where the bike lane overlaps with the sidewalk, slow down and signal with your bell (or your voice). Traffic lights apply the same as they do to cars.

If you want to go beyond short trips and actually enjoy riding, Hangang Park (한강공원) is the answer. A dedicated bike path runs the length of both banks of the Hangang River, and Ddareungi stations cluster densely near the entrances of major Hangang parks like Yeouido, Banpo, and Ttukseom — you can return the bike you rode in the city at a Hangang station, then, if you like, rent a fresh one there and ride just the riverside stretch as a separate leg.

⑧ 3 Panic Scenarios

1. You definitely returned it, but there's no charge confirmation — don't panic, go back to Step 3 of ④ and open the app's "currently in use" list yourself. If it still shows as rented, push the lever again and retry.

2. The QR won't scan — following the order in ⑤ (location → Bluetooth → the bike next to it) resolves most cases. No need to rush.

3. The station is full and there's no space to return the bike — don't force it in; check the app's map for the nearest other station and head there instead. Most of the time there's an open station within a 5-minute walk.

⑨ Plan B — If It Still Doesn't Work

  • Move to a different station: the app's map shows real-time open slots at docking racks. Route around a full station or a broken bike by using a different station
  • Contact customer service: report an unconfirmed return or a billing error through the customer service (inquiry) menu inside the app — keeping a screenshot (time and location) speeds up handling
  • If the bike itself is defective, leave it where it is and report it: don't force yourself to ride it — report the malfunction in the app and use a different bike
