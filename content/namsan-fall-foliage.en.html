Why are the autumn leaves on Namsan Mountain now — No. 1 for foreigners in the fall foliage in the city? Accessibility #1

The easiest place to see autumn leaves in downtown Seoul = Namsan (262m, central Seoul). Dulle-gil is a 5-minute walk from Myeongdong Station and Chungmuro ​​Station, 1.5 to 2 hours for a 7.5km walk, peak fall foliage from October 20 to November 5. N Seoul Tower·Namsan Hanok Village·Cable Car·Shuttle Bus 03 4 types of infrastructure are fully covered by foreigners at the same time as the autumn leaves (visitkorea·Seoul City·hanyangcity official).

Distinction compared to other fall foliage spots (Bukhansan Mountain, Naejangsan Mountain, Seoraksan Mountain):

  • Accessibility: 5 minutes from downtown Myeongdong, outskirts autumn foliage day-trip (#739 Gapyeong·Yangpyeong) not required
  • Full Set: Autumn leaves + N Seoul Tower + Hanok Village + Shopping (Myeongdong) = Full set for one day
  • Cost-effectiveness: Free course 0 won, full set 22,000 won (~17 USD)
  • Foreigner infrastructure: EN/JP/CN multilingual full cover (Korea Tourism Organization + Seoul Metropolitan Government + Hanok Village triple guide)

This matrix consists of 3 trails + 3 view spots for fall foliage + 3 nearby attractions + 3 infrastructure/seasons for foreigners = 12 routes and 4 axes.
