Mapo·Gongdeok Guide — 12 Picks for Foreign Tourists + 4 Deep Dives for Office Workers & MZ

Subtitle: Korean Food·Lunch + Cafes·Desserts·Bakeries + Bars·Izakayas + Hangang·Gongdeok Station·Sinchon-ro — Mapo 12 Picks + Deep Dive for Office Workers & Youth, Spread Across 4 Pillars

ISSUE 03 · #188 · 2026-05-18

🎯 Why a Standalone Mapo·Gongdeok Guide?

Mapo·Gongdeok (마포·공덕) is the No. 1 spot for office workers and MZ (Millennials and Gen Z) in Gongdeok Station (공덕역)·Ahyun (아현)·Dohwa (도화)·Daeheung (대흥) within Mapo-gu (마포구) — a deep dive into the main Mapo-gu area, separate from Hongdae (#138 Hongdae)·Yeonnam (연남)·Hapjeong (합정). It features Gongdeok Station (공덕역) (a 4-line transfer station for Lines 5, 6, Airport Railroad, and Gyeongui-Jungang Line) + Ahyun (아현), a traditional neighborhood + Dohwa (도화)·Daeheung (대흥), an MZ cafe cluster. This is KeyChinGu's first entry into the main Mapo-gu area (Mapo cluster with 177 entries).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Mapo (마포) = Hongdae (홍대)" — Undervaluing the main area: Mapo-gu (마포구) offers a deep dive into Gongdeok (공덕)·Ahyun (아현)·Dohwa (도화)·Daeheung (대흥)·Sangam DMC (상암 DMC) for office workers, MZ, and media, beyond Hongdae (#138 Hongdae).

2. "Kim Min-kyung Born in 1982 = Unknown": This is a collaboration with a bestselling Korean author + cafe — a deep dive into Korean books for foreigners.

3. "Sangam DMC (상암 DMC) = Too Far": It's located in western Mapo (마포) near Hangang (한강) and World Cup Stadium. Accessible via Digital Media City Station (디지털미디어시티역) on Line 6.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Gongdeok Station (공덕역) 4-line transfer for foreigners? It's a prime transfer hub, with a direct connection to Incheon Airport.

2. Kim Min-kyung Born in 1982 Cafe (82년생 김민경 카페)? A collaboration with bestselling author Cho Nam-joo, a top choice for MZ.

3. Sangam DMC (상암 DMC) MBC (MBC) tour? Advance reservations are available.

4. Mapo Hangang Park (마포 한강공원) cycling? Start your ride on the western Hangang (한강) bike path.

5. Mapo (마포) + Hongdae (홍대) itinerary? A 15-minute walk or one stop on Line 6.

6. World Cup Stadium (월드컵경기장)? The stadium from the 2002 Korea-Japan World Cup + a museum.
