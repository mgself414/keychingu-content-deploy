⚠ 3 Common Mistakes International Visitors Make

1. Thinking Gangnam = shopping & beauty clinics only

Many international visitors associate Gangnam purely with shopping and plastic surgery. In reality, Gangnam, Dosan, Apgujeong, and Nonhyeon are the heart of Korea's luxury café scene — home to the peak of Korea's third-wave specialty coffee movement and a dense cluster of acclaimed bakeries. If Hongdae cafés lean casual, Gangnam cafés lean luxury and refined. Pairing a shopping itinerary with 1–2 café stops is the #1 Gangnam deep-dive move.

2. Mixing up Apgujeong, Dosan, and Sinnonhyeon (신논현)

Gangnam-gu (강남구) contains four distinct sub-areas — Apgujeong (압구정) (MZ · luxury), Dosan (도산) (specialty · gallery), Nonhyeon (논현) (bakery · brunch), Gangnam Station (강남역) (chains · shopping). Each has a different café personality. We recommend sticking to one sub-area per outing. Dosan Park stroll + Dosan café-hop is the most quintessentially Gangnam itinerary for international visitors.

3. Only visiting chain cafés and missing indie spots

If you only see Starbucks and Ediya around Gangnam Station (강남역), you're missing half the picture. Indie venues like Standard Bread Dosan (스탠다드브레드 도산), Not Bad Coffee Dosan (낫배드커피 도산), Higgs Coffee (힉스커피), Hipsthetic Coffee (힙스테틱 커피), and Curarie (쿠라리에) are concentrated in Dosan (도산), Nonhyeon (논현), and Apgujeong (압구정). Make time for at least one indie stop. Weekdays 14:00–17:00 (2–5 PM) are the quietest.

Gangnam's Café Landscape

Gangnam-gu (강남구) is the epicentre of Korean luxury lifestyle — cafés are densely packed along Gangnam-daero (강남대로), around Dosan Park (도산공원), Apgujeong Rodeo (압구정로데오), Sinsa-dong (신사동), and Nonhyeon-dong (논현동). The vibe here is distinct from Hongdae (홍대) (indie · trendy), Jongno (종로) (hanok · gallery-style), and Seongsu (성수) (curated select-shop energy) — Gangnam's signature is luxury · refinement · MZ.

Dosan Park (도산공원) is the home of Korea's third-wave specialty coffee and gallery cafés — a cluster of gourmet spots including Standard Bread Dosan (스탠다드브레드 도산) and Not Bad Coffee Dosan (낫배드커피 도산). The #1 itinerary stop for international food lovers.

Apgujeong (압구정) · Apgujeong Rodeo (압구정로데오) are the MZ luxury café zone — within a 5-minute walk of Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역) you'll find Insta hotspots like twnty (twnty 압구정점) and Selected Nix (셀렉티드닉스). The #1 route for Korean trendy locals and international MZ visitors alike.

Nonhyeon (논현) · Sinsa (신사) are the bakery and brunch territory — featuring venues like NY Love Bagel Gangnam (뉴욕러브베이글 강남본점) and B-plate by Brown Bread (비플레이트 바이 브라운브레드).

Gangnam Station (강남역) · Gangnam-daero (강남대로) blends modern specialty coffee with shopping — Higgs Coffee (힉스커피), Hipsthetic Coffee (힙스테틱 커피), and Curarie (쿠라리에) all sit along or near the main boulevard.

① Bakery (3 Venues)

1. Standard Bread Dosan (스탠다드브레드 도산) ★5.0

  • Location: Around Dosan-daero (도산대로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul
  • Signature: Dosan's top bakery hotspot. Signature loaves + modern bakery — the #1 pairing for a Dosan Park (도산공원) stroll.
  • International-friendliness: ★★★★★ — English menu, foreign cards accepted, on the Dosan international visitor trail
  • Recommended orders: Signature bread, croissant, seasonal desserts
  • Price range: ₩5,500–12,000 (bread · drinks / ~$4–9 / ~30–65 RMB / ~530–1,160 JPY)
  • Tip: Works well both morning and afternoon on weekdays. Best paired with a Dosan Park stroll. Expect queues weekend lunchtimes.

2. NY Love Bagel Gangnam (뉴욕러브베이글 강남본점) ★4.8

  • Location: Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul
  • Signature: New York-style bagel Gangnam flagship. NY-style bagels + cream cheese and lox — instantly familiar to visitors from the US or Europe.
  • International-friendliness: ★★★★★ — English menu, familiar American format, foreign cards accepted
  • Recommended orders: Classic NY bagel, bagel sandwich, cream cheese combo
  • Price range: ₩5,500–11,000 (bagels · sandwiches)
  • Tip: Great Gangnam breakfast option. A go-to for business travellers and visitors from the US or Europe.

3. Bouquet de Pain (부케 드 뺑) ★4.78

  • Location: Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul
  • Signature: Gangnam's French bakery. Authentic French breads + seasonal pastries — recommended for international food enthusiasts. 5 foreign-language reviews on record.
  • International-friendliness: ★★★★☆ — partial English menu, French atmosphere
  • Recommended orders: Authentic French breads, croissant, seasonal pastries
  • Price range: ₩5,000–12,000 (bread)
  • Tip: Best for a weekday morning visit. Ideal for international food lovers.

② Specialty Coffee (3 Venues)

4. Not Bad Coffee Dosan (낫배드커피 도산) ★5.0

  • Location: Around Dosan-daero (도산대로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul
  • Signature: Dosan's authentic specialty hand-drip café. Small-batch single-origin beans + seasonal limited roasts. The pinnacle of Korea's third-wave coffee scene in Gangnam.
  • International-friendliness: ★★★★★ — English-friendly service, on the Dosan international visitor trail
  • Recommended orders: Hand-drip single origin, seasonal limited roast, decaf option
  • Price range: ₩6,500–11,000 (coffee)
  • Tip: #1 stop for coffee lovers. Quiet on weekday afternoons. Combine with a Dosan Park (도산공원) stroll for the full experience.

5. Higgs Coffee (힉스커피) ★4.8

  • Location: Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul
  • Signature: Gangnam specialty roastery. Signature espresso + hand-drip — a gourmet stop along Gangnam-daero (강남대로).
  • International-friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English menu, close to Gangnam Station (강남역)
  • Recommended orders: Signature espresso, hand-drip, seasonal drinks
  • Price range: ₩6,000–10,000 (coffee)
  • Tip: Walking distance from Gangnam Station (강남역). Top pick for business travellers.

6. Hipsthetic Coffee (힙스테틱 커피) ★4.72

  • Location: Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul
  • Signature: Gangnam's modern specialty café. Contemporary interior + signature drinks — a polished Gangnam trendy spot. 3 foreign-language reviews on record.
  • International-friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English menu, modern atmosphere
  • Recommended orders: Signature drinks, coffee, seasonal limited menu
  • Price range: ₩6,500–12,000 (drinks)
  • Tip: Great addition to a Gangnam café-hop itinerary. Weekday afternoons recommended.

7. Le Bon Gouter (르봉구떼) ★4.4

Q2. What are the opening hours and closing days?

A. Typical hours: Weekdays 10:00–22:00 (10 AM–10 PM), weekends 09:00–23:00 (9 AM–11 PM). Days off: Varies by café — check Instagram before visiting. Best time to visit: Weekdays 14:00–17:00 (2–5 PM) for a quieter experience. Weekends 11:00–14:00 (11 AM–2 PM) attract heavy Korean MZ crowds — expect 1-hour+ waits.

Q3. Where should I start for Korea's third-wave specialty coffee?

A. #1 pick: Not Bad Coffee Dosan (낫배드커피 도산) — the authentic Dosan hand-drip experience.

Modern specialty: Higgs Coffee (힉스커피) (along Gangnam-daero), Hipsthetic Coffee (힙스테틱 커피) (Gangnam modern).

Coffee lover's café-hop: Not Bad Coffee Dosan (낫배드커피 도산) → Higgs Coffee (힉스커피) → Hipsthetic Coffee (힙스테틱 커피).

🌸 Seasonal & Timing Tips

Spring (March–May): Dosan Park (도산공원) + Standard Bread Dosan (스탠다드브레드 도산) + Not Bad Coffee Dosan (낫배드커피 도산) — cherry blossoms + bakery + specialty coffee. Best on weekday mornings in April.

Summer (June–August): Cool-interior cafés + Hipsthetic Coffee (힙스테틱 커피) + Selected Nix (셀렉티드닉스) — modern café + MZ trendy. Quietest weekdays 14:00–17:00 (2–5 PM) in July–August.

Autumn (September–November): Apgujeong (압구정) + twnty Apgujeong (twnty 압구정점) + Curarie (쿠라리에) — autumn foliage + MZ café-hop. Weekday afternoons in October–November.

Winter (December–February): Warm indoor cafés + VIEW376 night views + Le Bon Gouter (르봉구떼) + B-plate by Brown Bread (비플레이트 바이 브라운브레드) — views + brunch. Evening time slots December–February.

Sunset / Night View Golden Hour: VIEW376 at 18:00 (6 PM), just before sunset — view, sunset, and city lights all at once. The #1 luxury evening pick for international visitors.
