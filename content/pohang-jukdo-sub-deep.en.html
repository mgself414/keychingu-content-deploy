Pohang Market Guide — Jukdo Market's 200-Plus Raw Fish Restaurants, Guryongpo Gwamegi Culture Center and Modern History Museum, Homigot

Subtitle: Jukdo Market (죽도시장), 08:00–22:00, open year-round · Guryongpo Gwamegi Culture Center (구룡포과메기문화관), 09:00–18:00, free · Guryongpo Modern History Museum (구룡포 근대문화역사관), 10:00–17:30, free · Homigot Sunrise Plaza (호미곶 해맞이광장) and Yeongildae Beach (영일대해수욕장), always open — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

For markets in Pohang, Jukdo Market in the city center is the main one. It is one of the largest traditional markets on the east coast, with raw fish restaurants gathered inside the seafood auction hall. In Guryongpo-eup (구룡포읍), Nam-gu, there is the Guryongpo Gwamegi Culture Center, which introduces gwamegi (semi-dried Pacific herring or saury), and the Guryongpo Modern History Museum, which opened in a restored Japanese-built house.

This guide covers only Jukdo Market, Guryongpo, and two seaside spots (Homigot and Yeongildae), and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). For the rest of a Pohang trip, follow the Related Content below.

🐟 Jukdo Market — 200-Plus Raw Fish Restaurants Inside the Auction Hall

Jukdo Market at 13 Jukdosijang 13-gil, Buk-gu, Pohang (포항시 북구 죽도시장13길 13), Jukdo-dong (죽도동), ☎ Jukdo Market Merchants Association 0507-1387-3776, began when street vendors set up on the reedy marshland of Pohang's inner harbor. The Jukdo Market Merchants Association was formally established in October 1969, and the market is a traditional market that takes pride in its large scale on North Gyeongsang's east coast.

  • Opening hours: 08:00–22:00, open year-round.
  • Location: 500m east of the five-way intersection at the center of Pohang is Jukdo Fish Market, the largest permanent market on the east coast.
  • How to eat raw fish: 200-plus raw fish restaurants are gathered inside the seafood auction hall. You pay only for ingredients such as chojang (spicy vinegar sauce) at a nearby shop, and you can eat right away.
  • What they sell: Seafood and dried seafood.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available.

🐠 Guryongpo Gwamegi Culture Center — The Story of Gwamegi

Guryongpo Gwamegi Culture Center at 28-8 Guryongpo-gil 117beon-gil, Guryongpo-eup, Nam-gu, Pohang (포항시 남구 구룡포읍 구룡포길117번길 28-8), ☎ 054-270-2865, introduces the gwamegi culture of Guryongpo, which was designated a gwamegi special zone in 2007, and opened in November 2017.

  • Opening hours: 09:00–18:00, last entry 17:30.
  • Closed days: Every Monday, the day after a public holiday, January 1, and Chuseok Day.
  • Admission: Free. Parking is also free.
  • What to see: A gwamegi promotion hall (origin of the name, history, and industry), the Guryongpo history hall, a 3D video experience hall, and a children's ocean experience hall.

🏠 Guryongpo Modern History Museum — The Japanese House Street

Guryongpo Modern History Museum at 153-1 Guryongpo-gil, Guryongpo-eup, Nam-gu, Pohang (포항시 남구 구룡포읍 구룡포길 153-1), ☎ 054-276-9605, is a 2-story Japanese-style wooden house built in the 1920s as a residence by Hashimoto Zenkichi (하시모토 젠기치), who came from Kagawa Prefecture, Japan. Pohang City bought and restored it in 2010 and opened it as a history museum.

  • Opening hours: 10:00–17:30, last entry 17:20. Closed every Monday.
  • Admission: Free.
  • House street: The Guryongpo Japanese House Street, where the museum stands, still has 47 Japanese-style wooden buildings. It is also the site of a painful history in which Japanese fishermen settled during the Japanese colonial period and took away Korean fishing rights. Pohang City created this street in 2010.

🌅 Two Seaside Spots — Homigot and Yeongildae

  • Homigot Sunrise Plaza (호미곶 해맞이광장) (Daebo-ri (대보리), Homigot-myeon (호미곶면), Nam-gu, Pohang (포항시 남구 호미곶면 대보리), ☎ 054-270-5855): Always open. It is the easternmost point of the Korean Peninsula, which by terrain corresponds to the tiger's tail. There is a bronze sculpture, "Hands of Coexistence" (상생의 손), with one right hand on the sea and one left hand on land, and a statue of Yeooran and Seonyeo (연오랑세오녀상), and a sunrise festival is held every year.
  • Yeongildae Beach (영일대해수욕장) (Duho-dong (두호동), Buk-gu, Pohang (포항시 북구 두호동)): Always open. It is the largest beach on the east coast, with a view of the POSCO steelworks and Yeongil Bay (영일만). There are shower rooms, changing rooms, restrooms, and parking.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Going to Guryongpo on a Monday — Both the Guryongpo Gwamegi Culture Center and the Guryongpo Modern History Museum are closed every Monday.

2. Missing the last entry time — The Gwamegi Culture Center stops admission at 17:30, and the Modern History Museum at 17:20.

3. Thinking you cannot eat Jukdo Market's raw fish right at the market — Buy sashimi at a restaurant inside the auction hall; you can also eat right away by paying only for ingredients such as chojang at a nearby shop.
