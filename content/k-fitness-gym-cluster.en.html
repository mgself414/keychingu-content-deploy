9 Day-Pass Gyms and Workouts in Seoul — Train for Just One Day in Hongdae, Itaewon, Gangnam, and Seongsu

Subtitle: 6 gyms, 2 climbing gyms, and 1 K-pop dance studio in Seoul where you pay a one-day fee with no long-term membership. They cluster around Hongik Univ. Station, Itaewon Station, and Gangnam Station, so it is easy to pick one near your hotel. For each venue we noted the one-day price, how to get in, workout clothes, towels, showers, and opening hours.

#769 · 2026-11-24 revised · Seoul Workouts

🎯 Who This Is For

This piece is for people who want to keep their workout routine going while traveling. Seoul gyms normally sell memberships by the month, but many also sell a one-day pass (day pass). We kept only the places where the day-pass or single-entry price could be confirmed on an official page or in a dated visit review. All of them show as open on Kakao Map (카카오맵) and have reviews or photos posted in October to November 2026.

⚠ How we label things: Opening hours are Kakao Map values (confirmed 2026-11-24). Where the owner registered hours by day of the week we wrote "registered by owner on Kakao Map", and where only this week's display exists we wrote "Kakao Map displayed hours". Values missing from Kakao Map come from an official page or a dated review, with the source attached. For prices we used the official page value (confirmed 2026-11-24) first, and if there was none, the value from a Naver blog visit review written after 2024-11-24. Prices may change by branch. Station exits and walking times are Kakao Map directions values.

① Hongik Univ. Station — 3 Gyms + 1 Climbing Gym

All four are a 1~2 minute walk from Hongik Univ. Station (Line 2 · AREX · Gyeongui-Jungang Line). It is the neighborhood you reach straight from Incheon Airport on AREX, so it also works for a workout on the day you arrive.

Able Gym Hongik Univ. Station (에이블짐 홍대입구역점)

A large gym on basement floor 2 of the LC Tower. The hours registered by the owner on Kakao Map are open nonstop from Monday 06:00 to Friday 24:00, and 09:00~21:00 on public holidays. Weekend hours are not on Kakao Map. A blog review (2026-01-10) gives Sat 00:00~21:00, Sun 09:00~21:00, closed on the second Sunday of each month. According to a blog review (2025-09-29), the day pass is 30,000 won, or 35,000 won with parking. You register at the info desk and then check in with an app (review 2025-04-25). Workout clothes are available by size, together with towels (same review). The review does not say whether they come with the day pass. The day-entry notice says it allows 1 entry only and that the gym is "not 24 hours". It also says you cannot use the gym without indoor shoes and that valuables should be left at the counter (review 2025-07-27). The address is 186 Yanghwa-ro, Mapo-gu (마포구 양화로 186), 1 minute from Exit 4 of Hongik Univ. Station.

Healthboy Gym Pilates Girl Hongdae (헬스보이짐필라걸 홍대점)

It runs a gym and equipment Pilates together. The hours are open nonstop from Mon 06:00 to Fri 24:00, Sat 00:00~21:00, Sun and public holidays 10:00~21:00 (registered by owner on Kakao Map). The company's official blog (2025-09-22) lists a day pass at 25,000 won (Day Pass: KRW 25,000) along with "Foreigners can use it too", written in English alongside the Korean. A 2026-09-13 review also says 25,000 won, and adds that writing a Naver review gets you a discount to 20,000 won. A 2026-06-21 review says workout clothes and towels are laid out in generous supply. The review does not say whether they come with the day pass. The address is basement floor 1, 176 Yanghwa-ro, Mapo-gu (마포구 양화로 176 지하 1층), 2 minutes from Exit 8 of Hongik Univ. Station.

Gymboxx Fitness Hongik Univ. Station (짐박스피트니스 홍대입구점)

It is on basement floors 1~2 of the Mercure Ambassador Seoul Hongdae building. The official website lists a 1-time pass (1 TIME PASS) at 15,000 won, 1 use per day, member clothes included. It is the cheapest in this piece. The official hours are weekdays 06:00~24:00, weekends 09:00~21:00, open every day of the year, and the Kakao Map displayed hours are the same. According to a 2026-02-15 review, you check in, go to the locker room, towels are provided, and the shower room has 6 stalls. The address is 144 Yanghwa-ro, Mapo-gu (마포구 양화로 144), 2 minutes from Exit 9 of Hongik Univ. Station.

The Climb Yeonnam (더클라임 연남점)

A climbing gym that specializes in bouldering, where you climb low walls without ropes. According to the official membership page, the day pass is 22,000 won (1 branch for the day). Climbing shoe rental at 3,000 won and chalk at 1,000 won are paid separately (official English page). The official Q&A says that if you have no climbing experience at all, you should go with an experienced climber or take a trial lesson before using the gym. The trial lesson at 30,000 won includes the required lesson, one day of use, and climbing shoe rental. The trial lesson is by reservation at set times for each branch, with a limit of 4 people (official Q&A). The hours registered by the owner on Kakao Map are Mon–Fri 07:30~24:00, Sat·Sun and public holidays 08:00~22:00. The official English page lists weekdays from 07:00. A blog review (2026-10-12) says it is busy even on weekday daytimes and has many foreigners. The address is 186 Yanghwa-ro, Floor 3, Mapo-gu (마포구 양화로 186 3층), in the same building as Able Gym Hongik Univ. Station. It is 1 minute from Exit 4 of Hongik Univ. Station.

② Itaewon — 2 Gyms

Itaewon Gym (이태원짐)

An above-ground gym on the 6th floor of Dongho Plaza (동호프라자). The hours registered by the owner on Kakao Map are Mon–Fri 24 hours, Sat·Sun and public holidays 09:00~21:00, and the same Kakao Map lists regular closure on the third Sunday of every month (2026-11-24). Two 2025-07 reviews say it opens at 06:00 on Monday. The day pass is 22,000 won (reviews 2025-07-29, 2025-07-31). The author of a 2025-03-05 review wrote that member clothes and a towel seem to be included in the day pass. The same review says the women's locker room and shower are on the 6th floor, and the men's are one floor below. A Kakao Map reviewer on 2026-10-22 said they would use the day pass when coming to Itaewon. The address is 8 Itaewon-ro 17-gil, Yongsan-gu (용산구 이태원로17길 8), 5 minutes from Exit 1 of Itaewon Station.

This Is Gym (디스이즈짐)

A gym on the 3rd floor facing the main road, Itaewon-ro. You do not use the ground-floor door. You climb the stone steps on the left side of the building to reach the entrance (review 2025-09-07). The hours are weekdays 06:00~23:00, Sat·Sun 10:00~18:00, public holidays 12:00~17:00 (registered by owner on Kakao Map). The day pass is 22,000 won (reviews 2025-10-20, 2025-09-07). According to the 2025-10-20 review, there are no shared workout clothes, and only a towel is provided. There is a shower room, and you need to bring your own indoor shoes. Wear your workout clothes there or pack them separately. The address is 216 Itaewon-ro, Yongsan-gu (용산구 이태원로 216), 7 minutes from Exit 3 of Itaewon Station.

③ Gangnam Station — 1 Gym + 1 Climbing Gym

Healthboy Gym Gangnam Station (헬스보이짐 강남역점)

A large gym in the basement of the Fastfive Building in front of Exit 3 of Gangnam Station. The Kakao Map displayed hours (2026-11-24) are Mon–Fri 06:00~24:00, Sat·Sun 08:00~21:00. The day pass is 25,000 won (review 2026-09-12). According to a 2025-02-23 review, the day pass includes a towel and workout clothes, and the locker is free of extra charge too. The same review says there are many shower booths and hair dryers. The address is 27 Teheran-ro 2-gil, Gangnam-gu (강남구 테헤란로2길 27), 3 minutes from Exit 3 of Gangnam Station.

The Climb Gangnam (더클라임 강남점)

It belongs to the same company as Yeonnam, so the prices and trial lesson rules are the same. The day pass is 22,000 won and climbing shoes are 3,000 won (official), and the day pass can be used at only one branch that day. The hours registered by the owner on Kakao Map are Mon–Fri 09:30~23:30, Sat·Sun and public holidays 08:00~22:00. The official English page lists weekdays from 07:00. The same page tells you to enter by the entrance below the outside stairs. The address is basement floor 1, 21 Teheran-ro 8-gil, Gangnam-gu (강남구 테헤란로8길 21 지하 1층), 8 minutes from Exit 3 of Gangnam Station.

④ Seongsu — 1 Dance Studio

1MILLION Dance Studio (원밀리언댄스스튜디오)

A dance studio known for its K-pop choreography videos. The ticket page of the official website (English, Korean, Japanese) lists 1 Class 30,000 KRW. You can buy a single ticket without a regular class pass and take one class. The Kakao Map displayed hours (2026-11-24) are Mon–Fri 16:00~22:00, Sat·Sun 15:00~21:30, and for actual classes you check the timetable on the website. According to a blog post (2026-10-10) that carries an affiliate-commission notice, the process is as follows. You sign up on the website or app, upload a profile photo showing your face, buy a ticket, and then reserve a class. At the studio you enter with the QR code in My Page. The same post says you can cancel until 60 minutes before class, and if you simply skip it the ticket is deducted. A Kakao Map English review (2026-08-02) said the space has a great atmosphere but did not suit learning a dance step by step. If it is your first time, pick a beginner class first. The address is 33 Ttukseom-ro 13-gil, Seongdong-gu (성동구 뚝섬로13길 33), 9 minutes from Exit 3 of Seongsu Station.

🗺 2 Day Combos

A. Hongdae arrival day — straight from AREX

  1. Take AREX from Incheon Airport and get off at Hongik Univ. Station
  2. At Exit 4, try bouldering with the trial lesson (30,000 won) at The Climb Yeonnam (더클라임 연남점) on floor 3 of the LC Tower. The trial lesson is by reservation and runs at set times for each branch with a limit of 4 people, so book a time ahead on the official website or Naver Reservation
  3. To add strength training, move to Able Gym (30,000 won) on basement floor 2 of the same building or Gymboxx (15,000 won) at Exit 9. A day pass is normally 1 entry per day, so it is better to finish at one place

B. Itaewon weekend morning

  1. Work out at Itaewon Gym (이태원짐), which opens at 09:00 on Sat·Sun. It is closed on the third Sunday of every month (Kakao Map)
  2. On the third Sunday, or if your morning runs late, switch to This Is Gym (디스이즈짐), which opens at 10:00. It closes at 18:00 on Sat·Sun
  3. After the workout, walk down toward Itaewon Station for lunch

⚠ Common Mistakes 3

  1. Going without indoor shoes — Able Gym Hongik Univ. Station says you cannot use the gym without indoor shoes, and a review says to bring indoor shoes to This Is Gym (디스이즈짐) as well. Pack a clean pair of sneakers that is different from the shoes you wore outside.
  2. Assuming any big neighborhood chain will do — The official Q&A of Spoany (스포애니) only explains the 1-year membership and the Prestige membership, with no day-pass information. Day passes differ by venue, so go to a place where the one-day price has been confirmed, as in this piece.
  3. Assuming weekend hours are the same as weekdays — Gyms that stay open all night on weekdays often close at 21:00 on weekends. This Is Gym (디스이즈짐) closes at 18:00 on Sat·Sun. Able Gym Hongik Univ. Station is closed on the second Sunday of each month, and Itaewon Gym (이태원짐) on the third Sunday.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. Do I need a passport? — The official information for the 9 places in this piece says nothing about requiring a passport to buy a day pass. For 1MILLION, a blog post that carries an affiliate-commission notice says you upload a profile photo showing your face when you sign up.
  2. Can I go without workout clothes? — The places confirmed to include workout clothes in the day pass are Gymboxx (official, member clothes included) and Healthboy Gym Gangnam Station (review 2025-02-23). For Itaewon Gym (이태원짐), a review author wrote that they seem to be included. This Is Gym (디스이즈짐) provides only a towel. At climbing gyms, bring long pants and socks as the official guide says.
  3. It's my first time climbing. Is it OK to buy just a day pass? — The official Q&A of The Climb says that if you have no experience at all, you should come with an experienced climber or take a trial lesson before using the gym. The trial lesson (30,000 won) includes one day of use and climbing shoe rental. By the official standard, anyone in middle school year 2 (age 15) or older can use it.
