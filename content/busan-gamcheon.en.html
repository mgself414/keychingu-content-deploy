Busan Gamcheon Culture Village·Sanbok-doro — 10 Spots: Visiting-Hour Rules, the 168 Stairs Elevator, the Museum and the UN Cemetery

Subtitle: For first-time visitors to Busan, this guide groups 10 spots in 3 zones: Gamcheon, the Choryang hillside road, and Daeyeon·Bujeon. Gamcheon Culture Village was designated a Special Management Area on 2026-08-01, and its notice gives the visiting hours for the densely residential area as 09:00–18:00. Beside the Choryang 168 Stairs there is an inclined elevator, "Choryang 168 Stairs Haneul-gil". Busan Museum is closed on Mondays and free. The UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea closes at 17:00 as of 2026-11-24. Hours and fares are the values seen on 2026-11-24.

ISSUE 06 · #245 · 2026-11-24 · Busan Gamcheon·Sanbok-doro

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 10

This list is made for foreign travelers visiting Busan for the first time. Use it on a day when you want to see Busan's hillside neighborhoods and the traces of the Korean War (한국전쟁), not the seaside. Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을) and Choryang Ibagu-gil (초량 이바구길) are both walks through neighborhoods where houses are stacked up the slope. The museum and the UN Memorial Cemetery in Daeyeon-dong (대연동) are on the same road.

It is divided into 3 zones.

  • Gamcheon zone (2 spots): Gamcheon Culture Village, Ami-dong Tombstone Culture Village (아미동 비석문화마을).
  • Choryang Sanbok-doro zone (5 spots): Choryang Ibagu-gil, 168 Stairs · Choryang 168 Stairs Haneul-gil (168계단·초량168계단 하늘길), Ibagu Gongjakso (이바구공작소), Yu Chi-hwan's Mailbox (유치환의 우체통), Choryang Chinatown (Shanghai Street) (초량 차이나타운(상해거리)).
  • Daeyeon·Bujeon zone (3 spots): Busan Museum (부산박물관), UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea (유엔기념공원), Busan Citizens Park (부산시민공원).

The first thing to read in this issue is the Special Management Area (특별관리지역) rule of Gamcheon Culture Village. Rules with dates attached have appeared for visiting hours, vehicle traffic and group visits. They are collected in a separate section right below.

Gamcheon Culture Village and Ami-dong Tombstone Culture Village are neighborhoods where residents still live. Of the 10 spots, the UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea is a place of remembrance. For all three, conditions are attached to how you look around. They are written in the entry for each.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours, fares, distances and directions are values read on 2026-11-24. Some walking times from stations are Kakao Map (카카오맵) directions values, and the line itself says "Kakao Map". Fares are for adults. Opening hours, fares and bus routes can change.

📌 First, the Gamcheon Culture Village Special Management Area — Rules by Date

Gamcheon Culture Village was designated and publicly notified as a Special Management Area on 2026-08-01. What follows are the values written in the notice on the official Gamcheon Culture Village website (issued by Saha-gu, read 2026-11-24).

What the notice says

  • Visiting hours: For the densely residential area, tourist visiting hours are 09:00–18:00.
  • Zoning: The guide map in the notice manages the village in divisions. The densely residential area has visiting hours, the mixed residential-commercial area has etiquette guidance and photo-zone order management, and the area of interest is monitored.
  • Vehicle traffic restriction: On part of Gamnae 2-ro (감내2로), the stretch of about 1 km from the information center entrance to the "Shadow of Memory" photo zone, the notice restricts the passage of vehicles carrying tourists from its enforcement date (2027-01-01), 09:00–18:00. Taxis are included.
  • Group visits: Group visitors of 10 or more people must buy the village's official map. The notice gives the enforcement date of this item as 2026-08-01.
  • Large buses: Operated by advance reservation.
  • Behavior being managed: Illegal parking and stopping, dumping of trash, noise and invasion of privacy.

What to do if you go alone or in a small group

  • Go and return within 09:00–18:00. The information center's operating hours are also 09:00–18:00. If your trip is after 2027-01-01, put these hours into your schedule as they are.
  • Go by village bus. Vehicles carrying tourists, including taxis, are restricted on this stretch. For the way there, use the village buses Seogu 2, Seogu 2-2 and Sahagu 1-1 that the official website guides to.
  • If 10 or more of you go together, buy the village's official map. The map is sold at the information center.
  • Groups going by large bus should reserve in advance.

Visiting hours, vehicle traffic, map purchase and bus reservation are rules. Lowering your voice in the alleys and not photographing front gates are manners, written in the section below. Read the two separately.

🚦 Before You Walk — Shared Safety and Manners

  • Gamcheon and Ami-dong are neighborhoods where residents live. For Ami-dong Tombstone Culture Village, Visit Busan wrote "Actual residents live here, so please look around quietly." The official Gamcheon Culture Village introduction also asks visitors to respect residents' daily lives.
  • Alley manners (this issue's recommendation): The notice lists noise and invasion of privacy as things being managed. What follows is this issue's recommendation. It is better to lower your voice in the alleys. We suggest not putting front gates, the inside of windows or laundry hung out to dry into your photos. It is better not to stand blocking someone's front door to take photos. We suggest taking your trash with you.
  • There are many slopes and stairs. Gamcheon Culture Village is a terraced residential area on the slope of Cheonmasan Mountain (천마산), and Choryang Ibagu-gil has the 168 Stairs. Wear sneakers.
  • Watch for Mondays. Busan Museum, Ibagu Gongjakso and Yu Chi-hwan's Mailbox are closed on Mondays.
  • You cannot enter the UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea wearing slippers or flip-flops. Neat clothing is recommended.
  • Call 119 for accidents or emergencies and 112 to report a crime.

① Gamcheon Zone — 2 Spots

This is the zone you go up to by village bus from Subway Line 1 Toseong Station and Jagalchi Station. Gamcheon Culture Village and Ami-dong Tombstone Culture Village are close to each other, and in its Tombstone Culture Village guide Visit Busan also says Gamcheon Culture Village is adjacent.

Gamcheon Culture Village

  • What it is: According to the official Gamcheon Culture Village introduction, it is a hillside village (산복마을) formed when Korean War refugees settled. Its present form was in place when the Taegukdo religious community (태극도 신앙촌) was built in the 1950s. Houses were built in terraces on the slope of Cheonmasan Mountain, in a structure that does not block the sunlight and view of the houses behind.
  • What led to its present look: The 2009 Village Art Project (마을미술프로젝트) of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism (문화체육관광부).
  • Information center: 09:00–18:00, open year-round. The address is 203 Gamnae 2-ro, Saha-gu (사하구), Busan, and the phone number is 051-204-1444. It gives tourist information, sells the alley tour map, runs a luggage storage room, and handles village guide (docent) tours.
  • Visiting hours: For the densely residential area, 09:00–18:00 (notice on the official Gamcheon Culture Village website). Read the Special Management Area section above first.
  • What to see: The official website introduces the Haneulmaru Observatory (하늘마루 전망대) (051-202-5557), the Small Museum (작은박물관), the Little Prince and Desert Fox sculpture, and the souvenir shop below it, "Asteroid B612" (소행성 B612).
  • Spending that stays in the village: The official website says the profits of facilities run by the residents' council go back to residents' welfare and the community. The Asteroid B612 souvenir shop is one such facility.
  • Experience programs: Experiences such as pottery and woodworking run by reservation 09:00–16:00.
  • How to get there (official website guide):
  • From Subway Line 1 Toseong Station Exit 6 (Pusan National University Hospital, 부산대병원), transfer to village bus Seogu 2, Seogu 2-2 or Sahagu 1-1.
  • From Subway Line 1 Jagalchi Station Exit 1 (Chungmu Market stop, 충무시장), transfer to village bus Seogu 2, Seogu 2-2 or Sahagu 1-1.
  • City bus 61 and village buses Seogu 2, Seogu 2-2, Sahagu 1 (사하구1) and Sahagu 1-1 run there.
  • If you go by car: The official website says village parking is short and recommends public transport. The public parking lots have 78 spaces and 40 spaces. Large buses must reserve. On part of Gamnae 2-ro, from the notice's enforcement date (2027-01-01), the passage of vehicles carrying tourists is restricted 09:00–18:00.

Ami-dong Tombstone Culture Village

  • What it is: According to Visit Busan, it is a hillside that was a Japanese cemetery during the Japanese colonial period (일제강점기). During the Korean War, refugees built houses here and it became a village, using the cemetery's tombstones as stair treads and as foundation stones of houses. You can see the tombstones used in the stair treads and the house foundation stones.
  • Hours · fare: Operates daily and is open year-round. Free.
  • Address: 49 Ami-ro (아미로), Seo-gu (서구), Busan.
  • How to get there (Visit Busan guide):
  • About a 15 minute walk from Exit 8 of Subway Line 1 Toseong Station.
  • Take village bus Sahagu 1-1 or Seogu 2 and get off at "Sansang Church (Tombstone Culture Village)" (산상교회(비석문화마을)).
  • By Kakao Map directions it is 770 m from Toseong Station Exit 8, a 20 minute walk.
  • Parking: Amigol Public Parking Lot (아미골 공영주차장).
  • How to look around: Visit Busan wrote "Actual residents live here, so please look around quietly." When you look closely at the tombstones, it is better not to point your camera toward the inside of the houses.
  • How to combine it: The same village bus numbers as Gamcheon Culture Village (Sahagu 1-1, Seogu 2) appear in the guides for both spots. Combine them into one half day on the same day.

② Choryang Sanbok-doro Zone — 5 Spots

All 5 spots are reached on foot from Busan Station. Ibagu-gil is a walking course that starts at Busan Station Exit 7, and the 168 Stairs, Ibagu Gongjakso and Yu Chi-hwan's Mailbox come up in order on that course. Chinatown is right in front of Busan Station. Sanbok-doro (hillside road) is the road on the hillside above the old downtown.

Choryang Ibagu-gil

  • Course (official Busan Dong-gu Office guide): It starts from Subway Line 1 Busan Station Exit 7. With the Namseon Warehouse (site) (남선창고(터)) as the starting point, it is 2.67 km, 56 minutes in total.
  • Order: Former Baekje Hospital (구 백제병원) · Namseon Warehouse (site) → Wall Gallery (담장갤러리) · Dong-gu Figures History Wall (동구 인물사담장) → 168 Stairs · Kim Min-bu Observatory (김민부 전망대) · 168 The Deck (168 더 데크) → Myeongran Brand Institute (명란브랜드연구소) → Ibagu Gongjakso → Mangyang-ro Sanbok-doro Exhibition Hall (망양로 산복도로 전시관) → Jang Gi-ryeo Memorial Danasum Center (장기려기념 더나눔센터) → Ibagu Camp (이바구 캠프) → Yu Chi-hwan's Mailbox.
  • Nature of the path: It starts in front of flat Busan Station and climbs up to Sanbok-doro, the road on the hillside. The course is 2.67 km, taking 56 minutes (Dong-gu Office guide). The time to go inside Ibagu Gongjakso and Yu Chi-hwan's Mailbox is added separately.
  • Day of the week: Ibagu Gongjakso and Yu Chi-hwan's Mailbox on the course are closed on Mondays and public holidays. Pick one day from Tuesday to Sunday.

168 Stairs · Choryang 168 Stairs Haneul-gil

  • What it is: The third point on the Ibagu-gil course. In the middle of the stairs are Kim Min-bu Observatory and 168 The Deck.
  • The ride that is there now: The official Busan Dong-gu Office guide introduces the facility beside the stairs as an inclined elevator, "Choryang 168 Stairs Haneul-gil", and writes that it is a new landmark of Ibagu-gil.
  • Monorail: The official Dong-gu Office guide introduces the facility beside the stairs as the inclined elevator "Choryang 168 Stairs Haneul-gil". Do not plan a monorail ride.
  • Planning: Plan your itinerary on the basis of walking the stairs up and down.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is 543 m from Busan Station Exit 7, a 13 minute walk.

Ibagu Gongjakso

  • What it is: The fifth point on the Ibagu-gil course. You come to it after the 168 Stairs and the Myeongran Brand Institute.
  • Operating hours (official Busan Dong-gu Office guide): Tue–Sat 10:00–19:00, Sun 09:00–18:00.
  • Closed: Mondays, public holidays.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is 737 m from Busan Station Exit 7, a 19 minute walk.

Yu Chi-hwan's Mailbox

  • What it is: The last point on the Ibagu-gil course. It has an observatory.
  • Operating hours (official Busan Dong-gu Office guide): Tue–Sat 10:00–19:00, Sun 09:00–18:00.
  • Closed: Mondays, public holidays. The observatory is always open. Even if you go on a closed day, you can look down from the observatory.
  • How to get there: From Busan Station Exit 7, walk the Ibagu-gil course to the end. You come to it after passing Ibagu Gongjakso, Mangyang-ro Sanbok-doro Exhibition Hall, Jang Gi-ryeo Memorial Danasum Center and Ibagu Camp in order.

Choryang Chinatown · Shanghai Street

  • What it is: According to the official Busan Dong-gu Office guide, if you cross the main road in front of Busan Station you come to the red paifang gate (패루) called Shanghai Gate (상해문), and inside it is Shanghai Street (상해거리).
  • History (official Busan Dong-gu Office guide):
  • In 1884 the Qing dynasty consulate opened and the area became Cheonggwan Street (청관거리).
  • After Busan and Shanghai became sister cities on 1993-08-24, it got the name "Shanghai Street".
  • From 2007 to 2023 it was a "Chinatown Special Zone". The special zone has now been lifted.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, Shanghai Gate is 40 m from Busan Station Exit 1, a 1 minute walk.
  • How to combine it: Ibagu-gil is on the Busan Station Exit 7 side and Shanghai Gate on the Exit 1 side. Stop by before you walk Ibagu-gil or after you come back down. It also suits the time left before your train.

③ Daeyeon·Bujeon Zone — 3 Spots

Busan Museum and the UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea are both reached by leaving Subway Line 2 Daeyeon Station through Exit 3. Their addresses are also on the same road (63 Yuenpyeonghwa-ro, 93 Yuenpyeonghwa-ro; 유엔평화로). From Busan Station, bus 134 goes to both. Busan Citizens Park is in Yeonji-dong (연지동), Busanjin-gu (부산진구), and by Kakao Map directions it is a 12 minute walk from Bujeon Station Exit 2.

Busan Museum

  • Viewing hours (official Busan Museum website, 2026-11-24): Tue–Sun 09:00–18:00. Entry closes at 17:00.
  • Closed: Every Monday and January 1. If Monday is a public holiday, it is closed on the next weekday.
  • Admission: Free.
  • Address: 63 Yuenpyeonghwa-ro, Nam-gu (남구), Busan.
  • How to get there (official website guide):
  • About a 10 minute walk from Exit 3 of Subway Line 2 Daeyeon Station toward UN Rotary (UN로터리). By Kakao Map directions it is 690 m, 11 minutes.
  • Take bus 134 (via Busan Station and Nampo-dong), 51 or 68 and it is about a 5 minute walk after you get off.
  • Take bus 138, 138-1 or 1006 and get off at the "Busan Museum·Busan Cultural Center" stop (시립박물관·부산문화회관) for a 2 minute walk.
  • Inquiries: 051-610-7111.
  • Note: Kakao Map lists it as daily 09:00–18:00 (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-24). The museum's official website gives it as closed on Mondays. Follow the official website.

UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea

  • What it is: A cemetery where UN soldiers killed in the Korean War rest. The park's official guide says this is a place of remembrance and not a space for strolling or relaxing.
  • Opening hours (UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea official guide, 2026-11-24): It is open 365 days a year with no closing days.
Season Opening time Last entry Closing time
Winter (Oct–Apr) 09:00 16:45 17:00
Summer (May–Sep) 09:00 17:45 18:00
  • Now: November is in the winter season. You must enter by 16:45, and it closes at 17:00.
  • Admission: None.
  • Visiting etiquette (official guide):
  • Reverent and neat clothing is recommended.
  • You cannot enter wearing slippers or flip-flops.
  • You cannot eat food inside the park or bring it in. Eat food at the Sculpture Park (조각공원), the Peace Park (평화공원) and the Cultural Center plaza (문화회관 광장).
  • Personal commemorative photos are allowed.
  • Filming for public or public-interest purposes, including YouTube and drone filming, must be arranged with the management office. Commercial filming is not permitted.
  • Address: 93 Yuenpyeonghwa-ro, Nam-gu, Busan.
  • How to get there (official guide):
  • About a 15 minute walk from Exit 3 of Subway Line 2 Daeyeon Station.
  • From Busan Station, take bus 134 and get off at "UN Park" (유엔공원).
  • From Seomyeon (서면), take bus 68.
  • If you go by car: Visitors' cars are free for 1 hour 30 minutes.
  • Foreign-language information: In addition to Korean, the park website lets you choose English, French and Turkish screens.

Busan Citizens Park

  • Operating hours (official Busan Citizens Park guide, 2026-11-24): Year-round 05:00–24:00.
  • Fare: Open free of charge. Rental facilities and the parking lot are paid.
  • Hayalia Lawn Plaza (하야리아잔디광장): It is open Tuesday to Sunday from April through January of the following year, and you cannot enter on Mondays. In February and March it is fully closed because the grass is sprouting. As of 2026-11-24, it is open Tuesday to Sunday.
  • Location: Yeonji-dong, Busanjin-gu, Busan.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is 710 m from Exit 2 of Donghae Line Bujeon Station, a 12 minute walk.
  • How to combine it: It is open the latest of this issue's 10 spots. Put it in the evening, after the museum and the UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea have closed.

🗺 Routes — Half Day and Full Day

The order is set to match each spot's opening and closing times. The durations the agencies give are 56 minutes for the Ibagu-gil course, about 15 minutes on foot from Toseong Station Exit 8 to Tombstone Culture Village, about 10 minutes from Daeyeon Station Exit 3 to the museum, and about 15 minutes to the UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea.

Half Day A — Gamcheon Culture Village and Ami-dong (any day of the week)

  1. Subway Line 1 Toseong Station Exit 6 or Jagalchi Station Exit 1 → village bus Seogu 2, Seogu 2-2 or Sahagu 1-1 → Gamcheon Culture Village.
  2. Get tourist information at the information center (from 09:00). If you have bags, leave them at the luggage storage room.
  3. See Haneulmaru Observatory, the Small Museum, and the Little Prince and Desert Fox sculpture.
  4. Village bus Sahagu 1-1 or Seogu 2 → "Sansang Church (Tombstone Culture Village)" → Ami-dong Tombstone Culture Village.
  5. From Tombstone Culture Village to Toseong Station Exit 8, about a 15 minute walk (Visit Busan guide).

The densely residential area of Gamcheon Culture Village is visited 09:00–18:00. Start this half day in the morning.

Half Day B — Choryang Ibagu-gil (Tue–Sun)

  1. Busan Station Exit 7 → Former Baekje Hospital · Namseon Warehouse (site) → Wall Gallery.
  2. The 168 Stairs. Rest at Kim Min-bu Observatory and 168 The Deck.
  3. Ibagu Gongjakso (Tue–Sat 10:00–19:00, Sun 09:00–18:00).
  4. Follow the course to Yu Chi-hwan's Mailbox. Look down from the observatory.
  5. Come down toward Busan Station for Shanghai Gate and Shanghai Street.

The course is 2.67 km and takes 56 minutes (Dong-gu Office guide). Tue–Sat, Ibagu Gongjakso opens at 10:00. If you start earlier than that, you will wait at step 3.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Thinking of Gamcheon Culture Village as a place to go at any time — Gamcheon Culture Village was designated a Special Management Area on 2026-08-01. The visiting hours for the densely residential area are 09:00–18:00, and the notice's enforcement date is 2027-01-01. On part of Gamnae 2-ro, a stretch of about 1 km, vehicles carrying tourists, including taxis, are restricted 09:00–18:00 from the notice's enforcement date (2027-01-01). Do not plan to go after dark to photograph the alleys, or to take a taxi deep into the village.
  2. Looking for a monorail at the 168 Stairs — The official Dong-gu Office guide introduces the facility beside the stairs as the inclined elevator "Choryang 168 Stairs Haneul-gil". Do not plan a monorail ride. Plan on walking up the stairs and match your shoes.
  3. Thinking of the UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea as a park for strolling — The UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea is a place of remembrance. You cannot enter wearing slippers or flip-flops, and you cannot eat food inside the park. In November, entry closes at 16:45 and it closes at 17:00. If you are looking for a place to walk and rest late, go to Busan Citizens Park, which is open 05:00–24:00.

⚙ 3 FAQs

  1. What time should I go to Gamcheon Culture Village? Do I have to buy a map even if I go alone? — Go and return within 09:00–18:00. The information center opens 09:00–18:00, and the visiting hours for the densely residential area are also 09:00–18:00. The map purchase obligation is on group visitors of 10 or more people. Right now (as of 2026-11-24) it is before the notice's enforcement date (2027-01-01). Fines attach to violations of the Gamnae 2-ro traffic restriction and start on 2028-01-01. The source is the notice on the official Gamcheon Culture Village website.
  2. What is closed if I go on a Monday? — Busan Museum, Ibagu Gongjakso and Yu Chi-hwan's Mailbox are closed on Mondays. The Hayalia Lawn Plaza of Busan Citizens Park cannot be entered on Mondays either. The places the guides list as open on Mondays are the Gamcheon Culture Village information center (open year-round), Ami-dong Tombstone Culture Village (open year-round), the UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea (open year-round) and Busan Citizens Park (year-round). The observatory at Yu Chi-hwan's Mailbox is always open.
  3. Are there places that charge admission? — Busan Museum, Ami-dong Tombstone Culture Village and Busan Citizens Park are free, and the UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea has no admission fee. Busan Citizens Park's rental facilities and parking lot are paid. At the UN Memorial Cemetery in Korea, visitors' cars can park free for 1 hour 30 minutes. The pottery and woodworking experiences at Gamcheon Culture Village run by reservation 09:00–16:00.
