⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Gamcheon (감천) = 1 hour is enough": Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을) is the #1 priority for foreigners = requires a 3-4 hour deep dive (terraced + colorful village + #1 Instagram spot) + a full day package when combined with 168 Stairs (168 계단)·Ibagu-gil (이바구길).

2. "Sanbok-ro (산복도로) = difficult": Sanbok-ro (산복도로) is foreigner-friendly + free monorail (168 Stairs (168 계단)) + English guidance + #1 foreigner favorite. Walking·shuttle OK.

3. "Gamcheon (감천) = just photos": Gamcheon (감천) = photos + colorful cafes + #1 foreigner vlog favorite + foreigner-friendly shops + authentic Korean neighborhood full package.

4. "Busan Station (부산역) Chinatown (차이나타운) = not great": Busan Station (부산역) Chinatown (차이나타운) = 1884 Korea's #1 Chinatown (차이나타운) + #1 foreigner favorite + authentic Busan inbound favorite.
