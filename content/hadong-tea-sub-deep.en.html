Hadong Hwagae, Wild Tea Museum, and 1200-year-old sub-deep selection for foreigners

Category: A Outskirts/Nature

Parents deep: #675 Traditional Tea·#676 Boseong sub

12 Hadong Hwagae and Wild Tea Museums

1. Hadong (1200 years of birthplace of Korean tea)

  • Discrimination: First cultivation of tea in 828 by King Heungdeok of Silla·Birthplace of Korean tea 1200 years·Fertilization of tea trees near Ssanggyesa Temple in Hwagae
  • kr cross: #675 Traditional Tea·#676 Boseong sub

2. Hadong Wild Tea Museum (newly opened in 2017)

  • Discrimination: 2003 Tea Culture Exhibition Hall → 2017 Wild Tea Museum reopening·Hadong tea full set·Free tea room tea ceremony experience
  • Operation: 09:00-18:00·Closed on Mondays·Admission fee 1,000 won
  • Experience: Traditional tea making·Hadong green tea tea ceremony (free)·Experience center auxiliary tools
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP Mania·Free tea ceremony 1st place

3. Ssanggyesa (Korean tea tree fertilization)

  • Discrimination: Foundation of Silla in 723, Sambeophwasang·Korea #1 Tea tree fertilization (Monument to the birthplace of Korean tea)·Buddhist temple
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Orthodox enthusiast·#509 Temple cross

4. Hadong Hwagae Market (5-day market)

  • Discrimination: Hadong Authentic 5-day Market (1st and 6th)·Jo Young-nam Song Hwagae Market·Seomjingang Riverside Mania
  • Signature: Jaecheopguk, scallion, wild greens, green tea
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiast·#380 market cross

5. Sipni Cherry Blossom Road (Korea #1 Cherry Blossom Road)

  • Discrimination: Hadong Hwagae ~ Ssanggyesa 4km cherry blossom road·Korea #1 Simni Cherry Blossom Road·Best time in the first week of April·Planted in 1928
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ·SNS Spring No. 1

6. Hadong Wild Tea Culture Festival (May)

  • Discrimination: Every May, Hadong #1 Tea Festival·Tea ceremony + tea making + tea ceremony experience·Designated as a cultural and tourism festival
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·#675 cross

7. Hwagae wild green tea signature (handmade, small quantity)

  • Difference: Hadong wild green tea = hand picked + roasted 9 times, authentic·Boseong vs. Hadong difference: Boseong = cultivated in plains·Hadong = wild in production area
  • Signature: Hwagae Ujeon·600m above sea level+ Wild tea 100,000 won~/100g
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Mania·Souvenir 1st place

8. Cheonghak-dong + Samseong Palace (Orthodox Taoism/Feng Shui)

  • Discrimination: Cheonghak-dong = Korean #1 orthodox village·Samseonggung = Korean orthodox Taoist temple·Traditional clothing·Feng Shui
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP No. 1 authentic enthusiast

9. Jiri Mountain National Park (Korea #1 hiking)

  • Discrimination: Korea #1 National Park 1967·Jiri Mountain 1915m·Cheonwangbong Peak + Nogodan Full Set·Near Hadong Hwagae
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP hiking enthusiast·#511·#539 cross

10. Seomjin River + Pyeongsa-ri (novel land)

  • Discrimination: Background of Park Kyung-ni’s novel “Land”·Choi Champandaek + Pyeongsa-ri Field·Seomjin River Bicycle Path
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP Authentic Mania·#510 day-trip cross

11. Hadong 1 night 2 days + Ssanggyesa Temple 1 night

  • Access: Seoul → Hadong KTX (Gurye Station) about 3 hours·Seoul → Hadong Bus 5 hours and 30 minutes
  • City Tour: Ssanggyesa Temple - Wild Tea Museum - Hwagae Market - Simni Cherry Blossom Road full day / 60,000 won
  • Foreigners fit: ★★★★ EN·JP 1 night stay recommended (Ssanggyesa Temple stay OK)

12. Hadong + Boseong + Gwangju tea cluster (3 cities, 2 days and 1 night)

  • Discrimination: Full set of authentic Korean tea (Hadong, the birthplace of 1200 + #1 tea plantation Boseong + near Gwangju)·KTX 1-day cluster OK
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·No. 1 tea authentic·#675·#676 cross

FAQ 6

Q1. Difference between Hadong and Boseong? A. Hadong = 1200 birthplace + production area of ​​wild green tea (600m+ above sea level) · Authentic enthusiasts·Boseong = #1 tea fields + flat land cultivation·tourism enthusiasts 1st place.

Q2. Wild Tea Museum admission fee? A. Adult 1,000 won·09:00-18:00·Closed on Mondays·Tea ceremony experience free.

Q3. When is the Sipni Cherry Blossom Road? A. Best time is the first week of April every year·About 1 week·4km trail.

Q4. Ssanggyesa Temple entrance fee? A. 4,000 won for adults·08:00-18:00·No. 1 among tea tree fertilization enthusiasts in Korea.

Q5. Hadong Wild Tea Culture Festival? A. Every May·Culture and Tourism Festival·Tea Ceremony + Tea Making Full Set.

Q6. Boseong + Hadong + Gwangju cluster? A. KTX 1 night 2 days OK·Hadong → Boseong (transfer at Gurye Station, about 1 hour) → Gwangju (KTX 1 hour).
