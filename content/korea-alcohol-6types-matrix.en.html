Korean Alcohol Matrix: 6 Types — A Complete Guide to Korean Drinking Culture, from Soju to Traditional Liquors

Subtitle: A Tourist's Guide to 6 Types of Korean Alcohol: Soju, Beer, Makgeolli, Cheongju, Traditional Liquors, and Highballs + Signature Pairings for Enthusiasts

ISSUE 03 · #828 · 2026-06-08

🎯 Why a Korean Alcohol Matrix?

A common pain point for foreign tourists regarding Korean alcohol is "only knowing soju (소주)." Korean alcohol is the #1 Korean culture with global recognition after K-drama and K-pop, and it's also the #1 pairing for hoesik (회식, company dinners), chimaek (치맥, chicken and beer), and hanjeongsik (한정식, Korean set meals). Even though it's all "Korean alcohol," soju (소주) (like Chamisul (참이슬) and Cheoeumcheoreom (처음처럼), globally recognized), maekju (맥주, beer) (Cass (카스), Terra (테라), Hite (하이트)), makgeolli (막걸리, traditional fermented rice wine), cheongju (청주, traditional clear rice wine), jeontongju (전통주, traditional Korean liquors) (Andong Soju (안동소주), Hansan Sogokju (한산소곡주), Jeonju Moju (전주 모주)), and highballs (하이볼) (whisky + soda, an MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) trend) each have different alcohol percentages, pairings, and cultural contexts. Foreigners often don't know (a) that soju (소주) has an alcohol percentage of about 16-17%, (b) the etiquette for shaking makgeolli (막걸리) and pouring drinks, (c) the ideal ratio for somaek (소맥, soju and beer mix), or (d) how to politely decline a "bomb shot" (폭탄주, poktanju). This guide provides a matrix of 6 types of Korean alcohol, along with pairing suggestions and a recommended priority for foreign visitors.

🥃 Deep Dive into 6 Types of Korean Alcohol

🍶 Soju (소주) — #1 Globally

  • Signature: Korea's #1 globally recognized alcohol + #1 pairing for hoesik (회식, company dinners), chimaek (치맥, chicken and beer), and BBQ.
  • Types:
  • Chamisul (참이슬) (JINRO) — #1 market share in Korea (Jinro (진로) founded in 1924)
  • Cheoeumcheoreom (처음처럼) — Emphasizes smoothness
  • Saero (새로) — Low-alcohol (16.5% → 12%)
  • Fruit Soju (과일 소주): Grapefruit, Peach, Green Grape, Strawberry (Tourists' #1 entry point)
  • ABV: 16~17% (low-alcohol 12~14%)
  • Tourist-Friendly: ★★★★★ (Global recognition)
  • Price: ₩4,000~6,000/bottle (restaurant) (~$3-4.5 / ~20-30 RMB / ~400-600 JPY) / ₩1,500~2,500 (pyeonuijeom (편의점, convenience store))
  • Recommended Pairings: BBQ (samgyeopsal (삼겹살, pork belly)), Chicken, Hoe (회, sliced raw fish), Gukbap (국밥, rice soup)

🍺 Maekju (맥주)

  • Signature: Korea's #1 beginner-friendly alcohol + Chimaek (치맥, chicken and beer) global trend
  • Types:
  • Cass (카스) — #1 market share in Korea (good value)
  • Terra (테라) — Clean, light
  • Hite (하이트) — Classic
  • Imported Beers: Tsingtao, Asahi, Heineken, Guinness (popular in Itaewon (이태원) pubs ★)
  • ABV: 4~5%
  • Tourist-Friendly: ★★★★★ (Global recognition)
  • Price: ₩4,000~8,000/bottle / ₩5,000~8,000/glass (draft beer)
  • Recommended Pairings: Chicken, BBQ, Hoetjip (횟집, raw fish restaurant), Bunsik (분식, Korean street food)

🍯 Makgeolli (막걸리) — #1 Traditional

  • Signature: Traditional Korean fermented rice wine, rice-based, sweet, and smooth.
  • Types:
  • Jangsu Makgeolli (장수 막걸리) — Korea's #1 brand
  • Kooksoondang (국순당) — Traditional + MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) trend
  • Neurin Maeul (느린마을) — Premium
  • Makgeolli (막걸리) Breweries: Andong (안동) and Jinju (진주) for traditional enthusiasts
  • MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) Trend Makgeolli (막걸리): Milk Makgeolli (우유 막걸리), Peach Makgeolli (복숭아 막걸리), Strawberry Makgeolli (딸기 막걸리)
  • ABV: 5~8%
  • Tourist-Friendly: ★★★★★ (Smoothness + Traditional for enthusiasts)
  • Price: ₩5,000~10,000/bottle (750ml)
  • Recommended Pairings: Kimchijeon (김치전, kimchi pancake), Haemul Pajeon (해물파전, seafood green onion pancake), Bossam (보쌈, boiled pork wraps), Nokdujeon (녹두전, mung bean pancake) (#1 on rainy days)

🍵 Cheongju (청주) — Authentic

  • Signature: Korean clear rice wine (similar to Japanese sake) · Pairs well with authentic Korean cuisine.
  • Types:
  • Kooksoondang Cheongju (국순당 청주)
  • Baekhwasubok (백화수복) — Traditional prestigious brand
  • Non-alcoholic Cheongju (무알콜 청주) — Pairs with Korean course meals
  • ABV: 13~17%
  • Tourist-Friendly: ★★★★ (Authentic · Korean course meals)
  • Price: ₩8,000~25,000/bottle
  • Recommended Pairings: Hanjeongsik (한정식, Korean set meal), Gungjung Yori (궁중요리, Korean royal court cuisine), Hoe (회, sliced raw fish), Japanese cuisine
