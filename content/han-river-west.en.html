⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Han River = Banpo (반포) only": The Han River actually has 11 parks (4 clusters: West, Central, Core, East).

2. "Gangseo (강서)·Nanji (난지) = too far": Gangseo Hangang Park (강서 한강공원) is adjacent to Gimpo Airport (김포공항) (#175), making it a favorite for foreign enthusiasts.

3. "Haneul Park (하늘공원) = inaccessible": Haneul Park (하늘공원) (Nanji (난지)) is foreigners' #1 spot for nature.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Foreigners' #1 Priority for Han River West: The 4 parks of Gangseo (강서)·Nanji (난지)·Mangwon (망원)·Yanghwa (양화).

2. Foreigners' #1 Cycling Spot: Mangwon (망원)·Gangseo (강서) (#149).

3. Foreigners' Camping·BBQ: Gangseo (강서)·Nanji (난지) (foreigner-friendly).

4. Foreigners' Night View: Yanghwa (양화) Han River bridges.
