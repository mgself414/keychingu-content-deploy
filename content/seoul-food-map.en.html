⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Korean food = Tteokbokki (떡볶이)·Chicken·BBQ only" — Unaware of Michelin·fermented·seasonal Korean cuisine: First-time foreign visitors often perceive Korean food as only street food and BBQ. In reality, there's a diverse range, including Michelin 3-star Mingoo's (밍글스) + 1-star Hansikgonggan (한식공간)·Onjium (온지음) + Korean fermentation (Kimchi (김치)·Doenjang (된장)·Gochujang (고추장)) and seasonal dishes (Bomnamul (봄나물, spring greens)·summer Patbingsu (팥빙수, red bean shaved ice)·autumn Gotgam (곶감, dried persimmon)·winter Tteokguk (떡국, rice cake soup)). For stays of 1 week or more, we recommend a full experience of 4 axes: Michelin once + Market + BBQ + Street Food.

2. "Gastronomy = Myeongdong (명동)·Hongdae (홍대) only" — Not utilizing the 8-category full experience: A common pattern is to stay in Myeongdong (명동)·Hongdae (홍대) and repeatedly eat Korean BBQ. In reality, there are category signatures by area, such as Itaewon (이태원) (International·Halal) + Gwangjang (광장)·Noryangjin (노량진) (Market) + Cheongdam (청담) (Michelin) + Jongno 3-ga (종로3가) (Pojangmacha (포장마차, street food tent/stall)) + Nowon (노원) (value BBQ #179). For a 1-week course, a full experience of the area + category matrix is recommended.

3. "Gastronomy = skip solo dining" — Unaware of companion·time-specific signatures: Korean BBQ·Hanjeongsik (한정식) are signature experiences for 2-4 people (not recommended for solo dining, or honbap (혼밥) in Korean) + Gwangjang Market (광장시장)·Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진 수산시장) are signature for lunch·dawn (not dinner) + Michelin requires reservations 1-3 months in advance. Prior awareness of the time·companion matrix is recommended.

Seoul Food Map Landscape

Seoul boasts the #1 diversity in gastronomic routes among OECD capital cities — with 5,000 years of Korean cuisine + multinational options from Japan, China, Southeast Asia, Western, Middle Eastern, and Indian cuisines + 2 Michelin 3-star restaurants and 30+ 1-2 star restaurants + a full set of markets like Gwangjang (광장), Tongin (통인), Noryangjin (노량진) + K-BBQ signatures + street food + an abundance of cafes·bakeries·desserts. A full experience is difficult even with a 1-week stay — repeated visits across all four seasons are recommended.

5,000 Years of Korean Cuisine: Kimchi (김치)·Doenjang (된장)·Gochujang (고추장)·fermentation + seasonal dishes (Bomnamul (봄나물, spring greens)·summer Patbingsu (팥빙수, red bean shaved ice)·autumn Gotgam (곶감, dried persimmon)·winter Tteokguk (떡국, rice cake soup)) + regional signatures (Jeonju Hanjeongsik (전주 한정식, full course meal)·Gangwon-do Makguksu (강원도 막국수, buckwheat noodles)·Busan Dwaeji Gukbap (부산 돼지국밥, pork soup with rice)·Jeju Heukdwaeji (제주 흑돼지, black pork)) + Korean fine dining Michelin.

Multinational Full Set: Itaewon (이태원)·Hannam (한남)·Haebangchon (해방촌) with USFK·American·Mexican·Indian·Middle Eastern·African cuisines + Hannam (한남)·Cheongdam (청담)·Gangnam (강남) with Western·Italian·French·Japanese cuisines + Dongdaemun (동대문) with Central Asian·Southeast Asian + Magok (마곡) with LG·diverse foreign residents.

Michelin Full Set: Michelin Guide Korea 2024 — 2 3-star restaurants (Mingoo's (밍글스)·La Yeon (라연)) + 30+ 1-2 star restaurants. Korean fine dining + Western + Japanese + Korean fermentation·seasonal + dessert clusters.

Market Full Set: Gwangjang Market (광장시장) (foreigner #1·day·evening) + Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진 수산시장) (dawn 02:00-06:00) + Dongdaemun Night Market (동대문 새벽 시장) (22:00-04:00 clothing + street food) + Tongin Market (통인시장) Dosirak (도시락) Cafe (11:00-17:00) + Namdaemun Market (남대문 시장) (goods·food ingredients) + Majang Meat Market (마장 축산물시장) (wholesale meat).

K-BBQ Signature: Saemaul Sikdang (새마을식당)·Hanam Pig House (하남돼지집)·Nolboo Bossam (놀부보쌈)·Wang Pig (왕피그)·Junggye Gogipati (중계고기파티) (#179) + Galbi (갈비, ribs)·Samgyeopsal (삼겹살, pork belly)·Chadol (차돌, beef brisket)·LA Galbi (LA갈비, LA-style short ribs)·Usamgyeop (우삼겹, thinly sliced beef belly). Value for foreigners at ₩25,000-50,000 per person.

Street Food·Pocha (포차): Myeongdong (명동) Street Food 18:00-24:00 (Hotteok (호떡)·Bungeoppang (붕어빵)·Kkochi (꼬치)) + Jongno 3-ga (종로3가) Pojangmacha (포장마차) Street (Jeon (전)·Guksu (국수)·Kkochi (꼬치) + Soju (소주)·Makgeolli (막걸리), K-drama scenery) + Euljiro Nogari Alley (을지로 노가리골목) (Nogari (노가리, dried pollack snack) + draft beer yajang (야장, outdoor dining area)).

Cafe·Dessert: London Bagel Museum (런던베이글뮤지엄) (last year's ₩93.8 billion sales) + Cafe Onion (카페 어니언) (1920s Hanok (한옥)) + Samcheong-dong (삼청동)·Bukchon (북촌)·Seochon (서촌) Hanok (한옥) cafes + Seongsu (성수)·Hongdae (홍대) MZ trends + Korean desserts (bread·Tteok (떡, rice cake)·cookies·fruit).

Recommended Itineraries

[A] Seoul Food Map Full Package (1 Week)

Day 1: Gwangjang Market (광장시장) lunch + Myeongdong (명동) Hanjeongsik (한정식) dinner

Day 2: Itaewon (이태원) Halal + American·Mexican (afternoon·evening)

Day 3: Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진 수산시장) dawn seafood (02:00-06:00) → Gwangjang Market (광장시장) (daytime)

Day 4: Mingoo's (밍글스) or Hansikgonggan (한식공간) (Michelin enthusiast dinner)

Day 6: Saemaul Sikdang (새마을식당) BBQ + Junggye Gogipati (중계고기파티) (#179 Nowon (노원))

Day 7: Cafe Onion (카페 어니언) + London Bagel (morning·lunch) + Samcheong (삼청) Hanok (한옥) Cafe (afternoon) + Tongin (통인) Dosirak (도시락) (evening)

  • Recommendation: The classic full gastronomic experience for a 1-week foreign visit.

[D] Market·Street Food Enthusiast (2 Days)
