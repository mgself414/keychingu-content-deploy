🥗 Diets — Halal, Vegetarian and 1330

  • Halal (VisitSeoul official): Seoul has just one Korea Muslim Federation (KMF)-certified Korean restaurant, Eid. The area around Seoul Central Mosque (서울중앙성원) (39 Usadan-ro 10-gil) in Itaewon (이태원) is the street of halal and Muslim-friendly restaurants (Makan Halal, Kervan, Mr. Kebab and others are named on the page). The older guide's "30+ KMF-certified cluster" was wrong — certified and friendly are not the same thing.
  • Vegetarian (VisitKorea official): state your restrictions before ordering, and watch for stew broth, egg in gimbap and meat in bibimbap. Restaurant names (Maji (마지), Sanchon (산촌), Balwoo Gongyang (발우공양) and others) are secondary.
  • 1330: its existence is official. The language list (English, Japanese, Chinese and Russian 24 hours; Vietnamese, Thai and Indonesian 08:00–19:00) is secondary.

🗺 Two Routes

  • Day A — Markets: Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역) Exit 2 → Tongin Market Dosirak Cafe (통인시장 도시락카페) (opens at 11:00, closed Mondays and the third Sunday) → Jongno 5-ga Station (종로5가역) and the Gwangjang Market (광장시장) food alley (open until evening) → after dark, Jongno 3-ga Pocha Street (종로3가 포장마차거리).
  • Day B — Night to dawn: Namdaemun Market (남대문시장) from Hoehyeon Station (회현역) (late hours on weekdays) → Dongdaemun Market (동대문시장) 24 hours → Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진수산시장) auction 01:00–04:00 (note the "closed Sundays" listing).
  • Sundays: the Gwangjang Market (광장시장) main market and Namdaemun Market (남대문시장) are closed, and the Tongin cafe is closed on the third Sunday → head for the food alley, Myeongdong (명동) or Dongdaemun Market (동대문시장).

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Going to a market on a Sunday — the Gwangjang Market (광장시장) main market and Namdaemun Market (남대문시장) are closed, and the Tongin cafe is closed on the third Sunday. Go to the food alley, Dongdaemun Market (동대문시장) or Myeongdong (명동).

2. Treating Noryangjin as dawn-only — it runs 24 hours. Only the auction is 01:00–04:00.

3. Visiting Gwangjang only at lunch — the food alley runs 09:00–23:00.

4. Expecting "30 KMF-certified places" — there is one certified Korean restaurant (Eid). The rest are Muslim-friendly.

5. Trusting the old price ranges — none are officially posted. Only the Bib Gourmand's under ₩45,000 is official.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. A half day on your first day? — Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역) Exit 2, Tongin Market Dosirak Cafe (통인시장 도시락카페) (11:00–15:00) → the Gwangjang Market (광장시장) food alley.

2. How much does it cost? — Market and street food prices are not posted officially. The Bib Gourmand benchmark is under ₩45,000 per person · BBQ YUL 30,000–50,000 won per person (posted on VisitSeoul).

3. Halal? — The area around Seoul Central Mosque (서울중앙성원) in Itaewon (이태원). The KMF-certified Korean restaurant is Eid.

4. Vegetarian? — Tell staff before ordering and watch the broth (VisitKorea). 1330.

5. How many Michelin places? — Seoul 2026: 1 three-star, 10 two-star, 31 one-star, 51 Bib Gourmand.

6. What is open 24 hours? — Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진수산시장) (auction 01:00–04:00) and Dongdaemun Market (동대문시장). Namdaemun Market (남대문시장) is weekdays 00:00–23:00.
