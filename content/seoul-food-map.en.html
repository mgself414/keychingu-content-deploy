🥗 Diet — Halal, Vegetarian, 1330

  • Halal (VisitSeoul official): Seoul has just one Korea Muslim Federation (KMF)-certified Korean restaurant, Eid. The area around the Seoul Central Mosque (서울중앙성원) (39 Usadan-ro 10-gil) in Itaewon (이태원) is the street of halal and Muslim-friendly restaurants (Makan Halal, Kervan, Mr. Kebab and others named on the page). The old guide's "KMF-certified cluster of 30+" was wrong — certified differs from Muslim-friendly.
  • Vegetarian (VisitKorea official): tell staff your restrictions before ordering, and watch for stew broth, egg in gimbap, and meat in bibimbap. Restaurant names (Maji (마지), Sanchon (산촌), Balwoo Gongyang (발우공양) and others) are from secondary sources.
  • 1330: its existence is official. The language list (English, Japanese, Chinese, Russian 24 hours; Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian 08–19) is from secondary sources.

☕ Two Cafes — Anguk (VisitKorea Official)

  • Cafe Onion Anguk (5 Gyedong-gil): weekdays 07–22 · weekends 09–22, open year-round. London Bagel Museum Anguk (20 Bukchon-ro 4-gil): 08–18, open year-round. Neither posts prices. The old lines "93.8 billion won in sales" and "a line at 7 a.m. on weekdays" have no source, so we removed them.
  • Saemaeul Sikdang (새마을식당) and Hanam Pig House (하남돼지집) (chains): official hours unverified → check each branch on a map app.

🗺 Two Routes

  • Day A — Markets: Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역) Exit 2 → Tongin Market Dosirak Cafe (opens at 11, closed Mondays and the 3rd Sunday) → Gwangjang Market Food Alley at Jongno 5-ga Station (open until evening) → after dark, Jongno 3-ga Pocha Street.
  • Day B — Night to dawn: Namdaemun Market at Hoehyeon Station (late into the night on weekdays) → Dongdaemun Market 24 hours → Noryangjin auction 01–04 (note the "closed Sundays" wording).
  • Sundays: the Gwangjang main market and Namdaemun are closed, and the Tongin cafe is closed on the 3rd Sunday → head to the Food Alley, Myeongdong and Dongdaemun.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Visiting markets on Sunday — the Gwangjang main market and Namdaemun are closed, and the Tongin cafe is closed on the 3rd Sunday. Go to the Food Alley, Dongdaemun or Myeongdong.

2. Treating Noryangjin as dawn-only — it is open 24 hours. Only the auction runs 01–04.

3. Visiting Gwangjang Market only at lunch — the Food Alley is open 09–23.

4. Expecting "30 KMF-certified restaurants" — there is one certified Korean restaurant (Eid). The rest are Muslim-friendly.

5. Trusting old price ranges — no official price is posted. Only the Bib Gourmand's under 45,000 won is official.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. A half day on the first day? — Gyeongbokgung Station Exit 2 to Tongin Market (11–15) → Gwangjang Market Food Alley.

2. How much does it cost? — Not officially posted. By the Michelin Bib Gourmand benchmark, under 45,000 won per person.

3. Halal? — The area around the Central Mosque in Itaewon. The KMF-certified Korean restaurant is Eid.

4. Vegetarian? — Tell staff before ordering and watch the broth (VisitKorea). 1330.

5. How many Michelin restaurants? — Seoul 2026: 3 Stars 1, 2 Stars 10, 1 Star 31, Bib Gourmand 51.

6. What is open 24 hours? — Noryangjin (auction 01–04) and Dongdaemun. Namdaemun is weekdays 00–23.
