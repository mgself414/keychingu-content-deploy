FAQ 6

Q1. Where is the best hiking for a foreigner’s first mountain?

  • Beginner: Dobongsan Mountain Trail (#8·2~3 hours) or Gwanaksan Lake Park + Autumn leaves reflected in the lake
  • Intermediate: Cheonggyesan Mountain Maebong (#1·3~4 hours) or Gwanaksan Mountain Summit (#4·4~5 hours)
  • Mania: Dobongsan Jaunbong (#7) or full-course course (#3·#9)

Q2. Where is the mountaineering tourism center?

  • Bukhansan: Bulgwang Station/Dobongsan Station (Gangbuk)
  • Gwanaksan: Seoul National University Station (outskirts of Gangnam)
  • Ansan: Seodaemun-gu Office Station (west of the city center)
  • Equipment rental: Hiking shoes, sticks, crampons 5,000-10,000 won
  • Multilingual: EN·JP·CN·Korean full cover

Q3. When is the peak autumn foliage hiking season?

  • Altitude 600m+: 10/27 Mon ~ 11/5 Thu (1 week, city center +3~5 days)
  • Altitude 700m+ (Dobongsan Mountain, Bukhansan Mountain): 10/25 Sat ~ 11/3 Sun (autumn leaves decrease, early at the summit)
  • best weekdays: 10/28 Tue, 29 Wed, 30 Thu (crowds
  • Backup: Fallen leaves course after 11/6 (atmosphere #1)

Tip 5

Tip 1 — Use the mountaineering tourism center (#1 among foreign enthusiasts)

  • All 3 locations are free: Bukhansan Mountain, Gwanaksan Mountain, and Ansan Mountain
  • Multilingual EN·JP·CN·Korean: Full cover
  • Equipment rental: Hiking shoes 5,000 won, sticks 5,000 won, crampons 5,000 won (small amount)
  • On-site registration: Recommended arrival at 09:00 (after lunch)
  • Dictionary: Seoul Tourism Foundation website (visitseoul.net·EN)

Tip 2 — Foreigner Safety Guide

  • **Solo
  • Freezing at summit (11/mid+): Crampons required
  • Yeonjudae Cliff (#4): Guide recommended (safe)
  • Emergency: 119 call, mountain rescue team 24 hours a day

Tip 3 — Appropriate clothing + supplies

  • Average 12°C in late October (5°C at the top, strong wind)
  • Top: 3 layers of thermal insulation (thin padding + shirt + tee)
  • Bottoms: Hiking clothes or sportswear (jeans x)
  • Shoes: Hiking shoes required (rental available)
  • What you need: 1.5L water, lunch box or gimbap, cell phone, EN map (Visit Seoul app)

Tip 4 — Weekdays vs. Weekends (crowds ↑ peak fall foliage)

  • Weekend crowds: Peak week (10/27~11/2) 5 times the pool of hiking enthusiasts
  • Best weekdays: 10/28 Tue, 29 Wed, 30 Thu (crowds
  • Worst Avoided: Sat. 10/31, Sun. 11/1 (crowds + possibility of rain)
