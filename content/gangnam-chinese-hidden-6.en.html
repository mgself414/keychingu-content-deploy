Gangnam Hidden Chinese 6 Picks for Foreigners

> 6 hidden Chinese restaurants in Gangnam (강남), Dogok (도곡), and Yeoksam (역삼) that are a great fit for foreigners — uncovered spots + spicy food fan matrix.

The Gangnam (강남) Chinese restaurant cluster showed a strong signal on Reddit (162). Among 6 establishments, 4 were previously uncovered in our content. This guide curates these 4 uncovered spots plus 2 additional foreigner-friendly selections. It differentiates itself from Itaewon (이태원) fine dining (#1094) by focusing on casual and everyday options in Gangnam (강남).

1. 4 Uncovered Restaurants (Topic Gap Anchor)

Salon de Hou Sum Dogok (살롱드호우섬 도곡점)

  • Address: Dogok-dong, Gangnam-gu (강남구 도곡동)
  • Signature: Modern Chinese + Cantonese fusion
  • Recommendation: Perfect for a foreign couple's date. Visually appealing for Instagram.

Daegabang (대가방 본점)

  • Address: Gangnam Main Branch (강남 본점)
  • Signature: Authentic Cantonese cuisine and dim sum (딤섬, Chinese bite-sized portions of food)
  • Recommendation: Great for foreign families or group gatherings. English menu available.

Hwanggeumseong Dogok (황금성 도곡)

  • Address: Dogok-dong (도곡동)
  • Signature: Authentic Korean-style Chinese restaurant (한국식 중국집)
  • Recommendation: Ideal for foreigners to experience Korean-style Chinese cuisine, such as Jjajang (짜장, black bean sauce noodles), Jjamppong (짬뽕, spicy seafood noodle soup), and Tangsuyuk (탕수육, sweet and sour pork).

Chengdu Malaxiangguo Gangnam Branch (청두마라샹궈 강남점)

  • Address: Gangnam Station (강남역)
  • Signature: Authentic Sichuan Malaxiangguo (마라샹궈, spicy stir-fry dish with numbing and spicy flavors) and Malatang (마라탕, spicy and numbing hot pot soup)
  • Recommendation: A must-visit for foreigners who love spicy food.

2. 2 Additional Foreigner-Friendly Restaurants

Jinsim Mandu Gangnam (진심만두 강남)

  • Address: Gangnam (강남)
  • Signature: Specializes in Mandu (만두, Korean dumplings) and Dim Sum (딤섬, Chinese bite-sized portions of food)
  • Recommendation: Great for foreigners looking for a light meal or snack.

Chinatown Dogok (차이나타운 도곡)

  • Address: Main Dogok-dong (도곡동)
  • Signature: Diverse menu featuring Cantonese and Sichuan cuisines
  • Recommendation: Perfect for foreigners who want to try a variety of dishes.

3. Foreigner-Friendly Tips

English Menu Availability

  • High: Daegabang (대가방), Salon de Hou Sum (살롱드호우섬), Chengdu Malaxiangguo (청두마라샹궈) (Accustomed to serving foreigners)
  • Medium-High: Jinsim Mandu (진심만두), Chinatown (차이나타운) (Picture menus available)
  • Local: Hwanggeumseong (황금성) (Primarily Korean, recommend using a translation app)

Area-Specific Itineraries

  • Gangnam Main (강남 메인): Daegabang (대가방) (Lunch) → Chengdu Malaxiangguo (청두마라샹궈) (Spicy dinner)
  • Dogok (도곡) Route: Salon de Hou Sum (살롱드호우섬) → Hwanggeumseong (황금성) (Casual) → Chinatown (차이나타운)
  • Light Meal: Jinsim Mandu (진심만두) (For solo diners (혼밥, eating alone) or snacks)

Price Range

  • Jjajang (짜장) / Jjamppong (짬뽕): ₩9,000 (~$7 / ~50 RMB / ~900 JPY) to ₩14,000
  • Malatang (마라탕) / Malaxiangguo (마라샹궈): ₩15,000 to ₩25,000
  • Cantonese Course Meal: ₩35,000 to ₩80,000
  • Fine Dining Cantonese: ₩80,000+ (Salon de Hou Sum (살롱드호우섬))
  • Reservations: Weekend evenings are recommended to book in advance via Catchtable (catchtable, a popular Korean restaurant reservation platform).

By Persona

  • Fine Dining Date: Salon de Hou Sum (살롱드호우섬) → Daegabang (대가방)
  • Spicy Food Fan: Chengdu Malaxiangguo (청두마라샹궈)
  • Family / Group: Daegabang (대가방) → Chinatown (차이나타운)
  • Solo Light Meal: Jinsim Mandu (진심만두) → Hwanggeumseong (황금성)

4. 5 Steps to Korean Gangnam Chinese Cuisine

1. First Try: Hwanggeumseong (황금성) Jjajangmyeon (짜장면, Korean black bean sauce noodles) (Introduction to Korean-style Chinese food)

2. Dim Sum: Jinsim Mandu (진심만두) (Light meal)

3. Authentic Cantonese: Daegabang (대가방) Course Meal

4. Spicy Challenge: Chengdu Malaxiangguo (청두마라샹궈) Malatang (마라탕)

5. Fine Dining Anniversary: Salon de Hou Sum (살롱드호우섬)
