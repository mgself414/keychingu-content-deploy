Seoul Dulle-gil (서울둘레길) 21 Courses Guide: 156.5 km Linking Suraksan, Achasan, Daemosan, Gwanaksan, Anyangcheon and Bukhansan, with Section, Distance and Walking Time for Each Course

Subtitle: Total length of the 21 courses 156.5 km (per the introduction) · 4.6 km~10.7 km per course, about 2 hours 5 minutes~4 hours 50 minutes · Open at all times, every day of the year · The Anyangcheon sections of Courses 13 and 14 are also usable in the evening · Information center ☎ 02-779-7902~4 — checked against Korea Tourism Organization tourism information (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Seoul Dulle-gil (서울둘레길) is a walking trail that goes all the way around Seoul. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it has a total length of 156.5 km and 21 courses, and it was made by weaving Seoul's history, culture and natural ecology into stories. There are rest facilities, book cafes and rest areas along the trail.

This guide gathers the section, distance, walking time and nearest subway stations of the 21 courses in one table. For each course, the start and end points are registered in the section column. All 21 courses are registered as open at all times, every day of the year.

The operating information is taken from the values registered in Korea Tourism Organization tourism information (TourAPI).

🥾 Courses 1~5: Suraksan, Buramsan, Yongmasan and Achasan

  • Course 1 Suraksan (수락산): The theme is "the path of people of old who did not leave the secular world". The trail goes up and down repeatedly, but it is introduced as having observation decks from which you can view the mountains surrounding Seoul.
  • Course 2 Deongneung Pass (덕릉고개): The theme is "an ecological path linking city and nature". It is a trail where you can see rocks and flowers.
  • Course 3 Buramsan (불암산): The theme is "a soft forest path embracing the sky". According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the most comfortable forest walk among the Seoul Dulle-gil (서울둘레길) courses.
  • Course 4 Mangu·Yongmasan (망우·용마산): The theme is "a path you meet after leaving the city". Yongmasan is well maintained and easy to walk, and the course is introduced as having good views.
  • Course 5 Achasan (아차산): The theme is "a path linking people and history". It has good views and many historical and cultural resources, and there is the Achasan Ecological Park (아차산 생태공원). The Achasan area is in the #740 Seoul Northeast Forest and Park Guide.

🥾 Courses 6~9: Godeoksan, Iljasan, Tancheon and Daemosan

  • Course 6 Godeoksan (고덕산): The theme is "a history path unfolding along the Han River". It is a relatively flat course.
  • Course 7 Iljasan (일자산): The theme is "a small forest path you meet in the city center". It is introduced as easy because the elevation is not high. The Iljasan area is in the #715 Gangdong Guide.
  • Course 8 Jangji·Tancheon (장지·탄천): The theme is "an urban stream path with restored natural ecology". You walk along a stream. The Tancheon area is in the #697 Yangjaecheon and Tancheon Guide.
  • Course 9 Daemo·Guryongsan (대모·구룡산): The theme is "a natural ecology excursion path". It is introduced as having low elevation, rich forest resources and good views of Seoul. At a registered distance of 10.7 km, it is the longest of the 21 courses.

🥾 Courses 10~12: Umyeonsan, Gwanaksan and Hoamsan

  • Course 10 Umyeonsan (우면산): The theme is "a path to see Seoul from within the mountain". It is introduced as having gentle slopes where you can take forest walks and forest bathing.
  • Course 11 Gwanaksan (관악산): The theme is "a path where folk belief and Buddhism meet". There are historical and cultural sites throughout. The Gwanaksan area is in the #738 Seoul Cheonggyesan and Gwanaksan Guide.
  • Course 12 Hoamsan (호암산): The theme is "a path holding folktales, geomancy and history". You can see historical and cultural sites.

🥾 Courses 13~16: Anyangcheon, Haneul Park and Bongsan

  • Course 13 Upper Anyangcheon (안양천 상류): The theme is "a cherry blossom path walking along the railway". Cherry blossoms bloom in spring. It is registered that the whole Anyangcheon course can be used in the evening, and that there are subway stations throughout.
  • Course 14 Lower Anyangcheon (안양천 하류): The theme is "a path of old Anyangcheon memories". It is a path along the stream and the Han River, with exercise facilities, walking paths, convenience facilities and rest facilities throughout. It can be used in the evening. The Anyangcheon area is in the #699 Jungnangcheon and Anyangcheon Guide.
  • Course 15 Noeul·Haneul Park (노을·하늘공원): The theme is "a restored nature path". Passing Gayang Bridge, you can look out over the Han River and Gangnam. Haneul Park and Noeul Park are in the #741 Seoul West Park Guide.
  • Course 16 Bongsan·Aengbongsan (봉산·앵봉산): The theme is "a mountain path that feels the quiet along the ridge". Steep slopes are well maintained, and there is historical heritage such as Suguksa Temple (수국사) and Seooreung Royal Tombs (서오릉).

🥾 Courses 17~21: Bukhansan Sections

It is registered that Courses 17 to 20 have sections that overlap with Bukhansan Dulle-gil (북한산 둘레길). Course 21 has no registered value for overlapping sections.

  • Course 17 Bukhansan Eunpyeong (북한산 은평): The theme is "a path touching the sky". The slope is not steep, but it is introduced as possibly a little difficult. From Seollimsa Temple (선림사), it overlaps with Bukhansan Dulle-gil (북한산 둘레길) Course 8 Gureumjeongwon-gil (구름정원길).
  • Course 18 Bukhansan Jongno (북한산 종로): The theme is "a city path where valley and mountain meet". It has a village-path atmosphere and exhibition facilities throughout. It overlaps with Bukhansan Dulle-gil (북한산 둘레길) Course 7 Yetseong-gil (옛성길) and Course 6 Pyeongchangmaeul-gil (평창마을길). It is registered that the end point of the course can be reached by bus and subway.
  • Course 19 Bukhansan Seongbuk (북한산 성북): The theme is "a quiet forest path walked in contemplation". Most of it is forest path, with a temple along the way. It overlaps with Bukhansan Dulle-gil (북한산 둘레길) Course 5 Myeongsang-gil (명상길), Course 4 Solsaem-gil (솔샘길) and Course 3 Huingureum-gil (흰구름길). It is registered that the start point of the course can be reached by subway and bus.
  • Course 20 Bukhansan Gangbuk (북한산 강북): The theme is "a path to think of the nation in front of graves". There are many sites of historical figures and of culture and arts figures. It overlaps with Bukhansan Dulle-gil (북한산 둘레길) Course 3 Huingureum-gil (흰구름길), Course 2 Sullye-gil (순례길) and Course 1 Sonamusup-gil (소나무숲길).
  • Course 21 Bukhansan Dobong (북한산 도봉): The theme is "a path with the resonance of deep history". There are several historical and cultural sites.

⚠ 4 Things to Know in Advance

  1. Walking time is not proportional to distance for each course — Course 14 Lower Anyangcheon (안양천 하류) is 10.2 km in about 2 hours 25 minutes, and Course 16 Bongsan·Aengbongsan (봉산·앵봉산) is 9.1 km in about 4 hours 15 minutes.
  2. The places registered as usable in the evening are the Anyangcheon sections of Courses 13 and 14 — all 21 courses are registered as open at all times, every day of the year.
  3. For Course 18, the subway station guidance in the registered information has the same wording as Course 1 — in the subway station column of the table, Dobongsan Station (도봉산역) and Danggogae Station (당고개역) are written as registered, and the fact that the wording is the same is noted. Whether they match the section (Bukhansan Ecological Park to Hyeongjebong Entrance) cannot be determined from the registered values alone.
  4. The total length in the introduction differs from the sum of the course distances — the introduction gives a total length of 156.5 km, and adding the registered distances of the 21 courses gives 153.5 km.

⚙ FAQ 3

Q. Is there an admission fee?

A. According to the registered Korea Tourism Organization information, all 13 courses with a registered admission fee (Courses 3, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 14~21) are free. The remaining 8 courses (Courses 1, 2, 4~8 and 13) have no admission fee registered.

Q. What are the shortest and longest courses?

A. By registered distance, Course 5 Achasan (아차산) is the shortest at 4.6 km, and Course 9 Daemo·Guryongsan (대모·구룡산) is the longest at 10.7 km.

Q. Where do I make inquiries?

A. The Seoul Dulle-gil (서울둘레길) information center, ☎ 02-779-7902~4. The registered value for Course 13 is 02-779-7902. The Seoul Dulle-gil (서울둘레길) website is gil.seoul.go.kr/gil.
