Seongsu Hidden Italian & Western 6 Picks for Foreigners

> 6 Western restaurants in Seongsu (성수) that are a perfect fit for foreign visitors — featuring uncovered hidden gems + complementing existing guides #001 and #145.

Seongsu (성수) is a district that perfectly matches the "Instagrammable hotspot" persona for foreign tourists. While the Western cuisine cluster shows strong signals (Reddit boost 102), four restaurants remain uncovered. This guide curates six spots: four hidden gems + two additional foreigner-friendly picks.

1. Uncovered Hidden Gems (4 Restaurants)

Seongsu Darak (성수다락)

  • Address: Trendy golmok (alley) in Seongsu-dong (성수동)
  • Signature: Casual Western cuisine + wine pairing
  • Recommended for: Instagrammable visuals ★★★. Perfect for a date night for foreign visitors.

Sunny Nook Seongsu (써니눅 성수)

  • Address: 5-minute walk from Seongsu Station (성수역)
  • Signature: California-style Western cuisine + brunch
  • Recommended for: A weekend brunch hub for foreign visitors. High possibility of English menus.

Omil Seongsu (오밀 성수)

  • Address: Seongsu (성수) hotspot area
  • Signature: Modern Western cuisine + cocktails
  • Recommended for: A great fit for foreign visitors seeking fine dining. Ambiance ★★★

La Morale Seongsu (라모랄 성수)

  • Address: Seongsu-dong (성수동)
  • Signature: Italian bistro
  • Recommended for: Chef's course. Perfect for foreign fans of authentic Italian cuisine.

2. Additional Foreigner-Friendly Picks (2 Restaurants)

Sleepy Punch Seongsu (슬리피 펀치 성수)

  • Address: Seongsu (성수) main area
  • Signature: Western cuisine + whiskey bar
  • Recommended for: A great fit for foreign visitors looking for nightlife. Ambiance ↑

Parallels Seongsu (패럴렐스 성수)

  • Address: Seongsu-dong (성수동)
  • Signature: Concept dining + wine
  • Recommended for: Instagram-friendly · perfect for a date night for foreign visitors.

3. Foreigner-Friendly Tips

English Menu Availability

  • High: Sunny Nook (써니눅), Omil (오밀), Sleepy Punch (슬리피 펀치) (Accustomed to serving foreign guests)
  • Medium-High: Seongsu Darak (성수다락), Parallels (패럴렐스) (Popular with Instagram tourists)
  • Local: La Morale (라모랄) (Primarily Korean-speaking staff)

Itinerary by Area

  • Seongsu (성수) Hotspot Course: Sunny Nook (써니눅) (Brunch) → Seongsu Darak (성수다락) (Lunch) → Omil (오밀) (Dinner Date)
  • Nightlife Course: Sleepy Punch (슬리피 펀치) → Parallels (패럴렐스) (Wine) → (Seongsu (성수) clubs)
  • Fine Dining Date: Omil (오밀) → La Morale (라모랄) → Parallels (패럴렐스)

Price Range

  • Brunch: ₩18,000 (~$13 / ~95 RMB / ~1,900 JPY) - ₩28,000 (~$20 / ~150 RMB / ~2,900 JPY)
  • Dinner: ₩35,000 - ₩70,000 (Courses separate)
  • Wine Pairing: +₩25,000 - ₩50,000
  • Reservations: Catchtable (캐치테이블) or Naver (네이버) (Advance booking essential for weekend evenings)
