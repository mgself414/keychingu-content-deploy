⚠ Taean’s 3 mistakes

1. "Taean = Malli Full" — Anmyeondo Mirecognition: Recognition of Malli Full. In reality, Anmyeondo Island (Korea #1 West Coast Island) + Kkotji Sunset + Natural Recreation Forest full experience.

2. "Flower Festival Every Two Years" — Season Unrecognition: Recognition of Absence. In fact, it is a full experience of Anmyeondo International Flower Expo every other year from April to May + all four seasons of spring and fall.

3. "Tidal flat = children only" — Mania Miji: Children's movements only. In reality, Sinduri coastal sand dunes, Taean mudflat experience, and octopus catching mania full experience.
