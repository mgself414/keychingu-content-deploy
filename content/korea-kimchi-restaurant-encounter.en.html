🎯 Why a guide to the introductory route to restaurant kimchi?

If [#822 6 types of kimchi matrix](#822) is the Korean kimchi signature perspective, the pain point for foreign tourists is "What kind of kimchi should I actually encounter in a restaurant and when should I handle it?" is the movement line perspective. 99% of foreigners go to a Korean restaurant on the first day they arrive in Korea, but (a) they don't know the difference between 1 to 4 types of kimchi that are served as basic side dishes, (b) they don't read the "free refills" sign, (c) they don't know the differences in kimchi by store (long-established Korean restaurant vs. snack bar vs. BBQ vs. soup restaurant), (d) they don't know the priority of kimchi pairing when ordering a menu, and (e) they don't know how to ask for "regular kimchi" when returning.

This guide includes 4 steps: Entering the restaurant → Receiving side dishes → Requesting a refill → Re-visit + Kimchi matrix by store type + Menu-Kimchi pairing table + English speaking kit.

🚪 Step 1 — Entry + Seat (Kimchi = Automatic free side dish)

5 key points for foreigners when entering

1. "How many people are you?" = How many people? — Mark OK with your finger

2. "Please sit here" = This way please — Seating instructions

3. Basic side dishes automatically provided = 1 to 5 kinds of kimchi are automatically provided — **Separate request

4. Water/wet towel automatic = Separate charge

5. English menu = English is OK for main restaurants in Myeongdong, Itaewon, Hongdae, Gangnam, and Jongno.

Kimchi served as soon as you sit down (by store)

  • Gukbap Restaurant: 1 type of radish radish kimchi, 1 type of cabbage kimchi = 2 bowls
  • BBQ: Cabbage kimchi, radish radish, ssamjang, lettuce = 4 to 6 bowls
  • Hanjeongsik: Cabbage kimchi, white kimchi, dongchimi, radish radish kimchi, bachelor kimchi = 5 types of full set
  • Snack: Pickled radish and cabbage kimchi = 2 bowls
  • Cafe: Kimchi

English Speaking Kit (Step 1)

  • “Is the kimchi free?” → Is kimchi free? = YES (all Korean restaurants)
  • “Can I get more kimchi?” → Can I get more kimchi? = YES·Unlimited
  • "Which kimchi is least spicy?" → What is the least spicy kimchi? = White Kimchi·Dongchimi Request

Request for non-spicy kimchi in English

  • "Do you have white kimchi?" → Do you have white kimchi? (Not spicy)
  • “Do you have water kimchi?” → Do you have water kimchi (dongchimi, nabak)? (Not spicy)
  • "Mild kimchi please" → Please use non-spicy kimchi

🔄 Step 3 — Free unlimited refills (all restaurants)

Standard Procedure for Refill Requests

1. When the kimchi bowl is empty = Automatic refill signal

2. "Please give me more kimchi" = Can I get more kimchi? — Call the boss/employee

3. Additional bowl = Arrives in 5 seconds to 1 minute

4. Free/Unlimited = All Korean restaurants (Korean food, soup, BBQ, snacks)

5. **Exception store

Cases where refills cannot be received (although rare)

  • Buffet/Self-service store = Users go and get their own food
  • Chain unlimited refill store = Time limit (1 hour and 30 minutes, etc.)
  • Airport/train station stores = Some one-time offer (although rare)

Refill frequency (foreigners vs. Koreans)

  • Korean average: 1-2 refills per meal
  • Foreigner average: 1 or 0 times (unsure of request)
  • Foreign Mania: 3~4 times (Korean level)

English Speaking Kit (Step 3)

  • "More kimchi please" = Please give me more kimchi
  • “Different kimchi?” = Are there other types of kimchi? (Korean food/multi-item store)
  • "Same kimchi" = same kimchi (on request)

🎯 Step 4 — Re-visit + Regular know-how

Kimchi know-how for return visits

1. Greetings from the owner = Long-established Korean store = Store where the owner makes kimchi himself

2. "The owner's kimchi is delicious" = This kimchi is really good — The owner's pride and service kimchi is OK

3. Kimchi season request = Spring (beginning of young radish), summer (young radish, nabak), fall (preparing kimchi), winter (kimchi, dongchimi). Signatures vary by store.

4. Request for fermentation stage = “Please give me old kimchi” / “Please give me geotjeori” — Regular Matrix

5. The owner teaches kimchi = Regular visitor = The owner shares kimchi secrets, ingredients, and season information

⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Kimchi = Separate request/charge": Korean restaurant kimchi = Automatic free side dish·Unlimited refills. Separate order

2. "Kimchi refill not requested (quiet end)": Korean standard = refill requested = normal. “More kimchi please” natural. The average Korean refills 1-2 times per meal.

3. "Expect a variety of kimchi in a soup restaurant": Gukbap restaurant = 1 type of kkakdugi (specialized store). Various kimchi = Korean food, home cooking.

4. "Not sure about kimchi pairing at BBQ": BBQ ripe kimchi = oil jus matrix. Japanese #1·Chinese #1 enthusiasts.

5. "I thought there was no kimchi in snacks and chicken": Snack = pickled radish + cabbage kimchi. Chicken = chicken radish + cabbage kimchi. Kimchi = Korean restaurant standard.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Recommended first Korean restaurant for foreigners: Gukbap Restaurant (simple radish radish, warm soup) → BBQ (rich kimchi) → Hanjeongsik (various). First visit = soup restaurant.

2. "Free unlimited kimchi" signal: Menu board, signboard X, obvious Korean restaurant standard. Store entry = automatically applied.

3. Refill frequency: Korean = 1~2 times, Foreign enthusiast = 3~4 times, Foreigner new = 0~1 time. Enthusiast = Store regular.

4. Aged paper store: Long-established store, 30-year+ home-style food, and kimchi stew specialty store = Stockpiled aged paper. Chain = aged paper

5. Seasonal kimchi store: Spring (starting of young radishes from April to May), summer (June to August for nabak and young radishes), fall (October to November for kimchi preparation), winter (November to February for kimchi and dongchimi) — Home-cooked food, long-established signature.

6. Allergy: Shrimp sauce (crustaceans), anchovy fish sauce, garlic, red pepper powder, soybeans (soy sauce), prior notice. White kimchi·Dongchimi = Spicy

⚠ Medical/Safety/Dietary disclaimer

Kimchi = Fermented food (salinity ↑, spiciness high). Pregnant women, high blood pressure, kidney patients = Be careful of salt. Children = White kimchi and dongchimi (not spicy) recommended. Allergy (shrimp salt, anchovy fish sauce, crustacean, garlic, red pepper powder) prior notice. If food poisoning is suspected, call ☎ 1339 (Medical, English).

Dietary restrictions fallback:

  • Halal·Kosher: Regular kimchi = non-halal·non-kosher (including salted shrimp). → Temple Food Kimchi = Vegetarian, Halal, Kosher available
  • Vegetarian/Vegan: Regular kimchi = Non-vegetarian (shrimp salt, anchovy fish sauce). → Separate store for temple food or vegan kimchi (soy sauce-based)
  • Gluten free: OK if you leave out the soy sauce. White kimchi·Dongchimi = Soy sauce

✈️ Pain point for foreigners #1 — Difference between restaurant kimchi and supermarket kimchi

  • Restaurant Kimchi = The owner himself makes the kimchi (long-established store) or delivers it to the kimchi factory (chain) — Fresh, Seasonal, Store Signature
  • Mart Kimchi = Bulk kimchi factory (Jonggajip, Pulmuone, CJ, etc.) — Standard/Vacuum/Long-term storage
  • Experience difference: Restaurant = freshness/seasonal changes / supermarket = standard consistency
  • Return gift: Supermarket vacuum-packed kimchi = Checked baggage OK (liquids sorted/100ml+) — Carry-on X
  • Recommended purchase place: Incheon Airport Duty Free Shop (vacuum packaging), E-Mart, Lotte Mart (cost-effective)
