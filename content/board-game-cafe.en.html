Board Game Cafe Guide — How Foreigners Use Them in Seoul: the Red Button Chain, Hourly Pricing, English Games (2026)

Subtitle: Red Button has 124+ branches nationwide and an English site (official) / pricing is hourly per person plus one drink each, around 3,000 won at the Hongdae branch (secondary source) / Dice Latte is now DCC Cafe (secondary) / Board Game Festa 2026 ended in April — as of 2026-10-08, official Red Button and Korea Board Games pages are separated from secondary sources. 🎯 How to Use This Guide

Board game cafes are one of the easiest indoor pastimes in Korea, but official sites hardly publish prices or branch hours — even the official Red Button page has no price list. So this guide labels what it reports: (1) what we read on official pages (chain size, English site, Board Game Festa) and (2) secondary sources (Hongdae branch hours, pricing, the one-drink-per-person rule). Choosing a shop is left to your map app. The old guide's "Tabletop Cafe Gangnam", "Players Cafe Hongdae", "Itaewon English-OK board games", "VIP rooms" and "Klook package" could not be confirmed in any source, so we removed them.

🎲 How Play Works — Korean Board Game Cafe Rules

  • Hourly pricing: The bill is number of people × hours. At the Red Button Hongdae branch it is about 3,000 won per person per hour, paid when you leave (secondary source). The first-hour rate and 10-minute extension charges are not officially published.
  • One drink per person: Many branches require a drink order at entry (secondary source).
  • Game explanations: Staff explain the rules. Whether they can do it in English varies by branch and there is no official statement — Red Button, which has an English site, is the safer bet.
  • Reservations: The Hongdae branch is walk-in (secondary source). Expect a wait on weekend evenings.

🏬 Chains and Shops

  • Red Button (레드버튼): The largest chain, with 124+ branches nationwide (official). The official page has no list of Seoul branches, so search for "Red Button" in your map app. The old guide's "200+ games" and "Gangnam–Hongdae cluster" have no basis.
  • DCC Cafe, formerly Dice Latte (DCC 카페, 구 다이스라떼): Introduced as a cafe aimed at foreigners, but its official site does not open, so location and hours are unconfirmed.
  • Korea Board Games (코리아보드게임즈): Korea's largest board game distributor. Its official store page lists no shop address or hours. Board M (보드엠) is an online shop based in Goyang (고양) (no Seoul store).
  • Pick a specific shop by the number of map-app reviews. This guide does not give shop-level prices or hours.

🎪 Events

  • Board Game Festa (보드게임 페스타) 2026: Apr 11–12 at SETEC (세텍) Hall 2 (Hakyeoul Station (학여울역)) — it is over. No autumn edition has been confirmed. For 2027, watch for a spring announcement.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Treating a blog's price as fact — not officially published. Check the price board before you go in.

2. Taking a seat without a drink — many branches require one drink per person.

3. Assuming English rule explanations — it varies by branch. Prefer Red Button, which has an English site.

4. Searching for the old guide's shop names — existence unconfirmed. Search "board game cafe" in your map app.

5. Going to Board Game Festa in autumn — the 2026 edition ended in April.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. About how much? — About 3,000 won per person per hour at Red Button Hongdae (secondary source), plus a drink. It varies by branch.

2. Are there English games? — There is an English site, but the number of games in stock is not officially published. Ask staff whether they have English rulebooks.

3. Reservations? — The Hongdae branch is walk-in (secondary). Expect a wait on weekend evenings.

4. A cafe for foreigners? — DCC Cafe, formerly Dice Latte, is introduced that way. Location and hours are unconfirmed.

5. How many people is ideal? — Since it is hourly, 3–4 people is the most efficient.

6. Events? — Board Game Festa (spring). The 2026 edition is over.
