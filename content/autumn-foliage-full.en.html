Autumn Foliage Full Guide — Top 1 Inbound Destination for Foreigners in Oct-Nov (Following #89)

> Subtitle: Bukhansan (북한산)·Gwanaksan (관악산)·Dobongsan (도봉산)·Achasan (아차산)·Naksan (낙산) + Olympic Park (올림픽공원)·Seoul Forest (서울숲)·Yangjae (양재) + Palaces·Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원)·Jongmyo Shrine (종묘) + Outlying Gangwon (강원)·Jeonnam (전남)

ISSUE 03 · #229 · 2026-05-19

🎯 Why an Autumn Foliage Full Guide?

Korean autumn foliage (October-November) is the #1 inbound season for foreign tourists. Following #89 Autumn Foliage TOP 8, this guide integrates various autumn foliage spots across different areas. Along with spring cherry blossoms, it's consistently the #1 topic for foreign Instagram and vlog content every year.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Foliage = Only Mountains": The 4 pillars of autumn foliage: Mountains (Bukhansan (북한산)·Gwanaksan (관악산)·Dobongsan (도봉산)·Achasan (아차산)) + City Parks (Olympic Park (올림픽공원)·Seoul Forest (서울숲)·Yangjae Citizens' Forest (양재 시민의숲)) + Palaces (Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원)·Jongmyo Shrine (종묘)) + Mountains & Observatories (Naksan (낙산)·Inwangsan (인왕산)·Eungbongsan (응봉산)).

2. "Foliage Season = October": Foliage season varies:

  • 1st~2nd week of October: Seoraksan (설악산)·Odaesan (오대산) in Gangwon-do (강원도)
  • 3rd~4th week of October: Bukhansan (북한산)·Gwanaksan (관악산)·Dobongsan (도봉산)·Achasan (아차산)
  • 1st~2nd week of November: City Parks·Palaces (Seoul Forest (서울숲)·Olympic Park (올림픽공원)·Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원))
  • 3rd week of November: Eungbongsan (응봉산)·Naksan (낙산)·Inwangsan (인왕산) City areas

3. "Changdeokgung Huwon = General Admission": Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원) (also known as Biwon (비원), the Secret Garden) = Advance reservation + Guided tour (4-5 times a day) + 1 hour 30 minute course + separate ₩5,000 (~$3.7 / ~27 RMB / ~550 JPY) admission fee. English guides are available.

① Mountains & National Parks (4)

1. Bukhansan National Park (북한산국립공원) ★ Korea's #1

  • Foreigner's Pick ★: ◎◎ — Korea's #1 urban national park
  • Foliage Season: 3rd~4th week of October
  • Details: #182 Eunpyeong (은평)·#177 Nowon (노원)·Dobong (도봉)

2. Gwanaksan (관악산)

  • Foreigner's Pick ★: ◎ — Seoul National University (서울대) foothills·Yeonjuam Temple (연주암 사찰) combination (#174)
  • Foliage Season: 3rd~4th week of October

3. Dobongsan (도봉산)·Suraksan (수락산)·Buramsan (불암산)

  • Foreigner's Pick ★: ◎ — Nowon (노원)·Dobong (도봉) 3 great mountains (#177)
  • Foliage Season: 3rd~4th week of October

4. Achasan (아차산)

  • Foreigner's Pick ★: ◎ — 287m·Gwangjin (광진) + Han River (한강) view + Hanyangdoseong (한양도성) Goguryeo (고구려) fortress (#178)
  • Foliage Season: 4th week of October ~ 1st week of November

9. Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원) ★ UNESCO Foliage (1997)

  • Foreigner's Pick ★: ◎◎ — Korea's #1 palace foliage + UNESCO

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Top 1 Foliage Spots for Foreigners: Bukhansan (북한산) (#182)·Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원) (UNESCO)·Olympic Park (올림픽공원)·Eungbongsan (응봉산).

2. English Guides for Foreigners: Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원)·Jongmyo Shrine (종묘)·Palaces + advance reservation for foreigners available.

3. Regional Foliage Comparison: Gangwon-do (강원도) (1st~3rd week of Oct) → Seoul Mountains (10월 셋째~넷째 주) → Seoul City Parks·Palaces (1st~2nd week of Nov) → Seoul Eungbongsan (응봉산)·Naksan (낙산) (2nd~3rd week of Nov).

4. Foliage + Hanbok (한복) for Foreigners: Changdeokgung (창덕궁)·Jongmyo Shrine (종묘)·Palaces (free admission in Hanbok (한복) (traditional Korean dress) #207).

5. K-drama Foliage Stages: Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원)·Bukchon (북촌)·Olympic Park (올림픽공원)·Seoul Forest (서울숲).

6. Foliage + KTX (Korea Train eXpress) 1 Night 2 Days: Naejangsan (내장산)·Seoraksan (설악산)·Jirisan (지리산) outlying areas combined with KTX·express bus.

🗺 4 Full-Day Autumn Foliage Courses

A. Korea's #1 Mountain Foliage (3rd week of October)

  • 09:00 (9 AM) Bukhansan (북한산) (#182 Eunpyeong (은평)) → 13:00 (1 PM) Lunch + Jingwansa Temple (진관사) → 16:00 (4 PM) Gwanaksan (관악산) (#174 Seoul National University (서울대))

B. City Park Foliage (1st~2nd week of November)

  • 10:00 (10 AM) Olympic Park (올림픽공원) (#82) → 13:00 (1 PM) Seoul Forest (서울숲) (#01) → 16:00 (4 PM) Yangjae Citizens' Forest (양재 시민의숲) (#173)

C. Palace·Traditional Foliage (2nd~3rd week of November)

  • 10:00 (10 AM) Changdeokgung (창덕궁) + Huwon (후원) (advance reservation) → 13:00 (1 PM) Jongmyo Shrine (종묘) (UNESCO) → 16:00 (4 PM) Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌 한옥마을) → 18:00 (6 PM) Eungbongsan (응봉산) Night View

D. Outlying 1 Night 2 Days (3rd week of October ~ 1st week of November)

  • Day 1: Naejangsan (내장산) (KTX 4 hours) — Korea's #1 outlying foliage spot
  • Day 2: Jeonju Hanok Village (전주 한옥마을) (#226) combination

🌸 5 Season & Time Hooks

  • 1st~3rd week of October: Seoraksan (설악산)·Odaesan (오대산) in Gangwon-do (강원도) (KTX combination)
  • 3rd~4th week of October: Seoul Bukhansan (북한산)·Gwanaksan (관악산)·Dobongsan (도봉산)·Achasan (아차산) (city mountains)
  • 1st~2nd week of November: Seoul Forest (서울숲)·Olympic Park (올림픽공원)·Yangjae Citizens' Forest (양재 시민의숲)·Namsan (남산) + Jongmyo Shrine (종묘)
  • 2nd~3rd week of November: Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원) (UNESCO)·Naksan (낙산)·Inwangsan (인왕산)·Eungbongsan (응봉산) city areas
  • 3rd~4th week of November: Last city foliage + winter illumination combination (#231)
