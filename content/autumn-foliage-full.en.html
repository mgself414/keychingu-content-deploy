⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Autumn leaves = mountains": 4 axes of autumn leaves — Mountains (Bukhansan Mountain, Gwanak Mountain, Dobong Mountain, Acha) + City Parks (Olympic Mountain, Seoul Forest, Yangjae) + Palaces (Changdeokgung Palace Huwon, Jongmyo Shrine) + Mountains and Observatories (Naksan Mountain, Inwangsan Mountain, Eungbongsan Mountain).

2. "Fall foliage season = October": Fall foliage season varies:

  • First to second week of October: Mt. Seorak and Mt. Odaesan, Gangwon-do
  • Third to fourth week of October: Bukhansan Mountain, Gwanaksan Mountain, Dobongsan Mountain, and Achasan Mountain
  • First to second week of November: City parks and palaces (sponsored by Seoul Forest, Olympic Games, and Changdeokgung Palace)
  • Third week of November: Eungbongsan, Naksan, and Inwangsan city centers

3. "Changdeokgung Palace Sponsorship = General Admission": Changdeokgung Palace Sponsorship (Secret Garden) = Advance reservation + guided tour (4-5 times a day) + 1 hour and 30 minutes course + separate 5,000 won admission fee. English guide OK.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. No. 1 for foreigners’ maple leaves: Bukhansan Mountain (#182), Changdeokgung Palace Sponsor (UNESCO), Olympic Park, Eungbongsan Mountain.

2. English guide for foreigners: Changdeokgung Palace Huwon, Jongmyo, and palaces + advance reservations OK for foreigners.

3. Comparison of fall foliage areas: Gangwon-do (1st to 3rd weeks of October) → Seoul Mountain (3rd to 4th weeks of October) → Seoul city parks and palaces (1st to 2nd weeks of November) → Eungbongsan Mountain and Naksan Mountain in Seoul (2nd to 3rd weeks of November).

4. Foreigner Autumn Leaves + Hanbok: Changdeokgung Palace, Jongmyo Shrine, and palaces (Free Hanbok admission #207).
