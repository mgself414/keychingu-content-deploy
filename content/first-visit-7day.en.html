⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make on their first visit

1. "Stay in one area for 3 days" — Korea is very diverse in each area. 1 area 0.5~1 day is appropriate.

2. "Hotel = Myeong-dong only" — Full set of foreign-friendly hotels in Myeong-dong as well as Gangnam, Hongdae, Itaewon, and Seorae Village.

3. "T-money card = No purchase" — Korea's No. 1 transportation card required (4,000 won, convenience store + recharge).

4. "Korean only" — Korea's No. 1 tourist/foreigner store English OK + Papago app is sufficient.

5. “Ignore K-ETA” — 2021~ Advance application for K-ETA electronic travel authorization required (#FAQ).

⚙ 8 FAQs

Q1. Is K-ETA prior application required?

Required — k-eta.go.kr 72 hours before departure (approximately 10,000 won, valid for 2 years).

Q2. Budget for one week in Korea?

Medium price: 2 to 3 million won (hotel + food + activities) / Cost-effectiveness: 1 to 1.5 million won.

Q3. Hotel area recommendations:

Myeongdong (No. 1 tourist destination)·Gangnam (luxury)·Hongdae (MZ·Indie)·Itaewon (Global)·Seorae (#173 Foreign residents).

Q4. Best season in one week in Korea?

Spring, April (cherry blossoms) + Fall, October-November (autumn leaves) 1st place. Summer from June to August (rainy season and heat waves), winter from December to February (below freezing).

Q5. Top 5 foods to try for a foreigner’s first try?

Korean BBQ (#199) + Korean food (#234 Baekban) + Tteokbokki + Kimchi stew + Korean chicken.

Q6. Foreign K-pop·K-drama mania added for 1 day?

Day 4 Gangnam K-pop follow-up → Day 5 HYBE Insight → Day 6 K-drama stage (Itaewon·Noryangjin #222).

Q7. Foreign family/children?

Children’s Grand Park (#178)·Seoul Forest (#01·#205)·Lotte World (#82)·N Seoul Tower.

Q8. Departure duty free refund?

30,000 won or more + foreign passport + Global Blue display store = Immediate refund. Airport refund counter.
