⚠ 5 Common Mistakes for First-Time Visitors

1. "Staying in one area for 3 days" — Korea has such diverse areas; 0.5 to 1 day per area is more appropriate.

2. "Only staying in Myeongdong for hotels" — Beyond Myeongdong (명동), Gangnam (강남), Hongdae (홍대), Itaewon (이태원), and Seorae Village (서래마을) offer a full range of foreigner-friendly hotels.

3. "No need to buy a T-money card" — A T-money card, Korea's top public transport card, is essential (₩4,000 (~$3 / ~20 RMB / ~400 JPY) at convenience stores (편의점) + top-up).

4. "Only Korean" — English is often spoken at major tourist spots and foreigner-friendly shops in Korea, and the Papago app is sufficient for translation.

5. "Ignoring K-ETA" — Since 2021, K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization) pre-application is mandatory (#FAQ).

⚙ 8 FAQs

Q1. Is K-ETA pre-application mandatory?

Mandatory — Apply at k-eta.go.kr at least 72 hours before departure (approx. ₩10,000 (~$7 / ~50 RMB / ~1,000 JPY), valid for 2 years).

Q2. What's the budget for a 1-week trip to Korea?

Mid-range to High-end: ₩2,000,000-3,000,000 (hotels + food + activities) / Budget-friendly: ₩1,000,000-1,500,000.

Q3. Recommended hotel areas:

Myeongdong (명동) (top tourist spot), Gangnam (강남) (luxury), Hongdae (홍대) (MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) / indie), Itaewon (이태원) (global), Seorae Village (서래마을) (#173 Foreigner Residence).

Q4. Best season for a 1-week trip to Korea?

Spring (April, cherry blossoms) + Autumn (October-November, fall foliage) are top choices. Summer (June-August) brings monsoon and heatwaves, winter (December-February) can be sub-zero.

Q5. Top 5 "challenge foods" for first-time visitors?

Korean BBQ (#199 Korean BBQ Guide) + Korean set meals (Baekban (백반) #234 Korean Set Meal Guide) + Tteokbokki (떡볶이, spicy rice cakes) + Kimchi Jjigae (김치찌개, kimchi stew) + Korean Fried Chicken.

Q6. Add an extra day for K-pop/K-drama enthusiasts?

Following Day 4 Gangnam (강남) K-pop → Day 5 HYBE Insight → Day 6 K-drama filming locations (Itaewon (이태원) / Noryangjin (노량진) #222 K-drama Filming Locations).

Q7. Traveling with family/children?

Children's Grand Park (어린이대공원) (#178 Childrens Grand Park), Seoul Forest (서울숲) (#01 Seoul Forest·#205 Hangang Park Guide), Lotte World (롯데월드) (#82 Jamsil Guide), N Seoul Tower (N서울타워).

Q8. Duty-free refund upon departure?

Purchases of ₩30,000 or more + foreign passport + Global Blue participating stores = immediate refund. Airport refund counters are also available.
