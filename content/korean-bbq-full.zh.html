⚠ 外国人常犯的3个错误

1. "Korean BBQ = Pork belly only": Korean BBQ 5 axis (Korean beef, pork belly, seasoned ribs, lamb skewers, intestines) — Korean beef grade 1++, pork belly, LA ribs, makchang, tripe, and lamb skewers full set challenge.

2. "Korean beef price is too expensive": Korean beef grade 1++ (KRW 60,000~120,000/person) + Korean beef lunch course (KRW 30,000~ reasonable)

3. "Lamb skewers = Korean food": Lamb skewers originated in the Uyghurs of Xinjiang, China + transformed into Korea (1990s expansion of overseas Chinese and Southeast Asia).九老大林中华街1号。

⚙ 6 个常见问题解答

1. 韩式烧烤礼仪：成人优先+双手倒酒+脱鞋（部分正宗店）

2. 韩国牛肉分级系统：1++ → 1+ → 1 → 2 → 3（1++为最佳）

3. 外国人友好商店：Daram、Hanam Pork House、88 Galbi、Kondae Meat Griller

4. 无限续杯与正品：无限续杯（性价比）与正品老店（品质）

5. 哪里有羊肉串？：九老·大林中华街1号（#180）

6. 麻将畜产品市场价格协商：可以，即食生鱼和烤肉批发价的30-50%
