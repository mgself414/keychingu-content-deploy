⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Night view = Namsan Tower only": Full set including Namsan Lotte Tower (#82), 63 Building (#169), Eungbongsan (#51), Naksan (#170), Banpo Fountain Show (#173), etc.

2. "Fountain show = If you don't know the time, you can't see it": Banpo Moonlight Rainbow Fountain Show April to October, nightly at 20:00, 20:20, 20:40, 21:00, 21:20 (20-minute intervals each, 4 times on weekdays and 5 times on weekends). Canceled in case of rain.

3. "DDP night = just a building": DDP has 45,000 LED exterior walls + changing patterns every hour. Climax between 22:00 and 24:00. No. 1 on foreign Instagram.

① Landmark Tower (3)

1. N Seoul Tower (Namsan Seoul Tower) ★ Korea’s No. 1

  • Address: 105 Namsan Park-gil, Yongsan-gu
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Korea’s No. 1 night view
  • Signature: Established in 1969, 265m + N Seoul Tower + Dule-gil + Love Locks
  • Admission fee: Observatory 16,000 won + Cable car 11,000 won (foreigners OK)
  • Time: 10:00~23:00

2. Lotte World Tower Seoul Sky (Jamsil)

  • Address: 300 Olympic-ro, Songpa-gu (#82 Jamsil)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — 5th in the world, 1st in Korea, 555m, 123 floors
  • Signature: 117th floor Sky Deck (glass floor) + Best ±30 minutes before sunset
  • Admission fee: 31,000 won

3. 63 Building (Yeouido)

  • Address: 50 63-ro, Yeongdeungpo-gu (#169 Yeouido)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Korea’s No. 1 80s landmark
  • Signature: Established in 1985, 60th floor observatory, Han River 360 view, Aqua Planet

② Han River/Fountain (3 + 8 bonus)

4. Banpo Hangang Park Moonlight Rainbow Fountain Show ★ Guinness Book of World Records

  • Address: Sinbanpo-ro area, Seocho-gu (#173)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — World’s longest bridge fountain
  • Time: 20:00, 20:20, 20:40, 21:00, 21:20 at night from April to October
  • Admission: Free

  • Details: #51 Eungbongsan Palgakjeong Night View·#189 Seongdong·Wangsimni

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Top 5 free night views: Eungbongsan Mountain, Naksan Park, Banpo Fountain Show, Cheonggyecheon, DDP LED.

2. Top 3 paid night views: N Seoul Tower, Lotte Tower, and 63 Building.

3. Moonlight Rainbow Fountain Show Time: Starts at 20:00 at night from April to October (5 times at 20-minute intervals).

4. No. 1 on foreign Instagram: Namsan Tower, Eungbongsan Mountain, Banpo Fountain, and DDP LED.

5. K-drama night scene favorites: Eungbongsan Mountain, Banpo Han River, N Seoul Tower, DDP.

6. Night Safety: 24-hour security at all spots + plainclothes police + CCTV.
