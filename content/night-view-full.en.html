🎯 Why a Full Guide to Korea's Night Views?

Seoul's (서울) night view is consistently the #1 choice for foreign Instagrammers and vloggers, and a frequent backdrop for K-dramas and variety shows. This guide integrates a follow-up to our #02 free night view spots, offering a full set across three axes: Han River (한강) views, mountain views, and city views.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Night view = Namsan Tower (남산타워) only": Beyond Namsan (남산), there's a full set including Lotte Tower (롯데타워) ([#82_jamsil](82_jamsil.md)) · 63 Building (63 빌딩) ([#169_yeouido](169_yeouido.md)) · Eungbongsan (응봉산) ([#51_eungbongsan](51_eungbongsan.md)) · Naksan (낙산) ([#170_daehangno](170_daehangno.md)) · Banpo (반포) Fountain Show ([#173_seocho](173_seocho.md)).

2. "Fountain show = Miss it if you don't know the time": The Banpo (반포) Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (달빛무지개분수) show runs April-October at 20:00 (8:00 PM), 20:20 (8:20 PM), 20:40 (8:40 PM), 21:00 (9:00 PM), 21:20 (9:20 PM) (each 20-minute interval, 4 times on weekdays · 5 times on weekends). It is suspended in case of rain.

3. "DDP (동대문디자인플라자) at night = Just a building": DDP (동대문디자인플라자) features an exterior with 45,000 LEDs + patterns that change every hour. The peak viewing time is 22:00-24:00 (10:00 PM - 12:00 AM). It's a top Instagram spot for foreigners.

① Landmark Towers (3)

1. Namsan Seoul Tower (N서울타워) ★ Korea's #1 Spot

  • Address: 105, Namsangongwon-gil (남산공원길), Yongsan-gu (용산구)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Korea's #1 night view
  • Signature: Built 1969 · 265m + Namsan Seoul Tower (N서울타워) + Dulle-gil (둘레길) (walking trail) + Locks of Love
  • Admission: Observatory ₩16,000 (~$12 / ~85 RMB / ~1,700 JPY) + Cable Car ₩11,000 (Foreigner OK)
  • Hours: 10:00-23:00 (10:00 AM - 11:00 PM)

2. Lotte World Tower (롯데월드타워) Seoul Sky (Jamsil (잠실))

  • Address: 300, Olympic-ro (올림픽로), Songpa-gu (송파구) ([#82_jamsil](82_jamsil.md) Jamsil (잠실))
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — World's 5th tallest · Korea's #1 at 555m · 123 floors
  • Signature: 117th floor Sky Deck (glass floor) + Best around sunset ±30 minutes
  • Admission: ₩31,000

3. 63 Building (63 빌딩) (Yeouido (여의도))

  • Address: 50, 63-ro (63로), Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구) ([#169_yeouido](169_yeouido.md) Yeouido (여의도))
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Korea's #1 80s landmark
  • Signature: Built 1985 · 60th floor observatory · Han River (한강) 360 view · Aqua Planet

② Han River (한강) · Fountains (3 + 8 Bonus)
