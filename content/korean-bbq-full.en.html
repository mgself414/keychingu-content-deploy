⚠ 3 Common Mistakes for Foreigners

1. "Korean BBQ = Samgyeopsal only": Korean BBQ has 5 pillars: Hanwoo (한우, Korean native beef), Samgyeopsal (삼겹살, pork belly), Yangnyeom Galbi (양념갈비, marinated ribs), Yangkkochi (양꼬치, lamb skewers), and Naejang BBQ (내장 BBQ, offal BBQ). Challenge yourself to the full set, including premium 1++ grade Hanwoo, Samgyeopsal, LA Galbi (LA갈비, thinly sliced marinated beef short ribs), Makchang (막창, grilled pork or beef large intestines), Gopchang (곱창, grilled beef or pork small intestines), and Yangkkochi.

2. "Hanwoo prices are too expensive": While 1++ grade Hanwoo (한우) can range from ₩60,000 to ₩120,000 per person (approx. $44-89 USD), you can find reasonable Hanwoo lunch courses starting from ₩30,000 (approx. $22 USD).

3. "Yangkkochi = Korean food": Yangkkochi (양꼬치) originated in Xinjiang Uyghur, China, and was adapted in Korea with the expansion of Chinese and Southeast Asian communities in the 1990s. Guro (구로) and Daerim (대림) Chinatown are the top spots for it.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Korean BBQ Etiquette: Elders first + pour drinks with two hands + remove shoes (at some traditional nofo (노포, 30+ year traditional restaurant) establishments).

2. Hanwoo (한우) Grading System: 1++ → 1+ → 1 → 2 → 3 (1++ is the highest).

3. Foreigner-Friendly Restaurants: Daram (다람)·Hanam Pig House (하남돼지집)·88 Galbi (88갈비)·Konkuk Univ. Gogigupneunnom (건대 고기굽는놈).

4. All-You-Can-Eat vs. Traditional: All-you-can-eat (value for money) vs. Traditional nofo (노포, 30+ year traditional restaurant) (quality).

5. Where to find Yangkkochi (양꼬치)?: Guro (구로)·Daerim (대림) Chinatown ([#180](180_guro_chinatown.md)) is the top spot.

6. Majang Meat Market (마장축산물시장) Price Negotiation: Yes, you can negotiate. Enjoy fresh sashimi or grilled meat at 30-50% wholesale prices.
