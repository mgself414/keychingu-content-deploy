⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Korean BBQ = Pork belly only": Korean BBQ 5 axis (Korean beef, pork belly, seasoned ribs, lamb skewers, intestines) — Korean beef grade 1++, pork belly, LA ribs, makchang, tripe, and lamb skewers full set challenge.

2. "Korean beef price is too expensive": Korean beef grade 1++ (KRW 60,000~120,000/person) + Korean beef lunch course (KRW 30,000~ reasonable)

3. "Lamb skewers = Korean food": Lamb skewers originated in the Uyghurs of Xinjiang, China + transformed into Korea (1990s expansion of overseas Chinese and Southeast Asia). 1 Guro-Daerim Chinese Street.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Korean BBQ Manners: Adults first + pouring alcohol with both hands + taking off shoes (some authentic stores)

2. Korean beef grading system: 1++ → 1+ → 1 → 2 → 3 (1++ is the best)

3. Foreigner-friendly stores: Daram, Hanam Pork House, 88 Galbi, Kondae Meat Griller

4. Unlimited refills vs. authentic: Unlimited refills (cost-effectiveness) vs. authentic, long-established stores (quality)

5. Where are the lamb skewers?: 1 Guro·Daerim Chinese Street (#180)

6. Majang Livestock Products Market Price Negotiation: OK, 30-50% wholesale price for instant raw fish and grilled meat
