Seoul Traditional Tea House Guide — Insadong Yetchatjip, Insadong Chatjip, Tteulgwa Dawon, Jeontong Dawon, Bukchon Osulloc and Pyeongang Yul, Yongsan Osulloc

Subtitle: 4 traditional tea houses in Insadong (all open year-round, until 9–10 PM) · 2 tea houses in Bukchon (Pyeongang Yul is closed on Tuesdays) · Osulloc Yongsan Park branch (09:00–19:00) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers 7 traditional tea houses and tea houses in Insadong (인사동), Bukchon (북촌) and Yongsan (용산). The Insadong tea houses serve traditional teas such as jujube tea (대추차), ssanghwa tea (쌍화차) and omija tea (오미자차), along with items like sweet red bean porridge (단팥죽) and grilled rice cake (가래떡구이). In Bukchon there is a tea house that combines traditional tea with a modern sensibility, and another that serves teas blended with traditional ingredients.

The operating details and menus are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI).

🍵 4 Traditional Tea Houses in Insadong

Insadong Culture Street is a street of hanok, galleries, and craft and antique shops. The street's business hours are registered as 10:00–18:00, but they differ by shop, and parking is registered as not available. All 4 tea houses below are on Insadong-gil and its side alleys.

Yetchatjip (옛찻집)

Yetchatjip (33-1 Insadong-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 인사동길 33-1), ☎ 0507-1409-8780) is introduced as a traditional tea house famous enough to be used as a film location, and it has an ondol floor. For its summer shaved ice, the red beans are boiled in-house.

  • Signature menu: Old-style red bean shaved ice (옛날팥빙수). Also soybean powder ice cream, jujube tea (대추차), lotus leaf tea, ssanghwa tea (쌍화차), cold omija tea (오미자냉차) and more.
  • Opening hours: 11:00–21:00, open year-round.

Insadong Chatjip (인사동찻집)

Insadong Chatjip (33-1 Insadong-gil, Jongno-gu, ☎ 02-723-4909) is a hanok tea house registered separately at the same address as Yetchatjip. All of its traditional teas are brewed in-house.

  • Signature menu: Sweet red bean porridge (단팥죽), grilled rice cake (가래떡구이). Traditional teas such as jujube tea (대추차), ssanghwa tang (쌍화탕), ginger tea (생강차) and omija tea (오미자차), plus coffee. The red beans for the summer shaved ice are also boiled in-house.
  • Opening hours: 10:00–21:00, open year-round.
  • Cards: Accepted. Parking: Not available. There are separate non-smoking and smoking seats.

Tteulgwa Dawon (뜰과다원)

Tteulgwa Dawon (34 Insadong-gil, Jongno-gu, ☎ 0507-1314-5310) is a hanok cafe with a courtyard, and it opened in June 2014. Its traditional teas are made in-house from 100% Korean-grown ingredients, and it also has organic coffee. Group seating is available.

  • Signature menu: Ssanghwa tea (쌍화차). Also jujube tea (대추차), ginger citron tea (생강유자차), sikhye (식혜), red bean porridge (팥죽) and more. Assorted rice cakes, snacks, and grilled mugwort rice cake (쑥가래떡구이) are also served.
  • Opening hours: 10:00–22:00 (last order 21:50), open year-round.
  • Parking: Not available.

Jeontong Dawon (전통다원)

Jeontong Dawon (11-4 Insadong 10-gil, Jongno-gu, ☎ 0507-1339-6305) is a tea house in a hanok that serves 15 kinds of Korean traditional tea. There is a room where, when you open the sliding door, you see a garden beyond the veranda, and gugak (traditional Korean music) plays.

  • Signature menu: Jujube tea (대추차). Also ssanghwa tea (쌍화차), ginger tea (생강차), omija tea (오미자차) and more.
  • Opening hours: 10:30–21:20, open year-round.

🏯 2 Tea Houses in Bukchon

Osulloc Tea House Bukchon (오설록 티하우스 북촌점)

Osulloc Tea House Bukchon (45 Bukchon-ro, Gahoe-dong, Jongno-gu, ☎ 070-4121-2019) is a tea house that combines traditional tea with a modern sensibility. The desserts served with the tea are menu items that emphasize their own distinct flavors.

  • Signature menu: Bukchon's Roof Tiles, Bukchon's Colors (북촌의 기와, 북촌의 색).
  • Opening hours: Mon–Thu 11:00–20:00, Fri–Sun 11:00–21:00. Open year-round.

Pyeongang Yul Flagship & Tea House (편강 율 플래그십&티하우스)

Pyeongang Yul Flagship & Tea House (35-4 Bukchon-ro 5ga-gil, Sogyeok-dong, Jongno-gu, ☎ 070-8844-0998) puts each floor to a different use. On the 1st-floor tea house you enjoy teas blended with traditional ingredients and dasik (다식, tea confections) together with the garden; on the 2nd floor you can try the herbal skincare products of the K-beauty brand Pyeongang Yul (편강율); and from the 3rd-floor rooftop you can take in Bukchon and the Gyeongbokgung Palace area at a glance.

  • Signature menu: Bonyeonyul tea (본연율차). Also sejak (세작), jaeksal (잭살), Four Seasons dasik (사계다식) and more.
  • Opening hours: 11:00–19:00 (last order 18:30). Closed every Tuesday.
  • Takeout: Available.

🏙 Osulloc Tea House Yongsan Park (오설록티하우스 용산파크점)

Osulloc Tea House Yongsan Park (100 Hangang-daero, Hangangno 2-ga, Yongsan-gu (용산구 한강대로 100), ☎ 070-5172-1170) is a tea space where you can experience the tea culture of the Jeju tea fields in the city, serving tea and desserts made with tea.

  • Opening hours: 09:00–19:00, open Mon–Sun.
  • Cards: Accepted. Parking: Available. Restrooms available.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Planning Pyeongang Yul for a Tuesday — It is closed every Tuesday.
  2. Arriving at Pyeongang Yul just before closing — Last order is 18:30.
  3. Bringing a car to an Insadong tea house — Insadong Chatjip and Tteulgwa Dawon are registered as having no parking, and Insadong Culture Street also does not allow parking.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Are Yetchatjip and Insadong Chatjip the same place?

A. The Korea Tourism Organization information registers them separately at the same address (33-1 Insadong-gil), with different phone numbers, opening hours and signature menus.

Q. Which place can I go to late at night?

A. By the registered hours, Tteulgwa Dawon is the latest, until 22:00 (last order 21:50).
