24_Gwangjang Market (광장시장) Complete Guide

One-Line Intro

Gwangjang Market (광장시장): Seoul's oldest and most iconic street food market, open since 1905.

From bindaetteok (빈대떡) to yukhoe (육회) to mayak gimbap (마약김밥) — it's all here, under one roof.

Who Should Visit

  • Travelers who want to experience Korean traditional market food culture firsthand
  • Anyone looking to try a wide variety of Korean foods in one place without breaking the bank

Recommended Itinerary (2 Hours)

1. 11:00 — Line 1, Jongno 5-ga Station (종로5가역), Exit 8 (3-min walk): Enter the main market gate

2. 11:10 — Start with Mayak Gimbap (마약김밥) (₩3,000–4,000): Order 1–2 rolls; don't skip the mustard sauce

3. 11:30 — Bindaetteok Alley (빈대떡 골목) (₩5,000): Fresh pan-fried mung bean pancake + one glass of makgeolli (막걸리) (₩8,000)

4. 12:00 — Yukhoe Corner (육회 코너) (₩15,000–20,000): Fresh hanwoo (한우) beef tartare topped with sesame oil and garlic

5. 12:30 — Sundae (순대) + Market Browse (₩5,000–7,000): A plate of sundae (순대) and a stroll through the hanbok (한복) fabric section

6. 13:00 — Basement Vintage Shopping (optional): Hunt for retro and secondhand finds

Tips for Foreign Visitors

  • Useful Korean Phrases
  • "이거 주세요" (Igeo juseyo) — "This one, please" — just point at what you want
  • "마약김밥 두 줄 주세요" (Mayak gimbap du jul juseyo) — "Two rolls of mayak gimbap, please"
  • "막걸리 한 잔 주세요" (Makgeolli han jan juseyo) — "One glass of makgeolli, please"
  • Cultural Note: The market gets narrow and crowded fast. Leave large suitcases behind. Haggling is generally considered rude — prices are mostly fixed.
  • Payment: Cash is king. More stalls are accepting cards these days, but always carry cash just in case.
  • Food Safety Tip: Freshness matters with yukhoe (육회). Choose a busy stall with high turnover. Skip it if you have a sensitive stomach.

Simple English Summary

Gwangjang Market (광장시장) is Seoul's oldest traditional market, famous for street food eaten right at the stall counter. Must-try items: mayak gimbap (마약김밥) (tiny rice rolls with mustard sauce, ₩3,000), bindaetteok (빈대떡) (mung bean pancake, ₩5,000), and yukhoe (육회) (Korean-style beef tartare). Bring cash — most vendors don't accept cards. Visit on weekday mornings to avoid weekend tourist crowds. The market is on Line 1 at Jongno 5-ga Station (종로5가역), Exit 8.

Estimated Total Cost

  • Standard: ₩35,000 — gimbap (김밥) + bindaetteok (빈대떡) + yukhoe (육회) + makgeolli (막걸리)
  • Splurge: ₩60,000 — full spread + hanbok (한복) fabric souvenir
