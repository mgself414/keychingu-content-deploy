Locals' Picks ③ Gangwon — 13 Eateries Korean Viewers Named in the Comments, From Four Makguksu Houses to the Border Towns

Subtitle: An axis untouched by the Gangneung KTX guide (#1106), Anmok coffee (#1037), Sokcho + Seorak (#1038), and the East Coast meta piece (#385) — picking only the 13 Gangwon spots out of a nationwide roundup of eateries Korean viewers recommended in YouTube comments, verified for existence, address, and business type on Naver local search, with no evaluation attached. Makguksu 4 → East Coast Sokcho/Gangneung 3 → inland rail Pyeongchang/Jeongseon/Yeongwol/Taebaek 4 → border towns Cheorwon/Yanggu 2. Yanggu, Cheorwon, Jeongseon, Yeongwol, and Taebaek are all first appearances on KeyChinGu.

ISSUE 06 · #1384 · 2026-09-18 · Locals' Picks ③

🎯 Why "Comment Recommendations" Is the Bar

This corner has one source. A Korean YouTube channel posted a "nationwide eateries" video, viewers wrote in their own neighborhood spots in the comments, and the most systematically organized comments were compiled by region into a table of 217 places. It's a list Koreans recommended to Koreans, so foreign reviews are scarce and many are small-town restaurants that never make tourist lists. This corner's criteria and limits are laid out in Part ① (#1382).

KeyChinGu checked only whether the name, address, and business type actually exist on Naver local search (2026-09-17). Of 16 Gangwon spots, 13 were confirmed; the 3 that weren't (one each in Mukho, Yanggu, and Goseong) were dropped. There are no visit reviews, rankings, or star ratings, and no hours or prices — check Naver Place on the day you go.

What sets the Gangwon piece apart from the previous two is that rail only covers half the map. Trains reach Gangneung, Jinbu, Yeongwol, and Taebaek; Sokcho, Goseong, Yangyang, Cheorwon, and Yanggu are bus-only. The Chuncheon–Sokcho East-West high-speed line (동서고속철도) is not yet open as of September 2026 (opening targeted for 2027), so Sokcho is still a bus town. On the other hand, since December 30, 2025, the Donghae Line (동해선) KTX-Eum connecting Busan's Bujeon Station and Gangneung has run 3 times a day, making it possible for the first time to link the Busan piece (#1382) and the Gangwon piece with a single train ride.

① Four Makguksu Houses — Goseong, Yangyang, Yanggu

Makguksu (막국수) is Gangwon's buckwheat noodle dish, served cold in broth or tossed with sauce. Chuncheon is the famous name, but the makguksu houses viewers wrote into the roundup were on the northern East Coast (Goseong, Yangyang) and the inland border (Yanggu). All four are in eup/myeon townships, so access assumes a taxi past the end of the bus line.

  • Sanbuk Makguksu (산북막국수) · 16 Sanbuk-gil, Geojin-eup, Goseong-gun, Gangwon (고성군 거진읍 산북길 16) · Korean · Makguksu · Bus 1/1-1 (Donghae Sangsa) from Sokcho Express Bus Terminal (Daepo-dong depot) — ~60 min to Ganseong, ~75 min to Geojin; last bus Sokcho→Ganseong 21:00

Near Geojin Port in Goseong, Gangwon's northernmost county. A half-day pairing with the Unification Observatory and Geojin Port while based in Sokcho.

  • Donghae Makguksu (동해막국수) · 54 Donghae-daero, Hyeonnam-myeon, Yangyang-gun, Gangwon (양양군 현남면 동해대로 54) · Korean · Makguksu · On National Route 7 (Donghae-daero) — public-transit access not confirmed; car/taxi

In Hyeonnam-myeon, south of Yangyang. In the same area as the surf beaches (Jukdo, Ingu), so it suits rental-car travelers.

  • Dochon Makguksu (도촌막국수) · 6 Guktojeongjungang-ro, Guktojeongjungang-myeon, Yanggu-gun, Gangwon (양구군 국토정중앙면 국토정중앙로 6) · Korean · Makguksu · Taxi from Yanggu Intercity Bus Terminal — local county bus to check
  • Yangjimalmoe Makguksu (양지말뫼막국수) · 40 Namdong-ro 321beon-gil, Guktojeongjungang-myeon, Yanggu-gun, Gangwon (양구군 국토정중앙면 남동로321번길 40) · Korean · Noodles · Same township as above — taxi, check

Guktojeongjungang-myeon (국토정중앙면) in Yanggu is named for sitting at the exact center of the Korean Peninsula map. Both makguksu houses are in the same township, so pick one per meal.

Tip for foreign visitors — Makguksu comes in two orders: mul (물, broth) and bibim (비빔, sauced). Adding the table's sugar, vinegar, and mustard to taste is the local way to eat it; mul in summer and bibim in other seasons is the safe default. Orders can be for one.

② East Coast — Sokcho x2, Gangneung x1

  • Saengtong Samgyeopsal (생통삼겹살) · 15 Bamgol-gil, Sokcho-si, Gangwon (속초시 밤골길 15) · Korean · Grilled Pork · Downtown Sokcho — check Naver Map for walking/taxi from the express bus terminal
  • Cheonghakdong Sageori Jeonjip (청학동사거리 전집) · 6 Jungang-ro 82beon-gil, Sokcho-si, Gangwon (속초시 중앙로82번길 6) · Bar · Jeon · Sokcho Jungang-dong (Jungang Market area) — check

Sokcho is known as a city of hoe (raw fish) and mulhoe (#1038), but the two spots viewers wrote in are a samgyeopsal (grilled pork belly) house and a jeonjip. A jeonjip (전집) is a bar that serves jeon (전, savory pancakes) as anju (안주, drinking food), so it's an evening pick.

  • Wonseong Sikdang (원성식당) · 2022-1 Gyeonggang-ro, Gangneung-si, Gangwon (강릉시 경강로 2022-1) · Chinese · Chinese Restaurant · A 10–20 min walk from KTX Gangneung Station along Wolhwa-geori (월화거리, the old rail line) to the old downtown

A Chinese restaurant in Gangneung's old downtown (Okcheon-dong, Jungang-dong). It sits on the downtown leg of the Gangneung coffee (#1037) and KTX overnight-trip (#1106) routes.

Tip for foreign visitors — Sokcho has no train as of September 2026 (the Chuncheon–Sokcho high-speed line is targeted for 2027). From Seoul, go by express or intercity bus; from Gangneung, by intercity bus. Samgyeopsal usually starts at a 2-person order; a jeonjip's base unit is one anju dish plus makgeolli (막걸리, rice wine).

③ Inland Rail — Pyeongchang Jinbu, Jeongseon, Yeongwol, Taebaek

  • Gobau Sikdang (고바우식당) · 90-17 Cheongsong-ro, Jinbu-myeon, Pyeongchang-gun, Gangwon (평창군 진부면 청송로 90-17) · Korean · KTX Jinbu (Odaesan) Station — taxi from the station to Cheongsong-ro, check

A Korean restaurant in Jinbu-myeon, the gateway to Odaesan and Woljeongsa Temple. Jinbu Station is a stop on the Gangneung Line KTX.

  • Jeongseon Ae Jangeo (정선애장어) · 13 Jangyeol-gil, Bukpyeong-myeon, Jeongseon-gun, Gangwon (정선군 북평면 장열길 13) · Korean · Eel · Jeongseon/Najeon Station — reached by the Jeongseon Arirang Train (정선아리랑열차, resumed 2026-05-22, Jecheon–Auraji section, runs weekends and Jeongseon's five-day market days); station to Jangyeol-gil to check

Specializes in grilled eel. The Jeongseon Arirang Train was suspended for a safety inspection from February to April 2026, resumed on May 22, and now runs a shorter Jecheon–Auraji section. The five-day market falls on the 2nd, 7th, 12th, 17th, 22nd, and 27th.

  • Jangneung Boribapjip (장릉보리밥집) · 178-10 Danjong-ro, Yeongwol-eup, Yeongwol-gun, Gangwon (영월군 영월읍 단종로 178-10) · Korean · Boribap · Yeongwol Station (Taebaek Line from Cheongnyangni) — taxi from the station to Danjong-ro, check

Jangneung (장릉) is the tomb of King Danjong. True to its name, this boribap (보리밥) house sits by Jangneung — boribap is barley rice topped with wild greens and mixed together.

  • Mul Dakgalbi (물닭갈비) · 9 Hwangjiyeonmot-gil, Taebaek-si, Gangwon (태백시 황지연못길 9) · Korean · Dakgalbi · Taebaek Station — about 3h30m from Cheongnyangni, 5 trains a day (first 07:34, last 19:10); station to Hwangjiyeonmot to check

Mul dakgalbi (물닭갈비) is Taebaek's take on dakgalbi (닭갈비, stir-fried chicken). Unlike the Chuncheon style cooked on a griddle, this version simmers chicken, vegetables, and noodles in broth. Hwangjiyeonmot is a pond in downtown Taebaek known as the source of the Nakdong River.

Tip for foreign visitors — Taking a Taebaek Line train (Mugunghwa or ITX-Maeum) from Cheongnyangni links Yeongwol and Taebaek on one line. There are only 5 runs a day, so check the timetable first; from the station to the restaurant, just show the taxi driver the Korean-language address.

④ Border Towns — Cheorwon, Yanggu

  • Solhyanggi (솔향기) · 31-10 Geumhak-ro, Dongsong-eup, Cheorwon-gun, Gangwon (철원군 동송읍 금학로 31-10) · Korean · Kalguksu & Mandu · Intercity bus to Dongsong from Dong Seoul Terminal — travel time to check

A kalguksu (칼국수, knife-cut noodle soup) and mandu (만두, dumpling) spot in Cheorwon's Dongsong-eup. Cheorwon is a base for DMZ tourism (Goseokjeong, the old Workers' Party Headquarters), and Dongsong is its town center.

  • Pungmi Sikdang (풍미식당) · 58-1 Punchbowl-ro, Dongmyeon, Yanggu-gun, Gangwon (양구군 동면 펀치볼로 58-1) · Chinese · Chinese Restaurant · Yanggu Intercity Bus Terminal → Dongmyeon (Punchbowl) — local county bus/taxi, check

Punchbowl is the nickname for the Haean Basin in Yanggu's Dongmyeon. A Chinese restaurant along the same route as the DMZ Punchbowl Perimeter Trail.

Tip for foreign visitors — Some border-area stretches have military checkpoints, and DMZ tours require ID (a passport). The restaurants themselves are ordinary town eateries, with no special procedure.

🚄 Fitting This Into a Regional Route

  • Gangneung overnight trip (#1106) — Wonseong Sikdang is in the old downtown, walkable from the station. Add Sokcho with an intercity bus from Gangneung.
  • Based in Sokcho — the samgyeopsal and jeonjip spots are downtown; Geojin's Sanbuk Makguksu is about 75 min away on bus 1/1-1. Last bus Sokcho→Ganseong is 21:00.
  • Busan → Gangneung on one train — since 2025-12-30, the Donghae Line KTX-Eum runs Bujeon↔Gangneung 3 times a day (ITX-Maeum also runs). It's now possible for the first time to move from the Busan piece (#1382) to Gangneung the next day. The Samcheok–Gangneung stretch is still conventional rail.
  • Cheongnyangni → Taebaek Line — Yeongwol and Taebaek are on the same train. Time Jeongseon to the Arirang Train's running days (weekends and market days).
  • Rental car only — Yangyang Hyeonnam, Yanggu's two makguksu spots, and Punchbowl all involve long taxi legs if you're on public transit.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Looking for a train to Sokcho — there isn't one as of September 2026. The East-West high-speed line is targeted for 2027. It's bus-only.

2. Trying the Jeongseon Arirang Train on a weekday — it only runs on weekends and Jeongseon's five-day market days (2nd and 7th), and the route is now shorter, running Jecheon–Auraji.

3. Trying to reach eup/myeon restaurants by public transit the whole way — in Gangwon, a taxi leg from the end of the bus line to the restaurant is common. Call a taxi for the return trip ahead of time too.

⚙ 3 FAQs

1. Who picked this list? — It's a table of local eateries Korean YouTube viewers wrote into the comments. KeyChinGu only verified existence, address, and business type, and did not rank anything.

2. Why isn't Chuncheon makguksu on here? — There was no Chuncheon entry in the roundup. This piece covers only what's in the roundup.

3. What should I pick traveling solo? — Makguksu, boribap, kalguksu, and the Chinese restaurants take single orders. Samgyeopsal, dakgalbi, and the jeonjip are easier with 2 or more.
