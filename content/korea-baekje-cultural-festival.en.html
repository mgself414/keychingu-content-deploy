Baekje Cultural Festival Guide: The 72nd Edition, 2026 Gongju and Buyeo (Oct 3-11), Official Program, Fees and Getting There

Subtitle: Geumgang Singwan Park and Gongsanseong Fortress in Gongju, and the Jeongnimsa Temple Site, Seoktap-ro and Gwanbuk-ri in Buyeo: the festival runs on the same dates in two separate cities. Per the official sites as of 2026-10-07 · Ungjin Fantasia Oct 7-10 at 21:00 · Gongsanseong media art every night 19:30-22:00 · Buyeo historical procession Oct 9 · night parade and Nakhwa fire show Oct 10 · closing Oct 11

ISSUE 04 · #1068 · 2026-06-13

🎯 How to Use This Guide

The Baekje Cultural Festival (백제문화제) is two separate events that Gongju (공주) and Buyeo (부여) hold over the same period. There are two official sites (Gongju: gongju.baekje.org / Buyeo: baekjeculturalfestival.kr), and the programs and stages differ. Older guides mention a "main stage at Gudeurae (구드래)" or a "Baekje procession at Gongsanseong Fortress (공산성) at 11:00", but neither appears in the official schedule. What follows is based on what the two official sites showed on 2026-10-07 for the 72nd edition (2026-10-03 to 10-11, 9 days), and it lists only what remains after Oct 7. The festival is held every year in September or October, and the next year's schedule usually appears on the official sites in the first half of the year.

Already over: the Oct 3 opening, the Gongsanseong site-specific performance "Jeop-gyeong: The Night the Queen Crossed" (Oct 4-5), and the Ungjin Fortress parade (Oct 4). Fireworks and a drone show appear only in news reports; we could not find them in the official schedule.

🏯 Gongju: Geumgang Singwan Park and Gongsanseong Fortress

  • Theme: "The Bronze Mirror: Reflecting 1,500 Years of Baekje Light." The main stage is Geumgang Singwan Park, on the Geumgang riverbank, and the nightly media art is at Gongsanseong Fortress (UNESCO).
  • Fees (as posted officially): tickets for Mireu Island (미르섬) and the floating bridge (배다리) on the Geumgang are 7,000 won for adults and 6,000 won for teens. Entry is free for visitors in Baekje costume, ages 65 and over, elementary school students and Gongju residents, and the ticket includes a 3,000-won coupon. No fees are posted for the stage performances or media art, so we do not say whether they are free or paid.
  • Gongsanseong itself is separate from the festival: adults 3,000 won, 09:00-18:00 (#1043). A combined ticket with the Tomb of King Muryeong (무령왕릉) and the Royal Tombs is 6,000 won.
  • Getting there (official directions): KTX or SRT to Gongju Station (공주역) (about 1.5 hours from Yongsan), then a city bus or taxi; by car, use Gongju IC. The parking and shuttle information is posted only as images and we could not read it, so follow the on-site guidance.
  • We could not find an English menu on the Gongju site's front page (unconfirmed).

🪷 Buyeo: Jeongnimsa Temple Site, Seoktap-ro and Gwanbuk-ri

  • Theme: "Beautiful Baekje, Shining Sabi." The stages are at the Jeongnimsa Temple Site, Seoktap-ro and the Gwanbuk-ri ruins. Gudeurae is the setting for the Nakhwa fire show and the cosmos field.
  • English site available: baekjeculturalfestival.kr/en.
  • Key events still to come: the Oct 9 Baekje Historical and Cultural Procession at 15:30 · the Oct 10 night parade at 19:30 plus the Nakhwa fire show at 19:00 on the Gudeurae riverbank · the Oct 11 closing.
  • Heritage-site fees are separate from the festival: Jeongnimsa Temple Site (museum free) · Busosanseong Fortress (부소산성) 2,000 won · National Museum of Buyeo (국립부여박물관) free (closed Mondays) · Baekje Cultural Complex 6,000 won (closed Mondays) (#1041).
  • Getting there: Buyeo has no railway. Take an intercity bus from Seoul Nambu Terminal, or from Gongju (check travel time on the booking site). The shuttle and parking pages are images, so those details are unconfirmed.

🚶 One Night in Gongju and Buyeo (Based on the Remaining Days)

  • Oct 9 (Fri): afternoon, Buyeo historical procession at 15:30 → evening, Korea-China-Japan performance at 19:00 → stay overnight in Buyeo.
  • Oct 10 (Sat): daytime, Jeongnimsa Temple Site and Busosanseong Fortress → pick only one evening plan: either Buyeo's night parade at 19:30 plus the Nakhwa fire show at 19:00 (the same evening), or Gongju's Ungjin Fantasia at 21:00 plus the Gongsanseong media art. Do not shuttle between the two cities in the evening.
  • Oct 11 (Sun): Gongju's Tomb of King Muryeong and Gongju National Museum (국립공주박물관) → closing ceremony at 19:00.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Treating it as one festival and seeing only one city: the Gongju and Buyeo programs differ, and there are two official sites.

2. The old link baekje.kr: it does not open. Use gongju.baekje.org and baekjeculturalfestival.kr.

3. Going to see the site-specific performance or the Ungjin Fortress parade: they ended on Oct 4-5.

4. Switching between the two cities in the evening: taking the Gongju-Buyeo bus means you will miss performances. Stay in one city for the evening.

5. Assuming "everything is free": Mireu Island and the floating bridge are paid (7,000 won), and no fees are posted for the rest.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. What are the 2026 dates? Oct 3 (Sat) to Oct 11 (Sun), 9 days, the 72nd edition (official).

2. What can I see at night? In Gongju, media art 19:30-22:00 (every night) plus Ungjin Fantasia at 21:00 (through Oct 10); in Buyeo, the night parade and Nakhwa fire show on Oct 10.

3. What if I wear hanbok? Visitors in Baekje costume enter Mireu Island and the floating bridge in Gongju free (official).

4. Is there English information? The Buyeo site has an English version. Gongju is unconfirmed.

5. What about next year? Usually September or October. Check the official sites in the first half of the year.

6. Where should I stay? Gongju Hanok Village (공주한옥마을) (#1043) or downtown Buyeo. Rooms fill up early on festival weekends.
