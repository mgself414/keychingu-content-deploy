Korea Entry, Customs & Tax Refund Checklist: Before Departure, On Arrival and Before Leaving, Settled by the Numbers (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: K-ETA temporary exemption through 2026-12-31 · fee KRW 10,000 (Ministry of Justice (법무부)) · e-Arrival electronic arrival card opens 3 days before arrival, no fee (Ministry of Justice) · automated immigration clearance for 42 countries and regions (Ministry of Justice) · duty-free allowance USD 800 + liquor 2L / USD 400 + 200 cigarettes + 100ml perfume (Korea Customs Service (관세청)) · most meat and fresh fruit banned, up to KRW 10,000,000 for failing to declare (Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs (농림축산식품부)) · tax refund KRW 15,000 or more per receipt, immediate refund under KRW 1,000,000 per transaction and KRW 5,000,000 total (Ministry of Government Legislation (법제처) · Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사))

ISSUE 03 · #434 · 2026-05-26

🎯 How to use this guide

When two or three flights land at once, the line in front of the immigration booths at Incheon Airport (인천공항) gets long. Next to it is a shorter line for the automated gates, where you put your passport down, show your face and the gate opens, and since March 2026 passports from 42 countries and regions can use it (Ministry of Justice). Past baggage claim, the signs in front of customs say "Declare meat and fruit." This guide gathers only the numbers to check in three rounds: the week before your trip, the day you arrive and the day you leave. Every value was read on 2026-10-29 from the original pages of the Ministry of Justice, Korea Customs Service, Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs, Ministry of Government Legislation and Korea Tourism Organization, and for detailed procedures such as the screen-by-screen steps, each item links to its dedicated guide.

① One week before departure: two documents and a bag check

  • K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization): This is the permit that nationals who can enter without a visa get before boarding. The Ministry of Justice has extended the temporary exemption, introduced to boost tourism, by one more year and applies it from January 1 to December 31, 2026 (K-ETA notice, 2025-12-23). To find out whether your nationality is exempt, start an application on the K-ETA site and scan the personal data page of your passport, and a "K-ETA exemption" pop-up will tell you. Even if you are exempt you can still apply, and doing so earns you the benefit of skipping the arrival card. The fee is KRW 10,000 (about USD 7-8), review usually takes within 72 hours, a permit you already hold stays valid until its expiry date, and there are no refunds (K-ETA portal). The only official site is k-eta.go.kr.
  • e-Arrival Card (electronic arrival card): You submit it online instead of filling in a paper arrival card. You can report from 3 days before arrival and there is no fee (Ministry of Justice, e-arrivalcard.go.kr). In the site's "Navigator," choose which of visa, K-ETA or alien registration card you hold and it tells you whether you need to report. Travelers with K-ETA approval can skip the arrival card (Ministry of Justice K-ETA notice). A site that asks for payment details is not the official one (Ministry of Justice warning about look-alike sites). For the screen-by-screen steps, see #1289 e-Arrival manual.
  • Medicine you take: Medicines containing narcotic or psychotropic ingredients need prior approval from the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (식품의약품안전처) → #1352 Bringing medication into Korea.
  • What to take out of your bag: Processed meats such as jerky, sausage and ham, fresh fruit and seeds usually cannot be brought into Korea (see ④). Taking them out before you leave is easier than declaring them at the airport.
  • Clothes and air: For monthly temperatures and what to wear, see #518 Monthly weather and outfits. For spring and winter fine dust, check live readings on AirKorea (에어코리아) of the Ministry of Environment (airkorea.or.kr, and an app of the same name).

② Arrival: choosing an immigration line

  • Regular booths: Have your passport and your K-ETA approval or e-Arrival submission ready, and register your fingerprints and face.
  • Automated immigration clearance (SES): It started on 2025-11-03 with 4 countries and regions (Germany, Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macao), grew to 18 on 2025-12-01, and reached 42 countries and regions on March 16, 2026 (Ministry of Justice, 2026-06-01). The list includes Japan, the UK, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, Singapore, Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macao, and the US and mainland China are not on it. The gates are in the east and west arrival halls of Incheon Airport Terminal 1 (인천공항 제1여객터미널) and Terminal 2 (제2여객터미널), and in the Jeju Airport (제주공항) arrival hall. On departure you can use the automated gates without registering → #1412 Automated clearance & Smart Pass.
  • From the airport into the city: For express train versus all-stops train, start with #1285 Incheon Airport to the city, and for the airport railroad, #1249 AREX.

③ Customs: duty-free allowance and declarations (Korea Customs Service)

  • Basic allowance: The total taxable value of goods bought overseas and in duty-free shops is USD 800 or less.
  • Separately exempt items: Liquor is 2L or less in total volume and USD 400 or less in total price (no limit on the number of bottles) · tobacco is 200 filter cigarettes (for e-cigarettes, 20ml of nicotine liquid with nicotine content under 1%) · 100ml of perfume.
  • Under age 19 (by year of birth) there is no duty-free allowance for liquor or tobacco.
  • If you go over: Declaring voluntarily gets you a 30% reduction of the duty, while being caught without declaring adds a penalty tax of 40% of the tax due (60% for 2 or more offenses within 2 years).
  • Money: Payment instruments worth more than USD 10,000 (cash, checks and so on) must be declared.
  • What you cannot bring: Obscene materials and counterfeit or altered currency and securities are banned. Firearms, narcotics and CITES-protected plants and animals and their products are restricted.
  • What you must declare: Animals (including meat, leather and fur) and fruit must be declared to customs. This leads into ④.

④ Quarantine: leave the meat and fruit behind (Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs)

  • Livestock products (meat and processed products) and fresh fruit are mostly banned from import, and even permitted items cannot be brought in without the exporting country's quarantine certificate (Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs, 2024-09-01). If you did bring them in, you must declare them to the Animal and Plant Quarantine Agency (농림축산검역본부) at the airport or port.
  • If you are caught without declaring, you may be fined up to KRW 10,000,000 (Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs).
  • If you open your bag and remember, put the items in the arrival hall's voluntary declaration bin or tell a quarantine officer in person, and they are disposed of on the spot with no fine (government Policy Briefing (정책브리핑)). If the same person brings them in again, it is treated as intentional and subject to a fine.

⑤ While shopping: three tax refund numbers

  • Who and from how much: Foreigners staying in Korea for less than 6 months, at stores with a Tax Free sign, KRW 15,000 or more per receipt, and you must take the goods out of the country within 3 months of purchase (Korea Tourism Organization, 2025-10-20).
  • Immediate refund (paying a price with tax already taken off at the store): Available only when the value of one transaction including tax is under KRW 1,000,000 and the total transaction value of immediate-refund purchases since entering Korea is KRW 5,000,000 or less (Special Provisions for Foreign Tourists, Article 6 paragraph 2, in force 2026-01-02).
  • Downtown refund counters: KRW 6,000,000 or less per case (Korea Tourism Organization).
  • Airport refund: On departure, show the goods, the refund slip and your passport to customs to get the export confirmed, then collect the refund at a refund counter or an unmanned kiosk. The airport refund does not have the KRW 1,000,000 cap of the immediate refund (Korea Tourism Organization). For the kiosk screen-by-screen steps, see #1304 Tax refund kiosk, and for the whole system, #1231 Tax refund guide.

⑥ Departure: four steps in order

1. Tax refund: Customs export confirmation → counter or kiosk → refund (⑤).

2. Duty-free pickup: Items bought at downtown or online duty-free shops are collected at the pickup area inside the departure hall → #1332 Duty-free pickup area.

3. Self check-in and bag drop: #1293 Departure self check-in.

4. Automated departure clearance: Short-stay foreigners can also pass without registering (Ministry of Justice) → #1412 Automated clearance & Smart Pass. For a full check before you head to the airport, see #1396 Pre-departure checklist.

🧰 Practical: phone, payment and manners in one line each

  • Phone: Tourist interpretation hotline 1330 (from abroad, +82-2-1330, in Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese) · police 112 · fire and ambulance 119. For hospital and pharmacy routes, see #433 Korea Emergency Map.
  • Payment: Cards are the default, and small amounts of cash are handy at markets and old shops. There is no tipping → #1275 Tipping and paying manners.
  • Manners: Take your shoes off at floor-seating restaurants and in hanok (한옥, traditional Korean houses), leave the priority seats on the subway empty, and queue at the back. For how to order when you can't communicate, see #1339 Ordering etiquette.

⚠ 4 common mistakes

1. "I'm K-ETA exempt, so I don't have to file anything": Without K-ETA you still have to file the e-Arrival electronic arrival card. It opens 3 days before arrival and is free (Ministry of Justice).

2. Paying on a look-alike site at the top of the search results: The official sites are k-eta.go.kr and e-arrivalcard.go.kr, and e-Arrival has no fee. A site that asks for payment details is not official (Ministry of Justice).

3. Leftover fruit or jerky from your in-flight meal in your bag: Up to KRW 10,000,000 fine if you are caught without declaring (Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs). If you put it in the arrival hall's voluntary declaration bin, there is no fine.

4. Expecting an in-store immediate refund on an item over KRW 1,000,000: The immediate refund covers only transactions under KRW 1,000,000. For anything above that, take the slip and go to the airport refund counter or kiosk (Ministry of Government Legislation · Korea Tourism Organization).
