⚠ 3 Common Practical Mistakes

1. "Visa-free = no application needed" — not knowing K-ETA: People only know "visa-free". In fact, K-ETA (electronic travel authorization, advance application, ₩30,000 (~$23), valid 90 days) is mandatory for the 32 visa-free countries (since 2021).

2. "Tax Free = automatic" — not knowing the refund process: People assume it's automatic. In fact, you need a ₩30,000+ (~$23+) purchase at a member store + a Tax Free receipt + the Incheon Airport refund counter. Refund rate: 7–10%.

3. "Korean etiquette = same as Japan" — not knowing the differences: People assume they are identical. In fact, Korean etiquette = removing shoes (Korean restaurants, hanok (한옥) traditional houses), respecting elders, Article 8 senior seats, queuing + more casual than Japan.
