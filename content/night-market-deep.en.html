Korean Night Markets Open This Winter — Busan Bupyeong Kkangtong, Jeju Dongmun, Jeonju Nambu Market, the December Gwanghwamun Market, and the Ones That Are Resting Now

Subtitle: Busan Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market and Jeju Dongmun Night Market, which open every day, Jeonju Nambu Market Hanok Village Night Market, which opens on Fridays and Saturdays, Daegu Seomun Night Market, which the Daegu city plan says runs through December, and Seoul's Gwanghwamun Market, which opens on December 11. For each place we wrote the opening days and hours, winter closures, how to get there, and dietary information. Payment methods and prices are listed only where an announcement exists, and where there is none we say so plainly. The places where winter operation is unconfirmed and the places that are resting now are grouped separately.

ISSUE 03 · #544 · 2026-11-25

🎯 Who This Is For

This piece is for travelers who come to Korea between late November and February and want to eat market food at night. Korean night markets come in two main kinds. One is the permanent night market, which the market itself opens on set days each week. The other is the seasonal night market, which a local government opens for a set period, such as spring to autumn or the year-end season. In winter most seasonal markets rest, and some permanent night markets also rest in winter.

So this piece puts first the places where we opened the market, local government, or tourism organization notices on 2026-11-25 and confirmed winter operation (for Daegu Seomun Night Market (서문야시장), the basis is the Daegu city plan of March). Places we could not confirm from a notice come next, labeled 'winter operation unconfirmed'. Places whose 2026 season has ended, or where we found no notice for this year, are moved to the last section, 'Resting now'.

⚠ Notation rule: Next to every time and period we attached the source and the check date. "Official" is used only for values taken directly from the original text of the operating body (the market merchants' association, the local government, or the regional tourism organization). "Korea Tourism Organization listing" means the value registered in the Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) tourism information database (TourAPI). Kakao Map (카카오맵) values are the ones registered on the place page. Night markets may close early or rest because of weather, holidays, or sold-out ingredients, so check the notice again the day before you go.

① Permanent Night Markets That Open in Winter Too — 3 Places

1. Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market (부평깡통야시장) (Bupyeong-dong (부평동), Jung-gu (중구), Busan)

The name "Kkangtong Market" (깡통시장), literally "Can Market", came from the U.S. military goods, imported goods, and canned food that circulated here after the Korean War (Korea Tourism Organization listing, introduction). In the evening, food stalls line the market street. According to the Korea Tourism Organization's introduction, along a street of about 110m there are 11 local-food stalls, 6 multicultural food stalls (Japanese, Filipino, and others), and 13 clothing and accessory stalls.

  • Days and hours: Every day 19:30~24:00, no closing days (hours vary by shop) (Visit Busan (비짓부산), 2026-11-25). The Korea Tourism Organization listing gives the night market as 19:30~23:30, and Kakao Map (카카오맵) gives every day 19:30~24:00 (2026-11-25). The closing time differs by source, so arriving before 23:00 is safer.
  • Winter closure: The Visit Busan guide says no closing days. We found no winter closure notice (2026-11-25).
  • Getting there: From Exit 3 of Jagalchi Station (자갈치역) on Busan Metro Line 1, 9 minutes on foot. By bus, take 8, 11, 96, 103, 113, or Jung-gu 1 (중구1번), get off at Bupyeong Market (부평시장), and walk 2 minutes (Visit Busan). For parking, use a nearby paid lot.
  • Payment: The Visit Busan guide gives no payment methods. It may differ from stall to stall, so bring some cash.
  • Food and prices: Visit Busan lists red tteokbokki (spicy rice cakes, 떡볶이), mul-tteok (rice cakes on a skewer in fish broth, 물떡), old-style fried whole chicken (옛날 통닭), eomuk (fish cakes, 어묵), and yubu-jeongol (a fried tofu pouch hot pot, 유부전골). Prices are only given as "vary by shop" (가게별 상이), and there is no official price list. We could not confirm whether these signature foods also appear at the night market stalls.
  • Dietary notes: Eomuk (어묵) is made from fish meat. Fish cakes often go into the tteokbokki broth too, so if you avoid seafood, ask before you order. We could not confirm any vegetarian or halal certified stalls.

2. Dongmun Night Market (동문야시장) (Ildo 1-dong (일도일동), Jeju City (제주시))

A food street that opens in the evening inside Dongmun Traditional Market (동문재래시장). Dongmun Traditional Market was founded right after liberation in 1945 and is Jeju's representative permanent market (Korea Tourism Organization listing, introduction). It is close to the airport and Jeju City's center (Visit Jeju (비짓제주)).

  • Days and hours: No closing days (holidays included). Winter season (11~4, November to April) 18:00~24:00, summer season (5~10, May to October) 19:00~24:00. It closes when ingredients run out (Visit Jeju, 2026-11-25). On Kakao Map, "Jeju Dongmun Traditional Night Market" (제주동문재래야시장) is listed as every day 18:00~23:00 (2026-11-25). The closing time differs by source, so going before 23:00 is safer.
  • Winter closure: None. In winter it opens at 18:00, an hour earlier than in summer (Visit Jeju).
  • Getting there: Buses that pass both Jeju International Airport and Dongmun Rotary (동문로터리) are 316, 325, and 43-2 (Jeju Bus Information System (제주버스정보시스템), checked 2026-11-07). Get off at Dongmun Rotary. The adult fare on a trunk bus is 1,150 won with a transit card and 1,200 won in cash (Jeju Provincial Government (제주도청), checked 2026-11-07). Check the route again before you board.
  • Payment: The Visit Jeju market guide gives no payment methods. Bring some cash.
  • Food and prices: Visit Jeju lists omegi-tteok (Jeju millet rice cakes, 오메기떡), bing-tteok (buckwheat crepes rolled around radish filling, 빙떡), Hallabong and tangerines (한라봉·귤), and black pork (흑돼지) dishes, and a photo caption mentions night market stalls selling grilled black pork skewers and lobster dishes. On Kakao Map, "Night Market Mobile Stall No. 2" (야시장 이동식판매대 2호) lists black pork bulgogi rice (흑돼지 불고기 라이스) and black pork bulgogi yakisoba (흑돼지 불고기 야끼소바) at 9,000 won each (one stall's price registered on 2025-02-03, viewed 2026-11-25, and it may differ from now). Check other stalls' prices on site. We could not confirm whether the market's signature foods that Visit Jeju lists are also at the night market stalls.
  • Dietary notes: Many dishes are black pork (pork) and seafood. If you avoid pork, choose the rice cakes and fruit, which contain no meat, and ask the stall about ingredients.

3. Jeonju Nambu Market Hanok Village Night Market (전주 남부시장 한옥마을야시장) (Jeon-dong (전동), Wansan-gu (완산구), Jeonju)

A night market that opens on weekend nights inside Nambu Market (남부시장), next to Jeonju Hanok Village (전주 한옥마을). The market merchants' association calls it a "permanent weekend night market" (상설주말 夜시장) (merchants' association notice, 2026-11-13).

  • Days and hours: Every Friday and Saturday 17:00~23:00. Hours may change from time to time. When Thursday is a holiday it opens Thu·Fri·Sat, and when Monday is a holiday it opens Fri·Sat·Sun. These are the operating terms in the "2027 Night Market Stall Operator Recruitment" (2027 야시장 매대 운영자 모집) notice the merchants' association posted on 2026-11-13, and the Kakao Map registration (Fri·Sat 17:00~23:00, closed other days) is the same (2026-11-25).
  • Winter closure: It opened last winter. On 2025-12-09 the merchants' association announced extended openings for Christmas and New Year (12/25~27, 1/1~3, 17:00~23:00), and on 2026-02-07 an extended opening for the Seollal (Lunar New Year) holiday (2/13~15). But on 2023-12-22 it also announced a January and February 2024 closure for "renovation" (재정비), and there is precedent for resting in holiday weeks (closed 9/29~30 for Chuseok 2023, and a rest announced for 9/25~26 in the 2026 Chuseok week). As of 2026-11-25 there is no closure notice for this winter in the notice list. If you plan to go during the 2027 Seollal holiday, check the merchants' association blog notice first.
  • Getting there: The night market is around Pungnammun 2-gil 49 (풍남문2길 49) (Kakao Map). The address of Nambu Market is Pungnammun 1-gil 19-3 (풍남문1길 19-3) (Korea Tourism Organization listing). It is near Pungnammun (Jeonju's South Gate, 풍남문), and the merchants' association calls this night market the "Hanok Village Night Market" (한옥마을야시장).
  • Payment: The Korea Tourism Organization listing says card payment at Nambu Market is "possible (varies by shop)" (가능(점포마다 상이)). Bring some cash for the mobile stalls.
  • Food and prices: The price cap for mobile stall food differs by merchants' association notice. The 2026-02-07 notice said 6,000 won or less (shops excluded), and the 2026-11-13 recruitment notice for 2027 operators said 7,000 won or less. Check the actual prices this winter on site. The Korea Tourism Organization introduction lists pi-sundae jeongsik (blood sausage set meal, 피순대 정식), mung bean pancake (녹두전), and bibimbap roll (비빔밥 롤) as popular dishes. We could not confirm whether these also appear at the night market stalls.
  • Dietary notes: Pi-sundae (피순대) is a sausage with pig's blood, glass noodles, and vegetables stuffed into pig intestine. Mung bean pancake (녹두전) too is sometimes made with pork at some shops, so if you are vegetarian, ask before you order.

② Seasonal Night Markets Open Only in Winter or Through December — 2 Places

4. Daegu Seomun Night Market (서문야시장) (Daesin-dong (대신동), Jung-gu (중구), Daegu) — Through December

A night market held around Daegu Seomun Market (서문시장), which has carried on since the Joseon period.

  • Days and hours: Fri·Sat 19:00~23:30, Sun 19:00~22:30. The 2026 season opened on March 27, and the Daegu city plan runs from March to December (Daegu city news, 2026-03-26). The Korea Tourism Organization listing gives the same hours and says it rests Mon~Thu.
  • Winter closure: When it announced the reopening in March 2026, Daegu city wrote that it had ended the "roughly 3-month winter closure" (동절기 휴장) (Daegu city news, 2026-03-26). If the March plan holds, it will again rest after operating in December this winter. We have not yet found a notice of the last December operating day (2026-11-25). If you go in December, check Daegu city or Seomun Market notices again. We also found no guide to the night market's operating period on the Seomun Market website. For night market inquiries, call 053-710-6353 (Korea Tourism Organization listing).
  • Getting there: Daegu Metro Line 3, Seomun Market Station (서문시장역). Kakao Map puts the walk at 3 minutes from Exit 3 to Seomun Market (2026-11-25).
  • Payment: The Daegu city guide gives no payment methods.
  • Food and prices: The dishes Daegu city lists are stir-fried octopus in butter (문어버터볶음), lamb skewers (양꼬치), grilled makchang (grilled beef or pork intestine, 막창구이), kabetsuyaki (a cabbage pancake, 카베츠야키), and gochujang bulgogi tacos (고추장 불백 타코) (Daegu city news, 2026-03-26). There is no official price list, and the Kakao Map night market stall page lists no prices either (2026-11-25).
  • Dietary notes: The lamb skewers are lamb, and the stir-fried octopus is seafood and butter. You cannot tell from the menu name alone whether makchang (막창) and bulgogi (불백) are beef or pork, so ask the stall.

5. Gwanghwamun Market (광화문 마켓) (Gwanghwamun Square (광화문광장), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul) — 2026-12-11~2027-01-10

The market of the Seoul Winter Festival (서울윈터페스티벌), Seoul's year-end festival. Booths stretch from the south side of Gwanghwamun Square to the Garden of Gratitude (감사의 정원) and Sejongno Park (세종로공원), with Christmas goods and food booths, booths run together with the Namdaemun Market (남대문시장) merchants' association, and a regional specialty market called "Seoro Jangteo" (서로장터) (Seoul Media Hub (서울시 미디어허브) 2026-11-25). It is not a permanent night market, but it is a place to buy market food in Seoul in the evening this winter.

  • Period and hours: 2026-12-11~2027-01-10 (Seoul Media Hub 2026-11-25 · Visit Seoul (비짓서울)). Sun~Thu 17:30~22:00, Fri·Sat 17:30~22:30 (Visit Seoul English guide, revised 2026-11-06). Admission is free (Visit Seoul).
  • Getting there: 181m from Exit 9 of Gwanghwamun Station (광화문역) on Line 5 (Visit Seoul).
  • Payment and prices: The Seoul city and Visit Seoul guides give no payment methods or food prices. Inquiries: 02-3788-8168 (Visit Seoul).
  • Dietary notes: The booth lineup has not been announced yet, so we could not confirm.

③ Winter Operation Unconfirmed — 4 Places

The four places below have operating hours in the Korea Tourism Organization listing or on Kakao Map, but we could not open a notice from the operating body about this winter (2026-11-25). Confirm by phone or the official social media before you go.

6. Jeju Sumokwon-gil Night Market (수목원길 야시장) (Yeon-dong (연동), Jeju City (제주시))

A night market set up inside a pine forest, with handmade goods, food trucks, and game experiences (Korea Tourism Organization listing, introduction).

  • Listed hours: The Korea Tourism Organization listing gives no closing days, 9~5 (September to May) 18:00~22:00 and 6~8 (June to August) 18:00~23:00 (revised 2026-11-05). Kakao Map gives every day 18:00~22:00 for 10~5 (October to May) and 18:00~23:00 for 6~9 (June to September) (2026-11-25). Both say it opens in winter, but we could not open the operator's notice.
  • Address and inquiries: Eunsu-gil 69 (은수길 69), Sumokwon Theme Park (수목원테마파크) 064-742-3700 (Korea Tourism Organization listing). Parking is registered as free.

7. Ulsan Keunaegi Youth Night Market (울산큰애기 청년야시장) (Okgyo-dong (옥교동), Jung-gu (중구), Ulsan)

A night market inside Ulsan Jungang Traditional Market (울산 중앙전통시장). The Korea Tourism Organization introduction says you can eat the market's specialties, gomjangeo (hagfish, 곰장어) and old-style fried whole chicken (옛날 통닭), together.

  • Listed hours: 18:30~23:30, closed every Mon and Tue (Korea Tourism Organization listing, revised 2026-11-20). Kakao Map is the same (2026-11-25). The night market's official blog has had no posts since December 2018.
  • Dietary notes: Gomjangeo (곰장어) is hagfish (a sea fish). The fried chicken is chicken.
  • Inquiries: Jungang Traditional Market merchants' association 052-246-6660 (Korea Tourism Organization listing).

8. Gyeongju Jungang Market Night Market (경주 중앙시장 야시장) (Seonggeon-dong (성건동), Gyeongju)

  • Listed hours: The Korea Tourism Organization introduction says it opens every Thu~Sun and registers no hours (checked 2026-11-07). The Gyeongju city webzine (no publication date shown) says Thu~Sun 19:00~23:00, closed on the 1st and 15th of each month. Kakao Map says Wed~Sun 18:00~23:00 (2026-11-25). The three sources disagree, so we cannot tell whether it operates this winter.
  • Address: Geumseong-ro 295 (금성로 295) (Kakao Map).

9. Jinju Nongae Market Olppam Saturday Night Market (진주 논개시장 올빰토요야시장) (Jangdae-dong (장대동), Jinju)

  • Listed hours: Every Saturday 18:00~23:00. The Korea Tourism Organization listing says to check the website because "opening and closing dates are set each year" (매년 개장일과 폐장일이 정해져 있으므로) (revised 2026-05-14). In 2024 it reopened on August 3 and ran through October (Sisa Journal (시사저널) 2024-08-02). We could not find the 2026 closing date.
  • Inquiries: 055-790-9238 (Korea Tourism Organization listing).

  • Pohang Yeongilman Friends Night Market (포항 영일만친구야시장) — By the Korea Tourism Organization listing it opens every year from mid-August to the end of September, Fri~Sun 18:00~22:00 (revised 2025-11-04).

🗺 Routes — 2 Ways

A. First evening in Jeju City (November to April)

  1. From the airport, take bus 316, 325, or 43-2 to Dongmun Rotary (동문로터리)
  2. Dongmun Night Market (동문야시장) after 18:00. Stalls close first when ingredients run out, so go early in the evening

B. Night in the old downtown of Busan (every day)

  1. Look around the old downtown near Jagalchi Station (자갈치역) around sunset
  2. Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market (부평깡통야시장) after 19:30. 9 minutes on foot from Exit 3 of Jagalchi Station

Jeonju's night market opens only on Fridays and Saturdays, so time a one-night stay in Jeonju to a Friday or Saturday night.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going on any day — Jeonju Nambu Market opens only Fri·Sat, and Daegu Seomun only Fri·Sat·Sun. The places this piece confirmed as open every day are just two: Bupyeong Kkangtong and Dongmun.
  2. Going on the summer schedule — Dongmun Night Market opens at 18:00 in 11~4 (November to April) and at 19:00 in 5~10 (May to October) (Visit Jeju). Daegu Seomun Night Market rests after December, and Chilseong Night Market runs only to November (Daegu city).
  3. Looking for the Han River Night Market in winter — For the Seoul Han River Night Market, the last round confirmed from official material was in 2022, the last by press reports was Yeouido in September and October 2023, and we found no announcement for 2024~2026. This winter in Seoul, Gwanghwamun Market (광화문 마켓) opens from December 11.

⚙ 3 FAQs

  1. Can I pay by card? — Among the night markets in this piece, the number where we confirmed a payment method from an official guide is 0. Jeonju's card "possible (varies by shop)" (가능(점포마다 상이)) is a value the Korea Tourism Organization registered for Nambu Market shops, so bring cash for the night market's mobile stalls. Bring some cash for the others too.
  2. Can I avoid pork, or eat vegetarian? — We could not confirm any halal or vegetarian certified stalls. Judging by the dishes in the Korea Tourism Organization and Visit Jeju introductions, pork dishes are central at Jeju Dongmun (black pork) and Jeonju (pi-sundae), and Busan Bupyeong Kkangtong has seafood dishes such as eomuk (어묵). Rice cakes and fruit are the meat-free choices, and for pancakes (전), ask the stall whether pork goes in.
  3. What about the market's daytime hours? — The city market guides cover daytime hours and closing days. Busan #610, Jeju #634, Jeonju #642, Daegu #641, Gwangju #643, Gyeongju #664, Jinju #665.
