Korea's Film Festival Calendar: 7 Festivals Worth Visiting as a Foreigner, with 2026 Official Dates and How to Go

Subtitle: Busan (BIFF, October) · Jeonju (JIFF, late April–May) · Bucheon (BIFAN, July) · Jecheon Music Film Festival (September) · DMZ Docs (September, Goyang) · Ulju Mountain Film Festival (September) · Seoul International Women's Film Festival (August). Edition numbers, dates and venues follow each festival's official site as checked on 2026-10-07. Which ones have an English site, and how to book.

Operating info check: 2026-05-31 WebSearch (#223, #548 + VisitKorea and official BIFF).

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Korea's festival year has a clear shape: Jeonju in spring, Bucheon in summer, Busan in autumn, with a run of music, documentary and mountain film festivals through September. For foreign visitors we kept only 7 festivals that have an English site and a confirmed official schedule. The editions, dates and venues below are the values read on each festival's official site on 2026-10-07. Ticket prices are not posted on the official pages year-round, so we do not list them. Check the official site when booking opens.

As of October 7, 2026, BIFF is under way. If you missed it this year, next year's schedule usually appears on the official site in the first half of the year.

🌊 Busan: BIFF (October)

  • Where: Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당) in Centum City is the main venue, and Shinsegae Centum City Culture Hall (신세계 센텀시티 문화홀) is used alongside it (official). If you stay around Haeundae or Centum City, you can get around on foot.
  • How: Check screening times and book on the official English site. Opening films and popular titles often sell out right after booking opens, so confirm the opening date and time first.
  • Pair it with: Haeundae (해운대) and Gwangalli (광안리) night views (#243 to #247), and Busan pork-and-rice soup, dwaeji-gukbap (#807).

🌸 Jeonju: JIFF (Late April–May)

  • In 2026 it runs Apr 29–May 8 (27th edition). Screens are spread around downtown Jeonju (전주), and Jeonju Hanok Village (전주 한옥마을) is close, so it is easy to stroll between films (#384).
  • The official site is jeonjufest.kr (English available). The old address jiff.or.kr does not open because of a certificate error.

👻 Bucheon: BIFAN (July)

  • A genre and fantastic film festival. In 2026 it runs Jul 2–12 (30th edition). From Seoul, take subway Lines 1 and 7 to Bucheon (부천).
  • The official abbreviation is BIFAN. ('BFFC' in the old guide is a wrong abbreviation.)

🍂 September: Music, Documentary and Mountain Films

  • Jecheon International Music & Film Festival JIMFF (제천국제음악영화제), Sep 3–8 (22nd edition): known for music films, concerts and outdoor screenings at Cheongpungho Lake (청풍호).
  • DMZ International Documentary Film Festival (DMZ국제다큐멘터리영화제), Sep 10–16 (18th edition, Goyang (고양)): documentaries only. With an English catalog, it is the easiest documentary festival for foreign visitors.
  • Ulju Mountain Film Festival UMFF (울주세계산악영화제), Sep 18–22 (11th edition, Ulju, Ulsan (울산 울주)): mountain and adventure films, screened outdoors in the foothills of the Yeongnam Alps (영남알프스).

🎥 Seoul: Seoul International Women's Film Festival SIWFF (August)

  • 28th edition, held in August (official). Check the official notice for exact dates and venues. An English site is available.

📌 Festivals Without Official Confirmation (For Reference Only)

  • Busan International Short Film Festival (April), Seoul Independent Film Festival (Nov–Dec), EBS International Documentary Festival (August), Mise-en-scene Short Film Festival (June). Their 2026 schedules could not be read on the official sites (403 errors, redirects or nothing posted), so this guide gives no dates for them. To go, check each festival's official site directly.

🎟 Booking and Language Tips

  • Festivals with an English site: BIFF, JIFF, BIFAN and SIWFF. DMZ has an English catalog. The rest have Korean-language sites, so use a translation app.
  • Subtitles: Many screenings add Korean subtitles to foreign films and English subtitles to Korean films, but it differs from film to film. Check the subtitle note in the screening information (the official pages state no blanket policy).
  • Price: Not posted on the official pages year-round. Check the price for each screening when booking opens.
  • Lodging: Rooms in Haeundae and Centum City during BIFF, and in Jeonju Hanok Village during JIFF, fill up early.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Assuming Jeonju is only in May: In 2026 it starts on April 29. Look at the late-April schedule.

2. Searching for 'BFFC': Bucheon's festival is BIFAN.

3. Looking for BIFF near Busan Station: Busan Cinema Center is in Centum City (Haeundae District).

4. Going to the DDP 'VR film festival': It is not a film festival (it was an item in the old guide). For documentaries, go to the DMZ festival in Goyang.

5. Taking subtitles for granted: Check the subtitle note for each screening.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Where should I go first? BIFF in October. It is the biggest in scale, English support and city appeal.

2. Which festivals can I do as a day trip from Seoul? Bucheon BIFAN (July), Goyang DMZ Docs (September) and Seoul SIWFF (August).

3. What if I come in spring? Jeonju JIFF (Apr 29–May 8). Pair it with Hanok Village.

4. Which have outdoor screenings? Jecheon (Cheongpungho Lake) and Ulju (Yeongnam Alps).

5. How much are tickets? Not posted on the official pages year-round. Check when booking opens.

6. What about 2027 dates? As of 2026-10-07, none of the festivals has been officially announced.
