Geoje Guide — Oedo Botania, Haegeumgang Cruise, POW Camp and Jungle Dome at Official Prices: A 2-Day, 1-Night Trip for Foreign Visitors (October 2026)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

The old guide said "cruise 22,000–30,000" (the official range is 16,000–22,000) and "an island tended by one individual for 30 years" (official: tended since 1969 by the couple Lee Chang-ho and Choi Ho-suk). It placed the botanic garden in the Gohyeon (고현)/Okpo area (it is actually in Geoje-myeon (거제면)) and Hakdong Mongdol Beach in Nambu-myeon (남부면) (it is in Dongbu-myeon (동부면)). Its rental car, pension and oyster prices, and "1 hour from Busan (부산) over the Geoga Bridge (거가대교)," had no source. This time we list only values read on the official Geoje City culture and tourism, Oedo Botania and Geoje Botanic Garden pages on 2026-10-08. Values not on the official pages (Windy Hill fee, POW camp hours, Geoga Bridge toll, Busan bus) are left as "not posted / unverified."

⛴ Getting to Oedo — 6 Piers, and You Add the Two Fees Together

  • The cruise fare and the 11,000 Oedo admission are separate. Example: Jangseungpo (장승포) weekend adult 22,000 + 11,000 = 33,000 won. The old "22,000–30,000" left out the admission.
  • Piers: Jangseungpo (장승포) (138 Jangseung-ro, 055-681-6565) · Jisepo (지세포) (72 Jisepo Haean-ro, Iun-myeon, 055-682-5577) · Wahyeon (와현) (46 Wahyeon Haebyeon-gil, 055-681-2211) · Gujora (구조라) (81-2 Gujora-ri, 055-681-1188) · Haegeumgang (해금강) (270 Haegeumgang-ro, Nambu-myeon, 055-633-1352) · Dojangpo (도장포) (53 Dojangpo 1-gil, 055-632-8787). The route lands you on Oedo and adds an onboard view of Haegeumgang; sailings are cancelled in bad weather.
  • Oedo has been tended since 1969 by the couple Lee Chang-ho and Choi Ho-suk (official). Summer 08–19, winter 08:30–17.

🏛 Gohyeon — POW Camp and Monorail

  • Geoje POW Camp Historic Park (거제포로수용소유적공원) 7,000 won (official fee table). Hours and closing days are not on the Geoje City page (secondary: 09–18, closed Tuesdays), so call before you go.
  • Geoje Tourist Monorail (거제 관광모노레일) 09–18 (last boarding 16:00), open year-round, day 18,000 (park admission included) and night 23,000 (official). If you pair the park with the monorail, the one monorail ticket covers both.

🌴 Geoje-myeon — Jungle Dome

  • 09:30–18 (entry 17:00), closed Wednesdays, Jan 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok. 5,000 won, free for ages 6 and under and 65 and over, same-day on-site tickets only (official). The old "largest dome in Korea" claim is unverified, and a secondary source's "closed Mondays, Naver booking" differs from the official page. The address is not in Gohyeon but in Geoje-myeon (거제면), 3595 Geoje Namseo-ro.

🌊 South and East — Windy Hill, Hakdong, Maemiseong

  • The official Geoje City page gives only addresses and phone numbers, with no fees or hours: Windy Hill (바람의언덕) and Sinseondae (신선대) in Nambu-myeon (남부면) (Galgot-ri), Hakdong Black Pearl Mongdol Beach (학동 흑진주 몽돌해변) in Dongbu-myeon (동부면), and Maemiseong (매미성) in Jangmok-myeon (장목면). We do not assume any of them is free.

🚌 Getting There — What We Could Not Confirm

  • Busan to Geoje intercity bus: only the main page of the unified booking site opened, so fares and times are unverified. The Geoga Bridge (거가대교) toll is not stated because secondary sources conflict (10,000 vs 2,000). The old "1 hour from Busan" also had no source.
  • Geoje Island Flower Festival (거제 섬꽃축제): the official page says only "October–November," and the 2026 schedule is not posted. There are cancellation reports, but we could not confirm their text, so check Geoje City announcements before you plan.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Going to the Jungle Dome on a Wednesday — it is closed.

2. Counting only the cruise fare — Oedo admission of 11,000 is separate.

3. Assuming Windy Hill is free — the official page does not say.

4. Trusting POW camp hours — not officially posted. Call.

5. Fixing a date for the Island Flower Festival — 2026 not posted, cancellation reports unconfirmed.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Total cost for Oedo? — Cruise 16,000–22,000 + admission 11,000 (adult). Jangseungpo on a weekend is 33,000.

2. Which pier? — There are 6. From Jangseungpo (장승포), Oct 8 departures are 09:30 and 13:30 (official).

3. Jungle Dome reservations? — Same-day on-site tickets only, closed Wednesdays.

4. POW camp plus monorail? — The 18,000 monorail ticket includes the park.

5. How do I get there from Busan? — Bus fare and times are unverified. Use the unified booking site.

6. Maemuldo? — The Geoje City page only links to the ferry operator. Unverified.
