Foreigner's Geoje Travel Guide — Oedo Botania·Windy Hill·Maemuldo on the South Coast

> Subtitle: Complete guide to Geoje (Gyeongnam) for foreigners — a "south-sea island scenery + camellia garden" town unlike Busan·Tongyeong · Oedo Botania·Windy Hill·Sinseondae·Maemuldo + cruise·oyster·rental-car matrix

ISSUE 04 · #1227 · 2026-07-03

🎯 Why a "Geoje" Guide

Geoje (거제, Gyeongnam) = Korea's 2nd-largest island·the Oedo Botania garden island·the cliffs of Windy Hill. The biggest foreigner misconception = "Gyeongnam islands = only Tongyeong." In reality Geoje stands apart from Tongyeong·Busan on an Oedo (marine garden)·Windy Hill·pebble beach·cruise axis. Pain points = (a) Tongyeong bias hides Geoje's existence (b) access·the Geoga Bridge from Busan (c) how to take the Oedo·Maemuldo cruise (d) local oyster·codfish. "Geoje island Korea / Oedo Botania" is a long-tail search on Reddit·YouTube.

This guide covers Geoje's 12 signatures + Oedo·Windy Hill + Maemuldo cruise + rental-car routes + oyster matrix.

🏝 The 6 Major Geoje Spots — Deep Dive

① Oedo Botania (외도 보타니아)

  • Signature: a marine botanical island one family tended for 30 years·camellia·palm·exotic garden·reached by cruise
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★★ (marine garden·cruise·SNS)
  • Tip: Jangseungpo/Gujora cruise·pair with Haegeumgang course·spring camellia·summer flowers

② Windy Hill (바람의언덕)

  • Signature: a grassy hill above the sea·windmill·drama filming site
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★★ (grassland·scenery·photos)
  • Tip: bundle with adjacent Sinseondae·at sunset·Dojangpo village

③ Sinseondae (신선대)

  • Signature: rock coast·sea view·next to Windy Hill
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆ (rocks·view·walk)
  • Tip: together with Windy Hill·park then walk

④ Maemuldo (매물도)

  • Signature: lighthouse island·trekking·archipelago scenery
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆ (island trekking·cruise·nature)
  • Tip: check cruise times·Somaemuldo lighthouse·prepare for trekking

⑤ Mongdol Beach (Hakdong 몽돌)

  • Signature: black pebble beach·wave sounds·swimming
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆ (pebbles·beach·summer)
  • Tip: Hakdong pebbles·famous for wave sounds·summer swimming

⑥ POW Camp Heritage Park (포로수용소)

  • Signature: Korean War POW-camp history·heritage park·museum
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆ (history·education·exhibits)
  • Tip: Gohyeon downtown·historical context·monorail

✈️ Access·Getting Around (foreigner practical)

  • Access: 1 hour from Busan via the Geoga Bridge (Busan Sasang/Seobu Terminal bus)·Seoul → Busan KTX then Geoje bus·close to Tongyeong
  • Getting around: rental car strongly recommended (large island·spread-out spots)·cruises from Jangseungpo/Gujora docks
  • Base: Gohyeon·Jangseungpo·Hakdong resorts/pensions·one night+
  • Payment: card·cash·book cruises in peak season
  • Emergency: ☎ 1330 (tourism)·112·119

⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "Gyeongnam islands = only Tongyeong": Geoje is separate on an Oedo·Windy Hill·cruise axis.

2. "Take the Oedo boat anytime": Check cruise times·peak-season booking·weather cancellations.

3. "Public transit covers it": Geoje is large so a rental car is essential·spots spread out.

4. "Day trip": 1h from Busan, but so many spots that a night+ is recommended.

5. "Nothing to eat": Rich in winter oyster·codfish soup·sea squirt·seafood.

⚙ FAQ (6)

1. How to get there?: 1h from Busan via Geoga Bridge·Seoul-Busan KTX then Geoje bus·close to Tongyeong.

2. How to reach Oedo?: Jangseungpo/Gujora cruise·pair with Haegeumgang·book in peak season·check weather.

3. Need a car?: Strongly recommended·large island·spread-out spots·cruises from docks.

4. What to see?: Oedo·Windy Hill·Sinseondae·Maemuldo·Mongdol Beach·POW Camp.

5. Food?: Winter oyster·codfish soup·sea squirt·seafood·fish market.

6. Recommended personas: Scenery = Windy Hill·Oedo / island = Maemuldo / history = POW Camp.

⚠ Medical·Safety·Dietary Disclaimer

Geoje means cruises·coastal cliffs·be cautious (cancellations·weather). Fitness·gear for Maemuldo trekking. Slippery Mongdol beach. Drive coastal roads safely. Allergens·freshness of winter oyster and shellfish. Tongyeong/Busan hospitals for emergencies. Emergency ☎ 1330·112·119.
