Geoje 2-Day Guide (Geoje): Oedo Cruise, Windy Hill and the Jungle Dome, With Fares and Hours Checked (Official Values, October 2026)

🎯 How to use this guide

When the cruise leaves Jangseungpo Pier (장승포 선착장), the salty wind hits your face first, and about 20 minutes later you see an island in the middle of the sea with terraced gardens stacked layer upon layer — that is Oedo. It lies about 4km from Geoje Island, and the official Geoje City introduction says its 740 or so subtropical plant species, tended by one couple since 1969, feel "like a Mediterranean beach moved here." The 2002 drama Winter Sonata was filmed here. Even if you come alone, one cruise ticket and one admission ticket get you there.

Day two is the southern coast. A silhouette against the sea in front of the Windy Hill windmill, white waves breaking on the strange rocks of Sinseondae across the way, and at Hakdong Mongdol Beach the sound of black stones knocking against each other and rolling with a rattle every time a wave pulls back — this sound is on the list of "100 Sounds of Korean Nature." When it rains, switch to Geoje POW Camp Historic Park in Gohyeon and the Jungle Dome in Geoje-myeon.

This guide plans a 2-day trip using only the fares and hours read on October 29, 2026 from the official pages of Geoje City Culture & Tourism, Oedo Botania, Geoje POW Camp Historic Park, Geoje Botanic Garden, Busan Gangseo-gu Office and the Geoje Bus Information System. It is tailored to people who come just to see Oedo, people adding one night in Geoje to a Busan or Tongyeong itinerary, and people who want one good photo.

⛴ Oedo & Haegeumgang — add the admission fee to the cruise fare

  • There are two prices: the cruise fare (adult 16,000~22,000 won) + Oedo admission 11,000 won. From Jangseungpo on a weekend, 22,000 + 11,000 = 33,000 won; from the Haegeumgang pier on a weekday, 16,000 + 11,000 = 27,000 won. Fares are per person, so traveling alone costs the same.
  • 6 boarding piers (Geoje City Oedo fare page, adult weekday/weekend): Jangseungpo 20,000/22,000 (138 Jangseung-ro, 055-681-6565) · Jiseipo 20,000/22,000 (72 Jiseipohaean-ro, Ilun-myeon, 055-682-5577) · Wahyeon 18,000/19,000 (46 Wahyeonhaebyeon-gil, Ilun-myeon, 055-681-2211) · Gujora 19,000/20,000 (81-2 Gujora-ri, Ilun-myeon, 055-681-1188) · Haegeumgang 16,000/17,000 (270 Haegeumgang-ro, Nambu-myeon, 055-633-1352) · Dojangpo 20,000 (53 Dojangpo 1-gil, Nambu-myeon, 055-632-8787). The Dadae pier is not operating at present. The Geoje City combined fare table lists some piers lower than this (Jangseungpo regular cruise 17,000/19,000) and peak-season fares are separate, so the operator's fare on the day is what counts.
  • Course: landing on Oedo + viewing Haegeumgang from the ship. Haegeumgang is an uninhabited island about 500m out at sea south of Haegeumgang Village, designated a scenic site in March 1971 (the second after Sogeumgang in Gangneung), and this 116m-high rock island has Lion Rock, Maitreya Rock, Candlestick Rock and the cross-shaped Cross Cave. Geoje City advises looking for the traces of the "Seobulgwacha (서불과차)" inscription on the Ujebong cliff as the ship passes.
  • On Oedo: allow about 2 hours. The whole island is no smoking and no alcohol, no outside food, and no photos taken by stepping into the flower beds (Oedo Botania notice). Summer 08:00~19:00 · winter 08:30~17:00.
  • Which pier? To see only Oedo, take Jangseungpo, where the Busan bus arrives; to combine with Windy Hill and Sinseondae, Dojangpo; for a cheaper ride close to Haegeumgang, Haegeumgang. If the wind is strong the ships are cancelled, so ask the pier in the morning whether they are running before you set out.

🌬 Southern coast — Windy Hill, Sinseondae, Hakdong, Maemulseong

  • Windy Hill (14-47 Galgot-ri, Nambu-myeon): the hill north of Dojangpo village. Its original name was "Ttibatneul (띠밭늘)"; it has been called its current name since 2002, and the windmill went up in November 2009. Several dramas and films were shot here. The coast across the way is Sinseondae (21-19 Galgot-ri) — white waves crashing on strange rocks, the horizon and the many islands all fit in one frame. It connects directly to the Dojangpo cruise pier.
  • Hakdong Black Pearl Mongdol Beach (276-5 Hakdong-ri, Dongbu-myeon): a black pebble beach about 1.2km long and 50m wide. The sound of waves rolling the pebbles was chosen for the "100 Sounds of Nature," and in the camellia grove you can hear the pitta's call. There is a photo zone at the entrance.
  • Maemulseong (29 Bokhang-gil, Jangmok-myeon): after Typhoon Maemi in 2003 took his farmland, a local citizen, Baek Sun-sam (백순삼), built up a stone wall alone to protect his crops, and it turned into something like a medieval European castle. Geoje City's introduction says the scale is hard to believe for something built without blueprints. It is on the Geoga Bridge side (Jangmok-myeon), so it is a good stop on the way in from Busan by car.
  • All three are open-air coastal sites with no fee or hours listed on the Geoje City page. You do not need to hunt for a ticket booth.

🏛 Gohyeon — POW Camp Historic Park and the monorail

  • Geoje POW Camp Historic Park (61 Gyeryong-ro, Gohyeon-dong): open 09:00~18:00, ticket sales and entry end 17:00, closed every Tuesday (next weekday if Tuesday is a public holiday) and on Seollal and Chuseok day. Adult (ages 19~64) 7,000 won · youth/soldier 5,000 won · child 3,000 won. Shooting experience 2,000 · mirror maze 3,000 · VR 4,000 are separate, and car parking is 2,000 won for 3 hours.
  • Geoje Tourist Monorail (same address): the daytime fare of 18,000 won includes park admission, so if you want both, one monorail ticket is enough. The night fare of 23,000 won does not include the park (the park is open only 09:00~18:00). Running 09:00~18:00 with last boarding at 16:00 are values checked on 2026-10-08.

🌴 Geoje-myeon — Jungle Dome

  • Geoje Botanic Garden Jungle Dome (3595 Geojenamseo-ro, Geoje-myeon): 09:30~18:00 (ticket sales and entry end 17:00), outdoor garden 09:00~18:00. Closed every Wednesday (next weekday if a public holiday), January 1, and Seollal and Chuseok day. General (ages 19~64) 5,000 won · youth (ages 13~18) 4,000 won · child (ages 7~12) 3,000 won, free for ages 6 and under and 65 and over. Only same-day on-site tickets are sold.
  • From Gohyeon Terminal take city bus 71 or 71-1 (about 1 hour) or 50-2 (about 30 minutes) to the "Geoje Botanic Garden (거제식물원)" stop. From Busan about 2 hours by car (Geoje Botanic Garden directions).

🚌 Getting there and around — Busan bus, Geoga Bridge, city buses

  • Busan→Geoje intercity bus (Intercity Bus Integrated Booking, checked 2026-10-26): Busan Seobu (Sasang) Terminal→Geoje (Gohyeon) 40 trips a day, 06:00~22:10, 1 hr 10 min to 1 hr 27 min. Busan Seobu→Jangseungpo (toward the Oedo pier) 18 trips a day, 06:30~22:20, 1 hr 28 min to 1 hr 44 min, adult 10,600 won (22:20 late-night bus 12,700 won · child 5,300 won · middle/high school 8,500 won). The adult fare to Gohyeon is not listed on the integrated booking site. For booking and same-day times, see the #1297 Express & Intercity Bus Manual.
  • Geoga Bridge (Busan Gangseo-gu Office): passenger car toll 10,000 won (disabled drivers 2,900 won), total length 8.2km, opened December 2010. If you cross from Busan in a rental car you pay each time you cross — #439 Rental Car & Driving Guide.
  • City buses on the island (Geoje Bus Information System): adult cash 1,700 won · transit card 1,650 won (youth 1,100/1,050 · child 850/800), village bus 1,200/1,150. Pay with a transit card and tap the reader when you get off to get one free transfer to a different route within 40 minutes (cash and the same route are excluded).
  • Route numbers (Geoje BIS route guide): Gohyeon→Jangseungpo→Neungpo 10 · 10-2 · 10-3 · 11 · Gohyeon→Gujora 22 · 22-1 · 23 · 23-1 (via Jiseipo) · 4000 (via Jiseipo) · Terminal→Haegeumgang 55 · 55-1 · Terminal→Hakdong, Jiseipo→Neungpo 67, 67-1 (via Hakdong, Gujora, Jiseipo and Jangseungpo) · Nambu 1 (남부1) Jeogu~Dojangpo (Windy Hill) · Gohyeon Terminal→Geoje Botanic Garden 71 · 71-1 · 50-2. The page has no trip counts or first/last bus times, so pick your route in the BIS app and check that day's timetable.
  • One more island (Geoje City combined fare table): Maemuldo ferry round trip, adult, Daemaemuldo weekday 21,900 · weekend 24,000, Somaemuldo 24,000 · 26,300 (peak season separate, Maemuldo Haeun 055-633-0051) · Jisimdo boat round trip 14,000 · Naedo boat round trip 12,000.

📸 Photo spots — scenes from the official introductions

  • Windy Hill windmill: the windmill and sea, built in November 2009. In the afternoon it is backlit, so you get a silhouette; in the morning the windmill blades come out white.
  • Sinseondae: strange rocks, white waves and the horizon — walk across to the coast opposite Windy Hill.
  • Oedo: terraced gardens, sculptures and the sea. You cannot step into the flower beds, so shoot from the path.
  • Haegeumgang sunrise and sunset: the reason Geoje City writes that it is "a place photographers often visit." View it from the Haegeumgang pier side.
  • Hakdong Mongdol entrance photo zone: black pebbles and a camellia forest. Use video to capture the sound too.
  • Maemulseong stone wall: a wall built of squared stones and cement, with the sea beyond.

🧰 Geoje practicalities — getting there, paying, asking

  • Sample itinerary (per person): Day 1 Busan Seobu→Jangseungpo bus 10,600 + cruise (Jangseungpo, weekday) 20,000 + Oedo admission 11,000 = 41,600 won. Day 2 city bus 1,650 × 2~3 rides + POW camp 7,000 (or monorail 18,000) + Jungle Dome 5,000. Meals and lodging not included.
  • Payment: for cards and mobile pay, see #527 Korean Payment Systems and #431 Payment & SIM Map. The Jungle Dome is same-day on-site purchase only, so you do not need to look for online booking.
  • Transit card: the Geoje city bus transfer discount applies only when you pay with a transit card. For card types, see #1298 Choosing from 3 Transit Cards — whether the card you used in Seoul or Busan works on Geoje buses is answered by the Geoje City Transportation Administration Division (거제시 교통행정과, 055-639-4534).
  • Dietary needs (less spicy, vegan, halal): for show-and-point cards, see the #1300 Dietary Request Manual.
  • Asking: Tourist Information Hotline 1330 (Korea Tourism Organization, multilingual). The 2026 Island Flower Festival schedule and peak-season cruise fares are not yet on the Geoje City page, so asking here is faster.

⚠ 4 common mistakes

1. Jungle Dome on Wednesday, POW camp on Tuesday — each is closed that day. Rearrange the days.

2. Counting only the cruise fare — Oedo admission of 11,000 won is separate.

3. Booking a cruise on a windy day — cancellations are frequent. Ask the pier in the morning whether ships are running before you set out.

4. Paying cash on the bus and expecting a transfer — the free transfer applies only if you pay with a transit card and tap the reader when you get off.

⚙ FAQ 5

1. Total cost for Oedo? — Cruise 16,000~22,000 + admission 11,000 (adult). Jangseungpo weekend 33,000 won, Haegeumgang weekday 27,000 won.

2. Which pier? — If you come by Busan bus, Jangseungpo; to combine with Windy Hill, Dojangpo; cheapest is Haegeumgang (weekday 16,000).

3. Jungle Dome reservation? — None. Same-day on-site purchase, closed Wednesdays.

4. POW camp + monorail? — The daytime monorail at 18,000 won includes park admission. Park only is 7,000 won.

5. How from Busan? — Busan Seobu (Sasang)→Gohyeon 40 trips a day (1 hr 10 min to 27 min), →Jangseungpo 18 trips · 10,600 won (checked 2026-10-26). By rental car, the Geoga Bridge toll is 10,000 won. On the island, city bus 1,650 won (transit card).
