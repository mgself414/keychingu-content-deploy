7 Places to Watch Korean Films in Seoul and Busan — English-Subtitle Screenings, Cinematheques, Film Museums

Subtitle: Cinematheque KOFA in Sangam, which shows Korean classics for free and tags English-subtitle screenings with an 'E', the free Korean Film Museum in the same building, Emu Cinema in Gwanghwamun, which marks '*Eng Subtitle' on its English schedule, Indiespace, the independent-film theater in Hongdae, Seoul Film Center in Chungmuro, and Busan Cinema Center and the Busan Museum of Movies in Busan. For each place we wrote how to get there, how to check for English subtitles, fees, and closing days.

ISSUE 03 · #530 · 2026-11-25

🎯 Who This Is For

This piece is for travelers who want to watch a Korean film in a Korean theater. The hard part is subtitles. We searched on 2026-11-25 but found no regular-screening notice for English subtitles on Korean films at CGV, Megabox, or Lotte Cinema (롯데시네마). So this piece puts places that mark English-subtitle screenings separately first, then adds film museums that are worth a visit even without subtitles. Theaters that show Korean independent films come after that. Their English subtitles differ from screening to screening, so we wrote how to check.

⚠ Notation rule: Next to every time and fee we attached the source and the check date. "Official" is used only for values taken from the original page of the operating organization, opened directly. Kakao Map (카카오맵) values are the ones registered on the place page (checked 2026-11-25). Walking times follow Kakao Map, so check directions again on the day. Screening schedules change every week.

① Venues That Mark English-Subtitle Screenings Separately — 2 Places

1. Cinematheque KOFA (시네마테크KOFA) (Sangam-dong (상암동), Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul)

A movie theater run by the Korean Film Archive (한국영상자료원). It is on the basement 1st floor of the Korean Film Archive building (Kakao Map, 2026-11-25). Every screening is free (official English guide, 2026-11-25).

  • How to check for English subtitles: The legend of the Korean-language schedule page has "E English Subtitles" (E 영어자막). A screening with an E next to the title is shown with English subtitles (official, 2026-11-25). The English schedule page has two Es in its legend. One means Event and one means English Subtitles, so look for the E shown in the 'English Subtitles' color (official English schedule). English-subtitle screenings are rare. In the schedule for November 25 to December 12 that we looked at on 2026-11-25, only two screenings carried an E: "There's Nowhere to Look" (눈 둘 데가 없네) on Wed, December 2 at 17:00 and Tue, December 8 at 19:00. Find the E screenings first before you go. All non-Korean films shown have Korean subtitles (wording from the official legend).
  • Booking: Online booking opens 5 days before the screening and refreshes every day at 11 a.m. The limit is 2 tickets per person. The online booking screen is currently in Korean only (official English guide). On-site tickets are issued only on the day of the screening, from seats left after online booking. The box office opens 1 hour before the first screening (same guide). Screenings start on time, and latecomers may not be let in.
  • Closed: Screenings run Tue~Sat. Closed on Sun·Mon, January 1, the Korean Film Archive founding anniversary (January 18), May 1, and the Seollal (설날) and Chuseok (추석) holidays (official English guide).
  • Getting there: The address is 400 Worldcupbuk-ro, Mapo-gu (마포구 월드컵북로 400). The official guide gives two ways. In front of Exit 2 of Digital Media City Station (디지털미디어시티역) (Line 6, Airport Railroad (공항철도), Gyeongui-Jungang Line (경의중앙선)), take bus 771, 6715, 7711, or 7730 and get off at Nurimkum Square (누리꿈스퀘어) (about 5 minutes). Or from Susaek Station (수색역) on the Gyeongui-Jungang Line, enter the DMC complex through the underground passage and walk about 10 minutes (official English guide). Kakao Map shows a 13-minute walk from Exit 1 of Susaek Station.

2. Emu Cinema (에무시네마) (Sinmunno (신문로), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul)

A movie theater on the 2nd floor of the complex cultural space "Emu" (에무) in an alley behind Gyeonghui Palace (경희궁) (Seoul Media Hub (서울시 미디어허브) 2026-06-12). It mainly shows independent films by emerging directors and diversity films (same article).

  • How to check for English subtitles: On the Emu website menu, go to 'English' → 'emu Cinema' to find the English schedule. When we opened it on 2026-11-25, the posted schedule was for October 8~11, and some films carried a '*Eng Subtitle' mark (official English page). A screening with this mark is a candidate for English subtitles. The latest schedule may reach the English page late, so check with the theater (02-730-5604). GQ Korea (GQ코리아) introduced the theater as regularly showing "independent and art films with English subtitles" (2025-08-02). 10 Magazine also wrote that some screenings show Korean films with English subtitles.
  • Fee: We could not find a fee table on the first page of the Emu website. Check on the booking screen (Dtrix (디트릭스)). Seoul Media Hub put the adult standard fee at three art-film theaters around Gwanghwamun (광화문) at roughly 9,000~12,000 won (2026-06-12).
  • Getting there: The address is 7 Gyeonghuigung 1-ga-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 경희궁1가길 7). Kakao Map shows a 13-minute walk from Exit 7 of Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역) on Line 3 (2026-11-25). Parking has only 2~3 spaces, so we recommend public transport (Seoul Media Hub 2026-06-12). Theater phone 02-730-5604 (official).

② Film Museums — 2 Places

3. Korean Film Museum (한국영화박물관) (Sangam-dong (상암동), Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul)

It is on the 1st floor of the same Korean Film Archive (한국영상자료원) building as Cinematheque KOFA (시네마테크KOFA) (Kakao Map, 2026-11-25). The permanent exhibition "Seeing Korean Cinema" (한국 영화를 보다) shows about 100 years of Korean film era by era. Real objects appear, such as posters, filming equipment, film reels, and projectors (Seoul Media Hub (서울시 미디어허브) 2026-07-23). Special exhibitions change two or three times a year (official English guide).

  • English: The audio docent app you get at the information desk has English commentary (official English guide, 2026-11-25).
  • Fee: Free (official English guide, 2026-11-25).
  • Reservation: The official guide recommends booking ahead. Online reservations can be made from the day itself up to 2 days before, and open every day at noon. On-site tickets are for the same day only, issued from when the session starts until 30 minutes before it ends. On-site quantities may be small (official English guide, 2026-11-25).
  • Hours: They differ by source. The official English text says Tue~Sat 10:30~19:00, with entry until 30 minutes before the session ends. The TODAY display on the first page of the same website says 10:30~18:30, and Kakao Map says 10:00~19:00 (all 2026-11-25). Entering before 18:00 is safer.
  • Closed: Sun·Mon, January 1, January 18, May 1, and the Seollal (설날) and Chuseok (추석) holidays (official English guide).
  • Getting there: Same as Cinematheque KOFA. It pairs well as the museum in the morning and a Cinematheque screening in the afternoon.

4. Busan Museum of Movies (부산영화체험박물관) (Donggwang-dong (동광동), Jung-gu (중구), Busan)

A hands-on film museum that its website describes as a place to "see and feel everything about film". It has a permanent exhibition, experience zones on the 3rd and 4th floors, and a separate exhibition hall called "Cine Museum" (씨네뮤지엄) (official website, 2026-11-25).

  • English: The website has an EN menu and introduces a mobile exhibition-guide app (official). We could not confirm whether the app supports English.
  • Fee: Museum adult 10,000 won, children and youth (36 months or older through high school) 7,000 won. The combined ticket that includes Cine Museum is 14,000 won for adults and 11,000 won for children and youth (official, 2026-11-25).
  • Hours: 10:00~18:00, with entry and ticketing closing at 17:00 (official).
  • Closed: Every Monday (the next day if it is a public holiday), January 1, and the day of Seollal (설날) and Chuseok (추석) (official).
  • Getting there: The address is 12 Daecheong-ro 126beon-gil, Jung-gu (중구 대청로126번길 12). Kakao Map shows a 5-minute walk from Exit 1 of Jungang Station (중앙역) on Line 1 (2026-11-25). It is in the old downtown (원도심), away from Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당) in Haeundae (해운대) Centum City (센텀시티), so allow plenty of travel time if you pair the two in one day.

③ Theaters That Show Korean Independent and Art Films — 3 Places (Check English Subtitles per Screening)

For the three places below we could not find a notice that marks English-subtitle screenings separately (2026-11-25). Before you go, look for an 'English subtitles' mark in the screening information, and if there is none, ask by phone.

5. Indiespace (인디스페이스) (Donggyo-dong (동교동), Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul)

A theater dedicated to independent film (Kakao Map tag). It is on the 8th floor of the Lotte Cinema Hongdae Ipgu (롯데시네마 홍대입구) building in front of Hongik University Station (홍대입구역) (official notice).

  • Fee: Adult 10,000 won, early bird (before 11 a.m.) 6,000 won, youth·senior 6,000 won. The theater notice was posted 2022-02-08 and matches the value registered on the Kakao Map menu on 2025-11-03 (checked 2026-11-25). For discounts you must show proof when buying at the counter (official notice).
  • Hours: Kakao Map lists daily 10:00~20:00 (2026-11-25). The box office opens 30 minutes before the screening, and entry is allowed only until 10 minutes after the screening starts (official notice).
  • Getting there: Leave Hongik University Station (홍대입구역) (Line 2, Airport Railroad (공항철도), Gyeongui-Jungang Line (경의중앙선)) by Exit 8, go straight, enter through the WISEPARK (와이즈파크) entrance on the right, and take the elevator to the 8th floor (official notice). It is a 2-minute walk by Kakao Map. Inquiries 02-738-0366 (official).

6. Seoul Film Center (서울영화센터) (Chodong (초동), Jung-gu (중구), Chungmuro (충무로), Seoul)

A public movie theater that the Seoul city government (서울시) opened on 2025-11-28. It has 3 screens and 312 seats (Seoul Media Hub (서울시 미디어허브) 2025-12-01). It shows independent films, art films, special programs, and film-festival titles. On Friday nights from May to October there is a rooftop screening called "Cinema Sky" (시네마스카이) (Seoul Media Hub 2026-07-15).

  • Fee: Adult 7,000 won, youth 6,000 won. Screenings after 5 p.m. on Wednesday "Culture Day" (문화가 있는 날) and the first screening on weekdays are 5,000 won (Seoul Media Hub 2026-07-15).
  • Hours: Tue~Sun 10:00~20:00, closed Monday (Seoul Media Hub 2026-07-15). Kakao Map also shows closed every Monday (2026-11-25).
  • Getting there: The address is 38 Mareunnae-ro, Jung-gu (중구 마른내로 38). A 4-minute walk from Exit 9 of Euljiro 3-ga Station (을지로3가역) on Lines 2 and 3 (Kakao Map, 2026-11-25). Check the screening schedule on the Seoul Film Center website or Dtrix (디트릭스) (Seoul Media Hub).

7. Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당) (Centum City (센텀시티), Haeundae-gu (해운대구), Busan)

A film-only complex in Haeundae (해운대) Centum City (센텀시티). Even outside the film-festival period it rotates through several screening programs (official current screening programs, 13 listed when opened 2026-11-25). The current Cinematheque special program is a Theo Angelopoulos retrospective (테오 앙겔로풀로스 회고전), so it is not Korean film (as of 2026-11-25).

  • Korean film programs (as of 2026-11-25, official current screening programs): "Cinema Sarangbang Korean Classic Film Regular Screenings" (영화사랑방 한국고전영화 정기상영회) is free and runs until December 14. The "November Korean Independent Film Program" (11월 한국독립영화 프로그램) is adult 8,000 won, youth 7,000 won, and runs until November 30. Neither program notice carries an English-subtitle mark.
  • Fee: The 2026 popular-film program is adult 8,000 won, youth 7,000 won. The Cinematheque special program (11-24~12-12) is adult 7,000 won (official, as of 2026-11-25). The fee differs by program.
  • Closed: The facility is open with no closing day. Kakao Map shows daily 09:00~21:00 (2026-11-25). Only the Cinematheque special program has no screenings on Monday (official screening guide).
  • Getting there: The address is 120 Suyeonggangbyeon-daero, Haeundae-gu (해운대구 수영강변대로 120). A 5-minute walk from Exit 12 or Exit 6 of Centum City Station (센텀시티역) on Line 2 (official directions page). Kakao Map shows 14 minutes from Exit 6, so allow extra time. The on-site box office is the movie-theater box office on the 6th floor (ticketing location in the official program guide).

📌 Bonus — A Cinematheque Where We Could Not Find Evidence of English Subtitles

Seoul Art Cinema (서울아트시네마) (Jeongdong (정동), Jung-gu (중구), Seoul) — A cinematheque that shows world classics, auteur films, and retrospectives (Seoul Media Hub (서울시 미디어허브) 2026-06-12). It is run by the Korea Cinematheque Council (한국시네마테크협의회) (official website). The address is 3 Jeongdong-gil, Kyunghyang Arthill 2nd floor (정동길 3 경향아트힐 2층) (official website). Special program schedules are posted through December 2026 (official website, opened 2026-11-25). However, we could not confirm the share of Korean films or English-subtitle screenings, so we did not count it in the 7. Kakao Map shows an 8-minute walk from Exit 5 of Seodaemun Station (서대문역) on Line 5.

🗺 Routes — 2 Courses

A. A Day in Seoul Sangam (Tue~Sat)

  1. Morning Korean Film Museum (한국영화박물관) (10:30~, online reservation recommended). Get the English audio-guide app at the information desk
  2. Afternoon Cinematheque KOFA (시네마테크KOFA) in the basement of the same building. On-site tickets start 1 hour before the first screening, from remaining seats, up to 2 tickets per person

Pick a day that has an E-marked screening. Both are closed on Sun·Mon.

B. A Day in Busan

  1. Morning Busan Museum of Movies (부산영화체험박물관) in the old downtown (10:00~, closed Monday)
  2. Afternoon One screening at Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당) in Centum City (센텀시티)

The two places are apart, one in the old downtown and one in Haeundae (해운대). Check travel time on the day with Kakao Map directions.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going on a Monday — Cinematheque KOFA and the Korean Film Museum (Sun·Mon), Seoul Film Center, and the Busan Museum of Movies are closed on Monday. At Busan Cinema Center the facility is open, but the Cinematheque special program has no screenings on Monday (each official guide · Seoul Media Hub).
  2. Booking any screening and expecting English subtitles — At Cinematheque KOFA, an E-marked screening has English subtitles. On the English schedule, look for the E shown in the 'English Subtitles' color so you do not confuse it with the E that means Event. At Emu Cinema, a screening marked '*Eng Subtitle' is a candidate for English subtitles, so check with the theater. Without a mark, Korean dialogue may have no English subtitles.
  3. Giving up because Cinematheque KOFA online booking does not work for you — The online booking screen is in Korean only, but if seats remain, you can get a free ticket on site from 1 hour before the first screening on the day (official English guide). The 2-ticket-per-person limit applies on site too.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. Can I watch a new Korean release with English subtitles at a big chain theater? — In our 2026-11-25 search we found no regular English-subtitle screening notice for CGV, Megabox, or Lotte Cinema (롯데시네마). Look for an 'English subtitles' mark yourself in the screening information in the chain's app.
  2. How do I pick English subtitles during a film festival? — The English screening schedule of the Busan International Film Festival (부산국제영화제) gives each screening a subtitle symbol. With no mark it is 'English subtitles + Korean dialogue', and KK means 'Korean subtitles + Korean dialogue', so there are no English subtitles (legend of the biff.kr English schedule, opened 2026-11-25). Festival schedules and how to get there are in #611 Korean Film Festival Calendar and #482 Busan International Film Festival Viewing Guide.
  3. What if I want to visit places from the films? — Film and drama filming locations are collected in #452, only the ones whose addresses are confirmed.
