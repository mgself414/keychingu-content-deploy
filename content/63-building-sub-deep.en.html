📍 Parks — Yeouido Park, Yeouido Hangang Park, Yeouido Saetgang Ecological Park

Yeouido Park (여의도공원) (68 Yeoui-gongwon-ro (여의공원로), Yeongdeungpo-gu, ☎ 02-761-4078~9) opened in 1971 as Yeouido Plaza on the site of a former airstrip, and in January 1999 the asphalt was removed and it became a green park. It has the Forest of Natural Ecology (자연생태의숲), Culture Square (문화의 마당), a lawn square and the Forest of Korean Tradition (한국 전통의 숲). The Forest of Korean Tradition holds a pond (지당) with three small artificial islands symbolizing Seoul's past, present and future, along with the Samojeong (사모정) pavilion and the Yeouijeong (여의정) pavilion. The spot where the C-47 transport plane is displayed in the park is where the Korean Liberation Army (한국광복군) advance unit (Jeongjindae / 정진대) landed with a US OSS unit on August 18, 1945. Walking paths and a bike path circle the edge of the park, and an underpass at Exit 9 connects it to Hangang Citizens' Park (한강시민공원).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Facilities: Kiosk, restrooms, in-line skate rental. Parking: Available.

Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도한강공원) (330 Yeoui-dong-ro (여의동로), Yeongdeungpo-gu, ☎ 02-3780-0561~6) is Yeouido's park on the Han River. Spring brings a cherry blossom festival, and autumn brings events such as the Seoul International Fireworks Festival (세계불꽃축제), performances and marathons. Bamseom Island (밤섬) and the Yeouido Saetgang (샛강) channel remain relatively natural. Facilities include Mulbit Square (물빛광장), a floating stage and fountain (수상무대와 수상분수), the Waterfall of Light (빛의 폭포), the Piano Waterway (피아노물길), Festival Land (페스티벌랜드) and a yacht marina (요트마리나).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available (1,782 spaces).

Yeouido Saetgang Ecological Park (여의도샛강생태공원) (48 Yeoui-dong-ro, Yeongdeungpo-gu, ☎ 02-3780-0561) is an ecological park running from the Saetgang channel behind the National Assembly to the middle of Gayang Bridge (가양대교). It has stands of willow, reed and silver grass, and groundwater from nearby subway stations feeds a stream waterfall and ponds. The 6km walking path has native-plant signs every 20~30m, and the park is home to the natural monument Eurasian kestrel (황조롱이), spot-billed ducks, grey herons and beodeulchi (버들치). No benches or kiosks were installed to avoid harming nature, and no streetlights were installed so the plants and animals can rest. It runs ecology programs such as ecology talks, nature observation and nature play, and the information center has a firefly breeding room.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Access to some sections may be restricted during animal breeding season. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Location: 700m from Exit 1 of Yeouido Station (여의도역) on Subway Line 5. Parking: Available.

📍 National Assembly — National Assembly Building and National Assembly Museum

The National Assembly Building (국회의사당) (1 Uisadang-daero (의사당대로), Yeongdeungpo-gu, ☎ 02-6788-2114) was completed in August 1975 and adds Korean traditional beauty to a modern style. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is called the largest single legislative building in the East. The gray-green dome was made of copper plate, was red at first, and took on its present color as it rusted. The dome is 64m across at the base and weighs 1,000 tons. The 24 columns, each 32.5m high, symbolize the 24 solar terms, and the 8 columns on the front symbolize the 8 provinces of the country.

  • Opening hours and closed days: No registered value.

The National Assembly Museum (국회박물관) (same address, ☎ 02-6788-3664) is a national museum that displays materials on the National Assembly's activities and parliamentary democracy from the Provisional Legislative Assembly (임시의정원) to the present. The permanent exhibition has 4 rooms on 2 floors: you view Exhibition Rooms 1 and 2 on the 2nd floor, then go down to Exhibition Rooms 3 and 4 on the 1st floor. At the National Assembly Experience Hall (국회체험관), built to resemble the plenary chamber, you can watch videos and try plenary voting, and there is also a children's museum where kids learn about the National Assembly and democracy through play.

  • Opening hours: Weekdays 10:00~18:00, Saturday 10:00~13:00. Closed days: Legal holidays, Labor Day (May 1), National Assembly Foundation Day (May 31).
  • Fee: Free. Parking: 2,000 won once past the basic 30 minutes, then 300 won per 10 minutes.

📍 Shopping — The Hyundai Seoul

The Hyundai Seoul (더현대 서울) (108 Yeoui-daero (여의대로), Yeongdeungpo-gu, ☎ 1588-3650) is the flagship store of Hyundai Department Store (현대백화점), which opened on February 26, 2021. Based on the architectural philosophy of the British architect Richard Rogers, it reinterprets the vermilion columns and dancheong (단청) painting of traditional Korean architecture in a modern way. On the 5th floor is Sounds Forest (사운즈 포레스트), an indoor garden of about 1,000 pyeong, and natural light comes in through a glass ceiling about 20m high. On the 1st floor is Waterfall Garden (워터폴 가든), a 12m-high artificial waterfall that runs down from the 3rd floor. There is also a culture salon, CH1985, and an exhibition space, ALT.1.

  • Opening hours: Mon~Thu 10:30~20:00, Fri~Sun 10:30~20:30. Restaurant floor 10:30~22:00. Closed days: Varies by month.
  • Parking: Free for the first 30 minutes, then 2,000 won every 10 minutes. Free parking conditions vary depending on the purchase amount. Restrooms: Available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The National Assembly Museum is open only 10:00~13:00 on Saturday — it is closed on legal holidays, Labor Day (May 1) and National Assembly Foundation Day (May 31).
  2. Saetgang Ecological Park has no benches, kiosks or streetlights — benches and kiosks were left out to avoid harming nature, and streetlights so the plants and animals can rest. Access to some sections may be restricted during breeding season.
  3. The Hyundai Seoul's closed days vary by month — no fixed closing day is registered.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which places can I enter for free?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, the National Assembly Museum is free. Yeouido Park, Yeouido Hangang Park and Yeouido Saetgang Ecological Park are always open.

Q. What events are held in Yeouido in spring and autumn?

A. At Yeouido Hangang Park, a cherry blossom festival is held in spring, and in autumn the Seoul International Fireworks Festival, performances and marathon events are held.
