63 Building · Yeouido — 12 Picks: 63 SkyPicnic, Centre Pompidou Hanwha and the Han River (2026 Renewal)

Category: A Outskirts/Nature

Parent deep: #700 Han River·#703 Night view·#704 Cruise ship

🎯 In June 2026, the 63 Building Was Remade from Top to Bottom

The 63 Building (63빌딩) finished a full renovation on June 4, 2026. Centre Pompidou Hanwha opened in the annex, the 60th-floor observatory reopened as 63 SkyPicnic (including a rooftop on the 63rd floor), and 25 new restaurants, cafes and shops moved into the lower commercial floors. On the other hand, Aqua Planet 63 in the basement closed on June 30, 2024, so old blogs and old discount-ticket listings will send you on a wasted trip. The operating details below are based on the official sites (63skypicnic.com · centrepompidou-hanwha.kr · aquaplanet.co.kr) and the Hanwha press release (2026-06-01), checked on 2026-10-07.

63 Building · Yeouido: 12 Picks

1. 63 Building — Opened 1985, 63 Floors, About 250m

  • A golden glass tower at the eastern tip of Yeouido (여의도), on the south side of the Han River. It opened in 1985 and has long been a symbol of the Seoul skyline, so it stands out from every Han River bridge.
  • Today it is a complex of museum, observatory and restaurants. Address: 50 63-ro, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul.

2. 63 SkyPicnic — 60th-Floor Observatory + 63rd-Floor Rooftop

  • See the Han River and downtown in 360 degrees from 250m up. The 60th floor is divided into Sky Infinity, the main mirror-lined observation deck; Sky Voyage Theater, a five-screen immersive theater; and Sky Stage, a hologram exhibit. The 63rd-floor rooftop helipad is open as the Rooftop (the first rooftop opening in about 16 years). A Starbucks (63 SkyPicnic branch) is inside.
  • Hours: daily 10:00–22:00, ticketing 09:50–21:30, no regular closing days (except private-event days).
  • Admission: adults (ages 13–64) ₩27,000 · children (36 months–12 years) ₩23,000 · age 65+ ₩23,000 · under 36 months free (with a guardian).
  • Notes: you must wear the shoe covers provided at entry, and stiletto heels, work boots and studded shoes are not allowed. Large bags and outside food are not allowed. Wheelchairs cannot enter the observation area and strollers are not allowed indoors (official notice).

3. Centre Pompidou Hanwha — Annex Art Museum

  • Opened on June 4, 2026 through a partnership between the Centre Pompidou in Paris and the Hanwha Foundation of Culture. The annex, designed by architect Jean-Michel Wilmotte around a "box of light" concept, has two large exhibition halls.
  • Visiting: Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun 10:00–18:00 (last entry 17:30) · Wed, Sat 10:00–21:00 (last entry 20:30) · closed Mondays, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Admission: adults from ₩28,000 (reduced rates for children, teens and seniors). With a same-day exhibition ticket, parking in the 63 Building basement is free for 1 hour on weekdays and 2 hours on weekends.
  • Current exhibition: 〈Garden Project: What Is Etched〉, 2026-09-06 to 2027-03-07. The opening exhibition 〈Cubists: Innovators of Vision〉 ended on October 4. A free lecture runs at 14:00 (180 seats); paid docent tours at 11:30 and 16:30 require advance booking.

4. 63 Oudolf Garden — The Garden Below the Building

  • A garden by landscape designer Piet Oudolf, who also designed New York's High Line and Chicago's Lurie Garden. The design leaves the plants' whole year in place, from seeds and new shoots to flowers and dried stalks, so there is something to see in autumn and winter too. You pass it on the way from the observatory down to the Han River park.

5. Where to Eat — New Restaurants and Cafes from the Renewal

  • New arrivals per the Hanwha press release: Michelin Guide-listed pyeongyang naengmyeon Seoryeong (서령), the first Korean branch of Tokyo's Tabelog No. 1 ramen shop Ramenya Shima (라멘야 시마), Garosu-gil Korean dining Bistro Sanho (비스트로 산호), Thai food Kkapom (까폼), Japanese food Gohyeon (고현), Hawaiian coffee and acai bowls Island Vintage Coffee, Neapolitan-style wood-fired sandwiches Pajak (파작), and ice cream Benson (벤슨).
  • Hours and prices differ by shop, so check on site. The 63 Buffet is being reborn as "63 Buffet Pavilion The Premium", and the wine dining spot "CESTONE" has been announced for a year-end opening (as of the June press release — confirm before you visit).

6. 63 Building Night LED — A Free Night View from Outside

  • After sunset the golden facade lights up. From Yeouido Hangang Park or the Wonhyo Bridge (원효대교) side, you can get its reflection on the river in the same shot. It costs nothing.

7. Yeouido Hangang Park

  • Head down toward the river from the 63 Building and you are in the park. It is the stage for spring cherry blossoms and the autumn fireworks festival, and on ordinary days it is a park of picnic mats, bikes and chicken and beer (#700).

8. Han River Cruise (#704)

  • E.Land cruises depart from the Yeouido pier. It is the easiest way to see the 63 Building's night lights from the river. For schedules and fares, see #704.

9. IFC Mall + The Hyundai Seoul

  • Two shopping malls near Yeouido Station (여의도역, Line 5). They connect to the 63 Building by bus or on foot. A good rainy-day alternative.

10. Shops — Curated Stores from the Renewal

  • Nordic lifestyle store ROOMING&HAY, subculture select shop Ropa Seoul (로파 서울), gift curation Present Moment Hoho (프레젠트모멘트 호호), and artist postcards Posset (포셋) (per the Hanwha press release). Worth a stop in place of observatory souvenirs.

11. April — Yeouido Cherry Blossoms

  • Early April brings cherry blossoms along Yunjung-ro (윤중로) in Yeouido and in the Han River park. Many spots give you the 63 Building as a backdrop for blossom photos (#753).

12. October — Seoul International Fireworks Festival

  • A fireworks festival hosted by Hanwha. It takes place every year on a Saturday evening in early October around Yeouido Hangang Park, and viewing is free (check the announcement each year for the date). The riverbank on the 63 Building side is the best spot, so people claim their places from midday.

FAQ 6

Q1. What about the old combined ticket (Aqua + observatory + art, ₩13,500)? A. It no longer exists. Aqua Planet 63 closed on 2024-06-30 and 63 Art became Centre Pompidou Hanwha. Now you book 63 SkyPicnic (₩27,000) and Centre Pompidou Hanwha (from ₩28,000) separately.

Q2. What are 63 SkyPicnic's hours? A. Daily 10:00–22:00, ticketing 09:50–21:30. No regular closing days (except private-event days). You can see the night view after sunset until 22:00.

Q3. When is Centre Pompidou Hanwha open? A. Closed Mondays. Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun 10:00–18:00, and Wed and Sat until 21:00. Also closed on January 1, Seollal and Chuseok. 〈Garden Project: What Is Etched〉 runs until March 7, 2027.

Q4. How do I get to the 63 Building? A. From Yeouinaru Station (여의나루역), Line 5, Exit 1, take bus 5634, 5633 or 261 to the "63 Building" stop / from Yeouido Station, Line 5, Exit 5, take bus 11 / from Saetgang Station (샛강역), Line 9, Exit 3, take 5633 or 5634. On weekdays a free shuttle runs from Daebang Station (대방역, Line 1), Yeouinaru Station, Yeouido Station and Saetgang Station (no weekend service). You can also walk from Yeouinaru Station along the Han River park.

Q5. When is the fireworks festival? A. Every year on a Saturday evening in early October at Yeouido Hangang Park. Viewing is free. Check that year's announcement for the date and traffic controls.

Q6. What about Aqua Planet 63? A. Closed on June 30, 2024 (Aqua Planet official notice). For an aquarium in Seoul, try Lotte World Aquarium (#198) or COEX Aquarium (#440).

Full-Day Courses 3

A. Museum + Observatory (Daytime)

  • 10:00 Centre Pompidou Hanwha (1–1.5 hours)
  • 12:00 Lunch — pick one restaurant inside the 63 Building (Seoryeong, Ramenya Shima or Bistro Sanho)
  • 14:00 63 SkyPicnic (60th floor → 63rd-floor Rooftop)
  • 16:00 63 Oudolf Garden → stroll in Yeouido Hangang Park
  • Note: on Wed or Sat, swap the order — observatory first, and Pompidou in the evening (open until 21:00).

B. Night View + Cruise

  • 17:00 63 SkyPicnic — sunset through night view from one spot
  • 19:00 E.Land Cruise (#704) — the 63 Building LED from the river
  • 20:30 Chicken and beer in Yeouido Hangang Park → or continue on to the Banpo Rainbow Fountain (반포 무지개분수, #702)

C. October Fireworks Festival (Festival Day)

  • 12:00 Arrive at Yeouido Hangang Park and claim a spot on the riverbank on the 63 Building side
  • 15:00 Eat and use the restrooms inside the 63 Building (crowded on festival day)
  • Evening: Seoul International Fireworks Festival
  • Note: after the show, subway stations are controlled and crowded — walking out to the next station is faster.

Season 5

  • Spring: early-April Yeouido cherry blossoms + new shoots in the Oudolf Garden
  • Summer: Rooftop, cruise and Han River park after dark
  • Fall: early-October fireworks + the observatory on clear days
  • Winter: indoor course — Centre Pompidou Hanwha + SkyPicnic + 63 Building restaurants
  • Year-round: SkyPicnic has no regular closing days; Pompidou is closed Mondays

Tip 5

1. There is no combined ticket — book SkyPicnic and Pompidou separately. If you want both, clear a whole day.

2. Pompidou is open until 21:00 on Wed and Sat — observatory by day, museum in the evening is the easy order.

3. SkyPicnic dress code — shoe covers required; heels and work boots not allowed. Strollers are not allowed indoors and wheelchairs cannot enter the observation area. Plan ahead if you have children or a companion who needs them.

4. Parking — for both SkyPicnic and Pompidou, 1 hour free on weekdays and 2 hours on weekends (with a ticket), paid after that.

5. On fireworks day the 63 Building's indoor facilities are crowded too — book and eat early.
