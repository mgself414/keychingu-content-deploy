🏛 5-axis deep dive

① Incheon Bridge (Korea #1 Bridge·2009~)

  • Signature: 18.4km·Korea #1 Bridge·Asia #4 Cable Stayed Bridge·Opened in 2009
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (English Signer/View Photo Matrix)
  • Recommended: Yeongjongdo → Songdo cross by car/airport bus to Mania/Observatory + Instagram

② G Tower (33rd floor, observatory)

  • Signature: Songdo IBD Mania Matrix·33rd floor observatory·Free
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ (English, payment card, MZ + Instagram fit)
  • Time: 09:00~18:00·Free·View = Incheon Bridge, Central Park, Seoul night view
  • Recommended: Cafe + View = 1 hour course

③ Songdo Central Park (1.2km²·deck boat)

  • Signature: 1.2km² artificial lake + deck boat·future city + natural matrix
