5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Korean bread is nothing special": Korean bakeries include MAILLET (Seorae Village, French baking since 1995), Napoleon Bakery (since 1968), Taegeukdang (since 1946), Sungsimdang (since 1956), and Iseongdang (since 1945) — 80- to 120-year-old institutions and a top draw for foreign enthusiasts.

2. "Sungsimdang is only in Daejeon": Sungsimdang is Korea's top bakery (in Daejeon, see #320, famous for tuigim-soboro and a top spot for foreign vlogs), with occasional pop-ups in Seoul as well.

3. "London Bagel Museum is everywhere": London Bagel Museum is Korea's No. 1 bagel spot (Anguk, Dosan, Jamsil) and the top Instagram pick for foreign visitors — expect a one- to two-hour wait.

4. "Knotted is just donuts": Knotted is Korea's top donut, cake, and signature-dessert brand, with multiple locations in Gangnam, Itaewon, Dosan, and Jamsil, and a favorite for MZ-generation Instagram.

5. "Sulbing is everywhere": Sulbing is the birthplace of Korea's No. 1 patbingsu, opened in 2013 at the Ewha flagship location, a favorite of foreign enthusiasts, and has expanded globally to Japan and the United States.
