⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Hongdae = only indie/clubs at night" — Skipping brunch

This is the most common mistake for foreign tourists. They often perceive Hongdae (홍대) as solely an area for indie music, clubs, and street food at night, missing out on 12+ brunch spots in the Hongdae (홍대), Yeonnam (연남), Hapjeong (합정), and Mangwon (망원) areas. In reality, the Hongdae area is Korea's top region for brunch cafes — signature brunch, modern cafes, pancakes & bagels, and Mangwon (망원) neighborhood brunch are all concentrated within one itinerary. A morning brunch + Hongdae (홍대) culture makes for the most foreigner-friendly Hongdae (홍대) one-day itinerary.

2. Viewing Yeonnam, Hapjeong, and Mangwon as separate neighborhoods

Foreigners often only look at Hongdae (홍대) and search for brunch in Gangnam (강남) or Itaewon (이태원), but Hongdae Station (홍대입구역) + Yeonnam (연남) + Hapjeong (합정) + Mangwon (망원) = the top brunch itinerary. Over 8 brunch spots are within a 15-minute walk from Hongdae Entrance Station (홍대입구역). A stroll through Yeonnam (연남) gollmok (골목, alley) combined with one brunch spot is the best morning course. Instead of going to Gangnam (강남) or Itaewon (이태원) for brunch, solve it within the Hongdae (홍대) area.

3. Not considering brunch times or reservations

Popular brunch spots (Urban Plant (어반플랜트), Dot Blanket (도트블랭킷), Beekend (비캔드)) typically have 1-2 hour waits on weekends. Weekdays from 10:00 to 12:00 are best for a less crowded experience — on weekends, right after opening at 10:00 or after 14:00 is also okay. Some places are closed on Mondays and Tuesdays — it's recommended to check their Instagram or Catchtable (캐치테이블) / Naver (네이버) in advance. Brunch spots often have limited seating, so weekdays are preferable.

Hongdae, Yeonnam, Hapjeong, Mangwon Brunch Scene

Since the early 2020s, the Hongdae (홍대) area has established itself as Korea's top region for brunch cafes. It differentiates itself from Gangnam (강남) (upscale, modern), Itaewon (이태원) (trendy, international), and Hannam (한남) (Michelin, course meals) with its signature, modern, and neighborhood vibes.

Yeonnam (연남) is the top neighborhood for signature brunch — with many signature brunch spots around Seongmisan gollmok (성미산 골목, Seongmisan alley) and the Donggyoro (동교로) area. Dot Blanket Yeonnam (도트블랭킷 연남), Hippo Brunchhaus (히포 브런치하우스), Raw and Cooked The Brunch (로앤쿡드 더브런치), Jamjam (잼잼), etc., are top spots for foreigners. They are also Instagram hotspots.

Hapjeong (합정) offers modern cafes + pancakes & bagels — modern signature cafes + pancake & bagel spots are located in the gollmok (골목, alleys) between Hapjeong Station (합정역) and Mangwon Station (망원역). Urban Plant Hapjeong (어반플랜트 합정), Justina (저스티나), Burnt Orange (번트 오렌지), O Bagel House (오베이글하우스), etc., offer great ambiance. Cafes combining green and plant themes are highly recommended.

Mangwon (망원) features signature neighborhood brunch — local brunch spots near Mangwon Market (망원시장) and Mangwon Hangang Park (망원 한강). Beekend (비캔드), Alive214, Clovelly (클로벨리), Photosynthesis Cafe (광합성카페), etc., are neighborhood spots with reasonable prices. They offer a local atmosphere and are foreigner-friendly.
