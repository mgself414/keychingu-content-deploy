⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. See “Hongdae = Night Indie Club Only” — Brunch Pass

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. They only recognized Hongdae as an area for indie music, clubs, and street food at night, and missed out on more than 12 brunch places in Hongdae, Yeonnam, Hapjeong, and Mangwon. In fact, Hongdae area is the No. 1 area for brunch cafes in Korea — Signature Brunch, Modern Cafe, Pancake/Bagel, and Mangwon neighborhood brunch are concentrated in one direction. Morning brunch + Hongdae culture = The most foreigner-like Hongdae one-day route.

2. Yeonnam, Hapjeong, and Mangwon are considered separate neighborhoods

Foreigners only look at Hongdae and look for brunch in Gangnam and Itaewon, but Hongdae Station + Yeonnam + Hapjeong + Mangwon = #1 brunch route. More than 8 brunch places within a 15-minute walk from Hongik University Station. A walk through the alleys of Yeonnam + brunch at one place is the best morning course. Instead of going to Gangnam or Itaewon for brunch, settle down in the Hongdae area.

3. Brunch time/reservation not considered

For popular brunches (Urban Plant, Dot Blanket, Becand), a 1-2 hour wait is standard on weekends. Best in Hansan on weekdays from 10 to 12 PM — On weekends, it is also OK after 10 or 14 immediately after opening. Stores are closed on Mondays and Tuesdays. — Checking Instagram, Catch Table, or Naver in advance is recommended. Brunch restaurants have limited seating, so weekdays are given priority.

Hongdae, Yeonnam, Hapjeong, Mangwon Brunch Scenery

Hongdae area has been the #1 area for brunch cafes in Korea since the early 2020s. Signature, modern, neighborhood differentiated from Gangnam (high-end, modern), Itaewon (trendy, multinational), and Hannam (Michelin, course).

Yeonnam is the #1 neighborhood for signature brunch — many signature brunch stores in the Seongmisan alley and Donggyo-ro area. Dot Blanket Yeonnam, Hippo Brunch House, Law & Cooked The Brunch, Jam Jam, etc. are the number one stores for foreigners. Instagram hotspot.

Hapjeong is a modern cafe + pancake and bagel — a modern signature cafe + pancake and bagel store in the alley between Hapjeong Station and Mangwon Station. Atmosphere stores such as Urban Plant Hapjeong, Justina, Burnt Orange, and O Bagel House. The best cafe that combines green and plants.

Mangwon is a neighborhood brunch signature — a neighborhood brunch near Mangwon Market and Mangwon Han River. Reasonably priced local stores such as B.Cand, Alive214, Clovelly, and Photosynthesis Cafe. Neighborhood atmosphere + foreigner friendly.
