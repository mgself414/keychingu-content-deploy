Incheon Airport Transit Guide — Finding Your Terminal, Moving Between Terminals, What to Do by Layover Length, Getting to Seoul (2026 Official Info)

Subtitle: Is your airline at T1 or T2? (Asiana is now T2) · Free shuttle 15–18 min · What to do on 2h, 4h, 6h, 12h and 24h layovers · Airport transit tours free to $4 · AREX express train ₩9,500 · Late-night bus ₩17,000–18,000 · Darakhyu · Michelin Bib Gourmand Hwangsaengga Kalguksu

ISSUE 04 · #1015 · 2026-06-08

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Incheon International Airport (ICN) has two terminals. Once you know which terminal your airline uses and how many hours you have, the plan almost makes itself. With 2 hours, shower and eat inside the transit area. With 3–5 hours, take an airport-run transit tour (most are free) and come back. Past 6 hours, head into Incheon city; past 12 hours, you can go all the way to Seoul. Operating details below are per the airport's official pages as of 2026-10-07 (transit, terminal transfer, transit tours, late-night buses, food and beverage); AREX fares are per the AREX website.

🛫 T1 vs T2 — Which Terminal Is Your Flight?

Terminal 1 (T1) Terminal 2 (T2)
Airlines (airport route info, 2026-10) Jeju Air, Garuda Indonesia, Cebu Pacific, Vietnam Airlines, Singapore Airlines, and all airlines other than those at T2 Korean Air, Asiana Airlines, Jin Air, Air Seoul, Air Busan, Delta, Air France, KLM, China Airlines, Xiamen Airlines, SAS, Virgin Atlantic
Airport Railroad (AREX) station Incheon Airport Terminal 1 Station Incheon Airport Terminal 2 Station (end of the line)
Transit tour desk Desks 19 and 20 on Level 1, near Exits 1 and 2 Desks 5 and 6 on Level 1, across from Exit 4
Darakhyu capsule hotel Transportation Center, Level 1 (landside) Transportation Center, Level B1 (landside)
  • Watch out for codeshares: A codeshare flight departs from the terminal of the airline that actually operates it. The airport's official guidance also says to check your e-ticket or the airport flight-information search. Asiana is at T2 as of 2026 (older info says T1).
  • Moving between terminals (airport official):
  • Free shuttle bus: T1 Level 3, center, Exit 8 → T2 about 15 min / T2 Level 3, center, between Exits 4 and 5 → T1 about 18 min. About every 10 minutes, 05:00–23:00. For a wheelchair-lift vehicle, request in advance at 032-741-3217.
  • Airport Railroad (AREX): About 6 minutes between the two stations, ₩1,050.
  • Transit passengers inside the airside area: After security, take the underground shuttle train (every 5 minutes). Once you board, you cannot come back.
  • Keep 1 hour of buffer, enough to cover going to the wrong terminal.

🍜 One Last Meal at the Airport — Hwangsaengga Kalguksu (2026 Update)

This knife-cut noodle shop's Bukchon (북촌) flagship makes the Michelin Guide Seoul Bib Gourmand list every year (per the official Michelin 2026 guide), and it has a branch inside Incheon Airport. Konest, a Japanese-language travel outlet, featured it as the "last meal before heading home" in September 2026 and drew a big response. The beef-bone broth is hot and not spicy, so one bowl is an easy pre-flight choice.

  • T1 branch (listed on the airport's official site): Terminal 1, Level 3, airside near Gate 31 — eat after passing departure immigration and before boarding. 06:00–22:00, ☎ 0507-1462-1385.
  • T2 branch: Per Konest's Sept 10 post, Terminal 2 landside, Level B1, near the AREX fare gates (use it right after arrival or before departure check-in). The airport's official food and beverage list only includes the T1 branch, so confirm the T2 one on site.
  • Menu and prices (per Konest's Sept 10 menu photo): Traditional beef-bone kalguksu ₩12,000 · Yukgaejang kalguksu ₩14,000 · Dumpling soup / king dumplings (7 pieces) ₩12,000 · Set meals ₩18,000–22,000.
  • Ordering: Touch-screen kiosk with photos, in Japanese and English. Pay cash, Alipay, or WeChat at the counter.
  • Tip: Finish check-in and security early and head toward Gate 31 to eat without a line. It is an airside shop, so you cannot go together with anyone seeing you off.

⏱ What to Do by Layover Length

Layover What you can do Key points
Up to 2 hours Stay in the airside area Shower (T1 Level 4, near Gates 25 and 29, transit passenger shower room, 05:00–23:00, paid — per airport facilities guide) · Hwangsaengga Kalguksu (T1, near Gate 31) · duty-free shops
3–5 hours Airport transit tour Group tours run by the airport. Many courses are free; paid ones cost $3–4. See the table below
6 hours+ Incheon city AREX all-stop train to Chinatown (#1012) or Songdo (#1013). Subtract 1–2 hours for immigration plus travel time and plan for 2–3 hours of activity
12 hours+ Seoul Express train 43 min (T1) / 51 min (T2), or limousine bus. 6–8 hours of activity
24 hours+ Overnight Airport capsule hotel Darakhyu or a Seoul stay. For 24–72 hours, the airport K-Stopover package ($115+, desk 07:00–18:00)

  • How to sign up: Register at least 30 minutes before tour departure at the Level 1 transit tour desk (T1: Desks 19 and 20 near Exits 1 and 2 / T2: Desks 5 and 6 across from Exit 4). A reservation holds until 30 minutes before departure; after that, open seats go to the waitlist. Open 07:00–22:00. ☎ T1 032-741-3139 · T2 032-741-0060.
  • Luggage: Checked bags are usually transferred automatically, but it depends on the airline. Take only your carry-on bag when you go out.

🚆 Getting to Seoul — AREX, Limousine Bus, Late-Night Bus

Option Time Fare (adult) Notes
AREX express train (to Seoul Station (서울역)) T1 43 min · T2 51 min ₩9,500 (transit card fare, AREX website, Oct 2026) Reserved seats, connects to the City Airport Terminal at Seoul Station
AREX all-stop train (to Seoul Station) T1 59 min · T2 66 min T1 ₩4,750 · T2 ₩5,350 (transit card) Stops at Hongik Univ. Station, Gongdeok, and more; crowded if you have lots of luggage
Limousine 6015 (Myeongdong (명동), Chungjeongno, Gongdeok, Mapo) About 60–70 min ₩17,000 (Airport Limousine official) Buy tickets at the T1 Level 1 ticket booth; first bus 04:24
Late-night bus (from 00:00) — N6000 Gangnam Express Bus Terminal (강남고속터미널) ₩17,000 · N6002 Cheongnyangni (청량리) ₩18,000 · N6701 DDP ₩18,000 · N6703 Gwangnaru Station (광나루역) ₩18,000 T1 Level 1 stops 3B (N6701, N6703) and 6A (N6000, N6002) (airport official)
  • Limousine buses to Seoul leave from the T1 Level 1 stops in front of Exits 3–7 (Gyeonggi buses at 7–10, regional buses at 11–13). Allow 1–2 hours for immigration.
  • For fares and times for Gangnam and COEX routes (such as 6703), check at the Level 1 ticket booth or the airport bus search.

🛏 Sleeping at the Airport — Darakhyu and Lounges

  • Darakhyu (capsule hotel, Walkerhill): T1 Transportation Center Level 1 and T2 Transportation Center Level B1, both landside (use before departure or after arrival). Open 24 hours. Rates are hourly — per the Walkerhill listing, a 3-hour Day-Use Double is ₩31,500 and a 12-hour Overnight is ₩70,000 (confirm when booking). ☎ T1 032-743-5000 · T2 032-743-5008.
  • Inside the airside area: Transit hotels and transit passenger facilities are clustered in the Level 4 airside area of T1 and T2 (airport guide). For a shower only, use the T1 Level 4 shower room near Gates 25 and 29.
  • Lounges: Besides airline and PP card lounges, there are paid lounges. Fees vary by lounge, so check on site.

🛒 Duty-Free and Tax Refunds

  • Lotte, Shilla, and Shinsegae duty-free shops are in the airside areas of T1 and T2. Purchases at airport duty-free are tax-free on the spot, so there is no separate refund.
  • Items bought at tax-free shops in the city are handled at the customs and refund counter before departure (VAT refund window in the T1 Level 3 airside area). See #1231 and #1304 for the procedure.
  • Currency exchange, roaming, and pharmacies are also in the T1 Level 3 airside area.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Finding your terminal from old info — Asiana, Jin Air, Air Seoul, and Air Busan are now at T2. For a codeshare, use the terminal of the airline that actually operates the flight.

2. Trying to join a transit tour on the spot on the day — Desk registration must be done at least 30 minutes before departure, and you need your passport and both boarding passes.

3. Taking the airside shuttle train and trying to come back — It is one-way. Check your concourse or terminal before boarding.

4. Remembering the express train fare as over ₩10,000 — It is ₩9,500 by transit card (AREX website, 2026-10). The all-stop train is ₩4,750 from T1.

5. Looking for Darakhyu in the airside area — Darakhyu is landside (Transportation Center). Transit hotels in the airside area are separate, on Level 4.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. I have a 3-hour layover. Can I leave the airport? — There are 3-hour transit tour courses (Sinpo Market free, Seoul Botanic Park $4). Going out on your own is tight because of immigration time.

2. How long between T1 and T2? — Free shuttle bus 15–18 min (every 10 minutes, 05:00–23:00), or AREX 6 min for ₩1,050.

3. What is the fastest way to Seoul Station? — Express train 43 min (T1) / 51 min (T2), ₩9,500. If your hotel is in Myeongdong, Limousine 6015 (₩17,000) goes there without a transfer.

4. I arrive before dawn. Is there a bus to Seoul? — Late-night buses N6000 (Gangnam Express Bus Terminal), N6002 (Cheongnyangni), N6701 (DDP), and N6703 (Gwangnaru Station), ₩17,000–18,000, at T1 Level 1 stops 3B and 6A.

5. I want to lie down for a few hours at the airport — Darakhyu (landside, hourly rates). If you cannot leave the airside area, use the Level 4 transit hotel.

6. A proper Korean meal at the airport — Hwangsaengga Kalguksu near Gate 31 in the T1 Level 3 airside area (Michelin Bib Gourmand, 06:00–22:00), beef-bone kalguksu ₩12,000. See the 🍜 section above.
