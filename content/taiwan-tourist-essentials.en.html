⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Taiwanese Tourists

1. "Korean markets are the same as Taiwanese night markets" — Lack of differentiation awareness: Taiwanese enthusiasts of Shilin (士林)·Shenlin (深林)·Siyil (四日)·Dalin (忱林) night markets assume Korean markets are the same. In reality, Gwangjang Market (광장시장) (day·evening)·Dongdaemun (동대문) (dawn)·Myeongdong (명동) (evening)·Noryangjin (노량진) (dawn) each have different timings, menus, and experiences. Taiwanese "all-in-one place" pattern → Korean "market hopping by time slot" recommended.

2. "K-drama filming locations = just scenery" — Underutilization of Taiwanese enthusiast packages: Although the proportion of Taiwanese K-drama enthusiasts is high, they perceive filming locations for 'Winter Sonata (겨울연가)·Goblin (도깨비)·Extraordinary Attorney Woo (이상한 변호사 우영우)' as just scenery. In reality, there are many KTO (Korea Tourism Organization) + Taiwanese Hanjin Travel (한진관광)·Easy Travel (이지트래블) English·Chinese K-drama package tours. Nami Island (남이섬) + Incheon (인천) Goblin (도깨비) + Buam-dong (부암동) 1-night course is the answer for a full experience.

3. "Korea-Taiwan visa-free = short visits are OK" — Unawareness of 5-night recommendation: Among 800,000 Taiwanese, 30% are short-term 1-2 night visitors. In reality, a full experience of the 4 pillars: K-drama·Night Markets·K-beauty·K-pop·BBQ = average 5 nights+. With 25+ daily Korea-Taiwan LCC routes and round-trip fares of ₩300,000-500,000 (~$220-370 / ~1,600-2,700 RMB / ~32,000-53,000 JPY), revisiting every 1-2 years is recommended.

① Night Market·Gourmet (3)

1. Gwangjang Market (광장시장) Night Market for Taiwanese

  • Signature: #1 priority for Taiwanese night market enthusiasts. Mayak Gimbap (마약김밥, "drug gimbap" — addictive mini gimbap)·Bindaetteok (빈대떡, mung bean pancake)·Kkwabaegi (꽈배기, twisted donut)·Jinokhwa Dakhanmari (진옥화 닭한마리, whole chicken soup).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — 80% English·Chinese menus, popular among Taiwanese SNS users
  • Recommended Menu: Mayak Gimbap (마약김밥) (with mustard) + Bindaetteok (빈대떡) (mung bean pancake) + Kkwabaegi (꽈배기) (rice cake-like texture) + Jinokhwa Dakhanmari (진옥화 닭한마리)
  • Tips: Jongno 5-ga Station (종로5가역) (Line 1). Peak at 12:00 (noon) and 19:00 (7 PM) for lunch and dinner, quieter on weekdays from 14:00 (2 PM) to 17:00 (5 PM). Card + cash payment accepted.

2. Dongdaemun (동대문) Dawn·Night Market

  • Signature: 22:00 (10 PM) - 04:00 (4 AM) saebyeok sijang (새벽 시장, dawn market) + Doota (두타)·Migliore (밀리오레)·Hello APM (헬로APM). A signature for comparison with Taiwan's Siyil (四日)·Dalin (忱林) night markets.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Excellent Chinese guidance
  • Recommended Categories: Wholesale clothing (22:00-03:00 / 10 PM-3 AM), night market street food, DDP (동대문디자인플라자) night lighting
  • Tips: Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station (동대문역사문화공원역) (Lines 2·4·5). Enter after 22:00 (10 PM). Full Taiwanese-style night market experience.

3. Myeongdong (명동) Street Food Night

  • Signature: Myeongdong (명동) Hotteok (호떡, sweet pancake)·Bungeoppang (붕어빵, fish-shaped pastry)·fried snacks·skewers at nojeom (노점, street stalls) from 18:00 (6 PM) to 24:00 (12 AM). Fits Taiwanese street food enthusiasts.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Chinese menus, pointing to pictures
  • Recommended Menu: Hotteok (호떡) (honey·nuts)·Bungeoppang (붕어빵) (red bean·custard cream)·skewers·fried snacks·Gyeranppang (계란빵, egg bread)
  • Tips: Myeongdong Station (명동역) (Line 4). Full set of street stalls from 18:00 (6 PM) to 22:00 (10 PM).

② K-drama·Tradition (2)

4. Bukchon (북촌) Hanbok + Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁)

  • Signature: Taiwanese K-drama enthusiast signature — Hanbok (한복, traditional Korean dress) + gunggwol (궁궐, royal palace) = Taiwanese Instagram·LINE signature.
  • Recommended Itinerary: Anguk Station (안국역) → Hanboknam (한복남)·Oya Hanbok (오야 한복) → Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) free admission → Bukchon Eight Views (북촌 8경)
  • Tips: Enter between 09:00 (9 AM) and 13:00 (1 PM). Hanbok (한복) rental ₩15,000-30,000 (~$11-22 / ~80-160 RMB / ~1,600-3,200 JPY).

5. K-drama Filming Spots — Nami Island (남이섬)·Goblin (도깨비)·Extraordinary Attorney Woo (우영우)

  • Signature: Fully satisfies the craving of Taiwanese K-drama enthusiasts. 'Winter Sonata (겨울연가)' Nami Island (남이섬) (Gapyeong (가평)) + 'Goblin (도깨비)' Incheon (인천) Chinatown + 'Extraordinary Attorney Woo (이상한 변호사 우영우)' Buam-dong (부암동).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — English·Chinese guides, Hanjin Travel (한진관광)·Easy Travel (이지트래블) packages
  • Recommended Course: Nami Island (남이섬) day trip (ITX Cheongchun (ITX청춘) 1h) or Incheon (인천) Goblin (도깨비) 1-day trip
  • Tips: Utilize KTO (Korea Tourism Organization) English·Chinese packages. For enthusiasts, consider a 1-night stay (Nami Island (남이섬) + Gangneung (강릉)).

③ Shopping·K-pop (3)

6. Olive Young (올리브영) Myeongdong Town

  • Signature: 95% foreigners — Taiwanese K-beauty enthusiasts.
  • Recommended Categories: Mask pack boxes, skincare, toners, sunblocks
  • Tips: Myeongdong Station (명동역) Exit 9. Tax-free for purchases over ₩50,000 (~$37 / ~270 RMB / ~5,300 JPY).

7. Myeongdong (명동) K-pop Goods + Weverse

  • Signature: Myeongdong (명동) Weverse·LINE FRIENDS·SM·JYP goods cluster. TWICE's Tzuyu (쯔위) from Taiwan is a signature + BTS Taiwanese ARMY's #1 priority.
  • Recommended Categories: TWICE goods (Tzuyu solo limited edition), BTS·NewJeans Weverse exclusive, K-pop albums·photocards
  • Tips: Walking distance from Myeongdong Station (명동역). Limited during concert seasons.
