🎯 How to Use This Guide

The old guide was built on figures like "Taiwan 800,000 (2024) · #6 segment · 3.5% of the population visits Korea · 1,100 USD spend per visitor · 95% stay 5 nights · ARMY 2 million+ · 8+ LCC flights a day · Gwangjang Market daytime 11–17 + evening 17–21 · Myeongdong street stalls 18–24 · Dongdaemun 22–04 · cable car 8,500 won · fan goods at the HYBE building via Weverse," but they had no source or were wrong. This time we list only values read on official pages on 2026-10-08 (VisitSeoul, VisitKorea, N Seoul Tower, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Weverse Shop, DDP) and statistics confirmed through press coverage of Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) releases. Three things matter to Taiwanese readers — numbers (Korea's No. 3 market), entry (the K-ETA exemption deadline), and nights (how late night markets and early-morning shopping really stay open). We do not name restaurants because there is no official source.

📊 Taiwan Market — Three Numbers

  • 1.47 million in 2024 (3rd) — China 4.60 million · Japan 3.22 million · Taiwan 1.47 million · US 1.32 million. 1.89 million in 2025 (3rd), a 181.4% recovery against 2019, the highest among major markets. Both confirmed through press coverage of Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) releases (the Data Lab original is a PDF and was not opened). The old "800,000 · #6 · 3.5% of population" was wrong.
  • Spend per visitor, nights stayed, repeat-visit rate, ARMY count, and number of LCC routes have no source, so we do not list them.

🛂 Entry — K-ETA

  • The K-ETA temporary exemption is extended through 2026-12-31 (two official notices from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (외교부), plus VisitKorea). Whether Taiwanese passports are on the exemption list, and whether it means 90 days visa-free, is secondary because we could not open the official list → check at k-eta.go.kr before departure. The exemption may end from 2027.

🌙 Night Course — Night Markets and Early-Morning Shopping

  • Evening: Gwangjang Market Food Alley (until 23:00, open Sundays too) → Myeongdong street stalls (hours vary by stall, not officially posted) → Dongdaemun Migliore (until 04:30) and Doota (Fri–Sat until 05:00). The old "Myeongdong 18–24 · Dongdaemun 22–04" differs from mall to mall.
  • Night view: Namsan Cable Car (남산케이블카) runs until 23:00, round trip 15,000 won (official). The old 8,500 won was a group child one-way fare.

🎬 K-drama and K-pop — Only Officially Confirmed Places

  • Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) closed Tuesdays, 3,000 won (the free-entry rule for hanbok wearers was not confirmed this time) · Nami Island (남이섬) 19,000 won, 08–21 (Winter Sonata (겨울연가)) · Goblin (도깨비) filming locations are in #1068.
  • K-pop: Weverse Lucky Store (위버스 럭키스토어) is on the 7th floor of Yongsan I'Park Mall (용산 아이파크몰) (expanded reopening 2026-03-14, official). HYBE INSIGHT has not reopened since closing in 2023-01 (secondary, official site returned 403) → see the HYBE building from the outside only. The old "limited Weverse goods at the HYBE building" and "Weverse store in Myeongdong" claims have no basis.
  • Shopping: Olive Young Myeongdong Town (올리브영 명동타운) (secondary, 10–22:30) and Lotte Mart Seoul Station (롯데마트 서울역점) (secondary, 10–24, closed 2nd and 4th Wednesdays) — we could not read the official body text for either, so both are secondary.

🗺 3 Nights 4 Days Skeleton

  • Day 1 Myeongdong street stalls → Namsan Cable Car (until 23:00) · Day 2 Gyeongbokgung Palace (not on Tuesday) → Gwangjang Market Food Alley → Dongdaemun early morning · Day 3 Nami Island day trip · Day 4 Yongsan Weverse Lucky Store → Lotte Mart Seoul Station → airport. For restaurants, go by review counts in a map app.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Gyeongbokgung on Tuesday — closed.

2. Gwangjang main market on Sunday — closed. The Food Alley is open.

3. Cable car at 8,500 won — an adult round trip is 15,000.

4. Goods at the HYBE building — INSIGHT is closed (secondary). Go to the Yongsan Lucky Store.

5. Assuming K-ETA exemption — through 2026-12-31 (official); check the official list for Taiwan's inclusion.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. How many Taiwanese visitors? — 1.89 million in 2025, third place (Korea Tourism Organization release, via press).

2. Is K-ETA needed? — Temporary exemption through 2026-12-31 (official). For Taiwan's inclusion, check k-eta.go.kr.

3. What stays open latest? — Dongdaemun Migliore until 04:30. Gwangjang Food Alley until 23:00.

4. Nami Island fee? — Adults 19,000 won (official).

5. Where are Weverse goods? — Lucky Store, Yongsan I'Park Mall 7th floor (official notice).

6. Chinese-language 1330? — The existence of 1330 is official; Chinese-language support and 24-hour service were not confirmed this time.
