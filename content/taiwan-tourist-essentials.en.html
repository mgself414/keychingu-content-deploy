⚠ 3 common mistakes Taiwanese tourists make

1. "Korean market = the same as Taiwan's night market" — Not aware of the difference: Taiwan's Shilin, Simlin, Siil, and Dangling night market enthusiasts assume the same for the Korean market. In reality, Gwangjang Market (daytime, evening), Dongdaemun (dawn), Myeongdong (evening), and Noryangjin (dawn) have different times, menus, and experiences. Taiwanese ‘all in one place’ pattern → Korean style ‘market hopping by time zone’ recommended.

In reality, K-drama·night market·K-beauty·K-pop·BBQ 4-axis full experience = average of 5 nights+. ① Night market·Gourmet (3)

1. Gwangjang Night Market for Taiwanese

  • Signature: No. 1 night market enthusiast in Taiwan. Drug gimbap, Bindaetteok, Twist, Jinokhwa chicken.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — 80% English/Chinese menu, Taiwanese SNS enthusiast
  • Recommended menu: Yakjak kimbap (mustard) + Bindaetteok (mung bean pancake) + Twist (rice cake texture) + Jinokhwa chicken
  • Tip: Jongno 5-ga Station (Line 1). Lunch and dinner peak at 12:00 and 19:00, quiet on weekdays from 14:00 to 17:00. Card + cash combined.

2. Dongdaemun Night Market

  • Signature: 22:00~04:00 early morning market + Doota, Migliore, Hello APM. Taiwan's four-day night market comparison signature.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Excellent Chinese guidance
  • Recommended Category: Clothing wholesale (22-03 p.m.), night market street food, DDP night lighting
  • Tip: Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (Lines 2, 4, and 5). Entry after 22:00. A full Taiwanese night market experience.

3. Myeongdong Street Food Night

  • Signature: Myeongdong hotteok, bungeobbang, fried food, and skewers street stall from 18 to 24 hours. Fit for Taiwanese street food enthusiasts.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Chinese menu, point to photo
  • Recommended menu: Hotteok (honey, nuts), Bungeoppang (red bean, cream puff), skewers, fried food, egg bread
  • Tip: Myeongdong Station (Line 4). Full set of street stalls from 18:00 to 22:00.

② K-drama·Tradition (2)

4. Bukchon Hanbok + Gyeongbokgung Palace (Bukchon Hanbok)

  • Signature: Taiwanese K-drama mania signature — Hanbok + palace = Taiwanese Instagram/Line signature.
  • Recommended route: Anguk Station → Hanboknam/Oya Hanbok → Gyeongbokgung Palace admission fee exemption → Bukchon 8 Scenic Views
  • Tip: Enter from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm. Hanbok 15,000~30,000 won.

5. K-drama filming locations — Nami Island, Goblin, Woo Young-woo (K-drama Filming Spots)

  • Signature: Quenches the thirst of Taiwanese K-drama enthusiasts. 'Winter Sonata' Nami Island (Gapyeong) + 'Goblin' Incheon Chinatown + 'Weird Lawyer Woo Young-woo' Buam-dong.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — English/Chinese guide, Hanjin Tour/Easy Travel package
  • Recommended course: Nami Island day trip (ITX Youth 1h) or Incheon Dokkaebi 1 day
  • Tip: Use the KTO English/Chinese package. For enthusiasts, stay for 1 night (Nami Island + Gangneung).

③ Shopping·K-pop (3)

6. Olive Young Myeongdong Town

  • Signature: 95% foreigners — Taiwanese K-beauty enthusiasts.
  • Recommended Category: Mask pack box, skin care, toner, sunblock
  • Tip: Myeongdong Station Exit 9. Tax free 50,000 won+.

7. Myeongdong K-pop Goods + Weverse (Myeongdong K-pop Goods)

  • Signature: Myeongdong Weverse, Line Friends, SM, JYP goods cluster. TWICE Tzuyu Taiwanese signature + BTS Taiwanese ARMY 1st place.
  • Recommended Category: TWICE goods (Tzuyu solo only), BTS/New Jeans Weverse only, K-pop albums/Poka
  • Tip: Walking distance from Myeongdong Station. Concert season only.

8. HYBE HQ Yongsan

  • Signature: BTS, New Jeans, and Seventeen Taiwanese mania mecca. Taiwan ARMY pool 2 million+.
  • Recommended: Weverse store exclusive goods, full concert season mania experience
  • Tip: 10 minutes walk from Yongsan Station or 5 minutes from Hangangjin Station.

④ Gourmet, Landmark, Shopping (4)

9. Myeongdong Korean BBQ

  • Signature: Korean BBQ enthusiast Taiwanese fan — ribs, pork belly, brisket, LA ribs.
  • Recommended stores: Saemaeul Restaurant, Hanam Pork House, Nolbu Bossam, Wang Pig, Hongdae BBQ cluster

10. Gwangjang Mayak Gimbap & Twisted Donut Full Experience

  • Signature: Taiwanese night market fan mania — rice cake texture pretzel + spicy kimbap (mustard) + banquet noodles.

11. N Seoul Tower + Cable Car (N Seoul Tower)

  • Signature: Taiwanese family/couple mania — Love Lock/Rendezvous/Observatory.
