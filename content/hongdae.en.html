Guide to Hongdae in Seoul — Comprehensive guide for foreign tourists

> Subtitle: From arrival and movement route to busking, shopping, Gyeongui Line Forest Trail, K Indie music, design, food and experiences — a comprehensive guide to foreigners' "things to do in Hongdae" (including 8 hot spots curated by Key Friend)

ISSUE 03 · #138 · 2026-05-11 (SERP reorder 2026-06-25)

🎯 왜 홍대인가

In Reddit r/koreatravel foreign demand-side signal analysis, Hongdae was mentioned 8 times, ahead of Gangnam (4 times) and Myeongdong (3 times). The place most frequently mentioned by foreign tourists in “Honest thoughts on Seoul from a Brit”, “Spontaneous solo trip”, etc. Indie music, design, and MZ lifestyle condensed into one neighborhood, with a distinctly different tone from Myeongdong (shopping) and Gangnam (luxury).

What is Hongdae famous for — ① K-Indie music/clubs ② Street busking (K-pop dance cover) ③ Character/K-beauty shopping such as Kakao Friends ④ Strolling Gyeongui Line Forest Trail (Yeontral Park) ⑤ MZ cafe/design goods. A neighborhood where there are more “doing and playing” experiences than “seeing” sightseeing.

⚠ 3 common mistakes first-time foreigners make

1. Hongdae=Hongik University only sees the main gate

For foreign tourists, Hongdae is located in the area between Hongik University Station (Line 2) Exit 9 ~ Eulmadang-ro ~ Wausan-ro rather than the school's main gate. The place where street performances, clubs, and design shops are concentrated is not the school itself, but the nearby streets. The correct answer is to take a picture of the front gate and then go out into the street.

2. Mistaken belief that clubs and live houses always accept foreign IDs

Live houses such as Rolling Hall and KT&G Sangsang Madang Passport ID is absolutely required (Korean regulations for ages 19 and above). Even foreigners will be refused entry without an ID. DJ Club admission fee is ₩20,000~30,000 + 1 drink included.

3. I only saw Hongdae and missed Mangwon and Yeonnam

It is common for foreigners to visit Hongdae alone, but within a 10-minute walk, Mangwon Market (market experience) + Yeonnam-dong (trendy cafe alley, Gyeongui Line Forest Road) + Hapjeong (OBJECT main store) are in the same area. If you combine the routes, it is a full day course.

Hongdae & Around Scenery

Hongdae is the 'Greater Hongdae' area that connects Seogyo-dong, Donggyo-dong, Yeonnam-dong, Hapjeong, and Mangwon in Mapo-gu. Foreigners usually start at Hongik University Station (Lines 2, 6, Airport Railroad) → Eulmadang-ro Street → Clubs/Live Houses → Mangwon Market or Yeonnam-dong Cafe Alley all day long. Unlike Myeongdong and Gangnam, MZ, indie, and underground tones are overwhelming.

🚇 Arrival and route (Exit 9 course)

The standard entry point for Hongik University tourism for foreigners is Hongik University Station Exit 9. The walking route starting here is the most efficient.

  • Exit 9 → Hongdae Shopping Street: Kakao Friends Flagship, Olive Young, Road Shop (5-minute walk)
  • → Eulmadang-ro → Hongdae Playground: Street Performance/Busking No. 1 (5-minute walk)
  • → Wausan-ro area: Sangsang Madang, club, and live house concentrated
  • Towards Exit 3 → Gyeongui Line Forest Road (Yeontral Park) → Yeonnam-dong: Walking and cafe alley
  • One stop on Line 6 or 15 minutes on foot → Mangwon Market: Market experience

Busking is a signature of Hongdae — On weekend evenings (17:00~22:00), different K-pop dance cover and indie band live performances take place every day at the Hongdae playground (free, details in ④ Street/Experience below).

🛍 Shopping — Kakao Friends·K Beauty Street

Kakao Friends Hongdae Flagship

A landmark character store on the main shopping street in Hongdae. Kakao Friends goods such as Ryan, Chunsik, Apeach, etc. — dolls, stationery, clothing, household items + large photo zone. #1 souvenir/instagram for foreign tourists.

  • Location: Hongdae main shopping street (Hongik University Station Exit 9 direction, 5-minute walk)
  • Opening: Daily (usually 10:30~22:00, recommended to check before visiting)
  • Signature: Kakao Friends Character Flagship + Photo Zone
  • Why: K-character goods + photos. Tax refund available.

Olive Young & K-beauty Street

A large Olive Young store + cosmetics road shop and SPA brands are concentrated in the Hongdae shopping street. K-beauty shopping is not as crowded as Myeongdong.

  • Location: Hongdae main shopping street area
  • Signature: K-beauty drugstore + select shop/vintage
  • Why: Many tax refund stores. Good for connecting with cafes and food.

🌳 Gyeongui Line Forest Trail · Yeontral Park (Yeonnam Walk)

Gyeongui Line Forest Park / Yeontral Park

A walking trail that regenerated an abandoned railroad into a linear park. The Yeonnam-dong section is called Yeontral Park (Yeonnam + Central Park) and is a popular picnic, pet, and cafe spot. The complete opposite of downtown Hongdae – a laid-back local atmosphere.

  • Location: Right in front of Exit 3 of Hongik University Station towards Yeonnam-dong
  • Operating: 24 hours (free)
  • Signature: Abandoned railroad linear park + Yeonnam-dong cafe alley
  • Why: If you get tired of downtown Hongdae, take a green walk. Spring cherry blossoms and summer evening picnics. Connect to Yeonnam Cafe Tour on foot.

① Music·Live (2 locations)

Rolling Hall

Opened in 1995, Korea’s first generation live house. It is the headquarters of the Korean indie rock scene and the starting point for countless bands such as Jannabi, Hyukoh, and Guckkasten. Close proximity with 200 to 300 seats. Foreign tourists are recommended to reserve tickets after checking the K-Indie set list in advance.

  • Address: 18, Eulmadang-ro 6-gil, Mapo-gu, B1
  • Opening: Depending on the performance schedule (normally 19:00 doors / 20:00 start)
  • Tickets: ₩40,000~₩77,000 (depending on the band)
  • Signature: K-Indie 1st generation live house
  • Why: An authentic course to experience Korean indie music. Some English information and ticket sites are available.

Club FF (Club FF, Funky Funky)

Hongdae Representative DJ Indie Club. Live + DJ set every Friday and Saturday. High proportion of foreign clubbers — electronica, house, techno. Admission fee usually includes one drink.

  • Address: 5-23, Eulmadang-ro 6-gil, Mapo-gu, B1 (Seogyo-dong)
  • Operating: Friday and Saturday 22:00~05:00 (Season changes)
  • Admission Fee: ₩20,000~₩30,000 (1 drink included)
  • Signature: Foreigner-friendly DJ club
  • Why: English guidance is OK. ID (passport) required, age 19 or older.

② Design·Art (3 places)

KT&G Sangsangmadang Hongdae

Sponsored by KT&G Indie Comprehensive Cultural Space. 1F Design Shop, 2F Cafe, 3~4F Gallery, B1 Indie Movie Theater. Design goods + indie exhibition + movies all in one place. A popular course for foreign tourists.

  • Address: 65 Eulmadang-ro, Mapo-gu (Seogyo-dong)
  • Operating: Daily 11:00~22:00 (excluding movie theaters)
  • Signature: Comprehensive design goods, galleries, and indie movies
  • Why: A one-hour compressed experience of Korean design and indie culture. Visited by many foreigners, English guidance available.

THANKS BOOKS — #135 connection

Bookstore+Cafe+Gallery 3-in-1. 1F Korean Design/Art Select Book (each book contains a handwritten note by an employee), 2F Gallery/Seminar. For foreigners, design books and art magazines are best if you want to see the Korean perspective.

  • Address: 57-6 Yanghwa-ro 6-gil, Mapo-gu (Hongdae)
  • Open: Daily 12:00–21:00
  • Signature: Curation of handwritten notes + design/art selection
  • Why: Long-established store/curation axis in the #135 bookstore guide. Rest for 30~60 minutes at the Hongdae course + shop for goods.

OBJECT (object)

K-Indie Lifestyle Goods Shop in Hongdae and Hapjeong. Focusing on collaboration products with Korean illustration and design artists (stickers, notes, eco bags, mugs). The number one souvenir/gift for foreign tourists.

  • Address: 13 Wausan-ro 35-gil, Mapo-gu (Hongdae) — Hapjeong and Seogyo branches available
  • Operating: Daily 12:00~21:00
  • Signature: K-Indie Illustration/Design Goods
  • Why: Not just a souvenir, but a collaboration with Korean artists. Price range ₩3,000~₩30,000.

③ Food/Old stores (2 locations)

Hongdae Gopchang Alley

The area of Eulmadang-ro 5-gil, Hongdae Night-time gopchang and makchang alley. Although it is an old-fashioned neighborhood restaurant with a larger proportion of Koreans than foreigners, it is a good course for foreigners to experience the atmosphere of an old-fashioned night market.

  • Address: Area of Eulmadang-ro 5-gil, Mapo-gu (Seogyo-dong)
  • Sales: By store / Usually 17:00~02:00
  • Menu: Gopchang, makchang, and daechang per serving ₩15,000~₩25,000
  • Signature: Long-established gopchang alley at night
  • Why: Korean style night drinking atmosphere. Kimchi stew, ramen side + soju, beer.

Mangwon Market

15 minutes walk from Hongik University or one stop on Line 6. No. 1 experience in the foreign tourist market. Tteokbokki, fried foods, pancakes, soup, street food + fruit and vegetable market atmosphere. It is smaller than Gwangjang Market but has a strong local flavor.

  • Address: 22 Mangwon-ro, Mapo-gu (Mangwon)
  • Operating: Daily 09:00~22:00 (Closed varies by store)
  • Signature: Foreigner-friendly local market + street food
  • Why: If you feel uncomfortable because Gwangjang Market has become too touristy, go to Mangwon Market. Variety of street food around ₩5,000~₩10,000.

④ Street/Experience (1 place)

Hongdae Playground (formal name: Eulmadang)

3-minute walk from Exit 9 of Hongik University Station. #1 Instagram spot for foreign tourists + street performance (busking). 주말 저녁 K-pop 댄스 커버·인디 밴드 라이브 매일 다른 공연. Free viewing.

  • Address: Eulmadang-ro, Mapo-gu (Seogyo-dong)
  • 영업: 24시간 (공연은 보통 18:00~22:00)
  • 시그니처: 외국인 버스킹·K-pop 댄스 인스타 스폿
  • Why: Hongdae starting point. 30 minute walk + Instagram photo. Weekend evenings are the best.

🎮 체험 — 노래방 · VR방 · 반지공방

Hongdae is a neighborhood where there is more “doing” experience than “seeing”**. This course is especially good on rainy days and evenings.

Noraebang, Singing Room

Coin karaoke rooms throughout Hongdae (₩500~1,000 per song) + regular karaoke rooms (₩15,000~ per hour). 외국인 친구·커플 필수 체험. Many English pop songs and K-pop songs.

  • Why: Korean-style private karaoke culture. #08 Karaoke guide explains stores and usage instructions.

VR Arcade

Many VR game rooms in Hongdae — roller coaster, zombie shooting, escape room. ₩15,000~25,000 per person.

  • Why: Indoor experience no matter the weather. 그룹·커플에 인기.

Ring-making Workshop

나만의 실버 링을 직접 만드는 공방 카페(예: Hongdae Ring Cafe). 커플·친구 기념품으로 인기, 예약 권장, 1~2시간 소요.

  • Why: Souvenirs to “make” rather than buy. A hands-on experience unique to Hongdae.

⚙ 11 FAQs

Q1. What is your first course of visit to Hongdae?

Hongik University Station Exit 9 → Hongdae Shopping Street (Kakao Friends) → Hongdae Playground (30 minutes busking) → Sangsang Madang (1 hour design shop/gallery) → OBJECT (30 minutes goods) → Eul Madang-ro lunch → Gyeongui Line Forest Road walk → dinner Rolling Hall or Club FF.

Q2. 외국인 클럽 ID 정책은?

여권 ID 무조건 필요. 만 19세 이상 (한국 음주 가능 연령). Club entrance stamp + 1 free drink is standard.

Q3. Where to get live music tickets

롤링홀 공식 사이트 또는 인터파크·예스24. Partial support for English interface. 인기 밴드는 1~2주 전 매진.

Q4. 망원시장 vs 광장시장은?

광장시장: 종로, 관광지화 됨, 마약김밥·빈대떡 등 시그니처. 망원시장: 마포, 로컬색 강함, 떡볶이·튀김·전. 외국인이 흐름에 휩쓸리기 싫으면 망원, 시그니처 코스를 노리면 광장.

Q5. Can a foreigner who is new to tripe try it?

Daechang is the easiest (less fat and good texture). Gopchang and makchang have a strong scent, so people like and dislike them. 깻잎·마늘·고추로 향 잡기. 김치찌개·라면을 사이드로 시키면 안전.

Q6. 비 오는 날 홍대 코스?

Indoor-focused — Sangsang Madang (4th floor general) + Thanks Books (indoor bookstore) + OBJECT (goods) + Karaoke, VR room, and ring workshop experience + Eulmadang-ro cafe. Clubs and live houses are at night, so rain has nothing to do with it.

Q7. K-pop 굿즈는 어디?

홍대는 K-pop보다 인디·디자인 굿즈가 메인. K-pop goods are more abundant at K-Star Road in Myeongdong or SM Town COEX in Gangnam. 홍대 OBJECT는 작가 콜라보 라이프스타일, 카카오프렌즈는 캐릭터 위주.

Q8. 휠체어 / 유모차 접근?

홍대 놀이터·상상마당·OBJECT 합정점·망원시장·경의선숲길은 평탄 동선. 롤링홀·클럽 FF·땡스북스·홍대 곱창골목은 좁은 골목·계단 다수로 접근 어려움.

Q9. Is it worth going to Hongdae? (Is Hongdae worth visiting?)

인디 음악·거리 버스킹·MZ 카페·캐릭터 쇼핑을 한 동네에서 보고 싶으면 강력 추천. However, Jongno is better for old palaces and traditional shopping, and Gangnam and Myeongdong are better for luxury and cosmetics shopping.

Q10. 홍대는 뭘로 유명한가? (What is Hongdae known for?)

K-Indie music/club, street busking (K-pop dance cover), character/K-beauty shopping such as Kakao Friends, Gyeongui Line Forest Trail (Yeontral Park), MZ cafe/design goods.

Q11. Hongdae vs Myeongdong? (Hongdae vs Myeongdong?)

Myeongdong = Shopping, tourism, cosmetics mecca, crowded with foreigners, efficient shopping. Hongdae = Indie, nightlife, local MZ atmosphere, street performances, clubs. Myeongdong for shopping efficiency, Hongdae for atmosphere and nightlife.

🌸 5 season/time zone hooks

1. Friday/Saturday night club peak: Club FF peaks from 22:00 to 02:00. If you go early, you can get to Hansan.

2. Weekend evening busking: Hongdae Playground 17:00~22:00, K-pop dance cover/indie band different performances every day.

3. Spring Cherry Blossoms/Summer Picnic: Gyeongui Line Forest Trail (Yeontral Park) Spring Cherry Blossom Walk + Summer Evening Picnic. A photo spot for foreigners.

4. Fall Indie Festival: September-October KT&G Sangsang Madang Indie Film Festival + Hongdae Street Festival.

5. Winter warm old restaurant: Hongdae Gopchang Alley + Makgeolli old restaurant from December to February (link to #136 Makgeolli Guide).
