Philippines to Korea Travel Guide: Tourist Visa (C-3-9) Documents, Fees, Processing Time, e-Arrival, Direct Airlines (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: Philippine ordinary passports are not visa-free; a tourist visa (C-3-9 short-term visa) is required (K-ETA official eligible-country list, Ministry of Foreign Affairs mission page) · visa fee waived for stays of 59 days or less (Embassy of Korea in the Philippines) · processing time 12 working days from October 1 (embassy notice) · if you enter with a visa, submit the e-Arrival Card · Philippine Airlines, Cebu Pacific and Philippines AirAsia serve Incheon (Incheon Airport). As of 2026-10-21

ISSUE 03 · #466 · 2026-05-27

🎯 How to Use This Guide

On the 9th floor of the visa application center in Manila's BGC, someone clutching a queue number double-checks the envelope holding a bank certificate. This guide lists what you need to know before joining that line, using only values from the official pages of the Korean Embassy in the Philippines, K-ETA and Incheon Airport: which visa you get, which documents you submit, how long it takes, and which airlines fly there. It is for Philippine passport holders preparing their first trip to Korea, and for people preparing documents together with family or friends.

The old guide wrote "30 days visa-free (preferential for Philippine passports)". That was wrong. Philippine ordinary passports are not on the K-ETA official eligible-country list or in the Ministry of Foreign Affairs missions' table of visa-free entry countries, and the Korean Embassy in the Philippines directs tourist visits to the short-term tourist visa (C-3-9). "350,000 per year", "80% Catholic" and "500,000+ BTS Philippines ARMY" were also removed because we could not find them on the official pages we opened this time. Overseas employment and residence (OFW, EPS) are outside the scope of tourism and are not covered.

🛂 Entry Documents: Visa, and the e-Arrival Card

  • It is not visa-free: the Philippines is not on the K-ETA official site's list of eligible countries. In the "Countries whose nationals may enter Korea without a visa" table posted by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs missions (as of February 2024, revised 2026-01-08), the Philippines appears only in the diplomatic/official passport column (Ministry of Foreign Affairs mission page). Ordinary passport holders obtain a visa
  • K-ETA: visa holders are exempt from applying for K-ETA (K-ETA official site). For reference, the temporary K-ETA exemption that applies to visa-free countries runs from January 1 to December 31, 2026 (K-ETA notice); it has nothing to do with Philippine ordinary passports
  • Visa type: C-3-9 short-term tourist visa. A single-entry visa is valid for 3 months from the date of issue (Embassy of Korea in the Philippines official notice, updated 2026-08-06)
  • Where to submit: since 2023-08-29, all visa applications go to the visa application center (KVAC, 9F Brittany Hotel BGC, Taguig) (embassy official notice). Philippine nationals' C-3-9 applications can also be submitted by mail via W Express courier: PHP 1,900 per case, about 14 working days (embassy notice, 2024-04-25)
  • Groups: for e-group visas for ordinary tourists in groups of 3 or more, submission through designated travel agencies is in full operation from 2026-06-27 (embassy notice). The embassy separately announces suspensions for some agencies, so check the embassy notices for whether your travel agency is currently designated
  • e-Arrival Card: if you did not actually obtain a K-ETA, submit the e-Arrival Card. It is free at e-arrivalcard.go.kr from 3 days before arrival, and a paper form is also accepted (Ministry of Justice, #1289 e-Arrival Card manual). Philippine travelers entering with a visa fall under this

Basic Documents for Employees (embassy official notice)

Category Documents
Basic Visa application form (Form No. 17), 1 color passport photo (3.5×4.5 cm, white background, within 6 months), original passport and a copy of the photo page, original bank certificate
Employees Original certificate of employment (position, hire date, salary, HR contact, within 3 months), copy of tax payment proof (BIR 2316 or 1701)
If applicable Copies of visas or entry stamps from OECD countries in the last 5 years, copy of PRC or IBP card

Self-employed people, students, retirees and housewives have different columns; the notice PDF separates them by occupation. Throughout 2026, holders of BDO, BPI, RCBC, METROBANK or PNB gold or elite credit cards in their own name can submit a card copy and the last 3 months of statements instead of bank documents (embassy notice, 2026-01-01 to 12-31).

🥗 Dietary Needs: If Someone in Your Party Needs Halal

If anyone in a Philippine party keeps halal, choose restaurants by the Korea Tourism Organization's 4 categories: Halal Certified (certified by a recognized body such as the KMF) · Self-certified (a Muslim owner's own declaration, every menu item halal) · Muslim Friendly (only some menu items halal, alcohol may be sold) · Pork free (no halal menu, only no pork) (VisitKorea).

VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (Usadan-ro 10-gil 15 (우사단로10길 15), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00 to 21:00) as one of the few restaurants the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF, 한국이슬람교중앙회) recognizes as halal. Certification can change, so check before you visit. For more restaurants see #453 vegan and halal guide, and for a card to show when ordering see #1300 dietary request manual.

✈ Flights

  • Incheon Airport's official list of operating airlines (regular flights of the previous month) includes 3 Philippine airlines: Philippine Airlines (PR), Cebu Pacific (5J) and Philippines AirAsia (Z2) (Incheon Airport official site)
  • Departure cities in the Philippines, days and times are not in that list; search by date on the airline's website. We could not confirm Busan (Gimhae) routes

👥 With Family: Documents Connect

The embassy notice says that if a family member traveling with you already holds a valid visa, you submit a copy of that visa and proof of relationship (original PSA birth or marriage certificate). If a family member pays the expenses, submit the sponsor's income and bank documents together with PSA proof of relationship. Eligible sponsors extend to parents, siblings, uncles, aunts, nieces/nephews and cousins (embassy official notice). For student applicants, the parents' financial documents are required.

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Philippine passports get 30 days visa-free": ordinary passports are not on the K-ETA eligible-country list. You obtain a tourist visa (C-3-9) (K-ETA official site, embassy)

2. "I have my visa, so I should apply for K-ETA too": visa holders are exempt from applying for K-ETA. Submit the e-Arrival Card instead (K-ETA official site, Ministry of Justice)

3. "Submitting 2 weeks ahead is enough": from October 1, regular processing is 12 working days. If additional documents are requested, the period is counted again (embassy notice and guidance)

4. "I'll take it to the embassy myself": since 2023-08-29, applications go to the visa application center (KVAC) (embassy official notice)
