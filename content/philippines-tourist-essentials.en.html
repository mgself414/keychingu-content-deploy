⚠ 3 Common Mistakes About the Philippines

1. "Philippines = Southeast Asian Muslim" — missing that it's 80% Catholic: The Muslim assumption. In reality, the Philippines is 80% Catholic (Southeast Asia's #1 Catholic country) + Hyehwa Catholic Church (혜화 성당) & Myeongdong Cathedral (명동 성당) enthusiast cluster + Korea's #1 Catholic infrastructure.

2. "Filipinos don't speak English" — missing the 90% English proficiency: The no-English assumption. In reality, 90% of Filipinos speak English (British/American colonial influence) + compatible with the #194 US English cluster + English-friendly Gwangjang (광장), Myeongdong (명동), and Itaewon (이태원).

3. "Filipino visits = simple tourism" — missing the OFW family visits: The simple-tourism assumption. In reality, 500K+ OFWs residing in Korea (shipyard, automotive, Samsung, LG workers) + 1st/2nd-generation family visits — a standalone segment pool.
