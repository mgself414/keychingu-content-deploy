Korea New Year Sunrise Guide: Jeongdongjin and Anmok Sunrise Park in Gangneung, Homigot Sunrise Square in Pohang, Ganjeolgot in Ulsan, Yeongdeok Sunrise Park, Seongsan Ilchulbong in Jeju

Subtitle: Jeongdongjin (open year-round) · Anmok Sunrise Park (always open) · Homigot Sunrise Square (always open) · Ganjeolgot (always open) · Yeongdeok Sunrise Park (always open) · Seongsan Ilchulbong (November to February 06:00~18:00) · 2026-27 sunrise events not yet registered — confirm via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places that many people visit in Korea to see the first sunrise of the new year. They are 2 places in Gangneung, 3 places in Pohang, Ulsan and Yeongdeok, and 1 place in Jeju, Seongsan Ilchulbong. Apart from Seongsan Ilchulbong, all are registered as always open or open year-round.

The December 31, 2026 to January 1, 2027 sunrise events were not yet registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data as of November 16, 2026, when this guide was written. The event information below is the values registered for last year (December 31, 2025 to January 1, 2026).

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Gangneung — Jeongdongjin and Anmok Sunrise Park

Jeongdongjin (정동진) [Jeongdongyeok-gil 17 (정동역길 17), Gangdong-myeon (강동면), Gangneung-si, ☎ 033-640-4536~7] is a seaside area in Jeongdongjin-ri (정동진리), Gangdong-myeon, Gangneung-si. The name means a village with a ferry landing lying due east of Gwanghwamun (광화문) in Hanyang (한양). It is about 18km south of downtown Gangneung along the East Coast. Many people come to see the sunrise at New Year, and in 2000 it hosted the Millennium Sunrise Festival (밀레니엄 해돋이축전) as a nationally designated event. It is known as a filming location of the TV drama 'Sandglass (모래시계)', and it became famous when a sunrise train started running from Cheongnyangni Station (청량리역). The sunrise train can be boarded at several stations, including Busan (부산), Dongdaegu (동대구), Daejeon (대전), Gwangju (광주), Jeonju (전주) and Chuncheon (춘천).

  • Jeongdongjin Station (정동진역): According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is listed in the Guinness Book of Records as the station closest to the sea in the world. A rail bike runs from the station around Sandglass Park (모래시계공원).
  • Pungeoje (풍어제): A fishing festival rite that doubles as a village ritual is held twice a year, on Jeongwol Daeboreum (정월 대보름) and Dano (단오).
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available (4 spaces for people with disabilities).

Anmok Sunrise Park (안목해맞이공원) [Gyeonso-dong (견소동), Gangneung-si, ☎ Gangneung Station Tourist Information Center (강릉역관광안내소) 033-642-8692] is a park connected to Anmok Beach (안목해변) and a sunrise spot. It has an 'ANMOK' lettering sculpture, benches for resting while looking at the sea, and a small stage. Performances are held on the stage on special event days and in the summer holiday season.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available. Stroller rental: Not available.

Past event — Byeongo Year Sunrise Event (병오년 해맞이 행사) [☎ Gangneung City 033-640-5130] was held from December 31, 2025 to January 1, 2026 at Gyeongpo Beach (경포해변) and Sandglass Park in Jeongdongjin. A sunset event was held on December 31 from 20:00 to 00:30, and a sunrise event on January 1 from 06:00 to 08:00. It was free.

📍 Pohang, Ulsan and Yeongdeok — Homigot Sunrise Square, Ganjeolgot and Yeongdeok Sunrise Park

Homigot Sunrise Square (호미곶 해맞이광장) [Daebo-ri (대보리), Homigot-myeon (호미곶면), Nam-gu (남구), Pohang-si, ☎ 054-270-5855] lies at Homigot (호미곶), the eastern tip of the Korean Peninsula, which corresponds to the tail of the tiger in the peninsula's shape. The nationally designated sunrise festival was held twice, and the Hanminjok Sunrise Festival (한민족 해맞이 축전) is held every year. The square has the bronze sculpture 'Hands of Coexistence (상생의 손)', a right hand and a left hand standing in the sea and on land, an Eternal Flame Box (영원의 불씨함), and a statue of Yeonorang and Seonyeo (연오랑세오녀상), the protagonists of a local tale.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available. Stroller rental: Not available.

Ganjeolgot (간절곶) [Ganjeolgot 1-gil 39-2 (간절곶1길 39-2), Seosaeng-myeon (서생면), Ulju-gun, Ulsan, ☎ Ulju-gun Forest and Parks Division (울주군 산림공원과) Park Team (공원팀) 052-204-1751~6] is where many people come to see the sunrise on New Year's Day. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is the place where the sun rises first on the Korean Peninsula, about 1 minute earlier than at Homigot in Pohang and about 5 minutes earlier than at Jeongdongjin in Gangneung. Ganjeolgot Lighthouse (간절곶등대), built in the 1920s, forms a walking path together with the park. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it has the world's largest wish postbox (소망우체통), and a postcard put in it is delivered anywhere in the country. Ganjeolgot Haeolje (간절곶해올제), a shop selling Ulju County specialty products, hands out free postcards.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Past event — Ulju Ganjeolgot Sunrise (울주 간절곶 해맞이) [Ganjeolgot, ☎ Ulju Cultural Foundation (울주문화재단) 052-980-2232] began on December 31, 2025 with a year-end concert, followed on the early morning of January 1, 2026 by a fireworks show and a drone show, along with exhibitions and hands-on activities. It was free.

Yeongdeok Sunrise Park (영덕해맞이공원) [Changpo-ri (창포리), Yeongdeok-eup (영덕읍), Yeongdeok-gun, ☎ 054-730-7052] is a coastal nature park built beside the 64km coastal road that runs from Gangu-myeon (강구면) to Chuksan-myeon (축산면). It was created by restoring land burned in a fire in 1997. More than 1,500 wooden steps form a walking path linking the coastal road and the sea, and there are two viewing decks. The symbol of the park is the Changpomal Lighthouse (창포말 등대). Many people come to see the sunrise not only at New Year but also on weekdays.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

📍 Jeju — Seongsan Ilchulbong

Seongsan Ilchulbong (성산일출봉) [UNESCO World Natural Heritage] [Ilchul-ro 284-12 (일출로 284-12), Seongsan-eup (성산읍), Seogwipo-si, ☎ 064-783-0959] is 180m above sea level, a volcanic cone formed about 5,000 years ago by an underwater explosion in the sea. It was originally a volcanic island, but sand and gravel built up and joined it to the land of Jeju Island. The summit has a crater 600m across. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, the sunrise seen from the summit has traditionally been ranked first among the Yeongju 10 Views (영주 10경), the scenic spots of Jeju (제주).

  • Opening hours: November to February 06:00~18:00 (last ticket 17:00). March, April, September and October 05:00~19:00 (last ticket 18:00). May to August 04:30~20:00 (last ticket 19:00).
  • Closed days: First Monday of every month. If it is a public holiday, it closes the next day.
  • Parking: Available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The 2026-27 sunrise events are not yet registered — the event information in this guide is for December 31, 2025 to January 1, 2026.
  2. Seongsan Ilchulbong opens at 06:00 from November to February — the last ticket is sold at 17:00, and it closes on the first Monday of every month.
  3. The sun rises at Ganjeolgot about 1 minute earlier than at Homigot and about 5 minutes earlier than at Jeongdongjin — this is the value from the Korea Tourism Organization introduction.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where does the sun rise first on the Korean Peninsula?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is Ganjeolgot (간절곶) in Ulsan. The sun rises about 1 minute earlier than at Homigot in Pohang and about 5 minutes earlier than at Jeongdongjin in Gangneung. Ganjeolgot is always open.

Q. Is there a sunrise train?

A. The Korea Tourism Organization introduction says Jeongdongjin became known when a sunrise train ran from Cheongnyangni Station (청량리역), and that a sightseeing train can be boarded at several stations, including Busan (부산), Dongdaegu (동대구), Daejeon (대전), Gwangju (광주), Jeonju (전주) and Chuncheon (춘천).
