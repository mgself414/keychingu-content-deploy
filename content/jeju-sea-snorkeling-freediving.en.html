Jeju Sea Hobbies — Snorkeling, Freediving, and Intro Scuba Diving: When, Where, and With Whom

Subtitle: From December to March, the sea off Seogwipo averages about 15.7–19.2℃ by month. The beaches are closed, and bare-body snorkeling at Panpo Port (판포포구) and Sehwa (세화) is a summer story. What remains in winter is the diving and freediving products at Seogwipo shops, but Kakao Map lists only this week's business hours and no winter boat departure schedule. This piece covers four seas with evidence of underwater activity (Panpo Port, Sehwa, the Munseom Island (문섬) area, Bomok Port (보목포구)), 7 Seogwipo shops shown as open on Kakao Map, and what the law actually sets (night ban, permit-required waters, and more), with article numbers. For beach opening dates and fees, see #1059

🎯 How to Use This Piece

This piece covers only hobbies where you get in the water: snorkeling, freediving, intro scuba diving, and fun diving. Beach swimming on the sand, and beach opening periods, fees, and facilities, are in the #1059 Jeju beach guide. Hyeopjae and Hamdeok are covered in #1059; Geumneung and Gimnyeong are not covered in this piece.

First, timing. The 2026 Jeju beach opening period was 6/24–9/6, and all 11 beaches in Visit Jeju's 2026 opening information are closed as of today (2026-11-24). The season table below separates the reality of December–March from waiting until summer.

Winter wetsuits: Water at 15–19℃ is hard to endure in a thin summer wetsuit.

📍 4 Spots — Only Places With Evidence of Underwater Activity

① Panpo Port (판포포구) — Summer Snorkeling (Hangyeong-myeon, Jeju-si)

Selected in the Korea Tourism Organization's Spot Discovery 100×100 (53 public votes) — theme "Taste-Focused Nano Community."

  • What it is: A port that Visit Jeju introduces as a snorkeling spot. The bottom is sand and the water is clear. The water is deeper than at other western beaches (Visit Jeju).
  • Address: 2877-3 Panpo-ri, Hangyeong-myeon, Jeju-si. Hours are registered as "always open."
  • Facilities: Parking lot and showers. The Korea Tourism Organization tourism information overview says the Panpo-ri youth association rents out equipment in summer.
  • Caution: There is a notice telling people not to jump or dive from the port. Lifeguards are said to be present during the swimming season, but not at all times (Visit Jeju). There is a notice to check the tides (high and low) before entering, and cooking, personal mats, and tents are prohibited.
  • In winter: Panpo Port is not on the list of designated beaches. Bare-body snorkeling in winter is not recommended. Come in summer.

② Sehwa (세화) — The Beach Where Freedivers Gather (Gujwa-eup, Jeju-si)

Selected in the Korea Tourism Organization's Spot Discovery 100×100 (54 public votes) — theme "Taste-Focused Nano Community." The Spot Discovery recommendation reason introduces it as a place where freedivers gather.

  • What it is: A beach on Olle Route 20, between Sehwa Folk Five-Day Market and the Haenyeo Museum.
  • Not a designated beach: It is not a designated beach, so there is no opening period or lifeguard operation notice (Visit Jeju, Korea Tourism Organization tourism information).
  • Caution: At high tide the sand shrinks a lot or is submerged. One blog review of a December 2023 visit wrote that the water is very cold because of spring water (fresh water welling up from the ground).
  • Facilities: Public parking lot and restrooms. Inquiries 064-728-7783.
  • In winter: Not a place to enter alone. Go in summer, with companions.

③ Munseom Island (문섬) Area (Munseom, Seopseom, Beomseom) — Boat Diving Off Seogwipo

Munseom: Selected in the Korea Tourism Organization's Spot Discovery 100×100 (41 public votes) — theme "Taste-Focused Nano Community." In the same vote's "K-Sports & Adventure" theme it received 400 votes.

  • What it is: Islands off Seogwipo Port. You cannot swim out there on your own; you go by boat from shops near Seogwipo Port and Bomok Port (보목포구). All Blue Scuba Diving (올블루 스쿠버다이빙)'s Kakao Map description says the fun diving points are Seopseom, Munseom, and Beomseom, decided by that day's weather and sea conditions (text registered 2024-05).
  • Natural reserve: Munseom and Beomseom are Natural Monument No. 421, the "Munseom and Beomseom Natural Reserve" (designated 2000-07-18). According to the Korea Heritage Portal, they are designated as a restricted-access area, so to enter the islands for management, academic, or similar purposes you need permission from the Administrator of the Korea Heritage Service. You can assume that an individual cannot land on the islands. For activities on the sea side, you follow only the points and schedule the shop sets.
  • In winter: Kakao Map lists only this week's (11/24–30) business hours and no winter boat departure schedule. Points may change or be canceled due to weather.

④ Bomok Port (보목포구) — Departure Point Toward Munseom and Beomseom (Seogwipo-si)

Selected in the Korea Tourism Organization's Spot Discovery 100×100 (224 public votes) — theme "K-Sports & Adventure."

  • What it is: The Spot Discovery recommendation reason introduces it as a place where you depart from Bomok Port, swim around the Munseom and Beomseom area, and see soft coral. There are several diving and freediving shops in Bomok-dong (Jeju Freediving School (제주프리다이빙스쿨), Jeju Badang Freediving (제주바당), and Scuba King Diving Resort (스쿠버킹다이빙리조트) in the shop list below).
  • Address: 46 Bomokpo-ro, Seogwipo-si. Restrooms and parking.
  • Caution: Korea Tourism Organization tourism information has a notice to check the high-tide time.

🏄 With Whom — Choose by Your Situation

  • First time and no certification → A shop's intro diving. The instructor handles the equipment and pre-entry instruction. The law places on the shop (the business operator) the duties of equipment-wearing measures and pre-boarding safety training (Underwater Leisure Act Article 11(1)4).
  • You have a scuba certification → Fun diving. Wow Divers (와우다이버스) and All Blue Scuba Diving (올블루 스쿠버다이빙) registered scuba fun diving as a product for certification holders (shop rules). Bring your certification card and logbook.
  • You want to learn or continue freediving → Depth training, fun diving, and AIDA courses at freediving shops in Bomok-dong and Seogui-dong. AIDA is an international freediving education organization, and names like AIDA1 and AIDA2 are used as that organization's course names.
  • With children → All Blue's Kakao Map description says intro diving is from age 10 (text registered 2024-05, seller's description). The law prevents a shop from renting equipment to, or taking aboard, anyone under 14 who is not accompanied by a guardian (Article 21(3)). A guardian goes along.
  • Traveling alone → Do not do bare-body snorkeling or freediving alone. If you join a shop program, you enter the water together with the instructor and other participants.

🛟 Safety and Law — Separating What the Law Sets From What Is Advised

What the law sets — the Act on the Safety and Promotion of Underwater Leisure Activities (수중레저활동의 안전 및 활성화 등에 관한 법률) (Act No. 20944, in force 2026-04-23). The activities the law covers are skin diving, scuba diving, and other activities set by Presidential Decree (Article 2, item 2). Article 2 of the Enforcement Decree sets underwater leisure activities as skin diving and scuba diving. Whether bare-body snorkeling and freediving fall under this is not defined in the statutes. Either way, do not enter the water at night.

  1. Night ban — Underwater leisure activities must not be done from 30 minutes after sunset until 30 minutes before sunrise (Article 13(1)). The exception is only where night safety equipment and safety management personnel are provided. Violation carries an administrative fine of up to 1,000,000 won (Article 32(1), item 2). A shop also must not operate during these hours (Article 21(3)). In winter the sun sets early, so keep the afternoon schedule short.
  2. Bad weather — The head of the Coast Guard station or the head of a local government may order activity hours to be restricted when weather or sea-surface conditions worsen (Article 13(2)). They may order a shop to restrict business hours or suspend operations temporarily (Article 22). If a shop cancels departure that day, comply.
  3. Equipment — A person engaging in the activity must wear the underwater leisure equipment needed for personal life safety in accordance with the safety management rules, and must not leave the designated activity area (Article 8). Article 8(1) also says to comply with the matters set by Article 33(3) of the Maritime Traffic Safety Act. Not wearing equipment and leaving the area each carry an administrative fine of up to 500,000 won (Article 32(2)).
  4. Certification is a shop-side requirement — Under the Act and Enforcement Decree, individuals are not required to hold a certification, and the qualification requirement lies on the shop-side "underwater leisure educator" who teaches (Article 2, item 10, and Article 11(4)). A shop must register with the Commissioner of the Korea Coast Guard (Article 15(1)). Wow Divers and All Blue registered scuba fun diving as a product for certification holders (shop rules).
  5. Going far out — An outing that goes 10 nautical miles or more is subject to reporting to the Coast Guard (Article 12(1)).
  6. Permit-required waters — Seogwipo Coast Guard Station Notice No. 2026-01 (2026-06-30) designated parts of the sea surface of Seogwipo Port, Hwasun Port, and Seongsanpo Port as "marine leisure activity permit-required waters" (basis: Article 33(3) of the Maritime Traffic Safety Act and Article 14(1) of its Enforcement Decree). The notice advises that doing skin diving, scuba diving, and similar activities in these waters without a permit, or violating permit conditions, carries an administrative fine of up to 3,000,000 won, and tells people to obtain the permit in advance from the head of the Seogwipo Coast Guard Station. The coordinates and map of the waters are in the notice. Inquiries: Seogwipo Coast Guard Station Marine Safety Division 064-793-2349.

What is advised (safety rules that are not law provisions)

  • Do not enter the water alone. There is no buddy (2-person team) provision in the body of the law, but freediving and snorkeling should be done with two or more people watching each other.
  • Use a life jacket or buoyancy equipment. Especially for shore snorkeling such as at Panpo Port.
  • Do not enter on days you have been drinking or are unwell. The law prevents a shop from renting equipment to, or taking aboard, a person who is intoxicated (Article 21(3), item 1).
  • Check the tides. Both Panpo Port and Bomok Port have notices telling you to check the tides and high-tide times.

In an accident, call 119 — According to the Policy Briefing (2022-10-11), sea accidents are now also reported to 119. Crime is 112. For travel information inquiries, the Jeju Tourist Information Center is 064-740-6000.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going in winter with the summer plan unchanged — The Seogwipo sea in December–March averages about 15.7–19.2℃ by month (private temperature site badatime, Seogwipo 2022–2025 average). If you go in winter after seeing Panpo Port snorkeling photos, the water is cold, and it is not the beach opening period either (#1059).
  2. Jumping in from the port — Panpo Port has a notice telling people not to jump or dive from the port. Even if the water is clear and looks shallow, do not jump in.
  3. Trying to land on Munseom — Munseom and Beomseom are restricted-access areas, so entering the islands requires permission from the Administrator of the Korea Heritage Service. Boat diving is done only at points the shop sets.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. What is the Jeju sea like in December? — The December sea temperature off Seogwipo is 18.4–20.6℃ (Korea Meteorological Administration), the daily-average range over 5 years (2019–23) at the Marado and Seogwipo buoys. Beaches are in their closed period, and Panpo Port and Sehwa have no winter lifeguard operation notice. Of the 7 Seogwipo shops, 5 show this week's (11/24–11/30) hours on Kakao Map, but no operation or departure schedule from December onward is posted.
  2. Can I do scuba fun diving without a certification? — Wow Divers and All Blue registered scuba fun diving as a product for certification holders (shop rules). Under the Act and Enforcement Decree, individuals are not required to hold a certification, and a shop has duties of equipment-wearing measures, safety training, and placing educators (Article 11(1)). If you have no certification, there are separate intro diving products.
  3. Where should I go if I only want to snorkel? — Go to Panpo Port in summer (July–September). The bottom is sand and the water is clear, and Korea Tourism Organization tourism information says the village youth association rents equipment in summer. For beach opening periods and facilities, see #1059.
