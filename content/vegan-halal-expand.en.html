🎯 Why a vegan, halal, and gluten-free extended guide?

Dietary restrictions (vegan, halal, gluten-free, kosher, lactose intolerance) for foreigners when visiting Korea are increasing every year (especially in Itaewon, Seorae Village, and Yeonhui-dong foreign residence areas). #69 Follow-up to the vegan/halal full guide — Full set of vegan/halal/multicultural food stores in Itaewon, Seorae, Yeonhui, and Hongdae for foreign residents.

⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Korean food = Vegan difficulty": Authentic Korean temple food, vegetables, and beans are World's No. 1 Vegan Diet — Jingwansa Temple (#182) Temple Stay + Bibimbap, Wild Greens, Tofu, etc.

2. "Halal = Only Itaewon": Itaewon + Yeonhui-dong, Seorae, Hongdae Foreign resident areas, all halal stores + supermarkets.

3. "Korean Gluten Free = None": Many authentic Korean dishes are Gluten Free — rice, soup, stew, grilled food, steamed food, pancake (no flour). However, mixed noodles, ramen, black bean noodles, pancakes, and fried foods contain gluten.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Itaewon Halal Mart?: Near Itaewon Mosque + many Halal marts (Malaysian, Indian, Turkish groceries).

2. Vegan certified store?: Certified by Plant, Loving Hut, I Can See Your Vegetables, etc.

3. Safe Korean vegan store?: Temple food (Jingwansa) + authentic Korean vegetables and beans store.

4. Gluten-free Korean food?: Rice, soup, stew, grilled food, steamed food, and pancakes are mostly OK. Ramen, noodles, fried food

5. How to find stores with dietary restrictions for foreigners?: Happy Cow (vegan) + Crescent Rating (Halal) app.

6. Yeonhui-dong/Seorae Village Halal?: Seorae Village Foreign Residents (#173) + Some Halal stores in Yeonhui-dong (#156).
