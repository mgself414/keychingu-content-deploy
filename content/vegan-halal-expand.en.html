🎯 Why an Expanded Guide to Vegan, Halal, and Gluten-Free?

The number of foreigners visiting Korea with dietary restrictions (vegan, halal, gluten-free, kosher, lactose intolerance) is increasing every year, especially in expat residential areas like Itaewon (이태원), Seorae Village (서래마을), and Yeonhui-dong (연희동). Following our #69 full guide to vegan and halal, this guide provides a complete set of vegan, halal, and multicultural dining options by expat residential areas in Itaewon (이태원), Seorae (서래), Yeonhui (연희), and Hongdae (홍대).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Korean Food = Difficult for Vegans": Korean temple food (사찰음식), namul (나물, seasoned vegetables), and traditional bean dishes are the world's top vegan diet — experience it at Jingwansa (진관사) (#182) temple stay, featuring bibimbap (비빔밥, mixed rice with vegetables), sannnamul (산나물, wild mountain greens), dubu (두부, tofu), and more.

2. "Halal = Itaewon (이태원) Only": Itaewon (이태원) + expat residential areas like Yeonhui-dong (연희동), Seorae (서래), and Hongdae (홍대) all have halal restaurants + markets.

3. "Korean Gluten-Free = Non-existent": Many traditional Korean dishes are gluten-free — bap (밥, rice), guk (국, soup), jjigae (찌개, stew), gui (구이, grilled dishes), jjim (찜, steamed dishes), and jeon (전, savory pancakes made without wheat flour). However, bibim guksu (비빔국수, spicy mixed noodles), ramyeon (라면, instant noodles), jjajangmyeon (짜장면, black bean noodles), jeon (전, savory pancakes made with wheat flour), and twigim (튀김, fried dishes) contain gluten.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Halal Marts in Itaewon (이태원)?: Numerous halal marts (Malaysian·Indian·Turkish groceries) near Itaewon Mosque (이태원 모스크).

2. Certified Vegan Restaurants?: Plant (플랜트)·Loving Hut (러빙헛)·Neoui Chaesoga Boyeo (너의 채소가 보여), and others are certified.

3. Safe Vegan Korean Restaurants?: Temple food (사찰음식) (Jingwansa (진관사)) + traditional Korean namul (나물, seasoned vegetables) and bean restaurants.

4. Gluten-Free Korean Food?: Most bap (밥, rice)·guk (국, soup)·jjigae (찌개, stew)·gui (구이, grilled dishes)·jjim (찜, steamed dishes)·jeon (전, savory pancakes made without wheat flour) are OK. Ramyeon (라면, instant noodles)·guksu (국수, noodles)·twigim (튀김, fried dishes) are not.

5. How to Find Restaurants for Foreigners with Dietary Restrictions?: Use Happy Cow (for vegan) + Crescent Rating (for halal) apps.

6. Halal in Yeonhui-dong (연희동)·Seorae Village (서래마을)?: Seorae Village (서래마을) expat residential area (#173) + some halal restaurants in Yeonhui-dong (연희동) (#156).
