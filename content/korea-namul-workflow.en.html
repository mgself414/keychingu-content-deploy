Korean Namul Cooking, Hanjeongsik & Temple Food Workflow — From Spinach to Spring Mountain Vegetables

Subtitle: 5-step workflow for foreign tourists + vegetarian etiquette + 5-color bibimbap + English phrases

ISSUE 03 · #877 · 2026-06-09

🎯 Why a Korean Namul Workflow Guide

Even if you know the #876 namul 6-type matrix — cooking, hanjeongsik (한정식), and temple food are a separate story. Korean namul (나물) comes through (a) hanjeongsik banchan (반찬) (unlimited refills of 4–6 kinds, free) (b) sachal eumsik (사찰음식) temple cuisine courses (authentic full vegetarian set) (c) 5-color bibimbap (비빔밥) (Jeonju original) (d) home-style baekban (백반) (2–3 home-style kinds) (e) old market eateries & MZ Korean restaurants (spring mountain vegetables). Foreign visitors typically don't know that (1) the 5 colors of bibimbap = a full namul set (2) temple cuisine = no five pungent vegetables (3) spring mountain vegetables have devoted fans (4) unlimited refills = free. This guide covers the 5 steps + vegetarian etiquette + English phrases.

★ The 5-Step Workflow at a Glance

Step Action Time Key point for foreigners
1️⃣ Choose a channel Hanjeongsik / temple cuisine / bibimbap / home-style / market Instant Pick by goal: free, vegetarian, authentic, trendy
2️⃣ Order Course / set meal / bibimbap / baekban / 1 pack 1–5 min Hanjeongsik & home-style = free namul
3️⃣ Receive & set up 4–6 kinds of namul + kimchi, pickles, soup, rice 5–15 min Unlimited refills OK
4️⃣ Eat & pair Rice, soup, meat, fish + namul 30–90 min Bibimbap = mix it up
5️⃣ Pay Card OK / market = some cash / no tipping 1–3 min Temple cuisine = separate cost

Total time: Hanjeongsik = 1.5–2.5h / temple cuisine = 1.5–2h / bibimbap = 30 min–1h / home-style = 30 min–1h

🌿 Workflow by Channel

Type A — Hanjeongsik Banchan Full Set (#837)

  • Signature: 4–6 kinds of namul, unlimited refills, free — #1
  • Hours: 11:30–22:00
  • Price: Hanjeongsik course ₩40,000–150,000 (~$30–113) (namul free)
  • Menu: 4–6 kinds — spinach (시금치), doraji (도라지, bellflower root), kongnamul (콩나물, soybean sprouts), gosari (고사리, fernbrake), etc.
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★

Type B — Temple Cuisine Course (Temple Cuisine)

  • Signature: Authentic vegetarian, no osinchae (오신채) — the five pungent vegetables (garlic, green onion, wild chive, chives, asafoetida) — halal OK
  • Hours: Lunch & dinner (reservation recommended)
  • Price: Temple cuisine course ₩25,000–80,000 (~$19–60) per person
  • Menu: 6 kinds of namul + tofu, mushrooms, wild greens
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (global vegetarian fans)
  • Recommended: Jingwansa (진관사), Jogyesa (조계사), Bongeunsa (봉은사), mountain temples nationwide (near Gwanaksan and Gyeongbokgung)

Type C — Jeonju Bibimbap (5-Color Original) (#856)

  • Signature: 5-color namul full set + egg + gochujang (고추장)
  • Hours: 11:00–22:00
  • Price: Bibimbap = ₩8,000–25,000 (~$6–19) per person
  • Menu: 5 colors (spinach, carrot, kongnamul, gosari, doraji) + egg + gochujang
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (Jeonju original, globally recognized)

Type D — Home-Style Baekban & MZ Korean Restaurants (#854)

  • Signature: Home-style, 2–3 kinds of namul free
  • Hours: 11:00–22:00
  • Price: ₩8,000–15,000 (~$6–11) per person
  • Menu: Rice + soup + main + 2–3 kinds of namul + kimchi
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★

Type E — Old Market Eateries (Spring Mountain Vegetables)

  • Signature: Spring mountain vegetables, Gangwon & Jeolla originals
  • Hours: 09:00–22:00
  • Price: 1 pack ₩3,000–25,000 (~$2–19)
  • Menu: Spring mountain vegetables (doraji, chwi (취, aster leaves), minari (미나리, water dropwort), gosari)
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★ (Korean-language, old-school)

🌶 Sample English Phrases for Foreign Visitors

  • "비빔밥 5색" = "5-color bibimbap with 5 vegetable sides"
  • "사찰음식 오신채 없이" = "Temple cuisine, no five pungent vegetables (no garlic·onion)"
  • "한정식 반찬 무한 리필" = "Free refills of side dishes (banchan), please"
  • "봄 산나물" = "Spring mountain vegetables (Doraji·Chwi·Minari·Gosari)"
  • "채식·비건" = "Vegan / vegetarian — but check for fish sauce·anchovy in some namul"
  • "카드 돼요?" = "Card accepted?"

⚠ 7 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Namul = just grass": Namul = a hanjeongsik banchan classic with devoted fans.

2. "Bibimbap = only the main dish": Bibimbap = a 5-color namul full set (spinach, carrot, kongnamul, gosari, doraji).

3. "Temple cuisine = only greens": Temple cuisine = namul + tofu, mushrooms, wild greens, with no osinchae (garlic, green onion).

4. "Vegetarian = always safe": Vegetarian caution — some namul may contain anchovy fish sauce (check in advance).

5. "Hanjeongsik banchan = extra cost": Hanjeongsik banchan = free with unlimited refills.

6. "Spring mountain vegetables = year-round": Spring mountain vegetables (doraji, chwi, minari) = best in spring, March–May.

7. "Jeonju bibimbap = ordinary": Jeonju bibimbap = the 5-color namul original #1, in the royal bibimbap cluster.

⚙ FAQ — 8 Questions

1. First namul — which channel: Jeonju bibimbap (5-color full set) → then hanjeongsik banchan.

2. Instagram & vlog: Hanjeongsik banchan, Jeonju bibimbap, temple cuisine at Jingwansa.

3. MZ trends: Authentic temple cuisine, spring namul at MZ Korean restaurants, 5-color bibimbap.

4. Halal, vegetarian, vegan: All 6 kinds OK; temple cuisine #1 (no osinchae; check for anchovy fish sauce).

5. Spring mountain vegetables: Doraji, chwi, minari, gosari (March–May).

6. Allergies & pregnancy: See the #876 disclaimer.

7. Payment & tipping: Card OK / market = some cash / no tipping.

8. VAT refund: Food and beverages are generally not refundable.

⚠ Medical, Safety & Dietary Disclaimer

Namul = ordinary plant-based food. Pregnant women and children = OK. Declare allergies in advance (garlic; gluten in soy sauce; doraji as a medicinal herb; anchovy fish sauce added at some places — check first). If you suspect food poisoning, call ☎ 1339. This guide is general information, not medical advice.

Dietary restriction fallback:

  • Halal & kosher: All 6 kinds OK; temple cuisine #1.
  • Vegetarian & vegan: All 6 kinds OK; check in advance for anchovy fish sauce at some places.
  • Gluten-free: Soy sauce seasoning = gluten (substitute tamari).
  • Temple cuisine (no osinchae): OK once garlic seasoning is excluded.
