Why K-pop Pilgrimage by Subway — A Fan's Day Is a Battle of Routing

The K-pop fan pilgrimage revolves around 6 sites — the HYBE headquarters area in Yongsan (용산), the Seoul Forest (서울숲) SM area (Seongsu (성수) fan pop-up district), K-star Road (the bear statues of Apgujeong Rodeo (압구정로데오)), Hongdae (홍대) (busking and merch), Myeongdong (명동) (photocard and merch arcades), and the Express Bus Terminal (고속터미널) underground mall (budget merch). The problem: they are scattered across Gangbuk (강북) and Gangnam (강남). But every one of them is within walking distance of a subway station, so a one-day pilgrimage on a single T-money card is entirely possible.

This guide is the third axis, orthogonal to the line-by-line series (#1241–#1249) and the hub series (#1250–#1254), and the hands-on subway companion to #207 (course proposal). It is the sixth installment of the subway course series (Phase 2, themed journeys).

6 K-pop Pilgrimage Sites × Subway Access — Which Station Do You Get Off At?

🏢 HYBE Headquarters Area (Yongsan) — BTS Fan Photo Spot

  • 🚇 Access: Line 4 Sinyongsan Station (신용산역) Exit 2, 5-min walk · Line 1 Yongsan Station, 8-min walk
  • ✨ Highlights: Fan photo spot in front of the HQ (exterior) · pairs with Yongnidan-gil (용리단길) cafes · no interior tours — check whether exhibitions are running
  • ⏱ Time: 30 min–1 hr (+ Yongnidan-gil)
  • cross-ref: #1243 Line 4 (Sinyongsan) · #1258 art museums (near APMA)

🌳 Seoul Forest SM Area (Seoul Forest) — Fan Pop-ups & Seongsu District

  • 🚇 Access: Suin–Bundang Line Seoul Forest Station (서울숲역) Exit 4, right there · Line 2 Ttukseom Station (뚝섬역), 10-min walk
  • ✨ Highlights: SM headquarters area · the Seongsu fan pop-up district (photo booths and pop-up stores rotating constantly) · Seoul Forest stroll
  • ⏱ Time: 1–1.5 hrs
  • cross-ref: #1250 Wangsimni (왕십리) Hub (Seoul Forest) · #1241 Line 2 (Seongsu)

🧸 K-star Road (Apgujeong Rodeo) — Street of Bear Statues

  • 🚇 Access: Suin–Bundang Line Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역) Exit 2, right there
  • ✨ Highlights: Street of idol GangnamDol (강남돌) bear statues · Galleria luxury shopping district · photo-op route
  • ⏱ Time: 40 min–1 hr
  • cross-ref: #1242 Line 3 (Apgujeong)

🎤 Hongdae (Hongdae) — Busking, Merch, Light Sticks

  • 🚇 Access: Line 2 / Gyeongui–Jungang Line / AREX Hongik University Station (홍대입구역) Exit 9
  • ✨ Highlights: Street busking (weekend evenings) · dense cluster of K-pop merch shops · random play dance spots
  • ⏱ Time: 1.5–2 hrs
  • cross-ref: #1253 Hongik Univ. Station Hub · #1241 Line 2

💿 Myeongdong (Myeongdong) — Photocard & Album Arcades

  • ✨ Highlights: Albums and specialist photocard arcades · large merch stores · combine with currency exchange and shopping
  • ⏱ Time: 1–1.5 hrs
  • cross-ref: #1243 Line 4 (Myeongdong) · #1259 night views (Namsan (남산) pairing)

🛍 Express Bus Terminal Underground Mall (Express Bus Terminal) — Budget Merch

  • 🚇 Access: Lines 3/7/9 Express Bus Terminal Station (고속터미널역), direct underground connection (Goto Mall (고투몰))
  • ✨ Highlights: Large underground mall for budget merch and fancy goods · rainy-day alternative · pairs with Banpo (반포) Hangang (#1256)
  • ⏱ Time: 1 hr
  • cross-ref: #1247 Line 9 (Express Bus Terminal) · #1256 Hangang line (Banpo)

🎯 Common Fan Pilgrimage Tips — Before You Go

  • 📱 Pop-ups are living things: Fan pop-ups and exhibitions turn over every few weeks — checking official SNS before you set out is half the pilgrimage.
  • 📸 HQ etiquette: Company buildings are exterior photos only — no attempts to enter, and refraining from staking out artists' commutes is fandom manners.
  • 💳 Photocard prices: Photocards trade at market rates — compare Myeongdong and online prices before buying.
  • 💡 Routing tip: Split Gangbuk (Hongdae, Myeongdong, Yongsan) and Gangnam (Seoul Forest, Apgujeong Rodeo, Express Bus Terminal) into one day each, or cherry-pick the essentials into a single day.

5 Transfer & Riding Tips — Get 200% Out of Your K-pop Pilgrimage

Tip 1. Every Site Sits on a Different Line

Yongsan = Line 4, Seoul Forest and Apgujeong Rodeo = Suin–Bundang, Hongdae = Line 2, Myeongdong = Line 4, Express Bus Terminal = Lines 3/7/9. Plan by splitting Gangbuk and Gangnam.

Tip 2. Pop-ups First, Fixed Spots Later

Pop-ups are limited-run — anchor your day to the pop-up schedule and slot in the fixed spots (K-star Road, arcades) around it.

Tip 3. Busking Peaks on Weekend Evenings

Hongdae busking and random play dance peak on weekend evenings — put them at the end of your course.

Tip 4. Concert Days: Avoid or Aim

Lodging and transit near concert venues (Jamsil (잠실), Gocheok (고척), etc.) grind to a halt on show days — avoid them without a ticket, or if you have one, work backward from merch booth hours.

Tip 5. One Transit Card for Everything

One T-money card covers every line to all 6 sites. Allow 30+ minutes for Gangbuk ↔ Gangnam crossings.

FAQ 6 — K-pop Pilgrimage Questions Foreign Visitors Ask Most

Q1. Can I cover everything in one day?

  • All 6 is a stretch — one Gangbuk day / one Gangnam day, or a realistic combo of 3 core sites.

Q2. How is this different from the #207 K-pop 1-day?

  • #207 is a course proposal; this piece is the hands-on guide to subway routing, exits, and merch districts. Use them as a pair.

Q3. Can I go inside the company buildings?

  • No (exterior photos only). Exhibitions and experience halls come and go — confirm officially.

Q4. Where is merch cheapest?

  • The Express Bus Terminal underground mall for value, Myeongdong for variety. For photocards, compare market prices.

Q5. When does the busking happen?

  • Hongdae weekend evenings are peak. It flexes with weather and events — luck of the day.

Q6. Where do I find pop-up info?

  • Artists' and agencies' official SNS plus Seongsu pop-up accounts. Checking before you set out is half the pilgrimage.
