⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Korea = only four seasons": Korea's four seasons = Spring (March-May plum blossoms, cherry blossoms, azaleas) + Summer (June-August rainy season, Sambok) + Autumn (September-November peak foliage) + Winter (December-February -10℃·Pyeongchang Snow Festival) + Foreign enthusiast matrix·#385·#506 integrated.

2. "Korean winter = warmer than Japan and Europe": Korean winter = January average -6℃·Feels like -15℃·Padding, underwear, gloves, hot pack required** + Foreign enthusiasts·Pyeongchang Snow Festival·#385·#456 integrated.

3. "Korean summer = same as America and Europe": Korean summer = July-August 30-35℃ + humidity 80%+ + rainy season (6.20-7.20) + Sambok health food (#506·#388) + Foreign mania·#06 Han River chicken and beer.

4. "Autumn foliage = November": Korean autumn foliage = Seorak Mountain, mid-October → Naejangsan Mountain and Nami Island, late October → Seoul, early November + #385·#229·#273 Matrix·No. 1 foreign enthusiast.

5. "Yellow dust and fine dust = only spring": Yellow dust and fine dust = Spring (March-May #1)·Winter (December-February #2)·Mask required + Naver Map fine dust check·KF94 mask·Foreign enthusiasts.
