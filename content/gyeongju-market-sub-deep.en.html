Gyeongju Jungang Market and Silla Millennium sub-deep 12 selections for foreigners

Category: A Outskirts/Nature

Parent deep: #380·#618·#244 sub

Gyeongju Jungang Market, Silla Millennium 12th Place

1. Gyeongju Jungang Market (permanent + 2-7 day market)

  • Discrimination: 1971 · Permanent establishment + parallel 2nd and 7th day oil market · Dombegi (salted shark meat) Gyeongsang-do native
  • Foreigner visit: visitkorea Open Tourism Everyone’s Travel Selection
  • Someori Gukbap Village: A cluster of restaurants specializing in savory and rich beef head soup

2. Gyeongju Hwangridan-gil (Shopping·Cafe)

  • Discrimination: MZ No. 1 Gyeongju hot spot·Hwangnam-dong·Hanok cafe·Souvenir·SNS spot
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ MZ EN·JP·CN·#583 Insadong cross

3. Bulguksa Temple (UNESCO)

  • Discrimination: 1995 First Korean to list “Seokguram Grotto and Bulguksa Temple” on UNESCO·Kim Dae-seong in 751
  • Dabotap pagoda, Seokgatap pagoda: Korean traditional stone pagoda essence, 10 won, 1,000 won notes
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN 1st place·#663 Dosan Seowon cross

4. Seokguram Grotto (UNESCO)

  • Discrimination: UNESCO registration in 1995·Main Buddha + 11-faced Bodhisattva Avalokitesvara + 11 disciples·8th century Silla
  • Location: Tohamsan Mountain behind Bulguksa Temple, 15 minutes by car
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

5. Cheomseongdae

  • Distinction: Oldest East Asian Astronomical Observatory·Queen Seondeok in 647·Height 9.17m 365 stones
  • Location: 5 minutes walk next to Hwangridan-gil
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN·No. 1 certification shot

6. Daereungwon (Cheonmachong Tomb)

  • Discrimination: Excavated from Cheonmachong Tomb in 1973, horse painting, birch saddle, gold crown, gold earrings
  • Location: 5 minutes walk from Cheomseongdae, Hwangridan-gil cluster
  • Foreign fit: ★★★★ Authentic enthusiast

7. Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (Anapji Pond) - Night view

  • Discrimination: Silla Palace in 674·MZ No. 1 night view Gyeongju #1·LED after sunset
  • Light on: 22:00 after sunset·MZ photo spot
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ MZ JP·CN Night View Mania·#658 Yeosu cross

8. Yangdong Village (UNESCO)

  • Discrimination: 2010 UNESCO registration (along with Hahoe Village), 600 years, Wolseong Son clan, Yeogang Lee clan
  • Location: 20 minutes by car from downtown Gyeongju
  • kr cross: #663 Hahoe Village sub

9. Hwangnam Bread

  • Discrimination: 1939·Gyeongju Signature Red Bean Bread·Hwangnam-dong·National Courier Popularity
  • Store: Hwangnam Bread Main Branch (Hwangnam-dong) · Return to home country OK after purchase
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ JP No. 1 bread enthusiast/souvenir

10. Gyeongju Bread

  • Distinction: Walnuts + sweet red beans, imitation of Hwangnam bread, Gyeongju souvenir bestseller
  • Store: Gyeongju Station, Hwangridan-gil, and highway rest areas

11. Gyeongju Hanbok Experience

  • Discrimination: Hanbok + Hwangridan-gil + Cheomseongdae + Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond full set·SNS certification
  • Price: 15,000 won for 1 hour, 25,000 won for 3 hours
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ JP·CN MZ 1st place·#588 Hanbok cross

12. KTX Singyeongju Station + City Tour

  • Access: Seoul Station → Singyeongju KTX 2 hours and 10 minutes (2010 KTX Gyeongjeon Line opened)
  • City Tour: Hwangridan-gil - Bulguksa Temple - Seokguram Grotto, full day, 6 hours / 40,000 won
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN day trip

FAQ 6

Q1. Is Gyeongju day-trip possible? A. KTX 2 hours 10 minutes, full day OK / Hwangridan-gil + Bulguksa Temple first.

Q2. Bulguksa Temple entrance fee? A. Adults 6,000 won, Seokguram Grotto separately 6,000 won, set 11,000 won.

Q3. Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond night view time? A. After sunset 22:00·Summer 19:00-·Winter 17:00-. Free (separate admission fee).

Q4. Hwangnam bread vs. Gyeongju bread? A. Hwangnam bread = red bean only / Gyeongju bread = walnut + red bean. The origin of Hwangnam Bread (1939).

Q5. Recommended store for Hanbok experience? A. Many in Hwangridan-gil (Hanboknam, Hanbokjaein, etc.) - 15,000 won per hour~.

Q6. Transfer between Singyeongju Station and Dongdaegu Station? A. Direct KTX to Singyeongju approximately 10 times a day, approximately 3 hours when transferring to Dongdaegu.
