⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Hangang Park = All the same" — 11 Hangang Parks in Kaesong

There are 11 Hangang Parks in Seoul (Yeouido, Banpo, Ttukseom, Jamsil, Jamwon, Ichon, Mangwon, Nanji, Gangseo, Yanghwa, Gwangnaru, Gangbuk Riverside, etc.). Each park has a different personality:

  • Yeouido = Yunjung-ro Cherry Blossoms, E-Cruise, Moonlight Square (city center, landmark)
  • Banpo = Jamsu Bridge · Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (Instagram · Night view)
  • Ttukseom = Bicycle, Yacht, BBQ (Activities)
  • Jamsil = Adjacent to Family/Olympic Park (#82)
  • Mangwon·Nanji = Secluded·Nature (west of Seoul)
  • Gwangnaru = Starting point on the east side of the Han River Bicycle (#183)

2. "Children's Grand Park = Only for children" — Korea's No. 1 free urban zoo Miji

Children's Grand Park (established in 1973 by President Park Chung-hee's order) is Korea's No. 1 urban zoo, amusement park, and botanical garden (560,000 pyeong) with free admission. 1st place with foreign children + Korean traditional garden + family walk. Directly connected to Children's Grand Park Station on Line 5.

3. "3 major national parks = hiking only"

In addition to hiking, Bukhansan, Gwanaksan, and Namsan Dulle-gil (OK for beginners and families) + Temple (Jingwansa Temple on Bukhansan, Yeonjuam on Gwanaksan, Dongguk University on Namsan) + No. 1 photo spot for foreigners. Also OK for beginners and families.

⚙ 8 FAQs

Q1. 1st place for foreigners out of 11 Hangang parks?

Spring, April: Yeouido (Cherry blossoms in Yunjung-ro) / Summer: Ttukseom (activities, BBQ) / Year-round night view: Banpo (Jamsu Bridge, Moonlight Rainbow Fountain). Recommended at once = Ttukseom (the best in variety).

Q2. How do I bike to Hangang Park?

Ttareungi (Seoul City public bicycle) — 1,000 won for 1 hour. Gwangnaru → Jamsil → Ttukseom → Banpo → Yeouido 30km course (Korea’s No. 1 bicycle course). Foreign credit card OK.

Q3. Han River BBQ designated area?

Ttukseom, Banpo, Jamsil, Yeouido Hangang Park Some designated areas. Advance reservation (Seoul Hangang Park website, English OK). Zone 1 30,000 won/4 people. 1st place from May to October.

Q4. Children’s Grand Park free?

Free + 24 hours (Zoo 10:00~18:00). 1st priority for traveling with foreign children. Children’s Grand Park Station, Line 5.

Q5. Seoul Forest + Hangang Park route?

Seoul Forest (Bundang Line Seoul Forest Station) → 5-minute walk → Ttukseom Hangang Park. Korea’s No. 1 “Forest + Han River” combination route.

Q6. Beginner hiking among Bukhansan, Gwanaksan, and Namsan?

Namsan (265m, 30 minutes to 1 hour) → Gwanaksan Mountain Dulle-gil → Bukhansan Mountain Dulle-gil. All trails are suitable for beginners and families.

Q7. TOP 3 spring cherry blossom parks?

1st place Yeouido Yunjung-ro (#169) + 2nd place Seoul Forest spring flowers + 3rd place Yangjae Citizen's Forest, Banpo Hangang Park, Olympic Park. Peak in the first to second weeks of April.

Q8. TOP 3 fall foliage parks?

1st place Bukhansan Mountain (836m·#182) + 2nd place Olympic Park (#82) + 3rd place Yangjae Citizen’s Forest, Gwanaksan Mountain, Namsan. Peak season October-November.
