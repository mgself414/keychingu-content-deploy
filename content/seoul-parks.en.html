🎯 Why a Full Guide to Seoul Parks?

Seoul (서울) is the world's No. 1 park city, with 11 Hangang (한강) Parks + 5 major urban parks (Seoul Forest (서울숲), Olympic Park (올림픽공원), Children's Grand Park (어린이대공원), Yangjae Citizens' Forest (양재 시민의숲), Seoseoul Lake Park (서서울호수공원)) + 3 national parks (Namsan (남산), Bukhansan (북한산), Gwanaksan (관악산)) all concentrated within one city. This is a top content priority for foreign tourists' Korea (한국) vlogs and Instagram, meeting the full demand of families, children, MZ generation (MZ세대, Millennials and Gen Z), and hiking enthusiasts.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "All Hangang (한강) Parks are the same" — Not recognizing the unique characteristics of the 11 Hangang (한강) Parks.

There are 11 Hangang (한강) Parks in Seoul (서울) (Yeouido (여의도), Banpo (반포), Ttukseom (뚝섬), Jamsil (잠실), Jamwon (잠원), Ichon (이촌), Mangwon (망원), Nanji (난지), Gangseo (강서), Yanghwa (양화), Gwangnaru (광나루), Gangbukgangbyeon (강북강변), etc.). Each park has its own distinct personality:

  • Yeouido (여의도) = Yeouido Cherry Blossom Road (윤중로 벚꽃), E-Cruise (이크루즈), Moonlight Square (달빛광장) (Urban · Landmark)
  • Banpo (반포) = Jamsu Bridge (잠수교), Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (달빛무지개분수) (Instagram · Night View)
  • Ttukseom (뚝섬) = Cycling, Yachting, BBQ (Activities)
  • Jamsil (잠실) = Family-friendly, adjacent to Olympic Park (올림픽파크) (#82 jamsil_183_gangdong_sister.md)
  • Mangwon (망원) · Nanji (난지) = Tranquil · Nature (Western Seoul (서울))
  • Gwangnaru (광나루) = Eastern starting point for Hangang (한강) cycling (#183 jamsil_183_gangdong_sister.md)

2. "Children's Grand Park (어린이대공원) is only for kids" — Not recognizing Korea's (한국) No. 1 free urban zoo.

Children's Grand Park (어린이대공원) (established in 1973 by order of President Park Chung-hee (박정희)) is Korea's (한국) No. 1 urban zoo, amusement park, and botanic garden (560,000 pyeong / 1.85 million sqm) with free admission. It's a top choice for foreign visitors with children, offering traditional Korean gardens and family strolls. Directly connected to Children's Grand Park Station (어린이대공원역) on Line 5.

3. "The 3 National Parks are only for hiking"

Bukhansan (북한산), Gwanaksan (관악산), and Namsan (남산) offer more than just hiking: Dulle-gil (둘레길, walking trails) (suitable for hiking beginners and families) + temples (Jingwansa (진관사) in Bukhansan (북한산), Yeonjuam (연주암) in Gwanaksan (관악산), Dongguk University (동국대) in Namsan (남산)) + top photo spots for foreigners. Suitable for hiking beginners and families.

⚙ FAQ 8 Questions

Q1. Among the 11 Hangang (한강) Parks, which is the No. 1 for foreigners?

Spring (봄) April (4월): Yeouido (여의도) (Yeouido Cherry Blossom Road (윤중로 벚꽃)) / Summer (여름): Ttukseom (뚝섬) (Activities · BBQ) / Year-round Night View: Banpo (반포) (Jamsu Bridge (잠수교) · Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (달빛무지개분수)). Recommended for a single visit = Ttukseom (뚝섬) (strongest in variety).

Q2. How to cycle in Hangang (한강) Parks?

Ttareungi (따릉이, Seoul (서울) Public Bicycle) — ₩1,000 for 1 hour. Gwangnaru (광나루) → Jamsil (잠실) → Ttukseom (뚝섬) → Banpo (반포) → Yeouido (여의도) 30km course (Korea's (한국) No. 1 cycling course). Foreign credit cards OK.

Q3. Designated BBQ areas in Hangang (한강) Parks?

Some designated areas in Ttukseom (뚝섬) · Banpo (반포) · Jamsil (잠실) · Yeouido (여의도) Hangang Parks. Advance reservation (Seoul (서울) Hangang Park (한강공원) website, English OK). ₩30,000 per area for 4 people. Top choice from May (5월) to October (10월).

Q4. Is Children's Grand Park (어린이대공원) free?

Free + 24 hours (Zoo 10:00-18:00 (10 AM - 6 PM)). Top choice for foreign visitors with children. Children's Grand Park Station (어린이대공원역) on Line 5.

Q5. How to get from Seoul Forest (서울숲) to Hangang Park (한강공원)?

Seoul Forest (서울숲) (Seoul Forest Station (서울숲역) on Bundang Line (분당선)) → 5-minute walk → Ttukseom Hangang Park (뚝섬 한강공원). Korea's (한국) No. 1 "Forest + Hangang (한강)" combined route.

Q6. Among Bukhansan (북한산), Gwanaksan (관악산), and Namsan (남산), which is best for hiking beginners?

Namsan (남산) (265m, 30 minutes - 1 hour) → Gwanaksan (관악산) Dulle-gil (둘레길) → Bukhansan (북한산) Dulle-gil (둘레길). All Dulle-gil (둘레길) are suitable for hiking beginners and families.

Q7. TOP 3 Spring (봄) Cherry Blossom Parks?

1st Yeouido Cherry Blossom Road (윤중로) (Yeouido (여의도) #169 yeouido_guide.md) + 2nd Seoul Forest (서울숲) Spring Flowers + 3rd Yangjae Citizens' Forest (양재 시민의숲) · Banpo Hangang Park (반포 한강공원) · Olympic Park (올림픽공원). Peak in the first-second week of April (4월).

Q8. TOP 3 Autumn (가을) Foliage Parks?

1st Bukhansan (북한산) (836m · #182 eunpyeong_177_nowon_dobong_sister.md) + 2nd Olympic Park (올림픽공원) (#82 jamsil_183_gangdong_sister.md) + 3rd Yangjae Citizens' Forest (양재 시민의숲) · Gwanaksan (관악산) · Namsan (남산). Peak in October (10월) - November (11월).
