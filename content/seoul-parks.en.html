12 Seoul Parks — Choose by Closing Time, Paid Facilities, and the Nearest Station

Subtitle: For travelers who want to spend half a day in a Seoul park, this guide groups 12 parks by closing time. Seoul Children's Grand Park is not open 24 hours; it is open 05:00–22:00. Olympic Park lets you in on foot from 05:00 to 22:00, and only the plaza area stays open until 24:00. World Cup Park opens at 06:00 and closes at 21:00 from October to March. At Seoul Botanic Park only the Themed Garden, which has the greenhouse, charges an adult fee of 5,000 won, and in winter (November–February) its last entry is at 16:00. Ordinary cars and taxis cannot go up Namsan, so you take Green Circulation Bus 01A or 01B. The Moonlight Rainbow Fountain at Banpo Hangang Park runs only from March 15 to October, so it rests in winter. Hours and fares are values read on 2026-11-25, and fares are adult list prices.

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 12

This list is made for foreign travelers who want to spend half a day in a Seoul park. Among these 12 parks, the only place whose operating body lists an admission fee is the Themed Garden (주제원) of Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원). Closing times and paid facilities differ from park to park. So for every park this issue first writes down "what time it closes, where you pay, and which station and exit".

The parks are divided into three groups.

  • Parks with closing times (5 spots): Olympic Park (올림픽공원), Seoul Children's Grand Park (서울어린이대공원), World Cup Park (월드컵공원) (Haneul Park (하늘공원)), Seonyudo Park (선유도공원), Seoul Botanic Park.
  • Parks that are always open (5 spots): Seoul Forest (서울숲), Naksan Park (낙산공원), Seoul Iris Garden (서울창포원), Dream Forest (북서울꿈의숲), Namsan Park (남산공원). Even when the park is open, the facilities inside have closing times. Seoul Forest follows the Kakao Map registered value (카카오맵 등록값).
  • Hangang parks (2 spots): Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도한강공원), Banpo Hangang Park (반포한강공원). The fountains have fixed operating periods.

Among these, Seoul Forest, Naksan Park, Seonyudo Park and Seoul Iris Garden are places that received votes in the nature park category of the Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) "Spot Discovery 100×100" public vote. Seoul Forest is 9th with 398 votes, Naksan Park is 30th with 302 votes, Seonyudo Park is 48th with 260 votes, and Seoul Iris Garden is 85th with 202 votes (spot100x100.kr, received 2026-11-16).

⚠ 4 Easy Mistakes

  1. Seoul Children's Grand Park is not open 24 hours. Per the official Seoul Facilities Corporation guide (read 2026-11-25), it is open 05:00–22:00 all year. The zoo is 10:00–17:00. The stations are also confusing. The main gate is Line 7 Children's Grand Park Station Exit 1, and if you come by Line 5, you leave at Achasan Station Exit 4 and enter by the rear gate.
  2. At Seoul Botanic Park, the only place you pay is the Themed Garden. Per the official Seoul Botanic Park guide (read 2026-11-25), the admission fee applies only to the greenhouse and the themed gardens, and the Open Forest (열린숲), Lake Garden (호수원) and Wetland Garden (습지원) are always free and open. In winter (November–February) the Themed Garden closes at 17:00 and entry ends at 16:00. The Themed Garden is closed on Mondays.
  3. You cannot go up Namsan by car or taxi. The Seoul Metropolitan Government notice says that ordinary passenger cars and taxis have been banned since May 1, 2005, and large buses (tour buses) since August 2021. Take Green Circulation Bus 01A or 01B (녹색순환버스 01A·01B), or go up on foot.
  4. You cannot enter World Cup Park at night. The official Seoul Metropolitan Government guide (read 2026-11-25) says "entry is controlled at night to protect species". It closes at 21:00 in October–December and in January–March. Seonyudo Park also has a closing time, 06:00–24:00.

① Parks With Closing Times — 5 Spots

If you are going in the evening, look at the closing time first. Among the five, the only park that opens the whole park until 24:00 is Seonyudo Park (Olympic Park is 24:00 only in the plaza area). The places where facilities end early are the Themed Garden of Seoul Botanic Park (17:00 in winter) and the zoo of Seoul Children's Grand Park (17:00).

Olympic Park (올림픽공원)

The values below are the official guide (read 2026-11-25) on the Olympic Park website of Korea Sports Promotion Foundation (한국체육산업개발).

  • Period of use: open all year.
  • Entry hours: on foot or by bicycle, 05:00–22:00. The plaza area is until 24:00. For vehicles, 06:00–22:00.
  • After 22:00: for the safety of facilities, the personal protection of visitors and the protection of the cultural property Mongchontoseong Earthen Fortress (몽촌토성), entry to the inside of the park is prohibited after 22:00 (official guide). On days with late-night events, the opening of the gates is adjusted.
  • Fare: use is free. Parking is paid.
  • Address: 424 Olympic-ro (올림픽로), Songpa-gu (송파구), Seoul.
  • How to get there: Line 5 Olympic Park Station Exits 3·4, Line 8 Mongchontoseong Station Exit 1, Line 9 Olympic Park Station Exits 3·4, Line 9 Hanseong Baekje Station Exit 2.
  • Information centers: at Peace Plaza (평화의광장) and Meeting Plaza (만남의광장). They open 09:00–19:00 and close 12:00–13:00. If you leave an ID card, they lend a wheelchair free for 3 hours.
  • Rose Plaza (장미광장): open all year. The flowers bloom in May–June and September–October.
  • Rules: it is a non-smoking park. Cooking is not allowed. Dogs must be on a leash and may not enter the lawns. Drones and electric kickboards are banned. Pedestrians have priority on the fortress walking paths.
  • Timing: if you walk after sunset, leave the inside of the park before 22:00. The place to stay late is the plaza area, which is open until 24:00.

Seoul Children's Grand Park

The values below are the official guide (read 2026-11-25) from Seoul Facilities Corporation.

  • Park opening: open all year, 05:00–22:00.
  • Fare: park entry is free, and the paid amusement facilities inside the park have a separate fare table from the operating body.
  • Zoo visiting hours: zoo 10:00–17:00 (only the outdoor zoo operates and the bird houses are not open).
  • Address: 216 Neungdong-ro (능동로), Gwangjin-gu (광진구), Seoul.
  • How to get there: leaving by Line 7 Children's Grand Park Station Exit 1 brings you to the main gate. Leaving by Line 5 Achasan Station Exit 4 brings you to the rear gate.
  • Scale: it opened on May 5, 1973. The area is 536,094.50㎡ and the perimeter is 4 km.
  • Botanical garden: operation is suspended from May 27, 2026 until the second half of 2027 (planned).
  • Timing: the zoo ends at 17:00, so see the zoo first and put the park stroll after it. The park is open until 22:00.

World Cup Park — Haneul Park (World Cup Park · Haneul Park)

The values below are the official "Parks of Seoul" guide (read 2026-11-25) of the Seoul Metropolitan Government. Kakao Map values are marked on each line. Seoul does not split the hours by park and publishes one timetable for World Cup Park as a whole.

  • Opening: 06:00 all year.
  • Closing: 21:00 in January–March, 22:00 in April–September, 21:00 in October–December.
  • Night entry: it is written that "entry is impossible outside opening hours".

  • Firefly Eco Center (반딧불이생태관): Tuesday–Saturday 10:00–17:00, closed Mondays and Sundays, free.
  • Parking: 150 won per 5 minutes for passenger cars.

② Parks That Are Always Open — 5 Spots

The parks themselves have no closing time. Even so, facilities inside them such as exhibition halls, observatories and botanical gardens have hours, and many close on Mondays.

Seoul Forest (Seoul Forest)

The values below are the official Seoul Forest guide (read 2026-11-25) on the Seoul Metropolitan Government "Parks of Seoul" site. Kakao Map values are marked on each line.

  • Visiting hours: the wording of the official guide is "open all year (some facilities closed on Mondays)". The Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25) is 24 hours, free entry.
  • Ecological Forest (deer enclosure · pedestrian bridge): 05:30–21:30. It is an ecological conservation area. Deer feed has not been sold since January 1, 2020.
  • Insect Garden (곤충식물원): March–October it is 10:00–17:00. November–February it is 10:00–16:00, with entry until 15:30. It is closed on Mondays.
  • Butterfly Garden (나비정원): it opens 10:00–17:00 in May–October and is closed on Mondays. From November to April it is in its closed season.
  • Visitor Center (방문자센터): 09:00–18:00. If you leave an ID card, they lend a wheelchair and a stroller free for up to 2 hours.
  • Fountains: the ground fountain (바닥분수) runs in May–September and is inspected on Mondays. After that it rests. The lake fountain runs in March–October at 12:00, 13:00 and 18:00.
  • Address: 273 Ttukseom-ro (뚝섬로), Seongdong-gu (성동구), Seoul. Inquiries 02-460-2905.
  • Scale: it opened on June 18, 2005. The area is 480,994㎡.
  • How to get there: Suin-Bundang Line Seoul Forest Station Exit 4, about 2 min walk (Seoul Metropolitan Government guide). Per Kakao Map directions, it is 12 min (712 m) from the same exit. From Line 2 Ttukseom Station Exit 8 it is about a 10 min walk.
  • Restrooms: 8 are open 24 hours.
  • Timing: the Ecological Forest with the deer enclosure closes at 21:30. The Insect Garden admits only until 15:30 in November–February, so put it first.

Namsan Park (Namsan Park)

The values below are the official "Parks of Seoul" guide (read 2026-11-25) of the Seoul Metropolitan Government.

  • Opening: open at all times.
  • Address: 231 Samil-daero (삼일대로), Jung-gu (중구), Seoul (Yejang-dong (예장동)). Inquiries 02-3783-5900.
  • Scale: the area is 2,902,602㎡ and the height is 270 m. It was designated a park on March 12, 1940 and opened on September 10, 1968.
  • Vehicle control: the Seoul Metropolitan Government notice says that ordinary passenger cars and taxis have been banned since May 1, 2005, and large buses (tour buses) since August 2021. Vehicles of people holding a disability welfare card (grades 1–3) are an exception.
  • By bus: Green Circulation Bus 01A and 01B go to Namsan Seoul Tower (남산서울타워) via Myeongdong, Chungmuro (충무로), Dongguk Univ. Station (동대입구역) and the Yejang Park transfer center (예장공원 환승센터). Take 01B at the stop behind Line 3 Dongguk Univ. Station Exit 6.

  • Namsan Oreumi (남산오르미): an inclined elevator near Myeongdong Station Exit 4. It is free. It does not go to the summit.
  • Paid facilities: the Namsan Cable Car and the observation deck of Namsan Seoul Tower are paid facilities.
  • Library side: take bus 402 from Seoul Station (서울역) Exit 9-1 and get off at Namsan Library (남산도서관).
  • Dulle-gil trail: it is 7.5 km. The north side is 3.4 km and the south side is 4.1 km.

  • Namsan Korean Forest Garden (남산 한국숲정원): the area of the former Namsan Outdoor Botanical Garden was newly renovated and opened on June 27, 2026. It is about 30,000㎡. From Line 6 Hangangjin Station (한강진역) Exit 2, take bus 402 or 405 and get off at Hyatt Hotel or the Korean Forest Garden entrance.
  • Timing: the park has no closing time. The ways to go up are fixed, so decide where to board first. For the bus, it is behind Dongguk Univ. Station Exit 6, and for walking, Myeongdong Station or Hoehyeon Station.

③ Hangang Parks — 2 Spots

Many people go to the Hangang parks for the fountains. The fountains have fixed operating periods and times, and they rest in winter.

The values below are the official guide (read 2026-11-25) on the Seoul Hangang Parks website. Kakao Map values are marked on each line.

  • Hours of use: the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25) is 24 hours, open all year.
  • How to get there: Line 5 Yeouinaru Station Exits 2·3, 1 min walk (Seoul Metropolitan Government guide). You come out at the center of the park. Per Kakao Map directions, it is 4 min (245 m) from Exit 3. The elevator is near Exit 2. From Line 9 National Assembly Station Exit 1, a 1 min walk brings you to the back of the National Assembly. It is 10 min from Line 9·Sillim Line Saetgang Station (샛강역) Exit 4, and 15–20 min from Line 5·Line 9 Yeouido Station (여의도역) Exit 1.
  • Hangang Bus (한강버스): the Yeouido pier connects directly to the park.
  • Mulbit Square Fountain (물빛광장분수): it is downstream of the south end of Mapodaegyo Bridge (마포대교). The water depth is 15 cm. The period of use in the Seoul Metropolitan Government notice is April–October. In April–June and September–October it runs for 30 minutes each at 12:00, 17:00, 18:00, 19:00 and 20:00. In July–August it runs on the hour from 12:00 to 21:00. It is closed on Mondays. On Fridays it starts at 18:00 after cleaning. It stops when the wind speed is 5 m/s or more or when rain is forecast (modified 2026-07-30).
  • Water fountain (beside Mulbit Stage (물빛무대)): in May, June, September and October it runs for 20 minutes each at 12:00, 17:00, 18:00, 19:00 and 20:00. In July–August it is 12:00–21:00. It runs only Tuesday–Thursday and Sunday (guide modified 2024-04-11).
  • Other facilities: there are three bicycle rental shops (Mapo branch, Wonhyodaegyo branch, Yeouinaru branch). There are the Bamseom Eco Experience Center (밤섬생태체험관) and a cruise boat pier.
  • Rules: fishing is banned throughout the park. Cooking is also banned. The fine for dumping trash is 100,000 won.
  • Timing: if the fountain is your goal, choose a non-Monday in April–October. In winter Mulbit Square is used as a small performance venue.

Banpo Hangang Park — Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (Banpo Hangang Park · Moonlight Rainbow Fountain)

The fountain values are the official guide (modified 2026-09-14, read 2026-11-25) on the Seoul Hangang Parks website. Kakao Map values are marked on each line.

  • Fountain location: Banpodaegyo Bridge (반포대교) (Jamsugyo (잠수교)) at Banpo Hangang Park, 647 Banpo-dong (반포동), Seocho-gu (서초구).
  • Operating period: from March 15 to October. The peak season is June–September. From November to March 14 it is not the operating period.
  • Show times (March 15–May, October): 12:00, 19:30, 20:00, 20:30, 21:00. 20 minutes each time.
  • Show times (June–September): 12:00, 19:30, 20:00, 20:30, 21:00, 21:30. 20 minutes each time.
  • Fare: free. You watch on site without a reservation.
  • Stop conditions: it stops when the wind speed is 7 m/s or more, when rain is forecast during operating hours, and when the Hangang turbidity is 15 NTU or more. Times can change when there is an event or a test run. Inquiries 02-3780-0578~9.
  • Record: the Hangang Parks guide says that on November 7, 2008 it was registered with Guinness as "the world's longest bridge fountain". Upstream and downstream of Banpodaegyo Bridge together make 1,140 m. The opening ceremony was held in April 2009.
  • Park address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25): 115-5 Banpo-dong, Seocho-gu, Seoul.
  • How to get there: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 17 min walk (1,025 m) from Express Bus Terminal Station Exit 8-1 to the point registered on Kakao Map (Banpo-dong 115-5). The fountain location is Banpodaegyo Bridge (Banpo-dong 647) per the Seoul Metropolitan Government guide.
  • Timing: the first evening show is 19:30. It is a 17 min walk only to the point registered on Kakao Map, and Banpodaegyo Bridge, where the fountain is, has a different address from that point, so leave the station before 19:00. If you go in winter, you cannot see the fountain.

For the Seonyudo Park Story Hall, the World Cup Park Noeul Campground (노을캠핑장), the Seoul Children's Grand Park restrooms and the winter use of Yeouido Mulbit Square, the notices say only "winter season" and do not say from which month.

⚙ 4 FAQs

  1. Which places cost money and which are free? — Among these 12 parks, the only place whose operating body lists an admission fee is the Themed Garden of Seoul Botanic Park. It is 5,000 won for adults, 3,000 won for teenagers and 2,000 won for children. The Maengkkongi Electric Cart at World Cup Park is 2,000 won one-way and 3,000 won round trip for regular fares. Seoul Children's Grand Park has free park entry, and Olympic Park is also free to use. The Open Forest, Lake Garden and Wetland Garden of Seoul Botanic Park, and viewing the Moonlight Rainbow Fountain, are also free. All are values seen on the operating bodies' websites on 2026-11-25.
  2. Are Namsan and Gwanaksan national parks? — No. The national park in Seoul is Bukhansan (북한산). Namsan and Gwanaksan (관악산) are not on Korea National Park Service's list of 24 national parks (official Korea National Park Service guide, read 2026-11-25).
  3. How much is Ttareungi (Seoul Bike) (따릉이), and how do foreigners pay? — Per the official Seoul Bike Ttareungi fare guide (read 2026-11-25), the one-day pass (일일권) is 1,000 won for the 1-hour pass, 2,000 won for the 2-hour pass and 3,000 won for the 3-hour pass. The one-day pass can be used from the first rental until the same time the next day, and the 1-, 2- and 3-hour passes are the basic time for one rental and ride. If you go over, 200 won is added per 5 minutes (official Seoul Bike guide). If you return within the time and rent again, there is no extra fee. If you do not return it for 4 hours after renting, it is treated as stolen. The list of payment methods includes "Discover Pass", which is only for foreigners.
  4. What is closed if I go on a Monday? — The Themed Garden of Seoul Botanic Park, the Insect Garden and Butterfly Garden of Seoul Forest, the observatory and exhibition hall of Dream Forest, the Story Hall of Seonyudo Park and the Firefly Eco Center of World Cup Park are closed on Mondays. The Mulbit Square Fountain in Yeouido also does not run on Mondays. The parks themselves are open on Mondays too. On Monday, choose Naksan Park, Seoul Iris Garden, Namsan Park and Olympic Park, where the purpose is a stroll rather than facilities.

🗺 Routes — Half a Day

The order is set to match the closing time of each place.

Half Day A — Winter Daytime, Seoul Botanic Park (Avoid Mondays)

  1. Line 9 Yangcheon Hyanggyo Station Exit 8 → Themed Garden (10 min walk). In winter, entry closes at 16:00.
  2. After leaving the Themed Garden, walk the Lake Garden and the Open Forest. It is free.
  3. Leave by Magongnaru Station Exits 3·4, which connect to the Open Forest.

Half Day B — Seoul Forest (Avoid Mondays)

  1. Suin-Bundang Line Seoul Forest Station Exit 4 → Seoul Forest. The Seoul Metropolitan Government guide says about 2 min walk, and per Kakao Map directions it is 12 min (712 m) from the same exit.
  2. Insect Garden. In November–February it is 10:00–16:00 with entry until 15:30.
  3. The deer enclosure and pedestrian bridge of the Ecological Forest. It closes at 21:30.

Half Day C — Naksan Park

  1. Line 4 Hyehwa Station Exit 2 → Naksan Park (10 min walk).
  2. Naksan Exhibition Hall. It is 09:00–17:00.
  3. Go down toward Line 4 Dongdaemun Station Exit 1 (21 min walk).

Half Day D — Namsan

  1. Take Green Circulation Bus 01B at the stop behind Line 3 Dongguk Univ. Station Exit 6.
  2. Ride 01B up toward Namsan Seoul Tower.
  3. If you choose to go up on foot, it is 10 min from Myeongdong Station Exits 3·4 toward the cable car, and 8 min from Hoehyeon Station Exit 4 to Baekbeom Square.

Half Day E — Haneul Park (Based on December–February)

  1. Line 6 World Cup Stadium Station Exit 1 → the Maengkkongi Electric Cart ticket office at Nanjicheon Park (14 min walk per Kakao Map directions, 857 m).
  2. Take the Route 1 cart up to the top of Haneul Park. The regular round trip is 3,000 won.
  3. In December–February Route 1 runs 10:00–18:00, so the last cart going up is 17:30. In October–November it is 09:00–19:00. The park closes at 21:00.

Evening — Hangang Fountains (Only When the Season Fits)

Spot When you can go Time to match
Yeouido Hangang Park Mulbit Square Fountain April–October, except Mondays In April–June and September–October the last show is at 20:00. Line 5 Yeouinaru Station Exits 2·3, 1 min walk (Seoul Metropolitan Government guide); per Kakao Map directions 4 min (245 m) from Exit 3
Banpo Hangang Park Moonlight Rainbow Fountain March 15–October In the evening, every 30 minutes from 19:30 to 21:00, with 21:30 added in June–September. 17 min walk (1,025 m, per Kakao Map directions) from Express Bus Terminal Station Exit 8-1 to the point registered on Kakao Map (Banpo-dong 115-5)
