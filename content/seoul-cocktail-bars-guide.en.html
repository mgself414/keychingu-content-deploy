🎯 How to Use This Guide

The old guide was written from 2025 rankings, and some of it was wrong or unsourced: Bar Cham "6th" (33rd in 2026), Le Chamber "50th" (88th in 2026), "listed every year since 2016" (the official profiles show only 25th in 2023 and 50th in 2025), Cobbler (코블러) in a "Yeonhui-dong (연희동) hanok" (the official address is Sajik-ro 12-gil (사직로12길), Jongno-gu), Pine & Co 52nd, Gong Gan 63rd, Charles H 96th (official ranks unconfirmed), H Bar "8 seats, 150,000 won course", and "cocktails 20,000–25,000 won, no tipping needed". This time we opened the Asia's 50 Best Bars 2026 official list (the50.com) and list only the rank and address of the 8 Seoul bars. The 4 bars ranked 51–100 come from the official news article summary, so a bar may be missing. Hours are not listed because the official profiles publish only weekdays.

🍸 By Neighborhood — What You'll Drink (Per Official Profiles)

  • 4 bars on Gangnam's Dosan-daero: Zest (제스트) is zero-waste — a "Jeju Garibaldi" with Hallabong (한라봉) and Gujwa carrot, and an Omegisul (오메기술) Gibson. Alice (앨리스) is a speakeasy, with signatures Hippity Hoppity (gin, ice wine, kiwi, camellia, dill) and Did I Melt?. Le Chamber (르 챔버) has an entrance you reach by pulling a bookcase, about 200 whiskies, and a hardcover-book menu — Time Out (secondary source) says daily 19:30–04:00, Exit 4 of Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역). M+MS Bar (M+MS 바): only the address is confirmed.
  • 3 hanok and hotel bars in Jongno: Bar Cham (바 참) is a Seochon (서촌) hanok bar with cocktails made from regional traditional liquors (such as a Deodeok (더덕) Negroni). Gong Gan (공간) is a hanok with a courtyard and cocktails drawn from childhood memories. Charles H (찰스 H) is in the basement of Four Seasons Seoul, with a Velvet Martini (Hallabong (한라봉)) and a Coconut Grove.
  • 1 bar in Hannam: Soko (소코) has a dry martini, a peanut-whiskey cocktail called Mr. Peanut, and a 1920s-style classic feel.
  • The old guide's Cricket, Elements, Pomme, Antidote, Notes, Kiosk, H Bar, Pine & Co, and Cobbler (코블러) are either not on this official list or we could not find a source for their claims, so we dropped them from the table.

🌙 Getting Around at Night and Rules (Official)

  • Alcohol cannot be sold to anyone under 19, and you may be asked for ID (Youth Protection Act (청소년보호법)). The subway runs about 05:30 to midnight (VisitSeoul). Late-night regular taxi fares are 140 won per 131 m from 23:00 to 02:00 and 120 won per 131 m from 22:00 to 23:00 and 02:00 to 04:00 (VisitSeoul fare table). Tourist hotline: 1330.
  • Reservations, dress code, and cover charge are not in the official profiles → check the bar's official channel.

🗺 Two Routes

  • A. Jongno hanok bars: Bar Cham in Seochon (서촌) → Gong Gan in Anguk (안국) → Charles H in Gwanghwamun (광화문) (it's a hotel, so easy to find late).
  • B. The 4 on Dosan-daero: Exit 4 of Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역) → Le Chamber, Zest, Alice, and M+MS Bar sit close together on Dosan-daero 55-gil and 49-gil (walk between them).

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Expecting the 2025 ranks — Bar Cham went from 6 to 33, Le Chamber from 50 to 88.

2. Looking for Cobbler in Yeonhui-dong — the official address is Sajik-ro 12-gil in Jongno (not on this year's list).

3. Assuming opening hours — the official profiles give weekdays only. Get hours from the bar's channel.

4. Assuming cocktail prices in won — the list gives only USD averages (Bar Cham 17 to Le Chamber 25).

5. Going without ID — you may be asked to prove you're 19 or older.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Which is Seoul's No. 1? — Zest, #2 in Asia in 2026 (also #2 in 2025).

2. Where are the hanok bars? — Bar Cham (Seochon) and Gong Gan (Anguk).

3. Where is the hotel bar? — Charles H (Four Seasons Seoul, #87).

4. Can I visit several in one neighborhood? — Yes, 4 bars on Gangnam's Dosan-daero 55-gil and 49-gil.

5. How much will it cost? — The list's averages are USD 17–25 (only for bars that show a price).

6. When do they open? — The official profiles give weekdays only. Only Le Chamber has a secondary-source 19:30–04:00.
