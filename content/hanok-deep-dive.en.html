⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Hanok = Bukchon only": In addition to Bukchon, Eunpyeong (#182 newly built), Ikseon-dong, Seochon, Namsan Hanok Village 5+ Hanok clusters + Palaces (Gyeongbokgung Palace, Changdeokgung Palace, Changgyeonggung Palace, Deoksugung Palace)·Hanok Cafe (#157·#152).

2. "Bukchon = Crowd Explosion": Bukchon is foreigner #1, but it is quiet after 9-10 o'clock + 18 o'clock on weekdays. Eunpyeong Hanok Village (2007) includes newly built Hanoks outside of Bukchon + OK accessibility to the countryside and downtown (Gupabal Station, Line 3).

3. "Hanok experience = Hanbok rental only": Full set of Hanok experience = Hanbok rental (Bukchon/Gyeongbokgung main gate) + Tea ceremony experience (Insa-dong) + Hanok stay (Rakgojae/Han Palace) + Hanok Korean food (Cheongryu #182) + Palace open at night (Gyeongbokgung spring/fall season only).

① Hanok Village (5)

1. Bukchon Hanok Village ★ Foreigner #1

  • Address: Area of Gyedong-gil and Gahoedong-gil, Jongno-gu (Anguk Station, Line 3)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Korean foreigner #1 Hanok cluster
  • Signature: Jongno authentic Hanok street + 8 scenic photo spot + Hanbok rental store
  • Details: #12 Bukchon·#18 Hanbok Experience

2. Eunpyeong Hanok Village (#182) ★ Newly built Hanok No. 1

  • Address: Jingwan-dong, Eunpyeong-gu (Gupabal Station, Line 3)
  • Foreigners ★: ◎ — 2007 newly built Korean hanok + adjacent to Jingwansa Temple
  • Signature: Combination of Hanok + Korean food (Cheongryu) + Jingwansa Temple
  • Details: #182 Eunpyeong·Bukhansan Mountain

3. Ikseondong Hanok Alley (#141)

4. Seochon Hanok Alley (#141)

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Bukchon Hanok photo spot: 8 scenic views of Bukchon + quiet on weekdays from 9 to 10 p.m.

2. Free admission to palace hanbok: Gyeongbokgung Palace, Changdeokgung Palace, Changgyeonggung Palace, and Deoksugung Palace are all free if you wear hanbok.

3. Hanok stay: Some stays in Rakgojae, Han Palace, and Eunpyeong Hanok Village.

4. Korea’s No. 1 suburban hanok: Jeonju (No. 1 outskirts), Andong, Gyeongju (UNESCO).

5. Hanok cafe cluster: Ikseon-dong, Bukchon, Seochon, Eunpyeong 4 clusters.

6. Hanok + Hanbok + Tea ceremony full set course: Bukchon → Hanbok rental → Insa-dong tea ceremony → Ikseon-dong cafe.
