Why Line 6 — The Ochre Line Connecting Global and Hipster

Line 6 (ochre) is Seoul's most 'global' and 'hip' line. The multinational culture of Itaewon, the expat vibe of Gyeongnidan-gil (경리단길) and Haebangchon, the hipster cafés of Mangnidan-gil and Sangsu — the neighborhoods that MZ Koreans and foreigners love are clustered on Line 6. The spectrum is wide, too, from a Michelin 3-star (Mosu (모수)) to a secondhand market (Dongmyo (동묘)).

This guide organizes Line 6's 8 key tourism stations station by station. It pairs Itaewon and Mangwon attractions (linked to existing deep dives) with restaurants pulled by nearest-neighbor mapping per station from accumulated data. This is the fifth installment of the subway line-by-line series.

3 Day Courses on Line 6 — Riding the Ochre Line

Course 1. Global Itaewon (Yongsan (용산))

  • Itaewon #1 (multinational food · shopping) → Noksapyeong #2 (Gyeongnidan & Haebangchon rooftops) → Hangangjin #3 (Leeum Museum · fine dining)
  • fit: Multinational · hip · fine dining. Cap the evening on a Haebangchon rooftop (#1236).

Course 2. Hipster Riverside (Mapo (마포))

  • Mangwon #4 (Mangnidan-gil · Mangwon Market) → Sangsu #5 (café street) → Hapjeong #6 (Mecenatpolis · Hangang)
  • fit: Local hip spots · cafés · Hangang. MZ vibe.

Course 3. Local & Vintage (Northeast Downtown)

  • Sindang #7 (Tteokbokki Town · Jungang Market) → Dongmyo #8 (secondhand market · flea market)
  • fit: Local markets · vintage shopping. Cheap · offbeat.

5 Line Tips — Getting 200% Out of Line 6

Tip 1. Line 6 Is the 'Global & Hip Line'

Multinational Itaewon, the expat vibe of Gyeongnidan & Haebangchon, hip cafés in Mangnidan & Sangsu. The most international and trendy line in Seoul.

Tip 2. The Itaewon Area Is Best in the Evening

Gyeongnidan & Haebangchon rooftops (#1236) and Itaewon pubs & bars run evening to night. Visit after sunset. Halal restaurants (#192) are in Itaewon, too.

Tip 3. The Culinary Spectrum Is Wide

From a Michelin 3-star (Mosu · Hangangjin) to Mangwon Market street food and Dongmyo gukbap. Whatever your budget and taste.

Tip 4. Hipster Neighborhoods Are Relaxed on Weekday Afternoons

Mangnidan-gil and Sangsu cafés get crowded on weekends. Weekday afternoons are quiet.

Tip 5. With a Single Transit Card

One T-money card covers Line 6, transfers (Lines 2 & 1), and buses. Itaewon to Mangwon on a single line.

FAQ 6 — Common Line 6 Questions from Foreigners

Q1. How is Line 6 different from the other lines?

  • Line 6: Global · hip (Itaewon · Gyeongnidan · Haebangchon · Mangnidan-gil · Sangsu). The most international · trendy in Seoul.

Q2. What do you do in Itaewon?

  • Multinational food · shopping · antique furniture · Islamic Street. In the evening, Gyeongnidan (#1236), Haebangchon rooftops, and Itaewon pubs. Halal restaurants (#192), too.

Q3. Which neighborhoods have hip cafés?

  • Mangwon (Mangnidan-gil) · Sangsu · Gyeongnidan. MZ local hip spots. Relaxed on weekday afternoons.

Q4. Museums & fine dining?

  • Hangangjin (Leeum Museum · Mosu Michelin 3-star · Hannam fine dining). Reservations required.

Q5. Is the restaurant information accurate?

  • It's based on a Naver Map crawl, so it's for reference. Re-checking a map app before visiting is essential.

Q6. Where's cheap and local?

  • Sindang (Tteokbokki Town) · Dongmyo (secondhand market · gukbap) · Mangwon Market. Cheap · offbeat · local.
