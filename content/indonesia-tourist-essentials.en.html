Indonesia to Korea Travel Guide: Visa vs. Temporary Group Visa-Free Entry (15 Days), 4 Halal Restaurant Categories, Prayer Rooms, Direct Flights (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: Indonesian ordinary passports are not covered by K-ETA and need a visa (K-ETA official site) · exception: groups of 3 or more gathered by a travel agency designated by the Ministry of Justice get 15 days visa-free from 2026-05-28 to 12-31 (Embassy of Korea in Indonesia notice) · Korea Tourism Organization's 4 categories of Muslim-friendly restaurants · Seoul Central Mosque and Incheon Airport prayer rooms · Garuda Indonesia serves Incheon (Incheon Airport). 🎯 How to Use This Guide

Climb about 500 m up the slope from Itaewon Station (이태원역) exit 3 and a white dome and minaret come into view. This guide lays out, using only values from official pages, the three forks travelers coming to Korea from Indonesia face before departure: a visa if you go alone or with family, temporary visa-free entry if you go with a travel agency group, and, once you arrive, halal meals and prayer. Anything not confirmed on an official page is stated as unconfirmed. This edition is for Indonesian Muslim travelers visiting Korea for the first time and their companions.

The old guide wrote "30 days visa-free (preferential for Indonesian passports)". That was wrong. In the K-ETA official eligible-country list and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs missions' table of visa-free entry countries, only Indonesian diplomatic and official passports (30 days) are visa-free; ordinary passport holders obtain a visa. The only road open right now is the temporary group visa-free entry of 2026. "450,000 per year", "2,000,000+ BTS Indonesia ARMY", "average spend of $1,150" and "30+ KMF halal-certified businesses" were removed because no official source could be found.

🛂 Entry Documents: Visa, Temporary Group Visa-Free Entry, e-Arrival

  • Ordinary passports need a visa: Indonesia is not on the K-ETA official site's list of eligible countries. In the Ministry of Foreign Affairs mission table (as of February 2024, revised 2026-01-08), Indonesia appears only in the diplomatic/official passport column, as "30 days/reciprocity" (Ministry of Foreign Affairs mission page)
  • Temporary group visa-free entry: tour groups of 3 or more recruited by a specialized travel agency in Indonesia (designated by the Ministry of Justice) may stay 15 days for tourism or transit (B-2) and travel to all areas of Korea. The period is 2026-05-28 to 2026-12-31. The domestic travel agency uploads the name list to HiKorea at least 24 hours before arrival, and anyone not on the list must obtain an individual visa (Ministry of Justice notice posted by the Embassy of Korea in Indonesia). It does not apply to individual travelers. The embassy announces suspensions of group-visa acceptance for some agencies from time to time, so check whether your chosen agency is on the designated list in the embassy notice (designated list of 2026-06-10). Whether it will be extended into 2027 has not been announced
  • Individual visa (C-3-9): the required documents on the embassy page are the original and copy of the passport, an application form with a photo, family relationship documents (Kartu Keluarga), a certificate of employment, and financial proof (annual tax payment proof or the last 3 months of bank statements). Processing takes at least 6 working days. However, that page was posted on 2015-10-01, so reconfirm fees and documents with the embassy before you submit
  • K-ETA: visa holders are exempt from applying for K-ETA (K-ETA official site). The temporary K-ETA exemption that applies to visa-free countries (January 1 to December 31, 2026, K-ETA notice) has nothing to do with Indonesian ordinary passports
  • e-Arrival Card: if you did not actually obtain a K-ETA, submit the e-Arrival Card. It is free at e-arrivalcard.go.kr from 3 days before arrival, and a paper form is also accepted (Ministry of Justice, #1289 e-Arrival Card manual)

🕌 Where to Pray

  • Seoul Central Mosque (서울중앙성원): Usadan-ro 10-gil 39 (우사단로10길 39), Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seoul, 477 m from Itaewon Station (이태원역) Line 6 exit 3. Opened on May 21, 1976, and open daily. Prayer times shift with sunrise, so check the monthly timetable on the mosque's website. Sleeveless tops, short skirts and shorts are not allowed, and you can borrow a skirt or hijab from the changing room next to the guard post. Non-Muslims may enter, but refrain from taking photos. Inquiries +82-2-793-6908 (VisitSeoul, revised 2026-09-17). For the surrounding streets see #144 Itaewon and Hannam guide
  • Incheon Airport prayer rooms: the Incheon Airport official site lists 2 prayer rooms in Terminal 1 (3rd floor near Gate 24, and the 4th-floor west transit lounge), open 24 hours, inquiries 1577-2600. The Incheon city tourism page says Terminal 2 also has prayer rooms in the general area on the 1st basement floor and in the duty-free area on the 4th floor (Incheon Tour, revised 2026-06-22)
  • The Korea Tourism Organization has distributed prayer mats and qibla compasses to hotels with multi-faith or prayer rooms (VisitKorea); ask your hotel in advance

✈ Flights

  • Garuda Indonesia (GA) is on Incheon Airport's official list of operating airlines (regular flights of the previous month) (Incheon Airport official site). Departure cities, days and times are not in that list
  • Direct flights from Bali or Surabaya other than Jakarta, and Korean airlines' routes to Indonesia, could not be confirmed on official pages; search by date on the airline's website

👥 Traveling Companions

Even within one family, entry methods split if passport types differ (diplomatic/official versus ordinary) (Ministry of Foreign Affairs mission page). Group visa-free entry applies only to people on the name list, so if one member of your party plans to move separately, that person obtains an individual visa. For meals, match the strictest person in the party and look for Halal Certified and Self-certified places first.

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Indonesian passports get 30 days visa-free": the 30-day visa-free entry is for diplomatic and official passports. Ordinary passports need a visa or the temporary group visa-free entry (Ministry of Foreign Affairs mission page, K-ETA official site)

2. "It's group visa-free, so three friends can just get together": only groups recruited by a specialized travel agency designated by the Ministry of Justice, with the name list uploaded in advance, qualify. The stay is 15 days (embassy notice)

3. "Muslim Friendly = halal restaurant": only some menu items are halal and alcohol may be sold. In the Korea Tourism Organization's definitions, "all food is halal" is stated for Self-certified; Halal Certified means certified by an accredited body such as the KMF (Korea Tourism Organization)

4. "I can enter the mosque in any clothes": sleeveless tops and shorts are not allowed, and you borrow a skirt or hijab from the changing room (VisitSeoul)
