🍢 Trendy K-Snack Types — Deep Dive

① Tanghulu (탕후루)

  • Signature: sugar-coated fruit (strawberry·grape·tangerine)·crunchy + juicy·explosive SNS hype
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★★ (visual·sweet·photo-worthy)
  • Tip: Myeongdong·Hongdae shops·street stalls·₩3,000~5,000·still popular past the peak

② Dalgona (달고나)

  • Signature: sugar + baking-soda caramel·shape-cutting·globally famous from Squid Game
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★★ (K-content·challenge·hands-on)
  • Tip: markets·traditional stalls·dalgona-making experience·₩2,000~4,000

③ Tornado potato (회오리감자)

  • Signature: whole potato spiral-cut and fried·on a skewer·seasoned·market star
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆ (visual·savory·filling)
  • Tip: Myeongdong·markets·₩3,000~5,000

④ Seed hotteok·bungeoppang·gyeranppang (winter street)

  • Signature: seed hotteok (Busan)·bungeoppang (red bean/custard)·gyeranppang·winter street stars
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★★ (warm·cheap·nostalgic)
  • Tip: winter stalls·₩1,000~3,000·have cash ready

⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "All bunsik is the same": Classic bunsik (tteokbokki·gimbap) and new viral snacks (tanghulu·croffle) are separate.

2. "Dalgona is just candy": It's a Squid Game shape-cutting challenge experience·market stalls.

3. "Streets are cash-only": Specialty shops take card·stalls need cash — mixed.

4. "The trend is over": Tanghulu·dalgona have settled in as tourist staples past the peak.

5. "Seed hotteok = Seoul": Seed hotteok originates from Busan International Market — check regional origins.

⚙ FAQ (6)

1. Where's tanghulu?: Myeongdong·Hongdae shops·street stalls·₩3,000~5,000.

2. Dalgona experience?: Markets·traditional stalls·dalgona-making·Squid Game shape-cutting.

3. Tornado potato?: Spiral-cut fried whole potato·Myeongdong·market skewer snack.

4. Is it hygienic?: Choose high-turnover stalls·specialty shops·sanitize hands.

5. Winter snacks?: Bungeoppang·seed hotteok·gyeranppang·roasted sweet potato = winter street stars.

6. Recommended personas: SNS·Gen-Z = tanghulu·croffle / experience = dalgona / nostalgia = bungeoppang·hotteok.

⚠ Medical·Safety·Dietary Disclaimer

Beware allergies to nuts (hotteok)·wheat·dairy·sugar. Tanghulu·dalgona = high-sugar·mind teeth. Watch street hygiene·hot oil. Emergency ☎ 1339·1330·119.
