The Ultimate Hongdae (홍대) Guide: 269 Spots — Curated for International Visitors

Subtitle: 60 Restaurants & Bars + 89 Cafés & Desserts + 60 Culture & Experiences + 60 Shopping & Photo Spots — Category-by-category curation so you never get lost in Hongdae (홍대), with 16 deep-dive picks

ISSUE 03 · #158 · 2026-05-14

🎯 5 Essential Tips for International Visitors

1. Yeonnam (연남) · Hapjeong (합정) · Mangwon (망원) = All Part of the Hongdae Route: 4 sub-areas packed within a 10–15 minute walk from Hongdae-ipgu Station (홍대입구역). Link them in one route for 5+ café and restaurant stops.

2. Weekday 14:00–17:00 (2–5 PM) Golden Hours: Weekends and late nights mean 1-hour+ queues. Foreign-friendly spots are quietest on weekday afternoons — best English service too.

3. Counter Seats & Reservations: Japanese-style compact spots (Chihiro (치히로), Uwa (우와), Yakitori Naruto (야키토리 나루토), etc.) are mostly counter seating. Weekday around 19:00 (7 PM) is ideal. English-capable spots use Naver Reservations or KakaoTalk Channel.

4. Foreign Cards Welcome at Most Spots: Hongdae is a top international tourist corridor, so foreign cards, Samsung Pay, and Kakao Pay (간편결제 — Korean mobile payment apps like KakaoPay and Samsung Pay, widely accepted instead of cash) work almost everywhere. For nofo (노포 — 30+ year traditional restaurants) and tiny shops, carry around ₩30,000 (~$22 / ~160 RMB / ~3,200 JPY) in cash just in case.

5. Instagram & Photo Routes: Hanok (한옥 — traditional Korean wooden house) alleyways, galleries, vintage interiors, and themed cafés are everywhere. For the best shots, aim for 11:00 AM or after 19:00 (7 PM) when crowds thin out.

⚠ 3 Mistakes International Visitors Often Make

1. Skipping Yeonnam (연남), Hapjeong (합정), and Mangwon (망원): About 70% of the best restaurants and cafés are in these sub-areas — all within walking distance of Hongdae-ipgu Station (홍대입구역).

2. Only Going to Chain Cafés: Skip Starbucks and Ediya (이디야) at least once and try a local specialty roaster like Anthracite (앤트러사이트) or Funnl (퍼넬).

3. Showing Up Unplanned on Holidays or Weekends: Popular spots will have 1–2 hour waits. Go on weekdays between 14:00–17:00 (2–5 PM), or right at opening time.

🎯 Deep-Dive Pick — Sanullim1992 (산울림1992)

The essence of Hongdae's Korean drinking house scene. As the name says, this place has held its ground since 1992, spiritually connected to the legendary Sanullim Small Theater (산울림 소극장) nearby. The menu stays true to traditional Korean cuisine — not fusion — with a solid lineup of jeontongjoo (전통주 — traditional Korean liquor) and yakju (약주 — refined Korean rice wine made from fermented grains, mild and aromatic). Prices run slightly higher than a neighborhood izakaya, but regulars always say "the food is genuinely so good I keep coming back." Perfect for when you want Korean food and a quiet drink without the noise.

🎯 Deep-Dive Pick — Jusindang (주신당)

A cocktail bar themed around the 12 zodiac animals and Korean shamanism (무속신앙). Walking in, you'll wonder if you're actually in a shop — it's that dim and mysterious. The signature concept: cocktails named after each of the 12 zodiac signs — Ja (쥐, rat), Chuk (소, ox), In (호랑이, tiger)… order based on your own birth year sign. One wall is covered in visitors' handwritten wish slips. Food is Korean-based fusion. Hours are 18:00–02:00 (6 PM–2 AM), making it better as a second or third stop of the night.

🎯 Deep-Dive Pick — Okdongsik (옥동식)

Opened in 2017 in Seogyodong (서교동), this pork gomtang (돼지곰탕) specialist is named directly after its chef, Ok Dongsik. The signature bowl uses a clear golden broth simmered from only the front and hind leg meat of Jirisan Berkshire K black pigs — completely unlike the milky-white broth of regular pork gukbap (돼지국밥). It's been on the Michelin Guide Bib Gourmand list every year since 2018, and in 2023 earned a rare spot on the New York Times "Best Food of the Year" list as a Korean dish. The counter-centric space seats roughly 10, and with a daily cap of 100 bowls, lunch often sells out before noon. If you're going, aim for right at opening or early lunch.

🎯 Deep-Dive Pick — Jeongdon (정돈)

Hongdae's defining tonkatsu queue. Rosu-katsu (ロースカツ, pork loin) and Hire-katsu (ヒレカツ, tenderloin) are the two mains, both keeping their juiciness all the way through despite their thickness. The small space means even weekday lunches have a wait, and on weekends expect 30–60 minutes. For anyone wanting textbook Japanese tonkatsu in Seoul.

🎯 Deep-Dive Pick — Gyodong Jip (교동집)

Tucked in a golmok (골목 — Korean narrow back-alley, the kind that connects neighborhoods) inside Hongdae, this dak-hanmari (닭한마리) and samgyetang (삼계탕) specialist serves a full course: rich chicken broth, vegetables, noodles, and dumplings all in sequence. The shop is small and turnover is slow, so waits can be long — but one bowl is so filling that a late lunch or early dinner here covers the whole day.

🎯 Deep-Dive Pick — Kim Jinhwan Confectionery (김진환 제과점)

Long beloved among bread enthusiasts around Sinchon (신촌) and Hongdae (홍대), this is a proper neighborhood bakery. The variety is overwhelming and sampling is encouraged, making a single lap around the shop genuinely joyful. The praise always centers on the same quality: not flashy, but chewy and clean-tasting — the kind of bread that doesn't get old. It builds regulars not on eye-catching design but on flavor you could eat every day. The shop posts fresh-bake times out front, so aim for those windows. Conveniently right next to Sanullim1992 (산울림1992) — great to combine into one route.

🎯 Deep-Dive Pick — Aoi Tori (아오이토리)

Named after "Blue Bird (青い鳥)" in Japanese, this bakery makes Japanese-style anpan (あんパン, red bean bread) and melon bread (メロンパン) by the book. It consistently ranks in the top 5 Hongdae bakeries on big data restaurant platforms like Dining Code (다이닝코드). The shop is small and queues form often — a weekday morning visit is the easiest option.

🎯 Deep-Dive Pick — Puha Cream Bun (푸하하크림빵)

The name says it all: a cream bun specialist. Milk cream base, with rotating seasonal flavors — Earl Grey, sweet corn, pistachio, and more. Sells out 30 minutes to an hour after opening on a regular basis, so checking the opening time before you go is non-negotiable.

🎯 Deep-Dive Pick — Café Comma (카페꼼마)

A books-and-coffee café operated by Munhakdongne (문학동네), one of Korea's major literary publishers. The name "Comma (꼼마)" means a pause — exactly what the space intends to be for readers. Curated books are placed throughout the café and can be freely picked up and read. Seats are plentiful and ceilings are high, making it ideal for laptop work too. Hongdae has no shortage of "books + coffee" cafés, but in terms of concept depth and operational know-how, Café Comma is the most consistently satisfying.
