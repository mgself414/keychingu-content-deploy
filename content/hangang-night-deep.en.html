Why is it night time on the Han River? — The #1 free night view hub for foreigners

The Han River is Korea's #1 river (41km out of 495km in Seoul) that runs through downtown Seoul. It is the #1 hub for foreign night enthusiasts with 11 Hangang parks + 24-hour free night access + global hot spot Hangang Bus/Seoul Dal. In particular, Banpo Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (Korea #1 Fountain, completely free)·Jamsugyo Bridge Festival (days and traffic-free walking every week from April to June)·Hangang Night View Tour (Banpo Moonlight Road·Yeoui Starlight Road·free)·Hangang Bus Foreigner Card Payment 35% = Explosion of global awareness.

Clear differentiation from Jongno 3-ga Pocha (#585 night hub) = "Free·Outdoor·Nature·Park·Romantic·Chicken and Beer at Han River" cluster. Suitable for foreign dates, MZ, family, and solo. 24h accessible + No. 1 for night enthusiasts.

Axis 1: Main night Hangang Park cluster (line 3)

#1. Banpo Hangang Park — Korea’s #1 No. 1 foreign night enthusiasts
