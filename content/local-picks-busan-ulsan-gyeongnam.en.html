Locals' Picks ① Busan, Ulsan & Gyeongnam — 15 Eateries Korean Viewers Named in the Comments, Naver-Verified

Subtitle: An axis untouched by Busan's gukbap, milmyeon & sashimi (#1320), the KTX 2-night-3-day trip (#1114), and the cafe pieces (#1321, #1324, #1381) — from a crowdsourced list of regional eateries Korean viewers recommended in YouTube comments, only the 15 in Busan, Ulsan & Gyeongnam were picked, their existence, address, and business type checked on Naver Local Search, with no evaluation. Busan 7 → Ulsan 1 → Changwon, Jinju, Yangsan, Changnyeong, Geochang 7, with station/terminal access and one foreign-visitor tip per city.

ISSUE 06 · #1382 · 2026-09-18 · Locals' Picks ①

🎯 Why 'Comment Recommendations' Is the Bar

KeyChinGu's regional food pieces have run on two signals so far — places where foreign reviews showed up first (#1381), and a city's signature menu (#1320). This corner is the third signal. Viewers left comments naming their neighborhood's eateries under a Korean YouTube channel's 'nationwide matjip' video, and the most systematically organized set of those comments, sorted by region into a table, covers 217 places. It's a list Koreans recommended to Koreans, so foreign reviews are almost nonexistent, and it's heavy on old-school, small-town places that never show up on Instagram.

KeyChinGu did exactly one thing — checked on Naver Local Search whether the name, address, and business type actually exist (2026-09-17). 192 of the 217 were confirmed, and for Busan, Ulsan & Gyeongnam it's 15 of 16. The one that couldn't be confirmed was dropped. There's no visit review, ranking, or star rating. Hours and prices aren't in this piece either — that's what Naver Place is for, on the day you go.

① Busan — 7 Places, Reachable by Subway and the Donghae Line

Busan's three signature dishes — dwaeji gukbap (돼지국밥), milmyeon, and hoe — are covered down to how to order them in #1320. These seven are the neighborhood eateries Korean viewers wrote in outside that list — two more gukbap places, and four dishes that rarely make tourist lists: dakbokkeumtang, dolsot bibimbap, mandu, and juk.

  • Yeonsan Dakdoritang (연산닭도리탕) · 236 Gwajeong-ro, Yeonje-gu, Busan (부산 연제구 과정로 236) · Korean · dakbokkeumtang (닭볶음탕) · Yeonsan Station (연산역), Lines 1 & 3, walking distance

Dakbokkeumtang (닭볶음탕), also called dakdoritang (닭도리탕), is a pot dish of a whole chicken braised in spicy broth with potatoes and onions. It's sold by the whole chicken, so it's built for 2–3 people.

  • Ujeong Dolsot Bibimbap (우정돌솥비빔밥) · 3 Dongcheon-ro 85beon-gil, Busanjin-gu, Busan (부산진구 동천로85번길 3) · Korean · bibimbap (비빔밥) · Seomyeon Station (서면역), Lines 1 & 2, walking distance toward Dongcheon-ro

Dolsot bibimbap (돌솥비빔밥) is bibimbap served in a hot stone pot — the scorched rice crust at the bottom is the whole point. It sits in the middle of Seomyeon, the easiest of the fifteen to slot into the #1114 route.

  • Uiryeong Sikdang (의령식당) · 15 Udong 1-ro 50beon-gil, Haeundae-gu, Busan (해운대구 우동1로50번길 15) · Korean · gukbap (국밥) · Haeundae Station (해운대역), Line 2, Exit 4, 3–4 min walk

A gukbap place tucked in the alley behind Haeundae Station along the old rail line, in Haeridan-gil (해리단길). Works as a meal before or after Haeundae Beach.

  • Hapcheon Gukbap (합천국밥) · 235 Yongho-ro, Nam-gu, Busan (남구 용호로 235) · Korean · gukbap · Bus 24 or 27 from Jigegol Station (지게골역), Line 2 — transferring at Gyeongseongdae∙Bukyeongdae Station (경성대·부경대역) also works

Yongho-dong is a residential area the subway doesn't reach directly. Pairing it with a day trip to Igidae or Oryukdo is the natural move.

  • Bokseong Mandu (복성만두) · 5-8 Daepyeong-ro 34beon-gil, Yeongdo-gu, Busan (영도구 대평로34번길 5-8) · Snacks · mandu · Yeongdo has no subway — bus to Yeongdo from Nampo Station (남포역), Line 1

A mandu place on the way to Yeongdo coffee (#1321) or Huinnyeoul Culture Village (흰여울마을). Mandu travels well packed to go, so it doubles as an on-the-move snack.

  • Songwon Sikdang (송원식당) · 23 Seongsan-gil, Seo-gu, Busan (서구 성산길 23) · Korean · Toseong Station (토성역), Line 1, Exit 6 → village bus (Saha 1-1, Seo-gu 2, or Seo-gu 2-2) from the Busan National University Hospital stop

A Korean restaurant in the residential streets right next to Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을). Same bus as the village tour.

  • Kkeutjip (끝집) Gijang Kkeutjip Ilgwang branch · 293 Ilgwang-ro, Ilgwang-eup, Gijang-gun, Busan (기장군 일광읍 일광로 293) · Korean · juk · Ilgwang Station (일광역), Donghae Line commuter rail, walking distance

A juk specialist. Ilgwang Beach (일광해수욕장) is a walk from the station, so it fits in the same half-day as the beach.

Foreign-visitor tip — At a Busan gukbap place, salted shrimp (새우젓), minced seasoning, and chives (jeongguji, 정구지) come out separately once you sit down. Adding them into the broth to season it yourself is the local way to eat it, and "not spicy" just means skipping the minced seasoning. Ordering phrases, milmyeon, and hoe are in #1320.

② Ulsan — 1 Place, Beside the Taehwagang

  • Daebatgol Dwaeji Gukbap (대밭골돼지국밥) · 229-2 Taehwagang-gukgajeongwon-gil, Jung-gu, Ulsan (울산 중구 태화강국가정원길 229-2) · Korean · gukbap · About 15 min by taxi from Taehwagang Station (태화강역), Donghae Line (bus is about 40 min, with sparse service) — from KTX Ulsan Station, the 5001 limousine bus takes about 49 min, considerably farther

Right beside Taehwagang National Garden (태화강국가정원, the Simni Bamboo Grove/십리대숲). Coming from Busan, Taehwagang Station on the Donghae Line electric rail sits closer to the garden than the KTX does, and anyone coming from Seoul by KTX should know upfront that Ulsan Station sits on the western edge of the city, away from downtown.

Foreign-visitor tip — Dwaeji gukbap is a shared breakfast dish across Busan and Ulsan. Many places open early, and in exchange, some close early in the afternoon once they run out of ingredients. Check hours on Naver Place.

③ Gyeongnam — 7 Places, Changwon, Jinju, Yangsan, Changnyeong & Geochang

The five Gyeongnam cities split into ones the KTX/SRT reaches (Changwon Masan, Changwon Jungang, Jinju) and ones reachable only by bus (Changnyeong Ibang, Geochang). That Yangsan is the end of Busan's Line 2 is also useful for this list.

  • Pyeongyang Naengmyeon (평양냉면) · 73 Yangdeokyet 2-gil, Masanhoewon-gu, Changwon-si (창원시 마산회원구 양덕옛2길 73) · Korean · naengmyeon · Around Masan Station (every KTX train stops) — bus or taxi from the station: check Naver Map

The name is the menu. Pyeongyang-style naengmyeon is known for its mild, understated broth, so you season it yourself at the table with vinegar and mustard.

  • Jeonangjang (전앙장) · 116 3·15-daero, Masanhappo-gu, Changwon-si (창원시 마산합포구 3·15대로 116) · Chinese · Chinese restaurant · Old downtown Masan — bus transfer from Masan Station (route: check Naver Map)

A Chinese restaurant in Masan's original old town. The Korean-Chinese staples — jjajangmyeon (짜장면), jjamppong (짬뽕), and tangsuyuk (탕수육) — are all available as single orders.

  • Butaok (부타옥) · 225 Yongji-ro, Seongsan-gu, Changwon-si (창원시 성산구 용지로 225) · Korean · Bus from Changwon Jungang Station (창원중앙역), KTX

A Korean restaurant in Changwon's newer district — Sangnam-dong (상남동) and Yongji-ro. Changwon Jungang Station serves KTX, ITX-Saemaeul, and Mugunghwa trains.

  • Jeil Sikdang (제일식당) · 37-8 Jungangsijang-gil, Jinju-si (진주시 중앙시장길 37-8) · Korean · City bus or taxi from Jinju Station (진주역), KTX/SRT (route: check Naver Map)

A restaurant inside Jinju Central Market (진주중앙시장). Jinju is known as a city of bibimbap and naengmyeon, and restaurants inside the market follow that same script.

  • Haeyangsan Gukbap (해양산국밥) · 10 Samseong 6-gil, Yangsan-si (양산시 삼성6길 10) · Korean · gukbap · Around Yangsan Station (양산역), the end of Busan's Line 2 — walking or bus from the station: check Naver Map

A Gyeongnam gukbap place reachable on the Busan subway. Ride Line 2 to the very end and you're in Yangsan.

  • Ibang Sikdang (이방식당) sugure gukbap · 1 Ogya-gil, Ibang-myeon, Changnyeong-gun (창녕군 이방면 옥야길 1) · Korean · gukbap · County bus or taxi from Changnyeong Intercity Bus Terminal (창녕 시외버스터미널) (in Changnyeong-eup/창녕읍) — whether it runs direct to Ibang-myeon: check

Sugure (수구레) refers to the meat just under a cow's hide. Sugure gukbap is a local dish tied to Changnyeong's Ibang market (이방장), and of everything on this list, it's the one that reads most 'local.' Same direction as Upo Wetland (우포늪).

  • Chuncheon Saemto Chamsut Dakgalbi (춘천샘토참숯닭갈비) · 17 Gongsudeul 4-gil, Geochang-eup, Geochang-gun (거창군 거창읍 공수들4길 17) · Korean · dakgalbi · Geochang Intercity Bus Terminal (거창 시외버스터미널) (mainly served from Daegu Seobu) — walking distance

Geochang runs on Daegu's commuting sphere, so buses run more often from Daegu than from Busan. Dakgalbi is chicken, cabbage, and rice cake stir-fried on a griddle, and it's a 2-person minimum order.

Foreign-visitor tip — More eup/myeon-level restaurants and market stalls take cards now, but cash and bank transfer are still the culture's default comfort zone. #1301 covers cash situations in general. Gukbap and naengmyeon can be ordered for one; dakbokkeumtang and dakgalbi default to 2 servings or more.

🚄 How to Fold This Into a Regional Route

  • Working it into the Busan 2-night-3-day trip (#1114) — Seomyeon (Ujeong Dolsot Bibimbap), Haeridan-gil (Uiryeong Sikdang), and Gijang Ilgwang (Kkeutjip) sit right at stations already on that route. Yeongdo (Bokseong Mandu) and Gamcheon (Songwon Sikdang) each need a bus after getting off at Nampo or Toseong Station, so plan them in half-day blocks.
  • Busan → Ulsan — Donghae Line electric rail to Taehwagang Station. Taehwagang National Garden and Daebatgol Dwaeji Gukbap in one trip.
  • Seoul → Jinju, Changwon — KTX/SRT to Jinju Station, KTX to Masan Station or Changwon Jungang Station. Jinju Central Market (Jeil Sikdang) still needs a ride into downtown from Jinju Station.
  • A Golden Route (#1372–1374) variant — Anyone coming down to Busan from Gyeongju and spending one day in Gyeongnam is realistically better off picking just Masan or just Jinju, not both.
  • Bus-only places — Changnyeong Ibang and Geochang. Since the routes run mainly out of Daegu Seobu Terminal (대구서부정류장), pairing them with the Daegu-Gyeongbuk piece (#1383) makes more sense.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Heading straight from KTX Ulsan Station to the Taehwagang garden — Ulsan Station sits on the western edge of downtown, about 49 minutes away by limousine bus. Coming from the Busan side, Donghae Line's Taehwagang Station is the right answer.

2. Showing up at a gukbap place in the evening — Gukbap places run on a breakfast-and-lunch rhythm, and some close early once they're out of ingredients. Check hours on Naver Place the same day.

3. Ordering dakbokkeumtang or dakgalbi solo — They're sold by the whole chicken or in 2-serving minimums. Solo, gukbap, naengmyeon, mandu, or juk is the better call.

⚙ FAQ 3

1. Who picked this list? — It's a table built from regional eateries Korean YouTube viewers named in the comments. KeyChinGu only confirmed that they exist, plus their address and business type — it didn't rank them.

2. Can I order without speaking Korean? — For gukbap, naengmyeon, or mandu, pointing at the menu works fine. "Igeo hana juseyo" (이거 하나 주세요, "one of these, please") is enough, and #1320 covers order phrasing in detail.

3. Can I make a reservation? — Most places of this type run on a walk-in, wait-in-line basis, no reservation. A group needs to call ahead, and that number is on Naver Place.
