⚠ 3 common mistakes Southeast Asian tourists make

1. "Korea = unfriendly to Muslims" — Hotel meals and getting lost in regular restaurants

The most common mistakes made by first-time Southeast Asian Muslim tourists (Indonesia, Malaysia, Brunei). Korea = Pork and alcohol are considered common in restaurants and only rely on hotel room service. In fact, there are 30+ Korean Muslim Federation (KMF) certified halal restaurants + many stores with prayer rooms in the Itaewon Islamic Masjid area + parts of Myeong-dong**. Some mosque-certified hotels in Myeongdong and Dongdaemun also operate. KMF English map + Halal friendly stores recommended before arrival.

2. "K-drama filming location = only in the city center" — Inside the outskirts of Gapyeong and Incheon

There is a pattern of K-drama enthusiast Southeast Asian tourists staying only in the city centers (Myeongdong, Hongdae, Gangnam) and not quenching their thirst for K-drama. In fact, K-drama signature filming locations such as Nami Island (Winter Sonata, Gapyeong ITX Youth 1h), Goblin Street (Incheon Chinatown 1h), and Strange Lawyer Woo Young-woo filming locations (Buam-dong, Jongno) are located in the outskirts. You can experience the full K-drama by combining a 2-day, 1-night or one-day suburban course with an urban course. K-drama exclusive package tour (operated by Korea Tourism Organization and KTO) is also recommended.

3. "Average stay of 15 nights = staying in one neighborhood" — time wasting pattern

Southeast Asian tourists stay longer than other segments (2 nights in Japan, 4 nights in China), with an average of 15 nights. Repeated visit pattern to Myeongdong and Hongdae, based on one hotel. In reality, 15 nights allows for the full experience of 1 week in the city center + 1 week in the outskirts (Busan, Jeonju, Gyeongju, Jeju). K-drama filming location + Halal restaurant + Hanbok experience + K-pop goods + Han River + 1 week outskirt course is the answer to the full package in the Southeast Asia segment.

① Muslim·Halal (3)

1. Itaewon Islamic Mosque + KMF certified halal restaurant cluster (Itaewon Islamic Mosque & Halal)

  • Signature: Korea #1 Muslim Infrastructure. Seoul Central Mosque (1976) + 30+ KMF certified restaurants nearby. Mania of India, Malay, Indonesia, Turkey, and the Middle East.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — English, Indonesian, Malay, Arabic menu, clear KMF label
  • Recommended menu: Mughal (Indian curry, tandoori), Salam (Malay, Indonesian nasi goreng, rendang), Islamabad (Pakistani biryani), Turkish kebab
  • Tip: 5-10 minutes walk from Exit 3 of Itaewon Station (Line 6) — Islamic Masjid cluster. Friday 13:00 Muslim service — Restaurant opens 12-14:00 peak. Enter the restaurant after checking the KMF certification label.

2. Myeongdong Halal Store + Prayer Room (Myeongdong Halal & Prayer)

  • Signature: Part of the Myeongdong Core Halal cluster. Interior/Malay menu + hotel prayer room operation.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English and Japanese language support, partial KMF certification
  • Recommended category: Myeongdong KMF certified restaurant (Indonesian/Malaysian restaurants + some Korean Muslim-friendly), Myeongdong hotel prayer room
  • Tip: Myeongdong Station (Line 4) area — Check the KMF certification label clearly. Check before booking whether the hotel prayer room is open (Lotte, Westin Chosun, some 4-star hotels, etc.).

3. Dongdaemun Hotel + Mosque Certification (Dongdaemun Muslim-Friendly Hotels)

  • Signature: Dongdaemun 24h shopping cluster + part of Muslim-friendly hotel. Dawn Wholesale + Muslim Worship Infrastructure.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Prayer rooms and halal room service in some hotels
  • Recommended Category: Dongdaemun KMF certified restaurant + Muslim-friendly hotel (near Migliore, DDP area)
  • Tip: Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (Lines 2, 4, 5) — 24h early morning wholesale + double use of Muslim infrastructure. Check KMF·HalalTrip·CrescentRating certification.

② K-drama·Tradition (3)

4. Bukchon Hanbok Experience + Instagram (Bukchon Hanok & Hanbok)

  • Signature: Southeast Asian Instagram/TikTok signature. Hanboknam, Oya, etc. respond in English, Indonesian, and Thai. No. 1 to quench the thirst of K-drama enthusiasts.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Rich in Southeast Asian mania reviews
  • Recommended Category: Hanbok rental (KRW 15,000-30,000, 1-4 hours), Gyeongbokgung Palace admission fee exemption, Bukchon 8 Views Instagram
  • Tip: 7 minutes walk from Anguk Station (Line 3). Recommended entry between 9am and 12pm — Hanbok line after 1pm. Full experience of Bukchon, Samcheong-dong, Insa-dong, and Gyeongbokgung 4-axle bundle.

5. K-drama filming locations — Nami Island, Goblin Street, Strange Lawyer Woo Young-woo (K-drama Filming Spots)

  • Signature: Quenches the thirst of Southeast Asian K-drama enthusiasts. Nami Island (Winter Sonata), Incheon Chinatown (Goblin), Buam-dong (Weird Lawyer Woo Young-woo), etc.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — English guide + KTO K-drama tour package
  • Recommended course: Day trip — Nami Island (Gapyeong ITX Cheongchun 1h, walk + shuttle) or Incheon Chinatown (Incheon Line 1, Airport Railroad 1h)
  • Tip: KTO (Korea Tourism Organization) English K-drama tour + Take advantage of the Hanjin Tour/Hana Tour limited package. For enthusiasts, Nami Island + Gangneung (the last of Goblin) 1 night course.

6. Hangang River Chicken & Bike

  • Signature: No. 1 on MZ Instagram in Southeast Asia. Chicken and beer, mats, and bicycles at Yeouido, Ttukseom, and Banpo Hangang Park.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — English menu, Ttareungi English app
  • Recommended menu: Chicken delivery (30-minute delivery from BHC, BBQ, Kyochon, Goobne, etc.) + Beer (convenience store) + Mat (convenience store 5,000 won)
  • Tip: Yeouido Hangang (Yeouinaru Station Line 5) or Ttukseom (Lines 2 and 7) — Instagram signature scenery. Spring and fall peak between 18:00 and 22:00. Southeast Asian Muslims can substitute beer (drink + non-alcoholic chicken).

③ Shopping·K-beauty (3)

7. Olive Young Myeongdong Town

  • Signature: Foreign sales 95% — No. 1 in the country. 2024 Foreign sales: 1 trillion won. No. 1 K-beauty in Southeast Asia.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — Information in English, Chinese, Indonesian, and Thai
  • Recommended category: Mask pack box (Laneige·Mediheal·Innisfree), skin care, toner, sunblock
  • Tip: 5-minute walk from Myeongdong Station (Line 4) Exit 9. Peak: 17:00 - 21:00 PM. No. 1 K-beauty store among Indonesian/Malay Southeast Asian enthusiasts.

8. Myeongdong Pharmacy District

  • Signature: +19 new pharmacies in 6 months. Foreign sales +142%. Functional skin care, ointment, and nutritional supplements. Partial response from Indonesian and Malay pharmacists.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Some information in English and Japanese
  • Recommended Category: Functional ointment (atopy/acne), Centella/Cica skin care, herbal nutritional supplements
  • Tip: Myeongdong exit 6 pharmacy cluster. Muslim enthusiasts recommend alcohol free skin care + animal ingredient free label check.

9. Lotte Mart Seoul Station

  • Signature: Foreign sales 40%+. Carrier shopping + overseas shipping + currency exchange + 3-hour storage. Southeast Asian ramen, snacks, and beauty signature shopping.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — English/Japanese guidance, foreigner corner
  • Recommended category: Korean ramen (Nongshim, Samyang + some halal certified), snacks (Pepero, Mongshell, etc. — Check halal certification label), seaweed, red pepper paste, banana-flavored milk
  • Tip: 3 minutes walk from Seoul Station (Lines 1, 4, Airport Railroad). Check Halal certified food label (some Nongshim, some Samyang) before purchasing. 3 hour course before going to the airport.
