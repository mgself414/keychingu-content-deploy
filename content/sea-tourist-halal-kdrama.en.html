🛂 Entry Documents: K-ETA and the e-Arrival Card

  • Visa-free countries (Brunei, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand): the rule is K-ETA approval before departure. The fee is ₩10,000 (~$7.50) (K-ETA official site)
  • Temporary K-ETA exemption: it runs from January 1 to December 31, 2026 (K-ETA official notice). The notice does not list the eligible countries, so we could not confirm whether each country is included. Scan your passport on the k-eta.go.kr application screen and it will tell you whether you are "exempt"
  • Who does not need K-ETA: people aged 17 or under or 65 or over on the day of entry, visa holders, holders of diplomatic or official passports, transit passengers and others (K-ETA official site)
  • Indonesia and the Philippines: ordinary passport holders obtain a visa. For Indonesia, only groups of 3 or more recruited by a specialized travel agency designated by the Ministry of Justice get 15-day visa-free entry, from 2026-05-28 to 12-31 (embassy notice). Documents and processing times are compiled in #468 and #466
  • e-Arrival Card: if you did not actually obtain a K-ETA, submit the e-Arrival Card. It is free at e-arrivalcard.go.kr from 3 days before arrival, and a paper form is also accepted (Ministry of Justice, #1289 e-Arrival Card manual)

🥗 Dietary Needs: 4 Categories of Halal Restaurants

Category Meaning (Korea Tourism Organization) Alcohol
Halal Certified Certified by a recognized body such as the KMF (Korea Muslim Federation, 한국이슬람교중앙회) —
Self-certified A Muslim owner declares the restaurant halal on their own; every menu item is halal —
Muslim Friendly Only some menu items are halal May be sold
Pork free No halal menu, but no pork is used May be sold
  • If your party's standards differ, match the strictest person and look for Halal Certified and Self-certified places first. Dishes with pork may be served alongside at Muslim Friendly hotel restaurants and buffets (VisitKorea)
  • VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (Usadan-ro 10-gil 15 (우사단로10길 15), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00 to 21:00) as one of the few restaurants the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF) recognizes as halal. Certification can change, so check before you visit.

🕌 Where to Pray

  • Seoul Central Mosque (서울중앙성원): Usadan-ro 10-gil 39 (우사단로10길 39), Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seoul, 477 m from Itaewon Station (이태원역) Line 6 exit 3. Opened on May 21, 1976, and open daily. Prayer times shift with sunrise, so check the monthly timetable on the mosque's website. Sleeveless tops, short skirts and shorts are not allowed, and you can borrow a skirt or hijab from the changing room next to the guard post. Inquiries +82-2-793-6908 (VisitSeoul, revised 2026-09-17)
  • Incheon Airport prayer rooms: the airport's official site lists 2 rooms in Terminal 1 (3rd floor near Gate 24, and the 4th-floor west transit lounge), open 24 hours, inquiries 1577-2600. The Incheon city tourism page says Terminal 2 also has prayer rooms in the general area on the 1st basement floor and in the duty-free area on the 4th floor (Incheon Tour, revised 2026-06-22)
  • The Korea Tourism Organization has distributed prayer mats and qibla compasses to hotels with prayer rooms (VisitKorea); ask your hotel in advance

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "All of Southeast Asia is visa-free": Indonesian and Philippine ordinary passports are not on the K-ETA eligible-country list. You obtain a visa (K-ETA official site)

2. "Visa-free means 90 days for everyone": Brunei is 30 days (K-ETA official list)

3. "My country is exempt, so I submit nothing": if you did not obtain a K-ETA, you must still submit the e-Arrival Card (Ministry of Justice)

4. "Muslim Friendly = halal restaurant": only some menu items are halal and alcohol may be sold (Korea Tourism Organization)
