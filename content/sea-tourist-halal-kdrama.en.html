⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Southeast Asian Tourists

1. "Korea = Muslim-Unfriendly" — Struggling with hotel meals and general restaurants

The most common mistake for first-time Southeast Asian Muslim tourists (Indonesia, Malaysia, Brunei). They perceive Korea as having pork and alcohol commonly in general restaurants and rely solely on hotel room service. In reality, the area around Itaewon Islamic Mosque (이태원 이슬람 성원) + parts of Myeongdong (명동) have 30+ KMF (Korea Muslim Federation) certified halal restaurants (Korea Muslim Federation (KMF) certification is a standard for halal food and services in Korea) + many establishments operating prayer rooms. Some hotels in Myeongdong (명동) and Dongdaemun (동대문) also operate with mosque certification. It's recommended to bookmark KMF English maps + halal-friendly establishments before arrival.

2. "K-drama Filming Locations = Only in the City" — Not visiting Gapyeong or Incheon outskirts

K-drama enthusiast Southeast Asian tourists often stay only in the city center (Myeongdong (명동), Hongdae (홍대), Gangnam (강남)), leaving their K-drama cravings unfulfilled. In reality, signature K-drama filming locations are in the outskirts, such as Nami Island (남이섬) ('Winter Sonata', Gapyeong (가평) ITX Cheongchun 1h), Goblin Street (도깨비 거리) (Incheon Chinatown (인천 차이나타운) 1h), and Extraordinary Attorney Woo filming locations (Buam-dong (부암동), Jongno (종로)). Combining a 1-night, 2-day or day trip to the outskirts with a city course allows for a full K-drama experience. It's also recommended to utilize K-drama exclusive package tours (operated by Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사, KTO)).

3. "Average 15-Night Stay = Staying in One Neighborhood" — Wasting Time Pattern

Southeast Asian tourists have an average stay of 15 nights, a longer duration compared to other segments (Japan 2 nights, China 4 nights). A pattern of repeatedly visiting only Myeongdong (명동) and Hongdae (홍대) based out of one hotel. In reality, 15 nights allow for a full experience of 1 week in the city + 1 week in the outskirts (Busan (부산), Jeonju (전주), Gyeongju (경주), Jeju (제주)). A 1-week course combining K-drama filming locations, halal restaurants, Hanbok (한복, traditional Korean clothing) experience, K-pop goods, Hangang (한강, Han River), and the outskirts is the perfect full package for the Southeast Asian segment.
