Busan KTX 3 days 2 nights full guide for foreigners

Korea’s second city + beach + Jagalchi Fish Market — Seoul KTX 2.5 hours → Haeundae, Gwangalli, Gamcheon Culture Village, Taejongdae 3 days and 2 nights

Busan is the second most popular KTX destination for foreign long-term travelers after Gangneung, Jeonju, and Gunsan. Seoul KTX 2 hours and 30 minutes → Deep dive for 3 days and 2 nights to Haeundae Beach, Jagalchi Fish Market, Gamcheon Culture Village, and Taejongdae. This guide is a standard route suitable for foreigners + 12 stores + expanded ferry to Busan, Japan.

1. How to get to Busan

Options Take price Recommended
KTX Gyeongbu Line (Seoul Station) 2h 30m ₩59,800 ★★★ Foreigner default
KTX-Sancheon / SRT 2h 35m ₩52,000~ SRT slightly cheaper
Intercity Bus (Gangnam) 4h 30m ₩30,000 low budget
Aviation (Gimpo·Incheon) 1h ₩60,000~120,000 Time is short

Reservation: Korail English (https://www.letskorail.com/) — required 1-2 weeks before weekends

2. Standard 2 nights and 3 days itinerary

Day 1 (Arrival + Haeundae)

  • 07:00 Seoul Station KTX
  • 09:30 Arrival at Busan Station
  • 10:30 Hotel luggage storage (Haeundae or Gwangalli)
  • 11:30 Haeundae Beach + Haeundae Market
  • 13:00 Haeundae lunch (milmyeon or raw fish)
  • 15:00 Dongbaek Island + Nurimaru APEC House
  • 17:00 Haeundae Blue Line Park (Sky Capsule/Coastal Train)
  • 19:00 Haeundae dinner (seafood dining)
  • 21:00 Haeundae Beach night view + Gwangan Bridge view

Day 2 (Gamcheon + Jagalchi + Gwangalli)

  • 09:00 Hotel breakfast
  • 10:00 Gamcheon Culture Village (color village resembling Santorini + Machu Picchu)
  • 12:30 Jagalchi Market Lunch (raw fish, raw fish, seafood)
  • 14:00 BIFF Square + Gukje Market
  • 16:00 Yongdusan Park + Busan Tower
  • 18:00 Arrival at Gwangalli Beach
  • 19:00 Gwangalli dinner (beach dining·Milmyeon)
  • 21:00 Gwangan Bridge night view + cafe/bar

Day 3 (Taejongdae + Return)

  • 09:00 Hotel check-out
  • 10:00 Taejongdae (Yeongdo-gu, nature, lighthouse)
  • 13:00 Yeongdo/Songdo lunch (seafood)
  • 15:00 Arrival at Busan Station
  • 15:30 KTX bound for Seoul
  • 18:00 Arrival at Seoul Station

3. 12 stores and experiences suitable for foreigners

Haeundae area

1. Haeundae Beach — Busan’s No. 1 beach, frequented by foreigners

2. Blue Line Park Sky Capsule — Haeundae ~ Songjeong Coast (Foreigner SNS signature)

3. Dongbaek Island + Nurimaru — APEC 2005 venue, free walking

4. Haeundae Market — Raw fish and food (partial information in English)

Gamcheon, Jagalchi, Gukje Market area

5. Gamcheon Culture Village — Busan #1 Instagram Signature (Resembling Santorini, English instructions)

6. Jagalchi Market — Korea’s largest fish market (raw fish on the 1st floor, dining on the 2nd floor)

7. BIFF Square — Home of the Busan International Film Festival + street food

8. International Market — Traditional market formed after the Korean War

9. Yongdusan Park·Busan Tower — City night view ★

Gwangalli·Taejongdae area

10. Gwangalli Beach — Gwangan Bridge night view ★

11. Taejongdae — Yeongdo, nature, lighthouse, heart photo zone

12. Songdo Cable Car — Songdo Beach ↔ Songdo Skywalk

4. Busan signature food (fit for foreigners)

Busan only food

  • Milmyeon — Busan version of Pyongyang naengmyeon (transformed in Busan by Pyongyang people during the Korean War)
  • Pork Gukbap — Busan Signature Gukbap
  • Seed Hotteok — Busan BIFF Square Signature (Hotteok + Nuts)
  • Busan fish cake — Bupyeong Kkangtong Market cluster

Seafood

  • Jagalchi raw fish — 1st layer sashimi + 2nd layer processing
  • Mulhoe — Spicy raw fish soup
  • Spring eel — Jagalchi·Haeundae

Recommended stores

  • Milmyeon Long-standing Store: Gaya Milmyeon, Naeho Naengmyeon
  • Pork Gukbap: Hapcheon First Class Pork Gukbap (Seomyeon)
  • Seed Hotteok: BIFF Square Stall

5. Foreign fit tips

English language availability

  • Top: Haeundae (English signage, frequented by foreigners)·Gamcheon Culture Village (English information)
  • Middle class: Jagalchi Market 1st floor, BIFF Square (picture menu)
  • Local: Mil-myeon No-Store·Gukje Market (translation app)

Budget (3 days and 2 nights per person)

  • KTX round trip: ₩119,600
  • 2 nights accommodation (hotel): ₩140,000~400,000
  • 7 meals: ₩100,000~200,000
  • Entrance fee/experience (Blue Line, Taejongdae, etc.): ₩30,000~60,000
  • City transportation (subway, taxi): ₩40,000
  • Total average ₩430,000~820,000

By persona

  • American Persona: Haeundae + Gamcheon + Jagalchi + Gwangalli night view
  • European Persona: Gamcheon Culture Village + Yeongdo·Songdo + Busan Gallery·Design
  • foodie: Jagalchi (raw fish) + wheat noodles + pork soup + seed hotteok 4-hour gourmet hop
  • K-drama fan: Haeundae·Gwangalli (filming location for many dramas)
  • Family fit: Haeundae Beach + Blue Line Park + Nurimaru

By season

  • Spring (April-May): Haeundae Cherry Blossoms + Gwangalli Walk
  • Summer (June-August): Haeundae·Gwangalli Beach ★ peak season
  • Fall (October-November): Gamcheon + Taejongdae fall foliage
  • Winter (December-February): Haeundae (fewer tourists, cheaper hotels)

Accommodation Recommendations

  • Luxury: Signiel Busan (Haeundae), Paradise Hotel (Haeundae)
  • Mid-Range: Haeundae Grand Hotel, Gwangalli Hotel cluster
  • Guesthouse: Haeundae·Seomyeon (₩40,000~80,000/night)
