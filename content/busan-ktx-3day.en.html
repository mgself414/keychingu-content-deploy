Busan KTX 3-Day Full Guide for Foreign Travelers

> Korea's second-largest city + beaches + Jagalchi Fish Market — 2.5 hours by KTX from Seoul → Haeundae, Gwangalli, Gamcheon Culture Village, Taejongdae for 3 days and 2 nights

Busan (부산) is a popular KTX destination for long-term foreign travelers, following Gangneung (강릉), Jeonju (전주), and Gunsan (군산). Take a 2.5-hour KTX ride from Seoul (서울) for a 3-day, 2-night deep dive into Haeundae Beach (해운대 해변), Jagalchi Fish Market (자갈치 수산시장), Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을), and Taejongdae (태종대). This guide offers a standard itinerary optimized for foreign travelers, featuring 12 recommended spots, plus an optional extension for Japan-Busan ferry travel.

1. How to Get to Busan

Option Duration Price Recommendation
KTX Gyeongbu Line (경부선) (Seoul Station (서울역)) 2h 30m ₩59,800 (~$44 / ~320 RMB / ~6,000 JPY) ★★★ Foreign traveler default
KTX-Sancheon (산천) / SRT 2h 35m ₩52,000~ SRT slightly cheaper
Intercity Bus (시외버스) (Gangnam (강남)) 4h 30m ₩30,000 Budget-friendly
Flight (Gimpo (김포)·Incheon (인천)) 1h ₩60,000~120,000 Shorter travel time

Booking: Korail English (https://www.letskorail.com/) — Essential to book 1-2 weeks in advance for weekends.

2. Standard 3-Day, 2-Night Itinerary

Day 1 (Arrival + Haeundae)

  • 07:00 (7 AM) KTX from Seoul Station (서울역)
  • 09:30 (9:30 AM) Arrive at Busan Station (부산역)
  • 10:30 (10:30 AM) Hotel luggage storage (Haeundae (해운대) or Gwangalli (광안리))
  • 11:30 (11:30 AM) Haeundae Beach (해운대 해변) + Haeundae Market (해운대 시장)
  • 13:00 (1 PM) Lunch in Haeundae (해운대) (Milmyeon (밀면) or Hoe (회))
  • 15:00 (3 PM) Dongbaekseom Island (동백섬) + Nurimaru APEC House (누리마루 APEC 하우스)
  • 17:00 (5 PM) Haeundae Blueline Park (해운대 블루라인 파크) (Sky Capsule (스카이캡슐)·Coastal Train (해안 열차))
  • 19:00 (7 PM) Dinner in Haeundae (해운대) (Seafood Dining)
  • 21:00 (9 PM) Haeundae Beach (해운대 해변) night view + Gwangandaegyo Bridge (광안대교) view

Day 2 (Gamcheon + Jagalchi + Gwangalli)

  • 09:00 (9 AM) Hotel breakfast
  • 10:00 (10 AM) Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을) (a colorful village resembling Santorini and Machu Picchu)
  • 12:30 (12:30 PM) Lunch at Jagalchi Market (자갈치 시장) (Hoe (회)·Mulhoe (물회)·Seafood)
  • 14:00 (2 PM) BIFF Square (BIFF 광장) + Gukje Market (국제시장)
  • 16:00 (4 PM) Yongdusan Park (용두산공원) + Busan Tower (부산타워)
  • 18:00 (6 PM) Arrive at Gwangalli Beach (광안리 해변)
  • 19:00 (7 PM) Dinner in Gwangalli (광안리) (Beachside Dining·Milmyeon (밀면))
  • 21:00 (9 PM) Gwangandaegyo Bridge (광안대교) night view + Cafe/Bar

Day 3 (Taejongdae + Return)

  • 09:00 (9 AM) Hotel checkout
  • 10:00 (10 AM) Taejongdae (태종대) (Yeongdo-gu (영도구)·nature·lighthouse)
  • 13:00 (1 PM) Lunch in Yeongdo (영도)·Songdo (송도) (Seafood)
  • 15:00 (3 PM) Arrive at Busan Station (부산역)
  • 15:30 (3:30 PM) KTX to Seoul (서울)
  • 18:00 (6 PM) Arrive at Seoul Station (서울역)

3. 12 Foreign Traveler-Friendly Spots & Experiences

Haeundae (해운대) Area

1. Haeundae Beach (해운대 해변) — Busan's (부산) top beach, a favorite among foreign tourists.

2. Blueline Park Sky Capsule (블루라인 파크 스카이캡슐) — Coastal ride from Haeundae (해운대) to Songjeong (송정) (an SNS signature for foreign travelers).

3. Dongbaekseom Island (동백섬) + Nurimaru APEC House (누리마루) — Venue of APEC 2005, free for a scenic walk.

4. Haeundae Market (해운대 시장) — Raw fish (회) and street food (some English signage available).

Gamcheon (감천)·Jagalchi (자갈치)·Gukje Market (국제시장) Area

5. Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을) — Busan's (부산) #1 Instagram signature spot (resembles Santorini, English guides available).

6. Jagalchi Market (자갈치 시장) — Korea's largest fish market (1F for raw fish (회), 2F for dining).

7. BIFF Square (BIFF 광장) — Home of the Busan International Film Festival (부산국제영화제) + street food stalls.

8. Gukje Market (국제시장) — A traditional market formed after the Korean War (6.25 전쟁).

9. Yongdusan Park (용두산공원)·Busan Tower (부산타워) — Great for city night views ★.

Gwangalli (광안리)·Taejongdae (태종대) Area

10. Gwangalli Beach (광안리 해변) — Famous for the Gwangandaegyo Bridge (광안대교) night view ★.

11. Taejongdae (태종대) — Located in Yeongdo (영도), known for its nature, lighthouse, and heart photo zone.

12. Songdo Cable Car (송도 케이블카) — Connects Songdo Beach (송도해변) ↔ Songdo Skywalk (송도 스카이워크).

4. Busan's Signature Dishes (Foreign Traveler-Friendly)

Busan (부산)-Only Food

  • Milmyeon (밀면) — Cold wheat noodles, Busan's (부산) version of Pyeongyang Naengmyeon (평양냉면), adapted by Pyeongyang (평양) refugees in Busan (부산) during the Korean War (한국전쟁).
  • Dwaeji Gukbap (돼지국밥) — Pork soup with rice, Busan's (부산) signature gukbap (국밥).
  • Ssiat Hotteok (씨앗호떡) — Sweet pancake with seeds, a signature snack at Busan's (부산) BIFF Square (BIFF 광장) (hotteok (호떡) + nuts).
  • Busan Eomuk (부산 어묵) — Fish cakes, a cluster of shops found in Bupyeong Kkangtong Market (부평 깡통시장).

Seafood

  • Jagalchi Hoe (자갈치 회) — Raw fish sashimi from Jagalchi Market (자갈치 시장) (1F for raw fish, 2F for preparation).
  • Mulhoe (물회) — Spicy cold raw fish soup.
  • Kkomjangeo (꼼장어) — Grilled hagfish, found in Jagalchi (자갈치) and Haeundae (해운대).

Recommended Restaurants

  • Milmyeon (밀면) Nofo (노포): Gaya Milmyeon (가야밀면)·Naeho Naengmyeon (내호냉면)
  • Nofo (노포): A traditional restaurant with 30+ years of history.
  • Dwaeji Gukbap (돼지국밥): Hapcheon Illyu Dwaeji Gukbap (합천일류돼지국밥) (Seomyeon (서면))
  • Ssiat Hotteok (씨앗호떡): BIFF Square (BIFF 광장) street vendors

5. Foreign Traveler-Friendly Tips

English Availability

  • High: Haeundae (해운대) (English signage·popular with foreign tourists)·Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을) (English guide)
  • Medium-High: Jagalchi Market (자갈치 시장) 1F·BIFF Square (BIFF 광장) (picture menus)
  • Local: Milmyeon Nofo (밀면 노포)·Gukje Market (국제시장) (translation app needed)

Budget (1 person, 3 days 2 nights)

  • KTX round trip: ₩119,600
  • Accommodation 2 nights (Hotel): ₩140,000~400,000
  • Meals 7 times: ₩100,000~200,000
  • Admission fees·Experiences (Blueline (블루라인)·Taejongdae (태종대) etc.): ₩30,000~60,000
  • City transportation (subway·taxi): ₩40,000
  • Total Average: ₩430,000~820,000

By Persona

  • US Persona: Haeundae (해운대) + Gamcheon (감천) + Jagalchi (자갈치) + Gwangalli (광안리) night view
  • European Persona: Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을) + Yeongdo (영도)·Songdo (송도) + Busan (부산) galleries·design
  • Foodie: Jagalchi (자갈치) (raw fish (회)) + Milmyeon (밀면) + Dwaeji Gukbap (돼지국밥) + Ssiat Hotteok (씨앗호떡) 4-hour food hop
  • K-drama Fan: Haeundae (해운대)·Gwangalli (광안리) (many drama filming locations)
  • Family-friendly: Haeundae Beach (해운대 해변) + Blueline Park (블루라인 파크) + Nurimaru (누리마루)

By Season

  • Spring (April-May): Haeundae (해운대) cherry blossoms + Gwangalli (광안리) stroll
  • Summer (June-August): Haeundae (해운대)·Gwangalli (광안리) beaches ★ peak season
  • Autumn (October-November): Gamcheon (감천) + Taejongdae (태종대) autumn leaves
  • Winter (December-February): Haeundae (해운대) (fewer tourists·cheaper hotels)

Accommodation Recommendations

  • Luxury: Signiel Busan (시그니엘 부산) (Haeundae (해운대)), Paradise Hotel (파라다이스 호텔) (Haeundae (해운대))
  • Mid-range: Haeundae Grand Hotel (해운대 그랜드 호텔), Gwangalli (광안리) hotel cluster
  • Guesthouse: Haeundae (해운대)·Seomyeon (서면) (₩40,000~80,000/night)

8. Japan Ferry Extension (Optional)

Busan (부산) is a departure point for ferries to Shimonoseki (시모노세키), Fukuoka (후쿠오카), and Osaka (오사카) in Japan:

  • Shimonoseki (시모노세키), Japan: Bu-Kwan Ferry (부관훼리) (12 hours·1 night)
  • Fukuoka (후쿠오카), Japan: Beetle (비틀호) (3 hours·same day)
  • Osaka (오사카), Japan: Busan Ferry (부산 페리) (16 hours·1 night)

→ This offers a unique option for Japan + Korea combo personas (e.g., European multi-destination travelers).
