🎯 How to Use This Guide

Split open a pink cherry-blossom-shaped bun and pink bean paste appears, with a floral scent rising first — Jinhae's cherry blossom bread is made with cherry blossom extract and cherry blossom bean paste (VisitKorea). This guide covers shops that sell desserts made with strawberry, cherry blossom and mugwort (ssuk, 쑥, the aromatic spring herb). In each section we list the places you can eat in Seoul first and the producing regions (Cheongdo, Sancheong, Jinhae, Gongju, Yeoju) after. Every shop is listed with a menu item using that ingredient on the Korea Tourism Organization's tourism data (VisitKorea, TourAPI).

The menu items here are items listed on VisitKorea, with no "spring only" label. However, many shops serve strawberry items only in strawberry season, and seasonal menus change every year — check on official social media or by phone before you go. Spring festival dates in the producing regions change every year, so we list only the dates of past editions. Winter strawberry season is covered in #476 Winter Snack Guide, and rice cakes such as mugwort tteok in #473 Tteok Guide.

🍓 Strawberry — 1 in Seoul, 4 in the Regions

  • Earth Us (얼스어스) — 150 Seongmisan-ro (성미산로), Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul. A cafe that uses no disposable containers, so takeout is not possible unless you bring your own container or tumbler. The popular item is the strawberry cream cheese cake with whole strawberries inside (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours
  • Deoknam (덕남) — 33-11 Yeonjian-gil (연지안길), Hwayang-eup (화양읍), Cheongdo-gun (청도군), Gyeongbuk (경북). The signature item is the strawberry fresh cream cake made with 100% dairy fresh cream and locally grown strawberries; there is also a fresh strawberry latte. It has a large garden, and pets are allowed outdoors only. Close to the Cheongdo Rail Bike. Official channel Instagram @deoknam_cheongdo (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours
  • Cafe Sancheongyo (카페산청요) — 106-13 Gangnubangmok-ro 499beon-gil (강누방목로499번길), Danseong-myeon (단성면), Sancheong-gun (산청군), Gyeongnam (경남). A cafe attached to a pottery workshop (Sancheongyo, 산청요) on the Namgang River, where every cup and dish is handmade pottery. The menu lists Sancheongyo strawberry bingsu, and the description recommends the bingsu with house-simmered red beans (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours
  • Planet Coffee Sancheong (플래닛커피 산청점) — 27 Gyeongho-ro (경호로), Geumseo-myeon (금서면), Sancheong-gun (산청군), Gyeongnam (경남). A cafe serving drinks and desserts made with local specialties, with a Sancheong strawberry latte on the menu. Second-floor rooftop; small dogs allowed (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours
  • Hanok Cafe Sobuk (한옥카페소북) — 526-9 Sincha-ro (신차로), Sindeung-myeon (신등면), Sancheong-gun (산청군), Gyeongnam (경남). A cafe in a renovated old hanok with an independent bookshop, Mildang Chaekbang (밀당책방), inside. The menu has the Dangye strawberry latte (단계딸기라떼) (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours

🌸 Cherry Blossom — 2 Places in Jinhae

  • Mijin Confectionery (미진과자점) — 4 Chungjang-ro 130beon-gil (충장로130번길), Jinhae-gu (진해구), Changwon (창원시), Gyeongnam (경남). 07:00–22:00, open every day. The signature item is the cherry blossom honey madeleine (벚꽃꿀마드렌느); there are also cherry blossom bread, cherry blossom cream cheese tart and cherry blossom roll. Known as Jinhae's oldest bakery, a "Century Shop" (백년가게), it developed the cherry blossom bread together with Jinhae Confectionery. Takeout and cards accepted. Tel. 055-545-3133 (VisitKorea)
  • Jinhae Confectionery (진해제과) — 4 Jungwon-ro 43beon-gil (중원로43번길), Gwanghwa-dong (광화동), Jinhae-gu (진해구), Changwon (창원시), Gyeongnam (경남). The signature bread is the cherry blossom bread. It also has cherry blossom cream cheese tart, Jinhae cherry blossom sandwich cookies and lemon bread, and the breads come boxed, which makes good gifts (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours
  • For a Jinhae cherry blossom route, see Jinhae Gunhangje Cherry Blossom Festival

🌿 Mugwort — 4 in Seoul, 2 in the Regions

  • Sawol-ui Mulgogi (사월의물고기) — 62-5 Insadong-gil (인사동길), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul (in an Anguk-dong (안국동) alley). A bakery cafe that uses mugwort as its main ingredient. The signature mugwort Vienna (쑥 비엔나) and mugwort cream latte are topped with mugwort cream and pieces of mugwort rice cake. In season it also serves a mugwort cream salt bread (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours
  • Naeja Sanghoe (내자상회) — 3 Sajik-ro 10-gil (사직로10길), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul (near Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역)). 10:00–22:00, open every day. The signature item is the ssukjeolmi castella (쑥절미 카스테라, mugwort and injeolmi, or roasted-soybean-powder rice cake, sponge cake); there are also injeolmi castella, Naeja monaka (wafer sweets) and mugwort latte. A hanok cafe on the site of Naejasi (내자시), which handled royal court food in the Joseon era. Tel. 070-7755-0142 (VisitKorea)
  • Come to Rest (컴투레스트) — 161-11 Hoegi-ro (회기로), Dongdaemun-gu (동대문구), Seoul (near Kyung Hee University (경희대)). The signature drink is the Ein-ssukpener (아인쑥페너), a cold brew latte topped with mugwort cream. It has single-person tables (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours
  • Magpie & Tiger Seongsu Tea Room (맥파이앤타이거 성수티룸) — 97 Seongsui-ro (성수이로), Seongdong-gu (성동구), Seoul. 11:00–21:30, open every day. A tea room serving tea and desserts, with a mugwort tiramisu on the menu. Tel. 0507-1306-1629 (VisitKorea)
  • Enhagore (엔학고레) — 113-12 Buljanggol-gil (불장골길), Banpo-myeon (반포면), Gongju (공주시), Chungnam (충남). A cafe overlooking a reservoir, with mugwort latte on the menu (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours
  • Cafe Yeoju (카페 여주) — 703 Gangmun-ro (강문로), Gangcheon-myeon (강천면), Yeoju (여주시), Gyeonggi (경기). A hanok cafe with a yard, with mugwort latte on the menu (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Price range: None of the 13 places has a price in its VisitKorea listing. Prices are not published — check by phone or on the shop's menu board
  • Dairy and eggs: Deoknam's strawberry fresh cream cake uses 100% dairy fresh cream (VisitKorea). Cheesecake, tiramisu, castella and madeleines usually contain dairy and eggs — ask before ordering if you are vegan
  • Gluten: Bread, cake and castella may contain wheat flour. No place in this guide is described as using rice or other flours
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (이태원) (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil (우사단로10길), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00–21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF, 한국이슬람교중앙회). Certification can change, so confirm before you visit. None of the shops in this guide carries a halal label
  • For a show-it card, see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Spring Dessert Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Ordering, Getting There

  • Solo: Slices of cake, bread and drinks come in single servings, so going alone is easy. Come to Rest (컴투레스트) has single-person tables (VisitKorea). For a map of solo-friendly spots, see Seoul Solo Diner Map — 12 Signature Spots for Foreigners' Solo Dining, Bars and Cafes
  • Takeout: Earth Us (얼스어스) does not do takeout unless you bring your own container. Mijin Confectionery (미진과자점) offers takeout, and Jinhae Confectionery (진해제과) breads come boxed (VisitKorea)
  • Payment: The only place listed as accepting cards is Mijin Confectionery (미진과자점) (VisitKorea). For cards and mobile payment, see Foreigner Korea Payment System Deep Dive
  • Ordering: Many cafes have you order at the counter and take a buzzer — The Korean Cafe Buzzer: The No-Panic Manual — What to Do After You Order. When you cannot communicate, see Manners When Words Fail — Ordering Etiquette in the Age of Translation Apps and Kiosks
  • Getting there: In Seoul, see Choosing Your Korea Transit Card: The No-Panic Manual — T-money, WOWPASS, or Climate Card. For Cheongdo, Sancheong, Jinhae, Gongju and Yeoju, see Korean Express & Intercity Buses: The No-Panic Manual — Knowing They Use Different Apps Is Half the Battle and Taking the KTX for the First Time: The No-Panic Manual — Yes, There's No Ticket Gate

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Strawberry festival dates are the same every year" — The 2026 editions on VisitKorea's festival data were the Wanju Samnye Strawberry Festival 3/6–3/8, the Nonsan Strawberry Festival 3/26–3/29 and the Jinju Strawberry Festival 4/10–4/12. The 2027 schedule has not been announced. Nonsan is described as preparing for the 2027 Nonsan World Strawberry Industry Expo (VisitKorea)

2. "Jinhae cherry blossom bread is sold only during the Gunhangje Festival" — Mijin Confectionery (미진과자점) is open every day and lists cherry blossom bread on its VisitKorea menu (VisitKorea) — call to check whether it is on the menu. The 2026 Jinhae Gunhangje Festival ran 3/27–4/5, and the 2027 schedule has not been announced

3. "You have to go to the countryside to eat mugwort desserts" — Four places in central Seoul list mugwort items (Sawol-ui Mulgogi, Naeja Sanghoe, Come to Rest and Magpie & Tiger, VisitKorea)

4. "Last spring's limited menu will be back this year" — For this edition we could not find official announcements of spring limited menus at chains and hotels. Check the store's notice before you go
