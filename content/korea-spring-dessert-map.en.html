⚠ 3 Common Mistakes with Spring Desserts

1. "Korean spring desserts = only strawberries" — Unaware of cherry blossoms, mugwort, plum blossoms: Only strawberries. In reality, you can fully experience cherry blossoms (벚꽃) (April Global Starbucks), mugwort (쑥) (March-May traditional herb), plum blossoms (매화) (Gwangyang (광양) March), tulips (튤립) (Goyang (고양) April-May), and bomnamul (봄나물, spring wild vegetables).

2. "Cherry blossoms = just scenery" — Unaware of dessert fit: Only scenery. In reality, there are cherry blossom macarons, lattes, and cakes limited to April + dessert cafe clusters in Yeouido (여의도) and Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) (#421 Spring, #339 Photo).

3. "Hotel bingsu = only summer" — Unaware of spring limitations: Only summer. In reality, Shilla (신라), Signiel (시그니엘), and Four Seasons (포시즌스) offer spring strawberry and cherry blossom limited desserts (same pattern as #469 hotel bingsu (빙수), March-May).

🍯 5 Tips for Foreigners

1. Strawberry (딸기) Season = January-May — Korea's #1 spring fruit, chain stores (Sulbing (설빙), Twosome (투썸)) launch season-limited menus.

2. Cherry Blossom (벚꽃) Desserts = April — Global Starbucks cherry blossom, cafe clusters in Yeouido (여의도) and Gyeongbokgung (경복궁).

3. Mugwort (쑥) = March-May Herb — Dear Daon (디어다온) (#469) Yeonnam (연남) mugwort bingsu (빙수), traditional ssukttek (쑥떡) (#473).

4. Hotel Spring Limited = Reservations Required — Shilla (신라), Signiel (시그니엘), Four Seasons (포시즌스) (same pattern as #469 summer).

5. #421 Spring Linkage — Combine dessert itineraries with Gwangyang (광양) Plum Blossoms, Jinhae Gunhangje (진해 군항제) (Cherry Blossom Festival), Yeouido (여의도) Cherry Blossoms, and Taean Flower Festival (태안 꽃박람회).
