Korean Alcohol Deep Dive — Makgeolli, Soju, Cheongju, Traditional Liquor, Highball, Craft Beer

Subtitle: Full Set of Korean Alcohol + Deep Dive into Korean Drinking Culture for Foreigners (Follow-up to #136 Makgeolli (막걸리) & Traditional Liquor (전통주))

ISSUE 03 · #203 · 2026-05-19

🎯 Why a Korean Alcohol Deep Dive Guide?

A top priority for foreigners to deep dive into Korean drinking culture — Makgeolli (막걸리), Soju (소주), Cheongju (청주), jeontongju (전통주), highball, craft beer, and fruit soju (과일소주). This is a full set deep dive guide to Korean alcohol, beyond #136 Makgeolli (막걸리) and jeontongju (전통주).

★ 12 Types at a Glance

# Alcohol ABV Signature
1 Makgeolli (막걸리) 6~8% Korea's #1 jeontongju (전통주, traditional rice wine)
2 Soju (소주) 16~20% Korea's #1 distilled liquor
3 Cheongju (청주, Sake-like) 13~16% Authentic Korean cheongju (청주, clear rice wine)
4 Fruit Soju (과일소주) 12~15% Grapefruit, Peach, Strawberry
5 Chamisul (참이슬) & Jinro Is Back (진로이즈백) (Jinro) 16~17% Korea's #1 Soju (소주) brand
6 Cass (카스) & Terra (테라) (Beer) 4~5% Korea's #1 Beer
7 Craft Beer (수제 맥주) 5~8% Authentic Euljiro (을지로) & Hongdae (홍대)
8 Highball (하이볼) 7~9% Korea's #1 for MZ (엠지, Millennials and Gen Z)
9 Makgeolli (막걸리) & Dongdongju (동동주) 6~8% Makgeolli (막걸리) + floating rice grains (dongdongju (동동주, traditional rice punch))
10 Jeontongju (전통주, Korean Traditional Liquor) Various Andong (안동), Munbae (문배), Ihwaju (이화주)
