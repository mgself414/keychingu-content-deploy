Why a Korean Alcohol Deep Dive Guide?

The No.1 priority for foreigners diving into Korean drinking culture — makgeolli, soju, cheongju, jeontongju, highball, craft beer, and fruit soju. Beyond #136 Makgeolli & Traditional Liquor, this is a full-set deep dive guide to Korean alcohol.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Makgeolli is just alcohol": Makgeolli is Korea's No.1 fermented liquor (rice + nuruk) — the No.1 chapter in 6,000 years of Korean drinking history. Foreigners' top pick to try first.

2. "Soju is like vodka": Soju is 16~20% — weaker than vodka (40%), but 3~4 glasses equal one shot of vodka. First-time foreigners should pace themselves with the standard soju glass (50ml).

3. "Korean drinking manners are all the same": The full set of Korean drinking manners includes pouring and receiving with both hands, turning your head away to drink, letting elders go first, and finishing one glass at a time — the Korean style.

  • Recommended: Chamisul, Jinro Is Back, Chum-Churum, Good Day
  • Price: ₩1,500~4,000/bottle
  • Foreign demand: Korea's No.1 distilled liquor + a No.1 subject in foreign vlogs

③ Cheongju (Sake-like)

  • Recommended: Baekhwasubok, Cheongha, Charyeju
  • Price: ₩5,000~15,000/bottle
  • Foreign demand: Korea's authentic cheongju + often paired conceptually with Japanese sake

④ Fruit Soju ★ Foreign MZ No.1 Pick

  • Varieties: Grapefruit, peach, strawberry, green grape, plum
  • Price: ₩1,500~3,500/bottle
  • Foreign demand: No.1 choice for a foreigner's first Korean drinking challenge (sweet taste + a lower-ABV feel)

⑤ Chamisul & Jinro Is Back

  • Chamisul Fresh: The standard soju
  • Jinro Is Back: A 1970s throwback release + a No.1 subject in foreign vlogs
  • Price: ₩1,500~2,500/bottle

⑥ Cass & Terra (Beer)

  • Cass: Korea's No.1 beer
  • Terra: Pairs with Jinro soju for somaek
  • Price: ₩2,500~5,000/bottle

⑦ Craft Beer

  • Euljiro: The Booth, Mystery Brewing
  • Hongdae: Craft Hans, Galmegi Brewing
  • Price: ₩7,000~12,000/glass

⑧ Highball ★ MZ No.1 Pick

  • Recommended: Available at nearly every Korean izakaya and dining spot
  • Price: ₩8,000~15,000
  • Foreign demand: The Japanese-style highball (whisky + soda) is Korea's No.1 pick among the MZ generation

⑨ Dongdongju

  • Signature: Makgeolli with floating rice grains
  • Price: ₩6,000~15,000/kettle
  • Foreign demand: A deep-dive favorite among authentic traditional-liquor fans

⑩ Jeontongju (Korean Traditional Liquor)

  • Andong Soju, Munbaeju, Ihwaju, Igangju
  • Signature: Korea's regional traditional liquors + intangible cultural heritage status
  • Foreign demand: A deep-dive favorite among Korean-alcohol enthusiasts

⑪ Somaek

  • Signature: Soju + beer = Korea's No.1 drinking-table cocktail
  • Foreign demand: The No.1 pick for Korean office workers and the MZ generation
  • How to make it: In a beer glass, 1/3 soju + 2/3 beer (the Korean way)

⑫ Pokjuju

  • Signature: The centerpiece of Korean office dinners and drinking challenges
  • Varieties: Somaek bomb shots + whisky bomb shots
  • Foreign demand: The No.1 deep-dive subject in Korean drinking culture

5 Korean Drinking Manners

1. Pour and receive with both hands: Hold and pour with both hands in front of elders or superiors — the No.1 rule of authentic drinking etiquette

2. Turn your head away to drink: Turn your head 90° away from elders or superiors while drinking — the No.1 mistake foreigners make

3. Let elders go first: Wait until an elder or superior raises their glass — a foreign favorite once learned

4. Finish one glass at a time (one-shot): The Korean-style single-glass finish — a foreign challenge; the 50ml soju glass is recommended

5. Don't refill a glass that isn't empty: The Korean-style manner — you top off a glass once it's empty, and foreigners learn this by observing

Extra Tips for Foreign Enthusiasts

  • Drinking toasts: "Wihayeo!" ("Cheers!"), "One-shot!", "Jjan!" — the full set for foreign enthusiasts
  • Second and third rounds: A Korean company dinner's 1st round (meal + somaek), 2nd round (karaoke, #436), 3rd round (pocha bar, #454 club)
  • A foreigner's limit: One bottle of soju (16% × 360ml ≈ 1.4 shots of vodka) — first-time foreigners should pace themselves to 1/3 of a bottle

⚙ FAQ (6 Questions)

1. What should a foreigner try first? Fruit soju (grapefruit, peach at ₩1.5K) → makgeolli (Jangsu at ₩1.5K) → Cass + Chamisul somaek → if ready for a challenge, Chamisul Original (20.1%).

2. Best value for foreigners? Convenience-store soju at ₩1.4K, beer at ₩2K, makgeolli at ₩1.5K, Filite for No.1 value, somaek at ₩3.5K (about the price of one vodka shot).

3. Where do foreign enthusiasts go for the authentic experience? Gwangjang Market makgeolli (#380), Uraeok's munbaeju (#388), Insadong traditional-liquor gallery (#297), the #362 deep dive.

4. Where do foreign luxury enthusiasts go? Signiel Bar 81 (#356, #360), Michelin-starred Rayeon + jeontongju (#356, #358), Cheongdam Wine21, Haechang Makgeolli (₩50,000+).

5. The foreigner's challenge (the Chamisul challenge)? One bottle of Chamisul Original (20.1%) equals about 1.4 shots of vodka — foreign enthusiasts are advised to cap themselves at 1/3 to 1/2 a bottle.

6. A foreigner's 1-night-2-day distillery trip? Andong (#310 intangible-heritage Andong soju), Jeonju (#255 Igangju), Haenam (Haechang), integrated with the #362 deep dive.
