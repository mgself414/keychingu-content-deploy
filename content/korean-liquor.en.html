⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Makgeolli = just alcohol": Makgeolli is Korea's No. 1 fermented alcohol (rice + malt) — No. 1 in 6,000 years of Korean alcohol history. No. 1 challenge for foreigners.

2. "Soju = like vodka": Soju 16-20% — weaker than vodka (40%), but 3-4 glasses = 1 shot of vodka. For foreigners, step-by-step soju glasses (50ml) are required.

3. "Korean drinking etiquette = all the same": Full set of Korean drinking etiquette — Pour with both hands·Turn your head to drink·Adult first·Empty 1 glass (Korean style)

5 Korean drinking etiquette

1. Pour and receive with both hands: Lift and pour a glass with both hands in front of an adult or superior (No. 1 in authentic mania)

2. Turn your head to drink: Turn your head 90° and drink in front of an adult or superior (foreigner's mistake #1)

3. Adults first: Adults, wait until the boss raises his glass, foreign enthusiasts

4. Empty 1 glass (one shot): Empty 1 glass Korean style · Challenge for foreigners · 50ml soju glass recommended

5. If you empty the glass, don't pour again: Korean manners - If you leave alcohol in the glass, pour more · Learning from foreign enthusiasts
