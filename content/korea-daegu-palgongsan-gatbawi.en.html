⚠ Medical/Safety disclaimer

Hiking = stairs + steep slope/altitude 850-1192m. Pregnant women, high blood pressure, heart patients = Consultation with a hiking doctor. Children = Gatbawi Course OK, Normal Course Emergency ☎ 1339 (English) / ☎ 119 (Fire/Emergency).

Dietary Restrictions:

  • Halal, Kosher, Vegetarian, Vegan: Donghwasa Temple food = Full set fallback (Halal, Kosher, Vegan OK)
  • Gluten free: Some temple food is OK, except for soy sauce.
