🎯 Why a Guide to Daegu Palgongsan Gatbawi?

Daegu Palgongsan (팔공산) (1192m) = one of Korea's 7 Great Mountains · Gatbawi (갓바위) (Daejwabul (대좌불), 850m) = Korea's #1 prayer spot. Common pain points for foreigners: (a) the misconception that "Gatbawi (갓바위) is just a Buddha statue," (b) the 1 Wish Per Year Matrix (prayers for Suneung (수능 - College Scholastic Ability Test), job seeking, marriage, health), (c) Donghwasa Temple (동화사) (founded in 493 AD during the Silla (신라) Dynasty by King Soji (소지왕), 1500+ years old), (d) even non-Buddhists among Koreans are enthusiasts (spiritual matrix), (e) 1-2 hours of hiking, then prayer + walking after reaching Gatbawi (갓바위), (f) a perfect fit for spiritual, wellness, and MZ foreign enthusiasts.

This guide covers Gatbawi (갓바위) + Donghwasa Temple (동화사) signatures + prayer matrix + hiking guide + spiritual enthusiasts for foreigners.
