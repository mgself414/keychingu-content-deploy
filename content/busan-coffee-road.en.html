Busan Coffee Road — How to Drink Coffee in the City of a World Champion

Subtitle: It's no accident that Korea's first World Barista Champion (Jeon Ju-yeon (전주연) of Momos Coffee (모모스커피), 2019) came out of Busan. A port where green coffee beans arrive, roasteries built into converted warehouses, first-generation specialty coffee that started in Jeonpo (전포) — Busan runs on a different coffee grammar than Seoul. This piece maps four roasteries and the Yeongdo (영도) coffee-street route, plus the "everything closes at 6 PM" trap. The coffee-focused deep dive for the Jeonpo neighborhood piece (#246) and the Yeongdo piece (#271).

ISSUE · #1321 · 2026-08-23

🎯 Why Busan Is a Coffee City

Two numbers make the case. One: most of the green coffee beans entering Korea come through Busan Port (부산항) — roasteries clustering in a port city is simple logistics. Two: Jeon Ju-yeon, the barista who became Korea's first-ever World Barista Championship winner in 2019, heads Momos Coffee, a Busan-born brand. In 2026, the Financial Times' "World's 100 Best Coffee Shops" ranking placed Momos at world No. 22 — the highest ranking of any Korean cafe. If Seoul's Seongsu and Yeonnam cafe scene competes on "space," Busan has always competed on roasting and the coffee itself.

① Momos Coffee — A Champion's Coffee, Two Home Bases

Where to Go

  • Yeongdo Roastery & Coffee Bar (영도 로스터리 & 커피바) (Bongnae-dong (봉래동), Yeongdo) — a large space built into a former logistics warehouse. The green-bean storage, roasting facility, and coffee bar all sit in one building, so the whole "how coffee gets made" process is on display. Since opening in late 2021, it's become the de facto number-one stop on any Busan coffee trip.
  • Oncheonjang Flagship (온천장 본점) — the original location, started in 2007. Smaller than Yeongdo, but this is where the story begins: a neighborhood coffee shop that produced a world champion.
  • There's also a Haeundae Marine City branch, and Domoheon (도모헌) (2024), built into a former Busan mayor's residence — go to Domoheon if you'd rather avoid the crowds.

⚠ Where People Get Caught Off Guard: 6 PM Closing

Every Momos location closes at 6 PM (a "baristas get to have evenings too" policy in place since 2022). If your travel rhythm defaults to "coffee after dinner," you'll show up to a locked door. The basic grammar of a Busan coffee road: schedule coffee for daytime, in the morning through early afternoon.

② Jeonpo — The Original Alley of First-Generation Specialty Coffee

Jeonpo Cafe Street (전포카페거리, #246) already got its neighborhood-focused piece, but looking at it through a coffee lens reveals a lineage:

  • FM Coffee (에프엠커피) — started in Jeonpo in 2008, one of the first-generation pioneers of Busan specialty coffee alongside Momos and Blackup.
  • Blackup Coffee (블랙업커피) — the other pillar of Busan's first-generation specialty scene. Local reviews consistently praise the flagship's consistency.
  • Werk Roasters (베르크 로스터스) — an indie roastery that's grown into one of the "Busan Specialty Big Four." The Jeonpo location works as a showroom for their own coffee, so it's a good stop for buying beans plus a tasting. They also show up regularly at coffee events (Coffee Expo) near Gwangalli.

Ordering Tips (Applies to Any Specialty Cafe)

1. First pick filter (brewed) vs. espresso-based off the menu — filter is the main event at a roastery.

2. If there are two or more beans to choose from, just ask staff whether it's "fruity or nutty" — those exact English words work fine too.

3. When buying beans, check the roast date — roastery-direct beans are almost always roasted within the past 1–2 weeks.

③ Yeongdo Bongnae-dong — Where Warehouses Become Cafes

The Bongnae-dong area of Yeongdo is a former port-warehouse district in the middle of turning into a coffee-focused street. With the Momos roastery as its anchor, a string of warehouse-converted cafes runs along with harbor and crane views. Pair it with the Huinnyeoul Culture Village (흰여울문화마을) route from the Yeongdo piece (#271) and you've got a half-day plan: a walk through Huinnyeoul → warehouse cafes in Bongnae-dong → (cut coffee off here, before 6 PM) → dinner at a Jagalchi (자갈치) chojang-jip (#1320).

🗺 Sample One-Day Route

Time Route
Morning Jeonpo — a cup of filter coffee plus bean shopping at FM Coffee or the Werk Roasters showroom
Lunch Milmyeon (밀면) in Seomyeon (서면) (#1320)
Afternoon Head to Yeongdo — Huinnyeoul Culture Village (#271) → Momos Roastery & Coffee Bar in Bongnae-dong
Evening Coffee's done by 6 PM — hoe (회) at a Jagalchi chojang-jip (#1320) or the Gwangalli (광안리) night view (#243)
