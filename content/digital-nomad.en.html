🛂 D-10 Job Seeking Visa Full Set (Foreigner Nomad Enthusiast Hub No. 1)

D-10-1 (General Job Seeking):

  • Duration: 6 months · Renewable once (max 2 years)
  • Requirements: Bachelor's degree+ · Financial proof (3,000 USD/month) · Criminal record certificate (apostille)
  • Application: Korean Embassy in home country or Immigration Office
  • Fee: ₩60,000 (application)
  • Extension: 4 months before expiration · Hi Korea (하이코리아) · 1345 Call Center (English)

D-10-2 (Tech Startup·Startup):

  • Duration: 1 year · Renewable
  • Requirements: Global K-Startup · Recommendation from Ministry of SMEs and Startups · 100 million KRW capital
  • Special Benefits: Convertible to F-2 (after 3 years · permanent residency stage)

D-10-3 (Internship):

  • Duration: 3~6 months
  • Requirements: Bachelor's degree+ · Government-certified internship · OECD country

Conversion Visas:

  • D-10 → E-2 (English teacher · 24-36M) · E-7 (Professional IT/Design/Finance · 40-80M) · F-2-7 (Points-based · after 3+ years) · F-5 (Permanent Residency · after 5+ years)

Key Point: D-10 = Entry point for the foreigner nomad full set. 6 months~2 years of free residence + freedom to work (part-time OK · full-time job enthusiast matrix).
