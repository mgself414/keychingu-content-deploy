6 Digital Nomad Coworking Spaces in Seoul — Work on a Day Pass, and Sort Out the Visa, eSIM and Payments

Subtitle: 6 Seoul coworking spaces where you can sit for a one-day fee with no monthly contract (Gongdeok, Wangsimni, Hongdae, Hapjeong, Seongsu). For each place we list the day price, Wi-Fi and outlets, hours, and how to pay. After that, from official sources, we cover the Seoul income requirement for the digital nomad (workation) visa F-1-D, a one-month eSIM, and ways to pay without a Korean bank account.

ISSUE 03 · #278 · 2026-11-25

🎯 Who This Is For

This piece is for remote workers who do their overseas job on a laptop while staying in Seoul for a few weeks to a few months. Seoul shared offices are normally monthly contracts, but some sell a day pass (1-day pass, One Day). We kept only the places where the day price could be confirmed on an official page, an operator or district-office blog, the Kakao Map (카카오맵) owner-registered menu, or a dated visit review. All 6 show as open on Kakao Map (confirmed 2026-11-25).

The amount on the payment screen can differ by branch, date, and discount. The SPARKPLUS day price is a discounted price and we could not confirm the list price. A '—' in the table means the item could not be confirmed from an official notice or a dated review. We found no Wi-Fi mention for SPARKPLUS Gongdeok, SPARKPLUS Hongdae, and Kkokkoworks. Walking times are Kakao Map directions values.

① 3 SPARKPLUS Lounges — Inside Subway Stations, and Hongdae

SPARKPLUS is a shared-office company, and it sells shared seats through its Lounge Pass. There are two one-day kinds. According to the official lounge page (viewed 2026-11-25), Metro One Day gives you one day at one of the lounges inside subway stations (Gongdeok Station, Wangsimni Station, Madeul Station (마들역)), and Weekend One Day gives you one day at one branch lounge on Saturday or Sunday. The official blog (2026-10-02) table shows Metro One Day from 9,900 won and Weekend One Day from 20,000 won. That blog table is from a post about the October discount period of up to 40% off, and we could not confirm the list price.

According to the official notice, you pay for One Day on the web with no app install, and the QR code for lounge entry arrives by KakaoTalk (카카오톡) Alimtalk (알림톡). Have a phone with KakaoTalk installed. The official notice does not say whether a Korean number is needed to receive Alimtalk (unconfirmed). The payment page is in Korean. Same-day use can be bought until 20:00, and only the person who booked can enter. Meeting rooms, focus rooms, printers and lockers cannot be used with One Day. The lounge has power strips and USB ports at every seat for charging, and coffee and tea are included (official).

SPARKPLUS Lounge Gongdeok Station (스파크플러스라운지 공덕역)

It is on basement level 1 inside Gongdeok Station, toward Exit 3 (official). The Metro lounge opens daily 08:00~22:00 only, following the station's operating hours (official FAQ, same value on Kakao Map). A visit review from 2026-02-01 says the restroom is inside the station and there is no parking. At launch (2026-02) the Gongdeok One Day was a promotional price of 12,900 won (review 2026-02-04). We could not confirm the list price, so check the payment-screen amount each day.

SPARKPLUS Lounge Wangsimni Station (스파크플러스라운지 왕십리역)

Follow the signs for Line 5 toward Banghwa at Wangsimni Station and it is next to the stairs (official). From outside, Exit 4 is close (Kakao Map, 1 minute). Hours are daily 08:00~22:00 (official FAQ, Kakao Map). A visit review from 2026-06-30 mentions Metro One Day at 9,900 won a day (promotional price for new customers), power strips at every seat and 5G Wi-Fi, and sofa seats with wireless chargers.

SPARKPLUS Hongik Univ. Station Lounge (스파크플러스 홍대입구역 라운지) (Weekend-Only One Day)

It is 41m from Exit 4 of Hongik Univ. Station, with a community desk on the 6th floor of LC Tower (official). It is not a lounge inside the station but a lounge inside a shared-office branch, so it is open 24 hours (official FAQ, Kakao Map shows 24 hours). However, the only day pass is Weekend One Day, so you can enter only on Saturday and Sunday. Public-holiday use appears only in the Weekend subscription FAQ and not in the One Day notice. The community desk is staffed only on weekdays 10:00~17:00 (official) and is closed on weekends (Kakao Map).

② Seongsu — 1 Shared Office Run by the District Office

Seonggong Space (성공스페이스)

This is the Seongdong-style shared office introduced on the official Seongdong-gu Office (성동구청) blog (2026-02-25). It is open to "anyone who needs a workspace", with 110 work seats, 3 meeting rooms, a studio, and a creator room. Free-seat 1-day pass 15,000 won, hours 09:00~22:00 (district office blog, same value on Kakao Map). You book and pay on a mobile web page or on site, and verify with a QR code at the door (district office blog). The booking page (https://www.alicorn-space.ai/seongdonggu-office) has a language selector and shows English text. We could not confirm whether the whole booking flow is in English. According to a review from 2026-03-19, the 1-day pass uses a free seat rather than an assigned seat, there is Wi-Fi, and snacks and tea are set out. A review from 2026-11-15 also showed the 1-day pass at 15,000 won. The address is 3F, Sangga-dong Building, 135 Wangsimni-ro, Seongdong-gu (성동구 왕십리로 135 상가동 3층), 6 minutes from Exit 1 of Ttukseom Station. Meeting rooms (20,000 won per hour) are booked separately (district office blog).

③ Hongdae·Hapjeong — 2 Neighborhood Shared Offices

Kkokkoworks (크크웍스)

A small community-style shared office. The Kakao Map owner-registered menu lists a fixed-seat 1-day pass 15,000 won (2025-12-26) and a 1-week pass 70,000 won (2025-12-28). The 1-day pass is not in the latest operator post (2026-07-27) and is only in the Kakao menu (2025-12-26). The operator blog (2026-07-27) says it runs a free 1-day trial pass (first visit only, 1 time) Tue~Sat 14:00~20:00. The 5,000 won deposit is returned when you visit. The same post says there is a group dinner at 6 p.m. Desks come with an office chair, a power strip, and a personal light (operator blog). Kakao Map shows it open 24 hours. You book through the operator (Naver Booking (네이버 예약) · Instagram DM, and Kakao Map has a booking ticket). The address is 3F, 27 Donggyo-ro 27-gil, Mapo-gu (마포구 동교로27길 27 3층), 7 minutes from Exit 2 of Hongik Univ. Station.

Owl Granary Shared Office (부엉이곳간 공유오피스)

The official website describes itself as a 'local space for digital nomads'. The Kakao Map owner-registered menu (2026-06-04) lists a One Day lounge free seat 13,000 won, 09:00~22:00. The official website gives the 1 Day hours as weekdays 09:00~19:00. The two values differ, so if you plan to work into the evening, check the hours when you book. A review says 13,000 won was a coupon price, so we could not confirm the list price. The official website has a Wi-Fi notice. In the lounge you pick any seat you like, and coffee and drinks are unlimited (official). The address is B1 and 3F, 25-11 Seongji-gil, Mapo-gu (마포구 성지길 25-11 지층·3층), 4 minutes from Exit 7 of Hapjeong Station.

🛂 Stay Practicals 1 — Digital Nomad (Workation) Visa F-1-D, Seoul Standard

The full visa requirements, documents, and regional workation spaces such as Busan are in #459 Korea Workation Guide. Here we give only the values that change when you stay in Seoul.

  • Income requirement (Seoul is the capital region): The attachment to the Ministry of Justice (법무부) press release of 2026-07-07 gives, for the capital region (excluding population-decline (watch) areas), "ages 18~34: 1.5x GNI (7,862만원)" and "age 35 and over: 2x GNI (10,483만원)". That is 78,620,000 won and 104,830,000 won. It is based on 2025 GNI (52,410,000 won), which is the previous-year value as of the application date, so it changes every year. Non-capital regions are one step lower. If you come to the capital region with family, it is 2x GNI regardless of age (same attachment, viewed 2026-11-25).
  • Where to apply and length of stay: The original text gives the visa application as "each overseas diplomatic mission (Embassy of the Republic of Korea)", and the stay period as "1 year granted from the date of entry upon foreigner registration, extendable 1 year per extension of 1 time (up to 3 years)". Full operation began 2026-06-30.
  • What you cannot do: The original says "employment and profit-making activities restricted". This is not a visa for taking Korean company work.
  • Insurance: "Private medical insurance with coverage of one hundred million won (1억원) or more is required" (same attachment).

For a short stay, tourist entry is enough. For entry documents, see #248 Korea Visa & K-ETA Guide.

📶 Stay Practicals 2 — An eSIM for More Than a Month

The data plans in the KT Korea SIM/eSIM guide for foreigners (2026-10 edition, viewed 2026-11-25) are 30 days 71,500 won, 60 days 107,200 won, 90 days 143,000 won. It is high speed up to 11GB a day, and after that you keep using it at up to 5Mbps. You get a 010 number, and once the entry check (identity verification) is done you can receive calls and texts. To make calls, top up at least 5,500 won. The guide says this product is for short-term visitors and can be extended only up to 90 days from the activation date. The guide says the eSIM period runs in 24-hour units from the moment you install the QR, so install it on airport Wi-Fi after you arrive. Step-by-step screens are in #1303 Korea eSIM Activation Manual.

💳 Stay Practicals 3 — Paying Without a Korean Bank Account

  • WOWPASS: The official site (viewed 2026-11-25) says you can top up with a credit card, e-wallet, or cash, cash top-up accepts 16 currencies, and overseas cards top up through Mastercard, JCB, and VISA (PayPal). It also works as a transit card, and you can withdraw cash up to 100,000 won at a time for a 1,000 won fee.
  • Paying for coworking: Check on each payment screen whether overseas cards work. Seonggong Space accepts payment on site (district office blog). We could not confirm whether overseas cards are approved at the 6 places in this piece or whether WOWPASS works there.
  • Cards, mobile payments, and currency exchange are covered in full in #431 Korea Payment & Connectivity Guide.

🗺 2 One-Day Combinations

A. Gongdeok Station on a weekday

  1. Get off at Gongdeok Station (Lines 5·6, Gyeongui-Jungang Line, AREX, Kakao Map)
  2. Work from 08:00 on Metro One Day at SPARKPLUS Lounge Gongdeok Station (스파크플러스라운지 공덕역) inside the station. Same-day payment is possible until 20:00
  3. It closes at 22:00, so if an evening call runs late, move to your lodging

B. Seongsu on a weekday

  1. Go from Exit 1 of Ttukseom Station to Seonggong Space (성공스페이스) and sit at a free seat at 09:00 with the 1-day pass (15,000 won)
  2. If you have a video meeting, book a meeting room (20,000 won per hour) in advance
  3. It closes at 22:00. If you move to Hongdae on the weekend, use Weekend One Day at SPARKPLUS Hongik Univ. Station Lounge (스파크플러스 홍대입구역 라운지)

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Assuming any shared office sells a day pass — The official WeWork Seoul page lists only monthly membership prices and tells you to inquire about daily prices. The bigger the chain, the more likely it has no day-unit product or sets it separately. Go where the day price has been confirmed.
  2. Walking into any SPARKPLUS branch with One Day — The weekday One Day works only at the Metro lounges inside stations (Gongdeok Station, Wangsimni Station, Madeul Station), and Weekend One Day works at one branch on Saturday and Sunday (official).
  3. Taking Korean work on a nomad visa — The Ministry of Justice original says "employment and profit-making activities restricted". Seoul (the capital region) also has a higher income requirement than non-capital regions.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. Can I book in English? — The Seonggong Space booking page has a language selector and shows English text, but we could not confirm whether the whole flow is in English. For SPARKPLUS One Day, the QR arrives by KakaoTalk (카카오톡) Alimtalk (알림톡). The official notice does not say whether a Korean number is needed to receive Alimtalk (unconfirmed), and the payment page is in Korean.
  2. Do I need to worry about charging my laptop? — The official notice says SPARKPLUS lounges have power strips and USB at every seat. At Kkokkoworks every desk has a power strip (operator blog).
  3. Is a day pass better if I stay more than a month? — If you go out only a few days, a day pass is better. For a month or more, Seonggong Space's free-seat 30-day pass at 200,000 won (district office blog), Owl Granary's 24-hour free seat for 1 month at 150,000 won (Kakao menu), and SPARKPLUS Everyday from 159,000 won a month (official) are cheaper than paying for a day pass every day. Kkokkoworks is 450,000 won for 1 month (VAT excluded, operator blog), so the 1-day pass or 1-week pass is better.
