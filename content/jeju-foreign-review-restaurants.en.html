14 Jeju Restaurants — Only Where Foreign Reviews Showed Up First

Subtitle: After the black pork, haenyeo, and hairtail sashimi piece (#1060), the local favorites and the Oil Market piece (#1348), and Local Favorites Part 7 (#1388), here is the next axis — eomgomtang near the airport, noodles in the old downtown, wood-fired pizza in Songdang, a haenyeo table on Udo, and braised hairtail in Jungmun. 🎯 Why "Three-Language Reviews" Is the Bar

There are already three Jeju food pieces that picked venues — the black pork, haenyeo, and hairtail sashimi piece (#1060), the local favorites and Oil Market piece (#1348), and Local Favorites Part 7 with Jeju Imje, Tamna Ganjang Gejang, and Miyeong-ne (#1388). This piece collects only venues that appear in none of the three. Even where the type of dish overlaps with #1060 — black pork, hairtail, a haenyeo table — the venues don't overlap.

There's one selection rule — restaurants on Naver Map (네이버 지도) with English, Japanese, and Chinese reviews together. Not Korean popularity rankings — the judgment is that a place foreign visitors have already found and left traces in three languages is the less unfamiliar pick for the next one. Review counts are a 2026-11 snapshot, and this piece lists that count for every venue. There is one exception — Gimgogi (김고기) has no Japanese reviews (4·0·1), but it's an evening meat restaurant in downtown Seogwipo whose menu is registered in grams per serving, so it's in.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours, closed days, and menu prices are the values the owner registered on Kakao Map (카카오맵) (checked 2026-11-23). Prices are given for one serving or one bowl of a signature dish, and for 2-person menus or whole-pie menus the basis is stated alongside. Where the menu price was registered more than 2 years ago, the cell says "check Kakao Map on the day." Dietary cues are only what can be read from menu names — ask staff before ordering about ingredients and allergies. Jeju is big, so moving between zones means a car or a bus. This piece doesn't give travel times — check them on the day with Kakao Map directions.

① Near the Airport·Sinjeju — 3 Venues

Near Jeju Airport and the Sinjeju side. A good place for your first meal on arrival day or your last meal on departure day. All three open around 9 a.m.

  • Namgyeong Eogomtang (남경어곰탕) — an eogomtang (fish soup) and eogaejang (fish stew) place, with broth made from fish. Namgyeong eogomtang (남경어곰탕) 18,000 won, namgyeong eogaejang (남경어개장) 18,000 won, boneless grilled tilefish (순살옥돔구이) 20,000 won. With 22 foreign reviews in total (EN·JA·ZH counts 5·3·3), it has the most in this piece. Daily 08:00–21:00, break 15:00–17:00, no closed days. 96 Yeonsam-ro, Jeju-si (제주시 연삼로 96). Dietary cues: the soup and the grilled dish are fish, and the black pork chan-suyuk (cold boiled pork) (흑돼지 찬수육) (22,000 won) is pork. There are no raw menu items.
  • Eojanggun (어장군) — a Jeju local Korean restaurant serving tilefish radish soup and full spreads. Okdom-mu-guk (옥돔무국) (tilefish and radish soup) 18,000 won, Jeju gosari-bibimbap (제주고사리비빔밥) (bracken bibimbap) 13,000 won, Udo seongge-al-bibimbap (우도성게알비빔밥) (sea urchin roe bibimbap) 23,000 won. For groups there are also 3–4-person spreads (120,000–220,000 won) — the Sadon spread (사돈한상) (assorted sashimi) and the Hyangto spread (향토한상) (mackerel sashimi) include raw fish. Daily 09:00–21:00. 72 Sinsae-ro, Jeju-si (제주시 신대로 72). Dietary cues: the tilefish and sea urchin roe are seafood, and the dombe-gogi (돔베고기) (sliced boiled pork) set (45,000 won) is pork. If you want to avoid raw food, go for the okdom-mu-guk or the dombe-gogi set, and ask staff whether the sea urchin roe in the sea urchin roe bibimbap is raw. The menu item without meat in its name is the gosari-bibimbap, but ask about the broth and garnish ingredients too.
  • Jangmo Siktak (장모식탁) — a Korean set-meal place serving stone-pot rice with stew. Stone-pot rice + stew (돌솥밥+찌개) 10,000 won, gondeure stone-pot rice + stew (곤드레돌솥밥+찌개) 11,000 won, abalone stone-pot rice + stew (전복돌솥밥+찌개) 15,000 won, Jangmo set (장모정식) 17,000 won — 10,000–17,000 won per person. Mon–Sat 09:00–20:30, closed Sunday. 51 Yeonsin-ro, Jeju-si (제주시 연신로 51). Dietary cues: the Jangmo set (17,000 won) comes with ganjang-gejang (soy-marinated crab) (간장게장), abalone in soy sauce (전복장), and jeyuk-bokkeum (spicy stir-fried pork) (제육볶음). Ganjang-gejang is raw crab marinated in soy sauce and jeyuk-bokkeum is pork, so if you avoid raw food or don't eat pork, pick the stone-pot rice + stew instead of the set. The gondeure stone-pot rice is a wild-greens rice, but the ingredients in the stew broth need checking.

② Jeju City Noodles·Gukbap — 3 Venues

Noodle and gukbap places in Jeju City. Gwangmyeong Sikdang and 24-Si Guksu Hoegwan are in the old downtown, and Gongju-ne Guksu is in Bonggae-dong (봉개동) on the eastern side of Jeju City, so check the location in your map app first. All three run 7,000–10,000 won per bowl.

  • 24-Si Guksu Hoegwan (24시국수회관) — the name says 24 hours, but the Kakao Map registered hours are 06:30–23:40, closed Tuesday. Myeolchi-guksu (멸치국수) (anchovy-broth noodles) 7,000 won, gogi-guksu (고기국수) (pork noodle soup) 8,000 won, sundae-gukbap (순대국밥) (blood sausage and rice soup) 8,000 won, sagol-mom-guk (사골몸국) (beef-bone gulfweed soup) 8,000 won. The 06:30 opening is the earliest of the 14 venues in this piece — it suits breakfast after an early-morning walk or a bowl late in the evening. 122-1 Namseong-ro, Jeju-si (제주시 남성로 122-1). Dietary cues: the gogi-guksu, sundae, and pork skin (돼지껍데기) are pork, and the broths are anchovy and beef bone. In the sagol-mom-guk, mom-guk (몸국) is a Jeju soup made by simmering gulfweed (mom) in pork broth. There are no raw menu items.
  • Gwangmyeong Sikdang (광명식당) — an old-timer in the old downtown serving gukbap, sundae, and gogi-guksu. Gukbap (국밥) 9,000 won, gogi-guksu (고기국수) 9,000 won, ttaro-gukbap (따로국밥) (soup and rice served separately) 10,000 won. It has only 3 foreign reviews, but one in each of the three languages (1·1·1). Mon, Tue, and Thu–Sun 11:00–18:00, closed Wednesday, and it closes at 6 p.m. 38 Jungang-ro 13-gil, Jeju-si (제주시 중앙로13길 38). Dietary cues: it centers on pork and offal such as gukbap, sundae, and makchang (pork large intestine) (막창), so it isn't a fit if you don't eat pork.
  • Gongju-ne Guksu (공주네국수) — a gogi-guksu and mom-guk place. Gogi-guksu (고기국수) 8,500 won, myeolchi-guksu (멸치국수) 7,000 won, mom-guk (몸국) 10,000 won, kong-guksu (콩국수) (cold soybean noodles) 10,000 won. Tue–Sun 09:00–20:00, break 15:00–16:30, closed Monday. 353 Sambong-ro, Jeju-si (Bonggae-dong) (제주시 삼봉로 353 (봉개동)). Dietary cues: the gogi-guksu is pork. Mom-guk is a Jeju soup made by simmering gulfweed (mom) in pork broth, so if you avoid pork, leave it out. Even for menu items with no meat in their names, like the myeolchi-guksu and kong-guksu, ask separately about the broth.

③ East (Gujwa·Seongsan·Udo) — 3 Venues

A zone to fit into days around Seongsan Ilchulbong, Udo, and Bijarim. The three are split between Songdang, Seongsan, and Udo, so rather than cramming all of them into one day, pick the one that matches where you're going that day. All three have 13 or more foreign reviews, with 4 or more in each of the three languages.

  • Dough Boy (도우보이) — a sourdough wood-fired pizza place in Songdang (송당). Margherita (마르게리타) 23,000 won, Pungi (풍기) 28,000 won, White Pie (화이트파이) 29,000 won — pizza is sold by the whole pie, so these aren't per-person prices. Polenta (폴렌타) 15,000 won. Open only Thu–Sun 11:00–15:00 — closed Mon, Tue, and Wed. Menu names are registered in English on Kakao Map too (BLUEBERRY, FENNEL & GINGER SODA, etc.) — the only venue in this piece where English menu names are confirmed. 15 foreign reviews (5·5·5). 2136 Bijarim-ro, Gujwa-eup (구좌읍 비자림로 2136). Dietary cues: some pizzas, like the Margherita, Pungi, and Spinach & Sesame, have no meat in their names, but ask staff about the toppings. The shop description mentions charcuterie (샤퀴테리) (cured meats — mostly pork). There are no raw menu items.
  • Onpyeong Bada Hangeureut (온평바다한그릇) — a seafood and raw fish place on the coast of Onpyeong-ri (온평리), Seongsan. Gul-pajeon (굴파전) (oyster scallion pancake) 17,000 won, seokhwa (석화) (raw oysters) 10,000 won, bangeo-hoe (방어회) (amberjack sashimi) 20,000 won, assorted seafood (해산물모듬) small 45,000 won, large 65,000 won. The portion size and the number of people aren't registered for the sashimi and assortments, so when you order, ask how many people the portion feeds. Daily 12:00–22:00, break 15:30–17:00. 467 Hwanhaejangseong-ro, Seongsan-eup (성산읍 환해장성로 467). Dietary cues: it centers on seafood, and the sashimi and raw oysters are raw. The assorted seafood may include raw fish too, so ask what's in it. If you want to avoid raw food, go for the gul-pajeon.
  • Udo Haenyeo Sikdang (우도해녀식당) — a haenyeo seafood table on Udo island itself. Bomal-bajirak-kalguksu (보말바지락칼국수) (sea snail and clam noodle soup) 15,000 won, sora-juk (소라죽) (turban shell porridge) 15,000 won, seonggae-bibimbap (성게비빔밥) (sea urchin bibimbap) 20,000 won, ppul-sora-hoe (뿔소라회) (raw horned turban shell) 25,000 won (portion not stated). Daily 08:30–18:00 — closes at 6 p.m. 440 Udo-haean-gil, Udo-myeon (우도면 우도해안길 440). You have to take a boat to Udo, so check the Udo ferry timetable first and plan lunch around the boat back out. There are several haenyeo restaurants with similar names on Udo, so find it by the address. Dietary cues: it centers on seafood, and the turban shell, abalone, and sea squirt sashimi are raw. If you want to avoid raw food, go for the noodle soup or porridge.

🗺 Route — Pick by Where You're Staying

Jeju's zones are a car or bus ride apart, so a route that hops through several zones in one day doesn't work. Pick one zone based on where you're staying and that day's plans.

A. Staying in Jeju City (airport·Sinjeju·old downtown)

  1. Breakfast 24-Si Guksu Hoegwan in the old downtown (from 06:30, closed Tue) or Namgyeong Eogomtang near the airport (from 08:00)
  2. Lunch Gwangmyeong Sikdang gukbap in the old downtown (11:00–18:00, closed Wed) or Jangmo Siktak stone-pot rice in Jeju City (closed Sun)
  3. Dinner Eojanggun okdom-mu-guk or full spread in Sinjeju (until 21:00), or Gongju-ne Guksu in Bonggae-dong (closed Mon, until 20:00)
  4. Departure day Namgyeong Eogomtang — no closed days, opens at 08:00 (avoid the 15:00–17:00 break)

B. East-side days (Seongsan·Udo·Songdang)

  1. Udo day Check the ferry timetable → lunch at Udo Haenyeo Sikdang (closes 18:00)
  2. Seongsan-side dinner Onpyeong Bada Hangeureut (dinner service after the 15:30–17:00 break)
  3. Songdang·Bijarim day Lunch at Dough Boy if it's Thu–Sun (11:00–15:00). It's closed Mon–Wed, so swap in a Jeju City restaurant from A or Onpyeong Bada Hangeureut.

C. Staying in Seogwipo·Jungmun

  1. Lunch Sagye Bada if you're near Sanbangsan (for 2), or the 1-person braised hairtail at Unjeong-ne in Jungmun if you're alone
  2. Lunch on a west-coast day Bareunmul in Hallim
  3. Dinner Donirang black pork in Jungmun (until 01:00) or Gimgogi in downtown Seogwipo (16:00–22:00)

Gongju-ne Guksu and Dough Boy are closed on Monday, 24-Si Guksu Hoegwan and Dough Boy on Tuesday, Gwangmyeong Sikdang and Dough Boy on Wednesday, and Jangmo Siktak on Sunday. If you're going by bus, check the last bus with Kakao Map directions before you leave. To fit in the five-day market, see #1348.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Trusting the "24-hour" name and going at dawn — 24-Si Guksu Hoegwan's Kakao Map registered hours are 06:30–23:40, and it's closed on Tuesday. If you're looking for a place open past midnight, in this piece it's only Donirang (until 01:00).
  2. Heading east without checking days and closing times — Dough Boy is open only Thu–Sun 11:00–15:00. Go all the way to Songdang on a Mon, Tue, or Wed and you've made the trip for nothing. Udo Haenyeo Sikdang closes at 18:00, and so does Gwangmyeong Sikdang. Check the breaks too (Namgyeong Eogomtang, Gongju-ne Guksu, Onpyeong Bada Hangeureut).
  3. Going to a different branch with the same name — Donirang has a Seongsan branch, Sagye Bada has Aewol and Pyoseon branches, and Udo has several haenyeo restaurants. In your map app, search by this piece's street addresses, not the venue names.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. If I could pick just one? — The venue with the most foreign reviews in total is Namgyeong Eogomtang (22, EN·JA·ZH counts 5·3·3). The venues with 5 or more in each of the three languages are Dough Boy and Donirang (both 15, 5·5·5). If you're eating alone, Unjeong-ne, which has a 1-person menu registered (boneless braised hairtail, 1 person, 12,000 won).
  2. Which places have English menus? — Dough Boy is the only place where English alongside the menu names was confirmed on Kakao Map. For the rest, nothing is known about whether an English menu exists, so have a translation app ready.
  3. If I want to know more about black pork, hairtail, and the haenyeo table? — Black pork cuts and how to choose, the haenyeo table, and hairtail sashimi are in #1060. Market days and market food are in #1348. Jeju Imje, Tamna Ganjang Gejang, and Miyeong-ne are in #1388.
