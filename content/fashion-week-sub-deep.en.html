Three mistakes foreign visitors often make

Mistake 1. "I can just walk in" — the main runway shows are invite-only

SFW's main runway shows require a designer's invitation, media credentials, or buyer registration. Waiting in line on-site will not get you in. Instead, target the DDP outdoor pop-ups, exhibition halls, and fashion street areas, which are open to the public.

Mistake 2. "I thought SFW was in September" — it runs twice a year

SFW happens twice a year: February for F/W (fall/winter collections) and September for S/S (spring/summer collections). Check the official site (seoulfashionweek.org or english.seoul.go.kr) for the season that fits your trip — dates shift by one to two weeks each year.

Mistake 3. "DDP is only worth visiting during Fashion Week"

DDP — Dongdaemun Design Plaza — is a permanently open exhibition space. Beyond SFW, it hosts the Seoul Design Festival, curated exhibitions, and year-round activity in the surrounding Dongdaemun fashion district. Pair it with the #583 Dongdaemun sub for more than half a day of content on DDP alone.

12 picks, 4 axes — the deep dive

Axis 1: Seoul Fashion Week and DDP (3 picks)

1. DDP Seoul Fashion Week — the main venue

  • Location: DDP — Dongdaemun Design Plaza — 281 Eulji-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul
  • Transit: Subway lines 2, 4, 5, Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station, exit 1 (1-minute walk)
  • Season: February F/W, September S/S — always confirm the official schedule
  • Facilities: Eoulim Square (Eoulim Square, 어울림광장) outdoors, Sallimteo (Sallimteo, 살림터) exhibition hall, Baeumteo (Baeumteo, 배움터) lecture hall, DDP Art Hall 1 and 2
  • Fit for foreign visitors: English program books distributed and English-speaking staff on site, but only during the SFW run
  • Areas open to the general public: Eoulim Square pop-ups and DDP's permanent exhibition halls (open beyond the SFW dates too)
  • Main runway shows: invitation, media, or buyer credentials required — no general walk-in access
  • Practical tip: SFW's official Instagram (@seoulfashionweek) posts separate announcements for open events and public programs, with an English account running in parallel
  • Recommended check: confirm the schedule and open programs on the official site each season

2. MÜNN opening show, 2026 F/W

  • Brand intro: MÜNN (MÜNN, 뮌) — a Korean genderless designer brand founded by designer Han Hyun-min (Han Hyun-min, 한현민), with a track record across Seoul, New York, and Paris
  • 2026 F/W role: opened the SFW 2026 F/W season
  • Why foreign visitors care: one of SFW's most closely watched shows, regularly covered by global media such as WWD and Vogue Korea
  • Viewing: invitation or media credentials required. General viewers can watch via SFW's official YouTube livestream or VOD instead, free of charge
  • Practical tip: check MÜNN's official Instagram for show footage and behind-the-scenes content

3. 15 runway shows plus 9 presentations (all 24 brands)

  • Scale: for 2026 F/W, 15 runway shows plus 9 static presentations (exhibition-style)
  • Presentation vs. show: presentations sometimes have more relaxed capacity limits than runway shows, making them relatively easier for foreign fashion professionals and press to access
  • How foreign enthusiasts can get in: check the "General Admission" or "Open Schedule" section on the official SFW site — some brands run separate open shows
  • Recommended check: which brands open their shows varies by season, so confirm through official announcements

Axis 2: K-fashion brands (3 picks)

4. 102 K-fashion brands — the full SFW participant ecosystem

  • Background: SFW ranks alongside Tokyo and Shanghai among Asia's major global fashion weeks. 102 K-fashion brands took part in 2026 F/W.
  • Buyer countries: 23 countries, 99 buyers, including global retail names such as Harvey Nichols (UK), Urban Outfitters (US), and Club21 (Singapore)
  • Why foreign visitors care: brands that show at SFW are designer labels actively pursuing global distribution. Their showrooms and concept stores can be visited around Seoul outside Fashion Week too.
  • Where to find their showrooms and concept stores: Seongsu-dong (Seongsu-dong, 성수동), which has a dense cluster of designer concept stores, plus Hannam-dong (Hannam-dong, 한남동) and Cheongdam-dong (Cheongdam-dong, 청담동). Recommended check: confirm individual store details via each brand's official social media.

5. Korean minimalism and quiet luxury, the 2026 F/W trend

  • 2026 F/W keywords: restrained silhouettes, genderless design, sustainable materials — a Korean take on minimalism that dovetails with the global "quiet luxury" movement
  • Why enthusiasts care: international fashion media such as Vogue and Business of Fashion have framed SFW as a showcase for "the refinement of K-fashion"
  • Practical tip: DDP's exhibition hall sometimes runs a parallel trend exhibition during SFW, which is open to the general public

6. Global buyers — Harvey Nichols, Urban Outfitters, Club21

  • Why it matters: SFW brands are already building ties with overseas retail networks, so for visiting foreigners it is a place to scout "brands I can still buy once I'm home"
  • Practical use: after SFW, some of these brands reach global markets through Harvey Nichols (London, Dubai, and others), Urban Outfitters (US, Europe), and Club21 (Southeast Asia). Note the brand names before you leave and check availability back home.

8. SFW's business scale — 300 attendees, an international fashion industry

  • 2026 F/W figure: roughly 300 domestic and international fashion industry professionals attended the Fashion Forum

Practical info — transit, booking, language

Getting to DDP

  • Subway: lines 2, 4, and 5 to Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station, exits 1-2 (within a 1-minute walk)
  • Bus: numerous routes stop at DDP. Search "DDP" on the English version of Google Maps to confirm.
  • Parking: DDP has an underground lot, but it gets crowded during SFW — public transit is recommended.
  • Late night: the last subway trains run until roughly 01:00 on weekdays. The Dongdaemun area around DDP is a 24-hour shopping district, so late-night movement is safe.

FAQ 6

Q1. Can foreign visitors see SFW's runway shows?

The main runway shows require invitation, media, or buyer credentials. General foreign visitors can enjoy the atmosphere through DDP's outdoor pop-ups, Eoulim Square, and SFW's official YouTube livestream. Some seasons run open shows, so check the official site.

Q2. Is there an admission fee?

The runway shows themselves run on free invitations. DDP's outdoor pop-ups are free. DDP's permanent exhibition halls charge admission (amount varies by exhibition, recommended check). Trazy packages are paid.

Q3. Can I get information in English?

Yes, full English support is available. seoulfashionweek.org's English version, english.seoul.go.kr, trazy.com, and visitkorea.or.kr/eng are all in English. SFW's official Instagram also runs English captions alongside Korean.

Q4. When are SFW's seasons?

Twice a year: February for F/W (fall/winter collections), September for S/S (spring/summer collections). Exact dates shift each year, so check the official site.

Q5. Is DDP worth visiting outside the SFW dates?

Absolutely. DDP is a tourist landmark in its own right, designed by Zaha Hadid. It runs permanent and curated exhibitions plus the Seoul Design Festival (November-December) year-round. Pair it with the #583 Dongdaemun fashion town for a full day's itinerary.

Q6. Can I buy K-fashion brands directly near Fashion Week?

Some brands sell products at DDP's outdoor pop-ups during SFW. After SFW, participating brands can be found at designer concept stores in Seongsu-dong, Hannam-dong, and Cheongdam-dong. Recommended check: pop-up participation varies by season.

Pain-point guide for foreign visitors

  • Language: SFW's official materials come in English. Korean dominates on-site, but English staff are placed during SFW. Tourist interpretation: call 1330 (English, Chinese, Japanese).
  • Payment: card payment runs 100% at DDP. Some old-school Dongdaemun shops and street stalls are cash-only.
  • VAT refund: use Tax Refund-marked stores in the Dongdaemun malls near DDP. Purchases over 50,000 won qualify for airport refund counters.
  • Emergency: 1339 (medical, English, 24h), 1330 (tourist interpretation), 112 (police), 119 (fire and ambulance)
