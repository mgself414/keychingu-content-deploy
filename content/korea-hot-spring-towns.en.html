Korea Hot Spring Towns and Hot Spring Trips Guide — the Hot Spring Areas of Onyang, Suanbo, Bugok and Baegam, the Mountainside Hot Springs of Deokgu, Osaek and Hagasan, and Jeju Sanbangsan Carbonated Hot Spring: 19 Places

Subtitle: A winter trip to regional hot spring areas and hot spring facilities, not Seoul jjimjilbang · Hot springs from the "My Own Jjimjilbang & Spa" theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" public vote, and hot spring areas registered as tourist attractions with the Korea Tourism Organization · Registered single-visit fees (adult basis) run from Andong Hagasan Hot Spring regular 6,500 won to Asan Spavis facility fee 50,000 won · Places with closed days are Suanbo Footbath Path, Hagasan Hot Spring, Geojedo Seawater Health Hot Spring, Bugok Footbath and Asan Spavis (2 weeks in March) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide covers 19 regional hot spring areas and hot spring facilities outside Seoul. Seoul and nearby jjimjilbang, Icheon Termeden (이천 테르메덴) and Busan Heosimcheong (부산 허심청) are in #749 Korea Winter Jjimjilbang Hot Spring Top 9, and Daejeon Yuseong (대전 유성) and Busan Dongnae (부산 동래) each have their own guide. #749 also briefly mentions Deokgu, Onyang, Suanbo and Asan Spavis, but the confirmation dates differ, so the values may differ. For example, the Deokgu Hot Spring Spa World peak season fee is written as 36,000 won in #749 (confirmed 2026-10-12) and as regular 38,000 won in this guide (confirmed 2026-11-19).

The places were chosen by two criteria. One is hot springs picked in the "My Own Jjimjilbang & Spa" (나만의 찜질방·스파) theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" (명소발굴 100×100) (candidates recommended by travel experts, voted on by the public) and selected from among them; the table lists the vote rank and vote count. For Baegam Hot Spring, the Special Tourist Zone itself placed 34th in the vote. The other is hot spring areas and facilities registered as tourist attractions with the Korea Tourism Organization that were not picked as an area in the vote (Onyang Hot Spring District, Suanbo Hot Spring Special Tourist Zone, Suanbo Hot Spring Footbath Path and Bugok Hot Spring Special Tourist Zone), marked "Area not in the vote" in the table.

Where the name in the vote differs from the Korea Tourism Organization name (for example, the vote's "The K Hotel Gyeongju Spa Hot Spring" = Korea Tourism Organization "The K Hotel Gyeongju"), they were matched by address and Korea Tourism Organization number. This guide does not include every hot spring picked in the vote, and some places, such as Yangyang Seolhaewon (양양 설해원) and Yesan Splash Resom (예산 스플라스 리솜), were not included.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI), and fields with no registered value are marked "No registered value". The disease-effect wording in the Korea Tourism Organization descriptions is not medical evidence, so it was not copied.

📍 Hot Spring Areas — Onyang, Suanbo, Bugok and Baegam

Onyang Hot Spring District (Oncheon-dong, Asan / 아산시 온천동, ☎ Onyangoncheon Station Tourist Information Center 041-540-2517) is an old hot spring area, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, records remain of several Joseon (조선) kings building hot spring palaces (온궁) and staying there. Bathhouses and lodging are clustered here, along with cultural heritage such as Sinjeongbi Monument (신정비) and Yeonggoedae (영괴대). Onyang Tourist Hotel Main Hot Spring Bath (온양관광호텔 대온천탕) in the same area placed 67th (82 votes) in the vote, but it is not listed separately because the Korea Tourism Organization has no operating information for it.

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: Registered as varying by hot spring.

Paradise Spa Dogo (176 Dogo-oncheon-ro, Dogo-myeon, Asan / 아산시 도고면 도고온천로 176, ☎ 041-537-7100) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, a large hot spring resort that is Chungcheong Province No. 1 of the National Health Hot Spring designated by the Ministry of the Interior and Safety. Across a total of 24,622㎡ it has a bade pool (바데풀), a main sulfur hot spring bathing hall and water play facilities, and in winter it is introduced as running acanthopanax baths (오가피탕), ginger baths (생강탕) and tangerine-peel baths (귤껍질탕).

  • Opening hours, fee: Registered as varying by season. Closed days: Open year-round.

Asan Spavis (67 Asan-oncheon-ro 157beon-gil, Eumbong-myeon, Asan / 아산시 음봉면 아산온천로157번길 67, ☎ 041-539-2000) is a full hot spring facility that uses Asan hot spring water. It has an indoor bade pool, an outdoor hot spring pool, a hot spring hall, a 75m wave pool and slides.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Open year-round (closed for 2 weeks in March every year). Fee: Registered as a facility fee of 50,000 won.

Suanbo Hot Spring Special Tourist Zone (12 Jujeongsan-ro, Suanbo-myeon, Chungju / 충주시 수안보면 주정산로 12, ☎ 043-846-3605) is an old hot spring resort. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is a weakly alkaline hot spring that comes from 250m underground, and Chungju City manages the hot spring water directly. Most visitors stay at hotels and motels in the area and bathe in hot springs, and at the center is Multang Park (물탕공원), where hot spring water flows. The tourist information center is registered as having maps in English, Chinese and Japanese. Suanbo Oncheon Land (수안보 온천랜드) in the same area placed 44th (94 votes) in the vote, but it is not listed separately because the Korea Tourism Organization has no operating information for it.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free (the Special Tourist Zone itself).

Suanbo Hot Spring Footbath Path (35 Jujeongsan-ro, Suanbo-myeon, Chungju / 충주시 수안보면 주정산로 35, ☎ Suanbo Tourist Information Center 043-845-7829) is a footbath facility where you soak your feet in Suanbo hot spring water. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the Annals of the Joseon Dynasty (조선왕조실록) record that Taejo Yi Seong-gye (태조 이성계) used the Suanbo hot spring.

  • Opening hours: Months 3~11 12:00~20:00, months 12~2 12:00~17:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday, and Monday and Tuesday in winter. Fee: Free.

Suanbo Park Hotel (36 Tapgol 1-gil, Suanbo-myeon, Chungju / 충주시 수안보면 탑골1길 36, ☎ 043-846-2331) is a hotel with an open-air hot spring bath, a natural hot spring and an indoor swimming pool. It has 111 rooms.

  • Check-in: 15:00, Check-out: 11:00. Hot spring hours and fee: No registered value.

Bugok Hot Spring Special Tourist Zone (Geomun-ri, Bugok-myeon, Changnyeong / 창녕군 부곡면 거문리, ☎ Changnyeong County Office Hot Spring Team 055-530-1591) was designated a Special Tourist Zone in 1981. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it was called Bugok (釜谷 / 부곡) because it was said to look like a cauldron, and hotels, condos and bathhouses are clustered here. There are two footbaths (Hanul Park / 한울공원 and Small Park / 소공원).

  • Footbath: Sat, Sun and public holidays 14:00~18:00. Closed in months 7~8, on January 1, and on the day of Seollal and Chuseok. Footbath fee: No registered value.
  • Lodging and bathhouses: Registered as varying by facility.

Baegam Hot Spring Special Tourist Zone (5 Oncheon-ro, Onjeong-myeon, Uljin / 울진군 온정면 온천로 5, ☎ 054-789-5480) was designated a Special Tourist Zone in 1979. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is a hot spring known since the Silla (신라) era, where you can see both the Baekdudaegan (백두대간) mountains and the East Sea together. The hot spring water is colorless and odorless.

  • Opening hours, closed days: Registered as varying by facility.

📍 Mountainside and Resort Hot Springs — Deokgu, Osaek, Cheoksan, Hagasan and Solsaem

Deokgu Hot Spring Spa World (924 Deokgu-oncheon-ro, Buk-myeon, Uljin / 울진군 북면 덕구온천로 924, ☎ 054-782-0677) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the Gyeongsangbuk-do No. 1 National Health Hot Spring recognized by the Ministry of Government Administration and Home Affairs (행정자치부, now the Ministry of the Interior and Safety), and a naturally flowing hot spring halfway up Eungbongsan (응봉산). It is introduced as neither heating nor cooling the hot spring water. English and Japanese guidance is registered as available.

  • Opening hours: Main hot spring hall 06:00~22:00, Spa World weekdays 10:00~19:00, weekends 10:00~20:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee (facility fee): Off-season weekday regular 26,000 won, off-season weekend regular 32,000 won, peak season regular 38,000 won.

Osaek Carbonated Hot Spring (34 Daecheongbong-gil, Seo-myeon, Yangyang / 양양군 서면 대청봉길 34, ☎ 033-670-0880) is a hot spring inside the Osaek Greenyard Hotel (오색그린야드호텔), 647m above sea level. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is the only complex hot spring hotel inside South Seoraksan National Park (남설악산 국립공원).

  • Opening hours: Weekdays 06:00~22:00, weekends 06:00~23:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: Adult 18,000 won, child (ages 8~13) 13,000 won. Registered as subject to change.

Cheoksan Hot Spring Resort Village (327 Gwangwang-ro, Sokcho / 속초시 관광로 327) is a hot spring lodging facility that opened in 1985. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it has 53℃ hot spring water, hot spring jacuzzis in the guest rooms, and guests can use the public hot spring once for free. It has a jjimjilbang (찜질방), a Geumgangsong pine (금강송) walking trail and an observation deck on the Seoraksan ridge.

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value.

Andong Hagasan Hot Spring (14 Hagasan-oncheon-gil, Seohu-myeon, Andong / 안동시 서후면 학가산온천길 14, ☎ 054-850-4690) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, on the southeast side of Hagasan (학가산), and is an alkaline hot spring from bedrock 690~940m underground that can hold around 1,200 people at once. The rooftop has a viewing facility for Hagasan and Cheondeungsan (천등산).

  • Opening hours: 06:00~20:00. Closed days: First and third Monday of every month, Seollal and Chuseok day.
  • Fee: Adult regular 6,500 won, child (ages 4~6) and senior (age 65 and over) 5,000 won. Registered as subject to change.

Sonobel Cheongsong Solsaem Hot Spring (38 Sono-ro, Juwangsan-myeon, Cheongsong / 청송군 주왕산면 소노로 38 소노벨 청송 지하3층, ☎ 1588-4888) is a hot spring inside a resort. It has a hinoki bath (히노끼탕) and underwater massage facilities.

  • Opening hours: 07:00~21:00 (break 13:00~14:00). Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: Adult 20,000 won, child 15,000 won.

📍 Southern Hot Springs — Gyeongju, Changwon, Geoje and Gochang

The K Hotel Gyeongju (45 Expo-ro, Gyeongju / 경주시 엑스포로 45, ☎ 054-745-8100) is a hotel in the Bomun Tourist Complex (보문단지), and according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, you can combine sightseeing at historic sites with hot spring bathing. It has a hot spring sauna and indoor and outdoor swimming pools, and 267 rooms. In the vote it was listed as "The K Hotel Gyeongju Spa Hot Spring" (더케이호텔경주 스파온천).

  • Check-in: 15:00, Check-out: 11:00. Hot spring hours and fee: No registered value.

Changwon Mageumsan Wontang (1167 Cheonju-ro, Buk-myeon, Uichang-gu, Changwon / 창원시 의창구 북면 천주로 1167, ☎ 055-298-4400) is also called Bukmyeon Hot Spring (북면온천). According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is a weakly alkaline hot spring with a water temperature of 55℃ or higher, and the hot spring water is recorded in the Dongguk Yeoji Seungnam (동국여지승람). It has a family bath for 2 people. The Korea Tourism Organization data was last modified in March 2025, so the fees and hours may have changed.

  • Opening hours: Hot spring 05:00~21:00, family bath 24 hours. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: Public bath adult 8,000 won, family bath (daytime rental) 2 hours for 2 people 25,000 won. Registered as not allowing mixed-gender bathing in the public bath for ages 4 and over.

Geojedo Seawater Health Hot Spring (507 Suyang-ro, Geoje / 거제시 수양로 507, ☎ 0507-1432-3011) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the No. 10 National Health Hot Spring in Korea designated by the Ministry of the Interior and Safety. It is a weakly alkaline, weakly saline hot spring from bedrock 800m underground, with an indoor hot spring, a family hot spring, a jjimjilbang and a swimming pool.

  • Opening hours: 05:00~22:30. Closed days: Second Friday of every month.
  • Fee: Regular 10,000 won, family bath regular 40,000 won. Registered as reservable for the family hot spring only.

Gochang Seokjeong Hot Spring Hyu Spa (173 Seokjeong 2-ro, Gochang-eup, Gochang / 고창군 고창읍 석정2로 173, ☎ 063-560-7500) is a hot spring that uses germanium-containing hot spring water. It has an indoor spa and a summer outdoor spa.

  • Opening hours: Hot spring weekdays 06:00~20:00, weekends 06:00~21:00, off-season indoor spa 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: Hot spring 12,000 won, off-season indoor spa 28,000 won.

📍 Jeju Carbonated Hot Springs — Sanbangsan and Orev

Jeju Sanbangsan Carbonated Hot Spring (192 Sagyebuk-ro 41beon-gil, Andeok-myeon, Seogwipo / 서귀포시 안덕면 사계북로41번길 192, ☎ 064-792-8300) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, a carbonated hot spring that is hard to find in Korea. The ceiling and walls are glass, so you can bathe while looking at the Jeju scenery. It placed 4th in the vote for this theme.

  • Opening hours: Indoor hot spring 06:00~23:00, jjimjilbang 06:00~22:00, open-air bath (swimming pool) 10:00~22:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: Indoor hot spring adult 14,000 won, child 7,000 won, open-air bath 5,000 won, jjimjilbang 2,000 won.

Orev Hot Spring & Spa (152 Taepyeong-ro, Seogwipo / 서귀포시 태평로 152, ☎ 064-901-3300) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, a hot spring spa that is the only one in Jeju designated as a health hot spring by the Ministry of the Interior and Safety. It uses carbonated hot spring water from 2,003m underground, and has 8 open-air baths and an outdoor infinity pool.

  • Opening hours: Hot spring hall 10:00~22:00 (last ticket sales 21:00). Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: Registered as changing by period, with a note to inquire by phone.

⚠ 3 Things to Know in Advance

  1. Check the closed days — Suanbo Hot Spring Footbath Path closes every Monday (Monday and Tuesday in winter), Hagasan Hot Spring on the first and third Monday of every month, and Geojedo Seawater Health Hot Spring on the second Friday of every month. Bugok Footbath closes in months 7~8 and on January 1, Seollal and Chuseok day, and Asan Spavis closes for 2 weeks in March every year.
  2. Fees can change — Seasonal fees are registered for Deokgu Hot Spring and Seokjeong Hot Spring, and a "subject to change" notice is registered for Osaek Carbonated Hot Spring and Hagasan Hot Spring. Paradise Spa Dogo and Orev are registered only as varying by season or by period.
  3. Health effects are not in this guide — The disease-effect wording in the Korea Tourism Organization descriptions was not copied. If you have a medical condition, consult a doctor before use.

⚙ FAQ 3

Q. Which places are easy to reach from the Seoul metropolitan area?

A. The places in this guide that are in the same central region as the Seoul metropolitan area are Asan in Chungnam (Onyang Hot Spring District, Paradise Spa Dogo and Spavis) and Suanbo in Chungju, Chungbuk. There is no registered travel time, so check with a route-finding app. For jjimjilbang near Seoul, see #749 Korea Winter Jjimjilbang Hot Spring Top 9.

Q. Where can I try a hot spring without spending money?

A. Suanbo Hot Spring Footbath Path is registered as free. Bugok Hot Spring Special Tourist Zone also has two footbaths, but there is no registered fee value.

Q. How can I combine a hot spring with lodging?

A. There are Suanbo Park Hotel (open-air hot spring bath), Cheoksan Hot Spring Resort Village (one free public hot spring visit for guests), The K Hotel Gyeongju (hot spring sauna) and Osaek Carbonated Hot Spring (hot spring inside the hotel).
