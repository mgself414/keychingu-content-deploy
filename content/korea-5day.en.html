5-day course for foreigners’ first visit — compressed + 2 days and 1 night in Busan

> Subtitle: #228 7 days → 5 days compressed + 2 days and 1 night in Busan added Variant

ISSUE 03 · #265 · 2026-05-20

🎯 Why 5-day course for foreigners

Balanced itinerary for foreigners inbound to Korea = 4 nights and 5 days (1st priority for long-distance foreigners, MZ). 3 days in Seoul + 2 days and 1 night in Busan or 5 days in Seoul deep dive full set.

★ 5-day full set matrix

Option A — 3 days in Seoul + 2 days and 1 night in Busan (No. 1 destination for foreign enthusiasts)

Day 1 — Arrival + Myeongdong·N Seoul Tower

  • Incheon Airport → Airport Railroad → Myeongdong, N Seoul Tower, duty free

Day 2 — Palace, Hanok, Hongdae

  • Gyeongbokgung Palace, Hanbok, Bukchon, Ikseon-dong, Hongdae

Day 3 — KTX Busan → Haeundae

  • 09:00 KTX Seoul → Busan (#239) → Haeundae (#243) + Gwangalli night view → Busan 1 night

Day 4 — Busan Jagalchi·Gamcheon·Return

  • Jagalchi (#244) + Gamcheon (#245) → KTX Busan → Seoul → Jamsil (#82)

Day 5 — Gangnam, Han River, Departure

  • Gangnam, HYBE, Banpo Han River, Incheon Airport

Option B — 5-day deep dive in Seoul (City Mania)

  • Day 1 Myeongdong·N Seoul Tower / Day 2 Palace·Hanok / Day 3 Gangnam·Hongdae / Day 4 Seongsu·Itaewon / Day 5 Han River·Departure

⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make in 5 days

1. "5 days = Can't go to Busan": 5 days = Busan 2 days and 1 night possible (KTX 2.5h) + No. 1 destination for foreign enthusiasts.

2. "5 days = K-pop is diverse": 5 days K-pop = HYBE·SM·YG·KARTS·No. 1 foreign mania full set.

3. "5 days = too tight": 5 days = Busan + No. 1 foreign mania full set + Rest OK.

Variant 5 days

  • A Seoul + Busan (standard in this guide): KTX 1 night 2 days Busan
  • B Seoul deep dive: 5 deep dives out of 25 districts
  • C Family·Children (#232): Children’s Grand Park, Lotte World, Seoul Forest, Aquarium, NMK
  • D K-pop Mania (#221): HYBE·SM·YG·KARTS·KBS·MBC
  • E Food Mania: Gwangjang·Noryangjin·Gangnam·Hongdae·Jeonju (#255) 1 night
