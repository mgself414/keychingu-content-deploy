First-Time Visitor 5-Day Itinerary — Condensed + Busan (부산) 1 Night 2 Days

🎯 Why a 5-Day Itinerary for Foreign Visitors?

The balanced inbound itinerary for foreign visitors is 4 nights and 5 days (top choice for long-haul travelers and MZ tourists). This typically includes 3 days in Seoul (서울) + 1 night 2 days in Busan (부산), or a full 5-day deep dive into Seoul (서울).

★ 5-Day Full Set Matrix

Option A — Seoul (서울) 3 Days + Busan (부산) 1 Night 2 Days (Foreign Enthusiast's Top Choice)

Day 1 — Arrival + Myeongdong (명동) · N Seoul Tower (N서울타워)

  • Incheon International Airport (인천국제공항) → Airport Railroad (공항철도) → Myeongdong (명동) · N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) · Duty-Free

Day 2 — Palaces · Hanok (한옥) · Hongdae (홍대)

  • Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) · Hanbok (한복) · Bukchon (북촌) · Ikseondong (익선동) · Hongdae (홍대)

Day 3 — KTX Busan (부산) → Haeundae (해운대)

  • 09:00 (9 AM) KTX from Seoul (서울) → Busan (부산) ([#239 KTX Guide](239_korea_ktx_guide.md)) → Haeundae (해운대) ([#243 Busan Haeundae Guide](243_busan_haeundae_guide.md)) + Gwangalli (광안리) Night View → 1 Night in Busan (부산)

Day 4 — Busan (부산) Jagalchi (자갈치) · Gamcheon (감천) · Return to Seoul

  • Jagalchi (자갈치) ([#244 Busan Jagalchi Guide](244_busan_jagalchi_guide.md)) + Gamcheon (감천) ([#245 Busan Gamcheon Guide](245_busan_gamcheon_guide.md)) → KTX from Busan (부산) → Seoul (서울) → Jamsil (잠실) ([#82 Seoul Jamsil Guide](82_seoul_jamsil_guide.md))

Day 5 — Gangnam (강남) · Hangang (한강) · Departure

  • Gangnam (강남) · HYBE · Banpo Hangang Park (반포 한강공원) · Incheon International Airport (인천국제공항)

Option B — Seoul (서울) 5-Day Deep Dive (City Enthusiast)

  • Day 1 Myeongdong (명동) · N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) / Day 2 Palaces · Hanok (한옥) / Day 3 Gangnam (강남) · Hongdae (홍대) / Day 4 Seongsu (성수) · Itaewon (이태원) / Day 5 Hangang (한강) · Departure

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make on a 5-Day Trip

1. "5 days = Can't visit Busan (부산)": A 5-day trip can include 1 night 2 days in Busan (부산) (KTX 2.5 hours) + it's a top choice for foreign enthusiasts.

2. "5 days = Not enough K-pop variety": A 5-day K-pop trip can include a full set of HYBE · SM · YG · KARTS + it's a top choice for foreign enthusiasts.

3. "5 days = Too packed": A 5-day trip can be a full set including Busan (부산) + top choice for foreign enthusiasts + still allow for relaxation.

💡 Foreigner Pain Point Guide

  • Language: Prioritize stores with full English · Japanese · Chinese support
  • Payment: 80% card payment · Some nofo (노포, 30+ year traditional restaurant) may require cash
  • VAT Refund: Look for "Tax Refund" signs at stores · Refund for purchases over ₩50,000 (~$38 / ~260 RMB / ~5,000 JPY) · Airport refund counters
  • Emergency: ☎ 1339 (Medical · English) · ☎ 1330 (Tourism Interpretation) · ☎ 112 (Police) · ☎ 119 (Fire · Ambulance)
