🎯 Why Busan Haeundae·Gwangalli?

Busan (부산) = Korea's #1 Beach City + #1 inbound destination outside Seoul for foreigners. Haeundae (해운대) (Korea's #1 beach) + Gwangalli (광안리) (Gwangandaegyo Bridge (광안대교) night view) + Marine City (마린시티) (Korea's #1 skyscraper district) + Dalmaji-gil Road (달맞이길) and Dongbaekseom Island (동백섬) full package. ⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Busan (부산) = Haeundae (해운대) only": Busan's #1 full package for foreigners = Haeundae (해운대) + Gwangalli (광안리) + Marine City (마린시티) + Jagalchi (자갈치) (#244) + Gamcheon (감천) (#245) + Seomyeon (서면) (#246) + BIFF (#247), spread across 7 spots. A 2-3 day full package.

2. "Gwangandaegyo Bridge (광안대교) = only seen during the day": Gwangandaegyo Bridge (광안대교)'s #1 appeal for foreigners = Night View (19:00-22:00 / 7 PM-10 PM) + Gwangalli (광안리) white sand beach + combined with hoe (회, Korean raw fish sashimi) & beer. Busan's #1 night view for foreigner vlogs.

3. "Haeundae (해운대) = only for summer": Haeundae (해운대)'s #1 appeal for foreigners = Year-round + Summer (swimming) · Autumn (sunrise) · Winter (Haeundae's #1 sunrise) + deep dive into Marine City (마린시티) urban area.

4. "Marine City (마린시티) = compared to Dubai": Marine City (마린시티) = Korea's #1 skyscraper city (80+ stories including 100-story LCT) + #1 for Dubai/Hong Kong-loving foreigner Instagrammers. Busan's #1 for foreigner vlogs.

5. "Haeundae (해운대) hoe (회) = expensive": Haeundae (해운대) hoe (회) = Signiel (시그니엘) · Ramada (라마다) luxury + Haeundae Market (해운대 시장) + Hoe (회) / Hoe Restaurants (횟집) ₩50,000-₩150,000 (~$37-110 / ~260-780 RMB / ~5,500-16,500 JPY) per person standard for #1 value. Foreigner favorite for an authentic Busan hoe (회) deep dive.
