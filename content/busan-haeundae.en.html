Busan Haeundae·Gwangalli — 12 Spots: Beaches, Beach Train, Observatories and Drone Show, with Hours and Fares

Subtitle: For first-time visitors to Busan, this guide groups 12 spots in 3 zones: Haeundae, Dongbaekseom Island and Gwangalli. Haeundae Beach is 1.5 km of sand and Gwangalli Beach is 1.4 km. The Beach Train covers the 4.8 km from Mipo to Songjeong in about 25 minutes one way, and the Sky Capsule is charged per capsule. The Gwangalli M Drone Light Show runs on winter Saturdays at 19:00 and 21:00. Fares are the adult regular prices as seen on 2026-11-24.

ISSUE 06 · #243 · 2026-11-24 · Busan Haeundae·Gwangalli

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 12

This list is made for foreign travelers visiting Busan for the first time. Use it when you set aside a day or two from Seoul to see Busan's eastern seaside. It centers on two beaches and adds places reachable from them on foot or by Subway Line 2.

It is divided into 3 zones.

  • Haeundae zone (6 spots): Haeundae Beach (해운대해수욕장), Haeundae Blueline Park (해운대블루라인파크), Dalmaji-gil Road Moontan Road (달맞이길 문탠로드), BUSAN X the SKY (부산엑스더스카이), SEA LIFE Busan Aquarium (SEA LIFE 부산아쿠아리움), Haeundae Traditional Market (해운대전통시장).
  • Dongbaekseom Island zone (3 spots): Dongbaekseom Island (동백섬) Coastal Trail (해안산책로), Nurimaru APEC House (누리마루 APEC하우스), The Bay 101 (더베이101).
  • Gwangalli zone (3 spots): Gwangalli Beach (광안리해수욕장), Gwangalli M Drone Light Show (광안리 M 드론라이트쇼), Igidae Coastal Walk (이기대 해안산책로).

As of 2026-11-24 it is November. It is not swimming season. So this issue is written around walking, riding, viewpoints and night sights, not swimming. The Beach Train (해변열차) and Sky Capsule (스카이캡슐) run on the winter timetable for Nov–Feb, and the drone show is held at its winter times.

Four of the 12 spots received votes in the Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사) "100×100 Spot Discovery" (명소발굴 100×100) public vote. Each of those entries has one line with its votes.

✈ First, from Gimhae Airport to Haeundae

According to the Busan City English notice (read 2026-11-24), the Haeundae routes of the airport limousine were reorganized from the first bus on 2026-01-05. Fares are 9,500 won for adults, 7,600 won for teenagers and 4,800 won for children.

  • Jangsan–Haeundae–Airport route: 90 minutes. The first bus leaving the airport is 07:10 and the last is 16:50. Buses leaving Jangsan (장산) run from 05:30 to 19:00. Intervals toward Jangsan are 20–60 minutes.
  • Gijang–Haeundae–Airport route: 110 minutes. The first bus leaving the airport is 10:10 and the last is 21:40.
  • Where to get off: The stop name has changed to "Haeundae Beach (09116)".

The last Jangsan-route bus from the airport is 16:50. If you arrive later than that, take the Gijang (기장) route, which runs until 21:40. The two routes differ by 20 minutes in travel time.

🚦 Before You Walk — Shared Safety and Manners

  • November is not swimming season. In November, Haeundae and Gwangalli are places to walk the sand and look at the sea.
  • Change your plans if the weather is bad. The Beach Train runs normally in rain but does not run in heavy rain or typhoons. The drone show can be cancelled or delayed because of weather or technical problems.
  • The Igidae Coastal Walk has uphill sections. It is a 4.7 km route that takes about 2 hours 30 minutes. Wear sneakers and bring water.
  • The entrance to Dongbaekseom Island has a slope. The Korea Tourism Organization accessibility guide says there is a section of around 10 degrees at the entrance to the walking path.
  • Call 119 for accidents or emergencies and 112 to report a crime.

① Haeundae Zone — 6 Spots

This zone uses Subway Line 2 Haeundae Station and Jung-dong Station. You walk from Haeundae Station to the beach, aquarium and market. You walk to the observatory from Jung-dong Station, and you change to a village bus from Haeundae Station for Moontan Road. The Beach Train and Sky Capsule are boarded at Mipo Station.

Haeundae Beach

  • Size: Haeundae-gu Office gives the sand as 1.5 km long, 70–90 m wide, with an area of 120,000㎡. Going end to end and back is 3 km.
  • Night: According to the Haeundae-gu Office guide, the scenic lights are on every day of the year from after sunset until midnight. During swimming season it is until 02:00.
  • Season: November is not swimming season. Plan around walking the sand and looking at the sea.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is an 11 minute walk (688 m) from Exit 5 of Subway Line 2 Haeundae Station.
  • From the airport: The stop name of the Haeundae route of the airport limousine is "Haeundae Beach (09116)".

Haeundae Blueline Park — Beach Train · Sky Capsule

These are two rides that run along the seaside rail line. The Beach Train goes from Mipo (미포) to Songjeong (송정), and the Sky Capsule goes from Mipo to Cheongsapo (청사포). The values below are as shown on the official Blueline Park website on 2026-11-24.

Beach Train

  • Route: It makes a round trip of 4.8 km from Mipo through Cheongsapo to Songjeong. It runs at an average of 15 km per hour and takes about 25 minutes one way. It runs 3 times an hour.
  • Stations (cumulative distance on the official website): Mipo (0.3 km, terminus) → Dalmaji Tunnel (0.8 km) → Haewol Observatory (1.1 km) → Cheongsapo (2.3 km) → Daritdol Observatory (2.9 km) → Gudeokpo (3.4 km) → Songjeong (4.8 km, terminus).
  • Operating hours: In November (winter season, Nov–Feb) it is 09:00–19:00. The last train leaving Mipo and Songjeong is 19:00. The shoulder season (Mar, Apr, Oct) is 09:00–19:30, the peak season (May, Jun, Sep) is 09:00–20:30, and the high peak season (Jul, Aug) is 09:00–21:30.
  • Weather: It runs normally in rain. It does not run in heavy rain or typhoons.

Sky Capsule

  • Route: Mipo to Cheongsapo, 2.0 km. It travels at an average of 4 km per hour on rails 7–10 m high. One way takes about 30 minutes. 80 four-seat capsules run on the line.
  • Operating hours: Winter season 08:30–18:00. Shoulder season 08:30–18:30, peak season 08:30–19:30, high peak season 08:30–20:30.

Dalmaji-gil Road Moontan Road

  • Public vote: "Haeundae Dalmaji-gil Road" received 130 votes and ranked 16th in the Korea Tourism Organization 100×100 Spot Discovery public vote on the running course theme.
  • Tell the two paths apart:
  • Dalmaji-gil Road: Haeundae-gu Office describes it as an 8 km drive course on the mid-slope of Mt. Wausan, on the way from Haeundae Beach to Songjeong Beach. It has more than 15 bends and is also called the 15-bend road. At the top are Dalmaji Hill, Haewoljeong Pavilion (1997) and the Millennium Commemorative Clock Tower (2000).
  • Moontan Road: This is the walking path. Visit Busan describes it as a 2.2 km loop that takes a full hour for one lap.
  • Lighting: The Moontan Road lights are on from after sunset until 23:00, and from 5:00 until sunrise.
  • How to get there (Visit Busan guide):
  • From Exit 1 of Line 2 Haeundae Station, take village bus Haeundae-gu 2 or Haeundae-gu 10, get off at "Haewoljeong Entrance·Hillside Supermarket", and walk 7 minutes.
  • Take bus 200 or the late-night bus 141, get off at "Dongbaek Elementary School", and walk 9 minutes.
  • The "Dalmaji-gil Road" stop on the City Tour Bus Haeundae Beach (Blue Line) route.
  • Entrance address: 190 Dalmaji-gil, Haeundae-gu, Busan.

BUSAN X the SKY

  • What it is: An observatory on floors 98–100 of the LCT Landmark Tower of the LCT complex. The operator describes the building as the second tallest in Korea (411.6 m).
  • Operating hours: Mon–Sun 10:00–21:00. Weekdays, weekends and holidays are the same. On-site ticket sales close 30 minutes before closing. The operator states that hours can change without prior notice.
  • Fare (official website, 2026-11-24): Adult (age 13 and over) 29,000 won, child (36 months to under age 13) 26,000 won. People aged 65 and over pay the child fare, and children under 36 months are free.
  • Ticket conditions: Valid only on the day, and you can enter only at the date and time printed on the ticket.
  • Booking: Tickets are booked through Naver Reservation.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is a 15 minute walk (889 m) from Exit 7 of Line 2 Jung-dong Station. The address is 30 Dalmaji-gil, Haeundae-gu, Busan (Jung-dong).
  • Inquiries: 051-731-0098, 051-731-0099.

SEA LIFE Busan Aquarium

  • Operating hours (official website, 2026-11-24): Mon–Fri 10:00–19:00 with last entry at 18:00. Sat·Sun 10:00–20:00 with last entry at 19:00. These can change depending on on-site conditions and weather.
  • Fare note: Children under 36 months are free. It says that booking online in advance gives up to 31% off.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is a 9 minute walk (566 m) from Exit 5 of Line 2 Haeundae Station. The address is 266 Haeundaehaebyeon-ro, Haeundae-gu, Busan (Jung-dong).
  • Inquiries: ARS 051-740-1700.

Haeundae Traditional Market

  • Location (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-24): 22-1 Gunam-ro 41beon-gil, Haeundae-gu, Busan.
  • Opening hours (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-24): Daily 09:00–22:00. They may differ by shop.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is a 7 minute walk (456 m) from Exit 3 of Line 2 Haeundae Station.

② Dongbaekseom Island Zone — 3 Spots

All 3 spots are reached by leaving Subway Line 2 Dongbaek Station through Exit 1. If you go around the walking path once, Nurimaru APEC House comes up along the way.

Dongbaekseom Island Coastal Trail

  • Public vote: Dongbaekseom Island received 136 votes and ranked 10th in the Korea Tourism Organization 100×100 Spot Discovery public vote on the running course theme.
  • Distance: Visit Busan gives the coastal trail around Dongbaekseom Island as "about 950 m".
  • Walking order: The first viewing deck, the Mermaid Statue, the Swaying Bridge, the observatory and white lighthouse, then Nurimaru APEC House.
  • Slope: According to the Korea Tourism Organization accessibility guide, there is a section of around 10 degrees at the entrance to the path, and the ramps to the rest areas and viewing decks are 5–9 degrees.
  • Hours · fee: Always open, year-round, with no admission fee.
  • How to get there: Leave Line 2 Dongbaek Station through Exit 1. Visit Busan gives it as a 17 minute walk from Exit 1. By bus, take 1003 and get off at "Dongbaekseom".
  • Facilities: There are toilets. The Busan City Nurimaru guide says the Dongbaek Park annex parking lot is paid, and that parking around Dongbaekseom Island is limited and fills up on weekends and holidays, so it recommends public transport.

Nurimaru APEC House

  • Operating hours: 09:00–18:00. Entry closes 1 hour before closing. You must arrive before 17:00 to get in.
  • Closed: The first Monday of each month.
  • Fare: Free.
  • How to get there: Line 2 Dongbaek Station Exit 1, or the bus 1003 "Dongbaekseom" stop. You meet it at the end of the Dongbaekseom Island Coastal Trail.
  • Inquiries: 051-743-1974~5.

The Bay 101

  • Location (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-24): 52 Dongbaek-ro, Haeundae-gu, Busan.
  • Opening hours (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-24): Daily 08:00–24:00.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is a 13 minute walk (751 m) from Exit 1 of Line 2 Dongbaek Station.
  • How to combine it: It uses the same station and the same exit as Dongbaekseom Island. See Nurimaru (closes at 18:00) and the walking path first, then stop by in the evening. The 24:00 closing time is the Kakao Map registered value.

③ Gwangalli Zone — 3 Spots

This zone is Gwangalli Beach, the Gwangalli M Drone Light Show and the Igidae Coastal Walk. The Igidae Coastal Walk is south of Gwangalli, on the coast of Busan's Nam-gu (남구). The station is different too. Gwangalli is served by Geumnyeonsan Station and Gwangan Station, while for Igidae you change to a bus at Kyungsung Univ.·Pukyong Nat'l Univ. Station.

Gwangalli Beach

  • Public vote: Gwangalli Beach received 148 votes and ranked 4th in the Korea Tourism Organization 100×100 Spot Discovery public vote on the running course theme.
  • Size: Busan City gives it as "total area 82,000㎡, beach length 1.4 km, beach width 25–110 m". Going end to end and back is 2.8 km.
  • Hours · fee: Visit Busan gives it as always open, year-round and free. It describes walking and cycling on the beach theme street, and performances held in summer.
  • Night: Visit Busan says the lights of Gwangandaegyo Bridge (광안대교) change color every second.
  • Season: November is not swimming season.
  • How to get there: Exit guidance differs by agency.
  • Suyeong-gu guide: Subway Line 2 Geumnyeonsan Station Exit 3, 5 minutes on foot, Gwangan Station Exit 3, 10 minutes on foot.
  • Visit Busan guide: Line 2 Gwangan Station Exit 5, about 13 minutes on foot.
  • Bus: Suyeong-gu says to take 41, 49, 38, 42, 51, 62, 83, 83-1 or 108-1 and get off at "Gwangalli Beach", then 3 minutes. Visit Busan lists 42 and 62.
  • Address: Around 219 Gwangan Haebyeon-ro, Suyeong-gu, Busan (Gwangan-dong).
  • Parking: Visit Busan guides to "nearby public parking lots".
  • Nearby: Visit Busan lists the Gwangandaegyo Bridge night view, Millak Raw Fish Street (민락횟집거리), the Waterside Park, and the Namcheon-dong Cherry Blossom Street as nearby sights.

Gwangalli M Drone Light Show

  • When: According to the Busan City guide, regular performances are held every Saturday. In the winter season of Oct–Feb there are two shows, at 19:00 and 21:00.
  • Cancellation conditions: It can be cancelled or delayed because of weather or technical problems.
  • Day of the week: There are no regular performances on weekdays and Sundays. Plan around whether your Busan itinerary includes a Saturday.
  • How to get there: Measured from Gwangalli Beach, it is a 5 minute walk from Exit 3 of Geumnyeonsan Station (Suyeong-gu guide).
  • Fitting the timing: To see the 19:00 show, you run into the last winter Beach Train at 19:00. Ride Blueline Park in the daytime and come over to Gwangalli in the evening.

Igidae Coastal Walk

  • Public vote: "Busan Igidae Coastal Walk" received 125 votes and ranked 2nd in the Korea Tourism Organization 100×100 Spot Discovery public vote on the trail running course theme.
  • Location: It is the southern coast of Gwangalli. The administrative district is Busan's Nam-gu.
  • Section · distance: Visit Busan gives it as 4.7 km in total from Dongsaengmal (동생말) through Eoulmadang (어울마당) and Nongbawi (농바위) to Oryukdo Islets Ferry Terminal (오륙도선착장), about 2 hours 30 minutes on foot.
  • Nature of the path: It has uphill sections. Treat it as a path that needs more time and stamina than a stroll on the sand.
  • Hours · fee: Always open and free.
  • How to get there: Change to a bus at Subway Line 2 Kyungsung Univ.·Pukyong Nat'l Univ. Station. The buses are 20, 22, 24, 27, 39 and 131, and the village buses are Nam-gu 2 and Nam-gu 8.
  • Parking: There is an Igidae public parking lot, and it is paid.
  • Planning: It is a path where you enter at one end and leave at the other. If you combine it with Gwangalli, set aside half a day, walk it in the daytime, and return to Gwangalli in the evening.

🗺 Routes — Half Day and Full Day

The order is set to match each spot's opening and closing times. The durations the agencies give are about 30 minutes for the capsule, about 25 minutes one way for the train, a full hour for Moontan Road, and about 2 hours 30 minutes for Igidae.

Half Day A — Haeundae by Day (any day of the week)

  1. Line 2 Haeundae Station Exit 5 → Haeundae Beach (11 minutes on foot by Kakao Map directions). Walk the 1.5 km of sand.
  2. One of SEA LIFE Busan Aquarium or BUSAN X the SKY, both opening at 10:00.
  3. Mipo Station → Sky Capsule to Cheongsapo (about 30 minutes).
  4. Cheongsapo → Beach Train to Mipo or Songjeong. The single ticket from Cheongsapo is 8,000 won.
  5. Return toward Haeundae Station and visit Haeundae Traditional Market (open until 22:00 by the Kakao Map registered value).

In the winter season the Sky Capsule runs until 18:00, and the Beach Train's last departure from Mipo and Songjeong is 19:00. Do not push steps 3 and 4 late into the afternoon.

Half Day B — Dongbaekseom Island to Gwangalli in the Evening (Saturday)

  1. Line 2 Dongbaek Station Exit 1 → Dongbaekseom Island Coastal Trail, about 950 m.
  2. Nurimaru APEC House. Go in before 17:00. It is closed on the first Monday.
  3. Dongbaek Station → Line 2 Geumnyeonsan Station Exit 3 → Gwangalli Beach (5 minutes on foot).
  4. 19:00 Gwangalli M Drone Light Show. If you miss it, there is one more show at 21:00.

If it is not a Saturday, drop step 4, and you can swap step 3 for The Bay 101 (open until 24:00 by the Kakao Map registered value).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Thinking the Sky Capsule fare is per person — The Sky Capsule is priced per capsule: 50,000 won for the 2-seater, 55,000 won for the 3-seater, 60,000 won for the 4-seater. With 4 people it comes to about 15,000 won each, but with 2 people about 25,000 won each. A Beach Train single ticket also cannot be used again once you get off, so to get off at a middle station and continue, buy the all-station pass (16,000 won).
  2. Going to see the drone show on just any evening — Regular shows are on Saturdays, and in winter there are two, at 19:00 and 21:00. It can be cancelled or delayed because of weather or technical problems. Do not stack other bookings on a Saturday night.
  3. Treating Igidae as a short walk next to Gwangalli — The Igidae Coastal Walk is the Nam-gu coast south of Gwangalli, and it takes about 2 hours 30 minutes for 4.7 km. It also has uphill sections. You reach it by changing to a bus from the station. Dalmaji-gil Road is similar. Dalmaji-gil Road itself is an 8 km drive course, and the walking path is Moontan Road, a 2.2 km loop.

⚙ 3 FAQs

  1. Is Haeundae worth visiting in November? — November is not swimming season, so no in-the-sea plans are made. Instead, indoor spots and rides open on winter hours. SEA LIFE Busan Aquarium is open until 19:00 Mon–Fri and 20:00 Sat·Sun, and BUSAN X the SKY until 21:00 daily. The Beach Train and Sky Capsule run on the winter timetable (until 19:00 and 18:00). The Haeundae Beach scenic lights are on from after sunset to midnight, and Gwangalli Beach is always open.
  2. Which places in the table charge a fare and which are free? — The places that charge are BUSAN X the SKY (adult 29,000 won), Haeundae Blueline Park (Beach Train single ticket 8,000–10,000 won, Sky Capsule 50,000–60,000 won per capsule) and SEA LIFE Busan Aquarium. The places that are free or have no admission fee are Gwangalli Beach, the Dongbaekseom Island Coastal Trail, Nurimaru APEC House and the Igidae Coastal Walk. All fares are values seen on 2026-11-24.
  3. How do I get from Gimhae Airport to Haeundae? — There is the Haeundae route of the airport limousine. It costs 9,500 won for adults, and you get off at the "Haeundae Beach (09116)" stop. The routes were reorganized on 2026-01-05, and it now runs on two branches: toward Jangsan (90 minutes, 07:10–16:50 from the airport) and toward Gijang (110 minutes, 10:10–21:40 from the airport).
