Locals' Picks ⑧ Seoul — 29 Eateries Korean Viewers Named, in the Seoul Outside the Tourist Circuit

Subtitle: KeyChinGu's Seoul coverage is already dense neighborhood by neighborhood — this piece is the opposite. The 29 Seoul eateries Korean viewers named in YouTube comments are scattered across residential pockets in 16 districts tourists rarely visit, places like Changdong, Myeonmok-dong, Galhyeon-dong, and Nangok, and KeyChinGu checked only existence, address, and business type on Naver. Downtown/NW 9 → NE Seoul 5 → Gangnam/Songpa 8 → SW Seoul 7, one station name and one foreign-visitor tip per group. Neither the source nor the neighborhoods overlap with the Jongno/Gwanghwamun civil-servant eateries piece (#1191).

ISSUE 06 · #1389 · 2026-09-18 · Locals' Picks ⑧

🎯 Why "Comment Recommendations" Is the Bar

This corner has one source. A Korean YouTube channel posted a "nationwide matjip" video, viewers left comments naming their neighborhood eateries, and the most systematically organized comments by region got turned into a table of 217 spots. Since it's a list Koreans recommended to Koreans, foreign reviews are nearly nonexistent. This corner's criteria and limits are covered in Part ① (#1382).

KeyChinGu checked only whether the name, address, and business type actually exist on Naver local search (2026-09-17). Of 30 Seoul spots, 29 were confirmed; the 1 that couldn't be confirmed (Hyehwa-dong) was dropped. There are no visit reviews, rankings, or star ratings, and no hours or prices either.

Seoul was the toughest region in this corner. KeyChinGu alone already runs hundreds of Seoul pieces, and neighborhood-by-neighborhood guides are already dense. But plotting the roundup's 29 Seoul spots on a map showed they're scattered across 16 districts, most of them off the tourist circuit — Changdong, Bulamsan, Myeonmok-dong, Galhyeon-dong, Nangok, Wolgok, Singil. No Myeong-dong, no Hongdae. This piece carries that distribution over as-is. Two spots overlap with earlier pieces, and that's disclosed in the verification notes.

⚠ Notation principle: Each store line follows Name (Romanization) · street address · Naver category · access. Access lists only confirmed stations; where the station is split between two options or couldn't be pinned down, it's left as "unconfirmed." Walking minutes are given only for confirmed cases — everything else stops at "walking distance." Hours, closed days, and prices are entirely uncollected — check Naver Place the day you go.

① Downtown & Northwest — 9 Spots, Right Next to the Sights but Never on the List

Jongno, Jung-gu, Mapo, Yongsan, Eunpyeong. This is the easiest group to reach by subway, which makes it the easiest to slot into a trip.

  • Hapcheon Dwaeji Gukbap (합천돼지국밥) · 418-3 Samil-daero, Jongno-gu (종로구 삼일대로 418-3) · Korean · gukbap · Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역), Line 1/3/5, walking distance — its secondary station name is Tapgol Park

Dwaeji gukbap (돼지국밥) is known as a Busan/Gyeongnam dish (#1320, #1382), yet a shop carrying that name in the roundup sits right in downtown Seoul.

  • Maeun Jokbal (매운족발) · 23 Jongno 51-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 종로51길 23) · Korean · jokbal/bossam · Dongdaemun Station (동대문역), Line 1/4, ~2 min on foot. It's in Changsin-dong, but Dongdaemun Station is closer than Changsin Station

Jokbal (족발) is pig foreleg braised in soy sauce and sliced. This shop is the spicy-sauce version. It's the same shop that appeared in KeyChinGu's Line 4 and Line 6 station-by-station pieces (#1243, #1245).

  • Manna Son Kalguksu (만나손칼국수) · 11 Seoae-ro 1-gil, Jung-gu (중구 서애로1길 11) · Korean · noodles · Chungmuro Station (충무로역), Line 3/4, walking distance

Chungmuro is a neighborhood of print shops and old film-company offices. It's an easy stop on the way to Namsan.

  • Gyegogijip (계고기집) · 1 Baekbeom-ro 24-gil, Mapo-gu (마포구 백범로24길 1) · Korean · chicken dishes · Gongdeok Station (공덕역), Line 5/6, AREX, Gyeongui-Jungang Line, walking distance

Since AREX stops here, it's a direct ride from Incheon Airport — a realistic spot for a meal on arrival day.

  • Yegang Seolnongtang (예강설농탕) · 30 Sinchon-ro 12-gil, Mapo-gu (마포구 신촌로12길 30) · Korean · gomtang/seolleongtang · Sinchon Station (신촌역), Line 2, ~6 min on foot

Seolleongtang (설렁탕) is a milky broth simmered for hours from beef bones. You season it yourself with salt and scallions at the table.

  • Yukjeupteo (육즙터) · 8 Samgae-ro 5-gil, Mapo-gu (마포구 삼개로5길 8) · Korean · grilled pork · Mapo Samgae-ro — nearest station not confirmed — Mapo Station (마포역) or Gongdeok Station candidates. Check the route on Naver Map first
  • Jeongnim Sikdang (정림식당) · 48-9 Baekbeom-ro 87-gil, Yongsan-gu (용산구 백범로87길 48-9) · Restaurant · Korean · Namyeong Station (남영역), Line 1, walking distance, also within the Sookmyung Women's Univ. Station commercial strip
  • Minsok Sutbul Galbi (민속숯불갈비) · 303-12 Yeonseo-ro, Eunpyeong-gu (은평구 연서로 303-12) · Korean · meat dishes · Bulgwang/Yeonsinnae area — which of the two stations is closer is not confirmed
  • Halmeoni Tteokbokki (할머니떡볶이) · 8-1 Tongil-ro 87-gil, Eunpyeong-gu (은평구 통일로87길 8-1) · Bunsik · tteokbokki · Galhyeon-dong, Yeonsinnae Station (연신내역), Line 3/6 area

The name is common — it's a different shop from Sindang-dong's famous instant-tteokbokki original (#810).

Foreign-visitor tip — Gukbap, seolleongtang, and kalguksu can be ordered solo; galbi and grilled meat usually start at 2 servings. The salt, pepper, and scallions on a seolleongtang table are there for you to season yourself.

② Northeast Seoul — 5 Spots, Neighborhoods at the End of the Line

Seongdong, Seongbuk, Dobong, Nowon, Jungnang. Apart from Seongsu on Line 2, these are districts tourists rarely visit.

  • Milbon (밀본) · 4 Achasan-ro 7-gil, Seongdong-gu (성동구 아차산로7길 4) · Korean · kalguksu/mandu · Seongsu Station (성수역), Line 2 area

Seongsu-dong is a neighborhood KeyChinGu has covered many times (#01), but this shop is a kalguksu place, not a cafe or pop-up.

  • Dumesangol Ssambap (두메산골쌈밥) · 119-9 Hwarang-ro, Seongbuk-gu (성북구 화랑로 119-9) · Restaurant · Korean · Wolgok Station (월곡역), Line 6 area

Ssambap (쌈밥) is rice and meat wrapped in lettuce or perilla leaves.

  • Jongno Jumak (종로주막) · 15 Madeul-ro 11ga-gil, Dobong-gu (도봉구 마들로11가길 15) · Restaurant · Korean · Changdong Station (창동역), Line 1/4, walking distance

The name has "Jongno" in it, but it's actually in Changdong, Dobong-gu. It's a Line 1/4 transfer station, so access is easy.

  • Tojong Hanu Saenggogi Maeul (토종한우생고기마을) · 812 Deokneung-ro, Nowon-gu (노원구 덕릉로 812) · Naver classifies it as a butcher shop · Bulamsan Station (불암산역), Line 4

It's likely the "butcher-restaurant" (정육식당) format, where you buy meat at the counter and grill it on the spot, but this piece only states what's confirmed — the classification is butcher shop. Call ahead to confirm whether meals are served before you visit.

  • Jjajjaru (짜짜루) · 3 Yongmasan-ro 89-gil, Jungnang-gu (중랑구 용마산로89길 3) · Chinese · Chinese restaurant · Myeonmok-dong, Yongmasan Station (용마산역), Line 7 area — the station sits on high ground, so the walk can feel longer than it looks

Foreign-visitor tip — All five of these are in residential areas. Go in the evening and it'll be all locals, and there's a good chance there's no English menu. Photo-translating the menu or pointing at the next table's food is the fastest fix.

③ Gangnam & Songpa — 8 Spots (7 Shops), Between Office Blocks and Residential Streets

Gangnam, Seocho, Songpa. These districts overlap the tourist circuit, but most of the shops on this list serve office workers and residents.

  • Bokdoni Gamasot Sundaeguk (복돈이 가마솥순대국) · 22 Teheran-ro 37-gil, 1F, Gangnam-gu (강남구 테헤란로37길 22 1층) · Korean · sundae/sundaeguk · Yeoksam Station (역삼역), Line 2, ~8 min on foot
  • Yeoksam Ramen (역삼라멘) · 38 Eonju-ro 107-gil, 1F, Gangnam-gu (강남구 언주로107길 38 1층) · Japanese · ramen · Eonju Station (언주역), Line 9, walking distance

Two lunch spots for the Teheran-ro office district. Lines run long around noon on weekdays.

  • Sinmaekdeok (신맥덕) · 83-14 Bangbae-ro, B1F, Seocho-gu (서초구 방배로 83-14 지하1층) · Bar · beer/hof · Bangbae Station (방배역), Line 2, right nearby

Hof (호프) is the Korean term for a bar serving draft beer and anju (snacks). Evening menu.

  • China Bistro (차이나비스트로) · 20 Banpo-daero 3-gil, Seocho-gu (서초구 반포대로3길 20) · Chinese · Chinese restaurant · Both Seocho Station (서초역), Line 2, and Gyodae Station (교대역), Line 2/3, are walking distance — the two stations sit close enough that neither is clearly the one
  • Goindol (고인돌) · 4-1 Garak-ro 18-gil, Songpa-gu (송파구 가락로18길 4-1) · Japanese · donkkaseu · Songpa Station (송파역), Line 8, ~4-5 min on foot

Donkkaseu (돈가스) has become the flagship dish of Korean-style "gyeongyangsik" cafeteria food. The name is common, so it's easy to confuse with same-named shops elsewhere.

  • Pungnyeon Ppyeodagwi Haejangguk (풍년뼈다귀해장국) · 94 Garak-ro, Songpa-gu (송파구 가락로 94) · Korean · haejangguk · Same Garak-ro, Songpa Station area

Ppyeodagwi haejangguk (뼈다귀해장국) is soup simmered with pork spine bones, in the same family as gamjatang.

  • Yudamdeulbap Daonjae (유담들밥 다온재) · 135 Baekjegobun-ro, 1F Unit 101, Songpa-gu (송파구 백제고분로 135 1층 101호) · Korean · hanjeongsik · Samjeon Station (삼전역), Line 2 nearby

The roundup listed "Yudamdeulbap" and "Daonjae" separately, but they share the same address. On checking, they're registered across several platforms under a single name, "Yudamdeulbap Daonjae" — it's the same shop. It's also the same shop as the Jamsil entry in KeyChinGu's Korean hanjeongsik full guide (#234).

Foreign-visitor tip — Sundaeguk, haejangguk, and donkkaseu can be ordered solo; hanjeongsik usually starts at 2 people. Gangnam's office district is busiest 12-1pm on weekdays, so shifting by just 30 minutes makes it much easier.

④ Southwest Seoul — 7 Spots, Gwanak and Yeongdeungpo

Gwanak, Yeongdeungpo. These rank among the districts with the least foreign foot traffic in Seoul, which makes this group show the roundup's character clearly.

  • Gijeolchopung Wang Sundae (기절초풍 왕순대) · 2 Bongcheon-ro 62-gil, Gwanak-gu (관악구 봉천로62길 2) · Korean · sundae/sundaeguk · Nakseongdae Station (낙성대역), Line 2, walking distance

Sundae (순대) is pig intestine steamed and stuffed with glass noodles and vegetables. Nakseongdae is named after the birthplace of Goryeo general Gang Gam-chan.

  • Mirim Bunsik (미림분식) · 553 Hoam-ro, Gwanak-gu (관악구 호암로 553) · Bunsik · tteokbokki · Hoam-ro, Seoul National Univ. Venture Town Station (서울대벤처타운역), Sillim Line area

The Sillim Line (신림선) is a light-rail line that opened in 2022, a short route toward Seoul National University.

  • Mian (미안) · 6 Nangok-ro 65-gil, 1F, Gwanak-gu (관악구 난곡로65길 6 1층) · Restaurant · Vietnamese food · Nangok-ro — nearest station not confirmed (a bus is the realistic option). No walking minutes are given
  • Gaeseong Kalguksu (개성칼국수) · 45 63-ro, Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구 63로 45) · Korean · kalguksu/mandu · Yeouido, Yeouinaru Station (여의나루역), Line 5 area

Gaeseong (개성) is a North Korean city name, often used in shop names to signal North Korean-style mandu and kalguksu.

  • Gogyeongho Hwangso Gopchang (고경호황소곱창) · 36 Yeongsin-ro, 1F, Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구 영신로 36 1층) · Korean · gopchang/makchang · Yeongdeungpo Station (영등포역), Line 1 area
  • Ttosunine (또순이네) · 16 Seonyu-ro 47-gil, 1-2F, Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구 선유로47길 16 1~2층) · Korean · grilled beef · Seonyudo Station (선유도역), Line 9, and Dangsan Station (당산역), Line 2/9, area
  • Hosu Samgyetang (호수삼계탕) · 276 Dorim-ro, Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구 도림로 276) · Korean · baeksuk/samgyetang · Dorim-ro — not confirmed which is closer, Daerim Station (대림역) or Singil Station (신길역)

Samgyetang (삼계탕) is a whole chicken simmered with ginseng and glutinous rice, a health-food dish. One bird is one serving, making it one of the few meat dishes that works well solo.

Foreign-visitor tip — Gopchang and grilled beef usually start at 2 servings; sundae, tteokbokki, kalguksu, and samgyetang can be ordered solo. Some spots in Gwanak and Yeongdeungpo need a bus transfer from the subway station, so check the route before you set out.

🚄 How to Fit This Into a Seoul Trip

  • Arrival day — Gongdeok Station has an AREX stop. Gyegogijip in Group ① is the only spot you can reach straight from Incheon Airport, luggage and all.
  • If you only want spots that overlap the tourist circuit — Jongno 3-ga (Hapcheon Dwaeji Gukbap), Dongdaemun (Maeun Jokbal), Chungmuro (Manna Son Kalguksu), Sinchon (Yegang Seolnongtang), Seongsu (Milbon). The rest are places you go to on purpose.
  • Office-district lunch — Yeoksam, Eonju, Seocho, and Gyodae get busy at noon on weekdays. Shift by 30 minutes.
  • Pair with the neighborhood pieces — Seongsu #01, Nowon #179, and standalone guides for Yeongdeungpo/Yeouido and Gwanak already exist. This piece fills in the shops those pieces don't cover.
  • Heading out into the metro area — Incheon and northwest Gyeonggi are in #1390, southeast Gyeonggi in #1391.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Assuming a shared name means the same shop — Eunpyeong's Halmeoni Tteokbokki is different from the Sindang-dong original (#810), and Songpa's Goindol also has several same-named shops elsewhere. Check the address.

2. Expecting an English menu at a residential-area restaurant — Groups ② and ④ are mostly neighborhood eateries. A translation app and a pointed finger are the fastest tools.

3. Looking for "X minutes on foot" throughout this piece — Outside the five confirmed spots, everything else stops at "walking distance." Check the exact minutes on Naver Map on the spot.

⚙ 3 FAQs

1. Who picked this list? — It's a table of regional eateries Korean YouTube viewers named in comments. KeyChinGu checked only existence, address, and business type, and assigned no ranking.

2. How is this different from the Jongno/Gwanghwamun locals' picks (#1191)? — #1191 is limited to Jongno and Gwanghwamun, chosen from civil-servant and office-worker visit data. This piece's source is YouTube comments, its scope is 16 Seoul districts, and the two pieces share no shops.

3. Can I go alone? — Gukbap, sundaeguk, kalguksu, tteokbokki, samgyetang, donkkaseu, and ramen can be ordered solo. Galbi, gopchang, grilled beef, and hanjeongsik usually start at 2 people.
