🎯 Why an exclusive guide to Yeouido?

Yeouido is Korea's No. 1 financial and media hub in Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul and a hot spot for foreign tourists — the National Assembly, KBS, MBC, financial company headquarters, Yeouido Hangang Park, 63 Building, The Hyundai Seoul, and IFC Mall condensed into one area. Although it is hidden from Myeong-dong and Gangnam in the flow of foreign tourists, it is an area where only those in the know can experience the spring Yunjung-ro cherry blossoms, the night view of the Han River, and high-rise view dining .

Compared to Hongdae (MZ, indie), Gangnam (luxury, shopping), and Itaewon (global), Yeouido has finance, city, and landmark. When foreign tourists are traveling to Seoul for 4-5 days, Spring April Yunjung-ro Cherry Blossom Season or Han River Night View + E-Cruise are the top destinations. The Hyundai Seoul/IFC Mall is an area optimized for foreign shopping + immediate refund.

Following #138 Hongdae, #142 Gangnam, #144 Itaewon, and #156 Yeonhui-dong, it joins ISSUE 03 ⑩ section — Seoul Entering the “5 Areas Foreigners Want to Visit” series. Key Friend’s first deep dive in Yeongdeungpo-gu and Yeouido.

⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Myeongdong and Gangnam are the main areas" — Yeouido Pass

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. Yeouido is only recognized as a “business area” over Myeongdong and Gangnam, but in reality, it is a condensed area of ​​5 hot spots for foreigners in Seoul, including Yunjung-ro cherry blossoms in spring + night view of the Han River + E-Cruise + The Hyundai Seoul. In particular, the cherry blossoms in Yunjung-ro during the first and second weeks of April are an annual #1 hotspot on Korean Instagram and foreign vlogs. "Cherry blossoms during the day + E-cruise in the evening + The Hyundai at night" = a perfect one-day course in one route.

2. "Yeouido Lunch = All office workers can't go and eat" — 11:30~13:30 Avoidance Not Considered

Yeouido lunch peak is 11:30~13:30 — Lines at popular stores such as Solsot, Rungrado, and London Bagel Museum are 30 minutes+. Foreigners are recommended to detour to after 14:00 for lunch or after 18:00 for dinner + Han River walking course. In spring and fall, the Han River lunch box + takeout from a nearby bakery is the best. Absence of office workers on holidays and weekends → OK anywhere.

3. "Yeouido Hangang Park = Just pass because it's the Han River"

Yeouido Hangang Park is an area where three seasonal hot spots are gathered in one place**:

  • Spring April: Yunjung-ro Cherry Blossoms 1.7km — Korean/Foreigner #1 Instagram hotspot
  • Summer June~August: Han River at night + Pulbit Square Night Market + E-Cruise
  • Fall October: Seoul Fireworks Festival (first week of October, 1 million foreign visitors)

Foreigners just pass without knowing the season — No. 1 area for season matching.

Yeouido scenery

Yeouido is an artificial island-like administrative district located in the middle of the Han River. After the reclamation of the Han River in 1968, it was created as a core financial and media area. It is currently Seoul's "Manhattan" No. 1, where the National Assembly, KBS, MBC, SBS, headquarters of financial companies, the 63 Building, and Yeouido Hangang Park are condensed.

Yeoui-daero is the main street — National Assembly → The Hyundai Seoul → IFC Mall → 63 Building Line. A 30-40 minute walk is possible. Four subway stations cover one area: Yeouido Station and Yeouinaru Station on Subway Line 5 and Nodullo and National Assembly Station on Line 9.

International Finance Road is a street of IFC and financial buildings — financial company headquarters + foreign dining + cafes concentrated. Weekday lunch hot zone for office workers.

Yeouido Hangang Park is 1.7km along the Hangang River — Yunjung-ro Cherry Blossom Road + Hangang River View Cafe + E-Cruise Ticket Office + Bicycle Path. A hot spot for spring cherry blossoms, summer night views, and fall fireworks.

The Hyundai Seoul (opening in 2021) is Korea's largest department store, immediate duty-free refund for foreigners + many LBM bagel and food and beverage stores. IFC Mall is adjacent to Conrad Hotel + has a foreign-friendly dining lineup.

Transportation: Subway Line 5 Yeouido Station (2-minute walk - IFC Mall) or Yeouinaru Station (Hangang Park). Kakao Taxi recommended. Bicycle rental is OK (Hangang Park 1.7km course).

⚙ FAQ (8)

Q1. When can I go to Yeouido for lunch and not have a line?

After 14:00 or before 11:30 on weekdays. 12:00~13:30 is the lunch peak for office workers, so Solsot, London Bagel Museum, and Rungrado are all reduced by 30 minutes to 1 hour. No office workers on weekends and holidays → Walk-in anywhere is OK.

Q2. When exactly is the cherry blossom season in Yunjung-ro?

First to second week of April every year (varies between late March and mid-April). It is necessary to check the Seoul Metropolitan Government's official "Yeouido Spring Flower Festival" schedule in advance. Quiet after 9-10 or 18:00 on weekdays — low crowds on weekends.

Q3. How do I rent a bicycle at Hangang Park?

Ttareungi (Seoul City public bicycle) is standard — app download + credit card registration for foreigners OK. 1,000 won per hour, rental locations throughout Hangang Park. Or, a private rental shop in Hangang Park (family bicycles for 4-6 people OK).

Q4. Is reservation required for this cruise dinner cruise?

Reservation required 1 week in advance for spring flower season in April and fireworks festival in October. Walk-in is OK in normal times, but advance reservations are recommended one month before the ticket office. Dinner cruise costs approximately 90,000 won/person.

Q5. What about immediate tax-free refund at The Hyundai Seoul?

30,000 won or more + foreign passport + Global Blue display store = Immediate refund upon payment. Request “Tax Free Refund” from store staff. The duty-free counter is on the B2 floor (basement 2).

Q6. Chicken and beer available at Yeouido Hangang Park?

Available — Convenience store and delivery app within Hangang Park OK. However, drinking is allowed in designated areas (some areas in Citizens' Park where drinking is restricted). Hotspots for Koreans during the spring cherry blossom season and summer night view season. For foreigners, we recommend experiencing the Korean delivery app (Baedal Minjok).

Q7. What are the routes of Yeouido Station, Yeouinaru Station, and 63 Building?

Line 5 Yeouido Station (IFC Mall, National Assembly) → Line 5 Yeouinaru Station (Hangang Park) → Line 9 Nodeul-ro (63 Building). A 15-20 minute walk is OK. Hangang Park Ttareungi bike ride 10 minutes.

Q8. What is a foreigner-friendly lunch restaurant?

The Hyundai Seoul Food Court + IFC Mall Dining + Mad for Garlic, Traga, Lak Hee-an = English menu + card payment + foreigner friendly, authentic. Authentic Korean dishes such as Solsot and Neungrado are also available in English.
