🎯 Why a Dedicated Yeouido (여의도) Guide?

Yeouido (여의도), located in Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구), Seoul, is Korea's financial and media hub and a popular spot for foreign tourists. It's often called "Seoul's Manhattan" because it concentrates the National Assembly (국회), KBS (한국방송공사), MBC (문화방송), major financial company headquarters, Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도 한강공원), 63 Building (63 빌딩), The Hyundai Seoul (더현대 서울), and IFC Mall (IFC몰) into one area. While it might be overshadowed by Myeongdong (명동) and Gangnam (강남) in typical tourist itineraries, those in the know discover spring cherry blossoms on Yunjung-ro (윤중로), stunning Hangang (한강) night views, and high-rise dining.

Compared to Hongdae (홍대) (for MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) and indie culture), Gangnam (강남) (for luxury and shopping), and Itaewon (이태원) (for global vibes), Yeouido (여의도) has a financial, urban, and landmark character. It's a top priority for foreign tourists on a 4-5 day Seoul trip, especially during the spring April cherry blossom season on Yunjung-ro (윤중로) or for an evening Hangang (한강) night view + E-Cruise (이크루즈) itinerary. The Hyundai Seoul (더현대 서울) and IFC Mall (IFC몰) are also optimized for foreign shopping + immediate tax refunds.
