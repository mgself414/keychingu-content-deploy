Hongdae Food and Street Guide — Exit-by-Exit Routes at Hongik Univ. Station, Red Road, Free Market and Gyeongui Line Forest Park, Official Values for 2026

Subtitle: Hongik Univ. Station on Line 2, AREX and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line (Incheon Airport T1 ₩4,650) · Red Road about 2 km with 11 busking zones (Exit 9 for R1, Exit 8 for R2) · Hongdae Free Market Saturdays 13:00–18:00, March–November (Sangsu Station Exit 2) · Hongik Culture Park 10:00–18:00, closed Mondays · Gyeongui Line Forest Park 6.3 km, free (Exit 3) — based on the official VisitSeoul, Korea Tourism Organization and Airport Railroad pages as of 2026-10-08. For food we list only the type of food on each street; for restaurants and prices, use a map app

🎯 How to Use This Guide

In Hongdae (홍대), which exit you take decides your whole route — Exit 9 leads to busking zone R1 on Red Road (레드로드), Exit 8 to the R2 busking plaza, Exit 3 to Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길) and Yeonnam-dong (연남동), and Exit 4 is the closest to the station (VisitSeoul). Everything below was read from the official VisitSeoul, Korea Tourism Organization and Airport Railroad pages on 2026-10-08. The older guide's restaurant hours and prices (for example, tonkatsu 11:00–21:00 for ₩10,000–14,000) had no source, so we dropped them all — for food we give only the type of food on each street, and you check the restaurant, price and hours in a map app on the day.

🚇 Hongik Univ. Station — Three Lines, Four Exits

  • Line 2, the AREX airport railroad (공항철도) and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line (경의중앙선) meet here (VisitSeoul). The older guide left out the Gyeongui-Jungang Line.
  • AREX all-stops train: Hongik Univ. to Incheon Airport T1 ₩4,650, T2 ₩5,250 (transit card fare, per the official Airport Railroad site). Travel time is not on the fare page, so it is not posted.
  • Exits: Exit 4 (closest to the station) · Exit 9 (Red Road R1, 220 m) · Exit 8 (R2 busking plaza, 170 m) · Exit 3 (Gyeongui Line Forest Park, 447 m).

🎤 Red Road — The New Name for the Walking Street

  • Called "Red Road" since 2023 (about 2 km). Busking happens only in the 11 designated zones, and only pre-registered performances (VisitSeoul) — R1 (Exit 9) and R2 (Exit 8) are the main ones. The Red Road Festival is on record for May 13–14, 2023 (no 2026 schedule posted).
  • The Korea Tourism Organization page lists the Hongdae street address as 20 Hongik-ro (Seogyo-dong), with the Picasso Street (피카소 거리) and Club Street (클럽 거리) areas, inquiries at 02-334-7878.

🧺 Free Market and Hongik Culture Park — Saturday Afternoon

  • Hongdae Free Market (홍대 프리마켓): since 2002, Saturdays 13:00–18:00, March–November, at Hongik Culture Park (홍익문화공원) (19-3 Wausan-ro 21-gil). The nearest station is Line 6 Sangsu Station (상수역) Exit 2, 533 m (VisitSeoul) — Sangsu is closer than the older guide's "Hongik Univ. Station Exit 9". Whether it runs in the 2026 season is not officially posted (after the park's renovation).
  • Hongik Culture Park: renovated in December 2025, 10:00–18:00, closed Mondays, free (VisitSeoul).

🌳 Gyeongui Line Forest Park — A Walk Through Yeonnam-dong

  • A 6.3 km linear park built on a former rail corridor, free (VisitSeoul). The Yeonnam-dong (연남동) section is known as Yeontral Park (연트럴파크) (the 1,268 m length comes from a secondary source). 447 m from Exit 3.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Expecting busking anywhere — only the 11 designated zones, pre-registered performances.

2. Going to the Free Market on a weekday — only Saturdays 13:00–18:00, March–November.

3. Visiting Hongik Culture Park on a Monday — it is closed.

4. Heading to the Free Market from Hongik Univ. Station — Sangsu Exit 2 is closer.

5. Trusting the older guide's price list — no source. Use a map app.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Straight from the airport? — AREX all-stops train, T1 ₩4,650 · T2 ₩5,250.

2. Where is the busking? — Exit 9 for R1, Exit 8 for R2.

3. What to do on Saturday? — Free Market 13:00–18:00 (March–November) → Red Road → an evening food street.

4. A walk? — Exit 3, Gyeongui Line Forest Park (Yeonnam-dong).

5. Maps and information? — Mapo Tourist Information Center 10:00–19:00, Hongdae Tourist Information Center 10:00–20:00.

6. How much does it cost? — Not in this guide. Check map app reviews and menus.
