21_Completely conquer Hongdae’s famous restaurants

One-line introduction

Hongdae's best eats: where K-pop energy meets local food culture.

Hongdae is Seoul's largest youth cultural district with clubs, performances, and arts, and a hot zone with a variety of delicious restaurants.

Recommended target

  • Backpackers in their 20s and 30s, travelers who need a meal after a club tour
  • K-pop fans and travelers who want to enjoy Hongdae street performances and stop by delicious restaurants

Recommended course (3 hours)

1. 12:00 Brunch in Yeonnam-dong (KRW 10,000~15,000): Visit cafes and brunch while walking along the Gyeongui Line forest path

2. 13:30 Hongik University street performance viewing (free): Performance plaza in front of Exit 8 of Hongik University Station

3. 14:30 Lamb Skewers Alley Tour (KRW 15,000~20,000/person): Early bird dinner or snack

4. 16:00 Cafe Dessert (6,000~9,000 won): Korean cake and drinks at a hipster cafe in Hongdae

5. 18:00 Seafood Pancake + Makgeolli (KRW 15,000/person): Dinner, Korean drinking culture experience

Tips for foreign tourists

  • Essential Korean expressions
  • "Two servings, please" — When ordering dakgalbi or pork belly
  • “Not spicy, please”
  • "One bottle of makgeolli, please"
  • Cultural Note: Many food stalls only accept cash. Petty cash is required.
  • Payment Method: Most cards accepted. Cash is preferred at outdoor stalls.
  • Transportation: Most locations are within a 5-minute walk from Exit 9 of Hongik University Station (Line 2, Airport Railroad).

English Guide (Simple English Summary)

Hongdae is Seoul's youth hub, packed with restaurants, street food stalls, and live music. Start with brunch in Yeonnam-dong along the forest path, then explore the lamb skewer alley for dinner. End your night with street-side tteokbokki and Korean makgeolli rice wine. Most restaurants stay open past midnight, perfect for night owls. Carry some cash for outdoor pojangmacha (street stalls); cards work at most sit-down restaurants.

Estimated total cost

  • Savings: 25,000 won (gukbap + convenience store snacks + subway)
  • Standard: 55,000 won (brunch + dinner + cafe + makgeolli)
  • Luxury: 90,000 won (fine dining style + cocktail bar)
