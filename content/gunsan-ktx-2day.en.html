Gunsan KTX 1 night 2 days full guide for foreigners

Mecca of Korean modern architecture and Japanese colonial era heritage — KTX Iksan + Gunsan Line → Time travel to Japanese-style houses, banks, and Haemang-dong

Gunsan is the city with the best preserved traces of Korea's modern history (1900-1945). Japanese-style house, modern bank building, old Joseon Bank, Japanese-style house in Shinheung-dong, etc. Fits foreign history and architecture persona. This guide covers the standard route for foreigners for 2 days and 1 night + 10 stores. Jeonju combo recommended.

1. How to get to Gunsan

Options Take price Recommended
KTX Iksan + Gunsan Line (Seoul Station) 1h 50m + 1h ₩33,000 ★★★ Jeongseok
KTX Iksan + intercity bus 1h 50m + 1h 30m ₩30,000 alternative
Intercity Bus (Gangnam) 3h 30m ₩19,800 Low budget/late night

Jeonju combo recommended: Iksan Station base → 1 night in Gunsan + 1 night in Jeonju (fits 2 nights and 3 days)

Reservation: Korail English (https://www.letskorail.com/)

2. Standard 2 days and 1 night itinerary

Day 1 (Arrival + Modern Culture Street)

  • 07:00 Seoul Station KTX Honam Line
  • 08:50 Transfer at Iksan Station → Gunsan Line
  • 10:00 Arrival at Gunsan Station
  • 10:30 Modern History Museum (English audio + Gunsan Port and modern history)
  • 12:00 Lunch (Iseongdang Bakery — Korea’s oldest bakery since 1945)
  • 14:00 Japanese-style house in Sinheung-dong (former Hirotsu house, English information)
  • 15:30 Dongguksa Temple (Korea’s only Japanese-style temple)
  • 17:00 Haemang-dong (Gunsan Port + Japanese Colonial Fish Market)
  • 19:00 Dinner (Gunsan jjambbong, water tumbleng, seafood)
  • 21:00 Hotel check-in

Day 2 (Wolmyeong-dong/Time Travel + Return)

  • 08:00 Hotel breakfast
  • 09:30 Wolmyeong-dong (Gunsan 1900-1945 time travel street)
  • 11:00 Former Joseon Bank (Modern Architecture + Gunsan Museum)
  • 12:30 Lunch (Iseongdang Bakery, Jungdong Steamed Bun or Gunsan Jjamppong)
  • 14:00 Saemangeum Seawall (time fits) or Seonyudo/Gogunsan Islands 1-day tour
  • 16:30 Gunsan Station
  • 17:00 Transfer to KTX Iksan
  • 19:30 Arrival at Seoul Station

3. 10 stores and experiences suitable for foreigners

Modern architecture and history

1. Japanese-style house in Shinheung-dong (formerly Hirotsu House) — Gunsan No. 1, drama filming location, English information

2. Dongguksa — Korea’s only Japanese-style temple (1909)

3. Former Chosun Bank (Gunsan Modern Architecture Museum) — Renaissance architecture

4. Modern History Museum — Gunsan 1899 port opening, Japanese colonial period deep dive (English audio)

5. Wolmyeong-dong Time Travel Street — 1900-1945 Preservation Street

Food/Cafe

6. Lee Seongdang — Korea’s oldest bakery (1945~·No. 1 among foreigners·picture menu)

7. Middle Eastern steamed bread — Gunsan specialty (cheap, local)

8. Gunsan Jjamppong — Gunsan-specific (meat/seafood mix)

9. Multombeong — Gunsan Signature Seafood

Nature/Expansion

10. Seonyudo + Gogunsan Archipelago — 1-day tour (Day 2 option, Saemangeum seawall → Munyeodo → Seonyudo)

4. Foreign fit tips

English language availability

  • Top: Modern History Museum (English audio)·Sinheung-dong Japanese-style house (English guide)·Dongguksa Temple
  • Slander: Former Chosun Bank, Wolmyeong-dong (English sign)
  • Local: Lee Seongdang, Gunsan Jjamppong, Market (picture menu, translation app)

Budget (2 days and 1 night per person)

  • KTX round trip: ₩66,000
  • Accommodation per night (hotel): ₩60,000~150,000
  • 4 meals: ₩40,000~80,000 (Iseongdang + Jjambbong + Korean food)
  • Entrance fee/experience: ₩15,000~30,000
  • City transportation (taxi, walking): ₩15,000
  • Seonyudo Tour (Optional): ₩30,000
  • Total average ₩200,000~370,000

By persona

  • European history buff: Modern History Museum + Sinheung-dong + Former Joseon Bank + Dongguksa Temple (all available in 1 day)
  • architecture lover: Japanese-style house + Renaissance bank + Japanese-style temple unique combo
  • foodie: Lee Seongdang (bread) + Gunsan Jjamppong (Chinese food) + Multeombeong (seafood)
  • Photo fit: Japanese-style house in Sinheung-dong + Wolmyeong-dong + Saemangeum
  • Nature/Beach added: Day 2 Seonyudo 1-day tour (boat/car)

Heritage of the Japanese Colonial Period - Guide to Recognition for Foreigners

  • Gunsan was a Korean grain exploitation port during the Japanese colonial period (1910-1945). Many Japanese-style buildings are heritage from that era.
  • contextualize to foreigners: "Japanese colonial era heritage — preserved as historical museum, not glorification"
  • Clearly indicated on English signage (Korean government’s official position)
  • Simultaneous recognition of historical and architectural value

By season

  • Spring (April-May): Wolmyeong-dong + Seonyudo cherry blossoms
  • Summer (June-August): Seonyudo Beach + Saemangeum
  • Fall (October-November): Wolmyeong-dong autumn leaves + Dongguksa Temple
  • Winter (December-February): Modern Architecture Indoor Tour + Iseongdang Bread + Gunsan Jjambbong

5. Jeonju combo (3 days and 2 nights highly recommended)

1 night in Gunsan + 1 night in Jeonju = 3 days and 2 nights based at Iksan Station is the most efficient for KTX and time:

Day 1

  • Seoul Station → KTX → Iksan → Gunsan Line → Gunsan
  • 1 day in Gunsan (modern architecture + Lee Seongdang)
  • 1 night at Gunsan hotel

Day 2

  • Gunsan morning (Seonyudo or Wolmyeong-dong)
  • Gunsan → Iksan → Jeonju
  • Jeonju Hanok Village + Bibimbap
  • Hanok stay 1 night

Day 3

  • Jeonju morning (bean sprout soup + Gaekridan-gil)
  • Iksan → KTX → Seoul
