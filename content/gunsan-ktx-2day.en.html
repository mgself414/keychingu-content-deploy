Gunsan KTX 2-Day Full Guide for Foreign Travelers

> The Mecca of Korean Modern Architecture and Japanese Colonial Era Heritage — KTX Iksan (익산) + Gunsan Line (군산선) → Japanese-style Houses, Banks, and a Time Travel to Haemang-dong (해망동)

Gunsan (군산) is a city where traces of modern Korean history (1900-1945) are best preserved. With Japanese-style houses, modern bank buildings, the former Joseon Bank, and the Japanese House in Sinheung-dong (신흥동 일본식 가옥), it perfectly fits foreign history and architecture enthusiasts. This guide provides a standard 2-day itinerary for foreign visitors, including 10 recommended spots. A Jeonju (전주) combo is also recommended.

1. How to Get to Gunsan (군산)

Option Duration Price Recommendation
KTX to Iksan (익산) + Gunsan Line (군산선) (from Seoul Station (서울역)) 1h 50m + 1h ₩33,000 (~$24 / ~175 RMB / ~3,400 JPY) ★★★ Standard
KTX to Iksan (익산) + Intercity Bus 1h 50m + 1h 30m ₩30,000 Alternative
Intercity Bus (from Gangnam (강남)) 3h 30m ₩19,800 Budget / Late Night

Jeonju (전주) combo recommended: Use Iksan Station (익산역) as a base → 1 night in Gunsan (군산) + 1 night in Jeonju (전주) (fits a 3-day, 2-night trip).

Booking: Korail English (https://www.letskorail.com/)

2. Standard 2-Day Itinerary

Day 1 (Arrival + Modern Culture Street)

  • 07:00 AM KTX Honam Line from Seoul Station (서울역)
  • 08:50 AM Transfer at Iksan Station (익산역) → Gunsan Line (군산선)
  • 10:00 AM Arrive at Gunsan Station (군산역)
  • 10:30 AM Gunsan Modern History Museum (근대역사박물관) (English audio + Gunsan Port (군산항) & Modern History)
  • 12:00 PM Lunch (Lee Sung Dang (이성당) Bakery — Korea's oldest bakery, established in 1945)
  • 02:00 PM Japanese House in Sinheung-dong (신흥동 일본식 가옥) (former Hirotsu House, English guide available)
  • 03:30 PM Dongguksa Temple (동국사) (Korea's only Japanese-style temple)
  • 05:00 PM Haemang-dong (해망동) (Gunsan Port (군산항) + Japanese colonial era fish market)
  • 07:00 PM Dinner (Gunsan Jjamppong (군산 짬뽕) (spicy seafood noodle soup), Multeombeong (물텀벙) (anglerfish stew), or other seafood)
  • 09:00 PM Hotel check-in

Day 2 (Wolmyeong-dong (월명동) & Time Travel + Return)

  • 08:00 AM Hotel breakfast
  • 09:30 AM Wolmyeong-dong (월명동) (Gunsan's 1900-1945 Time Travel Street)
  • 11:00 AM Former Joseon Bank (구 조선은행) (modern architecture + Gunsan Museum)
  • 12:30 PM Lunch (Lee Sung Dang (이성당) Bakery, Jungdong Jjinppang (중동찐빵) (steamed buns), or Gunsan Jjamppong (군산 짬뽕))
  • 02:00 PM Saemangeum Seawall (새만금 방조제) (if time permits) or Seonyudo (선유도) & Gogunsan Archipelago (고군산군도) 1-day tour
  • 04:30 PM Gunsan Station (군산역)
  • 05:00 PM KTX transfer at Iksan (익산)
  • 07:30 PM Arrive at Seoul Station (서울역)

3. 10 Foreigner-Friendly Spots & Experiences

Modern Architecture & History

1. Japanese House in Sinheung-dong (신흥동 일본식 가옥) (former Hirotsu House) — Gunsan's top spot, drama filming location, English guide available.

2. Dongguksa Temple (동국사) — Korea's only Japanese-style temple (established 1909).

3. Former Joseon Bank (구 조선은행) (Gunsan Modern Architecture Hall) — Renaissance architecture.

4. Gunsan Modern History Museum (근대역사박물관) — Deep dive into Gunsan's opening as a port in 1899 and the Japanese colonial era (일제강점기) (English audio).

5. Wolmyeong-dong Time Travel Street (월명동 시간여행 거리) — A preserved street from 1900-1945.

Food & Cafes

6. Lee Sung Dang (이성당) — Korea's oldest bakery (since 1945, top choice for foreigners, picture menu available).

7. Jungdong Jjinppang (중동찐빵) — Gunsan's specialty (affordable, local).

8. Gunsan Jjamppong (군산 짬뽕) — Gunsan's unique style (mix of meat and seafood).

9. Multeombeong (물텀벙) — Gunsan's signature seafood dish (anglerfish stew).

Nature & Extension

10. Seonyudo (선유도) + Gogunsan Archipelago (고군산군도) — 1-day tour (Day 2 option, via Saemangeum Seawall (새만금 방조제) → Munyeodo (무녀도) → Seonyudo (선유도)).

4. Foreigner-Friendly Tips

English Availability

  • High: Gunsan Modern History Museum (근대역사박물관) (English audio), Japanese House in Sinheung-dong (신흥동 일본식 가옥) (English guide available), Dongguksa Temple (동국사).
  • Medium-High: Former Joseon Bank (구 조선은행), Wolmyeong-dong (월명동) (English signboards).
  • Local: Lee Sung Dang (이성당), Gunsan Jjamppong (군산 짬뽕), Markets (picture menus, translation apps).

Budget (1 person, 2 days/1 night)

  • KTX round trip: ₩66,000 (~$48 / ~350 RMB / ~6,800 JPY)
  • Accommodation 1 night (hotel): ₩60,000~150,000
  • Meals 4 times: ₩40,000~80,000 (Lee Sung Dang (이성당) + Jjamppong (짬뽕) + Hanjeongsik (한정식) (Korean full-course meal))
  • Admission fees & experiences: ₩15,000~30,000
  • City transportation (taxi & walking): ₩15,000
  • Seonyudo (선유도) tour (option): ₩30,000
  • Total average ₩200,000~370,000 (~$145-270 / ~1,060-1,970 RMB / ~20,600-38,000 JPY)

By Persona

  • European history buff: Gunsan Modern History Museum (근대역사박물관) + Japanese House in Sinheung-dong (신흥동 일본식 가옥) + Former Joseon Bank (구 조선은행) + Dongguksa Temple (동국사) (all possible in one day).
  • Architecture lover: Unique combo of Japanese-style houses + Renaissance bank + Japanese-style temple.
  • Foodie: Lee Sung Dang (이성당) (bread) + Gunsan Jjamppong (군산 짬뽕) (Chinese-Korean) + Multeombeong (물텀벙) (seafood).
  • Photography enthusiast: Japanese House in Sinheung-dong (신흥동 일본식 가옥) + Wolmyeong-dong (월명동) + Saemangeum (새만금).
  • Nature & beach addition: Day 2 Seonyudo (선유도) 1-day tour (boat/car).

Japanese Colonial Era Heritage — Guidance for Foreigners

  • Gunsan (군산) was a port used to plunder Korean grain during the Japanese colonial era (일제강점기, 1910-1945). Many of the Japanese-style buildings are legacies from that period.
  • To contextualize for foreigners: "Japanese colonial era heritage — preserved as historical museum, not glorification."
  • Clearly indicated on English signboards (official stance of the Korean government).
  • Both historical and architectural values are recognized.

By Season

  • Spring (April-May): Wolmyeong-dong (월명동) + Seonyudo (선유도) cherry blossoms.
  • Summer (June-August): Seonyudo (선유도) beach + Saemangeum (새만금).
  • Autumn (October-November): Wolmyeong-dong (월명동) autumn foliage + Dongguksa Temple (동국사).
  • Winter (December-February): Indoor modern architecture tours + Lee Sung Dang (이성당) bread + Gunsan Jjamppong (군산 짬뽕).

5. Jeonju (전주) Combo (Highly Recommended 3 Days/2 Nights)

1 night in Gunsan (군산) + 1 night in Jeonju (전주) = A 3-day, 2-night trip based at Iksan Station (익산역) is the most efficient for KTX travel and time:

Day 1

  • Seoul Station (서울역) → KTX → Iksan (익산) → Gunsan Line (군산선) → Gunsan (군산)
  • Gunsan (군산) Day 1 (modern architecture + Lee Sung Dang (이성당))
  • 1 night at Gunsan (군산) hotel

Day 2

  • Gunsan (군산) morning (Seonyudo (선유도) or Wolmyeong-dong (월명동))
  • Gunsan (군산) → Iksan (익산) → Jeonju (전주)
  • Jeonju Hanok Village (전주 한옥마을) (traditional Korean house village) + Bibimbap (비빔밥) (mixed rice with vegetables)
  • 1 night Hanok (한옥) stay (traditional Korean house)

Day 3

  • Jeonju (전주) morning (Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥) (bean sprout soup) + Gaeknidan-gil (객리단길) street)
  • Iksan (익산) → KTX → Seoul (서울)
