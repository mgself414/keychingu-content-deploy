Jeju Oreum Guide: Seongsan Ilchulbong, Sangumburi and Geomun Oreum, Saebyeol Oreum, Yongnuni Oreum and Darangswi Oreum, Sarasoreum and Witseoreum, 20 Places in All

Subtitle: 20 places chosen from the "Jeju Oreum" theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" public vote · By registered values, the places that charge admission are Seongsan Ilchulbong (adults 5,000 won), Sangumburi (adults 7,000 won) and Geomun Oreum (adults 2,000 won) · Geomun Oreum is closed on Tuesdays, with departures every 30 minutes from 09:00 to 14:00 · About 20 minutes to the summit of Saebyeol Oreum, about 30 minutes for Ttarabi Oreum, Gunsan Oreum and Baegyagi Oreum — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Oreum (오름) is the word for Jeju's small volcanic cones. The Korea Tourism Organization introduction says that about 360 parasitic volcanoes are spread across Jeju Island. This guide covers 20 places, out of the 53 chosen in the "Jeju Oreum" theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" (명소발굴 100×100) (candidates recommended by travel experts, voted on by the public), whose operating information was confirmed in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data. The ranks and vote counts in the table are the results of that vote.

Most are free, and 10 of the 20 are registered as always open. Among the places with a registered fee, the ones that charge admission are three: Seongsan Ilchulbong, Sangumburi and Geomun Oreum, and Jeoji Oreum has no registered fee value. Geomun Oreum departs at set times. Sanbangsan is free, but it is registered as allowing visits only along the path from the ticket office to Sanbanggulsa Temple (산방굴사).

Sarasoreum, Witseoreum, Andol Oreum, Abu Oreum and Gunsan Oreum carry a notice to call and ask because access may be restricted, and Sarabong carries a notice to check the restricted areas by phone. The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI).

📍 The Three Places with Registered Admission Fees — Seongsan Ilchulbong, Sangumburi and Geomun Oreum

Seongsan Ilchulbong (성산일출봉) (Ilchul-ro 284-12 (일출로 284-12), Seongsan-eup, Seogwipo-si, ☎ 064-783-0959) is a volcanic cone 180m above sea level that formed about 5,000 years ago in an underwater eruption in the sea. It is a UNESCO World Natural Heritage site. At the summit there is a crater 600m in diameter. It was originally a volcanic island, but sand and gravel piled up and joined it to the mainland. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, the sunrise seen from the summit has long been ranked first among the 10 Views of Yeongju (영주 10경).

  • Opening hours: Nov~Feb 06:00~18:00 (last ticket 17:00), March, April, September and October 05:00~19:00 (last ticket 18:00), May~August 04:30~20:00 (last ticket 19:00).
  • Closed days: First Monday of every month (the next day if it is a public holiday).
  • Fee (individual): Adults 5,000 won, teenagers, soldiers and children 2,500 won. Parking: Free.
  • Guidance: Natural heritage interpreter tours 09:00~17:00 (wait at the visitor center). It is registered that foreign-language guidance is available in Chinese.

Sangumburi (산굼부리) (Bijarim-ro 768 (비자림로 768), Jocheon-eup, Jeju-si, ☎ 064-783-9900) is a crater more than 2km around and a Natural Monument. A volcano that only exploded, without spewing lava or ash, and left just a hole is called a maar (Maar), and the Korea Tourism Organization introduction says there is only one in Korea. Inside the crater the vegetation splits: the north cliff holds a warm-temperate evergreen broadleaf forest and the south cliff a temperate deciduous forest.

  • Opening hours: March~June and September~October 09:00~18:40 (last entry 18:00), July~August and Nov~Feb 09:00~17:40 (last entry 17:00). Closing times may change depending on the weather.
  • Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee (individual): Adults 7,000 won, teenagers and children (age 4 and over) 6,000 won. Visitors aged 65 and over pay 5,000 won with an ID.

Geomun Oreum (거문오름) (Seongyo-ro 569-36 (선교로 569-36), Jocheon-eup, Jeju-si, ☎ 064-710-8981) is also called Geomeun Oreum (검은오름), meaning black oreum, because it is covered in forest and looks dark. Lava that flowed out of the crater made a valley stretching about 4km.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~14:00, with departures every 30 minutes. 11 tours a day are possible, and it is registered that the detailed tour times are on the official website.
  • Closed days: Every Tuesday, Seollal and Chuseok holidays. It is fully closed in bad weather.
  • Fee (individual): Adults 2,000 won, teenagers and soldiers 1,000 won, children 1,000 won. Free for ages 6 and under and 65 and over.

📍 Seongsan, Gujwa and Pyoseon — Seopjikoji, Yongnuni Oreum, Darangswi Oreum, Abu Oreum, Andol Oreum, Baegyagi Oreum and Ttarabi Oreum

According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, Seopjikoji (섭지코지) (Seopjikoji-ro 262 (섭지코지로 262), Seongsan-eup, Seogwipo-si, ☎ 064-784-2810) is a landform that juts out on the coast of Sinyang-ri (신양리), Seongsan-eup. "Koji" is a Jeju dialect word meaning cape. On the hill there is Hyeopjayeondae (협자연대), an old beacon tower, and the peak to its northeast is Bulgeun Oreum (붉은오름), made of red volcanic ash (scoria). An iron staircase leads to the white lighthouse at the summit.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Yongnuni Oreum (용눈이오름) (Jongdal-nonggil (종달논길), Gujwa-eup, Jeju-si, ☎ Jeju Tourist Information Center (제주관광정보센터) 064-740-6000) is 247.8m above sea level, 88m high and 2,685m around. The Korea Tourism Organization introduction says it is the only one of the 360 or so oreum with 3 craters. It is covered with grass in spring and summer and silver grass in autumn and winter.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Darangswi Oreum (Wollangbong) (다랑쉬오름(월랑봉)) (San 6, Sehwa-ri (세화리), Gujwa-eup, Jeju-si, ☎ 064-740-6000) is a cone-shaped oreum with steep slopes on every side. At the top there is a deep funnel-shaped crater. It is said to be named Darangswi because the crater looks round like the moon.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Not available.

Abu Oreum (아부오름) (San 164-1, Songdang-ri (송당리), Gujwa-eup, Jeju-si, ☎ 064-740-6000) is introduced as an oreum you can climb around the rim without difficulty. At the summit there is a gumburi (굼부리) (crater) as round as a wooden bowl, and cedar trees grow in a circle inside it.

  • Opening hours: Some access may be restricted, so call before visiting. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Andol Oreum (안돌오름) (San 66-2, Songdang-ri, Gujwa-eup, Jeju-si, ☎ 064-740-6000) is a grassy oreum with a horseshoe-shaped crater. Trees grow thick into a forest only in the gullied part inside the crater.

  • Opening hours: May be restricted, so call before visiting. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Baegyagi Oreum (백약이오름) (San 1, Seongeup-ri (성읍리), Pyoseon-myeon, Seogwipo-si, ☎ 064-740-6000) got its name because many medicinal herbs grow there. After about 30 minutes of climbing you reach the top and a gumburi hollowed out like a circular arena. Along the track-shaped top you can see many surrounding oreum.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Ttarabi Oreum (따라비오름) (San 62, Gasi-ri (가시리), Pyoseon-myeon, Seogwipo-si, ☎ 064-740-6000) is made up of 3 craters and 6 peaks. It takes about 30 minutes to reach the summit. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, the silver grass blooms most in October to November and lasts into winter.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

📍 Aewol, Hallim and Hangyeong — Saebyeol Oreum, Geumoreum and Jeoji Oreum

Saebyeol Oreum (새별오름) (San 59-8, Bongseong-ri (봉성리), Aewol-eup, Jeju-si, ☎ Jeju Tourist Information Center 064-740-6000) got its name because it is said to stand alone like the morning star in the evening sky. It has 5 peaks. In October silver grass blooms on the oreum. It takes about 20 minutes to reach the summit.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Geumoreum (금오름) (San 1-1, Geumak-ri (금악리), Hallim-eup, Jeju-si, ☎ 064-740-6000) is 427.5m in elevation and 2,861m around. At the top there is a circular crater 52m deep. The lake inside the crater once held plenty of water, but it is registered that its bottom is now exposed.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Jeoji Oreum (저지오름) (Hangyeong-myeon, Jeju-si, ☎ 064-728-7952) is an oreum with a steep funnel-shaped crater about 900m around and about 60m deep. The slopes are a dense forest in which black pine is the main species. In 2007 it won the Life Award (Grand Prize) at the 8th Beautiful Forest National Competition. There are the Jeoji Oreum rest area and an observatory.

  • Closed days: Open year-round. There are no registered values for opening hours and fee.

📍 Andeok and Daejeong — Sanbangsan, Gunsan Oreum and Songaksan

Sanbangsan (산방산) (San 16, Sagye-ri (사계리), Andeok-myeon, Seogwipo-si, ☎ 064-794-2940) is a dome-shaped volcano rising above flat terrain. From Sanbanggul Cave (산방굴) on its southwest slope you can see Yongmeori Coast (용머리해안). Because it is a restricted-access area to protect a scenic site, it is registered that only the path from the ticket office to Sanbanggulsa Temple can be visited.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~17:50 (last entry 17:30). It may change or be restricted depending on sunset time and weather.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

Gunsan Oreum (군산오름) (San 564, Changcheon-ri (창천리), Andeok-myeon, Seogwipo-si, ☎ 064-740-6000) has a paved path and takes about 30 minutes on foot. At the summit there are two rocks standing up like horns. From the summit you can see Hallasan (한라산), Jungmun Tourist Complex (중문관광단지), Marado (마라도) and Sanbangsan.

  • Opening hours: Partly restricted at times, so call before visiting. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Gunsan Oreum parking lot, free.

Songaksan (송악산) (Songak-gwangwang-ro 421-1 (송악관광로 421-1), Daejeong-eup, Seogwipo-si, ☎ Seogwipo City Hall Climate and Environment Division (서귀포시청 기후환경과) 064-760-2917) is a double-crater volcano in which a second crater formed inside the first. From the trail around the rim, which Jeju Olle Trail Route 10 (제주 올레 10코스) passes through, you can see Hyeongjeseom Island (형제섬), Gapado (가파도) and, far off, Marado. The path is not rough, but it is windy. On the coastal cliffs there are 15 artificial caves that the Japanese army dug during World War II by mobilizing Jeju people.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

📍 Oreum on the Hallasan Hiking Routes and a Wetland Oreum — Sarasoreum, Witseoreum and Mulyeongari Oreum

Sarasoreum (사라오름) (San 2-1, Sinrye-ri (신례리), Namwon-eup, Seogwipo-si, ☎ 064-740-6000) lies south of the Hallasan Seongpanak hiking route. There is a summit crater lake where water has collected in the crater, and the area around the lake was designated a Scenic Site in 2011. The Korea Tourism Organization introduction says the frozen lake and snow-covered scenery at an altitude of 1,300m are spectacular and draw many hiking enthusiasts.

  • Opening hours: May be restricted, so call to ask. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Witseoreum (윗세오름) (San 183-1, Gwangnyeong-ri (광령리), Aewol-eup, Jeju-si, ☎ Hallasan Office (한라산 사무소) 064-713-9950) is three oreum in a row along the Hallasan Yeongsil hiking route. From the top they are called Bulgeun Oreum, Nuun Oreum (누운오름) and Jogeun Oreum (족은오름). In spring royal azaleas and azaleas bloom. Beside the hiking trail there is Noroset Spring (노루샘).

  • Opening hours: Partly restricted at times, so call before visiting. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Mulyeongari Oreum (물영아리오름) (Taesu-ro 552 (태수로 552), Namwon-eup, Seogwipo-si, ☎ 064-728-6200) is an oreum that holds a wetland in its crater. It was designated a Ramsar wetland in 2006. The visit begins after walking half way around a pasture. It is registered as the place where the film "A Werewolf Boy" (늑대소년) was shot.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

📍 Jeju City — Sarabong

Sarabong (사라봉) (Sarabong-dong-gil 61 (사라봉동길 61), Jeju-si, ☎ 064-740-6000) is an oreum on the coast east of Jeju Port. The sunset seen from Sarabong, "Sabongnakjo (사봉낙조)", is counted among the 10 Views of Yeongju. From the summit you can see the sea to the north, Hallasan to the south and Jeju City below. The whole area is a park, and there is an octagonal pavilion.

  • Opening hours: Always open (call before visiting about restricted areas). Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. You cannot enter Geomun Oreum at just any time — departures are every 30 minutes from 09:00 to 14:00, and it is closed on Tuesdays and during the Seollal and Chuseok holidays.
  2. Sanbangsan has a set visiting section — it is registered as allowing visits only along the path from the ticket office to Sanbanggulsa Temple, and it may be restricted depending on sunset time and weather.
  3. Call before going to oreum marked "may be restricted" — Sarasoreum, Witseoreum, Andol Oreum, Abu Oreum and Gunsan Oreum are registered that way. For Sarabong, it says to check restricted areas by phone, and Geomun Oreum is fully closed in bad weather.

⚙ FAQ 3

Q. Which oreum can I climb in a short time?

A. The places where the registered information gives a time are Saebyeol Oreum (about 20 minutes to the summit), Ttarabi Oreum (about 30 minutes), Baegyagi Oreum (about 30 minutes) and Gunsan Oreum (about 30 minutes on foot).

Q. Which places cost money?

A. Seongsan Ilchulbong (adults 5,000 won), Sangumburi (adults 7,000 won) and Geomun Oreum (adults 2,000 won). The rest are registered as free, and Jeoji Oreum has no registered fee value.

Q. Where can I see silver grass?

A. The places where the Korea Tourism Organization introduction mentions silver grass are Saebyeol Oreum (October), Ttarabi Oreum (blooms most in October to November and lasts into winter) and Yongnuni Oreum (autumn and winter).
