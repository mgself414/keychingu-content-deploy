Korean Chicken Ordering & Delivery Workflow — From Baemin to Store Pickup

> Subtitle: 5-step Workflow for Foreign Tourists + Delivery App, Phone, Store, Payment, and Chimaek Pairing Tips

ISSUE 03 · #817 · 2026-06-08

🎯 Why a Korean Chicken Workflow Guide?

Even if you know the [#816 Chicken 6-Type Matrix](#816_korean_chicken_6_type_matrix.md), the ordering and receiving process is a separate matter. Korean chicken means (a) Delivery System #1 (Baemin (배민), Yogiyo (요기요), Coupang Eats (쿠팡이츠) – global #1 delivery infrastructure), (b) Store Pickup (often with discounts), (c) Chimaek (치맥) Restaurant Dining (best atmosphere), and (d) Hotel Room Delivery (top priority for foreigners). Foreigners often find it confusing due to (1) delivery apps being mostly in Korean, (2) entering hotel addresses, (3) choosing spicy levels or Banban (반반), (4) Chimaek (치맥) pairings, and (5) payment methods like cards or bank transfers. This guide provides a 5-step workflow, delivery app usage, English phrases, and hotel delivery tips.

★ 5-step Workflow at a Glance

Step Action Time Key Point for Foreigners
1️⃣ Choose Channel Delivery app / Phone / Store / Hotel concierge Immediate Hotel Delivery #1 priority (English OK for foreigners)
2️⃣ Order Menu Select brand + type + Banban (반반) / spicy level 3~10 min Banban (반반) = #1 for foreigners
3️⃣ Payment + Address Card / bank transfer / delivery fee + hotel address 1~3 min Hotel = "Hotel Name + Room Number"
4️⃣ Receive 30~60 min delivery / 15~30 min store pickup 30~60 min Delivery = arrival notification / Pickup = fast
5️⃣ Eat + Chimaek Chicken + beer / Soju (소주) pairing 30~60 min Chimaek (치맥) = Fried chicken + draft beer #1

> Total Time: Delivery = 40~75 min (order~eat) / Store pickup = 20~35 min / Chimaek (치맥) restaurant dining = 1~2h

📱 Delivery System Deep Dive

🥇 Baemin (배민, 배달의민족) — Korea's #1

  • Features: Korea's #1 delivery app, includes all chicken restaurants, some English support.
  • System:

1. Download app (SMS verification with foreign phone number).

2. Register address (Hotel = "Hotel Name + Room Number" in English OK).

3. Category → Chicken → Select brand.

4. Menu + options (Banban (반반), spicy level).

5. Payment (Card + bank transfer + Baemin (배민) Pay).

  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (Partial English, but app UI is mostly Korean)
  • Time Required: 30~60 min (after ordering)

🥈 Yogiyo (요기요)

  • Features: Most English support, very foreigner-friendly ★
  • System: Similar to Baemin (배민).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (English menus, partial English UI)

🥉 Coupang Eats (쿠팡이츠)

  • Features: Fast delivery (15~30 min) is a strong point, some chicken options.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★ (Medium Korean)

🏨 Hotel Concierge — #1 for Foreigners

  • Features: Just tell the hotel front desk, "Please order chicken for me."
  • Pros: English assistance, no address input needed, payment via hotel room charge, room delivery.
  • Cons: Hotel service charge added (10~20%).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (#1 recommended)

🍗 Menu Ordering Matrix

Type Selection

1. Banban (반반) — Half Yangnyeom (양념) (seasoned) and half Fried chicken (#1 recommended for beginners).

2. Single — Single type of Yangnyeom (양념), Fried, or Ganjang (간장) (soy sauce) chicken.

3. One-Third — Three types, one-third each (available at some restaurants).

4. Single Portion / Half Chicken — For one person (₩10,000~14,000 (~$7-10 / ~50-70 RMB / ~1,000-1,400 JPY)).

5. Set Meal — Chicken + drink/beer/side.

Options (All Restaurants)

  • Spicy Level: Not spicy / Regular / Spicy / Very spicy.
  • Extra Toppings: Cheese / Bburin Powder (뿌링 분말) (cheese powder) (additional charge).
  • Sides: Chicken-mu (치킨무) (pickled radish) (free) / Coke / Beer (separate charge).
  • Takeout vs. Delivery: Delivery = delivery fee ₩3,000~5,000.

English Phrases for Foreigners

  • "반반으로 주세요" = "Banban (반반) (half-half), please."
  • "안 맵게 해주세요" = "Less spicy, please."
  • "치즈 추가요" = "Add cheese, please."
  • "콜라 한 병" = "One Coke, please."
  • "호텔로 배달이요" = "Delivery to hotel, please."
  • "카드 돼요?" = "Card accepted?"

🍻 Chimaek (치맥) Pairing Workflow

Chimaek (치맥) = Korean Invention (K-drama My Love from the Star (별그대) Effect)

Classic Pairings:

  • Fried chicken + Draft beer = #1 Classic (Value for money ★★★★★)
  • Yangnyeom (양념) + Bottled beer = MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) value for money
  • Ganjang (간장) + Makgeolli (막걸리) (Korean rice wine) = Traditional (Kyochon (교촌))
  • Spicy + Soju (소주) (Korean distilled spirit) = Hangover cure

Chimaek (치맥) Restaurant vs. Delivery Differences:

Item Chimaek (치맥) Restaurant Delivery
Atmosphere ★★★★★ (Draft beer enthusiasts) ★★★ (Hotel/Home)
Price ₩15,000~25,000 per person Same + delivery fee
Time 1~2h dining 30~60 min + eating
Foreigner Friendliness ★★★ (Korean menus) ★★★★ (Hotel OK)

🏨 Hotel Room Delivery Workflow

Using Hotel Concierge (Easiest)

1. Hotel Front Desk: "치킨 주문 부탁드려요" (Could you order chicken?)

2. Select Brand/Menu (English menu often provided).

3. Room Number + Payment (Room charge OR card).

4. Delivery Arrival: Concierge → Room delivery.

5. Hotel Service Charge 10~20% added.

Ordering Directly (Cost-Effective)

1. Download Yogiyo (요기요) app.

2. Register Hotel Address ("Hotel Name + Room Number" in English).

3. Order Menu.

4. Hotel Lobby Pickup OR Room Delivery (depending on hotel policy).

> Key for Foreigners: 5-star hotels like Shilla (신라), Lotte (롯데), Westin (웨스틴) = Concierge #1. 3-star hotels or below = Order directly.

⚠ 7 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Banban (반반) = Not on the menu": All chicken restaurants offer it. Ask for "Banban (반반)" or "Half and half."

2. "Delivery fee = Free": Delivery fee of ₩3,000~5,000 is separate. Some restaurants offer free delivery above a certain amount.

3. "Hotel delivery = Difficult": Hotel concierge is the #1 system for foreigners. English is OK.

4. "Spicy level = Not indicated": All restaurants indicate spicy levels. "An Maeum (안 매움)" (not spicy) is possible.

5. "Chimaek (치맥) = Dine-in only": Hotel rooms, streets, and Hangang (한강) Citizen's Park are all OK.

6. "1 whole chicken = 1 person's meal": 1 whole chicken = 2~3 servings. For one person = half chicken or a single portion.

7. "Payment = Cash only": All delivery apps accept cards. Korea has the #1 card usage rate globally.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. First chicken = Which channel?: Hotel concierge #1 (English, convenient). For value = Store pickup.

2. Foreigner-friendly delivery app?: Yogiyo (요기요) (English ★) > Baemin (배민) > Coupang Eats (쿠팡이츠).

3. Popular Banban (반반) combinations?: Yangnyeom (양념) + Fried (#1) / Ganjang (간장) + Yangnyeom (양념) / Bburinkle (뿌링클) + Fried.

4. Recommended Chimaek (치맥) restaurants?: Chimaek (치맥) restaurant clusters in Jongno (종로), Euljiro (을지로), Gangnam (강남), Hongdae (홍대), Itaewon (이태원).

5. 24-hour delivery?: Some restaurants (mainly in Gangnam (강남) and Itaewon (이태원)) offer 24h delivery.

6. Allergies/Pregnancy?: Refer to [#816 disclaimer](#816_korean_chicken_6_type_matrix.md). Be cautious with gluten, soy, egg, nuts.

7. Payment/Tips?: Cards OK / Hotel concierge = 10~20% service charge / No tipping.

8. VAT Refund?: Food and beverages generally not eligible for refund.

⚠ Medical, Safety, and Dietary Disclaimer

Chicken = high-fat fried food. Pregnant women and those with weakened immune systems should be cautious with spicy food and overeating. For children, mild Yangnyeom (양념) or Fried chicken is recommended. Delivered chicken = recommended to consume within 30 min~1h (risk of food poisoning). Disclose allergies (gluten, soy, egg, nuts) in advance. If food poisoning is suspected, call ☎ 1339 (medical, English). This guide provides general information, not medical advice.

Restaurant/Brand Information Disclaimer: Delivery app and brand restaurant policies and menus change frequently — it is recommended to check NAVER/Kakao map or official websites/apps before use.

Dietary Restriction Fallback:

  • Halal/Kosher: Korean chicken is rarely certified. → Some Halal restaurants in Itaewon (이태원) OR BBQ (비비큐) global branches with Halal options.
  • Vegetarian/Vegan: Chicken = non-vegetarian. → Refer to [#799 Vegetarian/Vegan](#799_seoul_vegetarian_vegan_guide.md).
  • Gluten-free: Frying batter = wheat flour. Goobne (굽네) (oven-baked) offers some gluten-free options.

💰 Essential Tourist Tips

  • Average Cost per Person (as of June 2026):
  • 1 whole chicken = ₩20,000~28,000
  • Chimaek (치맥) at a restaurant per person = ₩15,000~25,000
  • Delivery = Same + delivery fee ₩3,000~5,000
  • Hotel concierge = Same + 10~20% service charge
  • Delivery App System:
  • Baemin (배민) > Yogiyo (요기요) (English ★) > Coupang Eats (쿠팡이츠) (fast)
  • Payment: Cards OK / Delivery apps = card, bank transfer, app pay / No tipping
  • Emergency: ☎ 1339 (medical, English) / ☎ 1330 (tourism interpretation)
  • VAT Refund X: General food and beverages
