Indie Bookstore + Cafe Guide for Visitors — 4 in Seoul, 2 in Busan, Only Places Confirmed on Their Own Sites or Official Tourism Pages (2026-10)

Subtitle: Hoehyeon The Book Society · Yeonhui Your Mind · Yeonnam UHJJUHDAH · Yeongdeungpo Manhole Coffee Western Bookstore · Busan Bosu-dong Book Street · Scuola di Atene — addresses, hours and closure days are the values read on 2026-10-07 from each shop's site and from VisitSeoul and VisitBusan. 🎯 How to Use This Guide

Indie bookstores move and close often (Your Mind (유어마인드) moved to a new space in April 2026, The Book Society (더북소사이어티) relocated from Seochon to Hoehyeon (회현), and Changbi Busan closed in November 2025). So this guide lists in the main text only the 6 places whose own site or official tourism page (VisitSeoul, VisitBusan) we opened and read directly on 2026-10-07. Places we could not open appear by name only in the "Not Yet Confirmed" section at the bottom. Book and coffee prices are not shown on any of those pages, so we do not list them. The "Bukchon, Itaewon and Jeonju combo" in the old guide used placeholders instead of real shop names.

🏙 Four in Seoul

1. The Book Society — Hoehyeon (Moved, So Check the Address)

  • A bookstore and project space run by the publisher Mediabus. It carries contemporary art, design and indie publishing, plus Korean and international titles (per its own site).
  • As of 2026 the address is 2, Toegye-ro 4-gil, 3F, Jung-gu (Namchang-dong). If you go to the old Seochon (Okin-dong) address, it is not there. No phone, email only.
  • Open Wed–Sun 13:00–19:00, closed Mon and Tue. Namdaemun Market and Hoehyeon Station (회현역) are a short walk away.

2. Your Mind — Yeonhui

  • It closed its old space on February 15, 2026 and reopened in April at Room 302, Gyogyo Building, 122 Yeonhui-ro (per its own site). Open 13:00–20:00, tel. 070-8821-8990.
  • The focus is photography, illustration and indie publishing. A secondary source says foreign-language books are few, so do not go expecting English titles.

3. UHJJUHDAH — Yeonnam

  • Listed on VisitSeoul. 159, Seongmisan-ro, Room 101. Weekdays 13:00–21:00 (break 17:00–18:00), weekends 13:00–20:00, closed the first Wednesday of every month. 800 m from Hongik Univ. Station Exit 3.
  • A curated shop that features one author each month. The official page gives only the Roman spelling, so the Korean name UHJJUHDAH Book Shop, Yeonnam (유정 연남) is our inference.

4. Manhole Coffee Western Bookstore — Yeongdeungpo

  • A VisitSeoul-listed book cafe. Floors 1–3, house-roasted coffee, a Western mountain-lodge concept. 12:00–21:30, closed Mon and Tue. 393 m from Yeongdeungpo-gu Office Station Exit 6.
  • If you want a "bookstore + cafe" in a single building, this is the only place in Seoul we have confirmed.

🌊 Two in Busan

5. Bosu-dong Book Street

  • A used-book alley dating from the 1950s, with about 30 shops. Per VisitBusan, closed on the 1st and 3rd Tuesdays, closing around 20:00 daily (varies by shop). 11 minutes from Jagalchi Station (Line 1) Exit 3.
  • English paperbacks are mentioned only by a blog source, so that is unconfirmed — walk the street and look for yourself.

6. Scuola di Atene

  • A four-story building next to the book street with a facade of stacked books (opened March 2023). Three bookshops on the 1st floor, a cafe on floors 2–4 (bookshelves on floors 3–4). Wed–Mon 10:30–18:00, closed Tue. 63 Daecheong-ro.
  • Paired with Bosu-dong, it is the only "book street + cafe" combination confirmed in Busan.

📌 Not Yet Confirmed — Names Only (Check Instagram Before You Visit)

  • Thanks Books (땡스북스) (Hongdae·Hapjeong, since 2011): a secondary source says daily 12:00–21:00, but its own site fails to open because of a certificate error, so it is unconfirmed.
  • The Foreign Bookstore (외국서적) (Itaewon·Noksapyeong, since 1973, used English-language books): hours differ by source, so unconfirmed. If you want only English books in Seoul, this is the candidate.
  • Still Books (스틸북스) (Sounds Hannam (사운즈 한남)): the site cannot be reached and whether it still operates is unconfirmed.
  • Jeonju: an official tourism-site search turned up no indie bookstore we could confirm, so we dropped it from this guide.

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. Going to an old address — The Book Society (Hoehyeon) and Your Mind (122 Yeonhui-ro) both moved in 2026.

2. Busan on a Tuesday — Bosu-dong is closed on the 1st and 3rd Tuesdays, and Scuola di Atene is closed every Tuesday.

3. Seoul on a Monday — The Book Society and Manhole Coffee Western Bookstore are closed.

4. Expecting English books at indie shops — apart from The Book Society (Korean and international titles), the stock is mostly Korean-language. For used English books, try The Foreign Bookstore in Itaewon (unconfirmed).

⚙ FAQ 5

1. What does a one-day route look like? — Seoul: afternoon at Your Mind in Yeonhui → UHJJUHDAH in Yeonnam → a Yeonnam cafe. Busan: Jagalchi Station → Bosu-dong alley → the Scuola di Atene cafe.

2. Which places have the cafe inside the bookstore? — Only two: Manhole Coffee Western Bookstore (Seoul) and Scuola di Atene (Busan).

3. Can I take photos of the books? — It differs by shop. If there is no sign, ask.

4. How do I pay? — Cards are generally accepted. Small shops may take cash only, so carry a little cash.

5. Why 6 places instead of 5? — The old guide's 5 had no real shop names. We list only confirmed places and dropped Jeonju.
