🎯 Why is it an exclusive guide to Gangbuk and Suyu?

Gangbuk/Suyu is a combination area of Gangbuk-gu's Korean democratization, Bukhansan Ui, and traditional neighborhood — 4/19 National Cemetery (1960 April 19 Revolution, Korea's first democratization site) + Bukhansan Ui Gate (Korea's first urban national park) + Suyu Station·Mia Intersection (neighborhood unknown to foreigners) + Ui·Beon-dong combination. Korean democratization deep dive + hiking Mt. Bukhan + native neighborhood deep dive #1.

Compared to Hongdae (MZ), Gangnam (luxury), and Itaewon (global), Gangbuk and Suyu are democratization, nature, and native-born. Although it is overlooked by Gangnam and Hongdae in the flow of foreign tourists, April 19 Cemetery is the No. 1 site of Korean democratization (burial of the 1960 April Revolution) + Bukhansan Ui (#182 Eunpyeong·#177Nowon pair) = No. 1 deep dive in Korean history and nature for foreigners. ⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. **"Gangbuk = Tourism

Foreigners perceive Gangbuk as “not a tourist area,” but April 19 National Cemetery is the first site of Korean democratization (burial site of the 1960 April Revolution) — The first site of Korean modern history deep dive. Korea's No. 1 deep dive in veterans affairs and democratization (No. 1 place for democratization in Korea other than the National Cemetery).

2. "Bukhansan = Eunpyeong·Nowonman" — Unrecognized value of Ui Gate

Bukhansan Mountain includes Eunpyeong (#182), Nowon (#177) and Ui Gate (northern part of Gangbuk-gu) — 1st place to enter Insubong and Baekundae + Uicheon Bicycle Road. The #1 climbing gate for citizens and foreigners in Gangbuk-gu.

3. "Suyu, lost child = foreigner unknown"

Suyu·Mia is a hidden gem area that few foreigners know about — the 1st deep dive in anthropology of a native Korean/low-class neighborhood + rich in authentic Korean rice cake shops, traditional Korean snacks, and bakeries (deep dive in authentic Korean desserts for foreigners).

Scenery of Gangbuk and Suyu

Gangbuk and Suyu are in the central part of Gangbuk-gu — Line 4 Suyu Station, Mia Intersection Station, Mia Station, Beondong Station + Ui Sinseol Line Bukhansan Ui Station, Samyang Intersection Station, Samyang Station, Solbat Park Station. Native Korean neighborhood in the 1960s and 1980s + 1960 April 19 Revolution Saddle + Bukhansan Ui Gate.

Transportation: Line 4 Suyu Station, Mia Intersection Station, Mia Station, Beondong Station / Ui Sinseol Line Bukhansan Ui Station, Samyang Intersection Station. Kakao Taxi recommended.

⚙ FAQ (6)

Q1. Foreigners allowed into 4/19 Cemetery?

Free + 06:00~18:00 — Free entry for foreigners. No. 1 place for democracy in Korea + English guide. Uisinseolseon 5-minute walk from the April 19th Democratic Cemetery area. Memorial ceremony in the second week of April.

Q2. Climbing Bukhansan Ui Gate?

Available — 10-minute walk from Bukhansan Ui Station on Ui Sinseol Line. No. 1 access to Insubong and Baekundae + Uicheon bike path. The trail is OK for beginners in hiking.

Q3. Breastfeeding/lost child foreigner-friendly?

Available — Although it is a native Korean neighborhood, stores with English menus (Kkosup Restaurant, Lala Bread, etc.) + card payment. Foreigners’ daily life in Korea, deep dive #1.

Q4. Mia Intersection Jongno Rice Cake House and Min Seolgi Foreigner Gift?

OK — Korean traditional rice cake and Korean snacks gift box. 1st choice for souvenirs and gifts for foreigners (#48 TOP 20 essential purchases in Korea).

Q5. Gangbuk + Nowon·Eunpyeong connection?

OK — Transfer to Line 4 and Uisinseol Line. #177 Nowon·Dobong hiking + #182 Eunpyeong·Bukhansan + Gangbuk·Suyu 4·19 Cemetery = 1 day Korean nature and democratization full set.

Q6. 4/19 Cemetery + Bukhansan Mountain route?

4/19 Cemetery (Ui New Seol Line) → Bukhansan Ui Station (1 stop) → Bukhansan Ui Gate Climbing → Uicheon Bicycle → Suyu Station Dinner = 1 full day.
