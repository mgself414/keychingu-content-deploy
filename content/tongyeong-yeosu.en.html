⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Tongyeong (통영) = Anywhere": Tongyeong (통영) = Korea's #1 Fishery Town + Dongpirang Mural Village (동피랑 벽화마을) · Gangguan (강구안) · Jungang Market (중앙시장) + Foreigner Instagram #1 Priority.

2. "Yeosu (여수) = KTX End": Yeosu (여수) = KTX Honam Line Terminus (3 hours) + Korea's #1 Night Sea + 2012 Yeosu Expo (여수 엑스포) + Foreigner Enthusiast #1 Spot.

3. "Namhae (남해) = Small Island": Namhae (남해) = Korea's #2 Largest Island by Area + Daraengi Village (다랭이마을) · Boriam (보리암) · Geumsan (금산) + Foreigner Enthusiast #1 Spot + Sunset #1 Spot.

4. "Geoje (거제) = Can't Go": Geoje (거제) = Korea's #1 Largest Island by Area (after Jeju) + Oedo (외도) · Maemisan (매미산) + Foreigner Enthusiast.

5. "Jinju (진주) = Not Close": Jinju (진주) = Southern Coast Entry Hub + Jinju Fortress (진주성) · Jinju Namgang (남강) Night View + Foreigner Enthusiast #1 Spot.
