Seoul Night Running — Where You Can Run After Dark, Closing Times and What Stops in Winter

Subtitle: There are 3 things to know in advance if you want to run in Seoul after dark: which place closes at what time, what stops in winter, and where to wash up and pick up your bags after the run. Olympic Park ends inside access at 22:00, and the Yeouido Hangang Park shower room also closes at 22:00. The Runner Support Spaces at subway stations are open until 23:00. Banpo's Moonlight Rainbow Fountain runs only from March 15 to October, so it cannot be seen from November to March 14 of the next year. Of the Seoul running courses that appeared in the public vote, we picked 7 that have agency guidance related to the night, and laid them out mainly with values the agencies gave.

ISSUE 06 · #147 · 2026-11-24 · Seoul Night Running

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 7

This piece is written for travelers who sightsee by day and want to run for about an hour in the evening or at night. When you run by day, you only need to know the length and how to get there, but at night there are more things to weigh: the time the park closes, the time the place where you left your bags closes, and facilities that switch off with the season.

There are 2 selection standards. First, it must be a Seoul course that appeared in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme. Second, the managing agency or Seoul must have stated something related to the night (closing times, lighting, facilities used at night). Of the courses that meet both conditions, this piece covers 7: Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도한강공원), Banpo Hangang Park (반포한강공원), Seoul Forest (서울숲), Cheonggyecheon Stream (청계천), Olympic Park (올림픽공원), Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길) and Namsan Northern Circular Road (남산 북측순환로).

The length and directions of all the daytime courses, including Ttukseom Hangang Park (뚝섬한강공원), 9th in the same vote (136 votes), are laid out in #146. This piece cuts the place introductions that overlap with #146 and gathers only the information needed at night.

⚠ Notation principles: Hours, fees, facilities and directions are values read on 2026-11-24 from Seoul Metropolitan Government Hangang Project Headquarters (서울시 미래한강본부) (Hangang parks), Seoul Facilities Corporation (서울시설공단) (Cheonggyecheon Stream), Korea Sports Industry Development (한국체육산업개발) (Olympic Park), Seoul Parks (서울의공원) (parks.seoul.go.kr) (Gyeongui Line Forest Park) and announcements by the Seoul Metropolitan Government. Items the agencies did not state are not listed. For that reason, the times lights turn on and off, sunset times and running times are not in this piece. The walking time to Cheonggye Plaza (청계광장) is a Kakao Map route value (2026-11-24). Votes and ranks are values from the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme. Operating hours and access methods may change.

🚦 Before Running at Night — Common Safety and Manners

None of the 7 is a path made only for running. They are shared with walkers, bicycles and tourists. We write separately the rules set by agencies and the general manners to follow on paths that many people share.

Rules Set by Agencies

  • Bicycle rules in Hangang parks. According to Seoul Metropolitan Government Hangang Project Headquarters, in Hangang parks bicycles ride only on the bike lane, at 20 km/h or less, keep to the right and ride in single file. At night, turn on the light.
  • Cheonggyecheon Stream. You cannot ride bicycles or inline skates, and you cannot bring in animals (except guide dogs for people with disabilities). Smoking and drinking are also on the prohibited list. Running is not on this list.
  • Olympic Park. After 22:00 you cannot enter deeper into the park. On the walking path inside the earthen fortress, pedestrians have priority and dogs must be on a leash.
  • Hangang parks. Non-smoking. The fine is 50,000 won if a dog is not on a leash, and 100,000 won for leaving trash behind.

Night Path Manners

The points below are not regulations but manners on a path you share.

  • Runners do not enter the bike lane. Because bicycles are supposed to ride only on the bike lane, runners using the walking path means fewer collisions. The Banpo Hangang Park introduction says the bike lane and the walking path are separated.
  • Wear bright clothes or reflective material. Dark clothes come into the view of bicycles and other pedestrians late.
  • Keep your earphone volume low. You need to hear the bell of a bicycle coming from behind and the announcements. Even if it is not raining around Cheonggyecheon Stream, an evacuation announcement can be broadcast.
  • Slow down on narrow paths. Cheonggyecheon Stream and the Yeonnam-dong (연남동) section of Gyeongui Line Forest Park are in the middle of tourist areas. Walk in crowded sections.

In an Emergency

  • For an accident or emergency, call 119; to report a crime, call 112.
  • If you are stranded at Cheonggyecheon Stream by sudden rain, call the Cheonggyecheon Comprehensive Control Room at 02-2290-7111~3.

① Hangang — 2 Places

The directions pages for Yeouido and Banpo Hangang Parks list the Information Center enforcement duty as 00:00~24:00 and administrative work as 09:00~18:00.

Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도한강공원)

  • Public vote: 162 votes · 2nd in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • Facilities used at night: An open shower room where you can wash after running is on the 1st floor of the park Information Center. It opens daily 06:30~22:00. Inside Line 5 Yeouinaru Station there is Runner Station (러너스테이션), with a changing room and lockers. The details of the two facilities are written below in "Places to Wash Up and Leave Bags".
  • Closing time: The shower room closes at 22:00. You must finish a shower within 20 minutes, so set the time you finish running well before 22:00.
  • How to get there:
  • Line 5 Yeouinaru Station Exits 2 and 3: 1 minute on foot, the center of the park. The elevator is near Exit 2.
  • Line 9 National Assembly Station (국회의사당역) Exit 1: 1 minute on foot, behind the National Assembly.
  • What stops in winter: The fountain at Mulbit Plaza (물빛광장) runs only from April to October.

Banpo Hangang Park (반포한강공원)

  • Public vote: 143 votes · 6th in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (달빛무지개분수): A fountain installed on Banpo Bridge (반포대교) with a total length of 1,140 m. Seoul Metropolitan Government Hangang Project Headquarters introduces it as using scenic lighting at night to stage a night view. Viewing is free, with no reservation, on site.
  • Operating period: From March 15 to October. From November to March 14 of the next year it does not run. If you run Banpo from November to March 14, you will be running along a riverside without the fountain.
  • Shows (20 minutes each): From March 15 to May, and in October: 12:00, 19:30, 20:00, 20:30 and 21:00. From June to September, 21:30 is added to these. There is no weekday and weekend distinction. Looking only at the evening shows, they run at 30-minute intervals from 19:30.
  • When it stops running: When wind speed is 7 m/s or more, when rain is forecast during operating hours, or when Hangang turbidity is 15 NTU or more. Times may change if there is an event. Inquiries 02-3780-0578~9.
  • Jamsugyo Bridge (잠수교): According to the park introduction, Jamsugyo Bridge has reduced car lanes, a widened sidewalk and a bicycle-only lane.
  • How to get there:
  • Line 9 Sinbanpo Station Exit 1: enter through Banpo Interchange (반포나들목), about 15 minutes on foot.
  • Lines 3, 7 and 9 Express Bus Terminal Station (고속터미널역) Exits 8-1 and 8-2 (or GOTO Mall (고투몰) G1): about 15 minutes on foot toward Banpo Bridge.
  • Lines 4 and 9 Dongjak Station (동작역) Exits 1 and 2: walk along the Hangang toward Seoraeseom Island (서래섬).
  • From any station there is walking time to the park. To match the show times, add this time when you set out.
  • What stops in winter: The Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (November to March 14). The Hangang shuttle cart (Haechi Car) (한강순환관람차(해치카)) in the park operates from May to November, and runs until 20:00 (Monday to Friday 14~20, weekends 11~20, closed 16~17).

② Parks and Forest Paths — 3 Places

The 3 have different night situations. Seoul stated that Seoul Forest is open at night, and Olympic Park ends inside access at 22:00. For Gyeongui Line Forest Park, the Seoul Parks guide lists 2 park restrooms, and 1 of them closes in the evening.

Seoul Forest (서울숲)

  • Public vote: 130 votes · 15th in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • Hours: The Seoul announcement (2026-10-30) says Seoul Forest is open 24 hours a day, year-round. Some facilities, such as the Ecological Forest (생태숲), have separate hours.
  • At night: A Seoul article of 2026-10-30 introduced an autumn night garden walk following the lights after work.
  • Park paths: According to the Seoul announcement, the core park paths inside Seoul Forest, 22,747.6 m in total, are pedestrian-only paths, so you must walk your bicycle.
  • How to get there: From Suin-Bundang Line Seoul Forest Station Exit 4 to the park is about 2 minutes on foot.

Olympic Park (올림픽공원)

  • Public vote: 118 votes · 24th in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • Closing time: This is the only one of the 7 that states the time inside access ends.
  • Entry on foot and by bicycle is 05:00~22:00.
  • The plaza area is open until 24:00.
  • After 22:00 you cannot enter deeper into the park.
  • To protect cultural heritage, the Mongchontoseong Fortress (몽촌토성) zone blocks entry inside outside of operating hours.
  • On days with late-night events, the times the gates open and close may be adjusted.
  • Fees: Using the park is free, and parking is charged separately.
  • Courses to choose at night: There are 5 paths that Korea Sports Industry Development, which operates the park, guides as "walking/jogging courses". Each has a set lap distance, so it is easy to choose a length you can finish before 22:00.
  • Hobanui-gil (Lakeside Road, 호반의 길) 1,302 m, Toseongui-gil (Fortress Road, 토성의 길) 2,210 m, Jeoleumui-gil (Youth Road, 젊음의 길) 3,150 m, Chueogui-gil (Memory Road, 추억의 길) 3,350 m and Yeoninui-gil (Lovers' Road, 연인의 길) 4,290 m. The route of each course is in #146.
  • Toseongui-gil (Fortress Road), Chueogui-gil (Memory Road) and Yeoninui-gil (Lovers' Road) pass through the Mongchontoseong Fortress zone. This zone blocks entry outside operating hours, so avoid it late at night.
  • Jeoleumui-gil (Youth Road) is a bicycle loop road. Run while watching for bicycles.
  • How to get there: There are 3 stations, on different lines.
  • Line 8 Mongchontoseong Station Exit 1
  • Lines 5 and 9 Olympic Park Station (올림픽공원역) Exits 3 and 4
  • Line 9 Hanseong Baekje Station (한성백제역) Exit 2
  • The park guide groups East Gate 1 on the Olympic Park Station side, and South Gate 4 and Peace Gate Plaza (평화의문광장) on the Mongchontoseong Station and Hanseong Baekje Station side.

Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길)

  • Public vote: 104 votes · 33rd in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • What to weigh at night — restrooms: The Seoul Parks guide lists 2 park restrooms. The management office restroom opens only on weekdays 09:00~18:00, and the Yeomni-dong restroom is open 24 hours. The only park restroom you can use after 6 p.m. is the one in Yeomni-dong. Use the restroom at the station before you run.
  • Length: The Seoul Parks guide lists the whole at about 6.3 km. The Yeonnam-dong section is about 1.2 km. Choose one section and run it back and forth.
  • How to get there: Each section has different stations.
  • Yeonnam-dong section: Hongik Univ. Station (Line 2, Airport Railroad, Gyeongui-Jungang Line) and Gajwa Station (가좌역) (Gyeongui-Jungang Line). The Seoul announcement (2025-03) lists it as 438 m from Hongik Univ. Station Exit 3.
  • Wau Bridge (와우교) section: Hongik Univ. Station, Sogang Univ. Station (서강대역) (Gyeongui-Jungang Line)
  • Sinsu, Daeheung and Yeomni-dong (신수·대흥·염리동) section: Line 6 Gongdeok Station (공덕역), Line 6 Daeheung Station (대흥역)
  • Saechanggogae and Wonhyo-ro (새창고개·원효로) section: Line 6 Hyochang Park Station (효창공원앞역)

③ City Center — 2 Places

Cheonggyecheon Stream (청계천)

  • Public vote: 128 votes · 18th in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • At night: Seoul Facilities Corporation lists the lighting of Cheonggyecheon Stream as 10,419 lights.
  • Length: Seoul Facilities Corporation lists the total length as 8.12 km and the restored section as 5.84 km.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map route, it is 3 minutes on foot (about 120 m) from Line 5 Gwanghwamun Station Exit 5 to Cheonggye Plaza. Inside Gwanghwamun Station there is a Runner Support Space (러너지원공간) open until 23:00.
  • It closes when it rains: Cheonggyecheon Stream is a place that closes by weather, not by time.
  • Entry is closed when floodgates are expected to open because of rain.
  • Even if it is not raining around Cheonggyecheon Stream, leave the stream right away if an evacuation announcement is broadcast. Water also rises from rain that fell upstream.
  • When the water level rises, climb up through the nearest exit following the announcements and the safety staff.
  • Do not shelter from rain under a bridge. When it rains, floodgates suddenly open and under a bridge is dangerous.
  • There are emergency ladders at 39 places for people stranded by sudden rain.
  • What you cannot do: Using bicycles and inline skates, bringing in animals (except guide dogs for people with disabilities), fishing, swimming, camping and cooking, and smoking and drinking. If you plan to run with a dog, choose another place.

Namsan Northern Circular Road (남산 북측순환로)

  • Public vote: 85 votes · 59th in the Korea Tourism Organization "100×100 Spot Discovery" public vote on the running course theme.
  • At night: A Seoul article (2018-08) says of this path that when night falls, the lights come on one by one and the charm of the fortress wall path deepens.
  • Cars and bicycles: Seoul introduced this road as a walking path where car and bicycle traffic is entirely banned and only pedestrians use it. The full ban on vehicles began in June 1996.
  • Section and distance: The Seoul article lists about 3.3 km from the Education Science Institute (교육과학원) to the National Theater of Korea (국립중앙극장). A round trip is about 6.6 km. In a map app, set as the destination whichever of the two is closer to your accommodation.
  • Do not confuse it with the Southern Circular Road: According to a Seoul article (2025-06), Namsan Southern Circular Road (남측순환로) is a road shared by pedestrians and cars, with vehicles and bicycles running one way. Because Namsan Southern Circular Road is a road where cars and bicycles run one way, this piece covers only the north side.

🚿 Places to Wash Up and Leave Bags

When you run at night, you often head out right away without going back to your accommodation. We list places where you can leave bags and change clothes, and places where you can wash, together with their closing times.

  • Runner Support Spaces (러너지원공간) at 3 subway stations: Seoul announced them in October 2025. They are at Gwanghwamun Station (Line 5), Hoehyeon Station (회현역) (Line 4) and World Cup Stadium Station (월드컵경기장역) (Line 6). They open daily 05:30~23:00 and run in line with subway service hours. Seoul stated there is no separate usage fee for entry. Entry is by Naver QR or KakaoTalk verification. There are men's and women's changing rooms, personal lockers, a powder room and a vending machine for running gear. If you start from Cheonggye Plaza, there is one at Gwanghwamun Station.
  • Open shower room at Yeouido Hangang Park: It opens daily 06:30~22:00. 08:30~09:30 and 15:30~16:30 are cleaning times. It is free, and you bring your own towel and toiletries. Entry is by Naver app QR code verification. There are 5 shower booths each for men and women, 13 lockers and 2 changing rooms. Finish your shower within 20 minutes; laundry is not allowed. For inquiries, call the Yeouido Information Center at 02-3780-0563.
  • Runner Station (러너스테이션) (Yeouinaru Station): A facility Seoul announced in May 2024. It is on floors B1 to M1 inside Line 5 Yeouinaru Station. It has a changing room, a powder room and 58 lockers (42 on floor M1, 16 on floor B1).

Runner Support Spaces are entered by Naver QR or KakaoTalk verification, and the shower room by Naver app QR verification. Before the day you plan to use them, first see whether the verification screen opens on your own phone. In case verification does not work, also make a plan to change clothes at your accommodation before heading out.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to Banpo in winter to see the fountain — The Moonlight Rainbow Fountain runs only from March 15 to October. From November to March 14 of the next year, no fountain comes on even if you go in the evening. The Mulbit Plaza fountain at Yeouido also runs only from April to October.
  2. Planning Olympic Park as a place to run late at night — Entry on foot is until 22:00, and after that you cannot enter deeper into the park. Only the plaza area is open until 24:00. If you run in the evening, plan your time so that you leave before 22:00.
  3. Running past the closing time of the place where you left your bags — The Runner Support Spaces at subway stations close at 23:00 and the Yeouido Hangang Park shower room at 22:00. Count in the time to come back after finishing your run. The shower room has a 20-minute limit, so if you arrive just before 22:00 there will not be enough time to wash.

⚙ 3 FAQs

  1. Where is open until late at night? — Seoul stated that Seoul Forest is open 24 hours a day, year-round. At Olympic Park, entry on foot is until 22:00, and only the plaza area is open until 24:00. The enforcement duty at the Yeouido and Banpo Information Centers is 00:00~24:00. Cheonggyecheon Stream closes entry when floodgates are expected to open because of rain.
  2. What facilities stop in winter? — Banpo's Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (runs March 15 to October), the Mulbit Plaza fountain at Yeouido (runs April to October) and the Hangang shuttle cart (Haechi Car) at Banpo Hangang Park (operates May to November).
  3. Is there a place to wash after running? — The open shower room is on the 1st floor of the Yeouido Hangang Park Information Center. Daily 06:30~22:00, free, entry by Naver app QR code verification. Bring a towel and toiletries. The Runner Support Spaces at 3 subway stations are places where you use changing rooms and personal lockers.
