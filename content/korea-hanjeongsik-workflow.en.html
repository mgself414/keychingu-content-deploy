Korean Hanjeongsik Reservation, Dress Code, and Etiquette Workflow — From Shilla La Yeon 1-2 Weeks Prior Reservation to 6-Course Meal

🎯 Why a Korean Hanjeongsik Workflow Guide?

Even if you know the #831 Hanjeongsik 6-Course Matrix — the reservation, etiquette, and payment process are separate. Korean Hanjeongsik (한정식) (Korean set meal) involves (a) reservation systems (CatchTable (캐치테이블), Naver, phone 1-2 weeks in advance) (b) dress code (hotels = smart casual / Insadong (인사동) = casual) (c) 6-course serving order and etiquette (juk (죽) → jeon (전) → juyori (주요리) → tang (탕) → bap (밥) → husik (후식)) (d) free unlimited banchan (반찬) (Korean side dishes) refills (e) 10% service charge + 10% VAT (hotels). Foreigners often (1) find reservation systems only in Korean (2) are unaware of dress codes (3) get confused by the course order (4) are surprised by service charges when paying. This guide provides a 5-step workflow + etiquette matrix + English phrases + authentic hotel tips.

🍽 6-Course Etiquette

1️⃣ Juk (죽) Etiquette

  • Order: Served first (to whet the appetite)
  • Etiquette: Small bowl = eat in small spoonfuls / pepper can be added

2️⃣ Jeon (전) Etiquette

  • Order: Second (appetizers · banchan (반찬) (free Korean side dishes) begin)
  • Etiquette: No fingers (use chopsticks) / Pair with makgeolli (막걸리) (Korean rice wine) · cheongju (청주) (Korean traditional clear rice wine)

3️⃣ Juyori (주요리, Main Dish) Etiquette

  • Order: Middle · Main (1-3 courses)
  • Etiquette: Galbijjim (갈비찜) (braised short ribs) · Bulgogi (불고기) (marinated grilled beef) = chopsticks + some knife/fork / Gujeolpan (구절판) (platter of nine delicacies) = select from 9 compartments

4️⃣ Tang (탕) Etiquette

  • Order: After main dishes or with rice
  • Etiquette: Sinseollo (신선로) (royal hot pot) = staff boils → serve one portion at a time

5️⃣ Bap (밥) Etiquette

  • Order: Almost last (finishing the meal)
  • Etiquette: Banchan (반찬) (free Korean side dishes) 12+ types = eat a variety / Kimchi (김치) (fermented cabbage) · Kkakdugi (깍두기) (diced radish kimchi) free unlimited refills ("Kimchi (김치) jom deo juseyo" - "More kimchi (김치), please")

6️⃣ Husik (후식) Etiquette

  • Order: Last
  • Etiquette: Pair with yakgwa (약과) (traditional honey cookie) · sujeonggwa (수정과) (cinnamon punch) · sikhye (식혜) (sweet rice punch) · tea

🙏 7 Key Hanjeongsik Etiquette Points

Korean Dining Etiquette (Traditional)

1. Elders start first: Begin eating after the eldest person picks up their spoon and chopsticks.

2. Use of utensils: Chopsticks + spoon (Korean style).

3. Do not lift bowls: Bowls = keep on the table while eating (traditional Korean).

4. No noise: Avoid slurping or smacking sounds (traditional formality).

5. Rice · Soup = Spoon: Use a spoon for rice and soup.

6. Banchan (반찬) · Jeon (전) · Grilled = Chopsticks: Use chopsticks for side dishes.

7. Lower your glass when toasting elders: Alcohol etiquette (#829_korean_alcohol_etiquette).

💰 Payment Workflow

Hotel Hanjeongsik

1. Card payment (room charge OR individual)

2. 10% service charge + 10% VAT automatically added

3. Total price = Menu + 20%

4. No tipping (Korea = no tipping culture)

General Hanjeongsik (Insadong · Jeonju · Nofo)

1. Card payment OR some cash

2. No service charge (general restaurants)

3. VAT = included in menu price (no separate charge)

4. No tipping

Split Payment

  • Hotels: One person pays → room charge OR transfer
  • General: One person pays → Kakao Pay (카카오페이) · Toss (토스) (Korean mobile payment services) transfer

⚠ 7 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "No reservation = OK": Shilla La Yeon (라연) · Samcheonggak (삼청각) = 1-2 weeks prior reservation essential.

2. "No dress code": Hotels = smart casual (no jeans · sleeveless tops · shorts).

3. "6 courses served together": 6 courses = served in order (1.5-3h).

4. "Service charge = tip": Korea = no tipping. Hotel service charge 10% = automatically billed.

5. "Banchan (반찬) = 1 refill": Free unlimited refills (request "More, please").

6. "Lifting bowls": Traditional Korean = bowls = kept on the table; use spoon · chopsticks.

7. "Takeaway = X": Some restaurants = takeaway possible ("Pojangiyo (포장이요)" - "Takeaway, please"). Hotels = generally no.

2. Instagram · Vlog: Shilla La Yeon (라연) (Michelin 3-star) · Samcheonggak (삼청각) · Insadong (인사동) hanok (한옥) (traditional Korean house) Hanjeongsik (한정식).

⚠ Medical · Safety · Dietary Disclaimer

Hanjeongsik (한정식) = 6-course meal (1.5-3h). Pregnant women · immunocompromised individuals = cooked menu recommended (avoid raw fish · sashimi courses). Pre-notify for allergies (seafood · beef · pork · egg · nuts · honey). If food poisoning is suspected ☎ 1339 (medical, English). This guide provides general information, not medical advice.

Restaurant Information Disclaimer: Hotel Korean restaurants · Insadong (인사동) Hanjeongsik (한정식) operations · prices · menus may change — check NAVER/Kakao map or official websites before visiting.

Dietary Restriction Fallback:

  • Halal · Kosher: General Hanjeongsik (한정식) = non-halal. → Sachal Eumsik (사찰음식) (temple food) Hanjeongsik (한정식) (#800_temple_food).
  • Vegetarian · Vegan: Sachal Eumsik (사찰음식) (temple food) OR request vegetarian Hanjeongsik (한정식) in advance (japchae (잡채) (stir-fried glass noodles) · jeon (전) (savory pancake) · jjigae (찌개) (stew) vegetarian options).
  • Gluten-free: Hanjeongsik (한정식) sauces (ganjang (간장) (soy sauce) · gochujang (고추장) (chili paste)) are common. Check with the restaurant in advance.
