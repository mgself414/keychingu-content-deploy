⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Korean cafe = same as Starbucks": 4th axis of Korean cafe — Authentic long-established store (Haklim, Imunseolnongtang) + Trendy MZ (Noted, LBM) + Global (Blue Bottle, MAILLET) + Chain cost-effective (Mega Coffee). 4 results require a deep dive.

2. "Haklim = just a cafe": Haklim is Korea's first authentic cafe in 1956 — a Sarangbang for Korean intellectuals including Park Kyeong-ni, Lee Ho-cheol, and Kim Ji-ha. World cafe history = Starts with Haklim in Korea.

3. "LBM = Korean Bagel": LBM (London Bagel Museum) 1st branch in Anguk in 2018 → 4th branch in Jamsil, Yeouido and Gasan = The starting point of the Korean bagel trend. No. 1 on foreign Instagram.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Korean Cafe 4 Axis: Authentic long-established store / Trendy MZ / Global specialty / Chain cost-effectiveness.

2. No. 1 cafe on Instagram for foreigners: Haklim (authentic), MAILET (French), LBM (bagel), Noted (donuts), Blue Bottle (specialty).

3. 24-hour cafe: Mega Coffee, some 24-hour Ediya stores, and Hongdae 24-hour cafe street.

4. K-pop·K-drama collaboration cafe: YG·SM season limited + Kim Min-kyung, born in 1982 (Mapo) + D Museum.

5. Korean cafe price: Cost-effective 1,500~3,000 (Mega/Ediya) / Regular 5,000~7,500 / Specialty 6,500~9,500.

6. Cafe Manners: Wi-Fi, outlets, 24-hour access + foreign-friendly English menu + credit card payment.
