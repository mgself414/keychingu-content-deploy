Lotte World School Uniform Rental, No-Panic Manual — Cash Deposit and Every Step Down to the 9 PM Return

Subtitle: Why everyone's wearing a school uniform at the amusement park · the basic set pricing system · rental master flow · the deposit trap (foreigners = cash only) · the weekend 1-hour wait · handling the return deadline

ISSUE 04 · #1397 · 2026-09-22 · korea-first-timer series ㊿

🎯 What This Piece Covers — And What It Doesn't

This piece doesn't cover how to pick a uniform style or pose ideas. It covers only "exactly what steps you go through when renting a school uniform at Lotte World, and what you pay for the deposit, in what amount." In particular, it tackles head-on the point where international visitors most often get stuck on-site — the fact that the deposit is cash-only.

① Why a School Uniform — the Amusement-Park Version of Hanbok

Just as people wear hanbok to visit the palaces (#1296), wearing a school uniform to Lotte World has become the standard play script for Korea's MZ generation (Millennials and Gen Z) — search Lotte World on Instagram and the feed fills up with people in school uniforms. It's a cosplay-style experience of spending the day in the very uniform from a K-drama campus romance, but unlike hanbok, there's no admission-fee perk — the point isn't saving money, it's the content itself. Rental shops cluster in and around Lotte World, and the procedures are already standardized around foreign customers — ID and deposit rules explicitly spell out a separate clause for foreigners.

  • The rental is a full same-day deal, and the deadline is set by the return time (⑥) — it's not charged by the hour, so the earlier you rent in the morning, the more you get out of it.
  • Size exchanges are unlimited — try it on and swap as many times as you need if it doesn't fit. Even if you're on the larger side, shops carry larger sizes in stock, so just ask.

③ Rental Master Flow — From Reservation to Return

④ The Deposit Trap — Foreigners Need "₩10,000 Cash + ID," No Cards Allowed

This is the whole reason this piece exists. Standard rules at Jamsil rental shops:

Item Rule
ID held by the shop Group of 1–3 = 1 ID · 4 or more = 2 IDs (passport accepted)
Deposit Students and foreigners pay ₩10,000 cash per person — fully refunded on a normal return
Payment method The deposit is cash only — no substituting card, bank transfer, student ID, or check card
  • Hanbok rental (#1296) gives you a choice between "passport vs. ₩50,000 cash," but here there's no choice — holding your ID and a cash deposit come as a set.
  • So bring ₩10,000 cash per person before you go in. Stepping out of Lotte World and back just to withdraw cash is the single biggest time sink on this outing.
  • If handing over a passport makes you uneasy, only one person in the group needs to leave theirs (1 ID for groups of 1–3) — it's standard industry practice to get it back the moment you return the uniform.

⑤ The Weekend Trap — Even With a Reservation, You Wait an Hour

On weekends, public holidays, and Korean holidays, it gets busy enough that shops post notices of an hour or more wait regardless of your time slot. The field-tested formula:

  • Visiting on a weekday is best; if it has to be a weekend, arriving right at opening (around 9 AM) is the next-best option.
  • The Adventure B1 branch (Gamsunggyobok (감성교복) Lotte World branch) is open 09:00–21:30, every day of the year — the standard route is to change right after opening and start your day.
  • If you arrive in the afternoon, an outside branch (around Jamsil Station) may be relatively less crowded.

⑥ Where to Rent — 3 Spots in Jamsil + Busan

  • Gamsunggyobok Lotte World branch (Adventure B1 · 09:00–21:30, every day) — 1 minute from the ticket booth, with the strength of changing right after you enter.
  • Gamsunggyobok Jamsil Main Branch (outside Lotte World) — return by 21:00.
  • Gangnamgyobok (강남교복) Jamsil branch (5 minutes on foot from Jamsil Station · same-day booking available) / Ihwagyobok (이화교복) and other nearby shops — prices are roughly similar across the board, so just pick based on the wait.
  • Lotte World Adventure Busan also has a Gamsunggyobok branch — the rules (ID + cash deposit) are the same as Jamsil.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Showing up without cash — the deposit doesn't take cards. This one trap alone can cost you a 30-minute round trip to an ATM.

2. Going on a weekend afternoon without a reservation — some days even people with reservations wait an hour. Go on a weekday or right after opening instead.

3. Not asking the return time — the deadline (21:00, etc.) differs by branch. When you rent, save the return time as a photo or note and set an alarm.

⚙ 3 FAQs

1. Is there an admission discount like with hanbok? — No. A school uniform is a content-driven experience, not a perk-driven one — you buy your Lotte World admission ticket separately (see the #82 Jamsil piece for pre-purchasing).

2. If it's a couple or a group of friends, does everyone have to hand over their ID? — No. Groups of 1–3 hand over 1 ID, and groups of 4 or more hand over 2. The deposit (₩10,000 cash) is charged per person.

3. I'm worried about sizing — Exchanges are unlimited, and larger sizes are in stock too. Try it on at the shop and decide from there.
