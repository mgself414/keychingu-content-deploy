⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Russian Tourists

1. "Korea-Russia LCC direct flights are common" — Unaware of transfer detours: Since 2022, direct flights between Korea and Russia have been reduced → transfer patterns via Vladivostok, Moscow, or Khabarovsk + connecting flights (China, Japan, Central Asia). Book tickets 1-2 months in advance + Korea visa-free for 60 days within 24h transfer.

2. "DMZ (Demilitarized Zone) = just a tourist spot" — Not fully utilizing the Korean War + Russian participation enthusiast experience: While Russians are #1 DMZ (Demilitarized Zone) enthusiasts, they often only purchase English/Russian 'DMZ Tour' packages → lacking a full experience. The actual full experience is the Imjingak (임진각) + DMZ Peace Park (DMZ 평화공원) + Dorasan Observatory (도라산 전망대) + The 3rd Tunnel (제3땅굴) + War Memorial of Korea (전쟁기념관, Yongsan) + Seoul National Cemetery (국립서울현충원, Dongjak) 5-pillar enthusiast course.

3. "Lack of Russian menus" — Unaware of insufficient English/Russian-friendly infrastructure: Very few establishments have Russian menus. 100% English-friendly is generally OK, but Russian is limited to some hotels and DMZ (Demilitarized Zone) packages. Utilizing English/Russian dictionaries or translation apps is essential.

① DMZ, Peace, War Memorial (3)

1. DMZ Peace Park (DMZ 평화공원) & Imjingak (임진각)

  • Signature: The #1 destination for Korean War + Russian (Soviet) participation enthusiasts. A 5-stop route: Imjingak Peace Park (임진각 평화공원) + Bridge of Freedom (자유의 다리) + Peace Bell (평화의 종) + Dorasan Observatory (도라산 전망대) + The 3rd Tunnel (제3땅굴) + Tongil (Unification) Observatory (통일전망대).
  • International-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English and some Russian guides, KTO (Korea Tourism Organization) English DMZ (Demilitarized Zone) packages
  • Recommended Route: Imjingak (임진각) → Bridge of Freedom (자유의 다리) → Dorasan Observatory (도라산 전망대) → The 3rd Tunnel (제3땅굴) → Tongil Observatory (통일전망대) (day trip, 8 hrs)
  • Tip: Take the KTX from Seoul Station (서울역) or Line 1 toward Imjingak (임진각), plus a shuttle. An English-language KTO DMZ tour is recommended. Passing a Civilian Control Line (민통선) checkpoint is required.

2. War Memorial of Korea (전쟁기념관, Yongsan 용산)

  • Signature: Korea's #1 war museum + free admission + some English/Russian information. A full Korean War + Russian (Soviet) participation enthusiast experience.
  • International-Friendliness: ★★★★★ — English tours 5 times a day, English QR codes, free
  • Recommended Route: 1F (Korean War) → 2F (ancient and modern warfare) → 3F (navy and air force) → 4F (special exhibits) (4–5 hrs total)
  • Tip: Directly connected to Samgakji Station (삼각지역, Lines 4·6). Closed on Mondays. Don't miss the outdoor exhibits (tanks, fighter jets, artillery).

3. Seoul National Cemetery (국립서울현충원)

  • Signature: Korea's #1 national cemetery (est. 1956). Korean War / June 25th (6·25) war dead + graves of presidents and generals.
  • International-Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English information, free
  • Recommended Route: Main gate → Korean War (6·25) war dead memorial → presidential graves (Syngman Rhee 이승만, Park Chung-hee 박정희, Kim Young-sam 김영삼, Kim Dae-jung 김대중, etc.) → Han River (한강) view
  • Tip: 10-min walk from Dongjak Station (동작역, Lines 4·9). Free, open 09:00–18:00.

② Royal Palace, Tradition (3)

4. Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) & Royal Guard Changing Ceremony (수문장 교대)

  • Signature: The largest palace of the Joseon (조선) Dynasty. The Royal Guard Changing Ceremony at 10:00 and 14:00 is the signature moment. Free admission when wearing hanbok (한복).
  • International-Friendliness: ★★★★★ — English and some Russian guides
  • Tip: Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역), Exit 5. Closed on Tuesdays.

5. Changdeokgung Palace (창덕궁) Secret Garden (후원, UNESCO)

  • Signature: UNESCO (United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization) World Heritage since 1997. An English guide is essential for the Huwon (후원, Secret Garden).
  • International-Friendliness: ★★★★★ — English tours 4 times a day
  • Tip: 5-min walk from Anguk Station (안국역). Closed on Mondays. Book the English Huwon (후원) tour in advance.

6. Bukchon Hanbok (북촌 한복) & Insadong (인사동)

  • Signature: 1920s hanok (한옥, traditional Korean houses) + hanbok (한복) + traditional refreshments in Insadong (인사동).
  • Tip: 7-min walk from Anguk Station (안국역). Hanbok (한복) rental costs 15,000–30,000 KRW.

③ Foodie (2)

7. Gwangjang Market (광장시장) Korean Food Enthusiast

  • Signature: Mayak gimbap (마약김밥, "addictive" mini gimbap), bindaetteok (빈대떡, mung bean pancake), kkwabaegi (꽈배기, twisted doughnut), Jinokhwa Dakhanmari (진옥화 닭한마리).
  • Tip: Jongno 5-ga Station (종로5가역). Quietest on weekdays 14:00–17:00.

8. Myeongdong (명동) Korean BBQ & Street Food

  • Signature: A Korean BBQ enthusiast destination + street food (hotteok 호떡, bungeoppang 붕어빵, skewers).
  • Tip: Myeongdong Station (명동역). BBQ runs 25,000–40,000 KRW per person; street food 2,000–5,000 KRW.

④ Hallyu, Shopping (4)

9. HYBE HQ (사옥) & Weverse

  • Signature: A pilgrimage site for Russian ARMY (BTS's fanbase, 500,000+) and NewJeans enthusiasts.
  • Tip: 10-min walk from Yongsan Station (용산역), or 5 min from Hangangjin Station (한강진역).

10. Myeongdong (명동) K-pop Goods & LINE FRIENDS

  • Signature: A cluster of Weverse + LINE FRIENDS + SM/JYP goods stores.
  • Tip: Myeongdong Station (명동역). Tax-free.

11. Olive Young Myeongdong Town (올리브영 명동타운점)

  • Signature: 95% of customers are foreign tourists — a Russian K-beauty enthusiast hotspot.
  • Tip: Myeongdong Station (명동역), Exit 9. Tax-free on purchases over 50,000 KRW.

12. Lotte Mart Seoul Station (롯데마트 서울역점)

  • Signature: 40%+ foreign customers — ramyeon (라면), snacks, beauty products, and Banana Milk (바나나맛 우유).
  • Tip: 3-min walk from Seoul Station (서울역). Luggage storage available for 3 hrs.
