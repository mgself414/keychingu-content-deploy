① DMZ·Peace·War Memorial (3)

1. DMZ Peace Park & Imjingak

  • Signature: No. 1 fan of the Korean War + Russia (Soviet Union). Imjingak Peace Park + Freedom Bridge + Peace Bell + Dorasan Observatory + 3rd Tunnel + Unification Observatory 5th axis.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English/partial Russian guide, KTO DMZ English package
  • Recommended course: Imjingak → Freedom Bridge → Dorasan Observatory → 3rd Tunnel → Unification Observatory (1 day trip 8h)
  • Tip: Seoul Station KTX or Line 1 to Imjingak + shuttle. KTO English DMZ Tour recommended. Need to pass the Civilian Control Line checkpoint.

2. War Memorial of Korea, Yongsan

  • Signature: Korea #1 War Museum + Free admission + Some information in English and Russian. Full experience for enthusiasts who participated in the Korean War and Russia (Soviet Union).
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — English guide 5 times a day, English QR, free
  • Recommended route: 1st floor (Korean War) → 2nd floor (Ancient/Modern War) → 3rd floor (Navy/Air Force) → 4th floor (Special War) (total 4-5 hours)
  • Tip: Directly connected to Samgak area (Lines 4 and 6). Closed on Mondays. Full experience of outdoor exhibitions (tanks, fighter planes, cannons).

3. Seoul National Cemetery

  • Signature: Korea #1 National Cemetery (1956). Korean War·6·25 dead + president and general graves.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English guide, free
  • Recommended route: Main gate → Korean War Memorial Cemetery → Presidential Mausoleum (Rhee Syngman, Park Chung-hee, Kim Young-sam, Kim Dae-jung, etc.) → Han River view
  • Tip: 10-minute walk from Dongjak Station (Lines 4 and 9). Free, 09:00~18:00.

② Old palaces and traditions (3)

4. Gyeongbokgung Palace + Changing of the Guards (Gyeongbokgung Palace)

  • Signature: Joseon’s largest palace. Guardian change at 10:00 and 14:00 Signature. Hanbok admission fee waived.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — Some guides in English and Russian
  • Tip: Gyeongbokgung Station Exit 5. Closed on Tuesdays.

5. Sponsorship of Changdeokgung Palace (Changdeokgung Secret Garden, UNESCO)

  • Signature: UNESCO 1997. Sponsorship (Secret Garden) English guide required.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — English guide 4 times a day
  • Tip: 5 minutes walk from Anguk Station. Closed on Mondays. Reserve your sponsored English guide in advance.

6. Bukchon Hanbok + Insadong

  • Signature: 1920s Hanok + Hanbok + Insa-dong traditional refreshments.
  • Tip: 7 minutes walk from Anguk Station. Hanbok 15,000~30,000 won.

③ Gourmet (2)

7. Gwangjang Market for Russians

  • Signature: Drug gimbap, Bindaetteok, Twist, Jinokhwa chicken.
  • Tip: Jongno 5-ga Station. Quiet on weekdays from 14:00 to 17:00.

8. Myeongdong Korean BBQ + Street Food

  • Signature: Korean BBQ enthusiast + street food (hotteok, bungeobbang, skewers).
  • Tip: Myeongdong Station. BBQ 25,000~40,000 won per person, street food 2,000~5,000 won.

④ Korean Wave/Shopping (4)

9. HYBE Headquarters + Weverse (HYBE HQ Yongsan)

  • Signature: BTS·NewJeans Russian ARMY 500,000+ mania sanctuary.

10. Myeongdong K-pop Goods + Line Friends (Myeongdong K-pop Goods)

  • Signature: Weverse + Line Friends + SM·JYP goods cluster.
  • Tip: Myeongdong Station. Tax free.

11. Olive Young Myeongdong Town

  • Signature: 95% foreigners — Russian K-beauty enthusiasts.
  • Tip: Myeongdong Station Exit 9. Tax free 50,000 won+.
