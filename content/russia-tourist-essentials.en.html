⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Russian Tourists

1. "Korea-Russia LCC direct flights are common" — Unaware of transfer detours: Since 2022, direct flights between Korea and Russia have been reduced → transfer patterns via Vladivostok, Moscow, or Khabarovsk + connecting flights (China, Japan, Central Asia). Book tickets 1-2 months in advance + Korea visa-free for 60 days within 24h transfer.

2. "DMZ (Demilitarized Zone) = just a tourist spot" — Not fully utilizing the Korean War + Russian participation enthusiast experience: While Russians are #1 DMZ (Demilitarized Zone) enthusiasts, they often only purchase English/Russian 'DMZ Tour' packages → lacking a full experience. The actual full experience is the Imjingak (임진각) + DMZ Peace Park (DMZ 평화공원) + Dorasan Observatory (도라산 전망대) + The 3rd Tunnel (제3땅굴) + War Memorial of Korea (전쟁기념관, Yongsan) + Seoul National Cemetery (국립서울현충원, Dongjak) 5-pillar enthusiast course.

3. "Lack of Russian menus" — Unaware of insufficient English/Russian-friendly infrastructure: Very few establishments have Russian menus. 100% English-friendly is generally OK, but Russian is limited to some hotels and DMZ (Demilitarized Zone) packages. Utilizing English/Russian dictionaries or translation apps is essential.
