Winter Day Trips from Seoul, 4 Picks — A 12-Degree Cave, a Train to Chuncheon, Suwon's 2,000 won Haenggung, Gapyeong's Starlight Garden (After the Season Is Announced): A Day With No Hiking and Less Cold

Subtitle: Gwangmyeong Cave (광명동굴) stays around 12 degrees all year · adults 10,000 won · closed Mondays · Chuncheon (춘천) by ITX-Cheongchun (departing Yongsan 06:00~22:48 on weekdays) + the Dakgalbi Alley (닭갈비골목) + Kim Yu-jeong Literary Village (김유정문학촌) 2,000 won · Suwon Hwaseong Haenggung (수원 화성행궁) adults 2,000 won, combo ticket 4,000 won · Morning Calm Arboretum (아침고요수목원) Five-Color Starlight Garden Festival: 2025-26 season 12/5~3/15, lit after 17:00 (2026-27 not yet announced) — as of 2026-10-31

🎯 Seoul in Winter — Why "No Hiking"

In spring and fall, day trips from Seoul are about mountains and forests (#754, #739). Winter is different. Seoul's average morning low in January is -5.5℃ [Secondary — table citing Korea Meteorological Administration normals]. Walking snowy mountain paths in sneakers is risky, and standing outdoors for long makes the whole day a struggle (#1318).

So this guide picks only places where you spend time indoors, warm up on the train, or see lights briefly after dark. On Reddit's r/KoreaTravelAdvice, a question like "Day Trip from Seoul during December" was posted in October [Secondary — checked 2026-10-30]. Places to go specifically for snow are in #747, sledding hills inside Seoul in #1416, and hot springs and jjimjilbang in #749.

Hours and fees come from each facility's or local government's official notice (viewed 10/31). Many bus numbers and travel times between points are not in official notices, so we left them out. Before you set out, check a map app (Naver Map, KakaoMap) or the tourist hotline 1330.

① Gwangmyeong Cave (광명동굴) — Below Zero Outside, an Average of 12 Degrees Inside

  • What it is: A former metal mine turned into a tourist cave. Inside it is 12 degrees on average, 12 degrees or below all year, with 60~99% humidity, so you need long sleeves (Gwangmyeong Cave official). When it is below zero outside, the cave feels relatively less cold, but it is damp, so bring an outer layer
  • Hours and fees: 09:00~18:00, last admission 17:00, closed every Monday. Individual adults 10,000 won, teens 5,000 won, children 3,000 won, ages 65 and over 3,000 won (official fee table, marked "applies from 2024.1.1")
  • Getting there (official): Line 7 Cheolsan Station (철산역) Exit 2 → the stop across from 2001 Outlet → bus 17 to the last stop (Gwangmyeong Cave). If you arrived by KTX: KTX Gwangmyeong Station Exit 8 → bus 77. The official notice does not give travel times between points
  • Heads-up: No smoking, food or pets inside the cave, and strollers are left at the entrance (official)
  • Half a day is enough (recommended duration). Go in the morning and link the afternoon to an indoor plan in Seoul. For the story of wine aged in the cave, see #574

② Chuncheon (춘천) — The Train Is Your Heater

  • ITX-Cheongchun: An express train from Yongsan and Cheongnyangni to Chuncheon. From Yongsan, the first train is 06:00 and the last is 22:48 on weekdays, 06:15 and 22:05 on Saturdays and holidays; from Chuncheon, weekdays 06:08~22:14, holidays 06:06~22:13 (Chuncheon City transport notice). Timetables can be revised, so check fares and travel times together in the Korail app (KORAIL Talk)
  • Myeongdong Dakgalbi Alley (명동 닭갈비골목): Around 2 Myeongdong-gil (명동길), Chuncheon. The alley is always open, and each restaurant has its own hours (Chuncheon City tourism). Many places take orders of 2 servings or more for iron-plate dakgalbi [Secondary]. For dakgalbi in Seoul see #131, and for restaurants in Gangwon see #1384
  • Kim Yu-jeong Literary Village (김유정문학촌): A literary museum in the home village of the novelist Kim Yu-jeong. In winter 09:30~17:00, closed Mondays, 1/1, Seollal and Chuseok, admission 2,000 won, 25 Silre-gil (실레길), Chuncheon (Chuncheon City tourism). It is about 0.2km from the old Kim Yu-jeong Station
  • Soyanggang Skywalk (소양강스카이워크): A glass-floor lookout stretching out over the river. In winter 10:00~17:30 (admission closes 30 min before closing), closed every Tuesday. Pay 2,000 won to enter and you get back a Chuncheon Love gift voucher worth 2,000 won, usable in shops and markets downtown (Chuncheon City tourism). 2663 Yeongseo-ro (영서로), Chuncheon. It is windy, so 10~20 min is enough (recommended duration)
  • The order that keeps you least exposed to the cold is skywalk in the morning (short) → dakgalbi for lunch → Literary Village in the afternoon (indoors) → train home in the evening. This order works Wed~Sun (the Literary Village is closed Mondays and the skywalk Tuesdays). Check the trip between downtown and the Literary Village on a map app

③ Suwon Hwaseong Haenggung (수원 화성행궁) — A Joseon King's Temporary Palace for 2,000 won

  • Hours and fees: 09:00~18:00, open year-round. Individual adults 2,000 won, teens and soldiers 1,500 won, children 1,000 won. The 4,000 won combo ticket covers the Haenggung plus the Hwaseong Museum and the Suwon Museum (Suwon City tourism). On the last Wednesday of each month, "Culture Day", Haenggung admission is free (evenings excluded). 825 Jeongjo-ro (정조로), Paldal-gu, Suwon
  • Some notices give a winter closing time of 17:00, so if you are going late in the afternoon, confirm by phone (1330)
  • Cold-weather route: The Haenggung courtyard is outdoors, so plan under 1 hour (recommended duration) and spend the rest inside the museums with the combo ticket. The closing days of the two museums are not in the Suwon City notice, so check in advance. For the fortress wall loop (about 5.7km), walk only some sections in winter [Secondary]
  • Getting there: Take a bus from Suwon Station (Line 1 and Suwon-Bundang Line [Secondary]). Bus numbers differ between notices (not on the official page), so check a map app. For the whole picture of Suwon see #321, and for 12 Gyeonggi day trips see #512

④ Gapyeong Morning Calm Arboretum (가평 아침고요수목원) — The "Five-Color Starlight Garden Festival" After Dark

  • Five-Color Starlight Garden Festival (오색별빛정원전): An event that fills the arboretum with lights on winter nights. The 2025-26 season ran 2025-12-05~2026-03-15, 17:00~21:00 (until 23:00 on Saturdays) (VisitKorea English). The 2026-27 opening was not yet announced as of 10/31, and the arboretum only says every year "early 12 to mid 3" by month number
  • Hours and fees (official): Regular 08:30~19:00 (last admission 18:00), open year-round. Adults 11,000 won, teens 8,500 won, children (36 months to elementary school) 7,500 won. Whether fees differ during the festival, check when the opening is announced. The website lists festival-period hours as 10:00~21:00 (Sat 23:00). 432 Sumokwon-ro (수목원로), Sangmyeon, Gapyeong-gun, Gyeonggi-do, 1544-6703
  • Getting there (official directions): The starting point is Cheongpyeong, not Gapyeong Station. From Yongsan or Cheongnyangni take ITX-Cheongchun or the Gyeongchun Line to Cheongpyeong Station, or an intercity bus to Cheongpyeong Terminal → bus 30-6 to the arboretum's last stop; by taxi it is about 20 min. The Gapyeong City Tour Bus (1-day pass for adults 8,000 won, no service on Wednesdays) runs 9 times a day from Cheongpyeong Station, 08:10~17:25 (arboretum page notice). However, the City Tour Bus's last departure from the arboretum is 18:25, so it does not fit the hours when the lights are on. For the evening ride home, the last bus 30-6 leaves the arboretum at 20:00 (official); after that, take a taxi to Cheongpyeong (about 20 min). Check the last train times from Cheongpyeong to Seoul in advance
  • Cold-weather route: The realistic plan is to see only the first 1 hour (recommended duration) after the lights come on, then leave. It is outdoors, so hand warmers and gloves are a must (#1318)
  • There are reports that nearby Petite France (쁘띠프랑스) also holds a winter starlight event, but we could not confirm its 2026-27 schedule or fees. For the whole picture of Gapyeong see #1033

🗓 Which Day Should You Pick?

What you want Pick Time needed
Just half a day, the warmest option ① Gwangmyeong Cave Half a morning
Enjoying the train ride itself ② Chuncheon A full day
Cheap, one historic site ③ Suwon Hwaseong Haenggung + museums Half a day to a full day
Winter lights, an evening plan ④ Morning Calm (after the season is announced) Afternoon to night

You can also pair two, such as ① Gwangmyeong Cave in the morning + ③ Suwon in the afternoon. But the trip between them involves many transfers, so check the time on a map app first.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Gwangmyeong Cave on a Monday — It is closed. Kim Yu-jeong Literary Village is also closed on Mondays.

2. Heading to Gapyeong Station for Morning Calm — The arboretum's official directions start from Cheongpyeong Station and Cheongpyeong Terminal. The last evening bus (30-6) leaves the arboretum at 20:00.

3. Trusting the 2025-26 festival dates as this year's — 2026-27 has not been announced. Check for the early-December opening announcement.

4. Short sleeves in the cave — The cave is around 12 degrees. Carry a winter coat in with you.

5. Missing the last train from Chuncheon — The last ITX-Cheongchun to Yongsan leaves Chuncheon at 22:14 on weekdays and 22:13 on holidays. If dinner over dakgalbi runs long, watch the time.

⚙ FAQ 3

1. Is it OK on a snowy day? — ① Gwangmyeong Cave and the ③ Suwon museums are barely affected by weather. ② The skywalk and ④ the festival (if it opens after the announcement) are outdoors, so keep the visit short in snow or strong wind.

2. Do foreigners also get the Chuncheon Love gift voucher? — The notice only says the voucher is returned when you pay the skywalk admission. Spend it in downtown shops and markets.

3. If I could pick only one? — For a first-time winter traveler, ① Gwangmyeong Cave is the safest choice. If you want to ride a train, ② Chuncheon.
