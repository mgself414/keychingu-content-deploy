📅 Bongnal 2026 — Official Dates

  • Chobok (초복) 7/15 (Wed) · Jungbok (중복) 7/25 (Sat) · Malbok (말복) 8/14 (Fri) — the 2026 almanac of the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute (한국천문연구원). This is a "wolbok year," with 20 days between Jungbok (중복) and Malbok (말복). The old guide's 7/16, 7/26 and 8/15 were all one day off. It is common knowledge that Samgyetang (삼계탕) shops draw long lines on Bongnal (복날) itself, but there is no official figure.

🍗 What Is Samgyetang — the VisitKorea Definition

  • "A traditional health food of young chicken, cleaned and simmered with ginseng, jujube, sweet rice and assorted nuts" (VisitKorea Tosokchon (토속촌) page). The opened pages carry no official definition of "boyangsik" itself.
  • Three places in Seoul with hours on an official page (VisitKorea): Tosokchon (토속촌) (near Gyeongbokgung (경복궁), 10–22, last order 21:00, open year-round, cards accepted), Gobong Samgyetang (고봉삼계탕) (Myeongdong (명동), 09–22) and Baekje Samgyetang (백제삼계탕) (Myeongdong (명동), 09–22, since 1971). Prices are not posted for any of the three. Old lines such as Tosokchon (토속촌)'s "founded in the 1980s, English menu, Michelin fans" have no source.

🏨 Hotels — La Yeon's Stars Are the Only Thing Confirmed

  • The Shilla Seoul (신라호텔) La Yeon (라연): Michelin Seoul 2026 two stars (Michelin official), La Liste 2026 Top 1,000 (hotel official). Hours and course prices are not on the official page → confirm when booking. No menu called a "restorative course" can be confirmed.
  • The Westin Josun Seoul (웨스틴 조선 서울): the official dining list (Josun Deli, Aria, Hong Yuan, Sushi Cho, The Ninth Gate, Lounge & Bar, Rubrica) has no Korean restaurant — the old "Korean set meal" entry is deleted.
  • Lotte Hotel Seoul Mugunghwa (롯데호텔 무궁화), Four Seasons and Banyan Tree: unverified because the page did not render or returned 403. The service-charge and VAT sentence also has no official confirmation.

🌿 Hanbang and Yaksun — Official Coverage Stops at Medical-Tourism Guidance

  • VisitKorea's Hanbang (한방) page: treatment that looks at body and mind together, with acupuncture, Chuna (추나), herbal medicine and exercise therapy prescribed to suit constitution and symptoms. The official page lists 3 clinics, 3 experience centers and 1330. Yaksun (약선) restaurants and Yaksun (약선) course prices are on no official page, so we do not list them.

🚜 Rural and Regional — Welchon and Busan

  • Welchon (웰촌) (welchon.com, official rural-tourism portal): farm stays, themed experience villages, seasonal experiences (apple picking, tea making and so on), a package shop and VR village tours. The portal has no eel or black-goat "health-food farm."
  • Busan Gomjangeo (곰장어): Visit Busan points to Jagalchi (자갈치), Oncheonjang (온천장), Haeundae Market (해운대시장) and Gijang (기장) as charcoal-grilled conger eel areas (no hours or prices). The old "Seomyeon eel street" is not on the page.
  • Gochang (고창) Pungcheon eel, Suwon (수원) ox-head soup, Namwon (남원) loach soup and Gangwon black goat: the official pages could not be opened, so we do not list them.

🥗 Dietary Needs

  • Seoul has one Korea Muslim Federation (KMF)-certified Korean restaurant (Eid, VisitSeoul) — there is no official halal marking for Samgyetang (삼계탕) shops. For vegetarian needs, check before ordering (VisitKorea). 1330.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Using the old Bongnal dates — 2026 is 7/15, 7/25 and 8/14.

2. Budgeting from the old price ranges — officially not posted.

3. Expecting a Korean set meal at The Westin Josun Seoul (웨스틴 조선 서울) — no Korean restaurant (official).

4. Booking a Yaksun (약선) course — no official page.

5. "Seomyeon eel" — Visit Busan lists Jagalchi (자갈치), Oncheonjang (온천장), Haeundae Market (해운대시장) and Gijang (기장).

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. When is Bongnal? — 7/15, 7/25 and 8/14 (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute (한국천문연구원)).

2. Where can I eat Samgyetang? — Tosokchon (토속촌) 10–22 · Gobong Samgyetang (고봉삼계탕) and Baekje Samgyetang (백제삼계탕) 09–22 (Myeongdong (명동)). Prices not posted.

3. Hotel health food? — Only La Yeon's two stars are official. Menu and prices not posted.

4. Hanbang experiences? — The clinic and experience-center list on VisitKorea's Hanbang (한방) page + 1330.

5. On a farm? — The Welchon (웰촌) portal. There is no dedicated health-food farm.

6. Halal or vegetarian? — One KMF-certified Korean restaurant (Eid). Check before ordering.
