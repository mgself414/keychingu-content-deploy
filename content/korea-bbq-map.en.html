⚠ 3 Common BBQ Mistakes

1. "K-BBQ = samgyeopsal only" — missing the 5 axes: Samgyeopsal only. In reality, it's the samgyeopsal · galbi · hanwoo · gopchang · dwaeji gukbap pairing 5 axes, plus the full experience of direct-from-source hanwoo at Majang Meat Market and the chains (Saemaeul Sikdang, Parkdaegamne).

2. "Hanwoo = always expensive" — missing the value cluster: Assuming it's pricey. In reality, there's a value-for-money full experience — Majang Meat Market (direct-from-source hanwoo, ₩50,000–80,000 (~$38–60) per person) and chain hanwoo (₩30,000–50,000 (~$23–38) per person) — kept separate from luxury (#344 · Apgujeong, ₩100,000–300,000 (~$75–225) per person).

3. "BBQ = Korean only" — missing the English-friendly cluster: Korean only. In reality, Saemaeul Sikdang · Parkdaegamne · Iseobang (English menus 99%) · Myeongdong · Itaewon English-menu BBQ · some #453 KMF halal BBQ are foreigner-friendly.

🍯 5 Tips for Foreign Visitors

1. Samgyeopsal = one geun (180g portions) per person — ₩5,000–8,000 (~$4–6) · Korea's #1 everyday BBQ · #1 among foreign fans

2. Majang Meat Market = direct-from-source hanwoo — value hanwoo at ₩50,000–80,000 (~$38–60) per person · half the price of chain hanwoo

3. English menus = Saemaeul Sikdang · Parkdaegamne · Iseobang — Korea's #1 English-friendly BBQ chains

4. 24h gopchang = Jongno 3-ga — #338 cluster · #450 nighttime · #472 sool pairing
