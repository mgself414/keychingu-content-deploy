Korean BBQ Guide — Samgyeopsal, Pork Galbi, Beef Galbi and Hanwoo: 14 Grill Restaurants and the Majang Meat Market (Geumdwaeji Sikdang, Samwon Garden, Gabojeong Galbi, Hoengseong Hanwoo Maeul) (October 2026 Official Data)

Subtitle: Geumdwaeji Sikdang (금돼지식당) pork belly on a briquette-heated cast-iron griddle, 11:30~23:00 (VisitKorea, Michelin page) · Samwon Garden (삼원가든) traditional galbi (VisitKorea) · Bon Suwon Galbi (본수원갈비) in Suwon, opened 1973 (VisitKorea) · Majang Meat Market (마장 축산물시장) 03:00~23:00, hours vary by stall (VisitKorea) · Eid (이드), a KMF-certified Korean restaurant in Seoul (VisitSeoul) — as of 2026-10-21

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Before anything else you hear the fat spitting as thick slices of pork belly land on a red-hot cast-iron griddle. Korean grill restaurants split by cut, by restaurant, and by how the meat is cooked (briquettes, charcoal, or whether staff grill it for you). This guide sorts them into five branches: samgyeopsal (삼겹살, pork belly) and moksal (목살, pork neck), pork galbi (돼지갈비, marinated pork ribs), Seoul beef galbi (소갈비), regional galbi (Suwon and Gwangju Songjeong), and hanwoo (한우, Korean native beef). Names, addresses and hours come only from the official VisitKorea, Michelin and VisitSeoul pages, and none of the restaurants posted a price.

This is for anyone who wants to grill a proper Korean meal at least once. How to handle the grill, the scissors and ordering once you are seated is covered in #1290 Korean BBQ Table System, so it is not repeated here.

From the old guide we removed "samgyeopsal 1 geun (근) per person, 180g, ₩5,000~8,000 (~$4~$6)", "Majang hanwoo ₩50,000~80,000 (~$38~$60) per person", "99% English menus" and the chain rankings, because none had a source. Gopchang (곱창, grilled intestines) and dwaeji-gukbap (돼지국밥, pork soup with rice) differ in character from grill restaurants, so they were dropped from this edition (see Related Content below).

🐖 Samgyeopsal and Moksal — Briquettes and Black Pork

  • Geumdwaeji Sikdang (금돼지식당) — 149 Dasan-ro (다산로), Jung-gu, Seoul (Sindang-dong, 신당동). The signature is bon-samgyeopsal (본삼겹살, plain pork belly); others include nunkkot-moksal (눈꽃목살, snowflake pork neck), deung-moksal (등목살, back neck) and kkopdegi (껍데기, pork skin). The pork is a Yorkshire-Berkshire-Duroc crossbreed called "YBD", grilled on a cast-iron griddle heated over briquettes (yeontan, 연탄). 11:30~23:00, open year-round, no parking, 0507-1307-8750 (VisitKorea). The Michelin page lists it as barbecue, price ₩, and says the restaurant manages reservations itself (Michelin page); a 2021 Michelin article lists it as Bib Gourmand (Michelin article 2021-09-13). Price not posted
  • Donsadon (돈사돈) — 19 Upyeong-ro (우평로), Jeju-si, Jeju. Jeju black pork five-layer belly (oggyeopsal, 오겹살) grilled over briquettes, and staff grill it for you. Dip it in meljeot (멜젓, salted anchovy-type fish sauce). 12:00~22:00, closed every Tuesday, 064-746-8989 (VisitKorea). Price not posted
  • Sukseongdo Jeju Main Branch (숙성도 제주본점) — 30 Jewon-gil (제원길), Jeju-si, Jeju, 2nd and 3rd floors. Jeju black pork aged by alternating water-aging and dry-aging; instead of belly it serves cuts such as loin and front leg. 11:30~22:00 (last order 21:10), open year-round (VisitKorea). Price not posted
  • Meat alleys: Jeju Black Pork Street (제주 흑돼지거리, Ildo 1-dong (일도일동), Jeju-si, near Tapdong Plaza at Jeju Port, hours vary by shop) · Cheongju Seomun Market Samgyeopsal Street (청주 서문시장 삼겹살거리, created 2012, Cheongju style: soaked in soy sauce before grilling or sprinkled with coarse salt) — both VisitKorea

🍖 Pork Galbi — A Day in the Marinade

  • Seongbuk-dong Dwaeji Galbi (성북동돼지갈비) — 115 Seongbuk-ro (성북로), Seongbuk-gu, Seoul. Opened 1975. Meat marinated the day before is grilled and served as a set meal with rice (baekban, 백반): pork galbi set, pork bulgogi set, tteokgalbi set. 09:00~20:30, closed every Tuesday, 02-764-2420. Lettuce wraps and side dishes (banchan, 반찬) are at a self-service bar (VisitKorea). Price not posted
  • Majang Galbi (마장갈비) — 292-1 Majang-ro (마장로), Seongdong-gu, Seoul. In front of Majang Station since 1982. The signature is king pork galbi marinated 3 days in a 12-ingredient sauce. Weekdays 11:00~22:00 (break 15:30~16:30), Sunday 11:00~21:00, closed every Monday, 0507-1370-8601 (VisitKorea). Price not posted
  • Bongpiyang Bangi Branch (봉피양 방이점) — 1-4 Yangjae-daero 71-gil (양재대로71길), Songpa-gu, Seoul. Pyeongyang naengmyeon (평양냉면, cold buckwheat noodles) and pork neck bon-galbi (돼지목심본갈비). 11:30~22:00 (last order 21:00), open year-round, 02-415-5527. The VisitKorea overview says "listed in the Michelin Guide Seoul 2023" (VisitKorea). Price not posted
  • Meat alley: Mapo Food Culture Street (마포음식문화거리, Mapo Station Exit 1, 37 restaurants serving juumeolleok [주물럭, marinated grilled pork], kkopdegi, pork galbi and more, hours vary by shop, VisitKorea)

🐂 Seoul Beef Galbi — Big Ribs over Charcoal

  • Samwon Garden (삼원가든) — 835 Eonju-ro (언주로), Gangnam-gu, Seoul. Traditional galbi, bulgogi, grilled hanwoo. Weekdays 11:30~21:30 (break 14:30~17:30, last order 21:00), weekends 11:00~21:00 (last order 20:30), open year-round, 02-548-3030. More than 40 private rooms (VisitKorea). Price not posted
  • Byeokje Galbi Bangi Branch (벽제갈비 방이점) — 1-4 Yangjae-daero 71-gil (양재대로71길), Songpa-gu, Seoul (same address as Bongpiyang Bangi). Seolhwa raw galbi (설화 생갈비), flower sirloin (꽃등심), tenderloin and marinated galbi. 11:30~22:00 (last order 21:00), open year-round, 02-415-5522, non-smoking throughout (VisitKorea). Official site bjgalbi.com. Price not posted
  • Mongtan (몽탄) — 50 Baekbeom-ro 99-gil (백범로99길), Yongsan-gu, Seoul (Samgakji, 삼각지). Known for udae-galbi (우대갈비, thick-cut beef rib), plus jippul hangjeongsal (짚불항정살, straw-fire pork jowl) and yukhoe (육회, beef tartare). 12:00~22:00 (last order 21:00), open year-round, 02-794-8592, cards accepted. VisitKorea says "waiting in advance is a must" (VisitKorea). Price not posted

🔥 Regional Galbi — Suwon King Galbi and Gwangju Songjeong Tteokgalbi

  • Gabojeong Galbi (가보정갈비) — 282 Jangdari-ro (장다리로), Paldal-gu, Suwon-si, Gyeonggi. Opened 1992. Marinated hanwoo galbi grilled over charcoal. Weekdays 11:30~21:30, Saturday 11:00~22:00, Sunday 11:00~21:30, open year-round, 1600-3883 (VisitKorea). Price not posted
  • Bon Suwon Galbi (본수원갈비) — 41 Jungbu-daero 223beon-gil (중부대로223번길), Paldal-gu, Suwon-si, Gyeonggi. Opening date 1973-10-12. Choose between raw galbi and marinated galbi. Monday and Sunday 11:30~21:00, Tuesday to Saturday 11:30~21:30, closed during Seollal (설, Lunar New Year) and Chuseok (추석, Korean Thanksgiving) holidays, 031-211-8434 (VisitKorea). Price not posted
  • Songjeong Tteokgalbi Branch No. 1 (송정떡갈비 1호점) — 1 Gwangsan-ro 29beon-gil (광산로29번길), Gwangsan-gu, Jeonnam-Gwangju Integrated Special City (전남광주통합특별시, formerly Gwangju). The original shop of the Songjeong tteokgalbi alley, started in 1976. Beef rib meat and pork belly are mixed 5 to 5. 09:30~21:30 (last order 21:00), closed every Sunday and the first Monday of each month, 062-944-1439, cards accepted (VisitKorea). Price not posted

🥩 Hanwoo — Buy at the Market, Grill It Yourself

  • Majang Meat Market (마장 축산물시장) — 53 Majang-ro 33-gil (마장로33길), Seongdong-gu, Seoul. 03:00~23:00, open year-round (varies by stall), 02-2281-4446, official site mjmm.co.kr. Origin and price labeling are mandatory, and the VisitKorea overview says "up to 30% cheaper than large supermarkets" (VisitKorea). Market guide: #592 Majang Meat Market
  • Majang-dong Hanwoochon (마장동 한우촌) — 68 Majang-ro 35-gil (마장로35길), Seongdong-gu, Seoul, in the Majang-dong eating alley near Yongdu Station Exit 4. Hanwoo galbi, and wang-galbitang (왕갈비탕, big-rib soup) as a meal. 11:00~22:00, open year-round, 02-2281-9292 (VisitKorea). Price not posted
  • Hoengseong Hanwoo Maeul (횡성한우마을) — 348 Sunam-ro (수남로), Ucheon-myeon, Hoengseong-gun, Gangwon. A jeongyuk sikdang (정육식당, butcher-shop restaurant) where you buy meat on the 1st floor and grill it on the 2nd floor, with no table-setting fee. 10:00~21:00, open year-round, 033-343-6005 (VisitKorea). Price not posted
  • To decide between a butcher-shop restaurant, a BBQ specialist and a market, see #806 Jeongyuk Sikdang vs BBQ Specialist vs Market; for cut names, see #804 BBQ Cut and Grade Matrix

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Price range: None of the 14 restaurants in this guide has a won price on its official page. Michelin gave Geumdwaeji Sikdang (금돼지식당) only a ₩ symbol. Confirm by phone
  • Vegetarian: Grill restaurants have few vegetarian options, and even the dipping sauces are animal-based. Jeju black pork is dipped in meljeot (멜젓, salted fish sauce) (VisitKorea, Donsadon [돈사돈]), and the Pyeongyang naengmyeon broth at Bongpiyang (봉피양) is made from hanwoo (VisitKorea). Ask about the broth before ordering naengmyeon or doenjang-jjigae (된장찌개, soybean paste stew) too
  • Halal: Not applicable to the pork branches. A KMF (Korea Muslim Federation, 한국이슬람교중앙회) certified Korean restaurant in Seoul is Eid (이드) — 15 Usadan-ro 10-gil (우사단로10길), Yongsan-gu, open daily 12:00~21:00, price range about ₩10,000 (~$7.50) (VisitSeoul). None of the beef galbi or hanwoo restaurants in this guide carries a halal label
  • Allergies: Marinade ingredients are not fully disclosed (the Songjeong tteokgalbi sauce has "ingredients the owner does not reveal", VisitKorea). If you have allergies, choose raw galbi or raw meat
  • For a card to show staff, see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Practical Tips — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Ordering, Getting There

  • Solo: Some grill restaurants take orders for two or more people. Places with one-person menus, like the set meals at Seongbuk-dong Dwaeji Galbi (성북동돼지갈비), are easier. For solo-friendly spots, see #348 Seoul Solo Diner Map
  • Reservations: Geumdwaeji Sikdang (금돼지식당) manages reservations itself (Michelin page). Mongtan (몽탄) assumes you will wait (VisitKorea). For big restaurants like Samwon Garden (삼원가든) and Byeokje Galbi (벽제갈비), a phone call is the surest way
  • Payment: Mongtan (몽탄) and Songjeong Tteokgalbi Branch No. 1 (송정떡갈비 1호점) accept cards (VisitKorea). The others do not say on their pages — #527 Korean Payment Systems
  • Ordering and grilling: who grills, why the scissors are there, and when to call for a griddle change are in #1290 Korean BBQ Table System. If you cannot communicate, see #1339 Ordering Etiquette
  • Getting there: for Suwon, Hoengseong and Gwangju, see #1297 Express and Intercity Bus Manual; for transit cards, #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "At a grill restaurant I always grill it myself" — at Donsadon (돈사돈) staff grill for you (VisitKorea). It differs by restaurant

2. "Majang Galbi on a Monday" — Majang Galbi (마장갈비) is closed every Monday, and Seongbuk-dong Dwaeji Galbi (성북동돼지갈비) and Donsadon (돈사돈) every Tuesday (VisitKorea)

3. "Tteokgalbi is beef" — some Songjeong tteokgalbi mixes beef and pork (the original Songjeong Tteokgalbi Branch No. 1, VisitKorea). If you must avoid pork, ask beforehand

4. "Butcher-shop restaurants charge a separate table-setting fee" — Hoengseong Hanwoo Maeul (횡성한우마을) charges nothing for table setting (VisitKorea). It differs by restaurant, so ask before you pay
