Why Line 2 — The Loop Line That Connects the Most Tourist Hotspots

The subway line foreigners ride most in Seoul is Line 2 (the loop line). Hongdae (홍대)·Gangnam (강남)·Jamsil (잠실)·Seongsu (성수)·Euljiro (을지로)·Konkuk Univ. (건대) — it links the tourist hotspots in a single loop. Ride just this one green loop line and most of Seoul's youth culture and food is within reach.

This guide organizes not subway usage (#237) but "what to eat and see when you get off at this station." It reorganizes the place data KeyChinGu has accumulated (4,642 places) by station, pairing verified (foreigner-suitable·popularity-signal) restaurants·cafes with representative attractions. It's a curation for filling a day by getting off at each Line 2 station.

This edition covers the 8 key tourist stations of Line 2. (The first edition of the subway line series — next editions expand to Line 3, Gyeongui-Jungang, etc.)

Line 2 Station-by-Station Curation — What to Do When You Get Off

🟢 #1. Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역) — Youth·Ramen·Strolling

  • 🚪 Exits: Exit 3 (Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길)·Yeonnam (연남)) · Exit 9 (Hongdae Walking Street (홍대 걷고싶은거리))
  • 🍜 Eat: Hongdae Ramen Street (a cluster of Japanese-style ramen shops such as Menya Su (멘야수)·Ramen Bar Shikou (라멘바 시코우)) · Yeonnam-dong (연남동) cafes
  • 👀 Play: Stroll Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길, aka Yeontral Park) · busking on Hongdae Walking Street (홍대 걷고싶은거리) · knick-knack shops
  • 🔁 Transfer: Airport Railroad·Gyeongui-Jungang Line (connects your arrival route)
  • cross-ref: #138 Hongdae deep dive · #1236 Summer night market (Yeonnam Gyeongui Line Forest Park)

🟢 #2. Hapjeong Station (합정역) — Yeonnam·Mangridan-gil·Han River

  • 🚪 Exits: Toward Hongdae (홍대)·Sangsu (상수)·Mangwon (망원)
  • 🍜 Eat: Yeonnam-dong (연남동) Western food·brunch (Yeonnam-dong Y-Top (연남동와이탑)·Spring Bistro (스프링비스트로), etc.) · Mangridan-gil (망리단길) cafes
  • 👀 Play: Mecenatpolis (메세나폴리스)·Kyobo Book Centre (교보문고) · toward the Han River (Mangwon Han River Park (망원한강공원)) · Delight Square (딜라이트스퀘어)
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 6
  • cross-ref: #1235 Quiet Seoul (Yeonnam Gyeongui Line Forest Park)

🟢 #3. Gangnam Station (강남역) — Biggest Commercial District·Restaurants·Shopping

  • 🚪 Exits: Both directions of Gangnam-daero (강남대로) · toward Sinnonhyeon (신논현)
  • 🍜 Eat: Vite Dosan (비테도산) (brunch cafe) · Tteurak (뜨락) (beef BBQ) · Notreu (노뜨르) (French) · a cluster of Gangnam Western restaurants
  • 👀 Play: Shopping at the Gangnam Station underground mall · Garosu-gil (가로수길) (toward Sinsa (신사)) · COEX (코엑스) (toward Samseong (삼성))
  • 🔁 Transfer: Sinbundang Line (express to Pangyo (판교)·Jeongja (정자))
  • Foreigner tip: Seoul's largest foot traffic. Crowded in the evening — earlier hours recommended.

🟢 #4. Samseong Station (삼성역) — COEX One-Stop

  • 🚪 Exits: Directly connected to COEX Mall (코엑스몰)
  • 🍜 Eat: COEX Mall (코엑스몰) dining · Samseong-dong (삼성동) Western food (Huje (후제)·Cantique (칸티크), etc.)
  • 👀 Play: COEX Aquarium (코엑스 아쿠아리움)·Starfield Library (별마당도서관)·Starfield (스타필드) (indoor one-stop) · Bongeunsa Temple (봉은사) (across the street)
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 9 (near Bongeunsa Station (봉은사역))·GTX (planned)
  • cross-ref: #1240 Rainy-day indoor family (COEX)

🟢 #5. Seongsu Station (성수역) — Seoul's Brooklyn

  • 🚪 Exits: Yeonmujang-gil (연무장길)·Seongsu-dong (성수동) Cafe Street
  • 🍜 Eat: Uchon Charcoal Galbi (우촌숯불갈비) (Korean) · I'm Donut? Seongsu (아임도넛 성수) (dessert) · Seongsu French·Italian
  • 👀 Play: Seoul Forest (서울숲) (linked to Line 4 Ttukseom (뚝섬)) · Yeonmujang-gil (연무장길) pop-ups·select shops · Seongsu-dong (성수동) Cafe Street
  • 🔁 Transfer: Bundang Line (near Seoul Forest Station (서울숲역))
  • cross-ref: #001 Seongsu pop-up · #1236 Seongsu terrace · #586 Ttukseom Han River

🟢 #6. Konkuk Univ. Station (건대입구역) — MZ Commercial District·Han River

  • 🚪 Exits: Common Ground (커먼그라운드)·Konkuk Univ. food alley (건대 먹자골목)
  • 🍜 Eat: International food at the Konkuk Univ. food alley (건대 먹자골목) · Achasan Tong Galbitang (아차산통갈비탕), etc. · Eunbis Bread (은비스브레드) (bakery)
  • 👀 Play: Common Ground (커먼그라운드) (container shopping mall) · Ttukseom Han River Park (뚝섬한강공원) (toward Konkuk Univ.) · Konkuk Univ. Rodeo (건대 로데오)
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 7
  • cross-ref: #586 Han River at night (Ttukseom)

🟢 #7. Jamsil Station (잠실역) — Theme Park·Tower·Lake

  • 🚪 Exits: Directly connected to Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰)·Lotte World (롯데월드)
  • 🍜 Eat: Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) dining · Jamsil Western food (Restaurant Jemi (레스토랑제미), etc.)·bakery (Bongttang (봉땅))
  • 👀 Play: Lotte World Adventure (롯데월드 어드벤처)·Lotte World Aquarium (롯데월드 아쿠아리움) · Lotte World Tower Seoul Sky (롯데월드타워 서울스카이) (observatory) · Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) (spring cherry blossoms)
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 8
  • cross-ref: #1238 Night-view date (Seoul Sky) · #1240 Indoor family (Lotte World)

🟢 #8. Euljiro 1-ga Station (을지로입구역) — Old-School Eateries·Hipjiro·Myeongdong

  • 🚪 Exits: Toward Myeongdong (명동) · Euljiro (을지로) old-school eatery alleys
  • 🍜 Eat: Gaehwa (개화)·Andongjang (안동장) (old-school Chinese) · Ppama (빠마) (cafe) · Euljiro Nogari Alley (을지로 노가리골목)
  • 👀 Play: Hipjiro (힙지로) (hidden cafes·bars) · Myeongdong (명동) (shopping·Myeongdong Cathedral (명동성당)) · exploring the Euljiro printing alley (을지로 인쇄골목)
  • 🔁 Transfer: Within walking distance — Myeongdong (Line 4)·City Hall (시청) (Line 1)
  • cross-ref: #1236 Summer night market (Euljiro) · #1239 600 years of history (Euljiro·Myeongdong)

Five Line Tips — Get 200% out of Line 2

Tip 1. Check the loop's direction

Line 2 is a loop, so it has inner-loop and outer-loop directions. If your destination is the opposite way, you could circle all the way around — so check the direction with Naver/Kakao Map.

Tip 2. Save your exit number in advance

Even at the same station, different exits lead to completely different places (Hongik Univ. Exit 3 = Yeonnam / Exit 9 = Hongdae street). Check your destination's exit in advance with a map app.

Tip 3. Expand via transfers

Line 2 meets almost every other line (Hongik Univ. = Airport Railroad·Gyeongui-Jungang / Gangnam = Sinbundang / Seongsu = Bundang). It connects your arrival route and even the outskirts.

Tip 4. Avoid peak hours

Gangnam·Hongdae·Jamsil are very crowded in the evenings and on weekends. Restaurants are more relaxed early, and attractions on weekday mornings.

Tip 5. One transit card

A single T-money card covers Line 2, transfers, and buses. Foreigners can buy and top up at convenience stores.
