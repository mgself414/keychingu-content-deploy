Ferry from Busan to Japan, Cruise into Busan — The No-Panic Manual: 4 Routes for 2026, Booking & Check-In Deadlines, Yeongdo Cruise Terminal Connections

Subtitle: "Is there still a boat from Busan to Fukuoka?" — the answer as of September 2026: the high-speed boat is gone, but the overnight car ferries still run. Route tables, how to reach the terminal, peak-season booking, cancellation traps — and the fact that cruise ships land at two different terminals in Busan

🎯 Why This Manual

On the TripAdvisor Busan forum, questions like "I'm taking the Busan→Fukuoka ferry in October — do I need to book ahead?", "Is the Camellia rough for seasickness?", "Does the boat run on Lunar New Year?", and "How do I get into downtown Busan from the cruise terminal?" have kept repeating from 2024 through 2026, and Japanese-side communities are still asking about the "Hakata Port → Busan → KTX to Seoul" route as recently as this year. The problem is that the web is still full of information about the high-speed boat, Queen Beetle (퀸비틀), which was withdrawn at the end of 2024. This manual covers only the boats actually running, as of September 2026.

★ As of September 2026 — 4 Scheduled Routes from Busan to Japan

Busan ↔ Fukuoka (Hakata) Camellia Line (카멜리아라인) — New Camellia (뉴카멜리아) 1 round trip daily. Departs Busan 22:30 → arrives Hakata 07:30 next day (overnight, about 9 hours) / departs Hakata 12:30 → arrives Busan 18:30 (daytime, about 6 hours) From shared cabins (2nd class) up to 4-person rooms and 2-person first class. Departs Busan 21:00 → arrives Shimonoseki 07:45 next day / departs Shimonoseki 19:45 → arrives Busan 08:00 next day (about 11-12 hours, overnight) The oldest Korea-Japan ferry route. An overnight sailing means you save a night's hotel cost

Departs around 15:00 → arrives around 10:00 the next day (about 18-19 hours) A new ship that entered service in April 2025.   • Don't buy a ticket even if a page selling Queen Beetle tickets is still live — some booking sites still have the old product page up. If a boat isn't on the operator's official site, it isn't running.

🏢 Terminal — Busan Port International Passenger Terminal

  • The terminal is the Busan Port International Passenger Terminal (부산항 국제여객터미널), at 206 Chungjang-daero, Dong-gu, Busan. All four routes above depart from here (North Port, behind Busan Station).
  • From Exit 7 (back exit) of Busan Station (부산역), cross Chungjang-daero — about a 15-minute walk. If you have a large suitcase, take the terminal shuttle bus that loops from the back of Busan Station and near Jungang Station (중앙역) (every 20-30 minutes, ₩1,130) or a taxi (base-fare distance).
  • Line 1 stations Busan Station, Jungang Station, and Choryang Station (초량역) are all within walking distance, but there's no subway station right at the terminal door.
  • There's a duty-free shop inside the terminal. The currency exchange counter, baggage storage, and lounge are all small, so it's safer to finish exchanging money in the city first (#435).

🎫 Booking · Documents · Check-in Deadline

1. Booking: Direct through the operator (Camellia Line, Kampu Ferry, Panstar — Japanese/English pages) or a Korean/international OTA (Trip.com, Klook, KKday, etc.). The operator's own site is the most accurate on remaining seats; OTAs are easier to pay through. During year-end/New Year, Golden Week, and Obon, 4-person rooms and first class sell out first — book a month ahead for those periods.

2. Documents: Passport, plus Visit Japan Web's 2D code for entering Japan (it's also accepted for ferry and cruise arrivals). Coming back into Korea follows the same entry procedure as by air (K-ETA/e-Arrival, see #1396) — treat it exactly like a flight, not differently just because it's a boat.

3. Check-in deadline (the thing people miss most often): for the New Camellia's 22:30 departure, arrive at the terminal by 18:00, with check-in and boarding closing 19:00-19:30 — the door closes 3 hours before departure. Panstar check-in opens 2 hours before departure and closes 1 hour 30 minutes before.

4. After arriving in Hakata: from Hakata Port International Terminal to Hakata Station, take Nishitetsu (니시테츠) bus No. 11, 19, 50, or 80, about 20-25 minutes. There's no walking transfer involved.

⚠ 4 Traps to Avoid

1. Winter crossings of the Korea Strait get cancelled — once waves top 3m and wind hits 14m/s, cancellation risk rises. The overnight car ferries handle rough seas better than a high-speed boat would, but they stop for typhoon-strength weather. If cancelled, the fallback is a Busan-Fukuoka flight — build in more than a day of slack before your return flight.

2. Seasickness — under 1.5m of swell you'll barely notice it; above that, a lower deck or a cabin near the middle of the ship beats a spot on the shared-room floor. Take motion sickness medication 30 minutes before boarding.

3. 2nd class is a tatami shared room — book a 4-person room or first class if you want your own space. Most "it's cheap" reviews are talking about 2nd class.

4. Old information — any review or blog post mentioning Queen Beetle (withdrawn) or Panstar Dream (retired) has times and fares that no longer apply. Only trust posts from 2025 onward.

🚢 Arriving in Busan by Cruise — Two Terminals

  • The rule: small and mid-size cruise ships that can clear the Busan Harbor Bridge (부산항대교) dock inside the bridge, at the North Port International Passenger Terminal (북항 국제여객터미널) (same address as above, walking distance from Busan Station); large and mega ships that can't clear it dock outside the bridge, at the Yeongdo International Cruise Terminal (영도 국제크루즈터미널) (17 Haeyang-ro 301beon-gil, Yeongdo-gu, Dongsam-dong). Check the terminal name in your cruise line's notice first.
  • Yeongdo Terminal to downtown: bus No. 101 or 88 from in front of Busan Station → get off at Dongsam Intersection (동삼교차로) → about a 15-minute walk (same route works in reverse). If you're short on time, take a taxi (20-30 minutes to Busan Station or Nampo-dong). A dedicated shuttle sometimes runs on days a ship is in port — check with the terminal information desk on the day.
  • Scale in 2026: per the Busan Port Authority (부산항만공사, BPA), the first half of 2026 alone saw 219 port calls and 320,000 passengers — taxi lines get long at disembarkation peaks. Calling a Kakao T (#1273) is faster.
  • Half-day combo: Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을) (#1024) → Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장) and Gukje Market (#1022) → Haeundae (해운대) (#1023) if you have time left. There's no official "standard course" — this is just the most common combination. Carry cash only for small purchases at market stalls; use a card for everything else (#435).

⚙ 3 FAQs

1. Is there really no same-day boat to Fukuoka anymore? — No (as of September 2026). Either sleep through it on the overnight sailing, or run the daytime option (departs Hakata 12:30 → arrives Busan 18:30) in the other direction.

2. Is the K-ETA/entry process different if you arrive by boat? — No official source confirms an exception for sea arrivals. Prepare exactly as you would for a flight, and you're safe (#1396).

3. If my cruise stop is only 8 hours, can I make it to Seoul? — No (a KTX round trip alone takes 5 hours). Stay within Busan, and only consider Gyeongju (#1115) if you're in port for 12 hours or more.
