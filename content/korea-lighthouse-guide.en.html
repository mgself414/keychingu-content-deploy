Korea Lighthouse Travel Guide — Iho Tewoo Horse Lighthouses, Odongdo, Homigot, Ulgi Lighthouse, Sokcho, Jumunjin, Mukho, Up to Oido Red Lighthouse: 20 Lighthouses and the National Lighthouse Museum

Subtitle: 21 places chosen from the "Lighthouse Keeper's Secret Map" (lighthouses) theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" public vote · Every place with a registered admission fee is free · The places closed on Mondays are Odongdo Lighthouse, Ulgi Lighthouse, Yeongdo Lighthouse, Hwaamchu Lighthouse, Nueseom Lighthouse Observatory and the National Lighthouse Museum · Oryukdo Lighthouse has not been open to the public since 2018, and Gadeokdo Lighthouse is registered as not accepting tour applications — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide covers 20 lighthouses and the National Lighthouse Museum, out of the 76 chosen in the "Lighthouse Keeper's Secret Map" (등대지기의 비밀 지도) (lighthouses) theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" (명소발굴 100×100) (candidates recommended by travel experts, voted on by the public), whose operating information was confirmed in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data. The ranks and vote counts in the table are the results of that vote.

Every place with a registered admission fee is free (Odongdo Lighthouse, Oido Red Lighthouse and Marado Lighthouse have no registered admission fee value). However, opening hours and closed days differ from lighthouse to lighthouse. Six places are closed on Mondays. Iho Tewoo Horse Lighthouses (이호테우 말 등대), Homigot Lighthouse (호미곶 등대), Seuldo Lighthouse (슬도등대) and Jebudo Lighthouse (제부도등대) are registered as always open, and Songdaemal Lighthouse (송대말 등대) as always open for the lighthouse only (exhibition hall 09:00~18:00).

Some places are registered as closed to the public or to tours. Oryukdo Lighthouse (오륙도 등대) became unmanned in 2018 and is not open to the public (the opening hours field also says "always open"), and Gadeokdo Lighthouse (가덕도 등대) has restricted access and is noted as not accepting tour applications for the time being because of military base security. The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI).

📍 Gyeonggi — Oido Red Lighthouse, Jebudo Lighthouse and Nueseom Lighthouse Observatory

Oido Red Lighthouse (오이도 빨강등대) (Oido-ro 170 (오이도로 170), Siheung-si (시흥시), ☎ 031-310-2901~4) is a facility built in 2005 under a fishing village experience tourism village project. The first-floor exhibition hall shows information on Siheung's culture, tourism and history, and from the observatory you can see Sihwa Industrial Complex (시화공단), Baegot New Town (배곧 신도시) and Incheon Songdo New City (송도 신도시). The "Siheung Dream Shop" (시흥꿈상회), connected to the first floor, sells souvenirs.

  • Opening hours: 10:30~17:30. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Jebudo Lighthouse (제부도등대) (Haean-gil 421-17 (해안길 421-17), Seosin-myeon (서신면), Hwaseong-si (화성시), ☎ Jebudo Management Office (제부도 관리사무소) 031-355-3924) is a red lighthouse on the coast of Jebudo. Next to it are a ferry pier and a viewing deck reaching out over the sea, and a walking path starting at the lighthouse follows the coast to Jebudo Beach (제부도 해수욕장). Jebudo is an island whose sea path opens twice a day.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Nueseom Lighthouse Observatory (누에섬 등대전망대) (Seongam-dong (선감동), Danwon-gu (단원구), Ansan-si (안산시), ☎ Ansan Fishing Village Folk Museum (안산어촌민속박물관) 031-440-8310) stands on Nueseom, an uninhabited island near Tando Port (탄도항). At low tide the tidal flat is exposed for 4 hours twice a day, and you can walk in. The first and second floors are exhibition space, and the third floor holds the observatory and the lighthouse.

  • Opening hours: Mar~Oct 10:00~18:00, Nov~Feb 10:00~17:00. It is registered that operating hours change with the high and low tide times, so check the visiting timetable before you go.
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok holidays. Fee: Free.

📍 Gangwon — Sokcho Lighthouse Observatory, Jumunjin Lighthouse, Mukho Lighthouse and Daejin Lighthouse

Sokcho Lighthouse Observatory (속초등대전망대) (Yeonggeumjeong-ro 5-gil 8-28 (영금정로5길 8-28), Sokcho-si (속초시), ☎ Sokcho Navigation Aids Management Office (속초항로표지관리소) 033-633-3406) is a lighthouse that first shone its light in 1957 and is now also open as an observatory. A wooden deck trail through a bamboo grove leads up to the observatory. From the observatory you can see the East Sea, Cheongchoho Lake (청초호) and Seoraksan (설악산). Yeonggeumjeong (영금정) is right next to it.

  • Opening hours: Observatory 09:00~17:00, outdoor space 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Jumunjin Lighthouse (주문진 등대) (Yet-deungdae-gil 24-7 (옛등대길 24-7), Jumunjin-eup (주문진읍), Gangneung-si (강릉시), ☎ 033-662-2131) was built on March 20, 1918, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction it is now the oldest lighthouse in Gangwon Special Self-Governing Province (강원특별자치도). The 10m-high tower, built of brick, is coated with white lime mortar. It was damaged in the Korean War and restored in 1951.

  • Opening hours: Outdoor space 09:00~18:00, indoor facilities 09:00~17:00. Entry at night is prohibited.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

Mukho Lighthouse (묵호등대) (Haemaji-gil 289 (해맞이길 289), Donghae-si (동해시), ☎ Mukho Navigation Aids Management Office (묵호항로표지관리소) 033-531-3258) was built near Mukho Port (묵호항) on June 8, 1963, and stands 67m above sea level. At night it lights up with multicolored LED lights. A small park comes with it.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~17:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Daejin Lighthouse (대진등대) (Hannaru-ro 4-gil 16-38 (한나루로4길 16-38), Hyeonnae-myeon (현내면), Goseong-gun (고성군), ☎ 033-682-0172) is an octagonal concrete tower on a cape between Gejin Port (거진항) and Macha-jin (마차진). From the observatory on top of the tower you can see the East Sea, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, on clear days you can see as far as Haegeumgang (해금강) and the North Korean side.

  • Opening hours and closed days: It is registered that you should call to ask. Fee: Free.

📍 Gyeongbuk — Homigot Lighthouse, National Lighthouse Museum, Jukbyeon Lighthouse and Songdaemal Lighthouse

Homigot Lighthouse (호미곶 등대) (Homigot-gil 99 (호미곶길 99), Homigot-myeon (호미곶면), Nam-gu (남구), Pohang-si (포항시), ☎ 054-284-9814) first shone its light on December 20, 1908. The Korea Tourism Organization introduction describes it as the tallest lighthouse in Korea. It was designated a Gyeongsangbuk-do Monument (경상북도 기념물) in 1982.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

National Lighthouse Museum (국립등대박물관) (Haemaji-ro 150beon-gil 20 (해맞이로150번길 20), Homigot-myeon, Nam-gu, Pohang-si, ☎ 054-284-4857) opened in February 1985, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction it is the only museum in Korea devoted to lighthouses. It preserves and exhibits lighthouses and other navigation aid facilities and equipment.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next weekday if it is a public holiday), the days of Seollal and Chuseok. Fee: Free.

Jukbyeon Lighthouse (죽변등대) (Deungdae-gil 52 (등대길 52), Jukbyeon-myeon (죽변면), Uljin-gun (울진군), ☎ 054-783-7104) was the first lighthouse built in the Uljin area, in November 1910. It is an octagonal four-story concrete tower 15.6m high. It lost its function to bombing during the Korean War and was restored in October 1951.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Songdaemal Lighthouse (송대말 등대) (Cheoksa-gil 18-94 (척사길 18-94), Gampo-eup (감포읍), Gyeongju-si (경주시), ☎ 054-744-3233) is north of Gampo Port (감포항). The old lighthouse is no longer in use, and next to it a new five-story lighthouse building modeled on the stone pagoda at Gameunsaji (감은사지) has been built. The first and second floors are a light experience exhibition hall, and floors 3 to 5 serve as the lighthouse. A sea pine forest about 300 to 400 years old surrounds the lighthouse.

  • Opening hours: Songdaemal Lighthouse always open, lighthouse exhibition hall 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

📍 Ulsan — Ulgi Lighthouse, Seuldo Lighthouse and Hwaamchu Lighthouse

Ulgi Lighthouse (울기등대) (Deungdae-ro 155 (등대로 155), Dong-gu (동구), ☎ 052-251-2125) is inside Daewangam Park (대왕암공원). Wildflowers bloom along the 600m path to the lighthouse, and in April the tunnel of Yoshino cherry trees (왕벚나무) is in full bloom. Go down the steps past the lighthouse and you reach Daewangam (대왕암).

  • Opening hours: Apr~Sep 09:00~18:00, Oct~Mar 09:00~17:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next non-holiday day if it is a public holiday). Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

Seuldo Lighthouse (슬도등대) (Seongkkeut-gil 122 (성끝길 122), Dong-gu, ☎ Bangeo-dong Community Service Center (방어동행정복지센터) 052-209-4177) is reached by crossing the arched Seuldo Bridge (슬도교). Nearby is a statue of a whale carrying her calf on her back (Saekki Eobeun Gorae (새끼 업은 고래)), and at the Sound Experience Hall (소리체험관) at the entrance you can hear the sounds of whales and the sea. The Korea Tourism Organization introduction describes it as a sunset spot rare on the East Sea.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

Hwaamchu Lighthouse (화암추 등대) (Hwaam-deungdae-gil 50 (화암등대길 50), Dong-gu, ☎ 052-201-2602) is a turtle-shaped lighthouse built in December 1994. Take the elevator up to the observatory and you can see Ulsan Port (울산항) and the industrial complex.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~17:00. Closed days: Every Monday. Fee: Free.

📍 Busan — Oryukdo Lighthouse, Yeongdo Lighthouse and Gadeokdo Lighthouse

Oryukdo Lighthouse (오륙도 등대) (Oryukdo-ro 130 (오륙도로 130), Nam-gu (남구), ☎ Haeparang-gil Busan Tourist Information Center (해파랑길 부산관광안내소) 051-607-6395) is on the lighthouse island farthest from the mainland among Oryukdo's (오륙도) six islands. It is registered as having become unmanned in 2018 and not being open to the public, and it is controlled remotely from Yeongdo Lighthouse. The opening hours field also says "always open," so the registered values contradict each other. At the Haeparang-gil tourist information center you can get tourism information about Oryukdo Lighthouse.

  • Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Yeongdo Lighthouse (영도 등대) (Jeonmang-ro 181 (전망로 181), Yeongdo-gu (영도구), ☎ 051-405-1201) first shone its light in December 1906. Remodeling was completed in August 2004, turning it into a maritime culture space with a marine library and a gallery.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~17:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok holidays. It is registered that you should call for details. Fee: Free. Parking: Not available.

Gadeokdo Lighthouse (가덕도 등대) (Oeyangpo-ro 10 (외양포로 10), Gangseo-gu (강서구), ☎ 051-971-9710) is a lighthouse lit in December 1909 and designated a Busan Metropolitan City Tangible Cultural Property (부산광역시 유형문화유산) in 2003. A plaque above the entrance carries a plum blossom (오얏꽃) crest.

  • How to get in: It is inside a military zone, so access is restricted, and it is registered as not accepting tour applications for the time being because of military base security. Ask before you visit. Fee: Free.

📍 Jeonnam Yeosu — Odongdo Lighthouse

Odongdo Lighthouse (오동도 등대) (Odongdo-ro 238-32 (오동도로 238-32), Yeosu-si (여수시), ☎ 061-662-3999) is at the top of Odongdo. It first shone its light on May 12, 1952, and in 2002 it was rebuilt as an octagonal tower 27m high. There is an elevator for the observatory on the outside and an exhibition room on the second floor of the office building. The road up to the lighthouse forms a tunnel of camellia trees (동백나무), whose buds burst around November and peak around March, as it is described.

  • Opening hours: Apr~Sep 09:00~18:00, Oct~Mar 09:00~17:00. Closed days: Every Monday. Parking: Available.

📍 Jeju — Iho Tewoo Horse Lighthouses, Sanji Lighthouse and Marado Lighthouse

Iho Tewoo Horse Lighthouses (이호테우 말 등대) (Seohae-an-ro 58 (서해안로 58), Jeju-si (제주시), ☎ Jeju Tourist Information Center (제주관광정보센터) 064-740-6000) are lighthouses shaped like Jeju ponies (조랑말) on Iho Tewoo Beach (이호테우해변). A red horse lighthouse and a white horse lighthouse, each 12m high, stand one on the inner breakwater and one on the outer breakwater. The Korea Tourism Organization introduction says it is close to Jeju Airport.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

Sanji Lighthouse (산지 등대) (Sarabong-dong-gil 108-1 (사라봉동길 108-1), Jeju-si, ☎ 064-740-6000) is on the northern slope of Sarabong (사라봉). It was first installed as an unmanned lighthouse in October 1916, and a new tower was built in December 1999. There are a cafe and a gallery.

  • Opening hours: Summer 09:00~19:30, winter 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: 10 small cars.

Marado Lighthouse (마라도 등대) (Mara-ro 165 (마라로 165), Daejeong-eup (대정읍), Seogwipo-si (서귀포시), ☎ 064-792-8507) is on Marado (마라도), the southernmost point of the country. It is a white octagonal lighthouse 16m high, first built in March 1915 and rebuilt in March 1987. In front of the lighthouse are models of famous lighthouses around the world.

  • How to get there: Take a ferry from Unjin Port Passenger Terminal (운진항 여객터미널) or Sanisudong Port (산이수동항). It is registered that calling ahead is essential.
  • Opening hours: Marado Lighthouse 09:00~18:00. Unjin Port Passenger Terminal 09:40~15:50, Sanisudong Port ferry 09:20~16:10.
  • Ferry fare: Adults 21,000 won, middle and high school 20,800 won, elementary school 10,500 won, preschool 10,000 won, seniors (age 65 and over) 16,000 won.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Oryukdo Lighthouse and Gadeokdo Lighthouse are registered as closed to the public and to tours — Oryukdo Lighthouse has not been open to the public since 2018, and Gadeokdo Lighthouse has restricted access and is not accepting tour applications for the time being. Ask before you visit.
  2. Six places are closed on Mondays — Odongdo Lighthouse, Ulgi Lighthouse, Yeongdo Lighthouse, Hwaamchu Lighthouse, Nueseom Lighthouse Observatory and the National Lighthouse Museum.
  3. Check the tides first for Nueseom Lighthouse Observatory — you can walk in at low tide, and the operating hours change with the high and low tide times.

⚙ FAQ 3

Q. Which lighthouses have an observatory or exhibition space?

A. The places whose registered information lists an observatory or exhibition space are Oido Red Lighthouse (exhibition hall and observatory), Nueseom Lighthouse Observatory, Sokcho Lighthouse Observatory, Odongdo Lighthouse (elevator and exhibition room), Hwaamchu Lighthouse (elevator and observatory), Daejin Lighthouse (observatory), Songdaemal Lighthouse (exhibition hall), Yeongdo Lighthouse (library and gallery), Jebudo Lighthouse (observatory), Sanji Lighthouse (gallery) and the National Lighthouse Museum (exhibits).

Q. Which lighthouses are registered as always open?

A. Iho Tewoo Horse Lighthouses, Homigot Lighthouse, Seuldo Lighthouse, Jebudo Lighthouse and Songdaemal Lighthouse (the lighthouse only; the exhibition hall is 09:00~18:00). Oryukdo Lighthouse is also listed as "always open," but it is registered at the same time as not open to the public.

Q. Which is the oldest lighthouse?

A. Among the lighthouses in this guide, the one with the earliest registered lighting or construction date is Yeongdo Lighthouse (lit in December 1906). Next are Homigot Lighthouse (lit on December 20, 1908), Gadeokdo Lighthouse (lit in December 1909) and Jukbyeon Lighthouse (built in November 1910).
