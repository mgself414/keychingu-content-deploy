⚠ 3 Art mistakes

1. "Museum = Gyeongbokgung Palace only" — 4 National cluster unrecognized: Only MMCA Seoul is recognized. In reality, MMCA 4 halls (Seoul, Gwacheon, Cheongju, Deoksugung Palace) + National Museum of Korea = Korean art full experience matrix.

2. "Private = only Leeum" — Hoam·Amore Mania Miji: Only Leeum is recognized. In reality, Leum (Hannam) + Hoam (Yongin) + Amore Pacific (Yongsan) 3 private cluster + English guide mania.

3. "Gallery = Cheongdam Bay" — Awareness of discrimination in Samcheong and Insa-dong: Perception of luxury in Cheongdam Bay. In reality, Samcheong-dong (50+ Hanok Gallery) + Insa-dong (Traditional/Hanji) + Cheongdam (Luxury/Modern) 3 discrimination clusters.
