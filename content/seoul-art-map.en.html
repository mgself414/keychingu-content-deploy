Seoul Museum Guide — MMCA, Seoul Museum of Art, Leeum, Amorepacific, National Museum of Korea and Craft Museum, Official Info as of October 2026

Subtitle: MMCA Seoul is open on Mondays and free Wed & Sat 18:00–21:00 / Seoul Museum of Art Seosomun is free, closed Mon / Leeum combined ticket ₩25,000, permanent collection free, closed Mon / Amorepacific Sol LeWitt show ₩18,000, online booking / National Museum of Korea free, open until 21:00 Wed & Sat / Seoul Museum of Craft Art free, extended evening hours through 11/30 — based on each museum's official page, 2026-10-08

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Two questions untangle a Seoul museum route: "Who is open on Monday?" and "How far does free go?" On Mondays, MMCA Seoul (국립현대미술관 서울) and National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) are open (their only annual closures are Jan 1, Seollal and Chuseok), while Seoul Museum of Art, Seosomun (서울시립미술관 서소문본관), Leeum Museum of Art (리움미술관), Amorepacific Museum of Art, APMA (아모레퍼시픽미술관), Seoul Museum of Craft Art (서울공예박물관) and MMCA Deoksugung (국립현대미술관 덕수궁) are closed. Free entry covers Seoul Museum of Art (except special exhibitions), the National Museum of Korea (except special exhibitions), the Seoul Museum of Craft Art, the Leeum permanent collection, and MMCA Seoul Wed & Sat 18:00–21:00. Everything below is only what we read on each official page on 2026-10-08. Exhibition names are as of October, so check again when the month changes. Phrases from the older guide such as "mostly free", "3 MMCA branches" and "#1 with European enthusiasts" did not match the official pages, so we dropped them.

🏛 MMCA Seoul — Open on Mondays

  • Open 10:00–18:00, until 21:00 on Wednesdays and Saturdays, and free during those hours (18:00–21:00) (official). The combined ticket is ₩10,000. Its only annual closures are Jan 1, Seollal and Chuseok, so it is open on Mondays too — with a temporary closure on Dec 1.
  • The nearest exit is Anguk Station (안국역) Exit 1 (closer than Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역), which the older guide named), and Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역) Exit 4 and Gwanghwamun Station (광화문역)** Exit 2 also work. The official page lists no street address, so we left it out.
  • MMCA has 4 branches (Seoul, Gwacheon, Deoksugung, Cheongju) — the older guide's "3 branches" was wrong. MMCA Deoksugung (국립현대미술관 덕수궁) is closed on Mondays, so a one-day Seoul + Deoksugung route breaks on a Monday.

🖼 Seoul Museum of Art, Seosomun — Free

  • Tue–Thu 10:00–20:00, Fri 10:00–21:00, weekends 10:00–19:00 (winter until 18:00), closed Mondays. Free apart from special exhibitions. 61 Deoksugung-gil; City Hall Station (시청역) Exits 1, 10–12, Seodaemun Station (서대문역) Exit 5, Gwanghwamun Station (광화문역) Exit 6 (official).
  • Foreign-language docent tours Oct 16–24 (official).

🏺 Leeum and Amorepacific — Hannam and Yongsan

  • Leeum Museum of Art (리움미술관): Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00, closed Mon, combined ticket ₩25,000, permanent collection free, Hangangjin Station (한강진역) Exit 1. Digital guide in Korean, English, Chinese and Japanese. The official domain has changed to leeumhoam.org. October: Koo Jeong A 'Uthmos' and a women artists show.
  • Amorepacific Museum of Art, APMA (아모레퍼시픽미술관): Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00, closed Mon, Sol LeWitt show ₩18,000 with advance online booking, Sinyongsan Station (신용산역) Exit 1. Free 'APMA GUIDE' app.

🏛 National Museum of Korea and Seoul Museum of Craft Art — Two Free Options

  • National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관): Free (except special exhibitions), open until 21:00 on Wed & Sat, closed Jan 1, Seollal and Chuseok. 137 Seobinggo-ro. Exits and audio guide are not posted officially.
  • Seoul Museum of Craft Art (서울공예박물관): Free, 10:00–18:00, closed Mon, extended evening hours 9/15–11/30 (check the official table for hours by day), Anguk Station (안국역) Exit 1. 'Equinox' exhibition through 2027-03-07.

🚌 Hoam, SongEun and Art Sonje — Based on Official Summaries

  • Hoam Museum of Art (호암미술관) (Yongin): Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00, 100% advance booking (14 days ahead), shuttle service — read from the official page summary, so the fee and English guidance are unconfirmed.
  • SongEun (송은): Both exhibitions end Oct 10. Hours and fees unconfirmed. Art Sonje Center (아트선재센터): Tue–Sun 12:00–19:00 (official summary).
  • D Museum, Grounds Sisi, Kukje Gallery, Gallery Hyundai and the Samcheong-dong gallery street are left out because we could not read their official pages this time.

🗓 Routes by Day of the Week

  • Monday: MMCA Seoul (Anguk Station (안국역) Exit 1) → National Museum of Korea (free). Everything else is closed.
  • Wednesday & Saturday evening: MMCA Seoul free 18:00–21:00 → National Museum of Korea until 21:00.
  • Tue–Sun daytime: From Anguk Station (안국역) Exit 1, MMCA Seoul + Seoul Museum of Craft Art (free) → Seoul Museum of Art at City Hall Station (시청역) (free) / or Leeum at Hangangjin Station (한강진역) (permanent collection free) → APMA at Sinyongsan Station (신용산역) (booking required).

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Leeum, Seoul Museum of Art or APMA on a Monday — all closed. Go to MMCA Seoul or the National Museum of Korea instead.

2. Buying APMA tickets on site — it is online booking only.

3. Hoam on the same day — 100% booked 14 days ahead.

4. Assuming "everything is free" — Leeum special exhibition ₩25,000, APMA ₩18,000, MMCA combined ticket ₩10,000.

5. Going to MMCA via Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역) — Anguk Station (안국역)** Exit 1 is closer.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. A full day for free? — Seoul Museum of Art (Seosomun) → Seoul Museum of Craft Art → National Museum of Korea, all free. On Wed & Sat, add MMCA Seoul after 18:00.

2. English guidance? — Leeum digital guide in 4 languages, the APMA app, Seoul Museum of Art foreign-language docent tours (Oct 16–24). The two national museums are not posted officially.

3. Open at night? — MMCA Seoul and the National Museum of Korea until 21:00 Wed & Sat, Seoul Museum of Art until 21:00 Fri, the Craft Museum has evening hours through 11/30.

4. How much is Leeum? — Combined ticket ₩25,000; the permanent collection is free.

5. How to get to Hoam? — Shuttle (official); advance booking is required.

6. When do exhibitions change? — These are as of October. Several end Oct 10–11 and Oct 25, so check the official pages before you go.
