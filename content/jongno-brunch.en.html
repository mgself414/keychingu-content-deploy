⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by International Visitors

1. Ignoring Peak Hours (11 AM - 1 PM on Weekends) — Hanok establishments can have 1-2 hour waits. Weekdays from 9 AM - 11 AM or 2 PM - 4 PM are recommended.

2. Some Establishments are Cash-Only — Some hanok establishments may only accept cash. Prepare ₩30,000 (~$22 / ~160 RMB / ~3,200 JPY) in advance. Modern establishments usually accept cards.

3. Shoes & Dress Code — Some hanok establishments require you to remove your shoes. American-style establishments are generally casual.

8 Establishments in Detail

1. Dot Blanket Samcheong Hanok Branch (도트블랭킷 삼청한옥점)

  • Address: Samcheong-dong (삼청동), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul
  • Signature: A top spot combining a hanok courtyard with brunch. Enjoy a hanok ambiance with modern brunch menus.
  • International Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English menu available, located on a popular route for international visitors in Samcheong-dong (삼청동).
  • Recommended Menu: Signature Brunch Plate, Eggs Benedict, Seasonal Menu
  • Price Range: ₩18,000~28,000 (~$13~20) for one brunch set.
  • Tip: Expect a 1-hour wait on weekends. Weekday mornings are recommended.

2. Dot Blanket Anguk Branch (도트블랭킷 안국점)

  • Address: Anguk (안국) area, Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul

3. Ikseon Japbang (익선잡방)

⚙ FAQ

Q1. English Service & Payment?

A. All establishments offer English menus. Most accept cards and international cards. For some hanok establishments, cash is recommended.

Q2. Recommended Times & Days?

A. Weekdays 9 AM - 11 AM / 2 PM - 4 PM: Best for a relaxed experience. Weekends 11 AM - 1 PM: Peak hours — expect 1-2 hour waits. Some establishments are closed on Mondays and Tuesdays — check their Instagram in advance.

Q3. Recommendations by Price Range?

A. ₩7,000~14,000 (~$5~10): Elephant Bagel (코끼리베이글). ₩15,000~25,000 (~$11~18): American style, Ikseon Japbang (익선잡방), Lucid Seoul (루시드 서울), American Brunch Cafe (아메리칸 브런치 카페), Aslike Seochon (애즈라이크 서촌). ₩18,000~30,000 (~$13~22): Dot Blanket (도트블랭킷), Golden (골덴).

Q4. International Visitor-Friendly Establishments?

A. Familiar to US/European visitors: Elephant Bagel (코끼리베이글), American Brunch Cafe (아메리칸 브런치 카페).

For a Korean hanok atmosphere: Dot Blanket (도트블랭킷), Ikseon Japbang (익선잡방).

Modern & Instagrammable: Golden (골덴), Aslike Seochon (애즈라이크 서촌), Lucid Seoul (루시드 서울).

Q5. How to Combine with Gyeongbokgung (경복궁), Changdeokgung (창덕궁), and Bukchon (북촌)?

A. Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) + Dot Blanket Anguk (도트블랭킷 안국): 5-minute walk. Changdeokgung Palace (창덕궁) + Ikseon Japbang (익선잡방): 10-minute walk. Bukchon (북촌) + Dot Blanket Samcheong (도트블랭킷 삼청): 5-minute walk. Seochon (서촌) + Aslike Seochon (애즈라이크 서촌): Within walking distance.
