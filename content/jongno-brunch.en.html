8 Store Details

1. Dot Blanket Samcheong Hanok Branch

  • Address: Samcheong-dong area, Jongno-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: The #1 store that combines Hanok courtyard + brunch. Hanok atmosphere + modern brunch menu.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English menu, Samcheong-dong foreigner movement route
  • Recommended Menu: Signature Brunch Plate, Eggs Benedict, Seasonal Menu
  • Price range: ₩18,000~28,000 (brunch for 1 person / ~$13~20)
  • Tip: Wait 1 hour on weekends. Recommended on weekday mornings.

⚙ FAQ

Q1. English service/payment?

A. English menus available at all stores. Most credit cards and foreign cards are OK. Cash recommended at some Hanok stores.

Q2. What are your recommendations by time zone and day of the week?

A. Weekdays 9-11:00 / 14-16:00: Hansan Best. Weekends 11:00-13:00: Peak — 1-2 hour wait. Store closed on Mondays and Tuesdays — Check Instagram in advance.

Q3. Any recommendations by price range?

A. ₩7K~14K: Elephant Bagel. ₩15K~25K: American, Ikseon, Lucid, American, As Like. ₩18K~30K: Dot blanket·golden.

Q4. What are foreigner-friendly stores?

A. Familiar with the U.S. and Europe: Elephant Bagel and American Brunch Cafe.

Korean Hanok Atmosphere: Dot Blanket·Ikseon Japbang.

Modern Insta: Golden, As Like, Lucid.

Q5. How can it be combined with Gyeongbokgung Palace, Changdeokgung Palace, and Bukchon?

A. Gyeongbokgung Palace + Dot Blanket Anguk: 5 minutes on foot. Changdeokgung Palace + Ikseon Japbang: 10 minutes on foot. Bukchon + Dot Blanket Samcheong: 5 minutes walk. Seochon + As Like Seochon: Within walking distance.
