Seosan Dongbu Market and 12 West Sea markets

1. Seosan Dongbu Market (West Sea seafood)

  • Discrimination: Seosan authentic market·West Sea marine products + Seaweed rice cluster·Established in the 1980s
  • Signature: 6 cloves of Seosan garlic, Seosan pumpkin, mud flat oyster
  • kr cross: #380·#618·#243 Donghae

2. Seosan 6 cloves garlic

  • Discrimination: Korea #1 Garlic production area·6 cloves of garlic (different from regular garlic)·Harvest every May
  • Store: Dongbu Market, Haemi 5-day Market, Seosan Nonghyup
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP souvenir (preservation OK)

3. Haemieupseong Fortress (Historic Site No. 116)

  • Discrimination: Joseon Military City 1421·Chungcheong-do Jwasyeong·General Yi Sun-sin served
  • Catholic Persecution: Traces of Catholic persecution in 1866·Pope Francis visit in 2014
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·CN Orthodox enthusiasts/religious enthusiasts

4. Ganwolam (No. 1 West Sea sunset)

  • Discrimination: Island during tidal differences → land·No. 1 among West Sea sunset enthusiasts·Muhak Ambassador in 1376
  • Location: Entrance to Cheonsuman Bay, Ganwoldo Island
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ JP·EN No. 1 sunset enthusiast

5. Cheonsu Bay (West Sea mud flat)

  • Discrimination: Korea’s West Sea, one of the world’s five largest tidal flats·Listed in the 2007 Ramsar Convention·Bird habitat
  • Experience: Tidal flat experience, octopus, clam catching
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP Nature Mania

6. Anmyeondo (Natural Recreational Forest)

  • Discrimination: Korea #6 Big Island Anmyeondo·1968 Bridge Connection·Natural Recreational Forest·International Flower Expo
  • Signature: Kkotji Beach·Grandmother Rock + Grandfather Rock Sunset
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Nature·Sunset Mania

7. Kkotji Beach

  • Discrimination: Best beach in Anmyeondo·Grandmother Rock + Grandfather Rock sunset photo spot·4km white sand beach
  • Time: June-September for swimming, sunset all year round
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ JP·EN Beach Mania

8. Seosan Sea Rice Rice

  • Distinction: Authentic Seosan Gukbap·Near Haemieupseong Fortress·Clear brisket soup·Different from Pork Gukbap
  • Store: Haemi 5-day Market, Seosan City
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP authentic food

9. Seosan Oysters

  • Distinction: Tidal Flat Oysters·Season December-February·Cheonsu Bay Tidal Flats·Small but sweet
  • Cooking: Seasoned oysters, oyster ramen, oyster pancakes
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ JP Seafood Mania

10. Haemi 5-day market (1st and 6th)

  • Discrimination: Last day of every month, 1st and 6th day, Authentic 5-day market near Haemieupseong Castle, Haemisi rice alley
  • kr cross: #663 Andong Old Market sub (Oil Market)

11. Rock-carved Buddha Triad (National Treasure No. 84)

  • Discrimination: Baekje Smiling Buddha (Seosan Rock-carved Buddha Triad)·6th century Baekje authentic stone Buddha·National treasure
  • Location: Mt. Gayasan, Unsan-myeon
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

12. Seosan 2 days and 1 night + Chungnam cluster

  • Access: Seoul → Seosan Bus 1 hour and 30 minutes (transfer to KTX Cheonan Asan)
  • cluster: Seosan-Taean (Anmyeondo)-Dangjin (Hanjin Port) connection
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP 1 night stay recommended

FAQ 6

Q1. Is Seosan day-trip possible? A. 1 hour and 30 minutes by bus from Seoul, full day - Anmyeondo + 1 night recommended.

Q2. Ganwolam sunset time? A. Sunset all year round (time varies by season)·Entering on foot during low tide is OK - Check the tide table in advance.

Q3. How to get to Anmyeondo? A. 50 minutes by car from downtown Seosan·1968 Bridge·Public transportation difficult - rental car recommended.

Q4. Seosan 6 clove garlic harvest time? A. May-June every year·Preservation (storage) throughout the year·May-June is the best for fresh garlic.

Q5. Admission fee to Haemieupseong Fortress? A. Free (open 24 hours) · Haemi Catholic Church (nearby) is also free.

Q6. Cheonsu Bay Tidal Flat Experience? A. Recommended May-September·Tide table required·Experience cost 10,000 won~/person.
