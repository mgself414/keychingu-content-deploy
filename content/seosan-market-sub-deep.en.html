Seosan Market Guide — Dongbu Traditional Market's 280-Plus Shops, Haemi Eupseong Fortress, Ganwolam Hermitage Tides, Yonghyeon-ri Rock-Carved Buddha Triad

Subtitle: Seosan Dongbu Traditional Market (서산동부전통시장), 09:00–18:00, connected to the bus terminal · Seosan Haemi Eupseong Fortress (서산 해미읍성), March–October 05:00–21:00 · Ganwolam Hermitage (간월암), open depending on the tides · Seosan Yonghyeon-ri Rock-Carved Buddha Triad (서산 용현리 마애여래삼존상), 09:00–18:00, July–August until 21:00 — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

For markets in Seosan, Seosan Dongbu Traditional Market in the city center is the main one. It is the market that represents northwestern South Chungcheong, with shops selling west coast seafood, and it is connected to the Seosan Public Terminal. Beyond the market are Haemi Eupseong Fortress in Haemi-myeon (해미면), Ganwolam Hermitage, which you can walk into only when the water recedes, and the Baekje rock Buddha Yonghyeon-ri Rock-Carved Buddha Triad.

This guide covers only the market and those three places, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). A route that tours Ganwolam Hermitage and Haemi Eupseong Fortress together is covered separately in #1045 Seosan Ganwolam Hermitage + Haemi Eupseong Fortress.

🐟 Seosan Dongbu Traditional Market — West Coast Seafood and the Market Next to the Terminal

Seosan Dongbu Traditional Market (7-2 Sijang 3-gil, Seosan (서산시 시장3길 7-2), ☎ 041-667-7722) is a public market that first opened in 1956. It was rebuilt in a 2008 modernization project.

  • Opening hours: 09:00–18:00, differing by shop. Closed days also differ by shop.
  • Size: 280-plus shops and 200-plus stall vendors. The area is 22,943㎡.
  • What they sell: Seafood coming in from the nearby west coast, vegetables, silk, fabric, and clothing, hardware, and food.
  • How to get there: It is connected to the Seosan Public Terminal.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available.

🏯 Seosan Haemi Eupseong Fortress — The Walled Town You Walk Along the Walls

Seosan Haemi Eupseong Fortress (143 Namun 2-ro, Haemi-myeon, Seosan (서산시 해미면 남문2로 143), ☎ Haemi Eupseong Management Office 041-661-8005) is a flatland walled town built in 1491 under King Seongjong of Joseon. A stone wall about 1.8km in circumference and the East, South, and West Gates remain, and you can walk along the walls.

  • Opening hours: March–October 05:00–21:00, November–February 06:00–19:00. Open year-round. You cannot enter the fortress from 30 minutes before the gates close.
  • Activities: Tuho (arrow-throwing game), kite flying, and traditional archery experiences.
  • History: A place where many believers were tortured and martyred during the persecution of Catholics in the late Joseon period. The pagoda tree in front of the South Gate (Jinnamun) is estimated to be about 300 years old, and it is said that believers held in prison were hung from its branches and tortured. It is a Monument of South Chungcheong Province.
  • Offal restaurant (registered with the tourism organization): Haemi Usijang (해미우시장) (22 Eupseongmaeul 3-gil, Haemi-myeon (해미면 읍성마을3길 22), ☎ 041-688-5988), near the fortress, is not a market but a restaurant specializing in pork offal. 10:30–20:00 (preparation time 14:00–16:00), closed every Monday, no parking.

🌊 Ganwolam Hermitage — The Hermitage You Visit by the Tides

Ganwolam Hermitage (119-29 Ganwoldo 1-gil, Buseok-myeon, Seosan (서산시 부석면 간월도1길 119-29), ☎ 041-668-6624) is a small hermitage said to have been founded by the monk Muhak in the early Joseon period. At low tide it connects to the land, and at high tide it becomes an island and looks like it is floating on the water.

  • Opening hours and closed days: They vary with the tides. The tourism organization's information tells you to check the tide information on the website before going.
  • Facilities: Parking is available. Strollers cannot be used.
  • Oysters: Ganwoldo is an oyster-producing area, and the village has an eoriguljeot (어리굴젓, salted oysters) memorial tower. Every year at high tide on the first full moon of the lunar year, the Gulbureugi Gunwangje (굴부르기군왕제), a rite praying for a bountiful oyster harvest, is held.

🙂 Seosan Yonghyeon-ri Rock-Carved Buddha Triad — The "Baekje Smile"

Seosan Yonghyeon-ri Rock-Carved Buddha Triad (65-13 Maaesamjonbul-gil, Unsan-myeon, Seosan (서산시 운산면 마애삼존불길 65-13), ☎ 0507-1319-2538) is a Buddha image carved into rock around the 7th century, in the late Baekje period. It is also called the Seosan Maae Samjonbul, and it was designated a National Treasure in 1962.

  • Opening hours: September–June 09:00–18:00, July–August 09:00–21:00. Open year-round. Parking is available.
  • What to see: There is a standing Shakyamuni Buddha in the center, a pensive bodhisattva on the right, and a standing Bodhisattva Jehwagalra on the left. The facial expressions look different depending on when the sun shines on them.
  • Location: It is inside Yonghyeon Valley, and the Seosan Arame-gil Cheonnyeon Smile Trail passes by.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to Ganwolam Hermitage without checking the tides — At high tide it becomes an island and you cannot walk in. Check the tide information on the website first.
  2. Thinking Haemi Usijang is a market — The Haemi Usijang registered with the tourism organization is a pork offal restaurant, and it is closed every Monday.
  3. Going to Haemi Eupseong Fortress just before closing time — You cannot enter the fortress from 30 minutes before the gates close. In November–February it closes at 19:00.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where do I go to see seafood in Seosan?

A. Seosan Dongbu Traditional Market. It has shops selling west coast seafood, and it is open 09:00–18:00 (varies by shop).

Q. Can I use a card?

A. Whether cards are accepted is not registered in the tourism organization's data for the places in this guide.
