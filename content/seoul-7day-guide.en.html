⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make on their first visit

1. "If you look at Gangnam, Hongdae, and Myeong-dong, you can see all of Korea" — Jongno-Insa-dong Pass

The most common mistakes made by foreigners on their first visit. Gangnam, Hongdae, and Myeongdong are trendy and shopping areas, but the depth of Korean culture lies in Jongno, Insa-dong, and Bukchon. We recommend deep diving in Jongno and Bukchon at least 2 days out of 7.

2. Do not balance cafes, desserts, bars, and shopping by only looking at food

I only go to 8-10 Korean restaurants, but I skip cafes, desserts, bars, and shopping. In reality, cafes and desserts are the core of Korean MZ culture + select shops and K-beauty are shopping trends. A balance of 30% food, 20% cafes, 15% bars, and 35% shopping and experiences is recommended for 7 days.

3. Night course/24-hour cafe/sauna pass when adjusting to jet lag

No jet lag for the first 2-3 days — nighttime activities possible. 24-hour cafe (CAFE 00:00, #203) · Night market (#137 10 best night markets) · Jjimjilbang (#07, #34 etiquette) This is a signature area for foreigners adjusting to jet lag. In addition to the daytime route, 1-2 night courses are added.

4. Incheon Airport → City Center Travel Cost/Misunderstanding

Confusion over taxi, limousine, and airport rail options. Cost and time comparison with #87 Incheon Airport Guide. The optimal option varies depending on whether the first arrival is on a weekday, weekend, or late at night.

5. Seasonal/seasonal content not considered

Separate spring (cherry blossoms #44 Han River summer, #89 fall foliage, #90 winter illumination), summer, fall, and winter content from your 7-day schedule regardless of the time of year. In reality, visit timing matching is recommended — Han River in May-July, camping in August, autumn leaves in September-November, and illumination in December-February.
