Busan Jagalchi·Gukje Market·Yeongdo·Songdo — 11 Spots: Market Hours, Cable Car Fares and How to Get There

Subtitle: For first-time visitors to Busan, this guide groups 11 spots in 3 zones: the old downtown, Yeongdo and Songdo. Jagalchi Market is open 05:00–22:00 and closed on the first, third and fifth Tuesdays. Gukje Market is open 09:00–20:00 and closed on Sundays. Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market is open daily 19:30–24:00. The Songdo Marine Cable Car runs 09:00–21:00 (as shown on 2026-11-24, with separate winter hours from December), the adult Air Cruise round trip is 19,000 won, and it is closed for maintenance on 2026-11-30 to 12-01. Fares are the adult regular prices as seen on 2026-11-24.

ISSUE 06 · #244 · 2026-11-24 · Busan Old Downtown·Yeongdo·Songdo

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 11

This list is made for foreign travelers visiting Busan for the first time. Use it when you spend a day or two seeing the markets and streets of the old downtown near Busan Station (부산역), and the seaside of Yeongdo (영도) and Songdo (송도), which are one bus ride away.

It is divided into 3 zones.

  • Old downtown zone (6 spots): Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장), Gukje Market (국제시장), Bupyeong Kkangtong Market (부평깡통시장) (night market), BIFF Square (BIFF광장), Gwangbok-ro (광복로), Yongdusan Park (용두산공원)·Busan Tower (부산타워).
  • Yeongdo zone (2 spots): Huinnyeoul Culture Village (흰여울문화마을), Taejongdae (태종대) (Danubi Train, 다누비열차).
  • Songdo zone (3 spots): Songdo Beach (송도해수욕장), Songdo Marine Cable Car (송도해상케이블카), Songdo Cloud Trails (송도구름산책로).

The 6 old downtown spots are reached on foot from Subway Line 1 Nampo Station (남포역), Jagalchi Station (자갈치역) and Jungang Station (중앙역). Yeongdo and Songdo are reached by bus from in front of Nampo Station or Jagalchi Station. So for each spot this issue first gives "which exit of which station, and which bus to take".

Closing days differ from spot to spot in this area. The two markets close on different days, the Danubi Train at Taejongdae closes on Mondays, and the Songdo Marine Cable Car has a maintenance closure on 2026-11-30 to 12-01. What closes on which day is collected in a separate table below.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours, fares and directions are values read on 2026-11-24. Sources are Visit Busan (비짓부산), Busan City, the Taejongdae Resort website, the website of the company that operates the Songdo Marine Cable Car, and Korea Airports Corporation (한국공항공사). Fares are adult regular prices. The address and the walking time and distance from stations are Kakao Map (카카오맵) values, and the line itself says "Kakao Map". The old downtown exits are Kakao Map directions values, and the bus boarding exits for Yeongdo and Songdo are Visit Busan guide values. Opening hours, fares and bus routes can change.

✈ From Gimhae Airport to the Old Downtown

  • The airport limousine has no route to Nampo or Jagalchi. According to the Busan City English notice (read 2026-11-24), the airport limousine has run on two branches since the reorganization on 2026-01-05. The Haeundae·Gijang direction is 9,500 won and the Seomyeon·Bujeon direction is 6,000 won.
  • From the airport, take the light rail. The Korea Airports Corporation guide to Gimhae International Airport (김해국제공항) says to take the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail Transit (부산김해경전철) and transfer to Subway Line 2 at Sasang Station and to Line 3 at Daejeo Station.
  • The old downtown stations are all on Line 1. Nampo Station, Jagalchi Station and Jungang Station in this issue are on Subway Line 1 (Kakao Map). Busan Station is also a Line 1 station.
  • The station differs from spot to spot. Jagalchi Market is Nampo Station Exit 2, Gukje Market and BIFF Square are Jagalchi Station Exit 7, Bupyeong Kkangtong Market is Jagalchi Station Exit 3, and Yongdusan Park is Jungang Station Exit 1 (all by Kakao Map directions).

🚦 Before You Walk — Shared Safety and Manners

  • Huinnyeoul Culture Village is a village where residents live. Visit Busan asks visitors to observe courtesy for the residents.
  • If the weather is bad, the rides stop. The Danubi Train at Taejongdae stops running when it rains or the weather is bad. The Songdo Marine Cable Car may close early depending on weather and ticket sales. Access to the Songdo Cloud Trails can also be restricted because of weather and facility maintenance. Busan Tower's operation can also change with the weather.
  • The Taejongdae shoreline has a registered night-time access restriction. The Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-24) is 20:00–06:00.
  • Market hours differ by shop. Gukje Market's 09:00–20:00 is for the market as a whole and differs by shop.
  • Call 119 for accidents or emergencies and 112 to report a crime.

① Old Downtown Zone — 6 Spots

This zone uses Subway Line 1 Nampo Station, Jagalchi Station and Jungang Station. All 6 spots are reached on foot from a station. The 3 markets open at different times. Jagalchi Market opens early at 05:00, Gukje Market at 09:00 and Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market in the evening at 19:30. You can split them into morning, daytime and night within one day.

Jagalchi Market

  • What it is: A seafood market right on the waterfront.
  • Operating hours: 05:00–22:00 per the official Visit Busan guide. The Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-24) is the same, daily 05:00–22:00.
  • Closed: The official Visit Busan guide gives the first, third and fifth Tuesdays.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-24): 52 Jagalchihaean-ro (자갈치해안로), Jung-gu (중구), Busan.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is a 6 minute walk (355 m) from Exit 2 of Subway Line 1 Nampo Station. The official Visit Busan guide also gives a 6 minute walk from Nampo Station Exit 2.
  • Fitting the timing: It opens at 05:00, the earliest of the 3 old downtown markets. Put it before Gukje Market, which opens at 09:00.

Gukje Market

  • What it is: Visit Busan describes it as a market that grew up after the Korean War (한국전쟁).
  • Operating hours: 09:00–20:00 per the official Visit Busan guide. It differs by shop.
  • Closed: Sundays.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-24): 56-4 Sinchang-dong 4-ga (신창동4가), Jung-gu, Busan.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is an 8 minute walk (462 m) from Exit 7 of Line 1 Jagalchi Station.
  • Bus: The official Visit Busan guide gives buses 15, 40, 86, 126 and 186, getting off at "Gukje Market" (국제시장) and walking 4 minutes.
  • Fitting the timing: It closes at 20:00. Put it before Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market, which starts at 19:30. If you are in Busan on a Sunday, move Gukje Market to another day.

Bupyeong Kkangtong Market · Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market

  • Night market hours: Daily 19:30–24:00 per the official Visit Busan guide. It opens regardless of the day of the week. It is closed on traditional holidays.
  • Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-24): The same, daily 19:30–24:00.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-24): 82 Bupyeong-dong 2-ga (부평동2가), Jung-gu, Busan.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is a 10 minute walk (563 m) from Exit 3 of Line 1 Jagalchi Station. The official Visit Busan guide also gives about a 10 minute walk from Jagalchi Station Exit 3.
  • Fitting the timing: 19:30–24:00 is the night market's hours. The daytime business of the shops inside the market is separate for each shop. Put it last, around dinner time, after going around the old downtown by day.

BIFF Square

  • What it is: An outdoor square in Nampo-dong. The Visit Busan Nampo-dong guide lists BIFF Square as a main sight of the area.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-24): 15-1 Nampo-dong 3-ga (남포동3가), Jung-gu, Busan.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is a 4 minute walk (238 m) from Exit 7 of Line 1 Jagalchi Station.
  • How to combine it: It uses the same Exit 7 as Gukje Market. From the exit it is a 4 minute walk to BIFF Square and an 8 minute walk to Gukje Market.

Gwangbok-ro Fashion Street

  • What it is: A shopping street lined with shops. Visit Busan introduces it as "Gwangbok-ro Fashion Street".
  • Hours · fare: The official Visit Busan guide says it is always open, and the street itself is free. Business hours differ by shop.
  • How to get there: Walk from Subway Line 1 Nampo Station. The Visit Busan Gwangbok-ro guide gives a 5 minute walk from Nampo Station.
  • The way up to Yongdusan Park: According to the official Visit Busan guide, there is an 87 m escalator from Gwangbok-ro up to Yongdusan Park. Plan to walk Gwangbok-ro and then take this escalator up to the park.

Yongdusan Park · Busan Tower

  • Yongdusan Park: Free and open year-round per the official Visit Busan guide. The Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-24) is 24 hours.
  • Busan Tower operating hours: 10:00–22:00 per the official Visit Busan guide, with entry closing at 21:30. Operation can change with the weather. The Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-24) is also daily 10:00–22:00.
  • Fare: The park is free. The Busan Tower observatory charges a fare separate from the park.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-24): Yongdusan Park is 37-55 Yongdusan-gil (용두산길), Jung-gu, Busan, and Busan Tower is 37-30 Yongdusan-gil.
  • How to get there: By Kakao Map directions, it is a 10 minute walk (432 m) from Exit 1 of Line 1 Jungang Station to the park and a 12 minute walk (562 m) to Busan Tower.
  • Another way: From the Gwangbok-ro side, you come up by the 87 m escalator.
  • Fitting the timing: The tower is open until 22:00, but entry ends at 21:30. If you want to see Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market (19:30–24:00) first and then come up, you must reach the tower entrance before 21:30. It is easier to fit the times if you reverse the order, going up the tower around sunset and then down to the night market.

② Yeongdo Zone — 2 Spots

Yeongdo has no subway station. Both spots are reached by bus from Nampo-dong. Huinnyeoul Culture Village is a village where residents live, and Taejongdae is a resort park. At Taejongdae, the Danubi Train, a loop train, is closed on Mondays.

Huinnyeoul Culture Village

  • What it is: A village in Yeongdo. Residents still live there.
  • Hours: Open daily per the official Visit Busan guide.
  • Visiting etiquette: The official Visit Busan guide asks visitors to "observe courtesy for the residents". Before it is a tourist spot, it is people's homes and alleys.
  • How to get there (official Visit Busan guide): Leave Subway Line 1 Nampo Station through Exit 6 and take bus 7, 71 or 508. Get off at the stop toward Baengnyeonsa Temple (백련사) in Yeongseon-dong (영선동) and enter the village by the stairs.
  • Parking: There is a paid public parking lot at the entrance to the Jeolyeong Coastal Walk (절영해안산책로).
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-24): 605-3 Yeongseon-dong 4-ga (영선동4가), Yeongdo-gu (영도구), Busan.
  • Nature of the path: You enter the village by stairs from the bus stop.

Taejongdae · Danubi Train

  • What it is: A resort park in Yeongdo. The Danubi Train loops inside the resort.
  • Resort hours (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-24): Daily 05:00–24:00 (Kakao Map, winter season). Access to the shoreline is restricted 20:00–06:00.
  • Admission: Admission to the resort is free per the official Visit Busan guide. The Danubi Train requires a separate ticket.
  • Danubi Train operation (official Visit Busan guide): From October to May it runs from 09:20 to 17:30. Ticket sales start at 09:00 and end at 17:30. The Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-24) is the same, 09:20–17:30.
  • Closed: The Danubi Train is closed every Monday.
  • Weather: The Danubi Train stops running when it rains or the weather is bad.
  • How to get there (official Taejongdae Resort guide):
  • From opposite Lotte Department Store in Nampo-dong, buses 8, 17, 30, 88 and 186. About 30 minutes.
  • From opposite Kyowon Building (교원빌딩) at Busan Station, buses 17, 88 and 101. About 40 minutes.
  • Address: 24 Jeonmang-ro (전망로), Yeongdo-gu, Busan. The Kakao Map registered address is San 29-1 Dongsam-dong (동삼동), Yeongdo-gu, Busan.
  • Inquiries: Resort facilities 051-860-7866 (Busan Facilities Corporation, 부산시설공단), Danubi Train 051-405-8745~6 (Busan Tourism Organization, 부산관광공사).
  • Fitting the timing: In November the Danubi Train ends at 17:30. It takes about 30 minutes by bus from Nampo-dong, so if you leave late in the afternoon you will miss the train. Put it in the morning or early afternoon. If it is a Monday, move Taejongdae to another day.

③ Songdo Zone — 3 Spots

All 3 are reached by bus from Exit 2 of Subway Line 1 Jagalchi Station. For the beach and the Cloud Trails, get off at the "Songdo Beach" (송도해수욕장) stop, and for the cable car at the "Amnam-dong Community Center" (암남동주민센터) stop. The beach and the Cloud Trails are open and free 06:00–23:00, and only the cable car charges a fare.

Songdo Beach

  • What it is: Visit Busan describes it as a beach that opened in 1913.
  • Hours · fare: Daily 06:00–23:00 and free per the official Visit Busan guide.
  • How to get there (official Visit Busan guide): Leave Line 1 Jagalchi Station through Exit 2 and take bus 96, 26, 30, 7 or 71. Get off at "Songdo Beach" and walk 3 minutes.

Songdo Marine Cable Car · Busan Air Cruise

A cable car that crosses over the sea. The values below are as shown on the operator's official website on 2026-11-24.

  • Operating hours: 09:00–21:00. The Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-24) is also daily 09:00–21:00.
  • Ticket sales cutoff: Ticket sales end 30 minutes before closing. It may close earlier depending on weather and ticket sales.
  • Closed: It does not run for 2 days, 2026-11-30 to 12-01, for regular maintenance.
  • Winter hours: The winter operating hours for December 2026 to February 2027 are announced separately by the operator.

Fare table (adult regular price, 2026-11-24)

Cabin Round trip One way
Air Cruise 19,000 won 15,000 won
Crystal Cruise 24,000 won 19,000 won
  • Adult standard: In the operator's fare table, an adult is a middle school student (age 13) or older.
  • Round trip and one way: The round trip is 4,000 won more than one way for the Air Cruise and 5,000 won more for the Crystal Cruise. If you plan to come back by cable car, buy the round-trip ticket.
  • Address: 171 Songdohaebyeon-ro (송도해변로), Seo-gu (서구), Busan.
  • How to get there (official Visit Busan guide): Leave Line 1 Jagalchi Station through Exit 2 and take bus 6, 26, 30, 96, 71 or 96-1. Get off at "Amnam-dong Community Center" and walk about 10 minutes.
  • Fitting the timing: It ends at 21:00 and tickets close 30 minutes before. You must reach the ticket office before 20:30. On a bad-weather day it may close earlier, so do not leave it to the end of the evening.

Songdo Cloud Trails

  • What it is: A walking path reached by getting off at the "Songdo Beach" stop.
  • Hours · fare: 06:00–23:00 and free per the official Visit Busan guide.
  • Restriction: Access can be restricted depending on weather and facility maintenance.
  • How to get there (official Visit Busan guide): Leave Line 1 Jagalchi Station through Exit 2 and take bus 26, 30, 7, 71 or 96. Get off at "Songdo Beach".
  • How to combine it: You get off at the same stop as Songdo Beach. The Songdo Cloud Trails and Songdo Beach operate separately from the cable car, and the Visit Busan hours are daily 06:00–23:00.

🗺 Routes — Half Day and Full Day

The order is set to match each spot's opening and closing times. The travel times the agencies give are about 30 minutes by bus from Nampo-dong to Taejongdae and about 40 minutes from Busan Station to Taejongdae.

Half Day A — Old Downtown by Day (avoid Sundays and the first, third and fifth Tuesdays)

  1. Line 1 Nampo Station Exit 2 → Jagalchi Market (6 minutes on foot by Kakao Map directions). It opens from 05:00.
  2. Jagalchi Station Exit 7 → BIFF Square (4 minutes on foot from the exit).
  3. Gukje Market (8 minutes on foot from Jagalchi Station Exit 7). 09:00–20:00.
  4. Walk Gwangbok-ro and go up to Yongdusan Park by the 87 m escalator.
  5. Busan Tower. Entry is until 21:30.

On a Sunday, drop step 3, Gukje Market, and on the first, third and fifth Tuesdays drop step 1, Jagalchi Market.

Half Day B — Old Downtown in the Evening (any day of the week, except traditional holidays)

  1. Line 1 Jungang Station Exit 1 → Yongdusan Park (10 minutes on foot by Kakao Map directions) → Busan Tower (10:00–22:00).
  2. Take the escalator down to Gwangbok-ro and walk the street.
  3. Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market. It starts at 19:30 and is open until 24:00. It is a 10 minute walk from Jagalchi Station Exit 3.

The night market opens every day, so you do not need to consider the day of the week. If you see the tower first, you do not need to worry about the 21:30 entry cutoff.

Full Day — Yeongdo in the Morning, Songdo in the Afternoon, Old Downtown at Night (avoid Mondays and 2026-11-30 to 12-01)

Time Spot Time to meet
Morning Taejongdae (Danubi Train) Danubi Train 09:20–17:30, closed on Mondays. About 30 minutes by bus from Nampo-dong
Daytime Huinnyeoul Culture Village Open daily. Bus 7, 71 or 508 from Nampo Station Exit 6
Afternoon Songdo Beach → Songdo Cloud Trails Both 06:00–23:00, free. Bus from Jagalchi Station Exit 2
Around sunset Songdo Marine Cable Car 09:00–21:00 (winter hours separate), tickets until 30 minutes before closing
Night Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market Daily 19:30–24:00

Yeongdo and Songdo are in different directions. Plan to come back toward Nampo·Jagalchi and take a bus again for each. For the 2 Yeongdo spots, you take the bus from the Nampo Station side, and for the 3 Songdo spots from Jagalchi Station Exit 2.

If you have 2 days, spend one day on the 6 old downtown spots (Half Day A and B) and one day on Yeongdo and Songdo. On the old downtown day, avoid Sundays and the Tuesdays when Jagalchi Market is closed, and on the Yeongdo·Songdo day, avoid Mondays and the cable car maintenance days.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Not knowing the two markets' hours and closing days — Jagalchi Market is open 05:00–22:00 and closed on the first, third and fifth Tuesdays. Gukje Market is open 09:00–20:00 and closed on Sundays. To combine the two markets in one day, pick a day that leaves out Sundays and those Tuesdays. To see a market at night, go to Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market, which is open daily 19:30–24:00.
  2. Using the same station and exit for every spot — By Kakao Map directions, Jagalchi Market is Nampo Station Exit 2, Gukje Market and BIFF Square are Jagalchi Station Exit 7, Bupyeong Kkangtong Market is Jagalchi Station Exit 3, and Yongdusan Park is Jungang Station Exit 1. The bus to Songdo is taken from Jagalchi Station Exit 2, and the bus to Huinnyeoul Culture Village from Nampo Station Exit 6.
  3. Planning the Yeongdo·Songdo trip on a Monday — The Danubi Train at Taejongdae is closed every Monday and ends at 17:30 in November. The Songdo Marine Cable Car is closed for maintenance on 2026-11-30 to 12-01. Taejongdae takes about 30 minutes by buses 8, 17, 30, 88 and 186 from opposite Lotte Department Store in Nampo-dong.

⚙ 3 FAQs

  1. Which places in the table charge a fare and which are free? — The places that charge are the Songdo Marine Cable Car (adult Air Cruise round trip 19,000 won · one way 15,000 won, Crystal Cruise round trip 24,000 won · one way 19,000 won), Busan Tower and the Danubi Train at Taejongdae. The places that are free are Yongdusan Park, Gwangbok-ro, admission to the Taejongdae resort, Songdo Beach and the Songdo Cloud Trails. The cable car fares are values seen on the operator's website on 2026-11-24.
  2. How do I get from Gimhae Airport to Jagalchi·Nampo-dong? — The airport limousine has no route to Nampo or Jagalchi. The limousine has two branches: the Haeundae·Gijang direction (9,500 won) and the Seomyeon·Bujeon direction (6,000 won). As the Korea Airports Corporation guide says, take the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail Transit and transfer to the subway at Sasang Station (Line 2) or Daejeo Station (Line 3).
  3. Which places are open late at night? — Among the markets, Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market is the latest, daily 19:30–24:00. Yongdusan Park is 24 hours by the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-24), and Gwangbok-ro is always open. Songdo Beach and the Songdo Cloud Trails are open until 23:00, Busan Tower until 22:00 (entry closes 21:30), Jagalchi Market until 22:00, and the Songdo Marine Cable Car until 21:00 (as shown on 2026-11-24, with separate winter hours from December). Gukje Market ends at 20:00, and the Danubi Train ends at 17:30 in November. The Taejongdae shoreline restricts access from 20:00 by the Kakao Map registered value.
