Busan = Korea's #1 Seafood Market City + Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장) (Korea's #1 seafood market) + Gukje Market (국제시장) (Korea's #1 import-export market established in 1948) + BIFF Square (BIFF 거리) (for movie enthusiasts) + Nampo-dong (남포동) and Yongdusan Park (용두산공원) integrated. ⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Jagalchi = Just a regular market": Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장) = Korea's #1 seafood market + top pick for foreigner vlogs + early morning auction (새벽 경매) (05:00~07:00 / 5-7 AM) + premier spot for traditional raw fish (회) and seafood. Busan's #1 spot for a deep dive into traditional culture.

  • Hoe (회) is raw fish or sashimi, a popular Korean dish often served with various side dishes. Saebyeok Gyeongmae (새벽 경매) is an early morning auction where fresh seafood is traded.

2. "Gukje Market = Only from the movie": Gukje Market (국제시장) (established 1948) = Korea's #1 traditional import market + setting for the movie "Ode to My Father" (국제시장) + top pick for foreigners + integrated with Kkangtong Market (깡통시장) and Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market (부평 깡통야시장).

3. "Busan Hoe = All the same everywhere": Traditional hoe (회) at Jagalchi (자갈치) = Top pick for foreigner vlog enthusiasts + first-generation traditional hoe (회) + Korea's #1 for freshness. ₩50,000~150,000 per person (~$37-110 / ~260-780 RMB / ~5,500-16,500 JPY).

4. "Taejongdae = Too hard to get to": Taejongdae (태종대) = Busan's #1 natural attraction + cliffs and "Moon-Rising Spot" (달뜨는 자리) + top pick for foreigner enthusiasts + integrated with Jagalchi (자갈치) and Yeongdo (영도).

5. "Yongdusan Park = Only for seniors": Yongdusan Park (용두산공원) + Busan Tower (부산타워) 120m (394 ft) 360° views = #1 spot for foreigner Instagrammers + integrated with Jagalchi (자갈치) and BIFF (BIFF) routes.

④ Food & Enthusiasts (3 + 4 Extra)

10. Jagalchi Hoe (회) & Hoe-jip (횟집) (Traditional)

  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Deep dive into Korea's #1 traditional raw fish (회)
  • Hoe (회) is raw fish or sashimi, and Hoe-jip (횟집) is a restaurant specializing in raw fish.

11. Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market (부평 깡통 야시장) + Milmyeon (밀면)

  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Busan's traditional milmyeon (밀면) (first-generation Busan's #1)
  • Milmyeon (밀면) is a local Busan specialty, a refreshing wheat noodle dish typically served in a cold broth.

12. Nampo-dong Dwaeji Gukbap (돼지국밥)

  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Busan's traditional dwaeji gukbap (돼지국밥) (premier spot for foreigner enthusiasts)
  • Dwaeji Gukbap (돼지국밥) is a hearty and comforting Busan-style pork soup with rice, often served with various condiments.

2. Foreigner's #1 Pick for Jagalchi Hoe (회): Traditional hoe (회) & hoe-jip (횟집) at Jagalchi (자갈치) (₩50,000~150,000 per person) + saebyeok gyeongmae (새벽 경매) enthusiasts.

3. Foreigner's Gukje Market (국제시장) Enthusiast Spot: Setting for the 1948 movie + integrated with Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market (부평 깡통야시장).

A. Jagalchi & Gukje Market Day Trip (Busan's Premier Traditional Experience for Foreigners)

  • 09:00 Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장) + Saebyeok Gyeongmae (새벽 경매) → 12:00 Jagalchi hoe (회) & hoe-jip (횟집) → 15:00 Gukje Market (국제시장) & Kkangtong Market (깡통시장) → 17:00 Yongdusan Park (용두산공원) & Busan Tower (부산타워) → 19:00 BIFF Square (BIFF 거리) + Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market (부평 깡통야시장)

B. Yeongdo & Taejongdae Day Trip (Nature & Instagram for Foreigners)

  • 10:00 Yeongdo Huinnyeoul Culture Village (영도 흰여울 문화마을) → 13:00 Lunch in Yeongdo (영도) → 15:00 Taejongdae (태종대) (Cliffs & "Moon-Rising Spot" (달뜨는 자리)) → 17:00 Songdo Cable Car (송도 케이블카) & Songdo Beach (송도해수욕장)

C. Busan 2-Day 1-Night Full Set (Jagalchi + Haeundae)

  • Day 1: Haeundae (해운대) ([#243 Busan Haeundae](243_busan_haeundae_guide.md)) → Gwangalli (광안리) Night View
  • Day 2: Jagalchi (자갈치), Gukje Market (국제시장), Taejongdae (태종대), Yeongdo (영도)
