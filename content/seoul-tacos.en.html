Seoul Mexican/Taco Restaurant Guide

> From beginner to birria — authentic curation of 12 places suitable for foreigners

Seoul's Mexican and taco scene no longer resides in "Itaewon foreign restaurants." From authentic NYC restaurants to Jongno alley taquerias, Hannam birria restaurants, and Seongsu and Hapjeong dining bars — we curated 12 places in 5 categories where foreign segment personas can go “when they want to meet real Mexican food in Korea, too.”

The original pool of 50 locations came from the five series of Naver Clipbook Curator Taco Expedition, and this guide is the result of selecting 12 locations along three axes: (a) accessibility to foreigners (Gangnam, Itaewon, Hannam, Hapjeong, Seongsu), (b) English menu availability, and (c) category representativeness. Stores that appeared repeatedly in the three series emphasized their reliability with a "★" sign.

1. Introduction — Authentic Mexican for the first time (3 locations)

It is suitable for foreigner personas who have just arrived in Korea from the United States or Mexico, or who are trying authentic tacos in Korea for the first time. The menu composition is standard and the location is in a foreigner-friendly area (Gangnam, Songpa, Euljiro).

The Taco Booth NYC Apgujeong Branch

  • Address: Rooms 103 and 104, 1st floor, 647-13 Sinsa-dong, Gangnam-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: NYC-Style Authentic Tacos — Carne Asada, Al Pastor
  • Recommended Points: Same as the original NYC concept. Most likely to respond in English. Introduction + Mexican Rice Soup Series Appears 2 times★

Conmigo Songridan-gil Branch

  • Address: 1st floor, 51-8 Songpa-dong, Songpa-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Songridan-gil Trendy Mexican — Burrito, Quesadilla
  • Recommended Point: Can be combined with Jamsil Olympic Park and Lotte World route. dating mood

Oldy Starco Euljiro Main Branch

  • Address: 1st floor, 17-4 Cho-dong, Jung-gu, Seoul (Hanshin Building)
  • Signature: Euljiro Alley Casual Taco
  • Recommended point: Finish after taking a walk around the alley of Euljiro Nogari and Beer Pub. Good for foreigners to experience the “Hidden Seoul” atmosphere

2. Authentic Mexican — “Foundation” acknowledged by Mexicans (3 places)

For the local Mexican or LA authentic Mexican foodie persona. This store is a favorite among those looking for authentic Mexican taste rather than Koreanized fusion.

Taco Stand

  • Address: 45-9 Yongsan-dong 2-ga, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Authentic Carnitas/Al Pastor
  • Recommended Points: Mexican Rice Soup + Root + Offal Fish Appears 3 times ★★★ (Most duplicates out of 5 series = reputation anchor)

Taco La Mexicana

  • Address: Room B124, 1st basement level, SK Bukhansan City shopping center, 1353-5 Mia-dong, Gangbuk-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Local, authentic Mexican food
  • Recommended Points: Mexican Rice Soup + Fundamental Appears 2 times ★★. Gangbuk Mia — High value for “Local Discovery” as it is an area that foreigners rarely go to

Quere

  • Address: 1st floor, 1136 Sungin-dong, Jongno-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Mexico Date Store in Sungin-dong, Jongno
  • Recommended Points: Mexican Rice Soup + Tripe Fish Appears 2 times ★★. Combined with Dongdaemun and Jongno night view routes

3. Birria, tripe, fish — Mexican food a step further (3 locations)

For the persona of an enthusiast who goes beyond tacos and tries birria (beef stew), fish tacos, and giblet tacos. Gathered in Hannam and Itaewon foreign hubs and Dongdaemun locals.

Tac

  • Address: Ground floor, 683-63 Hannam-dong, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Authentic tacos and birria in Hannam-dong
  • Recommended points: Tripe fish + drinking atmosphere Appears twice ★★. Hannam foreign hub + atmosphere ↑

Jupaca Birria

  • Address: Basement 1, 729-31 Hannam-dong, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Birria taco specialty (quesadilla dipped in beef stew)
  • Recommended Point: Biria Signature. A rare store where you can find LA birria scene in Korea.

Pescaderia

  • Address: 1st floor, 486-29 Jegi-dong, Dongdaemun-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Fish Tacos — Baja California Style
  • Recommended Point: Specializing in fish tacos. Jegi-dong — Can be combined with Dongdaemun Market route

4. Drinks, Atmosphere, Date — Mexican Dining Bar (3 locations)

For a dinner date or a dining persona who enjoys Mexican food + mezcal, margaritas, and wine together. They are gathered in Itaewon, Hannam, and Hapjeong and have the highest dating suitability for foreigners.

Southside Parlor

  • Address: 1st and 4th floors, 527 Itaewon-dong, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Itaewon BAR concept Mexican dining
  • Recommended Point: The core of nightlife for foreigners in Itaewon. 1st floor bar + 4th floor dining duplex

Escondido

  • Address: Basement 1, 32-48 Hannam-dong, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Hannam Mexican Dining + Cocktails
  • Recommended Point: Hannam Dating Course. Style classification (Mexican/Latin fusion)

Ixchel Hapjeong Main Branch (Ixchel)

  • Address: 1st floor, 368-11 Hapjeong-dong, Mapo-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Hapjeong Mexican + Wine Pairing
  • Recommended point: Hapjeong·Hongdae route. Casual dining paired with wine

5. Foreign fit tips

English Menu Availability Store

  • Top: The Taco Booth NYC, Southside Parlor, Escondido, Tark (Hannam, Itaewon, Gangnam area, accustomed to serving foreigners)
  • Middle class: Ixchel Hapjeong, Conmigo Songridan-gil (adjacent to tourist attractions, English menu may be available)
  • Local: Taco La Mexicana, Quere, Pescaderia (Korean mainly, translation app recommended)

Recommended routes for each region

  • Gangnam·Apgujeong: The Taco Booth NYC + (Walking around Dosan Park)
  • Itaewon·Hannam: Tak → Jupaka Birria → Southside Parlor (evening hop)
  • Hapjeong·Hongdae: Ixchel Hapjeong + (Hongdae indie music nightlife)
  • Eulji-ro·Jongno: Old Distco → Gyore (Hidden Seoul walking course)
  • Local Exploration: Taco La Mexicana (Gangbuk) + Pescaderia (Dongdaemun) — An area rarely visited by foreigners

Special instructions for Latin segment personas

  • From Mexico/LA: Taco Stand, Taco La Mexicana, and Zupaca Birria (Orthodox Fundamental Cluster)
  • From the Eastern United States: The Taco Booth NYC (Authentic NYC concept)
  • Introductory from Europe and Asia: Conmigo Songridan-gil / Oldestaco Eulji-ro (casual introduction)

Price range/reservation guide (general)

  • Casual Lunch: 1 taco 6,000~9,000 won, set 18,000~25,000 won
  • Dining Dinner: Course 35,000~60,000 won (Southside Parlor, Escondido, Ixchel)
  • Reservation: Advance reservations for weekend evening dining in Hannam and Itaewon are recommended (Naver Reservation or catchtable)
