🎯 How to Use This Guide

At the Olive Young register, the cashier asks "Tax refund?" and asks for your passport. Is the discount on the spot the end of it, or do you need to go to a downtown refund booth, or stand in line again at the airport? With three routes, it gets confusing. This guide puts the conditions, limits, and payout timing of the three routes in one table so you can pick the route that fits your shopping. The airport kiosk screen sequence (#1304), the arrival and Customs checklist (#434), and tax refunds on electronics (#1413) each have a dedicated guide, so pick your route here and move on.

The figures in this guide are from the official pages of the law (Special Provisions Decree on VAT and Individual Consumption Tax for Foreign Tourists (외국인관광객 등에 대한 부가가치세 및 개별소비세 특례규정)), Korea Tourism Organization (한국관광공사), Incheon Airport (인천공항), Incheon Main Customs (인천본부세관), and Korea Customs Service (관세청), as seen on 2026-11-05. Fees by operator are not listed in this guide.

🧭 At a Glance — The Three Routes

Route Conditions · limit When and where you get the money What to do on departure
Route 1 · Instant refund at the store A single payment under 1,000,000 won including tax, and a total of 5,000,000 won or less in instant refunds after arrival (Decree Article 6(2)) You pay the price with tax already taken off at the register (immediately) Nothing — however, if any goods bought with a tax refund were not taken out within 3 months, later instant refunds are blocked (Article 6(4))
Route 2 · Downtown refund booth 6,000,000 won or less per receipt, bring a credit card issued overseas, only slips from that booth's operator are processed (Korea Tourism Organization) Cash, or card, Alipay, or WeChat (in advance), at a downtown booth Declare the refund and present the slip at Customs or the designated airport refund counter (Korea Tourism Organization)
Route 3 · Airport / port refund No purchase limit, slips from all operators are processed (Korea Tourism Organization) Card, Alipay, or WeChat refunds come 1~2 months later via kiosk or counter; cash is paid at the duty-free-zone counter after security (Korea Tourism Organization) Before checking your bags, use the kiosk → if it shows "Customs check required," bring the goods, slip, and passport to the Customs desk (Incheon Airport)

All three routes start with the common conditions (① below). If a single payment is 1,000,000 won or more, instant refund is not available, so get a post-purchase refund slip and process it at a downtown booth or the airport.

① Common Conditions — Who, From How Much, By When, in What Condition

  • Who: Foreigners staying in Korea under 6 months. Korean nationals who have lived abroad for more than 2 years or hold permanent residency in another country qualify when staying under 3 months (Korea Tourism Organization).
  • From how much: 15,000 won or more on a single receipt at a store with a 'Tax Free' or 'Tax Refund' sign (Incheon Airport · Korea Tourism Organization). Receipts can't be split, and receipts under 15,000 won don't qualify on any route.
  • By when: Take the goods out of the country within 3 months of the purchase date (Decree Article 6(1) · Korea Tourism Organization).
  • In what condition: Unopened and unused. If Customs asks to see the goods, you must show them unopened (Incheon Airport).
  • Passport: On all three routes, you show your passport at purchase. If the refund slip for downtown or airport refunds is missing the passport number, English name, nationality, or payment method, you can't get the Customs export confirmation (Incheon Main Customs) — check those four fields when you receive it.

② Route 1 — Instant Refund at the Store: It's Done at the Register

  • There are two conditions. A single transaction under 1,000,000 won including tax, and a total of 5,000,000 won or less in goods bought with instant refund since arrival (Decree Article 6(2), amended 2023-12-26). Hand over your passport and you pay the price with tax taken off, with no slip to keep track of.
  • The money you get back is not the full VAT. It is the amount after deducting the fee approved by the Commissioner of the National Tax Service for the refund operator (Article 6(3)). The rate differs by operator, so check the expected refund amount on the payment screen or receipt before paying.
  • If one purchase is 1,000,000 won or more, or the total goes over 5,000,000 won, you're not shut out — the method switches to a post-purchase refund (downtown booth · airport). For the register scene, see #1304 ②.
  • One trap: If any goods bought for a tax refund (instant or post-purchase) were not taken out within 3 months, instant refund does not apply to goods you buy after that (Article 6(4)). People who stay long and shop often get caught by this.

③ Route 2 — Downtown Refund Booth: Get the Money First, Declare at the Airport

  • A downtown refund booth refunds in advance up to 6,000,000 won per receipt. Bring the goods, the refund slip, your passport, and a credit card issued overseas; you get paid in cash, or by card, Alipay, or WeChat (Korea Tourism Organization).
  • A downtown booth processes only slips from that booth's operator. Check the operator's name at the top of the slip and find a booth of the same operator. Airport and port booths accept slips from all operators (Korea Tourism Organization).
  • Because you get the money first, on departure you declare the refund at Customs or the designated airport refund counter and present the slip (Korea Tourism Organization). Downtown refund works by putting up an amount equal to the tax as security and receiving the refund first (summarized from a Korea Customs Service announcement by Tax Times (택스타임스), 2026-04-03). Arrive at the airport with time to spare and declare at Customs or the designated refund counter.

④ Route 3 — Airport / Port Refund: No Limit, and the Order Is Everything

  • No limit. You get the refund at a refund counter or kiosk with no cap on what you bought during your stay (Korea Tourism Organization). Laptops and high-end phones over 1,000,000 won mostly go this route (#1413 ①).
  • The order (Incheon Airport): Carry the goods and documents instead of checking them in your luggage → scan your passport at the kiosk in the departure hall → if the kiosk marks "Customs check required," submit your passport, the unopened goods, and the slip at the Customs desk → get your refund (Incheon Airport). If the item has to be checked, tell the airline counter in advance and get a tag for the Customs check. For the screen steps, see #1304.
  • If you check your bag before the Customs check, you can't get the refund (Incheon Main Customs). This one line matters most.
  • How you get paid: Overseas-issued credit cards, Alipay, and WeChat are paid automatically within 1~2 months; cash is paid at the duty-free-zone counter after you pass security (Korea Tourism Organization). The kiosk scans your passport and processes right away, and for large amounts or if you don't want cash, you receive it on a card in your own name (Incheon Airport).
  • Incheon Airport counters and kiosks (Incheon Airport official): T1 refund counter near Gate 28 in the 3rd-floor duty-free zone, 07:00~22:00 (032-743-1009); T2 counter near Gate 253 on the 3rd floor, 07:00~21:30 (032-743-2732). Kiosks run 24 hours (T1 near Gate 28 and next to the pharmacy in the center of the 3rd floor of the concourse; T2 across from Gate 250 and near Gate 253). Airport customer center 1577-2600.
  • Ports (cruise) are also a "no limit, all operators" route, same as the airport (Korea Tourism Organization). Counter hours at Gimpo, Gimhae, and Jeju are not listed in this guide — check that airport's notice before you leave.

⑤ When You Can't Get a Refund — Check One Line at a Time

  1. A single receipt under 15,000 won (Incheon Airport · Korea Tourism Organization).
  2. Staying in Korea 6 months or more (Korea Tourism Organization).
  3. Leaving after 3 months from the purchase date (Decree Article 6(1)).
  4. Goods that were opened or used (Incheon Airport).
  5. Goods checked in your luggage before the Customs check (Incheon Main Customs).
  6. A slip missing the passport number, English name, nationality, or payment method (Incheon Main Customs).
  7. Goods bought at a duty-free shop (pre-purchase tax exemption) — the tax was already taken off, so they aren't eligible for a refund (Korea Tourism Organization). For how to choose a duty-free shop, see #915; for airport pickup, see #1332.
  8. Purchases where you didn't request the refund documents (slip) at the store — without the documents, you can't get a refund (Incheon Airport).

⑥ What Changed in 2026

  • Cruise tourists can also use instant refund at the store and downtown refund starting 2026-04-06 (Korea Customs Service announcement 2026-04-03, reported by Tax Times). Before that, it was hard to use these two routes.
  • The instant refund limits (under 1,000,000 won · 5,000,000 won or less) are the values in Article 6(2) of the current Decree (in force 2026-01-02) (provision amended 2023-12-26).

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

  1. "They refund everything at the airport" — If you check your bag before the Customs check, it's over. Carry the goods, and if you must check them, tell the counter first (④).
  2. "I got paid downtown, so I can skip the airport" — With a downtown refund, you still have to declare at Customs or the designated refund counter on departure (③).
  3. "It's an instant refund store, so any amount works" — It's under 1,000,000 won per purchase and 5,000,000 won in total. Over that, it switches to a post-purchase refund (②).
  4. "Two receipts add up to 15,000 won" — The standard is a single receipt (①).

⚙ FAQ 4

Q1. How much do I get back?

For instant refund, you get the tax amount minus the deduction the refund operator takes with the Commissioner of the National Tax Service's approval (Decree Article 6(3)). The deduction differs by operator, so check the expected refund amount on the payment screen or slip. A table of fees by operator is not included in this guide.

Q2. Do I have to scan items bought with instant refund again at the airport?

No. You already paid the price with tax taken off, so there's nothing more to process at the airport (#1304 ②). However, if any goods are not taken out within 3 months, later instant refunds are blocked (②).

Q3. What if I lost my slip?

Ask the store and the refund operator named at the top of the slip — for refund inquiries, the refund counter operator printed on the slip is the contact (Incheon Main Customs). Reissue conditions differ by operator and are not listed in this guide.

Q4. Where do I ask about the system itself?

Korea Customs Service (관세청) Customer Support Center 125, weekdays 09:00~18:00, from overseas +82-2-3438-5199 (Korea Customs Service). Inside Incheon Airport, customer center 1577-2600; for tourism in general, 1330 (#1299).
