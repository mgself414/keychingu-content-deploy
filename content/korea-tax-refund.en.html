🎯 Why a "Tax Refund" Guide

Korea tax refund = a system that returns the VAT (10%) foreigners pay on shopping. The biggest foreigner misconception = "they refund it all automatically." In reality you avoid losses only by knowing the immediate/post-purchase distinction·minimum amount·passport·customs check·kiosk steps. Pain points = (a) immediate refund vs airport post-purchase refund (b) minimum purchase·eligible stores (c) customs stamp·goods check (d) refund method (cash/card/app). "Korea tax refund / VAT refund airport" is searched constantly on Reddit·YouTube.

This guide covers 12 tax-refund signatures + immediate vs post-purchase + minimum·customs + airport kiosk + refund-rate matrix.

✈️ Refund Steps (foreigner practical)

  • At purchase: tax-free store·show passport·confirm ₩15,000+·get a refund slip (post-purchase) or immediate refund
  • At the airport: customs check (stamp/e-scan) → refund kiosk/counter → cash/card refund
  • Allow time: 2~3 hours before departure·customs+refund lines·carry the goods (confirm before check-in)
  • Caution: downtown pre-refund charges your card if customs isn't confirmed on departure·keep unopened
  • Emergency: ☎ 1330 (tourism)·125 (Customs Service)

⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "It's refunded automatically": Tax refund needs passport·customs check·kiosk steps.

2. "Any store works": Only tax-free-logo·₩15,000+ stores·some small businesses excluded.

3. "It's all done at the airport": Customs check first·carry goods·allow time.

4. "Downtown refund is final": Downtown pre-refund charges your card if customs isn't confirmed on departure.

5. "Refund after checking bags": Goods may be inspected, so do customs before check-in.

⚙ FAQ (6)

1. From how much?: ₩15,000+ per store·tax-free stores.

2. How much back?: VAT 10% − fee = ~7-8%.

3. What's immediate refund?: VAT deducted at the store·small-amount limits.

4. Airport steps?: Customs check (stamp/scan) → kiosk/counter → cash/card.

5. Downtown refund?: Myeongdong etc. pre-refund·departure customs check required (charged if not confirmed).

6. Recommended personas: Small = immediate / large = airport post-purchase / shoppers = downtown+airport.

⚠ Medical·Safety·Spending Disclaimer

Keep refund slips·passport. Downtown pre-refund requires departure customs check (charged if missed). Keep goods unopened. Do customs before checked baggage. Check operator·fees. Beware fraudulent refund pitches. Emergency ☎ 1330·125 (Customs)·112.
