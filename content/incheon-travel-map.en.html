⚠ 3 Incheon mistakes

1. "Incheon = a place where only the airport passes through" — Non-use of transfer tours and integrated resorts: Only transfers are recognized. In reality, it is free transfer tour (4·6·9h, city, traditional culture, nature, culture, and religion 5 courses) + Paradise City·Inspire Complex Resort signature full experience.

2. "Chinatown = only Chinese food" — Goblin K-drama·Unrecognized modern architecture: Recognizes only food. In reality, it is the last filming location of ‘Goblin’ + modern architecture and Jayu Park opened in 1883.

3. "Not knowing about Ganghwa Island" — Unrecognized UNESCO Dolmen and Mt. Manisan: Recognizing the absence of Ganghwa Island. In reality, UNESCO Dolmen (2000) + Manisan (Korea #1 cluster) + Jeondeungsa Ganghwa full experience.
