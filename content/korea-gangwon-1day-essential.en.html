🎯 Why a Gangwon-do Guide?

Gangwon-do (강원도) = Korea's #1 Nature Province · East Sea + Seoraksan (설악산) + Pyeongchang (평창) (2018 Winter Olympics) · Gangneung (강릉) = Korea's #1 Coffee City · Anmok Beach (안목해변). Foreigner pain points = (a) "Gangwon-do is far" (a misconception) (b) KTX Gangneung (강릉) = 1 hour 56 minutes from Seoul (day trip OK) (c) Gangneung (강릉) = Korea's #1 Coffee City · Anmok Beach (안목해변) + Gyeongpodae (경포대) + Jeongdongjin (정동진) (d) Sokcho (속초) = Seoraksan (설악산) 1708m · UNESCO Candidate · East Sea Beach (e) Pyeongchang (평창) = 2018 Winter Olympics · Daegwallyeong (대관령) · Sheep Farm (양떼목장) (f) Donghae (동해) + Samcheok (삼척) = Jukseoru (죽서루) · Marine Gold Mine.

This guide covers 4 axes, 12 signatures + 1-day full day + English phrases for foreigners · transportation.

🏛 4-Axis Deep Dive

① Gangneung (강릉) (Korea's #1 Coffee City)

  • Signature: Anmok Beach (안목해변) = Korea's #1 Coffee Street (40+ cafes) · Gyeongpodae (경포대) + Jeongdongjin (정동진) + Chodang Tofu (초당두부)
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (English · MZ + Instagram-fit)
  • Recommendation: Anmok Beach (안목해변) Cafe + Gyeongpodae (경포대) + Chodang Tofu (초당두부) = 4-5 hours

② Sokcho (속초) + Seoraksan (설악산) (1708m · UNESCO Candidate)

  • Signature: Seoraksan (설악산) = 1708m · National Park · UNESCO Candidate · Sokcho Beach (속초 해변) + Oeongchi (외옹치)
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (English · hiking + beach enthusiast)
  • Recommendation: Seoraksan (설악산) + Sokcho Market (속초 시장) + Oeongchi (외옹치) = 1 night recommended

③ Pyeongchang (평창) (2018 Winter Olympics)

  • Signature: 2018 Pyeongchang (평창) Winter Olympics · Daegwallyeong (대관령) · Yangtte Mokjang (양떼목장) · Memil (메밀)
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (English · family + enthusiast)
  • Recommendation: Yangtte Mokjang (양떼목장) + Daegwallyeong (대관령) + Memil (메밀) · Makguksu (막국수) = 3-4 hours

④ Donghae (동해) + Samcheok (삼척) (Beach + Gwandongpalgyeong)

  • Signature: Samcheok Jukseoru (죽서루) (1266 AD · Gwandongpalgyeong #1) · Donghae Mukho Port (묵호항) · Marine Gold Mine · Cave
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★ (Enthusiast Matrix)

⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "Gangwon-do (강원도) is far": KTX Gangneung (강릉) 1 hour 56 minutes · Day trip OK · 1 night recommended.

2. "Gangneung (강릉) is just a normal city": Korea's #1 Coffee City · Anmok Beach (안목해변) 40+ cafes.

3. "Seoraksan (설악산) is just a normal mountain": 1708m · UNESCO Candidate · Foreigner hiking destination.

4. "Pyeongchang (평창) is only for the Olympics": 2018 + Daegwallyeong (대관령) · Yangtte Mokjang (양떼목장) · Memil (메밀) · Year-round destination.

5. "Donghae (동해) · Samcheok (삼척) are unknown": Samcheok Jukseoru (죽서루) 1266 AD · Gwandongpalgyeong (관동팔경) #1.
