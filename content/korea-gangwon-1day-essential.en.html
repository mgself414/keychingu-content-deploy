🏛 4-axis deep dive

① Gangneung (Korea #1 Coffee City)

  • Signature: Anmok Beach = Korea #1 Coffee Street (40+ Cafe)·Gyeongpodae + Jeongdongjin + Chodang Tofu
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ (English, MZ + Instagram fit)
  • Recommended: Anmok Beach Cafe + Gyeongpodae + Chodang Tofu = 4-5 hours

② Sokcho + Mt. Seorak (1708m, UNESCO candidate)

  • Signature: Seoraksan Mountain = 1708m, National Park, UNESCO Candidate, Sokcho Beach + Oeongchi
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ (English, hiking + beach enthusiast)
  • Recommendation: Seorak Mountain + Sokcho Market + Oeongchi = 1 night recommended

③ Pyeongchang (2018 Winter Olympics)

  • Signature: 2018 Pyeongchang Winter Olympics, Daegwallyeong, Sheep Farm, Buckwheat
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (English, Family + Enthusiast)
  • Recommended: Sheep Ranch + Daegwallyeong + Buckwheat and Makguksu = 3-4 hours

④ East Sea + Samcheok (Beach + Eight Scenic Views of Gwandong)

  • Signature: Samcheok Jukseoru (1266, Eight Scenic Views of the Gwandong #1), East Sea Mukho Port, Marine Gold Mine, Cave
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★ (Mania Matrix)
