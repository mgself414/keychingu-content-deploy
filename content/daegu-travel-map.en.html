⚠ 3 Daegu mistakes

1. "Daegu = resembles Seoul" — Unaware of Yangnyeongsi and Modern Alley: Daegu is perceived as simply a city center. In reality, Yangnyeongsi (350 years of Oriental medicine) + Modern Alley (1907 national debt redemption) is Daegu’s signature signature.

2. "Gourmet food = only chicken and beer" — Makchang and flat dumplings are not recognized: Only chicken and beer is recognized. In reality, Makchang, ribs, flat dumplings, ttaro gukbap, mungtigi Daegu full experience.

3. "Day Trip" — Palgongsan Gatbawi Rock: Recognizes only the city center. In reality, Palgongsan Gatbawi Rock (Korea #1 Sowon) + 1 night recommended for Donghwasa Temple only.
