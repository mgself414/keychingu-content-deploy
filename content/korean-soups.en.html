🎯 Why a guide to Korean soups, stews, and stews?

The core of Korean meals is soup, soup, and stew — kimchi stew, soybean paste stew, galbitang, samgyetang, seolleongtang, budae stew, hangover soup, and spicy stew are Korea's No. 1 soup foods. 1st place for foreigners to deep dive into Korean food (most frequently attempted by foreigners other than vlogger BBQ).

⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Spicy = Kimchi stew, Budae stew": In addition to the spicy taste, Soybean paste stew, galbitang, seolleongtang, samgyetang Mild soup is also the best in Korea.

2. "Samgyetang = winter food": Samgyetang is the #1 summer health food (Korean Sambok — Chobok, Jungbok, Malbok July to August).

3. "Budae-jjigae = Korean Authentic": Budae-jjigae is a food for U.S. military units after the Korean War (1950s) + a Korean variation. 1 Dongducheon and Uijeongbu.

① Kimchi Stew ★★

  • Signature: All Korean restaurants in Korea + Baekban Set Meal
  • Price: 7,000~12,000 won
  • How to eat: Kimchi + pork/tuna + tofu + rice + pickled radish
  • Foreign demand: No. 1 food in K-drama
  • Why: Korea’s No. 1 home-cooked meal.

② Doenjang Stew

  • Signature: Authentic Korean restaurant
  • Price: 7,000~12,000 won
  • How to eat: Soybean paste + tofu + vegetables + rice
  • Why: Authentic Korean fermented soybean food.

③ Galbitang

  • Signature: Korean Galbi House + Baekban
  • Price: 12,000~18,000 won
  • How to eat: Galbi + radish + noodles + kimchi
  • Why: Authentic Korean rib soup.

④ Samgyetang ★ No. 1 summer health food

  • Signature: Tosokchon Samgyetang (Gwanghwamun)·Korea Samgyetang (Bukchang-dong)
  • Price: 18,000~25,000 won
  • How to eat: 1 chicken + ginseng, glutinous rice, jujube + garlic
  • Foreign demand: BBC·CNN regular + 1st place in summer July-August
  • Why: Korea’s No. 1 summer health food. The #1 Korean food deep dive for foreigners.

⑤ Seolleongtang ★ Korea’s No. 1 Beef Bone

  • Signature: Imunseolnongtang (Jongno, Korea’s No. 1 Nopo 1904)
  • Price: 12,000~18,000 won
  • How to eat: White beef bone + noodles, rice + green onions, salt, pepper, your own choice
  • Why: Korea’s No. 1 beef bone broth restaurant (Imunseolnongtang = one of Korea’s oldest restaurants).

⑥ Budae Jjigae, Army Stew ★★★

  • Signature: Nolbu Budae Jjigae·Dongducheon·Uijeongbu Authentic
  • Price: 12,000~18,000 won/person
  • How to eat: Ham, sausage, cheese, ramen, kimchi, beans + spicy soup
  • Foreign demand: K-drama No. 1 + foreigner-friendly ham and cheese combination
  • Why: After the Korean War (1950s) US military units + Korean style modification → Korean orthodoxy.

⑦ Haejangguk (see #198 Challenge Food)

⑧ Maeuntang ★★★★

  • Signature: Noryangjin Fish Market (#181 Dongjak·Noryangjin) Instant spicy spicy stew
  • Price: 15,000~30,000 won/person
  • How to eat: Instant raw fish + maeuntang (cod, croaker, monkfish, rockfish)
  • Why: Authentic Korean instant raw fish + spicy soup. Noryangjin Fish Market No. 1.

⑨ Sundubu Jjigae ★★

  • Signature: Bukchang-dong Soft Tofu·Authentic Korean restaurant
  • Price: 9,000~13,000 won
  • How to eat: Soft tofu + spicy seasoning + egg + rice
  • Why: Authentic Korean tofu food (#175 Magog·Maetdolman sisters).

⑩ Yukgaejang ★★★

  • Signature: All Korean restaurants in Korea
  • Price: 10,000~14,000 won
  • How to eat: Spicy beef broth + bracken, green onion, and red pepper oil
  • Why: Korea’s No. 1 spicy beef soup. The #1 spicy challenge for foreigners.

⑪ Tteokguk (Tteokguk)

  • Signature: Lunar New Year home meal + authentic Korean restaurant
  • Price: 9,000~13,000 won
  • How to eat: Rice cake + beef broth + seaweed and egg
  • Why: Korean New Year’s Day (January 1, Lunar Calendar) No. 1 food — One bowl = One year older.

⑫ Kongnamul Gukbap ★ Authentic Jeonju style

  • Signature: Jeonju-style bean sprout soup + Jongno Cheongjin-dong 24 hours
  • Price: 7,000~10,000 won
  • How to eat: Bean sprouts + eggs + rice + seaweed
  • Foreign demand: Open 24 hours + 1st place for foreigners to drink hangovers
  • Why: Authentic Jeonju style + 24-hour hangover.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Samgyetang Foreigner-Friendly Store: Tosokchon Samgyetang (Gwanghwamun), Koryo Samgyetang (Bukchang-dong) — English menu + foreigner vlog regular.

2. Seolleongtang Old Restaurant: Imun Seolnongtang (1904) — One of Korea’s oldest old restaurants.

3. The birthplace of budae-jjigae: Dongducheon and Uijeongbu (1950s US military base + Korean style).

4. Spicy level of spicy soup: Spicy level can be adjusted at the Noryangjin Fish Market store.

5. Are foreigners allowed to eat rice cake soup?: January Lunar New Year + holiday restaurant — Free entry for foreigners.

6. Kongnamul Gukbap 24 hours a day?: Cheongjin-dong (#198 Haejangguk) + Jeonju authentic store.
