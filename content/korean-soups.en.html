Korean Soups, Broths, and Stews Deep Dive — 12 Authentic Korean Dishes for Foreigners

Subtitle: Kimchi Jjigae, Doenjang Jjigae, Galbitang, Samgyetang, Seolleongtang, Budae Jjigae, Haejangguk, Maeuntang — A Full Set of Authentic Korean Soups

ISSUE 03 · #200 · 2026-05-19

🎯 Why a Korean Soups, Broths, and Stews Guide

The core of Korean meals is Guk, Tang, and Jjigae (국·탕·찌개) — Korean categories for soups, broths, and stews. Kimchi Jjigae, Doenjang Jjigae, Galbitang, Samgyetang, Seolleongtang, Budae Jjigae, Haejangguk, and Maeuntang are Korea's top soup dishes. They are a priority for foreigners looking for a deep dive into Korean cuisine (most frequently tried by foreign vloggers, besides BBQ).

Following KeyChinGu #14 Must-Try Korean Dishes and #62 Korean Chicken Dishes, this is a full deep dive into Korean Soups, Broths, and Stews.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes for Foreigners

1. "Spicy = Kimchi Jjigae·Budae Jjigae": Besides spicy dishes, Doenjang Jjigae, Galbitang, Seolleongtang, and Samgyetang are also Korea's top mild soup dishes.

2. "Samgyetang = Winter Food": Samgyetang (삼계탕) is the #1 summer bonyangsik (보양식, health-boosting food) (Korea's Sambok (삼복, the three hottest days of summer) — Chobok, Jungbok, Malbok in July-August).

3. "Budae Jjigae = Authentic Korean": Budae Jjigae (부대찌개) originated after the Korean War (1950s) from US military base food + Korean modifications. Dongducheon (동두천) and Uijeongbu (의정부) are its primary origins.

① Kimchi Jjigae (Kimchi Stew) ★★

  • Signature: All Korean restaurants + Baekban (백반, set meal) jeongsik (정식, Korean set meal)
  • Price: ₩7,000~12,000 (~$5-9 / ~37-63 RMB / ~770-1,330 JPY)
  • How to Eat: Kimchi + pork/tuna + tofu + rice + danmuji (단무지, pickled radish)
  • Foreigner Demand: #1 K-drama food
  • Why: Korea's #1 gajeongsik (가정식, home-style meal).

② Doenjang Jjigae (Doenjang Stew)

  • Signature: Authentic Korean restaurants
  • Price: ₩7,000~12,000
  • How to Eat: Doenjang (된장, fermented soybean paste) + tofu + vegetables + rice
  • Why: Authentic Korean balhyo kong (발효 콩, fermented soybean) dish.

③ Galbitang (Galbi Broth)

  • Signature: Korean galbi (갈비, short rib) restaurants + Baekban (백반, set meal)
  • Price: ₩12,000~18,000
  • How to Eat: Galbi (갈비, short ribs) + radish + noodles + kimchi
  • Why: Authentic Korean galbi (갈비, short rib) broth.

④ Samgyetang (Ginseng Chicken Soup) ★ Summer's #1 Health-Boosting Soup

  • Signature: Tosokchon Samgyetang (토속촌삼계탕) (Gwanghwamun (광화문)) · Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕) (Bukchang-dong (북창동))
  • Price: ₩18,000~25,000
  • How to Eat: Whole chicken + ginseng, glutinous rice, jujube + garlic
  • Foreigner Demand: BBC·CNN regular + #1 in July-August summer
  • Why: Korea's #1 summer bonyangsik (보양식, health-boosting food). #1 for foreigners' deep dive into Korean cuisine.

⑤ Seolleongtang (Ox Bone Soup) ★ Korea's #1 Sagol

  • Signature: Imun Seollongtang (이문설농탕) (Jongno (종로), Korea's #1 nofo (노포, 30+ year traditional restaurant) 1904)
  • Price: ₩12,000~18,000
  • How to Eat: Milky sagol (사골, beef leg bone broth) + noodles, rice + green onions, salt, pepper (self-service)
  • Why: Korea's #1 sagol (사골, beef leg bone broth) nofo (노포, traditional restaurant) (Imun Seollongtang (이문설농탕) = one of Korea's oldest restaurants).

⑥ Budae Jjigae (Budae Jjigae, Army Stew) ★★★

  • Signature: Nolboo Budae Jjigae (놀부 부대찌개) · Authentic Dongducheon (동두천) · Uijeongbu (의정부) style
  • Price: ₩12,000~18,000/person
  • How to Eat: Ham, sausage, cheese, ramyeon (라면, instant noodles), kimchi, beans + spicy broth
  • Foreigner Demand: #1 K-drama food + familiar combination of ham and cheese for foreigners
  • Why: Originated after the Korean War (1950s) from US military bases + Korean modifications → now an authentic Korean dish.

⑧ Maeuntang (Maeuntang, Spicy Fish Stew) ★★★★

  • Signature: Instant Maeuntang (매운탕) at Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진수산시장) (#181 Dongjak·Noryangjin)
  • Price: ₩15,000~30,000/person (~$11-22 / ~79-158 RMB / ~1,660-3,330 JPY)
  • How to Eat: Instant hoe (회, sliced raw fish) + Maeuntang (매운탕) (cod, croaker, anglerfish, rockfish)
  • Why: Authentic Korean instant hoe (회, sliced raw fish) + spicy broth. #1 at Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진수산시장).

⑨ Sundubu Jjigae (Soft Tofu Stew) ★★

  • Signature: Bukchang-dong Sundubu (북창동 순두부) · Authentic Korean restaurants
  • Price: ₩9,000~13,000 (~$7-10 / ~47-68 RMB / ~1,000-1,440 JPY)
  • How to Eat: Sundubu (순두부, soft tofu) + spicy seasoning + egg + rice
  • Why: Authentic Korean tofu dish (#175 Magok·Maetdolloman sister restaurant).

⑩ Yukgaejang (Yukgaejang) ★★★

  • Signature: All Korean restaurants
  • Price: ₩10,000~14,000 (~$7-10 / ~53-74 RMB / ~1,100-1,550 JPY)
  • How to Eat: Spicy beef broth + bracken, green onions, chili oil
  • Why: Korea's #1 spicy beef soup. #1 spicy challenge for foreigners.

⑪ Tteokguk (Tteokguk)

  • Signature: Seollal (설날, Korean New Year's Day) home-style dish + Authentic Korean restaurants
  • Price: ₩9,000~13,000
  • How to Eat: Tteok (떡, rice cakes) + beef broth + gim (김, seaweed), egg
  • Why: Korea's #1 dish for Seollal (설날, Korean New Year's Day) (January 1st · Lunar New Year) — one bowl = one year older.

⑫ Kongnamul Gukbap (Kongnamul Gukbap) ★ Authentic Jeonju-style

  • Signature: Jeonju-style Kongnamul Gukbap (전주식 콩나물국밥) + Jongno Cheongjin-dong (종로 청진동) 24 hours
  • Price: ₩7,000~10,000
  • How to Eat: Kongnamul (콩나물, bean sprouts) + egg + rice + gim (김, seaweed)
  • Foreigner Demand: 24-hour operation + #1 hangover cure for foreigners after drinking
  • Why: Authentic Jeonju (전주) style + 24-hour haejang (해장, hangover cure).

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Foreigner-friendly Samgyetang (삼계탕) restaurants: Tosokchon Samgyetang (토속촌삼계탕) (Gwanghwamun (광화문)), Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕) (Bukchang-dong (북창동)) — English menus + popular with foreign vloggers.

2. Seolleongtang (설렁탕) nofo (노포): Imun Seollongtang (이문설농탕) (1904) — one of Korea's oldest nofo (노포, traditional restaurants).

3. Budae Jjigae (부대찌개) origin: Dongducheon (동두천) · Uijeongbu (의정부) (1950s US military bases + Korean style).

4. Maeuntang (매운탕) spiciness levels: Spiciness can be adjusted at Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진수산시장) restaurants.

5. Is Tteokguk (떡국) foreigner-friendly?: January Seollal (설날) + holiday restaurants — foreigners are welcome.

6. Kongnamul Gukbap (콩나물국밥) 24 hours?: Cheongjin-dong (청진동) (#198 Haejangguk (해장국)) + authentic Jeonju (전주) restaurants.
