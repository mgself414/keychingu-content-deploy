🎯 Why a Seoul Hotel Swimming Pool Full Guide?

For foreign tourists, the #1 choice for a staycation (Korean term for a hotel vacation, hokangseu (호캉스)) and Instagram-worthy shots (insaengsat (인생샷), meaning "life shot" or "perfect photo") is Seoul's 12 top 5-star hotel swimming pools. Featuring summer pools, infinity pools, Han River (Hangang (한강)) and city views, these spots are a favorite among foreign vloggers. Peak season is July-September, but indoor pools are popular year-round.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Hotel pools are for guests only": Some hotels allow Day Pass (데이패스) external use (Banyan Tree ₩50,000 (~$37 / ~260 RMB / ~5,300 JPY), Shilla ₩80,000, Conrad ₩70,000~ standard). Advance reservations are essential.

2. "Infinity pools are only at Signiel": Korea's #1 infinity pools are Banyan Tree (남산) + Signiel (555m) + JW Marriott Dongdaemun (JW메리어트 동대문) + Hotel Naru (한강) – 4 enthusiast favorites.

3. "Only open in summer": Outdoor pools operate July-September, but indoor pools are open year-round (Shilla (신라), Banyan Tree (반얀트리), Signiel (시그니엘), Conrad (콘래드), JW). Staycations are possible all year.

4. "Hotel pools are always expensive": For value seekers, L7 Hongdae (L7 홍대) (₩150,000~₩250,000/night), Novotel Dongdaemun (노보텔 동대문) (₩120,000~₩200,000/night), Mondrian (몬드리안) (₩200,000~₩300,000) include pool access and are popular with foreign visitors.

5. "Foreigner-friendly means luxury only": L7 (L7), Novotel (노보텔), Mondrian (몬드리안), Hotel Naru (호텔 나루) are foreigner-friendly + offer full English, Chinese, and Japanese services + popular with MZ Instagrammers.
