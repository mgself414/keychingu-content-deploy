CHECK iN SEOUL Card Guide — Lotte Card's Prepaid Card for Foreign Visitors: 5,000 Won Issuance Fee, 2~3% to Top Up with an Overseas Card, 1,000 Won per Balance Withdrawal

Subtitle: Apply in the app and pick up at Incheon Airport or a kiosk in the city, a prepaid card for foreign visitors from Lotte Card · Issued from age 14 with just a passport · Two types, LOCAL (500,000 won limit) and VISA (2,000,000 won limit) · Won cash with no fee, 16 foreign currencies in cash, 2~3% for overseas cards · The transit balance is topped up and refunded separately · Compared side by side with WOWPASS and T-money — checked against the Lotte Card CHECK iN SEOUL website and the App Store description

🎯 How to Use This Guide

CHECK iN SEOUL is a prepaid card and app for foreign tourists issued by Lotte Card Co., Ltd. (롯데카드 주식회사). You load money onto the card in advance, pay at shops, and ride the subway and buses with the same card. In the app you can also get discount coupons, nearby recommended places, and buy SIM, airport railroad, and airport limousine tickets.

Every number in this guide was copied from the notices on the Lotte Card (롯데카드) CHECK iN SEOUL website (checkinseoul.lottecard.co.kr) and the official Lotte Card app description on the App Store. The launch news and the number of kiosks come from October 2026 press reports, and where they do, the sentence says it is the press.

For how to choose among the three transit cards (T-money, WOWPASS, Climate Card), see #1298 Choosing Among Korea's 3 Transit Cards; to go deep on T-money alone, see #238 T-money Guide. This guide focuses on the one CHECK iN SEOUL card.

🪪 1. Getting the Card — Apply in the App and Pick Up at the Airport

There are two routes.

Applying in advance in the app

  1. Install the app, sign up, and choose a card type.
  2. When you finish applying, the app shows a card pickup QR code.
  3. After you arrive in Korea, scan the QR code and your passport at a CHECK iN SEOUL kiosk at Incheon Airport (인천공항) or in the city.
  4. Enter the amount to top up, then insert a MASTER or JCB card or put in cash, and the card comes out.

Making it on the spot at a kiosk: choose instant issuance on the kiosk, scan your passport, and top up the same way to finish the issuance. The card made on the spot at a kiosk is LOCAL (500,000 won limit). You can apply for a VISA card only after finishing passport verification in the app.

Choosing a kiosk and scanning your passport: CHECK iN SEOUL kiosks are at more than 200 places, including Lotte Department Store (롯데백화점), Lotte Mart (롯데마트), and Incheon Airport (Metro Seoul (메트로신문) report, 2026-10-12). What each kiosk can do differs, so use Find kiosks in the app and apply filters (region, open now · open 24 hours, Collect card LOCAL · VISA, cash top-up · foreign currency top-up · card top-up, KRW withdrawal). Take the cover off your passport before scanning it.

The issuance fee is 5,000 won. When you pay at a kiosk, the top-up amount and the issuance fee are calculated together, and if you pay by card, the website says a top-up fee is added separately. At events such as the 2026 Seoul spring festival (서울 봄 축제) booth, the issuance fee has been waived before.

If you bought a SIM, airport railroad, or airport limousine ticket together with the card in the app store, pick up the card at the LG U+ (LG유플러스) booth at the airport or a CHECK iN SEOUL kiosk.

LOCAL or VISA: which to choose

LOCAL card VISA card
Top-up limit 500,000 won 2,000,000 won
Applying Right away in the app or at a kiosk After finishing passport verification in the app
Switching Switching from LOCAL to VISA is fee-free (may change at Lotte Card's discretion), and the prepaid card balance stays

Steps for switching from LOCAL to VISA

  1. Refund the transit card balance to the prepaid card first. When you get a replacement card, the transit card balance disappears.
  2. Finish passport verification in the app and apply for the replacement, and a replacement QR code appears.
  3. Choose pickup at a kiosk and scan the QR, and the new card comes out. The moment you receive the new card, the old card is deactivated.

If your shopping budget is likely to go over 500,000 won, applying for VISA from the start is better. For a VISA card, top up at a kiosk (Lotte Card guidance).

💳 2. Topping Up — The Fee Depends on What Money You Put In

What you put in Where Fee
Won cash Kiosk None
Foreign cash (16 currencies) Kiosk (passport identity verification, up to $4,000 per day) Exchange rate preference up to 90% (website wording: "cheaper than exchange")
Overseas credit or debit card App (VISA · Master · JCB) or kiosk (MASTER · JCB) 2~3% ($100 top-up, $2~3 fee)

Two things to know.

  • The currencies accepted differ by kiosk. Currencies or bills a kiosk does not handle come back out, but once money has been accepted, you cannot get it back.
  • Top-up in the app is for overseas cards only. For cash, you have to go to a kiosk.
  • If you have a VISA card, top up at a kiosk (Lotte Card guidance).

🛍 3. Where to Use It — Lotte Card Merchants, With Exceptions

The website says "all Lotte Card merchants except restricted merchants." The restricted merchants are online gift certificates and no-approval merchants that skip real-time approval (travel agencies, toll gates and expressway rest stops, in-flight sales, airline tickets, etc.). Bring another way to pay at rest stops and on planes.

Online payment works only inside the CHECK iN SEOUL app (guidance from the partner carrier LG U+).

Discount benefits: in the app's Benefits menu, get a coupon and show it with the card at the merchant. On October 12, 2026, the card called "Lotte Department Store Tourist Membership X CHECK iN SEOUL" (롯데백화점 투어리스트 멤버십 X 체크인서울) came out, merging in the Lotte Department Store foreign visitor membership. According to press reports (Metro Seoul · SBS Biz), it carries benefits at Lotte affiliates and more than 70 merchants in Korea, 5% off at the Lotte Department Store main store, and 10% off at Lotte Duty Free (롯데면세점), 7-Eleven (세븐일레븐), and Lotte Mart.

🚇 4. The Transit Card — The Balance Is 'Separate'

When you ride the subway or bus with CHECK iN SEOUL, a transit card balance builds up separately from the prepaid card balance. The two are linked, but the paths money can take between them are fixed.

  • The transit card is topped up only from the prepaid card balance. You can add in 1,000 won units, starting from 5,000 won, and if the prepaid card balance is less than 5,000 won, the top-up won't go through. The transit card balance can be filled up to 100,000 won.
  • After setting the amount in the app, hold the card to the back of your phone and the transit card balance is filled (turn on the phone's NFC). At a kiosk, choose Transit top-up and insert the card.
  • The subway and bus give a youth discount (ages 13~18, applied automatically after signing up in the app and registering the card).

Transit card refund: the transit card balance can be returned to the prepaid card balance in full only. The refund fee is 0.3%, up to 3 times a day, from 10 won and up. In the app, choose refund and hold the card to the back of your phone, or insert the card at a kiosk.

⚠️ If you lose the card, the transit card balance is not covered. The prepaid card balance can be used again after a reissue, but the transit card balance on the lost card is not refunded even with a reissue. If the card is still in your hand, as with damage, get the transit card refund done before reissuing. Keeping only a few days' worth on the transit card is safer.

If your trip outside Seoul is long, carry the T-money from #1298 as well.

💵 5. Before You Leave — Taking Out the Leftover Money

The prepaid card balance is refunded only by withdrawal at a kiosk.

Rule Details
Currency Won only
Unit 1,000 won units
Limit 100,000 won per time, up to 5 times a day (resets the next day)
Fee 1,000 won per time

The steps are these. Enter the amount in the app's withdrawal menu and a withdrawal QR code appears; hold the QR to the kiosk scanner and the cash comes out. You can also scan your passport at the kiosk, insert the card, and withdraw right away.

If a transit card balance remains, refund it to the prepaid card first, then withdraw. If you cancel the prepaid card, the transit card balance disappears.

Because the withdrawal costs 1,000 won each time, taking out 10,000 won costs 1,000 won and so does taking out 100,000 won. Taking out the leftover in one go costs less. Rewards received from a friend referral can be spent on payments but cannot be withdrawn.

In short: CHECK iN SEOUL suits people who will shop a lot at Lotte Department Store, Lotte Mart, and 7-Eleven, and people who bring won or foreign cash and want to put it straight into a kiosk instead of a currency exchange. If you need to take out large sums several times a day, WOWPASS, which has no limit on withdrawals, is more convenient. If you only need transit, T-money is enough.

🆘 7. When You Get Stuck

  1. The app can't read the passport chip — 2 App Store reviews (rating 1.3/5) describe the same difficulty. If applying in the app is blocked, there is a route of scanning your passport at a kiosk and issuing right there. The card that comes out then is LOCAL (500,000 won limit), and a VISA card can be applied for only with passport verification in the app.
  2. The balance doesn't show on the bus terminal — the transit card balance is separate from the prepaid card balance. Check the transit card balance in the app, and if it is empty, top up 5,000 won or more.
  3. You lost the card — use Suspend or Report lost at the bottom of MY > Account in the app. Once you report it lost, the card is deactivated right away. If you find it again, cancel the suspension or lost report and keep using it as is. For a reissue, the order is apply in the app at MY > Account → reissue QR code → pick up at a kiosk, and the reissue fee of 3,000 won is taken from the prepaid card balance when you receive the card. The transit card balance on the lost card is not refunded even with a reissue (if the card is in your hand, get the transit card refund first before reissuing).
  4. You can't make yourself understood — customer service 1577-6529. English every day 08:00~20:00, Japanese and Chinese on weekdays 11:00~20:00 (holidays included, weekends excluded).
