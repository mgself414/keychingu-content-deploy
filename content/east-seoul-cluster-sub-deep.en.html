🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to visit in Gangdong-gu (강동구), Seoul. They are the Amsa-dong Prehistoric Site (암사동 유적), the site of a Neolithic village, an ecological park and a bicycle park on the Han River (한강) bank, Gildong Ecological Park (길동생태공원), which you enter by reservation, Ilja Mountain Herb Astronomy Park (일자산 허브천문공원) with its constellation lights and Iljasan Neighborhood Park (일자산근린공원), a Seoul Trail course and a family campground, and neighborhood parks, a performance hall and a market. Gwangnaru Hangang Park (광나루한강공원) is in the #706 Hangang Parks Guide: 9 Hangang Parks, Nanji, Mangwon and Ichon on the North Bank, Gangseo, Yanghwa, Banpo, Jamwon, Jamsil and Gwangnaru on the South Bank.

Gildong Ecological Park is registered as entry after advance reservation, and the Amsa-dong Prehistoric Site and Gildong Ecological Park close on Mondays.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 A Neolithic Village — Seoul Amsa-dong Prehistoric Site and Prehistory Museum

The Seoul Amsa-dong Prehistoric Site (서울 암사동 유적) [Olympic-ro 875 (올림픽로 875), Gangdong-gu (강동구), ☎ 02-3425-6520] is a Neolithic site on the bank of the Han River. Radiocarbon dating confirmed it as a site spanning the 4,000 to 3,000 BC millennia. The house pits were dug with round floors or squares with rounded corners, about 5~6m long and 70~100cm deep. In the middle there is a hearth ringed with stones. Many comb-pattern pottery vessels with pointed bottoms were found, along with stone axes, net sinkers and burnt acorns. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the largest village-scale Neolithic site confirmed so far in Korea.

The Amsa-dong Prehistory Museum (암사동 선사유적박물관) [same address, ☎ 02-3425-6520] consists of a permanent exhibition hall and an outdoor exhibition area. The permanent exhibition hall has comb-pattern pottery, Neolithic tools, a preserved real pit-house site and a panoramic video. Outdoors there are a feature protection shelter (유구보호각) that shows part of the features found in the 2016~2017 excavation, a restored pit house (복원움집) and an experience pit house (체험움집) you can walk into.

  • Opening hours: 09:30~18:00 (last entry and ticket sales 17:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1. If Monday is a public holiday, it closes on the next first weekday.
  • Fee (individual): Adults 500 won. The child fee is registered inconsistently, 300 won under the site entry and 400 won under the museum entry. Children aged 6 and under, adults aged 65 and over, persons of national merit, persons with disabilities and others are free with supporting documents.
  • Parking: Available (105 spaces). Small cars 2,000 won, compact cars 1,000 won.
  • Registered activities: Prehistory experience education (선사체험교육), World Heritage education (세계유산교육), accessory making (장신구 만들기) and more.

📍 The Han River Bank — Amsa Ecological Park and Gwangnaru Bicycle Park

Gwangnaru Hangang Park Amsa Ecological Park (광나루한강공원 암사생태공원) [Seonsa-ro 83-106 (선사로 83-106), Gangdong-gu (강동구), ☎ 070-7788-9670] is an ecological park on the Han River bank that opened on December 28, 2008. It is designated an ecosystem conservation area, and has walking paths and visitor trails.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.
  • Parking: 1,000 won for the first 30 minutes, then 200 won every 10 minutes.
  • Registered programs: Barefoot Walking Healing School (맨발 걷기 힐링스쿨), Joyful Ecology School (즐거운 생태학교), Forest Rope Play (숲밧줄 놀이), Amsa Wildlife Exploration Team (암사 생물탐사대) and more.

Gwangnaru Bicycle Park (광나루자전거공원) [Seonsa-ro 83-19 (선사로 83-19), Gangdong-gu (강동구), ☎ Future Hangang Headquarters Gangnaru Information Center (미래한강본부 강나루안내센터) 02-3780-0501] is a bicycle park connected to Gwangnaru Hangang Park (광나루한강공원). Bicycles can be rented, and there is a tire air pump nearby. It has a children's bicycle training area and a bicycle track. The entrance is about 900m from Exit 3 of Amsa Station (암사역) on the subway.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

📍 An Ecological Park You Enter by Reservation — Gildong Ecological Park

Gildong Ecological Park (길동생태공원) [Cheonho-daero 1291 (천호대로 1291), Gangdong-gu (강동구), ☎ 02-472-2770] is an ecological park that opened on April 28, 1999. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it was the second ecological park created in Korea after Yeouido Saetgang Ecological Park (여의도 샛강생태공원). Over a total of 80,683㎡ it has a wetland zone, a forest zone, a grassland zone and a plaza zone, and 2,800 or so species of living things live there, including 7 kinds of natural monuments. It has a Firefly Experience Hall (반딧불이 체험관) and bird observation blinds (조류 관찰대). The bird observation blinds have observation windows about 30cm square cut into wooden partitions so that the birds are not startled.

  • Opening hours: Winter 08:00~17:00, summer 08:00~18:00. Last entry 16:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday. Fee: Free. Capacity: 400 people.
  • Reservation: It is registered that you enter after booking in advance on the Seoul parks website (서울의 공원) or the Seoul Public Service Reservation website (서울시 공공서비스 예약) (the domestic visitor reservation guide item).
  • Parking: There is no parking lot inside the park. It is written that you use the parking lot of the Gildong Ecological Culture Center (길동생태문화센터) across the street, and only weekday-system electronic tag vehicles can park.

📍 Iljasan — Herb Astronomy Park, Neighborhood Park, Seoul Trail Course 7 and Family Campground

Ilja Mountain Herb Astronomy Park (일자산 허브천문공원) [Dunchon-dong (둔촌동), Gangdong-gu (강동구), ☎ Gangdong-gu Office Green City Division (강동구청 푸른도시과) 02-3425-6452] is a park across from Gildong Ecological Park, and it belongs to Iljasan Neighborhood Park (일자산근린공원). There are 282 multicolored constellation lights on the park floor, a star chart 75m in diameter transferred onto the ground. To the east there is a viewpoint for watching the sunrise, and to the west an observation post for watching the sunset. It is described as holding a children's astronomical observation program at a small observatory every year from July to September.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Iljasan Neighborhood Park (일자산근린공원) [Dongnam-ro 660 (동남로 660), Gangdong-gu (강동구), ☎ Gangdong-gu Office Green City Division (강동구청 푸른도시과) 02-3425-8822] is a low mountain on the border of Dunchon-dong (둔촌동) in Gangdong-gu and Choi-dong (초이동) in Hanam-si (하남시). The top of the mountain is flat like the character "一", so it is called Iljasan (일자산). At the summit there is a New Year sunrise plaza, a lawn plaza, an X-Games court, a basketball court, a forest experience area for young children and a waterfall.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Seoul Trail Course 7 Iljasan Course (서울둘레길 7코스 일자산코스) [Sangil-dong (상일동), Gangdong-gu (강동구), ☎ Seoul Trail Information Center (서울둘레길 안내센터) 02-779-7902~4] is a course themed "a small forest path met in the city" among the Seoul Trail (서울둘레길), which circles Seoul in 21 courses totaling 156.5km. It runs from Myeongil Neighborhood Park (명일근린공원) to Ogeum Bridge 1 (오금1교), and the elevation is not high. It is close to Godeok Station (고덕역) and Olympic Park Station (올림픽공원역) on the subway.

  • Length and time: 7.7km in total, about 3 hours 15 minutes. Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.

The Gangdong Greenway Family Campground (강동그린웨이 가족캠핑장) [Cheonho-daero 206-gil 87 (천호대로206길 87), Gangdong-gu (강동구), ☎ 02-478-4079] is a campground in the forest of Iljasan. It has deck sites with tents already pitched. It is registered that online reservation is required.

  • Opening hours: Check-in 13:00, check-out 11:00.
  • Closed days: The second Tuesday of March, June to August and November. It operates without closed days in April to May and September to October. The registered value says "the second Tuesday of every month" and lists only March, June to August and November in parentheses, so the other months cannot be known from the registered value alone.

📍 Cheonho, Sangil and Amsa — Cheonho Park, Gangdong Art Center and Amsa Jonghap Market

Cheonho Park (천호공원) [Olympic-ro 702 (올림픽로 702), Gangdong-gu (강동구), ☎ 02-489-2776] is a park that opened on June 20, 1998, after the Seoul city government bought the former factory site of the fountain pen company Pilot (파이롯트). It has an outdoor stage, a pond and a basketball court, and the Gangdong-gu Public Haegong Library (해공도서관), which opened in 2008. "Haegong" (해공) is the pen name of the independence activist Shin Ik-hui (신익희), who was born nearby.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Not available.

The Gangdong Art Center (강동아트센터) [Dongnam-ro 870 (동남로 870), Gangdong-gu (강동구), ☎ 02-440-0500] is a performance hall inside Myeongil Neighborhood Park (명일근린공원). It has the 850-seat main theater "Hangang" (한강), the 250-seat small theater "Dream" (드림), an art gallery and 3 studios.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~22:00. Closed days: Every Sunday and Monday, Lunar New Year and Chuseok. Fee: Varies by performance.

Amsa Jonghap Market (암사종합시장) [Sangam-ro 11-gil 25 (상암로11길 25), Gangdong-gu (강동구), ☎ 02-442-1040] is a traditional market with a roofed arcade. It sells agricultural products, seafood, banchan (반찬) and bunsik (분식), and it is close to Amsa Station (암사역) on the subway.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Gildong Ecological Park is entered after advance reservation — it is closed on Mondays, and last entry is at 16:00. There is no parking lot inside the park.
  2. The Amsa-dong Prehistoric Site is closed on Mondays — it is open 09:30~18:00, and last entry is at 17:30.
  3. Gangdong Art Center is closed on Sundays and Mondays — online reservation is required for the family campground.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. What is the admission fee for the Amsa-dong Prehistoric Site?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, it is 500 won for an individual adult, and children aged 6 and under, adults aged 65 and over, persons of national merit, persons with disabilities and others are free. The child fee is registered with different values, 300 won under the site entry and 400 won under the museum entry.

Q. Which places are described for their night scenery?

A. Ilja Mountain Herb Astronomy Park is described as having installed 282 constellation lights on the floor for its night scenery, and it is always open. Gwangnaru Bicycle Park is described as letting you ride while watching the Han River night view and sunset, and it is always open.
