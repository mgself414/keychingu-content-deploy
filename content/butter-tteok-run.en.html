Seoul Butter Rice Cake "Butter-Run" Full Guide — 2026's Viral Open-Run Dessert

🎯 Why a "Butter Rice Cake" Guide

Butter Tteok (버터 떡) = a fusion dessert of traditional glutinous rice cake topped with warm melted butter, and Korea's most-searched dessert keyword of H1 2026. The key is "crispy outside, chewy inside" (겉바속쫀), and it took over Instagram·TikTok riding the wave from the Dubai Chewy Cookie (두쫀쿠) craze. The biggest foreigner pain points = (a) the misconception that "tteok = old-fashioned snack" — actually it's a trendy new dessert (b) where to buy it (specific Yeonnam·Seongsu shops) (c) afternoon sellouts — famous shops require a morning "Butter-Run" (d) types·price. Searches for "butter rice cake Seoul / 버터떡 맛집" are surging on Reddit·YouTube.

This guide covers signature butter-tteok shops·types + open-run strategy + areas (Yeonnam·Seongsu) + type matrix + FAQ.

🧈 The 6 Major Butter Tteok — Deep Dive

① Jeonggwajajeom (정과자점) — Yeonnam-dong

  • Signature: rich Japanese-style cheese + butter tteok·golden-brown crispy outside·chewy inside·butter aroma when warm
  • Location: 22 Donggyo-ro 38-gil, Mapo-gu (Yeonnam-dong)
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆ (Yeonnam access·photogenic)
  • Tip: centers on the plain taste of rice·not over-decorated·eat immediately while warm

② Iutjip Tongtongi (이웃집통통이)

  • Signature: Seoul's first Shanghai-style butter tteok·no-flour gluten-free·extra chewy
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆ (gluten-free demand·quality)
  • Tip: often sold out by lunch → a morning "Butter-Run" is essential

③ Oud (오우드) — Seongsu-dong

  • Signature: butter tteok baked in a madeleine shape·canelé-like crispy shell·chewy glutinous inside·Seongsu-mood cafe
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆ (Seongsu hotspot·cafe combo)
  • Tip: cafe vibe + butter tteok·pair with the Seongsu pop-up route

④ Obok Tteokjip (오복떡집) — Seongsu-dong

  • Signature: butter tteok from a traditional rice-cake shop·Seongsu favorite·tradition + new hybrid
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★☆☆ (traditional tteok-shop experience)
  • Tip: authentic chewiness from a traditional shop·Seongsu local

⑤ Heart's Bakery (하츠베이커리)

  • Signature: King-size butter tteok (왕버터떡) (bigger than standard)·SNS photo piece
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆ (visual·shareable)
  • Tip: king-size = share·photo·watch for sellouts

⑥ Ang Butter Tteok (앙버터떡)

  • Signature: injeolmi + sweet red bean + butter·chewy + sweet + savory triple balance
  • Foreigner-friendly: ★★★★☆ (ang = red bean, familiar + fresh)
  • Tip: red bean (sweet) + butter (savory)·great gateway to tradition + fusion

🍽 How to Eat & the "Butter-Run" (for foreigners)

  • Butter-Run: visit famous shops at morning opening time → avoid afternoon sellouts (especially Seongsu·Garosu-gil)
  • While warm: butter melts for peak aroma·texture → eat right after getting it
  • Payment: card·mobile pay·small cash (varies by shop)
  • Gluten-free: confirm no-flour shops like Iutjip Tongtongi
  • Emergency: ☎ 1330 (tourist interpretation)·112

⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "Tteok is an old-fashioned snack": butter tteok is a 2026 latest-trend dessert·hotspot new item.

2. "You can go anytime": famous shops sell out in the afternoon·a morning "Butter-Run" is essential.

3. "It's fine cold": butter tteok is best while warm for butter aroma·texture.

4. "Tteok = gluten": rice-based so mostly no flour·but confirm per shop (some use flour).

5. "Same as Dubai cookie": Dubai cookie (두쫀쿠, a cookie) ≠ butter tteok (glutinous rice)·many shops sell both.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. What is butter tteok?: traditional glutinous rice cake + warm butter·H1 2026's most-searched dessert·crispy-chewy.

2. Where to buy?: Yeonnam (Jeonggwajajeom)·Seongsu (Oud·Obok Tteokjip)·Iutjip Tongtongi (gluten-free).

3. Must I open-run?: famous shops often sell out in the afternoon → morning is safe.

4. Price range?: trend-dessert small price·varies by shop (roughly ₩3,000~8,000 each).

5. Gluten-free?: shops like Iutjip Tongtongi use no flour·but always confirm per shop.

6. Recommended persona: entry = Jeonggwajajeom / Seongsu = Oud / gluten-sensitive = Iutjip Tongtongi.

⚠ Medical·Safety·Allergy Disclaimer

Butter tteok contains glutinous rice·dairy (butter)·red bean → watch for lactose·nut·allergy issues. Even "gluten-free" shops may have cross-contamination → confirm directly for severe allergies. Tteok is a choking risk (children·elderly). Prices·hours·sellouts vary by shop/season → check before visiting. Emergency ☎ 1330·112.
