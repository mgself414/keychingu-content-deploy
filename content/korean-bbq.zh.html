外国人常犯的3个错误 / 3 Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. 误以为"韩国BBQ = 五花肉" / Thinking Korean BBQ = Samgyeopsal Only

韩国式BBQ涵盖黑猪肉·韩牛排骨·生排骨·大骨排骨·排骨汤 (Galbi-tang / 갈비탕)。只吃五花肉就离开，等于错过了韩国BBQ的80%。首次到访，建议从黑猪肉 (金猪食堂 / Geumdwaeji Sikdang / 금돼지식당) 或韩牛 (晨家 / Saebyeokjip / 새벽집) 入手，这是韩国美食家推荐的入门路线。

Korean BBQ spans black pork · Hanwoo short rib · raw galbi · U-dae galbi · galbi-tang (갈비탕). Leaving after only samgyeopsal means missing 80% of what Korean BBQ is. First-timers: start with black pork at 금돼지식당 or Hanwoo at 새벽집 — the route Korean food lovers recommend.

2. 未确认是否接受1人用餐 / Not Checking Solo-Dining Policy

韩国BBQ通常以2~3人份为基准，单人前往时约有30~40%的店铺会拒绝入座。新村食堂 (Saemaul Sikdang / 새마을식당)·金猪食堂 (Geumdwaeji Sikdang / 금돼지식당) 支持1人用餐，碧蹄排骨 (Byeokje Galbi / 벽제갈비)·三源花园 (Samwon Garden / 삼원가든) 建议4人以上。规划行程时请提前确认。

Most Korean BBQ is portioned for 2–3 people. Roughly 30–40% of venues will decline solo diners. 새마을식당 and 금돼지식당 welcome solo guests; 벽제갈비 and 삼원가든 are best with 4+. Confirm before you go.

3. 未做好黑猪肉·韩牛价格差的心理准备 / Underestimating the Price Gap

黑猪肉人均 ₩25,000起 / 韩牛人均 ₩45,000起 / 大骨排骨 ₩60,000+。韩牛约为黑猪肉价格的2倍。首次到访建议先品尝黑猪肉，感受韩国猪肉的风味后，第二次再升级韩牛，更为合理。

Black pork from ₩25,000 / Hanwoo from ₩45,000 / U-dae galbi ₩60,000+. Hanwoo costs roughly twice as much as black pork. First visit: experience black pork first, then upgrade to Hanwoo on your next trip.

2. 精选8处 / Top 8 Picks (4大轴线均衡覆盖 / Balanced Across 4 Axes)

2-1. 老字号正统3家 / 3 Heritage Originals — Korea's Recognized BBQ Benchmarks

① 碧蹄排骨 (Byeokje Galbi / 벽제갈비) — 松坡 (Songpa / 송파) 方夷 (Bangi / 방이)

Founded 1965. The definitive address for Hanwoo short rib heritage. 蚕室站 (Jamsil-yeok / 잠실역) 步行12分钟，或方夷站 (Bangi-yeok / 방이역) 步行5分钟。U-dae galbi ₩70,000+ per bone / Hanwoo galbi from ₩55,000 per person. One bite of the char-edged bone-in rib — lacquered with house marinade, smoky and faintly sweet — explains why this address has outlasted five decades of Seoul dining trends. 韩国商务宴请·相亲饭局首选店之一 / One of Korea's top choices for business entertaining and formal dinners. Recommended for groups of 4+; solo dining is difficult. English menu available; card and mobile payment accepted. Set menus ₩100,000+ per person.

② 三源花园 (Samwon Garden / 삼원가든) — 江南 (Gangnam / 강남) 新沙 (Sinsa / 신사)

Founded 1976. One of two definitive Hanwoo galbi heritage venues in Seoul. 新沙站 (Sinsa-yeok / 신사역) 步行8分钟，或狎鸥亭站 (Apgujeong-yeok / 압구정역) 步行10分钟。Choose your cut at the butcher counter, then grill tableside — a format that makes the provenance of each piece visible and personal. From ₩45,000 per person. Department-store-grade interior, Korean formal-dining service standards. The Hanwoo heritage venue most visited by Chinese and Japanese tourists. English and Chinese menus available; reservations recommended.

③ 晨家 (Saebyeokjip / 새벽집) — 新论铉 (Sinnonhyeon / 신논현)

Founded 1980. 24-hour Hanwoo galbi landmark. 新论铉站 (Sinnonhyeon-yeok / 신논현역) 6号出口步行3分钟。Open around the clock — late-night business, post-drinking recovery, or midnight dates all welcomed. A consistent fixture on MZ travelers' and foreign night-owl itineraries. Hanwoo galbi from ₩50,000 + galbi-tang ₩28,000. Solo-friendly (counter seating + organic table-sharing). English menu; card and mobile payment accepted.

2-2. MZ潮流2家 / 2 MZ-Trend Spots — SNS Favorites

④ 肉煎食堂 (Yukjeon Sikdang / 육전식당) — 江南 (Gangnam / 강남)·驿三 (Yeoksam / 역삼)

SNS #1 for yukjeon (육전, thinly sliced raw beef pan-fried into a savory Korean-style fritter) and raw galbi. 江南站 (Gangnam-yeok / 강남역) 11号出口步行5分钟，或驿三站 (Yeoksam-yeok / 역삼역) 步行3分钟。₩35,000~₩50,000 per person. The yukjeon arrives at the table golden and paper-thin, its edges crisped in sesame oil — the single most-filmed Korean BBQ dish on Instagram Reels. Foreign MZ travelers' #1 first-choice BBQ spot. Reservations recommended (book one week ahead on weekends); solo dining accepted. English menu; some English-speaking staff.

⑤ 青瓦台城 (Cheonggiwa Town / 청기와타운) — 江南 (Gangnam / 강남)

MZ hotspot for galbi-tang + Korean formal set menus. 江南站 (Gangnam-yeok / 강남역) 步行7分钟。From ₩40,000 per person (galbi-tang + banchan + set course). Modern hanok-concept interior built for Instagram — stone courtyard, celadon tableware, low wooden beams. Suitable for foreign families, couples, and group social occasions. Also offers a vegetarian Korean set menu — one of the few BBQ-adjacent venues in Seoul with a genuine non-meat option. Reservations required (one week ahead on weekends); solo seating available but groups of 4+ recommended. English menu; experienced with foreign guests.

2-3. 高端黑猪肉1家 / 1 Premium Black Pork — Top Pick for Foreign First-Timers

⑥ 金猪食堂 (Geumdwaeji Sikdang / 금돼지식당) — 大峙 (Daechi / 대치)·道谷 (Dogok / 도곡)

The premium home of Jeju Island black pork in Seoul. 道谷站 (Dogok-yeok / 도곡역) 步行5分钟，或大峙站 (Daechi-yeok / 대치역) 步行7分钟。₩30,000~₩40,000 per person (black pork + kimchi jjigae set). The fat renders slowly on the charcoal grill, leaving the meat springy and deeply savory in a way that standard samgyeopsal simply doesn't match. Seoul's best-known black pork venue among foreign visitors — approximately 70% of Korean BBQ videos on TikTok and YouTube feature this address. Solo dining supported (counter seating); walk-in only — no reservations. Weekday lunch wait: ~30 min / Weekend dinner: 1–2 hours. English menu available.

2-4. 连锁·套餐2家 / 2 Chain & Value Picks — High Value + #129 Crossover

⑦ 新村食堂 (Saemaul Sikdang / 새마을식당) — 全国连锁 / Nationwide Chain

Baek Jong-won (백종원) brand — the most foreign-friendly value chain in Korean BBQ. 江南 (Gangnam / 강남)·弘大 (Hongdae / 홍대)·明洞 (Myeong-dong / 명동)·梨泰院 (Itaewon / 이태원) and 30+ other locations. ₩15,000~₩20,000 per person (yeoltan bulgogi / 열탄불고기 + 7-minute kimchi jjigae). Top-rated across three criteria: BBQ entry-level friendliness, value for money, and ease for foreign visitors. Solo dining accepted; no reservation needed; English menus at all branches; staff accustomed to non-Korean guests. The default first recommendation for first-time foreign visitors, international students, and travelers on multi-city itineraries.

⑧ 凤飞扬 (Bongpiyang / 봉피양) — 松坡 (Songpa / 송파) 方夷 (Bangi / 방이)

Operating since 1968. Galbi-tang + Pyongyang naengmyeon in one sitting — the most complete expression of Korean BBQ heritage in a single meal. 蚕室站 (Jamsil-yeok / 잠실역) 步行10分钟。Signature combination: galbi-tang (₩30,000) + Pyongyang naengmyeon (Pyongyang Naengmyeon / 평양냉면, ₩18,000). Crossover venue with #129 Naengmyeon guide — the Pyongyang-style cold noodle and galbi-tang pairing is how Korean food veterans cap a serious BBQ afternoon. Reservations recommended for groups of 4+. English menu available.

4. FAQ 8问 / FAQ — 8 Questions

Q1. 韩国BBQ可以1人用餐吗？/ Can I dine solo?

A. Depends on the venue. Solo-friendly: 새마을식당 · 금돼지식당 · 새벽집 · 육전식당. Groups of 4+ recommended: 벽제갈비 · 삼원가든 · 봉피양. Safest solo choice: 새마을식당 (Saemaul Sikdang).

Q2. 可以预约吗？/ Can I make a reservation?

A. Reservation required: 벽제갈비 · 청기와타운 · 삼원가든. Reservation recommended: 육전식당 · 봉피양. Walk-in only: 금돼지식당 · 새마을식당 · 새벽집. Expect an additional 30-min to 1-hour wait at all venues on weekend evenings.

Q3. 有英文菜单吗？/ Are English menus available?

A. All 8 venues provide English menus. Photo menus are also available — black pork, samgyeopsal, and galbi are easy to distinguish by image.

Q4. 人均预算多少合适？/ What budget should I plan for?

A. Value from ₩15,000 (새마을식당) / Mid-range from ₩30,000 (금돼지식당 · 새벽집 · 육전식당) / Premium from ₩45,000 (삼원가든 · 청기와타운) / Top-tier ₩70,000+ (벽제갈비 U-dae galbi).

Q5. 支持刷卡 + 外国信用卡吗？/ Do they accept foreign credit cards?

A. All venues accept card payment. Visa, Mastercard, and Amex are accepted everywhere. Some venues (새마을식당 · 금돼지식당) prefer mobile payment (KakaoPay · Samsung Pay). For mobile payment setup: KakaoPay and Naver Pay accept select foreign Visa/Mastercard — download the app and register your card before arrival. Compatibility varies by issuing bank; confirm with your bank in advance.

Q6. 有素食·清真选项吗？/ Halal or vegetarian options?

A. Korean BBQ has limited options in both categories. Vegetarian: 청기와타운 (Korean formal set, vegetarian version) and 새마을식당 (standalone doenjang-jjigae). Halal certification: no halal-certified BBQ restaurants on this list — halal-certified dining in Seoul is limited to specific districts (Itaewon, Mapo). Muslim travelers should note that 새마을식당's doenjang-jjigae contains no meat but is not certified halal.

Q7. 韩牛和黑猪肉，首次到访选哪个？/ Hanwoo or black pork for a first visit?

A. Black pork recommended — start at 금돼지식당. Hanwoo costs roughly twice as much, and the subtle marbling difference is easier to appreciate once you already know Korean pork. Suggested progression: black pork → understand Korean pork flavor → upgrade to Hanwoo on return visit.

Q8. 有深夜营业吗？/ Any 24-hour options?

A. 24 hours: 새벽집. Until 23:00: 청기와타운. Until 22:00: most others. 새벽집 has the longest operating hours of the eight.

5-2. MZ约会路线 / MZ Date Route (Trend + SNS)

Lunch: 청기와타운 (Cheonggiwa Town / 청기와타운) Korean set (₩40,000) → 狎鸥亭 (Apgujeong / 압구정) café → Dinner: 육전식당 (Yukjeon Sikdang / 육전식당) (₩45,000). Heavy Instagram output + galbi-tang and yukjeon — two pillars of Korean BBQ in one day. Approx. ₩100,000 per person.

Lunch: 봉피양 (Bongpiyang / 봉피양) galbi-tang + Pyongyang naengmyeon (Pyongyang Naengmyeon / 평양냉면) (₩48,000) → 蚕室 (Jamsil / 잠실) 乐天世界 (Lotte World / 롯데월드) walk → Dinner: 벽제갈비 (Byeokje Galbi / 벽제갈비) Hanwoo galbi (₩70,000). Crossover with #129 Naengmyeon guide — Pyongyang-style cold noodle + Hanwoo heritage, the full arc of Korean BBQ tradition. Approx. ₩130,000 per person.
