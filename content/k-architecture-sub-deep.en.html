K-Architecture 12 Selections

1. Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP)

  • Distinction: Zha Hadid Design 2014·Korea’s Largest Irregular Architecture·LED Night View·MZ 1st Place
  • Location: Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station Exit 1 and 2
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ proof shot 1st place·#583 Dongdaemun cross

2. Hanok (traditional architecture)

  • Discrimination: Korean traditional house·Tile + floor + Ondol + Hanji window·1000 years+ History and nature friendly
  • Experience: Bukchon Hanok Village, Seochon, Eunpyeong Hanok Village
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN authentic 1st place·#588 Hanbok cross

3. Bukchon Hanok Village (Seoul)

  • Discrimination: Seoul #1 Hanok cluster·900 houses·Joseon noble house·MZ photo spot
  • Location: 5-minute walk from Exit 2 and 3 of Anguk Station
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ JP·CN Hanbok + Hanok cluster

4. Gyeongbokgung Palace (Joseon’s main palace)

  • Discrimination: 1395 King Taejo Yi Seong-gye, 600 years of the Joseon Dynasty’s royal palace, Geunjeongjeon, Gyeonghoeru, and guard changing ceremony
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN Authentic No. 1·#588 Hanbok

5. Jongmyo Shrine (UNESCO)

  • Discrimination: 1995 First Korea to be registered by UNESCO·Joseon royal shrine·Jongmyo Jerye (UNESCO intangible)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

6. Changdeokgung Palace + Sponsorship (UNESCO)

  • Discrimination: 1997 UNESCO registration·Joseon Villa Palace 1405·Sponsored Secret Garden (reservation required)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN authentic 1st place

7. Leeum Museum of Art (Mario Botta, Rem Koolhaas, Jean Nouvel)

  • Distinction: Designed by three of the world's greatest masters·Samsung Collection Museum·Lee Kun-hee Collection·36 national treasures
  • Location: Hannam-dong, Itaewon Station
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Art Mania·#668 K-Art cross

8. Songdo Convensia (Talyor Cullity Lethlean)

  • Discrimination: Incheon Songdo Smart City·Korea #1 New Town IBD·International Convention·G Tower
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP Modern Mania·#509 Incheon Chinatown cross

9. Tadao Ando - Bontae Museum (Jeju)

  • Distinction: Designed by Japanese master Tadao Ando·Exposed concrete·Jeju nature
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ JP·EN architecture enthusiast

10. Korean modern architects

  • Kim Chung-up: Students of Le Corbusier·Korean modern first generation (1922-1988)
  • Kim Swoo-geun: Space Headquarters·Korean Modern 2nd Generation (1931-1986)
  • Hyeonjun Yoo: TV Celebrity Architect·“Where to Live” bestseller
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP architecture enthusiast

11. Andong Hahoe Village + Yangdong Village (UNESCO)

  • Discrimination: 2010 UNESCO registration·Hanok + 600 years of Dongseong Village·#663 Andong·#664 Gyeongju sub
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN authentic 1st place

12. Art museum full set (modern architecture)

  • MMCA Seoul: Designed by Kim Bong-ryeol, next to Gyeongbokgung Palace
  • DDP: Zaha Hadid
  • Lium: Mario Botta, Rem Koolhaas, Jean Nouvel
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN art + architecture enthusiasts

FAQ 6

Q1. DDP admission fee? A. Free outside + design market, separate special exhibitions (KRW 12,000~).

Q2. What is the difference between Bukchon Hanok Village and Ikseon-dong? A. Bukchon = 900 noblemen’s hanoks·Ikseon-dong = 90 urban hanoks + cafe cluster.

Q3. How do I make a reservation to sponsor Changdeokgung Palace? A. Advance reservation required (www.cdg.go.kr)·90-minute guided tour·6 times a day.

Q4. Korea #1 Hanok hotel? A. Laonzena (Gahoe-dong) · Nostalgia (Bukchon) · 300,000 won per night ~.

Q5. Zaha Hadid’s Korean work? A. DDP only (Korea’s only)·Completed in 2014·Worked two years before Zaha’s death.

Q6. Recommendation for a Korean architecture book? A. Hyun-Jun Yoo “Where should we live”·Hyo-Sang Seung “All old things are beautiful”.
