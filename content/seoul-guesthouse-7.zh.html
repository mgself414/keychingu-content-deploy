⚠ 外国人常犯的3个错误

1. “宾馆=太脏·吵”：现代宾馆=干净+WiFi+共用厨房·休息室·洗衣机矩阵#1。 Hostelworld评论8.0+ =爱好者矩阵+外国适合。宿舍（多人）/私人（私人房间）矩阵选择。

2. "Guesthouse = difficult to reserve": 3 official channels = (a) hostelworld = Foreign backpacker matrix #1 (b) booking.com / agoda = English interface + card payment (c) airbnb = Guesthouse-like rooms.外国狂热 = hostelworld 推荐。

3. “宾馆=英语不会说”：7家宾馆=英语工作人员/入住+国外背包客矩阵#1。弘大、梨泰院、明洞=外籍员工较多。钟路、江南、新村、东大门 = 英语可以。

⚙ 4 个常见问题解答

1. Recommended best route for foreign guesthouse: (a) nightlife = Hongdae (1)·Itaewon (2)·Sinchon (6) (b) Shopping·City center = Myeongdong (3)·Dongdaemun (7) (c) Hanok·City center = Jongno (4) (d) Business = Gangnam (5).国外爱好者=优先移动路线。

2. Dormitory vs Private Room: (a) Dormitory (30,000~70,000 won/night) = 4~12 people shared + foreign backpacker social matrix (b) Private Room (60,000~130,000 won/night) = Private room + shared toilet/shower or individual matrix.外国爱好者=第一次参观宿舍+第二次私人推荐。

3. 宾馆礼仪： (a) 入住 = 下午 2-4 点退房 = 上午 10-11 点 (b) 宿舍 = 安静的床上用品 + 耳机 (c) 共用厨房/休息室 = 使用后清洁 (d) 淋浴/厕所 = 短途 + 清洁 (e) WiFi/储物柜标准 (f) 英语工作人员 = 全部 7 可以。

4. Foreign Guesthouse Season: Spring flowers (April Jongno/Sinchon) / Summer (June~August Hongdae nightlife)/Fall foliage (October Jongno/Insa)/Winter (December~February 30~50% off best).国外热潮=春秋高峰+冬季价格热潮。
