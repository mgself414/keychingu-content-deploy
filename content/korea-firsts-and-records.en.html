Korea's Firsts and Bests Guide — the 'Firsts' of Cheomseongdae, Gwangjang Market and Sosu Seowon, the 'Biggest and Longest' of the Mireuksaji Stone Pagoda, Hwanseongul Cave and Towangseong Falls, and the 'Ends and Beginnings' of Marado, Ganjeolgot and Jeongdongjin Station: 20 Places

Subtitle: Among the places picked in the "Korea's Firsts & Bests" theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" public vote, 20 places whose Korea Tourism Organization descriptions state records such as "first", "largest" or "most" · Records follow the Korea Tourism Organization descriptions · The places with a registered entrance fee are Suncheonman Bay National Garden (adult 10,000 won), Hwanseongul Cave (adult 4,500 won), Manjanggul Cave (adult 4,000 won), Hwaseong Haenggung and Sosu Seowon (adult 2,000 won) · Manjanggul Cave closes on the first Wednesday of every month, Hwanseongul Cave on the 18th of every month, and Space Walk on the first Monday of every month, and Suncheonman Bay National Garden on the last Monday of every month — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide covers 20 of the places picked in the "Korea's Firsts & Bests" (한국의 최초&최고) theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" (명소발굴 100×100) (candidates recommended by travel experts, voted on by the public), where the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data description directly states a record such as "first", "largest" or "most". The ranks and vote counts in the table are the results of that vote.

Records like "first" or "best" tend to be told differently depending on the source. So only the records written in the Korea Tourism Organization descriptions were copied, not the vote's recommendation reasons, and the main text adds "according to the Korea Tourism Organization description". The candidates were chosen by checking the descriptions of those among the places up to around 60th in the vote that have a Korea Tourism Organization information number, and not all 100 places were checked. Some places with high vote counts, such as Seokguram (석굴암), the National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관), Hallasan (한라산) and Jongmyo (종묘), were left out because their descriptions do not state a record such as "first", "largest" or "most". Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) was left out for the same reason, because its description calls it the "foremost royal palace of the Joseon dynasty", which is a statement of rank and not a record. Where the name or address in the vote differs from the Korea Tourism Organization registration (Iksan Mireuksaji Stone Pagoda, Gangneung Jeongdongjin Station high-speed rail and others), the Korea Tourism Organization notation was followed.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Fields with no registered value are marked "No registered value".

📍 The 'Firsts' — Cheomseongdae, Sosu Seowon, Gwangjang Market, Songdo Beach, Jeondong Cathedral, Hwaseong Haenggung and Suncheonman Bay National Garden

Gyeongju Cheomseongdae (140-25 Cheomseong-ro, Gyeongju / 경주시 첨성로 140-25, ☎ Singyeongju Station Tourist Information Center 054-771-1336) is an astronomical observatory believed to have been built during the reign of Queen Seondeok (선덕여왕) of Silla (신라) (reigned 632~647). According to the Korea Tourism Organization description (citing Korea Heritage Service data), it is known as the oldest observatory in the East. It is about 9m tall and was built by stacking fan-shaped stones in 27 tiers.

  • Opening hours: Summer season 09:00~22:00, winter season 09:00~21:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Yeongju Sosu Seowon (2740 Sobaek-ro, Sunheung-myeon, Yeongju / 영주시 순흥면 소백로 2740, ☎ 054-639-5852) began in 1542 when Ju Se-bung (주세붕), the magistrate of Punggi (풍기), built a shrine to honor An Hyang (안향). In 1550, at the request of Yi Hwang (이황), it received the name "Sosu Seowon". According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the buildings are laid out freely because it is Korea's first early seowon, built before the seowon format was fixed.

  • Opening hours: Months 11~2 09:00~17:00, months 3~5 and 9~10 09:00~18:00, months 6~8 09:00~19:00. Entry until 1 hour before closing.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Fee (individual): Adult 2,000 won, youth and soldier 1,330 won, child 660 won.

Gwangjang Market (88 Changgyeonggung-ro, Jongno-gu / 종로구 창경궁로 88, ☎ 02-2267-0291) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, Korea's first permanent market, opened in 1905. Today it is a general market that handles hanbok (한복) fabric, textiles, clothing and food, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the food stalls where the East Gate, North Gate 2 and South Gate 1 meet are the busiest part of the market.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Registered as varying by shop.

Busan Songdo Beach (100 Songdohaebyeon-ro, Seo-gu, Busan / 부산 서구 송도해변로 100, ☎ 051-240-4000) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the first beach opened in Busan, in 1913. It is connected to Amnam Park (암남공원), a coastal ecological park.

  • Opening hours: 06:00~23:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Jeonju Jeondong Cathedral (51 Taejo-ro, Wansan-gu, Jeonju / 전주시 완산구 태조로 51, ☎ 063-284-3222) is a cathedral completed in 1914 on a site where Catholics were martyred in the Joseon (조선) era. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is the first Romanesque-style building built in the Honam (호남) region, and it has a Byzantine-style bell tower.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~17:00. Closed days: Open year-round.

Suwon Hwaseong Haenggung (825 Jeongjo-ro, Paldal-gu, Suwon / 수원시 팔달구 정조로 825, ☎ Ticket office 031-5191-4677) is the temporary palace where King Jeongjo (정조) stayed when he visited Hyeollyungwon (현륭원). According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, Hwaseong (화성) was not only selected as a World Heritage Site but is also regarded as Korea's first new-town development.

  • Opening hours: Months 3~10 09:00~18:00, months 11~2 09:00~17:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee (individual): Adult 2,000 won, youth and soldier 1,500 won, elementary school student 1,000 won.

Suncheonman Bay National Garden (47 Gukgajeongwon 1-ho-gil, Suncheon / 순천시 국가정원1호길 47, ☎ 061-749-3114) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, National Garden No. 1 of the Republic of Korea. It was created when the Suncheonman Bay International Garden Expo was held in 2013, and its 926,992㎡ holds traditional gardens and theme gardens from many countries.

  • Opening hours: Months 10~6 09:00~20:00, months 7~9 09:00~21:00 (last entry 19:00 in both periods). Closed days: Last Monday of every month.
  • Fee (individual): Adult 10,000 won, youth and soldier 7,000 won, child 5,000 won. Night ticket after 17:00: adult 5,000 won.

📍 The 'Biggest and Longest' — Jeonju Hanok Village, Mireuksaji, Gochang Dolmen Site, Hwanseongul Cave, Towangseong Falls, Yongmunsa Ginkgo Tree and Balwangsan Cable Car

Jeonju Hanok Village (99 Girin-daero, Wansan-gu, Jeonju / 전주시 완산구 기린대로 99, ☎ 063-282-1330) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, Korea's largest traditional hanok village, with more than 700 hanok, and the only urban hanok cluster in the country. It has been developed since 1910 and includes Gyeonggijeon (경기전), Omokdae (오목대) and the Hyanggyo (향교).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: 1,200 won for the first 30 minutes, 600 won for each additional 15 minutes.

Iksan Mireuksaji (362 Mireuksaji-ro, Geumma-myeon, Iksan / 익산시 금마면 미륵사지로 362, ☎ Tourist Information Center 063-859-3873) is the site of a temple built in the reign of King Mu (무왕) of Baekje (백제), and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the Mireuksaji Stone Pagoda, a National Treasure, is at its present height of 14.24m Korea's oldest and largest stone pagoda. Here "oldest" is the meaning of "best" (최고) in the Korean text, which the vote's recommendation reason writes as "oldest" (最古). The description gives as the reason its faithful reproduction in stone of the earlier wooden pagoda style. The east pagoda was restored in 1993 with 9 stories (27.67m tall).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Gochang Dolmen Site (74 Goindolgongwon-gil, Gochang-eup, Gochang / 고창군 고창읍 고인돌공원길 74, ☎ Dolmen Museum 063-560-8666) was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2000. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is the largest cluster of dolmens in Korea in a single area, and Gochang County has 1,748 dolmens (as of the 2023 survey; the vote's recommendation reason gives 447). The trail is 1.8㎞ for Courses 1~5 and 1.7㎞ for Course 6.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Samcheok Hwanseongul Cave (800 Hwanseon-ro, Singi-myeon, Samcheok / 삼척시 신기면 환선로 800, ☎ 1533-8362) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, Korea's largest limestone cave, formed about 530 million years ago. 47 species of animals have been found inside the cave. A monorail runs up to the entrance.

  • Opening hours: Months 3~10 09:00~17:00, months 11~2 09:00~16:00. Closed days: 18th of every month (next weekday if it falls on a holiday or long holiday).
  • Fee (individual): Adult 4,500 won, senior and youth 3,000 won, child and soldier 2,000 won. Monorail for ages 13 and over, round trip 7,000 won.

Seoraksan Towangseong Falls (Seoraksan-dong, Sokcho / 속초시 설악동, ☎ 033-801-0900) is a 3-tier waterfall with a 150m upper tier, 80m middle tier and 90m lower tier, 320m in total. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is Korea's longest cascading waterfall, and in winter it is known as Korea's largest ice waterfall. In 2015 the Biryong Falls (비룡폭포) trail was extended and opened up to the observation deck, which stands more than 1㎞ from the falls.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Yangpyeong Yongmunsa Ginkgo Tree (782 Yongmunsan-ro, Yongmun-myeon, Yangpyeong / 양평군 용문면 용문산로 782, ☎ 031-773-0088) is a natural monument estimated to be about 1,100 years old. It is 42.0m tall with a trunk circumference of 11.0m at breast height, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it holds the record for the oldest and tallest among Korea's ginkgo trees.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Small car 3,000 won.

Balwangsan Tourist Cable Car (715 Olympic-ro, Daegwallyeong-myeon, Pyeongchang, Dragon Plaza 2nd floor / 평창군 대관령면 올림픽로 715 드래곤프라자 2층) is a 7.4㎞ round-trip cable car with 100 eight-person cabins in a loop. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is Korea's longest (this value is a round-trip figure, and the vote's recommendation reason gives 3.7㎞). It takes 18 minutes to reach the summit of Balwangsan (발왕산), 1,458m above sea level. It stops in bad weather such as strong wind.

  • Opening hours, closed days, fee: No registered value.

📍 'Ends and Beginnings' — Ganjeolgot, Marado and Jeongdongjin Station

Ulsan Ganjeolgot (39-2 Ganjeolgot 1-gil, Seosaeng-myeon, Ulju-gun / 울주군 서생면 간절곶1길 39-2, ☎ 052-204-1751~6) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the place where the sun rises first on the Korean Peninsula, with sunrise about 1 minute earlier than at Pohang's Homigot (호미곶) and about 5 minutes earlier than at Gangneung's Jeongdongjin (정동진). According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it also has the world's largest wish postbox, and postcards dropped in it are actually delivered.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Jeju Marado (46 Mara-ro 101beon-gil, Daejeong-eup, Seogwipo / 서귀포시 대정읍 마라로101번길 46, ☎ 064-760-4014) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the southernmost island of Korea. It takes about 30 minutes by ferry from Unjin Port (운진항), and there is a Southernmost Point Monument (최남단비) at the southern tip of the island. Walking around the island takes 1~2 hours.

  • Opening hours: Always open (check ferry times separately). Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free (ferry fare separate).

Gangneung Jeongdongjin Station (17 Jeongdongyeok-gil, Gangdong-myeon, Gangneung / 강릉시 강동면 정동역길 17, ☎ Mosigye Tourist Information Center 033-640-4536) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the station closest to the sea in the country. It got its name because it lies due east of Gwanghwamun (광화문) at Gyeongbokgung, and it became a sunrise spot through the 1995 drama 'Mosigye' (Sandglass, 모래시계).

  • Opening hours: Registered as varying by line. Closed days: Open year-round.

📍 'World' Records — Manjanggul Cave, Upo Wetland and Space Walk

Jeju Manjanggul Cave (182 Manjanggul-gil, Gujwa-eup, Jeju / 제주시 구좌읍 만장굴길 182, ☎ 064-710-7903) is a lava tube about 7,400m long in total, part of the Jeju World Natural Heritage site. The general public enters through Entrance 2 and can see only 1㎞. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, the lava column about 7.6m tall at the end of the open section is known as the largest in the world.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00 (last entry 17:00). Closed days: First Wednesday of every month.
  • Fee (individual): Adult 4,000 won, youth, child and soldier 2,000 won.

Changnyeong Upo Wetland (220 Upoganeup-gil, Yueo-myeon, Changnyeong / 창녕군 유어면 우포늪길 220, ☎ 055-530-2121) is a wetland with an area of 2,505 thousand ㎡. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, on October 25, 2018, at the 13th Conference of the Parties to the Ramsar Convention, it became the world's first to be accredited as a Ramsar Wetland City. The wetland is introduced as having begun to form 140 million years ago.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

Pohang Hwanho Park Space Walk (30 Hwanhogongwon-gil, Buk-gu, Pohang / 포항시 북구 환호공원길 30, ☎ 054-270-5176) is a participatory artwork with a 333m track and 717 steps. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it was once selected as one of the world's 19 most beautiful stairways. Access is closed in rain or strong wind.

  • Opening hours: Months 4~10 weekdays 10:00~20:00, weekends 10:00~21:00; months 11~3 weekdays 10:00~17:00, weekends 10:00~18:00.
  • Closed days: First Monday of every month (the next day if it is a public holiday), and during bad weather or maintenance. Fee: Free.

⚠ 3 Things to Know in Advance

  1. Records follow the Korea Tourism Organization descriptions — "first", "largest" and "most" are the wording copied from the Korea Tourism Organization descriptions, and other sources may say it differently.
  2. Some places have unusual closed days — Manjanggul Cave closes on the first Wednesday of every month, Hwanseongul Cave on the 18th of every month, Space Walk on the first Monday of every month, and Suncheonman Bay National Garden on the last Monday of every month.
  3. Some places close depending on the weather — The Balwangsan cable car and Space Walk do not operate in bad weather such as strong wind, and for Marado you need to check the ferry times separately.

⚙ FAQ 3

Q. Which of these places can I see in Seoul?

A. Seoul has Gwangjang Market. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it is Korea's first permanent market, opened in 1905. In the Seoul metropolitan area there are also Suwon Hwaseong Haenggung and the Yangpyeong Yongmunsa Ginkgo Tree in Gyeonggi.

Q. Which places cost money?

A. The places with a registered entrance fee are Suncheonman Bay National Garden (adult 10,000 won), Hwanseongul Cave (adult 4,500 won), Manjanggul Cave (adult 4,000 won), Hwaseong Haenggung (adult 2,000 won) and Sosu Seowon (adult 2,000 won). Marado itself is free, but the ferry fare is separate, and the Balwangsan Tourist Cable Car has no registered fare value, so check it separately.

Q. Which places are famous for sunrise?

A. Ganjeolgot and Jeongdongjin Station. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, Ganjeolgot is the place where the sun rises first on the Korean Peninsula, with sunrise about 5 minutes earlier than at Jeongdongjin.
