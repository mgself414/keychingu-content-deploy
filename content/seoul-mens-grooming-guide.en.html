Seoul Men's Beauty and Grooming Guide — the Men's Brow Bar at Olive Young N Seongsu, Skin Diagnosis at Central Gangnam Town, Skin Diagnosis at Beauty Play, Beauty Counseling at Sulwhasoo, and a Barbershop You Can Book in English: 10 Places in Seoul

Subtitle: 9 Seoul places picked in the "Glow Up, Men's Beauty" theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" public vote + 1 barbershop you can book on an English website · Olive Young stores stay open past 10 p.m., and the Men's Brow Bar and skin consulting at Olive Young N Seongsu are on-site reservations at the store · Beauty Play is closed on Sundays, and the Sulwhasoo Dosan boutique is closed on Sundays and Mondays — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI) and each company's official website

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide bundles three things a male traveler in Seoul can do in half a day: buying men's skincare and grooming products, checking skin condition with a device, and getting a haircut at a barbershop.

The places were picked from the "Glow Up, Men's Beauty" (글로우 업, 맨스 뷰티) theme of the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and Korea Tourism Organization "Attraction Discovery 100×100" (명소발굴 100×100) (candidates recommended by travel experts, voted on by the public). The ranks and vote counts in the table are the results of that vote. Of the 100 places in this theme, 56 are in Seoul, and more than half of them are clothing and eyewear select shops, so only the places directly connected to beauty and grooming were kept. Among the Seoul barbershops in the vote, none had an English booking route that could be found on an official page, so 1 place outside the vote was added.

The operating details were copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI) and each company's official website, and a source is attached to every place. Service details come from texts published directly by the operators, such as the official Olive Young (올리브영) store pages, the CJ Olive Young (CJ올리브영) newsroom, and the official Amorepacific (아모레퍼시픽) and Sulwhasoo (설화수) websites.

How to choose an Olive Young store, sale cycles and tax refunds are covered separately in #47 Olive Young Real-Purchase Guide. For Korean hair salon and barbershop culture in general, see #1180 Korean Hair Salon and Hair Culture. This guide focuses only on places with men's shelves and men's services.

📍 Checking Your Skin and Shaping Your Brows — Olive Young N Seongsu, Central Gangnam Town, Beauty Play and Sulwhasoo

Olive Young N Seongsu (올리브영N 성수) (13 Yeonmujang 7-gil, Seongdong-gu / 성동구 연무장7길 13, Factorial Seongsu (팩토리얼 성수), 100m walk from Exit 4 of Seongsu Station (성수역), ☎ 02-497-6589) is an Olive Young store with services made specifically for men. On the reservation tab of the official Olive Young store page there is the "Men's Brow Bar" (맨즈 브로우 바) (15 minutes). It is a service where a beauty consultant helps you shape men's brows that suit your impression. The same tab also has "Skin/Scalp Consulting" (스킨/두피 컨설팅) (15 minutes), where you discuss skin and scalp concerns using professional diagnostic devices, and "Find Your Color" (파인 유어 컬러) (30 minutes), which diagnoses your personal color.

These three are on-site reservations taken on the 2nd and 3rd floors of the store. Longer services such as a home-care lesson are advance reservations made through Naver Reservation (네이버 예약), and next week's reservations open every Friday at 12 noon. According to a November 2025 CJ Olive Young newsroom announcement, 87% of the users of skin diagnosis consultations were foreigners.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~22:00 — Olive Young store page · TourAPI. Open days: Monday~Sunday — TourAPI.
  • Men's Brow Bar and Skin/Scalp Consulting: On-site reservation (2nd and 3rd floors) — Olive Young store page, reservation tab.

Olive Young Central Gangnam Town (올리브영 센트럴 강남 타운) (403 Gangnam-daero, Seocho-gu / 서초구 강남대로 403, B1 to 4th floor, about 1 minute on foot from Exit 10 of Gangnam Station (강남역), ☎ 02-591-6308) offers skin type diagnosis, scalp type diagnosis, skin/scalp consulting and personal color diagnosis one-on-one with a professional consultant, according to the official Olive Young store page. The page also says a carrier storage service is available, which makes it a good stop on check-out day.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~22:30 — Olive Young store page · TourAPI. Open days: Monday~Sunday. Parking: Not available — TourAPI.

Beauty Play Myeong-dong (뷰티플레이 명동) (73 Myeongdong-gil, Jung-gu / 중구 명동길 73, 3rd floor, ☎ 070-4114-5801) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization description, a K-beauty experience and promotion hall supported by the Ministry of Health and Welfare (보건복지부) and run by the Korea Cosmetic Industry Institute (대한화장품산업연구원). Anyone can enter for free, and you can receive either a partial makeup session or a skin diagnosis from an expert. A personal color experience is also available. The displayed products are from small and mid-sized Korean cosmetics companies and change every two months.

The official website says there is no separate reservation for beauty experiences. You just register on site.

  • Opening hours: Mon~Sat 10:00~19:00, beauty experiences 10:00~18:00. Closed days: Sunday — Beauty Play official website.
  • Fee: Free admission, 1 experience of either a skin diagnosis or a partial makeup session — TourAPI description.

Sulwhasoo Dosan Flagship Store (설화수 도산 플래그십 스토어) (18 Dosan-daero 45-gil, Gangnam-gu / 강남구 도산대로45길 18, ☎ 02-541-9270) is the flagship store of Sulwhasoo, which uses ginseng and herbal ingredients. According to the official Amorepacific website, at the store's "Boutique & Gift Service" (부티크&기프트 서비스) you can receive beauty counseling while trying all Sulwhasoo products. There is the Sulwhasoo Spa (설화수 스파) on the B1 floor and the Sulwhasoo Balance Spa (설화수 밸런스 스파) on the 4th floor. The Sulwhasoo website also has an English screen.

  • Boutique: 10:00~19:00. Closed every Sunday and Monday, January 1, and on the days of Seollal (설) and Chuseok (추석) — Sulwhasoo official website.
  • Spa: Daily 10:00~20:00. Closed on the first Monday of every month, January 1, and on the days of Seollal and Chuseok. Reservations ☎ Sulwhasoo Spa 02-541-9272, Balance Spa 02-541-9273 — Sulwhasoo official website.

Olive Young SkinScan (스킨스캔): According to the CJ Olive Young newsroom (December 2025), SkinScan is a service where you measure 6 items yourself, including skin type, pigmentation, sebum, pores and wrinkles, with an AI-based device. It operates at about 60 major stores nationwide, and the results can be viewed again in the Olive Young app. These measurements are for choosing cosmetics. They are not a medical diagnosis.

📍 Shopping with Men's Shelves — Olive Young Hongdae Playground, Myeong-dong and Gangnam

Olive Young Hongdae Playground (올리브영 홍대놀이터점) (29 Wausan-ro 21-gil, Mapo-gu / 마포구 와우산로21길 29, 1st to 3rd floors, ☎ 02-3141-7679) has a men's grooming and wellness zone, Men's Edit (맨즈 에디트), on the 1st floor. According to the store-opening announcement in the CJ Olive Young newsroom in June 2025, Men's Edit was placed on the 1st floor together with the sportswear brand HDEX, and there is also a "Men's Solution" (맨즈 솔루션) area where you can try styling products yourself. According to the same announcement, there are beauty advisors who speak English and Chinese, and a separate checkout counter for foreigners.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~23:00. Open days: Monday~Sunday. Parking: Not available — TourAPI.

Olive Young Myeongdong Town (올리브영 명동 타운점) (53 Myeongdong-gil, Jung-gu / 중구 명동길 53, ☎ 02-736-5290) tied for 1st place in this vote theme with 44 votes, together with Dongdaemun General Market (동대문종합시장). The recommendation reason in the vote is "offers a variety of men's beauty items, from skincare to grooming products, in one place".

  • Opening hours: 10:00~22:30. Open days: Monday~Sunday. Parking: Not available — TourAPI.

Olive Young Gangnam Town (올리브영 강남 타운) (429 Gangnam-daero, Seocho-gu / 서초구 강남대로 429, ☎ 02-532-7444) is on the same Gangnam-daero road as Central Gangnam Town. The recommendation reason in the vote is the "K-Beauty Now Zone" (K-뷰티 나우존), which gathers new products.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~22:30. Open days: Monday~Sunday. Parking: Available — TourAPI.

📍 Matching Your Clothes Too — Musinsa Standard Seongsu and The Hyundai Seoul

Musinsa Standard Seongsu (무신사 스탠다드 성수) (83 Yeonmujang-gil, Seongdong-gu / 성동구 연무장길 83, 1st to 2nd floors, ☎ 0507-872-3708) is a store of the basic clothing brand of the online fashion platform Musinsa (무신사). According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, it has fitting rooms and self-checkout counters. It is in the same Yeonmujang-gil (연무장길) area as Olive Young N Seongsu, so it is easy to combine into one route. The recommendation reason in the vote is "trendy men's fashion and beauty items".

  • Opening hours: 11:00~22:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Not available — TourAPI.

The Hyundai Seoul (더현대 서울) (108 Yeoui-daero, Yeongdeungpo-gu / 영등포구 여의대로 108, ☎ 02-767-2233) is the Yeouido (여의도) branch of Hyundai Department Store (현대백화점). It was listed in the vote as the "Men's Hall" (남성관), and the recommendation reason is "everything for men, from overseas luxury to trendy domestic brands".

  • Opening hours: 10:30~20:00. Closed days: One Monday a month. Parking: Available — TourAPI.

📍 Barbershop — LEKKER (Itaewon, Hongdae, Gangnam)

LEKKER is a barbershop with branches in Itaewon (17 Itaewon-ro 20ga-gil, Yongsan-gu / 용산구 이태원로20가길 17), Hongdae (74 Wausan-ro 22-gil, Mapo-gu / 마포구 와우산로22길 74) and Gangnam (25 Nonhyeon-ro 159-gil, Gangnam-gu / 강남구 논현로159길 25). There is one reason it was added from outside the vote. The official website (lkkr.kr) has an English menu, and the reservation page links straight to the Fresha (online booking service) booking screen of each of the three branches. The shop description says it offers men's grooming based on experience built up in Europe and the UK.

  • Opening hours (same at all three branches): Mon~Thu and Sun 10:00~20:00, Fri and Sat 10:00~21:00. Closed days: No weekday closures (per the Fresha weekday table) — each branch's Fresha booking page.
  • Prices (Itaewon): Hair cut and style from 54,000 won (30~45 minutes), student cut from 49,000 won — Fresha booking page. Beard trim, hot towel and down perm (다운펌) menus are also on the official website price list.
  • Contact: +82 10 2599 7229 (10:00~18:00) — official website.

The basics, such as Korean men's cut styles, the down perm and the no-tipping culture, are in #1180 Korean Hair Salon and Hair Culture.

⚠ 3 Things to Know in Advance

  1. Go to the Olive Young N Seongsu experiences at opening time — The Men's Brow Bar and skin/scalp consulting are on-site reservations at the store. The store opens at 10 a.m.
  2. The measurements are for cosmetics recommendations — Device measurements and consulting are used to choose suitable products. If you have a serious skin concern, it is right to look for a medical institution separately.
  3. Amore Seongsu is relocating — According to the Amorepacific global website, the current location closes on November 29, 2026 and reopens on Yeonmujang-gil in late January 2027.

⚙ FAQ 3

Q. Can I check my skin condition without spending money?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization description, anyone can visit Beauty Play Myeong-dong (뷰티플레이 명동) for free and receive either a skin diagnosis or a partial makeup session. You register on site with no reservation. It is closed on Sundays.

Q. Where is a place with only men's products?

A. It is Men's Edit (맨즈 에디트) on the 1st floor of Olive Young Hongdae Playground (올리브영 홍대놀이터점). According to the June 2025 CJ Olive Young newsroom announcement, it is a men's grooming and wellness zone, and the store is open until 11 p.m.

Q. Can I book a barbershop on an English screen?

A. LEKKER has an English menu on its official website, and it leads to the Fresha booking screen of each of the Itaewon, Hongdae and Gangnam branches. A hair cut and style at the Itaewon branch starts from 54,000 won.

🧳 Outside Seoul for Reference — 2 Places in Haeundae, Busan

Centum City Spa Land (센텀시티 스파랜드) (35 Centum Nam-daero, Haeundae-gu, Busan / 해운대구 센텀남대로 35, ☎ 1668-2850) has hot spring baths with 2 kinds of natural hot spring water and themed jjimjilbang (찜질방) saunas. 09:00~22:00 (last entry 21:00), open year-round, adult 26,000 won — TourAPI.

Club D Oasis (클럽디 오아시스) (30 Dalmaji-gil, Haeundae-gu, Busan / 해운대구 달맞이길 30, LCT (엘시티), ☎ 1566-8007) has a water park, saunas and a spa in one building. 10:00~22:00, closed every Tuesday, and the fee is registered as varying by season — TourAPI.
