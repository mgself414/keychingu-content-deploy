⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Drama stages = all the same area": Each drama has distinct areas:

  • Youth Romance = Yonsei University (연세대) · Korea University (고려대) · Hongdae (홍대) · Itaewon (이태원)
  • Office Workers = Noryangjin (노량진) · Jongno (종로) · Yeouido (여의도)
  • Fantasy · Luxury = DDP (Dongdaemun Design Plaza) · Lotte Tower (롯데타워) · Cheongdam-dong (청담동)

2. "Filming locations = unrecognizable even if I go": KeyChinGu (키친구) guides clearly mark K-drama stages + Instagram hotspots. #181 Noryangjin Gosichon (노량진 고시촌) = a frequent spot for Misaeng (미생) and Itaewon Class (이태원 클라쓰) is clearly indicated.

3. "K-drama fans = only Japan/China": International K-drama fan base is global — USA, Europe, Middle East, South America. Increasing annually due to Netflix.
