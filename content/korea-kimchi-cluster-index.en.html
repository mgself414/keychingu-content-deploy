⚠ Medical/Safety/Dietary disclaimer

Kimchi = Fermented food (salt, spicy). Ramen, stew, soup = processing, oil. Combined salt = daily recommendations may be exceeded. Pregnant women, high blood pressure, kidney patients = Be careful of frequency. Allergy full set: salted shrimp, anchovy fish sauce, crustaceans, garlic, red pepper powder, pork, beef, soybeans, wheat flour — package label + prior notification required by restaurants. In case of food poisoning or stomachache ☎ 1339.

Full set of dietary restrictions fallback:

  • Halal/Kosher: Regular kimchi = non-halal (shrimp salt standard). → Temple food (#800) = Vegetarian, Halal, Kosher OK
  • Vegetarian/Vegan: Regular kimchi = non-vegetarian (fish sauce). → Temple Food (#800) or Vegan Kimchi (soy sauce-based)
  • Gluten free: Kimchi itself = OK (soy sauce
