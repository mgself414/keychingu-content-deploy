Foreigner's Guide to Hampyeong Butterfly Festival — Korea's #1 Butterfly & Insect Festival in Jeollanam-do Matrix

> Subtitle: Hampyeong Butterfly Festival (April-May, Est. 1999) for Foreign Tourists · Korea's #1 Butterfly & Insect Festival · Jeollanam-do Matrix + Family Enthusiasts

ISSUE 04 · #1063 · 2026-06-13

🎯 Why a Hampyeong Butterfly Festival Guide?

The Hampyeong Butterfly Festival (함평 나비축제) is Korea's #1 butterfly and insect festival, boasting 25+ years of tradition since 1999, held annually for about 10 days from late April to early May in Hampyeong-gun (함평군), Jeollanam-do (전라남도). Foreign visitors often have pain points: (a) the misconception that "Hampyeong (함평) is unknown," (b) unawareness that it's Korea's #1 butterfly and insect matrix, attracting 300,000+ family enthusiasts annually, (c) its status as a matrix of butterflies, insects, flowers, and nature, making it #1 for families and children, (d) the availability of signature Jeollanam-do (전라남도) cuisine and hanjeongsik (한정식, Korean full-course meal), and (e) its appeal to MZ (Millennials and Gen Z) generations, families, and Instagram enthusiasts.

This guide covers 5 pillars, 12 signatures + the Butterfly Festival + family enthusiasts + English for foreigners.
