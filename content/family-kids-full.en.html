① Theme Parks / Amusement Parks (2)

1. Lotte World (롯데월드) + Lotte Tower (롯데타워) (Jamsil (잠실)) ★ Korea's No. 1 Theme Park

  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Korea's No. 1 Priority for Families & Kids
  • Signature: Adventure (어드벤처) + Magic Island (매직아일랜드) + Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) + Lotte Tower (롯데타워) Seoul Sky + Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) (#82)
  • Admission: All-day pass ₩66,000 (~$48 / ~350 RMB / ~6,800 JPY) + Night pass ₩47,000

2. Children's Grand Park (어린이대공원) (Gwangjin (광진)) ★ Korea's No. 1 Free Urban Zoo

  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Established 1973, Korea's No. 1 free urban zoo, amusement park, and botanic garden (approx. 560,000 pyeong / 460 acres)
  • Signature: Zoo (Free) + Amusement Park + Botanic Garden
  • Admission: Free + Zoo operating hours 10:00-18:00 (10 AM - 6 PM)
  • Details: #178 Gwangjin (광진) / #205 Parks (공원)
