Why Line 7 — The Olive Line Traversing MZ, Luxury, and Nature

Line 7 (olive) is a line that traverses from Seoul's northeastern outskirts down to Gangnam. Starting from the hiking trails of Dobongsan·Bukhansan (북한산), through the MZ commercial district around the Konkuk Univ. area (건대입구), the Cheongdam-dong (청담동) luxury street and K-star road, the fine dining of Gangnam-gu Office·Nonhyeon (논현), all the way to Banpo Hangang — it links nature·youth·luxury in a single line. It carries a different character from the earlier lines (downtown·tradition·shopping).

This guide organizes Line 7's 8 key tourist stations station by station. It pairs the attractions of Konkuk·Cheongdam (linked to existing deep dives) with restaurants selected by nearest-neighbor mapping per station from accumulated data. It is the sixth installment of the subway line-by-line series, and the edition completing Lines 1–7.

🫒 #1. Konkuk Univ. Station (건대입구역) — MZ Commercial District·Common Ground

  • 🚪 Exits: Common Ground (커먼그라운드) · Konkuk food alley (건대 먹자골목) · toward Rodeo (로데오)
  • 🍜 Eat: multinational food at Konkuk food alley · Jangmisulsang (장미술상, cooking pub) · Sedici (세디치, Italian) · Dowter (도우터, brunch)
  • 👀 Play: Common Ground (container shopping mall) · Konkuk Rodeo · Ttukseom Hangang Park (뚝섬한강공원) (#586) · Konkuk Star City (건대 스타시티)
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 2
  • cross-ref: #1241 Line 2 (Konkuk) · #586 Hangang (Ttukseom)

🫒 #2. Children's Grand Park Station (어린이대공원역) — Family·Zoo

  • 🚪 Exits: toward the Children's Grand Park (어린이대공원) main gate
  • 🍜 Eat: cafes near Children's Grand Park (Cafe Long (카페롱), etc.) · food toward Konkuk
  • 👀 Play: Children's Grand Park (free zoo·amusement park) · Seoul Sangsangnara (서울상상나라) (children's experience·#1240) · Neung-dong (능동) forest
  • 🔁 Transfer: none
  • cross-ref: #1240 indoor family (Seoul Sangsangnara) · #232 family & children

🫒 #3. Cheongdam Station (청담역) — Luxury·K-star

  • 🚪 Exits: Cheongdam-dong luxury street · toward Galleria (갤러리아)
  • 🍜 Eat: Cheongdam fine dining · Gangjeongi Neomchineun Jip (강정이넘치는집) · Cheongdam dessert·wine bars
  • 👀 Play: Cheongdam-dong luxury street (flagships·boutiques) · K-star road (entertainment·goods) · Galleria luxury hall (갤러리아 명품관) · SM Town (SM타운) (toward COEX (코엑스))
  • 🔁 Transfer: none (Cheongdam). Adjacent to Apgujeongrodeo (압구정로데오) (Bundang Line)
  • cross-ref: #387 K-pop agencies · #566 luxury watches

🫒 #4. Gangnam-gu Office Station (강남구청역) — Fine Dining·Near Apgujeong

  • 🚪 Exits: toward Apgujeong (압구정) · toward Hakdong-ro (학동로)
  • 🍜 Eat: Mingles (밍글스) (Michelin-star Korean) · Gongli (공리, Chinese) · Gangnam-gu Office·Apgujeong fine dining

  • cross-ref: #1242 Line 3 (Apgujeong) · #1236 summer outdoor bars (Apgujeong)

  • 🔁 Transfer: none

  • 👀 Play: Express Bus Terminal underground mall (고속터미널 지하상가) (fashion shopping) · Shinsegae Gangnam · Banpo Hangang Park (반포 한강공원) (Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (달빛무지개분수)·#586)

🫒 #7. Banpo Station (반포역) — Seorae Village·Hangang

🫒 #8. Dobongsan Station (도봉산역) — Hiking·Nature
