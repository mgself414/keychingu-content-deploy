🎯 Why the Jeju Full Guide?

Jeju (제주) = Korea's #1 resort island + UNESCO World Natural Heritage (2007). It's the second most visited city by international tourists (after Busan) + a full 1-day to 1-week package for international enthusiasts. A flight from Gimpo (김포) to Jeju (제주) on an LCC (Low-Cost Carrier) costs ₩30,000-₩80,000 (~$22-60 / ~150-400 RMB / ~3,200-8,500 JPY) and takes 30 minutes.
