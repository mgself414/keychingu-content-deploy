Seoul Department Stores, Malls and Outlets — 12 Spots Including Lotte Department Store Main Store, Shinsegae Main Store, The Hyundai Seoul, Starfield COEX Mall and Yeoju Premium Outlets (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: Lotte and Shinsegae main stores 10:30–20:00, closed one day a month — the date differs by store and by month, so check the official page for the announcement (each official page) · Starfield COEX Mall and IFC Mall 10:00–22:00, open every day (VisitKorea) · Yeoju Outlets switch to winter hours from November (VisitKorea) — as of 2026-10-21

ISSUE 03 · #449 · 2026-05-26

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Push through the revolving door and the first thing that hits you is perfume on the ground floor, and the smell of baking bread in the basement. This guide lists 12 department stores, malls and outlets in and around Seoul, branch by branch. Addresses, hours, closing days and foreign-visitor services use only values read directly today from VisitKorea tourism information (TourAPI) and each company's official store page. Duty-free shops go to the #915 Duty-Free Guide, and marts and convenience stores go to the #916 Mart and Convenience Store Guide.

The old guide called the Yeoju and Siheung outlets "Hyundai Outlets," but the official name of both listed on VisitKorea is Shinsegae Simon (신세계사이먼) Premium Outlets. Lines such as "Korea's best department store" and "luxury goods 30–70% off" had no source, so they were removed.

🏬 Department Stores — 4 Spots

  • Lotte Department Store Main Store (롯데백화점 본점) — 81 Namdaemun-ro (남대문로), Jung-gu (중구), Seoul (VisitKorea). Regular hours 10:30–20:00, extended hours 10:30–20:30, restaurant floors 10:30–21:00. Closed one day a month (October 12 and November 9, 2026 announced — both are the second Monday). Check the official page for later dates. Avenuel (에비뉴엘) keeps the same hours as the department store. Main phone 1577-0001 (Lotte Department Store official store page). An automatic currency exchange machine sits next to the information desk on basement level 1 of the main building and Avenuel (Lotte Department Store English official site). Refund method: immediate + post-purchase (VisitKorea tourism information)
  • Shinsegae Department Store Main Store (신세계백화점 본점) — 63 Sogong-ro (소공로), Jung-gu (중구), Seoul. The old building opened on October 24, 1930 (VisitKorea). Regular hours 10:30–20:00, extended hours 10:30–20:30, restaurant floors 11:00–21:30, closed one day a month (October 2026 announced for Sunday the 25th — the weekday is not fixed). Check the official page for that month's date before you go. Main phone 1588-1234 (Shinsegae Department Store official store page). Floors 8–12 of the same building are Shinsegae Duty Free Myeongdong (신세계면세점 명동점) (Shinsegae Duty Free official site)
  • The Hyundai Seoul (더현대 서울) — 108 Yeoui-daero (여의대로), Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구), Seoul. Opened on February 26, 2021. Mon–Thu 10:30–20:00, Fri–Sun 10:30–20:30, restaurant floors 10:30–22:00. Closing days differ each month, so check the website. An indoor garden on the 5th floor, "Sounds Forest (사운즈 포레스트)," and an artificial waterfall on the 1st floor, "Waterfall Garden (워터폴 가든)." Phone 1588-3650 (VisitKorea)
  • Galleria Department Store Luxury Hall (갤러리아백화점 명품관) — 343 Apgujeong-ro (압구정로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. Two halls, EAST and WEST, face each other across one street. Closed once a month, phone 1544-6600 (VisitKorea). Today's hours 10:30–20:00 (Galleria official site, viewed 2026-10-21). WEST hall refund method: immediate + post-purchase (VisitKorea tourism information)

🛍 Malls — 4 Spots

  • Starfield COEX Mall (스타필드 코엑스몰) — 513 Yeongdong-daero (영동대로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul. 10:00–22:00, open every day. Phone 02-6002-5300. About 320 shops plus about 100 restaurants and cafes, with the "Starfield Library (별마당 도서관)" in the middle (VisitKorea)
  • Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) — 300 Olympic-ro (올림픽로), Songpa-gu (송파구), Seoul. Hours and closing days differ by shop. Information 02-3213-5000 (VisitKorea). Lotte Duty Free World Tower (롯데면세점 월드타워점) in the same complex is open 10:30–19:30 (Lotte Duty Free official site), and Lotte Mart World Tower (롯데마트 월드타워점) is open 10:00–23:00 (Lotte Mart official store locator)
  • IFC Mall (IFC몰) — 10 Gukjegeumyung-ro (국제금융로), Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구), Seoul. 10:00–22:00, open every day. Phone 02-6137-5000 (VisitKorea). It is in the same Yeouido (여의도) district as The Hyundai Seoul, so the two are easy to combine in one day
  • Starfield Hanam (스타필드 하남) — 750 Misa-daero (미사대로), Hanam-si (하남시), Gyeonggi (경기). 10:00–22:00, open every day. Phone 1833-9001 (VisitKorea). Shinsegae Department Store, E-mart Traders (이마트 트레이더스) and Aqua Field (아쿠아필드) are all in one building (VisitKorea description)

🏷 Outlets — 4 Spots

  • Lotte Outlet Seoul Station (롯데아울렛 서울역점) — 405 Hangang-daero (한강대로), Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seoul. 10:30–21:00, closed January 1 and the Korean holidays (명절, Lunar New Year and Chuseok). Payment by cash, credit card, Samsung Pay, KakaoPay, Alipay, WeChat Pay and Apple Pay; refund immediate + post-purchase (VisitKorea tourism information). English and Chinese shopping guidance at the "Global Service Room" on the 2nd floor, and an automatic currency exchange machine at the 2nd-floor main entrance (next to Nike) (Lotte Department Store English official site). It is attached to Seoul Station (서울역), so it works well for the day before you fly out
  • Shinsegae Simon Yeoju Premium Outlets (신세계사이먼 여주 프리미엄 아울렛) — 360 Myeongpum-ro (명품로), Yeoju-si (여주시), Gyeonggi (경기). Opened on June 28, 2007. Summer season (May–October) general shops 10:30–21:00 / winter season (November–April) Mon–Thu 10:30–20:30, Fri–Sun and public holidays 10:30–21:00, restaurant floors 11:00–21:00. Closed on the day of Lunar New Year and Chuseok. Phone 1644-4001 (VisitKorea)
  • Shinsegae Simon Paju Premium Outlets (신세계사이먼 파주 프리미엄 아울렛) — 200 Pilseung-ro (필승로), Tanhyeon-myeon (탄현면), Paju-si (파주시), Gyeonggi (경기). Summer season 10:30–21:00 / winter season Mon–Thu 10:30–20:30, Fri–Sun 10:30–21:00. Check the website for closing days. Phone 1644-4001 (VisitKorea)
  • Lotte Premium Outlets Paju (롯데프리미엄아울렛 파주점) — 390 Hoedong-gil (회동길), Paju-si (파주시), Gyeonggi (경기). Opened on December 2, 2011. 10:30–21:00, open every day. Lotte Cinema (롯데시네마) on the 4th floor (VisitKorea)

🥗 Diet — In Food Halls and Restaurant Floors

  • Vegetarian: VisitKorea says the easiest way to spot vegan products while shopping is to look for the "vegan certification logo." It also notes that kimchi seasoning mostly contains salted fermented seafood (jeotgal), fish sauce or meat broth (VisitKorea vegetarian travel tips). When buying side dishes (banchan) and kimchi in a food hall, check the ingredient label first
  • Halal: We could not confirm any halal-certified restaurant listing on the official pages of the 12 spots in this guide. VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (이태원) (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil (우사단로10길), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00–21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF, 한국이슬람교중앙회). Certification can change, so check before you visit
  • For show-and-tell cards, see the #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Shopping Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Refunds, Getting Around

  • Solo and reservations: You walk into the shops without a reservation. The Shinsegae Main Store restaurant floors take last orders 30 minutes before closing, and close early if ingredients run out (Shinsegae Department Store official store page)
  • Payment: Lotte Department Store accepts VISA, Mastercard, UnionPay, JCB and AMEX, plus Alipay, WeChat Pay, Apple Pay and LINE Pay (Lotte Department Store English official site). For cards and mobile payment in general, see the #527 Payment System
  • Tax refund: At Lotte Department Store, the purchase must exceed ₩15,000 (~$11), you must leave Korea within 3 months of the purchase date, and you must be a foreigner who has stayed in Korea less than 6 months. Passport and receipt are required (Lotte Department Store English official site). For the procedure, see the #1231 Tax Refund Guide and the #1304 Airport Kiosk Step-by-Step Guide
  • Luggage: Lotte Department Store offers same-day item storage. It does not accept valuables, cash, laptops, tablets or fragile items (Lotte Department Store English official site). For other options, see the #1232 Luggage Storage Guide
  • Getting around: For subways and buses, see #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Department stores open every day" — They close once a month, and the date differs by store and by month (October 2026 examples: Lotte Main Store on the 12th, Shinsegae Main Store on the 25th, each official page). Check that month's closing day before you go

2. "Yeoju Outlets is a Hyundai Outlet" — The name listed on VisitKorea is Shinsegae Simon Yeoju Premium Outlets

3. "Outlet hours are the same all year" — Shinsegae Simon Yeoju and Paju switch to the winter season from November, so Mon–Thu they close at 20:30 (VisitKorea)

4. "They close at 20:00 on weekends too" — Lotte Main Store and The Hyundai Seoul stay open until 20:30 Fri–Sun (official pages, VisitKorea)
