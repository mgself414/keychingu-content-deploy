⚠ 3 Common Shopping Mistakes

1. "Department stores are the same as in Japan/US" — Unaware of Korean food halls: Assuming they are identical. In reality, Korean department store food halls (Hyundai (현대)·Shinsegae (신세계) Apgujeong (압구정)·Main Branch (본점)) are Michelin-level clusters of hansik (한식, Korean food), desserts, Japanese, and Chinese cuisine (#1 for foreign enthusiasts).

2. "Duty-free shops are only at the airport" — Not utilizing city duty-free shops: Only aware of airport duty-free. In reality, city duty-free shops (Lotte (롯데)·Shilla (신라)·Shinsegae (신세계) Myeongdong (명동)·Jamsil (잠실)) allow shopping after arrival and pickup at Incheon Airport (인천공항). Exchange rates and discount rates are often better.

3. "Luxury goods are only in department stores" — Not utilizing outlets: Only considering department stores. In reality, Hyundai (현대)·Shinsegae (신세계) Siheung (시흥)·Yeoju (여주) Outlets (1-1.5h from Seoul) offer 30-70% discounts on luxury goods + #1 for value.
