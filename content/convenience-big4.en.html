Why Compare the Big 4 — One Every 100m, Yet All Different

Korea has 50,000+ convenience stores nationwide — in central Seoul, you'll find one every 100 meters. For foreign visitors, the convenience store is an all-purpose base covering meals, snacks, T-money top-ups, and even ATMs (#1171). And yet CU, GS25, 7-Eleven, and emart24 each have different strengths: CU if you're after trendy desserts, GS25 for collabs and premium eats, 7-Eleven for familiar global vibes, and emart24 for quiet value-for-money.

This guide answers the question "I know how to use them (#289) and I know the culture (#1171), but which of the two convenience stores in front of me should I walk into" — a brand-axis comparison. What to buy is covered by its pair, #1262.

The 4 Brand Profiles — Starting With a One-Line Identity

🟢 CU — Most Stores, King of Trendy Desserts

  • One line: Top-tier store count + buzzworthy dessert destination. The brand with the most frequent new-dessert "open runs".
  • Strengths: Trend desserts (Dubai chocolate spinoffs, hyperrealism desserts, and other SNS hits) · in-house snack lines · ready meals
  • PB: HEYROO (헤이루) · Deuktem (득템) series (ultra value-for-money)
  • App: Pocket CU (포켓CU) (promotions · stock lookup)
  • Foreign visitor fit: If you're hunting for "that dessert" you saw on TikTok or Instagram, this is your first stop.

🔵 GS25 — The Collab and Premium Food Powerhouse

  • One line: The strongest collab planner — the most active in variety show, chef, and K-pop tie-in products. Solid premium dosirak and sandwich lines.
  • Strengths: Buzzy collabs (Culinary Class Wars (흑백요리사) chef ready meals, etc.) · Kim Hye-ja dosirak (김혜자 도시락) · premium sandwiches · ground zero for drink-combo trends like Eolbaksa (얼박사)
  • PB: YOU US (유어스)
  • App: Woori Dongne GS (우리동네GS) (promotions · pickup)
  • Foreign visitor fit: If you want to experience "what's hot in Korea right now" in one shot.

🟠 7-Eleven — Global Familiarity, Strong Alcohol Selection

  • One line: A sign everyone knows worldwide, so the psychological barrier for a first visit is lowest. Many stores carry a good wine and whisky selection.
  • Strengths: Global familiarity · 7-SELECT PB · alcohol-specialty stores · the fun of a "Korean-style Seven" whose lineup differs from Japan's 7-Eleven
  • PB: 7-SELECT (세븐셀렉트)
  • Foreign visitor fit: The fun of comparing with the 7-Eleven back home — especially for travelers from Japan and Taiwan.

🟡 emart24 — Quiet Value, Mart Affiliation

  • One line: The emart-affiliated brand with a value-for-money, private mood. Stores are relatively uncrowded, good for browsing slowly.
  • Strengths: I'm e (아임e) PB value · stores carrying No Brand (노브랜드) products · trust of the emart group
  • PB: I'm e (아임e)
  • Foreign visitor fit: When crowded tourist-area convenience stores feel overwhelming, or for value-focused grocery runs.

🎯 What to Know Across All 4 — Before You Walk In

  • ➕ 1+1 / 2+1 promotions: Core Korean convenience store culture. Same product: 2 for the price of 1 (1+1), or 3 for the price of 2 (2+1). Marked on the price sticker — rotates monthly.
  • 💳 Payment: Credit cards and transit cards mostly OK. Foreign cards vary by store and terminal — carrying small cash backup is recommended. T-money top-ups are safest in cash.
  • 🍜 Eat-in space: Hot water dispenser (cup ramyeon), microwave, and eat-in tables are standard equipment. You can eat on the spot (#289).
  • 🕐 24 hours: Most are 24h, but some stores (inside buildings, etc.) close late at night — check the notice on the door.

Situational Picking Guide 3

Situation 1. "Going to buy that dessert I saw on TikTok"

  • CU first → if not there, GS25. Buzzy desserts and hyperrealism new releases appear most often at CU; blockbuster collabs at GS25.
  • For new releases, right after morning restocking gives the best odds (popular items sell out by afternoon).

Situation 2. "Dinner plus a can of beer"

  • GS25 (premium dosirak · sandwiches) or 7-Eleven (alcohol selection). Dosirak + a 4-can deal: GS25; whisky highball: 7-Eleven.

Situation 3. "Quiet grocery run near my accommodation"

  • emart24. Uncrowded stores + I'm e value. Best for bulk-buying water, cup ramyeon, and breakfast supplies.

5 Usage Tips — Getting 200% Out of All 4

Tip 1. Choose by strength, not by sign

Desserts = CU, collabs & dosirak = GS25, alcohol & familiarity = 7-Eleven, value & quiet = emart24. If you have a purpose, the brand is the answer.

Tip 2. 1+1 differs by brand

The same drink might be 2+1 at CU this month and full price at GS25. Check the app or the shelf sticker.

Tip 3. You don't need the apps, but stock lookup is powerful

If you're hunting buzzy new releases, Pocket CU's stock lookup is useful (may require a Korean app store account — if it doesn't work, head straight to the shelves).

Tip 4. Tourist-area stores and residential stores are different

Stores in Myeongdong (명동) and Hongdae (홍대) are tourist-oriented (souvenirs, near currency exchange); residential stores focus on fresh food and promotions. Even the same brand carries a different lineup.

Tip 5. A 4-brand conquest tour is a trip in itself

Buying the same category (samgak-gimbap (삼각김밥), PB snacks) at all four brands and comparing them makes a half-day course on its own — pair it with the #1262 roundup.

FAQ 6 — Convenience Store Brand Questions Foreign Visitors Ask Most

Q1. Which convenience store is the best?

  • It depends on your purpose — desserts = CU, collabs & dosirak = GS25, alcohol & familiarity = 7-Eleven, value = emart24. For an all-around safe pick, CU or GS25.

Q2. Are there convenience stores other than the big 4?

  • Regional chains exist (Storyway (스토리웨이), etc.), but along tourist routes the big 4 are effectively everything.

Q3. Does 1+1 just work for foreigners too?

  • Yes. No conditions — grab 2 of the same product and go to the register; it applies automatically (check the sticker).

Q4. Can I top up T-money anywhere?

  • All 4 brands do it at the counter — cash top-up is the standard (#289 · #237).

Q5. Is it the same as 7-Eleven in Japan?

  • Only the sign is the same; the lineup is Korean-style. Even the samgak-gimbap and dosirak compositions differ, which makes comparing them fun.

Q6. Are they all open in the middle of the night?

  • Most are 24h, but there are exceptions like in-building stores — late at night, main-street stores are the safe bet (#282 late-night guide).
