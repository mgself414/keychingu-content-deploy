Jongmyo Daeje Viewing Guide: Twice a Year, Chunhyang in May and Chuhyang in November, Free Entry to the 2026 Chuhyang Daeje on November 7, Plus Regular English Tour Times (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: The Jongmyo Jerye (종묘제례) rites are held on the first Sunday of May and the first Saturday of November each year (Korea Heritage Service Royal Palaces and Tombs Center) · the 2026 Chuhyang Daeje (추향대제) is Saturday, November 7: Yeongnyeongjeon (영녕전) 10:00 and Jeongjeon (정전) 14:00, free with no advance reservation (Jongmyo notice) · the May Daeje requires advance reservation on Ticketlink (Korea Heritage Agency, 국가유산진흥원) · on ordinary days Jongmyo closes on Tuesdays, with English tours at 10:00, 12:00, 14:00 and 16:00 (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). As of 2026-10-21

🎯 How to Use This Guide

On the stone terrace (wolldae, 월대) of Jeongjeon (정전), the pyeonjong (bronze bell chimes) and pyeongyeong (stone chimes) ring out, and rows of dancers slowly raise their arms: the Munmu (문무), honoring civil virtue, is quiet and gentle, while the Mumu (무무), honoring martial merit, is vigorous (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). For travelers who want to see Jongmyo Daeje (종묘대제), the Joseon royal family's ancestral rite, in person, this guide lists when it takes place, how to get in, and how to visit Jongmyo on ordinary days, using only values from official notices.

The old guide wrote that Jongmyo Daeje is held "on the first Sunday of May, once a year". The Royal Palaces and Tombs Center states that the Jongmyo Jerye is held on the first Sunday of May and the first Saturday of November every year, and the autumn rite of 2026 (Chuhyang Daeje) has been announced for November 7. Sentences such as "600 years of the Joseon royal house (from 1392)" and "Jeongjeon, 101 m long, the longest in the world" were also corrected: Jongmyo was founded in 1395 (the 4th year of King Taejo), and the length of Jeongjeon and any world ranking are not on official pages.

📅 The Two Daeje: 2026 Held and the Next Schedule

  • 2026 Chunhyang Jongmyo Daeje (Sunday, May 3): the order was 10:00 Yeongnyeongjeon (영녕전) rite → 11:00 royal procession (eoga haengnyeol, 어가행렬) departing Gwanghwamun (광화문), arriving at Jongmyo at 12:00 → 14:00 to 16:30 Jeongjeon rite → 16:30 to 17:30 viewing of the spirit chambers (Korea Heritage Agency event page). It rained, but it went ahead on the principle of running without cancellation in rain (Korea Heritage Agency notice, Policy Reporters article [secondary]). Result figures such as attendance could not be confirmed on official pages
  • 2026 Chuhyang Daeje (Saturday, November 7): Yeongnyeongjeon rite 10:00 to 12:00, Jeongjeon rite 14:00 to 16:30. General viewing, free with no advance reservation; there is no foreign-language commentary that day. Inquiries: Jongmyo Chuhyang Daeje Organizing Committee 02-765-2124 (Jongmyo notice, posted 2026-10-21)
  • 2027 schedule: both the spring and autumn rites are not yet announced. By custom they fall on the first Sunday of May and the first Saturday of November, but wait for the notices of the Korea Heritage Agency and the Royal Palaces and Tombs Center for dates and booking schedules
  • The rite resumed in 1969, and from 1971 the spring rite day became the first Sunday of May (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center, Jongmyo history)

🎟 May Daeje Booking and the Day Itself: Based on the 2026 Notice

  • Booking: free, on Ticketlink, up to 2 tickets per person. In 2026 it opened on April 17 at 14:00. The notice states the principle of "100% advance reservation, no on-site sales" (Korea Heritage Agency)
  • Age: only ages 7 and over enter the rite space. School-age children and teenagers must come with a guardian (Korea Heritage Agency)
  • Check-in: at the reservation check desk in front of Jongmyo's Oedaemun (외대문) gate, 13:00 to 13:40 for the Jeongjeon rite. Bring the booker's own original ID and the reservation number; for foreigners, a passport. Admission until 13:50 (Korea Heritage Agency)
  • Jongmyo is open free of charge that day (Korea Heritage Agency). You can enter the grounds even without a ticket
  • Photography: flashes, tripods and bringing in food are prohibited (Korea Heritage Agency)

🏛 Jongmyo on Ordinary Days: Seeing Jeongjeon and Yeongnyeongjeon

  • Jeongjeon and Yeongnyeongjeon: Jeongjeon has 19 chambers and Yeongnyeongjeon 16 (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). It became a World Heritage site in 1995. The Jongmyo Jerye and Jongmyo Jeryeak were proclaimed a "Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity" in 2001 and inscribed on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2008 (UNESCO)
  • Address and route: Jongno 157 (종로 157), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul. A 5-minute walk from Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역) exits 11 and 8. There is no dedicated parking lot (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center)
  • Hours: 09:00 to 17:30 in Nov to Jan, 09:00 to 18:00 in Feb to May and Sep to Oct, 09:00 to 18:30 in Jun to Aug, last admission 1 hour before closing. Closed Tuesdays (open if a public holiday, then closed on the next first weekday) (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center)
  • How to visit: on Mon, Thu and Fri it is a timed visit where you enter at set times and walk with a guide for about 50 minutes. English at 10:00, 12:00, 14:00 and 16:00; Japanese at 09:40, 11:40, 13:40 and 15:40; Chinese at 11:00 and 15:00. Parties of fewer than 10 buy on site without a reservation. On Sat, Sun, public holidays and Culture Day it is a free visit (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center)
  • Fee: foreigners aged 19 to 64: ₩1,000 (~$0.75). Free if you wear a complete hanbok (traditional Korean dress) with jeogori top and skirt or trousers (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center)
  • Autumn event period: during the Korea Grand Festival, 2026-10-29 to 11-15, Jongmyo admission is free and the number of guided tours is reduced: English 10:00 and 14:00 (Jongmyo notice)

🎶 Jongmyo Jeryeak: What You Will Hear

  • It is music with instruments, song and dance together. The instruments include pyeonjong (bronze bell chimes), pyeongyeong (stone chimes), chuk, eo, bak, ajaeng, janggu and jeolgo; the songs are 11 pieces of Botaepyeong (보태평) and 11 of Jeongdaeop (정대업), and the dance divides into Munmu and Mumu. It began to be used officially in 1463 (the 9th year of King Sejo) (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center)
  • During the rite, the pieces and dances change with each step: to welcome the spirits, Botaepyeong music and dance (보태평지악·보태평지무); for the second and third cup offerings, Jeongdaeop music and dance (정대업지악·정대업지무) (Korea Heritage Agency 2026 program)
  • Even outside the Daeje days, there is a spring Jongmyo Jeryeak night performance (20:00 to 21:00) (Korea Heritage Agency). If you want to hear more gugak (traditional Korean music), see #686 traditional music and gugak

🚶 Places to Combine with Jongmyo

  • Bukshinmun (북신문) gate toward Changgyeonggung (창경궁): on Sat, Sun, public holidays and Culture Day, the Bukshinmun gate on Jongmyo's Yulgok-ro (율곡로) side opens (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center)
  • Changdeokgung (창덕궁): Yulgok-ro 99 (율곡로 99), Jongno-gu, Seoul, closed Mondays, a 5-minute walk from Anguk Station (안국역) exit 3. The Rear Garden (후원) opens for online first-come booking from 10:00 six days ahead (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). A good fit for Tuesdays, when Jongmyo is closed
  • Sajikdan (사직단): the state altar paired with Jongmyo. Open at all times, free (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center)

🥗 Dietary Needs

  • The ritual food is not for visitors: the offerings of the Jongmyo Jerye include beef, mutton and pork, rice, sorghum and glutinous millet, rice cakes, salted fermented seafood and fruit (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). You cannot bring food into the event venue (Korea Heritage Agency)
  • Vegetarian: at nearby Korean restaurants, even the kimchi seasoning often contains salted fermented seafood, fish sauce or meat stock (VisitKorea)
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (Usadan-ro 10-gil 15 (우사단로10길 15), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00 to 21:00) as one of the few restaurants the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF, 한국이슬람교중앙회) recognizes as halal. Certification can change, so check before you visit
  • For a show-the-server card, see #1300 dietary request manual; for Jongno eats, #141 Jongno and Gwangjang Market guide

🧰 Jongmyo Daeje Practicalities: Alone, Reservations, Payment, Getting Around

  • Alone: single-person booking works (up to 2 tickets per person). On ordinary days you can also join the timed visit alone by buying on site (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center)
  • Reservations: for the May Daeje, do not miss the Ticketlink booking in mid-April. Inquiries: Royal Palaces and Tombs Center program phone line 1522-2295, tickets 1588-7890 (Korea Heritage Agency)
  • Wheelchairs: notify 1522-2295 up to 2 days before the event and you will be guided along a separate route (Korea Heritage Agency)
  • Payment: regular admission is ₩1,000 (~$0.75); for cards and mobile payments, see #527 Korean payment systems
  • Getting around: Jongno 3-ga Station is on Lines 1, 3 and 5: #1298 choosing among three transit cards; when you cannot make yourself understood, see #1339 ordering etiquette

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Jongmyo Daeje is once a year, only in May": it is also held on the first Saturday of November (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center). In 2026 that is November 7

2. "I'll buy a ticket on site for the May Daeje": the 2026 notice said 100% advance reservation, no on-site sales. In return, Jongmyo admission is free that day (Korea Heritage Agency)

3. "I have to reserve the English tour in advance": for parties of fewer than 10, you can go at the tour time without a reservation (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center)

4. "Visiting Jongmyo on a Tuesday": Jongmyo closes on Tuesdays. Go to Changdeokgung that day (Royal Palaces and Tombs Center)
