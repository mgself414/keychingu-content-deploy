Yongsan Guide — Itaewon, Hannam, Ichon Hangang: Three Free Museums and a Children's Garden, 2026 Official Info

Subtitle: National Museum of Korea free, open until 21:00 Wed & Sat / War Memorial of Korea free, closed Mondays, 09:30–17:40 / Yongsan Children's Garden free (Tue–Sun, until 21:00 on Sat) / Yongsan Family Park open 24 hours / Leeum at Hangangjin Station, Amorepacific at Sinyongsan Station / Itaewon 10.29 Memorial Alley — as of 2026-10-08 per each institution's and the Seoul city official pages. 🎯 How to Use This Guide

Yongsan has the highest concentration of free museums in Seoul: the National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) (free, open until 21:00 Wed & Sat), the War Memorial of Korea (전쟁기념관) (free, closed Mondays), and the Yongsan Children's Garden (용산어린이정원) (free), which opened fully at the end of 2025. Add the Leeum Museum of Art (리움미술관) and Amorepacific Museum of Art (아모레퍼시픽미술관) in Hannam-dong (한남동), the Ichon stretch of the Hangang, and the Itaewon night streets, and all of it sits within three subway stops. The values below are only what we read on each institution's or the Seoul city official page on 2026-10-08. Anything we could not read on an official page, such as the facilities at Ichon Hangang Park (이촌한강공원), the exits of Itaewon Station (이태원역), and N Seoul Tower, is marked "unconfirmed." Four claims in the older guide were wrong and have been removed: "Yongsan Park opens in 2027" (the Children's Garden is already open; the rest is undecided), "Ichon Station on Line 1" (it is Line 4 and Gyeongui-Jungang), "Itaewon Station to Leeum" (it is Hangangjin Station Exit 1), and "Hangang fireworks festival at Ichon" (it is at Yeouido).

🏛 Three Museums — All Free

  • National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) (137 Seobinggo-ro): Permanent exhibits free, open until 21:00 on Wed & Sat, gardens 07:00–22:00, closed Jan 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok (official). For the October special exhibitions, see #350. You walk from Ichon Station (이촌역) (the exit number is not officially posted; secondary source, exit 2).
  • War Memorial of Korea (전쟁기념관) (29 Itaewon-ro): 09:30–17:40 (last entry 17:00), closed Mondays, free (official). Allow 1–2 hours for a full loop including the aircraft and tanks in the outdoor exhibition area. Take Samgakji Station (삼각지역).
  • National Hangeul Museum (국립한글박물관): Closed through the second half of 2028 (official banner). Do not plan a visit.

🌳 Yongsan Children's Garden and Yongsan Family Park — The Former US Base Today

  • Yongsan Children's Garden (용산어린이정원) (21 Hangang-daero 38-gil): Fully open with no booking since 2025-12-30 (Seoul city). Tue–Fri & Sun 09–18, Sat 09–21, closed Mondays. The older guide's "Yongsan Park (용산공원) opens in 2027" is not confirmed. As of August 2026 the plan to build housing on the site is undecided, with Seoul city and Yongsan District opposing it (press reports).
  • Yongsan Family Park (용산가족공원) (13 Yongsan-dong 4-ga): 24 hours, free, 673 m from Seobinggo Station (서빙고역) Exit 1 (Seoul city 2023 data, so an older figure).

🖼 Hannam — Leeum and Amorepacific

  • Leeum Museum of Art (리움미술관): Tue–Sun 10–18, closed Mondays, combined ticket ₩25,000, permanent collection free, Hangangjin Station (한강진역) Exit 1 (Line 6), not Itaewon Station (이태원역). Digital guide in 4 languages.
  • Amorepacific Museum of Art (아모레퍼시픽미술관): Tue–Sun 10–18, closed Mondays, Sol LeWitt exhibition ₩18,000 with online booking, Sinyongsan Station (신용산역) Exit 1. Details in #350.
  • We could not read the names of the galleries in Hannam-dong (한남동) on an official page, so we do not list them.

🌃 Itaewon — Night Streets and Remembrance

  • Itaewon (이태원) is known as Seoul's first tourist special zone (1997), but we could not open the official description page this time, so we attach no label. We do not list restaurant or bar names either.
  • 10.29 Memorial Alley (10·29 기억과 안전의 길): The alley next to the Hamilton Hotel (해밀톤호텔) where the 2022 tragedy took place, open as a memorial space since 2023 (secondary source; the official site has a certificate error). Please pass through quietly.
  • We could not open an official page for the Seoul Central Mosque (서울중앙성원) (Islamic mosque), so we do not give a year.

🌉 Ichon Hangang — After the Museum

  • Ichon Hangang Park (이촌한강공원) is the stretch of the Hangang you walk down to from the National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관). Bikes, ferry piers and amenities are unconfirmed because the official page body would not open. For the Hangang Bus, cruises and fountains, see #786.
  • The fireworks festival is at Yeouido (9/5, over), not Ichon.

🚶 One-Day Route

  • Day: Samgakji Station (삼각지역) War Memorial of Korea (free) → Yongsan Children's Garden (용산어린이정원) (closed Mon) → Ichon Station (이촌역) National Museum of Korea (free) → walk along Ichon Hangang.
  • Night: On Wed & Sat the museum is open until 21:00 → Hangangjin Station (한강진역) Leeum (closes at 18:00, so go by day) → dinner in Itaewon (이태원).

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. War Memorial, Children's Garden, Leeum and APMA on a Monday — all closed. Only the National Museum of Korea is open.

2. Going to the National Hangeul Museum — closed through the second half of 2028.

3. Taking Itaewon Station to Leeum — use Hangangjin Station Exit 1.

4. Believing "Yongsan Park is open" — only the Children's Garden is open. The rest is undecided.

5. Watching fireworks from Ichon — they are at Yeouido.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. A free full day? — War Memorial → Yongsan Children's Garden → National Museum of Korea, all free.

2. What is open at night? — National Museum of Korea on Wed & Sat until 21:00, Yongsan Children's Garden on Sat until 21:00.

3. With kids? — Yongsan Children's Garden (no booking) and the War Memorial's outdoor exhibits.

4. What about English? — Leeum has a 4-language guide. Not officially posted for the two national institutions.

5. Which lines serve Ichon Station? — Line 4 and Gyeongui-Jungang.

6. Where is the Itaewon memorial space? — The alley next to the Hamilton Hotel, 10.29 Memorial Alley.
