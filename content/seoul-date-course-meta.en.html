Seoul Date Course Meta Guide for Foreign Travelers

7 Districts × Time Slots (Lunch, Afternoon, Dinner, Night View, Late Night) × Persona (Couples, Honeymooners, Anniversaries) Full Matrix

When foreign couples or honeymooners ask, "Where should we go on a date in Seoul?", the answer varies by district. Hannam (한남) is about atmosphere, Hapjeong (합정) is trendy, Seongsu (성수) is Instagrammable, Yeonnam (연남) is casual, Seochon (서촌) is traditional hanok, Apgujeong (압구정) is luxurious, and Itaewon (이태원) is for nightlife. This guide curates a 7-district × time slot × persona matrix to help foreign segment personas plan a one-day or one-night-two-day date course.

2. Hannam·Itaewon (한남·이태원) — Foreign Hub·Dining Bar (★ Top Pick for Foreigners)

Lunch·Afternoon

  • Mosu (모수) (3-star·Hannam) — Special occasion·Book 1-2 months in advance
  • Southside Parlor (사우스사이드 팔러) (Itaewon) — Mexican dining + Cocktails

Evening (★ Foreigner Date Hub)

  • Escondido (에스콘디도) (Hannam) — Mexican·Latin vibe + Wine
  • Soeul Hannam (소설한남) (Hannam) — 1-star Korean fine dining
  • Jackson Pizza (잭슨피자) (Itaewon) — NYC pizza·Indie vibe

Late Night Nightlife

  • Southside Parlor (사우스사이드 팔러) 4F — BAR (Live music often)
  • Comme d'habitude (꼼다비뛰드) (Hannam) — Whiskey bar
  • Itaewon (이태원) nightlife strip — Clubs (Sound, Volnost, etc.)

Persona Fit

  • American 20s couple: Southside Parlor (사우스사이드 팔러) → Itaewon (이태원) clubs
  • European 30s honeymooners: Mosu (모수) (3-star dinner) → Hannam (한남) whiskey bar
  • Anniversary: Mosu (모수) + Hannam (한남) exclusive atmosphere

3. Hapjeong·Hongdae (합정·홍대) — Trendy·Indie·Nightlife

Lunch

  • Yeonnam-dong (연남동) Golmok (Alley) Cafes — Hanok (한옥) dining
  • Sangsu (상수) Korean Restaurants — Casual Korean set meal

Evening

  • Excell Hapjeong (익스첼 합정) (Mexican·Wine pairing)
  • Hapjeong-dong (합정동) Wine Bars — Many (adjacent to Hongdae)

Late Night Nightlife

  • Hongdae (홍대) Clubs: NB·EDM club cluster
  • Sangsu (상수) Indie Live Bars: Indie music·Live performances

Persona Fit

  • American 20s couple: Hapjeong (합정) wine → Hongdae (홍대) clubs (late night)
  • Indie music lover: Sangsu (상수) indie live bars

5. Yeonnam (연남) — Casual·Golmok (Alley)·Stroll

Lunch·Afternoon

  • Yeonnam (연남) Golmok (Alley) Cafes — Many hanok (한옥)·indie design spots
  • Yeonnam (연남) mostly cafes — Stroll fit

Evening

  • Yeonnam-dong (연남동) Wine Bars — Casual (₩40,000~80,000 (~$30-60 / ~200-400 RMB / ~4,000-8,000 JPY) for 2 people)
  • Yeonnam (연남) Indie Dining — Cluster of 1-person chef restaurants

Persona Fit

  • European relaxed persona: Yeonnam (연남) golmok (alley) stroll + wine bar
  • First date: Casual·Less pressure

6. Seochon·Bukchon (서촌·북촌) — Hanok (Traditional Korean House)·Tradition·Art

Lunch·Afternoon

  • Bukchon (북촌) Hanok (한옥) Dining (see #1097) — 7 curated spots
  • Seochon (서촌) Hanok (한옥) Cafes — See #1110 for deep dive

Evening

  • Dureyu (두레유) (Seochon Chebu-dong (서촌 체부동)) — Korean fine dining
  • Onjium (온지움) (Hyojadong (효자동)) — 1-star Royal Court Korean fine dining

Persona Fit

  • European hanok (한옥) lover: Bukchon (북촌) + Seochon (서촌) 1-day deep dive
  • Anniversary: Onjium (온지움) (1-star) + hanok (한옥) stay for 1 night

7. Apgujeong·Cheongdam (압구정·청담) — Luxury·Michelin

Evening

  • Jungsik (정식당) (2-star) — NYC flagship·Foreigner-fluent
  • L'Asse (라쎄) (2-star) — Scandinavian·Korean fusion
  • Kojima (코지마) (1-star) — Japanese Omakase
  • Kang Min Chul Restaurant (강민철 레스토랑) (2-star) — Contemporary Korean cuisine

Late Night

  • Cheongdam-dong (청담동) Luxury Bars — Hotel lounges·Whiskey bars

Persona Fit

  • Special occasion: Jungsik (정식당) + Cheongdam (청담) hotel lounge
  • Gourmet: Kang Min Chul (강민철) + L'Asse (라쎄) (two-day course)

8. Yeouido·Hangang (여의도·한강) — Night View·Nature (★ Free·Refreshing)

Evening·Night View

  • Hangang Park (한강공원) Picnic — Chicken·Beer delivery (unique experience for foreigners)
  • Sebitseom (세빛섬) (Banpo (반포)) — Artificial island on Hangang (한강)·Night view·Dining
  • 63 Building Sky (63빌딩) — Hangang (한강) view·Dessert cafe
  • Banpo Bridge Rainbow Fountain (반포대교 무지개 분수) (April-October) — Free·4 times a day

Persona Fit

  • All personas·Summer fit: Hangang (한강) picnic (high satisfaction for foreigners ★)
  • Anniversary: Sebitseom (세빛섬) dining + Hangang (한강) night view

9. 7 Districts Standard 1-Day Date Courses (4 Patterns)

Pattern A: Hannam·Itaewon (한남·이태원) All Day (Foreign Hub Fit)

  • 11:00 AM Hannam (한남) brunch cafe
  • 1:00 PM Hannam (한남) gallery or Leeum Museum of Art (리움 미술관)
  • 3:00 PM Hannam (한남) cafe·dessert
  • 6:00 PM Mosu (모수) or Escondido (에스콘디도) (Dinner)
  • 9:00 PM Hannam (한남) whiskey bar
  • 11:00 PM Itaewon (이태원) club (optional)

Pattern B: Seongsu·Apgujeong (성수·압구정) (Design + Luxury)

  • 11:00 AM Cafe Onion Seongsu (카페 어니언 성수)
  • 2:00 PM Seongsu (성수) Instagram hop (NUDAKE (누데이크)·Blue Bottle (블루보틀))
  • 5:00 PM Move to Apgujeong (압구정)
  • 6:00 PM Stroll around Dosan Park (도산공원)
  • 7:30 PM Jungsik (정식당) or Kang Min Chul (강민철) dinner
  • 10:00 PM Cheongdam (청담) hotel lounge

Pattern C: Seochon·Bukchon (서촌·북촌) (Tradition·Art) — European Persona

  • 10:00 AM Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) or Changdeokgung Palace (창덕궁)
  • 12:00 PM Bukchon (북촌) hanok (한옥) lunch
  • 2:00 PM Park No-soo Art Museum (박노수미술관) in Seochon (서촌)
  • 4:00 PM Seochon (서촌) hanok (한옥) cafe
  • 6:00 PM Dureyu (두레유) or Onjium (온지움) dinner
  • 9:00 PM Hanok (한옥) stay for 1 night (option)

Pattern D: Hangang (한강) Night View (Summer·Free·Romantic)

  • 5:00 PM Arrive at Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도 한강공원)
  • 6:00 PM Hangang (한강) picnic (chicken·beer)
  • 7:30 PM Banpo Rainbow Fountain (반포대교 무지개 분수) (April-October)
  • 9:00 PM Sebitseom (세빛섬) dessert cafe
  • 11:00 PM Hangang (한강) night view stroll

10. Foreigner-Friendly Tips

Restaurants with English Booking Available

  • Michelin Restaurants: Catchtable (English) (Jungsik (정식당), Kang Min Chul (강민철), L'Asse (라쎄), Joo Ok (주옥))
  • 3-star: Mosu (모수) official website, La Yeon (라연) hotel concierge
  • Trendy Restaurants: Call the restaurant directly or DM on Instagram (English available)
  • Hangang (한강) Picnic: No reservation needed, visit Hangang Park (한강공원) directly

Budget (for 2 people, 1 day)

  • Luxury (Apgujeong (압구정)·Michelin): ₩600,000~1,200,000 (~$450-900 / ~3,000-6,000 RMB / ~60,000-120,000 JPY) (2-star + hotel)
  • Dining (Hannam (한남)·Hapjeong (합정) dinner): ₩200,000~400,000
  • Casual (Seongsu (성수)·Yeonnam (연남)·Seochon (서촌)): ₩100,000~200,000
  • Hangang (한강) Picnic: ₩40,000~80,000 (Chicken + Beer + Dessert)

By Season

  • Spring (April-May): Hangang (한강) (cherry blossoms) + Seochon (서촌)·Bukchon (북촌) (cherry blossoms)
  • Summer (June-August): Hangang (한강) picnic·Sebitseom (세빛섬)·Banpo Rainbow Fountain (반포대교 무지개 분수)·Indoor cafes
  • Autumn (October-November): Bukchon (북촌)·Seochon (서촌)·Yeonnam (연남) (autumn foliage) + Hangang (한강)
  • Winter (December-February): Hanok (한옥) stay (ondol (traditional Korean floor heating system))·Indoor dining·Cheongdam (청담) lounge

By Persona

  • American 20s couple: Hannam (한남)·Itaewon (이태원) (Pattern A) + Hangang (한강) picnic
  • American 30s honeymooners: Apgujeong (압구정)·Cheongdam (청담) (Pattern B Michelin) + Hangang (한강) night view
  • European art lover: Seochon (서촌)·Bukchon (북촌) (Pattern C) + Seongsu (성수) design
  • Anniversary·Proposal: La Yeon (라연)·Mosu (모수)·Jungsik (정식당) (3·2-star) + Cheongdam (청담) hotel lounge
  • Honeymoon: 1 day dining + 1 day hanok (한옥) stay + 1 day Hangang (한강)
  • First date: Yeonnam (연남) (casual·less pressure)

Date + Hanok (한옥) Stay Combo

  • Seochon (서촌)·Bukchon (북촌) dinner → Hanok (한옥) stay for 1 night (Ragung (라궁)·Insahanida (인사하니다))
  • Special memory for foreign honeymooners

+. The "Next" Date Areas Locals Are Saving Right Now (Aug 2026 signal update)

The 7 areas above are the standard set — these 4 are where saves on local curation accounts have surged recently (66 course-type signals in our inbox, Aug 2026). Less crowded, so they fit a second-visit date especially well.

  • Euljiro (을지로) & Cheonggyecheon (청계천) — hardware alleys by day, unmarked second-floor bars by night. Walk the stream, then duck into a bar — connects to #11 Euljiro Retro. Best: evening to late night.
  • Mullae Art Village (문래창작촌) — artists' studios and cafés tucked between working ironworks. "A most-Mullae day" courses are a local-magazine staple. An uncrowded afternoon axis — same spirit as #1235 Quiet Seoul.
  • Seokchon Lake (석촌호수, Songpa·Jamsil) — a lap around the lake plus nearby dining. Local signals call it the course for couples who just started dating. In cherry-blossom season it jumps to standard-7 tier — pairs with #300 Jamsil 50.
  • Anguk (안국) & Bukchon (북촌) — quiet hanok-alley strolling. Overlaps the tourist route (#38 Bukchon), but for a date the local consensus is clear: weekday evenings.
