Seoul Date Course Guide — 7 Districts with Official Values as of October 2026: Free Museums, Galleries, Han River Night Views and Alley Walks

Subtitle: Hannam Leeum and Pace galleries (closed Mondays) · Hapjeong and Hongdae Red Road and Gyeongui Line Forest Park · Yeonnam walk · Seochon, Bukchon and Samcheong Kukje Gallery and MMCA (open Mondays) · Apgujeong and Cheongdam Dosan Park, Perrotin and White Cube · Yeouido and the Han River cruise at 22:00 and Banpo fountain 19:30–20:50 — based on each official page as of 2026-10-08 (reusing the fact tables of #216, #285, #350 and #786). No restaurant or café names are given.

🎯 How to Use This Guide

A date course comes down to "where do we go and what do we do," but the old guide had only district moods and a persona table — it never said whether a place is open or what it costs. This time we re-laid out only places checked today (2026-10-08) on their official pages — museums and galleries (#285, #350), Hongdae and Yeonnam (#216), the Han River (#786) and Yongsan (#710) — across 7 districts. We give no restaurant, café or bar names because there is no official source for them; leave those to "review counts in a map app." Two points matter most: on Mondays the galleries, Leeum and the Seoul Museum of Art are closed and only MMCA Seoul and the National Museum of Korea are open, and on Wednesday and Saturday evenings MMCA (free 18:00–21:00) and the National Museum of Korea (until 21:00) stay open.

🌆 Evenings and Night Views — Choose by Day of the Week

  • Wed and Sat: MMCA Seoul free 18:00–21:00 → National Museum of Korea until 21:00 → Banpo fountain, 3 shows (19:30–20:50, reduced 2026 operation).
  • Fri and Sat: Seouldal until 23:00 · Yeouido cruise Starlight at 22:00 (₩25,900).
  • Monday: galleries, Leeum and the Seoul Museum of Art are closed → go to MMCA Seoul, the National Museum of Korea and the Han River instead.
  • The Seoul World Fireworks Festival (9/5) and the Han River swimming pools (through 8/30) have already ended this year (#786).

🖼 Daytime — Two Walking Courses

  • Samcheong gallery walk (Tue–Sat): Anguk Station (안국역) Exit 1 → MMCA Seoul (국립현대미술관 서울) → Kukje Gallery (국제갤러리), PKM and Arario (no posted fee, usually free) → Museum Hanmi (뮤지엄한미) (₩15,000) → Seoul Museum of Craft Art (서울공예박물관) (free). For October exhibitions, see #285.
  • Hannam gallery walk (Tue–Sat): Leeum Museum of Art (리움미술관) at Hangangjin Station (한강진역) Exit 1 (permanent collection free) → Pace and Lehmann Maupin on Itaewon-ro → Thaddaeus Ropac on Dokseodang-ro → Amorepacific Museum of Art (아모레퍼시픽미술관) near Sinyongsan (reservation). On 10/9 (Hangul Day), Pace and Esther Schipper are closed.

🌳 Walks — Two That Cost Nothing

  • Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길), Yeonnam section: 447 m from Hongik Univ. Station Exit 3, a 6.3 km linear park, free (VisitSeoul).
  • Banpo Hangang Park (반포한강공원): Sinbanpo Station Exit 1 or Express Bus Terminal Station 8-1, free 24 hours, 3 night fountain shows.

🍽 Why There Are No Restaurants or Cafés

  • No official page designates any specific restaurant or café as a date course. Choose a venue by review counts in a map app plus whether it is open that day; this guide goes only as far as "which station, which street."

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. A Hannam or Samcheong gallery date on Monday — everything is closed. Go to MMCA Seoul or the National Museum of Korea instead.

2. Counting on a 21:00 Banpo fountain show — in 2026 there are 3 shows, at 19:30, 20:00 and 20:30.

3. Assuming cruise fares are ₩20,000 — Moonlight is ₩32,900 and the Fireworks cruise ₩45,900.

4. Walking in to the Amorepacific Museum of Art — book online.

5. Looking for Perrotin and White Cube in Hannam — they are in Cheongdam, at Dosan Park.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. A first-date course? — Wed or Sat: MMCA Seoul (국립현대미술관 서울) at Anguk Station (안국역) (free from 18:00) → evening in Samcheong → Han River fountain at 19:30.

2. Free only? — Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길) → Red Road (레드로드) busking in Hongdae → Banpo fountain. All free.

3. If it rains? — MMCA Seoul, the National Museum of Korea, and the Leeum permanent collection (free). See #280.

4. An anniversary evening? — The Moonlight cruise at 19:00 (₩32,900) or Starlight at 22:00.

5. On a Monday? — MMCA Seoul + the National Museum of Korea + the Han River.

6. Restaurant recommendations? — Not in this guide. Use review counts in a map app.
