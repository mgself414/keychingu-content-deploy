Foreigner Seoul Dating Course Meta Guide

> 7 regions × time zone (lunch, afternoon, evening, night view, late night) × persona (couple, honeymoon, anniversary) full matrix

In Seoul, foreign couples and honeymooners ask, “Where should we go on a date?” When asked, the answer is different for each region. Hannam has atmosphere, Hapjeong is trendy, Seongsu is Instagram, Yeonnam is casual, Seochon is hanok, Apgujeong is luxury, Itaewon is nightlife. This guide is curated using a 7-region × time zone × persona matrix so that foreign segment personas can plan a date course for one day or two days and one night.

1. Area 7 dating atmosphere matrix

Region Core Atmosphere fit persona Date time
Hannam·Itaewon Foreign hub·international·dining bar American hipster·LA native·diversity fit Evening~late night
Hapjeong·Hongdae trendy·indie·nightlife American 20s and 30s, indie music lover Evening~late night
Seongsu Instagram·Design·Warehouse Cafe European designer·creative Afternoon~Evening
Yeonnam Casual·Alley·Walk European Persona·Relaxation Lunch ~ Afternoon
Seochon·Bukchon Hanok·Tradition·Art European art lover·Hanok lover Lunch ~ Afternoon
Apgujeong·Cheongdam Luxury·Luxury·Michelin USA/Europe special occasion Dinner
Yeouido·Hangang Night view·Coolness·Nature All Persona·Summer fit night view

2. Hannam·Itaewon — Foreign hub/dining bar (★ No. 1 among foreigners)

Lunch/Afternoon

  • Mosu (3 star·Hannam) — special occasion·reservation 1-2 months in advance
  • Southside Parlor (Itaewon) — Mexican dining + cocktails

Dinner (★ Foreign dating hub)

  • Escondido (Hannam) — Mexican/Latin atmosphere + wine
  • Soseolhannam (Hannam) — 1 star·Korean fine dining
  • Jackson Pizza (Itaewon) — NYC pizza/indie atmosphere

Late night nightlife

  • Southside Parlor 4F — BAR (live music often)
  • Comda Vitude (Hannam) — Whiskey bar
  • Itaewon nightlife strip — Clubs (Sound·Volnost, etc.)

Persona fit

  • American couple in their 20s: Southside Parlor → Itaewon Club
  • Newlyweds in their 30s in Europe: Mosu (3 star dinner) → Hannam whiskey bar
  • anniversary: parameters + Hannam limited atmosphere

3. Hapjeong·Hongdae — trendy·indie·nightlife

Lunch

  • Yeonnam-dong Alley Cafe — Hanok Dining
  • Sangsoo Korean food — Casual Korean food

dinner

  • Ixchel Hapjeong (Mexican/Wine Pairing)
  • Hapjeong-dong Wine Bar — Dasu (adjacent to Hongdae)

Late night nightlife

  • Hongdae Club: NB·EDM club cluster
  • Sangsu Indie Live Bar: indie music and live performances

Persona fit

  • American couple in their 20s: Hapjeong Wine → Hongdae Club (late at night)
  • Indie music lover: Constant Indie Live Bar

4. Seongsu — Instagram, design, warehouse cafe

Afternoon (Insta signature)

  • Cafe Onion Seongsu — Bread·Cafe (warehouse + Hanok mix)
  • Nudake Seongsu — Design Cafe
  • Blue Bottle Seongsu — Foreign brand headquarters

dinner

  • Mesh Seongsu — Italian Dining (Insta Signature)
  • F1963 Seongsu — Complex cultural space + dining
  • Foset Seongsu — Western dining + atmosphere

Persona fit

  • European Designer: Cafe Onion → Nudake → Mesh Dinner
  • Honeymoon photo fit: Seongsu Instagram 4-hour hop

5. Yeonnam — casual, alley, walk

Lunch/Afternoon

  • Yeonnam Alley Cafe — Many hanok and indie designs
  • Yeonnam mostly cafe — walking fit

dinner

  • Yeonnam-dong Wine Bar — Casual (₩40,000~80,000/2 people)
  • Yeonnam indie dining — one-person chef store cluster

Persona fit

  • European leisure persona: Yeonnam alley walk + wine bar
  • First Date: Casual, low pressure

6. Seochon·Bukchon — Hanok, tradition, art

Lunch/Afternoon

  • Bukchon Hanok Dining (see #1097) — 7 curation locations
  • Seochon Hanok Cafe — See #1110 deep dive

dinner

  • Dooreyu (Seochon Chebu-dong) — Korean fine dining
  • Onjium (Hyoja-dong) — 1 star, royal Korean fine dining

Persona fit

  • European Hanok lover: Bukchon + Seochon 1 day deep dive
  • anniversary: Onzium (1 star) + Hanok stay for 1 night

7. Apgujeong·Cheongdam — Luxury·Michelin

dinner

  • Restaurant (2 stars) — NYC native/foreigner fluent
  • Lasse (2 star) — Scandinavian-Korean fusion
  • Kojima (1 star) — Japanese Omakase
  • Kang Min-cheol Restaurant (2 stars) — Contemporary Korean cuisine

Late night

  • Cheongdam-dong luxury bar — Hotel lounge·Whiskey bar

Persona fit

  • special occasion: Jeongjeongdang + Cheongdam Hotel lounge
  • Gourmet: Kang Min-cheol + Lasse (two-day course)

8. Yeouido·Han River — Night view·Nature (★ Free·Cool)

Evening/Night view

  • Hangang Park Picnic — Chicken and beer delivery (unique experience for foreigners)
  • Some Sevit (Banpo) — Artificial island on the Han River, night view, dining
  • 63 Building sky — Han River view·dessert cafe
  • Banpo Bridge Rainbow Fountain (April-October) — Free, 4 times a day

Persona fit

  • All Persona/Summer fit: Han River Picnic (Foreigner Satisfaction ★)
  • anniversary: Some Sevit Dining + Han River Night View

9. 7 regions 1-day date standard course (4 patterns)

Pattern A: Hannam·Itaewon all day (foreign hub fit)

  • 11:00 Hannam Brunch Cafe
  • 13:00 Hannam Gallery or Leeum Art Museum
  • 15:00 Hannam Cafe and Dessert
  • 18:00 Mosu or Escondido (dinner)
  • 21:00 Hannam whiskey bar
  • 23:00 Itaewon Club (optional)

Pattern B: Seongsu·Apgujeong (Design + Luxury)

  • 11:00 Seongsu Cafe Onion
  • 14:00 Seongsu Instagram hop (Nudake, Blue Bottle)
  • 17:00 Move to Apgujeong
  • 18:00 Walk around Dosan Park
  • 19:30 Jeongjeongdang or Kang Min-cheol dinner
  • 22:00 Cheongdam Hotel lounge

Pattern C: Seochon·Bukchon (Tradition·Art) — European Persona

  • 10:00 Gyeongbokgung Palace or Changdeokgung Palace
  • 12:00 Lunch at Bukchon Hanok
  • 14:00 Seochon Park No-soo Art Museum
  • 16:00 Seochon Hanok Cafe
  • 18:00 Dureyu or Onzium dinner
  • 21:00 Hanok stay for 1 night (option)

Pattern D: Han River night view (summer, free, romantic)

  • 17:00 Arrival at Yeouido Hangang Park
  • 18:00 Hangang Picnic (chicken, beer)
  • 19:30 Banpo Rainbow Fountain (April-October)
  • 21:00 Sebitseom Dessert Cafe
  • 23:00 Night view walk along the Han River

10. Foreign fit tips

Stores available for reservations in English

  • Michelin Store: catchtable English (Jeong Restaurant, Kang Min-cheol, Lasse, Juok)
  • 3 star: Mosu Official Site, Layeon Hotel Concierge
  • Trendy Store: Call the store directly or DM on Instagram (English available)
  • Han River Picnic: No reservation required, visit Hangang Park directly

Budget (2 people per day)

  • Luxury (Apgujeong·Michelin): ₩600,000~1,200,000 (2 star + hotel)
  • Dining (Hannam/Hapjeong Dinner): ₩200,000~400,000
  • Casual (Seongsu, Yeonnam, Seochon): ₩100,000~200,000
  • Han River Picnic: ₩40,000~80,000 (chicken + beer + dessert)

By season

  • Spring (April-May): Han River (cherry blossoms) + Seochon·Bukchon (cherry blossoms)
  • Summer (June-August): Hangang Picnic, Some Sevit, Banpo Rainbow Fountain, Indoor Cafe
  • Fall (October-November): Bukchon, Seochon, Yeonnam (autumn leaves) + Han River
  • Winter (December-February): Hanok stay (Ondol)·Indoor dining·Cheongdam lounge

By persona

  • American couple in their 20s: Hannam·Itaewon (Pattern A) + Han River Picnic
  • American newlyweds in their 30s: Apgujeong·Cheongdam (Pattern B Michelin) + Han River night view
  • European art lover: Seochon·Bukchon (Pattern C) + Seongsu Design
  • anniversary·proposal: Layeon, Mosu, Jeongsang restaurant (3·2 star) + Cheongdam Hotel lounge
  • honeymoon: 1 day dining + 1 day Hanok stay + 1 day Han River
  • First date: Yeonnam (casual, low pressure)

Date + Hanok stay combo

  • Seochon·Bukchon dinner → Hanok stay for 1 night (Ragung·Greetings)
  • Foreign honeymoon special memory
