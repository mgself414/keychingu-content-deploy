Taekwondo and Taekkyeon Guide: Kukkiwon (World Taekwondo Headquarters) in Seoul, Taekwondowon and Taekwon Meditation Forest Trail in Muju, Chungju Taekkyeon Center

Subtitle: Kukkiwon (free admission, open on weekdays) · Taekwondowon (free admission, taekwondo experience pass 4,000 won, monorail adults 4,000 won) · Taekwon Meditation Forest Trail (1,320m, always open) · Chungju Taekkyeon Center (free) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places where you can see and try taekwondo and the traditional martial art taekkyeon (택견). They are Kukkiwon (국기원) in Seoul, Taekwondowon (태권도원) in Muju (무주) with the forest trail around it, and the Chungju (충주) Taekkyeon Center. Kukkiwon and Taekwondowon have free admission, and fees are registered for the experience center and the monorail inside Taekwondowon.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Seoul — Kukkiwon (World Taekwondo Headquarters)

Kukkiwon (국기원) (32 Teheran-ro 7-gil (테헤란로7길), Gangnam-gu (강남구), ☎ 02-567-1058) is an institution that opened on November 30, 1972 to carry on and spread the spirit and skills of taekwondo, and it is also called the World Taekwondo Headquarters (세계태권도본부). 'Gukgi (국기)' means the distinctive skill of a nation, here taekwondo, and 'won (원)' means a house. Right next to it, the Kukkiwon Memorial Hall (국기원 기념관) opened on November 30, 1991 and displays relics of taekwondo history. Kukkiwon is about 500m from Exit 12 of Gangnam Station (강남역).

  • Opening hours: Kukkiwon Monday to Thursday 09:00~18:00, Friday 09:00~16:00. Memorial Hall 09:00~17:30 (lunch break 11:30~13:00).
  • Closed days: Weekends and public holidays.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

📍 Muju — Taekwondowon and Taekwon Meditation Forest Trail

Taekwondowon (태권도원) (1482 Museol-ro (무설로), Seolcheon-myeon (설천면), Muju-gun, ☎ 063-320-0114) is a space devoted to taekwondo where competition, experience, training, education, research and exchange all take place in one spot. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is the first single Olympic sport venue where competition, experience, training, education, research and exchange are all possible, and the world's only such taekwondo space. It has a stadium, a performance hall, training facilities such as Pyeongwongwan (평원관), and lodging and dining facilities. If you ride the monorail up to the observatory, you can see the whole of Taekwondowon.

  • Opening hours: Taekwondowon 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:00), monorail 10:00~17:00 (last boarding 16:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, and the day of and the day before Seollal and Chuseok. If a Monday or a connected weekday is a public holiday, it is closed on the next weekday.
  • Fee: Free admission. At Experience Center Yap! (체험관 Yap!), the taekwondo experience pass is 4,000 won, the VR·AR experience pass is 6,000 won and the combined pass is 8,000 won. The monorail is 4,000 won for adults (age 19 and over) and 2,000 won for children and teenagers (age 3~18).
  • Parking: Free.

The Taekwon Meditation Forest Trail (태권명상숲길) (same address, ☎ 063-320-0114) is a walking trail around Taekwondowon themed on taekwondo poomsae (품새), the Palgwae (팔괘) forms. It is 1,320m in total length, a 2-hour course, with facilities and sculptures for each poomsae. You can take a phytoncide walk, visit themed meditation zones and try the ecological forest experience.

  • Opening hours: Always open.

📍 Chungju — Chungju Taekkyeon Center

The Chungju Taekkyeon Center (충주시택견원) (3326 Jungwon-daero (중원대로), Chungju-si, ☎ 043-850-7304) is a hanok (한옥) building inside Hoamji Ecological Park (호암지 생태공원), built to preserve and promote the traditional martial art taekkyeon. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, taekkyeon was the first martial art to become a National Intangible Cultural Heritage. Inside there is a large training hall, and the center holds citizens' taekkyeon classes, regular taekkyeon training, training courses and taekkyeon competitions.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary with the program.
  • Fee: Free.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Kukkiwon is closed on weekends and public holidays — it closes at 16:00 on Friday, and the Memorial Hall has a lunch break (11:30~13:00).
  2. Taekwondowon is also closed on Mondays and the day before the holidays — for Seollal and Chuseok, both the day itself and the day before are closed.
  3. The Taekwondowon monorail closes boarding at 16:30 — it runs 10:00~17:00.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where can I try taekwondo myself?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, you can try it at Experience Center Yap! at Taekwondowon in Muju, with the taekwondo experience pass (4,000 won), the VR·AR experience pass (6,000 won) or the combined pass (8,000 won).

Q. Where can I see taekwondo in Seoul?

A. Kukkiwon and the Kukkiwon Memorial Hall (displaying taekwondo history relics) in Gangnam-gu. Admission is free, and Kukkiwon is open Monday to Thursday 09:00~18:00 and Friday 09:00~16:00 (closed on weekends and public holidays).
