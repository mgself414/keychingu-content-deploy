Korea Dessert Shop Guide — 10 Cake, Donut, Cookie and Old-Bakery Spots (Passion 5, Maillet, Donut Jeongsu, Taegeukdang, Colombang Bakery) (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: Passion 5 (패션5) bakery 07:30-22:00, 300-400 or so desserts (VisitKorea) · Maillet (마얘) French desserts in Seorae Village, with mille-feuille vanilla and macarons (VisitKorea) · Donut Jeongsu (도넛정수) Korean-style donuts inspired by Gaeseong juak (VisitKorea) · Taegeukdang (태극당) opened in 1945, 08:00-21:00 (VisitKorea) · Prices not published for all 10 shops — as of 2026-10-21

ISSUE 03 · #914 · 2026-05-27

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Press your nose to the showcase glass and the smell of butter reaches you first; the staff cut the cake with a knife and serve it on a plate (at Gamkkotdang (감꽃당), staff cut the cake for you instead of giving you a fork and knife, VisitKorea). This guide covers only shops where cake, donuts, cookies and bread, rather than coffee, are the stars. There are three groups: cakes and pâtisserie, donuts and cookies, and old bakeries. Every shop is listed in the Korea Tourism Organization's tourism data (VisitKorea, TourAPI). Cafes where coffee and space are the stars are in #913 Seoul Cafe Guide, and desserts built on autumn ingredients are in #475 Autumn Dessert Guide.

The old guide placed Isungdang (이성당) of Gunsan on a Jeonju route, but in VisitKorea Isungdang is at 177 Jungang-ro (중앙로 177), Gunsan-si, Jeonbuk. It also called London Bagel Museum (런던베이글뮤지엄) a "croissant shop," but VisitKorea introduces it as a bagel specialty shop. Rankings such as "Korea's best macarons" and "No. 1 among foreigners" were deleted because they had no source.

🎂 Cakes and Pâtisserie — 4 in Seoul

  • Passion 5 (패션5, Passion 5) — 272 Itaewon-ro (이태원로 272), Yongsan-gu, Seoul (Hannam-dong, 한남동). Bakery 07:30-22:00, gelato cafe 11:00-20:00, open every day. From cakes, handmade chocolates, cookies and puddings to bakery items from many countries, it displays 300-400 or so varieties, which you browse like a gallery as you choose. The signature is the Awesome Apple Pie, and it also has eclairs, croissants and canelés. With a purchase receipt, parking is free for 1 hour. Tel 0507-1416-9505 (VisitKorea)
  • Maillet (마얘, MAILLET) — 14 Sapyeong-daero 22-gil (사평대로22길 14), Seocho-gu, Seoul (Seorae Village in Banpo-dong, 반포동). 11:00-21:30, open every day. A traditional French dessert shop whose signatures are Mille-feuille Vanilla (밀푀유바니), Ttakteu Vanilla (딱뜨바니) and French-style macarons. Takeout available. Tel 02-749-1411 (VisitKorea)
  • Le Mont Blanc (르몽블랑) — 99-4 Sinheung-ro (신흥로 99-4), Yongsan-gu, Seoul (Haebangchon, 해방촌). Tue-Thu 12:00-19:00, Fri-Sat 12:00-20:00, closed Mon. It is a renovated building that was a knitwear factory for 20 years. That is why its signatures are a yarn mousse and a knit T-shirt cake. First floor is the workshop and showcase, second floor seating, third floor rooftop. Tel 070-8887-8200 (VisitKorea)
  • Gamkkotdang (감꽃당) — 38 Donhwamun-ro 11da-gil (돈화문로11다길 38), Jongno-gu, Seoul. 10:30-22:00, open every day. A hanok (traditional Korean house) cafe that preserves an old persimmon tree. Its signatures are an apple pie made with apples, fermented-butter scones and egg tarts. It does not give you a fork and knife; staff cut the cake for you and then bring a wet wipe. Tel 02-747-3999 (VisitKorea)

🍩 Donuts and Cookies — 3 in Seoul

  • Donut Jeongsu (도넛정수) — 40 Changsin 12-gil (창신12길 40), Jongno-gu, Seoul. 11:30-21:00 (last order 20:30), open every day. A Korean-style donut shop inspired by juak (주악), a Gaeseong hangwa (traditional Korean confection) of glutinous rice fried in oil, serving 9 kinds of donuts made with specialty products from across the country. Examples include milk (tarak, 타락), matcha and lemon yogurt donuts. It has both floor seating and chair seating, and you can see the Seoul view through the window. Tel 0507-1386-5775 (VisitKorea)
  • Overdose Donut & Coffee (오버도즈도넛앤커피) — 19 Wangsimni-ro 10-gil (왕십리로10길 19), Seongdong-gu, Seoul (about 110 m from Exit 7 of Ttukseom Station, 뚝섬역). 11:30-20:30, closed Mon. The custard is cooked at dawn every day, the cream is made the same day, and donuts that do not sell are thrown out that day. The signatures are the pistachio and raspberry donut and the Earl Grey cream donut. The shop is on the 1.5th floor. Tel 0507-1319-7369 (VisitKorea)
  • Jeongae Cookie (정애쿠키) — 33 Donggyo-ro 46-gil (동교로46길 33), Mapo-gu, Seoul (Yeonnam-dong, 연남동). 11:00-19:00, open every day. A grandmother owner bakes cookies with Korean-grown whole wheat flour and no butter. The signature is the gochu (chili pepper) cookie, and it also has Jeongae Cookie and chocolate chip cookies. Takeout available. Tel 02-747-0178 (VisitKorea)

🏛 Old Bakeries — 3 in Seoul, Mokpo and Jinhae

  • Taegeukdang (태극당) — 7 Dongho-ro 24-gil (동호로24길 7), Jung-gu, Seoul (Jangchung-dong 2-ga, 장충동2가). 08:00-21:00, open every day. The VisitKorea description introduces it as the oldest bakery in Seoul, opened around Liberation in 1945, now in its third generation. The signature is the milk-flavored ice cream monaka (a wafer sandwich with a filling), sold since the 1960s, and it also has red bean bread, butter bread, cream bread and old-style butter cake. Tel 02-2279-3152 (VisitKorea)
  • Colombang Bakery (코롬방제과점) — 7 Yeongsan-ro 75beon-gil (영산로75번길 7), Mokpo-si, Jeonnam (Muan-dong, 무안동). 08:00-21:00, closing day not published. The signatures are the cream cheese baguette and the shrimp baguette. Tel 061-244-0885 (VisitKorea)
  • Mijin Confectionery (미진과자점) — 4 Chungjang-ro 130beon-gil (충장로130번길 4), Jinhae-gu, Changwon-si, Gyeongnam. 07:00-22:00, open every day, cards accepted, takeout available. Known as the oldest bakery in Jinhae, it developed the cherry blossom bread, cherry blossom roll and cherry blossom honey madeleine together with Jinhae Bakery (진해제과). The cherry blossom bread's main ingredients are cherry blossom extract and pink cherry blossom red bean paste. The VisitKorea listed name carries a "Baeknyeon Gage" (백년가게, 100-year shop) label. Tel 055-545-3133 (VisitKorea)
  • Gunsan's Isungdang (이성당) and Daejeon's Sungsimdang (성심당) are covered separately — Foreigner's Gunsan KTX 2-Day Full Guide · Daejeon Sungsimdang Bakery

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Price range: None of the 10 shops has a price in its VisitKorea listing. Prices not published — check the showcase price tags or call
  • Vegetarian: Cakes, donuts and cookies usually contain butter, milk and eggs. Jeongae Cookie is described as using no butter (VisitKorea), but whether it contains eggs or milk could not be confirmed — if you are vegan, ask directly
  • Gluten: Jeongae Cookie uses Korean-grown whole wheat flour (VisitKorea) — it is a wheat flour dessert. For rice flour desserts, see the rice dessert shops in #475 Autumn Dessert Guide
  • Nuts and dairy: Overdose's signature donut contains pistachio, and Taegeukdang's monaka contains milk-flavored ice cream (VisitKorea)
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Itaewon's Eid (이드) (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil (우사단로10길 15), Yongsan-gu, daily 12:00-21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF). Certification can change, so check before you visit. None of the dessert shops in this guide carries a halal label. Gelatin in mousses and jellies and liquor in cake syrup differ from shop to shop, so ask before you order
  • For show-and-tell cards see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Dessert Shop Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Ordering, Getting Around

  • Solo: Picking one or two items from the showcase and taking them away is no burden when you are alone. The places listed as offering takeout are Maillet, Jeongae Cookie and Mijin Confectionery (VisitKorea). For a map of places to go alone, see #348 Seoul Solo Diner Map
  • Reservations: None of the 10 shops has reservation information listed. For tips on queuing and waiting for bread, see Korea Bread Buying and Waiting Workflow
  • Payment: The place listed as accepting cards is Mijin Confectionery (VisitKorea). For cards and mobile payment in general, see #527 Korea Payment Systems
  • Ordering: Just point at the showcase and say the number. For vibrating pagers see #1288 Cafe Pager Manual, and when you cannot make yourself understood, see #1339 Ordering Etiquette
  • Getting around: In Seoul, see #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards. For Mokpo and Jinhae, see #1287 Riding the KTX for the First Time and #1297 Express and Intercity Bus Manual

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Isungdang is a Jeonju bakery" — In VisitKorea, Isungdang's address is 177 Jungang-ro, Gunsan-si, Jeonbuk

2. "London Bagel Museum is a croissant shop" — VisitKorea introduces it as a bagel specialty shop

3. "Dessert shops stay open late every day" — Le Mont Blanc is closed Monday and closes at 19:00 Tue-Thu, and Jeongae Cookie closes at 19:00. Overdose Donut & Coffee is closed Monday (VisitKorea)

4. "You eat cake with a fork on your own" — At Gamkkotdang, staff cut the cake for you instead of giving you a fork and knife (VisitKorea)
