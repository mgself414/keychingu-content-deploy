14 Busan Pubs and Bars — Only Where Foreign Reviews Showed Up First

Subtitle: After the Jeonpo whiskey bars and Gwangalli seaside bars (#1364) and the nakgopsae, gomjangeo, and grilled beef intestine pieces (#1410), here is the next axis — Seomyeon LP bars, Jeonpo alley cocktails, Millak speakeasies and seafood pubs, and Namcheon-Suyeong jazz and skewers. Only pubs where foreign-language reviews appeared first on Naver Map made the cut — 14 of them, grouped into 4 zones and strung into an evening route. How to pick a bar, the 1-drink-per-person rule, and song-request culture are covered in #1364

ISSUE 06 · #1436 · 2026-11-23 · Busan Drinks Part 3

🎯 Why "Three-Language Reviews" Is the Bar

There are already two Busan drinking pieces — the Jeonpo and Gwangalli bar-hopping guide (#1364) and the nakgopsae, gomjangeo, and grilled beef intestine guide (#1410). This piece collects only venues that appear in neither. Even in the same Jeonpo and Gwangalli alleys, the venues don't overlap.

There's one selection rule — pubs on Naver Map (네이버 지도) with English, Japanese, and Chinese reviews together. Not Korean popularity rankings — the judgment is that a place foreign visitors have already found and left traces in three languages is the safest pick for the next one. Review counts are a 2026-11 snapshot, and this piece lists that count for every venue. There are two exceptions — Robata Aki (로바타아키) and Pyoryu (표류) have only English reviews, but their character fits this piece's axis, so they're in. Venue character and review language don't always line up — even the izakaya Robata Aki has English-only reviews.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours, closed days, and menu prices are the values the owner registered on Kakao Map (카카오맵) (checked 2026-11-23). Where nothing is registered, the cell says "check Kakao Map on the day." Prices are given only per 1 drink or per 1 plate of food — no per-person budget is stated, because it depends on how many drinks and plates you order. Walking times from stations are Kakao Map walking-direction values (measured from the exit). How to pick a bar, the 1-drink-per-person rule, and song-request culture are in #1364.

① Seomyeon·Bujeon — 3 Venues

Within about 5–6 minutes' walk of the exits of Seomyeon Station (서면역). Of the three venues in this piece with English menu names registered, two are here.

  • Mangmi Blues (망미블루스) — a whiskey music pub that spins LPs. The lineup is whiskey, highballs, beer, and small food, and the Kakao Map menu names are registered in English too (whiskey / highball / beer / small food). With 10 foreign reviews (4·3·3), it has the most in this piece, with 3 or more in each of the three languages. Opens 19:00, until 01:00 Sun–Thu and 02:00 Fri·Sat, closed Tuesday. About 5 minutes on foot from Exit 8 of Seomyeon Station, 3rd floor. The name dates from its Mangmi-dong (망미동) days, but it has now moved to Seomyeon — if your map app shows the old Mangmi-dong address, search again with the Seomyeon address (Jungang-daero 680beon-gil 80-20, 3F, Busanjin-gu (부산진구 중앙대로680번가길 80-20 3층)). Check prices on Kakao Map on the day.
  • Siwol (시월) — a whiskey, cocktail, and wine LP bar. Kakao Map menu names are registered in English such as Wine Selection, Highball Selection, and Non-Alcoholic Selection, and there is a separate non-alcoholic section — you can come along even with a friend who doesn't drink. Tue–Thu·Sun 18:00–01:00, until 02:00 Fri·Sat, closed Monday. About 6 minutes on foot from Exit 8 of Seomyeon Station, 2nd floor, Room 202. Check prices on Kakao Map on the day.
  • Robata Aki (로바타아키) — a robata izakaya where you sit at a dacchi (다찌) counter in front of the charcoal. Assorted grilled vegetables (야채모듬구이) 8,000 won, Boston sea urchin (보스턴우니) 50g 29,000 won, 6-piece sashimi (6품 사시미) 34,000 won — plates run 8,000–34,000 won each. English reviews only, 4 of them. Wed–Mon 18:00–01:00, closed Tuesday. About 5 minutes on foot from Exit 4 of Seomyeon Station. The sashimi and sea urchin are raw, so if you want to avoid raw food, pick from the grilled side.

② Jeonpo Alleys — 3 Venues

The alleys toward Exits 4 and 7 of Jeonpo Station (전포역). Same area as the Jeonpo whiskey bars in #1364, but different venues. All three are open daily, so there's no weekday worry.

  • Holiday Bar (홀리데이바) — a cocktail bar playing jazz and LPs, with a generous food lineup. Pies (파이류) 16,000 won, burrata (부라타) 16,000 won, meat chili cheese fries (미트칠리치즈프라이) 17,000 won. Check cocktail prices on Kakao Map on the day. Daily 18:30–01:30. About 5 minutes on foot from Exit 4 of Jeonpo Station.
  • Poster Bar (포스터바) — an outdoor-terrace cocktail bar on the 1st floor of the electrical and electronics market building (전기전자상가). Food 6,000–18,000 won (Basque cheesecake (바스크 치즈케이크) 10,000 won, burrata 18,000 won). Check drink prices on Kakao Map on the day. Daily 18:00–02:00. About 7 minutes on foot from Exit 7 of Jeonpo Station. The terrace is cold in winter — check Kakao Map photos first to see whether there's indoor seating.
  • Huildam (후일담) — an LP cocktail bar. Highballs, wine, and bottled beer 9,000 won; whiskey and cocktails 10,000 won — the lower of the two venues in this piece where a per-drink price is confirmed. Daily 18:00–02:00. About 7 minutes on foot from Exit 7 of Jeonpo Station, 2nd floor. It and Poster Bar are both a 7-minute walk from Exit 7.

③ Gwangalli·Millak — 4 Venues

The alleys running from Gwangalli Beach (광안리 해변) toward Millak-dong (민락동). It's 13–15 minutes' walk from Exit 1 of Gwangan Station (광안역), so it's easier to walk in from the beach than from the station. henry17 and Konabari are on the same street, Millak-ro 27beon-gil (민락로27번길).

  • henry17 (앙리17) — a 2nd-floor speakeasy cocktail and whiskey bar. The sign reads "Angri 17 (앙리17)", so try both names in your map app. Highballs from 16,000 won (per the English guide from tablejourney) — the higher of the two venues where a per-drink price is confirmed. Food: buffalo wings (버팔로윙) 15,000 won, Spanish ham platter (스패니쉬햄플래터) 30,000 won. Hours aren't registered on Kakao Map, and the English guide lists 19:00–03:00 (opens 20:00 on Mondays) — check Kakao Map on the day. The same guide recommends reserving through Naver. About 13 minutes on foot from Exit 1 of Gwangan Station.
  • Pyoryu (표류) — a highball bar whose signature is the soy-milk highball (두유하이볼). All 5 reviews are in English. Food 12,000–17,000 won (cheese toast (치즈토스트) 12,000 won, gnocchi gratin (뇨끼 그라탕) 17,000 won). Check highball prices on Kakao Map on the day. Daily 18:00–02:00. About 14 minutes on foot from Exit 1 of Gwangan Station. It's on the same street as Basement in #1364, so it's easy to mix the two pieces into a two-bar crawl.
  • Konabari (코나바리) — a Japanese-style pub serving seasonal seafood. Mapo tofu clay pot (마파두부 뚝배기) 16,000 won, Pohang cuttlefish (포항 무늬오징어) 30,000 won, daily mixed platter (당일바리모듬) 60,000 won — plates run 16,000–60,000 won each, built for sharing among several people. Reviews in three languages, 2·2·2. Daily 18:00–03:00. About 15 minutes on foot from Exit 1 of Gwangan Station. On the same street as henry17, so they link up: Konabari first, henry17 second.
  • Sudal Bar Gwangan (수달바 광안) — a Korean pub where you sit at a dacchi counter and drink traditional liquor (전통주). Jjolmyeon sundubu stew (쫄면순두부찌개) 15,000 won, Korean beef yukhoe rice bowl (한우육회강밥) 19,000 won, fish-cake hot pot with rice-cake skewers (물떡어묵탕) 25,000 won — plates run 15,000–25,000 won each. Sun–Thu 17:00–02:00, until 03:00 Fri·Sat. No walking time from the station is registered, so check with Kakao Map walking directions. Yukhoe is raw beef.

④ Namcheon·Suyeong·Mangmi — 4 Venues

Namcheon-dong (남천동) west of Gwangalli and the area around Suyeong Station (수영역). Deeper in the alleys than the Gwangalli beachfront, and two of the venues are close to Suyeong Station exits.

  • Jazzpresso (재즈프레소) — a cafe-bar playing jazz and LPs, with live-performance notices posted too. Food 12,000–18,000 won (burrata 17,000 won, grilled sausage and potatoes (구운 소세지와 감자) 18,000 won). Check drink prices and the live schedule in Kakao Map notices on the day. Tue–Sat 19:00–24:00, closed Monday and Sunday — the only venue in this piece with two days off. About 13 minutes on foot from Exit 3 of Namcheon Station (남천역), 2nd floor.
  • Chohissatsu (초힛사츠) — a charcoal skewer izakaya. Yakitori pork (야끼톤) 3,500 won each (order from 2), fried spare ribs (등갈비튀김) 12,000 won, tomato-egg omelet (도페이야끼) 13,000 won — skewers are priced each, food in the 12,000–13,000 won range. Daily 17:00–02:00. About 5 minutes on foot from Exit 3 of Geumnyeonsan Station (금련산역).
  • Musicology (뮤직올로지) — a basement-level (B1) LP bar with cocktails, whiskey, and wine. Kakao Map menu names are registered in English too (Cocktail, Whisky, red wine). Opens at 15:00, so you can time it for daytime drinking or an early evening. Mon·Tue·Thu·Sun until 24:00, Fri·Sat until 01:00, closed Wednesday. Curry rice (카레라이스) 13,000 won; check other prices on Kakao Map on the day. About 8 minutes on foot from Exit 2 of Suyeong Station.
  • Bogwangdangtangjewon (보광당탕제원) — a cooking pub serving yukhoe and sashimi dishes. Crab-miso noodles (카니미소누들) 16,000 won, sea urchin yukhoe (우니육회) 37,000 won, mixed sashimi (사시미모둠) 40,000 won — plates run 16,000–40,000 won each. Mon–Sat 18:00–24:00, closed Sunday. About 4 minutes on foot from Exit 3 of Suyeong Station. The food is mostly raw, so if you want to avoid raw food, go for the crab-miso noodles (check ingredients and allergens with staff before ordering).

🗺 Evening Routes — Two Branches

A. Seomyeon·Jeonpo Night (Seomyeon Station → Jeonpo Station)

  1. Round 1: Exit 4 of Seomyeon Station → Robata Aki charcoal dishes (from 18:00, closed Tue)
  2. Round 2: toward Exit 8 of Seomyeon Station → Mangmi Blues or Siwol LP bar (Mangmi Blues on Monday, Siwol on Tuesday)
  3. Round 3: Jeonpo alleys → 1 drink at Huildam (9,000–10,000 won) → Poster Bar toward the same Exit 7, or Holiday Bar toward Exit 4 (until 01:30)

B. Gwangalli·Namcheon·Suyeong Night (Suyeong Station → Gwangalli)

  1. Early evening: Exit 2 of Suyeong Station → Musicology (from 15:00, closed Wed), or Exit 3 of Geumnyeonsan Station → Chohissatsu skewers (from 17:00)
  2. Round 1: Exit 3 of Suyeong Station → Bogwangdangtangjewon (closed Sun), or Namcheon's Jazzpresso (Tue–Sat)
  3. Round 2: Gwangalli Beach → Millak alley Konabari seafood → henry17 on the same street
  4. Last stop: Pyoryu highballs, or Sudal Bar Gwangan traditional liquor (until 03:00 Fri·Sat)

On Tuesday the Round 1 of A (Robata Aki) is closed, and on Sunday the Round 1 of B (Bogwangdangtangjewon and Jazzpresso) is closed, so start from the Round 2 venue. If you're going back by subway, check the last Busan subway train time before you set out. If you miss the last train, take a taxi. To add the night view, see #1326.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Mangmi Blues and Robata Aki on Tuesday, Siwol on Monday, Musicology on Wednesday, Bogwangdangtangjewon on Sunday — each is closed that day. Jazzpresso is closed two days, Monday and Sunday. Check the day of the week first.
  2. Going without ID — the legal drinking age in Korea is 19 in Korean counting (age by calendar year — everyone turns a year older on January 1) (Article 2 of the Youth Protection Act (청소년보호법)). Foreigners are sometimes asked to show a passport at the door or when ordering, so carry it.
  3. Skipping "check Kakao Map on the day" — henry17's hours, drink prices at several bars, and Jazzpresso's live schedule aren't registered. Before you go, the business info and notices on Kakao Map are that day's answer.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. If I could pick only one? — The venue with the most foreign reviews is Mangmi Blues (10 reviews, 4·3·3); the lower of the venues with a confirmed per-drink price is Huildam (9,000–10,000 won); and with a friend who doesn't drink, Siwol (non-alcoholic menu).
  2. Which have English menus? — Menu names are registered in English at three venues: Mangmi Blues, Siwol, and Musicology. For the rest, it isn't known whether an English menu exists, so have a translation app ready.
  3. Bar manners and how to order? — The 1-drink-per-person rule, song requests, and how to pick a bar seat are in #1364. The tables at pocha and gopchang places are in #1410.
