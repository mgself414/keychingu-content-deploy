3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Thinking "makgeolli (막걸리) = just rice wine"

Korean makgeolli (막걸리) comes in 50+ varieties by region, ABV, and fermentation method — Pyeongchang (평창) sweet pumpkin makgeolli, Jeju (제주) Udo (우도) peanut makgeolli, Seoul 7.8-degree makgeolli, and more. From a foreign English review of 7.8 Makgeolli First Branch (7.8 막걸리 1호점): "Wide variety of makgeolli from all over the peninsula" — you can sample makgeolli from across the Korean peninsula in one sitting. On your first visit, ask for a "multi-variety sampling" first.

2. Assuming "traditional liquor = low ABV"

  • Makgeolli (막걸리): 6–8% ABV (slightly stronger than beer)
  • Yakju (약주) · Cheongju (청주): 13–17% ABV (wine-level)
  • Soju (소주): 16–25% ABV (spirit-level)
  • Traditional distilled soju (안동소주, Andong Soju, etc.): 40–50% ABV (whisky-level)

When tasting at Korean Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리) or Boksoondoga (복순도가), 4 samples ≈ 1 glass of wine. From a foreign Japanese review of Boksoondoga (복순도가): "試飲もさせてくれます。繊細な口当たりのマッコリが本当に美味しい" — even if it feels smooth on the palate, be aware of the actual ABV.

3. Thinking "brewery = factory tour"

Downtown Seoul breweries like Boseok Brewery (보석양조) and Euljiro Brewing (을지맥옥) follow a microbrewery model where the drinking space IS the brewing space — you taste freshly brewed liquor right next to the fermentation tanks. From a foreign Japanese review of Euljiro Brewing (을지맥옥): "乙支路はレトロの聖地ですもんね。ビールは相変わらず美味しいです" — a cornerstone of Euljiro's (을지로) retro cluster.

Type ABV Characteristics Per-Person Budget Recommended Pairing

Yakju (약주) 13–17% Clarified layer above makgeolli, golden · dry ₩15,000~₩30,000 Hanjeongsik (한정식) · traditional anju (안주) · dumplings

3. FAQ — 6 Questions Foreign Visitors Frequently Ask

Q1. Payment — Is WeChat Pay or Alipay accepted?

  • All 8 venues accept card, KakaoPay (카카오페이), and Samsung Pay (삼성페이).
  • Boksoondoga Seoul Station (복순도가 서울역점), Korean Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리), and Ace Four Club (에이스포클럽) are on popular foreign visitor routes and support diverse payment options.
  • Breweries like Boseok Brewery (보석양조) and some hanok venues: cash recommended as backup.

Q2. Are English menus available?

  • Boksoondoga (복순도가) / Korean Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리) / Ace Four Club (에이스포클럽) / Uyajae (우야재) / Eulji Haok (을지하옥): English menus ✓ (English-speaking staff)
  • 7.8 Makgeolli (7.8 막걸리) / Euljiro Brewing (을지맥옥): Partial English menus (multilingual tablet ordering)
  • Boseok Brewery (보석양조): Primarily Korean (ask staff for recommendations)

Q3. Can I visit solo?

  • All 8 venues welcome solo visitors. Solo drinking is natural in Korean drinking culture.
  • Uyajae (우야재) and Eulji Haok (을지하옥) are small — advance reservation recommended.

Q4. Vegan or halal options?

  • Makgeolli (막걸리), yakju (약주), and cheongju (청주) are vegan as fermented grain beverages (no eggs or dairy). However, anju (안주, snacks) typically contain animal-derived ingredients.
  • No halal-certified venues in this guide. Alcohol itself is prohibited in Islam — venues that serve food only are outside the scope of this guide.

Q5. Can I bring children?

  • This guide is alcohol-focused — not recommended for children.
  • That said, Uyajae (우야재) and Eulji Haok (을지하옥) offer meal-focused courses where children may be accommodated. Korean Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리) allows family visits for the gallery exhibition portion (outside of tasting).

Q6. Where to buy carry-on gifts?

  • Boksoondoga Seoul Station (복순도가 서울역점): Direct airport connection, free cold packaging, carry-on approved. Top recommendation.
  • Korean Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리): Some gift-packaged liquors sold in-store.
  • 7.8 Makgeolli (7.8 막걸리): Small-format bottles available at select branches.
