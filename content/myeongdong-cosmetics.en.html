5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. Going to Only One Olive Young (올리브영)

Myeongdong (명동) has 5 Olive Young (올리브영) stores — including Myeongdong Central (명동중앙점), Myeongdong Town (명동타운점), and Lotte Young Plaza (롯데영플라자점). The Myeongdong Central branch is the largest flagship + foreign-language staff are widely available. No price difference between branches.

2. Not Claiming the Instant Tax Refund

Receipt of ₩30,000+ + foreign passport = instant 9–10% deduction. Show your passport at the checkout counter and it's automatic. No separate process needed. Faster and easier than airport refunds.

3. Not Knowing About Foreigner-Exclusive Packages

Innisfree (이니스프리), Etude (에뛰드), and Laneige (라네즈) flagship stores in Myeongdong (명동) carry foreigner-exclusive boxes (English guide + mini-size sets + gift bags). Not available at regular branches — a Myeongdong (명동) flagship exclusive.

4. Only Buying Sheet Masks

Sheet masks are great value, but Korea-exclusive lines (rom&nd (롬앤), Dear Dahlia (디어달리아), Innisfree Green Tea line (이니스프리 그린티 라인)) are the real find for foreigners. One signature skincare set is more authentically Korean than 100 sheet masks.

5. Not Keeping Your Receipts

Even after receiving an instant refund, keep your receipt and voucher. They may be verified at airport departure, prevent double refunds, and are needed for returns or exchanges.

FAQ

Q1. Does English work?

100%. Myeongdong (명동) stores are Korea's most experienced with foreign visitors.

Q2. Instant refund vs. airport refund — what's the difference?

Instant refund: Automatic deduction at checkout counter / Airport refund: Departure counter + card reimbursement (1–2 weeks). Instant is faster and easier.

Q3. Can foreigners join memberships?

Olive Young (올리브영), Innisfree (이니스프리), Etude (에뛰드) = foreign phone + email OK. Additional 5–10% discount.

Q4. How much is the refund?

9–10% (single receipt ₩30,000+). Some stores may offer up to 11%.

Q5. Where are Korea-exclusive lines?

Innisfree (이니스프리) · Laneige (라네즈) · Sulwhasoo (설화수) Myeongdong flagships = exclusive boxes. Olive Young (올리브영) = seasonal exclusives.

Q6. What about carry-on liquids on flights?

100ml liquid limit → pick up at airport duty-free or pack in checked luggage.
