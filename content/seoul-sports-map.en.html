⚠ 3 Sports Mistakes

1. "Korean baseball = not tourism" — KBO cheering culture not recognized: Assumption of absence of foreign baseball tourism. In reality, KBO cheering culture (chicken and beer, chicken cheering, macro cheering, English guidance for foreigners) is a Korean baseball signature — a full experience for Japanese and American enthusiasts.

2. "Soccer = general game" — K-League, national team, 2002 FIFA legacy not recognized: Lack of awareness of the K-League. In reality, 2002 FIFA semi-final myth + FC Seoul-Suwon rivalry + national team mania.

3. "Taekwondo=absence of tourism" — Kukkiwon/Taekwondowon enthusiasts are not aware: Assuming absence of Taekwondo experience. In reality, it is Kukkiwon (Gangnam) foreigner 1-day experience + Taekwondowon (Muju KTX) camp full experience.
