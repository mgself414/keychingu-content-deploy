🥬 Kimchi Types and Diet — VisitKorea Official

  • The five types the page lists: Baechu Kimchi (배추김치), Kkakdugi (깍두기), Nabak Kimchi (나박김치) (a water kimchi, not spicy), Oi Sobagi (오이소박이) and Yeolmu Kimchi (열무김치). Baechu kimchi is seasoned with red pepper flakes, garlic and fish sauce and then fermented.
  • Vegetarian tip (VisitKorea): "Most kimchi seasoning contains Jeotgal (젓갈), fish sauce or broth." Regional kimchi can also include abalone, cutlassfish or pork. → Vegetarians and vegans should ask before ordering. The official page does not say whether Baek Kimchi (백김치), Dongchimi (동치미) or Chonggak Kimchi (총각김치) are vegan, so we do not claim it.
  • The old guide's halal and kosher claims and its "temple-food kimchi" had no source — Seoul has one KMF-certified Korean restaurant (Eid, #337), where KMF stands for Korea Muslim Federation (KMF).
  • The Kimjang (김장) tradition is on the UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage list, 2013 (official).

🏛 Museum Kimchikan — Insadong

  • Tue–Sun 10–18 (last entry 17:30), closed Mon, 1/1, Seollal, Chuseok and 12/25, 02-2088-8531 (official). Kimchi history, kimchi-making videos and exhibits (VisitKorea). The official site lists hands-on programs only as a menu, with no names or prices visible → ask before booking. There is an English toggle, but the English page would not open.
  • Entry was free on Kimchi Day (김치의 날) (11/22) in 2024 (Seoul city). Whether this repeats in 2026 is unconfirmed.

🎪 Two Kimjang Festivals — Seoul in November, Gwangju in October

  • Korea Kimjang Festival (대한민국 김장대축제) (Seoul): On 11/22 in 2025 at aT Center, with hands-on kimjang alongside master makers, a UNESCO exhibition and direct sales from producers; about 100 multicultural family members and honorary foreign citizens took part (Seoul city, news reports). No 2026 schedule yet — check the Seoul city announcement in early November. It is different from the old "Seoul Kimjang Culture Festival" at City Hall Plaza.
  • Gwangju Kimchi Festival (광주김치축제): The official site shows the 33rd edition but no dates. A secondary source gives 2026-10-23 to 25. The 2025 32nd edition ran 10/31–11/2 with 57,000 visitors. It is designated a Culture and Tourism Festival for 2026–27 (news reports). Sources disagree on the venue, so we do not list it.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Going to Museum Kimchikan on Monday — it is closed.

2. Treating kimchi as vegetarian — most has jeotgal or fish sauce (VisitKorea). Check before ordering.

3. Assuming a date for the November Seoul kimjang festival — 2026 is not announced. 2025 was 11/22.

4. Treating the Gwangju festival dates as official — the official site has no dates (secondary: 10/23–25).

5. Trusting the old price tiers and restaurants — the old 12 picks had no sources. Only the 4 listed places.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. How do I learn about kimchi? — Museum Kimchikan exhibits (Tue–Sun); ask about hands-on programs. The class has only a secondary source.

2. When are the kimjang festivals? — Seoul 2025 11/22 (2026 not announced) · Gwangju 2026 10/23–25 (secondary).

3. Is there vegan kimchi? — Not on the official page. Baechu kimchi is seasoned with fish sauce.

4. Where are the kimchi restaurants? — Gwanghwamun Jip, Yetgol and Eunjujeong, listed on VisitSeoul (table above). For jokbal and kimchi pancakes, choose on site at Gwangjang Market or Jangchung-dong.

5. What is the entry fee? — Not posted officially for Museum Kimchikan (secondary: 5,000). Free on Kimchi Day (2024).

6. Is kimjang on the UNESCO list? — Yes, inscribed in 2013 (official).
