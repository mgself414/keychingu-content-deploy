🥬 Kimchi Types and Diet — VisitKorea Official

  • The five types the page lists: Baechu Kimchi (배추김치), Kkakdugi (깍두기), Nabak Kimchi (나박김치) (water kimchi, not spicy), Oi Sobagi (오이소박이) and Yeolmu Kimchi (열무김치). Baechu kimchi is seasoned with chili powder, garlic and fish sauce and then fermented.
  • Vegetarian tip (VisitKorea): "Most kimchi seasoning contains salted seafood, fish sauce or broth." Regional kimchi can also include abalone, hairtail or pork. → Vegetarians and vegans should ask before ordering. The official page does not say whether Baek Kimchi (백김치), Dongchimi (동치미) or Chonggak Kimchi (총각김치) is vegan, so we do not claim it.
  • The halal and kosher sections and the "temple-food kimchi" claim in the older guide have no source — Seoul has one Korea Muslim Federation (KMF)-certified Korean restaurant (Eid, #337).
  • The Kimjang (김장) culture is on the UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage list, 2013 (official).

🏛 Museum Kimchikan (뮤지엄김치간) — Insadong (인사동)

  • Tue–Sun 10–18 (last entry 17:30), closed Mondays, Jan 1, Lunar New Year, Chuseok and Dec 25, 02-2088-8531 (official). Videos on kimchi history and kimchi making, plus exhibits (VisitKorea). The official site shows only a menu for hands-on programs, with no names or prices → ask before booking. There is an English toggle, but the English page would not open.
  • Admission was free on Kimchi Day (김치의 날) (11/22) in 2024 (Seoul city). Whether this repeats in 2026 is unverified.

🎪 Two Kimjang (김장) Festivals — Seoul in November, Gwangju in October

  • Korea Kimjang Festival (대한민국 김장대축제) (Seoul): on 11/22 in 2025 at aT Center — kimjang hands-on with master makers, a UNESCO exhibition, direct sales from producers, and about 100 participants from multicultural families and foreign honorary citizens (Seoul city · press). No 2026 schedule yet — check the Seoul city announcement in early November. It is different from the older "Seoul Kimjang Culture Festival" at City Hall Plaza.
  • Gwangju Kimchi Festival (광주김치축제): the official site displays the 33rd edition but gives no dates. A secondary source says 2026-10-23 to 25. The 2025 32nd edition ran 10/31–11/2 with 57,000 visitors. It was designated a culture and tourism festival for 2026–27 (press). The venue is not stated because sources differ.

🍲 Why There Are No Restaurants

  • The new Michelin Seoul 2026 Bib Gourmand list has no kimchi, bossam or jokbal place, and no other official source names a specific restaurant. For kimchi jjigae, kimchi pancakes and bossam, choose in Gwangjang Market (광장시장) Food Alley (daily 09–23) by map-app review counts and what is open that day. "Free unlimited refills", "30-year old-timer = made in house" and "aged kimchi 6+ months" also have no source, so we removed them.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Visiting Museum Kimchikan on a Monday — it is closed.

2. Treating kimchi as vegetarian — most contains salted seafood or fish sauce (VisitKorea). Check before ordering.

3. Assuming the November Seoul kimjang festival date — 2026 is not announced. 2025 was 11/22.

4. Taking the Gwangju festival dates as official — the official site shows no dates (secondary 10/23–25).

5. Trusting the old price ranges and restaurants — none have a source.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. How can I learn about kimchi? — The Museum Kimchikan exhibits (Tue–Sun); ask about hands-on programs. For classes there is only a secondary source.

2. When are the kimjang festivals? — Seoul 2025 on 11/22 (2026 not announced) · Gwangju 2026 on 10/23–25 (secondary).

3. Is there vegan kimchi? — Not on the official pages. Baechu kimchi is seasoned with fish sauce.

4. Which kimchi restaurants? — None in this guide. Use Gwangjang Food Alley plus a map app.

5. What is the admission fee? — Museum Kimchikan has not posted it officially (secondary 5,000). Free on Kimchi Day (2024).

6. Is kimjang a UNESCO item? — Yes, listed in 2013 (official).
