Regional Market Guides Collection — Index of 27 City Guides for Gangwon, Chungcheong, Jeolla, Gyeongsang, Gyeonggi, Incheon, and Jeju

Subtitle: An index that groups the city-by-city market guides outside Seoul by region. The operating details in each guide are values confirmed through Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI) and official sources.

🎯 How to Use This Guide

KeyChinGu covers markets outside Seoul with one separate guide per city. This guide is a list that gathers those market guides by region. After choosing a city, check market days and operating details in each guide.

Many markets have market days. A five-day market is set by the last digit of the date. For example, a "2nd-and-7th market" is held on the 2nd, 7th, 12th, 17th, 22nd, and 27th. Even permanent markets have different closed days by shop, so look at the "Closed days" column of each guide first.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Thinking market days are weekdays — A five-day market is held by the last digit of the date, not the day of the week. A "4th-and-9th market" means the 4th, 9th, 14th, 19th, 24th, and 29th.
  2. Thinking night markets open every day — Daegu Seomun Market's night market is open Fri, Sat, and Sun, Gyeongju Jungang Market's night market Thu–Sun, and Gwangju Daein Market's night market and Jinju Nongae Market's night market on Saturday.
  3. Going by the market name alone — A single city has several markets of different character. Look at the items and closed days in each guide's "At a Glance" table first.
