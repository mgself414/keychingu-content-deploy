Why scones now — British-Korean fusion of the K-cafe scene

Since 2015, the Seoul cafe scene has exploded with ① authentic British cream tea + ② casual everyday scones + ③ Korean flavor experiments simultaneously. Scones are good value for money (3,500 to 6,500 won), can be taken out, and pair well with tea and coffee, so they are the top choice for foreigners' first K-cafe introductory menu. In particular, authentic stores such as SCOFF Bakehouse (Buam-dong, 2014~), run directly by a British baker, On Hyehwa (Daehakro), which combines British cream tea with a Hanok cafe, multiple classic scone restaurants using gourmet butter, and Korean variations such as Teddy Scones at High West Ikseon and Spread Seoul's 10+ Flavor Rotation coexist.

This matrix was classified into 4 axes by adding 6 seed stores (foreigner Instagram awareness ↑) + 6 verified authentic and experimental stores. Jongno 4, Seongdong 1, Songpa 1, Gwangjin 1, Jungnang 1, Seongbuk 1, Mapo 1, Seodaemun 1 — covers 8 autonomous districts of Seoul.

FAQ 6 — Frequently asked scone questions for foreigners

Q1. What is the difference between British, casual and Korean scones?

  • British (UK): Small, crunchy, not sweet → Clotted cream + jam pairing required. SCOFF Buam-dong (#1), Quiet Cream Tea (#2), and On Hye-hwa (#3) are authentic.
  • Casual Everyday: Feel free to eat at a regular cafe bakery → Individual consumption and takeout are OK. Scope Seochon (#4), Cafe Onion (#5), Eunbis Bread (#6).
  • Korean (KR): Experiment with Korean ingredients (Earl Grey, ink, chives, mugwort and soybean paste) → Insta and MZ are strong. Seongsu Baking (#7), Spread Seoul (#8), and High Waist Ikseon (#9).

Q2. Where do you recommend for a foreigner’s first visit?

  • British Authentic Mania (EN·JP): SCOFF Bakehouse Buam-dong #1 (British baker, English OK)
  • Insta·Visual Mania (MZ EN·JP·CN): Cafe Onion Anguk #5 + Layered Yeonnam #11
  • Cost-effective/Casual (student/budget): Eunvis Bread Konkuk University #6 / Scope Seochon #4
  • Korean experiment (curious foodie): High waist Ikseon #9 (Teddy scone) + Spread Seoul #8 (10+ flavors)
  • Early Bird (7-8 am): Cafe Onion Anguk #5 (07:00) / Seongsu Baking #7 (08:00)

Q3. Average scone price range?

  • Full set of British authentic cream tea: KRW 12,000~18,000 (2 scones + tea + clotted cream + jam)
  • Individual scones: 3,500~6,500 won (based on onion 5,500 won)
  • Signature drink + scone: KRW 7,000~10,000
  • Student price·Cost-effectiveness: 3,500~4,500 won (Eunbis Bread)

Q4. Where can I get a full set of cream tea?

  • SCOFF Bakehouse Buam-dong #1 (Authentic British cream tea + Tea selection)
  • Quiet Cream Tea Songpa #2 (Korean-style British cream tea specialty store)
  • Onhyehwa Daehakro #3 (Hanok + British cream tea fusion + gourmet butter)

Q5. Recommended menu for foreign enthusiast season?

  • Spring (March~May): Strawberry, peach, apricot season → Spread Seoul #8·SCOFF #1 season limited
  • Summer (June~August): Earl Gray Ice → Quiet Cream Tea #2·Layered #11
  • Fall (September~November): Fig, pumpkin, cinnamon → SCOFF #1 Seasonal limited·Plutt Coffee #10
  • Winter (December~February): Gingerbread·Cinnamon + Hot Chocolate → SCOFF #1·On Hyehwa #3

Q6. Tips on weekdays, weekends, opening hours?

  • Early Bird (07:00~): Cafe Onion Anguk #5 (Weekdays 07:00) / Seongsu Baking #7 (Daily 08:00)
  • Long time (~22:00): High waist Ikseon #9 (~22) / Layered Yeonnam #11 (~22) / Quiet cream tea #2 (~21)
  • Recommended before 11AM on weekends: SCOFF Buam #1·Scope Seochon #4·Layered #11 (waiting 30 minutes+)
  • Weekdays 2-4PM Hansan: Paul & Paulina #12·Plut #10·Eunbis Bread #6
  • Stores open on Monday: Cafe Onion #5, Seongsu Baking #7, High Waist #9, Layered #11 (Closed on Mondays. Note 5 locations: SCOFF, Quiet, Onhyehwa, Eunbis, Plut)
