13 Korean Duty Free Shops — Downtown Stores in Seoul, Busan and Jeju, Airport Stores, and the Tax Refund Rules for Immediate and After-Purchase Refunds

Subtitle: Addresses and hours (official branch pages, 2026-11-24) for 5 downtown duty free shops in Seoul, 3 downtown duty free shops in Busan and Jeju, and 5 airport duty free shops at Incheon, Gimpo, Gimhae and Jeju, plus the Tax Refund rules for the Immediate Tax Refund and the After-Purchase Refund, all in one piece. You need your passport and your departure flight ticket to buy at a duty free shop, and you collect the goods at the airport pickup counter on your departure day.

ISSUE 06 · #775 · 2026-11-24 · Duty-Free Shopping

🎯 Who This Piece Is For

This piece is for travelers who want to buy cosmetics, perfume, watches and bags tax-free before leaving Korea. First, the two systems kept apart.

  • Duty Free shop (면세점): a shop that sells to people leaving the country with customs duty and VAT removed. If you pay at a downtown duty free shop, you get an exchange voucher instead of the goods, and you collect the goods at the airport pickup counter on your departure day (Lotte Duty Free (롯데면세점) official usage guide).
  • Tax Refund (택스리펀) (Tax Free · Tax Refund): a system that returns the VAT on goods bought at ordinary stores such as department stores and Olive Young (올리브영). You carry the goods out of the store on the spot. There is the Immediate Tax Refund (즉시환급), where the tax is taken off at the register, and the After-Purchase Refund (사후환급), where you get a slip and claim the money at a downtown booth or at the airport.

This piece puts the location and hours of 13 duty free shops and the number rules of both systems on one page. How to choose a downtown duty free shop is covered in more detail in #915, and the comparison of the three Tax Refund routes is in #1231.

⚠ Notation principle: Branch addresses and hours are the values on each duty free shop's official branch page (checked 2026-11-24). Business status was checked as "open" (영업) on Kakao Map (카카오맵) (2026-11-24). For the Incheon Airport stores, only one terminal per operator (Lotte T1, Shilla T2) was checked on Kakao Map, and the other terminals use the official branch page values. Lotte Duty Free notes on its branch pages that closing times may differ by brand store. Discount rates, gifts and exchange rates change often and are not listed.

📋 Rules to Know First

Duty Free Rules

  • Who can buy: anyone leaving Korea for overseas, Korean or foreign. You cannot buy for domestic trips, including to Jeju (Lotte Duty Free official usage guide, read 2026-11-24).
  • What to bring: bring your own passport and a flight ticket (E-ticket) that shows your departure details to the store. Shilla Duty Free (신라면세점) says you can buy from 60 days before your departure date (Shilla Duty Free official shopping guide, read 2026-11-24). At the internet duty free shop you enter your passport and entry and exit details on the order form, and if you enter them wrongly you cannot receive the goods (Lotte Duty Free shopping procedure).
  • How much you can buy: there is no purchase limit on duty free goods when leaving, for Koreans and foreigners alike (Lotte Duty Free official usage guide). The US$800 duty free allowance applies when you enter Korea. For the allowance when you enter your own country, check that country's rules separately.
  • Where to collect: goods bought downtown or online are collected at the designated pickup counter (인도장) on your departure day. At the pickup counter you show the exchange voucher, your physical passport and your boarding pass, have your passport details checked, and sign for receipt. Lotte Duty Free tells you to come to the pickup counter 1 hour before departure. If you do not collect within 15 days after your departure date, the order is cancelled (Lotte Duty Free official pickup counter guide and usage guide, read 2026-11-24). For goods bought at Shilla, Shinsegae (신세계) and Hyundai (현대), follow each operator's pickup counter guide. The pickup counter locations in this piece are Lotte Duty Free's. The order for finding a pickup counter is in #1332.

  • The Immediate Refund means you show your passport when you pay and are charged the price with the tax taken off. If a purchase is 1,000,000 won or more at once, or the total passes 5,000,000 won, you get a slip instead of the Immediate Refund and claim it at a downtown booth or at the airport.
  • The After-Purchase Refund is mainly claimed at the airport or port. The order is ① check in and get your boarding pass, but do not check your bags yet ② show the goods and the refund slip to a customs officer and get the customs export stamp (반출확인 도장) ③ check your bags ④ hand over your passport and slip at the nearest refund counter or kiosk and apply for the refund. Cash refunds are received in the duty free area after security (Korea Tourism Organization). The screen order is in #1304.
  • Goods must be new, unopened and unused to get the export confirmation and the refund (Korea Tourism Organization, updated 2025-10-21).
  • If one receipt is over 6,000,000 won, you cannot claim at a downtown booth and can only get the refund at the airport or port (Korea Tourism Organization).
  • Goods bought at a duty free shop already have the tax taken off, so they are not eligible for a Tax Refund.

🏙 Seoul Downtown Duty Free — 5 Venues

1. Lotte Duty Free Myeongdong Main Store (롯데면세점 명동본점)

It is on floors 9~12 of the Lotte Department Store (롯데백화점) Main Branch building. The address is 30 Eulji-ro, Jung-gu (중구 을지로 30), and the hours are 09:30~20:00. Lotte Duty Free notes that store operation and shopping closing times differ by brand. The branch phone is 02-759-6600 and the main phone is 1688-3000 (Lotte Duty Free official branch page). Kakao Map lists the same building as 81 Namdaemun-ro (남대문로 81). The nearest station is Euljiro Ipgu Station (을지로입구역) (Line 2).

2. Lotte Duty Free World Tower Store (롯데면세점 월드타워점)

It is on floors 8·9 of Avenuel Jamsil Store (에비뉴엘 잠실점) next to Lotte World Tower (롯데월드타워). The address is 300 Olympic-ro, Songpa-gu (송파구 올림픽로 300), and the hours are 10:30~19:30. The branch phone is 02-3213-3700 (Lotte Duty Free official branch page). The nearest station is Jamsil Station (잠실역) (Lines 2·8). It closes at 19:30, the earliest of the 5 downtown stores, so go before your evening plans.

3. Shilla Duty Free Seoul Store (신라면세점 서울점)

It is next to the Shilla Hotel (신라호텔). The address is 249 Dongho-ro, Jung-gu (중구 동호로 249), and the hours are 09:30~20:00, open year-round. The main phone is 1688-1110. From Exit 5 of Dongdaeipgu Station (동대입구역) (Line 3), go to the Shilla Hotel main gate and take the hotel shuttle bus. If you came by taxi, hand your taxi receipt to the outside information desk and get a same-day prepaid card worth 10,000 won (once a month). Valet parking is available whether or not you buy anything, and the fee is separate (Shilla Duty Free official branch page).

4. Shinsegae Duty Free Myeongdong Store (신세계면세점 명동점)

It is on floors 8~12 of the Shinsegae Department Store (신세계백화점) Main Branch. The address is 77 Toegye-ro, Jung-gu (중구 퇴계로 77). The hours are Mon~Thu 10:30~20:00, and Fri~Sun and public holidays 10:30~20:30. Leave through Exit 7 of Hoehyeon Station (회현역) (Line 4). Phone consultation is 1661-8778 (daily 09:00~18:00) (Shinsegae Duty Free official branch page). It is in the same Jung-gu (중구) as Lotte Myeongdong Main Store, so you can put both in one day's plan.

5. Hyundai Duty Free Trade Center Store (현대면세점 무역센터점)

It is on floors 8·9 of the Hyundai Department Store (현대백화점) Trade Center building. The address is 517 Teheran-ro, Gangnam-gu (강남구 테헤란로 517). The hours are Mon~Thu 10:30~20:00 and Fri~Sun 10:30~20:30, open year-round. Exit 5 of Samseong Station (삼성역) (Line 2) connects directly to the department store's basement floor 1. There are membership and group desks on floor 9. The main phone is 1811-6688 (Hyundai Duty Free official branch page).

🌊 Busan·Jeju Downtown Duty Free — 3 Venues

The branch phone is 051-810-3880 (Lotte Duty Free official branch page). The nearest station is Seomyeon Station (서면역) (Busan Lines 1·2). If you buy to match a departure from Gimhae Airport, the Lotte pickup counter is on floor 2 of Gimhae International Airport (김해국제공항), opposite Gates 12·13 (06:00~22:00, Lotte Duty Free pickup counter guide).

7. Lotte Duty Free Jeju Store (롯데면세점 제주점)

It is in Yeondong (연동), Jeju-si. The address is 83 Doryeong-ro (도령로 83), and the hours are 10:00~19:00. The branch phone is 064-793-3000 (Lotte Duty Free official branch page). Domestic passengers flying from Jeju to another region cannot buy. Only people leaving for overseas can buy (Lotte Duty Free official usage guide). The Lotte pickup counter is behind immigration on floor 3 of Jeju International Airport (제주국제공항) (05:30~22:00, Lotte Duty Free pickup counter guide).

8. Shilla Duty Free Jeju Store (신라면세점 제주점)

It is in the same Yeondong (연동) as Lotte Jeju Store. The address is 69 Noyeon-ro, Jeju-si (제주시 노연로 69), and the hours are 10:00~19:00, open year-round. The main phone is 1688-1110. Regular buses 202·251·282 and others stop at the Halla Hospital (한라병원) stop (Shilla Duty Free official branch page). Like Lotte Jeju Store, you cannot buy if your trip is domestic flights only.

✈ Airport Duty Free — 5 Venues

You enter airport duty free shops only after clearing departure immigration. The pickup counters for goods bought downtown are also behind immigration.

9. Lotte Duty Free Incheon Airport Store (롯데면세점 인천공항점) (T1·T2)

It is on floor 3 of Terminal 1 (272 Gonghang-ro (공항로 272)) and floor 3 of Terminal 2 (446 Terminal 2-daero (제2터미널대로 446)). Both terminals open 06:30~21:30 and run year-round. Night stores open 21:30~06:30. The main T1 stores are near Gates 11·29·34·117, and at T2 near Gates 245·248·249·251~253·255 (Lotte Duty Free official branch page). Lotte T1 pickup counters are in the east wing (floor 4 opposite Gate 11, from 05:30), the west wing (floor 4 opposite Gate 45, from 04:30), and the Concourse (after getting off the Shuttle Train, floor 4 at Gate 119, from 04:30). At T2 they are the east wing (toward Gate 274, take the escalator down opposite the Korean Traditional Culture Center (한국전통문화센터) East Hall (동관), from 05:00) and the west wing (toward Gate 225, take the escalator down opposite the Korean Traditional Culture Center, from 05:00) (Lotte Duty Free pickup counter guide).

10. Shilla Duty Free Incheon Airport Store (신라면세점 인천공항점) (T1·T2)

Both T1 (272 Gonghang-ro (공항로 272)) and T2 (446 Terminal 2-daero (제2터미널대로 446), which Shilla's official page writes as 446 Gonghang-ro (공항로 446)) are open 06:30~21:30. Find the main stores at T1 near departure hall 1 and Gate 11, and at T2 near departure hall 2 and Gate 251 (Shilla Duty Free official branch page). 11. Lotte Duty Free Gimpo Airport Store (롯데면세점 김포공항점)

It is on floor 3 of the Gimpo Airport International Terminal (김포공항 국제선청사) (38 Haneul-gil, Gangseo-gu (강서구 하늘길 38)). The hours are 06:10~20:30. The branch phone is 02-2669-6705 (Lotte Duty Free official branch page). Use it when you leave on an international flight from Gimpo Airport. The Lotte pickup counter is up the escalator opposite Gate 34 of the international terminal (07:00~20:00, changes with the day's flight times).

12. Lotte Duty Free Gimhae Airport Store (롯데면세점 김해공항점)

It is on floor 2 of the Gimhae International Airport International Terminal (김해국제공항 국제청사) (108 Gonghang-jinip-ro, Gangseo-gu, Busan (부산 강서구 공항진입로 108)). The hours are 06:30~21:30. The branch phone is 051-979-1900 (Lotte Duty Free official branch page). By Kakao Map, the nearest station is Airport Station (공항역) on the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail (부산김해경전철).

13. Lotte Duty Free Jeju Airport Store (롯데면세점 제주공항점)

It is in the floor 3 international departures hall (국제선 출국장) of Jeju International Airport (제주국제공항) (2 Gonghang-ro, Jeju-si (제주시 공항로 2)). The hours are 06:00~22:00. The branch phone is 064-798-4000 (Lotte Duty Free official branch page). Use it only when you leave on an international flight from Jeju Airport.

🗺 Routes — Two Options

A. A Myeongdong Day (the day before leaving Seoul)

  1. Morning Lotte Duty Free Myeongdong Main Store (롯데면세점 명동본점) (09:30~). Bring your passport and E-ticket
  2. After lunch Shinsegae Duty Free Myeongdong Store (신세계면세점 명동점) (10:30~). It is open until 20:30 Fri~Sun
  3. Afternoon Ordinary shopping at Olive Young (올리브영) and department stores. Look for the Tax Free sign and show your passport when you pay to get the Immediate Refund (under 1,000,000 won per transaction)
  4. Departure day At the airport pickup counter, show your exchange voucher, passport and boarding pass and collect your duty free goods

B. Gangnam·Jamsil Half Day

  1. Morning Hyundai Duty Free Trade Center Store (현대면세점 무역센터점) (10:30~, Exit 5 of Samseong Station (삼성역))
  2. Afternoon Lotte Duty Free World Tower Store (롯데면세점 월드타워점) (~19:30). Line 2 goes straight to Jamsil Station (잠실역)

Check the subway ride and travel time with Kakao Map directions on the day.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to a downtown duty free shop without your passport — the store checks your own passport and a flight ticket (E-ticket) that shows your departure details (Shilla Duty Free shopping guide). Save the E-ticket on your phone.
  2. Arriving at the pickup counter right before departure — the pickup counter is crowded on departure day. Lotte Duty Free tells you to come 1 hour before departure. If your English name and passport number differ from the order information, you cannot receive the goods (Lotte Duty Free pickup counter guide).
  3. Forgetting the Immediate Refund limit — the in-store Immediate Refund applies to one transaction under 1,000,000 won including tax, with a total of 5,000,000 won or less since entry (Special Provisions Article 6 ②). Above that, take the slip and handle it as an After-Purchase Refund at a downtown booth or the airport. If one receipt is less than 15,000 won, no Tax Refund applies (Korea Tourism Organization).

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. Should I buy at a downtown or an airport duty free shop? — A downtown duty free shop is good when you want a bigger store and have time to spare. But you collect the goods at the pickup counter on your departure day. At an airport duty free shop, you carry the goods on board right after immigration.
  2. Can a foreigner who lives in Korea buy at a duty free shop? — Anyone leaving for overseas, Korean or foreign, can buy at a duty free shop (Lotte Duty Free). The Tax Refund is for foreigners staying in Korea less than 6 months (Korea Tourism Organization).
  3. Do goods bought at a duty free shop also get a Tax Refund? — No. Duty free prices already have the tax taken off. A Tax Refund applies only to goods bought at ordinary stores with a Tax Free sign. The conditions for each refund route are in #1231.
