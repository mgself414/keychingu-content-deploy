🎯 Why This Suncheon Bay (순천만) Guide

Suncheon Bay (순천만) = Korea's #1 National Garden (국가정원) (designated 2015, 920,000m²) · Suncheon Bay Wetland (순천만 습지) · one of the world's top 5 coastal wetlands · UNESCO Tentative List · Nagan Eupseong (낙안읍성) (Joseon Dynasty, 1397~). Common pain points for foreigners = (a) "Suncheon Bay (순천만) = just a regular garden" misconception (b) Korea's #1 National Garden (국가정원), after Taehwagang (태화강) (#1017) (c) Wetlands (습지) = one of the world's top 5 coastal wetlands and a reed field matrix (d) *Nagan Eupseong (낙안읍성) = Korea's #1 preserved eupseong (읍성, walled town) from the Joseon Dynasty (조선) (1397~)* (e) Autumn reed field matrix #1.

This guide covers 5 pillars, 12 signatures + National Garden (국가정원) + Wetlands (습지) + Nagan Eupseong (낙안읍성).

🏛 5 Pillars Deep Dive

① Suncheon Bay National Garden (순천만 국가정원) (Korea's #1, 2015)

  • Signature: 2015 Korea's #1 National Garden (국가정원), 920,000m², 12 themed gardens · International gardens (Korean, Japanese, Chinese, Turkish, Italian, etc.)
  • Foreigner-friendly rating: ★★★★★ (English, MZ + family, Instagram)
  • Hours: 09:00 AM - 6:00 PM · ₩12,000~16,000 (~$9-12 / ~65-85 RMB / ~1,300-1,700 JPY)

② Suncheon Bay Wetland (순천만 습지) (World's Top 5 Coastal)

  • Signature: World's Top 5 Coastal Wetlands · Reed fields, Black-faced Spoonbill enthusiast matrix #1
  • Foreigner-friendly rating: ★★★★★ (English, MZ + enthusiasts)
  • Recommended Season: Oct-Nov reeds + Instagram matrix #1

③ Nagan Eupseong (낙안읍성) (Joseon Dynasty 1397~, Korea's #1 Preserved)

  • Signature: *Founded 1397 by King Taejo, Korea's #1 preserved Joseon eupseong (읍성, walled town) · 100+ chogajip (초가집, traditional thatched-roof houses) matrix*
  • Foreigner-friendly rating: ★★★★ (English, MZ + family)
  • Price: ₩4,000 (~$3 / ~20 RMB / ~430 JPY) per person

④ Suncheon (순천) Food (Hanjeongsik, Jjanttung-eotang)

  • Signature: Jeollado (전라도) Hanjeongsik (한정식, Korean full-course meal) · *Suncheon (순천) Jjanttung-eotang (짱뚱어탕, mudskipper soup) signature matrix*
  • Foreigner-friendly rating: ★★★★ (Not spicy, good for beginners)

⑤ Overnight Stay (Pension/Hotel)

  • Signature: Suncheon Bay (순천만) morning enthusiasts · Reed fields + morning bird matrix

2. "Wetlands (습지) = just grass": World's Top 5 Coastal Wetlands, Reed Field Matrix #1.

3. *"Nagan Eupseong (낙안읍성) = just a regular eupseong (읍성)": Joseon Dynasty 1397, Korea's #1 preserved · 100+ chogajip* (초가집).

5. *"Jjanttung-eotang (짱뚱어탕) = just a regular soup": Suncheon (순천) signature · Enthusiast matrix*.
