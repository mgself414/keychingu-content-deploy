⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreign Tourists

1. "K-beauty = Myeongdong (명동) Only": Beyond Myeongdong (명동), explore the full range including Gangnam (강남) (Innisfree (이니스프리) · Laneige (라네즈) Flagship Stores) · Hongdae (홍대) (MZ - Millennial and Gen Z) · Olive Young (올리브영) (nationwide stores) · Amorepacific Headquarters (아모레퍼시픽 본사) (Yongsan (용산)) · Duty-Free Shops (Gangnam (강남) · Myeongdong (명동) · The Hyundai (더현대)).

2. "Korean Jjimjilbang (찜질방) = Scary": Korean Jjimjilbang (찜질방 - bathhouse/spa) are foreigner-friendly, open 24 hours, cost ₩6,000-₩25,000 (~$4.5-$18 / ~30-135 RMB / ~600-2,500 JPY), and are Korea's No. 1 traditional relaxation spot. Dragon Hill Spa (드래곤힐스파) (Yongsan (용산)) · Spa Land (스파랜드) (Jamsil (잠실)) · Hansol Jjimjilbang (한솔찜질방) (Hongdae (홍대)) are foreigner-guided friendly.

3. "Personal Color = Traditional Korean": Personal Color is Korea's No. 1 K-beauty trend of the 2010s and a top inbound draw for foreign enthusiasts. Advance reservations are essential for the premier consulting shops in Cheongdam (청담) and Gangnam (강남).
