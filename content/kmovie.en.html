⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by International Visitors

1. "K-movie = Parasite only": K-movie includes many other masterpieces like Oldboy, Train to Busan, A Taxi Driver, and Ode to My Father.

2. "BIFF = October only": Besides BIFF (October), JIFF (Jeonju (전주), May) and BFFC (Bucheon (부천), July) are also popular among international film enthusiasts.

3. "Parasite filming locations = inaccessible": Parasite filming locations include Ahyeon (아현) & Seodaemun (서대문) (poor neighborhoods) + Seongbuk (성북) (Mr. Park's house exterior), popular for international vlogs.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Top K-movies for International Visitors: Parasite, Oldboy, Train to Busan, A Taxi Driver.

2. Film Festivals for International Visitors: BIFF (Busan (부산), October), JIFF (Jeonju (전주), May), BFFC (Bucheon (부천), July).

3. Directors Popular with International Fans: Bong Joon-ho, Park Chan-wook, Lee Chang-dong.
