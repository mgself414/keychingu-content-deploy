Korea's Emergency Line 1330, No-Panic Guide — The First Number to Dial When Things Go Wrong

① The Number Worth Memorizing Before the Emergency Numbers — "When in Doubt, 1330"

Before you memorize the police (112) or fire department (119), save this number first: 1330. The real problem usually isn't danger — it's the language barrier. Most panic moments come from not knowing how to report an injury or a lost item in Korean. 1330 is the window that removes that barrier. If you remember nothing else from this piece but "whatever the situation, start with 1330," it's done its job.

② What Is 1330 — Tourist Hotline Profile

Item Detail
Operated by Korea Tourism Organization (KTO) travel info & interpretation hotline
Number 1330, no area code needed
Hours 24 hours, every day
Languages Korean + 8 foreign languages (English, Japanese, Chinese, Russian, Vietnamese, Thai, Malay)
Cost Free (only your regular call charges apply)
Channels Phone + real-time text chat

③ 3 Numbers — Which One, and When

Number Handles When
1330 Tourist info, interpretation, and any kind of trouble in general If you don't know what to dial, start here no matter what
112 Police Reporting crime, loss, or accidents — can also be connected via 1330
119 Fire / paramedics Fire or medical emergencies — can also be connected via 1330
  • You can dial 112 or 119 directly, but if Korean isn't understood, the call can fall apart quickly. Go through 1330 first and you get transferred with interpretation already attached.

④ The Core Feature — the 112/119 Three-Way Interpretation Bridge

1330's real value isn't information — it's this feature. Stay on the line and just follow these steps in order.

1. Dial [ 1 ] [ 3 ] [ 3 ] [ 0 ] → you'll hear 「Korea Travel Hotline-imnida, mueoseul dowa-deurilkkayo? (Korea Travel Hotline입니다, 무엇을 도와드릴까요?) — "This is Korea Travel Hotline, how can I help you?"」 (they respond in English)

2. Explain the situation in English ("My wallet was stolen near the subway station.")

3. The operator judges the situation and connects 112 or 119 in a three-way call — don't hang up, just stay on the line

4. While the operator talks to police or paramedics in Korean, they'll keep interpreting for you in English at the same time

5. The operator stays on the line and keeps interpreting until the report/dispatch is fully filed

In other words, all you have to do is speak English — the operator handles everything from placing the emergency call to interpreting it, on your behalf.

⑥ Making the Call — Exactly as It Appears

  • Dial [ 1 ] [ 3 ] [ 3 ] [ 0 ] (no area code) then the call button
  • If you're on a Korean SIM or roaming plan, that's it. If it won't connect while on international roaming, add the country code and try again as +82-2-1330
  • If it still won't connect, don't waste more time trying — the fastest option is to ask the hotel front desk to make the call for you (see ⑨)

⑦ If a Phone Call Feels Like Too Much — Text Chat

If speaking feels intimidating, you can use 1330's real-time text chat instead of calling. You type out the situation in English, so there's no worry about pronunciation or background noise. That said, for urgent situations that need an immediate 112/119 connection like the three-way interpretation bridge, a phone call is faster — save chat for non-urgent questions or interpretation requests.

⑨ Panic Scenarios — What Actually Happens and How to Handle It

1. You're at the ER and nothing gets through — hand your phone to the front desk or medical staff with 1330 dialed and on speakerphone. The operator interprets everything, from explaining symptoms to giving instructions.

2. The police showed up but there's no communication — same move: call 1330 and put it on speakerphone so the officer can listen along. The operator interprets everything from filing the report to taking your statement.

The key point in both cases: the process doesn't stall just because you don't speak Korean.

⑩ Plan B — If Even 1330 Doesn't Work Out

  • Hotel front desk: staff will often make the call for you or explain the situation directly. If your accommodation is nearby, this is the fastest option.
  • Papago translation screen: type the situation in English and show the translated screen as-is — app setup is covered in #1230 Essential apps guide.
