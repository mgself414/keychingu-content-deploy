Hongseong Market Guide — Hongseong Traditional Market's 1st-and-6th Market Days, Gwangcheon Traditional Market and Cave-Aged Salted Shrimp, Namdang Port, Hongjuupseong Fortress

Subtitle: Hongseong Traditional Market (홍성전통시장), opened in 1943, market days on the 1st and 6th · Gwangcheon Traditional Market (광천전통시장), market days on the 4th and 9th, cards accepted · Gwangcheon Cave Salted Shrimp Complex (광천토굴새우젓단지), open year-round · Namdang Port (남당항), always open · Hongjuupseong Fortress (홍주읍성) and Hongjuseong History Museum (홍주성역사관), museum free, closed Mondays — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

For markets in Hongseong, there are two. At Hongseong Traditional Market in Hongseong-eup (홍성읍), a five-day market is held on the 1st and 6th, and at Gwangcheon Traditional Market in Gwangcheon-eup (광천읍), on the 4th and 9th. Gwangcheon Market is in the Gwangcheon Salted Shrimp Complex, known for salted shrimp, and along Gwangcheon-ro is the Gwangcheon Cave Salted Shrimp Complex, where salted shrimp is aged in caves. There is Namdang Port on Cheonsu Bay, and Hongjuupseong Fortress in Hongseong-eup.

This guide covers only five places and two birthplaces of independence activists from Hongseong, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI).

🛒 Hongseong Traditional Market — 1st-and-6th Market Days

Hongseong Traditional Market (13-6 Uisa-ro 43beon-gil, Hongseong-eup, Hongseong-gun (홍성군 홍성읍 의사로43번길 13-6), ☎ Hongseong Periodic Market Merchants' Association 041-632-8833) is a market that opened in 1943.

  • Market days: The 1st, 6th, 11th, 16th, 21st, and 26th. On market days it is crowded with merchants and customers from Hongseong and the surrounding area.
  • Opening hours and closed days: They differ by shop.
  • What is sold: Vegetables, fish, meat, clothing, household goods, and snack-bar food. As befits the Cheonsu Bay area, it also sells blue crab, bird clams, webfoot octopus, large shrimp, and gizzard shad.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available.
  • Nearby: It is close to Hongju Martyrs' Shrine (홍주순교성지), Course 5 of the Naepo Catholic Pilgrimage Route.

🦐 Gwangcheon Traditional Market and the Gwangcheon Cave Salted Shrimp Complex

Gwangcheon Traditional Market (8-16 Gwangcheon-ro 285beon-gil, Gwangcheon-eup, Hongseong-gun (홍성군 광천읍 광천로285번길 8-16), ☎ 041-641-2164) is a market in the Gwangcheon Salted Shrimp Complex. It sells salted seafood and seaweed made directly at the source.

  • Market days: Along with the permanent market, a five-day market is held on the 4th, 9th, 14th, 19th, 24th, and 29th.
  • Opening hours and closed days: They differ by shop.
  • What is sold: Cave-aged salted shrimp, Gwangcheon traditional seaweed, farm products, seafood, livestock products, and clothing.
  • Cards: Accepted. Parking is available.

Gwangcheon Cave Salted Shrimp Complex (91 Gwangcheon-ro, Gwangcheon-eup, Hongseong-gun (홍성군 광천읍 광천로 91), ☎ Gwangcheon Cave Salted Shrimp Promotion Hall 041-642-2913) is a place where salted shrimp shops line Gwangcheon-ro.

  • Caves: Salted shrimp began to be aged in mine tunnels dug during the Japanese colonial period, and there are now about 40 caves, large and small. Inside the caves it is 14–15 degrees all year.
  • Experience: You look around a cave yourself, then buy salted shrimp.
  • Opening hours: They differ by store. Open year-round.
  • Cards: Registered as "none." The public parking lot next to the promotion hall is free.
  • Festival: The Gwangcheon Cave Salted Shrimp and Gwangcheon Seaweed Festival is held every October (month 10).

🦪 Namdang Port — Bird Clams and Large Shrimp of Cheonsu Bay

Namdang Port (Namdang-ri, Seobu-myeon, Hongseong-gun (홍성군 서부면 남당리), ☎ Hongseong County Office Sports and Tourism Division 041-630-1362) is a port on Cheonsu Bay, with many raw fish restaurants around it.

  • Opening hours: Always open, open year-round. Parking is available.
  • Seafood: Large shrimp, rockfish, bird clams, blue crab, shrimp, and flatfish. Bird clams, counted among Cheonsu Bay's delicacies, are especially well known.
  • Festivals: A large shrimp festival has been held since 1996, and a bird clam festival is held in winter.
  • Scenery: The sunset is beautiful, and Anmyeondo Island can be seen in the distance.

🏯 Hongjuupseong Fortress and Hongjuseong History Museum

Hongseong Hongjuupseong Fortress (20-1 Amun-gil, Hongseong-eup, Hongseong-gun (홍성군 홍성읍 아문길 20-1), ☎ Hongseong County Cultural Heritage Division 041-630-1691) is a town fortress of which about 800m remains of a wall about 1,772m long. Construction began in the reign of Sejong of Joseon, and it was rebuilt in 1451. It has the east gate, Joyangmun Gate (restored in 1975).

  • Opening hours: Always open, open year-round.
  • Hongjuseong History Museum (20 Amun-gil, ☎ 041-630-9240): Inside the fortress, it exhibits the history of Hongseong and the Naepo region. March–October 09:00–18:00, November–February 09:00–17:00. Closed every Monday (the next first weekday if it is a public holiday), January 1, and the days of Seollal and Chuseok. Free.

🏠 Two Birthplaces of Independence Activists

  • Birthplace of Manhae Han Yong-un (만해 한용운선생 생가지) (83 Manhae-ro 318beon-gil, Gyeolseong-myeon, Hongseong-gun (홍성군 결성면 만해로318번길 83), ☎ 041-630-9231): The thatched birthplace of Han Yong-un, who was born here in 1879. During the March 1st Movement, Han Yong-un signed the Declaration of Independence as one of the 33 national representatives. 09:00–18:00, closed every Monday, January 1, and the days of Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Birthplace of General Kim Jwa-jin (김좌진 장군 생가지) (12 Baegya-ro 546beon-gil, Galsan-myeon, Hongseong-gun (홍성군 갈산면 백야로546번길 12), ☎ 041-634-6952): The house where the independence activist Kim Jwa-jin was born and grew up, a Chungnam Monument. A shrine, a memorial hall, and Baegya Park are together here. 09:00–17:00, closed every Monday (Tuesday if it is a public holiday), January 1, and the days of Seollal and Chuseok.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Mixing up the market days of the two markets — Hongseong Traditional Market's market days are the 1st and 6th, and Gwangcheon Traditional Market's are the 4th and 9th.
  2. Trying to use a card at a cave salted shrimp shop — In the tourism organization's information, the salted shrimp complex is registered as "no cards." Gwangcheon Traditional Market accepts cards.
  3. Touring the museum and birthplaces on a Monday — Hongjuseong History Museum, Han Yong-un's birthplace, and Kim Jwa-jin's birthplace are all closed every Monday. The fortress is always open.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where do I buy salted shrimp?

A. At the Gwangcheon Cave Salted Shrimp Complex and Gwangcheon Traditional Market in Gwangcheon-eup. You can buy after looking around a cave.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. According to the tourism organization, cards are accepted at Gwangcheon Traditional Market, while the Gwangcheon Cave Salted Shrimp Complex is "no cards." Card information is not registered for Hongseong Traditional Market and Namdang Port.
