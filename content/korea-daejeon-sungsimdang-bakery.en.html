⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Seongsimdang = just a bakery": 1956 ~ Korea #1 Bakery 70 years+·1st place Soboro .

2. "Fried Soboro = Regular Bread": 1980~40+·Korea #1 Mania Matrix Bread·Limit 2 pieces per person.

5. "Delivery = Difficulty for foreigners": Nationwide delivery matrix in Korea·Delivery after foreigners return home

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. No. 1 introduction for foreigners: Fried soboro + chive bread + salt bread + coffee.

2. Main branch line: Weekdays at 9:00 am, within 30 minutes, weekends, lunch, dinner = 1 hour+. Alternative = Bank branch, Lotte Department Store branch.

3. Return home gift: 6~12 boxes (KRW 15,000~25,000)·Checked baggage OK·Expiration date 3 days·Purchase right before returning home.

4. Online delivery: OK nationwide within Korea·Korean hotel/guesthouse address OK for foreigners (Notify the hotel in advance when checking in).

5. Allergy: Milk, eggs, flour, nuts, salted shrimp (part of fried beef) = prior notice + allergen labeling on English menu.

6. Recommended persona: Family/Senior = Chive bread + Salt bread / MZ/Spicy Mania = Tempura Soboro + Cafe / Instagram = Cafe dessert.

⚠ Medical/Safety/Dietary disclaimer

Seongsimdang Bread = Milk, egg, flour, and nut allergens. Package label + prior notice to store required. Pregnant women, diabetics, and high blood pressure patients = Be careful of frequency. Emergency ☎ 1339 (English).

Dietary Restrictions:

  • Halal/Kosher: Some breads (fried beef loaf/salt bread) = Non-halal containing milk and eggs / Chive bread = Some may be vegan
  • Vegetarian/Vegan: Some chive bread/salt bread = Vegan OK/Fried beef loaf = Vegan
  • Gluten free: All Seongsimdang bread = contains gluten (flour)
