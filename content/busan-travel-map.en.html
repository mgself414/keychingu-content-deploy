⚠ 3 Busan mistakes

1. "Busan = Haeundae Bay" — Gwangalli·Songjeong Miji: Haeundae Bay is recognized. In reality, Haeundae·Gwangalli·Songjeong + Igidae·Taejongdae·Ganjeolgot 5 Beach·Coastal cluster + Night view·Surfing·Trekking discrimination.

2. "Busan gourmet = raw fish only" — Pork soup and Milmyeon Miji: Recognition of only raw fish. In reality, it is pork soup, wheat noodles, Dongrae green onion pancake, sea eel, seed hotteok, a full experience of Busan’s signature gourmet food.

3. "Busan = 1 night only" — Outskirts (Ganjeolgot, Gijang, Haedong Yonggungsa Temple) Unknown: Only 1 night in the city center is recognized. In reality, 3 nights and 4 days recommended — downtown + beach + outskirts (Ganjeolgot, Haedong Yonggungsa Temple) · BIFF season (October) full experience.
