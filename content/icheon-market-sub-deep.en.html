Icheon Gwango Market and 12 pottery selections

  • Discrimination: Icheon authentic market + 5-day market on the 2nd and 7th of every month·Icheon rice + pottery alley·Established in the 1980s
  • Foreigner visit: visitkorea·Icheon Tourism

2. Icheon Ceramics (UNESCO Creative City)

  • Discrimination: Korea #1 Ceramic production area 240+ workshops·UNESCO Creative City Craft Sector 2010·Icheon Ceramic Village
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania #1·#600 Porcelain cross

3. Icheon World Ceramic Center

  • Distinction: Opened in 2001·Icheon Ceramic #1 Museum·Home of the World Ceramic Biennale·Experience + Exhibition
  • Operation: Tue-Sun 10:00-18:00, Closed on Mon

  • Discrimination: April-May every year, about 1 week, Icheon Ceramic Village, Korea’s largest ceramic festival, 50,000+ visitors

FAQ 6

Q1. Is Icheon day-trip possible? A. 1 hour in the metropolitan area (Gyeonggang Line)·Full day OK / Pottery village + rice + market priority.

Q2. Pottery making experience? A. Ceramic Village Sagimaggol·1 hour 30,000 won~/per person·Finished product delivered by courier 1 week later (Overseas shipping separately).

Q3. When is the ceramics festival? A. Approximately 1 week in April-May every year·World Ceramic Biennale every other year (scheduled for 2027).

Q4. Recommendation for Icheon rice? A. Icheon Rice Restaurant (1979)·Cheongmok (Korean cuisine)·King’s classic version·15,000-25,000 won/person.

Q5. Termeden vs. Miranda? A. Termeden = Sauna + Water Park (KRW 50,000 per day~)·Miranda = Hotel + Hot Spring (KRW 200,000 per night~).

Q6. Icheon + Yeoju + Gwangju ceramics full set? A. 3 cities = 3 major producing regions of Korean ceramics·Full set available for 2 days and 1 night (Icheon → Yeoju → Gwangju).
