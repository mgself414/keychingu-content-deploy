Icheon Guide — Gwango Traditional Market, Icheon Ceramic Art Village (Yespark), Doyecheon Rice Street, Seolbong Park and Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum

Subtitle: Gwango Traditional Market (관고전통시장), started in 1930, permanent and periodic market · Icheon Ceramic Art Village (이천도자예술마을, Yespark), always open · Doyecheon Rice Street (도예촌 쌀밥거리), cards accepted · Seolbong Park (설봉공원), always open · Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum (경기도자미술관), 10:00–18:00, closed Mondays — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Icheon is known for rice and ceramics. You can group it in this order: shop at Gwango Traditional Market in the city center, walk through the workshops of the Icheon Ceramic Art Village (Yespark) in Sindun-myeon (신둔면), and eat Icheon rice in a stone pot at the nearby Doyecheon Rice Street. In Gwango-dong (관고동) in the city are Seolbong Park and the Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum.

This guide covers only five places, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Sagimakgol Pottery Village is covered separately in #724 Icheon Sagimakgol Pottery Village.

🛒 Gwango Traditional Market — A Traditional Market in Downtown Icheon

Gwango Traditional Market (22 Jungnicheon-ro 31beon-gil, Gwango-dong, Icheon (이천시 중리천로31번길 22, 관고동), ☎ 031-633-4243) is an Icheon traditional market that has continued since 1930. It is in the center of downtown Icheon.

  • Market days: It is a permanent market, and the periodic market is registered in the tourism organization's information as "the 2nd and 7th of each month."
  • Opening hours and closed days: They differ by shop.
  • What is sold: Icheon specialties such as rice, along with farm products, seafood, livestock products, vegetables, fruit, and household goods.
  • Parking: Nearby public parking lots can be used.

🏺 Icheon Ceramic Art Village (Yespark) — A Village of Walking Through Workshops

Icheon Ceramic Art Village (109 Dojayesul-ro 5beon-gil, Sindun-myeon, Icheon (이천시 신둔면 도자예술로5번길 109), ☎ 0507-1461-1996) is a village where workshops gather, also called Yespark. Each workshop has different works, so you can look around and buy what you like, and rest at a cafe along the way.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Open year-round, but it differs by shop.
  • Experiences: Some workshops offer pottery-making experiences (ages 5 and up).
  • Parking: About 40 spaces.
  • Icheon Ceramics Festival: An event held in this village and the Sagimakgol area. The 40th edition in 2026 was held free of charge from April 24 to May 5, 10:00–18:00.

🍚 Doyecheon Rice Street — Icheon Rice in a Stone Pot

Doyecheon Rice Street (3194 Gyeongchung-daero, Sindun-myeon, Icheon (이천시 신둔면 경충대로 3194), ☎ 031-644-2000) is a street of hanok restaurants centered on the Icheon Doyecheon pottery village.

  • What you eat: A rice set meal cooked in a stone pot with Icheon rice, jujubes, black beans, chestnuts, ginkgo nuts, and ginseng.
  • Opening hours and closed days: They differ by business.
  • Cards: Accepted. Parking is available.

🌳 Seolbong Park and the Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum

Seolbong Park (128 Gyeongchung-daero 2709beon-gil, Gwango-dong, Icheon (이천시 경충대로2709번길 128, 관고동), ☎ Icheon City Hall Parks and Greenery Division 031-645-3849) is a park in downtown Icheon. It has the Icheon Municipal Museum, the Icheon Municipal Woljeon Art Museum, an international sculpture park, a music fountain, and a reproduction site of a traditional wood-fired kiln.

  • Opening hours: Always open, open year-round. Parking is available.
  • Information: The Icheon Comprehensive Tourist Information Center is near Parking Lot 1.

Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum (263 Gyeongchung-daero 2697beon-gil, Gwango-dong, Icheon (이천시 경충대로2697번길 263, 관고동), ☎ 031-645-0730) is a ceramics museum that opened in 2001 and holds curated and special exhibitions of contemporary ceramic art.

  • Opening hours: 10:00–18:00, last entry 17:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), and the days of New Year's Day, Seollal, and Chuseok.
  • Cards: Accepted. Parking is available.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Thinking the market and the ceramic village are in the same place — Gwango Traditional Market is in Gwango-dong downtown, while Yespark and Doyecheon Rice Street are in Sindun-myeon.
  2. Going to the Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum on a Monday — It is closed every Monday.
  3. Thinking all Yespark workshops open at the same time — The village is always open, but each workshop has different opening hours.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where do I eat Icheon rice?

A. At Doyecheon Rice Street in Sindun-myeon, you can taste a stone-pot rice set meal made with Icheon rice, jujubes, chestnuts, ginseng, and more.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. According to the tourism organization, cards are accepted at Doyecheon Rice Street and the Gyeonggi Ceramic Museum. Card information is not registered for Gwango Traditional Market and Yespark.
