Daegu Chimaek Guide — The Summer Daegu Chimaek Festival (Duryu Park) and 5 Year-Round Chicken Spots (Pyeonghwa Market Chicken Gizzard Alley's Samah Tongdak, Dongseong-ro's Wonju Tongdak) (October 2026 Official Values)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Chicken gizzards fried with no batter crunch between your teeth, and cold beer washes the grease away — Daegu's Pyeonghwa Market Chicken Gizzard Alley (평화시장 닭똥집골목) names the batter-free "nude gizzard" (누드똥집) as its original menu item (the alley's official site). This guide splits how to eat chicken and beer together in Daegu into two kinds. One is the Daegu Chimaek Festival (대구치맥페스티벌, "chimaek" = chicken + maekju, beer), held every summer at Duryu Park. The other is the chicken alley and old-school whole-chicken shops that stay open in months with no festival.

The 2026 festival is already over, and the 2027 schedule has not been announced yet. This edition is for both travelers who plan a trip around the festival and those who stop in Daegu at any time and just want a round of chimaek.

🎪 Daegu Chimaek Festival — Summer, Duryu Park

  • 2026 14th edition: July 1 (Wed)–5 (Sun), around Duryu Park (두류공원), Daegu, from 18:00. Hosted by the Korea Chimaek Industry Association (사단법인 한국치맥산업협회), organized by the Daegu Chimaek Festival organizing committee (대구치맥페스티벌 조직위원회), sponsored by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs, Daegu Metropolitan City, the Korea Tourism Organization and others (festival official). VisitKorea's festival data lists the same dates. Inquiries 053-248-9998 (VisitKorea)
  • 2027: Schedule not yet announced (as of 2026-10-21 on the festival's official site and VisitKorea). The festival's official site announced its selection as a 2026–2027 "Korea Local 100" (대한민국 로컬100) festival
  • Past dates differ every year: the 13th edition in 2025 ran 7/2–7/6, the 12th in 2024 ran 7/3–7/7, and the 11th in 2023 ran 8/30–9/3 (festival official history). The first edition was in 2013
  • 2026 zones: the "Daeprica Waterpia" (대프리카 워터피아) at 2·28 Liberty Plaza (2·28 자유광장) (concerts you watch while being sprayed with water, water EDM), the "Chimaek Singalong Club" (치맥 떼창 클럽) at the 2·28 Memorial Tower Parking Lot (2·28기념탑주차장), "K-Chimaek Street" (K-치맥 스트리트) along Duryu Park-ro (두류공원로), and "Chisang Nakwon EGG Island" (치상낙원 EGG섬) at the Kolon Open-Air Concert Hall (코오롱야외음악당). A fireworks show from E-World at 20:00 on 7/4 (Sat) was on the schedule (festival official detailed schedule)
  • Chicken booths: the 2026 participating vendors were Gatteuigin Fried (갓튀긴후라이드), Dakdonggari (닭동가리), Mangyehwa (만계화), Bamsaeuneun Dak (밤새우는닭), Smof Chicken (스모프치킨), Changjo Chicken (창조치킨), Jjimhaetdak Tonghaetdak (찜했닭통했닭) and Han & Dul Chicken (한앤둘치킨), with Kyochon Chicken (교촌치킨), Cass and others as sponsors (festival official). Menus and prices by vendor came out in a PDF attached to the 7/1 notice, which we could not open for this edition
  • Admission: We could not find admission information on the festival's official site. The main page has a link to a paid "Premium Advance Reservation Zone"

🍗 Pyeonghwa Market Chicken Gizzard Alley — Year-Round, 4 Places

Dakddongjip (닭똥집) is the chicken gizzard (the alley's official site). According to VisitKorea, in 1972 Samah Tongdak (삼아통닭) fried leftover gizzards and served them as makgeolli (막걸리, rice wine) snacks to day laborers at the labor market, which started the alley, and in the 2000s soy gizzards and garlic gizzards appeared. The alley's official site describes the menu as nude gizzard (the original, fried without batter), fried gizzard (똥집튀김), seasoned gizzard (invented in the 1980s), soy gizzard and garlic gizzard. Each shop makes its own sauce, so the flavor differs.

  • Samah Tongdak (삼아통닭) — 10 Ayang-ro 9-gil (아양로9길), Dong-gu (동구), Daegu (Sinam-dong (신암동)). Tel. 053-952-3650 (alley official). Described as the alley's first shop (VisitKorea)
  • Pyeonghwa Tongdak (평화통닭) — 6-6 Ayang-ro 9-gil (아양로9길), Dong-gu (동구), Daegu (Sinam-dong (신암동)). Tel. 053-958-0816 (alley official)
  • Daegu Tongdak (대구통닭) — 6-6 Ayang-ro 9-gil (아양로9길), Dong-gu (동구), Daegu (Sinam-dong (신암동)). Tel. 053-941-9871 (alley official)
  • Jinmi Tongdak (진미통닭) — 10 Ayang-ro 9-gil (아양로9길), Dong-gu (동구), Daegu (Sinam-dong (신암동)). Tel. 053-954-6580 (alley official)
  • We could not confirm opening hours for any of the four. The alley map marks some shops "currently closed" (현재 가게는 휴업 중), so call before you go (alley official)
  • The alley's merchants' association announced that it held its own "Pyeonghwa Market Chimaek Festival" (평화시장 치맥페스티벌) in 2026 as well (posted 2026-06-12, alley official). We could not confirm the dates in the notice

🐔 Dongseong-ro Old-School Whole Chicken — 1 Place

  • Wonju Tongdak (원주통닭) — 42-6 Dongseong-ro 6-gil (동성로6길), Jung-gu (중구), Daegu. A chicken restaurant that opened in Dongseong-ro in 1978 and is still in business. It sells fried chicken (튀김통닭) with a crispy exterior, sweet-and-salty seasoned chicken (양념통닭) and spicy vegetable braised chicken (야채찜닭), all made with domestic chicken (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours
  • For other food in downtown Daegu, see Daegu Seomun Night Market

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Price range: We could not find an officially published price for any of the 5 places. Prices are not published — check by phone or on the shop's menu board. Festival booth prices are in the PDF attached to the festival's official notice
  • Offal: Dakttongjip (닭똥집) is the chicken gizzard (alley official). If you do not eat organ cuts like gizzard, choose whole fried chicken
  • Allergies and gluten: The batter on fried gizzards and whole chicken usually contains wheat flour. The nude gizzard is fried without batter (alley official) — ask the shop whether it shares oil. Seasoned and soy sauces may contain soy and wheat
  • Alcohol: Beer is alcohol. If you do not drink, order a drink separately
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (이태원) (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil (우사단로10길), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00–21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF, 한국이슬람교중앙회). Certification can change, so confirm before you visit. We could not confirm halal labels at the shops in this guide or at the festival booths
  • For a show-it card, see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Chimaek Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Ordering, Getting There

  • Solo: Whole fried chicken is often sold by the whole bird, which may be a lot for one person — ask before ordering whether a half is possible. For a map of solo-friendly spots, see Seoul Solo Diner Map — 12 Signature Spots for Foreigners' Solo Dining, Bars and Cafes
  • Getting to the festival: Get off at Duryu Station (두류역) on subway Line 2. The festival's official directions are about 700 m (about a 15-minute walk) from Exit 15 of Duryu Station to the Duryu Park baseball field; from Dongdaegu Station or Daegu Station, transfer from Line 1 to Line 2 at Banwoldang Station (반월당역) and use Exit 14 or 19 of Duryu Station. The festival is extremely crowded on the day and directs cars to outside parking lots, so public transportation is recommended (festival official)
  • Getting to Pyeonghwa Market: VisitKorea describes it as easy to reach because Dongdaegu Station is close. For intercity travel, see Taking the KTX for the First Time: The No-Panic Manual — Yes, There's No Ticket Gate and Korean Express & Intercity Buses: The No-Panic Manual — Knowing They Use Different Apps Is Half the Battle
  • Payment: For cards and mobile payment, see Foreigner Korea Payment System Deep Dive
  • Ordering: For how ordering works at chicken shops, see Korean Chicken Order & Delivery Workflow Guide. When you cannot communicate, see Manners When Words Fail — Ordering Etiquette in the Age of Translation Apps and Kiosks

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "The Chimaek Festival is held on the same days every July" — The 11th edition in 2023 ran 8/30–9/3 (festival official history). The 2027 schedule has not been announced, so wait for the notice on the festival's official site

2. "Once the festival ends, there is no chimaek in Daegu" — Pyeonghwa Market Chicken Gizzard Alley and Dongseong-ro's Wonju Tongdak (원주통닭) are listed as shops that operate regardless of the festival (VisitKorea, alley official)

3. "Dakddongjip is chicken poop" — It is the chicken gizzard (the muscular stomach) (alley official)

4. "The chimaek festival exists only in Daegu" — VisitKorea's festival data also lists the 2026 Pohang Chimaek Festival (8/21–8/22). In Seoul, people do chimaek in Hangang parks — Hangang Chimaek Picnic
