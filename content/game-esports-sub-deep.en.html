Why a Gaming & Esports Sub Now — LCK World #1 + Krafton, NCSOFT Global

#562 gaming, esports, and LCK have already been covered. This #613 sub-deep is a gaming/esports-only matrix — LoL Park, T1, Gen.G, Krafton, NCSOFT, Nexon, G-STAR, and foreigner mania.

Korea is the birthplace of the League of Legends pro scene (LCK) and the world's strongest league. T1, led by Faker, has won the World Championship 4 times (verification recommended), and LCK teams post dominant results at international tournaments every year. Demand among foreign fans is growing fast — not for simple game-watching, but as a gaming-culture pilgrimage.

12 Picks Across 4 Axes — Deep Dive

Axis 1: LoL Park & LCK (3 picks)

1. LoL Park (Jongno-gu, Seoul)

Location: 26 Ujeongguk-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul (near Gwanghwamun, formerly a KT building). About a 5-minute walk from Exit 2, Gwanghwamun Station, Line 5.

Overview: The world's only esports arena dedicated to League of Legends, run in official partnership with Riot Games Korea. LCK (League of Legends Champions Korea) regular-season home matches are held here.

Foreigner fit: English commentary (an English caster is on hand during the season) and multilingual signage are fully in place. If you're an LoL fan, immersion on-site is high regardless of the language barrier. Real-time match data is shown on screens from your seat, so even first-time viewers can follow along.

Ticket/entry: LCK regular-season tickets are bought on Naver Ticket (네이버 티켓) or Melon Ticket. Foreigners can pay directly with an overseas credit card too (verification recommended). Price ₩20,000~₩55,000 (by seat grade, 2026). The lobby and exhibit space are free to access even on non-match days (checking hours is recommended).

Timing: LCK's Spring Split (Jan-Apr), Summer Split (May-Aug), Fall Playoffs (Sep-Oct). Match schedules can be checked in English on lolesports.com.

Caution: Popular matches (T1 vs Gen.G, etc.) sell out within 30 minutes of tickets opening. Booking at least a week ahead is recommended.

2. T1 (SK Telecom T1)

Overview: A top-tier world esports team, centered on Faker (Lee Sang-hyeok), with multiple LoL World Championship titles. As of 2024, it had the #1 global fandom size (verification recommended).

Fan touchpoints: Jerseys and merch can be bought directly from T1's official online store (t1.gg/shop). Pop-up stores and offline events run irregularly in Gangnam, Seoul (check the schedule on social media/the official site). A T1 merch booth operates on-site when watching a match at LoL Park.

Foreigner fit: A complete official English site, with international shipping support. How much English on-site staff speak varies by match, so keeping a translation app handy is recommended.

3. Gen.G, KT Rolster, DRX, DK

Overview: Major LCK teams. Gen.G (젠지), which received global investment, runs an active English fan channel. KT Rolster is a long-running telecom-backed team. DRX and DK (Dplus KIA) have been strong teams through the 2020s.

Viewing: All LCK regular-season matches run at LoL Park, so you watch at the same venue regardless of team. Cheering merch can be bought at each team's official store or on-site booths.

Axis 2: Game Company Headquarters (3 picks)

4. Krafton — the Developer of PUBG

Location: 410 Ttukseom-ro, Seongdong-gu, Seoul (near Seongsu Station). About a 10-minute walk from Seongsu Station, Line 2.

Overview: The developer of PUBG (PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds), ranking among the top in the global PC/mobile game market. The HQ's exterior is a modern building; a brief brand experience is possible in the lobby (no public interior tours).

Foreigner fit: General visitors cannot access the interior. Visits are limited to the exterior and photo purposes. For PUBG fans, combining a photo in front of the HQ with a Seongsu cafe tour is recommended.

Caution: Interior tours without a prior appointment are not possible. Visiting expecting a "game company HQ tour" can be disappointing, so plan it as an exterior-only visit.

5. NCSOFT — Lineage, Blade & Soul, Aion

Location: 225 Pangyoyeok-ro, Bundang-gu, Seongnam-si, Gyeonggi-do (Pangyo Techno Valley). About a 10-minute walk from Pangyo Station, Shinbundang Line.

Overview: The company that pioneered Korean MMORPGs with the Lineage (리니지) series. Holds a large campus inside Pangyo Techno Valley. Some exhibits/experience programs run irregularly at a space run by its game-culture foundation (checking the official homepage is recommended).

Foreigner fit: General visitors have restricted access to the campus interior. Since Pangyo Techno Valley itself is an IT-company-dense area, combining it with cafes and restaurants around Pangyo Station into a half-day course is efficient.

6. Nexon — MapleStory, Dungeon & Fighter, KartRider

Location: 220 Pangyoyeok-ro, Bundang-gu, Seongnam-si, Gyeonggi-do (Pangyo Techno Valley, near NCSOFT).

Overview: A company with a global gaming fandom built on MapleStory. Especially well known in Japan and North America. The Pangyo HQ exterior can be visited; the interior is not publicly open.

Foreigner fit: Global MapleStory fans can visit for a "pilgrimage" photo. Since there's no substantive experience content, it's better combined with NCSOFT and startup campuses into a Pangyo IT-district tour.

Axis 3: G-STAR & Events (3 picks)

7. G-STAR Busan (November)

Location: 55 APEC-ro, Haeundae-gu, Busan, the BEXCO exhibition hall.

Overview: One of Asia's largest game shows, held every November at BEXCO, Busan. Major domestic game companies (Nexon, NC, Krafton, Netmarble, etc.) and global partners take part, running new-title demos, esports events, and an indie-game showcase.

Entry: General admission ₩15,000~₩25,000 (verification recommended). Discounted with online advance purchase. Runs an English information desk for global visitors.

Foreigner fit: Many booths offer multilingual guidance (English, Chinese, Japanese). New-title demos can be experienced regardless of language. Checking the schedule ahead for cosplay events and fan meetings is recommended.

Timing: Mid-November each year (check the official homepage for the exact date that year). Efficiency is maximized when combined with a Busan trip (#244 cross) of 1 night 2 days or more.

Transport: Get off directly at BEXCO Station, Busan Subway Line 2. From Seoul, about 2 hours 20 minutes by KTX (to Busan Station) → transfer to the subway.

8. NEXTRISE

Overview: A startup/IT conference held every year at COEX, Seoul. Many gaming and tech companies also take part. Schedule and format vary significantly by year, so checking the official homepage (nextrise.co.kr) is recommended.

Foreigner fit: The share of English-language sessions varies by year. It suits experiencing Korea's IT ecosystem more than networking specifically within the gaming industry.

9. WCG & Global Esports Events

Overview: WCG (World Cyber Games) is a globally run esports competition that originated in Korea and has a history of being revived and relaunched. Whether it's held, where, and when varies a great deal by year, so checking the latest information before visiting is essential.

Foreigner fit: Global esports events tend to run largely in English and draw many foreign fans. Once a run is confirmed, booking tickets and lodging early is recommended.

Axis 4: Foreigner Mania (3 picks)

10. Watching LCK (the #1 Recommendation for Foreigners)

LCK is the #1 esports experience for foreign gaming fans visiting Korea. Seeing the "strongest league" in the LoL world live, in person, is irreplaceable.

Practical checklist:

  • Tickets: Naver Ticket, Melon Ticket (overseas card payment accepted, verification recommended)
  • Entry: concert-hall-level security screening. Bringing in food is restricted
  • Language: Korean commentary is the default during matches. Some English subtitle screens are provided. Check per match whether an English caster is present
  • Merch: on-site booths mostly accept credit cards
  • Getting around: Gwanghwamun's LoL Park → nearby restaurants (around Jongno, Gwanghwamun, Cheonggyecheon)

11. G-STAR Busan Mania Course (#244, #610 cross)

1-night-2-day plan: Seoul → KTX → Busan Station → stay at Haeundae/Gwangalli → a full day at G-STAR BEXCO → join gaming events → return.

Combined with the Busan travel cluster (#244), you can take in tourism and the game show together, which is especially efficient for long-haul foreign travelers.

12. Game Merch Shops, Arcades & Experience Spaces

Gaming-related experience spaces in Seoul:

  • Around Hongdae: A dense cluster of arcade game rooms, board-game cafes, and VR experience centers. Within walking distance of Hongik Univ. Station.
  • Gangnam COEX Mall: Game pop-up stores and merch shops open irregularly. A permanent arcade space (verification recommended).
  • Yeongdeungpo Times Square: Runs a large arcade game zone (verification recommended).

3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

Mistake 1: Trying to Buy an LCK Ticket On-Site on Match Day

Popular LCK matches (days T1 or Gen.G play) often sell out the moment they open. On-site sales are extremely limited or nonexistent. Always book in advance on Naver Ticket or Melon Ticket.

Mistake 2: Expecting an Interior Tour of a Game Company HQ

Krafton, NCSOFT, and Nexon all bar general visitors from the interior. Visiting expecting an "HQ tour" leaves you taking only exterior photos before heading back. Always check the official homepage beforehand for any public experience programs.

Mistake 3: Traveling to Busan Without Checking G-STAR's Dates or Tickets

G-STAR runs every mid-November, but the exact date changes year to year. On-site ticket purchase can mean long lines or a sellout, so buying online in advance is recommended. During G-STAR, lodging around Haeundae and BEXCO in Busan fills up fast.

FAQ 6

Q1. First time doing gaming/esports tourism in Korea — where do I start?

Watch an LCK regular-season match at LoL Park. Check the English schedule on lolesports.com beforehand and book through Naver Ticket. Even a one-day plan gives you plenty of immersion.

Q2. What are entry/ticket prices?

LCK match tickets ₩20,000~₩55,000 (by seat grade, 2026). G-STAR Busan entry ₩15,000~₩25,000 (verification recommended). Visiting a game company HQ exterior is free.

Q3. Can I enjoy it in English?

LCK has a full official English channel (lolesports.com) and multilingual on-site signage. G-STAR also has many English-language booths. Visiting a game company HQ has almost no English signage, so a translation app is essential.

Q4. Where can I see more series content?

See the full set: #562 (gaming/esports parent), #387 (K-pop agencies), #572 (K-content OTT), #443 (PC-bang/VR), #613 (this piece).

Q5. When is G-STAR, and how do I get there?

Every mid-November at BEXCO, Busan. About 2 hours 20 minutes from Seoul by KTX. Buying entry tickets in advance (official homepage) and booking Busan lodging early are essential.

Q6. Which company has the highest name recognition with global fans?

Krafton (PUBG), Nexon (MapleStory), and NCSOFT (Lineage) all have global fan bases. PUBG is especially well recognized in North America and Southeast Asia, and MapleStory in Japan and North America.

5 Tips for Foreign Fans

1. Book LoL Park's LCK matches at least a week ahead — on-site purchase on match day is essentially impossible

2. G-STAR Busan in November — booking entry tickets and lodging in advance is essential. Haeundae lodging prices more than double during the event

3. Pangyo game company HQs are exterior-visit only — don't expect an interior tour; combine with cafes and the startup street

4. Check the schedule in English on lolesports.com — match schedules, team matchups, and ticket links are all provided in English

5. Combine on-site and official online stores for merch — T1 and Gen.G's official stores offer international shipping

Practical Info — Transport, Booking, Language

Transport

  • LoL Park (Gwanghwamun): Exit 2, Gwanghwamun Station, Line 5 → 5-min walk
  • Pangyo game company HQs: Pangyo Station, Shinbundang Line → 10-15 min walk
  • BEXCO G-STAR: directly at BEXCO Station, Busan Subway Line 2
  • Seoul↔Busan: about 2 hours 20 minutes by KTX (departing from Suseo Station is also possible — formerly SRT, merged into KTX on 2026-09-01)

Booking

  • LCK tickets: naver.me/ticket or ticketmelon.com — checking whether overseas credit card payment works at the payment screen is recommended
  • G-STAR entry tickets: gstar.or.kr (official site, has an English page)
  • KTX: korail.com (English) or the Rail Plus app

Language

  • Match commentary: Korean by default. English commentary is provided on lolesports.com's official stream
  • On-site at G-STAR: English, Chinese, and Japanese information desks operate
  • Visiting game company HQs: almost no English support → a translation app (Papago, Google Translate) is essential

FAQ (Additional)

Q. Can I enjoy it with a companion who doesn't know LoL?

The on-site atmosphere and cheering energy can be enjoyed fully even by someone with no gaming experience. Real-time data is shown on the arena screens, and cheering along with the crowd around you is itself a cultural experience.

Q. Is there an official "tour" program at game company HQs?

There is no public tour (as of 2026). Some companies open on a limited basis during events or hiring seasons, so checking event notices on the official homepage is the only way to know.

Q. Planning a Busan trip during the same week as G-STAR?

During G-STAR (around mid-November), booking lodging around Haeundae/BEXCO six months ahead is safest. Combining the game show visit with Busan's coastal tourism gives good value against your flight/KTX cost.
