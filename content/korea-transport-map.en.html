🎯 Why the "Korea Transport Map" Meta

Transportation infrastructure for foreign visitors to Korea — Climate Card ₩65,000 (~$49)/30 days (#1 among the 5 reasons in #176 walkable_seoul). ⚠ 3 Common Transport Mistakes

1. "Climate Card = all transportation" — not realizing Incheon Airport & KTX are separate: Treating the Climate Card as all-purpose. In reality it covers Seoul city subway, buses, and Ttareungi only. The AREX Airport Railroad (Incheon Airport), KTX, and express buses are separate.

2. "T-money does everything" — not using the K-Pass: Only knowing T-money. In reality there is the K-Pass for foreigners (alien registration card or passport + 30% monthly cashback).

3. "Taxis are expensive" — not knowing Kakao T in English + Uber: Only hailing taxis on the street. In reality, Kakao T & Uber English apps + deluxe taxis & international taxis are foreigner-friendly.
