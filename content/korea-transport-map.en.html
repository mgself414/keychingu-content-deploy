🎯 How to Use This Guide

In front of the convenience store in the Incheon Airport (인천공항) arrivals hall, a T-money card and a WOWPASS sit side by side on the rack, and the ticket machine next to them shows "Climate Card." Do you need all three, or just one? This guide is a hub for choosing the right card, mode and app for your situation. The step-by-step procedure for using each mode (ticket machine screens, fare gates, app registration) has its own guide, so choose here and then move on to that guide.

The only figures this guide states directly are values seen on 2026-11-04 on the official pages of Seoul Metropolitan Government, T-money, WOWPASS, KORAIL, AREX and Seoul Bike. For frequently changing figures such as the base subway fare and KTX fares, follow the guide for that mode and the official app screen.

💳 Cards and Passes — What Tourists Can and Cannot Buy

Climate Card (기후동행카드) short-term pass for foreigners (Seoul Metropolitan Government, viewed 2026-11-04)

Type 1 day 2 days 3 days 5 days 7 days
Price ₩5,000 ₩8,000 ₩10,000 ₩15,000 ₩20,000
  • The physical card costs ₩3,000 separately. Buy it at the new-type transit card vending machines at Line 1–8 stations; since 2026-03-17 you can also pay with a foreign-issued credit or debit card. Mobile (Mobile T-money, Samsung Wallet, Android 12 or later) also works. You cannot buy it in advance from overseas.
  • The day you load it is the start date (no loading in advance). It runs from the first train at 04:00 on the start date to the last train on the expiry date.
  • Coverage from 2026-10-01: Seoul subway; Seoul-licensed city, village, night and circular buses; Incheon Airport T1 and T2 exit only (no boarding). Not valid on the Shinbundang Line (신분당선), GTX, stations outside Seoul, express buses, airport buses or buses in other regions. Seoul Bike, Hangang Bus and taxis are not included either.

T-money Travel Card (T-money 트래블카드) — ₩4,000. Buy it at convenience stores and at the bus ticket counters at Incheon Airport (T-money). It works on public transit and taxis nationwide. For top-ups see #238; for refund procedures see #1298 ⑥ Refunds.

WOWPASS — A prepaid card for foreigners. Load it with a card, e-wallet or cash; there is a ₩0 payment fee when you spend, and cash withdrawal costs ₩1,000 per withdrawal (maximum ₩100,000 per withdrawal) (WOWPASS). The page gives no top-up fee, so check it in the app. For transit it works through its T-money function.

KORAIL PASS Plus (코레일패스 플러스) (foreigners only, adult, KORAIL press release of 2025-12-29) — 2 days ₩131,000 · 3 days ₩186,000 · 4 days ₩234,000 · 5 days ₩275,000. Adding the transit card function costs ₩8,000 extra. Check refund and change conditions on the purchase screen.

Not for tourists — K-Pass (public transit refund) requires an Alien Registration Number to sign up, according to Seoul's English guidance, and the Climate Pass (기후동행패스) (successor to the 30-day pass) is a product for Seoul residents. Short-term visitors can effectively not use either.

🚇 Downtown — Subway and Bus: "What Your Hands Do" Matters More Than "Which Line"

  • The base subway fare changes often, so follow the ticket machine or transit card screen. For the single-journey ticket deposit and refund procedure, the 6 ticket machine steps, the fare gates and transfer signs, see #1281; for lines, see #237.
  • For where to get off and explore on each line, there is a series from Line 2 through Line 9 — #1241 Line 2 · #1247 Line 9 · transfer hub #1253 Hongik Univ. Station.
  • On city buses you tap twice, once when boarding and once when getting off, and you exit by the rear door — #1271. For onboard manners see #1379.

✈ Airport — Express or All-Stop

  • AREX airport express (공항철도 직통열차): Seoul Station↔Incheon Airport T1 43 min, T2 51 min, T1↔T2 8 min (AREX official). Buy at the underground ticket machines at Seoul Station, T1 and T2, or online and in the app; foreign-issued credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Amex, UnionPay, etc.) are accepted. Express fares can change, so follow the value on the booking screen — for a station-by-station exit guide see #1249.
  • AREX all-stop train: A distance-based fare: ₩1,550 base for the first 10 km, then ₩100 added per 5 km from 10 to 50 km (AREX fare guide). The Climate Card short-term pass works at Incheon Airport T1 and T2 stations only when exiting.
  • For airport bus comparisons see #87; for limousine buses, late-night airport buses and transfers see #1015 · #1395. Gimpo Airport (김포공항) Station is inside Seoul, so it falls within the short-term pass coverage.

🚄 Nationwide — KTX and Buses

  • The KTX has no fare gates. For the sequence from arriving at the station to your seat see #1287; for the 3 tickets for Seoul→Gyeongju→Busan see #1374. Fares and travel times follow the KORAIL app and website screens.
  • Express and intercity buses use a different app at each terminal — #1297.
  • The customer service number listed on KORAIL's English website is 1599-7777 (08:00–20:00).

🌙 Night, Taxis and Bikes

  • Owl Bus (올빼미버스) (N Bus): 14 routes, 23:00–06:00, ₩2,500 (Seoul Metropolitan Government). The last bus on each route is around 03:10–04:10, so there may be none at 5 a.m. (Seoul Metropolitan Government route table) — #1395.
  • Taxi: Seoul standard taxi base fare ₩4,800, with a 20% surcharge 22:00–23:00 and 02:00–04:00 and a 40% surcharge 23:00–02:00 (Seoul Metropolitan Government, fare table in force since 2023-02). For the fare table and how to handle overcharging see #501; for hailing by app see #1273.
  • Seoul Bike (따릉이): Seoul's public bike. Non-member day pass ₩1,000 for 1 hour · ₩2,000 for 2 hours · ₩3,000 for 3 hours; the payment screen has a foreigner-only "Discover Pass" option and you can choose English, Chinese or Japanese (Seoul Bike official). Customer service 1599-0120. It is not included in the Climate Card short-term pass.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

  1. Riding the Shinbundang Line, airport buses or express buses with a short-term pass — These are out of range. Only the Seoul subway and Seoul-licensed buses (plus exit at Incheon Airport T1 and T2).
  2. Loading the short-term pass the day before departure — The day you load it is the start date. Buy it the morning of the day you start using it.
  3. Boarding at Incheon Airport Station with the short-term pass — It works for exit only. When going to the airport, use T-money or an express train ticket.
  4. "Foreigners also get K-Pass refunds" — Signing up requires an Alien Registration Number, so short-term visitors find it hard to meet the condition.
  5. Buying all three cards — Most people need only one T-money card; add the short-term pass only if you will stay in Seoul several days and ride the subway a lot (#1298).

⚙ 4 FAQs

Q1. How many subway rides a day make the short-term pass worth it?

If you take the transit card base fare as ₩1,550 (the value reported for the 2025-06 increase, excluding distance surcharges), the 1-day pass at ₩5,000 breaks even at 4 rides a day, and with the ₩3,000 card cost on top there is almost nothing left over. Counting the ₩3,000 card cost, the 2-day pass pays off at about 4 rides a day and the 3-, 5- and 7-day passes at about 3 rides a day. If you already have a physical card, calculate without the card cost. ₩1,550 is the same value as the metropolitan-area base fare in the AREX fare guide; for exact fares, see the ticket machine or app screen.

Q2. Can I buy the short-term pass from overseas in advance?

No. Foreign-issued cards can be used at the new-type machines at Line 1–8 stations from 2026-03-17 (Seoul FAQ). Seoul's guidance does not mention foreign cards for convenience-store payment, so bring cash.

Q3. KORAIL PASS Plus or regular KTX tickets: which is cheaper?

With ₩131,000 for 2 days as the baseline, it is worth comparing if your itinerary includes a round trip or more on the KTX within two days. Fares follow the KORAIL screen, and you should read the refund and change conditions before buying. For the cost-benefit calculation see #1374.

Q4. Who can I call?

KORAIL 1599-7777 (as listed on its English website, 08:00–20:00), Seoul Bike 1599-0120, AREX 1599-7788, and 1330 for tourism in general (#1299).
