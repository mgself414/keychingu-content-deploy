⚠ 3 Noodle Mistakes

1. "Korean noodles = naengmyeon only" — missing the 5 axes: Naengmyeon only, they assume. In reality, it is the full experience of Pyongyang naengmyeon, Jinju naengmyeon, janchi-guksu, kalguksu, bibim-guksu, kongguksu, ramyeon, and budae-jjigae.

2. "Pyongyang naengmyeon = same as Japanese ramen" — missing the spicy vs. clean-flavor contrast: They assume it is the same. In reality, Pyongyang naengmyeon = clean and refreshing (#190 Japanese enthusiasts' #1) vs. Japanese ramen = rich broth. A full experience for enthusiasts of the contrast.

3. "Noodles = Korean-language only" — missing the English-friendly clusters: Korean only, they assume. In reality, Myeongdong & Itaewon English-menu kalguksu · janchi-guksu (#471) · Gwangjang Market, foreigners' fan-favorite #1 are English friendly.
