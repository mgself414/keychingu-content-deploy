🎯 How to Use This Guide

Only once an order comes in is the buckwheat dough pressed, and freshly extruded noodles go straight into the bowl — Michelin writes that Yangyang Memil Makguksu (양양 메밀 막국수) in Seocho-dong (서초동) works this way. This guide divides Korean noodles into four branches: Pyeongyang naengmyeon (평양냉면, cold buckwheat noodles in beef broth), buckwheat noodles and makguksu (막국수, rough buckwheat noodles), kalguksu (칼국수, knife-cut noodle soup), and Busan milmyeon (밀면, wheat noodles) and naengmyeon (냉면, cold noodles). We picked only places listed on the official Michelin Guide site and in VisitKorea tourism data. This edition is for travelers who want a light meal in a single bowl of noodles, and for people trying naengmyeon for the first time.

We could not verify the old guide's naengmyeon place "Udeugi (우드기)" or "Jinju naengmyeon (진주냉면), 4.5 hours by KTX", and "Uraeok (우래옥) for ₩10,000 (~$7.50)" had no source (a 2017 VisitKorea article gives ₩13,000 (~$9.75) — not today's price). "Ramyeon and budae-jjigae" are not noodle shops, so we dropped them from this edition. Budae-jjigae is in #486.

🍜 Pyeongyang Naengmyeon — 3 Places in Seoul

  • Uraeok (우래옥) — 62-29 Changgyeonggung-ro (창경궁로), Jung District, Seoul. Tue–Sun 11:30–21:00 (last order 20:20), closed every Monday and on January 1 (VisitKorea, Michelin page). Opened in 1946. A broth made from simmered Korean-beef arong-sat'ae (아롱사태, shank), seasoned only with salt and soy sauce. Bibim-naengmyeon (비빔냉면, spicy mixed cold noodles), on-myeon (온면, warm noodles in broth) and kimchi-mari naengmyeon (김치말이냉면, noodles in kimchi broth); many people add bulgogi (불고기). Phone 02-2265-0151, parking and valet (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Pildong Myeonok (필동면옥) — 26 Seoae-ro (서애로), Jung District, Seoul (near Namsangol Hanok Village (남산골 한옥마을)). 11:00–20:20, break 16:00–17:00, closed every Sunday (VisitKorea). A clean meat broth, with pork suyuk (수육, boiled sliced meat) as the signature side. Described as a good place to eat alone. Phone 02-2266-2611 (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Jeongin Myeonok (정인면옥) — 10 Gukhoe-daero 76-gil (국회대로76길), Yeongdeungpo District, Seoul. Mon–Fri 11:00–15:00 and 17:00–21:15, Sat–Sun 11:00–15:30 and 17:00–21:00. Noodles made from pure buckwheat with no wheat flour, in a Korean-beef broth seasoned with sun-dried sea salt aged over 3 years. Arong-sat'ae suyuk (아롱사태 수육) and mandu (만두, dumplings) are also served. Wheelchair accessible (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • For a full list of Seoul naengmyeon places, see #1130 Naengmyeon Index for Foreigners

🌾 Buckwheat Noodles and Makguksu — 3 Places in Seoul

  • Mijin (미진) — 19 Jong-ro (종로), Lemeilleur Jongno Town (르메이에르 종로타운) Unit 116-2, Jongno District, Seoul. Daily 10:30–21:00 (last order 20:30), open year-round (VisitKorea, Michelin page). Opened in 1952. Cold buckwheat noodles in a soy broth richer than Japanese soba tsuyu, with the broth and noodles made in an underground factory. Memil-jeonbyeong (메밀전병, buckwheat crepes) filled with bean sprouts, tofu, kimchi and pork can be ordered as a half plate. Phone 02-730-6198 (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Yurimmyeon (유림면) — 139-1 Seosomun-ro (서소문로), Jung District, Seoul. Mon–Sat 11:00–20:00, closed Sun. In business in Seosomun (서소문) for over 50 years, moved to its current spot in 1980, now in its third generation. Noodles made from only Bongpyeong (봉평) buckwheat and salt from Bigeumdo (비금도) island in Sinan. Signature dishes are bibim memil-guksu (비빔 메밀국수, mixed buckwheat noodles) and garak-guksu (가락국수, thick wheat noodles) topped with fish cake. Counter seating (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Yangyang Memil Makguksu (양양 메밀 막국수) — 10 Donggwang-ro 15-gil (동광로15길), Seocho District, Seoul. Daily 11:00–15:00 and 17:00–21:00, closed about ten days during the November kimjang (김장, kimchi-making) season. 100% pure buckwheat, with noodles made after you order. Memil makguksu (메밀 막국수) and kimchi bibim-guksu (김치 비빔국수) made with house kimchi (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)

🔪 Kalguksu — 3 Places in Seoul

  • Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자) — 29 Myeongdong 10-gil (명동10길), Jung District, Seoul. Daily 10:30–21:00. Family-run since 1966, with four menu items. Famous for kalguksu and mandu (만두). Lines often form. Phone +82 2-776-5348 (Michelin page; no Michelin selection or Bib Gourmand mark). VisitKorea tourism data also lists a branch at 129 Toegye-ro (퇴계로), with kalguksu, mandu, bibim-guksu (비빔국수) and kongguksu (콩국수, cold soy milk noodles) on the menu
  • Hwangsaengga Kalguksu (황생가 칼국수) — 78 Bukchon-ro 5-gil (북촌로5길), Jongno District, Seoul. 11:00–21:30 (last order 20:30), closed on Seollal (설) and Chuseok (추석) days (VisitKorea). Opened in 2001 as "Bukchon Kalguksu (북촌칼국수)" and renamed in 2014. Kalguksu in bone broth, wang-mandu (왕만두, large dumplings) shaped at the entrance every morning, and summer-only kongguksu (콩국수) (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Im Byeong-ju Sandong Kalguksu (임병주 산동 칼국수) — 65 Gangnam-daero 37-gil (강남대로37길), Seocho District, Seoul. Tue–Fri 11:00–21:30, Sat–Sun 11:00–21:00, closed Mon. Opened in 1988, with hand-pulled noodles every day in a clear broth generously loaded with clams. Pyeongyang-style wang-mandu (왕만두) and kongguksu (콩국수) are also available. Cash only, solo dining possible (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)

🌊 Busan Milmyeon and Naengmyeon — 3 Places

  • Busan Naeho Naengmyeon (부산 내호냉면) — 17 Uam-beonyeong-ro 26beon-gil (우암번영로26번길), Nam District, Busan (Uam Market (우암시장) alley). 10:30–19:00 (last order 18:30), open year-round. Mul-naengmyeon (물냉면, noodles in cold broth), bibim-naengmyeon (비빔냉면) and bibim-milmyeon (비빔밀면). The noodles refugees made from rationed flour during the Korean War were the start of Busan milmyeon, and the fourth generation carries it on today. Phone 0507-1333-6195 (VisitKorea)
  • Gaegeum Milmyeon (개금밀면) — 9-4 Gaya-daero 482beon-gil (가야대로482번길), Busanjin District, Busan (Gaegeum-dong (개금동)). 11:00–19:40, open year-round. Mul-milmyeon (물밀면) and bibim-milmyeon (비빔밀면). A self-service restaurant: you order and pay at the entrance first, then carry your food yourself when your number buzzes. The broth includes herbal ingredients such as licorice, angelica root and cinnamon. Phone 051-892-3466 (VisitKorea)
  • Buda Myeonok (부다면옥) — 36 Jungdong 1-ro (중동1로), 2nd floor, Haeundae District, Busan. Pyeongyang naengmyeon made from 100% pure buckwheat, in a Korean-beef broth blended with vegetable stock. The sokkori gomtang (소꼬리 곰탕, oxtail soup) is also recommended. Moved from Banyeo-dong (반여동) in 2021. Phone +82 51-746-8872 (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand). The day-by-day hours on the Michelin page show only one day of the week, so we could not confirm business hours — call to check
  • For Busan food in general, see #1320 Three Things You Only Eat in Busan

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Price range: None of the 12 places has an officially posted won price. The Michelin pages show only the ₩ symbol (the lowest tier)
  • Vegetarian: Naengmyeon broth is made from beef (Uraeok (우래옥), Jeongin Myeonok (정인면옥), Buda Myeonok (부다면옥)), and kalguksu from bones (Hwangsaengga (황생가)) or clams (Im Byeong-ju (임병주)) (Michelin pages). Milmyeon broth is also made from chicken, beef and pork bones (VisitKorea). VisitKorea lists kongguksu as a food suitable for vegetarians, but notes that an egg may be added as a garnish — the kongguksu at Hwangsaengga (황생가) and Im Byeong-ju (임병주) is a summer menu item
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil (우사단로10길), Yongsan District, daily 12:00–21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF). None of the noodle places in this guide carries a halal label
  • Allergies: Buckwheat (Mijin (미진), Yurimmyeon (유림면), Yangyang (양양), the Pyeongyang naengmyeon places), wheat (kalguksu, milmyeon), shellfish (Im Byeong-ju's (임병주) clams), soy (kongguksu)
  • For a show-it-to-the-staff card, see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Noodle Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Ordering, Getting There

  • Solo: Noodles are one bowl per person, so it is easy to go alone. Michelin marks Pildong Myeonok (필동면옥) and Im Byeong-ju Sandong Kalguksu (임병주 산동 칼국수) as good for dining alone, and Yurimmyeon (유림면) has counter seating. For a solo-dining map, see #348 Seoul Solo Diner Map
  • Reservations: The official pages all just say "call the restaurant directly". Be ready to queue at lunch (Jeongin Myeonok (정인면옥) and Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자), Michelin pages)
  • Payment: Im Byeong-ju Sandong Kalguksu (임병주 산동 칼국수) is cash only (Michelin page). Gaegeum Milmyeon (개금밀면) is prepaid at the entrance (VisitKorea). For cards and mobile payment, see #527 Korea's Payment Systems
  • Ordering: For naengmyeon you choose between mul (물, in cold broth) and bibim (비빔, mixed with sauce). For how to eat noodles, see #814 Ordering and Eating Korean Noodles Workflow; if there is a language barrier, see #1339 Ordering Etiquette
  • Getting there: For Seoul, see #1298 Choosing from the 3 Transit Cards; for Busan, #1325 Busan Public Transit Grammar

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Uraeok (우래옥) on Monday" — Uraeok is closed every Monday (VisitKorea, Michelin page). Pildong Myeonok (필동면옥) and Yurimmyeon (유림면) rest on Sunday

2. "Kongguksu is always available" — The kongguksu (콩국수) at Hwangsaengga Kalguksu (황생가 칼국수) is summer-only (Michelin page)

3. "Cards accepted everywhere" — Im Byeong-ju Sandong Kalguksu (임병주 산동 칼국수) takes only cash (Michelin page)

4. "Milmyeon is the same as naengmyeon" — Milmyeon is made from wheat flour and starch, so it is softer than naengmyeon, and it is a Busan local noodle served in a broth made with herbal ingredients (VisitKorea)
