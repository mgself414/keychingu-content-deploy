🎯 Why a Jangsaengpo Whale + Bangudae Petroglyphs Guide?

Ulsan Jangsaengpo (울산 장생포) = Korea's #1 whaling base (before the 1986 IWC moratorium) and Jangsaengpo Whale Museum (장생포 고래 박물관) (established 1986). Bangudae Petroglyphs (반구대 암각화) = 7000-year-old Neolithic site, UNESCO Tentative List (2010), featuring whale, deer, and tiger carvings. Foreigner pain points = (a) Misconception that "whale museum = small museum" — it's Korea's #1 whale museum (b) Jangsaengpo (장생포) was Korea's #1 whaling base before the 1986 moratorium (c) Bangudae (반구대) has the earliest human record of whale hunting (7000 years ago) (d) UNESCO Tentative List (2010, candidate for official registration) (e) Full English information & foreigner enthusiast matrix.

This guide covers 5 axes, 12 signatures + museum + UNESCO candidate + foreigner-friendly English information.

2. "Jangsaengpo (장생포) = Ordinary Fishing Village": Korea's #1 whaling base before the 1986 IWC moratorium.

3. "Bangudae (반구대) = Just a Rock": 7000-year-old Neolithic petroglyphs·UNESCO Tentative List (2010)·Earliest human record of whale hunting.

4. "Bangudae (반구대) = OK Year-Round": Summer rainy season = Submerged (No viewing)·Autumn·Winter = Low water level → Petroglyphs exposed.

5. "Whaling = Still Ongoing": Korea = Whaling prohibited after the 1986 IWC moratorium·Only museum + cultural matrix now.
