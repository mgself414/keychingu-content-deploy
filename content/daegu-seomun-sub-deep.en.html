Daegu Seomun Market, Day and Night — Day Market 09:00~18:00, Night Market Fri-Sun Only

Subtitle: Seomun Market (서문시장) (09:00~18:00, closed on the 1st and 3rd Sunday of each month) · Seomun Night Market (서문시장 야시장) (Fri-Sat 19:00~23:30, Sun 19:00~22:30, closed Mon-Thu) · Keunjang-gil Bedding Street (큰장길 침구류 명물거리) · Chilseong Night Market — for operating information, see Korea Tourism Organization tourism information (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Daegu locals have long called Seomun Market "Keunjang," or "the Big Market" (큰장). Daegu's market was counted alongside Pyongyang and Ganggyeong among the three great markets of the Joseon era, and today it is still packed with fabric, clothing, tableware, and dried seafood shops. After dark, food stalls line up and the night market opens. But the night market is open only on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday evenings.

This guide is ordered as the day market · the night market · the surrounding lanes, and the operating information is based mainly on values registered with Korea Tourism Organization tourism information (TourAPI). Values confirmed only through news reports are marked separately.

🧵 Seomun Market by Day — "Keunjang"

Seomun Market (Keunjang-ro 26-gil 45, Jung-gu, Daegu / 대구 중구 큰장로26길 45) got its name because it stood to the west of Daegu's fortress wall. It began as a market held on the 2nd and 7th of each month about 300m from the wall, and the Korea Tourism Organization says it moved to its present site in the 1920s after a pond called Cheonhwangdangji (천황당지) was filled in. Old lane names such as "Hongdukkae Jeon" and "Dak Jeon" survive today.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed on the 1st and 3rd Sunday of each month.
  • What is sold: Textile-related goods are the core. Silk and hanbok cloth, knitted underwear, crafts, men's and women's clothing, tableware, and dried seafood.
  • Facilities: There are restrooms and a parking lot. For tourist information, contact the Cheongna Hill (청라언덕) Tourist Center ☎ 053-421-3339.
  • If you plan to buy bulky items such as hanbok fabric or blankets, go during the day.

🌙 Seomun Night Market — Fri, Sat, Sun Evenings

The Seomun Night Market opened in June 2016 (per news reports). Operating hours, based on information registered with the Korea Tourism Organization, are as follows.

  • Friday and Saturday: 19:00~23:30
  • Sunday: 19:00~22:30
  • Monday to Thursday: Not open.

Get off at Line 3 Seomun Market Station and you come out right by the night market (per news reports). If your Daegu schedule covers only weekdays, see the day market instead of the night market, and keep in mind that Chilseong Night Market, which runs on the same weekly schedule, is also closed on weekdays.

🛏 Keunjang-gil Bedding Street

Next to Seomun Market, Keunjang-gil Bedding Street (Keunjang-ro 35, Seo-gu, Daegu / 대구 서구 큰장로 35, ☎ 053-555-2227) is home to about 70 shops selling blankets, pillows, and needlework. Wholesale and retail bedding shops opened one by one starting about 30 years ago, and the street grew from there. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, Daegu's bedding manufacturers account for roughly 27% of the national total (578 companies). Opening hours vary by shop.

🌃 Another Night Market — Chilseong Night Market

Chilseong Market (칠성시장) (Chilseong-sijang-ro 28, Buk-gu, Daegu / 대구 북구 칠성시장로 28) is, along with Seomun Market, one of the largest traditional markets in Daegu. In November 2019, Byeolbyeol Sangsang Chilseong Night Market (별별상상 칠성야시장) opened on the Sincheon Stream embankment beside the market, with 60 kinds of food and a 400-seat rest area.

  • Chilseong Market: 05:00~20:00 (varies by shop), open year-round (varies by shop).
  • Chilseong Night Market: Fri-Sat 19:00~23:30, Sun 19:00~22:30, closed Mon-Thu. Inquiries ☎ 053-710-6353.
  • Line 1 Chilseong Market Station (칠성시장역) is right next to the market.
  • Parking: 500 won for the first 30 minutes, then 250 won per 10 minutes, up to 5,000 won per day.

🥟 What to Eat in Daegu — Napjak-mandu

Napjak-mandu, flat dumplings (납작만두), are a food you can try in Daegu, as introduced by the Korea Tourism Organization. They contain just three fillings, which are scallions, garlic chives, and glass noodles, and are eaten with soy sauce drizzled on top.

  • Misungdang Napjak Mandu (Myeongdeok-ro 93, Jung-gu, Daegu / 대구 중구 명덕로 93, ☎ 053-255-0742): A napjak-mandu specialist run across generations since 1963 (information registered with the Korea Tourism Organization). Weekdays 10:30~21:00, weekends 10:30~20:00, closed every Monday and on Seollal and Chuseok days. It is on Myeongdeok-ro, not inside Seomun Market.
  • For eateries picked by Daegu locals, see #1383 Local Picks 2: Daegu and Gyeongbuk.

🚇 Getting There and Nearby

  • Subway: Get off at Seomun Market Station on Daegu Metro Line 3 (per news reports). For Chilseong Night Market, use Line 1 Chilseong Market Station.
  • Cheongna Hill (Dalgubeol-daero 2029, Jung-gu / 중구 달구벌대로 2029): A hill with three early-20th-century missionary houses, the 90 Steps, and the March 1st Independence Movement Road, open at all times. It is the starting point of Daegu Alley Tour Course 2. Continue your walk through the old alleys with #1003 Daegu Modern Alleys + Kim Kwang-seok Street.
  • Daegu Tourist Information Center ☎ 053-627-1337 · Tourist interpretation service ☎ 1330.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Going to the night market on a weekday evening — both Seomun Night Market and Chilseong Night Market are closed Mon-Thu.

2. Visiting the market during the day on the 1st or 3rd Sunday — the day market at Seomun is closed on the 1st and 3rd Sunday of each month. The night market is registered in Korea Tourism Organization information as closed only Mon-Thu.

3. Trying to buy fabric or blankets during night market hours — the day market closes at 18:00. Split your trip: shopping by day, food by night.

⚙ FAQ 3

Q. How late is the night market open on Sundays?

A. Based on information registered with the Korea Tourism Organization, Sunday is 19:00~22:30, which closes one hour earlier than Friday and Saturday (until 23:30).

Q. Can I see both the day market and the night market in one day?

A. On Friday, Saturday, or a Sunday that is not the 1st or 3rd Sunday, yes. The order that fits the operating hours is to browse the day market until 18:00 and stop by Cheongna Hill before the night market opens at 19:00.

Q. Can I eat napjak-mandu inside the market?

A. The Misungdang Napjak Mandu listed in this guide is on Myeongdeok-ro in Jung-gu, not in Seomun Market.
