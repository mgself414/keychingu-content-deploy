🎯 Why is Yangcheon and Mokdong an exclusive guide?

Yangcheon and Mok-dong are located in Yangcheon-gu's Korea's No. 1 middle-class, education and broadcasting district — Mok-dong (1985 New Town, Korea's No. 1 middle-class apartment complex, Korea's No. 1 education district) + SBS Broadcasting Station (Korea's top three broadcasting stations) + Seoseoul Lake Park (family-friendly) + Mokdong Playground and Ice Rink + Sinjeong-Sinwol Intersection Authentic Neighborhood combined. No. 1 deep dive in Korea’s middle class, education, and broadcasting.

Compared to Hongdae (MZ), Gangnam (luxury), and Itaewon (global), Yangcheon and Mokdong are low middle class, education, and broadcasting. Although it is overlooked by Gangnam and Hongdae in the flow of foreign tourists, Mok-dong is Korea's No. 1 new town/education hot zone + SBS K-drama/entertainment deep dive + Seoseoul Lake Park family-friendly = No. 1 deep dive for foreigners' daily life in Korea and the middle class. ⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Mok-dong = just an apartment complex" — Korea's No. 1 middle class and education center is not aware of the value

Mok-dong is not just an apartment complex, but 1985 New Town, Korea's No. 1 middle class + No. 1 education district — Korea's prestigious academy district, No. 1 school + middle-class family, and education hot zone. Part of the K-drama "SKY Castle" stage.

2. "SBS Broadcasting Station = Cannot enter" — Appearance and tour value unknown

SBS Deungchon Building's exterior and partial tours are OK (advance reservations required). Korean K-drama/entertainment deep dive. A regular stage for SBS’s popular entertainment shows “Running Man,” “My Little Old Boy,” and “Same Bed, Different Dreams.”

3. "West Seoul Lake Park = just a park"

Seoseoul Lake Park is Yangcheon 1st Lake Park + Sound Fountain (Foreigner Instagram) + Family Walk + Adjacent to Yangcheon-gu Office. Spring cherry blossoms + fall foliage #1.

Scenery of Yangcheon and Mokdong

Yangcheon and Mok-dong are in the center of Yangcheon-gu — the area of Line 5 Omokgyo Station, Mokdong Station, Sinjeong Station, and Shinwol Station. Korea's 1985 new town + 1990s middle class + 2000s established as No. 1 in education.

Transportation: Line 5 Omokgyo Station, Mokdong Station, Sinjeong Station, Shinwol Station / Line 9 Yeomchang Station, Deungchon Station, Jeungmi Station. Kakao Taxi recommended.

⚙ FAQ (6)

Q1. Foreign tour of SBS broadcasting station?

Available — SBS Deungchon Building exterior + partial tour (advance reservation required, SBS website in English is OK). K-drama·entertainment deep dive.

Q2. Seo Seoul Lake Park entrance fee?

Free + 24 hours — Free entry for foreigners. Lake + sound fountain + walking trail. 15-minute walk from Sinjeongnegeori Station on Line 5.

Q3. Mokdong Ice Rink?

Operated all year round — Korea's No. 1 ice rink. General admission for foreigners OK (seasonal price difference).

Q4. Mokdong + Gimpo Airport connection?

OK — Transfer to Lines 5 and 9. Mok-dong (Line 5) → Gimpo Airport (Directly connected to Line 5).

Q5. What are the foreigner-friendly stores in Mokdong?

Ilmirak, Cheonggiwa Town, Bread Time, and Non-Off have English menus + card payments.

Q6. Yangcheon + Magog connection?

OK — Line 5, Station 1. Magok (Seoul Botanical Garden·LG) + Yangcheon (SBS·Mokdong) = 1 full day.
