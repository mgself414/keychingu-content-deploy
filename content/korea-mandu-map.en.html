Korean Mandu Guide — Gaeseong and North-Style Mandu Soup and Chinese-Style Dumplings at 8 Places (Gaeseong Mandu Gung (개성만두 궁), Jaha Son Mandu (자하손만두), Mandujip (만두집), Sinbalwon (신발원)) in Seoul and Busan (Official Figures, October 2026)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Big dumplings, wrapped in dough fermented for three hours, arrive floating in a clear meat broth — this is the mandutguk (만둣국, dumpling soup) at Gaeseong Mandu Gung (개성만두 궁), a hanok (한옥, traditional Korean house) in Insadong (인사동) (Michelin page). This guide covers only places where mandu (만두, dumplings) are the star. There are three branches: hanok mandu houses (Gaeseong-style and Buam-dong), North-style mandutguk, and Chinese-style dumplings. Mandu served as a side at kalguksu and naengmyeon places (Myeongdong Gyoja (명동교자), Hwangsaengga Kalguksu (황생가 칼국수), Jeongin Myeonok (정인면옥) and others) appear only in the #480 Noodle Guide. We picked only places listed on the official Michelin Guide site and in VisitKorea tourism data. This edition is for people who want a bowl of mild broth and for those who want to compare mandu.

The old guide described Jaha Son Mandu (자하손만두) as "carrying on royal-court mandu in Seongbuk-dong (성북동)". On the official pages, Jaha Son Mandu (자하손만두) is in Buam-dong (부암동), Jongno District (12 Baekseok-dong-gil (백석동길)), and the owner, Park Hye-gyeong (박혜경), has served mandutguk since 1993 using the home cooking skills handed down from her grandmother and mother (Michelin page, VisitKorea). There was no basis for the "royal-court mandu" claim. We also could not find "Buam Pyeongyang Mandutjip (부암 평양만둣집)" on any official page, so we dropped it.

🏠 Hanok Mandu Houses — 2 Places in Seoul

  • Gaeseong Mandu Gung (개성만두 궁) — 11-3 Insadong 10-gil (인사동10길), Jongno District, Seoul. Weekdays 11:30–21:30 (break 15:00–16:00, last order 20:50), Sat 11:30–21:30, Sun 11:30–20:00, open year-round (VisitKorea). Started in 1970 by a grandmother from Gaeseong (개성), now in its third generation. Filling generous with cabbage and mung-bean sprouts, brisket broth. Meat mandu hot pot (gogi-mandu-jeongol (고기만두전골)), kimchi mandu hot pot (kimchi-mandu-jeongol (김치만두전골)) and jorengi-tteok mandutguk (조랭이떡 만둣국, with small cocoon-shaped rice cakes). A renovated hanok space. Phone 02-733-9240 (VisitKorea, Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Jaha Son Mandu (자하손만두) — 12 Baekseok-dong-gil (백석동길), Jongno District, Seoul (Buam-dong (부암동)). 11:00–21:00 (last order 20:15), closed every Monday and on Seollal (설) and Chuseok (추석) days (VisitKorea). Since 1993. A clear broth seasoned with homemade Joseon soy sauce and no chemical seasoning, with dumpling wrappers made from domestic wheat flour. Mandutguk, tteok-mandutguk (떡만둣국, with sliced rice cakes), mandu-jeongol (만두전골, mandu hot pot) and pyeonsu (편수, square-wrapped dumplings). A terrace with a view of Inwangsan (인왕산) and seating where you take your shoes off. Phone 02-379-2648 (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand). Its Michelin price symbol is ₩₩, the only one a tier higher in this guide

🥟 North-Style Mandutguk — 3 Places in Seoul, 1 in Busan

  • Mandujip (만두집) — 338 Apgujeong-ro (압구정로), Gangnam District, Seoul. Mon–Sat 12:00–20:00, closed Sun. In business in Cheongdam-dong (청담동) for over 30 years. The signature is a Pyeongan-style (평안도식) mandutguk with handmade mandu in brisket broth, and you can ask for it less spicy. Nokdu-jeon (녹두전, mung-bean pancake) and gochu-jeon (고추전, stuffed pepper pancake) are also served. Phone +82 2-544-3710 (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Mipildam (미필담) — 22 Seongji 3-gil (성지3길), Mapo District, Seoul (an alley in Hapjeong-dong (합정동)). Wed–Sun 11:30–14:30 and 17:30–20:30, closed Mon–Tue. North-style handmade mandutguk simmered by a couple using the recipe of the wife's maternal grandmother from Hwanghae (황해도) Province. Seating for solo diners, counter seats. Phone +82 10-5455-5041 (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)
  • Ibuk Mandu (이북만두) — 17-13 Mugyo-ro (무교로), Jung District, Seoul (back alley of Seoul City Hall). Weekdays 11:00–21:00 (break 14:30–16:30), weekends 10:30–20:00, open year-round. Ibuk mandu-guk (이북만두국): not an ox-bone broth but a clear one, topped with just a large dumpling, spring onion and pepper; kimchi-mari-bap (김치말이밥, rice in kimchi broth) in summer. Phone 02-776-7361, non-smoking throughout (VisitKorea)
  • Pyeongyangjip (평양집) (Busan) — 21 Geumgok-daero 20beon-gil (금곡대로20번길), Buk District, Busan. Mon–Sat 10:30–15:30, closed Sun. North-style handmade mandu filled with kimchi, glass noodles, tofu and vegetables, served in a brisket and shank broth. Choose between kimchi mandu (김치만두) and tofu mandu (두부만두); nokdu-jeon (녹두전) is also available. Phone +82 51-331-5445 (Michelin page, Bib Gourmand)

🥢 Chinese-Style Dumplings — 2 Places in Busan and Seoul

  • Sinbalwon (신발원) — 62 Daeyeong-ro 243beon-gil (대영로243번길), Dong District, Busan (Choryang-dong (초량동)). 11:00–21:00 (last order 20:20), closed every Tuesday. 90 m from Exit 5 of Busan Station. Meat dumplings (gogi-mandu (고기만두)), fried dumplings (gun-mandu (군만두)), shrimp dumplings (saeu-mandu (새우만두)) and chive-and-glass-noodle dumplings (buchu-dangmyeon-mandu (부추당면만두)); kongguk (콩국, soy milk soup) and kong-gwaja (콩과자, soybean sweets) are also served. Takeout available. Phone 0507-1440-0195 (VisitKorea). For the whole Choryang Chinatown, see #1409 One Hour Outside Busan Station
  • Gubok Mandu (구복만두) — 7 Duteopbawi-ro (두텁바위로), Yongsan District, Seoul. A small dim sum (딤섬) shop run by a Korean husband and his Chinese wife, with four menu items. The signature is the pan-fried pork-and-kimchi "Gubok kimchi mandu (구복 김치만두)", made to order so there is a short wait. Phone +82 2-797-8656 (Michelin page; no Michelin selection or Bib Gourmand mark). The day-by-day hours on the Michelin page show only one day of the week, so we could not confirm business hours — call to check

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Price range: None has an officially posted won price. By Michelin symbol, Jaha Son Mandu (자하손만두) is ₩₩ and the other 5 Michelin-listed places are ₩ (Michelin pages)
  • Vegetarian: Most mandu fillings contain pork (Michelin pages for Gaeseong Mandu Gung (개성만두 궁) and Gubok Mandu (구복만두)), and the mandutguk broth is made from brisket or shank (Mandujip (만두집), Pyeongyangjip (평양집), Gaeseong Mandu Gung (개성만두 궁)). A Michelin photo caption says Gaeseong Mandu Gung (개성만두 궁) has mushroom mandu (버섯만두), but we could not confirm its broth or filling ingredients. The tofu mandu at Pyeongyangjip (평양집) also comes in a meat broth. VisitKorea notes that most kimchi seasoning contains salted seafood or fish sauce — it is safest to assume kimchi mandu is not vegetarian
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil (우사단로10길), Yongsan District, daily 12:00–21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF). None of the mandu places in this guide carries a halal label
  • Allergies: Wheat (dumpling wrappers), soy and tofu (tofu mandu, Sinbalwon's (신발원) kongguk), shrimp (Sinbalwon's (신발원) shrimp dumplings)
  • For a show-it-to-the-staff card, see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Mandu Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Ordering, Getting There

  • Solo: Mandutguk is one bowl per person, so it is easy to go alone. Michelin marks solo-diner seating at Mipildam (미필담). Hot pots (Gaeseong Mandu Gung (개성만두 궁), Jaha Son Mandu (자하손만두)) are best chosen when going with several people. For a solo-dining map, see #348 Seoul Solo Diner Map
  • Reservations: The official pages all just say "call the restaurant directly"
  • Payment: The 6 Michelin-listed places accept credit cards (Michelin pages). For cards and mobile payment, see #527 Korea's Payment Systems
  • Ordering: Sinbalwon (신발원) does takeout (VisitKorea) — a good place to pick something up at Busan Station before boarding a train. If there is a language barrier, see #1339 Ordering Etiquette
  • Getting there: For Seoul subway and buses, see #1298 Choosing from the 3 Transit Cards; for Busan, #1325 Busan Public Transit Grammar

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Jaha Son Mandu is a royal-court mandu house in Seongbuk-dong" — It is in Buam-dong, and it carries on the home mandu of a grandmother and mother (Michelin page, VisitKorea)

2. "Mandu in Buam-dong on a Monday" — Jaha Son Mandu (자하손만두) is closed every Monday (VisitKorea, Michelin page). Mipildam (미필담) also rests on Monday and Tuesday

3. "Busan's Pyeongyangjip for dinner" — Pyeongyangjip (평양집) is open only 10:30–15:30 (Michelin page)

4. "Sinbalwon is open every day" — It is closed every Tuesday (VisitKorea)
