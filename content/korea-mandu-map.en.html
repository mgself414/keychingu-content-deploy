⚠ 3 Mandu Mistakes

1. "Korean mandu = same as Chinese dumplings" — missing the differences: The sameness assumption. In reality, Pyongyang mandu (clean and mild, fits #480 naengmyeon) · Jaha Son royal court mandu (#1 with #190 Japanese fans) · Seoul-style wang mandu (oversized) stand apart from Chinese dumplings (small jiaozi) — each with its own devotees.

2. "Mandu = anywhere in Korea" — missing the legacy-house clusters: The anywhere assumption. In reality, the Woo Lae Oak · Pyongyang Myeonok · Jaha Son (Korea's #1 legacy houses) · Buam Pyongyang Mandu House legacy cluster plus 60+ years of devotee history make the full experience.

3. "Mandu = season-agnostic" — missing tteok-manduguk: The no-season assumption. In reality, Seollal tteok-manduguk (January) · Dongji tteok-manduguk (December · #473 · #476) plus the #473 tteok cluster are seasonal-only.
