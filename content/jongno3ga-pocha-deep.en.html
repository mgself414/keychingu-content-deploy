Why Jongno 3-ga Pocha now? — Seoul #1 Pojangmacha Yajang hub

Jongno 3-ga Pocha Street is a Seoul #1 food stall yard hub formed in the 200m section between exits 5 and 6** of Jongno 3-ga Station (transfer station for lines 1, 3, and 5). As the sun sets, orange tents cover the streets, plastic tables spread out on the sidewalks — a street where the essence of Korean “pocha culture” is still alive.

In particular, Exit 13 = No. 1 No. 1 street for gun carriages with 20+ years of experience + "Songhae-gil" designated as an honorary road in 2016 (240m to Supyo-ro, Jongno 2-ga Yukuijeon~Nakwon Shopping Center) = Memorial street for teacher Song Hae, who was active in Nagwon-dong, Jongno for 50+ years. Gwanghwamun, Jonggak, City Hall office workers, MZ + Ikseon-dong, Insa-dong tourism cross + foreigner vlog (selected by many from Trazy·Shuttledelivery·trip.com) A hub where everyone gathers.

Clear differentiation from Namdaemun (#581 long-established Korean food), Plaza (#582 vlog street food), and Dongdaemun (#583 24h fashion) = "Night-time pocha·hub atmosphere·hub shared seating·Seoul office worker culture" cluster. As an alternative to Itaewon, foreign gay clusters (near Bar MoU, etc.) enthusiasts also join in — diverse segment fit.

This matrix is ​​classified on 4 axes: cluster by area + signature menu + nearby cross, not by store name. Pocha fits foreign enthusiasts based on “which exit, which tent, and signature menu” rather than the store name.

FAQ 6 — Frequently asked questions about Jongno 3-ga Pocha by foreigners

Q1. What is Pojangmacha? How about your first visit?

  • Pocha = Pojangmacha = "covered wagon" Korean-style street tent bar/restaurant
  • Jongno 3-ga = Seoul #1 Pocha hub + Ikseon-dong·Insa-dong cross
  • First visit plan: Exits 5 and 6 (Main) → Sit down in a tent you like → Check menu → Order
  • dress code: Casual (suit/dress awkward)
  • Shared Seat: Possibility to share the same table with strangers (Korean culture — opportunity to become friends)

Q2. Where should I start on my first visit as a foreigner?

  • Main cluster (vlog 1st priority): #1 exit 5 or #2 exit 6 (200m main)
  • Old Store Mania (Orthodox): #3 Exit 13 (20+ years long-established store operator)
  • Honor Road Song Hae-gil (Korean culture enthusiasts): #4 Song Hae-gil (240m·Nakwon Shopping Center)
  • Menu fit: Snacks and soup (#8 cost-effective) → Snacks and meat (#9 K-drama) → Seafood (#7 challenge)

Q3. Average menu/price range?

  • Snack and soup (cost-effectiveness): Tteokbokki 5,000~8,000 won, fried food 5,000~10,000 won, udon 6,000~8,000 won
  • Snacks/Meat: Chicken feet 12,000~18,000 won, octopus 25,000~35,000 won, egg rolls 8,000~12,000 won
  • Seafood (challenge): Live octopus 25,000~40,000 won, sea squirt, sea cucumber 20,000~30,000 won
  • Alcohol: Soju 5,000 won, beer 5,000 won, makgeolli 6,000-8,000 won
  • Average per person: Soju + snacks = 15,000~25,000 won (#1 cost-effectiveness)
  • Group (3-4 people): KRW 60,000~120,000 (2-3 snacks + alcohol)

Q4. Payment/language/translation tips?

  • Payment: Cash/bank transfer only — Card very limited (KRW 30,000~50,000 recommended)
  • ATM: Many near Jongno 3-ga Station (KB, Shinhan, Woori, Kookmin)
  • English menu: Almost none — translation app (Papago Camera Translation) required
  • Simple Korean:
  • “Menu” / “One of these (igeo hana = one of this)”
  • “Soju (soju)” / “Beer (maekju)” / “Makgeolli (makgeolli)”
  • “eolma = how much” / “thank you (gamsahamnida = thank you)”
  • Picture menu pointing: Korean menu photo-based pointing OK

Q5. Foreign enthusiast time zone/day plan?

  • Early Bird (17:00-18:00): No. 1 spot for enthusiasts (Hansan + tent set up photo enthusiasts)
  • Peak (19:00-23:00): Foreign vlog mania + Atmosphere #1 + Wait 30 minutes+ Available
  • late night (23:00-dawn): End of office workers’ company dinner, MZ late night snack + Hansan
  • Dawn (00:00-02:00): No. 1 long-established store (#3) enthusiast + Foreigner night owl
  • Weekends (Friday, Saturday): Open until 03-04am (02:00 on weekdays) — MZ/Foreigner magic time
  • Sunday, Monday: Some stores are closed — Main cluster safety at exits 5 and 6

Q6. Jongno 3-ga Pocha vs. Gwangjang (#582) vs. Namdaemun (#581) — Which one fits?

  • Jongno 3-ga Pocha (#585): Night·hub·shared seat·office worker culture·soju pairing → MZ·foreigner vlog·LGBTQ+ cluster
  • Gwangjang Market (#582): Daytime·Netflix Street Food·Bindaetteok·Yukhoe·Makyak Gimbap vlog → MZ·SNS·Short Meal
  • Namdaemun Market (#581): Lunch, long-established Korean food, cutlassfish, Pyeongyang cold noodles, 1414 → JP·EN authentic Korean food enthusiasts
  • Full set plan (2 days and 1 night): Day 1 (Plaza #582 lunch + Jongno 3-ga #585 dinner and night) → Day 2 (Namdaemun #581 lunch + Dongdaemun #583 shopping)
  • Recommended: Night Mania = Jongno 3-ga #1, vlog = Plaza, Authentic Korean Food = Namdaemun

Foreign Mania 5 Tips

Tip 1. Jongno 3-ga Station Lines 1, 3, and 5 = Base Camp

  • Exit 5: Main Pocha Street starting point + Ikseon-dong cross
  • Exit 6: Main 200m + Directly connected to Ikseon-dong (No. 1 in vlog)
  • Exit 13: No. 1 well-established gun carriage experience with 20+ years of experience
  • First train in the morning: Check the first train timetable for lines 1, 3, and 5 (usually 5:30 AM~)

Tip 2. Cash payment strategy/bank transfer

  • Cash 30,000~50,000 won recommended: Covers 1 store per person
  • Bank transfer: OK if you have a Korean account (not recommended for foreigners — currency exchange required)
  • Card: Almost never — Only some large stores accept it.
  • ATM: Many near exits 1, 5, 6, and 13 of Jongno 3-ga Station (KB, Shinhan, Woori)

Tip 3. Menu ordering strategy (beginner → enthusiast)

  • Introductory (1-2 visits): Snacks, soup (#8) — Tteokbokki, fried food, udon (cost-effectiveness + safety)

  • Soju and beer pairing: Soju and beer mix) = Introduction to Korean office worker culture

Tip 4. Utilize shared seating and social culture

  • Shared seats: Share tent seats with strangers OK (Korean culture)
