The "Seoul's No. 1 pocha hub" line, "18:00–02:00, weekends 03:00–04:00," "cash only," "closed Sun/Mon," menu prices and the "17.5 million visitors a year" statistics block all had no official source. This time we list only values read on 2026-10-08 from the Time Out article, VisitKorea, VisitSeoul and Seoul city pages. The Time Out No. 1 ranking is confirmed from the official article, while pocha prices and hours are left out because the official page says only "varies by store."

🏆 Time Out 2026 — Why It Ranks No. 1

  • The 2026-09-17 Time Out article "Jongno 3-ga: Why this Seoul neighbourhood is the coolest in the world in 2026" — No. 1 in the world for 2026, up from No. 3 in 2021 (official article). We could not open the article body for the 2nd–5th cities, so they are not listed.
  • What the article singles out is the density of a pocha street, Ikseon-dong (익선동) and old-school restaurants around a single station. The old line that rephrased this as "Seoul's No. 1 pocha hub" is not an official expression (VisitKorea says "one of the representative pocha streets").

🍢 Pocha Street — How to Do the Night

  • Take Exit 6 of Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역) toward Nakwon Instrument Arcade (낙원상가) (VisitKorea). The official page says only "varies by store" for hours, closures and prices, so the old "18:00–02:00, weekends 03:00–04:00, cash only, closed Sun/Mon, soju prices" have no basis. Check the menu board on site.
  • The subway runs about 05:30 to midnight, varying by station (VisitSeoul) — for the last train at Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역), check the station signage or an app. We found no official page on drinking or smoking rules on the street, so they are not listed. 1330 (the tourist hotline) is official.

🏘 Ikseon-dong and Songhae-gil — Daytime into Evening

  • Ikseon-dong (익선동) Hanok Street: Exit 4, 123 m, about 801 m, free, hours vary by store (VisitSeoul · VisitKorea). The old "access via Exit 6" is unverified.
  • Songhae-gil (송해길): an honorary road name on Supyo-ro (수표로). Jongno-gu (종로구) extended it for 5 years in May 2021 (press). We could not confirm "240 m, designated 2016."

🏛 Jongmyo — Skip Tuesdays, Reserve on Weekdays

  • Closed Tuesdays, 1,000 won, Exit 8, 220 m (VisitSeoul) — the old "5 minutes from Exit 1" was wrong. Weekdays (Mon, Wed–Fri) allow timed guided entry only (English 10–16, 200 per tour, ticketed after reserving 3 days ahead); weekends and holidays are free viewing (Feb–May and Sep–Oct 09–18, Nov–Jan 09:30–17:30). The Main Hall is under restoration, so access is restricted.

🗺 Two Routes

  • Evening A: Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역) Exit 4 to Ikseon-dong (익선동) (at dusk) → Exit 6 Jongno 3-ga Pocha Street (종로3가 포장마차거리) → Songhae-gil (송해길). Choose shops by review count in a map app.
  • Daytime B: Exit 8 Jongmyo Shrine (종묘) (reserve on weekdays, not Tuesday) → Sewoon Plaza (세운상가) (until 19:00, closed Sundays and holidays) → Gwangjang Market (광장시장) food alley at Jongno 5-ga Station (종로5가역) (until 23:00).

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Walking into Jongmyo on a weekday — timed guided entry, reserve 3 days ahead. Closed Tuesdays.

2. Using old pocha hours and prices — officially "varies by store." Use the menu board on site.

3. Bringing "cash only" — no basis. Payment depends on each shop.

4. Sewoon Plaza on Sunday — closed.

5. Reading Time Out as "Seoul's No. 1 pocha hub" — the article picks the whole neighbourhood (the density of pocha, Ikseon-dong (익선동) and old-school restaurants).

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Why did it become famous? — Time Out 2026 coolest neighbourhood in the world No. 1 (article of 2026-09-17).

2. Where is the pocha street? — Exit 6 of Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역), toward Nakwon Instrument Arcade (낙원상가) (VisitKorea).

3. What time should I go? — No official hours (varies by store). The subway runs until about midnight.

4. What about prices? — Not officially posted. Check the menu board.

5. What to do in the daytime? — Jongmyo Shrine (종묘) (closed Tue, reserve on weekdays) → Sewoon Plaza (세운상가) → Ikseon-dong (익선동).

6. What about English? — 1330. English guided tours at Jongmyo Shrine (종묘) run weekdays 10–16 (VisitSeoul).
