⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "Gamcheon (감천) = just a regular village": It's Busan's Santorini · CNN 2012 · BBC 2014 Enthusiast Matrix · No. 1 Instagram Spot.

2. "Gamcheon (감천) = merely for Instagram": It has a history since 1950s as a refugee settlement from the Korean War (한국전쟁) · Taegeukdo (태극도) Village.

3. "Bosudong (보수동) = just a regular bookstore": Since 1950s, it's Korea's No. 1 Used Bookstore Street · MZ + Vintage Enthusiasts.

4. "Steep alleys = not for foreigners": It's a mix of flat and steep alleys (골목, narrow streets) · Comfortable shoes are recommended.

5. "Hanbok (한복) = only for palaces": Gamcheon (감천) + Hanbok (한복) = No. 1 for Instagram Enthusiasts.
