⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by International Visitors in Yeonhui-dong

1. "Yeonhui-dong (연희동) = Part of Yeonnam (연남)" Perception

A common mistake in international visitor itineraries. Many perceive Yeonhui-dong (연희동) as part of Yeonnam-dong (연남동). In reality, Yeonhui-dong (연희동) (Seodaemun-gu (서대문구)) ≠ Yeonnam-dong (연남동) (Mapo-gu (마포구)) – they are different administrative districts. However, they are connected by the Yeonhui-ro (연희로) walking path.

2. Perceiving the External #156 Yeonhui-dong Guide as a Complete Set

While #156 was the first deep dive into Yeonhui-dong (연희동), it didn't cover many establishments in the cluster. This Phase 2 selection of 8 places supplements that.

3. Skipping Yeonhui-dong's (연희동) Hidden Dining — International Resident Spots

Yeonhui-dong (연희동) is a top area for international residents — their favorite local spots are often missing from external guidebooks. Sareureu (사르르), Sae Raul (새라울), and Pellucid (펠루시드) are some of these hidden gems for international residents.

4. Unaware of Yeonhui-dong's (연희동) Traditional Korean Dining Scene

International visitors often limit their Yeonhui-dong (연희동) itineraries to Western restaurants and cafes. In reality, Yeonhui-dong (연희동) boasts traditional hansik (한식, Korean food) (e.g., Nokwon Ssambap (녹원쌈밥), Geumokdang (금옥당)) which are top hidden gems for Korean food fans.

5. Unaware of the Yeonhui-dong (연희동) → Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길) → Yeonnam (연남) Itinerary

From Yeonhui-dong (연희동), you can take a walk along the Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길) and connect to Yeonnam-dong (연남동) cafes and restaurants. A combined Yeonhui-dong (연희동) and Yeonnam-dong (연남동) one-day course is possible.

① Yeonhui Western Hidden Gems (2 Places)

1. Sareureu Yeonnam (사르르 연남)

  • Signature: Yeonhui-ro (연희로)'s strongest hidden Western restaurant. 16 international reviews (signal 80).
  • International Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Abundant international reviews
  • Recommended Menu: Signature Western course, seasonal menu
  • Tip: Yeonhui-ro 1-gil 63. Dinner reservations recommended. Top Phase 2 recommendation.

2. Sae Raul (새라울)

  • Signature: A combination of Yeonhui (연희) cafe and Western dining. 10 international reviews (signal 45.1).
  • Recommended Menu: Signature Western dishes + cafe beverages
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Yeonhui-dong (연희동). Signature combination of Western food and cafe.

② Yeonhui Signature Cafes (2 Places)

3. Pellucid Coffee (펠루시드커피)

  • Signature: Yeonhui (연희) specialty coffee cafe. 10 international reviews (signal 42.9).
  • International Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English store name, specialty concept
  • Recommended Menu: Signature hand-drip coffee, seasonal beverages
  • Tip: Located on Yeonhui-dong (연희동) main street. Recommended for specialty coffee fans.

4. Cafe Ma (카페 마)

  • Signature: Yeonhui (연희) trendy cafe. 10 international reviews (signal 37.1).
  • Recommended Menu: Signature beverages, desserts
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Yeonhui-dong (연희동). Instagram-worthy spot.

③ Yeonhui Brunch & Bakery (1 Place)

5. Grain Seoul (그레인서울)

  • Signature: Yeonhui (연희) brunch hidden gem. 9 international reviews (signal 41.7).
  • International Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — English store name
  • Recommended Menu: Signature brunch, bakery items
  • Tip: Located on Yeonhui-dong (연희동) main street. Weekend brunch peak hours are 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM (10:00-14:00).

④ Yeonhui Traditional Korean Dining (2 Places)

6. Geumokdang Yeonhui (금옥당 연희점)

  • Signature: Yeonhui (연희) traditional hansik (한식, Korean food) hidden gem. A top priority for international Korean food fans.
  • Recommended Menu: Traditional hansik (한식) course, seasonal menu
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Yeonhui-dong (연희동). A favorite among international residents.

7. Nokwon Ssambap (녹원쌈밥 연희점)

  • Signature: Yeonhui (연희) traditional ssambap (쌈밥, a Korean dish where you wrap rice and various side dishes in leafy vegetables). Authentic Korean hansik (한식).
  • Recommended Menu: Ssambap jeongsik (쌈밥 정식, a set meal featuring ssambap), hansik (한식) side dishes
  • Tip: Located on Yeonhui-dong (연희동) main street. Recommended for international visitors new to Korean food.

⑤ Design & Lifestyle (1 Place)

8. Awesome 9 (어썸9)

  • Signature: Yeonhui (연희) hidden vintage clothing store. Vintage & lifestyle.
  • Recommended Category: Vintage clothing, lifestyle goods
  • Tip: Within walking distance of Yeonhui-dong (연희동). Cross-reference with the #202 Hongdae (홍대) Select Shop Guide.
