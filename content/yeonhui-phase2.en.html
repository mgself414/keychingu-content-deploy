⚠ Five major mistakes foreigners make in Yeonhui-dong

1. Recognition of “Yeonhui-dong = part of Yeonnam”

Common mistakes in foreign travel routes. Yeonhui-dong is recognized as part of Yeonnam. In reality, Yeonhui-dong (Seodaemun-gu) ≠ Yeonnam-dong (Mapo-gu) administrative districts are different. However, it is connected to Yeonhui-ro Trail.

2. External #156 Yeonhui-dong Guide = Full set recognition

#156 is the first deep dive in Yeonhui-dong, but many stores are not covered in the cluster. Phase 2 8 lines are reinforced.

3. Yeonhui-dong hidden dining — Foreign resident store area pass

Yeonhui-dong ranks first among foreign residents — stores frequented by foreign residents are not listed in external guidebooks. Sarr, Saraul, Pelusid, etc. are foreign resident hidden gems.

4. Not sure about the authentic Korean food area in Yeonhui-dong

For foreigners, Yeonhui-dong's movement only consists of Western food and cafes. In fact, Yeonhui-dong authentic Korean food (Nokwon Ssambap, Geumokdang, etc.) is the number one Korean food fan hidden.

5. Yeonhui-dong → Gyeongui Line Forest Trail → Yeonnam route unknown

Walk along Gyeongui Line Forest Trail in Yeonhui-dong → Connect to cafes and restaurants in Yeonnam-dong. Yeonhui-dong·Yeonnam-dong bundled one-day course available.
