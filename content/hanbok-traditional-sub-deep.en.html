📍 Hanbok Exhibitions and Experiences — Korea Hanbok Promotion Center (Sangju) and Gangneung Hanbok Culture Creation Center

The Korea Hanbok Promotion Center (한국한복진흥원) (1591 Muun-ro (무운로), Hamchang-eup (함창읍), Sangju-si, ☎ 054-541-9524) is a public cultural facility for hanbok that opened in April 2021. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is the first public cultural facility related to hanbok in Korea. It is located inside the Hamchang Silk Theme Park (함창명주테마파크). The main hall on the 1st floor of the exhibition hall has a sculpture that captures the colors of hanbok, and at the Hanbok Experience Hall (한복 체험관) you can experience traditional hanbok and everyday wear. The Traditional Costume Story Hall (전통복식 이야기관) shows traditional costume from the past to the modern era, and the Video Hall (영상관) screens Korean Wave films and dramas centered on hanbok.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~17:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

The Gangneung Hanbok Culture Creation Center (강릉한복문화창작소) (9 Dongbu Market 3-gil (동부시장3길), Gangneung-si, ☎ 033-642-2871) has served as a regional hanbok culture creation center since 2023. It is hosted by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism (문화체육관광부), organized by the Korea Craft & Design Foundation (한국공예디자인문화진흥원) and Gangneung City, and carried out by the Gangneung Cultural Center (강릉문화원). The main exhibition room on the 1st floor holds hanbok exhibitions, and the conference room is sometimes used as an exhibition space.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Every Saturday and Sunday, legal holidays.
  • Fee: Free.

📍 Hanbok Markets and Streets — Gwangjang Market and Dongdaemun Market (Seoul), Bupyeong-dong Hanbok Street (Busan)

The Gwangjang Market Hanbok Shops (광장시장 한복매장) (88 Changgyeonggung-ro (창경궁로), Jongno-gu, ☎ Gwangjang Market Merchants Federation (광장시장 상인총연합회) 02-2272-0967) are the hanbok shops inside Gwangjang Market (광장시장), which opened in 1904. From custom-made hanbok to everyday hanbok, hanbok come in many designs and colors, and custom orders are also made. Hanbok and silk fabric are sold.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Varies by shop. Closed days: Open year-round. Varies by shop.
  • Parking: Available. Credit cards: Accepted. Restrooms: Available.

The Dongdaemun Market Hanbok Stores (동대문종합시장 한복상가) (266 Jong-ro (종로), Jongno-gu, ☎ 02-2262-0114) are the hanbok stores inside Dongdaemun Market (Dongdaemun Jonghap Sijang / 동대문종합시장), which opened on December 23, 1970. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, Dongdaemun Market is the largest single market in the East, and its hanbok stores are also the largest in Korea. Fabric, everyday hanbok, accessories and sewing supplies are sold in one place. Dongdaemun Market consists of 3 buildings and 1 Dongdaemun Shopping Town (동대문 쇼핑타운) building.

  • Opening hours: 08:00~18:00. Varies by store. Closed days: Every Sunday. Wedding goods stores open on Sundays at their own discretion.
  • Credit cards: Accepted. Restrooms: Available.

The Bupyeong-dong Hanbok Street (부평동한복거리) (44 Bupyeong 2-gil (부평2길), Jung-gu, Busan, ☎ Busan Jung-gu Office Jobs and Economy Division (부산 중구청 일자리경제과) 051-600-4471) is two alleys that start from the hanbok street landmark across from Gukje Market (국제시장). During the Korean War (6·25), refugee women sewed hanbok at home to make a living, and once Gukje Market opened, they bought fabric at the fabric shops and sewed it by hand to sell, which is how it began. Hanbok shops grew in number from the 1960s on, and a hanbok landmark was put up in 1997. Today there are about 100 hanbok specialty shops.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop.
  • Parking: Available. Restrooms: Available.

📍 Hanbok Event — 2026 Hanbok Culture Week

The 2026 Hanbok Culture Week (2026 한복문화주간) (137 Seobinggo-ro (서빙고로), Yongsan-gu, the Open Plaza (열린마당) of the National Museum of Korea (국립중앙박물관) and others) is a nationwide hanbok event held every year in the third week of October, centered on Hanbok Day (한복의 날), October 21. In 2026, for the ninth time, it was held October 19~25 at more than 410 places nationwide, including the Open Plaza of the National Museum of Korea. It was hosted by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and organized by the Korea Craft & Design Foundation. Hours were 10:00~18:00, admission was free, and visitors of all ages could attend.

  • Programs (including related programs): A commemorative ceremony at the Open Plaza of the National Museum of Korea on October 21, 17:00~18:30 (a commemorative performance and a hanbok fashion show), the Hanbok Life UNESCO Academic Symposium (한복생활 유네스코 학술 심포지엄) at the Gangneung Cultural Center on October 16, the Book Festival X Hanbok Culture Week (북 페스티벌 X 한복문화주간) around the Music Plaza (음악광장) of the Seoul Arts Center (예술의전당) on October 16~18 and more were held.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Gangneung Hanbok Culture Creation Center is closed on weekends — it is closed every Saturday and Sunday and on legal holidays, and opens 09:00~18:00.
  2. Dongdaemun Market Hanbok Stores are closed on Sundays — only the wedding goods stores open on Sundays at their own discretion.
  3. 2026 Hanbok Culture Week has ended — it was held October 19~25. It is held in the third week of October every year.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where can I experience hanbok?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, you can experience traditional hanbok and everyday wear at the Hanbok Experience Hall (한복 체험관) of the Korea Hanbok Promotion Center (free admission, 10:00~17:00, open year-round).

Q. Where can I buy or custom-order hanbok?

A. They are the Gwangjang Market Hanbok Shops in Seoul (custom orders available), the Dongdaemun Market Hanbok Stores (fabric, everyday hanbok, sewing supplies) and Busan's Bupyeong-dong Hanbok Street (about 100 hanbok specialty shops).
