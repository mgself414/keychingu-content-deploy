Hangang Parks Guide: 9 Hangang Parks, Nanji, Mangwon and Ichon on the North Bank, Gangseo, Yanghwa, Banpo, Jamwon, Jamsil and Gwangnaru on the South Bank

Subtitle: All 9 Hangang parks always open, open year-round · Banpo Hangang Park (Moonlight Rainbow Fountain, 669 parking spaces) · Jamwon Hangang Park (722 parking spaces) · Nanji Hangang Park (riverside water play area, maximum depth 80㎝) · Gwangnaru Hangang Park (water source protection zone) · Yanghwa Hangang Park (roses from May) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers the 9 Hangang parks (한강공원) along the Han River in Seoul, split into 3 on the north bank (Nanji, Mangwon, Ichon) and 6 on the south bank (Gangseo, Yanghwa, Banpo, Jamwon, Jamsil, Gwangnaru). All 9 are registered as always open and open year-round, and no fee is registered for any of them. Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도한강공원) is in guide #705, and Ttukseom Hangang Park (뚝섬한강공원) is in guide #709.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 North of the River — Nanji, Mangwon and Ichon Hangang Parks

Nanji Hangang Park (난지한강공원) (162 Hangang Nanji-ro (한강난지로), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ 02-3780-0611~3) lies between Hongjecheon Bridge (홍제천교) and Nanjicheon Bridge (난지천교). It has the Central Connection Bridge (중앙연결브릿지), the Peace Park Connection Bridge (평화의 공원 연결브릿지) and a combined connection passage, which link to Noeul Park (노을공원) and Haneul Park (하늘공원). The Mirror Fountain (거울분수) in front of the Peace Park Connection Bridge is the landmark of this park. In summer you can use the riverside water play area (강변물놀이장), which has a maximum depth of 80㎝, and from the ecological wetland garden pedestrian bridge (생태습지원 보행연결브릿지) you can see animals that live near the water. At the nearby Nanji Campground (난지캠핑장) you can have a barbecue and camp. The bike path leads east to Ttukseom Park (뚝섬공원) and west to the south end of Haengju Bridge (행주대교) in Goyang-si (고양시) and to Noeul Park and Haneul Park.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available (618 spaces).

Mangwon Hangang Park (망원한강공원) (467 Mapo-naru-gil (마포나루길), Mapo-gu, ☎ 02-3780-0601) is on the north side of the river between Wonhyo Bridge (원효대교) and Seongsan Bridge (성산대교). The riverside has lawns, walking paths, exercise facilities and a bike path, and on the water people enjoy water leisure such as windsurfing, water skiing and motorboats. Nearby are historic sites such as Mangwonjeong Pavilion (망원정) and Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine (절두산 성지).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Ichon Hangang Park (이촌한강공원) (62 Ichon-ro 72-gil (이촌로72길), Yongsan-gu (용산구), ☎ 02-3780-0551~4) is on the north side of the river between Jungnangcheon Bridge (중랑천교) in Geumho-dong (금호동) and Wonhyo Bridge in Ichon-dong (이촌동). In autumn, reeds, silvergrass and cosmos bloom along the embankment. It has a youth plaza (청소년광장), an X-Games park (X-게임장), an international-scale in-line skating rink, a basketball court, tennis courts and a gateball court, and the Han River crossing experience ground (한강도하체험장) is used as a group teamwork training ground.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available (371 spaces).

📍 South Bank, West — Gangseo and Yanghwa Hangang Parks

Gangseo Hangang Park (강서한강공원) (279-23 Yangcheon-ro 27-gil (양천로27길), Gangseo-gu (강서구), ☎ 02-3780-0621~3) runs from Gayang Bridge (가양대교) to the Seoul-Gimpo city boundary. It combines a wetland ecological park and a sports park, and was selected as one of the 30 ecotourism sites of Seoul. Gangseo Wetland Ecological Park (강서습지생태공원) has restored a river islet (하중도), a nature observation trail and a wet meadow. There is fitness equipment under Banghwa Bridge (방화대교), in front of the family picnic area (가족피크닉장) and under Haengju Bridge (행주대교).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Yanghwa Hangang Park (양화한강공원) (221 Nodeul-ro (노들로), Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구), ☎ 02-3780-0581~3) is on the south side of the river from the Yeouido Saetgang (여의도 샛강) estuary to Gayang Bridge (가양대교). The riverside has a wide lawn, and a bike path runs from the Saetgang estuary to Gayang Bridge. Beside the bike path under Seonyu Bridge (선유교), roses bloom from May.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available (501 spaces). Strollers: Allowed.

📍 South Bank, Central and East — Banpo, Jamwon, Jamsil and Gwangnaru Hangang Parks

Banpo Hangang Park (반포한강공원) (40 Sinbanpo-ro 11-gil (신반포로11길), Seocho-gu (서초구), ☎ 02-3780-0541~4) is on the south side of the river between Hannam Bridge (한남대교) and Dongjak Bridge (동작대교), centered on Banpo Bridge (반포대교), centered on Banpo Bridge (반포대교) and Jamsu Bridge (잠수교). The Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (달빛무지개분수), installed on both sides of Banpo Bridge, is 1,140m long in total, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction it entered the Guinness World Records in 2008 as the longest bridge fountain in the world. At night it sprays water together with about 200 lights. From the observation decks Noeul Cafe (노을카페) and Cloud Cafe (구름카페) at the south end of Dongjak Bridge you can view the Han River. On Seoraeseom Island (서래섬), an artificial island connected to the park, canola and buckwheat are planted each season, and in spring the 'Seoraeseom Butterfly and Canola Flower Festival (서래섬 나비·유채꽃 축제)' is held. There are also a water-drop playground (물방울놀이터), a dedicated in-line track, a soccer field, a basketball court, a boat dock and a picnic area.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available (669 spaces).

Jamwon Hangang Park (잠원한강공원) (221-124 Jamwon-ro (잠원로), Seocho-gu, ☎ 02-3780-0531~3) is on the south side of the river from Yeongdong Bridge (영동대교) past Dongho Bridge (동호대교) and Hannam Bridge to Banpo Bridge, and is 5.4㎞ long. It has an athletics field, a soccer field, a volleyball court, a swimming pool, tennis courts, a fitness area and a riverside bike path. It has a silkworm learning center (누에체험학습장) and a nature ecology learning garden (자연생태학습장), and in summer many people enjoy the outdoor swimming pool, windsurfing and motorboats.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available (722 spaces).

Jamsil Hangang Park (잠실한강공원) (65 Hangaram-ro (한가람로), Songpa-gu (송파구), ☎ Future Hangang Headquarters (미래한강본부) Jamsil Information Center (잠실안내센터) 02-3780-0511~4) is on the south side of the river between Jamsil Railway Bridge (잠실철교) and Yeongdong Bridge. It is close to Jamsil Sports Complex (잠실종합운동장), Lotte World (롯데월드) and Olympic Park (올림픽공원). It has a nature learning garden (자연학습장) where flowers, wildflowers and crops are grown, and ecology programs run at the fishway of the Jamsil submerged weir (잠실 수중보).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Gwangnaru Hangang Park (광나루한강공원) (83-106 Seonsa-ro (선사로), Gangdong-gu (강동구), ☎ 02-3780-0501~4) is between Gangdong Bridge (강동대교) and Jamsil Railway Bridge (잠실철교). It has a sandbar formed by soil carried down from upstream and a large reed bed. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is the only water source protection zone in Seoul, so boating and water and leisure activities are prohibited. It has a reed field of 2㎞ along the riverside, and migratory birds live there. Nearby are historic sites such as Amsa-dong Prehistoric Settlement Site (암사동 선사주거지).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available (389 spaces).

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. You cannot do boating or water and leisure activities at Gwangnaru Hangang Park — it is a water source protection zone, so they are prohibited.
  2. Mangwon and Jamwon are the places with registered windsurfing, water skiing and motorboats — Mangwon has windsurfing, water skiing and motorboats, and Jamwon has windsurfing and motorboats in summer.
  3. The Nanji riverside water play area is a summer facility — its maximum depth is 80㎝. At Jamwon, many people use the outdoor swimming pool in summer.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which Hangang park lets you camp or have a barbecue?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, you can have a barbecue and camp at Nanji Campground (난지캠핑장), near Nanji Hangang Park.

Q. Which Hangang parks have registered flowering seasons?

A. At Yanghwa Hangang Park roses bloom under Seonyu Bridge from May, at Seoraeseom Island in Banpo Hangang Park canola flowers bloom in spring, and at Ichon Hangang Park reeds, silvergrass and cosmos bloom in autumn.
