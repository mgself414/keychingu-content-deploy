Jamsil Fares and Opening Hours Sheet: Lotte World Adventure, Seoul Sky, Aquarium, Olympic Park and 3 More (Checked October 2026)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

There is a moment at the ticket window when you have your wallet out and cannot say which age bracket your child falls into, or what time ticket sales stop, and you get pushed to the back of the line. Around Jamsil Station (잠실역) there are three paid attractions (Lotte World Adventure (롯데월드 어드벤처), Seoul Sky (서울스카이), Lotte World Aquarium (롯데월드 아쿠아리움)) and four free ones (Seokchon Lake (석촌호수), Olympic Park (올림픽공원), Hanseong Baekje Museum (한성백제박물관), National Sports Museum (국립스포츠박물관)). We gathered their fares, age brackets, opening hours, closing days and booking rules as read from each official site on 2026-10-22. A full one-day route is in #82 Jamsil Full One-Day Plan. Opening hours change by date, so read the times below as "the value on the date we checked."

  • Magic Pass vs. Park Pass: The Magic Pass covers the Adventure rides plus the Folk Museum (민속박물관) (last entry 18:00); the Park Pass covers the Adventure rides only. Paid facilities such as game arcades are excluded from both (Lotte World official site)
  • After4: Entry from 4 p.m. onward (Lotte World official site)
  • Babies: Under 12 months, park entry and the toddler rides are free. For 12~under 36 months, park entry is free (groups excluded), but you need a Baby ticket to ride the toddler rides (Lotte World official site)

  • Entry conditions: A screenshot on your phone is not accepted; bring the physical ticket. For toddler fares, show ID or documents that confirm age (Lotte World official site). Foreign visitors should bring their passport
  • Closures: Each attraction has regular closure days and daily inspection days. Check the date-by-date closure calendar on the official site in advance

🌳 Olympic Park and 2 Museums: Free Facilities

  • Olympic Park (올림픽공원): 424 Olympic-ro (올림픽로), Songpa-gu (송파구), Seoul (VisitKorea). Open year-round, free admission. On foot or by bicycle 05:00~22:00 (plaza area until 24:00), by vehicle 06:00~22:00. After 22:00, entry deeper into the park is prohibited. Olympic Park Station (올림픽공원역) on Subway Lines 5 and 9, Exits 3 and 4; Mongchontoseong Station (몽촌토성역) on Line 8, Exit 1; Hanseong Baekje Station (한성백제역) on Line 9, Exit 2. Main phone 02-410-1114 (Olympic Park official site). Some zones are restricted due to construction on the swimming pool, KSPO DOME and other sites (Olympic Park official site notice)
  • Olympic Park four-wheel bicycles: Peak season (March~November) weekdays (Tue~Fri) 10:00~18:00, weekends and public holidays 10:00~19:00. In winter (December~February) not operated on weekdays; weekends and public holidays 10:00~17:00. 3-seater, 30 minutes: ₩9,000 (~$7) on weekdays, ₩13,000 (~$10) on weekends. Drivers must be age 20 or over; pay on site at the kiosk next to the rental station (Olympic Park official site)
  • Hanseong Baekje Museum (한성백제박물관): 71 Wiryeseong-daero (위례성대로), Songpa-gu (송파구), Seoul. Regular hours 09:00~18:00; during the extended night-opening period (9.15~11.30), open until 21:00 on Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat and Sun. Admission free. Closed every Monday and January 1 (open if Monday is a public holiday). About 341m from Hanseong Baekje Station (한성백제역), Line 9, Exit 2. Phone 02-2152-5800 (Hanseong Baekje Museum official site)
  • National Sports Museum (국립스포츠박물관): Opened October 15, 2026 near West Gate 1 of Olympic Park (올림픽공원), Mongchontoseong Station (몽촌토성역) on Line 8, Exit 1 (Olympic Park official site). 09:30~17:30, last entry 30 minutes before closing. Admission free. Closed every Monday, January 1, and on the day of Seollal (Lunar New Year) and Chuseok (Korean harvest festival) (National Sports Museum official site)

🧰 Practical Notes for Jamsil Facilities: Solo, Booking, Payment, Ordering, Getting Around

  • Solo: Some Lotte World Adventure (롯데월드 어드벤처) attractions have a dedicated single-rider queue; ask a staff member (Lotte World official site)
  • Booking: Regular Seoul Sky (서울스카이) tickets can be bought online, on mobile, or at the on-site ticket counter. Sky Bridge Tour booking opens 1 month ahead (Seoul Sky official site). We did not find a foreigner-only discount on the official sites of the three paid facilities
  • Payment: For cards and mobile payments, see #527 Foreigners' Korea Payment Systems Deep Dive
  • Ordering: A package ticket must be used at both places on the same day. Once one is used, partial refunds are not possible (Seoul Sky and Aquarium official sites); buy it only if you will visit both in one day
  • Getting around: Lotte World, Seoul Sky and the Aquarium are at Jamsil Station (잠실역) (Lines 2 and 8); Olympic Park and the two museums are at Olympic Park Station (올림픽공원역), Mongchontoseong Station (몽촌토성역) and Hanseong Baekje Station (한성백제역). For transit cards see #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards; with children see #533 Traveling in Korea with Children

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "I'll get in with a screenshot of my ticket": Neither Lotte World Adventure (롯데월드 어드벤처) nor Seoul Sky (서울스카이) accepts screenshots (each official site). Lotte World asks you to bring the physical ticket, while Seoul Sky states that web tickets and paper tickets have the same validity

2. "I can arrive right before closing": Seoul Sky (서울스카이) and the Aquarium (롯데월드 아쿠아리움) end ticket sales and entry 1 hour before closing (each official site)

3. "The teen age is the same everywhere": Lotte World Adventure (롯데월드 어드벤처) charges a separate teen fare for ages 13~18, but at Seoul Sky (서울스카이) and the Aquarium (롯데월드 아쿠아리움) people age 13 and over pay the adult fare (each official site)

4. "Museums are open every day": Both Hanseong Baekje Museum (한성백제박물관) and National Sports Museum (국립스포츠박물관) are closed on Mondays (each official site)
