⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Challenge only spicy taste" — Unrecognized value of fermentation and internal organs: Korean challenge food = Spicy taste and Fermentation (skate), internal organs (offal, beef), texture (live octopus, pupa) 3 axes. If you try spicy food, you will only experience half of the deep dive in Korean cuisine.

2. "Sweet octopus = dangerous": Sweet octopus is an authentic Korean fresh dish — Freshly cut and paired with sesame oil and salt. Don't swallow it whole. Chew it well. Freshness ranks first (only when cut directly at Noryangjin Fish Market).

3. "Skate = Inedible" — No. 1 Korean gourmet food : Skate (authentic from Jeollanam-do, No. 1 Heuksando) is the highest fermented food in Korean gastronomy — One of the top three fermented foods in the world, along with Japanese funazushi and Swedish surströmming. Samhap (skate + aged kimchi + braised pork) is the number one pairing.

① Live Octopus ★★★★★ Highest challenge difficulty

  • Signature Store: Noryangjin Fish Market·Sinnamdo Fisheries (Foreigner vlog #1)
  • Price: 30,000~50,000 won (1 animal, cut on the spot)
  • How to eat: Freshly cut, dipped in sesame oil and salt → Chew well (swallow whole)
  • Foreign demand: BBC, Anthony Bourdain, foreign vlogger regulars
  • Why: No. 1 freshness in Korea + No. 1 challenge food in K-vlog. Noryangjin Fish Market is a foreign vlog #1 hotspot.

② Beondegi ★★★★ Texture Challenge

  • Signature: Myeongdong/Dongdaemun Street + Canned Food (Convenience Store)
  • Price: Street food 3,000 won/cup / Canned food 1,500 won
  • How to eat: Native flavor + salty + rich in protein
  • Foreign demand: K-drama and mukbang regular (#1 foreign challenge food)
  • Why: Silkworm pupa = Protein supplement food in Korea in the 1960s and 1970s. No. 1 in flavor and texture challenge.

③ Hongeo ★★★★★ Strongest fermentation

  • Signature: Gwangjang Market, Jongno long-established store, Jeolla-do restaurant
  • Price: 30,000~80,000 won per plate
  • How to eat: Samhap (skate + old kimchi + braised pork) + Makgeolli pairing
  • Foreign demand: The world's top three fermented foods (together with Japanese funazushi and Swedish surströmming)
  • Why: Authentic Heuksando, Jeollanam-do + the highest level of Korean gastronomy. No. 1 Korean food deep dive for foreigners.

④ Tripe, Makchang, Lamb (Beef Intestines) ★★★ Authentic

  • Signature: Sinchon Ox Tripe (#171 Sinchon/Ewha Womans University Guide)·Sinseol-dong Busan Lamb Tripe (#172 DDP/Dongdaemun Night)·Wangsimni Majang Livestock Products Market (#189 Seongdong/Wangsimni)
  • Price: KRW 25,000~50,000/person
  • How to eat: Grilled + salt and oil sauce + perilla leaf and lettuce wrap + soju pairing
  • Foreign demand: No. 1 in Korean K-BBQ transformation challenge
  • Why: Authentic Korean offal BBQ + No. 1 foreign challenge food.

⑤ Chicken feet (Dakbal) ★★★★ Spicy challenge

  • Signature: 119 Chicken Feet Cheonho (#183 Gangdong·Cheonho)·Alley Chicken Feet Street
  • Price: 18,000~28,000 won/person
  • How to eat: Spicy chicken feet + added noodles + makgeolli/soju pairing
  • Foreign demand: Korean spicy food + texture (bone) combination challenge
  • Why: Korean Spicy Food + Texture Challenge No. 1 — 119 is an “emergency” joke brand.

⑥ Golbaengi Spicy Snails ★★★

  • Signature: Euljiro Golbaengi Street (#11 Euljiro Retro + #181 Dongjak Sadang Station Pocha)
  • Price: 22,000~35,000 won/person
  • How to eat: Spicy seasoned salad + somen + perilla leaves + soju pairing
  • Foreigner demand: Korean bar regular snack + challenge food
  • Why: Korean-style spicy + texture (snail) challenging snack.

⑦ Haejangguk (Hangover Soup) ★★

  • Signature: Cheongjin-dong Haejangguk Street (Jongno) + Yangpyeong Haejangguk
  • Price: KRW 10,000~18,000/person
  • How to eat: Open 24 hours + Bone Haejangguk, Bean Sprout Haejangguk + Soju Haejangguk
  • Foreign demand: Korean drinking culture + recovery food
  • Why: Korea’s No. 1 recovery food. Open 24 hours + 1st place for foreigners after drinking.

⑧ Sophie·Pig Blood (Blood Soup) ★★★★ Visual challenge

  • Signature: Jongno old restaurant and hangover soup store
  • Price: 8,000~15,000 won/person
  • How to eat: Seonjiguk·Sundae + Soju pairing
  • Foreign demand: Korean authentic + visual challenge
  • Why: Authentic long-established Korean food from the 1960s and 1970s — visual challenge + rich in nutrition.

⑨ Mussel Soup ★

  • Signature: A staple snack for all Korean hops
  • Price: 8,000~15,000 won (free sides at most pubs)
  • How to eat: Free side at a pub + spicy soup
  • Foreigner demand: Korean bar culture + signature free snacks
  • Why: Korean pub = free mussel soup. 1st place for foreigners in Korean Hope Deep Dive.

⑩ Pork Soup Rice ★★ Authentic Busan style

  • Signature: Busan-style restaurant (Many Busan restaurants and pork soup stores in Seoul)
  • Price: 8,000~12,000 won/person
  • How to eat: White soup + rice + seasoned chives, salted shrimp, and rice cake, self-serve
  • Foreign demand: K-drama “Reply 1988”·“Cheese in the Trap” regular stage
  • Why: Korean cost-effectiveness + authentic Busan cuisine + K-drama stage.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. What is the safest store for foreign challenge food?: Noryangjin Fish Market (live octopus), Sinchon Hwangso Gopchang (offal), 119 Chicken Feet (chicken feet), Cheongjin-dong Haejangguk (haejangguk).

2. Can you choose the level of spiciness?: OK at most stores (mild, medium, spicy). For foreigners, it is recommended to start with a mild flavor.

3. K-vlogger’s favorite store?: Noryangjin Fish Market (BBC·Anthony Bourdain) · Gwangjang Market Bindaetteok · Skate + Myeongdong Chrysalis Street.

4. Allergy/Halal/Vegan challenge food?: Refer to #69 Vegan/Halal guide. Challenge foods mostly animal-based.

5. K-drama regular stage?: Noryangjin Fish Market ("Itaewon Class") · Cheongjin-dong hangover soup ("Goblin") · Gopchang ("Misaeng") · Pork soup ("Reply 1988").

6. Foreigner Instagram/vlog hotspot?: Noryangjin Fish Market (#181 Dongjak·Noryangjin) #1 + Gwangjang Market (#24·#58) #2 + Myeongdong Street #3.
