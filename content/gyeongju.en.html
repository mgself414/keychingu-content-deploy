🎯 Why a full guide to Gyeongju?

⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Gyeongju = 1 night and 2 days OK": Gyeongju foreign enthusiasts = 2 nights and 3 days ~ 3 nights and 4 days full set + Bulguksa Temple, Seokguram Grotto, Cheomseongdae, Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond, Yangdong Village + Outskirts of Andong and Pohang.

2. "Bulguksa Temple = Same as Seokguram Grotto": Bulguksa Temple = Silla 751 No. 1 temple in Korea (Seokgatap·Dabotap UNESCO) + Seokguram Grotto = Cave temple 4km east of Bulguksa.

3. "Cheomseongdae = Simple Tower": Cheomseongdae = Silla 632 No. 1 astronomical observatory in the world (currently the highest in East Asia) + No. 1 foreign enthusiasts.

4. "Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond = Daytime Only": Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (Anapji Pond) = Night view of Silla No. 1 (7-10 p.m.) + Foreigner vlog No. 1.

5. "Yangdong Village = Hanok everywhere": Yangdong Village = UNESCO 600 Years Hanok Village (2010 with Hahoe) + No. 1 foreign enthusiasts.
