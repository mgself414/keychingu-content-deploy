Gyeongju Full Guide — Korea's Top Historic City for International Enthusiasts

🎯 Why the Gyeongju Full Guide?

Gyeongju (경주) = Silla's (신라) 1,000-year capital city (BC 57–AD 935) + Korea's top UNESCO city (Bulguksa Temple (불국사) & Seokguram Grotto (석굴암) 1995, Gyeongju Historic Areas (경주 역사 유적) 2000, Yangdong Historic Village (양동 한옥) 2010). The number one destination for international Korean history enthusiasts. Just 2 hours by KTX to Singyeongju Station (신경주역).

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by International Visitors

1. "Gyeongju (경주) = 1 night 2 days OK": For international Gyeongju (경주) enthusiasts = 2 nights 3 days to 3 nights 4 days full set + Bulguksa Temple (불국사), Seokguram Grotto (석굴암), Cheomseongdae (첨성대), Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (동궁과월지), Yangdong Folk Village (양동마을) + outskirts Andong (안동) and Pohang (포항).

2. "Bulguksa Temple (불국사) = Same as Seokguram Grotto (석굴암)": Bulguksa Temple (불국사) = Silla 751, Korea's number one sachal (사찰, temple) (Sakka-tap (석가탑) & Dabotap (다보탑) UNESCO) + Seokguram Grotto (석굴암) = a cave temple 4km east of Bulguksa Temple (불국사).

3. "Cheomseongdae (첨성대) = Just a tower": Cheomseongdae (첨성대) = Silla 632, the world's first observatory (the oldest existing in East Asia) + Top destination for international enthusiasts.

4. "Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (동궁과월지) = Only for daytime": Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond (동궁과월지) (Anapji (안압지)) = Silla's number one yagyeong (야경, night view) (19:00–22:00 / 7:00 PM–10:00 PM) + Top spot for international vlogs.

5. "Yangdong Folk Village (양동마을) = Hanok (한옥) everywhere": Yangdong Folk Village (양동마을) = UNESCO 600-year-old hanok (traditional Korean house) village (along with Hahoe (하회) in 2010) + Top destination for international enthusiasts.
