⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Nowon = only hiking" — #177 pattern of returning to hotel immediately after hiking

The pattern of climbing the three famous mountains covered in #177, eating only dakgalbi and cold noodles at a restaurant near Nowon Station, and then returning to the city (Myeongdong, Hongdae). In reality, lifestyle stores in Nowon, Junggye, and Gongneung (Cubang Plus, Pasta Hanki, God It, etc.) have stronger potential for recognition by foreigners than mountain climbing. We recommend completing the Nowon Deep Dive as a hiking + lifestyle one-night course.

2. "Bedtown = Chain heaven, no signature" — Starbucks/Paris Baguette Pass

The most common mistakes foreigners make when visiting Nowon for the first time. The area around Nowon, Junggye, and Gongneung is recognized as mainly chains such as Starbucks (8,279 rv), Paris Baguette (5,266 rv), and Mega Coffee (5,038 rv). In fact, 60+ pool of local signature stores (Cubang Plus 8,324 rv 4.89★, Pasta Meal 4,936 rv, God It 4,897 rv, etc.) have higher reviews and ratings than chains. Bedtown ≠ No signature.

3. "Nowon Cafe = Simple neighborhood cafe" — Specialty/Roasters Full Miji

I don't know if there is a specialty coffee/roasters store in Nowon. In reality, the specialty/roasters cluster is strong, including Blue Miles Coffee Roasters (2,462 rv, covered in #177) + Hoipoloi Coffee Roasters (1,852 rv) + Cubang Plus (8,324 rv 4.89★). Cafe hopping after hiking or on weekday afternoons is the signature route of the full cafe experience in Korea’s bed towns.

Lifestyle scenery of Nowon, Junggye, and Gongneung

Nowon-gu is a bed town in northeastern Seoul with a population of 500,000 and an area of 35.4㎢. Sanggye, Junggye, Gongneung, Hagye, and Wolgye The five districts are connected to five lines (Line 4, Line 7, Uisinseol Line, Line 1, and Gyeongchun Line) and can be accessed from anywhere in the city within 50 minutes.

Nowon Station (Lines 4 and 7) area is the commercial and transportation center of the northeastern part of Nowon. Foreigner review 2,000~5,000+ stores such as Gubeopwon Croquette, Pasta Hanki, Godit, Daham Dakgalbi, Sangmu Sushi, and Bomibori Rice are concentrated along the streets of Sanggye-ro and Dongil-ro. No. 1 base for foreigners visiting Nowon for the first time.

The Junggye Station (Line 7) area is the depth of Nowon’s lifestyle. Bakeries, Korean food, and buffets such as Sinuiju Glutinous Rice Sundae, Junggye Meat Party, Ashley Queens, Bon Noel, and Metropolis are distributed in the alleys of Dongil-ro 204-gil, Junggye-ro, and Hangeulbiseok-ro. #1 local + lifestyle deep dive.

The area around Gongneung Station (Line 7, Gyeongchun Line) is the home of Nowon Specialty Coffee. Specialty roasters clusters such as Blue Miles Coffee Roasters (#177), Kota Bread (#177), Hoi Poloi Coffee Roasters, and Cubang Plus (Nowon-ro 26-gil) are formed at 190 Dongil-ro and Gongneung-ro. No. 1 cafe hopping.
