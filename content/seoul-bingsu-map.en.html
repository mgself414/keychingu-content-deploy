⚠ 3 Bingsu Mistakes

1. "Bingsu = pat-bingsu only" — not knowing the 2026 global trends: Red-bean only. In reality, 2026 bingsu = Dubai chocolate · ube · yangzhi ganlu (양쯔깐루) · tomato sorbet + reinterpretations of ssuk (쑥) mugwort · pat (팥) red bean · injeolmi (인절미) (a boom era for both global and traditional).

2. "One bingsu per person" — not knowing the sizes: One each. In reality, the average bowl serves 2–3 people + sharing one bowl is the etiquette + hotel bingsu is also ₩130,000 (~$97) for two.

3. "Hotel bingsu = walk right in" — not knowing reservations are required: Walk-in. In reality, Shilla · Signiel · Four Seasons are May–September seasonal only · open run · reservations required + on weekdays, arriving right after the 10:00 opening is recommended.

🍯 5 Tips for Foreign Visitors

1. Share one bowl — the average serving feeds 2–3; one bingsu per person is too much

2. Injeolmi (인절미) is rice cake, pat (팥) is red bean — the two toppings foreigners confuse most

3. Take your photo within 1 minute, before it melts — visuals are the point of trend bingsu

4. Stick to credit cards, Samsung Pay, and Seoul Pay (#431 payment SIM · enable your overseas card)

5. Hotel bingsu requires reservations — Shilla · Signiel · Four Seasons are impossible without the open run
