Seoul Bingsu Guide — Red Bean Shaved Ice, Hanok Tea Houses and Fresh-Fruit Bingsu at 9 Spots (Dongbinggo, Bubing, Suyeon Sanbang, La Fraiche Fruit), Summer Seasonal and Year-Round Shops (October 2026 Official Values)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Push in a spoon and the finely shaved milk ice collapses softly, and house-simmered red beans rise from underneath. Dongbinggo (동빙고) serves patbingsu (팥빙수, red bean shaved ice) piled with beans, milk and rice cake, which you mix together as you eat (VisitKorea). This guide groups the places in Seoul that put bingsu on the menu into three kinds: red bean bingsu specialists, hanok tea house bingsu, and fresh-fruit and matcha bingsu. Every shop is listed with bingsu on the Korea Tourism Organization's tourism data (VisitKorea, TourAPI).

Many places serve bingsu as a summer menu, and flavors and prices change every year. So this guide lists only shop names, addresses and menu items on the official listings. Check this year's menu and prices on the shop's official social media or by phone before you go. We have separated the year-round bingsu specialist (Dongbinggo) from the tea houses that serve bingsu only in summer, so pick by season.

🍧 Red Bean Bingsu Specialists — 2 Places

  • Dongbinggo (동빙고) main branch — 319 Ichon-ro (이촌로), Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seoul, Hyundai Apartments (현대아파트) (near Exit 1 of Seobinggo Station (서빙고역) on the Gyeongui-Jungang Line). 10:30–22:00, open every day. The signature item is patbingsu; the menu also has danpatjuk (단팥죽, sweet red bean porridge), misutgaru patbingsu (미숫가루팥빙수, roasted grain powder red bean bingsu), strawberry bingsu, green tea bingsu and coffee bingsu. The basic original bingsu is topped with just red beans and rice cake, and you can add more rice cake. The shop is small, so they hand out waiting tickets. Takeout, reservations and cards accepted; no parking (use a nearby public lot). Tel. 02-794-7171 (VisitKorea). It is close to the National Museum of Korea and the National Hangeul Museum
  • Bubing (부빙) — 136 Changuimun-ro (창의문로), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul (Buam-dong (부암동)). 13:00–19:30 (last order 19:00), closed Mon and Tue. The name is short for "Buam-dong bingsu shop" (부암동 빙수집). Patbingsu is the signature item; there are also strawberry, caramel and matcha bingsu, and the sweet corn, potato and fig bingsu are described as popular. Every menu item is one serving, and the red beans are grown on contract by the shop itself. No parking. Tel. 02-394-8288 (VisitKorea)

🏠 Hanok Tea House Bingsu — 4 Places

  • Suyeon Sanbang (수연산방) — 8 Seongbuk-ro 26-gil (성북로26길), Seongbuk-gu (성북구), Seoul (Seongbuk-dong (성북동)). Wed–Fri 11:30–17:50 (last order 17:00), Sat 11:30–20:00 (last order 19:10), Sun 11:30–19:50 (last order 19:00), closed Mon and Tue. A traditional tea house run by the granddaughter of Yi Tae-jun (이태준) in the Seoul Folk Cultural Heritage "Sangheo Yi Tae-jun House" (상허 이태준 가옥), and its signature item is sweet pumpkin bingsu. Tel. 02-764-1736 (VisitKorea)
  • Banhadang (반하당) — 36 Seongbuk-ro 5-gil (성북로5길), Seongbuk-gu (성북구), Seoul (Seongbuk-dong 1-ga (성북동1가)). A hanok cafe with its walls opened up, serving red bean bingsu topped with dried jujubes in summer. The jujubes and red beans are described as 100% Korean-grown. Handmade fruit-syrup drinks are also served (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours
  • Yetchatjip (옛찻집) — 33-1 Insadong-gil (인사동길), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul. A traditional tea house with ondol (온돌, heated) floors that simmers the red beans for its bingsu, a summer menu item, itself. Roasted soybean powder ice cream and bokbunja (복분자, Korean black raspberry) ade are also served (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours. For a tea house route through Insa-dong, see Ikseondong·Insadong Hanok Cafes & Traditional Desserts 12 Best
  • Dal Cafe (달 카페) — 94-1 Samcheong-ro (삼청로), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul (Samcheong-dong (삼청동)). A hanok cafe whose signature item is traditional jar bingsu made with coarse ice. The waffles are mixed fresh in the shop every morning (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours

🍑 Fresh-Fruit and Matcha Bingsu — 3 Places

  • La Fraiche Fruit (라프레플루트) — 8-8 Seoulsup 2-gil (서울숲2길), Seongdong-gu (성동구), Seoul (Exit 8 of Ttukseom Station (뚝섬역), near Seongsu Middle School (성수중학교)). A fruit specialist cafe whose signature items are peach bingsu and apple mango bingsu; it makes bingsu from seasonal fruit, not frozen fruit. Pets allowed (VisitKorea). Its official channel is Instagram @rafre_fruit (listed on VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours
  • Forest Seoulsup (포레스트 서울숲) — 20-12 Seoulsup 2-gil (서울숲2길), Seongdong-gu (성동구), Seoul. It has a terrace overlooking Seoul Forest and sells fresh-fruit bingsu made with fresh, not frozen, fruit, plus handmade fresh-fruit mochi (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours
  • Cafe Toda (카페 토다) — 39 Seongchon 6-gil (성촌6길), Seocho-gu (서초구), Seoul (Exit 5 of Yangjae Citizen's Forest Station (양재시민의숲역) on the Shinbundang Line). A cafe whose main menu is bagels and brunch, with matcha bingsu on the menu. It is a Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs "safe restaurant" (안심식당), allows pets, and has paid valet parking (VisitKorea). We could not confirm opening hours

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Price range: None of the 9 places has a price in its VisitKorea listing. Prices are not published — check the shop's menu board or call. For reference, 2026 apple mango bingsu at Seoul's five-star hotels was reported at ₩83,000–₩149,000 (~$62–$112) (Korea Economic Daily 2026-05-01 [secondary]) — check official prices on each hotel's channel
  • Dairy: Dongbinggo's patbingsu has milk poured over the ice (VisitKorea). Many places use milk ice or condensed milk in their bingsu, so ask before ordering if you are lactose intolerant or vegan
  • Vegetarian: Red beans, rice cake and fruit are plant-based, but toppings may include condensed milk, cream or ice cream. Whether animal ingredients can be left out depends on the shop
  • Allergies: Watch for misutgaru and roasted soybean powder (grain, soy) and nut toppings. Dongbinggo's misutgaru patbingsu and Yetchatjip's roasted soybean powder ice cream (VisitKorea)
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (이태원) (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil (우사단로10길), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00–21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF, 한국이슬람교중앙회). Certification can change, so confirm before you visit. None of the shops in this guide carries a halal label
  • For a show-it card, see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Bingsu Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Ordering, Getting There

  • Solo: Every menu item at Bubing (부빙) is one serving, so it is easy to go alone (VisitKorea). At other places, ask how big a bowl is before ordering. For a map of solo-friendly spots, see Seoul Solo Diner Map — 12 Signature Spots for Foreigners' Solo Dining, Bars and Cafes
  • Reservations: The only place listed as accepting reservations is the Dongbinggo (동빙고) main branch. If there is a wait, take a waiting ticket (VisitKorea)
  • Payment: The only place listed as accepting cards is the Dongbinggo (동빙고) main branch (VisitKorea). For cards and mobile payment, see Foreigner Korea Payment System Deep Dive
  • Ordering: Many cafes have you order at the counter and take a buzzer — The Korean Cafe Buzzer: The No-Panic Manual — What to Do After You Order. When you cannot communicate, see Manners When Words Fail — Ordering Etiquette in the Age of Translation Apps and Kiosks
  • Getting there: Dongbinggo is at Exit 1 of Seobinggo Station (서빙고역), La Fraiche Fruit at Exit 8 of Ttukseom Station (뚝섬역), Cafe Toda at Exit 5 of Yangjae Citizen's Forest Station (양재시민의숲역) (VisitKorea). For transit cards, see Choosing Your Korea Transit Card: The No-Panic Manual — T-money, WOWPASS, or Climate Card

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Bingsu is only sold in summer" — The Dongbinggo (동빙고) main branch serves patbingsu all year, open every day. By contrast, Banhadang (반하당) and Yetchatjip (옛찻집) present bingsu as a summer menu (VisitKorea) — in winter there may be no bingsu, so call to check

2. "You share one big bowl of bingsu" — At Bubing (부빙) every menu item is one serving (VisitKorea). It varies by shop

3. "You can eat hotel bingsu any time" — The apple mango bingsu at The Library (더 라이브러리) at The Shilla Seoul (서울신라호텔) went on sale on May 1, 2026, reported at ₩130,000 (~$98) (Korea Economic Daily [secondary]). It is a seasonal item, so check dates, price and reservations on the hotel's official channel

4. "The menu I had last year will be there this year" — Seasonal bingsu changes every year. Go by the shop name, but confirm the menu on official social media or by phone
