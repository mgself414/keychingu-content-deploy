🎯 Why is this an exclusive guide to Dongjak and Noryangjin?

Dongjak and Noryangjin are Dongjak-gu's Korea's No. 1 fish market, gosi village, and youth culture deep dive — Noryangjin Fish Market (opened in 1971, Korea's largest and No. 1 fish market) + Noryangjin Gosichon (Korea's No. 1 gosi academy district, K-drama regular stage) + Noryangjin Cupbap Street + Seoul National Cemetery (Korea's No. 1 veterans' center) + Sadang·Isu (foreign residence/transfer station) compressed into one area. The most frequently mentioned Korean market and youth culture in foreign vlogs and Instagram.

Compared to Hongdae (MZ, indie), Gangnam (luxury), and Itaewon (global), Dongjak and Noryangjin are market, public examination, youth, and veterans. It is passed over by Gangnam and Hongdae in the foreign tourist movement, but Noryangjin Fish Market ranks first in foreign vlogs (BBC, CNN, Japanese NHK foreign media regulars + foreign vlogger Instagram ranks first) + Noryangjin Gosichon is a regular stage for K-drama "Sky Castle", "Itaewon Class", and "Incomplete Life" = No. 1 deep dive into Korean culture for foreigners.

In particular, Noryangjin Fish Market (now newly built in 2016 + old in 1971) is the No. 1 wholesale and retail site for seafood directly sent from Korean producers — Auctions from 03 to 5 a.m., instant raw fish for breakfast, raw fish for lunch = undisputed No. 1 hot spot for foreigners’ Instagram and vlog. Noryangjin Gosichon is a cross-sectional area of ​​Korean society where 500,000+ Korean youth gather every year. ⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Noryangjin Fish Market = just a market" — Dawn auction, instant sashimi value unknown

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. Noryangjin Fish Market is recognized as a simple “market,” but in reality, it is Korea’s No. 1 seafood wholesale and retail market — Auction from 03:00 to 05:00 (foreigners can enter, free) + Instant raw fish and raw fish bowl from 06:00 to 10:00 in the morning + Hot zone for foreign tourists for lunch from 11:00 to 14:00. Foreign vlog·Insta #1 hotspot. A regular stage for foreign media such as BBC, CNN, and NHK. No. 1 Korean seafood deep dive.

2. "Noryangjin Gosichon = Korean youth academy only" — K-drama regular stage value unknown

Noryangjin Gosichon (Noryangjin Academy District) is not just a school district, but a K-drama regular stage — many PPLs for Korean youth and youth dramas, such as “Sky Castle,” “Incomplete Life,” “Itaewon Class,” and “Cheese in the Trap.” Noryangjin Cupbap Street = No. 1 place for Korean youth culture. No. 1 foreign K-drama enthusiast deep dive.

3. "Sadang/Isu = just a transfer station" — Foreign resident/youth area unknown

Sadang Station (Lines 2 and 4) and Isu Station (Lines 4 and 7) are not simple transfer stations, but residence areas for foreign students and office workers + Korean youth bars, pochas, and izakaya hot zones. Sadang Station Pocha, Baeksojeong Isu Station, Chongshin University Youth Street is a deep dive into Korean youth drinking culture + foreigner-friendly. Close to Gangnam + 1st place in cost-effectiveness.

Dongjak·Noryangjin scenery

Dongjak and Noryangjin are located in the center of Dongjak-gu Korea's No. 1 fish market, gosichon, youth, and veterans' combined area — Noryangjin Fish Market opened in 1971 + Noryangjin Academy District emerged in the 1980s + Seoul National Cemetery was established in 1955 + Sadang-Isu transfer station youth area No. 1 deep dive in the cross-section of Korean society.

Noryangjin sub-area is near Noryangjin Station on Lines 1 and 9 — Noryangjin Fish Market (newly built in 2016) + Noryangjin Gosichon (Cupbap Street and Academy Street) + Hangang Bridge and Dongjak Station Han River. 1st place in the cross-section of Korean society.

Sadang·Isu sub-area is Sadang Station on Lines 2 and 4 and Isu Station on Lines 4 and 7 — residence for foreign students and office workers + youth bar, bar, and izakaya hot zone. Close to Gangnam + cost-effective.

Heukseok·Chung-Ang University sub-area is Heukseok Station on Line 9 + Chung-Ang University — Korean comprehensive university + authentic Korean food and Han River view.

Sangdo-dong·Chongshin University sub-area is Sangdo Station/Chongshin University Station on Line 7 — expanded residence for foreigners + cafes and dining.

Dongjak·National Cemetery sub-area is Dongjak Station on Lines 4 and 9 + Seoul National Cemetery — No. 1 place for veterans in Korea + No. 1 spring cherry blossoms.

Transportation: Line 1, Line 9 Noryangjin Station (Noryangjin Fish Market, Gosichon) / Line 2, Line 4 Sadang Station / Line 4, Line 7 Isu Station / Line 9 Heukseok Station, Dongjak Station. Kakao Taxi recommended.

  • Address: 674 Nodeul-ro, Dongjak-gu, Seoul (5-minute walk from Noryangjin Station on Lines 1 and 9)

⚙ FAQ (8)

Q1. What are the entry times for foreigners at Noryangjin Fish Market?

24 hours OK — Auction from 03:00 to 05:00 (free admission for foreigners, 1st place for Korean foreign vlog) / Breakfast from 06 to 10:00 Instant raw fish and raw fish rice bowl / Lunch from 11 to 14:00 Hot zone for foreign tourists. 5-minute walk from Noryangjin Station on Lines 1 and 9.

Q2. Noryangjin Fish Market instant sashimi price?

25,000~45,000 won per person (wholesale price + instant raw fish). Sashimi delivered directly from the production area in the early morning. Price negotiation by stores such as Sinnamdo Fisheries and Son Seonjang is OK. Foreigner price negotiation friendly + English partially OK.

Q3. Where is the Noryangjin Gosi Village K-drama stage?

Noryangjin Station Exit 1 → Dongjak-daero → Academy District 1.5km — A regular stage for Korean youth dramas such as “Sky Castle” (Edu Big Bang), “Misaeng” (youth at work), “Itaewon Class” (youth Park Sae-roi), and “Cheese in the Trap”. Noryangjin Cupbap Street (adjacent to the academy district) = 1st place in Korean youth culture.

Q4. What foreigner-friendly stores are Dongjak and Noryangjin?

Sinnamdo Fisheries, Stairs House, Baeksojeong Isu, Jean Boulangerie, Nodeul Pot Lid English menu + card payment + foreigner friendly standard. Noryangjin Fish Market's instant sashimi menu is mainly in Korean, but price negotiation + clerk friendliness is OK.

Q5. Foreigners admitted to Seoul National Cemetery?

Free + 09:00~18:00 — Free entry for foreigners OK. No. 1 cherry blossoms in spring and April (Foreigner’s Instagram) + Deep dive into Korean veterans’ and war history. 5-minute walk from Dongjak Station on Lines 4 and 9.

Q6. Noryangjin + Yeouido + Sadang route?

Noryangjin (Lines 1 and 9) → Yeouido (Line 9, 1st stop) → Sadang (Transfer to Line 4) → Gangnam (Transfer to Line 2). Noryangjin + Yeouido Han River + Sadang Bar = 1 full day.

Q7. How to eat Korean-style raw fish at Noryangjin Fish Market?

Step 1: Purchase raw fish at the wholesale store on the 1st floor (price negotiation, usually KRW 30,000~50,000/person) → Step 2: Go to the restaurant on the 2nd floor → Step 3: Additional order of spicy fish soup, side dishes, and soju at the restaurant (place value + spicy soup KRW 1,000~10,000/person). Foreigner-friendly guide available.

Q8. Is Noryangjin safe at night?

24-hour safety — Noryangjin Fish Market is open 24 hours a day + Gosichon youth liveliness + CCTV and plainclothes police standards. Foreigner night vlog OK. Kakao Taxi recommended after midnight.
