12-Line 4-Axis Matrix — Dawn Wholesale Full Set

Axis 1: Fisheries Dawn Market (3rd line) — No. 1 foreign vlog

#1. Noryangjin Fish Market — Korea #1 Fishery Wholesale (Central Seoul)

  • 📍 Location: Noryangjin-dong, Dongjak-gu (directly connected to Noryangjin Station on Lines 1 and 9)
  • 🕐 Business (Auction): Shellfish 01:00·High-quality fresh fish 01:30·Popular food 00:00·High-quality live fish 03:00·Frozen 03:00
  • 🚫 Closed: Sunday (retail zone) / wholesale zone every day
  • Signature: Fisheries Auction Dawn (live fish, fresh fish, shellfish, frozen) + Instant cooking (nearby restaurant)
  • Foreigner infrastructure: English information + card OK (retail) + many foreigner vlogs (selected by Trip Advisor·KoreaToDo·zenkimchi)
  • Foreigner tip: Arrive before 5 a.m. = Watch the auction #1. After purchasing at the store, it is cooked at the restaurant next door for 5,000-10,000 won. Noryangjin Station on Lines 1 and 9 = 24h transfer possible.

#2. Garak Market — Seoul’s largest wholesale market (Eastern/Local Mania)

  • 📍 Location: Garak-dong, Songpa-gu (directly connected to Garak Market Station on Line 8)
  • 🕐 Business (Wholesale): Dawn 00:00-08:00 (Fisheries, Fruits and Vegetables Auction) + Daily 06:00-23:00 (Retail)
  • 🚫 Closed: Sundays (most stores)
  • Signature: Full set of fish + fruits and vegetables + meat + agricultural products (Seoul’s largest comprehensive wholesaler)
  • Foreigner Infrastructure: Lack of English guide (mainly local enthusiasts) → Translation app recommended
  • Foreigner tip: Local restaurant owner’s mania course = Cheaper/authentic. Hansan rather than Noryangjin. Instagram·MZ awareness rises ↑.

#3. Mapo (Western Seoul Fisheries) — Noryangjin Alternative

  • 📍 Location: Mapo-gu (Line 6 Mapo Station, Line 5 Gongdeok Station)
  • 🕐 Business: Daily 05:00-22:00 (depending on store)

  • Signature: Mapo wholesale/retail fisheries + Mapo Bridge cross + Near the Han River
  • Foreigner tip: When waiting in Noryangjin, plan B. Han River (#586) cross course.
