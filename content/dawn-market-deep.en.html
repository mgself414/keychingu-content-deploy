Seoul Dawn Wholesale Market Guide: Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market Auction and Noryangjin Cup-bap Street, Gangseo Agricultural Wholesale Market and Suhyup Gangseo Fish Market

Subtitle: Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (live fish auction from 03:00, sales open every day of the year) · Noryangjin Cup-bap Street (varies by shop) · Gangseo Agricultural Wholesale Market (varies by shop, near Magok Station) · Suhyup Gangseo Fish Market (08:00~20:00, restaurant floor 10:00~22:00, open every day of the year) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers Seoul's wholesale markets and Noryangjin Cup-bap Street. They are the Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진수산물도매시장) with its dawn auctions, Noryangjin Cup-bap Street (노량진컵밥거리), which sells cup-bap developed at the street stalls of the Noryangjin cram-school district, and the Gangseo Agricultural Wholesale Market (강서농산물도매시장) and Suhyup Gangseo Fish Market (수협 강서수산시장) in the southwest. The Noryangjin auction is closed on Sundays, but sales are registered as open every day of the year. Garak Market is in the #616 Garak Market Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Seafood Auction — Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market

Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진수산물도매시장) [Nodeul-ro 674 (노들로 674), Dongjak-gu (동작구), ☎ 02-2254-8000~1] is a wholesale seafood market that began in 1927 on Uiju-ro (의주로), next to today's Seoul Station (서울역), and was newly built in Noryangjin in June 1971. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the largest inland specialized seafood wholesale market in Korea and accounts for 50% of the trading volume in the Seoul metropolitan area. In 2002 Suhyup (수협) took over the market and modernized it. The daily trading volume is about 250~300 tons.

Prices are set by the volume that comes in that day, so it is described as a good idea to check the auction prices on the website. Retail prices are about 10~20% higher than auction prices. The introduction says auctions start at 1:00 a.m., but the registered auction start times include 00:00 for the popular category, so the two values disagree, and the live fish auction held at 3:00 a.m. is described as a sight to see.

  • Auction start: popular category 00:00~, shellfish 01:00~, premium fresh fish 01:30~, premium live fish 03:00~, frozen category 03:00~.
  • Sales (wholesale and retail): premium 00:00~24:00, popular 01:30~22:00, frozen 03:30~22:00, shellfish 01:00~22:00, dried fish 23:00~19:00, salted seafood 03:00~19:00.
  • By floor: 1st floor auction hall, retail hall, shellfish, live fish, frozen and fresh fish; 2nd floor restaurants, dried fish, food ingredients and salted seafood stores.
  • Closed days: Auction is closed every Sunday, January 1, and the Seollal and Chuseok holidays. Sales are open every day of the year.
  • Parking: Available (1,259 spaces). Credit cards: Accepted.

📍 Street Food — Noryangjin Cup-bap Street

Noryangjin Cup-bap Street (노량진컵밥거리) [Noryangjin-ro 174 (노량진로 174), Dongjak-gu (동작구)] is a street where cup-bap street stalls of the Noryangjin cram-school district gather. Cup-bap (컵밥) is a street food developed at the stalls of the Noryangjin cram-school district, made with exam candidates and students in mind. At first it was called pocha-bap (포차밥), and the name cup-bap has been used since around 2011. Kimchi fried rice is put in a disposable bowl and topped with toppings such as egg, sausage, ham and tuna. It averages 4,000~5,000 won, and it is described as a meal where you pick your own toppings. In 2018 the Dongjak-gu Office (동작구청) put up information signs, and later built a rest area. There are also stalls for snack foods, rice noodles, hot dogs and waffles.

  • Opening hours, closed days and fees: Vary by shop. Parking: Not available.

📍 The Southwest — Gangseo Agricultural Wholesale Market and Suhyup Gangseo Fish Market

Gangseo Agricultural Wholesale Market (강서농산물도매시장) [Balsan-ro 40 (발산로 40), Gangseo-gu (강서구), ☎ Seoul Agro-Fisheries and Food Corporation Gangseo Branch (서울시농수산식품공사 강서지사) 02-2640-6000] is a farm produce wholesale market in the southwest area opened by the Seoul Metropolitan Government. It sells farm, fishery, livestock and industrial products. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it introduced the market wholesaler system for the first time in Korea alongside the auction system, so both distribution methods exist together. The general public can also buy farm produce and fruit, and it is described as being near Magok Station (마곡역) on the subway.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Credit cards: Accepted.
  • Parking: Available (2,570 spaces). Free for the first 10 minutes, 1,000 won up to 180 minutes, and 500 won for every 10 minutes after that.

Suhyup Gangseo Fish Market (수협 강서수산시장) [Gangseodomaesijang-ro 130 (강서도매시장로 130), Gangseo-gu (강서구), ☎ 02-2669-9000] is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, the largest fish market in southwest Seoul. Every day at dawn it supplies seafood gathered from the production areas through auctions. It sells raw fish, fish, shellfish, crustaceans, dried fish and salted seafood, and seafood bought at the market can be cooked right away at the restaurant floor on the 2nd floor.

  • Opening hours: Fish market 08:00~20:00, restaurant floor 10:00~22:00, Suhyup Sea Mart (수협바다마트) 05:00~21:00.
  • Closed days: Open every day of the year.
  • Parking: Free for the first 10 minutes, 1,000 won from 10 minutes up to under 180 minutes. Free from 2:00 p.m. on Saturday until midnight on Sunday.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The Noryangjin auction is closed on Sundays — sales are registered as open every day of the year.
  2. The live fish auction starts at 03:00 — the popular category auction starts at 00:00, and shellfish from 01:00.
  3. Parking is not available at Noryangjin Cup-bap Street — the Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market has parking for 1,259 vehicles.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. What time can I see the auction?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, auctions at the Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market start at 00:00 for the popular category, 01:00 for shellfish, 01:30 for premium fresh fish, and 03:00 for premium live fish and the frozen category. There is no auction on Sundays.

Q. Can I eat the fish I buy right away?

A. Suhyup Gangseo Fish Market is described as cooking seafood bought at the market right away at the restaurant floor on the 2nd floor. The restaurant floor is open 10:00~22:00. The Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market also has restaurants on the 2nd floor.
