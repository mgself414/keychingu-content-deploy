English Availability Matrix

  • High: English menus, stores popular with foreigners (brands, chain stores)
  • Mid-High: English signage, picture menus available
  • Local: Korean-focused (Naver Map, Papago, translation apps recommended)

Time Slot Hop Matrix

  • Morning (07:00-10:00 AM): Quiet, local atmosphere, good for photos
  • Lunch (11:00 AM-02:00 PM): Peak hours, queues, recommended for short visits
  • Afternoon (02:00-05:00 PM): Good for cafe and dessert hopping
  • Evening (05:00-09:00 PM): Dining, bars, main hours
  • Late Night (09:00 PM+): Nightlife, late-night venues only

Recommendations by Persona

  • American Persona: Priority on K-pop, K-drama, SNS signatures
  • European Persona: Focus on tradition, art, history, hidden gems
  • Family Fit: Strong on English information, picture menus, accessibility
  • Special Occasion: Luxury, Michelin, hotel lounges
  • K-drama Fans: Filming locations, drama signatures
  • MZ & Instagram: Trendy, warehouse concepts, design, photo-friendly

Seasonal Fit

  • Spring (April-May): Cherry blossoms, outdoor activities, walks
  • Summer (June-August): Indoor cool, Han River (한강), beaches
  • Autumn (October-November): Fall foliage, outdoor activities, camping
  • Winter (December-February): Indoors, ondol (floor heating), hanjeongsik (한정식), jjimjilbang (찜질방) (Korean public bathhouse and spa)

Average Budget per Person

  • Casual Dining: ₩30,000-80,000
  • Mid-range Dining: ₩80,000-200,000
  • Luxury & Michelin: ₩200,000-500,000
  • Hotel Lounge & Special Occasion: ₩300,000-800,000

Advance Reservations & Payment

  • Catchtable / Kakao T: English support, for Michelin and luxury restaurants. Catchtable is a restaurant reservation app, and Kakao T is a transportation and booking app.
  • Cards: Most international cards accepted; cash recommended for markets and nofo (노포).
  • Tax Refund: Available at stores with ₩30,000+ purchases; airport refund.
  • Tip: No tipping culture in Korea (100% of the bill is the final price).
