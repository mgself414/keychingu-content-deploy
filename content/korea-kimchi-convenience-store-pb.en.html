⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Convenience store kimchi = not as good as restaurant kimchi": PB kimchi ↑ Fermentation, domestic cabbage, traditional method. Very cost effective ↑.

2. "All 5 PB channels are the same": Differences in price (1,500 vs 2,500), capacity (200g vs 500g), spiciness level, and store accessibility.

3. "Convenience store PB vacuum packaging = checked baggage OK": Convenience store PB = regular packaging (stored for 1-2 days). Return gift = Separate vacuum packaging at supermarkets and duty-free stores.

4. "White kimchi (not spicy) OK at all convenience stores": White kimchi = CU, no brand = stable stock. GS25, E-Mart 24, 7-Eleven = some stores only (large stores OK, small stores X).

5. "Kimchi storage in hotel minibar = OK for 1 week": Hotel minibar 4°C regular refrigerator Store for 1-2 days·3-5 days when vacuum packed.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. First purchase recommendation: GS25 You Us Cabbage Kimchi 200g (KRW 1,500) — Cost-effective #1·Accessible nationwide.

2. Adjust spicyness: White kimchi (not spicy) = CU Heiru and No Brand stable stock. GS25·E-Mart 24 = Large stores only.

3. Ramen + Kimchi Combo: Shin Ramyun·Jin Ramyun + PB Cabbage Kimchi = Mania #1 Combo ([#833](#833) details).

4. Checked baggage: Convenience store PB = Normal packaging (not included in checked baggage) → Supermarket/duty-free shop Vacuum packaging Purchased separately.

5. Allergy: Salted shrimp (crustaceans), anchovy fish sauce, garlic, red pepper powder = Contains standard PB kimchi. Check the English label on the packaging (Import OK).

6. VAT refund: Convenience store food and beverage = Refund X (general supermarket, restaurant, and convenience store food and beverage refund X).

⚠ Medical/Safety/Dietary disclaimer

Convenience store PB kimchi = regular kimchi (salinity ↑, spiciness). Store at 4°C / 1-3 days after opening. Allergies (shrimp sauce, anchovy sauce, garlic, red pepper powder) Check the package label. If food poisoning is suspected, call ☎ 1339 (Medical, English).

Dietary restrictions fallback:

  • Halal/Kosher: Convenience store PB = Non-halal/non-kosher (contains standard salted shrimp). → temple food kimchi separately
  • Vegetarian·Vegan: Convenience store PB = Non-vegetarian (contains fish sauce). → Vegan Kimchi (Soy sauce-based, sold separately in some supermarkets)
  • Gluten free: PB kimchi = OK as long as it does not contain soy sauce (check the label)

✈️ Foreigner Pain Point #1 — Returning Home Gift Guide

  • Convenience store PB ≠ For homecoming gift: Normal packaging, stored for 1-2 days = Not suitable as homecoming gift
  • Return gift = Vacuum packaging at supermarket/duty-free shop: Incheon Airport duty-free shop/E-mart/Lotte Mart
  • Checked baggage OK: Kimchi = Liquid classification (>100ml liquid) = Checked baggage only, carry-on X
  • Importation restrictions by country: Australia, New Zealand, parts of the U.S. = Restrictions on importing kimchi (fermented/vegetable) — Required to check country’s regulations in advance
  • Expiration date: Vacuum packed kimchi = 30~90 days (sealed)·1~3 days after opening
