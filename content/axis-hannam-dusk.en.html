Seoul N-S Axis ② Hannam, at Dusk — The Walking Order from the North End of Hannam Bridge to the Foot of Namsan

Subtitle: Hannam-dong has been covered many times as a list, but almost never as a walking order. This piece gets off at the first stop where Bus 140 crosses the Han River, then walks south to north — up the Hannam-daero incline, through Hannam Five-Way Junction, along Itaewon-ro's showroom strip, up the Hangangjin Station hill, and through the Daesagwan-ro alleys — departing at 4 PM and following the fading light. About two hours. Only one shop is named. Seoul N-S Axis, Part 2.

ISSUE · #1354 · 2026-09-07

🎯 The Frame — A Neighborhood That Starts at the Bridge's North Foot

When Hannam Bridge (한남대교) went up in 1969, the south side of the bridge was farmland and the north side was already a neighborhood. That bridge made Hannam-dong (한남동) the hinge between Gangnam and downtown — the Han River (한강) to the south, Namsan (남산) to the north, and on the slope between them, embassies, art museums, brand showrooms, and old houses stacked in layers. #1353's overview walked through here for just 15 minutes; this piece walks it in order, across two hours as the sun goes down.

Existing Hannam guides (#293, #144, #79, #1073) tell you what's there. This piece tells you what order and what time to walk it in. Shop and restaurant detail is left to those pieces — here, we just follow the road.

  • Total time: about 2 hours (about 55 minutes walking + time spent)
  • Best time: depart at 4 PM — the showrooms are still open, and the sun starts to fade around the time you reach the Hangangjin Station hill. Sunset time shifts by season (around 7 PM in summer, around 5 PM in winter), so set your departure time about an hour and a half before sunset
  • Incline: moderate to steep — the Hannam-daero climb and the hill behind Hangangjin Station. There are stairs. Wear comfortable shoes

🚶 The Route — In the Order You Walk It

There are two possible starting points. If you're arriving by Bus 140, cross Hannam Bridge and get off at the first stop, "Soonchunhyang University Hospital (순천향대학병원)" (heading toward Dobongsan). By subway, exit Hangangjin Station (한강진역) Exit 2 on Line 6 and walk south along Hannam-daero (한남대로) to start at the north end of the bridge (about 10 minutes — in this case the course becomes a there-and-back).

① North End of Hannam Bridge → Up the Hannam-daero Incline → Hannam Five-Way Junction (🚶 about 10 minutes · uphill)

  • Looking north from the stop, the road climbs gently toward Namsan. It's a wide sidewalk along a busy main road with buses passing constantly — the stretch where you feel this axis's noise in your body.
  • 📖 Story 1 — A Neighborhood Between a Bridge and a Tunnel: Hannam-daero is the short road that runs from the north end of Hannam Bridge to the entrance of Namsan 1st Tunnel (남산1호터널). December 1969 brought the bridge, August 1970 the tunnel — this road was widened to connect the two. Every car going from Gangnam to downtown passes up this incline. Hannam-dong being known only as a "wealthy neighborhood" came later; first, it was a passage.
  • Hannam Five-Way Junction (한남오거리): the five-way intersection where Hannam-daero meets Itaewon-ro (이태원로). This is where you leave the main road.

② Hannam Five-Way Junction → Itaewon-ro Showroom Strip (🚶 about 8 minutes)

  • From the junction, turn west onto Itaewon-ro. From here, brand flagship stores and concept-shop showrooms line both sides of the street. People call this stretch the so-called "Comme des Garçons street" (꼼데가르송길) — not an official road name, just a nickname taken from a flagship store.
  • The shop windows are the exhibit. Even with nothing to buy, it's worth the walk. Individual store guides are in #293 (the standalone Hannam-dong piece).
  • If picking just one window seat on this street, it's Anthracite Hannam (앤트러사이트 한남) (Itaewon-ro 240, roasting on the 1st floor, lounge on the 2nd-3rd floors) — one representative example. Coffee before you start walking, or a stop to close out the evening after coming down from the alleys at dusk.

③ Showroom Strip → Hangangjin Station Exit 1 → In Front of the Museum on the Hill (🚶 about 10 minutes · uphill)

  • Keep heading west along Itaewon-ro and you reach Hangangjin Station on Line 6. Turn right into the first alley next to Exit 1 and climb the hill, and within 5 minutes you're in front of Leeum Museum of Art (리움미술관).
  • In this piece, Leeum is a pass-through point — it's closed on Mondays and reservation-only, so if you're planning to go in, book ahead on the official site (viewing tips are in #144 and #79). Even without a reservation, the ramp and courtyard in front of the building make for good dusk photos.

④ In Front of the Museum → Daesagwan-ro (대사관로) Alleys (🚶 about 15 minutes · hill and stairs)

  • Head into the residential alleys behind the museum. The road name in this area is Daesagwan-ro — literally named for the embassies and residences clustered here. The walls are high and the alleys are quiet.
  • 📖 Story 2 — A Neighborhood on a Slope: Hannam-dong sits on Namsan's south face. The higher you go, the older the single-family houses and embassy residences; the lower you go, the newer the buildings and showrooms — elevation is chronology here. As the sun fades, Namsan's shadow comes down first while the Han River side is still bright. That's the reason to walk this alley at this hour.
  • There are almost no shops in this alley — that absence is the content of this stretch. The list of 7 bars and dining spots is in #1073, wine bars in #1075.

⑤ Daesagwan-ro → Wrap Up at Hangangjin Station Exit 2 (🚶 about 10 minutes · downhill) — or Re-board Bus 140

  • Coming down out of the alleys, you're back on Itaewon-ro. Hangangjin Station Exit 2 is the endpoint.
  • To keep tracing the axis: head back to the main road and re-board Bus 140 toward Dobongsan at the "Soonchunhyang University Hospital" stop on Hannam-daero. Through Namsan 1st Tunnel at dusk and on into downtown — an option for closing out #1353's segments ⑥ through ⑧ in a night version.

🙏 Alley Manners — the Core of This Course

  • This alley is both a residential area and a diplomatic space. Don't photograph embassy or residence front gates and guard posts. Don't raise a camera over a wall.
  • Keep your voice down in the alleys. Evening is when residents are coming home.
  • On the showroom strip, ask staff before photographing inside a store. Photographing the window displays from outside is generally fine.
  • Don't block the hill stairs with your group — they're narrow and residents use them often.

💡 Where You Might Get Thrown Off

  • Stop names change with direction: for Bus 140, it's "Soonchunhyang University Hospital" heading toward Dobongsan and "Hannam Five-Way Junction" heading toward Yangjae. Check the direction in your app.
  • Leeum is closed on Mondays. Show up without a reservation and you may not even get into the permanent collection — doesn't matter if you're only planning to pass by.
  • Hangangjin Station has only three exits: Exit 1 (museum hill) and Exit 2 (toward Hannam Bridge and the performance hall). Get your bearings underground before you come up.
  • The incline is steeper than it looks: the Hannam-daero climb, the museum hill, and the Daesagwan-ro stairs. Not a course for rolling a suitcase.

6 FAQs

Q1. Are Itaewon and Hannam the same neighborhood?

  • They're adjacent but different. West of Itaewon Station is multinational and nocturnal; east of Hangangjin Station, Hannam is quiet residential streets, showrooms, and museums. This piece walks only Hannam. A comparison of the two neighborhoods is in #79.

Q2. Does it have to be at dusk?

  • Daytime works too, but the course is designed to start within showroom hours and put you on the hill as Namsan's shadow comes down. At night the alleys are dark and residential, so it's not recommended.

Q3. How do I get into Leeum?

  • Book on the official site (the permanent collection is free but reservation-only; special exhibitions are paid — confirm on the official site before visiting). Closed on Mondays. Set aside more than an hour for a visit and the course runs to 3 hours.

Q4. Where do I eat?

  • This piece covers only the route. Hannam dining and bar lists are in #293 and #1073, wine bars in #1075, Chinese food in #1094.

Q5. Can I do this without Bus 140?

  • Yes. Starting and finishing at Hangangjin Station on Line 6 makes it an all-subway there-and-back course. That said, the feel of the axis — "getting off at the first stop after crossing the Han River" — comes through better on the bus.

Q6. Does this overlap with the Haebangchon (해방촌) alley walk (#1341)?

  • No overlap. Haebangchon is west of Noksapyeong Station (녹사평역) on Namsan's southwest face; Hannam is east of Hangangjin Station on Namsan's southeast face. Same Namsan foothills, but opposite sides.
