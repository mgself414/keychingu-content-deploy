K-Drama 2026-27 New Releases: 11 Titles — Platforms and Release Dates

🎯 Who This Is For

This issue is for people picking new dramas to watch before or after a trip to Korea, and for people who want to time a trip around new release dates. It includes only titles whose name, cast, release timing and platform were confirmed through an official platform announcement or a major press report. For titles already released as of today (2026-11-25) we give the release date; for titles not yet released we give the announced timing with the source and its date. Titles that were announced for production but have no release timing were left out.

⚠ Notation rule: The parentheses next to a release date name the announcement or article where we first confirmed that date, and its publication date. Schedules for unreleased titles can change because of broadcaster or platform circumstances. In fact, SBS's Seungsan Itseumnida (승산 있습니다) was dropped from the February Friday-Saturday slot and replaced by Full Count (풀카운트). Its new slot is undecided (Ilgan Sports 2026-10-19). We marked availability overseas only where the official announcement says "released worldwide." Check availability in each country by searching the title in the app you use.

① 6 Titles Already Released in 2026

  • Lady Dua (Netflix) — A Netflix series in which Shin Hye-sun plays Sara Kim (사라킴) and Lee Jun-hyuk plays Mugyeong (무경), the detective on her trail. Sports Kyunghyang reported in its 2026-02-06 article that "it premieres on Netflix on the 13th." The release date is 2026-02-13.
  • Made in Korea Season 2 (Disney+) — The second season of the period drama in which Hyun Bin (Baek Gi-tae (백기태)) and Jung Woo-sung (Jang Geon-young (장건영)) face off again. Woo Do-hwan (우도환) (Baek Gi-hyeon (백기현)), Seo Eun-soo (서은수), Won Ji-an (원지안), Jung Sung-il (정성일) and Noh Jae-won (노재원) also appear. It was released at 2 episodes every Wednesday from 2026-09-09, 6 episodes in all (Sports Kyunghyang 2026-08-04). The story picks up 9 years after Season 1, so watch Season 1 first (Sports Kyunghyang 2026-08-04).
  • Take Charge of My Heart (Netflix) — A romantic comedy in which a drama writer with electric powers and a third-generation chaebol heir (Korean conglomerate family) whose artificial-heart battery has run down enter a contract relationship to "recharge." Kim Young-kwang and Chae Soo-bin star, and Park Su-won (박수원) directs. It is based on a web novel of the same name. The release date is 2026-10-09 (Netflix official announcement 2026-09-10). It suits anyone looking for a light romance.
  • The Remarried Empress (Disney+) — A court romance fantasy adapted from a webtoon with a cumulative two billion nine hundred seventy million (29억 7000만) global views (the original webtoon, Ilgan Sports 2026-08-26). Shin Min-a plays Empress Navier (나비에), Joo Ji-hoon plays Emperor Sovieshu (소비에슈), Lee Se-young plays Rastah (라스타) and Lee Jong-suk plays Prince Heinley (하인리) of the Western Kingdom. It is a Disney+ Original, released on 2026-11-04 (same article). Fans who have read the original webtoon can enjoy comparing the actors' interpretations of the characters.
  • The Blue Road (Netflix) — Two detectives, from Seoul and Tokyo, chase a cross-border serial killing marked by contorted corpses and cryptic messages. Son Suk-ku plays Seoul Metropolitan Police detective Kim Seok (김석) and Eita Nagayama plays Tokyo Metropolitan Police detective Inose (이노세). Kim Sin-rok (김신록), Choi Seong-eun (최성은) and Jung Jae-young (정재영) also appear. The director is Han Jun-hee (한준희). Netflix's announcement of 2026-11-05 gave the release as 2026-11-20 (Fri). It is a procedural in which a Korean and a Japanese detective cooperate across the border.
  • Daeri Suneung (TVING) — A thriller in which seniors at an elite private high school become entangled before the CSAT (the Korean college entrance exam) and slide into crime. Bae Hyun-sung plays Nam Jae-yeop (남재엽), a model student who looks perfect on the surface. It was released exclusively on TVING (티빙) on 2026-11-23 (Ilgan Sports 2026-11-04). You watch it in the TVING app, not on Netflix or Disney+.

② 5 Titles Coming Soon (December 2026 – 2027)

  • Tantara (Netflix, 2026-12-11) — The story of people with nothing who chase success in the record business (set in the 1960~80s). Song Hye-kyo, Gong Yoo, Kim Seol-hyun (김설현), Cha Seung-won (차승원) and Lee Ha-nee (이하늬) appear. The script is by Roh Hee-kyung (노희경) and the director is Lee Yoon-jung (이윤정). Netflix's official announcement of 2026-10-22 gave the release as 2026-12-11 (Fri) at 5 PM. The same announcement says it is released worldwide. The Korean title is Cheoncheonhi Gangryeolhage (천천히 강렬하게). Its 22 episodes are released in three batches, on December 11, 18 and 25 (What's on Netflix 2026-11-23). If you are in Korea in December, you can time your viewing to the Friday releases.
  • Full Count (SBS, February 2027) — A new SBS Friday-Saturday drama about baseball coaches. Kim Rae-won plays acting manager and battery coach Hwang Jin-ho (황진호), and Park Hoon plays pitching coach Jo Dong-hui (조동희), seen as a candidate for the next manager. SBS confirmed the February 2027 slot (Ilgan Sports 2026-10-19).
  • Akmong (SBS, 2027) — The story of designing nightmares, instead of prison, for criminals the law cannot punish. Kim Nam-gil plays detective Kim Tae-i (김태이), who returns to Nambu Precinct (남부서) after a long undercover assignment. Lee Yu-mi plays Jang Gyu-eun (장규은), a victim-support police officer who becomes a "dream architect." Ilgan Sports reported in its 2026-03-18 article that "it will premiere in 2027."
  • Widaehan Bang Ok-suk (tvN, first half of 2027) — A story in which an apartment residents' women's association trying to protect home prices clashes with a redevelopment union head after the redevelopment profits, billed as an "ajumma noir." Jeon Do-yeon plays Bang Ok-suk (방옥숙), the real power in the association, and Park Hae-soo plays redevelopment union head Yun Tae-ung (윤태웅). Kim Sun-young (김선영), Kim Geum-sun (김금순), Kim Guk-hee (김국희), Baek Hyun-jin (백현진) and others join them. The director is Kim Hyung-sik (김형식) and the script is by Lee Gi-cheol (이기철). It was reported as "premiering in the first half of 2027" (Ilgan Sports 2026-09-10).
  • Koreans (Disney+, 2027) — A spy drama that moves the American FX series The Americans to an apartment complex in Seoul in 1990. Lee Byung-hun plays Kim Myeong-jun (김명준), a North Korean agent who has lived in South Korea for 10 years, and Han Ji-min plays Yun Hwa-gyeong (윤화경), his cover wife and fellow agent. Lee Hee-joon (이희준) plays Cha Gi-seok (차기석), a former violent-crimes detective now in the anti-communist investigation division, who moves in next door. The director is Ahn Gil-ho (안길호) and the script is by Park Eun-gyo (박은교). Disney+ officially announced an exclusive 2027 release (Sports Kyunghyang 2026-03-25). The exact month has not been announced.

🗓 Matching Titles to Your Travel Dates

  • If you are in Korea in December 2026 — Tantara is released in batches on December 11, 18 and 25 (Fri) at 5 PM (Netflix 2026-10-22, What's on Netflix 2026-11-23). Friday evenings in your accommodation are a good time to keep watching.
  • If you travel in February 2027 — SBS's Full Count is scheduled to air in February (Ilgan Sports 2026-10-19). As a Friday-Saturday drama, it airs on Friday and Saturday nights. Check the SBS schedule announcement for the exact first air date.
  • To watch on the plane — All 6 episodes of Made in Korea Season 2 are out (Disney+, twice a week, Sports Kyunghyang 2026-08-04). For the rest, check in the app whether each episode is out.

None of these 11 titles has a filming location confirmed by an official announcement yet. If you want to walk K-drama filming locations, see #1101 (Seoul), #1102 (regions) and #452 (map).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Planning a trip around an air date as if it were final — Most 2027 titles only have timing such as "February," "first half" or "2027." Schedules can slip. SBS's Seungsan Itseumnida was dropped from the February Friday-Saturday slot and replaced by Full Count, and its new slot is undecided (Ilgan Sports 2026-10-19). Fit your plans to the first air date after the broadcaster announces it.
  2. Searching for a TVING title from an overseas account — Daeri Suneung is a TVING exclusive (Ilgan Sports 2026-11-04). It will not appear if you search on Netflix or Disney+.
  3. Starting from Season 2 — Made in Korea Season 2 is set 9 years after Season 1 (Sports Kyunghyang 2026-08-04). The relationships between characters carry over, so start with Season 1.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. Can I watch these overseas? — The only title whose official announcement says "released worldwide" is Tantara (Netflix 2026-10-22). For the others, each platform decides availability country by country. Search the title in the app you use.
  2. Where do I watch SBS and tvN dramas? — In Korea, watch them on the channel that broadcasts them. The articles we checked do not name an overseas streaming platform for Full Count, Akmong or Widaehan Bang Ok-suk. Check the broadcaster's announcement as the air date nears.
  3. Are there no more Netflix Korean dramas for 2027? — Netflix announced The Twilight Between Us (혼: 삼각의 시간) (Kang Dong-won (강동원)·Kim Go-eun (김고은), announced 2026-11-12) and The Trauma Code: Heroes on Call (중증외상센터) Seasons 2 and 3 (production confirmed 2026-10-19). But it has not yet given release timing, so they are not among these 11 titles.
