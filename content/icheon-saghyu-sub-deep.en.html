Icheon Sagimakgol Pottery Village Guide — Ceramics Traditional Market, Hands-On at Doyegongbang Deulkkotmaeul, Icheon Ceramics Festival

Subtitle: Sagimakgol Pottery Village (사기막골 도예촌), 11:00–18:00, open year-round · Doyegongbang Deulkkotmaeul (도예공방 들꽃마을), 10:00–18:00, break 12:00–13:00, open year-round · Icheon Ceramics Festival (이천도자기축제), 40th edition in 2026, April 24 – May 5, free — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Sagimakgol Pottery Village is a nationally recognized traditional market and, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, the only traditional market in Korea made up of a single product, ceramics. "Sagimakgol" means "the place where there was a hut for firing ceramics."

This guide covers only Sagimakgol Pottery Village, the hands-on workshop in Sindun-myeon (신둔면), and the Icheon Ceramics Festival, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Icheon Ceramics Art Village (Yespark, 예스파크), the Pottery Village Ssalbap Street (쌀밥거리), and the Gyeonggi Ceramic Art Museum are covered in the #673 Icheon Guide.

🏺 Sagimakgol Pottery Village — Traditional Market of a Single Product, Ceramics

Sagimakgol Pottery Village (56 Gyeongchung-daero 2993beon-gil, Sa-eum-dong, Icheon-si (이천시 경충대로2993번길 56, 사음동), ☎ 031-638-8388) is a village surrounded by Mt. Seolbong (설봉산). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is a place where it is easy to find the clay, kilns, wood, and glaze materials needed to make ceramics.

  • Sights: Past the entrance with its sculptures, a variety of ceramics line the street, from traditional porcelain to everyday porcelain. Native potters who grew up treating kiln sites as playgrounds gather with potters who studied ceramics at university or at ceramics-industry high schools, each presenting their own work.
  • Opening hours: 11:00–18:00, open year-round.
  • Parking: Available.

🖐 Doyegongbang Deulkkotmaeul — Hands-On and Lodging

Doyegongbang Deulkkotmaeul (95 Dojayesul-ro 5beon-gil, Sindun-myeon, Icheon-si (이천시 신둔면 도자예술로5번길 95), ☎ 010-7335-6832) is a workshop that opened on May 1, 2001, offering pottery-making experiences together with lodging. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, teachers who majored in ceramics at university and graduate school give instruction, teaching according to level, from kindergarteners to foreigners, groups, and seniors.

  • Facilities: Garden, lawn field, gallery workshop building, exhibition room.
  • Opening hours: 10:00–18:00 (break 12:00–13:00), open year-round.
  • Parking: Available.
  • Nearby: Seolbong Park (설봉공원), Icheon Hot Spring (이천온천), Sansuyu Village (산수유마을).

📅 Icheon Ceramics Festival — April 24 – May 5, 2026

The Icheon Ceramics Festival, the 40th edition in 2026, was held around Icheon Ceramics Art Village and Sagimakgol Pottery Village from April 24 to May 5, 10:00–18:00, free of charge. It was hosted by Icheon City and the Icheon Cultural Foundation (이천문화재단, ☎ 031-645-3683).

  • Exhibitions: Master craftsman exhibition, contemporary artist competition, special exhibition, invited foreign artists' exhibition and demonstrations, Korea Institute of Ceramic Engineering and Technology exhibition.
  • Sales: The Korea Tourism Organization program listing lists ceramics exhibitions and sales by 240 or so participating pottery workshops.
  • Hands-on: Potter's wheel experience, making a ceramic cup, writing wishes on a giant ceramic piece, ceramics playground.
  • Side events: Master craftsman workshop, open-air firing ceramics making, premium ceramics auction, temple food demonstration and tasting.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to Sagimakgol early in the morning — The registered opening time starts at 11:00.
  2. Timing a visit to Deulkkotmaeul for lunchtime — 12:00–13:00 is the break time.
  3. Treating Sagimakgol and Yespark as the same place — Sagimakgol Pottery Village is in Sa-eum-dong, while Icheon Ceramics Art Village (Yespark) is in Sindun-myeon, so the addresses differ.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Does Sagimakgol Pottery Village have a closed day?

A. It is registered as open year-round in the Korea Tourism Organization information.

Q. Was there an admission fee during the festival?

A. The 2026 Icheon Ceramics Festival was registered as free.
