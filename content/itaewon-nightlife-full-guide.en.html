Full guide to Itaewon nightlife for foreigners

> Whiskey, cocktail, club, live — 25 foreign hubs of 5 types + time zone hop matrix

Itaewon has been a foreign nightlife hub for 80 years since it was a US military base in 1945. Even after the relocation of the U.S. military base to Pyeongtaek (2017), it remains No. 1 in nightlife for foreigners in Seoul in connection with Hannam and Haebangchon. 25 places of 5 types including whiskey bar, cocktail dining, club, live music, and indie pub + foreigner fit time zone hop matrix + safety, budget, dress code matrix.

1. Itaewon and Hannam nightlife scene

Foreigner ratio

  • Approximately 10,000 foreigners visit Itaewon daily (Seoul City Statistics 2024)
  • Foreigner ratio on weekend evenings is 30-40% (20 times the Korean average of 1.5%)
  • Many stores fluent in English and diverse nationalities (USA, Europe, Middle East, Southeast Asia)

Time zone

  • 17-19:00: Dining/Happy Hour (inexpensive)
  • 19-22:00: Dinner + Cocktails
  • 22-02:00: Whiskey·Club·Live
  • 02-06:00: Club, late-night pub, 24-hour restaurant

Region sub-cluster

  • Itaewon Main: Top club/dining bar density
  • Gyeongnidan-gil: Indie pub, alley, MZ
  • Hannam: Adjacent to whiskey bar, luxury, and gallery
  • Haebangchon: hill cafe + indie pub·sunset

8. Foreign fit tips

English language availability

  • Top: Comda Vitude, South Side Parlor, Escondido, Volnost, Hotel Lounge (English fluent, foreign regulars)
  • Slander: Jackson Pizza·Made·Sound (English menu·basic English)
  • Local: Nogari Alley/Traditional Liquor Pub (picture menu/translation app)

Dress code

  • Club (Volnost·Made·Sound): smart casual·sneaker OK·shorts·slippers
  • Whiskey bar: smart casual~formal (Comme da Vitude·Cask)
  • Hotel lounge: Formal/jacket recommended
  • Indie Pub Live: casual OK

Budget (per person, per night)

  • Pattern A whiskey deep: ₩150,000~400,000 (dinner + whiskey)
  • Pattern B Club: ₩80,000~200,000 (Admission + Drink)
  • Pattern C Live: ₩60,000~150,000
  • Pattern D Korea unique: ₩50,000~120,000

By persona

  • American hipster: Pattern B (Itaewon club·DJ)
  • European sophisticate: Pattern A (Hannam Whiskey)
  • Music lover: Pattern C (Live)
  • foodie + pub: Pattern D (Korean unique)
  • special occasion: Hotel lounge + Hannam whiskey
  • LGBTQ+ friendly: Cake Shop·Itaewon main strip (multiple stores)

Safety Information

  • Itaewon is a safe area: Foreign hub, 24-hour crowds
  • Returning home after drinking: Taxi (Kakao T) or subway (first train at 24:00 and 5:00 AM)
  • Cash + Card: Card OK for clubs and bars / Cash for street stalls and traditional liquor pubs
  • Drink spiking caution: Do not leave your drink unprotected. (Recommended for global nightlife)
  • Itaewon Station, Line 6: Last train at 00:00 AM → Taxi recommended
  • Safety for Foreigners: Korean nightlife is safer than the global average (low violent crime rate)

By season

  • Spring (April-May): Itaewon/Hannam outdoor dining bar
  • Summer (June-August): Haebangchon sunset live + Han River outdoor
  • Fall (October-November): Whiskey and cigar lounge peak season
  • Winter (December-February): Indoor whiskey bar + hotel lounge (warm)

9. Korean nightlife etiquette (foreigner fit)

Do

  • Receiving and pouring drinks with both hands (especially when drinking with Koreans)
  • Cheomjan
  • Alcohol refusal OK (driving, pregnancy, religion specified)
  • Karaoke option (Korea unique, foreigners find it fascinating)

Don't

  • Pouring a glass with one hand
  • Drinking straight ahead
  • tip X (no Korean tip culture)
  • Street drinking — legal in Korea, but good manners are recommended
