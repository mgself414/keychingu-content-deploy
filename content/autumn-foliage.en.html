5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Missing the Foliage Season

Korean fall foliage moves southward — late September in Gangwon (강원) → mid-October in Seoul (서울) → early November in the south. Peak lasts only 1–2 weeks — miss it and you lose half the color. Mid-October – early November is Seoul's peak.

2. Going on Weekends and Getting Stuck in Crowds

Bukhansan (북한산) and Naejangsan (내장산) fill up from 5 AM on weekends. Weekday mornings 9–11 AM are best — plenty of room for photos and strolls.

3. Dressing Too Lightly

From October to November, Korea's daily temperature swing exceeds 10°C. Morning can be 8°C, afternoon 18°C. Layering (thin outer jacket + shirt) is essential.

4. Hiking Without Proper Footwear

Bukhansan (북한산) and Seoraksan (설악산) have rocky surfaces and steep inclines — sneakers will slip. Lightweight hiking boots or trekking shoes are strongly recommended.

5. Expecting Exactly What You Saw in K-Dramas

Dramas use CGI and camera color grading. Real foliage is a mix of reds, yellows, and browns. Adjust your expectations and you'll enjoy it even more.

① Changdeokgung Secret Garden (창덕궁 후원) — Foreign Visitors' #1 Foliage Spot

Location: 99 Yulgok-ro (율곡로), Jongno-gu (종로구) — 5-min walk from Anguk Station (안국역), Line 3

Admission: Secret Garden ₩8,000 (Changdeokgung Palace (창덕궁) ₩3,000 separate)

Highlights:

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site + textbook-perfect fall foliage
  • Advance booking required (on-site tickets frequently sell out) — available in English on the Korea Cultural Heritage Foundation website
  • Guided tours in Korean, English, Chinese, and Japanese (90 min)
  • Buyongji Pond (부용지) and Gwallam-jeong Pavilion (관람정) are the #1 photo spots

One-liner: "Korean palace + fall foliage = foreign visitors' #1 Instagram moment"

② Namsan (남산) — City Foliage + Night View

Location: Myeongdong (명동) · Namsan Station (남산역)

Admission: Free (Namsan Circular Trail (남산 둘레길)) / Cable car ₩14,000 / N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) observation deck ₩21,000

Highlights:

  • Myeongdong (명동) → Namsan Cable Car → foliage walk → night view
  • 11 AM – 2 PM sunlight gives the best foliage colors
  • Circular trail on foot: 1 hour — difficulty ★ for foreign visitors

One-liner: "Myeongdong (명동) shopping + foliage + night view all in one go"

③ Bukhansan National Park (북한산 국립공원) — Full-Scale Foliage Hiking

Location: Gangbuk-gu (강북구) — Suyu Station (수유역), Line 4 + bus

Admission: Free (national park)

Highlights:

  • Korea's signature hiking destination — foliage sections on Bukhansan Dulle-gil (북한산 둘레길)
  • Start at 9 AM on a weekday, wrap up by 4 PM
  • Hiking boots required, bring at least 1L of water

One-liner: "Introduction to Korean hiking + full foliage experience — weekday mornings recommended"

④ Deoksugung Stolle-gil (덕수궁 돌담길) — City Stroll

Location: City Hall Station (시청역)

Admission: Deoksugung Palace (덕수궁) ₩1,000

Highlights: Jeongdong-gil (정동길) + Deoksugung Stone Wall path = classic Korean autumn stroll for foreign visitors

⑤ Seoul Forest (서울숲) — Family & Picnic Foliage Spot

Location: Seongsu-dong (성수동) — Seoul Forest Station (서울숲역), Suin-Bundang Line (수인분당선)

Admission: Free

Highlights: Urban park + deer · spotted deer + fall foliage / #1 for families and picnics

⑥ Seoraksan (설악산) — Korea's Foliage Heartland

Location: Sokcho (속초), Gangwon-do (강원도)

Admission: ₩3,500 + cable car ₩15,000

Peak Period: Late September – mid-October (earliest foliage in Korea)

Highlights:

  • The origin of Korean fall foliage — deepest color saturation
  • Sinheungsa Temple (신흥사) · Ulsanbawi Rock (울산바위) · Cheonbuldong Valley (천불동계곡)
  • Sokcho (속초) overnight stay recommended — Express Bus from Seoul's Dong Seoul Terminal (동서울터미널), approx. 2.5 hours

One-liner: "Korea's truest foliage colors — peaks one week before Seoul"

⑦ Naejangsan (내장산) — Jeonbuk's (전북) Iconic Foliage Mountain

Location: Jeongeup (정읍), Jeollabuk-do (전라북도)

Admission: ₩3,000 + cable car ₩9,000

Peak Period: Late October – early November

Highlights:

  • "Naejangsan (내장산) fall foliage" = the mountain Koreans visit most for autumn leaves
  • Cable car + trail = accessible even for foreign visitors who find hiking challenging
  • KTX to Jeongeup Station (정읍역) from Seoul: approx. 1.5 hours

⑧ Nami Island + Gapyeong Day Tour (남이섬 + 가평) — #1 Day Tour for Foreign Visitors

Location: Gapyeong (가평), Gyeonggi-do (경기도)

Admission: Nami Island (남이섬) ₩16,000 (ferry included)

Highlights:

  • Filming location of K-drama "Winter Sonata (겨울연가)" — #1 day tour destination for foreign visitors
  • Metasequoia avenue + fall foliage + hanbok (한복) photo spots
  • ITX Cheongchun (ITX 청춘열차) from Yongsan Station (용산역) to Gapyeong (가평): 1 hour + 5-min ferry
  • Full day possible: Gapyeong (가평) + Petite France (쁘띠프랑스) + Jarasum Island (자라섬)

One-liner: "The definitive answer for a foreign visitor's autumn day trip"

8 Tips for Foreign Visitors

✅ Timing — mid-October to early November is best

✅ Weekday mornings 9–11 AM = fewer crowds + best foliage colors

✅ Layer your clothes (daily swing of 8°C–18°C)

✅ Hiking boots (Bukhansan (북한산) · Seoraksan (설악산) · Naejangsan (내장산))

✅ Advance booking (Changdeokgung Secret Garden (창덕궁 후원) · Nami Island (남이섬) ferry)

✅ Cash backup ₩30,000 (some mountain kiosks are cash-only)

✅ Sunscreen (autumn sun is still strong)

✅ Watch your belongings and phone (mountain theft is rare but stay alert)

FAQ

Q1. Can foreign visitors hike too?

Bukhansan Dulle-gil (북한산 둘레길) is mostly flat — foreign visitors can handle it. Bukhansan (북한산) summit requires hiking boots and 4–5 hours to spare.

Q2. Are English guides available?

Changdeokgung Secret Garden (창덕궁 후원) and Nami Island (남이섬) have English signage and apps. Mountain trails are mainly in Korean, but trail markings are clear.

Q3. Is it suitable for families with children?

Changdeokgung Secret Garden (창덕궁 후원), Seoul Forest (서울숲), and Nami Island (남이섬) are child-friendly. Mountain destinations (Bukhansan (북한산), Seoraksan (설악산), Naejangsan (내장산)) are not recommended for young children.

Q4. What if I only have one day?

Changdeokgung Secret Garden (창덕궁 후원) (morning) + Namsan (남산) (afternoon) + Myeongdong (명동) (evening) = a complete one-day Seoul foliage itinerary.

Q5. What if it rains?

Foliage colors are still beautiful in the rain (a lush, moody feel). On mountain trails, rain makes surfaces slippery — switch to an in-city route to be safe.

Q6. Is this guide useful outside autumn?

Seoul Forest (서울숲), Deoksugung (덕수궁), and Changdeokgung (창덕궁) are great year-round. Mountains offer their own appeal in spring (fresh greenery), summer (lush canopy), and winter (snow scenery).
