📜 Origin and history of Kongguksu

Health food for noblemen during the Joseon Dynasty

  • 17th century Joseon — Soybeans = Donguibogam recorded as “longevity food” (#546 UNESCO 2009)
  • Noble family summer health food — Combination of ground soybeans and soy milk + noodles
  • Class spread — Spread to areas such as Pyongyang and Jinju during the Japanese colonial period
  • Establishment of modern times — Jinju Hall and Jinju House in Seoul were standardized in the 1950s
