Winter Daebangeo Season Guide — The Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries' December-January Pick, Bought at Noryangjin Fisheries Market and Eaten at a Market Restaurant, Plus Moseulpo Port Restaurants and Gijang Daebyeon Port Raw-Fish Restaurants

Subtitle: A guide focused on one fish, the large yellowtail, which the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries' "Seafood of the Month" introduced as tasting best from December through January · In Seoul, buy at a raw-fish shop in Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market and eat at a market restaurant; in Jeju, a Moseulpo Port restaurant (the "jari yellowtail" caught with jari damselfish as bait); in Busan, a raw-fish restaurant at Gijang Daebyeon Port · The smallest plate (small) of daebangeo sashimi runs in the 60,000s to 80,000s of won on shop menus · The fat content differs by cut, so start with the lean body meat · It is raw fish, so choose cooked dishes if you are pregnant — checked against the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries, Noryangjin Fisheries Co. auction prices, Korea Tourism Organization tourism information (TourAPI), Yonhap News, Seoul Shinmun, Naver Place and Kakao Map menus

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Yellowtail (bangeo) is the raw fish Koreans wait for every winter. The Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries (해양수산부) has picked yellowtail as its "Seafood of the Month" several times (December 2019, December 2023, January 2025), and its 2023 announcement said, "In season, yellowtail tastes best from December through January." The 2025 announcement explains why: "The colder the weather gets, the more the flesh fills out and the richer the fat becomes," so it tastes best in winter.

When a Korean restaurant menu lists "daebangeo" separately, it means a large yellowtail. The Ministry says, "Large yellowtail (daebangeo, 대방어) tastes different by cut," and advises eating the leaner cuts first. One fish gives both lean flesh and fatty belly, so a group sharing a big yellowtail is the shape of a winter company dinner.

This guide covers only one fish, yellowtail. For which sashimi tastes best in each month, see #789 Korea's Seasonal Sashimi by Month; for the whole sequence of having sashimi cut at a market and carrying it to a restaurant, see #790 Sashimi Market → Table Workflow; and for choosing among a raw-fish restaurant, a Japanese restaurant, and a market, see #791 Raw-Fish Restaurant vs Japanese Restaurant vs Market.

🐟 1. What Kind of Fish Is Daebangeo

Size: I could not find an official weight standard for from how many kg a fish is called daebangeo. The Moseulpo production-area grading reported by Seoul Shinmun (서울신문) (2025-12-15) is extra-large yellowtail 8kg or more, daebangeo 4-8kg, medium yellowtail under 4kg, and small yellowtail 2.5kg or less, sorted by eye by the captain and crew. Some Seoul menus state a weight, such as "daebangeo set (8kg-11kg)" (Hyeongje Susan (형제수산)) and "extra-large daebangeo of 12 kilos or more" (Sudong Sanghoe (수동상회)). When buying at a market, ask how many kg the fish is.

Moseulpo's "jari yellowtail": Fishers at Moseulpo (모슬포), in southwest Jeju, catch yellowtail with jari damselfish as bait. That is why it is called "jari yellowtail," and when the bait runs out they end the day's fishing (Seoul Shinmun 2025-12-15). Deokseung Sikdang (덕승식당) in Moseulpo wrote "only Moseulpo yellowtail" on its menu (Naver Place (네이버 플레이스)).

Nutrition: The Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries introduced yellowtail as higher in protein than other fish, rich in fat, and high in vitamin D and niacin (2019-12).

Eating order: The order the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries recommends is to eat "starting with the lean body cuts and alternating with the fatty cuts." If you eat only belly from the start, it gets greasy fast.

Sides: Korean style adds, besides soy sauce and wasabi, chojang (초장) (a sauce of gochujang mixed with vinegar), seaweed, and leafy vegetables for wrapping. The Gangbyeon Sanghoe (강변상회) menu in Noryangjin lists "ginger, pickled shallots, wasabi set, chojang, soy sauce, ssamjang, seaweed, and sweet vinegar included," and the Moseulpo Minyeongi menu lists "rinsed aged kimchi and seaweed" on the side (Naver Place).

🏙 3. Seoul — Buy at the Market, Eat at a Market Restaurant

The common way to eat daebangeo in Seoul is to have sashimi cut at a fish-market shop, carry it over, and get a seat, vegetables, sauces, and spicy fish stew at a restaurant inside the market. The full sequence is in #790. Every market is set up differently.

🌊 4. Jeju — Moseulpo Is Yellowtail Country

Moseulpo Port · Southernmost Yellowtail Festival (최남단 방어축제) — Daejeong-eup, Seogwipo-si (서귀포시 대정읍) · Yonhap News 2026-11-08

  • The 26th festival in 2026 was held around Moseulpo Port on November 19~22. The program was bare-handed yellowtail catching, sea-cage yellowtail fishing, a yellowtail auction, a fishing-success ritual, and a street parade. Even after the festival, the port restaurants sell yellowtail sashimi through the winter (menus below).
  • Getting there: From Jeju Airport, express bus 151 goes toward Daejeong and Moseulpo (#1388).
  • At the Moseulpo auction house, an extra-large yellowtail (8kg or more) normally goes for 170,000-180,000 won per fish, and on the day reported in the Seoul Shinmun article of 2025-12-15 it sold for 250,000 won (auction price basis).

Wholesale prices at the production area are different. According to Seoul Shinmun (2025-12), extra-large yellowtail at the Moseulpo auction house normally trades at 170,000-180,000 won per fish (wholesale bid price).

Wholesale price reference — Noryangjin Fisheries Co. (노량진수산(주)) "Today's Auction Prices": On the website of the Noryangjin fish market's wholesale corporation (www.susansijang.co.kr, Seafood Price Information → Today's Auction Prices), pick the species "yellowtail" and that day's auction winning prices appear. Among the values checked on November 23, 2026, the per-kg average prices on the live-yellowtail lines labeled with the size "1mi" were 19,900 won for Donghae-si, 21,700 won for Geojin, 16,600 won for Daejin, 14,800 won for Goseong, and 20,300 won for Japanese-origin. The site does not explain what the size "1mi" means. These are wholesale auction prices. The Korea Tourism Organization's description says "retail prices are about 10-20% higher than auction prices," and the price of cutting the sashimi and setting the table is separate. If you want to know that day's price, check this price page.

Buy to match your party size: Most menus list by party size, such as small (2 people), medium (2~3 people), and large (3~4 people). At a shop that does not list party sizes, tell them how many people you are to match the amount.

🥢 8. Dietary Information — Before You Eat

  • Raw fish: Yellowtail sashimi is uncooked fish. If you are pregnant or have a weak immune system, it is safer to avoid raw fish, and you should consult a doctor. Cooked options include spicy fish stew, clear fish soup, and grilled yellowtail. The Noryangjin table-setting restaurant lets you choose between spicy fish stew and clear fish soup (Cheonghaejin), and the Moseulpo Minyeongi 1-person menu comes with yellowtail clear soup and grilled yellowtail.
  • Fish allergy: If you have a fish allergy, avoid it. The spicy fish stew and clear soup broths also contain fish.
  • Sauces: Soy sauce mostly contains soy and wheat. Chojang is gochujang-based, so it is spicy.
  • Side dishes: Kimchi often contains salted fermented seafood. If someone in your group avoids seafood entirely, tell them in advance.
  • Religious diets: Market stalls and restaurants have no certification and share knives, cutting boards, and sauces. If you observe strictly, talk it over with your group. To ask whether the sauce or stew contains cooking wine (맛술), use "Does it contain cooking wine (mirin)?" from the table above.
  • Vegetarian: Market restaurant menus center on fish, so there is little to choose from. Wrap vegetables and a bowl of rice, about that much.

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

  1. Trusting only the grade name — what counts as "daebangeo" in weight differs by shop. Ask for both the weight (kg) and the price.
  2. Eating belly first and only belly — as the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries advises, start with the lean body meat and alternate.
  3. Going to Seobudu Fisheries Market in the daytime — it opens only 05:00~09:00.
  4. Not checking the day of the week — Namhang Hoetjip is closed Thursdays and Geoje Haegeumgang Hoe Center is closed Mondays. Geoje Haegeumgang Hoe Center opens in the afternoon.

⚙ FAQ

Q. Is there daebangeo in November?

A. The Moseulpo port restaurants and the Noryangjin shops have yellowtail sashimi on their menus as of November (Sections 3-4 above). The time the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries says tastes best is December-January. For the sashimi that tastes best each month, see #789.

Q. Can I eat it if I go alone?

A. Some shops have a 1-person menu (Noryangjin Lotto Susan, Moseulpo Minyeongi). Confirm prices at the shop.

Q. Which market should I choose in Seoul?

A. The place where you can check the daebangeo menu and table-setting fee in advance is Noryangjin, based on this survey (Section 3 above). For Garak Mall and Gangseo, I confirmed only how the market restaurants work.
