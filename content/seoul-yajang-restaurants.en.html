What is Yajang (야장)?

"Ya (野) + Jang (場)" — Outdoor Tables Become the Pub

Yajang (야장) refers to Seoul's (서울) street dining culture where people eat and drink at plastic tables and chairs set up in alleys (골목), sidewalks, or plazas outside the restaurant, rather than inside.

Literal Meaning:

  • Ya = Outdoor (野外)
  • Jang = Place/Yard (場)

Why is it Special for Foreign Tourists?

1️⃣ "A Seoul (서울) Scene" Where the Street Becomes a Restaurant

One of the most photographed scenes in Seoul's (서울) alley (골목) landscape for tourists — on a night in Euljiro 3-ga (을지로3가), orange lights illuminate the street, and plastic chairs stretch endlessly along both sides of the alley (골목). This isn't a guidebook photo; it happens every day.

This scene, where the boundary between road and restaurant disappears, is a limited freedom allowed only in Seoul (서울). Tokyo, Beijing, and Singapore do not have this kind of density.

2️⃣ "Nofo (노포) × Yajang (야장)" Combo — A Taste Money Can't Buy

Seoul's (서울) representative yajang (야장) restaurants are mostly nofo (노포, 30+ year traditional restaurant). Since its opening in 1978, Mansun Hof (만선호프) has been considered the originator of the nogari (노가리) hof culture in Euljiro (을지로). Poseokjeong (포석정) and Cheongjin Yeongyang Center (청진영양센타) have also been operating in the same spot for decades. Yajang (야장) is a mechanism that allows you to directly feel this accumulation of time.

3️⃣ Solo, 4 People, or 20 People — All OK

The concept of reservations is rare for yajang (야장). You just go and sit on an empty chair. If there are many people, tables are combined on the spot, and if you're alone, it's common to share a plate of nogari (노가리, dried young pollock) with customers at the next table. This is a Korean-style drinking spot with the lowest risk of "a group of 4 tourists being flustered, not knowing what to order."

Seoul (서울) Yajang (야장) Season and Time

🌡️ Monthly Recommendation

Period Recommendation Feel
March~April ⭐⭐ Below 10°C (50°F) at night, a jacket is essential. Perfect if it aligns with cherry blossom season.
May~June ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Optimal period. Low humidity and cool night air.
July~August Monsoon ⭐ Rain, mosquitoes, humidity – a triple combo. Indoor dining recommended.
Late August~September ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ True golden age after avoiding mid-summer. Continues until early October.
Mid-October ⭐⭐⭐ Just before it gets chilly, overlaps with autumn foliage season.
November~February ❌ Most yajang (야장) close, operate indoors. Only a few operate with heaters.

🕐 By Time of Day

```

17:30 ~ 19:00 (5:30 PM ~ 7:00 PM) — Golden time to secure a spot (before office workers start their toegeungil (퇴근길, way home from work))

19:00 ~ 21:00 (7:00 PM ~ 9:00 PM) — Peak. Euljiro (을지로) and Jongno (종로) alleys (골목) are completely saturated.

21:00 ~ 23:00 (9:00 PM ~ 11:00 PM) — Returns to a relaxed atmosphere, second-round customers are leisurely.

23:00 ~ 01:00 (11:00 PM ~ 1:00 AM) — Nofo (노포) close, only hof (beer) places remain.

```

Tourist Recommendation: Arrive at 18:45 (6:45 PM) → Secure a table just before 19:00 (7:00 PM) → Stay until 21:30 (9:30 PM) and move on to the next spot.

TOP 7 Seoul (서울) Yajang (야장) Restaurants

📍 Based on local verification for 2024-2025. It is recommended to check Naver Map (네이버 지도) for the latest operating hours and relocation status before visiting.

1️⃣ Mansun Hof Nogari Chain Main Branch (만선호프 노가리체인본점) — The Holy Land of Euljiro's (을지로) Nogari (노가리) Hof

Item Content
Address 19, Euljiro 13-gil (을지로13길), Jung-gu (중구), Seoul (서울)
Phone 02-2274-1040
Price Range ₩2,000 ~ ₩13,000 (Nogari (노가리) 1 piece around ₩3,000)
Reviews 3,200+ on Naver Map (네이버 지도)
Subway Euljiro 3-ga Station (을지로3가역) (Line 2/3), Exit 4, 2-minute walk

Since its opening in 1978, this original restaurant has transformed the entire Euljiro (을지로) alley (골목) into the "OB Beer (former HiteJinro direct management) Nogari (노가리) Holy Land." In the evening, the alley (골목) in front of the restaurant fills with plastic chairs. The nogari (노가리, dried young pollock) + draft beer combo is the Seoul-style (서울식) combination defined by this place.

Recommended Order:

  • Nogari (노가리) (grilled dried young pollock) — Order 3 pieces as a base
  • Fish and Chips or Meoktae-gui (먹태구이, grilled dried pollock) — Add one grilled item
  • Draft beer 500cc — The first drink is always draft beer

Tourist Tip: No English menu, but you can point at pictures. Just saying "Fish + draft beer" will work.

2️⃣ Dalmajigwangjang BBQ Main Branch (달맞이광장바베큐 본점) — The Epitome of Euljiro (을지로) Yajang (야장) BBQ

Item Content
Address 1F, 112, Euljiro (을지로), Jung-gu (중구), Seoul (서울)
Phone 07-1498-6715
Price Range ₩16,000 ~ ₩27,000 (per serving of meat)
Reviews 530+ on Naver Map (네이버 지도)
Branches Main Branch (Euljiro 3-ga (을지로3가)) / Namyeong Branch (남영점, Yongsan (용산)) / Seongsu Branch (성수점)

Located in the center of the Euljiro 3-ga (을지로3가) yajang (야장) belt. This restaurant allows you to grill charcoal BBQ directly at outdoor tables, and the smoke and grill smell fill the alley (골목) from the entrance. The sequence of "cold beer at Mansun Hof (만선호프), then meat at Dalmajigwangjang (달맞이광장)" is the classic second-round course for Euljiro (을지로) yajang (야장).

Recommended Order:

  • Samgyeopsal (삼겹살, pork belly) or Moksal (목살, pork neck) 1 serving each
  • Vegetable + Ssam (쌈, wrap) basic set
  • Gyeranjjim (계란찜, steamed egg custard) (a special treat)

3️⃣ Between Mansun Hof (만선호프) and Dalmajigwangjang BBQ (달맞이광장바베큐) — The Entire Euljiro Nogari (노가리) Alley (골목)

Beyond the Mansun Hof (만선호프) Main Branch, other branches like Mansun Hof 2 (만선호프2) and Mansun Hof 9 (만선호프9) are distributed throughout the alley (골목). If the main branch is crowded, just walk one more block down the alley (골목), and you'll find a row of similar yajang (야장) hof (beer) places. The answer to the question, "Where is the highest density of yajang (야장) in Seoul (서울)?" is here, Euljiro 13-gil (을지로13길) + Chungmu-ro 9-gil (충무로9길).

4️⃣ Poseokjeong (Seocho Branch) (포석정 서초점) — Pork BBQ Near Express Bus Terminal

Item Content
Address 194, Sinbanpo-ro (신반포로), Seocho-gu (서초구), Seoul (서울) (near Seoul Express Bus Terminal (서울고속버스터미날))
Category Korean Food > Pork BBQ
Subway Express Bus Terminal Station (고속터미널역) (Line 3/7/9), 5-minute walk

If Yajang (야장) in Gangbuk (강북) centers around Euljiro (을지로) and Jongno (종로), then Poseokjeong (포석정) is the representative yajang (야장) spot in the Gangnam (강남) area. It's located right across the street from the Express Bus Terminal (고속버스터미널) underground shopping center. It's a good place for tourists to visit before or after traveling to nearby cities (Jeonju (전주), Busan (부산)) by express bus.

Features: Operates both a spacious indoor hall and yajang (야장) seating extended onto the sidewalk. Can accommodate group tourists.

5️⃣ Cheongjin Yeongyang Center (청진영양센타) — The Originator of Gwanghwamun (광화문) Samgyetang (삼계탕)

Item Content
Address 44-1, Sambong-ro (삼봉로), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul (서울)
Category Samgyetang (삼계탕) & Chicken Dishes (categorized as chicken in API, but actually a nofo (노포) specializing in electric-grilled whole chicken + samgyetang (삼계탕))
Subway Jonggak Station (종각역) (Line 1), 3-minute walk / Gwanghwamun Station (광화문역) (Line 5), 5-minute walk

A nofo (노포) that has been serving nourishing meals to Gwanghwamun (광화문) office workers since the 1960s. While perhaps a bit modest to be called full-fledged yajang (야장), the sight of people enjoying electric-grilled whole chicken at a few tables set up on the sidewalk in front of the restaurant on a summer night is one of the original forms of Seoul's (서울) yajang (야장). For tourists, it's a valuable option to experience "yajang (야장) + traditional nourishing meal" at once.
