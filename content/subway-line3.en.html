Why Line 3 — The Orange Line Linking Palaces, Hanok & Tradition

If Line 2 (#1241) is the loop line of youth and food, Line 3 (the Orange Line) is the line that connects Seoul's history and tradition. Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) · Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌한옥마을) · Ikseon-dong (익선동) · Jongmyo (종묘) — Joseon's palaces and hanok alleys line up along Line 3's Gangbuk (강북) section. And once you cross the Han River (한강), it continues into the luxury and trends of Apgujeong (압구정) and Sinsa (신사). On a single line, you move between 600 years of tradition and 21st-century Gangnam (강남).

This guide organizes Line 3's 8 key tourism stations station by station. It pairs palace / hanok attractions (linked to existing deep dives) with verified eateries and cafes drawn from accumulated data via per-station nearest-mapping. This is the second installment in the subway line series.

Line 3 Station-by-Station Curation — What to Do When You Get Off

🟠 #1. Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역) — Joseon's Main Palace and Seochon

  • 🚪 Exits: Exit 5 (Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) · Seochon (서촌)) · Exit 3 (Cheong Wa Dae (청와대) · Hyoja-dong (효자동))
  • 🍜 Eat: Tosokchon Samgyetang (토속촌 삼계탕) (a samgyetang spot famous among foreigners) · Ppyeotanjip Seochon (뼈탄집 서촌) · Jalppajin Memil Seochon (잘빠진메밀 서촌) (makguksu) · Tongin Market yeopjeon lunchbox (통인시장 엽전 도시락)
  • 👀 Play: Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) (Royal Guard Changing Ceremony) · Seochon (서촌) hanok alleys · Tongin Market (통인시장) · Cheong Wa Dae (청와대) (now open to the public)
  • 🔁 Transfer: None (Line 3 only)
  • cross-ref: #37 Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) · #1110 Seochon (서촌) · #1235 Quiet Seoul (Seochon)

🟠 #2. Anguk Station (안국역) — Bukchon · Samcheong-dong · Insadong

  • 🚪 Exits: Exit 1 (Insadong (인사동)) · Exit 2 (Bukchon (북촌) · Samcheong-dong (삼청동))
  • 🍜 Eat: Cafe Layered Anguk (카페 레이어드 안국) (scones) · Hoard (호아드) (gallery cafe) · Mangata (만가타) · Betekaten (베테카텐) (Western) · Samcheong-dong sujebi (삼청동 수제비)
  • 👀 Play: Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌한옥마을) · Samcheong-dong (삼청동) galleries & cafes · Insadong (인사동) traditional shops · Changdeokgung (창덕궁) · Changgyeonggung (창경궁) (#1235)
  • 🔁 Transfer: None
  • cross-ref: #38 Bukchon (북촌) · #1235 Quiet Seoul (Changgyeonggung · Bukchon) · #1239 600 Years of History

🟠 #3. Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역) — Ikseon-dong Hanok Alleys · Jongmyo

  • 🚪 Exits: Exit 4 · Exit 6 (Ikseon-dong (익선동)) · Exit 1 (Jongmyo (종묘))
  • 🍜 Eat: Seoul Coffee Ikseon (서울커피 익선) (an Ikseon-dong hanok cafe) · Wooz Bakery Ikseon (우즈베이커리 익선) · Ababe Bakery (아베베 베이커리) · Jangsu Samgyetang (장수삼계탕) · Ikseon-dong brunch
  • 👀 Play: Ikseon-dong (익선동) 1920s hanok alleys · Jongmyo (종묘) (UNESCO) · Tapgol Park (탑골공원) · Gwangjang Market (광장시장) (near #582)
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 1 · Line 5
  • cross-ref: #910 Ikseon-dong (익선동) · #1235 Quiet Seoul (Ikseon-dong) · #582 Gwangjang Market (광장시장)

🟠 #4. Chungmuro Station (충무로역) — Gateway to Namsan · Myeongdong

  • 🚪 Exits: Toward Namsangol Hanok Village (남산골한옥마을) · Myeongdong (명동)
  • 🍜 Eat: Mongmyeok Sanbang (목멱산방) (Korean food at the foot of Namsan (남산)) · Namsan Arae Coffee Roasters (남산아래 커피로스터즈) · Chungmuro (충무로) old-school restaurants · Myeongdong (명동) street food
  • 👀 Play: Namsangol Hanok Village (남산골한옥마을) (traditional garden) · N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) (cable car) · Myeongdong (명동) (shopping · Myeongdong Cathedral (명동성당)) · Chungmuro printing alley (충무로 인쇄골목)
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 4
  • cross-ref: #40 Namsan (남산) · #1238 Night Views (Namsan Tower) · #1239 600 Years of History (Myeongdong)

🟠 #5. Dongguk Univ. Station (동대입구역) — Jangchung-dong · DDP · Namsan

  • 🚪 Exits: Toward Jangchung-dong (장충동) · Dongdaemun (동대문)
  • 🍜 Eat: Jangchung-dong Jokbal Alley (장충동 족발골목) (the original jokbal) · Russia Cake (러시아케익) (bakery) · Projec D (pizza)
  • 👀 Play: Dongdaemun Design Plaza (동대문디자인플라자) (DDP) · Jangchungdan Park (장충단공원) · Namsan (남산) trail · Dongdaemun (동대문) night market
  • 🔁 Transfer: None
  • cross-ref: #1236 Summer Night Markets (Euljiro (을지로) · Dongdaemun (동대문)) · #1239 600 Years of History

🟠 #6. Express Bus Terminal Station (고속터미널역) — Gateway to Shopping · the Han River

  • 🚪 Exits: Toward Central City (센트럴시티) · Shinsegae (신세계) · Banpo Han River (반포 한강)
  • 🍜 Eat: Central City (센트럴시티) · Shinsegae Gangnam (신세계 강남) dining · Poseokjeong (포석정) (grilled pork) · Express Bus Terminal (고속터미널) underground food
  • 👀 Play: Express Bus Terminal Underground Mall (고속터미널 지하상가) (fashion shopping) · Shinsegae Gangnam (신세계 강남) (department store) · Banpo Han River Park (반포 한강공원) (Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (달빛무지개분수) #586) · Seorae Village (서래마을) (French Village)
  • 🔁 Transfer: Line 7 · Line 9
  • cross-ref: #586 Han River (Banpo (반포) Moonlight Rainbow Fountain) · #406 Duty-Free Shops

🟠 #7. Apgujeong Station (압구정역) — Luxury · Dosan Park

  • 🚪 Exits: Toward Apgujeong Rodeo (압구정 로데오) · Hyundai Department Store (현대백화점)
  • 🍜 Eat: Yuwon (유원) (grilled beef) · Not Bad Coffee Dosan (낫배드커피 도산) · Kurarie (쿠라리에) · Apgujeong (압구정) · Dosan (도산) fine dining
  • 👀 Play: Apgujeong Rodeo Street (압구정 로데오거리) · Dosan Park (도산공원) (K-fashion · flagships) · Hyundai Department Store main branch (현대백화점 본점) · Galleria (갤러리아)
  • 🔁 Transfer: None (Apgujeongrodeo (압구정로데오) is a separate Bundang Line station)
  • cross-ref: #1236 Summer Night Markets (Apgujeong (압구정) Cucciolo terrace)

🟠 #8. Sinsa Station (신사역) — Garosu-gil

  • 🚪 Exits: Toward Garosu-gil (가로수길) · Serosu-gil (세로수길)
  • 🍜 Eat: Garosu-gil (가로수길) brunch & cafes · Negi Dining Lounge (네기다이닝라운지) (izakaya) · Serosu-gil (세로수길) eateries
  • 👀 Play: Garosu-gil (가로수길) (fashion · select shops) · Serosu-gil (세로수길) (back-alley cafes) · flagship stores · plastic surgery & beauty district
  • 🔁 Transfer: Sinbundang Line (express to Gangnam (강남) · Pangyo (판교))
  • cross-ref: #295 Garosu-gil (가로수길) deep dive

3 Line 3 Day Courses — Riding the Orange Line

Course 1. Palace · Hanok Tradition (Gangbuk)

  • Gyeongbokgung #1 (경복궁) (palace · Seochon (서촌) · samgyetang) → Anguk #2 (안국) (Bukchon (북촌) · Samcheong-dong (삼청동)) → Jongno 3-ga #3 (종로3가) (Ikseon-dong (익선동) · Jongmyo (종묘))
  • fit: Joseon tradition · hanok · palaces. The essence of Line 3. Wearing hanbok recommended.

Course 2. Namsan · Traditional Culture (City Center)

  • Chungmuro #4 (충무로) (Namsangol Hanok Village (남산골한옥마을) · N Tower) → Dongguk Univ. #5 (동대입구) (Jangchung-dong (장충동) jokbal · DDP)
  • fit: Namsan · tradition · night views. Wrap up the evening at N Tower.

Course 3. Gangnam Luxury (Gangnam)

  • Express Bus Terminal #6 (고속터미널) (Shinsegae (신세계) · Banpo Han River (반포 한강)) → Apgujeong #7 (압구정) (Rodeo · Dosan Park (도산공원)) → Sinsa #8 (신사) (Garosu-gil (가로수길))
  • fit: Shopping · luxury · trends. Gangnam high-end.

5 Line Tips — Get the Most Out of Line 3

Tip 1. Line 3 is the 'tradition line'

Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) · Bukchon (북촌) · Ikseon-dong (익선동) · Jongmyo (종묘) — the Gangbuk (강북) section is the heart of Joseon tradition. Wear hanbok and enter the Four Grand Palaces free (#1239). On Line 3 alone, you walk through 600 years.

Tip 2. Cross the Han River and the character changes

Gangbuk (강북) (Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) ~ Chungmuro (충무로)) is tradition; Gangnam (강남) (Express Bus Terminal (고속터미널) ~ Sinsa (신사)) is luxury & trends. You can move between tradition and modernity in one day.

Tip 3. Check your exit

As with Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역) Exit 5 (Seochon (서촌)) · Anguk Station (안국역) Exit 2 (Bukchon (북촌)) · Jongno 3-ga (종로3가) Exit 4 (Ikseon-dong (익선동)), the exit determines which attraction you reach. Check in advance with a map app.

Tip 4. Expand via transfers

Jongno 3-ga (종로3가) (Lines 1 · 5) · Chungmuro (충무로) (Line 4) · Express Bus Terminal (고속터미널) (Lines 7 · 9) · Sinsa (신사) (Sinbundang). Connects across the whole city center and Gangnam (강남).

Tip 5. One transit card for everything

A single T-money card covers Line 3, transfers, and buses. Buy and top up at convenience stores.

FAQ 6 — Common Line 3 Questions from Foreigners

Q1. What's the difference between Line 3 and Line 2?

  • Line 3: palaces · hanok · tradition (Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) · Bukchon (북촌) · Ikseon-dong (익선동) · Jongmyo (종묘)) + Gangnam luxury (Apgujeong (압구정) · Sinsa (신사)). Line 2 (#1241): the youth & food loop line (Hongdae (홍대) · Gangnam (강남) · Seongsu (성수)).

Q2. Which station for palaces and hanok?

  • Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역) (Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) · Seochon (서촌)) · Anguk Station (안국역) (Bukchon (북촌) · Samcheong-dong (삼청동) · Changdeokgung (창덕궁)) · Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역) (Ikseon-dong (익선동) · Jongmyo (종묘)). These three Gangbuk (강북) stations are the heart of tradition.

Q3. Do I get free entry in hanbok?

  • The Four Grand Palaces (Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) · Changdeokgung (창덕궁) · Changgyeonggung (창경궁) · Deoksugung (덕수궁)) are free when wearing hanbok. Many rental shops near Anguk (안국) and Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) stations. (see #1239)

Q4. What's on the Gangnam side?

  • Express Bus Terminal (고속터미널) (Shinsegae (신세계) · Banpo Han River (반포 한강)) · Apgujeong (압구정) (Rodeo · Dosan Park (도산공원)) · Sinsa (신사) (Garosu-gil (가로수길)). Shopping · luxury · K-fashion.

Q5. Is the restaurant info accurate?

  • Since it's based on Naver Map crawling, it's for reference. Re-check operation and location with a map app before visiting (closures and changes possible).

Q6. What about day courses?

  • Tradition (Gyeongbokgung (경복궁) → Anguk (안국) → Jongno 3-ga (종로3가)) / Namsan (Chungmuro (충무로) → Dongguk Univ. (동대입구)) / Gangnam (Express Bus Terminal (고속터미널) → Apgujeong (압구정) → Sinsa (신사)). See the three courses above.
