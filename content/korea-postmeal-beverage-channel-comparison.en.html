Korean Meal-Ending Beverages for Foreign Visitors — Hanjeongsik vs. Home-Style vs. MZ Cafe vs. Mart Comparison

Subtitle: A full comparison of Korea's 4 meal-ending beverage venues, plus the top pick for 5 visitor types

ISSUE 03 · #872 · 2026-06-09

🎯 Why This 4-Venue Meal-Ending Beverage Comparison Guide

Even after reading [the 6-Type Meal-Ending Beverage Overview](#870) and [the ordering workflow guide](#871), the next decision is which venue to pick. Korea's meal-ending beverage venues break down into (1) the last course of a hanjeongsik (한정식, Korean course-meal) meal (free Sik-hye (식혜) or Sujeong-gwa (수정과), the #1 choice) · (2) a home-style dessert drink (free Sik-hye or Maesil-cha (매실차)) · (3) MZ (MZ, Korea's young trend-setting generation) cafes and trend spots (Sik-hye twists, sparkling plum drinks, the Dong-dong-ju (동동주) trend) · (4) marts and convenience stores (편의점, Korea's ubiquitous minimarts) (canned or bottled, budget-friendly) — 4 venues in total. Prices, fan appeal, accessibility, and trends all differ. This guide compares all 4 venues head-to-head, plus the top pick for each visitor type (solo, couple, family, MZ, and budget travelers).

🎯 Top Pick by Visitor Segment

👤 Solo Travelers

  • 1st pick: Marts & convenience stores (24h, budget-friendly, single can)
  • 2nd pick: MZ cafes (solo-friendly, Instagram-worthy)
  • 3rd pick: Home-style lunch (solo dessert)
  • Skip: Hanjeongsik (less suited to solo dining)

💑 Couples

  • 1st pick: Hanjeongsik finale (a classic, romantic way to end a date)
  • 2nd pick: MZ cafes (Sik-hye twists for a date)
  • 3rd pick: Home-style meals (budget-friendly date)
  • Skip: Marts (less romantic atmosphere)

👨‍👩‍👧 Families

  • 1st pick: Home-style dessert (the #1 family choice, comfort-food style)
  • 2nd pick: Hanjeongsik finale (a traditional family choice)
  • 3rd pick: Marts (budget-friendly for families)
  • Skip: MZ cafes (not really "family time")

🎨 MZ Trendsetters

  • 1st pick: MZ cafes (the #1 trend, Instagram-worthy)
  • 2nd pick: Hanjeongsik (the #1 traditional Instagram shot)
  • 3rd pick: Marts (convenience-store budget option)
  • Skip: Home-style meals (not really the MZ vibe)

🎒 Backpackers & Budget Travelers

  • 1st pick: Marts & convenience stores (the #1 budget, 24h option)
  • 2nd pick: Home-style meals (free dessert included)
  • 3rd pick: Hanjeongsik lunch (free finale)
  • Skip: MZ cafes (pricier)

🍱 4-Venue Deep Dive

🍱 Hanjeongsik Finale (Hanjeongsik Final Drinks)

Core value: The finale of a 6-course meal, with free Sik-hye or Sujeong-gwa — the #1 choice.

  • Recommended venues:
  • Shilla Hotel's La Yeon (라연) (Michelin 3-star) — traditional
  • Samcheonggak (삼청각) and Insadong (인사동) hanjeongsik restaurants — traditional
  • Price anchor: Included free with the hanjeongsik course (₩40,000–150,000, ~$31–115, for the full course)
  • Hours: 11:30–22:00
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★

🍚 Home-Style Dessert (Home-style Post-meal)

Core value: A free home-style dessert — Sik-hye or Maesil-cha — with family-friendly appeal.

  • Recommended venues:
  • Baekban-jip (백반집, home-style set-meal diners) in Jongno (종로), Gangnam (강남), and Myeongdong (명동) — budget-friendly
  • Hansot (한솥) and Bon Dosirak (본도시락) (lunchbox chains) — budget-friendly ([see #854](#854))
  • Price anchor: Included free with a home-style baekban meal (₩8,000–15,000, ~$6–12)
  • Hours: 11:00–22:00
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★ (budget-friendly)

☕ MZ Cafe Trend (MZ Cafe Trend)

Core value: The MZ trend — Sik-hye twists, sparkling plum drinks, and the Dong-dong-ju trend.

  • Recommended venues:
  • MZ Korean-style cafes (Seongsu (성수), Hongdae (홍대), Gangnam) — trendy
  • Traditional tea houses (Bukchon (북촌), Insadong) — traditional with an MZ twist
  • Price anchor: ₩5,000–10,000 (~$4–8) per drink
  • Hours: 10:00–22:00
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (MZ, English-friendly, Instagram-worthy)

🛒 Mart & Convenience Store (Mart·Convenience Store)

Core value: 24-hour access, plus the #1 budget-friendly canned/bottled drinks.

  • Recommended venues:
  • Convenience stores (CU, GS25, 7-Eleven (세븐일레븐), Emart24 (이마트24)) — 24h
  • E-mart (이마트), Lotte Mart (롯데마트), Homeplus (홈플러스) — supermarkets
  • Price anchor: ₩2,000–7,000 (~$2–5) per can or bottle
  • Hours: 24h (convenience stores)
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (24h, canned drinks, English labels)

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. "All 4 venues are basically the same": Prices, fan appeal, and trends are completely different. Free = hanjeongsik and home-style / trendy = MZ cafes / 24h = marts.

2. "Hanjeongsik and home-style drinks cost extra": Hanjeongsik and home-style meal-ending drinks are included free.

3. "MZ cafes only serve tea": MZ cafes serve Sik-hye twists and sparkling plum drinks (trend beverages).

4. "Marts only have basic options": Marts and convenience stores offer 24h access, good value, and 12+ choices.

5. "Dong-dong-ju is a cafe drink": Dong-dong-ju is a 6–8% alcohol liquor, served as an alcoholic drink even at MZ cafes.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. Best venue to try first: The hanjeongsik course finale (the #1 free choice) → marts for a budget option.

2. Instagram & vlog: Hanjeongsik Sik-hye (#1) and MZ cafe Sik-hye twists.

3. MZ trend: MZ cafes (#1) — Sik-hye twists and sparkling plum drinks.

4. Digestion & hangover relief: Maesil-cha (marts or home-style meals).

5. Alcohol & pregnancy: Dong-dong-ju — not for pregnant women or drivers.

6. Allergies & pregnancy: see [the disclaimer in #870](#870).

7. Payment & tipping: Card OK / no tipping.

8. VAT refund: Food and beverages are generally not eligible.

⚠ Medical, Safety & Dietary Disclaimer

All 4 meal-ending beverage venues serve everyday foods. Pregnant women and children: Sik-hye, Sujeong-gwa, and Maesil-cha are OK (Dong-dong-ju is not — it's alcoholic). Declare allergies (pine nuts, ginger) in advance. If food poisoning is suspected, call ☎ 1339 (medical, English available). This guide is general information, not medical advice.

Dietary restriction fallback:

  • Halal & Kosher: Sik-hye, Sujeong-gwa, and Maesil-cha = OK / Dong-dong-ju = not OK, contains alcohol.
  • Vegetarian & vegan: 5 of the 6 drink types are OK.
  • Gluten-free: all 6 types are OK.

💰 Essential Tourist Tips

  • Average cost across the 4 venues (as of June 2026):
  • Marts & convenience stores, per can = ₩2,000–7,000 (~$2–5)
  • MZ cafes, per drink = ₩5,000–10,000 (~$4–8)
  • Home-style dessert (with pickles and Sik-hye included) = free (meal ₩8,000–15,000, ~$6–12)
  • Hanjeongsik finishing drink = free (course ₩40,000–150,000, ~$31–115)
  • Payment & tipping: Card OK / no tipping
  • Emergency: ☎ 1339 (medical, English available) / ☎ 1330 (tourist interpretation)
