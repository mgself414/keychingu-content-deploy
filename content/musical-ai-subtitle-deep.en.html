📍 Daehangno — Daehangno, Daehangno Arts Theater, Daehangno Free Theater, Daehangno TOM

Daehangno (104 Daehak-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 대학로 104), Dongsung-dong) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, a place where young people gathered since the days when Seoul National University's College of Liberal Arts and Sciences and College of Law were there. After Seoul National University moved its campus to the foot of Gwanak Mountain in 1975, Marronnier Park (마로니에공원) was built on the site, and theaters for plays and musicals began to open one by one. In May 1985 the Seoul city government designated Daehangno a "Street of Culture and Arts", and in 2004, after Insadong, its second "Cultural District".

  • Opening hours: Open at all times. Closed days: Open every day of the year.
  • Parking: Not possible. Strollers: Not allowed.

Daehangno Arts Theater (17 Daehak-ro 10-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 대학로10길 17), ☎ 02-3668-0007) is a venue aimed at invigorating the performing arts in Daehangno. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it has a 504-seat main hall and a 150-seat small hall; the main hall has fan-shaped seating and a picture-frame stage, with an orchestra pit in the lower stage.

  • Hours, closed days and fees: Differ from show to show.

Daehangno Free Theater (31 Daehak-ro 12-gil, Dongsung-dong, Jongno-gu (종로구 대학로12길 31), ☎ 0507-1370-0661) is a theater that PMC Production (PMC 프러덕션), the Nanta producer, opened in March 2005 to promote the development of Korean original musicals. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it staged mainly Korean original musicals, starting with Nanta as its opening work, followed by Dalgona (달고나) and Music in My Heart (뮤직 인 마이 하트), and as a basement small theater it keeps the audience close to the stage. It is 200 m from Exit 1 of Hyehwa Station.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~22:00. Closed days: Open every day of the year.
  • Parking: Not possible.

Daehangno TOM (85 Daehak-ro 8ga-gil, Dongsung-dong, Jongno-gu (종로구 대학로8가길 85), ☎ 1544-8303) is a multi-venue complex with 2 theaters, rehearsal rooms and the TOM Academy (티오엠 아카데미). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, Hall 1 with 335 seats is on basement floors 1~2 and Hall 2 with 249 seats is on basement floor 3, with performances held throughout the year. It is 220 m from Exit 1 of Hyehwa Station on Line 4.

  • Hours, closed days and fees: Differ from show to show.

📍 Major Seoul Venues — Blue Square, Charlotte Theater, Sejong Center, Chungmu Arts Center, Seoul Arts Center

Blue Square (294 Itaewon-ro, Hannam-dong, Yongsan-gu (용산구 이태원로 294), ☎ 1544-1591) is a cultural complex that opened in 2011. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it has the musical-only Woori Bank Hall (우리은행홀) and the multipurpose Woori WON Banking Hall (우리WON뱅킹홀), plus exhibition space, a Book Park Lounge (북파크라운지), a cafe and a restaurant. It connects directly to Hangangjin Station.

  • Hours, closed days and fees: Differ from show to show.

Charlotte Theater (240 Olympic-ro, Jamsil-dong, Songpa-gu (송파구 올림픽로 240), ☎ 1877-6077) is a musical-only theater that the Lotte Group began building in 2004 and opened on October 28, 2006. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the first musical-only theater in Korea, and it has 1,260 seats. Opera glasses are available for rent during opera performances.

  • Seats: 1st floor 744 seats, 2nd floor 516 seats.
  • Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Parking: Audience 4,000 won for 4 hours (1,000 won per 10 minutes after that), regular visitors 1,000 won for the first 30 minutes (1,000 won per 10 minutes after that).

Sejong Center for the Performing Arts (175 Sejong-daero, Sejong-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 세종대로 175), ☎ 02-399-1000) is a cultural and arts institution established by the Seoul Metropolitan Government and completed in 1978. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it has the Sejong Grand Theater (세종대극장), Sejong Chamber Hall (세종체임버홀), Sejong M Theater (세종M씨어터), Sejong S Theater (세종S씨어터), Sejong Museum of Art (세종미술관) and more.

  • Seats: Sejong Grand Theater 3,022 seats, Sejong M Theater about 609 seats, Sejong Chamber Hall 443 seats, Sejong S Theater about 300 seats.
  • Hours, closed days and fees: Differ by show or exhibition.
  • Parking: 430 won per 5 minutes.

Chungmu Arts Center (387 Toegye-ro, Heungin-dong, Jung-gu (중구 퇴계로 387), ☎ 02-2230-6600) is a cultural complex run by the Seoul Jung-gu Cultural Foundation (서울시 중구문화재단). It opened as Chungmu Art Hall (충무아트홀) on March 25, 2005 and took its current name on June 1, 2016. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the name comes from "Chungmu", the posthumous title of Admiral Yi Sun-sin, and it runs a Grand Theater, Medium Theater Black, Cinema and Gallery, staging classical music, plays, musicals and more.

  • Hours and fees: Vary by show and exhibition. Closed days: Vary by show and exhibition schedule.
  • Parking: Basic 1,000 won per 10 minutes, show audience 5,000 won for 4 hours.

Seoul Arts Center (2406 Nambusunhwan-ro, Seocho-dong, Seocho-gu (서초구 남부순환로 2406), ☎ 1668-1352) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, a cultural and arts complex at the foot of Umyeon Mountain that brings together 7 performance halls and 3 art museums and a museum. It has the Opera House with theaters staging opera, plays, dance and musicals, the Concert Hall with halls dedicated to classical music, Hangaram Art Museum (한가람미술관), Hangaram Design Museum (한가람디자인미술관) and the Seoul Calligraphy Museum (서울서예박물관).

  • Hours, closed days and fees: Differ by show or exhibition.
  • Parking: Free if you leave within 30 minutes, regular 1,000 won per 10 minutes, show audience 6,000 won for 5 hours, exhibition audience 4,000 won for 3 hours.

📍 Daegu — Daegu International Musical Festival (DIMF)

The 20th Daegu International Musical Festival (☎ 053-422-1945, Daegu Metropolitan City (대구광역시) and the Daegu International Musical Festival organization (사단법인 대구국제뮤지컬페스티벌)) is registered as running from June 19 to July 6, 2026, and was held at Daegu Culture and Arts Center (대구문화예술회관), Keimyung Art Center (계명아트센터), Suseong Artpia (수성아트피아), Ayang Art Center (아양아트센터), Daedeok Culture Center (대덕문화전당), Bongsan Cultural Center (봉산문화회관) and other venues. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is a musical festival held in Daegu every summer, presenting official invited works including overseas musicals, creative support works, a university student musical festival and more.

  • 2026 works: 34 works in total, including 13 official invited works, 6 creative support works, 2 special performances, 8 university student musical festival works and 5 reading performances. The program list also includes a performance after the registered period, on July 10~12 (the musical Piano Forest, Daegu Culture and Arts Center).
  • 2026 performance examples: the musical Turandot (June 19~27, Daegu Culture and Arts Center), Into the Woods (July 3~4, Keimyung Art Center), Les Virtuoses (June 25~28, Suseong Artpia), Frankenstein: The Musical Concert (June 26~28, Ayang Art Center).
  • Hours and fees: Differ from show to show.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Showtimes and prices are not in the venue data — for most of the 8 venues, hours and fees are registered as "varies by show".
  2. Charlotte Theater is closed every Monday — Daehangno Free Theater is registered as open every day of the year.
  3. Daehangno Free Theater has no parking — the Daehangno area is also registered as no parking. Daehangno Free Theater and Daehangno TOM are 200~220 m from Exit 1 of Hyehwa Station.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. How many seats does each venue have?

A. According to Korea Tourism Organization registered information, the Sejong Grand Theater at Sejong Center has 3,022 seats, Charlotte Theater 1,260, the main hall of Daehangno Arts Theater 504, and Hall 1 of Daehangno TOM 335. Seat counts are not registered for Blue Square, Chungmu Arts Center and Seoul Arts Center.

Q. Which venues are close to a subway station?

A. According to Korea Tourism Organization registered information, Blue Square connects directly to Hangangjin Station, and Daehangno Free Theater (200 m) and Daehangno TOM (220 m) are close to Exit 1 of Hyehwa Station.
