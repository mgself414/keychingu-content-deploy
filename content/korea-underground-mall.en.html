1. "Shopping = Myeongdong·department stores": The underground mall is the #1 value-shopping spot.

2. "Fixed price only": Many shops allow bargaining for bulk·cash·have cash ready.

3. "You'll get lost": Underground malls are mazes — use exit numbers·zone signs·Naver Map.

4. "Tax refund everywhere": Small-business shops often have no tax refund·differs from department stores.

5. "GOTO Mall is small": GOTO Mall is among the world's longest at 620m·half a day of shopping.
