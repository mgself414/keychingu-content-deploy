🎯 Why a Han River Camping & BBQ Guide?

Han River camping is the ultimate Korea activity for foreign tourists — pitching a tent, grilling BBQ, and soaking in the city skyline, all in the heart of Seoul. Nanji Hangang Camping Ground (난지 한강캠핑장) is the only official Han River campsite in Seoul, but combine it with Noeul Camping Ground (노을캠핑장), Uidong (우이동), and private glamping sites, and you have a complete urban-to-suburban lineup. Keywords like "Seoul camping" and "Han River BBQ" pop up constantly on Reddit r/koreatravel. This is Activity Series #4 — alongside #146 Running / #147 Night Running / #148 Hiking / #151 Camping & BBQ.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Assuming BBQ is allowed anywhere along the Han River

Open-flame grilling in general Han River parks is strictly prohibited. BBQ is only permitted at designated zones such as the Nanji Hangang Camping Ground BBQ Zone (₩10,000–₩20,000, same-day ticket available). At regular Han River parks (Banpo, Yeouido, Ttukseom), packed lunches and convenience-store ramen are fine — but no open flames.

2. Not knowing that Nanji Camping Ground requires advance reservations

Bookings open on the 15th of each month at 14:00 (2:00 PM) for the following month via yeyak.seoul.go.kr — 100% prepayment required. Foreign visitors need a Korean mobile number and Korean bank account; asking your hotel or a Korean friend for help is strongly recommended. The BBQ Zone is the only area that allows same-day walk-up tickets.

3. Assuming gear rental is available

Standard and free camping zones require you to bring your own tent and sleeping bag. Only the Glamping Zone (₩100,000 / ~$72 / ~520 RMB / ~10,800 JPY) provides a tent and bedding — making it the best first-time camping option for foreign visitors. Noeul Camping Ground and Uidong also require self-supplied gear for standard plots. Equipment rental is available only at private campsites.

① Official Campsites (3 Facilities)

1. Nanji Hangang Camping Ground — Standard & Free Camping Zone

Seoul's only official Han River campsite — 83 standard pitches (gravel & deck types) + 36 grass free-camping plots, all inside Nanji Hangang Park (난지 한강공원), Mapo. Han River views and direct access to the cycling path. ₩15,000–₩20,000 (~$11–$14) per night. Bring your own tent and sleeping bag.

  • Location: Hangang Nanji-ro, Mapo-gu — 15 min walk from Line 6 Mapogu Office Station (마포구청역) or World Cup Stadium Station (월드컵경기장역)
  • Reservation: yeyak.seoul.go.kr — opens 15th of each month at 14:00 for the following month / 100% prepayment
  • Signature: Seoul's only official Han River campsite · gravel / deck / grass — 3 pitch types
  • Best Season: Spring & Autumn (March–May / September–November)
  • Why Go: #1 urban camping spot in Seoul. Han River views + cycling access.

2. Noeul Camping Ground (Noeul Park)

Camping ground inside Noeul Park (노을공원), Mapo — Seoul's best sunset and night skyline views. Han River panorama + Noeul Park walking trails + Mapo city lights at night. Advance reservation required from the 15th of each month. ₩15,000–₩25,000.

  • Location: Haneul Park-ro, Noeul Park, Mapo-gu — Line 6 World Cup Stadium Station (월드컵경기장역) + shuttle bus
  • Reservation: yeyak.seoul.go.kr
  • Signature: Sunset + Seoul night skyline + walking trails
  • Best Season: Spring & autumn sunset season
  • Why Go: Combine Nanji + Noeul for a 2-night Mapo riverside camping itinerary.

3. Uidong Family Camping Ground

Family-friendly site at the foot of Bukhansan (북한산) — Dobong-gu, Uidong (우이동). Served directly by the Ui-Sinseol Line. Pair a Bukhansan hike with camping. Kids' play area and BBQ facilities on-site.

  • Location: Uidong, Dobong-gu — walk from Ui-Sinseol Line Bukhansan Ui Station (북한산우이역)
  • Reservation: yeyak.seoul.go.kr
  • Signature: Bukhansan foothills + family-friendly + children's facilities
  • Best Season: Autumn foliage season (October–November)
  • Why Go: Cross-link with #148 Hiking Guide — complete Bukhansan hiking + camping package.

② Glamping & Luxury (1 Facility)

4. Nanji Hangang Camping Ground — Glamping Zone

5-unit glamping within Nanji Camping Ground — tent, bedding, table, and chairs all provided. ₩100,000 (~$72 / ~520 RMB / ~10,800 JPY) per night. The best option for foreign visitors on their first camping trip (zero gear burden). Only 5 units — sells out fast on the 15th of each month at 14:00.

  • Location: Glamping Zone within Nanji Hangang Camping Ground (난지 한강캠핑장)
  • Reservation: Opens 15th of each month at 14:00 — 5 units only, expect quick sellout
  • Price: ₩100,000/night (up to 4 guests)
  • Signature: Tent & bedding included · perfect for a foreign visitor's first camping night
  • Why Go: Luxury camping for foreign enthusiasts and families. Pack nothing — just show up.

③ BBQ Zone (1 Facility)

5. Nanji Hangang Camping Ground — BBQ Zone

26-plot BBQ zone — same-day walk-up tickets available · no tent required. ₩10,000–₩20,000 per plot. Ideal for lunch or dinner grill parties. Best for foreign groups and hweshik (회식, Korean-style group dining/outing). Grill rental and charcoal available separately.

  • Location: BBQ Zone within Nanji Hangang Camping Ground (난지 한강캠핑장)
  • Reservation: Same-day walk-up tickets accepted (advance booking also OK)
  • Price: ₩10,000–₩20,000 per plot (4–8 guests)
  • Signature: Han River views + BBQ · no tent needed · instant access
  • Why Go: The purest Han River BBQ experience — perfect for foreign groups and family grill parties.

④ Free Han River Picnics (3 Facilities)

6. Banpo Hangang Park Picnic Zone

Free picnic area with Moonlight Rainbow Fountain views. No open flames — bring packed lunches, convenience-store ramen, or order chimaek (치맥, fried chicken + beer — the iconic Korean Han River combo). 10 min walk from Line 9 Sinbanpo Station (신반포역). Bike rental ₩3,000/hour.

  • Location: South side of Banpo Bridge, Seocho-gu
  • Food Options: Packed lunches · convenience-store ramen · fried chicken (delivery: 1577-0190)
  • Signature: Moonlight Rainbow Fountain + free picnic
  • Best Time: April–October fountain season + sunset hours
  • Why Go: The quintessential Korean Han River culture experience for foreign visitors (delivery chicken + ramen).

7. Ttukseom Hangang Park Picnic Zone

Jageundung (자벌레) Observatory + direct access to Seoul Forest (서울숲). Line 7 Ttukseom Resort Station (뚝섬유원지역). Outdoor pool, BMX track, and inline skating facilities — the most activity-rich Han River park.

  • Location: Gangbyeon-buk-ro, Gwangjin-gu + Jageundung Observatory
  • Food Options: Free picnic · convenience store · chimaek delivery
  • Signature: Jageundung Observatory + Seoul Forest connection + outdoor pool
  • Best Time: Summer outdoor pool season + spring & autumn
  • Why Go: Best Han River park for foreign families — cross-link with #138 Hongdae Guide.

8. Yeouido Hangang Park Picnic Zone

63 Building skyline + Yunjungno (윤중로) cherry blossoms + abundant food options. Line 5 Yeouinaru Station (여의나루역). Spring cherry blossoms along Yunjungno, summer Han River night views. The #1 first-stop Han River experience for foreign visitors.

  • Location: Yeouido Hangang Park, Yeongdeungpo-gu
  • Food Options: Free picnic · convenience store · chimaek delivery
  • Signature: 63 Building + Yunjungno cherry blossoms + Hangang Railway Bridge night view
  • Best Time: Spring (cherry blossoms) + summer evenings
  • Why Go: Top recommendation for a foreign visitor's first Han River outing. Easy access and plenty of food stalls.

⚙ FAQ — 8 Questions

Q1. How do foreign visitors book Nanji Camping Ground?

Go to yeyak.seoul.go.kr (Seoul Public Service Reservation) — a Korean mobile number and Korean credit/debit card are required. Foreign visitors are strongly advised to ask their hotel concierge or a Korean friend for help. The BBQ Zone accepts same-day walk-up tickets — the most foreigner-friendly option.

Q2. Where do I get BBQ equipment and groceries?

  • Grill rental: On-site store at Nanji Camping Ground (₩10,000–₩20,000)
  • Charcoal & lighter: Available at the on-site store
  • Groceries: Purchase in advance at E-mart (이마트) or Homeplus (홈플러스), or buy on-site
  • BBQ kits for foreigners: Some hotels offer BBQ packages (groceries + reservation assistance)

Q3. Can I BBQ anywhere in Han River parks?

No. Open-flame grilling is prohibited in general Han River parks (Banpo, Yeouido, Ttukseom). Use only the Nanji Camping Ground BBQ Zone or private campsite facilities. Packed lunches, convenience-store ramen, and chimaek delivery are all fine at regular parks.

Q4. How do I order Han River delivery?

Call 1577-0190 (Han River Delivery) → give your location → food arrives in 30–60 minutes. Some operators speak basic English, but using your hotel concierge is recommended. Menu includes: fried chicken, pizza, jokbal (족발, braised pork trotters), and ramen.

Q5. What does the Glamping Zone ₩100,000 package include?

  • Tent (up to 4 guests)
  • Mattress & sleeping bags (4 sets)
  • Table & chairs
  • Electricity (power outlet)
  • Shared restrooms & shower facilities (within the campsite)
  • Grill & groceries sold separately (BBQ add-on option)

Q6. What's the best season?

  • Spring (April–May): Yunjungno cherry blossoms + ideal sunshine
  • Summer (July–August): Heatwave + mosquitoes — best windows are 05:00–07:00 AM or 19:00–22:00 PM
  • Autumn (September–November): Autumn foliage + comfortable temperatures (overall best season)
  • Winter (December–February): Sub-zero temperatures — only the Glamping Zone is recommended (heated facilities)

Q7. Any foreign group camping communities?

Seoul Foreign Camping Community (Facebook) — weekend Han River group BBQ meetups. Meetup Seoul — foreign camping & hiking events. English-friendly.

Q8. What's a good post-camping wind-down?

  • Mapo jjimjilbang (찜질방) — a Korean-style sauna and bathhouse (think: 24-hour relaxation spa) — perfect for showering and unwinding after camping (see #7 Jjimjilbang Guide)
  • Han River dawn ramen & haejangguk (해장국) — restorative hangover soup — see #133 Gukbap Guide
  • Mapo & Hongdae (홍대) cafés & bars — see #138 Hongdae Guide cross-link
