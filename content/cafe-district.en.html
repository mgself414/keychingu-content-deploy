7 Seoul Café Streets to Walk — Seongsu, Yeonnam, Ikseon-dong, Anguk, Mangwon, Hannam, Mullae: From the Station Exit to 17 Neighborhood Cafés

Subtitle: Seongsu, with cafés in old factory buildings; Yeonnam, beside Yeontral Park; Ikseon-dong, an alley of hanok; Anguk and Samcheong, with hanok cafés and coffee in front of a museum; Mangwon, a residential neighborhood; Hannam, with dessert cafés; and Mullae, among the steel workshops. For each neighborhood we wrote the station and exit, the walking range, the busy times (only where there is evidence), and the address, hours, closing days, and prices of 2~3 cafés confirmed open.

ISSUE 03 · #381 · 2026-11-25 · Seoul Café

🎯 How to Use This Piece

In Seoul, a "café street" is not one main road. It is a neighborhood where cafés follow one another as you walk the alleys from a station. This piece picks 7 neighborhoods and first writes which station, which exit, and how far to walk. Then it adds 2~3 cafés to stop at in each neighborhood.

We included only cafés that passed both tests. One: the name appears on a VisitSeoul (Seoul Tourism Organization) or VisitKorea (Korea Tourism Organization) English listing page, or in a Korea JoongAng Daily article. Two: Kakao Map (카카오맵) shows the café open, and blog posts or photos were uploaded in October~November 2026.

⚠ Notation rule: Hours, closing days, prices, and walking times from the exit are the values registered on the Kakao Map place page (checked 2026-11-25). Next to each price we attached the year and month the menu item was registered. Items registered more than 2 years ago, or with no registered price, are left as "check on Kakao Map on the day". Café descriptions come from the outlet in parentheses. Busy times are written only where an article or a listing page states them. Cafés close or change hours often, so look at Kakao Map once more before you set out.

① Seongsu (성수) — Old Factory Buildings and a Café Street

Onion Seongsu is a café in a building that was a small-parts factory in the 1970s. It kept the factory's iron door (VisitSeoul). VisitSeoul describes Scene as a large café on Seongsu-dong's café street.

  • Getting there and walking range: Line 2, Seongsu Station. Onion is a 3-minute walk from Exit 2 and Scene from Exit 4 (Kakao Map). There is one on each side of the exits.
  • Busy times: In a 2019 article, Onion Seongsu was a place where a line formed for seats. About 40% of sales came from foreign customers (Korea JoongAng Daily 2019-08-07). We found no evidence by day or time of day.

Onion Seongsu (어니언 성수) (listed on VisitSeoul) — 8 Achasan-ro 9-gil, Seongdong-gu (성동구 아차산로9길 8), floors 1~2. It has more than 20 kinds of bread, and VisitSeoul recommends the flat white (플랫화이트). Mon~Fri 08:00~22:00, Sat·Sun 09:00~22:00, last order 21:30 (Kakao Map).

  • Prices (registered on Kakao Map): Signature Milk Tea (시그니처 밀크 티) 8,000 won, Handmade Cola (수제콜라) 7,000 won, Vegan Strawberry Latte (비건 스트로베리 라떼) 8,500 won (all registered 2026-10). The registration for coffee prices such as the Americano (아메리카노) is 2024-01, so check Kakao Map on the day.

Scene (쎈느) (listed on VisitSeoul) — 20 Yeonmujang 5-gil, Seongdong-gu (성동구 연무장5길 20), floor 1. The 1st floor is a café and the 2nd floor is a select shop. It lends space to pop-up stores. It has outdoor seating, and pets are allowed inside (VisitSeoul). Daily 10:00~22:00 (Kakao Map and VisitSeoul agree).

  • Prices (Kakao Map, registered 2026-09): Americano (아메리카노) 7,000 won, Café Latte (카페라떼) 9,000 won, Flat White (플랫화이트) 9,000 won, Orange Ade (오렌지에이드) 12,000 won. This is the high end in this piece.

For more Seongsu alleys, there is #1342, which covers a red-brick alley walk.

② Yeonnam (연남) — Alleys Beside Yeontral Park

Folv Yeonnam is on the cherry-blossom path beside Yeontral Park (연트럴파크) (VisitSeoul). You walk north from Exit 3 of Hongik University Station to all three.

  • Getting there and walking range: Hongik University Station (Line 2, Airport Railroad, Gyeongui-Jungang Line), Exit 3. Antique Coffee 7 minutes, Folv 10 minutes, Parole & Langue 13 minutes (Kakao Map).
  • Busy times: Parole & Langue has a waiting line. Reservations can be made in English, Chinese, and Japanese (VisitSeoul). We found no evidence for the other two.

Antique Coffee Yeonnam (앤티크커피 연남점) (listed on VisitSeoul) — 25-1 Yeonhui-ro, Seoa Building, floor 1, Mapo-gu (마포구 연희로 25-1 서아빌딩 1층). The address is in Donggyo-dong (동교동), and VisitSeoul lists it under the Hongdae area as "Yeonnam Branch". The signature items are Dirty & Cream Coffee (더티앤크림 커피), Cherry Chocolat Cake (체리 쇼콜라 케이크), and Dirty & Cream Croissant (더티앤크림 크루아상) (VisitSeoul). Daily 10:00~22:00.

  • Prices (Kakao Map, registered 2026-08): Dirty & Cream (더티앤크림) 6,500 won, Americano (아메리카노) 5,500 won, Soy Latte (소이라떼) 6,500 won.

Parole & Langue Yeonnam Main Store (파롤앤랑그 연남본점) (listed on VisitSeoul) — 8 Seongmisan-ro 29an-gil, Mapo-gu (마포구 성미산로29안길 8). A dessert café in a renovated house. It is known for its square signature pie (VisitSeoul). 13:00~21:00, closed Monday (Kakao Map and VisitSeoul agree). It opens at 1 p.m.

  • Prices: The Kakao Map menu was registered in 2024-01, so check on the day.

Folv Yeonnam (폴브 연남) (listed on VisitSeoul) — 32 Donggyo-ro 41-gil, Bbrick B02, Mapo-gu (마포구 동교로41길 32 비브릭 B02호). It serves pie, French toast, and hand-drip coffee. The pie is baked in a method learned from an artisan in Karuizawa, Nagano Prefecture, Japan (VisitSeoul). Kakao Map says Sun~Fri 10:00~21:00 and Sat 10:00~22:00. VisitSeoul writes 10:00~21:30, so the two values differ.

  • Prices (Kakao Map, registered 2026-10): Americano (아메리카노) 5,000 won, Apple Pie (애플파이) and Fig Pie (무화과파이) 9,500 won each, hand-drip (핸드드립) 8,500~9,000 won. The Kakao Map tags include pet-friendly and terrace.

The whole Hongdae and Yeonnam alley area is in #711.

③ Ikseon-dong (익선동) — A Hanok Alley You Cannot See From the Main Road

VisitSeoul introduces Ikseon-dong as the oldest hanok village in Seoul. The alley starts right at Exit 4 of Jongno 3-ga Station, but it cannot be seen from the main road. On a first visit it is easy to miss the entrance (VisitSeoul). Both cafés carry the Kakao Map tag hanok café (한옥카페).

  • Getting there and walking range: Lines 1, 3, 5, Jongno 3-ga Station, Exit 4. Both are a 3-minute walk (Kakao Map).
  • Busy times: At Dongbaek Yanggwajeom you may have to wait for a seat. Waiting guests get a welcome tea (VisitSeoul). We found no evidence by time of day.

Dongbaek Yanggwajeom Ikseon (동백양과점 익선점) (listed on VisitSeoul) — 17-24 Supyo-ro 28-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 수표로28길 17-24), floor 1. VisitSeoul introduces it as the best-known café in the Ikseon-dong area. The Strawberry Souffle Pancake (딸기 수플레 팬케이크) is the signature item. Daily 09:00~22:00, last order 21:30 (Kakao Map and VisitSeoul agree).

  • Prices (registered on Kakao Map): Souffle Pancake, plain (수플레 팬케이크 플레인) 19,500 won, Strawberry Mochi Souffle (딸기모찌 수플레) 26,000 won (registered 2025-05), Ikseon 1937 Drip Coffee (익선 1937 드립커피) 7,500 won (registered 2026-06).

Seoul Coffee Ikseon (서울커피 익선점) (listed on VisitSeoul) — 33-3 Supyo-ro 28-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 수표로28길 33-3), floor 1. It serves desserts and drinks made with Korean ingredients. The signature item is the Angbutter Rice Bread (앙버터 쌀빵) at 5,000 won (VisitSeoul). Daily 11:00~22:00, last order 21:40, open all year (VisitSeoul).

  • Prices (registered on Kakao Map): Angbutter Toast (앙버터식빵) 5,000 won, Vienna Coffee (비엔나커피) 7,500 won (registered 2024-12), Black Sesame Cream Latte (흑임자크림라떼) 8,000 won (registered 2026-06).

The night pojangmacha and open-air street of Jongno 3-ga is in #585.

④ Anguk · Samcheong (안국·삼청) — Hanok Cafés and Coffee in Front of a Museum

Onion Anguk is on Gyedong-gil (계동길), right in front of Exit 3 of Anguk Station. Walk up toward Exit 1 and you reach the two Blue Bottles on Samcheong-ro (삼청로) and in Sogyeok-dong (소격동). Blue Bottle Samcheong Café is a 3-story building near Gyeongbokgung that looks down on the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art (Korea JoongAng Daily 2019-07-02).

  • Getting there and walking range: Line 3, Anguk Station. Onion is 2 minutes from Exit 3 and the two Blue Bottles are 11 minutes from Exit 1 (Kakao Map).
  • Busy times: In a 2019 article, Onion Anguk had a line on weekday lunch and after dinner, and all day on weekends. More than 60% of sales came from foreign customers (Korea JoongAng Daily 2019-08-07). It is an old article, so we cannot guarantee it is still the same.

Onion Anguk (어니언 안국) (listed on VisitKorea) — 5 Gyedong-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 계동길 5), floor 1. A hanok from the 1920s. It was renovated with the daecheong maru (대청마루) hall floor and the courtyard kept (VisitKorea). The signature item is the Vanilla Bean Latte (바닐라빈 라떼), and the panettone (팡도르) and salt bread (소금빵) are popular. Mon~Fri 07:00~22:00, Sat·Sun·public holidays 09:00~22:00 (Kakao Map and VisitKorea agree).

  • Prices (Kakao Map, registered 2026-09): Vanilla Bean Latte (바닐라빈 라떼) 7,000 won (vegan milk 8,000 won), Americano (아메리카노) 5,500 won, Café Latte (카페라떼) 6,000 won (vegan milk 7,000 won).

Blue Bottle Samcheong Café (블루보틀 삼청 카페) (Korea JoongAng Daily 2019 report) — 76 Bukchon-ro 5-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 북촌로5길 76), floors 1~3. Daily 09:00~19:00 (Kakao Map). In a piece where many places stay open until 22:00, this one closes early in the evening.

  • Prices (registered on Kakao Map): Latte (라떼) 6,900 won (2026-06), Americano (아메리카노) 5,900 won (2025-03).

Blue Bottle Samcheong Hanok (블루보틀 삼청 한옥) (Korea JoongAng Daily 2019 report: a hanok space next to the main building. We treat it as the same place as the store registered on Kakao Map) — 40-3 Samcheong-ro 2-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 삼청로2길 40-3), floor 1. A small hanok next to the main building. The exterior was left as it was and the inside was renovated (same article). Mon·Thu~Sun 11:00~18:30, closed Tue·Wed and the day of Chuseok (Kakao Map). Kakao Map has a CatchTable reservation link.

  • Prices (Kakao Map, registered 2025-11): Coffee Cream Top (커피 크림 탑) 10,000 won.

The alleys toward the Bukchon hanok village are in #018.

⑤ Mangwon (망원) — A Residential Neighborhood to Walk by Day

Korea JoongAng Daily (2026-04-23) introduces Mangwon as a neighborhood for the daytime, with a residential feel. It is two subway stops from Hongdae. It contrasts this with Hongdae as a neighborhood with a night mood.

  • Getting there and walking range: Line 6, Mangwon Station. Anthracite is 3 minutes from Exit 1, Petrol Place 6 minutes from Exit 2, and 604 8 minutes from Exit 2 (Kakao Map). By address, only Anthracite is in Seogyo-dong (서교동).
  • Busy times: 604's buns often sell out before noon (same article). 604 closes at 15:00, so go in the morning. Petrol Place opens at 12:00.

Anthracite Seogyo (앤트러사이트 서교점) (Korea JoongAng Daily report) — 11 World Cup-ro 12-gil, Mapo-gu (마포구 월드컵로12길 11). A converted 3-story house. It plays no music, so it is quiet, and it has a stone-garden terrace that is good to sit on in sunny weather. Prices are a little high (same article). Daily 09:00~22:00, closed on the days of Seollal (설날) and Chuseok (Kakao Map).

  • Prices (registered on Kakao Map): Passion Fruit Ade (패션후르츠 에이드) 9,000 won (2024-12). Coffee prices such as hand-drip (핸드드립) were registered long ago, so check on the day.

604 (Korea JoongAng Daily report) — 49 Donggyo-ro, Jeongha Building, floor 1, Mapo-gu (마포구 동교로 49 정하빌딩 1층). A sandwich and coffee shop known to Mangwon residents. The Seymour Cream Coffee (시모어 크림 커피) is the signature drink (same article). Tue~Sat 08:00~15:00, last order 14:30, closed Sunday (Kakao Map). Monday hours are not registered on Kakao Map.

  • Prices: The Kakao Map menu was registered in 2022~2023, so check on the day.

Petrol Place (페트롤플레이스) (Korea JoongAng Daily report) — 67 Donggyo-ro, Mapo-gu (마포구 동교로 67). Decorated with navy walls, paintings, and plants. It serves a breve coffee, Matilda cake, and a fig-jam tiramisu (same article). Thu~Mon 12:00~22:00, closed Tue·Wed (Kakao Map).

  • Prices: The Kakao Map menu was registered in 2023, so check on the day.

The route that continues to Mangwon Market (망원시장) and the Han River is in #708.

⑥ Hannam (한남) — Between Hangangjin and Itaewon Stations

Passion 5 is right in front of Exit 3 of Hangangjin Station. Follow Itaewon-ro (이태원로) toward Itaewon Station and you reach Itaewon-ro 27-gil (이태원로27길), where sobb is.

  • Getting there and walking range: Line 6. Passion 5 is 3 minutes from Exit 3 of Hangangjin Station, and sobb is 15 minutes from Exit 2 of Itaewon Station (Kakao Map).
  • Busy times: sobb opens at 07:30 on weekdays and weekends, and you may have to wait (VisitSeoul). We found no evidence by time of day.

Passion 5 Hannam (패션5 한남점) (listed on VisitSeoul) — 272 Itaewon-ro, Yongsan-gu (용산구 이태원로 272), floor 1. VisitSeoul introduces it as a landmark of the Hannam-dong area. It says the prices are a little high but the refined interior and desserts make up for it. Daily 07:30~22:00, open all year (Kakao Map and VisitSeoul agree).

  • Prices: Kakao Map lists only the whole cake "Strawberry Waruruk" (딸기가 와르르) at 99,000 won (registered 2026-01). Check the prices of single pieces of bread and drinks in the store.

sobb (소브) (listed on VisitSeoul) — 101 Itaewon-ro 27-gil, Yongsan-gu (용산구 이태원로27길 101), floor 1. The Seoul branch of the café Wiyi (위이) from Seogwipo, Jeju. The café and brunch spaces are separate, and on the terrace you can sit with a pet (VisitSeoul). 07:30~22:00, closed Tuesday (Kakao Map and VisitSeoul agree).

  • Prices: No prices are registered on Kakao Map. Check on the day.

The order for walking from the north end of Hannam Bridge (한남대교) to the foot of Namsan (남산) is in #1354.

⑦ Mullae (문래) — Between Steel Workshops and Studios

In the Mullae Creative Village (문래 창작촌), factory-style workshops, cafés, bars, and restaurants are mixed in among artists' studios. The steel workshops still run (VisitSeoul). We could not find the workshops' closing days or operating hours.

  • Getting there and walking range: Line 2, Mullae Station. Rust Bakery is 9 minutes from Exit 7 and Mariniere 8 minutes from Exit 1 (Kakao Map). The two are about 260m apart in a straight line, so you can walk them in a row (calculated from Kakao Map coordinates).
  • Busy times: We found no evidence.

Rust Bakery (러스트베이커리) (listed on VisitSeoul) — 15 Gyeongin-ro 79-gil, Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구 경인로79길 15), floor 1. It uses an old factory building. It bakes more than 30 kinds of bread and sweets, including cakes, puddings, and pastries, in the shop (VisitSeoul). Daily 10:30~22:00 (Kakao Map and VisitSeoul agree). The Kakao Map tags include rooftop.

  • Prices (Kakao Map, registered 2026-03): Americano (아메리카노) 5,500 won, Latte (라떼) 6,000 won, Milk Tea (밀크티) 6,000 won.

Mariniere (마리니에르) (listed on VisitSeoul) — 8-3 Dorim-ro 141ga-gil, Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구 도림로141가길 8-3), floor 1. A Japanese-style Western restaurant with the concept of a small dining room inside a yacht. By day it is a café serving coffee, tea, bread, and desserts, and at night it becomes a wine bar (VisitSeoul). Kakao Map lists the business type as Western food (양식). 12:00~24:00, closed Tuesday (Kakao Map and VisitSeoul agree).

  • Prices (registered on Kakao Map): Spaghetti Napolitan (스파게티 나폴리탄) 11,000 won (2025-10), Beef Bourguignon Curry (비프 부르기뇽 카레) 13,000 won (2025-11).

The walk through the Mullae steel-workshop alleys is in #1343.

💰 Price Range and Dietary Notes

Price range (based on prices registered on Kakao Map)

  • One cup of coffee: Americano 5,000~7,000 won (Folv 5,000 won to Scene 7,000 won), latte-type drinks 6,000~9,000 won (Café Latte at Antique Coffee, Onion Anguk, and Rust Bakery 6,000 won to Café Latte at Scene 9,000 won). Dongbaek Yanggwajeom's Drip Latte (드립라떼) is 8,500 won (registered 2025-09).
  • Desserts: pie 9,500 won (Folv), souffle pancake 19,500~26,000 won (Dongbaek Yanggwajeom).
  • Meals: Mariniere pasta and curry 11,000~13,000 won.
  • By the registered prices on Kakao Map, Scene is the high end in this piece. For Passion 5 and Anthracite, the outlets wrote that prices are on the high side.

Dietary notes

  • Vegan milk: At Onion Anguk you can swap vegan milk into the latte, cappuccino, and flat white (Kakao Map menu, 1,000 won extra). Onion Seongsu has a vegan strawberry latte. Antique Coffee has a soy latte. 604 has an oat-milk item registered (registered in 2023, so check on the day).
  • Bread and desserts: We could not confirm whether the bakery and dessert menus in this piece contain butter, milk, or eggs. If you are vegetarian, ask the store before ordering.
  • Halal: Of the 17 cafés in this piece, we confirmed halal certification at none. Mariniere's curry is beef (비프), as the menu name says. We could not confirm what meat goes into the Napolitan.

🗺 Routes — Two Neighborhoods a Day

If you group by the same line, you can get around without transferring.

A. A Line 6 Day — Mangwon in the Morning, Hannam in the Afternoon (avoid Tuesday and Wednesday)

  1. Morning 604 at Exit 2 of Mangwon Station (08:00~15:00). The buns often sell out before noon
  2. After lunch Petrol Place (12:00~, closed Tue·Wed) or Anthracite Seogyo
  3. Afternoon Passion 5 at Exit 3 of Hangangjin Station on Line 6 (~22:00)

B. A Line 2 Day — Seongsu in the Morning, Mullae in the Afternoon

  1. Morning Onion Seongsu at Exit 2 of Seongsu Station (weekdays 08:00~)
  2. Late morning Scene near Exit 4 (10:00~)
  3. Afternoon Rust Bakery at Exit 7 of Mullae Station on Line 2 (~22:00). Unless it is Tuesday, keep walking to Mariniere (~24:00), about 260m away

C. A Jongno Day — Anguk in the Morning, Ikseon-dong in the Afternoon

  1. Morning Onion Anguk at Exit 3 of Anguk Station (weekdays 07:00~)
  2. Midday Blue Bottle Samcheong Café near Exit 1 (~19:00). The hanok space is closed Tue·Wed
  3. Afternoon Ikseon-dong at Exit 4 of Jongno 3-ga Station. Dongbaek Yanggwajeom (~22:00) or Seoul Coffee (11:00~22:00)

Set aside a separate half day for Yeonnam from Hongik University Station. Parole & Langue opens at 13:00. The rules inside a café, such as saving seats, mugs, and return stations, are in #1378.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to Mangwon or the Samcheong hanok on Tuesday or Wednesday — Petrol Place is closed Tue·Wed, and so is Blue Bottle Samcheong Hanok. sobb and Mariniere are closed Tuesday, Parole & Langue on Monday, and 604 on Sunday (Kakao Map).
  2. Going to 604 in the afternoon — 604 closes at 15:00. Last order is 14:30 (Kakao Map).
  3. Looking for the Ikseon-dong entrance from the main road — The alley cannot be seen from the main road. Leave by Exit 4 of Jongno 3-ga Station and use Kakao Map directions to find the alley entrance (VisitSeoul).

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. If I go to only one neighborhood? — The café closest from the exit is in Anguk (Onion Anguk, 2 minutes from Exit 3). Ikseon-dong (Exit 4 of Jongno 3-ga Station), Seongsu (Exits 2 and 4), Anthracite in Mangwon (Exit 1), and Passion 5 in Hannam (Exit 3 of Hangangjin Station) are 3 minutes. If you want to see a hanok alley, pair Anguk and Ikseon-dong, one stop apart on Line 3.
  2. Which places open early in the morning? — Onion Anguk opens at 07:00 on weekdays, Passion 5 and sobb at 07:30, and Onion Seongsu and 604 at 08:00 (Kakao Map).
  3. Which places stay open late at night? — Mariniere until 24:00 (closed Tuesday), then the places that close at 22:00. Blue Bottle Samcheong Café closes at 19:00 and Blue Bottle Samcheong Hanok at 18:30 (Kakao Map).
