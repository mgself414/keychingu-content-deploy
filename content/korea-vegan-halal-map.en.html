Korea Vegan & Halal Guide — 12 Seoul Restaurants (Eid, Legume, Balwoo Gongyang, Osegyehyang, Gosari Express) and Prayer Rooms (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: Eid (이드) is one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (VisitSeoul) · Legume (레귬) is a Michelin 1-star vegan restaurant (Michelin page) · Balwoo Gongyang (발우공양) is open 11:30~21:00, closed Sundays (VisitKorea) · Seoul Central Mosque (서울중앙성원) opened in May 1976 (VisitSeoul) · Incheon Airport has 4 prayer rooms open 24 hours (Incheon official tourism site) — as of 2026-10-21

ISSUE 03 · #453 · 2026-05-26

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Kalguksu (hand-cut noodles) sink into a perilla-seed broth while mushroom dumplings steam in a hot pot — a Korean meal simmered without meat (Chorokddeul Restaurant (초록뜰식당), VisitSeoul). This guide is a map for travelers who do not eat meat or fish and Muslim travelers who need halal meals. There are four branches: halal restaurants, temple food and vegetarian Korean food, Michelin-listed vegan and vegetable dishes, and vegan cafes and bakeries. Two prayer rooms are attached. Restaurants were picked only from places listed on VisitSeoul, VisitKorea tourism information, and the Michelin Guide.

The old guide said "30+ KMF-certified halal restaurants." There is no basis for that. The KMF-certified restaurants listed in the VisitSeoul Muslim travel guide (written in 2018, revised in 2022) numbered 10, and the Eid address listed there (Usadan-ro 10-gil 67) is also different from the current address (15). Lists change even within a few years. We dropped phrases such as "kosher restaurant" and "celebrity chef" because we could not find sources for them.

🕌 Halal Restaurants — 3 in Itaewon

  • Eid (이드) — VisitSeoul introduces Eid in Itaewon (이태원) (Usadan-ro 10-gil 15 (우사단로10길 15), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00~21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF). Recognition can change, so check before you visit. Three banchan (side dishes) and a soup are made daily (VisitSeoul English). The Korean edition has two Eid pages: the page revised 2026-06-29 lists 11:30~21:00 (last order 20:30) and 070-8899-8210, while the other lists 0507-1404-8219. The revised page says a Muslim cook is on staff and no alcohol is sold; bulgogi ₩15,000 (~$11) (VisitSeoul Korean edition)
  • ADMS MANDI (아담스만디) — Nokssapyeong-daero 218 (녹사평대로 218), Yongsan-gu (용산구). 11:00~24:00 (last order 23:00), closed Mondays. Yemeni and Arab dishes by an Arab chef. Mandi is lamb or chicken slow-cooked over low heat and served on spiced rice. "Uses only 100% halal-certified ingredients." 02-797-7776 (VisitSeoul, registered 2026-10-13)
  • PB Plus (PB플러스) — Usadan-ro 33 (우사단로 33), 1st floor, Yongsan-gu (용산구). Burgers and pizza made with halal-certified ingredients. Beef cheeseburger ₩7,500 (~$6), pepperoni pizza (M) ₩17,000 (~$13). Daily 12:00~23:45 (page revised 2026-10-19; the 2019 page said 13:00~23:00). 0507-1369-4883 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)
  • Distinction: A "restaurant recognized as halal by KMF" is different from a "restaurant that uses halal-certified ingredients." For the four restaurant categories, see #467 Malaysian Traveler Guide

🍚 Temple Food and Vegetarian Korean — 3 in Seoul

  • Temple food restaurant Balwoo Gongyang (발우공양) — Ujeongguk-ro 56 (우정국로 56), Jongno-gu (종로구). 11:30~21:00, break 15:00~18:00, closed Sundays. Courses: Seonsik, Wonsik, Maeumsik, Huisik, Beopsik. Reserve by phone (02-733-2081) or on the website; non-smoking throughout (VisitKorea). Course prices are not in the tourism information
  • Vegetarian restaurant Osegyehyang (오세계향) — Insadong 12-gil 14-5 (인사동12길 14-5), Jongno-gu (종로구). 11:30~21:00, last lunch order 15:00, break 16:00~17:00, last dinner order 20:00. Closed Thursdays and during the Seollal and Chuseok holidays. Sweet-and-sour plum vegetables, shiitake rolls, seasoned "chicken," vegan tteokbokki (spicy rice cakes). 02-735-7171 (VisitKorea)
  • Chorokddeul Restaurant (초록뜰식당) — Mangu-ro 18-gil 36 (망우로18길 36), Dongdaemun-gu (동대문구). 11:00~20:00, closed Saturdays. Mushroom dumpling hot pot, perilla kalguksu (hand-cut noodles), vegetarian dumplings, rice noodles ₩10,000 (~$7.50), mukbap (acorn jelly with rice) ₩7,000 (~$5). No alcohol sold. 02-2243-7733 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)
  • For more temple food restaurants, see #493 Full Guide to Seoul Temple Food Restaurants

🥬 Michelin-Listed Vegan and Vegetable Dishes — 3 Restaurants

  • Legume (레귬) — Gangnam-daero 652 (강남대로 652), Sinsa Square 2F, Room 207, Gangnam-gu (강남구). Tue~Sat 12:00~15:00 and 18:00~22:00, closed Sun and Mon. Michelin 1 star, vegan and vegetarian, ₩₩. Chef Seong Si-u (성시우) serves a course made only of vegetables. Counter seats, credit cards accepted. +82 50-71365-1567 (Michelin page)
  • Base Is Nice (베이스 이즈 나이스) — Dohwa 2-gil 20 (도화2길 20), Mapo-gu (마포구). Tue~Sun 11:00~15:00, closed Mon. Vegetarian, ₩. Vegetable rice, soup, and side dishes served in a Korean set format; the menu changes every 3~4 weeks. The chef runs it alone, so reservations are required. +82 50-71306-6724 (Michelin page)
  • Gosari Express (고사리 익스프레스) — Toegye-ro 85-gil 12-10 (퇴계로85길 12-10), inside Sindang Jungang Market, Jung-gu (중구). Tue~Fri 11:00~14:00 and 17:00~20:30, Sat 11:00~20:30, closed Sun and Mon. Vegan, ₩. Noodles with a fernbrake (gosari) oil sauce and Taiwanese-style flatbread. Reservations by phone only. +82 2-2039-3140 (Michelin page)

☕ Vegan Cafes and Bakeries — 3 Places

  • Prana Table (프라나 테이블) — Seongsu-il-ro 3-gil 3-9 (성수일로3길 3-9), 2nd floor and rooftop, Seongdong-gu (성동구). Mon~Fri 10:00~20:00, Sat and Sun 09:00~19:00, no closing days. Acai bowl ₩15,000 (~$11), tofu veggie wrap ₩12,000 (~$9). The berry bowl comes with halal-certified granola. 010-9842-5127 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)
  • The Rabbit (더래빗) — Bangbaecheon-ro 4an-gil 38 (방배천로4안길 38), 1st floor, Seocho-gu (서초구). 12:00~20:00. Plant-based bagels and cream cheese, mashed potato bagel ₩4,800 (~$3.60). Alcohol is sold. 0507-1353-7847 (VisitSeoul)
  • Vegan Cafe Dalnyang (비건카페 달냥) — Myeongnyun 1-ga 31-20 (명륜1가 31-20), 2nd floor, Jongno-gu (종로구). 11:00~21:00 (last order 20:30), closed Tue and Wed. Soy ice cream, Thai green curry, vegetable-packed bibimbap (rice mixed with vegetables). 0507-1467-0388 (VisitKorea)

🛐 Prayer Rooms — Itaewon and Incheon Airport

  • Seoul Central Mosque (서울중앙성원) — Usadan-ro 10-gil 39 (우사단로10길 39), Yongsan-gu (용산구), 477 m from Itaewon Station (이태원역) Exit 3. Korea's first mosque, opened May 21, 1976. Open daily; prayer times change with sunrise, so check the mosque website. Sleeveless tops, short skirts, shorts, and smoking are not allowed; you can borrow a skirt or hijab at the changing room next to the guard post. Non-Muslims may enter, but do not raise a camera in front of people praying. 02-793-6908 (VisitSeoul, revised 2026-09-17)
  • Incheon International Airport — 4 rooms. Terminal 1: duty-free zone 3F and Concourse east side 4F; Terminal 2: general area B1 and duty-free zone 4F. Open year-round 00:00~24:00 (Incheon official tourism site, revised 2026-06-22)

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Vegetarian traps: Most kimchi seasoning contains salted fermented seafood (jeotgal), fish sauce, or meat broth, and sundubu-jjigae (soft tofu stew) and dubu-jorim (braised tofu) usually contain salted shrimp. Most bibimbap comes topped with meat or egg, and few restaurants have a separate vegetarian menu (VisitKorea English)
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid in Itaewon (이태원) (Usadan-ro 10-gil 15 (우사단로10길 15), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00~21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF). Recognition can change, so check before you visit. Even some vegan restaurants sell alcohol, like The Rabbit (더래빗) (VisitSeoul)
  • Allergies: Gosari Express (고사리 익스프레스) uses sesame powder, and Prana Table (프라나 테이블) uses granola and hemp seeds (Michelin page, VisitSeoul). Ask before ordering
  • Price range: Won amounts come only from VisitSeoul menu prices (5 places); the three Michelin places have only the ₩ and ₩₩ symbols
  • For a show-it card, see #1300 Dietary Request Manual; for spice worries, see #1398 Non-Spicy Korean Food Map

🧰 Vegetarian and Halal Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Ordering, Getting Around

  • Solo: Legume (레귬) and Gosari Express (고사리 익스프레스) have counter seats (Michelin page)
  • Reservations: Base Is Nice (베이스 이즈 나이스) requires reservations, and Gosari Express (고사리 익스프레스) takes phone reservations only (Michelin page). Balwoo Gongyang (발우공양) takes phone and website reservations (VisitKorea)
  • Payment: Credit cards accepted at all three Michelin places (Michelin page). For the general picture, see #527 Korea's Payment System
  • Ordering: Show the card saying "No meat, fish, egg, or jeotgal (salted fermented seafood), please." For translation apps, see #1339 Ordering Etiquette
  • Getting around: The 3 Itaewon halal restaurants and the mosque are within walking distance of Itaewon Station (이태원역) and Noksapyeong Station (녹사평역) (VisitSeoul). For transit cards, see #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "All halal restaurants in Itaewon are KMF-certified" — Eid (이드) is the one listed with KMF recognition. Adams Mandi (아담스만디) and PB Plus (PB플러스) "use halal-certified ingredients" (VisitSeoul)

2. "The only KMF-certified Korean restaurant in Seoul" — That was wording from lists around 2017~2018. The current Eid English page says "one of the few restaurants"

3. "Tofu and vegetable side dishes are vegetarian" — Kimchi often has jeotgal and sundubu-jjigae often has salted shrimp (VisitKorea English)

4. "Photos are fine anywhere in the mosque" — Avoid shooting in front of people praying (VisitSeoul)
