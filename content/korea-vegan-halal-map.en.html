1. "Vegetarian/halal = not a thing in Korea" — missing Itaewon and KMF: Assuming Korea has none. In reality, the Seoul Central Mosque in Itaewon · 30+ KMF halal-certified restaurants · Indian vegetarian spots · Hongdae vegan cafes form Korea's #1 clusters.

2. "Temple cuisine = generic vegetarian food" — missing its global #1 status: Assuming it's ordinary. In reality, it's a global celebrity-chef cluster: Sunjae by Ven. Jeong Kwan featured on Netflix Chef's Table · Jingwansa temple cuisine designated National Intangible Cultural Heritage.
