Why the Gongdeok Hub — the Mapo gateway where you enter from the airport and scatter out

Gongdeok Station (공덕역) is a quad transfer station where Line 5·Line 6·Gyeongui-Jungang Line·Airport Railroad meet. In particular, the Airport Railroad runs through it, making it an arrival gateway reached directly from Incheon Airport (인천공항), and it is Mapo's intersection from which you scatter out to Yeouido (여의도) (Line 5)·Itaewon (이태원) (Line 6)·Hongdae (홍대)·Yongsan (용산) (Gyeongui-Jungang). Gongdeok itself is also a local food hub flanked by a food alley·the Gyeongui Line Forest Park.

This guide is a new axis orthogonal to the per-line series (#1241~#1249). "Radial in 4 directions from one Gongdeok station" — it weaves together what you get by which line you board, centered on the hub. It is the second edition of the Subway Course Series (Phase 2·Transfer Hub).

Gongdeok Hub 4-Direction Curation — if you transfer here

🟣 Direction 1. Line 5 — Yeouido·Gwanghwamun (river·downtown)

  • 🚇 Direction: Yeouido (3 stops)·Gwanghwamun (광화문) (downtown)

  • Time: Yeouido 8 min

🟤 Direction 2. Line 6 — Itaewon·Mangwon (global·hip)

  • 🚇 Direction: Itaewon (global)·Mangwon (망원) (hip)·Sangsu (상수)
  • 🍜 Eat: Itaewon (FOTO·Kkuttijareudang (꾸띠자르당) world cuisine) · Mangwon (Mangnidan-gil (망리단길)·Duduridupang (두두리두팡))

  • Time: Itaewon 6 min·Mangwon 4 min
  • cross-ref: #1245 Line 6 (Itaewon·Mangwon)

🚈 Direction 3. Gyeongui-Jungang Line — Hongdae·Yongsan (riverside·lodging)

  • 🚇 Direction: Hongdae Ipgu (홍대입구) (foreigner lodging)·Yongsan (I'Park Mall (아이파크몰))·Sogang University (서강대)

  • 👀 Play: the Gyeongui Line Forest Park (Gongdeok~Yeonnam walk·#1235) · Hongdae·Yeonnam (#1248) · Yongsan I'Park Mall

✈️ Direction 4. Airport Railroad — Incheon Airport·Seoul Station (arrival·gateway)

  • 🚇 Direction: Incheon Airport (arrival)·Hongdae Ipgu·Seoul Station (서울역)
  • 🍜 Eat: Gongdeok food alley (Gop (곱)·Seoriwon (서리원)) · Mapo old-timer eateries
  • 👀 Play: Direct connection to Incheon Airport (arrival route·#1249) · Hongdae Ipgu · Seoul Station (#1243) gateway
  • Time: Incheon Airport approx. 50 min·Seoul Station 5 min
  • Foreigner tip: Gongdeok has a direct connection to Incheon Airport via the Airport Railroad — the first base for Mapo lodging after arrival (see #1249 arrival route).

Transfer·Usage Tips 5 — make 200% of the Gongdeok Hub

Tip 1. Gongdeok is an 'arrival-gateway-type hub'

Direct connection to Incheon Airport via the Airport Railroad (#1249). If your lodging is in Mapo·Hongdae·Yeouido after arrival, Gongdeok is your first base.

Tip 2. The 4 directions have different characters

Line 5=Hangang·downtown, Line 6=global·hip, Gyeongui-Jungang=lodging·walk, Airport=arrival. Choose your direction to match your purpose.

Tip 3. Walk the Gyeongui Line Forest Park

A walk in the abandoned-railway park (#1235) from Gongdeok to Yeonnam. The fun of walking instead of taking the subway.

Tip 4. Account for the transfer walk

The 4 lines take 5~7 min by depth. The Airport Railroad is the deepest. Allow extra time.

Tip 5. With a single transit card

All 4 lines with one T-money card. The Airport Railroad regular train also takes T-money.

FAQ 6 — Common Gongdeok Hub Questions from Foreigners

Q1. What are the strengths of the Gongdeok Hub?

  • Line 5·6·Gyeongui-Jungang·Airport quad transfer + direct connection to Incheon Airport (Airport Railroad). An arrival gateway and an intersection that scatters out to Mapo·Yeouido·Itaewon·Hongdae.

Q2. How does it differ from the per-line editions?

  • The per-line editions are "one line, vertical"; this edition is "radiating in 4 directions from one Gongdeok station" = orthogonal.

Q3. Can you come directly from Incheon Airport?

  • Yes. Direct to Gongdeok via the Airport Railroad. If your lodging is in Mapo·Hongdae, it is your first base (#1249 arrival route).

Q4. What is in each direction?

  • Line 5=Yeouido Hangang·The Hyundai / Line 6=Itaewon·Mangwon / Gyeongui-Jungang=Hongdae·Gyeongui Line Forest Park / Airport=Incheon Airport·Seoul Station.

Q5. Is the restaurant information accurate?

  • It is based on a Naver Map crawl, so it is for reference. Re-checking in a map app before visiting is essential.

Q6. Where is worth walking?

  • The Gyeongui Line Forest Park (Gongdeok~Yeonnam·#1235). A walk in the abandoned-railway park.
