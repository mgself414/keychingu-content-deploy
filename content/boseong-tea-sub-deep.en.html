Boseong Green Tea Guide — Daehan Dawon Tea Fields, Korea Tea Museum, Boseong Green Tea First Planting Site, Yulpo Seawater Green Tea Center, Boseong Five-Day Market

Subtitle: Daehan Dawon (대한다원), March–October (months 3–10) 09:00–18:00, November–February (months 11–2) 09:00–17:00, open year-round · Korea Tea Museum (한국차박물관), 10:00–17:00, closed Mondays · Boseong Green Tea First Planting Site (보성녹차 시배지), open at all times · Yulpo Seawater Green Tea Center (율포해수녹차센터), 06:00–20:00 · Nokchagol Boseong Local Market (녹차골보성향토시장), market days on the 2nd and 7th — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Boseong in Jeonnam (전남) is a green tea region. Daehan Dawon, known for tea fields like a green carpet, is a famous tourist spot that visitors come to in all four seasons, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, and the Korea Tea Museum (775 Nokcha-ro (녹차로 775)) is on the same Nokcha-ro road. In Yulpo, on Deukryang Bay (득량만), there is the Yulpo Seawater Green Tea Center, where you bathe in seawater steeped with green tea.

This guide covers only Boseong's tea-related places and its five-day market, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI).

🍃 Daehan Dawon — Boseong's Famous Tea Fields

Daehan Dawon (763-65 Nokcha-ro, Boseong-eup, Boseong-gun (보성군 보성읍 녹차로 763-65), ☎ 061-852-4540) is a farm of over 300,000 pyeong (30여만 평) on a mountain slope that overlooks the Bongsan-ri (봉산리) area. Huge cedar trees stand in rows at the entrance, and if you pass through the tea fields and climb to the 350m peak, you can see the sea in the distance. It is a filming location for the dramas "Summer Scent" (여름향기), "The Legend of the Blue Sea" (푸른 바다의 전설), and "Rebel: Thief Who Stole the People" (역적).

  • Opening hours: March–October (months 3–10) 09:00–18:00, November–February (months 11–2) 09:00–17:00. Open year-round.
  • Cards: Accepted. Parking: Available; large vehicles use the dedicated large-vehicle parking lot.
  • The farm produces a tea named "Bongro" (봉로, 峯露).

Daehan Dawon No. 2 Farm (제2대한다원) — A Less Crowded Tea Field

Daehan Dawon No. 2 Farm (317-94 Ilrimsan-gil, Hoecheon-myeon, Boseong-gun (보성군 회천면 일림산길 317-94), ☎ 061-852-2181) is the second farm of Daehan Daeop Co. (대한다업), and because it is in Hoeryeong Village (회령 마을), it is also called "Hoeryeong Dawon." It is close to Yulpo Beach (율포해수욕장). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the navigation system sometimes guides you to a dead end. You can park at the dead end and walk in, but it is hard to drive in, and locals say to look for the signposts on the road from Hoecheon Elementary School (회천초등학교) to Ungchi (웅치). Cards are registered as "none."

🏛 Korea Tea Museum — Tea Culture Experiences

Korea Tea Museum (775 Nokcha-ro, Boseong-eup, Boseong-gun (보성군 보성읍 녹차로 775), ☎ 061-852-0918) is a building with 1 basement floor and 5 above-ground floors, divided into the Tea Culture Hall on the 1st floor, the Tea History Hall on the 2nd floor, and the Tea Life Hall on the 3rd floor.

  • Experiences: Tea ceremony lessons, tea making, tea food making.
  • Opening hours: 10:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, the days of Seollal and Chuseok. Parking available.

🌱 Boseong Green Tea First Planting Site — Tea Trees of Daewonsa

Boseong Green Tea First Planting Site (506-8 Juksan-gil, Mundeok-myeon, Boseong-gun (보성군 문덕면 죽산길 506-8), ☎ 061-852-1755) is a tea tree grove at Daewonsa Temple (대원사) on Mt. Cheonbong (천봉산), and it is National Important Agricultural Heritage No. 11 of the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs. Daewonsa is a temple that the monk Ado Hwasang (아도화상) opened in year 3 of King Muryeong of Baekje, and in the mid-Goryeo period Master Jajin Wono (자진원오국사) rebuilt the temple and expanded the tea garden.

  • Opening hours: Open at all times, open year-round. Parking available.

🌊 Yulpo — Seawater Green Tea Center and Beach

Yulpo Seawater Green Tea Center (21 Uam-gil, Hoecheon-myeon, Boseong-gun (보성군 회천면 우암길 21), ☎ 061-850-8740, 061-853-4566) is a bathing facility directly run by Boseong County that opened in 1998. You bathe in bedrock seawater pumped up from 120m underground, steeped with tea leaves from Boseong's tea gardens.

  • Opening hours: 06:00–20:00 (last entry 19:00). Parking available.

Yulpo Beach (24 Uam-gil, Hoecheon-myeon (회천면 우암길 24), ☎ 061-850-5448) is a sandy beach 60m wide and 1.2km long, and a pine forest of 100-year-old trees continues from it. It is open at all times, open year-round, and parking is registered as not available.

🛒 Nokchagol Boseong Local Market — Five-Day Market on the 2nd and 7th

Nokchagol Boseong Local Market (42-1 Hyeonchung-ro, Boseong-eup, Boseong-gun (보성군 보성읍 현충로 42-1), ☎ 061-852-3360) is a periodic market that began as a cattle market in the late Joseon period. It runs a permanent market and a five-day market together.

  • Market days: The 2nd, 7th, 12th, 17th, 22nd, and 27th of each month.
  • What is sold: Farm and fishery products such as Boseong green onions (보성쪽파), Beolgyo cockles (벌교꼬막), and Boseong kiwi (보성키위). There is a specialty market for nokdon (녹돈) pork and a specialty goods shop.
  • Opening hours: Varies by shop; the permanent market closes at 21:00. Free parking (about 200 small vehicles), restrooms available.

📅 Boseong Dahyang Festival (보성다향대축제) — May (month 5)

The Boseong Dahyang Festival is held at Korea Tea Culture Park (한국차문화공원) and the Boseong tea fields. The 2026 edition (the 49th) ran May 1–5, 10:00–19:00, free of charge, with tea-leaf picking and tea-making experiences. The 2027 schedule is not yet posted in the Korea Tourism Organization information.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Thinking it is open until 18:00 even in winter — Daehan Dawon closes at 17:00 in November–February (months 11–2).
  2. Booking a museum experience on a Monday — The Korea Tea Museum is closed every Monday.
  3. Trusting only the navigation to reach Daehan Dawon No. 2 Farm — It may guide you to a dead end, and it is hard to drive in. Locals say to look for the signposts toward Ungchi from Hoecheon Elementary School.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. What are the addresses of the tea field and the museum?

A. Daehan Dawon is registered at 763-65 Nokcha-ro, Boseong-eup, and the Korea Tea Museum at 775 Nokcha-ro.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, cards are accepted at Daehan Dawon. Daehan Dawon No. 2 Farm is registered as "none," and the other places have no card information.
