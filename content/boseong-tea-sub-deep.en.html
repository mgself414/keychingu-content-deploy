12 sub-deep Boseong Daehan Tea Gardens and Green Tea Fields for foreigners

Category: A Outskirts/Nature

Parent deep: #675 Traditional Tea·#534 Metropolitan City·#380 Market

Boseong Daehan Tea Garden and 12 green tea fields

1. Daehan Tea Garden (1957·Korea #1 Tea Field)

  • Discrimination: Opened in 1957, approximately 300,000 pyeong (500,000 pyeong data includes outsourced farmland), Korea #1 tea fields, millions of visitors per year
  • Operation: Open 365 days a year / Summer (March-October) 09:00-18:00 / Winter (November-February) 09:00-17:00
  • Admission fee: Adults 4,000 won, Teenagers 3,000 won, Local residents 2,000 won
  • Foreigners visit: visitkorea·TripAdvisor Travelers' Choice
  • kr cross: #675 Traditional Tea·#380·#534

2. Daehan 2 Tea Garden (near Korea Tea Museum)

  • Discrimination: Branch of Daehan Dawon·Secluded compared to Dawon 1·Recommended for family enthusiasts·Trail + Observatory
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Nature Mania·Alternative when Dawon 1 is crowded

3. Boseong Tea Plantation Light Festival (May, November-January)

  • Discrimination: Started in 2002, lights up every year from November to January + May at night·Boseong #1 night view spot·About 1km light trail
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN MZ·No. 1 night view SNS

4. Korean Tea Museum (No. 1 tea culture in Boseong)

  • Distinction: Opened in 2010·Korean tea history + tea ceremony full set·Experience + exhibition + tea ceremony class
  • Experience: Tea ceremony experience 5,000 won·Tea leaf picking (peak season in May)·Green tea making
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Mania·#675 cross

5. Boseong May Dahyangje (Tea Leaf Picking Festival)

  • Discrimination: Every year in early May, approximately 1 week, Boseong #1 Tea Festival·Tea leaf picking + tea ceremony experience + tea ceremony·Approximately 100,000 visitors
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·May right-of-way peak

6. Green tea ice cream, latte, cold noodles (signature food)

  • Discrimination: Boseong authentic green tea food·Green tea ice cream 3,500 won·Green tea latte·Green tea cold noodles 12,000 won
  • Signature: Concession stand and green tea movie theater café within Daehan Dawon
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ JP·CN·EN MZ·SNS #1

7. Bohyang Tea Garden + Borimjeda + Cafe Choru (small-scale tea garden/experience)

  • Discrimination: Daehan Dawon and Boseong small tea garden cluster·Hand-made tea making experience OK·More quiet
  • Experience: Bohyang Tea Garden Tea Ceremony + Tea Picking 1-2 hours 10,000-30,000 won
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

8. Yulpo Seawater Green Tea Bath (Korea #1 Green Tea Hot Springs)

  • Discrimination: The world's only green tea hot spring·Near Boseong Yulpo Beach·Simultaneous bathing in green tea and sea water
  • Operation: 06:00-21:00·Admission fee 8,000 won
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ JP wellness enthusiast·#365 jjimjilbang cross

9. Illimsan Mountain and Jeamsan Mountain (Boseong hiking + tea field scenery)

  • Discrimination: Boseong #1 hiking course Illim Mountain 626m·Azalea colony peak in May·Tea field + mountain scenery full set
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Nature Mania·#511 hiking cross

10. Boseong Botjae + Hwajeongsan Mountain (tea field trail)

  • Distinction: Tea field trail separate from the Daehan Dawon·Botjae 800m·Tea field scenery along the road between Hyeongsan Mountain·Mania spot
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP Authentic nature enthusiast

11. Boseong day-trip + 1 night recommended

  • Access: Yongsan Station → Boseong KTX 3 hours and 30 minutes (transfer to Gwangju Songjeong)·Seoul → Boseong Bus 4 hours and 30 minutes
  • City Tour: Daehan Tea Garden - Tea Museum - Yulpo Full Day 6 hours / 40,000 won
  • Foreigners fit: ★★★ EN·JP 1-night stay recommended (1 day trip tight)

12. Boseong + Suncheon + Gwangju cluster

  • Differential: Boseong-Suncheon (KTX 30 minutes)·Suncheon Bay Reeds + Naganeupseong·Gwangju (KTX 1 hour) + #643 Gwangju sub
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP 1 night 2 days cluster·#643 cross

FAQ 6

Q1. Is Boseong day-trip possible? A. KTX 3 hours and 30 minutes + time is tight·1 night recommended (Yulpo + Tea Museum the next day).

Q2. Daehan Tea Garden admission fee? A. Adults 4,000 won, Youth 3,000 won, Local residents 2,000 won·Open 365 days a year 09:00-18:00 (17:00 in winter).

Q3. Tea leaf picking experience in May? A. 1 week of multi-flavor tea in early May + Korean Tea Museum experience OK·5,000-10,000 won·Reservation recommended.

Q4. When is the light festival? A. Every year from November to January + May at night·Daehan Dawon lights up at night·1km light trail.

Q5. Admission to Yulpo Green Tea Hot Springs? A. 8,000 won·06:00-21:00·World's only green tea + seawater simultaneous.

Q6. Boseong + Suncheon + Gwangju cluster available? A. KTX 30 minutes - 1 hour cluster·1 night 2 days full set OK·#643 cross.
