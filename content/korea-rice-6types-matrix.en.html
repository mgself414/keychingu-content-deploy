Korean Bap 6-Type Matrix — Eomuk-cheolpan, Myeongran-bap, Gonggi-bap, Sot-bap, Yeongyang-bap & Cheese-bokkeum-bap Full Set

> Subtitle: Value-for-money lunch for foreign tourists + MZ trends + hanjeongsik

ISSUE 03 · #897 · 2026-06-09

🎯 Why a Korean Bap Matrix

The foreign visitor's pain point with Korean bap (밥) = "I only know gonggi-bap (공기밥) and no other rice dishes". Korean bap = hanjeongsik (한정식) tradition + MZ trends + value for money. Even under the same label "Korean rice", eomuk-cheolpan (어묵철판) (bunsik (분식) cluster cross · #1 value), myeongran-bap (명란밥) (MZ trend #1 · salty), gonggi-bap (the basic · hanjeongsik cross), sot-bap (솥밥) (hanjeongsik traditional · five-color cluster cross), yeongyang-bap (영양밥) (hanjeongsik five-color cluster), and cheese-bokkeum-bap (치즈볶음밥) (MZ global #1) each have their own devoted fans. Foreign visitors typically don't know that (a) eomuk-cheolpan = bunsik value pick, (b) myeongran-bap = MZ Instagram hit, (c) yeongyang-bap = hanjeongsik five-color dish, (d) sot-bap = the traditional one. This guide = a 6-type matrix + hanjeongsik cross.

🍚 6-Type Deep Dive

🍳 Eomuk-cheolpan (어묵철판) — Value #1

  • Signature: bunsik cluster cross · value #1
  • Ingredients: fish cake + rice + vegetables & seasoning (stir-fried on a griddle)
  • Recommended venues: bunsik shops · MZ Korean restaurants · chains
  • Price: ₩7,000~12,000 (~$5–9) per person
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (#1 value)

🥚 Myeongran-bap (명란밥) — MZ #1

  • Signature: MZ trend #1 · salty · Instagram
  • Ingredients: myeongran (pollock roe) + rice + egg & gim (김)
  • Recommended venues: MZ Korean restaurants · Hongdae (홍대) · Gangnam (강남) · Itaewon (이태원)
  • Price: ₩10,000~18,000 (~$8–14) per person
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (MZ #1)

🍚 Gonggi-bap (공기밥) — The Basic

  • Signature: the basic single bowl · hanjeongsik cross
  • Ingredients: white rice (or multigrain)
  • Recommended: home-style meals · hanjeongsik · MZ Korean restaurants (as a side)
  • Price: ₩1,000~3,000 (~$1–2) per bowl
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★ (basic)

🥘 Sot-bap (솥밥) — Hanjeongsik Traditional

  • Signature: hanjeongsik traditional · piping hot · crispy nurungji (누룽지)
  • Ingredients: rice + beans, mixed grains, lotus seeds + a pot
  • Recommended: hanjeongsik ([#837](#837)) · MZ Korean restaurants
  • Price: hanjeongsik course ₩40–150k (~$30–113) (included) / à la carte ₩12,000~25,000 (~$9–19)
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★

🌰 Yeongyang-bap (영양밥) — Hanjeongsik Five-Color

  • Signature: hanjeongsik five-color cluster · traditional · mixed grains & nuts
  • Ingredients: white rice + mixed grains, beans, nuts, carrot, shiitake
  • Recommended: hanjeongsik · Jeonju (전주) hanjeongsik
  • Price: free with hanjeongsik / à la carte ₩10,000~20,000 (~$8–15)
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★

🧀 Cheese-bokkeum-bap (치즈볶음밥) — MZ Global #1

  • Signature: MZ global #1 · cheese fusion · trend
  • Ingredients: fried rice + cheese, egg, kimchi (optional)
  • Recommended: MZ Korean restaurants · cheese cafes · Hongdae · Gangnam
  • Price: ₩12,000~20,000 (~$9–15) per person
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (MZ global #1)

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. "Bap = one thing": Bap = a 6-type matrix.

2. "Gonggi-bap = free": Gonggi-bap = ₩1,000~3,000 (~$1–2) (free when included in hanjeongsik).

3. "Myeongran-bap = just salty": Myeongran = MZ Instagram #1 · ₩10–18k (~$8–14).

4. "Sot-bap = ordinary": Sot-bap = hanjeongsik traditional · crispy nurungji.

5. "Cheese-bokkeum-bap = foreign food": Cheese-bokkeum-bap = authentic Korean MZ fusion.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. Top starter picks for foreign visitors: eomuk-cheolpan + myeongran-bap + cheese-bokkeum-bap.

2. Instagram & vlog: myeongran (#1) · cheese-bokkeum-bap (#1).

3. MZ trend: myeongran · cheese-bokkeum-bap.

4. Value for money: eomuk-cheolpan (#1).

5. Hanjeongsik: sot-bap · yeongyang-bap.

6. Allergies & pregnancy: declare fish roe, seafood, dairy, and nuts in advance.

7. Payment & tips: Card OK / No tipping.

8. VAT refund: food and beverages generally not refundable.

⚠ Medical, Safety & Dietary Disclaimer

Bap = ordinary food. Pregnant women & children = OK (moderate myeongran saltiness · cheese dairy). Declare allergies (fish roe, seafood, dairy, nuts, soy sauce) in advance. If food poisoning is suspected ☎ 1339.

Dietary restriction fallback:

  • Halal & Kosher: some of the 6 types OK · confirm in advance at MZ Korean restaurants.
  • Vegetarian & vegan: gonggi, sot, yeongyang-bap = vegetarian OK / myeongran, cheese-bokkeum-bap = confirm in advance.
  • Gluten-free: soy sauce = substitute Tamari · most of the 6 types are gluten-free.
