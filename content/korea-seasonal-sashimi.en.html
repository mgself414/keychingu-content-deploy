Korean Seasonal Raw Fish Monthly Guide — What's Best to Eat Now?

> Subtitle: 12-Month Matrix for Foreign Tourists + Top 5 Markets + Hoe vs. Sashimi Differences for Enthusiasts

ISSUE 03 · #789 · 2026-06-07

🎯 Why a Korean Seasonal Raw Fish Monthly Guide?

Foreign tourists' #1 pain point regarding Korean raw fish (생선회, saengseonhoe) = "What's good to eat now?" The essence of Korean raw fish culture is seasonality (spawning season, fat content, and peak flavor differ for each fish species). The difference from Japanese sushi: Korea focuses on hwareo (활어, live fish) + chojang (초장, spicy gochujang-based sauce) + ssam (쌈, wrap) culture, while Japan centers on aged fish, soy sauce, and wasabi. Even the same Gwang-eo (광어, Olive Flounder) tastes and costs differently in December vs. July. SEO monthly traffic data shows Bangeo (방어, Yellowtail) peaks in January/December, Jeon-eo (전어, Gizzard Shad) in September-October, and Mineo (민어, Croaker) in July.

🌸 Seasonal Deep Dive: 4 Sections

🌱 Spring (March-May) — Fresh and Firm Flavors

This season, Dodari (도다리, Frog Flounder), Domi (도미, Sea Bream), and Nong-eo (농어, Sea Bass) become firm after their spawning season. March's Dodari Ssukguk (도다리쑥국, frog flounder and mugwort soup) is a spring delicacy in Korea. In April, Jjukkumi (주꾸미, Webfoot Octopus) is full of roe, with festivals held in Tongyeong (통영) and Seocheon (서천). May's Hanchi (한치, Squid) is caught by night jigging in Tongyeong (통영) and Jeju (제주). A key point for foreigners to know = "hwareo (활어, live fish) vs. aged" — Korean raw fish is primarily hwareo (활어), with aged fish being a separate menu item.

☀ Summer (June-August) — Fat, Nourishment, and Caution

From June, Chamchi Baetsal (참치 뱃살, Tuna belly) and Mineo (민어, Croaker) start to gain fat. Mineo (민어) is a Korean summer health food (historically enjoyed by Joseon Dynasty nobility). Be cautious of food poisoning in July-August — freshness indicators: (1) clear eyes, (2) faint fishy smell, (3) firm flesh. Recommended for foreigners = lunch hoe-deopbap (회덮밥, raw fish over rice) (good value) → evening hwareo-hoe (활어회, live fish sashimi). Mineo-hoe (민어회) is the top choice for enthusiasts during the Sambok (삼복, the three hottest days of summer) season.

🍁 Autumn (September-November) — Jeon-eo and Bangeo Golden Age

The proverb "Even a runaway daughter-in-law returns for autumn gizzard shad smell" highlights the deliciousness of September's Jeon-eo (전어), recommended both as raw fish and grilled. Bangeo (방어, Yellowtail) season begins in October, reaching its peak in November at Jagalchi (자갈치) and Guryongpo (구룡포). Gwamegi (과메기, semi-dried Pacific saury/herring) is a specialty of Guryongpo (구룡포) from November to February, eaten wrapped in gim (김, seaweed) with green onions and miyeok (미역, sea mustard).

❄ Winter (December-February) — Bangeo Golden Age + Full Meal Set

December-February = peak Bangeo (방어) season. Busan's Jagalchi Market (자갈치 시장) offers a piece of raw fish + chojang (초장) + ssam (쌈), while Noryangjin Market (노량진 시장) has a wholesale atmosphere. A Korean-style raw fish meal course includes: (1) raw fish + chojang (초장)·ganjang (간장, soy sauce)·wasabi, (2) ssam (쌈) vegetables·garlic·chili, (3) maeuntang (매운탕, spicy fish stew) made from the leftover bones after filleting, and (4) rice. Gwang-eo (광어) and Domi (도미) in January-February are often farmed, but some restaurants indicate natural (자연산, jayeonsan) fish.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Hoe (회) = Japanese sushi": Korean hoe (회) is hwareo (활어, live fish) + chojang (초장, gochujang-based sauce) + ssam (쌈, wrap) culture. Japanese sushi is aged fish + rice + soy sauce·wasabi. The texture, sauces, and presentation are all different. It's essential to understand that hoe (회) ≠ sashimi.

2. "All fish species are OK year-round": Outside of peak season, farmed (양식, yangsik) or frozen fish may be used. Check the menu for "jayeonsan (자연산, natural) vs. yangsik (양식, farmed)" labels. Prices can also differ by 1.5-2 times.

3. "Gwang-eo (광어) = flounder": Korean Gwang-eo (광어) is Olive Flounder (Paralichthys olivaceus). Do not confuse it with Halibut. If there's no English menu, say "Olive flounder, please" or show a picture.

4. "Just eat raw fish and leave": A Korean-style raw fish course includes raw fish + maeuntang (매운탕, spicy fish stew) + rice. Maeuntang (매운탕) is made from the bones and head leftover after filleting the fish. Order "maeuntang (매운탕) add-on" or "hoe (회) + maeuntang (매운탕) set" at the restaurant.

5. "Price = per fish": Markets usually price by kg (kilogram) or geun (근, 600g). After purchasing fish at the market, you move to a restaurant to have it prepared (sangcharimbi (상차림비, table setting fee) is separate, ₩5,000 (~$3.7 / ~27 RMB / ~530 JPY) to ₩10,000 (~$7.4 / ~54 RMB / ~1,060 JPY) per person). Japanese restaurants charge per person. Confusion over units can lead to overcharging.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Top season for foreigners' raw fish: Bangeo (방어, Yellowtail) season (October-February) + Jeon-eo (전어, Gizzard Shad) season (September-October) are prime for enthusiasts.

2. Best value season for raw fish: January, February, June, September (off-peak, direct market visit: ₩25,000 (~$18.5 / ~135 RMB / ~2,650 JPY) to ₩40,000 (~$29.6 / ~216 RMB / ~4,240 JPY) per person).

3. Hwareo (활어, live fish) vs. aged: Korea primarily serves hwareo (활어). Aged fish restaurants are marked "Aged Hoe (회) Specialty" (many in Gangnam (강남) and Seoul (서울) basement Japanese restaurants).

4. Raw fish + alcohol pairing: Soju (소주, Korean distilled spirit) > Cheongha (청하, Korean rice wine) > Makgeolli (막걸리, Korean traditional rice wine). Beer is not recommended (masks the flavor). Japanese restaurants may offer sake.

5. Market vs. restaurant difference: Markets = purchase fish, then move to a table setting restaurant (cheaper, local experience); Restaurants = full course (convenient but 1.5x more expensive).

6. Raw fish allergies/pregnancy: Must declare shellfish allergies in advance. Pregnant individuals should be cautious of mercury (tuna belly), and cooked maeuntang (매운탕) is recommended.

🛒 Top 5 Recommended Markets

  • 🥇 Jagalchi Market (자갈치 시장, Busan) — No. 1 raw fish market year-round, Bangeo (방어)·Domi (도미)·Galchi (갈치) peak in autumn/winter. For details, see [#590 Jagalchi deep dive](590_jagalchi_market_deep_dive.md)
  • 🥈 Noryangjin Fisheries Market (노량진수산시장, Seoul) — Dawn auction (03:00~) + 24-hour raw fish. Dawn wholesale info [#587 Dawn Wholesale deep dive](587_dawn_wholesale_market_deep_dive.md)
  • 🥉 Tongyeong Central Live Fish Market (통영중앙활어시장, Tongyeong, Gyeongnam) — No. 1 for spring Domi (도미)·summer Nong-eo (농어)·May Hanchi (한치)
  • Sorae Pogu (소래포구, Incheon) — Enthusiasts' choice for spring Dodari (도다리)·autumn Jeon-eo (전어) season, good for a day trip from the Seoul Metropolitan Area
  • Guryongpo (구룡포, Pohang, Gyeongbuk) — Home of autumn/winter Gwamegi (과메기)·Bangeo (방어), No. 1 for enthusiasts from November-February

💰 Essential Tourist Tips

  • Raw fish price per person (2025 standard): Market ₩25,000-₩40,000 / Restaurant ₩50,000-₩80,000 / Japanese restaurant ₩80,000~
  • Payment: Market = cash·card mixed (cash recommended for small stalls), Restaurants·Japanese restaurants = card OK
  • Food poisoning·Emergency: ☎ 1339 (Medical Call Center, English available) · ☎ 1330 (Tourist Information & Interpretation)
  • Raw fish + Maeuntang (매운탕) course budget (for 2 people): Market ₩80,000-₩120,000 / Restaurant ₩150,000-₩200,000
  • Allergy declaration: Cross-contamination with shellfish·shrimp possible. Declare "I'm allergic to shrimp" in advance.
  • No live fish transport: Live fish cannot be taken out of the country due to quarantine. Filleted raw fish also has duty-free import restrictions (quarantine intensity varies by country, check your country's regulations in advance) — best enjoyed at the restaurant.

> ⚠ Medical·Allergy Disclaimer: This guide provides general information and is not medical or allergy advice. Pregnant individuals, allergy sufferers, and those with weakened immune systems should consult a specialist beforehand. Maeuntang (매운탕) uses a common pot, so cross-contamination with shrimp·clams is possible; allergy sufferers should request separate preparation or avoid it.
