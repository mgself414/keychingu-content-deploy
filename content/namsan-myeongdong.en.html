🎯 Why a Namsan-Myeongdong-Only Guide

Namsan (남산)'s N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) is Seoul's No. 1 night-view landmark — the most recognized spot among foreign visitors. It connects on foot to Myeongdong (명동) — night views + shopping + K-beauty + late-night snacks. The top-pick night route for foreign visitors on a one-night stay.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Cable car vs. on foot vs. taxi — the cable car is ₩14K round trip and easiest. On foot takes 30 min (a hike). A Kakao Taxi runs ₩7K–10K.

2. N Seoul Tower observatory vs. free outdoor view — the free outdoor view is fine. The observatory costs an extra ₩16K (per person). Photos from outside are plenty good.

3. Check for operational changes from the weight of the Locks of Love — the locks are occasionally partly removed due to their weight. Check Instagram or the official site beforehand.

⚙ FAQ

Q1. Cable car hours and reservations?

A. The cable car operates 10:00–23:00, every day. No reservation needed — walk-ins OK. ₩14K round trip / ₩11K one way. Lines can run 30 min–1 hr (weekend evenings).

Q2. What about the N Seoul Tower observatory?

A. The observatory costs ₩16K (per person). The free outdoor view is also fine. Night photos are plenty good from outside.

Q3. What's a good Myeongdong combo course?

A. 16:00 Myeongdong Cathedral → 16:30 Myeongdong shopping (#63) → 17:30 cable car → 18:30 N Seoul Tower → 21:00 Hankook (combine with #155).

Q4. Recommendations by season?

A. Spring (cherry blossoms in April): Namsan cherry blossoms + night view, the top pick. Summer: cool sunsets. Fall (foliage in November): fall colors + night view. Winter: the best night views, but dress warm.

Q5. Do I need a reservation for Hankook's N Seoul Tower branch?

A. Reserve 2–3 weeks ahead (see #155). Sunset and night-view hours are the most popular.
