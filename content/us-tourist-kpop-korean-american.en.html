⚠ 3 common mistakes American tourists make

1. "K-pop headquarters are just headquarters" — Goods, concerts, mania infrastructure unknown

The most common mistakes made by first-time K-pop fans. HYBE, SM, JYP, and YG headquarters are recognized as simple company buildings. In fact, you can experience the full Mania Signature Goods Shop + Cafe + Photo Zone + Concert Limited Store in each building . Weverse (BTS, New Jeans, Seventeen, etc.) store + SM Town artium (COEX, NCT, aespa, EXO) + JYP and YG office building store. Concert season (June-August, November-December) For the full Mania experience, reservations made through Interpark English are recommended 1-3 months in advance.

2. "Korean food = the same as a Korean-American restaurant" — I don't know the difference from mainland Korean food

Korean American (1, 2, 3 years old) Common mistakes when visiting Korea for the first time. LA, New York, NJ Koreatown restaurants = recognized as Korean food from the mainland of Korea. In reality, Korean food in mainland Korea is more diverse than Korean-American restaurants in LA and New York + 1/2 to 1/3 the price + many seasonal and regional signatures. For nostalgia and full experience Gwangjang Market drug gimbap, bindae rice cake, and twisted kkwabaegi + Myeong-dong and Insa-dong Korean food + Michelin-starred Korean food + market gourmet Full experience recommended. When visiting a Korean-American family, the first-generation perfume experience is ranked #1 when accompanied by first-generation parents.

3. "English-friendly only in Itaewon" — Awareness of English infrastructure spreading throughout Korea

American tourists perceive English-friendly routes as limited to Itaewon and Gangnam. In reality, Korea's English-friendly infrastructure will expand explosively from 2020 to 2025 — Myeongdong, Hongdae, Jongno, Seongsu, and Jamsil all offer 70% English menus + 100% English information + 99% payment through Apple Pay, Visa, and Master. Independent travel 80% US segment offers full experience throughout Korea in English. Limited awareness of Itaewon → Recommended expansion to full experience in Myeongdong, Hongdae, Jamsil, and Seongsu.

② Korean American Perfume (4)

5. Gwangjang Korean Food Heritage

  • Signature: No. 1 Korean-American perfume. Drug gimbap, Bindaetteok, Twist, Jinokhwa chicken — mainland signatures that cannot be found in Koreatowns in LA and New York.
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ — 80% English menu, point to photo
  • Recommended menu: Drug kimbap + banquet noodles, mung bean bindaetteok + makgeolli, Jinokhwa chicken + kalguksu, garlic
  • Tip: 3-5 minutes walk from Jongno 5-ga Station (Line 1). Line 11-14:00 / Quiet from 14-17:00. A full experience of nostalgia when accompanied by a first-generation Korean-American family.

6. Myeongdong·Insadong Hanjeongsik

  • Signature: Korean Korean meal course + ribs and bulgogi — Family Signature. No. 1 Korean-American family gathering.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — English menu, English service, full Korean food set
  • Recommended menu: Korean cuisine course (5-10 courses, 50,000-150,000 won/person), ribs, bulgogi, bibimbap
  • Tip: Myeongdong — within walking distance of Myeongdong Station (Line 4). Insadong — Anguk Station (Line 3) or Jongno 3-ga Station (Lines 1, 3, and 5). Reservations recommended for families of 4 to 6 people.

7. Bukchon Hanbok + Gyeongbokgung

  • Signature: No. 1 visit for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd generation Korean American families. Hanbok + Gyeongbokgung + Bukchon cluster full experience.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — English service, family size
  • Recommended route: Hanboknam and Oya Hanbok rental → Gyeongbokgung Palace admission fee waived → Bukchon 8 scenic spots → Insa-dong
  • Tip: 5-10 minutes walk from Anguk Station (Line 3). Gyeongbokgung Palace admission fee is waived if you wear Hanbok. Full-experience one-day course for families aged 1, 2, and 3 years old.

8. Samcheong-dong Hanok Cafes

  • Signature: Korean-American Instagram signature. 1920s Hanok + Cafe Onion, Blue Bottle Samcheong-dong, Samcheong 1 and 2, etc.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ — English and Japanese response, Instagram cluster
  • Recommended category: Cafe Onion Samcheong (Hanok) + Blue Bottle Samcheong-dong + Hanok bakery cluster
  • Tip: 10-15 minutes walk from Anguk Station (Line 3). Bukchon, Samcheong-dong, Insa-dong bundled full day (Hanbok → Gyeongbokgung → Bukchon → Samcheong-dong Cafe → Insa-dong).
