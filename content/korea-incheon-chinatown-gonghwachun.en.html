🍜 Gonghwachun (1905~ originator of Jajangmyeon) deep

Signature

  • Launched: 1905 (Qing Dynasty Street, Incheon)·Origin of Korean Jajangmyeon
  • Store: Gonghwachun Museum (building in 1908, nationally registered cultural property), Gonghwachun Restaurant
  • Signer Menu: Jajangmyeon (Korea #1)·Jjambbong·Sweet and sour pork·4 major premium dumplings (grilled dumplings, steamed dumplings, water dumplings, fried dumplings)
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (English menu/pick-up/payment card)
  • Price: Jajangmyeon 8,000~14,000 Won, Jjamppong 10,000~16,000 Won, Sweet and Sour Pork 25,000~45,000 Won

Foreign Pain Points

  • "Jajangmyeon = the same everywhere" Misconception·Gonghwachun = Original + Signer Matrix
  • Line line 30 minutes to 1 hour·Weekday lunch recommended
  • Gonghwachun Museum: 1908 building, nationally registered cultural property, 1905~ Jajang-myeon history cover

Mania Fair

  • Jajangmyeon + Sweet and Sour Pork = Korean Mania Matrix #1
  • Jjamppong + Dumpling = Korean Mania Matrix #2
  • Jajangmyeon + Kakdugi ([#828 cross](#828)) = Korean standard pair

⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Chinatown = Small Street": Korea #1·1884~·140 years+ History·Romance of the Three Kingdoms mural + Jayu Park + Consulate cluster.

2. "Jajangmyeon = the same everywhere": Gong Hwa-chun (1905~ original) = Korean Mania Matrix #1.

3. "Gonghwachun Line = Small Store": Gonghwachun Museum + Restaurant = 30 minutes to 1 hour line · Weekday lunch recommended.

4. "Romance of the Three Kingdoms Mural = Simple Mural": 160m·77 Murals·#1 on Instagram·MZ + Foreign Mania Matrix.

5. "Jayu Park = General Park": 1888~ Korea #1 Modern Park·1882 Monument to the 100th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations between Korea and the United States.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. No. 1 introduction for foreigners: Gonghwachun Jajangmyeon + Three Kingdoms mural + Jayu Park + Museum.

2. Gonghwachun Line: 30 minutes to 1 hour·Weekday lunch recommended·Museum + restaurant separate admission.

3. Mooncake return gift: Mid-Autumn Festival (September), OK as usual, OK for vacuum-packed checked baggage.

4. Airport Transfer Mania: Airport Railroad 1 hour·6h+ transfer = 2-3h Mania Matrix.

5. Allergy: Jajangmyeon, Jjamppong, Sweet and Sour Pork = Soybean (soy sauce), shrimp, pork, beef, wheat allergen prior notice.

6. Recommended persona: Family·Senior·Mania = Gonghwachun + Museum / MZ·Insta = Romance of the Three Kingdoms mural + Cafe.

⚠ Medical/Safety/Dietary disclaimer

Jajangmyeon, Jjamppong, Sweet and Sour Pork = Soybean (soy sauce), shrimp, pork, beef, wheat allergen. Pregnant women, high blood pressure, kidney patients = Be careful of frequency. Emergency ☎ 1339.

Dietary Restrictions:

  • Halal·Kosher: Jajangmyeon·Jjamppong·Sweet and sour pork = Non-halal (pork·soy sauce) / Vegetarian option in some stores
  • Vegetarian·Vegan: Jajangmyeon vegetarian option·**Sweet and sour pork = Vegan
  • Gluten free: Jajangmyeon = Contains gluten (flour)·**Some stores
