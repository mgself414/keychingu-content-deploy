Hongdae shopping full guide for foreigners

MZ, Indie, Trendy, Vintage 4 axes — Hongdae, Sangsu, Hapjeong, Yeonnam shopping cluster 20 shopping cluster matrix

Hongdae is the mecca of Korean MZ and indie fashion. K-fashion, indie brand, vintage, souvenirs, and K-pop goods are dense in the main street, Sangsu, Hapjeong, and Yeonnam 4 sub-clusters. This guide includes 20 places that fit the foreign segment·fashion lover·vintage hunter·MZ shopper persona + daily movement matrix.

1. Hongdae shopping scene

4 sub-clusters

  • Hongdae Main Street (Hongik University Station Exit 9): Trendy brands·K-fashion·souvenirs
  • Constant: Indie designer·vintage·sample sale
  • Hapjeong: Cost-effectiveness, SPA, beauty
  • Yeonnam: Alley, indie designer, design props

MZ Trends

  • Origin of many K-fashion brands (Ader Error·Open YY·Roh Yoonhee, etc.)
  • No. 1 in the vintage market in Korea
  • K-pop goods (multiple stores + Dongdaemun wholesale linkage)
  • Indie design brand Korea’s largest cluster

2. K-Fashion Brand (Sangsu, Hongdae, Yeonnam) ★ No. 1 among foreigners

1. Ader Error (Apgujeong Main Store + Hongdae Store)

  • Signature: Korean contemporary brand, popular in the US and Europe
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ (English fluent, foreigner regular)
  • Price: ₩100,000~500,000

2. Roh Yoonhee (Constant)

  • Signature: Korean contemporary/minimal
  • Foreigner fit: ★★

3. Andersson Bell (constant)

  • Signature: Scandi/Korean fusion
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ (global brand)

4. Open YY (constant)

  • Signature: K-pop stars often wear
  • Foreigner fit: ★★ (K-pop fan fit)

5. Mardi Mercredi (Hapjeong)

  • Signature: Daisy print·MZ signature
  • Foreigner fit: ★★

7. Standard daily route (3 patterns)

Pattern A: K-fashion mania

  • 11:00 Sangsu K-brand hop (Roh Yoonhee·Andersson Bell·Open YY)
  • 14:00 Lunch (Sangsu Korean food or Hapjeong brunch)
  • 15:30 Ader Error·Hongdae Main
  • 17:30 Hapjeong SPA + Beauty

Pattern B: Vintage hunter

  • 10:00 Dongmyo Old Market (morning peak)
  • 13:00 Gwangjang Market 2F Vintage
  • 16:00 Sangsu·Yeonnam vintage cluster
  • 18:00 Hongdae Main Vintage

Pattern C: MZ·K-pop·souvenirs (family·tourists)

  • 11:00 Hongdae Main KPop Spot·Line Friends
  • 13:00 Hongdae lunch
  • 15:00 Hapjeong SPA·Beauty
  • 17:00 Yeonnam Design Props/Indie

8. Foreign fit tips

English language availability

  • Top: Ader Error·Andersson Bell·KPop Spot·Line Friends (English fluent)
  • Slander: Roh Yoonhee·Open YY·SPA brand (English menu)
  • Local: Vintage cluster·Indie designer (picture·translation app)

Payment/tax refund

  • Card OK: Most (many foreign cards)
  • Cash: Some vintage markets and indie stores
  • Tax Refund: K-brand, SPA, and souvenirs available for many items (KRW 30,000 or more)
  • Airport Refund + Instant Refund Option

Daily budget (per person)

  • K-fashion deep: ₩200,000~1,000,000
  • Vintage hunter: ₩50,000~300,000
  • MZ·Souvenirs: ₩50,000~200,000
  • Hapjeong SPA·Beauty: ₩30,000~150,000

By persona

  • K-pop 팬: KPop Spot·SMTown + 명동 (compare)
  • fashion lover: Pattern A (K-brand)
  • 빈티지·구제: Pattern B (1일 cluster)
  • MZ·Insta: Yeonnam Design Props·Hapjeong SPA
  • European art: Yeonnam indie designer/gallery

By season (sale season)

  • Spring/Fall: K-brand new season (April, September)
  • Summer (June-August): Season sale (~50%)
  • Winter (December-February): Year-end sale·outlet
  • 블랙프라이데이 (11월): 글로벌 brand sale
