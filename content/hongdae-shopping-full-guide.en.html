Hongdae Shopping Full Guide for Foreign Travelers

> A 4-pillar matrix of MZ, Indie, Trendy, and Vintage — 20 shopping clusters in Hongdae, Sangsu, Hapjeong, and Yeonnam.

Hongdae is the mecca of Korean MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) and indie fashion. The main street, Sangsu (상수), Hapjeong (합정), and Yeonnam (연남) form four sub-clusters dense with K-fashion, indie brands, vintage items, souvenirs, and K-pop merchandise. This guide provides a matrix of 20 places perfectly suited for foreign segments, fashion lovers, vintage hunters, and MZ shoppers, plus a one-day itinerary.

1. Hongdae Shopping Landscape

4 Sub-Clusters

  • Hongdae Main Street (Hongdae-ipgu (홍대입구) Station Exit 9): Trendy brands, K-fashion, souvenirs
  • Sangsu (상수): Indie designers, vintage, sample sales
  • Hapjeong (합정): Value for money, SPA brands, beauty
  • Yeonnam (연남): Golmok (골목, alleyways), indie designers, design goods

MZ Trends

  • Origin of many K-fashion brands (e.g., Ader Error, Open YY, Roh Yoonhee)
  • Korea's No. 1 vintage market
  • K-pop merchandise (numerous stores + linked with Dongdaemun (동대문) wholesale)
  • Korea's largest cluster of indie design brands

2. K-Fashion Brands (Sangsu, Hongdae, Yeonnam) ★ Top Pick for Foreigners

1. Ader Error (Apgujeong (압구정) Main Store + Hongdae Store)

  • Signature: Korean contemporary brand, popular in the US and Europe
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (English fluent, popular with foreign regulars)
  • Price: ₩100,000~500,000 (approx. ~$75-375 / ~540-2700 RMB / ~10,000-50,000 JPY)

2. Roh Yoonhee (Sangsu)

  • Signature: Korean contemporary, minimalist
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★

3. Andersson Bell (Sangsu)

  • Signature: Scandi-Korean fusion
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (Global brand)

4. Open YY (Sangsu)

  • Signature: Often worn by K-pop stars
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★ (Good fit for K-pop fans)

5. Mardi Mercredi (Hapjeong)

  • Signature: Daisy print, MZ signature
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★

3. Vintage & Secondhand (Sangsu, Hongdae, Yeonnam)

6. Gwangjang Market (광장시장) Vintage Alley (★ Outside Jongno (종로) but recommended for foreigners)

  • Gwangjang Market (광장시장) 2F Vintage ([#1122 Korean Market Index for Foreigners](1122_korean_market_index_for_foreigners.md))

7. Dongmyo (동묘) Flea Market (★ Outside Jongno)

  • Top pick for foreign vintage hunters

8. Sangsu Vintage Cluster

  • 5-7 Vintage Stores in Sangsu (상수): Indie designers, samples
  • Price: ₩20,000~150,000

9. Yeonnam Vintage Golmok (골목)

  • 3-5 Vintage Stores in Yeonnam (연남): Golmok, MZ
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★ (Good for photos)

10. Hongdae Main Vintage

  • Hongdae-ipgu (홍대입구) Vintage Cluster

4. K-Pop & Souvenirs (Hongdae Main)

11. KPop Spot (Hongdae)

  • Signature: K-pop merchandise, albums, photo cards
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★★ (No. 1 pick for K-pop fans)

12. LINE FRIENDS Hongdae Store

  • Signature: LINE characters, good fit for Japanese tourists
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★

13. SMTown Hongdae (Branch Store)

  • A branch store, in addition to the main store in Gangnam (강남)

14. Hongdae Souvenir Street

  • Numerous hanbok (한복, traditional Korean dress), pottery, and traditional souvenirs

5. Indie Design & Goods (Yeonnam, Sangsu)

15. Yeonnam Design Goods Cluster

  • 5-7 Design Stores in Yeonnam (연남): Pottery, general goods, indie brands
  • Foreigner Fit: ★★ (European designer vibe)

16. Sangsu Indie Designers

  • Cluster of individual designer stores

17. Yeonhui-dong (연희동) Indie (Adjacent, [#1128 Yeonhui-dong Guide](1128_yeonhui_dong_guide.md))

6. SPA & Beauty (Hapjeong, Hongdae Main)

18. ABLE Cosmetics Cluster

  • Numerous K-beauty stores (Hongdae Main)

19. Hapjeong SPA Brands (Zara, H&M, Spao)

  • Value-for-money stores

20. Megabox & Lotte Mall Hongdae (Movie + Shopping Combo)

7. Standard One-Day Itineraries (3 Patterns)

Pattern A: K-Fashion Enthusiast

  • 11:00 Sangsu K-brand hop (Roh Yoonhee, Andersson Bell, Open YY)
  • 14:00 Lunch (Korean food in Sangsu or brunch in Hapjeong)
  • 15:30 Ader Error, Hongdae Main
  • 17:30 Hapjeong SPA + Beauty

Pattern B: Vintage Hunter

  • 10:00 Dongmyo (동묘) Flea Market (morning peak)
  • 13:00 Gwangjang Market (광장시장) 2F Vintage
  • 16:00 Sangsu & Yeonnam Vintage Cluster
  • 18:00 Hongdae Main Vintage

Pattern C: MZ, K-Pop & Souvenirs (Families, Tourists)

  • 11:00 Hongdae Main KPop Spot, LINE FRIENDS
  • 13:00 Lunch in Hongdae
  • 15:00 Hapjeong SPA, Beauty
  • 17:00 Yeonnam Design Goods, Indie

8. Foreigner-Friendly Tips

English Availability

  • High: Ader Error, Andersson Bell, KPop Spot, LINE FRIENDS (English fluent)
  • Medium-High: Roh Yoonhee, Open YY, SPA brands (English menus)
  • Local: Vintage clusters, indie designers (use pictures, translation apps)

Payment & Tax Refund

  • Card OK: Most places (many accept international cards)
  • Cash: Some vintage markets and indie stores
  • Tax Refund: Available at many K-brands, SPA stores, and souvenir shops (for purchases over ₩30,000)
  • Airport Refund + Instant Refund Options

One-Day Budget (Per Person)

  • K-fashion deep: ₩200,000~1,000,000
  • Vintage hunter: ₩50,000~300,000
  • MZ & Souvenirs: ₩50,000~200,000
  • Hapjeong SPA & Beauty: ₩30,000~150,000

By Persona

  • K-pop fans: KPop Spot, SMTown + Myeongdong (명동) (for comparison)
  • Fashion lovers: Pattern A (K-brands)
  • Vintage & Secondhand: Pattern B (one-day cluster)
  • MZ & Instagrammers: Yeonnam design goods, Hapjeong SPA
  • European art lovers: Yeonnam indie designers, galleries

By Season (Sale Season)

  • Spring/Autumn: K-brand new season (April, September)
  • Summer (June-August): Seasonal sales (~50% off)
  • Winter (December-February): Year-end sales, outlets
  • Black Friday (November): Global brand sales
