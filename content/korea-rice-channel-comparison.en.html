🎯 Why a 4-Channel Rice Comparison Guide

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "The 4 channels = similar": Completely different.

2. "Gonggi-bap = free": Free only at hanjeongsik & gajeongsik.

3. "MZ = walk right in": MZ = expect a wait.

4. "Gajeongsik = just expensive": Gajeongsik = ₩8,000–15,000 (~$6–11), great value.

5. "Bunsik = for kids": Bunsik = MZ trend, totally OK.

⚙ 8 FAQs

5. Family: Gajeongsik (#1).
