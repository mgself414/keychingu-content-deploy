🎯 Why a Gyeongsang-do (경상도) Guide?

Gyeongsang-do (경상도) = Korea's #1 UNESCO Matrix (Gyeongju (경주)·Andong (안동)) + Tongyeong (통영)·Jinju (진주) Enthusiast Destinations. Gyeongju (경주) = Silla (신라) Millennium Capital (BC 57~AD 935·UNESCO 1995)·Andong (안동) = Hahoe Folk Village (하회마을) UNESCO 2010. Tongyeong (통영) Hansando Island (한산도)·Jinju (진주) Chokseoknu (촉석루). Foreigner Pain Points = (a) Misconception that "Gyeongsang-do (경상도) = Busan (부산) only" (b) Gyeongju (경주) = UNESCO 1995 (Bulguksa Temple (불국사)·Seokguram Grotto (석굴암)) + Silla (신라) Millennium Capital (c) Andong (안동) = UNESCO 2010 Hahoe Folk Village (하회마을)·Dosanseowon (도산서원) (d) Tongyeong (통영) = Hansando Island (한산도)·Yi Sun-sin (이순신)·Maritime National Park (e) Jinju (진주) = Chokseoknu (촉석루)·Jinju Fortress (진주성)·Jinju Naengmyeon (진주냉면) (Korean cold noodles)·Yudeung Festival (유등축제) (lantern festival).

This guide covers 4 axes, 12 signatures + a full-day itinerary + UNESCO sites + English for foreigners.

🏛 4-Axis Deep Dive

  • Signature: Bulguksa Temple (불국사) (751 AD·UNESCO)·Seokguram Grotto (석굴암) (UNESCO)·Cheonmachong Tomb (천마총)·Gyeongju National Museum (국립경주박물관)·Silla (신라) Millennium Capital (BC 57~AD 935)
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (Full English Support·#1 UNESCO Enthusiast Matrix)
