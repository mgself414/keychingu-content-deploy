5 Mistakes Foreign Visitors Often Make

1. Thinking Itaewon (이태원) = Only Club Street

Itaewon (이태원) is also strong on multicultural restaurants, antique furniture, halal eateries, and trendy cafés. Skipping everything but clubs means missing half the neighborhood. See the full daytime guide for Hannam (한남) · Haebangchon (해방촌) cafés and galleries below.

2. Confusing Hannam (한남) and Itaewon (이태원)

Itaewon (이태원) (west · multicultural · busy) vs Hannam (한남) (east · luxury · upscale) vs Haebangchon (해방촌) (north of Itaewon · hillside · MZ-gen · alleyways) — the vibes are completely different. See §3-Neighborhood Comparison below.

3. Underestimating the Haebangchon (해방촌) Hill Climb

Haebangchon (해방촌) sits on a steep slope along Namsan (남산) Mountain. Sneakers are essential; high heels are strongly discouraged. Plan for a 30-minute to 1-hour walking course.

4. Not Being Aware of Late-Night Safety on Club Street

Itaewon (이태원) club street sees massive crowds from midnight to 04:00 on Fridays and Saturdays. Solo foreign visitors are advised to go with a group, or to wrap up early, by 22:00–24:00.

5. Not Reserving Hannam (한남) Restaurants and Galleries in Advance

Hannam (한남) restaurants and galleries — including Leeum Museum of Art (리움미술관) and Gallery Hyundai (갤러리현대) — require reservations. Walk-ins are rarely accepted.

★ 3-Neighborhood Deep Comparison: Itaewon (이태원) vs Hannam (한남) vs Haebangchon (해방촌)

Three neighborhoods, all close to Line 6 Itaewon Station (이태원역) and Hangangjin Station (한강진역) — yet they have completely different vibes, price levels, and visitor patterns.

1️⃣ Itaewon (이태원) — Multicultural · Bustling · 24 Hours

Location: Around Line 6 Itaewon Station (이태원역)

Vibe: Multicultural · bustling · 24-hour · #1 for foreign resident ratio

Price Range: ₩15,000–50,000 (meals) / ₩10,000–30,000 (drinks)

Highlights:

  • 100+ multicultural restaurants — American, Latin American, European, Southeast Asian, Arab, African
  • Halal restaurant alley near Seoul Central Mosque (서울 중앙 모스크)
  • Club street (near Hamilton Hotel (해밀튼호텔)) — open until 04:00 on Fri & Sat
  • Vintage furniture and antique alleyways
  • Foreign resident ratio 20%+ (vs. Korea's national average of ~1%)

One-liner: "Korea's most international neighborhood — streets alive until 4 a.m."

Best for: Multicultural food / clubs & bars / 24-hour access / meeting other foreign visitors

2️⃣ Hannam (한남) — Luxury · Upscale · Gallery

Location: Around Line 6 Hangangjin Station (한강진역) + east of Itaewon Station (이태원역)

Vibe: Luxury · sophisticated · gallery scene · upscale dining

Price Range: ₩30,000–150,000 (meals) / ₩15,000–50,000 (drinks & cafés)

Highlights:

  • Leeum Museum of Art (리움미술관) (Samsung Foundation — Korea's top-tier art museum) — advance reservation required
  • Gallery Hyundai Hannam (갤러리현대 한남) · Arario Gallery (아라리오 갤러리) and other trending galleries
  • Luxury dining along Hannam-daero (한남대로) (Korean fine dining · Italian)
  • Hannam The Hill (한남더힐), Hannam Reserve (한남리저브) — among Korea's most prestigious residences
  • Trendy boutiques and select shops

One-liner: "Korea's luxury + gallery district — the #1 choice for a relaxed afternoon."

Best for: Galleries & art / luxury dining / dates / photogenic spots

3️⃣ Haebangchon (해방촌) — MZ-Gen · Alleys · Panoramic Views

Location: Itaewon Station (이태원역) Exit 1 or 2 → up the Namsan (남산) hillside

Vibe: MZ-gen alleyways · trendy · panoramic views · hilly streets

Price Range: ₩10,000–25,000 (cafés & restaurants)

Highlights:

  • Sinheung Market (신흥시장) (old market + trendy cafés & bars combined) — MZ hot spot
  • Namsan (남산) hillside — Seoul skyline and N Seoul Tower (N서울타워) views
  • Alley cafés · studios · galleries
  • Post-Korean War refugee settlement → now transformed into an MZ-gen neighborhood
  • #1 walking course (1–2 hours)

One-liner: "The MZ alley neighborhood on the hill above Itaewon (이태원) — alley cafés, views, and trends."

Best for: Café tours / alley strolls / night views / Instagram content

④ Multicultural Restaurants — Itaewon (이태원)

Representative Lineup:

  • American-style burgers: ₩18,000–30,000
  • Mexican & Brazilian: ₩20,000–40,000
  • Greek, Turkish & Middle Eastern: ₩20,000–35,000
  • Southeast Asian (Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian): ₩15,000–30,000
  • African & Latin American: ₩20,000–50,000

Key Point: Korea's densest concentration of multicultural restaurants / 100% English menus / #1 pick for dining with foreign friends

⑤ Halal Restaurants — Near Seoul Central Mosque (서울 중앙 모스크)

Location: Itaewon Station (이태원역) → Seoul Central Mosque alley (5-min walk)

Price: ₩15,000–30,000

Key Points:

  • Halal street adjacent to Seoul Central Mosque (서울 중앙 모스크), Korea's main mosque
  • Turkish, Pakistani, Egyptian, and Malaysian restaurants
  • #1 destination for Muslim visitors / many halal-certified establishments
  • English and Arabic menus available

⑥ Leeum Museum of Art (리움미술관) + Gallery Hyundai (갤러리현대) — Hannam (한남)

Location: Hannam-dong (한남동) — 5-min walk from Hangangjin Station (한강진역) Exit 1

Admission: Leeum (리움) ₩20,000 / Gallery Hyundai (갤러리현대) ₩10,000–15,000

Key Points:

  • Leeum (리움) = Samsung Foundation's world-class art museum — advance reservation required (English website available)
  • Korean traditional art + contemporary art by global artists
  • Gallery Hyundai (갤러리현대) = Korea's first-generation commercial gallery + contemporary art

⑦ Sinheung Market (신흥시장) + Alley Cafés — Haebangchon (해방촌)

Location: Itaewon Station (이태원역) Exit 1 → 10-min walk uphill (hill entrance)

Price: Cafés ₩6,000–10,000

Key Points:

  • Sinheung Market (신흥시장) = old traditional market fused with MZ-gen cafés, bars, and galleries
  • Independent alley cafés, studios, and select shops
  • #1 neighborhood for Korean MZ-gen trends
  • Abundant Instagram-worthy content

⑧ Haebangchon (해방촌) Night View — Namsan (남산) Panorama

Location: Around the top of the Haebangchon (해방촌) hillside

Admission: Free

Key Points:

  • 360° views of Namsan (남산), N Seoul Tower (N서울타워), and the Seoul skyline
  • #1 free night-view spot in the area
  • Arrive 30 minutes before sunset for the best light

8 Tips for Foreign Visitors

✅ Itaewon (이태원) club street — group company recommended after midnight

✅ Hannam (한남) galleries & restaurants — advance reservations essential

✅ Haebangchon (해방촌) hill — sneakers or trekking shoes required

✅ 100% card payment accepted everywhere

✅ Halal & vegan — check menus in advance (see #69)

✅ After midnight, use Kakao T — subway service ends

✅ Photography — some galleries and restaurant interiors prohibit photos

✅ Watch your belongings and phone at night — narrow alleys and dense crowds

FAQ

Q1. Does English work here?

100%. Itaewon (이태원), Hannam (한남), and Haebangchon (해방촌) have the best English accessibility in Korea.

Q2. Family-friendly?

Hannam (한남) galleries and Haebangchon (해방촌) cafés = family OK. Itaewon (이태원) club street at night = not recommended for families. Daytime multicultural restaurants are fine for all ages.

Q3. Solo travelers?

Haebangchon (해방촌) and Hannam (한남) = solo OK. Itaewon (이태원) clubs = group recommended.

Q4. Vegan or halal?

Itaewon (이태원) = Korea's #1 neighborhood for vegan and halal options. For detailed restaurant info, see #69.

Q5. Only 4 hours?

Sinheung Market (신흥시장) in Haebangchon (해방촌) (1h) + Leeum (리움) or a gallery in Hannam (한남) (1h) + dinner in Itaewon (이태원) (2h) — compressed course.

Q6. How do I book a gallery?

Leeum (리움) English website — reservation recommended one month in advance. Gallery Hyundai (갤러리현대) — one week in advance.
