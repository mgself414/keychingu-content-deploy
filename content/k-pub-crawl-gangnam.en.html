9 Pubs and Bars in Gangnam and Itaewon — Two Walkable Routes Linking the Cheongdam Cocktail Alley and the Haebangchon Hill

Subtitle: 2 craft beer pubs at Gangnam Station, 3 cocktail bars on Dosan-daero 55-gil in Cheongdam, 3 places in Noksapyeong and Haebangchon, and 1 brewery in Sinyongsan. The 3 Cheongdam bars sit in one alley within 160m of each other, so they are easy to walk between, and the hill road from Noksapyeong to Haebangchon is a little over 600m. For each venue we noted the price of a glass of beer or a cocktail, the price of snacks, the cover charge, and dietary cues. We also put together Korea's legal drinking age and ID rules.

#779 · 2026-11-24 revised · Seoul Pubs & Bars

🎯 Who This Is For

This piece is for travelers who want to walk between two or three places after dinner and drink along the way. Instead of sitting in one place for a long time, you pick one neighborhood and stroll between venues, a "pub crawl" style. We kept only places that are walkable from a subway station, have business information registered by the owner on Kakao Map (카카오맵), and have visit reviews or photos posted in October to November 2026.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours and closed days are the values registered by the owner on Kakao Map (카카오맵) (checked 2026-11-24). For prices, we used only Kakao Map menu items registered within the last 2 years. For places whose registered prices are old, such as the cocktail bars, we used the prices from dated Naver blog visit reviews and put the source next to them. Dietary cues are only what can be read from menu names, so ask staff about ingredients and allergies before ordering. Walking distances between venues are measured on OpenStreetMap walking routes, and the walking time and exit from the station to each venue are Kakao Map directions values.

① Gangnam Station — 2 Craft Beer Venues

The two places are on opposite sides of Gangnam Station, 1.2km apart (a little over 15 minutes on foot). It is better to pick one of the two.

Goose Island Brewhouse (구스아일랜드 브루하우스)

A 2-floor pub carrying the name of Goose Island, the Chicago brewery in the US. The Kakao Map tags include rooftop. It is open Mon–Sat 11:30~24:00 and Sunday 11:30~23:00, so daytime drinking works too. The prices in a Naver blog review (2026-11-15) are 7,500 won for the Black Hole stout, 9,000 won for the Gangnam Style Saison, 10,000 won for the Sweet Persimmon Tripel, and 36,000 won for the Cheeseburger Chicago pizza. Another review (2026-11-22) lists a weekday lunch set for 2 people at 38,000 won. The address is 118 Yeoksam-ro, Gangnam-gu (강남구 역삼로 118), 8 minutes on foot from Exit 4 of Gangnam Station (Line 2 · Sinbundang Line).

  • Dietary cues: Chicago pizza has thick layers of cheese, and the cheeseburger pizza contains beef. The menu centers on meat dishes such as the barbecue platter. No vegetarian menu has been confirmed.

Beer Room (비어룸)

A craft beer and wood-fired pizza place in an alley north of Gangnam Station. It is open Wed–Sun 11:30~23:30 and closed Mon·Tue. Drinks are also sold by the glass, but the all-you-can-drink option stands out. The Kakao Map registered price is 30,000 won for unlimited (2025-08-07), and a blog review (2026-10-12) lists two options, 15,000 won for 1 hour and 30,000 won for 3 hours. Unlimited includes craft beer, wine, and highballs. Registered snack prices are wedge fries 6,000 won, nachos with Buldak cheese sauce 5,000 won, Margherita 26,000 won (2025-12-11), and Diavola pizza 36,000 won. The address is 22 Gangnam-daero 98-gil, Floor 1, Gangnam-gu (강남구 강남대로98길 22 1층), 7 minutes from Exit 11 of Gangnam Station.

  • Dietary cues: The Margherita has no meat but has cheese. Diavola and salami dishes usually use processed pork. The fries are a meat-free dish. The Buldak cheese sauce served with the nachos is spicy and contains dairy.

② Cheongdam Dosan-daero 55-gil — 3 Cocktail Bars

Three venues cluster in one alley, 2~4 minutes from Exit 4 of Apgujeong Rodeo Station (Suin-Bundang Line). All three take reservations through Catchtable. Le Chamber and Alice charge a separate per-person cover charge.

ZEST (제스트)

A "zero waste" bar that reuses discarded ingredients, such as fruit peels, in its cocktails. It is listed at No. 7 on The World's 50 Best Bars 2026 list (the50.com, checked 2026-11-24). It is open Mon–Fri 18:30~02:00 and Sat·Sun 15:00~02:00, so on weekends it opens in the afternoon. A blog review posted on 2026-11-05 lists cocktails at 26,000 won per glass and the non-alcoholic Natural Cola at 15,000 won, but those are the prices from the author's visit about a year earlier. A Kakao Map review from 2026-02-21 says the bartenders explain things well in English. The address is 26 Dosan-daero 55-gil, Floor 1, Gangnam-gu (강남구 도산대로55길 26 1층).

  • Dietary cues: The ingredients differ by cocktail, such as seasonal fruit, herbs, and sherry. The Kakao Map summary carries a "partial no-kids zone" tag.

Le Chamber (르챔버)

A basement bar entered through a hidden bookshelf door. You press a book on the top shelf to the right of the entrance and the door opens (blog review 2026-09-27). It is open Mon–Thu 19:00~03:00, Fri·Sat 19:00~04:00, Sun and public holidays 19:00~02:00, the latest in this piece. A cover charge of 10,000 won per person applies separately from orders (blog review 2026-10-05). Besides cocktails, you can order a whole bottle of whisky and have it stored for you (same review). The Kakao Map summary says there is live piano. A Kakao Map English review from 2026-05-20 said the staff were good and the prices were on the high side. The address is 42 Dosan-daero 55-gil, Basement 1, Gangnam-gu (강남구 도산대로55길 42 지하 1층).

  • Dietary cues: According to the Kakao Map summary, potato chips and pretzels come as the default snacks. It is a drink-focused bar, so do not expect meal dishes.

Alice (앨리스)

A basement cocktail bar decorated with an "Alice in Wonderland" theme. It is open daily 18:00~02:00. According to a blog review (2026-11-20), the cover charge is 10,000 won per person (welcome drink included) with some nibbles served alongside, and every signature cocktail is 27,000 won. The menu is shaped like a playing card, and the card number shows the alcohol strength. Some glasses use Korean ingredients, like a cocktail mixing soju, strawberry, and gochujang. The address is 47 Dosan-daero 55-gil, Basement 1, Gangnam-gu (강남구 도산대로55길 47 지하 1층).

  • Dietary cues: The cocktail ingredients include soju, fruit, and even fermented sauces, so tell staff before ordering if you have an allergy. If you do not drink alcohol, check the welcome drink in advance too.

③ Noksapyeong·Haebangchon — 3 Venues

A hill road leads from Noksapyeong-daero in front of Noksapyeong Station (Line 6) to Sinheung-ro in Haebangchon. Genie Pub and DANKYARD are closed on Mondays. Southside Parlor is also registered on Kakao Map as closed on Mondays, but a 2026-11-19 blog review lists it as open on Mondays.

Southside Parlor (사우스사이드팔러)

A Texas-style taco and cocktail bar on Floor 4, with darts and rooftop among its Kakao Map tags. Both a blog review (2026-04-26) and the Kakao Map summary say there are many foreign guests. The Kakao Map registered hours are Tue–Thu 18:00~01:00, Fri·Sat 18:00~02:00, Sun 18:00~01:00, closed Mon. However, a 2026-11-19 blog review lists Monday service and a 17:00 weekend opening, so check the Instagram notice on Mondays. Registered prices (2026-07-01) are 3 tacos 16,000 won, quesadilla 16,000 won, buffalo or fried chicken sandwich 18,000 won, and Caesar salad 12,000 won. By blog reviews, cocktails are 18,000 won (2026-11-19, based on 2 seasonal signature cocktails), and the Magpie Pale Ale and Galmaegi IPA are 9,000 won (2026-09-29). The same November review says that when you order a drink with it, tacos are 4,000 won each on Tuesday and wings are half price on Wednesday. The address is 218 Noksapyeong-daero, Floor 4, Yongsan-gu (용산구 녹사평대로 218 4층), 7 minutes from Exit 3 of Noksapyeong Station.

  • Dietary cues: The menu has a vegan chorizo taco. The fish taco is fish, and the chicken taco and wings are chicken. Ask about the taco tortilla and the batter ingredients when ordering.

Genie Pub (지니펍)

A casual 3rd-floor cocktail pub on Sinheung-ro, the main street of Haebangchon. It is open Tue–Thu 18:00~01:00, Fri 18:00~02:00, Sat 17:00~02:00, Sun 18:00~01:00, closed Mon. Kakao Map registered prices (2026-10-06) are Peach Crush and Cuba Libre 12,000 won, Moscow Mule 13,000 won, Negroni and Long Island Iced Tea 14,000 won, and the signature "Noksapyeong Station Exit 2" 15,000 won, so 12,000~16,000 won overall. A blog review (2026-11-03) says beer is under 10,000 won. From the window seats you can look down on the Haebangchon street. The address is 37 Sinheung-ro, Floor 3, Yongsan-gu (용산구 신흥로 37 3층), 12 minutes from Exit 2 of Noksapyeong Station.

  • Dietary cues: The Kakao Map tags include pizza pub, but no snack prices are registered. Go thinking of it as a cocktail place.

DANKYARD Seoul (댕크야드 서울)

A 2nd-floor brewery and listening bar serving its own brewed beer along with music. It is open Tue–Thu 18:00~24:00, Fri·Sat 18:00~01:00, Sun 16:00~23:00, closed Mon. Registered beer prices (2025-12-02) are lager 9,000 won, Neugeut 11,500 won, porter and Genie Stout 12,500 won, Gyeongpo Double IPA 16,000 won, and Eisbock 27,000 won. For food, lasagna 20,000 won (2026-06-16) is registered. A Kakao Map review (2026-02-07) says the beer lineup changes every month. The address is 11 Sinheung-ro 5-gil, Floor 2, Yongsan-gu (용산구 신흥로5길 11 2층), 45m from Genie Pub.

  • Dietary cues: The Kakao Map summary carries a "vegetarian menu" tag. Ask which dish it is when you order. Lasagna usually contains cheese and a meat sauce.

④ Sinyongsan — 1 Venue

Hand and Malt Brew Lab (핸드앤몰트 브루랩)

The taproom of the craft beer brewer Hand and Malt, in "Yongridan-gil" behind Sinyongsan Station. It is open Mon–Thu 17:00~23:00, Fri 17:00~24:00, Sat 14:00~24:00, Sun 14:00~23:00. By a blog review (2026-10-29), a 340mL Seoul IPA is 8,600 won. Registered snack prices (2025-09-13) are gochujang ragu tteokbokki 17,900 won, nurungji cheese potato pancake and Kkamang fish and chips 19,900 won, and salt-grilled chicken neck 22,900 won. One wall on Floor 1 is a full glass window. The address is 19 Hangang-daero 38ga-gil, Yongsan-gu (용산구 한강대로38가길 19), 5 minutes from Exit 1 of Sinyongsan Station (Line 4). It is 2.2km from Noksapyeong, so take a bus or taxi to link it with an Itaewon route.

  • Dietary cues: According to the Kakao Map menu descriptions, the gochujang ragu tteokbokki is rice cakes in a ragu sauce simmered with ground beef, topped with grilled beef brisket, and the Kkamang fish and chips is flounder fried in squid ink batter. The potato pancake contains cheese. Chicken neck is chicken.

🗺 Two Walking Routes

A. Cheongdam Cocktails (160m in total between the three places)

  1. 18:30 Exit 4 of Apgujeong Rodeo Station → ZEST (4 minutes). On weekends it opens from 15:00
  2. Around 20:00 Move to Le Chamber (118m, 2 minutes). It opens at 19:00
  3. Around 21:30 Move to Alice (42m, 1 minute)

Even with one drink each, the cover charge applies twice (20,000 won per person). If you want to keep going late on Friday and Saturday, put Le Chamber, which is open until 04:00, last.

B. Noksapyeong → Haebangchon (about 650m between venues, hill road)

  1. 18:00 Exit 3 of Noksapyeong Station → Southside Parlor (7 minutes). Tacos and beer stand in for dinner
  2. Around 20:00 Follow Sinheung-ro to Genie Pub, 610m (a little over 8 minutes on foot, uphill)
  3. Around 21:30 Finish with beer at DANKYARD, 45m next door. On Fri·Sat it is open until 01:00

This route is built for Tue–Sun. On Mondays Genie Pub and DANKYARD are closed, and Southside Parlor is closed on Kakao Map but listed as open in the 2026-11-19 review, so check the Instagram notice before going.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Planning Route B on a Monday — Genie Pub and DANKYARD are closed on Mondays. Southside Parlor is closed on Mondays on Kakao Map, but the 2026-11-19 review lists it as open on Mondays. Beer Room is closed Mon·Tue.
  2. Walking in without knowing about the cover charge — Le Chamber charges 10,000 won per person separate from orders, and Alice charges 10,000 won per person with a welcome drink included. At Alice, one signature drink plus the cover charge comes to 37,000 won per person.
  3. Going out without ID — Bars and pubs check age at entry or when you order. For foreigners, bringing your original passport is the surest way.

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. From what age can you drink? — The Youth Protection Act treats anyone under 19 as a youth, but excludes them once January 1 of the year they turn 19 has passed. It is counted by birth year regardless of birthday, so in 2026 people born in 2007 or earlier can buy alcohol. The same Act bars selling alcohol to youths and requires the seller to check age and identity. The venues in this piece center on alcohol, so a minor who goes in with a group cannot order a drink, and some places do not admit them at all.
  2. What if you are vegetarian or do not eat pork? — The vegan chorizo taco at Southside Parlor and the Margherita and fries at Beer Room are confirmed by menu names. DANKYARD shows a vegetarian menu in its Kakao Map summary. Cocktail ingredients can include butter, eggs, and dairy, so vegetarians and vegans should check when ordering.
  3. Do you need to reserve? — For the three Cheongdam venues, Southside Parlor, and Hand and Malt, a Catchtable reservation page is linked on Kakao Map. If you go to Cheongdam on a Friday or Saturday night, it is better to reserve. A blog review (2026-10-26) says the Beer Room unlimited pass was bought through Naver reservation.
