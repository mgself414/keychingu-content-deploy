Changwon·Jinhae Travel Map — Full Meta Guide for Foreign Tourists: Cherry Blossoms, Navy, and 12 Signature Spots in Changwon·Jinhae

Subtitle: Jinhae Cherry Blossoms, Navy, and Changwon Robots. Outside Seoul City Series Vol. 14

ISSUE 03 · #410 · 2026-05-26

🎯 Why the "Changwon·Jinhae Travel Map" Meta Guide?

Visiting Changwon (창원) and Jinhae (진해) for foreign tourists — 3 hours from Seoul (서울) by KTX. Key attractions: (1) Jinhae Gunhangje (진해 군항제) (Korea's #1 cherry blossom festival in April, since 1963) (2) Jinhae Naval Base (진해 해군기지) (Korea's #1 naval headquarters) (3) Changwon Robot Land (창원 로봇랜드) (Korea's #1 robot theme park) (4) Masan Happo Bay (마산 합포만) and Agujjim (아구찜, spicy braised anglerfish) (5) Jinhaeru (진해루) and Yeojwacheon (여좌천) Cherry Blossoms.

This guide covers 4 main themes and 12 signature spots in Changwon (창원) and Jinhae (진해). It's Vol. 14 of our Outside Seoul City Series.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes When Visiting Changwon·Jinhae

1. "Only Jinhae cherry blossoms" — Unaware of Navy & Robots: Tourists often only know about cherry blossoms. In reality, you can have a full experience including the Naval Base (해군기지)·Naval Academy (해군사관학교) + Changwon Robot Land (창원 로봇랜드).

2. "Only April" — Unaware of year-round full experience: Tourists often only think of April. In reality, you can have a full experience including April (cherry blossoms) + year-round Navy, Robots, and Masan Agujjim (아구찜).

3. "Don't know Masan" — Unaware of Agujjim & Happo Bay: Tourists often don't realize Masan (마산) is part of the area. In reality, you can have a full gastronomic experience including the Masan Agujjim (아구찜) hub + Happo Bay (합포만) coast.
