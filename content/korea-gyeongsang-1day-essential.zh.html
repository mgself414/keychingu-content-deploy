🎯 Why Gyeongsang-do Guide?

Gyeongsang-do (Gyeongsang-do) = Korea #1 UNESCO Matrix (Gyeongju·Andong) + Tongyeong·Jinju Mania·Gyeongju = Millennium Ancient City of Silla (57 BC~AD 935·UNESCO 1995)·Andong = Hahoe Village UNESCO 2010·Tongyeong Hansando·Jinju Chokseokru. Foreigner's Pain Point = (a) Misconception that "Gyeongsang-do = Busan Bay" (b) Gyeongju = UNESCO 1995 (Bulguksa Temple, Seokgulam Grotto) + Silla Millennium Ancient City (c) Andong = UNESCO 2010 Hahoe Village, Dosan Seowon (d) Tongyeong = Hansando, Yi Sun-sin, Haesang National Park (e) Jinju =草石路、晋州城、晋州冷面、柳灯节。

本指南包括 4 个轴、12 个签名 + 1 天 + 联合国教科文组织 + 外国人英语。
