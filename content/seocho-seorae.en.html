🎯 Why is this an exclusive guide to Seocho and Seorae Village?

Seocho and Seorae are the No. 1 foreign residential area and hot zone for the Korean middle class in Seocho-gu. In particular, Seorae Village (Banpo 4-dong) is the largest French community area in Korea and the No. 1 foreign residential area where LFS (Lycée Français de Séoul, Seoul French School, opened in 1974) is located. "Little Paris in Seoul" where authentic French bakeries, pastries, and French dining, including MAILLET·fincafe, are concentrated in every alley.

Compared to Gangnam, Itaewon, and Hongdae, Seocho and Seorae have foreign residents, families, and middle class. Although it is overshadowed by Myeongdong, Gangnam, and Itaewon in the flow of foreign tourists, foreigners who know it consider it "the most foreigner-friendly neighborhood in Korea". There are also plenty of family-friendly spots, including Kyodae and Legal Town (Seocho Main) + Banpo Hangang Park + Gangnam Terminal Duty Free + Yangjae Citizen's Forest.

In particular, Seorae Village MAILLET is an authentic Korean French pastry store founded in 1995 — Korea’s No. 1 French bakery that supplies to Sejong-ro Hotel Shilla and Seoul Shilla Hotel. ⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Seocho = a neighborhood like Gangnam" — Not considered the No. 1 foreign residence in Seorae Village

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. Seocho and Seorae are perceived as "united with Gangnam" due to their proximity to Gangnam-gu, but in reality, Seorae Village is the largest French community in Korea + No. 1 foreign school/residence for foreigners = separate from Gangnam (luxury/shopping). French bakery clusters such as MAILLET and fincafe are the No. 1 deep dive for foreigners living in Korea.

2. "Gyodae = Beopjotown only" — Korean food/Pyeongnaeng cluster unknown

Foreigners perceive Gyodae only as a “lawyer’s neighborhood,” but in reality, it is the No. 1 lunch spot for the Korean middle class and lawyers, with a concentration of authentic Korean restaurants such as Deojinmipyeongnaeng, Seocho Myeonok, and Gyodae Two-story House. Pyeongnaeng (Pyongyang Naengmyeon) is the number one choice for foreigners to experience authentic Korean cold noodle dishes — Deojin Mi Pyeongnaeng is an authentic version of Seocho Pyeongnaeng.

3. "Banpo Hangang Park = Just the Han River" — Jamsu Bridge and Moonlight Rainbow Fountain Unknown

Banpo Hangang Park Jamsu Bridge (single-story bridge, submerged in water when it rains) + Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (rainbow-colored water sprayed from the Banpo Bridge) = No. 1 signature on Instagram for foreigners. Summer April~October + Night (20:00·20:20·20:40·21:00·21:20) Fountain show + Jamsu Bridge walk = No. 1 choice for foreign dates and families.

Scenery of Seocho and Seorae

Seocho and Seorae are located in the center of Seocho-gu A combination area of foreign residents and the Korean middle class — A Korean middle-class neighborhood formed with the development of Gangnam in the 1970s and 1980s + After the establishment of LFS (French School) in 1985, Seorae Village has established itself as Korea's largest French community.

Seorae Village (Banpo 4-dong) The sub-area is centered around Seorae-ro and Seorae Village Square — French school LFS + 50+ French bakeries, pastries, and French dining. A compact neighborhood where you can walk the entire area in just 30 minutes. 10-15 minute walk from Banpo Station and Express Bus Terminal Station on Lines 7 and 9.

Gyodae·Law Town sub-area near Gyodae Station (Lines 2 and 3) — Korean food, lunch for lawyers, and middle-class stores. A concentration of authentic Korean food stores.

Banpo·Hangang Park sub-area is a key spot on the Han River where Banpo Bridge, Jamsu Bridge, and Moonlight Rainbow Fountain are located. Han River bike path + walking + night fountain show.

Gangnam Express Bus Terminal sub-area is Honam Line KTX + Shinsegae Gangnam Duty Free Shop + Gangnam Terminal Underground Shopping Mall (#1 shopping destination for foreigners).

Yangjae·Maheon sub-area is Yangjae Citizen’s Forest, Yangjaecheon + foreign family-friendly stores. Line 9 Maeheon Station/Sinnonhyeon Station.

Transportation: Line 7 Banpo Station (Seorae Village) / Lines 2 and 3 Gyodae Station / Line 9 Maeheon Station·Sinnonhyeon Station / Lines 3, 7 and 9 Express Terminal Station. Kakao Taxi recommended. Bicycle Ttareungi OK.

⚙ FAQ (8)

Q1. Why is Seorae Village the number one French residence?

Established in 1974 as LFS (Lycée Français de Séoul) — Started as a school for the children of French diplomats and businessmen stationed in Seoul. French parents' residences formed near the school → From the 1990s, French bakeries, pastries, and dining were concentrated in alleys → Established as Korea's largest French community. Currently, approximately 3,000 to 5,000 French + foreigners live there.

Q2. How to get to MAILLET/reservation?

5-minute walk from Seorae Village (10-15-minute walk from Banpo Station or Sinbanpo Station) — Walk-in is OK without reservation, but pre-order is recommended for holiday and season-limited pastries. Because it is a hotspot, there is a line available for lunch and weekends. Quiet on weekdays from 14:00 to 17:00.

Q3. What time and season is the Moonlight Rainbow Fountain?

Summer night from April to October: 20:00, 20:20, 20:40, 21:00, 21:20 (every 20 minutes) — 4 times on weekdays and 5 times on weekends. free. The #1 observatory near Jamsu Bridge in Banpo Hangang Park. Operation suspended in case of rain.

Q4. What are the foreigner-friendly stores in Seorae Village?

MAILLET·fincafe·Seorae Village French Dining Store English/French menu + card payment + foreigner-friendly standard. Deojinmipyeongnaeng and Seocho Myeonok are mainly in Korean, but their photo menus and clerk-friendly menus are OK.

Q5. Where does the Gangnam Terminal underground shopping center start?

Lines 3, 7, and 9 Directly connected to Express Bus Terminal Station Exit 8. GOTO Mall (No. 1 Korean clothing wholesale and retail underground shopping mall) + CENTRAL CITY (Shinsegae Gangnam Duty Free Shop + Honam Line KTX ticket office). 50-70% of wholesale price.

Q6. Is it possible to bike or walk on Jamsu Bridge?

OK — Both walking and biking are possible. When it rains, it is flooded and controlled (Korea's only single-story bridge). Banpo Hangang Park Bicycle Road + Jamsu Bridge → Dongjak Bridge → Hannam Bridge → Dongho Bridge = Approximately 8km Hangang Bicycle Course.

Q7. Yangjae Citizen’s Forest Walking Time for Foreigners?

10-12:00 AM + 14-16:00 PM is best — Korean family + foreign residents walking. Spring Cherry Blossoms in April + Fall Foliage in October-November 1st place. 5-minute walk from Maeheon Station on Line 9 + free admission to the Maeheon Yun Bong-gil Memorial Hall.

Q8. Seocho + Gangnam + Han River connection route?

OK — Seocho·Seorae Village → Gangnam (Subway Lines 7 and 9, 2~3 stops) → Banpo Hangang Park (15 minutes on foot) → Jamsil or Yeouido (30 minutes to 1 hour by bicycle). One full day course available.
