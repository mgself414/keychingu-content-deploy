🚲 Public Bikes — Seoul and Daejeon

  • Ddareungi (따릉이) (Seoul Bike) — run by the Seoul city government. The day pass is ₩1,000 (~$0.75) for 1 hour, ₩2,000 (~$1.50) for 2 hours and ₩3,000 (~$2.25) for 3 hours. After the first rental, you can rent again as many times as you like that day as long as you return within the base time (60, 120 or 180 minutes). Go over the base time and ₩200 (~$0.15) is added every 5 minutes; if the bike is not returned within 4 hours of rental, it is treated as stolen and the operator contacts the police. Payment methods are mobile phone, credit card, PAYCO, KakaoPay, ZeroPay and the Discover Pass (foreigners only). Operations center 1599-0120 (365 days, 24 hours) (Seoul Bike official site)
  • Long-term passes (7, 30, 180 and 365 days) are for members only (Seoul Bike official site)
  • You can rent and return at any station, but the dock type you can return to (LCD type or QR type) depends on the bike model, according to the guidance (Seoul Bike official site)
  • Tashu (타슈) (Daejeon) — Daejeon city's public bikes. Age 15 and over; rentals 05:00–24:00 (24 hours in peak season, months 3–11, March to November), returns 24 hours. The first hour is free; beyond that it is ₩500 (~$0.38) per 30 minutes, capped at ₩5,000 (~$3.75) in extra fees per rental per day. If you return within the hour and rent again, you can keep riding with no extra fee (Tashu official site). We could not confirm the sign-up procedure for foreigners
  • For cycling routes, see Korea Bicycle Trail Guide

🫙 Refill Shop — Almang Shop (알맹상점), Mangwon Branch

  • Almang Shop (알맹상점) Mangwon branch — 47 Worldcup-ro 25-gil (월드컵로25길), Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul, 3rd floor. Tue–Sun 12:00–20:00, closed Mondays, no parking (Almang Shop official site)
  • What you can do: refill cosmetics, detergent, food staples and more, taking only as much as you carry. Almang Shop (알맹상점) describes itself as "Korea's first refill station" (Almang Shop official site)
  • Also: there are menus for a waste-collection campaign, zero-waste education and docent tours (Almang Shop official site). We did not open which items are accepted this time — check the "waste collection" menu on the site before you visit
  • Payment: the shop also sells prepaid gift vouchers (Almang Shop official site). We could not confirm on the official page whether cards are accepted
  • If your luggage has no empty container, rinse a small bottle you finished at your lodging and bring that

♻ Upcycling Space — Seoul Upcycling Plaza (서울새활용플라자)

  • Seoul Upcycling Plaza (서울새활용플라자) — 49 Jadongchasijang-gil (자동차시장길), Seongdong-gu (성동구), Seoul. Mon–Sat 10:00–18:00; on Saturdays only visitors with a tour reservation are admitted. Closed every Sunday and over the Seollal and Chuseok holidays. Main phone 02-2153-0400 (0401). Operated by the Seoul Design Foundation (서울디자인재단) (Seoul Upcycling Plaza official site)
  • What's on now: as of the notice dated 2026-10-19, the site lists a resource-circulation citizen event, "Eco Wonderland with Alice", and a "K-UP Pop-up Store" at the DDP Design Store (Seoul Upcycling Plaza official site). Check the notice itself for event dates and venues

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "A 1-hour Ddareungi (따릉이) pass lets me ride all day in one go" — you have to keep returning within 60 minutes and renting again. Go over and ₩200 (~$0.15) is added every 5 minutes (Seoul Bike official site)

2. "If a bike is returned late, I just pay more" — Ddareungi (따릉이) treats a bike not returned within 4 hours as stolen (Seoul Bike official site)

3. "I can visit the Upcycling Plaza any time on a weekend" — it is closed on Sundays, and on Saturdays only visitors with a tour reservation can enter (Seoul Upcycling Plaza official site)

4. "Refill shops are open on Mondays too" — Almang Shop (알맹상점) Mangwon branch is closed on Mondays (Almang Shop official site)
