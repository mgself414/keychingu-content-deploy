12 lines and 4 axes — deepening of real places

Axis 1: Parasite and Bong Joon-ho filming locations (line 3)

1. Ssangmun-dong Alley — Parasite semi-basement neighborhood

Work: Parasite (2019), Director Bong Joon-ho, Academy Award for Best Picture.

The alley outside the semi-basement house where Kiwoo's family lives was filmed in the Ssangmun-dong area of ​​Dobong-gu (not an indoor set, but an outdoor alley shot). Since it is an actual residential area, it is a living space for residents.

  • Location: Ssangmun-dong, Dobong-gu, Seoul (10-15 minutes walk from Ssangmun Station on Subway Line 4). The exact alley can be found in many pins when searching for “Parasite filming location Ssangmun-dong” on Google Maps.
  • Admission: Free, outdoor. Includes some private land and residential areas.
  • Foreigner fit: Bong Joon-ho’s fandom’s #1 sacred place for foreigners. Popular as a photo spot.
  • Timing: Weekly recommended (consideration of residents). Parasite’s unique atmosphere on a rainy day.
  • Manner: Residential alleys — Be quiet, absolutely do not litter without permission, and refrain from taking photos of windows and entrances.
  • Access Tip: Ssangmun Station Exit 2 → 10 minutes on foot. Searching for “Parasite filming location” on a Korean map app (Naver/Kakao Map) has high accuracy.

2. Jahamun Tunnel (near Changuimun Gate) — Parasite rainy night scene

Work: Parasite (2019). The scene where Ki-woo's family comes down the stairs on a night of heavy rain. The background is the inclined stairs near Jahamun-ro and Changuimun and the entrance to Jahamun Tunnel.

  • Location: Changuimun-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul (10 minutes by bus or taxi from Gyeongbokgung Station on Subway Line 3, or 30 minutes on foot).
  • Admission: Free, outdoor road. Tunnels are vehicular passageways.
  • Foreigner fit: A photo spot that recreates the Parasite heavy rain scene. Great atmosphere when visiting on a rainy day.
  • Timing: Maximize the atmosphere at night or in rainy weather. However, be careful of slipping on the slope.
  • Manner: Do not obstruct sidewalk traffic. Risk of walking in vehicular tunnels.

3. Ihwa-dong Stairs (Naksan area) — Gyeongji District, the setting for Parasite and various dramas

Work: Parasite Slope Alley worldview matches. Although it is not the main filming location, it is a similar terrain that fans visit together.

  • Location: Ewha Village, Ihwa-dong, Jongno-gu, Seoul (behind Ewha Girls’ High School, toward Naksan Park). 15-minute walk from Exit 2 of Hyehwa Station, Subway Line 4.
  • Admission: Free.
  • Foreigner fit: It is also famous as a mural village, so not only Parasite fans but also general tourists visit. Naksan Fortress and Seoul night view routes can be combined.
  • Timing: Recommended late afternoon to evening golden hour (night view + mural lighting).

Axis 2: Goblin and Mr. Sunshine filming locations (line 3)

4. Ewha Womans University ECC·Campus — Goblin’s representative filming location

Work: Goblin (2016-2017, tvN). The scene where Gong Yoo first meets Eun Tak (Kim Go Eun) on the Ewha Womans University campus was filmed on the stairs in front of the ECC (underground campus building) and around the main gate.

  • Location: 52 Ewhayeodae-gil, Seodaemun-gu, Seoul (Ewha Womans University). 5-minute walk from Exit 3 of Ewha Womans University Station on Subway Line 2.
  • Admission: Free, campus open (weekdays 09:00~18:00, shortened hours available on weekends, limited for events). Cafe available inside ECC.
  • Foreigner fit: Goblin fandom’s #1 essential course when visiting Korea. High proportion of JP·CN·Southeast Asian fans. No foreign language guidance (use maps + Google navigation).
  • Timing: Spring (cherry blossoms, April) and fall (gingko trees, October) are at their peak.
  • Manner: Do not disturb students in class. Entry is restricted during event and exam periods.

5. Ganghwa Island Oepori and Toemori Ports — Goblin Beach Scene

Work: Goblin (2016-2017). Some of the beach and port scenes where Gong Yoo and Kim Go-eun walk were filmed in the Oepo-ri Port area of ​​Ganghwa Island (the Quebec, Canada scenes were actually filmed in Canada). The portions of Ganghwa Island were filmed domestically.

  • Location: Oepo-ri dock or port near Hwado-myeon, Ganghwa-gun, Incheon. 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours by bus from Seoul (via Ganghwa Bus Terminal).
  • Admission: Free, outdoor.
  • Foreigners fit: One-night course combining drama fans + Ganghwa Island history (Dolmen, Jeondeungsa Temple) available.
  • Timing: 30 minutes before sunset (golden hour beach photo).
  • Manner: Fishing port — no interference with fishing.

6. Gunsan Modern Cultural District — Goblin Street Scene·Modern Architecture

Work: Gunsan street scene from Goblin (2016-2017). The background is the Modern History Museum, an old Japanese-style building, and the Lee Seongdang Bakery area.

  • Location: The area of ​​Jangmi-dong, Gunsan-si, Jeollabuk-do. 20 minutes by bus or taxi from KTX Gunsan Station.
  • Admission: Modern History Museum, adults 2,000 won. Outdoor street free.
  • Foreigner fit: A combination of Korean modern architecture + drama sacred sites. Includes visit to Lee Seongdang (bakery founded in 1945).
  • Timing: Recommended during the day on weekdays (congested on weekends).
  • Manner: No flash allowed in the museum.

Axis 3: Itaewon Class·Crash Landing on You·Seoul Forest (3 selections)

7. DDP (Dongdaemun Design Plaza) — Itaewon Class and many others

Work: Itaewon Class (2020, JTBC), many other Netflix dramas. DDP exterior and night lighting frequently appear in the background of modern Seoul.

  • Location: 281 Eulji-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul. Connected to Exit 1 of Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station on Subway Lines 2, 4, and 5.
  • Admission: Free in outdoor space/exhibition hall. Fees apply for internal exhibitions (varies by exhibition).
  • Foreigner fit: Open 24 hours outdoors. No. 1 night lighting photo spot. A must for fashion/K-content fans.
  • Timing: Best for taking photos at night (after 21:00) when the lights are on.
  • Manner: Refrain from touching or leaning on the exhibition glass exterior.

8. Seoul Forest — Crash Landing on You: City Park filming location

Work: Crash Landing on You (2019-2020, tvN), many other drama walking and dating scenes.

  • Location: 273 Ttukseom-ro, Seongdong-gu, Seoul. 3-minute walk from Exit 3 of Seoul Forest Station on the Suin Bundang Subway Line.
  • Admission: Free (open all year round).
  • Foreigner fit: Combination of picnic near the Han River + drama sacred site. It is also popular with families as it has a deer release area (free admission).
  • Timing: Best for spring (cherry blossoms, April) and fall (autumn leaves, October to November).
  • Manner: There is a no-barbecue zone. Pet leash required.

9. Gwangjang Market — A regular background for market scenes in squid games and dramas

Works: Many dramas set in traditional markets in Seoul, including Squid Game Season 1 (2021). The Gwangjang Market’s unique structure surrounded by food stalls appears repeatedly in market scenes in dramas and movies.

  • Location: 88 Changgyeonggung-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul. 15-minute walk from City Hall Station on Subway Lines 1 and 2 and Jongno 3-ga Station on Line 5, or 10-minute walk from Euljiro 1-ga Station on Line 2.
  • Admission: Free. Food stalls cost 4,000 to 8,000 won per serving.
  • Foreigner fit: Bindaetteok, raw meat, and drug gimbap = 3 essential foods for foreigners. Many English menus and English-speaking merchants.
  • Timing: Lunch 11:00~14:00 or Dinner 17:00~20:00 (Night stalls open until 22:00).
  • Manner: Check the vendor’s notice before taking pictures of food. Be careful with your luggage in the narrow aisles during rush hour.

Axis 4: Foreigner Infrastructure — BIFF·Tour·Multilingual (3 lines)

10. Busan Cinema Center (BIFF Official Screening Theater) — No. 1 among cinephiles

Work: Busan International Film Festival (BIFF) official venue. Held every October. Korean film world premiere/director GV (conversation with audience) held.

  • Location: 120 Suyeonggangbyeon-daero, Haeundae-gu, Busan. 10-minute walk from Centum City Station on Subway Line 2.
  • Admission: Free in outdoor space. Screenings are paid (tickets 1,000 to 30,000 won, foreigners can make online reservations — biff.kr).
  • Foreigner fit: English program guide provided. Cinephiles from around the world gather. Beware of early closing of hotels in Busan during BIFF period in October.
  • Timing: BIFF period in October (approximately 10 days). Even during the non-BIFF period, general screenings are available at CGV and Lotte Cinema stores.
  • Manner: Cell phone lighting or filming is strictly prohibited during the screening (will be expelled).

11. Korean Film Archive Cinematheque KOFA — Parasite·Bong Joon-ho Archive

Work: Bong Joon-ho and Park Chan-wook filmography retrospective, regular screening of Korean classic films.

  • Location: 400 Worldcupbuk-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul. 5-minute walk from Mapo-gu Office Station on Subway Line 6 or take a bus toward DMC.
  • Admission: Regular movie 3,000~5,000 won. English guidance available (official site eng.koreafilm.or.kr).
  • Foreigner fit: A theater dedicated to Korean movies with subtitles. During special exhibitions, English subtitled versions are screened frequently.
  • Timing: Visit is recommended after checking the special exhibition schedule (monthly program announcement).

12. K-content tour — Klook·KKday official package

Work: A bundled tour of filming locations including Squid Game, Parasite, and Goblin.

  • Operation: Searchable on Klook (klook.com) and KKday (kkday.com) with keywords “drama filming location tour,” “parasite filming location tour,” and “goblin shrine tour.”
  • Cost: KRW 30,000~KRW 80,000/person (half day~1 day, guide included).
  • Foreigner fit: Guide options in English, Chinese, and Japanese. Transportation is included, so it is efficient for first-time foreigners.
  • Timing: Reservation recommended at least 3 days in advance. BIFF season, spring and fall close early.

❌ The 3 biggest mistakes foreigners make

1. Assumed to be “Squid Game filming location = specific building” — Most of Season 1 was filmed on a complete set (Gyeonggi-do set, external undisclosed location). The actual background distance is only partial. Beware of false advertising by tour companies.

2. Canadian scene mistaken for Ganghwa Island — The Goblin Canada (Quebec) scene is an actual Canadian location. Ganghwa Island only has domestic ports.

3. Expect same-day BIFF tickets — Popular BIFF screenings in October sold out within minutes after ticket sales opened. Foreigners also need to make advance online reservations at biff.kr.

  • Busan (BIFF): KTX Seoul → Busan 2 hours and 20 minutes. Directly connected to Centum City Station on Busan Subway Line 2.

FAQ 5

Q1. Can I go to the actual filming location of Squid Game?

The set was filmed at an undisclosed Gyeonggi-do studio. Related atmosphere experiences are available in Gwangjang Market (similar to a market scene) and the Gangnam overpass area (city background). An official tour may be released after Season 3 is released.

Q2. Can I enter the parasite semi-basement house directly?

Physical residence — no indoor entry allowed. Only outside alleys and stairs are allowed. Residents complain when they enter rudely.

Q3. When is the best time to visit Goblin Ewha Womans University?

Cherry blossom season in April and ginkgo tree foliage season in October. Hansan during vacation periods (January-February, July-August).

Q4. How do I reserve tickets for BIFF for foreigners?

biff.kr → Convert to English → Sign up (email OK) → Online payment (Visa·Mastercard). Tickets are issued at the on-site kiosk at the Busan Cinema Center. Reservations open in early September every year.

Q5. K-content tour vs independent travel — which is better?

If it's your first visit, I recommend the Klook half-day tour (30,000-50,000 won) - efficient with transportation included and guide explanation. If you are returning, the free course (subway + Naver Map) is flexible.
