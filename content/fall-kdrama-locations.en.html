🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide pairs autumn foliage with K-drama filming locations. We rewrote it on two principles: ① foliage timing follows the 2026 forecasts (Korea Tourism Organization Sep 8, Korea Forest Service Sep 22, Gangwon Regional Meteorological Administration Sep 28), listed as each source gives it, and ② a drama title appears only where the Korea Tourism Organization's official page names it for that place. That is why we dropped the old guide's "Yeongjongdo = Goblin", "Songdo = Mr. Sunshine", "Jeonju Hanok Village = Love in the Moonlight", "Nami Island 16,000 won, 07:30–21:40" and "peak foliage Oct 27–Nov 2", which do not match the official sources. Hours and fees are as of the VisitKorea English pages on 2026-10-08, so they may lag behind the operators' own sites. Check once more before you go.

🎬 Three Places Officially Tied to Dramas

  • Jumunjin Breakwater (주문진 방파제) (Gangneung (강릉)): VisitKorea names it as a filming location for Goblin (2016). Free and outdoors. There is no foliage here, but it pairs well with Gangneung's autumn sea. The old guide's "Goblin at Yeongjongdo" does not appear on any official page.
  • Boseong Daehan Dawon Tea Plantation (보성 대한다원): The official page lists Summer Scent, Legend of the Blue Sea and The Rebel. Admission 4,000 won. Green tea fields through November, with foliage nearby.
  • Andong Hahoe Village (안동 하회마을): It is mentioned as a drama filming location, but no title appears in the text we reviewed, so we do not list one. Admission 5,000 won. Hahoe Village's own website could not be reached.

🍂 Foliage Itself — Four Mountains, One Island, One Road

  • Seoraksan (설악산): First color observed Sep 28 (Gangwon Regional Meteorological Administration), peak Oct 20 (Korea Forest Service) to Oct 25 (Korea Tourism Organization). The cable car announces its operating hours the day before and its fee is not posted (VisitKorea).
  • Naejangsan (내장산): Peak Nov 4–11. Cable car 8,000 won round trip, 09:00–18:00 (VisitKorea). The foliage festival is listed only as "October" — Jeongeup (정읍) has not posted 2026 dates.
  • Bukhansan (북한산): Peak Nov 4, free. The foliage mountain closest to Seoul (for the trail loop, see #196).
  • Nami Island (남이섬) (Chuncheon (춘천)): 08:00–21:00, adult 19,000 won (16,000 won is the discounted rate) — a correction to the old guide's figure. The official site (namisum.com) returns a 403, so the figure is from VisitKorea.
  • Damyang Metasequoia Road (담양 메타세쿼이아길): 2,000 won, closed only on Lunar New Year and Chuseok. Golden-brown in mid-November.

🏯 Seoul Palaces — Memorize the Closed Days

  • Changdeokgung Huwon (창덕궁 후원): Closed Mondays, palace 3,000 + Huwon 5,000 won, entry by guided tour only. The 2026 dates for the autumn special viewing and the moonlight tour are not officially confirmed (the Changdeokgung site could not be reached).
  • Gyeongbokgung (경복궁): Closed Tuesdays, 3,000 won, hours vary by season. The old guide's "both open 09:00–18:00, 3,000 won" was wrong.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Planning around "peak Oct 27–Nov 2" — the 2026 forecasts run from Oct 20 to Nov 13 depending on the mountain. See the table above.

2. Nami Island at 16,000 won — the adult fee is 19,000 won.

3. Changdeokgung on Monday, Gyeongbokgung on Tuesday — both are closed.

4. Treating Huwon as free-roaming — guided tour only, 5,000 won extra.

5. Looking for Goblin at Yeongjongdo — the official location is Jumunjin Breakwater.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Where can I see foliage in Seoul on a day trip? — Bukhansan (peak Nov 4) or Changdeokgung Huwon (closed Mondays).

2. Is it worth going to Seoraksan now? — It started turning on Sep 28, with peak Oct 20–25.

3. Which drama locations are good in autumn? — Boseong Daehan Dawon (4,000 won) and Nami Island (19,000 won).

4. What about the Naejangsan festival? — October; 2026 dates are not officially posted.

5. How much is the cable car? — Naejangsan is 8,000 won round trip. Seoraksan's fee is not posted and hours are announced the day before.

6. Why do the forecasts differ? — The Korea Tourism Organization (Sep 8), Korea Forest Service (Sep 22) and Meteorological Administration (Sep 28) published at different times and use different methods, so they differ by up to a week.
