How to Choose a Korean Duty-Free Shop — 8 Shops (5 Downtown, 3 Airport: Lotte Myeongdong Main Store, Shilla Seoul, Shinsegae Myeongdong, Hyundai Trade Center and More), Online Duty Free, and the Passport and Departure Requirements (Official Values, October 2026)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

At the register the staff checks your passport and hands you a receipt instead of the goods — the shopping bag is yours at the airport on the day you leave. This guide splits which duty-free shop to use and how to buy into three branches for travelers leaving Korea: downtown duty-free shops in the city center, airport duty-free shops in the departure hall, and online duty-free shops on the web and in apps. Branch addresses and hours use only values read directly today from the official branch guides of Lotte, Shilla, Shinsegae and Hyundai.

Tax refunds (a VAT refund for goods bought in regular shops) are a different system from duty free, so they go to the #1231 Tax Refund Guide. The airport pickup counter procedure has its own page, the #1332 Pickup Counter Manual. The Dongdaemun (동대문) wholesale market in the old guide is not a duty-free shop, so it was removed. For Dongdaemun shopping, see #583 Dongdaemun Market Shopping.

The old guide put Shilla Duty Free in "Hannam (한남)" and Shinsegae Duty Free in "Gangnam (강남)." On the official branch guide, Shilla Duty Free Seoul is at 249 Dongho-ro (동호로), Jung-gu (중구) (next to The Shilla Hotel). The Shinsegae Duty Free official branch guide lists only Myeongdong and Incheon Airport. Lines such as "No. 1 in sales" and "better discount rates" had no source, so they were removed.

🏙 Downtown Duty Free — 5 Spots

  • Lotte Duty Free Myeongdong Main Store (롯데면세점 명동본점) — 30 Eulji-ro (을지로), Jung-gu (중구), Seoul, floors 9–12. 09:30–20:00 (closing times differ by brand). Branch phone 02-759-6600, main line 1688-3000 (Lotte Duty Free official branch page)
  • Lotte Duty Free World Tower (롯데면세점 월드타워점) — 300 Olympic-ro (올림픽로), Songpa-gu (송파구), Seoul, Avenuel Jamsil (에비뉴엘 잠실점) floors 8 and 9. 10:30–19:30. Branch phone 02-3213-3700 (Lotte Duty Free official branch page)
  • Shilla Duty Free Seoul (신라면세점 서울점) — 249 Dongho-ro (동호로), Jung-gu (중구), Seoul. 09:30–20:00, open every day. Main line 1688-1110. From Exit 5 of Dongguk Univ. Station (동대입구역) on Line 3, go to the main gate of The Shilla Hotel (신라호텔) and take the hotel shuttle. Show your taxi receipt at the information desk to get a same-day prepaid card worth ₩10,000 (~$7.50) (once a month) (Shilla Duty Free official branch page)
  • Shinsegae Duty Free Myeongdong (신세계면세점 명동점) — 77 Toegye-ro (퇴계로), Jung-gu (중구), Seoul, floors 8–12 of the Shinsegae Department Store Main Store. Mon–Thu 10:30–20:00, Fri–Sun 10:30–20:30 (public holidays included). Hoehyeon Station (회현역) on Line 4, Exit 7. Customer center 1661-8778 (Shinsegae Duty Free official branch page)
  • Hyundai Duty Free Trade Center (현대면세점 무역센터점) — 517 Teheran-ro (테헤란로), Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul, floors 8 and 9. Mon–Thu 10:30–20:00, Fri–Sun 10:30–20:30, open every day. From Exit 5 of Samseong Station (삼성역) on Line 2, it connects directly to basement level 1 of the department store. Main line 1811-6688 (Hyundai Duty Free official branch page)

✈ Airport Duty Free — 3 Spots

  • Shilla Duty Free Incheon Airport T2 (신라면세점 인천공항점 T2) — Incheon Airport Terminal 2. 06:30–21:30. The main shops are near gates 251, 252 and 253 on the basis of Departure Hall 2 (Shilla Duty Free official branch page)
  • Shinsegae Duty Free Incheon Airport (신세계면세점 인천공항점) — Incheon Airport Terminals 1 and 2, 3rd floor. Terminal 1 06:30–21:30 (open every day, some shops 24 hours), Terminal 2 06:30–21:30 (Shinsegae Duty Free official branch page)
  • Lotte Duty Free Gimpo Airport (롯데면세점 김포공항점) — Gimpo Airport international terminal, 3rd floor. 06:10–20:30. Use it when leaving Gimpo for Japan, China or Taiwan (Lotte Duty Free official branch page)
  • Airport shops can only be entered after you clear departure inspection. For automated immigration, see the #1412 SES and Smart Pass Manual

💻 Online Duty Free — The Order Cutoff Depends on Your Departure Time

  • You log in and buy with the traveler's own ID. If the nationality, date of birth, sex, English name as printed on the passport, or passport number entered at sign-up and ordering differs from your passport, you cannot receive the goods (Lotte Duty Free official usage guide)
  • The order cutoff is set by where the stock is and when you depart. Example: to receive goods from Lotte Myeongdong Main Store stock when you depart from Incheon between 00:00 and 02:00, order by 14:30 the day before departure (Lotte Duty Free official order-time page). For Hyundai Trade Center stock with a 03:00–16:59 departure, the cutoff is 19:30 the day before (Hyundai Duty Free official shopping guide)
  • A departure date can be changed up to 3 days before departure, and only to a date within 150 days of the order date (Lotte Duty Free official usage guide)

📋 Who Goes, and What to Bring — Common Requirements

  • Who: Koreans and foreigners leaving the country for overseas. You cannot buy for domestic travel, Jeju Island included. The person departing must make the purchase personally (Lotte Duty Free official usage guide)
  • What: Your physical passport. At the airport pickup counter you show your boarding pass and physical passport, have the passport details matched, and then sign for receipt (Lotte Duty Free official pickup counter guide)
  • Limit: There is no limit on duty-free purchases by foreigners when leaving. When you re-enter Korea, the duty-free allowance per person is US$800 (Lotte Duty Free official usage guide)
  • Pickup: Goods bought downtown or online are received only at the designated pickup counter on your departure day. There is no home delivery. The pickup counter is crowded on departure day, so go 1 hour before departure. If you do not pick up within 15 days of the departure date, the order is canceled (Lotte Duty Free official usage guide and pickup counter guide)

🥗 Diet — At the Duty-Free Food Corners

  • Shilla Seoul says it sells food, and Lotte Gimpo Airport says it sells red ginseng (hongsam, 홍삼) (each official branch page). First check whether the ingredient list includes pork, gelatin or salted fermented seafood (jeotgal)
  • Vegetarian: VisitKorea advises checking for the "vegan certification logo" while shopping (VisitKorea vegetarian travel tips)
  • Halal: We could not confirm any halal-certified product labeling on the duty-free official pages. VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (이태원) (15 Usadan-ro 10-gil (우사단로10길), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00–21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF, 한국이슬람교중앙회). Certification can change, so check before you visit
  • For show-and-tell cards, see the #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Duty-Free Shopping Practicalities — Solo, Reservations, Payment, Pickup, Getting Around

  • Solo and reservations: Go without a reservation. Just bring your passport and your departure flight details
  • Payment: For cards and mobile payment in general, see the #527 Payment System
  • Pickup: For pickup counter locations and order of steps, see the #1332 Pickup Counter Manual. Liquids and gels bought when you re-enter Korea must always go in checked baggage (Lotte Duty Free official usage guide)
  • When you confuse it with a tax refund: For refunds on goods bought in department stores and marts, see the #1304 Airport Kiosk Step-by-Step Guide
  • Getting around: For the subway to downtown duty-free shops, see #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards. For a route that combines them with department stores, see the #449 Department Store and Mall Guide

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "I carry out what I bought at a downtown duty-free shop right away" — You receive it on your departure day at the airport or port pickup counter (Lotte Duty Free official usage guide)

2. "Duty free also works on domestic flights to Jeju" — You cannot buy for domestic travel, Jeju included (Lotte Duty Free official usage guide)

3. "Shilla Duty Free is in Hannam and Shinsegae Duty Free is in Gangnam" — Shilla Seoul is at 249 Dongho-ro (동호로), Jung-gu (중구), and the official Shinsegae Duty Free branches are Myeongdong and Incheon Airport

4. "A rough spelling of my English name is fine" — If the order details differ from your passport, you cannot take delivery (Lotte Duty Free official pickup counter guide)
