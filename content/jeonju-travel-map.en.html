⚠ Jeonju’s 3 mistakes

1. "Jeonju = only bibimbap" — No bean sprout soup/Korean food experience: Only bibimbap is recognized. In reality, it is a full experience of bean sprout soup, Korean food, and makgeolli.

2. "Hanok village = daytime only" — Night view and no use of hanbok: Daytime movement only. In reality, it is Night Hanok Village Lighting/Hanbok Night Walk signature.

3. "Day trip" — 1 night Hanok stay Miji: Day trip recognition. In fact, one night of Hanok stay full experience recommended (KTX 1.5h).
