Jeonju Guide — Hanok Village, Gyeonggijeon, Jeondong Cathedral, Nambu Market and Bibimbap: 2026 Official Values and How to Get There

Subtitle: Hanok Village open 24 hours · tourist information centers 09:00–18:00 / Gyeonggijeon Shrine ₩3,000, free on the last Wednesday / Jeondong Cathedral 09:00–17:00 / Jeonju Hyanggyo closed Mondays / Nambu Market · Youth Mall · night market / Jeonju Hanji Museum free / KTX from Yongsan about 1 h 40 min — based on the official Jeonju City culture and tourism and Hanok Village pages as of 2026-10-08

ISSUE 03 · #384 · 2026-05-25

🎯 How to Use This Guide

The heart of a Jeonju trip is Jeonju Hanok Village (전주 한옥마을) (open 24 hours), and Gyeonggijeon Shrine (경기전), Jeondong Cathedral (전동성당), Omokdae and Imokdae (오목대·이목대), Jeonju Hyanggyo (전주향교) and Nambu Market (남부시장) are all within walking distance. The hours, fees and addresses below are only the values read on 2026-10-08 from the official Jeonju City culture and tourism page (tour.jeonju.go.kr) and the official Hanok Village page (hanok.jeonju.go.kr); where the official pages give no fee or hours, we write "not posted." For bibimbap (비빔밥) restaurants, we list only the 8 shown on the city's official bibimbap page and give no prices. Several claims from the older guide were dropped because they differ from the official sources: "700+ hanok," "8% of foreign visitors," "KTX 1.5 hours," "Pungnammun and Pungpaejigwan as one stop," and "1339 as a medical hotline" (1339 is an infectious-disease consultation line; the tourist interpretation line is 1330).

🏘 Hanok Village — 24 Hours, Leave the Car Outside

  • Officially open 24 hours, year-round. 6 public parking lots: Lots 1–3 are ₩1,200 per 30 minutes (₩600 per extra 15 minutes, daily maximum ₩14,400); Lot 4 and the National Intangible Heritage Center's surface lot are free (official). A free shuttle runs on weekdays 10:00–18:30 between Lot 4 and Taejo-ro Station (태조로역) via Jaman Mural Village (자만벽화마을).
  • The 4 tourist information centers are open 09:00–18:00 (lunch break) and offer interpretation and luggage storage. Hanbok experiences are on the official menu, but vendors and prices are not posted.
  • At night, the lantern-lit alleys and the evening view from Omokdae and Imokdae (오목대·이목대). The information centers and the cathedral close at 17:00–18:00, so see the sights by day and save the night for a stroll.

🏛 Gyeonggijeon, Jeondong Cathedral and Omokdae — One Walking Loop

  • Gyeonggijeon Shrine (경기전) (home of King Taejo's portrait): 09:00 to 18:00–20:00 depending on the season, adult ₩3,000, free on the last Wednesday and half price on the last Saturday (official). 44 Taejo-ro.
  • Jeondong Cathedral (전동성당): across the street. 09:00–17:00 (varies with the main hall's schedule), built 1908–1914 (official). Whether visits are restricted during Mass is not posted.
  • Omokdae and Imokdae (오목대·이목대): 55 Girin-daero, an evening-view spot (official) — the older guide's word "observatory" does not appear in the official sources.
  • Jeonju Hyanggyo (전주향교): Mar–Oct 09:00–18:00, Nov–Feb 10:00–17:00, closed Mondays.

🍚 Bibimbap and Kongnamul-gukbap — Restaurants on the Official Page

  • The city's official bibimbap page: 30+ ingredients, and the restaurants it introduces are 8 — Gapgi Hoegwan (갑기회관), Gogung (고궁), Gajok Hoegwan (가족회관), Giwa (기와), Kkotdamjip (꽃담집), Seongmidang (성미당), Hangukjip (한국집) and Baeksong Hoegwan (백송회관) (prices not posted). Waengi-jip and Sambaek-jip from the older guide are not on the official page.
  • Kongnamul-gukbap (콩나물국밥) (the toryeom style, with the rice rinsed in hot broth) and pi-sundae (blood sausage) are around Nambu Market (남부시장) (#642).

🎎 Hanji and Traditional Culture — Two Places a Bit Farther Out

  • Jeonju Hanji Museum (전주한지박물관) (59 Palbok-ro): 09:00–17:00, closed Sundays, Mondays and public holidays, free (activities included). It is away from Hanok Village, so take a taxi or bus.
  • Korea Traditional Culture Center (한국전통문화전당) (20 Hyeonmu 1-gil): 5 minutes on foot from Hanok Village (official). Hours and activity fees not posted.

🚄 Getting There

  • KTX from Yongsan to Jeonju Station (전주역) takes about 1 h 40 min (official Jeonju City; Yongsan Station (용산역) is recommended over Seoul Station).
  • Bus numbers and times from Jeonju Station to Hanok Village are not posted officially — search "Jeonju Hanok Village" in a map app.
  • Tourist interpretation line 1330. The free Lot 4 shuttle (weekdays) takes you to Taejo-ro Station (태조로역).

📅 This Year's Festivals Are Over

  • Jeonju International Film Festival 2026, 4/29–5/8 · Jeonju World Sori Festival 2026, 8/12–16 — both have ended. Check the official pages in the first half of next year for 2027 dates.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Taking the KTX from Seoul Station — departing from Yongsan is better (official).

2. Visiting the Hyanggyo or the Hanji Museum on Monday — both are closed (the Hanji Museum is also closed on Sunday).

3. Walking past Gyeonggijeon without knowing the fee — it is free on the last Wednesday.

4. Treating Pungnammun and Pungpaejigwan as one place — they are different places. Pungnammun Gate (풍남문) is next to Nambu Market.

5. Calling 1339 — that is an infectious-disease consultation line. For tourism, call 1330.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. What if I only have one day? — Morning at Gyeonggijeon Shrine (경기전) and Jeondong Cathedral (전동성당) → lunch of bibimbap (the 8 official restaurants) → afternoon in Hanok Village and Omokdae and Imokdae (오목대·이목대) → evening at Nambu Market (남부시장) (night market Fri and Sat).

2. What if I drive? — Free Lot 4 plus the free weekday shuttle.

3. What about hanbok? — The official menu lists only the experience itself; vendors and prices are not posted. Ask at an information center.

4. What about luggage? — The tourist information centers store luggage (official).

5. What is there to see at night? — The evening view at Omokdae, the Hanok Village alleys, and the Fri–Sat night market.

6. What about English? — Interpretation at the tourist information centers (official) plus 1330.
