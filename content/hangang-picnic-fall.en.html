West Seoul Parks Guide: World Cup Park, Haneul Park, Metasequoia Forest Path, Noeul Park, Seonyudo Park, Boramae Park, Seoul Trail Course 15

Subtitle: World Cup Park (hours vary by month) · Haneul Park (05:00–20:30) · Haneul Park Metasequoia Forest Path (always open) · Noeul Park (05:30–20:00) · Seonyudo Park (06:00–24:00) · Boramae Park (always open) · Seoul Trail Course 15 (7.7km, about 2 hours 10 minutes) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers the large parks in western Seoul. They are World Cup Park, which transformed the Nanjido landfill, with Haneul Park and Noeul Park inside it, Seonyudo Park, a former water purification plant; Boramae Park, on the site of the Air Force Academy; and Seoul Trail Course 15 on the Noeul·Haneul Park section. All are registered as open year-round. The Han River parks are in the #706 Hangang Parks Guide: 9 Hangang Parks, Nanji, Mangwon and Ichon on the North Bank, Gangseo, Yanghwa, Banpo, Jamwon, Jamsil and Gwangnaru on the South Bank.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 World Cup Park — World Cup Park, Haneul Park, Metasequoia Forest Path and Noeul Park

World Cup Park (월드컵공원) [World Cup-ro 243-60 (월드컵로 243-60), Mapo-gu, ☎ 02-300-5500] is the Nanjido (난지도) landfill, where Seoul's garbage was buried for 15 years from 1978 to 1993, turned into a park to commemorate the 2002 World Cup and the new millennium. It covers 2,700,000㎡ and is made up of five theme parks: Peace Park (평화의 공원), Haneul Park, Noeul Park, Nanjicheon Park (난지천공원) and Nanji Hangang Park (난지한강공원). It has 733,000 trees of 92 species, the World Cup Park Exhibition Hall (월드컵공원 전시관), which shows the history of Nanjido, and Nanji Pond (난지연못), where you can watch birds.

  • Opening hours: They differ by month. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available. Stroller rental: Not available.

Haneul Park (하늘공원) [Haneulgongwon-ro 95 (하늘공원로 95), Mapo-gu, ☎ World Cup Park 02-300-5500] is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, the park closest to the sky inside World Cup Park, and it shows the barren ground of the landfill returning to nature. It has a silver grass planting area, a mixed-grass meadow, a rock garden, a sunflower planting area, an observation rest stop and wind turbines. In autumn silvery silver grass flowers bloom, Bukhansan (북한산) and the Han River can be seen, and it is known for its sunsets and night views.

  • Opening hours: 05:00–20:30. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Haneul Park Metasequoia Forest Path (하늘공원 메타세쿼이아 숲길) [Sangam-dong (상암동), Mapo-gu, ☎ Western Park Leisure Center (서부공원여가센터) 02-300-5501] is a path created in 1999 so you can see metasequoias along a 450m walkway on the east side and a 900m walkway on the south side of Haneul Park. In 2023 a garden-style green space was created below the east path and a four-season flower path along the south walkway.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Noeul Park (노을공원) [Sangam-dong, Mapo-gu, ☎ World Cup Park 02-300-5500] is a park with sculptures and observation decks on a wide lawn, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization many people come for its views at sunset. It has a park golf course, Noeul Camping Ground (노을캠핑장), a silkworm ecology experience area, a firefly ecology center and an urban farmer garden.

  • Opening hours: 05:30–20:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

📍 Han River Island — Seonyudo Park

Seonyudo Park (선유도공원) [Seonyu-ro 343 (선유로 343), Yeongdeungpo-gu, ☎ 02-2631-9367~8] is on Seonyudo, a small peak island in the middle of the Han River. It was a water purification plant that supplied tap water to southwestern Seoul from 1978 to 2000, and it was regenerated as an ecological park in April 2002. It has the Story Hall (이야기관), an exhibition hall made from the former water-pumping room, the Garden of Green Columns (녹색기둥의 정원), where the roof of the clear-water basin was removed and only the columns were left, the Water Quality Purification Garden (수질정화원), where aquatic plants purify the water, and four circular spaces. You enter from the south side of the Han River by the pedestrian bridge Seonyugyo (선유교).

  • Opening hours: 06:00–24:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Not available.

📍 Dongjak — Boramae Park

Boramae Park (보라매공원) [Yeouidaebang-ro 20-gil 33 (여의대방로20길 33), Dongjak-gu, ☎ 02-2181-1114] is a park developed from 1985 on the site of the Air Force Academy (공군사관학교) and opened on May 5, 1986. Its name comes from 'Boramae' (보라매), the symbol of the Air Force Academy. It has exercise facilities and rest areas.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

📍 Walking Trail — Seoul Trail Course 15 Noeul·Haneul Park Course

Seoul Trail Course 15 Noeul·Haneul Park Course (서울둘레길 15코스 노을·하늘공원코스) [Gayang-dong (가양동), Gangseo-gu, ☎ 02-779-7902~4] is a course themed 'a restored natural path' on the Seoul Trail (서울둘레길), which circles Seoul in 21 courses totaling 156.5km. Passing Gayang Bridge (가양대교), you can see the Han River and Gangnam. It is close to Gayang Station (가양역) and Jeungsan Station (증산역) on the subway.

  • Length and time: 7.7km in total, about 2 hours 10 minutes. Opening hours: Always open.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Haneul Park opens 05:00–20:30 and Noeul Park opens 05:30–20:00 — The hours for World Cup Park as a whole differ by month.
  2. You cannot park at Seonyudo Park — It opens 06:00–24:00, and you enter by the pedestrian bridge Seonyugyo.
  3. Seoul Trail Course 15 takes about 2 hours 10 minutes — It is 7.7km in total and close to Gayang Station and Jeungsan Station.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where do you go to see silver grass?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, Haneul Park inside World Cup Park has a silver grass planting area, and silvery silver grass flowers bloom in autumn. It opens 05:00–20:30.

Q. Which park was once a water purification plant?

A. Seonyudo Park. It was a water purification plant from 1978 to 2000 and was regenerated as an ecological park in April 2002. It opens 06:00–24:00 and you cannot park there.
