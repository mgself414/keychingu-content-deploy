8 Seoul Meditation Places — From Seon Sitting in Temples to One-Day Classes at City Centers: English Classes, Single-Visit Prices, How to Book

Subtitle: Three temples that list day or evening programs on the official Templestay site (Lotus Lantern International Meditation Center, Jogyesa's tea program, and Gilsangsa), Hwagyesa's international Zen center that takes sign-ups through its own site, one free English Seon sitting group across from Jogyesa, and three city meditation centers you can enter for a single visit. All are confirmed as open on Kakao Map. Prices and times are only values we opened ourselves: the official Templestay booking pages, the centers' official sites, owner-registered values on Kakao Map, and Airbnb.

ISSUE 06 · #768 · 2026-11-25 · Seoul Meditation·Seon Single Visits

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 8

This list is for travelers who want to sit down and watch their breath at least once while staying in Seoul. Temple meditation is usually bundled as 'Templestay' and known as a 1-night, 2-day product. But when you open the official Templestay site (templestay.com), the Seoul temples also list separate day programs of two or three hours and free evening Seon sessions. This article picks mainly those day and evening programs.

Outside the temples, we looked for meditation centers you can enter once without a membership. Korean meditation centers mostly sell one-month or three-month courses. Of those, we included only places that publish a one-day or single-visit trial price on an official site, Kakao Map, or Airbnb.

There are three selection rules.

  1. The Kakao Map place info says "open for business."
  2. An official schedule notice, a blog review, or a Kakao review was posted in 2026.
  3. The single-visit fee (free included) and the length of the session were confirmed from an official booking page, an official site, a value the owner registered on Kakao Map (registered within the last 2 years), or Airbnb.

We do not cover the Templestays at Bongeunsa, Geumseonsa, and Myogaksa again here. See the Korea Wellness Retreat Guide (#463) for those.

⚠ Notation principle: Prices, times, and what to bring for temple programs are values from the official Templestay booking pages (viewed 2026-11-25). Each program runs only within a set period. 'Open days' are dates marked as bookable on the booking page's calendar on 2026-11-25. Next to meditation center prices we put the source and the registration date. 'English' is carried over only as far as an official notice or booking platform says it.

① Meditation in Temples — 4 Places

  • Lotus Lantern International Meditation Center (국제선센터) — A temple in Mokdong (목동), listed on the official Templestay site as a 'temple for foreigners.' The easiest program for a traveler to join is the free Saturday-evening vipassana meditation (Dhamma talk, vipassana meditation). Every Saturday from 18:30~21:00 it offers sitting and walking meditation and a Dharma talk in English. First-timers should arrive for the 18:30 orientation. There is no fee, and donations are encouraged. Advance booking is required, and bookings are accepted until the day before. As of 2026-11-25 every Saturday is open, and the program runs through 2026-12-31 (Templestay official). Bring comfortable clothes, a personal cup, and socks. The day program 'K-meditation K-templestay: Luminous Lotus' is open only on 12/3 and 12/17 (Thu) as of 2026-11-25 (Templestay calendar). From 10:00~14:00 it covers Seon meditation theory, posture, and practice, a temple lunch, lantern making, and tea time with a monk, and costs 30,000 won per person (Templestay official, runs 2026-06-03~12-31). A vest-style experience outfit is provided, and the page says to avoid sleeveless tops and slippers. The same page gives the booking deadline as 'until 2 days before,' while the rules section says 'only those who booked 3 days before can attend,' so booking 3 days before is safer. The address is 167 Mokdongdong-ro, Yangcheon-gu (양천구 목동동로 167); the nearest station is Omokgyo Station (오목교역) on Line 5, Exit 8, a 12-minute walk (Kakao Map).
  • Hwagyesa International Zen Center (화계사 국제선원) — A Seon meditation group for foreigners inside Hwagyesa (화계사) at the foot of Bukhansan (북한산). According to the Sunday schedule on the Zen center's site, 12:30 is orientation for first-timers, 13:00 is sitting meditation (three rounds of 30 minutes sitting and 10 minutes walking), and 15:00 is an English Dharma talk. There is no fee, and first-timers are welcome. The site does not describe a booking procedure; inquiries go by email (hwagyesazen@gmail.com) or WhatsApp (Zen center site, viewed 2026-11-25, no page update date shown). If you want to stay overnight, the 1-night, 2-day 'Only Don't Know' program, booked on the Templestay English screen, is 90,000 won (Templestay official, runs through 2026-12-31). The address is 117 Hwagyesa-gil, Gangbuk-gu (강북구 화계사길 117); the nearest station is Hwagye Station (화계역) on the Ui-Sinseol Line, Exit 2, a 17-minute walk (Kakao Map).
  • Jogyesa (조계사) — A temple in the middle of Jongno (종로), booked on the Templestay English screen. The day program is the 'Seon of Tea' tea program at 50,000 won per person; you gather at the information center (left of the main gate) at 14:50, then have tea from 15:00~16:00 and a temple tour from 16:00~16:40. The tea session is led by a professional instructor and may be canceled if the minimum number of participants is not met (Templestay official, runs 2025-12-01~2027-01-31). As of 2026-11-25, the bookable dates are Mondays 11/30·12/7·12/28 (Templestay calendar). The program that includes meditation and tea time with a monk is the regular 1-night, 2-day Templestay, at 80,000 won. It includes lantern making, meditation and tea time, and evening and dawn chanting services (optional) (Templestay official, runs through 2027-01-31). It recommends paying by card through online booking on a PC; card payment on site is not available. A full refund requires canceling 7 days before. The grounds are open 04:30~21:00 by the Kakao Map displayed hours. The address is 55 Ujeongguk-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 우정국로 55); the nearest station is Anguk Station (안국역) on Line 3, Exit 6, an 8-minute walk (Kakao Map).
  • Gilsangsa (길상사) — A temple on a hill in Seongbuk-dong (성북동), which carries the 'Clear and Fragrant' (맑고 향기롭게) spirit of Venerable Beopjeong. The official Templestay site lists a 2-hour 'Templelife' meditation. 'Theory and Meditation' is held on the second Saturday of each month from 14:00~16:00, led by an outreach monk in the Seolbeopjeon (설법전) Dharma hall, and costs 30,000 won (Templestay official, runs 2026-02-01~2027-12-31). As of 2026-11-25 the open date is 12/12 (Sat) (Templestay calendar). The page does not state the language of instruction, and says foreigners should apply after a prior consultation. Consultations are on weekdays 09:00~17:00 at 02-3672-5945. Payment must be made within 3 days of booking to be confirmed, and canceling 7 days before gets a 90% refund. What to bring: comfortable clothes, socks, and a personal cup (Templestay official). The address is 68 Seonjam-ro 5-gil, Seongbuk-gu (성북구 선잠로5길 68). From Hansung Univ. Station (한성대입구역) on Line 4, Exit 6, take village bus Seongbuk 02 (성북02) and get off at Gilsangsa (Templestay official).

② Urban Meditation Spaces — 4 Places

  • Bohwa Seonwon (보화선원) — A small Seon center in a building across from Jogyesa (조계사). It announces its schedule through the Meetup group 'Urban Seon.' According to the group description, all gatherings are free and donations are voluntary. On Saturdays there is sitting meditation from 08:00~11:00 (you can come and go at any time) and a Dharma talk and Q&A from 11:00~12:00. The Saturday Dharma talk is connected by video from California, USA, in English with Korean interpretation. This Saturday schedule is set through 2026-12-12. On Wednesdays at 19:00 there is a beginner Seon class in English and Chinese (Meetup, viewed 2026-11-25). Session-by-session schedules are posted on the Meetup event pages, and first-timers are welcome. For inquiries call 010-5338-8699. The address is 3F, 58 Ujeongguk-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 우정국로 58 3층); the nearest station is Anguk Station (안국역) on Line 3, Exit 6, a 4-minute walk (Kakao Map). The meeting room is on the 2nd floor on Wednesdays and the 3rd floor on Saturdays (Meetup description).
  • Seonmudo Meditation Yoga Center Seoul Main Center (선무도 명상요가센터 서울본원) — A Seonmudo (moving meditation based on Seon practice) training hall. According to a Kakao review dated 2025-02-05, it is under the Jogye Order (조계종) and non-Buddhists can take part. It accepts travelers through the Airbnb service 'K-Martial Arts & Meditation: Seonmudo in Seoul.' The displayed price on 2026-11-25 is 24,000 won per person for a 1-hour 'Moving Meditation' (list price 30,000 won), and the 2-hour one-day class is 80,000 won per person, bookable from a minimum of 200,000 won. Ages 10 and over, up to 15 people, and a full refund if canceled by the day before. The page does not state the language of instruction (Airbnb). The Kakao Map owner-registered menu has 'Seonmudo training experience' (선무도 수련 체험) at 35,000 won (registered 2026-08-07). By owner-registered values, hours are Mon·Wed·Fri 06:30~22:00, Tue·Thu 09:00~22:00, Sat 09:00~18:30, and it is closed on Sundays and public holidays. The address is 3F, 93 Donhwamun-ro 11ga-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 돈화문로11가길 93 3층); the nearest station is Anguk Station (안국역) on Line 3, Exit 3, a 7-minute walk (Kakao Map).
  • Hyeonjon Meditation Center (현존명상센터) — A meditation education center in the alley by the Choi Sun-u Old House (최순우 옛집) in Seongbuk-dong (성북동). The meditation one-day class on its official site is 40,000 won and about 90 minutes. It is a small-group class based on yoga asanas, with tea and Q&A (official site, viewed 2026-11-25). Book with the 'Book a meditation one-day class' button on the site or at 010-3188-3105. The phone is answered on weekdays 09:00~18:00 (official). The site gives no information on the language of the class or what to wear. The Kakao Map displayed hours are Mon·Wed·Fri 09:00~21:30, Tue 09:00~20:30, Thu 09:00~20:00, Sat·Sun 10:00~16:00, but the official site says 'closed Sat, Sun,' so whether a weekend one-day class runs is not confirmed. The address is 2F, 15-2 Seongbuk-ro 15-gil, Seongbuk-gu (성북구 성북로15길 15-2 2층); the nearest station is Hansung Univ. Station (한성대입구역) on Line 4, Exit 5, a 13-minute walk (Kakao Map).
  • Brain Training Center Gwanghwamun Meditation Branch (브레인트레이닝센터 광화문명상점) — A meditation center in Gwanghwamun (광화문), one branch of the Brain Training Center, which has locations nationwide. The Kakao Map owner-registered menu has 'Group daily class, single-visit trial' (그룹 데일리클래스 1회 체험) at 20,000 won and 'Rest, breathing meditation' (쉼, 호흡 명상) at 50,000 won (registered 2025-07-08 and 2025-08-31 respectively). According to one review (2026-10-22), it was a one-day singing bowl and aroma meditation; the reviewer arrived 20 minutes before the booking time and changed into comfortable clothes, and skirts are not allowed because it is moving meditation. It is not confirmed whether this program is the same product as the 20,000 won group class. By owner-registered values, hours are Mon~Fri 10:00~21:00, and it is closed on Sat, Sun, and public holidays. The address is Room 406, 4F, Hyoryeong Building, 32 Mugyo-ro, Jung-gu (중구 무교로 32 효령빌딩 4층 406호); the nearest station is Gwanghwamun Station (광화문역) on Line 5, Exit 5, a 6-minute walk (Kakao Map). We did not find any notice of English instruction, so it is better to go expecting a class in Korean.

🗺 Route — A Day with One Meditation Session, Two Ways

A. Saturday in Jongno (Bohwa Seonwon + Jogyesa + Insa-dong)

  1. Morning Saturday sitting meditation at Bohwa Seonwon (as much as you like between 08:00~11:00), English Dharma talk at 11:00
  2. Lunch Lunch around Anguk (안국) and Insa-dong (인사동)
  3. Afternoon A walk around the Jogyesa grounds and Insa-dong

B. Half a day in Seongbuk-dong (weekday, or Saturday 12/12)

  1. Morning One-day class at Hyeonjon Meditation Center (90 minutes, book a time on the site; whether weekend classes run is not confirmed)
  2. After lunch Walk along Seongbuk-ro (성북로) to see the Choi Sun-u Old House (최순우 옛집), then walk up to Gilsangsa (길상사)
  3. If it is Saturday 12/12 14:00 Gilsangsa 'Theory and Meditation' (prior consultation and booking required)

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Assuming you can just show up for temple meditation — The Saturday vipassana meditation at the Lotus Lantern center is free but needs advance booking. Jogyesa and Hwagyesa Templestay take bookings only until 3 days before. The Lotus Lantern day program lists its booking deadline as '2 days before,' but the rules section also says 'only those who booked 3 days before can attend' (Templestay official). Gilsangsa asks foreigners to have a prior consultation first.
  2. Sleeveless tops and slippers — The booking pages of the Lotus Lantern center, Jogyesa, and Hwagyesa all say to avoid sleeveless tops and slippers. You do not enter the main hall (법당) barefoot, so bring socks.
  3. Trying to pay by card on site — Jogyesa does not take card payment on site, and a full refund requires canceling 7 days before. At Gilsangsa, the booking is confirmed only if you pay within 3 days of booking (Templestay official).

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. Where is it run in English? — The Saturday-evening vipassana meditation at the Lotus Lantern center (in English), the Sunday Dharma talk at Hwagyesa International Zen Center (English), and Bohwa Seonwon (Korean and English together; Wednesdays in English and Chinese). Templestay at Jogyesa, Hwagyesa, and the Lotus Lantern center is booked on the English screen of the official Templestay site. Gilsangsa (foreigners consult first) and the three city meditation centers give no information on the language.
  2. How can I try it without spending money? — The Lotus Lantern center's Saturday vipassana meditation, the Hwagyesa Zen Center's Sunday Seon sitting, and the Bohwa Seonwon gatherings are all free (donation encouraged at the Lotus Lantern center, voluntary at Bohwa Seonwon). Among the paid options, the Brain Training Center group single-visit trial (20,000 won) and the 1-hour Seonmudo class on Airbnb (24,000 won displayed on 2026-11-25) are on the low end.
  3. Where can I go on a Sunday? — The Sunday Seon sitting at Hwagyesa International Zen Center (12:30 orientation). Seonmudo and the Brain Training Center are closed on Sundays, and whether Hyeonjon Meditation Center runs a weekend one-day class is not confirmed.
