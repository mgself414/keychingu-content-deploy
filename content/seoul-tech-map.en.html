🎯 Why a "Seoul Tech" Meta Guide

Tech-focused visitors to Korea — IT conference-goers, game festival fans, VR/AR enthusiasts, startup watchers. Korea means Samsung, LG, SK, Naver, and Kakao headquarters + the Pangyo (판교), Gangnam, Teheran Valley (테헤란밸리), and Guro-Gasan (구로·가산) IT clusters + a gaming industry ranked in the global Top 3 — Nexon (넥슨), NCsoft (엔씨소프트), Krafton (크래프톤) — + a mature VR/AR infrastructure.

This guide covers 12 signatures across 4 tech axes. Category meta guide, part 13.
