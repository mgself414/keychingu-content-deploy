Han River Cruise and Water Leisure Guide: E-Land Cruise and Seoul Cruise (Yeouido), Some Sevit and Some Sevit Tubester (Banpo), Hangang Yacht Tour Sailing Paradise and Hyundai Yacht Seoul Branch

Subtitle: E-Land Cruise (Tuesday to Sunday 11:00~22:00, Monday 15:00~22:00) · Seoul Cruise (Yeouido ↔ Ara Waterway) · Some Sevit · Some Sevit Tubester (March to October) · Sailing Paradise (10:00~22:00, age 2 and over) · Hyundai Yacht Seoul Branch (12:00~21:00, closed Mondays) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places in Seoul where you can ride a boat or play on the Han River (한강). It covers 2 cruise operators in Yeouido (여의도), Some Sevit and its Tubester attraction in Banpo (반포), and 2 yacht experiences. No fee is registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Yeouido Cruises — E-Land Cruise and Seoul Cruise

E-Land Cruise (이랜드크루즈) (Han River Cruise) (280 Yeoui-dongno (여의동로), Yeongdeungpo-gu (영등포구), ☎ 02-6291-6900) is a Han River cruise that first set sail on October 26, 1986. Today E-Land Cruise Co., Ltd. (㈜이랜드크루즈) runs two terminals, Yeouido (여의도) and Jamsil (잠실). There is the Story Cruise (스토리크루즈) for daytime views, the Music Cruise (뮤직크루즈) for night views and the Banpo Bridge Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (반포대교 달빛무지개분수), and the Moonlight Cruise (달빛크루즈) that pairs night views with a jazz performance, along with the onboard buffets, the Lunch Buffet Cruise (런치 뷔페크루즈) and the Dinner Buffet Cruise (디너 뷔페크루즈).

  • Opening hours: Monday 15:00~22:00, Tuesday to Sunday 11:00~22:00. You must arrive at the ticket office 20 minutes before departure. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Age: All ages. Parking: Available.

Seoul Cruise (서울크루즈) (231 Yeouido Hangang Park-gil (여의도한강공원길), Yeongdeungpo-gu, ☎ 1551-1132) is a cruise company that Hyundai Cruise (현대유람선), founded in 1983, renamed when it built a new terminal in Yeouido. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is the first marine-tourism cruise operator in Korea, and the only passenger and cruise company that connects the Han River and the Ara Waterway (Arabaetgil) (아라뱃길) through the Ara Lock Gate (아라갑문) and sails from the Han River to the West Sea. It runs the Yeouido Cruise Terminal (여의도유람선터미널) and the Ara Gimpo Passenger Terminal (아라김포여객터미널). Alongside products that show the Han River scenery, there is a weekend Dinner Fireworks Cruise (디너 불꽃 크루즈) that passes through the Ara Waterway to watch fireworks in Gimpo (김포).

  • Opening hours: Vary by product. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Capacity: About 800 people. Age: All ages. Parking: Not available.

📍 Banpo — Some Sevit and Some Sevit Tubester

Some Sevit (Sebitseom) (세빛섬) (2085-14 Olympic-daero (올림픽대로), Seocho-gu (서초구), ☎ 1566-3433) is a complex cultural space built on the Han River. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is a large floating structure, with buildings built on a buoyant structure floating on the water. It is made up of three islands, Chaebit (채빛), Solbit (솔빛) and Gavit (가빛), and Yevit (예빛), a media art gallery that screens videos and content.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by facility. Parking: Available.

Some Sevit Tubester (세빛섬 튜브스터) (same address, ☎ 02-790-7090) is a water leisure activity at Some Sevit where you play on the water in tubes and boats. Inside the Tubester there are a round table, LED lighting and parasols.

  • Opening hours: March to April, weekdays 15:00~23:00, weekends and public holidays 13:00~23:00. May to October, weekdays 16:00~24:00, weekends and public holidays 14:00~24:00.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Closed in bad weather.

📍 Yachts — Sailing Paradise (Yeouido) and Hyundai Yacht Seoul Branch (Banpo)

Hangang Yacht Tour Sailing Paradise (한강요트투어 세일링파라다이스) (252 Yeoui-dongno, Yeongdeungpo-gu, ☎ 02-761-0120) is a place to ride a sailing yacht on the Han River. You ride with a professional skipper and can see the city night view and natural scenery. It runs on advance reservation and has safety equipment such as life jackets.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~22:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Age: 2 and over.

Hyundai Yacht Seoul Branch (현대요트 서울지점) (350 Dongjak-daero (동작대로), Seocho-gu, Banpo Hangang Park The River (반포한강공원 더리버), ☎ 0507-1360-1976) is a place for water sports such as yachts, dinghy yachts (small 1~3 person sailing yachts with no engine or cabin) and kayaks. It has the yachts Black Cat (블랙캣) and Blue Jean (블루진), and around sunset you can watch the sunset over the Han River and Seoul's forest of buildings from the water.

  • Opening hours: 12:00~21:00. Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Parking: 1,000 won for the first 30 minutes, then 200 won added every 10 minutes.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. No fee is registered — none of the six places has fee information registered.
  2. E-Land Cruise opens later on Monday — it opens at 15:00 on Monday and 11:00 Tuesday to Sunday, and you must go to the ticket office 20 minutes before departure.
  3. Some Sevit Tubester has registered hours only for March to October — it closes in bad weather.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which cruises have night views and performances?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, they are E-Land Cruise's Music Cruise (night view and Banpo Bridge Moonlight Rainbow Fountain) and Moonlight Cruise (night view and jazz performance). Seoul Cruise has a weekend Dinner Fireworks Cruise that passes through the Ara Waterway to watch fireworks in Gimpo.

Q. Where can I ride a boat or try water leisure activities?

A. They are Sailing Paradise (Hangang Yacht Tour Sailing Paradise, sailing yacht with a professional skipper, age 2 and over, advance reservation), Hyundai Yacht Seoul Branch (yachts, dinghy yachts and kayaks) and Some Sevit Tubester (tubes and boats).
