Dongdaemun Markets Guide: Dongdaemun General Market with the Accessory and Hanbok Arcades, Pyeonghwa Market and Jeil Pyeonghwa Market, Dongdaemun Stationery and Toy Street, Dongmyo Flea Market and Dongdaemun History and Culture Park

Subtitle: Dongdaemun General Market (fabrics 08:00~18:00, closed Sundays, wedding goods and home interior partly open) · Accessory Arcade (09:30~19:00) · Hanbok Arcade (08:00~18:00) · Seoul Pyeonghwa Market (22:00~18:00 the next day, closed Saturdays and Sundays) · Jeil Pyeonghwa Market (open by day and by night) · Dongdaemun Stationery and Toy Street (Mon~Fri 08:00~19:00) · Dongmyo Flea Market (all day on weekends) · Dongdaemun History and Culture Park (always open, free) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide sorts the markets around Dongdaemun (동대문) in Seoul by item. It covers Dongdaemun General Market (동대문종합시장) for fabrics and sewing supplies, Pyeonghwa Market (평화시장) and Jeil Pyeonghwa Market (제일평화시장), the wholesale clothing markets that open at night, the stationery and toy street, Dongmyo Flea Market (동묘벼룩시장) for secondhand goods, and Dongdaemun History and Culture Park (동대문역사문화공원) on the site of the old stadium. Opening hours differ greatly from market to market. Pyeonghwa Market opens at 22:00 at night and closes at 18:00 the next day, while the General Market is closed on Sundays and the Stationery and Toy Street is closed on Saturdays and Sundays, and both open by day.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Fabrics and Supplies — General Market, Accessory Arcade and Hanbok Arcade

Dongdaemun General Market (동대문종합시장) [Jongno 266 (종로 266), Jongno-gu (종로구), ☎ 02-2262-0114] is an arcade specializing in clothing materials that opened in December 1970. It sells fabrics, clothing supplies, accessories and wedding goods. It connects to the underground passage at Exit 9 inside Dongdaemun Station (동대문역).

  • Opening hours: Fabrics and clothing supplies 08:00~18:00, accessory supplies 09:30~19:00, wedding goods and home interior 08:00~19:00.
  • Closed days: Every Sunday. Wedding goods and home interior are partly open on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Sundays, and the whole section is closed on the 2nd and 4th Sundays.
  • Parking: Available.

Dongdaemun General Market Accessory Arcade (동대문종합시장 액세서리상가) [same address, ☎ 02-2262-0114] is an accessory supplies arcade on the 5th floor of Buildings A, B and C. The three buildings are connected by passages, and it sells everything from materials for making accessories to finished products.

  • Opening hours: 09:30~19:00. Closed days: Every Sunday. Parking: Available.

Dongdaemun General Market Hanbok Arcade (동대문종합시장 한복상가) [same address, ☎ 02-2262-0114] is a place that sells fabrics, everyday hanbok, and hanbok accessories and supplies all in one spot. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the largest traditional hanbok shop complex in the country.

  • Opening hours: 08:00~18:00 (varies by store). Closed days: Every Sunday. Wedding goods are registered as open at the shops' discretion on Sundays, which conflicts with the General Market's registered value (the whole section closed on the 2nd and 4th Sundays).
  • Credit cards: Accepted.

📍 Wholesale Clothing That Opens at Night — Pyeonghwa Market and Jeil Pyeonghwa Market

Seoul Pyeonghwa Market (서울 평화시장) [Cheonggyecheon-ro 274 (청계천로 274), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-2265-3531] is a wholesale market that makes and sells clothes, and it is described as having a 60-year history. It has factories that make clothes, hats and scarves directly, and everything from design to production and delivery happens within 72 hours. It faces Dongdaemun, beside Cheonggyecheon (청계천).

  • Opening hours: 22:00~18:00 the next day. Closed days: Every Saturday and Sunday. Parking: Available.

Jeil Pyeonghwa Market (제일평화시장) [Majang-ro 13 (마장로 13), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-2252-6744] is a women's wholesale and retail fashion mall that opened in 1979. It has 1 basement floor and 5 floors above ground. The 1st floor is classic women's clothing, the 2nd and 3rd floors are women's and children's clothing, the 4th floor is restaurants, and the 5th floor is sundries and accessories.

  • Opening hours: Weekdays, daytime 09:00~17:30, night 20:00~05:00. Saturday 09:00~17:00, Sunday 20:00~05:00.
  • Closed days: Night business is closed every Friday (per the official website). Check before visiting whether Saturday night is open. Parking: Available.

📍 Stationery and Secondhand Goods — Dongdaemun Stationery and Toy Street and Dongmyo Flea Market

Dongdaemun Stationery and Toy Street (동대문 문구완구거리) [Jongno 52-gil 21-1 (종로52길 21-1), Jongno-gu (종로구), ☎ 02-743-7424] is also called the Changsin-dong (창신동) stationery and toy market. Enter the alley beside the German Pharmacy (독일약국) from Exit 4 of Dongdaemun Station, and about 120 stationery shops are gathered there. It began in the 1960s. It sells school supplies such as notebooks, crayons, pencils and bags, as well as children's gifts, fancy goods and party goods. It is described as 30~40% cheaper than consumer prices, with prices dropping the more you buy at once.

  • Opening hours: Mon~Fri 08:00~19:00. Closed days: Every Saturday and Sunday. Both vary by shop.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: Not available.

Dongmyo Flea Market (동묘벼룩시장) [Sungin-dong (숭인동), Jongno-gu (종로구), ☎ 02-120] is a flea market that began in the late 1980s. It sells clothing, shoes, wallets, watches, electronics, old books and movie posters. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the most popular item is secondhand clothing, with most clothes around 1,000 won, fur or leather in the 10,000 won range, and luxury brands sometimes asking 100,000 won. On weekdays only part of the market is open, and on weekends it is open all day. The Korea Tourism Organization writes that the best time to shop is a holiday afternoon, when about 250 street vendors set up their stalls. It is a 5-minute walk from Exit 3 of Dongmyoap Station (동묘앞역) on Subway Line 1.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Not available.
  • Opening date: The registered value is 2026-11-02, which conflicts with the description that it began in the late 1980s.

📍 A Place to Rest — Dongdaemun History and Culture Park

Dongdaemun History and Culture Park (동대문역사문화공원) [Eulji-ro 281 (을지로 281), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-2153-0000] is a park that opened on October 27, 2009, on the site of the old Dongdaemun Stadium (동대문운동장). When the stadium was torn down in 2008 to build the park, 44 building sites and more than 1,000 artifacts came to light, including the Igansu Gate (이간수문) and bastions of the Joseon-era Seoul City Wall (서울성곽), and the sites of Hadogam (하도감) and Hullyeondogam (훈련도감). They are preserved and displayed throughout the park. At the entrance is the Dongdaemun Stadium Memorial Hall (동대문운동장 기념관). It is 26m from Exit 1 of Dongdaemun History and Culture Park Station (동대문역사문화공원역) on Subway Lines 2, 4 and 5.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Pyeonghwa Market opens at 22:00 at night — it stays open until 18:00 the next day and is closed on Saturdays and Sundays.
  2. The General Market is closed on Sundays and the Stationery and Toy Street on Saturdays and Sundays — the General Market's wedding goods and home interior are registered as partly open on Sundays.
  3. Dongmyo Flea Market is open all day on weekends — only part of it is open on weekdays.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. What is open on Sundays?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, Jeil Pyeonghwa Market opens 20:00~05:00 on Sundays, the General Market's wedding goods and home interior are partly open on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Sundays, and Dongmyo Flea Market is open all day on weekends. Dongdaemun History and Culture Park is always open.

Q. Where do I buy hanbok fabric?

A. The Dongdaemun General Market Hanbok Arcade sells fabrics, everyday hanbok, and hanbok accessories and supplies. It opens 08:00~18:00 and is closed on Sundays.
