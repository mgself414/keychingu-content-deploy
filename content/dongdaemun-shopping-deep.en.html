Why shopping in Dongdaemun now — Asia’s largest 24h fashion hub

Dongdaemun Market is Asia's largest fashion hub with 26 shopping malls + 30,000+ stores (annual wholesale transaction volume of $15 billion). It is not a “long-established food enthusiast” cluster like Gwangjang Market (#582), which started in 1905, and Namdaemun Market (#581) in 1414, but a cluster centered on “Fashion·Wholesale·24-hour shopping”. No. 1 foreign fashion enthusiast, MZ, CN, JP wholesale buyer.

In particular, DOOTA Mall (until 5 a.m. Mon-Sat, English staff, tax-free)·Fashion wholesale on the 13th floor of Diot·No. 1 clothing wholesale in Pyeonghwa Market·Zaha Hadid DDP (2014)·Wholesale market 30-50% cheaper (20-5 p.m. at night, cash only) — Learning the system and operating hours is essential for a foreigner's first visit.

Namdaemun #581 (authentic Korean food restaurant)·Plaza #582 (Netflix Street Food vlog)·Dongdaemun #583 (24h fashion·wholesale·DDP) — Full set cover of Seoul’s three major markets. Q2. Dongdaemun vs Namdaemun (#581) vs Gwangjang Market (#582) — Which one fits?
