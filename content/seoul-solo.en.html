⚠ 3 solo mistakes

1. "Korean food = 2 or more people" — Single-person BBQ/Korean food store misrecognition: Korean food is perceived as unfriendly for a single person in a household of 2-4 people. In reality, 1-person BBQ store (Saemaeul Restaurant 1-person set/Hanam Pork House) + 1-person Korean food course (hotel Korean restaurant) + 1-person Korean food 100% meal/gukbap = Full experience available for 1 person.

2. "Solo = hotel stay" — Awareness of 24h solo-friendly infrastructure: Recognition of anxiety about going out at night. In reality, OECD safety #1 + 24h convenience store + 24h jjimjilbang + 24h coin karaoke + late-night bus N bus = Solo 24h full experience possible.

3. "Solo = only museums and tourist attractions" — Mania's full experience is not utilized: Solo foreigners only recognize the routes of museums and tourist attractions. In reality, it is a full experience of solo mania, including full experience of K-pop goods + mountain climbing mania + early morning market mania + one-person cafe hopping.
