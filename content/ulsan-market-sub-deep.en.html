Ulsan Travel Guide — Markets, Taehwagang Garden, Whales and Petroglyphs

Subtitle: Jungang Traditional Market (중앙전통시장, began as Okgyo Market in 1922) · Taehwagang National Garden (태화강 국가정원) and Simni Bamboo Forest (십리대숲) (open 24 hours) · Jangsaengpo Whale Museum (장생포 고래박물관) (closed Mondays) · Petroglyphs along the Bangucheon Stream (반구천의 암각화) (World Heritage site in 2025) · Hyundai Motor Ulsan Plant tour (현대자동차 울산공장 견학) (weekdays, individual tour, up to 4 people per booking) — checked against the Ulsan city, Korea Tourism Organization and Hyundai Motor websites

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Ulsan is known as an industrial city that builds cars and ships, but the Taehwagang River runs through the middle of downtown, and a bamboo forest about 4km long stays open all night along its banks. On the coast at Jangsaengpo there is a whale village that revives an old whaling town, and in the mountains the rocks at Bangucheon still carry whale-hunting scenes carved by prehistoric people, which became a World Heritage site in 2025.

This guide splits Ulsan into three threads — markets · river and whales · rock art and the factory — and focuses on operating details checked against the original pages of the Ulsan city government, the Korea Tourism Organization (VisitKorea) and the operators' own websites. Values confirmed only through news reports are marked separately.

🛒 Markets — Jungang Traditional Market, Sinjeong Market, Suam Market

Jungang Traditional Market (중앙전통시장) (2 Jungangsijang-gil, Jung-gu, Ulsan (중앙시장길 2), Okgyo-dong) began as Okgyo Market in 1922 and is the best-known market in downtown Ulsan. It is lined with shops selling wedding goods, curtains, dishes, ritual ware and clothes, and the Korea Tourism Organization presents its arcade as the longest arcade in the country.

Sinjeong Market (신정시장) (201 Jungang-ro, Nam-gu (중앙로 201), Sinjeong-dong) took shape in the 1970s. It sells fish, groceries and clothes, and is known for its many rice cake shops.

Suam Market (수암시장) (Suam-ro 128beon-gil 12, Nam-gu (수암로128번길 12), Yaeum-dong) has been reported to hold a night market on Friday and Saturday evenings.

  • Payment: At small stalls inside markets, many prefer cash. Check first whether there is a card terminal.
  • Ordering: English menus are rare. Read the price board with Papago photo translation, and point to the food you want.

🌿 Taehwagang National Garden and Simni Bamboo Forest

Taehwagang National Garden (태화강 국가정원) (5 Saetgangnam-gil, Jung-gu (샛강남길 5), Taehwa-dong, ☎ 052-229-3147) is open 24 hours, year-round according to the Korea Tourism Organization. Inside, Simni Bamboo Forest (십리대숲) is a bamboo grove stretching about 4km along the river, lit up at night. The tourism organization's page lists no admission fee, and parking is 500 won per 30 minutes with a daily maximum of 10,000 won.

  • It is open 24 hours, so it is easy to fit into an evening plan.

🐋 Jangsaengpo — Whale Museum and Whale Culture Village

Jangsaengpo is a seaside area that once had a whaling village, and today it is home to a museum and a village themed around whales.

  • Jangsaengpo Whale Museum (장생포 고래박물관) (244 Jangsaengpogorae-ro, Nam-gu (장생포고래로 244)): 09:30~18:00, closed Mondays and on Lunar New Year and Chuseok days (Ulsan city tourism website).
  • Jangsaengpo Whale Culture Village (장생포 고래문화마을) (271-1 Jangsaengpogorae-ro, Nam-gu (장생포고래로 271-1), ☎ 052-226-0980): a recreation of the old whaling village streets. 09:00~18:00, tickets until 17:30, closed Mondays, Lunar New Year and Chuseok.
  • Both close on Mondays. If your day trip from Busan falls on a Monday, drop Jangsaengpo and switch the plan to the Taehwagang side.
  • Inquiries: Jangsaengpo Whale Cultural Special Zone (장생포 고래문화특구) ☎ 052-256-6301~2.

🪨 Petroglyphs along the Bangucheon Stream — World Heritage Site in 2025

In July 2025 the Daegok-ri Bangudae Petroglyphs and the Cheonjeon-ri Engraved Rocks, carved into rocks along the Bangucheon Stream in Ulju-gun, were inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List under the name "Petroglyphs along the Bangucheon Stream" (반구천의 암각화) (Korea's 17th World Heritage site, per news reports). The Bangudae Petroglyphs include whales and whale-hunting scenes.

  • How to view: The Bangudae Petroglyphs have been described as viewed from a distance from a viewing platform (per news reports).
  • Getting there: From April 24, 2026, a free shuttle loop was reported to run Wed–Sun 09:50~17:50, 8 times a day (hourly, 50 minutes per loop). It links the Bangudae Petroglyphs parking lot → Petroglyph Museum (암각화박물관) → Ulsan Daegok Museum (울산대곡박물관) → the Cheonjeon-ri entrance. There is no shuttle on Mondays and Tuesdays.

🚗 Hyundai Motor Ulsan Plant Tour

Individuals can also apply for a tour of the Hyundai Motor Ulsan Plant. This is based on the original text of the factory tour reservation page on the Hyundai Motor website.

  • Individual tour: up to 4 people per booking, Mon–Fri (excluding public holidays), free. The notice lists the duration as both about 60 minutes and about 1 hour 30 minutes. Inside the plant you travel by Hyundai Motor bus.
  • Reservations: Taken in 3-month blocks, and reservations open at 09:00 on the first working day of each month. Changes and cancellations are accepted up to 2 days before.
  • Language: The individual tour information has no language option. The field for choosing nationality and language (Korean, English, Chinese) appears only on the group application form.
  • Inquiries: ☎ 052-215-1021~1024 (weekdays 09:00~17:00, excluding lunch 11:10~12:10) · ulsantour@hyundai.com

  • KTX Ulsan Station is not in the city center. It is in Ulju-gun, about 22km from City Hall and about 32 minutes by car. To reach downtown and Taehwagang, take limousine bus 5001. Limousine bus 5005 serves the airport.
  • Taehwagang Station is the downtown station. If you come from Busan Bujeon Station on the Donghae Line or take the KTX-Eum, you get off at this station.
  • Buy train tickets on the Korail+ app (formerly Korail Talk) or the Korail website. The booking steps are covered in #1287 Riding the KTX for the First Time.
  • On the 2nd floor of Taehwagang Station there is a tourist information center that opened in May 2025 and offers foreign-language interpreter support (per news reports).
  • Tourist interpretation line ☎ 1330 · emergency ☎ 119 · police ☎ 112.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Getting off at KTX Ulsan Station and trying to walk into town — City Hall is about 22km away. Take limousine bus 5001, or, if you are coming from Busan, take the Donghae Line to Taehwagang Station from the start.

2. Going to Jangsaengpo on a Monday — the Whale Museum and the Whale Culture Village both close on Mondays.

3. Scheduling the Hyundai Motor plant tour on a weekend — tours run on weekdays only, and reservations open at 09:00 on the first working day of each month. Once your travel dates are set, check that month's reservation opening day first.

⚙ FAQ 4

Q. Can I do it as a day trip from Busan?

A. Yes. Take the Donghae Line from Busan Bujeon Station and you arrive directly at Taehwagang Station in downtown Ulsan. For one day, either Taehwagang National Garden + Jungang Traditional Market, or Jangsaengpo (not on Monday), is a comfortable route.

Q. Is there an admission fee for Taehwagang National Garden?

A. The Korea Tourism Organization's page lists no admission fee, and it is open 24 hours. If you bring a car, you pay for parking (500 won per 30 minutes, maximum 10,000 won a day).

Q. Can I see the Bangudae Petroglyphs up close?

A. Per news reports, you view them from a distance from a viewing platform. The free shuttle loop passes the Petroglyph Museum.

Q. Is the Hyundai Motor plant tour conducted in English?

A. The individual tour information has no language details. If you need it in English, ask at ulsantour@hyundai.com before booking.
