12 sub-deep selections of Korean tea utensils, tea bowls, tea cups, loaves, and tea plates for foreigners

Category: D Culture/Orthodoxy

Parent deep: #675 Traditional Tea·#679 Icheon Ceramics+Tea·#600 Ceramics

12 Korean Dagu districts

1. Dagwan (tea with side handle)

  • Distinction: Korean authentic Dagwan = side-handled earthenware kettle·Distinction from Japanese kiyusu + Chinese jasaho·Goryeo celadon + Joseon white porcelain
  • Material: Celadon (Goryeo), white porcelain (Joseon), Buncheong (Joseon), pottery (common people)
  • Price: KRW 50,000-500,000·Purchase directly at Icheon Ceramics Village
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN No. 1 souvenir·#679 cross

2. Tea cup (tea cup/tea cup, cup without handle)

  • Discrimination: Korean traditional teacup = cup without handle (two hand rest)·tea bowl (bowl type) + teacup (cup type)
  • Size: 100-200ml·5 people, 1 group
  • Price: 10,000-100,000 won/piece
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·No. 1 on SNS

3. Sukwoo (ripe, water cooler 80°C)

  • Distinction: Korean tea ceremony signature tool·Boiling water → Cool to 70-80°C (amino acid preservation)·Green tea 1st place
  • Reason: Below 80°C = Green tea flavor ranks 1st·100°C = Astringency + Increased caffeine
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Mania·Tea Ceremony Difference (Japan vs Korea)

4. Sewage device (waste water bowl)

  • Discrimination: Collection of wastewater from teapot/teacup preheating/cleaning·Essential tools for the 5th stage of tea ceremony
  • Material: Ceramics, pottery
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiast·Tea ceremony full set

5. Tea meal plate (tea meal molding mold)

  • Discrimination: Dasik molding tool since the Goryeo Dynasty·Misu powder + honey → Compressed into a Dasik plate·Wood + Ceramics
  • Type: Authentic patterns such as plum blossom, chrysanthemum, lotus flower, etc.
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Authentic enthusiasts·Popular souvenirs

6. Chatak (tea cup holder)

  • Discrimination: Teacup holder·Ceramic + Wood + Bamboo·Hand protection (hot teacup)
  • Material: Lacquered wood, celadon, bamboo
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Mania·5 people per group

7. Full set of tea tools (1 set of 5-10 tools)

  • Discrimination: Full set of authentic Korean tea utensils = tea cup + 5 tea cups + Sukwoo + water dispenser + tea plate + tea table 5·10-15 tools
  • Price: 100,000-500,000 won/full set·Icheon + Yeoju + Gwangju Pottery Village
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN No. 1 souvenir·No. 1 gift

8. Icheon Ceramics Village Dagu (produced in #1, Korea)

  • Discrimination: Icheon Sagimaggol Ceramic Village 240+ Workshops·Dagu No. 1 Production Site·UNESCO Creative City Crafts 2010
  • Experience: Dagu making 1 hour 30K~/1 person·Finished product delivered 1 week later (overseas OK)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania #1·#679 cross

9. Gangjin celadon tea set (UNESCO authentic celadon)

  • Discrimination: Gangjin celadon + tea bowl·Goryeo celadon #1 production area·1300 years old·Push for UNESCO registration
  • Experience: Gangjin Cheongjachon Experience + Tea Purchase
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP No. 1 authentic enthusiast·#657 Gangjin sub cross

10. Buncheong·Onggi Teaware (Common People’s Teaware)

  • Discrimination: Buncheon ware (Joseon)·Onggi (common people, fermentation + thermal insulation)·Tea utensils + tea preservation 1st priority
  • Signature: Earthenware + Fermented tea + Yellow tea preservation
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Mania·#600 cross

11. Tea utensil + tea ceremony workshop (experience + making 1 night)

  • Discrimination: Icheon 1 night, 2 days tea set + tea ceremony full set·Miranda Hotel + experience + tea ceremony·Price KRW 250,000~/per person

FAQ 6

Q1. Price range for a full set of tea utensils? A. Introductory = 100K-200K (tea pipe + 5 tea cups + sukwoo) · Authentic = 300K-500K (10-15 tools) · Luxury = 1M+ (author's celadon, white porcelain).

Q2. Tea utensil making experience? A. Icheon Sagimaggol·1 hour 30K~/1 person·Full set of tea cups, tea cups, and tea plates OK·Finished product delivered by courier 1 week later (overseas OK).

Q3. Korean Dagwan vs. Japanese Kiyus vs. Chinese Jajaho? A. Korea = side-handled earthenware, celadon, white porcelain (authentic + natural)·Japan = side-handled porcelain (authentic + sophisticated)·China = jasaho without handles (practical + rainforest).

Q4. Required for fermentation (80°C cooling)? A. Required for green tea enthusiasts·Under 80°C flavor + amino acid preservation·100°C = bitterness + increase in caffeine.

Q5. Can I bring in the tea set duty-free? A. 1 set OK (personal use)·Full set of tea cup + 5 tea cups = 150-200K, 1st place for introductory.

Q6. Popular tea gift? A. 1 set of tea cups + 5 tea cups = 100-300K·Tea plate + 5 tea bowls = 30-100K·EN/JP No. 1 gift.
