🎯 How to Use This Guide

The "12 picks" in the old guide were only YouTube view counts, Netflix titles and cross-references to other series, so there was not a single place a foreign visitor could actually go. This time we collected only "places you can visit": two comics and animation museums (Bucheon (부천) and Chuncheon (춘천)), the animation exhibition space on Namsan (남산), character stores (LINE FRIENDS and Kakao Friends), and the October Bucheon International Animation Festival. Values we read on an official page on 2026-10-08 are labeled "official." Values we had to read from secondary sources because the official site would not open are labeled "secondary." Re-check secondary values before you go.

🏛 Two Museums — Bucheon and Chuncheon

  • Korea Manhwa Museum (한국만화박물관) (Bucheon, official): 10:00–18:00, 1 Gilju-ro. Closed days, fees and the station exit are not in the body of the official page, so we do not list them. "Bucheon Manhwa Museum" in the old guide is not the formal name. In October it is one of the BIAF screening venues.
  • Chuncheon Animation Museum and Toy Robot Hall (춘천 애니메이션박물관·토이로봇관) (secondary): 10:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00), closed Mondays, Jan 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok, adults 7,000 won, 854 Baksa-ro, Seo-myeon. Transit from Chuncheon Station is unconfirmed. The official site (animationmuseum.com) would not open because of a certificate error, so every value here is from secondary sources.

🗼 Namsan — What Became of the Seoul Animation Center

  • A press report (undated) says Comic House (코믹하우스) (1F, free) and Aniso Pung (애니소풍) (2F, combo ticket) now operate on the site of the former Seoul Animation Center (서울애니메이션센터) (Namsan Central Tower), open 10:00–20:00 and closed Mondays. The center's official 2026 status and address are unconfirmed — check the notice from the Seoul Business Agency (서울산업진흥원) before you go.

🛍 Character Stores — LINE FRIENDS and Kakao Friends

  • LINE FRIENDS Square Myeongdong (라인프렌즈 스퀘어 명동) (43 Myeongdong-gil) and LINE FRIENDS K-POP Square Hongdae (라인프렌즈 K-POP 스퀘어 홍대) (141 Yanghwa-ro): addresses from the official store list. Opening hours are not on the official page.
  • Kakao Friends Hongdae flagship (카카오프렌즈 홍대 플래그십) (162 Yanghwa-ro): 10:30–22:00, open year-round (secondary). The official site refused the connection, so this is unconfirmed.

🎬 BIAF 2026 — Oct 23–27 in Bucheon

  • The 28th Bucheon International Animation Festival, BIAF (부천국제애니메이션페스티벌), 2026-10-23 to 27 (press). Screenings are at the Korea Manhwa Museum, CGV Bucheon, Bucheon Arts Center and Bucheon City Hall. The official site (biaf.or.kr) could not be read this time, so the dates are from press reports — confirm officially before you book.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Going to Chuncheon or Namsan on a Monday — both are closed Mondays (secondary).

2. Searching for "Bucheon Manhwa Museum" — the name is Korea Manhwa Museum.

3. Trusting secondary values as they stand — the Chuncheon fee and the Kakao hours are not officially confirmed. Re-check them.

4. Pororo Park in Seoul — a Seoul location is unconfirmed.

5. Treating the BIAF dates as final — Oct 23–27 comes from press reports. Confirm officially.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. A half day in Seoul? — Namsan Comic House (free, secondary) → LINE FRIENDS Square Myeongdong → Kakao Friends Hongdae.

2. A day in Bucheon? — Korea Manhwa Museum (10:00–18:00), plus BIAF in October.

3. What about Chuncheon? — Animation museum 10:00–18:00, closed Mondays, adults 7,000 won (secondary). Works as a Chuncheon day trip.

4. English guidance? — None of these places shows English guidance on its official page (unconfirmed).

5. What is officially confirmed? — The Korea Manhwa Museum hours and address, and the addresses of the two LINE FRIENDS stores.

6. What about 'Cat Biggie' and 'Ally' from the old guide? — They are a work and a video, not places, so we dropped them: the CJ AI short 'Cat Biggie' (CJ AI 캣 비기) and Bong Joon-ho's 'Ally' (앨리).
