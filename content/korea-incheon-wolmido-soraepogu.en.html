⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Wolmido = small amusement park": Coastal No. 1·Monorail 2.5km Korea #1 Sea·Amusement park + night view + cafe cluster.

2. "Monorail = Simple Transportation": Wolmi Sea Train = Korea #1 Marine Monorail·Insta + MZ Mania Matrix.

3. "Sorae Port = General Market": Korean Mania Matrix #1·Season Matrix (Summer/Fall #1)·Freshness of raw fish and seafood.

4. "Sashimi = Fishy taste": Flatfish · Rockfish = Not fishy · Introduction to foreigners #1 · Mullet · Oysters · Abalone · Crab = Mania Matrix.

5. "Summer/Fall only": Season matrix = Oysters, crabs (fall/winter), abalone (summer/fall), flatfish, rockfish = OK all year round.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. No. 1 introduction for foreigners: Wolmido monorail + night view + Soraepogu raw fish rice bowl.

2. Monorail: Round trip 8,000~12,000 won/person·Weekday evenings·weekends = Instagram mania season.

3. Sorae Port Market vs. Raw Fish Restaurant: Market = Cost-effectiveness (raw fish bowl 8,000~15,000 won)·Rawfish Restaurant = Signer Mania (50,000~120,000 won/2-3 people).

4. Season: Summer·Fall = Fresh Sashimi Matrix #1·Winter = Oysters·Abalone·Crab·Indoor Mania·Spring=Crab·Mania.

5. Allergy: Sashimi/seafood = crustacean/fish allergy·Prior notice + freshness check required.

6. Recommended Persona: Family·Senior = Hoedeopbap + Wolmido / MZ·Spicy Mania = Monorail + Night View + Raw Fish Restaurant / Mania = Season Matrix.

⚠ Medical/Safety/Dietary disclaimer

Sashimi/seafood = Allergy (crustacean/fish)·Freshness + Hygiene check required. Pregnant women, immunocompromised, elderly = Raw raw fish x recommended (cooking option). If food poisoning is suspected ☎ 1339.

Dietary Restrictions:

  • Halal/Kosher: Raw fish/seafood = Non-halal (raw raw fish)/Cooked raw fish + some clean fish species OK
  • Vegetarian/Vegan: Hoedeopbap/raw fish restaurant = non-vegetarian / Wolmido Cafe = vegetarian OK
  • Gluten free: Sashimi and seafood are OK on their own, OK without soy sauce

✈️ Foreigner Pain Point #1 — Raw fish hygiene + freshness

  • Market: Check freshness in person·Live fish (live sashimi) recommended
  • Rawfish Restaurant: Signer Store = Hygiene/Freshness Full Set/Foreigner-Friendly Matrix
  • Hoedeopbap: Safety matrix, fit for market enthusiasts, cost-effectiveness
  • Allergy: Shellfish/Fish Prior notice + English labeling of allergens at stores
  • Freshness Signal: Live fish + Freshness indication in store + Cleaning by the owner himself
