3. Foreign fit tips

Payment/Reservation

  • App English: S-Lab·TOZ (English mode)
  • Drop-in available: WeWork/Fast Five day pass
  • Cash/Card: All accepted. Foreign card (Visa/MC) OK

WiFi speed

  • GB level: WeWork · Fast Five (dedicated paid network)
  • High-speed: S-Lab·TOZ (free for public use)
  • General: Cafe Think · Faker Cafe

Operating hours

  • 24h: S-Lab·Study Planner (unmanned)
  • Until midnight: Cafe Think · Faker Cafe
  • business hours: WeWork, Fast Five, TOZ (07:00~22:00)

Price range

  • Part-time: 2,000~5,000 won/h (Study Cafe)
  • One-day ticket: 25,000~45,000 won/day (co-working)
  • Monthly fee: 250,000~600,000 won/month
  • Conference Room: Separate hourly schedule
