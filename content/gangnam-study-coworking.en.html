Gangnam Study Cafe & Coworking Guide for Foreigners

Gangnam (강남), Yeoksam (역삼), and Seolleung (선릉) — 7 foreigner-friendly spots — 24/7 operation, English-friendly, digital nomad matrix

Gangnam (강남) is a hub for foreign digital nomads, exchange students, and workation travelers. With English-speaking staff, 24/7 operation, high-speed WiFi, and reservation systems, it's the best area in Korea for foreigners to work or study. 1. Reddit Signal Anchor — S-Lab Study Cafe & Study Room Gangnam 4th Branch (에스랩 스터디카페 & 스터디룸 강남4호점)

  • Address: Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul (near Gangnam Station (강남역) and Yeoksam Station (역삼역))
  • Signature: 24-hour unmanned study cafe + study rooms (for individuals and groups)
  • Foreigner Fit ★★★: English kiosk, QR payment, language-friendly
  • Recommendation: 24h, hourly, and monthly passes all available. Safe for foreigners' first try.

2. 7 Foreigner-Friendly Spots in Gangnam (강남)

WeWork Gangnam (위워크 강남)

  • Address: Gangnam Finance Center (강남파이낸스센터), Samseong Station (삼성역)
  • Signature: Global coworking brand
  • Recommendation: 100% English, default choice for foreign digital nomads

FastFive Gangnam (패스트파이브 강남)

  • Address: Multiple branches in Gangnam (강남) and Yeoksam (역삼)
  • Signature: Korea's #1 coworking space
  • Recommendation: Daily day pass available. English membership.

Cafe Think Gangnam Branch (카페띵크 강남점)

  • Address: Near Gangnam Station (강남역)
  • Signature: Cafe + study hybrid
  • Recommendation: Casual atmosphere. English menu.

Faker Cafe Yeoksam Branch (페이커카페 역삼점)

  • Address: Yeoksam Station (역삼역)
  • Signature: Individual booths, laptop-friendly
  • Recommendation: Popular among foreign freelancers.

Study Planner Seolleung Branch (스터디플래너 선릉점)

  • Address: Seolleung Station (선릉역)
  • Signature: 24-hour unmanned + lockers
  • Recommendation: Suitable for long-term foreign residents.

Cafe Mocha Gold Gangnam (카페 모카골드 강남)

  • Address: Gangnam-daero (강남대로)
  • Signature: Large cafe + outlets, WiFi
  • Recommendation: Unofficial coworking spot. Great atmosphere.

Toz Gangnam Branch (토즈 강남점)

  • Address: Gangnam Station (강남역)
  • Signature: Hourly rental for meeting rooms and study rooms
  • Recommendation: Suitable for foreign group meetings and Zoom calls.

3. Foreigner-Friendly Tips

Payment & Reservation

  • English Apps: S-Lab (에스랩), Toz (토즈) (English mode)
  • Drop-in Available: WeWork (위워크), FastFive (패스트파이브) day pass
  • Cash/Card: Both accepted. Foreign cards (Visa/MC) OK.

WiFi Speed

  • Gigabit-level: WeWork (위워크), FastFive (패스트파이브) (dedicated paid network)
  • High-speed: S-Lab (에스랩), Toz (토즈) (shared free network)
  • Standard: Cafe Think (카페띵크), Faker Cafe (페이커카페)

Operating Hours

  • 24/7: S-Lab (에스랩), Study Planner (스터디플래너) (unmanned)
  • Until Midnight: Cafe Think (카페띵크), Faker Cafe (페이커카페)
  • Business Hours: WeWork (위워크), FastFive (패스트파이브), Toz (토즈) (07:00-22:00 / 7 AM - 10 PM)

Price Range

  • Hourly: ₩2,000-5,000/h (study cafes) (~$1.5-3.7 / ~10-25 RMB / ~200-500 JPY)
  • Daily Pass: ₩25,000-45,000/day (coworking) (~$18.5-33 / ~125-225 RMB / ~2,500-4,500 JPY)
  • Monthly Pass: ₩250,000-600,000/month (~$185-445 / ~1,250-3,000 RMB / ~25,000-60,000 JPY)
  • Meeting Rooms: Hourly rates apply separately.
