5 Bukchon Hanok dining options for foreigners

> Bukchon, Anguk, Samcheong 5 Hanok dining places suitable for foreigners — Uncovered stores + Hanok atmosphere deep dive

Bukchon, Anguk, and Samcheong are areas where foreign tourists encounter Hanok culture. Restaurant cluster has strong signal (Reddit 106) 2 Store contents not covered. This guide is an extended curation of Me Cover 2 + Foreigner Hanok Dining Fit 3.

1. Mecover 2 store (topic_gap anchor)

Anguk Pharmacy

  • Address: Near Anguk Station, Jongno-gu
  • Signature: Oriental medicine/traditional medicinal tea dining
  • Recommended: Foreign wellness/traditional culture persona. insta fit

Onmigwan Anguk

  • Address: Anguk/Bukchon Hanok Village
  • Signature: Hanok traditional Korean food
  • Recommended: Introduction to Korean food for foreigners + satisfying the Hanok atmosphere at the same time

2. Foreign fit expansion 3 stores

Dugaheon (Bukchon Hanok Korean Food)

  • Address: Bukchon Hanok Village
  • Signature: Course Meal (Hanok House)
  • Recommended: Fine dining for foreigners + Hanok experience. Stores recommended by Korea Tourism Organization

Dajeong

  • Address: Samcheong-dong Hanok Alley
  • Signature: Korean tea and traditional desserts
  • Recommended: Tea ceremony experience for foreigners. insta fit

Peaks (Bukchon Korean Cuisine)

  • Address: Bukchon Hanok Village
  • Signature: Modern Korean food in Hanok
  • Recommendation: Foreign date + Hanok atmosphere fit

3. Foreign fit tips

English menu/service

  • Top: Dugaheon·Onmigwan (regular among foreign tourists, English service provided)
  • Middle class: Dajeong·Bongwi (adjacent to tourist attractions, picture menu)
  • Local: Anguk Pharmacy (specialized pharmacy, translation app recommended)

Movement routes by region

  • Bukchon Hanok 1-day course: Dugaheon (lunch course) → (walking around Bukchon Hanok Village) → Dajeong (afternoon tea) → Onmigwan (dinner)
  • Samcheong-dong course: Dajeong (tea) → (Samcheong-dong gallery) → Peak (dinner)
  • Wellness Course: Anguk Pharmacy (lunch tea) → Dugaheon (dinner)

Price range

  • Tea·Dessert: 12,000~25,000 won
  • Full lunch: 30,000~55,000 won
  • Dinner course: KRW 65,000~120,000 (limited fine dining)
  • Reservation required: Dugaheon·Onmigwan (catchtable·Naver)

Hanok atmosphere fit

  • Traditional Hanok (garden, veranda): Dugaheon, Onmigwan, Peak
  • Modernized Hanok: Dajeong·Anguk Pharmacy
  • Photo fit ★★★: Du Ga-heon, Da-jeong, On Mi-gwan

4. 5 Steps to Introduction to Hanok Dining for Foreigners

1. First attempt: Dajeong (tea, dessert, low-cost Hanok experience)

2. Korean Set Meal: Bonguri (Modern Korean Cuisine)

3. wellness: Anguk Pharmacy (Yakcha)

4. fine dining: Onmigwan (traditional Korean food)

5. Anniversary: Dugaheon (Course + Hanok Garden)
