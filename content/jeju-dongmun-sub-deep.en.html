A Day in Jeju's Dongmun Market and Old Downtown — Night Market, Gwana, Sanjicheon by Bus

Subtitle: Dongmun Traditional Market (07:00~21:00, open year-round) · Dongmun Night Market (Nov~Apr 18:00~24:00, May~Oct 19:00~24:00) · Jeju-mok Gwana Government Office (09:00~18:00) and Gwandeokjeong Pavilion · Airport↔Dongmun Rotary buses 316, 325 and 43-2 — checked against the Jeju Tourism Organization (visitjeju), Korea Tourism Organization and Jeju Provincial Government websites

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Many travelers pick up a rental car the moment they land in Jeju and head straight east or west. But in old downtown Jeju City, reachable by bus from the airport, a market that dates back to around Korea's liberation, a Joseon-era government office and a stream that runs out to the sea all sit in one neighborhood. It is a good place to fit into your first evening or your last morning.

This guide is split into three parts, Dongmun Market · Dongmun Night Market · Old Downtown Walk, and is based mainly on operating information confirmed on the Jeju Tourism Organization (visitjeju), Korea Tourism Organization, Jeju Provincial Government and Ministry of Justice websites. For island-wide routes, follow the Jeju guides in the related content below.

🛒 Dongmun Traditional Market — A Market Dating Back to Liberation

Dongmun Traditional Market (동문재래시장) (20 Gwandeok-ro 14-gil (관덕로14길 20), ☎ 064-752-3001) is a permanent market that the Jeju Tourism Organization describes as "the biggest and oldest market in Jeju." It formed in 1945, right after liberation, and moved to its present site after a fire in 1954 (Korea Tourism Organization). The market is open 07:00~21:00, year-round, and hours differ a little from shop to shop.

  • To buy: Seasonal fruit such as tangerines, farm and sea produce, Jeju specialties, clothes and souvenirs.
  • To eat: According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the signature foods are omegi-tteok (오메기떡), bing-tteok (빙떡), raw fish such as hairtail sashimi, octopus bread and abalone tteok-galbi (rib patties).
  • Dongmun Seafood Market (동문수산시장) (64-1 Gwandeok-ro (관덕로 64-1)) is a separate seafood market nearby. It opened in 1970 and runs 07:00~20:00. You can have seafood you pick at the market cooked at a nearby restaurant, and the public parking lot is free for up to 30 minutes (visitjeju).
  • Dongmun Market Co. Indoor Shopping Center (동문시장 주식회사) (16 Dongmun-ro (동문로 16)) keeps its own hours, 08:00~21:00.

🌙 Dongmun Night Market — Opening Hours Change by Season

Dongmun Night Market (동문야시장) is a street of evening food stalls at Dongmun Traditional Market. Per visitjeju, it opens 18:00~24:00 from November to April and 19:00~24:00 from May to October, and is open year-round, holidays included. It does close early when ingredients run out.

  • In winter it starts an hour earlier. Stalls close one by one as they run out of ingredients, so the earlier you go, the more there is to choose from.
  • If the line at a stall is long, buying one item at a time and sharing is the better way to taste several things.

🏛 Old Downtown Walk — Jeju-mok Gwana, Gwandeokjeong and Sanjicheon

On Gwandeok-ro, close to Dongmun Market, the administrative center of Joseon-era Jeju still stands.

  • Jeju-mok Gwana Government Office (제주목 관아) (25 Gwandeok-ro (관덕로 25), ☎ 064-710-6717): 09:00~18:00, tickets until 17:30, open year-round. The on-site parking lot has been closed for excavation since May 22, 2023, so if you drive, use the public parking lot west of Gwandeokjeong (free on weekends, holidays and at night). Night opening from May to October was free, Wed~Sun 18:00~21:00, as of 2025.
  • Gwandeokjeong Pavilion (관덕정) (19 Gwandeok-ro (관덕로 19), ☎ 064-710-6718): Built in 1448, it is the oldest surviving building in Jeju and a Treasure. It is on the same Gwandeok-ro as Jeju-mok Gwana.
  • Sanjicheon Stream (산지천): A stream that passes by Dongmun Market and flows to Jeju Port. Jeju Olle Route 18 starts here. The stream continues toward Jeju Port.

🚌 Getting There from the Airport

  • Bus: The routes that pass both Jeju International Airport and Dongmun Rotary (동문로터리) are 316 · 325 · 43-2 (Jeju Bus Information System route map). Get off at Dongmun Rotary. Routes can change, so check again on the Jeju Bus Information System before you board.
  • Fare: For trunk buses, adult fare is 1,150 won by transit card · 1,200 won in cash (Jeju Provincial Government). If you transfer to another route within 40 minutes of getting off with a transit card, you get a transfer discount, up to 2 times. With cash you pay the fare every time.
  • Taxi: Base fare is 3,000 won for a compact and 4,300 won for a mid-size, with a 20% late-night surcharge from 23:00 to 04:00 (Jeju Province notice, July 2024). For island-wide taxis, see #1402 Jeju Taxi Tour.
  • How to get around the whole island by bus without a rental car is covered in #1212 Jeju on Foot (No Car).

🛂 Entry — Jeju Visa-Free Program

  • According to a September 2025 Ministry of Justice press release, Chinese nationals can enter Jeju visa-free for up to 30 days, for both individual and group travel. The conditions for Chinese travelers entering mainland Korea are in #191 Mainland China Traveler Guide.
  • For other nationalities, whether visa-free entry applies, and the conditions for leaving Jeju, differ by nationality. Before you leave, check the criteria for your own nationality on the Ministry of Justice website (immigration.go.kr) and the K-ETA website (k-eta.go.kr). The temporary K-ETA exemption has been extended to December 31, 2026 (Korea Tourism Organization), and the list of exempt countries follows the K-ETA website.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Assuming the night market's hours are the summer ones — It opens at 18:00 from November to April and at 19:00 from May to October. If you show up at 19:00 in winter, you lose an hour.
  2. Looking for the five-day market inside Dongmun Market — Jeju Minsok Five-Day Market (제주민속오일시장) (26 Oiljangseo-gil (오일장서길 26)) is a separate market in Dodu-dong (도두동), and is held only on the 2nd, 7th, 12th, 17th, 22nd and 27th of each month (08:00~18:00). For more on the five-day market, see #1348 Jeju Local Eats and the Five-Day Market.
  3. Trying to park at Jeju-mok Gwana — The on-site parking lot is closed for excavation. Use the public parking lot west of Gwandeokjeong.

⚙ FAQ 4

Q. Can I go straight there from the airport with my luggage?

A. Yes. According to the Jeju Bus Information System route map, buses 316, 325 and 43-2 all pass both the airport and Dongmun Rotary.

Q. Does the night market open every day?

A. Per visitjeju, it is open year-round, holidays included. But it closes early, even before midnight, when ingredients run out.

Q. Can I buy raw fish and eat it right away?

A. The Jeju Tourism Organization says seafood you pick at Dongmun Seafood Market can be cooked and eaten at a nearby restaurant.

Q. Can I see the market and the old downtown in one day?

A. Dongmun Market, Jeju-mok Gwana and Gwandeokjeong, and Sanjicheon are all within Jeju City's old downtown. Start with the government office in the afternoon (ticket sales end at 17:30), then head to the night market in the evening: that order fits the opening hours.
