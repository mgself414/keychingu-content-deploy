🎯 How to Use This Guide

Most of Korea's luxury hotels are in Seoul, and they fall into three types: tower-view hotels (Signiel, Park Hyatt, Conrad), palace and historic-district hotels (Four Seasons, Westin Josun, The Plaza), and Namsan-hillside resort hotels (The Shilla, Grand Hyatt, Banyan Tree). Busan means the Haeundae (해운대) seafront, Jeju means Jungmun (중문) beach, and Pyeongchang (평창) means skiing and golf. The addresses, room counts and facilities in the table below were checked on 2026-10-07 against each hotel's official site or a Korea Tourism Organization (KTO) page; cells we could not confirm say "Unconfirmed." No rates are listed. They can move by more than double depending on season, weekday and booking channel, so the only reliable way is to enter your dates on the official site.

🏙 Seoul: Choosing by Character

  • If night views come first: Signiel Seoul (floors 76-101), Park Hyatt Seoul (Samseong Station, top-floor lobby) or Conrad Seoul (Han River at Yeouido). The view from the window is the sightseeing.
  • Palaces and Myeong-dong on foot: Four Seasons (Gwanghwamun), Westin Josun and The Plaza (City Hall and Deoksugung). They pair well with the palace night openings and the royal-court night program in #1316.
  • To relax on the Namsan hillside: The Shilla (Urban Island pool), Grand Hyatt (outdoor pool and ice rink), Banyan Tree (Oasis pool). For all three, taxi is the default way in.
  • For a Gangnam shopping and beauty trip: Josun Palace (Teheran-ro), Park Hyatt and JW Marriott (direct access from the Express Bus Terminal, plus Shinsegae Gangnam and Sweet Park in #1118).

🌊 Busan, Jeju and Pyeongchang

  • Busan Haeundae: Signiel Busan (Dalmaji-gil, pet-friendly rooms) and Paradise (right on the beach, 532 rooms). For the beach and the Gwangandaegyo Bridge night view, see #243-#247.
  • Jeju Jungmun: The Shilla Jeju (1990) and Lotte Hotel Jeju (heated pool, casino) sit side by side on the same road. Jungmun beach and the Jusangjeolli cliffs are within walking distance. See #253.
  • Pyeongchang: InterContinental Alpensia, a ski-and-golf resort hotel. From Seoul, take the KTX to Jinbu Station (진부역).

🧭 Booking and Stay Tips

  • Check rates by entering your dates on the official site. The same room can differ greatly between weekdays, weekends, holidays and year-end. Joining the membership often brings member rates or breakfast-included terms.
  • Club lounges (The Plaza, Alpensia and others) include breakfast and happy hour, which makes a difference for families and long stays. Check on the booking screen that the room includes lounge access.
  • Pool and spa hours and extra charges differ by hotel (for example, Signiel Seoul posted a paid-sauna notice in 2023-05). Check the official "Facilities" page.
  • Check-in 15:00 / check-out 11:00 is the usual standard at Seoul luxury hotels (Signiel Seoul official). For a very early arrival, book from the night before or rest at an airport capsule hotel (#1015).
  • Pets are allowed only at hotels with dedicated pet rooms, like Signiel Busan. For Seoul hotels, check the official page.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Thinking Banyan Tree is in Gangwon Province: it is at 60 Jangchungdan-ro on Namsan in Seoul.

2. Treating "Shilla" as one hotel: The Shilla Seoul (Jangchung-dong) and The Shilla Jeju (Jungmun) are different hotels. Signiel is also two, in Seoul and Busan.

3. Budgeting from blog prices: rates vary so much by season that nothing but the official booking screen means anything.

4. Trying to reach the Namsan hotels by subway: The Shilla, Grand Hyatt and Banyan Tree are far from stations, so taxi or shuttle is the default.

5. Booking on the assumption that club lounge is included: lounge access comes with the room category. Check what is included on the booking screen.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. If I choose on the view alone? Signiel Seoul (floors 76-101). The window shows all of Seoul.

2. Which are within walking distance of the palaces and Myeong-dong? Four Seasons (Gwanghwamun), Westin Josun and The Plaza (City Hall).

3. With family and kids? Grand Hyatt (outdoor pool and ice rink), Lotte Hotel Jeju (heated pool), Signiel Busan (kids' lounge).

4. Which is the oldest hotel? The Westin Josun Seoul, 1914 (KTO).

5. Which is the ski and golf resort type? InterContinental Alpensia Pyeongchang.

6. How much does it cost? This guide does not list rates. Entering your dates on the official site is the only way to get a correct figure.
