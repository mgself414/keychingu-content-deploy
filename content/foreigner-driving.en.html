⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "IDP = International = Korea OK": An IDP (International Driving Permit) must be carried simultaneously with your home country's driver's license. An IDP alone is invalid – this is the number one rule for foreign enthusiasts.

2. "IDP = Permanent": An IDP is only valid for 1 year after being issued in your home country + foreign enthusiasts need to renew/extend it.

3. "Foreigner's Korean License = Difficult": Converting a foreign license to a Korean one is a top-tier system for foreign enthusiasts + foreigner-friendly for permanent residency and marriage visas + relatively easy for foreign enthusiasts.

4. "Rental Car = Insurance = Optional": Rental car insurance is a top-priority essential for foreign enthusiasts + foreigner-friendly full package + top-tier foreigner-friendly support in case of an accident.
