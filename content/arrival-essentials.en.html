Your First 3 Hours in Korea — Immigration, Data, Money and Transit, in Order (as of 2026-10)

Subtitle: Before your flight (K-ETA temporary exemption · e-Arrival Card · eSIM install) → arrivals hall → 20 minutes on the airport 1st floor (eSIM activation · transit card · cash) → heading to Seoul (AREX express ₩9,500 · limousine bus · night bus) → first day in the city (public Wi-Fi · Naver Map · 1330). Details are in the dedicated guide for each step.

ISSUE 03 · #398 · 2026-05-26

🎯 How to Use This Guide — Order Is Everything

First-time visitors don't get stuck at the airport for lack of information. They get stuck because they don't know the order. They try to install an eSIM at the airport and have no internet to do it, stand at a subway gate without a transit card, or book a bus ticket and then find immigration takes over an hour. This hub splits that order into five steps. The figures for each step come from the dedicated guides (#1289 · #1298 · #1303 · #1015 and others) and from official pages checked on 2026-10-07; the detailed screen-by-screen flows are in those guides.

✈️ Step 0 · Before Your Flight — Finish at Home

🛬 Step 1 · Arrivals Hall — Allow 1–2 Hours

  • Order: quarantine → immigration (e-Arrival QR · passport) → baggage → customs. Incheon Airport (인천공항)'s official bus guide also tells you to factor in "1–2 hours for arrival procedures." If you pre-booked a bus or train to Seoul, add this time.
  • If your bag doesn't come out, go to your airline's counter next to the baggage carousel. For a transfer, see #1015 (moving between terminals · transit tours).

📶 Step 2 · 20 Minutes on the Airport 1st Floor — Data, Transit Card, Cash

1. Data: Turn on roaming and activate your eSIM (see the right-after-arrival checklist in #1303). If it won't connect, get a physical SIM at the 1st-floor carrier booths (KT · SK Telecom · LG U+).

2. Transit card (#1298, pick 1 of 3):

  • T-money — buy it at a convenience store (CU · GS25 · 7-Eleven · Emart24) for about ₩4,000 for the card itself; top up with cash (in ₩10,000 increments). Works on subways and buses nationwide, taxis, and some convenience stores.
  • WOWPASS — insert foreign currency at kiosks at the airport, major stations and Myeongdong (명동) to get the card; works for transit plus regular payments.
  • Climate Card (기후동행카드) short-term pass for foreigners — time-based passes such as ₩5,000 for 1 day and ₩8,000 for 2 days. It is Seoul-only, so the catch is that it doesn't work on the Incheon Airport section or in Gyeonggi Province.

3. Cash: Almost everywhere in Korea takes cards (Visa · Mastercard · Amex). Keep only a small amount of cash for transit-card top-ups and markets. Exchange offices in downtown Myeongdong tend to give better rates than the airport (#435). For withdrawals with a foreign card, use ATMs marked "Global ATM."

4. Payments for Chinese visitors (#1333): Alipay works at 120,000+ merchants such as convenience stores, Olive Young (올리브영) and department stores, but not on transit. WeChat Pay has worked only at Zero Pay QR merchants since 2026-04. UnionPay needs advance preparation.

🚆 Step 3 · To Seoul — AREX, Limousine Bus, Night Bus, Taxi (as of 2026-10)

Option Time · fare Notes
Airport Railroad (AREX) express train → Seoul Station (서울역) Incheon Airport Terminal 1 (인천공항 제1터미널, T1) 43 min · Terminal 2 (T2) 51 min · ₩9,500 (transit card, AREX website) Reserved seats; comfortable with lots of luggage
AREX all-stop train → Seoul Station T1 59 min · T2 66 min · T1 ₩4,750 · T2 ₩5,350 (transit card) Stops at Hongik Univ. Station for Hongdae (홍대) and at Gongdeok (공덕). Board directly with your transit card
Limousine bus 6015 → Myeongdong · Chungjeongno (충정로) · Gongdeok · Mapo (마포) About 60–70 min · ₩17,000 (Airport Limousine official) Seoul-bound stop in front of Exits 3–7 on the T1 1st floor; buy your ticket at the ticket office
Night buses (from 00:00) N6000 Gangnam Express Bus Terminal (강남고속터미널) ₩17,000 · N6002 Cheongnyangni (청량리) · N6701 DDP · N6703 Gwangnaru Station (광나루역) ₩18,000 T1 1st floor 3B (N6701 · N6703) · 6A (N6000 · N6002) — airport official
Taxi Metered fare + tolls · late-night surcharge Kakao T: if no card is registered, it switches to "pay the driver directly" and still dispatches (#1273)
  • If your stay is in Myeongdong or Hongdae, take the train; if it's in Gangnam (강남), the limousine bus and night bus go there without transfers. For terminal locations and transfers, see #1015.

🏙 Step 4 · First Day in the City — Three Places You Get Stuck

  • Wi-Fi: "SEOUL" (open) / "SEOUL_Secure" (id and password are both seoul) on streets, in parks and at markets (#1302). In cafes, the password is on the receipt or the wall.
  • Navigation: Set Naver Map to English and look at the exit number on the results screen. Google Maps is weak in Korea for walking and public transit (#1295).
  • Help: ☎ 1330 tourist helpline (Korean · English · Japanese · Chinese and more) · ☎ 119 ambulance and fire · ☎ 112 police · Incheon Airport ☎ 1577-2600 (07:00–22:00).

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Installing an eSIM at the airport — install at home, activate only after you arrive (#1303).

2. Riding AREX with a Climate Card short-term pass — it's Seoul-only and doesn't cover the Incheon Airport section. At the airport, use T-money or WOWPASS.

3. Trying to top up T-money by card — convenience stores and older top-up machines are cash-only (#1298).

4. Paying for buses or subways with Alipay — it doesn't work on transit. Buy a transit card separately (#1333).

5. Booking a bus based on your landing time — add 1–2 hours for immigration. If you arrive at night, check the night bus routes first.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. Do I need a K-ETA? — Temporarily exempt through 2026-12-31. Check on the official K-ETA site whether your nationality is exempt (#1396).

2. What about the arrival card? — There is no paper form. Submit the e-Arrival Card online from 3 days before arrival (#1289).

3. What's the lowest fare to Seoul Station? — The AREX all-stop train at ₩4,750 (T1). The fast option is the express at ₩9,500 · 43 min.

4. If I buy only one transit card? — T-money, which works everywhere in Korea (#1298). If you'll stay in Seoul only for 3 days or more, compare the Climate Card short-term pass.

5. How much cash? — Cards work almost everywhere, so keep a small amount for transit-card top-ups and markets. Use ATMs marked Global ATM (#435).

6. A meal at the airport? — Hwangsaengga Kalguksu (황생가칼국수) near Gate 31 (31번 게이트) in the T1 3rd-floor duty-free area (Michelin Bib Gourmand, 06:00–22:00) — for when you depart. For arrivals, use the 1st-floor convenience stores and food court.
