Korea Strawberry Picking Guide: Nonsan Strawberry Scent Rural Theme Park, Dojeong Strawberry Village, Nonsan Strawberry Festival, Strawberry Farms in Namyangju and Yongin, Gimhae Gaya Strawberry Theme Park

Subtitle: Strawberry Scent Rural Theme Park (Nonsan, closed Mondays) · Dojeong Strawberry Village (Nonsan, 09:00~17:00) · The Dream Strawberry Farm (Namyangju, closed May to November) · Byeolmaeul Strawberry Farm (Namyangju, 10:00~17:00) · A Good Day for a Strawberry Picnic (Yongin, open year-round) · Gimhae Gaya Strawberry Theme Park (Gimhae, activities December to May) · 2026 Nonsan Strawberry Festival (March 26~29, ended) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places in Korea where you can pick strawberries and make strawberry treats. They are a theme park, an experience village and a festival in Nonsan (논산), the main strawberry-growing area, three experience farms in Namyangju (남양주) and Yongin (용인) in Gyeonggi, and a strawberry theme park in Gimhae (김해), Gyeongnam.

Each farm has different closed days and experience periods. Gimhae Gaya Strawberry Theme Park offers its experiences from December to May, when the strawberries grow, and The Dream Strawberry Farm is closed from May to November. The Dream Strawberry Farm, Byeolmaeul Strawberry Farm and Gimhae Gaya Strawberry Theme Park are registered as requiring a reservation in advance.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Nonsan — Strawberry Scent Rural Theme Park, Dojeong Strawberry Village and the Nonsan Strawberry Festival

Strawberry Scent Rural Theme Park (딸기향농촌테마공원) [665 Bujeok-ro (부적로), Bujeok-myeon (부적면), Nonsan-si, ☎ 041-746-6650] is a theme park built around strawberries, a specialty of Nonsan. It runs experience programs in a strawberry learning center, an agriculture experience center and a cultivation greenhouse, and has a strawberry rest area, walking paths, a strawberry garden and a shop selling local specialties. It sits next to Tapjeong Reservoir (탑정저수지), so you can visit it together with the Tapjeongho Suspension Bridge (탑정호 출렁다리) and the Tapjeongho Lakeside Ecological Park (탑정호수변생태공원).

  • Opening hours: March to October 09:00~18:00 (lunch break 12:00~13:00). For November to February, the registered hours value (19:00~17:00) does not match, so it is not copied here.
  • Closed days: Every Monday. If Monday is a legal holiday, it closes the next day.
  • Experiences: VR experience, berry cooking class and more. Parking: Available.

Dojeong Strawberry Village (도정딸기마을) [15 Hwangsanbeol-ro 380beon-gil (황산벌로380번길), Yangchon-myeon (양촌면), Nonsan-si, ☎ 041-741-5454] is a village that offers strawberry experiences in spring and dried persimmon experiences in autumn. Eleven zelkova trees hundreds of years old line the village, and strawberry greenhouses stand on both sides of the approach road. The Yangchon-myeon area is also a growing region for Durigam (두리감), also called Wolhagam (월하감), persimmons.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~17:00.
  • Experiences: In spring, strawberry picking and making strawberry injeolmi (인절미) and strawberry jam; in autumn, digging chestnut sweet potatoes and making dried persimmons (곶감). Experiences may change depending on the season and the village's circumstances.
  • Parking: Available.

2026 Nonsan Strawberry Festival (논산딸기축제) [Nonsan Citizens' Family Park (논산시민가족공원), ☎ Nonsan City 041-730-2982] was held from March 26 to 29, 2026, from 10:00 to 20:00. Admission was free and some experiences were paid. It offered a strawberry harvest experience, strawberry jam and dessert making, strawberry K-food cooking and tasting, a global street parade, and multilingual guidance for foreign tourists. It is a 2026~2027 culture and tourism festival designated by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism (문화체육관광부), and the Nonsan World Strawberry Industry Expo (논산세계딸기산업엑스포) is planned for 2027.

📍 Gyeonggi — Strawberry Experience Farms in Namyangju and Yongin

The Dream Strawberry Farm (더드림딸기체험농장) [82-25 Bukhangang-ro 626beon-gil (북한강로626번길), Joan-myeon (조안면), Namyangju-si, ☎ 010-9427-8435] is a strawberry harvest experience farm with Farm 1 (6,611㎡, next to the Bukhangang riverside walking path and bike path) and Farm 2 (inside the Toddler Forest Experience Area and the Water Garden). Both farms have a kids' room and a nursing room.

  • Opening hours: 10:30~16:00. Closed days: Every Monday. It is closed from May to November every year.
  • Experiences: Strawberry experience, making strawberry jam, making griddle strawberry ice cream and making cupcakes. A reservation in advance is required, and children under 24 months enter free.
  • Parking: Available.

Byeolmaeul Strawberry Farm (별마을딸기체험농장) [216-35 Jingeonoham-ro (진건오남로), Jingeon-eup (진건읍), Namyangju-si, ☎ 0507-1336-7775] is a farm that grows organic-certified and GAP-certified strawberries in 730 pyeong (평) of greenhouses, using honeybee pollination and hydroponics. Strawberry picking can be combined with making strawberry waffles or strawberry fresh cream cakes. A family of rabbits lives on the farm.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~17:00.
  • Reservation: Registered as requiring an online reservation in advance. Children under 36 months enter free.
  • Parking: Available.

A Good Day for a Strawberry Picnic (딸기랑소풍가기좋은날) [8 Jeonwon-ro (전원로), Namsa-eup (남사읍), Cheoin-gu (처인구), Yongin-si, ☎ 010-4565-5024] is an experience farm where you can observe strawberry fruit and flowers and pick and taste them yourself. It also offers experiences such as making ice cream. One or two teams enter each time slot in 3 or 4 experience classrooms. Items from experiences such as making strawberry chocolate are packed to go and cannot be eaten inside the greenhouse. Children must take part with a guardian.

  • Opening hours: Weekdays 10:30~12:30, weekends 10:30~16:30. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available. Credit cards: Not accepted.

📍 Gimhae — Gimhae Gaya Strawberry Theme Park

Gimhae Gaya Strawberry Theme Park (김해가야딸기테마파크) [128-62 Chilsan-ro 210beon-gil (칠산로210번길), Gimhae-si, ☎ 0507-1350-9854] is a strawberry farm near Lotte Outlet Jangyu (롯데아웃렛) in Gimhae, growing strawberries for three generations. Its experiences are strawberry harvest, making strawberry cake, strawberry fondue, making macarons and collecting eggs from the chicken coop.

  • Experience period and times: December to May, when the strawberries grow. Three times a day, at 10:30, 13:00 and 15:00. Reserving in advance is convenient.
  • Opening hours: Weekdays 11:30~16:30 (break 12:30~14:00), weekends 10:30~17:00 (break 12:10~12:45).
  • Closed days: Every Monday and Tuesday. Parking: Available.
  • The registered experience times (10:30, 13:00, 15:00) and opening hours values do not match each other.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The Dream Strawberry Farm (더드림딸기체험농장) is closed from May to November — it also closes every Monday.
  2. Experiences at Gimhae Gaya Strawberry Theme Park (김해가야딸기테마파크) run from December to May — it closes on Mondays and Tuesdays.
  3. The Nonsan Strawberry Festival's Korea Tourism Organization registered value is the 2026 schedule — in 2026 it was held March 26~29.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which strawberry experience farms are in Gyeonggi Province?

A. Among the places in this guide, there are The Dream Strawberry Farm (10:30~16:00, closed May to November) and Byeolmaeul Strawberry Farm (10:00~17:00) in Namyangju-si, Gyeonggi, and A Good Day for a Strawberry Picnic (weekdays 10:30~12:30, weekends 10:30~16:30) in Yongin-si.

Q. When can I do strawberry experiences?

A. Gimhae Gaya Strawberry Theme Park offers its experiences from December to May, when the strawberries grow, and The Dream Strawberry Farm is closed from May to November. Dojeong Strawberry Village offers strawberry experiences in spring.
