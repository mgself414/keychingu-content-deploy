⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. **"Jungnang = Tourism

Foreigners pass Jungnang as “not a tourist area,” but the #1 deep dive in anthropology for native Koreans and working-class people. Myeonmok Woorim Market is a hidden gem that few foreigners know about — a deep dive into an authentic Korean traditional market. Ordinary Korean daily life + native culture.

2. "Mangu Park = Just a Park" — Value Miji, the No. 1 Book in Modern and Contemporary Korean Literature

Mangu History and Culture Park is the No. 1 tomb of modern and contemporary Korean literature and artists — The tomb of Korea's No. 1 literary figures, poets, and artists, including Park In-hwan, Park Wan-seo, Lee Jung-seop, and Han Yong-un. No. 1 deep dive in modern and contemporary Korean history (Joseon royal tombs, etc.).

⚙ FAQ (6)

Q1. Is Mangu History and Culture Park free for foreigners?

Free + 24 hours — Free entry for foreigners. The graves of Korea's leading literary figures, including Park In-hwan, Park Wan-seo, Lee Jung-seop, and Han Yong-un, are buried. 15-minute walk from Mangu Station on Line 1.

Q2. Is Myeonmok Woorim Market open to foreigners?

Possible — Foreigner friendly. An authentic Korean traditional market. 10-minute walk from Myeonmok Station on Line 7. #1 weekend night market.

Q3. Jungnangcheon bike?

Available — Ttareungi rental OK. Uijeongbu → Han River 30km. Departs near Myeonmok Station and Meokgol Station on Line 7.

Q4. What are the foreign-friendly stores in Jungnang and Meokgol?

Pak Siu Vietnamese Coffee, Daham Dakgalbi, Beef Meoknal, and Dam Ga Brazier have English menus + foreigner friendly.

Q5. Jungnang + Jamsil·Gangdong connection?

OK — Transfer between Lines 7 and 1 within 30 minutes.

Q6. Foreigners climbing Yongmasan Mountain?

Available — 348m light trek. Line 7 Yongmasan Station or Myeonmok Station.
