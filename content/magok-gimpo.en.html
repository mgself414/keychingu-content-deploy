🎯 Why a Dedicated Magok·Gimpo Airport Guide?

Magok (마곡) and Gimpo Airport (김포공항) are Gangseo-gu's (강서구) No. 1 hub for international transit and inbound tourism, and a new town. Gimpo International Airport (김포국제공항) (GMP, opened 1958, international + domestic flights) + Magok New Town (마곡 신도시) (a planned urban development, established 2014, home to LG Science Park and LG Arts Center) + Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원) (opened 2019, Korea's largest botanical garden) are all concentrated in one area. This combination of international tourists' first entry point to Korea and a deep dive into Magok New Town (마곡 신도시) makes it "Seoul's Western Gateway."

Compared to Hongdae (홍대) (MZ (Millennials and Gen Z, a demographic group), indie culture), Gangnam (강남) (luxury), and Itaewon (이태원) (global), Magok (마곡) and Gimpo Airport (김포공항) combine transit, a new town, and nature. International tourists often overlook Magok (마곡) and Gimpo Airport (김포공항) in favor of Incheon Airport (인천공항) — but in reality, Gimpo Airport (김포공항) → short-haul routes to Japan/Southeast Asia + Magok New Town (마곡 신도시) + Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원) + LG Arts Center (LG 아트센터) are top hotspots for international inbound and transit travelers.

In particular, Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원) (opened 2019) is Korea's largest botanical garden and a top choice for international families and strolls. Gimpo Airport (김포공항) Duty-Free Shop and Lotte Mall (롯데몰) are also top choices for transit and refunds outside Incheon Airport (인천공항). Magok New Town (마곡 신도시), established in 2014, is an advanced Korean new town (home to global companies like LG, Kolon, E-Land) — offering a deep dive into Korea's future cities. Following [#138 Hongdae](138_hongdae_guide.md), [#142 Gangnam](142_gangnam_guide.md), [#170 Daehakro](170_daehakro_guide.md), [#171 Sinchon](171_sinchon_guide.md), [#172 DDP](172_ddp_guide.md), [#173 Seocho](173_seocho_guide.md), and [#174 Seoul National University](174_seoul_national_university_guide.md), this is ISSUE 03, Part 16 — KeyChinGu's (키친구) first entry into Gangseo-gu (강서구) (31 clusters).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Airport = Incheon Airport Only" — Underestimation of Gimpo Airport's (김포공항) Value

Foreigners often only recognize Incheon Airport (ICN) as Korea's airport, but Gimpo Airport (GMP) is Korea's No. 1 Airport (opened 1958) + a hub for short-haul routes to Japan/Southeast Asia (Tokyo, Osaka, Fukuoka, Taipei, Shanghai, etc.) + a domestic flight hotspot. Gimpo Airport (김포공항) → Gangnam (강남) 30 minutes / Hongdae (홍대) 25 minutes (subway). It's the No. 1 choice for short-haul international transit + domestic business trips in Korea.

2. "Magok (마곡) = New Town = Nothing to See" — Unawareness of Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원)·LG Arts Center (LG 아트센터)

Magok New Town (마곡 신도시) (established 2014) is Korea's No. 1 new town + home to global companies (LG Science Park (LG 사이언스파크) 2018·LG Arts Center (LG 아트센터) 2022·Kolon·E-Land, etc.). Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원) (opened 2019, Korea's largest botanical garden, integrating 5 plant museums) + LG Arts Center (LG 아트센터) (Korea's No. 1 performance venue, relocated from Gangnam (강남) to Magok (마곡)) = top choices for international family and cultural deep dives.

3. "Gimpo Airport (김포공항) Duty-Free = Less than Incheon Airport (인천공항)"

Gimpo Airport (김포공항) Duty-Free Shop and Lotte Mall (롯데몰) are smaller than Incheon Airport (인천공항) but are foreigner-friendly + duty-free + a refund hotspot. In particular, Lotte Mall Gimpo Airport (롯데몰 김포공항점) offers standard immediate tax refunds for foreigners + a full range of Korean dining and cafes. It's the No. 1 choice for short-haul departing passengers for transit and duty-free shopping (an option besides Incheon Airport (인천공항)).

Magok·Gimpo Airport Landscape

Magok (마곡) and Gimpo Airport (김포공항) are a foreigner transit + new town combined area located in the eastern and western parts of Gangseo-gu (강서구). Following the opening of Gimpo Airport (김포공항) in 1958 as Korea's No. 1 airport, the establishment of Magok New Town (마곡 신도시) in 2014, the opening of Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원) in 2019, and the relocation of LG Arts Center (LG 아트센터) in 2022, it has become a hotspot for a deep dive into Korea's future cities.

The Gimpo International Airport (김포국제공항) (GMP) sub-area is directly connected to Gimpo Airport Station (김포공항역) on Subway Line 5, Line 9, and the Airport Railroad (공항철도) — featuring the International Terminal (for Japan/Southeast Asia) + Domestic Terminal (for Jeju, Busan, Gwangju, etc.) + Lotte Mall Gimpo Airport (롯데몰 김포공항점) + Duty-Free Shop.

The Magok New Town (마곡 신도시) sub-area is around Magok Station (마곡역), Magoknaru Station (마곡나루역), and Balsan Station (발산역) on Subway Line 5, Line 9, and the Airport Railroad (공항철도) — home to LG Science Park (LG 사이언스파크) + LG Arts Center (LG 아트센터) + Kolon and E-Land. It's the No. 1 choice for international business.

The Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원) sub-area is a 5-minute walk from Magoknaru Station (마곡나루역) — Korea's largest botanical garden (approx. 1.65 million square meters) + 5 plant museums + a lake garden + No. 1 for international families and strolls.

The LG Arts Center (LG 아트센터) sub-area is near LG Science Park (LG 사이언스파크) in Magok (마곡) — Korea's No. 1 performance venue, relocated from Gangnam (강남) in 2022. English subtitles are available for some performances.

Transportation: Gimpo Airport Station (김포공항역) on Subway Line 5, Line 9, and Airport Railroad (공항철도) / Magok Station (마곡역), Magoknaru Station (마곡나루역), Balsan Station (발산역) on Subway Line 5 and Line 9. Incheon Airport (인천공항) → Gimpo Airport (김포공항) by Airport Railroad (공항철도) in 18 minutes (Korea's No. 1 transit line).

⚙ FAQ (8 Questions)

Q1. What's the difference between Gimpo Airport (김포공항) and Incheon Airport (인천공항)?

Gimpo Airport (김포공항): Excellent city accessibility (Gangnam (강남) 30 minutes), short-haul international flights to Japan/Southeast Asia + domestic flights. Incheon Airport (인천공항): Korea's No. 1 international airport, hub for long-haul routes to the Americas, Europe, and the Middle East. For Japanese and Southeast Asian foreigners, Gimpo Airport (김포공항) is friendlier.

Q2. What are the admission fees and hours for Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원)?

Botanic Garden Main Building (5 plant museums) is ₩5,000 (~$3.7 / ~27 RMB / ~550 JPY) / Lake Garden·Walking paths are free. 09:30-18:00 (9:30 AM - 6:00 PM) (closed on Mondays). English guidance available for foreigners. 5-minute walk from Magoknaru Station (마곡나루역).

Q3. Are LG Arts Center (LG 아트센터) performances foreigner-friendly?

YES — English subtitles are available for some performances (musicals·plays·ballets). Interpark (인터파크) English UI + foreigner seat reservations are available. A deep dive into Korea's No. 1 performances.

Q4. How do I do transit and duty-free shopping at Gimpo Airport (김포공항)?

Gimpo Airport (김포공항) International Terminal → directly connected to the Duty-Free Shop·Lotte Mall Gimpo Airport (롯데몰 김포공항점). Immediate tax refunds for foreigners are standard (for purchases of ₩30,000 (~$22 / ~160 RMB / ~3,300 JPY) or more + passport). It's the No. 1 transit option outside Incheon Airport (인천공항).

Q5. Which Magok (마곡) establishments are foreigner-friendly?

Cafe Gongmyeong (카페 공명)·Coffee with Flowers (커피에꽃을담다)·Don Gumeong (돈구멍)·Cozy House (코지하우스) offer English menus + card payment + standard foreigner-friendly services. As a new town, it has rich business infrastructure for foreigners.

Q6. Is a one-day course for Magok (마곡) + Gimpo Airport (김포공항) possible?

YES — Magok New Town (마곡 신도시) (Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원)·LG Arts Center (LG 아트센터)·cafes·dining) + Gimpo Airport (김포공항) (duty-free·transit) = a full day. Directly connected by Subway Line 5, Line 9, and Airport Railroad (공항철도).

Q7. What's the route from Magok (마곡) to Gangnam (강남) or Hongdae (홍대)?

Magok Station (마곡역) (Subway Line 5·Line 9·Airport Railroad (공항철도)) → Gangnam (강남) (35 minutes by subway) / Hongdae (홍대) (15 minutes by Airport Railroad (공항철도) + 5 minutes by Line 9). It's an adjacent area. Foreigner transit is available.

Q8. What are the best seasons for Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원)?

Spring (April) for cherry blossoms + Autumn (October-November) for autumn foliage are top choices. Summer (June-August) for the Lake Garden + illumination (seasonal). Winter (December-February) for a warm deep dive into the greenhouse (Tropical Hall·Mediterranean Hall).
