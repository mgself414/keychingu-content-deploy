⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Airport = Incheon Airport only" — Gimpo Airport value unknown

Foreigners only recognize Korean airports as Incheon Airport (ICN), but Gimpo Airport (GMP) is Korea's No. 1 airport (opened in 1958) + Japan/Southeast Asia short-distance route hub (Tokyo, Osaka, Fukuoka, Taipei, Shanghai, etc.) + Domestic flight hot zone. Gimpo Airport → Gangnam 30 minutes / Hongdae 25 minutes (subway). Short-distance foreign transfer + #1 domestic business trip in Korea.

2. "Magog = new city = nothing to see" — Seoul Botanical Garden·LG Art Center Miji

Magok New Town (created in 2014) is Korea’s first new city + global companies moving in (LG Science Park 2018, LG Art Center 2022, Kolon, E-Land, etc.). Seoul Botanical Garden (Opened in 2019, Korea's largest botanical garden, integrated with 5 plant museums) + LG Art Center (Korea's first performance hall, relocated from Gangnam to Magok) = No. 1 foreign family and cultural deep dive.

3. "Gimpo Airport duty free = less than Incheon Airport"

Gimpo Airport Duty Free Shop and Lotte Mall are smaller than Incheon Airport, but are foreigner-friendly + duty free + refund hot zone. In particular, Lotte Mall Gimpo Airport Branch offers standard instant refund for foreigners + full set of Korean-style dining and cafe. No. 1 for transfer/duty exemption for short-distance departures (Options other than Incheon Airport).

Scenery of Magok and Gimpo Airport

Magok/Gimpo Airport is located in the east and west of Gangseo-gu. Foreigner transfer + new city combination area — Korea's first airport since Gimpo Airport opened in 1958 + Magok new city was created in 2014 + Seoul Botanical Garden opened in 2019 + LG Art Center relocated in 2022, making it Korea's future city deep dive hot zone.

Gimpo International Airport (GMP) sub-area is directly connected to Gimpo Airport Station on Line 5, Line 9, and Airport Railroad — International Terminal (Japan, Southeast Asia) + Domestic Terminal (Jeju, Busan, Gwangju, etc.) + Lotte Mall Gimpo Airport Branch + Duty Free Shop.

Magok New Town sub-area is Line 5, Line 9, and Airport Railroad Magok Station, Magongnaru Station, and Balsan Station area — LG Science Park + LG Art Center + Kolon and E-Land. No. 1 foreign business.

Seoul Botanical Garden sub-area is a 5-minute walk from Magongnaru Station — Korea's largest botanical garden (500,000 pyeong) + 5 plant museums + lake garden + No. 1 for foreign families and walks.

LG Arts Center sub-area is near Magok LG Science Park — Korea's first performance hall relocated from Gangnam to Magok in 2022. English subtitles are OK for some performances.

Transportation: Line 5, Line 9, Airport Railroad Gimpo Airport Station / Line 5, Line 9 Magok Station, Magongnaru Station, Balsan Station. Incheon Airport → Gimpo Airport, 18 minutes by Airport Railroad (Korea No. 1 transfer line).

⚙ FAQ (8)

Q1. Difference between Gimpo Airport and Incheon Airport?

Gimpo Airport: Excellent access to downtown (30 minutes from Gangnam), short-distance international flights to Japan and Southeast Asia + domestic flights. Incheon Airport: Korea’s No. 1 international airport, hub for long-distance routes to the Americas, Europe, and the Middle East. For foreigners from Japan and Southeast Asia, Gimpo Airport is a friendlier.

Q2. Seoul Botanical Garden entrance fee/hours?

Botanical garden main building (5 plant museums) 5,000 won / Lake garden and walking trails free. 09:30~18:00 (Closed on Mondays). English guidance for foreigners OK. 5 minutes walk from Magongnaru Station.

Q3. Are foreigners allowed to perform at LG Arts Center?

OK — Some performances (musicals, plays, ballets) with English subtitles. Interpark English UI + Foreigner seat reservation OK. Korea’s No. 1 performance deep dive.

Q4. How to transfer/tax free at Gimpo Airport?

Gimpo Airport International Terminal → Directly connected to duty-free shop and Lotte Mall Gimpo Airport branch. Instant tax-free refund for foreigners Standard (over 30,000 won + passport). The first option for transfers other than Incheon Airport.

Q5. What are the foreigner-friendly stores in Magog?

Cafe Gongmyeong, Coffee with Flowers, Money Hole, and Cozy House have English menu + card payment + foreigner-friendly standard. Because it is a new city, there is ample business infrastructure for foreigners.

Q6. Is Magok + Gimpo Airport 1-day course available?

OK — Magok New Town (Seoul Botanical Garden, LG Art Center, Cafe, Dining) + Gimpo Airport (Duty Free, Transfer) = 1 full day. Directly connected to Line 5, Line 9, and Airport Railroad.

Q7. Magog → Gangnam/Hongdae route?

Magok Station (Line 5, Line 9, Airport Railroad) → Gangnam (35 minutes by subway) / Hongdae (15 minutes by Airport Railroad + 5 minutes by Line 9). adjacent area. Foreign transfer OK.

Q8. Seoul Botanical Garden season?

No. 1 cherry blossoms in spring and April + autumn leaves in fall from October to November. Summer June-August Lake Garden + Illumination (season limited). Winter December-February Greenhouse deep dive.
