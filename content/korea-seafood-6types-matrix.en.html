🎯 Why a Korean Seafood Overview

The most common pain point for foreign visitors is knowing only raw fish (회, hoe) and nothing about the rest of Korea's seafood. Korean seafood breaks down into 3 seas — the East Sea (동해), South Sea (남해), and West Sea (서해) — plus four-season signatures and market favorites. Even under the umbrella of "Korean seafood," clams (조개, Jogae — West Sea specialty, bajirak, mussels), oysters (굴, Gul — winter #1, Tongyeong specialty), blue crab (꽃게, Kkotge — West Sea autumn #1, soy-marinated crab), squid (오징어, Ojingeo — East Sea specialty, Ulleungdo, Donghae), shrimp (새우, Saewoo — nationwide, an MZ (Korea's Gen-Z/Millennial trend-setters) favorite), and octopus (낙지, Nakji — West Sea specialty, Mokpo, live octopus) each belong to a different region and season. Most foreign visitors don't realize: (a) raw fish and seafood are different categories entirely, (b) Tongyeong oysters are the top winter pick, (c) blue crab is an authentic autumn dish loved for soy-marinated crab, and (d) live octopus is Mokpo's signature specialty. This guide covers the 6-type overview, the 3 seas, and what foreign visitors love most.

🦐 6-Type Deep Dive

🐚 Clams (Jogae, 조개 · bajirak, mussels, comb pen shell) — the #1 starter pick

  • Signature: Year-round; bajirak clam noodle soup, mussel soup
  • Ingredients: bajirak (clams), mussels, comb pen shell (scallops)
  • Tourist-friendliness: ★★★★ (#1 starter pick, globally familiar)
  • Recommended markets & spots:
  • Soraepogu (소래포구) · Ganghwa (강화) — authentic West Sea
  • Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장) — South Sea
  • MZ-favorite clam-noodle & mussel-soup spots — great value
  • Season: Year-round, best in spring (Mar-May)
  • Price: Market = ₩10,000~25,000/kg (~$7-18) / clam noodle soup = ₩8,000~15,000

🦪 Oysters (Gul, 굴) — winter #1

  • Signature: Authentic #1 from Tongyeong, South Sea; raw oysters, a Dec-Mar favorite
  • Ingredients: oysters, raw or cooked
  • Tourist-friendliness: ★★★★★ (globally familiar oysters, a winter favorite)
  • Recommended markets & spots:
  • Tongyeong oyster specialty shops — the authentic #1
  • Seoul oyster rice soup shops (Gangnam, 강남 · Jongno, 종로) — great value
  • Gwangjang Market (광장시장) — authentic market pick
  • Season: Winter, Dec-Mar #1 (raw oysters)
  • Price: Tongyeong = ₩10,000~25,000/kg / oyster rice soup = ₩8,000~15,000

🦀 Blue Crab (Kkotge, 꽃게) — autumn #1 · soy-marinated crab

  • Signature: West Sea, autumn Sep-Nov #1; the #1 favorite for soy-marinated crab
  • Ingredients: blue crab, live, prepared with soy-sauce marinade or spicy marinade
  • Tourist-friendliness: ★★★★★ (soy-marinated crab, a global #1 favorite)
  • Recommended spots:
  • Mapo (마포) · Gangnam soy-marinated crab specialty shops — the authentic #1
  • Anmyeondo (안면도) · Taean (태안) · Soraepogu, West Sea — authentic autumn pick
  • Blue crab stew shops — great value
  • Season: Autumn, Sep-Nov #1
  • Price: Soy-marinated crab per person = ₩30,000~80,000 / blue crab stew = ₩15,000~30,000

🦑 Squid (Ojingeo, 오징어) — East Sea specialty

  • Signature: East Sea specialty — Ulleungdo (울릉도), Gangwon (강원) — plus year-round, peaking in summer (Jun-Sep)
  • Ingredients: squid — raw, steamed, grilled, or MZ-style ice-blended
  • Tourist-friendliness: ★★★★ (globally familiar squid)
  • Recommended markets & spots:
  • Ulleungdo · Gangwon's East Sea coast — authentic
  • Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진수산시장) — live seafood
  • MZ-style squid fusion cafes (a Haenam, 해남, favorite)
  • Season: Summer Jun-Sep, also year-round
  • Price: Market = ₩5,000~15,000 each / raw squid = ₩15,000~30,000 per person

🦐 Shrimp (Saewoo, 새우 · king prawn) — nationwide, autumn

  • Signature: Nationwide; autumn king prawn is the #1 pick; soy-marinated raw shrimp is an MZ trend
  • Ingredients: king prawn — salt-grilled, or soy-marinated raw
  • Tourist-friendliness: ★★★★ (globally familiar shrimp)
  • Recommended markets & spots:
  • Anmyeondo · Taean, West Sea — authentic autumn king prawn
  • MZ-favorite soy-marinated-shrimp spots (Hongdae, 홍대 · Gangnam) — trending
  • Season: Autumn, Oct-Nov king prawn #1
  • Price: Market = ₩15,000~30,000/kg / soy-marinated raw shrimp = ₩25,000~50,000 per person

🐙 Octopus (Nakji, 낙지 · live octopus) — Mokpo #1, West Sea

  • Signature: Authentic West Sea Mokpo specialty, the #1 pick for live-octopus fans; minced live octopus
  • Ingredients: octopus, small-legged octopus, live octopus
  • Tourist-friendliness: ★★★ (live octopus is an acquired taste — eaten while still moving)
  • Recommended spots:
  • Mokpo Comprehensive Fisheries Market (목포 종합수산시장) — the authentic #1
  • Seoul live-octopus specialty shops (Mapo · Jongno) — authentic
  • Season: Autumn, also year-round
  • Price: Live octopus, one serving = ₩15,000~40,000 / octopus stew = ₩15,000~30,000
  • Caution: Live octopus is cut into small live pieces and eaten immediately (choking risk — chew slowly)

🌊 The 3 Seas' Signatures

🌊 East Sea (동해 · Gangwon · Ulleungdo)

  • Signature: Squid (the East Sea's #1), snow crab (Sokcho, 속초), octopus
  • Season: Year-round, summer squid
  • Recommended markets: Mukho (묵호), Samcheok (삼척), Sokcho, Gangneung (강릉) on the East Sea

🏝 South Sea (남해 · Tongyeong · Jagalchi · Mokpo)

  • Signature: Oysters #1 (Tongyeong, Dec-Mar), sea bream, octopus (Mokpo)
  • Season: Winter oysters, spring sea bream, autumn octopus
  • Recommended markets: Jagalchi, Tongyeong, Mokpo, Yeosu (여수)

🌅 West Sea (서해 · Incheon · Soraepogu · Anmyeondo)

  • Signature: Blue crab #1 (autumn, Sep-Nov), king prawn, clams
  • Season: Autumn blue crab and king prawn, spring clams
  • Recommended markets: Soraepogu, Ganghwa, Anmyeondo, Taean

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. "Raw fish = seafood": Raw fish (hoe) means live-fish sashimi, while seafood covers clams, oysters, blue crab, shrimp, and more (usually cooked, not raw).

2. "Oysters are available year-round": Oysters peak in winter, Dec-Mar (out of season means farmed, with a higher food-poisoning risk).

3. "Blue crab is available all year": Blue crab peaks in autumn, Sep-Nov (the authentic season for soy-marinated crab).

4. "Live octopus is dangerous": Live octopus should be chewed slowly — there's a choking risk, so cut it small and take your time.

5. "Tongyeong oysters are nothing special": Tongyeong oysters are the authentic #1 from the South Sea — raw, coastal, and fresh.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. Best starting point for beginners: Clams (bajirak) + shrimp + squid → then oysters (in winter).

2. Instagram & vlog spots: Tongyeong oysters, West Sea blue crab, Mokpo live octopus, Jagalchi Market.

3. MZ trends: Soy-marinated raw shrimp, MZ-style squid, soy-marinated crab.

4. Soy-marinated crab favorites: Mapo (#1), authentic Gangnam spots.

5. Allergies: Disclose shellfish and mollusk allergies in advance.

6. Choking risk: With live octopus, chew slowly and take small bites.

7. Payment & tipping: Markets = cash or card / raw-fish restaurants = card OK / no tipping.

8. VAT refund: Food and drink generally aren't eligible for a refund.

⚠ Medical, Safety & Dietary Disclaimer

Seafood carries a food-poisoning risk (especially oysters in spring and summer). Pregnant travelers: cooked seafood is fine, but avoid raw oysters (per CDC guidance). Children: limited (live octopus poses a choking risk — recommended for ages 5+). Disclose allergies in advance (shellfish, mollusks, soy-sauce gluten). If you suspect food poisoning, call ☎ 1339 (medical hotline, English available). This guide provides general information and is not medical advice.

Dietary restriction fallback:

  • Halal & Kosher: All 6 types are shellfish/mollusks — partially halal-acceptable (opinions vary; check with the venue) / not kosher, since shellfish and mollusks aren't kosher.
  • Vegetarian & vegan: None of the 6 types are vegetarian; try bibimbap or temple food instead.
  • Gluten-free: Raw and grilled dishes are fine; soy-marinated crab and soy-marinated shrimp contain some gluten (ask for a tamari substitute).
