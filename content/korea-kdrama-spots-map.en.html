⚠ 3 Common K-drama Spots Mistakes

1. "K-drama = Goblin only" — missing the newer titles: Only knowing Goblin. In reality, there is a new global #1 cluster every year — Itaewon Class 2020, Squid Game 2021, Reborn Rich 2022, The Glory 2023, Queen of Tears 2024 (Netflix, Disney+, HBO).

2. "Filming locations = Seoul only" — missing Gangneung, Incheon, and the rest of the country: Seoul only. In reality, a nationwide cluster: Anmok in Gangneung (Goblin), Incheon Chinatown (Goblin), Unjicheon (Squid Game), Bongpyeong (Goblin · #423) (KTX 1–3h).

3. "Dramas = no guides" — not using K-tour packages: Assuming no guides exist. In reality, KTO K-tour packages, the Goblin 1-day tour, and the Itaewon Class walking tour (in English for foreigners) offer a full fan experience.
