Korean Drama and Film Location Guide: Parasite, Itaewon Class, Squid Game, Goblin and Winter Sonata, Only Places With Confirmed Addresses

Subtitle: Parasite (기생충) 6 spots (Dongjak, Mapo, Jongno, Seongbuk) · Itaewon Class (이태원 클라쓰) Danbam street (Noksapyeong-daero 40-gil) · Squid Game (오징어 게임) Ssangmun-dong Baegun Market · Guardian: The Lonely and Great God (Goblin) (도깨비) Jumunjin Breakwater (Gangneung, 24 hours) · Winter Sonata (겨울연가) Nami Island (admission KRW 19,000, round-trip boat included) — checked on Korea Tourism Organization (VisitKorea) and Nami Island websites

ISSUE 03 · #452 · 2026-05-26

🎯 How to use this guide

The narrow, steep stairs the Ki-taek family walked down toward home in the pouring rain really exist, in an alley in Changsin-dong, Jongno-gu, Seoul. Pizza Sidae (피자시대), where they folded pizza boxes, is still open in Dongjak-gu, and the breakwater where the two leads of Goblin first met is open 24 hours a day on the Jumunjin coast road in Gangneung. Stepping into the scenes you saw on screen is what a filming-location trip is.

This guide splits those locations into three branches, central Seoul · the Seoul outskirts and Incheon · the Gangwon coast, and lists only places whose addresses were confirmed on the Korea Tourism Organization (VisitKorea) or operator websites. A route that follows a single drama in depth links to its dedicated guide at the bottom.

🍕 Parasite: from the semi-basement to the house on the hill, six spots in Seoul

The highs and lows of class in the film were shot on real hills and stairs in Seoul. All six spots have addresses on the Korea Tourism Organization English website.

  • Sky Pizza (스카이피자): 86 Noryangjin-ro 6-gil, Dongjak-gu, Seoul (동작구 노량진로6길 86). The film's "Pizza Sidae". Where the family folded pizza boxes and plotted to push out the Parks' housekeeper. Open 10:00~20:30.
  • Dwaeji Ssal Super (돼지쌀슈퍼): 32 Sonkijeong-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul (마포구 손기정로 32). The film's "Uri Super". The scene where Ki-woo drinks soju under a parasol and his friend Min-hyuk offers him the tutoring job.
  • Bukahyeon-dong stairs (북아현동 계단): 3 Sonkijeong-ro 6-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul (마포구 손기정로6길 3), right next to the super. The stairs Ki-jung climbs carrying peaches.
  • Changsin-dong alley (창신동 골목): 38 Jibong-ro 11-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul (종로구 지봉로11길 38). The narrow, steep stairs they walk down toward home in the downpour.
  • Jahamun Tunnel (자하문터널): 219 Jahamun-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul (종로구 자하문로 219). The tunnel the family passes after fleeing the Parks' house (Cheongun-dong to Buam-dong).
  • Seongbuk-dong residential area (성북동 주택가): 18 Seonjam-ro 8-gil, Seongbuk-gu, Seoul (성북구 선잠로8길 18). The neighborhood Ki-woo climbs to tutor. The Parks' house was a film set that was torn down after shooting, so the house itself is gone.

📸 The Bukahyeon-dong stairs, Changsin-dong alley and Seongbuk-dong are neighborhoods where people live. Keep your voice down and do not photograph front gates or windows.

🌙 Itaewon Class: a night on the Danbam street

Sae-royi Park's pocha (포차) "Danbam" is on Noksapyeong-daero 40-gil in Itaewon. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it now runs under the name SEOUL BAM, a restaurant where you can try the dishes from the drama (Korea Tourism Organization).

  • SEOUL BAM (Danbam in the drama): 57 Noksapyeong-daero 40-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul (용산구 녹사평대로40길 57). Weekdays 18:00~23:00, weekends 18:00~24:00 (per the Korea Tourism Organization; confirm it is open before you go).
  • Noksapyeong footbridge (녹사평 육교): 19 Noksapyeong-daero 40-gil, Seoul (녹사평대로40길 19), in front of the Itaewon Elementary School education and culture center. A footbridge that became a photo spot after the drama took off.
  • Angel Wings mural stairs (엔젤 윙스 벽화 계단): 116-18 Itaewon-dong, Yongsan-gu, Seoul (용산구 이태원동 116-18).
  • G Guesthouse (G 게스트하우스): 14-38 Bogwang-ro 60-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul (용산구 보광로60길 14-38). Still run as lodging, with a rooftop that looks over N Seoul Tower and Itaewon.
  • Baekbeom Square, Namsan Park (백범광장 (남산공원)): 100-115 Hoehyeon-dong 1-ga, Jung-gu, Seoul (중구 회현동1가 100-115).

📸 Danbam and the guesthouse are working businesses and lodging. Ask first before you photograph inside. For Itaewon night routes, see #450 Seoul Night Guide.

🦑 Squid Game: from Ssangmun-dong alleys across the sea to Gyodong Island

  • Ssangmun-dong Baegun Market and Paldo Dried Fish (쌍문동 백운시장·팔도건어물): 36 Samyang-ro 154-gil, Dobong-gu, Seoul (도봉구 삼양로154길 36). The scene where Gi-hun stops by "Sang-woo's Fish". There is a Squid Game experience hall inside the market.
  • CU Ssangmun Uicheon store (CU 쌍문의천점): 11 Uicheon-ro 39-gil, Dobong-gu, Seoul (도봉구 의천로39길 11). The convenience store where Il-nam and Gi-hun drank soju with cup ramen.
  • Toin, Insadong (토인 (인사동)): 48 Insadong-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul (종로구 인사동길 48). A shop known for its dalgona sets.
  • My Land, Incheon Wolmido (마이랜드 (인천 월미도)): 7 Wolmi-ro 234beon-gil, Jung-gu, Incheon (인천 중구 월미로234번길 7). The scene where Deok-su's gang meets in the rain. The oldest amusement park on Wolmido.
  • Gyodong Elementary School, Incheon Ganghwa (교동초등학교 (인천 강화)): 29 Daeryongan-gil, Gyodong-myeon, Ganghwa-gun, Incheon (강화군 교동면 대룡안길 29). The childhood Squid Game scene. You cannot go inside the school; you can only view it from outside (Korea Tourism Organization).

📸 The market and the convenience store are open for business. Do not block customers who are waiting in line.

❄ Winter Sonata: Nami Island, 5 minutes by boat

Nami Island earned its name as a filming location of the drama Winter Sonata, and today Koreans also visit it for the island's own forest paths and sights (Korea Tourism Organization). The island's address is 1 Nami-seom-gil, Namsan-myeon, Chuncheon-si, Gangwon (강원 춘천시 남산면 남이섬길 1), but the pier where you board the boat is at 1024 Bukhangangbyeon-ro, Gapyeong-eup, Gapyeong-gun, Gyeonggi (경기 가평군 가평읍 북한강변로 1024).

📸 For island photos, aim for the morning hours when the first boat leaves. Boat hours can change with weather and season, so check the website before you set out. For a one-day Gapyeong route, see #1033 Gapyeong, Yangpyeong and Nami Island.

🌊 Goblin and Sandglass: the Gangwon coast

  • Jumunjin Breakwater, Goblin (주문진 방파제 (도깨비)): 1609 Haean-ro, Jumunjin-eup, Gangneung-si, Gangwon (강원 강릉시 주문진읍 해안로 1609). 00:00~24:00. The breakwater where the two leads first met, and many people still come to re-shoot that scene (Korea Tourism Organization). The Korea Tourism Organization suggests you bring a red scarf and a bouquet to recreate the scene. Yeongjin Beach in Jumunjin is a quiet, clean beach (Korea Tourism Organization).
  • Jeongdongjin Station, Sandglass, 1995 (정동진역 (모래시계)): 17 Jeongdongyeok-gil, Gangdong-myeon, Gangneung-si, Gangwon (강원 강릉시 강동면 정동역길 17). When the 1995 drama Sandglass became hugely popular, the station right in front of the sea became known across the country. A sunrise train runs from Cheongnyangni Station in Seoul (Korea Tourism Organization).

📸 The breakwater is a structure out over the sea. It is dark and slippery at night, so go in daylight, and do not go out on rainy days or when the waves are high. Do not climb onto the tetrapods (concrete blocks) on the outer side. For a two-day Gangneung route, see #1106 Gangneung by KTX, 2 Days 1 Night.

🧰 Practical tips: location manners and costs

  • Residential areas and businesses: The Parasite stairs and alleys, the Ssangmun-dong market and the Danbam street are places where people live and work. Avoid group shoots in the early morning or late at night, and do not shoot at angles that show into homes.
  • Schools: Ganghwa Gyodong Elementary School is viewed from outside only.
  • Paying: There is no tipping at Korean restaurants and cafes → #1275 Tipping and Paying Manners
  • Transit card: The Seoul subway and city buses work with one transit card. The Yongsan to Gapyeong ITX-Cheongchun needs a separate ticket → #1298 Choosing Among 3 Transit Cards

⚠ 4 common mistakes

  1. "I'm going to Seongbuk-dong to see the Parks' house": That house was a set and has been torn down. What you can see in Seongbuk-dong is the alley Ki-woo climbed.
  2. "Goblin breakwater = Gangneung Anmok Coffee Street": The Gangneung place that the Korea Tourism Organization introduces as a Goblin filming location is the Jumunjin Breakwater (1609 Haean-ro). Anmok is a coffee street you visit separately → #1037 Gangneung Anmok Beach Coffee.
  3. "Nami Island is right at Gapyeong Station": From Gapyeong Station you go about 1.5km to the pier and then take a boat. The last boat to the island is 21:00.
  4. "A photo of the Squid Game scene in the Gyodong Elementary School yard": Entering the school is prohibited. View it from outside the wall only.

⚙ FAQ 4

Q1. How many titles can I see in one day?

The central Seoul branch (Parasite, Itaewon Class, Squid Game in Ssangmun-dong) is all inside Seoul, but in different districts: the six Parasite spots alone span four districts, Dongjak, Mapo, Jongno and Seongbuk. Plan about half a day per title and check travel times on a map app. Give Nami Island and the Gangwon coast a separate day each.

Q2. Which places charge admission?

Nami Island is the one place where we confirmed an admission fee on the official website (adult KRW 19,000, round-trip boat included). We could not confirm fees for My Land (the amusement park) or the experience hall inside the Ssangmun-dong market, so check before you go. The rest are streets, neighborhoods and shops.

Q3. Which places are fine to visit at night?

Danbam (SEOUL BAM) opens at 6 pm (per the Korea Tourism Organization). Jumunjin Breakwater has no entry-hour limit, but it is dark and slippery at night, so go in daylight. Visit residential filming locations in the daytime too.

Q4. What if I want to take a photo exactly like the scene in the drama?

A red scarf and a bouquet at Jumunjin Breakwater, a dalgona set at Toin in Insadong: one prop brings a scene to life. For scene-by-scene photo tips, see #010 K-Drama Filming Location Tour.
