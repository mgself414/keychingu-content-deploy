5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Thinking "all Korean cafés are the same"

Korean café chains have three clearly defined price tiers: budget (Mega Coffee (메가커피) ₩1,500) → mid-range (Starbucks (스타벅스) ₩4,500) → premium (Blue Bottle (블루보틀) ₩7,000). The same americano can cost anywhere from ₩1,500 to ₩7,000 — a 5× gap — depending on ambiance, roasting quality, and bean grade. See the §Big 3 Budget Chain Comparison section below.

2. Underestimating the Mega Coffee (메가커피) "1L Mega Size"

The Mega Coffee (메가커피) signature Mega Size (1L, ₩4,000+) is roughly double a standard café large cup. First-time visitors often order one and immediately think, "Why is this so huge?" Rule of thumb: 1 person + 1 cup + thirst = regular size; 2–3 people sharing = Mega Size.

3. Not using Siren Order (스타벅스 모바일 앱 — Starbucks mobile app)

The Korean Starbucks (스타벅스) Siren Order (사이렌 오더) app works for foreigners with a Korean phone number and a foreign card. Skip the line, pick up your drink — essential during busy weekday lunches and weekend afternoons. English UI supported.

4. Confusing a Korean "dessert café" with a "coffee specialty shop"

Places like A Twosome Place (투썸플레이스), Paris Baguette (파리바게뜨), and Tous les Jours (뚜레쥬르) are primarily dessert and cake destinations — coffee is secondary. If you're after serious coffee, head to specialty spots like Blue Bottle (블루보틀), Terarosa (테라로사), Paul Bassett (폴바셋), or Onion (어니언). Mixing these up on your first visit can leave you with the wrong impression of Korean coffee.

5. Assuming all Korean cafés are open 24 hours

24-hour cafés in Korea exist only in select branches in core areas like Gangnam (강남), Hongdae (홍대), and Itaewon (이태원). Regular neighborhood branches close around 10–11 PM. For late-night caffeine, Gimbap Cheonguk (김밥천국) diners and convenience store coffee are more reliable.

★ Korea's Big 3 Budget Café Chains — Ediya (이디야) vs. Mega Coffee (메가커피) vs. Paik's Coffee (빽다방)

These are the chains you'll bump into most within a 1 km radius anywhere in Seoul (서울). Prices look similar at ₩1,500–2,500, but signature drinks, store atmosphere, foreigner-friendliness, and 24-hour availability are all quite different.

1️⃣ Ediya Coffee (이디야 커피) — Korea's #1 by Branch Count, Everywhere You Go

Brand Message: "Better coffee for more people" — #1 in branch count (4,000+), balancing value and consistency

Signature Menu: Toffeenut Latte (토피넛 라떼), Tiramisu Latte (티라미수 라떼), Cold Brew (콜드브루), limited seasonal drinks

Pricing (April–May 2026):

  • Americano: ₩2,500 (Ice ₩2,800)
  • Toffeenut Latte (토피넛 라떼): ₩4,000–4,500 (signature)
  • Tiramisu Latte (티라미수 라떼): ₩4,500
  • Cold Brew (콜드브루): ₩4,000
  • Desserts (bagels, muffins): ₩2,500–4,000

Store Highlights:

  • 4,000+ branches nationwide = Korea's #1 — within a 5-minute walk in virtually any neighborhood
  • Average 8–15 seats; dining in and laptop work are welcome
  • English menus available at select branches (primarily photo menus with pictograms)
  • Payment: card + cash + mobile + kiosk all accepted

One-Line Verdict: "The go-to answer for reliable, value-for-money coffee anywhere in Korea" — top marks for branch count + price + foreigner-friendliness

Best For: First-time visitors / near your hotel on a weekday lunch / laptop work / your default answer to "which café should we go to?"

2️⃣ Mega MGC Coffee (메가커피) — #1 Value with the 1L Mega Size

Brand Message: "Mega Size, Mega Value" — the cup size is the value proposition

Signature Menu: 1L Mega Size (available for any drink), Mega Latte (메가 라떼), limited seasonal flavors (chestnut, sweet potato, strawberry)

Pricing:

  • Americano (Hot/Ice): ₩1,500 / ₩2,000 (cheapest of the Big 3)
  • Mega Size (1L) Americano: ₩4,000–4,500 (cup diameter ~25 cm)
  • Mega Latte (메가 라떼): ₩2,500
  • Mega Size (1L) Latte: ₩5,500
  • Desserts: ₩2,500–3,500

Store Highlights:

  • 2,500+ branches nationwide — the fastest-growing new chain
  • Many branches are takeout-focused (limited seating — average 5–10 seats)
  • Limited English menus; primarily photo menus and pictograms
  • Payment: card + mobile (cash accepted only at some branches)

One-Line Verdict: "₩1,500 americano + Mega Size 1L = #1 value"

Best For: Budget-first travelers / groups of 2–3 sharing a Mega Size / takeout + a stroll / that quintessential Korean street café photo

3️⃣ Paik's Coffee (빽다방) — Baek Jong-won's Brand: Generous Portions and Flavor

Brand Message: A café by Korean food expert Baek Jong-won (백종원) — generous portions with a focus on flavor

Signature Menu: Baek Size (백사이즈, 1L), Paik's Cream Latte (빽크림라떼), Black Sesame Latte (흑임자라떼), limited seasonal drinks

Pricing:

  • Americano: ₩1,500–2,000 (on par with Mega Coffee)
  • Paik's Cream Latte (빽크림라떼): ₩3,500–4,000 (signature)
  • Baek Size (백사이즈, 1L) Americano: ₩4,500–5,000
  • Black Sesame Latte (흑임자라떼): ₩4,000 (traditional Korean ingredient)
  • Desserts (cookies, muffins, bagels): ₩2,500–4,000

Store Highlights:

  • 1,500+ branches nationwide — fastest growth among mid-tier newcomers
  • Clean store atmosphere; laptop work-friendly with an average of 10–20 seats
  • Menus featuring traditional Korean ingredients (black sesame (흑임자), injeolmi (인절미), misugaru (미숫가루)) — ideal for foreigners wanting an authentic Korean flavor experience
  • Payment: card + mobile + kiosk

One-Line Verdict: "Baek Jong-won's (백종원) generous portions + traditional Korean ingredient lattes = value meets cultural experience"

Best For: Trying traditional Korean ingredient drinks (black sesame (흑임자), injeolmi (인절미)) / laptop work / coffee + dessert in one stop

8 Must-Know Tips for Foreign Visitors (Checklist)

✅ Set up Siren Order (사이렌 오더 — Starbucks app)

  • Works with a Korean phone number + foreign card
  • English UI supported
  • Skip the line and pick up — essential on weekday lunches and weekend afternoons

✅ Understand the size naming differences

  • Korean café sizes: Tall (R) / Grande (L) / Venti / Mega (1L)
  • Mega Size is a Mega Coffee (메가커피) and Paik's Coffee (빽다방) thing — too large for one person

✅ Payment methods

  • Card + mobile is almost 100% standard
  • Virtually no café is cash-only — if you don't have a card, try Naver Pay (네이버페이) or Kakao Pay (카카오페이)
  • If your foreign card is declined at the counter, try again at the kiosk

✅ Allergies and dietary needs

  • Milk → soy or oat milk options available (Starbucks (스타벅스) and Blue Bottle (블루보틀) are safe bets)
  • Vegan → options at specialty cafés like Onion (어니언) and Blue Bottle (블루보틀)
  • Caffeine → decaf options available at all chains (extra charge ₩300–500)

✅ Working on a laptop / meetings

  • Ediya (이디야), Paik's Coffee (빽다방), Compose Coffee (컴포즈커피), Starbucks (스타벅스), A Twosome Place (투썸플레이스) = laptop-friendly
  • Mega Coffee (메가커피), Blue Bottle (블루보틀) = limited seating, not ideal for work
  • WiFi: 100% free WiFi at Korean café chains (chain ID + password)

✅ 24-hour branches

  • Only select branches in core areas like Gangnam (강남), Hongdae (홍대), and Itaewon (이태원)
  • Regular neighborhood branches close around 10–11 PM
  • For late-night needs → Gimbap Cheonguk (김밥천국) diners and convenience store coffee are more reliable

✅ Mug and tumbler souvenirs

  • Starbucks (스타벅스) Korea-exclusive tumblers and mugs = the #1 souvenir for foreign visitors
  • New releases every month and every season — limited stock at Incheon Airport (인천공항) duty-free
  • Blue Bottle (블루보틀) Korea merchandise is also popular

✅ Café etiquette

  • Bring only the café's own drinks inside (no outside beverages)
  • Saving a seat with a laptop, bag, or belongings while you step away = totally normal Korean café culture
  • Keep phone calls quiet; use earphones for video calls

FAQ

Q1. Does English work?

Starbucks (스타벅스), Blue Bottle (블루보틀), and Ediya (이디야) have English menus as standard. Mega Coffee (메가커피), Paik's Coffee (빽다방), and Compose Coffee (컴포즈커피) rely mainly on photo menus, but pointing at what you want works just fine.

Q2. Is decaf available?

Yes, at all chains. Just say "decaf (디카페인)" and they'll understand. Extra charge of ₩300–500.

Q3. Are soy and oat milk options available?

Starbucks (스타벅스), Blue Bottle (블루보틀), Onion (어니언), and Paul Bassett (폴바셋) = soy and oat milk widely available. Ediya (이디야), Mega Coffee (메가커피), and Paik's Coffee (빽다방) = available at select branches only; check in advance.

Q4. Can I work on a laptop?

Ediya (이디야), Paik's Coffee (빽다방), Compose Coffee (컴포즈커피), Starbucks (스타벅스), and A Twosome Place (투썸플레이스) = laptop-friendly (outlets + WiFi + adequate seating). Mega Coffee (메가커피) and Blue Bottle (블루보틀) = limited seating; not ideal. If you plan to stay 2+ hours, order another drink — that's standard Korean café etiquette.

Q5. What if they don't take cards?

Rarely happens, but if it does, try mobile payment (Naver Pay (네이버페이) · Kakao Pay (카카오페이)) or cash. If your foreign card is declined, try the kiosk.

Q6. Where can I find Korea-exclusive Starbucks (스타벅스) mugs and tumblers?

Starbucks (스타벅스) Korea exclusives drop monthly and seasonally. A limited selection is available at Incheon Airport (인천공항) duty-free at roughly the same price as in-store — a top souvenir option.

Seasonal Tips

Spring (March–May)

  • Limited-edition menus (cherry blossom lattes, spring mugs) — available for one month only
  • Tourist numbers surge — weekday mornings are recommended

Summer (June–August)

  • Mega Size 1L is the top pick for summer — beat the heat and stay hydrated
  • Korean bingsu (빙수, shaved ice) + café combo (A Twosome Place (투썸플레이스), Sulbing (설빙))

Autumn (September–November)

  • Peak Korean café season — autumn foliage + café culture
  • Seasonal limited menus (chestnut (밤), sweet potato (고구마), pumpkin (호박))

Winter (December–February)

  • Season for warm lattes, green tea, and desserts
  • Paik's Coffee (빽다방) Black Sesame (흑임자) and Injeolmi (인절미) = the definitive Korean winter drink choice
