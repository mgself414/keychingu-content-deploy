Why Line 9 — Gimpo Airport to Gangnam by Express

Line 9 (Gold)'s weapon is the 'express.' From Gimpo Airport to Yeouido, Express Bus Terminal (고속터미널), Gangnam, and Jamsil, the express train speeds through only the major stations. Get off at Gimpo Airport via the Airport Railroad (공항철도) from Incheon Airport (인천공항), hop on the Line 9 express, and you reach Gangnam in no time. Add to that Line 9's signature spots like Noryangjin Fish Market, Bongeunsa, and Seokchon Lake.

This guide organizes Line 9's 8 key tourist stations station by station. It pairs Hangang, fish-market, temple, and lake attractions (linked to existing deep dives) with eateries pulled by per-station nearest-neighbor mapping from accumulated data. It is the seventh installment of the subway line-by-line series, and the line added by newly securing coordinates.

Line Tips ×5 — Get 200% Out of Line 9

Tip 1. The Express Is the Heart of Line 9

The express train stops only at major stations, so it's fast (Gimpo Airport · Yeouido · Express Bus Terminal · Sinnonhyeon · Bongeunsa, etc.). Check the platform vs. the local train. The express saves airport-to-Gangnam travel time.

Tip 2. A Shortcut for the Arrival Route

Incheon Airport → Airport Railroad → Gimpo Airport → Line 9 express → Gangnam. Often faster than the airport bus.

Tip 3. Noryangjin Fish Market Is Line 9's Delicacy

Pick seafood and have it as sashimi at a chojang restaurant. The dawn auction is a sight too. Cup Bap Street offers cheap street food.

Tip 4. Bongeunsa = Tradition in the City Center

A 1,200-year-old temple across from COEX (#1240). Temple Stay (#509) · temple food. The modern (COEX) and the traditional (Bongeunsa) face each other.

Tip 5. With a Single Transit Card

One T-money card covers the Line 9 express, transfers, and buses. The Airport Railroad (Gimpo Airport) connection covers the arrival route too.

FAQ 6 — Common Foreigner Questions About Line 9

Q1. What are Line 9's strengths?

  • The express. Gimpo Airport ~ Yeouido ~ Express Bus Terminal ~ Gangnam ~ Jamsil, fast. Great for arrival (Airport Railroad connection) and Gangnam travel.

Q2. How do I ride the express?

  • The platform shows an express/local distinction. The express stops only at major stations (some stations are skipped). Check express status in a map app.

Q3. What about Noryangjin Fish Market?

  • Noryangjin Station. Pick seafood and have it as sashimi at a chojang restaurant. Dawn auction · Cup Bap Street too. Line 9's signature food.

Q4. What about Bongeunsa and COEX?

  • Bongeunsa Station. A 1,200-year-old temple (#509) and COEX (Aquarium · Byeolmadang · #1240) face each other. Tradition + modern.

Q5. Is the restaurant info accurate?

  • It's based on a Naver Map crawl, so it's for reference. Re-confirm in a map app before visiting.

Q6. How do I get to Gangnam after arriving?

  • Incheon Airport → Airport Railroad → Gimpo Airport → Line 9 express. Luggage delivery (#1232) · SIM (#414) are available at Gimpo Airport · Seoul Station (서울역).
