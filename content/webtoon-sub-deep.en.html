⚠️ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

Mistake 1: "I need to download LINE Webtoon again once I'm in Korea"

Fact: LINE Webtoon (webtoon.com) is a global platform. You can keep using the version downloaded from your home country's app store — no need for the Korean domestic app store. However, the Naver Webtoon KR app (the domestic Korean service) and LINE Webtoon Global are separate apps. To read Korean originals in English, use the LINE Webtoon Global app.

Mistake 2: "I can read the entire Solo Leveling original for free on Kakao Page"

Fact: Kakao Page runs on a wait-to-unlock system — each episode opens for free after a set amount of time. Reading the full series instantly requires paying with coins. Foreign cards (Visa, Mastercard) work, but signing up without a Korean phone number can limit some features. Trying Google or Apple social login instead of a Kakao account first is recommended.

Mistake 3: "Pop-up stores and merch shops are always open"

Fact: Webtoon pop-up stores are, by default, run for a limited period. Pop-ups tied to Tower of God and Solo Leveling change location and operating period by season. Checking the official social media (Twitter, Instagram) or webtoon.com's official events page before visiting is essential. Many visitors arrive on-site only to find the pop-up already ended.

12 Picks Across 4 Axes — Deep Dive

Axis 1: Naver Webtoon Platforms (3 picks)

1. Naver Webtoon — the Global Entry Point

Platform info

  • Website: webtoon.com (global EN) / comic.naver.com (KR originals)
  • App: Free download on both iOS and Android
  • Language: English, French, German, Spanish, Indonesian, Thai, Chinese, Japanese, and 10+ more

How foreigners use it

  • Go to webtoon.com or the LINE Webtoon app → set your language → sign up (email, Google, or Apple social login)
  • There's an update lag between the KR original and the EN translation (usually 1-3 days). To read the newest chapter fast, you can access the Korean original directly, though some content is Korea-IP-first
  • Fast Pass (paid early access): purchased with Cookies (in-app currency). Foreign card payment is accepted

Cost

  • Free by default. Fast Pass runs about $1 USD/episode (local pricing may differ, so checking the in-app price is recommended)
  • Many titles can be unlocked for free by watching ads

Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ — Full English UI, international card payment without a Korean bank account

2. Naver Webtoon's Newest Hits — the Front Line of Korean Trends

Features

  • The weekly top 10 by domestic Korean readers can be checked on the KR app (comic.naver.com)
  • New releases face an EN translation delay (weeks to months). Foreign fans who want to keep up with trends fast use the KR app alongside
  • Major genres: romance, fantasy, action, thriller. English translation speed varies significantly by genre

Practical tips for foreigners

  • Reading the Korean original: a Google Translate browser extension can give a rough translation on the web version (imperfect)
  • The Naver app can be joined with email or a Google account even without a Korean phone number (some age-verification restrictions apply)
  • Just checking the KR app's ranking tab is enough to track trends

3. LINE Webtoon (Global EN) — the #1 Entry Point for Foreigners

Platform info

  • webtoon.com / LINE Webtoon app (iOS, Android)
  • Naver Webtoon's global brand name. The same service
  • Global MAU of 82 million+ (2024 official figure)

Why it fits foreigners

  • English UI by default. Thousands of English-translated titles
  • The wait-to-unlock system lets you finish most titles without paying
  • Community comments and episode ratings are active in English, enabling fandom participation

What's different when visiting Korea

  • Since LINE Webtoon is a digital platform, there's no "visiting" — it's just app use. You can use it the same way during a Korea trip
  • However, experiences only possible in Korea include: webtoon pop-up stores, official merch shops, and bookstore webtoon corners (see Axis 4 below)

Axis 2: Kakao Page & Daum (3 picks)

4. Kakao Page — the Solo Leveling Original Platform

Platform info

  • App: Kakao Page (iOS, Android)
  • The platform where Solo Leveling — Na Honjaman Level-up (나 혼자만 레벨업) — originally serialized and concluded
  • Sign up with a Kakao account or Google/Apple social login

Solo Leveling status

  • Original web novel: serialized and completed on Kakao Page
  • Webtoon version: serialized and completed on Kakao Page (2022)
  • English translation: search "Solo Leveling" on LINE Webtoon EN to read the English version
  • Anime: produced by A-1 Pictures (Season 1 in 2024, Season 2 in 2025). Foreign viewers searching out the original webtoon after watching the anime have surged

How foreigners use it

  • Accessing the English translation: searching "Solo Leveling" on the LINE Webtoon EN app is recommended (far more accessible to foreigners than the Korean Kakao Page)
  • To read the Korean original: Kakao Page app → sign up with a Google account → buy coins (Korean Visa/Mastercard payment works, verification recommended)

Cost

  • Wait-to-unlock: opens for free after a set time. Coins are needed to read the full series instantly
  • Coins: about 100 KRW per coin (varies by title and episode)

5. Kakao Webtoon's Popular Titles

Features

  • The Kakao Webtoon app is separate from Kakao Page. Strong mainly in romance and fantasy genres
  • Japanese (JP) and Chinese (CN) translation services: separate apps exist — Piccoma (JP) and Kakao Webtoon Global (CN, Southeast Asia)
  • For Japanese fans, the Piccoma app is the best route to access Kakao originals

Practical info for foreigners

  • EN support is weak. English-speaking foreigners should prioritize LINE Webtoon EN
  • JP and CN readers should use the Piccoma (JP) and Kakao Webtoon Global (CN) apps respectively
  • The app itself can be installed and used in Korea, but the payment environment is tied to your home-country app store

6. Daum Webtoon / Merged into Kakao Page

Current status

  • Daum Webtoon fully merged into Kakao Page after 2023. "Daum Webtoon" as a standalone service has effectively ended
  • Some former Daum Webtoon titles can still be read on Kakao Page
  • Foreigners don't need to specifically seek out "Daum Webtoon." Use Kakao Page or LINE Webtoon EN instead

Practical info for foreigners

  • If looking for legacy former-Daum-Webtoon titles: search the title name directly on Kakao Page
  • Most popular titles have an English translation on LINE Webtoon EN

Axis 3: Filming Sites & Pop-Up Experiences (3 picks)

7. Tower of God-Related Experiences

Title info

  • Tower of God — Sinui Tap (신의 탑): original by SIU, serialized on Naver Webtoon. Anime Season 1 (2020, Telecom Animation Film), Season 2 (2024, produced by MAPPA)
  • Global cumulative views of 4.9 billion+ (Naver Webtoon official)

Experience options (as confirmed to exist)

  • Pop-up stores: Tower of God pop-ups run seasonally in major Seoul districts (Hongdae, Gangnam, Seongsu, etc.). When running, entry ranges free~paid, with merch sales. Always check current operating status via the official Twitter (@towerofgod_en, etc.) or webtoon.com's events page before visiting
  • Bookstore merch corners: Tower of God merch and art books can be bought at the webtoon/comics corners of major bookstores like Kyobo Bookstore (교보문고), Yes24 (예스24), and Aladdin (알라딘) (stock-check recommended)
  • Anime cafes: Collaboration cafes run for a limited period. Checking social media before visiting is essential

General location guide (when a pop-up is running)

  • Hongdae: Hongik Univ. Station, Line 2/Airport Railroad (the main district toward Hapjeong)
  • Gangnam: around Gangnam Station, Lines 2/9
  • Seongsu: Seongsu Station, Line 2 (a growing pop-up-dense district)

Foreigner fit: ★★★★ — Pop-ups provide some English signage. The language barrier for buying merch is low

8. Solo Leveling-Related Pop-Ups & Merch

Title info

  • Solo Leveling: original web novel by Chugong, adapted into a webtoon (art by Jang Sung-rak). Serialized and completed on Kakao Page
  • Anime: A-1 Pictures Season 1 (2024), Season 2 (2025). Global fandom growing fast

Experience options (as confirmed to exist)

  • Pop-up stores/collaboration events: Pop-ups run in major Seoul districts around the anime's broadcast season, either as Kakao's official merch shop or an outside collaboration
  • Official online merch: kakao.com/gifts or the Kakao Friends shop sometimes sells Solo Leveling merch during certain seasons (watch for stock/seasonal limits)
  • Bookstore comics/art books: Printed volumes of the original web novel (Korean) are available at major bookstores like Kyobo Bookstore. The English print edition is published through the global publisher Yen Press — English-edition stock at Korean bookstores is limited, so checking ahead is recommended

Cost

  • Pop-up entry: free~about 30,000 KRW (varies by event)
  • Merch: acrylic stands, posters, art books, etc., ranging from 5,000 KRW to tens of thousands of KRW

Foreigner fit: ★★★★ — A high share of foreign anime-fan visitors. Pop-ups get advance English social-media notices

Axis 4: Foreigner Mania & Experiences (3 picks)

10. The LINE Webtoon EN App — the Perfect Entry Route for Foreigners

Foreigner onboarding steps

1. Search "Webtoon" on webtoon.com or your app store → download

2. Sign up with an email, Google, or Apple account (no Korean phone number needed)

3. Set your genres → start reading recommended titles

4. Search "Tower of God," "Solo Leveling," "True Beauty," and more → read the English versions

Tips for staying connected during a Korea trip

  • The app works the same with data roaming or a Korean SIM card while traveling
  • Check webtoon.com's events tab or the official @webtoon social account for Korean official merch/pop-up info
  • After arriving in Korea, check pop-up event notices like "Webtoon World" to plan an in-person visit

Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ — the only official, legal, free route to read thousands of titles in English without paying

11. Kakao Page CN/JP Mania Route

Chinese (CN) readers

  • Kakao Webtoon Global app (for Southeast Asia/CN): access Chinese translations of Kakao originals
  • However, access may be restricted depending on the Chinese app store environment (checking local VPN regulations is recommended)
  • Alternative: set the LINE Webtoon app's language to Chinese to read Chinese-translated titles

Japanese (JP) readers

  • Piccoma app: a Japanese-translation service for Kakao originals. Installed via the Japanese app store (usable in Korea too)
  • For Japanese webtoon fans visiting Korea: keep using the Piccoma app, or use the Naver Webtoon JP version (japan.webtoons.com)

Payment

  • Piccoma: Japanese card payment accepted
  • Kakao Webtoon Global: uses the payment method of your country's app store

12. Webtoon Merch & Bookstore Experiences (#628, #652 cross)

Kyobo Bookstore

  • Location: the Gwanghwamun branch (near Gwanghwamun Square, Seoul, a 5-minute walk from Gwanghwamun Station, Line 5), the Gangnam branch, and more
  • Foreigner fit: runs a corner for webtoon volumes, art books, and merch. A separate English-books corner exists
  • Korean-language webtoon volumes are available for purchase. English-edition stock is limited (verification recommended)
  • The Gwanghwamun branch: the B1 comics/webtoon corner is relatively large

Yes24

  • A major domestic online bookstore. Online stock is richer than physical stores
  • Foreign card payment accepted online. Domestic shipping only (can ship to your accommodation)
  • You can order online while staying in Korea and receive it at your accommodation

Aladdin

  • Strong in used books and merch. Runs physical stores in major districts like Hongdae and Gangnam
  • You can buy used webtoon-original volumes cheaply (check prices on-site)
  • Foreigner-friendliness: moderate. English signage is limited, but you can communicate via price tags and barcode scans

FAQ 5

Q1. I'm starting webtoons for the first time. Which app, which title?

Start with whichever of "Solo Leveling," "Tower of God," or "True Beauty" fits your genre interest, on the LINE Webtoon app. All three have complete English translations and are free to read.

Q2. Is there merch I can only buy in Korea?

Pop-up-exclusive merch is often domestic-only. But since the operating period is short, checking the pop-up schedule on official social media before your trip is essential. Bookstore art books and volumes can also be bought online (Yes24) and shipped.

Q3. Can I pay on Kakao Page with a foreign card?

In-app payments through Google Play or the Apple App Store accept foreign cards. Paying directly on the Kakao Page website sometimes requires a Korean card. Using the app-store payment route first is recommended (verification recommended).

Q4. Where can I check pop-up store info in real time?

Search the title name on official social media (Twitter/X, Instagram). webtoon.com → the Events tab. Korean fan communities (Reddit's r/webtoons, official Discord servers) also tend to be fast.

Q5. Where should webtoon fans stay?

Lodging near Hongdae or Seongsu offers good pop-up access. Gangnam and Myeongdong are convenient for combining with bookstores and shopping. Any lodging within a 10-minute walk of a subway station makes getting around easy.

5 Tips for Foreign Fans

1. Start with the LINE Webtoon EN app — the only official free route to start right away in English without a Korean bank account or phone number

2. Check pop-up schedules on social media, not on-site — checking after you arrive is too late. Follow the official Twitter and Instagram accounts

3. Kyobo Bookstore's Gwanghwamun comics corner — the most accessible bookstore for buying physical webtoon-original volumes and art books

4. Read the Solo Leveling English edition on LINE Webtoon EN — you can read the full series in English without installing the Kakao Page KR app

5. Gather the latest pop-up/event info from fandom communities (Reddit's r/webtoons) — local fans post in real time
