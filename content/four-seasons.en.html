⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Cherry blossoms = April only": Korean cherry blossoms = Late March (Jeju, Busan), Early April (Seoul, Jinhae), Mid-April (Gangwon) + Jung-ro Yoon (#303 Korea #1) + No. 1 on Instagram for foreigners.

2. "Summer festival = Han River chicken and beer only": Summer = Boryeong Mud Festival (July in Korea #1)·Busan Sea Festival·Hangang Fireworks (September) + No. 1 foreign enthusiasts (#06 combined).

3. "Autumn leaves = everywhere": Autumn leaves = Seoraksan, mid-October (Korea #1) → Naejangsan and Nami Island, late October → Seoul, early November (#229·#273 autumnal foliage matrix).

4. "Winter Snow = Gangwon Bay": Winter = Taebaek·Pyeongchang Snow Festival (January) + Hwacheon Trout Festival + Skiing + DDP Illumination (downtown) + No. 1 destination for foreign enthusiasts.

5. "Korea's 4 seasons = outdoor only": 4 seasons = #357 temple food·#359 tea·tea ceremony·#365 oriental medicine·#364 hotel spa (indoor) + integrated foreign enthusiasts.
