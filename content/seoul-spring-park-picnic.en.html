Seoul Park Walk Guide: Bukseoul Dream Forest, Gyeongui Line Forest Park, Naksan Park, Seoul Botanic Park and Lake Park, Western Seoul Lake Park, Seoul Grand Park and Forest Bathing Trail in Gwacheon

Subtitle: Bukseoul Dream Forest (always open) · Gyeongui Line Forest Park (about 6.3km, always open) · Naksan Park (always open) · Seoul Botanic Park (Themed Garden closed Mondays) · Western Seoul Lake Park (always open) · Seoul Grand Park (9:00 to 17:00 from November to February) · Seoul Grand Park Forest Bathing Trail (about 2 hours 30 minutes) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers parks in Seoul and neighboring Gwacheon that are good for walking and resting. They are Bukseoul Dream Forest (북서울꿈의숲) in the north, Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길) on an old rail line, Naksan Park (낙산공원) beside the Hanyang Doseong city wall, Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원) in Gangseo, Western Seoul Lake Park (서서울호수공원) in Yangcheon, and Seoul Grand Park (서울대공원) in Gwacheon. Only the Themed Garden (greenhouse) of Seoul Botanic Park closes on Mondays; the rest are registered as open year-round. The big parks in the west are in the #741 Western Seoul Park Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 North — Bukseoul Dream Forest

Bukseoul Dream Forest (북서울꿈의숲) [Wolgye-ro 173 (월계로 173), Gangbuk-gu (강북구), ☎ 02-2289-4000] is a park built on more than 680,000㎡ of the former Dreamland (드림랜드) site, and it opened on October 17, 2009. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the third largest park in Seoul after World Cup Park (월드컵공원) and Olympic Park (올림픽공원). It is surrounded by Byeogosan (벽오산) and Opaesan (오패산), and it has a cherry blossom path, a maple forest, the pond Woryeongji (월영지) with Wolgwang Waterfall (월광폭포), a 49.7m observation tower with views of Bukhansan (북한산), Dobongsan (도봉산) and Suraksan (수락산), and the Dream Forest Art Center (꿈의숲아트센터).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available (386 spaces).

📍 Downtown — Gyeongui Line Forest Park and Naksan Park

Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길) [Yeonnam-dong (연남동), Mapo-gu (마포구), ☎ Gyeongui Line Forest Park Community Center (경의선숲길공원 커뮤니티센터) 02-719-8830] was made in 2016 by turning the above-ground track of the Yongsan Line (용산선) section, which was moved underground, into a forest path. The Gyeongui Line (경의선) was the railway that linked Yongsan (용산) and Sinuiju (신의주) in the early 1900s. The path runs about 6.3km from Hyochang Park-front Station (효창공원앞역) to Gajwa Station (가좌역). The Mapo-gu section continues from Gongdeok-dong (공덕동) through Yeomni-dong (염리동) and Daeheung-dong (대흥동), past the Waugyo (와우교) section with the Gyeongui Line Book Street (경의선 책거리), to the Yeonnam-dong section known as "Yeontral Park (연트럴파크)".

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.

Naksan Park (낙산공원) [Naksan-gil 41 (낙산길 41), Jongno-gu (종로구), ☎ 02-743-7985~6] is a park linked to Daehangno (대학로) and Dongdaemun (동대문), and it opened as a park in 2002. Because the terrain resembles a camel's back it was called Naktasan (낙타산), and because the royal ranch that supplied milk to the palace was here it was also called Taraksan (타락산). The central plaza has the Naksan Exhibition Hall (낙산전시관), with materials on the origin of Naksan and the Hanyang Doseong (한양도성) city wall, and a central stage, and the pavilion Naksanjeong (낙산정) looks down over Seoul. On weekends there are free programs about Naksan and the Seoul City Wall (서울한양도성). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is considered a place with the most beautiful night view in Seoul. It is one of the places that appeared in the Netflix animation 'KPop Demon Hunters (케이팝 데몬 헌터스)'.

  • Walking routes: Ihwa-dong Mural Village (이화동 벽화마을), the Dongdaemun course toward Heunginjimun Gate (흥인지문) and the Hanyang Doseong Trail (한양도성길), and the course toward the Hansung University entrance (한성대 입구).
  • Opening hours: Always open. Naksan Exhibition Hall 9:00 to 17:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available (29 spaces).

📍 West — Seoul Botanic Park, Lake Park and Western Seoul Lake Park

Seoul Botanic Park (서울식물원) [Magok-dong-ro 161 (마곡동로 161), Gangseo-gu (강서구), ☎ 02-2104-9716] is a botanical garden and park that opened in Magok (마곡) in May 2019, with an area the size of 70 soccer fields. It is divided into four zones: Open Forest (열린숲), Lake Garden (호수원), Wetland Garden (습지원) and Themed Garden (주제원). The exhibition greenhouse in the Themed Garden holds native plants from 12 tropical and Mediterranean cities. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the exhibition greenhouse is the only greenhouse in the world shaped like a concave dish.

  • Opening hours: Themed Garden (greenhouse and themed gardens) 9:30 to 18:00 from March to October (last entry 17:00), 9:30 to 17:00 from November to February (last entry 16:00).
  • Closed days: Themed Garden every Monday. Open Forest, Lake Garden and Wetland Garden are open year-round. Other closure days are also registered separately.
  • Parking: Available. Stroller rental: Available.

Seoul Botanic Park Lake Park (서울식물원 호수공원) [same address, ☎ 02-2104-9716] is a "park within a park" connected to Seoul Botanic Park. Along the lake there are walking paths and waterside observation decks, plus a pedestrian bridge across the lake, a children's playground and a water play area.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Western Seoul Lake Park (서서울호수공원) [Nambusunhwan-ro 64-gil 20 (남부순환로64길 20), Yangcheon-gu (양천구), ☎ 02-2604-3004] opened in October 2009 on the site of the former Sinwol Water Treatment Plant (신월정수장), which began in 1959 as the Gimpo Water Treatment Plant (김포정수장), with the themes of "water" and "regeneration". Its area is 217,946㎡. It has an open meadow, the Table for 100 (100인의 식탁), a regeneration garden, the Mondrian Garden (몬드리안정원), a central lake, a sound fountain and a rooftop garden.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

📍 Gwacheon — Seoul Grand Park and Forest Bathing Trail

Seoul Grand Park (서울대공원) [Daegongwon-gwangjang-ro 102 (대공원광장로 102), Gwacheon-si (과천시), Gyeonggi, ☎ 02-500-7335] is a theme park that opened in 1984, with Seoul Zoo (서울동물원), a botanical garden of more than 1,600 species, a healing forest (치유의 숲) on the slopes of Cheonggyesan (청계산), a forest bathing trail and a campground. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the Seoul Grand Park botanical garden is the first public arboretum in Korea. Seoul Land (서울랜드) and the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art (국립현대미술관) are next to it.

  • Opening hours: 9:00 to 18:00 (last entry 17:00) from March to April and September to October, 9:00 to 19:00 (last entry 18:00) from May to August, 9:00 to 17:00 (last entry 16:00) from November to February. Exhibition greenhouse 10:00 to 17:00 (last entry 16:30).
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.
  • Activities: Forest healing programs, the Elephant Train (코끼리 열차), animal classes and more.

Seoul Grand Park Forest Bathing Trail (서울대공원 산림욕장) [Maggye-dong (막계동), Gwacheon-si (과천시), ☎ Seoul Grand Park Landscape Division (조경과) 02-500-7541] is a forest path entered from the entrance of the Australia House (호주관) at the zoo or from the North Gate (북문), and it passes 12 themed forests. It takes about 2 hours 30 minutes, and three side paths, at the South America House (남미관), the reservoir (저수지) and the Carnivore House (맹수사), can shorten it to around 1 hour. There are 6 deck shelters with picnic tables. The registered information gives the trail length as both 7km and 8km, and the two figures differ.

  • Opening hours: Same as Seoul Grand Park (9:00 to 17:00 from November to February, last entry 16:00). Entry may be blocked depending on the weather.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The Themed Garden (greenhouse) of Seoul Botanic Park is closed on Mondays — from November to February, last entry is at 16:00.
  2. Seoul Grand Park is in Gwacheon-si, Gyeonggi — from November to February it opens at 9:00 and last entry is at 16:00, with closing at 17:00.
  3. Naksan Exhibition Hall opens 9:00 to 17:00 — Naksan Park itself is always open.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Can I see plants indoors in winter?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, the Themed Garden greenhouse of Seoul Botanic Park is open 9:30 to 17:00 from November to February (closed Mondays), and the exhibition greenhouse of Seoul Grand Park is registered as open 10:00 to 17:00 with no seasonal difference, last entry 16:30.

Q. Which park lets you walk along an old rail line?

A. Gyeongui Line Forest Park (경의선숲길). It runs about 6.3km from Hyochang Park-front Station (효창공원앞역) to Gajwa Station (가좌역) and is always open.
