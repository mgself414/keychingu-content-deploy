Korea Jazz and Blues Festival Guide — Gwacheon Jazz Picnic, Gunsan Craft Beer & Blues Festival, Yuseong Jazz & Beer Festa, Suwon Jazz Festival, and Gapyeong Jara Island

Subtitle: Gwacheon Jazz Picnic (과천재즈피크닉, May 1~2, 2026, free, ended) · Gunsan Craft Beer & Blues Festival (군산 수제맥주&블루스 페스티벌, June 12~14, 2026, 6,000 won, ended) · Yuseong Jazz & Beer Festa (유성재즈&맥주페스타, Aug 28~30, 2026, free, ended) · Suwon Jazz Festival (수원재즈페스티벌, Sept 18~19, 2026, free, ended) · Jara Island, Gapyeong (가평 자라섬, home of the Jarasum International Jazz Festival) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers 4 festivals and 1 island that hosts a jazz festival where you can see jazz and blues performances in Korea, in the order they are held. Seoul jazz bars and clubs are covered in #470 Seoul Jazz Bar Guide.

The operating details are copied from 2026 values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). All 4 festivals have finished their 2026 runs.

📍 Spring — Gwacheon Jazz Picnic

Gwacheon Jazz Picnic (64 Gwanmun-ro, Gwacheon-si (과천시 관문로 64), Gwacheon Citizens' Square, ☎ 02-2009-9731, Gwacheon Foundation for the Arts and Culture (과천문화재단)) took place on May 1~2, 2026. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is a jazz festival anyone can attend for free, where visitors spread picnic mats on the lawn and enjoyed the performances like a picnic. 10 Korean and international jazz acts performed, focusing on vocal jazz, and the festival ran both pre-booked seating and a free-viewing area.

  • Hours: 18:30~21:40 (Saturday 14:20~21:40).
  • Fee: Free.
  • Side programs: Record and book sales booths, autograph sessions, a slow mailbox, a flea market, photo zones, food trucks, a percussion performance parade, children's activities and more. There was also a separate delivery pickup zone.

📍 Early Summer — Gunsan Craft Beer & Blues Festival

Gunsan Craft Beer & Blues Festival (224 Haemang-ro, Gunsan-si (군산시 해망로 224), Gunsan Modern History Museum (군산근대역사박물관) parking lot, ☎ 063-454-5252, Gunsan City (군산시)) took place on June 12~14, 2026. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is a festival that combines Gunsan Malt craft beer made from Gunsan barley with live performances by 16 Korean and international blues bands.

  • Hours: 12:00~22:00.
  • Fee: 6,000 won (5,000 won local gift voucher provided).
  • 2026 blues bands: Kang San-e Band (강산에밴드), Kim Jong-seo Band (김종서밴드), Kim Kyung-ho Band (김경호밴드), Sinchon Blues (신촌블루스), Hurricane Yukawa & Kazuhiko Ohnogi, Dave Spector, Sonny King and others.
  • Beer: 4 Gunsan breweries (Maincoon Brewing (메인쿤브루잉), Unryong Brewery (운룡양조), Craft Wolmyeong (크래프트월명), Pirate Brewing (해적브루잉)), Gunsan Malt local beer, and beers from sister cities in China, Japan, Taiwan and the US. 30 Gunsan restaurants took part with booths.

📍 End of Summer — Yuseong Jazz & Beer Festa

Yuseong Jazz & Beer Festa (27 Eoeun-ro, Yuseong-gu, Daejeon (대전 유성구 어은로 27), Yurim Park, ☎ 042-611-2080, Yuseong-gu, Daejeon Metropolitan City (대전광역시 유성구) and Yuseong Cultural Center (유성문화원)) took place on August 28~30, 2026. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is a festival held at Yurim Park every year at the end of August, where you enjoy live jazz performances together with beer from craft breweries across the country.

  • Hours: 17:00~23:00.
  • Fee: Free.
  • 2026 jazz performances: August 28: Yuseong Jazz Orchestra (유성재즈악단), Zip4, Kim Gi-cheol Band & Yang Ji (김기철밴드&양지), Woong San (웅산). August 29: Oldie but Goodie (올디 벗 구디), Maria Kim (마리아킴), Ko Sang-ji (고상지) with Latin Chicken Luna (라틴 치킨 루나), Youn Sun Nah (나윤선). August 30: Jasmujicus (자스무지쿠스), Kuilo Trio X Yun Deok-hyeon (쿠일오트리오X윤덕현), Malo Band (말로밴드), Han Sang-won Band (한상원밴드). There was a drone light show on the 28th and 29th.
  • Side programs: Craft beer tasting (12 companies from across the country), a city pop performance, a cocktail bar at Jazz Square, and the Jazz Beer Salon with a jazz expert.

📍 Autumn — Suwon Jazz Festival

Suwon Jazz Festival (165 Gwanggyohosu-ro, Yeongtong-gu, Suwon-si (수원시 영통구 광교호수로 165), Jaeminan Bat (재미난 밭) (Sports Climbing Center) at Gwanggyo Lake Park (광교호수공원), ☎ 070-4945-9580, Suwon City (수원시)) took place on September 18~19, 2026. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, Korean and international jazz artists performed traditional jazz, blues, funky jazz and more against the backdrop of Gwanggyo Lake Park.

  • Hours: 17:30~21:30.
  • Fee: Free.
  • 2026 lineup: September 18: Common Stella (커먼스텔라), Nan A-jin (난아진), g0nny (거니), Jung Dong-hwan (정동환) of MeloMance (멜로망스) x Funk Messengers. September 19: Moon Mi-hyang Quartet (문미향 퀄텟), Jo Jae-beom Latin Unit (조재범라틴유닛), Funky Tonight (펑키투나잇), Lee Eun-mi (이은미) x Min Kyung-in Trio (민경인 trio).
  • Food: Food vendors including food trucks were operated, and menu prices were registered as 1,000~15,000 won.

📍 The Island That Hosts a Jazz Festival — Jara Island, Gapyeong

Jara Island (60 Jarasum-ro, Gapyeong-eup, Gapyeong-gun (가평군 가평읍 자라섬로 60), ☎ 031-8078-8028) is an island formed in the Bukhan River (북한강) when Cheongpyeong Dam (청평댐) was built in 1943. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it consists of 4 islands, Dongdo, Seodo, Jungdo and Namdo, with an auto camping ground on Seodo and an eco-cultural park with a lawn plaza more than 100 m in diameter on Jungdo. It is also known as the home of the Jarasum International Jazz Festival.

  • Opening hours: Open at all times. Closed days: None, open every day of the year.
  • Parking: Available.
  • The Korea Tourism Organization data does not list the schedule of the Jarasum International Jazz Festival.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. All 2026 dates have ended — the dates and lineups in the table are 2026 values.
  2. Gunsan is the only paid festival — Gunsan Craft Beer & Blues Festival costs 6,000 won (5,000 won local gift voucher provided), while Gwacheon, Yuseong and Suwon are registered as free.
  3. Start times differ by festival — Gwacheon started at 18:30 (Saturday 14:20), Yuseong at 17:00, Suwon at 17:30 and Gunsan at 12:00.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which festivals have blues?

A. According to Korea Tourism Organization registered information, 16 Korean and international blues bands took part in the Gunsan Craft Beer & Blues Festival. The Suwon Jazz Festival description also lists blues as a performance genre.

Q. Which festivals can I enjoy with beer?

A. Gunsan Craft Beer & Blues Festival (Gunsan Malt craft beer) and Yuseong Jazz & Beer Festa (craft beer tasting from 12 companies nationwide) are the festivals that feature beer.
