Korean Jeon 6-Type Matrix — Nokdu-bindaetteok, Pajeon, Haemul-pajeon, Gamja-jeon, Kimchi-jeon, Donggeurang-ttaeng: The Full Set

ISSUE 03 · #882 · 2026-06-09

🎯 Why a Korean Jeon Matrix

The pain point foreign visitors have with Korean jeon (전) = "I only know pajeon, not the other jeon." Korean jeon = Gwangjang Market vlog #1 + rainy-day makgeolli (막걸리) pairing + authentic holiday food. Even under the same label "Korean savory pancake," nokdu-bindaetteok (녹두빈대떡) (Gwangjang vlog #1 · authentic mung bean) · pajeon (파전) (global #1 · authentic green onion) · haemul-pajeon (해물파전) (hoe cluster cross-link · seafood) · gamja-jeon (감자전) (Gangwon authentic · Gangwon makgeolli pairing) · kimchi-jeon (김치전) (hangover cure · MZ Instagram) · donggeurang-ttaeng (동그랑땡) (holiday · ancestral-rite authentic) each has different ingredients and fans. Foreigners don't know (a) the Gwangjang Market vlog bindaetteok (빈대떡), (b) the rainy-day makgeolli pairing, (c) the authentic holiday donggeurang-ttaeng, (d) that gamja-jeon = Gangwon authentic. This guide covers the 6-type matrix + Gwangjang vlogs + makgeolli pairings.

🥞 6-Type Deep Dive

🥒 Nokdu-bindaetteok — Gwangjang Vlog #1

  • Signature: Gwangjang Market vlog #1 · authentic mung bean · old-time traditional food
  • Ingredients: mung beans (ground) + pork + bean sprouts · fernbrake · kimchi
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (#1 Gwangjang vlog)
  • Recommended venues:
  • Gwangjang Market bindaetteok alley — vlog #1 authentic
  • Namdaemun bindaetteok — authentic
  • Season: year-round
  • Price: Gwangjang Market = ₩5,000–12,000 (~$4–9) each

🌿 Pajeon — Rainy Day #1

  • Signature: Global #1 · rainy-day makgeolli pairing #1
  • Ingredients: green onion + flour · egg
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★★ (global #1)
  • Recommended venues: hanjeongsik (한정식) restaurants · traditional pubs · MZ Korean restaurants · Gwangjang Market
  • Season: year-round · rainy day #1 (Korean culture)
  • Price: ₩8,000–20,000 (~$6–15) per plate

🦐 Haemul-pajeon — Busan Authentic

  • Signature: Busan authentic · seafood cross-link
  • Ingredients: green onion + squid · shrimp · clams · seafood + flour
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★ (seafood fans)
  • Recommended venues: Busan authentic · Jagalchi · MZ Korean restaurants
  • Price: ₩15,000–30,000 (~$11–23) per plate

🥔 Gamja-jeon — Gangwon Authentic

  • Signature: Gangwon authentic · thin · Gangwon makgeolli pairing
  • Ingredients: potato (ground · thin) + minimal flour
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★ (Gangwon authentic)
  • Recommended venues: Gangwon long-established restaurants · Pyeongchang · Jeongseon · MZ Korean restaurants
  • Price: ₩8,000–15,000 (~$6–11) per plate

🌶 Kimchi-jeon — Hangover Cure · MZ

  • Signature: hangover cure · MZ Instagram · good value
  • Ingredients: kimchi + flour · egg
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★★ (good value)
  • Recommended venues: home-style eateries · MZ Korean restaurants · chains
  • Price: ₩7,000–15,000 (~$5–11) per plate

🥩 Donggeurang-ttaeng — Holidays · Ancestral Rites

  • Signature: holiday · ancestral-rite authentic #1 · Seollal · Chuseok
  • Ingredients: minced meat (pork · beef) + tofu · carrot · onion + egg coating
  • Foreigner-friendliness: ★★★ (holiday authentic)
  • Recommended venues: hanjeongsik · home-style holiday cooking
  • Season: Lunar New Year (Seollal) · Chuseok ([#846](#846)) · holiday meals
  • Price: included free at hanjeongsik / markets = ₩10,000–20,000 (~$8–15) per pack

🍶 Rainy-Day Makgeolli Pairing (Korean Culture)

☔ The "Rainy Day = Pajeon + Makgeolli" Tradition

  • Korean culture: rainy day = pajeon · bindaetteok + makgeolli pairing
  • Why: the sound of rain + the sizzle of frying pajeon + makgeolli = classic Korean atmosphere
  • MZ trend: rainy-day pajeon posts are SNS · Instagram #1
  • Recommended venues: Gwangjang Market bindaetteok · traditional pubs · MZ Korean restaurants

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. "Jeon = only pajeon": Jeon = a 6-type matrix (nokdu · pa · haemul · gamja · kimchi · donggeurang-ttaeng).

2. "Rainy day = coincidence": Rainy day = pajeon + makgeolli, #1 Korean culture (authentic tradition).

3. "Nokdu = ordinary beans": Nokdu (mung bean) = the authentic bindaetteok base · different from regular beans.

4. "Gwangjang Market bindaetteok = ordinary": Gwangjang Market bindaetteok = vlog #1 authentic.

5. "Donggeurang-ttaeng = a regular meatball": Donggeurang-ttaeng = holiday · ancestral-rite authentic · the egg coating is traditional.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. #1 starter for foreigners: Pajeon + kimchi-jeon (global) → nokdu-bindaetteok (Gwangjang vlog).

2. Instagram · vlogs: Gwangjang nokdu-bindaetteok (#1) · rainy-day pajeon · Gangwon gamja-jeon.

3. MZ trend: Kimchi-jeon · MZ Korean restaurants · hot bar.

4. Makgeolli fans: Pajeon + makgeolli (#1 on rainy days).

5. Holiday fans: Donggeurang-ttaeng (Seollal · Chuseok authentic).

6. Allergies: Declare wheat gluten · seafood (haemul-pajeon) · pork · egg · mung bean in advance.

7. Payment · tipping: Markets = cash · cards / Korean restaurants = cards OK / no tipping.

8. VAT refund: Food and drink generally not refundable.

⚠ Medical · Safety · Dietary Disclaimer

Jeon = ordinary food · cooked in oil. Pregnant women · children = OK. Declare allergies in advance (wheat gluten · seafood · pork · egg · mung bean). If you suspect food poisoning, call ☎ 1339 (medical, English). This guide is general information, not medical advice.

Dietary restriction fallback:

  • Halal · kosher: Vegetable pajeon · gamja-jeon · kimchi-jeon = OK (some venues include pork · shellfish — confirm in advance).
  • Vegetarian · vegan: Pajeon · gamja-jeon · kimchi-jeon can be vegetarian (egg optional) / nokdu-bindaetteok · haemul-pajeon · donggeurang-ttaeng = non-vegetarian.
  • Gluten-free: Flour-based → gluten-free is difficult · gamja-jeon = least flour (confirm in advance).
