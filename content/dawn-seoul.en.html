⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Dawn = Dangerous": Dawn in Korea = the world's safest city + foreigners' top spot.

2. "Dawn Auction = Simple": Noryangjin (노량진) and Jagalchi (자갈치) dawn auctions = the #1 culture for foreign vlogs + traditional Korea.

3. "Sunrise = Only Outskirts": Eungbongsan (응봉산), Namsan (남산), Banpo (반포) = Seoul's top city center sunrise spots + foreigner favorites.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Foreigners' Top Dawn Priority: Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진수산시장) + Eungbongsan (응봉산) sunrise + dawn haejang (hangover cure).

2. Foreigners' Dawn Safety: Korea's #1 safest country + foreigners' top spot.

3. Foreigners' Dawn Food: Traditional seonji (ox blood), kongnamul (bean sprouts), gomtang (beef bone soup), kongnamul-gukbap (bean sprout soup with rice).

4. Foreigners' Cheotcha (First Train/Bus): Subway at 5:30 AM + Airport Railroad + foreigner favorite.
