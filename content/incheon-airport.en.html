5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. Confusing Terminal 1 and Terminal 2

Incheon Airport has 2 terminals — T1 and T2, connected by a 15-minute shuttle bus. Always check your ticket. Going to the wrong terminal costs an extra 30 minutes. T2 serves Korean Air, Delta, KLM, and Air France; T1 serves all others.

2. Exchanging All Your Money at the Airport

Airport exchange rates are 2–3% worse than Myeongdong (명동) or city centers. At the airport, exchange only ₩50,000 as pocket money (for taxi or transport card), then do your main exchange in the city. The street exchange booths in Myeongdong (명동) offer the best rates (see Guide #63).

3. Mixing Up AREX Express and Regular Trains

AREX Express = direct to Seoul Station (서울역) in 43 min for ₩11,000 (seat reservation required, 06:00–22:00)

AREX Regular = 11 stops, ~1 hour, ₩4,500 (24 hours, no reservation needed)

Same line, very different time and price. Go regular if luggage is light; choose express if time matters.

4. Doing SIM and Currency Exchange Separately

Inside the airport, get your SIM card + exchange ₩50,000 in one stop to save time. SK Telecom (에스케이텔레콤) and KT (케이티) booths are right next to the exchange counters near the exit.

5. Taking Tout or Meter-Off Taxis

Airport taxi touts are illegal. Official taxis pick up from designated queues only. For regular taxis, confirm the meter is running and ask for a receipt. The KakaoT (카카오T) app in English is the safest option.

★ Incheon Airport First 6 Hours — Complete Guide

Step 1: Land → Immigration (15–30 min)

  • K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization) — required before travel for visa-free nationals (90 countries): https://www.k-eta.go.kr
  • Arrival card filled out on the plane (English OK)
  • Foreigner lane = general foreigners / K-ETA holders = fast track
  • Weekday early mornings are fast; weekends can take 30 min–1 hour

Step 2: Baggage Claim (10–30 min)

  • Check the arrivals board for your flight
  • Lost luggage: report at your airline's counter (English assistance available)
  • Carts are free

Step 3: Customs (5 min)

  • Nothing to declare = Green Lane (most foreigners)
  • Duty-free limits: ₩800,000 in goods + 1L alcohol + 200 cigarettes
  • Over the limit = Red Lane

Step 4: Currency Exchange + SIM (15–20 min)

  • Exchange counters at multiple exit points (exchange ₩50,000 only — recommended)
  • SIM card booths (SK Telecom (에스케이텔레콤), KT (케이티), LG U+ (엘지유플러스)) — 1/5/10/30-day options, ₩5,000–₩50,000
  • e-SIM option available — easier if your phone supports it
  • Korean number from SIM → register for KakaoT (카카오T), delivery apps, etc.

Step 5: City Transfer (1–1.5 hrs)

→ See the 4 Options Comparison section below

① AREX Express — Best for Speed

Route: Incheon Airport (인천공항) T1 & T2 → Seoul Station (서울역) non-stop

Price: ₩11,000 (adult) / ₩4,500 (child)

Travel Time: 43 min (from T2) / 51 min (from T1)

Operating Hours: 06:00–22:00 (every 30 min)

Booking: Airport Railroad English app/website / walk-up ticketing OK

Key Points:

  • Assigned seating + luggage fits beside your seat
  • Full WiFi + power outlets
  • Immediate connections to subway and KTX at Seoul Station (서울역)
  • Best for time-sensitive travelers with 1–2 bags

One Line: "43 min non-stop = #1 choice when time is your priority"

② AREX Regular — Best Value

Route: Incheon Airport (인천공항) T1 & T2 → Seoul Station (서울역) (11 stops)

Price: ₩4,500

Travel Time: 1 hour (from T2) / 1 hr 7 min (from T1)

Operating Hours: 24 hours (every 5–10 min)

Booking: No reservation needed (card payment at machine)

Key Points:

  • Same seating as regular subway — slightly tricky with large luggage
  • Stops at Gimpo Airport (김포공항), Hongdae (홍대입구), Seoul Station (서울역) — easy subway transfers
  • Biggest advantage: 24-hour operation for late-night or early-morning arrivals
  • ₩4,500 budget fare = #1 pick for cost-conscious travelers

One Line: "₩4,500 + 24 hours = the answer for budget travelers and late-night arrivals"

③ Airport Bus (Limousine Bus) — Direct to Your Hotel

Key Routes:

  • 6001: T1 & T2 → Jamsil (잠실) / Gangnam (강남)
  • 6015: T1 & T2 → Myeongdong (명동) / Seoul Station (서울역)
  • 6021: T1 & T2 → Hongdae (홍대) / Sinchon (신촌)
  • 6101: T1 & T2 → Gangnam (강남) / Apgujeong (압구정)
  • 30+ additional routes covering Dongdaemun (동대문), Itaewon (이태원), Yeouido (여의도), and more

Price: ₩15,000–17,000

Travel Time: 1–1.5 hours (traffic dependent)

Operating Hours: 05:00–23:00 (every 15–30 min)

Booking: 1F ticket counters at airport + English apps (KOBUS, EZL)

Key Points:

  • Stops at or near your hotel — no transfer with heavy luggage
  • Separate luggage compartment = ideal for families with large suitcases
  • English announcements on board
  • Best for families and elderly travelers

One Line: "Direct to hotel + luggage hold = the answer for families and big suitcases"

④ KakaoT (카카오T) Taxi — Convenience + Groups

Price: ₩60,000–80,000 (T1/T2 → Gangnam (강남) / Hongdae (홍대) / Myeongdong (명동))

Travel Time: 1–1.5 hours

Operating Hours: 24 hours

Booking: KakaoT (카카오T) English app (foreign phone + foreign card accepted)

Key Points:

  • Group of 3–4: splits to ₩15,000–20,000 per person (comparable to bus)
  • Late-night arrival + heavy luggage = top choice
  • Door-to-door — straight to your hotel entrance
  • Some English-speaking drivers available (request via app)

One Line: "Groups + late night + door-to-door = KakaoT (카카오T) is the answer"

Incheon Airport Essentials

Duty-Free Shopping (Arrival & Departure)

  • Major duty-free stores in T1 & T2 (Shilla (신라), Lotte (롯데), Shinsegae (신세계))
  • Korean cosmetics, red ginseng (홍삼), and seaweed snacks (김) = most popular among foreign visitors
  • Pre-order at city duty-free stores up to 24 hours before departure for pickup
  • Pay with foreign card + passport — no tax refund needed (already tax-free)

Lounges

  • International lounges in T1 & T2 (Priority Pass, Lounge Key accepted)
  • Meals + showers + beer and wine
  • Great for transit passengers and cardholders

Food & Cafes

  • Food courts in T1 & T2: Korean, Chinese, Japanese, Western
  • Some 24-hour spots (convenience stores, select cafes)

Medical & Pharmacy

  • Medical centers + pharmacies in T1 & T2
  • English assistance available
  • Emergency: 119

Restrooms & Showers

  • Restrooms with English signage and wheelchair access throughout
  • Showers available at some lounges and VIP rooms

FAQ

Q1. Will English work?

Absolutely. Incheon Airport (인천국제공항) is Korea's most internationally equipped airport. All signage, services, and apps are in English.

Q2. What if I arrive late at night?

AREX Regular runs 24 hours — ₩4,500. Or use KakaoT (카카오T) Taxi anytime. Currency exchange and SIM booths also operate late at night.

Q3. What if I didn't get my K-ETA in time?

You can reapply upon arrival (results within 1 hour). However, if denied, entry may be refused — strongly recommended to apply before departure.

Q4. Which countries need a visa?

Korea grants visa-free entry to 90+ countries (USA, Japan, EU, Australia, etc. — K-ETA only). All others need a visa — apply at the Korean consulate in advance.

Q5. What if I exceed the duty-free limit?

Declare at customs → pay applicable duties. Limits: 1L alcohol, 200 cigarettes, goods up to ₩800,000.

Q6. Lost something?

T1 & T2 Lost & Found (1F) — English assistance available. For items lost on the plane, contact your airline's counter.

Seasonal Notes

Spring (March–May)

  • Surge in foreign visitors — immigration may take 30 min–1 hour
  • AREX and airport buses add extra services every 30 min

Summer (June–August)

  • Airport buses are air-conditioned for the heat (standard on all coaches)
  • Peak holiday season — expect early-morning arrivals and late-night departures

Autumn (September–November)

  • #1 season for foreign visitors to Korea
  • All transport options run at 1.5x normal capacity

Winter (December–February)

  • Watch for snow and ice — road conditions may delay taxis and airport buses
  • AREX runs on schedule regardless of weather (minimal weather impact)
