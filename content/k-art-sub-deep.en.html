12 K-Arts

1. Frieze Seoul (Korea #1 Art Fair)

  • Discrimination: First held in 2022·Every September·COEX·Asia #1 Art Fair·London·New York·LA·Seoul 4 cities
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN Art Mania #1·#536 K-Art cross

2. KIAF Seoul (Korean traditional art fair)

  • Discrimination: Started in 2002, Korean Gallery Association, held simultaneously with Frieze every September, domestic galleries
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN Discovering Korean artists

3. MMCA (National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art) - Seoul, Gwacheon, Deoksugung Palace, Cheongju

  • Discrimination: Korea #1 National Museum of Art·4 branches operated·100+ exhibitions per year·Lee Kun-hee Collection
  • Location: Seoul Museum (next to Gyeongbokgung Palace), Gwacheon Museum, Deoksugung Palace, Cheongju Museum
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN 1st place

4. Leeum Museum of Art (Leeum)

  • Discrimination: Samsung Collection·36 National Treasures·Part of Lee Kun-hee’s Collection·Mario Botta Design
  • Location: Hannam-dong, Itaewon Station No. 1
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN 1st priority·Free admission (permanent)·#667 K-Architecture cross

5. Korean global writer

  • Lee Bul: Venice Biennale 1999, New York MoMA, Guggenheim
  • Kim Whanki: Korean #1 abstract painter·Auction 22 billion won (highest price in Korea)
  • Park Seo-Bo: Dansaekhwa 1st generation, MoMA collection
  • Lee Ufan: Monoha + Dansaekhwa·Solo exhibition at the Palace of Versailles
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiasts

6. Dansaekhwa movement

  • Discrimination: Korean abstraction movement in the 1970s, Park Seo-bo, Jeong Sang-hwa, Ha Jong-hyun, Lee Ufan
  • Global: Global rediscovery in the 2010s·Auction prices skyrocket (10 times+)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ No. 1 EN·JP·CN mania

7. Nam June Paik (Father of Video Art)

  • Discrimination: Father of World Video Art (1932-2006)·New York activities·Guggenheim retrospective
  • Domestic: Nam June Paik Art Center (Yongin)·More Is Better MMCA Gwacheon
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ No. 1 EN·JP·CN mania

8. Insa-dong, Samcheong-dong, Hannam-dong (art gallery cluster)

  • Insa-dong: Authentic Korean painting, antiques, and ancient art
  • Samcheong-dong: Modern Gallery cluster·Gukje Gallery·Gallery Hyundai·Hakgojae
  • Hannam-dong: Luxury Gallery·Face·Thaddeus Ropac·Perrotin·#583 Hannam cross
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiasts

9. Gwangju Biennale

  • Discrimination: First held in 1995·Asia #1 Biennale·Every other year·September-November
  • 2024 Topic: Artificial Sun (Pansori)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Mania·#643 Gwangju sub

10. Busan Biennale

  • Discrimination: First held in 2002, every other year, September-November, Busan, International Art
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP Mania·#244 Busan sub

11. Korean NFT/digital art

  • Discrimination: K-Digital Art Global Rise·Seoul NFT Korea Festa
  • Writer: Mr. Woo Ji-su, UJ, NUC
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP MZ enthusiast

12. Korean art museum full set, 2 days and 1 night

  • Seoul day 1: MMCA Seoul + Leeum + Hannam Gallery
  • Metropolitan area day 2: Nam June Paik Art Center (Yongin) + National Hyundai Gwacheon
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ No. 1 EN·JP·CN mania

FAQ 6

Q1. Frieze Seoul admission fee? A. 1-day pass 50,000 won, 2-day pass 70,000 won, 4 days in September (advance reservation recommended).

Q2. MMCA Seoul admission fee? A. 4,000 won (permanent + special)·Free on the last Wednesday of every month.

Q3. Leeum admission fee? A. Permanent free · Separate special exhibitions (KRW 10,000~).

Q4. Auction by Korean artist #1? A. Kim Hwan-ki's Space (1971) - KRW 13.2 billion (2019 Christie's Hong Kong) · Highest price in Korea.

Q5. No. 1 recommended Dansaekhwa artist? A. Park Seo-bo (art)·Sang-hwa Jeong (white)·Lee U-hwan (line)·Ha Jong-hyeon (splicing) 4th class.

Q6. Gwangju vs. Busan Biennale? A. Gwangju = Asia #1 Authentic·Busan = Maritime + International. Gwangju ranks first in global reputation.
