K-Space (Nuriho, KARI, KASI) sub-deep 12 selections for foreigners

ID: 669

Category: D K-content·MZ

Sub-cluster: Category sub-deep #549·#561·#662 sub

Parent deep: #549 K-tech·#662 K-science

Pattern: 12-pick 4-axis matrix·Differentiated from #662 (#662 = science in general·#669 = space specific)

K-Space 12

1. Nuri (KSLV-II)

  • Discrimination: Korea's #1 self-developed launch vehicle·2022 2nd successful launch (world's #7 self-powered launcher)·2023 3rd success·Practical satellite
  • 2026: 4th launch (next-generation small satellite), 5th launch (review)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN Mania·#662 K-science cross

2. Naroho (KSLV-I) - History

  • Discrimination: Korea #1 launch vehicle·3rd successful launch in 2013·Russian cooperation (1st stage)·Korea self-power 70%
  • Naro Space Center: Goheung, Jeollanam-do
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

3. Danuri (Korea #1 Lunar Rover)

  • Discrimination: 2022 August SpaceX Falcon 9 launch·Korea #1 Lunar Orbiter·2023 Moon arrival·1-year mission
  • 2024: Successful high-resolution photography of the lunar surface · Exploration of lunar resources
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiasts

4. KARI (Korea Aerospace Research Institute)

  • Discrimination: Korea #1 Space Research Institute 1989·Daejeon Daedeok Research Complex·Nuriho·Danuri·Arirang
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Mania·Advance reservation for tour (group)

5. KASI (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute)

  • Discrimination: Korea #1 Astronomical Research Institute 1974·Daejeon·Bohyeonsan 1.8m Telescope World #11
  • Affiliation: KAGRA·EHT (black hole photography)·LIGO cooperation
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP astronomy enthusiasts

6. Bohyeonsan Observatory (Yeongcheon, Gyeongbuk)

  • Discrimination: Korea #1 Telescope 1.8m·1996 operated·Bohyeonsan Mountain 1124m·Elevation #1 Korea
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP Astronomy Mania·Sky No. 1

7. KARI Aerospace Administration (newly established in 2024)

  • Discrimination: Launched in May 2024·Gyeongnam Sacheon Headquarters·NASA·JAXA·ESA cooperation
  • Goal: 2026 Lunar Lander, 2030 Mars Exploration
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP enthusiast

8. Korean Satellite Series

  • Arirang Satellite: 1999 No. 1, 2024 No. 7 (resolution 30cm), ground observation
  • Cheollian: Geostationary meteorological satellite·2010·2018·2020
  • Defense satellite: 425 (SAR·EO)·5 reconnaissance satellites
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP enthusiast

9. Astronaut Lee So-yeon (Korea #1)

  • Discrimination: 2008 April, Korea #1 Astronaut·Soyuz TMA-12·11-day stay on ISS
  • Status: 2014 NASA PhD/Private (Controversial)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP·CN enthusiasts

10. Comparison of SpaceX vs. Nuri

  • SpaceX: Reuse·Starship·Mars·Civilian #1
  • Nuri: National magnetism, practical satellite, lunar exploration, government + KARI
  • Gap: Nuriho vs. Falcon 9 about 10 years (expert estimate)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP enthusiast

11. K-Space Industry/Private Enterprise

  • Hanwha Aerospace: Nuriho Systems Corporation·Korea #1
  • KAI (Korea Aerospace Industries): KF-21·Aircraft·Danuri
  • Innospace: Small launch vehicle (Hanbit)·Civilian #1
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP enthusiast

12. Korea Space Experience/Tour

  • Naro Space Center (Goheung, Jeollanam-do): Space Science Museum·Tour + Launch Vehicle Exhibition
  • KARI (Daejeon): Advance reservation for group tour
  • Gwacheon National Science Museum: Astronomical observatory and exhibition
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN family enthusiasts

FAQ 6

Q1. Next launch of Nuri? A. 4th 2026 (next-generation small satellite) planned/5th under review.

Q2. Danuri mission ends? A. First mission ends in December 2024·Extended operation in 2025 (approximately one additional year).

Q3. Aerospace Administration tour? A. General public tour

Q4. Next for Korean astronauts? A. Around 2030 (Planned)·Currently under review by the Aerospace Administration.

Q5. Korea #1 telescope? A. Bohyeonsan Mountain 1.8m·Yeongcheon, Gyeongsangbuk-do·operated in 1996·Altitude 1124m Korea #1.

  • Day 2: Naro Space Center (Goheung)·Space Science Museum → Return home

B. Daejeon Space Day 1

  • 09:00 Arrival at Daejeon KTX
  • 10:00 KARI exterior tour + National Science Museum
  • 14:00 KASI (Astronomy Research Institute) Exhibition
  • 17:00 Daejeon → Return home

C. 1 night at Astronomy Mania

  • Day 1: Bohyeonsan Observatory (Yeongcheon, Gyeongsangbuk-do) · Night observation
  • Day 2: Daegu + Gyeongju (#664 cross) → Return home

Season 5

  • Spring: Launch of Nuri in April-May (scheduled)·KARI field trip
  • Summer: Bohyeonsan star observation from June to August (excluding rainy season)
  • Autumn: September-October Autumn launch of Nuriho·Height of astronomy mania
  • Winter: December-February Bohyeon Mountain stars (cold air, clear)
