Gwangju ACC (National Asia Culture Center) — Asia's #1 Cultural Complex since 2014 for Foreigners

> Subtitle: ACC for foreign tourists since 2014 ~ 113,000m² + May 18th Old Provincial Office + Children's Culture Center + Full English Set

ISSUE 04 · #1027 · 2026-06-08

🎯 Why a Gwangju ACC (광주 ACC) Guide?

The ACC (Asia Culture Center·국립아시아문화전당) is Asia's #1 Cultural Complex since 2014, spanning 113,000m², and an Asian culture, art, and performance matrix. Foreign tourist pain points include: (a) the misconception that "ACC is just a regular museum," (b) its status as Asia's #1 cultural matrix, opened in 2014, (c) its integration with the May 18th Old Provincial Office (5·18 옛 도청) site, a democratization matrix, (d) the Children's Culture Center (어린이문화원) as a #1 family enthusiast spot, and (e) its foreigner-friendly matrix with a full English setup and card payment options.

This guide covers 5 pillars, 12 signatures + ACC + Children's Culture Center + May 18th Old Provincial Office.
