⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Gwangjang Market only = Korean market": Gwangjang is for foreigners #1, but Noryangjin Fisheries (vlog #1), Dongdaemun general (wholesale), Majang Livestock (meat), and Pyeonghwa Market (clothing wholesale) full set = Korean market full deep dive.

2. "Wholesale market = Foreigners cannot enter": Comprehensive of Pyeonghwa, Majang, Garak, and Dongdaemun All foreigner-friendly + Price negotiation + Wholesale price 50-70% (30-50% cheaper than retail). Early morning auction, instant dismantling, market restaurant OK.

3. "Tongin, Mangwon, Myeonmok = Don't know": Hidden gem that foreigners don't know about — Avoid crowds in the plaza + Authentic native market No. 1. Tongin (lunch box Tongin 5,000 won)·Mangwon (MZ)·Myeonmok (#184 native).

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. TOP 3 markets visited by foreigners: Plaza (#1) + Noryangjin Fisheries (vlog #1) + Dongdaemun Night Market (24 hours).

2. Markets available for early morning wholesale/auction: Noryangjin Fisheries (03~05), Pyeonghwa Market (22~16), Garak Agricultural and Fisheries (04~10), Majang Livestock (instant wholesale).

3. K-vlog · Mukbang regulars : Gwangjang Market (Bindaetteok, drug gimbap), Noryangjin Fisheries (live octopus, instant sashimi), Dongmyo Rescue, Tongin Lunchbox.

4. Price negotiation for foreigners OK: All markets (especially Pyeonghwa, Dongdaemun General, Majang Livestock, and Noryangjin Fisheries).

5. Instant tax exemption for foreigners: Dongdaemun Night Market (Doosan Tower, Migliore, apM) over 30,000 won + passport.

6. K-drama market stage: Gwangjang (“Goblin”, “Gambling”, etc.), Noryangjin Fisheries (“Itaewon Class”), Tongin (“Reply 1988”).
