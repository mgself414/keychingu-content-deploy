⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Only Gwangjang Market (광장시장) = Korean Market": While Gwangjang (광장) is foreigners' #1, a full set of Noryangjin Fish (노량진수산) (vlog #1), Dongdaemun General (동대문 종합) (wholesale), Majang Livestock (마장축산) (meat), and Pyeonghwa Market (평화시장) (clothing wholesale) offers a full deep dive into Korean markets.

2. "Wholesale Markets = Inaccessible to Foreigners": Pyeonghwa (평화), Majang (마장), Garak (가락), and Dongdaemun General (동대문 종합) are all foreigner-friendly + allow price negotiation + offer wholesale prices (50-70% of retail, 30-50% cheaper than retail). Early morning auctions, on-the-spot butchering/preparation, and market restaurants are all available.

3. "Unaware of Tongin (통인), Mangwon (망원), Myeonmok (면목)": These are hidden gems often unknown to foreigners — perfect for avoiding Gwangjang (광장) crowds + experiencing authentic local markets #1. Tongin (통인) (Dosirak Tongin (도시락 통인) for ₩5,000 (~$3.7 / ~27 RMB / ~550 JPY)), Mangwon (망원) (MZ), and Myeonmok (면목) ([#184](184_jungnang_meokgol_sister.md) local).

① Foreigners' Hot Spot #1 (3)

1. Gwangjang Market (광장시장) ★ Foreigners' #1

  • Address: 88, Changgyeonggung-ro (창경궁로), Jongno-gu (종로구) (Jongno 5-ga (종로5가) Station on Lines 1 & 5)
  • Foreigner Rating ★: ◎◎ — Korea's #1 market for foreigners
  • Signature: Established 1905·Bindaetteok (빈대떡) (Korean mung bean pancake)·Mayak Gimbap (마약김밥) (addictive mini gimbap)·Yukhoe (육회) (Korean beef tartare)·Nokdujeon (녹두전) (mung bean pancake)·Mandu (만두) (Korean dumplings) + a full set of Korean market foods
  • K-vlog: A favorite of BBC, Anthony Bourdain, and foreign vloggers
  • Hours: 09:00-23:00 (9 AM-11 PM) (food stalls), 24 hours (exterior)
  • Details: [#24](24_gwangjang_market.md)·[#58](58_gwangjang_bindaetteok.md)·[#50](50_gwangjang_vintage.md)

2. Noryangjin Fish Market (노량진수산시장) ★ K-vlog #1

  • Address: 674, Nodeul-ro (노들로), Dongjak-gu (동작구) ([#181](181_dongjak_noryangjin_sister.md))
  • Foreigner Rating ★: ◎◎ — Foreigners' #1 vlog·Instagram spot
  • Signature: Established 1971·Early morning auction from 03:00-05:00 (3-5 AM) + Freshly sliced raw fish (hoe) (회)·Maeuntang (매운탕) (spicy fish stew) (1F wholesale + 2F restaurants)
  • Details: [#181](181_dongjak_noryangjin_sister.md) Dongjak (동작)·Noryangjin (노량진) Sister

3. Dongdaemun General Market (동대문 종합시장)

  • Address: 240, Jong-ro (종로), Jung-gu (중구) ([#172](172_ddp_dongdaemun.md))

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Top 3 Markets for Foreign Visitors: Gwangjang (광장) (#1) + Noryangjin Fish (노량진수산) (vlog #1) + Dongdaemun General (동대문 종합) & Night Markets (동대문 야시장) (24-hour).

2. Markets for Early Morning Wholesale & Auctions: Noryangjin Fish (노량진수산) (03:00-05:00)·Pyeonghwa Market (평화시장) (22:00-16:00)·Garak Agricultural & Fisheries (가락농수산) (04:00-10:00)·Majang Livestock (마장축산) (on-the-spot wholesale).

3. K-vlog & Mukbang (먹방) (eating show) Favorites: Gwangjang Market (광장시장) (Bindaetteok (빈대떡)·Mayak Gimbap (마약김밥))·Noryangjin Fish (노량진수산) (Sannakji (산낙지) (live octopus)·freshly sliced raw fish (hoe) (회))·Dongmyo Flea (동묘 구제)·Tongin Dosirak (통인 도시락).

4. Price Negotiation OK for Foreigners: All markets (especially Pyeonghwa (평화)·Dongdaemun General (동대문 종합)·Majang Livestock (마장축산)·Noryangjin Fish (노량진수산)).

5. Instant Tax Refund for Foreigners: Dongdaemun Night Market (동대문 야시장) (Doosan Tower (두산타워)·Migliore (밀리오레)·apM) for purchases of ₩30,000 (~$22 / ~160 RMB / ~3,300 JPY) or more + passport.

6. K-Drama Market Stages: Gwangjang (광장) ("Goblin"·"The Gambling", etc.)·Noryangjin Fish (노량진수산) ("Itaewon Class")·Tongin (통인) ("Reply 1988").
