🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to go in Guri, Gyeonggi-do (경기도). They are a wholesale market, Donggureung (동구릉), where 9 Joseon royal tombs gather, Guri Tower (구리타워), which uses the chimney of the Guri resource recovery facility as an observation deck, a Hangang riverside park with flower fields, and a walking trail that runs from Achasan Mountain (아차산) to Wangsukcheon Stream (왕숙천). Donggureung is closed on Mondays, but if the Monday is a public holiday or substitute holiday it is open and closed on the next non-holiday day instead. Achasan Mountain is also in the #740 Northeast Seoul Forests and Parks Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Wholesale Market — Guri Agricultural and Fisheries Wholesale Market

Guri Agricultural and Fisheries Wholesale Market (구리 농수산물도매시장) [Domaesijang-ro 68 (도매시장로 68), Guri-si (구리시), ☎ 031-560-5100] is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, the second largest wholesale market in Korea after the Garak-dong Agricultural and Fisheries Wholesale Market (가락동 농수산물도매시장). Guri-si and the Seoul Metropolitan Government built it together in June 1997. The maximum annual handling volume is 836,000 tons, and it has a vegetable and fruit building, a fisheries building and a second fisheries building.

Trading is done wholesale at night and at dawn. 90% of the incoming volume is sold at auction, and the rest is moved to the stores of intermediate wholesalers and sold to wholesalers and retailers. It handles 162 kinds of fruit and vegetable items such as napa cabbage, radish, mushrooms, watermelon, apples and tomatoes, and 110 kinds of seafood items such as mackerel, hairtail, cockles, blue crab, pollock and oysters.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Available.

📍 Joseon Royal Tombs — Donggureung

Donggureung (동구릉) [Donggureung-ro 197 (동구릉로 197), Guri-si (구리시), ☎ 031-579-4900] means "9 tombs in the east." Over about 450 years, 7 kings and 10 queens were buried here, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization it is the largest group of royal tombs in Joseon. In 1408 the Geonwonneung (건원릉) of King Taejo (태조) was built first, followed in turn by the tombs of Munjong (문종), Seonjo (선조), Hyeonjong (현종), Yeongjo (영조), Heonjong (헌종) and others. Several forms are gathered here: danneung (단릉), where only a king or queen is enshrined, ssangneung (쌍릉), where they lie side by side, habjangneung (합장릉), where a king and queen are enshrined together, and samyeonneung (삼연릉), where a king and two queens lie side by side.

  • Opening hours: Nov~Jan 06:30~17:30 (last entry 16:30). Feb~May and Sep~Oct 06:00~18:00 (last entry 17:00), Jun~Aug 06:00~18:30 (last entry 17:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday. If the Monday is a public holiday or substitute holiday it is open, and closed on the next non-holiday day.
  • Fee: Individual 1,000 won, groups of 10 or more 800 won. Parking: Available.

📍 Observation Deck and Riverside — Guri Tower and Guri Hangang Citizens' Park

Guri Tower (구리타워) [Wangsukcheon-ro 49 (왕숙천로 49), Guri-si (구리시), ☎ 031-550-2880] is a tower that uses the chimney of Guri-si's resource recovery facility as an observation deck. From the 100m observation deck above ground, you look down on Guri and Seoul through 48-sided glass windows. The Sky Gallery (하늘갤러리) on the observation deck exhibits works by local artists.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~23:00. Closed days: Open every day of the year. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Guri Hangang Citizens' Park (구리한강시민공원) [Koseumoseu-gil 14beon-gil 249 (코스모스길14번길 249), Guri-si (구리시), ☎ Guri City Hall Parks and Green Spaces Division (구리시청 공원녹지과) 031-550-2472] is also called Cosmos Park (코스모스공원). It has a climbing hydrangea tunnel, rapeseed and cosmos fields, a Family Healing Campground (가족힐링 캠핑장), a nature study area and walking paths. It is described as hosting a rapeseed flower festival in May and a cosmos festival in autumn. The Family Healing Campground has shade, restrooms and drinking fountains, but camping and cooking are not allowed, and it is used on a first-come basis.

  • Opening hours: The park is always open. The Family Healing Campground is open 10:00~18:00 from January to May, 09:00~21:00 from June to September, and 10:00~18:00 from October to December.
  • Closed days: Open every day of the year. Fee: Free.
  • Parking: About 800 spaces. Free for the first 10 minutes, 1,000 won within 30 minutes, then 200 won for every 10 minutes, up to 10,000 won per day.

📍 Walking Trail — Guri Dulle-gil Courses 1~3

Guri Dulle-gil (구리 둘레길) is a trail connecting Achasan Mountain (아차산), the Hangang River (한강) and Wangsukcheon Stream (왕숙천), with 4 courses totaling about 32km. Courses 1~3 are registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data [☎ Guri-si Parks and Green Spaces Division (구리시 공원녹지과) 031-550-2909].

  • Course 1 (Path Where History and Nature Live, 역사와 자연이 살아있는 길): 7.9km from Achasan Mountain to Mangusan Mountain (망우산), about 4 hours. The starting point, Goguryeo Blacksmith Village (고구려대장간마을), is a filming location for the dramas 'Tale of the Four Guardians' (태왕사신기) and 'Queen Seondeok' (선덕여왕). It passes the Achasan Fortress Cluster (아차산 보루군, a historic site) and ends at Mangu Cemetery (망우 묘역), where the graves of Manhae Han Yong-un (만해 한용운) and Juksan Jo Bong-am (죽산 조봉암) are, and Mangu History Park (망우역사공원). It is described as having relatively few rough sections.
  • Course 2 (Path Connecting Guneungsan Mountain and Galmae Village, 구릉산과 갈매마을을 잇는 길): It starts at Ttalgi-won Entrance (딸기원 입구), which the Course 2 description gives as the end point of Course 1 (the Course 1 description says it ends at Mangu History Park, so the two values disagree), and follows Guneungsan Mountain (구릉산), which surrounds Donggureung, and Galmaecheon Stream (갈매천). 7.8km, about 3 hours 30 minutes.
  • Course 3 (Path Where Wangsukcheon Stream and Urban Living Parks Are in Harmony, 왕숙천과 도심생활공원이 조화로운 길): From Sano Interchange (사노 나들목) it passes Wangsuk Sports Park (왕숙체육공원), the Guri Agricultural and Fisheries Wholesale Market (구리 농수산물도매시장) and the Wangsukcheon Ecological Wetland (왕숙천 생태습지) and goes on to the Guri resource recovery facility (구리자원회수시설). 7.8km, about 2 hours 30 minutes. It is the same route as the Wangsukcheon bicycle path.

📍 Stars and Camping — Guri Children's Observatory and Guri Topyeong Family Camping Site

Guri Children's Observatory (구리어린이천문대) [Donggureung-ro 427, 3rd floor (동구릉로 427, 3층), Guri-si (구리시), ☎ 031-523-3245] is a children's observatory that opened in 2020. Astronomy classroom lessons are held on the 3rd floor, and telescope observation is held at the observation room on the 4th floor. It has 4 classrooms, a dome and a rooftop observation room, and is equipped with 11 telescopes.

  • Opening hours: 15:00~23:00. Closed days: Every Sunday and Monday, public holidays. Fee: Varies by program.

Guri Topyeong Family Camping Site (구리토평가족캠핑장) [Wangsukcheon-ro 11-140 (왕숙천로 11-140), Guri-si (구리시), ☎ 031-550-3788] is a campsite in Topyeong-dong (토평동), where the Hangang River and Wangsukcheon Stream meet. It has shared restrooms, shower rooms, a dishwashing area and electricity. It is registered as not allowing entry with pet dogs.

  • Opening hours: Check-in 14:00, check-out 11:00. Closed days: Open every day of the year.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Donggureung is closed on Mondays, and when the Monday overlaps a public holiday or substitute holiday it is closed on the next non-holiday day — in winter (Nov~Jan) it opens 06:30~17:30 and stops admitting visitors at 16:30.
  2. Wholesale market trading takes place at night and at dawn — opening hours are registered as varying by shop.
  3. Guri Children's Observatory is closed on Sundays, Mondays and public holidays — it is open 15:00~23:00.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. What is the Donggureung admission fee?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, it is 1,000 won for individuals and 800 won for groups of 10 or more.

Q. Where can I go at night?

A. Guri Tower is open until 23:00 and the Guri Children's Observatory is open 15:00~23:00. Guri Hangang Citizens' Park is always open.
