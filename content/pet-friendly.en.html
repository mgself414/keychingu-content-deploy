⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Pets = Foreigners can't bring them in":

Reality: Korea has a foreigner-friendly system for pet entry, including quarantine, vaccinations, and foreigner-friendly services.

2. "Dog cafes = Everywhere":

Reality: Korean dog cafes popular with foreigners are mainly in Gangnam (강남), Hongdae (홍대), and Itaewon (이태원), offering English-friendly and foreigner-friendly services.

3. "Pet hotels = Expensive":

Reality: Pet hotels typically cost ₩50,000-150,000 (~$37-110 / ~260-780 RMB / ~5,300-15,900 JPY) per night, with many foreigner-friendly and English-speaking options available.

4. "Vet clinics = Foreigners can't go":

Reality: Foreigner-friendly vet clinics are available in Gangnam (강남), Itaewon (이태원), and Hannam (한남), popular with foreigners and offering English services.

5. "Pet-friendly parks = None":

Reality: Seoul Forest (서울숲), Olympic Park (올림픽공원), Ttukseom (뚝섬), Banpo (반포), and Yeouido (여의도) are top pet walking spots, very popular and friendly for foreigners.
