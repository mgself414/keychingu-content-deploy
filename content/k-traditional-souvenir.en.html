7 Seoul Traditional Souvenir Shops — From Insadong Craft Shops to Museum Stores

Subtitle: Ssamziegil, the KCDF Craft Garden, Tongin Gage, and the Beautiful Tea Museum in Insadong, plus the Seoul Museum of Craft Art museum shop, the Osulloc Tea House Bukchon, and the K-Heritage Store at the National Palace Museum of Korea around Anguk, Bukchon, and Gyeongbokgung. Only 7 places that Kakao Map shows as open made the cut, and for the shops inside museums we also cross-checked the museum's official visiting information. They are split into 2 zones so you can link them on foot from Insadong to Gyeongbokgung.

ISSUE 06 · #774 · 2026-11-24 · Seoul Traditional Souvenirs

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 7

This list is for travelers who want to carry home things you can only buy in Korea. There are crafts like mother-of-pearl (najeon), hanji paper, ceramics, and lacquerware, stationery with dancheong and minhwa patterns, goods modeled on museum relics, and tea. Many things in craft shops carry the name of the artist who made them.

There are two selection rules. The Kakao Map (카카오맵) place info has to say "open for business," and there has to be a recent sign of visits. For the two shops inside museums, we also cross-checked the hours and closed days in the museum's official visiting information. The craft villages outside Seoul in the old manuscript, such as Jeonju, Icheon, and Gangjin, were dropped from this piece, and only shops inside Seoul remain.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours and closed days are the values registered on Kakao Map (checked 2026-11-24). For museums we also wrote the visiting information from the official website. The shops don't register prices on Kakao Map, so we copied the prices shown in the operating organizations' online shops and in blog reviews, with dates. Online shop prices and in-store prices can differ. Nearby stations and exits are from Kakao Map directions.

① Insadong — 4 Venues

All of them are reachable on foot along Insadong-gil (인사동길). Ssamziegil and the Craft Garden are closest to Exit 6 of Anguk Station, and Tongin Gage and the Beautiful Tea Museum to Exit 5 of Jongno 3-ga Station. The Craft Garden is closed on Monday.

  • Ssamziegil (쌈지길) — a building in the middle of Insadong-gil. A 2026-10 Kakao Map review wrote that there are no stairs; you climb along a spiral path past workshops and shops. The Kakao Map summary reads "a complex cultural space where a variety of accessory shops and workshops sit on every floor." A 2025-07 review said to be sure to go up to the rooftop. The "Ssamziegil Experience Workshop (쌈지길체험공방)" is registered separately on basement floor 1. Each shop sells different things at different prices, so there is no price on Kakao Map (2026-11-24). It is open daily 10:30–20:30 and closed on Seollal and Chuseok day. The address is Insadong-gil 44 (인사동길 44), Jongno-gu (종로구). The nearest station is Anguk Station (Line 3) Exit 6, a 4-minute walk. A 2026-11-07 blog review wrote that there were many foreign visitors.
  • Craft Garden (공예정원) (KCDF) — a craft shop run by the Korea Craft & Design Foundation (KCDF) (한국공예디자인문화진흥원). The homepage link on Kakao Map leads to the KCDF online shop (kcdfshop.kr). The foundation's official blog introduces the "excellent craft products" (우수공예품) picked here together with the artists' names. Online shop prices are a bandaji (반닫이) card wallet at 29,000 won, easy-grip cutlery at 30,000 won for 1 piece, a Chochungdo (초충도) parasol at 60,000 won, and a mother-of-pearl tumbler at 65,000 won. An artist's ceramic lidded bowl (hap, 합) is 700,000 won (2026-11-24). Displays and prices in the store can differ from online. Hours are Tue–Sun 10:00–19:00, and it is closed on Monday. The address is Insadong 11-gil 8 (인사동11길 8), 1st floor, Jongno-gu (종로구). The nearest station is Anguk Station (Line 3) Exit 6, a 5-minute walk.
  • Tongin Gage (통인가게) — a craft shop on Insadong-gil. Its Kakao Map category is ceramics. A 2026-02-19 blog review says floor 1 is a craft store and floor 2 is a cafe. Kakao Map hours are Mon–Sat 10:30–18:30 and Sun·public holidays 12:00–17:00. Another blog's closed-Monday and Tue–Sat 10:30–18:00 from the same month are the hours of the floor-2 cafe "Tongin Cafe Beom (통인 카페 범)," so we did not use them for the shop. No price is registered on Kakao Map (2026-11-24). The address is Insadong-gil 32 (인사동길 32), Jongno-gu (종로구). The nearest station is Jongno 3-ga Station (Lines 1·3·5) Exit 5, a 6-minute walk.
  • Beautiful Tea Museum (아름다운차박물관) — a hanok teahouse that displays tea and teacups. A 2026-10-23 blog review said it has been in this spot since 1998. The same review lists Keemun black tea, Assam, and lychee black tea at 8,000 won each. A 2026-10-30 review wrote that the moment you open the door, the displayed tea and teacups catch your eye. Hours are daily 11:30–20:00. A 2026-10 blog wrote that it closes on New Year's Day and Chuseok day. The address is Insadong-gil 19-11 (인사동길 19-11), Jongno-gu (종로구). The nearest station is Jongno 3-ga Station (Lines 1·3·5) Exit 5, a 6-minute walk. Dietary cues: black tea and green tea contain caffeine. A 2026-02 review mentions an order of sweet red bean porridge (danpatjuk, 단팥죽). Danpatjuk is a sweet dish made with red beans and sugar.

② Anguk·Bukchon·Gyeongbokgung — 3 Venues

From Anguk Station you can link the Seoul Museum of Craft Art and the Osulloc in Bukchon on foot, and then the National Palace Museum of Korea toward Gyeongbokgung Station. The Seoul Museum of Craft Art is closed on Monday, and the National Palace Museum of Korea on the last Monday of every month.

  • Seoul Museum of Craft Art Museum Shop (서울공예박물관 박물관가게) — the shop of a craft museum run by the city of Seoul. It is on floor 1 of the museum's information building. The museum's official visiting information (2026-11-24) says the exhibition halls open 10:00–18:00, the museum is closed every Monday (open if it is a public holiday), and permanent exhibition viewing is free. The Kakao Map registered hours are Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00. The culture goods shop is on the official information's 2026-09-15 to 11-30 night opening table. The museum shop runs extended night hours through 11-30 and closes at 18:00 from December. No price is registered on Kakao Map (2026-11-24). The address is Yulgok-ro 3-gil 4 (율곡로3길 4), Jongno-gu (종로구). The nearest station is Anguk Station (Line 3) Exit 1, a 3-minute walk.
  • Osulloc Tea House Bukchon (오설록 티하우스 북촌점) — the Bukchon teahouse of Osulloc, a Jeju green tea brand. It uses floors 1 to 3, and a 2026-11-10 blog review wrote that you can see the hanok roofs from floor 3. Floor 1 is the Osulloc store selling tea products, and the same review wrote that it bought a product exclusive to the Bukchon branch. The review lists a tea course at 39,000 won per person and says they booked 1–2 days ahead on CatchTable. Hours are Mon–Thu 10:00–19:00 and Fri–Sun 10:00–20:00. A 2026-08 Kakao Map review wrote that there were no seats. On weekends, be ready to wait. The address is Bukchon-ro 45 (북촌로 45), Jongno-gu (종로구). The nearest station is Anguk Station (Line 3) Exit 2, a 10-minute walk. Dietary cues: green tea and matcha drinks contain caffeine. For lattes and the like, ask when you order whether dairy goes in.
  • K-Heritage Store National Palace Museum of Korea (K-헤리티지 스토어 국립고궁박물관점) — a cultural heritage goods shop run by the Korea Heritage Agency (국가유산진흥원). The official website of the National Palace Museum of Korea (국립고궁박물관) announced this shop's renewal reopening on 2026-11-04. The same brand's online shop (K-Heritage) shows a minhwa notebook at 2,500 won, a palace ballpoint pen set at 5,600 won, a dancheong keycap keyring at 19,800 won, a Morodancheong (모로단청) parasol at 38,000 won, and a Hojakdo (호작도) wine stopper at 38,000 won (2026-11-24). In-store prices can differ. The shop hours on Kakao Map are Mon–Fri·Sun 09:30–17:30 and Saturday 09:30–21:30. The museum's official visiting hours are Mon–Fri·Sun 09:30–17:30 and Saturday 09:30–21:00. It is also open until 21:00 on the last Wednesday of every month. It is closed on the last Monday of every month (the next day if it is a public holiday), on January 1, and on Seollal and Chuseok. The address is Hyoja-ro 12 (효자로 12), 1st floor, Jongno-gu (종로구). The nearest station is Gyeongbokgung Station (Line 3) Exit 5, a 3-minute walk.

🗺 Route — One Full-Day Course

A. Insadong → Anguk → Gyeongbokgung (Tue–Sun, except the last Monday)

  1. Morning K-Heritage Store at the National Palace Museum of Korea (from 09:30). Pair it with a Gyeongbokgung visit
  2. After lunch Seoul Museum of Craft Art and its museum shop at Anguk Station (10:00–18:00, closed Mon)
  3. Afternoon Insadong Ssamziegil → Craft Garden (closed Mon) → Tongin Gage (until 18:30 Mon–Sat)
  4. Before evening A cup of tea at the Beautiful Tea Museum (until 20:00). If you'd rather stay in Bukchon, swap in the Osulloc Tea House

On Monday the Craft Garden and the Seoul Museum of Craft Art are closed. Plan the Insadong day for Tue–Sun.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Packing Insadong and Anguk into a Monday — the Craft Garden and the Seoul Museum of Craft Art are closed on Monday. The National Palace Museum of Korea is closed on the last Monday of every month.
  2. Taking online shop prices as in-store prices — the KCDF and K-Heritage prices in this piece are online shop prices. Displays and prices in the store can differ. Even for the same artist's piece, check the price again in the store.
  3. Heading to a museum shop in the evening — the shops at the Seoul Museum of Craft Art and the National Palace Museum of Korea close around 18:00 on weekdays. Move evening shopping to Ssamziegil (until 20:30).

⚙ FAQ 3

  1. If I only go to one place? — The place with the widest choice is Ssamziegil (daily 10:30–20:30). For artist-named crafts, Craft Garden (closed Mon). On a Gyeongbokgung day, K-Heritage Store National Palace Museum of Korea.
  2. Souvenirs under 10,000 won? — By K-Heritage online shop prices, there is a minhwa notebook at 2,500 won and a palace ballpoint pen set at 5,600 won (2026-11-24). Check in the store that the price is the same.
  3. If I want to make a craft myself? — An experience workshop is registered on basement floor 1 of Ssamziegil. The ceramics classes are in #1083, and the background on traditional crafts is in #1209.
