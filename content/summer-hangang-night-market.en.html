Why summer Han River night markets and food trucks

Seoul summer (mid-June to early September) = between monsoon and heatwave, evening Han River is the No. 1 night course in foreigner vlogs + food trucks·chimaek·live music·night views full set + Seoul Bamdokkaebi Night Market (Yeouido·Banpo etc.) operates Fri·Sat·Sun.
