⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Korea = Difficult for Vegans": Korea's vegan scene is a hub for traditional temple food and modern vegan cuisine.

2. "Temple Food = Only Vegetarian": Temple food excludes the ogye (오계 - five pungent vegetables: garlic, green onion, chives, wild garlic, and asafoetida), which appeals to foreign enthusiasts.

3. "No Vegan Stores": Hannam (한남) and Itaewon (이태원) are top hubs for foreign vegan enthusiasts.

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Top Picks for Foreign Vegans: Temple food + Plant, Loving Hut, Veg-able.

2. Foreigner-Friendly Temple Food: Jingwansa (진관사) (#182), Sanchon (산촌), Balwoo Gongyang (발우공양) are top spots.

3. Foreigner-Friendly Vegan Cafes: Hannam (한남) and Itaewon (이태원) are foreigner-friendly.

4. Foreign Vegan Overnight Stay: Templestay + full temple food experience.
