Namdaemun Market Guide: Bondong Clothing Arcade, Jungang Arcade and Jonghap Arcade, the Accessory, Eyewear and Stationery Arcades, Namdaemun Ginseng Market, Galchi-jorim Alley and Sungnyemun

Subtitle: Namdaemun Market (about 10,000 shops, varies by shop) · Bondong Clothing Arcade (about 10 minutes on foot from Hoehyeon Station Exit 5) · Jungang Arcade (folk crafts on the 2nd floor) · Jonghap Arcade (closed Sundays, varies by shop) · Accessory Arcade (about 2,000 specialty shops) · Eyewear Arcade (more than 150 shops) · Stationery Arcade · Namdaemun Ginseng Market (about 20 shops) · Galchi-jorim Alley · Sungnyemun (09:00~18:00, closed Mondays, free) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide covers Namdaemun Market (남대문시장) in Seoul, arcade by arcade. It includes the Bondong Clothing Arcade (본동의류상가) and Jungang Arcade (중앙상가) for clothes, the Jonghap Arcade (종합상가), which even sells camera parts, the Accessory, Eyewear and Stationery Arcades (액세서리상가, 안경상가, 문구상가) where specialty shops gather by item, the ginseng market, and Galchi-jorim Alley (갈치조림골목) for lunch, plus the city gate Sungnyemun (숭례문). The arcades and shops are registered as having opening hours and closed days that vary by shop. To see several markets at once, read the #604 Seoul Traditional Market Beginner's Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 The Whole Market — Namdaemun Market

Namdaemun Market (남대문시장) [Namdaemun-sijang 4-gil 21 (남대문시장4길 21), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-753-2805] is a wholesale and retail market where about 10,000 shops stretch around Sungnyemun (숭례문). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, an average of 300,000 people visit each day, and it sells more than 1,700 kinds of goods. It sells children's, men's and women's clothing, accessories, kitchenware, folk crafts, food, sundries, and farm and fishery products. It is described as drawing not only domestic retailers but also merchants from China, Japan, Southeast Asia, Europe, the United States and the Middle East. Food picks include oxtail soup shops (꼬리곰탕), Galchi Alley (갈치골목), Kalguksu Alley (칼국수골목), yachae-hotteok (야채호떡), dak-gomtang (닭곰탕) and North Korean-style naengmyeon shops (이북식 냉면집).

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Available.

📍 Clothes and Household Goods — Bondong Clothing Arcade, Jungang Arcade and Jonghap Arcade

Namdaemun Bondong Clothing Arcade (남대문 본동의류상가) [Namdaemun-sijang 2-gil 3-2 (남대문시장2길 3-2), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-753-2805] is a 2-story arcade building inside the market. It has clothing discount stores, women's and men's clothing, fashion accessory wholesalers and cafes. Around the building there are a galchi-jorim alley (갈치조림 골목), butcher shops, and food and sundries stores. It is about 10 minutes on foot from Exit 5 of Hoehyeon Station (회현역) on Subway Line 4.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Not available.

Namdaemun Jungang Arcade (남대문 중앙상가) [Namdaemun-sijang 4-gil 21 (남대문시장4길 21), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-753-2805] is a building with 1 basement floor and 4 floors above ground. The 1st floor has women's clothing and accessory shops, and the 2nd floor has folk crafts and traditional crafts, fabrics for hanbok (포목), needlework, bedding and traditional wedding goods. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the folk crafts on the 2nd floor are the most popular with foreigners.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Available. Credit cards: Accepted.

Namdaemun Jonghap Arcade (남대문 종합상가) [Namdaemun-sijang 4-gil 21 (남대문시장4길 21), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-753-2805] is an arcade from basement 1 to the 3rd floor. It is known as a place where you can find things that are hard to get elsewhere, and it sells camera parts and regional specialties from around the country.

  • Opening hours: Vary by shop. Closed days: Every Sunday (varies by shop).

📍 Arcades of Specialty Shops by Item — Accessory, Eyewear and Stationery Arcades

Namdaemun Accessory Arcade (남대문 액세서리상가) [Namdaemun-sijang 4-gil 21 (남대문시장4길 21), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-778-8932] is a place where about 2,000 accessory specialty shops stand in several arcades on the Toegye-ro (퇴계로) side and the Namdaemun-ro (남대문로) side. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the largest in the country. Many shops specialize in a single item, and they make and sell everything from hairpins to necklaces by hand.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop.

Namdaemun Eyewear Arcade (남대문 안경상가) [Namchang-dong (남창동), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-753-2805] has more than 150 wholesale and retail eyewear shops gathered together. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the eyewear distributed here is more than 50% of the national total. Some of the large shops make and sell their own brands, and others deal directly with subcontract factories, so you can reportedly buy about 30~50% cheaper than the market price. It sells eyeglasses, sunglasses and contact lenses.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by store. Parking: Available.

Namdaemun Stationery Arcade (남대문 문구상가) [Namdaemun-ro 6-2 (남대문로 6-2), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-753-2805] is a street where specialty shops for stationery, toys and packaging supplies gather, and it is called "Mungugol" (문구골). School supplies, fancy goods, office supplies and art supplies are displayed by building and floor, and more items can be bought one at a time than at other wholesale arcades.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by store. Parking: Available.

📍 Ginseng — Namdaemun Ginseng Market

Namdaemun Ginseng Market (남대문인삼시장) [Namdaemun-sijang-gil 25-8 (남대문시장길 25-8), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-752-1012] is a place inside the market where about 20 ginseng shops face each other across a central passage. They do wholesale and retail together, and by dealing directly with farmers at the growing areas and processing companies they are described as usually 20~30% cheaper than the market price. At the Namdaemun Ginseng Distribution Center (남대문인삼유통센터) on the 2nd floor of the Namdaemun Theater Building (남대문 극장 빌딩), they sell fresh ginseng and processed products such as ginseng tea, powder and jeonggwa (정과), as well as red ginseng products, honey and traditional teas. Ginseng prices rise and fall with that year's harvest.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Available.

📍 Lunch Alley and City Gate — Galchi-jorim Alley and Sungnyemun

Namdaemun Galchi-jorim Alley (남대문 갈치조림골목) [Namdaemun-sijang-gil 16-17 (남대문시장길 16-17), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ Namdaemun Market Tourist Information Center (남대문시장 관광안내소) 02-752-1913] came about around 1988. It was originally just a restaurant row, but word spread that cheap hairtail had been braised spicy and hot to suit merchants' tastes, and the shops around it changed their signs to galchi-jorim (갈치조림) one by one. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the first places to feature galchi-jorim were Huirak Restaurant (희락식당) and Wangseong Restaurant (왕성식당). The source of the fish differs a little by restaurant, but they use domestic hairtail from places like Yeosu (여수), Jeju (제주), Mokpo (목포) and Busan (부산), and the cooking also differs a little. It is described as advice that if the galchi-jorim is too spicy, you can go to a place that also sells grilled fish.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop.

Sungnyemun (숭례문) [Sejong-daero 40 (세종대로 40), Jung-gu (중구), ☎ 02-779-8547] is the main south gate of the Joseon (조선) capital wall, and it is also called Namdaemun (남대문). It was designated a National Treasure on December 20, 1962. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is the oldest wooden building left in Seoul and the largest of Seoul's four great gates. It was first built in 1396, the 5th year of King Taejo (태조) of Joseon. The signboard is written vertically. The 2-story gate tower burned in a fire on February 10, 2008, and after restoration it reopened on May 4, 2013.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00 (last entry 17:50). Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: Not available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Opening hours differ by arcade — no common business hours are registered, and the Jonghap Arcade is registered as closed every Sunday but varying by shop.
  2. Sungnyemun is closed on Mondays — it opens 09:00~18:00 and stops admitting visitors at 17:50.
  3. Parking is not available at the Bondong Clothing Arcade and Sungnyemun — Namdaemun Market, the Jungang Arcade, the Eyewear Arcade, the Stationery Arcade and the ginseng market are registered as having parking.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where do I buy traditional crafts?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, the 2nd floor of the Jungang Arcade has folk crafts and traditional crafts, fabrics for hanbok, bedding and traditional wedding goods.

Q. How much cheaper is the ginseng?

A. Namdaemun Ginseng Market deals directly with the growing areas and is described as usually 20~30% cheaper than the market price. Prices rise and fall with that year's harvest.
