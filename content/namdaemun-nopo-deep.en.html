Why it is now a long-established Namdaemun restaurant — a Korean food base camp for foreigners that started in 1414

Namdaemun Market is Korea's #1 oldest market with 610 years of history, starting in 1414 during the reign of King Taejong of the Joseon Dynasty (#380 cross-ref). It is not just a simple shopping hub, but food alleys such as Hairtail Alley, Kalguksu Alley, and King Dumpling Alley are formed as a microcosm inside the market, making it the number one choice for foreigners to introduce Korean food. Just a 2-minute walk from Exit 5 of Hoehyeon Station, you can immediately sample the full set of cutlassfish alley, king dumplings, Pyeongyang naengmyeon, tail gomtang, and ribs.

In particular, real long-established restaurants such as Jungang Galchi Restaurant in 56 years·Heerak Galchi in 50+ years·Buwon Myeonok in 65 (2020 Baeknyeon Store)·Chicken Jinmi Gangwon House in 60+·Gamegol Sonwang Mandu in 40+ years—the number one place for foreign enthusiasts and authentic Korean food hunters as the essence of Korean Korean food has been preserved as is. There are challenges to get used to Korean food, such as Cash payment-oriented and English menu restrictions**, but this is the quickest course to experience “Korea itself.”

This matrix categorized 7 seed stores + 5 verification expansions = 12 lines into 4 axes. Hairtail Alley, Kalguksu Alley, King Dumpling Alley, long-established stores outside the market — pool cover inside Namdaemun Market.
