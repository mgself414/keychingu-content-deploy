① Why Delivery Apps Are One of the Biggest Obstacles in Korea

Delivery is everyday infrastructure in Korea — a country where you can order in even for late-night snacks or after convenience stores close. But for foreign travelers, the real barrier isn't the app's screen language, it's the address system. A Korean address is made up of a road-name address plus a detailed building/unit number, and apartments and officetels have automatically locked shared entrances — without that detail, the delivery rider can't even get into the building. So this manual spends more than half its length not on comparing apps, but on "how to register your accommodation address correctly, the first time."

② Baemin vs. Coupang Eats — Which One Fits Your Trip

  • If you're only using it once, go with Baemin — the multilingual screens plus Apple Pay combination carries the lowest risk of card rejection.
  • If you already have a Coupang account (e.g., Rocket Delivery), go with Coupang Eats — no need to register a new card, just log in and you're done.

③ Signup Master Flow — Overseas Number + SMS Verification

Step What's on screen What to do
1 App Store / Play Store search Search for the app by its exact name and install
2 Country code selector on the signup screen Choose your home country code (+1, +44, +81, etc.) instead of Korea (+82), then enter your home number — no Korean SIM required
3 Verification code popup Enter the 6-digit SMS code you receive, exactly as sent
4 Terms of service agreement Check the box, then [ Done (완료) ]

If your roaming or eSIM plan is data-only and can't receive SMS, the verification text may never arrive. In that case, the fastest fix is to buy a Korean SIM right after landing (see #1230, the essential-apps edition) and retry.

④ Accommodation Address Master Flow — This Is the Real Obstacle

This step blocks more tourists than the app's screen language ever does. The sequence below never varies.

Step Screen What to do Trap
1 "Search delivery address (배달 주소 검색)" Search by road name or accommodation name For hotels, typing the English name as-is often works
2 Search results list Select your accommodation's road-name address If it doesn't come up, move the pin manually on the map screen
3 Detailed address popup Enter your building/unit number (apartment/officetel) or room number (hotel) Copying the address exactly from your booking confirmation or check-in instructions is the safest bet
4 Shared-entrance passcode field (if present) Note down the door-lock code you get at check-in beforehand and enter it Leaving this blank often means the rider just ends up calling from outside the building and giving up
5 Special request (directions memo) Save a one-line note like "in front of the 1st-floor lobby" or "door next to the elevator" Once saved, it's automatically reused on your next order

Tip: Ask for the shared-entrance passcode at check-in and save it in your phone's notes app right away. For Airbnb, it's often already written in the booking confirmation message.

Minimum order amount and delivery fee — always check before you pay

  • Minimum order amount: a floor each restaurant sets on its own. If you're under it, the pay button is grayed out and the shortfall is shown on screen — add more to your cart to unlock it.
  • Delivery fee: not like an American tip. It isn't cash you hand the rider on the spot — it's a delivery charge the app calculates automatically by distance and time of day and folds into your total. It's shown on screen before you pay, and you don't need to hand over any extra cash.

⑥ Pickup Options — "Leave at the Door" + Hotel vs. Airbnb

"Choose how you'd like to receive it (받는 방법을 선택하세요)"

[ Leave at the Door (문 앞에 놓아주세요) ]

[ I'll Receive It in Person (직접 받을게요) ]

[ Leave with Security (경비실에 맡겨주세요) ]

  • Leave at the Door (문 앞에 놓아주세요): Contactless pickup. The rider leaves it at the door without ringing the bell and sends a photo through the app — when the notification comes, you just go grab it. This is the most comfortable option for tourists worried about the language barrier.
  • I'll Receive It in Person (직접 받을게요): The rider rings the bell or calls, and you open the door to receive it. Even face-to-face, a simple "gamsahamnida (감사합니다)" — thank you — is enough.

Hotel vs. Airbnb pickup differences

  • Hotel: The custom at most hotels is to pick up at the lobby/front desk. Riders often can't go up to the guest-room floors, so it's most reliable to enter your address as "hotel front desk" or "lobby" and go down when the arrival notification comes. It also helps to tell the front desk at check-in to call you when food arrives.
  • Airbnb/officetel: The shared-entrance passcode from ④ is the key. Once you've registered it, delivery reaches all the way to your door, which pairs best with [ Leave at the Door (문 앞에 놓아주세요) ].

⑦ The Payment Fork — When Your Overseas Card Gets Blocked, That's the Real Story

Situation What to do
Trying to pay with an overseas credit card on Baemin Starting June 2, 2026, overseas card payment via Apple Pay became possible. If your iPhone has Apple Pay set up, this is the most reliable route
Not using Apple Pay (e.g., Android) Try registering your card directly in the app → if it's rejected, retry with a card from a different issuer → if that still doesn't work, switch from delivery to walking over and paying at the counter/kiosk instead (see #1270, the kiosk edition)
If you're a repeat traveler Pre-registering a prepaid card balance like WOWPASS as your app payment method lets you avoid the card-rejection risk (see #527, the payment deep-dive, for details)
Using Coupang Eats Just use the payment method already registered on your Coupang account — if your card cleared verification when you signed up for Coupang, you don't need to try again in the delivery app

⑧ 2 Panic Scenarios — What Actually Happens, and How to Cope

1. A call comes in from an unknown number — This is coming through the safe number (a virtual relay number), not the rider's actual number. It's fine if you can't understand it. A short "ne (네)" — yes — is enough, or you can just not answer at all; most of the time it still gets left at the door. If you'd rather avoid the call entirely, just write "please leave it at the door instead of calling" in the special request field.

2. You realize too late that you entered the wrong address — If it's right after ordering, you can often reach out directly through the cancel/inquiry button on the "Order status (주문내역)" screen. If the rider has already left, try answering the call from the safe number to correct it, and if that doesn't resolve it, asking the front desk or your host for help is the fastest fix.

⑨ Plan B — If the App Just Won't Work

  • Walk over and switch to counter ordering or takeout (see #1270, the kiosk edition)
  • Fall back on convenience store ready meals — no delivery fee or minimum order to worry about
  • If you're at a hotel, ask the front desk about room service first
  • Ask the front desk or your Airbnb host to order on your behalf — especially reliable when address registration just won't go through
