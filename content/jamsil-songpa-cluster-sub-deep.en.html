🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to visit in Songpa-gu (송파구), Seoul, centered on Baekje sites and markets. They are two Baekje earthen fortresses and two tomb clusters, an urban wetland and a neighborhood park, a book museum, and markets and shopping streets. The four sites and the museum are registered as free to enter. Lotte World (롯데월드), Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) and Olympic Park (올림픽공원) are in the #707 Jamsil Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Baekje Earthen Fortresses — Mongchontoseong and Pungnap-dong Earthen Fortress

Mongchontoseong (몽촌토성) [Olympic-ro 424 (올림픽로 424), Songpa-gu (송파구), ☎ 02-2147-2001] is a Baekje earthen fortress inside Olympic Park (올림픽공원). Its walls were built by packing clay on natural terrain beside a tributary of the Han River (한강), and traces of a wooden fence were found along the north wall. It has a structure of overlapping outer and inner fortresses, and it is a historic site made in the Hanseong period (한성시대) of the 3rd to 4th centuries. If you go toward Mongchontoseong from Olympic Park North Gate 2 (북2문), there is the Mongchon History Hall (몽촌역사관), which displays excavated artifacts.

  • Opening hours: On foot or by bicycle 05:00~22:00, by vehicle 06:00~22:00. Mongchon History Hall 09:00~18:00.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Free for the first 10 minutes, then 600 won every 10 minutes.

Pungnap-dong Earthen Fortress (풍납동 토성) [Pungnap-dong (풍납동), Songpa-gu (송파구), ☎ Songpa-gu Office Cultural Heritage Division (송파구청 문화유산과) 02-2147-2600] is a flatland fortress of earth built in the Baekje Hanseong period, shaped like a boat. The west wall facing the Han River has disappeared, so about 2.7km remains now, and the original circumference is estimated at 3.7km. The wall was first 10.8m high, and it was raised twice, reaching a maximum of 13.3m. During the great flood of 1925 a bronze chodu (청동초두) pot, belt ornaments and other items were found, and it has been a nationally designated historic site since 1963. Traces of an early Baekje road and the site of a large building were confirmed inside.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

📍 Baekje Tombs — Bangi-dong Tomb Cluster and Seokchon-dong Tomb Cluster

The Bangi-dong Tomb Cluster (방이동 고분군) [Ogeum-ro 219 (오금로 219), Songpa-gu (송파구), ☎ Songpa-gu Office Cultural Heritage Division (송파구청 문화유산과) 02-2147-2001] consists of tombs made on the ridge of a low hill, and after excavation in the 1970s it was restored and became a park. Eight tombs remain now, 4 on the high ground in the west and 4 on the low ground in the east. Silla pottery was found in Tomb No. 6 and it was once regarded as a Silla tomb, but as Baekje stone-chamber tombs with corridor entrances (굴식돌방무덤) were found one after another in places such as Umyeon-dong (우면동) in Seoul, Gwangam-dong (광암동) in Hanam (하남) and Pangyo (판교) in Seongnam (성남), the claim that these are Baekje tombs is described as gaining strength.

  • Opening hours: 08:00~18:00. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: 100 won every 5 minutes.

The Seokchon-dong Tomb Cluster (석촌동 고분군) [Garak-ro 7-gil 21 (가락로7길 21), Songpa-gu (송파구), ☎ Songpa-gu Office Cultural Heritage Division (송파구청 문화유산과) 02-2147-2608] is a group of tombs of kings and nobles of the Baekje Hanseong period. A survey during the Japanese colonial period reported more than 300 tombs around Seokchon-dong (석촌동) and Garak-dong (가락동), but now only a western part remains. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the largest, Tomb No. 3, is a three-tiered stone-mound tomb 50.8m wide and 4.5m high, and it is presumed to be the tomb of King Geunchogo (근초고왕) of Baekje. A survey after 2015 turned up Baekje pottery, gold ornaments and more.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

📍 Wetland, Park and Museum — Bangi-dong Ecological and Scenic Conservation Area, Ogeum Park and Songpa Book Museum

The Bangi-dong Ecological and Scenic Conservation Area (방이동 생태경관 보전지역) [Dongnam-ro 397 (동남로 397), Songpa-gu (송파구), ☎ Songpa-gu Office Parks and Greenery Division (송파구청 공원녹지과) 02-2147-3401] is an artificial wetland where pits formed in the early 1970s when soil for making bricks was dug out. Living things settled there once human access was restricted. You can see wetland plants such as water silvergrass (물억새) and lesser bulrush (애기부들), and birds such as the mallard (청둥오리) and the common kingfisher (물총새). Because it is a conservation area, collecting is prohibited and pets cannot be brought in.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~17:00. Closed days: Every Monday. Fee: Free.
  • Parking: Not available.

Ogeum Park (오금공원) [Ogeum-dong 51 (오금동 51), Songpa-gu (송파구), ☎ 02-400-0978] is a park made from a low hill near Ogeum Station (오금역) on Subway Line 5. It has a nature study area where about 30 kinds of wild flowering plants and 20 kinds of shrubs grow, 5 plazas, 9 walking courses, a children's playground and exercise facilities.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

The Songpa Book Museum (송파책박물관) [Songpa-daero 37-gil 77 (송파대로37길 77), Songpa-gu (송파구), ☎ 02-2147-2486] is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, the first public book museum in the country. On the 1st floor there are the stepped multipurpose space "Eoullim Hall" (어울림홀) and the children's hands-on exhibition room "Bookium" (북키움), and on the 2nd floor there are permanent and special exhibition rooms themed on books and reading, and a media library.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30). Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok days.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: Free for the first 30 minutes, then 150 won every 5 minutes.

📍 Markets and Shopping Streets — Garak Mall, Bangi Market, Pungnap Dokkaebi Market and Munjeong-dong Rodeo Street

Garak Mall (가락몰) [Yangjae-daero 932 (양재대로 932), Songpa-gu (송파구), ☎ 02-3435-1000] is a modern shopping center next to the Garak Agricultural and Fishery Wholesale Market (가락농수산물도매시장), with 7 buildings. In the sales building, basement 1 is fruit and vegetables, the 1st floor is seafood, dried seafood and meat, the 2nd floor is a general shopping arcade and kitchenware, and the 3rd floor is a specialty restaurant hall.

  • Opening hours: Vary by shop. Closed days: Open year-round (varies by shop). Parking: Available.

Bangi Market (방이시장) [Garak-ro 243 (가락로 243), Songpa-gu (송파구), ☎ 02-413-2100] is an alley-type market that has grown since the late 1980s. More than 140 shops sell clothes, vegetables, fish, fruit and sundries, and there is a lot of food such as bunsik (분식), tteok (떡) and banchan (반찬).

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Available.

Pungnap Dokkaebi Market (풍납도깨비시장) [Barameduri-gil 37-1 (바람드리길 37-1), Songpa-gu (송파구), ☎ 070-8792-4500] began as a "dokkaebi market" (도깨비시장) where peddlers with bundles appeared by day and vanished in the evening, and it is settling into a permanent market. It sells agricultural and fishery products, meat, clothes and food. Pungnap-dong Earthen Fortress (풍납동 토성) is nearby.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by shop. Parking: Available.

Munjeong-dong Rodeo Street (문정동 로데오거리) [Dongnam-ro 4-gil 10 (동남로4길 10), Songpa-gu (송파구), ☎ 02-400-1633] is a street where domestic and foreign brand discount stores gather, and for more than 30 years it has established itself as a road outlet. There are sports, casual, shoe, formal wear and golf wear stores.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by store. Parking: 300 won every 10 minutes.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The Bangi-dong Tomb Cluster is open only 08:00~18:00 — Pungnap-dong Earthen Fortress and the Seokchon-dong Tomb Cluster are always open.
  2. The Bangi-dong Ecological and Scenic Conservation Area and the Songpa Book Museum are closed on Mondays — the conservation area is open 09:00~17:00, and pets cannot be brought in.
  3. Opening hours differ by shop at the markets and Rodeo Street — no common business hours are registered.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Do the Baekje sites charge a fee?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, entry is free at Mongchontoseong, Pungnap-dong Earthen Fortress, the Bangi-dong Tomb Cluster and the Seokchon-dong Tomb Cluster.

Q. Which place is presumed to be the tomb of King Geunchogo?

A. Tomb No. 3 of the Seokchon-dong Tomb Cluster. It is a three-tiered stone-mound tomb 50.8m wide and 4.5m high.
