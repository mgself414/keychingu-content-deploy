🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places in Korea where you can see calligraphy (seoye / 서예) works and try making a brush or doing calligraphy. It covers 2 places in Seoul and Gwacheon (과천), 5 places in Jeonju (전주), Inje (인제), Cheonan (천안), Yesan (예산) and Chuncheon (춘천), and 1 place in Jeju (제주). 6 of the 8 are registered as free admission.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Seoul and Gwacheon — Seoul Calligraphy Art Museum and Chusa Museum

The Seoul Calligraphy Art Museum (서울서예박물관) (2406 Nambu Sunhwan-ro (남부순환로), Seocho-gu, at the Seoul Arts Center (예술의전당), ☎ 0507-1382-1656) is a calligraphy exhibition hall that opened in 1988 together with the Seoul Arts Center concert hall (음악당), and it became a specialized calligraphy museum in March 2002. It holds modern and contemporary calligraphy-history materials such as Geunyeok Seohwasa (근역서화사), and more than 1,300 contemporary East Asian calligraphy works. It has continued to hold calligraphy and calligraphy-and-painting exhibitions, including the Korean Calligraphy History Special Exhibition (한국서예사특별전), which began in 1988.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~19:00. Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Fee: Varies with each exhibition.

The Chusa Museum (추사박물관) (78 Chusa-ro (추사로), Gwacheon-si, ☎ 02-2150-3650) is a museum that researches, exhibits and offers experiences of the life, scholarship and art of Chusa Kim Jeong-hui (추사 김정희), a scholar of the late Joseon period, and it opened on June 3, 2013. The permanent exhibition has 3 rooms: Chusa's life on the 2nd floor, Chusa's scholarship and art on the 1st floor, and the Fujitsuka Donation Room (후지쓰카 기증실) on the 1st basement floor. The 1st basement floor also has a special exhibition room, a permanent experience room, a children's library and a museum shop, and outdoors there are a restored dok-umul (독우물), Gwajichodang (과지초당) and Chusa Madang (추사마당). A special exhibition is held 1~2 times a year.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00 (last admission 17:00). Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok holidays, and closure days set by the mayor of Gwacheon (과천시장).
  • Fee: Individual adults 2,000 won, middle and high school students and soldiers 1,000 won, elementary school students 500 won. Groups of 20 or more: adults 1,000 won, middle and high school students and soldiers 500 won, elementary school students 300 won. Free for ages 6 and under and ages 65 and over.

📍 Regional Calligraphy Museums — Jeonju Gangam, Inje Yeocho, Cheonan Chwimukheon, Yesan Korea Calligraphy Stone Monument Museum

The Gangam Calligraphy Museum (강암서예관) (74 Jeonjucheon-dong-ro (전주천동로), Wansan-gu (완산구), Jeonju-si, ☎ 063-285-7442) is a specialized calligraphy museum opened by the calligrapher Gangam Song Seong-yong (송성용) with the wish to give back to society the collection and property he gathered over his lifetime. It holds more than 1,300 items, including calligraphy and paintings, books and the Four Treasures of the Study (문방사우), and it is in the Jeonju Hanok Village (전주 한옥마을). The building has 1 basement floor and 2 floors above ground.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00. Closed days: Every Sunday, on the day of Lunar New Year and Chuseok.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

The Yeocho Calligraphy Museum (여초서예관) (154 Manhae-ro (만해로), Buk-myeon (북면), Inje-gun, ☎ 033-461-4081) is a specialized calligraphy museum in Yongdae-ri (용대리), Buk-myeon, Inje-gun, honoring the calligrapher Yeocho Kim Eung-hyeon (김응현). According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is the largest-scale specialized calligraphy museum in Korea. It holds more than 6,000 items, including Kim Eung-hyeon's works and personal belongings, calligraphy materials from Korea and abroad, and books. The architect Lee Seong-kwan (이성관) designed it to draw in the surrounding pine forest, lifted the exhibition facilities one floor with piloti, and made a pond using the stream that originally flowed there.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00 (last admission 17:30). Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, on the day of Lunar New Year and Chuseok.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

The Chwimukheon Calligraphy Museum (취묵헌서예관) (3 Jonghap-hyuyangji 1-gil (종합휴양지1길), Mokcheon-eup (목천읍), Dongnam-gu (동남구), Cheonan-si, ☎ Cultural Policy Team (문화정책팀) 041-521-5167) is a calligraphy museum opened by renovating the management office building of the Cheonan Integrated Resort Tourism Complex (천안종합휴양관광단지). It exhibits the calligraphy, archive and videos of Chwimukheon In Yeong-seon (인영선), who was born in Asan (아산), South Chungcheong, and worked on his art for about 50 years.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00 (last admission 17:30). Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, on the day of Lunar New Year and Chuseok. It is temporarily closed while exhibitions are being prepared.
  • Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

The Korea Calligraphy Stone Monument Museum (한국서예비림박물관) (7 Nokmunbaksalmi-gil (녹문박살미길), Sinyang-myeon (신양면), Yesan-gun, ☎ 0507-1414-8610) is a place that exhibits more than 700 works in which the calligraphy and the Four Gentlemen paintings (사군자) of modern and contemporary calligraphers and painters are carved into stone in intaglio and relief. You can see works in seal script, clerical script, regular script, semi-cursive script, cursive script and Hangul (한글) calligraphy.

  • Opening hours: 09:30~17:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: Free.

📍 Chuncheon and Jeju — Brush Story Museum and Jeju Chusa Memorial Hall

The Brush Story Museum (붓이야기박물관) (906 Baksa-ro (박사로), Seo-myeon (서면), Chuncheon-si, ☎ 033-255-0580) is a brush museum founded in 2017 to make traditional brushes known. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is the only brush-specialized museum in Korea. It exhibits works by director Park Kyung-soo (박경수), the holder of the Chuncheon brush maker (춘천필장) title, a Gangwon Special Self-Governing Province Intangible Heritage, and by several artists. On the 1st floor you can see the making process, in which a master's hands touch a single brush more than 100 times, and on the 2nd floor you can try making a calligraphy piece and making your own brush. In the brush workshop you can watch a master make brushes.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00 (last admission 17:30). Closed days: Every Monday, on the day of Lunar New Year and Chuseok.
  • Fee: Free. Visit time: About 1 hour. Parking: Free.

The Jeju Chusa Memorial Hall (제주추사관) (44 Chusa-ro, Daejeong-eup (대정읍), Seogwipo-si, ☎ 064-710-6865) is an exhibition hall built in December 2010 to honor the life, scholarship and art of Chusa Kim Jeong-hui. It replaced the Chusa Relics Exhibition Hall (추사유물전시관) built in 1984, and was rebuilt when Chusa's place of exile (추사유배지) became a nationally designated heritage site in October 2007. It has a Chusa Memorial Hall (추사기념홀), 3 exhibition rooms and an education room, and it exhibits 'the Complete Relics of the Chusa Head Family of Yesan (예산김정희종가유물일괄)', Chusa's signboard calligraphy and Chusa's letter calligraphy.

  • Opening hours: 09:00~18:00 (last admission 17:30). Closed days: Every Monday (the first non-holiday day after if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, on the day of Lunar New Year and Chuseok.
  • Fee: Free.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Most close on Monday — Gangam Calligraphy Museum closes on Sunday, and the Korea Calligraphy Stone Monument Museum is open year-round. The other 6 close every Monday.
  2. The place with a registered fee is the Chusa Museum — adults 2,000 won, and the Seoul Calligraphy Art Museum varies with each exhibition. The other 6 are free.
  3. Two places cover Chusa Kim Jeong-hui — the Chusa Museum in Gwacheon and the Jeju Chusa Memorial Hall in Seogwipo.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where can I make a brush or try calligraphy?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, on the 2nd floor of the Brush Story Museum (붓이야기박물관) in Chuncheon (춘천) you can try making a calligraphy piece and making your own brush (free admission, closed on Monday). The Chusa Museum (추사박물관) in Gwacheon has a permanent experience room.

Q. Which calligraphy museums are in Seoul and Gwacheon?

A. They are the Seoul Calligraphy Art Museum (서울서예박물관) in the Seoul Arts Center (예술의전당) in Seocho-gu (10:00~19:00, closed on Monday) and the Chusa Museum (추사박물관) in Gwacheon (09:00~18:00, closed on Monday).
