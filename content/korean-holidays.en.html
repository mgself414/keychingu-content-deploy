Complete Guide to Korean Traditional Holidays — Seollal, Chuseok, Sambok, Dongji

Subtitle: Korea's 4 Major Traditional Holidays + Top Spots for Foreign Enthusiasts

ISSUE 03 · #279 · 2026-05-20

🎯 Why a Complete Guide to Korean Traditional Holidays?

The #1 traditional Korean cultural experience for foreigners = 4 Major Holidays (Seollal, Chuseok, Sambok, Dongji). Deep dive into traditional Korean food, rituals, and top spots for foreign enthusiasts.

🍂 2026 Chuseok Practical Guide — Sep 24 (Thu)–27 (Sun), If You're in Seoul

This year's Chuseok (추석, Korean Thanksgiving) falls on September 25 (Fri), with the holiday running 9/24 (Thu)–26 (Sat) plus Sunday for a real 4-day break. If you're traveling in Seoul during this window:

  • What closes: Markets and small-business restaurants (mostly around the holiday days themselves), some department stores (on the day itself). Old-school and local eateries — always reconfirm hours right before the holiday.
  • What stays open: Palaces and royal tombs traditionally offer free admission during the holiday (check official notices) — crowds are thinner too, making it the best window for a palace visit. Large chains, convenience stores, and hotel dining run as usual.
  • Dining open through the holiday (based on past years' pattern — confirm for the current year): Samwongarden (삼원가든, a large Gangnam galbi restaurant) · Byeokjegalbi Bangi (벽제갈비 방이점) · Tteurak (뜨락) · Sukseongyukgwan (숙성육관) — premium hanwoo (한우, Korean beef) and galbi (갈비, grilled short ribs) restaurants that stay open for holiday family dining demand.
  • Getting around: Highways and trains get jammed on the first and last days of the holiday — save inter-city trips for outside those dates. Seoul sightseeing itself is actually quieter than usual.
  • Heads up: The 2026 Seoul International Fireworks Festival (서울세계불꽃축제) was moved up to Sept 5 to avoid Chuseok — don't expect a "fireworks festival during Chuseok week," it won't happen.

2. "Sambok (삼복) = Just Food": Sambok (초복, 중복, 말복 - Three Dog Days of Summer) = traditional Korean nourishing culture + #1 spot for foreign enthusiasts.

3. "Dongji (동지) = Missed It": Dongji (12/22 - Winter Solstice) = Patjuk (팥죽 - red bean porridge) + Temples (Jingwansa, #207) + foreign enthusiasts.
