Gangjin Market Guide — Ogamtong Market's 4th and 9th Day Markets, Maryang Nolto Seafood Market, Goryeo Celadon Museum, Gawudo, Dasan Chodang

Subtitle: Gangjin Ogamtong Market (강진오감통시장), market days on dates ending in 4 and 9 each month · Maryang Nolto Seafood Market (마량 놀토수산시장), every Saturday, April–October · Goryeo Celadon Museum (고려청자박물관), 09:00–18:00, closed Mondays · Gawudo (가우도), always open · Dasan Chodang (다산초당), 09:00–18:00 — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide covers two markets in Gangjin. One is Gangjin Ogamtong Market, the permanent market in Gangjin-eup (강진읍) that also holds a market on the 4th and 9th days, and the other is Maryang Nolto Seafood Market at Maryang Port (마량항), which opens only on Saturdays from April to October. We have added the Goryeo Celadon Museum, for seeing Goryeo celadon, Gawudo, an island you walk into, and Dasan Chodang, where Jeong Yak-yong (정약용) stayed.

This guide covers only the two markets and those three places, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). For the rest of a Gangjin trip, follow the Related Content below.

🛒 Gangjin Ogamtong Market — Permanent Market and 4th and 9th Day Market

Gangjin Ogamtong Market (17-14 Sijang-gil, Gangjin-eup, Gangjin-gun (강진군 강진읍 시장길 17-14), ☎ 061-430-5513) is the traditional market of Gangjin-eup. It has 300-plus permanent shops, and a 5-day market is also held on days ending in 4 and 9 each month.

  • Market days: The 4th, 9th, 14th, 19th, 24th, and 29th.
  • Opening hours and closed days: They differ by shop.
  • What they sell: Seafood, farm produce, and sundry goods.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available.
  • Gangjin Ogamtong (2 Ogam-gil, Gangjin-eup (강진읍 오감길 2), ☎ Music Creation Center 061-433-3636): A complex cultural space. It has a music creation center, a food market, and a Korean table d'hote experience hall, and it is open 09:00–24:00, year-round.

🐟 Maryang Nolto Seafood Market — The Saturday Harbor Market

Maryang Nolto Seafood Market (164 Mihang-ro, Maryang-myeon, Gangjin-gun (강진군 마량면 미항로 164), ☎ Gangjin County Marine and Forestry Division 061-430-3263) is a seafood market held at Maryang Port only on Saturdays, from April to October (4–10).

  • Open days: Every Saturday, April–October. It is not open from Sunday to Friday.
  • What they sell: Seasonal seafood, raw fish and shellfish, dried seafood, and Gangjin farm and livestock specialties.
  • Things to enjoy: A Saturday concert is held every Saturday during the same period. Parking is available.
  • Maryang Mihang (마량미항) (☎ 061-430-3267): Always open. There is a breakwater walkway, and at the harbor entrance an island called "Kkamakseom" (까막섬) because its forest looks black. A gizzard shad festival is held in autumn.

🏺 Goryeo Celadon Museum — Seeing and Making Celadon

Gangjin Goryeo Celadon Museum (33 Cheongjachon-gil, Daegu-myeon, Gangjin-gun (강진군 대구면 청자촌길 33), ☎ 061-430-3755) is a museum that exhibits Goryeo celadon and runs an experience where you shape celadon from clay.

  • Opening hours: Museum 09:00–18:00 (last entry 17:30). Digital museum 10:00–17:00 (preparation time 12:00–13:00, last entry 11:30 and 16:30).
  • Closed days: Every Monday (if Monday is a public holiday, it opens and closes on the next first weekday). The celadon-making experience hall and the celadon shop are open year-round.
  • Admission: Adults 2,000 won, teenagers 1,500 won, children 1,000 won. Parking is free.
  • Celadon-making experience hall: 09:00–18:00, with 12:00–13:00 as preparation time. Last entry is 11:00 and 17:00.
  • Celadon shop: 09:00–17:30, with 12:00–13:00 as preparation time.

🌉 Gawudo — The Island You Walk Into Over a Bridge

Gawudo (Singi-ri, Doam-myeon, Gangjin-gun (강진군 도암면 신기리), ☎ Culture and Tourism Division 061-430-3349) is the only inhabited island among the 8 islands of Gangjin Bay. You walk in over two suspension bridges that carry no cars (Cheongja Bridge and Dasan Bridge).

  • Opening hours: Always open, open year-round.
  • Walking: The ecological trail around the island, "Hamkkehae-gil" (함께해 길), is 2.5km and takes 1 hour to 1 hour 30 minutes on foot.
  • Monorail (☎ 061-433-8856): Runs every 15 minutes to Celadon Tower (청자타워) at the island's summit. October–February 09:00–16:30, Mar and Sep (3, 9) 09:00–17:00, April–August 09:00–18:00, with 12:00–13:00 as lunchtime. Tickets are round-trip.
  • Note: You must carry your trash off the island, and you cannot bring food in.

📜 Dasan Chodang — Where Jeong Yak-yong Stayed

Dasan Chodang (68-35 Dasanchodang-gil, Doam-myeon, Gangjin-gun (강진군 도암면 다산초당길 68-35), ☎ 061-430-3911) is where Jeong Yak-yong, a scholar of the Practical Learning school in the late Joseon period, stayed for over 10 years from 1808 during his exile in Gangjin, teaching disciples and writing books. Mokminsimseo and Gyeongseyupyo were completed during this period.

  • Opening hours: 09:00–18:00, open year-round. Parking is available.
  • What to see: The restored Dongam and Seoam, the "Jeongseok" (정석, 丁石) said to have been carved by Jeong Yak-yong himself, Yakcheon (약천), where tea was boiled, and Cheonilgak (천일각).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Thinking Maryang Nolto Seafood Market is open every day — It opens only on Saturdays, April–October.

2. Going to the Goryeo Celadon Museum on a Monday — The museum is closed every Monday. The celadon-making experience hall and the shop are open.

3. Bringing a packed lunch to Gawudo — Bringing in food is prohibited, and you must carry your trash off the island.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. When are Gangjin's market days?

A. Gangjin Ogamtong Market holds its market on days ending in 4 and 9 each month (the 4th, 9th, 14th, 19th, 24th, and 29th).

Q. Can I use a card?

A. Whether cards are accepted is not registered in the tourism organization's data for the markets in this guide.
