⚠ Top 5 mistakes foreigners make when adjusting to jet lag

1. "Day 1 sleeping in a hotel bed" — Jet lag failure

On the first day of arrival as a foreigner, I couldn't sleep early in the morning due to the time difference. If you lie down in the hotel, your condition will be worse the next day. CAFE 00:00 Night activities at the same 24-hour cafe·Jjimjilbang·Yangjang → Adapt to jet lag the next day due to natural fatigue.

2. "I only go near the hotel" — safety excuse

Foreigners only stay in hotels due to safety concerns during nighttime activities. In reality, Korea's nighttime safety is also very high (especially Gangnam, Hongdae, Itaewon, and Myeongdong). Guided night stores (#137 Yajang·#05 24-hour restaurant) are foreigner friendly + safe.

3. I don’t know where the 24-hour cafe/restaurant is

There is a lack of information when foreigners look for cafes and restaurants in the early morning. #203 CAFE 00:00 (Gangnam, 24 hours) · #201 Hongdae Cafe Phase 2 (Mangwon hidden) · #05 24-hour restaurant · #137 Top 10 Night Markets, etc. Take advantage of key friend deep dive.

4. Unknown etiquette for first visit to a sauna

My first jjimjilbang as a foreigner was stressful because I didn't know etiquette (bathing, clothes, items). #07 Jjimjilbang Guide + #34 Jjimjilbang Etiquette Foreigners Only Prior study required.

5. Dawn night view/sunrise spot not considered

Due to jet lag, foreigners wake up at 5-7 am. Rather than a hotel bed, use Banpo Han River dawn sunrise (#147 night running) · Namsan sunrise (#13) · Free night view (#02) to adapt to jet lag + Instagram hot cuts.
