Middle East Travelers' Korea Guide: Visa-Free Days by Country (UAE, Qatar, Kuwait 90 Days; Saudi Arabia, Oman, Bahrain 30 Days), 4 Halal Categories, Prayer Rooms, Direct Flights (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: Permitted stays from the official K-ETA eligible-country list: UAE, Qatar, Kuwait and Türkiye 90 days; Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain 30 days · Egypt and Jordan ordinary passports are not on the list · temporary K-ETA exemption through 2026-12-31 (K-ETA notice) · Seoul Central Mosque and Incheon Airport prayer rooms · Emirates, Etihad and Qatar Airways serve Incheon (Incheon Airport). 🎯 How to Use This Guide

Climb the slope of Usadan-ro (우사단로) in Itaewon (이태원), a little out of breath, and a white dome and minaret rise in front of you: Seoul Central Mosque (서울중앙성원). This guide lists the things that trip up travelers coming to Korea from the Gulf and the Middle East before departure, using only values from official pages: how many days visa-free your passport gets, whether you must apply for K-ETA, how to choose a halal restaurant, where to pray, and which airlines fly there. Even within the Middle East, entry conditions differ by country, so each country is written out separately. This edition is for Muslim travelers visiting Korea for the first time with family.

The old guide's "150,000 per year from the Middle East", "average spend $1,700+", "80% independent travel", "30+ KMF halal restaurants" and unfounded ranking expressions were removed because no official source could be found. Egypt, which was in the old title, has no ordinary-passport entry on the official K-ETA eligible-country list, so the old structure that grouped it with visa-free countries was wrong. Medical tourism has its own visa and treatment conditions, so this edition does not cover it.

🛂 Entry Documents: K-ETA and the e-Arrival Card

  • Visa-free countries (UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Bahrain, Türkiye): the rule is K-ETA approval before departure. The application fee is ₩10,000 (~$7.50) (K-ETA official site)
  • Temporary K-ETA exemption: it runs from January 1 to December 31, 2026 (K-ETA official notice). The notice does not list the eligible countries, so we could not confirm whether each Gulf country is included. Scan your passport on the k-eta.go.kr application screen and it will tell you whether you are "exempt"
  • Age exemption: people aged 17 or under or 65 or over on the day of entry, holders of diplomatic or official passports, and visa holders are exempt from applying for K-ETA (K-ETA official site). Check separately for children and grandparents in your family
  • Egypt and Jordan: not on the K-ETA official list. In the Ministry of Foreign Affairs mission table, Egypt appears only for diplomatic/official passports (90 days) and Jordan only for diplomatic passports (90 days). We could not confirm the visa type or documents for ordinary passport holders; check with the Korean embassy in Cairo or Amman
  • e-Arrival Card: if you did not actually obtain a K-ETA, submit the e-Arrival Card. It is free at e-arrivalcard.go.kr from 3 days before arrival, and a paper form is also accepted (Ministry of Justice, #1289 e-Arrival Card manual)

  • VisitSeoul introduces Eid (이드) in Itaewon (Usadan-ro 10-gil 15 (우사단로10길 15), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00 to 21:00) as one of the few restaurants the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF) recognizes as halal. Certification can change, so check before you visit.

🕌 Where to Pray

  • Seoul Central Mosque (서울중앙성원): Usadan-ro 10-gil 39 (우사단로10길 39), Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seoul, 477 m from Itaewon Station (이태원역) Line 6 exit 3. Opened on May 21, 1976, and open daily. Prayer times shift with sunrise, so check the monthly timetable on the mosque's website. Sleeveless tops, short skirts and shorts are not allowed, and you can borrow a skirt or hijab from the changing room next to the guard post. Inquiries +82-2-793-6908 (VisitSeoul, revised 2026-09-17). For the surrounding area see #144 Itaewon and Hannam guide
  • Incheon Airport prayer rooms: the airport's official site lists 2 rooms in Terminal 1 (3rd floor near Gate 24, and the 4th-floor west transit lounge), open 24 hours, inquiries 1577-2600. The Incheon city tourism page says Terminal 2 also has prayer rooms in the general area on the 1st basement floor and in the duty-free area on the 4th floor (Incheon Tour, revised 2026-06-22)
  • Incheon city: the Incheon city tourism page introduces the mosque at Majang-ro 5 (마장로 5), Bupyeong-gu (부평구) (daily 06:00 to 21:00, +82-32-512-2612) and Incheon hotels equipped with prayer mats and Qurans (Incheon Tour)
  • The Korea Tourism Organization has distributed prayer mats and qibla compasses to hotels with prayer rooms (VisitKorea); ask the hotel when you book

✈ Flights

  • Incheon Airport's official list of operating airlines (regular flights of the previous month) includes Emirates (EK) and Etihad Airways (EY) (United Arab Emirates), Qatar Airways (QR), and Turkish Airlines (TK) (Incheon Airport official site). Departure cities and days are not in the list; search by date on the airline's website
  • We could not find airlines from Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman or Bahrain in that list. From those countries, look first at connecting flights on the airlines above

👥 With Family: Documents Differ by Person

Even within one family, children and grandparents may fall under the K-ETA age exemption, and family members holding diplomatic or official passports are exempt separately (K-ETA official site). If your family has mixed nationalities, recheck each person's passport against the table above. When the family visits the mosque together, apply the dress code (no sleeveless tops or shorts) to the children too. For routes with children, see #210 Seoul guide for families.

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "All Gulf countries get 90 days": Saudi Arabia, Oman and Bahrain get 30 days (K-ETA official list, Ministry of Foreign Affairs mission table)

2. "Egypt is visa-free too": Egyptian ordinary passports are not on the K-ETA eligible-country list. Check visa conditions with the Korean embassy locally

3. "My country is exempt, so I submit nothing": if you did not obtain a K-ETA, you must still submit the e-Arrival Card (Ministry of Justice)

4. "A 5-star hotel buffet is halal": Muslim Friendly hotel buffets may also serve pork dishes (Korea Tourism Organization)
