Seoul Sledding Hills Guide — From 6,000-Won Hangang Slopes to Free Theme-Park Runs, the Fees, Dates and Subway Exits for Families Seeing Snow for the First Time

Subtitle: The three Hangang sledding hills at Ttukseom, Jamwon and Yeouido charge 6,000 won for all ages with a tube sled included, and the slopes close every day from 1 to 2 PM for grooming (Seoul city official) · The SLP Children's Hall sledding hill in Children's Grand Park costs 15,000 won · At Seoul Land and Everland the sled runs are free once you buy park admission (secondary sources) · District-run hills in Nowon, Seocho and elsewhere cost 2,000 to 8,000 won — all dates and fees are from the 2025-26 season notices, so check the December announcement for 2026-27

ISSUE 03 · #1416 · 2026-10-19

🎯 The Question This Guide Answers

"My kids have never seen snow — can they go sledding in Seoul?", "We don't want to go all the way to a ski resort; can we finish in half a day?" — family travelers from Southeast Asia, Taiwan and Hong Kong ask this every winter. The answer is "yes, and it is cheap." The city of Seoul builds artificial-snow slopes in three Hangang (한강) riverside parks, the district offices set up temporary hills on a university field and a swimming-pool site, and the theme parks outside the city let admission holders sled for free. This guide puts the fees, dates and subway exits of 9 places in one table and fills in the two rules that guides for foreigners always skip: the grooming hour and the proof you need for free entry.

⚠ Dates and fees are from the 2025-26 season (December 2025 to February 2026) notices. Sledding hills announce each year's plan in early to mid December, and the advertised opening day sometimes slips. In 2025-26 the Hangang hills were announced for December 19 and actually opened on December 31. Before you leave, open the Seoul winter guide page in the SOURCES below once. USD figures use ₩1,000 ≈ $0.75 (October 2026 rate).

① The Three Hangang Sledding Hills — the City-Run Standard

The rules are the same at all three. Below is the Seoul city notice (2025-26) as published.

  • Dates and hours: 2025-12-31 to 2026-02-18, 50 days with no closing day · daily 10:00 to 17:00 (the Seoul city guide page also lists a 09:00 start for Yeouido, so the sources conflict — if you want to go early, check on site)
  • Grooming hour: every day from 13:00 to 14:00 the slopes are closed for snow grooming. Time your lunch for this hour and you lose nothing
  • Fee: ₩6,000 (~$4.50) for every age. A tube sled, the snow playground (nunnori-dongsan, 눈놀이동산) and the rest areas are included, and there is no separate sled rental fee
  • Free entry and discounts: children under 36 months enter free with a document proving their date of birth (for safety they may not ride the sleds) · visitors 65 and over, people with disabilities, persons of national merit and holders of the Seoul Multi-Child Card (다둥이카드) get 50% off with proof. The proof the Seoul city notice names is a document such as a resident registration certificate or a health insurance card, and a screenshot photo is not accepted — the notice does not say whether the original passport of a foreign child is accepted, so check by phone or on site first, and if it is not, plan on paying ₩6,000 (~$4.50)
  • Slopes: large slope 80 m (age 6 to adults) · small slope 30 m (36 months to age 6) · a snow playground for toddlers
  • Safety: all three sites run a first-aid station with an AED

In 2025-26 the three hills were themed around Pororo (뽀로로): "Pororo World Snow Festival" at Ttukseom, "Loopy (루피) and the Snowflake Party" at Yeouido, and "Pororo Forest Village Under Snow" at Jamwon. At Yeouido there was a character parade at 11:00 and sing-along shows at 12:30 and 14:30, and the tented food court sold ramyeon (라면), tteokbokki (떡볶이), cup rice (컵밥), hot dogs and sandwiches. Luggage storage was ₩5,000 (~$3.75) with a Pororo bag provided, and paid rides such as pond-smelt fishing (bingeo-nakksi, 빙어낚시) and a mini Viking ride were in the ₩4,000 to 7,000 (~$3 to 5.25) range (secondary).

How to get to each hill (measured by Seoul Media Hub)

  • Ttukseom (뚝섬): Line 7 Jayang (Ttukseom Hangang Park) Station Exit 2 → 5-minute walk. This station was renamed from Ttukseom Yuwonji Station (뚝섬유원지역) in February 2024 and is the same station — it is the same place as "Ttukseom Yuwonji Station Exit 2" on the Seoul guide page, so you can search for either name in a map app. Inquiries 02-452-5955
  • Jamwon (잠원): Line 3 Jamwon Station Exit 4 → 20-minute walk. The farthest of the three. With a stroller or small children, Ttukseom or Yeouido is easier. Night opening until 20:00 ran daily from Dec 31 to early January, then on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays — the night schedule dates differ between sources, so confirm before you go. Inquiries 02-536-8263
  • Yeouido (여의도): Line 9 National Assembly Station Exit 1 → 15-minute walk. The hill is on the Yeouido Hangang Park swimming-pool site, next to Parking Lot 3. Inquiries 02-785-0478

Tickets: buy at the on-site ticket booth. We could not confirm whether cards are accepted — bring some cash.

② SLP Children's Hall Sledding Hill, Children's Grand Park — Sledding Plus Pond-Smelt Fishing in One Set

  • Fees: sledding hill admission ₩15,000 (~$11.25) · pond-smelt fishing (bingeo-japi, 빙어잡이) ticket ₩6,000 (~$4.50), net and bucket provided
  • Dates: 2025-12-21 to 2026-02-23 (Seoul city SLP guide page), subject to weather. The main Seoul winter guide page lists Dec 20 to Mar 2, so the two official pages give different periods. Hours are not in the notice either, so call to confirm (1800-5309)
  • Slopes: two, beginner and advanced. Children under 36 months may not use the advanced slope, and children from 36 months to under age 7 use the beginner slope only
  • Getting there: Line 7 Children's Grand Park Station Exit 2
  • It costs 2.5 times the Hangang hills, but pond-smelt fishing is attached, and since Children's Grand Park itself is free to enter, the zoo and playgrounds fill out a half day

③ Two Theme Parks — Buy Admission and the Sledding Is Free

  • Seoul Land Snow Fun Land, Gwacheon: a 120 m general slope plus a 50 m slope for small children, about 8,500 ㎡ in all. The sledding hill and snow playground are free for every Seoul Land visitor; pond-smelt fishing costs extra and is first come, first served on site. Opened in late December 2025 (the report says only "recently opened", so we could not confirm the exact date), with no closing date in the notice (usually February). From Line 4 Seoul Grand Park Station Exit 2 take the Elephant Train (paid) or walk (Newsis 2025-12-29 [Secondary])
  • Everland Snow Buster, Yongin: opened in stages — the racing course and Snow Yard on December 12, then the Snow Playground (snow slide, body slide, snowball targets, wooden sled course) on December 19. The 200 m Express course (round tubes, up to 4 people) was due to start "early next month" (early January) as of the report, and that changes with the weather. Free for Everland visitors, usually until mid February. Free shuttle from Everland Station on the Yongin EverLine (Herald Economy 2025-12-10 [Secondary])
  • Admission prices at both parks change every year and there are many discount products for foreign visitors, so this guide does not list them. Check the official sites

④ District Sledding Hills — Free for Residents, an "Everyone Else" Fee for Travelers

These are temporary hills that district offices run for a little over a month in winter. They are cheaper than the Hangang hills or free, but the season is short and many close on Mondays.

  • Nowon Ssing-Ssing Sledding Hill: on the main field of Seoul National University of Science and Technology (서울과학기술대학교). 2025-12-23 to 2026-01-25, 10:00 to 17:00. Adult slope 90 m with 7 lanes, children's slope 50 m with 5 lanes (automatic starting gates). Free for Nowon residents, ₩3,000 (~$2.25) for everyone else. Pond-smelt fishing, roasted chestnuts (gunbam, 군밤) and a Euro bungee. Closed two days in January for university entrance exams — the dates differ between sources, so confirm before you go (Seoul Shinmun 2025-12-08 [Secondary])
  • Seocho Yangjaecheon Winter Snow Playground: at the Yangjaecheon (양재천) Swimming Pool. 2025-12-20 to 2026-02-22, Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 17:00, closed Mondays and on Seollal (설날) itself (Feb 17). ₩6,000 (~$4.50) for Seocho residents, ₩8,000 (~$6) for everyone else. Adult and toddler slopes, a trampoline, an inflatable bounce house for toddlers, pond-smelt fishing, curling and a snack bar (Herald Economy 2025-12-17 [Secondary])
  • Dobong Winter Snow Land: on the Sungkyunkwan University baseball field site near Line 1 Dobong Station (30 Dobong-ro 168-gil, 2024-25 basis [Secondary]). In the 2024-25 season, Dec 27 to Feb 2, Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 17:00, free for Dobong residents and ₩2,000 (~$1.50) for everyone else, with bumper cars, a mini train and pond-smelt fishing. We could not confirm whether it ran in 2025-26 — do not put it in your plans until the Dobong District notice appears (Seoul Media Hub 2025-01-21)
  • Resident fees are based on resident registration, so travelers always pay the "everyone else" fee. Even so, ₩2,000 to 8,000 (~$1.50 to 6) is the same as or less than the Hangang hills

⑤ Three Half-Day Routes

A. Ttukseom (east side, aim for opening time)

10:00 Jayang (Ttukseom Hangang Park) Station Exit 2 → 5-minute walk → Ttukseom sledding hill (the wait is short right after opening) → finish sledding at 12:50 → ramyeon at the food court during the 13:00 to 14:00 grooming hour → at 14:00 ride once more, or Jayang (Ttukseom Hangang Park) Station → one stop on Line 7 → Konkuk University Station (건대입구역) for the restaurant alley and Common Ground (커먼그라운드)

B. Yeouido (west side, combined with an indoor escape)

10:30 National Assembly Station Exit 1 → 15-minute walk → Yeouido sledding hill → 11:00 parade → lunch during the 13:00 grooming hour → 14:30 sing-along show → Yeouido Station (여의도역) → warm up at The Hyundai Seoul (더현대 서울), connected underground to Yeouido Station. The parade and show times are based on the 2025-26 Pororo theme, so check them again in the 2026-27 notice

C. Children's Grand Park (two or more children)

10:00 Children's Grand Park Station Exit 2 → SLP Children's Hall sledding hill → pond-smelt fishing → the zoo and playgrounds of Children's Grand Park (free) → one stop on Line 7 → Konkuk University Station

⑥ Clothing, What to Bring and On-Site Rules

  • Waterproof pants, gloves and waterproof shoes: tube sleds soak your pants. Pack a spare pair of socks and hand warmers (hot pack, 핫팩, ₩1,000 to 2,000 (~$0.75 to 1.50) at convenience stores). For cold-weather preparation in general, see #1318
  • Age rules: the large slope is for age 6 and up, and children under 36 months may not sled (free entry only). On the small slope too, a guardian must accompany the child
  • 13:00 to 14:00: the three Hangang hills stop for grooming. Use it as lunch time
  • Crowds: weekend afternoons have long lines. Weekdays or right after opening are better
  • Weather: the snow is artificial, so the hills open even without snowfall. They may shorten hours or close during rain, strong wind or a cold-wave warning, so check the day's notice

⚠ Five Traps People Actually Fall Into

1. Arriving at 1 PM — it is the grooming hour, so you wait an hour. Aim for 10 AM or 2 PM.

2. The 20-minute walk from Jamwon Station — hard with a stroller or small children. Choose Ttukseom (5 min) or Yeouido (15 min).

3. Free entry under 36 months only with proof — the notice's proof is a resident registration certificate or health insurance card, so whether a foreign passport works must be checked on site. If it does not, you pay ₩6,000 (~$4.50).

4. "Free" at district hills means residents only — travelers pay ₩2,000 to 8,000 (~$1.50 to 6).

5. Planning around the advertised opening day — in 2025-26 the Hangang hills were announced for Dec 19 and opened Dec 31. If you travel in mid December, check the notice again before leaving.

⚙ FAQ 3

1. Can we not sled if it does not snow? — The snow is artificial, so it does not matter. In 2025-26 the three Hangang hills ran for 50 days without a closing day.

2. Can adults ride? — The large slope is for everyone from age 6 to adults, and the fee is the same for all ages.

3. Where can we skate? — Seoul Plaza Skating Rink (서울광장 스케이트장) is ₩1,000 (~$0.75) per one-hour session, helmet and skate rental included, 2025-12-19 to 2026-02-08 (Seoul city official). For snow scenery and ice rinks, see #1319.
