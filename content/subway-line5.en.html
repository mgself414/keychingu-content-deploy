Why Line 5 — the purple line linking plazas, the Han River, and greenery

If Line 2 is youth, Line 3 is tradition, and Line 4 is shopping, then Line 5 (purple) is the line of plazas, the Han River, and greenery. From the downtown symbolism of Gwanghwamun Square, to the Han River and financial district of Yeouido, to Seoul Botanic Park in Magok (마곡) — it links Seoul's wide-open spaces. Reaching all the way to Gimpo Airport (domestic terminals · Lotte Mall (롯데몰)) at the western edge of Gangseo, it also serves as a gateway.

This guide organizes Line 5's 8 key tourist stations station by station. It pairs plaza, Han River, and botanic-park attractions (linked to existing deep dives) with restaurants pulled from accumulated data via nearest-station mapping. This is the fourth installment of the subway line-by-line series.

Line 5 station-by-station curation — what to do when you get off

🟣 #1. Gwanghwamun Station (광화문역) — the symbolic downtown plaza

  • 🚪 Exits: toward Gwanghwamun Square · Sejong Center (세종문화회관) · Kyobo Book Centre (교보문고)
  • 🍜 Eat: Gwanghwamun office-worker old-timer eateries and Korean food · restaurants in Seochon (서촌) and Tongui-dong (통의동) (toward Gyeongbokgung) · Gwanghwamun Michelin Korean cuisine
  • 👀 Play: Gwanghwamun Square (statues of King Sejong (세종대왕) and Yi Sun-sin (이순신)) · Gyeongbokgung (#37) · Sejong Center · Kyobo Book Centre · the starting point of Cheonggyecheon (청계천)
  • 🔁 Transfer: none (Gwanghwamun). City Hall (시청) (Lines 1·2) and Gyeongbokgung (Line 3) are within walking distance
  • cross-ref: #37 Gyeongbokgung · #1239 600 years of history (Gwanghwamun) · #1242 Line 3 (Gyeongbokgung)

Line 5 day courses 3 — riding the purple line

Course 1. Downtown plazas & palace (downtown)

  • Gwanghwamun #1 (Gwanghwamun Square · Gyeongbokgung) → Yeouido #2 (The Hyundai · Han River) → Yeouinaru #3 (Han River picnic)
  • fit: downtown landmarks · Han River · shopping. Best in the spring cherry blossom season.

Course 2. Greenery & botanic park (Gangseo)

  • Magok #4 (Seoul Botanic Park · LG Arts Center) → Gimpo Airport #5 (Lotte Mall) → domestic-flight transfer/shopping
  • fit: greenery · architecture · gateway. Greenhouse and mall on a rainy day.

Course 3. Food & riverside (Mapo · Gangdong)

  • Gongdeok #6 (food alley · Gyeongui Line Forest Park) → (via downtown) → Cheonho #8 (Rodeo · Gwangnaru Han River)
  • fit: local food · walking.

Line tips 5 — getting 200% out of Line 5

Tip 1. Line 5 is 'the line of plazas, the Han River, and greenery'

Gwanghwamun Square · the Han River at Yeouido · Seoul Botanic Park — it links Seoul's wide-open spaces. The spring Yeouido cherry blossoms and the autumn fireworks festival are the highlights.

Tip 2. Yeouido's weapon is its seasons

Spring cherry blossoms on Yunjung-ro, the autumn Seoul International Fireworks Festival, summer chimaek by the Han River. Yeouido Hangang Park (#586) is accessible via Line 5 (Yeouinaru) and Line 9.

Tip 3. Magok = indoor greenery on a rainy day

The large greenhouse at Seoul Botanic Park (#1240) is a walk regardless of the weather. The LG Arts Center architecture is also worth seeing.

Tip 4. Gimpo Airport = the domestic-flight gateway

Take Line 5 when transferring to Jeju/Busan domestic flights. One-stop souvenirs and groceries at Lotte Mall.

Tip 5. With a single transit card

A single T-money card covers Line 5, transfers, and buses. The Airport Railroad connection (Gongdeok · Gimpo Airport) also handles your arrival route.

FAQ 6 — Line 5 questions foreigners often ask

Q1. How is Line 5 different from other lines?

  • Line 5: plazas, the Han River, and greenery (Gwanghwamun · Yeouido · Seoul Botanic Park) + a gateway (Gimpo Airport). A different flavor from Line 2 (youth), Line 3 (tradition), and Line 4 (shopping).

Q2. Which station for the Han River?

  • Yeouinaru (direct connection to Yeouido Hangang Park) · Yeouido (The Hyundai · IFC). Spring cherry blossoms · autumn fireworks · summer chimaek. See #586.

Q3. What about Seoul Botanic Park?

  • Magok Station / Magongnaru. The large greenhouse is good even on a rainy day (#1240). Also the LG Arts Center architecture.

Q4. What is there to see at Gwanghwamun?

  • Gwanghwamun Square (King Sejong · Yi Sun-sin) · Gyeongbokgung (#37) · Sejong Center · Kyobo Book Centre. Within walking distance of Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역) on Line 3.

Q5. Is the restaurant information accurate?

  • It is based on Naver Map crawling, so it is for reference. Re-confirming on a map app before visiting is essential.

Q6. Is Gimpo Airport international?

  • It is the domestic-flight gateway (Jeju · Busan, etc.). For international flights, use Incheon Airport (인천공항). Convenient for Lotte Mall shopping and transfers.
