K-drama Masterpiece Filming Locations Deep Dive — Goblin, Itaewon Class, SKY Castle

> Subtitle: The complete guide for fans of Goblin, Itaewon Class, SKY Castle, Crash Landing on You, and Hotel del Luna

ISSUE 03 · #315 · 2026-05-20

Why K-drama Masterpiece Filming Locations?

When K-drama fans visit Korea, their first question is often: "Where are those places from the dramas, in real life?" This article provides actual filming locations + practical on-site information that foreign fans can visit directly. We won't just list places without descriptions.

3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "You can enter the Danbam (단밤) store."

The exterior of Danbam (단밤) in Itaewon Class was filmed on Itaewon Main Street (이태원 메인 거리). The store itself in the drama is not an actual operating business. You can take photos in front of the building, but there's no interior access or drama prop experience. It's more realistic to walk along Itaewon Street (이태원 거리) and soak in the atmosphere.

2. "Goblin (도깨비) was all filmed overseas."

Many fans think there's nothing to see in Korea because the "red umbrella" scene in Goblin (도깨비) was filmed in Quebec, Canada. In reality, Signiel Seoul (시그니엘 서울) (Jamsil (잠실)) was an official partnership filming location, repeatedly appearing as Gosu (공유) (Gong Yoo)'s apartment in the drama. Sitting in the lobby cafe can evoke scenes from the drama. Also, the stairs and bench scenes in Incheon Jayu Park (인천 자유공원) are actual places Goblin (도깨비) fans can visit.

3. "Filming locations operate as official tourist attractions."

Most K-drama filming locations are everyday spaces like residential areas, commercial streets, and parks. In most cases, there are no admission fees or drama-related exhibitions. It's basic etiquette to visit quietly and take photos without disturbing residents or businesses.

Deep Dive into Actual Filming Locations

1. Itaewon Class (이태원클라쓰) — Itaewon Main Street (이태원 메인 거리) [#144]

Drama Connection: The story of Park Sae-ro-yi (박새로이) (Park Seo-joon) starting Danbam (단밤) and growing with Itaewon Street (이태원 거리) as the backdrop. Itaewon (이태원)'s main streets repeatedly appear as the setting.

Location: Itaewon-ro (이태원로) area, Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seoul (서울) (Itaewon Station (이태원역) Exit 1 → 3-minute walk)

Access: Subway Line 6 Itaewon Station (이태원역). Taxis and buses are also convenient. Vehicle traffic can be heavy on weekend afternoons.

Admission: Free (public street). No restrictions.

Foreigner Fit: Itaewon (이태원) has the highest proportion of foreign residents and visitors in Seoul (서울). English menus are almost 100% available. Halal restaurants, the Islamic mosque (Itaewon Central Mosque, 이태원 중앙성원), and multinational food streets are all together, making it enjoyable even for non-drama fans.

Timing: 6 PM to 10 PM (18:00-22:00) best captures the drama's atmosphere. It's quieter during the day. Saturday and Sunday nights are crowded.

Manners: Be mindful of noise in residential areas within the gollmok (alleys). Check shoe-wearing and dress codes in front of the Islamic mosque.

2. Itaewon Class (이태원클라쓰) — Haebangchon (해방촌) and Huam-dong (후암동)

Drama Connection: Many scenes related to Oh Soo-ah (오수아) (Kim Da-mi) and gollmok (alley) shots were filmed in this area.

Location: Haebangchon-ro (해방촌로) area, Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seoul (서울) (Noksapyeong Station (녹사평역) Exit 2 → 10-minute walk, or 15-minute walk from Itaewon Station (이태원역))

Access: Subway Line 6 Noksapyeong Station (녹사평역). It's a hilly area, so walking requires stamina.

Admission: Free. Spending naturally occurs if you use cafes or vintage shops inside Sinheung Market (신흥시장).

Foreigner Fit: Famous as a vlog filming location for foreign creators. Independent cafes, vintage clothing stores, and mural gollmok (alleys) are quieter and less commercial than Itaewon (이태원). A 1-2 hour stroll is sufficient.

Timing: 10 AM to 4 PM (10:00-16:00). Some cafes open after noon. Many shops close at night.

Manners: The gollmok (alleys) are narrow and mixed with residential areas. Be careful with camera angles towards private homes when filming.

3. Goblin (도깨비) — Signiel Seoul (시그니엘 서울) (Jamsil (잠실))

Drama Connection: The luxurious apartment, hallway, and lobby scenes where Gosu (공유) (Gong Yoo) lives were filmed at Signiel Seoul (시그니엘 서울). It is an official partnership building for the drama.

Location: 300, Olympic-ro (올림픽로), Songpa-gu (송파구), Seoul (서울) (Lotte World Tower (롯데월드타워) 123-story building, lower floors are the hotel)

Access: Subway Line 2 and 8 Jamsil Station (잠실역) Exit 1 → direct connection to Lotte World Tower (롯데월드타워), 5-minute walk.

Admission: Even if you're not a hotel guest, the lobby cafe (Signature Lounge, 7th floor) can be used after ordering a drink. A drink costs around ₩15,000 (~$11 / ~80 RMB / ~1,600 JPY) to ₩25,000. The upper observatory (Seoul Sky, 서울스카이) has a separate admission fee of ₩27,000.

Foreigner Fit: Can be integrated with Lotte World Tower (롯데월드타워) observatory, aquarium, and shopping mall. English guidance is fully available. 100% card payment available.

Timing: The period right after opening on weekend mornings (10 AM to 11 AM / 10:00-11:00) is good for taking photos without lobby congestion. For Seoul Sky (서울스카이) observatory, clear days between 3 PM and 5 PM (15:00-17:00) or evening hours (7 PM to 9 PM / 19:00-21:00) are recommended.

Manners: Do not obstruct guest traffic inside the hotel lobby. If you ask staff, "Is this a Goblin (도깨비) filming location?", most will kindly guide you.

4. Goblin (도깨비) — Incheon Jayu Park (인천 자유공원)

Drama Connection: The stairs and bench scenes where the Goblin's Bride (김고은) (Kim Go-eun) and Goblin (공유) (Gong Yoo) walked. Known as a real-life pilgrimage site among drama fans.

Location: Jayu Park Buk-ro (자유공원북로), Jung-gu (중구), Incheon (인천) (Incheon Station (인천역) Exit 1 → 20-minute walk or 5-minute taxi ride)

Access: KTX (Korea Train eXpress) or regular trains from Seoul Station (서울역) to Incheon Station (인천역) takes about 35 minutes. Subway Line 1 terminates at Incheon Station (인천역). Can be combined with Incheon Chinatown (인천 차이나타운) and Open Port Cafe Street (개항장 카페 거리) for a day trip.

Admission: Free. The entire park is open.

Foreigner Fit: Can be integrated into a day course with Incheon Chinatown (인천 차이나타운) (15-minute walk), Sinpo International Market (신포국제시장) (10-minute walk), and Open Port Cafe Street (개항장 카페 거리). Finding the "Goblin (도깨비) stairs" first and then moving to Chinatown (차이나타운) is common among fans.

Timing: 10 AM to 3 PM (10:00-15:00). Sunset is also nice, but it's a hillside, so it's recommended to leave before it gets dark.

Manners: This is a public resting area for citizens. Refrain from noisy behavior like mimicking drama lines.

> Recommendation: Incheon Jayu Park (인천 자유공원) is a known spot among fans. It is not officially designated as a filming location, and it's recommended to check the current access status via Naver Map (네이버 지도) before visiting.

5. SKY Castle — Cheongdam-dong (청담동) and Dosan Park (도산공원) Area [#296]

Drama Connection: Set in an affluent residential area. Cheongdam-dong (청담동) Street and the exterior of upscale Gangnam (강남) buildings represent the drama's atmosphere.

Location: Cheongdam-dong (청담동) and Dosan-daero (도산대로) area, Gangnam-gu (강남구), Seoul (서울) (Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역) Exit 5 → 5-minute walk)

Access: Subway Suin-Bundang Line Apgujeong Rodeo Station (압구정로데오역). Or Subway Line 3 Apgujeong Station (압구정역) → 15-minute walk.

Admission: Free (street and park). Dosan Park (도산공원) itself is freely open.

Foreigner Fit: Luxury brand stores, Hannam (한남) and Cheongdam (청담) galleries, and upscale Hansik (한식, Korean cuisine) restaurants are concentrated here. Even if you're not a SKY Castle fan, it's worth visiting to experience the "Seoul (서울) luxury district." Apgujeong Rodeo Street (압구정 로데오 거리) is also a hub for MZ (Millennials and Gen Z) brand shops.

Timing: Weekday afternoons from 2 PM to 6 PM (14:00-18:00). Weekends see crowds for brunch and shopping. Dosan Park (도산공원) has many morning walkers, so early hours are also good for quiet photos.

Manners: The gollmok (alleys) within Cheongdam (청담) residential areas are private living spaces. Do not photograph private home gardens without residents' consent.

6. Crash Landing on You — Nami Island (남이섬) [#Outskirts]

Drama Connection: Many scenes of Ri Jeong-hyeok (리정혁) (Hyun Bin) and Yoon Se-ri (윤세리) (Son Ye-jin) walking together in the birch forest and by the lakeside. Various locations within Nami Island (남이섬).

Location: 1, Namiseom-gil (남이섬길), Gapyeong-gun (가평군), Gyeonggi-do (경기도) (Naminara Republic)

Access: From Seoul (서울) Yongsan Station (용산역) or Cheongnyangni Station (청량리역) → ITX-Cheongchun (ITX-청춘) train to Gapyeong Station (가평역) (approx. 1 hour 10 minutes) → shuttle bus or taxi for 10 minutes → Nami Island Ferry Terminal (남이섬 선착장) → ferry (Naminaru Ferry Terminal, 나미나루 선착장) for 5 minutes.

Admission: Admission fee ₩16,000 (adult, includes ferry ticket). Card payment available. Advance booking possible on the official website (namisum.com).

Foreigner Fit: Nami Island (남이섬) itself has comprehensive guidance for foreign fans (English, Chinese, Japanese). It's also visitable in winter, and the snowy birch forest scenery is most similar to the drama scenes. You can also combine it with mountains and rafting in nearby Gapyeong (가평).

Timing: 9 AM to 4 PM (09:00-16:00) (calculate backward from the last ferry time). The birch forest in winter (December-February) and autumn foliage (October-November) are closest to the drama's aesthetic.

Manners: Bicycle rentals are available on the island. Observe the separated signs for bicycle and pedestrian zones.

7. Crash Landing on You — Jamsil Lotte Tower (잠실 롯데타워) Area

Drama Connection: Some of Hyun Bin and Son Ye-jin's Seoul (서울) scenes were filmed around Jamsil Lotte World (잠실 롯데월드). Lotte World Mall (롯데월드몰) interior passages appear.

Location: 300, Olympic-ro (올림픽로), Songpa-gu (송파구), Seoul (서울) (Same building complex as Signiel Seoul (시그니엘 서울) mentioned above)

Access: Direct connection from Jamsil Station (잠실역) Exit 1.

Admission: Shopping mall (Lotte World Mall, 롯데월드몰) is free to enter. Lotte World Adventure (롯데월드 어드벤처) theme park has a separate fee of ₩62,000 (adult).

Foreigner Fit: Can integrate the routes for Goblin (도깨비) (Signiel (시그니엘)) and Crash Landing on You (롯데타워 주변). Cover two drama filming locations in one day.

8. Hotel del Luna — Insa-dong Ssamziegil (인사동 쌈지길) [#297]

Drama Connection: Man-wol (만월) (Lee Ji-eun / IU)'s hotel is set against the atmosphere of the Insa-dong (인사동) area. Insa-dong (인사동) gollmok (alleys) and Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌 한옥마을) match the drama's aesthetic.

Location: 44, Insadong-gil (인사동길), Jongno-gu (종로구), Seoul (서울) (Ssamziegil (쌈지길)) / Anguk Station (안국역) Exit 6 → 5-minute walk

Access: Subway Line 3 Anguk Station (안국역). Within walking distance of Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) and Bukchon (북촌).

Admission: Free entry to Ssamziegil (쌈지길) interior. Craft items and tea experiences are separate.

Foreigner Fit: Many English signboards. You can rent Hanbok (한복, traditional Korean dress) (in the Bukchon (북촌) area) to match the drama's costume vibe. Insa-dong (인사동) Main Street (메인 거리) is the number one spot for traditional crafts and souvenir shopping.

Timing: 10 AM to 6 PM (10:00-18:00). Insa-dong (인사동) galleries and tea houses are quiet on weekdays and busy on weekend afternoons.

Manners: Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌 한옥마을) residential areas are residents' living spaces. Avoid loud noises in the gollmok (alleys) and visiting late at night. Follow "Please be quiet" signs.

9. Reply 1988 (응답하라 1988) — Ssangmun-dong (쌍문동) Alley

Drama Connection: The gollmok (alleys) of Ssangmun-dong (쌍문동), Seoul (서울) in the 1980s are the main setting. Actual filming for the drama used real gollmok (alleys) in Ssangmun-dong (쌍문동), Dobong-gu (도봉구) + some sets.

Location: Ssangmun-dong (쌍문동) area, Dobong-gu (도봉구), Seoul (서울) (Subway Line 4 Ssangmun Station (쌍문역))

Admission: Free. However, the drama set is currently not open to the public. The drama's gollmok (alley) atmosphere can be partially felt in the general residential areas around Ssangmun Station (쌍문역).

> Recommendation: While visiting Ssangmun-dong (쌍문동) gollmok (alleys) is known among Reply 1988 (응답하라 1988) fans, the actual filming sets have been relocated or dismantled. When visiting, it's recommended to approach it as an experience of current general residential gollmok (alleys) in Seoul (서울) rather than expecting a "1988 atmosphere." It may be difficult to identify specific buildings without local guidance.

K-drama Filming Location Tour Options

Self-Guided (Free)

  • Most locations are accessible with a T-money card + Naver Map (네이버 지도) (English settings).
  • Itaewon (이태원), Insa-dong (인사동), Jamsil (잠실), and Cheongdam (청담) can cover 2-3 spots in one day.

Tour Packages (Paid)

  • Search for "K-drama filming location tour Seoul" on Klook or Viator for various options.
  • Includes English-speaking guide and drama-related explanations. 4-8 hours, around ₩50,000 to ₩80,000.
  • Nami Island (남이섬) + Chuncheon (춘천) day trips can also be booked on Klook.

Caution: Some tours promote visiting "official sets," but in reality, they are often just visits to general streets. Always check reviews before booking.

Language

  • Itaewon (이태원), Jamsil (잠실), Insa-dong (인사동): Sufficient English information. Basic communication in English is possible.
  • Ssangmun-dong (쌍문동), Incheon Jayu Park (인천 자유공원): Mostly Korean. Recommended to prepare Naver Map (네이버 지도) (English) + Papago (번역 app).
  • Signiel Seoul (시그니엘 서울): 100% English as it's a hotel.
  • Nami Island (남이섬): English, Chinese, and Japanese information fully available.

Essential Apps: Naver Map (네이버 지도) (English UI settings), Papago (번역 app), Kakao T (카카오 T) (taxi hailing).

FAQ

Q1. How can I plan a one-day K-drama tour course in Seoul (서울)?

Morning → Insa-dong (인사동) and Bukchon (북촌) (Hotel del Luna vibe, Subway Line 3 Anguk Station (안국역)). Lunch → Itaewon (이태원) (Itaewon Class, Subway Line 6). Afternoon → Jamsil (잠실) Signiel (시그니엘) lobby (Goblin). Evening → Cheongdam (청담) and Apgujeong (압구정) (SKY Castle vibe). All possible with one T-money card, total travel time approx. 1.5 hours.

Q2. Is a day trip to Nami Island (남이섬) actually feasible?

Yes, it is. Depart Seoul (서울) at 7-8 AM, arrive at Nami Island (남이섬) at 10 AM, depart at 3-4 PM, return to Seoul (서울) at 5-6 PM. ITX-Cheongchun (ITX-청춘) train tickets can be purchased with KorailPass (for foreigners) or on-site. During peak season (spring/autumn weekends), ferry tickets may sell out early, so advance booking on namisum.com is recommended.

Q3. Are there any filming locations with actual drama props or exhibitions?

Currently (as of 2026), there are no permanently operating official K-drama exhibition halls in Seoul (서울). There are related exhibitions at Hallyu World (한류월드) (Goyang-si (고양시), Gyeonggi-do (경기도)), but accessibility is low, and they operate seasonally. Drama-related goods and souvenirs can be purchased at Insa-dong (인사동) souvenir shops and Myeong-dong (명동) Hallyu stores.

Q4. If I want to experience a similar vibe to Goblin (도깨비)'s "Quebec scenes" in Korea?

Jeongdongjin (정동진) sunrise scenes in Gangwon-do (강원도) [#254] are known among Goblin (도깨비) fans as an alternative. Jeongdongjin Station (정동진역) is a train station right by the sea, accessible via the KTX Donghae Line. It's possible to depart early morning → watch the sunrise → return to Seoul (서울) on a day trip.

Q5. Are there any places to be cautious about when visiting alone as a foreigner?

It's recommended to visit Ssangmun-dong (쌍문동) residential areas and Incheon Jayu Park (인천 자유공원) during the daytime, as some areas can be secluded. Itaewon (이태원), Jamsil (잠실), Insa-dong (인사동), and Cheongdam (청담) are areas where foreigners can move around safely alone. It's best to avoid walking alone in general residential gollmok (alleys) at night (after 10 PM / 22:00).
