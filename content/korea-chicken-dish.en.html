5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. Assuming All Korean Chicken Is Spicy

Korean chicken divides sharply into spicy (dakgalbi (닭갈비), jjimdak (찜닭)) and mild (samgyetang (삼계탕), dakhanmari (닭한마리)). If you can't handle heat, start with samgyetang (삼계탕) or dakhanmari (닭한마리)—skipping them means missing half of Korean chicken culture.

2. Ordering Dakgalbi Solo Without Checking

Dakgalbi (닭갈비) is almost always a minimum 2-serving order (₩28,000+). Single diners are largely out of luck → solo travelers should head to a dakgalbi alley in Chuncheon (춘천) or a Yoogane (유가네) location with a solo menu.

3. Skipping the Fried Rice Finale

The real payoff of dakgalbi (닭갈비) and jjimdak (찜닭) is the finishing bokkeumbap (볶음밥, fried rice). After finishing the chicken, add rice + seaweed + egg for ₩2,000–3,000, mix it into the spicy sauce — that's the Korean way to end the meal. Skipping this means missing half the experience.

4. Being Scared of Dakbal and Dakddongzip

Dakbal (닭발, chicken feet) and dakddongzip (닭똥집, chicken gizzard) are Korean late-night snack essentials. Foreigners often refuse based on looks alone, but the spicy seasoning + chewy texture make them a classic Korean drinking snack. Totally worth trying.

5. Thinking Samgyetang Is Only for Summer

Koreans eat samgyetang (삼계탕) on Boknal (복날) days (mid-July to mid-August), but foreigners can enjoy it year-round. Samgyetang restaurants in Myeongdong (명동) and Jongno (종로) are open 365 days — no need to wait for summer.

★ The Big 3 Dakgalbi — Chuncheon Dakgalbi (춘천닭갈비) vs Yoogane (유가네) vs Saemaeul Sikdang (새마을식당)

The three dakgalbi (닭갈비) chains foreigners encounter most in Seoul. Spend the same ₩14,000 and you'll get a completely different experience in terms of spice level, sauce, sides, and solo-dining availability.

1️⃣ Chuncheon Dakgalbi (춘천닭갈비) — The Birthplace of Dakgalbi

Brand: Originated in Chuncheon (춘천), Gangwon Province (강원도). Seoul branches include Chuncheon Dakgalbi Bonjum (본점) and Myeongdong (명동) locations.

Pricing:

  • Dakgalbi (닭갈비) for 2: ₩28,000–32,000 (~₩14,000–16,000 per person)
  • Udon noodle add-on: ₩2,500
  • Fried rice finale: ₩2,500–3,000
  • Cheese add-on: ₩3,000–4,000

Key Points:

  • Original Chuncheon-style sauce — gochujang (고추장) base with a touch of sweetness, spice level ★★★★ (manageable for most foreigners)
  • Generous portions of cabbage, sweet potato, and tteok (떡, rice cake)
  • Foreigner-friendly: English menu + photo menu

One-line Review: "The authentic home of Korean dakgalbi — the #1 choice for your first dakgalbi experience."

2️⃣ Yoogane (유가네) — Solo-Friendly + Global Chain

Brand: A global chain with locations in Korea, Japan, and Southeast Asia. Solo menu available — top pick for solo travelers.

Pricing:

  • Solo dakgalbi (닭갈비): ₩13,500–15,500 (the only chain among the Big 3 with a single-serving option)
  • 2-person serving: ₩27,000–31,000
  • Cheese dakgalbi (닭갈비): ₩16,000–18,000
  • Fried rice: ₩2,500

Key Points:

  • Solo seating + solo menu = #1 pick for lone travelers
  • Spice level ★★★ (slightly milder than Chuncheon)
  • 100% English menu — globally standardized thanks to international branches
  • Compact, clean fast-casual format with 30–50 seats on average

One-line Review: "Solo-friendly, English menu, global standard — the definitive answer for solo travelers."

3️⃣ Saemaeul Sikdang (새마을식당) — Baek Jong-won's Value Pick

Brand: Baek Jong-won's (백종원) The Born Korea (더본코리아) — 1,000+ locations nationwide.

Pricing:

  • 7-minute pork stew + lightly spicy dakgalbi (닭갈비): ₩14,000–16,000
  • Yeoltan Bulgogi (열탄불고기) (signature dish): ₩13,000–15,000
  • Fried rice: ₩2,000

Key Points:

  • Baek Jong-won (백종원) brand — value-for-money + homestyle Korean dakgalbi
  • Spice level ★★★ — approachable for spice-curious foreigners
  • Cozy neighborhood diner vibe, avg 20–30 seats
  • Saemaeul Sikdang (새마을식당) is a general Korean restaurant, not a dakgalbi specialist — dakgalbi is a supporting menu item

One-line Review: "Baek Jong-won value + Korean home cooking — great when you want dakgalbi alongside other Korean dishes."

Key Points: Young chicken stuffed with ginseng, garlic & glutinous rice / not spicy / #1 pick for foreigners who can't handle heat / open 365 days

④ Dakhanmari (닭한마리) — Jinokwa (진옥화) & Myeongdong (명동)

Location: Jinokwa (진옥화) flagship, Jongno (종로) / Myeongdong Dakhanmari (명동닭한마리)

Price: 2 pax ₩28,000–36,000

Key Points: Whole chicken simmered in clear broth / add tteok (떡), mandu (만두, dumplings), or noodles / not spicy / #1 pick for group dining with non-spice-eaters

8 Foreigner Tips (Checklist)

✅ Spice level — say "non-spicy" or "matji anke (맵지 않게)"

✅ Dakgalbi (닭갈비) minimum 2 servings — solo travelers go to Yoogane (유가네) only

✅ Fried rice finale ₩2,500 — the standard way to finish dakgalbi (닭갈비)

✅ Dakhanmari (닭한마리) & samgyetang (삼계탕) are not spicy — #1 for spice-averse visitors

✅ Dakbal (닭발) & dakddongzip (닭똥집) may look intimidating — trust the seasoning and texture

✅ Market tongdak (통닭) is freshest in the evening

✅ Card + mobile pay accepted almost everywhere (some market stalls cash only)

✅ Allergy note: chicken, egg, soy (common in marinades)

FAQ

Q1. Is English spoken?

Yoogane (유가네), Chuncheon Dakgalbi (춘천닭갈비), Tosokchon (토속촌), Goryeo Samgyetang (고려삼계탕), and Myeongdong Dakhanmari (명동닭한마리) all have English menus. Market stalls and old neighborhood spots are mostly Korean-only.

Q2. I can't eat spicy food.

Go for samgyetang (삼계탕), dakhanmari (닭한마리), ginseng chicken soup (인삼닭곰탕), or market tongdak (통닭) — all non-spicy. Saying "matji anke (맵지 않게)" or "non-spicy" works at most restaurants.

Q3. Can I dine solo?

Yoogane (유가네) dakgalbi (닭갈비) = solo menu. Samgyetang (삼계탕) & dakhanmari (닭한마리) = solo-friendly. Chuncheon Dakgalbi (춘천닭갈비) & jjimdak (찜닭) = minimum 2 servings.

Q4. Any vegan options?

Essentially none in the chicken category. Vegans should refer to guide #69 (vegan & halal guide).

Q5. Traveling with kids?

Samgyetang (삼계탕) & dakhanmari (닭한마리) = not spicy, family-safe. Dakgalbi (닭갈비) = spice level adjustable upon request.

Q6. Tight budget?

Saemaeul Sikdang (새마을식당) at ₩14,000 = best value pick. Market tongdak (통닭) at ₩15,000 = share a whole bird with your group.

Spring (Mar–May) — Best season for dakgalbi (닭갈비) & jjimdak (찜닭)

Summer (Jun–Aug) — Samgyetang (삼계탕) Boknal (복날) season (mid-July to mid-August)

Fall (Sep–Nov) — Dakhanmari (닭한마리) & ginseng chicken soup (인삼닭곰탕) + fall foliage
