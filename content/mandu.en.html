3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Makgeolli (막걸리) = just rice wine"

Korean makgeolli comes in 50+ varieties by region, ABV, and fermentation method — Pyeongchang sweet pumpkin makgeolli, Jeju Udo (우도) peanut makgeolli, Seoul 7.8% makgeolli, and more. From a foreign English review of 7.8 Makgeolli (7.8 막걸리): "Wide variety of makgeolli from all over the peninsula" — the first branch lets you taste makgeolli from across the entire Korean peninsula in one sitting. On your first visit, ask for a sampling of several varieties.

2. "Traditional liquor = low ABV"

  • Makgeolli (막걸리): 6–8% ABV (slightly higher than beer)
  • Yakju (약주) / Cheongju (청주): 13–17% ABV (wine level)
  • Soju (소주): 16–25% ABV (distilled spirit)
  • Traditional distilled soju (안동소주, Andong Soju, etc.): 40–50% ABV (whisky level)

When tasting at the Korean Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리) or Boksoondoga (복순도가), 4 samples ≈ roughly 1 glass of wine. From a Japanese review of Boksoondoga: "試飲もさせてくれます。繊細な口当たりのマッコリが本当に美味しい" — even a smooth, delicate palate should stay mindful of the actual ABV.

3. "Brewery = factory tour"

Downtown Seoul breweries like Boseok Brewery (보석양조) and Euljiro Brewing (을지맥옥) follow a microbrewery model where the drinking space is the brewing space itself — you sip freshly made beer right next to the fermentation tanks. From a Japanese review of Euljiro Brewing: "乙支路はレトロの聖地ですもんね。ビールは相変わらず美味しいです" — a core asset in the Euljiro (을지로) retro cluster.

1. Korean Makgeolli & Traditional Liquor — 5-Minute Intro

1-1. 4 Types of Korean Traditional Liquor — Quick Reference Table

Type ABV Character Budget per Person Recommended Pairing
Makgeolli (막걸리) 6–8% Fermented rice + yeast; cloudy, sweet, slightly sour ₩6,000–₩12,000 Jeon (전, savory pancakes), pajeon (파전), seafood, pork
Yakju (약주) 13–17% Clarified layer above makgeolli; golden, dry ₩15,000–₩30,000 Hanjeongsik (한정식, Korean table d'hôte), traditional snacks, dumplings
Cheongju (청주) 13–17% Clear grain wine; cousin of Japanese sake ₩20,000–₩40,000 Sashimi, seafood, Japanese-style bites
Soju (소주) 16–25% (40+ if distilled) Grain distillate; clean and strong ₩6,000–₩20,000 Samgyeopsal (삼겹살), jeon (전), gukbap (국밥, rice soup)

1-2. Seasonal Recommendations

Season Pick Why
Spring (Mar–May) Makgeolli seasonal varieties — sweet pumpkin, strawberry Sweet seasonal brews; hanok outdoor seating
Summer (Jun–Aug) Chilled makgeolli or cold cheongju (청주) Light at ~6% ABV; perfect for outdoor evening spots
Fall (Sep–Nov) Yakju (약주) & distilled spirits Deeper flavor; pairs well with hanjeongsik (한정식) and jeon (전)
Winter (Dec–Feb) Warm makgeolli or distilled spirits Warm pairings; cozy hanok interiors

"Good!! Nice place!!" (short but unanimous 5 stars)

Pillar B. Traditional Liquor Introduction — Tasting & Discovery Venues

3. Korean Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리)

  • Category: Cultural institution (traditional liquor exhibition & education)
  • Location: Bukchon-ro (북촌로), Jongno-gu, Seoul (5-min walk from Anguk (안국역) Station, entrance to Bukchon Hanok Village (북촌 한옥마을))
  • Foreign signal: 0 non-Korean reviews (cultural institution, rarely reviewed online) — a hidden cultural gem for foreign visitors
  • Signature: Tasting of 50+ varieties of Korean traditional liquor; curated brewery exhibitions; multilingual brochures
  • Foreigner advantage: The #1 introduction to Korean traditional liquor — compare 50+ varieties in a gallery-style setting. Guides available in English and Japanese. Free entry or partially ticketed tasting sessions.
  • Key tip for foreigners: Advance reservation recommended for tasting sessions (via website or phone). The best introductory experience for first-time visitors to Korea.
  • Hours: Tue–Sun 10:00–19:00 (closed Mondays)
  • Budget: Tasting course ₩10,000–₩20,000 (4–6 varieties)

"ソウル駅で空港行きArexへ向かう入り口から降りるエスカレーターの途中にあります。とても美味しくお土産にピッタリ。試飲もさせてくれます。"

"ソウル駅内にある人気のマッコリのお店。飛行機持ち込める？と聞くと大丈夫、と教えてくれたので2本購入。保冷剤と簡易保冷バッグをつけてくれます。"

3. FAQ — 6 Questions Foreigners Often Ask

Q1. Payment — Can I use WeChat Pay or Alipay?

  • All 8 venues accept card, KakaoPay (카카오페이), and Samsung Pay.
  • Boksoondoga Seoul Station (복순도가 서울역점), the Korean Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리), and Ace Four Club (에이스포클럽) are on popular foreigner routes and tend to support a wider range of payment options.
  • Breweries like Boseok Brewery (보석양조) and some hanok (한옥) venues may prefer cash in some cases.

Q2. Are English menus available?

  • Boksoondoga (복순도가) / Korean Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리) / Ace Four Club (에이스포클럽) / Uyajae (우야재) / Eulji Haok (을지하옥): English menus ✓ (English-speaking staff)
  • 7.8 Makgeolli (7.8 막걸리) / Euljiro Brewing (을지맥옥): Partial English menus (multilingual tablet ordering)
  • Boseok Brewery (보석양조): Primarily Korean — ask staff for recommendations

Q3. Can I visit solo?

  • All 8 venues welcome solo visitors. Solo drinking is completely natural in Korean bar culture.
  • Uyajae (우야재) and Eulji Haok (을지하옥) have limited seating — advance reservations recommended.

Q4. Any vegan or halal options?

  • Makgeolli (막걸리), yakju (약주), and cheongju (청주) are grain-fermented beverages and are vegan (no eggs or dairy). However, most food pairings contain animal-based ingredients.
  • None of these venues carry halal certification. Alcohol itself is religiously prohibited for Muslims — venues with halal-only food options are outside the scope of this guide.

Q5. Can I bring children?

  • This guide is alcohol-focused — not recommended for children.
  • That said, Uyajae (우야재) and Eulji Haok (을지하옥) also offer food-focused courses that may be suitable for families. The Korean Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리) is family-friendly as a gallery visit (outside the tasting portions).

Q6. Where can I buy liquor to take home or bring on the plane?

  • Boksoondoga Seoul Station (복순도가 서울역점): Directly connected to the AREX (공항철도) airport line, free insulated packaging, carry-on confirmed OK. Top pick.
  • Korean Traditional Liquor Gallery (전통주갤러리): Some gift-packaged bottles available in-store.
  • 7.8 Makgeolli (7.8 막걸리): Smaller bottles sometimes available for purchase.
