How Korean Brand Collabs Come Together — Convenience Store × Idols and Music, Beauty × K-pop and Characters: 4 Types, Plus Past Cases from Official Press Releases (Confirmed October 2026)

Subtitle: CU × RESCENE (리센느) "BAKE405" 5 breads, with 1 random transparent photocard out of 27 in each pack (BGF Retail official press release 2026-09-15) · CU × RIIZE (라이즈) "Little RIIZE" 6 items and a 16-day Seongsu (성수) pop-up (BGF Retail official press release 2026-06-15) · Amorepacific × BTS Lip Sleeping Mask limited editions, 2 rounds (Amorepacific official newsroom 2021·2022) · All of these are limited-time, so check separately whether anything is on sale now — as of 2026-10-21

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Tear open a convenience-store bread bag and a transparent photocard is the first thing you touch, before the bread. If the same member keeps turning up, you end up buying one more bag. Most Korean collab products are built on this structure. This guide lays out which types Korean collabs come in and, for each type, past cases confirmed from company press releases, with years. Once you know the planning structure, you can tell at a glance what is limited and what is random the next time you run into a new collab in Seoul.

Every product listed here is a limited-time item that has already ended or is about to end. For where to buy now, plus the permanent stores that run collabs year-round and the official channels, see How to Find Korean Collab Products — Permanent Stores (KWANGYA@SEOUL, K-Pop Square Hongdae, Ktown4u COEX, Olive Young Myeongdong) and How to Read Pop-Ups and Official Channels.

🍞 Convenience Store × K-pop Artists — Random Goods in Breads and Desserts

The most common type. A pack printed with the artist's art holds a random photocard or Ddibuseal (띠부씰, a peel-off sticker). BGF Retail explains that bakery items sit at the ₩3,000–5,000 level (~$2–4), so they are an easy buy, and that random goods stir fans' urge to collect (BGF Retail official press release 2026-09-15).

  • CU × RESCENE (리센느) "BAKE405" 5 items — September 2026, limited time — Each member took charge of one flavor she likes. Wonyi (원이)'s corn cream bread, Minami (미나미)'s melon bread, May (메이)'s cinnamon roll, Jena (제나)'s strawberry sandwich, Liv (리브)'s choco hotteok (호떡, a filled Korean sweet pancake). They rolled out in stages from September 17, and each pack holds 1 random card out of 27 limited-edition transparent photocards. The members took part directly in development (BGF Retail official press release 2026-09-15). A later October press release said RESCENE breads passed 1,000,000 units cumulative sales (BGF Retail official press release 2026-10-13)
  • CU × RIIZE (라이즈) "Little RIIZE" 6 items — June–July 2026, already ended — Tied to the release of RIIZE's second mini album "II". It consists of 3 breads (Strawberry Butsse (딸기 붓세), Honey Danish (허니 데니쉬), Choco Angba (초코앙바)) with 1 of 54 Ddibuseal (띠부씰) stickers, 2 macarons with 1 of 12 epoxy stickers, and 1 bottle candy released July 8. The pop-up store ran 16 days at the CU Seongsu Dessert Park branch (성수디저트파크점), June 20 – July 5. BGF Retail wrote that foreign customers account for more than 30% of this store's sales (BGF Retail official press release 2026-06-15)
  • CU × ITZY "Twinji" (트윈지) — October 2024, already ended — It features "Twinji", a character series that draws the 5 members as cats. The Twinji Macaron (트윈지카롱, 5 pieces, ₩9,500, ~$7) and Twinji Jelly (트윈지젤리, ₩2,500, ~$2) contained Ddibuseal stickers. Pop-ups ran through October 28 at the CU Olympic Gwangjang branch (올림픽광장점), Hongdae Sangsang branch (홍대상상점), and Sinchon Lemeilleur branch (신촌르메이에르점) (BGF Retail official press release 2024-10-15)

In the same press release, BGF Retail summarized that it had put out collab products with aespa, Key, ITZY, TXT, CHUU, Ahn So-hee (안소희), QWER and others (BGF Retail official press release 2026-06-15).

🎵 Convenience Store × Music — The Store That Sells Songs

This type puts a song in something edible or displays albums themselves.

  • CU Song Candy — July 2026 (Vol. 1) · September (Vol. 2) · October (Vol. 3) — Switch it on and bite the candy, and a bone-conduction method carries vibration through the jawbone so you hear a song for about 40 seconds. Vol. 3 (₩5,000, ~$4) was announced for release on October 14 and holds one song picked at random from RESCENE's "Runaway", CHUU's "XO, My Cyberlove", and EVNNE (이븐)'s "Baeteo" (뱉어). Vol. 1 was "Sonagi" (소나기, "Shower"), the OST of the drama "Lovely Runner" (선재 업고 튀어), and Vol. 2 was a (G)I-DLE (아이들) song (BGF Retail official press release 2026-10-13). We could not confirm whether Vol. 3 is in stores now
  • Idol album sales — CU has sold albums mainly in districts with many foreign visitors, such as Hongdae (홍대) and Seongsu (성수). In 2026 it sold albums by ENHYPEN, TOMORROW X TOGETHER, and CORTIS (BGF Retail official press release 2026-10-13)
  • CU "Chuu-High" (츄-하이) — February 2024 — A highball with the solo artist CHUU as its model (BGF Retail official press release 2024-02-07). It is alcohol, so you may be asked for ID at checkout; bring your passport

💄 Beauty × K-pop — Limited Editions of Steady Sellers

Rather than make a new product, this type dresses an already popular product in artist design.

  • BTS | Amorepacific Lip Sleeping Mask Purple Edition — November 2021, already ended — A limited edition based on the LANEIGE (라네즈) Lip Sleeping Mask. Planned to mark the "BTS PERMISSION TO DANCE ON STAGE" tour, it went on sale from November 26 on Amoremall (아모레몰) (Amorepacific official newsroom 2021-11-22)
  • BTS | Amorepacific Lip Sleeping Mask Lip & Pop Edition — October 2022, already ended — The second collab limited edition. It went on sale from October 24 on the online Amoremall (아모레몰) (Amorepacific official newsroom 2022-10-24)

Both times the sales channel was the online Amoremall (아모레몰). Beauty collabs sometimes drop exclusively online rather than in stores, so check the sales channel first in the announcement.

🧸 Character × Beauty and Snacks — Collabs Without K-pop

This type uses domestic and international characters instead of idols. They often come exclusively through one channel such as Olive Young (올리브영).

  • Mise-en-scène (미쟝센) Hello Cream Zanmang Loopy Edition · Illiyoon (일리윤) Sanrio Characters Edition — May 2023, already ended — The Zanmang Loopy (잔망루피) edition sold in online malls such as Olive Young (올리브영), Amoremall (아모레몰), Coupang (쿠팡), and Market Kurly (마켓컬리); the Sanrio Characters edition sold on the Olive Young (올리브영) channel. The Sanrio products and goods sold out the prepared stock one day after the official-store pre-order opened in April (Amorepacific official newsroom 2023-05-02)
  • CU Pat & Mat (패트와 매트) Banban Bar (반반바) — July 2026 — Strawberry and mango ice cream made in a dedicated mold shaped like the characters (₩3,900, ~$3). It also said that Frozen Hello Kitty Sorbet and Frozen Kuromi Sorbet (₩5,900 each, ~$4) were due out on the 15th of the same month (BGF Retail official press release 2026-07-13). It is a summer-season item, so we could not confirm whether it is on sale now

🧰 Collab Product Practicalities — Solo, Booking, Payment, Getting Around

  • How to read it: The words that repeat in press releases are "industry-exclusive", "staged release", "random enclosure", and "pop-up for N days". Exclusive means only at that chain; staged means the items differ by date. Random means you may have to buy several until the member you want comes up (BGF Retail official press release 2026-06-15·2026-09-15)
  • Solo: Convenience-store and Olive Young (올리브영) items are easy to buy alone. Pop-ups open briefly at just a few designated stores (3 for ITZY, 1 for RIIZE — BGF Retail official press release)
  • Booking: Like the Amorepacific Sanrio edition, an official-store pre-order sometimes opens first (Amorepacific official newsroom 2023-05-02). We could not confirm whether the pre-order accepts foreign cards or overseas shipping
  • Payment: Foreigner Korea Payment System Deep Dive; for unmanned registers, Self-Checkout and Night Unmanned Stores in Korea: The No-Panic Manual — From Door Tap to Payment
  • Getting around and stores: For differences between chains, see Korea's Big 4 Convenience Stores Compared: CU vs GS25 vs 7-Eleven vs emart24. For permanent collab stores, see "How to Find Korean Collab Products" under Related Content below
  • When words fail: Manners When Words Fail — Ordering Etiquette in the Age of Translation Apps and Kiosks

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "I'll go buy the collab I saw in an old article" — The RIIZE, ITZY, and BTS collabs listed here were all limited products with fixed sales periods (BGF Retail official press release · Amorepacific newsroom). Check what is on now through the official channels

2. "Buy just one and the card of the member I want comes out" — RESCENE breads hold 1 card out of 27, and RIIZE breads 1 Ddibuseal out of 54, at random (BGF Retail official press release)

3. "The pop-up is at any store of that chain" — Collab pop-ups open at a few designated stores for a set period (BGF Retail official press release 2024-10-15·2026-06-15)

4. "Beauty collabs are in the store" — Both BTS editions were sold on the online Amoremall (아모레몰) (Amorepacific official newsroom)
