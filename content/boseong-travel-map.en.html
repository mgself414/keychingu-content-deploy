Boseong Travel Map — Full Meta Guide to Boseong Green Tea Fields for Foreign Tourists: 12 Signature Experiences

Subtitle: Korea's #1 Green Tea Fields, Deungnyangman Bay, and Sunrise. Seoul Outside City Series Episode 21

ISSUE 03 · #426 · 2026-05-26

🎯 Why the "Boseong Travel Map" Meta Guide?

For foreign visitors to Boseong (보성) — KTX transfer at Gwangju (광주) or Suncheon (순천) takes 4 hours + Korea's #1 green tea origin (since 1939). Key Highlights: (1) Daehan Dawon (대한다원) (Korea's #1 green tea field + Instagram signature spot) + Korea Tea Museum (한국차박물관) (2) Dahyangje Festival (다향제 축제) (May green tea festival) (3) Yulpo Seawater Green Tea Bath (율포 해수녹차탕) & Deungnyangman Bay (득량만) (4) Boseong Tea Plantation Light Festival (보성 차밭 빛축제) (Dec-Jan) + Connection to Gangjin (강진) #418.

This guide covers Boseong's (보성) 4 Pillars and 12 Signature Experiences.
