Busan Dongnae & Oncheonjang Guide — Hurshimchung Hot Springs, Free Foot Bath, Geumgang Park Cable Car, Chungnyeolsa Shrine (2026 Official Figures)

Subtitle: Hurshimchung 05:30–22:00, adult weekday ₩15,000 / open-air foot bath free / Geumgang Park cable car round trip ₩11,000, closed Mondays / Dongnae Eupseong History Museum and Chungnyeolsa Shrine free — per the VisitBusan, Hotel Nongshim and Dongnae District Office pages as of 2026-10-07. Dongnae Eupseong History Festival Oct 16–18

ISSUE 03 · #269 · 2026-05-20

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Dongnae and Oncheonjang let you string together a hot spring (Hurshimchung (허심청)), a free foot bath and the cable car at Geumgang Park (금강공원) on foot from a single stop, Oncheonjang Station (온천장역) on Busan Subway Line 1. Only Chungnyeolsa Shrine (충렬사) sits on Line 4, at Chungnyeolsa Station (충렬사역). The hours and fees below are values read on 2026-10-07 from the VisitBusan English page, the official Hotel Nongshim (호텔농심) site and the Dongnae District Office (동래구청) page; anything we could not read is marked "unconfirmed." We dropped lines from the older guide such as "Korea's No. 1 hot spring since 2000" and "Hurshimchung, Korea #1" because they have no source — VisitBusan describes Hurshimchung only as "the largest hot spring facility in the East."

♨ Hurshimchung — Hot Spring + Jjimjilbang

  • A hot spring facility inside Hotel Nongshim. Hot spring 05:30–22:00 (last entry 21:30), jjimjilbang 06:30–21:00, no closing days (Hotel Nongshim, official).
  • Fees: adult ₩15,000 on weekdays and ₩18,000 on weekends/holidays; elementary school ₩11,000 and ₩12,000; sauna clothes rental ₩4,000. Time limits: 4 hours for the hot spring, 6 hours for hot spring plus jjimjilbang, 7 hours maximum — beyond that, ₩2,000 per hour (official).
  • No entry with tattoos or skin conditions (official). Whether swimsuits are allowed and whether English guidance is offered are not on the official page, so they are unconfirmed — the Korean hot spring norm is to undress and bathe individually.
  • Address: 32 Oncheonjang-ro 107beon-gil. 9-minute walk from Oncheonjang Station Exit 1 (VisitBusan).

🦶 Open-Air Foot Bath — Free

  • An outdoor foot bath run by the Dongnae District Office. Free, at 21 Geumgangpark-ro 26beon-gil; inquiries 051-550-6601~6604 (District Office page).
  • Hours and closing days are not on the official page, and secondary sources disagree (closed Wed/Fri vs. Mon/Tue), so we don't list them. If it's open on your way to Hurshimchung, soak for 10–15 minutes.

🌲 Geumgang Park — Cable Car and the Gateway to Geumjeongsan

  • The park is free and open year-round. The cable car is closed Mondays; round trip is adult ₩11,000 · child ₩8,000 · senior ₩9,000, one way ₩7,000 · ₩5,000 · ₩6,000 (VisitBusan). Operating hours are not on the official page, so they are unconfirmed.
  • The park's botanical garden has been closed since 2026-01-01 until further notice (VisitBusan).
  • Trekking to Geumjeongsanseong Fortress (금정산성), on Geumjeongsan (금정산), starts at the top of the cable car. From Oncheonjang Station Exit 1 it is a 22-minute walk to the park entrance.

🏯 Dongnae Eupseong Fortress and History Museum · Chungnyeolsa Shrine

  • Dongnae Eupseong Fortress Site and History Museum: Tue–Sun 09:00–18:00, closed Mondays, free (VisitKorea). There is a miniature model of the fortress (VisitBusan). The museum's own hours and English guidance are unconfirmed.
  • Chungnyeolsa Shrine: A shrine honoring those who died defending Dongnae in the Imjin War. Apr–Sep 09:00–21:00, Oct–Mar 09:00–20:00, main hall and shop 09:00–18:00, free, open every day (VisitBusan). 1 minute from Chungnyeolsa Station Exit 1 on Line 4 — not Oncheonjang Station.

🥞 Dongnae Pajeon

  • Dongnae pajeon (동래파전) is Dongnae's signature dish, per VisitBusan. The one restaurant listed on the same page is Somunnan Dongnae Pajeon (소문난동래파전): 10:00–22:00, closed the first and third Mondays, 55 Geumgangpark-ro, 051-553-5464. No prices are posted.

🎉 Dongnae Eupseong History Festival — Oct 16–18

  • 32nd edition, 2026-10-16 (Fri) to 18 (Sun), around Dongnae Eupseong Fortress. Designated a 2026–27 Culture and Tourism Festival by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, with side events around Oncheonjang (per news reports — the District Office festival page would not open, so this is not yet officially confirmed). No festival named "Dongnae Hot Spring Festival" could be confirmed.

🚇 Getting There · Half-Day Route

  • Oncheonjang Station (Line 1) Exit 1 → free foot bath → Hurshimchung (2–3 hours in the hot spring) → Geumgang Park cable car (not on Mondays) → Dongnae pajeon. Half a day is enough.
  • Go to Chungnyeolsa Shrine separately, from Chungnyeolsa Station on Line 4. The Dongnae Eupseong Fortress History Museum is closed Mondays.
  • Travel times from Busan Station and Haeundae are not on the official pages, so we don't list them — check the route to Oncheonjang Station in a subway app.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Showing tattoos — Hurshimchung does not admit visitors with tattoos (official).

2. Cable car and history museum on Monday — both are closed Mondays. Chungnyeolsa Shrine, Hurshimchung and the park are open.

3. Going over 7 hours — Hurshimchung's maximum is 7 hours; beyond that, ₩2,000 per hour.

4. Looking for Chungnyeolsa Shrine from Oncheonjang Station — it is Exit 1 of Chungnyeolsa Station on Line 4.

5. Expecting the botanical garden — it has been closed since January 2026.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. How much for just the hot spring? — Adult ₩15,000 on weekdays, ₩18,000 on weekends. Add ₩4,000 for sauna clothes if you also use the jjimjilbang.

2. Can I go early in the morning? — Hurshimchung opens at 05:30. The jjimjilbang opens at 06:30.

3. A free taste of the hot spring experience? — The open-air foot bath (free). Hours are not posted, so go only if it's open.

4. How much is the cable car? — ₩11,000 round trip, ₩7,000 one way. Closed Mondays.

5. When is the festival? — Dongnae Eupseong History Festival, Oct 16–18 (2026).

6. Is there English guidance? — Neither Hurshimchung nor the history museum lists English guidance on its official page, so it is unconfirmed. Check the VisitBusan English page beforehand.
