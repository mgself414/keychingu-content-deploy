🎯 Why Busan Dongnae·Oncheonjang?

Busan's Dongnae (동래) = Korea's #1 2000-year-old hot spring city (an expansion of the #246 Busan Seomyeon (서면) sub-area). It offers authentic Korean hot spring culture and is a top destination for foreign enthusiasts. A deep dive into an area adjacent to Seomyeon (서면) and Haeundae (해운대).
