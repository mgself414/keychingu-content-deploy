⚠ 5 Common Mistakes for K-pop Fan 1-Day Itineraries

1. "Visiting SM Town (SM타운) is enough to see all of K-pop" misconception

SM Town (SM타운) (COEX (코엑스)) is often the top priority for international K-pop fans. However, the Apgujeong (압구정) K-pop Agency Street (SM (에스엠) · HYBE (하이브) · YG (와이지) · JYP (제이와이피) · Source Music (쏘스뮤직)), Hongdae (홍대) Trend Pot (트렌드팟) K-pop collaboration stores, and Myeongdong (명동) K-Beauty megastores often lead to higher satisfaction for international fans than SM Town (SM타운) alone. Just visiting COEX (코엑스) can be a waste of time.

2. Ignoring the location and timing for photo spots at Apgujeong (압구정) K-pop agency headquarters

The exterior photo spots for SM (에스엠) · HYBE (하이브) · YG (와이지) · JYP (제이와이피) headquarters vary in location, accessibility, and etiquette. HYBE Headquarters (본사) (Hanganjin, Yongsan-gu (용산구 한강진)) is the most recognized internationally but is separate from the Gangnam (강남) route — requiring a separate course. SM Headquarters (본사) (Seongsu (성수)) is in Seongsu-dong (성수동). YG Headquarters (본사) (Hapjeong (합정)) is in the Hongdae (홍대) area. Don't try to see all of them in one day; choose 1-2.

3. Unawareness of Trend Pot (트렌드팟) by Olive Young (올리브영)

Myeongdong (명동) Olive Young (올리브영) is often the top K-Beauty destination for international visitors. However, Hongdae (홍대) Trend Pot (트렌드팟) (a limited-edition store with K-pop collaborations like BTS (방탄소년단) · NewJeans (뉴진스) · SEVENTEEN (세븐틴)) allows direct purchase of exclusive merchandise not available in Myeongdong (명동). For K-pop fans, Myeongdong (명동) + Trend Pot (트렌드팟) is a must-do combination.

4. Time zone adjustment vs. K-pop fan itinerary mismatch

International K-pop fans need time to adjust to the time difference on their arrival day — a 09:00-21:00 course can be too much. For time zone adjustment day (Day 1), an afternoon start is recommended (e.g., just the Apgujeong (압구정) walk from [#19 K-POP Fan Guide](content/kpop-fan.html)); proceed with this full 1-day course on Day 2+.

5. Not combining Korean food (Hansik (한식)) and dessert spots with the K-pop fan itinerary

Focusing solely on K-pop and skipping meals or cafes can lead to fatigue. Combining Apgujeong (압구정) lunch (#206 Mutan (무탄)), Gangnam (강남) cafes (#142·#203 Per E Pièce (페르에피스)), and Hongdae (홍대) desserts (#205 K-Cafe Signatures) is essential for a truly fulfilling 1-day itinerary.

K-pop Fan Itinerary Etiquette · Precautions

Essential Etiquette:

1. Photos of agency headquarters only from the exterior — No entry inside, no photos of staff.

2. Yield to Korean fans in K-pop artist stores/lines — Prioritize Korean fan etiquette.

3. Trend Pot (트렌드팟) · SM Town (SM타운) limited edition merchandise has a per-person purchase limit — Check store guidelines.

4. K-pop fan etiquette — sounds · noise · occupying space — Do not block store pathways.

Precautions:

  • For K-pop agency headquarters exterior photo spots, no intrusive photography (company etiquette).
  • Merchandise stores often have English payment options available (Trend Pot (트렌드팟) · SM Town (SM타운)).
  • Check HYBE Insight (하이브 인사이트) operating hours in advance (exhibitions change frequently).

Ultimately, the K-pop Fan 1-Day Itinerary is:

09:00 AM Apgujeong (압구정) K-pop Agencies → 2:00 PM SM Town (SM타운) → 5:00 PM Gangnam (강남) Cafes · Desserts → 7:00 PM Hongdae (홍대) Trend Pot (트렌드팟) → 9:00 PM Nightlife — a signature time-based itinerary.

True satisfaction for international K-pop fans is determined more by Trend Pot (트렌드팟) limited editions than SM Town (SM타운). Skip Myeongdong (명동) and prioritize Hongdae (홍대) Trend Pot (트렌드팟).
