⚠ 5 Common Mistakes for K-pop Fan 1-Day Itineraries

1. "Visiting SM Town is enough to see all of K-pop" misconception

SM Town (COEX (코엑스)) is often the top priority for international K-pop fans. However, the Apgujeong (압구정) K-pop Agency Street (SM·HYBE·YG·JYP·Source Music) + Hongdae (홍대) Trend Pot (트렌드팟) K-pop collaboration stores + Myeongdong (명동) K-beauty megastores often lead to higher satisfaction for international fans than SM Town alone. Visiting only COEX (코엑스) can be a waste of time.

2. Not considering the location and timing for Apgujeong (압구정) K-pop agency headquarters photo spots

The exterior photo spots for SM·HYBE·YG·JYP headquarters vary in location, accessibility, and etiquette. HYBE Headquarters (Yongsan-gu (용산구) Hangangjin (한강진)) has the highest international recognition but is separate from the Gangnam (강남) route — requiring a separate course. SM Headquarters (Seongsu (성수)) is in Seongsu-dong (성수동). YG Headquarters (Hapjeong (합정)) is in the Hongdae (홍대) area. Don't try to see them all in one day; choose 1-2.

3. Unawareness of Trend Pot (트렌드팟 by Olive Young (올리브영))

Myeongdong (명동) Olive Young (올리브영) is the top priority for international K-beauty itineraries. However, Hongdae (홍대) Trend Pot (트렌드팟) (a limited-edition store with K-pop collaborations like BTS·NewJeans·SEVENTEEN) allows direct purchase of exclusive merchandise not available in Myeongdong (명동). For K-pop fans, Myeongdong (명동) + Trend Pot (트렌드팟) is a must-do combination.

4. Jet lag adjustment not matching K-pop fan itinerary times

International K-pop fans often need to adjust to jet lag on their first day of arrival — a 09:00 AM-09:00 PM course can be too much. It's recommended to start with an afternoon activity on Day 1 (jet lag adjustment day) (e.g., just the #19 KPOP Apgujeong (압구정) stroll), and proceed with this full 1-day course on Day 2+.

5. Not combining Korean food and dessert stores with the K-pop fan itinerary

Focusing only on K-pop and skipping meals or cafes can lead to fatigue. Combining Apgujeong (압구정) Lunch (#206 Mutan (무탄)) + Gangnam (강남) Cafes (#142·#203 Per E Pièce (페르에피스)) + Hongdae (홍대) Desserts (#205 K-Cafe Signatures) is essential for a truly fulfilling 1-day itinerary.

K-pop Fan Itinerary Etiquette·Precautions

Essential Etiquette:

1. Photos of agency headquarters only from the exterior — No entry inside, no photos of staff.

2. Yield in lines and for merchandise at K-pop artist stores — Korean fan priority etiquette.

3. Trend Pot (트렌드팟)·SM Town limited edition merchandise has a per-person purchase limit — Check store guidelines.

4. K-pop fan etiquette — sounds·noise·occupancy — Do not block store pathways.

Precautions:

  • For K-pop agency headquarters exterior photo spots, avoid camera covers or disruptive behavior (company etiquette).
  • Merchandise stores primarily accept English payments (Trend Pot (트렌드팟)·SM Town).
  • Check HYBE Insight operating hours in advance (exhibitions change frequently).
