⚠ 5 mistakes K-pop fans make in their daily movements

1. Recognition of “If you go to SM Town, you can see all K-pop”

The number one destination for foreign K-pop fans is SM Town (COEX). In reality, Apgujeong K-pop Company Street (SM·HYBE·YG·JYP·Source Music) + Hongdae Trend Pot K-pop Collaboration Store + Myeongdong K-beauty Megastore has higher foreign fan satisfaction than SM Town. Just going to COEX is a waste of time.

2. Apgujeong K-pop company headquarters photo spot location and time not taken into account

SM·HYBE·YG·JYP headquarters exterior photo spots vary in location, accessibility, and manners. HYBE Headquarters (Hangangjin, Yongsan-gu) is the most recognized by foreigners, but it is separated from the Gangnam route — a separate course. SM Headquarters (Seongsu) is located in Seongsu-dong. YG Headquarters (Hapjeong) is in the Hongdae area. Don't try to see them all in one day, but choose 1-2.

3. Trend Pot by Olive Young Miji

The number one K-beauty destination for foreigners is Olive Young in Myeongdong. In fact, Hongdae Trend Pot (limited store for K-pop collaborations such as BTS, New Jeans, Seventeen, etc.) allows you to directly purchase limited edition goods that you cannot buy in Myeong-dong. For K-pop fans, the Myeongdong + Trendpot bundle is a must.

4. Adjusting to time difference vs K-pop fan movement time not matching

K-pop fan Foreigners need to adjust to time difference on their first day of arrival — 09:00~21:00 course is difficult. Time lag day (Day 1) starts in the afternoon (#19 KPOP Apgujeong walk only), Day 2+ This 1-day full course is recommended.

5. Korean food and dessert stores are not connected to K-pop fan routes

Meal/cafe pass to only watch K-pop → fatigue. Apgujeong Lunch (#206 Mutan) + Gangnam Cafe (#142·#203 Per Epis) + Hongdae Dessert (#205 K-Cafe Signature) Must be combined for a truly one-day itinerary.
