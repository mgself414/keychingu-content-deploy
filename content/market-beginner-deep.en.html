Seoul Traditional Market Beginner's Guide — Gwangjang Market, Namdaemun Market, Tongin Market, Mangwon Market, Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market, Gyeongdong Market, Jungbu Market, Opening Hours and Closed Days

Subtitle: Gwangjang Market (광장시장, hours vary by shop) · Namdaemun Market (남대문시장, hours vary by shop) · Tongin Market (통인시장, 07:00~21:00, Dosirak Cafe Tong (도시락카페 통) 11:00~15:00) · Mangwon Market (망원시장) · Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진수산물도매시장, sales open year-round, auction closed on Sundays) · Gyeongdong Market (경동시장, 08:00~18:00, closed on alternate Sundays, varies by shop) · Jungbu Market (중부시장, 04:00~19:00, varies by shop) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide is for first-time visitors to traditional markets in Seoul. It gathers the opening hours, closed days and main goods of 7 Seoul markets in one table. The food and shops of each market are covered in that market's own guide: #582 Gwangjang Market, #581 Namdaemun Market, #589 Tongin Market, #596 Mangwon Market.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Gwangjang Market, Namdaemun Market and Mangwon Market are registered as having opening hours and closed days that differ from shop to shop.

📍 Jongno and Jung-gu — Gwangjang Market, Namdaemun Market, Tongin Market and Jungbu Market

Gwangjang Market (88 Changgyeonggung-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 창경궁로 88), Yeji-dong (예지동), ☎ 02-2267-0291) opened in 1905. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is now a general market trading silk fabrics and bolt cloth (hanbok), textiles, clothing, bedding, mother-of-pearl lacquerware, fruit and vegetables, dried seafood, fish, meat and more, and its busiest spot is the food market street where the East Gate, North Gate 2 and South Gate 1 meet.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Varies by shop.
  • Parking: Available.

Namdaemun Market (21 Namdaemunsijang 4-gil, Jung-gu (중구 남대문시장4길 21), Namchang-dong (남창동), ☎ 02-753-2805) is, according to Korea Tourism Organization data (citing the Namdaemun Special Tourist Zone), a wholesale and retail market centered on Sungnyemun with about 10,000 shops and about 1,700 kinds of goods. Oxtail soup restaurants, Galchi Alley (hairtail alley), Kalguksu Alley (knife-cut noodle alley) and vegetable hotteok are introduced as the market's specialties.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Varies by shop.
  • Parking: Available. Strollers: Not allowed.

Tongin Market (18 Jahamun-ro 15-gil, Jongno-gu (종로구 자하문로15길 18), Tongin-dong (통인동), ☎ 0507-1378-0940) grew out of a public market that opened in June 1941. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it has operated Dosirak Cafe Tong (도시락카페 '통(通)') since January 2012.

  • Opening hours: Tongin Market shops 07:00~21:00, Dosirak Cafe 11:00~15:00.
  • Closed days: Varies by shop.
  • Parking: Available.

Jungbu Market (35 Eulji-ro 36-gil, Jung-gu (중구 을지로36길 35), ☎ 02-2267-5617) opened on February 28, 1957. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is a market where you can buy dried seafood such as laver, wakame (sea mustard), yellow croaker, anchovy, squid, dried pollack and filefish jerky.

  • Opening hours: 04:00~19:00 (varies by shop).
  • Closed days: Varies by shop.
  • Parking: Not available.

📍 Mapo and Dongdaemun — Mangwon Market and Gyeongdong Market

Mangwon Market (27 Poeun-ro 6-gil, Mapo-gu (마포구 포은로6길 27), Mangwon-dong (망원동), ☎ 02-335-3591) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, a market selling street food, farm and livestock products and household goods, located near Mangwon Station on Subway Line 6.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Varies by shop.
  • Parking: Available.

Gyeongdong Market (3 Gosanja-ro 36-gil, Dongdaemun-gu (동대문구 고산자로36길 3), Jegi-dong (제기동), ☎ 0507-1311-8721) is a farm-products market that opened in June 1960. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, after the Gyeongdong Building was built in 1985, a ginseng market and a district of traditional herbal medicine shops appeared, and it now sells farm products, medicinal herbs, ginseng and seafood.

  • Opening hours: 08:00~18:00 (varies by shop).
  • Closed days: Alternate Sundays (varies by shop).
  • Parking: Not available.

📍 Dongjak — Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market

Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (674 Nodeul-ro, Dongjak-gu (동작구 노들로 674), ☎ 02-2254-8000~1) built a new wholesale market in Noryangjin in June 1971. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, about 250~300 tons of seafood are traded per day, with an auction hall, retail floor, live fish and shellfish on the 1st floor, and restaurants, dried fish and salted seafood stalls on the 2nd floor. The live fish auction is introduced as being held at 3:00.

  • Sales (wholesale and retail) hours: Premium 00:00~24:00, general 01:30~22:00, frozen 03:30~22:00, shellfish 01:00~22:00, dried fish 23:00~19:00, salted seafood 03:00~19:00.
  • Auction start: General category 00:00~, shellfish category 01:00~, premium fresh fish 01:30~, premium live fish and frozen category 03:00~.
  • Closed days: Sales are open year-round. The auction is closed every Sunday, January 1, and Seollal and Chuseok holidays.
  • Card payment: Available. Parking: Available (1,259 spaces).

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. At many markets, hours and closed days differ from shop to shop — Gwangjang Market, Namdaemun Market and Mangwon Market are registered as having opening hours and closed days that differ by shop.
  2. Some shops at Gyeongdong Market close on alternate Sundays — the Korea Tourism Organization registered information lists closing on alternate Sundays (varies by shop).
  3. There is no auction at Noryangjin on Sundays — wholesale and retail sales are open year-round, but the auction is closed on Sundays, January 1, and Seollal and Chuseok holidays.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which markets are open early in the morning?

A. According to Korea Tourism Organization registered information, Jungbu Market opens at 04:00 and Gyeongdong Market at 08:00 (both vary by shop), and Tongin Market shops from 07:00. Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market starts sales from 11 p.m. to midnight depending on the item. Hours are not registered for Gwangjang, Namdaemun and Mangwon markets because they vary by shop.

Q. Can I use a card at the markets?

A. Among the 7 places in this guide, the only one with card payment listed as available in the Korea Tourism Organization registered information is Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market. The other markets have no card payment entry registered.
