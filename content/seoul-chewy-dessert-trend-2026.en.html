2026 Seoul Chewy Dessert Trend — Yogurt Mochi and Pizza Seolgi, 10 Fall Spots Where Tteok Became Dessert

Subtitle: After Butter Tteok (#1233), what filled Seoul's social feeds next was fresh-fruit Yogurt Mochi (요거트모찌) and Pizza Seolgi (피자설기). Fall 2026 — when tteok (떡) turned from "holiday food" into a "line-up dessert" — the numbers the press cited, 5 yogurt mochi shops · 4 fusion tteok shops · 1 small-batch tteok shop, and how to try it first at a convenience store. Same-day eating · sellout hours · reservation-only shops, so foreign visitors don't make a wasted trip.

🎯 Why "Chewy Dessert"?

The basics of Korean tteok (떡) itself — injeolmi (인절미), songpyeon (송편), jeolpyeon (절편) — are covered by #42 Seoul Tteok Shops and #473 Korea Tteok Map. This piece is different — it looks only at the 2026 phenomenon of tteok becoming a dessert trend.

The numbers speak first. Blog mentions of "tteokji-sullye" (떡지순례, a play on "tteok" + "pilgrimage to a holy site") hit 1,077 in the past month alone, up 2,278% year-on-year, and the yogurt-chapssaltteok (요거트 찹쌀떡) that convenience store chain GS25 made with tteok cafe Hanjeongseon (한정선) sold 380,000 pieces in 13 days after launch (Asia Economy, 2026-09-22). Pizza Seolgi (피자설기) — baekseolgi (백설기) topped with tomato sauce and cheese — started in Busan and saw Google search interest jump more than 10x in 2 weeks (Money Today, 2026-09-05). Meanwhile, the number of traditional tteok shops and rice mills in Seoul fell from 1,186 at the end of 2021 to 1,021 in September 2026 — old-school tteok shops are shrinking while lines form only at the new fusion ones.

The trend cycle is short, too. After Butter Tteok (버터떡), Dubai Chewy Cookie (두쫀쿠), and Gaeseong-juak (개성주악), now Pizza Seolgi — some analyses say dessert trends now flip to the next item roughly every 15 days (Daum News, 2026-09-25). So this piece isn't a list of "forever must-visits" — it's a list of what to eat this fall, right now.

⚠ Valid as of: confirmed late September 2026. Desserts with fresh fruit and yogurt commonly sell out early depending on the day's stock, and trend shops change their menus often. Shop existence and addresses were confirmed via Naver Map listings; menu items marked "per Instagram notice" are noted as such — check the shop's latest Instagram post before visiting.

🍓 Yogurt Mochi (요거트모찌) — Fresh Fruit and Yogurt Inside Chapssaltteok

Yogurt mochi is tteok made by wrapping fresh fruit (kiwi, strawberry, mango, etc.) and yogurt cream inside a thin glutinous-rice skin. A homemade version made with rice paper first went viral on social media abroad, and it caught on again in Korea once tteok cafes started selling it ready-made.

  • Hanjeongseon (한정선), Seongsu: the shop at the center of this trend. Its signature is chapssaltteok with a whole piece of fruit inside — gold kiwi, daewang strawberry — and it's well-known enough to have launched a collab with GS25. On weekend afternoons, kiosk waitlist sign-ups have reportedly closed by around 4:30 pm — a weekday morning or right at opening is more realistic. Walk from Seongsu Station (성수역). Same neighborhood as the Seongsu character pop-ups (#1403), so it's easy to combine into a half-day trip.
  • Oneul Mochi (오늘모찌), Mokdong: the one shop with a confirmed price — fruit mochi at about ₩3,000 each. It's a residential area near Omokgyo Station (오목교역), off the usual tourist route, but less crowded than Seongsu. Closed Sundays, no parking.
  • Ssalblanc (쌀블랑), Gangdong: a tteok-dessert cafe known for cream tteok (크림떡) like New York cheese-cream tteok. Yogurt mochi is a menu item posted by a local Instagram account, so confirm before visiting whether it's a standing item.
  • 1020 Room (1020룸), Euljiro · Mallangdang (말랑당), Oedae-ap: shops listed in a local account's "Seoul Yogurt Mochi 14" post (2,600+ likes). Both are registered on Naver Map as cafe/dessert spots, and their yogurt mochi is sold per Instagram notice. Euljiro connects with the #1311 Sindang · Euljiro route.

🍕 Pizza Seolgi (피자설기) and Fusion Tteok — Tomato Sauce and Cheese on Baekseolgi

Pizza seolgi is baekseolgi (백설기, white rice tteok) topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and pepperoni. It started at a tteok shop in Busan's Yeongdo-gu (영도구) and spread through social media; now Seoul neighborhood tteok shops each put out their own version. One eating tip — many reviews say microwaving it for about 30 seconds brings out the chewy dough and melted-cheese texture.

  • Baekgun Tteokbang (백군떡방), Pungnap-dong, Songpa: pizza seolgi and angko-jeolpyeon (앙꼬절편). Listed with the same hours (08:00–20:00) by two separate local accounts. It's a neighborhood tteok shop that opens early, so a morning visit means less sellout risk.
  • Pyeon (편), Guui, Gwangjin: registered on Naver as a tteok cafe. Pizza seolgi sales are per local-account notice.
  • Sweet Plume Cake × Seolgi Sodam (스윗플룸케이크×설기소담), Jangan-dong, Dongdaemun: a collab shop between a cake brand and a tteok shop. It best represents this year's blurring line between cake and tteok.
  • Seogo (서고), Bangbae, Seocho: a small tteok shop that sells by reservation only (Kakao Channel "Tteokjip Seogo," 떡집서고). You can't just walk in and buy — if you don't have a Korean phone number, one option is asking your accommodation's front desk to make the reservation for you.

🎁 Small-Batch Tteok Shop and Convenience Store — The Two Easiest Entry Points

  • Goem (굄), Bomun, Seongbuk: a shop that makes tteok for first-birthday parties and anniversaries, but it also does small-batch packs for tea tables, so travelers can buy just one or two. Mon–Sat 10:00–19:00, Sunday for reservation-holders only. Same area as the Seongbuk-dong alley walk (#1345).
  • GS25 × Hanjeongseon Yogurt Chapssaltteok: if you don't have time to queue, try it at a convenience store first. It was popular enough at launch that initial stock at Gangnam and Incheon Airport stores sold out early, and stock now varies by location. Look in the refrigerated dessert corner. For convenience-store food in general, see #1262.

🗓 Half-Day Course — One Loop East

Seongsu → Guui → Pungnap: morning at Hanjeongseon in Seongsu (right after opening) → Line 2 to Guui Station for Pyeon (pizza seolgi) → bus or subway to Baekgun Tteokbang in Pungnap-dong. One loop heading east. In the afternoon, continue to Jamsil (#1404 Lotte World) or the fall foliage at Olympic Park (#273).

Euljiro side trip: 1020 Room at Euljiro 3-ga (을지로3가) → the market and tteokbokki alley of Sindang-dong (신당동) (#1311).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Buying some and saving it for the next day — mochi with fresh fruit and yogurt is meant to be eaten same-day, and even refrigerated, it should be finished within 2 days including the purchase date (per Hanjeongseon's in-store guidance). It doesn't work as a gift to bring home.

2. Showing up late in the afternoon — popular shops commonly sell out by morning or early afternoon. Notices repeatedly say the day's available quantity depends on that day's fruit delivery.

3. Just showing up at a reservation-only shop — some shops, like Seogo, sell by reservation only. Check the "reservation only" note in the table first.

⚙ 3 FAQs

1. If I can only try one? — Hanjeongseon's whole-fruit chapssaltteok. If the line feels like too much, try the GS25 collab version first.

2. How is it different from Butter Tteok? — Butter Tteok (#1233) is the savory, warm style — grilled tteok with butter added. Yogurt mochi is the cold, fresh-and-tangy style. Pizza seolgi is the salty, meal-like style. All three are 2026 trends, so it's worth comparing them side by side.

3. Card payment and English menus? — Unconfirmed for each shop. Small tteok shops rarely have English menus, so it's faster to save a screenshot of the shop name and a menu photo to show the staff.
