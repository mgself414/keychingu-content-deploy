🏛 5 Pillars Deep Dive

① Hahoe Village (하회마을) (UNESCO 2010 · Korea's #1 Yangban (양반))

  • Signature: UNESCO 2010 · Korea's #1 yangban (양반) village (traditional Korean aristocratic class) · 600+ year-old yangban (양반) village · Ryu Clan Head House
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (English · Multilingual Full Set)
  • Time: 09:00~18:00 · ₩5,000 (~$3.7 / ~26 RMB / ~550 JPY)

② Dosan Seowon (도산서원) (Toegye Yi Hwang · 1501-1570)

  • Signature: Toegye Yi Hwang (퇴계 이황) (Korea's #1 Confucian scholar enthusiast matrix #1) · seowon (서원) (Confucian academy) established 1560~
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (English · Enthusiast)
  • Time: 09:00~18:00 · ₩1,500 (~$1.1 / ~8 RMB / ~165 JPY)

③ Yangdong Village (양동마을) (UNESCO 2010)

  • Signature: Near Gyeongju (경주) · Korea's Yangban (양반) Matrix #2 (Hahoe (하회) + Yangdong (양동) = UNESCO Full Set)
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (English · Culture Enthusiast)

④ Andong Jjimdak (안동 찜닭) + Gan-godeungeo (안동 간고등어) (Signature)

  • Signature: Korea's #1 jjimdak (찜닭) signature (braised chicken, a savory and slightly sweet dish) · Andong gan-godeungeo (안동 간고등어) (salted mackerel, often grilled) + hanjeongsik (한정식) (traditional Korean full-course meal)
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (Not too spicy · Foreigner entry-level)
  • Price: Jjimdak (찜닭) = ₩25,000-₩45,000 (~$18.5-$33 / ~130-235 RMB / ~2,750-4,950 JPY) for 2-3 people

⑤ Overnight Stay (Hanok (한옥) Guesthouse)

  • Signature: Hahoe Village (하회마을) hanok (한옥) (traditional Korean house) + overnight stay = Foreigner Enthusiast Matrix #1
  • Price: Hanok (한옥) overnight = ₩100,000-₩250,000 (~$74-$185 / ~520-1,300 RMB / ~11,000-27,500 JPY) per night

⚠ 5 Common Foreigner Mistakes

1. "Andong (안동) = unknown": UNESCO 2010 Hahoe Village (하회마을) · Korea's Yangban (양반) Matrix #1.

2. "Hahoe Village (하회마을) = ordinary village": UNESCO + 600+ year-old yangban (양반) + Ryu Clan Head House.

3. "Dosan Seowon (도산서원) = ordinary seowon (서원)": Toegye Yi Hwang (퇴계 이황) 1501-1570 · Korea's #1 Confucian scholar.

4. "Jjimdak (찜닭) = same everywhere": Korea's #1 jjimdak (찜닭) signature matrix · different matrices.

5. "Byeolsingut Talnori (별신굿탈놀이) = ordinary performance": 1980 Intangible Cultural Heritage · UNESCO Enthusiast.

⚠ Medical, Safety, Dietary Disclaimer

Andong (안동) = safe · enthusiast-friendly. Jjimdak (찜닭) contains soy and salted shrimp allergens; please inform staff in advance. Emergency calls: ☎ 1339 (Medical) · 1330 (Tourism).
