7 Spots in Eunpyeong and Bukhansan — Bukhansanseong Course, Hanok Village, Jinkwansa Temple, Seooreung Royal Tombs, with Bus Numbers and Closing Times

Subtitle: For travelers who come to Eunpyeong on Subway Line 3, this guide groups 2 spots on Bukhansan, 4 spots around the hanok village in Jingwan-dong, and 1 spot next to Eunpyeong, Seooreung Royal Tombs. Bukhansan has designated entry hours, so trail entrances are closed from 16:00 in December–February and from 17:00 in March–November (treat November and March as 16:00). The Bukhansanseong Course is 3.4 km from the Bukhansanseong Visitor Support Center to Baegundae Peak, takes 2 hours 40 minutes, and has difficulty "medium". Take bus 704 from Gupabal Station and get off at "Bukhansanseong Entrance". Eunpyeong History & Hanok Museum is open 09:00–18:00, closed Mondays, and costs 1,000 won for adults. The temple food experience at Jinkwansa Temple costs 80,000 won and is run in Korean only. Seooreung Royal Tombs is in Goyang-si, Gyeonggi-do, and is open 07:00–17:30 from November to January and closed on Mondays. The values were read on 2026-11-25.

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 7

This list is made for foreign travelers who come to Eunpyeong on Subway Line 3. Use it when you climb the mountain or walk the Dulle-gil trail at the northern end of Eunpyeong-gu (은평구), and when you see the hanok village, the temple and the royal tombs.

The spots are divided into three groups.

  • Bukhansan (2 spots): Bukhansanseong Course (북한산성 코스), Bukhansan Dulle-gil (북한산 둘레길) Eunpyeong sections (Sections 7–10).
  • Around the hanok village in Jingwan-dong (4 spots): Eunpyeong Hanok Village (은평한옥마을), Eunpyeong History & Hanok Museum (은평역사한옥박물관), Samgaksan Geumam Art Museum (삼각산금암미술관), Jinkwansa Temple (진관사).
  • Next to Eunpyeong (1 spot): Seooreung Royal Tombs (서오릉).

The address of Seooreung Royal Tombs is not Eunpyeong-gu, Seoul, but Deogyang-gu, Goyang-si (고양시 덕양구), Gyeonggi-do. It is right next to Eunpyeong, and the buses that go there leave from Line 3 Nokbeon Station (녹번역) and Hongje Station (홍제역) and Line 6 Yeokchon Station (역촌역) and Gusan Station (구산역), so it is grouped in this issue.

The seven spots in this issue are reached by bus from a subway station. So for every spot this issue first writes down "which station, which bus number, and which stop to get off at". Closing times also differ from spot to spot and change with the season in some places, so they are set out separately.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours, fares and directions are values read on 2026-11-25. Sources are Korea National Park Service (국립공원공단), Eunpyeong-gu Office (Eunpyeong History & Hanok Museum and Eunpyeong Culture & Tourism), the Jinkwansa Temple website, the templestay reservation website (templestay.com), the Royal Palaces and Tombs Center (궁능유적본부) of the Korea Heritage Service, and the website of the GTX-A operator. The line on when Eunpyeong Hanok Village was built is a value from the Seoul Hanok Portal (서울한옥포털) of the Seoul Metropolitan Government, and that line says so. Some addresses and some business hours are Kakao Map values, and those lines say "Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25)". Fares are adult list prices. The Korea National Park Service transport guide and Dulle-gil section guide carry no update date. Check bus numbers and stop names once more in a map app before you leave. Operating hours and fares can change.

⚠ 3 Easy Mistakes

  1. You cannot enter Bukhansan at any time you like. The Bukhansan table in the official Korea National Park Service guide (read 2026-11-25) gives entry at 04:00 and closure at 16:00 in the winter season (December–February). The summer season (March–November) is entry at 04:00 and closure at 17:00. After the closing time you cannot go up from the trail entrance. The explanatory text under the same Park Service table gives the winter season as November–March and the summer season as April–October, which differs from the table header (December–February / March–November). For November and March, plan to pass the entrance before 16:00.
  2. Seooreung Royal Tombs is not a place you reach from Gupabal Station. The public transportation line in the official Royal Palaces and Tombs Center guide (read 2026-11-25) does not include Gupabal Station. From the central bus stop at Line 3 Hongje Station Exit 2 or from Nokbeon Station Exit 4 (toward Eunpyeong-gu Office), take bus 702A, 702B or 707 and get off at "Seooreung Entrance". The address is also Deogyang-gu, Goyang-si, Gyeonggi-do.
  3. Eunpyeong Hanok Village is not a tourist facility but homes where people live. The Seoul Hanok Portal introduces it as a "residential complex exclusively for new-style hanok". People live in every house. The places with fixed closing times are the museum and the art museum inside the village. The museum closes at 18:00 and entry ends at 17:00.

🚦 Before You Walk — Common Safety and Manners

  • On a mountain day, look at the control information first. The Korea National Park Service course guide says to be sure to check the designated entry-hours system (입산시간지정제) and the trail control status before visiting. Controlled sections and dates change with notices.
  • Come down before sunset. The 2 hours 40 minutes in the Park Service guide was read as the value for the way up (the Park Service guide gives no one-way or round-trip marking). Add the time to come down and leave the center in the morning. This is this issue's recommendation.
  • Walk quietly in the hanok village. This is this issue's recommendation. Do not look inside the gates of houses or enter yards, and do not make loud noise in front of houses.

① Bukhansan — 2 Spots

According to the official introduction of Korea National Park Service (read 2026-11-25), Bukhansan National Park (북한산국립공원) was designated in 1983 as Korea's 15th national park. The area is 76.922㎢. With Uiryeong (우이령) as the boundary, the north is the Dobongsan area and the south is the Bukhansan area. The Park Service says it has "Bukhansanseong Fortress with 2,000 years of history" and "more than 100 temples and hermitages". On the Eunpyeong side, the Bukhansanseong Visitor Support Center (북한산성탐방지원센터) is the starting point of the mountain path, and the Dulle-gil trail runs along the foot of the mountain.

Bukhansanseong Course — From the Bukhansanseong Visitor Support Center to Baegundae Peak (Bukhansanseong Course)

  • Section: per the official Korea National Park Service course guide (read 2026-11-25), it is Bukhansanseong Visitor Support Center – Daeseomun Gate (대서문) – Borisa Temple (보리사) – Baegundae Peak (백운대).
  • Distance: 3.4 km.
  • Time it takes: 2 hours 40 minutes. The schedule is written as "day trip".
  • Difficulty: medium.
  • How to read the time: the section in the Park Service guide is one direction, from the center to Baegundae Peak. The Park Service guide gives no one-way or round-trip marking. This issue reads the 2 hours 40 minutes as the value for the way up. Plan the time to come down by adding it separately.
  • Entry hours: per the official Korea National Park Service designated entry-hours system guide (read 2026-11-25), the section from Bukhansanseong Entrance to Wimun Gate is 04:00–16:00 in the winter season (December–February) and 04:00–17:00 in the summer season (March–November) (treat November and March as 16:00). 16:00 and 17:00 are the times the entrance is closed.
  • Starting point address: Bukhansanseong Visitor Support Center, 45 Daeseomun-gil (대서문길), Eunpyeong-gu, Seoul.
  • How to get there: per the official Korea National Park Service transport guide (read 2026-11-25), take bus 704 from Line 3 Gupabal Station and get off at "Bukhansanseong Entrance". The Dulle-gil Section 10 guide says to take the same bus 704 from Gupabal Station Exit 1.
  • Parking: Bukhansanseong Parking Lots 1 and 2 (280, 278 Jingwan-dong (진관동), Eunpyeong-gu, Seoul).
  • Inquiries: Bukhansan National Park Office 02-909-0497.
  • Timing: in December–February you must pass the entrance before 16:00. Add the time to come down to the 2 hours 40 minutes read as the way-up value, and in winter leave the center in the morning. This is this issue's recommendation.

Bukhansan Dulle-gil Eunpyeong Sections — Sections 7–10 (Bukhansan Dulle-gil, Sections 7–10)

The official Korea National Park Service Dulle-gil guide (read 2026-11-25) introduces the Bukhansan Dulle-gil as "a low-ground level walking trail built so that you can walk gently along the foot of Bukhansan". The total length is 71.8 km, and it is divided into 21 themes. Four sections that pass through the Eunpyeong side were chosen.

Trailheads and buses (per the Korea National Park Service section guide)

  • Sections 7 and 8 (top of Bukhansan Eco Park): take 7022 or 7211 from across Bulgwang Station Exit 2, get off at "Dokbakgol", and walk 7 minutes.
  • Sections 8 and 9 (in front of Jingwan Eco Bridge): take 7211 from Yeonsinnae Station Exit 3, get off at "Jingwansa Entrance (진관사입구)", and walk 15 minutes.
  • Sections 9 and 10 (in front of Bangpae Training Unit): take 704 from Gupabal Station Exit 1, get off at "Ipgok Samgeori", and walk 5 minutes.
  • Section 10 (Hyojadong public cemetery): take 704 from Gupabal Station Exit 1, get off at "Hyojadong Maeul Geumgo (효자동마을금고)", and walk 5 minutes.

  • How to choose: if it is your first walk, link Sections 9 and 10, which have difficulty "easy". The two sections together are 5.0 km, about 2 hours 30 minutes.
  • Uiryeong-gil: the Uiryeong-gil (우이령길) of the Dulle-gil is run by reservation on weekends and public holidays. Its starting point is in Gangbuk-gu (강북구), Seoul, so it is not an Eunpyeong section. Reservations are taken on the Korea National Park Service reservation website and by phone (1670-9202).

② Around the Hanok Village in Jingwan-dong — 4 Spots

The hanok village, the museum, the art museum and the temple are gathered in one place in Jingwan-dong. The bus numbers you take overlap too. The stop name in the official guide of Eunpyeong History & Hanok Museum (read 2026-11-25) is "Hanago, Jingwansa, Samcheonsa Entrance".

Eunpyeong Hanok Village (Eunpyeong Hanok Village)

  • What it is: per the official Eunpyeong-gu Office guide (read 2026-11-25), it is a designated hanok zone (한옥지정구역) across from Hana Academy Seoul (하나고등학교). The area is 65,500㎡ and the scale of development is 156 hanok lots. The houses were built at 2 stories or lower. The land was supplied by SH Corporation (SH공사).
  • When it was made: according to the official Eunpyeong-gu Office guide, the development plan was reviewed in November 2010, and in October 2012 the Seoul Metropolitan Government designated it a special building zone. Land sales ran from September 2012 to November 2014. The Seoul Hanok Portal of the Seoul Metropolitan Government says it was built in December 2014.
  • That people live there: these are houses where residents actually live. You can walk the alleys, but you cannot see inside the houses. Walk quietly and do not photograph inside the gates. This is this issue's recommendation.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25): 7-10 Yeonseo-ro 50-gil (연서로50길), Eunpyeong-gu, Seoul.
  • How to get there: per the official guide of Eunpyeong History & Hanok Museum, take bus 701, 709, 7723 or 7211 and get off at "Hanago, Jingwansa, Samcheonsa Entrance". From Yeonsinnae Station (Lines 3·6) Exit 3, 701, 709 or 7211 takes about 15 minutes. From the Gupabal Station transfer center (Exit 4), 7723 takes about 10 minutes. Bus 7211 from Gupabal Station Exit 2 also takes about 10 minutes.
  • Surroundings: the Seoul Hanok Portal introduces this area as having Eunpyeong History & Hanok Museum, Jinkwansa Temple and Samgaksan Geumam Art Museum, and as easy to reach Bukhansan Dulle-gil Section 9.

Eunpyeong History & Hanok Museum (Eunpyeong History & Hanok Museum)

This is a museum run by Eunpyeong-gu Office. The values below are the 2026-11-25 notation of the official museum usage guide. The official Eunpyeong-gu Office guide gives the opening as October 2014.

  • Visiting hours: 09:00–18:00. Entry closes at 17:00. The hours in the official usage guide are one set.
  • Closing days: every Monday, January 1, the Lunar New Year and Chuseok holidays. If Monday is a public holiday, it closes on Tuesday.
  • Fare: individual adult 1,000 won. Elementary, middle and high school students and soldiers pay 500 won. The fare table has no separate category for foreigners.
  • Free admission: preschool children age 6 and under, and people age 65 and over.
  • Free day: the last Wednesday of every month, "Culture Day" (문화가 있는 날).
  • Address: 8 Yeonseo-ro 50-gil, Eunpyeong-gu, Seoul (Jingwan-dong). Inquiries 02-351-8556.
  • How to get there: a 3 min walk from the "Hanago, Jingwansa, Samcheonsa Entrance" stop. If you came by bus 7211 from Gupabal Station Exit 2, cross the crosswalk and walk 3 minutes.
  • Timing: entry ends at 17:00. Go in before Jinkwansa Temple and the hanok village alleys.

Samgaksan Geumam Art Museum (Samgaksan Geumam Art Museum)

  • What it is: per the official Eunpyeong-gu Office guide (read 2026-11-25), it is a cultural complex space that renovated a trial hanok (시험한옥) into an art museum. It exhibits everything from antique furniture to contemporary works. It opened in April 2018.
  • Address: 21-2 Jingwan-gil, Eunpyeong-gu, Seoul. Phone 02-351-8554.
  • Opening hours: Tuesday–Sunday 10:00–18:00, with 12:00–13:00 a break (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25). Monday is listed as a closing day.
  • How to get there: the address registered on Kakao Map is 21-2 Jingwan-gil, Eunpyeong-gu, Seoul. It is inside Eunpyeong Hanok Village, so stop by together when you walk the hanok village.
  • Timing: the museum opens at 09:00 and the art museum at 10:00. See the museum first and then go to the art museum. Avoid 12:00–13:00.

Jinkwansa Temple (Jinkwansa Temple)

  • What it is: the official history of Jinkwansa Temple (read 2026-11-25) says that the 8th king of Goryeo, Hyeonjong (현종), founded it in 1011 for the Great Monk Jingwan (진관대사). Construction began in the autumn of 1011 and finished in the autumn of 1012. It was ruined by bombing in the Korean War and then restored. The official introduction of Jinkwansa Temple lists this temple as the western one among "one of the four famous temples near Seoul".
  • Address: 73 Jingwan-gil, Eunpyeong-gu, Seoul. Phone 02-359-8410.
  • Temple food experience (Temple food experience): the official guide of the templestay reservation website (templestay.com) (read 2026-11-25) lists a one-day program, "temple food experience". You eat the monastic meal (발우공양, Balwoogongyang). You arrive from 10:30 and it ends at 14:00.
  • Experience fare and language: it is 80,000 won and is run in Korean only. The reservation website (templestay.com) says this program is run in Korean for Korean participants.
  • Reservation: reserve by 3 days before the start. It can be canceled if fewer than 15 people apply. The schedule changes according to the temple's circumstances.
  • How to get there: the official Jinkwansa Temple website says to take 7211 or 701 from Yeonsinnae Station, or 7211 or 7723 from Gupabal Station, and get off at "Hanago". The templestay reservation website says to take 704 from Gupabal Station Exit 4, get off at "Jingwansa Ap", and walk 10 minutes.
  • Timing: the temple food experience starts at 10:30, so go to Jinkwansa Temple first that day. The experience ends at 14:00, and entry to the museum closes at 17:00.

③ Next to Eunpyeong — 1 Spot

Seooreung Royal Tombs (Seooreung Royal Tombs, Goyang)

This is a place where five Joseon royal tombs are gathered. It is right next to Eunpyeong-gu, but the address is in Gyeonggi-do. The values below are the 2026-11-25 notation of the official guide of the Royal Palaces and Tombs Center of the Korea Heritage Service.

  • Address: 334-32 Seooreung-ro (서오릉로), Deogyang-gu, Goyang-si, Gyeonggi-do. Inquiries Joseon Royal Tombs Western District Office (조선왕릉서부지구관리소) 02-359-0090.
  • The five tombs: Gyeongneung (경릉) (posthumously honored Deokjong and Queen Sohye), Changneung (창릉) (8th king Yejong and Queen Ansun), Myeongneung (명릉) (19th king Sukjong and Queens Inhyeon and Inwon), Ingneung (익릉) (Sukjong's queen Ingyeong), Hongneung (홍릉) (21st king Yeongjo's queen Jeongseong).
  • World Heritage: 40 Royal Tombs of the Joseon Dynasty (조선왕릉) were inscribed as UNESCO World Heritage (세계유산) on June 30, 2009. The five tombs of Seooreung are included.

  • Timing: in November–January entry ends at 16:30. Do not put it late in the afternoon of the day you saw the hanok village area; put it on another day in the morning, or as the first stop of the day.

❄ What Changes in Winter (December–February)

  • The Bukhansan entrance closing moves up to 16:00. The closing time in December–February is 16:00. In March–November it is 17:00 (treat November and March as 16:00).
  • Seooreung Royal Tombs closes early. In November–January it closes at 17:30 and entry ends at 16:30. In February it closes at 18:00 and entry ends at 17:00.
  • The museum hours are one set. The hours in the official usage guide of Eunpyeong History & Hanok Museum are 09:00–18:00 with last entry at 17:00. It is closed on January 1 and during the Lunar New Year holidays.
  • Look at notices for controlled sections. In the official Korea National Park Service control information list seen on 2026-11-25, no notice closing the Bukhansanseong Course was posted. Control changes with notices, so open the control information page on the morning you go.
  • The paths freeze. This is this issue's recommendation. After snow, rocks and stairs are slippery. In winter, Dulle-gil Sections 9 and 10, which have difficulty "easy", are easier to walk than the course to the summit.

⚙ 3 FAQs

  1. It is my first time hiking. Where should I walk? — Choose Dulle-gil Section 9 Masil-gil (1.5 km, about 45 min, difficulty easy) and Section 10 Naesimyoyeok-gil (3.5 km, about 1 hour 45 min, difficulty easy). Korea National Park Service introduces the Dulle-gil as a low-ground level walking trail. The Bukhansanseong Course is 3.4 km, 2 hours 40 minutes, difficulty "medium". If it is your first time, walk the Dulle-gil first. This is this issue's recommendation.
  2. How far can I go from Gupabal Station? — Bus 704 stops at "Bukhansanseong Entrance" of the Bukhansanseong Course and at "Ipgok Samgeori" and "Hyojadong Maeul Geumgo" of the Dulle-gil. Bus 7723 at the transfer center (Exit 4) and bus 7211 at Exit 2 reach the stop in front of the hanok village and the museum in about 10 minutes. For Seooreung Royal Tombs, you take the bus not from Gupabal Station but from Nokbeon, Hongje, Yeokchon, Gusan and Wondang Stations.
  3. Where can I go on a Monday? — Eunpyeong History & Hanok Museum and Seooreung Royal Tombs are closed on Mondays. If Monday is a public holiday, the museum is closed on Tuesday, and Seooreung Royal Tombs is closed on the next first non-holiday. You can walk the Bukhansanseong Course, the Dulle-gil and the hanok village alleys regardless of the day of the week.

🗺 Routes — Half a Day and a Full Day

The order is set to match the opening and closing time of each spot.

Half Day A — Around the Hanok Village in Jingwan-dong (Avoid Mondays)

  1. From the Gupabal Station transfer center (Exit 4), bus 7723 → "Hanago, Jingwansa, Samcheonsa Entrance" (about 10 min).
  2. Eunpyeong History & Hanok Museum (3 min walk from the stop). It opens at 09:00. Adult 1,000 won.
  3. Samgaksan Geumam Art Museum. Per the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25) it opens at 10:00. It is closed 12:00–13:00.
  4. The alleys of Eunpyeong Hanok Village. Walk quietly.
  5. Jinkwansa Temple (73 Jingwan-gil).
  6. Check the stop for the way back in a map app.

Half Day B — Dulle-gil Sections 9 and 10

  1. From Yeonsinnae Station Exit 3, bus 7211 → "Jingwansa Entrance" (the name in the Dulle-gil guide) → in front of Jingwan Eco Bridge (15 min walk from the stop).
  2. Section 9 Masil-gil, 1.5 km, about 45 min.
  3. At Bangpae Gyoyukdae Ap (방패교육대 앞), Section 10 Naesimyoyeok-gil, 3.5 km, about 1 hour 45 min.
  4. Finish at the Hyojadong public cemetery. Check the stop for the way back in a map app.

Full Day — Bukhansanseong Course (Leave in the Morning)

When What to do Time to match
Morning Bus 704 from Gupabal Station → "Bukhansanseong Entrance" → Bukhansanseong Visitor Support Center Entry from 04:00
Morning to midday Center → Daeseomun Gate → Borisa Temple → Baegundae Peak 3.4 km, 2 hours 40 min, difficulty medium
Midday to afternoon Come down the same way Reach the center before sunset (this issue's recommendation)
Evening Go via Gupabal Station to Line 3 Yeonsinnae Station Check the stop for the way back in a map app

Half Day C — Seooreung Royal Tombs (Avoid Mondays)

  1. From Line 3 Nokbeon Station Exit 4 (toward Eunpyeong-gu Office), bus 702A, 702B or 707 → "Seooreung Entrance".
  2. Go in front of the main information board in time for the 10:30 on-site commentary. It takes about 1 hour.
  3. Walk the five tombs. In November–January it closes at 17:30.
  4. Check the stop for the way back in a map app.

If you have two days, split them into one day for the Bukhansanseong Course and one day for Seooreung Royal Tombs (morning) and the hanok village area (afternoon). Avoid Monday on the second day.
