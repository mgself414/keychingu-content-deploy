⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Transfer = Other route = New payment": T-money transfer within 30 minutes Free. 4 Transfers are OK. Same card tag required.

2. "Airport Railroad = Direct Only": Airport Railroad Type 2 — Direct (Express, KRW 9,500, 43 minutes) + Regular (KRW 4,250, 58 minutes). MZ · Budget = General OK. 14kg+ carrier = direct.

3. "Last train = midnight": Last train 23:30~00:30 (varies by route and station). It's dangerous to assume it's OK at 1 a.m. Must depart 30 minutes before the last train.

4. "Line 9 = regular cars only": Line 9 Express/Regular 2 types. Express Gimpo Airport → Jamsil 25 minutes / Regular 50 minutes. For foreigners, in the long term, express is number one. However, the express does not stop at some stations.

5. "Ticketing for foreigners = difficult": One-time T-money ticket machine, English OK + refundable deposit of 500 won. T-money rechargeable (convenience store/station) foreigner-friendly. Mobile T-money (Naver Pay·Toss·KakaoPay payment OK 2024 expansion).

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. Issuance of T-money for foreigners: Convenience store (CU, GS25, 7-Eleven, E-Mart 24) 4,000 won card + recharge. The refund is for the card balance of 500 won and the deposit is non-refundable.

2. Mobile T-money: Android NFC + Naver Pay·KakaoPay·Toss payment OK (expanded in 2024). iPhone not supported.

3. Transfer rule: T-money tag Transfer to other routes/buses within 30 minutes Free (maximum 4 transfers). Re-entry on the same route is allowed once.

4. Fee: Basic KRW 1,400 (up to 10 km) + KRW 100 additional per 5 km. Foreigners general route 1,400~2,400 won.

5. Operation hours: Usually 05:30~24:00. The last train varies by route and station — real-time checking of Naver Map and Kakao Map is required.

6. Foreigner-friendly: Lines 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 9, Airport Railroad = Full set of English, Chinese, Japanese, KR 4 ​​lang information. Outer routes only include KR and EN.

7. Card payment: Credit card (overseas issued Visa·Master·JCB OK) direct payment at some stations (expanded in 2024). T-money recommended.

8. Photos/Recordings: Subway interior photos OK (for personal use)/Live broadcasts not recommended (electric vehicle safety priority).
