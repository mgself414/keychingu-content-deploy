🎯 Why a Seoul Subway Full Guide?

The #1 pain point for inbound tourists to Korea = subway. Seoul Subway boasts 24 lines, 750+ stations, and is a world-leading public transportation system. It offers foreigner-friendly English, Chinese, and Japanese guidance, plus 24-hour T-money services. This guide provides a complete set of travel tips.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Transfer = Different Line = New Payment": T-money transfers within 30 minutes are free. Up to 4 transfers are OK. You must tag the same card.

2. "Airport Railroad = Express Only": Airport Railroad has 2 types — Express (직통, ₩9,500 (~$7 / ~50 RMB / ~1,000 JPY)·43 min) + All-stop (일반, ₩4,250 (~$3 / ~22 RMB / ~450 JPY)·58 min). For MZ (Millennials and Gen Z) tourists and budget travelers, the All-stop train is fine. For 14kg+ luggage, the Express train is recommended.

3. "Last Train = Midnight": Last trains run from 11:30 PM to 12:30 AM (varies by line and station). It's risky to assume 1:00 AM is always OK. You must depart at least 30 minutes before the last train.

4. "Line 9 = All-stop Only": Line 9 has 2 types: Express/All-stop. Express from Gimpo Airport (김포공항) to Jamsil (잠실) takes 25 minutes / All-stop takes 50 minutes. For foreigners traveling long distances, the Express train is highly recommended. Note that the Express train skips some stations.

5. "Foreigner Ticket Purchase = Difficult": Single-use T-money ticket machines are available in English. A ₩500 deposit is refundable. Rechargeable T-money cards (available at pyeonuijeom (convenience store) and stations) are foreigner-friendly. Mobile T-money (supports ganpyeon gyeolje (simple payment) like Naver Pay, Toss, KakaoPay, expanded in 2024) is also an option.

⚙ FAQ 8

1. Foreigner T-money Issuance: Pyeonuijeom (convenience store) (CU·GS25·7-Eleven·E-mart24) ₩4,000 card + top-up. For refunds, the ₩500 card deposit is non-refundable.

2. Mobile T-money: Android NFC + Naver Pay·KakaoPay·Toss ganpyeon gyeolje (simple payment) OK (expanded in 2024). Not supported on iPhone.

3. Transfer Rules: T-money tag within 30 minutes for free transfers to other lines or buses (max 4 transfers). Re-entry to the same line counts as one transfer.

4. Fares: Base fare ₩1,400 (up to 10km) + ₩100 additional per 5km. Foreigners typically pay ₩1,400 to ₩2,400 for general lines.

5. Operating Hours: Usually 5:30 AM to 12:00 AM (midnight). Last trains vary by line and station — real-time check on Naver Map or Kakao Map is essential.

6. Foreigner-Friendly: Lines 1·2·4·5·6·9 and Airport Railroad offer full 4-language (English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean) announcements. Outlying lines usually only have Korean and English.

7. Card Payment: Credit cards (internationally issued Visa·Master·JCB OK) for direct payment at some stations (expanded in 2024). T-money is recommended.

8. Photos·Recording: Photos inside the subway are OK (for personal use)·Live streaming is not recommended (prioritize train safety).
