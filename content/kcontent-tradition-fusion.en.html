K-content × traditional fusion explainer

> A bridge for foreigners who like K-pop, K-drama, and webtoons to naturally expand into traditional Korean culture — fusion case 6 cluster

The No. 1 entry point for foreigners' interest in Korea is K-content (K-pop, K-drama, webtoon, games). However, traditional cultural elements are abundant in K-content — BTS' hanbok stage, Goblin's hanok cafe, Kingdom's God, and Blackpink's hanji costumes. This explainer organizes 6 fusion clusters so that K-content fans can naturally deep dive into traditional culture.

1. K-pop × Hanbok

Representative examples

  • BTS — UN speech hanbok, SUGA hanbok pictorial, Jungkook hanbok concert
  • Blackpink — Hanji/Hanbok motif costume
  • NewJeans — Historical drama-style music video
  • MAMAMOO — Hanbok concept stage
  • (G)I-DLE — ‘Tomboy’ Hanbok insert

Foreigner fit experience

  • Bukchon·Seochon Hanbok Experience (#1097·#1110)
  • Free Hanbok entrance to Gyeongbokgung Palace
  • Hanbok brand: Danha·ChaiKim Youngjin (Modern Hanbok)

2. K-drama × Hanok

Representative filming locations

  • Goblin — Gangneung Hanok (Seongyojang #1106)
  • Kingdom — Changdeokgung Palace and Jeonju Hanok Village (#1107)
  • Strange Lawyer Youngwoo Woo — Seoul Hanok Cafe
  • Reply Series — Alleyway, long-established store

Foreigner fit route

  • Bukchon Hanok Village (#1097)·Seochon (#1110)
  • Jeonju Hanok Village (#1107)
  • Gangneung Seongyojang (#1106)
  • Refer to #1101 K-drama filming location Meta

3. Webtoon × Mythology and Folklore

Representative works

  • With God — Korean afterlife and folk beliefs
  • Moving — Superpower + Korean society
  • Only I level up — Korean game + mythology
  • Itaewon Class — Itaewon (#1121) Multicultural

Foreigner fit experience

  • Shamanism and Myth Museum (Korean Folk Museum)
  • Itaewon (#1121) — Webtoon background
  • Temple experience with God (#1208 Religion/Spirituality)

Foreigner fit experience

  • Gwangjang Market (#1120·#1122) — Long-established Korean food
  • Temple food explainer (#1137 series)
  • #1109 Michelin Guide — Modern Korean fine dining

Representative examples

6. K-beauty × tradition

American K-pop fans 1 day
