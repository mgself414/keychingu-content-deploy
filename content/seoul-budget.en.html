⚠ 3 Budget Mistakes

1. "Myeongdong·Gangnam=expensive" — Cost-effective store cluster Miji: Assuming that both Myeongdong and Gangnam are expensive. In reality, Myeongdong street food 2,000~5,000 + Gangnam cost-effective Korean food + Nowon and Junggye cost-effective BBQ (#179) Cost-effective full experience.

2. "Transportation = Taxi · Uber" — Not using the Climate Card for cost-effectiveness: Taxi costs 15,000 won per time and 50,000 won per day. In reality, Climate Card 65,000 won, unlimited for 30 days = 5 days + full amount of stay + unlimited Ttareungi.

3. "Museum = Paid" — Not utilizing the full free museum experience: Museums are usually perceived as paid. In fact, National Center, National Hangul, National Science, War Memorial of Korea, and National Folk Museum are all free + Free English guide. One week full experience available.

Seoul Budget Scenery

Seoul Global city cost-effectiveness #1 — 1/2 to 1/3 compared to Tokyo, Hong Kong, and Singapore. Full experience available for 10,000 won per day (meals + transportation + free museum). Full experience available for 50,000 won per week (hostel + transportation + cost-effective restaurant).

Cost-effective restaurant: Gwangjang Market 5,000~10,000 per person + Nowon/Junggye cost-effective BBQ + Myeongdong street food + Gimbap, lunch box, ramen for 1 person + Korean food 6,000~10,000 + Student cafeteria (near university).

Free museums and parks: National Museum of Korea (Yongsan, free) + National Hangeul Museum + National Science Museum + War Memorial of Korea (Yongsan) + National Folk Museum (within Gyeongbokgung Palace) + Children's Grand Park + Seoul Forest + Hangang Park + All city parks.

Cost-effective trekking: Bukhansan Mountain, Inwangsan Mountain, Dobongsan Mountain, Suraksan Mountain, and Gwanaksan Mountain are all free + English signs + Seoul Dullegil 156km free.

Cheap accommodation: Myeongdong, Hongdae, Itaewon hostels and guesthouses 20,000~50,000/night. Some 24-hour saunas (Dragon Hill Spa) are available for 15,000~25,000/night.
