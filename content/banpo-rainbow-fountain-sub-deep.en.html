Banpo Bridge Moonlight Rainbow Fountain Guide: Fountain Operating Times, Banpo Hangang Park Moonlight Plaza and Seorae Island, Sebitseom and Tubester, Jamsugyo Walking Festival and Seorae Island Picnic Concert

Subtitle: Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (operating March 15 to October, 20 minutes per show, not operating November to March 14) · Moonlight Plaza (always open) · Seorae Island (always open) · Sebitseom (varies by facility) · Tubester (open year-round, hours vary by season) · 2026 Jamsugyo Walking Festival (September 6 to October 25, ended) · 2026 Seorae Island Picnic Concert (October 9~11, ended) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to visit around the Moonlight Rainbow Fountain on Banpo Bridge in Seoul and the Banpo Hangang Park next to it. They are one fountain, a plaza and an artificial island inside the park, Sebitseom (a cultural space on the water) and a water leisure spot, and two festivals held in autumn 2026.

The fountain does not operate from November to March 14. As of November 15, 2026, when this guide was written, the fountain is in its rest period, and it next operates from March 15.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Banpo Bridge Moonlight Rainbow Fountain

Banpo Bridge Moonlight Rainbow Fountain [Banpo-dong, Seocho-gu, ☎ Rainbow Fountain Operations Office (무지개분수 운영사무실) 02-3780-0578~9] is a bridge fountain installed on Banpo Bridge. The project began in June 2007, and the opening ceremony was held in April 2009. To stage the whole width of the Han River, which is about 700m, it has 380 nozzles along a 1,140m section on both the upstream and downstream sides of Banpo Bridge, and it drops water pumped up from the Han River onto the surface about 20m below. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it was listed in the Guinness World Records on November 7, 2008 as the 'world's longest bridge fountain'.

By day it makes more than 100 shapes of water jets, including shapes modeled on willow branches and willow leaves, and at night 200 lights turn it into a rainbow-colored fountain. The fountain moves to background music.

  • Operating times: March 15 to June and September to October at 12:00, 19:30, 20:00, 20:30 and 21:00. In July and August 21:30 is added. Each show runs 20 minutes. In 2026, from June 1, it ran only 3 shows, at 19:30, 20:00 and 20:30, to save energy (Seoul city notice).
  • Rest period: January to March 14 and November to December. It may stop depending on the weather.
  • Parking: Available (669 spaces).

📍 Banpo Hangang Park — Moonlight Plaza and Seorae Island

Banpo Hangang Park Moonlight Plaza [Banpo-dong, Seocho-gu, ☎ 02-3780-0541~4] is a plaza in the middle of Banpo Hangang Park, at the south end of Banpo Bridge and Jamsugyo Bridge (잠수교), built in the shape of the moon. With Jamsugyo Bridge between them, there are 2 crescent ridges (초승마루) on the east and 3 on the west. Around it are the Moonlight Rainbow Fountain, installed on both sides of Banpo Bridge, and Jamsugyo Bridge, now a pedestrian-centered bridge. You can see Sebitseom and the night view of the buildings around the river.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available. Credit cards: Accepted.

Banpo Seorae Island [Banpo-dong, Seocho-gu, ☎ Seoul City Hall (서울시청) Parks and Leisure Division (공원여가과) 02-3780-0838] is an artificial island between Banpo Bridge and Dongjak Bridge (동작대교). It was made in 1982~1986 during the construction of Olympic-daero (올림픽대로) and the Hangang Comprehensive Development (한강종합개발), and it is connected to Banpo Hangang Park by 3 bridges called Seorae Bridge (서래교). Rapeseed and buckwheat are planted each season, and the Rapeseed Blossom Festival (유채꽃축제) is held in spring and the Buckwheat Blossom Festival (메밀꽃축제) in autumn.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

📍 On the Water — Sebitseom and Sebitseom Tubester

Sebitseom [Olympic-daero 2085-14 (올림픽대로 2085-14), Seocho-gu, ☎ 1566-3433] is a complex cultural space on the Han River. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is a large floating structure, with buildings built on floating bodies that float on the water. It consists of three islands, Chaebit (채빛), Solbit (솔빛) and Gabit (가빛), and Yebit (예빛), a media art gallery that screens video and content.

  • Opening hours and closed days: Vary by facility.
  • Parking: Available.

Sebitseom Tubester [Same address, ☎ 02-790-7090] is a water leisure activity at Sebitseom where you ride tubes and boats. Inside Tubester there is a round table, LED lighting and a parasol.

  • Opening hours: March to April weekdays 15:00~23:00, weekends and public holidays 13:00~23:00. May to October weekdays 16:00~24:00, weekends and public holidays 14:00~24:00. No hours are registered for November to February.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. It closes in bad weather.

📍 Autumn 2026 Festivals — Jamsugyo Walking Festival and Seorae Island Picnic Concert

The 2026 Car-Free Jamsugyo Walking Festival [Jamsugyo Bridge and the Banpo Hangang Park area, ☎ Seoul Future Hangang Headquarters (서울시 미래한강본부) 02-6401-9717] was a free street festival hosted by the Seoul Future Hangang Headquarters. In 2026 it ran from September 6 to October 25, from 14:00 to 22:00. Some programs were paid.

  • Regular programs: 'Moonlight Playground' (달빛 놀이터), 'Moonlight Restaurant' (달빛 식당) with food from different countries, 'Moonlight Shop' (달빛 상점), 'Moonlight Shelter' (달빛 쉼터), 'Moonlight Workshop' (달빛 공방), 'Moonlight Gallery' (달빛 갤러리), 'Ttuttu Camel Hump Canvas' (뚜뚜낙타봉 캔버스).
  • Special programs: They differed from week to week. They included a water gun festival, 'Jamsugyo Water Wars' (잠수교 워터워즈), 'Jamsugyo Autumn Sports Day' (잠수교 가을 운동회), 'Moonlight Chuseok Gathering' (달빛 한가위 한마당), 'Jamsugyo Gourmet Road' (잠수교 미식로드) and 'Moonlight Jamsugyo' (달빛 잠수교).

The 2026 Hangang Seorae Island Picnic Concert 'Autumn Romance' (가을낭만) [Seorae Island Bridge 1 Picnic Area (서래섬 1교 피크닉장), Banpo Hangang Park, ☎ 02-120] was held October 9~11, 2026. It ran from 13:00 to 19:00 (experiences until 18:00), with music performances, experience programs and a rest area. The performances, experiences and rest area were free, and some items such as the 'Picnic Food Market' (피크닉 푸드마켓) were paid.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. The Moonlight Rainbow Fountain does not operate from November to March 14 — it operates again from March 15, and it may stop depending on the weather.
  2. The fountain operates only 20 minutes per show — at 12:00, 19:30, 20:00, 20:30 and 21:00, with 21:30 added in July and August.
  3. The 2026 Jamsugyo Walking Festival and Seorae Island Picnic Concert are over — they were held September 6 to October 25 and October 9~11 respectively.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. When can I see the Rainbow Fountain?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, it operates at 12:00, 19:30, 20:00, 20:30 and 21:00 from March 15 to June and in September to October, and until 21:30 in July and August, for 6 shows a day. Each show is 20 minutes. It does not operate from November to March 14. In 2026, from June 1, it ran only 3 shows, at 19:30, 20:00 and 20:30, to save energy (Seoul city notice).

Q. Is there a place to rest near the fountain?

A. Banpo Hangang Park Moonlight Plaza is at the south end of Banpo Bridge and Jamsugyo Bridge. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, the Moonlight Rainbow Fountain, installed on both sides of Banpo Bridge, is around it. The plaza is always open.
