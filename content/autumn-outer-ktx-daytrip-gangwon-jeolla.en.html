⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Autumn leaves = the same time everywhere" — Gangwon vs Jeolla = 2 weeks difference

Many foreigners = "Korea autumn = late October". Actual = Gangwon mid-October to late October (Seoraksan Mountain, Jeongdongjin, peak of the East Sea) / Jeonnae Naejangsan Mountain early-November to mid-November (last autumn foliage nationwide) (Korea Meteorological Administration/National Park Service average year). Last week of October, Gangwon = peak / 1st to 2nd week of November, Jeolla = peak. If a foreigner arrives in Korea in mid-November, the peak of Naejangsan Mountain in Jeolla-do is possible even after the fall foliage in Gangwon is over. Rule = Autumn foliage season = Golden 3 weeks from October 25 to November 15 (Gangwon → Seoul → Jeolla in that order).

2. "KTX = Expensive" — KORAIL PASS = Unlimited for foreigners only

Foreigners 30%+ = “KTX is too expensive → give up on the day.” Actual KORAIL PASS = Unlimited pass for foreigners only (3 days, 5 days, 7 days) · Price for foreigners only (cheaper than Koreans) · 3-day pass = about 121,000 won (3 days KTX, Saemaeul, Rose of Sharon pool operation) · 5-day pass = about 159,000 won · 3 days = 1 night in Gangwon + 1 night in Jeolla + 1 day in Seoul = autumn leaves in Korea Full tour. Information for foreigners only = visitkorea·Korail English website.

3. "Naejangsan Cable Car = to the top" — In fact, all the way to Yeonjabong

Many foreigners = “Cable car → Easily reach the top.” Actually Naejangsan Cable Car = Visitor Information Center ↔ Yeonjabong (460m above sea level) · Yeonjabong → Summit of Sinseonbong (763m) = Approximately 1 hour of additional hiking required. Normal Challenge = Requires 2~3 stamina. Cable car only = Yeonjabong autumn foliage view + entrance OK. Rule = Naejangsan Cable Car = Fall Foliage View Introduction OK / Summit Challenge = Additional 1 hour.

📅 Gangwon·Jeolla KTX day trip season guide 5

1. Mid-October (October 15-21) — Gangwon Seoraksan Mountain and East Sea peak begins: Seoraksan Mountain, Jeongdongjin, and East Sea autumn foliage peak. Foreign vlog ramp-up begins.

2. Last week of October (October 22-31) — Gangwon peak + late Pyeongchang: Gangwon fall vlog viral peak · Golden 7 days.

3. 1st week of November (November 1-7) — Late part of Gangwon + start of peak of Naejangsan Mountain in Jeolla: Autumn leaves in the second half of Gangwon + first peak of Naejangsan Mountain in Jeolla.

4. 2nd week of November (November 8-14) — Jeolla Naejangsan Mountain and Gochang peak + National autumn foliage last: Jeolla vlog viral peak · Korea autumn foliage season closing circle.

5. After the 3rd week of November (November 15+) — The fall foliage in Gangwon and Jeolla ends. December = Illumination (#745) season begins.

FAQ 6

Q1. Gangwon vs Jeolla Foreigner Recommendation?

A. First outlying day trip = Gangneung, Gangwon (4) on the same day (shortest round trip, 4-5 hours) · Full fall foliage = Mt. Seorak, Gangwon (1) 1 night · Last season of fall foliage = Naejangsan Mountain in Jeolla (7) 1 night. Period = Mid-October to late October in Gangwon / Early November to mid-November in Jeolla.

Q2. KTX English for foreigners?

A. English OK. KORAIL English website (letskorail.com) · KORAIL app English mode · KTX seat information partially in English · KORAIL PASS Pass for foreigners only.

Q3. Can foreigners climb Mt. Seorak?

A. Small Park·Gwongeumseong Cable Car = Introduction OK (1 stamina). Cheonbuldong·Ulsanbawi = Full-scale mountain climbing (strength 3·hiking shoes required). Foreigners climbing Mt. Seorak = Korean Mountaineering Club cross (#148·#177).

Q4. Foreigner Naejangsan cable car = to the top?

A. To Yeonjabong (460m) · Summit Sinseonbong (763m) = Additional 1 hour of walking. Introductory = only cable car is OK · Real = challenge to the summit.

Q5. What if it rains during the fall foliage season?

A. Gangwon = Rain usually once a week in fall · Jeolla = Little rain in November. Raincoat and extra clothes are required. Hotel indoor in case of rain + #903 rainy season indoor alternative.

Q6. Foreign KTX accident/emergency?

A. KORAIL = 119 + English OK·KTX Free emergency OK for foreigners (medical information separate). See #405 Full Guide to Emergency Medical Services for Foreigners.
