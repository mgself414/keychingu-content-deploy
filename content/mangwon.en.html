⚠ Five major mistakes foreigners make in Mangwon

1. Recognition of “Mangwon = Mangwon Market only”

Common mistakes in foreign travel routes. Mangwon is recognized as a single spot in the market. In reality, Mangwon Cafe, Brunch, and Italian Hidden Gems are concentrated — Dinner and dessert courses are possible after a walk through the market.

2. Mangwon Market + Han River + Brunch route unknown

Foreigners' travel route to Mangwon ends after seeing the market. In reality, Walking around Mangwon Market → Be Canned Brunch → Mangwon Han River → Cafe Dessert A full route of 4-5 hours is possible.

3. Becand (No. 1 in Mangwon brunch cluster) Miji

The #1 brunch store in Mangwon for foreigners (signal 112.2, 25 foreign reviews). Although it is a hot spot on Korean Instagram, it is absent from foreign guidebooks.

4. Mangwon Market business hours + food etiquette unknown

Mangwon Market 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Tteokbokki, jeon, seafood, market food Foreigner friendly. Market Food Bakery + Korean Signature.

5. Mangwon → Hapjeong·Yeonnam Walk Miji

Mangwon Station ↔ Hapjeong Station 12 minutes on foot, Mangwon ↔ Yeonnam 15 minutes on foot. Good to tie together in one line.
