Why the Gyeongui-Jungang Line — The Line Where the Hangang Flows Past Your Window

Most of the Seoul subway runs underground, but the Gyeongui-Jungang Line (jade) runs above ground along the north bank of the Hangang. From Yongsan to Wangsimni, the Hangang and riverside scenery stream past the train window. It is the only line in Seoul where the ride itself becomes sightseeing. Add to that the city-center stretch of Hongik Univ. (홍대입구) (Gyeongui Line Forest Park) and Gongdeok, plus the riverside highlights of Ichon (museums), Hannam (fine dining), and Eungbong (forsythia night views).

This guide covers the Gyeongui-Jungang Line's 8 key tourism stations, one by one. With newly secured coordinates (37 stations), eats were pulled from accumulated data via nearest-station mapping and paired with riverside attractions. This is the eighth installment of the subway line-by-line series.

5 Line Tips — Get 200% Out of the Gyeongui-Jungang Line

Tip 1. Sit by the window

The Yongsan~Wangsimni stretch runs above ground along the north bank of the Hangang. Take a window seat on the river side for Hangang views — the ride itself is sightseeing.

Tip 2. Check the train intervals

The Gyeongui-Jungang Line runs at longer intervals than the subway (10–15 minutes). Check train times on a map app before you move.

Tip 3. Riverside spots peak at dusk

Dalmajibong in Oksu and Eungbongsan are at their best from sunset into night views (#1238). Save them for an evening course.

Tip 4. Use the transfer hubs

Gongdeok (5 · 6 · AREX) · Wangsimni (2 · 5 · Suin-Bundang) · Hongik Univ. (2 · AREX) · Yongsan (1 · KTX). The Gyeongui-Jungang Line is a bridge linking the other lines along the river.

Tip 5. One transit card does it all

A single T-money card covers the Gyeongui-Jungang Line, transfers, and buses. Same metropolitan rail fare system.

FAQ 6 — Gyeongui-Jungang Line Questions Foreigners Ask Most

Q1. What makes the Gyeongui-Jungang Line special?

  • Hangang views from the train window. On the above-ground Yongsan~Wangsimni stretch, the Hangang flows past your window. The only line where the ride itself is sightseeing.

Q2. How is it different from the subway?

  • It is metropolitan rail (operated by KORAIL) with the same transit card and transfers. But intervals are longer (10–15 minutes) — check the times.

Q3. Where can I see the Hangang from the train?

  • The Ichon~Wangsimni stretch (Ichon · Seobinggo (서빙고) · Hannam · Oksu · Eungbong). A window seat on the river side is recommended.

Q4. What are the free attractions?

  • The National Museum of Korea & National Hangeul Museum (Ichon) · Dalmajibong (Oksu) · Eungbongsan (Eungbong) · Gyeongui Line Forest Park (Hongik Univ.). Mostly free.

Q5. Is the restaurant info accurate?

  • It is based on a Naver Map crawl, so treat it as reference only. Re-check on a map app before visiting.

Q6. Night views?

  • Eungbongsan (#1238 — night views over Seoul Forest and the Hangang) · Dalmajibong sunset in Oksu. In spring, Eungbongsan glows yellow with forsythia.
