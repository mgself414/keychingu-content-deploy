Golf in Korea — 4 Capital-Region Courses Foreigners Can Play as Non-Members: Official Green Fees, Caddie and Cart (October 2026)

Subtitle: Pine Creek (Anseong, public, 27 holes) October weekday 180,000–215,000 / weekend 240,000–280,000 · Lakeside (Yongin) public-type East-South course weekday 199,000 / weekend 259,000 · East Valley (Gwangju Gonjiam) non-member 240,000 / 290,000 · Jack Nicklaus GC (Songdo, Incheon, members-only) non-member 380,000 / 480,000 — each from the official rate sheet, 2026-10-08. Caddie 150,000–170,000 and cart 100,000–120,000 are charged separately per group. The old "18 holes 120–170K", "caddie 20,000 per person" and "9 holes 60K" figures were wrong.

🎯 How to Use This Guide

The old "12 picks" list got five locations wrong (Jack Nicklaus placed in Yeongjongdo (영종도), East Valley in Yongin (용인), Pine Creek in Gwangju (광주), Lakeside in Goyang (고양), and Bear Creek in Yongin (용인)), and every green fee was lower than the real official rate ("18 holes 120–170K", "caddie 20–25K per person", "9 holes 60K, no caddie"). This time we list numbers only for the 4 courses whose official rate sheets we opened directly on 2026-10-08, and mark the rest "not confirmed officially." Green fees change by month and tee time, so the Pine Creek CC (파인크리크 CC) figures are "as of October." Operating hours and tee-off times appear on no official page, so we do not list them. Old lines such as 1330 interpretation, VAT refund and caddie tipping had no official basis and were removed.

💰 Cost Structure — Green Fee Is Per Person, Caddie and Cart Are Per Group

  • Green fee per person + caddie 150,000–170,000 per group (about 40,000 each for four players) + cart 100,000–120,000 per group (25,000–30,000 each). For four players on a weekday public course (Pine Creek CC (파인크리크 CC) 2nd round 215,000), the total comes to about 280,000 won per person. The old "caddie 20,000 per person" mistook a group fee for a per-person fee.
  • Weekends and public holidays run 50,000–100,000 higher than weekdays. Substitute holidays are charged at weekend rates (Pine Creek CC (파인크리크 CC) on Oct 5: 220,000 for every tee time).
  • Monthly changes: Pine Creek CC (파인크리크 CC) has already posted its November rate sheet. Check the official rate sheet again just before booking.
  • Bad weather is settled per hole (at East Valley CC (이스트밸리 CC), green fee × holes played / 18; at Jack Nicklaus Golf Club Korea (잭 니클라우스 GC 코리아), per hole in case of natural disaster).

📍 5 Location Corrections

  • Jack Nicklaus Golf Club Korea (잭 니클라우스 GC 코리아) is in Songdo (송도), not Yeongjongdo (영종도) — and "directly linked to Incheon Airport (인천공항)" has no basis · East Valley CC (이스트밸리 CC) is in Gwangju Gonjiam (광주 곤지암), not Yongin (용인) · Pine Creek CC (파인크리크 CC) is in Anseong (안성), not Gwangju (광주) · Lakeside CC (레이크사이드 CC) is in Yongin Cheoin-gu (용인 처인구), not Goyang (고양) · Bear Creek Golf Club (베어크리크 GC) is in Pocheon (포천) and Chuncheon (춘천), not Yongin (용인). Blue Heron CC (블루헤런 CC) is in Yeoju (여주) (secondary).

🗓 Booking and Language

  • All 4 courses take real-time or online booking on the official site (usually with a member login) plus a main phone line. The Jack Nicklaus Golf Club Korea (잭 니클라우스 GC 코리아) rate sheet has a "foreign-language caddie" line (220,000). East Valley CC (이스트밸리 CC) has an ENG link and Haesley Nine Bridges (해슬리 나인브릿지) has an English site, but we have not confirmed what they contain.
  • We could not open the Kakao Golf Booking (카카오골프예약), XGOLF or kimcaddie pages, so the old "No.1 domestic platform / real-time green fees" line was deleted.

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Budgeting from the old "18 holes 120–170K" — even public courses run 180,000–215,000 on weekdays.

2. Leaving out caddie and cart — that adds 250,000–290,000 per group.

3. Placing Jack Nicklaus next to the airport — it is in Songdo (송도).

4. Booking Bear Creek Pocheon in mid-November — closed from 11/16.

5. Stating screen golf fees as fact — not posted officially.

⚙ FAQ 6

1. What is the lowest-priced 18 holes? — Among the officially confirmed: Pine Creek CC (파인크리크 CC) October weekday 1st round 180,000 (before 7:30) · Lakeside CC (레이크사이드 CC) East-South course 199,000.

2. What is the total per person for four players? — About 280,000 on a weekday public course (green fee 215,000 + share of caddie and cart).

3. Is there an English-speaking caddie? — Jack Nicklaus Golf Club Korea (잭 니클라우스 GC 코리아) lists a foreign-language caddie at 220,000 (official). The others are unconfirmed.

4. Can non-members play members-only courses? — East Valley CC (이스트밸리 CC), Jack Nicklaus Golf Club Korea (잭 니클라우스 GC 코리아) and the Lakeside CC (레이크사이드 CC) West course post non-member rates. Haesley Nine Bridges (해슬리 나인브릿지) does not.

5. Is there a 9-hole option at a lower price? — Korea Public CC (코리아퍼블릭CC) secondary 75,000–95,000, not confirmed officially.

6. What about hours and season? — Operating and tee-off times are not on the official pages. Judge by the monthly rate sheets.
