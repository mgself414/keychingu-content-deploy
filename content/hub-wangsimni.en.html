Why the Wangsimni Hub — Seongdong's Gateway Where 4 Lines Meet

Wangsimni Station is a quad transfer station where Line 2 · Line 5 · the Gyeongui-Jungang Line · the Suin-Bundang Line all meet. As the gateway to eastern Seoul, it branches out in 4 directions from a single station. On top of that, Wangsimni itself is a food mecca hugging the gopchang (grilled beef intestines) alley (곱창골목) and the Majang Livestock Market (마장동 축산시장). It is a travel base where you can plan "where to eat what and where to transfer" all at one station.

This guide is a new axis orthogonal to the line-by-line series (#1241~#1249). Where the line-by-line editions introduced places "vertically along a single line," this edition radiates in 4 directions from the single station of Wangsimni — weaving together which line takes you to what, centered on the hub. It is the first installment of the Subway Course Series (Phase 2).

5 Transfer & Usage Tips — Get 200% Out of the Wangsimni Hub

Tip 1. Use Wangsimni as a 'radial base'

Plan your lodging and itinerary around Wangsimni, and 4 directions open up from a single station — Line 2 (Seongsu) · Line 5 (Majang) · Gyeongui-Jungang (Hangang) · Bundang (Seoul Forest · Gangnam).

Tip 2. Transfer walks are long

With 4 lines at different depths, transfer walks run 5–8 minutes. Leave yourself some buffer time. Wait it out — and shop — at the station complex (E-Mart · CGV).

Tip 3. Seoul Forest from either direction

Seoul Forest is reachable both via Line 2 (walk from Ttukseom) and the Bundang Line (one stop to Seoulsup Station (서울숲역)). It is the closest large park to Wangsimni (#1235).

Tip 4. Majang-dong is a must for gourmet manias

The Majang Livestock Market (one stop on Line 5) is Korea's largest livestock market. Get fresh hanu at market price at a butcher's-restaurant — a gourmet belt with Wangsimni gopchang.

Tip 5. One transit card

A single T-money card transfers across all 4 lines. From Wangsimni to anywhere in the city in one go.
