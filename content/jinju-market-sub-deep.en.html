Jinju Jungang Market and Namgang sub-deep 12 selections for foreigners

Category: A Outskirts/Nature

Parent deep: #380·#618·#244 sub

Jinju Jungang Market and Namgang 12 Lines

1. Jinju Jungang Market (140 years of history)

  • Discrimination: Opened in 1884·Korea’s longest arcade 980m·Hanbok, Food Alley, Agriculture and Fisheries Zone 3
  • Foreigner visit: candidate for visitkorea, open tourism, traditional market K-Tourism Market
  • kr cross: #380 Market full set·#663Andong·#664Gyeongju sub

2. Jinju Bibimbap (Korea’s 7th Bibimbap)

  • Distinction: Jinju authentic bibimbap·Jeonju, Haeju, Jinju 3 major bibimbap·Seonji + diced vegetables
  • Signature: Emperor’s Restaurant (1929), Jinju main branch, branches nationwide
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ JP·CN No. 1 authentic Korean food

3. Jinju cold noodles

  • Discrimination: Pyongyang, North Korea → Orthodoxization of Jinju·Seafood broth + buckwheat noodles·Cold seasoning sauce
  • Signature: Ha Yeon-ok (1945), Jinju main branch, 4-5 branches nationwide
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ JP·CN Cool cotton enthusiast·Summer #1

4. Jinju Castle (Historic Site No. 118)

  • Discrimination: Imjin War 1592 Battle of Jinju, Kim Si-min·1593 Battle of Jinju Castle, 70,000 citizens protest
  • Attractions: Chokseoknu, Uigi Nongae Uiam, and Jinju Museum
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

5. Chokseoknu (Namgang Cliff Pavilion)

  • Discrimination: Korea's three major pavilions (Anapji in Gyeongju, Bupyeoknu in Pyeongyang, Chokseoknu in Jinju)·1365·Right on the cliff of the Namgang River
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP proof shot ranked 1st

6. Namgang Lantern Festival (UNESCO Creative Cities)

  • Discrimination: Started in 2002 · Every October · UNESCO Creative Cities Network · Floating lanterns on the Namgang River
  • When: Early October every year, about 2 weeks
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN night view and festival first place

7. Jinju Hanbok (Historical Attraction)

  • Distinction: Jinjuseong Fortress + Chokseokru + Hanbok·Secluded, different from Hwangridan-gil in Gyeongju
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ MZ Authentic Mania·#588 Hanbok cross

8. Pearl silk

  • Discrimination: Korea #1 Silk production area·Production of 70% of silk nationwide·Silk Museum
  • Experience: Jinju Silk Museum, silk scarf purchase, souvenirs ranked 1st
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP souvenir enthusiasts

9. Jinju Nongae Festival

  • Discrimination: Third week of May·Memorial of Uigi Nongae·Spirit of resistance against the Japanese invasions of Korea
  • Performance: Paddy field wreath-laying, civic ceremony, demonstration
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiasts

10. Jinyang Lake (lake)

  • Discrimination: Jinju downtown lake·Namgang Dam construction in 1969·Sunset mania
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN·JP nature day-trip

11. Jinju Hanjeongsik

  • Distinction: Jinju authentic Korean cuisine·Emperor’s Restaurant·Jinju 8 delicacies (8 kinds of delicacies)
  • Recommended: Jinju Hanjeongsik Main Branch·30,000 won~/person
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP·CN authentic food and beverage enthusiasts

12. KTX Jinju Station + City Tour

  • Access: Seoul Station → Jinju KTX 3 hours and 30 minutes (2012 KTX Jinju extension)
  • City Tour: Market - Jinjuseong - Chokseokru - Namgang full day, 6 hours / 30,000 won
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP 1 night stay recommended (1 day trip tight)

FAQ 6

Q1. Is Jinju day-trip possible? A. KTX 3 hours and 30 minutes, full day - 1 night recommended (Namgang night view + Jinju Castle the next day).

Q2. Where is Emperor’s Restaurant vs. Ha Yeon-ok? A. Emperor’s Restaurant = Bibimbap / Ha Yeon-ok = Naengmyeon. Both authentic Jinju restaurant signatures - Only the main branch is recognized.

Q3. When is the Jinju Lantern Festival? A. Every year in early October, about 2 weeks Namgang River night view peak - Advance hotel reservation in September is required.

Q4. Chokseokru entrance fee? A. Jinju Castle entrance fee included (adults 2,000 won) · Chokseokru free (within the fortress).

Q5. Where to buy pearl silk? A. Jinju Silk Museum·Silk Complex (Silk scarf 30,000 won~).

Q6. Is it possible to walk between Jinjuseong Fortress and Chokseokru? A. Location of Chokseoknu within Jinjuseong Fortress, 5 minutes on foot (after entering).
