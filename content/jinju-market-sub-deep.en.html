Jinju Market Guide — Jungang Market's 980m Arcade, Nongae Market's Olppam Saturday Night Market, Jinjuseong Fortress and Chokseongnu Pavilion, Jinyangho Lake

Subtitle: Jinju Jungang Market (진주중앙시장), opened in 1884, cards accepted · Nongae Market (논개시장) Olppam Saturday Night Market (올빰 토요야시장), Saturdays 18:00–23:00, opening period set separately · Jinjuseong Fortress (진주성), March–October 05:00–23:00 · Chokseongnu Pavilion (촉석루), 09:00–18:00 · Jinyangho Lake (진양호), always open — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

For markets in Jinju, two markets joined under one roof are the main ones. One is Jinju Jungang Market, which opened in 1884, and the other is Nongae Market, which opened in 1962. At Nongae Market, the Olppam Saturday Night Market is held on Saturday nights. Near the markets are Jinjuseong Fortress and the Chokseongnu Pavilion inside it, and in Pammun-dong (판문동) there is the lake Jinyangho.

This guide covers only the two markets and Jinjuseong Fortress, Chokseongnu Pavilion, and Jinyangho Lake, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). The Namgang Lantern Festival is covered separately in #1067 Jinju Lantern Festival in Autumn.

🛒 Jinju Jungang Market — The 140-Year-Old Arcade Market

Jinju Jungang Market (8-1 Jinyangho-ro 547beon-gil, Jinju (진주시 진양호로547번길 8-1), ☎ 055-741-2151) is a traditional market opened in 1884, which began at the Jinju Sangmusa (진주상무사). It was selected as a culture and tourism market in 2011.

  • Zones: Under a 980m-long retractable arcade, it is divided into three zones: hanbok and fabric shops, a food alley, and agricultural and seafood products.
  • Dawn market: From 2 a.m., vegetable and fish auctions are held. Jirisan farm produce and southern coast seafood are traded.
  • Bidangil Youth Mall (비단길 청년몰): On the market's 2nd floor are cafes, restaurants, and workshops run by young merchants.
  • Opening hours and closed days: They differ by shop.
  • Cards: Accepted.
  • Facilities: Restrooms and parking are available.

🍜 Nongae Market and the Olppam Saturday Night Market

Nongae Market (☎ 055-745-0120) is a market joined under the same roof as Jungang Market, and it opened in 1962 under the name Jangdae Market (장대시장). It sits where the west side of Jungang Market meets the north side of the fruit and vegetable market.

  • Closed days: Open year-round. Opening hours differ by store. Cards accepted (the same listing as Jungang Market).
  • Food: There is a Noodle Road gathering noodle dishes, and a food alley. At the two markets you can eat yukjeon (육전, pan-fried beef slices), Jinju bibimbap, and snack-bar food in one place.
  • Olppam Saturday Night Market (Jinyangho-ro 563beon-gil (진양호로563번길), ☎ 055-790-9238): A night market whose name combines "owl" and "night" (올빼미 + 밤). It opens every Saturday 18:00–23:00 and is not open Sunday to Friday. It sells flat bibim dumplings, grilled ice cream, yukjeon, and bul-jokbal (불족발, pig's trotters). The opening and closing dates are set separately each year, so check the market website before going.
  • Parking: 500 won for the first 30 minutes, then 200 won every 10 minutes.

🏯 Jinjuseong Fortress and Chokseongnu Pavilion

Jinjuseong Fortress (626 Namgang-ro, Jinju (진주시 남강로 626), ☎ 055-749-5171) is a fortress wall along the Namgang River, with a circumference of about 1,760m. In 1592, in the first Battle of Jinjuseong during the Imjin War, 3,800-plus soldiers defeated nearly twenty thousand Japanese troops. In the second battle in 1593, all the civilians, officials, and soldiers inside the fortress died for their country, and it was then that Nongae threw herself into the Namgang River holding an enemy general.

  • Opening hours: March–October 05:00–23:00, November–February 05:00–22:00. Open year-round. Parking is available.
  • Chokseongnu Pavilion (☎ 055-749-5171): A pavilion inside Jinjuseong Fortress, on the cliff above the Namgang River. It was first built in 1241, and the one burned in the Korean War was restored in 1960 by citizens pooling their efforts. It is open 09:00–18:00.
  • Parking: The parking lot in front of Chokseokmun Gate takes only compact cars. The Gongbukmun Gate parking lot and the public health center parking lot can also be used.

🌅 Jinyangho Lake — Lake and Observatory

Jinyangho Lake (Pammun-dong, Jinju (진주시 판문동), ☎ Jinyangho Park Office 055-749-5961) is an artificial lake made in 1970 where the Gyeongho River and the Deokcheon River meet.

  • Opening hours: Always open, open year-round. Parking is available.
  • Jinyangho Park: From the 3rd-floor observatory you can see the lake and the mountain ridges toward Jirisan, and it is known as a place with beautiful sunsets.
  • Jinyangho Zoo: March–October 09:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00), November–February 09:00–17:00 (last entry 16:00). It is closed every Tuesday (the next day if Tuesday is a public holiday).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Thinking the Saturday night market is open every day — The Olppam Saturday Night Market opens only on Saturdays 18:00–23:00, and its opening period is set separately each year.

2. Thinking you can see Chokseongnu Pavilion as late as Jinjuseong Fortress — The fortress is open into the night, but Chokseongnu Pavilion is 09:00–18:00.

3. Going to Jinyangho Zoo on a Tuesday — The zoo is closed every Tuesday. The lake and the park are open.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Where do I eat Jinju bibimbap and yukjeon?

A. At the connected Jungang Market and Nongae Market, you can eat yukjeon, Jinju bibimbap, and snack-bar food in one place.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. According to the tourism organization, cards are accepted at Jinju Jungang Market and Nongae Market (the two markets are registered as one listing). Card information is not registered for the Olppam Saturday Night Market.
