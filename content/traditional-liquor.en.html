⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Korean alcohol = Chamisul·Chorumlike only": Chamisul·Chorumlike = Diluted soju (16~20%) + Korean traditional alcohol = Andong soju·Igangju·Munbaeju (40~45% distilled) + Foreign enthusiasts deep dive.

2. "Makgeolli = the same everywhere": Makgeolli = Seoul Jangsu (1962 Foreigner's No. 1), Boksundoga (Premium), Song Myeong-seop, Haechang (Premium's No. 1) Full set + Foreign Mania.

3. "Traditional liquor = Bissamman": Traditional liquor = Jangsu Makgeolli 2K, Kooksoondang Baekseju 8K Cost-effectiveness + Boksundoga·Munbaeju 30~50K No. 1 premium foreign enthusiasts.

4. "Moonbaeju·Lee Kangju = Can't drink it": Moonbaeju (Pyongyang 1,000 Years Intangible Cultural Heritage)·Lee Kangju (Jeonbuk Joseon's three famous liquors) = No. 1 foreign enthusiasts + Korean traditional gallery/store OK.

5. "Outskirts Brewery = Can't get it": Andong Soju Museum·Jeongseon Makgeolli Brewery·Haenam Song Myeong-seop = Foreign Mania Outskirts 1 Night 2 Days + Klook English Package.

📜 Deep dive into Korean alcohol history and culture

6,000 years of history of Korean alcohol

era Main alcohol Features
Gojoseon·Three Kingdoms Period (BC ~ 7C) Grain wine and fruit wine Korean alcohol prototype and ceremonial use
Goryeo Dynasty (10~14C) Settlement in Cheongju and Yakju Nobleman culture and court
Joseon Dynasty (15~19C) Three famous liquors (Lee Kang-ju, Jukryeok High School, Gamhongro) · Gyodong Beopju 1000+ types of gayangju by family
Japanese Colonial Period (1910~1945) Liquor tax and self-brewing prohibited Crisis of discontinuation of traditional liquor·Diluted soju (predecessor of Chamisul) appears
Hyundai (1995 revival) Gayangju license reinstated (1995) Restoration of Andong Soju, Lee Kang-ju, and Munbaeju intangible cultural assets
2010s (Premium revival) Boksundoga, Haechang, etc. Premium Foreign Mania·MZ Authentic Revival
