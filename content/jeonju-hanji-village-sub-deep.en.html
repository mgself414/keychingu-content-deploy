🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers 7 culture and history facilities and 1 street performance in and around Jeonju Hanok Village where you can see Jeonju's fan, publishing, liquor and craft culture and its history. The full Hanok Village route, including Gyeonggijeon Shrine (경기전) and Jeondong Cathedral (전동성당), is covered in the #384 Jeonju Guide, and the Jeonju Traditional Hanji Center and Jeonju Hanji Museum are covered in the #601 Korean Hanji Travel Guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). All 7 places are closed on Mondays (Jeonju Hyanggyo is closed the next day if Monday is a public holiday). The Wanpanbon Culture Center, Traditional Liquor Museum, Woodblock Calligraphy and Painting Experience Center and Jeonju Hyanggyo close at different times depending on the season.

📍 Fans and Publishing — Jeonju Fan Culture Center, Wanpanbon Culture Center and Jeonju Woodblock Calligraphy and Painting Experience Center

The Jeonju Fan Culture Center (93 Gyeonggijeon-gil, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si (전주시 완산구 경기전길 93), Pungnam-dong 3-ga (풍남동3가), ☎ 063-231-1774) opened in 2011. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, Jeonju, home of the Jeolla Provincial Governor's Office (전라감영), had Seonjacheong (선자청), an office that made and managed fans, and the center is made up of Jiseon Hall (지선실), a special exhibition room, Cheongpung Hall (청풍실), which permanently displays more than 60 fan relics, and an experience room. You can try painting a round fan or a folding fan, and the Baram Shop (바람가게) sells fans and souvenirs by Jeonju fan masters.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~20:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok days.

The Wanpanbon Culture Center (24 Jeonjucheondong-ro, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si (전주시 완산구 전주천동로 24), Gyo-dong (교동), ☎ 063-231-2212) opened on October 18, 2011. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, "Wanpanbon" (완판본) means old books and their editions published in Wansan, the old name of Jeonju, and the exhibition rooms show Korean classic novels, the Wanpanbon production process and replica printing blocks. Experience programs include woodblock printing, hanji postcards and making old-style books.

  • Opening hours: Winter (December~February) 10:00~17:00, March~May and November 10:00~18:00, summer (June~October) 10:00~20:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Admission: Free. Experience programs are priced per program.

The Jeonju Woodblock Calligraphy and Painting Experience Center (46 Jeonjucheondong-ro, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si (전주시 완산구 전주천동로 46), Gyo-dong (교동), ☎ 063-231-5694) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, a place that restores the woodblocks and printed editions of Yongbieocheonga (용비어천가) and Wanpanbon classic novels as woodblocks. Experiences include coloring a woodblock-print hanji wall hanging, making a woodblock-print postcard envelope and making old-style books.

  • Opening hours: Winter 10:00~17:00, summer 10:00~18:00 (the Korea Tourism Organization registered information says the hours may change).
  • Closed days: Every Monday, Seollal and Chuseok.
  • Parking: Available.

📍 Liquor and Crafts — Jeonju Traditional Liquor Museum and Jeonju Crafts Exhibition Hall

The Jeonju Traditional Liquor Museum (74 Hanji-gil, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si (전주시 완산구 한지길 74), ☎ 063-287-6305) is a public museum that exhibits Korean traditional liquor. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the museum sells local traditional liquor on consignment inside and runs traditional liquor education programs.

  • Opening hours: Tuesday~Sunday 10:00~18:00, until 20:00 in June~October. The Sueul Gallery (수을갤러리) is open Tuesday~Friday 14:00~17:00 and Saturday and Sunday 10:00~18:00, with a lunch break 12:00~13:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok days.
  • Admission: Free.

The Jeonju Crafts Exhibition Hall (15 Taejo-ro, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si (전주시 완산구 태조로 15), ☎ 063-281-1610) is, according to the Korea Tourism Organization, made up of an exhibition hall, an experience hall and a sales hall, and you can see everything from traditional works designated as Jeonbuk State (전북특별자치도) tangible cultural heritage to crafts by emerging artists. There are craft-making experience programs.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok days.
  • Admission: Free (experience fee separate).

📍 History — Jeonju Hanok Village History Museum and Jeonju Hyanggyo

The Jeonju Hanok Village History Museum (17-10 Choemyeonghui-gil, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si (전주시 완산구 최명희길 17-10), Pungnam-dong 3-ga (풍남동3가), ☎ 063-286-5125) opened in 2018 after two hanok buildings were renovated. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, the permanent exhibition room shows the history of Hanok Village on a timeline, and you can also watch resident interview videos and try a virtual reality experience. On weekends there is a regular guided tour through the exhibition rooms.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:45).
  • Closed days: Every Monday.
  • Admission: Free.

Jeonju Hyanggyo (139 Hyanggyo-gil, Wansan-gu, Jeonju-si (전주시 완산구 향교길 139), Gyo-dong (교동), ☎ 063-288-4548) is a hyanggyo, a state educational institution of the Goryeo and Joseon periods, with buildings such as Daeseongjeon (대성전) and Myeongnyundang (명륜당). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, Daeseongjeon was rebuilt in the 4th year of King Hyojong (1653), there is a ginkgo tree more than 400 years old in the front yard of Daeseongjeon, and the site is known as a filming location for dramas such as 'Love in the Moonlight (구르미 그린 달빛)' and 'Sungkyunkwan Scandal (성균관 스캔들)'.

  • Opening hours: Winter (November~February) 10:00~17:00, summer (March~October) 09:00~18:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday (the next day if Monday is a public holiday).
  • Parking: Available.

📍 Street Performance — Jeonju Hanok Village Traditional Performance Parade 'Nosang Noriya'

The Traditional Performance Parade 'Nosang Noriya' (노상놀이야) was held every Saturday from April 18 to October 31, 2026, 15:00~16:00, on Taejo-ro in Jeonju Hanok Village (Namcheongyo Bridge to Gyeonggijeon Shrine) (☎ 063-236-1577). According to the information registered with the Korea Tourism Organization, it started from Namcheongyo Bridge with a pungmul procession led by a dragon flag, put on madang-nori and traditional performances, and ended in front of Gyeonggijeon Shrine with a Jeonju gijeopnori dragon flag performance. It was free and anyone could join the procession.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. All 7 places are closed on Mondays — avoid Mondays when you plan your visit (Jeonju Hyanggyo is closed the next day if Monday is a public holiday).
  2. Closing times change with the season — the Wanpanbon Culture Center closes at 20:00 in June~October and at 17:00 in December~February. The Traditional Liquor Museum is open until 20:00 in June~October. Jeonju Hyanggyo (November~February) and the Woodblock Calligraphy and Painting Experience Center (winter) close at 17:00.
  3. Experiences at the Wanpanbon Culture Center and Crafts Exhibition Hall have separate fees — the Wanpanbon Culture Center is registered with a fee per program, and the Crafts Exhibition Hall with a separate experience fee.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which places let me make something myself?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, you can try experiences at the Jeonju Fan Culture Center (fan painting), the Wanpanbon Culture Center (woodblock printing, hanji postcards, making old-style books), the Jeonju Woodblock Calligraphy and Painting Experience Center (coloring woodblock prints, making old-style books) and the Jeonju Crafts Exhibition Hall (craft making).

Q. Which places are free?

A. The Wanpanbon Culture Center, Jeonju Traditional Liquor Museum, Jeonju Crafts Exhibition Hall and Jeonju Hanok Village History Museum are registered as free. Experience fees are registered only for the Wanpanbon Culture Center (per program) and the Jeonju Crafts Exhibition Hall (experience fee separate).
