Suwon Yeongdong Market and Hwaseong Guide — 40-plus Hanbok Shops at Yeongdong Market, Paldalmun Gate, Hwaseong Haenggung Palace, Banghwasuryujeong Pavilion, Jangan-mun Geobuk Market, Haenggung-dong Mural Village

Subtitle: Suwon Yeongdong Market (수원 영동시장), 09:00–20:00, cards vary by store · Suwon Nammun Market and Nammun Rodeo Market (수원 남문시장·남문로데오시장) · Paldalmun Gate and Hwaseong Haenggung Palace (팔달문·화성행궁), March–October 09:00–18:00, November–February 09:00–17:00, open year-round · Banghwasuryujeong Pavilion and Haenggung-dong Mural Village (방화수류정·행궁동 벽화마을), always open · Jangan-mun Geobuk Market (장안문거북시장) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Around Paldalmun Gate, the south gate of Suwon Hwaseong Fortress, several markets gather to form the Suwon Nammun Market commercial district. Among them, the one known for hanbok is Yeongdong Market. East of Paldalsan Mountain is King Jeongjo's Hwaseong Haenggung Palace, on the cliff east of Hwahongmun Gate is the pavilion Banghwasuryujeong, and north of Jangan-mun Gate, the main gate of Hwaseong, is Jangan-mun Geobuk Market. Haenggung-dong (행궁동) is the name for the 12 neighborhoods around Hwaseong, and in its alleys is Haenggung-dong Mural Village.

This guide covers the hanbok market and the sights on the Hwaseong side, and the operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). Food markets such as the fried chicken street, Jidong Market sundae, and Motgol Market are covered separately in #648 Suwon Market Guide.

👘 Suwon Yeongdong Market — Hanbok and Textiles

Suwon Yeongdong Market (6 Suwoncheon-ro 255beon-gil, Paldal-gu, Suwon, Gyeonggi (경기 수원시 팔달구 수원천로255번길 6), ☎ 031-251-0171) is a market that began as a cattle market in the reign of Jeongjo and is now 200-plus years old. According to the tourism organization's description, it is the largest traditional market in Suwon, with modern shopping buildings mixed with a traditional market.

  • Hanbok: Of the 300-plus stores, 40-plus are pure hanbok shops. It specializes in hanbok and textiles to the point that Yeongdong Market brings hanbok to mind.
  • What is sold: Hanbok, textiles, fabric, clothing, and sundry goods. There are also farm and livestock product stores.
  • Opening hours: 09:00–20:00. Closed days differ by shop.
  • Cards: Accepted, but it differs by store. Parking is available, and there are restrooms.

🛍 Suwon Nammun Market and Nammun Rodeo Market

Suwon Nammun Market (9 Paldalmun-ro, Paldal-gu (팔달구 팔달문로 9), ☎ Suwon City Hall Local Economy Division 031-228-3270) is not a single market but a commercial district where several markets around Paldalmun Gate gather. Each market has its own signature item, like tools at Gucheon-dong Tool Arcade, hanbok at Yeongdong Market, sundae at Jidong Market, and fried chicken at Paldalmun Market. Opening hours and closed days differ by shop.

Nammun Rodeo Market (88 Haenggung-ro, Paldal-gu (팔달구 행궁로 88), Gyo-dong, ☎ 031-258-0336) is a street that formed naturally as shops gathered, and many young people visit. A Saturday market is held, and there is a youth culture performance hall. Opening hours and closed days differ by shop.

🏯 Paldalmun Gate — The South Gate of Hwaseong

Paldalmun Gate (780 Jeongjo-ro, Paldal-gu (팔달구 정조로 780), ☎ 031-228-2765) is the south gate of Suwon Hwaseong. Outside the gate it is wrapped by a jar-shaped barbican, with watchtowers standing on the left and right. Construction began on February 28, 1794, the same day as Jangan-mun Gate. The nameplates carved with the names of the building, the construction supervisors, and the stonemasons from the time of construction, and the signboard, remain in their original form, and in 2013 the entire gate tower was repaired for the first time.

  • Opening hours: March–October (months 3–10) 09:00–18:00, November–February 09:00–17:00. Open year-round.
  • Parking: Free.

👑 Hwaseong Haenggung Palace — King Jeongjo's Temporary Palace

Hwaseong Haenggung Palace (825 Jeongjo-ro, Paldal-gu (팔달구 정조로 825), Namchang-dong, ☎ Ticket Office 031-5191-4677 · Information Center 031-5191-4480) is where King Jeongjo stayed temporarily when he made trips to Hyeollyungwon. It was used as a government office at other times. It was built east of Paldalsan Mountain when Hwaseong was constructed in the 18th to 20th years of Jeongjo's reign, and Hwaseong is a World Heritage site.

  • Opening hours: March–October 09:00–18:00, November–February 09:00–17:00. Open year-round.
  • Experiences: The tourism organization's description lists archery on the Hwaseong fortress, ringing the bell at Hyowon on the summit of Paldalsan Mountain, traditional culture experiences at the palace, and trying on Daejanggeum costumes.
  • Cards: Accepted. Parking is available.

🌿 Banghwasuryujeong Pavilion — The Pavilion Regarded as Hwaseong's Most Original Building

Banghwasuryujeong Pavilion (44-6 Suwoncheon-ro 392beon-gil, Paldal-gu (팔달구 수원천로392번길 44-6), Maehyang-dong, ☎ Suwon Comprehensive Tourist Information Center 031-228-4672) is a pavilion built in 1794 (the 18th year of Jeongjo) when the fortress walls were built. Its official name is Dongbuk Gakru, and it carries a hall name meaning "seeking flowers and wandering along the willows." It was rebuilt in 1848 and designated a Treasure in 2011. From the pavilion you can see Yeonmudae and Dongbuk Gongsimdon to the east, and Jangan-mun Gate and Paldalsan Mountain to the west.

  • Opening hours: Always open, open year-round. Parking is available.

🐢 Jangan-mun Geobuk Market and Haenggung-dong Mural Village

Jangan-mun Geobuk Market (24 Suseong-ro 382beon-gil, Jangan-gu, Suwon (수원시 장안구 수성로382번길 24), Yeonghwa-dong, ☎ 031-242-5833) is a market north of Jangan-mun Gate, the main gate of Hwaseong. The former landowner's nickname was "Geobugi" (turtle), and people going to shop said they were going to "Geobugi's place," which gave the market its name. The outer walls of the shops are unified in brick, and there is a street called "Yeonghwa Old Road" that remembers the old Yeonghwa Station.

  • What is sold: Farm products, seafood, and livestock products.
  • Opening hours and closed days: They differ by shop.
  • Parking: 1 hour free, and after that, if you use the market, the fee can be settled at the shop.

Haenggung-dong Mural Village (9-7 Hwaseomun-ro 72beon-gil, Paldal-gu (팔달구 화서문로72번길 9-7), Buksu-dong, ☎ 031-244-4519) is a place where murals were painted in the alleys of "Haenggung-dong," the name for the 12 neighborhoods around Hwaseong. There are 6 themed alleys, with photo zones in front of the murals. There are many workshops and cafes. Always open, open year-round.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Thinking it is open until 18:00 in winter too — Paldalmun Gate and Hwaseong Haenggung Palace close at 17:00 from November to February.
  2. Looking for Nammun Market as one market — It is a name for the markets around Paldalmun Gate grouped together. Hanbok is Yeongdong Market, fried chicken is Paldalmun Market, and sundae is Jidong Market.
  3. Going without knowing about the hanbok market — Yeongdong Market is a hanbok and textile specialty market with 40-plus pure hanbok shops among 300-plus stores.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. How much is admission to Hwaseong Haenggung Palace?

A. The Hwaseong Haenggung Palace admission fee is not registered in the tourism organization's information. Parking at Paldalmun Gate is registered as free.

Q. Can I use a card?

A. According to the tourism organization, cards are accepted at Hwaseong Haenggung Palace, and at Yeongdong Market it differs by store. Card information is not registered for the other markets.
