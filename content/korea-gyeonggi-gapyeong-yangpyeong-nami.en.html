1. "Nami Island (남이섬) = small island": Korea's #1 Island · Winter Sonata (겨울연가) (2002) Global Enthusiast Matrix #1.

2. "Garden of Morning Calm (아침고요) = ordinary arboretum": Korea's #2 · Est. 1996 · Spring·Autumn Matrix #1 · Winter Night Lighting.

3. "Dumulmeori (두물머리) = ordinary river": Yangpyeong (양평) Instagram Hotspot #1 · Confluence of Hangang (한강) + Bukhangang (북한강) Rivers · MZ Enthusiast.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. Top priority for first-time foreigners: Nami Island (남이섬) + The Garden of Morning Calm (아침고요수목원) + Yangpyeong Dumulmeori (양평 두물머리) = 1 day.

2. Nami Island (남이섬) seasons: Spring (cherry blossoms) · Summer (grass·biking) · Autumn (foliage) · Winter (Winter Sonata (겨울연가) Enthusiast #1).

3. Garden of Morning Calm (아침고요) night lighting: December~February Enthusiast · Instagram Enthusiast Matrix.

4. Dumulmeori (두물머리) sunrise·night view: Seasonal Enthusiast · Instagram Hotspot #1.

5. Allergies: Gapyeong Dakgalbi (닭갈비) (spicy stir-fried chicken) · Makguksu (막국수) (buckwheat noodles) = pre-notify for soy·nuts.

6. Recommended personas: Families·Seniors = Nami Island (남이섬) + Garden of Morning Calm (아침고요) / MZ·Instagram = Dumulmeori (두물머리) + Cafe / Enthusiasts = 1-night stay + Pool Villa.
