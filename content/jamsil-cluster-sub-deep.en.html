Jamsil Guide: Lotte World Adventure, Lotte World Tower Seoul Sky, Lotte World Aquarium; Seokchon Lake, Songridan-gil; Olympic Park, SOMA Museum of Art, Seoul Baekje Museum

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places you can visit in and around Jamsil (잠실), Songpa-gu (송파구), Seoul. It covers 3 places around Lotte World, 2 around Seokchon Lake and 3 around Olympic Park. The places with registered fees are Lotte World Aquarium (롯데월드 아쿠아리움) and SOMA Museum of Art (소마미술관), and Seoul Baekje Museum (한성백제박물관) is registered as free.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Lotte World — Adventure, Seoul Sky and Aquarium

Lotte World Adventure (롯데월드 어드벤처) (240 Olympic-ro (올림픽로), Songpa-gu, ☎ 1661-2000) is an urban complex leisure space run by the Lotte Group (롯데그룹). It is made up of the indoor theme park Adventure and Magic Island (매직아일랜드), an outdoor theme park on Seokchon Lake (석촌호수), and it also has a shopping mall, a folk museum, an ice rink, a hotel and a department store. There are attractions, parades, laser shows and performances, and at the main stage Garden Stage (가든스테이지) seasonal musical shows are held. The Folk Museum (민속박물관) has a history exhibition hall, a model village and a marketplace street.

  • Opening hours: Monday~Thursday and Sunday 10:00~21:00 (last entry 20:00), Friday~Saturday 10:00~22:00 (last entry 21:00).
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Depending on the weather, some Magic Island attractions may stop, and depending on conditions on site they may be suspended or under maintenance.
  • Parking: Available (about 5,400 spaces).

Lotte World Tower Seoul Sky (롯데월드타워 서울스카이) (300 Olympic-ro, Songpa-gu, ☎ 1661-2000) is the observation deck at the top of Lotte World Tower (롯데월드타워). According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, it is the highest observation deck in Korea, with a 360-degree view of Seoul. It has experience facilities 500m above the ground, and also exhibitions, coffee and souvenirs. The Sky Shuttle (스카이셔틀) takes you from the 1st basement floor to the 118th floor in 1 minute. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, the Sky Shuttle is the world's first to have monitors on the ceiling and three walls. At the media table, you can see Joseon-era Seoul recreated in 3D.

  • Opening hours: Sunday~Thursday 10:30~22:00 (last entry 21:00), Friday~Saturday 10:30~23:00 (last entry 22:00). Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available.

Lotte World Aquarium (롯데월드 아쿠아리움) (same address, ☎ 1661-2000) is an aquarium where you see marine life in 13 theme zones. You walk along the flow of the ecosystem from rivers to the coast to the sea. According to the Korea Tourism Organization introduction, the 25 m tank is the largest in Korea, and the 14 hands-on education programs are the most in Korea.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~20:00 (last entry 19:00). Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Fee: Adults and teenagers 35,000 won, children and seniors 31,000 won.
  • Parking: With an Aquarium ticket, 200 won per 10 minutes (up to 4 hours), and 200 won per 10 minutes after that.

📍 Around Seokchon Lake — Seokchon Lake and Songridan-gil

Seokchon Lake (석촌호수) (Jamsil-dong (잠실동), Songpa-gu, ☎ Songpa-gu Office Parks and Green Spaces Division (송파구청 공원녹지과) 02-2147-3380) was made into a park in the early 1980s from the part of a Han River side channel that was left as a lake when the channel was filled in 1969. Songpa-daero (송파대로) divides it into East Lake (동호) and West Lake (서호), and it has a jogging course and walking paths. West Lake has Seoul Norimadang (서울놀이마당) and Lotte World Magic Island. When the cherry blossoms bloom in spring, it is crowded day and night. It is 560m from Exit 2 of Seokchon Gobun Station (석촌고분역) on Subway Line 9.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

Songridan-gil (송리단길) (23 Baekjegobun-ro 43-gil (백제고분로43길), Songpa-gu, ☎ Songpa Tourist Information Center (송파관광정보센터) 02-2147-2109) is a street that runs from Seokchon Lake East Lake to Songpanaru Station (송파나루역). Korean, Japanese, Chinese and Western restaurants, dessert cafes, small goods shops and bookstores line the alleys.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

📍 Around Olympic Park — Olympic Park, SOMA Museum of Art and Seoul Baekje Museum

Olympic Park (올림픽공원) (424 Olympic-ro, Songpa-gu, ☎ 02-410-1114) is a park completed in 1986 for the 86 Asian Games and the 88 Seoul Olympics, and it is run by the Korea Sports Promotion Foundation (국민체육진흥공단). Six stadiums are arranged in a semicircle around Mongchontoseong Fortress (몽촌토성), a restored Baekje (백제) site in the middle. Over an area of 430,000 pyeong (평), it has lawns, Mongchon Moat (몽촌해자), the Peace Plaza (평화의 광장), Olympic commemorative sculptures and outdoor sculptures, and it has performance venues such as KSPO DOME, Ticketlink Live Arena (티켓링크 라이브 아레나), Olympic Hall (올림픽홀), Woori Financial Art Hall (우리금융아트홀) and Muse Live (뮤즈라이브).

  • Opening hours: On foot or by bicycle 05:00~22:00 (plaza area 24:00), by vehicle 06:00~22:00. During late-night events, gate opening is adjusted.
  • Closed days: Open year-round. Parking: Available.

SOMA Museum of Art (소마미술관) (same address, ☎ 02-425-1077) is an art museum inside the Olympic Park sculpture park. A yellow-clay path leads from the museum into the sculpture park, and there are Hall 1, with indoor exhibition space, and Hall 2, connected to Subway Line 9.

  • Opening hours: 10:00~18:00 (last entry 17:30). Closed days: Every Monday, January 1, Seollal (설) and Chuseok (추석) days.
  • Fee: Individual adults (age 25~64) 5,000 won, teenagers (age 13~24) 4,000 won, children (age 7~12) 3,000 won. Groups of 20 or more pay adults 2,500 won, teenagers 2,000 won, children 1,500 won. The group size standard may vary by exhibition. These apply separately to Hall 1 and Hall 2, and special exhibitions have separately set fees.
  • Parking: 600 won per 10 minutes. 1 hour free with a paid admission of 5,000 won or more (entry registration at the information desk required).

Seoul Baekje Museum (한성백제박물관) (71 Wiryeseong-daero (위례성대로), Songpa-gu, ☎ 02-2152-5800) is a museum that protects and manages the sites of the Baekje royal capital and carries out research and exhibitions on them. In the lobby is a symbolic exhibit, a thin slice cut from a cross-section of the Pungnaptoseong Fortress (풍납토성) wall. Exhibition Hall 1 covers the Paleolithic, Neolithic, Bronze Age and Early Iron Age cultures of the Han River basin, Exhibition Hall 2 covers the 493 years of the Hanseong period (한성도읍기), when Baekje founded its state in what is now Seoul and grew, and Exhibition Hall 3 covers the rivalry among Baekje, Goguryeo (고구려) and Silla (신라) around the Han River and Baekje after the fall of Hanseong (한성).

  • Opening hours: Summer (March~October) weekdays and weekends 09:00~19:00, winter (November~February) weekdays 09:00~19:00 and weekends 09:00~18:00. Fridays 09:00~21:00.
  • Closed days: Every Monday (open if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, and closing days set by the mayor.
  • Fee: Free. Special exhibitions may charge a fee.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. SOMA Museum of Art and Seoul Baekje Museum close on Monday — SOMA Museum of Art closes every Monday, January 1, and Seollal and Chuseok days, and Seoul Baekje Museum closes every Monday (open if it is a public holiday) and January 1. The other places are open year-round.
  2. Some places open late on Fridays and Saturdays — Lotte World Adventure is open until 22:00 on Friday~Saturday, Seoul Sky until 23:00 on Friday~Saturday, and Seoul Baekje Museum until 21:00 on Friday.
  3. Lotte World Magic Island depends on the weather — Because it is an outdoor theme park, some attractions may stop depending on the weather.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which places can I see for free?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, Seoul Baekje Museum is free (special exhibitions may charge a fee). Seokchon Lake, Songridan-gil and Olympic Park are always open.

Q. Which places are open late at night?

A. Seoul Sky is open until 23:00 on Friday~Saturday (last entry 22:00), Lotte World Adventure until 22:00 on Friday~Saturday (last entry 21:00), and Olympic Park is open to people on foot until 22:00 (plaza area 24:00).
