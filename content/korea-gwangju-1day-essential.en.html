🎯 Why a Gwangju Guide?

Gwangju (광주) = Korea's #5 Metropolitan City (Gwangyeoksi) · Population 1.45 million · Democratization Movement (Minjuhwa Undong) in 1980 + ACC (Asia Culture Center) in 2014 + Mudeungsan (Mount Mudeung) 1,187m + Korea's #1 Hansik (Korean Food) Hub. Foreigner pain points = (a) The misconception that "Gwangju is unknown" (b) The May 18th Democratization Movement (1980, Jeon Doo-hwan's military regime) = The matrix of Korean democracy (c) ACC (2014~) = Asia's #1 Cultural Complex (Munhwa Bokhap) · A matrix for foreign enthusiasts (d) Mudeungsan (무등산) = 1,187m · National Park · Foreigner hiking (e) Hanjeongsik (Korean set meal) is Korea's #1 matrix (Jeollado (전라도) = Korea's #1 Hanbok (Korean food) home base) (f) KTX Gwangju Songjeong Station (광주송정역) is 1 hour 30 minutes away.

This guide covers 4 pillars, 12 signatures + a full 1-day itinerary + English communication, transportation, and payment for foreigners.

🏛 4 Pillars Deep Dive

① ACC (Asia Culture Center, 2014~)

  • Signature: Since 2014, Asia's #1 Cultural Complex · 113,000m² · Asia culture, art, performance matrix
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★★ (Full English set · MZ (Millennial and Gen Z, younger generations) + Family enthusiast)
  • Hours: 09:00-18:00 · Closed on Mondays · Free or ₩5,000 (~$3.7 / ~27 RMB / ~550 JPY) (special exhibitions)
  • Recommended: Children's Culture Center + Former Jeollanam-do Provincial Office (옛 도청) + Performances = 2-3 hours

② Mudeungsan (Mount Mudeung, 1,187m, National Park)

  • Signature: Gwangju enthusiast hiking · 1,187m · National Park · Seoseokdae (서석대) and Ipseokdae (입석대) columnar joints (Jusangjeolli)
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★ (English signage · Hiking enthusiast)
  • Recommended: Jeungsimsa Temple (증심사) → Seoseokdae (서석대) = 3-4h · Enthusiast course 5-6h

③ May 18th Democratization Movement Matrix (1980)

  • Signature: May 18th Democratization Movement of 1980 · Jeon Doo-hwan's military regime · Korea's #1 democracy matrix
  • Locations: May 18th Democracy Square (5·18 민주광장) (Former Jeollanam-do Provincial Office) · May 18th Memorial Park (5·18 기념공원) · Museum (박물관)
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★★ (Full English set · Educational · History enthusiast #1)
  • Recommended: Museum + Park + Square = 2-3 hours · Essential for Korea enthusiast matrix

④ Hanjeongsik (Korean set meal)·Market Cluster (Gwangju = Korea's #1 Hansik (Korean Food) Home Base)

  • Signature: Gwangju Hanjeongsik (광주 한정식) · Jeollado (전라도) Hanjeongsik (한정식) Korea's #1 matrix · Songjeong Station Market (송정역시장) · Chungjang-ro (충장로)
  • Foreigner-Friendliness: ★★★★★ (Partial English menu · Card payment)
  • Price: Hanjeongsik (한정식) (per person) = ₩25,000-₩80,000 (~$18-$59 / ~135-430 RMB / ~2,700-8,800 JPY)

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Gwangju = Unknown": Korea's #5 Metropolitan City (Gwangyeoksi) · Democratization in 1980 · ACC in 2014 · Mudeungsan (무등산) · Hanjeongsik (한정식) hub.

2. "May 18th = Old News": The 1980 Democratization Movement = Korea's #1 democracy matrix · Essential for foreign enthusiasts.

3. "ACC = Just a Museum": Asia's #1 Cultural Complex (Munhwa Bokhap) since 2014 · 113,000m².

4. "Hanjeongsik = Same Everywhere": Gwangju = Korea's #1 Hansik (Korean food) home base · Jeollado (전라도) Hanjeongsik (한정식) matrix.

5. "Mudeungsan = Just a Mountain": 1,187m · National Park · Seoseokdae (서석대) and Ipseokdae (입석대) columnar joints (Jusangjeolli) = Korea's #1 matrix.
