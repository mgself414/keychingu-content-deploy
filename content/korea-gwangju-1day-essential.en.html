1. "Gwangju = unknown": Korea #5 Metropolitan City·Democracy 1980·ACC 2014·Mudeungsan·Hanjeongsik 1st.

4. "Hanjeongsik = the same everywhere": Gwangju = Korea #1 Korean food origin·Jeolla-do Korean cuisine matrix.

5. "Mudeungsan Mountain = General Mountain": 1187m·National Park·Seoseokdae·Ipseokdae Columnar Joint = Korean Matrix #1.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. No. 1 for foreigners: ACC + 5·18 + Hanjeongsik + Yangnim-dong = 1 day matrix.

2. 5·18 Museum English: English full set·Foreign Mania Matrix #1·Educational.

3. Gwangju Hanjeongsik: 5~7 side dishes·Jeolla-do Korea #1 Matrix·25,000~80,000 won/person.
