Busan Winter & Christmas Guide 2026 — December 5~6 Degrees Warmer Than Seoul, a Tree Street, Christmas Village and the Haeundae Light Festival in One Day

Subtitle: Christmas Village Busan 2026 runs 11/26~12/25 at Busan Cinema Center (영화의전당), paid 5,900~14,900 won (YES24 sales page) · Haeundae Light Festival ran 11/29~1/18 in 2025, 18:00~23:00, free · Gwangbok-ro Winter Light Tree Festival ran 12/5~2/22 in 2025, 17:30~22:00 · Blueline Park beach train winter hours 09:00~19:00 · 2027 Lunar New Year holiday 2/6~2/9 — as of 2026-10-31

🎯 Why Busan in Winter — Same December, Different Temperature

For anyone in Seoul wondering where to take a winter trip, Busan is the "less cold option." By the Korea Meteorological Administration 1991~2020 normals, December in Busan averages a daytime high of 10.4℃ and a morning low of 2.0℃, while Seoul in the same month is 4.2℃ and -3.4℃. In January, Busan's low barely brushes freezing at -0.1℃, while Seoul drops to -5.5℃ [secondary — table quoting KMA normals]. It is a seaside city and the wind bites, so pack gloves and a scarf (#1318).

Last season, in December, Busan had Christmas in three places at once: the tree street on Gwangbok-ro (광복로) in the old downtown, the light festival on Gunam-ro (구남로) in Haeundae (해운대), and the Christmas Village outdoors at Busan Cinema Center. The names are similar and easy to mix up, so just remember two street festivals with no admission fee announced, and one paid market. Only the Village has confirmed dates for this year. On Reddit's r/koreatravel, posts such as "Busan before Christmas" and "Busan: 1 weekend in december or 4 days in february" appeared in October [secondary — checked 2026-10-30].

The Christmas Village is the only event with a published 2026-27 schedule (as of 10/31). The rest are listed with 2025 actuals, and this issue will be updated once announcements come out.

① Christmas Village Busan 2026 — The Only Event With Dates Out for This Year

  • Dates · Location: 2026-11-26~12-25, outdoors at Busan Cinema Center (120 Suyeonggangbyeon-daero, Haeundae-gu). Hosted by Market Changgo (마켓창고), organized by Food Travel and Busan Cinema Center, inquiries 070-4680-1718 (KTO English page)
  • Hours: Weekdays 16:00~22:00, weekend daytime 11:00~22:00, weekend evening 16:00~22:00, Christmas season 11:00~22:00 (YES24)
  • Price: Weekday 5,900 won · weekend daytime 5,900 won · weekend evening 7,900 won · Christmas season 14,900 won, all ages admitted (YES24 sales page, viewed 10/31). Other listings are circulating with 7,900~16,900 won, so go by the amount on the payment screen. Tickets open 11/2 at 15:00, and same-day bookings cannot be canceled, refunded or changed (YES24)
  • Operating days and season dates — note: This is an open-date ticket, so the sales page lists no operating days, and it does not say from which day the "Christmas season" starts. Some listings say it is open only Thursday to Sunday until 12/20, so if you plan to go Monday to Wednesday, check with the organizer (070-4680-1718) first. Late-December weekends may fall under the season price (14,900 won)
  • Getting there: Busan subway Line 2 Centum City Station. It is in the same area as Shinsegae Centum City, so it pairs well with shopping (#1327)
  • An event with the same name also exists in Seoul. See #746 for our 12 Korean Christmas markets

② Haeundae Light Festival — A Free Lit-Up Street in Front of the Sea

  • 2025, 12th edition: 2025-11-29 (Sat)~2026-01-18 (Sun), lights on 18:00~23:00, Gunam-ro Haeundae Square and the Haeundae Beach area, free, theme "STELLAR HAEUNDAE" (Busan city event listing). The Starlight Square experience area ran 18:00~22:00. Inquiries: Haeundae-gu Office 051-749-4062
  • The 2026-27 13th edition has not been announced by Haeundae-gu yet. Recent editions started in late November and ended in mid-January, so a December trip will likely fall within the run
  • Getting there: Get off at Line 2 Haeundae Station and walk toward the sea along Gunam-ro, and the festival street continues right on
  • For daytime routes in Haeundae see #243 and #1023, and for the whole Busan night-view picture see #1326

③ Gwangbok-ro Winter Light Tree Festival — A Winter Street in the Old Downtown

  • 2025-26 actuals: 2025-12-05 (Fri)~2026-02-22 (Sun), lights on 17:30~22:00, Gwangbok-ro and Gwangbok Jungang-ro area, Jung-gu, inquiries 051-600-4085 (Busan city event listing). The 2026-27 schedule has not been announced
  • Walk in from Line 1 Nampo Station. It sits right next to Jagalchi Market (자갈치시장), Gukje Market (국제시장) and BIFF Square (BIFF 광장), so link them as market by day, lights at dusk (#244 · #1022)
  • On the 2025 schedule it ran through late February, so people who missed December could still see it in January~February

④ Blueline Park — How to Ride the Sea on a Winter Day

To fill the hours before dark, the beach train running along the coast from Haeundae Mipo to Songjeong and the Sky Capsule on an elevated rail are good picks. Winter (11~2) operation is as follows (Blueline Park official).

  • Heavy rain or a typhoon can stop service, and weekend and holiday hours may change (official notice). It runs in light rain
  • For a round trip, the 2-ride ticket (14,000 won) is better. A single-ride ticket is one way, so paying 10,000 won out and 8,000 won back from Cheongsapo separately comes to 18,000 won
  • The Sky Capsule is priced per capsule, so 2~4 people is a better deal than going alone. If you are solo, the beach train alone gives plenty of sea views

⑤ Year-End and New Year — How It Went in 2025

  • Gwangalli drone show: From 22:00 on 2025-12-31 to 00:10 on 2026-01-01, 2,500 drones went up and 70,000 people were expected. For the sunrise at Haeundae Beach, 100,000+ people were expected and subway Lines 1~4 added trains and extended hours (Herald Economy report, 2025-12-29)
  • The 2026 New Year's Eve event has not been announced. If the format is the same, lodging near Gwangalli Beach fills up early. For all the sunrise spots see #750
  • The Polar Bear Swimming Festival (a sea-plunge event): the 38th edition was held 2/7~9 in February 2025 at Gwangalli, with an entry fee of 30,000 won (Busan city event listing). We could not find a schedule for later editions this time, and since the timing and venue change every year, there is no 2027 schedule yet either

The 2027 Lunar New Year (Seollal, 설날) is 2/7 (Sun), and the 2/6~2/8 holiday gets a substitute holiday on 2/9 (Tue), making 4 days (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute calendar). If you go in February, either avoid those four days or deliberately pick places that stay open through the holiday (#752).

🍲 What to Eat in Busan in Winter

  • Amberjack (방어): Winter (around 12~2) is counted as its peak season [secondary — Korea JoongAng Daily]. For the Jagalchi Market raw-fish restaurant route see #244
  • Pork soup (돼지국밥) and milmyeon (밀면): On a cold day, hot pork soup comes first. How to order and where to go: #1320
  • Grilled hagfish (곰장어) and nakgopsae (낙곱새): For a winter-evening drinking table see #1410
  • Dietary note: Pork soup uses pork broth, so it does not fit halal or vegetarian diets. Raw fish is relatively OK on the halal side but not for vegetarians, and the chili sauce and seasoning may contain salted seafood, so ask before ordering

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes

1. Thinking the Gwangbok-ro festival and the Christmas Village are the same place — Gwangbok-ro (old downtown, Line 1 Nampo Station) and Busan Cinema Center (Haeundae-gu, Line 2 Centum City Station) take 40+ minutes by subway with a transfer at Seomyeon [estimate]. If you want both in one day, plan the route.

2. Thinking the Christmas Village is free — It is paid. The Christmas season price (14,900 won) is more than double the weekday price (5,900 won).

3. Underestimating the sea wind — Even if it is warmer than Seoul, the beach is windy. Viewing the evening lights means standing outside for 1~2 hours.

4. Scheduling markets over the February Lunar New Year holiday — On 2027 2/6~2/9, many markets and restaurants close and KTX sells out (#1374).

5. Trusting 2025 dates as 2026 dates — The light festival, tree festival and New Year events have not been announced. Check the Busan city event listing again before you leave.

⚙ FAQ 3

1. Can I see all of Busan in December in one day? — The Haeundae side (Village, light festival, Blueline Park, with the light festival on the 2025 basis) fits in a day (Route A), and the old-downtown tree festival needs one more day (Route B).

2. Can I go on Christmas Day itself? — The Village is open through 12/25. But it is the Christmas season price and the most crowded. Among operating days, weekday evenings and weekend daytime are cheapest at 5,900 won, and weekday evenings are the quietest.

3. Can I do it as a day trip from Seoul? — By KTX it is about 2 hours 40 minutes one way (per the #1372 timetable), so it is possible, but the lights come on in the evening, so we recommend staying 1 night. Ticket booking is in #1374, and the 3-day 2-night route is in #1114.
