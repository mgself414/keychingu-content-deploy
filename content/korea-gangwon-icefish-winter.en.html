Gangwon Ice Fishing Festivals — Hwacheon Sancheoneo and Pyeongchang Trout, a Practical 1-Night Winter Guide

Subtitle: The festivals you can actually attend now are Hwacheon Sancheoneo and Pyeongchang Trout (the Inje Smelt Festival has been cancelled three years in a row, 2024–2026) · January season · Dates and fees change every year · Practical tips on warmth, safety and transport

ISSUE 04 · #1069 · 2026-06-13 (revised 2026-10-20)

🎯 Why a guide to the Gangwon ice fishing festivals?

Holes line up across a frozen river, and the fingertips of your gloves holding the fishing line go numb in no time. A Gangwon winter ice fishing festival is a trip to see this one scene. The big festivals a foreign traveler can plan for right now are the Hwacheon Sancheoneo Ice Festival (화천 산천어축제) and the Pyeongchang Trout Festival (평창 송어축제).

Earlier guides bundled in the Inje Smelt Festival (인제 빙어축제), but because the water level at Soyang River Dam (소양강댐) kept the ice from forming, it was cancelled three years in a row: 2024, 2025 and 2026. Whether it will be held in 2027 has not been decided yet. If you want to include Inje in your itinerary, check that year's official announcement first.

For both festivals, if the weather is warm, the opening may be delayed or some programs may change. Dates and fees differ every year, so check the official site again right before you leave.

🏛 Festival-by-festival deep dive

① Hwacheon Sancheoneo Ice Festival (화천 산천어축제)

  • The main event is catching sancheoneo in the ice fishing zone. You can have your catch cleaned and grilled on site.
  • In 2026, part of the admission fee was returned as local gift certificates or vouchers. For 2027, a plan to raise both the admission fee and the size of the refund is under review (MTN report, 2026-10-02; not confirmed).
  • A day trip is possible, but traveling to and from Seoul by bus takes a long time, so one night is more comfortable.

② Pyeongchang Trout Festival (평창 송어축제)

  • The main event is trout fishing on the ice of Odaecheon Stream. In 2026, the 20th anniversary, it ran for 32 days from Jan 9 to Feb 9, and after the official schedule ended, free fishing for the released trout continued for about another week (2026 report).
  • KTX Jinbu (Odaesan) Station (진부오대산역) is available, which suits train travelers. It pairs well with Pyeongchang Daegwallyeong Sheep Farm (평창 양떼목장), ski resorts and Daegwallyeong (대관령) (#1039).

③ Inje Smelt Festival (인제 빙어축제) (currently on hiatus)

  • Cancelled for three years in a row, 2024–2026, because the ice could not form. The period shown on the official website was "TBD" (checked 2026-10-13).
  • We will update this guide when it is held again. Until then, leave it out of your plans.

🧰 Before you go — practical checklist

  • Dates and fees: Check that year's announcement on the official site. Do not rely on dates from previous years.
  • Warmth: You will be standing or sitting on the ice for a long time. Bring a thick padded jacket, waterproof boots, spare gloves, hand warmers and anti-slip ice cleats. Whether rentals are available on site, and at what price, differs by festival, so check on site.
  • Transport: For Hwacheon, many people take an intercity bus from Seoul; for Pyeongchang, many take the KTX to Jinbu (Odaesan) Station (진부오대산역). Shuttle service to the festival grounds is announced each year.
  • Lodging: Gangwon lodging fills up quickly when the dates overlap with weekends or the Lunar New Year (Seollal, 설) holiday. Book as soon as the dates are set.
  • Payment: Many places accept cards, but cash can be handier at small stalls.
  • Emergency: ☎ 119 · Interpretation and tourist information ☎ 1330

⚠ 4 common mistakes foreigners make

1. Booking based on last year's dates — Festival dates change every year, and in a warm winter the opening can be delayed. Confirm transport and lodging after the official announcement is out.

2. Putting the Inje Smelt Festival (인제 빙어축제) in your itinerary — It was cancelled three years in a row, 2024–2026. Do not include it until you have confirmed that year's announcement that it will be held.

3. Going in city winter clothes — There is nowhere to shelter from the wind on the ice. Waterproof boots and spare gloves make the difference.

4. Walking anywhere on the ice — Move only inside the zones the festival manages for safety. Do not step onto ice beyond the control lines.

⚙ FAQ

1. Where should I start if it is my first time? — If you are traveling by bus, Hwacheon is easier; if by train, Pyeongchang (KTX Jinbu (Odaesan) Station (진부오대산역)) is easier.

2. What about the 2027 schedule? — As of October 2026, neither festival has been announced. Check the official site for announcements.

3. What about the Inje Smelt Festival (인제 빙어축제)? — Cancelled three years in a row, 2024–2026. Whether it will be held in 2027 is undecided.

4. What are the fees? — Hwacheon: 2026 admission ₩15,000 (partly refunded), with an increase under review for 2027. Pyeongchang: check that year's announcement.

5. Is it okay to go with children? — Many visitors come as families. Prepare for the cold and for slipping even more carefully than you would for adults.

⚠ Medical and safety disclaimer

Do ice activities only in the zones the festival manages. Watch for frostbite on hands, feet and ears during long exposure, and if you start to shiver, go into an indoor shelter. Emergency ☎ 119, tourist interpretation ☎ 1330.
