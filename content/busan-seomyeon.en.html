Busan Seomyeon·Jeonpo·Dongnae — 8 Spots: Underground Shopping Center and Cafe Street, Hot Springs and Museums, Hours and Fares

Subtitle: For first-time visitors to Busan, this guide groups 4 spots in the Seomyeon zone and 4 spots in the Dongnae zone. Seomyeon Mall in the Seomyeon Underground Shopping Center is open 10:00–22:00 and closed on the Tuesday of the first week of every month. Lotte Department Store Busan Main Branch is open 10:30–20:00 on regular business days. Jeonpo Cafe Street is a 6 min walk from Line 2 Jeonpo Station Exit 7. In Dongnae, the hot spring facility Heosimcheong is open 05:30–22:00 all year round, and the adult fare is 15,000 won on weekdays and 18,000 won on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. Bokcheon Museum and Dongnae Eupseong History Hall are free and closed on Mondays. From Gimhae Airport to Seomyeon, Busan Airport Limousine 2 costs 6,000 won for adults. Fares are the adult regular prices as seen on 2026-11-25.

ISSUE 06 · #246 · 2026-11-25 · Busan Seomyeon·Jeonpo·Dongnae

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why These 8

This list is made for foreign travelers visiting Busan for the first time. Use it when you want to walk through shopping and cafe streets in downtown Busan, away from the beach, and see hot springs and museums in Dongnae (동래).

It is divided into 2 zones.

  • Seomyeon zone (4 spots): Seomyeon Underground Shopping Center (서면 지하도상가) (Seomyeon Mall (서면몰)·Bujeon Mall (부전몰)), Jeonpo Cafe Street (전포카페거리), Lotte Department Store Busan Main Branch (롯데백화점 부산본점), Bujeon Market (부전시장).
  • Dongnae zone (4 spots): Heosimcheong (허심청), Dongnae Hot Spring Outdoor Foot Bath (동래온천 노천족욕탕), Bokcheon Museum (복천박물관), Dongnae Eupseong History Hall (동래읍성역사관).

The Seomyeon zone is reached on foot from the subway stations Seomyeon (서면역), Jeonpo (전포역) and Bujeon (부전역). In the Dongnae zone, the 2 hot spring spots are reached on foot from Line 1 Oncheonjang Station (온천장역), and the 2 museums from Line 4 Suan Station (수안역), measured from Bokcheon Museum. So for each spot this issue first gives "which exit of which station, and how many minutes".

Closing days differ from spot to spot. They are collected in a separate table below.

⚠ Notation principle: Hours, fares and directions are values read on 2026-11-25. Kakao Map business hours are values read for the 7 days starting 2026-11-25, so days of the week and closures follow that week. Opening hours, fares and bus times can change.

✈ From Gimhae Airport to Seomyeon, and the Stations in the Zone

  • Busan Airport Limousine 2 (부산 공항 리무진 2) stops in Seomyeon. According to the Busan City English notice (in effect from 2026-01-05), Limousine 2 runs 15.7 km on Bujeon–Seomyeon–Gimhae Airport. The stops are Bujeon Station, Bujeon Market, Seomyeon Station (Lotte Department Store), Dongeui University Station (동의대역), Jurye Station (주례역) and Gimhae Airport (김해공항).
  • Fare: adults 6,000 won, teenagers 4,800 won, children 3,000 won. From 2026-01-05 the adult fare dropped from 7,500 won to 6,000 won.
  • First and last buses: from Bujeon, the first bus is 06:00 and the last bus is 20:00. From the airport, the first bus is 07:20 and the last bus is 21:30. The interval is about 60–80 min. If you miss 1 bus you may wait more than 1 hour, so match the times first.
  • By subway: the Korea Airports Corporation guide to Gimhae Airport says to take the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail Transit (부산김해경전철) from Airport Station and transfer to Subway Line 2 at Sasang Station (사상역) and to Line 3 at Daejeo Station (대저역). Taking Line 2 from Sasang Station gets you to Seomyeon Station.
  • Subway fares: per the Busan Transportation Corporation fare guide (read 2026-11-25), adults pay 1,600 won for 1 section and 1,800 won for 2 sections with a transit card or mobile payment. A QR ticket is 1,700 won for 1 section and 1,900 won for 2 sections.
  • The station differs from spot to spot. Seomyeon Station is the transfer station where Line 1 and Line 2 meet. Jeonpo Station is on Line 2, Bujeon Station and Oncheonjang Station are on Line 1, and Suan Station is on Line 4. Busan Station (부산역) is also a Line 1 station (Kakao Map). Seomyeon Station, Bujeon Station and Oncheonjang Station are on the same Line 1.

🚦 Before You Walk — Shared Safety and Manners

  • Most cafes on Jeonpo Cafe Street are small shops run by individuals. The Korea Tourism Organization introduces them that way. Closing days and closing times differ by shop, so if you look at only 1 place you may find the door closed.
  • The outdoor foot bath closes in bad weather. The Dongnae-gu Office hot spring guide lists bad weather, public holidays and summer (July–August) as closing days.
  • Call 119 for accidents or emergencies and 112 to report a crime.

① Seomyeon Zone — 4 Spots

This is the area around Seomyeon Station, where Subway Line 1 and Line 2 meet. The underground shopping center and the department store are reached on foot from Seomyeon Station, the cafe street from Jeonpo Station and the market from Bujeon Station. Opening times differ by spot. Bujeon Market is 07:00–19:00 per the Visit Busan guide, Seomyeon Mall opens at 10:00 and Lotte Department Store opens at 10:30.

Seomyeon Underground Shopping Center — Seomyeon Mall·Bujeon Mall

  • What it is: an arcade of shops lined up along the underground passage connected to Seomyeon Station (서면역). The name splits into Seomyeon Mall (서면몰) and Bujeon Mall (부전몰) depending on the section. Busan Facilities Corporation (부산시설공단) gives the operating guide.
  • Seomyeon Mall hours: 10:00–22:00 per the official Busan Facilities Corporation guide.
  • Seomyeon Mall closing day: the Tuesday of the first week of every month.
  • Seomyeon Mall location: per the official Busan Facilities Corporation guide, it is at 717 Jungang-daero, underground (Bujeon-dong, 부전동), Busanjin-gu (부산진구), and the section is "Subway Seomyeon Station to Jyudisu Taehwa (쥬디스태화)".
  • Seomyeon Mall directions: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 5 min walk (283 m) from Seomyeon Station Exit 2.
  • Bujeon Mall: the Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25) is 10:00–22:00. Per Kakao Map directions, it is a 4 min walk (226 m) from Seomyeon Station Exit 12.
  • Timing: it is open until 22:00, the latest-closing shopping place in this zone. See Lotte Department Store, which closes at 20:00, first and put the underground shopping center after it. If you are in Seomyeon on the Tuesday of the first week of the month, move Seomyeon Mall to another day.

Jeonpo Cafe Street

  • What it is: the Korea Tourism Organization introduces it as a street where 30 or so cafes are gathered across from Seomyeon 1-beon-ga (서면 1번가). It became known after 2010, when small cafes began to open one by one on a street of shops selling tools and hardware, and most are small cafes run by individuals.
  • Opening hours: always open with no closing day, per the official Korea Tourism Organization guide. This is about the street itself, and cafe hours differ by shop.
  • Directions: per the official Visit Busan guide, it is a 6 min walk from Subway Line 2 Jeonpo Station (전포역) Exit 7. Kakao Map directions also give a 6 min walk (382 m) from Jeonpo Station Exit 7, the same.
  • Finding the entrance: Visit Busan says the buildings next to Nolimaru (놀이마루) (a school building and playground) mark the entrance of the street. The representative address is set differently by each guide agency, so instead of pinning an address, use the 6 min walk (382 m) from Jeonpo Station Exit 7 as the reference and take the buildings next to Nolimaru as the entrance marker.
  • Inquiries: Busanjin-gu Office Economy and Tourism Division 051-605-4522.

Alleys to walk on from here

  • Jeonpo Gonggu-gil (전포공구길): per the official Visit Busan guide, it starts across a large road after you walk up along the cafe street. After you pass Busanjin Fire Station (부산진소방서), there are 4 narrow alleys side by side. The address is 20 Seojeon-ro 37beon-gil, Busanjin-gu. The Visit Busan transport guide gives 'Subway Line 1 Seomyeon Station Exit 10, Line 2 Exit 8, 7 min walk'.
  • Jeonposaitgil (전포사잇길): Busanjin-gu Office introduces it as a specialized street. The district office guide says it is the street you reach by crossing a large boulevard from Jeonpo Cafe Street.

Lotte Department Store Busan Main Branch

  • What it is: a department store next to Seomyeon Station. The Seomyeon stop of Airport Limousine 2 is named 'Seomyeon Station (Lotte Department Store)'.
  • Hours: per the official Lotte Department Store branch guide, regular business is 10:30–20:00. There are extended-hours periods when it opens until 20:30 or 21:00, and shortened-hours periods when it closes at 15:00. The restaurant floors are 10:30–21:00.
  • Kakao Map registered value (7 days from 2026-11-25): Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu 10:30–20:00, Fri, Sat, Sun 10:30–20:30.
  • Closing days: the closing days of the month are shown in the official branch guide.
  • Address: 772 Gaya-daero, Busanjin-gu (Bujeon-dong). Main phone 1577-0001.
  • Directions: the official Lotte Department Store guide gives Subway Line 1 and Line 2 Seomyeon Station. Per Kakao Map directions, it is a 5 min walk (264 m) from Seomyeon Station Exit 7.
  • Timing: if you reached Seomyeon at 10:00, walk the underground shopping center, which opens at 10:00, first. On regular business days it closes at 20:00, so do not put it at the end of an evening schedule.

Bujeon Market — Bujeon Market Town

  • What it is: a market in front of Bujeon Station (부전역). Visit Busan introduces it under the name 'Bujeon Market Town'.
  • Hours: 07:00–19:00 per the official Visit Busan guide. It differs by shop.
  • Closing days: differ by shop.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25): 23 Jungang-daero 783beon-gil, Busanjin-gu.
  • Directions: per the official Visit Busan guide, it is a 3 min walk from Subway Line 1 Bujeon Station Exit 1. Kakao Map directions also give a 3 min walk (147 m) from Bujeon Station Exit 1, the same.
  • From the airport: Airport Limousine 2 has a 'Bujeon Market' stop.
  • Timing: opening times differ by shop, so it is open from early morning. Put it in the morning, before Seomyeon Mall (10:00) and Lotte Department Store (10:30) open.

② Dongnae Zone — 4 Spots

These are 4 spots in Dongnae-gu (동래구). The 2 hot spring spots are reached on foot from Line 1 Oncheonjang Station, and the 2 museums from Line 4 Suan Station, measured from Bokcheon Museum. The stations differ, so move the hot spring side and the museum side as separate groups. Dongnae-gu Office introduces the water of Dongnae Hot Spring (동래온천) as saline and weakly alkaline.

Heosimcheong

A hot spring facility run by Hotel Nongshim. The values below are from the official Hotel Nongshim usage guide as shown on 2026-11-25.

  • Hot spring hours: 05:30–22:00. Entry is until 21:30. Open all year round. The Kakao Map registered value (2026-11-25) is also daily 05:30–22:00 with reception closing at 21:30, the same.
  • Jjimjil facility (찜질 시설): 06:30–21:00.

Fare table (adult regular price, 2026-11-25)

Category Weekdays Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays
Hot spring (regular) 15,000 won 18,000 won
Jjimjil facility (robe rental) 4,000 won extra 4,000 won extra
  • Adult basis: "regular" in the fare table means middle school students and older.
  • Time limits: 4 hours for the hot spring, 6 hours for the hot spring and jjimjil, and up to 7 hours if you also use the extra facilities. If you go over, you pay 2,000 won more per hour.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25): 32 Oncheonjang-ro 107beon-gil, Dongnae-gu, Busan, 4th–5th floors. Phone 051-550-2200.
  • Directions: per Kakao Map directions, it is a 10 min walk (506 m) from Subway Line 1 Oncheonjang Station Exit 1.
  • Timing: weekday and weekend fares differ by 3,000 won. If you can move your schedule, put it on a weekday. It is open until 22:00, so see the museums and the foot bath first and put it last. Entry ends at 21:30.

Dongnae Hot Spring Outdoor Foot Bath

  • What it is: an outdoor foot bath facility.
  • Hours (per Dongnae-gu Office hot spring guide): open Wednesday to Sunday. November–February it is 11:00–16:00, and March–October it is 10:00–17:00.
  • Closing days (per Dongnae-gu Office hot spring guide): Mondays, Tuesdays, public holidays, summer (July–August) and days of bad weather.
  • Address (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25): 21 Geumgang Park-ro 26beon-gil, Dongnae-gu, Busan. Phone 051-550-6602.
  • Directions: per Kakao Map directions, it is an 11 min walk (626 m) from Line 1 Oncheonjang Station Exit 5.
  • Timing: in winter (November–February) it is open only 5 hours a day and ends at 16:00. Put it before Heosimcheong, which is open until 22:00. On Mondays, Tuesdays and public holidays, leave out the foot bath and plan only Heosimcheong.

Bokcheon Museum

  • Hours: 09:00–18:00 per the official Visit Busan guide. Entry closes at 17:00.
  • Closing days: Mondays, January 1.
  • Fare: free.
  • Address: 63 Bokcheon-ro, Dongnae-gu, Busan. Phone 051-554-4263.
  • Directions: per the official Visit Busan guide, it is about an 18 min walk from Subway Line 4 Suan Station Exit 5.
  • Timing: it closes at 18:00, but entry ends at 17:00. It is about an 18 min walk from the station, so do not go in the late afternoon and put it in the morning, when it opens at 09:00.

Dongnae Eupseong History Hall

  • Hours: 09:00–18:00 per the official Dongnae-gu Office guide.
  • Closing days: Mondays (the next day if Monday is a public holiday), January 1, and days set by the head of the district office.
  • Fare: free.
  • Address: 18 Dongnaeyeoksagwan-gil, Dongnae-gu, Busan (Bokcheon-dong, 복천동). Phone 051-550-6634.
  • Directions: per the official Dongnae-gu Office guide, it is about 100 m from Bokcheon Museum. Walk on after you see Bokcheon Museum.
  • Surroundings: Dongnae-gu Office says the Dongnae Eupseong area has an outdoor performance stage and a multipurpose plaza at Bukmun Plaza (북문광장), and a 456 m walking path.

🗺 Routes — Half Day and Full Day

The order is set to match each spot's opening and closing times.

Half Day A — Seomyeon by Day (avoid the Tuesday of the first week of every month)

  1. Line 1 Bujeon Station Exit 1 → Bujeon Market (3 min walk). The hours in the Visit Busan guide are 07:00–19:00.
  2. Move to Seomyeon Station and go to Seomyeon Mall (5 min walk from Seomyeon Station Exit 2 per Kakao Map directions). It opens at 10:00.
  3. Lotte Department Store Busan Main Branch (5 min walk from Seomyeon Station Exit 7). It opens at 10:30.
  4. Line 2 Jeonpo Station Exit 7 → Jeonpo Cafe Street (6 min walk). The 4 cafes named in the article open at 11:00–12:00.
  5. Walk up along the cafe street and cross the large road to Jeonpo Gonggu-gil.

Half Day B — Dongnae (Wednesday to Sunday that is not a public holiday)

  1. Line 4 Suan Station Exit 5 → Bokcheon Museum (about 18 min walk). It opens at 09:00.
  2. Dongnae Eupseong History Hall (about 100 m from Bokcheon Museum).
  3. Line 1 Oncheonjang Station Exit 5 → Dongnae Hot Spring Outdoor Foot Bath (11 min walk per Kakao Map directions). November–February it is 11:00–16:00.
  4. Heosimcheong on the Oncheonjang Station Exit 1 side (10 min walk from the exit). Entry is until 21:30.

The 2 museums are at Suan Station and the 2 hot spring spots are at Oncheonjang Station, so you change stations between 2 and 3. On Tuesdays leave out the foot bath in 3, and on Mondays only Heosimcheong in 4 remains.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Lotte Department Store Busan Main Branch opens at 10:30, not 10:00. Regular business is 10:30–20:00. The place that opens at 10:00 is Seomyeon Mall in the underground shopping center, and Seomyeon Mall is open until 22:00 but closed on the Tuesday of the first week of every month. Put the underground shopping center first in the morning and the department store first in the evening.
  2. Jeonpo Cafe Street is a 6 min walk from Line 2 Jeonpo Station Exit 7, not from Seomyeon Station. The scale given by the Korea Tourism Organization is 30 or so cafes. The street is always open, but cafes have different closing days, so even among the 4 named in the Visit Busan article, 1 is closed on Tuesdays and 2 are closed on Wednesdays (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25).
  3. The 2 hot springs in Dongnae have very different opening times. Heosimcheong is open 05:30–22:00 all year round and charges a fare. The outdoor foot bath is open only Wednesday to Sunday and 11:00–16:00 in November–February, and is also closed on public holidays, in summer (July–August) and on days of bad weather (per Dongnae-gu Office hot spring guide). The exits also differ: Oncheonjang Station Exit 1 for Heosimcheong and Exit 5 for the foot bath.

⚙ 3 FAQs

  1. Which spots charge a fare and which are free? — The place that charges a fare is Heosimcheong. The adult (middle school students and older) hot spring fare is 15,000 won on weekdays and 18,000 won on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays, and using the jjimjil facility costs 4,000 won more. If you go over the time limit, 2,000 won is added per hour. The free places are Bokcheon Museum and Dongnae Eupseong History Hall. Walking along Jeonpo Cafe Street costs nothing. The Heosimcheong fare is the value seen on the Hotel Nongshim website on 2026-11-25.
  2. How do I get from Gimhae Airport to Seomyeon? — Airport Limousine 2 stops at Seomyeon Station (Lotte Department Store), Bujeon Market and Bujeon Station. It costs 6,000 won for adults, down from 7,500 won on 2026-01-05. The first bus from the airport is 07:20 and the last is 21:30, and the first bus from Bujeon to the airport is 06:00 and the last is 20:00. The interval is about 60–80 min, so if the times do not match, use the subway. As the Korea Airports Corporation guide says, take the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail Transit, transfer to Line 2 at Sasang Station and get off at Seomyeon Station.
  3. Where can I go on a Monday? — Seomyeon Mall is closed only on the Tuesday of the first week of the month, and none of the 4 cafes in Jeonpo is closed on Mondays (Kakao Map registered value, 2026-11-25). In the Dongnae zone, Bokcheon Museum, Dongnae Eupseong History Hall and the outdoor foot bath are closed on Mondays, so only Heosimcheong remains. On Mondays, plan to walk Seomyeon Mall and Jeonpo and visit only Heosimcheong in Dongnae.
