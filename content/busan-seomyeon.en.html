⚠ 5 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Busan (부산) = Haeundae (해운대) only": Seomyeon (서면) = Busan's No. 1 Downtown + Top Inbound Destination for Foreigners + A full set of Busan shopping, cafes, and restaurants. Haeundae (해운대) + Seomyeon (서면) for a 1-night, 2-day trip is a premier choice for enthusiasts.

2. "Jeonpo Cafe Street (전포카페거리) = just any cafe street": Jeonpo Cafe Street (전포카페거리) = Busan's No. 1 MZ Cafe Street + Foreigner Instagram Hotspot + 200+ diverse MZ cafes like 5GU and Flat.

3. "Busan (부산) university areas = inaccessible": Busan National University (부산대) · Kyungsung University (경성대) = Busan's No. 1 MZ Enthusiast Spot + Korea's Premier Busan University Area + A deep dive into youth culture for foreign enthusiasts.

4. "Dongnae (동래) · Oncheonjang (온천장) = only for seniors": Dongnae Oncheonjang (동래 온천장) = Korea's No. 1 Hot Spring (popular among long-time enthusiasts) + No. 1 Hotspot for Foreign Enthusiasts. 2,000 years of traditional Korean hot springs.

5. "Seomyeon (서면) nightlife = dangerous": Seomyeon (서면) nightlife = Busan's No. 1 Nightlife Hotspot for Foreigners + Foreigner-friendly + Kakao T Taxi (카카오T 택시) + No. 1 safe spot for foreign enthusiasts.
