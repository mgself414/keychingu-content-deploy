13 Day Trips from Seoul to Gyeonggi-do — Suwon, Yongin, Paju DMZ, Gapyeong and Yangpyeong: Four Winter Day Routes

Subtitle: Suwon Hwaseong Fortress and Hwaseong Haenggung Palace with two fried-chicken restaurants, Everland and the Korean Folk Village in Yongin, the DMZ Peace Tourism and Peace Gondola departing from Imjingak in Paju, Nami Island and the star-light garden at The Garden of Morning Calm in Gapyeong, and Dumulmeori and Semiwon in Yangpyeong. We grouped them into four routes you can do in one day from Seoul by public transport. Hours and fares come from the operators' official pages or Kakao Map listings, which we opened ourselves on 2026-11-25, and every value carries its source.

ISSUE 04 · #1031 · 2026-11-25 · Gyeonggi-do Day Trips

🎯 Who This Is For, and Why Four Routes

Gyeonggi-do surrounds Seoul, so a "Gyeonggi-do day" is really a choice of direction. South (Suwon and Yongin), north (Paju DMZ) and east (Gapyeong and Yangpyeong) run on different lines, so you pick only one direction per day.

This guide is for travelers staying in Seoul who want to spend one day in Gyeonggi-do. We marked places that close or change hours in winter (where we could not confirm, we said so). The detailed stories for each place are in #1032, #1033, #1034 and #1035; here we only sorted out direction, grouping and closing times.

⚠ How we mark values: Hours and fares are values from the operator's official website or from Kakao Map (카카오맵) (all confirmed 2026-11-25). The source is written next to each value. Travel times are copied only where the official guide states them; for the rest, check the route on Kakao Map (카카오맵) that day.

① Suwon — A Walk Around the Fortress and Fried-Chicken Street

From Hwaseong Haenggung Palace (화성행궁) you can reach the fortress walls and Chicken Street (통닭거리) on foot.

  • Suwon Hwaseong Fortress (수원화성) — A fortress wall built under Joseon's King Jeongjo (정조), now a walking path around the city center. The Suwon Cultural Foundation (수원문화재단) visitor guide says "Jan~Dec open at all times, no closed days, free admission", and because it is open-style you can walk it at night too (confirmed 2026-11-25). The foundation's recommended courses run 1~3 hours. The Seojangdae (서장대) side is uphill.
  • Hwaseong Haenggung Palace (화성행궁) — The palace the king used while staying in Suwon. 09:00~18:00, last entry 1 hour before closing, no closed days. Adults ₩2,000, teenagers ₩1,500. The combined ticket for Hwaseong Haenggung Palace, Suwon Hwaseong Museum (수원화성박물관) and Suwon Museum (수원박물관) is ₩4,000 for adults (all from Suwon Cultural Foundation (수원문화재단), 2026-11-25). Wearing hanbok (한복) (including modified hanbok) is listed as eligible for exemption or discount (the official table does not distinguish exemption from discount). The last Wednesday of each month is free. Night opening ran 2026-05-01~11-01, so there is none in winter. The address is Jeongjo-ro 825, Paldal-gu (팔달구 정조로 825).
  • Jinmi Tongdak (진미통닭) — A fried-chicken restaurant on Chicken Street (Jeongjo-ro 800beon-gil / 정조로800번길). Tue~Sun 11:00~23:00, closed Mondays, and also closed over the Seollal and Chuseok holidays. Registered prices are fried and whole chicken ₩21,000, seasoned and half-and-half ₩22,000, curry chicken and spicy-jjamppong chicken ₩25,000, and draft beer 500cc ₩4,500 (Kakao Map (카카오맵) owner registration, menu registered 2026-05~06, confirmed 2026-11-25). The address is Jeongjo-ro 800beon-gil 21, 2F, Paldal-gu (팔달구 정조로800번길 21 2층). Dietary notes: it is fried chicken, so the batter may contain wheat flour. No pork dishes are registered. The seasoned sauce mixes sweet and spicy. They also sell draft beer and a Jim Beam whiskey set.
  • Yongseong Tongdak Main Branch (용성통닭 본점) — A few doors up the same alley. The hours registered on Kakao Map (카카오맵) are 11:00~23:00, but 12/1 (Tue) is marked as closed. No regular closing day is registered, so check Kakao Map (카카오맵) before you go. Registered prices are fried and country-style chicken ₩21,000, seasoned and half-and-half ₩22,000, and king galbi ₩24,000 (Kakao Map (카카오맵) owner registration, menu registered 2026-08-07, confirmed 2026-11-25). The address is Jeongjo-ro 800beon-gil 15, Paldal-gu (팔달구 정조로800번길 15). Dietary notes: it is fried chicken, and the batter may contain wheat flour. No menu description is registered for the king galbi. No alcohol menu is registered.

Getting there (Suwon Cultural Foundation (수원문화재단) "How to get to main points", 2026-11-25): From Gangnam Station (강남역) take express bus 3000 and get off at Paldalmun (팔달문); the palace is about 5 minutes on foot. From Jamsil Station (잠실역) take bus 1007 (get off at Hwaseong Haenggung / Yeomingak (화성행궁·여민각)). By subway, take Line 1 or the Suin-Bundang Line to Suwon Station (수원역), go to the Novotel stop at Exit 4, take bus 11 or 13, and get off at Hwaseong Haenggung / Suwon Seongji (화성행궁·수원성지).

② Yongin — Everland or the Korean Folk Village

Both take a full day, so choose only one.

  • Everland (에버랜드) — A theme park with rides and a safari. The hours registered on Kakao Map (카카오맵) are 10:00~22:00 daily for 11/25~12/1 (confirmed 2026-11-25). For hours from 12/2, check the official app by date. The one-day pass is priced by date grade. Since 2026-10-06 prices rose by ₩3,000 each, to a top adult price of ₩71,000, ₩65,000 in peak season and ₩55,000 on regular and off-peak days (Money Today (머니투데이) 2026-09-07). Because December was ₩52,000 before the increase (CEO Score Daily (CEO스코어데일리) 2026-09-24), December is estimated at around ₩55,000. Check the actual price on the payment screen. The address is Everland-ro 199, Pogok-eup, Cheoin-gu (처인구 포곡읍 에버랜드로 199).
  • Korean Folk Village (한국민속촌) — An outdoor folk village recreating a Joseon-era town. The hours registered on Kakao Map (카카오맵) are Mon~Thu 10:00~19:00 and Fri~Sun 10:00~20:00, and the official main page shows "11/25 10:00~19:00" (2026-11-25). We could not confirm the Dec~Feb hours on the official site (it announces they change with season and weather). The one-day pass is ₩37,000 for adults on weekdays and ₩39,000 on weekends. Foreigners pay ₩22,000 if they show a passport or alien registration card (official admission fees, "applies through 2026", confirmed 2026-11-25). The 2 PM entry ticket on weekends from Dec~Mar is ₩25,000 for adults. The address is Minsokchon-ro 90, Giheung-gu (기흥구 민속촌로 90).

Getting there: To Everland there is express bus 5002 from the Gangnam side, and since 2026-08-01 there is bus 4105 from the Seoul Station (서울역) transfer center via Myeong-dong (명동) and Itaewon (이태원) (about 1 hour, Korea Times (코리아타임스) report of 2026-07-30). To the Korean Folk Village there are bus 5001-1 from Sinnonhyeon Station (신논현역), bus 1560 from Gangnam Station (강남역) and bus 4101 from Seoul Station (서울역). You can also transfer at Exit 4 of Suwon Station (수원역) to bus 37 or 10-5 (Korean Folk Village official transport guide, 2026-11-25).

③ Paju — The DMZ from Imjingak

Individual travelers can take the Paju City DMZ Peace Tourism bus from Imjingak (임진각) to near the DMZ. Foreigners need the original passport or alien registration card (no copies, screenshots or expired documents), and the tour does not run on Mondays.

  • DMZ Peace Tourism (DMZ 평화관광) — Buy a ticket at the Imjingak (임진각) ticket office, then a shuttle bus takes you around the Third Tunnel (제3땅굴), Dora Observatory (도라전망대) and Tongilchon (통일촌) and back to Imjingak (about 3 hours). On-site ticketing and reservation ticketing run 09:00~14:30, and tickets are issued only when the whole party has arrived. It sells out early on many days. There is an online reservation menu, but the official site does not say whether foreigners can use it. It is closed every Monday, on weekday public holidays, and on Seollal and Chuseok day, so confirm in advance whether it runs on 12/25 and 1/1 (weekdays). The adult fare is ₩12,200 for the monorail course and ₩9,200 for the walking course. There is no foreigner discount or exemption. On Tue~Fri buses leave 10 times from 09:20 to 14:40, and on Sat and Sun 12 times. If the monorail stops, the course switches to walking, and it may be canceled for security or weather reasons (Paju City DMZ Peace Tourism official English page, 2026-11-25; the English service is in preparation and may differ from the Korean guide). There is a road-closure notice for the Dora Observatory (2026-10-23), so the course may change; check with Paju City (031-940-4114) before you go.
  • Imjingak Pyeonghwa Nuri (임진각 평화누리) — An outdoor park with the Bell of Peace and a wide grass hill. Entry is free, parking is paid (small car ₩2,000), and the parking lot runs 08:00~20:00 (Gyeonggi-do English tourism page, 2026-11-25). Look around while you wait for the Peace Tourism bus.
  • Imjingak Peace Gondola (임진각 평화곤돌라) — A gondola that crosses the Imjin River. Open daily 09:00~18:00 with no closed days, but it is closed for a safety inspection on 12/7 (Mon). A standard cabin costs ₩12,000 for adults. You cannot ride without ID, and the official site does not say whether a foreigner's passport is accepted (Peace Gondola official, 2026-11-25).

Getting there (DMZ Peace Tourism official English guide, 2026-11-25): It is about 20 minutes on foot from Imjingang Station (임진강역) on the Gyeongui-Jungang Line. There are also shuttle buses 058A and 058B from Imjingang Station (임진강역). To make the 14:30 ticket cutoff, leave Seoul early in the morning.

④ Gapyeong and Yangpyeong — East Along the Rivers

Gapyeong (Gyeongchun Line) and Yangpyeong (Gyeongui-Jungang Line) are on different lines, so pick one of the two for the day.

  • Nami Island (남이섬) — An island on the North Han River (북한강). The address is Chuncheon, Gangwon (강원 춘천), but the boat leaves from the Gapyeong (가평) pier. Boats run daily from 08:00, with the last entry at 21:00 and last departure at 21:05. The crossing takes about 5 minutes (2026 notice). The adult admission ticket is ₩19,000 including the round-trip boat (Nami Island official, confirmed 2026-11-25). From Gapyeong Station (가평역) to the pier is about 20 minutes on foot, or about 5 minutes by bus 10-4 or taxi. There is also a direct shuttle bus from Seoul every day (departs Hongik Univ. Station (홍대입구역) at 09:00, departs Nami Island at 16:30; book through the designated operator Ktourstory).
  • The Garden of Morning Calm (아침고요수목원) — A private arboretum in Gapyeong. Until 12/3 it is open 08:30~19:00 (last entry 18:00). From 12/4~2027-03-14, during the Five-Color Starlight Garden Festival (오색별빛정원전), it is open 10:00~21:00, until 23:00 on Saturdays only (Garden official admission guide use01.php, 2026-11-25). Lighting starts at 17:00 (Kakao Map (카카오맵) listing). The official site does not say whether the last entry and fare during the festival are the same as usual. The usual adult price is ₩11,000, and tickets are sold at the on-site ticket office only. Take bus 30-6 from Cheongpyeong Station (청평역) or Cheongpyeong Terminal (청평터미널) to the last stop. The last bus from Cheongpyeong Terminal to the garden is 19:30, and the last from the garden to Cheongpyeong Terminal is 20:00. If you want to stay until 21:00, take a taxi back (about 20 minutes to downtown Cheongpyeong, official directions page).
  • Dumulmeori (두물머리) — A riverside walking path where the South Han River (남한강) and North Han River (북한강) meet. There is no admission fee and no closed days (Kakao Map (카카오맵) listing, 2026-11-25).
  • Semiwon (세미원) — A water-and-flower garden next to Dumulmeori (두물머리). 09:00~18:00, last entry 17:30. Closed every Monday from Dec to Mar (the next day if it is a public holiday). Adults ₩7,000 (Semiwon official visitor guide, confirmed 2026-11-25). It is 700m on foot from Yangsu Station (양수역) on the Gyeongui-Jungang Line, and bus 2000-1 from Gangbyeon Station (강변역) and bus 2000-2 from Jamsil Station (잠실역) also run (limited weekday service).

Getting there: Gapyeong Station (가평역) is about 60 minutes from Yongsan (용산) by ITX-Cheongchun (ITX-청춘) and about 42 minutes from Cheongnyangni (청량리). By Gyeongchun Line train it is about 50 minutes from Sangbong (상봉) (Nami Island official directions, 2026-11-25).

🗺 Itineraries — Sample Winter Days

A. Suwon (daily, but Jinmi Tongdak (진미통닭) is closed Mondays)

  1. Morning: Hwaseong Haenggung Palace (화성행궁) (from 09:00). Wearing hanbok (한복) is eligible for exemption or discount
  2. After lunch: climb Seojangdae (서장대) behind the palace and walk the walls toward Hwaseomun (화서문) and Janganmun (장안문)
  3. Evening: fried chicken on Chicken Street (통닭거리). Jinmi (진미) is closed Mondays, and for Yongseong (용성) check its closing day on Kakao Map (카카오맵) first

B. Gapyeong by day and night (after 12/4)

  1. Morning: ITX-Cheongchun (ITX-청춘) to Gapyeong Station (가평역), then walk to the Nami Island (남이섬) pier
  2. Afternoon: circle the island, return to Gapyeong Station (가평역), take the Gyeongchun Line toward Seoul and get off at Cheongpyeong Station (청평역). Bus 30-6 to The Garden of Morning Calm (아침고요수목원)
  3. Evening: Five-Color Starlight Garden Festival (오색별빛정원전) (lighting from 17:00). The last bus 30-6 from the garden is at 20:00; after that take a taxi out to Cheongpyeong (about 20 minutes)

C. DMZ (Tue~Sun)

  1. Early morning: Gyeongui-Jungang Line to Imjingang Station (임진강역), then the Imjingak (임진각) ticket office (from 09:00)
  2. Morning to lunch: Peace Tourism bus (about 3 hours)
  3. Afternoon: stroll Pyeonghwa Nuri (평화누리), and if time remains, the Peace Gondola (평화곤돌라) (until 18:00)

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Going to the DMZ on a Monday — DMZ Peace Tourism is closed every Monday. On the same Monday, Semiwon (세미원) (Dec~Mar) and Jinmi Tongdak (진미통닭) are closed too. On Mondays, pick places registered as having no closed days, such as the Suwon fortress walls, Everland (에버랜드), Nami Island (남이섬) and The Garden of Morning Calm (아침고요수목원).
  2. Bringing only a passport photo or copy — DMZ Peace Tourism requires the original passport or alien registration card from foreigners and does not accept copies, screenshots or expired passports. The Peace Gondola (평화곤돌라) also will not let you ride without ID. The Korean Folk Village (한국민속촌) foreigner price (₩22,000) also requires showing a passport or alien registration card.
  3. Planning two directions in one day — Suwon and Gapyeong, or Paju and Yongin, cut across Seoul. Pick one route per day, and on a DMZ day leave early enough to arrive before the 14:30 ticket cutoff.

⚙ 3 FAQ

  1. What if it is my first time and I have only one day? — If you like walking, choose Suwon (fortress walls free, palace ₩2,000). If you want to see the site of the division, go to the DMZ Tue~Sun. With children, go to Everland (에버랜드) or the Korean Folk Village (한국민속촌).
  2. Can I also visit Panmunjeom (판문점) (JSA)? — We did not include it in this guide's routes. On 2026-11-25 we could not find an official notice that general foreigner tours are operating.
  3. Where is there something to see on a winter evening? — The Five-Color Starlight Garden Festival (오색별빛정원전) at The Garden of Morning Calm (아침고요수목원) (12/4~, lighting 17:00, until 21:00, Sat until 23:00) and Everland (에버랜드) (until 22:00 on Kakao Map (카카오맵)). The Suwon Hwaseong Fortress (수원화성) walls are open at night, but the night opening of Hwaseong Haenggung Palace (화성행궁) ended on 11/1.
