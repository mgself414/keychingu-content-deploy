🏛 4-axis deep dive

① Suwon Hwaseong (UNESCO 1997, King Jeongjo 1796)

  • Signature: Listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997·King Jeongjo, 1796
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ (Full set in English, MZ + family)
  • Time: 09:00~18:00·Free or 5,000 won

  • Signature: Nami Island (1965~) = Korea #1 Island·Winter Sonata (2002) Matrix·Cheongpyeong·Arboretum of Morning Calm·Dumulmeori
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ (English, MZ + Instagram fit)

③ DMZ (Panmunjom, Imjingak, Peace Park)

  • Signature: Foreign Mania Matrix #1·Panmunjeom·Imjingak·Dorasan
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ (English full set·Mania #1)
  • Advance reservation: Reservation required 1-2 weeks in advance of DMZ tour·Passport required

  • Signature: Korea #1 Theme Park (1976~)·Zoo·Tulip Festival·T-Express (Korea #1 Roller Coaster)
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★★★ (Full set in English, MZ + family)
  • Price: Free pass = KRW 60,000~80,000/person
