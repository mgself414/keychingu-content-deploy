⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Korean Plastic Surgery = Only Gangnam (강남)": Gangnam (강남) is #1, but Sinsa (신사)·Cheongdam (청담)·Apgujeong (압구정) are also top choices for foreigners, plus Seorae Village (서래마을) (#173) is a prime medical area for foreign residents. Many clinics are spread out.

2. "Plastic Surgery = All Foreigner OK": Korean plastic surgery is a top foreigner-friendly destination + English, Chinese, Japanese, Russian, Arabic interpretation + government-certified clinics (K-Medical certified) that are top choices for foreigners.

3. "General Check-up = Unavailable": Korean general check-ups are foreigner-friendly + 1-night, 2-day full package (₩200,000 (~$145 / ~1,050 RMB / ~21,000 JPY) to ₩1,000,000 (~$725 / ~5,250 RMB / ~105,000 JPY)) + English results + top choice for foreigner enthusiasts.

4. "Dentistry = Anywhere": Korea's top choices for foreigner dentistry enthusiasts = Ewha Dental Street (이대 치과 거리)·Gangnam Dental Street (강남 치과 거리) + #1 for implants + 30-70% cheaper for foreigners (compared to the US).

5. "Hanbang (한방) = Not for Foreigners": Korean Hanbang (한방) and Hanuiwon (한의원) (Korean traditional medicine clinic) are foreigner-friendly OK + English guidance + top destination for Hanbang (한방) enthusiasts (Gangnam (강남)·Cheongdam (청담) Hanuiwon (한의원)).
