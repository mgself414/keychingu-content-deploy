⚠ Five major mistakes foreigners make in Guro and Gasan

1. Recognition of “Guro·Gasan = industrial complex·outlet only”

Common mistakes in foreign travel routes. Guro and Gasan are recognized only as industrial complexes and Mario outlet areas (#180 general guide flow). In reality, Hidden stores for office workers in Guro Digital Complex and Gasan Digital Complex are the true lunch and dinner culture of Korean industrial complexes — Real hidden gems beyond the Noted and Starbucks chains.

2. No separation between local office workers’ movement routes and foreigners’ general movement routes

The first route for foreigners to Guro and Gasan is Mario Outlet + Noted + Starbucks. In fact, Local office workers’ Instagram curation stores (Wollae Martyrs’ Center, Guro Donga, Four Seasons Heung Noodles) are the No. 1 cultural experiences for foreign Korean office workers. Industrial complex + lunch and dinner hidden bundle.

3. Chinese food, noodles, gyoza authentic area pass

The number one Korean food destination for foreigners is BBQ and pork belly. In fact, Guro and Gasan Chinese and noodles are the essence of Korean Chinese living and industrial complex office worker culture — Wolae Martyrs’ Center (gyoja), Four Seasons Jeolheung Noodles Restaurant (noodles) and Jinyeong Myeonok (naengmyeon) are three stores that deep dive into Chinese and noodles in Korea.

4. Gasan night store business hours and manners unknown

Gasan Digital Complex night stores (Kunsang Gasan Main Branch, Izakaya, etc.) open 18-24 hours. The peak is between 18:00 and 21:00 due to the flow of office workers. Weekday dinner or Weekend lunch recommended. English menu available at some stores.

5. Guro vs Geumcheon administrative district + industrial complex route unknown

  • Guro-gu = Guro Digital Complex (Southern Ring Road, Digital Road) — Wolae Martyrs’ Museum, Grumble Archive, Guro Donga, Four Seasons Noodle Restaurant, Shaolin Temple
  • Geumcheon-gu = Gasan Digital Complex (Beotkkot-ro, Gasan-ro) — Kunsang, Jinyeong Myeonok, Uiryeong Buckwheat House

The two complexes are a 20-minute walk away — can be combined on one line (Subway Line 7).

① Lunch, Noodles (2 places)

1. Wollae Martyrs’ Hall (Wollaesun Gyoja-gwan) ★4.33

  • Address: 1st floor, 13 Digital-ro 19-gil, Guro-gu, Seoul
  • Signature: Authentic Guro Chinese food and gyoza. Foreign master rating ★4.33 — Phase 1 rating verification store. Authentic areas of Korean Chinese and Chinese cuisine.
  • Foreign Friendliness: ★★★★☆ — Rating verification, foreign Chinese/Chinese food fans
  • Recommended menu: Signature gyoza (dumplings), authentic Chinese menu, noodles
  • Tip: Walking distance from Guro Digital Complex Station. Recommended lunch/dinner times for office workers. Peak weekday lunch 12-14pm — recommended for weekday evenings or weekends.

2. Sagyejeol Heungmyeon

  • Address: 8 Uma-gil, Guro-gu, Seoul (Main Branch) + 33 Guro-dong-ro 26-gil (2nd branch)
  • Signature: Guro four seasons knowledge hidden. 2nd branch - Guro noodle signature store.
  • Foreigner friendliness: ★★★☆☆ — Korean-speaking service, friendly meeting concept
  • Recommended Menu: Four Seasons Signature Noodles, Korean Chinese Sides
  • Tip: Walking distance to Uma-gil or Guro-dong-ro. Bundling of two stores, head office and branch, is possible. Weekday lunch peak 12-14 p.m.
