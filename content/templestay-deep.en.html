⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "All temples are the same": Korean temples are categorized into UNESCO's 7 Mountain Temples (2018), Korea's Three Jewel Temples (Tongdosa (통도사), Haeinsa (해인사), Songgwangsa (송광사)), and urban/outlying temples.

2. "Templestays are only for Koreans": Korean templestays are foreigner-friendly #1 + offer full English programs + cost ₩50,000 (~$37 / ~260 RMB / ~5,300 JPY) to ₩150,000 (~$110 / ~780 RMB / ~16,000 JPY) for a 1-night, 2-day stay.

3. "Haeinsa (해인사) is only about the Tripitaka Koreana (팔만대장경)": Haeinsa (해인사) is home to the world's premier Tripitaka (대장경), designated UNESCO 1995 + features 81,258 wooden printing blocks of the Tripitaka Koreana (팔만대장경).

⚙ 4 FAQs

1. Top temples for foreigners: Jingwansa (진관사), Bongeunsa (봉은사), Haeinsa (해인사), Tongdosa (통도사), Beopjusa (법주사).

2. Templestays for foreigners: Jingwansa (진관사) (Seoul (서울)), Songgwangsa (송광사), Haeinsa (해인사) offer foreigner-friendly English programs.

3. UNESCO sites for foreigners: The 7 Mountain Temples (2018) + Haeinsa (해인사) (1995).

4. Cost for 1-night stay for foreigners: ₩50,000 to ₩150,000, a top choice for enthusiasts.
