⚠ 3 Jazz Bars Mistakes

1. "Korean jazz = only K-pop" — missing 50 years of history: People only think of K-pop. In reality, Korean jazz has 50 years of history (All That Jazz 1976·Janus 1978·Chunnyundongando 1987) + a deep scene tracing Sinchon→Daehangno→Ewha rear gate→Cheongdam→Seocho→Apgujeong→Gwanghwamun (Janus's 6 relocations in 47 years).

2. "Jazz bars = Korean-only" — missing the English-friendly cluster: Assumed to be Korean-only. In reality, Awesome Ground (English-speaking staff)·Dido (CatchTable English booking)·SEOB's (Berklee alum, fluent English)·All That Jazz (Itaewon, full English menu) are all friendly to international jazz fans.

3. "Applaud = at the end of the song" — missing post-solo applause etiquette: People assume you clap at the end of the tune. In reality, the timing is the moment each player's improvised solo ends + when unsure, follow the guests next to you + a no-photos vibe at some venues (Club Evans, etc.).

  • 18:00 Chunnyundongando (Nagwon·Jonggak) — browse the instrument shops on floors 1~3 of Nagwon Instrument Arcade, then catch the 2nd-floor live show
  • 21:00 Janus (Gwanghwamun) — Park Sung-yeon tribute stage, Tuesdays·Wednesdays recommended
  • Late-night wrap-up: Kakao T English app (the subway stops after midnight)

🍯 5 Tips for International Visitors

1. Booking = Instagram DM is fastest — SNS replies beat phone calls and websites·English responses come more naturally

2. Carry ₩50,000 (~$38) in cash — nopo (노포) spots (Chunnyundongando·Sound Dog) may not have card terminals (#431 Payments·SIM tie-in)

3. Applaud = after the solo — not at the start or end of the tune, but the moment an improvised solo finishes + following the guests next to you is a safe bet

4. Dress code — Once in a Blue Moon in Cheongdam = smart casual·everywhere else, neat everyday wear is OK

5. Getting home after midnight — the subway stops·install the Kakao T English app in advance (#430 Transport tie-in)
