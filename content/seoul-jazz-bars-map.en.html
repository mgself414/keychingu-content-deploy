Seoul Jazz Bar Guide — 6 Places in Itaewon, Hongdae, Seongbuk, and Gwangjin (All That Jazz, Boogie Woogie, Evans, Yeonnam 5701, Jazz Story, Dido Jazz Lounge) (Official Values, October 2026)

Subtitle: All That Jazz (올댓재즈) is open year-round, Mon~Thu no cover, just a drink (VisitSeoul) · Evans (에반스) cover ₩15,000 (~$11) (VisitSeoul) · Jazz Story (째즈스토리) performance fee ₩5,000 (~$3.75), live music 20:00~23:00 (VisitSeoul) · Dido Jazz Lounge (디도 재즈 라운지) performance ticket ₩12,000 (~$9) (VisitSeoul) · Alcohol is allowed from January 1 of the year you turn 19 (Easy Law, Korea Ministry of Government Legislation) — as of 2026-10-21

ISSUE 03 · #470 · 2026-05-27

🎯 How to Use This Guide

Go down the stairs and live jazz spreads under the yellow glow of incandescent bulbs — Jazz Story (째즈스토리) is introduced as "less a place to watch a show while drinking than a house for listening to music" (VisitSeoul). This guide is a map for travelers who want to hear live jazz over a drink in Seoul. There are three branches: Itaewon and Gyeongnidan, Hongdae and Yeonnam, and neighborhood jazz bars that charge a separate performance fee (Seongbuk and Gwangjin). Businesses were picked only if they are introduced on Seoul's official tourism site VisitSeoul as jazz bars and had their information revised in 2026.

The old guide listed Once in a Blue Moon, Janus, Cheonnyeon Dongan-do, Awesome Ground, SEOB's, and Sound Dog with hours and prices. This time we could not confirm their current operating information on official pages, so all were dropped. Phrases such as "Korea's top 3 jazz clubs" and "the deepest jazz scene in Asia" also have no source. The opening year of All That Jazz that the old guide fixed at 1976 differs between the VisitSeoul Korean edition (1976) and the English edition (1978).

🎷 Itaewon and Gyeongnidan — 2 Places

  • All That Jazz (올댓재즈) — Itaewon-ro 216 (이태원로 216) (Hannam-dong (한남동)), Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seoul. Sun~Thu 18:00~24:00, Fri and Sat 18:00~01:00, open year-round. It is introduced as the first jazz club in Korea, opened in Itaewon (이태원) by Chinese-American Ma Myeong-deok (마명덕), and it holds about 2,000 LPs and about 600 CDs. Mon~Thu there is no separate cover; just order a drink of about ₩5,000 (~$3.75). Price range in the ₩20,000s. It moved from its first location to near the Hamilton Hotel. 333 m from Itaewon Station (이태원역) Exit 3. Phone 02-795-5701 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)
  • Boogie Woogie (부기우기) — Hoenamu-ro 21 (회나무로 21) (Itaewon-dong (이태원동)) 2F, Yongsan-gu (용산구), Seoul. Mon~Thu 18:30~01:30, Fri~Sun and public holidays 17:30~00:30. A live bar for hearing piano and jazz with a strong, fast beat. If you reserve, there are set discounts and priority for popular seats. 549 m from Noksapyeong Station (녹사평역) Exit 2. Phone 0507-1490-3061 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)

🎹 Hongdae and Yeonnam — 2 Places

  • Evans (에반스) — Wausan-ro 63 (와우산로 63) (Seogyo-dong (서교동)), Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul. Hours of use 19:00~22:10, closed on major holidays. One of the oldest clubs in the Hongdae (홍대) area, with a different program each day and musicians changing every month. Cover ₩15,000 (~$11) per person (drinks extra). It says the band's live set starts after 9 p.m. 279 m from Sangsu Station (상수역) Exit 1. Phone 02-337-8361 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)
  • Yeonnam 5701 (연남 5701) — Donggyo-ro 23-gil 64 (동교로23길 64) (Yeonnam-dong (연남동)) basement, Mapo-gu (마포구), Seoul. Thu~Mon 18:30~23:00, closed Tue and Wed. There are seats just for focusing on the performance and separate seats for watching while you eat. Food and wine. The performance lineup is posted on Instagram and Facebook. 600 m from Hongik University Station (홍대입구역) Exit 1. Phone 010-2129-8161 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)

🎺 Seongbuk and Gwangjin — 2 Places That Charge a Separate Performance Fee

  • Jazz Story (째즈스토리) — Hyehwa-ro 88 (혜화로 88) (Seongbuk-dong (성북동)), Seongbuk-gu (성북구), Seoul. Tue~Sun 17:00 to 01:00 the next day, closed Mondays. Live music 20:00~23:00, performance fee ₩5,000 (~$3.75) per day. It moved from Samcheong-dong (삼청동) to Seongbuk-dong (성북동), and incandescent bulbs light the basement space. 862 m from Hansung University Station (한성대입구역) Exit 5. Phone 02-747-6537 (VisitSeoul Korean edition, registered 2026-07-06)
  • Dido Jazz Lounge (디도 재즈 라운지) — Jayang-ro 18-gil 56 (자양로18길 56) (Guui-dong (구의동), Geosong Building (거송빌딩)), Gwangjin-gu (광진구), Seoul. Wed~Sun and public holidays 19:00~24:00, closed Mon and Tue. Performance ticket ₩12,000 (~$9). Live music every day, and the lineup is posted on Instagram. It serves food and wine together. 111 m from Guui Station (구의역) Exit 2. Phone 0507-1354-2815 (VisitSeoul Korean edition)

🥗 Dietary Notes

  • Food: Yeonnam 5701 (연남 5701) and Dido Jazz Lounge (디도 재즈 라운지) serve food and wine (VisitSeoul). None of the six lists a vegetarian menu — ask about ingredients before ordering
  • Halal: VisitSeoul introduces Eid in Itaewon (이태원) (Usadan-ro 10-gil 15 (우사단로10길 15), Yongsan-gu (용산구), daily 12:00~21:00) as one of the few restaurants recognized as halal by the Korea Muslim Federation (KMF). Recognition can change, so check before you visit. Every jazz bar in this guide serves alcohol
  • If you do not drink: At All That Jazz (올댓재즈), one drink is enough Mon~Thu (VisitSeoul). Ask the venue whether it has non-alcoholic drinks
  • Price range: The only officially posted values are the cover and performance fees (₩5,000~15,000, ~$3.75~11) and "₩20,000s" (All That Jazz (올댓재즈), Evans (에반스)) (VisitSeoul Korean edition). Food and drink prices are not posted
  • For a show-it card, see #1300 Dietary Request Manual

🧰 Jazz Bar Practicalities — Age, Solo, Reservations, Payment, Show Times, Getting Around

  • Age: Under Korean law a "minor" is anyone under 19, and a person who has reached January 1 of the year they turn 19 is excluded (Easy Law, Korea Ministry of Government Legislation). Bring your passport
  • Solo: The official pages do not say whether they seat solo diners. Yeonnam 5701 (연남 5701), with separate performance seats, or Jazz Story (째즈스토리), a "house for listening to music," is the easy choice for listening alone (based on the VisitSeoul descriptions)
  • Reservations: At Boogie Woogie (부기우기), reserving gets set discounts and priority seating (VisitSeoul). For the rest, there is no reservation route on the official pages, so ask by phone or Instagram
  • Show times: Evans (에반스) after 21:00, Jazz Story (째즈스토리) 20:00~23:00 (VisitSeoul). Check the lineup on the Dido and Yeonnam 5701 Instagram accounts
  • Payment: For cards and mobile payments, see #527 Korea's Payment System. If you cannot make yourself understood, see #1339 Ordering Etiquette
  • Getting around: Boogie Woogie (부기우기) (01:30), All That Jazz (올댓재즈) on weekends (01:00), and Jazz Story (째즈스토리) (01:00) close after the last subway. #1395 If You Missed the Last Train

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "Jazz bars have no cover" — Evans (에반스) ₩15,000 (~$11), Dido (디도) ₩12,000 (~$9), Jazz Story (째즈스토리) ₩5,000 (~$3.75). Only All That Jazz (올댓재즈) works with just a drink Mon~Thu, no cover (VisitSeoul)

2. "Jazz bars are open every day" — Dido (디도) rests Mon and Tue, Yeonnam 5701 (연남 5701) Tue and Wed, and Jazz Story (째즈스토리) on Mondays (VisitSeoul)

3. "All That Jazz definitely opened in 1976" — The VisitSeoul Korean edition says 1976 and the English edition says 1978

4. "You can go in only after your 19th birthday" — The standard is January 1 of the year you turn 19 (Easy Law, Korea Ministry of Government Legislation)
