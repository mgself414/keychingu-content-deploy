⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreign Visitors

1. “Yangcheon (양천) = Mok-dong (목동) Only” — Unaware of Sinjeong (신정) and Sinwol (신월) deep dives: #185 focused on Mok-dong (목동), but there's a separate pool of Sinjeong (신정) (Seoul Kkot Sam (서울꽃삼)) and Sinwol (신월) (Sinwoldang (신월당)) signatures.

2. “Yangcheon (양천) = Just Bedroom Community Cafes” — Unaware of Bakery and Gopchang (곱창) Clusters: Ppangui Sigan (빵의시간), Yours Bake Shop (유어스베이크샵), and Sinwoldang (신월당) form the Yangcheon (양천) bakery cluster (totaling 9,000+ reviews from 3 stores). Omokgyo Gopchang (오목교곱창) is a hidden gem for gopchang (Korean grilled intestines) in Yangcheon (양천).

3. “Unaware of Broadcasting Station Cluster” — Unaware of SBS, EBS, and KBS locations: Mok-dong (목동) hosts the SBS, EBS, and KBS broadcasting station cluster, making it a possible itinerary for K-drama and K-pop enthusiasts. Some tours can be booked in advance.
