Busan Transit Grammar — A City You Can Read With Just Two Lines

Subtitle: Busan's subway map is far simpler than Seoul's — Line 1 running north-south, Line 2 tracing the coast — these two lines cover 90% of a tourist's route. The catch is the first gate: Gimhae Airport (김해공항) has no airport rail link. From the airport entry that's solved with one light-rail transfer, to the beach train that's transit and sightseeing in one, to the bus grammar of the hillside neighborhoods (산복도로) — here's how not to get thrown off by getting around in Busan.

ISSUE · #1325 · 2026-08-23

🎯 The Big Picture of Getting Around Busan

Busan is a coastal city stretched long east-west. Its transit grammar comes down to three lines:

1. Line 1 (north-south axis) — the tourist artery, threading Busan Station (부산역) · Nampo (남포) · Jagalchi (자갈치) · Seomyeon (서면) · Dongnae (동래) · Oncheonjang (온천장).

2. Line 2 (coastal axis) — bends at Seomyeon (서면) and connects Gyeongsung University (경성대) · Gwangan (광안) · Haeundae (해운대) · Centum (센텀).

3. Everything else is a support line: Line 3 (toward Mandeok, 만덕), the Donghae Line commuter rail (동해선, toward Gijang and Osiria), and the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail (부산김해경전철, to the airport).

You don't need to memorize nine lines like in Seoul — just remember that Seomyeon (서면) is the hub of every transfer.

① Airport Entry — The First Point of Confusion

Gimhae Airport (김해공항) has no airport rail link like Seoul does. There are three options:

Method How Feel
Light rail + subway Busan-Gimhae Light Rail to Sasang Station (사상역), 3 stops (about 7–10 min) → transfer to Line 2 The standard route toward Seomyeon, Gwangan, Haeundae
Airport limousine bus Two routes: Seomyeon (서면)/Bujeon (부전)-bound and Haeundae (해운대)/Gijang (기장)-bound (service resumed April 2026) For when you have a lot of luggage. Feels like a direct line to Haeundae
Taxi Call one via Kakao T (카카오T) Reasonable for a late-night arrival or a group of 3+
  • Fares got lighter: with the regional transfer fare abolished between Busan, Gimhae, and Yangsan (from Sept. 2025), a transit card gets you through the whole trip, transfer included, for 1,600 won (zone 1) or 1,800 won (zone 2).
  • Transit cards are T-money (티머니) / Cashbee (캐시비) compatible — the same card you used in Seoul works here. Rechargeable at airport convenience stores.

② Subway Grammar — Reading Sights by Line

  • Line 1: Busan Station (부산역, KTX arrival, #1114) → Nampo (남포, Gwangbok-ro 광복로 · BIFF Square BIFF광장) → Jagalchi (자갈치, #244) → Seomyeon (서면) → Dongnae (동래, #269 hot springs · #1323 BASECOFFEE · #1324 Baekpatjip) → Oncheonjang (온천장). The southern end runs to Dadaepo Beach (다대포해수욕장, #272).
  • Line 2: Seomyeon (서면) → Gyeongsung University·Pukyong National University (경성대·부경대) → Gwangan (광안, #243) → Haeundae (해운대) → Centum City (센텀시티). Coastal sightseeing runs along this line.
  • Line 3: toward Mandeok (만덕) — the Lego Village axis of the K-pop pilgrimage piece (#1323).
  • Donghae Line (동해선): Gijang·Osiria (기장·오시리아, #270) — Yonggungsa Temple (용궁사) and Lotte World Busan are reached by this commuter rail, not the subway.
  • Trains run a bit less frequently than in Seoul — for routes with transfers, Kakao Map (카카오맵) or Naver Map (네이버맵) is more accurate than Google Maps.

③ The Beach Train — Transit and Sightseeing in One

Haeundae Blueline Park (해운대 블루라인파크): a scenic coastal train connecting Mipo (미포) → Cheongsapo (청사포, the ocean-view cafe belt from #1324) → Songjeong (송정). About 3 departures an hour, roughly 30 minutes one way. It runs on the old, decommissioned Donghae Nambu Line (동해남부선) track, keeping the sea beside you the whole ride — the route itself is the attraction.

  • ⚠ You can't ride it with a transit card — it needs a separate ticket (online booking available). The Sky Capsule (스카이캡슐, a 2–4 person capsule car) often sells out at popular times.
  • The standard combo: get off at Cheongsapo (청사포) for a cafe, then walk the coastal path to Songjeong (송정).

④ Bus and Taxi Grammar — A City of Hills

  • Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을, #245) and the hillside neighborhoods (산복도로) are bus territory — the structure is subway to the nearest station, then a transfer to a village bus. The slopes are steep, so walking in isn't recommended.
  • For taxis, calling one via Kakao T (카카오T) is standard. It's faster than worrying about a short-distance ride getting refused.
  • On city buses, it's press the bell + exit through the back door, tagging your transit card. Cash fares are effectively disappearing, so plan on a card by default.

⚠ Five Points of Confusion

1. There's no airport rail link — transfer via light rail at Sasang (사상), or take the limousine bus.

2. The Seomyeon (서면) transfer gets crowded — avoid rush hour (8–9am, 6–7pm).

3. The beach train doesn't take transit cards — you need a separate ticket.

4. Osiria (오시리아) and Yonggungsa (용궁사) are on the Donghae Line, not the subway — marked separately on the route map.

5. Gamcheon (감천) is far from the station — a village bus transfer is part of the deal.
