⚠ 3 Common 24h Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Korea = Dangerous at Night" — Hotel confinement after 22:00 (10 PM) pattern: Global urban safety perceptions are directly applied to Seoul. In reality, it has the lowest crime rate among OECD countries + 1 million CCTVs + abundant 24h pyeonuijeom (편의점) and people. Areas like Myeongdong (명동), Hongdae (홍대), Itaewon (이태원), Gangnam (강남), and Jongno (종로) are active 24h with people and businesses. Night walks in Myeongdong (명동) and Jongno (종로) after 22:00 (10 PM) are recommended.

2. "02:00-06:00 (2 AM - 6 AM) = Sleeping Time" — Unawareness of Noryangjin (노량진) & Dongdaemun (동대문) dawn activities: Foreigners typically only consider itineraries from 09:00-22:00 (9 AM - 10 PM) → underutilizing dawn full experiences. In reality, Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market (노량진 수산시장) dawn auction (02:00-06:00, 2 AM - 6 AM) + Dongdaemun (동대문) clothing wholesale & street food (22:00-04:00, 10 PM - 4 AM) + 24h pyeonuijeom (편의점) & jjimjilbang (찜질방) are signature 24h full experiences. These are top priorities for Japanese, Hong Kong, and Taiwanese enthusiasts.

3. "21:00-02:00 (9 PM - 2 AM) = Only Bars & Clubs" — Unawareness of Yajang (야장) & Nogari Golmok (노가리 골목): Foreigners perceive nighttime only as club itineraries. In reality, Euljiro Nogari Golmok (을지로 노가리골목) (nogari (노가리) + draft beer yajang (야장)), Jongno 3-ga Pocha (포차) Street (전, jeon (Korean pancake) & guksu (국수, noodles) + makgeolli (막걸리)), and Hongdae (홍대) nosang table (노상 테이블, street-side table) are K-drama scenery enthusiast signatures. Bars/clubs ≠ yajang (야장) culture.
