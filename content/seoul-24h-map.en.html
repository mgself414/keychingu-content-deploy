⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners make 24 hours a day

1. "Korea = Nighttime Danger" — Hotel stay-at-home pattern after 22:00: Global urban safety awareness applies to Seoul. In reality, lowest crime rate in OECD + 1 million CCTVs + 24-hour convenience store and abundance of people. The areas around Myeongdong, Hongdae, Itaewon, Gangnam, and Jongno are active with people and commercial districts 24 hours a day. It is recommended to take a walk in Myeongdong and Jongno after 22:00.

2. "02~06:00 = sleeping time" — Noryangjin·Dongdaemun dawn recognition: Foreigners usually only recognize the movement route from 09~22:00 → do not utilize the early morning pool experience. In reality, Noryangjin Fisheries Dawn Auction (02~06) + Dongdaemun Clothing Wholesale/Street Food (22~04) + 24h Convenience Store/Jjimjilbang is the 24h full experience signature. No. 1 fanatics in Japan, Hong Kong, and Taiwan.

3. "Midnight 21-02 o'clock = only bars and clubs" — Night market and nogari alley misrecognition: Foreigners perceive nighttime only as a club movement route. In fact, Euljiro Nogari Alley (nogari + draft beer night market), Jongno 3-ga street food stall street (jeon, noodles + makgeolli), and Hongdae street tables are the signatures of K-drama scenery enthusiasts. Bar/club ≠ night club culture.
