11 Korean Jjimjilbang and Hot Spring Spas — From Seoul's 24-Hour Jjimjilbang to Icheon, Asan, Sokcho, and Busan Hot Springs

Subtitle: 4 jjimjilbang in Seoul (Gangbyeon, Dongmyo, Bongwonsa-gil, Sinsa), 2 large spas in the capital region (Aquafield Hanam and Termeden in Icheon), 2 Onyang hot springs in Asan, Seorak Waterpia in Sokcho, and 2 in Busan (Centum and Dongnae). Admission fees, hours, and whether you can stay overnight are taken from official pages or from the values the owners registered on Kakao Map. The order of use and etiquette for a jjimjilbang are in #34, and how to use the key wristband is in #1284.

#766 · 2026-11-24 Revised · Jjimjilbang·Hot Springs

🎯 Who This Is For

This is aimed at travelers who want to try Korean bathing and jjimjil (heated-room sweating) once, people who spend the night at a jjimjilbang to save on lodging, and people who want a day trip out of Seoul to a hot spring. Look at three things when choosing. The admission fee, how many hours you can stay, and whether you can stay the whole night. The table gives the admission fee, hours, and overnight status for all 11 venues. In the table, only Aquafield Hanam (아쿠아필드 하남) (max 6 hours) and Spaland Centum (스파랜드 센텀) (max 4 hours) cap the length of stay.

⚠ Notation principle: Admission fees, hours, and overnight rules are from each facility's official website or the values the owner registered on Kakao Map (카카오맵) (checked 2026-11-24). Where the two sources differ, both are listed. Amounts are for one adult. Tattoo and children's entry rules are included only for venues where the official page or the owner's registered values state them. Nearby stations are the station, exit, and distance from Kakao Map directions.

① Seoul — 4 Venues

Three venues are open 24 hours: Riverside Spa Land, Sparex Dongmyo, and Spalei. Supsok Hanbang Land closes at 22:00.

Riverside Spa Land (강변스파랜드)

A large jjimjilbang in the basement of a commercial building in front of Gangbyeon Station (강변역). The owner's description mentions a pine-log fire kiln. Per the official website it is open 24 hours, 365 days, and the rates differ between daytime (05~20) and night (20~05). The sauna is 13,000 won in the daytime, the jjimjilbang 15,000 won in the daytime and 17,000 won at night (for ages 7 and under 9,000, 11,000, and 13,000 won). After 15 hours in the jjimjilbang or 6 hours in the sauna, 1,000 won per hour is added. The address is Guui-gangbyeon-ro 45 (구의강변로 45), Seongjin Plaza (성진프라자) basement floors 1~2, Gwangjin-gu (광진구), 412 m from Gangbyeon Station (Line 2) Exit 4.

  • Rules (official): People under 19 without a legal guardian are not admitted from 22:00~05:00, and ID checks may be requested. Boys aged 4 and over cannot enter the women's bath. Outside food is not allowed, and if you leave during your visit you cannot re-enter.

Sparex Dongmyo (스파렉스 동묘점)

A jjimjilbang on floors 12~13 of the building in front of Dongmyo-ap Station (동묘앞역). It is registered on Kakao Map as 24 hours, open all year. The registered prices (2026-03-05) are adult daytime sauna (05:00~20:00) 14,000 won, adult night sauna (20:00~05:00) 16,000 won, and child 10,000 won. The address is Jibong-ro 19 (지봉로 19), Season Building (시즌빌딩) floors 12~13, Jongno-gu (종로구), 89 m from Dongmyo-ap Station (Lines 1·6) Exit 5. A separate branch with the same name, Goodmorning City (굿모닝시티점) (Euljiro 6-ga), exists, so find it by address.

  • Rules (owner registration): Teens must leave within 21:30 after entering, and can stay overnight only with a guardian.

Supsok Hanbang Land Main Branch (숲속한방랜드 본점)

A charcoal-kiln jjimjilbang on Bongwonsa-gil (봉원사길) in Bongwon-dong, Seodaemun-gu (서대문구). It is registered as daily 06:30~22:00, open all year, so you cannot stay the night. The registered prices (2025-10-15) are charcoal kiln 16,000 won for adults through middle schoolers, 9,000 won for elementary schoolers, and 7,000 won for preschoolers, and 10,000 won for an adult who only bathes. Snack-bar items registered are sikhye (식혜) (sweet rice drink) 3,500 won, garaetteok (가래떡) (rice cake sticks) 1,500 won, and mandu (만두) (dumplings) 4,000 won. The address is Bongwonsa-gil 75-7 (봉원사길 75-7). Kakao Map directions list no nearby subway station, so check the route in directions before you leave. Kakao Map lists parking as available.

Spalei (스파레이) (Women Only)

A women-only sauna and jjimjilbang in front of Sinsa Station (신사역). The official English page states 24-hour, year-round operation, and Kakao Map also registers 24 hours. The official website admission is 22,000 won per person, and after 8 hours 2,000 won per hour is added (the website shows no posting date). It has an open-air bath, a seawater bath, a bulhanjeungmak (불한증막) (fire-sweat kiln) (opens 10:00), and loess, charcoal, and salt rooms. The address is Gangnam-daero 107-gil 5 (강남대로107길 5), Cresin Building (크레신빌딩) basement floors 1~2 and floor 3, Seocho-gu (서초구), 269 m from Sinsa Station (Line 3 · Shinbundang Line (신분당선)) Exit 5. Men cannot enter.

② Large Spas Near Seoul — 2 Venues

Both combine a jjimjil space with indoor water play, and both close at night.

Aquafield Hanam (아쿠아필드 하남)

A jjimjil spa and water park on floors 3~4 of Starfield Hanam (스타필드 하남). Per the official usage guide, jjimjil spa 10:00~22:00, indoor water park 10:00~19:00. Rates are jjimjil spa adult 26,000 won and child 21,000 won, water park 48,000 won and 38,000 won, and the multi pass for using both 58,000 won and 48,000 won. Adult means middle school and older, and child means 36 months to elementary school. The jjimjil spa and water park allow up to 6 hours and the multi pass 9 hours, and past that it is 5,000 won per hour. The Kakao Map owner notice says the outdoor water park is running at reduced scale. The address is Misa-daero 750 (미사대로 750) Starfield Hanam, Hanam-si (하남시), 734 m from Hanam Geomdansan Station (하남검단산역) (Line 5) Exit 1.

  • Rules (official): Children aged 10 and under need a guardian, ages 4 and over cannot enter the opposite-sex sauna or changing room, entry is barred to anyone who has been drinking, and photos are banned in the locker room and sauna. The water park requires swimsuit-material clothing.

Termeden (테르메덴)

An open-air hot spring pool and spa in Moga-myeon (모가면), Icheon (이천). Rates and hours change with the season (official website).

  • Middle season (through 11/30): Pool and spa all-day pass adult 49,000 won · child 40,000 won, afternoon pass (after 14h) 27,000 won, weekend evening pass (Sat·Sun after 17h) 22,000 won, hot spring bath pass 18,000 won. Indoor pool weekdays (Mon~Fri) 09:00~17:00 · weekends 09:00~20:00, jjimjil spa weekdays 11:00~17:00 · weekends 11:00~20:00, hot spring bath weekdays 09:00~18:00 · weekends 09:00~21:00
  • High season (12/1~3/1): All-day pass 55,000 won · 45,000 won, afternoon pass 29,000 won, evening pass (after 17h) 25,000 won, hot spring bath pass 18,000 won. Indoor pool Mon~Thu 09:00~19:00 · Fri~Sun and holidays 09:00~21:00, jjimjil spa Mon~Thu 11:00~19:00 · Fri~Sun and holidays 10:00~21:00, hot spring bath Mon~Thu 09:00~20:00 · Fri~Sun and holidays 09:00~22:00

You must rent a jjimjil outfit (5,000 won) at the locker room to use the jjimjil spa. Child means 36 months to elementary school, and children under 36 months are free if you show supporting documents. The address is Sasil-ro 984 (사실로 984), Moga-myeon, Icheon-si (이천시), and it is far from any station, so go by car or taxi. The official page notes that hours may differ from other sites and navigation apps.

③ Asan Onyang Hot Springs — 2 Venues

Near Onyang Oncheon Station (온양온천역) (Line 1), next to each other, 10 street numbers apart on the same road.

Onyang Tourist Hotel Grand Hot Spring Bath (온양관광호텔 대온천탕)

A public hot spring bath inside a hotel. Per the official website, Sun~Fri 06:00~20:00 (last entry 19:00), Saturday 06:00~22:00 (last entry 21:00), and the closed day is stated two ways even within the official page (third Tuesday of each month / every Tuesday). The Kakao Map owner registration is 06:00~20:00 (last entry 19:30) with every Tuesday closed. It is safest to avoid Tuesday. Admission is adult (from elementary school) 12,000 won and senior (65 and over) 10,000 won. It has an open-air bath and dry and wet saunas. The address is Oncheon-daero 1459 (온천대로 1459), Asan-si (아산시), 579 m from Onyang Oncheon Station Exit 1.

Wontang Sincheontang (원탕신천탕)

A neighborhood hot spring bath whose owner description notes a 65-year history. It is registered as daily 05:00~18:00. The registered prices (2026-01-06) are adult 10,000 won and senior 8,000 won. The student and child rates were registered more than 2 years ago, so confirm them on site. The address is Oncheon-daero 1469 (온천대로 1469), 339 m from Onyang Oncheon Station Exit 1. It closes at 18:00, so if you arrive late in the afternoon, the Grand Hot Spring Bath is the better fit.

④ Sokcho — 1 Venue

Seorak Waterpia (설악워터피아)

A hot spring water park inside Hanwha Resort Seorak (한화리조트 설악). Per the official usage and rates guide, the current period (low season, 10/12~12/10) is daytime pass 10:00~17:00 (last entry 16:30), Night Spa 18:00~20:30 (last entry 20:00). The hours the owner registered on Kakao Map, 10:00~21:30, differ from the official Night Spa closing. Regular rates are daytime pass adult 57,000 won · child 49,000 won, Night Spa 42,000 won · 36,000 won, and all-day pass 76,000 won · 64,500 won. In the silver season (12/11~3/1) the daytime pass rises to 62,000 won and the all-day pass to 80,000 won. The official page says online tickets get a 38% discount. Right now some outdoor play facilities are closed, while the Spa Valley and indoor pools are open. You must wear a swimsuit, and rental is 10,000 won. The address is Misiryeong-ro 2983beon-gil 88 (미시령로2983번길 88), Sokcho-si (속초시).

  • Rules (official): Child means born 2014~2022, and those born 2023 or later are free with supporting documents. Children aged 4 and over cannot enter the sauna with a guardian of the opposite sex.

⑤ Busan — 2 Venues

Spaland Centum (스파랜드 센텀)

A hot spring jjimjil spa inside Shinsegae Centum City (신세계 센텀시티). The official page (Centum Spaland within the Aquafield website) lists 2 types of hot spring water drawn from 1,000 m underground. It is 09:00~22:00, last entry 21:00, and the Kakao Map owner registration says it extends by 2 hours to 08:00~23:00 from 2026-12-01~2027-02-28. Admission is the same on weekdays and weekends: adult 26,000 won and student 21,000 won. One entry allows up to 4 hours, and past that it is 5,000 won per hour (6 hours if you spend 10,000 won or more inside). Inside is Ottogi Ramyeon Land (오뚜기 라면랜드) (a ramyeon experience space). The address is Centumnam-daero 35 (센텀남대로 35), Haeundae-gu (해운대구), 135 m from Centum City Station (센텀시티역) (Line 2) Exit 12.

  • Rules (official): Entry starts from elementary school (born 2019), and elementary schoolers use it with a same-sex guardian. Travel bags cannot go in the inside locker room, so use the lockers in front of the ticket office (3,000 won for 5 hours).

Hurshimchung (허심청) (Hotel Nongshim (호텔농심))

A large hot spring bath in Dongnae Hot Springs. Per the official usage guide, hot spring 05:30~22:00 (last entry 21:30), open all year, and the jjimjil facility 06:30~21:00. Regular (middle school and older) is 15,000 won on weekdays and 18,000 won on Sat·Sun and holidays, elementary school 11,000 won · 12,000 won, and child (12 months to preschool) 7,000 won, and the jjimjil facility costs an extra 4,000 won for gown rental. You pay on the way out. The hot spring alone allows 4 hours, hot spring plus jjimjil 6 hours, and past that it is 2,000 won per hour. The address is Oncheonjang-ro 107beon-gil 32 (온천장로107번길 32), Hotel Nongshim floors 4~5, Dongnae-gu (동래구), 506 m from Oncheonjang Station (온천장역) (Line 1) Exit 1.

  • Rules (official): People with tattoos and people with skin disease are listed as barred from entry. Mixed bathing is allowed only for children who are accompanied by a guardian and are both 48 months or younger and under 100 cm tall.

🗺 Combos — Two Routes

A. An overnight in Seoul: After dinner, go in after 20:00 to Riverside Spa Land (강변스파랜드) or Sparex Dongmyo (스파렉스 동묘점) at the night rate. At Riverside Spa Land there is no extra charge for up to 15 hours in the jjimjilbang. If you are a woman staying in Gangnam, there is Spalei (스파레이).

B. An Asan day trip: Get off at Onyang Oncheon Station (온양온천역) on Line 1, and link Wontang Sincheontang (원탕신천탕) (05:00~18:00) in the morning and the Onyang Tourist Hotel Grand Hot Spring Bath (온양관광호텔 대온천탕) (weekday last entry 19:00) in the afternoon. The two are next to each other on the same road. On a Tuesday, first check whether the Grand Hot Spring Bath is closed. The official page's notation is also split two ways.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

  1. Trying to stay overnight anywhere — In this piece, the places you can stay overnight are Riverside Spa Land (강변스파랜드), Sparex Dongmyo (스파렉스 동묘점), and Spalei (스파레이). Aquafield Hanam (아쿠아필드 하남) and Spaland Centum (스파랜드 센텀) have time limits (6 hours · 4 hours) and close at night.
  2. Planning an overnight with teenagers — Riverside Spa Land does not admit people under 19 without a guardian from 22:00~05:00, and Sparex Dongmyo (스파렉스 동묘점) allows teen overnight stays only with a guardian.
  3. Going to Termeden (테르메덴) based only on the navigation app's hours — Hours differ by facility and season, and they change from 12/1. Check that week's schedule on the official website.

⚙ 3 FAQ

  1. What if I have a tattoo? — Among the official pages and Kakao Map registered values opened for this piece, only Hurshimchung (허심청) states a tattoo rule, and it is no entry. The others state no rule, so ask at the ticket office first.
  2. Which venues need a swimsuit? — The Aquafield Hanam (아쿠아필드 하남) water park (swimsuit-material clothing) and Seorak Waterpia (설악워터피아) (swimsuit required, rental 10,000 won).
  3. Which venues work for kids? — To combine water play, Aquafield Hanam (아쿠아필드 하남), Termeden (테르메덴), and Seorak Waterpia (설악워터피아), which have child rates officially listed, are the fit. Spaland Centum (스파랜드 센텀) admits only from elementary school. At Hurshimchung (허심청), the official rates are elementary school 11,000 won (Sat·Sun and holidays 12,000 won) and child (12 months to preschool) 7,000 won. In winter, Termeden's Water Play Zone and kids' slide (season ends 3/1) and some of Seorak Waterpia's outdoor play facilities are closed, so plan around indoor spaces and the hot springs.
