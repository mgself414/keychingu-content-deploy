Korean Dessert 6-Type Matrix — From Hotteok to Bingsu, a Complete Instagram Set for Foreigners

Subtitle: 6 Types of Korean Desserts for Foreign Tourists — Hotteok, Dalgona, Bungeoppang, Gukhwappang, Yakgwa, Bingsu + Seasonal Signatures + Foreigner's Guide

ISSUE 03 · #819 · 2026-06-08

🎯 Why a Korean Dessert Matrix?

Foreigners' pain point with Korean desserts = "Only knowing Dalgona (달고나, Korean honeycomb toffee) from the K-drama Squid Game (오징어 게임)." Korean desserts are a complete set across 3 categories: traditional street food (Hotteok (호떡, Korean sweet pancake), Bungeoppang (붕어빵, fish-shaped pastry), Gukhwappang (국화빵, chrysanthemum-shaped pastry)), royal/traditional (Yakgwa (약과, traditional Korean honey cookie), Hangwa (한과, traditional Korean confectionery)), and modern (Bingsu (빙수, Korean shaved ice dessert), Dalgona (달고나) cafes). Even though they are all "Korean desserts," Hotteok (호떡) (1900s street food), Dalgona (달고나) (1970s school front → Squid Game (오징어 게임) 2021 global), Bungeoppang (붕어빵) (1930s), Gukhwappang (국화빵), Yakgwa (약과) (Joseon (조선) royal court), and Bingsu (빙수) (1900s Japanese colonial period) each have different histories, ingredients, and seasons. Foreigners often (a) only know Dalgona (달고나) and haven't tried other desserts, (b) confuse Hotteok (호떡) with Japanese hot cakes, (c) misunderstand Bingsu (빙수) as a single menu item, or (d) confuse Yakgwa (약과) as just a type of Hangwa (한과). This guide provides a 6-type matrix + seasonal options + foreigner's introduction priority.

🥞 Dessert 6-Type Deep Dive

🥞 Hotteok (호떡) — Foreigner's Introduction #1

  • Signature: Settled in Korea with the influx of Qing Dynasty (청나라) Chinese immigrants during the Gaehwagi (개화기, late Joseon Dynasty) (1880s). (Etymology: 胡 (Ho) = Qing Dynasty (청나라), 餠 (tteok) = rice cake)
  • Types:
  • Ssiat Hotteok (씨앗 호떡, Seed): Nuts·sunflower seeds (Busan (부산) No. 1 spot)
  • Kkul Hotteok (꿀 호떡, Honey): Brown sugar + honey
  • Nokcha Hotteok (녹차 호떡, Green tea): MZ (엠지) trend
  • Cheese Hotteok (치즈 호떡): Mozzarella (MZ (엠지) trend)
  • Recommended Stores:
  • Busan BIFF Gwangjang Hotteok (부산 BIFF 광장 호떡) — Busan (부산) signature (Ssiat Hotteok (씨앗 호떡) Korea #1)
  • Myeongdong (명동)·Hongdae (홍대)·Jongno (종로) street food — Foreigner enthusiasts
  • Insadong (인사동) Hanok (한옥) Hotteok (호떡) cluster
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (Many English menus·Instagram ★)
  • Price: ₩2,000~4,000
  • Season: Winter Top 1 Priority (warmth)

🍯 Dalgona (달고나) — Global Recognition ★

  • Signature: Late 1960s~70s school front street food + global explosion with Squid Game (오징어 게임) (2021).
  • Types:
  • Star·triangle·umbrella shape: Squid Game (오징어 게임) traditional
  • Dalgona Latte (달고나 라떼): Instagram cafe #1 trend
  • Dalgona (달고나) muffins·cookies: MZ (엠지) cafes
  • Recommended Stores:
  • Insadong (인사동)·Jongno (종로) street food — Traditional Squid Game (오징어 게임) experience (₩1,000~2,000)
  • MZ (엠지) cafes (all areas of Seoul (서울)) — Dalgona Latte (달고나 라떼)·muffins
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (Global recognition #1)
  • Price: ₩1,000~3,000 (street food) / ₩6,000~9,000 (latte)
  • Season: Year-round

🐟 Bungeoppang (붕어빵) — Winter Street Food

  • Signature: Introduced during the Japanese colonial period (일제강점기) in the 1930s from Japanese Taiyaki (鯛焼き) → transformed into a Korean pat (팥, red bean) traditional variation.
  • Types:
  • Pat Bungeoppang (팥 붕어빵, Red bean): Basic
  • Choux Cream Bungeoppang (슈크림 붕어빵, Custard cream): MZ (엠지) trend
  • Pizza Bungeoppang (피자 붕어빵): Variation
  • Recommended Stores: Street stalls (winter season November-March)
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (Instagram shape ★)
  • Price: ₩1,000~2,000/pc (3 pcs for ₩5,000 generally)
  • Season: Winter limited (November-March)

🌼 Gukhwappang (국화빵) — Bungeoppang Cousin

  • Signature: Settled as street food pre- and post-Korean War (한국전쟁) - chrysanthemum shape.
  • Types: Pat Gukhwappang (팥 국화빵, Red bean) (basic) / Cream Gukhwappang (크림 국화빵) (trend)
  • Recommended Stores: Insadong (인사동)·Jongno (종로) traditional stalls
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★ (Instagram shape ★)
  • Price: ₩1,000~2,000/pc
  • Season: Autumn~Winter

🌾 Yakgwa (약과) — Royal Tradition + MZ Trend

  • Signature: Joseon (조선) royal court dessert (honey+sesame oil+flour)
  • Types:
  • Traditional Yakgwa (약과): Insadong (인사동)·traditional Hangwa (한과) shops
  • MZ (엠지) Yakgwa (약과): Yakgwa Latte (약과 라떼)·Yakgwa Croissant (약과 크루아상) (Hongdae (홍대)·Gangnam (강남) cafes)
  • Yakgwa Ice Cream (약과 아이스크림): Sulbing (설빙)·MZ (엠지) cafes
  • Recommended Stores:
  • Insadong (인사동) traditional Hangwa (한과) shops (traditional)
  • MZ (엠지) cafes (Hongdae (홍대)·Seongsu (성수)·Gangnam (강남)) — Yakgwa Latte (약과 라떼)·Yakgwa (약과) bread (Instagram No. 1 spot)
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★ (MZ (엠지) trend ★★★★★)
  • Price: ₩1,500~5,000/pc
  • Season: Year-round (Autumn·Winter ↑)

🍧 Bingsu (빙수) — Summer #1

  • Signature: Influenced by Kakigori (かき氷) during the Japanese colonial period (일제강점기) in the 1920s + combined with Joseon Dynasty (조선시대) Seokbinggo (석빙고, ice storage) ice dessert tradition → traditionalization of Patbingsu (팥빙수, red bean Bingsu).
  • Types:
  • Patbingsu (팥빙수, Red bean): Traditional
  • Mango Bingsu (망고빙수): MZ (엠지) trend
  • Strawberry Bingsu (딸기빙수): Spring·early summer
  • Nokcha Bingsu (녹차빙수, Green tea matcha): MZ (엠지)
  • Hotel Bingsu (빙수, Hotel premium): Shilla (신라)·Lotte (롯데) signature (₩40,000~80,000)
  • Recommended Stores:
  • Sulbing (설빙, chain) — Korea #1 Bingsu (빙수) chain (cost-effective)
  • Shilla Hotel Bingsu (신라호텔 빙수) — Luxury (₩50,000~80,000)
  • Hongdae (홍대)·Gangnam (강남)·Itaewon (이태원) MZ (엠지) Bingsu (빙수) cafes
  • Foreigner Friendliness: ★★★★★ (Summer enthusiasts #1·Instagram ★)
  • Price: ₩8,000~25,000 (general) / ₩40,000~80,000 (hotel)
  • Season: Summer Top 1 Priority (June-September)

⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Korean Desserts = Only Dalgona (달고나)": There's a full set of 6 types + cafe clusters. Hotteok (호떡) (winter #1) + Bingsu (빙수) (summer #1) are popular.

2. "Hotteok (호떡) = Japanese Hot Cakes": Korean Hotteok (호떡) = brown sugar + nuts + warm street food (1900s). Japanese hot cakes are a different dish.

3. "Bingsu (빙수) = Single Menu Item": Bingsu (빙수) = 6+ types (pat (팥)·mango·strawberry·green tea·luxury hotel). Hotel Bingsu (빙수) = ₩40,000~80,000 luxury.

4. "Dalgona (달고나) = Difficult to Make": In Insadong (인사동)·Jongno (종로) street food areas in Korea = instant experience for ₩1,000~2,000. Experience the Squid Game (오징어 게임) tradition.

5. "Bungeoppang (붕어빵) = All Seasons": Bungeoppang (붕어빵) = winter limited (November-March). Street stalls are rarely found in summer.

⚙ FAQ 8 Questions

1. Top 1 Introduction for Foreigners: Hotteok (호떡) (winter) / Bingsu (빙수) (summer) / Dalgona (달고나) (year-round).

2. Top 1 for Instagram/Vlog: Dalgona Latte (달고나 라떼)·Yakgwa Latte (약과 라떼)·Hotel Bingsu (빙수)·Insadong (인사동) Hanok (한옥) cafes.

3. MZ (엠지) Trend: Dalgona Latte (달고나 라떼)·Yakgwa Croissant (약과 크루아상)·Sulbing (설빙)·Blue Bottle+Yakgwa (약과).

4. Luxury·Hospitality: Shilla (신라)·Lotte (롯데)·Westin Hotel Bingsu (빙수) (₩40,000~80,000).

5. Seasonal Enthusiasts: Spring=Yakgwa (약과) / Summer=Bingsu (빙수) / Autumn=Hotteok (호떡)·Gukhwappang (국화빵) / Winter=Hotteok (호떡)·Bungeoppang (붕어빵)·Dalgona (달고나).

6. Allergies: Gluten (wheat)·nuts (Hotteok (호떡) seeds)·milk (Bingsu (빙수))·eggs (Yakgwa (약과))·honey (Yakgwa (약과)) should be declared in advance.

7. Payment·Tips: Street stalls = cash recommended / Cafes·Chains = card OK / Hotels = 10% service charge / No tips.

8. VAT Refund: General food and beverage are not eligible for VAT refund.

⚠ Medical·Safety·Dietary Disclaimer

Hotteok (호떡)·Bungeoppang (붕어빵)·Gukhwappang (국화빵) = hot desserts (90~100°C) — caution for burns (especially children). Bingsu (빙수) = cold (caution for milk allergies·high sugar content). Pregnant women = appropriate amounts of nuts·honey. Declare allergies (gluten·nuts·milk·eggs·honey·soy) in advance. In case of suspected food poisoning, call ☎ 1339 (medical, English). This guide provides general information, not medical advice.

Store Information Disclaimer: Street stalls·seasonally limited stores change frequently — checking NAVER (네이버)/Kakao Map (카카오맵) or local search before visiting is recommended.

Dietary Restrictions Fallback:

  • Halal·Kosher: Few certified stores among the 6 types. → Yakgwa (약과)·Dalgona (달고나) = no animal ingredients (some Halal·Kosher possible). Hotteok (호떡)·Bungeoppang (붕어빵)·Bingsu (빙수) = milk·pork fat possible (needs confirmation).
  • Vegetarian·Vegan: Yakgwa (약과)·Dalgona (달고나) = vegetarian OK (check for honey·milk) / Hotteok (호떡)·Bungeoppang (붕어빵)·Bingsu (빙수) = much milk·eggs → separate vegan cafes.
  • Gluten-free: All 6 types contain much flour. Only Bingsu (빙수) can be an option (by removing flour toppings).
