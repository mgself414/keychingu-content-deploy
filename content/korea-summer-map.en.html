⚠ 3 summer mistakes

1. "Hangang = only chicken and beer" — Fountain show/magic carpet misrecognition: Only chicken and beer is recognized. In reality, Banpo Rainbow Fountain (Korea #1) + Yeouido Magic Carpet Fountain Show 19/20/21:00 Summer signature.

2. "Busan = Beach Bay" — Yangyang·Yeosu unknown: Busan Bay recognized. In reality, Yangyang Surfing (Korea #1) + Yeosu Night Sea (Korea #1 Night View) Korea’s national summer pool experience.

3. “Not knowing the summer festival” — Boryeong Mud Mystery: Recognizing absence. Actually, it is Boryeong Mud Festival (Korea #1 summer festival in mid-July, foreigners 500,000+).
