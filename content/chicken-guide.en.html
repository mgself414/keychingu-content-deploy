28_Korean Fried Chicken Restaurant Guide

One-Line Intro

Korean fried chicken is different — crunchier, saucier, and better paired with beer.

Korean fried chicken stands apart from its American counterpart: it's crispier, comes in a wide variety of sauces, and is best enjoyed as a late-night snack alongside beer — a culture known as chimaek (치맥).

Who This Is For

  • Travelers who want to experience Korean late-night food culture (chimaek (치맥))
  • Travelers who want to sit back, relax, and enjoy Korean-style fried chicken with a cold beer

Recommended Itinerary (3 Hours)

1. 19:00 — Arrive at the chicken spot (dine-in or delivery app order both work)

2. 19:10 — Place your order: Banban (반반) half-and-half + 2 bottles of beer (₩35,000–40,000 for 2 people)

3. 20:00 — Chicken arrives: Expect a 30–40 minute wait; start with the Yangnyeom (양념) side — as it cools, the sauce soaks in and it gets even tastier

4. 21:00 — Second round of beer (or Somaek (소맥) — a mix of soju (소주) and beer): a genuine taste of Korean drinking culture

5. 22:00 — Move on: Wrap up with a stroll to the Han River (한강) night view or a nearby café

Tips for Foreign Travelers

  • Useful Korean Phrases
  • "Huraideu hana juseyo (후라이드 하나 주세요)" — One original fried chicken, please
  • "Maekju du byeong juseyo (맥주 두 병 주세요)" — Two bottles of beer, please
  • "Banban euro juseyo (반반으로 주세요)" — Half-and-half, please (Huraideu (후라이드) + Yangnyeom (양념))
  • Cultural Note: Fried chicken is fundamentally a late-night food in Korea. Many shops don't open in the morning and typically start from 15:00–18:00.
  • Payment: Card payment is widely accepted. Delivery apps like Baemin (배달의민족) require a Korean phone number.
  • Chimaek Tip: The chicken + beer combo is called "chimaek (치맥)." Enjoying it by the Hangang River (한강) is especially iconic.

Simple English Summary

Korean fried chicken — best known through the culture of chimaek (치맥, chicken + maekju (맥주) / beer) — is a national institution. The key difference from American fried chicken: the coating is thinner and crispier, and sauce options are far more varied — honey garlic, spicy-sweet, soy garlic, and cheese powder. A whole chicken (₩20,000–28,000) easily feeds two people. Most chains like Kyochon (교촌치킨), bhc (bhc치킨), and BBQ (BBQ치킨) are open from 3pm until midnight. Try the banban (반반) — half original, half sauced — if you can't decide.

Estimated Total Cost

  • Budget: ₩20,000 (half chicken + convenience store beer)
  • Standard: ₩40,000 (whole chicken + 2 bottles of beer, for 2 people)
  • Splurge: ₩60,000 (chicken + side dishes + extra soju (소주) and beer, for 2 people)
