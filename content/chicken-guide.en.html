Korean Fried Chicken Guide — Full Guide to Korean Chicken for Foreign Tourists

Subtitle: Fried, yangnyeom, soy garlic, spicy + chimaek culture + chains vs. local favorites — 8 categories of Korean chicken + a one-day course

ISSUE 02 · #28 · 2026-05-15 (v2 full expansion)

🎯 Why Korean Chicken Deserves Its Own Guide

Korean fried chicken is a category that appears in 100% of foreign visitors' itineraries — driven by Netflix and YouTube exposure through K-dramas and the global recognition of "chimaek (치맥)" (chicken + beer) culture. It's the essence of Korean food that foreign tourists experience everywhere — Han River (한강) picnics, hotel rooms, and chicken shops alike.

8 Chimaek Set (치맥 세트) ₩30K–45K One whole chicken + beer

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Not checking spice level in advance — Buldak and spicy yangnyeom sauces are very hot by Korean standards. Ask for "not spicy please" ahead of time.

2. Forgetting delivery fees — Korean chicken delivery adds ₩3,000–5,000. Delivery to hotels and Han River (한강) parks is available.

3. Not distinguishing chains from local favorites — Chains like BHC and Kyochon (교촌) are consistent and reliable; neighborhood spots offer a more authentic Korean take.

⚙ FAQ

Q1. English menus and ordering?

A. Chains (BHC, Kyochon, Goobne) offer English menus and apps (Yogiyo, Baedal Minjok). Neighborhood spots are mostly Korean-only.

Q2. Essential Korean phrases?

A. "Chicken hana juseyo (치킨 한 마리 주세요)" (1 chicken please) / "Maepji anke (맵지 않게)" (not spicy) / "Pojanghae juseyo (포장해주세요)" (to go) / "Maekju han byeong chuga (맥주 한 병 추가)" (1 more beer please).

Q3. Recommendations by budget?

A. Budget ₩15K: Dakgangjeong or single-serving menus. Standard ₩22K–28K: one whole fried or yangnyeom chicken. Luxury ₩30K+: chimaek sets, local favorites.

Q4. Combining with a Han River chimaek picnic?

A. Hangang Park (한강공원) → order chicken delivery (BHC, Kyochon, BBQ) + convenience store beer = ₩35K–50K, the #1 Han River picnic combo.

Q5. Vegetarian options?

A. Chicken shops have almost no vegetarian menu items. Goobne offers some vegetarian side dishes. Vegetarian visitors should check #69's vegan/halal guide.

💡 Foreign Visitor Pain-Point Guide

  • Language: shops with full English, Japanese, and Chinese signage are recommended first
  • Payment: cards accepted at about 80% of shops; some old-school stalls are cash-only
  • VAT refund: look for the "Tax Refund" sign · minimum ₩50,000 purchase · refund counters at the airport
  • Emergencies: ☎ 1339 (medical, English available) · ☎ 1330 (tourist interpretation) · ☎ 112 (police) · ☎ 119 (fire & ambulance)
