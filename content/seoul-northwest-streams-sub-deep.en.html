Hongjecheon and Bulgwangcheon Guide: Hongje Waterfall and Waterfall Bookstore Areumin Library in Seodaemun, Bulgwangcheon in Eunpyeong

Subtitle: Hongje Waterfall (08:00~21:00, 25m high) · Waterfall Bookstore Areumin Library (open year-round) · Bulgwangcheon (about 9.21km, always open) — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers places to visit along Hongjecheon (홍제천) and Bulgwangcheon (불광천), the streams that flow through northwest Seoul. They are an artificial waterfall on Hongjecheon in Seodaemun-gu (서대문구) and the Pokpo Chaekbang Areumin Library (폭포책방 아름인도서관), and the Bulgwangcheon walking path that starts in Eunpyeong-gu (은평구). All three places are registered as open year-round.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Hongjecheon — Hongje Waterfall and Waterfall Bookstore Areumin Library

Hongje Waterfall (홍제폭포) [170-181 Yeonhui-dong (연희동), Seodaemun-gu, ☎ 02-2236-9134] is an artificial waterfall built along Hongjecheon in 2011, 25m high and 60m wide. Depending on the season you can see streams of water, an ice wall, spring flowers and autumn leaves. There is a resting place around the waterfall, and across from it there are a waterside cafe, an outdoor terrace and a small library.

  • Opening hours: 08:00~21:00. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Available. Stroller rental: None.

Waterfall Bookstore Areumin Library (폭포책방 아름인도서관) [Yeonhui-ro 262-24 (연희로 262-24), Seodaemun-gu, ☎ 02-3217-0693] is a library where you can read to the sound of the waterfall.

  • Closed days: Open year-round.

📍 Bulgwangcheon — From Eunpyeong to Mapo

Bulgwangcheon (불광천) [Jeungsan-ro 478 (증산로 478), Eunpyeong-gu, ☎ Eunpyeong-gu Office (은평구청) Flood Control Division (치수과) 02-351-7982] is a stream of about 9.21km that starts in Bulgwang-dong (불광동), Eunpyeong-gu, and flows past Yeokchon-dong (역촌동), Eungam-dong (응암동), Jeungsan-dong (증산동), Bukgajwa-dong (북가좌동) in Seodaemun-gu, and Seongsan-dong (성산동) in Mapo-gu (마포구). It took its present form in 2002, when a sewage prevention facility was installed and groundwater was pumped up to keep the water flowing. The name comes from the nearby Bulgwangsa Temple (불광사). Upstream there is a waterside grandstand where fountain shows are held. Along the stream there are a bike path and a walkway, and you can see landscaping that changes with the season and a restored ecosystem.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round.
  • Parking: Not available.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Hongje Waterfall is open 08:00~21:00 — it is not always open.
  2. Bulgwangcheon has no parking — Hongje Waterfall has parking.
  3. Hongje Waterfall is an artificial waterfall — it was built in 2011.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. When can I see Hongje Waterfall?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registered information, it is open 08:00~21:00, year-round. Across from the waterfall there are a waterside cafe, an outdoor terrace and a small library.

Q. Can I cycle along Bulgwangcheon?

A. There are a bike path and a walkway along the stream. It is always open.
