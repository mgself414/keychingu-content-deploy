🎯 Why a Full Guide to Korea's Bookstores & Independent Bookstores?

Korean bookstores and independent bookstores are a top priority for foreign book lovers and the MZ (Millennials and Gen Z) lifestyle. Previously, information was scattered across #135 Bookstores, #156 Yeonhui (Your Mind), and #188 Mapo (Kim Min-kyung's 82-Born Cafe) areas, so we've created a comprehensive guide.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Bookstore = Only Kyobo Book Centre": Korean bookstores have 4 pillars — Large (Kyobo·Youngpoong·YES24·Aladdin) + Independent Bookstores (Your Mind·Goyoseosa·Novel List) + MZ·Theme (Starfield Library·Arc.N.Book·Kim Min-kyung's 82-Born Cafe) + Outskirts·Tourist (Bukchon·Insadong). Explore all four types!

2. "Starfield Library (별마당 도서관) = Just a Library": Starfield Library, opened in 2017 within COEX (코엑스) with a 13m (42.6 ft) wall bookshelf, is a top Instagram spot for Korean MZ and a must-visit photo spot for foreigners. Free admission + free access to books.

3. "Independent Bookstores = Too Small to Find": Your Mind (유어마인드) (Yeonhui), Goyoseosa (고요서사) (Seongsu), and Novel List (소설리스트) (Itaewon) are signature stores in their respective areas + cross-reference with area guides.

① Large Bookstores (4)

1. Kyobo Book Centre Gwanghwamun (교보문고 광화문) ★ Korea's No. 1 Spot (1981)

  • Address: 1, Jong-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 종로 1)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Korea's No. 1 large bookstore
  • Signature: Established in 1981 · Foreign language book section · Stationery · Merchandise
  • Hours: 09:30 AM - 22:00 PM

2. Youngpoong Bookstore Jongno Branch (영풍문고 종로점) (1992)

  • Address: 41, Cheonggyecheon-ro, Jongno-gu (종로구 청계천로 41)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Jongno's (종로) No. 1 spot + adjacent to Cheonggyecheon (청계천)

3. YES24 Live Hall (예스24 라이브홀) (Hongdae)

  • Address: Yanghwa-ro, Mapo-gu (마포구 양화로) (#138 Hongdae)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — YES24 Concert Hall + Bookstore combination

4. Aladdin Used Bookstore (알라딘 중고서점) (Gangnam·Jongno)

  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Korea's No. 1 used bookstore + popular with English book enthusiasts

② Independent & Lifestyle Bookstores (4)

5. Your Mind (유어마인드) ★ Korea's No. 1 Independent Bookstore

  • Address: 122, Yeonhui-ro, Seodaemun-gu (서대문구 연희로 122) (#156 Yeonhui)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — Korea's No. 1 spot for the independent bookstore revival, established in 2009
  • Signature: Yeonhui-dong (연희동) · Sister guide to #156 Yeonhui

6. Goyoseosa (고요서사) (Seongsu)

  • Address: Seongsu-dong, Seongdong-gu (성동구 성수동) (#01·#145)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Seongsu (성수) independent bookstore + design combination

7. Novel List (소설리스트) (Itaewon)

  • Address: Itaewon, Yongsan-gu (용산구 이태원) (#144)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — No. 1 spot for mystery/thriller (foreigner-friendly in Itaewon)

8. House of Yi Sang (이상의집) (Bukchon)

  • Address: Bukchon area, Jongno-gu (종로구 북촌 일대) (#12)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Commemorating Korea's No. 1 poet Yi Sang + hanok (traditional Korean house) combination

③ MZ & Theme Bookstores (4)

9. Starfield Library (별마당 도서관) (COEX) ★ MZ Instagram No. 1 Spot

  • Address: 513, Yeongdong-daero, Gangnam-gu, COEX Mall (강남구 영동대로 513 코엑스몰) (#142)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎◎ — 13m (42.6 ft) wall bookshelf + Korea's No. 1 MZ Instagram spot
  • Signature: Opened in 2017 · Free admission · Partially 24-hour · Foreigner photo spot

10. Arc.N.Book (아크앤북) (Euljiro)

  • Address: Euljiro area, Jung-gu (중구 을지로 일대) (#11 Euljiro sister guide)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Euljiro (을지로) lifestyle bookstore · design store combination

11. Kim Min-kyung's 82-Born Cafe (82년생 김민경 카페) (Mapo Gongdeok)

  • Address: Gongdeok, Mapo-gu (마포구 공덕) (#188)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Collaboration cafe with the author of the Korean bestseller "Kim Ji-young, Born 1982"

12. The Hyundai Books & Bookstore (더현대 도서·서점) (Gangnam)

  • Address: The Hyundai Seoul, Yeongdeungpo (영등포 더현대 서울) (#169) or Gangnam Hyundai Department Store (강남 현대백화점)
  • Foreigner ★: ◎ — Department store + curated bookstore
