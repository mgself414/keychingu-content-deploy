3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. The Misconception That "K-pop Dance = Only Idol Cover Dance"

Korea's dance scene is far broader than cover dance. 1Million became the #1 YouTube channel in the world not because of idol covers, but because of original choreography. Choreographers at 1Million like Lia Kim, Dr. Woo, and Naky Kim create official MV choreography for BTS, BLACKPINK, and TWICE while keeping their own style. JustJerk focuses on street genres like hip-hop, locking, and popping. Foreigners are often caught off guard, saying, "I came to learn K-pop covers, but it was full-on street dance." Checking the class genre before booking is essential.

2. The Assumption That "Same-Day Walk-Ins Are OK"

Foreign demand for the 1Million Visit Class is so high that booking 2-4 weeks ahead is standard. Lia Kim's and Dr. Woo's classes in particular fill up the moment they open. JustJerk's foreigner-only classes also need booking 1-2 weeks ahead. Same-day walk-ins are only possible in beginner classes that haven't filled up. Checking the schedule on the official Instagram or website and locking in a spot with online payment is the only way.

3. The Perception That "You Need to Already Be Skilled to Join"

Both the 1Million Visit Class and JustJerk's foreigner classes run separate beginner and intermediate levels. Class difficulty is stated in the notice. Many foreigner reviews actually say things like "they taught me slowly even with zero dance experience." Signing up for a beginner class first, without pressure, is fine. That said, while watching a street battle event is free, actually competing in a battle realistically requires intermediate skill or above.

Why an Indie Dance Sub Now — 1Million World #1 + Street K-pop Goes Global

#552 covered K-pop dance academies. This #614 sub-deep is an indie dance/street-only matrix — 1Million, JustJerk, K-pop choreographers, street dance, and foreign dancer mania.

1Million Dance Studio's YouTube subscriber count passed 30 million as of 2024, and its 1B+ cumulative views make it the #1 K-pop dance YouTube channel in the world. What that number means isn't just popularity — it means people in Thailand, Indonesia, Brazil, and the Middle East who copied this choreography are coming to visit the studio in Seoul in person. 1Million runs a studio in Gangnam, Seoul, and regularly opens a foreigner-only Visit Class, building a structure where students can learn directly from the choreographer.

JustJerk is a street dance crew with a history of collaborating with YG Entertainment, and is one of the few official channels where you can experience hip-hop-, locking-, and popping-centered crew culture directly in Seoul.

12 Picks Across 4 Axes — Venues, Studios & Events Deep Dive

Axis 1: 1Million & JustJerk Studios (3 picks)

1. 1Million Dance Studio (Gangnam)

  • Location: Gangnam-gu, Seoul (the exact address is given in official guidance after your booking is confirmed — check the official Instagram @1milliondance or the website)
  • Features: the studio behind the world's #1 K-pop choreography YouTube channel. Famous choreographers like Lia Kim and Dr. Woo teach in person
  • Foreigner-only Visit Class: a 1-2 hour class. Runs in English or with interpretation support. Beginner-to-intermediate levels split out
  • Booking: the official website or an Instagram DM. Booking 2-4 weeks ahead is required. Card payment accepted
  • Price: ₩60,000~₩80,000/class (2026 basis, varies by choreographer and level)
  • English available: Yes (foreigner classes run in English)
  • Timing tip: weekend morning classes fill up fastest. Weekday evening classes tend to have more room
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ — the #1 bucket-list item for dance fans worldwide

2. JustJerk Studio (Near Hongdae)

  • Location: near Hongdae, Mapo-gu, Seoul (check the current studio location via the official Instagram @justjerk or the website)
  • Features: a street dance crew studio with a history of YG Entertainment collaboration. Centered on hip-hop, locking, popping
  • Foreigner classes: runs classes in English. Includes a beginner foundation class
  • Booking: the official website/Instagram. Booking 1-2 weeks ahead is recommended
  • Price: ₩50,000~₩70,000/class (2026 basis)
  • English available: Yes (foreigner classes in English)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★☆ — for street dance fans and visitors wanting to experience hip-hop crew culture beyond K-pop covers

3. HYBE Academy, SM Culture & Arts, JYP Dance Academy (Gangnam, Cheongdam)

  • Features: the official dance academies of the 4 major agencies (HYBE, SM, JYP, YG). Experience the official choreography lineage of BTS, IU, and TWICE firsthand
  • Foreigner classes: each academy runs a short foreigner experience class (check the schedule on the official website)
  • Price: ₩60,000~₩100,000/class (varies by agency and level, 2026 basis)
  • Booking: advance booking on the official website is required. Foreigner classes have a separate application page
  • English available: agency academies have an English support system
  • Cross-ref: see the #387 K-pop's 4 major agencies guide
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ — top priority for visitors who are fans of a specific idol

Axis 2: Street Dance & Battle Scene (3 picks)

4. Hongdae Street Performances & Street Dance Cluster

  • Location: around Hongdae's Walking Street, Mapo-gu, Seoul (a 5-minute walk from Exit 9, Hongik Univ. Station)
  • Features: grassroots street-dancer performances on weekend afternoons (14:00-19:00) in front of Hongdae Plaza. Many locking, popping, and b-boying crews
  • Entry: free to watch. No advance booking needed
  • Timing: weekend afternoons are peak. Sporadic on weekdays. Clear-weather Saturdays 15:00-17:00 are the most active
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★☆ — accessible with no booking if you're just there to watch. For visitors wanting a hip-hop/street atmosphere
  • Tip: asking a performer directly for contact info sometimes connects you to a studio or class. Making eye contact with the performer to confirm before filming on your phone is essential

5. K-pop Choreography Class Cluster (Mixed Across Gangnam & Hongdae)

  • Features: beyond 1Million and JustJerk, many independently run K-pop choreography studios exist around Gangnam and Hongdae. Structured around learning a specific idol song's choreography intensively
  • How to book: an Instagram DM to each studio, or Naver Reservation. Searching "K-pop dance class foreigner" surfaces a list of studios running foreigner classes
  • Price: ₩40,000~₩70,000/class (2026 basis, varies by studio and song difficulty)
  • English available: varies by studio — checking at booking is essential
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★☆ — for fans who want to directly learn the choreography of their favorite idol song

6. Street Battle Event Cluster (Hongdae/Gangnam Clubs & Venues)

  • Features: street dance battle events held 2-3 times a year at venues/clubs around Hongdae and Gangnam. Runs separate categories for hip-hop, popping, locking, and b-boying
  • Watching: ₩10,000~₩20,000 (entry fee). Competing in a battle requires advance registration + intermediate-level skill or above
  • Info channel: the Instagram hashtags #SeoulDanceBattle and #seoulstreetdance, or check event notices reposted by 1Million/JustJerk's official accounts
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★☆☆ (watching) · ★★☆☆☆ (competing) — as an inside-the-scene event, accessibility for foreigners is low. Following info channels beforehand is essential

FAQ 6

Q1. Can I take a class with zero dance experience?

Beginner classes run separately. The 1Million Visit Class and K-pop choreography workshops (Klook, Airbnb Experience) regularly run classes marked as beginner level. Just be sure to check the posted difficulty (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced) when booking. With no dance experience, choosing a Beginner or "K-pop dance experience"-tagged class is a low-pressure choice.

Q3. Is it conducted in English?

The 1Million Visit Class, JustJerk, and agency academies run their foreigner-only classes in English. Klook and Airbnb Experience tourism products run in English by default. For independent studio classes, confirming English availability by DM when booking is the safe move.

Q4. What order should I go through the series in?

The natural order is #552 (K-pop dance academies, parent) → #614 (indie/street mania) → #387 (4 major agencies) → #607 (K-pop fan events). If it's your first visit, get an overview from #552 first, then pick the axis you're interested in from #614 and book.

Q5. How far ahead should I book?

For the 1Million Visit Class (famous choreographers' classes), 2-4 weeks ahead is standard. Since some fill up the moment they open, following the official Instagram and turning on notifications is recommended. JustJerk is 1-2 weeks ahead. For Klook/Airbnb Experience tourism products, booking 3-7 days ahead is usually enough.

Q6. If I'm a K-pop fan, which class should I pick?

The academy of your favorite idol's agency is the top recommendation. HYBE Academy for BTS/NewJeans fans, SM-related classes for aespa/SHINee fans, JYP Dance Academy for TWICE/Stray Kids fans. 1Million suits visitors who love the K-pop dance scene itself more than a specific idol. All of them list the related idol and genre in the class description at booking, so compare and choose.
