Korea Dance Competition & Battle Festival Guide — Busan Yonggol Dance Festa, Anyang Dance Festival, Cheonan Heungtaryeong Dance Festival, Gwangju Busking World Cup Street Dance Battle

Subtitle: 2026 Yonggol Dance Festa (6/20, Yongdusan Park, Busan, ended) · Anyang Dance Festival (9/18~20, ended) · Cheonan Heungtaryeong Dance Festival 2026 (10/1~5, International Street Dance Championship, ended) · Gwangju Busking World Cup (10/7~11, street dance battle 10/9, ended) — all free — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers the 4 festivals in 2026 where you can watch dance competitions, battles and random play dance in Korea, in the order they were held. All 4 are registered as free, and all of the 2026 dates have ended.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI) for 2026. This guide covers only the 4 festivals, so dance academies and classes are left to #552 K-pop Dance Academy Deep Dive Guide.

📍 June — Busan 2026 Yonggol Dance Festa

2026 Yonggol Dance Festa (Yongdusan-gil 37-55, Jung-gu, Busan, around Yongdusan Park) was held on June 20, 2026. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, "Yonggol (Yongdusan Park)" was the birthplace of Korea's dance boom in the '90s and a holy ground of B-BOY culture around the world. 2026 was the 4th edition, and it is a dance competition festival where children and teenagers who love dancing take the stage regardless of genre.

  • Hours: 13:00~18:00. Fee: Free.
  • Programs: Youth dance competition, random play dance, dance battle, food trucks and more.

📍 September — Anyang Dance Festival

Anyang Dance Festival (Pyeongchon Central Park and Samdeok Park) was held on September 18~20, 2026. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it began in 2000 as the Anyang Citizens' Festival and is a festival with citizen participation. Under the slogan "Walk, run, and dance!", it aims to be a local K-Dance culture and arts festival.

  • Hours: 12:00~21:00. Fee: Free.
  • Main programs: Crown Competition dance contest, Stain to groove dance battle, dance concert and more.
  • Side programs: Citizen performances, Anyang Golden Dance Contest, random play dance, screenings of dance-related films and more.

📍 Early October — Cheonan Heungtaryeong Dance Festival 2026

Cheonan Heungtaryeong Dance Festival 2026 (Beonyeong-ro 208, Seobuk-gu, Cheonan-si, Cheonan Sports Complex and Cheonan Samgeori Park) was held on October 1~5, 2026. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is a dance festival first held in 2003, selected 8 times as a Culture and Tourism Festival by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and designated an Honorary Culture and Tourism Festival since 2020. It is a global dance festival staged in the city center.

  • Hours: 10:00~22:00. Fee: Free.
  • Main programs: National dance competition, international dance competition, street dance parade.
  • Side programs: International Street Dance Championship (CIDC), Pump It Up (펌프잇업) World Championship, 2026 Korea Dance Grand Prize.
  • Also: Overseas team parade, random play dance, fringe performances, experience booths, food zone and food trucks.

📍 October — Gwangju Busking World Cup Street Dance Battle

Gwangju Busking World Cup, at 5·18 Democracy Square around Chungjang-ro (충장로), was an international street performance music festival held on October 7~11, 2026. According to the Korea Tourism Organization, street performance artists from Korea and abroad compete on vocal, band, acoustic and performance stages. A street dance battle is included in the registered programs.

  • Hours: 13:00~22:00. Fee: Free.
  • Street dance battle: October 9, 14:00, 5·18 Democracy Square.
  • Other schedule: Busking live competition top-8 round on October 10 and final on October 11, international student busking on October 8 at 15:00.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. All of the 2026 dates have ended — the dates and programs in the table are 2026 values.
  2. All 4 are registered as free — Yonggol Dance Festa, Anyang Dance Festival, Cheonan Heungtaryeong Dance Festival and Gwangju Busking World Cup all list the fee as "free".
  3. 3 festivals have random play dance — random play dance is registered in the programs of Yonggol Dance Festa, Anyang Dance Festival and Cheonan Heungtaryeong Dance Festival.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. Which festivals have overseas dancers taking part?

A. According to Korea Tourism Organization registered information, Cheonan Heungtaryeong Dance Festival has an international dance competition, the International Street Dance Championship and an overseas team parade, and the Gwangju Busking World Cup street dance battle is introduced as "a showdown of dancers from around the world".

Q. Which festival can teenagers perform at?

A. 2026 Yonggol Dance Festa is registered as a dance competition festival for children and teenagers.
