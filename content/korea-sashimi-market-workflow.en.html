Korean Hoe (Raw Fish) Ordering, Market, Restaurant, and Mae-untang Workflow — From Jagalchi to Noryangjin

> Subtitle: A 5-step Workflow for Foreign Tourists + Market, Hoe (Raw Fish) Restaurant, Mae-untang Course, and English Phrases

ISSUE 03 · #865 · 2026-06-09

🎯 Why a Korean Hoe (Raw Fish) Workflow Guide?

Even if you know the [#864 6 Types of Hoe (Raw Fish) Overview](864_korean_hoe_overview.md), ordering, market navigation, hoe (raw fish) restaurants, and Mae-untang (매운탕 - spicy fish stew) are separate challenges. Korean hoe (회 - raw fish) comes in several forms: (a) Traditional Market Style (live, freshly-caught fish from Noryangjin (노량진), Jagalchi (자갈치), Tongyeong (통영), Soraepogu (소래포구)), (b) Hoe (Raw Fish) Restaurants (traditional full set meals in Gangnam (강남), Hongdae (홍대), Itaewon (이태원)), (c) All-You-Can-Eat Tuna & Salmon (cost-effective for MZ tourists), and (d) Han-jeongsik (한정식 - traditional Korean set meal) Hoe (Raw Fish) Course (traditional luxury). Foreign visitors often don't know (1) the distinction between the 1st floor (raw fish counter) and 2nd floor (restaurant) in markets, (2) the etiquette for Chojang (초장 - spicy red sauce) vs. wasabi, (3) that the Mae-untang (매운탕) course is the finale, or (4) how to negotiate live fish prices. This guide provides a 5-step workflow + market & restaurant tips + English phrases.

★ 5-Step Workflow at a Glance

Step Action Time Key Point for Tourists
1️⃣ Channel Selection Market / Hoe Restaurant / All-You-Can-Eat / Han-jeongsik Immediate Purpose: Cost-effective, Traditional, MZ, Luxury
2️⃣ Menu Ordering Live fish 1kg / 1 Gwan (관 - approx. 2-3 servings) / 1 serving 5~15 min Market = kg unit
3️⃣ Receiving & Setup Hoe (raw fish) + Chojang (초장), Ssamchaeso (쌈채소 - wrap vegetables), garlic, Mae-untang (매운탕) (optional) 10~20 min Chojang (초장) & Ssam (쌈 - wrap) etiquette
4️⃣ Eating & Pairing Chojang (초장) + Ssam (쌈) + Soju (소주 - Korean distilled liquor) / Beer 30~90 min Mae-untang (매운탕) = Final Course
5️⃣ Payment Card OK / Market = some cash / No tipping 1~5 min Market = Price negotiation possible

> Total Time Required: Market = 1~2h / Hoe Restaurant = 1.5~3h / All-You-Can-Eat = 1~2h / Han-jeongsik = 2~3h

🐟 Workflow by Channel

Type A — Market (Noryangjin (노량진), Jagalchi (자갈치), Tongyeong (통영), Soraepogu (소래포구), Guryongpo (구룡포))

  • Top Picks: Live, freshly-caught fish, traditional, cost-effective, 1st floor raw fish counter + 2nd floor restaurant
  • Hours: Varies by market (Noryangjin (노량진) = early morning wholesale & general 09:00~22:00)
  • Price: Live fish 1kg = ₩20,000 (~$15 / ~110 RMB / ~2,200 JPY) to ₩60,000 (~$44 / ~330 RMB / ~6,600 JPY) + Charimbi (차림비 - table setting fee) ₩5,000 (~$3.7 / ~27 RMB / ~550 JPY) to ₩15,000 (~$11 / ~83 RMB / ~1,650 JPY)/person
  • Menu: Live fish 1kg (olive flounder, yellowtail, sea bream, gizzard shad, etc.) + Ssamchaeso (쌈채소), Mae-untang (매운탕)
  • Tourist Friendliness: ★★★★★ (English menus, tourist-friendly)
  • Noryangjin (노량진): ([#587 Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market Early Morning Guide](587_noryangjin_fish_market_early_morning_guide.md)) — Early morning wholesale & general restaurants
  • Jagalchi (자갈치): ([#590 Jagalchi Market Deep Dive](590_jagalchi_fish_market_deep_dive.md)) — Busan's #1, traditional yellowtail

Type B — Hoe (Raw Fish) Restaurant (Gangnam (강남), Hongdae (홍대), Itaewon (이태원))

  • Top Picks: Traditional full set meal, mid-range price
  • Hours: 17:00~24:00
  • Price: ₩50,000 (~$37 / ~275 RMB / ~5,500 JPY) to ₩150,000 (~$111 / ~825 RMB / ~16,500 JPY)/person (traditional full set meal)
  • Menu: Hoe (raw fish) + Chojang (초장) + Ssamchaeso (쌈채소) + Mae-untang (매운탕) + Alcohol
  • Tourist Friendliness: ★★★★ (English menus)

Type C — Tuna & Salmon All-You-Can-Eat (MZ, Cost-effective)

  • Top Picks: MZ Instagram-friendly, #1 for cost-effectiveness, Hongdae (홍대), Gangnam (강남), Sinchon (신촌)
  • Hours: 11:30~24:00
  • Price: ₩25,000 (~$18.5 / ~138 RMB / ~2,750 JPY) to ₩50,000 (~$37 / ~275 RMB / ~5,500 JPY)/person
  • Menu: Tuna toro (fatty tuna) / red meat / Salmon hoe (raw fish) + Ssamchaeso (쌈채소), Udon, Mae-untang (매운탕)
  • Tourist Friendliness: ★★★★★ (Global MZ #1)

Type D — Han-jeongsik (Traditional Korean Set Meal) Hoe (Raw Fish) Course (Traditional)

  • Top Picks: Han-jeongsik (한정식) 6-stage 1st-2nd stage ([#837 Han-jeongsik (Traditional Korean Set Meal) Area Guide (Hoe Course)](837_hanjeongsik_area_guide.md)), traditional luxury
  • Hours: 11:30~22:00
  • Price: Han-jeongsik (한정식) course ₩40,000 (~$30 / ~220 RMB / ~4,400 JPY) to ₩150,000 (~$111 / ~825 RMB / ~16,500 JPY)
  • Menu: Hoe (raw fish) (sea bream, olive flounder) + Course meal
  • Tourist Friendliness: ★★★★

🌶 Chojang (초장) vs. Wasabi, Ssam (쌈), Mae-untang (매운탕) Etiquette

Chojang (초장) (Gochujang (고추장))

  • Composition: Gochujang (고추장 - Korean chili paste) + Vinegar + Sugar
  • Usage: Traditional for live hoe (raw fish), Korean style
  • For Tourists: "Spicy red sauce" – spicy and sweet

Wasabi

  • Usage: For tuna, salmon, aged hoe (raw fish), Japanese style
  • For Tourists: "Japanese horseradish" – use sparingly

Ssam Ssa Meokgi (쌈 싸 먹기 - eating as a wrap) (Traditional for Live Hoe)

1. Place Ssamchaeso (쌈채소 - wrap vegetables) (lettuce, perilla leaf, napa cabbage) on your palm.

2. Add hoe (raw fish) + garlic + Chojang (초장) + Doenjang (된장 - fermented soybean paste) (optional).

3. Wrap it with your hand and eat in one bite.

4. Chew with your mouth closed (Korean etiquette).

Mae-untang (매운탕 - spicy fish stew) as the Final Course

  • Order: Hoe (raw fish) → Mae-untang (매운탕) (last)
  • Ingredients: Leftover fish scraps from hoe preparation + spicy seasoning + tofu, bean sprouts
  • For Tourists: "Spicy fish stew finale" – traditional

🌶 English Phrases for Tourists

  • "활어 광어 1kg" = "1kg of live olive flounder, please"
  • "방어 정통" = "Yellowtail, the winter specialty"
  • "매운탕 코스" = "Spicy fish stew finale, please"
  • "안 맵게" = "Less spicy, please (for Chojang (초장)) "
  • "1/N" = "Split the bill, please"
  • "카드 돼요?" = "Card accepted?"

⚠ 7 Common Mistakes by Tourists

1. "Market 1st floor = dining": Market 1st floor = Hoe-pan (회판 - raw fish counter) / 2nd floor = Sikdang (식당 - eating area) — they are separate.

2. "Live fish = expensive": Market live fish 1kg = ₩20,000 (~$15 / ~110 RMB / ~2,200 JPY) to ₩60,000 (~$44 / ~330 RMB / ~6,600 JPY) (cost-effective #1).

3. "Chojang (초장) = Wasabi": Chojang (초장 - spicy red chili paste sauce) = Gochujang (고추장 - Korean chili paste) (Korean traditional) / Wasabi = Japanese style — different sauces.

4. "Mae-untang (매운탕) = separate meal": Mae-untang (매운탕 - spicy fish stew) = the final course after hoe (raw fish) (hoe → Mae-untang (매운탕) order).

5. "No price negotiation": Market live fish = some price negotiation possible (by kg unit, freshness).

6. "Pregnant women OK": Pregnant women = No hoe (raw fish) (CDC recommendation) — cooked Mae-untang (매운탕) is OK.

7. "All-you-can-eat = live fish": All-you-can-eat = tuna & salmon aged/frozen (not live fish).

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. First Hoe (Raw Fish) = Which Channel: Tuna & Salmon All-You-Can-Eat (MZ, cost-effective) → Market Olive Flounder (traditional).

2. Instagram/Vlog: Noryangjin (노량진) early morning, Jagalchi (자갈치) yellowtail, tuna toro Instagram.

3. MZ Trends: Salmon & tuna all-you-can-eat, MZ hoe (raw fish) restaurants.

4. Soju (소주) Enthusiast: Hoe (raw fish) + Mae-untang (매운탕) + Soju (소주) = traditional.

5. Market Hours: Noryangjin (노량진) = early morning wholesale & general 09:00~22:00.

6. Allergies/Pregnancy: Refer to [#864 6 Types of Hoe (Raw Fish) Overview disclaimer](864_korean_hoe_overview.md) (No hoe for pregnant women).

7. Payment/Tips: Market = cash/card / Hoe restaurant = card OK / No tipping.

8. VAT Refund: General food and beverage are not eligible for VAT refund.

⚠ Medical, Safety, and Dietary Disclaimer

Hoe (raw fish) = uncooked fish; caution for food poisoning. Pregnant women = No hoe (raw fish) (CDC recommendation); Mae-untang (매운탕) = OK. Children = limited (cooked food recommended for ages 5+). Notify staff in advance for allergies (fish, shellfish, soy sauce gluten). In case of suspected food poisoning, call ☎ 1339 (Medical, English). This guide provides general information and is not medical advice.

Dietary Restriction Fallback:

  • Halal/Kosher: All 6 types of hoe (raw fish) are fish = Halal OK / Kosher requires scales + fins = OK.
  • Vegetarian/Vegan: Hoe (raw fish) = non-vegetarian / Vegetarian options = Bibimbap (비빔밥 - mixed rice with vegetables), Sachal Eumsik (사찰음식 - Korean temple food).
  • Gluten-free: Hoe (raw fish) itself OK / Ganjang (간장 - soy sauce), Chojang (초장) = some gluten (Tamari alternative).
