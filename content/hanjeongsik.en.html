🎯 Why a Complete Guide to Korean Hanjeongsik?

Korea's premier authentic cuisine = Hanjeongsik (한정식) (a jeongsik (Korean set meal) featuring 10-20 banchan (free Korean side dishes)). It's the top choice for foreigners to deep dive into Korean food, combining K-drama and royal court (gungjung) experiences. Previously scattered across various guides like [#14 Korean Must-Eats], [#143 Gangnam Korean Food], [#155 Itaewon Food], and [#170 Cheongiwatown], this is now a consolidated full guide.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreigners Make

1. "Hanjeongsik = Expensive": Hanjeongsik prices vary — Gungjung Hanjeongsik (Royal Court Set Meal) (₩50,000~200,000 (~$37-150 / ~250-1000 RMB / ~5,000-20,000 JPY) per person) vs Traditional Hanjeongsik (₩25,000~70,000) vs Modern Hanjeongsik (₩20,000~50,000) vs Baekban·Jeongsik (백반·정식) (Everyday Set Meal) (₩6,000~12,000). For your first try, Baekban·Jeongsik is a great option.

2. "Banchan = All the same": Hanjeongsik banchan consists of 10-20 course dishes (usually 12-16 banchan) + 1-3 main dishes + 1-2 guk (soup)·jjigae (stew) + dessert. Each banchan has its own signature + a top Instagram pick for foreigners.

3. "Hanjeongsik = Korean only": Most Hanjeongsik restaurants offer English menus + photos + card payment is accepted. Restaurants in Insadong (인사동)·Bukchon (북촌)·Gangnam (강남)·Seorae (서래) are considered foreigner-friendly standard.

⚙ 6 FAQs

1. First Hanjeongsik experience: Baekban·Jeongsik (백반·정식) (₩6,000~12,000) → Traditional Hanjeongsik (₩25,000~) → Gungjung Hanjeongsik.

2. Foreigner-friendly: Insadong (인사동)·Bukchon (북촌)·Gangnam (강남)·Seorae (서래)·The Shilla Hotel.

3. Hanbok + Hanjeongsik: Insadong (인사동)·Bukchon (북촌) Hanjeongsik restaurants + hanbok rental combination.

4. K-drama Hanjeongsik: "Sura (수라)" (Joseon royal court cuisine)·"Mr. Sunshine" and others are frequently featured.

5. Vegan·Halal Hanjeongsik: Jingwansa Temple (진관사) sachal eumsik + [#69 Vegan·Halal]·[#202 Follow-up].

6. Recommended Hanjeongsik Course for Foreigners: The Shilla Hotel La Yeon (신라호텔 라연)·Yongsusan (용수산)·Gogung Insadong (고궁 인사동).
