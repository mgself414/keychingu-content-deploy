⚠ 3 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "Hanjeongsik = Expensive": Prices of Hanjeongsik vary — Gungjeongsik (50,000-200,000 won/person) vs. Traditional Hanjeongsik (25,000-70,000 won) vs. Modern Hanjeongsik (20,000-50,000 won) vs. Baekban/Jeongsik (6,000-12,000 won). First challenge = Baekban and formal OK.

2. "Side dishes = all the same": Korean side dishes = 10~20 courses (usually 12~16 side dishes) + 1~3 main dishes + 1~2 soups/stews + dessert. Signature of each side dish + 1st place on foreign Instagram.

3. "Hanjeongsik = Korean only": Most Hanjeongsik stores English menu + photos + card payment OK. Insadong, Bukchon, Gangnam, and Seorae stores have foreigner-friendly standards.
