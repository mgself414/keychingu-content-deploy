Andong Old Market and Hahoe Village 12 selections

1. Andong Old Market (five-day market on the 2nd and 7th)

  • Discrimination: 1976, Andong’s authentic market, Birthplace of Jjimdak Alley, 1980s, Ground mackerel
  • Location: Junggu-dong, Andong / Last 2nd and 7th of every month (every 5 days)
  • Foreigner visit: Visitkorea Selected as “Top 10 K-Tourism Markets”, Open Tourism, Everyone’s Travel
  • kr cross: #380 Market full set·#618 Traditional Market·#655 Pohang sub

2. Andong Jjimdak Alley (inside the old market)

  • Discrimination: Originated in the old market in the 1980s·Chicken + soy sauce + vermicelli + potatoes + cabbage · Sweet and spicy
  • Signature: Andong Old Jjimdak (many representative stores) · Spicy adjustment
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ JP, CN authentic, soy sauce-based familiarity

3. Andong Mackerel

  • Discrimination: Andong authentic preserved food·Salted mackerel·Limited to the inland region·Salty
  • Origin: Preservation of mackerel in the inland city of Andong → 200 years of history
  • Store: Dasu and ground mackerel set meal in the old market

4. Hahoe Village (UNESCO World Heritage Site)

  • Discrimination: 2010 UNESCO registration, 600 years of history, Pungsan Ryu Clan Dongseong Village, Hahoe Mask
  • Attractions: Yangjindang, Chunghyodang, and Hahoe mask dance performances (Saturdays in March, April, September, and October)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ EN·JP·CN authentic 1st place
  • kr cross: #657 Gangjin UNESCO·#600 Cheongjachon

5. Hahoe Mask Dance Performance

  • Discrimination: UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, satire, nobility, gaksi, imae, middle class, halmi
  • Performance: Every Saturday and Sunday from March to December, 14:00 (60 minutes), free
  • Location: Hahoe Village entrance training center

6. Dosan Seowon (UNESCO)

  • Discrimination: 2019 UNESCO “Korean Seowon” registration·Lee Hwang 1561·1000 won banknote
  • Location: 30 minutes by car from Dosan-myeon/Hahoe Village, Andong-si
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ EN·JP authentic enthusiast·1000 won bill proof shot

7. Buseoksa Temple (UNESCO)

  • Discrimination: 2018 UNESCO “Sansa Mountain Monastery of Korea” listed · Founded by Uisangdaesa Temple in 676
  • Muryangsujeon: Oldest wooden building in existence 13th century
  • Location: Yeongju (close to Andong)·40 minutes by car

8. Wolyeong Bridge (night view)

  • Discrimination: Korea Longest wooden bridge, 387m, 2003, Lee Eung-tae’s grave and his wife’s story
  • Night view: LED lighting and water fountain after sunset (April-October)
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★ JP Night View Mania·#658 Yeosu cross

9. Andong Soju (traditional liquor)

  • Discrimination: 45-degree distilled liquor, traditional from the Yuan Dynasty of the Goryeo Dynasty, Andong authentic
  • Experience: Andong Soju Museum (traditional liquor workshop)·tasting·purchase
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★ EN Liquor Mania·#203 Korean Liquor cross

10. Andong Hanji

  • Discrimination: UNESCO registration promotion, 1000-year preservation, royal hanji, paper crafts
  • Experience: Andong Hanji Museum/Experience Center (Hanji making)
  • kr cross: #621 Hanji Tradition

11. Comparison of Andong Jjimdak vs. Sindang-dong Tteokbokki

  • Andong Jjimdak: Soy sauce base, sweet, vermicelli rich
  • Sindangdong Tteokbokki: Gochujang base, spicy, instant tteokbokki
  • Foreigner fit: Comparison of two authentic street foods

12. KTX Andong Station + City Tour

  • Access: Cheongnyangni Station → Andong KTX 1 hour 50 minutes (KTX Jungang Line opened in 2020)
  • City Tour: Old Market - Hahoe Village - Dosanseowon Full Day 8 hours / 50,000 won
  • Foreigner fit: ★★★★★ JP day-trip

FAQ 6

Q1. Is Andong day-trip possible? A. KTX 1 hour 50 minutes, full day OK (day trip 8 hours) / Old Market + Hahoe Village first.

Q2. Hahoe Village entrance fee? A. Adults 5,000 won, shuttle bus free (entrance → village 5 minutes).

Q3. Hahoe Talchum performance time? A. Every Saturday and Sunday from March to December, 14:00, 60 minutes, free. Cancellation possible if it rains.

Q4. Recommended Andong Jjimdak store? A. “Andong Old Jjimdak”, “Jungang Jjimdak”, and “Fresh Jjimdak” in the old market. Price 25,000-35,000 won/2-3 people.

Q5. Andong Soju alcohol content? A. 45% (distilled liquor) · Be careful when tasting · Can be brought into the country after purchase (1L duty free).

Q6. How to get to Dosan Seowon? A. Recommended Andong City Bus 67 and Andong City Tour (bundled with Hahoe Village).

  • Year-round: Five-day market at Old Market on the 2nd and 7th of every month
