26_Convenience Store Snack Guide (For Tourists)

One-Line Intro

Korean convenience stores are a cultural experience — here's what to actually buy.

Korean convenience stores aren't just mini-marts — they're a full cultural experience. Here's a complete guide to what every tourist should try.

Who This Is For

  • Travelers who want to taste Korean food culture on a budget
  • Travelers who need a quick bite late at night

Key Items & Activities

Item Convenience Store Price Range Why We Recommend It Things to Note
Samgak Gimbap (삼각김밥) — triangle rice balls GS25 (지에스25) · CU (씨유) · Emart24 (이마트24) ₩1,200–1,800 A quick, filling meal — comes in tuna, bulgogi (불고기), mentaiko, and more Ask a staff member how to open the wrapper
Cup Noodle (컵라면) All convenience stores ₩1,500–2,500 Authentic Korean ramen flavors, wide range of spice levels Free hot water available at the counter
Banana Milk (바나나맛 우유) All convenience stores ₩1,800–2,000 An iconic Korean drink — sweet, creamy, and instantly recognizable Look for the classic round-jar packaging
Honey Butter Chips (허니버터칩) All convenience stores ₩1,800–2,200 Korea's beloved sweet-and-salty snack May be out of stock occasionally
Instant Tteokbokki (즉석 떡볶이) — spicy rice cakes GS25 (지에스25) · CU (씨유) ₩3,500–4,500 Just 2 minutes in the microwave — tastes like the real thing Quite spicy, no way to adjust heat level
Convenience Store Bento Box (편의점 도시락) All convenience stores ₩5,000–8,000 Variety of rice bowls — bulgogi (불고기), tonkatsu (돈가스), spicy pork (제육볶음) Much tastier when heated
Soju (소주) All convenience stores ₩1,700–2,500 Korea's national spirit — 16–25% alcohol No sales to minors; ID check may apply
Chamisul (참이슬) · Cheoum Cheoreom (처음처럼) All convenience stores ₩1,800–2,500 The two most iconic soju (소주) brands in Korea If drinking at the Han River (한강), buy cups separately
Pokachip (포카칩) · Oh Yes (오예스) · Kobukcheap (꼬북칩) All convenience stores ₩1,500–2,500 Three of Korea's most popular snacks — great as souvenirs too Kobukcheap (꼬북칩) is especially popular

Recommended Route (1 Hour)

1. Enter a convenience store: Find a GS25 (지에스25) or CU (씨유) — within 100m virtually anywhere in Seoul (서울)

2. Samgak Gimbap (삼각김밥) + Banana Milk (바나나맛 우유) (₩3,500–4,000): A simple, satisfying breakfast

3. Cup Noodle (컵라면) + Instant Tteokbokki (즉석 떡볶이) (₩5,000–7,000): Eat at the in-store table

4. Snack & drink shopping (₩5,000–10,000): Pick up Honey Butter Chips (허니버터칩) and Kobukcheap (꼬북칩) as souvenirs

5. Soju (소주) & beer (₩3,000–4,000): Grab these for a Han River (한강) picnic

Tips for Foreign Tourists

  • Useful Korean Phrases
  • "Ttatteutage haejuseyo (따뜻하게 해주세요)" — "Please heat this up" — for bento boxes and hot dogs
  • "Ttegeoun mul juseyo (뜨거운 물 주세요)" — "Hot water, please" — for cup noodles
  • "Bongtu juseyo (봉투 주세요)" — "A bag, please" — plastic bags are paid (₩50–100)
  • Cultural Note: Eating at the in-store table is totally fine. Please dispose of your trash in the convenience store bin.
  • Payment: Both card and cash accepted. You can also pay with a T-money (티머니) transit card.
  • Brand Colors to Look For: GS25 (지에스25) = green, CU (씨유) = purple, Emart24 (이마트24) = yellow, 7-Eleven (세븐일레븐) = red

Simple English Summary

Korean convenience stores — GS25 (지에스25), CU (씨유), and Emart24 (이마트24) — are open 24/7 and sell far more than snacks: hot meals, alcohol, and even K-beauty products. Must-buy items include Banana Milk (바나나맛 우유) (₩1,800), Honey Butter Chips (허니버터칩) (₩2,000), and Samgak Gimbap (삼각김밥) triangle rice balls (₩1,500). You can heat food in the microwave at no extra charge. Staff can assist in most cases, and card payment is universally accepted. Many travelers report convenience stores as a highlight of their Seoul (서울) trip.

Estimated Total Cost

  • Budget: ₩5,000 (2 triangle rice balls + 1 drink)
  • Standard: ₩15,000 (1 full meal + snacks + drink)
  • Splurge: ₩30,000 (variety of items + soju (소주) & beer)
