Why BTS and NewJeans MV Filming Locations

Visiting MV filming locations is a core ritual of K-pop fan travel. Because BTS and NewJeans music videos use real Korean locations as backdrops, fans can recreate scenes they've watched on screen in person. The snowy fields around Jeongdongjin Station (정동진역), the courtyard of Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁), the alleys of Hongdae (홍대), Incheon International Airport — all of these are public places. Free-to-low admission, easy access by public transit, and ideal conditions for recreating Instagram shots make these spots stand out.

3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

① Visiting Gyeongbokgung on a weekday afternoon — For a BLACKPINK "How You Like That" recreation shot, the palace courtyard is relatively less crowded between 9:00 and 10:00 in the morning. Weekend daytime gets packed with group tours, making it harder to frame the MV angle.

② Attempting Jeongdongjin as a same-day trip from Seoul — A direct KTX to Gangneung (about 2 hours 10 minutes) makes a same-day trip possible, but round-trip travel time leaves only 2-3 hours on the ground. Catching the first KTX out of Seoul (departing 06:00-07:00) is necessary for a comfortable visit.

③ Trying to enter the HYBE headquarters building — The HYBE headquarters in Yongsan is an exterior-only fan destination. The building's interior is not open to the general public. HYBE Insight (the official exhibition space) requires advance reservation and charges separate admission.

A Deep Dive into Real Filming Locations

1. Jeongdongjin Station and Beach — BTS "Spring Day"

Background: The Jeongdongjin Station area in Gangneung is known among fans as the backdrop for the train and snowfield scenes in BTS's "Spring Day" (2017) music video. Fans refer to it as the "Spring Day pilgrimage site."

  • Location: 17 Jeongdongjin-ro, Gangneung, Gangwon State (at Jeongdongjin Station)
  • Access: From Cheongnyangni Station in Seoul or from Gangneung Station, transfer to a Mugunghwa or Nuriro train to Jeongdongjin Station. About 35 minutes from Gangneung Station. The KTX runs direct to Gangneung Station (about 2 hours 10 minutes from Seoul), then transfer to a Mugunghwa train or bus to Jeongdongjin.
  • Admission: Entering the Jeongdongjin Station platform requires a train ticket (if riding the train). The beach is free and open to the public.
  • Foreigner fit: Excellent for ARMY enthusiasts. The angle from the end of the platform, with the sea in the background, is a well-known Instagram photo spot shared throughout the ARMY community.
  • Best timing: Right after sunrise (6-7 a.m.) or at dusk. Visiting in winter (December-February) when it snows recreates the "Spring Day" snowfield mood. Spring and summer pair the beach with a sunrise viewpoint instead.
  • Etiquette: Stay behind the platform safety line. Do not approach moving trains. Be mindful of sharing the space with regular travelers.

2. Incheon International Airport — BTS "DNA" and "FAKE LOVE"

Background: Fan communities have identified parts of Incheon International Airport, inside and out, as locations used in BTS's "DNA" (2017) and "FAKE LOVE" (2018) music videos.

  • Location: Terminal 1 and Terminal 2, Incheon International Airport (272 Gonghang-ro, Jung-gu, Incheon)
  • Access: Direct connection via Seoul Subway Line 9 or the Airport Railroad (about 43 minutes from Seoul Station). No transfer needed from Gimpo.
  • Admission: Free (the departure area beyond security requires a boarding pass). The 1st- and 2nd-floor departure and arrival halls are open without a boarding pass.
  • Foreigner fit: Since the MV locations overlap with the arrival and departure route, no extra trip is needed — visit right after landing or before your flight home for a recreation shot.
  • Best timing: Early morning or late night, when it's quieter and the space feels more open. Daytime gets crowded with travelers, making it harder to frame a shot.
  • Etiquette: No photography near security checkpoints or immigration counters. Follow airport regulations and stay out of other travelers' way.

3. Daegwallyeong and Pyeongchang Snowfields — BTS "Spring Day" Snow Scenes

Background: The highlands along the Pyeongchang-Gangneung border share a mood with the snowfield scenes in BTS's "Spring Day" video. The Daegwallyeong (대관령) area is referenced among fans as a "Spring Day-feel location" rather than a single confirmed filming spot — it's more of a mood-recreation destination.

  • Location: Daegwallyeong-myeon, Pyeongchang, Gangwon State (Daegwallyeong Wind Farm, Samyang Ranch, and similar sites)
  • Access: From Gangneung KTX Station, continue by rental car or bus toward Jeongdongjin/Daegwallyeong. Samyang Ranch runs a shuttle bus.
  • Admission: The exterior of the Daegwallyeong Wind Farm is free. Samyang Ranch costs KRW 14,000 for adults (2026 rate).
  • Foreigner fit: The winter snow and wide-open pastures echo the MV's mood well. Rather than an exact scene recreation, this is more about experiencing the "Spring Day" feeling.
  • Best timing: Within two days of snowfall, December-February. Spring (April-May) offers different appeal with green pastures.
  • Etiquette: Follow posted guidelines when approaching ranch animals. Do not enter the fenced areas around the wind turbines.

4 & 5. Bupyeong, Songdo, and Hongdae — NewJeans "Ditto"

Background: NewJeans's "Ditto" (2022) music video is set against school hallways, bus stops, and everyday street scenes, and fans have identified outdoor locations around Bupyeong (부평) and Songdo (송도) in Incheon, as well as near Hongdae in Seoul.

Bupyeong and Songdo (Incheon)

  • Location: Bupyeong-gu, Incheon / Songdo International City, Yeonsu-gu
  • Access: Seoul Subway Line 1 to Bupyeong Station (about 1 hour). Songdo is accessible via Central Park Station on Incheon Subway Line 1.
  • Admission: Free (street locations)
  • Recommended: Visit during the day. Be mindful of student foot traffic during weekday school commute hours.

Hongdae (Seoul)

  • Location: Wausan-ro and Eoulmadang-ro area, Mapo-gu, Seoul
  • Access: Hongik University Station, Subway Line 2 or the Airport Railroad, Exit 9, 5-minute walk.
  • Admission: Free
  • Foreigner fit: Hongdae is itself a K-pop and indie-music pilgrimage site. Beyond the "Ditto" location, the density of busking, cafes, and boutique shops makes it a top spot for fan routines.
  • Best timing: Weekend afternoon into evening (busking is lively). Weekday daytime is quieter, giving more room to shoot.
  • Etiquette: Don't block the space in front of a busking performance. Avoid occupying space in front of cafes and shops for too long.

6. Jongno Area — NewJeans "Super Shy"

Background: NewJeans's "Super Shy" (2023) music video was filmed against downtown Seoul streets, and fans have identified locations around the alleys and cafes of Jongno.

  • Location: Jongno (종로), Insa-dong (인사동), and Ikseon-dong (익선동) area, Jongno-gu, Seoul
  • Access: Jongno 3(sam)-ga Station, Subway Lines 1, 3, or 5, or Anguk Station, Line 3, both within walking distance.
  • Admission: Free (streets)
  • Foreigner fit: Combine with Ikseon-dong's hanok alleys and the Insa-dong alleyways for maximum K-culture density — a "Super Shy" mood recreation alongside hanok cafes and traditional streets.
  • Best timing: 9-11 a.m. (quiet alleys), or 7-9 p.m. (atmospheric lighting).
  • Etiquette: Avoid filming near the residential parts of Ikseon-dong late at night or early in the morning. Don't occupy outdoor space for long periods without patronizing a cafe.

7. Gyeongbokgung Palace — BLACKPINK "How You Like That"

Background: One of the signature scenes in BLACKPINK's "How You Like That" (2020) music video shows the members in hanbok against the backdrop of Gyeongbokgung's Geunjeongjeon Hall and Gyeonghoeru Pavilion. It's the top pilgrimage spot for foreign BLINKs.

  • Location: 161 Sajik-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul (Gyeongbokgung Palace)
  • Access: Gyeongbokgung Station, Subway Line 3, Exit 5, 5-minute walk.
  • Admission: KRW 3,000 for adults (same rate for foreign visitors). Closed Tuesdays.
  • Foreigner fit: Combining an MV recreation shot with hanbok rental (about a dozen shops around Gyeongbokgung, from KRW 15,000 for 1-2 hours) is the golden routine for BLINK enthusiasts. Admission is free while wearing hanbok.
  • Best timing: 9-10 a.m. right after opening (fewer tourists). April-May for blossoming trees, October-November for foliage.
  • Etiquette: Do not climb on or lean against cultural heritage structures. During special night openings, stay within the designated areas.

8 & 9. Jongno and Insa-dong / Anseong Farmland — BLACKPINK "Pink Venom"

Background: BLACKPINK's "Pink Venom" (2022) music video mixes traditional and modern settings. Fan communities point to the traditional alleys of Jongno and Insa-dong, along with the outdoor space at Anseong Farmland (안성 팜랜드).

Jongno and Insa-dong

  • Access: Anguk Station, Subway Line 3, or Jongno 3(sam)-ga Station, Line 1.
  • Admission: Free
  • Highlight: The Insa-dong Ssamziegil alley and the route toward Bukchon Hanok Village — traditional alleys that echo the MV's mood.

Anseong Farmland

  • Location: 28 Daesindu-gil, Gongdo-eup, Anseong, Gyeonggi Province
  • Access: A direct bus from Gangnam Express Bus Terminal in Seoul (about 1 hour), or a taxi (about 30 minutes) from Seojeongni Station on the Seoul Metropolitan Subway Line 1.
  • Admission: KRW 12,000 for adults (2026 rate). Rates vary during seasonal events.
  • Foreigner fit: Less about an exact MV recreation and more about wide-open pastures and hands-on farm experiences — a good fit for families as well as K-pop fans. A same-day trip from Seoul is feasible.
  • Best timing: Spring (April-May) for tulips and canola flowers, fall (October) for pink muhly grass.
  • Etiquette: Follow the posted rules in the animal-experience areas.

10. HYBE Headquarters — A Fan Pilgrimage Point

Background: The HYBE headquarters in Yongsan-gu, Seoul, is the agency building for BTS and NewJeans. It's not an official MV filming location, but it's an essential pilgrimage stop for ARMY and NewJeans fans.

  • Location: Hangang-daero 42-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul (near Seoul Station and Yongsan Station)
  • Access: Seoul Station, Subway Lines 1 or 4, Exit 1, 15-minute walk, or Yongsan Station, Line 1 or the Gyeongui Line, 10-minute walk.
  • Admission: Free to visit the exterior. HYBE Insight (the indoor exhibition) requires a separate reservation and paid admission.
  • Foreigner fit: Taking a photo in front of the exterior wall and visiting the nearby fan-art wall are part of the fan routine. HYBE Insight offers complete English guidance; advance reservation is recommended.
  • Best timing: Weekday mornings (fewer exterior visitors). Weekends see heavier fan traffic.
  • Etiquette: The building interior and parking area are off-limits. Be considerate of privacy at nearby residential and commercial buildings.

FAQ

Q1. Where exactly was BTS's "Spring Day" filmed?

The main locations identified by fan communities are Jeongdongjin Station and beach in Gangneung, and the Daegwallyeong snowfield area. Not every scene in the video comes from a single location — it's known to be a composite of multiple locations. "Jeongdongjin" is the most frequently cited destination.

Q2. Do I have to wear hanbok to visit Gyeongbokgung?

It's not required. But wearing hanbok gets you free admission to Gyeongbokgung, and it makes for a higher-quality recreation of the BLACKPINK "How You Like That" scene. Numerous rental shops cluster near the palace's east and north gates (from KRW 15,000 for 1-2 hours).

Q3. Can I go inside the HYBE headquarters?

General visitors can only access the exterior. The official exhibition, HYBE Insight, requires advance reservation (book through the English site at insight.hybecorp.com). Admission is charged separately.

Q4. How many MV filming locations can I cover in one day in Seoul?

For a downtown-focused route, Gyeongbokgung (morning) → Jongno and Insa-dong (lunch) → Hongdae (afternoon) → HYBE exterior (evening) makes for a comfortable 4-stop day. Adding Incheon (Bupyeong and Songdo) brings the total time, including travel, to about 8-9 hours.

Q5. Is Jeongdongjin worth visiting regardless of season?

Yes. For the "Spring Day" snowfield mood, a December-February winter visit works best. Spring through summer offers a different appeal, pairing the sunrise with the beach. Fall is less crowded. That said, Gangwon Province winters commonly drop below minus 10 degrees Celsius, so pack warm.

Pain Points for Foreign Visitors

  • Language: Gyeongbokgung, Hongdae, and HYBE Insight offer full English, Japanese, and Chinese support. Use Papago or Google Translate alongside at Jeongdongjin and Anseong.
  • Payment: Cards work at Gyeongbokgung, tour operators, and HYBE Insight. Some small restaurants near Jeongdongjin prefer cash.
  • VAT refund: Look for the Tax Refund sign at stores for purchases above KRW 50,000, then claim at an airport refund counter.
