46 · Korea SIM · eSIM · Roaming Complete Guide — Get Your Data Online in 30 Minutes

> Master List ID: 082 (H-Practical) · Priority: P1 · Targets: EN● · JP● · CN●

> Sources: Incheon Airport (인천국제공항) carrier-booth operations data (2026-04 reference), public price sheets from major global eSIM platforms (Airalo · Klook · KKday · Holafly), and the three Korean carriers (KT · SKT · LG U+) short-stay foreigner plans — all verified as of 2026-04-27.

> Fact basis date: 2026-04-27

One-line Summary

For mobile data in Korea, the value order is eSIM > physical SIM > pocket Wi-Fi > home-carrier roaming. The standard play is to get online within 30 minutes of landing at Incheon Airport (인천국제공항), and a fair price for 5G unlimited × 7 days lands between ₩15,000–25,000. This guide answers the three questions every foreigner asks — which option, where, how much — in a single pass.

Who This Is For

  • iPhone XS+ or Galaxy S20+ owners — eSIM first. Activate before takeoff so data flips on the moment you land.
  • Solo travelers, 7 days or fewer — one SIM or eSIM and you're done.
  • Groups of 2 or more — one shared pocket Wi-Fi is the cheapest per head.
  • Multi-country trips (Korea · Japan · SE Asia) — a single regional eSIM (Airalo Asialink) keeps logistics minimal.
  • Business travelers · long stays (15+ days) — Korean local carrier short-stay plans (KT olleh) win on per-GB price.
  • Families with children — pocket Wi-Fi for the kids + parent home-carrier roaming as backup is the safest combo.

2-2. eSIM Activation in 3 Steps

1. 1–2 days before departure — Pay on the platform → receive QR by email or in-app.

2. Before takeoff or in-flight (Wi-Fi connected) — Phone Settings > Cellular > Add eSIM > scan QR.

3. After landing at Incheon Airport (인천국제공항) — Switch off airplane mode and KT or LG U+ 5G picks up automatically. Don't forget to set the eSIM as your data line.

Watch out: If you still need to receive calls or SMS on your home number, keep data on the eSIM and leave the home SIM as voice/SMS only.

3-3. SIM Swap in 5 Steps (about 5 minutes)

1. Power phone off.

2. Open the SIM tray with a paper-clip-style ejector (front desks at most hotels can lend you one).

3. Store your home SIM in the small case the booth provides — losing it costs more than the trip's data ever did.

4. Insert the Korean SIM and power back on.

5. APN auto-configures — Korean SIMs are recognized by IMSI. If not, ask the booth on the spot.

Watch out: Replacement of a lost home SIM through your home carrier almost always costs more than this Korea SIM. Keep the home SIM and ejector pin together in one zip-lock bag, deep inside your carry-on.

4-2. The Catch — Pocket Wi-Fi Trade-offs

  • Extra device + charger to carry — eats carry-on space.
  • Battery dies fast — bring a power bank for any day longer than 8 hours out.
  • Mandatory return — drop at the counter on departure (lost device deducts ₩100,000+ from the deposit).
  • When the group splits up — one side loses data and KakaoTalk location-sharing breaks.

→ Not recommended for solo travelers. Use it only when you're with at least one other person.

5-2. When Roaming Actually Wins

  • Business trips of 3 days or fewer — eSIM/SIM setup time isn't worth it.
  • Heavy SMS-OTP usage on your home number — banks, brokerages, anything that needs the home number every 5 minutes.
  • Phone doesn't support eSIM or has a locked SIM tray — older or carrier-locked devices.

For the other 99% of foreign tourists, eSIM or physical SIM is the right answer.

7. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. Can I really be online within 30 minutes of landing?

A. With eSIM, yes. Add the eSIM and scan the QR before takeoff — once you switch off airplane mode at Incheon, it auto-connects. Physical SIMs add 5–15 minutes of queue at the booth.

Q2. Can I sign up for Korean apps (KakaoTalk · Naver · Coupang) without a Korean number?

A. KakaoTalk works with your home number. Naver and Coupang often require a Korean number — short-stay tourists usually don't need them.

Q3. Can I get a Korean phone number on an eSIM?

A. The KT olleh short-stay foreigner eSIM plan (7 days, ₩27,500) does include a Korean number. Generic Airalo / Klook eSIMs are data-only with no number.

Q4. I need to receive voice calls on my home number. How?

A. Use dual-SIM mode. Korea data on the eSIM, home voice/SMS on the physical SIM. Both stay active simultaneously on iPhone and Galaxy.

Q5. The phone shows 5G but speed feels like LTE.

A. KT and LG U+ 5G is solid downtown — Gangnam (강남) · Hongdae (홍대) · Itaewon (이태원). In some rural areas or on the subway it falls back to LTE. That's expected behavior, not a fault.

Q6. Is "unlimited" really unlimited?

A. Nominally yes, but most plans throttle (QoS) once you cross 5–10 GB per day. For all-day video streaming, the KT olleh short-stay plan (7 days, ₩27,500) is genuinely uncapped.

Q7. Will Twitter · Google · WhatsApp work in Korea?

A. Yes — Korea has no internet censorship and all global services run normally. Some Korean broadcasters block foreign IPs, but with a Korean SIM you have a Korean IP, so this isn't an issue.

Q8. I added the wrong eSIM. Refund possible?

A. Airalo and Klook refund eSIMs that haven't been activated yet (in-app chat). Once activated, no refund. Defer activation until after you land in Korea to keep the option open.
