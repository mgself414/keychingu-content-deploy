⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "KTX = regular seats only": KTX 3 types — (1) Economy (2) Premium (3) Premium (3) Plus. Foreigner Economy Class = Best value for money for foreigners. Special room = foreign enthusiast + 30%+ expensive.

2. "KTX reservation = Same-day OK": No. 1 KTX foreign enthusiasts = Advance reservation required (No. 1 in Korea sold out on holidays and weekends). Korea Railroad (KORAIL Talk·KORAIL English) + Klook·Trip.com English OK.

3. "Express bus = low price only": Express bus premium + premium + regular 3 types. Excellent (KTX 70% price) + 1st place among foreign enthusiasts. Distributed across 3 terminals: East Seoul, Seoul Express, and Seoul South.

4. "Domestic flights = expensive": Korean domestic flights (Jeju Air, T'way, Eastar, Jin Air, Aero K LCC) = Gimpo → Jeju 30,000~80,000 won (Cheaper than KTX Busan 70,000 won for foreigners). Jeju·Yeosu No. 1 LCC.

5. "Outskirt ferry = foreigners go crazy": Korean ferry (Jeju, Wando, Hongdo, Ulleung) foreigner-friendly OK + Incheon, Mokpo, Yeosu, Pohang ferry terminal English information + Korea's No. 1 outskirts enthusiast.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. KTX foreigner reservation: KORAIL English·KORAIL Talk + Klook·Trip.com English OK + Advance reservation required on holidays and weekends.

2. KTX Foreigner Pass (Korail Pass): 3-day, 5-day, 7-day, and 10-day pass for foreigners only. No. 1 foreign KTX enthusiast + foreign passport required.

3. Express bus vs KTX: KTX fast (Seoul → Busan 2.5 hours, 60,000 won) / Premium express bus (4.5 hours, 35,000 won) + No. 1 among foreign enthusiasts.

4. Domestic LCC: Gimpo → Jeju 30,000~80,000 won + Jeju, Yeosu, Ulsan, Busan LCC enthusiasts OK.

5. Foreigners renting a car: International Driving Permit (IDP) + foreign passport + home country license required. Licenses over 21 years of age + less than 1 year are not permitted.

6. Outer town ferry: Incheon, Mokpo, Yeosu, and Pohang ferry terminal information in English + Foreign enthusiasts OK every week + Some outlying ferries welcome foreigners.

7. Airport Limousine: Incheon Airport/Seoul city hotel 18,000 won/1 hour and 30 minutes + No. 1 for foreign enthusiasts.

8. Recommended for 2 days and 1 night outside the outskirts: KTX Busan (#243~#247 series)·KTX Gangneung (#231 ski)·KTX Gwangju·Yeosu.
