Seoul Running Course Guide — Top 8 Picks for Foreign Visitors

> Subtitle: Han River Views + Downtown & Palaces + Parks & Nature + Unique Niche — 8 Seoul Running Courses Across 4 Axes (Series #1, General)

ISSUE 03 · #146 · 2026-05-12

🎯 Why a Seoul Running Guide?

Seoul is a globally recognized running destination with 150km of Han River paths + downtown, palace, park, and mountain routes packed into one city. On Reddit r/koreatravel, "Seoul running" and "Han river run" appear constantly in foreign visitor threads, and countless international vloggers have produced "Running in Seoul" videos on YouTube. This is KeyChinGu's first entry into the Activity & Sports category — lifestyle guide series #1, beyond food and area guides.

A night running deep dive is covered separately in #147 Seoul Night Running Course Guide.

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes Foreign Visitors Make

1. Knowing only the Han River and missing downtown, parks, and Nodeul Island

Most foreigners think "Seoul running = Han River." In reality, routes span downtown (Jongno (종로) · Cheonggyecheon (청계천)), parks (Seoul Forest (서울숲) · Olympic Park (올림픽공원)), Nodeul Island (노들섬) (island in the Han River), and Bugak Skyway (북악스카이웨이) — five distinct axes with very different vibes. Group them as an A-course bundle.

2. Running between 11:00–16:00 during summer

Seoul summers (July–August) bring intense heat waves, and Han River paths have very little shade. Early morning 05:00–07:00 (sunrise) or evening 19:00–21:00 (after sunset) is strongly recommended. Use the shaded zones on Jamsu Bridge (잠수교).

3. Not knowing about Runner's Station and running shop free facilities

The Runner's Station at Yeouinaru Station (여의나루역) offers free lockers, changing rooms, and fitness assessments. No need to lug gear from your hotel. Foreign visitors can also register with a passport.

Seoul Running Landscape

Seoul's running infrastructure:

  • 150km of Han River paths — 11 Han River parks connected by cycling and running tracks
  • Downtown 5km — Palace and historic views at Gwanghwamun (광화문), Cheonggyecheon (청계천), and Jongmyo (종묘)
  • Park routes 5–10km — Seoul Forest (서울숲), Olympic Park (올림픽공원), Ttukseom (뚝섬), Banpo (반포) — dirt and flower-lined trails
  • Hill challenges — Namsan (남산), Bugak (북악), Inwangsan (인왕산) and other urban mountains, 6.6–10km
  • Running culture — Running crews (Han River running crews, Banpo running clubs, and more), beginner-friendly groups (called reonini — Korean running newbies), Han River night running (covered separately in #147)

4 types of foreign visitor intent:

1. Han River views + sunrise/sunset routes (Banpo, Yeouido)

2. Downtown & palace sightseeing combined (Jongno, Namsan)

3. Park & nature relaxation (Seoul Forest, Olympic Park)

4. Unique challenge courses (Nodeul Island, Bugak Skyway)

① Han River Views (2 Courses)

1. Banpo Hangang Park (반포 한강공원) — Jamsu Bridge Course

Seoul's #1 running pick — Han River scenery & Moonlight Rainbow Fountain. Start at the south end of Banpo Bridge (반포대교) and follow Jamsu Bridge (잠수교) for an 8.2km round trip across the Han River. The Moonlight Rainbow Fountain runs nightly from April to October (Seoul's top Han River night-view attraction). A staple in foreign vlogger videos.

  • Start point: Seoul Metro Line 9 Sinbanpo Station (신반포역) Exit 1 → 5-min walk to south end of Banpo Bridge (반포대교)
  • Distance: 8.2km round trip (Jamsu Bridge course)
  • Scenery: Jamsu Bridge (잠수교) — a unique low-water crossing where the Han River flows over the road — plus Moonlight Rainbow Fountain (19:30–21:30, hours vary by season)
  • Signature: Han River night view + fountain + Instagram photos
  • Recommended time: 18:00–21:00 (evening fountain hours) or 05:00–07:00 (sunrise)
  • Why: The #1 first-run course for foreign visitors in Seoul. Scenery, infrastructure, and accessibility all top-tier.

2. Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도 한강공원) — Runner's Station + Yunjungno

Free Runner's Station facilities + spring cherry blossom route along Yunjungno (윤중로), 4.5km. The Runner's Station is a 3-minute walk from Yeouinaru Station (여의나루역) Exit 4 — free lockers, changing rooms, and fitness assessments. Register with your passport. Cherry blossoms along Yunjungno peak in early-to-mid April.

  • Start point: Metro Line 5 Yeouinaru Station (여의나루역) Exit 4 → register at Runner's Station, then begin
  • Distance: 4.5km Yunjungno (윤중로) / 10km full Han River / 6.5km Han River + Saetgang (샛강) mix
  • Signature: Runner's Station + spring cherry blossoms + Han River night view
  • Recommended time: Spring (March–April) cherry blossom season · Autumn (September–November) · Evenings
  • Why: Best foreign visitor-friendliness (English signage + free facilities). Zero hotel gear hassle.

② Downtown & Palaces (2 Courses)

3. Jongno (종로) · Cheonggyecheon (청계천) Course

5.1km loop starting at Gwanghwamun Plaza (광화문광장) — the essence of Korean palace and historical scenery. Route: Gwanghwamun (광화문) → Jongmyo (종묘) → Yulgok Tunnel (율곡터널) → Cheonggyecheon Stream (청계천) → back to Gwanghwamun Plaza. The best course for combining first-time Seoul sightseeing with a run. Morning light is ideal — recommended 07:00–09:00.

  • Start point: Metro Line 5 Gwanghwamun Station (광화문역) or Line 1 Jonggak Station (종각역)
  • Distance: 5.1km loop
  • Signature: Gwanghwamun Plaza (광화문 광장) · Jongmyo (종묘) (UNESCO World Heritage) · Cheonggyecheon (청계천) streamside path
  • Recommended time: 07:00–09:00 (fewer tourists)
  • Why: Running + sightseeing combined. The quintessential Seoul experience. Best first course for foreign visitors.

4. Namsan (남산) Loop Trail — North Circular Road

6.6km round trip on the Namsan Loop Trail (남산 둘레길) — N Seoul Tower and 360° panorama. The North Circular Road (북측순환로) is 3.3km one-way with gentle inclines and descents. The summit offers a full 360° panorama of Seoul. Instagram favorite for foreign visitors. Peaks during spring azalea season and autumn foliage.

  • Start point: Metro Line 4 Myeongdong Station (명동역) or Chungmuro Station (충무로역), 10-min walk to North Circular Road entrance
  • Distance: 3.3km one way × round trip = 6.6km
  • Difficulty: Intermediate (gentle hills)
  • Signature: N Seoul Tower (남산타워) + Seoul panorama + spring azaleas
  • Recommended time: Sunrise 05:00–07:00 or sunset 18:00–20:00
  • Why: Urban mountain + 360° view. Pairs perfectly with Myeongdong (명동) sightseeing.

③ Parks & Nature (2 Courses)

5. Seoul Forest (서울숲) + Ttukseom Hangang Park (뚝섬한강공원) Connection

3km inside Seoul Forest (서울숲) + 3.5km one way to Ttukseom Hangang Park = Han River and forest at once. The park is car-free, making it safe and pleasant. Great for foreign families and couples. Best during spring cherry blossoms and autumn foliage.

  • Start point: Bundang Line Seoul Forest Station (서울숲역) Exit 4 → enter Seoul Forest (서울숲)
  • Distance: 3km inside the park / 6.5km with Ttukseom (뚝섬) connection
  • Signature: Han River + forest simultaneously · Ttukseom Hangang Park (뚝섬한강공원) · deer enclosure
  • Recommended time: 09:00–11:00 or sunset 17:00–19:00
  • Why: Car-free park + best urban nature in Seoul. Ideal for families and couples.

6. Olympic Park (올림픽공원)

1988 Olympic heritage + a clean 5km scenic loop. Features the Olympic Sculpture Park (올림픽 조각공원), flower-lined paths, and some dirt trails (joint-friendly). Recommended for foreign travelers interested in Korean history and art. Peaks during autumn foliage and spring flowers.

  • Start point: Metro Line 8 Olympic Park Station (올림픽공원역) Exit 3
  • Distance: 5km park loop
  • Signature: Olympic Sculpture Park (올림픽 조각공원) + Peace Gate (88 평화의 문) + dirt trails
  • Recommended time: Morning (fewer tourists) or sunset
  • Why: The most well-kept scenery in the city. Pairs well with Gangnam (강남) and Jamsil (잠실) sightseeing.

④ Unique Niche (2 Courses)

7. Nodeul Island (노들섬)

A small island in the middle of the Han River — 1.5km perimeter, 2–3 lap course (3–4.5km). Access via Metro Line 9 Nodeul Island Station (노들섬역) or on foot across Han River Bridge (한강대교). Offers a full 360° city view from within the Han River itself — a niche Instagram gem for foreign visitors.

  • Start point: Line 9 Nodeul Island Station (노들섬역)
  • Distance: 1.5km × 2–3 laps = 3–4.5km
  • Signature: Island in the Han River · city skyline view · Instagram spots
  • Recommended time: Sunset 18:00–20:00 (best for photos)
  • Why: Short course + scenery + photos. Best for beginners and Instagram-focused travelers.

8. Bugak Skyway (북악스카이웨이)

Urban nature escape and hill challenge, 7km. Starting at the Jahamun Tunnel (자하문터널) entrance, the route follows the base of Bugak Mountain (북악산) toward Pyeongchangdong (평창동) and Jeongneung (정릉). A niche course for runners who love urban nature and elevation. Autumn foliage here is spectacular.

  • Start point: Jahamun Tunnel (자하문터널) entrance (20-min walk from Line 3 Gyeongbokgung Station (경복궁역), or take a bus)
  • Distance: 7km (hilly)
  • Difficulty: Advanced (hill challenge)
  • Signature: Bugak Mountain (북악산) · elevated city view · natural surroundings
  • Recommended time: 07:00–09:00 or sunset
  • Why: For running enthusiasts who want a challenge. Best during autumn foliage season.

⚙ FAQ — 8 Questions

Q1. Do I need to carry gear from my hotel to the Han River?

Yeouinaru Station (여의나루역) Runner's Station = free lockers, changing rooms, and showers. Foreign visitors can register with a passport. Zero hotel gear stress. Note: Banpo (반포) and Ttukseom (뚝섬) have limited locker facilities.

Q2. Can I rent running shoes or gear?

Rental facilities are largely unavailable — bring your own. For purchases, Nike and Adidas flagship stores in Myeongdong (명동) and Itaewon (이태원) carry running shoes (₩100,000~, ~$73 / ~530 RMB / ~10,700 JPY). Best value Korean running gear: Decathlon (데카트론) in Gangnam (강남) and Jamsil (잠실).

Q3. Can foreign visitors join a running crew?

Seoul Hash House Harriers (international weekly running club, every Saturday) and Han River Running Crew (Instagram @han_river_runners and similar accounts). English-friendly. High foreign visitor welcoming.

Q4. What's the best time of day?

  • Spring & Autumn: 08:00–10:00 or sunset 17:00–19:00
  • Summer (June–August): Early morning 05:00–07:00 (sunrise) or evening 20:00–22:00. Avoid direct sun 11:00–16:00.
  • Winter (December–February): Midday 12:00–14:00 (warmest). Early morning and night can be dangerously cold (below freezing).

Q5. Is it safe?

Running safety along Seoul's Han River and downtown areas is excellent (extensive CCTV coverage). Night running on the Han River is generally fine, but Nodeul Island (노들섬) and Bugak Skyway (북악스카이웨이) have limited lighting at night — daytime recommended for those routes.

Q6. Where can I find water and restrooms?

Hangang parks, Seoul Forest (서울숲), and Olympic Park (올림픽공원) all have free water dispensers and restrooms every 200–500m. For Jongno (종로), Namsan (남산), and Bugak (북악), use convenience stores (pyeonuijeom — Korea's 24/7 convenience stores, ubiquitous everywhere). Carrying a water bottle is still recommended.

Q7. What if it rains?

Alternatives:

  • Your hotel fitness center
  • K-Athletics Jamsil (잠실) (some sections of the indoor track are free)
  • Hotel fitness facilities
  • Cheonggyecheon (청계천) — portions under bridges provide partial rain cover

Q8. What's the classic Korean post-run food and café routine?

  • Han River: instant noodles + makgeolli (막걸리 — lightly sparkling Korean rice wine) from kiosks inside the Hangang parks
  • Downtown: gimbap (김밥 — Korean seaweed rice rolls) or haejangguk (해장국 — Korean hangover/restorative soup) around Gwanghwamun (광화문) and Jongno (종로)
  • Parks: cafés and brunch spots (Seoul Forest (서울숲) hanok cafés, Apgujeong (압구정), Jamsil ARC·N·BOOK, etc.)
  • See also: #142 Gangnam Café Guide / #145 Seongsu Café Guide / #141 Jongno Guide

🗺 3 Recommended Full-Day Running + Sightseeing Itineraries

Course A — "Han River Deep Dive" (Running enthusiasts, 8 hours)

05:30 → Banpo Hangang Park (반포 한강공원) 8.2km Jamsu Bridge run (sunrise views, ~1.5 hrs) → Banpo café breakfast → Line 9 → Yeouido Hangang Park (여의도 한강공원) Runner's Station shower (30 min) → Yunjungno (윤중로) additional 4.5km run (peak during cherry blossom season) → Yeouido (여의도) lunch → afternoon rest

Course B — "Downtown History + Running" (Sightseeing combined, 6 hours)

07:00 → Jongno (종로) · Cheonggyecheon (청계천) 5.1km run (Gwanghwamun (광화문) · Jongmyo (종묘) · Cheonggyecheon (청계천)) → Gwanghwamun café breakfast → Gyeongbokgung Palace (경복궁) sightseeing (2 hrs) → lunch → Namsan Loop Trail (남산 둘레길) 6.6km run (16:00–18:00, sunset views) → Myeongdong (명동) dinner

Course C — "Full Park & Nature Set" (Foreign families, 7 hours)

09:00 → Seoul Forest (서울숲) 3km + Ttukseom (뚝섬) connection 3.5km run (Han River + forest) → Seoul Forest café breakfast → Line 8 → Olympic Park (올림픽공원) 5km run (14:00–16:00) → Jamsil (잠실) ARC·N·BOOK café → dinner

🌸 5 Seasonal & Time Hooks

1. Spring (March–April) Yunjungno (윤중로) Cherry Blossoms at Yeouido: 4.5km cherry blossom course at its absolute peak. Foreign visitors' #1 Instagram moment. One-week peak window in early-to-mid April.

2. Summer (July–August) Sunrise Early Morning Runs: Beat the heat and catch sunrise views. Banpo (반포), Yeouido (여의도), and Namsan (남산) at 05:00–07:00 are spectacular.

3. Autumn (September–November) Namsan (남산) & Bugak (북악) Foliage: The 6.6km and 7km hill courses explode with autumn colors. Peak: late October to mid-November.

4. Winter (December–February) Han River Midday Runs: Sub-zero temperatures but midday sun on the Han River can bring it up to 5–10°C. Finish with Han River night views and makgeolli (막걸리 — Korean rice wine, similar to Japanese nigori sake).

5. Golden Hour Sunrise (All Seasons): 05:30–07:00 early morning along the Han River (Banpo (반포), Yeouido (여의도)) — the most-filmed time slot by foreign vloggers, any season.
