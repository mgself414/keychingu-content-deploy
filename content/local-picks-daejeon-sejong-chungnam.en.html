Locals' Picks ⑤ Daejeon, Sejong & Chungnam — 27 Eateries Korean Viewers Named, From the Kalguksu City to a West Sea Port

Subtitle: An axis the Daejeon day-trip meta (#1006), Sungsimdang (#1007), and the Daejeon kalguksu piece (#1010) never covered — pulled 27 Daejeon, Sejong, and Chungnam spots out of a nationwide roundup of local eateries Korean viewers recommended in YouTube comments, verified existence, address, and business type on Naver local search, and skipped ratings entirely. Daejeon 12 → Sejong 3 → Chungnam 12 (Seosan 5, Gongju 2, Cheonan, Nonsan, Dangjin, Asan). Cheonan, Nonsan, Dangjin, and Asan are all first appearances on KeyChinGu.

ISSUE 06 · #1386 · 2026-09-18 · Locals' Picks ⑤

🎯 Why "Comment Recommendations" Is the Bar

This corner has one source. A Korean YouTube channel posted a "nationwide matjip" video, viewers left comments naming their neighborhood eateries, and the most systematically organized comments by region got turned into a table of 217 spots. Since it's a list Koreans recommended to Koreans, foreign reviews are nearly nonexistent, and many are small-town restaurants that never make it onto tourist lists. This corner's criteria and limits are covered in Part ① (#1382).

KeyChinGu checked only whether the name, address, and business type actually exist on Naver local search (2026-09-17). Of 30 Daejeon, Sejong, and Chungnam spots, 27 were confirmed; the 3 that couldn't be confirmed (2 in Daejeon, 1 in Cheonan) were dropped. There are no visit reviews, rankings, or star ratings, and no hours or prices either — check Naver Place the day you go.

In this region, what counts as a meal differs city by city. In Daejeon, two kalguksu (칼국수) shops came up from different neighborhoods (why Daejeon is called the city of kalguksu is in #1010); in Sejong, two sundaeguk (순댓국) shops sit in old myeon towns; and in Chungnam, two of Seosan's five spots are in Samgilpo Port. Rail covers only half the ground here — Seosan and Dangjin have no railway, and Hongseong's station gained a second line when the Seohae Line (서해선) opened in November 2024.

① Daejeon — 12 Spots, One Line and What's Beyond It

Daejeon's urban rail is a single line — Line 1 (Panam~Banseok). The Line 2 tram broke ground in late 2024, but its opening has been pushed to late 2030, so for a 2026 traveler, Line 1 and buses are how you get around. Six of the twelve spots on this list are explained by Line 1 station names; the rest are bus routes.

  • Jinrojip (진로집) · 45-5 Junggyo-ro, Jung-gu, Daejeon (대전 중구 중교로 45-5) · Restaurant · Korean · Jungangno Station area — old downtown, walking minutes unconfirmed
  • Hanbat Kalguksu (한밭칼국수) · 6 Mokcheok 4-gil, Jung-gu (중구 목척4길 6) · Korean · kalguksu (칼국수)/mandu · Around Mokcheokgyo, same Jungangno Station area
  • Dongeunseong (동은성) · 532-1 Daejong-ro, Jung-gu (중구 대종로 532-1) · Chinese · Chinese restaurant · Jungangno Station area

Three spots in the old downtown. It's one Line 1 stop from Daejeon Station, the old center of the city. Kalguksu is Daejeon's most common meal — the background is in #1010.

  • Banseok Donkkaseu (반석돈가스) · 17 Gyeryong-ro 874beon-gil, 2F, Jung-gu (중구 계룡로874번길 17 2층) · Japanese · donkkaseu · Seodaejeon-negeori Station area — Oryu-dong
  • Hanyeong Sikdang (한영식당) · 6 Gyeryong-ro 874beon-gil, Jung-gu (중구 계룡로874번길 6) · Korean · chicken dishes · Same alley

A donkkaseu shop and a chicken-dish shop sit side by side on the same beon-gil — two options, one alley.

  • Mijin (미진) · 28 Dunsanjung-ro 40beon-gil, Seo-gu (서구 둔산중로40번길 28) · Restaurant · Korean · Sicheong Station, Exit 7, walking distance
  • Taepyeongso Gukbap (태평소국밥) · 52 Dunsan-ro 31beon-gil, Seo-gu (서구 둔산로31번길 52) · Korean · gukbap (국밥) · Dunsan government-complex area — a 10-minute walk from Jeongbu Cheongsa Station (정부청사역)

Dunsan is the newer part of town, packed with the Daejeon Government Complex and offices. Weekday lunches get crowded here, so timing around the rush helps.

  • Oncheon Kalguksu (온천칼국수) · 61 Oncheonbuk-ro, Yuseong-gu (유성구 온천북로 61) · Korean · kalguksu/mandu · Yuseong Oncheon Station, 7-10 min on foot
  • Pizzeria Daali (피제리아다알리) · 40-5 Jijok-ro 349beon-gil, Yuseong-gu (유성구 지족로349번길 40-5) · Restaurant · Italian food · Jijok Station, walking distance

Yuseong is where the hot springs (#1008) and the university district meet. Kalguksu and pizza, both sitting on the same line.

  • Io Siktak (이오식탁) · 97 Yongso-ro 39beon-gil, Seo-gu (서구 용소로39번길 97) · Restaurant · Thai food · Whether it's walking distance from a subway station is unconfirmed — bus or taxi
  • Seoul Sikdang (서울식당) · 38 Munjeong-ro 170beon-gil, Seo-gu (서구 문정로170번길 38) · Restaurant · Korean · Bus or taxi — unconfirmed
  • Myeongnang Sikdang (명랑식당) · 56-20 Taejeon-ro, Dong-gu (동구 태전로 56-20) · Korean · jjigae/jeongol · Bus or taxi — distance from Daejeon Station not confirmed

These three don't map onto the subway. Check the route from your starting point on Naver Map first.

For foreign visitors — Kalguksu works for solo diners, and in Daejeon it's common to order dubu duruchigi (두부두루치기) alongside it. At gukbap shops, you season your own bowl with salted shrimp and minced seasoning.

② Sejong — 3 Spots, New Town and Old Myeon Centers

Sejong has no urban rail — BRT (bus rapid transit) is the backbone. Coming by KTX, you get off at Osong Station and take the BRT into downtown (you board right on Osong Station's ground floor). The three spots split into one in the new town and two in old myeon centers.

  • Myeongpum Hanu (명품한우) · 13 Dajeong-ro, Sejong (세종시 다정로 13) · Korean · grilled beef · New-town Dajeong-dong — bus (BRT) from Osong Station into downtown; the Dajeong-dong stop and route are unconfirmed
  • Chungnam Sundae (충남순대) · 97-11 Yongpo-ro, Geumnam-myeon, Sejong (세종시 금남면 용포로 97-11) · Korean · sundae (순대)/sundaeguk · Geumnam-myeon — access unconfirmed
  • Bugangok (부강옥) · 103 Bugangoecheon-ro, Bugang-myeon, Sejong (세종시 부강면 부강외천로 103) · Korean · sundae/sundaeguk · Bugang Station area — Gyeongbu Line Mugunghwa trains stop here

Sejong is an administrative city launched in 2012, so its "old neighborhoods" sit outside the new town. Geumnam-myeon and Bugang-myeon are those places, and the two sundaeguk shops are there. Bugang-myeon is one of the few reachable by train, since Gyeongbu Line passenger trains stop at Bugang Station.

For foreign visitors — Sundaeguk works for solo diners; grilled beef runs from 2 servings up. Sejong's new town has similar strip-mall layouts block to block, so showing the address directly is the fastest way.

③ Chungnam — 12 Spots, a Province Rail Only Half Reaches

Chungnam is crossed by the Janghang Line (장항선) (toward Cheonan, Asan, Hongseong, Daecheon, Gunsan), the Honam Line (호남선) (Nonsan), and the Seohae Line, which opened in November 2024 (Hongseong~Seohwaseong). Seosan and Dangjin have no railway. The twelve spots below reflect that directly.

  • Mandal Gomjip (만달곰집) · 220 Dujeong-ro, Seobuk-gu, Cheonan (천안시 서북구 두정로 220) · Korean · gukbap · Dujeong Station (Seoul Metro Line 1) area

Cheonan has three stations and they're easy to mix up — Cheonan-Asan Station (천안아산역) is high-speed-rail only (KTX/SRT) and sits in Asan's Baebang-eup. Cheonan Station (천안역) serves regular trains and Metro Line 1; Dujeong Station serves Line 1. Coming from Seoul by subway, Dujeong Station is the closer one.

  • Sinjeong Sikdang (신정식당) · 18 Simin-ro 409beon-gil, Asan (아산시 시민로409번길 18) · Korean · noodles · Onyang Oncheon Station (Janghang Line + Metro Line 1) area

Asan's downtown station is Onyang Oncheon Station. The similarly named Asan Station sits on the Baebang side with Cheonan-Asan Station — a different place.

  • Cheonbukjip (천북집) · 49 Amun-gil, Hongseong-eup, Hongseong-gun (홍성군 홍성읍 아문길 49) · Restaurant · Korean · Walking distance from Hongseong Station to central Hongseong-eup — a 10-minute walk from the station to the intercity terminal

Hongseong Station became a stop on both the Janghang Line and the Seohae Line when the Seohae Line opened on 2024-11-02. Still, there's no train running straight through to central Seoul yet — the Seohwaseong~Wonsi section is slated to open in 2027, so a transfer is still needed for now.

  • Janggeumi Sikdang (장금이식당) · 60 Cheyuk-ro, Nonsan (논산시 체육로 60) · Korean · duck dishes · Nonsan Station (Honam Line) — a 5-minute walk from the station to the intercity bus terminal
  • Chowon Kongguksu (초원콩국수) · 14 Dongmun 1-gil, Myeoncheon-myeon, Dangjin (당진시 면천면 동문1길 14) · Korean · noodles · Myeoncheon-myeon has no railway. Even Dangjin's only station, Hapdeok Station (합덕역, Seohae Line), is 17km from downtown, so you'd need several bus transfers — a rental car or taxi is the practical option
  • Maehyang (매향) · 18 Baekmigoeul-gil, Gongju (공주시 백미고을길 18) · Restaurant · Korean · Downtown Gongju — Gongju Station (호남고속선) is a 20-30 minute drive from downtown. Use the Gongju Intercity Bus Terminal as your downtown reference point
  • Dabok Son Kalguksu (다복손칼국수) · 50 Singeum 2-gil, Gongju (공주시 신금2길 50) · Korean · kalguksu/mandu · Singwan-dong — unconfirmed

Gongju is the city of the Royal Tomb of King Muryeong and Gongsanseong Fortress, but its KTX station sits far from downtown. From Seoul, an intercity bus is often simpler.

  • Chon Sikdang (촌식당) · 34 Jamdong 4-gil, Seosan (서산시 잠동4길 34) · Restaurant · Korean · Seosan has no railway — the Seosan Intercity Bus Terminal is the transport hub
  • Seoraedam (서래담) · 34 Yulji 16-ro, Seosan (서산시 율지16로 34) · Korean · kalguksu/mandu · Downtown Seosan — unconfirmed
  • Galsan Jangeo (갈산장어) · 11 Galsan 9-gil, Seosan (서산시 갈산9길 11) · Korean · eel · Downtown Seosan — unconfirmed
  • Mirae Susan (미래수산) · 47 Samgilpo 1-ro, Daesan-eup, Seosan (서산시 대산읍 삼길포1로 47) · Japanese · Japanese restaurant · Samgilpo Port — a bus runs from the terminal, but travel time is unconfirmed
  • Seonchang Sikdang (선창식당) · 35 Samgilpo 1-ro, Daesan-eup, Seosan (서산시 대산읍 삼길포1로 35) · Korean · kalguksu/mandu · Same Samgilpo Port

Two of Seosan's five spots are in Samgilpo Port, Daesan-eup. It's a West Sea fishing port where raw fish and kalguksu sit in the same alley, one more leg in from downtown Seosan.

For foreign visitors — More Chungnam eup/myeon restaurants take cards these days, but a cash-friendly habit lingers (#1301). Eel and beef run from 2 servings up; kalguksu, kongguksu (콩국수), and noodles work for solo diners. Kongguksu is a summer dish, so many shops drop it in winter.

🚄 Fitting This Into a Regional Route

  • A Daejeon day (#1006) — The three old-downtown spots line up on Jungangno Station; the two Dunsan spots on Sicheong/Jeongbu Cheongsa Stations; the two Yuseong spots on Yuseong Oncheon/Jijok Stations. One Line 1 string threads them all.
  • Osong Station transfer — Your gateway when arriving by KTX for Sejong or Cheongju. Sejong's downtown is a BRT ride from Osong Station.
  • Seoul → Cheonan/Asan by subway — Metro Line 1 runs all the way to Cheonan and Onyang Oncheon. Just remember KTX's Cheonan-Asan Station is a different station.
  • Hongseong → Seosan/Dangjin — Hongseong Station is the Janghang/Seohae Line transfer point. Seosan and Dangjin have no railway, so you switch to a bus here.
  • Gongju — Its KTX station is far from downtown. An intercity bus from Seoul is often simpler, and it pairs with a Buyeo-Gongju Baekje-history route.
  • Carrying on to Chungbuk — From Daejeon toward Cheongju and Chungju, that's Part ⑥ (#1387).

⚠ 3 Common Mistakes

1. Looking for downtown Cheonan at Cheonan-Asan Station — Cheonan-Asan Station is in Asan's Baebang-eup. Old-downtown Cheonan is at Cheonan Station; Dujeong-dong is at Dujeong Station.

2. Waiting for a through train to Seoul at Hongseong Station — The Seohae Line has only opened the Hongseong~Seohwaseong stretch, as of November 2024. A through connection to Seoul is slated for 2027.

3. Treating Gongju Station as downtown Gongju — It's a 20-30 minute drive away. Plan your downtown schedule around the terminal instead.

⚙ FAQ 3

1. Who picked this list? — It's a table built from local eateries Korean YouTube viewers named in the comments. KeyChinGu checked only existence, address, and business type, and did not rank anything.

2. Why does Daejeon have two kalguksu shops? — That's how they came up in the source roundup. Why Daejeon is called the city of kalguksu, and the dubu duruchigi pairing, are covered in #1010.

3. How do you get to Seosan and Dangjin? — There's no railway. An intercity bus from Hongseong Station or from Seoul's Nambu Terminal / Central City is standard, and Samgilpo Port is one more leg in from downtown Seosan.
