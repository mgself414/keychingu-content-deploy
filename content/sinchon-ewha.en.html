🎯 Why is this an exclusive guide to Sinchon and Ewha University?

Sinchon and Ewha Womans University are Korea's No. 1 university and youth center in Seodaemun-gu — Yonsei University, Ewha Womans University, Sogang University, Hongik University 4 university belts are compressed into one area, making them a global university hot zone. Ewha Womans University is Korea's No. 1 women's university and the world's No. 1 study abroad destination for foreign female students, and Yonsei University is Korea's prestigious private university with the largest number of foreign short-term language training and exchange students.

Compared to Hongdae (MZ, indie), Gangnam (luxury), and Itaewon (global), Sinchon and Ewha University are university, youth, and orthodox. Although it is 5 minutes away from Hongdae in the flow of foreign tourists, it is not recognized as a separate area, so pass — Photo spot at the main gate of Ewha Womans University + Sinchon Korean-style youth neighborhood experience is ranked #1 by foreigners.

In particular, bakery and dessert clusters near the main gate of Ewha Womans University, such as Sulbing Ewha Womans University branch (Korean shaved ice main branch), Motherinler Bagel (authentic bagels from Dae-gil Lee), and La Bonne Tart (authentic tart at the main gate of Ewha Womans University) are ranked 1st by foreign students and MZ Demographics. ⚠ 3 mistakes foreigners often make

1. "Pass because it's next to Hongdae" — Not considering separate area recognition

The most common mistake in foreign travel routes. Sinchon and Ewha Womans University are a 10-minute walk or 1 subway stop away from Hongik University Station, and it is recognized as a “Sinchon and Ewha Womans University Pass because I’m going to Hongik University.” In reality, Sinchon and Ewha Womans University (university, youth, orthodoxy) are differentiated from Hongdae (commercial, indie). If bundled in one route, "Hongdae Indie + Sinchon/Ewha University + Yeonnam Cafe" = 1-day full set in 3 areas of Seodaemun-gu and Mapo-gu available.

2. "Ewha Womans University = just a school" — The value of Korea's No. 1 women's university is unknown

Ewha Womans University, founded in 1886 by American missionary Mary Scranton, Korea's first and world's largest women's university — is ranked #1 in the world for foreign female students studying abroad. Ewha Womans University main gate + main building + Palace Chapel + Hangaram Square is a hot zone for foreigner photo spots. Ewha 100th Anniversary Museum (Korean women's history + exhibition at Korea's first comprehensive university) Free admission. In addition to shopping for cosmetics in Myeongdong and cafes in Hongdae, it is the number one place to experience Korean studies.

3. "Sinchon = only bars and karaoke rooms" — Cafe and Bakery Cluster Miji

Foreigners only perceive Sinchon as a “Korean college student bar and karaoke room,” but in reality, the Ewha Womans University main gate + Yonsei-ro is home to a cluster of bakeries and desserts, including Motherinler Bagel, La Bonne Tart, Sulbing Ewha Womans University, and Secret Bakery. In particular, Sulbing Ewha Womans University Branch is the main Korean shaved ice branch and ranks #1 on Instagram for foreigners. 1-6pm walk to bakery/café + dinner bar/karaoke Full-day course recommended.

Scenery of Sinchon and Ewha Womans University

Sinchon/Ewha University is located in the center of Seodaemun-gu Korean university/youth belt — It started with the establishment of Ewha Womans University in 1886 + Yonsei University in 1885, and established itself as the number one place for Korean youth, democratization movement, and university culture in the 1960s and 1980s. Currently, it is a Korean university hot zone where the largest number of foreign students and MZ youth gather.

Sinchon Station (Line 2) area is the main street — Sinchon Rotary + Yonsei-ro + Sinchon Station Street line. There are 50+ cafes, bars, karaoke rooms, and restaurants crowded together. Korean college students + foreign youth neighborhood.

Ewha Womans University main gate + Ewha Womans University road is Ewha Womans University sub-area — Ewha Womans University main gate photo spot + Motherline, La Bonne, and Sulbing main gate cluster. No. 1 on foreign Instagram.

Yonsei University Sinchon Campus + Yonsei-ro is the Yeonsei sub-area — Yonsei University main gate + campus walk + Hangaram Square. Experience at a prestigious Korean university.

Hongik University and Sogang University are adjacent areas (10-15 minutes on foot) — In addition to the Sinchon and Ewha University guides, separate #138 Hongdae guides + separate areas.

Transportation: Line 2 Sinchon Station (Exits 1 and 2) or Ewha Womans University Station (Exits 1 and 2). 1 stop from Hongik University Station. Kakao Taxi recommended. Bicycle Ttareungi OK.

⚙ FAQ (8)

Q1. Can I enter the Ewha Womans University campus?

Available — Free entry for foreigners + free distribution of campus maps at the main gate. 9-17:00 is best (busy for students and faculty). Quiet on Sunday. ECC Basement Campus + Felice Chapel + Main Building + Hangaram Square Free photo OK. Entry to the student cafeteria is OK.

Q2. Can I enter the Yonsei University campus?

Available — Free entry for foreigners + Entry from Sinchon main gate (toward Yeonhui-dong) or east gate (toward Ewha Womans University). Yonsei University Baekyang Hall + Underwood Hall + Cheongsongdae Authentic building photo OK. Korea’s #1 prestigious campus experience.

Q3. Is Sulbing the original Korean shaved ice store?

Korean shaved ice signature brand founded in Busan in 2013 — Ewha Womans University is a Sinchon hotspot. The #1 shaved ice experience for foreigners. Injeolmi shaved ice + red bean shaved ice + mango shaved ice are the signatures. OK in both summer and winter (Shaved ice is popular even in winter in Korea).

Q4. What are the foreigner-friendly stores in Sinchon and Ewha?

Sulbing, Mother Linler, Jama Burger, Mentsuru, Lover's Karaoke English menu + card payment + foreigner friendly standard. Dangna Balpocha is mainly in authentic Korean, but has a photo menu and is staff-friendly.

Q5. Is Lover's Karaoke available to foreigners?

Available — 5,000+ songs in Korean and other languages English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish** Full lineup. Foreigner-friendly karaoke standard. Card payment + 16,000~25,000 won per hour for 4 people.

Q6. Is it possible to connect Sinchon/Ewha University + Hongdae?

OK — 10-15 minutes walk from Sinchon/Ewha University to Hongik University or 1 subway stop (Hongik University Station). Sinchon lunch + Ewha Womans University cafe + Hongdae dinner and indie performance = 1 full day OK.

Q7. What about Ewha Womans University’s Palace Chapel?

American-style chapel founded by Mary Scranton in 1899 — A key building on the Ewha Womans University campus. #1 photo spot for foreigners. Sunday English worship service OK (tourists allowed).

Q8. Is it safe at night in Sinchon/Ewha?

Safety in Korean universities — 24-hour safety. Korean plainclothes police patrol + CCTV. Foreigner-friendly night bars and karaoke bars. Kakao Taxi recommended after midnight.
