Foreign Fan's Guide to Attending K-pop Music Shows — How to Apply, Based on 5 Open Halls (Inkigayo, M Countdown, Music Bank, Show! Music Core, Show Champion) and 9 Agency Audience Notices (Confirmed October 2026)

Subtitle: Inkigayo (인기가요) every Sunday at 3:20 (SBS official) · Show! Music Core (쇼! 음악중심) Saturday at 3:20 p.m. (MBC official) · Applications open only on agency fan-club notices, first-come-first-served for just 10 to 30 minutes (9 Weverse (위버스) notices) · Foreign ID means the physical passport or Alien Registration Card (CORTIS and ILLIT notices) · Headcount checks begin at 4 a.m. (ENHYPEN and NCT WISH notices) — as of 2026-10-21

ISSUE 04 · #1221 · 2026-07-03

🎯 How to Use This Guide

It is four in the morning, still pitch dark outside the Sangam (상암) MBC Open Hall (공개홀). You are gripping your passport and light stick, waiting for your number to be called. NCT WISH's Show! Music Core (쇼! 음악중심) pre-recording really did start its headcount check at this hour (NCT WISH official notice, recording on 2026-10-15).

This is a practical guide for international fans who want a seat in a music show audience during a trip to Korea. Attending is not a concert where you buy a ticket. The agency opens first-come-first-served applications for a few minutes through a fan-club notice, and only people who pass an ID check at dawn on the day get in. The 9 notices quoted here are all for sessions that have already ended. Dates and headcounts change every time, so use them to learn how to read what a notice demands. For the big picture on fan events, see "5 Types of K-pop Fan Events in Korea" in the 🔗 section below.

📺 How Attendance Opens — From Notice to Wristband

On the broadcasters' own websites, we found no application window usable by foreigners. The SBS Inkigayo (인기가요) page has an "audience application guide" menu, but as of October 21 its body is empty (SBS official). Every application we actually saw opened was run by the agency as an artist notice on Weverse (위버스), and the flow was nearly identical.

1. Notice: A "Pre-recording Participation Guide for OOO" appears in the artist notices on Weverse (위버스)

2. Application window: One or two days before recording, a first-come-first-served form opens at a set time for only 10 to 30 minutes. ILLIT's M Countdown (엠카운트다운) ran August 19, 15:00 to 15:10, and NCT WISH's Show! Music Core (쇼! 음악중심) ran October 13, 23:30 to 23:40 (KST). The form opened 5 to 15 minutes early so you could fill in personal details in advance (ILLIT and NCT WISH notices)

3. Number announcement: Check your result and entry number under "Event Application History" (이벤트 신청내역) in the Weverse (위버스) app (TWS and NCT WISH notices)

4. Headcount check: Two or three hours before recording, ID and required items are checked in time slots by number range. For ENHYPEN's Music Bank (뮤직뱅크) pre-recording, numbers 1 to 150 were 04:00 to 04:59 and numbers 151 to 300 were 05:00 to 05:55, with recording starting at 07:00 (ENHYPEN notice)

5. Wristband and stamp: After the check you receive a hand stamp and a wristband. If you lose or damage the wristband, entry is restricted (TXT and ILLIT notices)

Live broadcast audiences are far smaller than pre-recording ones. TXT Yeonjun's Music Bank (뮤직뱅크) live show (2026-07-17, 17:05) took 5 people plus 3 standby, and CLOSE YOUR EYES' Show Champion (쇼챔피언) live broadcast (2026-09-09) took 20 (each official notice). HUI's Show Champion (쇼챔피언) notice stated that fans are not admitted to the live broadcast (HUI notice).

🪪 Foreign ID and Your Name — Where Most People Get Rejected

  • Accepted ID: For foreigners, a passport, Alien Registration Card (외국인등록증), Domestic Residence Report Card (국내거소신고증), or Permanent Resident Card (영주증) (ILLIT, TWS, and ENHYPEN notices). Only physical documents are accepted. Photos, copies, printouts, and phone screenshots do not count, and neither does a student ID (CORTIS and ILLIT notices). Expired ID is not accepted either (HUI notice)
  • English name: If you wrote your name in English on the application, you are verified by passport only (CORTIS notice). If the name on your application differs from your ID, entry can be refused. The TWS notice gave the example of the passport saying "TWS" while the application said Tuoseu (투어스) (TWS notice)
  • Age: As of the application date, age 15 or older (international age) for Korea and the US, and age 16 or older for all other countries and regions. Anyone under the minimum cannot enter even with a guardian (TXT and ENHYPEN notices)

🚫 On-Site Rules and No-Show Penalties

  • No filming: No photos, video, or audio recording inside the studio. Recording equipment cannot be brought in, and violators are removed immediately and their files deleted on the spot (TXT and ENHYPEN notices). One notice even banned binoculars (HUI notice)
  • Cheering items: Only the official light stick is allowed. Slogans, banners, plush toys, fans, and phone LED apps are banned (ILLIT and TWS notices). NCT WISH even checked that the light stick's LED turns on (NCT WISH notice)
  • Gifts: Only letters may be handed over, through staff (CORTIS and ENHYPEN notices)
  • Arrival time: You must arrive 5 minutes before the set time, and arriving exactly on time counts as late (HUI notice). Latecomers enter last, from a separate line (TXT notice)
  • No-show penalties: If you win and don't show up, or leave partway, you are barred from public broadcasts during that album's promotion period (TXT and ENHYPEN notices). NCT WISH barred no-shows from applying until the end of the following month and gave violators who transferred a number or filmed a 6-month ban, and after the event it published the numbers of 21 no-shows and 3 transfers (NCT WISH notice)
  • Cancellation and transfer: You cannot cancel or transfer for personal reasons (CLOSE YOUR EYES notice). After the announcement, even the album you bought to apply cannot be refunded (ILLIT notice)

🧰 Practical Matters — Going Solo, Preparing, Paying, Getting There

  • Going solo: Applications are per person, and only you enter. Applying on someone's behalf and transferring numbers are banned (TWS and CORTIS notices). Going alone puts you at no disadvantage
  • Prepare (before coming to Korea): Set up the artist's fan-club membership, a Weverse (위버스) account, and a Melon (멜론) account in advance. Most notices required membership, an album purchase, or Melon records, and the application window opened for only 10 to 30 minutes, one or two days before recording (ILLIT, NCT WISH, and TXT notices). Turn on Weverse (위버스) push notifications
  • Payment: Only albums bought from the seller named in the notice (such as Weverse Global with shipping within Korea) counted for applications (CORTIS, ILLIT, and ENHYPEN notices). For paying in Korea, see Foreign Visitors' Korean Payment Systems deep dive
  • Getting there and lodging: Many sessions started headcount checks between 04:00 and 06:30 (ENHYPEN, NCT WISH, HUI, and CORTIS notices). Staying near the open hall is better. Ilsan (일산) Dream Center is outside Seoul. For transit cards, see Choosing 1 of 3 Korean Transit Cards, a Zero-Panic Manual — T-money, WOWPASS, Climate Companion Card (기후동행): Which to Buy

⚠ 4 Common Mistakes

1. "I can just show up and line up to get in" — One notice states that without advance application you cannot enter the live broadcast on site (CLOSE YOUR EYES notice)

2. "A passport photo saved on my phone is enough" — Only physical ID is accepted (CORTIS and ILLIT notices)

3. "I can apply under my Korean name and be verified by passport" — If the application name and the passport name differ, entry can be refused. Write the name exactly as in the passport's English (CORTIS and TWS notices)

4. "I can show my Melon records with a phone screenshot" — The download history was required as a paper printout (CORTIS and ILLIT notices)
