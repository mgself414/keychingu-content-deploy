Cheorwon Hantangang Winter Guide: Ice Trekking Festival, Mulwitgil, Jusangjeolli Trail, Goseokjeong, Jiktang Falls and DMZ Peace Tourism

Subtitle: Cheorwon Hantangang Ice Trekking Festival (registered for January 16 to February 14, 2027) · Hantangang Mulwitgil (open October to March, 09:00 to 17:00) · Jusangjeolli Trail (3.6km, winter 09:00 to 15:00) · Eunhasugyo Bridge and Hwaetbul Observatory · Goseokjeong, Jiktang Falls and Sambuyeon Falls · Peace Observatory and Second Tunnel (guided tour) · Former Labor Party Headquarters · Migratory bird sanctuary — confirmed via Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI)

🎯 How to Use This Guide

This guide gathers winter destinations along the Hantangang River (한탄강) in Cheorwon-gun (철원군), Gangwon (강원), and around it. The Hantangang area is a UNESCO Global Geopark. They are Mulwitgil (물윗길), a trail that walks over the river, and the ice trekking festival held on it; the Jusangjeolli Trail (주상절리길) hung on the cliffs; Goseokjeong (고석정) and the waterfalls in the Hantangang canyon; the DMZ peace tourism course; the Former Labor Party Headquarters (노동당사), a trace of the division; and the Cheorwon Plain (철원평야), where winter migratory birds arrive.

Many places close on Tuesdays — Mulwitgil, the Jusangjeolli Trail, Hwaetbul Observatory, Peace Observatory, the Second Tunnel and the Cheorwon Tourist Information Center. The Peace Observatory and the Second Tunnel depart at set times from the same reception desk, and the Second Tunnel is registered as not open to individual visits, only with a guide.

The operating details are copied from values registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data (TourAPI). This guide covers only places registered in the Korea Tourism Organization tourism data.

📍 Walking Over the River in Winter — Ice Trekking Festival and Mulwitgil

The Cheorwon Hantangang Ice Trekking Festival (철원 한탄강 얼음트레킹 축제) [Jangheung-ri 725 (장흥리 725), Dongsong-eup (동송읍), around the Mulwitgil trekking course, ☎ Cheorwon Cultural Foundation (철원문화재단) 033-455-7072] is registered for January 16 to February 14, 2027, 10:00 to 17:00. It is a festival linked to the Hantangang Mulwitgil trekking course, and the main venue is under Seungilgyo Bridge (승일교). According to the Korea Tourism Organization, it is in the Hall of Fame of the Korea Festival Content Awards (대한민국 축제콘텐츠 대상) and was designated an Excellent Festival for 2026-27.

  • Fee: 10,000 won, with 5,000 won refunded in Cheorwon Sarang Gift Vouchers (철원사랑상품권). It is registered as linked to the Mulwitgil trekking fee.
  • Registered programs (based on 2026 content): ice trekking course, main venue under Seungilgyo Bridge, the Ttongbaram Altong Gubo Daehoe contest (똥바람알통구보대회), sledding hill, food market, local produce sales, Gumuteo (구움터), stage performances, weekday singing contest. The 2027 programs are noted as still being updated.

Hantangang Mulwitgil (한탄강 물윗길) [Sangsa-ri (상사리), Galmal-eup (갈말읍), ☎ 033-455-7072] is a trekking course for walking on the water while viewing the columnar joints of the Hantangang River. According to the registered information, it opens every October and runs until March of the following year. The floating bridge, which had been laid on some sections such as Goseokjeong and Sundam Valley (순담계곡), was extended from 2017 to the whole section from Taebongdaegyo Bridge (태봉대교) to Sundam Valley, and according to the Korea Tourism Organization, more than 100,000 people visit every year.

  • Opening hours: 09:00 to 17:00 (last entry 16:00).
  • Closed days: Every Tuesday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok day.

📍 Walking Along the Cliffs — Jusangjeolli Trail, Eunhasugyo Bridge and Hwaetbul Observatory

The Hantangang Jusangjeolli Trail (한탄강 주상절리길), a cliffside walkway [Guntan-ri (군탄리), Galmal-eup (갈말읍), ☎ Sundam ticket office (순담매표소) 0507-1431-2225, Deureuni ticket office (드르니매표소) 0507-1374-9825] is a 3.6km path hung midway up a vertical cliff 20 to 30m above the water. It starts from the Sundam Gate (순담 게이트) or the Deureuni Gate (드르니 게이트) and takes about 1 hour 30 minutes one way. Starting from the Deureuni Gate has many downhill stairs, and starting from the Sundam Gate has many uphill stairs. There are 10 scenic rest stops. On weekends a shuttle bus is introduced as running between both entrances.

  • Opening hours: Winter (December to February) 09:00 to 15:00, summer (March to November) 09:00 to 16:00. Operation stops when a heavy rain advisory is issued.
  • Closed days: Every Tuesday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok day.

Eunhasugyo Bridge (은하수교) [Jangheung-ri (장흥리), Dongsong-eup (동송읍), ☎ Cheorwon-gun Office Tourism Policy Division (철원군청 관광정책과) 033-450-5530] is a suspension bridge 180m long and 3m wide laid on the Hanyeoul-gil (한여울길) at Songdaeso (송대소). It connects Jusangjeolli Trail Course 1 (Jangheung-ri, Dongsong-eup) and Course 2 (Sangsa-ri, Galmal-eup).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

Hwaetbul Observatory (횃불전망대) [Galmal-ro 653-3 (갈말로 653-3), Galmal-eup (갈말읍), ☎ 033-452-1104] is an observatory about 53m high on the Hanyeoul-gil route together with Eunhasugyo Bridge. The upper level has a transparent tempered glass floor.

  • Opening hours: Winter 09:00 to 17:30 (ticket sales close 16:30), summer 09:00 to 18:00 (ticket sales close 17:00).
  • Closed days: Every Tuesday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok day.
  • Fee: Individual adults 6,000 won, children 3,000 won. It is also registered as adults 3,000 won and children 1,000 won with a "Cheorwon gift voucher exchange".

📍 Hantangang Canyon and Waterfalls — Goseokjeong, Jiktang Falls and Sambuyeon Falls

At Goseokjeong (고석정) [Taebong-ro 1825 (태봉로 1825), Dongsong-eup (동송읍), ☎ 033-450-5558], there is a granite rock called Goseok (고석), about 15m high, inside the Hantangang canyon, with a pavilion called Goseokjeong beside it, so the whole canyon area is also called Goseokjeong. Goseok is granite formed underground about 110 million years ago; it was covered by basalt lava during volcanic activity and then exposed again as the Hantangang River cut away the rock.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Small vehicles 2,000 won, large vehicles 5,000 won.

Jiktang Falls (직탕폭포) [Jangheung-ri (장흥리), Dongsong-eup (동송읍), ☎ Hantangang Geopark Visitor Center (한탄강지질공원 방문자센터) 033-450-4810] is a waterfall on the main stream of the Hantangang River. It formed over flat basalt and is about 3m high but about 80m wide, spreading out broadly like steps.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free.

Sambuyeon Falls (삼부연폭포) [Sincheorwon-ri (신철원리), Galmal-eup (갈말읍), ☎ Cheorwon-gun Office (철원군청) 033-450-4810] is a three-tier waterfall about 20m high on the slope of Myeongseongsan (명성산). The water turns three times and the bottom is hollowed like a cauldron, so it is called "Sambuyeon (삼부연)". From the parking lot you walk through a tunnel to the observation deck.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Available.

📍 DMZ Peace Tourism — Peace Observatory and Second Tunnel

The Cheorwon Peace Observatory (철원 평화전망대) [Yangji-ri 457-7 (양지리 457-7), Dongsong-eup (동송읍), ☎ 033-450-5559, Cheorwon DMZ Peace and Security Tourism reception desk (철원DMZ평화·안보관광 접수처)] is a three-story observatory built in 2007 that looks down on the Demilitarized Zone (DMZ) and North Korean territory. You can see the Pyeonggang Plateau (평강고원), Seonjeon Village (선전마을), Baengmagoji (백마고지) and more. You can ride up on a 50-seat monorail.

The Second Tunnel (제2땅굴) (same reception desk, ☎ 033-450-5559) is a tunnel discovered in 1975. Of its total length of 3.5km, 500m can be toured.

  • Departure times: 09:30, 10:30, 13:30, 14:30. In winter the last departure is 14:00 (registered as "winter" for the Peace Observatory and "November to February" for the Second Tunnel). Reception closes 15 minutes before departure.
  • How to enter: Because the Second Tunnel is inside the Civilian Control Zone, it is registered as not open to individual visits, and visitors depart together following a guide. For the Second Tunnel's domestic visitor guidance, you bring an ID on the day of the visit, fill in an application at the reception desk and receive an entry pass. The reception location is registered both as "1st floor of the Goseokjeong Tourist Information Office (고석정 관광안내소)" and as "DMZ Crane Peace Town Peace and Security Tourism reception desk (DMZ두루미평화타운 평화·안보관광 접수처), Yangji-ri 457-7".
  • Fee (individual): Peace Observatory adults 4,000 won, teenagers and soldiers 3,000 won, children and seniors 2,000 won. Second Tunnel general 4,000 won, teenagers 3,000 won, children 2,000 won. The monorail is separate: adults 2,000 won, teenagers and soldiers 1,500 won, children and seniors 1,000 won.
  • Closed days: Peace Observatory every Tuesday, January 1, Seollal and Chuseok holiday periods. Second Tunnel is registered as every Tuesday, January 1, holiday periods and International Marathon days (no registered value for the marathon date).

📍 Traces of Division and Winter Migratory Birds — Former Labor Party Headquarters, Bird Sanctuary and Tourist Information Center

The Cheorwon Former Labor Party Headquarters (철원 노동당사) [Geumgangsan-ro 265 (금강산로 265), Cheorwon-eup (철원읍), ☎ 033-450-5520] is a three-story building put up in 1946, when the area was North Korean territory after liberation, as the Cheorwon county office of the Workers' Party of Korea. It stands just as it was, scorched in the war and still bearing shell and bullet marks. It became a National Registered Cultural Heritage in 2002 and is where the music video for Seo Taiji and Boys' "Dreaming of Balhae" (발해를 꿈꾸며) was filmed.

  • Opening hours: Always open. Closed days: Open year-round. Fee: Free. Parking: Free.

The Cheorwon Migratory Bird Sanctuary (Cheorwon Plain) (철원 철새도래지) [Cheorwon-eup (철원읍), ☎ 033-450-5114] is a place where warm water comes out of the ground even in winter, so it does not freeze and migratory birds stay. From mid-September to mid-October, red-crowned cranes, white-naped cranes, geese and others come down and spend the winter. It was designated a Natural Monument in 1973. It is introduced as a place where you can join a crane bird-watching program at DMZ Crane Peace Town (DMZ 두루미 평화타운).

  • Opening hours: Always open. Fee: Free. Parking: Not available.

The Cheorwon Tourist Information Center (철원관광정보센터) [Taebong-ro 1825 (태봉로 1825), Dongsong-eup (동송읍), inside Goseokjeong National Tourist Site (고석정 국민관광지), ☎ 033-450-5558] is an information facility that introduces Cheorwon's history, security, the Cheorwon Plain and tourist attractions through exhibits.

  • Opening hours: 09:00 to 18:00. Closed days: Every Tuesday, Seollal and Chuseok holiday periods, and DMZ Marathon day. Fee: Free.

⚠ 3 Things to Know Before You Go

  1. Many places close on Tuesdays — Mulwitgil, the Jusangjeolli Trail, Hwaetbul Observatory, the Peace Observatory and Second Tunnel, and the Cheorwon Tourist Information Center are registered as closed on Tuesdays.
  2. The winter operating hours of the Jusangjeolli Trail are registered as 09:00 to 15:00 — it takes about 1 hour 30 minutes one way.
  3. The last DMZ peace tourism departure in winter is 14:00 — reception closes 15 minutes before departure, and the Second Tunnel is visited with a guide.

⚙ FAQ 2

Q. When is the ice trekking festival held?

A. According to the Korea Tourism Organization registration, it is January 16 to February 14, 2027, 10:00 to 17:00. The fee is 10,000 won and 5,000 won is returned in Cheorwon Sarang Gift Vouchers (철원사랑상품권). The programs are registered based on 2026 content.

Q. Which places cost nothing?

A. Goseokjeong, Jiktang Falls, Sambuyeon Falls, Eunhasugyo Bridge, the Former Labor Party Headquarters, the migratory bird sanctuary and the Cheorwon Tourist Information Center are registered as free. Parking at Goseokjeong is 2,000 won for small vehicles.
