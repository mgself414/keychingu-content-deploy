⚠ 5 common mistakes foreigners make

1. "K-ETA = Can work": K-ETA·Visa-free 90 days = Tourism·Short-term only. To work, you need a separate visa such as F-2-2, E-2, E-7, E-9, D-10-3.

2. "F-2-2 Warhol = All enthusiasts": F-2-2 = 18-30 years old + 27 qualification countries + Embassy draw once a year + No. 1 foreign enthusiasts (1,000 US, 4,000 Canadian, 1,500 Australian quota).

3. "D-10-3 Internship = Qualified": D-10-3 = D-2 (Study Abroad) 3-6 months after graduation Internship + Korea's No. 1 IT/corporate training center for foreign enthusiasts.

4. "Foreigner = Exemption from taxes and social insurance": Foreigner = Income tax withholding + mandatory health insurance subscription for at least 6 months + No. 1 foreign enthusiast (visa renewal denied in case of non-compliance).

5. "Illegal job placement = common": Korea's No. 1 foreigner protection = Illegal job placement/fake TEFL/visa overstay punishment + Hi Korea 1345·KCTU foreigner branch report.

① F-2-2 Working Holiday Visa (4)

1. F-2-2 Working Holiday Visa ★ Korea’s No. 1

  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — 18-30 years old, 1-2 years, non-professional work OK
  • Eligibility: 18-30 years old (35 years old in some countries) + Residence in home country + certain funds (USD $2,000+ recommended)
  • Application: Korean embassy in the home country, lottery once a year, number 1 for foreign enthusiasts

2. 27 countries eligible for F-2-2

  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, EU, Japan, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Israel, etc.
  • Enthusiasts: 1,500 in Australia, 4,000 in Canada, 1,000 in the US, 10,000 in Japan (quota)

3. F-2-2 application (Embassy/annual lottery)

  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — Embassy of home country + Lottery drawn every year from January to March
  • No. 1 for foreign enthusiasts to prepare in advance

4. Activities after possession of F-2-2

  • Foreigners ★: ◎◎ — English instructors, service industry, foreigner-friendly stores
