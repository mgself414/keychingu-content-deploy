⚠ 5 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "K-ETA = Can Work": K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization) and visa-free 90 days are for tourism and short-term stays only. To work, you need a separate visa such as F-2-2, E-2, E-7, E-9, or D-10-3.

2. "F-2-2 Working Holiday = For Everyone": The F-2-2 visa is for ages 18-30 + 27 eligible countries + annual embassy lottery + a top choice for foreigner enthusiasts (with quotas like 1,000 for the US, 4,000 for Canada, 1,500 for Australia).

3. "D-10-3 Internship = Automatically Eligible": The D-10-3 visa is for 3-6 month internships after graduating with a D-2 (Study Abroad) visa + a top choice for foreigner enthusiasts seeking IT and corporate practical training in Korea.

4. "Foreigners = Exempt from Taxes and Social Insurance": Foreigners are subject to income tax withholding + mandatory health insurance enrollment for stays over 6 months + a top priority for foreigner enthusiasts (failure to comply may result in visa renewal denial).

5. "Illegal Job Placement = Common": Korea prioritizes foreigner protection = punishment for illegal job placement, fake TEFL certificates, and visa overstays + report to Hi Korea 1345 or the KCTU (Korean Confederation of Trade Unions) Foreigner Branch.
