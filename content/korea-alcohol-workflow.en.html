Korean Alcohol Ordering and Pairing Workflow — From Making Somaek to Shaking Makgeolli

> Subtitle: A 5-step workflow for foreign tourists + drinking etiquette, somaek ratios, refusing pressure, and payment tips

ISSUE 03 · #829 · 2026-06-08

🎯 Why a Korean Alcohol Workflow Guide?

Even if you know the [#828 Korean Alcohol 6-Type Matrix](#828_korean_alcohol_6_type_matrix.md), ordering, drinking, and etiquette are separate matters. Korean drinking culture involves (a) somaek (소맥) (a mix of Soju and Beer) ratios and mixing etiquette, (b) shaking makgeolli (막걸리) (Korean rice wine) to mix the sediment + pouring with two hands, (c) turning your head away when drinking in front of elders as traditional etiquette, (d) refusing pressure at hoesik (회식) (company dinners or social gatherings), and (e) payment patterns where one person pays and others transfer money (via KakaoPay (카카오페이) or Toss (토스), popular mobile payment services in Korea). Foreigners often face pain points like (1) not knowing somaek ratios, (2) not shaking makgeolli, (3) not knowing how to refuse pressure, and (4) not adhering to drinking limits. This guide provides a 5-step workflow + etiquette matrix + English phrases + alcohol safety tips.

🍶 Somaek (소맥) (Soju+Beer) Making Etiquette

Somaek (소맥) Definition & Ratio

  • Somaek (소맥) = Soju (소주) (Korean distilled spirit) + Beer = Korea's #1 classic pairing for hoesik (회식)
  • Standard Ratio: 1:3 (Soju 1 : Beer 3) or 1:2.5 (higher Soju (소주) content)
  • 1:2 Ratio = Excessive Soju (소주) → Get drunk faster (not recommended for foreigners)

Steps to Make Somaek (소맥)

1. Prepare a beer glass (350-500ml)

2. Pour beer up to 70% (fill 70% of the glass)

3. Prepare one soju glass (45ml)

4. Pour one soju (소주) shot into the beer

5. Shake OR gently stir with chopsticks

6. Cheers and drink

Somaek (소맥) Enthusiast Tips for Foreigners

  • Somaek (소맥) = Drink Quickly: Drink within 30 seconds to 1 minute (to prevent alcohol evaporation)
  • Somaek (소맥) = Accumulates Quickly: 1 glass = 1.5-2 standard drinks, 2 glasses = 3 standard drinks = get drunk faster
  • Refusing Somaek (소맥) Pressure is OK: Say "I don't drink shots" or hold your glass with your palm over it

🍯 How to Drink Makgeolli (막걸리)

Shaking and Pouring Makgeolli (막걸리)

1. Shake: Mix the sediment (yeast) at the bottom of the bottle with the clear liquid at the top by shaking.

  • Gently shake the makgeolli (막걸리) bottle up and down 5-10 times.

2. Pouring into a Glass: Pour for an elder/superior with both hands.

3. Receiving: When an elder/superior pours for you, receive the glass with both hands.

4. Cheers: Say "건배! (Geonbae!)" (Cheers!) or "위하여! (Wi-hayeo!)" (To our health!)

5. Drinking (in front of elders): Turn your head away when drinking (traditional etiquette).

6. First Glass: Take a sip or drink it all (personal preference).

Makgeolli (막걸리) Pairing Enthusiast

  • Kimchi-jeon (김치전) (kimchi pancake) + Makgeolli (막걸리) = #1 on a rainy day
  • Haemul Pajeon (해물파전) (seafood green onion pancake) + Makgeolli (막걸리) = Traditional Korean meal
  • Nokdu-jeon (녹두전) (mung bean pancake) + Makgeolli (막걸리) = Traditional makgeolli (막걸리) bar pairing
  • Bossam (보쌈) (boiled pork belly) + Makgeolli (막걸리) = Traditional hoesik (회식)
  • MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) Trend: Milk makgeolli (막걸리), peach makgeolli (막걸리), strawberry makgeolli (막걸리)

🙏 7 Essential Drinking Etiquette Tips

Korean Drinking Etiquette (Traditional)

1. Two-Hand Pouring: When pouring alcohol for an elder/superior, use both hands.

2. Two-Hand Receiving: When an elder/superior pours for you, receive the glass with both hands.

3. Turn Head Away When Drinking: In front of elders/superiors, turn your head to the side when drinking (traditional).

4. Empty Your Glass: It's recommended to empty your glass if an elder/superior is about to pour for you (modern culture = no pressure).

5. Cheers Position: When clinking glasses, keep your glass slightly lower than an elder's/superior's (traditional).

6. Do Not Fill Your Own Glass: Someone else should pour for you.

7. Elders First for the First Drink: Elders/superiors drink first, then you.

Refusing Pressure Etiquette (Modern)

  • "I don't drink" = Direct refusal is OK.
  • "한 잔만 (Han janman)" = "Just one shot, please" (try one, then refuse further).
  • Holding a cup with your palm over it = Non-verbal refusal signal.
  • Taking medication = "약 먹어요 (Yak meogeoyo)" ("I'm taking medication") (medical reason).
  • Driving = "운전해요 (Unjeonhaeyo)" ("I'm driving") (driving reason).

> Modern Korean hoesik (회식) culture = refusing pressure is common. Refusing does not violate etiquette.

🌶 English Phrases for Foreigners

  • "소맥 한 잔 (Somaek han jan)" = "One somaek (소맥), please"
  • "막걸리 한 병 (Makgeolli han byeong)" = "One makgeolli (막걸리), please"
  • "청주 한 병 (Cheongju han byeong)" = "One cheongju (청주) (Korean clear rice wine), please"
  • "하이볼 (Haiboll)" = "Highball"
  • "건배! (Geonbae!)" = "Cheers!"
  • "I don't drink" = Korean = "안 마실래요 (An masillaeyo)"
  • "한 잔만 (Han janman)" = "Just one shot, please"
  • "카드 돼요? (Kadeu dwaeyo?)" = "Card accepted?"
  • "카카오페이 송금 (KakaoPay songgeum)" = "Send via KakaoPay (카카오페이)"

💰 Payment Workflow

Korean Payment Pattern

1. One person pays with a card (usually the hoesik (회식) host).

2. Money is transferred via KakaoPay (카카오페이) or Toss (토스) (settlement per attendee).

3. Amount = Total ÷ N (evenly divided per person).

Dutch Pay Establishments

  • MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) establishments: Many have Dutch pay systems (individual payment).
  • General hoesik (회식) establishments: No Dutch pay (one person pays).
  • Convenience stores and cafes: Individual card payment is OK.

⚠ 7 Common Mistakes by Foreigners

1. "Soju (소주) is as strong as vodka": Soju (소주) is 16-17% alcohol (less than half of vodka's 40%). It's smooth but accumulates quickly.

2. "Makgeolli (막걸리) = don't shake": Always shake (to mix sediment and clear liquid).

3. "Somaek (소맥) = free ratio": Standard is 1:3 or 1:2.5. 1:2 is too much Soju (소주).

4. "In front of elders = drink facing them": Traditional etiquette = turn your head away when drinking.

5. "Refusing pressure = etiquette violation": Modern Korea = refusing pressure is OK.

6. "Drunk driving = minor offense": 0.03% BAC (Blood Alcohol Content) = license suspension (Korean enforcement is strict).

7. "Payment = Dutch pay standard": Korean style = one person pays → transfer money is common.

⚙ 8 FAQs

1. First alcohol = which enthusiast: Beer (beginner) → Makgeolli (막걸리) (traditional) → Soju (소주) (global) → Somaek (소맥) (hoesik (회식)) progression.

2. Instagram / vlog: Makgeolli (막걸리) shaking, somaek (소맥) making, highball bar MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) vlogs.

3. MZ (Millennial and Gen Z) trends: Fruit-flavored soju (소주), milk makgeolli (막걸리), highball bars, low-alcohol soju (소주).

4. Hoesik (회식) / Business Entertainment: Soju (소주) / Beer (general) / Cheongju (청주) / Traditional Korean liquor (traditional).

5. Drinking Etiquette: Two-hand pouring, receiving, turning head away when drinking (in front of elders/superiors).

6. Drunk Driving: 0.03% BAC (Blood Alcohol Content) for license suspension + criminal penalties.

7. Allergies: Gluten (beer), soy (makgeolli (막걸리) / cheongju (청주)), fruit (fruit-flavored soju (소주)) – inform in advance.

8. VAT Refund: General food and beverages are not eligible for VAT refund.

⚠ Medical, Safety, Legal Disclaimer

Korean alcohol = contains alcohol (4-45% ABV). Drunk driving at 0.03% BAC (Blood Alcohol Content) results in license suspension + fines + criminal penalties. Pregnant individuals and those on medication = no alcohol. Under 19 years old = legally prohibited from drinking. Inform in advance about allergies (gluten, soy, fruit, nuts). In case of suspected food poisoning or excessive drinking, call ☎ 1339 (medical assistance, English available). This guide provides general information and is not medical advice.

Drinking Limit (varies by individual):

  • Standard drink = 14g alcohol (1 soju (소주) shot, 1 beer, 1 glass of wine)
  • For foreign enthusiasts = 2-3 standard drinks recommended (for 1-2 hours of meal + alcohol)
  • Beware of accumulation: 1 somaek (소맥) = 1.5-2 standard drinks

Dietary Restriction Fallback:

  • Halal / Kosher: Alcohol is non-Halal / non-Kosher. → Non-alcoholic options (Sikhye (식혜) (sweet rice punch), Sujeonggwa (수정과) (cinnamon punch), non-alcoholic beer).
  • Non-alcoholic: Sikhye (식혜), Sujeonggwa (수정과), chrysanthemum tea (국화차) (traditional) OR non-alcoholic beer, non-alcoholic cheongju (청주).
